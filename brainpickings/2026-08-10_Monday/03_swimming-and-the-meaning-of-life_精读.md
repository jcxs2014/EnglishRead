# Swimming and the Meaning of Life（游泳与生命的意义）— 精读
> 源：Brain Pickings / The Marginalian｜引 Roger Deakin《Waterlog: A Swimmer's Journey Through Britain》｜8,117 字｜URL: https://www.themarginalian.org/2026/08/08/roger-deakin-waterlog/
> 体裁：思想随笔（自然/身体哲学）｜入选理由：临在感、身体与自我

---

## 一、概述
**主题**：借 Roger Deakin《水记》谈"在自然水域中游泳如何让人重获'临在感'"，作为对抗现代"生产性文化"的**一种修行**。
**核心命题**：**一旦我们把行动指向"成就"，就给活动抽干了快乐**；唯有"为游泳而游泳"，才能抵达"超越性"。
**关键人物**：作者本人（从竞技游泳到中年回归野泳）/ Roger Deakin / Rachel Carson / Elaine Morgan / Sir Alister Hardy / Bill Hayes / Lisa Congdon / Robert Macfarlane。
**亮点**：①"存在"vs"成就"的对立 ②"水生起源说"（人本是半水生物）③"下水=穿过镜面的变形" ④"行走/骑行/游泳是颠覆性活动"。

---

## 二、逐段精读

### 1. 童年记忆（临在感）
> "One of my **earliest** and most **vivid** childhood memories is of **swimming in a cool pool bounded by boulders** in the middle of a **river** in the mountains of Bulgaria, the late-afternoon **sun casting komorebi on the water** through the **rustling leaves**. I can still **hear the feeling-tone in my body**, the **strange and lovely simultaneity of absolute presence and absolute peace**. I **didn't yet know the word for transcendence**."

- **earliest / vivid** 最早的 / 鲜活的；**swimming in a cool pool bounded by boulders** 在巨岩围成的凉水潭中游泳（bounded by = 被……围住）；**river** 河；**mountains of Bulgaria** 保加利亚群山。
- **sun casting komorebi on the water** **阳光透过树叶洒在水面的"木漏れ日"**（komorebi は木漏れ日 = 日语"叶隙阳光"，作者借词）；**rustling leaves** 沙沙作响的树叶。
- **hear the feeling-tone in my body** 还能"听见身体里的感受之调"（feeling-tone = 情感色调/感觉基调）；**strange and lovely simultaneity** 奇妙又动人的**并存**；**absolute presence and absolute peace** **绝对的临在与绝对的平静**——**并列的最高级，是全篇"临在"主题的第一次亮相**。
- **didn't yet know the word for transcendence** 当时还**不知道"超越性"这个词**（transcendence = 超越性）——**暗示童年的临在感，其实就是"超越性"的前身**。
- **为什么这样写**：用一段**极感官的童年记忆**开场，**先给出"临在+平静"的身体体验**，再点出这就是"超越性"——**让读者先"感觉"，再"命名"**。

### 2. 竞技游泳 vs 野泳（成就 vs 临在）
> "Not long after that, I began **swimming competitively in a chlorinated Olympic pool**, **investing long hours in perfecting my stroke** and **bettering my lap times**. Those four years became a **hard initiation into a culture that prizes productivity above presence**. At eleven, I was beginning to **see how** the **moment we incline action toward achievement, we drain the activity of joy**; how **anything we approach transactionally will never yield transcendence**. I **stopped swimming abruptly**, **disaffected** and **worn out**. ... **It would take me a quarter century to return to the water** — it was **only when** I was **drowning in the 800-page manuscript of my first book** that I began **swimming daily in the open ocean** to **think through the edits**, to **feel myself in the womb of the world** while trying to **birth something bigger than myself**."

- **swimming competitively** 竞技游泳；**chlorinated Olympic pool** 加氯的奥运标准泳池；**investing long hours in perfecting my stroke** 花大量时间打磨泳姿（stroke=泳姿）；**bettering my lap times** 提高每圈用时。
- **hard initiation into** 对……的艰难入门；**a culture that prizes productivity above presence** **一种把"生产力"看得比"临在"还重的文化**——**全篇核心对立**：productivity（产出）vs presence（临在）。
- **see how the moment we incline action toward achievement, we drain the activity of joy** **看出：一旦我们把行动"倾向"成就，就给活动"抽干了"快乐**（incline toward = 倾向；drain...of = 抽干……）——**全篇最有名的句子**。
- **anything we approach transactionally will never yield transcendence** 任何我们"交易性地"对待的事物，都**永远不会产出超越性**（transactionally = 交易地、功利地；yield = 产出）。
- **stopped swimming abruptly** 突然停止游泳；**disaffected** 疏离/不满的；**worn out** 疲惫不堪；**a quarter century** 四分之一个世纪（25 年）。
- **only when I was drowning in the 800-page manuscript** 直到我**淹没在 800 页书稿里**（drowning in = 被……淹没，双关：又回到"水"）；**swimming daily in the open ocean** 每天在开放海域游泳；**think through the edits** 在游泳中"想透"修改。
- **feel myself in the womb of the world** **感觉自己在"世界的子宫"里**（womb = 子宫，比喻"回到本源"）；**birth something bigger than myself** 分娩出比我自己更大的东西（=写出那本书）。
- **为什么这样写**：**用作者自身 25 年的"离开又回归"**佐证核心命题——**竞技游泳（成就导向）抽干了快乐**，**野泳（无目的的临在）才让他"在世界的子宫里"思考、分娩出作品**。**成就与临在，不是并列的两种游泳，而是对立的两极**。

### 3. Deakin 的引文（"水即记忆与思考的空间"）
> "**Such indelible swims are like dreams**, and **have the same profound effect on the mind and spirit**,' he **writes** of the **transcendences he discovered** when, **suffused with sadness at the end of a long love**, he began **swimming in rivers**, **scribbling in his notebook**:
> '**All water, river, sea, pond, lake, holds memory and the space to think.**'"

- **indelible swims** 难以磨灭的游泳（indelible = 不可磨灭的）；**like dreams** 像梦一样；**profound effect on the mind and spirit** 对心智与精神的深远影响；**transcendences he discovered** 他所发现的"超越性时刻"。
- **suffused with sadness at the end of a long love** **被一段长久爱情结束后的悲伤"浸透"**（suffused with = 浸满）——**Deakin 开始野泳的契机是"失恋"**。
- **swimming in rivers** 在河中游泳；**scribbling in his notebook** 在笔记本上涂鸦/速记。
- **All water, river, sea, pond, lake, holds memory and the space to think** **一切水——河、海、池塘、湖——都承载着记忆，和"思考的空间"**——**Deakin 的名句，也是本段的诗眼**。
- **为什么这样写**：**用一句诗化的总结**（水 = 记忆 + 思考的空间）**把"游泳 = 修行"点透**。水不是背景，是"容纳思考的容器"。

### 4. 水生起源说（人类本是半水生物）
> "Our **profound response to water** appears to be our **evolutionary inheritance** — we **came out of the ocean**, of course, but **never fully**. **Drawing on** marine biologist **Sir Alister Hardy's aquatic theory of human evolution**, later **deepened by** evolutionary historian **Elaine Morgan** in her classic *The Descent of Woman*, Deakin **writes**:
> '**We spent ten million years of the Pliocene era** of world drought **evolving into uprightness as semi-aquatic waders and swimmers** in the sea shallows and on the **beaches of Africa**. We **went through a sea change** to become what we are, and our **subsequent life on dry land is a relatively recent, short-lived affair**. ... **Apart from the proboscis monkey of Borneo**, we are the **only primate that regularly takes to the water for the sheer joy of it**. We are also **singularly hairless like dolphins**, and, **alone amongst the primates**, **have a layer of subcutaneous fat analogous to the whale's blubber**, **ideal for keeping warm in the water**.'"

- **profound response to water** 对水深刻的反应；**evolutionary inheritance** 进化的遗产；**came out of the ocean, of course, but never fully** 当然来自海洋，但**从未完全离开**（"never fully"是全句的钩子）；**Drawing on** 援引/借鉴。
- **aquatic theory of human evolution** 人类"水生起源说"；**deepened by** 被……深化；**The Descent of Woman** 《女性的渊源》（Elaine Morgan 名著，类标题戏仿达尔文《人类的由来》）。
- **ten million years of the Pliocene era** 上新世的千万年；**world drought** 全球干旱期；**evolving into uprightness as semi-aquatic waders and swimmers** **作为"半水生的涉水者与游泳者"进化出直立姿态**（uprightness = 直立；wader = 涉禽/涉水者）——**关键**：**人之所以直立，是因为在浅海中涉水/游泳**。
- **sea change** **脱胎换骨的变化**（莎士比亚借喻：to go through a sea change）；**subsequent life on dry land** 此后在陆地的生活；**a relatively recent, short-lived affair** 一段相对"新近而短暂"的经历——**陆地生活才是"新近的、短暂的"**。
- **proboscis monkey of Borneo** 婆罗洲的长鼻猴（唯一另一种"为乐趣下水"的灵长类）；**the only primate that regularly takes to the water for the sheer joy of it** **唯一一种"纯粹出于快乐"而定期下水的灵长类**（sheer joy = 纯粹的快乐）；**singularly hairless like dolphins** 像海豚一样"异常地无毛"。
- **alone amongst the primates** 灵长类中独此一家；**a layer of subcutaneous fat analogous to the whale's blubber** **一层"类似于鲸鱼鲸脂"的皮下脂肪**（subcutaneous = 皮下的；analogous to = 类似于；blubber = 鲸脂）；**ideal for keeping warm in the water** 在水中保暖的天生装备。
- **为什么这样写**：**用进化论给"人爱水"一个科学根基**——**人本是半水生物，陆地生活才是"新近的、短暂的"**。**所以"下水"不是消遣，是"回家"**。这一段的科学细节（直立、无毛、皮下脂肪）让"爱水"从诗意升为**物种记忆**。

### 5. Hardy 的"体毛即流线"洞察
> "**Hardy had arrived as his theory by way of a single, startling insight** — that the **vestigial hairs on our bodies are arranged in a pattern completely unique among apes**; that **when a human swims through a water tunnel**, the **hydrodynamic lines representing the trajectory of water flow map exactly onto the lines drawn by the pattern of body hairs**."

- **arrived as his theory by way of a single, startling insight** 通过"一个惊人的洞察"**抵达**了他的理论（by way of = 借助）；**vestigial hairs** 退化的体毛（vestigial = 退化的）；**arranged in a pattern completely unique among apes** 排列出"在猿类中独一无二"的模式。
- **when a human swims through a water tunnel** 当人游过水流通道；**hydrodynamic lines** 流体动力线；**representing the trajectory of water flow** 代表水流轨迹；**map exactly onto** **精确地"对应到"**（map onto = 映射到）；**the lines drawn by the pattern of body hairs** 体毛模式"画"出的线。
- **为什么这样写**：**Hardy 的惊人洞察**——**人体退化的体毛排列模式，正好"对应"水流的流体动力线**。**身体本身就是"为水而设计"的证据**。这是"水生起源说"最硬核的细节。

### 6. Carson 的回响
> "In **consonance with** Rachel **Carson's** **recognition that** because '**our origins are of the earth**… there is **in us a deeply seated response to the natural universe**, which is **part of our humanity**,' **Swimming appears to be our most direct way of contacting our creaturely belonging with the world**."

- **in consonance with** 与……一致/共鸣（consonance = 和谐）；**Rachel Carson** 蕾切尔·卡森（《寂静的春天》作者，海洋生物学家）；**recognition that** 她的一个认识。
- **our origins are of the earth** 我们的起源属于大地；**in us a deeply seated response to the natural universe** 我们体内对"自然宇宙"有"深植的反应"（deeply seated = 深植的）；**part of our humanity** 是我们人性的一部分。
- **Swimming appears to be our most direct way of contacting our creaturely belonging with the world** **游泳似乎是我们与"作为生物的世界归属"最直接的接触方式**（creaturely belonging = 作为生物的归属感）。
- **为什么这样写**：**用 Carson 为"爱水即爱人性"背书**——**对自然的反应"深植于我们体内、是人性的一部分"**；游泳是**触碰这种"生物性归属"最直接的途径**。

### 7. Deakin："下水 = 变形"
> "'When you swim, you **feel your body for what it mostly is — water** — and it **begins to move with the water around it**… The **swimmer experiences the terror and the bliss of being born**. So **swimming is a rite of passage**, a **crossing of boundaries**: the line of the **shore**, the **bank** of the river, the **edge of the pool**, the **surface** itself. When you **enter the water**, **something like metamorphosis happens**. **Leaving behind the land**, you go **through the looking-glass surface** and **enter a new world**, in which **survival, not ambition or desire, is the dominant aim**… You are **in nature, part and parcel of it**, in a far more **complete and intense way** than on dry land, and your **sense of the present is overwhelming**.'"

- **feel your body for what it mostly is — water** **感觉到你的身体"主要是水"**（人体约 60% 是水）；**begins to move with the water around it** 开始与周围的水"一起动"；**terror and the bliss of being born** **出生的恐惧与幸福**（游泳 = 回到"出生"的体验）。
- **rite of passage** **过渡仪式/成人礼**；**crossing of boundaries** 跨越边界；**the line of the shore / the bank of the river / the edge of the pool / the surface itself** 海岸线 / 河岸 / 池边 / 水面本身——**一连串的"边界"**。
- **enter the water** 进入水中；**something like metamorphosis happens** **某种"变形"发生了**（metamorphosis = 变形/蜕变）；**leaving behind the land** 把陆地抛在身后；**through the looking-glass surface** **穿过"镜中之镜"的水面**（looking-glass = 镜子，戏仿《爱丽丝镜中奇遇》：穿过镜子进入另一世界）。
- **enter a new world** 进入一个新的世界；**survival, not ambition or desire, is the dominant aim** **支配目标的是"生存"，而非"野心或欲望"**——**水世界里，"成就文化"失效了，只剩"活着"**。
- **part and parcel of it** **它（自然）不可或缺的一部分**（part and parcel = 组成部分）；**in a far more complete and intense way** 远比在陆地上更完整、更强烈；**sense of the present is overwhelming** **临在感压倒一切**（overwhelming = 压倒性的）——**回扣开篇"absolute presence"**。
- **为什么这样写**：**Deakin 关于游泳的核心哲学**——**下水 = 穿过镜子进入新世界**；在那个世界里**支配目标是"生存"而非"野心"**；临在感压倒一切。**"野心与欲望"在岸上、"生存与临在"在水里**——**两个世界的对照**。

### 8. Deakin 的"颠覆性活动"
> "**Such homilies on presence are also an act of resistance**, of **reclamation**, of **revolutionary rapture against the tyrannies of our present**:
> 'Most of us live in a world where more and more places and things are **signposted, labelled**, and **officially 'interpreted.'** There is **something about all this** that is **turning the reality of things into virtual reality**. **It is the reason why** walking, cycling and swimming **will always be subversive activities**. **They allow us to regain a sense of what is old and wild**… by **getting off the beaten track** and **breaking free of the official version of things**… [to access] **that part of our world which, like darkness, mist, woods or high mountains, still retains most mystery**.'"

- **homilies on presence** 关于临在的"布道/说教"（homily = 布道词，自嘲）；**act of resistance** 反抗行为；**reclamation** 收复/ reclaim（失地）；**revolutionary rapture** 革命性的狂喜；**tyrannies of our present** 我们当下的"暴政"（=现代生活的种种压迫）。
- **signposted, labelled, and officially 'interpreted'** 被"立牌指路、贴标签、官方'解读'"——**现代社会的"过度管理"三连**；**something about all this that is turning the reality of things into virtual reality** 这一切中有些东西，正在**把"事物的真实"变成"虚拟现实"**（=过度管理让真实变成虚拟）。
- **it is the reason why** 这就是……的原因；**walking, cycling and swimming will always be subversive activities** **行走、骑行和游泳，永远是"颠覆性的活动"**（subversive = 颠覆的、反抗的）；**regain a sense of what is old and wild** 重新获得"古老而野性"的感觉。
- **getting off the beaten track** **离开"被踏平的小径"**（=走出套路）；**breaking free of the official version of things** 挣脱"事物的官方版本"；**that part of our world which... still retains most mystery** 我们世界中"仍保有最多神秘"的那部分（like darkness, mist, woods or high mountains = 像黑暗、雾、树林或高山那样）。
- **为什么这样写**：**把游泳/行走/骑行提升到"政治反抗"的高度**——**现代世界把一切都"立牌、贴标签、官方解读"，把真实变成虚拟**；**行走/骑行/游泳之所以"颠覆"，是因为它们让我们"离开被踏平的小径"、挣脱"官方版本"，回到"仍保神秘的世界"**。**这是全篇最有"行动号召力"的一段**。

## 三、语法专项
1. **how + 宾语从句**：I was beginning to see **how** the moment we incline action toward achievement, we drain the activity of joy.
2. **drain A of B / make A of B**：drain the activity **of** joy / make much of negatives。
3. **过去分词作定语**：chlorinated pool / rustling leaves / bounded by boulders / suffused with sadness。
4. **并列的最高级**：absolute presence **and** absolute peace。
5. **隐喻式的被动**：drowning in the manuscript（双关：又回到"水"）。

## 五、地道表达速查
- **bounded by** 被……围住；**drain A of B** 抽干 A 的 B；**go through a sea change** 脱胎换骨；**take to the water** 下水；**map onto** 映射到；**rite of passage** 过渡仪式；**part and parcel of** ……不可分割的一部分；**get off the beaten track** 走出套路；**break free of** 挣脱。

## 六、写作技巧笔记
1. **"临在 vs 成就"的对立框架**：全篇围绕 presence vs productivity/achievement 展开，是贯穿始终的主轴。
2. **"先体验、再命名"**：开篇先给临在感的身体记忆，再点出"transcendence"；让读者先"感觉"再"概念化"。
3. **科学为诗意背书**：用 Hardy 的"体毛即流线"、Morgan 的水生起源说，把"爱水"从抒情升为物种记忆。
4. **双关与回扣**：drowning in the manuscript（"淹在书稿里" → 又回到"水"）；absolute presence 回扣开篇。
5. **"下水 = 穿过镜子"的比喻**：looking-glass surface 把野泳变成"进入另一世界"的仪式。
6. **把日常活动政治化**：行走/骑行/游泳 = "颠覆性活动"，对抗"官方解读"的现代世界。
