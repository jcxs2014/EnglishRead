## 第一段（儿童责任与年龄）

> **原句 1:** HOW DO YOU judge whether children are responsible for their actions?

**中文理解：** 你如何判断儿童是否对自己的行为负责？

**句子结构：** `HOW DO YOU judge [whether children are responsible for their actions]` —— 疑问句 + whether 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `responsible for their actions` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对自己的行为负责。例句：*Held responsible for his actions.*
- `judge` ⭐⭐ — 判断。

**为什么这样写：** 开篇提问（标题式疑问）：`HOW DO YOU judge whether ... responsible` —— "你怎么判断孩子是否该负责？"（whether 引导的间接疑问作宾语）。

> **原句 2:** The answer often depends on age.

**中文理解：** 答案往往取决于年龄。

**句子结构：** `The answer often depends on age` —— 主系宾。

**关键词：**
- `depends on age` ⭐⭐⭐ — 取决于年龄。例句：*The law depends on age.*

**为什么这样写：** 自答：`The answer often depends on age` —— "答案往往取决于年龄"（点出核心变量：年龄）。

> **原句 3:** Given how slowly human character develops, most countries do not hold the youngest to be criminally responsible, even if they commit heinous acts.

**中文理解：** 鉴于人类性格发展之缓慢，大多数国家不认为最年幼者应负刑事责任，即使他们犯下滔天罪行。

**句子结构：** `[Given how slowly human character develops], most countries do not hold the youngest to be criminally responsible, [even if they commit heinous acts]` —— Given 分词状语 + 主句 + even if 让步从句。

**关键词：**
- `Given how slowly` ⭐⭐⭐ — 鉴于……之缓慢。例句：*Given how fast things change.*
- `character develops` ⭐⭐⭐ — 性格发展。例句：*Moral character develops over time.*
- `hold ... to be criminally responsible` ⭐⭐⭐ — 认定……应负刑责。例句：*Held responsible for the crime.*
- `heinous acts` ⭐⭐⭐ — 滔天罪行（heinous = 令人发指的）。例句：*Heinous crimes.*

**为什么这样写：** 逻辑起点：`Given how slowly human character develops` —— "鉴于性格发展之慢"（Given = 鉴于）。`do not hold the youngest to be criminally responsible` —— "不认为最年幼者负刑责"。`even if they commit heinous acts` —— "即使犯滔天罪"（heinous = 最严重的）——让步句强调"无关罪行轻重，只关年龄"。

> **原句 4:** In the eyes of the law the least mature, who cannot grasp the consequences of what they do, are reckoned to be doli incapax—incapable of evil.

**中文理解：** 在法律眼中，那些最不成熟、无法理解自己行为后果的人，被认为 doli incapax——"无作恶能力"。

**句子结构：** `[In the eyes of the law] [the least mature, [who cannot grasp the consequences of what they do]], are reckoned to be [doli incapax]—[incapable of evil]` —— 状语 + 主语 + who 定语从句 + 被动谓语 + 补语 + 破折号解释。

**关键词：**
- `In the eyes of the law` ⭐⭐⭐ — 在法律眼中。例句：*In the eyes of the law, all are equal.*
- `the least mature` ⭐⭐⭐ — 最不成熟者。例句：*The least mature of the group.*
- `grasp the consequences` ⭐⭐⭐ — 理解后果。例句：*Grasp the implications.*
- `are reckoned to be` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被认为。例句：*Reckoned to be the best.*
- `doli incapax` ⭐⭐⭐ — 无作恶能力（拉丁法律术语）。例句：*The doctrine of doli incapax.*
- `incapable of evil` ⭐⭐⭐ — 无作恶能力。例句：*Incapable of wrongdoing.*

**为什么这样写：** 核心概念登场：`doli incapax` —— 拉丁法律术语（"无作恶能力"，对幼童的豁免原则），破折号后 `incapable of evil` 直接英译解释。`In the eyes of the law` —— "在法律眼中"（客观陈述）。`who cannot grasp the consequences of what they do` —— 定语从句定义"不成熟"（无法理解后果）。

**段落逻辑：** 提问（1）→ 自答：看年龄（2）→ 原则：最幼者不担责（3）→ 概念：doli incapax（4）。

## 第二段（各国差异与下调趋势）

> **原句 5:** Yet that raises a second question: how old is old enough to count?

**中文理解：** 然而这引出第二个问题：多大才算"够大"？

**句子结构：** `Yet that raises a second question: [how old is old enough to count?]` —— Yet 转折 + 主句 + 冒号 + 疑问句。

**关键词：**
- `raises a question` ⭐⭐⭐ — 引出问题。例句：*Raises a question about.*
- `old enough to count` ⭐⭐⭐ — 大到足以算数（count = 有分量）。例句：*Big enough to count.*

**为什么这样写：** 第二问：`how old is old enough to count?` —— "多大才够算数？"（count = 作数）。`Yet` 从"是否负责"转到"多大算负责"。

> **原句 6:** Countries answer this in wildly different ways, as they wrestle with the task of defining the age of criminal responsibility.

**中文理解：** 各国以迥异的方式回答这个问题，因为它们都在艰难地界定刑事责任年龄。

**句子结构：** `Countries answer this in wildly different ways, [as they wrestle with the task of defining the age of criminal responsibility]` —— 主句 + as 原因从句。

**关键词：**
- `in wildly different ways` ⭐⭐⭐ — 以迥异方式。例句：*Approach it in wildly different ways.*
- `wrestle with` ⭐⭐⭐ — 与……搏斗/苦思。例句：*Wrestling with the problem.*
- `the age of criminal responsibility` ⭐⭐⭐ — 刑事责任年龄。例句：*Raising the age of responsibility.*

**为什么这样写：** 差异：`in wildly different ways` —— "千差万别"（wildly 加强）。`as they wrestle with the task of defining` —— "在艰难界定中挣扎"（wrestle with = 苦斗）。

> **原句 7:** Now, regrettably, lawmakers in many places are pushing it downwards.

**中文理解：** 现在，遗憾的是，许多地方的立法者正在把它（年龄）往下压。

**句子结构：** `Now, [regrettably], [lawmakers in many places] are pushing it downwards` —— 状语 + 插入语 + 主句。

**关键词：**
- `regrettably` ⭐⭐⭐ — 遗憾的是。例句：*Regrettably, it failed.*
- `lawmakers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 立法者。例句：*Lawmakers passed the bill.*
- `pushing it downwards` ⭐⭐⭐ — 往下压（降低）。例句：*Pushing the age down.*

**为什么这样写：** 编辑部立场：`regrettably`（遗憾地）插入语表明态度。`pushing it downwards` —— "往下压"（把年龄降下来）。

> **原句 8:** In February Argentina lowered its age from 16 to 14.

**中文理解：** 2 月，阿根廷把年龄从 16 岁降到 14 岁。

**句子结构：** `[In February] Argentina lowered its age from 16 to 14` —— 时间状语 + 主句。

**关键词：**
- `lowered ... from ... to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 从……降到……。例句：*Lowered the age from 16 to 14.*

**为什么这样写：** 首个案例：阿根廷降龄（16→14）。

> **原句 9:** Others, from the Maldives to South Korea, may do something similar.

**中文理解：** 其他国家——从马尔代夫到韩国——可能也会效仿。

**句子结构：** `Others, [from the Maldives to South Korea], may do something similar` —— 主句 + from...to 范围。

**为什么这样写：** 更多国家：`from the Maldives to South Korea` —— "从马尔代夫到韩国"（from A to B 的范围枚举）。

> **原句 10:** Gangs have been a scourge in Sweden for many years.

**中文理解：** 多年来，黑帮一直是瑞典的一大祸害。

**句子结构：** `Gangs have been a scourge in Sweden for many years` —— 主句（现在完成时）。

**关键词：**
- `scourge` ⭐⭐⭐ — 祸害、灾祸。例句：*The scourge of crime.*

**为什么这样写：** 瑞典背景：`Gangs have been a scourge` —— "黑帮是祸害"（scourge = 鞭子/灾祸）。

> **原句 11:** It now plans to reduce the age of responsibility for the most serious crimes.

**中文理解：** 它（瑞典）现在计划降低最严重犯罪的刑事责任年龄。

**句子结构：** `It now plans to reduce the age of responsibility for the most serious crimes` —— 主句 + to reduce。

**为什么这样写：** 瑞典动向：`reduce the age of responsibility for the most serious crimes` —— "对最严重犯罪降龄"。

> **原句 12:** Last month Northern Ireland blocked a motion that would have raised the age from just ten—the joint-lowest in Europe—to 14.

**中文理解：** 上个月，北爱尔兰否决了一项本可将刑事责任年龄从区区 10 岁——欧洲并列最低——提高到 14 岁的动议。

**句子结构：** `[Last month] Northern Ireland blocked a motion [that would have raised the age from just ten—the joint-lowest in Europe—to 14]` —— 时间状语 + 主句 + that 定语从句 + 破折号插入。

**关键词：**
- `blocked a motion` ⭐⭐⭐ — 否决动议。例句：*Blocked the motion.*
- `would have raised` ⭐⭐⭐ — 本会提高（虚拟，未发生）。例句：*Would have raised the age.*
- `the joint-lowest` ⭐⭐⭐ — 并列最低。例句：*Joint-lowest in Europe.*
- `from just ten ... to 14` ⭐⭐ — 从 10 岁到 14 岁。

**为什么这样写：** 反向案例：`blocked a motion that would have raised the age from just ten ... to 14` —— "否决了本可把 10 岁（欧洲最低）提到 14 岁的动议"（would have = 本可实现的虚拟——阻止了进步）。`the joint-lowest in Europe` —— 破折号插入"10 岁已是欧洲并列最低"。

**段落逻辑：** 第二问（5）→ 各国迥异（6）→ 趋势：降龄（7）→ 案例：阿根廷（8）→ 他国（9）→ 瑞典（10-11）→ 北爱尔兰逆流（12）。

## 第三段（公众愤怒可理解）

> **原句 13:** Politicians are listening to voters angry about dramatic and well-publicised crimes—and you can understand why.

**中文理解：** 政客们在倾听那些对耸人听闻、广为人知的犯罪感到愤怒的选民——而你能理解为什么。

**句子结构：** `Politicians are listening to voters [angry about dramatic and well-publicised crimes]—and you can understand why` —— 主句 + 分词定语 + 破折号补充。

**关键词：**
- `listening to voters` ⭐⭐⭐ — 倾听选民。例句：*Listening to public opinion.*
- `well-publicised crimes` ⭐⭐⭐ — 广为人知的犯罪（publicise = 宣传）。例句：*A well-publicised case.*
- `understand why` ⭐⭐⭐ — 理解为什么。例句：*You can see why.*

**为什么这样写：** 同理心：`Politicians are listening to voters angry about ... crimes` —— "政客听从愤怒的选民"。`and you can understand why` —— "你能理解为何（降龄受欢迎）"（先共情，后反驳）。

> **原句 14:** Indonesians were horrified when a 12-year-old stabbed her mother 26 times, killing her.

**中文理解：** 当一个 12 岁女孩捅了母亲 26 刀致死时，印尼人吓坏了。

**句子结构：** `[Indonesians] were horrified [when a 12-year-old stabbed her mother 26 times, killing her]` —— 主句 + when 时间从句 + 分词结果。

**关键词：**
- `horrified` ⭐⭐⭐ — 吓坏。例句：*Horrified by the news.*
- `stabbed ... 26 times` ⭐⭐⭐ — 捅了 26 刀。例句：*Stabbed repeatedly.*
- `killing her` ⭐⭐ — 将其杀死（分词结果）。

**为什么这样写：** 案例一：12 岁杀母（26 刀）。`horrified when` —— "吓坏"（when 从句给细节）。`26 times` —— 具体刀数（增加冲击力）。

> **原句 15:** In Colombia a 15-year-old boy last year shot and killed a senator campaigning to be president.

**中文理解：** 在哥伦比亚，去年一名 15 岁男孩枪杀了一位正在竞选总统的参议员。

**句子结构：** `[In Colombia] [a 15-year-old boy] last year shot and killed a senator [campaigning to be president]` —— 地点状语 + 主句 + 分词定语。

**关键词：**
- `shot and killed` ⭐⭐⭐ — 枪杀。例句：*Shot and killed a rival.*
- `campaigning to be president` ⭐⭐⭐ — 竞选总统（campaign = 竞选）。例句：*Campaigning for office.*

**为什么这样写：** 案例二：15 岁枪杀参议员。`campaigning to be president` —— "正在竞选总统"（受害者身份显要=案件轰动）。

> **原句 16:** In Sweden and elsewhere, gangs use children to attack properties or people.

**中文理解：** 在瑞典等地，黑帮利用儿童攻击财物或人。

**句子结构：** `[In Sweden and elsewhere], gangs use children [to attack properties or people]` —— 地点状语 + 主句 + 不定式目的。

**关键词：**
- `use children to attack` ⭐⭐⭐ — 利用儿童攻击。例句：*Using minors to commit crimes.*

**为什么这样写：** 案例三：黑帮利用儿童。`use children to attack properties or people` —— "用儿童搞破坏或伤人"（瑞典黑帮利用未成年）。

**段落逻辑：** 政客听民意（13）→ 案例一（14）→ 案例二（15）→ 案例三（16）。

## 第四段（黑帮为何招募儿童）

> **原句 17:** Criminals like to recruit young foot soldiers because they are cheap and pliable.

**中文理解：** 犯罪分子喜欢招募年轻"小兵"，因为他们廉价又易摆布。

**句子结构：** `Criminals like to recruit young foot soldiers [because they are cheap and pliable]` —— 主句 + because 原因从句。

**关键词：**
- `recruit` ⭐⭐⭐ — 招募。例句：*Recruited into gangs.*
- `foot soldiers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 小兵、马仔（基层成员）。例句：*Gang foot soldiers.*
- `pliable` ⭐⭐⭐ — 易摆布的、柔顺的。例句：*Pliable and easily led.*

**为什么这样写：** 招募动机一：`cheap and pliable` —— "廉价+易摆布"（pliable = 像软泥一样可塑）。`foot soldiers` —— "小兵/马仔"（战争隐喻）。

> **原句 18:** They often lack the impulse-control and judgment that most adults possess.

**中文理解：** 他们往往缺乏大多数成年人所具有的冲动控制和判断力。

**句子结构：** `They often lack [the impulse-control and judgment that most adults possess]` —— 主句 + that 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `impulse-control` ⭐⭐⭐ — 冲动控制。例句：*Poor impulse control.*
- `judgment` ⭐⭐ — 判断力。
- `possess` ⭐⭐⭐ — 拥有。例句：*Possess the necessary skills.*

**为什么这样写：** 招募动机二：缺乏冲动控制。`impulse-control and judgment that most adults possess` —— "成年人拥有的自控和判断（他们缺）"。

> **原句 19:** Neuroscientists suggest that the brain keeps developing well into adulthood, perhaps even into the mid-20s, which may be why so much crime is committed by the young.

**中文理解：** 神经科学家认为，大脑持续发育到成年以后，甚至可能到 20 多岁中期，这或许就是为什么这么多犯罪由年轻人犯下。

**句子结构：** `Neuroscientists suggest [that the brain keeps developing well into adulthood, perhaps even into the mid-20s], [which may be why so much crime is committed by the young]` —— 主句 + that 宾语从句 + which 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `Neuroscientists` ⭐⭐⭐ — 神经科学家。例句：*Neuroscientists study the brain.*
- `well into adulthood` ⭐⭐⭐ — 一直持续到成年以后（well into = 远远进入）。例句：*Well into the 20s.*
- `the mid-20s` ⭐⭐ — 25 岁左右。

**为什么这样写：** 科学论据：`the brain keeps developing well into adulthood, perhaps even into the mid-20s` —— "大脑发育到成年后，甚至到 20 多岁"（well into = 深入）。`which may be why so much crime is committed by the young` —— "这或许就是年轻人犯罪多的原因"（非限制性定语从句说明全句）。

> **原句 20:** Teens are often enticed into gangs by the promise of quick money.

**中文理解：** 青少年常常被快速来钱的承诺诱入黑帮。

**句子结构：** `Teens are often enticed into gangs [by the promise of quick money]` —— 被动主句 + by 施事。

**关键词：**
- `enticed into` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被诱入（entice = 引诱）。例句：*Enticed into crime.*
- `the promise of quick money` ⭐⭐⭐ — 快速来钱的承诺。例句：*The lure of quick money.*

**为什么这样写：** 招募动机三：`enticed into gangs by the promise of quick money` —— "被快钱的承诺诱入"（entice = 诱惑）。

> **原句 21:** Criminals also calculate that the youngest may dodge punishment if caught.

**中文理解：** 犯罪分子还盘算着：最年幼者即使被抓也可能逃脱惩罚。

**句子结构：** `Criminals also calculate [that the youngest may dodge punishment if caught]` —— 主句 + that 宾语从句 + if 省略从句。

**关键词：**
- `calculate` ⭐⭐⭐ — 盘算。例句：*Calculated the odds.*
- `dodge punishment` ⭐⭐⭐ — 逃避惩罚。例句：*Dodged punishment.*
- `if caught` ⭐⭐⭐ — 如果被抓（省略 if they are）。例句：*If caught, they'll run.*

**为什么这样写：** 招募动机四（最关键的）：`the youngest may dodge punishment if caught` —— "最年幼者即使被抓也逃脱惩罚"（if caught 省略句）。**犯罪分子的理性计算**——这正好说明"降龄"也许能拆掉这个算盘。

> **原句 22:** In Britain drug-dealers use children precisely because they are under-age.

**中文理解：** 在英国，毒贩用儿童恰恰是因为他们未成年。

**句子结构：** `[In Britain] drug-dealers use children [precisely because they are under-age]` —— 地点状语 + 主句 + precisely because 原因从句。

**关键词：**
- `drug-dealers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 毒贩。例句：*Drug dealers exploit children.*
- `precisely because` ⭐⭐⭐ — 恰恰因为。例句：*Precisely because it's cheap.*
- `under-age` ⭐⭐⭐ — 未成年。例句：*Under-age offenders.*

**为什么这样写：** 英国例证：`use children precisely because they are under-age` —— "用儿童恰恰因为他们未成年"（precisely because = 恰恰因为，强调因果）。

**段落逻辑：** 招募动机（17-18）→ 大脑未成熟（19）→ 快钱诱惑（20）→ 免刑盘算（21）→ 英国例证（22）。

## 第五段（降龄的诱惑）

> **原句 23:** You can see, therefore, why lawmakers want to criminalise the acts of ever younger people.

**中文理解：** 因此，你能理解为什么立法者想把越来越年幼的人的行为定罪。

**句子结构：** `You can see, [therefore], why lawmakers want to criminalise the acts of ever younger people` —— 主句 + 插入语 + why 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `criminalise` ⭐⭐⭐ — 定罪。例句：*Criminalise the behaviour.*
- `ever younger` ⭐⭐⭐ — 越来越年幼。例句：*Ever younger offenders.*

**为什么这样写：** 小结：`criminalise the acts of ever younger people` —— "把越来越幼者的行为定罪"（ever younger = 一代比一代小）。

> **原句 24:** Authorities may hope that doing so will remove an incentive for gangs to recruit them.

**中文理解：** 当局或许希望这样做能消除黑帮招募他们的动机。

**句子结构：** `Authorities may hope [that doing so will remove an incentive for gangs to recruit them]` —— 主句 + that 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `remove an incentive for` ⭐⭐⭐ — 消除……的动机。例句：*Removed the incentive to cheat.*

**为什么这样写：** 降龄者的逻辑：`doing so will remove an incentive for gangs to recruit them` —— "降龄可拆掉黑帮的算盘"（incentive = 动机）。

> **原句 25:** They should reconsider, if only because lowering the age of responsibility is unjust—especially if that involves children younger than the low teens.

**中文理解：** 他们应当重新考虑——仅仅因为降低责任年龄是不公正的，尤其是当涉及低于十来岁的儿童时。

**句子结构：** `They should reconsider, [if only because lowering the age of responsibility is unjust—especially if that involves children younger than the low teens]` —— 主句 + if only because + 破折号 + if 从句。

**关键词：**
- `should reconsider` ⭐⭐⭐ — 应重新考虑。例句：*Reconsider the policy.*
- `if only because` ⭐⭐⭐ — 仅仅因为。例句：*If only because it's cheap.*
- `unjust` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不公正的。例句：*An unjust law.*
- `the low teens` ⭐⭐⭐ — 十三四岁左右（teens 早段）。例句：*Children in the low teens.*

**为什么这样写：** 立场转折（should reconsider）：`if only because lowering the age of responsibility is unjust` —— "仅仅因为降龄不公就该重想"（if only because = 哪怕只因为这个理由）。`especially if that involves children younger than the low teens` —— "尤其涉及低于低龄少年者"（the low teens = 13-14 岁左右）。

**段落逻辑：** 降龄动机（23-24）→ 但应重想：因为不公（25）。

## 第六段（现实：降龄无效）

> **原句 26:** Even if you disagree with the moral argument, you should heed the practical one.

**中文理解：** 即使你不同意道德论证，也应当重视实用论证。

**句子结构：** `[Even if you disagree with the moral argument], you should heed the practical one` —— Even if 让步 + 主句。

**关键词：**
- `disagree with` ⭐⭐ — 不同意。
- `heed` ⭐⭐⭐ — 重视、听从。例句：*Heed the warning.*
- `the practical one` ⭐⭐ — 实用的那个（论证）。

**为什么这样写：** 论证升级：`Even if you disagree with the moral argument, you should heed the practical one` —— "即使不接受道德论，也该听实用论"（Even if 让步）。`heed` —— "留意"（书面语）。

> **原句 27:** Experience suggests that lowering the age will not work.

**中文理解：** 经验表明，降低年龄不会起作用。

**句子结构：** `Experience suggests [that lowering the age will not work]` —— 主句 + that 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `Experience suggests` ⭐⭐⭐ — 经验表明。例句：*Experience shows that.*

**为什么这样写：** 核心判断：`lowering the age will not work` —— "降龄不奏效"（简洁断言）。

> **原句 28:** When Denmark took it from 15 to 14 in 2010, crime rates rose.

**中文理解：** 丹麦 2010 年把它从 15 岁降到 14 岁时，犯罪率上升了。

**句子结构：** `[When Denmark took it from 15 to 14 in 2010], crime rates rose` —— When 时间从句 + 主句。

**关键词：**
- `crime rates rose` ⭐⭐⭐ — 犯罪率上升。例句：*Rates rose sharply.*

**为什么这样写：** 丹麦反例：`When Denmark took it from 15 to 14 in 2010, crime rates rose` —— "丹麦降龄后犯罪率升了"（实证反驳）。

> **原句 29:** The country reversed course soon after.

**中文理解：** 该国很快改弦更张。

**句子结构：** `The country reversed course soon after` —— 主句。

**关键词：**
- `reversed course` ⭐⭐⭐ — 改弦更张（reverse course = 掉头）。例句：*Reversed course after criticism.*

**为什么这样写：** 丹麦补救：`reversed course` —— "掉头"（改了回来）。

> **原句 30:** Britain found that, when the age of responsibility drops, gangs just recruit even younger children.

**中文理解：** 英国发现，当责任年龄降低时，黑帮就转而招募更年幼的儿童。

**句子结构：** `Britain found [that, [when the age of responsibility drops], gangs just recruit even younger children]` —— 主句 + that 宾语从句 + when 时间从句。

**关键词：**
- `even younger children` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更年幼的儿童。例句：*Recruit even younger kids.*

**为什么这样写：** 英国证据：`when the age of responsibility drops, gangs just recruit even younger children` —— "降龄后黑帮就招更小的"（水涨船高、道高一尺魔高一丈）。**关键机制**：降龄只是把"免罪年龄线"往下移，黑帮跟着往下找更小的孩子。

**段落逻辑：** 升级实用论证（26）→ 断言无效（27）→ 丹麦反例（28-29）→ 英国机制（30）。

## 第七段（降龄制造惯犯）

> **原句 31:** Prosecuting young children as criminals is also a way of ensuring that ever more of them will emerge from the system as hardened villains.

**中文理解：** 把年幼儿童当罪犯起诉，也是在确保他们中会有越来越多的人以惯犯的身份走出司法系统。

**句子结构：** `[Prosecuting young children as criminals] is also a way of ensuring [that ever more of them will emerge from the system as hardened villains]` —— 动名词主语 + 主系表 + that 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `Prosecuting` ⭐⭐⭐ — 起诉。例句：*Prosecuted for the crime.*
- `hardened villains` ⭐⭐⭐ — 铁杆罪犯（hardened = 顽固的）。例句：*Hardened criminals.*
- `emerge from the system` ⭐⭐⭐ — 走出（司法）系统。例句：*Emerged from prison.*

**为什么这样写：** 第三宗罪：`Prosecuting young children as criminals is also a way of ensuring that ... they will emerge ... as hardened villains` —— "起诉幼童=确保他们成为惯犯"。`hardened villains` —— "铁杆恶棍"（hardened = 变硬/顽固）。

> **原句 32:** In Queensland, Australia, where ten-year-olds are counted as criminally responsible, 96% of children who are released from prison go on to reoffend within a year.

**中文理解：** 在澳大利亚昆士兰——那里 10 岁即被视为承担刑责——从监狱获释的儿童中，96% 会在一年内再次犯罪。

**句子结构：** `[In Queensland, Australia, [where ten-year-olds are counted as criminally responsible]], [96% of children who are released from prison] go on to reoffend within a year` —— 地点状语 + where 定语从句 + 主句 + who 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `are counted as criminally responsible` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被算作承担刑责。例句：*Counted as adults.*
- `released from prison` ⭐⭐⭐ — 获释。例句：*Released on parole.*
- `reoffend` ⭐⭐⭐ — 再次犯罪。例句：*A high reoffending rate.*

**为什么这样写：** 昆士兰数据：`where ten-year-olds are counted as criminally responsible` —— "10 岁就算刑责"（where 定语从句）。`96% of children who are released from prison go on to reoffend within a year` —— "获释儿童 96% 一年内再犯"——**数据触目惊心**（系统制造再犯）。

**段落逻辑：** 制造惯犯（31）→ 昆士兰 96% 再犯率（32）。

## 第八段（更明智的是改造）

> **原句 33:** It is wiser to try rehabilitation.

**中文理解：** 尝试改造更为明智。

**句子结构：** `It is wiser to try rehabilitation` —— It 形式主语 + to try rehabilitation。

**关键词：**
- `wiser to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更明智。例句：*Wiser to wait.*
- `rehabilitation` ⭐⭐⭐ — 改造、复健。例句：*Focus on rehabilitation.*

**为什么这样写：** 转向对策：`It is wiser to try rehabilitation` —— "改造更明智"（rehabilitation = 改造）。

> **原句 34:** That will often mean securing young wrongdoers safely away from wider society.

**中文理解：** 这通常意味着把年轻的犯罪者安全地隔离在更广阔的社会之外。

**句子结构：** `That will often mean [securing young wrongdoers safely away from wider society]` —— 主句 + 动名词宾语。

**关键词：**
- `securing ... away from` ⭐⭐⭐ — 隔离。例句：*Secured away from society.*
- `wrongdoers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 犯罪者。例句：*Young wrongdoers.*

**为什么这样写：** 折中：`securing young wrongdoers safely away from wider society` —— "安全隔离"（不否认需要隔离，但目的是改造）。

> **原句 35:** But rather than punishing them, the goal should be to tackle the social, educational and other factors that first drew them into criminality.

**中文理解：** 但与其惩罚他们，目标更应是解决那些最初把他们引入犯罪的社交、教育和其他因素。

**句子结构：** `But [rather than punishing them], the goal should be to tackle [the social, educational and other factors [that first drew them into criminality]]` —— 转折 + 对比短语 + 主系表 + that 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `rather than punishing` ⭐⭐⭐ — 与其惩罚。例句：*Rather than punish, educate.*
- `tackle` ⭐⭐⭐ — 解决（难题）。例句：*Tackle the root causes.*
- `drew them into criminality` ⭐⭐⭐ — 把他们引向犯罪（draw into = 卷入）。例句：*Drawn into crime.*

**为什么这样写：** 改造核心：`rather than punishing them, the goal should be to tackle the factors` —— "与其惩罚，不如解决诱因"。`that first drew them into criminality` —— "最初把他们拖入犯罪的（因素）"（draw into = 卷入）。

> **原句 36:** One focus should be on those brains.

**中文理解：** 一个重点应放在那些大脑上。

**句子结构：** `One focus should be on those brains` —— 主系表。

**关键词：**
- `focus should be on` ⭐⭐⭐ — 重点应放在。例句：*Focus on the root cause.*

**为什么这样写：** `One focus should be on those brains` —— "一个重点是（孩子的）大脑"（回指神经科学——大脑缺陷）。极简的转折。

> **原句 37:** In Britain a fifth of children in the youth-justice system have learning difficulties.

**中文理解：** 在英国，少年司法体系中的儿童有 1/5 存在学习障碍。

**句子结构：** `[In Britain] [a fifth of children in the youth-justice system] have learning difficulties` —— 地点状语 + 主句。

**关键词：**
- `the youth-justice system` ⭐⭐⭐ — 少年司法体系。例句：*Reform the youth-justice system.*
- `learning difficulties` ⭐⭐⭐ — 学习障碍。例句：*Children with learning difficulties.*

**为什么这样写：** 数据一：`a fifth of children in the youth-justice system have learning difficulties` —— "少年司法系统 1/5 有学习障碍"（大脑发育问题的佐证）。

> **原句 38:** Another should be to deal with social problems early by, say, providing mentors.

**中文理解：** 另一个重点应是尽早处理社会问题——比如提供导师。

**句子结构：** `Another should be to deal with social problems early by, [say], providing mentors` —— 主系表（不定式表语）+ by + say 插入。

**关键词：**
- `deal with ... early` ⭐⭐⭐ — 尽早处理。例句：*Deal with problems early.*
- `mentors` ⭐⭐⭐ — 导师。例句：*Provide mentors for at-risk youth.*

**为什么这样写：** 对策二：`deal with social problems early by, say, providing mentors` —— "尽早处理社会问题，例如配导师"（say 插入语=举例）。

> **原句 39:** In Sweden around half of all kids who are investigated for a serious crime were flagged to social services for worrying behaviour before the age of 12.

**中文理解：** 在瑞典，因严重犯罪被调查的儿童中，约一半在 12 岁前就因令人担忧的行为被标记并报告给社会服务机构。

**句子结构：** `[In Sweden] [around half of all kids who are investigated for a serious crime] were flagged to social services for worrying behaviour before the age of 12` —— 地点状语 + 主句 + who 定语从句 + 被动谓语。

**关键词：**
- `investigated for` ⭐⭐⭐ — 因……被调查。例句：*Investigated for fraud.*
- `flagged to social services` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被标记报告给社会服务（flag = 标记）。例句：*Flagged as at risk.*
- `worrying behaviour` ⭐⭐⭐ — 令人担忧的行为。例句：*Worrying behaviour at school.*

**为什么这样写：** 数据二：`half of all kids who are investigated ... were flagged to social services ... before the age of 12` —— "一半重罪儿童 12 岁前已被标记"——**早期预警信号存在**（可提前干预）。

**段落逻辑：** 改造更明智（33）→ 可隔离（34）→ 而非惩罚：治因（35）→ 重点：大脑（36-37）→ 重点：社会问题（38-39）。

## 第九段（结论：罚成年人+救孩子）

> **原句 40:** Nothing will stop all wrongdoing by children.

**中文理解：** 没有什么能阻止儿童所有的错误行为。

**句子结构：** `Nothing will stop [all wrongdoing by children]` —— 主句（Nothing 否定）。

**关键词：**
- `wrongdoing` ⭐⭐⭐ — 错误行为、恶行。例句：*Wrongdoing by minors.*

**为什么这样写：** 现实前提：`Nothing will stop all wrongdoing by children` —— "无法阻止所有"（承认局限）。

> **原句 41:** There should be severe criminal penalties for adults who recruit and exploit the young.

**中文理解：** 对招募和剥削年轻人的成年人，应予以严厉的刑事处罚。

**句子结构：** `There should be severe criminal penalties for [adults who recruit and exploit the young]` —— There be 主句 + who 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `severe criminal penalties` ⭐⭐⭐ — 严厉刑罚。例句：*Severe penalties for offenders.*
- `exploit the young` ⭐⭐⭐ — 剥削年轻人。例句：*Exploiting children for crime.*

**为什么这样写：** 惩罚对象（成年人）：`severe criminal penalties for adults who recruit and exploit the young` —— "严惩招募/剥削的成年人"——**把火力对准真正的元凶**。

> **原句 42:** But the goal for children should be to create better childhoods, with fewer reasons to go into crime, and to prevent criminal children from becoming criminal adults.

**中文理解：** 但对儿童而言，目标应是创造更好的童年——更少走上犯罪的理由——并防止犯罪的儿童变成犯罪的成年人。

**句子结构：** `But the goal for children should be [to create better childhoods, [with fewer reasons to go into crime]], and [to prevent criminal children from becoming criminal adults]` —— 主系表 + 不定式并列 + with 短语。

**关键词：**
- `create better childhoods` ⭐⭐⭐ — 创造更好的童年。例句：*Better childhoods mean less crime.*
- `prevent ... from becoming` ⭐⭐⭐ — 防止……变成。例句：*Prevent them from reoffending.*
- `criminal adults` ⭐⭐ — 犯罪的成年人。

**为什么这样写：** 收尾主张：`create better childhoods, with fewer reasons to go into crime` —— "创造更好的童年，少一点犯罪理由"。`and to prevent criminal children from becoming criminal adults` —— "防止犯罪的儿童变成犯罪的成人"——**首尾押韵式的收束**（criminal children ... criminal adults，把"儿童-成人"对仗）。

**段落逻辑：** 无法全禁（40）→ 严惩成年人（41）→ 儿童：更好的童年（42）。

## 长难句专项

### 🔍 原句 4（主语 + who 定语从句 + 破折号解释）

> **原句:** In the eyes of the law the least mature, who cannot grasp the consequences of what they do, are reckoned to be doli incapax—incapable of evil.

**① 找主干：** the least mature are reckoned to be doli incapax
**② 找修饰：** In the eyes of the law（状语）；who cannot grasp the consequences of what they do（定语从句）；破折号 incapable of evil（解释）
**③ 找从句：** who cannot grasp ...（定语从句）；what they do（介词宾语从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 法律眼中，最不成熟者（无法理解行为后果的人）被认为无作恶能力。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 主语 the least mature 被 who 定语从句拉长（主语-谓语距离大）；what they do 是 of 的宾语从句；`be reckoned to be`（被认为）被动陈述；破折号给出拉丁术语的英文释义。

### 🔍 原句 19（that 宾语从句 + which 定语从句指代全句）

> **原句:** Neuroscientists suggest that the brain keeps developing well into adulthood, perhaps even into the mid-20s, which may be why so much crime is committed by the young.

**① 找主干：** Neuroscientists suggest that the brain keeps developing
**② 找修饰：** well into adulthood（程度）；perhaps even into the mid-20s（补充）；which may be why ...（非限制性定语从句）
**③ 找从句：** that the brain keeps developing（宾语从句）；which may be why ...（定语从句，指代整句观点）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 神经科学家认为大脑发育到成年后甚至 20 多岁，这或许就是年轻人犯罪多的原因。
**⑤ 整体理解：** that 宾语从句主谓宾 + 状语（well into ... perhaps even ... 递进）；which 从句指代前面整个观点（不是单个名词），内部再嵌 why 从句。

### 🔍 原句 25（if only because + 破折号 + if 从句）

> **原句:** They should reconsider, if only because lowering the age of responsibility is unjust—especially if that involves children younger than the low teens.

**① 找主干：** They should reconsider
**② 找修饰：** if only because ... is unjust（原因从句）；破折号 especially if ...（条件补充）
**③ 找从句：** if only because lowering ... is unjust（原因从句）；if that involves children ...（条件从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 他们应重新考虑，仅因降龄不公——尤其当涉及低龄少年以下的孩子。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 主句极短 + 两个从句递进（if only because = 至少因为；especially if = 尤其如果）；动名词 lowering the age of responsibility 作从句主语；the low teens 用"十几岁早段"界定。

### 🔍 原句 32（where 定语从句 + who 定语从句嵌套）

> **原句:** In Queensland, Australia, where ten-year-olds are counted as criminally responsible, 96% of children who are released from prison go on to reoffend within a year.

**① 找主干：** 96% of children go on to reoffend
**② 找修饰：** In Queensland（地点）；where ten-year-olds are counted ...（定语从句）；who are released from prison（定语从句）
**③ 找从句：** where ... are counted（定语从句）；who are released ...（定语从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 昆士兰（10 岁即担责）中，获释儿童 96% 一年内再犯。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 地点状语 + where 定语从句先交代背景；主句主语 children 带 who 定语从句（被动 released）；`go on to`（接下来）表连续；96% 数字置于句首强调。

### 🔍 原句 39（who 定语从句 + 被动 + 多层状语）

> **原句:** In Sweden around half of all kids who are investigated for a serious crime were flagged to social services for worrying behaviour before the age of 12.

**① 找主干：** around half of all kids were flagged
**② 找修饰：** In Sweden（地点）；who are investigated for a serious crime（定语从句）；to social services（对象）；for worrying behaviour（原因）；before the age of 12（时间）
**③ 找从句：** who are investigated ...（定语从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 瑞典约半数重罪儿童在 12 岁前就因担忧行为被标记给社会服务。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 主语带 who 定语从句（被动 investigated for）；谓语 were flagged to ... for ... before ... 连续四个状语（对象/原因/时间）依次展开；`before the age of 12` 放句尾作关键时间点。
