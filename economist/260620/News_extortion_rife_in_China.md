# News extortion is rife in China

**文章概览：** 《经济学人》中国栏目报道。核心论点：中国的"新闻敲诈"（news extortion）现象猖獗——自媒体、地方媒体甚至部分官方媒体以负面报道相威胁，向企业和个人索要"封口费"或"广告费"。文章剖析了敲诈的运作机制（流量经济 + 监管真空 + 地方财政压力）、受害者的处境（不敢报警、怕曝光更糟），并指出这与新闻自由受限形成讽刺对比。

**文体特征：** 社会问题调查报道。具体案例（企业被勒索、博主被抓）切入 + 机制分析（流量/监管/财政）+ 制度背景（新闻业管制）。语调冷峻，暗含讽刺。

**全文结构：**
- 第 1 段：案例切入——企业被勒索
- 第 2 段：敲诈的运作机制
- 第 3 段：流量经济的推手
- 第 4 段：受害者的两难
- 第 5 段：监管的真空与矛盾
- 第 6 段：结论——敲诈的土壤

## 一、逐句精读

### 第 1 段：案例切入 —— 企业被勒索

> **原句 1:** A COMPANY director in Wuhan received a phone call he did not expect.

**中文理解：** 武汉一位公司董事接到了一个他意想不到的电话。

**句子结构：** `A COMPANY director in Wuhan received a phone call [he did not expect]`——主谓宾 + 定语从句（省略 that）。

**关键词：**
- `director` ⭐⭐⭐ — 董事。
- `did not expect` ⭐⭐⭐ — 没想到。

**表达方式：** **A COMPANY director in Wuhan received a phone call he did not expect**：`A COMPANY director in Wuhan received a phone call he did not expect`——received a phone call he did not expect（接到意想不到的电话）——悬念开场。

**为什么这样写：** 用"意想不到的电话"制造悬念——引出敲诈故事。

> **原句 2:** A man on the other end said he was a journalist, and demanded 30,000 yuan to "not publish" a critical article about the company.

**中文理解：** 电话那头的男子自称记者，并要求 3 万元以"不发表"一篇批评该公司的文章。

**句子结构：** `A man on the other end said [he was a journalist], and demanded 30,000 yuan to "not publish" a critical article about the company`——并列谓语 + 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `on the other end` ⭐⭐⭐ — 电话那头。
- `demanded` ⭐⭐⭐ — 索要。
- `"not publish"` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不发表（敲诈话术）。

**表达方式：** **A man on the other end said he was a journalist, and demanded 30,000 yuan to "not publish" a critical article**：`A man on the other end said he was a journalist, and demanded 30,000 yuan to "not publish" a critical article`——said he was a journalist（自称记者）+ demanded 30,000 yuan to "not publish"（索 3 万买不发表）——敲诈的标准话术。

**为什么这样写：** 直接呈现敲诈过程（自称记者、索财、以不发表为条件）——案例的核心事实。

> **原句 3:** When the director refused, the article ran online the next morning, with hundreds of thousands of views by lunchtime.

**中文理解：** 当这位董事拒绝后，文章第二天早上就在网上发布了，到中午就有几十万浏览量。

**句子结构：** `When [the director refused], the article ran online the next morning, [with hundreds of thousands of views by lunchtime]`——when 从句 + 主句 + with 短语。

**关键词：**
- `refused` ⭐⭐⭐ — 拒绝。
- `ran online` ⭐⭐⭐ — 上网发布。
- `hundreds of thousands of views` ⭐⭐⭐ — 几十万浏览量。

**表达方式：** **the article ran online the next morning, with hundreds of thousands of views by lunchtime**：`When the director refused, the article ran online the next morning, with hundreds of thousands of views by lunchtime`——refused（拒绝）→ ran online（发布）+ hundreds of thousands of views（数十万浏览）——不交钱的后果：文章照发且爆量。

**为什么这样写：** 用"拒绝→照发→爆量"的因果链——显示敲诈者的资源与威慑力。

> **原句 4:** He had to pay eventually.

**中文理解：** 他最终不得不付钱。

**句子结构：** `He had to pay eventually`——主谓 + 情态短语。

**关键词：**
- `had to pay` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不得不付钱。

**表达方式：** **He had to pay eventually**：`He had to pay eventually`——had to pay eventually（最终不得不付）——结局：破财消灾。

**为什么这样写：** 用简短的结局（had to pay）——敲诈成功，受害者屈从。

**段落逻辑：** 意外来电 → 自称记者索财（不发表为条件）→ 拒绝即发文爆量 → 最终付钱。第 1 段完成"案例"。---

### 第 2 段：敲诈的运作机制

> **原句 5:** This is not an isolated case.

**中文理解：** 这不是孤立的事件。

**句子结构：** `This is not an isolated case`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `an isolated case` ⭐⭐⭐ — 孤立事件。

**表达方式：** **This is not an isolated case**：`This is not an isolated case`——not an isolated case（非孤立事件）——从案例到普遍。

**为什么这样写：** 用"非孤立"——把个案推广为普遍现象。

> **原句 6:** Journalists and self-media operators across China are increasingly using the threat of bad publicity to extract money from companies and individuals.

**中文理解：** 中国各地的记者和自媒体运营者正越来越多地用曝光威胁从企业和个人那里勒索钱财。

**句子结构：** `Journalists and self-media operators across China are increasingly using the threat of bad publicity to extract money from companies and individuals`——主谓宾 + 不定式目的。

**关键词：**
- `self-media operators` ⭐⭐⭐ — 自媒体运营者。
- `bad publicity` ⭐⭐⭐ — 负面曝光。
- `extract money from` ⭐⭐⭐ — 从……勒索钱财。

**表达方式：** **using the threat of bad publicity to extract money**：`Journalists and self-media operators across China are increasingly using the threat of bad publicity to extract money from companies and individuals`——the threat of bad publicity（负面曝光威胁）+ extract money（勒索钱财）——敲诈的定义。

**为什么这样写：** 点明敲诈的主体（记者 + 自媒体）与手段（曝光威胁）——用 extract money 精准定义。

> **原句 7:** The practice has a name: "news extortion".

**中文理解：** 这种操作有个名字："新闻敲诈"。

**句子结构：** `The practice has a name: "news extortion"`——主谓宾 + 冒号说明。

**关键词：**
- `The practice` ⭐⭐⭐ — 这种做法。
- `news extortion` ⭐⭐⭐ — 新闻敲诈。

**表达方式：** **The practice has a name: "news extortion"**：`The practice has a name: "news extortion"`——The practice has a name（有专门名称）+ news extortion（新闻敲诈）——给现象命名。

**为什么这样写：** 用"有名字"句式点题——标题的"news extortion"有了定义。

> **原句 8:** It flourishes in the gap between China's strict media controls and its booming, barely regulated online-media market.

**中文理解：** 它在中国严格的媒体管制与蓬勃、几乎不受监管的在线媒体市场之间的缝隙里繁盛。

**句子结构：** `It flourishes in the gap between [China's strict media controls] and [its booming, barely regulated online-media market]`——主谓 + 介词短语。

**关键词：**
- `flourishes` ⭐⭐⭐ — 繁盛。
- `strict media controls` ⭐⭐⭐ — 严格的媒体管制。
- `barely regulated` ⭐⭐⭐ — 几乎不受监管的。

**表达方式：** **It flourishes in the gap between China's strict media controls and its booming, barely regulated online-media market**：`It flourishes in the gap between China's strict media controls and its booming, barely regulated online-media market`——flourishes in the gap（在缝隙中繁盛）+ strict controls vs barely regulated market（严管 vs 失管）——敲诈的土壤：管制的缝隙。

**为什么这样写：** 用"在 A 与 B 的缝隙中繁盛"——揭示敲诈滋生的制度背景：官方严管下的自媒体市场无人监管。

**段落逻辑：** 非孤立事件 → 记者与自媒体普遍用曝光威胁勒索 → 命名"新闻敲诈" → 土壤：严管与失管的缝隙。第 2 段完成"机制"。

### 第 3 段：流量经济的推手

> **原句 9:** The economics of the attention economy make the threat credible.

**中文理解：** 注意力经济的逻辑让这种威胁可信。

**句子结构：** `The economics of the attention economy make the threat credible`——主谓宾 + 宾补。

**关键词：**
- `the attention economy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 注意力经济。
- `make the threat credible` ⭐⭐⭐ — 让威胁可信。

**表达方式：** **The economics of the attention economy make the threat credible**：`The economics of the attention economy make the threat credible`——attention economy（注意力经济）+ make the threat credible（令威胁可信）——威胁为什么管用：流量即威慑。

**为什么这样写：** 解释敲诈的威慑力来源——负面报道有流量，流量就是杀伤力。

> **原句 10:** A critical article about a company, written in the right tone, can go viral within hours, devastating its reputation.

**中文理解：** 一篇语气得当的批评某公司的文章，几小时内就能病毒式传播，摧毁其声誉。

**句子结构：** `A critical article about a company, [written in the right tone], can go viral within hours, [devastating its reputation]`——主谓 + 分词插入 + 结果分词。

**关键词：**
- `go viral` ⭐⭐⭐ — 病毒式传播。
- `devastating` ⭐⭐⭐ — 摧毁。
- `written in the right tone` ⭐⭐⭐ — 语气得当的。

**表达方式：** **A critical article ... can go viral within hours, devastating its reputation**：`A critical article about a company, written in the right tone, can go viral within hours, devastating its reputation`——go viral within hours（数小时爆红）+ devastating its reputation（摧毁声誉）——负面文章的杀伤力。

**为什么这样写：** 描述负面报道的传播威力——go viral + devastating——敲诈的武器库。

> **原句 11:** Firms, especially those without public-relations teams, often calculate that paying is cheaper than fighting.

**中文理解：** 公司，尤其是没有公关团队的公司，常常盘算着给钱比对抗更划算。

**句子结构：** `Firms, [especially those without public-relations teams], often calculate that [paying is cheaper than fighting]`——主谓宾 + that 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `public-relations teams` ⭐⭐⭐ — 公关团队。
- `calculate that` ⭐⭐⭐ — 盘算着。
- `paying is cheaper than fighting` ⭐⭐⭐ — 付钱比抗争划算。

**表达方式：** **Firms ... often calculate that paying is cheaper than fighting**：`Firms, especially those without public-relations teams, often calculate that paying is cheaper than fighting`——calculate that paying is cheaper than fighting（算账：付钱比打官司划算）——受害者的理性选择：破财免灾。

**为什么这样写：** 从受害者视角算账——没有公关团队的小公司只能花钱消灾。

**段落逻辑：** 注意力经济让威胁可信 → 负面文章数小时爆红毁声誉 → 公司算账：付钱比抗争划算。第 3 段完成"流量推手"。---

### 第 4 段：受害者的两难

> **原句 12:** Victims rarely report the crime.

**中文理解：** 受害者很少报警。

**句子结构：** `Victims rarely report the crime`——主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `report the crime` ⭐⭐⭐ — 报警。

**表达方式：** **Victims rarely report the crime**：`Victims rarely report the crime`——Victims rarely report（受害者很少报案）——受害者的沉默。

**为什么这样写：** 短句点出反常：被敲诈却不敢报警。

> **原句 13:** Some fear that going to the police will draw even more attention to the original allegation.

**中文理解：** 一些人担心报警会把更多注意力引向最初的指控。

**句子结构：** `Some fear that [going to the police will draw even more attention to the original allegation]`——主谓 + that 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `draw attention to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 把注意力引向。

**表达方式：** **Some fear that going to the police will draw even more attention to the original allegation**：`Some fear that going to the police will draw even more attention to the original allegation`——fear that going to the police（担心报警）+ draw even more attention（引更多注意）——两难一：报警反而引关注。

**为什么这样写：** 解释沉默的原因之一：报警会让负面新闻二次发酵。

> **原句 14:** Others worry that officials, under pressure to attract investment, may not want to see a company's name dragged through the mud.

**中文理解：** 其他人担心，迫于招商引资压力的官员可能不愿看到一家公司的名字被抹黑。

**句子结构：** `Others worry that [officials, [under pressure to attract investment], may not want to see a company's name dragged through the mud]`——主谓 + that 从句 + 插入语。

**关键词：**
- `under pressure to attract investment` ⭐⭐⭐ — 迫于招商引资压力。
- `dragged through the mud` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被抹黑。

**表达方式：** **officials, under pressure to attract investment, may not want to see a company's name dragged through the mud**：`Others worry that officials, under pressure to attract investment, may not want to see a company's name dragged through the mud`——officials under pressure to attract investment（官员有招商压力）+ dragged through the mud（被抹黑）——两难二：官方也不愿公司被抹黑。

**为什么这样写：** 从官员角度解释——地方政府怕企业名声受损影响招商引资，同样不愿深究。

> **原句 15:** The result is a system where extortion is low-risk for the perpetrators.

**中文理解：** 结果是一个对实施者来说敲诈风险很低的环境。

**句子结构：** `The result is a system [where extortion is low-risk for the perpetrators]`——主系表 + where 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `low-risk` ⭐⭐⭐ — 低风险。
- `perpetrators` ⭐⭐⭐ — 实施者、作恶者。

**表达方式：** **The result is a system where extortion is low-risk for the perpetrators**：`The result is a system where extortion is low-risk for the perpetrators`——where extortion is low-risk（敲诈低风险）+ perpetrators（作恶者）——结构性结果：敲诈成本极低。

**为什么这样写：** 用 where 定语从句给结论——无人报警、官方不愿管，敲诈者几乎零风险。

**段落逻辑：** 受害者少报警 → 怕报警引关注 → 官方也不愿公司被抹黑 → 结果：敲诈低风险。第 4 段完成"两难"。

### 第 5 段：监管的真空与矛盾

> **原句 16:** The regulatory environment adds to the problem.

**中文理解：** 监管环境加剧了这个问题。

**句子结构：** `The regulatory environment adds to the problem`——主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `regulatory environment` ⭐⭐⭐ — 监管环境。
- `adds to the problem` ⭐⭐⭐ — 加剧问题。

**表达方式：** **The regulatory environment adds to the problem**：`The regulatory environment adds to the problem`——regulatory environment（监管环境）+ adds to the problem（雪上加霜）——归因：监管。

**为什么这样写：** 引入第三个因素：监管环境。

> **原句 17:** There is no independent journalism to expose the practice, since all media outlets must be licensed and can be closed down.

**中文理解：** 没有独立的新闻业来曝光这种行径，因为所有媒体都必须获得许可，而且可能被关停。

**句子结构：** `There is no independent journalism to expose the practice, [since all media outlets must be licensed and can be closed down]`——存在句 + since 原因从句。

**关键词：**
- `independent journalism` ⭐⭐⭐ — 独立新闻业。
- `must be licensed` ⭐⭐⭐ — 必须获许可。
- `closed down` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被关停。

**表达方式：** **There is no independent journalism to expose the practice, since all media outlets must be licensed and can be closed down**：`There is no independent journalism to expose the practice, since all media outlets must be licensed and can be closed down`——no independent journalism（无独立媒体）+ since must be licensed and can be closed down（因需许可且可被关停）——讽刺：媒体受控反而让敲诈无监督。

**为什么这样写：** 揭示制度悖论：新闻业被严格控制，反而没了监督敲诈的独立力量。

> **原句 18:** The irony is that officials sometimes tolerate the extortionists because they are useful against critical journalists.

**中文理解：** 讽刺的是，官员有时容忍敲诈者，因为他们对对付批评性记者有用。

**句子结构：** `The irony is that [officials sometimes tolerate the extortionists because [they are useful against critical journalists]]`——主系表 + that 表语从句 + because 原因从句。

**关键词：**
- `The irony` ⭐⭐⭐ — 讽刺之处。
- `tolerate` ⭐⭐⭐ — 容忍。
- `useful against critical journalists` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对对付批评性记者有用。

**表达方式：** **The irony is that officials sometimes tolerate the extortionists because they are useful against critical journalists**：`The irony is that officials sometimes tolerate the extortionists because they are useful against critical journalists`——tolerate the extortionists（容忍敲诈者）+ useful against critical journalists（对压制批评有用）——讽刺：敲诈者被官方利用。

**为什么这样写：** 点出最深的讽刺——敲诈者因能"对付批评记者"而受容忍。

> **原句 19:** The crackdown on critical reporting and the rise of extortion are two sides of the same coin.

**中文理解：** 对批评性报道的打压与敲诈的兴起是同一枚硬币的两面。

**句子结构：** `The crackdown on critical reporting and the rise of extortion are two sides of the same coin`——双主语并列 + 系表。

**关键词：**
- `crackdown on` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对……的打压。
- `two sides of the same coin` ⭐⭐⭐ — 同一枚硬币的两面。

**表达方式：** **The crackdown on critical reporting and the rise of extortion are two sides of the same coin**：`The crackdown on critical reporting and the rise of extortion are two sides of the same coin`——crackdown on critical reporting（打压批评报道）+ rise of extortion（敲诈兴起）+ two sides of the same coin（一体两面）——金句：打压与敲诈同源。

**为什么这样写：** 用"同一硬币两面"收束分析——对批评的打压与敲诈的泛滥同根同源。

**段落逻辑：** 监管加剧问题 → 无独立媒体曝光（需许可可关停）→ 讽刺：官员容忍敲诈者（有用）→ 金句：打压与敲诈一体两面。第 5 段完成"矛盾"。---

### 第 6 段：结论 —— 敲诈的土壤

> **原句 20:** There is little prospect of reform.

**中文理解：** 改革的前景渺茫。

**句子结构：** `There is little prospect of reform`——存在句。

**关键词：**
- `little prospect` ⭐⭐⭐ — 前景渺茫。

**表达方式：** **There is little prospect of reform**：`There is little prospect of reform`——little prospect of reform（改革前景渺茫）——结论一：改不动。

**为什么这样写：** 给出悲观的判断——敲诈的土壤短期内不会改变。

> **原句 21:** As long as the media remain tightly controlled and the online market lucrative, extortion will find a way to thrive.

**中文理解：** 只要媒体保持严格控制、在线市场有利可图，敲诈就会找到兴旺的途径。

**句子结构：** `As long as [the media remain tightly controlled and the online market lucrative], extortion will find a way to thrive`——As long as 条件从句 + 主句。

**关键词：**
- `As long as` ⭐⭐⭐ — 只要。
- `lucrative` ⭐⭐⭐ — 有利可图的。
- `find a way to thrive` ⭐⭐⭐ — 找到兴旺之道。

**表达方式：** **As long as the media remain tightly controlled and the online market lucrative, extortion will find a way to thrive**：`As long as the media remain tightly controlled and the online market lucrative, extortion will find a way to thrive`——As long as media controlled and market lucrative（只要严控+有利可图）+ find a way to thrive（必有兴旺之道）——敲诈的存活条件。

**为什么这样写：** 用 As long as 条件句——敲诈的存续由结构条件决定。

> **原句 22:** The victims will keep paying, and the extortionists will keep collecting.

**中文理解：** 受害者将继续付钱，敲诈者将继续收钱。

**句子结构：** `The victims will keep paying, and the extortionists will keep collecting`——两个并列将来时。

**关键词：**
- `keep paying` ⭐⭐⭐ — 继续付钱。
- `keep collecting` ⭐⭐⭐ — 继续收钱。

**表达方式：** **The victims will keep paying, and the extortionists will keep collecting**：`The victims will keep paying, and the extortionists will keep collecting`——keep paying（继续付）vs keep collecting（继续收）——对称句式收束：现状延续。

**为什么这样写：** 用"付钱/收钱"的对称句式结尾——给敲诈的恶性循环一个冷峻的定格。

**段落逻辑：** 改革前景渺茫 → 只要严控+有利可图敲诈必兴旺 → 受害者付钱/敲诈者收钱（对称收束）。全文完成。---

## 三、长难句专项

### 长难句 1（原句 8）

**原句：** It flourishes in the gap between China's strict media controls and its booming, barely regulated online-media market.

**找主干：** It flourishes in the gap。

**找修饰：**
- between China's strict media controls and its booming, barely regulated online-media market——介词短语（解释 gap 的两端）

**找从句：** 无从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 它在严管与失管的缝隙中繁盛。

**整体理解：** between A and B 结构——用"缝隙"两端概括制度背景。

### 长难句 2（原句 14）

**原句：** Others worry that officials, under pressure to attract investment, may not want to see a company's name dragged through the mud.

**找主干：** Others worry that officials may not want to see a company's name dragged through the mud。

**找修饰：**
- under pressure to attract investment——插入（解释官员处境）

**找从句：** that 宾语从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 有人担心，迫于招商压力，官员不愿看到公司被抹黑。

**整体理解：** 宾语从句内插入介词短语——"看 + 宾语 + 过去分词"结构。

### 长难句 3（原句 18）

**原句：** The irony is that officials sometimes tolerate the extortionists because they are useful against critical journalists.

**找主干：** The irony is that officials sometimes tolerate the extortionists。

**找修饰：**
- because they are useful against critical journalists——原因从句

**找从句：** that 表语从句 + because 原因从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 讽刺在于：官员容忍敲诈者，因为他们对压制批评记者有用。

**整体理解：** 表语从句嵌套原因从句——双层逻辑。

### 长难句 4（原句 21）

**原句：** As long as the media remain tightly controlled and the online market lucrative, extortion will find a way to thrive.

**找主干：** extortion will find a way to thrive。

**找修饰：**
- As long as the media remain tightly controlled and the online market lucrative——条件从句（省略 remain 于第二并列项）

**找从句：** As long as 条件从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 只要媒体严控且市场有利可图，敲诈必有兴旺之道。

**整体理解：** 条件从句 + 主句——注意 and 后省略动词的并列结构。

## 四、精读结束总结

### 核心词汇

本文是"社会问题调查"文本，词汇集中在：**敲诈与勒索**（extortion, extract money, demanded, perpetrators）；**传播与曝光**（bad publicity, go viral, draw attention, devastated）；**监管与控制**（media controls, regulatory environment, licensed, crackdown）；**权衡与算计**（calculate, paying is cheaper than fighting, lucrative, tolerate）。这组词是分析"制度漏洞下灰色产业"的工具包。

### 表达方式

1. **悬念开场**：received a phone call he did not expect——用悬念带出案例。
2. **案例-机制-土壤结构**：个案 → 普遍机制 → 流量经济 → 监管矛盾——由具体到抽象。
3. **命名句式**：The practice has a name: "news extortion"——给现象命名。
4. **金句式概括**：two sides of the same coin——用俗谚收束分析。
5. **对称收尾**：The victims will keep paying, and the extortionists will keep collecting——对称句式冷峻定格。
6. **讽刺笔法**：The irony is that ...——点出制度悖论。

### 语法要点

- **省略 that 定语从句**：a phone call he did not expect。
- **并列谓语**：said he was a journalist, and demanded 30,000 yuan。
- **分词插入**：A critical article, written in the right tone, can go viral。
- **where 定语从句**：a system where extortion is low-risk。
- **As long as 条件句**：As long as the media remain controlled。
- **过去分词作宾补**：see a company's name dragged through the mud。

### 长难句

最值得模仿的是"between A and B 缝隙"（原句 8）与"As long as 条件句"（原句 21）——前者适合概括制度背景，后者适合陈述结构性必然。

### 写作技巧

- **案例开场**：用一个具体受害者的故事开头，比抽象论述更有力。
- **多因素归因**：机制/流量/监管——敲诈不是单一原因，而是系统产物。
- **受害者视角**：为什么不敢报警——从两难解释沉默。
- **制度悖论**：严控媒体反而让敲诈无监督——讽刺是调查报道的锐器。

### 可迁移表达

- "X is not an isolated case" — 用于"从个案到普遍"。
- "The practice has a name: ..." — 用于"给现象命名"。
- "A and B are two sides of the same coin" — 用于"一体两面"。
- "There is little prospect of reform" — 用于"改革前景渺茫"。
- "As long as A and B, X will find a way to thrive" — 用于"结构性存续条件"。
