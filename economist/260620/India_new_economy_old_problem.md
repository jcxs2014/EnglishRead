# India's new economy still faces an old problem

**文章概览：** 《经济学人》Leaders 社论，2026-06-17。核心论点：印度正经历"新经济"热潮（AI、半导体、高端制造），但"旧问题"依旧——就业与生产率不足、大企业与小企业割裂、教育没跟上。文章认为：新经济是印度的机会，但若想惠及大众，必须同时解决老问题（基础教育、劳动力市场、中小企业融资），否则繁荣只是少数人的狂欢。

**文体特征：** 经济发展评述。用"新 vs 旧"的对照结构贯穿（new economy / old problem）；用具体数字（IT 产业占比、就业缺口、生产率）支撑；既有对印度前景的乐观，也有对结构性问题的清醒。

**全文结构：**
- 第 1 段：新经济热潮——AI、半导体、制造回流
- 第 2 段：但旧问题依旧——就业与生产率
- 第 3 段：大企业狂欢 vs 小企业挣扎
- 第 4 段：教育的瓶颈
- 第 5 段：机会窗口——改革正当其时
- 第 6 段：结论——新经济需要新配套

---

## 一、逐句精读

### 第 1 段：新经济热潮 —— AI、半导体、制造回流

> **原句 1:** INDIA IS experiencing a tech-and-manufacturing boom.

**中文理解：** 印度正在经历一场科技与制造热潮。

**句子结构：** `INDIA IS experiencing a tech-and-manufacturing boom`——主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `a tech-and-manufacturing boom` ⭐⭐⭐ — 科技与制造热潮。

**表达方式：** **INDIA IS experiencing a tech-and-manufacturing boom**：`INDIA IS experiencing a tech-and-manufacturing boom`——INDIA IS experiencing（印度正经历）+ a tech-and-manufacturing boom（科技制造热潮）——开场直陈繁荣。

**为什么这样写：** 用一个简短而自信的句子开门见山——先承认繁荣，为后文"但"铺垫。

> **原句 2:** Factories are opening, from Gujarat to Tamil Nadu; AI start-ups are attracting record venture capital; and multinationals are shifting supply chains away from China.

**中文理解：** 从古吉拉特邦到泰米尔纳德邦，工厂纷纷开张；AI 初创公司吸引创纪录的风险投资；跨国公司正把供应链从中国转移出来。

**句子结构：** 三个并列分句（分号连接）：`Factories are opening, [from Gujarat to Tamil Nadu]; [AI start-ups are attracting record venture capital]; and [multinationals are shifting supply chains away from China]`——三连排比。

**关键词：**
- `from Gujarat to Tamil Nadu` ⭐⭐⭐ — 从古吉拉特到泰米尔纳德。
- `record venture capital` ⭐⭐⭐ — 创纪录的风投。
- `shifting supply chains away from China` ⭐⭐⭐ — 把供应链移出中国。

**表达方式：** **Factories are opening ...; AI start-ups are attracting record venture capital; and multinationals are shifting supply chains away from China**：`Factories are opening, from Gujarat to Tamil Nadu`——分号三连（工厂/风投/供应链）——新经济的三条证据。

**为什么这样写：** 用分号排比罗列三个信号（工厂/风投/供应链转移）——繁荣不是空话，有具体迹象。

> **原句 3:** The optimism is understandable.

**中文理解：** 这种乐观情绪是可以理解的。

**句子结构：** `The optimism is understandable`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `The optimism` ⭐⭐⭐ — 乐观情绪。

**表达方式：** **The optimism is understandable**：`The optimism is understandable`——The optimism is understandable（乐观可理解）——承认乐观的合理性。

**为什么这样写：** 给乐观情绪一个公道——为接下来的转折铺路（"但……"）。

> **原句 4:** Yet the boom risks being confined to a narrow slice of the country.

**中文理解：** 然而，这场热潮有被局限于国家一小部分地区的风险。

**句子结构：** `Yet the boom risks being confined to a narrow slice of the country`——Yet + 主谓宾 + 动名词宾语。

**关键词：**
- `risks being confined to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 有被局限的风险。
- `a narrow slice of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 很小的一部分。

**表达方式：** **Yet the boom risks being confined to a narrow slice of the country**：`Yet the boom risks being confined to a narrow slice of the country`——Yet（然而）+ risks being confined to（有被局限的风险）+ a narrow slice（一小部分）——繁荣的隐忧：覆盖面窄。

**为什么这样写：** Yet 转折点题：繁荣虽好，但可能只惠及少数人——引出"旧问题"。

**段落逻辑：** 繁荣直陈（科技制造热潮）→ 三条证据（工厂/风投/供应链）→ 乐观可理解 → 但风险：局限少数人。第 1 段完成"新经济现状"。---

### 第 2 段：但旧问题依旧 —— 就业与生产率

> **原句 5:** India's old problems have not gone away.

**中文理解：** 印度的老问题并没有消失。

**句子结构：** `India's old problems have not gone away`——主谓。

**关键词：**
- `old problems` ⭐⭐⭐ — 老问题。
- `have not gone away` ⭐⭐⭐ — 没有消失。

**表达方式：** **India's old problems have not gone away**：`India's old problems have not gone away`——old problems（老问题）+ have not gone away（没有消失）——点题句：旧问题仍在。

**为什么这样写：** 直扣标题（old problem）——新经济之外，老问题依旧。

> **原句 6:** Half the workforce is still in agriculture, mostly as subsistence farmers, while the share of manufacturing in GDP has barely risen.

**中文理解：** 一半劳动力仍在农业，大多为自给自足的农民，而制造业在 GDP 中的占比几乎没涨。

**句子结构：** `Half the workforce is still in agriculture, [mostly as subsistence farmers], while [the share of manufacturing in GDP has barely risen]`——主系表 + 插入 + while 对比从句。

**关键词：**
- `subsistence farmers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 自给自足的农民。
- `the share of manufacturing in GDP` ⭐⭐⭐ — 制造业占 GDP 比重。

**表达方式：** **Half the workforce is still in agriculture ... while the share of manufacturing in GDP has barely risen**：`Half the workforce is still in agriculture, mostly as subsistence farmers`——Half the workforce（一半劳动力）+ subsistence farmers（糊口农业）+ while ... barely risen（而制造占比几乎没涨）——就业结构未变。

**为什么这样写：** 用"一半在农业 + 制造业占比停滞"两个数据——新经济之外的旧经济现实。

> **原句 7:** Productivity growth has stalled, which is why wages have stagnated.

**中文理解：** 生产率增长停滞，这就是工资停滞的原因。

**句子结构：** `Productivity growth has stalled, [which is why wages have stagnated]`——主谓 + 非限制性定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `Productivity growth` ⭐⭐⭐ — 生产率增长。
- `stalled` ⭐⭐⭐ — 停滞。
- `stagnated` ⭐⭐⭐ — 停滞（工资）。

**表达方式：** **Productivity growth has stalled, which is why wages have stagnated**：`Productivity growth has stalled, which is why wages have stagnated`——Productivity growth has stalled（生产率停滞）+ which is why（这就是……的原因）+ wages have stagnated（工资停滞）——停滞的因果链。

**为什么这样写：** 用 which is why 揭示因果：生产率停滞 → 工资停滞——旧问题的核心。

> **原句 8:** For the boom to help ordinary Indians, this must change.

**中文理解：** 要让这场繁荣惠及普通印度人，这一点必须改变。

**句子结构：** `For the boom to help ordinary Indians, [this must change]`——For 不定式短语（目的）+ 主句。

**关键词：**
- `For the boom to help ordinary Indians` ⭐⭐⭐ — 要让繁荣惠及普通印度人。
- `this must change` ⭐⭐⭐ — 这必须改变。

**表达方式：** **For the boom to help ordinary Indians, this must change**：`For the boom to help ordinary Indians, this must change`——For ... to help（要让……惠及）+ this must change（必须改变）——繁荣惠众的前提。

**为什么这样写：** 用 For 不定式表必要条件——繁荣要惠众，生产率问题必须解决。

**段落逻辑：** 旧问题仍在 → 就业结构（一半农业）+ 制造停滞 → 生产率停滞→工资停滞 → 要惠众必须改变。第 2 段完成"旧问题盘点"。---

### 第 3 段：大企业狂欢 vs 小企业挣扎

> **原句 9:** The boom is also uneven within the economy.

**中文理解：** 这场繁荣在经济体内部也是不平衡的。

**句子结构：** `The boom is also uneven within the economy`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `uneven` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不平衡的。

**表达方式：** **The boom is also uneven within the economy**：`The boom is also uneven within the economy`——also uneven（也不均衡）——从"地区不均"转到"企业规模不均"。

**为什么这样写：** also 承接上段——繁荣不均的第二层：企业规模层面。

> **原句 10:** Large firms are thriving, but the millions of small firms that employ most Indians are being squeezed.

**中文理解：** 大企业蒸蒸日上，但雇佣了大多数印度人的数百万小企业正被挤压。

**句子结构：** `Large firms are thriving, but [the millions of small firms [that employ most Indians] are being squeezed]`——并列句 + that 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `thriving` ⭐⭐⭐ — 蒸蒸日上。
- `being squeezed` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被挤压。

**表达方式：** **Large firms are thriving, but the millions of small firms that employ most Indians are being squeezed**：`Large firms are thriving, but the millions of small firms that employ most Indians are being squeezed`——Large firms are thriving（大企繁荣）vs being squeezed（小企被挤）——繁荣的两极。

**为什么这样写：** 用 but 对照大企繁荣/小企被挤压——"繁荣的 M 型化"。

> **原句 11:** Regulations that protect workers in big companies make it hard for small ones to hire, even as the informal sector absorbs most new workers.

**中文理解：** 保护大公司工人的法规让小公司难以招聘，即便非正规部门吸收了大多数新工人。

**句子结构：** `Regulations [that protect workers in big companies] make it hard for small ones to hire, [even as the informal sector absorbs most new workers]`——主谓宾 + that 从句 + even as 从句。

**关键词：**
- `make it hard to hire` ⭐⭐⭐ — 让招聘变得困难。
- `the informal sector` ⭐⭐⭐ — 非正规部门。
- `even as` ⭐⭐⭐ — 即便在……时。

**表达方式：** **Regulations that protect workers in big companies make it hard for small ones to hire, even as the informal sector absorbs most new workers**：`Regulations that protect workers in big companies make it hard for small ones to hire`——make it hard to hire（难以招人）+ even as informal sector absorbs（即便非正规部门吸纳）——制度的反效果。

**为什么这样写：** 指出制度性症结：保护大企业劳工的法规，恰恰让小企业雇不起人——工人涌入非正规部门。

> **原句 12:** The result is a dual economy, split between a protected core and an unprotected periphery.

**中文理解：** 结果是一个二元经济：分裂为受保护的核心与不受保护的外围。

**句子结构：** `The result is a dual economy, [split between a protected core and an unprotected periphery]`——主系表 + 分词短语。

**关键词：**
- `a dual economy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 二元经济。
- `a protected core` ⭐⭐⭐ — 受保护的核心。
- `an unprotected periphery` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不受保护的外围。

**表达方式：** **The result is a dual economy, split between a protected core and an unprotected periphery**：`The result is a dual economy, split between a protected core and an unprotected periphery`——a dual economy（二元经济）+ split between core and periphery（核心/外围分裂）——"二元经济"概念。

**为什么这样写：** 用"核心/外围"二分法概括印度经济结构——受保护的核心企业与无人保护的外围劳动者。

**段落逻辑：** 繁荣也不均 → 大企繁荣/小企被挤 → 法规反效果（小企难雇人）→ 二元经济（核心/外围）。第 3 段完成"二元经济"。---

### 第 4 段：教育的瓶颈

> **原句 13:** Education is the deepest bottleneck.

**中文理解：** 教育是最深的瓶颈。

**句子结构：** `Education is the deepest bottleneck`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `the deepest bottleneck` ⭐⭐⭐ — 最深的瓶颈。

**表达方式：** **Education is the deepest bottleneck**：`Education is the deepest bottleneck`——Education（教育）+ the deepest bottleneck（最深瓶颈）——短句重锤：教育卡脖子。

**为什么这样写：** 用超短句引出教育问题——点明"旧问题"的根源。

> **原句 14:** The AI boom demands a workforce with strong numeracy and problem-solving, yet a large share of Indian schoolchildren cannot read or do basic arithmetic by the age of ten.

**中文理解：** AI 热潮需要具备强大数理能力和解题能力的劳动力，然而很大一部分印度学童到十岁还不会读写或做基本算术。

**句子结构：** `The AI boom demands a workforce [with strong numeracy and problem-solving], yet [a large share of Indian schoolchildren cannot read or do basic arithmetic by the age of ten]`——并列句 + with 短语 + yet 转折。

**关键词：**
- `numeracy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 数理能力。
- `problem-solving` ⭐⭐⭐ — 解决问题的能力。
- `basic arithmetic` ⭐⭐⭐ — 基本算术。

**表达方式：** **The AI boom demands a workforce with strong numeracy and problem-solving, yet a large share of Indian schoolchildren cannot read or do basic arithmetic by the age of ten**：`The AI boom demands a workforce with strong numeracy and problem-solving, yet ... cannot read or do basic arithmetic`——demands a workforce（需要劳动力）vs cannot read or do arithmetic（不会读写算术）——AI 需求与教育现实的鸿沟。

**为什么这样写：** 用 yet 制造反差：AI 要数理人才，基础教育却连读写都没过关——需求与供给脱节。

> **原句 15:** Reforming schools is politically hard, but it is the most important thing India could do to make its boom last.

**中文理解：** 改革学校在政治上很困难，但这是印度为了让繁荣持续所能做的最重要的事。

**句子结构：** `Reforming schools is politically hard, but [it is the most important thing [India could do to make its boom last]]`——并列句 + 定语从句（省略 that）。

**关键词：**
- `politically hard` ⭐⭐⭐ — 政治上困难。
- `make its boom last` ⭐⭐⭐ — 让繁荣持续。

**表达方式：** **Reforming schools is politically hard, but it is the most important thing India could do to make its boom last**：`Reforming schools is politically hard, but it is the most important thing India could do to make its boom last`——politically hard（政治难）but most important thing（最重要之事）——难做但必做。

**为什么这样写：** 用"难但最重要"的让步结构——给教育改革以最高优先级。

**段落逻辑：** 教育是最深瓶颈 → AI 需求 vs 基础教育鸿沟 → 改革难但最重要。第 4 段完成"教育瓶颈"。

### 第 5 段：机会窗口 —— 改革正当其时

> **原句 16:** India's moment is now.

**中文理解：** 印度的时刻就是现在。

**句子结构：** `India's moment is now`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `India's moment` ⭐⭐⭐ — 印度的时刻。

**表达方式：** **India's moment is now**：`India's moment is now`——India's moment is now（印度的时刻就是现在）——短句宣言：时机已到。

**为什么这样写：** 用四个字的短句造势——机会窗口正开。

> **原句 17:** Global firms are diversifying away from China, interest rates have fallen, and a youthful population gives India a demographic edge.

**中文理解：** 全球企业正从中国多元化转移，利率已下降，年轻的印度人口给了它人口结构的优势。

**句子结构：** 三连并列句（分号/逗号）：`Global firms are diversifying away from China, [interest rates have fallen], and [a youthful population gives India a demographic edge]`——三连排比。

**关键词：**
- `diversifying away from` ⭐⭐⭐ — 从……多元化转移。
- `a demographic edge` ⭐⭐⭐ — 人口结构的优势。

**表达方式：** **Global firms are diversifying away from China, interest rates have fallen, and a youthful population gives India a demographic edge**：`Global firms are diversifying away from China, interest rates have fallen, and a youthful population gives India a demographic edge`——三连（供应链/利率/人口）——三个有利条件叠加。

**为什么这样写：** 用三连排比列出印度天时地利：供应链转移、低利率、人口红利。

> **原句 18:** The government should seize this moment to tackle the old problems rather than bask in the new boom.

**中文理解：** 政府应该抓住这个时刻解决老问题，而不是沉浸在新繁荣中沾沾自喜。

**句子结构：** `The government should seize this moment to tackle the old problems [rather than bask in the new boom]`——主谓宾 + 不定式 + rather than 对比。

**关键词：**
- `seize this moment` ⭐⭐⭐ — 抓住这个时刻。
- `tackle the old problems` ⭐⭐⭐ — 解决老问题。
- `bask in` ⭐⭐⭐ — 沉浸于、享受。

**表达方式：** **seize this moment to tackle the old problems rather than bask in the new boom**：`The government should seize this moment to tackle the old problems rather than bask in the new boom`——seize this moment（抓时机）+ tackle old problems（解旧题）+ rather than bask in（而非沉迷于）——政策建议：借势改革而非躺赢。

**为什么这样写：** 用 seize ... rather than bask 的对比给出核心建议——机会要用来改革。

**段落逻辑：** 时刻就是现在 → 三个有利条件（供应链/利率/人口）→ 政府应借机解决旧问题而非沉浸繁荣。第 5 段完成"机会窗口"。---

### 第 6 段：结论 —— 新经济需要新配套

> **原句 19:** The lesson of other emerging markets is that booms without structural reform fizzle out.

**中文理解：** 其他新兴市场的教训是：没有结构改革的繁荣终将昙花一现。

**句子结构：** `The lesson of other emerging markets is that [booms without structural reform fizzle out]`——主系表 + that 表语从句。

**关键词：**
- `emerging markets` ⭐⭐⭐ — 新兴市场。
- `structural reform` ⭐⭐⭐ — 结构改革。
- `fizzle out` ⭐⭐⭐ — 无疾而终、虎头蛇尾。

**表达方式：** **booms without structural reform fizzle out**：`The lesson of other emerging markets is that booms without structural reform fizzle out`——booms without structural reform（无改革之繁荣）+ fizzle out（虎头蛇尾）——历史教训：无改革必昙花一现。

**为什么这样写：** 用"其他新兴市场"作镜子——没有结构改革的繁荣无法持久。

> **原句 20:** India should not repeat that mistake.

**中文理解：** 印度不应该重蹈覆辙。

**句子结构：** `India should not repeat that mistake`——主谓宾 + 否定。

**关键词：**
- `repeat that mistake` ⭐⭐⭐ — 重蹈覆辙。

**表达方式：** **India should not repeat that mistake**：`India should not repeat that mistake`——should not repeat that mistake（别重蹈覆辙）——直接呼吁。

**为什么这样写：** 用短句直接呼吁——避免重蹈其他市场的覆辙。

> **原句 21:** The new economy will be judged not by its headline numbers, but by whether it lifts the lives of ordinary Indians.

**中文理解：** 新经济将不是以它的头条数字来评判，而是看它是否提升了普通印度人的生活。

**句子结构：** `The new economy will be judged [not by its headline numbers, but by whether it lifts the lives of ordinary Indians]`——主谓被动 + not...but 并列。

**关键词：**
- `headline numbers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 头条数字（宣传性数据）。
- `lifts the lives of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 提升……的生活。

**表达方式：** **judged not by its headline numbers, but by whether it lifts the lives of ordinary Indians**：`The new economy will be judged not by its headline numbers, but by whether it lifts the lives of ordinary Indians`——judged not by ... but by（评判标准转换）+ headline numbers vs lifts the lives of ordinary Indians（头条数字 vs 民生改善）——评判标准：民生。

**为什么这样写：** 用 not...but 给出评判标准——繁荣的成色要看普通人的生活是否提升。

> **原句 22:** That is the real test of whether India's moment has truly arrived.

**中文理解：** 那才是印度的时刻是否真正到来的真正检验。

**句子结构：** `That is the real test of [whether India's moment has truly arrived]`——主系表 + of 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `the real test` ⭐⭐⭐ — 真正的检验。
- `truly arrived` ⭐⭐⭐ — 真正到来。

**表达方式：** **That is the real test of whether India's moment has truly arrived**：`That is the real test of whether India's moment has truly arrived`——the real test（真正检验）+ whether ... has truly arrived（是否真正到来）——呼应"moment is now"，用民生检验繁荣。

**为什么这样写：** 收束呼应第 5 段"India's moment is now"——用"民生是否改善"作为真正检验。

**段落逻辑：** 他国教训（无改革繁荣昙花一现）→ 别重蹈覆辙 → 评判标准（民生而非数字）→ 真正检验（时刻是否到来）。全文完成。---

## 二、长难句专项

### 长难句 1（原句 2）

**原句：** Factories are opening, from Gujarat to Tamil Nadu; AI start-ups are attracting record venture capital; and multinationals are shifting supply chains away from China.

**找主干：** 三个并列分句：Factories are opening / AI start-ups are attracting record venture capital / multinationals are shifting supply chains away from China。

**找修饰：**
- from Gujarat to Tamil Nadu——地点范围短语（修饰 factories）
- away from China——方向短语（修饰 shifting）

**找从句：** 无从句，全为并列分句。

**恢复逻辑：** 工厂开张、AI 创投破纪录、跨国公司撤华——三个并列信号。

**整体理解：** 典型的分号排比句，三个证据并列呈现，节奏急促有力。

### 长难句 2（原句 6）

**原句：** Half the workforce is still in agriculture, mostly as subsistence farmers, while the share of manufacturing in GDP has barely risen.

**找主干：** Half the workforce is still in agriculture / the share of manufacturing in GDP has barely risen（while 连接两个分句）。

**找修饰：**
- mostly as subsistence farmers——插入（限定 agriculture 的性质）
- in GDP——介词短语

**找从句：** while 对比从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 一半劳动力还在农业（多为自给自足），而制造业占 GDP 比重几乎未升。

**整体理解：** while 表对比：农业就业多 vs 制造占比停滞——用分号结构呈现就业结构的不变。

### 长难句 3（原句 11）

**原句：** Regulations that protect workers in big companies make it hard for small ones to hire, even as the informal sector absorbs most new workers.

**找主干：** Regulations make it hard for small ones to hire（主谓宾 + 宾补）。

**找修饰：**
- that protect workers in big companies——定语从句修饰 regulations
- for small ones——不定式逻辑主语
- even as the informal sector absorbs most new workers——让步从句

**找从句：** 定语从句（that）+ 让步从句（even as）。

**恢复逻辑：** 保护大企业工人的法规让小企业难招人，即便非正规部门吸纳了大多数新工人。

**整体理解：** 主干是"法规让招聘变难"，that 从句限定法规性质，even as 从句补充反衬（非正规部门在吸纳）——制度与现实的悖论。

### 长难句 4（原句 17）

**原句：** Global firms are diversifying away from China, interest rates have fallen, and a youthful population gives India a demographic edge.

**找主干：** 三个并列分句。

**找修饰：**
- away from China——方向短语
- a demographic edge——双宾语（India 为间接宾语）

**找从句：** 无从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 全球企业从中国转移、利率下降、年轻人口提供人口红利。

**整体理解：** 三连排比列出三个有利条件，节奏铿锵。

## 三、精读结束总结

### 表达方式

1. **"新 vs 旧"对照结构**：new economy / old problem——全文骨架。
2. **三连排比罗列证据**：Factories ...; AI start-ups ...; multinationals ...——用分号排比呈现繁荣。
3. **短句重锤**：Education is the deepest bottleneck / India's moment is now——短句造势。
4. **not...but 评判标准**：judged not by its headline numbers, but by whether it lifts the lives——用对仗给标准。
5. **他国经验引证**：The lesson of other emerging markets——借他国教训警示。
6. **首尾呼应**：India's moment is now ... whether India's moment has truly arrived——首尾回环。

### 语法要点

- **分号三连排比**：Factories are opening; ...; and multinationals are ...——并列分句用分号。
- **while 对比从句**：Half the workforce is still in agriculture, while the share of manufacturing ...——对比转折。
- **even as 让步**：even as the informal sector absorbs most new workers——表"即便同时"。
- **形式宾语 make it hard to**：make it hard for small ones to hire——it 形式宾语 + 不定式。
- **双重否定/否定式平衡**：not entirely without foundation / not by ... but by——对仗否定。
- **For 不定式表目的**：For the boom to help ordinary Indians, this must change——表必要条件。

### 长难句

最值得模仿的是"分号三连排比"（原句 2、17）：三个并列分句一气呵成，节奏感强。其次是 even as 让步（原句 11）：用让步句制造"制度悖论"。

### 写作技巧

- **先扬后抑**：先承认繁荣（乐观可理解），再点风险（局限少数人）——评论的辩证开场。
- **二元概念概括**：dual economy, split between core and periphery——用一个概念统摄复杂结构。
- **借他国教训**：other emerging markets——用先例增加说服力。
- **以民生定成败**：judged ... by whether it lifts the lives of ordinary Indians——把评判标准锚定在普通人。

### 可迁移表达

- "booms without structural reform fizzle out" — 用于"无改革之繁荣不持久"。
- "a dual economy, split between a protected core and an unprotected periphery" — 用于描述经济分化。
- "seize this moment to tackle old problems rather than bask in the new boom" — 用于"借势改革"。
- "judged not by its headline numbers, but by whether it lifts people's lives" — 用于"以民生为检验标准"。
