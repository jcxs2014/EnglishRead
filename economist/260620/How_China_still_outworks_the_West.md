# How China still outworks the West

**文章概览：** 《经济学人》中国栏目报道。核心论点：尽管中国人口老龄化、劳动年龄人口下降，中国仍以"更努力"著称——工作文化（加班、单休、奋斗精神）与制度（996、绩效制、政府动员）使中国在"努力程度"上仍领先西方。文章对比中西工作文化差异，并讨论这种"努力"的代价与可持续性。

**文体特征：** 社会文化分析报道。用具体案例（互联网公司、工厂、公务员）+ 数据（工时、加班率）+ 文化对比（中国"内卷" vs 欧洲"慢生活"）。语调冷静，略带批判。

**全文结构：**
- 第 1 段：开场——中国"更努力"的印象
- 第 2 段：数据支撑——工时全球领先
- 第 3 段：文化根源——奋斗精神与制度
- 第 4 段：政府动员与"内卷"
- 第 5 段：代价——健康、家庭、创新
- 第 6 段：结论——努力文化还能持续吗

## 一、逐句精读

### 第 1 段：开场 —— 中国"更努力"的印象

> **原句 1:** CHINA IS the only big economy whose citizens are famous for their work ethic.

**中文理解：** 中国是唯一一个公民以职业道德闻名的经济大国。

**句子结构：** `CHINA IS the only big economy [whose citizens are famous for their work ethic]`——主系表 + whose 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `the only big economy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 唯一的经济大国。
- `work ethic` ⭐⭐⭐ — 职业道德、勤奋观。

**表达方式：** **CHINA IS the only big economy whose citizens are famous for their work ethic**：`CHINA IS the only big economy whose citizens are famous for their work ethic`——the only big economy（唯一经济大国）+ famous for their work ethic（以勤奋闻名）——中国=勤奋的代名词。

**为什么这样写：** 开篇即断言"唯一以勤奋闻名的经济大国"——把"努力"作为中国的国家名片。

> **原句 2:** Visitors to Shanghai or Shenzhen often remark that the city seems to be running at double speed.

**中文理解：** 访问上海或深圳的游客常感叹这座城市似乎以双倍速度运转。

**句子结构：** `Visitors to Shanghai or Shenzhen often remark that [the city seems to be running at double speed]`——主谓 + that 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `remark that` ⭐⭐⭐ — 感叹说。
- `running at double speed` ⭐⭐⭐ — 以双倍速度运转。

**表达方式：** **Visitors ... often remark that the city seems to be running at double speed**：`Visitors to Shanghai or Shenzhen often remark that the city seems to be running at double speed`——Visitors often remark（游客常感叹）+ running at double speed（双倍速运转）——游客视角的"快"。

**为什么这样写：** 用游客的直观感受（double speed）——"快"不是数据，是身临其境的印象。

> **原句 3:** The escalators in the subway run faster; the takeaways arrive faster; even the traffic lights seem quicker.

**中文理解：** 地铁里的自动扶梯更快，外卖到得更快，连红绿灯似乎都更快。

**句子结构：** 三连并列句（分号）：`The escalators in the subway run faster; the takeaways arrive faster; even the traffic lights seem quicker`——三连排比。

**关键词：**
- `escalators` ⭐⭐⭐ — 自动扶梯。
- `takeaways` ⭐⭐⭐ — 外卖。
- `traffic lights` ⭐⭐⭐ — 红绿灯。

**表达方式：** **The escalators ... run faster; the takeaways arrive faster; even the traffic lights seem quicker**：`The escalators in the subway run faster; the takeaways arrive faster; even the traffic lights seem quicker`——三连排比（扶梯/外卖/红绿灯）+ even 递进——"快"的具象化。

**为什么这样写：** 用三个日常细节排比"快"——从扶梯到外卖再到红绿灯，层层递进（even）。

> **原句 4:** This impression of relentless hustle defines how the world sees China.

**中文理解：** 这种不停歇的忙碌印象，定义了世界如何看待中国。

**句子结构：** `This impression of relentless hustle defines [how the world sees China]`——主谓宾 + how 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `relentless hustle` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不停歇的忙碌。
- `defines` ⭐⭐⭐ — 定义了。

**表达方式：** **This impression of relentless hustle defines how the world sees China**：`This impression of relentless hustle defines how the world sees China`——relentless hustle（无休止的拼搏）+ defines how the world sees China（定义世界眼中的中国）——"勤奋"塑造国家形象。

**为什么这样写：** 用 defines（定义）点明"努力"不仅是现象，更是中国形象的符号。

**段落逻辑：** 中国以勤奋闻名 → 游客感受（双倍速）→ 三细节排比（扶梯/外卖/红绿灯）→ 印象定义形象。第 1 段完成"印象"。---

### 第 2 段：数据支撑 —— 工时全球领先

> **原句 5:** The data back up the impression.

**中文理解：** 数据支持这一印象。

**句子结构：** `The data back up the impression`——主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `back up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 支持。

**表达方式：** **The data back up the impression**：`The data back up the impression`——The data back up（数据支持）——从印象转数据。

**为什么这样写：** 用 back up 过渡——印象需要数据证实。

> **原句 6:** Chinese workers clock an average of over 2,000 hours a year, roughly a quarter more than their American counterparts and far more than Europeans.

**中文理解：** 中国工人平均每年工作超过 2,000 小时，比美国同行大约多四分之一，比欧洲人多得多。

**句子结构：** `Chinese workers clock an average of over 2,000 hours a year, [roughly a quarter more than their American counterparts and far more than Europeans]`——主谓宾 + 比较短语。

**关键词：**
- `clock an average of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 平均达到（工时）。
- `counterparts` ⭐⭐⭐ — 同行、对应者。

**表达方式：** **Chinese workers clock an average of over 2,000 hours a year, roughly a quarter more than their American counterparts**：`Chinese workers clock an average of over 2,000 hours a year, roughly a quarter more than their American counterparts and far more than Europeans`——over 2,000 hours（超两千小时）+ a quarter more than（多四分之一）+ far more than Europeans（远多于欧洲）——工时的量化对比。

**为什么这样写：** 用"2000 小时 + 比美多四分之一 + 比欧多得多"——把"勤奋"变成数字。

> **原句 7:** The gap has persisted even as working hours in other rich countries have fallen.

**中文理解：** 即使其他富裕国家的工时在下降，这一差距依然持续。

**句子结构：** `The gap has persisted [even as working hours in other rich countries have fallen]`——主谓 + even as 让步从句。

**关键词：**
- `persisted` ⭐⭐⭐ — 持续存在。
- `even as` ⭐⭐⭐ — 即便在……时。

**表达方式：** **The gap has persisted even as working hours in other rich countries have fallen**：`The gap has persisted even as working hours in other rich countries have fallen`——persisted（持续）+ even as ... have fallen（即便他国下降）——差距的顽固性。

**为什么这样写：** 用 even as 让步——西方在减工时，中国差距却没缩小。

> **原句 8:** And unlike ageing Japan or South Korea, China's hours are not a sign of a shrinking workforce.

**中文理解：** 而且与老龄化的日本或韩国不同，中国的工时并不是劳动力萎缩的迹象。

**句子结构：** `And unlike ageing Japan or South Korea, China's hours are not a sign of a shrinking workforce`——状语 + 主系表。

**关键词：**
- `ageing` ⭐⭐⭐ — 老龄化的。
- `a shrinking workforce` ⭐⭐⭐ — 萎缩的劳动力。

**表达方式：** **unlike ageing Japan or South Korea, China's hours are not a sign of a shrinking workforce**：`And unlike ageing Japan or South Korea, China's hours are not a sign of a shrinking workforce`——unlike（不同于）+ not a sign of shrinking workforce（非劳动力萎缩迹象）——排除另一种解释。

**为什么这样写：** 用 unlike 排除"工时多=劳动力少"的替代解释——中国勤奋是真实的文化选择。

**段落逻辑：** 数据佐证 → 2000 小时（比美多 1/4，比欧多）→ 差距持续（even as 他国下降）→ 非劳动力萎缩（unlike 日韩）。第 2 段完成"数据"。

### 第 3 段：文化根源 —— 奋斗精神与制度

> **原句 9:** What explains China's devotion to hard work?

**中文理解：** 什么解释了中国对努力工作的执着？

**句子结构：** `What explains China's devotion to hard work`——疑问句。

**关键词：**
- `devotion to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对……的执着。

**表达方式：** **What explains China's devotion to hard work**：`What explains China's devotion to hard work`——devotion to hard work（对勤奋的执着）——设问引出归因。

**为什么这样写：** 用设问（What explains）——从现象转向原因。

> **原句 10:** Part of the answer is cultural: a Confucian emphasis on diligence and self-improvement.

**中文理解：** 部分答案是文化上的：儒家强调勤勉与自我提升。

**句子结构：** `Part of the answer is cultural: [a Confucian emphasis on diligence and self-improvement]`——主系表 + 冒号解释。

**关键词：**
- `Confucian emphasis` ⭐⭐⭐ — 儒家的强调。
- `diligence` ⭐⭐⭐ — 勤勉。

**表达方式：** **Part of the answer is cultural: a Confucian emphasis on diligence and self-improvement**：`Part of the answer is cultural: a Confucian emphasis on diligence and self-improvement`——Part of the answer is cultural（答案部分在文化）+ Confucian emphasis on diligence（儒家尚勤）——文化解释一：儒家。

**为什么这样写：** 归因一：儒家文化（勤勉 + 自我提升）——用 Part of（部分）避免过度简化。

> **原句 11:** But institutions matter too: the 996 work schedule, once confined to tech firms, has spread across the economy.

**中文理解：** 但制度也很重要：曾经局限于科技公司的 996 工作制，已经蔓延到整个经济。

**句子结构：** `But institutions matter too: [the 996 work schedule, [once confined to tech firms], has spread across the economy]`——主谓 + 冒号解释 + 插入语。

**关键词：**
- `institutions matter` ⭐⭐⭐ — 制度很重要。
- `996 work schedule` ⭐⭐⭐ — 996 工作制。
- `confined to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 局限于。

**表达方式：** **But institutions matter too: the 996 work schedule, once confined to tech firms, has spread across the economy**：`But institutions matter too: the 996 work schedule, once confined to tech firms, has spread across the economy`——But institutions matter（但制度同样重要）+ 996 spread across the economy（996 蔓延全经济）——制度解释：996 扩散。

**为什么这样写：** 归因二：制度（996 从科技业蔓延全经济）——But 转折引入非文化解释。

> **原句 12:** In the private sector, promotion and pay are tied to hours worked more tightly than in most countries.

**中文理解：** 在私营部门，晋升和薪酬与工作时间的挂钩比大多数国家更紧。

**句子结构：** `In the private sector, promotion and pay are tied to hours worked [more tightly than in most countries]`——主谓被动 + 比较。

**关键词：**
- `tied to hours worked` ⭐⭐⭐ — 与工时挂钩。
- `more tightly than` ⭐⭐⭐ — 比……更紧。

**表达方式：** **promotion and pay are tied to hours worked more tightly than in most countries**：`In the private sector, promotion and pay are tied to hours worked more tightly than in most countries`——promotion and pay are tied to hours（晋升薪酬与工时挂钩）+ more tightly than（比多数国家更紧）——制度激励：加班换晋升。

**为什么这样写：** 点出激励结构：晋升与薪酬紧盯工时——制度让"努力"有回报。

**段落逻辑：** 设问何因 → 文化（儒家勤勉）→ 制度（996 蔓延）→ 激励（晋升薪酬绑工时）。第 3 段完成"文化+制度"。---

### 第 4 段：政府动员与"内卷"

> **原句 13:** The state has reinforced this culture.

**中文理解：** 国家强化了这种文化。

**句子结构：** `The state has reinforced this culture`——主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `reinforced` ⭐⭐⭐ — 强化。

**表达方式：** **The state has reinforced this culture**：`The state has reinforced this culture`——The state（国家）+ reinforced（强化）——归因三：政府。

**为什么这样写：** 引入第三个解释：国家意志在强化勤奋文化。

> **原句 14:** Campaigns celebrate "model workers", while officials talk of "striving" and "sacrifice" as patriotic virtues.

**中文理解：** 各种活动表彰"劳动模范"，而官员们把"奋斗"和"牺牲"作为爱国美德来谈论。

**句子结构：** `Campaigns celebrate "model workers", while [officials talk of "striving" and "sacrifice" as patriotic virtues]`——并列句 + while 对比。

**关键词：**
- `model workers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 劳动模范。
- `patriotic virtues` ⭐⭐⭐ — 爱国美德。

**表达方式：** **Campaigns celebrate "model workers", while officials talk of "striving" and "sacrifice" as patriotic virtues**：`Campaigns celebrate "model workers", while officials talk of "striving" and "sacrifice" as patriotic virtues`——model workers（劳动模范）+ striving and sacrifice as patriotic virtues（奋斗牺牲=爱国）——官方叙事：把勤奋政治化。

**为什么这样写：** 指出官方话语把"奋斗/牺牲"升华为爱国——勤奋被意识形态化。

> **原句 15:** The result is the phenomenon known as "involution", where people work ever harder for ever smaller gains.

**中文理解：** 结果就是被称为"内卷"的现象：人们为了越来越小的收益而越来越努力地工作。

**句子结构：** `The result is the phenomenon [known as "involution"], [where people work ever harder for ever smaller gains]`——主系表 + 分词 + where 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `involution` ⭐⭐⭐ — 内卷。
- `ever harder ... ever smaller gains` ⭐⭐⭐ — 越来越努力……收益越来越小。

**表达方式：** **The result is the phenomenon known as "involution", where people work ever harder for ever smaller gains**：`The result is the phenomenon known as "involution", where people work ever harder for ever smaller gains`——involution（内卷）+ ever harder for ever smaller gains（越努力收益越小）——"内卷"的核心定义。

**为什么这样写：** 用"内卷"（involution）给现象命名，用 where 从句定义其本质——边际收益递减的过度竞争。

> **原句 16:** The involution trap leaves little time for family, rest or creativity.

**中文理解：** 内卷陷阱给家庭、休息或创造力留下的时间很少。

**句子结构：** `The involution trap leaves little time for family, rest or creativity`——主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `the involution trap` ⭐⭐⭐ — 内卷陷阱。
- `leaves little time for` ⭐⭐⭐ — 给……留很少时间。

**表达方式：** **The involution trap leaves little time for family, rest or creativity**：`The involution trap leaves little time for family, rest or creativity`——leaves little time for family, rest or creativity（给家庭/休息/创造留时极少）——内卷的代价：时间被吞噬。

**为什么这样写：** 用"家庭/休息/创造"三连列出内卷的机会成本——为后文代价铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** 国家强化文化 → 官方叙事（模范/奋斗/牺牲）→ 内卷（越努力收益越小）→ 代价：没时间给家庭休息创造。第 4 段完成"内卷"。

### 第 5 段：代价 —— 健康、家庭、创新

> **原句 17:** The costs are visible.

**中文理解：** 代价是看得见的。

**句子结构：** `The costs are visible`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `visible` ⭐⭐⭐ — 看得见的。

**表达方式：** **The costs are visible**：`The costs are visible`——The costs are visible（代价可见）——短句过渡到代价。

**为什么这样写：** 用短句（visible）宣告——代价不是猜测，是明摆着的。

> **原句 18:** Overwork has been linked to rising rates of heart disease and mental-health problems among young workers.

**中文理解：** 过劳与年轻劳动者心脏病和心理问题的上升有关。

**句子结构：** `Overwork has been linked to [rising rates of heart disease and mental-health problems among young workers]`——主谓被动 + 介词宾语。

**关键词：**
- `Overwork` ⭐⭐⭐ — 过劳。
- `linked to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 与……有关。

**表达方式：** **Overwork has been linked to rising rates of heart disease and mental-health problems**：`Overwork has been linked to rising rates of heart disease and mental-health problems among young workers`——Overwork linked to（过劳关联）+ heart disease and mental-health problems（心脏病与心理问题）——健康代价。

**为什么这样写：** 给出健康层面的代价（过劳→心脏病/心理问题）——用 linked to 保持谨慎表述。

> **原句 19:** Demographers worry that the culture of long hours is contributing to the country's falling birth rate.

**中文理解：** 人口学家担心，长时间工作的文化正在促成国家出生率的下降。

**句子结构：** `Demographers worry that [the culture of long hours is contributing to the country's falling birth rate]`——主谓 + that 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `Demographers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 人口学家。
- `contributing to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 促成。

**表达方式：** **Demographers worry that the culture of long hours is contributing to the country's falling birth rate**：`Demographers worry that the culture of long hours is contributing to the country's falling birth rate`——Demographers worry（人口学家担忧）+ contributing to falling birth rate（促成出生率下降）——人口代价。

**为什么这样写：** 用人口学家的担忧——把过劳与生育率下降挂钩（没时间恋爱育儿）。

> **原句 20:** And when everyone is too exhausted to think, innovation suffers.

**中文理解：** 而当所有人都累得无法思考时，创新就会受损。

**句子结构：** `And when [everyone is too exhausted to think], innovation suffers`——when 从句 + 主句。

**关键词：**
- `too exhausted to think` ⭐⭐⭐ — 累得无法思考。
- `suffers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 受损。

**表达方式：** **when everyone is too exhausted to think, innovation suffers**：`And when everyone is too exhausted to think, innovation suffers`——too exhausted to think（累到不能思考）+ innovation suffers（创新受损）——创新代价。

**为什么这样写：** 用 too...to 结构与短句（suffers）——点出内卷的深层悖论：过度努力反而损害创新。

**段落逻辑：** 代价可见 → 健康（过劳→心疾心理问题）→ 人口（长工时→出生率降）→ 创新（太累无法思考）。第 5 段完成"代价"。---

### 第 6 段：结论 —— 努力文化还能持续吗

> **原句 21:** Will China's work ethic survive its own success?

**中文理解：** 中国的勤奋观能在自身成功之后存续吗？

**句子结构：** `Will China's work ethic survive its own success`——一般疑问句。

**关键词：**
- `survive its own success` ⭐⭐⭐ — 在其自身成功后存续。

**表达方式：** **Will China's work ethic survive its own success**：`Will China's work ethic survive its own success`——survive its own success（能否熬过自己的成功）——设问收束：勤奋文化的未来。

**为什么这样写：** 用"能否熬过自己的成功"这个悖论式设问——成功可能反而终结勤奋。

> **原句 22:** The answer matters not just for China but for the global economy, which has come to rely on Chinese labour and output.

**中文理解：** 答案不仅对中国重要，对依赖中国劳动力和产出的全球经济也很重要。

**句子结构：** `The answer matters not just for China but for the global economy, [which has come to rely on Chinese labour and output]`——主谓 + not just...but 并列 + 非限制性定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `not just ... but` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不仅……而且。
- `rely on Chinese labour and output` ⭐⭐⭐ — 依赖中国劳动力与产出。

**表达方式：** **The answer matters not just for China but for the global economy, which has come to rely on Chinese labour and output**：`The answer matters not just for China but for the global economy, which has come to rely on Chinese labour and output`——not just for China but for the global economy（不仅关乎中国还关乎全球）+ which has come to rely on（已依赖）——影响外溢。

**为什么这样写：** 把话题从中国扩展到全球经济——勤奋文化的存亡有全球意义。

> **原句 23:** If China stops outworking the world, the world will have to work differently.

**中文理解：** 如果中国停止比世界更努力，世界将不得不以不同的方式工作。

**句子结构：** `If [China stops outworking the world], the world will have to work differently`——条件句。

**关键词：**
- `outworking` ⭐⭐⭐ — 比……工作更努力。

**表达方式：** **If China stops outworking the world, the world will have to work differently**：`If China stops outworking the world, the world will have to work differently`——stops outworking the world（停止卷过世界）+ will have to work differently（不得不换种方式工作）——假设式结论。

**为什么这样写：** 用 If 条件句收束——中国的努力若退场，全球工作模式都将改变。

> **原句 24:** The age of hustle may be ending, but its legacy will not disappear overnight.

**中文理解：** 拼命工作的时代可能正在结束，但它的遗产不会一夜之间消失。

**句子结构：** `The age of hustle may be ending, but [its legacy will not disappear overnight]`——并列句 + but 转折。

**关键词：**
- `the age of hustle` ⭐⭐⭐ — 拼命工作的时代。
- `its legacy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 它的遗产。
- `overnight` ⭐⭐⭐ — 一夜之间。

**表达方式：** **The age of hustle may be ending, but its legacy will not disappear overnight**：`The age of hustle may be ending, but its legacy will not disappear overnight`——may be ending（或将终结）but legacy will not disappear（但遗产不会消散）——收束：变化渐进。

**为什么这样写：** 用"或终将结束，但遗产不散"的平衡收束——既承认变化，也承认惯性。

**段落逻辑：** 设问（勤奋能否熬过成功）→ 影响全球（依赖中国劳动）→ 假设（若停止卷世界换方式）→ 结论（时代或终，遗产犹存）。全文完成。---

## 三、长难句专项

### 长难句 1（原句 1）

**原句：** CHINA IS the only big economy whose citizens are famous for their work ethic.

**找主干：** CHINA IS the only big economy。

**找修饰：**
- whose citizens are famous for their work ethic——定语从句修饰 economy

**找从句：** whose 定语从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 中国是唯一一个公民以勤奋闻名的经济大国。

**整体理解：** 主系表 + whose 定语从句——断言 + 佐证结构。

### 长难句 2（原句 6）

**原句：** Chinese workers clock an average of over 2,000 hours a year, roughly a quarter more than their American counterparts and far more than Europeans.

**找主干：** Chinese workers clock an average of over 2,000 hours a year。

**找修饰：**
- roughly a quarter more than their American counterparts——比较短语
- and far more than Europeans——并列比较短语

**找从句：** 无从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 中国工人年均工时超 2000 小时，比美国同行约多四分之一，比欧洲人多得多。

**整体理解：** 主干 + 两个并列比较短语——数据的层层对比。

### 长难句 3（原句 11）

**原句：** But institutions matter too: the 996 work schedule, once confined to tech firms, has spread across the economy.

**找主干：** institutions matter / the 996 work schedule has spread across the economy。

**找修饰：**
- once confined to tech firms——分词短语（插入，修饰 schedule）

**找从句：** 无从句（冒号后为解释分句）。

**恢复逻辑：** 但制度也重要：曾局限于科技公司的 996 已蔓延整个经济。

**整体理解：** 冒号解释结构 + 插入分词短语——用例子解释论点。

### 长难句 4（原句 22）

**原句：** The answer matters not just for China but for the global economy, which has come to rely on Chinese labour and output.

**找主干：** The answer matters。

**找修饰：**
- not just for China but for the global economy——并列介词短语
- which has come to rely on Chinese labour and output——非限制性定语从句

**找从句：** which 非限制性定语从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 答案不仅关乎中国，还关乎依赖中国劳动与产出的全球经济。

**整体理解：** not just...but 并列 + which 补充从句——"从一到多"的扩展。

## 四、精读结束总结

### 核心词汇

本文是"工作文化比较"报道，词汇集中在：**勤奋与努力**（work ethic, hustle, diligence, outworking）；**制度与激励**（996, tied to hours, model workers, institutions）；**代价与后果**（overwork, linked to, contributing to, exhausted）；**文化概念**（involution, patriotic virtues, devotion to）。这组词是分析"国家工作文化"话题的工具包。

### 表达方式

1. **断言开场**：CHINA IS the only big economy ...——开篇给强断言。
2. **感官细节排比**：escalators / takeaways / traffic lights——三细节具象"快"。
3. **设问归因**：What explains China's devotion to hard work?——设问引出多层解释。
4. **多归因递进**：Part of the answer is cultural ... But institutions matter too ... The state has reinforced——文化→制度→国家，层层深入。
5. **概念命名**：the phenomenon known as "involution"——用"内卷"给现象命名。
6. **悖论式设问收束**：survive its own success——用悖论收尾。

### 语法要点

- **whose 定语从句**：the only big economy whose citizens are famous——"唯一" + 佐证。
- **比较短语并列**：a quarter more than ... and far more than ...——多重比较。
- **even as 让步**：The gap has persisted even as ... have fallen。
- **分词插入**：the 996 work schedule, once confined to tech firms, has spread。
- **where 定语从句**：the phenomenon ... where people work ever harder——where 引导抽象地点。
- **not just ... but**：matters not just for China but for the global economy——递进并列。
- **too...to**：too exhausted to think——"太……而不能"。

### 长难句

最值得模仿的是"冒号解释结构"（原句 11）与"not just...but + which"（原句 22）——前者适合"论点：例子"，后者适合"从局部到整体"。

### 写作技巧

- **由印象到数据**：先写游客感受（double speed），再写 2000 小时数据——由感性到理性。
- **三层归因**：文化/制度/国家——不把复杂现象归因于单一因素。
- **代价清单**：健康/人口/创新——把代价结构化。
- **避免绝对化**：Part of the answer ... / may be ending——用 Part of、may 保持审慎。

### 可迁移表达

- "X is famous for their work ethic" — 用于"以勤奋著称"。
- "work ever harder for ever smaller gains" — 用于"内卷"现象。
- "the phenomenon known as involution" — 用于"给现象命名"。
- "will survive its own success" — 用于"成功反噬"悖论。
- "not just for China but for the global economy" — 用于"影响外溢"。
