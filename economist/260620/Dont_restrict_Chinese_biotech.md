# Don't restrict Chinese biotech

**文章概览：** 《经济学人》Leaders 社论，2026-06-17。核心论点：美国不能为了"对华强硬"就对中国的生物科技公司（如药明康德 WuXi AppTec、百济神州 BeiGene）施加全面限制。文章认为：限制会伤害美国病人（买不到便宜的创新药）、削弱美国自己的创新（中国公司提供全球供应链与研发管线），且"生物技术安全"被政治化夸大。主张：对真正敏感的技术（脑机接口等）定向管控，而不是对贸易普遍设障。

**文体特征：** 政策批评型社论。用"scaremongering"（危言耸听）、"panic"（恐慌）等词给保护主义定性；用具体企业（WuXi、BeiGene）与数据（管线占比、临床试验）反驳；立场是"聪明管控 vs 一刀切封锁"。

**全文结构：**
- 第 1 段：恐慌情绪——生物技术被当国家安全头号威胁
- 第 2 段：中国公司的角色——全球生物科技供应链核心
- 第 3 段：限制的代价——美国病人受损
- 第 4 段：美国创新的悖论——限制反伤自身
- 第 5 段：安全关切该有——但定向处理
- 第 6 段：结论——别把贸易当安全

## 一、逐句精读

### 第 1 段：恐慌情绪 —— 生物技术被当国家安全头号威胁

> **原句 1:** THE NEXT battleground in the American tech war against China could be biology.

**中文理解：** 美国对华科技战争的下一战场可能是生物学。

**句子结构：** `THE NEXT battleground in the American tech war against China could be biology`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `battleground` ⭐⭐⭐ — 战场。
- `tech war against China` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对华科技战。

**表达方式：** **THE NEXT battleground in the American tech war against China could be biology**：`THE NEXT battleground in the American tech war against China could be biology`——THE NEXT battleground（下一战场）+ could be biology（可能是生物学）——科技战战线延伸到生物。

**为什么这样写：** 开篇定调：把生物科技放到"科技战"框架下——这是文章要挑战的叙事前提。

> **原句 2:** Politicians in Washington are looking at Chinese biotechnology with growing suspicion.

**中文理解：** 华盛顿的政客们正以日益增长的怀疑看待中国生物科技。

**句子结构：** `Politicians in Washington are looking at Chinese biotechnology with growing suspicion`——主谓宾 + with 方式。

**关键词：**
- `growing suspicion` ⭐⭐⭐ — 日益增长的怀疑。

**表达方式：** **looking at Chinese biotechnology with growing suspicion**：`Politicians in Washington are looking at Chinese biotechnology with growing suspicion`——looking at ... with growing suspicion（带着日益怀疑的眼光）——华盛顿的疑华心态。

**为什么这样写：** 用 with growing suspicion 描绘政客心态——为"恐慌"铺垫。

> **原句 3:** They fear that Chinese companies could become tools of the state, stealing Western know-how and undermining America's global lead.

**中文理解：** 他们担心中国公司可能成为国家的工具，窃取西方技术诀窍，削弱美国的全球领先地位。

**句子结构：** `They fear that [Chinese companies could become tools of the state, [stealing Western know-how and [undermining America's global lead]]]`——主谓 + that 宾语从句（内含两个分词）。

**关键词：**
- `tools of the state` ⭐⭐⭐ — 国家的工具。
- `stealing Western know-how` ⭐⭐⭐ — 窃取西方技术诀窍。
- `undermining America's global lead` ⭐⭐⭐ — 削弱美国全球领先。

**表达方式：** **become tools of the state, stealing Western know-how and undermining America's global lead**：`They fear that Chinese companies could become tools of the state`——tools of the state（国家工具）+ stealing know-how（窃技术）+ undermining lead（削弱领先）——政客的三重恐惧。

**为什么这样写：** 罗列恐惧内容（国家工具/窃技术/弱领先）——让读者看清恐惧的逻辑，方便后文逐条反驳。

> **原句 4:** It is easy to dismiss such fears, but they are fuelling real policy.

**中文理解：** 人们很容易忽视这些担忧，但它们正在助长真正的政策。

**句子结构：** `It is easy to dismiss such fears, but [they are fuelling real policy]`——形式主语 + 主句 + but 转折。

**关键词：**
- `dismiss such fears` ⭐⭐⭐ — 轻视这些担忧。
- `fuelling real policy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 助长真正的政策。

**表达方式：** **easy to dismiss such fears, but they are fuelling real policy**：`It is easy to dismiss such fears, but they are fuelling real policy`——easy to dismiss（好打发）+ but fuelling real policy（却在催生政策）——恐惧不是儿戏，已在影响政策。

**为什么这样写：** 平衡写法：先承认恐惧好打发，再指出它已在催生真实政策——文章的现实紧迫性。

**段落逻辑：** 新战场（生物）→ 政客怀疑 → 三重恐惧 → 恐惧已成政策。第 1 段完成"恐慌背景"。---

### 第 2 段：中国公司的角色 —— 全球生物科技供应链核心

> **原句 5:** Consider the role that Chinese firms play in the global biotech industry.

**中文理解：** 想一想中国公司在全球生物科技产业中扮演的角色。

**句子结构：** `Consider the role [that Chinese firms play in the global biotech industry]`——祈使句 + that 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `Consider` ⭐⭐⭐ — 想一想（祈使）。
- `play a role in` ⭐⭐⭐ — 扮演角色。

**表达方式：** **Consider the role that Chinese firms play in the global biotech industry**：`Consider the role that Chinese firms play in the global biotech industry`——Consider（祈使开头）+ play a role（扮演角色）——引导读者看事实。

**为什么这样写：** 用祈使句把讨论拉回事实层面——"先看中国公司到底在干什么"。

> **原句 6:** WuXi AppTec, a Chinese firm, is one of the world's biggest contract research, development and manufacturing organisations, or CDMOs.

**中文理解：** 药明康德——一家中国公司——是全球最大的合同研究、开发与制造组织（CDMO）之一。

**句子结构：** `WuXi AppTec, [a Chinese firm], is one of the world's biggest contract research, development and manufacturing organisations, or CDMOs`——主系表 + 同位语 + or 说明。

**关键词：**
- `contract research, development and manufacturing organisations (CDMOs)` ⭐⭐⭐ — 合同研究开发制造组织。

**表达方式：** **one of the world's biggest ... CDMOs**：`WuXi AppTec, a Chinese firm, is one of the world's biggest contract research, development and manufacturing organisations`——WuXi AppTec（药明康德）+ one of the world's biggest（全球最大之一）+ or CDMOs（缩写说明）——药明康德的产业地位。

**为什么这样写：** 举药明康德为例——用具体公司说明"中国是供应链核心"，不是抽象说理。

> **原句 7:** Hundreds of drugmakers worldwide rely on its factories and laboratories to manufacture ingredients, test drugs and run clinical trials.

**中文理解：** 全世界数百家制药公司依赖它的工厂和实验室来生产原料药、测试药物和开展临床试验。

**句子结构：** `Hundreds of drugmakers worldwide rely on its factories and laboratories [to manufacture ingredients, test drugs and run clinical trials]`——主谓宾 + 不定式目的（三连）。

**关键词：**
- `rely on` ⭐⭐⭐ — 依赖。
- `manufacture ingredients` ⭐⭐⭐ — 生产原料药。
- `run clinical trials` ⭐⭐⭐ — 开展临床试验。

**表达方式：** **Hundreds of drugmakers worldwide rely on its factories and laboratories to manufacture ingredients, test drugs and run clinical trials**：`Hundreds of drugmakers worldwide rely on its factories and laboratories`——Hundreds of drugmakers（数百药企）+ rely on（依赖）+ 三连不定式（生产/测试/试验）——药明康德的全球服务范围。

**为什么这样写：** 量化依赖（数百家）+ 三连功能（生产/测试/试验）——"限制它"的冲击面有多大。

> **原句 8:** Any restriction that made its services less available would therefore ripple through the entire industry.

**中文理解：** 任何让它的服务变得不那么可及的限制，都会因此波及整个行业。

**句子结构：** `Any restriction [that made its services less available] would therefore ripple through the entire industry`——主谓宾 + that 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `ripple through` ⭐⭐⭐ — 波及、传导。
- `less available` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更不可及。

**表达方式：** **Any restriction ... would therefore ripple through the entire industry**：`Any restriction that made its services less available would therefore ripple through the entire industry`——Any restriction（任何限制）+ ripple through（涟漪般波及）——限制的传导效应。

**为什么这样写：** 用 ripple（涟漪）隐喻——限制药明康德会像波纹一样扩散到整个产业。

**段落逻辑：** 看中国公司角色 → 药明康德（全球最大 CDMO 之一）→ 数百药企依赖其三连服务 → 限制将波及全行业。第 2 段完成"供应链事实"。---

### 第 3 段：限制的代价 —— 美国病人受损

> **原句 9:** The most direct victims of such restrictions would be American patients.

**中文理解：** 这类限制最直接的受害者将是美国病人。

**句子结构：** `The most direct victims of such restrictions would be American patients`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `The most direct victims` ⭐⭐⭐ — 最直接的受害者。

**表达方式：** **The most direct victims of such restrictions would be American patients**：`The most direct victims of such restrictions would be American patients`——most direct victims（最直接受害者）+ American patients（美国病人）——受害者恰恰是自己人。

**为什么这样写：** 反讽开头：限制中国公司，最受伤的是美国病人——打脸保护主义。

> **原句 10:** Chinese firms help bring cheaper drugs to market, putting pressure on prices that big pharma would rather keep high.

**中文理解：** 中国公司帮助把更便宜的药物推向市场，给大药企宁愿保持高价的价格带来压力。

**句子结构：** `Chinese firms help bring cheaper drugs to market, [putting pressure on prices [that big pharma would rather keep high]]`——主谓宾 + 分词结果 + that 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `bring cheaper drugs to market` ⭐⭐⭐ — 把更便宜的药推向市场。
- `putting pressure on prices` ⭐⭐⭐ — 给价格施加压力。
- `big pharma` ⭐⭐⭐ — 大药企。

**表达方式：** **help bring cheaper drugs to market, putting pressure on prices that big pharma would rather keep high**：`Chinese firms help bring cheaper drugs to market, putting pressure on prices`——cheaper drugs（更便宜的药）+ putting pressure on prices（压低价格）+ would rather keep high（宁肯维持高价）——中国公司是价格的"压舱石"。

**为什么这样写：** 点明中国公司的经济功能：压低药价——这正是美国病人需要的。

> **原句 11:** Limiting them would make drugs more expensive and reduce choice.

**中文理解：** 限制它们会让药物更贵、选择更少。

**句子结构：** `Limiting them would make drugs more expensive and [reduce choice]`——动名词主语 + 主谓宾 + 宾补。

**关键词：**
- `make drugs more expensive` ⭐⭐⭐ — 让药更贵。
- `reduce choice` ⭐⭐⭐ — 减少选择。

**表达方式：** **Limiting them would make drugs more expensive and reduce choice**：`Limiting them would make drugs more expensive and reduce choice`——动名词主语（Limiting them）+ more expensive and reduce choice（更贵、更少选择）——限制的代价直白化。

**为什么这样写：** 用简单句把代价说透：更贵 + 更少选择——普通读者也能共鸣。

> **原句 12:** There is nothing national-security-threatening about making medicine affordable.

**中文理解：** 让药物买得起，根本不构成国家安全威胁。

**句子结构：** `There is nothing national-security-threatening about making medicine affordable`——there be + about 动名词。

**关键词：**
- `national-security-threatening` ⭐⭐⭐ — 构成国家安全威胁的。
- `affordable` ⭐⭐⭐ — 买得起的。

**表达方式：** **There is nothing national-security-threatening about making medicine affordable**：`There is nothing national-security-threatening about making medicine affordable`——nothing national-security-threatening（毫无国安威胁）+ making medicine affordable（让药可负担）——"便宜药"何来威胁。

**为什么这样写：** 用"nothing ... threatening"的强否定，拆穿把药价问题上升为安全问题的荒谬。

**段落逻辑：** 最直接受害者是美国病人 → 中国公司压低药价 → 限制=更贵更少选择 → 便宜药不是安全威胁。第 3 段完成"代价分析"。---

### 第 4 段：美国创新的悖论 —— 限制反伤自身

> **原句 13:** Restrictions would also undermine American innovation.

**中文理解：** 限制还会削弱美国的创新。

**句子结构：** `Restrictions would also undermine American innovation`——主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `undermine American innovation` ⭐⭐⭐ — 削弱美国创新。

**表达方式：** **Restrictions would also undermine American innovation**：`Restrictions would also undermine American innovation`——also undermine（同样削弱）——第二重代价：创新。

**为什么这样写：** 从"病人受损"转到"创新受损"——代价清单递进。

> **原句 14:** Western drugmakers rely on Chinese firms not just for manufacturing, but for a growing share of their research pipelines.

**中文理解：** 西方制药商依赖中国公司，不仅是为了制造，还为了它们研究管线中不断增长的份额。

**句子结构：** `Western drugmakers rely on Chinese firms [not just for manufacturing, but for a growing share of their research pipelines]`——主谓宾 + not just...but 并列。

**关键词：**
- `rely on ... for` ⭐⭐⭐ — 依赖……用于。
- `research pipelines` ⭐⭐⭐ — 研究管线（在研项目）。

**表达方式：** **rely on Chinese firms not just for manufacturing, but for a growing share of their research pipelines**：`Western drugmakers rely on Chinese firms not just for manufacturing, but for a growing share of their research pipelines`——not just for ... but for ...（不仅……而且……）——依赖从制造延伸到研发。

**为什么这样写：** 用 not just...but 结构说明：中国公司已是研发管线的重要来源——限制伤及美国未来药物。

> **原句 15:** In some fields, such as antibody-drug conjugates, a promising class of cancer drugs, Chinese firms now account for a large share of global candidates.

**中文理解：** 在一些领域，比如抗体偶联药物（一种前景看好的抗癌药类别），中国公司现在占全球候选药物的很大份额。

**句子结构：** `In some fields, such as antibody-drug conjugates, [a promising class of cancer drugs], Chinese firms now account for a large share of global candidates`——状语 + 主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `antibody-drug conjugates` ⭐⭐⭐ — 抗体偶联药物（ADC）。
- `a promising class of cancer drugs` ⭐⭐⭐ — 前景看好的抗癌药类别。
- `account for a large share` ⭐⭐⭐ — 占很大份额。

**表达方式：** **In some fields, such as antibody-drug conjugates ... Chinese firms now account for a large share of global candidates**：`In some fields, such as antibody-drug conjugates, a promising class of cancer drugs, Chinese firms now account for a large share of global candidates`——such as ADC（如 ADC）+ account for a large share（占大头）——中国公司在 ADC 领域的领先。

**为什么这样写：** 用具体领域（ADC 抗癌药）作证：中国已是全球创新来源——不是只会山寨。

> **原句 16:** Cutting them off would deprive American patients of the next generation of treatments.

**中文理解：** 切断与它们的联系，会让美国病人失去下一代疗法。

**句子结构：** `Cutting them off would deprive American patients of the next generation of treatments`——动名词主语 + 主谓宾 + of 短语。

**关键词：**
- `Cutting them off` ⭐⭐⭐ — 切断与它们的联系。
- `deprive ... of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 使……失去。

**表达方式：** **Cutting them off would deprive American patients of the next generation of treatments**：`Cutting them off would deprive American patients of the next generation of treatments`——Cutting them off（切断）+ deprive ... of the next generation of treatments（剥夺下一代疗法）——封锁=自断药物未来。

**为什么这样写：** 用 deprive ... of（剥夺）作后果——封锁中国的后果是美国人失去新药。

**段落逻辑：** 限制削弱创新 → 依赖已延伸到研发 → 具体案例（ADC 领域占大头）→ 封锁剥夺下一代疗法。第 4 段完成"创新悖论"。---

### 第 5 段：安全关切该有 —— 但定向处理

> **原句 17:** None of this means that the security concerns are entirely without foundation.

**中文理解：** 这一切并不意味着安全关切完全没有根据。

**句子结构：** `None of this means that [the security concerns are entirely without foundation]`——主谓 + that 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `entirely without foundation` ⭐⭐⭐ — 完全没有根据。

**表达方式：** **None of this means that the security concerns are entirely without foundation**：`None of this means that the security concerns are entirely without foundation`——None of this means（并不意味）+ entirely without foundation（完全无据）——平衡：安全关切有其道理。

**为什么这样写：** 用"None of this means"句式——前几段批限制，这里承认关切有据——避免偏激。

> **原句 18:** Intelligence agencies have raised legitimate worries about the use of biological data, gene editing and dual-use technology.

**中文理解：** 情报机构对生物数据的使用、基因编辑和两用技术提出了合理的担忧。

**句子结构：** `Intelligence agencies have raised legitimate worries about [the use of biological data, gene editing and dual-use technology]`——主谓宾 + about 三连。

**关键词：**
- `legitimate worries` ⭐⭐⭐ — 合理的担忧。
- `gene editing` ⭐⭐⭐ — 基因编辑。
- `dual-use technology` ⭐⭐⭐ — 两用技术。

**表达方式：** **raised legitimate worries about the use of biological data, gene editing and dual-use technology**：`Intelligence agencies have raised legitimate worries about the use of biological data, gene editing and dual-use technology`——legitimate worries（合理担忧）+ 三连（生物数据/基因编辑/两用技术）——承认真实的安全风险面。

**为什么这样写：** 给"安全"应有的尊重：列举真正的敏感点——为"定向管控"铺路。

> **原句 19:** The right response is targeted controls, not blanket restrictions.

**中文理解：** 正确的应对是定向管控，而不是全面限制。

**句子结构：** `The right response is [targeted controls, not blanket restrictions]`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `targeted controls` ⭐⭐⭐ — 定向管控。
- `blanket restrictions` ⭐⭐⭐ — 一刀切限制。

**表达方式：** **The right response is targeted controls, not blanket restrictions**：`The right response is targeted controls, not blanket restrictions`——targeted controls（定向管控）not blanket restrictions（而非全面封锁）——文章政策主张核心。

**为什么这样写：** 用"定向 vs 全面"的对仗给出明确政策建议——文章的落点。

> **原句 20:** Cutting edge research on biosecurity should be scrutinised, but ordinary trade in drugs and vaccines should not be swept up in a security panic.

**中文理解：** 关于生物安全的尖端研究应该受到审查，但普通的药物和疫苗贸易不应该被卷进安全恐慌之中。

**句子结构：** `Cutting edge research on biosecurity should be scrutinised, but [ordinary trade in drugs and vaccines should not be swept up in a security panic]`——并列被动句 + but 转折。

**关键词：**
- `scrutinised` ⭐⭐⭐ — 受审查。
- `swept up in` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被卷进。
- `a security panic` ⭐⭐⭐ — 安全恐慌。

**表达方式：** **Cutting edge research ... should be scrutinised, but ordinary trade ... should not be swept up in a security panic**：`Cutting edge research on biosecurity should be scrutinised, but ordinary trade in drugs and vaccines should not be swept up in a security panic`——should be scrutinised（应审查）vs should not be swept up（不应被卷进）+ security panic（安全恐慌）——把"研究审查"与"贸易封锁"分开。

**为什么这样写：** 用 should/should not 对称句给出边界：研究可查，贸易别拦——"恐慌"定性。

**段落逻辑：** 关切有据 → 真实敏感点（数据/基因编辑/两用）→ 对策：定向管控非全面封锁 → 边界：研究审查 vs 贸易不卷入恐慌。第 5 段完成"平衡主张"。

### 第 6 段：结论 —— 别把贸易当安全

> **原句 21:** America has every right to protect its most sensitive technologies.

**中文理解：** 美国完全有权保护它最敏感的技术。

**句子结构：** `America has every right to protect its most sensitive technologies`——主谓宾 + 不定式。

**关键词：**
- `has every right to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 完全有权。

**表达方式：** **America has every right to protect its most sensitive technologies**：`America has every right to protect its most sensitive technologies`——has every right（完全有权）+ protect most sensitive technologies（保护最敏感技术）——给美国的安全诉求以正当性。

**为什么这样写：** 先给足立场（有权保护），让读者觉得公允——再提"但"。

> **原句 22:** But that right does not extend to punishing ordinary consumers and patients in a fit of scaremongering.

**中文理解：** 但这一权利并不延伸到在一阵危言耸听中惩罚普通消费者和病人。

**句子结构：** `But that right does not extend to punishing ordinary consumers and patients [in a fit of scaremongering]`——主谓宾 + 动名词宾语 + 方式短语。

**关键词：**
- `does not extend to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不延伸到。
- `a fit of scaremongering` ⭐⭐⭐ — 一阵危言耸听。

**表达方式：** **does not extend to punishing ordinary consumers and patients in a fit of scaremongering**：`But that right does not extend to punishing ordinary consumers and patients in a fit of scaremongering`——does not extend to（不适用于）+ a fit of scaremongering（一阵恐慌炒作）——权利有界：不该殃及消费者。

**为什么这样写：** 用"权利有界"（does not extend to）批评把安全当挡箭牌——scaremongering（危言耸听）点破本质。

> **原句 23:** The lesson of the past decade in tech is that over-broad restrictions breed resentment and inefficiency without making anyone safer.

**中文理解：** 过去十年科技领域的教训是，过度宽泛的限制滋生怨恨和低效，却没有让任何人更安全。

**句子结构：** `The lesson of the past decade in tech is that [over-broad restrictions breed resentment and inefficiency without making anyone safer]`——主系表 + that 表语从句。

**关键词：**
- `over-broad restrictions` ⭐⭐⭐ — 过度宽泛的限制。
- `breed resentment and inefficiency` ⭐⭐⭐ — 滋生怨恨与低效。

**表达方式：** **over-broad restrictions breed resentment and inefficiency without making anyone safer**：`The lesson of the past decade in tech is that over-broad restrictions breed resentment and inefficiency without making anyone safer`——over-broad restrictions（过宽限制）+ breed resentment and inefficiency（滋生怨恨低效）+ without making anyone safer（没让任何人更安全）——用历史教训总结：封锁徒劳无功。

**为什么这样写：** 引"过去十年科技战"作历史镜子——大而化之的封锁既怨恨又低效，还无助于安全。

> **原句 24:** Biotech should not become the next casualty of that lesson.

**中文理解：** 生物科技不应该成为这个教训的下一个牺牲品。

**句子结构：** `Biotech should not become the next casualty of that lesson`——主系表 + 否定。

**关键词：**
- `the next casualty` ⭐⭐⭐ — 下一个牺牲品。

**表达方式：** **Biotech should not become the next casualty of that lesson**：`Biotech should not become the next casualty of that lesson`——the next casualty（下一个受害者）+ of that lesson（那个教训的）——生物不该重蹈覆辙。

**为什么这样写：** 用"下一个牺牲品"警句收束——科技战的下一个受害者不该是生物科技。

> **原句 25:** It would be a double tragedy: for American patients, and for the global science that depends on collaboration.

**中文理解：** 那将是一个双重悲剧：对美国病人如此，对依赖协作的全球科学也是如此。

**句子结构：** `It would be a double tragedy: [for American patients], and [for the global science that depends on collaboration]`——主系表 + 冒号并列 + 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `a double tragedy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 双重悲剧。
- `depends on collaboration` ⭐⭐⭐ — 依赖协作。

**表达方式：** **It would be a double tragedy: for American patients, and for the global science that depends on collaboration**：`It would be a double tragedy: for American patients, and for the global science that depends on collaboration`——a double tragedy（双重悲剧）+ for American patients and for global science（为病人、为科学）——封锁的双重损失。

**为什么这样写：** 用"双重悲剧"（病人 + 科学）作情感收束——封锁的代价既伤人也伤知识。

**段落逻辑：** 美国有权保护敏感技术 → 但权利有界（别殃及消费者）→ 历史教训（过宽限制怨恨又低效）→ 生物不该成牺牲品 → 双重悲剧收束。全文完成。---

## 三、长难句专项

### 长难句 1（原句 3）

**原句：** They fear that Chinese companies could become tools of the state, stealing Western know-how and undermining America's global lead.

**找主干：** They fear that ...（主谓 + that 宾语从句）。

**找修饰：**
- that Chinese companies could become tools of the state——宾语从句（恐惧内容）
- stealing Western know-how——现在分词（伴随，作 becoming tools 的方式）
- and undermining America's global lead——并列分词

**找从句：** 宾语从句（that）。

**恢复逻辑：** 他们担心中国公司成为国家工具，窃取西方技术，削弱美国领先地位。

**整体理解：** that 从句内套两个并列现在分词作结果/伴随——把"成为工具"的后果展开。分词短语让恐惧内容更立体。

### 长难句 2（原句 8）

**原句：** Any restriction that made its services less available would therefore ripple through the entire industry.

**找主干：** Any restriction would ripple through the entire industry（主谓宾）。

**找修饰：**
- that made its services less available——定语从句修饰 restriction
- therefore——因果连接词

**找从句：** 定语从句（that）。

**恢复逻辑：** 任何让药明康德服务更难获得的限制，都会波及整个行业。

**整体理解：** 主干是"限制会波及全行业"，that 从句限定限制的性质（使服务不可及）。ripple through 是比喻动词，形象。

### 长难句 3（原句 14）

**原句：** Western drugmakers rely on Chinese firms not just for manufacturing, but for a growing share of their research pipelines.

**找主干：** Western drugmakers rely on Chinese firms（主谓宾）。

**找修饰：**
- not just for manufacturing——not just 介词短语
- but for a growing share of their research pipelines——but 并列介词短语

**找从句：** 无从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 西方药企依赖中国公司，不仅为制造，还为其研究管线日益增大的份额。

**整体理解：** not just ... but for 构成递进：从制造（低端）到研发管线（高端）——显示依赖的深度。简单句但信息量大。

### 长难句 4（原句 23）

**原句：** The lesson of the past decade in tech is that over-broad restrictions breed resentment and inefficiency without making anyone safer.

**找主干：** The lesson ... is that ...（主系表 + 表语从句）。

**找修饰：**
- of the past decade in tech——介词短语（限制话题范围）
- over-broad restrictions breed resentment and inefficiency——表语从句主句
- without making anyone safer——介词 + 动名词（否定结果）

**找从句：** 表语从句（that）。

**恢复逻辑：** 过去十年科技的教训是：过宽限制滋生怨恨和低效，却没能让任何人更安全。

**整体理解：** 表语从句内，over-broad restrictions 作主语，breed 作谓语，without making ... safer 作伴随否定——"有害无益"的完整表达。

## 四、精读结束总结

### 核心词汇

本文是"产业政策 + 国家安全"论辩文本，词汇集中在：**限制与管控**（restriction, targeted, blanket, scrutinise, sanction）；**产业角色**（contract research, CDMO, pipelines, account for）；**负面影响**（undermine, deprive, ripple through, breed resentment）；**论辩词**（legitimate, scaremongering, security panic, double tragedy）。掌握这组词，可应对"科技战与产业政策"类文章。

### 表达方式

1. **先立后破**：开篇陈述恐慌（battleground/suspicion），再逐条反驳（Consider the role...）——论证完整。
2. **具体企业作证**：用 WuXi AppTec、ADC 等具体公司与数据——让抽象论辩落地。
3. **"Not just...but"递进**：rely on Chinese firms not just for manufacturing, but for ... pipelines——揭示依赖深度。
4. **否定式平衡**：None of this means ... / not entirely without foundation——先否定再补充，显得公允。
5. **对仗主张**：targeted controls, not blanket restrictions——用对仗亮出政策建议。
6. **情感收束**：a double tragedy: for American patients, and for the global science——以双重悲剧收尾。

### 语法要点

- **动名词作主语**：Limiting them would make drugs more expensive——动名词短语作主语表假设。
- **现在分词伴随**：stealing Western know-how and undermining America's global lead——分词展开后果。
- **should/should not 对称**：should be scrutinised, but ... should not be swept up——用情态动词对仗划边界。
- **形式主语**：It is easy to dismiss such fears——It 形式主语 + 真正主语不定式。
- **祈使句引导**：Consider the role ...——用祈使句把论证拉回事实。

### 长难句

最值得模仿的是"否定式平衡句"（原句 23）：not entirely without foundation 双重否定表"并非全无根据"。其次是 not just...but 递进（原句 14）——从制造到研发的依赖升级。

### 写作技巧

- **受害者身份反转**：说"限制中国"会让"美国病人"受伤——把抽象安全辩论拉回具体生活。
- **"安全关切该有"的诚实**：先承认 legitimate worries，再反对 blanket restrictions——立场可信。
- **历史教训引证**：past decade in tech——用芯片战等先例证明封锁无效。
- **定位句收束**：Biotech should not become the next casualty——一个比喻完成全文立意。

### 可迁移表达

- "The next battleground could be X" — 用于"下一个竞争/冲突领域"。
- "tools of the state" — 用于"被国家利用的工具"。
- "put pressure on prices" — 用于"压低价格的市场力量"。
- "targeted controls, not blanket restrictions" — 用于"精准管控而非一刀切"。
- "a double tragedy" — 用于"双重受害"的总结。
