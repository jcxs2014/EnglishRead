# David Hockney believed in working from the heart

**文章概览：** 《经济学人》讣告（obituary）栏目。追忆英国画家大卫·霍克尼（David Hockney，1937–2026）。霍克尼以游泳池系列、拼贴摄影（joiners）、iPad 绘画著称，是 20 世纪最受欢迎的艺术家之一。讣告强调他的核心信念："从内心出发作画"（working from the heart）——对感知、色彩与快乐的艺术信仰，以及他那句"艺术必须带来欢乐"的著名主张。

**文体特征：** 讣告文体。以一位标志性画面（泳池）开篇 + 生平脉络（约克郡→洛杉矶→艺术实验）+ 人物性格（乐观、反理论、好辩）+ 艺术哲学（从心作画）。语调温情克制，结尾升华。

**全文结构：**
- 第 1 段：开场——标志性画面（泳池）
- 第 2 段：艺术哲学——从心作画
- 第 3 段：生平——约克郡到洛杉矶
- 第 4 段：艺术实验——拼贴与 iPad
- 第 5 段：性格——乐观好辩
- 第 6 段：结论——欢乐的遗产

## 一、逐句精读

### 第 1 段：开场 —— 标志性画面（泳池）

> **原句 1:** DAVID HOCKNEY'S most famous painting shows a man swimming in a Californian pool, painted in a style so flat that it feels both real and unreal.

**中文理解：** 大卫·霍克尼最著名的画作描绘一个男人在加州泳池中游泳，画风如此扁平，让人觉得既真实又不真实。

**句子结构：** `DAVID HOCKNEY'S most famous painting shows a man [swimming in a Californian pool], [painted in a style so flat that it feels both real and unreal]`——主谓宾 + 分词 + 结果从句。

**关键词：**
- `a style so flat that` ⭐⭐⭐ — 如此扁平的风格以至于。
- `both real and unreal` ⭐⭐⭐ — 既真实又不真实。

**表达方式：** **shows a man swimming in a Californian pool, painted in a style so flat that it feels both real and unreal**：`DAVID HOCKNEY'S most famous painting shows a man swimming in a Californian pool, painted in a style so flat that it feels both real and unreal`——swimming in a Californian pool（加州泳池游泳）+ so flat that ... both real and unreal（平到既真又不真）——泳池画：既写实又梦幻。

**为什么这样写：** 用标志性泳池画开篇——第一句就确立霍克尼的视觉签名。

> **原句 2:** The painting is famous, but the man's face is deliberately left blank.

**中文理解：** 这幅画很有名，但男人的脸被刻意留白。

**句子结构：** `The painting is famous, but [the man's face is deliberately left blank]`——并列句 + but 转折。

**关键词：**
- `deliberately left blank` ⭐⭐⭐ — 刻意留白。

**表达方式：** **The painting is famous, but the man's face is deliberately left blank**：`The painting is famous, but the man's face is deliberately left blank`——famous（有名）but face deliberately left blank（脸却刻意留白）——悖论：名画的脸是空白的。

**为什么这样写：** 用 but 点出泳池画的标志性细节——脸留白，人人可代入。

> **原句 3:** It is a portrait of everyone and no one.

**中文理解：** 这是一幅关于每个人、又谁也不是的肖像。

**句子结构：** `It is a portrait of everyone and no one`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `a portrait of everyone and no one` ⭐⭐⭐ — 关于每个人又谁也不是的肖像。

**表达方式：** **It is a portrait of everyone and no one**：`It is a portrait of everyone and no one`——everyone and no one（每个人又谁也不是）——留白的意义：普世性。

**为什么这样写：** 用"每个人又谁也不是"概括留白——泳池里的男人是人类的集体影像。

> **原句 4:** That summed up his art, and his life.

**中文理解：** 那概括了他的艺术，也概括了他的一生。

**句子结构：** `That summed up his art, and his life`——主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `summed up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 概括。

**表达方式：** **That summed up his art, and his life**：`That summed up his art, and his life`——summed up his art, and his life（概括其艺术与一生）——由画及人。

**为什么这样写：** 用"艺术与人生"双宾语——泳池画是理解霍克尼的钥匙。

**段落逻辑：** 名画开场（泳池）→ 悖论（脸留白）→ 意义（每个人又谁也不是）→ 由画及人（概括其一生）。第 1 段完成"开场"。---

### 第 2 段：艺术哲学 —— 从心作画

> **原句 5:** Hockney believed in working from the heart.

**中文理解：** 霍克尼信奉从内心出发作画。

**句子结构：** `Hockney believed in working from the heart`——主谓宾（介词宾语）。

**关键词：**
- `believed in` ⭐⭐⭐ — 信奉。
- `working from the heart` ⭐⭐⭐ — 从内心作画。

**表达方式：** **Hockney believed in working from the heart**：`Hockney believed in working from the heart`——working from the heart（从心作画）——标题句：核心信念。

**为什么这样写：** 第一句直接点题——"从心作画"是霍克尼的信条。

> **原句 6:** He distrusted theory, believing it got in the way of the eye and the hand.

**中文理解：** 他不信任理论，认为理论妨碍了眼睛和手。

**句子结构：** `He distrusted theory, [believing it got in the way of the eye and the hand]`——主谓宾 + 现在分词。

**关键词：**
- `distrusted` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不信任。
- `got in the way of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 妨碍。

**表达方式：** **He distrusted theory, believing it got in the way of the eye and the hand**：`He distrusted theory, believing it got in the way of the eye and the hand`——distrusted theory（不信理论）+ got in the way of the eye and the hand（妨碍眼与手）——反理论立场。

**为什么这样写：** 用现在分词解释原因——理论会挡住最直接的感知（eye and hand）。

> **原句 7:** For him, painting was a way of seeing, and seeing was a form of joy.

**中文理解：** 对他而言，绘画是一种观看的方式，而观看是一种欢乐的形式。

**句子结构：** `For him, painting was a way of seeing, and [seeing was a form of joy]`——并列句。

**关键词：**
- `a way of seeing` ⭐⭐⭐ — 观看的方式。
- `a form of joy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 欢乐的形式。

**表达方式：** **painting was a way of seeing, and seeing was a form of joy**：`For him, painting was a way of seeing, and seeing was a form of joy`——painting was a way of seeing（绘画=观看）+ seeing was a form of joy（观看=欢乐）——回环句式：绘画→观看→欢乐。

**为什么这样写：** 用"painting/seeing/joy"的回环递进——确立"艺术即欢乐"的哲学。

> **原句 8:** "Art should make you happy," he would say.

**中文理解：** "艺术应该让你快乐，"他常说。

**句子结构：** `"Art should make you happy," he would say`——直接引语。

**关键词：**
- `make you happy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 让你快乐。

**表达方式：** **"Art should make you happy," he would say**：`"Art should make you happy," he would say`——Art should make you happy（艺术该让人快乐）——名言：艺术的功能是快乐。

**为什么这样写：** 用直接引语给出霍克尼的著名主张——"艺术必须带来欢乐"。

**段落逻辑：** 从心作画（点题）→ 反理论（妨碍眼与手）→ 回环（绘画=观看=欢乐）→ 名言（艺术该让人快乐）。第 2 段完成"艺术哲学"。

### 第 3 段：生平 —— 约克郡到洛杉矶

> **原句 9:** Born in 1937 in Bradford, a mill town in northern England, he moved to California in the 1960s, lured by the light, the space and the swimming pools.

**中文理解：** 1937 年出生于英格兰北部磨坊小镇布拉德福德，他在 1960 年代被光线、空间和游泳池吸引，搬到了加州。

**句子结构：** `Born in 1937 in Bradford, [a mill town in northern England], he moved to California in the 1960s, [lured by the light, the space and the swimming pools]`——分词开头 + 同位语 + 主谓 + 分词。

**关键词：**
- `a mill town` ⭐⭐⭐ — 磨坊小镇。
- `lured by` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被……吸引。

**表达方式：** **Born in 1937 in Bradford ... he moved to California in the 1960s, lured by the light, the space and the swimming pools**：`Born in 1937 in Bradford, a mill town in northern England, he moved to California in the 1960s, lured by the light, the space and the swimming pools`——Born in Bradford（生于布拉德福德）+ lured by the light, the space and the swimming pools（被光、空间、泳池吸引）——从灰暗英格兰到阳光加州。

**为什么这样写：** 用"生于灰暗磨坊镇"与"被加州的光/空间/泳池吸引"对照——人生转折的意象。

> **原句 10:** There he found his subjects: the swimming pools, the palm trees, the tanned bodies.

**中文理解：** 在那里他找到了他的主题：游泳池、棕榈树、晒黑的身体。

**句子结构：** `There he found his subjects: [the swimming pools, the palm trees, the tanned bodies]`——主谓宾 + 冒号列举。

**关键词：**
- `found his subjects` ⭐⭐⭐ — 找到他的主题。
- `tanned bodies` ⭐⭐⭐ — 晒黑的身体。

**表达方式：** **There he found his subjects: the swimming pools, the palm trees, the tanned bodies**：`There he found his subjects: the swimming pools, the palm trees, the tanned bodies`——found his subjects（找到主题）+ 三连列举（泳池/棕榈/晒黑的身体）——加州三元素。

**为什么这样写：** 用冒号列举霍克尼的加州题材——泳池、棕榈、身体。

> **原句 11:** His pool paintings made him famous, but fame never made him complacent.

**中文理解：** 他的泳池画让他成名，但名气从未让他自满。

**句子结构：** `His pool paintings made him famous, but [fame never made him complacent]`——并列句 + but 转折。

**关键词：**
- `complacent` ⭐⭐⭐ — 自满的。

**表达方式：** **His pool paintings made him famous, but fame never made him complacent**：`His pool paintings made him famous, but fame never made him complacent`——made him famous（成名）but never complacent（却从未自满）——成名而不停滞。

**为什么这样写：** 用"成名但未自满"——为后文"不断实验"铺垫。

> **原句 12:** He kept changing his medium, as if afraid of being pinned down.

**中文理解：** 他不断更换媒介，仿佛害怕被固定下来。

**句子结构：** `He kept changing his medium, [as if afraid of being pinned down]`——主谓宾 + as if 省略句。

**关键词：**
- `changing his medium` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更换媒介。
- `pinned down` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被固定。

**表达方式：** **He kept changing his medium, as if afraid of being pinned down**：`He kept changing his medium, as if afraid of being pinned down`——kept changing his medium（不断换媒介）+ as if afraid of being pinned down（仿佛怕被定型）——艺术家的不安分。

**为什么这样写：** 用 as if 推测动机——不断实验源于"怕被定型"。

**段落逻辑：** 生平（布拉德福德→加州）→ 主题（泳池/棕榈/身体）→ 成名但未自满 → 不断换媒介（怕被定型）。第 3 段完成"生平"。---

### 第 4 段：艺术实验 —— 拼贴与 iPad

> **原句 13:** In the 1980s he pioneered the photo-collage, stitching dozens of Polaroids into a single cubist view.

**中文理解：** 1980 年代他开创了照片拼贴，把几十张宝丽来照片缝合成立体主义式的单一视角。

**句子结构：** `In the 1980s he pioneered the photo-collage, [stitching dozens of Polaroids into a single cubist view]`——主谓宾 + 现在分词。

**关键词：**
- `pioneered` ⭐⭐⭐ — 开创。
- `photo-collage` ⭐⭐⭐ — 照片拼贴。
- `cubist view` ⭐⭐⭐ — 立体主义视角。

**表达方式：** **he pioneered the photo-collage, stitching dozens of Polaroids into a single cubist view**：`In the 1980s he pioneered the photo-collage, stitching dozens of Polaroids into a single cubist view`——pioneered the photo-collage（开创拼贴）+ stitching dozens of Polaroids（缝合几十张宝丽来）——拼贴实验。

**为什么这样写：** 用具体手法（几十张宝丽来拼成立体视角）——展示霍克尼的媒介实验。

> **原句 14:** Decades later, he took up the iPad, delighted by the fact that he could draw with light.

**中文理解：** 几十年后，他拿起 iPad，为能用光来作画而高兴。

**句子结构：** `Decades later, he took up the iPad, [delighted by the fact that [he could draw with light]]`——主谓宾 + 分词 + 同位语从句。

**关键词：**
- `took up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 拿起、开始使用。
- `draw with light` ⭐⭐⭐ — 用光作画。

**表达方式：** **he took up the iPad, delighted by the fact that he could draw with light**：`Decades later, he took up the iPad, delighted by the fact that he could draw with light`——took up the iPad（拿起 iPad）+ draw with light（用光作画）——媒介的延续：光。

**为什么这样写：** 用"能用光作画"（draw with light）连接新旧媒介——从颜料到屏幕，不变的是"光"。

> **原句 15:** His critics called it a gimmick; his defenders saw it as proof that he never stopped looking.

**中文理解：** 他的批评者称之为噱头；他的捍卫者则认为这证明他从未停止观看。

**句子结构：** `His critics called it a gimmick; [his defenders saw it as proof that [he never stopped looking]]`——两个并列句（分号）+ that 同位语从句。

**关键词：**
- `a gimmick` ⭐⭐⭐ — 噱头。
- `proof that` ⭐⭐⭐ — 证明。

**表达方式：** **His critics called it a gimmick; his defenders saw it as proof that he never stopped looking**：`His critics called it a gimmick; his defenders saw it as proof that he never stopped looking`——critics called it a gimmick（批评者称噱头）vs defenders saw it as proof（捍卫者视为证明）——分号对仗：两种评价。

**为什么这样写：** 用分号让两种评价对峙——iPad 绘画引起的争议，正是霍克尼的延续性。

> **原句 16:** For him, the screen was just another window.

**中文理解：** 对他而言，屏幕只是另一扇窗。

**句子结构：** `For him, the screen was just another window`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `just another window` ⭐⭐⭐ — 只是另一扇窗。

**表达方式：** **For him, the screen was just another window**：`For him, the screen was just another window`——the screen was just another window（屏幕只是又一扇窗）——媒介观的统一：一切媒介都是窗。

**为什么这样写：** 用"屏幕是另一扇窗"总结媒介观——油画、拼贴、iPad 都是观看的窗。

**段落逻辑：** 1980 拼贴（宝丽来缝合）→ iPad 作画（用光）→ 争议（噱头 vs 证明）→ 媒介观（屏幕只是窗）。第 4 段完成"实验"。

### 第 5 段：性格 —— 乐观好辩

> **原句 17:** Hockney was famously cheerful, and famously argumentative.

**中文理解：** 霍克尼以开朗著称，也以好辩著称。

**句子结构：** `Hockney was famously cheerful, and famously argumentative`——主系表 + 并列表语。

**关键词：**
- `famously cheerful` ⭐⭐⭐ — 以开朗著称。
- `argumentative` ⭐⭐⭐ — 好辩的。

**表达方式：** **Hockney was famously cheerful, and famously argumentative**：`Hockney was famously cheerful, and famously argumentative`——cheerful（开朗）and argumentative（好辩）——性格双面：乐观 + 好辩。

**为什么这样写：** 用"开朗而好辩"并列——性格的两个侧面。

> **原句 18:** He could pick a fight about colour theory at a dinner party.

**中文理解：** 他能在一场晚宴上为色彩理论吵起来。

**句子结构：** `He could pick a fight about colour theory at a dinner party`——主谓宾 + 介词短语。

**关键词：**
- `pick a fight` ⭐⭐⭐ — 挑起争吵。

**表达方式：** **He could pick a fight about colour theory at a dinner party**：`He could pick a fight about colour theory at a dinner party`——pick a fight about colour theory（为色彩理论吵）——好辩的具体化：晚宴上较真。

**为什么这样写：** 用"晚宴上为色彩理论吵架"的轶事——好辩的幽默呈现。

> **原句 19:** But his arguments were almost always in service of joy.

**中文理解：** 但他的争论几乎总是服务于欢乐。

**句子结构：** `But his arguments were almost always in service of joy`——主系表 + 介词短语。

**关键词：**
- `in service of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 服务于。

**表达方式：** **But his arguments were almost always in service of joy**：`But his arguments were almost always in service of joy`——in service of joy（服务于欢乐）——好辩的初衷：为欢乐而争。

**为什么这样写：** 用 But 转折——好辩背后是"为欢乐而辩"的信念。

> **原句 20:** He argued not because he was angry, but because he cared.

**中文理解：** 他争论不是因为他愤怒，而是因为他在乎。

**句子结构：** `He argued not because [he was angry], but because [he cared]`——主谓 + not...but 并列原因从句。

**关键词：**
- `not because ... but because` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不是因为……而是因为。

**表达方式：** **He argued not because he was angry, but because he cared**：`He argued not because he was angry, but because he cared`——not because angry（非因愤怒）but because cared（而在乎）——金句：争论源于在乎。

**为什么这样写：** 用 not...but 的对称——把"好辩"重新定义为"在乎"。

**段落逻辑：** 开朗又好辩 → 晚宴上为色彩理论吵 → 但争论服务于欢乐 → 争论不是愤怒而是在乎。第 5 段完成"性格"。---

### 第 6 段：结论 —— 欢乐的遗产

> **原句 21:** Hockney died on July 8th, aged 89.

**中文理解：** 霍克尼于 7 月 8 日去世，享年 89 岁。

**句子结构：** `Hockney died on July 8th, aged 89`——主谓 + 时间 + 同位短语。

**关键词：**
- `aged 89` ⭐⭐⭐ — 享年 89 岁。

**表达方式：** **Hockney died on July 8th, aged 89**：`Hockney died on July 8th, aged 89`——died on July 8th（7 月 8 日去世）+ aged 89（享年 89）——讣告的标准信息句。

**为什么这样写：** 讣告体例——在结尾交代死讯（obituary 惯例）。

> **原句 22:** He leaves behind a vast body of work and the conviction that art should celebrate life, not merely reflect it.

**中文理解：** 他留下了大量作品和一个信念：艺术应该赞美生活，而不仅仅是反映生活。

**句子结构：** `He leaves behind a vast body of work and the conviction that [art should celebrate life, not merely reflect it]`——主谓宾 + that 同位语从句。

**关键词：**
- `a vast body of work` ⭐⭐⭐ — 大量作品。
- `celebrate life` ⭐⭐⭐ — 赞美生活。

**表达方式：** **He leaves behind a vast body of work and the conviction that art should celebrate life, not merely reflect it**：`He leaves behind a vast body of work and the conviction that art should celebrate life, not merely reflect it`——vast body of work（大量作品）+ conviction that art should celebrate life（艺术应赞美生活的信念）+ not merely reflect it（而非只反映）——遗产双件套：作品 + 信念。

**为什么这样写：** 用"作品 + 信念"并列——霍克尼留下的不仅是画，还有"艺术赞美生活"的主张。

> **原句 23:** His pool has no diver, but it will always be full of light.

**中文理解：** 他的泳池里没有跳水者，但它将永远充满光。

**句子结构：** `His pool has no diver, but [it will always be full of light]`——并列句 + but 转折。

**关键词：**
- `full of light` ⭐⭐⭐ — 充满光。

**表达方式：** **His pool has no diver, but it will always be full of light**：`His pool has no diver, but it will always be full of light`——no diver（没有跳水者）but always full of light（却永远充满光）——收束意象：空泳池盛满光。

**为什么这样写：** 呼应开篇泳池画——人走了，光仍在；画面留白，欢乐常在。

> **原句 24:** That, in the end, was his greatest work: making the world look lighter.

**中文理解：** 那终究是他最伟大的作品：让世界看起来更明亮。

**句子结构：** `That, [in the end], was his greatest work: [making the world look lighter]`——主系表 + 冒号解释 + 动名词。

**关键词：**
- `his greatest work` ⭐⭐⭐ — 他最伟大的作品。
- `making the world look lighter` ⭐⭐⭐ — 让世界看起来更明亮。

**表达方式：** **That, in the end, was his greatest work: making the world look lighter**：`That, in the end, was his greatest work: making the world look lighter`——his greatest work（最伟大的作品）+ making the world look lighter（让世界更明亮）——升华收束：一生的作品是"让世界变亮"。

**为什么这样写：** 以"让世界更明亮"作结——把画家的一生提炼为一个动词短语，呼应"欢乐"主题。

**段落逻辑：** 死讯（7 月 8 日，89 岁）→ 遗产（作品+信念：艺术赞美生活）→ 意象（空泳池盛光）→ 升华（最伟大的作品是让世界变亮）。全文完成。---

## 二、长难句专项

### 长难句 1（原句 1）

**原句：** DAVID HOCKNEY'S most famous painting shows a man swimming in a Californian pool, painted in a style so flat that it feels both real and unreal.

**找主干：** DAVID HOCKNEY'S most famous painting shows a man。

**找修饰：**
- swimming in a Californian pool——现在分词（修饰 man）
- painted in a style——过去分词（修饰 painting/man）
- so flat that it feels both real and unreal——so...that 结果从句

**找从句：** that 结果从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 名画描绘加州泳池游泳的人，画风平到既真又不真。

**整体理解：** 主干 + 两个分词 + so...that 从句——多层修饰环绕。

### 长难句 2（原句 9）

**原句：** Born in 1937 in Bradford, a mill town in northern England, he moved to California in the 1960s, lured by the light, the space and the swimming pools.

**找主干：** he moved to California in the 1960s。

**找修饰：**
- Born in 1937 in Bradford——分词开头（出生信息）
- a mill town in northern England——同位语（Bradford）
- lured by the light, the space and the swimming pools——分词（原因）

**找从句：** 无从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 生于布拉德福德（英格兰北部磨坊镇），1960 年代被光/空间/泳池吸引搬去加州。

**整体理解：** 两个分词 + 一个同位语包裹主干——讣告式生平句的典型。

### 长难句 3（原句 15）

**原句：** His critics called it a gimmick; his defenders saw it as proof that he never stopped looking.

**找主干：** His critics called it a gimmick; his defenders saw it as proof。

**找修饰：**
- that he never stopped looking——同位语从句（修饰 proof）

**找从句：** that 同位语从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 批评者称之为噱头；捍卫者视之为他从未停止观看的证明。

**整体理解：** 分号对仗的两个分句 + 同位语从句——"A 说 X；B 说 Y"的对抗结构。

### 长难句 4（原句 22）

**原句：** He leaves behind a vast body of work and the conviction that art should celebrate life, not merely reflect it.

**找主干：** He leaves behind a vast body of work and the conviction。

**找修饰：**
- that art should celebrate life, not merely reflect it——同位语从句（修饰 conviction）

**找从句：** that 同位语从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 他留下大量作品与"艺术应赞美生活而非只反映"的信念。

**整体理解：** 双宾语并列 + 同位语从句——"实物 + 精神"的遗产句式。

## 三、精读结束总结

### 表达方式

1. **意象开场**：泳池画（脸留白）——用一幅画作传主名片。
2. **由画及人**：That summed up his art, and his life——从作品切入人生。
3. **回环递进**：painting was a way of seeing, and seeing was a form of joy——概念链。
4. **名言引用**："Art should make you happy"——用直接引语立人物。
5. **对照结构**：critics vs defenders / cheerful vs argumentative——用对照呈现复杂。
6. **意象收束**：His pool has no diver, but it will always be full of light——首尾呼应。

### 语法要点

- **分词开头生平句**：Born in 1937 in Bradford, ... he moved to California。
- **so...that 结果从句**：a style so flat that it feels both real and unreal。
- **分号对仗**：His critics called it a gimmick; his defenders saw it as proof。
- **not...but 并列原因**：not because he was angry, but because he cared。
- **同位语从句**：the conviction that art should celebrate life。
- **as if 省略句**：as if afraid of being pinned down。

### 长难句

最值得模仿的是"分词生平句"（原句 9）与"分号对仗"（原句 15）——前者适合人物传记开头，后者适合呈现争议。

### 写作技巧

- **一句成画**：用一幅画写活一个人——开头即高潮。
- **细节立人**：晚宴上为色彩理论吵架——一个轶事胜过十句评价。
- **哲学与轶事交替**：从心作画（哲学）→ 晚宴吵架（轶事）——虚与实互证。
- **首尾呼应**：泳池开场，泳池收束——意象闭环。

### 可迁移表达

- "working from the heart" — 用于"凭直觉/真心做事"。
- "a portrait of everyone and no one" — 用于"普世性"。
- "painting was a way of seeing" — 用于"X 是理解世界的方式"。
- "in service of joy" — 用于"为……服务"的高级表达。
- "X died, leaving behind a body of work and a conviction" — 用于讣告/追忆收尾。
