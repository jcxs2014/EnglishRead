# 精读分析：《经济学人》—— Why criticism of the Qing dynasty scares the Communist Party

> 原文标题：Why criticism of the Qing dynasty scares the Communist Party
> 来源：The Economist（China 板块 / Chaguan 专栏）
> 精读日期：2026-08-09（来自 2026-07-25 期）

## 第一段（汉服现象）

> **原句 1:** Visitors to historic sites around China see not only palaces and pagodas, but also scores of men and women in period dress posing for photos. To the uninitiated, one flowing robe looks like any other.

**中文理解：** 中国各历史景点的游客看到的不只是宫殿和宝塔，还有大量穿着古装摆拍的人。对于不了解情况的人来说，一件飘逸的长袍和其他长袍没什么两样。

**关键词：**
- `period dress` — 古装/时代服装。
- `To the uninitiated` — 对不熟悉的人来说（`uninitiated` = 不熟悉内情的人）。
- `one flowing robe looks like any other` — 外行看热闹：一件长袍和另一件没区别。

> **原句 2:** But for some donning the historical threads, they are a display with political undertones: a celebration of Han traditions and a rejection of the "foreign" Qing, China's last imperial dynasty, whose final emperor abdicated in 1912.

**中文理解：** 但对于一些穿上历史服装的人来说，这是一种带有政治内涵的展示：庆祝汉族传统，拒绝"外族"的清朝——中国最后一个王朝，其末代皇帝于 1912 年退位。

**关键词：**
- `donning the historical threads` — 穿上历史服装（`don` = 穿上，`threads` = 衣服的俚语）。
- `with political undertones` — 带有政治内涵（`undertone` = 潜在的含义/底色）。
- `a rejection of the "foreign" Qing` — 拒绝"外族"的清朝（引号标注的"foreign"——这是关键概念，暗示叙事把满清视为非我族类）。
- `abdicated` — 退位（1912 年溥仪退位，清朝结束）。

> **原句 3:** It is a view that has gained currency, to the alarm of the Communist Party.

**中文理解：** 这种观点已流行开来，令共产党感到警觉。

**关键词：**
- `gained currency` — ⭐⭐⭐ 流行起来/传播开来（`currency` = 流通/货币，比喻观点像货币一样流通）。
- `to the alarm of` — 令……警觉/惊慌（介词短语表结果）。

## 第二段（故宫偶遇）

> **原句 4:** On a recent stroll around the Forbidden City, once the emperor's home in the centre of Beijing, Chaguan quickly found people with these sartorial sensibilities.

**中文理解：** 最近在故宫漫步时——故宫曾是北京市中心皇帝的居所——Chaguan 专栏很快找到了具有这些服饰敏感度的人。

**关键词：**
- `sartorial sensibilities` — ⭐⭐⭐ 服饰品味/对服装的政治敏感（`sartorial` = 服装的，正式词）。
- `Chaguan` — 茶馆（专栏名称，作者自称）。

> **原句 5:** "Before, we might have worn Qing clothing. We're more historically aware now. The Qing were barbarians," says one woman, a university student in a billowing light-green dress, alongside her boyfriend in a subdued scholarly robe.

**中文理解：** "以前我们可能穿清装。现在我们更有历史意识了。满清是蛮族，"一位穿着飘动浅绿色裙子的女大学生说，她的男友穿着素雅的学究长袍。

**关键词：**
- `The Qing were barbarians` — 满清是蛮族（直接引语，展示反清叙事最直白的表述）。
- `billowing light-green dress` — 飘动的浅绿色连衣裙（衣物的动态描写）。
- `subdued scholarly robe` — 素雅学究长袍（`subdued` = 柔和的/素雅的）。

> **原句 6:** Not all opting for pre-Qing clothing care much about the politics. "It's just more refined and aesthetic," says Ms Xu, a visitor from Fujian province, explaining her choice of an off-white Ming-style noblewoman's outfit rather than flashier Qing garb.

**中文理解：** 并非所有选择前清服饰的人都关心政治。"它只是更精致、更有美感，"来自福建省的游客徐女士说，解释她选择米白色明式贵妇装束而非更花哨的清式服装。

**关键词：**
- `refined and aesthetic` — 精致且具美感的（审美理由）。
- `off-white Ming-style noblewoman's outfit` — 米白色明式贵妇装束。

## 第三段（"1644 历史观"）

> **原句 7:** Clothing is the most superficial part of the anti-Qing thinking now growing more prevalent. Discourse about the dynasty and its misdeeds has long featured in online forums, summarised as the "1644 historical view", referring to the year that the Qing overthrew the Ming.

**中文理解：** 服装只是日益盛行的反清思想中最表面的部分。关于清王朝及其恶行的讨论长期以来出现在网络论坛中，被概括为"1644 历史观"——指满清推翻明朝的那一年。

**关键词：**
- `the most superficial part` — 最表面的部分（服装只是冰山一角）。
- `the "1644 historical view"` — "1644 历史观"（核心概念：以 1644 年为历史分界）。
- `overthrew the Ming` — 推翻明朝。

> **原句 8:** (The Ming rulers were Han, China's dominant ethnic group, whereas the Qing were Manchus, a smaller minority group from the north.) Social media have helped the 1644 view spread to more mainstream audiences.

**中文理解：** （明朝统治者是汉族——中国占主导地位的民族；而满清是满族——来自北方的一个较小少数民族。）社交媒体帮助"1644 观点"传播到了更主流的受众。

**关键词：**
- `China's dominant ethnic group` — 占主导地位的民族。
- `whereas the Qing were Manchus` — 而满清是满族（对比结构）。

---

## 第四段（官方叙事 vs 反清叙事）

> **原句 9:** In China's official narrative, the country suffered a "century of humiliation" of foreign invasions, starting with the first opium war in 1840 and ending only with Mao's rise to power in 1949.

**中文理解：** 在中国官方叙事中，国家遭受了外敌入侵的"百年国耻"——始于 1840 年第一次鸦片战争，止于 1949 年毛泽东掌权。

**关键词：**
- `"century of humiliation"` — "百年国耻"（官方历史叙事的核心概念）。
- `foreign invasions` — 外国入侵。

> **原句 10:** But the 1644 view—cheekily expressed by some online as "three centuries of humiliation"—argues that subjugation began with the Qing.

**中文理解：** 但"1644 观点"——网上有些人俏皮地表达为"三个世纪的耻辱"——认为屈辱始于满清。

**关键词：**
- `cheekily expressed as "three centuries of humiliation"` — 俏皮地表达为"三个世纪的耻辱"（对"百年国耻"的戏仿——这是全文最有张力的讽刺：官方说 100 年，反清派说 300 年）。
- `subjugation` — 征服/屈从（`subjugation began with the Qing` = 屈辱始于满清）。

> **原句 11:** According to this school, the Qing were anti-Han interlopers and, making matters worse, feckless in repelling foreign forces.

**中文理解：** 按照这一派观点，满清是反汉的侵入者，更糟糕的是，他们在抵御外国势力方面无能。

**关键词：**
- `anti-Han interlopers` — 反汉的侵入者（`interloper` = 闯入者/不速之客）。
- `feckless in repelling foreign forces` — 抵御外敌无能（`feckless` = 无能的/不负责任的）。

---

## 第五段（党的困境）

> **原句 12:** Dredging up all this history is uncomfortable for the party. The territorial boundaries claimed by modern China—including Tibet, Xinjiang, Inner Mongolia and Taiwan—were brought into the Chinese empire by the Qing, not the Ming.

**中文理解：** 挖掘这段历史对党来说令人不适。现代中国声称拥有的领土边界——包括西藏、新疆、内蒙古和台湾——是满清而非明朝并入中华帝国的。

**关键词：**
- `Dredging up` — ⭐⭐⭐ 挖掘/翻出（`dredge` = 疏浚/挖掘，比喻重新翻出旧事）。
- `claimed by modern China` — 现代中国声称拥有的（领土主张）。

> **原句 13:** Grievances about the Manchus also cut against the ideal of ethnic harmony advanced by the party.

**中文理解：** 对满族的怨恨也与党推进的民族和谐理想相抵触。

**关键词：**
- `cut against` — ⭐⭐⭐ 与……相抵触/相违背（`cut against` = 与……相悖）。
- `the ideal of ethnic harmony` — 民族和谐的理想。

> **原句 13b:** Although much of what forms modern Chinese identity is derived from Han traditions, it is unseemly to talk openly about the dominance of this one ethnic group, which accounts for 90% of the Chinese population.

**中文理解：** 尽管构成现代中国身份的许多东西源于汉族传统，但公开谈论这个占中国人口 90% 的民族的主导地位是不体面的。

**关键词：**
- `it is unseemly to talk openly about` — 公开谈论是不体面的（`unseemly` = 不得体的/不合适的）。
- `accounts for 90% of the Chinese population` — 占中国人口的 90%（汉族占绝对多数，而满族是少数——正是敏感性的来源）。

> **原句 14:** The party looks askance at "Han chauvinism", believing, with reason, that it may lead to more resentment from non-Han groups.

**中文理解：** 党对"汉族沙文主义"侧目而视，有理由地相信它可能导致非汉族群体更多的不满。

**关键词：**
- `looks askance at` — ⭐⭐⭐ 侧目而视/怀疑地看（`askance` = 斜视地，表达怀疑/不认可）。
- `"Han chauvinism"` — "汉族沙文主义"（官方警惕的概念）。
- `with reason` — 有理由地（作者认可这种担心是有道理的）。

---

## 第六段（浙江官方发声）

> **原句 15:** Late last year, the communications arm of the Zhejiang provincial government—known for strong opinion pieces—became the first official body to wade into the discussion. It formally condemned the 1644 view, labelling it a harmful distortion.

**中文理解：** 去年年底，以发表犀利评论文章著称的浙江省政府宣传部门成为第一个介入讨论的官方机构。它正式谴责"1644 观点"，称其为有害的歪曲。

**关键词：**
- `wade into` — ⭐⭐⭐ 介入/涉足（`wade` = 涉水，比喻大胆介入争议）。
- `labelling it a harmful distortion` — 将其定性为有害歪曲。

> **原句 16:** The Zhejiang authorities warned that it was an opening for hostile Westerners to push the "new Qing history", which emphasises the unique Manchu identity of Qing rulers.

**中文理解：** 浙江当局警告说，这是敌对的西方人推动"新清史"的入口——"新清史"强调清朝统治者的独特满族身份。

**关键词：**
- `the "new Qing history"` — "新清史"（西方学术流派，强调清朝的满洲帝国属性，被视为对汉化叙事的挑战）。

> **原句 16b:** Too much blame, it argued, was placed on the Qing for China's troubles: the Ming had also been plagued by economic and social problems.

**中文理解：** 它辩称，中国的问题被过多归咎于清朝：明朝同样饱受经济和社会问题困扰。

**关键词：**
- `Too much blame was placed on the Qing` — 过多归咎于清朝（浙江官方的辩护逻辑：明朝也有问题）。
- `plagued by economic and social problems` — 饱受经济和社会问题困扰（`plague` = 使……受苦）。

---

## 第七段（官方整肃）

> **原句 17:** That appears to have been the opening shot in an official rectification campaign. Since then, censors have deleted social-media accounts critical of the Qing and scrubbed many such videos and comments.

**中文理解：** 这似乎是官方整肃行动的第一枪。此后，审查者删除了批评满清的社交媒体账号，并清理了许多此类视频和评论。

**关键词：**
- `the opening shot` — 第一枪/开端的信号。
- `rectification campaign` — 整肃运动/整风运动（中国特有政治术语）。
- `scrubbed` — 清理/擦除（审查行为）。

> **原句 18:** A forthcoming film, "The Belief", is intended as a nationalist tribute to the naval expedition in 1683 that facilitated the Qing's annexation of Taiwan. But many online have been more sympathetic towards the conquered—Han loyalists of the Ming dynasty based in Taiwan—than to the Qing soldiers sent to subdue them.

**中文理解：** 一部即将上映的电影《信念》旨在作为对 1683 年水师远征收复台湾的民族主义致敬。但许多网民对征服者——明朝在台湾的汉族忠臣——比对派去镇压他们的清军更同情。

**关键词：**
- `a nationalist tribute` — 民族主义致敬。
- `the Qing's annexation of Taiwan` — 清朝对台湾的吞并。
- `sympathetic towards the conquered` — 对被征服者同情（网民站队：同情明末汉人）。

> **原句 19:** In fact the 1644 view is far from novel. An early proponent of something like it was Sun Yat-sen, celebrated as a father of modern China. A leading voice for the Qing's overthrow at the turn of the 20th century, he railed against the Manchus as outsiders.

**中文理解：** 事实上，"1644 观点"远非新颖。类似观点的一个早期倡导者是孙中山——被尊为现代中国之父。作为 20 世纪之交推翻满清的主要声音，他痛斥满族为外人。

**关键词：**
- `far from novel` — 远非新颖。
- `railed against` — 痛斥/怒骂（`rail against` = 猛烈抨击）。

---

## 第八段（106 卷清史）

> **原句 20:** The party commissioned an official history of the Qing and was close to completing it as a mammoth 106-volume project. But now it seems to have been scuppered by leaders because of disagreements about "new Qing" contamination.

**中文理解：** 党委托编写一部官方清朝历史，作为庞大的 106 卷项目已接近完成。但现在似乎因领导人之间关于"新清史"污染的争执而被搁置。

**关键词：**
- `a mammoth 106-volume project` — 庞大的 106 卷项目（`mammoth` = 巨大的）。
- `scuppered` — ⭐⭐⭐ 破坏/搁置（`scupper` = 使……失败）。

> **原句 21:** "It is very clear that the state was ruled by people who were not identical to the bulk of the population, so it's hard to fit it into the kind of Chinese nationalism that the party wants," says David Porter, a scholar of imperial China at McGill University.

**中文理解：** "很明显，国家被一群与大多数人口不同的统治者统治，所以很难把它纳入党想要的那种中国民族主义叙事，"麦吉尔大学研究帝制中国的学者大卫·波特说。

**关键词：**
- `ruled by people who were not identical to the bulk of the population` — 被与多数人口不同的统治者统治（清朝历史的内在困境）。

---

## 第九段（评价）

> **原句 22:** The anti-Qing voices probably do not add up to anything significant for China's contemporary politics. They are not about to coalesce into a movement that challenges party rule. They are discomfiting, not destabilising.

**中文理解：** 反清声音可能不会对中国当代政治构成任何重大影响。它们不会凝聚成挑战党的统治的运动。它们令人不安，但不具破坏性。

**关键词：**
- `add up to anything significant` — 构成任何重大影响。
- `coalesce into a movement` — 凝聚成一场运动（`coalesce` = 合并/聚拢）。
- `discomfiting, not destabilising` — 令人不安，而非动摇（精准的区分）。

> **原句 23:** China stands out for its inability to hold a full-throated debate about them. The party is too thin-skinned for that.

**中文理解：** 中国因其无法就这些问题展开一场畅所欲言的公开辩论而显得突出。党对此过于敏感。

**关键词：**
- `full-throated debate` — 畅所欲言的辩论（`full-throated` = 声音洪亮的/毫无保留的）。
- `too thin-skinned` — ⭐⭐⭐ 过于敏感/脸皮太薄（`thin-skinned` = 脸皮薄的，对批评敏感）。

---

## 第十段（结尾）

> **原句 24:** For their part, nationalists are guilty of over-simplifying Chinese history. Romanticising pre-Qing epochs as pure expressions of Han strength omits that China was a multi-ethnic, even cosmopolitan, entity during one of its most glorious periods, under the Tang dynasty more than a millennium ago.

**中文理解：** 就民族主义者而言，他们犯了过度简化中国历史的错误。将前清时代浪漫化为汉族力量的纯粹表现，忽略了中国在其最辉煌时期之一——一千多年前的唐朝——是一个多民族、甚至世界主义的实体。

**关键词：**
- `guilty of over-simplifying` — 犯了过度简化的错误。
- `Romanticising ... as pure expressions of Han strength` — 将……浪漫化为汉族力量的纯粹表现。
- `cosmopolitan entity` — 世界主义的实体（唐朝的多民族属性）。

> **原句 25:** An honest accounting of this record would argue for China's leaders today to be more tolerant and open, and to be confident of what such permissiveness would yield. Just do not hold your breath. ■

**中文理解：** 对这段历史的诚实清算会主张今天中国的领导人更加宽容开放，并相信这种宽容会带来什么。只是别抱太大希望。

**关键词：**
- `an honest accounting` — 诚实的清算/记述。
- `Just do not hold your breath` — ⭐⭐⭐ 别抱太大希望（`hold your breath` = 屏息等待，此处反讽：别指望党会变得宽容）。

3. **`dredge up`** — 翻出旧事（负面）。
4. **`cut against`** — 与……相抵触。
5. **`looks askance at`** — 怀疑地看待。

## 重要语法

1. **`To the uninitiated`**：介词短语作状语（对不了解的人来说）。
2. **`whose final emperor abdicated`**：非限制性定语从句（whose 引导）。
3. **`a view that has gained currency, to the alarm of ...`**：同位语 + 介词短语表结果。
4. **`whereas`**：对比连词（The Ming rulers were Han ... whereas the Qing were Manchus）。
5. **`far from novel`**：`far from` + 形容词（远非……）。
6. **`believing, with reason, that ...`**：现在分词短语作状语 + 插入语。
7. **`Just do not hold your breath`**：反讽的祈使句。

---

## 写作技巧总结

1. **以具体场景切入抽象话题**：从故宫汉服偶遇开始，逐步展开到"1644 历史观"、领土叙事、整肃运动——让宏大的政治分析有了具体的文化现象入口。
2. **"百年国耻" vs "三个世纪的耻辱"**：用网民对官方叙事的戏仿制造张力——一个数字（100→300 年）就精准揭示反清叙事的锋芒。
3. **双重困境的并置**：领土（清并入了现代中国所有争议领土）vs 民族和谐（反清叙事冒犯少数民族）——两个问题互相矛盾，正是党的叙事困境核心。
4. **"discomfiting, not destabilising" 的精确分级**：区分"令人不安"与"动摇统治"——严谨的分析者不夸大威胁。
5. **`too thin-skinned` 与 `don't hold your breath` 的讽刺收尾**：用温和的讽刺而非激烈谴责，反而更有力。
