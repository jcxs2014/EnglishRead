# 精读分析：《经济学人》—— When a president stops pretending that voters count, disaster beckons

> 原文标题：When a president stops pretending that voters count, disaster beckons
> 来源：The Economist（Leaders 社论板块）
> 精读日期：2026-08-09（来自 2026-07-25 期）

---

| 项目     | 说明                                                                                                                                                          |

## 第一段（假选举的功能）

> **原句 1:** "Hypocrisy is the homage that vice pays to virtue," wrote François de la Rochefoucauld, a French moralist. This is especially true in politics.

**中文理解：** "虚伪是恶行向美德缴纳的贡品，"法国道德家弗朗索瓦·德·拉罗什福科写道。这在政治中尤其如此。

**关键词：**
- `the homage that vice pays to virtue` — 恶行向美德缴纳的贡品（`homage` = 敬意/贡品，`pay homage to` = 致敬）。
- `This is especially true in politics` — 这在政治中尤其如此（从格言切入政治）。

> **原句 2:** Autocrats usually put on a big show of holding elections. These may be phoney, as in Russia or Iran, but they allow rulers to claim to govern by popular consent, which lends them at least the appearance of legitimacy at home and abroad.

**中文理解：** 独裁者通常大张旗鼓地举行选举。这些选举可能是假的，如俄罗斯或伊朗，但它们让统治者得以声称依靠民众同意执政，这至少赋予他们在国内外的合法表象。

**关键词：**
- `put on a big show of` — ⭐⭐⭐ 大张旗鼓地表演（`put on a show` = 做戏）。
- `phoney` — 假的（英式拼写，美式 `phony`）。
- `govern by popular consent` — 依靠民众同意执政。
- `the appearance of legitimacy` — 合法性的表象（`appearance` = 表象而非实质）。

---

## 第二段（Ortega 宣布取消选举）

> **原句 3:** So it is highly unusual for the leader of a country that holds elections to say: no more. Yet that is what Daniel Ortega, Nicaragua's co-president, did on July 19th.

**中文理解：** 因此，一个举行选举的国家的领导人说"不再举行了"是极不寻常的。然而，这正是尼加拉瓜联合总统丹尼尔·奥尔特加于 7 月 19 日所做的。

**关键词：**
- `highly unusual` — 极不寻常。
- `Yet that is what ... did` — 强调句式（这正是他所做的）。

> **原句 4:** "There will never be elections here again," he announced, while the world's attention was on the football World Cup final.

**中文理解：** "这里再也不会有选举了，"他宣布——而此时全世界的注意力都在足球世界杯决赛上。

**关键词：**
- `while the world's attention was on the football World Cup final` — 全世界注意力都在世界杯决赛上（刻意选择时机掩盖消息——作者点出这个细节暗示其用心）。

> **原句 5:** He complained that opposition parties wanted to use elections "to seize power". That is indeed what elections are for.

**中文理解：** 他抱怨反对党想利用选举"夺取权力"。这恰恰是选举的用途。

**关键词：**
- `That is indeed what elections are for` — 这恰恰是选举的用途——极简的讽刺反驳（一针见血）。

---

## 第三段（尼加拉瓜恶化史）

> **原句 6:** Globally, democracy has been in retreat for two decades or so. The worst deterioration has been in places that were free or partly free and have gradually become less so.

**中文理解：** 全球范围内，民主已经倒退约二十年。恶化最严重的是那些曾经自由或半自由、然后逐渐变得不那么自由的地方。

**关键词：**
- `democracy has been in retreat` — 民主一直处于倒退中。
- `free or partly free ... have gradually become less so` — 从自由/半自由逐渐变得不那么自由（`so` 替代前面形容词的用法）。

> **原句 7:** power-hungry leaders have squeezed the media, nobbled the courts and persecuted the opposition.

**中文理解：** 渴望权力的领导人挤压媒体、破坏法院、迫害反对派。

**关键词：**
- `power-hungry` — 权力欲强的。
- `nobbled the courts` — ⭐⭐⭐ 使法院丧失效力（`nobble` = 使……失效/行贿，英式俚语）。

> **原句 8:** According to Freedom House, an American watchdog, Nicaragua is the second-worst backslider of the past 20 years, after Mali.

**中文理解：** 根据美国监督机构自由之家，尼加拉瓜是过去 20 年中第二严重的民主倒退者，仅次于马里。

**关键词：**
- `the second-worst backslider` — 第二严重的倒退者（`backslider` = 倒退者/故态复萌者）。

> **原句 9:** Mr Ortega was freely elected in 2006, but then set about dismantling every institution that could challenge him. He locked up and sometimes tortured his rivals. He forced the media to praise the awful poetry of his wife and co-president, Rosario Murillo. Roughly a tenth of the population fled.

**中文理解：** 奥尔特加 2006 年自由当选，但随后开始拆除每一个可能挑战他的机构。他关押、有时拷打对手。他强迫媒体赞美他妻子兼联合总统罗萨里奥·穆里略的糟糕诗歌。大约十分之一的人口逃离。

**关键词：**
- `set about dismantling every institution` — 着手拆除每一个机构（`set about` + doing = 着手做）。
- `forced the media to praise the awful poetry` — 强迫媒体赞美糟糕的诗歌（讽刺细节：连诗都要管）。
- `Roughly a tenth of the population fled` — 约十分之一人口逃离。

---

## 第四段（"For Daniel, the lyin's done"）

> **原句 10:** Until now, however, Mr Ortega has allowed elections, albeit fake ones. In this he has behaved like other strongmen, who typically rig elections rather than scrap them. Even coup leaders with guns on their hips typically say they will hold a ballot "as soon as circumstances allow".

**中文理解：** 然而，直到现在奥尔特加都允许选举——尽管是假的。在这方面，他的行为像其他强人一样，他们通常操纵选举而不是废除选举。即使是腰上别着枪的政变领导人通常也说他们会"在条件允许时尽快"举行投票。

**关键词：**
- `albeit fake ones` — 尽管是假的（`albeit` = 尽管）。
- `rig elections rather than scrap them` — 操纵选举而非废除（核心区分）。
- `with guns on their hips` — 腰间别着枪（细节塑造政变者形象）。
- `"as soon as circumstances allow"` — "一旦条件允许"（政变者的标准托词，带引号暗示这是套话）。

> **原句 11:** Now Mr Ortega has "taken off the mask", says Félix Maradiaga, a former presidential candidate who was stripped of his citizenship and deported.

**中文理解：** 现在奥尔特加已经"摘下了面具"，前总统候选人费利克斯·马拉迪亚加说——他曾被剥夺公民身份并被驱逐。

**关键词：**
- `"taken off the mask"` — "摘下了面具"（直接引语作为标题级比喻）。
- `stripped of his citizenship` — 被剥夺公民身份。

> **原句 12:** He has made explicit what other autocrats feel compelled to hide: that they will not submit to the will of the people.

**中文理解：** 他把他国独裁者不得不隐藏的东西公开化了：他们不会服从人民的意志。

**关键词：**
- `made explicit what others hide` — 把别人隐藏的公开化。
- `submit to the will of the people` — 服从人民的意志。

---

## 第五段（两大问题）

> **原句 13:** Nicaragua highlights two further problems that the global decline of democracy is likely to aggravate. First, leaders who cannot be removed will age in office. Mr Ortega is 80. Perhaps mental infirmity contributed to his latest bombshell.

**中文理解：** 尼加拉瓜凸显了全球民主衰退可能加剧的两个进一步的问题。首先，无法被罢免的领导人会在位变老。奥尔特加 80 岁了。也许心智衰退促成了他最近的震撼表态。

**关键词：**
- `age in office` — 在位变老（`age in office` = 任职期间衰老）。
- `mental infirmity` — 心智衰退（`infirmity` = 虚弱/衰老）。
- `bombshell` — 重磅炸弹/震惊消息。

> **原句 14:** Second, succession. The peaceful transfer of power is tricky in a dictatorship. Mr Ortega wants his unpopular wife to take charge when he dies, and then his equally odious son.

**中文理解：** 其次，继承问题。在独裁统治下，权力的和平交接很棘手。奥尔特加希望在他死后由他不受欢迎的妻子掌权，然后是他同样可憎的儿子。

**关键词：**
- `peaceful transfer of power` — 权力的和平交接。
- `his equally odious son` — 他同样可憎的儿子（`odious` = 可憎的）。

---

## 第六段（毒性组合）

> **原句 15:** This is a toxic combination. Ageing rulers-for-life seldom improve with each passing decade, and dynasties foisted on unwilling citizens are rarely a recipe for good government.

**中文理解：** 这是一个有毒的组合。终身统治者随着年龄增长很少变得更好，强加给不情愿的公民的王朝很少成为好政府的配方。

**关键词：**
- `a toxic combination` — 有毒的组合。
- `rulers-for-life` — 终身统治者。
- `dynasties foisted on unwilling citizens` — 强加给不情愿公民的王朝（`foist` = 强加/塞给）。
- `rarely a recipe for good government` — 很少是好政府的配方。

> **原句 16:** Just ask the people of Uganda, where the 81-year-old strongman's son, army chief and presumed heir has threatened to muzzle the press and castrate the main opposition leader.

**中文理解：** 只需问问乌干达人民——那里的 81 岁强人的儿子、军队首脑兼假定继承人威胁要钳制媒体，并对主要反对派领袖施以阉割。

**关键词：**
- `presumed heir` — 假定继承人。
- `muzzle the press` — 钳制媒体（`muzzle` = 给……戴口套，比喻压制言论）。
- `castrate` — 阉割（震惊的细节）。

> **原句 17:** Or, depending on where you live, you may be able to think of an example closer to home.

**中文理解：** 或者，取决于你住在哪里，你也许能想到一个更接近家乡的例子。

**关键词：**
- `closer to home` — 更接近本国（对读者的暗示：你的国家可能也有类似问题——把尼加拉瓜个案普遍化）。

---

## 第七段（区别的重要性）

> **原句 18:** The difference between rigging elections and abolishing them may sound trivial, but is not.

**中文理解：** 操纵选举与废除选举之间的区别听起来可能微不足道，但实际上并非如此。

**关键词：**
- `may sound trivial, but is not` — 听起来微不足道，但实际不然（经典的先抑后扬）。

> **原句 19:** So long as rulers pay lip service to democratic norms, dissidents can try to hold them to their own professed standards, and embarrass them when they fall short.

**中文理解：** 只要统治者口头上尊重民主规范，异见人士就可以试图用他们自己宣称的标准约束他们，并在他们达不到时让他们难堪。

**关键词：**
- `pay lip service to` — ⭐⭐⭐ 口头上承认/敷衍（`lip service` = 口惠而实不至）。
- `hold them to their own professed standards` — 用他们自己宣称的标准约束他们。
- `fall short` — 达不到标准。

> **原句 20:** So long as elections are held, there is a chance the strongman will miscalculate and lose. Sometimes the popular will prevails despite a tilted playing field, as Viktor Orban discovered in Hungary this year—and Mr Ortega discovered when a free election ended his first spell in office, in 1990.

**中文理解：** 只要举行选举，强人就有失算和失败的可能。有时人民意志会在不平等的赛场上获胜，正如欧尔班今年在匈牙利发现的——奥尔特加也在 1990 年发现过，一场自由选举结束了他第一个任期。

**关键词：**
- `the popular will prevails` — 人民意志获胜。
- `a tilted playing field` — 不平等的赛场（`tilted` = 倾斜的，比喻不公平的竞争环境）。
- `his first spell in office` — 他第一个任期（`spell` = 一段时期）。

---

## 第八段（结尾呼吁）

> **原句 21:** So the precedent being set in Managua is troubling. A world where autocrats no longer pretend to care what the people think would be worse than today's.

**中文理解：** 因此马那瓜树立的先例令人不安。一个独裁者不再假装在乎人民想法的世界将比今天更糟。

**关键词：**
- `the precedent being set` — 正在树立的先例。
- `A world where autocrats no longer pretend ... would be worse` — 独裁者不再假装在乎的世界会更糟（回扣开篇"虚伪是贡品"论点：连虚伪都有价值）。

> **原句 22:** All the more reason for democrats everywhere to insist on the most basic of rights: to choose their own rulers. ■

**中文理解：** 更有理由让世界各地的民主主义者坚持最基础的权利：选择自己的统治者。

**关键词：**
- `All the more reason for` — 更有理由……（收尾呼吁）。
- `the most basic of rights: to choose their own rulers` — 最基础的权利：选择自己的统治者。

## 核心表达（可直接迁移）

1. **`the homage that vice pays to virtue`** — 虚伪是恶行向美德缴纳的贡品（格言）。
2. **`put on a big show of`** — 大张旗鼓地做样子。
3. **`govern by popular consent`** — 依靠民众同意执政。
4. **`nobble the courts`** — 使法院失效。
5. **`rig elections rather than scrap them`** — 操纵而非废除（重要区分）。
6. **`taken off the mask`** — 摘下假面具。
7. **`submit to the will of the people`** — 服从人民意志。
8. **`age in office`** — 在位变老。
9. **`foisted on unwilling citizens`** — 强加给不情愿的公民。
10. **`pay lip service to`** — 口头敷衍。
11. **`hold them to their own professed standards`** — 用其自称标准约束之。
12. **`a tilted playing field`** — 不平等的赛场。

---

## 重要语法

1. **`"Hypocrisy is the homage that vice pays to virtue"`**：定语从句（that vice pays to virtue 修饰 homage）。
2. **`govern by popular consent, which lends them ...`**：非限制性定语从句（which 指代前面整句）。
3. **`it is highly unusual for sb to do`**：形式主语 it。
4. **`while the world's attention was on the World Cup final`**：while 时间对比。
5. **`places that were free or partly free and have gradually become less so`**：`so` 替代形容词（become less free）。
6. **`Mr Ortega was freely elected in 2006, but then set about dismantling`**：`set about` + doing。
7. **`albeit fake ones`**：`albeit` = 尽管（让步）。
8. **`So long as rulers pay lip service ...`**：`so long as` = 只要（条件状语）。
9. **`The difference ... may sound trivial, but is not`**：`may ... but` 让步转折。
10. **`A world where autocrats no longer pretend ... would be worse`**：`where` 定语从句 + 虚拟语气。

---

## 写作技巧总结

1. **以格言开篇并贯穿全文**：拉罗什福科的名言"虚伪是恶行向美德缴纳的贡品"开篇，第八段"A world where autocrats no longer pretend ... would be worse"回扣——首尾呼应，论点完整闭环。
2. **"That is indeed what elections are for" 的极简讽刺**：对 Ortega 抱怨"反对党想夺权"的回应只有一句——选举的用途就是夺权，你的抱怨等于承认选举本质。
3. **`rig elections rather than scrap them` 的关键区分**：全文论证核心不是"选举好坏"，而是"假选举与无选举的差别"——一个看似琐碎但重要的区别。
4. **细节增强叙事冲击力**："forced the media to praise the awful poetry"、"castrate the main opposition leader"、"with guns on their hips"——具体的荒唐/残酷细节让抽象论述有血肉。
5. **从个案到普遍**："you may be able to think of an example closer to home"——把尼加拉瓜个案推及所有读者，暗示这不只是别人的问题。
6. **克制但不软弱**："may sound trivial, but is not"——承认对方可能的异议然后反驳，比直接攻击更有说服力。
