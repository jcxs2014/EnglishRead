## 三、长难句专项

### 🔍 原句 3（which 从句 + 同位语比喻）

> **原句:** The World Cup, which starts on June 11th, is being hosted by America, Canada and Mexico, the international equivalent of going to a dinner party and hearing the hosts rowing in the kitchen.

**① 找主干：** The World Cup is being hosted by America, Canada and Mexico.
**② 找修饰：** which starts on June 11th（非限定定语从句）；the international equivalent of ...（同位语）
**③ 找从句：** which starts on June 11th（定语从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 世界杯 6 月 11 日开赛，由美加墨合办——在国际上等同于去晚宴却听见主人在厨房吵架。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 主干（被主办）+ which 非限定从句（日期）+ 同位语（比喻）。同位语是名词短语（the international equivalent of ...），其中 going to a dinner party and hearing ... 是动名词并列。把握"名词同位语 + 动名词"结构。

### 🔍 原句 5（although 让步 + for neutrals 状语）

> **原句:** Although watching matches that feature your own nation is dependably exciting, for neutrals the average game is likely to be pretty drab.

**① 找主干：** the average game is likely to be pretty drab.
**② 找修饰：** Although watching matches ... is dependably exciting（让步从句）；for neutrals（介词短语）
**③ 找从句：** Although ... exciting（让步从句，内含 that feature your own nation 定语从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 尽管看自家球队的比赛必定刺激，对中立观众来说每场平均比赛可能相当乏味。
**⑤ 整体理解：** although 让步从句中，watching matches 是动名词主语，that feature your own nation 是定语；主句 for neutrals（对中立者）限定视角。把握"Although + 动名词主语"。

### 🔍 原句 6（too...to + 动名词主语）

> **原句:** The stakes at a World Cup are too high for players to take big risks, and hanging on for a draw often pays off for less fancied teams.

**① 找主干：** The stakes are too high, and hanging on for a draw often pays off.
**② 找修饰：** at a World Cup（介词短语）；for players to take big risks（too...to 补足）；for less fancied teams（介词短语）
**③ 找从句：** 无从句
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 世界杯赌注太高，球员不敢冒险；死守平局对弱队常很划算。
**⑤ 整体理解：** too...for sb to do（太……以致不能）；and 后 hanging on for a draw 动名词作主语，pays off（划算）谓语。把握"too...to + 动名词主语"。

### 🔍 原句 8（whose 定语 + such as 举例）

> **原句:** Arsène Wenger, a manager whose credentials include making Arsenal bearable to watch, has proposed changes such as tweaking the offside rule and turning throw-ins into kick-ins.

**① 找主干：** Arsène Wenger has proposed changes.
**② 找修饰：** a manager whose credentials ...（同位语，含 whose 定语从句）；such as tweaking ... and turning ...（举例）
**③ 找从句：** whose credentials include making Arsenal bearable to watch（定语从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 温格——资历包括"让阿森纳值得看"的主帅——提议了微调越位、界外球改踢之类的改革。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 主干（主谓宾）+ 同位语（a manager whose ...）+ such as 举例（两个动名词）。把握"同位语含 whose 从句"。

### 🔍 原句 16（分词定语 + 双宾）

> **原句:** A neutral team, made up of the highest-ranked players from these countries, would give everyone a second-favourite side.

**① 找主干：** A neutral team would give everyone a second-favourite side.
**② 找修饰：** made up of the highest-ranked players from these countries（分词定语）
**③ 找从句：** 无从句
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 一支由这些国家最高排名球员组成的中立队，会给每个人一支第二喜欢的球队。
**⑤ 整体理解：** give everyone a second-favourite side 是双宾结构（give sb sth）；made up of（由……组成）分词定语。把握"双宾 + 分词定语"。

### 🔍 原句 17（as 时间从句 + 被动）

> **原句:** As the match progresses, the goalkeeper is allowed to use less and less of his body to make saves.

**① 找主干：** the goalkeeper is allowed to use less and less of his body.
**② 找修饰：** As the match progresses（时间从句）；to make saves（不定式目的）
**③ 找从句：** As the match progresses（时间从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 随着比赛推进，门将被允许用身体越来越少来做扑救。
**⑤ 整体理解：** as（随着）时间从句 + 被动（is allowed to）+ less and less of（越来越少的部分）。把握"as + less and less"渐进结构。

### 🔍 原句 22（完成时不定式 + 条件句）

> **原句:** If a player is judged to have feigned injury, the next foul on them will be unpunishable.

**① 找主干：** the next foul on them will be unpunishable.
**② 找修饰：** If a player is judged to have feigned injury（条件从句）
**③ 找从句：** If a player is judged ...（条件从句，含 to have feigned 完成时不定式）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 若球员被判定曾假装受伤，下次对他犯规将不受处罚。
**⑤ 整体理解：** is judged to have feigned（被判定曾假装）——to have feigned 是完成时不定式（表示"过去曾"）；be unpunishable（不可处罚）。把握"被动 + 完成时不定式"。

### 🔍 原句 30（被动 + until 双终止）

> **原句:** Play will be allowed to continue until the ball goes off the field or the crowd starts booing.

**① 找主干：** Play will be allowed to continue.
**② 找修饰：** until the ball goes off the field or the crowd starts booing（时间从句）
**③ 找从句：** until ... booing（时间从句，含 or 并列）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 比赛将被允许继续，直到球出界或观众开始喝倒彩。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 被动（will be allowed to continue）+ until 时间从句（or 连接两个终止条件）。把握"until A or B"。

### 🔍 原句 39（spend 结构 + 分词）

> **原句:** Even the best teams spend inordinate amounts of time passing the ball from one side of the pitch to the other, apparently unaware that the goal is in front of them.

**① 找主干：** Even the best teams spend inordinate amounts of time.
**② 找修饰：** passing the ball from ... to ...（分词状语）；apparently unaware that ...（分词短语）
**③ 找从句：** that the goal is in front of them（宾语从句，unaware 的宾语）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 即使最好的球队也花大量时间横传，显然没意识到球门就在面前。
**⑤ 整体理解：** spend time doing（花时间做）+ 分词状语（passing）+ 形容词分词（unaware that ...）。把握"spend time doing + unaware that"。

### 2. 重要语法

- **对仗反转**：`the beautiful game` → `the boring game`
- **which 非限定从句**：`The World Cup, which starts on June 11th, is being hosted by ...`
- **同位语比喻**：`the international equivalent of going to a dinner party and hearing the hosts rowing in the kitchen`
- **although 让步 + 动名词主语**：`Although watching matches that feature your own nation is dependably exciting ...`
- **too...to 结构**：`too high for players to take big risks`
- **动名词主语**：`hanging on for a draw often pays off`
- **whose 定语从句**：`a manager whose credentials include making Arsenal bearable to watch`
- **such as 举例**：`such as tweaking the offside rule and turning throw-ins into kick-ins`
- **双宾结构**：`give everyone a second-favourite side`、`award a penalty against a team`
- **as 时间从句 + 渐进**：`As the match progresses ... less and less of`
- **条件句 + 完成时不定式**：`If a player is judged to have feigned injury`
- **Either...or 双结局**：`Either there will be less rolling around on the floor or there will be the drama of violent retribution`
- **被动规则句**：`a random member of the crowd is chosen to participate`
- **until 双终止**：`until the ball goes off the field or the crowd starts booing`
- **Nothing like 句式**：`There's nothing like the promise of permanent residency to make sport exciting`
- **who 非限定从句**：`Roger Milla, who did a dance by the corner flag when he scored`
- **spend time doing**：`spend inordinate amounts of time passing the ball`
- **形式主语 it**：`It is just possible that these ideas will not be adopted`

### 3. 写作技巧

1. **先立后破**：`the beautiful game` → `the boring game`——用经典美誉开头，一个转折立起全文问题（美丽 vs 无聊）
2. **家庭比喻**：`going to a dinner party and hearing the hosts rowing in the kitchen`——把三国合办世界杯比作"主人厨房吵架"，用家庭场景消解宏大赛事
3. **经济学解释足球**：`The stakes are too high ... pays off`——用"风险收益"（stakes/pays off）分析球队为何保守——经济学视角贯穿体育
4. **一本正经的荒诞**：每条建议都用正式规则语言（must be blindfolded、will be unpunishable、award a penalty）包装"脸扑救、观众上场、蒙眼角球"等离谱内容——"规则话语 + 荒诞内容"的反差制造幽默
5. **双关**：`green card`（绿牌/美国绿卡）、`the world game`（世界运动/让世界兴奋）、`the beautiful game`（美誉/反问）——一词多义制造笑点
6. **递进到极点**：门将用身体"一手、二手、最后只能用脸"——把规则从"递减"推演到"脸扑救"的荒诞高潮
7. **自嘲式论证**：`If that makes football sound boring, our point is made`——用"连角旗舞都无聊"反证"世界杯更无聊"，自嘲中强化论点
8. **逻辑推演包装荒诞**：`Competition is always a good thing ... by having two balls on the field`——用"市场经济原则"推演"两个球"，把经济学逻辑套用到球场
9. **诚实招供的笑点**：`mainly be fun because of the effect that it will have on referees and commentators`——坦白"建议是为了看裁判抓狂"，诚实本身就是笑点
10. **英式幽默收束**：`It is just possible that these ideas will not be adopted ... It could be six and a half weeks of golf`——自嘲"建议不会被采纳"，再用"六周半高尔夫"反衬"世界杯还行"——自我拆穿 + 反向安慰
