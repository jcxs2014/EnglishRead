## 第一段（manosphere 悖论开场）

> **原句 1:** CHINA HAS no direct translation of the word "manosphere" but it is plagued by one nonetheless—indeed, the abuse of women on social media is every bit as poisonous there as it is in democracies.

**中文理解：** 中文里没有"manosphere"这个词的直接译法，但中国照样深受其害——事实上，社交媒体上对女性的谩骂，在中国与在民主国家的毒性不相上下。

**句子结构：** `CHINA HAS no direct translation ... but it is plagued by one nonetheless`（转折并列，one 回指 manosphere）+ 破折号 + `indeed, the abuse of women ... is every bit as poisonous ... as it is in democracies`（同位语式补充，`every bit as ... as` 同级比较）。

**关键词：**
- `plagued by` ⭐⭐⭐ — 深受……之苦（plague = 瘟疫，作动词"折磨"）。例句：*The region is plagued by drought.*
- `nonetheless` ⭐⭐⭐ — 尽管如此（书面转折）。例句：*It was raining; nonetheless, we set off.*
- `every bit as ... as` ⭐⭐⭐ — 完全一样地……（同级比较加强）。例句：*She is every bit as talented as her brother.*
- `poisonous` ⭐⭐ — 有毒的、恶毒的（比喻网络谩骂）。

**为什么这样写：** 开头先借"翻译缺失"这个语言事实（呼应同刊另一篇 China manosphere），再用 `every bit as poisonous as it is in democracies` 立刻拉平中国与西方——中国的厌女毒性与民主国家一样严重，为全篇"中国并不例外"的框架立标。

> **原句 2:** The difference is that, in a country where censors rush to silence posts they dislike, the authorities tolerate the manosphere's vitriol and instead focus their energy on women. The cyberspace regulator's crackdown on harmful content specifically lists "extreme feminism", including posts that promote singledom.

**中文理解：** 区别在于：在一个审查者急于封禁其不喜欢的帖子的国家里，当局却容忍 manosphere 的恶毒谩骂，反而把精力集中到女性身上。网络空间监管者对有害内容的整治，专门把"极端女权主义"列为对象，包括宣扬单身的帖子。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `The difference is that, in a country where censors rush to silence posts they dislike, the authorities tolerate ... and instead focus their energy on women`（that 表语从句 + where 定语从句 + 转折并列）。第二句 `The cyberspace regulator's crackdown ... specifically lists "extreme feminism", including posts that promote singledom`（现在分词 including 作补充）。

**关键词：**
- `censors` ⭐⭐ — 审查者（censor 名词）。
- `rush to silence` ⭐⭐⭐ — 急于封禁/压制（rush to = 急于做）。例句：*The firm rushed to silence the whistle-blower.*
- `tolerate` ⭐⭐ — 容忍、纵容。
- `vitriol` ⭐⭐⭐ — 尖酸刻薄、恶毒（本义"硫酸"，喻"刻薄言辞"）。例句：*The debate descended into vitriol.*
- `crackdown on` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对……的打击/整治。例句：*The government launched a crackdown on tax evasion.*
- `singledom` ⭐⭐⭐ — 单身状态（single + -dom，比 singlehood 更常用）。例句：*The state frowns on singledom.*

**为什么这样写：** 这是全篇的"支点句"——把中国网络治理的双重标准压缩进一个对照：审查者急不可耐地封禁（rush to silence）却对厌女谩骂网开一面（tolerate），还专门打击"极端女权主义"。`specifically lists "extreme feminism"` 引号内的"极端女权"带着讽刺——官方连"鼓励单身"都算有害内容。一句话立起"选择性审查"的批评框架。

**段落逻辑：** 现象（1：与西方同样毒）→ 悖论（2：审查却不罚厌女）。全段用"双标"定调。

## 第二段（女性被边缘化的制度现实）

> **原句 3:** This is just one symptom of a much broader problem that China's old, male rulers have with the opposite sex. Unlike Asia's other two big powers, Japan and India, China has never been led by a woman in modern times.

**中文理解：** 这只是中国年迈的男性统治者与异性之间一个更广泛问题的症状之一。与亚洲另外两大国日本和印度不同，中国在现代史上从未由女性领导过。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `This is just one symptom of a much broader problem that China's old, male rulers have with the opposite sex`（that 定语从句）。第二句 `Unlike Asia's other two big powers, Japan and India, China has never been led by a woman in modern times`（Unlike 介词短语提前 + 同位语）。

**关键词：**
- `one symptom of a much broader problem` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更广泛问题的症状之一（症状隐喻：表面现象 vs 深层病因）。例句：*The protests are a symptom of a broader malaise.*
- `the opposite sex` ⭐⭐ — 异性。
- `Unlike` ⭐⭐⭐ — 与……不同（对比连接词）。例句：*Unlike its rivals, the firm grew.*
- `in modern times` ⭐⭐ — 在现代（历史分期）。

**为什么这样写：** `symptom` 一词把 manosphere 现象降级为"表象"，暗示要挖"更广的问题"。`old, male rulers` 三个词（old + male + rulers）为后文"年老男性统治层"的批评埋伏笔。用日本、印度作对照——两国都有女性总理/总统，中国从未有过——用邻国事实凸显中国异常。

> **原句 4:** As China's leader, Xi Jinping, has consolidated power, he has also sidelined women. The powerful Politburo, which usually has 24 members, has been exclusively male since 2022. No woman has ever made it to the more important Politburo Standing Committee.

**中文理解：** 随着中国领导人习近平集权，他也把女性边缘化了。权力很大的政治局通常有 24 名成员，自 2022 年以来清一色为男性。从未有女性进入更重要的政治局常委会。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `As ... Xi Jinping has consolidated power, he has also sidelined women`（As 时间/伴随状语从句）。第二句 `The powerful Politburo, which usually has 24 members, has been exclusively male since 2022`（非限制性定语从句 + 现在完成时）。第三句 `No woman has ever made it to the more important Politburo Standing Committee`。

**关键词：**
- `consolidated power` ⭐⭐⭐ — 集权、巩固权力（consolidate = 巩固）。例句：*The general consolidated his power over the army.*
- `sidelined` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被边缘化、靠边站（sports 术语：被换下板凳）。例句：*She was sidelined from key decisions.*
- `exclusively male` ⭐⭐ — 清一色男性。
- `made it to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 成功进入、达到（非正式短语）。例句：*Few women made it to the executive suite.*

**为什么这样写：** `As ... has consolidated power, he has also sidelined women` 用"As...也..."的伴随关系暗示"集权与边缘化女性同步发生"——作者不作评判，只并置两个事实让读者自己关联。`exclusively male since 2022` 与 `No woman has ever made it` 用"现在 + 从未"两个时态维度强化女性缺席的彻底性。

> **原句 5:** No wonder China has been tumbling down international rankings of gender equality.

**中文理解：** 难怪中国在性别平等的国际排名中一路下滑。

**句子结构：** `No wonder`（省略句，= It is no wonder）+ 主句 `China has been tumbling down international rankings of gender equality`。

**关键词：**
- `No wonder` ⭐⭐⭐ — 难怪（口语/书面通用的推论短句）。例句：*No wonder sales fell—the product was recalled.*
- `tumbling down` ⭐⭐⭐ — 一路下滑（tumble = 翻滚跌落，比 decline 更剧烈）。例句：*Ratings tumbled after the scandal.*
- `international rankings of gender equality` ⭐⭐ — 性别平等国际排名（如世界经济论坛全球性别差距报告）。

**为什么这样写：** `No wonder` 用推理短句把前文的制度事实（政治局全男）与结果（排名下滑）接上——一个两词短句完成"因→果"的推理，是英语高效的逻辑压缩。`tumbling` 比 falling 更有"摔落"的动态感，暗示排名下跌不是缓慢下降而是断崖。

**段落逻辑：** 症状（3：不只是 manosphere）→ 制度事实（4：女性被边缘化）→ 后果（5：排名下滑）。

## 第三段（核心错误：说教压制而非倾听）

> **原句 6:** At the heart of Communist Party policies towards women is a crushing error. The party is worried about the country's demography. But it imagines the solution lies in browbeating women and telling them, in traditional tones, to be "virtuous wives and good mothers".

**中文理解：** 共产党对女性政策的核心是一个毁灭性的错误。党担心国家的人口结构。但它却想象解决方案在于训斥女性，并用传统的腔调告诉她们要做"贤妻良母"。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `At the heart of ... is a crushing error`（介词短语提前的倒装：主语 a crushing error 后置）。第二句简单句 `The party is worried about the country's demography`。第三句 `But it imagines the solution lies in browbeating women and telling them, in traditional tones, to be "virtuous wives and good mothers"`（宾语从句 + 两个动名词并列 + 插入语）。

**关键词：**
- `At the heart of ... is` ⭐⭐⭐ — ……的核心是（倒装强调）。例句：*At the heart of the dispute is a question of ownership.*
- `crushing error` ⭐⭐⭐ — 毁灭性/粉碎性错误（crushing = 压碎性的）。例句：*The decision was a crushing error of judgement.*
- `demography` ⭐⭐ — 人口结构/人口学。
- `browbeating` ⭐⭐⭐ — 威吓、训斥、高压说教（brow 眉毛 + beat，喻"皱眉压制"）。例句：*Don't browbeat your employees into submission.*
- `virtuous wives and good mothers` ⭐⭐ — 贤妻良母（传统性别角色话语）。

**为什么这样写：** `At the heart ... is a crushing error` 倒装把"crushing error"推上句首作结论——开门见山宣判。`browbeating` 是全篇最锋利的动词：党的方案不是政策而是"训斥"。`in traditional tones` 插入语点出"腔调"的过时性；`"virtuous wives and good mothers"` 引导内引用官方话语，讽刺其陈腐。

> **原句 7:** The result is an approach to women that will not only fail to realise the party's narrow aims, but will also make countless women miserable.

**中文理解：** 其结果是：这种对待女性的方式，不仅无法实现党狭隘的目标，还会让无数女性痛苦。

**句子结构：** 主句 `The result is an approach to women` + `that will not only fail to realise the party's narrow aims, but will also make countless women miserable`（定语从句 + not only...but also 并列）。

**关键词：**
- `approach to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对待……的方式（approach + to 搭配）。例句：*The firm took a new approach to customer service.*
- `narrow aims` ⭐⭐⭐ — 狭隘的目标（narrow 与 broader problem 呼应——党只看人口目标，不看女性福祉）。例句：*A narrow focus misses the bigger picture.*
- `make countless women miserable` ⭐⭐ — 让无数女性痛苦（make + 宾语 + 形容词补语）。

**为什么这样写：** `not only fail to realise the party's narrow aims, but will also make countless women miserable` 用双重否定结构论证"政策双输"：既达不到党自己的人口目标，又让女性受苦。`narrow aims` 是刻意的贬义词——把"人口目标"定性为"狭隘"。这句是全段的总结论，一句概括"错误政策"的全部后果。

**段落逻辑：** 判定（6a：crushing error）→ 病因（6b-6c：人口焦虑 + browbeating）→ 后果（7：双输）。

## 第四段（一孩政策后果）

> **原句 8:** Mao Zedong famously claimed that women hold up "half the sky", but you would never know it today. The party's disastrous one-child policy, in place until 2015, led to millions of female fetuses being aborted.

**中文理解：** 毛泽东曾有名言：妇女能顶"半边天"，但如今你根本看不出来。党灾难性的独生子女政策（2015 年才废止）导致了数以百万计的女性胎儿被流产。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Mao Zedong famously claimed that women hold up "half the sky", but you would never know it today`（转折并列）。第二句 `The party's disastrous one-child policy, in place until 2015, led to millions of female fetuses being aborted`（同位语式插入 + lead to + 动名词）。

**关键词：**
- `famously claimed` ⭐⭐ — 有名地宣称（famously 强调"众所周知"）。
- `hold up "half the sky"` ⭐⭐⭐ — 顶起"半边天"（毛语录，中国的女性赋权口号）。
- `you would never know it today` ⭐⭐ — 你今天根本看不出来（would 表推测，间接批评现状与口号脱节）。
- `disastrous` ⭐⭐ — 灾难性的。
- `led to ... being aborted` ⭐⭐⭐ — 导致……被流产（lead to + 动名词被动）。例句：*The conflict led to thousands being displaced.*
- `female fetuses` ⭐⭐ — 女性胎儿。

**为什么这样写：** 用"半边天"口号与现实的反差做全段的第一记重锤——`famously claimed` 与 `you would never know it today` 的落差，是"口号 vs 现实"的讽刺结构。`disastrous` 直接给一孩政策定性；`millions of female fetuses being aborted` 用被动动名词把"政策导致堕胎"的责任明确指向政策本身。

> **原句 9:** The inevitable result is that millions of women are now missing. Their absence leads to other terrible outcomes. Counting men aged 23-37 and women aged 22-36, China has 22.5m more men than women.

**中文理解：** 不可避免的结果是：如今有数百万女性"缺失"。她们的不在场导致了其他可怕的后果。按 23-37 岁男性和 22-36 岁女性计算，中国男性比女性多出 2250 万。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `The inevitable result is that millions of women are now missing`（表语从句）。第二句 `Their absence leads to other terrible outcomes`。第三句 `Counting men aged 23-37 and women aged 22-36`（现在分词条件状语）+ 主句 `China has 22.5m more men than women`。

**关键词：**
- `The inevitable result is that` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不可避免的结果是（因果推理句式）。例句：*The inevitable result is that wages stagnate.*
- `missing` ⭐⭐ — 缺失的（此处"缺席/不存在"的委婉强说法）。
- `lead to other terrible outcomes` ⭐⭐ — 导致其他可怕后果。
- `Counting A and B` ⭐⭐⭐ — 若把 A 和 B 算进来（现在分词表"按……计算"）。例句：*Counting overtime, she works 60 hours a week.*

**为什么这样写：** `millions of women are now missing` 用"missing"（失踪）这个本用于人的词制造冷峻冲击——政策直接"消灭"了一代人。`Counting men aged 23-37 and women aged 22-36` 用具体年龄区间交代统计口径，保持数据严谨。

> **原句 10:** To be a catch, a man needs an education, a solid job, a flat and, in many regions, to pay a hefty fee to his bride's family. Around a third of young male migrants who intend to marry think they have only a 50% or lower chance of doing so by the age of 30.

**中文理解：** 要想成为一个"抢手货"，男人需要有学历、稳定的工作、一套房，并且——在很多地区——还要给新娘家付一笔可观的彩礼。大约三分之一的、有意结婚的年轻男性外来务工者认为，自己到 30 岁能成婚的几率只有 50% 或更低。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `To be a catch, a man needs an education, a solid job, a flat and, in many regions, to pay a hefty fee to his bride's family`（目的状语 + 并列宾语）。第二句 `Around a third of young male migrants who intend to marry think they have only a 50% or lower chance of doing so by the age of 30`（定语从句 + 宾语从句）。

**关键词：**
- `a catch` ⭐⭐⭐ — 抢手货、理想对象（口语俚语）。例句：*He's quite a catch.*
- `a solid job` ⭐⭐ — 稳定的工作。
- `a hefty fee` ⭐⭐ — 一笔可观的费用（hefty = 分量重的）。
- `migrants` ⭐⭐ — 外来务工者（此处指农民工）。
- `a 50% or lower chance of doing so` ⭐⭐ — 只有 50% 或更低的成功几率。

**为什么这样写：** `a catch` 用婚恋俚语把"男性婚配市场"具象化——要当"抢手货"需要学历/工作/房/彩礼四件套。`only a 50% or lower chance` 用概率量化男性困境。整个段落把"女性缺失"的宏观数字落地到"个体男性的婚配焦虑"。

**段落逻辑：** 口号反差（8a）→ 政策后果（8b 堕胎）→ 数量化（9：2250 万）→ 个体化（10：结婚条件）。

## 第五段（怨恨蔓延）

> **原句 11:** As a result, resentments abound: many men, especially rural ones, are chauvinists. The sense of bitterness is likely to spread. Women make up more than half of those in higher education; men who lack education or economic prospects risk falling ever further behind.

**中文理解：** 结果是怨恨四起：许多男性，尤其是农村男性，是大男子主义者。这种苦涩感很可能会蔓延。女性占了高等教育学生的半数以上；缺乏学历或经济前景的男性，则可能被甩得越来越远。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `As a result, resentments abound: many men, especially rural ones, are chauvinists`（冒号引出解释）。第二句 `The sense of bitterness is likely to spread`。第三句 `Women make up more than half of those in higher education; men who lack education or economic prospects risk falling ever further behind`（分号并列 + 定语从句）。

**关键词：**
- `resentments abound` ⭐⭐⭐ — 怨恨四起（abound = 大量存在）。例句：*Conspiracy theories abound online.*
- `chauvinists` ⭐⭐ — 大男子主义者（chauvinism 沙文主义）。
- `The sense of bitterness` ⭐⭐ — 苦涩感/怨气。
- `make up more than half` ⭐⭐ — 占一半以上（make up = 构成）。
- `falling ever further behind` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被甩得越来越远（fall behind = 落后，ever 加强）。例句：*The region risks falling further behind the coast.*

**为什么这样写：** `resentments abound` 用冒号引出一个"诊断"（农村男性大男子主义），再给出结构原因：女性教育反超男性。`falling ever further behind` 的"ever further"暗示"差距自我扩大"——男性在婚恋市场的劣势会随着教育差距恶化，形成负反馈。

**段落逻辑：** 现状（11a：怨恨）→ 蔓延风险（11b）→ 结构性根源（11c：教育差距）。

## 第六段（转折：可补救）

> **原句 12:** The Chinese government cannot suddenly conjure up millions of missing women to alleviate the situation. But it could introduce policies that boost the marriage rate among Chinese women while improving the choices available to them.

**中文理解：** 中国政府无法突然"变出"数百万缺失的女性来缓解局面。但它可以推出一些政策，在提升中国女性结婚率的同时，改善她们可获得的选项。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `The Chinese government cannot suddenly conjure up millions of missing women to alleviate the situation`。第二句 `But it could introduce policies that boost the marriage rate among Chinese women while improving the choices available to them`（that 定语从句 + while 现在分词）。

**关键词：**
- `conjure up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 变魔术般凭空变出（conjure = 施魔法变出）。例句：*No one can conjure up new talent overnight.*
- `alleviate` ⭐⭐⭐ — 缓解、减轻（比 ease 正式）。例句：*The aid alleviated the suffering.*
- `boost the marriage rate` ⭐⭐ — 提升结婚率。
- `while improving the choices available to them` ⭐⭐ — 同时改善她们可获得的选项（while + 现在分词表同时）。

**为什么这样写：** `conjure up` 用"变魔术"意象点破人口问题的不可逆性（缺失的女性变不回来）——先否定不现实的期待，再转向"可行的政策"（But it could introduce policies）。`boost the marriage rate ... while improving the choices available to them` 是作者的核心主张：提高结婚率必须以"改善女性选择"为途径，而非靠压榨。

**段落逻辑：** 否定幻想（12a）→ 提出可行方向（12b）。全篇从"诊断"转向"药方"的转折点。

## 第七段（建议 1：婚姻内权利）

> **原句 13:** One idea is to ensure that women have proper rights within marriages. The enforcement of laws on harassment, marital rape and domestic violence is woefully inadequate.

**中文理解：** 一个想法是确保女性在婚姻中享有应有的权利。关于骚扰、婚内强奸和家庭暴力的法律执行，糟糕地严重不足。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `One idea is to ensure that women have proper rights within marriages`（表语不定式 + that 宾语从句）。第二句 `The enforcement of laws on harassment, marital rape and domestic violence is woefully inadequate`。

**关键词：**
- `proper rights` ⭐⭐ — 应有的权利。
- `enforcement` ⭐⭐⭐ — 执行、执法（法律术语）。例句：*Weak enforcement undermines the law.*
- `harassment` ⭐⭐ — 骚扰。
- `marital rape` ⭐⭐ — 婚内强奸（婚姻关系内的强奸）。
- `domestic violence` ⭐⭐ — 家庭暴力。
- `woefully inadequate` ⭐⭐⭐ — 糟糕地严重不足（woefully = 令人痛心地）。例句：*The response was woefully inadequate.*

**为什么这样写：** `woefully inadequate` 用两个词（woefully + inadequate）把"执法不力"的严重性推到顶点。作者列出的三项（骚扰/婚内强奸/家暴）恰好呼应前文 manosphere 议题——网上的厌女言论正是现实中女性权利不彰的折射。

> **原句 14:** More might marry if they were assured of fair treatment in cases of divorce, including payment of child support. If it were easier for wives to divorce bad husbands, more might remarry.

**中文理解：** 如果女性在离婚案件中能确保获得公平对待（包括子女抚养费的支付），愿意结婚的人可能更多。如果妻子更容易与坏丈夫离婚，更多人可能再婚。

**句子结构：** 两句皆含虚拟语气。第一句 `More might marry if they were assured of fair treatment in cases of divorce`（if + were 虚拟现在）。第二句 `If it were easier for wives to divorce bad husbands, more might remarry`（if + were 虚拟）。

**关键词：**
- `were assured of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被确保获得（assure A of B = 向……保证）。例句：*Investors were assured of a fair return.*
- `child support` ⭐⭐ — 子女抚养费。
- `bad husbands` ⭐⭐ — 糟糕的丈夫（口语化，刻意朴素）。
- `might marry` / `might remarry` ⭐⭐ — 可能结婚/再婚（might 表"潜在可能性"）。

**为什么这样写：** 两句都用虚拟语气（if ... were / might）表达"条件性后果"——作者在描述"如果制度更公平会怎样"的理想场景，而非断言现实。`more might marry ... more might remarry` 的对称结构（marry/remarry 押韵式对仗）把"结婚率"与"离婚公平"直接挂钩——直觉上反直觉（离婚公平→结婚率上升），正是作者的论证亮点。

**段落逻辑：** 原则（13a）→ 现状（13b 执法不力）→ 假设（14：公平离婚 → 结婚/再婚率上升）。

## 第八段（建议 2：离婚与彩礼）

> **原句 15:** Fairer divorce settlements might also hasten the end of bride prices. Research suggests a young male migrant must save for six years to afford a bride price of 127,300 yuan ($18,780).

**中文理解：** 更公平的离婚清算，可能也会加速彩礼制度的终结。研究显示，一位年轻的男性外来务工者必须储蓄六年，才付得起 12.73 万元（合 1.878 万美元）的彩礼。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Fairer divorce settlements might also hasten the end of bride prices`。第二句 `Research suggests a young male migrant must save for six years to afford a bride price of 127,300 yuan ($18,780)`（宾语从句）。

**关键词：**
- `settlements` ⭐⭐ — 清算、和解（法律术语，离婚财产分割）。
- `hasten the end of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 加速……的终结（hasten = 加速）。例句：*The scandal hastened the end of his career.*
- `bride prices` ⭐⭐ — 彩礼（bride price 人类学/社会学术语）。
- `save for six years to afford` ⭐⭐ — 储蓄六年才买得起（afford = 负担得起）。

**为什么这样写：** 作者提出一个"因果链"：离婚更公平 → 女性不必靠彩礼托底 → 彩礼制度退潮。用六年储蓄的数据（127,300 元）把彩礼负担量化，让"为什么彩礼该废"有了具体依据。

> **原句 16:** A law bans extortionate bride prices already, but it is ambiguous and poorly enforced. Many parents still set great store by the price a daughter can command; the desperation of men to find a partner means that those with money will pay; female divorcees sometimes retain a chunk of it if marriages fail.

**中文理解：** 法律已经禁止漫天要价的彩礼，但这条法律含糊且执行不力。许多父母仍然十分看重女儿能"要价"多少；男性找伴侣的绝望意味着有钱的人会付；女性离异者有时在婚姻失败后仍能保有其中一部分。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `A law bans extortionate bride prices already, but it is ambiguous and poorly enforced`（转折并列）。第二句三部分分号并列：`Many parents still set great store by the price a daughter can command`（定语从句省略 that）+ `the desperation of men ... means that those with money will pay`（宾语从句）+ `female divorcees sometimes retain a chunk of it if marriages fail`。

**关键词：**
- `extortionate` ⭐⭐⭐ — 漫天要价的、敲诈性的（extort 敲诈）。例句：*The prices at the resort were extortionate.*
- `ambiguous` ⭐⭐ — 含糊不清的。
- `set great store by` ⭐⭐⭐ — 十分看重（store = 重视）。例句：*She sets great store by punctuality.*
- `a daughter can command` ⭐⭐ — 女儿能"要价"的（command = 开价、拥有）。
- `retain a chunk of it` ⭐⭐ — 保留其中一部分（chunk = 大块）。

**为什么这样写：** 三个分号并列呈现彩礼的"多方博弈"：父母看价、男性绝望付钱、离婚女性留钱——用并列句呈现复杂现实，说明"禁止"解决不了结构性需求。`extortionate` 一词承认部分彩礼的敲诈性，但 `a daughter can command` 又暗示其背后的家庭经济逻辑，为"更公平离婚→彩礼退潮"的渐进方案铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** 主张（15a：离婚公平终结彩礼）→ 数据（15b：六年储蓄）→ 现实阻碍（16：法律含糊 + 多方博弈）。

## 第九段（建议 3：住房与落户）

> **原句 17:** More generally, the government could lower the hurdles to home ownership and work that stand in the way of couples. It should buy more of China's vast tracts of unsold housing, converting it into affordable rental homes favoured by young families.

**中文理解：** 更广泛地说，政府可以降低挡在情侣/夫妻面前的购房与就业门槛。它应该收购中国大量未售出的住房，将其改造成受年轻家庭欢迎的平价租赁住房。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `the government could lower the hurdles to home ownership and work that stand in the way of couples`（that 定语从句）。第二句 `It should buy more of China's vast tracts of unsold housing, converting it into affordable rental homes favoured by young families`（现在分词作结果状语 + 过去分词作定语）。

**关键词：**
- `lower the hurdles` ⭐⭐⭐ — 降低门槛/障碍（hurdle = 跨栏，喻障碍）。例句：*The reform lowered the hurdles to entrepreneurship.*
- `stand in the way of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 挡在……的路上、阻碍。例句：*Lack of capital stands in the way of expansion.*
- `vast tracts of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 大片大片的（tract = 大片土地）。例句：*Vast tracts of farmland lie fallow.*
- `unsold housing` ⭐⭐ — 未售出的住房（中国房地产库存）。
- `favoured by young families` ⭐⭐ — 受年轻家庭欢迎的（favour = 青睐）。

**为什么这样写：** `lower the hurdles ... that stand in the way` 用跨栏隐喻把"住房/就业"比作婚姻路上的障碍物。`vast tracts of unsold housing` 点出中国的房地产库存问题，`converting it into affordable rental homes` 给出处置方案——作者关注的是"政策可操作性"，用市场手段（收购存量房）解决婚姻成本问题。

> **原句 18:** Recent relaxations in the hukou system of internal passports, which make it easier for newcomers to register themselves and their families for schools and other public services in cities, are welcome.

**中文理解：** 最近对户籍（内部护照）体系的放宽——这让新来者更容易为本人及家人在城市注册学校和其他公共服务——值得欢迎。

**句子结构：** 主句 `Recent relaxations in the hukou system ... are welcome` + 插入语 `of internal passports` + `which make it easier for newcomers to register themselves and their families for schools and other public services in cities`（非限制性定语从句，主语为复数 relaxations）。

**关键词：**
- `relaxations` ⭐⭐ — 放宽（政策/法律的放松）。
- `hukou system` ⭐⭐⭐ — 户籍制度（拼音音译词，中国的内部护照体系）。
- `internal passports` ⭐⭐ — 内部护照（对户籍制度的英文解释）。
- `make it easier for ... to ...` ⭐⭐⭐ — 使……更容易。例句：*The new app makes it easier for users to book tickets.*
- `are welcome` ⭐⭐ — 值得欢迎的。

**为什么这样写：** `hukou system of internal passports` 用"内部护照"解释户籍制度给外国读者——翻译式同位语是 Economist 处理中国特色词汇的惯例。`which make it easier for newcomers to register themselves and their families for schools` 的长定语从句交代放宽的具体内容，`are welcome` 以"欢迎"表态——作者在批评之余不吝肯定进步，保持论述公正。

**段落逻辑：** 原则（17a：降门槛）→ 具体（17b：收购存量房）→ 肯定进步（18：户籍放宽）。

## 第十段（收尾：去问女人）

> **原句 19:** Traditionalist men, including those at the top of the Communist Party, may see some of these ideas as foreign impositions. If they stopped chiding Chinese women and tried asking them for their opinions, they might hear a different story. ■

**中文理解：** 传统主义男性——包括中共高层那些人——可能把其中一些想法视为外来的强加。如果他们停止训斥中国女性，转而试着征求她们的意见，他们可能会听到一个不一样的故事。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Traditionalist men, including those at the top of the Communist Party, may see some of these ideas as foreign impositions`（同位语插入 + see A as B）。第二句 `If they stopped chiding Chinese women and tried asking them for their opinions, they might hear a different story`（虚拟语气：if + stopped/tried，主句 might）。

**关键词：**
- `Traditionalist` ⭐⭐ — 传统主义的、守旧的。
- `foreign impositions` ⭐⭐⭐ — 外来的强加（imposition = 强加之物）。例句：*Critics called the new rules a foreign imposition.*
- `chiding` ⭐⭐⭐ — 责备、训斥（chide = 温和的责怪）。例句：*She chided him for being late.*
- `asking them for their opinions` ⭐⭐ — 征求她们的意见（ask A for B 搭配）。
- `hear a different story` ⭐⭐ — 听到一个不一样的故事（习语：另一面说法）。

**为什么这样写：** 结尾用"对话"替代"训斥"作最终主张：`stopped chiding ... and tried asking them for their opinions` 把全文的"browbeating"批评收束为一个简单行动——去问女性。虚拟语气（stopped/tried/might）保留"他们不会这么做的"的现实暗讽，但又指向可行出路。`hear a different story` 用"故事"收尾，暗示政策制定者只听到自己想听的，没听女性的版本。

**段落逻辑：** 预判阻力（19a：被斥为"外来强加"）→ 主张对话（19b：去问女性）。

## 长难句专项

### 🔍 原句 2（表语从句 + 多重从句嵌套）

> **原句:** The difference is that, in a country where censors rush to silence posts they dislike, the authorities tolerate the manosphere's vitriol and instead focus their energy on women.

**① 找主干：** The difference is that ...
**② 找修饰：** in a country where ...（地点状语 + where 定语从句）；they dislike（定语从句省略 that）
**③ 找从句：** that 表语从句（含两个并列谓语 tolerate ... and focus ...）；where censors rush ...；they dislike
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 中国与西方的差异是：这个能封帖子的国家，却容忍厌女谩骂、专打女性。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 全篇支点句——把"选择性审查"的双重标准压缩进一个表语从句。

### 🔍 原句 16（三部分分号并列）

> **原句:** Many parents still set great store by the price a daughter can command; the desperation of men to find a partner means that those with money will pay; female divorcees sometimes retain a chunk of it if marriages fail.

**① 找主干：** 三个并列分句：parents set store by price；desperation means that ...；divorcees retain a chunk
**② 找修饰：** a daughter can command（price 的定语从句）；those with money（介词短语定语）
**③ 找从句：** a daughter can command；that those with money will pay；if marriages fail
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 彩礼的多方博弈：父母定价、男性付钱、离异女性留钱——三方都在维系这个制度。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 用分号并列展示彩礼的"结构性"，解释为何单纯禁止无效。

### 🔍 原句 18（长插入语 + 非限制性定语从句）

> **原句:** Recent relaxations in the hukou system of internal passports, which make it easier for newcomers to register themselves and their families for schools and other public services in cities, are welcome.

**① 找主干：** Recent relaxations ... are welcome
**② 找修饰：** in the hukou system of internal passports（定语）；which make it easier ... in cities（非限制性定语从句）
**③ 找从句：** which make it easier for newcomers to register ... for schools and other public services
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 户籍放宽（内容由 which 从句详述）值得欢迎。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 主语与谓语（are welcome）被一个长插入语分隔——"主谓分隔"是英语书面语常见修辞。
