## 第一段（岳阳：从塔到街）

> **原句 1:** YUEYANG, A CITY in Hunan province, is famous for its tower, an elegant three-storey pavilion immortalised in millennium-old prose still memorised by schoolchildren. But these days it is the streets around the tower that matter more, at least for those concerned with Chinese governance.

**中文理解：** 岳阳，湖南省的一座城市，以它的塔而闻名——一座优雅的三层楼阁，被千年古文中不朽地铭记，学童们至今仍在背诵。但如今，对关心中国治理的人来说，更重要的是塔周围的街道。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `YUEYANG, A CITY in Hunan province, is famous for its tower, an elegant three-storey pavilion immortalised in millennium-old prose still memorised by schoolchildren`（同位语 + 过去分词链：immortalised in prose + memorised by schoolchildren）。第二句 `But these days it is the streets around the tower that matter more, at least for those concerned with Chinese governance`（it is ... that 强调句）。

**关键词：**
- `is famous for` ⭐⭐ — 以……闻名。
- `immortalised in` ⭐⭐⭐ — 在……中被永久铭记（immortalise = 使不朽）。例句：*The battle was immortalised in verse.*
- `millennium-old prose` ⭐⭐ — 千年古散文（指《岳阳楼记》）。
- `it is ... that matter more` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更重要的是……（强调句）。例句：*It is the details that matter.*
- `concerned with` ⭐⭐ — 关心、涉及。

**为什么这样写：** 开篇用"岳阳楼"的千年文化符号引入（《岳阳楼记》每个中国学生都会背），再以 `it is the streets around the tower that matter more` 的强调句转折——"文化名楼"让步给"治理新街"。这种"熟悉事物 → 意外转折"的开头是专栏写作的经典手法，让国际读者先看到一个亲切的文化锚点。

> **原句 2:** They feature an ambitious project to revitalise the city. More than that, they represent a new turn in China's economic management, as officials try to ease fiscal straits by squeezing more out of state assets.

**中文理解：** 这些街道上有一个雄心勃勃的城市振兴项目。更重要的是，它们代表着中国经济管理的新转向——官员们试图通过从国有资产中榨取更多来缓解财政困境。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `They feature an ambitious project to revitalise the city`。第二句 `More than that, they represent a new turn in China's economic management, as officials try to ease fiscal straits by squeezing more out of state assets`（as 原因状语 + by 方式状语）。

**关键词：**
- `feature` ⭐⭐⭐ — 以……为特色、容纳（此处作动词：街道上呈现着一个项目）。例句：*The exhibition features local artists.*
- `revitalise` ⭐⭐⭐ — 振兴、使恢复活力（vital = 生命）。例句：*The plan aims to revitalise the old district.*
- `More than that` ⭐⭐ — 更重要的是、不仅如此（递进）。
- `ease fiscal straits` ⭐⭐⭐ — 缓解财政困境（straits = 困境，常用复数）。例句：*The bailout eased the firm's financial straits.*
- `squeezing more out of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 从……榨取更多（squeeze out of = 挤出）。例句：*They squeezed more value out of old assets.*
- `state assets` ⭐⭐ — 国有资产。

**为什么这样写：** 标题动词 `wring more from` 在此首次以 `squeezing more out of` 变体出现——"拧干"与"榨出"同义呼应，都是"用力挤"的意象。`More than that` 的递进把报道从"一个项目"抬升到"经济管理新转向"，快速亮出全篇论点。`ease fiscal straits` 交代动机（财政困顿）。

**段落逻辑：** 文化引子（1）→ 治理新街（2a）→ 论点立起（2b）。从具体到抽象的"漏斗式"开篇。

## 第二段（街道改造的物理变化）

> **原句 3:** The physical transformation is stunning. The area covers three square kilometres—roughly the size of New York's Central Park—and until recently featured dilapidated buildings, messy markets and a run-down harbour.

**中文理解：** 物理层面的转变令人惊叹。该区域占地三平方公里——大约相当于纽约中央公园的大小——直到最近还呈现着破败的建筑、混乱的市场和年久失修的港口。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `The physical transformation is stunning`（短句断言）。第二句 `The area covers three square kilometres—roughly the size of New York's Central Park—and until recently featured dilapidated buildings, messy markets and a run-down harbour`（破折号插入 + 三词并列 + until recently 时间对比）。

**关键词：**
- `transformation is stunning` ⭐⭐⭐ — 转变令人惊叹（stunning = 惊人的）。例句：*The renovation produced a stunning transformation.*
- `covers three square kilometres` ⭐⭐ — 占地三平方公里。
- `roughly the size of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 大约相当于……的大小（对比参照）。例句：*The park is roughly the size of Monaco.*
- `dilapidated buildings` ⭐⭐⭐ — 破败的建筑（dilapidated = 年久失修的）。例句：*Dilapidated factories dotted the town.*
- `messy markets` ⭐⭐ — 杂乱的市场。
- `a run-down harbour` ⭐⭐⭐ — 年久失修的港口（run-down = 破旧失修的）。例句：*A run-down harbour awaited redevelopment.*

**为什么这样写：** `stunning` 一词下判语（这是好事的确认）。`roughly the size of New York's Central Park` 用国际读者熟悉的参照系（中央公园）量化规模——《经济学人》给全球读者做"空间换算"的标准手法。`dilapidated ... messy ... run-down` 三个贬义形容词堆叠过去景象，与后文"pedestrian spaces / spruced up waterfront"的今貌形成强烈对比。

> **原句 4:** Crucially, these properties were almost all owned by local authorities and many lay unused. The city has converted streets into attractive pedestrian spaces, renovated the buildings and spruced up the waterfront.

**中文理解：** 关键是，这些房产几乎全部归地方政府所有，其中许多闲置未用。该市已把街道改造成宜人的步行空间，翻新了建筑，并整饰了滨水区。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Crucially, these properties were almost all owned by local authorities and many lay unused`（Crucially 副词置首 + 并列句 + lay unused 系动词结构）。第二句 `The city has converted streets into attractive pedestrian spaces, renovated the buildings and spruced up the waterfront`（三并列谓语：converted ... renovated ... spruced up）。

**关键词：**
- `Crucially` ⭐⭐⭐ — 关键是（副词引导重要信息）。例句：*Crucially, the data are missing.*
- `lay unused` ⭐⭐⭐ — 处于闲置状态（lay + 形容词 = 处于某状态）。例句：*The factory lay idle for years.*
- `converted ... into ...` ⭐⭐ — 改造成。
- `pedestrian spaces` ⭐⭐ — 步行空间。
- `spruced up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 整饰、打理（spruce up = 打扮/整修）。例句：*They spruced up the old shopfront.*

**为什么这样写：** `Crucially` 单挑出全文最关键的前提：**这些房产几乎全是政府自有、且闲置**——这是"盘活存量"模式可行的根本（政府对自己资产有权处置）。`lay unused` 的"躺平闲置"为后文"make use of it"做铺垫。三谓语并列（converted/renovated/spruced up）展示改造的完整动作链。

> **原句 5:** Restaurants, shops and hotels have opened up in the new digs, generating rent for municipal coffers. On a recent summer evening, tourists and locals flocked to outdoor tables. A local official who has helped oversee the project told Chaguan that his mission was far from accomplished: "The next big challenge is to make money."

**中文理解：** 餐馆、商店和酒店在"新居"中开张，为市政金库带来租金。在最近一个夏夜，游客和本地人涌向户外餐桌。一位参与监督该项目的当地官员告诉 Chaguan，他的使命远未完成："下一个大挑战是赚钱。"

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Restaurants, shops and hotels have opened up in the new digs, generating rent for municipal coffers`（现在分词伴随）。第二句 `On a recent summer evening, tourists and locals flocked to outdoor tables`。第三句 `A local official who has helped oversee the project told Chaguan that his mission was far from accomplished: "The next big challenge is to make money."`（who 定语从句 + told that + 冒号引语）。

**关键词：**
- `opened up` ⭐⭐ — 开张营业。
- `the new digs` ⭐⭐⭐ — 新居所/新地盘（digs = 住处，俚语）。例句：*We visited their new digs.*
- `generating rent for municipal coffers` ⭐⭐⭐ — 为市政金库创造租金（coffers = 金库）。例句：*The tax fills municipal coffers.*
- `flocked to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 涌向（flock = 成群结队）。例句：*Crowds flocked to the festival.*
- `oversee the project` ⭐⭐ — 监督项目。
- `far from accomplished` ⭐⭐⭐ — 远未完成（far from + 过去分词）。例句：*The task is far from accomplished.*
- `the next big challenge is to make money` ⭐⭐⭐ — 下一个大挑战是赚钱（引语点题：改造易、赚钱难）。

**为什么这样写：** `municipal coffers` 用"金库"这一古典意象给"租金收入"增添质感。段尾引语 `"The next big challenge is to make money"` 是全篇的"钩子"——官员亲口承认"改造完成 ≠ 赚到钱"，把"物理改造"与"商业回报"的落差摆上桌面，引出后文"盘活资产以增收"的真正议题。

**段落逻辑：** 规模（3）→ 前提与改造（4）→ 开张与官员自白（5）。从"看得见的变"到"尚未解决的赚钱问题"。

## 第三段（财政危机背景）

> **原句 6:** Cities throughout China need cash as the country's property market remains in a tailspin. Land sales accounted for about 40% of total revenues for Chinese local governments in 2021, the high-water mark for home prices nationally.

**中文理解：** 中国各地的城市都需要现金，因为该国房地产市场仍处于急剧下滑之中。2021 年土地出让约占中国地方政府总收入的 40%——那是全国房价的顶峰。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Cities throughout China need cash as the country's property market remains in a tailspin`（as 原因状语）。第二句 `Land sales accounted for about 40% of total revenues for Chinese local governments in 2021, the high-water mark for home prices nationally`（同位语 the high-water mark）。

**关键词：**
- `remains in a tailspin` ⭐⭐⭐ — 处于急剧下坠（tailspin = 螺旋式下跌）。例句：*The industry is in a tailspin.*
- `accounted for about 40% of` ⭐⭐ — 约占……的 40%。
- `total revenues` ⭐⭐ — 总收入。
- `the high-water mark` ⭐⭐⭐ — 历史最高点（高水位标记，喻峰值）。例句：*That year was the high-water mark for the market.*
- `nationally` ⭐⭐ — 全国性地。

**为什么这样写：** `remains in a tailspin` 用航空术语"尾旋"形容房地产的失控下坠——比 "declining" 更有失速感。`40%` 与 `high-water mark` 给出土地财政鼎盛期的"水位"，为后文"fallen by more than half"（跌破一半）提供基准。数据先立"高点"再跌，落差感更强。

> **原句 7:** Since then the value of those sales has fallen by more than half. That has blown a hole in local budgets, with some debt-saddled cities cutting wages for teachers, doctors and other public-sector workers.

**中文理解：** 自那以后，这些出让的价值已下跌一半以上。这给地方预算炸开了一个大洞，一些债台高筑的城市正在削减教师、医生和其他公共部门工作人员的工资。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Since then the value of those sales has fallen by more than half`（完成时 + by 数量状语）。第二句 `That has blown a hole in local budgets, with some debt-saddled cities cutting wages for ...`（with 复合结构 + debt-saddled 复合形容词）。

**关键词：**
- `has fallen by more than half` ⭐⭐ — 下跌一半以上（fall by + 幅度）。
- `blown a hole in` ⭐⭐⭐ — 在……炸开一个洞（比喻破坏预算）。例句：*The scandal blew a hole in the budget.*
- `debt-saddled` ⭐⭐⭐ — 债台高筑的（saddle = 使背负重担）。例句：*Debt-saddled cities cut services.*
- `public-sector workers` ⭐⭐ — 公共部门工作人员。

**为什么这样写：** `blown a hole in local budgets` 用"炸洞"的暴力意象描绘土地财政崩塌对预算的冲击——不温和的"缺口"，是"炸开"。`debt-saddled` 的"saddle"（马鞍）意象与 blow a hole 呼应，都在强调"负重"与"破洞"。`with ... cutting wages for teachers, doctors` 给出具体受难人群，把宏观财政危机落到个体。

> **原句 8:** Everywhere, officials have been looking for new ways to raise cash.

**中文理解：** 各地官员一直在寻找新的筹资方式。

**句子结构：** `Everywhere, officials have been looking for new ways to raise cash`（现在完成进行时 + 地点状语置首）。

**关键词：**
- `Everywhere` ⭐⭐ — 到处（地点状语置首强调普遍性）。
- `have been looking for` ⭐⭐ — 一直在寻找（完成进行时）。
- `raise cash` ⭐⭐⭐ — 筹集现金（raise = 筹措）。例句：*The city needs to raise cash quickly.*

**为什么这样写：** 一句独立的过渡句（single-sentence paragraph 的变体）——`Everywhere` 把"岳阳"个案扩展到"全国普遍现象"，`have been looking for` 的完成进行时暗示"一直在找、还没找到"。"找钱"成为全中国官员的共同任务，为下文"各种荒唐办法"做引。

**段落逻辑：** 危机（6-7）→ 普遍求钱（8）。先解释"为什么到处找钱"，再进入"找到了什么办法"。

## 第四段（荒唐的自救）

> **原句 9:** Some efforts have been almost comical. Several municipal governments engaged in "deep-sea fishing" expeditions in which local police reached across provincial lines to seize assets from entrepreneurs in wealthier provinces.

**中文理解：** 一些努力几乎到了可笑的地步。几个市政府参与了"深海捕捞"行动——当地警察跨省行动，从较富裕省份的企业家手中扣押资产。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Some efforts have been almost comical`（判断短句）。第二句 `Several municipal governments engaged in "deep-sea fishing" expeditions in which local police reached across provincial lines to seize assets from entrepreneurs`（in which 定语从句 + 不定式目的）。

**关键词：**
- `almost comical` ⭐⭐⭐ — 几乎可笑（comical = 滑稽的）。例句：*The attempts were almost comical.*
- `engaged in` ⭐⭐ — 从事。
- `"deep-sea fishing" expeditions` ⭐⭐⭐ — "深海捕捞"行动（带引号的隐喻：跨省抢资产）。例句：*The raids were called "deep-sea fishing".*
- `reached across provincial lines` ⭐⭐ — 跨过省界。
- `seize assets from` ⭐⭐ — 从……扣押资产。

**为什么这样写：** `almost comical` 一上来给"部分自救"定性为"近乎可笑"——作者对非正当手段的轻讽。`"deep-sea fishing"` 带引号的隐喻把"跨省抢钱"包装成"捕鱼"，讽刺其荒诞（本地警察去外省"捞"资产）。`in which` 定语从句展开"渔船"细节（跨省执法、扣押企业家资产）。

> **原句 10:** Many have also become more zealous in slapping fines on restaurants for hygiene violations or doling out parking tickets. Other efforts to deleverage have been more in line with textbook economics: local governments have swapped the high-interest, short-term debt of their subsidiary entities for lower-rate, longer-term official bonds.

**中文理解：** 许多地方政府也变得热衷于以卫生违规为由向餐馆开罚单，或大量开出停车罚单。其他去杠杆的努力则更符合教科书经济学：地方政府将其附属机构的高息短期债务，置换为较低利率的长期官方债券。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Many have also become more zealous in slapping fines on restaurants for hygiene violations or doling out parking tickets`（become zealous in + 并列动名词短语）。第二句 `Other efforts to deleverage have been more in line with textbook economics: local governments have swapped the high-interest, short-term debt of their subsidiary entities for lower-rate, longer-term official bonds`（冒号解释 + swapped A for B）。

**关键词：**
- `more zealous in` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更热衷于（zealous = 热心的）。例句：*They became zealous in enforcing the rules.*
- `slapping fines on` ⭐⭐⭐ — 随意开罚单（slap on = 强加）。例句：*The city slapped fines on street vendors.*
- `hygiene violations` ⭐⭐ — 卫生违规。
- `doling out parking tickets` ⭐⭐⭐ — 大量开出停车罚单（dole out = 分发）。例句：*Officers doled out tickets all day.*
- `deleverage` ⭐⭐⭐ — 去杠杆（finance 术语）。例句：*Firms deleveraged after the crisis.*
- `in line with textbook economics` ⭐⭐⭐ — 符合教科书经济学（in line with = 符合）。例句：*The policy is in line with textbook theory.*
- `swapped A for B` ⭐⭐ — 把 A 换成 B（swap = 交换）。

**为什么这样写：** 用"罚餐馆"、"开停车票"与"教科书式债转"形成对照：前者是捞偏门（slap on / dole out 的随意感），后者是正路（deleverage / swap 的规范性）。`in line with textbook economics` 的"教科书"暗示这种操作"标准、无争议"。两组形成"荒唐 vs 正规"的自救光谱，Yueyang 属于后者更高级的变体。

**段落逻辑：** 荒唐手段（9-10a）→ 规范手段（10b）。在"捞钱光谱"中为 Yueyang 定位。

## 第五段（Yueyang 思路：物尽其用）

> **原句 11:** Yueyang points to another angle of attack. Officials there are trying to get more from what they already have in their hands, especially by putting under-used assets to work.

**中文理解：** 岳阳指向了另一个进攻角度。那里的官员正努力从手中已有的东西中获取更多，尤其是通过让未充分利用的资产运转起来。

## 第七段（"Yueyang 模式"的具体项目）

> **原句 23:** But if the progress in Yueyang and elsewhere is sustained, it would support local budgets. Mr McMahon reckons that soon enough, the revitalisation campaign could boost Chinese growth.

**中文理解：** 但如果岳阳等地的进展能够持续，它将支持地方预算。McMahon 先生估计，用不了多久，振兴运动就能提振中国的增长。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `But if the progress in Yueyang and elsewhere is sustained, it would support local budgets`（if 条件 + 虚拟 would）。第二句 `Mr McMahon reckons that soon enough, the revitalisation campaign could boost Chinese growth`（reckons that + 宾语从句）。

**关键词：**
- `is sustained` ⭐⭐ — 得以持续。
- `support local budgets` ⭐⭐ — 支持地方预算。
- `reckons that` ⭐⭐⭐ — 估计、认为（reckon = 估量）。例句：*Analysts reckon that demand will rise.*
- `soon enough` ⭐⭐ — 用不了多久。
- `the revitalisation campaign` ⭐⭐ — 振兴运动。
- `boost Chinese growth` ⭐⭐ — 提振中国增长。

**为什么这样写：** `if ... is sustained, it would` 用条件句管理预期——成效的前提是"持续"（不持续就是一次性的）。`reckons that ... could boost` 用"reckon + could"双重缓和（估计 + 可能），保持"有望但未定"的谨慎乐观。

**段落逻辑：** 局限（22）→ 条件乐观（23）。承认"解不了债"，但肯定"能帮上忙"。

## 第十一段（深层意义：从粗放到集约）

> **原句 24:** The greater significance, however, may lie beyond economics. It is a reckoning with the "extensive" model of development seen in the past decade, as one Yueyang official puts it. That led to endless sprawl as cities swallowed up ever more land for development.

**中文理解：** 然而，更重大的意义可能超出经济层面。正如一位岳阳官员所说，这是与过去十年"粗放"式发展模式的一次清算。那种模式导致城市为发展而不断吞并更多土地，造成无休止的蔓延。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `The greater significance, however, may lie beyond economics`（however 插入 + lie beyond）。第二句 `It is a reckoning with the "extensive" model of development seen in the past decade, as one Yueyang official puts it`（reckoning with + 过去分词定语 + as 引述）。第三句 `That led to endless sprawl as cities swallowed up ever more land for development`（as 原因状语 + swallowed up）。

**关键词：**
- `The greater significance` ⭐⭐ — 更重大的意义。
- `lie beyond economics` ⭐⭐⭐ — 超出经济层面（lie beyond = 在……之外）。例句：*The issue lies beyond money.*
- `a reckoning with` ⭐⭐⭐ — 与……的清算（reckoning = 结算、清算）。例句：*The crisis was a reckoning with past excess.*
- `the "extensive" model` ⭐⭐⭐ — "粗放"模式（extensive = 外延式，引号点出官方话语）。例句：*The extensive model relied on land expansion.*
- `as one Yueyang official puts it` ⭐⭐ — 正如一位岳阳官员所说。
- `endless sprawl` ⭐⭐⭐ — 无休止的蔓延（sprawl = 城市无序扩张）。例句：*Urban sprawl consumed farmland.*
- `swallowed up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 吞并（swallow up = 吞噬）。例句：*The city swallowed up nearby villages.*

**为什么这样写：** `lie beyond economics` 把讨论从"经济账"升维到"发展模式"。`"extensive"` 与后文 `"intensive"` 是一对官方术语（粗放 vs 集约），作者加引号明确是转述官方话语。`swallowed up` 用"吞并"的暴力意象描绘土地扩张——城市像怪兽一样吃地，为"集约转向"提供对比靶子。

> **原句 25:** Now, the turn is inwards, towards an "intensive" model, with cities examining how they can make better use of what they already have. It is a more "refined" form of governance, the official says.

**中文理解：** 现在，转向是内向的，朝向"集约"模式——城市审视如何更好地利用已有之物。这位官员说，这是一种更"精细"的治理形式。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Now, the turn is inwards, towards an "intensive" model, with cities examining how they can make better use of what they already have`（towards + with 复合结构 + how/what 从句嵌套）。第二句 `It is a more "refined" form of governance, the official says`（引述倒装）。

**关键词：**
- `the turn is inwards` ⭐⭐⭐ — 转向是内向的（inwards 与 sprawl 的"向外"相对）。例句：*The company's focus turned inwards.*
- `the "intensive" model` ⭐⭐ — "集约"模式。
- `make better use of` ⭐⭐ — 更好地利用。
- `a more "refined" form of governance` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更"精细"的治理形式（refined = 精炼的/精细的）。例句：*The system needs a more refined approach.*

**为什么这样写：** `inwards`（向内）与上句 `sprawl`（向外扩张）形成空间方向上的对立——作者用"空间转向"这一具象概念承载"发展模式转型"这一抽象议题。`"refined"` 加引号转述官员的自我美化，为下句"别人可能说得没那么伟大"的冷处理做铺垫。

> **原句 26:** Others might put it in less grandiose terms: a financially chastened China is trying to get more from less. ■

**中文理解：** 其他人可能会用不那么宏大的措辞来表述：一个在财政上吃了教训的中国，正试图用更少的东西获得更多。

**句子结构：** `Others might put it in less grandiose terms: a financially chastened China is trying to get more from less`（might put it in terms + 冒号解释）。

**关键词：**
- `put it in less grandiose terms` ⭐⭐⭐ — 用不那么宏大的措辞表述（grandiose = 宏大的/浮夸的）。例句：*Critics put it in less grandiose terms.*
- `financially chastened` ⭐⭐⭐ — 财政上吃了教训的（chasten = 惩戒/使谦卑）。例句：*A chastened company cut its ambitions.*
- `get more from less` ⭐⭐⭐ — 用更少得到更多（对标标题 squeeze more out of）。例句：*The slogan is "more from less".*

**为什么这样写：** 结尾"冷处理"收束：官员自诩"更精细的治理"，作者用 `Others might put it in less grandiose terms` 把宏大叙事拉回朴素事实——`a financially chastened China is trying to get more from less`。`chastened`（吃了教训）一词既承接"房地产崩盘教训"的事实，又含蓄点出"非自愿的节约"。`get more from less` 与标题 `squeeze more out of state assets` 首尾呼应，用最朴素的英语点题，是《经济学人》标题-正文闭环的教科书范例。

**段落逻辑：** 意义升维（24）→ 转向集约（25）→ 朴素收束（26）。从"宏大意义"到"克制总结"。

## 长难句专项

### 🔍 原句 1（过去分词链）

> **原句:** YUEYANG, A CITY in Hunan province, is famous for its tower, an elegant three-storey pavilion immortalised in millennium-old prose still memorised by schoolchildren.

**① 找主干：** YUEYANG is famous for its tower
**② 找修饰：** A CITY in Hunan province（同位语）；an elegant three-storey pavilion（tower 的同位语）；immortalised in millennium-old prose（过去分词定语）；still memorised by schoolchildren（再一层过去分词，修饰 prose）
**③ 找从句：** 无从句（全部是同位语 + 分词短语链）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 岳阳以楼闻名 → 楼是三层阁楼 → 阁楼被千年散文铭记 → 散文至今被学童背诵。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 四个信息单元以"链条式同位语/分词"推进，一句讲清"城市-楼-文-童"的传承链。

### 🔍 原句 5（定语从句 + 引语）

> **原句:** A local official who has helped oversee the project told Chaguan that his mission was far from accomplished: "The next big challenge is to make money."

**① 找主干：** A local official told Chaguan that ...
**② 找修饰：** who has helped oversee the project（定语从句）；that his mission was far from accomplished（宾语从句）；冒号后引语
**③ 找从句：** who has helped（定语）；that his mission was ...（宾语）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 一位参与监管项目的官员告诉作者，任务远未完成，下一个大挑战是赚钱。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 定语从句交代身份，宾语从句交代判断，引语给出原话——三层信息一次到位。

### 🔍 原句 13（被动 + 时间 + 宾语从句）

> **原句:** This closet-rummaging exercise was formally launched by the central government in 2022 when it declared that provinces and cities should "revitalise existing state assets".

**① 找主干：** This closet-rummaging exercise was formally launched by the central government in 2022
**② 找修饰：** when it declared that ...（时间状语从句，含宾语从句）
**③ 找从句：** when it declared（时间）；that provinces and cities should "revitalise ..."（declared 的宾语）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 中央政府在 2022 年正式发起"翻衣柜"行动，当时它宣布省市应"盘活存量国有资产"。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 被动句交代"行动 - 发起者 - 时间"，when 从句交代"同时说了什么"。

### 🔍 原句 25（towards + with 复合结构 + 双重从句）

> **原句:** Now, the turn is inwards, towards an "intensive" model, with cities examining how they can make better use of what they already have.

**① 找主干：** the turn is inwards
**② 找修饰：** towards an "intensive" model（方向）；with cities examining ...（with 复合结构）；how they can make better use ...（examining 的宾语）；what they already have（make use of 的宾语）
**③ 找从句：** how they can ...（宾语从句）；what they already have（宾语从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 转向朝内、朝集约模式，城市审视如何更好地利用已有之物。
**⑤ 整体理解：** with 复合结构把"城市做什么"挂在主句上，how + what 双层宾语从句压缩了"方式 + 对象"。

### 3. 重要语法

- **强调句**：`it is the streets around the tower that matter more`

- **被动 + 时间 + 宾语**：`was launched ... when it declared that ...`

### 5. 写作技巧

1. **隐喻体系化**：从标题 `wring more` 到正文 `squeeze` / `closet` / `shirt` / `rummage`，一个"翻衣柜找旧衬衫"的生活隐喻贯穿全文——抽象政策"可感化"
2. **首尾闭环**：标题点题 `wring more from state assets`，结尾 `get more from less` 回扣；开头岳阳楼文化符号，结尾朴素总结——"从楼到衣"的收放
3. **受访者代言隐喻**：核心比喻（衣柜衬衫）由国企经理之口说出，作者借人物之口"植入"自己的分析框架，比直接打比方更有说服力
4. **数据的克制**：`40% → half → 10% → 5%` 数字给足，但始终限定范围（"十省"、"a bit"、"may be"、"could"）——严谨不浮夸
5. **先抑后扬再抑**：先承认"some efforts almost comical"（荒唐自救），再给 Yueyang 正面案例，最后 Even so 拉回"解不了债务"——完整呈现利弊
6. **结尾冷处理**：官方称"refined governance"，作者改用"less grandiose terms"收束——用反差制造幽默，也平衡了报道立场
7. **术语双关**：`extensive` / `intensive` / `aggressive` 都是官方经济话语的转引，加引号保持区隔与距离
