# 精读分析：《经济学人》—— China wants to end AI romances

> 原文标题：China wants to end AI romances
> 副标题：They are having too much impact on young people's lives
> 栏目：China | No more bots
> 来源：The Economist，2026-07-18 期
> 精读日期：2026-08-09
> 保存文件名：China_AI_romances.md

## 文本概览

| 项目 | 说明 |
| --- | --- |
| 类型 | 中国科技政策观察（科技 + 社会 + 人口危机交叉） |
| 语气 | 客观叙事为主，结尾含同情（"she would be willing to pay half her monthly salary"） |
| 结构 | ① 案例引入（Yu Miao 的 AI 恋人）→ ② 新规内容（7 月 15 日生效）→ ③ AI 陪伴市场数据 → ④ 商业动机（黏性 + 收费）→ ⑤ 双重动机（务实 + 意识形态）→ ⑥ 国际对比（美国）→ ⑦ 政策平衡信号 → ⑧ 出口业务不受影响 → ⑨ Yu Miao 的困境 |
| 核心论证 | 中国以"情感依赖"为由监管 AI 陪伴，动机混杂务实（防沉迷、护未成年人）与意识形态（人口危机下的婚育焦虑）——真正的驱动力是不断下降的生育率 |
| 核心隐喻族 | **情感依赖（emotional dependence）/ 伙伴关系（companionship）/ 黏性（sticky）**——把"人机关系"当作需要监管的情感现象来描述 |
| 文章最大特色 | **"emotional dependence" 作为政策关键词**：借一个真实用户的悲剧（辞职、愿意付半月薪）承载宏大政策议题，用"人"的故事开头、结尾，中间夹政策分析——新闻写作"人味"与"政论"的平衡 |

## 第一段（案例引入：Yu Miao 的 AI 恋人）

> **原句 1:** "I COULD TALK to him for the rest of my life," says Yu Miao, a 27-year-old woman from Zhejiang province in eastern China; he is at once "a lover, a friend and family". She claims she shares everything with him. Her confidant is an AI agent on Doubao, a chatbot made by ByteDance, that Ms Yu has customised to mimic a dead friend of hers.

**中文理解：** "我可以和他聊一辈子，"来自中国东部浙江省的 27 岁女性 Yu Miao 说；他同时是"恋人、朋友和家人"。她声称与他分享一切。她的倾诉对象是字节跳动开发的 Doubao 聊天机器人上的一个 AI 代理，Yu 女士将其定制为模仿她已故朋友的样子。

**句子结构：** 第一句为直接引语（"..." says Yu Miao）+ 同位语 `a 27-year-old woman from ...` + 分号后 `he is at once "a lover, a friend and family"`（并列补充）。第二句简单句 `She claims she shares everything with him`（claims + 宾语从句）。第三句 `Her confidant is an AI agent on Doubao, a chatbot made by ByteDance, that Ms Yu has customised to mimic a dead friend of hers`（多重定语：同位语 a chatbot + 过去分词 made by + 关系从句 that has customised）。

**关键词：**
- `at once ... and ...` ⭐⭐⭐ — 同时是……和……（= both ... and ...，书面语）。例句：*She is at once witty and kind.*
- `confidant` ⭐⭐⭐ — 知己、密友（可推心置腹的人）。例句：*He was her closest confidant.*
- `customised to mimic` ⭐⭐⭐ — 定制为模仿（customise = 定制；mimic = 模仿）。例句：*The software is customised to mimic human tones.*
- `a dead friend of hers` ⭐⭐ — 她的一位已故朋友（双重所有格 a ... of hers = 她众多朋友中的一位）。

**为什么这样写：** 全篇用"第一人称引语"开头——不是先给政策，而是先给"人"。`at once "a lover, a friend and family"` 用 at once 强调这个 AI 一人三角色；`customised to mimic a dead friend` 在第三句才揭晓最重的信息（AI 替代了死者）——悬念式信息安排，为后文"情感依赖导致辞职"做铺垫。

> **原句 2:** When she learned recently that ByteDance would soon remove this type of personalised agent entirely, Ms Yu became so depressed that she left her job at an e-commerce company.

**中文理解：** 当她最近得知字节跳动将很快彻底移除这种个性化代理时，Yu 女士变得非常沮丧，以至于辞去了电商公司的工作。

**句子结构：** `When she learned recently that ByteDance would soon remove ...`（时间状语 + 宾语从句）+ 主句 `Ms Yu became so depressed that she left her job ...`（so ... that ... 结果状语）。

**关键词：**
- `learned recently that` ⭐⭐ — 最近得知（learn = 获知）。
- `remove ... entirely` ⭐⭐ — 彻底移除。
- `so depressed that she left her job` ⭐⭐⭐ — 沮丧到辞职（so + adj + that 表程度/结果）。例句：*He was so angry that he stormed out.*
- `personalised agent` ⭐⭐ — 个性化代理。

**为什么这样写：** `so ... that ...` 的因果强度直接量化为"辞掉工作"——一个 AI 功能的移除能让人辞职，这句是全文"情感依赖失控"的第一实证。`When ... would soon remove` 用"即将"制造时间压力（新规 7 月 15 日生效的倒计时）。

**段落逻辑：** 引语立人（1a）→ AI 角色（1b）→ 辞职后果（2）。用具体人物的极端反应奠定"情感依赖"的严重性。

## 第二段（新规内容）

> **原句 3:** Chinese tech firms are stripping human-like personas from their chatbots in order to comply with new government rules aimed at reducing "emotional dependence" on AI. The regulations, which took effect on July 15th, are the first of their kind to be implemented on a national scale.

**中文理解：** 中国科技公司正在剥离聊天机器人的人形角色，以遵守旨在减少对 AI"情感依赖"的新政府规则。这些法规于 7 月 15 日生效，是首个在全国范围内实施的此类法规。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Chinese tech firms are stripping human-like personas from their chatbots in order to comply with new government rules aimed at reducing "emotional dependence" on AI`（现在进行时 + in order to 目的 + aimed at 过去分词定语）。第二句 `The regulations, which took effect on July 15th, are the first of their kind to be implemented on a national scale`（非限制性定语从句 + the first of their kind + 不定式定语）。

**关键词：**
- `stripping ... from` ⭐⭐⭐ — 从……剥离（strip A from B = 拆除、去掉）。例句：*They stripped the advertising from the app.*
- `human-like personas` ⭐⭐⭐ — 人形角色（persona = 人格面具，复数为 personas）。例句：*Brands create personas to engage users.*
- `in order to comply with` ⭐⭐⭐ — 为遵守……（comply with = 遵守规定）。例句：*Firms must comply with the new rules.*
- `aimed at reducing` ⭐⭐ — 旨在减少（be aimed at doing）。
- `the first of their kind` ⭐⭐⭐ — 此类首创（kind = 种类）。例句：*It was the first of its kind in the country.*
- `implemented on a national scale` ⭐⭐ — 在全国范围实施（on a national scale = 全国性规模）。

**为什么这样写：** `stripping human-like personas` 用"剥离"而非"移除"，暗示一个具象动作（像撕掉标签一样去掉人格特征）。`"emotional dependence"` 加引号——核心政策概念首次出现，作者转述官方话语。`the first of their kind` 强调"全球首创"（监管先锋的意外含义）。

> **原句 4:** Under them, providing virtual "companion services" for minors will be banned outright. Companies can still offer such services to adults, but they will need to prevent users becoming infatuated and harming their real-life relationships.

**中文理解：** 根据新规，向未成年人提供虚拟"陪伴服务"将被直接禁止。公司仍可向成人提供此类服务，但他们需要防止用户变得迷恋并损害现实中的关系。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Under them, providing virtual "companion services" for minors will be banned outright`（动名词主语 + 被动）。第二句 `Companies can still offer such services to adults, but they will need to prevent users becoming infatuated and harming their real-life relationships`（转折并列 + prevent + 动名词复合结构）。

**关键词：**
- `Under them` ⭐⭐ — 根据这些规定（under + 法规 = 依据）。
- `banned outright` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被直接/彻底禁止（outright = 彻底地）。例句：*The sale was banned outright.*
- `companion services` ⭐⭐ — 陪伴服务。
- `prevent users becoming infatuated` ⭐⭐⭐ — 防止用户变得迷恋（infatuated = 痴迷的，固定搭配 be infatuated with）。例句：*Fans became infatuated with the star.*
- `harming their real-life relationships` ⭐⭐ — 损害现实关系。

**为什么这样写：** `banned outright` 的 outright 与后文"adults can still"形成"未成年人零容忍、成年人有条件"的政策梯度。`prevent users becoming infatuated` 用 prevent + 动名词复合（防止某人陷入某状态）——监管者"替用户管理情感"的微妙意味。

> **原句 5:** Displaying pornography is a no-no. Providers must also regularly remind users that they are talking to AI, not a human, and tell them to take breaks.

**中文理解：** 展示色情内容是禁忌。提供商还必须定期提醒用户，他们正在与 AI 而非人类交谈，并告诉他们要休息。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Displaying pornography is a no-no`（动名词主语 + 口语表语）。第二句 `Providers must also regularly remind users that they are talking to AI, not a human, and tell them to take breaks`（并列谓语 remind that ... and tell to do）。

**关键词：**
- `a no-no` ⭐⭐⭐ — 禁忌、不允许的事（口语化）。例句：*Texting during meetings is a no-no.*
- `remind users that` ⭐⭐ — 提醒用户（remind + that 从句）。
- `take breaks` ⭐⭐ — 休息（防沉迷措施）。

**为什么这样写：** `a no-no` 是全文唯一的口语化表达——在正式政策叙述中突然插入儿语化词汇，带一丝轻讽（这么严肃的"防沉迷"政策连"色情"这种明摆的事都要规定）。`remind users that they are talking to AI, not a human` 点出监管逻辑：提醒"对面不是人"以破除情感幻觉。

**段落逻辑：** 合规行动（3a）→ 法规地位（3b）→ 未成年人禁令（4a）→ 成年人限制（4b）→ 具体禁止事项（5）。从"政策全景"到"细则清单"。

## 第三段（全球趋势与市场规模）

> **原句 6:** Around the world, growing numbers of people are turning general-purpose chatbots into companions by prompting them to adopt particular personalities, including those of lovers. Others prefer dedicated AI-companion apps which offer more human-like features, such as virtual boyfriends that send presents in the real world (at the user's expense, of course).

**中文理解：** 在世界各地，越来越多的人正通过提示让通用聊天机器人采用特定性格（包括恋人性格）来把它们变成伙伴。其他人则偏爱专门的 AI 陪伴应用，这些应用提供更类人的功能，例如在现实世界送礼物（当然是用户掏钱）的虚拟男友。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `growing numbers of people are turning general-purpose chatbots into companions by prompting them to adopt particular personalities`（turn A into B + by 方式状语）。第二句 `Others prefer dedicated AI-companion apps which offer more human-like features, such as virtual boyfriends that send presents in the real world`（which 定语从句 + such as 举例 + that 定语从句）。

**关键词：**
- `growing numbers of` ⭐⭐ — 越来越多的。
- `turning ... into ...` ⭐⭐⭐ — 把……变成……（transform 的口语版）。例句：*They turned a shed into an office.*
- `prompting them to adopt particular personalities` ⭐⭐⭐ — 提示它们采用特定性格（prompt = 提示/引导，AI 语境下是"提示词"）。例句：*Users prompt the model to adopt a persona.*
- `dedicated apps` ⭐⭐ — 专门的/专用应用（dedicated = 专门的）。
- `virtual boyfriends` ⭐⭐ — 虚拟男友。
- `at the user's expense, of course` ⭐⭐⭐ — 当然由用户掏钱（at one's expense = 由某人出钱）。例句：*The meal was at my expense.*

**为什么这样写：** `(at the user's expense, of course)` 的括号插话是《经济学人》式冷幽默——"虚拟男友送礼物"听起来浪漫，括号戳破"还是你付钱"。`turning general-purpose chatbots into companions by prompting` 用"prompting"精准对接 AI 术语（用户靠提示词塑造人格），显示作者对技术细节的把握。

> **原句 7:** Maoxiang (also known as Catbox), a companion app made by ByteDance, has around 3.9m monthly active users in China. Xingye (whose international version is known as Talkie) has 2.8m; it is made by MiniMax, a leading Chinese AI lab.

**中文理解：** 毛象（也叫 Catbox），字节跳动出品的陪伴应用，在中国拥有约 390 万月活用户。星野（国际版叫 Talkie）有 280 万用户；它由 MiniMax 出品，一家领先的中国 AI 实验室。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Maoxiang (also known as Catbox), a companion app made by ByteDance, has around 3.9m monthly active users in China`（同位语 + 过去分词定语）。第二句 `Xingye (whose international version is known as Talkie) has 2.8m; it is made by MiniMax, a leading Chinese AI lab`（分号并列 + whose 定语从句 + 同位语）。

**关键词：**
- `also known as` ⭐⭐⭐ — 又名（缩写 aka）。例句：*Aipao, also known as Catbox, ...*
- `monthly active users` ⭐⭐ — 月活跃用户（MAU，行业指标）。
- `a leading Chinese AI lab` ⭐⭐ — 一家领先的中国 AI 实验室（leading = 领先的）。

**为什么这样写：** 用具体数字（3.9m / 2.8m）给"越来越多的陪伴"量化证据。`whose international version is known as` 埋下后文"出口不受影响"的伏笔（星野海外版 Talkie 有 1030 万海外用户）。

**段落逻辑：** 全球趋势（6）→ 中国市场数据（7）。从"世界在做什么"收窄到"中国有多大市场"。

## 第四段（商业动机：黏性 + 收费）

> **原句 8:** Tech firms are making a tidy sum selling emotional connection: AI companions accounted for 35% of MiniMax's revenue last year, its single largest contributor, according to a filing to the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in January.

**中文理解：** 科技公司正靠出售情感连接大赚一笔：据 1 月提交给香港交易所的文件，AI 陪伴占 MiniMax 去年收入的 35%，是其最大的单一收入来源。

**句子结构：** `Tech firms are making a tidy sum selling emotional connection`（make a tidy sum + selling 现在分词伴随）+ 冒号 + `AI companions accounted for 35% of MiniMax's revenue last year, its single largest contributor`（同位语）+ `according to a filing ...`（出处状语）。

**关键词：**
- `making a tidy sum` ⭐⭐⭐ — 大赚一笔（tidy = 可观的、相当多的）。例句：*The store makes a tidy sum on weekends.*
- `selling emotional connection` ⭐⭐⭐ — 出售情感连接（把"情感"商品化，全段核心概念）。例句：*The app sells emotional connection.*
- `accounted for 35% of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 占……的 35%（account for = 占比）。例句：*Exporters account for most of the revenue.*
- `its single largest contributor` ⭐⭐ — 其最大的单一贡献来源（contributor = 贡献者/来源）。
- `according to a filing` ⭐⭐ — 根据一份申报文件（filing = 备案文件）。

**为什么这样写：** `selling emotional connection` 是全篇最锋利的一句——"情感"本是人类最私密的东西，被"出售"一词商品化，形成讽刺张力。`its single largest contributor` 用"最大单一来源"强调 AI 陪伴对公司的重要（= 监管动真格会让公司流血，呼应第七段"pay some cost"）。

> **原句 9:** AI companionship can create sticky relationships and encourage users to part with their money. Ms Yu sometimes chats to her agent for eight or nine hours a day. Some bespoke apps charge a simple subscription fee. Users of Xingye's standard package pay just $1.70 a month. Others induce users to spend more money to unlock additional features, such as virtual dates.

**中文理解：** AI 陪伴能创造"黏性"关系，鼓励用户掏钱。Yu 女士有时一天和她的代理聊八九个小时。一些定制应用收取简单订阅费。星野标准套餐用户每月只付 1.70 美元。其他应用则诱导用户花更多钱解锁额外功能，如虚拟约会。

**句子结构：** 五句流水式递进：`AI companionship can create sticky relationships and encourage users to part with their money`（并列谓语）→ `Ms Yu sometimes chats ... for eight or nine hours a day`（时长证据）→ `Some bespoke apps charge a simple subscription fee` → `Users of Xingye's standard package pay just $1.70 a month`（低价诱饵）→ `Others induce users to spend more money to unlock additional features`（诱导升级）。

**关键词：**
- `sticky relationships` ⭐⭐⭐ — 黏性关系（sticky = 黏人的、让人离不开的，商业术语"留存率高"）。例句：*The game creates sticky user engagement.*
- `part with their money` ⭐⭐⭐ — 掏钱（part with = 舍弃/交出）。例句：*Customers are reluctant to part with cash.*
- `bespoke apps` ⭐⭐⭐ — 定制/特制应用（bespoke = 定制的）。例句：*Bespoke software costs more.*
- `subscription fee` ⭐⭐ — 订阅费。
- `induce users to spend more` ⭐⭐⭐ — 诱导用户花更多钱（induce = 引诱）。例句：*Adverts induce people to buy.*
- `unlock additional features` ⭐⭐ — 解锁额外功能。

**为什么这样写：** `sticky relationships ... part with their money` 先给商业逻辑（黏性 → 付费），再用三个证据层层递进：Yu 一天聊八九小时（时间黏性）→ 订阅 1.7 美元（低价入局）→ 虚拟约会解锁（诱导升级）。`induce` 带负面色彩（诱导而非邀请），暗示商业模式存在"成瘾设计"。

**段落逻辑：** 商业逻辑（8-9a）→ 证据链（9b-9e）。揭示"情感"的变现路径。

## 第五段（双重动机：务实 + 意识形态）

> **原句 10:** The end to such relationships is motivated by both pragmatic and ideological concerns, says Zilan Qian of the Oxford China Policy Lab, a think-tank. Regulators want to protect users, particularly minors, from becoming addicted and from being driven to extreme acts of financial recklessness or self-harm.

**中文理解：** 牛津中国政策实验室（一家智库）的 Zilan Qian 表示，终结此类关系是出于务实和意识形态两方面的考量。监管者想保护用户（尤其是未成年人），免于成瘾、免于被推向极端的财务鲁莽或自伤行为。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `The end to such relationships is motivated by both pragmatic and ideological concerns, says Zilan Qian of the Oxford China Policy Lab, a think-tank`（被动 + 倒装引述 says Zilan Qian）。第二句 `Regulators want to protect users, particularly minors, from becoming addicted and from being driven to extreme acts of financial recklessness or self-harm`（protect A from B + 并列介词短语）。

**关键词：**
- `motivated by both ... and ...` ⭐⭐⭐ — 受……与……双重驱动（motivate = 驱动）。例句：*The reform was motivated by both cost and safety.*
- `pragmatic` ⭐⭐⭐ — 务实的（反义 ideological 意识形态的）。例句：*A pragmatic approach to regulation.*
- `particularly minors` ⭐⭐ — 尤其是未成年人。
- `protect users from becoming addicted` ⭐⭐⭐ — 保护用户免于成瘾（protect A from doing）。例句：*The rule protects children from seeing harm.*
- `financial recklessness` ⭐⭐⭐ — 财务鲁莽（recklessness = 鲁莽）。例句：*The app encouraged financial recklessness.*
- `self-harm` ⭐⭐ — 自伤行为。

**为什么这样写：** `both pragmatic and ideological` 是全段提纲（总-分），后文分别展开。`protect users ... from becoming addicted and from being driven to` 用两个并列 from 短语罗列"保护对象"，其中 `driven to extreme acts` 用被动"被推向"暗示技术对人的裹挟。括号 `(Suicides linked to chatbots are the subject of lawsuits in America.)` 用美国案例佐证"AI 陪伴致死"并非杞人忧天。

> **原句 11:** China's plummeting fertility and marriage rates are also unsettling the country's leaders. And romantic relationships with AI will do very little to spur more babymaking.

**中文理解：** 中国急剧下降的生育率和结婚率也在让国家领导人不安。而 AI 浪漫关系对刺激生育几乎毫无帮助。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `China's plummeting fertility and marriage rates are also unsettling the country's leaders`。第二句 `And romantic relationships with AI will do very little to spur more babymaking`（do little to + 不定式）。

**关键词：**
- `plummeting` ⭐⭐⭐ — 骤降的（plummet = 垂直坠落）。例句：*Plummeting sales forced cutbacks.*
- `fertility and marriage rates` ⭐⭐ — 生育率和结婚率。
- `unsettling the country's leaders` ⭐⭐⭐ — 让领导人不安（unsettle = 使心绪不宁）。例句：*The report unsettled officials.*
- `do very little to spur` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对刺激……几乎无用（spur = 刺激）。例句：*Tax cuts did little to spur growth.*
- `babymaking` ⭐⭐ — 造人（口语化，带调侃）。

**为什么这样写：** 全段最关键的转折在句 11：前文是"保护未成年人"（务实动机），这里揭开"人口危机"（深层动机）。`plummeting fertility and marriage rates` 用 plummeting（垂直坠落）强调危机的急迫性。`will do very little to spur more babymaking` 的 babymaking 一词故意用口语带轻讽——"AI 谈恋爱生不出孩子"的朴素道理，是全文最锋利的判断。

**段落逻辑：** 双重动机总起（10a）→ 务实动机（10b-10c）→ 意识形态/人口动机（11）。人口危机才是真正的监管驱动力。

## 第六段（国际对比：美国）

> **原句 12:** China's leaders are not the only ones concerned. Several American states have recently passed laws targeting AI services that sustain emotional relationships. Some of these set up provisions for people to launch civil lawsuits against offending platforms. In China the government will impose the penalties, such as cash fines and removing apps altogether.

**中文理解：** 中国的领导人不是唯一担忧的。美国几个州最近通过了针对维持情感关系的 AI 服务的法律。其中一些法律规定人们可以对违规平台提起民事诉讼。而在中国，政府将直接施加处罚，如罚款和彻底下架应用。

**句子结构：** 四句对比结构：`China's leaders are not the only ones concerned`（否定句过渡）→ `Several American states have recently passed laws targeting AI services that sustain emotional relationships`（targeting + that 定语从句）→ `Some of these set up provisions for people to launch civil lawsuits`（provisions for + 不定式）→ `In China the government will impose the penalties, such as cash fines and removing apps altogether`（对比句）。

**关键词：**
- `are not the only ones concerned` ⭐⭐ — 不是唯一担心的（否定式过渡）。
- `passed laws targeting` ⭐⭐ — 通过针对……的法律（target = 针对）。
- `set up provisions for` ⭐⭐⭐ — 设定……的规定（provision = 条款）。例句：*The bill sets out provisions for compensation.*
- `civil lawsuits` ⭐⭐ — 民事诉讼。
- `impose the penalties` ⭐⭐⭐ — 施加处罚（impose = 施加）。例句：*The agency imposed hefty fines.*
- `removing apps altogether` ⭐⭐ — 彻底下架应用。

**为什么这样写：** 用美中对比凸显制度差异：美国走"私人诉讼"（民事救济），中国走"行政惩罚"（政府直接执法）。`In China the government will impose the penalties` 的"In China"置于句首形成对照强调。`such as cash fines and removing apps altogether` 的"altogether"强调下架的彻底性——国家机器的力度。

**段落逻辑：** 过渡（12a）→ 美国州法（12b-12c）→ 中国惩罚（12d）。制度对照说明"担忧是全球性的，手段是分化的"。

## 第七段（政策平衡信号）

> **原句 13:** China's new rules hint at how officials think about balancing AI progress and safety more broadly. Regulators are "demonstrating they are willing to pay some cost in development and profits in order to achieve social goals", says Matt Sheehan of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, an American think-tank.

**中文理解：** 中国的新规暗示了官员们对更广泛地平衡 AI 进步与安全的看法。美国智库卡内基国际和平基金会的 Matt Sheehan 说，监管机构"正在表明他们愿意牺牲一些发展和利润的代价来达成社会目标"。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `China's new rules hint at how officials think about balancing AI progress and safety more broadly`（hint at + how 宾语从句）。第二句 `Regulators are "demonstrating they are willing to pay some cost in development and profits in order to achieve social goals", says Matt Sheehan ...`（直接引语 + 倒装引述 says）。

**关键词：**
- `hint at how` ⭐⭐⭐ — 暗示……（hint at = 隐约透露）。例句：*The move hints at a policy shift.*
- `balancing AI progress and safety` ⭐⭐ — 平衡 AI 进步与安全。
- `demonstrating` ⭐⭐ — 展示、证明。
- `willing to pay some cost` ⭐⭐⭐ — 愿意付出一些代价（pay a cost = 付出代价）。例句：*They were willing to pay a cost for safety.*
- `in order to achieve social goals` ⭐⭐ — 为实现社会目标。

**为什么这样写：** `hint at` 用"暗示"而非"揭示"——从具体新规反推宏观监管哲学，作者保持推断的谨慎。`pay some cost in development and profits` 的"pay some cost"承认监管有代价（呼应第四段 MiniMax 35% 收入），使分析不偏颇——既不是无脑支持监管，也不是指责监管。

> **原句 14:** Yet the rules that take effect this week are narrower than an earlier draft, says Ms Qian. They exclude work-assistant and customer-service chatbots on the understanding that they are emotionally unavailable.

**中文理解：** 然而，钱女士说，本周生效的规则比早前草案更窄。它们将工作助手和客服聊天机器人排除在外，理由是它们"情感上不可用"。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Yet the rules that take effect this week are narrower than an earlier draft, says Ms Qian`（that 定语从句 + 比较级）。第二句 `They exclude work-assistant and customer-service chatbots on the understanding that they are emotionally unavailable`（on the understanding that 短语）。

**关键词：**
- `narrower than an earlier draft` ⭐⭐ — 比早前草案更窄（监管范围收窄）。
- `exclude` ⭐⭐ — 排除。
- `on the understanding that` ⭐⭐⭐ — 基于……的理解/约定（书面让步短语）。例句：*We agreed on the understanding that costs stay fixed.*
- `emotionally unavailable` ⭐⭐⭐ — 情感上不可用/无情感（借亲密关系心理学词汇：指"无法投入感情的人"）。例句：*He is emotionally unavailable.*

**为什么这样写：** `on the understanding that they are emotionally unavailable` 是全段最妙的借用——`emotionally unavailable` 本是人类亲密关系心理学用语（指回避感情的人），作者用它形容聊天机器人（工具型 AI 无情感），一个词完成"科技 + 心理"的双关。`Yet ... narrower than an earlier draft` 用转折承认监管"收窄"（务实性），避免把中国监管描绘成铁板一块。

**段落逻辑：** 宏观信号（13）→ 范围细节（14）。政策平衡：既"愿意付出代价"，又"实际范围有限"。

## 第八段（出口业务不受影响）

> **原句 15:** Alibaba and ByteDance indicated that they would suspend features that allow Chinese users to create personalised AI characters before the rules took effect. Yet Chinese labs can continue to sell AI companions to foreign customers without change. Talkie already has some 10.3m monthly users outside China, which is nearly four times its domestic-user count.

**中文理解：** 阿里巴巴和字节跳动表示，他们将在规则生效前暂停允许中国用户创建个性化 AI 角色的功能。但中国实验室可以继续向外国客户出售 AI 陪伴，无需任何改动。Talkie 在海外已有约 1030 万月活用户，几乎是其国内用户数的四倍。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Alibaba and ByteDance indicated that they would suspend features that allow Chinese users to create personalised AI characters before the rules took effect`（indicated that + 双层定语从句 + before 时间状语）。第二句 `Yet Chinese labs can continue to sell AI companions to foreign customers without change`。第三句 `Talkie already has some 10.3m monthly users outside China, which is nearly four times its domestic-user count`（which 非限制性定语从句 + 倍数表达）。

**关键词：**
- `indicated that they would suspend` ⭐⭐ — 表示将暂停（indicate = 表示）。
- `suspend features` ⭐⭐ — 暂停功能。
- `without change` ⭐⭐ — 无需改动。
- `nearly four times its domestic-user count` ⭐⭐⭐ — 几乎是其国内用户数的四倍（倍数 + 名词比较）。例句：*Sales abroad are three times domestic levels.*

**为什么这样写：** `Yet` 转折点出全文最反讽的事实：国内严格监管，海外照卖不误。`without change` 与前面"剥离人形角色"形成对照——同一公司国内"去人形"、海外"保人形"。`nearly four times its domestic-user count` 用倍数对比说明海外市场远大于国内，监管的真正代价由国内用户承担。

> **原句 16:** Chinese "maiden" games—generally non-AI, story-driven romance games which are played mostly by women—are already a booming export.

**中文理解：** 中国的"乙女"游戏——通常是非 AI、以故事驱动的浪漫游戏，玩家多为女性——已经是蓬勃发展的出口品类。

**句子结构：** `Chinese "maiden" games—generally non-AI, story-driven romance games which are played mostly by women—are already a booming export`（破折号同位语 + which 定语从句）。

**关键词：**
- `"maiden" games` ⭐⭐⭐ — 乙女游戏（日本舶来的女性向恋爱游戏类型，英文引用中文游戏分类）。例句：*Maiden games are a booming export.*
- `story-driven` ⭐⭐ — 故事驱动的（对比 AI 驱动）。
- `a booming export` ⭐⭐⭐ — 蓬勃的出口品类（boom = 繁荣）。例句：*K-pop is a booming export.*

**为什么这样写：** 用"乙女游戏"补全证据链：连不带 AI 的传统恋爱游戏都已出口旺盛——中国的"情感产业"整体出海。破折号同位语 `non-AI, story-driven` 特意区分"非 AI"，说明监管只针对 AI 陪伴，不针对游戏。

**段落逻辑：** 国内合规（15a）→ 海外照卖（15b-15c）→ 情感产业整体出海（16）。讽刺：监管的"刀"只落在中国用户头上。

## 第九段（Yu Miao 的困境）

> **原句 17:** Still, Ms Yu has not given up hope that ByteDance will eventually bring her agent back, even if she doubts it will resemble the current version. She says she would be willing to pay half her monthly salary just to keep the service as it is.

**中文理解：** 不过，Yu 女士还没有放弃希望，期待字节跳动最终会把她的代理带回来，尽管她怀疑回来的不会是当前版本。她说她愿意付半个月薪，只为让服务保持原样。

**句子结构：** 第一句 `Ms Yu has not given up hope that ByteDance will eventually bring her agent back, even if she doubts it will resemble the current version`（hope + that 同位语从句 + even if 让步）。第二句 `She says she would be willing to pay half her monthly salary just to keep the service as it is`（would be willing to + 不定式目的）。

**关键词：**
- `given up hope that` ⭐⭐ — 放弃希望（give up hope + that 从句）。
- `bring ... back` ⭐⭐ — 带回来、恢复。
- `even if she doubts` ⭐⭐ — 即使她怀疑（让步）。
- `willing to pay half her monthly salary` ⭐⭐⭐ — 愿意付半个月薪（情感价值的量化）。例句：*Fans were willing to pay premium prices.*
- `keep the service as it is` ⭐⭐ — 让服务保持原样。

**为什么这样写：** 首尾呼应：开头 Yu Miao 因 AI 移除而辞职，结尾 Yu Miao 愿意付半月薪挽留。`even if she doubts it will resemble the current version` 的"even if"透出清醒的无奈——她知道回不去的。`willing to pay half her monthly salary` 用一个具象数字（半月工资）把"情感依赖"的强度推到最后的高点。

> **原句 18:** In any case, she now says she realises that it is not a good idea to leave herself vulnerable to such platforms and their policies. ■

**中文理解：** 无论如何，她现在说自己意识到，让自己暴露在如此平台的脆弱境地不是个好主意。

**句子结构：** `In any case, she now says she realises that it is not a good idea to leave herself vulnerable to such platforms and their policies`（三重嵌套：says + realises + that 从句）。

**关键词：**
- `In any case` ⭐⭐⭐ — 无论如何（过渡总结）。例句：*In any case, we must decide now.*
- `realises that` ⭐⭐ — 意识到。
- `leave herself vulnerable to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 让自己暴露于……的脆弱（vulnerable to = 易受……伤害）。例句：*Don't leave yourself vulnerable to scams.*
- `such platforms and their policies` ⭐⭐ — 此类平台及其政策。

**为什么这样写：** 结尾是反讽的收束：被 AI 陪伴伤害过的人，最终悟出的道理是"别让自己依赖这些平台"——她接受了"失去"，也明白了"依赖"的危险。`leave herself vulnerable to` 用一个"让自己暴露于脆弱"的短语，把 Yu Miao 的个人故事升华为对"平台依赖"的普遍警示。`In any case` 的过渡语气里带着认命与释然。

**段落逻辑：** 期待回来（17a）→ 愿意付钱（17b）→ 清醒认知（18）。从希望到接受现实。

## 长难句专项

### 🔍 原句 1（多重定语从句嵌套）

> **原句:** Her confidant is an AI agent on Doubao, a chatbot made by ByteDance, that Ms Yu has customised to mimic a dead friend of hers.

**① 找主干：** Her confidant is an AI agent
**② 找修饰：** on Doubao（地点）+ a chatbot made by ByteDance（同位语 + 过去分词）+ that Ms Yu has customised to mimic a dead friend of hers（关系从句，先行词是 agent）
**③ 找从句：** that Ms Yu has customised（关系从句）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 她的知己是一个 AI 代理，在字节跳动的 Doubao 上，被她定制为模仿一个已故朋友。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 三层定语链（位置 → 出品方 → 用户定制）一次交代清楚 AI 代理的身份与来历。

### 🔍 原句 3（in order to + 双层定语）

> **原句:** Chinese tech firms are stripping human-like personas from their chatbots in order to comply with new government rules aimed at reducing "emotional dependence" on AI.

**① 找主干：** Chinese tech firms are stripping human-like personas from their chatbots
**② 找修饰：** in order to comply with ... rules（目的状语）+ aimed at reducing ...（过去分词作定语修饰 rules）
**③ 找从句：** 无从句（只有短语）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 公司剥离人形角色，目的是遵守旨在减少情感依赖的新规。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 目的（in order to）+ 定语（aimed at）两个短语层层推进，一句交代"动作-目的-依据"。

### 🔍 原句 10（protect A from B）

> **原句:** Regulators want to protect users, particularly minors, from becoming addicted and from being driven to extreme acts of financial recklessness or self-harm.

**① 找主干：** Regulators want to protect users from ...
**② 找修饰：** particularly minors（插入同位语）；from becoming addicted and from being driven to ...（两个并列介词短语）
**③ 找从句：** 无从句
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 监管者想保护用户（尤其未成年人）免于成瘾、免于被推向财务鲁莽或自伤。
**⑤ 整体理解：** protect A from B1 and from B2 的平行结构，两个"保护对象"并列，被动"被推向"强调技术裹挟。

### 🔍 原句 15（双层定语 + before 时间状语）

> **原句:** Alibaba and ByteDance indicated that they would suspend features that allow Chinese users to create personalised AI characters before the rules took effect.

**① 找主干：** Alibaba and ByteDance indicated that they would suspend features
**② 找修饰：** that allow Chinese users to create personalised AI characters（定语从句修饰 features）；before the rules took effect（时间状语）
**③ 找从句：** that they would suspend（宾语）；that allow ...（定语）；before the rules took effect（时间）
**④ 恢复逻辑：** 两家公司表示将在新规生效前暂停允许用户创建个性化 AI 角色的功能。
**⑤ 整体理解：** 三层嵌套（宾语 + 定语 + 时间）构成"谁在何时暂停什么"的完整信息。

### 3. 重要语法

- **at once A and B**（同时是）
- **so ... that ...**（程度/结果）
- **protect A from doing**（双重介词并列）
- **prevent A doing**（防止某人做某事）
- **双重所有格**：`a dead friend of hers`
- **动名词主语**：`providing virtual companion services ... will be banned`
- **on the understanding that**（基于……的谅解）
- **倍数表达**：`nearly four times its domestic-user count`
- **would 表愿意/假设**：`she would be willing to pay half her monthly salary`

### 4. 长难句

- 原句 1（多重定语从句嵌套）
- 原句 3（in order to + 双层定语）
- 原句 10（protect A from B）
- 原句 15（双层定语 + before 时间状语）

### 5. 写作技巧

1. **以人开头、以人结尾**：从 Yu Miao 的辞职到 Yu Miao 的醒悟——用一个人的故事承载宏大政策，避免干巴巴的政策罗列
2. **政策数字具象化**：`half her monthly salary`、`$1.70 a month`、`eight or nine hours a day`——用具体数字让"情感依赖"可感
3. **双重动机的平衡笔法**：先务实（保护未成年人）后意识形态（人口危机），并承认监管收窄（narrower than an earlier draft），分析不偏颇
4. **心理学术语的双关借用**：`emotionally unavailable` 本是描述"回避感情的人"的亲密关系术语，移用于聊天机器人，一个词打通科技与心理
5. **讽刺性反差**：国内"剥离人形"、海外"without change"照卖——监管的刀只落在中国用户头上
6. **第三人称智库背书**：Zilan Qian、Matt Sheehan 的观点引述让判断有出处，保持报道客观

### 6. 可迁移表达

| 表达 | 适用场景 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `at once A and B` | 形容多重身份 | The device is at once a phone, a camera and a wallet. |
| `so ... that ...` | 强调程度 | The delay was so long that we missed the flight. |
| `comply with` | 合规表述 | All vendors must comply with the new policy. |
| `the first of its kind` | 首创性 | It is the first of its kind in the industry. |
| `part with money` | 消费行为 | Users are reluctant to part with cash. |
| `accounted for` | 数据占比 | Online sales accounted for 40% of revenue. |
| `plummeting` | 数据骤降 | Plummeting profits forced a restructuring. |
| `do little to` | 表达无效 | The subsidy did little to boost hiring. |
| `willing to pay a cost` | 权衡取舍 | The firm was willing to pay a cost for safety. |
| `on the understanding that` | 正式约定 | We proceed on the understanding that funds are secured. |
| `vulnerable to` | 风险描述 | The system is vulnerable to attack. |
| `at one's expense` | 指出代价 | The upgrade came at the user's expense. |
