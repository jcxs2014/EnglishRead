## 二、逐段精读

### 第 1 段：2019 背景 — 回顾核扩散会望

> **原句 1:** WHEN XI JINPING last visited North Korea, in 2019, international efforts to halt its nuclear weapons programme were still under way.

**中文理解：** 2019 年西迪上次访朝时，国际力图遏制核武计划的努力仍在进行。

**句子结构：** `WHEN XI JINPING last visited North Korea, [in 2019], [international efforts ... were still under way]`——When 时间从句 + 主语插入语 + 主系表。

**关键词：**
- `halt` ⭐⭐⭐ — 阻止。例句：*Halt the programme.*
- `under way` ⭐⭐⭐ — 进行中。
- `nuclear weapons programme` ⭐⭐⭐ — 核武计划。

**表达方式：** **When 抬头 + still under way 程度**：`international efforts to halt ... were still under way`——still under way（仍在进行）强调"曾有进程"。

**为什么这样写：** 2019 与 2026 强对比开篇 —— 过去有"核停滞希望"，现在完全下架 —— 由此建立时间反转。

> **原句 2:** China and Russia, North Korea's longtime patrons, had backed stiffer UN sanctions on the country as part of a American-led campaign of "maximum pressure" on its leader, Kim Jong Un.

**中文理解：** 中俄（朝鲜长期庇护所）曾在"最大压迫"运动下支持更严厉的联合国制裁，旨在施压金正恩。

**句子结构：** `China and Russia, [North Korea's longtime patrons], [had backed stiffer UN sanctions on the country] [as part of a American-led campaign of "maximum pressure" on its leader, Kim Jong Un]`——主谓 + 同位语 + 宾语 + 原因状语。

**关键词：**
- `longtime patrons` ⭐⭐⭐ — 长期庇护者。
- `stiffer UN sanctions` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更严联 sanction。
- `maximum pressure` ⭐⭐⭐ — 极大压力。
- `American-led campaign` ⭐⭐⭐ — 美主导运动。

**表达方式：** **longtime patrons + maximum pressure 双称谓**：`longtime patrons ... maximum pressure on its leader`——patrons（保护者）转为制裁压制对象——2019 年中俄合作模式。

**为什么这样写：** 2019 年中俄一致配合压制朝核 —— 现在两国 diverge（split） —— 历史对比铺开。

> **原句 3:** Mr Kim had just held two summits with Donald Trump, then in his first term in the White House.

**中文理解：** 金正恩刚刚与特朗普举办了两次峰会（彼时特朗普尚在第一任期）。

**句子结构：** `Mr Kim had just held two summits with Donald Trump, [then in his first term in the White House]`——主语 + 过去完成 + 主谓 + 时间插入语。

**关键词：**
- `summits` ⭐⭐⭐ — 峰会。

**表达方式：** **had just held + then in his first term**：`had just held two summits ... then in his first term`——just（刚刚）/ then（那时）勾勒 2019 年情境。

**为什么这样写：** 把 Kim-Xi 与当下的 Trump-Kim 对比 —— 2019 的"双 summit optimism" vs 2026 的"nukes off agenda"。

> **原句 4:** And though the second of those summits ended in failure, Mr Xi expressed hope on his North Korean visit that the process would continue, commending Mr Kim on his supposed efforts to denuclearise the Korean peninsula.

**中文理解：** 尽管第二次峰会以失败告终，西迪在访朝时仍表达了对进程的期望，并赞扬金正恩在"去核化半岛"上的(所谓)努力。

**句子结构：** `And though ... ended in failure, [Mr Xi expressed hope ... that the process would continue] [recommending Mr Kim on his supposed efforts to denuclearise the Korean peninsula]`——though 让步 + 主谓 + that 从句 + 分词状语。

**关键词：**
- `ended in failure` ⭐⭐⭐ — 失败收场。
- `commended` ⭐⭐⭐ — 称赞。
- `supposed efforts` ⭐⭐⭐ — 所谓努力。
- `denuclearise` ⭐⭐⭐ — 去核。

**表达方式：** **though 让步 + commended ... on + supposed 讽刺**：`though ... ended in failure ... commending Mr Kim on his supposed efforts`——supposed（所谓）讽刺 Xi 2019 仍在鼓励 ——"嘉奖未能兑现的努力"。

**为什么这样写：** Xi 2019 年对 Kim 的"称赞"（commended）带上 sarcastic 语气（supposed efforts）——铺第 2019→2026 的反差

**段落逻辑：** 2019 回顾 (WHEN Xi last visited / China+Russia backing maximum pressure / two Trump-Kim summits / second failed but Xi still commends)。完成"过去基线"。> **原句 5:** China's leader harboured no such hopes on his second visit to North Korea, which ended on June 9th.

**中文理解：** 中国领导人在他第二次访朝时不再怀有这样的希望——这次访问于 6 月 9 日结束。

**句子结构：** `China's leader harboured no such hopes [on his second visit to North Korea, [which ended on June 9th]]`——主谓 + such hopes + 时间/定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `harboured no such hopes` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不再怀有这样的希望。
- `such hopes` ⭐⭐⭐ — 那样的希望。

**表达方式：** **harboured + such hopes + which 定语**：`harboured no such hopes ... which ended on June 9th`——harboured（怀抱）+ such（那么）+ which（6 月 9 日）——与第 1 段的 "expressed hope" 直接对比。

**为什么这样写：** 2019（expressed hope）→ 2026（harboured no such hopes）——态度彻底反转。which 引导的定语补充时间，让"反转"有具体锚点。> **原句 5:** One of his priorities was to counterbalance Russian influence there, which has grown much stronger since Mr Kim sent troops to fight against Ukraine in 2024.

**中文理解：** 他的优先事项之一是 counterbalance 俄罗斯在那里日益增强的影响（自 2024 年金正恩派兵参战乌克兰以来）。

**句子结构：** `One of his priorities was to counterbalance Russian influence there, [which has grown much stronger since Mr Kim sent troops [to fight against Ukraine in 2024]]`——One of ... + 主系 + to 目的不定式 + which 定语从句 + since 从句。

**关键词：**
- `counterbalance` ⭐⭐⭐ — 平衡抵消。
- `priorities` ⭐⭐⭐ — 优先事项。
- `sent troops to fight against` ⭐⭐⭐ — 派兵参战。

**表达方式：** **One of ... + counterbalance + since**：`One of his priorities was to counterbalance Russian influence ... since Mr Kim sent troops`——counterbalance（平衡）+ since（自从）——把"派兵打乌克兰"当作俄影响扩张的转折点。

**为什么这样写：** Xi 访朝首要目的不是核问题，而是 counterbalance 俄罗斯 —— "朝核下架换俄罗斯上架"的战略置换。

> **原句 6:** Mr Xi also aimed to reassert China's clout as North Korea's primary economic partner in case Mr Trump renews his diplomatic outreach to Mr Kim, as many observers expect.

**中文理解：** 西迪还以巩固中国作为朝鲜主要经济伙伴作用，以防万一特朗普（众人预期如此）恢复对金正恩的外交邀请。

**句子结构：** `Mr Xi also aimed to reassert China's clout [as North Korea's primary economic partner] [in case Mr Trump renews his diplomatic outreach to Mr Kim, as many observers expect]`——主谭 + 同位语 + 条件状语 + as 让步。

**关键词：**
- `clout` ⭐⭐⭐ — 影响力。
- `reassert` ⭐⭐⭐ — 重新确立。
- `primary economic partner` ⭐⭐⭐ — 主要经济伙伴。
- `diplomatic outreach` ⭐⭐⭐ — 外交邀请。

**表达方式：** **clout + in case 预防 + as 众人预期**：`reassert China's clout ... in case Mr Trump renews ... as many observers expect`——in case（以防）+ as（如众人所料）——战略防备 Trump 回归。

**为什么这样写：** 写出 Xi 的双重策略：reassert clout（确立影响）+ in case Trump outreach（防范）——围棋式布局。

> **原句 7:** Some even speculate that Mr Xi may have been conveying overtures from Mr Trump.

**中文理解：** 甚至有人推测西迪可能是转达特朗普的邀约。

**句子结构：** `Some even speculate that Mr Xi may have been conveying overtures from Mr Trump`——Some even speculate + that 宾语从句（may have been doing）。

**关键词：**
- `conveying overtures` ⭐⭐⭐ — 转达邀约。
- `speculate` ⭐⭐⭐ — 推测。

**表达方式：** **Some even speculate + may have been conveying**：`Some even speculate that ... may have been conveying overtures from Mr Trump`——even（强调）+ speculate（猜测）→ conveying overtures（传递邀约）——推测层叠。

**为什么这样写：** "some even speculate" 把 "Xi 可能捎 Trump 话" 上升为"众人猜测"——暗示 Trump-Xi-Kim 三方博弈。

> **原句 8:** But stopping North Korea's nuclear weapons programme—which is far more advanced than Iran's—appears to have fallen off China's agenda.

**中文理解：** 但阻止朝鲜核武计划（远远先于伊朗）似已从中国的议事日程中删除。

**句子结构：** `But [stopping North Korea's nuclear-weapons programme—[which is far more advanced than Iran's]—appears to have fallen off China's agenda]`——But 转折 + 主谭（appear + have fallen off）。

**关键词：**
- `fallen off the agenda` ⭐⭐⭐ — 落议。
- `far more advanced than` ⭐⭐⭐ — 远超。
- `nuclear-weapons programme` ⭐⭐⭐ — 核武计划。

**表达方式：** **fallen off the agenda + em-dash 括号 + which 比较**：`appears to have fallen off China's agenda—which is far more advanced than Iran's`——fallen off agenda（脱离日程）+ em-dash（em-dash 括号）——"核问题从议事表掉下"中心论题 + 朝核>伊核。

**为什么这样写：** 转折段尾："核问题落议"（fallen off the agenda）+ em-dash 强调朝核远超伊核 —— 2026 政策彻底背离 2019。

**段落逻辑：** 2026 对比 2019（harboured no such hopes / 6 月 9 日结束）→ Xi 新目的 counterbalance 俄罗斯 → 预防 Trump outreach (reassert clout / in case renew) → 推测 Xi 捎 Trump (some even speculate conveying overtures) → 核问题从议事表落下 (fallen off agenda —— which is far more advanced than Iran's)。

### 第 3 段：官方话语 —— 历史关系与盟义

> **原句 10:** Neither China nor North Korea mentioned denuclearisation in their official accounts of the visit.

**中文理解：** 在官方描述中，中朝双方均未提及去核化。

**句子结构：** `Neither China nor North Korea mentioned denuclearisation in their official accounts of the visit`——Neither A nor B + 主谭 + 同位语。

**关键词：**
- `official accounts` ⭐⭐⭐ — 官方描述。
- `Neither ... nor` ⭐⭐⭐ — 两者皆不。

**表达方式：** **Neither A nor B 双重否定**：`Neither China nor North Korea mentioned denuclearisation`——Neither...nor（双重否定）——"去核化彻底缺席"。

**为什么这样写：** "Neither ... mentioned"（双双不提）强调核问题在官话里的完全消失。

> **原句 11:** Instead they emphasised the historical ties between the two countries: the pair fought together in the Korean war and then signed a mutual-defence treaty in 1961.

**中文理解：** 代之以强调两国间的历史关系：他们一起参战朝鲜战争，随后又签署 1961 年的 mutual-defence treaty。

**句子结构：** `Instead [they emphasised the historical ties between the two countries: [the pair fought together in the Korean war and then signed a mutual-defence treaty in 1961]]`——Instead 转折 + 主谕 + 冒号 + 并列举例。

**关键词：**
- `Instead` ⭐⭐⭐ — 代替。
- `emphasised the historical ties` ⭐⭐⭐ — 强调历史关系。
- `mutual-defence treaty` ⭐⭐⭐ — mutual-defence treaty。
- `fought together` ⭐⭐⭐ — 共同参战。

**表达方式：** **Instead + 冒号举例（fought together / signed treaty）**：`Instead they emphasised the historical ties between the two countries: the pair fought together ... and then signed`——Instead（代替去核）+ 冒号列举（共战 + 条约）——替"核"换"史交"。

**为什么这样写：** 核问题→历史关系的转向，用 "Instead" 作桥头堡——战争条约 (Korean war / 1961 treaty) = 伊朗关系的"硬柄"。

> **原句 12:** Mr Kim said strengthening the relationship was "the most important top-priority strategic work" and pledged to support China's claim to Taiwan.

**中文理解：** 金正恩称增进关系是"最重要的最优先的战略工作"，并承诺支持中国对台湾的主张。

**句子结构：** `Mr Kim said [strengthening the relationship was "the most important top-priority strategic work"] and pledged to support China's claim to Taiwan`——主谕 + 引语 + and pledged + 宾。

**关键词：**
- `top-priority` ⭐⭐⭐ — 头等重要。
- `strategic work` ⭐⭐⭐ — 战略功课。
- `claim to Taiwan` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对台声明。

**表达方式：** **最高级叠加 + 引号重复**：`"the most important top-priority strategic work"`——most important + top-priority 叠加——"战略性地拉高关系"。

**为什么这样写：** Kim 用"top-priority" 重复表达对中朝关系重要性，并顺水支持台湾——朝核下架换盟义。

> **原句 13:** Mr Xi said they reached an "important consensus" and called for closer economic and military ties and stronger strategic co-ordination.

**中文理解：** 西迪称双方达成"重要共识"，并呼吁加强经济、军事关系以及战略协调。

**句子结构：** `Mr Xi said [they reached an "important consensus"] and called for [closer economic and military ties and stronger strategic co-ordination]`——主谕 + 引语 + and called for + 并列名词短语。

**关键词：**
- `important consensus` ⭐⭐⭐ — 重要共识。
- `co-ordination` ⭐⭐⭐ — 协调。

**表达方式：** **important consensus + called for 邀请型**：`reached an "important consensus" ... called for closer ... and stronger ...` —— consensus（共识）→ called for（呼吁）——"达成 → 呼吁深化"的外交逻辑。

**为什么这样写：** 官方语言两两配对：Kim（pledged support to Taiwan）↔ Xi（reached consensus, called for closer ties）——朝中战略联帝确认。

**段落逻辑 / 合并段 3-4 逻辑：** 官方话语对比 (Neither mentioned denuclearisation → Instead emphasised historical ties → Korean war + 1961 treaty / Kim pledged + Xi called for) → 俄罗斯干预 (Putin partly to blame → In exchange for tormenting Ukraine → signed new treaty → in effect accepted nuclear / Lavrov called "closed issue") → 风险评估（All this unsettles Xi / Like Putin worried unified Korea / Yet Putin not share Xi's concern / Nor Russia worry / Xi still worries / China cannot / America cannot / Kim not committed / tested ICBMs / Iran war intensified determination to retain）

> **原句 14:** Russia's president, Vladimir Putin, is partly to blame for China's shifting priorities.

**中文理解：** 俄罗斯总统普京在一定程度上要对中国政策转变负责。

**句子结构：** `Russia's president, Vladimir Putin, is partly to blame for China's shifting priorities`——主语 + 同位 + 主系 + for 因为。

**关键词：**
- `partly to blame` ⭐⭐⭐ — 部分负责。
- `shifting priorities` ⭐⭐⭐ — 优先顺序变化。

**表达方式：** **partly to blame for**：`Putin is partly to blame for China's shifting priorities`——partly（部分）+ to blame（负责）——把俄罗斯标为"政策扰动因"。

**为什么这样写：** 开段就把俄罗斯定位为"政策扰动因"（partly to blame）——西方逻辑: external factor。

> **原句 15:** In exchange for help tormenting Ukraine, Russia has provided financial and other assistance that has bolstered North Korea's economy and military build-up.

**中文理解：** 为获得对乌克兰的扰乱所助，一方面，俄罗斯提供了财政及其他援助，从而支持朝鲜的经济和军事发展。

**句子结构：** `In exchange for help tormenting Ukraine, [Russia has provided ... assistance that has bolstered North Korea's economy and military build-up]`——In exchange for + 主谭 + that 定语。

**关键词：**
- `In exchange for` ⭐⭐⭐ — 作为交换。
- `tormenting Ukraine` ⭐⭐⭐ — 纠缠乌克兰。
- `bolstered` ⭐⭐⭐ — 增强、巩固。
- `military build-up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 军事扩军。

**表达方式：** **In exchange for ... + bolstered**：`In exchange for help tormenting Ukraine, Russia has provided ... bolstering North Korea`——交换逻辑 + bolstered（巩固）——俄-朝互助链条。

**为什么这样写：** 直写"交换关系"：俄罗斯用援助换朝鲜经浻军 —— "利益链"推动核扩。

> **原句 16:** The two countries have signed a new mutual-defence treaty.

**中文理解：** 两国签订了新的 mutual-defence treaty。

**句子结构：** `The two countries have signed a new mutual-defence treaty`——主谕。

**关键词：**
- `mutual-defence treaty` ⭐⭐⭐ — mutual-defence treaty（重复）。

**表达方式：** **简单陈述下冲击**：`The two countries have signed a new mutual-defence treaty`——短句+ new——直接落定"军事同盟"。

**为什么这样写：** 核外交转入军事同盟层面—— treaty（条约）升级政治绑定。

> **原句 17:** In addition, Russia has in effect accepted North Korea's nuclear status.

**中文理解：** 此外，俄罗斯实际上已经接受朝鲜的核国地位。

**句子结构：** `In addition, Russia has in effect accepted North Korea's nuclear status`——In addition + 主谭 + in effect + 宾。

**关键词：**
- `in effect` ⭐⭐⭐ — 事实上。
- `nuclear status` ⭐⭐⭐ — 核国地位。

**表达方式：** **In addition + in effect 承接**：`In addition, Russia has in effect accepted`——In addition（此外）+ in effect（事实上）——逐层推进俄罗斯认核。

**为什么这样写：** "in effect accepted"（事实承认）远强于正式承认——把俄罗斯绑在朝核认同上。

> **原句 18:** Sergei Lavrov, Russia's foreign minister, has called it a "closed issue".

**中文理解：** 俄罗斯外交部长拉夫罗夫称其为一个"已解决的问题"。

**句子结构：** `Sergei Lavrov, [Russia's foreign minister], has called it a "closed issue"`——主语 + 同位 + 主谭 + 宾。

**关键词：**
- `closed issue` ⭐⭐⭐ — 已解决问题。

**表达方式：** **closed issue 定语 + 官员公开**：`Lavrov ... has called it a "closed issue"`——外长公开表态——"核问题不再可议"。

**为什么这样写：** 用 Lavrov 这个官将公开表态“nukes are closed”，把俄罗斯对朝核的接受推向外交不可逆。---

### 第 5 段：风险评估 —— 俄罗斯不同步

> **原句 19:** All this unsettles Mr Xi, despite his own support for Russia's war.

**句子结构：** `China is also keen to gain access via North Korea to the Sea of Japan, says Mr Zhao`——主系 + 同位语。

**关键词：**
- `keen to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 热望。
- `gain access via` ⭐⭐⭐ — 通过……获取通道。

**表达方式：** **keen to + via**：`China is also keen to gain access via North Korea to the Sea of Japan`——keen（热望）+ via（经由）——“地图战略”。

**为什么这样写：** 把战略目的写简：“Sea of Japan access via North Korea” —— 地图利益。

**中文理解：** 尽管中国近期试图通过改善跨境基础设施来振兴经济关系，金正恩似乎对这些努力进展缓慢。

**句子结构：** `Although China has recently tried to revive its economic links [by improving cross-border infrastructure], [Mr Kim appears to have been slow-rolling those efforts]`——Although 让步 + 主谭 + by 方式 + appears to have been doing。

**关键词：**
- `revive its economic links` ⭐⭐⭐ — 复苏经贸关系。
- `slow-rolling` ⭐⭐⭐ — 拖延、磨延（slang）。
- `cross-border infrastructure` ⭐⭐⭐ — 跨境基础设施。

**表达方式：** **Although + appears to have been doing（进行时态）**：`Although China has recently tried to revive ... Mr Kim appears to have been slow-rolling`——Although（尽管）+ appears to have been（z状态进行态）——“China 推足，Kim 磨蹭”。

**为什么这样写：** slow-rolling（懒散拖延）描写 Kim 对中经合的消极应对——“态度软的同时拖延”。

> **原句 41:** Direct flights and train journeys between Beijing and Pyongyang, which were suspended during the pandemic, resumed in March.

**句子结构：** `Direct flights and train journeys between Beijing and Pyongyang, [which were suspended during the pandemic], resumed in March`——主谭 + who/which 定语 + 时间。

**关键词：**
- `resumed` ⭐⭐⭐ — 恢复。
- `during the pandemic` ⭐⭐⭐ — 在疫情期间。

**表达方式：** **which 定语（插入） + resumed**：`which were suspended during the pandemic, resumed in March`——插入“suspended during pandemic”+ resumed（恢复）——恢复进度。

**为什么这样写：** March resumed（3 月恢复） vs yet Kim slow-rolling——"恢复名义 vs 实际拖延"矛盾。

> **原句 42:** But North Korea has yet to let Chinese tourists return.

**中文理解：** 但朝鲜仍未允许中国游客返回。

**句子结构：** `But North Korea has yet to let Chinese tourists return`——But + 主谭 + has yet to。

**关键词：**
- `has yet to` ⭐⭐⭐ — 仍未……（否定含义）。

**表达方式：** **But + has yet to**：`But North Korea has yet to let Chinese tourists return`——But（但是）+ has yet to（仍未）——“口连通、客不进”。

**关键词：**

**关键词：**

- `nuclear-armed South Korea` ⭐⭐⭐ — 核武韩国。

**表达方式：** **Some scholars suggest + could tolerate**：`Some Chinese scholars suggest China could tolerate a nuclear-armed South Korea`——Some scholars suggest（学者称）+ could tolerate（可以容忍）——政策开放性。

> **原句 47:** That is because China hopes that, if South Korea develops the weapons, they would not be directed at Chinese targets and that South Korea's alliance with America might weaken.

**中文理解：** 原因是中国希望：如果韩国研发核武，它们不会指向中国目标，并且韩美同盟可能会削弱。

**句子结构：** `That is because China hopes that [, if South Korea develops the weapons, [they would not be directed at Chinese targets] and [that South Korea's alliance with America might weaken]]`——That is because + that 从句（双重条件+并列）。

**关键词：**
- `directed at` ⭐⭐⭐ — 指向。
- `alliance` ⭐⭐⭐ — 同盟。
- `might weaken` ⭐⭐⭐ — 可能会削弱。

**表达方式：** **because + hopes that + if ... would + and that might**：`China hopes that, if ... would not be directed and that ... might weaken`——because（因为）+ hopes (盼望) + if（条件）-—"核指向不对 + 同盟弱化" 双重算计。

**为什么这样写：** 把 nuclear-armed South Korea 算作"间接盟"——"不会指向中国 + 韩美同盟弱化" —— 战略牵一发而动全身。

> **原句 48:** China would be far less tolerant of Japan acquiring atomic arms, but thinks it is less likely because of domestic opposition there.

**中文理解：** 但中国对日本获得核武的容忍度要低得多，认为这不太可能，因为当地有国内反对势力。

**句子结构：** `China would be far less tolerant of Japan acquiring atomic arms, but [thinks it is less likely [because of domestic opposition there]]`——but 转折 + 主谭 + because。

**关键词：**
- `far less tolerant` ⭐⭐⭐ — 难以容忍。
- `domestic opposition` ⭐⭐⭐ — 国内反对。

**表达方式：** **far less ... but thinks ... less likely because**：`would be far less tolerant ... but thinks it is less likely because of domestic opposition`——far less tolerant（极不愿意）+ but thinks less likely（却认为不太可能）——朝鲜/日本双差别。

**为什么这样写：** 核扩容的双重态度：South Korea “tolerate”（可容忍）vs Japan “far less tolerant”（难以容忍）-— 历史仇怨逻辑。

**段落逻辑：** Xi 应对 (Xi also seems to think he can handle fallout) → 中专家预测 (Chinese experts say unlikely to upset) → 韩国政策 (left-wing Seodaemun + staged approach) → 学者建议 (tolerate nuclear South Korea) → if they would not be directed ... might weaken → far less tolerant of Japan because of domestic opposition). 第 11-12 段完成"核扩容政策"。

## 十二、精读结束总结

### 1. 核心主题
- "China's shifting priorities"（中国政策转变）
- 核政策悬而未决：tacit acceptance（默认）
- 战略布局：counterbalance Russia / complicate America / gain Sea of Japan access

### 2. 写作技巧
1. **“从 2019 到 2026”的时间反转对比**：2019 Xi expressed hope → 2026 harboured no such hopes.
2. **“官方话语消失”的微观分析**：readouts "did not mention denuclearisation" —— 官话的沉默即决策变化。
3. **“多方背书”堆砌**：Experts/Niu + think-tank/affiliated/government-linked/White House/Trump/PUTIN/Seoul left-wing ——官话链条。
4. **“战略目的三重叠”**：counterbalance Russia / complicate America's military planning / gain access to Sea of Japan.
5. **“核指向策略”的冷逻辑**：tolerate nuclear-armed South Korea (not be directed) / far less tolerant of Japan.
6. **“结尾三点反转”**：America bogged down / NK firepower enough / Kim position never stronger.
7. **“数据堆砌”**：50-60 warheads / 28,500 troops / 10-20 annually —— 数字即证据。

### 3. 可迁移表达
- **“从 X 到 Y”时间对比**：*When X last did Y, ... But on his second trip ...*。
- **“harboured no such hopes”**：表达态度转变。
- **“counterbalance influence”**：战略动词。
- **“in exchange for ... assistance”**：交换关系的客体+动宾。
- **“fallen off the agenda”**：政策下车术语（非常贴切）。
- **“tacitly accepted”**：默认状态。
- **“has yet to”**：政策“未完成”。
- **“has never looked stronger”**：绝境反弹。
- **“bogged down”**：外部事务陷入困境。
- **“exploit tensions between A and B”**：利用他国矛盾。
- **“made no reference to”**：官方话语删减。
