Jo drives east, through the early-morning dark in her uncle’s rust-bucket Renault || Jo 凌晨开叔叔的锈皮雷诺向东赶往祖母家；伦敦的雪积不起来，轮胎在没撒防滑砂的小路上打滑，Granny 仍无音讯、不接电话。
But when Jo woke at four, disquiet set in, sharpening as the minutes ticked past. She tried again. Straight to voicemail. || Jo 凌晨四点惊醒，不安随分钟加剧；再拨祖母电话，直接进入语音信箱，她索性起身穿衣，赶在破晓时分上路。
By the porch she stops, surprised to see a parcel tucked under the boot rack, hand-labelled in a familiar bossy cursive || 门廊靴架下塞着一个未被取走的包裹，贴着熟悉的专横草写手条「Deliveries HERE if no answer! Thank you!」。
But … hang on, the courier confirmed dispatch yesterday afternoon. Why hasn’t Granny seen it? || 包裹昨天下午已确认发出却至今无人取走；Jo 见寄件标签先如释重负（觉得「还赶得上」），随即起疑：为什么祖母还没看到它。
Inside, Dolly barks frantically. Grabbing the spare key, badly hidden under a terracotta pot, she lets herself in. Dolly leaps up, agitated, her coat damp. || Dolly 在门内狂吠；Jo 抓起陶盆下藏得不好的备用钥匙自行进门；Dolly 扑跳、焦躁、一身湿毛。
Jo opens the door – unlocked – and steps outside into the frozen air, catches a faint bonfire smell. Dolly noses the back of her knee, herding her into the garden, past the potting shed, towards the potager. || 后门没锁，Jo 走进冷空气闻到一丝篝火味；Dolly 用鼻子顶她膝弯，把她往花园、经育苗棚、朝菜园方向引。
Then, near the compost heap … a snow-covered leg on the path, a twist of chicken wire, a shattered phone, a scatter of newspaper and magazine pages and letters, thin blue airmail paper. The shortbread tin! || 堆肥堆旁的小路上露出被雪覆盖的一条腿，旁边有扭成一团的铁丝网、摔碎的手机、散落的报刊内页与信件、薄薄的蓝色航空信纸——还有那只短饼干锡。
She frantically palms her grandmother’s cheek, grey, the skin strangely firm, her lips lilac. Snow and mud on her hat, a gash on her chin, blood dried in her eyebrow, congealed in the lashes. She’s been here a while. Shaking with shock, Jo tries to lift her, but she’s too heavy. Cradling her grandmother’s head in her lap, she fumbles for her phone and dials 999. || Jo 摸到祖母的脸——灰白、皮肤硬得出奇、嘴唇青紫；帽子上有雪和泥，下巴有伤口，眉骨的血已干结在睫毛里，她判断祖母已在这儿躺了一阵；她想抱起祖母却抱不动，便把祖母的头搂在自己腿上，摸出手机拨打 999。
