EXCERPT FROM 1000 WAYS TO DIE: AN EMT’S HANDBOOK || 本章是 EMT 手册「溺死」条目的摘录。
death by drowning is rarely immediate—it is a progressive process caused by oxygen deprivation. || 溺死很少立即致死——它是缺氧造成的渐进过程。
Contact with water can trigger laryngospasm, a reflex closure of the vocal cords. || 触水可触发喉痉挛——声带反射性闭合。
Contrary to popular depiction, drowning victims rarely shout for help. The body’s entire effort is focused on trying to breathe. || 与流行描写相反，溺水者很少呼救——身体全部精力都在试图呼吸。
Death occurs not because the lungs fill with water, but because oxygen can no longer reach the blood. || 死因不是肺里灌满水，而是氧气到不了血液。
the process of “drowning,” and the associated pain and other negative experiences, can be prolonged. Extensively, in some cases. || 若气道浸没是间歇或部分的，「溺死」与相关痛苦可以被延长——某些案例中 extensively。
