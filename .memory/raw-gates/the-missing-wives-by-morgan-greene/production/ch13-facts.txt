INVESTIGATIVE LIMITS REGARDING MATERIAL WITNESSES || 条款主题：关于实质性证人的调查限制。
Officers may request, but not compel, a witness to remain on scene || 无令状时与证人的一切互动严格自愿——可请求、不可强迫。
A witness may terminate contact at any time. || 证人可随时终止接触；造成「不得离开」错觉的权威展示=非法逮捕。
all questioning must cease immediately. || 律师代表非嫌疑人证人到场——所有问话必须立即停止。
Statements obtained after counsel has requested release are presumed coerced || 律师要求放人后取得的陈述推定胁迫、可被排除。
if legal counsel is present to escort a witness, the officer shall offer no resistance. || 律师到场护送证人——警官不得阻拦。
