‘I will not take nude photographs for you, nor will I allow my friend to debase herself for your perverse pleasure.’ || 辛西娅以为辛克莱要她们拍裸照，当场拒绝。
I wouldn’t require you to be in any state of undress. You may stay exactly as you are… with a few minor changes. || 辛克莱说不用脱衣，只要按他的要求稍作改变。
‘Intimate photos. Isn’t that right, Sinclair?’ || 海伦娜点破他要的是两人亲密的照片。
‘Kills two birds with one stone, dunnit? I ’ave bargaining power over both of you.’ || 辛克莱承认照片能同时拿捏她们两人。
I can ensure these pictures never see the light of day. As long as you grant me a few favours from time to time. || 辛克莱以永不公开照片为条件，换取她们日后的“帮忙”。
‘I want Hubert’s letters of debt before I agree. All of them.’ || 辛西娅答应之前，先索要休伯特的全部债据。
‘I can ruin the film.’ || 海伦娜私下告诉辛西娅，她能毁掉底片。
‘I would drink the Thames dry before I let that man have any power over me. Or you.’ || 海伦娜说宁可喝干泰晤士河，也不让辛克莱掌控她或辛西娅。
