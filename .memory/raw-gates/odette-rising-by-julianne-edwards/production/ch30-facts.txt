it was shockingly magnificent || Beauclerc 巴黎探新翼：黑门变玻璃圆面、绿铁艺郁金香、大教堂式二层窗——「a riposte to the classical sensibilities」；「the future: optimistic, exploratory, undeniably womanly」。
She may confound us yet. || 工厂侧写：无经理、Dupont 瘦削苦撑盼他回归；Beauclerc 拍肩留话并内心承认：以为她会 fold——「even if she'd made mistakes... she was brave」；自悔 left them in the lurch。
It was a summons from Victor Gagnon || 一月冰封：陌生信使送来工整字迹的召唤函——Le Neptune 之约；「So, he knew about her after all」；她的恐惧是「新生活被 defiled」。
brutal and fetal || Gagnon 真容：壮硕、heavy-lidded、光滑粉额——brutal 且 fetal；Versailles 式仆童眼神 dead；声音却是 refined singsong；sweat 与 sandalwood。
I was happy to settle your bill that evening || 匿名恩人揭晓＝Gagnon：他早知那晚的 damsel 是谁（「早知就不那么慷慨」）；粗鄙性辱 Berthe——「still fragrant」「Too old, too well used」「what a shame about her face…」。
He skewered her like I have this mollusk. || 逻辑战：她坚持「父亲买下的是自由」——他大笑「foolish woman，他要的是拥有」、以叉上牡蛎比划 skewer；她怒吼 Shut up。
Twenty-five thousand francs. || 勒索加码：2.5 万法郎（她心算=十年经理薪资）——「然后你会放过我们所有人？」——他还要「对称」的第二根柱子：「I want your boy. The blonde one. Merry.」
looking into an abyss || 归家布防：探 Merry 家底（只有母亲、无父无亲）并许一日假探母；夜深指掐大腿——深渊感：「不能把 Merry 送给他，可不送又怕新全家遭殃」。
