had also cut out her spirit || 疗养期：Berthe 住进父亲旧床（mattress like stone、点名要 velvet 枕）——伤愈合而神被吓走（闻客即惊）；今日「几乎对我无礼」=开始复原。
He did it for fun, as a parting blow to Berthe. || Grimard 摊牌：Berthe 曾陷于一个 dangerous man（她推断 pimp）；遇 Edouard 后想赎身去爱——「Men like that care nothing for love」；父亲能做的只有 pay him off；毁容是 parting blow、for fun。
protected by the men he has corrupted || Victor Gagnon 其人：权势通天、腐蚀警察、violence 传闻累累——「Best we keep our heads down」；Ned 许诺守护她、Berthe 与 Feld 孩子们；「他会不会伤害孩子」之问无答案。
It will always be raw. || 镜子时刻：「Prepare me. How bad is it?」「It will always be raw」——她端详长疤、目光飘远、无言还镜；长颈依旧优雅、表情读不出。
I was its princess. || Doudou 童年：加勒比小岛小镇的公主；老师 Anchou 分她半块饼干；祖母唤她「Aphrodite of the Windward Islands」；Amélie 一拳打肿她的鼻子——「让你知道没有漂亮脸蛋你并不特别」。
Not a good father, perhaps, but he had love in his heart. || Edouard 判词：他总说她 without equal、beyond compare；她死前刚开始相信「他爱的是美之外的东西」——「可他走了，我的脸也没了」；「It is the end of my life. This life.」
I was taken to a brothel. || 真正身世：Gagnon 以「Uncle」之名来岛、许诺 ladies' maid 正差；父亲收钱放人（「Money! And I was trouble——直到我开始带刀」）；13 岁被送进妓院；姐姐 Grace 仍如奴隶困在岛上。
You took on his feelings of badness. || 相片抵达：Grant 全家福中母亲与她 mirror image——「They stared at one another through the years, meeting at last」；Ned 关键句：「He did not hate you. He was afraid of you... You took on his feelings of badness」——她把父亲的自我憎恨扛了二十年，「Perhaps it's time to let them go」；相片归她所有。
