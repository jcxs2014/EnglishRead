He was unhappy. Unmoored. || 1898 年末 Lille：Legrand 欲退休让厂（含养老条款）他迟迟不接；丧妻之痛+不适应；父 summer 亡故（perfunctory）、农场已卖、母依姐居。
dismissed Odette Huré as a fool || 迟来的忏悔：他判瓷与袜为 fripperies、判 Odette 为 fool、逼走全家（thin way、skating on the surface）——「He had let them all down」；此行是为寻「归属感」。
She had built a fantastical beast || 城堡真容：尖塔（ecclesiastical 风格）、无一处矩形门窗（曲线如叶、各不相同）——古典对称老宅的 antithesis；儿子 Giggle——久违的笑声。
she must have a curly mind || 父子问答：Is he a wizard?→She；「she must have a curly mind」；「Very, very, very much. I would like a house like this.」
an angel with a bright halo || 门灯次第亮起如宅醒；Odette 执图纸立于光中——plaited 金发 glowing、otherworldly 天使；他翻案：「blamed her for his fear and loneliness」→「quite magnificent, full, and beautiful. Yes, beautiful.」
I have nightmares where I suffocate in blood || Joseph 丧母（consumption 吐血）噩梦「在血中窒息」；Odette 分享自己的狼梦——「They stop eventually」——男孩眼中重现生机。
To the mothers, fathers, friends, and mice || 平安夜之邀：blinis、生蚝、香槟；无树、礼物只给小孩与狗——「向逝者举杯：母亲、父亲、朋友，与被深爱、被永远想念的鼠」；父亲内心：To return. If she would have him, he would come home.
