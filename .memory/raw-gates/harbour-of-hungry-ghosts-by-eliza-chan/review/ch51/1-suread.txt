Hard to believe with his knee pressed upon the chest of a dead man. He fell backwards, scrambling away, hands stained with blood. Horrified, as if he only now realised Reverend Nairns was dead. || 阿奇跪压在纳恩思牧师尸体的胸口上；她一问，他就往后跌倒爬开、双手沾血，一脸惊恐，仿佛此刻才意识到牧师已经死了。
“It was you. It was always you.” His disappearance at the Hungry Ghost Festival. At the opium den and the racecourse. || Kiamling 当面点破「一直是你」：把他在饿鬼节、鸦片馆和赛马场的失踪串起来，认定暗中跟踪她的凶兽就是阿奇。
“It’s for the greater good,” he said. “Whose good?” Before he could answer, his arm grew bulbous. || 阿奇辩称这一切「是为了大局」，Kiamling 反问「是谁的大局」；他还没答，手臂就鼓胀起来。
A small white snake dangled by its fangs from his wrist. He tossed it into the grass. His legs grew then too. || 一条小白蛇用毒牙挂在他手腕上，他把蛇甩进草丛；紧接着他的双腿也开始变形。
Sharp fangs protruded from his mouth and his eyes turned red as flame. Kiamling looked for a trace of humanity in him, for a mote of the Archie she knew. || 阿奇獠牙外突、双眼红如火焰；Kiamling 在他身上找不到一丝她认识的那个阿奇。
The shadow beast roared, the furnace heat of his mouth inches from her face. Closer he stalked. Closer still. Nose pressed against her exposed neck. || 变身后的影兽咆哮着一步步逼近她，口鼻的热气喷到她脸上，鼻子抵住她裸露的脖颈。
In the distance the whistles and barked orders of British officers. The church bells had been enough to wake the dead; they had certainly been enough to wake the living. || 远处传来英国军官的哨声与喝令；钟声既惊醒了死人，也惊醒了活人。
The lychgate at the entrance to the cemetery looked eerily like a doorway. That was how the British dead had returned as geongsi. || 墓地入口的停柩门看上去像一道门；按她的说法，英国死者正是这样变成 geongsi 回来的。
Kiamling curled her left fist, feeling a length of red thread from the broken coin sword still wrapped around her fingers. She called to the scattered coins, lying in the cemetery around her. || Kiamling 攥紧左拳，摸到断铜钱剑的红线仍缠在指间；她召唤散落在墓地各处的铜钱。
They trembled on the ground, resisting her. She was still out of qi. The teeth of the shadow beast scratched at her neck. || 铜钱在地上颤动、抗拒她的召唤，因为她仍未恢复 qi；影兽的牙同时刮着她的脖子。
The coins responded, pinging back up onto the thread in her hand. She snapped it forward like a whip, the coins lashing out against the beast’s flank. || 她不顾折寿硬催 qi，铜钱应声飞回她手里的红线上；她像甩鞭一样把铜钱抽在兽的侧腹。
He flinched, teeth biting down in response. A line of blood dripped down her collar from where he had broken her skin. Then he turned tail and ran. || 兽被抽得退缩、反口咬破她的脖子，血顺着她的衣领流下；随后它转身逃走。
Kiamling’s legs collapsed under her and she slumped to the ground. The spent coins and thread were ash in her hands. || Kiamling 双腿一软瘫坐在地；用尽的铜钱和红线在她手里化成了灰。
Around her, dozens of white snakelets curved through the cemetery ruins. Their forked tongues tasted the air as they surrounded her, coiling up and twisting through her fingers. || 几十条小白蛇在墓地废墟间游走，吐着分叉的舌头把她围住，还缠上她的手指。
Hoi gor traipsed after them, one of the snakelets wrapped around his neck like a collar, the serpent’s diamond head upon his shoulder. Of course he had kept them. || Hoi gor 跟着蛇群走来，一条小蛇像项圈一样缠在他脖子上、蛇头搭在肩头——她这才明白蛇一直是他养着的。
He said nothing. He searched her face for the answer she could not articulate, and doused the pain in a wordless embrace. || 她以为 Hoi gor 要骂她鲁莽，但他一言未发，只在她脸上寻找她说不出口的答案，用一个无言的拥抱压下了她的痛。
