The trail to Victoria Peak was a rough path snaking sharply upwards. Conversation between the four of them lulled until they reached a plateau. || Kiamling、Jingling、Hoi gor 与 Archie 四人沿太平山顶的崎岖山径往上走，一路无话，直到走到一处高地方才停歇。
It had been Kiamling’s idea to hunt the shadow monster, but the plan had seemed more watertight in the comforts of Man Mo temple. || 猎影兽是 Kiamling 的主意，但这计划在文武庙里显得周密，到了山上她开始犯疑。
The path disappeared entirely as the forest swallowed up the mountainside. Hoi used his guandou to cut a path through the hanging vines and thick undergrowth. || 树林吞没了山径，Hoi 挥关刀替众人砍开垂藤与密灌木；Kiamling 疑他真打起来不会使这把重弯刀，只当他在摆战神的架势。
Claw marks gouged into the rubber trees. Jingling squatted and splayed her fingers over them, but even her widest stretch could not match the handspan. || 他们到了 Kiamling 要找的地方：橡胶树干上被抓痕掏开，Jingling 蹲下摊开五指去比，张到最开也顶不上那掌距。
Rodents and small birds, like droppings along the path. Their bodies hollowed out, shrivelled and devoid of moisture. Drained of all their qi. || 沿路散着鼠与小鸟的尸体，像一串粪粒；尸身中空、干瘪无水分，气被抽尽——这是影兽来过最确凿的迹象。
Hoi gor picked up one of the dead rats by the tail. It swung back and forth. “Kiamling, exactly how do you know the shadow beast is up here?” || Hoi gor 拎起一条死鼠的尾巴晃了晃，追问 Kiamling 究竟凭什么断定影兽在这上头。
“The night of the fires, when it snatched Por Por, I chased it up here. There was a doorway, but it closed. I… I let it close. I was too afraid to go through.” || Kiamling 口干舌燥地坦白：大火那夜影兽抓走 Por Por，她一路追到这里，见过一道门，可门合上时她没有跟进去——她怕。
Hoi plonked a finger on her forehead and flicked her. “I’m relieved to hear there’s a bit of self-preservation in your utterly reckless brain.” || 她等着众人震惊、嫌她怯懦，Hoi 却一指点在她额头上弹了一下，说庆幸她那颗「全然莽撞」的脑子里还留着一点自保之心。
Behind her, Archie’s hands steadied her shoulders. “I’m fairly certain I hid in my room during the whole episode, so you’re braver than me.” || Archie 从背后双手扶稳她的肩，自嘲整场灾祸他都躲在自己房里，所以她比他勇敢。
“What do you think I’m doing? Here. Now! I’m trying to make sense of everything. I’ve seen Miss Kim run through walls and a bamboo forest sprout from the street. || Archie 被 Hoi 逼到反驳：他此刻上山正是在设法弄清这一切——他亲眼见过 Miss Kim 穿墙、见过街心长出竹林。
Kiamling called again. No sign of Archie. No Hoi. Jingling had disappeared now too. The dense forest felt claustrophobic as she waded forward. || Kiamling 再喊，Archie 与 Hoi 都不见踪影，连 Jingling 也消失了；密林闷得她透不过气，只能硬着头皮往前趟。
A revolver fired behind her. Once. Twice. Kiamling switched direction, running towards the sound, heart in her throat. || 身后左轮响了两枪（先一响，再一响），Kiamling 掉头朝枪声奔去，心提到嗓子眼。
A savage look filled his eyes, his jacket lost, his waistcoat torn on one side. He caught her before they both fell. “It’s here. I saw its eyes glowing.” || 她几乎撞进 Archie 怀里：他眼里透着凶相，外套丢了、马甲一侧撕破；他扶住她，说影兽就在这里，他看见它的眼睛在发光。
She pushed qi into her steps to move faster, cutting the aerial roots down as she sprinted. Hoi crouched on the ground, cradling Jingling’s head. “The shadow beast. It tried to take her,” he said. || 她运气加快脚步、一路砍断垂下的气根奔过去，只见 Hoi 蹲在地上托着 Jingling 的头，说影兽想把她抓走。
Kiamling cursed her foolish decision. They were not the predator; they were merely prey. “Can you carry her? I’ll keep watch.” Hoi nodded, picking Jingling up and hefting her onto his back. || Kiamling 骂自己蠢：他们不是猎手，只是猎物；她让 Hoi 背起 Jingling、自己断后，Hoi 点头把人扛上背。
The foliage hampered any fast movements, leaves crackling underfoot like sparks from a fire. A grey heap was crumpled on the ground. || 枝叶碍手碍脚，脚下落叶像火星般爆响；地上蜷着一团灰色的东西。
Nausea punched Kiamling hard in the gut. No, thank Chung Kwai, it was too small to be a body. It was Archie’s jacket. || 她一阵反胃，随即暗谢 Chung Kwai——那东西太小，不可能是尸体，是 Archie 的外套。
From the shadows, Archie raised his revolver and pointed it at Hoi. His expression hard. Cruel. || Archie 从暗处举起左轮对准 Hoi，脸色又硬又狠；Kiamling 低声唤他，他开了枪。
