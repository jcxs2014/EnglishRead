She only had enough qi left to roll it off her, not send it flying through the air as she had intended. || Kiamling 剩下的气只够把尸体从身上滚下去，没能照原意把它掀飞。

She threw more rice on the floor in front of her, making a path for the three of them to walk on. || 她又往身前地上撒米，为三人铺出一条能落脚的路。
