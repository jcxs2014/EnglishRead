The missionaries at St John’s Cathedral recognised Archie and let them in, but the reverend was out, calling on some of his congregation. || 圣约翰大教堂的传教士认出阿奇，放他与金玲入内；但牧师（内恩斯）外出，正去探访教区信众。
The choir rehearsal had since finished and their retreating footsteps echoed around. Only a handful of missionaries remained, busy with their duties. The church bells rang around them, eight times. || 唱诗班排练结束、脚步散去，只剩几名传教士各忙其事；教堂钟声在两人周围敲了八下。
“It’s Winter Solstice today. The shortest day and longest night. Families reunite and we make glutinous rice-flour balls together.” || 金玲对阿奇说当天是冬至——白昼最短、黑夜最长，家人团聚并一起搓糯米汤圆。
The protective layer around her heart had thawed completely, dripping like scalding candle wax. Oh no, she realised, much later than she really ought to have done. Jingling had been right after all. || 金玲的心防彻底融解；她迟了很久才醒悟：静玲说得没错（她已对阿奇动心）。
A longing filled the void in her stomach: a quiet cup of tea, a stroll by the harbour, even a day out at the races. || 金玲胃里的空落被渴望填满：她想要一杯安静的茶、海港边的散步、甚至去看一天赛马。
“You said it’s for family.” The tenderness in his voice threatened to cleave her in two. || 阿奇以温柔得几乎把她劈开的语气提醒她：汤圆是给家人吃的。
“I need to tell you something.” Archie’s words spilt in a great gush. He put two fingers under his collar, loosening it. Reached for her hand. || 阿奇急急开口说有事要告诉她，用两根手指松开衣领，并伸手去握她的手。
The church doors behind them opened and Reverend Nairns walked in, nodding to a couple of the missionaries on their way out. Archie sprang back from Kiamling. || 教堂大门在两人身后打开，内恩斯牧师走进来，向外出的传教士点头；阿奇立刻从金玲身边弹开。
Reverend Nairns looked like he might start wailing blasphemy, but his next words were unexpected. “I’ve already written to the bishop, even to some of the clergymen in India.” || 内恩斯牧师看似要痛斥亵渎，却说出了意外的话：他已写信给主教，甚至写给印度的一些教士。
“Vehemently not. All this hogwash about the little folk. Mass hysteria is what I’m concerned about, hallucinations due to the tropical heat. We do not have enough good physicians in the region as it is.” || 内恩斯断然否认相信，称小鬼之说是胡扯，把异象归为群体癔症与热带酷热造成的幻觉，并抱怨当地好医生不够。
Reverend Nairns put a hand to his forehead, shaking his head ruefully. “Sinful people engaged in gambling. I remain unsurprised at anything they purport to have seen.” || 内恩斯手按额头、摇着头，说目击者是赌博的罪人，他们声称看见什么都不足为奇（驳回数百人目击快活谷一事）。
“Barrister Ng came to this church on Sunday and repented for frequenting that den of filth.” The priest had a logical excuse for everything. || 内恩斯说吴律师（Barrister Ng）周日来教堂，为常去那处污秽之地忏悔；他对每件事都有说辞。
“Your grandmother? Oh, you mean that odious old woman? I do not detest her. I pity her and her heathen traditions. She would be more than welcome to join our congregation. We embrace everyone.” || 内恩斯说不恨金玲的祖母，只怜悯她和她的异教传统，还说欢迎她加入教会。
“If anything, you are to blame!” Finally! Her prodding had provoked him. Except the priest was not pointing an accusatory finger at her, the heathen yiugwai hunter, but at Archie. || 被激怒的内恩斯喊出「要怪就怪你」，但他指的不是异端猎妖人金玲，而是阿奇。
“Claiming to be devout, cavorting with this wicked woman, coming to me with talk of sin and transgressions. You asked me to save you, but you cannot be saved. || 内恩斯骂阿奇假装虔诚、与「邪恶女人」厮混，说他曾求自己拯救却无可救药。
Before Nairns could say another word, Brother Thomas collapsed forward. Archie caught him just in time to stop his skull smashing on the flagstones. || 内恩斯话没说完，托马斯修士向前倒下；阿奇及时扶住他，免他头颅砸在石板地上。
If Archie was on the left and Reverend Nairns in front, who was it making the terrible rasping noises behind her? Only then did she finally spot two neat puncture marks in Brother Thomas’s forearm. || 金玲发现阿奇在左、牧师在前，身后发出喘息声的另有其人；她这才看到托马斯修士前臂上两个整齐的咬痕。
Behind them, a dozen geongsi had ambled down the aisle and around the rear of the church. Wearing European three-piece suits or military uniforms, these undead were from the cemetery outside. || 十二只僵尸（geongsi）从教堂后方的走道绕过来；它们穿欧式三件套或军用制服，是葬在外边墓地、死于热带病或斗殴后被复生的欧洲人。
