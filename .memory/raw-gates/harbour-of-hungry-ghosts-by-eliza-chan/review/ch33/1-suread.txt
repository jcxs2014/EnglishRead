Kiamling did not recognise it as any of the eighteen levels of hell, nor the land of fae that Archie had prepared her for. || Kiamling 断定这片黑暗既不是十八层地狱中的任何一层，也不是 Archie 事先给她讲过的妖精之地，怀疑两界之间的路本身出了问题。
Tall figures in Chinese clothing stood on all sides of her, rigid as immovable pillars. All of them, in every direction, had their backs turned to her. || 黑暗中浮出成群穿中式衣衫的高大身影，把 Kiamling 四面围住，却全部背对着她，僵硬如柱。
Kiamling’s hands shook as she brought them up to her own face. She moved them frantically, but she could not feel anything. || Kiamling 双手发抖地摸自己的脸，狂乱地来回移动，却什么也摸不到——她的脸也变成了没有五官的光滑一片。
An insistent tug on her waist woke Kiamling up, along with the fierce itching on her face. || 腰间一阵猛拽把 Kiamling 拽醒，她脸上同时奇痒；一只人脸黑黄斑蝉在她旁边跺着六只脚叫骂。
The mound moved. Up and down, up and down in deep slumber. Her instinct was correct. || Kiamling 发现妖精们围圈跳舞的那座“土丘”正随深睡一呼一吸地起伏，确认自己的直觉没错：下面埋着人。
Kiamling dug still, finding Ng Choy’s face under a layer of loose soil. She toppled over the toadstool houses to free his upper limbs, tearing vines from where his legs had been bound together. || Kiamling 徒手挖开松土，露出 Ng Choy 的脸，又推倒蘑菇小屋、扯断捆住他双腿的藤蔓，把他上半身解放出来。
“No!” She threw herself on top of the barrister, attaching the red string to his belt, and tried to heft him around the waist. || Kiamling 扑到律师身上，把红线系到他腰带上，试图拦腰把他抱起来拖走，却已没有真气可借，只剩蛮力。
The winged demons flew backwards with a hiss, giving her space to look, to breathe, to realise she had reached into the wrong pouch entirely. She had thrown glutinous rice. || Kiamling 摸黑抓了一把朝妖精撒去，虫群嘶叫着后退，她这才发现自己摸错了袋子——撒出去的是糯米而不是盐。
They had amassed by the barriers she had scattered, fluttering their wings so that the air thrummed. The salt and rice grains rolled forward. || 妖精们聚到 Kiamling 撒下的盐米防线旁齐扇翅膀，震得空气发颤，把盐米粒一点点吹向前、破了她的护线。
She wrapped her arms and legs around Ng Choy as if he were a sack of rice and rolled on the ground. || Kiamling 手脚并用把 Ng Choy 当米袋一样缠住，抱着他顺坡滚向雾墙。
She instinctively jabbed out with a right hook, surprised when she hit something big and solid. Someone groaned and cursed her ancestors in Cantonese. || Kiamling 本能挥出右勾拳，打中一个又大又实的东西；对方闷哼一声，用粤语咒骂她的祖宗。
Her shoulders sagged with relief as the Englishman knelt beside her. “It’s been over a week.” || Archie 跪到 Kiamling 身边，她才知道自己进环时还是夜里，如今已过了一个多星期。
“It mauled two people the night you went missing.” Hoi gor side-eyed Archie. “The gweilo ran away.” || 海哥（Hoi gor）告诉 Kiamling，那头兽在她失踪那晚伤了两个人，又斜眼看向 Archie，说这个鬼佬当时跑了。
He held out three silver sixpences. His hands were stained with red lines, as if he had wound the threads round and round them. || 海哥摊开手，递出三枚六便士银币，手上满是像被线反复缠绕留下的红痕。
She lit three joss sticks and pushed them into the ground by the fae ring. Next to them, on a plate, she placed the three silver pieces and a mantau Hoi gor had procured for her. || Kiamling 在妖精环边插上三炷香，又把三枚银币和 Hoi gor 替她弄来的一个馒头摆到盘子里作供。
The taste of dragon-beard candy. Wisps of white sugar so fine she could inhale them. The warming crunch of peanuts at the centre. || 为满足妖精“要一段好记忆”的要求，Kiamling 献出自己珍视的童年记忆——龙须糖的味道：细得能吸进鼻子的白糖丝与中心温热的花生脆。
Gone. A grey fog in its place when she tried to recall it. A feeling of loss that she could not define. || 那段记忆随即消失，Kiamling 再回想时只剩一片灰雾，以及说不清的失落。
Out of the corner of her eye, she saw a black bug skittering in the gutter. She stamped on it, the shell crunching under her foot with a satisfying crack. || 妖精散去后，Kiamling 瞥见水沟里爬过一只黑虫，一脚踩下去，虫壳在脚下咔嚓碎开。
