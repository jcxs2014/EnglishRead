Archie laughed, but the sound escaping from his mouth was too high-pitched, too nervous to be indifferent. Kiamling gave him Por Por’s famous your ancestors are disappointed in you glare, and it did the trick. || 阿奇干笑，笑声尖细紧张、毫无从容；Kiamling 用婆婆（Por Por）那副「祖宗对你很失望」的招牌瞪眼压他，果然奏效。
“You are learning Cantonese. You want to learn the other local languages. Be indispensable to your country. Don’t you think you would do better if we found more common ground? To unpick these customs, you first have to understand them.” || Kiamling 从「文化交流」劝他：他在学粤语、还想学其他本地语言、想对国家有用，就该多找共同点；要废除这些习俗，得先弄懂它们——他动摇了。
Archie’s hand tightened around the crucifix. She had almost had him, but the word evil was a step too far. Hastily she repaired the damage. || 她说到「邪灵」时阿奇握紧十字架，眼看就要缩回去，她急忙改口补救。
“Yes, yes, he would.” A weight had been lifted from his shoulders. Quietly, not that there was anyone to hear them, he whispered, “You have fae.” || 阿奇顺着教会驱魔的话头如释重负，压低声音告诉 Kiamling：你们这儿有 fae（妖精）。
“Fae do not exist,” Kiamling agreed readily. The mental gymnastics was hurting her head. It was like persuading a feral beast to eat from her hand. || Kiamling 连声附和「妖精不存在」，心里觉得这套自我说服像哄一头野兽从她手里吃东西。
The circle around Mr Ng, well, it looked very much like a fairy ring, even if there were no toadstools to be seen. The stories say that if you enter a fairy ring, you cannot escape. || 阿奇把 Ng Choy 身边的圆圈认作「仙女环」（只是没长毒蘑菇），并说传说里进了仙女环就出不来。
As for tools…” He rubbed his chin thoughtfully. “Salt, of course. Iron, holy water, silver and rowan wood.” || 阿奇列出对付妖精的工具：盐、铁、圣水、银和花楸木（rowan wood）。
It was getting late now, the burn of the afternoon sun giving way to a pleasant evening. St John’s was empty; the reverend had departed for business in Macau. || 两人赶去圣约翰座堂求 Nairns 牧师帮忙，却扑了空——牧师已去澳门办事。
No one knew that she had frozen in fear on the night of the fires, letting the rift close. If she allowed herself to overthink it again, her nerves would get the better of her. || Kiamling 承认那晚大火时她吓得僵住、任裂隙合上；她怕再犹豫就没胆，所以必须今夜动手。
This one job would solve it, even if it was high-risk. She needed the money and she needed her reputation restored. This could do both in one go. || 她还背着讨债人：这一单高风险活能一次解决钱和名声；第三个（她说出口的）理由是再拖下去 Ng Choy 就永远回不来。
“Never mind what the Church says. What do you believe, Archie?” His mouth opened and closed as if afraid to say it aloud. || Kiamling 逼他「别管教会怎么说，你自己信什么」，阿奇张嘴又合上、不敢出声——他是个只会讨好上司的人。
Still, she’d procured an iron cleaver from Fung gor, the butcher, so that was something. The fae were weak against iron, that was what Archie’s mother had told him. || 银币没着落（Hoi gor 找不到、旁人当面笑她），她只从肉贩 Fung gor 处弄到一把铁菜刀；阿奇说妖精怕铁，是他母亲告诉他的。
“Just promise me you won’t let go.” His answer went unheard as Kiamling swivelled on the balls of her feet to face the wall. || Kiamling 只要求阿奇保证绝不松手，可他的回答她没听见——她已转身面向墙壁准备助跑。
The fu held, her qi and the talismans allowing her through wall after wall as she circled the poppy ring they had marked. Once. Twice. || 黄符与她的真气让她一堵接一堵穿过实心砖墙，绕着她标记的罂粟环转圈；真气像漏进下水道一样飞快耗尽。
Half rolling, half tumbling, every bone in her body aching, she somehow burst through the wall into the street outside. It felt like an elephant had sat on her. || 第九次穿出外墙，她半滚半摔跌在街上，浑身骨头像被大象坐过；她喘着气喊「看，我会数数！」，却没人接话。
“Archie?” She sat up, but his end of the spool lay on the ground, loops of red thread making idle patterns in puddle water. || 她坐起喊阿奇，无人应答——他那一端的线轴落在地上，红线圈在积水里打转，她仍被九圈线拴在房子上。
Red eyes blinked at her from the alley, a huge dark shape moving. The shadow beast. All at once, the night of the Hungry Ghost Festival came flooding back. || 巷中红眼巨影挪动，正是饿鬼节那夜的「影兽」；她自知不敌（它比婆婆还强），只记得它进不了罂粟馆的盐圈。
Sorry, Archie. She made up her mind, stepping backwards over the fairy circle towards the noise of the fae. || Kiamling 心里说了句「对不起，阿奇」，退步跨过仙女环、朝妖精的乐声走去，只盼他找到了藏身处。
