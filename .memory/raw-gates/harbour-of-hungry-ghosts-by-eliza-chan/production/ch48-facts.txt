“Now do you believe?” she said, coin sword in one hand, fu in the other. || 她一手铜钱剑一手符，问Nairns 牧师现在信不信。
She had been busy while Archie prayed, tucking the fu around the church just in case. || Archie 祷告的时候她没闲着，把符贴遍教堂备着以防万一。
She had expected to use them on Reverend Nairns, but plans change. || 那些符原本打算用在 Nairns 牧师身上，后来计划改了。
Smacked perfectly in place. Por Por would be proud. || 一张符正正贴在僵尸额头上，她想祖母会为她骄傲。
Her fu were only partially effective against these undead. || 她的符对这些僵尸只起了一半作用，它们仍在往前挪。
She sliced her coin sword across the undead’s neck. The brittle bones cracked, the geongsi’s head toppling to the floor. || 她用铜钱剑横切僵尸的脖子，脆骨裂开，头颅落到地上。
She had never faced so many before, not even with Por Por by her side. || 正门涌进来的鬼多到她一个人对付不了，从前有祖母在旁也没见过这么多。
The line of fu-paralysed monsters would soon rupture. || 被符定住的那一排怪物很快就要崩开。
Kiamling clambered over the temporary barrier the others had constructed across the side of the room, stacking pews three rows high. || 众人把长椅叠了三层横在教堂侧边当壁垒，她翻了过去。
“You, girl, you cursed witch! You did this!” the reverend said, thrusting a cross out in his shaking hands. || 牧师骂她是下咒的女巫、说这都是她干的，一面抖着手把十字架推出来。
It did not have the intended effect. || 牧师推出十字架这一手没起该起的作用。
“It wards off evil,” Archie replied, cutting through the tirade Reverend Nairns was about to deliver. || Archie 抢在牧师发作之前回答：十字架是用来驱邪的。
“The altar is the most sacred part of the church.” || 牧师被问到时勉强答出一句：祭坛是教堂里最神圣的地方。
They are attracted to life essence. || 她给众人上速成保命课，说这些东西被活人的气吸引。
She scooped a handful of water into her mouth from the font, an ornate stone plinth with a shallow bowl at the centre. || 她渴得从圣水盆里捧起一捧水喝下去。
Their skin bubbled where the water made contact. || 圣水溅到僵尸身上，它们的皮肉起泡，她才知道这水有用。
She dipped her coin sword into the font, pouring the water as best she could so it ran down the length of the weapon. || 她把铜钱剑浸进圣水盆，尽量让水顺着剑身流下去。
“Well, even you aren’t stupid enough to set a horde of geongsi on yourself. You can’t be behind this whole thing after all.” || 她向牧师道歉，说他不至于放一群鬼来咬自己，于是排除了他是幕后。
His skin shrivelled to ash, flaking away in her hands, his expression one of open-mouthed terror. || 那个传教士的气被瞬间抽干，皮肉缩成灰，从她手里剥落。
Archie smacked the groping arms and legs indiscriminately with his pilfered sconce, each blow sizzling, until they withdrew and the lock slid into place. || 最后 Archie 拿烛台打退伸进来的手脚，那些东西缩回去，门锁滑上了。
