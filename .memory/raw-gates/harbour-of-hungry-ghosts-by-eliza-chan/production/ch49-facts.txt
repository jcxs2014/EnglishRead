Kiamling pulled herself to her feet, thighs burning. || 她撑着发抖的腿从地上站起来。
“Make for the altar.” || 她下令三个人向祭坛移动。
With her qi channelled into pure strength, she picked up one of the pews and threw it at the geongsi. || 她把气灌成蛮力，抬起一条教堂长椅砸向活尸。
It took the heads off the front rows, then tumbled, knocking over those behind as it landed with a heavy crash. || 长椅削掉前排活尸的头，翻滚着砸倒后面的，落地一声巨响。
Coins cracked as it gnashed down, raining on her in broken fragments. || 活尸咬住她的铜钱剑，铜钱碎裂，碎片撒了她一身。
She only had enough qi left to roll it off her, not send it flying through the air as she had intended. || 她剩下的气只够把那具尸体从身上推开，做不到原想的把它震飞。
She tossed a handful of glutinous rice into the other geongsi’s faces, the grains sizzling like hot coals as the creatures shrieked and writhed. || 她朝另外几具活尸脸上撒糯米，米粒像炭火一样灼烧，鬼怪尖叫扭动。
Some of the corpses were in a terrible state, those who had died on the long voyage over. || 有些尸体烂得不成样子，是死在长途海路上的人。
No one to leave offerings and maintain their headstones. || 没有人给他们上供、打理墓碑。
One of the undead lunged for Reverend Nairns behind her, and he yelled, thrusting out his open Bible in a panic. || 一具活尸扑向她身后的 Nairns 牧师，他大叫着慌忙把摊开的《圣经》推出去。
As he pulled back, two pages ripped, stuck to the ooze on the geongsi’s face. || 他往回抽时撕下两页纸，纸粘在活尸脸上的黏液上。
The monster froze in place like it had been a fu talisman. || 那怪物当场僵住，像被贴了符。
“From a Bible? Sacrilege!” || 牧师抗议说从《圣经》上撕纸是亵渎。
“This is the King James Bible! Printed and shipped from London,” || 他强调这是从伦敦印好运来的钦定本《圣经》。
“Is it a specific book? Genesis?” Archie asked from the rear, his academic mind piqued. || Archie 在后面追问是不是特定某一卷，比如《创世记》。
The geongsi quietened down, their movements stilling, like a congregation listening to a sermon. || 活尸安静下来，动作停住，像听道的会众。
He swallowed, and the geongsi renewed their vigour. || 他一停一咽，活尸就重新有了劲。
Kiamling threw her spool of red thread to Archie, wanting him to make a barrier as she had done at the racecourse. || 她把一卷红线扔给 Archie，想让他像她当年在马场那样拉出一道屏障。
Outnumbered in a geongsi fight with about the worst companions she could have: a priest and an uncoordinated scholar. || 她心里判定：寡不敌众，身边的同伴是最糟的一个牧师加一个手脚不协调的读书人。
She did not want to be in the room when it finally snapped. || 她不想等到红线终于断掉时还留在厅里。
