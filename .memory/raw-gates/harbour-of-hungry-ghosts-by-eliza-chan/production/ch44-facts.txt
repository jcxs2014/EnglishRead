It had been Kiamling’s idea to hunt the shadow monster, but the plan had seemed more watertight in the comforts of Man Mo temple. || 猎影兽是她自己的主意，可这主意在文武庙里坐着时显得周密得多。
Here, where the ships in the harbour looked like nothing more than folded paper, she started having her doubts. || 站在高处，海港里的船小得像折纸，她开始犯疑。
Hoi used his guandou to cut a path through the hanging vines and thick undergrowth. || Hoi 用关刀在垂藤和厚灌木里劈出一条路。
Claw marks gouged into the rubber trees. || 橡胶树上留着被爪子剜出的痕。
Jingling squatted and splayed her fingers over them, but even her widest stretch could not match the handspan. || Jingling 蹲下把手掌张在爪痕上，张到最大也够不上那个掌印。
Their bodies hollowed out, shrivelled and devoid of moisture. Drained of all their qi. || 死掉的鼠鸟被掏空、干瘪失水，气被吸尽。
“Kiamling, exactly how do you know the shadow beast is up here?” || Hoi 拎着死鼠尾巴问她，凭什么断定影兽在这上头。
“The night of the fires, when it snatched Por Por, I chased it up here. There was a doorway, but it closed. I… I let it close. I was too afraid to go through.” || 她坦白：起火那夜祖母被掳，她追到此处，有一道门，门关上了，她太怕，没敢过去。
Hoi plonked a finger on her forehead and flicked her. || Hoi 一根手指戳在她额头上，弹了一下。
“I’m relieved to hear there’s a bit of self-preservation in your utterly reckless brain.” || 他说听出她那颗莽撞脑子里还留了点自保，倒是松了口气。
“I was in one of the flower boats,” Hoi said with a crooked smile. || Hoi 笑得有点歪，说那夜他在一只花艇上。
“Who is going to admit visiting brothels in Tai Ping Shan Street?” Archie said. || Archie 反问谁会承认自己去过太平山街的妓院。
Used to being ignored, he found the attention on him discomforting. || 他一向被人忽略，注意力落到他身上反而不自在。
Truth be told, they wanted her to go away. || 说实话，他们要的是她走开。
Kiamling called again. No sign of Archie. No Hoi. Jingling had disappeared now too. || 她再喊，Archie 不见、Hoi 不见，Jingling 也不见了。
It was not Jingling after all, but a patch of violet flowers. || 前方那抹紫色到底不是 Jingling，是一丛紫花。
A revolver fired behind her. Once. Twice. || 身后左轮响了一声，又一声。
“It’s here. I saw its eyes glowing.” || Archie 说它就在这儿，他看见它的眼睛在发光。
No, thank Chung Kwai, it was too small to be a body. It was Archie’s jacket. || 那团灰色太小，不像尸首，是 Archie 的外套。
From the shadows, Archie raised his revolver and pointed it at Hoi. || 阴影里，Archie 举起左轮对准 Hoi。
