It was dark. Not the darkness of night, which was lit with stars and the full moon overhead. || 她落在一片黑暗里，那不是有星星和满月的夜色。
Nor the darkness of a room, which had a shape and consistency to it, even if the eyes could not see the boundaries. || 也不是屋里那种有形状、有质地的黑。
Where eyes and a nose and mouth should be was a smooth surface like dough. || 四面站着的死者没有脸，五官处只有面团一样光滑的表面。
Funeral robes. || 她抓到的深蓝布料是丧服，围着她的是死人。
She had the length of her nine rotations around the opium den, she reassured herself. || 腰间红绳只够绕鸦片馆九圈，她这样安抚自己。
These were not the ethereal fair folk that Archie had described. || 这里的精怪不是 Archie 描述过的那种空灵仙灵。
Taking out the iron butcher’s cleaver, she hesitated before putting it away again, using her hands to dig instead. || 她掏出铁菜刀又收回去，改用手去挖。
Kiamling dug still, finding Ng Choy’s face under a layer of loose soil. || 她继续挖，在松土下面找到了 Ng Choy 的脸。
She had no qi left to boost her abilities; only raw strength and obstinacy remained. || 她没有气可用了，只剩力气和一股犟劲。
She had thrown glutinous rice. || 她抓错了口袋，扔出去的是糯米而不是盐。
They formed the face of an old woman, weather-beaten and irate. Por Por’s face. || 虫群拼出一张怒气冲冲的老妇人的脸，是祖母的脸。
She wrapped her arms and legs around Ng Choy as if he were a sack of rice and rolled on the ground. || 她像抱一袋米一样缠住 Ng Choy，抱着他在地上滚。
“Ah Kiam!” || 有人喊她“阿金”，她一拳打出去。
“It’s been over a week.” || Archie 告诉她，她在里面已经过了一个多星期。
“It mauled two people the night you went missing.” Hoi gor side-eyed Archie. “The gweilo ran away.” || 她失踪那夜怪物伤了两个人，Hoi 说洋人跑了。
“I hid,” Archie blurted out, ears reddening. || Archie 承认自己躲了起来，耳朵发红。
He held out three silver sixpences. || Hoi 摊开三枚六便士银币给她。
“They want more. A good memory. A piece of your happiness.” || Archie 说精怪还要更多：一段好记忆、一份快乐。
The taste of dragon-beard candy. || 她交出去的是龙须糖的滋味。
“Cockroaches,” she muttered with a tiny shudder. || 她踩死一只虫子，低声说“蟑螂”，轻轻打了个寒噤。
