The crowd was getting denser now, only the parasols levelled at head height keeping people at a distance. || 赛马场人群越来越挤，只有齐头高的阳伞把人和人隔开。
A fine fur stole had fallen onto the grass, and with petty glee she stood on it with her muddy shoes. || 她把气出在一条掉在地上的毛皮披肩上，穿着泥鞋站了上去。
It was Madam Yau, the brothel owner, glaring as if the yiugwai hunter had murdered her firstborn. || 被她踩到披肩的是妓院老板娘 Madam Yau，瞪她像瞪杀婴的凶手。
She was simply reciting all the food she had eaten in the last week, but the British racegoers avoided her eye like she was cursing them. || 她只是用粤语大声报自己这一周吃过的菜，英国看客却像躲咒骂一样躲开她的目光。
Archie was nowhere to be seen, so she asked the man next to her which horse was Sir Tristan. || Archie 不见踪影，她只好问旁边的人哪一匹是 Sir Tristan。
It didn’t look like a lovelorn knight; more like a chestnut thoroughbred with one white sock. || 那马不像失恋的骑士，只是一匹脚上有只白袜的栗色纯血马。
Then the starting pistol fired and all her attention was on the horses. || 发令枪一响，她的注意力全到了马身上。
Her horse was in the lead group, muzzle just behind the other two as they came round the second and third bends towards the finish line. || 她的马跑在第一集团，过第二三个弯时鼻口只落后前两匹。
The first horse crossed the finish line, then Sir Tristan, then the others in a messy clump. || 第一匹马过终点，接着是 Sir Tristan，再是乱作一团的其他马。
He flew through the air, falling with an audible crack. || 骑师被甩飞出去，落地时响了一声听得见的脆响。
It whinnied in distress, a sound that came out gravelly and low, unlike the nickers and neighs of the gentle parade but an hour before. || 那马痛苦地嘶鸣，声音沙哑低沉，和一小时前巡游时的轻嘶完全不同。
The saddle slid to the ground, and growths swelled along the animal’s back. || 马鞍滑落到地上，那牲口背上鼓起一个个肉瘤。
Kiamling felt at her belt, but she had deliberately left her coin purse at home, hadn’t she? || 她摸腰带才想起，钱袋是她自己特意留在家里没带的。
She squashed a dried goji berry against the coin, wrapping them both in a fu talisman and placing it under her tongue. || 她把干枸杞按在硬币上压扁，两样裹进符里塞到舌头底下。
Its body was lined in red, but it flickered and spluttered like a flame being blown out. || 那匹马的红光轮廓忽明忽灭，像被吹打着的火苗。
A horn sounded, low and long. A hunting horn. || 一声号角响起，低沉而长——是猎号。
She’d known the guardian lions had been positioned incorrectly, but Archie’s complacency had shut down her misgivings. || 她早知道石狮摆错了位置，却被 Archie 的笃定压下了疑虑。
She hesitated, then for good measure at the end added in English: devoted friends. || 她犹豫了一下，最后又保险起见用英文添上 devoted friends。
She slapped a fu onto the forehead of one guardian lion, then the other. || 她把符拍上一尊石狮的额头，再拍上另一尊。
“Fetch.” || 她只说了一个词：Fetch。
