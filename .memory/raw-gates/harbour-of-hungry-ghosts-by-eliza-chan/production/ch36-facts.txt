Tai Ping Shan Street was a forest. || 太平山街变成了一片树林。
Emerald-green bamboo stems towered in confident brushstrokes, thick and tall. || 碧绿的竹竿又粗又高，像毛笔自信画下的笔触一样立着。
Kiamling stared in confusion as the earth gave a deep growl beneath her. || 脚下的地发出一声低沉的咆哮，Kiamling 看得愣住了。
Kiamling would have put money on the heart of the forest being located at the Pavilion of Spring Buds. || 她断定这片林子的中心就在春芽阁。
Jingling was in there. || 妹妹 Jingling 就在里面。
Bamboo forests grew upon the slopes of Victoria Peak. || Victoria Peak 的山坡上也长着竹林。
For one, it was eerily quiet, without the birdsong or the long-tailed macaques swinging overhead. || 这里静得反常，没有鸟叫，也没有长尾猕猴在头顶荡。
A wooden sign advertising fine entertainment and refreshments had been yanked high into the canopy || 一块写着上好娱乐与茶点的木招牌被挑到了树冠之上
revealing a ghoulish face contorted in agony. The body of a European merchant. || 她刮开泥土，露出一张痛苦扭曲的鬼脸——一个欧洲商人的尸体。
like every last ounce of qi had been sucked out of him || 皮肤灰白、两颊干塌，像最后一分气都被吸干了。
Pretty yellow flowers grew out of his ears, curling like a snail from a shell. || 他耳朵里长出好看的黄花，卷得像蜗牛出壳。
The grains hissed as they touched the dead man’s skin, turning black. || 糯米碰到死者的皮肤就嘶嘶作响，变黑了。
A woman’s voice screamed in the distance, and all caution went to the wind. || 远处传来女人的尖叫，她把谨慎全丢开了。
“Jingling!” Kiamling slashed down through the bamboo blocking her way. || 她喊着"Jingling"，一剑劈开挡路的竹子。
Below her, the shadow beast from the Hungry Ghost Festival prowled. || 下方游走的是盂兰节上那只影子兽。
It was bigger too, nearly as big as an ox, with ears that curved like horns. || 它更大了，几乎有牛那么大，耳朵弯得像角。
“Nope, no thank you. We are closed. You are definitely not welcome here,” she said || 她一边把黄符糊在门缝上，一边说打烊了、这里不欢迎它。
Her ribs ached from where she had fallen, and she prodded them gently with her hand. A gasp escaped her lips. Broken. || 她肋骨作痛，用手轻轻一按，倒吸一口气——断了。
Inside the Pavilion of Spring Buds, the open room was unrecognisable. || 春芽阁里面已经完全认不出来了。
Were these European trees? || 她问自己：这些是欧洲的树吗？
