So, the British wanted to start another war. || 她开口就认定英国人又要打一场仗。
Those in the southern provinces had suffered hugely at the hands of rebellions, pirates and famine. || 她挂心的不是道德高地，而是南方各省已被叛乱、海盗和饥荒磨过一遍的百姓。
A few deep breaths settled her emotions. || 她深呼吸几次，把情绪压回去。
When she looked up, the singer from before was still standing beside the pianist. || 她抬头时，先前那位歌手仍站在钢琴师旁边。
She put down the tray, rubbing her eyes in disbelief. || 她放下托盘，揉着眼睛不敢相信眼前的人。
Her sister Jingling stood before the whole dining room, a warbling song breaking from her throat. || 站在整间餐厅前面唱歌的，是她的妹妹 Jingling。
Jingling was actually very good. || 她不得不承认妹妹唱得确实很好。
It held the whole Hongkong Hotel dining room in one entranced breath. || 香港酒店整间餐厅的人都被她唱得屏住了呼吸。
“I say, that’s a fine set of lungs,” the man at the next table said admiringly. || 邻桌的男人赞叹她有一副好嗓子。
“Indeed. I wonder if she gives private performances,” his companion remarked. || 他的同伴接着问她是否也接私下的场子。
They whispered behind their fans, but she heard the words lacking virtue || 身后的女客躲在扇子后面议论她“没有德性”。
Kiamling marched straight up to the piano, ignoring the glare of the head waiter and her sister’s startled expression. || 她径直走到钢琴边，无视领班瞪眼和妹妹的惊愕。
“Thank the eighteen hells that Por Por isn’t here to see this!” Kiamling hissed. || 她咬牙低声说，幸亏婆婆不在这里看见这一幕。
“Singing. Or do your ears not work?” Jingling was not cowed by her older sister’s response. || 妹妹回嘴说自己是在唱歌，并没有被姐姐吓住。
She scrubbed at Jingling’s mouth with it || 她拿手帕去擦妹妹的嘴。
The dining room was in an uproar at the comedic effect of their double act || 姐妹俩的滑稽对峙让餐厅里一片哄闹。
“On reconsideration, leave and don’t come back. You are fired.” || 经理改口，当场把妹妹解雇。
The chiding was enough to stop Jingling entirely. || 那一顿训斥把妹妹彻底噎住了。
“Don’t you get it? I don’t want to be your caged bird!” Jingling snapped. || 妹妹说得很清楚：她不要做姐姐关在笼里的鸟。
Kiamling did not know her at all. || 章末她承认自己根本不认识这个妹妹。
