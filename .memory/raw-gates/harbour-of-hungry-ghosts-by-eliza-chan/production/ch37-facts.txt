It did not take a professional yiugwai hunter to figure out this was the heart of the forest. || 不用职业猎妖人也看得出，这里是森林的心脏，是屏障变薄的中心。
Inside the gap, the figure of a woman with long dark hair was partially enfolded. || 树身裂口里嵌着一个长发女人的半边身子。
Kiamling unsheathed the short iron cleaver that she had never returned to Butcher Fung || 她抽出那把一直没还给屠夫冯的短铁菜刀。
Then the roots visibly tightened, pulling the captive further into the hollow. || 树根当场收紧，把被俘的人往树洞里拽。
“Get out, while you still can,” she said in a hoarse whisper. || 那女人嘶哑地低声叫她趁还能走的时候赶紧走。
It was Madam Yau, almost unrecognisable with her hair loose across her face. || 被缠住的是 Madam Yau，头发散在脸上，几乎认不出来。
“Find Jingling. She can…” || Madam Yau 让她去找 Jingling，话没说完。
“Are you one of them?” a voice said in English. || 有人用英语问她是不是“她们”里的一个。
“One of these strumpets who bewitch good men.” || 男孩说她们是勾引好男人的娼妇。
“But I caught her with her own trick. Trap me in a tree.” || 他说自己用她自己的法子逮住了她，把她困在树里。
He ripped the fu off, but its words of exorcism had smeared onto his skin like a tattoo. || 他撕下符纸，符上的驱邪字迹却像纹身一样印进了皮肤。
His face pockmarked, a dark beard covering his chin and a woodcutter’s axe in his hand. || 他变成一脸麻子、下巴留黑胡子、手里握着樵夫斧头的男人。
“Distract him, get him away from the big tree.” || Jingling 让她把那人引开，引离那棵大树。
“Trust me, Ah Kiam. I can get Madam Yau out. || Jingling 让她信自己，说自己能把 Madam Yau 弄出来。
She needed to trust. || 她对自己说，这一次要信妹妹。
She slammed her sword down into the crack, thrusting it until it would go no further, nearly to the hilt. || 她把剑砸进树身裂口，一直捅到几乎没柄。
“Viviane, Nimuë, Lady of the Lake. You are everywoman, and each of you an ambitious harlot.” || 他搬出湖中仙女的名字，说每个女人都是野心勃勃的娼妇。
“But they can’t,” he said. || 他回了一句“可她们撑不住”，随后朝下扑去。
His arms and legs became white hooves, and branches burst from his head. || 他的手和脚变成白色蹄子，头上长出枝杈。
Kiamling had been taught by the Ten Tigers of Canton. || 她的功夫是广东十虎教出来的。
