Madam Yau opened the second umbrella. Then the third. Each housed another ghost. || 老板娘连开第二、第三把伞，每把伞里各藏一个女鬼。
She leapt forward from her hiding position, coin sword in hand, flinging fu talismans towards the malevolent spirits || Kiamling 从藏身处跳出，手持铜钱剑向女鬼掷符。
Madam Yau’s fist closed over the tip of the sword, stopping the killing blow. || 老板娘徒手攥住剑尖，拦下了这一记杀招。
“You are bad at this, mui mui.” || 老板娘叫她“小妹妹”，说她这一行干得差。
“A wulei jing.” Kiamling recalled the fox spirit || Kiamling 认出对方是狐精 wulei jing。
“The only thing is… I made a promise to your grandmother that I would protect them.” || 老板娘说曾答应她祖母，要护住这些女鬼。
“Your Por Por organised this arrangement.” || 老板娘说这一切是她祖母 Por Por 安排的。
She sheathed her sword. “I think you’d better fill me in.” || 她收剑，要对方把事情说清楚。
“Can I feed my girls first? They are ravenous.” || 老板娘先要求喂她那群饿极了的女孩。
The stage, however, was empty, with no sign of Jingling after her performance. || 楼下舞台已空，Jingling 演完不见踪影。
Upstairs, Madam Yau led her into a private chamber. || 老板娘领她上楼，进了一间私室。
A wealthy Chinese merchant by the looks of his clothes. He thrust a hand rudely up the courtesan’s skirt || 一个衣着富态的华商把手粗暴地探进妓女的裙里。
“Open your third eye,” Madam Yau whispered. || 老板娘低声叫她开第三眼。
The hollows under her eyes filled out, her black hair lustrous as she gathered in the man’s qi. || 女鬼吸走男人的气，眼窝饱满起来、头发变得油亮。
He lay bruised and battered in a sticky mess of his own creation. || 男人躺在自己弄出的污糟里，遍体伤痕。
She had quenched her thirst on his qi, but he still lived. || 女鬼饮足了他的气，但他还活着。
Upstairs, the lui gwei provide the services of the bedchamber. || 楼上由女鬼承接床笫之役。
“You are still shortening lives.” || Kiamling 指出这仍在缩短人的寿命。
When do women ever get the chance to be anything other than prey? || 老板娘问：女人何时有过不当猎物的机会。
“Why do you think hungry ghosts have needle mouths? Because men do not want to see our teeth.” || 针口之由：男人不想看见女人的牙。
