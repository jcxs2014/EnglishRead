My head throbbed as I blinked awake. Not that I could see anything. The sack over my head had been replaced with a makeshift blindfold. The rough, torn fabric covered nearly the entire top half of my head, but at least I could breathe. I supposed I was still suffering from days of fatigue. I must have passed out at some point during my kidnapping, before I was then dropped on the lumpy ground, propped up against something rough, sturdy, likely the trunk of a tree. My wrists were sore from my hands being tied behind my back. My back ached as if I’d been like this for a few hours. Rushing water overlaid shuffling footsteps of people around me. I counted at least eight distinct voices—male and female. || 她醒来时头上换成了临时撕成的布条 blindfold，看不见东西；她以为自己大概在绑架途中昏过去，之后被丢在不平的地上、后背靠着粗糙结实的东西（很可能是树干）；双手被绑在身后，手腕酸痛；四周有流水声与拖沓脚步声，她数出至少八个不同的嗓音，男女都有。.

I tried to catch my breath. I knew they were dead—Oleg and Sal. What I didn’t know was what other horrors abounded in this forest. The last time I was here, I had been captured and almost eaten by a terrifying rainbow monster. A presence lowered to sit somewhere to my left, where either Oleg or Sam had been sitting. A chill wrapped around my ankles. But strangely, it brought me instant comfort. || 她认定 Oleg 和 Sal 已经死了；她想起上次来这片林子时曾差点被一只可怕的彩虹怪物吃掉；随后一个存在坐到她左边 Oleg 或 Sam 原先坐的位置，脚踝一凉，却带来莫名的安心。.
