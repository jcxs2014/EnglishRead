The sun had set and risen twice since I’d been dragged from my cozy clock tower and essentially put to work interrogating the Wicked King’s prisoners. || 她一直没睡；太阳已经落过一次又升起一次——她被从自己暖和的钟楼里拖走，被迫整天整夜审问国王的囚犯。
His Majesty would have me assess a fresh batch of suspected traitors or disloyal citizens. Various groups of people, possibly from different kingdoms across the land. || 每日流程：他让她评估新一批被指为叛徒或不忠的臣民，来的人来自不同国度。
On occasion that I came across a particularly hateful aura, the king would waste no time and take them away for punishment. || 遇到特别可恨的气场时，国王立刻把人带走处置。
I always tried to resist exposing these people’s thoughts, feelings, or motives, but I barely needed to speak before he would understand. || 她总想拒绝暴露这些人的想法、感受或动机，但几乎不必开口他就已经懂了。
The king would even drag away certain individuals before I had an opportunity to assess them. I had stopped asking why when it became clear I would never get any satisfactory answers. || 有时他会在她评估之前就把人拖走；她已不再追问为什么，因为不会有满意的答案。
He’d failed every time—much to the amusement of Chez and the Duchess. || 回忆：从前都是他在追求她、每次都失败——这件事曾让 Chez 与 Duchess 觉得好笑；“他”指的就是国王。
“You will play chess.” || 国王以一句没有商量余地的命令把对局变成任务。
Finally finding the doorknob, I turned it, quickly ducking out of his bodily cage, and slipped away into the darkened halls before my heart could explode out of my chest. || 结尾：她转动门把手、避开他的身体困住，逃进黑暗的走廊。
