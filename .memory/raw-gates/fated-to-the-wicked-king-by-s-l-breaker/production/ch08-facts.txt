I’d lost count of how many nights I hadn’t slept. || 她已经数不清自己有多少夜没睡。
Early morning sunlight streaked through the heavy brocade curtains of my castle chambers. As usual, tea had been magically set up in the sitting area. Behind the privacy screen, washing implements had been prepared. By all other accounts, it could have been considered luxurious living. || 她住在城堡内的房间（castle chambers）：茶由魔法备好，洗漱用具也备好，一切看起来奢华。
Out of nothingness, a large, furry purple cat materialized, crossing the floor. || 猫凭空出现在她的房间里。
“Perhaps. It’s hard to say.” He hopped across the way to curl up on the plush couch. “Either way, I am not dead yet, so he is either permitting my presence or he is wholly unaware of it.” || 猫说国王要么默许它的出现、要么完全不知情；它自己还没死。
“I regret to say, aside from your sudden disappearance, I’ve also come to confirm a rumor that has been spreading across Wünder. There is talk of His Majesty having acquired a new sorceress. She who decides which citizens live or die.” || 猫证实了一个正在 Wünder 流传的传闻：国王新得了一位女巫，她决定哪些百姓生死。
“The people. I wanted to help the people,” I corrected, before clutching at my chest. “My-my heart… It seems it still holds some residual power. I am able to sense other people’s intentions. I question the citizens, determine where their loyalties lie. I try to make sure he doesn’t hurt the innocent.” || 她解释自己在做的事：心里仍残留力量，能感知别人的意图，盘问百姓以判断忠诚归属，为的是让国王不伤及无辜。
Moving closer, Chez’s fur brushed against my arm. I knew it was his way of comforting me. “Perhaps I can help you escape. I’m still quite familiar with this old castle. I should be able to find a way.” || 猫提出帮她逃走，自称熟悉这座老城堡。
Not looking weary at having to go through this tedious exercise over and over again, His Majesty raised his hand. “Bring in the next group of prisoners.” || 大门再开、卫兵列队站定之后，国王下令带进下一批囚犯。
