Part of me is tempted to find the one from the fall of 1928 and save Quint ten years of heartache. || 午夜镜廊：Clara 认不出哪面镜子是 1928 秋——差点想找到它替 Quint 省十年心痛。
I was young, Clara, and I believed we could have the world together. It took a long time for that to wear off. || Quint 解释买镜：年轻时相信能一起拥有全世界，花了很久才磨掉——但「你太难摆脱」。
What an honor it was? How little I had to offer him of proof of my love in comparison to the tributes he’d paid me? || Clara 无言以对：她的 Granville House 献礼与他的 52 面镜相比太轻——「he’s a drug I need」。
Waking up next to him in a French chateau. || 想象中的未来清单：法国城堡同床、城里狂欢、车外吻、客厅共舞、他教女儿们舞步——Clara 第一次系统畅想留下来的生活。
He’s one of mine. || 窗下神秘人对暗号——Ben 雇的守卫：偷了价值百万的药丸不会让 Quint 宅不设防。
The man we have watching the house says Gibbons kicked up a fuss when he discovered the theft. || Mr. Perry 报信：Gibbons 发现失窃大闹、一刻钟前打车往这个方向——决战逼近。
Suppose I don’t send you back. || Ben 与 Clara 的对峙升级：他说「你是个女人回房去」，她回敬「你猜我不送你回去」——开门能力成了谈判筹码。
“I didn’t know if you were dead or alive, and I’ve been trying and failing to live a life without you for ten years. || Quint 的恳求武器化：「看着你再中枪一次，我十年的白活全白费」——Clara 被愧疚劝回房间。
I drift off to sleep, only to be woken a few hours later when a cold hand clamps over my mouth. || 章末：写作发泄后睡去几小时，一只冰冷的手捂住她的嘴——Gibbons（或谁？）摸进了她的房间。
