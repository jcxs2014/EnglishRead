Catherine has never been to Europe. || Catherine 从未去过欧洲；欧洲在她心里一直是「别人的事」。
In line to board the plane, she can feel her heart racing and butterflies in her stomach. || 登机排队时她心跳加速、胃里有蝴蝶。
Butterflies feel like love, but they come from fear. Fear of love not being requited. || 她把「蝴蝶」解析成「像爱、其实源于恐惧」——怕爱得不到回报。
When she was twenty-one, Elisabeth was already crawling. || 她二十一岁时 Elisabeth 已经会爬；她很年轻就做了母亲。
Catherine never got to have this freedom, this youth. || 她不曾拥有这份自由与这段青春。
It is the most natural thing in the world for a child to grow beyond the parent, though. || 她明白孩子长过父母是世上最自然的事。
She knows that. || 她「知道」这一点——知道与感觉到之间有一道缝。
She would never call this feeling envy. || 她绝不会把这种感受叫作「嫉妒」。
