like someone was taking to the sinews of my heart with razor wire || 三个月静默的账：她逃跑他懂（那也是他的天性）——不懂的是三个月不说话（particularly heartless）；如今恢复联系但 unsatisfactorily、皮夹克三个月后寄回；「she said she wanted a clean break...in the process she broke me apart; she knew how much it would hurt me, but she didn't care」；回溯：fire 前数月她一直在消失（meditation、fasting、lethargy）；当晚睡前控诉——「I trusted you, and you couldn't save my cat, you couldn't save the house...What good were you in a crisis?」；他想象她穿着火场脏衣坐上 pristine plane（ash、eucalyptus oil and fire 的 recurring image）；30 岁心智仍幼；自我辩护：不扇耳光她不走会死、也会连累他死——「Slapping her shook her out of her lassitude」；三个月后电话控诉时他破例没还嘴（自觉 fail）——只想圣经里悔改的强盗：「Today thou shalt be with Me in Paradise」——「I too would like to come into a Paradise – but an earthly one not a heavenly one.
