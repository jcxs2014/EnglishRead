the sun had this primitive and somewhat menacing appearance || 晚起赶车（早餐计划在 Roma Termini）；晨热已烈——太阳「primitive and somewhat menacing」、像要按千年来的老办法毁掉他们（正如它毁掉 Eternal City 里活过的百万人）；空街令 Katy 存在性害怕（empty street/sun/shadows）——但她会振作、stay in the present tense。
that dreadful island of ambition and neurosis || Katy 的逃亡地理学：高铁北上＝some imagined Cythera / Elysium / peace-harmony-tranquillity 之地（远离 quotidian thoughts and habits of mind）——但真正要逃的是她自己：「that dreadful island of ambition and neurosis, a place that continually made her unhappy」；叙述者：head full of night thoughts、soporific、body present mind elsewhere；Roma Termini 巴士——稳步慢行如 Bach fugue；赭色小楼＝「The Edward Hopper-like stillness of the morning. Everything out there is so still, so timeless, so eternal」——罗马是不变之城（他自 1980s 常来、中心未变）；空街讨论：North Fitzroy 此刻该有人在慢跑喝咖啡——罗马人昨夜狂欢仍在恢复（They do things differently here）；她觉核电站泄漏、全城疏散、只剩巴士上的他们。
avoiding the hexagon of mainland France || 切尔诺贝利插叙（1986 他正在巴黎）：官方初期通报称辐射云向北向南绕开了「法兰西六边形」——荒谬、旋被专家驳斥修正；巴黎人被告诫勿饮牛奶勿食叶菜（可能被辐射污染）。
the sun being seen as both a blessing and a curse by my father || 爬坡与告别：Esquiline Hill 高地；Termini 对面是 Diocletian 浴场旧址、米开朗基罗设计了其上的 basilica；「This trip has been too short」—— brilliant sunshine 勾起童年：Wimmera 麦田里长大、父亲视太阳为 blessing and curse（阳光雨水都要刚好、不然庄稼不生、全家破产）；收束句：「We shall leave the past behind. Our new adventure is about to begin.」→「I shall enjoy our train ride into the future」。
