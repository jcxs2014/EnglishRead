# ch10 Pilgrimage 素读事实清单
declare that the children should come with her on pilgrimage || Pegeen 盘算两天后决定：带 Vedra 与 Daley 随她去 Crannhyr 的 Holy Mircha 大神庙朝圣，次日出发。
the first Mircha had been sister and wife to great Brynioch || Pegeen 教给孩子的教义开头：初代 Mircha 是天神 Brynioch 的姐妹兼妻子，海是她的领域。
When my daughter is born, I will cease to be. || Mircha 亲口预言：女儿出生之时，她自己将不复存在。
The coppers that Pegeen gave us—I left them behind! || Vedra 逃得仓促，把 Pegeen 给的一笔铜板落在藏钱处，身上只剩第一笔酬金。
Daley wanted a badge, and though Vedra knew || 朝圣第一夜宿在 Killro 的 Oymos 神庙，Daley 要买徽章，Vedra 花掉一枚铜板。
a river of flame suddenly swept across the road || 林间空地两端各起一道火墙，把整支朝圣队困在路中间。
there are three score of them on either side of you || Flann 隔火喊话：路两侧林中各埋伏三十分（60）名弓手。
You are Flann Alderwood, bandit and killer! || 随行"修士"当场揭穿绑匪头目的身份：Flann Alderwood，强盗与杀手。
has used your pilgrimage to cloak a delivery of riches || Flann 说明：Crannhyr 的里夫 Hagan 借这支朝圣队偷运 Hernysadharc 国库的银子。
Daley was gone. || 章末 Flann 转身走开，Vedra 发现 Daley 不见了。
