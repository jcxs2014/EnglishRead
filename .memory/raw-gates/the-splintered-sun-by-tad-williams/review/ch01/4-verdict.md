## 已核实为正确
- 「母亲左手上那枚红宝石戒指」（原句 7 提示）：`her ring with its red gemstone still on her finger`（ch01 text）——看着像编，实为原文。
- 「twenty cubits / half dozen fathoms」：`protruded some twenty cubits above the cobbled courtyard`、`a half dozen fathoms below him`。
- 「母亲叫他 Yuvalles，他在外乡把名字改成 Yuval」：`his mother leaving Yuvalles—or “Yuval,” as he styled himself outside of his native Nabban`。
- 七人剧团：`two wagons, four mules, and six people to do all the work; seven including big, bearded Master Yuval`。
