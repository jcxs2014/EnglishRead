THE ice burned. || 章首只有三个词：冰在烧。伊莱在梦里被冰封住，冷是真切的。
It didn’t matter that it was a dream. || 这是梦，可这一点没让冷变轻。
Eli knew the cold wasn’t real, and yet he felt it, not just on his skin, but under it, as if his skeleton had frozen solid. || 伊莱知道冷不是真的，还是从皮肤一直感到骨头里，像整副骨架冻实了。
And it hurt, a persistent pain he couldn’t heal from, couldn’t escape. || 那是一种他治不好也躲不开的持续的疼。
He felt like he was back on the table in Haverty’s lab. Pinned open like a moth, caught in a state of constant dying. || 他觉得自己回到了哈弗蒂的实验室台子上，像一只被钉开的蛾子，停在一直垂死的状态里。
But this was Serena’s world, and it obeyed her rules. || 冰破不开，因为这是塞雷娜的世界，服从她的规则。
He was going to kill them both. || 他起念要杀了她们两个。
Put the Clarke sisters back in the ground, where they belonged, and— || 他要把克拉克姐妹埋回土里。
Feedback whined in Eli’s ear, high and sharp enough to pierce the veil. || 一阵反馈噪声在他耳边尖啸，高得能刺穿帷幕。
The noise—the pitch, the pain—enough to briefly fracture the illusion. || 那声音、音高和疼，只够短暂地把幻觉敲裂。
Eli tried to speak, but he couldn’t find the air. || 伊莱想说话，却找不进气。
His vision slipped—light, dark, ice, shack—caught between the two. || 他的视线在亮、暗、冰、棚屋之间滑动，被卡在两个世界中间。
He tasted blood, but kept trying, one boot shifting on the creaking floorboards before he seized again, Gabriel’s command of his body warring with Serena’s hold of his mind. || 他尝到血还在试，一只靴子在吱呀响的地板上挪了一下又僵住；加百列命令他的身体，塞雷娜攥着他的意识。
but Serena’s will was stronger, and soon the doctor’s voice dropped away, and Eli was left with nothing but the pulse and the pain and the ice. || 塞雷娜的意志更强，医生的声音很快消失，只剩心跳、疼和冰。
