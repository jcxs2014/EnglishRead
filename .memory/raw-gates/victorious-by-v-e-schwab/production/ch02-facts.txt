VICTOR sipped a whisky—neat—and counted. || 三年前的一间旅馆酒吧里，维克托喝着纯威士忌，一边数数。
He counted the number of states he’d crossed—six—and the number of finished drinks in front of him—two—and the number of empty seats to his left—four. || 他数穿过了几个州（六个）、面前喝空的酒杯（两只）、左边的空位（四个）。
He counted the number of hours since his last dose—seven—and the number until his next one—five. || 距上次服药七小时，距下一次还有五小时。
Ever since Sydney had brought Victor back from the dead, his power had been glitching, his body reenacting the same electrical surge that had stopped his heart back at Lockland, fifteen years before. || 自从西德妮把维克托从死里带回来，他的能力就一直在抽风，身体反复重演十五年前在洛克兰停住他心脏的那次电流冲击。
and the number of days—sixty-three—until he ran out of Haverty’s compound. Which wouldn’t be such a problem except the good doctor was dead. || 他只剩六十三天的哈弗蒂药剂，而这本来不算大问题——如果不是那位好医生已经死了。

The night Sydney had killed Eli. || 也是那一夜，西德妮杀了伊莱。
What bothered Victor most was that, for the first time in his life, he didn’t know what to do next. || 最让维克托难受的是：他有生以来第一次不知道下一步该做什么。
