VICTOR stood before the metal door. The one that led into EON. || 维克托站在那扇金属门前——通往 EON 的那一扇。
He knew what lay beyond the threshold. What was waiting for him there. || 他清楚门槛之后等着他的是什么。
Death. And the doctor. And Sydney. || 门后等着他的是死亡、那位医生，以及西德妮三样。
The door was unlocked, but as Victor brought his hand to the cold steel he paused, and drew the inhaler from his coat. || 门并没有上锁，但维克托把手按上冷钢时停住，从外套里取出吸入器。
He held it to the light, studying the violet liquid in the cartridge, calculating how much he’d need. || 他对着光打量药匣里的紫色液体，估算自己需要多少。
Enough, he told himself, pocketing the drug. || 他告诉自己「够用了」，把药收回口袋。
“Hey, Vic,” said a voice in his ear. “Are you ready?” || 耳中的声音唤他「维克」，问他是否准备好了。
How ironic, thought Victor Vale, that in the end, he’d chosen to be a hero. || 维克托·维尔心想，讽刺的是他最终选择去做一个英雄。
“I’m ready,” he said, pushing open the door. || 他答「我准备好了」，然后推开了门。
