### 原句 4
- **md**：引语（34 词 2 句）：It had taken less than a minute for the soldiers to go down, but Gabriel remembered Helena’s warning and kept his hand on the panel twice as long. To make sure they were dead.

### 原句 5
- **md**：引语（31 词 2 句）：He swallowed, gripped the table, and tried again. “I need…” But before he could find the words, the world gave way, and Gabriel Gray went down, his world collapsing into black.
