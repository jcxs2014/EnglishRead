- **md**：引语（37 词 3 句）：Every time Eli’s guard so much as lowered, he felt the doctor’s will wash over him. Eli felt like he was treading water, knew the moment he stopped fighting, he’d slide beneath the surface. He was tired.
- 中文理解：每当伊莱的防备稍稍放低，他就感到那位医生的意志漫过来。他觉得自己在踩水，清楚只要一停手就会滑到水面以下。他累了。
