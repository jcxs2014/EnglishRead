An explosion removed my best friend’s shoe, maybe her toes, maybe her Achilles tendon. || 一场爆炸炸掉了叙述者好友的鞋，也许还有脚趾、跟腱——叙述者试图回忆脚的构造。
He is taking off his shirt, tearing it. He is wrapping her foot like a present. || 好友那位「油滑的表哥」脱下衬衫撕开，像包礼物一样笨拙地替她包扎脚。
I am crying again because she is not. Why is she silent instead of screaming? || 叙述者在哭，受伤的好友却一声不吭——这种反常比尖叫更让她恐惧。
He will feed them his invented story and, because they know the fathers or the names of the fathers of those who have made the explosion, they will use terms like unfortunate circumstances, unstable individual, and accident. || 表哥打算编造一套故事，而大人们因为认识肇事者的父辈，会用「不幸的意外」「不稳定的人」「事故」这类词收场。
“I saved youse,” he said. “You were out of it. You didn’t know what was happening.” || 表哥对叙述者说「我救了你们」——她却不觉得被救，也不觉得安全。
Seven seconds—this is how long it takes me to bend, squeeze her hand, and whisper, “Almost there. You’ve got this.” || 叙述者用倒计时作为应对创伤的仪式，七秒之内弯腰、握紧好友的手，低声说「快到了，你可以的」。
