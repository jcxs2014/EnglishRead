Lucy takes in my room with a sadness that makes my chest ache. || 她站在他的房间里打量四周，那份难过让他胸口发疼。
“This is your home?” She’s dismayed, and wants to hide it from me but can’t. || 她问这是不是他的家，并努力不让他看出自己的失望。
No, I want to tell her. You’re my home. || 他心里想告诉她：她才是他的家。
“It’s not my home,” I say instead. “Only a bed, a table, a chair. It’s just where I stay.” || 他嘴上否认这是家，说这里只有一张床、一张桌子、一把椅子，不过是他落脚的地方。
“It’s dreadful.” She shudders. “I can’t believe you live somewhere so sad and empty.” || 她觉得这地方糟透了，说不敢相信他住在这么难过又空荡的地方。
Long-dormant instincts tell me I should have prepared for this moment better || 他沉睡已久的本能跳出来，说本该为这一刻准备得更充分些。
“You were brilliant. And so brave. You did all you could.” || 他对她说你很出色、很勇敢，你已经做了能做的一切。
If this moment together is to be one of our last, then I am determined to make it last. || 他下定决心：如果这是他们最后的时刻之一，他要让这个时刻延续下去。
For now, though, I close my eyes and pretend we can stay forever in these last few stolen moments. || 他闭上眼，假装两人可以永远留在这几个偷来的片刻里。
