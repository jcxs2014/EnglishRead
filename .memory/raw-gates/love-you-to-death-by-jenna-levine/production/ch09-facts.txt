When I open my eyes, I’m lying in the middle of the same street I died in with the worst headache I’ve ever had. || Lucy 在自己当初撞死的同一条街道中央醒来，头痛是她这辈子最糟的一次，恶心又一次住进胃底。
Looks like it made it back with me from the afterlife, too. || 她的包也随她从死后世界一起回来了，仍旧斜挂在肩上，与巴士第一次撞上她时一样。
“Give her some space,” a familiar deep voice says from somewhere to my right. Daniel. || Daniel 在她右侧开口，替她向人群说「Give her some space」，并对医护说「She’s had a shock」。
“I can’t allow that.” The paramedic who shined a light in my face is tall, with a deep voice that matches his sturdy build. || 说「我不能允许那样」的是那个给她照手电的救护员，他要送她上救护车做全面检查，说她被巴士撞了还能活着是个奇迹。
Before I can reply, Daniel scoops me up into his arms like I weigh nothing at all. || Daniel 抢在救护车之前把她打横抱起带离现场，她把头靠在他肩上，第一次觉得安全、被保护。
He sets my souvenir from Dollywood back down on the shelf. || 门边书架上放着她的 Dollywood 纪念品（粉色水晶球），Daniel 端详后放回原处。
In eight weeks I have to leave Wilf behind. He’s been the only constant in my life other than Jess, and now I’m leaving him. || 她只剩八周，要托付养了七年的猫 Wilfred；猫和妹妹是她生活里仅有的恒定之物。
“No.” He shakes his head. “I mean, yes. It was once. A very long time ago. But I don’t have a name now.” || Daniel 说「Daniel」是他很久以前用过的名字，他现在没有名字，其他同事也一样，他们互相叫「Friend」或干脆走开。
Then I watch as Daniel fades into nothing before my eyes. || 临走前他祝她把剩下的八周过成最好的日子，随后在她眼前淡入虚无般消失。
