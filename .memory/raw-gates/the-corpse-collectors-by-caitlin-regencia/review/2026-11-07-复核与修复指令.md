## 阻断（必须改）

### B1 · `00_概述.md:12` 引语后半句自造

- md：`Warm sunlight dappled through the trees of Mindanforth Forest, spattering the path ahead like someone had flicked a brush full of paint.（ch01）`
- 原文（`text/ch01_news.txt`）：`Warm sunlight dappled through the trees of Mindanforth Forest, spattering luminous patterns upon the great gray rocks that hung above a roaring waterfall.`
- epub 全库搜 `flicked a brush full of paint` = **0 命中**。
- **改**：换成原文整句（或截到 `…roaring waterfall.`）。

### B2 · `00_概述.md:16` 引语拼接

- md：`We lived in harmony, once. They banished our great-grandparents out of their home.（ch01）`
- 原文：`We lived in harmony, once. They banished our great-grandparents out of fear, not some ingrained hatred.`
- **改**：恢复原文（`out of fear, not some ingrained hatred`），否则改中文转述并标「（转述）」。

### B3 · `00_情感节点.md:16` 章号错 + 尾巴自造

- md：`"“It’s a good opportunity, Vix. You can’t tell me that it isn’t. I’ll go.”"（ch02）`
- 实际：该句在 **ch01**；原文后半是 `I’ll get to explore, see things outside of this forest, meet new people…`，`I’ll go.` 是自造尾巴。
- **改**：章号改 `（ch01）`；引语改回原文连续片段（到 `meet new people` 为止），不要拼 `I’ll go.`。

### B4 · `00_情感节点.md:34` 章号错

- md：`We have discovered what is happening to the vampires. It was us all along.（ch05）`
- 实际在 `text/ch10_just_act_natural.txt:10`（该句逐字正确）。
- **改**：`（ch05）` → `（ch10）`。

### B8 · `ch19 what cavalry.md:94` 台词与叙述并成一句

- md：`"Hey, asshole! There it was."`
- 原文（`text/ch19:244` 与 `:247`）是两个独立段落：

```
“Hey, asshole!”


There it was.
```

- 合并后读者会以为「There it was.」是她的台词，实际是叙述句。**逐字 flat 比对反而通过**（空白被压掉），所以 `verify_quotes` 287/287 全绿也抓不到。
- **改**：拆成两条，或只保留台词 `“Hey, asshole!”`。

### B9 · `ch19:76` 引语截断短于分析

- md 引语结在 `…chunks of ice fell to`，而同一块的中文理解/关键词覆盖到 `blood spattering as the tubes were ripped from their veins`。
- **改**：引语补全到分句结束处（`…ripped from their veins."`），分析随之对齐。

- **改**：10 章的人物弧线各自按本章写；至少要改掉 ch17（Vix 已死、本章无重逢）。
