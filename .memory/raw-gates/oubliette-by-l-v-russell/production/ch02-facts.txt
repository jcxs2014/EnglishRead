The red seeps in, too dark and thick to be wine || 母亲尖笑打翻酒杯，红液渗桌布——太黑太稠不是酒；宾客与幽灵齐声，「it all sounds the same」
I feed my mother. I will always feed my mother. || Rue 认清机制：Horewith 跨门槛即注定，陪座只是仪式——喂母是她终身职能
He smells of the living, and similar to my mother, I long to consume him || 对面年轻勋爵（蓝丝领巾、小鹿般的眼）心跳清晰可闻、薰衣草皂香——Rue 承认与母亲一样想「吃掉」他
I do not tell him how they sound the same when they die, pigs and men || Rue 说讨厌猪死时的尖叫，却不说猪与人死声相同——勋爵笑容僵住
my mother pulls and pulls and pulls until he lies unraveled over the table || 近晨母亲以红线远程操控 Horewith 自我拆解、喉骨剔净；Rue 怼「You promised him a kiss」→「could have waited before you ate him」
I help her slip into her shallow grave || 母亲歇息处＝铁梯末级下的 hollow；「rotting, but with sleep, she will rise anew」；母亲拉 Rue 躺进坟里（Lie with me）
