I will give it time. || 白猫 mercurial：躺床蹭暖但不熟、哈气露牙——「I will give it time. It is all I have to give her.」
she understands I will not help her || 图书馆哭书幽灵今晚不叫了——「仿佛她明白我不会帮她」；「I offer up no words of comfort; there is none to give.」
That is Anne’s wedding invitation. || 母亲咬她下巴（血滑下脖颈）、手里拎撕边请柬误认成 Rue 的婚约：「You will not wed. Not you.」——「你三天前还让我去」
will she forget me? || Rue 内白：母亲的神智在裂（memory stretched near to snapping）——「Her sanity seems a high price to pay for immortality」若成疯狂之器、行走朽墓——会忘了我吗
like one would an orange || 「I am hungry. Famished, daughter.」——母亲转身像剥橙一样撕下 Veronica 臂上的肉条；Veronica 眼翻着笑、伸手讨喂：「Take what you wish from me.」
chomping at the fragments of my mother’s affection || 母亲吐渣嫌腻（too distasteful）、一巴掌扇晕 Veronica（头撞地）；血手印按上 Rue 肩头、合着听不见的华尔兹摇摆——「I am not so unlike Veronica, chomping at the fragments of my mother’s affection」
Do not leave me to bear it alone. || 「For Anne. You would go for Anne, your friend?」附条款放行：「Come back to me, dearest, before dawn.」；Rue 扶起 Veronica（伤口她从不让人帮、I have since stopped asking）；结尾幽灵眼里第一次浮现怜悯——「And that is a terrible thing indeed.」
