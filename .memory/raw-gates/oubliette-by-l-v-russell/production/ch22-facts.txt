My mother’s nature is hunger; theirs is greed and boredom. || Rue 开门迎客：Ladies 曾 righteous glee 供祭品——「They are as I am, as my mother is, maybe more so」；无 Veronica 采购的讽刺「if I knew Mother would not eat them」
a small comfort I can deny them || 不给 Ladies 生火（取暖小恩也可以扣）；希望她们也吸进霉与尘——「I hope the Ladies feel it too.」
Would that lift the wretchness of your own suffering || Ladies 诉病（Bellamy 黑牙、Henrietta 咯血）——女仆不病她们病；Rue 咬着猪肉饼问「知道仆人陪你受苦会让你的惨好受点吗？」；鬼围观带 amusement——「It is a shame no one else can.」
Oh, how you wear her well. || 母亲入场：「look how death clings to you. Oh, how you wear her well.」——死亡被说成衣裳；Bellamy 跪求 Help me；一妇摔头「cracking like an egg」——母亲拍手「How splendid!」
I want to lay my head there || 母亲抱 Bellamy 于膝抚发（目光全程钉着 Rue）——Rue 嫉妒：「I want to lay my head there, to have my mother’s fingers in my hair...I long to rest my head in her lap.」
I will not be cleaning this up, || 母亲吸 Bellamy 喉血、咬穿牙龈老牙——「I linger until all is still」；拿酒走人「I will not be cleaning this up」——母亲沉默、无抚触——「I feel the absence of it, the emptiness beneath my skin only she can fill.」
