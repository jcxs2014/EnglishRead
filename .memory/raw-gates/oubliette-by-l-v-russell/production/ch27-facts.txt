There is nothing left of Oren except bone || Oren 只剩骨（晨光审判）：骨灰嵌进衣物皮肤——「The love I held for him turns leaden...A lending of love, but I have tasted it now, and I will starve without it.」「That love was not enough to save him. It was there, but my God, it was not enough.」
Their pity is worse || 回家：影子退避、鬼静观——「Their pity is worse; I do not want to share my sorrows...It is mine. I will not share it.」
I fit within the hollow of her neck, like I was made to be there || 下地窖抱母：featherlight、颈窝嵌合「a missing piece」——「I want to be held, to lie safely in the arms of another again and not taste dirt. I want it back. It is a cruel jest」
“Then show me,” she says || 母亲复活求示：「Forgive me. I know nothing else.」「But you could have.」「Then show me...Tell me what I must do, my darling girl.」——母亲要女儿教她爱
She wears Oren as I do || 「We are the only ones left here at Folkehaven, just you and me. Is that what you wanted?」「Yes.」「Until it falls down around us?」「We can live beneath the ruins...You will not have to leave me.」「I would miss the sunlight.」「You will forget in time as I have done.」
A wretched oubliette. || 内白：地窖终身判——「My little mausoleum under the clay, my soul so far away from Oren’s, from any stretch of salvation. A wretched oubliette.」
for the first time I hold her love, and it beats for me || 掏心：撕开胸口缝合的腔、握住心脏——「unlike her splintered bones, it is warm...it beats for me, quick and fearful. There is no love without fear, without pain.」
taking what is mine || 拉出：动脉崩断黑脓、血藤把她们缠在一起——「My mother’s hands are still on my cheek, nails digging in, taking my blood while I take hers. She arches, spine snapping, flesh withering.」
her last embrace will scar || 最后索爱：「Do you love me? I demand, needing it still, even now. Tell me you love me.」——母亲张口未答、黑泪后静止——「I have to rip her fingernails from my face; her last embrace will scar.」
