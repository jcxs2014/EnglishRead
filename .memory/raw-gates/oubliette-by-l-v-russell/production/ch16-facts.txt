The black gelding has blood on its hooves || 黑骟马血蹄血鬃如墨——她心想「好墨」并说给 Joan 听；Joan：「You are wicked...但他的报应不算过分」
Mother is wailing on her knees || 母亲跪在羊尿浸的雪里哀嚎；Piceus 牵缰抚马背——温柔的手让她「insides ache」；父亲死状：血螺旋、胸凹陷、头砸雪石
both of us wondering who will scrub the courtyard || Godfrey 哭到干呕，她与 Joan 冷眼旁观只想着谁刷院子；橙雨混血成异常的橙——「How I long to paint with it」
like a shattered cup || Godfrey 与 Piceus 把父亲抬进屋（肠子像宰猪）；颅骨碎片雪上闪光「像摔碎的杯子」；「He cannot hear you——不确定说父亲还是上帝」
For an awful moment I believe she is going to embrace me || 母亲把她推出门（要整理父亲遗容）、指尖在斗篷上流连——她一度以为母亲要抱她；门当面关死
My father’s head is leaking over the spot where I eat my sausages. || Piceus 搭话「a lady of the house, wetter and filthier than I」；她答「父亲的头正漏在我吃香肠的位置——有香肠吃的时候」
The thought of him leaving is an ache beyond hunger. || Piceus 问债怎么还——「You mean, how will we pay you?」；农场已无值钱物；「他离开的念头是超出血饥饿的痛」
tongue lapping at the crimson || Piceus 蹲下舔马腿上父亲的血、呻吟眯眼——她无法移开视线；他起身高挂血口、下巴红涎；「Come out of the rain, Sylvatica」——第一次叫名字；「The color of forgetting」
my prayers slip from my tongue with ease || Joan 撞见（她的手已在裙下——thrumming against me. Within me.）拽她进屋祈祷——祷告照旧溜出唇，一如既往
burning regret of not tossing me aside in infanthood || 母亲的手冻裂如书页翻开；「burning regret of not tossing me aside in infanthood」——后悔没扔她；「another mouth to feed, an ugly thing the village men did not want, a burden, a curse」
I wish she were my mother. || 「受苦的话，至少不必独自受」她的毒；Joan 说她「a temper wrapped up in spite」时带着几乎的暖——「I wish she were my mother. I wish she loved me, also.」
It is everywhere, splattered over the white || 鸡舍屠杀现场：门吊着（她昨晚明明锁死）、羽毛碎肋张喙的头、蛋被踩进雪羽——「There is nothing left for us」；母亲尖啸——她逃进森林
Mother will have you by your guts, dear sister || Godfrey 雪地追捕：「母亲会掏出你的肠子」；「Mother says you suckle at the devil's teat, and you'll not go hungry like us.」
I scream against it, turning terror into anger, into rage. || 树上追逐：恐惧在胸腔爆炸——「没用。我朝着它尖叫，把恐怖翻成怒火」；她越爬越高、Godfrey 更快
I chew on the piece of his flesh and suck it down. || 她咬进 Godfrey 的手臂（透过肉到骨）——他坠树「screaming heap」，头裂在树根上、脑浆渗雪；她从高处居高临下看——「I chew on the piece of his flesh and suck it down.」
