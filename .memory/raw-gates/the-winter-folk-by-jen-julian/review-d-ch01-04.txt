## 阻断型缺陷（必须修）

### 缺陷 1
- 文件：ch03 mr barkus.md
- 原句编号：原句 3
- md 行号：40（分析行）；引语行 34
- 缺陷类型：说话人/主体错配（分析层事实错误）
- md 现文：Moth's counter to Birch's "Lo is one of them" suspicion; "compensating desperation" is Moth's psychological reading of Birch, projecting her own insecurity onto Birch.
- 原文证据（text/ch03_chapter_3_mr_barkus.txt:137-139）：
  Wren whispered: "Are we sure she's human?"
  "She's human," I said. There was an emptiness to Birch's confidence, a compensating desperation that could only be human. "Without a doubt."
- 说明：原文中发问者是 Wren（怀疑 Birch 是否人类），Moth 为 Birch 辩护；md 分析却写成 "Birch's 'Lo is one of them' suspicion"，把发问者与对象都反了（Birch→Lo，且把 Wren 的疑问归到 Birch）。引语本身逐字正确，但分析层归因与原文不符。
- 建议修复：改为 "Moth's counter to Wren's suspicion that Birch is not human; 'compensating desperation' is Moth's psychological reading of Birch, projecting her own insecurity onto Birch."
