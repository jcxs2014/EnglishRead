## 语义二审：The Winter Folk ch05–ch08

### 阻断型（1 条）

1. **ch06 moonflowers.md · 原句 3** — 计数断言错误
   - 分析称「"Mine" is repeated three times, emphasizing ownership as Moth's core desire」，但该引语中 `mine` 仅出现 **1 次**（"I loved that it was mine."）；引语中重复三次的是 "I loved"（"I did love … / I loved that it was mine. / I loved that the moonflowers …"）。
   - 建议修复：改为「'I loved' 重复三次，'mine' 强调归属」，或直接删除计数断言。
   - 已整改：改为 "'I loved' is repeated three times, emphasizing Moth's attachment; 'mine' underscores ownership as her core desire."

### 提示型（只记不改）

2. **ch05 years end.md · 原句 2**（"We were a team. The Power Triad."）
   - 分析称「Moth's naming of the trio ('Power Triad')」，但原文仅写「I was pleased about the reputation we'd earned. We were a team. The Power Triad.」，未明确是谁命名；归属 Moth 无文本支撑。
   - 建议：改为「trio 被称作 'Power Triad'（命名者未明）」或删除归属。

3. **ch06 moonflowers.md · 原句 3**（月光花段落）
   - 分析把月光花的 "soapy fragrance" 直接链接到「Winter Folk's smell (Lo's 'soapy, clean smell')」；原文 ch06 将 fragrance 比作 "the cold smell of the Winter Folk"，而 "soapy, clean smell" 是 Lo 个人气味（ch01/ch02），两处措辞不同，分析 conflates 了两个短语。
   - 建议：拆开写「fragrance 接近 Winter Folk 的 cold smell；Lo 的个人气味则是 soapy, clean」。
