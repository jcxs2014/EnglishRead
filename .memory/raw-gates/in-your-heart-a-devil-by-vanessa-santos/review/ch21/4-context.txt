You will note that Alexander remains an elusive character in the book. As he was, I think, in legend. Alexander Skene, the original, was married with children, yet his family never featured in the stories told about him. When I first started looking into Alexander, the person behind the folktale, I was surprised to learn this – where, in all his adventures, was there time for a wife and children and, more to the point, what did they make of it all?
