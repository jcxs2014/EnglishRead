## D. 悬置与矛盾清单（一律不裁决）

1. **Leon 之死＝四种互斥版本并存**：①Dart 向 Harrison 复述的旧版——「Gazzie locking him in the breeding pond and finding him drowned?」（ch32 L144）；②Dart 此前在心里转述的另一「事故」版——「Gazzie forcing Leon to feed the gators as a punishment, then one pulling him in」（ch29 L138）；③Virginia 版——打、头撞石头、确认无脉后才推水、激鳄拖尸（ch41 L126-138）；④Gazzie 亲口版——以为死了才推下水、人未死、被鳄咬腿、求援被弃（ch43 L165-180）。（区间外 ch20/29/41/43 另有异文，不在本底稿范围。）不合并、不裁决。
2. **入水时 Leon 是否已死**：ch41 L138「When Gazzie confirmed he didn’t have a pulse, she rolled him into the water」与 ch43 L165「He wasn’t dead!」直接互斥。不裁决。
3. **Leon 之死的在场者**：Caroline 在场、目击跌倒后先离开（ch41 L114；ch43 L174「After Caroline left」、L228「she’d been there when he fell」）；Harrison 的父亲亦「saw it happen. The whole thing.」（ch40 L117-123）。两人是否同时在场、各自看见哪一段，原文未交代。不裁决。
4. **Gazzie 是否有杀心**：Dart 断言「You were going to kill him one way or another. On purpose.」（ch43 L192）vs Gazzie「I didn’t kill him. Gator did it for me. Though for a second—」自陈当时想收手、却被剧痛压回（ch43 L195）；Virginia 版则说「what she did next was intentional」但打人是「ran hot」（ch41 L138、L96）。Harrison 一句「wasn’t premeditated」为 Dart 的转述口径（ch42 L120 段首 Dart 语）。蓄意程度原文未定。不裁决。
5. **Mama 之死身体细节＝三说**：「her skull smacked the headboard」（ch35 L210）／「she hit her head on the bed frame」（ch42 L195）／「her neck snapped backward」（ch46 L111）。官方死因心梗一致（ch41 L222、ch42 L162），但致伤机制三种写法。不合并、不裁决。
6. **农场破坏的归因**：Dart 一度认定 Nova 主使（ch26 L108）；Nova 称单干＋雇人搬尸一次（ch29 L159）；Harrison 供认二人合伙、「two-person job」（ch35 L168-180）；但他供述未覆盖藻、霉、常春藤与「in-sync juvenile alligators」——Dart 明说放弃了追问（ch35 L198）与 ch26 L111 的内心否定；血漫办公室一事由 Dart 当场自断为超自然（ch33 L132），监控断档却另有「security company says someone deleted the recording」的人为解释（ch33 L144）。Gazzie 至死不撤 Ironheart 信念——「She genuinely believes a ghost alligator is terrorizing her farm, despite my confession.」（ch42 L228）。哪些现象有人类解释、哪些没有，原文未逐项闭合。不裁决。
7. **Nova 动机的三层说法**：「paying her back… For Leon」（ch29 L165）／Harrison 说她「into animal activism for a few years」（ch35 L186）／Dart 不信、断为想与 Harrison 重修旧好（ch35 L189）。另与 ch29 L159「It was just me」对 ch35 L168「we’d already agreed」互为矛盾证词。不裁决。

10. **ch30 L51-54 Leon 日记句的「he/her」指代**：「I’ll make her let me go or die trying.」——Dart 先想「he’d meant Gazzie」、又问「Had he been referring to Ida?」，区间内无解。不裁决。
