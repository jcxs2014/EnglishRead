## C. 母题与可引句子

**C-1 书名原句（三处，逐字不同）**
- 「The Greers built their fortune on alligator bones」(ch07 L43)｜语境：Mama 用银色记号笔写在 Caroline 卧室壁橱下段墙上的第一行字，Dart 蹲下就月光读出。「The words were written confidently—beautifully, even」(ch07 L47)；Dart 的第一判读「the phrase sounded old and formal, like a warning」(ch07 L47)、「Like a curse」(ch07 L50)。
- 「It’s like Caroline says. The Greers built their fortune on alligator bones. Can’t escape a past like that without a bit of the devil in you.」(ch23 L105)｜语境：Leon 1985 年画本里那幅墙由骨头构成的 Greer House 线描旁的说明文字；上行 ch23 L101 叙述者辨认出「Bones」。
- 「I’d asked the question before realizing why: It was the phrase on Mama’s closet wall, haunting me: The Greers built their fortune on alligator bones.」(ch13 L243)｜语境：Dart 在屠宰室外脱口问 Harrison「And where do the bones go?」(ch13 L237)。

**C-2 墙上的其余刻字（均在 ch07，逐条独立成行）**

## D. 悬置与矛盾清单（一律不裁决）

**D-1｜Michelle 之死的地点／场景有两种写法（最危险）**
- ch04 L168「Michelle had been discovered on a nearby shore; according to a news report, one of our classmates had nearly tripped over her body.」（语境：River Street 河边，Dart 望向 Savannah River）
- ch18 L321「My mind flashed to Michelle, lying on my middle school’s concrete playground. Her blood had been thin, like a watercolor wash… I’d grazed the puddle with a finger」
- ch02 L94 只说「After the incident with Michelle, when Mama had pulled me from middle school」，未说地点；ch06 L234 又是四年级游船时她在舵位。
- 三处口径不重合（河滩／中学操场／事件后转在家教育），原文在本区间未调和。**不裁决**；总览层不得写「Michelle 溺亡于萨凡纳河」或「死于校园」中的任何一种。

**D-2｜Leon 之死是多版本，且叙述者已预告会变**
- 版本 A（Virginia，海滩）：ch16 L228「He died in a terrible accident… I’ll tell you about it sometime」，细节拒答。
- 版本 B（Gazzie，卧室）：ch20 L390「We—I—locked him in」→ ch20 L396「A gator pulled him into the water. He drowned. Coroner confirmed it」→ ch20 L402「I shouldn’t have locked him in… but accidents happen on farms」。
- 叙述者明示未完：ch20 L420「The version of my uncle’s death Gazzie told me that afternoon—the first of several—was tragic… it would darken and darken until, months later…」
- 其他互相拉扯的碎片：ch23 L119「I’ll make her let me go or die trying」＋ch23 L129「Unless it wasn’t an accident」；ch17 L75 半件「a shirt torn in half, a dark substance crusting the bottom. Mud—or blood」；ch25 L66「Was she referring to Gazzie locking him in the breeding pond, or was there more to it?」；ch25 L63「Or left them in a grave」。
- **不裁决、不合并**；总览层只能写「本区间给出两版、叙述者预告更多版」。

**D-3｜Mama（Caroline）之死有两种说法**
- 医学版：ch02 L12「Here, too, was the reason for her heart attack: overexertion」＋同段「The doctor had explained heart attacks were a common kidney transplant complication」；ch02 L12 另有「blood had started seeping from her stitches」。
- 重构版（叙述者想象）：ch02 L141「it would take a woman sick as she was, hours from death, to stick her hand so far into the garbage she could’ve lost her balance, smacked her temple on the wood countertop, bled out on the kitchen floor」。
- 第三人转述版（谎言口径）：ch19 L102「She had lupus, so she needed a lot of help. Then she had a kidney transplant that didn’t go well, so.」＋同段「I couldn’t get into the night she died」。
- 遗言场景：ch07 L75「the whites of her eyes shining in the dark, her shaking finger pointed at me in accusation」——谁在场、地点为何，原文未交代。
- 序章另有「Mama were alive」(ch01 L60)。**不裁决**；尤其不得把 ch02 L141 的想象句当死因。

**D-4｜「Leon 是 Gazzie 的儿子」在本区间无直写**
- 直写只有两条：Caroline 是 Gazzie 的女儿 (ch20 L312「her daughter」)、Leon 是 Caroline 的弟弟 (ch16 L189「Your mother’s younger brother」，另 ch16 L177「Your uncle」)。
- Gazzie 的亲口叙述把 Leon 放在自家农场语境里（ch20 L351「When Leon was born, she wanted to take him right away」；ch20 L402「Thing is, I loved Leon, but Caroline—」），但**没有出现「my son」字样**。
- Leon 手记里的「Mom」(ch23 L46, L60) 未被叙述者解释。**不裁决**（结论依 ch38/ch43 的既有核定，本区间不得据此扩写为「原文直写」）。

**D-5｜Dart 的生父：两种说法＋与 Gazzie 丈夫是否同一人不明**
- ch20 L369「She didn’t know my husband, but she wouldn’t have been surprised that he disappeared. She wouldn’t have been surprised that your father ran off before you were born, either.」——相邻两句并列，语法上「my husband」与「your father」可以是两人也可以被读作同一人；原文未定。
- Dart 的既有认知：ch20 L372「Mama had claimed my no-good father divorced her after my birth」＋同段自问「Had they even been married at all?」。
- 另：Caroline 与 Leon 的父亲是谁、Henri 之外任何男性长辈身份，本区间均未交代。**不裁决**。

**D-6｜Greer House 的建造者、以及「Mother」的名字**

- Gazzie 与 Virginia 的母亲始终只有「Mother」「Our mother」「her mother」(ch11 L9, ch16 L129, ch25 L15)，无名字。
