In the beginning || 开篇创世记戏仿：起初是 existential opening——问题第一次弯回我、我成为问题之中心；voice 说 You do not know yourself——不知己的我知它是对的
Frozen in time || beginning 之前无 beginning：深处自觉知己、不能被带入问下；frozen in time、自视 self-evident、an obviousness——pathless 故 homeless；existential opening 到来前我 hover 于任何 path 之启示的可能之前——我甚至不算 lost；out of the blue，fundamental question 来——什么才有力 wrench 我向存在之问敞开
Who am I || existential opening 使四基本问题降生：Why am I here / How shall I live / What is it all about / What is truly real；追问到底皆归 essential question：Who am I?——它是一切基本问题的 ground：问 Why am I here 或 How shall I live 须预设 I 本身 stable 且 backgrounded（意义与 final aim 才可被问）；预设被照亮：Who is this I in Why am I here？What is the ontological status of the I in How shall I live？后两问同线——Who wants to know?；无 backstop question 超越 my ultimate nature 之问——此问是我最深最急迫 indeed final investigation 之基
no man’s land || existential opening 将我 squarely 置于 no man's land：有 knowledge of ignorance（知我不知己）亦 ignorant of knowledge（不知己为自身）；不再 ignoramus 亦非 sage；living the in-between——groundless；Yoga Vasishta Sara（Advaita Vedanta 之 distillation）：totally ignorant 者与已知 Truth 者皆不 eligible 读此书——唯想 I am bound; I must become free 者 entitled；totally ignorant=Socratic 式不知己无知者——veiling 仍在作用——于 ignoramus，书与 path 皆不可得；sage 无需 sacred texts——living embodiment of its teaching；generalizable：fully realized 者无 path 无 spiritual practice
sadhu || no man's land 介于 knowledge of ignorance 与 knowledge of knowledge 之间——标出 beginning；介于 ignoramus 与 sage 之间的形象=spiritual practitioner（sadhu）；对 sadhu，唯 path 能引出 no man's land；sadhana（spiritual practice）只为 existentially opened 而入 no man's land 者设——provide path home；earnest seeker 的 I am bound; I must become free=existential predicament 的自然心声——如 Second Patriarch 告 Bodhidharma：Your disciple is not yet at peace!；existentially opened 者已遇 existential ill-at-easedness——yearn for path to liberation；sadhana 之要点=close the gap between theoretical understanding and intuitive, unitive knowledge (jnana)——undo ignorance；若 existential opening 生 sadhana 之 life-need，则 sadhana 指路出 no man's land——pointer 恰于 seeker unrealizes ignorance of his very essence 时消失
continuous something || preliminary contemplations：ordinary activities（I walk / bend down pick up / see sparrows fluttering / want better life / thinking of past sorrows）皆变，却似有 continuous something 不随变——此物名 I——因其 nature 不 obvious 故 natural to ask：What is this I?
Leibniz || Leibniz 的 Indiscernibility of Identicals：x=y 则 x 之属性 y 皆有；我与 walking 同一否？否——walking subsided 时我仍 present；故我不与任何 bodily activity/sense perception（seeing/hearing）/physical sensation/mentation（desiring/thinking/feeling）同一——各物不能贯穿我存在之全程；thinking 例：thought 不常现而我常在——无可否认的 thought-free state 之经验；更锋利的否定：我非 gross body/非 sense organs/非 energy body/非 mind
knowing remains || 越探越深（inward turn）；回溯见 can't negate 者——第一 clue：I exist / I am——I-ness 而无 existence 不可能——experientially impossible；第二 clue：something knows/registering 各 objective experience 的 appearing/disappearing——它不随 experiences subside 而 perish——This knowing remains；mind 说不能：introspection=labeling（lumbering walking/swift walking）——thinking 本身亦 objective experience/mere appearance——须被 thinking 之外的什么所知——Something else knows this action of introspection——故此 knowing 非 mind
two different names || I 与 knowing 关系：若异则应能 discover difference——can I？若同则 share exact same properties——do we？test=direct experience（pure seeing without reference to any thought）；去 experience of I alone 与 knowing alone——can't find difference——两 experience 确认 I 与 knowing 非 two different things 而是 same substance 的两个不同名字；两 clues 合：I exist 与 I know/aware non-different——neither knowing nor existing 是 I 之 quality——三不同说法说同一自己：I alone=I exist=I am aware or knowing
Aham-vritti || 至 Ramana Maharshi 的 self-inquiry（atma vichara）之 trailhead：欲知 my essence（svarupa）——whatever exists and is aware；《Maharshi's Gospel》：I am 之 sense（Aham-vritti）是 the one irreducible datum of your experience——故 seeking its source 是 realise the Self 的唯一 practicable course；Aham-vritti 译作 I-thought/I-notion/I-sense（作者偏好后者）；objective experiences 皆 downstream from 我真正所是——heading upstream 只 experience awareness 与 existence 两 fact——合而为 I-aware-existing——此即 Sri Ramana 之 I-sense；quest 从此 sweep aside 一切 objective experiences、只查 I-sense 究竟是什么：有 personality？No——唯 aware presence evident in and as direct experience；age/gender/married-single？No；shape/size？No——not an object in space；remember/anticipate？No——memory/anticipation 是 thoughts=appearances；Nisargadatta：nothing predicated of I am is true——all predicates dispensed with
In my experience of space || 中心问题：I-sense 受空间/时间限制否？若受限则仍与某些 objects conjoined/mixed up；空间：所经验唯 here 与 there——两者皆 objects=appearances；不求诸 thought、只依 direct experience：我从未经验自己 out there 或 in here——非现于空间之 object；时间：time 之 appearance（或 illusion）=current thought（I went here / I am writing / I will go there）——thought=object——我非 thought 故非 object——thought-free 之自我经验无 time passing——I am not in time；I, aware and present——spaceless and timeless——true nature of I am/I am conscious？设 objects 一侧（perceptions/sensations/feelings/thoughts 含 memories、subtle thoughts 如 space/time）、stick with I-sense alone——self-inquiry begins in earnest；所揭示者：no man's land never existed
