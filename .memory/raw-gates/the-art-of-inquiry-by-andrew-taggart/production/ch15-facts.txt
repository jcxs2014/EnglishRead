David E. Cooper || 2012 年作者与 David E. Cooper（Durham 荣休教授、Aristotelian Society 前主席、著 A Philosophy of Gardens 与 Convergence with Nature: A Daoist Perspective）谈美与善的关系；起因：近来 beauty 及其在 good life 中位置的复兴
The Ancients || Ancients 认二者亲密相关：Greek/Sanskrit/Chinese 里译作 beautiful 的词在特定语境可译 good；modern（post-Renaissance）哲学此关联大失——伦理与审美被分成两域：把道德引入美之观念者被斥 pious moralizing、把审美引入伦理者被斥 aestheticism（缺道德严肃性）；作者认为 Ancients 对——两个连接：①recognize/appreciate 美（尤其不熟悉的形式）须 exercise 诸 virtue（impartiality/sympathy/humility）——prejudiced、不肯 empathize 其 ideals、坚持真美唯我传统独有者不会欣赏 Korean pottery；②Plato/Buddha/Kant：物之美在其 expressive of/exemplifying virtue——face/gesture 中见 compassion 之表达；文化审美差异=competing conceptions of the good——重 humility 的文化对别样美敏感
beauty in the appreciation || A 确认两连接（subject 须 exercise virtues 方能 appreciate；object 须 exemplify virtues 方能 count as beautiful）并问二者相干否；D：相干——exercise impartiality/humility 于审美者 typically manifest 于 comportment（faces/speech/gestures）→ comportment 本身有美 → beauty in the appreciation of beauty——receptive 之人的生活里有美；此想予 ancient 且 periodically resurrected 的 beautiful soul 观念 fresh sense；两理由：①显善的 comportment/face/speech 本身美——renders person attractive（draws us）故可称其人为美；②善人之 virtues 含审美 appreciation 之德——open/disposed to beauty 体验——being attuned to beauty 中有 beauty of soul
P and Q || A 的三位实践人物：P、Q 在成 beautiful soul 之路上——exercise salient virtues、诸德以某种方式显于 demeanors、demeanors strikes viewer 为美——voices soft and gentle、faces placid and calm、words simple and direct；R 则 generally virtuous、perceiving vase 为美时亦 exercise restraint/disinterestedness——但 not beautiful soul：something lacking 说不清、I don't feel at home in his presence（P 亦然）、doesn't draw me、not attractive
two qualifications || D 就 R 提两 qualification：①aesthetic virtues 非唯一诸德——有之者可能 strikingly lacking 于他德（honesty/courage）——或 R 虽 exercise restraint 但 certain vices（如 dishonesty）亦 show through demeanor 故 fail to attract/make you feel at home；②virtues typically 但非必然 manifest 于 demeanor——as it were unlucky：facial tic 到 Victorian upbringing 都可挡 natural expressions of goodness；或 R 眼里的 foxy/sly look 在他处非 scheming dishonesty 之兆
second-order harmony || A：R 的 vices 可能 tell against virtues 使 demeanor 不同于 beautiful soul；一个 salient vice（arrogance/vanity）或以 force/directness 显现使 beautiful soul impossible/highly unlikely——arrogant 者以 myriad ways 显其傲：harshness of demeanor、brusqueness of manner、caustic bite of words；两 further points：①诸德须是 salient 者（humility/simplicity/patience/compassion）——manly virtue 属战事、不属审美 appreciation；②beautiful soul 须 exhibit second-order harmony of virtues——其 demeanor 之 lack of discord/absence of strife 尤其美；focus of beauty 之问：P 之 general attunement to beautiful things（deep despair 时 not attuned、nothing beautiful）、Q 之 longing for beauty（觉 particular 美而无 longing）——皆指 general outlook、general way of being in the world
general attunement || D：dead right、happy to amend；西方美学之 real defect=focus on judgments of particular objects 及其 pleasure——ignore general attunement（constant sensitivity/receptiveness 之生命）；好的 phenomenology of aesthetic experience 不应限于 Korean vase 之反应、而当 articulate general way of being in the world——beautiful soul 与 aesthetic virtues 在 way of being 中 show up 而非在 particular judgment；Q 之 longing 对： pleasure 说太弱、不足以解释 beauty 之重要—— beauty 之重要（longing 之因）=beauty 与 good 的 intimacy；A 总结三条件：①salient virtues in appreciation ②generally attuned/engaged ③harmony discernible in demeanor/comportment；转问：transformation 如何发生
unselfing || D：transformation 是正词；Plato：true education=soul 的 turning around 朝 good and beautiful；此转化或名 unselfing——诸德（审美之德当然在内）须某种 release from the demands of the fat, relentless ego（Iris Murdoch 语）；无 single big strategy——right friends 重要（take you out of yourself）+ 远离日常 pressure（working/domestic——achieve/targets/plans/purposeful）之时——乡间或海边、与 plants/animals/birds 相伴
humbling || A：unselfing chimes；dilating metaphor；三步：①对 self-understanding 的 humbling——I thought I knew myself、知 greatest cares/commitments——如今不知；arrogance=stubbornness of believing 自己对己有 lucid/final/transparent 之知——events unsettled 我、显我 weakness；不能 ignore、不能 stubborn/recalcitrant、又 figure out 不了——humility=recognize 不真知己、dwelling in the space of not-knowing；D：此 humility 须抗 popular psychology 之 blandishments（人人皆自身之 final authority）——identity 大半=how I engage with the world and with other people；于此 engagement 与 identity 我不比熟悉我生活的人（如妻）更有权威——无 privileged first-person access——humility 须 admit 此；A：That one admit this——谦卑还在：可能存在更好的活法却不知是什么、不知如何照之生活
table of virtues || ②re-evaluating virtues/vices——经 Aristotle 之 friends of virtue 的 good practice，incipient beautiful soul undergo slow transformation in her table of virtues；friends of virtue indispensable——Robinson Crusoe 无 right fellows 之助难成 beautiful soul；德目位移：自 market（industry/resolution/discipline/ambition）、society（refinement/delicacy/fame）、sport（competitiveness/drive）、war（manly virtue）→ toward openness/sincerity/patience；③learning with time：不能 exercise 一 salient virtue 于孤立、或与其他德 dissonant/discordant——discover delicate harmony——there is measure 与 calmness——neither too precious nor overly coarse——discernible 于 movements/gestures/words；终局：beautiful soul 见自己 belonging to a beautiful world——world beautiful/soul beautiful/expansive outward-facing/harmony with the world
While not everything we find beautiful || D 补 harmony：非一切美物皆有 harmony/balance，但很多有绝非偶然；nose/eyes/lips 各美须 fit together 脸方美；soul 与 life 亦然——honest to a fault、oscillate wildly between devotion to different virtues 之人生=Nietzsche 所谓 lacking in style——缺 cohesion/form/grace；beauty of soul 的转化与 artist 发展 style 之转化有 affinity——Nietzsche：人生大业=becoming the artist of that life；A 收束：beautiful soul 亦是 exemplar——吸引 longing（以 humility/gratitude 之灵欲成 beautiful souls 之我们）；其 aesthetic sensibility 不在 artwork/sculpture/medium/genre 而在 making his life into a lifework；死时可无 great work 留下——唯 tranquil face 与 final words——如此教我们 how to die with lightness and grace
