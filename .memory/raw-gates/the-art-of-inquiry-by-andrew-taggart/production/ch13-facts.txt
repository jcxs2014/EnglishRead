To a hammer || virtue 之 case 暗含三德为 inquiry 可能性之必要；本章借 discernment 之德分 right/wrong questions——问题「frames/structures」何为可理解之答；hammer-nail 格言：锤子造出可理解性视域、对象只能以 nail 之形现身否则不 show up；How are you? 只能以某些方式答、答别的或许 evading；Sinatra 非续之例（Ol’ Blue Eyes）
wrong question || 错问题之两理由：①无法 underway：答案 obvious/self-evident（know-it-all 确信 lower/higher taxes unequivocally good）；unknowable——或因问法或因超人类理解力：「What is the ultimate and only meaning of life?」my mind may freeze up；mysterians（Colin McGinn）：consciousness 是 human intelligence will never unravel 的 mystery；at a loss——answerer 不知答案可能长什么样（unwieldy/overly narrow/unintelligible）；pseudo-question——irate father 之问非真问：moral appraisal/worldly counsel/threatening harm
strikes doxa || 理由二：inquiry 可 underway 但 can't go anywhere / gain traction：①early on 或趋近 existential weight 处撞上 doxa/common sense——答者 presume 人人皆信、we do things around here 就这么办、一直如此、commonly held true；common sense 之病=wheels start turning but without gripping on to any surface——问题被接收但 answerer clamp down、未 ready/open——common sense keeps the door shut
stuckness || ②stuckness：觉已多次达这些结论；only X or Y 且两者皆不 palatable；重复 well-worn thoughts 无处可去；或 half-illuminating a koan——更可能是 rehearsing dissatisfaction again and again；③discord：cognitive dissonance（说其为真却不欲信）/volitional dissonance（说该做却不欲做）；④sidetracked——纵容 peripheral matters：问题可 well-formulated 清楚可答，但所得之知不 central to good life 之本质
self-transformative || broader aim：显示 philosophical inquiry 是 self-transformative 的——带来对自身更大 clarity 而渐成 wiser beings；Ch7 分体裁、Ch9 的第一 inquiry 供 motivating force（thrown into the search for clarity）
I’m burning to know || specifications 三条接 Meno's challenge：①知我不知所后（若知何来 astonishing urge）；②对何为 good answer 有 vague idea；③有够强的求知欲——come alive to/weighed down by 已在 raise energy；alive to+existential concern+不知其为何——how can I refuse to engage in this search? I'm burning to know!
boxes in || set-up：经经验或先前的 inquiry 应已排除不能命中之答——hold fast to negative conclusions；知非 A/B/C 应收紧 specifications 以排除之——非 throwaways；如知数须大于 2 小于 4 即 boxes in 可能答集——not-A/not-B/not-C delimits existential inquiry 之 scope；positive beliefs：从一小集最相关的 true beliefs 抽取；right question lies somewhere in the neighborhood of these true beliefs——around here/like this/shape or form 之感；right question 现身时认出 crystallization
spirit of adventure || inquiry 由 adventure 精神驱动：courage 与 patience carry the pack、open-mindedness（受 right question 引导、受 specifications 约束）hunts for unimaginable clarity；临未知我 dare to seek 且 faith I shall find；途中或遇 doubts/worn-out notions/false beliefs 但 refuse to call off the search——直至 unknown becomes known、unfamiliar（not domesticated）revealed；经 inquiry 知我 hardy；bewilderment converted into inquisitiveness；following Socrates's lead begin again as often as needed——never despair or apathy 直到 right answer drops into awareness
art of improvisation || reasoning process 难以抽象描述——多体裁、鲜少由 premises 直达 conclusion——正 underscore 中心论题：philosophizing 是 art——an art of improvisation；然非 free associating/rambling/wandering about/conjuring up a dreamwork/spinning a yarn；如并肩而行、温柔而警觉地留意 sense of an ending；途中 questioner 须 humble perceptive、answerer receptive alert；渐至一 conclusion——事先谁都没看清、到时两人同时看见

be temperate || 问者收尾嘱托：made sense of your puzzle；now be temperate——勿 rash decisions；contemplate deeply what we discussed、rehearsing this line of inquiry、必要则 modify；be patient and let the clarity settle in；若 patient，intelligence 将在考虑何为时引导你
conclusion || 好 conclusion 为 questioner 与 answerer 共抱、在 clarity 中 shine forth、可感为 joy/knowing tears/lighthearted smiling/wholesome quietness；三类结论：①Affirmation/Realization/Discovery——Yes, of course! This is it!；②A Sense of Expansiveness——X is possible—who knew?；③A Sense of Inevitability/Ripeness/Opportuneness——X will happen in due course…intuition 说 time is right to begin Y
