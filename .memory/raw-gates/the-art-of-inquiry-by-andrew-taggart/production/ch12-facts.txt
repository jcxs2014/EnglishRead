existentially re-orienting || 开场：根本上我们困惑于如何生活；已知不知如何活且在乎生活最终成形——故被推动去追求 existentially re-orienting 的 clarity；proposal：培育 courage/patience/openness 三德以在 interregnum（空位期）中 carry on
Virtue is a state of character || Aristotle NE II.6 定义四条件：①state of character=settled disposition——勇敢者跨变化的 invokes-courage 情境持续勇为；单一勇举不构成 courage——要紧的是勇行可靠地自 character 流出而非 caprice 或 luck；②须出自 rational considerations 而非 impulse/compulsion/habit——不必每次深思但须知觉情境危险、courage is called for；外表像勇的行动可能不算——agent 也许更怕羞于怕死、被 social disgrace 的 preoccupation 驱动；③a mean relative to us=知觉此情境所需而不化为 invariant rule——courage 是 excess（rashness）与 defect（cowardice）间的平衡、unverbalized skill 的 just so—no more, no less；④标准=the man of practical wisdom 如何知觉与行动——目标是成为 exemplar 而非 parrot；学其实践智慧者的 conduct+assessments——salient 之知觉决定何德当运
pulling the brake || 培育法偏离严格亚氏、借 Raja Yoga 与 liberal arts；Patanjali《Yoga Sutras》第一支 yamas（restraints）开始于对不道德甚至 injurious 行为的约束——搁置知 Purusa 的 soteriological 工程，任何 ethical practitioner 须先 pull the brake 于害己害人的倾向；不宜 right out of the gates 就求勇敢——先培育 introspection 观察 rashness 与 cowardice 之常见例；excess 家族（rashness/impulsiveness/riskiness/hotheadedness/recklessness/boldness）与 defect 家族（freezing/taking flight/trembling/faintheartedness/spinelessness/cravenness）；非只扩词汇——getting the hang of fine-grainedness/nuance；introspection 服务于破长期积习——观冲动后 exert will 停止 act out；欲德先学禅坐（learn to meditate）
wine-tasting || 第二课：taste——细看勇敢个体以 render portrait of the person of practical wisdom；然 no grownups upstairs（Hine）故难 observe en vivo——转而研 literature/history/biography——liberal arts——composite 草图；Ryle 论 Austen 小说之 wine-tasting：此角色多此类 sense 少彼种 sensibility、另一角色不同的 mixture——sample and compare《Sense and Sensibility》诸角色；同样 taste 所有角色（历史/虚构/传记）以历诸 flavors of courage——depth 之教（courage 的 actualization 可多 subtle）+ breadth 之教（异时异地异条件下的勇敢者）；perceptive sensitivity 因练习而愈细；ponder/hash out/tarry 求 more complete interpretations；愈 taste 愈 interpret 愈 considerate（intellectual virtue）；后见 undercut 先评、invite refinements 与 about-faces——My Rembrandt, maturing, receives more dabs of paint；courage 定义：responding to perceived danger for the sake of the good；非 fearlessness（否则 competence alone suffices）、非 avoidance；与 fear/perceived danger/resistance in dialogue with 而克服之——listen to it but don’t abide it；须 care about the good 且渐知 general 与 specific 之 good——little by little 经 philosophical inquiry 找出真 good
age of anxiety || courage 是 unsettled 时代最 vital 之德：我们的时代被称 age of anxiety——technological society 加速变迁；尤其当前 AI——LLM 如 Claude——在 knowledge workers 中生更大 uncertainty；对 general shakiness 的合宜回应=培育 courage；禅坐观察 fear/resistance 诸 pattern（labeling 为 fear/anxiety/nervousness/resistance——barely felt、subside 成 petulance 或 linger 至避走）；inquiry 的独特贡献：questioner 之问使我们 turn toward 感知的危险/恐惧/阻抗——What, then, are you afraid of?——I don't know——此一瞬 courage on scene：认真接此问即须 recruit courage；face fear by turning toward the question；动机位移：避恐惧之欲→理解自己之高欲；aversion 之力 ceding ground to inquisitiveness（目的=self-knowledge/clarity 之 good）；subordinate fear to higher good——每回应一次 habit 更固——practicing courage by being courageous；更长远之问：Are you hardy enough to carry on when things don't make sense? / Do you have what it takes to see all the weighty questions through to the end?——多年对话使 perseverance 成为你自己的
megalomaniacs || patience：困惑于 what matters most；从 hubristic/pre-reflective（以为知己实则不知）被谦卑——无法 answer for our lives；想寻 clarity 却不知如何寻、不知 illuminating 答案什么样——能 empathize with Meno；perplexity 中两大诱惑：放手（illuminating 答案远超我等）vs 硬顶固执（死守熟悉）——两者皆拒 open minds 之 inquire；过快放弃成 cowards、死顶死路成 megalomaniacs——皆 foolishness（wisdom 之反）；解药 patience：to be patient is to hold out for the good in the face of difficulty；同 courage：先 introspect impatience（antsy/angry/whiny/pushy；aware 后 fidgety 几难忍受或转身避张力）；再观 exemplar 之 attitude/posture/demeanor——calm、movements unhurried、传达 there being a season for everything under heaven 之气；idle waiting 是 loiters in the outer vestibule——patience 则给我们深究 confusion 之所需而后 supersede 它；courage 说 Face, then persist；patience（courage 的近友）说 Stay here and think hard；无 patience 则无 exit/side door/key turning；有之则心开、attention 注入更广更深之问；在 patience 中学会 love the question、love the time of questioning——in time, learn to love it all
regulative ideas || open-mindedness：normal times 或许该守所承传统——然当下 no normal times——时代与我们 predominant 思维方式失配；许多人 closed：不愿生活航向陌生方向、鲜愿 pivot and start over（尤其 60-70 岁）；career path 之喻=信 stepping stones 由既有 institutions 铺就；遇 potentially transfiguring 之变则 feel clenched——stick to plans、play by the rules、hold fast to familiar ground、否则 feel shaky；inquiry 使我们 turn around：已入 confusion（以为知实不知）；凭 courage 直面无知；凭 patience 伸展向更 conscious/coherent/rational 的思维方式；凭 open-mindedness 学着依 Kant 的 regulative ideas 而活；Despair=logic 与 imagination 之 defect；《Critique of Pure Reason》：研自然者应 presume 它 organized into a systematic whole；同样欲认真 inquire 未知以求「it」须先 pos 姿 it 存在且 attainable——只从 past experience/history/sense perceptions 的 fund 取证则无由；唯敢想象「something」才能 earnest 地找；open mind 居 known 与 unknown 之间而向后者倾；regulative idea 是 open mind 的 fuel source、inquiry 之 condition、不逆 mounting evidence；它给两种 looks：peek beyond 经验之外+check fantasy 之飞；总结：patience slows us down、courage moves us along、openness points us in novel directions
examination of conscience || Pythagoreans 日三省（triple examination）：Where did I err? / What did I accomplish? / What duty did I leave undone?；前两问照旧、第三问改写：courage/patience/open-mindedness 三德我漏了哪一德？；睡前 honest inventory of conduct：对任一德 how did I err——overshoot the mark/undershoot/miss the mark completely？why？；次思今日之成与渐明之事（what is slowly becoming clearer）；再省 omissions：overlook 了什么？未 register danger？遇强 aversion 未 hold out？shut mind 接不下问题？最要紧：did I misrecognize the good in that situation?；以每日修习之 vow 收束——recite with unwavering conviction——Ink it, etch it on the front page of your mind
