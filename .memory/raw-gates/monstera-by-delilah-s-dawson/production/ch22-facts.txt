There was no sign of forced entry. There is no blood. Nothing is on fire. || Saskia 站在月光照着的公寓里查看：没有强行进入的痕迹，没有血迹，也没有东西着火。
“There was a man in my apartment.” || Lucy 穿着睡衣站在浴室洗手台前，对 Saskia 说公寓里进过一个男人。

“It was just a dream,” she murmurs. || Saskia 抱着 Lucy，低声告诉她那只是一个梦。
She is not supposed to get this close to Lucy, but she has to convince Lucy that it was a dream. || Saskia 明知自己不该贴这么近，却必须让 Lucy 相信那只是梦。
She’ll take care of the aftermath later. She always does. || 她打算事后再收拾善后，而且她一向如此。
there’s an old woman standing there with a knife || Saskia 讲自己从小就有夜惊：门开一条缝，一个拿刀的老妇人站在那里。
Saskia rolls onto her back, pulling Lucy on top of her. || Saskia 翻身仰躺、把 Lucy 拉到身上，两只手就都空出来了。
She will make Lucy see nothing but stars. || 结尾她要用这个夜晚让 Lucy 除了星星什么都看不见。
