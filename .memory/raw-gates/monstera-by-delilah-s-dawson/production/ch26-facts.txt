In the past day, Lucy has witnessed many glories. || 过去一天里，Lucy 目睹了许多奇景。
She has tasted sea and salt and pancakes made fresh just for her. || 她尝过海水、盐，还有专为她现做的煎饼。
But nothing can compare to the symphony of a marble bust caving in an old man’s face. || 叙述者说，什么都比不上大理石胸像砸进一个老人脸孔的那一幕。
“Oops,” she says, looking down at her hands with something akin to wonder. || 她低头看着自己的双手，说了一声 “Oops”，神情近乎惊叹。
And then she looks to Saskia, and fear grips her heart. || 随后她看向 Saskia，恐惧攫住了她的心。
“Oh my God,” Saskia says. || Saskia 开口说了 “Oh my God”。
She steps over Roger’s twitching corpse, takes Lucy in her arms, and kisses her. || 她跨过 Roger 仍在抽搐的尸体，把 Lucy 抱进怀里亲吻。
It could change everything. || 叙述者说，这可能会改变一切。
