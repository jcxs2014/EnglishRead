Saskia stands just to the side of her bay window, gazing through the fenestrations of a Monstera deliciosa, waiting for Roger to leave. || Saskia 站在凸窗一侧，透过龟背竹叶片上的孔洞往外看，等 Roger 走开。
It’s a good thing she saw him storming up the sidewalk or he might’ve caught her in Lucy’s apartment. || 幸亏她看见他气冲冲地沿人行道过来，否则他也许会撞见她待在 Lucy 的公寓里。
She does not want Roger to know that they are friendly. || 她不想让 Roger 知道她和 Lucy 交好。
He is deeply homophobic, and she is not interested in his rants and lectures. || Roger 恐同，她不想听他那些说教。
When he reaches the mailbox she shares with Lucy, he looks around furtively before opening it and pawing through whatever he finds within. || Roger 走到她与 Lucy 共用的邮箱前，先鬼鬼祟祟张望，再打开翻找。
Once he’s out of sight, she locks her own door and heads back up to Lucy’s apartment, locking the deadbolt behind her. || 等 Roger 走远，她锁上自己的门、上楼回 Lucy 的公寓，并反锁了插销。
“He let himself in. I thought he was you. I was changing. He saw.” || Lucy 说 Roger 自己开门进来，她以为是 Saskia，当时正在换衣服，被他看见了。
Lucy’s back is now wet with aloe gel, but Saskia keeps moving her fingers around gently, enjoying the glide of Lucy’s skin. || Saskia 用芦荟胶替 Lucy 抹背，手指还在轻轻打圈，享受着皮肤上滑动的触感。
She pulls her hand away. || Saskia 想起自己不该碰 Lucy，把手抽了回来。
“I made this soup for you. You have to take some. I can’t eat it all.” || Lucy 做了汤，要 Saskia 至少带走一些。
But now someone has made her soup, and she’s not even sick, and she is going to have to slurp up every bite, secure in the knowledge that she lied to the innocent girl who made the mistake of liking her. || 有人给她做了汤而她根本没生病，她得一口口喝光，心里清楚自己骗了这个喜欢上她的姑娘。
Lucy’s fingernail gently scrapes the corner of Saskia’s eye, and Saskia freezes, her entire body on alert as she realizes what Lucy has found. || Lucy 的指甲刮过 Saskia 的眼角，她僵住、全身警觉，意识到 Lucy 摸到了什么。
“What is this?” Lucy holds up a tiny seed trailing a green tendril. || Lucy 举着一颗拖着绿色卷须的小种子，问她这是什么。
Saskia does not have an answer. || Saskia 答不上来。
She doesn’t understand it herself. || 她自己也弄不明白。
