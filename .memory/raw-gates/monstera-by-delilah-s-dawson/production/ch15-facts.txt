Saskia stares in her bathroom mirror. || Saskia 站在浴室镜子前盯着自己。
She has pulled two seeds from the tender fold of her groin, three from her eyelashes, four from under her fingernails. || Saskia 从自己身上摘出种子：腹股沟的褶皱里两颗、睫毛上三颗、指甲缝里四颗。
She will make excuses to stay away from Lucy. || Saskia 打算找借口躲开 Lucy。
And then she woke up standing over Lucy, holding Lucy’s biggest kitchen knife in her hand. || 她醒来时站在 Lucy 上方，手里握着 Lucy 家最大的厨房刀。
Lucy, not in the sky with diamonds, but covered in blood. || 门口的 Lucy 浑身是血。
“I was cutting carrots and got my fingernail.” || Lucy 说自己是切胡萝卜时切到了指甲。
“I’m making soup,” Lucy says softly as Saskia unwraps her finger. || Lucy 轻声说她在给 Saskia 熬汤。
“The police—that’s why they were here. They think they found—her—her body.” || Lucy 说警察上门是因为他们认为找到了她母亲的遗体。

Then she goes up on her tiptoes and presses her lips to Saskia’s mouth. || Lucy 踮起脚，把嘴唇贴上 Saskia 的嘴。
