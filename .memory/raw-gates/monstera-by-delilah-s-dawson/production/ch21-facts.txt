Lucy stares at her open bedroom door, wishing she had a weapon. || Lucy 盯着自己敞开的卧室门，希望手上有件武器。
her phone and her big-ass knife are in the other room || 她的手机和那把大刀都放在另一个房间，卧室里最算得上威胁的东西是一只鞋。
she hears a furious grunting, the thump of a body hitting the ground || 她听到公寓别处传来愤怒的哼声与身体砸在地上的闷响。
“I’m going to kill you,” the man growls. || 那个男人低吼着说要杀了她。
The only thing she can see in the low light is a shape on the ground in front of the couch. || 微光里她只看得见沙发前地板上的一团形状。
She is quite certain she locked the door and wedged in her doorstopper before bed. || 她相当确定睡前锁了门、还塞上了门挡。
he’s wearing matching plaid pajamas and only one slipper || 那人穿着成套的格纹睡衣，脚上只剩一只拖鞋，不像街头流浪汉。
And he’s bleeding out on her floor from a stab wound. || 他正因一处刀伤在她家地板上流血将尽。
“That bitch,” the man growls, staring hate into her eyes. || 男人盯住她的眼睛、满怀恨意地骂出「那个婊子」。
When she opens her eyes again, the man is gone. || 她再睁眼时，男人不见了。
When she goes to the bathroom and turns on the light, there is no constellation of blood across her face || 她去浴室开灯，脸上没有那片血迹、没有散落如星的红色斑点。
