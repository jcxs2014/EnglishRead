# ch11 Chapter 10 素读清单（Alice 视角）
Alice was not judgemental, she was fair and tried to be kind. To see the best in people. || Alice 自述为人不评判、公正、尽量善良，愿意看见别人最好的一面。
since her parents had put her in charge of collecting the rent and paying it into an account set up for the purpose || 父母把收租与存入专用账户的事交给她，账户日后归她，所以她替室友的财务不靠谱买单。
He came from a wealthy family, had gone to a fee-paying school just like Alice had. || 她认定 JJ 家里有钱、念的是付费私校（和她一样），因此不可能真没钱。
They had struck up a friendship of sorts in first year, when they met through Matty, who Alice knew from her course. || 两人大一通过 Matty 认识，勉强算交了朋友；Emily 混进英语文学酒会并坐到她旁边。
In her role as de facto landlady, Alice had started sneaking into Emily’s bedroom when she was out. || Alice 以事实上的房东身份，趁 Emily 不在时溜进她的卧室查看。
She had never met Emily’s parents but knew her mother had walked out at some point when Emily was younger. || Alice 从未见过 Emily 的父母，只知道她母亲在 Emily 小时候某时离家出走。
In the end, she sympathised with Emily’s mother for leaving. She wished she could do the same. || 到最后她同情 Emily 的母亲离开，并希望自己也能一走了之。
Tightly wound after her argument with JJ, she paced her room, trapped inside her own flat. || 与 JJ 争吵后她神经紧绷，在自己房里来回踱步，觉得自己被困在这套公寓里。
