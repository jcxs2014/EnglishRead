# ch37 (Chapter 36) 素读事实
Inside The Observatory on Boar Lane, JJ stood alone. || JJ 独自站在 Boar Lane 的 The Observatory 酒吧里。
Arthur and Charlie hadn’t turned up yet, Matty was in the gents’, and God knew what would happen. || Arthur 与 Charlie 还没到，Matty 在男厕，JJ 不知道接下来会发生什么。
Hadn’t thought of a good enough story to explain Emily’s absence. || JJ 还没想好足够可信的说法来解释 Emily 为什么不在。
‘I’m Jake’s flatmate, Matthew. What are you two drinking, I’ll get this round?’ || Matty 自称是 Jake 的室友 Matthew，并主动提出请大家喝一轮。
So come on, fellas, tell us. What have you done with Emily? || Arthur 直接问他们把 Emily 怎么了。
The next thing JJ remembered of the night was walking the short distance to the club with the others, still in a daze. || JJ 对那晚接下来的记忆，是和众人一起恍惚地走短路去俱乐部。
At midnight, the place erupted. Somewhere above their heads, the number 2000 flashed in neon || 午夜全场爆发，头顶的霓虹数字 2000 闪起。
Watched as Matty turned on his heel and vanished, Gareth staring at Alice || Matty 转身消失，Gareth 盯着 Alice。
