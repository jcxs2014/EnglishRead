Her throat is raw, but she must still try to eat whatever he cooks. || 她喉咙生疼，却仍必须吃下他做的每一顿饭。
The power of his love once made her feel dizzy. || 他的爱曾让她晕眩、亢奋、所向披靡，像腹中燃起的野火。
The alarm that woke her this morning, set for six o’clock, seven days a week, is just one example of far too many. || 她每天六点被闹钟叫醒，一周七天，这只是她必须遵守的众多规矩之一。
She looks down at the journal resting on her lap: fragile, damaged, and barely holding itself together. || 她低头看着腿上那本脆弱破损、几乎散架的日记。
her friends and family are long gone, thanks to him. || 她的朋友和家人早已离她而去，都拜他所赐。
The unmistakable sharp click of the key turning in the lock reminds her that time is no longer hers. || 钥匙转动的一声脆响提醒她：时间已不再属于她。
It’s the game she must play to keep things from getting worse. || 钥匙声响起时她藏好日记、挤出笑容演戏，只为不让处境变得更糟。
