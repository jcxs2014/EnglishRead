Reaching to the side, I flick the stylus on my record player to the sky and reluctantly cut the dulcet tones of Stevie Nicks and her greatest hits. || 她停下黑胶唱机上 Stevie Nicks 的金曲，换上为这种日子准备的 YouTube 舒缓歌单。
Even in the comfort of my own home, I’m nothing but an imposter. A fraud. || 脑内声音提醒她：就算在自己家里，她也只是个冒牌货、骗子。
Suddenly I wonder how hard I would have to push my thumb or index finger into my skull before it finally does me a favour and just explodes. || 她按着太阳穴，想着要用多大力气头才会干脆爆炸，就不用面对这片自我厌恶的雾。
They had no qualms about smuggling quarter bottles of vodka in their backpacks, stashing them at the bottom underneath their skating boots. || 滑冰场记忆：同伴们把四分之一瓶伏特加藏进书包底下、塞在冰鞋里，毫无顾忌。
The same wish has trumped any other for many of my thirty years. || 三十年来每个生日吹蜡烛，她的愿望都是同一个：修好自己。
I spot Finlay Munro’s silhouette pacing back and forth on the other side of the stained-glass window, to the right-hand side of the door. || 她从彩色玻璃窗上看到 Finlay Munro 的剪影在门外来回踱步。
For me, it was a matter of life or death. I no longer wanted to go to sleep at night, wishing I’d never open my eyes again. || 她提醒自己为什么做这件事：那曾是生死问题，她曾不想在夜里睡去、盼着再不睁眼。
