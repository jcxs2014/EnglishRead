You killed them, Max. All of them. And it started with Jordan. || Sienna 的终极揭露：Reunion Killer 就是 Max 自己——她从手稿（Dead Girls Don't Lie）里读出了全部犯罪经过。
you pushed her. || Jordan 死因：她半夜抱怨噪音、Max 假装道歉让她开门，在阳台推了她——「解离记忆」中她听到了尖叫。
you crushed the packets of peanuts || Sydney 死因：Max 把 minibus 花生碾成粉末掺进 Sydney 饮料（她没吃所以是 easiest way）、藏起带 EpiPen 的手包在门厅篮子里。
held her head beneath the water || Brooke 死因：Max 偷主钥匙进房、把浴缸里的 Brooke 头按进水中溺死、用剃刀割腕伪装自杀——「fate would have it, happened to be right next to yours」。
She said something to trigger you || Amber 死因：Amber 说她知道 Max 杀了 Brooke「不会放过你」——Max 开车把她压在树上，「I did take joy in it. I savored every gasp.」
you picked up a rock and struck her || Delaney 死因：篝火之争后 Delaney 背身，Max 用石头击打+树上的绳套吊死她——「she was fighting for her life. But you just stood there, watching her struggle」。
You struck her from behind and knocked her into the water. || Misty 死因：Max 知道她每晨跑步路线、在湖边等她，从背后击倒、按入水中溺死。
You didn’t kill Kayla because you didn’t have to. || Kayla 不死的原因：她已经自己毁了自己——「Why bother ending her life when she's doing such a good job of ruining it on her own?」
was supposed to die tonight, too, || Peyton 是最后一个目标——Max 用 Crashing Echoes 中写的可燃化学品（黑市买、PA FedEx 寄来）处理了她的啦啦队服，用烟头点燃。
A confession disguised as a manuscript. || Sienna 的揭露方式：她撬开 Max 房间读了手稿——「你改了名字，但其余都在白纸上：谋杀、只有调查人员才该知道的细节」。
Maxine Morgan, you’re under arrest for murder. || 结局：Sienna 亲手给 Max 戴手铐——「I hate that I'm the one who has to do this to you」/「I'm glad it's you」。
Did it feel good? || 探监对话：Sienna 问「报复爽吗」——Max 承认有过快感但现在只剩 guilt and sadness；Sienna 说「not regret」——Max 没否认。
They spent years making you believe there was something wrong with you. || Sienna 的辩护：Reds 教会你恨自己——「if you want me to feel sorry for them, I don't. I believe in justice. In my eyes, you carried out that justice.」
The only person standing in this jail who deserves my sympathy is you. || Sienna 的立场：你们不会为他们掉一滴泪——只有你值得我同情；「the rest of the world can go fuck itself」。
And you know what the saddest part in all of this is? The Reds taught you how to hate, too. Not other people. Yourself. || 最终判词：Reds 教会 Max 的恨不是恨别人——是恨自己。
Then she smiles. || 章尾悬念：Max 问「你明知我要杀 Peyton 为什么不阻止我」——Sienna 沉默后微笑（不回答，留给读者/结局）。
