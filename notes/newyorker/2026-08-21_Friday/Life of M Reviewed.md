# "Life of M," Reviewed（精读分析）

## 概览

**来源**：The New Yorker, Book Review, 2026-08-24

**栏目**：Book Review

**副题**：Becca Rothfeld writes on Rachel Cusk's new novel, which follows a famous actress and an author trying to write her biography, and weighs the question of whether the main character is based on Cusk's real-life acquaintance Natalie Portman.

**主题**：Becca Rothfeld 评论 Rachel Cusk 的新小说《Life of M》——Cusk 从"Outline"三部曲向"后小说"形式的进一步演进。文章审视 Cusk 的创作生涯：从早期传统小说到中期的婚姻解体叙事，再到"Outline"三部曲的"人物崩溃"小说，最终到《Life of M》中三重叙事者（I、M、We）的彻底人格解体。

6. 《Life of M》的三重叙事
7. 奢华场景 vs 人物空洞
8. Natalie Portman 影射争议
9. 性别与婚姻的宿命论
10. 结尾：Cusk 尚不能实现"无人"的自我否定

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：砖墙隐喻

> **原句 1:** The protagonist of Rachel Cusk's new novel, "Life of M," has a peculiar technique for falling asleep.

**中文理解**：Rachel Cusk 新小说《Life of M》的主人公有一种独特的入睡技巧。

**句子结构**：主语 The protagonist + 插入语修饰 + 谓语 has + 宾语。

**关键词**：peculiar technique（独特的技巧）、falling asleep（入睡）

**表达方式**：插入语 of Rachel Cusk's new novel, "Life of M"。

**为什么这样写**：以"独特的入睡技巧"开场——以具体细节引入 Cusk 的抽象世界。

> **原句 2:** She "visualizes a brick wall. It fills her field of vision; its perimeters are out of sight. With a narrow implement she begins chipping at the mortar around a first brick, usually the one directly in front of her.

**中文理解**：她"想象一堵砖墙。它充满她的视野；它的边界不在视线内。她用一个窄的工具开始凿第一块砖周围的砂浆，通常是正前方的那块。

**句子结构**：直接引语（多句）。

**关键词**：brick wall（砖墙）、chipping at the mortar（凿砂浆）

**表达方式**：直接引语 + 现在分词短语 chipping at the mortar。

**为什么这样写**：以"凿砖墙"的入睡技巧展示 Cusk 的创作方法——拆解形式。

> **原句 3:** She works steadily and calmly. The mortar loosens and falls away piece by piece. When it has been removed she pushes the brick forward with her hand. It falls down the other side of the wall, and now in its place there's a small rectangle of blank white light.

**中文理解**：她稳定而平静地工作。砂浆松动、一片片脱开。当它被移除后，她用手把砖向前推。它掉到墙的另一边，现在原处有一个白色光芒的小矩形。

**句子结构**：多句直接引语。

**关键词**：mortar loosens（砂浆松动）、blank white light（白色光芒）

**表达方式**：多句动作序列（work → loosen → push → fall → light）——以拆墙过程展示 Cusk 的创作方法。

**为什么这样写**：以"白色光芒"的比喻展示 Cusk 超越形式后的"空白"——她的终极目标。

> **原句 4:** With her implement she begins chipping at the brick beside or above it, and then at the one beside or above that. The white shape made of light gets bigger and bigger. When it is big enough, she crawls through it."

**中文理解**：她用工具开始凿旁边或上面的砖，然后旁边或上面的再旁边或再上面。由光构成的白色形状越来越大。当它足够大时，她爬过去。"

**句子结构**：直接引语（多句）。

**关键词**：crawls through it（爬过去）

**表达方式**：crawls through 的"爬"动词——以"爬过白墙"展示 Cusk 的创作目标——从形式到虚无。

**为什么这样写**：以"凿墙→白墙→爬过去"的三段式展示 Cusk 的创作方法：拆解小说形式，进入空白。

> **原句 5:** It is as good an image as any for Cusk's own trajectory.

**中文理解**：这是对 Cusk 自身轨迹再好不过的形象。

**句子结构**：主语 It + 谓语 is as good an image as any + 介词短语 for。

**关键词**：as good an image as any（再好不过的形象）

**表达方式**：as good as any 的"再好不过"比较级。

**为什么这样写**：以"砖墙形象"比喻 Cusk 的创作路径——逐步拆解小说形式。

> **原句 6:** The reigning queen of autofiction, the literary subgenre in which the lines that divide narrator from author are knowingly smudged, she is renowned for her cool, glassy prose and her stark austerity of vision.

**中文理解**：autofiction 的统治女王——该文学子类型中叙事者与作者的界限被有意模糊——她以冷酷、玻璃般的散文与严峻的视野简洁而闻名。

**句子结构**：主语 The reigning queen + 同位语 + 谓语 is renowned for。

**关键词**：autofiction（自传小说）、cool, glassy prose（冷酷、玻璃般的散文）、stark austerity of vision（严峻的视野简洁）

**表达方式**：同位语 the literary subgenre in which + 形容词 cool, glassy。

**为什么这样写**：以"autofiction 女王"的称号定调 Cusk 的文学地位。

> **原句 7:** Her reputation rests on three works that she published to great fanfare in the twenty-tens—"Outline" (2014), "Transit" (2016), and "Kudos" (2018).

**中文理解**：她的声誉建立在她 2010 年代高调出版的三部作品上——《Outline》(2014)、《Transit》(2016)、《Kudos》(2018)。

**句子结构**：主语 Her reputation + 谓语 rests on + 宾语 + 定语从句 + 破折号列举。

**关键词**：rests on（建立于）、to great fanfare（高调地）

**表达方式**：rests on 的"建立于" + 破折号列举三部曲。

**为什么这样写**：以"三部曲"的列举建立 Cusk 的创作谱系。

> **原句 8:** Their reticent narrator, Faye, resembles her creator insofar as she is a divorced British writer with two children, but we learn almost nothing else about her, and she rarely speaks on her own behalf.

**中文理解**：她们沉默的叙事者 Faye 与她的创造者相似——她是一位离异的英国作家有两个孩子——但我们几乎不了解她其他事情，她很少代表自己发言。

**句子结构**：主语 Their reticent narrator + 谓语 resembles + but 转折 + 主句 we learn almost nothing。

**关键词**：reticent narrator（沉默的叙事者）、resembles her creator（与她的创造者相似）

**表达方式**：insofar as 的"在……程度上"+ but 转折 + rarely speaks on her own behalf。

**为什么这样写**：以"Faye = Cusk 但沉默"展示 Cusk 的 autofiction 原则——同名但不同人。

> **原句 9:** Instead, she operates like a recording device as she listens to a series of long-winded interlocutors at literary festivals and dinner parties.

**中文理解**：相反，她像录音设备一样运转，在文学节与晚宴上听一系列长篇大论的对话者。

**句子结构**：Instead 副词 + 主语 she + 谓语 operates like + as 时间状语从句。

**关键词**：operates like a recording device（像录音设备一样运转）、long-winded interlocutors（长篇大论的对话者）

**表达方式**：Instead 副词 + operates like a recording device 的"录音设备"比喻。

**为什么这样写**：以"录音设备"展示 Faye 的被动叙事——Cusk 的"人物消失"技术。

**段落逻辑：** 砖墙隐喻 → autofiction 女王 → 三部曲 → Faye 沉默的录音设备

### 第 2 段：早期作品

> **原句 10:** Cusk, by her own account, began to tire of traditional novels in 2012, when she endured a bout of what she described, in an interview with the Guardian, as "creative death" following the publication and hostile reception of her controversial memoir "Aftermath," which details her acrimonious separation from her husband.

**中文理解**：据 Cusk 自述，她 2012 年开始厌倦传统小说，当时她经历了一段她向《卫报》描述的"创作死亡"——在她有争议的回忆录《Aftermath》（详述她与丈夫的尖刻分离）出版并受到敌意对待之后。

**句子结构**：主语 Cusk + 插入语 by her own account + 谓语 began to tire of + 时间状语从句 + 定语从句。

**关键词**：creative death（创作死亡）、Aftermath（后果——Cusk 的回忆录）、acrimonious separation（尖刻的分离）

**表达方式**：by her own account 的"据自述"+ 定语从句 which details her acrimonious separation。

**为什么这样写**：以"creative death"展示 Cusk 的创作危机——从传统小说到后小说的转折点。

> **原句 11:** For several years, she could neither read nor write fiction, which had come to seem "fake and embarrassing" to her.

**中文理解**：有几年，她既不能读也不能写小说——小说对她来说已变得"虚假且尴尬"。

**句子结构**：时间状语 + 主语 she + 谓语 could neither read nor write + 定语从句。

**关键词**：neither read nor write（既不能读也不能写）、"fake and embarrassing"（"虚假且尴尬"）

**表达方式**：neither... nor... 的双重否定 + 直接引语 "fake and embarrassing"。

**为什么这样写**：以"虚假且尴尬"展示 Cusk 对传统小说的疏离感。

> **原句 12:** But a careful examination of Cusk's work reveals that she had been preparing to jettison the rudiments of the form well before then.

**中文理解**：但仔细审视 Cusk 的作品揭示，她早在那一时刻之前就一直在准备抛弃形式的基础。

**句子结构**：But 转折 + 主语 a careful examination + 谓语 reveals + that 宾语从句。

**关键词**：jettison the rudiments（抛弃基础）、well before then（远在那之前）

**表达方式**：But 转折 + had been preparing 的过去完成进行时 + well before 的"远在……之前"。

**为什么这样写**：以"远在 2012 年前就抛弃形式"展示 Cusk 的反叛是一贯的，并非突然。

> **原句 13:** She had scarcely started writing novels when she began taking them apart, brick by brick, in search of the white vacancy glaring beyond them.

**中文理解**：她几乎刚开始写小说就开始拆解它们——一块砖一块砖地——寻找它们背后闪耀的白色虚空。

**句子结构**：主语 She + 谓语 had scarcely started + when 时间状语从句 + 现在分词短语 in search of。

**关键词**：taking them apart, brick by brick（一块砖一块砖地拆解）、the white vacancy（白色虚空）

**表达方式**：had scarcely... when... 的"刚开始……就……"+ brick by brick 的重复+ 白色虚空呼应开篇砖墙。

**为什么这样写**：以"brink by brick"呼应开篇的砖墙隐喻——Cusk 从最早的作品就开始拆解小说。

**段落逻辑：** 2012 年创作死亡 → 小说"虚假且尴尬" → 但早已开始拆解 → 砖墙隐喻

### 第 3 段：中期作品

> **原句 14:** Cusk's first three books, two of which were published before she was thirty, are outfitted with all the traditional amenities: scenes, characters, plots, and, for good measure, self-conscious allusions to Jane Austen and the Brontës.

**中文理解**：Cusk 的前三本书——其中两本在她三十岁前出版——配备了所有传统便利设施：场景、人物、情节，以及，为了更保险，对 Jane Austen 与 Brontës 的自觉引用。

**句子结构**：主语 Cusk's first three books + 定语从句 + 谓语 are outfitted with + 宾语 + 冒号列举。

**关键词**：traditional amenities（传统便利设施）、self-conscious allusions（自觉的引用）

**表达方式**：are outfitted with 的"配备"动词 + 冒号列举的"场景/人物/情节/引用"。

**为什么这样写**：以"传统便利设施"的讽刺展示 Cusk 早期作品的"过度传统"。

> **原句 15:** These early efforts are funny, cynical, and so overwritten that it is hard to believe they were produced by the same woman later hailed for her spare, sleek style.

**中文理解**：这些早期作品有趣、愤世嫉俗，且写得如此过度，以至于很难相信它们是由同一位后来以简洁、流畅风格著称的女性所写。

**句子结构**：主语 These early efforts + 谓语 are funny, cynical, and so overwritten that。

**关键词**：overwritten（写得过度）、spare, sleek style（简洁、流畅的风格）

**表达方式**：so... that... 的程度句 + 名词短语 the same woman later hailed for。

**为什么这样写**：以"so overwritten"展示 Cusk 早期作品与后期风格的反差。

> **原句 16:** By Cusk's fourth novel, however, she was staging an understated revolt against the form she had chosen and had evidently already started to disdain.

**中文理解**：然而，到 Cusk 的第四部小说时，她在对她已选择并显然已开始鄙视的形式进行一场低调的叛乱。

**句子结构**：By 时间状语 + 主语 she + 谓语 was staging + 宾语 + 定语从句。

**关键词**：an understated revolt（一场低调的叛乱）、disdain（鄙视）

**表达方式**：By 的"到……时"+ 现在进行时 was staging + 定语从句 she had chosen and had started to disdain。

**为什么这样写**：以"低调的叛乱"展示 Cusk 从第四部小说开始的渐进化。

> **原句 17:** "[The Lucky Ones]" (2003), "[In the Fold]" (2005), "[Arlington Park]" (2006), and "[The Bradshaw Variations]" (2009)—the products of her middle and best period—are more like interlinked character sketches than like plotted novels.

**中文理解**：她中期的四部作品——更像互相关联的人物素描而非有情节的小说。

**句子结构**：主语四书名 + 谓语 are more like X than Y。

**关键词**：interlinked character sketches（互相关联的人物素描）

**表达方式**：more like... than... 的比较 + 书名破折号列举。

> **原句 18:** Each presents a group portrait of a handful of disaffected British suburbanites—husbands who resent their deadening jobs, toddlers who whine and interrupt, and, above all, hassled wives subsumed by a crush of household obligations.

**中文理解**：每一部都呈现了一群心怀不满的英国郊区居民——怨恨麻木工作的丈夫、啜泣打扰的幼儿，以及——最重要的是——被家庭义务压垮的忙碌妻子。

**句子结构**：主语 Each + 谓语 presents + 宾语 a group portrait + 破折号三并列。

**关键词**：disaffected British suburbanites（心怀不满的英国郊区居民）、hassled wives subsumed by a crush of household obligations（被家庭义务压垮的忙碌妻子）

**表达方式**：破折号三并列（husbands / toddlers / wives）+ 最重要的是 wives。

**为什么这样写**：以"压垮的妻子"展示 Cusk 中期作品的核心主题——婚姻的监狱。

> **原句 19:** These bleak, formidable novels of domestic desolation are crisply written but slightly old-fashioned, not only because they contain characters and dialogue (devices that Cusk would later dispense with) but because they are about upper-middle-class families living in spacious homes, typically on a single income.

**中文理解**：这些关于家庭荒芜的阴暗、强大的小说写得很干净，但略显过时——不仅因为它们包含人物与对话（Cusk 后来会抛弃这些手法），而且因为它们关于住在宽敞家里、通常靠单一收入的上中产阶级家庭。

**句子结构**：主语 These bleak, formidable novels + 谓语 are crisply written but slightly old-fashioned + not only... but because...。

**关键词**：domestic desolation（家庭荒芜）、crisply written（写得很干净）、upper-middle-class families（上中产阶级家庭）

**表达方式**：crisply written but slightly old-fashioned 的"干净但过时"+ 双原因（人物/对话 + 上中产阶级）。

**为什么这样写**：以"干净但过时"展示 Cusk 中期作品的矛盾——技术精湛但主题传统。

**段落逻辑：** 前三本过度传统 → 第四部低调叛乱 → 中期人物素描 → 家庭荒芜主题

### 第 4 段：中期的风格

**为什么这样写**：以"日益过时的生物"展示 Cusk 小说中主妇形象的时代局限。

> **原句 22:** In the prim suburbs that are Cusk's specialty, women are swallowed alive by parts they have no choice but to play.

**为什么这样写**：以"活活吞噬"展示 Cusk 对郊区婚姻的批评——女性被角色吞噬。

> **原句 23:** Motherhood, perhaps even more than marriage, serves to restrict and entrap them.

**中文理解**：母性——也许比婚姻更甚——起到限制与囚禁她们的作用。

**句子结构**：主语 Motherhood + 插入语 perhaps even more than marriage + 谓语 serves to restrict and entrap。

**关键词**：restrict and entrap（限制与囚禁）

**表达方式**：插入语 perhaps even more than marriage + 双谓语 restrict and entrap。

**为什么这样写**：以"比婚姻更甚"展示 Cusk 认为母性更甚于婚姻的囚禁性。

## 长难句专项

### 长难句 1

> Cusk, by her own account, began to tire of traditional novels in 2012, when she endured a bout of what she described, in an interview with the Guardian, as "creative death" following the publication and hostile reception of her controversial memoir "Aftermath," which details her acrimonious separation from her husband.

**句子结构**：主语 Cusk + 插入语 by her own account + 谓语 began to tire of + 时间状语 + 定语从句 when + 嵌套定语从句 what she described as "creative death" + 分词短语 following + 定语从句 which details。

**解剖**：五层嵌套（by her own account → when → what she described as → following → which details）把"2012 → 创作死亡 → Aftermath 回忆录 → 敌意接受 → 与丈夫尖刻分离"的因果链压缩进单句。by her own account 的"据自述"以客观化措辞包装 Cusk 的自我叙事。

### 长难句 2

> Cusk seems to see the conventions of womanhood and the conventions of fiction, in its most traditional and mannered incarnations, as isomorphic.

**句子结构**：主语 Cusk + 谓语 seems to see + 宾语 A and B + 插入语 in its most traditional incarnations + 宾语补足语 as isomorphic。

**解剖**：seems to see 的推测语气 + 宾语 A and B 的并列（womanhood / fiction）+ 插入语 in its most traditional and mannered incarnations 以"最传统、最做作"的形容词展示 Cusk 对形式主义的关注。宾语补足语 as isomorphic 以学术术语（"同构"）展示 Cusk 的核心论点——女性身份与小说形式是同构的，享有一种结构性的亲缘关系。

### 长难句 3

> The mush that is G is Cusk's best attempt to imagine what comes after selfhood. But she cannot quite picture it; formlessness is hard, by its very nature, to formalize, and "Parade" (which really is a parade, a procession of characters who bleed into one amorphous non-character) is full of toothless abstraction.

**句子结构**：主语 The mush that is G + 谓语 is + 宾语 + 不定式 to imagine + 从句。But 转折 + 主语 she + 谓语 cannot picture + 分号 + 主语 formlessness + 谓语 is hard + and + 主语 "Parade" + 谓语 is full of。

**解剖**：The mush that is G 的"糊状物"定语从句把 G 收缩为"糊状物"，展示 Cusk 对人物抽象化的极限。分号后的双句（formlessness is hard to formalize + "Parade" is full of toothless abstraction）以"无形式难以形式化"的悖论总结 Cusk 项目的内在矛盾——"toothless"（无牙）的形容词把抽象比作"无牙的咬合"。

## 精读结束总结

### 1. 本文核心表达
- **"I don't think character exists anymore"**：Cusk 的反叙事宣言
- **"the conventions of womanhood and the conventions of fiction as isomorphic"**：女性身份与小说同构
- **"a compound, a cell, a trap"**：母性的三重监狱比喻
- **"the white vacancy"**：Cusk 创作目标的白色虚空
- **"Faye's voice is indistinguishable from those of the people she meets"**：三部曲的叙事问题
- **"the mush that is G"**：Cusk 后三部曲的"糊状人物"
- **"toothless abstraction"**：Rothfeld 对 Cusk 的批评
- **"a floating being"**：《Life of M》的叙事者

### 2. 重要语法
- **As 插入语 + 比较级**：as good an image as any
- **定语从句嵌套**：the kind of shared perspective expressed by the chorus in a Greek play
- **破折号三并列**：so devoid of friction, so bereft of drama, so predictably boring
- **括号注脚**：(A Cusk novel is always set in a piece of aspirational real estate...)
- **直接引语+修饰语堆叠**：she "visualizes a brick wall. It fills her field of vision..."

### 3. 写作技巧
- **砖墙隐喻贯穿全文**：开篇的入睡技巧 → 中期"拆墙" → 三部曲"移除砖" → 后三部曲"白色光芒"
- **创作生涯的综述**：从早期过度传统→中期婚姻监狱→三部曲"合唱"→后三部曲"字母化"→《Life of M》最弱
- **文学参照的层级**：Jane Austen / Brontës / Edith Wharton / Henry James / Virginia Woolf
- **批评的"矛盾赞美"**："surprisingly cheesy lines are not always devoid of irony"
- **以"自由"作为关键词**：从 freedom 到 fate 的关键词串
- **结尾的悖论**：Cusk 尚不能实现"无人"——她仍在写作，证明她还有自我
