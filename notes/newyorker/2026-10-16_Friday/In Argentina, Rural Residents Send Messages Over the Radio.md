## 概览

**来源**：The New Yorker **栏目**：Magazine **副题**：In remote parts of the country that lack internet and cellphone service, isolated gauchos communicate through daily bulletins on AM stations. Jordan Salama reports from Patagonia.

**主题**：本文报道阿根廷乡村的 Mensajero Rural（乡村信使）传统：在没有网络与手机信号的偏远地区，孤立的 gaucho（高乔牧人）每天定时收听 AM 电台播出的私人短讯。这些讯息经由电话、WhatsApp 或手写纸条送达电台，再由主持人播出，成为出生、疾病、走失牲畜、生日祝福与死亡通知的生命线。作者从 2020 年在 Península Valdés 与牧人 Pereyra 共度的夜晚写起，经摄影师 Piccini 的档案、二战后电台史、马岛战争中的真实救援、Gastón 阑尾炎获救、女播音员 López 的一生，再到 Milei 时代 Starlink 的到来与一次为寻找 Pereyra 而发出的寻人讯息，呈现这一“第一代社交网络”如何在互联网时代依然作为社区纽带存续。

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：石头牧羊站的夜晚

> **原句 1:** One evening in March, 2020, a gaucho named Jorge Elías Pereyra was preparing dinner inside a stone sheepherding outpost when he remembered that it was time to turn on the radio.

**中文理解**：2020 年 3 月的一个傍晚，名叫 Jorge Elías Pereyra 的高乔牧人正在石头牧羊站里准备晚饭，忽然想起该打开收音机了。

**句子结构**：One evening in March, 2020（时间状语）+ a gaucho named Jorge Elías Pereyra was preparing dinner inside a stone sheepherding outpost（主句，过去进行时）+ when he remembered that it was time to turn on the radio（when 引导的从句表“正在做某事时忽然”）。

**关键词**：gaucho / stone sheepherding outpost / turn on the radio

**表达方式**：named 精确点出全名，赋予报道的纪实感；when 从句制造轻微的紧迫感。

**为什么这样写**：开篇即人物、时间、地点、动作俱全，把读者直接带进 Patagonia 的夜晚。

---

> **原句 2:** It was almost seven, and the light of late summer was growing long across the Patagonian steppe.

**中文理解**：快到七点了，夏末的光线在 Patagonia 草原上拉得很长。

**句子结构**：It was almost seven（主句一）+ and + the light of late summer was growing long across the Patagonian steppe（主句二并列）。

**关键词**：almost seven / late summer / growing long / Patagonian steppe

**表达方式**：growing long 把光线写成有生命的生长，画面感强。

**为什么这样写**：时间与光影交代南半球季节（夏末）与收听时刻的日常性。

---

> **原句 3:** He turned on his receiver—the program he wanted to catch lasted just a few minutes.

**中文理解**：他打开接收器——他想听的节目只有短短几分钟。

**句子结构**：He turned on his receiver（主句）+ the program he wanted to catch lasted just a few minutes（破折号后补充说明）。

**关键词**：receiver / catch / lasted just a few minutes

**表达方式**：破折号插入节目时长，强调“短暂”与“不能错过”。

**为什么这样写**：为后文“每天定时守候几分钟讯息”的仪式感埋笔。

---

> **原句 4:** His black radio was plugged into a small solar panel, which also powered the outpost's sole light bulb.

**中文理解**：他那台黑色收音机插在一块小太阳能板上，那块板还供着站点唯一的一盏灯泡。

**句子结构**：His black radio was plugged into a small solar panel（主句，被动）+ which also powered the outpost's sole light bulb（非限定性定语从句）。

**关键词**：plugged into / solar panel / sole light bulb

**表达方式**：sole 强调物质匮乏：一灯一机即全部现代设施。

**为什么这样写**：用具体物件写出隔绝程度，收音机与灯泡同等重要。

---

> **原句 5:** Pereyra, who was twenty-seven, lived alone on the coast of the Península Valdés, an egg-shaped landmass in southern Argentina which juts out into the Atlantic Ocean.

**中文理解**：二十七岁的 Pereyra 独居在 Península Valdés 的海岸上，那是一块位于阿根廷南部的蛋形陆地，向大西洋突出。

**句子结构**：Pereyra（主语）+ who was twenty-seven（非限定性定语从句）+ lived alone on the coast of the Península Valdés（谓语）+ an egg-shaped landmass in southern Argentina which juts out into the Atlantic Ocean（同位语加定语从句）。

**关键词**：lived alone / egg-shaped landmass / juts out into

**表达方式**：egg-shaped 的比喻让地图形状可视化；juts out 写出半岛的突兀。

**为什么这样写**：人物小传式交代年龄、独居与地理坐标，确立 remoteness。

---

> **原句 6:** His outpost was fifty miles from the nearest village—to get there, he had to navigate a rough dirt road across a desert—and more than a hundred miles from the nearest small cities with radio stations, Trelew and Puerto Madryn.

**中文理解**：他的站点离最近的村子五十英里——要去那里，他得开过一条穿过沙漠的崎岖土路——离有电台的最近小城 Trelew 和 Puerto Madryn 则一百多英里。

**句子结构**：His outpost was fifty miles from the nearest village（主句）+ to get there, he had to navigate a rough dirt road across a desert（破折号插入）+ and more than a hundred miles from the nearest small cities（省略 was 的并列）。

**关键词**：fifty miles / navigate a rough dirt road / more than a hundred miles

**表达方式**：双破折号插入路况细节，距离数字层层加码。

**为什么这样写**：用里程数量化孤立，为“靠电台联系世界”提供合理性。

---

> **原句 7:** He spent his days repairing wells and tending to flocks of sheep owned by one of the area's leading wool-producing families.

**中文理解**：他白天修水井、照看羊群，那些羊属于当地一个主要产羊毛家族。

**句子结构**：He spent his days repairing wells and tending to flocks of sheep（主句，spend doing）+ owned by one of the area's leading wool-producing families（过去分词短语修饰 sheep）。

**关键词**：repairing wells / tending to flocks / wool-producing families

**表达方式**：repairing 与 tending 并列，勾勒牧工日常的两项核心劳作。

**为什么这样写**：交代雇佣关系与生计，呼应后文大庄园主办电台的历史。

---

> **原句 8:** He had no internet access or cellphone service.

**中文理解**：他没有网络，也没有手机信号。

**句子结构**：He had no internet access or cellphone service（简单主谓宾句）。

**关键词**：no internet access / cellphone service

**表达方式**：短句直陈，no 一口气否定两种现代联系方式。

**为什么这样写**：全文前提的极简陈述，电台是唯一通道的原因。

---

> **原句 9:** Even the AM-radio reception was unstable.

**中文理解**：连 AM 电台的接收都不稳定。

**句子结构**：Even the AM-radio reception was unstable（简单主系表句，even 强调）。

**关键词**：Even / AM-radio reception / unstable

**表达方式**：Even 把“唯一通道也不可靠”的处境推向极致。

**为什么这样写**：为下一句拍打收音机的动作做铺垫，困境中见幽默。

---

> **原句 10:** He had to whack his receiver a few times before it stopped crackling.

**中文理解**：他得拍接收器几下，它才不滋滋作响。

**句子结构**：He had to whack his receiver a few times（主句）+ before it stopped crackling（时间状语从句）。

**关键词**：whack / a few times / stopped crackling

**表达方式**：whack 与 crackling 都是拟声感强的口语词，画面与声音俱全。

**为什么这样写**：以笨拙可爱的细节收束开篇段落，人物立刻立体。

---

**段落逻辑：** 夜晚备餐 → 想起开收音机 → 开机（节目很短）→ 太阳能供电 → 人物与半岛坐标 → 距离量化孤立 → 日常劳作 → 无网无信号 → 连 AM 都不稳 → 拍打收音机

### 第 2 段：节目机制

> **原句 11:** The program about to start was called “Mensajes al Poblador Rural,” or “Messages to the Rural Settler.”

**中文理解**：即将开始的节目叫“Mensajes al Poblador Rural”，即“致乡村定居者的讯息”。

**句子结构**：The program about to start（主语，about to start 修饰 program）+ was called（谓语）+ 节目名（宾语，西班牙语原名加英语翻译）。

**关键词**：about to start / was called / Messages to the Rural Settler

**表达方式**：西语原名加英语直译并置，尊重原文又照顾读者。

**为什么这样写**：点题：全文核心对象正式亮相，双语呈现其文化身份。

---

> **原句 12:** Several times each day, major AM-radio stations in Argentina broadcast short personal messages intended for residents of its remote and sparsely populated regions, most of them pastoralists who otherwise have little or no way of communicating with one another, or with the outside world.

**中文理解**：每天数次，阿根廷的主要 AM 电台播出简短私人讯息，对象是偏远人烟稀少地区的居民，他们多为牧民，否则几乎无法彼此联络，也无法联系外部世界。

**句子结构**：Several times each day（频率状语）+ major AM-radio stations broadcast short personal messages（主句）+ intended for residents of its remote and sparsely populated regions（分词短语）+ most of them pastoralists who otherwise have little or no way of communicating（同位语加定语从句）。

**关键词**：Several times each day / sparsely populated / pastoralists / otherwise

**表达方式**：otherwise 一词道尽别无选择的处境，长定语层层限定受众。

**为什么这样写**：一句话讲清机制的频率、渠道、受众与必要性，是全文的事实地基。

---

> **原句 13:** As long as a message can reach the nearest radio station—whether via phone call, WhatsApp, or a scrawled note delivered by hand—it is read aloud during that day's programming.

**中文理解**：只要一条讯息能送到最近的电台——无论经电话、WhatsApp，还是一张手递的手写纸条——就会在当天的节目里被朗读出来。

**句子结构**：As long as a message can reach the nearest radio station（条件状语从句）+ whether via phone call, WhatsApp, or a scrawled note delivered by hand（破折号插入三种渠道）+ it is read aloud during that day's programming（主句，被动）。

**关键词**：As long as / via / scrawled note delivered by hand / read aloud

**表达方式**：从 phone call 到手写纸条的渠道并列，写出技术层叠：新旧方式共存。

**为什么这样写**：强调门槛之低：只要送到，就播出；这是该传统普及的关键。

---

> **原句 14:** Every night, Pereyra listened at the same time, hoping to receive a brief update from his family members, most of whom worked on ranches scattered across thousands of square miles.

**中文理解**：每天晚上同一时间 Pereyra 都收听，希望收到家人的简短消息，他们大多在散布于数千平方英里上的牧场工作。

**句子结构**：Every night, Pereyra listened at the same time（主句）+ hoping to receive a brief update from his family members（分词短语表目的）+ most of whom worked on ranches scattered across thousands of square miles（非限定性定语从句）。

**关键词**：at the same time / brief update / scattered across thousands of square miles

**表达方式**：same time 与 scattered 形成对照：守时的人 vs 四散的家人。

**为什么这样写**：把机制落回人物：收听是 Pereyra 与家人唯一的纽带。

**段落逻辑：** 节目报名 → 播出频率与受众 → 送达即播出的低门槛 → Pereyra 每晚守候家人消息

### 第 3 段：疫情前夜的平淡讯息

> **原句 15:** At the time, I was travelling around Argentina and had found my way to the peninsula, which is famous for its wildlife.

**中文理解**：那时我正在阿根廷各地旅行，辗转到了这个以野生动物闻名的半岛。

**句子结构**：At the time（时间状语）+ I was travelling around Argentina and had found my way to the peninsula（主句，过去进行加过去完成）+ which is famous for its wildlife（非限定性定语从句）。

**关键词**：travelling around / found my way / famous for its wildlife

**表达方式**：found my way 暗示旅途的辗转偶然，第一人称进入现场。

**为什么这样写**：记者现身，确立目击者视角，为共听之夜背书。

---

> **原句 16:** Elsewhere, the *covid* pandemic was just taking hold, and would soon make extended isolation a strange new experience for billions of people.

**中文理解**：在别处，新冠疫情刚刚蔓延，很快会让长期隔离成为数十亿人的陌生新体验。

**句子结构**：Elsewhere（地点状语）+ the *covid* pandemic was just taking hold（主句一）+ and would soon make extended isolation a strange new experience for billions of people（主句二并列）。

**关键词**：Elsewhere / taking hold / extended isolation / strange new experience

**表达方式**：Elsewhere 与此处对照：世界将要体验的，正是这里的常态。

**为什么这样写**：时间锚点（2020 年 3 月）赋予故事全球共振：孤立即将成为人类共同经验。

---

> **原句 17:** It was eerie to be in a place where communal solitude had always been the norm.

**中文理解**：身处一个“共同的孤独”向来就是常态的地方，感觉诡异。

**句子结构**：It was eerie to be in a place（主句，形式主语）+ where communal solitude had always been the norm（定语从句）。

**关键词**：eerie / communal solitude / the norm

**表达方式**：communal solitude 是矛盾修辞：孤独而共同，恰是全文题眼。

**为什么这样写**：一句点出主题：在这里，孤独是集体生活方式。

---

> **原句 18:** The messages I remember listening to with Pereyra that night were surprisingly mundane.

**中文理解**：那晚我和 Pereyra 一起听的讯息，记得的几条都平淡得惊人。

## 长难句专项

**长难句 1**（原句 12）：Several times each day, major AM-radio stations in Argentina broadcast short personal messages intended for residents of its remote and sparsely populated regions, most of them pastoralists who otherwise have little or no way of communicating with one another, or with the outside world.

- 主干：Several times each day + stations broadcast messages
- 解析：频率状语开篇；intended for 分词限定受众；most of them pastoralists 同位语再加 who 定语从句，otherwise 一词收束别无选择；with one another / with the outside world 双重隔绝。

**长难句 2**（原句 58）：Although the Andes form the country's western border, much of Argentina is flat, particularly in large swaths of Patagonia and the Gran Chaco, allowing AM signals to travel hundreds of miles without obstruction.

- 主干：Although [concession] + much of Argentina is flat + allowing [result]
- 解析：让步先抑安第斯；particularly 插入细化平原范围；allowing 分词自然导出技术后果；without obstruction 点出 AM 在此的物理优势。

**长难句 3**（原句 75）：The Argentinean military, unsure of the condition of the ship and its crew, assumed that someone on board would have a battery-powered radio, and used a Mensajero Rural to relay a message to the captain, Sergio Gómez Roca, advising him to go to the port town of Puerto Deseado.

- 主干：military assumed that [clause] + used a Mensajero Rural to relay
- 解析：unsure 插入写出绝境；assumed 加 would have 层层假设；relay 点出军方借用民间渠道；advising 分词写出讯息内容，公私界限消融。

**长难句 4**（原句 103）：With Arteaga holding his ailing son across his lap, they set off across the mountain—a journey of more than six miles down sinuous trails, around narrow cliff ledges, and through icy, rushing waters.

- 主干：With [absolute] + they set off + a journey [appositive]
- 解析：独立主格写怀抱；破折号后同位语展开路程；down / around / through 三介词立体写险；icy, rushing 双形容词写水之刺骨。

**长难句 5**（原句 239）：I thought about how there are certain things we do for reasons of need, or want, and how there are other customs that we maintain even after they've stopped making sense, because we have never known another way.

- 主干：I thought about how [A] and how [B] + because [C]
- 解析：双 how 从句对照需要与习俗；even after 让步写出非理性维持；because 从句落到 never known another way，习惯的深渊即答案。

## 精读结束总结

1. 本文核心表达
- Mensajero Rural 是前互联网时代的“第一代社交网络”：送达即播出、免费、第三人称程式（communicates / wishes / is advised），在电话到不了处繁荣。
- 公放迫使私语发展出暗语美学：省细节、老地方、预设 code words；主持人保真责任重大，误读可致命。
- 电台的功能从急件转向社交符号：gossip、陪伴（companion）、电波聚会（gathered together）；互联网（Starlink）改变渠道，未改变仪式。
- 个人命运线：Pereyra 的失落与重逢、López 的一生展开（unfolded）、Molina 的淡然（listen for anybody）、Gladys 的等待创伤。

2. 重要语法
- 第三人称转述程式：communicates to him that… / wishes him… / is advised that…（间接引语的正式变体）。
-让步对照：Although… / Despite… / even though… / Unlike Pereyra…（转折是全文推进器）。
- 同位语加定语从句的人物登场程式：a host at…, who… / a sixty-eight-year-old rancher who…。
- 完成时的时间重量：has become entrenched / had been killed / had lost track of。

3. 写作技巧
- 双线叙事：医疗队出发与儿子病重（18–20）、寻人讯息发出与人链打通（45–48），交汇即高潮。
- 互文对照：Pereyra 落空 vs Molina 淡然；Chatwin 夜穿 vs 作者日行；López 父亲姿势 vs Molina 姿势。
- 实录主义：三则范例（16）、三则 11:30（34）、病讯与寻牛，全文靠真讯息推进而非议论。
- 物件象征：太阳能收音机、快没电的电池、墙角吉他，始于收音机终于吉他。

- 仪式定义：they all engaged in the same ritual: sitting in silence, hoping for a few seconds of information（共同仪式）
- 孤独题眼：communal solitude had always been the norm（共同的孤独向来是常态）
- 习惯经济学：they put batteries into their radios only for the Mensajero Rural（电池只配给最重要的）
