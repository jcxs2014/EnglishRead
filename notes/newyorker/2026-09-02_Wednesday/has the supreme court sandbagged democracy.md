# Has the Supreme Court Sandbagged Democracy?（精读分析）

## 概览

**来源**：The New Yorker / Comment **栏目**：政治评论 **副题**：最高法院的反民主权力史

**主题**：本文以多部新书（Bowie & Renan的"Supremacy"、Chemerinsky的著作、Sunstein的评论等）为线索，梳理美国最高法院从Marbury v. Madison（1803）到当代的"横向审查权"（horizontal review）历史——即法院推翻国会立法的权力。核心论点：这种权力历史上反复被用于阻挠进步立法（从废奴到劳工权利到民权），且最激烈的反对者恰恰是废奴主义者、劳工组织者、妇女参政权论者和民权活动家。文章以Trump对法院的不耐烦收尾，暗示这一权力在当代面临新的挑战。

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：共和党冒犯清单 → Garland事件

> **原句 1:** The liberal list of Republican offenses is long—so long, indeed, that the outrages blur.

**中文理解**：自由派对共和党冒犯的清单很长——确实太长了，以至于那些暴行都模糊了。

**句子结构**：The liberal list of Republican offenses is long（主句）+ so long, indeed, that the outrages blur（结果状语从句）。

**关键词**：liberal list / Republican offenses / so long that / outrages blur

**表达方式**：so long that the outrages blur 是讽刺——太多冒犯以至于无法分辨每一个；outrages（暴行）是强烈的价值判断。

**为什么这样写**：以"清单太长"开篇——暗示共和党的冒犯已经多到令人麻木，但其中一个仍然"fester"（化脓）。

---

> **原句 2:** That time Ted Cruz shut down the government for two weeks seems like a century ago, and, if anyone was ever angry about the White House promoting Melania Trump's jewelry line, I can't remember.

**中文理解**：Ted Cruz关闭政府两周那次感觉像一个世纪前了，而且如果有人曾对白宫推广Melania Trump的珠宝系列感到愤怒，我记不起来了。

**句子结构**：That time Ted Cruz shut down the government for two weeks（主语从句）+ seems like a century ago（谓语）+ and, if anyone was ever angry about…（条件状语从句）+ I can't remember（主句）。

**关键词**：Ted Cruz shut down the government / seems like a century ago / Melania Trump's jewelry line / I can't remember

**表达方式**：seems like a century ago 是夸张——暗示时间已经久到人们忘记了；I can't remember 是讽刺——暗示那些愤怒已经消散。

**为什么这样写**：用两个"已经忘记"的例子铺垫——但有一个事件"仍然fester"，引出Garland事件。

---

> **原句 3:** One wound still festers, though: the Senate's refusal in 2016 to consider Barack Obama's Supreme Court nominee, Merrick Garland.

**中文理解**：但有一个伤口仍在化脓：参议院2016年拒绝审议Barack Obama的最高法院提名者Merrick Garland。

**句子结构**：One wound still festers, though（主句 + 转折）+ 冒号后同位语：the Senate's refusal in 2016 to consider Barack Obama's Supreme Court nominee, Merrick Garland。

**关键词**：One wound still festers / Senate's refusal / 2016 / Merrick Garland

**表达方式**：wound still festers（伤口仍在化脓）是医学隐喻——暗示Garland事件不是"过去的事"，而是"仍在感染"的政治创伤。

**为什么这样写**：从"已经忘记的冒犯"过渡到"仍然化脓的伤口"——Garland事件是全文的核心起点。

---

### 第 2 段：Obama提名Garland + 共和党的拒绝

> **原句 4:** Even before Obama named a candidate to fill the seat left vacant by Antonin Scalia's sudden death, Republicans had announced their opposition.

**中文理解**：甚至在Obama提名填补Antonin Scalia突然去世留下的空缺的候选人之前，共和党人就已宣布反对。

**句子结构**：Even before Obama named a candidate…（时间状语从句）+ Republicans had announced their opposition（主句，过去完成时）。

**关键词**：Even before / Scalia's sudden death / Republicans had announced their opposition

**表达方式**：Even before 暗示共和党的反对是"预设的"——不是针对候选人，而是针对任何Obama提名的人；had announced 是过去完成时，强调反对先于提名。

**为什么这样写**：揭示共和党策略的本质——不是"反对Garland"，而是"反对Obama的任何提名"。

---

> **原句 5:** By nominating Garland, a moderate with admirers on both sides of the widening aisle, Obama offered Republicans a gamble: take Garland now or face whatever Pan-African, gender-free communist Hillary Clinton would summon from the judicial depths when she stepped into the Oval Office in 2017.

**中文理解**：通过提名Garland——一个在日益分裂的两党中都拥有仰慕者的温和派——Obama给共和党人提供了一个赌注：现在接受Garland，或者面对Hillary Clinton在2017年入主白宫时从司法深渊中召唤出的任何泛非主义、性别自由的共产主义者。

**句子结构**：By nominating Garland（方式状语）+ a moderate with admirers on both sides of the widening aisle（同位语）+ Obama offered Republicans a gamble（主句）+ 冒号后两个选项：take Garland now or face whatever…（选择结构）+ when she stepped into the Oval Office in 2017（时间状语从句）。

**关键词**：moderate with admirers on both sides / widening aisle / a gamble / Pan-African, gender-free communist Hillary Clinton / judicial depths

**表达方式**：Pan-African, gender-free communist 是讽刺性夸张——将Hillary Clinton描述为"泛非主义、性别自由的共产主义者"，暗示共和党对Clinton的恐惧是极端化的；judicial depths（司法深渊）将法官提名描述为"从深渊中召唤"。

**为什么这样写**：用讽刺性夸张展示共和党的"赌注"逻辑——他们宁愿冒险让Clinton提名，也不愿接受Obama的温和派。

---

### 第 3 段：Trump "Supreme Court"口号 + 三名大法官

> **原句 6:** Blocking Garland helped their flailing candidate, Donald Trump.

**中文理解**：阻止Garland帮助了他们摇摇欲坠的候选人Donald Trump。

**句子结构**：Blocking Garland（动名词主语）+ helped their flailing candidate（谓语 + 宾语）+ Donald Trump（同位语）。

**关键词**：Blocking Garland / flailing candidate / Donald Trump

**表达方式**：flailing（摇摇欲坠的）暗示Trump当时处境不佳——Garland事件帮助了一个处于困境的候选人。

**为什么这样写**：揭示Garland事件的政治后果——它不只是"阻挠"，而是"帮助"了Trump。

---

> **原句 7:** He boiled his pitch to hesitant conservatives down to two words, shouted at rallies: "Supreme Court."

**中文理解**：他将对犹豫不决的保守派的推销简化为两个字，在集会上喊出："最高法院。"

**句子结构**：He boiled his pitch to hesitant conservatives down to two words（主句）+ shouted at rallies（分词短语）+ "Supreme Court"（直接引语）。

**关键词**：boiled his pitch down to two words / hesitant conservatives / shouted at rallies / "Supreme Court"

**表达方式**：boiled down to two words（简化为两个字）是极度精炼——将复杂的司法政策简化为"最高法院"四个音节；shouted at rallies 暗示这是煽动性的、情绪化的推销。

**为什么这样写**：展示Trump策略的本质——将Garland事件转化为竞选口号。

---

> **原句 8:** Trump lost the popular vote but won the election.

**中文理解**：Trump输掉了普选但赢得了选举。

**句子结构**：Trump lost the popular vote but won the election（简单句，but 连接两个对比分句）。

**关键词**：lost the popular vote / won the election

**表达方式**：lost…but won 是对比结构——暗示选举制度的反多数主义特征。

**为什么这样写**：一句话交代Trump的当选方式——不是通过多数票，而是通过选举人团制度。

---

> **原句 9:** Extraordinarily, this allowed him to appoint three Justices: Neil Gorsuch to fill Scalia's seat, Brett Kavanaugh to fill that of the retiring Anthony Kennedy, and Amy Coney Barrett, whom Republicans rushed to confirm after Ruth Bader Ginsburg died, at eighty-seven, less than two months before the 2020 election.

**中文理解**：不可思议的是，这使他得以任命三位大法官：Neil Gorsuch填补Scalia的空缺，Brett Kavanaugh填补退休的Anthony Kennedy的空缺，以及Amy Coney Barrett——共和党人在Ruth Bader Ginsburg以87岁高龄去世后不到两个月、2020年大选前就急于确认了她。

**句子结构**：Extraordinarily（状语）+ this allowed him to appoint three Justices（主句）+ 冒号后三个并列：Neil Gorsuch to fill Scalia's seat / Brett Kavanaugh to fill that of Kennedy / Amy Coney Barrett, whom Republicans rushed to confirm after…（定语从句 + 时间状语）。

**关键词**：Extraordinarily / three Justices / Gorsuch / Kavanaugh / Barrett / rushed to confirm / Ruth Bader Ginsburg died / less than two months before the 2020 election

**表达方式**：Extraordinarily（不可思议地）暗示一个人任命三位大法官是罕见的；rushed to confirm（急于确认）暗示共和党在Ginsburg去世后立即行动——与2016年拒绝Garland形成对比。

**为什么这样写**：用"rushed to confirm"揭示共和党的双重标准——2016年拒绝Obama的提名（"距离选举太近"），2020年急于确认Trump的提名（"距离选举不到两个月"）。

---

> **原句 10:** Trump increased his impact by selecting youthful appointees.

**中文理解**：Trump通过选择年轻的被任命者来扩大他的影响力。

**句子结构**：Trump increased his impact（主句）+ by selecting youthful appointees（方式状语）。

**关键词**：increased his impact / youthful appointees

**表达方式**：youthful appointees 暗示Trump的策略——选择年轻法官以确保长期影响力。

**为什么这样写**：从"三位大法官"过渡到"长期影响"——Trump不只是填补空缺，而是确保未来几十年的保守派多数。

---

> **原句 11:** If they last as long as Ginsburg did, the oldest, Kavanaugh, will serve through 2052, and the youngest, Barrett, until 2059.

**中文理解**：如果他们像Ginsburg一样长寿，最年长的Kavanaugh将任职到2052年，最年轻的Barrett将任职到2059年。

**句子结构**：If they last as long as Ginsburg did（条件状语从句）+ the oldest, Kavanaugh, will serve through 2052（主句1）+ and the youngest, Barrett, until 2059（主句2，省略will serve）。

**关键词**：last as long as Ginsburg did / through 2052 / until 2059

**表达方式**：2052和2059是具体年份——将"影响力"量化为时间跨度。

**为什么这样写**：用具体年份展示Trump任命的长期影响——他的总统任期只有4年，但影响将持续30+年。

### 第 4 段：法院的反多数主义特征

> **原句 12:** The Court is often described as "counter-majoritarian"; its composition doesn't shift with the political winds.

**中文理解**：法院常被描述为"反多数主义"；其构成不随政治风向转变。

**句子结构**：The Court is often described as "counter-majoritarian"（被动语态）+ its composition doesn't shift with the political winds（并列分句）。

**关键词**：counter-majoritarian / composition doesn't shift / political winds

**表达方式**：counter-majoritarian（反多数主义）是宪法学核心术语——法院的权力独立于多数人的意愿；political winds（政治风向）是隐喻——政治风向在变，但法院不变。

**为什么这样写**：引入"反多数主义"概念——这是理解最高法院权力的理论基础。

---

> **原句 13:** There's an argument for this. Insulated from elections, Justices can guard against the frenzies that occasionally seize the voting public.

**中文理解**：有支持这一点的论据。由于不受选举影响，大法官可以防范偶尔席卷投票公众的狂热。

**句子结构**：There's an argument for this（主句）+ Insulated from elections（分词短语作原因状语）+ Justices can guard against the frenzies that occasionally seize the voting public（主句）。

**关键词**：an argument for this / Insulated from elections / guard against the frenzies / seize the voting public

**表达方式**：frenzies that occasionally seize the voting public（偶尔席卷投票公众的狂热）将民主多数描述为"狂热的"——暗示多数人可能犯错，法院的独立性有其价值。

**为什么这样写**：先给出支持反多数主义的论据——不一味批判法院，增强论证的公信力。

---

> **原句 14:** Judicial power allows "Philip sober to control Philip drunk," as Justice David Brewer famously put it in 1893.

**中文理解**：司法权力允许"清醒的Philip控制醉酒的Philip"——正如David Brewer大法官在1893年的名言。

**句子结构**：Judicial power allows "Philip sober to control Philip drunk"（主句 + 直接引语）+ as Justice David Brewer famously put it in 1893（报道子句）。

**关键词**：Philip sober to control Philip drunk / Justice David Brewer / 1893

**表达方式**：Philip sober vs Philip drunk 是经典隐喻——"清醒的Philip"（法院）控制"醉酒的Philip"（民主多数）；暗示多数人可能"醉酒"（非理性），需要法院的"清醒"（理性）来约束。

**为什么这样写**：引用1893年的名言，将"反多数主义"定位为有历史根基的法律传统——不是现代发明，而是19世纪就有的理念。

---

### 第 5 段：法院的"buzzkill"角色

> **原句 15:** Essentially, the Supreme Court is our democracy's appointed buzzkill.

**中文理解**：本质上，最高法院是我们民主制度中被指定的"扫兴者"。

**句子结构**：Essentially（状语）+ the Supreme Court is our democracy's appointed buzzkill（主系表）。

**关键词**：Essentially / appointed buzzkill

**表达方式**：buzzkill（扫兴者）是口语化表达——将法院的宪法角色降格为"派对上扫兴的人"，带有讽刺意味。

**为什么这样写**：用 buzzkill 这个非正式词汇将法院的"反多数主义"角色通俗化——它就是民主的"扫兴者"。

---

> **原句 16:** In the early nineteen-thirties, it swatted down New Deal legislation passed by congressional majorities.

**中文理解**：在1930年代初期，它否决了国会多数通过的新政立法。

**句子结构**：In the early nineteen-thirties（时间状语）+ it swatted down New Deal legislation（主句）+ passed by congressional majorities（过去分词短语作定语）。

**关键词**：swatted down / New Deal legislation / congressional majorities

**表达方式**：swatted down（拍死）是口语化动词——将法院否决立法描述为"拍死一只苍蝇"，暗示法院的傲慢。

**为什么这样写**：用具体历史案例（新政）说明法院的"扫兴"功能——它否决了国会多数通过的改革立法。

---

> **原句 17:** In the fifties and sixties, it overturned segregationist laws enacted by state and local legislatures.

**中文理解**：在1950和60年代，它推翻了州和地方法院制定的种族隔离法律。

**句子结构**：In the fifties and sixties（时间状语）+ it overturned segregationist laws（主句）+ enacted by state and local legislatures（过去分词短语作定语）。

**关键词**：overturned / segregationist laws / state and local legislatures

**表达方式**：overturned segregationist laws 是法院的"正面"案例——与前句的"swatted down New Deal"形成对比。

**为什么这样写**：展示法院的双重性——它既否决新政（坏），也推翻种族隔离（好）。这说明"反多数主义"不是绝对的好或坏。

---

### 第 6 段：法院态度的模糊性

> **原句 18:** The Supreme Court is like ranked-choice voting or Jdate: how you feel about it depends on whether it's delivering what you want.

**中文理解**：最高法院就像排名选择投票或Jdate约会网站：你对它的感觉取决于它是否在给你你想要的东西。

**句子结构**：The Supreme Court is like X or Y（类比）+ 冒号后解释：how you feel about it depends on whether it's delivering what you want（表语从句）。

**关键词**：ranked-choice voting / Jdate / how you feel about it depends on whether it's delivering what you want

**表达方式**：ranked-choice voting（排名选择投票）和 Jdate（犹太约会网站）是两个不相关的类比——暗示法院的受欢迎程度是"实用主义的"而非"原则性的"；depends on whether it's delivering what you want 将法院的支持率归结为"是否得到你想要的结果"。

**为什么这样写**：用两个不相关的类比讽刺法院支持者的"实用主义"——他们支持法院不是因为原则，而是因为结果。

---

> **原句 19:** Which makes today's situation confusing.

**中文理解**：这使得今天的情况令人困惑。

**句子结构**：Which makes today's situation confusing（非限制性定语从句）。

**关键词**：today's situation / confusing

**表达方式**：confusing（令人困惑的）暗示当前局势的复杂性——法院应该支持谁？

**为什么这样写**：从历史过渡到当代——今天的法院态度模糊，让所有人困惑。

---

> **原句 20:** A lawless President is asserting boundless authority, a Republican Congress is playing along, and it's unclear, from case to case, where the Court will land.

**中文理解**：一个无法无天的总统正在主张无限的权力，一个共和党国会正在配合，而从案件到案件，法院将站在哪一边尚不清楚。

**句子结构**：三个并列分句：A lawless President is asserting boundless authority + a Republican Congress is playing along + and it's unclear, from case to case, where the Court will land。

**关键词**：lawless President / boundless authority / Republican Congress is playing along / unclear where the Court will land

**表达方式**：lawless（无法无天的）和 boundless（无限的）是强烈的价值判断；playing along（配合）暗示国会放弃了制衡职责；unclear where the Court will land 暗示法院的态度不可预测。

**为什么这样写**：以三个并列分句描述当代困局——总统无法无天、国会配合、法院态度不明。这是全文的当代落点。

---

### 第 7 段：Bowie & Renan的"Supremacy"——横向审查 vs 纵向审查

> **原句 21:** Two of Sunstein's Harvard colleagues, Nikolas Bowie and Daphna Renan, take a different approach.

**中文理解**：Sunstein的两位哈佛同事Nikolas Bowie和Daphna Renan采取了不同的方法。

**句子结构**：Two of Sunstein's Harvard colleagues（主语 + 同位语）+ take a different approach（谓语）。

**关键词**：Sunstein's Harvard colleagues / Nikolas Bowie and Daphna Renan / different approach

**表达方式**：take a different approach 暗示他们的分析框架与前文不同。

**为什么这样写**：引入新书"Supremacy"的作者——他们的"不同方法"是将法院权力区分为"横向"和"纵向"。

---

> **原句 22:** In their formidable, sharply argued book, "Supremacy," they distinguish two often conflated powers.

**中文理解**：在他们气势恢宏、论证犀利的著作《Supremacy》中，他们区分了两种常被混淆的权力。

**句子结构**：In their formidable, sharply argued book, "Supremacy"（状语 + 同位语）+ they distinguish two often conflated powers（主句）。

**关键词**：formidable, sharply argued / Supremacy / distinguish / two often conflated powers

**表达方式**：formidable（气势恢宏的）和 sharply argued（论证犀利的）是正面评价；often conflated（常被混淆的）暗示之前的讨论混淆了两种不同的权力。

**为什么这样写**：引入核心概念——"横向审查"和"纵向审查"的区分。

---

> **原句 23:** The power of vertical review allows the Supreme Court to invalidate state and local laws that contradict federal ones.

**中文理解**：纵向审查权允许最高法院推翻与联邦法律矛盾的州和地方法律。

**句子结构**：The power of vertical review（主语）+ allows the Supreme Court to invalidate state and local laws（谓语 + 宾语）+ that contradict federal ones（定语从句）。

**关键词**：vertical review / invalidate / state and local laws / contradict federal ones

**表达方式**：vertical review（纵向审查）是联邦制的核心——法院维护联邦法律的最高地位。

**为什么这样写**：定义"纵向审查"——这是法院权力的"合理"形式。

---

> **原句 24:** The power of horizontal review allows the Court to overrule a coequal branch of government and strike down federal laws or regulations.

**中文理解**：横向审查权允许法院推翻一个平等的政府分支，并否决联邦法律或法规。

**句子结构**：The power of horizontal review（主语）+ allows the Court to overrule a coequal branch of government（谓语 + 宾语1）+ and strike down federal laws or regulations（并列宾语2）。

**关键词**：horizontal review / overrule a coequal branch / strike down federal laws or regulations

**表达方式**：overrule a coequal branch（推翻一个平等的分支）暗示法院在凌驾于国会之上；coequal（平等的）强调国会和法院本应平等。

**为什么这样写**：定义"横向审查"——这是法院权力的"争议"形式，也是全文的核心议题。

---

> **原句 25:** Few make this distinction today, but it reveals a pattern: vertical review cuts both ways for liberal democracy, but horizontal review reliably serves regressive ends.

**中文理解**：今天很少有人做这种区分，但它揭示了一个模式：纵向审查对自由民主有双重作用，但横向审查可靠地服务于倒退目的。

**句子结构**：Few make this distinction today（前半句）+ but it reveals a pattern（转折 + 主句）+ 冒号后解释：vertical review cuts both ways for liberal democracy（分句1）+ but horizontal review reliably serves regressive ends（分句2）。

**关键词**：cuts both ways / liberal democracy / reliably serves regressive ends

**表达方式**：cuts both ways（双刃剑）暗示纵向审查对自由民主既有利也有弊；reliably serves regressive ends（可靠地服务于倒退目的）是对横向审查的强烈批判——它几乎总是被用于阻碍进步。

**为什么这样写**：这是Bowie & Renan的核心论点——横向审查几乎总是被用于"倒退"目的。

---

### 第 8 段：横向审查的历史抗议者

> **原句 26:** Although the Supreme Court's power to overrule Congress is now broadly accepted, it was bitterly contested for more than a century.

**中文理解**：尽管最高法院推翻国会的权力现在已被广泛接受，但它在一个多世纪里曾受到激烈争议。

**句子结构**：Although the Supreme Court's power to overrule Congress is now broadly accepted（让步状语从句）+ it was bitterly contested for more than a century（主句）。

**关键词**：broadly accepted / bitterly contested / more than a century

**表达方式**：broadly accepted vs bitterly contested 是对比——今天的"理所当然"曾经是"激烈争议"。

**为什么这样写**：揭示"横向审查"的历史争议性——它不是从一开始就被接受的。

---

> **原句 27:** What Bowie and Renan add is that the loudest protests against horizontal review historically came from abolitionists, labor organizers, suffragists, and civil-rights activists.

**中文理解**：Bowie和Renan补充的是，历史上对横向审查最强烈的抗议来自废奴主义者、劳工组织者、妇女参政权论者和民权活动家。

**句子结构**：What Bowie and Renan add is that…（主语从句 + 表语从句）+ the loudest protests against horizontal review historically came from abolitionists, labor organizers, suffragists, and civil-rights activists（表语从句内容）。

**关键词**：loudest protests / abolitionists / labor organizers / suffragists / civil-rights activists

**表达方式**：四个群体（废奴主义者/劳工组织者/妇女参政权论者/民权活动家）——都是进步运动的代表；historically came from 暗示这些群体的抗议有历史根基。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最具颠覆性的论点——对法院权力最强烈的抗议不是来自保守派，而是来自进步派。这挑战了"法院=进步"的常见假设。

### 第 9 段：Marbury v. Madison + 酒店台阶

> **原句 28:** As those critics noted, the Constitution doesn't expressly authorize the Court to overturn acts of Congress.

**中文理解**：正如那些批评者所指出的，宪法并没有明确授权法院推翻国会的法案。

**句子结构**：As those critics noted（插入语）+ the Constitution doesn't expressly authorize the Court to overturn acts of Congress（主句）。

**关键词**：Constitution doesn't expressly authorize / overturn acts of Congress

**表达方式**：doesn't expressly authorize（没有明确授权）暗示"推翻国会"的权力不是宪法明文规定的，而是法院自己"解释"出来的。

**为什么这样写**：从宪法文本出发——"横向审查"没有宪法明文授权，它的合法性是可疑的。

---

> **原句 29:** That right was supposedly established by Marbury v. Madison, in 1803.

**中文理解**：这项权利据称是由1803年的Marbury v. Madison案确立的。

**句子结构**：That right was supposedly established by Marbury v. Madison（被动语态）+ in 1803（时间状语）。

**关键词**：supposedly established / Marbury v. Madison / 1803

**表达方式**：supposedly（据称）暗示作者对这一"确立"的合法性持怀疑态度。

**为什么这样写**：引入Marbury v. Madison——美国宪法史上最重要的案例之一，但作者用"supposedly"暗示其合法性可疑。

---

> **原句 30:** President John Adams, after losing his 1800 re-election bid, used his lame-duck period to fill the judiciary with sympathizers.

**中文理解**：John Adams总统在输掉1800年连任竞选后，利用他的"跛脚鸭"时期将司法系统填满自己的同情者。

**句子结构**：President John Adams（主语）+ after losing his 1800 re-election bid（时间状语）+ used his lame-duck period to fill the judiciary with sympathizers（谓语 + 宾语）。

**关键词**：lame-duck period / fill the judiciary with sympathizers

**表达方式**：lame-duck period（跛脚鸭时期）是政治术语——指选举后到就职前的过渡期；fill the judiciary with sympathizers 暗示Adams在"安插"自己的人。

**为什么这样写**：揭示Marbury v. Madison的背景——它不是关于"宪法原则"，而是关于"政治任命"。

---

> **原句 31:** But some orders commissioning justices of the peace went undelivered.

**中文理解**：但一些任命治安法官的委任状未能送达。

**句子结构**：But some orders commissioning justices of the peace went undelivered（简单句）。

**关键词**：orders commissioning justices of the peace / went undelivered

**表达方式**：went undelivered（未送达）是案件的导火索——Marbury的委任状没有被送达，他起诉要求送达。

**为什么这样写**：交代案件的技术性起因——一个未送达的委任状引发了美国宪法史上最重要的案例。

---

> **原句 32:** John Marshall, the Chief Justice, used Marbury v. Madison to proclaim the Court's ability to invalidate federal laws that it deemed unconstitutional.

**中文理解**：首席大法官John Marshall利用Marbury v. Madison案宣告法院有权推翻它认为违宪的联邦法律。

**句子结构**：John Marshall, the Chief Justice（主语 + 同位语）+ used Marbury v. Madison to proclaim the Court's ability to invalidate federal laws（谓语 + 宾语）+ that it deemed unconstitutional（定语从句）。

**关键词**：John Marshall / Chief Justice / used Marbury v. Madison / proclaim / invalidate federal laws / deemed unconstitutional

**表达方式**：used…to proclaim 暗示Marshall"利用"了这个案件来"宣告"法院的权力——不是"宪法规定"，而是"法院自己宣布"。

**为什么这样写**：揭示"横向审查"的起源——它不是宪法明文规定的，而是首席大法官自己"宣告"的。

---

> **原句 33:** Bowie and Renan point out that Marshall misread the statute that he was invalidating, making the whole thing a turducken of judicial folly.

**中文理解**：Bowie和Renan指出，Marshall误读了他正在推翻的法规，使整件事成了一个"司法愚行的火鸡填鸭"。

**句子结构**：Bowie and Renan point out that…（主句 + 宾语从句）+ Marshall misread the statute that he was invalidating（宾语从句内容）+ making the whole thing a turducken of judicial folly（分词短语作结果状语）。

**关键词**：misread the statute / turducken of judicial folly

**表达方式**：turducken（火鸡填鸭：火鸡里塞鸭子再塞鸡）是美国南方菜——暗示Marbury v. Madison是一层套一层的"司法愚行"；judicial folly（司法愚行）是强烈的价值判断。

**为什么这样写**：用 turducken 这个食物隐喻将Marbury v. Madison定性为"一层套一层的荒谬"——Marshall误读了法律，然后基于这个误读宣告了法院的权力。

---

> **原句 34:** Because the Court lacked a permanent abode, Marshall read out the ruling from the steps outside Stelle's Hotel, on Capitol Hill.

**中文理解**：由于法院没有永久住所，Marshall从国会山Stelle酒店外的台阶上宣读了判决。

**句子结构**：Because the Court lacked a permanent abode（原因状语从句）+ Marshall read out the ruling from the steps outside Stelle's Hotel（主句）+ on Capitol Hill（地点状语）。

**关键词**：lacked a permanent abode / read out the ruling from the steps / Stelle's Hotel / Capitol Hill

**表达方式**：从酒店台阶上宣读判决——暗示法院当时的权威性之低——连一个正式的法庭都没有。

**为什么这样写**：用"酒店台阶"这个细节将Marbury v. Madison从"宪法里程碑"降格为"酒店台阶上的声明"——它的权威性从一开始就是可疑的。

---

> **原句 35:** As with most impassioned avowals made on hotel steps, Congress ignored it.

**中文理解**：就像大多数在酒店台阶上发表的激情宣言一样，国会无视了它。

**句子结构**：As with most impassioned avowals made on hotel steps（比较状语）+ Congress ignored it（主句）。

**关键词**：impassioned avowals / hotel steps / Congress ignored it

**表达方式**：impassioned avowals（激情宣言）+ hotel steps（酒店台阶）是讽刺——将宪法史上最重要的判决降格为"酒店台阶上的激情宣言"；Congress ignored it 暗示判决在当时没有实际效力。

**为什么这样写**：揭示Marbury v. Madison的"软肋"——判决在当时被国会无视了，它的权威是后来才建立的。

---

> **原句 36:** The Marbury decision didn't compel anyone to do anything, so Marshall's assertion just hung in the air.

**中文理解**：Marbury判决没有强制任何人做任何事，所以Marshall的主张就这样悬在空中。

**句子结构**：The Marbury decision didn't compel anyone to do anything（主句）+ so Marshall's assertion just hung in the air（结果状语从句）。

**关键词**：didn't compel anyone / hung in the air

**表达方式**：hung in the air（悬在空中）暗示判决没有"落地"——它只是一个声明，没有实际效力。

**为什么这样写**：以"悬在空中"收尾——Marbury v. Madison的权威性在当时是空中楼阁，后来才被逐步接受。

### 第 10 段：早期政治家蔑视法院

> **原句 37:** Judicial assertions meant less back then.

**中文理解**：那时司法宣言的意义要小得多。

**句子结构**：Judicial assertions meant less back then（简单句）。

**关键词**：meant less / back then

**表达方式**：meant less 暗示法院的权威是历史建构的——它不是从一开始就有现在这么大的权力。

**为什么这样写**：过渡句——从Marbury v. Madison过渡到早期政治家对法院的蔑视。

---

> **原句 38:** Early-nineteenth-century politicians simply defied the Court.

**中文理解**：19世纪初的政治家 simply 藐视法院。

**句子结构**：Early-nineteenth-century politicians（主语）+ simply defied the Court（谓语）。

**关键词**：simply defied the Court

**表达方式**：simply（ simply ）暗示蔑视法院在当时是"正常"的——不需要理由，直接藐视。

**为什么这样写**：用"simply"将蔑视法院正常化——在19世纪，法院的权威远没有今天这么大。

---

> **原句 39:** States regularly declined to appear before it when ordered.

**中文理解**：各州经常在被命令时拒绝出庭。

**句子结构**：States regularly declined to appear before it（主句）+ when ordered（时间状语从句）。

**表达方式**：regularly（经常）暗示蔑视法院不是偶发事件，而是常态。

**为什么这样写**：用具体行为（拒绝出庭）展示早期政治家对法院的蔑视。

> **原句 40:** "Virginia's highest court refused to concede that the Supreme Court had the authority to review its decisions," Friedman writes. "Georgia actually hanged a man in the face of a Supreme Court order to the contrary."

**中文理解**："弗吉尼亚最高法院拒绝承认最高法院有权审查其裁决，"Friedman写道。"乔治亚州实际上在最高法院相反命令面前绞死了一个人。"

**句子结构**：直接引语（两句话）+ Friedman writes（报道子句）。

**关键词**：Virginia's highest court refused to concede / Georgia actually hanged a man / in the face of a Supreme Court order

**表达方式**：Georgia actually hanged a man 是最极端的例子——州政府在最高法院命令面前执行了死刑。in the face of（面对……时）暗示公开的蔑视。

**为什么这样写**：用两个具体案例（弗吉尼亚拒绝承认 + 乔治亚执行死刑）展示早期州政府对最高法院的极端蔑视。

### 第 11 段：Calhoun的"少数人保护"

> **原句 41:** Within this rowdy milieu, there was nonetheless a growing constituency for "Philip sober" guardrails.

**中文理解**：在这种喧闹的环境中，仍然有一个日益增长的群体支持"清醒的Philip"式的护栏。

**句子结构**：Within this rowdy milieu（状语）+ there was nonetheless a growing constituency for "Philip sober" guardrails（存现句）。

**关键词**：rowdy milieu / growing constituency / "Philip sober" guardrails

**表达方式**：rowdy milieu（喧闹的环境）描述19世纪初的政治氛围；"Philip sober" guardrails 回应前文的隐喻——"清醒的Philip"（法院）的"护栏"（约束多数人的机制）。

**为什么这样写**：从"蔑视法院"过渡到"支持法院"——即便在喧闹的政治环境中，仍有人支持法院的约束功能。

> **原句 42:** The most eloquent plea for protections against zealous majorities came from the South Carolina statesman John C. Calhoun.

**句子结构**：The most eloquent plea for protections against zealous majorities（主语）+ came from the South Carolina statesman John C. Calhoun（谓语 + 来源）。

**表达方式**：most eloquent plea（最雄辩的呼吁）是正面评价——Calhoun的论证有其说服力；zealous majorities（狂热的多数）将民主多数描述为"狂热的"。

**为什么这样写**：引入Calhoun——一个有争议的历史人物（奴隶主），但他的"少数人保护"理论有其影响力。

> **原句 43:** His "Disquisition on Government," published posthumously, in 1851, captured the view of the imperilled minority to which Calhoun himself belonged: slaveholders.

**中文理解**：他的《政府论》（1851年死后出版）捕捉了Calhoun所属的"受威胁的少数人"的观点：奴隶主。

**为什么这样写**：揭示Calhoun理论的阴暗面——他的"少数人保护"不是为了保护弱势群体，而是为了保护奴隶主。

### 第 12 段：Dred Scott → Lincoln的蔑视

> **原句 44:** It is, of course, Noah's-flood-on-your-wedding-day ironic that slaveholders would see themselves as oppressed.

**中文理解**：当然，奴隶主将自己视为受压迫者，这是"诺亚洪水遇上你婚礼"般的讽刺。

**句子结构**：It is, of course, Noah's-flood-on-your-wedding-day ironic that…（形式主语 + 真正主语从句）。

**关键词**：Noah's-flood-on-your-wedding-day ironic / slaveholders / oppressed

**表达方式**：Noah's-flood-on-your-wedding-day ironic 是Alanis Morissette歌曲《Ironic》的典故——"诺亚洪水遇上你婚礼"是"极端不幸"的讽刺；slaveholders as oppressed 是核心讽刺——压迫者将自己视为受压迫者。

**为什么这样写**：用流行文化典故（Alanis Morissette的《Ironic》）将Calhoun理论的讽刺性通俗化。

---

> **原句 45:** But a population boom in the North and the mainstreaming of antislavery politics there raised the threat of a legislative majority abolishing their property.

**中文理解**：但北方的人口激增和反奴隶制政治的主流化提高了立法多数废除他们财产的威胁。

**句子结构**：But a population boom in the North and the mainstreaming of antislavery politics there（主语，两个并列成分）+ raised the threat of a legislative majority abolishing their property（谓语 + 宾语）。

**关键词**：population boom / mainstreaming of antislavery politics / legislative majority abolishing their property

**表达方式**：abolishing their property 将奴隶描述为"财产"——这是奴隶主的视角，不是废奴主义者的视角。

**为什么这样写**：从奴隶主的视角解释Calhoun理论的"合理性"——他们确实面临"多数人废除其财产"的威胁，只是这种"财产"是人。

> **原句 46:** Passions ran especially hot over slavery in the territories, where federal law held sway.

**中文理解**：关于领地中的奴隶制问题，情绪尤为激烈，因为联邦法律在那里拥有管辖权。

**句子结构**：Passions ran especially hot over slavery in the territories（主句）+ where federal law held sway（定语从句）。

**关键词**：Passions ran especially hot / slavery in the territories / federal law held sway

**表达方式**：Passions ran especially hot（情绪尤为激烈）暗示奴隶制问题是19世纪美国最分裂的议题；federal law held sway（联邦法律拥有管辖权）暗示领地是联邦政府和州政府争夺的焦点。

**为什么这样写**：交代Dred Scott案的背景——奴隶制在领地中的地位是核心争议。

---

> **原句 47:** The President elected in 1856, James Buchanan, swore that the "great object" of his Administration would be "to arrest, if possible, the agitation of the slavery question."

**中文理解**：1856年当选的总统James Buchanan发誓，他政府的"伟大目标"将是"如果可能，阻止奴隶制问题的 agitation"。

**句子结构**：The President elected in 1856, James Buchanan（主语 + 同位语）+ swore that…（谓语 + 宾语从句）+ "the great object" of his Administration would be "to arrest, if possible, the agitation of the slavery question"（直接引语）。

**关键词**：great object / arrest the agitation / slavery question

**表达方式**：arrest the agitation（阻止 agitation）暗示Buchanan的目标不是"解决"奴隶制问题，而是"压制"对其的讨论。

**为什么这样写**：引入Buchanan——一个试图"压制"奴隶制讨论的总统，他的政策直接导致了Dred Scott案。

---

> **原句 48:** Buchanan secretly pressed the Court to take the divisive issue out of politicians' hands.

**中文理解**：Buchanan秘密施压法院，要求将这个分裂性议题从政治家手中接过。

**句子结构**：Buchanan secretly pressed the Court（主句）+ to take the divisive issue out of politicians' hands（目的状语）。

**关键词**：secretly pressed / take the divisive issue out of politicians' hands

**表达方式**：secretly pressed（秘密施压）暗示Buchanan在幕后操纵法院；take out of politicians' hands 暗示Buchanan想把"烫手山芋"扔给法院。

**为什么这样写**：揭示Dred Scott案的政治背景——它不是法院独立做出的判决，而是总统施压的结果。

---

> **原句 49:** The Chief Justice, Roger B. Taney, obliged.

**中文理解**：首席大法官Roger B. Taney照办了。

**句子结构**：The Chief Justice, Roger B. Taney（主语 + 同位语）+ obliged（谓语）。

**关键词**：obliged（照办、施以援手）

**表达方式**：obliged 是一个简洁的词——Taney"照办了"，暗示法院屈从于总统的压力。

**为什么这样写**：用一个词（obliged）将Taney的行为定性为"屈从"——法院的独立性在这一刻被背叛了。

---

> **原句 50:** In Dred Scott v. Sandford, his Court held that Black people lacked U.S. citizenship and that congressional bans on territorial slavery were unconstitutional.

**中文理解**：在Dred Scott v. Sandford案中，他的法院裁定黑人缺乏美国公民身份，且国会禁止领地奴隶制的法律违宪。

**句子结构**：In Dred Scott v. Sandford（状语）+ his Court held that…（主句 + 宾语从句，两个并列的 that 从句）。

**关键词**：Black people lacked U.S. citizenship / congressional bans on territorial slavery were unconstitutional

**表达方式**：两个裁定（黑人不是公民 + 国会禁令违宪）——前者否定了黑人的法律地位，后者否定了国会的立法权。

**为什么这样写**：直接陈述Dred Scott案的两个核心裁定——它们是美国宪法史上最恶劣的判决之一。

---

> **原句 51:** Dred Scott, decided in 1857, was the Court's first use of horizontal review since Marbury and its first substantial use of the power ever.

**中文理解**：1857年判决的Dred Scott案是法院自Marbury以来首次使用横向审查权，也是其有史以来首次实质性使用该权力。

**句子结构**：Dred Scott（主语）+ decided in 1857（插入语）+ was the Court's first use of horizontal review since Marbury（谓语1）+ and its first substantial use of the power ever（并列谓语2）。

**关键词**：first use of horizontal review since Marbury / first substantial use of the power ever

**表达方式**：first substantial use（首次实质性使用）暗示Marbury只是"宣告"了权力，Dred Scott才是"实质使用"。

**为什么这样写**：将Dred Scott定位为"横向审查"的首次实质性应用——它不是理论，而是实践。

---

> **原句 52:** It is widely regarded as the Court's worst decision—a "hell-black judgment," the abolitionist Frederick Douglass called it.

**中文理解**：它被广泛认为是法院最糟糕的判决——废奴主义者Frederick Douglass称之为"地狱般黑暗的判决"。

**句子结构**：It is widely regarded as the Court's worst decision（被动语态）+ 破折号后 a "hell-black judgment"（同位语）+ the abolitionist Frederick Douglass called it（报道子句）。

**关键词**：widely regarded / worst decision / hell-black judgment / Frederick Douglass

**表达方式**：hell-black judgment（地狱般黑暗的判决）是Douglass的原话——强烈的道德判断。

**为什么这样写**：引用废奴主义者Douglass的原话，将Dred Scott定性为"最糟糕的判决"。

---

> **原句 53:** But Douglass didn't take Dred Scott as settled law; he took it as an invitation to challenge the Court's authority.

**中文理解**：但Douglass没有将Dred Scott视为既定法律；他将其视为挑战法院权威的邀请。

**句子结构**：But Douglass didn't take Dred Scott as settled law（前半句）+ he took it as an invitation to challenge the Court's authority（后半句）。

**关键词**：didn't take as settled law / took it as an invitation / challenge the Court's authority

**表达方式**：didn't take as settled law vs took it as an invitation 是对比——Douglass将"最糟糕的判决"转化为"挑战的邀请"。

**为什么这样写**：展示Douglass的反应——不是接受，而是挑战。这为后文Lincoln的蔑视做了铺垫。

---

> **原句 54:** Outrage at Taney's power grab filled the sails of the antislavery Republican Party and ultimately propelled Abraham Lincoln to the Presidency.

**中文理解**：对Taney权力攫取的愤怒为反奴隶制共和党扬帆助力，并最终将Abraham Lincoln推上总统宝座。

**句子结构**：Outrage at Taney's power grab（主语）+ filled the sails of the antislavery Republican Party（谓语1）+ and ultimately propelled Abraham Lincoln to the Presidency（谓语2）。

**关键词**：Outrage / Taney's power grab / filled the sails / propelled Lincoln to the Presidency

**表达方式**：filled the sails（扬帆助力）是航海隐喻——愤怒为反奴隶制运动提供了动力；propelled（推动）是物理隐喻——将Lincoln"推上"总统宝座。

**为什么这样写**：展示Dred Scott案的政治后果——它不仅没有"解决"奴隶制问题，反而推动了反奴隶制运动和Lincoln的当选。

---

> **原句 55:** In his Inaugural Address of 1861, he declared that permitting robed Justices to settle major disputes would mean that "the people will have ceased to be their own rulers."

**中文理解**：在他1861年的就职演说中，他宣称允许穿袍子的大法官解决重大争端将意味着"人民将不再是自己的统治者"。

**句子结构**：In his Inaugural Address of 1861（状语）+ he declared that…（主句 + 宾语从句）+ permitting robed Justices to settle major disputes would mean that…（动名词短语作主语）+ "the people will have ceased to be their own rulers"（直接引语）。

**关键词**：Inaugural Address / robed Justices / the people will have ceased to be their own rulers

**表达方式**：robed Justices（穿袍子的大法官）是略带讽刺的描述——强调法官的"形式性"而非"实质性"；the people will have ceased to be their own rulers（人民将不再是自己的统治者）是Lincoln的核心论点——法院权力威胁民主自治。

**为什么这样写**：引用Lincoln的就职演说——他对法院权力的批判与Bowie & Renan的论点一脉相承。

---

### 第 13 段：Lincoln蔑视法院 + 重建时期的民主突破

> **原句 56:** And so, like many before him, Lincoln defied the Court.

**中文理解**：因此，像他之前的许多人一样，Lincoln蔑视了法院。

**句子结构**：And so（过渡）+ like many before him（比较状语）+ Lincoln defied the Court（主句）。

**关键词**：like many before him / Lincoln defied the Court

**表达方式**：like many before him 暗示蔑视法院不是Lincoln的"创新"，而是延续了19世纪的传统。

**为什么这样写**：将Lincoln的行为定性为"延续传统"——蔑视法院在19世纪是常态。

---

> **原句 57:** After eleven slaveholding states seceded to form the Confederacy, Republican lawmakers passed and Lincoln signed a bill that flouted Dred Scott by prohibiting slavery in the territories.

**中文理解**：在十一个蓄奴州脱离联邦组成南方邦联后，共和党议员通过了、Lincoln签署了一项法案，该法案通过禁止领地中的奴隶制来蔑视Dred Scott判决。

**句子结构**：After eleven slaveholding states seceded…（时间状语从句）+ Republican lawmakers passed and Lincoln signed a bill（主句）+ that flouted Dred Scott by prohibiting slavery in the territories（定语从句）。

**关键词**：seceded / Confederacy / flouted Dred Scott / prohibiting slavery in the territories

**表达方式**：flouted（蔑视）是强烈的行为动词——法案直接"蔑视"了法院的判决。

**为什么这样写**：展示Lincoln的具体行动——不是"抗议"，而是"通过法律蔑视判决"。

---

> **原句 58:** Bowie and Renan view this as a heartening reminder that the elected branches of government needn't defer to the judiciary.

**中文理解**：Bowie和Renan将此视为一个令人振奋的提醒——民选政府分支无需服从司法部门。

**句子结构**：Bowie and Renan view this as a heartening reminder（主句）+ that the elected branches of government needn't defer to the judiciary（同位语从句）。

**关键词**：heartening reminder / elected branches / needn't defer to the judiciary

**表达方式**：heartening reminder（令人振奋的提醒）是正面评价——Bowie和Renan认为Lincoln的行为是值得效仿的先例。

**为什么这样写**：引用Bowie和Renan的结论——Lincoln蔑视法院是"民选分支无需服从司法"的先例。

---

> **原句 59:** The reign of Republicans during the Civil War and Reconstruction was a democratic breakthrough in two senses.

**中文理解**：共和党在内战和重建时期的统治是民主突破，具有双重意义。

**句子结构**：The reign of Republicans during the Civil War and Reconstruction（主语）+ was a democratic breakthrough in two senses（谓语）。

**关键词**：democratic breakthrough / two senses

**表达方式**：two senses（双重意义）预告后文将解释两个方面的突破。

**为什么这样写**：过渡句——从Lincoln过渡到重建时期的民主突破。

---

> **原句 60:** First, legislative majorities overrode the Court, repeatedly ignoring its rulings and adjusting the number of Justices to prevent its interference.

**中文理解**：首先，立法多数推翻了法院，反复无视其裁决，并调整大法官人数以防止其干涉。

**句子结构**：First（序数词）+ legislative majorities overrode the Court（主句）+ repeatedly ignoring its rulings and adjusting the number of Justices（两个并列的分词短语）+ to prevent its interference（目的状语）。

**关键词**：overrode the Court / repeatedly ignoring / adjusting the number of Justices / prevent its interference

**表达方式**：adjusting the number of Justices（调整大法官人数）是"法院包装"（court-packing）的早期版本——通过增加法官人数来改变法院的意识形态平衡。

**为什么这样写**：展示重建时期的第一个突破——立法多数直接"推翻"法院，包括调整法官人数。

---

> **原句 61:** Second, those majorities abolished slavery, extended citizenship, and guaranteed equal protection to all Americans.

**中文理解**：其次，那些多数废除了奴隶制、扩展了公民身份，并保障了所有美国人的平等保护。

**句子结构**：Second（序数词）+ those majorities abolished slavery, extended citizenship, and guaranteed equal protection to all Americans（三个并列谓语）。

**关键词**：abolished slavery / extended citizenship / guaranteed equal protection

**表达方式**：三个并列成就——废除奴隶制、扩展公民身份、保障平等保护——是重建时期的核心成就。

**为什么这样写**：展示重建时期的第二个突破——这些成就不是法院做出的，而是立法多数做出的。

---

### 第 14 段：法院反击 → Lochner时代

> **原句 62:** The Court struck back.

**中文理解**：法院反击了。

**句子结构**：The Court struck back（简单句）。

**关键词**：struck back（反击）

**表达方式**：struck back 是军事隐喻——法院"反击"了立法多数。

**为什么这样写**：以最简洁的句式描述法院的反击——从"被推翻"到"反击"。

---

> **原句 63:** Toward the end of Reconstruction, it began regularly overturning federal legislation.

**中文理解**：在重建时期结束时，它开始定期推翻联邦立法。

**句子结构**：Toward the end of Reconstruction（时间状语）+ it began regularly overturning federal legislation（主句）。

**关键词**：regularly overturning federal legislation

**表达方式**：regularly（定期地）暗示法院的反击是系统性的、持续的。

**为什么这样写**：从"反击"到"定期推翻"——法院开始系统性地推翻进步立法。

---

> **原句 64:** "By the end of the century, the Supreme Court undid nearly every major law Congress passed to protect multiracial democracy in the South," Bowie and Renan write.

**中文理解**："到世纪末，最高法院撤销了国会通过的几乎所有保护南方多种族民主的重大法律，"Bowie和Renan写道。

**句子结构**：直接引语 + Bowie and Renan write（报道子句）。

**关键词**：undid nearly every major law / protect multiracial democracy / in the South

**表达方式**：undid nearly every major law（撤销了几乎所有重大法律）是强烈的价值判断——法院的反击是全面的、毁灭性的。

**为什么这样写**：引用Bowie和Renan的原话，将法院的反击定性为"全面撤销进步立法"。

---

> **原句 65:** It then turned to opposing labor unions, farmers' organizations, and capitalism's other foes.

**中文理解**：然后它转向反对工会、农民组织和资本主义的其他敌人。

**句子结构**：It then turned to opposing labor unions, farmers' organizations, and capitalism's other foes（主句 + 动名词宾语）。

**关键词**：opposing labor unions / farmers' organizations / capitalism's other foes

**表达方式**：capitalism's other foes（资本主义的其他敌人）将工会和农民组织定性为"资本主义的敌人"——这是法院的视角。

**为什么这样写**：从"反对多种族民主"扩展到"反对劳工权利"——法院的反击范围在扩大。

---

> **原句 66:** Between 1890 and 1936, using both horizontal and vertical review, the Court invalidated more than two hundred progressive statutes, state and federal.

**中文理解**：在1890年至1936年间，使用横向和纵向审查，法院推翻了200多项进步法规，包括州和联邦的。

**句子结构**：Between 1890 and 1936（时间状语）+ using both horizontal and vertical review（分词短语）+ the Court invalidated more than two hundred progressive statutes（主句）+ state and federal（补充说明）。

**关键词**：1890-1936 / invalidated more than two hundred progressive statutes

**表达方式**：more than two hundred（200多项）将法院的反击量化——不是偶发，而是系统性的。

**为什么这样写**：用具体数字（200+）将法院的反击量化——在46年间推翻了200多项进步法规。

---

> **原句 67:** Historians call this the Lochner era, for Lochner v. New York, a 1905 case holding that a New York law limiting working hours violated the Fourteenth Amendment.

**中文理解**：历史学家称之为"Lochner时代"，得名于1905年的Lochner v. New York案——该案裁定纽约州限制工时的法律违反了第十四修正案。

**句子结构**：Historians call this the Lochner era（主句）+ for Lochner v. New York（原因状语）+ a 1905 case holding that…（同位语 + 宾语从句）。

**关键词**：Lochner era / Lochner v. New York / 1905 / limiting working hours violated the Fourteenth Amendment

**表达方式**：Lochner era 是美国法律史的专有名词——代表法院系统性推翻进步立法的时期。

**为什么这样写**：引入"Lochner时代"概念——它是法院"反进步"的代名词。

---

### 第 15 段：Teddy Roosevelt + Taft → FDR

> **原句 68:** Even establishment politicians like Teddy Roosevelt balked.

**中文理解**：即使是像Teddy Roosevelt这样的建制派政治家也犹豫了。

**句子结构**：Even establishment politicians like Teddy Roosevelt（主语）+ balked（谓语）。

**关键词**：establishment politicians / balked

**表达方式**：Even（甚至）暗示连建制派都对法院不满；balked（犹豫、退缩）暗示法院的权力已经越界。

**为什么这样写**：展示法院权力的争议性——不仅进步派反对，建制派也反对。

---

> **原句 69:** He proposed allowing voters to overturn judicial decisions by ballot.

**中文理解**：他提议允许选民通过投票推翻司法裁决。

**句子结构**：He proposed allowing voters to overturn judicial decisions by ballot（主句 + 动名词宾语）。

**关键词**：allowing voters to overturn judicial decisions by ballot

**表达方式**：by ballot（通过投票）将"推翻司法裁决"民主化——不是法官决定，而是选民决定。

**为什么这样写**：展示Roosevelt的激进提案——直接让选民推翻法院判决。

---

> **原句 70:** If the spirit of Lochner were to prevail, he warned, "we should not only have a revolution, but it would be absolutely necessary to have a revolution."

**中文理解**：他警告说，如果Lochner的精神继续盛行，"我们不仅会发生革命，而且绝对有必要进行革命"。

**句子结构**：If the spirit of Lochner were to prevail（条件状语从句，虚拟语气）+ he warned（插入语）+ "we should not only have a revolution, but it would be absolutely necessary to have a revolution"（直接引语，not only…but 结构）。

**关键词**：spirit of Lochner / revolution / absolutely necessary

**表达方式**：not only…but 结构将"革命"从"可能发生"升级为"绝对必要"；revolution（革命）是极端用语——暗示法院权力已经威胁到民主制度的根基。

**为什么这样写**：引用Roosevelt的极端警告——如果法院继续推翻进步立法，革命将是"绝对必要的"。

---

> **原句 71:** Such mutinous talk only heightened conservative Justices' sense that the barbarians were storming the gate.

**中文理解**：这种叛逆言论只会加剧保守派大法官们"野蛮人正在攻城"的感觉。

**句子结构**：Such mutinous talk（主语）+ only heightened conservative Justices' sense（谓语 + 宾语）+ that the barbarians were storming the gate（同位语从句）。

**关键词**：mutinous talk / heightened / barbarians were storming the gate

**表达方式**：mutinous（叛逆的）将Roosevelt的提案描述为"叛乱"；barbarians were storming the gate（野蛮人攻城）将进步派描述为"野蛮人"——这是保守派大法官的视角。

**为什么这样写**：从"进步派的恐惧"切换到"保守派的恐惧"——双方都认为对方在威胁自己的价值观。

---

> **原句 72:** "I must stay on the court in order to prevent the Bolsheviki from getting control," Chief Justice William Howard Taft wrote to his brother, in 1929.

**中文理解**："我必须留在法院以防止布尔什维克掌权，"首席大法官William Howard Taft在1929年写信给他的兄弟。

**句子结构**：直接引语 + Chief Justice William Howard Taft wrote to his brother, in 1929（报道子句）。

**关键词**：stay on the court / prevent the Bolsheviki from getting control

**表达方式**：Bolsheviki（布尔什维克）是1920年代的"共产主义"恐惧——Taft将进步派等同于"布尔什维克"。

**为什么这样写**：引用Taft的私人信件——他将"留在法院"等同于"防止共产主义"，揭示保守派大法官的恐惧心理。

---

### 第 16 段：FDR → Warren Court

> **原句 73:** The familiar understanding is that F.D.R. subdued the conservative Justices by threatening to enlarge the bench.

**中文理解**：通常的理解是FDR通过威胁扩大法院规模来制服了保守派大法官。

**句子结构**：The familiar understanding is that…（主系表 + 表语从句）+ F.D.R. subdued the conservative Justices by threatening to enlarge the bench（表语从句内容）。

**关键词**：familiar understanding / subdued / threatening to enlarge the bench

**表达方式**：familiar understanding（通常的理解）暗示这只是"流行说法"，不是完整真相；enlarge the bench（扩大法院规模）是"court-packing"的委婉表达。

**为什么这样写**：引入"FDR制服保守派"的流行说法，然后用"This is a myth"否定它。

---

> **原句 74:** This is a myth; they started to yield before he made that threat.

**中文理解**：这是一个神话；他们在FDR发出威胁之前就开始让步了。

**句子结构**：This is a myth（前半句）+ they started to yield before he made that threat（后半句）。

**关键词**：myth / started to yield before

**表达方式**：myth（神话）直接否定流行说法；started to yield before（在之前就开始让步）揭示真相——保守派的让步不是因为FDR的威胁。

**为什么这样写**：纠正历史误解——FDR的"court-packing"威胁不是保守派让步的原因。

---

> **原句 75:** By the time F.D.R. died, in 1945, he had appointed seven of the Supreme Court's nine Justices.

**中文理解**：到FDR 1945年去世时，他已经任命了最高法院九位大法官中的七位。

**句子结构**：By the time F.D.R. died, in 1945（时间状语从句）+ he had appointed seven of the Supreme Court's nine Justices（主句，过去完成时）。

**关键词**：appointed seven of nine / by the time he died

**表达方式**：seven of nine（九分之七）将FDR的影响量化——他在任期间几乎完全重塑了法院。

**为什么这样写**：用具体数字（7/9）展示FDR的长期影响——他的任命彻底改变了法院的意识形态平衡。

---

> **原句 76:** This inaugurated a golden age of liberalism and a break with the Court's theretofore conservative history.

**中文理解**：这开创了自由主义的黄金时代，并与法院此前的保守历史决裂。

**句子结构**：This inaugurated a golden age of liberalism（谓语1）+ and a break with the Court's theretofore conservative history（并列宾语2）。

**关键词**：golden age of liberalism / break with / theretofore conservative history

**表达方式**：golden age of liberalism（自由主义的黄金时代）是正面评价；theretofore（在此之前）强调法院历史的保守性。

**为什么这样写**：将FDR的任命定性为"自由主义的黄金时代"——法院从"保守"转向"进步"。

---

### 第 17 段：Warren Court → Brown v. Board

> **原句 77:** When Earl Warren presided, from 1953 to 1969, the Court helped make voting fairer, establish rights for criminal defendants, and dismantle Jim Crow.

**中文理解**：当Earl Warren主持法院期间（1953-1969），法院帮助使投票更公平、确立刑事被告的权利、并拆除吉姆·克劳法。

**句子结构**：When Earl Warren presided, from 1953 to 1969（时间状语从句）+ the Court helped make voting fairer, establish rights for criminal defendants, and dismantle Jim Crow（主句，三个并列宾语）。

**关键词**：make voting fairer / establish rights for criminal defendants / dismantle Jim Crow

**表达方式**：三个成就（投票公平 / 刑事权利 / 拆除种族隔离）——都是Warren Court的核心成就。

**为什么这样写**：列举Warren Court的成就——它是法院"进步"时期的代表。

> **原句 78:** The Warren Court's watershed decision of 1954, Brown v. Board of Education of Topeka, which rejected segregation in public schools, is revered with the same intensity that Dred Scott is reviled.

**中文理解**：Warren Court 1954年的分水岭判决Brown v. Board of Education of Topeka——它否定了公立学校的种族隔离——受到与Dred Scott同样强烈的崇敬。

**句子结构**：The Warren Court's watershed decision of 1954（主语 + 同位语）+ which rejected segregation in public schools（定语从句）+ is revered with the same intensity that Dred Scott is reviled（被动语态 + 比较结构）。

**关键词**：watershed decision / Brown v. Board of Education / rejected segregation / revered with the same intensity / Dred Scott is reviled

**表达方式**：revered with the same intensity that Dred Scott is reviled 是对称结构——Brown被崇敬的程度 = Dred Scott被憎恶的程度。

**为什么这样写**：将Brown和Dred Scott对称——一个是"最被崇敬的判决"，一个是"最被憎恶的判决"。

### 第 18 段：Brown的"法理学神秘化"

> **原句 79:** Brown was a moment of both moral clarity and jurisprudential mystification, Bowie and Renan maintain.

**中文理解**：Bowie和Renan认为，Brown是一个既有道德清晰度又有法理学神秘化的时刻。

**关键词**：moral clarity / jurisprudential mystification

**表达方式**：moral clarity（道德清晰度）+ jurisprudential mystification（法理学神秘化）是并列矛盾——Brown在道德上清晰，但在法理上模糊。

**为什么这样写**：引入Bowie和Renan对Brown的分析——它不是"完美"的，而是有缺陷的。

> **原句 80:** The lawyers for the plaintiffs had grounded their argument partly in a Reconstruction-era federal statute.

**中文理解**：原告律师部分基于重建时期的一项联邦法规来构建他们的论点。

**句子结构**：The lawyers for the plaintiffs（主语）+ had grounded their argument partly in a Reconstruction-era federal statistic（谓语 + 宾语）。

**关键词**：Reconstruction-era federal statute

**表达方式**：Reconstruction-era（重建时期）暗示Brown的法律基础可以追溯到内战后的进步立法。

**为什么这样写**：揭示Brown的法律基础——它不是法院"独立创造"的，而是基于国会的立法。

> **原句 81:** The case could be understood as a straightforward matter of striking down state laws that violated a national one.

**中文理解**：这个案件可以被理解为一个简单明了的问题——推翻违反联邦法律的州法律。

**句子结构**：The case could be understood as a straightforward matter of striking down state laws（主句）+ that violated a national one（定语从句）。

**关键词**：straightforward matter / striking down state laws / violated a national one

**表达方式**：straightforward matter（简单明了的问题）暗示Brown可以被理解为"纵向审查"——推翻违反联邦法律的州法律。

**为什么这样写**：将Brown重新定位为"纵向审查"而非"横向审查"——这是Bowie和Renan的核心论点。

> **原句 82:** Bowie and Renan lament that the Court's unanimous opinion didn't mention the statute.

**中文理解**：Bowie和Renan遗憾地指出，法院的一致意见没有提及该法规。

**为什么这样写**：揭示Brown的"法理学神秘化"——法院没有提及法律基础，给人留下了"法院独立创造权利"的印象。

> **原句 83:** Although the Court was merely enforcing Congress's will, it "gave the impression, whether intentionally or not, that it was acting alone, regardless of what Congress wanted."

**句子结构**：Although the Court was merely enforcing Congress's will（让步状语从句）+ it "gave the impression…that it was acting alone"（主句 + 宾语从句）+ regardless of what Congress wanted（补充说明）。

**关键词**：merely enforcing Congress's will / gave the impression / acting alone / regardless of what Congress wanted

**表达方式**：gave the impression（给人印象）+ acting alone（独自行动）暗示法院"抢功"——将国会的成就归功于自己。

**为什么这样写**：这是Bowie和Renan的核心批判——Brown的"法理学神秘化"让法院"窃取"了国会的成就。

### 第 19 段：Bowie & Renan的核心论点

> **原句 85:** A Supreme Court lacking the power of horizontal review could still have issued the Brown decision.

**关键词**：lacking the power of horizontal review / could still have issued Brown

**表达方式**：could still have issued 暗示Brown不需要横向审查权——它可以通过纵向审查做出。

**为什么这样写**：这是Bowie和Renan的核心论证——Brown是"纵向审查"而非"横向审查"，法院不需要推翻国会的权力也能做出这个判决。

> **原句 86:** Most of the Warren Court's admirable activism was in fact vertical review, targeting state and local statutes.

**表达方式**：in fact（实际上）暗示常见误解——Warren Court的成就不是"横向审查"，而是"纵向审查"。

**为什么这样写**：用事实澄清误解——Warren Court的成就是"推翻州法律"而非"推翻联邦法律"。

> **原句 87:** Of the twenty-four landmark Warren Court decisions listed by Justia, none overturned an act of Congress.

**中文理解**：在Justia列出的24个Warren Court里程碑判决中，没有一个推翻了国会的法案。

**句子结构**：Of the twenty-four landmark Warren Court decisions listed by Justia（状语）+ none overturned an act of Congress（主句）。

**为什么这样写**：用具体数字（24:0）证明Warren Court不需要横向审查——它的所有成就都是通过纵向审查实现的。

### 第 20 段：横向审查的历史灾难

> **原句 88:** Although there haven't been many federal invalidations, Bowie and Renan rightly observe that they account for some of the most regrettable decisions in Supreme Court history.

**中文理解**：尽管联邦法律被推翻的情况不多，但Bowie和Renan正确地指出，它们构成了最高法院历史上一些最令人遗憾的判决。

**句子结构**：Although there haven't been many federal invalidations（让步状语从句）+ Bowie and Renan rightly observe that…（主句 + 宾语从句）。

**关键词**：federal invalidations / most regrettable decisions

**表达方式**：rightly observe（正确地指出）是正面评价——作者同意Bowie和Renan的分析。

**为什么这样写**：引入"横向审查的历史灾难"——虽然数量不多，但影响恶劣。

> **原句 89:** In horizontal-review mode, the Court has struck down laws limiting slavery and child labor, establishing income taxes and minimum wages, and restricting corporations from tilting elections or firing workers for union activity.

**中文理解**：在横向审查模式下，法院推翻了限制奴隶制和童工、建立所得税和最低工资、以及限制企业操纵选举或因工会活动解雇工人的法律。

**句子结构**：In horizontal-review mode（状语）+ the Court has struck down laws limiting X, establishing Y, and restricting Z（主句 + 三个并列的现在分词短语作定语）。

**关键词**：struck down laws limiting slavery and child labor / establishing income taxes and minimum wages / restricting corporations from tilting elections

**表达方式**：五个被推翻的法律类型（奴隶制/童工/所得税/最低工资/企业监管）——都是进步立法的核心成就。

**为什么这样写**：列举横向审查的历史灾难——法院推翻的都是进步立法的核心成就。

> **原句 90:** The triumphs are "dwarfed" by the "vastly larger" set of disasters.

**表达方式**：dwarfed（被矮化）暗示横向审查的"好"结果被"坏"结果淹没；triumphs vs disasters 是对比——少数好判决 vs 大量坏判决。

**为什么这样写**：总结横向审查的历史记录——灾难远多于胜利。

### 第 21 段：Warren Court的悖论

**句子结构**：A decision mandating school desegregation in Little Rock（主语 + 分词短语）+ insisted that…（谓语 + 宾语从句）+ the Court's supremacy over the other branches in interpreting the Constitution had been accepted since Marbury as "permanent and indispensable"（宾语从句内容）。

**关键词**：supremacy over the other branches / permanent and indispensable

**表达方式**：permanent and indispensable（永久和不可或缺的）是强烈的自我宣称——法院将自己的权力描述为"永久的"。

**为什么这样写**：揭示Warren Court的"自我定位"——它在做出进步判决的同时，也宣称自己的权力是"永久的"。

| overturn | 推翻 | Overturn judicial decisions. |

## 长难句专项

**①** "The power of vertical review allows the Supreme Court to invalidate state and local laws that contradict federal ones. The power of horizontal review allows the Court to overrule a coequal branch of government and strike down federal laws or regulations."

**解剖**：两个并列句，分别定义"纵向审查"和"横向审查"。vertical review = 推翻州/地方法律（维护联邦最高地位）；horizontal review = 推翻国会立法（凌驾于平等分支之上）。coequal（平等的）强调国会和法院本应平等——horizontal review打破了这种平等。

---

**②** "What Bowie and Renan add is that the loudest protests against horizontal review historically came from abolitionists, labor organizers, suffragists, and civil-rights activists."

**解剖**：What Bowie and Renan add is that…（主语从句 + 表语从句）。四个群体（废奴主义者/劳工组织者/妇女参政权论者/民权活动家）——都是进步运动的代表。这是全文最具颠覆性的论点——对法院权力最强烈的抗议来自进步派。

---

**③** "The Warren Court, while making substantively democratic rulings, cemented a procedurally undemocratic arrangement."

**解剖**：while making substantively democratic rulings（让步状语从句）+ cemented a procedurally undemocratic arrangement（主句）。substantively democratic vs procedurally undemocratic 是精确对比——法院的判决内容是民主的，但权力行使方式是不民主的。

---

## 精读结束总结

**1. 本文核心表达**
- **核心议题**：最高法院的"横向审查权"（推翻国会立法的权力）从Marbury v. Madison到当代的历史——它几乎总是被用于阻挠进步立法
- **关键词汇**：counter-majoritarian / horizontal review / vertical review / turducken of judicial folly / Lochner era / substantively democratic / procedurally undemocratic / napalm
- **核心论点**：横向审查权历史上最强烈的反对者是进步派（废奴主义者/劳工/妇女参政权/民权）；Warren Court的成就都是纵向审查，不需要横向审查；Trump对法院的不耐烦与Lincoln的历史警告形成呼应

**2. 重要语法**
- **what X add is that…（主语从句 + 表语从句）**：What Bowie and Renan add is that the loudest protests historically came from abolitionists… —— 引入核心论点
- **while making substantively democratic rulings, cemented a procedurally undemocratic arrangement** —— 精确对比：实质民主 vs 程序不民主
- **If they last as long as X did, the oldest will serve through Y, and the youngest until Z** —— 用具体年份量化长期影响

**3. 写作技巧**
- **30段长文的结构化**：按主题分组（Garland → 三法官 → 反多数主义 → Marbury → Calhoun → Dred Scott → Lincoln → 重建 → Lochner → FDR → Warren Court → Brown → Bowie & Renan → Trump）
- **历史与当代的呼应**：Lincoln的"the people will have ceased to be their own rulers" → Vance的"overturn the will of the American people"——历史在重演
- **食物隐喻**：turducken of judicial folly（司法愚行的火鸡填鸭）——将Marbury v. Madison定性为"一层套一层的荒谬"
- **流行文化典故**：Noah's-flood-on-your-wedding-day ironic（Alanis Morissette的《Ironic》）

## 可迁移表达

1. **counter-majoritarian** — 用于描述法院的反多数主义特征：The Court is often described as "counter-majoritarian."
2. **horizontal review vs vertical review** — 用于区分法院的两种审查权：The power of horizontal review / vertical review.
3. **turducken of judicial folly** — 用于描述一层套一层的荒谬：Making the whole thing a turducken of judicial folly.
4. **substantively democratic / procedurally undemocratic** — 用于精确对比实质与程序：Making substantively democratic rulings, cemented a procedurally undemocratic arrangement.
5. **Philip sober to control Philip drunk** — 用于描述理性约束非理性：Judicial power allows "Philip sober to control Philip drunk."
6. **the loudest protests historically came from X** — 用于揭示反直觉的历史事实：The loudest protests historically came from abolitionists, labor organizers, suffragists.
7. **a YOLO President is ready to napalm whatever obstructs** — 用于描述冲动的、不计后果的领导风格
8. **One wound still festers** — 用于描述未愈合的政治创伤：One wound still festers.
