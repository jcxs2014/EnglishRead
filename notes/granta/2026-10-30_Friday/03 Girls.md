### 第 7 段：与少女交谈

> **原句 18:** "The girls I met want a lot of the same things I wanted."
> **原句 19:** "Girls want true love. They want a boyfriend. They want a girlfriend."
> **原句 20:** "But they want to be understood and they really really really want someone to see them."

**中文理解：** 少女们的渴望和作者年轻时的渴望一模一样：爱情、被看见、被理解。
**句子结构：** 简单句的罗列；"really really really"的重复是语音上的强调。
**关键词：** want / understood / see them
**表达方式：** "really really really"的重复不像修辞，而像一句说不出口的话卡住了。
**为什么这样写：** 少女们没有用复杂的词语，但"want"的反复比任何高级词都更有力量。

### 第 8 段：结尾

> **原句 21:** "I was crying because I was hungry, because I wanted to eat this one specific sandwich, because I had developed sciatica in my ass from sitting in a stress position while driving, and I wanted to get out of that car."
> **原句 22:** "I realized I was no longer sad about our breakup, I was just angry."
> **原句 23:** "This shouldn't have been a surprise: your child self lives in the past and you have no right to go back there."

**中文理解：** 作者在沙漠里哭泣，连"为什么"都说不清，只知道疲惫、饥饿和旧日的情绪。
**句子结构：** 因为从句的排比（"because I was hungry..."）制造出一种"原因链"的窒息感。
**关键词：** crying / sciatica / child self
**表达方式：** "your child self lives in the past"把"后悔"说成一种地理上的误入。
**为什么这样写：** 以"你的孩子自我住在过去"收束全文，把"后悔"从情绪升华为一句关于时间的陈述。
