# Why can’t a tech-savvy China make its food safe?（精读分析）

## 概览

**来源**：[The Economist] **栏目**：（原文未明确标注） **副题**：Tainted vegetables are the focus of the latest scandal

**主题**：本文围绕新近的卷心菜甲醛丑闻，探讨科技精熟的中国为何仍无法让食品安全。作者先交代 Kangbao 县菜农用甲醛保鲜引发民愤，全国检查与三国进口加严；指出食品安全丑闻屡见不鲜，2008 乳粉与 2013 习近平“能不能确保食品安全” tied to legitimacy； despite 近年法律与农药改进，挑战仍艰巨；困难来自农业/物流的巨大碎片化、监管分权与利益扭曲；科技手段（直播厨房、区块链追溯、冷链）与市场方案（Sam’s Club、Hema）只能部分缓解，且成本推高食价；深层障碍在于企业快上市文化、基层官员地方产业激励、以及 Kangbao tero 的地方回护。全文强调廉价与安全之 tension，矛头指向“ everyone is maintaining a tacit balance between safety and economic growth”。

**结构**（11段）：
1. 卷心菜丑闻爆发与全国反应
2. 媒体谴责与“中国公众并不意外”
3. 民众怒火、历史大案与科技强国的尴尬
4. 已有进展：法律、农药、严格刑罚
5. 挑战规模仍 daunting：碎片化农业与流通链
6. 监管碎片与分工盲区（ resemblant 2024 食用油案）
7. 技术手段的希望：直播厨房、区块链、冷链
8. 成本上升与分级消费（Sam’s Club / Hema）
9. “可负担又可靠”的深根问题：企业文化与举报
10. 官方激励扭曲与“tacit balance”
11. Kangbao 的地方回护与媒体回避

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：卷心菜丑闻与全国 reacted

> **原句 1:** THIS TIME it is the cabbages that Chinese shoppers are avoiding.

**中文理解**：这次被中国购物者 avoidance 的是卷心菜。

**句子结构**：It is X that Y are avoiding（强调结构 cleft）。

**关键词**：THIS TIME / cabbages / Chinese shoppers / avoiding

**表达方式**：时间递进“又一轮”隐含 recurring scandals。

**为什么这样写**：Opening 用具体新案调动 reader，引出食品安全 recurring issue。

---

> **原句 2:** On August 22nd a blogger posted videos of truckers in Kangbao, a county a few hours’ drive from Beijing, dipping the vegetables in formaldehyde.

**中文理解**：8 月 22 日，一位 blogger posted videos，Kangbao（离京 a few hours drive 的县）的 truckers 正把 vegetables dipped in formaldehyde。

**句子结构**：On date + subject + verb + object; 同位语与定语 “a county… drive from Beijing”；participle “dipping…” 作宾语补足。

**关键词**：August 22nd / blogger / Kangbao / formaldehyde / dipping

**表达方式**：具象化丑闻（时间、地点、动作）。

**为什么这样写**：提供可验证细节，增强新闻可信度。

---

> **原句 3:** The chemical keeps their pale green leaves looking fresh on their journeys through the summer heat.

**中文理解**：该 chemical 使 pale green leaves 看起来 fresh on their journeys through summer heat。

**句子结构**：subject + keeps + object + complement + prepositional phrase.

**关键词**：chemical / pale green leaves / fresh / summer heat

**表达方式**：说明 motive（保鲜）而非恶意。

**为什么这样写**：解释 why truckers do it，引出 trade-off。

---

> **原句 4:** But formaldehyde can also cause cancer.

**中文理解**：但 formaldehyde 也会 carcinogenic (cause cancer)。

**句子结构**：But + subject + modal can + verb.

**关键词**：formaldehyde / cause cancer

**表达方式**：contrast 转向 harm。

**为什么这样写**： establishing risk，为 public outrage 铺垫。

---

> **原句 5:** Amid national outrage, authorities have set up checkpoints to test cabbages leaving the county.

**中文理解**：在全国 outrage 中，authorities set up checkpoints test leaving cabbages。

**句子结构**：Amid X + subject + have set up + object + infinitive.

**关键词**：national outrage / checkpoints / test / leaving the county

**表达方式**： governmental response.

**为什么这样写**： shows reaction，引出 “three detained”。

---

> **原句 6:** So far three people have been detained on criminal charges.

**中文理解**：So far 三人因 criminal charges 被 detained。

**句子结构**：So far + number + have been detained + on charges.

**关键词**：three people / detained / criminal charges

**表达方式**： legal accountability.

**为什么这样写**： quantify enforcement.

---

> **原句 7:** Singapore and South Korea have also stepped up inspections of Chinese cabbage imports.

**中文理解**：Singapore 和 South Korea 也 stepped up inspections of Chinese cabbage imports。

**句子结构**：X and Y have stepped up + noun.

**关键词**：Singapore / South Korea / stepped up / inspections / imports

**表达方式**：cross-border consequence.

**为什么这样写**：强调 exports stake and reputational damage.

---

**段落逻辑**：丑闻细节 → harmful chemical → public outrage → 国内 enforcement → 国际 inspection。

## 长难句专项

1. *“On August 22nd a blogger posted videos of truckers in Kangbao, a county a few hours’ drive from Beijing, dipping the vegetables in formaldehyde.”*
   - 结构：时间状语 + 主语 + 谓语 + 宾语；同位语 “a county a few hours’ drive from Beijing”；分词短语 “dipping…” 作宾语补足语。
   - 焦点：具体时空细节与非法动作。

2. *“Farmers now use less of them, thanks to training, crop testing and bans on the most toxic substances, notes Even Pay of Trivium China, a research firm.”*
   - 结构：主语 + now use less; thanks to 三并列；来源 attribution 尾注。
   - 焦点：农药减量因果链条。

3. *“In Kangbao, where GDP per person is about a third of Beijing’s, farming represents a substantial share of the economy.”*
   - 结构：In place + 非限定性定语从句（where） + 主句。
   - 焦点：地方经济依赖与监管 reluctance。

4. *“Shortly after this correspondent arrived in the county, four officers appeared in a police car, followed by another clutch of officials from the propaganda bureau.”*
   - 结构：时间 clause + 主句；participle “followed by…” 作方式/伴随。
   - 焦点：官方 media control 现场景象。

## 精读结束总结

1. **本文核心表达**：以 Kangbao 卷心菜丑闻 opening，解释-tech-savvy 中国 food-safety gap：法律与农药 progress 有，但 structural fragmentation（农业/物流/零售/餐饮）与 regulatory fragmentation + warped 官方 incentives & 企业 fast-to-market culture 使 cheap & safe 张力无解；tech (cold chain) and traceability help only paying tier; 全文 diagnostic 是 “tacit balance between safety and economic growth”，地方官员 media evasion 作 closing。
2. **重要语法**：cleft 强调 (It is … that …)；as X as Y；gerund purpose (To make …, China will…)；relative clauses；participle phrases (dipping; operating; followed by)；reporting verbs; quantified examples (5%, 1/3, 500/1,000, 300,000/6).
3. **写作技巧**：具体案例如 opening hook；contrast (progress vs daunting)；expert attribution; empirical figures; framing economic incentives; ironic ending（officials talk weather/birds, evade cabbages）; keeps ■ as source end marker。
