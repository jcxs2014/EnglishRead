# Back and shoulder surgery is often worse than useless

## 概览

- **来源/栏目/副题**：The Economist《经济学人》Leaders 社论，2026-09-26。题下小节标题 "Jack the knife"。
- **主题**：大量外科手术——尤其骨科中腰/肩相关手术——证据显示并不比非手术治疗更好，甚至风险大于收益；主因是对疗效缺乏临床数据；解法是公共系统自办随机试验推翻" assumed success"神话，并在医患双方间推行"共同决策"。
- **文体特征**：证据驱动的政策社论。Opening 用 "worse than useless" 定性，强因果句式 ("The main reason...")，parachute 比喻拆穿疗效illusion，数字锚点（£100m、6.2m、100,000）佐证改革必要性，Ending "But if your only tool is a scalpel..." 警句收束。
- **结构列表**：
  - 第 1 段：普遍手术与风险收益总账
  - 第 2 段：骨科手术具体无效比例
  - 第 3 段：无效手术对患者与系统的代价
  - 第 4 段：主因——疗效数据匮乏；parachute 比喻
  - 第 5 段：政府自办试验的可行路径
  - 第 6 段：试验结果如何影响报销与等待名单
  - 第 7 段： decades 习惯难改——医生心态
  - 第 8 段（Jack the knife）：共同决策与教育改革
- **段落脉络表**：

| 段号 | 主题 | 关键点 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 手术普遍，部分无意义甚至有害 | 6 in 10 Britons; $500bn/yr; 风险大于收益 |
| 2 | 骨科无效比例 | 10 项仅 3 项明确优于非手术；腰椎减压等 6 项无优势 |
| 3 | 代价 | 为患者带来身体疼痛+财务压力；系统浪费 |
| 4 | 主因与比喻 | 缺乏数据；parachute 假设成功；药物有 FDA 等证据 |
| 5 | 试验方案 | NHS 已试点跨医院试验 |
| 6 | 后续影响 | 拒付无效术式；释放医生资源 trimmed waiting lists |
| 7 | 习惯难解 | "scalpel makes everything ripe for cutting" |
| 8 | 共同决策 | 医学生与 mentor 培训；shared decision-making 标准化 |

- **核心金句**：
  - "millions of surgeries performed each year are unnecessary—and, given the risk of complications when a person is sliced open, often worse than useless."
  - "Six, including common ones like lumbar-spine decompression, are no better than drugs, physiotherapy or just Father Time."
  - "If the patient gets better, the parachute is assumed to have been a success."
  - "But if your only tool is a scalpel, everything looks ripe for cutting."

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：普遍手术与"往往有害"总论

> **原句 1:** SIX IN TEN Britons have surgery at some point.

**中文理解：** 十个英国人中就有六个一生中会做手术。

**句子结构：** `SIX IN TEN Britons have surgery at some point`——主谓宾（+ 时间状语）。

**关键词：**
- `SIX IN TEN` ⭐⭐⭐ — 十中有六。
- `at some point` ⭐⭐⭐ — 在某个时间点/一生中。

**表达方式：** **SIX IN TEN Britons have surgery at some point**：全句大写强调——先给读者设出" Surgery is normal"的底色。

**为什么这样写：** 先把手术塑造成"人人都会经历的常见事"——为引出 critique（其中不少没用）做铺垫。

> **原句 2:** So do lots of Americans, at a cost of perhaps $500bn a year, a third of all spending on adults in the world’s biggest health-care market.

**中文理解：** 很多美国人也是这样，每年花费可能高达 5000 亿美元——相当于全球最大医疗卫生市场中成年人总支出的三分之一。

**句子结构：** `So do lots of Americans, at a cost of perhaps $500bn a year, a third of all spending on adults in the world’s biggest health-care market`——助动词代动 + 无主句（承上省略）；appositive "a third of..."。

**关键词：**
- `$500bn a year` ⭐⭐⭐ — 每年约 5000 亿美元。
- `a third of all spending` ⭐⭐⭐ — 总支出的三分之一。
- `the world’s biggest health-care market` ⭐⭐⭐ — 全球最大的医疗卫生市场。

**表达方式：** **at a cost of perhaps $500bn a year, a third of ...**——用 "a third of" 把绝对数字换算成比例，让冲击更 intuitive。

**为什么这样写：** 用 "$500bn + 1/3 of all spending" 双重锚定——手术是美国医疗体系的最大开支之一。

> **原句 3:** Plenty of these procedures save lives, by removing tumours, transplanting new organs or mending broken ones.

**中文理解：** 其中很多步骤拯救生命：切除肿瘤、移植新器官或修补受损器官。

**句子结构：** `Plenty of these procedures save lives, by [removing tumours, transplanting new organs or mending broken ones]`——主谓宾 + 介词短语（三连不定式/动名词）。

**关键词：**
- `Plenty of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 很多。
- `remove tumours` ⭐⭐⭐ — 切除肿瘤。
- `transplanting new organs` ⭐⭐⭐ — 移植新器官。

**表达方式：** **by removing tumours, transplanting new organs or mending broken ones**：三个动名词并列，action-oriented（切除/移植/修补）——给 "worth it" 提供证据。

**为什么这样写：** 先承认外科的巨大贡献（避免 strawman），让后续 critique（很多不必要）更有可信度。

> **原句 4:** Many others make life better, say by restoring vision (cataract extraction) or hearing (tympanoplasty, for example).

**中文理解：** 另有许多手术改善生活，比如恢复视力（白内障摘除术）或听力（如鼓室成形术）。

**句子结构：** `Many others make life better, say by [restoring vision (cataract extraction) or hearing (tympanoplasty, for example)]` —— 主谓宾 + say by + 例证。

**关键词：**
- `make life better` ⭐⭐⭐ — 改善生活。
- `restoring vision` ⭐⭐⭐ — 恢复视力。
- `cataract extraction` ⭐⭐⭐ — 白内障摘除术。
- `tympanoplasty` ⭐⭐⭐ — 鼓室成形术。

**表达方式：** **say by restoring vision (cataract extraction) or hearing (tympanoplasty, for example)**——用括号内技术名 + "say by"（比如说）给读者具象 anchor。

**为什么这样写：** 列出明确受益的手术类型——白内障/听力——让 "some surgery is clearly beneficial" 成立。

> **原句 5:** Yet a growing body of evidence suggests millions of surgeries performed each year are unnecessary—and, given the risk of complications when a person is sliced open, often worse than useless.

**中文理解：** 然而越来越多的证据表明，每年进行的数百万台手术其实是不必要—and，考虑到人被切开时 complication 的风险——往往比 useless 还有害。

**句子结构：** `Yet [a growing body of evidence suggests [that] millions of surgeries performed each year are unnecessary—and, given ..., often worse than useless]`——Yet 转折 + 主谓宾 + that 宾语从句（含 em dash 作危及 qualifier）。

**关键词：**
- `a growing body of evidence` ⭐⭐⭐ — 越来越多的证据。
- `unnecessary` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不必要。
- `the risk of complications` ⭐⭐⭐ — 并发症风险。
- `when a person is sliced open` ⭐⭐⭐ — 当人被切开时（手术创伤）。
- `often worse than useless` ⭐⭐⭐ — 往往比 useless 还有害。

**表达方式：** **—and, given the risk of complications when a person is sliced open, often worse than useless**——em dash + given + 事实 + 强结论，修辞上把 "risk" 与 "useless" 绑定成因果链。

**为什么这样写：** "sliced open" 生猛动词（berieve in vividness as usual）让手术风险可视化；"worse than useless" 是题旨 phrase & title。

**段落逻辑：** 普遍手术 (01) + 美国规模 (02) → 成功病例 (03-04) → 转折 "Yet evidence" (05)：每年百万 surgeries unnecessary / often worse than useless, because of risk. 第 1 段完成"整体 framing"。---

### 第 2 段：骨科手术的无效比例

> **原句 6:** Of ten common orthopaedic procedures, a specialty which accounts for a quarter of all operations in Britain and a similar share in America, only three—for carpal tunnel and total replacement of knees and hips—offer outcomes that are clearly superior to non-operative care.

**中文理解：** 十种常见骨科手术中，骨科这一专科占英国所有手术的约四分之一，美国相似比例，却只有三项——腕管综合征手术与膝、髋关节全置换——能明确提供比非手术治疗更好的结果。

**句子结构：** `Of ten common orthopaedic procedures, ... only three—...—offer outcomes that are clearly superior to non-operative care`——介词短语作范围 + 主谓宾 + 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `orthopaedic procedures` ⭐⭐⭐ — 骨科手术。
- `a quarter of all operations` ⭐⭐⭐ — 所有手术的四分之一。
- `carpal tunnel` ⭐⭐⭐ — 腕管综合征（腕管松解）。
- `total replacement of knees and hips` ⭐⭐⭐ — 膝、髋关节全置换。
- `non-operative care` ⭐⭐⭐ — 非手术治疗。

**表达方式：** **only three—for carpal tunnel and total replacement of knees and hips—offer outcomes that are clearly superior to non-operative care**——em dash 列出 winners，定义 "clearly superior" 的 clinical threshold。

**为什么这样写：** 用 "one in ten list" 的 framing 定义 "clearly superior" 的高 bar，指出大多数 surgery 没达到.

> **原句 7:** Six, including common ones like lumbar-spine decompression, are no better than drugs, physiotherapy or just Father Time.

**中文理解：** 六项，包括常见的腰椎减压术在内，效果并不比药物、物理治疗或 mere time 更好。

**句子结构：** `[Six] ... are no better than [drugs, physiotherapy or just Father Time]`——主系表 (谓语 no better than)。

**关键词：**
- `lumbar-spine decompression` ⭐⭐⭐ — 腰椎减压术。
- `physiotherapy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 物理治疗/物理疗法。
- `Father Time` ⭐⭐⭐ — 时间（不可抗的自然疗法）。

**表达方式：** **no better than drugs, physiotherapy or just Father Time**——警句 "just Father Time" 把 time 打成 "provider"，强调 many symptoms resolve naturally.

**为什么这样写：** 用 "Father Time" 作比喻，把"自然康复" anthropomorphize 为 therapy——one of the article's cleverer moves。

> **原句 8:** Similarly, roughly two-thirds of excised appendices could have been fixed with antibiotics and many prostates, around 100,000 of which are operated on in Europe each year, are better off with watchful waiting.

**中文理解：** 同理，大约三分之二的被切除阑尾本可用 antibiotics 解决；而许多每年在欧洲动手术的 prostate（约 10 万例/年），更适合"watchful waiting"。

**句子结构：** `[roughly two-thirds of excised appendices could have been fixed with antibiotics] and [many prostates, around 100,000 of which are operated on in Europe each year, are better off with watchful waiting]`——并列独立句。

**关键词：**
- `excised appendices` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被切除的阑尾。
- `antibiotics` ⭐⭐⭐ — 抗生素。
- `prostates` ⭐⭐⭐ — 前列腺（指患者）。
- `operated on` ⭐⭐⭐ — 接受手术。
- `watchful waiting` ⭐⭐⭐ — 观察等待（主动监测）。

**表达方式：** **many prostates, around 100,000 of which are operated on in Europe each year**——"prostates"作 synecdoche for patients; relative clause quantifies scale.

**为什么这样写：** 跨专科扩展（阑尾 & 前列腺）证明 "useless surgery" 不仅骨科，模式广泛——"The list goes on" is earned.

> **原句 9:** The list goes on.

**中文理解：** 清单还很长。

**句子结构：** `The list goes on`——惯用表达（主谓宾+句末 up）。

**关键词：**
- `The list goes on` ⭐⭐ — 清单还很长。

**表达方式：** sentence-length micro-climax：一行短句收束 paragraph two, inviting reader to infer.

**为什么这样写：** 幽默/坚决地 signaling 不 exhaustive 的 list — "list goes on" means systemic, not anecdotal.

**段落逻辑：** 10 项骨科 → 仅 3 项明确 superior → 6 项 no better + 其他专科 (阑尾/前列腺) → "list goes on"：无效 surgical practice 跨专科普遍, 不仅 isolated outliers. 第 2 段完成"scope".--- 

### 第 3 段：无效手术的代价

> **原句 10:** For patients, needless surgery is a cause of physical pain and, especially in America, financial stress.

**中文理解：** 对患者而言，不必要的手术会带来身体疼痛，特别是在美国，还有财务压力。

**句子结构：** `For patients, needless surgery is a cause of [physical pain and, especially in America, financial stress]`——介词短语 + 主系表。

**关键词：**
- `needless surgery` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不必要的手术。
- `physical pain` ⭐⭐⭐ — 身体疼痛。
- `financial stress` ⭐⭐⭐ — 财务压力。

**表达方式：** **especially in America, financial stress**——position of "especially in America" after the comma adds urgency re US payment system.

**为什么这样写：** 以 patient-level harms 双重叙事（身体+财务），特别是美国，让 readers care.

> **原句 11:** For stretched health-care systems in ageing rich countries, it is a terrible waste of resources.

**中文理解：** 对在高龄富裕国 already stretched 的医疗系统来说，这是 terrible 的资源浪费.

**句子结构：** `For stretched health-care systems in ageing rich countries, it is a terrible waste of resources`——介词短语 + 主系表。

**关键词：**
- `stretched` ⭐⭐⭐ — strained / overburdened.
- `ageing rich countries` ⭐⭐⭐ — 老龄富裕国。
- `waste of resources` ⭐⭐⭐ — 资源浪费。

**表达方式：** **a terrible waste of resources**——straightforward moral-economic condemnation.

**为什么这样写：** 转向 system-level failure (opportunity cost)；"ageing rich countries" 用 demographics 定语。

> **原句 12:** A recent reduction in referrals for shoulder operations (just one of the six useless orthopaedic procedures) is saving England’s National Health Service (NHS) around £100m ($134m) a year, or 1% of its total surgical budget.

**中文理解：** 最近减少肩部手术转诊（仅仅六项无用骨科手术之一）就每年为英国 NHS 节省约 1 亿英镑（1.34 亿美元），或其总手术预算的 1%。

**句子结构：** `A recent reduction in referrals for shoulder operations (just one ... ) is saving [NHS] around £100m ($134m) a year, or 1% of its total surgical budget`——主谓宾 + appositive parentheses。

**关键词：**
- `reduction in referrals` ⭐⭐⭐ — 转诊减少。
- `shoulder operations` ⭐⭐⭐ — 肩部手术。
- `National Health Service (NHS)` ⭐⭐⭐ — 英国国家医疗服务体系。
- `£100m ($134m)` ⭐⭐⭐ — 1 亿英镑（1.34 亿美元）。
- `1% of its total surgical budget` ⭐⭐⭐ — 总手术预算的 1%。

**表达方式：** **just one of the six useless orthopaedic procedures ... saving around £100m ... or 1%**——用 "just one" 的数量 Anchor 和 "1%" 尾 trailing，强调 scale.

**为什么这样写：** 用 concrete case 证明 Avoiding useless surgery produces measurable savings — makes reform thinkable。

**段落逻辑：** 患者 harms (痛/财务) + system waste → 一小步 (reduce shoulder ops) → 100m/yr = 1% budget。第 3 段完成"costs & opportunity".---

### 第 4 段：无效手术存留的主因——缺乏数据与 parachute 比喻

> **原句 13:** The main reason for the persistence of useless procedures is a dearth of clinical data on whether they work.

**中文理解：** 无用手术持存的主要原因，是缺乏 regarding 是否 effective 的 clinical data。

**句子结构：** `The main reason for the persistence of useless procedures is [a dearth of clinical data on whether they work]`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `persistence` ⭐⭐⭐ — 持续存在。
- `dearth` ⭐⭐⭐ — 缺乏、匮乏。
- `clinical data` ⭐⭐⭐ — 临床数据。

**表达方式：** **a dearth of clinical data**——"dearth" softens "nothing" into "scarcity".

**为什么这样写：** establishing root cause succinctly before metaphor.

> **原句 14:** Surgeons liken asking about this to wondering whether parachutes are useful when leaping out of a plane.

**中文理解：** 外科医生 analogize asking about this to asking whether parachute is useful when leaping out of a plane。

**句子结构：** `Surgeons liken [asking about this] to [wondering whether parachutes are useful when leaping out of a plane]`——主谓宾 + analogy.

**关键词：**
- `liken ... to ...` ⭐⭐⭐ — 把……比作……。
- `parachutes` ⭐⭐⭐ — 降落伞。
- `leaping out of a plane` ⭐⭐⭐ — 跳出飞机。

**表达方式：** **liken asking about this to wondering ...**—— embedded metaphor; "leaping out of plane" paints urgent safety intuition。

**为什么这样写：** 暴露 surgeons' ethical intuition（ parachute analogy）as rhetorical shield against questioning efficacy.

> **原句 15:** If the patient gets better, the parachute is assumed to have been a success.

**中文理解：** 如果患者好转， parachute 就被 assumed succeeded 了。

**句子结构：** `If the patient gets better, the parachute is assumed to have been a success`——主句 + 宾语从句（ assumed to have been）。

**关键词：**
- `is assumed to have been` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被假设/被认作成功。

**表达方式：** passive "is assumed" 突出 "没有 causal proof"—— assumed successful vs proven effective.

**为什么这样写：** 这是 article 的 core epistemic critique: success is inferred, not tested. 

> **原句 16:** Regulators do not require proof that it actually was, as they do with new drugs.

**中文理解：** Regulators don't require proof that it actually was successful, as they do with new drugs.

**句子结构：** `Regulators do not require proof that it actually was, as they do with new drugs`——主谓宾 + 定语从句（that）+ as-comparative.

**关键词：**
- `Regulators` ⭐⭐⭐ — 监管者。
- `proof` ⭐⭐⭐ — 证据/证明。
- `new drugs` ⭐⭐⭐ — 新药。

**表达方式：** comparison "as they do with new drugs" reveals asymmetric regulatory standards.

**为什么这样写：** pharmaceuticals undergo RCT gate; surgery skips equivalent bar — structural unfairness.

> **原句 17:** Individual surgeons have neither the incentive nor the means to check for themselves.

**中文理解：** 个体外科医生既缺乏 incentive，也缺乏 means 来 self-check efficacy。

**句子结构：** `Individual surgeons have neither [the incentive] nor [the means] to check for themselves`——neither...nor.

**关键词：**
- `neither...nor` ⭐⭐⭐ — 既不……也不。
- `incentive` ⭐⭐⭐ — 动机/激励。
- `means` ⭐⭐⭐ — 手段/资源。

**表达方式：** **neither the incentive nor the means**—— balanced negation highlighting structural barrier.

**为什么这样写：** 说明 why status quo persists: incentives and tools both absent.

> **原句 18:** Contrast that with drugmakers, which bankroll big, costly randomised trials in the hope of making lots of money from pills that are proven effective.

**中文理解：** 对比药物企业：他们资助大型、costly 的 randomised trials，希望 profiting from pills proven effective。

**句子结构：** `Contrast that with drugmakers, which bankroll big, costly randomised trials in the hope of making lots of money from pills that are proven effective`——祈使句 + which relative clause + hope of + pills that are proven...。

**关键词：**
- `Contrast that with` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对比……与……。
- `bankroll` ⭐⭐⭐ — 资助。
- `randomised trials` ⭐⭐⭐ — 随机对照试验。
- `proven effective` ⭐⭐⭐ — 已证实有效。

**表达方式：** **in the hope of making lots of money from pills that are proven effective**——profit motive drives rigor — ironic contrast with public sector rationale.

**为什么这样写：** highlights market incentive for evidence; suggests public systems should fund trials now profit motive absent.

**段落逻辑：** 根因 = lack data (13) → surgeons analogy/parachute (14-15) → regulator asymmetry (16) → individual lacking incentive/means (17) → drugmakers contrast (18) showing private incentives exist. 第 4 段完成 "why useless persists"。--- 

### 第 5 段：政府自办试验的方案

> **原句 19:** Governments can start to correct this failure by running trials themselves.

**中文理解：** Governments can start correcting this failure by running trials themselves.

**句子结构：** `Governments can start to correct this failure by running trials themselves`——主语复合动词 + by-phrase.

**关键词：**
- `correct this failure` ⭐⭐⭐ — 纠正这一失败。
- `running trials themselves` ⭐⭐⭐ — 自己运行试验。

**表达方式：** **by running trials themselves** — direct actionable prescription.

**为什么这样写：** shifts responsibility to state, plausible for public systems (NHS).

> **原句 20:** The NHS has already begun doing this, with some success.

**中文理解：** NHS 已经开始这样做，并取得 certain success。

**句子结构：** `The NHS has already begun doing this, with some success`——主谓宾 + 并列/伴语。

**关键词：**
- `has already begun` ⭐⭐⭐ — 已经开始。
- `with some success` ⭐⭐⭐ — 取得一定成功。

**表达方式：** **with some success**—轻度 hedge, avoiding overclaim.

### 长难句 1（原句 5）
**原句：** Yet a growing body of evidence suggests millions of surgeries performed each year are unnecessary—and, given the risk of complications when a person is sliced open, often worse than useless.

- —and, given the risk of complications when a person is sliced open, often worse than useless — em dash + given 从句作 qualifier

**恢复逻辑：** 越来越多证据表明，每年百万手术不必要—and, considering risks, often worse than useless (given risk of complications when sliced open).

**整体理解：** em dash + given 把 "risk" 与 "worse than useless" 绑定成 causal chain, making the qualifying clause unavoidable.

## 精读结束总结

### 1. 本文核心表达
1. **"worse than useless" theme**: many ops don't improve outcomes, yet impose risk—thus net harm.
2. **Data gap root cause**: regulatory & incentive asymmetry—drugs require RCT proof; surgery doesn't.
3. **Policy levers**: government-run trials → evidence → payer refusal + reallocate surgical capacity → trim waits.
4. **Professional resistance**: ethical conviction ("doing right by patients") + scalpel bias; needs cultural change toward shared decision-making.

### 2. 重要语法
- **动名词主语**：Unlearning decades of modern medical practice will not come easily — 动名词短语作主语。
- **现在分词后置/作方式**：Surgeons freed from performing... could spend...; by removing tumours...
- **neither...nor balanced negation**：Individual surgeons have neither the incentive nor the means...
- **形式主语 + 不定式**：It is in everyone's interest to make it standard; It will be easier for payers...
- **hypothetical/qualifier with given**：—given the risk of complications when a person is sliced open—...
- **subjunctive parenthetical**：be they public systems or private insurers...
- **relative clauses**: which accounts for a quarter; of which are operated on; that includes lots...
- **em dash appositives**: listing winners (carpal tunnel/knees/hips), qualifiers.

### 3. 写作技巧
- **Opening with scaled claim**: SIX IN TEN + $500bn — grabs attention with scale, then narrows to evidence gap.
- **Acknowledgment of value**: "Many others make life better" builds credibility, avoids strawman.
- **Strong contrast**: drugmakers bankroll big RCTs; surgeons lack incentive/means — economic framing.
- **Parachute analogy**: vivid, exposes assumption fallacy ( assumed vs proven success ).
- **Rhetorical questions?** Not direct; uses "Yet..." pivot instead.
- **Em dash for sharp qualifiers**: "often worse than useless."
- **Concluding epigram**: "if your only tool is a scalpel..." memorable provocation; shared-decision consensus soft landing.

### 4. 可迁移表达
- "X is often worse than useless" — 评判控制介入 net harm.
- "a dearth of [data/evidence]" — 描述证据匮乏.
