# What Mao can teach China about managing America（精读分析）

## 概览

- **来源**：The Economist，原文日期 2026-09-26
- **栏目**：Leaders（评论性社论，匿名编委会署名）
- **副题**：无独立副题；文内二级分节标题为 `## **Not as good as checkmate**`

**主题**：文章借毛泽东 1938 年《论持久战》的三阶段框架（防御→战略相持→战略反攻），解释中国看待与管理中美对抗的方式——双方已进入“战略相持”，中国把 reaching 这一阶段视为 progress，但 stalemate 本身脆弱、痛苦且可能是 short-lived 的。

**结构**
1. 官方用语从 "war" 退到 "strategic stability"
2. 中国 thinkers 的 candid framing："strategic stalemate"
3. 毛泽东持久战框架的引入与证据边界
4. Mao 的三阶段序列
5. "strategic stalemate" 宣告的 significance 与角色分工
6. 第一阶段的 retreat：2018—2020 贸易协议
7. 第二阶段的 entrenchment：拜登任期内的半导体自力更生与 rare-earth counter-attack
8. parity discourse 与 "already progress"
9. 反攻第三阶段是否真在 agenda
10. `Not as good as checkmate`：stalemate 的 fragility（AI、rare earths）
11. rare-earth licensing 作为 relationship diagnostic
12. stalemate phase 的 pain 与 war-economy metaphor

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：对抗的 official naming

> **原句 1:** "TRADE WAR. Tech war. New cold war. All of these shorthand descriptions for frayed Sino-American ties have an air of foreboding."

中文理解：TRADE WAR、Tech war、New cold war——这些概括中美裂痕的 shorthand descriptions 都带着一股 foreboding（不祥）之气。

句子结构：三个并列名词短语作主语；主句 "All of these … have an air of foreboding"。

关键词：shorthand（简略/缩写式的）、frayed（裂痕处处的、磨损的）、foreboding（不祥预感）。

表达方式：连用三个 “war” label 堆积，先制造 anxiety，再为下句的反差铺垫。

为什么这样写：把 reader 陷在 “war” framing 里，下一句 official calm label 才显得 unexpected。

> **原句 2:** "Not so the label that the two countries agreed on earlier this year, at China’s urging."

中文理解：但 Earlier this year two countries agreed on 的那个 label，应中国 urging 而来，就不一样了。

句子结构：倒装 "Not so the label that …"，定语从句 "that the two countries agreed on …"。

关键词：at sb’s urging（应某方催促）。

表达方式："Not so X" 倒装，把 official label singled out。

为什么这样写：把 "constructive relationship of strategic stability" 作为 exception 引出来。

> **原句 3:** "They are now officially in a “constructive relationship of strategic stability”."

中文理解：双方现已 official 进入“建设性战略稳定关系”。

句子结构：主系表 "They are now officially in a …"。

关键词：constructive relationship of strategic stability（建设性战略稳定关系）。

表达方式：verbatim quote official phrase，保留 coined term 的 flavor。

为什么这样写：official term 必须原样引出，以便与 candid "strategic stalemate" 形成对照。

> **原句 4:** "If that fails to set your pulse racing, the cumbersome phrase has succeeded: it is meant to signify a calm approach for managing the superpowers’ tensions."

中文理解：如果该 phrase 未能 set your pulse racing，那这个 cumbersome phrase 已经 succeeded：它意在 signify 一种 calm approach for managing superpowers 的 tensions。

句子结构：条件句 "If that fails to set your pulse racing" + 主句 "the cumbersome phrase has succeeded"；冒号后 "it is meant to signify …"。

关键词：pulse racing（心跳加速）、cumbersome（笨重的）、signify（表征）。

表达方式："fail to set X racing … has succeeded" 的反讽；冒号引出设计意图。

为什么这样写：point out 命名的 design intent——calm 是 deliberate。

**段落逻辑：** 堆积 "war" framing 的不祥 → 反手点出 official "strategic stability" → 解读其 calm intent，引出 candid 对照。

### 第 2 段：中国 candid framing

> **原句 5:** "More candid, and more alarming, is how many thinkers in China now describe its relationship with America."

中文理解：More candid, and more alarming 的是，many Chinese thinkers now describe 其与美国的关系的方式。

句子结构：倒装 "More candid, and more alarming, is [how many thinkers …]"。

关键词：candid（坦率的）、alarming（ alarming/令人担忧的）。

表达方式：倒装强调 alarmingness。

为什么这样写：先 register "strategic stalemate" 比 official label 更 frank、更 alarming。

> **原句 6:** "Instead of strategic stability, they call it a “strategic stalemate”—a loaded term in China, evoking not diplomacy but a struggle for the upper hand."

中文理解：他们称之为 "strategic stalemate" 而非 strategic stability——一个在 China loaded 的 term，evoking 的不是 diplomacy，而是一场 struggle for the upper hand。

句子结构："Instead of A, they call it B" + 破折号 appositive "a loaded term … evoking …"。

关键词：loaded term（ loaded meaning/强内涵的术语）、upper hand（上风/优势）。

表达方式：破折号 appositive 解释 loadedness；"not diplomacy but struggle" 强对比。

为什么这样写：loaded term 必须解释，国际 reader 才能 grasp 其 resonance。

> **原句 7:** "Among those to do so recently: advisers to the Chinese cabinet, researchers under the Ministry of State Security, and scholars at leading universities."

中文理解：Recent 这样做的包括：Chinese cabinet 的 advisers、Ministry of State Security 下的 researchers、leading universities 的 scholars。

句子结构：冒号后三组并列名词短语。

关键词：Ministry of State Security（国家安全部）。

表达方式：institutional list 建立 sourcing 与 mainstream 性。

为什么这样写：显示这不是 marginal voices，而是 mainstream 官方/学界共识。

> **原句 8:** "Familiar to many in China, the words are drawn from Mao Zedong’s “On Protracted War” speeches, delivered in the remote Communist base of Yan’an in 1938."

中文理解：Familiar to many in China，这些 words are drawn from Mao Zedong《论持久战》speeches，delivered in remote Communist base Yan’an in 1938。

句子结构："Familiar to many in China" 过去分词作状语；主句 + 定语从句 "delivered in …"。

关键词：On Protracted War（《论持久战》）、Yan’an（延安）。

表达方式：source 的历史坐标。

为什么这样写：给 new term 一个 Maoist textual anchoring，铺下三阶段。

**段落逻辑：** 官方 label → candid alternative framing → institutional sourcing → Mao textual origin，完成 framing contrast。

### 第 3 段：analysis 的 evidence boundary

> **原句 9:** "Without giving undue weight to Mao’s musings from nine decades ago, the protracted-war framework yields valuable insights about how China views its contest with America and offers clues to how things might develop."

中文理解：Without giving undue weight to Mao 九十年前的 musings，protracted-war framework yields valuable insights about China 如何 view 其与 America 的 contest，并 offers clues to how things might develop。

句子结构：介词短语 "Without giving undue weight to X" + 主句双谓 "yields … and offers …"。

关键词：undue weight（不当的权重）、musings（沉思/漫谈）、yields（yield: 产生/提供）。

表达方式：concession + value claim。

为什么这样写：给 Mao 引用设 epistemic guardrail，防 reader overread。

> **原句 10:** "Ahead of Xi Jinping’s summit with Donald Trump in Washington on September 24th, what the old dictator had to say back in his guerrilla days is of interest."

中文理解：Ahead of Xi 与 Trump 在 Washington 9月24日的 summit，what that old dictator said back in his guerrilla days is of interest。

句子结构："Ahead of …" 时间状语；"what … is of interest" 主语从句。

关键词：summit（峰会）、guerrilla days（游击战时代）。

表达方式：time-bound relevance + dismissive-yet-interested "old dictator"。

为什么这样写：把老 Mao 与当下 summit episode 做 time bridge。

**段落逻辑：** 先设 boundary → 再以 summit timing justify interest。

### 第 4 段：三阶段 model

> **原句 11:** "Mao argued that in the first phase of a long war against a technologically superior adversary, scrappy revolutionaries must hunker down in defence."

中文理解：Mao argued，against 一个 technologically superior adversary 的 long war 第一阶段，scrappy revolutionaries must hunker down in defence。

句子结构："Mao argued that …" + 时间状语 "in the first phase of …"。

关键词：technologically superior adversary、scrappy（草根善斗的）、hunker down（ entrenched 防守/蛰伏）。

表达方式：Maoist heroism narrative。

为什么这样写：把弱方 framing 引入第一阶段，为 later 中国 roles 做准备。

> **原句 12:** "The next phase is strategic stalemate: the overstretched adversary can no longer advance and the revolutionary forces get better organised."

中文理解：下一 phase 是 strategic stalemate：the overstretched adversary can no longer advance，and revolutionary forces get better organised。

句子结构：主系表 + 冒号解释。

关键词：overstretched（过度延伸的）、get better organised（组织化/成体系）。

表达方式：Maoist schema 的定义性句。

为什么这样写：把 stalemate 定义为 adversary 过度 extension + revolutionary consolidation。

> **原句 13:** "Then comes the final phase, the counter-offensive, when the once-weaker revolutionaries rout the once-stronger enemy."

中文理解：Then comes final phase，the counter-offensive，when once-weaker revolutionaries rout once-stronger enemy。

句子结构："Then comes the final phase, the counter-offensive, when …"。

关键词：counter-offensive（反攻）、rout（全胜/彻底击败）。

表达方式：三阶段序列的 terminus，roles reversal 的 expectation。

为什么这样写：埋下 third-phase fantasy，下文 questioning whether Xi designs on it。

**段落逻辑：** 防御 → stalemate → counter-offensive progression，映射到中美 trajectory。

### 第 5 段：stalemate 的 freighted significance

> **原句 14:** "So declaring that China and America have reached strategic stalemate is freighted with significance."

中文理解：So declaring 中国与美国已 reach strategic stalemate，is freighted with significance。

句子结构："So [declaring …] is freighted with significance."。

关键词：freighted with significance（满载重大意义）。

表达方式：So 推论 + significance claim。

为什么这样写：把 earlier built-up 落到此 declaration。

> **原句 15:** "No one doubts which of the two powers is to be seen as the weary incumbent and which the energetic revolutionary."

中文理解：No one doubts which power 是 weary incumbent、which 是 energetic revolutionary。

句子结构："No one doubts which … and which …"。

关键词：weary incumbent（疲惫的 incumbent/在 incumbent 带权者）、energetic revolutionary（energetic 的 revolutionary）。

表达方式：rhetorical certainty。

为什么这样写：明说 mapping：中国 as revolutionary，美国 as weary incumbent。

> **原句 16:** "The trade war started in 2018, when Mr Trump slapped an initial batch of tariffs on China."

中文理解：Trade war started in 2018，when Trump slapped an initial batch of tariffs on China。

句子结构：时间从句 "when Mr Trump slapped …"。

关键词：slapped tariffs（动词 slap，kinetic shorthand）。

表达方式：origin marker。

为什么这样写：给当前 stalemate 一个 chronological anchor。

> **原句 17:** "For the rest of his first term, China was on the back foot."

中文理解：For the rest of his first term，China was on the back foot。

句子结构：时间状语 + "be on back foot"。

关键词：on the back foot（处于防守劣势）。

表达方式：idiom shorthand。

为什么这样写：assign phase 1 (retreat) to China。

> **原句 18:** "It was not entirely quiet, hitting back at America with its own tariffs."

中文理解：It was not entirely quiet，hitting back at America with its own tariffs。

句子结构：非谓语 "hitting back …" 作伴随状语。

关键词：hitting back（回击）。

表达方式：concede nuance，避免 caricature。

为什么这样写：保留 nuance，China 在劣势中仍 resisted。

> **原句 19:** "But it settled for a lopsided deal that if implemented in full would have led to China buying far more American goods and opening more widely to American investors."

中文理解：But it settled for a lopsided deal，which if implemented in full would have led to China buying far more American goods and opening more widely to American investors。

句子结构：主句 + 定语从句 + 定语从句内的 unreal conditional。

关键词：lopsided deal（asymmetric deal）、if implemented in full（全面落实的 conditional）。

表达方式：conditional perfect hypothetical。

为什么这样写：展示 first-phase retreat 的 asymmetric cost。

> **原句 20:** "In Maoist terms, the deal showed “the courage to retreat”, often necessary in the first phase of protracted war."

中文理解：In Maoist terms，the deal showed “the courage to retreat”，often necessary in first phase of protracted war。

句子结构：介词短语 + 主句 + 非限制性后置 "often necessary in …"。

关键词：courage to retreat（退却之勇气）。

表达方式：Maoist concept imported to legitimize retreat。

为什么这样写：把 retreat reframed 为 strategically virtuous。

**段落逻辑：** stalemate claim → 角色分工 → 2018 trade war → back foot → counter-tariffs → lopsided deal → "courage to retreat"，完成 first-phase narrative。

### 第 6 段：retreat 的 point

> **原句 21:** "The point of retreat is not to give up but to conserve resources for a fightback."

中文理解：The point of retreat is not to give up but to conserve resources for a fightback。

句子结构："The point of X is not Y but Z"。

关键词：conserve resources（ conserve 资源）、fightback（反击）。

表达方式：thesis sentence。

为什么这样写：turn from phase 1 to buildup。

> **原句 22:** "This is precisely what China did throughout Joe Biden’s presidency."

中文理解：This 是 precisely what China did throughout Joe Biden’s presidency。

句子结构：演示句 "This is precisely what …"。

关键词：throughout Biden’s presidency（拜登总统任期内）。

表达方式：historical relocation。

为什么这样写：locate second-phase evidence。

> **原句 23:** "Keenly aware of where America had immense leverage—notably, in advanced semiconductors—Chinese officials redoubled efforts to cultivate domestic alternatives, no matter how expensive."

中文理解：Keenly aware of where America had immense leverage——notably in advanced semiconductors——Chinese officials redoubled efforts to cultivate domestic alternatives，no matter how expensive。

句子结构：非谓语 "Keenly aware of …" 作状语（破折号插入 notably）；主句 "redoubled efforts …"；让步 "no matter how expensive"。

关键词：immense leverage（immense 的影响力）、semiconductors（半导体）、redoubled（加倍投入）、cultivate domestic alternatives（培养国内 alternatives）、no matter how expensive（不论多贵）。

表达方式：dash insertion specifying leverage；cost-acceptance concession。

为什么这样写：give redoubled efforts 的 motivation 与 cost tolerance。

> **原句 24:** "They also honed their counter-attack, creating a stronger system of export controls and tightening their grip on the Chinese companies that mine and process critical minerals."

中文理解：They also honed their counter-attack，creating a stronger system of export controls and tightening their grip on Chinese companies that mine and process critical minerals。

句子结构：动词 "honed" + 两非谓语分句（ creating … and tightening …）。

关键词：honed counter-attack（磨利的反攻）、export controls（出口管制）、critical minerals（ critical minerals）。

表达方式：parallel non-finite clauses 展示双轨 measure。

为什么这样写：genealogy of China’s rare-earth leverage 的 construction。

> **原句 25:** "When Mr Trump returned to office and cranked up tariffs, his officials were astonished by the ferocity of China’s pushback."

中文理解：When Trump returned to office and cranked up tariffs，his officials were astonished by the ferocity of China’s pushback。

句子结构：When 从句 + 主句（过去被动 "were astonished by"）。

关键词：cranked up tariffs（猛力提高 tariffs）、ferocity（ferocious/凶猛）、pushback（回推/反制）。

表达方式：shock collocation。

为什么这样写：dramatize cost of underestimating China’s phase-2 preparation。

> **原句 26:** "In blocking the exports of minerals, especially rare earths, China threatened to bring swathes of American industry to a halt."

中文理解：In blocking the exports of minerals, especially rare earths，China threatened to bring swathes of American industry to a halt。

句子结构："In [doing X], China threatened to …"。

关键词：swathes of（大片/大量）、bring to a halt（使 halt）。

表达方式：in doing X + threatened + result phrase。

为什么这样写：concrete demonstration of phase 2 counter-attack capability。

**段落逻辑：** point of retreat → Biden-period buildup → semiconductor leverage answer → export controls/minerals → Trump 2.0 shock → rare-earth threat，完成 phase 2 narrative。

### 第 7 段：parity 的 deeper meaning

> **原句 27:** "It has become commonplace to say that the superpowers have achieved rough parity in their ability to harm each other."

中文理解：It has become commonplace to say that superpowers have achieved rough parity in their ability to harm each other。

句子结构："It has become commonplace to say that …"。

关键词：commonplace（ commonplace/司空见惯的）、rough parity（rough 平价/ rough parity）。

表达方式：discursive marker。

为什么这样写：anchor common discourse before refinement。

> **原句 28:** "America has computer chips; China has rare earths."

中文理解：America has computer chips；China has rare earths。

句子结构：两分号短句并列。

关键词：chips vs rare earths（binary leverage inventory）。

表达方式：pithy antithesis。

为什么这样写：compress parity discourse into one line。

> **原句 29:** "Analysts call it “mutually assured disruption” or a “balance of vulnerability”, as if the two countries are, in economic terms, basically equal."

中文理解：Analysts call it “mutually assured disruption” or “balance of vulnerability”，as if the two countries are, in economic terms, basically equal。

句子结构：主句 + "as if …" 假设性从句。

关键词：mutually assured disruption（经济 mutually assured destruction 的 analog）、balance of vulnerability（vulnerability 平衡）。

表达方式：analytic jargon + skeptical as-if。

为什么这样写：prepare later refinement—analyst labels miss China’s self-assessment of progress。

> **原句 30:** "The “strategic stalemate” framing gets at something more profound about this parity: to have reached this far is already progress for China."

中文理解："strategic stalemate" framing gets at something more profound about this parity：to have reached this far is already progress for China。

句子结构：主句 + 冒号 + 不定式短语。

关键词：more profound、already progress。

表达方式：reframing parity as progress from China’s perspective。

为什么这样写：article 的 key interpretive claim。

**段落逻辑：** commonplace parity discourse → binary leverage inventory → analyst labels → reframe as China’s progress。

### 第 8 段：counter-offensive 是否在 agenda

> **原句 31:** "One obvious question is whether Mr Xi has designs on the third stage of the “protracted war”—a full-blown counter-offensive."

中文理解：One obvious question is whether Mr Xi has designs on the third stage of the “protracted war”—a full-blown counter-offensive。

句子结构："One obvious question is whether …" + dash appositive。

关键词：have designs on（图谋/锁定）、full-blown counter-offensive（全面反攻）。

表达方式：posing question, not answer。

为什么这样写：give third-phase ambition a question mark。

> **原句 32:** "Last year when trade hostilities with America were at their most intense, state media urged the public to revisit Mao’s speeches, to boost confidence in China’s resilience."

中文理解：Last year when trade hostilities with America were at their most intense，state media urged the public to revisit Mao’s speeches，to boost confidence in China’s resilience。

句子结构：When 从句 + 主句（urged sb to do，to do 是目的）。

关键词：state media（国媒）、boost confidence（boost 信心）、resilience（韧性）。

表达方式：evidence of Mao revivalism。

为什么这样写：show one potential third-phase signal。

> **原句 33:** "Yet the parallels with the great helmsman’s analysis go only so far."

中文理解：Yet the parallels with the great helmsman’s analysis go only so far。

句子结构：Yet + 主语 + 谓语。

关键词：great helmsman（伟大舵手/Mao）。

表达方式：concession + limits。

为什么这样写：prevent overreading evidence。

> **原句 34:** "Most Chinese thinkers believe it is impossible to score a decisive, enduring economic victory over America."

中文理解：Most Chinese thinkers believe it is impossible to score a decisive, enduring economic victory over America。

句子结构：believe + it is impossible to …。

关键词：decisive enduring economic victory（ decisive enduring 的 economic victory）。

表达方式：belief consensus。

为什么这样写：limit counter-offensive as unlikely objective。

> **原句 35:** "Maintaining a strategic stalemate looks like a more realistic objective."

中文理解：Maintaining a strategic stalemate looks like a more realistic objective。

句子结构：主语 "Maintaining …" + 系动词 + 比较。

关键词：realistic objective（realistic 的 objective）。

表达方式：comparative assessment。

为什么这样写：replace third-phase fantasy with sustainable stalemate。

> **原句 36:** "Huang Renwei of Shanghai’s Fudan University has said, hopefully, that it can last for 30 years."

中文理解：Huang Renwei of Shanghai’s Fudan University has said, hopefully, that it can last for 30 years。

句子结构：sb has said, hopefully, that …。

关键词：hopefully（ hopeful 的），30 years（30 年）。

表达方式：hopeful attribution + time horizon。

为什么这样写：give stalemate an optimistic horizon anchor。

**段落逻辑：** open third-phase question → Mao revival evidence → limits of parallel → thinker belief → realistic objective = stalemate → 30-year hopeful horizon。

### 第 9 段：stalemate 的 short-livedness

> **原句 37:** "The trouble with a stalemate is that it may prove short-lived."

中文理解：The trouble with a stalemate is that it may prove short-lived。

句子结构："The trouble with X is that …"。

## 长难句专项

### 长难句 1
> "Keenly aware of where America had immense leverage—notably, in advanced semiconductors—Chinese officials redoubled efforts to cultivate domestic alternatives, no matter how expensive."

解剖：主语 "Chinese officials"；谓语 "redoubled efforts to cultivate …"；状语 "Keenly aware of …"（破折号 "notably, in advanced semiconductors" 指明 leverage 所在）；让步 "no matter how expensive"。

难点："no matter how expensive" 是 cost-acceptance 让步，表明 willingness to pay。

可迁移：描述 strategic decision 时用 "Keenly aware of X—namely Y—Z redoubled efforts …, no matter how [adj]"。

### 长难句 2
> "But it settled for a lopsided deal that if implemented in full would have led to China buying far more American goods and opening more widely to American investors."

解剖：主句 "it settled for a lopsided deal"；定语从句 "that if implemented in full would have led to China buying far more American goods and opening more widely …"（relative clause 内 nested unreal conditional）。

难点：relative clause 内的 unreal conditional；"led to China buying … and opening …"（ caused someone to do）。

可迁移： "settled for a X that if Y would have led to Z buying A and doing B" 用于 policy retrospective。

### 长难句 3
> "Until Chinese AI models are as powerful as America’s—ie, until they have reached a stalemate in AI—China appears unwilling to slow its AI labs down."

解剖：时间状语 "Until X—ie, until Y—"（dash gloss 澄清）；主句 "China appears unwilling to slow its AI labs down."。

难点：double until + em-dash gloss。

可迁移： "Until A—namely B—C appears unwilling to do D" 常用于 threshold-dependent unwillingness。

### 长难句 4
> "A relentless focus on technological self-reliance has helped insulate China from American pressure."

解剖：主语 "A relentless focus on …"；谓语 "has helped insulate X from Y"。

难点：insulate from 的 economic-strategy sense（屏蔽/ insulation）。

可迁移： expressing policy outcome via "has helped insulate X from Y"。

## 精读结束总结

### 1. 本文核心表达
- 官方 phrase "constructive relationship of strategic stability" deliberate calm，≠ 中国 thinkers 的 "strategic stalemate"
- "strategic stalemate" 是 loaded Maoist term，evoking struggle for upper hand rather than diplomacy
- Mao 持久战三阶段（防御/相持/反攻）提供 mapping model，但 only so far
- China view：reach stalemate with US is already progress；不求 decisive victory 为 realistic
- stalemate is fragile (AI safety refusal; rare earths licensing retained as discipline leverage)
- stalemate is expensive：China's growth slows, welfare traded for industrial capacity, war-economy resemblance

### 2. 重要语法
- 倒装 "More candid, and more alarming, is how many thinkers …"
- 定语从句内 nested unreal conditional "a deal that if implemented in full would have led to …"
- "Until X—ie, until Y—Z" double-until gloss
- "urged the public to revisit Mao’s speeches, to boost confidence in China’s resilience"（目的不定式）

### 3. 写作技巧
- P1 堆积 war vocabulary 制造焦虑，再 reverse 以 calm official label
- 使用 Maoist textual authority（《论持久战》, 1938 Yan'an） legitimizing contemporary framing
- Counterfactual conditional（"could have created a transparent licensing system"） exposes claim-vs-reality gap
- 最后以 Mao warning echo（painful period, pessimism will grow） + war-economy metaphor darkening tone 收束
