## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：明年党代会与代际转向议题

> **原句 1:** Next year, at the biggest recurring event on China’s political calendar, a five-yearly Communist Party congress will confirm who will run the country until 2032.

**中文理解：** 明年，中国经济体最大的周期性政治事件——五年一次的党代会——将确认到2032年谁掌管国家。

**句子结构：** `Next year, [at the biggest recurring event on China’s political calendar], a [five-yearly Communist Party congress] will confirm [who will run the country until 2032]`——方式状语+主+宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `biggest recurring event` ⭐⭐⭐ — 最大面积的 recurring event，强调制度化。
- `until 2032` ⭐⭐⭐ — 区间 endpoint，可作 calendar 挑。

**表达方式：** **Next year, at the biggest recurring event on China’s political calendar, a five-yearly Communist Party congress will confirm who will run the country until 2032**——biggest recurring event（最大 recurring 事件）+ confirm who will run（决定谁做）——开篇把经济政治“ legitimize”的 institutional character（随时→ permanent）。

**为什么这样写：** 学者- framing：不是 NGO 对华分析，而是 because 事件 scheduled / institutionalized， preview progressive (preview 先刻 degree)。

> **原句 2:** The supreme position of Xi Jinping is in little doubt.

**中文理解：** 习近平“最高地位几乎毫无悬念”。

**句子结构：** `[The supreme position of Xi Jinping] is [in little doubt]`，主系表。

**关键词：**
- `supreme position` ⭐⭐⭐ — 最高地位。
- `in little doubt` ⭐⭐ — 几乎无懸念（but 最小 hedge，仍写 doubt）。

**表达方式：** **The supreme position of Xi Jinping is in little doubt**——supreme position（supreme position）+ in little doubt（almost no doubt）——把 focus 转向“下-level”.

**为什么这样写：** 先把习近平 (upper boundary) 锁定，这样 “hundreds of senior roles below him” 才 meaningful。

> **原句 3:** But hundreds of senior roles below him are up for grabs.

**中文理解：** 但其下数百个高级职位“ contention”.

**句子结构：** `[hundreds of senior roles below him] are [up for grabs]`。

**关键词：**
- `up for grabs` ⭐⭐⭐ — 使 open/ recontested（informal collocation）。
- `senior roles` ⭐⭐ — 高层职位。

**表达方式：** **But hundreds of senior roles below him are up for grabs**：but（转折）+ are up for grabs（争抢）——把权杖 battle 固化为 open hunting game。

**为什么这样写：** informal idiom creates dramatic incentive for reader attention.

> **原句 4:** A series of secretive meetings over the coming months will decide who gets them.

**中文理解：** 未来数月一系列秘密会议将决定谁得到这些职位。

**句子结构：** `[A series of secretive meetings ( over the coming months )] will decide [who gets them]`。

**关键词：**
- `secretive meetings` ⭐⭐⭐ — 秘密会议（中共不公开）。
- `over the coming months` ⭐⭐ — 时标。

**表达方式：** **A series of secretive meetings over the coming months will decide who gets them**——series of secretive meetings（private network）+ over coming months（future window）——emphasize opacity.

**为什么这样写：**  contrast with “Officially they will discuss…” → 中国政治 actual bargaining (historically, private bargaining) not public deliberation.

> **原句 5:** The dates of one such event have just been announced: a gathering from October 26th to 29th of the 370 or so leaders who form the party’s Central Committee.

**中文理解：** 其中一项活动日期刚公布：10月26日至29日，黨的中央委员会约370名领导人將聚集。

**句子结构：** `[The dates of one such event] have just been announced: [a gathering from Oct 26th to 29th of the 370 or so leaders who form the CC]`—— colon apposition.

**关键词：**
- `the 370 or so leaders` ⭐⭐⭐ — 无（现实数字）。
- `Central Committee` ⭐⭐ — 中央委员（最高权力 body）。

**表达方式：** **The dates of one such event have just been announced: a gathering from October 26th to 29th of the 370 or so leaders who form the party’s Central Committee**—— dates announced（transparency gesture）+ committee size（scope）。

**为什么这样写：** 具体数字让 opaque process readable。

> **原句 6:** Officially they will discuss “full and rigorous party self-governance”, meaning, in essence, obedience to Mr Xi.

**中文理解：** 官方议程是“全面从严治党”，实质上意味着对习近平的服从。

**句子结构：** `Officially they will discuss [“full and rigorous party self-governance”], meaning, in essence, [obedience to Mr Xi]`—— meaning phrase.

**关键词：**

**为什么这样写：** journalist vantage point: says nomenship?→ real ruleo persona.

> **原句 7:** Privately they will ponder a generational shift in Chinese politics.

**中文理解：** 私下里他们将 ponder 中国政治的代际转变。

**句子结构：** `[Privately they will ponder] a generational shift in Chinese politics`。

**关键词：**
- `generational shift` ⭐⭐⭐ — 代际转向， title 回响。

**为什么这样写：** 把 official “topical” agenda (self governance) 与 hidden agenda (generational shift)。

**段落逻辑：** 会议 framing & ambiguity: public agenda vs private  (Z described plus dates) → “full and rigorous self-governance” as euphemism for Xi obedience  → hidden question = generational shift.

## 精读结束总结
