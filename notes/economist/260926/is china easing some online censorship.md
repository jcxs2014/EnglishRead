- **来源**：The Economist，2026-09-26
- **栏目/副题**：China · 无独立副题（正文以评论/深度报道形式刊出）

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：党媒「异口同声」的宽松审查 rhetoric

> **原句 1:** “Treating differing voices as floods and terrifying beasts is a fundamental misunderstanding of the nature of governance,” would be an unsurprising homily from a liberal newspaper in a democracy.

**中文理解：** “把异见当作洪水猛兽，是对治理本质的根本误解”——这番话若出自民主国家的自由派报纸，毫不令人惊奇。

**句子结构：** 引语作宾语，主句 "`would be an unsurprising homily from a liberal newspaper in a democracy`"；条件/对比语境 "from a liberal newspaper..."。

**关键词：** homily（教训/说教）、differing voices（异见）

**表达方式：** 引语 + "would be an unsurprising homily from..." 构成 expectational contrast（先设 baseline 再翻转）。

**为什么这样写：** 先制造「这是常识」印象，为下句 "positively startling" 的来源翻转蓄势。

> **原句 2:** From the actual source, *Qiushi*, the Chinese Communist Party’s main theoretical journal, in September, it is positively startling.

**中文理解：** 而实际来源《求是》——中共主要理论刊物——9 月的表态， positively startling。

**句子结构：** "From the actual source, Qiushi, ..., in September" 作出处状语，主句 "it is positively startling"。

**关键词：** positively startling（ positively/极为 startling）

**表达方式：** "positively" 作强调副词，非 "正面" 含义。

**为什么这样写：** 来源身份的 shock effect——党的理论刊物发出与 liberal 论 tune 相通的 Voice。

> **原句 3:** Other party outlets have been sounding similarly enlightened.

**中文理解：** 其他党媒也 sustained sounding 同样 enlightened。

**句子结构：** 主语 "Other party outlets"，谓语 "have been sounding"，宾语 "similarly enlightened"。

**关键词：** enlightened（启蒙/开明的）

**表达方式：** "sound + adjective" = 发出某种 Tone（如 sound angry）。

**为什么这样写：** 说明这不是 Qiushi 一家孤例，而是一股 institutional trend。

> **原句 4:** “Wherever energy is spent on targeting the commenter and blocking channels of speech, almost without exception the result is an even greater crisis of trust,” said *China Youth Daily*, run by the Communist Youth League, in July.

**中文理解：** “无论在打击发声者、封锁言论渠道上下多少功夫，结果几乎无一例外都是更大的信任危机”，《中国青年报》（共青团主管）7 月 said。

**句子结构：** 引语作 said 的宾语；主语 "*China Youth Daily*, run by the Communist Youth League, in July"。

**关键词：** channels of speech（言论渠道）、crisis of trust（信任危机）

**表达方式：** "said (source), run by ..., in (time)" 引语 attributed 结构。

**为什么这样写：** 通过 youth-league 媒体 voices，让 "自由派" arguments 带上 institution 内部 legitimacy。

> **原句 5:** Some officials “seek only silence in public discourse, not the resolution of problems, and habitually block and delete posts instead of solving issues”, *China Comment*, a magazine run by the country’s official news agency, Xinhua, also weighed in that month.

**中文理解：** 有些官员 "only seek silence in public discourse，not resolution of problems，habitually block and delete posts instead of solving issues"，新华社主办的《瞭望》杂志也在那个月 weighed in。

**句子结构：** Some officials + 引语；主语 "*China Comment*, a magazine run by ..., Xinhua, also weighed in that month"。

**关键词：** weighed in（介入/发表意见）、public discourse（公共话语）

**表达方式：** "weighted" 引语 + source attributed，"also" 表并列 another voice。

**为什么这样写：** 用 official xinhua mouthpiece 的 direct accusation，给 liberal criticism 官方 stamped  legitimacy。

**段落逻辑：** 引语 accuracy → 来源身份 startling → 多 party outlets enlightened → China Youth Daily trust crisis → China Comment officials silence；从 "liberal newspaper" 的常见 argument 转到 "party voices"，制造 institutional pushback 的 appearance。

### 第 2 段： debunk「自由化回潮」

> **原句 6:** Is China going through one of the post-Mao era’s occasional phases when liberals in the establishment push back against conservatives through articles like these?

**中文理解：** 中国是否正经历后毛泽东时代偶发的「建制内 liberals 通过此类文章向 conservatives push back」阶段？

**句子结构：** 一般疑问句；"when liberals in the establishment push back against conservatives through articles like these" 作定语从句修饰 phases。

**关键词：** post-Mao era（后毛时代）、establishment（建制内）

**表达方式：** 通过 question 形式 hypothesis， readers 被 invited 思考。

**为什么这样写：** 设问引出 tempting narrative，马上 "Far from it" 反转。

> **原句 7:** Far from it.

**中文理解：** 远非如此。

**句子结构：** 固定 idiom 短句，作 emphatic denial。

**关键词：** Far from it（远非如此）

**表达方式：** 文章里 succinct twist，提供 rhetorical snap。

**为什么这样写：** 直接 answer question，bridge to data。

> **原句 8:** Since Xi Jinping took power in 2012, liberals have been all but silenced.

**中文理解：** 自习近平 2012 掌权以来， liberals 已被 all but silenced。

**句子结构：** "Since" 时间从句 + 主句 "liberals have been all but silenced"。

**关键词：** all but silenced（几乎完全 silenced）

**表达方式：** 现在完成时强调持续 state； "all but" = 几乎/除...外。

**为什么这样写：** 反例 evidence， undercut 制度派 liberal revive claim。

> **原句 9:** And censors have been as frantic as ever of late in their efforts to scrub the internet clean of grumbling about officialdom.

**中文理解：** 而且 lately， censors 一如既往 frantic， scrub internet clean of grumbling about officialdom。

**句子结构：** "And censors have been as frantic as ever of late" + "in their efforts to scrub..." 分词短语。

**关键词：** frantic（疯狂的）、grumbling（抱怨）、officialdom（官僚）

**表达方式：** "as...as ever" 厥 性 parallel； "scrub clean of" = 清理/抹掉。

**为什么这样写：** 背景 fact， supports " crackdown as usual" thesis。

> **原句 10:** In July news turned viral online of a parking-space dispute between an ordinary citizen and a cadre.

**中文理解：** 7 月，一则普通市民与 cadre 的 parking-space dispute online turned viral。

**句子结构：** "In July" 时间状语；主语 "news"；"turned viral online of a dispute..."。

**关键词：** parking-space dispute（车位纠纷）、cadre（干部）

**表达方式：** "turned viral" = 爆火/病毒式传播。

**为什么这样写：** concrete case， ground abstract censorship claim in everyday social conflict。

> **原句 11:** The censors deleted suggestions that an inquiry may have been rigged in the official’s favour.

**中文理解：** censors deleted suggestions that  inquiry 可能 rigged in official’s favor。

**句子结构：** 主语 "The censors"；谓语 deleted；宾语 "suggestions that an inquiry may have been rigged in the official’s favour"（宾语从句）。

**关键词：** rigged（被操纵/放水）

**表达方式：** "suggestions that..." 包裹 uncertainty-driven narrative。

**为什么这样写：** 例证 over-censorship 的 immediate trigger， qualifiers + gentle accusation delete。

> **原句 12:** The police are ever vigilant, too.

**中文理解：** 警察 likewise ever vigilant。

**句子结构：** 主语 "The police"；系动词 are；表语 "ever vigilant"；"too" adds。

**关键词：** ever vigilant（时刻警觉）

**表达方式：** 独立短句，emphasis securitization。

**为什么这样写：** broaden from censors to police， enforcement "true to form"。

> **原句 13:** Dare to post a message online that the death toll in last month’s flash flood on the Nepal-China border was higher in China than reported?

**中文理解：** 敢在网上发帖说 last month’s flash flood on Nepal-China border 的 death toll in China was higher than reported？

**句子结构：** "Dare to post..." 作 rhetorical question；"that the death toll ... was higher..." 宾语从句。

**关键词：** flash flood（山洪/暴洪）、death toll（死亡人数）

**表达方式：** rhetorical dare + interrogative framing， narrates prohibition。

**为什么这样写：** vivid extreme example， highlight no-dissent regime stakes。

> **原句 14:** Seven days in jail for that.

**中文理解：** for that， seven days in jail。

**句子结构：** 独立短语作 sentence， "Seven days in jail for that"。

**关键词：** jail（入狱）

**表达方式：**崇拜 minimalistic payoff， sharp punitive figure。

**为什么这样写：** 短句 punchline， emphasize concrete penalty。

> **原句 15:** A system that brooks no dissent is behaving true to form.

**中文理解：** 不 tolerance dissent 的 system，正在 behave true to form。

**句子结构：** 主语 "A system that brooks no dissent"（定语从句）；谓语 "is behaving"；宾语 "true to form"。

**关键词：** brooks no dissent（不容异见）、true to form（一如往常）

**表达方式：** "true to form" = mode 式 usual behavior。

**为什么这样写：** closing judgment， ties scooped examples to X 一致性。

**段落逻辑：** tempt liberal-thaw reading → Far from it → Xi era silencing → as frantic as ever → parking example → deletions → police vigilant → flash-flood dare → 7 days jail → true to form；Z 从 appearance to reality。

### 第 3 段：leaders worry 过度审查

> **原句 16:** But China’s leaders do sometimes worry that censorship can go too far.

**中文理解：** 但中国领导层 do sometimes worry that censorship can go too far。

**句子结构：** "But" 转折；主语 "China’s leaders"；"do worry"（do 强调）；宾语从句 "that censorship can go too far"。

**关键词：** do worry（did emphasis）

**表达方式：** "do worry" 施加 emphasis， reluctant admission。

**为什么这样写：** nuance twist， lead from "system true to form" to self-correction possibility。

> **原句 17:** Overly tight restrictions on everyday moaning, they suggest, can make it harder to get the feedback they need to improve government behaviour and thereby defuse public grievances before they explode on the streets.

**中文理解：** too tight restrictions on everyday moaning， they suggest， can make it harder to get feedback they need to improve government behaviour and thereby defuse public grievances before they explode on streets。

**句子结构：** 主语 "Overly tight restrictions..."；"they suggest" 插入；情态 "can make"；不定式 "to get the feedback they need..."； "and thereby defuse..." 并列。

**关键词：** everyday moaning（日常抱怨）、defuse（缓和/化解）

**表达方式：** "thereby" chain (get feedback → improve behaviour → defuse grievances → prevent explosion)。

**为什么这样写：** articulate government rationale， maintenance not freedom。

> **原句 18:** In China, calibrating how much censorship to apply is an art that officials often struggle to master in a way that pleases their bosses.

**中文理解：** 在中国， calibrating how much censorship to apply is an art that officials often struggle to master in a way that pleases bosses。

**句子结构：** 主语 "calibrating how much censorship to apply"（不定式短语）；谓语 "is"；表语 "an art that officials often struggle to master..."。

**关键词：** calibrating（校准/拿捏分寸）、pleases their bosses（讨好上级）

**表达方式：** metaphor "art" + "struggle to master" = skill valued by superiors。

**为什么这样写：** introduce system incentive， connect to later "zero public-opinion" metric。

> **原句 19:** In recent months several articles in state media have implied that many are not doing it right.

**中文理解：** 最近几个月 several state media articles implied many are not doing it right。

**句子结构：** "In recent months" 状语；主语 "several articles in state media"；"have implied that many are not doing it right"。

**关键词：** implied（暗示）

**表达方式：** hedge verb "implied" rather than state direct critique。

**为什么这样写：** transition to specific examples。

**段落逻辑：** leadership worries → everyday-moaning restrictions cost feedback → art to please bosses → media imply many wrong；lay out policy logic before Xi caution quote。

### 第 4 段：Xi 偏好「谨慎」

> **原句 20:** They would have been correct to think that Mr Xi prefers them to err on the side of caution when deciding whether to censor.

**中文理解：** They would have been correct to think that Mr Xi prefers them to err on side of caution when deciding whether to censor。

**句子结构：** "They would have been correct to think" + 宾语从句 "that Mr Xi prefers them to err..."；不定式 "to err on the side of caution"。

**关键词：** err on the side of caution（宁可谨慎）

**表达方式：** counterfactual "would have been correct" = past expectation confirmed。

**为什么这样写：** set up precedent Xi caution before possible tuning。

> **原句 21:** “Unhealthy discourse can become a narcotic for the public, a social divider, a soft knife that kills and a catalyst for unrest,” he told media bosses a decade ago.

**中文理解：** “不健康的言论会变成 public 的 narcotic， social divider， soft knife that kills， catalyst for unrest,” he told media bosses a decade ago。

**句子结构：** 引语（一 serial list 里四个 parallel nouns） + attribution "he told media bosses a decade ago"。

**关键词：** narcotic（麻醉剂/ addictive poison）、social divider（社会撕裂器）、soft knife that kills（柔性杀伤刀）、catalyst for unrest（动乱催化剂）

**表达方式：** quadruple metaphor parallelism， rhetorical stacking。

**为什么这样写：** 例证 Xi worldview， clarify why "err on side of caution" legitimate； article contrasts with possible fine-tuning。

**段落逻辑：** Xi prefer caution → quoted metaphors decade ago；indicate continuity of severe baseline。

### 第 5 段：Fine-tuning 与 local officials over-censorship

> **原句 22:** But Mr Xi may now want some fine-tuning.

**中文理解：** But Mr Xi may now want some fine-tuning。

**句子结构：** "But" 转折；主语 "Mr Xi"；情态 "may want"；宾语 "some fine-tuning"。

**关键词：** fine-tuning（微调/精细化调整）

**表达方式：** "may want" hedge， keep uncertainty。

**为什么这样写：** pivot， introduce change possibility while maintaining ambiguity。

> **原句 23:** Examples of over-censorship given by the media imply that his concerns relate to the deletion of comments that reflect badly on local officials.

**中文理解：** Examples of over-censorship given by media imply his concerns relate to deletion of comments reflecting badly on local officials。

**句子结构：** 主语 "Examples..."；谓语 "imply that his concerns relate to the deletion of comments that reflect badly..."。

**关键词：** reflect badly on（败坏名誉/危及）

**表达方式：** "given by the media" attribution + "imply" hedge。

**为什么这样写：** narrow scope， signals no party-level criticism.

> **原句 24:** There is no hint that he wants to encourage criticism of the party, let alone of himself.

**中文理解：** There is no hint that he wants to encourage criticism of party， let alone of himself。

**句子结构：** "There is no hint that..." + "let alone..." escalate (party → himself)。

**关键词：** let alone（更不用说）

**表达方式：** "no hint... let alone..." 双层 negation straits.

**为什么这样写：** core limitation warning， guard against overreading media rhetoric。

> **原句 25:** *China Comment* cited several examples of excessive twitchiness by officials, such as their blocking in May of a person’s social-media account for posting a complaint on a local tourism department’s account about the cleanliness of seating at concert.

**中文理解：** *China Comment* cited several examples of excessive twitchiness by officials， such as their blocking in May of a person’s social-media account for posting a complaint on a local tourism department’s account about cleanliness of seating at a concert。

**句子结构：** 主语 "*China Comment*"；谓语 "cited"；宾语 "several examples..."；"such as their blocking ... for posting a complaint ... about..." 嵌套。

**关键词：** twitchiness（神经紧张/多疑）、local tourism department（地方旅游部门）

**表达方式：** "such as" 例证；嵌套 for-posting-about 清晰 illustrate excessive reflexes。

**为什么这样写：** specific anecdote， concrete over-censorship instance blame local officials。

> **原句 26:** Another involved someone who last year posted a video online of a woman trying to assert her right of way on a narrow road by flashing her husband’s law-enforcement credentials.

**中文理解：** Another involved someone who last year posted a video online of a woman trying to assert her right of way on a narrow road by flashing her husband’s law-enforcement credentials。

**句子结构：** 主语 "Another"；谓语 "involved"；宾语 "someone who last year posted a video online of a woman trying to assert her right of way ..."。

**关键词：** assert her right of way（主张路权）、law-enforcement credentials（执法证件）

**表达方式：** 过去 participles（trying/flashing）service narrative。

**为什么这样写：** second vivid example， variety of overreach (credentials misuse)。

> **原句 27:** Police put pressure on the citizen to delete the footage.

**中文理解：** Police put pressure on citizen to delete footage。

**句子结构：** "put pressure on ... to delete" 经典 pressured infinitive。

**关键词：** footage（视频 footage）

**表达方式：** 简洁 statement， escalation。

**为什么这样写：** show institutional coercion， not voluntary deletion。

> **原句 28:** He did so, but later reposted an edited version that drew 10m views.

**中文理解：** He did so， but later reposted an edited version that drew 10m views。

**句子结构：** "He did so" 代 deletion；"but later reposted..." 异时态 conjunction；定语从句 "that drew 10m views"。

**关键词：** drew 10m views（引千万浏览）

**表达方式：** "drew views" = generate views； "edited version" hints workaround。

**为什么这样写：** payoff: censorship backfires， scale public attention。

**段落逻辑：** may want fine-tuning → concerns comments reflecting badly on locals → no party/self criticism → two twitchiness examples (concert seating; credentials) → police pressure → viral repost 10m；从 top intent to street-level demonstration。

### 第 6 段：Zero public-opinion 与 local officials

> **原句 29:** Mr Xi will hardly be surprised by such stories.

**中文理解：** Mr Xi will hardly be surprised by such stories。

**句子结构：** "will hardly be surprised" + "by such stories" agent phrase。

**关键词：** hardly be surprised（很难 surprise）

**表达方式：** "will hardly" expresses expectation。

**为什么这样写：** transition， normalize Xi awareness of local dodging。

> **原句 30:** Local governments often go to great lengths to prevent higher levels from learning about their mistakes.

**中文理解：** Local governments often go to great lengths to prevent higher levels from learning about mistakes。

**句子结构：** 主语 "Local governments"；"go to great lengths to prevent higher levels from learning..." 嵌套不定式。

**关键词：** go to great lengths（费尽心机）

**表达方式：** fixed phrase 定义 extraordinary effort。

**为什么这样写：** explain root incentive， makes following metric logical。

> **原句 31:** During Mr Xi’s rule, many authorities have used the achievement of “zero public-opinion [incidents]” to assess whether officials are fit for promotion.

**中文理解：** During Mr Xi’s rule， many authorities have used achievement of “zero public-opinion [incidents]” to assess whether officials fit for promotion。

**句子结构：** "During..." 时间状语；主语 "many authorities"；"have used ... to assess"；宾语 "the achievement of 'zero public-opinion [incidents]'"。

**关键词：** zero public-opinion [incidents]（零舆情事件）

**表达方式：** 引语 phrase in quotes preserved。

> **原句 32:** “The obsession with zero public opinion has given rise to a bizarre and inverted approach to handling public complaints, resulting in multiple deep-seated governance problems with significant harm,” thundered an article on the website of the party’s main mouthpiece, the *People’s Daily*, in July.

**中文理解：** “对零舆情的迷恋导致一种 handling 公众 complaints 的 bizarre and inverted approach， resulting in multiple deep-seated governance problems with significant harm，” thundered an article on website of party’s main mouthpiece，the *People’s Daily*， in July。

**句子结构：** 引语作主语（长 quote）；谓语 "thundered"；"an article on the website of..." 出处。

**关键词：** obsession（痴迷）、bizarre and inverted approach（奇特反转 approach）、deep-seated governance problems（深层治理问题）

**表达方式：** "thundered" = loudly declared（strong rhetoric）。

**为什么这样写：** official mouthpiece self-critique， author concludes local still won't risk it。

> **原句 33:** Local officials, however, will remain cautious. ■

**中文理解：** Local officials， however， will remain cautious。 ■

**句子结构：** 主语 "Local officials"；"however" concession；"will remain cautious"；原文 ending marker ■（Economist source marker, preserved in quote; not added by analyst）。

**关键词：** cautious（谨慎的）

**表达方式：** 简单 futurity + adverb， pessimistic ending。

**为什么这样写：** ultimate conclusion：fine-tuning no guarantee behavior change because incentives remain。

**段落逻辑：** Xi unsurprised → local cover-ups → zero-public-opinion metric for promotion → People's Daily thunder → local officials remain cautious；metrics over rhetoric。

## 段落逻辑

本文以 party media "loosening" rhetoric 为 hook， immediate debunk liberal-thaw thesis， introduceXi-era repression facts；then articulate government feedback rationale， Xi caution decade quote；then pivot to possible fine-tuning limited to local officials with two illustrative cases；finally identify "zero public-opinion" incentive distortion and end in resilient caution。Argument moves from appearance → underlying constraint → narrow possibility → structural obstacle。

## 长难句专项

### 长难句 1（原句 1）

> “Treating differing voices as floods and terrifying beasts is a fundamental misunderstanding of the nature of governance,” would be an unsurprising homily from a liberal newspaper in a democracy.

**结构拆解：** 引语动名词短语作主语；"would be an unsurprising homily from a liberal newspaper in a democracy" 作"if this were..." expectation；whole sentence sets expected baseline。

**翻译：** “把异见当作洪水猛兽是对治理本质的根本误解”——这番话若出自民主国家的自由派报纸，毫不令人惊奇。

### 长难句 2（原句 17）

> Overly tight restrictions on everyday moaning, they suggest, can make it harder to get the feedback they need to improve government behaviour and thereby defuse public grievances before they explode on the streets.

**结构拆解：** 主语 "Overly tight restrictions on everyday moaning"；插入口 "they suggest"；情态 "can make it harder"；不定式 "to get the feedback they need"（定语从句 "they need"）；"and thereby defuse..." 并列不定结构；"before they explode..." 时间从句。

**翻译：** 过度严格限制日常抱怨，他们指出，会让人更难获得改进政府行为所需的反馈，从而在 grievances 爆发之前就化解它们。

### 长难句 3（原句 25）

> *China Comment* cited several examples of excessive twitchiness by officials, such as their blocking in May of a person’s social-media account for posting a complaint on a local tourism department’s account about the cleanliness of seating at concert.

**结构拆解：** 主语 "*China Comment*"；谓语 cited；宾语 "several examples of excessive twitchiness"； "such as their blocking in May of a person’s social-media account for posting a complaint..." 嵌套例证。

**翻译：** 《瞭望》援引了 officials 过度神经紧张的 several examples，例如 5 月 they blocked a person’s social media account，只因为他在一个地方旅游局账户上发帖投诉 concert座位清洁。

### 长难句 4（原句 32）

> “The obsession with zero public opinion has given rise to a bizarre and inverted approach to handling public complaints, resulting in multiple deep-seated governance problems with significant harm,” thundered an article on the website of the party’s main mouthpiece, the *People’s Daily*, in July.

**结构拆解：** 引语主语 "The obsession with zero public opinion"；谓语 "has given rise to a bizarre and inverted approach to handling public complaints"；分词短语 "resulting in multiple deep-seated governance problems with significant harm"； attribution "thundered an article on the website... the People’s Daily, in July"。

**翻译：** “对零舆情的痴迷催生了一种 handling 公众投诉的奇特颠倒 approach， resulting in multiple deep-seated governance problems with significant harm，” July《人民日报》网站 article thundered。

## 精读结束总结

### 1. 本文核心表达

- 党媒出现「宽松审查」 rhetoric，但 Far from it：liberal 被 silencing
- leaders worry 过度 censorship 阻断 feedback，欲 fine-tuning
- fine-tuning 仅限 local officials，绝不触及 party / Xi
- “zero public-opinion [incidents]” 考核扭曲治理，地方 officials remain cautious

### 2. 重要语法

- “would be an unsurprising homily...” 起 baseline
- “do worry” 强调 reluctant admission
- “let alone” 双层否定：party → himself
- “such as their blocking ... for posting ... about ...” nested infinitive

### 3. 写作技巧

- opener quotation contrast：expected liberal newspaper → actual party journal startling
- rhetorical question + "Far from it" twist
- concession chain: "But ... may now want ... no hint ... let alone ..."
- concrete anecdotes (parking; concert; narrow road) grounding over-censorship
- pessimistic closer: local officials remain cautious

## 可迁移表达

- "Treating differing voices as floods and terrifying beasts..."（把异见当作洪水猛兽）
- "err on the side of caution"（宁可谨慎）
- "far from it"（远非如此）
- "let alone of himself"（更不用说他自己）
- "go to great lengths to..."（费尽心机去...）
- "thundered an article"（发表（anger/strong）声明）
- "remain cautious"（保持谨慎）
