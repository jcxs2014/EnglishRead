# China lavishes attention on a little-known competition（精读分析）

## 概览

- **来源/栏目**：The Economist，2026 年 10 月 16 日，中国栏目，By The Economist。
- **副题**：“It sees the ‘skills Olympics’ as a chance to boost its power”（它把这场“技能奥运”视为提振实力的机会）。
- **主题**：中国在“世界技能大赛”中的霸主地位：如何训练、如何宣传、并把它作为提振民族自豪感、投射软实力、以及扩大职业教育的工具。文章表面是体育式报道，实则揭示一种“以技能竞赛服务国家力量”的叙事。

**全文结构：**
- 第 1 段：引子——中国队创纪录夺 41 金，介绍赛事与规模
- 第 2 段：转折——这不是体育狂欢，西媒少有报道，中国却投入最多
- 第 3 段：历史——西班牙 1950 年发起；中国 2011 年参赛，如今两连冠式霸主
- 第 4 段：机制——政府选拔、基地训练、心理与体能准备
- 第 5 段：民族自豪——官媒日常转播、选手披 crimson 旗
- 第 6 段：软实力——Luban 工作坊、Luban 锁奖牌、李强呼吁合作
- 第 7 段：职业教育——克服歧视，解决 1000 万技工缺口
- 第 8 段：结尾—— cheering 易、下厂难（■）

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：引子——中国队创纪录

> **原句 1:** Once again Team China has crushed its rivals.

**中文理解：** 中国队又一次横扫对手。

**句子结构：** `Once again [Team China] has [crushed] [its rivals]`——时间副词 + 主 + 动 + 宾。

**关键词：**
- `Once again` ⭐⭐⭐ — 再一次。
- `crushed its rivals` ⭐⭐⭐ — 横扫对手。

**表达方式：** **Once again Team China has crushed its rivals**：`Once again Team China has crushed its rivals`——Once again（又一次）+ crushed its rivals（横扫）——以胜利开场，制造“ accustomed dominance” 的语感。

**为什么这样写：** 用 sports 语汇（crush rivals）把技能大赛娱乐化，先给读者“中国队无敌”的印象。

> **原句 2:** At the WorldSkills Competition it won a record 41 gold medals, ten times as many as South Korea, the runner-up.

**中文理解：** 在世界技能大赛上，它拿下了创纪录的 41 枚金牌——是亚军韩国的十倍。

**句子结构：** `At the WorldSkills Competition [it] won [a record 41 gold medals], [ten times as many as South Korea, the runner-up]`——地点状语 + 主谓宾 + 比较状语。

**关键词：**
- `WorldSkills Competition` ⭐⭐⭐ — 世界技能大赛。
- `a record 41 gold medals` ⭐⭐⭐ — 创纪录的 41 金。
- `ten times as many as` ⭐⭐⭐ — 是……的十倍。
- `the runner-up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 亚军。

**表达方式：** **it won a record 41 gold medals, ten times as many as South Korea, the runner-up**：`At the WorldSkills Competition it won a record 41 gold medals, ten times as many as South Korea, the runner-up`——record（创纪录）+ ten times as many（十倍）+ runner-up（亚军）——数据把“优势”量化。

**为什么这样写：** 用具体数字（41、十倍）让“ dominance” 可测量，而非空泛吹嘘。

> **原句 3:** Never heard of the event?

**中文理解：** 你从没听说过这项赛事？

**句子结构：** `Never heard of [the event]?`——省略主语的疑问句。

**关键词：**
- `Never heard of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 从未听说过。

**表达方式：** **Never heard of the event?**：`Never heard of the event?`——Never heard of（从未听说）+ the event（赛事）——直接与读者对话，制造“信息落差”。

**为什么这样写：** 主动招认西媒对赛事陌生，为后文“中国却重视”铺垫反差。

> **原句 4:** In China it was hard to ignore: the six-day extravaganza, which wrapped up in Shanghai on September 27th, involved some 1,400 young people from 60-odd countries competing before panels of judges amid Olympic-style razzmatazz (and as is now standard at show-off events in China: dancing robots).

**中文理解：** 在中国它很难被忽视：这场持续六天的盛事于 9 月 27 日在上海落幕，约 1,400 名青年来自 60 多个国家，在评委面前竞技，伴以奥运式的喧腾（而且如今在中国爱炫耀的活动里已成标配：会跳舞的机器人）。

**句子结构：** `In China it was hard to ignore: [the six-day extravaganza, which wrapped up in Shanghai on September 27th, involved some 1,400 young people from 60-odd countries competing before panels of judges amid Olympic-style razzmatazz (and as is now standard at show-off events in China: dancing robots)]`——冒号引出同位说明 + 定语从句 + 并列短语 + 括号插入。

**关键词：**
- `six-day extravaganza` ⭐⭐⭐ — 六天的 extravaganza/盛事。
- `wrapped up in Shanghai` ⭐⭐⭐ — 在上海落幕。
- `60-odd countries` ⭐⭐⭐ — 60 多个国家。
- `panels of judges` ⭐⭐⭐ — 评委组。
- `Olympic-style razzmatazz` ⭐⭐⭐ — 奥运式的喧腾/造势。
- `dancing robots` ⭐⭐⭐ — 跳舞机器人。

**表达方式：** **hard to ignore ... amid Olympic-style razzmatazz (and ... dancing robots)**：`In China it was hard to ignore: the six-day extravaganza, which wrapped up in Shanghai on September 27th, involved some 1,400 young people from 60-odd countries competing before panels of judges amid Olympic-style razzmatazz (and as is now standard at show-off events in China: dancing robots)`——hard to ignore（难被忽视）+ Olympic-style razzmatazz（奥运式造势）+ dancing robots（跳舞机器人）——形象化“ spectacle”。

**为什么这样写：** 用数字（1,400、60 余国）与细节（机器人）把“热度”具象化，同时 parenthetical 的机器人暗示作者对“ spectacle” 的讽刺。

**段落逻辑：** 中国队胜利（M1-2）→ 读者陌生（M3）→ 中国却充斥赛事 spectacle（M4）。第 1 段完成“引子”。---

### 第 2 段：转折——这不是体育

> **原句 5:** This was no sporting jamboree, however.

**中文理解：** 然而，这可不是什么体育狂欢。

**句子结构：** `This was no [sporting jamboree], however.`——主系表 + 状语。

**关键词：**
- `no sporting jamboree` ⭐⭐⭐ — 不是什么体育狂欢。

**表达方式：** **This was no sporting jamboree, however**：`This was no sporting jamboree, however.`——no sporting jamboree（非体育派对）+ however（然而）——转折：从“ spectacle” 转向实质。

**为什么这样写：** 用 however 转折，把读者从“奥运式热闹”拉回“技术竞赛”的本质。

> **原句 6:** Participants were tested on skills like bricklaying, machining and landscape gardening.

**中文理解：** 参赛者在砖砌、机加工与园林栽植之类技能上受测。

**句子结构：** `Participants were tested on [skills like bricklaying, machining and landscape gardening]`——被动句 + 介词短语。

**关键词：**
- `bricklaying` ⭐⭐⭐ — 砖砌。
- `machining` ⭐⭐⭐ — 机加工。
- `landscape gardening` ⭐⭐⭐ — 园林 gardening/造景。

**表达方式：** **tested on skills like bricklaying, machining and landscape gardening**：`Participants were tested on skills like bricklaying, machining and landscape gardening.`——tested on skills like（ tested on skills like）+ 三项并列——具体化“技能”而非运动。

**为什么这样写：** 列举“蓝领技能”（砖砌、机加工、园林），突出这是职业教育而非体育。

> **原句 7:** The tournament rarely makes headlines in the West.

**中文理解：** 这项赛事在西方很少上头条。

**句子结构：** `The tournament rarely makes headlines in the West.`——主谓。

**关键词：**
- `makes headlines` ⭐⭐⭐ — 上头条。
- `in the West` ⭐⭐⭐ — 在西方。

**表达方式：** **rarely makes headlines in the West**：`The tournament rarely makes headlines in the West.`——rarely makes headlines（很少上头条）+ in the West（西方）——与第 3 句呼应（西媒陌生）。

**为什么这样写：** 说明西媒忽视，反衬中国重视。

> **原句 8:** But China has poured more resources into it than anyone else.

**中文理解：** 但中国在这上面的投入比谁都多。

**句子结构：** `But [China] has poured [more resources into it] [than anyone else].`——主谓宾 + 比较从句。

**关键词：**
- `poured more resources into` ⭐⭐⭐ — 投入更多资源。

**表达方式：** **poured more resources into it than anyone else**：`But China has poured more resources into it than anyone else.`——poured more resources（ pour resources）+ than anyone else（比谁多）——投入优势。

**为什么这样写：** 为“ benefit far beyond arena” 铺垫：投入 disproportionate。

> **原句 9:** It sees benefits far beyond the arena.

**中文理解：** 它看到的是远超赛场的好处。

**句子结构：** `It sees [benefits far beyond the arena].`——主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `far beyond the arena` ⭐⭐⭐ — 远超赛场。

**表达方式：** **sees benefits far beyond the arena**：`It sees benefits far beyond the arena.`—— sees benefits（看到好处）+ far beyond the arena（远超赛场）—— thesis 句：信号“ beyond arena”。

**为什么这样写：** 一句 thesis，预告下文三 payoff（national pride / soft power / vocational training）。

**段落逻辑：** 非体育 jamboree（M5）→ 蓝领技能（M6）→ 西方少报道（M7）→ 中国投入最多（M8）→ stakes beyond arena（M9）。第 2 段完成“转折”。---

### 第 3 段：历史——从 1950 到 routinely dominates

> **原句 10:** Spain launched the competition in 1950 to encourage more young people to start apprenticeships.

**中文理解：** 西班牙在 1950 年发起这项赛事，以鼓励更多年轻人开始学徒生涯。

**句子结构：** `[Spain] launched [the competition] in 1950 [to encourage more young people to start apprenticeships].`——主谓宾 + 时间 + 目的不定式。

**关键词：**
- `launched` ⭐⭐⭐ — 发起、创立。
- `apprenticeships` ⭐⭐⭐ — 学徒制。

**表达方式：** **launched the competition in 1950 to encourage ... apprenticeships**：`Spain launched the competition in 1950 to encourage more young people to start apprenticeships.`——launched（发起）+ to encourage（目的）+ apprenticeships（学徒）——起源与动因。

**为什么这样写：** 先给历史源头，凸显“ vocational 起家”的基因。

> **原句 11:** It was not until 2011 that China began to take part.

**中文理解：** 直到 2011 年中国才开始参赛。

**句子结构：** `It was not until 2011 that [China began to take part].`——强调句。

**关键词：**
- `not until ... that` ⭐⭐⭐ — 直到……才……。

**表达方式：** **It was not until 2011 that China began to take part**：`It was not until 2011 that China began to take part.`——not until 2011（直到 2011）+ take part（参赛）——强调“加入晚”。

**为什么这样写：** 强调句突出“加入晚”，为“进步幅度”做 setup。

> **原句 12:** Then it won just a single medal, a silver in welding.

**中文理解：** 当年它只拿了唯一一枚奖牌——焊接银牌。

**句子结构：** `Then [it] won [just a single medal], [a silver in welding].`——主谓宾 + 同位语。

**关键词：**
- `just a single medal` ⭐⭐⭐ — 仅一枚奖牌。
- `a silver in welding` ⭐⭐⭐ — 焊接银牌。

**表达方式：** **won just a single medal, a silver in welding**：`Then it won just a single medal, a silver in welding.`——just a single medal（仅一枚）+ silver in welding（焊接银）——起步 modest。

**为什么这样写：** 强调“仅一枚银牌”的 humble origin，与今对比。

> **原句 13:** Now it routinely dominates the event, which is held every two years.

**中文理解：** 如今它常态性地在这项每两年举办一次的赛事中占统治地位。

**句子结构：** `Now [it] routinely dominates [the event], [which is held every two years].`——时间 + 主谓宾 + 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `routinely dominates` ⭐⭐⭐ — routine 地占据统治地位。
- `held every two years` ⭐⭐⭐ — 每两年举办。

**表达方式：** **routinely dominates the event, which is held every two years**：`Now it routinely dominates the event, which is held every two years.`——routinely dominates（ routine 霸主）+ held every two years（两年一办）——今昔对比收尾。

**为什么这样写：** 用 routinely 强调“稳定霸主”，非一运气。

> **原句 14:** Competitors have to complete tasks in their discipline, such as installing, testing and repairing a wind-turbine component.

**中文理解：** 参赛者必须完成本学科的任务，例如安装、测试与修理风电组件。

**句子结构：** `[Competitors] have to complete [tasks in their discipline], such as [installing, testing and repairing a wind-turbine component].`——主谓宾 + 例如并列。

**关键词：**
- `their discipline` ⭐⭐⭐ — 本学科/专业。
- `installing, testing and repairing` ⭐⭐⭐ — 安装、测试、修理。
- `wind-turbine component` ⭐⭐⭐ — 风电组件。

**表达方式：** **complete tasks ... such as installing, testing and repairing**：`Competitors have to complete tasks in their discipline, such as installing, testing and repairing a wind-turbine component.`——tasks in discipline + such as 三动词——具体化竞赛 format。

**为什么这样写：** 列举“安装/测试/修理”强调“实用技能评估”，非理论考试。

> **原句 15:** They are given points for such things as speed and precision.

**中文理解：** 他们因速度、精度等指标被评分。

**句子结构：** `[They] are given [points] for [such things as speed and precision].`——被动句。

**关键词：**
- `given points for` ⭐⭐⭐ — 因……得分。
- `speed and precision` ⭐⭐⭐ — 速度与精度。

**表达方式：** **given points for such things as speed and precision**：`They are given points for such things as speed and precision.`——given points（评分）+ speed and precision（速度与精度）——评分 criteria。

**为什么这样写：** 用“速度与精度”点出“工业级 standards”。

> **原句 16:** Drone piloting and cyber-security were among new categories added this year.

**中文理解：** 无人机操控与网络安全是今年新增的类别之一。

**句子结构：** `[Drone piloting and cyber-security] were among [new categories added this year].`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `Drone piloting` ⭐⭐⭐ — 无人机操控。
- `cyber-security` ⭐⭐⭐ — 网络安全。
- `new categories` ⭐⭐⭐ — 新增类别。

**表达方式：** **were among new categories added this year**：`Drone piloting and cyber-security were among new categories added this year.`——among new categories（ among new categories）—— category 增长反映“产业前沿”。

**为什么这样写：** 暗示 skills “与产业技术迭代” 对齐。

> **原句 17:** China won golds in both (though not in landscape gardening: France came first).

**中文理解：** 中国在这两项都拿下了金牌（不过园林项目不是：第一名是法国）。

**句子结构：** `[China] won [golds] in both (though not in landscape gardening: France came first).`——主谓宾 + 括号让步/补充。

**关键词：**
- `won golds in both` ⭐⭐⭐ — 两项都拿金牌。
- `landscape gardening` ⭐⭐⭐ — 园林。
- `France came first` ⭐⭐⭐ — 法国第一。

**表达方式：** **won golds in both (though not in landscape gardening: France came first)**：`China won golds in both (though not in landscape gardening: France came first).`——won golds in both（双金）+ though not...（让步：园林法国第一）—— parenthetical 体现 “作者不吹” 的 nuance。

**为什么这样写：** parenthetical 温和地“不失衡”—— 中国 dominance 也有例外，显示报道克制。

**段落逻辑：** 西班牙起源（M10）→ 中国 late entry（M11）→ 单银起步（M12）→ routine dominance（M13）→ 任务/评分（M14-15）→ 新增类别（M16）→ 双金但园林 exceptions（M17）。第 3 段完成“历史”。---

### 第 4 段：机制——举国体制复制到技能

> **原句 18:** As it does in producing Olympic stars, China’s government plays a big role.

**中文理解：** 正如在培养奥运明星时一样，中国政府发挥着重要作用。

**句子结构：** `As [it does in producing Olympic stars], [China’s government plays a big role].`——as 从句 + 主句。

**关键词：**
- `producing Olympic stars` ⭐⭐⭐ — 培养奥运明星。
- `plays a big role` ⭐⭐⭐ — 发挥重要作用。

**表达方式：** **As it does in producing Olympic stars, China’s government plays a big role**：`As it does in producing Olympic stars, China’s government plays a big role.`——As it does in...（正如...）+ plays a big role（ play big role）——把技能与奥运并置，暗示“体制复制”。

**为什么这样写：** 用 Olympic 作比，让读者直观“国家 machinery” 同构。

> **原句 19:** Promising young people are filtered through local, provincial and national contests before entering an elaborate training system.

**中文理解：** 有潜力的年轻人先经过地方、省级与全国层层选拔，再进入一套复杂的培训体系。

**句子结构：** `[Promising young people] are filtered through [local, provincial and national contests] before entering [an elaborate training system].`——被动 + 介词 + 时间。

**关键词：**
- `filtered through` ⭐⭐⭐ — 经筛选层层递进。
- `elaborate training system` ⭐⭐⭐ — elaborate training system。

**表达方式：** **filtered through local, provincial and national contests**：`Promising young people are filtered through local, provincial and national contests before entering an elaborate training system.`——filtered through（层层 filter）+ before entering（进入 elaborate system）—— hierarchy 选拔。

**为什么这样写：** 三层级（local / provincial / national）显示“ tournament 路径” institutionalization。

> **原句 20:** China has designated hundreds of bases—at vocational colleges, universities and companies—where contestants drill their skills under specialist coaches, sometimes including past champions, and receive physical and psychological preparation.

**中文理解：** 中国已指定数百个基地——设在职业院校、大学与企业——参赛者在专项教练指导下反复练习技能，教练有时包括历届冠军，同时也接受身体与心理准备。

**句子结构：** `[China] has designated [hundreds of bases—at vocational colleges, universities and companies—where contestants drill their skills under specialist coaches, sometimes including past champions, and receive physical and psychological preparation].`——主谓宾 + em dash 同位 + where 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `designated hundreds of bases` ⭐⭐⭐ — 指定数百个基地。
- `vocational colleges` ⭐⭐⭐ — 职业院校。
- `drill their skills` ⭐⭐⭐ — 反复练习技能。
- `specialist coaches` ⭐⭐⭐ — 专项教练。
- `past champions` ⭐⭐⭐ — 历届 champion。
- `physical and psychological preparation` ⭐⭐⭐ — 体能与心理准备。

**表达方式：** **hundreds of bases ... where contestants drill ... under specialist coaches ... and receive preparation**：`China has designated hundreds of bases—at vocational colleges, universities and companies—where contestants drill their skills under specialist coaches, sometimes including past champions, and receive physical and psychological preparation.`——bases（基地）+ drill（苦练）+ preparation（全准备）——“军备”式训练。

**为什么这样写：** 用 Olympic 训练 vocabulary（drill、physical and psychological preparation）把“技能”升格为“备战”。

- competing before panels of judges amid Olympic-style razzmatazz — 分词短语
- (and as is now standard ... dancing robots) — 括号插入

**找从句：** which 定语从句；as is now standard — as 从句（pour）。

**恢复逻辑：** 在中国难被忽视：这场盛事…… involve 1,400 young people … amid razzmatazz (and robots).

**整体理解：** colon appositive + 定语从句 + parenthetical： crowded but readable; 典型 Economist “crowded sentence”.

### 长难句 2（原句 17）

**原句：** China won golds in both (though not in landscape gardening: France came first).

**找主干：** China won golds in both.

**找修饰：**
- (though not in landscape gardening: France came first) — 括号让步/补充

**找从句：** 无形式从句；冒号引出说明。

**恢复逻辑：** 中国在这两项都拿金牌（不过园林 exceptions：法国第一）。

**整体理解：** 用括号 nuance，展示 dominance 的非 absolute。

### 长难句 3（原句 20）

**原句：** China has designated hundreds of bases—at vocational colleges, universities and companies—where contestants drill their skills under specialist coaches, sometimes including past champions, and receive physical and psychological preparation.

**找主干：** China has designated hundreds of bases.

**找修饰：**
- —at vocational colleges, universities and companies— — em dash 同位
- where contestants drill their skills under specialist coaches, sometimes including past champions — 定语从句
- and receive physical and psychological preparation — 并列谓语

**找从句：** where 定语从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 中国在职业院校、大学与企业 designated hundreds of bases, where contestants drill … and receive preparation.

**整体理解：** em dash + where + 并列谓语；一长句 packed 机制。

### 长难句 4（原句 28）

**原句：** They help advertise its “Luban workshops” in poorer countries: vocational-training centres named after a mythical Chinese carpenter.

**找主干：** They help advertise its “Luban workshops”.

**找修饰：**
- in poorer countries — 地点
- : vocational-training centres named after a mythical Chinese carpenter — colon appositive

**找从句：** named after ... — 过去分词短语（unnamed that-clause 省略）。

**恢复逻辑：** 它们助宣其在穷国的 Luban workshops：career training centres named after a mythical carpenter.

**整体理解：** colon 定义 + 分词短语；“神话 carpenter” 暗含 mythic founding。

### 长难句 5（原句 32）

**原句：** At the opening ceremony, Li Qiang, the prime minister, called for more co-operation on skills-building with the global south.

**找主干：** Li Qiang called for more co-operation.

**找修饰：**
- At the opening ceremony — 时间状语
- the prime minister — 同位语
- on skills-building with the global south — 介词短语

**找从句：** 无。

**恢复逻辑：** 开幕式上，总理李强呼吁与全球南部加强 skills-building cooperation。

**整体理解：** 信息句 packed names + pairing，但结构简单。

## 精读结束总结

### 1. 本文核心表达
- 中国把 WorldSkills 当成“ beyond arena”的工具：national pride, soft power, vocational training.
- 国家 machinery（ Olympic stars 式）复制到技能。
- 媒体 selective focus 产业强项 + 红旗叙事，social media hyperbole.
- Luban brand & standards export → soft power substance。
- vocational stigma vs 10m shortfall vs cheer-but-not-join paradox.

### 2. 重要语法
- as it does in producing Olympic stars（ as 从句）
- not until 2011 that（ emphasize）
- in the shape of（形如）
- attributed ... to（归因于）
- look down on（瞧不起）
- cheer on ... but do not want to join（并列不定式）

### 3. 写作技巧
- 用 sports vocabulary娱乐化 technical competition（ crushed rivals, razzmatazz）
- 数据 anchoring（41 gold、10x、1,400、60-odd、10m）
- 括号 nuance（though not in landscape gardening）保持 balanced
- third-party quote (organisational awestruck) rather than self-praise
- paradox 收尾：cheer workers but won't join factory

## 可迁移表达
- “benefits far beyond the arena” — 用于“ stakes beyond immediate field”。
- “unique in the resolve and scale of its embrace of …” — 用于“国家 machinery 介入某域”。
- “diplomatic sweetener” — 用于“不仅是外交糖衣”。
- “promote technology and standards that X wants to export” — 用于“ export standards via soft power”。
- “a 10m shortfall in skilled workers” — 用于“缺口 narrative”。
- “happy enough to cheer on their best workers, but do not want to join them in the factory” — 用于“nationalism vs individual career choice” paradox。
