# Chinese students abroad are more liberal than at home（精读分析）

## 概览

- **来源/栏目/副题**：The Economist（China column）；副题：They see faults in China’s system as well as in democracies, surveys show；日期：2026-10-01（URL）/刊登于 2026 年 10 月刊；作者：By The Economist。
- **主题一段话**：文章围绕一项追踪在美中国学生与国内学生四年政治态度变化的研究展开，指出出国读书并不能“改变”学生的思想——他们的意识形态在美期间相当稳定——但出国求学者一开始就比留国内的同龄人更自由主义；真正随时间变化的，是他们对中国共产党治国成效与爱国主义的评价，这种评价在日本封锁（零新冠）期间明显下降；同时，因部分女性为逃避性别偏见而西行、以及回国后又因竞争压力与青年失业重返澳洲等，留学本身更多是提供额外的选择，而非重塑政治。
- **结构列表**：
  1. 美国教育中国青年的“ missionary liberalism”传统与近期收窄。
  2. 西方与中方对青年交流的不同期待（大使/认知壁垒）。
  3. Fan Yingjie 等人的对照研究设计（在美 vs 国内 контроль group）。
  4. 研究的“rare & empirical heft”。
  5. 核心发现：思想稳定，但自选学生更自由主义；女性性别偏见解释。
  6. 政党治理评价的变化与零新冠加速。
  7. 西方 shortcomings 高亮（backwater 感）。
  8. 回国/再出走与少留美的美国人。
  9. 结论：留学不重塑政治，但给部分中国人更多选择。

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：美国传统与收窄

> **原句 1:** America should expect that educating China’s young people will give it “moral, intellectual and commercial influence”, wrote Edmund James, a university president, in 1906.

**中文理解：** 美国理应预期，教育中国的年轻人会为美国带来“道德、智识与商业影响”——这是大学校长埃德蒙·詹姆斯在 1906 年的断言（倒装结构）。

**句子结构：** 主句 America should expect that...（宾语从句 educating... will give it...）； followed by reporting inversion “wrote Edmund James, a university president, in 1906.”

**关键词：** moral, intellectual and commercial influence = 道德、智识与商业影响；wrote Edmund James = 倒装引文 attribution。

**表达方式：** 先抛出历史引语，再用倒装 “wrote ...” 把 attribution 挪到句末， mimic academic citation 的庄重与 distance。

**为什么这样写：** 以百余年前的乐观 expectation 开篇，建立“美国教育中国年轻人→改造中国”的 mission 论调，为后文“现在不欢迎了”的反转制造 baseline。

> **原句 2:** In subsequent years, dozens of China’s brightest went to study at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology on scholarships funded by the American government.

**中文理解：** 随后的数年里，数十位中国最顶尖的学生前往麻省理工学院学习，靠的是美国政府资助的奖学金。

**句子结构：** 介词短语 In subsequent years；主语 dozens of China’s brightest；谓语 went to study at MIT；分词短语 on scholarships funded by the American government。

**关键词：** dozens of China’s brightest = 数十位中国最优秀学生；scholarships funded by = 政府资助的奖学金。

**表达方式：** “China’s brightest” 是褒义 collective，强调 selecitivity 与早期精英通道。

**为什么这样写：** 用 concrete historical program（MIT scholarships）把 abstract mission 变成 real pipeline，方便读者理解“教育→影响”的假设如何 practiced。

> **原句 3:** Back then, American officials and teachers displayed a confident, missionary liberalism.

**中文理解：** 那时，美国官员与教师展现出一种自信、带有“传教”色彩的自由主义。

**句子结构：** 时间状语 Back then；主语 American officials and teachers；谓语 displayed；宾语 a confident, missionary liberalism。

**关键词：** missionary liberalism = 带有 missionary/传教色彩的自由主义。

**表达方式：** “missionary liberalism” 把政治文化比喻为 religious mission，带有 subtle critique of cultural imperialism。

**为什么这样写：** characterization of era’s ideology，对比“mould China in America’s image”的 paternalism。

> **原句 4:** They thought that educating young Chinese would help mould China in America’s image.

**中文理解：** 他们认为，教育年轻的中国人将有助于把中国塑造成美国的样貌。

**句子结构：** 主语 They；谓语 thought that...（宾语从句 educating... would help mould China in America’s image）。

**关键词：** mould China in America’s image = 把中国塑造成美国的样貌。

**表达方式：** “mould...in America’s image” metaphor 强调 social engineering ambition。

**为什么这样写：** 明确 early American expectation；与本篇 later finding “ideology stable, not reshaped” 形成对照。

> **原句 5:** But in recent years America has become less welcoming.

**中文理解：** 但近年来，美国变得更不欢迎外国学生了。

**句子结构：** 转折 But；時間状语 in recent years；主语 America；谓语 has become；表语 less welcoming。

**关键词：** less welcoming = 更不欢迎。

**表达方式：** 平句 concise reversal， sets up enrollment drop。

**为什么这样写：** 为 Xi’s counter-offer 的 context 做铺垫：当美国收窄，中国更自信。

> **原句 6:** The number of Chinese enrolled in American universities fell by nearly a third between 2019 and 2025, to 265,000.

**中文理解：** 2019 年到 2025 年间，就读美国大学的中国学生数量下降了近三分之一，降到 26.5 万。

**句子结构：** 主语 The number of Chinese enrolled in American universities；谓语 fell by nearly a third；时间状语 between 2019 and 2025；结果状语 to 265,000。

**关键词：** enrolled in = 在……注册/就读；fell by nearly a third = 下降了近三分之一。

**表达方式：** “fell by nearly a third ... to 265,000” 用 magnitude + endpoint 的 statistical expression。

**为什么这样写：** quantify shift in educational geopolitics； support “less welcoming / confident China” framing。

### 第 2 段：双方交流口号

> **原句 7:** China, however, is feeling confident.

**中文理解：** 然而，中国却感到自信。

**句子结构：** 转折 China, however,；谓语 is feeling；表语 confident。

**关键词：** is feeling confident = 感到自信。

**表达方式：** 与上段 “America has become less welcoming” 形成对照；“feeling confident” soft but decisive。

**为什么这样写：** 引出 Xi 招揽 американских学生 的 policy aspiration。

> **原句 8:** Its leader, Xi Jinping, wants more young Americans to visit and stay for their studies.

**中文理解：** 其领导人习近平希望更多美国年轻人来华访问，并留下学习。

**句子结构：** 主语 Its leader, Xi Jinping, 作 apposition；谓语 wants；宾语 more young Americans；不定式 to visit and stay for their studies。

**关键词：** visit and stay for their studies = 访问并留下学习。

**表达方式：** “visit and stay” captures two-step engagement (short visit vs. full study)。

**为什么这样写：** 布置 China 的 openness contrast，lead to 100,000 goal。

> **原句 9:** On September 24th, while at the White House, Mr Xi said he hoped 100,000 young Americans would visit China or study there in the next five years.

**中文理解：** 9 月 24 日，习近平在白宫表示，他希望未来五年有 10 万美国年轻人访问中国或在那里学习。

**句子结构：** 时间状语 On September 24th, while at the White House；主语 Mr Xi；谓语 said；宾语从句 he hoped 100,000... would visit or study... in the next five years。

**关键词：** 100,000 young Americans = 10 万美国年轻人；would visit China or study there = 访问或留学。

**表达方式：** 用 “said he hoped” 保留 diplomatic wish 的 softness；数字 goal 具象。

**为什么这样写：** provide quantified aspiration， setup contrast with 2022 baseline。

> **原句 10:** That is a far cry from the mere 350 American students resident there in 2022, amid the covid-19 pandemic and at a low point in bilateral relations.

**中文理解：** 这与 2022 年新冠疫情高压与双边关系低谷时期仅有的 350 名美国学生相比，差得很远。

**句子结构：** 主语 That；表语 is a far cry from；宾语 the mere 350 American students resident there in 2022；后置状语 amid... and at a low point...。

**关键词：** far cry from = 与……相差甚远；mere 350 = 仅 350 人；bilateral relations = 双边关系。

**表达方式：** “far cry from the mere 350” 用 huge gap 强调 ambition scale。

**为什么这样写：** provide historical baseline， highlight recovery from pandemic trough。

> **原句 11:** Mr Xi says that such exchanges could create “a new generation of ambassadors” to sustain the relationship between the two powers.

**中文理解：** 习近平说，此类交流可能培养出“新一代大使”，以维系两大国之间的关系。

**句子结构：** 主语 Mr Xi；谓语 says that；宾语从句 such exchanges could create “a new generation of ambassadors”；不定式 to sustain the relationship...。

**关键词：** “a new generation of ambassadors” = 新一代大使；sustain the relationship between the two powers = 维系两大国关系。

**表达方式：** “ambassadors” metaphor：学生=民间外交官，echo Edmund James 的 moulding ambition but reverse direction。

**为什么这样写：** surface China’s narrative that education/study = soft power influence； sets up “does experience change thinking?” question。

> **原句 12:** Visiting China “breaks down cognitive barriers”, notes Xinhua, an official news agency.

**中文理解：** 官方通讯社新华社指出，访问中国“能打破认知壁垒”。

**句子结构：** 主句 Visiting China “breaks down cognitive barriers”； reporting verb notes； attribution Xinhua, an official news agency。

**关键词：** breaks down cognitive barriers = 打破认知壁垒。

**表达方式：** 用 Xinhua 引语作为 official voice， then pivot with “But...”。

**为什么这样写：** include official claim about cognition， then contest/question it in next sentence。

> **原句 13:** But does experience of a different political environment change people’s thinking?

**中文理解：** 但是，身处不同的政治环境是否会改变人们的思维？

**句子结构：** 转折 But；疑问句 does experience of a different political environment change people’s thinking?

**关键词：** different political environment = 不同的政治环境；change people’s thinking = 改变思维。

**表达方式：** rhetorical question sums central question； frames rest of article as answer。

**为什么这样写：** pivot from narratives to empirical question； directly leads to Fan’s research。

### 第 3 段：研究设计

> **原句 14:** New research by Fan Yingjie of Princeton and co-authors explores whether it does among Chinese in America.

**中文理解：** 普林斯顿大学的范英杰及其合作者的新研究探讨了这个问题在美国的中国人身上是否成立。

**句子结构：** 主语 New research by Fan Yingjie of Princeton and co-authors；谓语 explores whether it does；介词短语 among Chinese in America。

**关键词：** co-authors = 合作者；explores whether = 探讨是否。

**表达方式：** academic framing “explores whether...”。

**为什么这样写：** 引入 empirical study， shift from anecdote/narrative to evidence。

> **原句 15:** The researchers enlisted almost 1,000 Chinese freshmen for their research in 2019.

**中文理解：** 研究人员在 2019 年招募了近 1000 名中国大一新生参与研究。

**句子结构：** 主语 The researchers；谓语 enlisted；宾语 almost 1,000 Chinese freshmen；介词短语 for their research in 2019。

**关键词：** enlisted = 招募/征募；freshmen = 大一新生。

**表达方式：** “enlisted” 让 research sound like longitudinal cohort construction。

**为什么这样写：** establish sample size/scope； base-year before US-China tension peaks。

> **原句 16:** Nearly 400 of them were in America and the rest—a control group—at public universities in China.

**中文理解：** 其中近 400 人在美，其余——作为 controle group——在中国的公立大学就读。

**句子结构：** 主句 Nearly 400 of them were in America；并列 and the rest—a control group—at public universities in China。

**关键词：** control group = 控制组；public universities in China = 中国公立大学。

**表达方式：** em dashes set off “a control group” as clarification； contrast US vs China environments。

**为什么这样写：** clarify comparative design (treatment vs control) that gives research validity。

> **原句 17:** Over the next four years, Ms Fan and her colleagues regularly put questions to the students to gauge their political leanings, as well as what they thought of China and its government.

**中文理解：** 在接下来的四年里，范女士及其同事定期向学生提问，以 gauging their political leanings, and also what they thought of China and its government。

**句子结构：** 时间状语 Over the next four years；主语 Ms Fan and her colleagues；谓语 regularly put questions to the students；不定式 to gauge...；并列宾语 as well as what...。

**关键词：** political leanings = 政治倾向；gauge = 测量/评估。

**表达方式：** “put questions to” rather than “survey” humanizes interaction; four-year panel design。

**为什么这样写：** specify outcome measures (ideology + regime evaluation); longitudinal tracking to distinguish selection vs treatment。

### 第 4 段：研究稀缺性

> **原句 18:** The work is remarkable because research that involves asking Chinese citizens in China for their views on sensitive political matters has become increasingly rare under Mr Xi’s rule.

**中文理解：** 这项研究之所以引人注目，是因为在习近平领导下，涉及向中国公民询问敏感政治议题的研究已变得越来越稀少。

**句子结构：** 主语 The work；表语 is remarkable because...（宾语从句 research that involves asking... has become increasingly rare under Mr Xi’s rule）。

**关键词：** sensitive political matters = 敏感政治议题；increasingly rare = 越来越少见。

**表达方式：** “remarkable because” elevates study; “increasingly rare” implicit critique of political climate。

**为什么这样写：** explain why study is valuable (rarity + access); frames Xi era constraints。

> **原句 19:** The ability to compare the answers of such a group with those of Chinese students in America gives the research unusual empirical heft.

**中文理解：** 能够把这样一个群体的答案与中国留学生在美答案进行对比，使这项研究具有不寻常的经验实证分量。

**句子结构：** 主语 The ability to compare A with B；谓语 gives；宾语 the research；宾语补足语 unusual empirical heft。

**关键词：** empirical heft = 经验实证分量/经验分量。

**表达方式：** “empirical heft” signals methodological weight， jargon-ish but effective。

**为什么这样写：** emphasize comparative design enables causal-ish inference on selection vs environment。

### 第 5 段：思想稳定+自选自由主义

> **原句 20:** Mr Xi would surely be disappointed by the results.

**中文理解：** 习近平肯定会因研究结果而失望。

**句子结构：** 主语 Mr Xi；谓语 would surely be disappointed；by the results。

**关键词：** would surely be disappointed = 肯定会 disappointed。

**表达方式：** short, punchy judgment; sets up disappointment narrative。

**为什么这样写：**  implies Chinese leadership hoped exchanges mold loyalty; results undercut that。

> **原句 21:** Ms Fan found that students’ ideological views were remarkably stable throughout their time in America—there was neither a backlash against Western liberalism nor a newfound zeal for it.

**中文理解：** 范女士发现，学生们的意识形态观点在他们留美期间相当稳定——既没有出现针对西方自由主义的 backlash，也没有产生新的 enthusiasm。

**句子结构：** 主语 Ms Fan；谓语 found that...（宾语从句 students’ ideological views were remarkably stable throughout their time in America）；破折号解释 there was neither X nor Y。

**关键词：** ideological views = 意识形态观点；remarkably stable = 相当稳定；backlash against Western liberalism = 对西方自由主义的 backlash；newfound zeal = 新生的 enthusiasm/狂热。

**表达方式：** “neither...nor...” 平衡 negation; “remarkably stable” decisive null-treatment finding。

**为什么这样写：** core null finding: environment didn’t move ideology; supports selection not treatment。

> **原句 22:** Yet students (and presumably their families) who decide to study in America in the first place are much more liberal than their homebody peers (see chart 1).

**中文理解：** 然而，当初决定去美国学习的学生（以及大概他们的家庭）本来就比留在国内的同龄人自由主义得多（见图 1）。

**句子结构：** 转折 Yet；主语 students (and presumably their families) who decide to study in America in the first place；谓语 are much more liberal than；宾语 their homebody peers；附注 (see chart 1)。

**关键词：** in the first place = 从一开始就；homebody peers = stay-at-home peers/留家同龄人；much more liberal = 更加自由主义。

**表达方式：** “in the first place” and “homebody peers” compactly convey selection effect。

**为什么这样写：** shift from “no change abroad” to “selected liberals to begin with”; key policy implication (can’t manufacture via exposure)。

> **原句 23:** They are more likely to support free markets, are less socially conservative and are less nationalistic.

**中文理解：** 他们更可能支持自由市场，社会上更不保守，也更少民族主义。

**句子结构：** 主语 They；并列谓语 are more likely to support free markets, are less socially conservative and are less nationalistic。

**关键词：** free markets = 自由市场；socially conservative = 社会保守；nationalistic = 民族主义的。

**表达方式：** three parallel adjectives compactly enumerate liberal dimensions。

**为什么这样写：** specify how “more liberal” manifests; gives concrete ideological profile。

### 第 6 段：性别偏见解释

> **原句 24:** One possible reason is that Chinese women tend to outnumber men in studying in the West.

**中文理解：** 一个可能的原因是，中国女性在西行求学中往往 outnumber 男性。

**句子结构：** 主语 One possible reason；系动词 is that；宾语从句 Chinese women tend to outnumber men in studying in the West。

**关键词：** tend to outnumber = 往往 outnumber（数量超过）；studying in the West = 在西方留学。

**表达方式：** “One possible reason” hedge， avoids overclaiming。

**为什么这样写：** propose demographic mechanism for selection (gender skew)。

> **原句 25:** Fran Martin of the University of Melbourne finds that some young women go overseas because of gender bias in China’s society and job market, and so are less traditional in their outlook.

**中文理解：** 墨尔本大学的弗兰·马丁发现，有些年轻女性因中国社会与就业市场的性别偏见而出国，因此在 outlook 上更少 traditional。

**句子结构：** 主语 Fran Martin of the University of Melbourne；谓语 finds that...；宾语从句 some young women go overseas because of...，and so are less traditional in their outlook。

**关键词：** gender bias = 性别偏见；less traditional in their outlook = outlook 更少传统。

**表达方式：** 因果链 “because of... and so are...” clean explanatory micro-logic。

**为什么这样写：** provides plausible selection mechanism via gender/ opportunity; adds nuance to liberal profile。

### 第 7 段：治国评价下降

> **原句 26:** Among Chinese students in America, ideological views did not change much while abroad, says Jennifer Pan of Stanford, a co-author of Ms Fan’s study.

**中文理解：** 斯坦福大学珍妮弗·潘——范女士研究的合作者——表示，留美中国学生在海外期间的意识形态观点并没有发生太多变化。

**句子结构：** 介词短语 Among Chinese students in America；主语 ideological views；谓语 did not change much while abroad；Reporting verb says Jennifer Pan... 后置。

**关键词：** did not change much while abroad = 海外期间变化不大。

**表达方式：** clause reiterates null finding via different co-author’s voice; “says ...” attributes independent confirmation。

**为什么这样写：** reinforce stability finding by second researcher; build credibility。

> **原句 27:** But they did update their views of how well the Communist Party is running China.

**中文理解：** 但他们确实更新了对共产党治理中国成效的看法。

**句子结构：** 转折 But；主语 they；谓语 did update；宾语 their views of how well the Communist Party is running China。

**关键词：** update their views of how well... = 更新对……治理成效的看法；Communist Party = 共产党。

**表达方式：** concession “did update” marks contrast with stable ideology; “how well ... is running” indirect question。

**为什么这样写：** introduce changeable dimension (evaluation of regime performance) distinct from ideology。

> **原句 28:** Support for it started lower among those studying in America, and fell sharply during China’s draconian “zero-covid” lockdowns of 2020-22 (see chart 2).

**中文理解：** 对它的支持在留美学生中一开始更低，并在 2020–22 年中国 draconian 的“零新冠”封锁期间急剧下降（见图 2）。

**句子结构：** 主语 Support for it；谓语 started lower among...；并列 and fell sharply during...；附注 (see chart 2)。

**关键词：** draconian = draconian/严厉的；“zero-covid” lockdowns = 零新冠封锁。

**表达方式：** time-series “started lower... fell sharply...” suggests relative drop from lower baseline。

**为什么这样写：** link regime support decline to salient zero-covid policy episode; provides timing.

> **原句 29:** Approval for the regime, and general patriotic feeling, fell among undergraduates in China also, but more slowly.

**中文理解：** 对政权的认可及总体爱国 sentiment，也在中国本科生中下降，但下降更慢。

**句子结构：** 并列主语 Approval for the regime, and general patriotic feeling；谓语 fell among undergraduates in China also；状语 but more slowly。

**关键词：** patriotic feeling = 爱国 sentiment/感情。

**表达方式：** “also, but more slowly” parallel decline comparison; “more slowly” carefully avoids “no drop at home”。

**为什么这样写：** establish shared trend with differential magnitude; avoid narrative of only-abroad dissent。

> **原句 30:** Being educated abroad, however, can highlight the West’s shortcomings.

**中文理解：** 然而，在海外接受教育也会让西方的 shortcomings 更加凸显。

**句子结构：** 主语 Being educated abroad；副词 however；谓语 can highlight；宾语 the West’s shortcomings。

**关键词：** Being educated abroad = 在海外接受教育；highlight the West’s shortcomings = 凸显西方的 shortcomings。

**表达方式：** “Being educated abroad, however, can highlight...” sets up counterpoint to earlier (overseas = more liberal) assumption。

**为什么这样写：** counterpoint: exposure can cut both ways; leads to Martin finding about backwater feeling。

> **原句 31:** Ms Martin’s research shows that as life in China has become technologically advanced, offering many conveniences, some young Chinese studying in the West feel they are living “in a bit of a backwater”.

**中文理解：** 马丁女士的研究表明，随着中国生活在 technological 方面变得更 advanced、提供更多便利，一些在西方留学的中国年轻人会觉得自己 living “in a bit of a backwater”。

**句子结构：** 主语 Ms Martin’s research；谓语 shows that...（宾语从句 as life in China has become technologically advanced, offering many conveniences, some young Chinese studying in the West feel they are living “in a bit of a backwater”）。

**关键词：** technologically advanced = 技术 advanced；offering many conveniences = 提供很多便利；“in a bit of a backwater” = 在偏僻/落后之地 (living)。

**表达方式：** causal chain “as life... has become..., offering..., some ... feel...”； quoted “backwater” gives colloquial texture。

**为什么这样写：** explain relative deprivation reversal; not all view West as superior; supports “more choices” conclusion。

### 第 8 段：回流与再迁移

> **原句 32:** Although most of the cohort followed by Ms Martin moved back to China after graduation, some have since returned to Australia after experiencing the competitive pressures of China’s work culture and high youth unemployment.

**中文理解：** 虽然马丁女士追踪的 cohort 大多数毕业后搬回中国，但部分人因体验过中国职场的 competitive pressures 与青年失业，随后又回到澳洲。

**句子结构：** 让步 Although most of the cohort... moved back to China after graduation；主句 some have since returned to Australia after experiencing...。

**关键词：** cohort = cohort/一代 (受试群体)；competitive pressures of China’s work culture = 中国职场 competitive pressures；high youth unemployment = 高 youth unemployment。

**表达方式：** “Although most... some have since returned” outcome mix; no simple return-home determinism。

**为什么这样写：** nuance migrants’ circular mobility; not unidirectional return。

> **原句 33:** In the American study, the share of students planning to move home after graduation dropped from 40% in 2020 to just over 10% by 2023.

**中文理解：** 在美国的研究中，毕业后计划回国的学生比例从 2020 年的 40% 降至 2023 年的刚过 10%。

**句子结构：** 介词短语 In the American study；主语 the share of students planning to move home after graduation；谓语 dropped from 40% in 2020 to just over 10% by 2023。

**关键词：** planning to move home = 计划回国；dropped from 40% to just over 10% = 从 40% 跌至刚过 10%。

**表达方式：** precise trend stat “from 40% in 2020 to just over 10% by 2023” anchors change trajectory。

**为什么这样写：** quantify rising reluctance to return; shows shifting “more choices” over time。

> **原句 34:** As for Americans studying in China, vanishingly few stay.

**中文理解：** 至于在中国学习的美国人，则几乎无一留居。

**句子结构：** 介词短语 As for Americans studying in China；主句 vanishingly few stay（few 主语）。

**关键词：** vanishingly few = 极少/微乎其微。

**表达方式：** “vanishingly few” hyperbolic succinctness; mirrors asymmetry of study-abroad outcomes。

**为什么这样写：** contrast with Chinese students abroad (many move back then re-migrate) to show lack of symmetric mutual influence。

> **原句 35:** Study abroad may not reshape either country’s politics, or even the students themselves, but it does give some Chinese more choices. ■

**中文理解：** 留学未必会重塑任何一个国家的政治，甚至未必会改变学生自己，但它确实给了部分中国人更多的选择。■

**句子结构：** 主语 Study abroad；谓语 may not reshape either country’s politics, or even the students themselves；转折 but it does give some Chinese more choices；结尾 ■。

**关键词：** may not reshape = 未必重塑；either country’s politics = 任何一方政治；more choices = 更多选择。

**表达方式：** double negation “may not reshape...but it does give...” sets bounded, realistic conclusion; ■ marks article end。

**为什么这样写：** 总结 thesis with modest claim (choices not transformation); echoes opening “mould image” irony。

## 长难句专项

1. **原句 1**：America should expect that educating China’s young people will give it “moral, intellectual and commercial influence”, wrote Edmund James, a university president, in 1906.
   - **结构**：主句宾语从句 + 倒装 attribution（wrote X, apposition, time）。
   - **难点**：引语 attribution 后置于句末；理解倒装。
   - **解剖**：[America should expect that [educating China’s young people will give it “moral, intellectual and commercial influence”]], [wrote Edmund James, a university president, in 1906].
   - **译法**：1906 年大学校长埃德蒙·詹姆斯写道，美国理应期待教育中国年轻人会带来道德、智识与商业影响。

2. **原句 16**：Nearly 400 of them were in America and the rest—a control group—at public universities in China.
   - **结构**：并列谓语 + 破折号 apposition + 介词短语。
   - **难点**：破折号插入控制组身份，主谓仍 “the rest ... at public universities”。
   - **解剖**：[Nearly 400 of them were in America] and [the rest—a control group—at public universities in China].
   - **译法**：其中近 400 人在美国，其余——作为对照组——在中国的公立大学。

3. **原句 22**：Yet students (and presumably their families) who decide to study in America in the first place are much more liberal than their homebody peers (see chart 1).
   - **结构**：转折 + 主语含括号 apposition + 定语从句 + 比较 + 附注。
   - **难点**：括号内 “presumably their families” 补充；“in the first place” 强调选择早于体验。
   - **解剖**：Yet [students (and presumably their families) who decide to study in America in the first place] are [much more liberal than [their homebody peers]] (see chart 1).
   - **译法**：然而，从一开始决定留美的学生（大概还有他们的家庭）本来就比留在国内的同龄人更自由主义。

4. **原句 28**：Support for it started lower among those studying in America, and fell sharply during China’s draconian “zero-covid” lockdowns of 2020-22 (see chart 2).
   - **结构**：并列谓语 + 介词短语时间 + 附注。
   - **难点**：Support for it 主语，started/fell 双动词；“it” 指 Communist Party performance，需追踪。
   - **解剖**：[Support for it [started lower among those studying in America], and [fell sharply during China’s draconian “zero-covid” lockdowns of 2020-22]] (see chart 2).
   - **译法**：对该党的支持在留美学生中一开始就更低，并在 2020–22 年严厉的零新冠封锁期间急剧下降。

5. **原句 32**：Although most of the cohort followed by Ms Martin moved back to China after graduation, some have since returned to Australia after experiencing the competitive pressures of China’s work culture and high youth unemployment.
   - **结构**：让步状语从句 + 主句 with “after experiencing...”。
   - **难点**：cohort 长宾语；“have since returned” 时间 sequence；experience 做动因。
   - **解剖**：[Although [most of [the cohort followed by Ms Martin] moved back to China after graduation]], [some have since returned to Australia after [experiencing the competitive pressures of China’s work culture and high youth unemployment]].
   - **译法**：尽管马丁 tracked 的大部分 cohort 毕业后回国，但部分人因体验过中国职场竞争压力与青年失业，随后又回到澳洲。

## 精读结束总结

1. **本文核心表达**：留学并不会“重塑”政治：学生思想在海外期间稳定；但出国求 scholars 本身就是更自由主义的人群，差异来自 seleciton（含性别偏见）；真正随时间变化的是对 governing 党与爱国 sentiment 的评价，受 zero-covid 等冲击；留学最终给部分中国人更多选择，而非改造中国/美国。
2. **重要语法**：
   - 倒装 attribution：..., wrote Edmund James...
   - 让步：Although most... , some...
   - 并列谓语：started lower... and fell sharply...
   - 因果链：as life... has become..., offering..., some... feel...
   - 双重否定式结论：may not reshape... but it does give...
3. **写作技巧**：
   - 历史回溯开头（1906 missionary liberalism）与现实反转形成 tension。
   - 用研究数据 (40%→10%) 与 chart 引用增强说服力。
   - 区分“ideology stable”与“regime evaluation mutable”两条 thread，避免 overclaim。
   - 妙用 “homebody peers”/“backwater”  colloquial 相比，增强 readability。
   - 谨慎 hedge（“may not reshape... but it does give more choices”）防止 overreach。
