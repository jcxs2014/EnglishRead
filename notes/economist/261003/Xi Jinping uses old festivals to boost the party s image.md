# Xi Jinping uses old festivals to boost the party’s image（精读分析）

## 概览

- **来源/栏目**：The Economist，2026 年 9 月 28 日，中国栏目，By The Economist。
- **副题**：“But ancient traditions may not always bend to his will”（然而古老传统未必总是如他所愿般驯服）。

- 第 2 段：中秋当下的消费图景——张州、双鱼岛、本地民俗消费
- 第 3 段：历史回溯——从共和历法更替到共产党的“封建迷信” labels

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：Glamping 与政治——节日 politicization 的引子

> **原句 1:** Glamping is not the most political of acts.

**中文理解：** 露营并不是最政治化的行动。

**句子结构：** `[Glamping] is [not the most political of acts].`——主系表 + 否定。

**关键词：**
- `Glamping` ⭐⭐⭐ — 豪华露营。
- `the most political of acts` ⭐⭐⭐ — 最政治化的行动。

**表达方式：** 不把 glamping 预设为政治，反而降低预期，后句反转。

**为什么这样写：** 先降门槛，让“中秋 camping 可以 political”显得 unexpected；soft opening。

> **原句 2:** It takes little courage to drive to a designer campsite, where white airy tents are already pitched and long wooden tables laden with food.

**中文理解：** 驱车前往一座已有白色 airy 帐篷支好、长桌摆满食物的 designer 营地，几乎不需要勇气。

**句子结构：** `It takes little courage to drive to [a designer campsite, where white airy tents are already pitched and long wooden tables laden with food].`——形式主语 + 不定式 + where 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `takes little courage` ⭐⭐⭐ — 几乎不需要勇气。
- `designer campsite` ⭐⭐⭐ — designer 营地。
- `white airy tents` ⭐⭐⭐ — 白色 airy 帐篷。
- `pitched` ⭐⭐⭐ — 搭起。
- `laden with food` ⭐⭐⭐ — 摆满食物。

**表达方式：** `where ... already pitched and ... laden with food`——where 引导定语从句，把环境细节堆叠。

**为什么这样写：** 用舒适细节勾勒“现代 glamping”，与后面 political 反差。

> **原句 3:** Yet choosing to go camping at this time of year in China, when the autumn moon is full, can be political—albeit rather enjoyably so.

**中文理解：** 然而在秋月满盈时节在中国露营，仍可能成为政治举动——尽管颇有趣味。

**句子结构：** `Yet [choosing to go camping at this time of year in China, when the autumn moon is full], can be political—albeit rather enjoyably so.`——主语动名词短语 + 定语从句 + 冒号破折号让步。

**关键词：**
- `at this time of year` ⭐⭐⭐ — 在今年这个时候。
- `when the autumn moon is full` ⭐⭐⭐ — 秋月满盈时。
- `albeit rather enjoyably so` ⭐⭐⭐ — 尽管相当Enjoyably。

**表达方式：** `when the autumn moon is full` 定时；em dash + albeit 引入 qualifying tone。

**为什么这样写：** 把“中秋”与“政治化”连结，点明 文章thesis。

> **原句 4:** It is evidence that traditional festivals, once forsaken by the government and neglected by many, have been re-established, with modern glosses, as fixtures of the Chinese year.

**中文理解：** 它证明，曾在政府层面被遗弃、在民众中被忽视的传统节日，已重新确立、并加上现代 glosses，成为中式年历中的 fixtures。

**句子结构：** `It is [evidence that traditional festivals, once forsaken by the government and neglected by many, have been re-established, with modern glosses, as fixtures of the Chinese year].`——It be evidence that...。

**关键词：**
- `once forsaken by the government` ⭐⭐⭐ — 曾被政府遗弃。
- `neglected by many` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被许多人忽视。
- `re-established` ⭐⭐⭐ — 重新确立。
- `modern glosses` ⭐⭐⭐ — 现代 gloss / 包装。
- `fixtures of the Chinese year` ⭐⭐⭐ — 中国年的 fixtures。

**表达方式：** `once forsaken ..., neglected ... have been re-established, with modern glosses, as fixtures`——“被遗弃→复兴加现代包装” 强调political adaptation。

**为什么这样写：** 作为全篇 thesis，先说明 festival revival 的 dual nature: recovered + modern reframed.

**段落逻辑：** 无害 glamping（M1-2）→ 其可 political（M3）→ 引出 festival revival with modern glosses（M4）。

### 第 2 段：中秋的消费图景——张州与双鱼岛

> **原句 5:** This year the mid-autumn festival fell on September 25th, making for a three-day weekend.

**中文理解：** 今年中秋落于 9 月 25 日，构成了一个三天的长假。

**句子结构：** `This year [the mid-autumn festival] fell on September 25th, making for [a three-day weekend].`——主谓宾 + making for + 宾。

**关键词：**
- `mid-autumn festival` ⭐⭐⭐ — 中秋节。
- `fell on September 25th` ⭐⭐⭐ — 落于 9 月 25 日。
- `making for a three-day weekend` ⭐⭐⭐ — 构成三天长假。

**表达方式：** `making for a three-day weekend`—— of休 holiday economy。

**为什么这样写：** 用 calendar fact grounding local scene.

> **原句 6:** It became a public holiday only in 2008.

**中文理解：** 它仅自 2008 年起成为公假。

**句子结构：** `[It] became [a public holiday] only in 2008.`——简单句。

**关键词：**
- `public holiday` ⭐⭐⭐ — 公假。
- `only in 2008` ⭐⭐⭐ — 仅自 2008。

**表达方式：** `only in 2008`——强调 revival 的 recency。

**为什么这样写：** 反衬 party’s late but decisive move.

> **原句 7:** Not until recently did people find more ways to spend it than munching on mooncakes (dry pastries loved by some, loathed by others).

**中文理解：** 直到最近，人们才找到比 merely 啃月饼更多的方式来过节（月饼是爱之或厌之的干点心）。

**句子结构：** `Not until recently did [people] find [more ways to spend it] [than munching on mooncakes (dry pastries loved by some, loathed by others)].`——强调句。

**关键词：**
- `Not until recently` ⭐⭐⭐ — 直到最近才。

**表达方式：** `Not until recently did people find ...` + parenthetical characterization。

**为什么这样写：** the festival’s consumption expansion beyond food.

> **原句 8:** Chaguan visited Zhangzhou, a city in Fujian province, in the lead-up.

**中文理解：** The Economist 的 Chaguan 专栏在节前走访了福建省漳州市。

**句子结构：** `[Chaguan] visited [Zhangzhou, a city in Fujian province], [in the lead-up].`——主谓宾 + 时间。

**关键词：**
- `Chaguan` ⭐⭐⭐ — 《茶馆》专栏（The Economist 中国专栏）。

- `in the lead-up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 节前。

**表达方式：** `a city in Fujian province` appositive；`in the lead-up` 定时。

**为什么这样写：** 地点 becomes case study of southern festival revival.

> **原句 9:** The old town was festooned with moon-themed decorations.

**中文理解：** 老城被 festooned with 月亮主题装饰。

**句子结构：** `[The old town] was festooned with [moon-themed decorations].`——被动句。

**关键词：**
- `festooned with` ⭐⭐⭐ — festooned with（挂满）。
- `moon-themed decorations` ⭐⭐⭐ — 月亮主题装饰。

**表达方式：** `festooned with moon-themed decorations`——visual vividness。

**为什么这样写：** contrast party's abstract national rhetoric with local aesthetic.

> **原句 10:** One hour away, Double Fish Island, on the edge of the Taiwan Strait, was offering al fresco dinner for two for 298 yuan ($44) at its luxury campsite—all the better to admire the moon, the festival’s focal point.

**中文理解：** 一小时外，位于台湾海峡边缘的双鱼岛在 luxury campsite 推出双人 al fresco dinner， priced 298 元（44 美元）—— 更好地 admiring the moon 这一 festival focal point。

**句子结构：** `One hour away, [Double Fish Island, on the edge of the Taiwan Strait], was offering [al fresco dinner for two for 298 yuan ($44)] at its luxury campsite—all the better to admire the moon, the festival’s focal point.`——同位 + 介宾链。

**关键词：**
- `Double Fish Island` ⭐⭐⭐ — 双鱼岛。
- `Taiwan Strait` ⭐⭐⭐ — 台湾海峡。
- `al fresco dinner for two` ⭐⭐⭐ — 户外双人晚餐。
- `298 yuan ($44)` ⭐⭐⭐ — 298 元（44 美元）。
- `luxury campsite` ⭐⭐⭐ — luxury campsite。
- `all the better to admire the moon` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更好地 admiring the moon。
- `the festival’s focal point` ⭐⭐⭐ — 节日 focal point。

**表达方式：** `all the better to admire...` 因果目的；`the festival’s focal point` 定义月亮 symbol。

**为什么这样写：** 显示“傳統 holidays become local tourism product”， complicating purely political framing.

**段落逻辑：** 中秋 holiday fact（M5-7）→ Chaguan 地点（M8）→ local decoration（M9）→ double-fish tourism (M10)。

### 第 3 段：历史回溯——现代性 vs 传统历法

> **原句 11:** In the early 20th century reformers railed against the lunar calendar and its festivals, seeing them as antithetical to modernity.

**中文理解：** 20 世纪初，改革者 rail against lunar calendar 及其 festival，视其为 antithetical to modernity。

**句子结构：** `[In the early 20th century reformers] railed against [the lunar calendar and its festivals], seeing [them as antithetical to modernity].`——主谓宾 + seeing 伴随。

**关键词：**
- `railed against` ⭐⭐⭐ — rail against（ railing）。
- `lunar calendar` ⭐⭐⭐ — 农历。
- `antithetical to modernity` ⭐⭐⭐ — 与现代性 opposed。

**表达方式：** `seeing them as antithetical to modernity` 伴随结构解释 motive。

**为什么这样写：** 建立 Modernity assault as historical origin of erasure.

> **原句 12:** In 1912, after the fall of the Qing dynasty, China’s republican government adopted the Gregorian calendar.

**中文理解：** 1912 年，清廷倒台后，中华民国政府 adopted Gregorian calendar。

**句子结构：** `[In 1912, after the fall of the Qing dynasty], [China’s republican government] adopted [the Gregorian calendar].`——时间状语 + 主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `fall of the Qing dynasty` ⭐⭐⭐ — 清廷倒台。
- `republican government` ⭐⭐⭐ — 民国政府。
- `Gregorian calendar` ⭐⭐⭐ — 公历。

**表达方式：** after + adopt: sequence of state modernization.

**为什么这样写：** 国家层 reset time reckoning; creates contrast with today’s party using same festivals.

> **原句 13:** The Communists went further, branding many festivals as feudal superstitions.

**中文理解：** 共产党更进一步， labeling many festivals as feudal superstitions。

**句子结构：** `[The Communists] went further, branding [many festivals] [as feudal superstitions].`——主谓宾 + 现在分词。

**关键词：**
- `went further` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更进一步。
- `branding` ⭐⭐⭐ — 标签/ labeling。
- `feudal superstitions` ⭐⭐⭐ — 封建迷信。

**表达方式：** `went further, branding` 表示 escalation；党在历法 modernization 之上 ideological condemnation.

**为什么这样写：** 建立 CP as sworn foe; setup later audacious about-face.

**段落逻辑：** reformer rebellion（M11）→ republican calendar（M12）→ CP label superstition（M13）。

### 第 5 段：习近平的加大政治投入

**句子结构：** `[State media] seize on [holidays] to amplify [an old idea much liked by Mr Xi: that nation and family are interwoven].`——主谓宾 + to amplify + colon appositive。

## 长难句专项

### 长难句 1（原句 4）

**原句：** It is evidence that traditional festivals, once forsaken by the government and neglected by many, have been re-established, with modern glosses, as fixtures of the Chinese year.

**找主干：** It is evidence that... festivals have been re-established as fixtures。

**找修饰：**
- once forsaken by the government and neglected by many — 过去分词短语
- with modern glosses — 介词短语
- as fixtures of the Chinese year — 结果/身份短语

**找从句：** that festivals ... have been re-established 作 evidence 的同位从句。

**恢复逻辑：** festivals 曾被 forsaken/neglected → now re-established with glosses → fixtures of year。

**整体理解：** 一长句把“遗弃—复兴—现代化” compressed。

### 长难句 2（原句 10）

**原句：** One hour away, Double Fish Island, on the edge of the Taiwan Strait, was offering al fresco dinner for two for 298 yuan ($44) at its luxury campsite—all the better to admire the moon, the festival’s focal point.

**找主干：** Double Fish Island (appositive) was offering al fresco dinner ... at campsite。

**找修饰：**
- One hour away — 固定状语
- on the edge of Taiwan Strait — 同位
- for two / for 298 yuan ($44) — 介词短语
- all the better to admire the moon — 目的
- the festival’s focal point — 同位

**找从句：** 无形式从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 地点 details + 价格 + purpose（admire moon focal point）。

**整体理解：** rich noun phrase + dash purpose，显示旅游 commodification。

### 长难句 3（原句 17）

**原句：** It was not until 2005, however, that the party issued a document calling for the revival of other holidays—especially the mid-autumn celebration, tomb-sweeping day in the spring and the dragon-boat festival in late spring or early summer.

**找主干：** It was not until 2005 that the party issued a document。

**找修饰：**
- however — 状语
- calling for the revival of other holidays — 分词短语
- especially ... — 例证列表

**找从句：** that 引导强调句宾语从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 2005 才 issued document，calls revival of other holidays（中秋/清明/端午）。

**整体理解：** 强调句 + 破折号 enumeration; 精准 bureaucratic detail。

### 长难句 4（原句 27）

**原句：** “The younger generation, people born after 1990, are mostly away for their studies or work. It’s only at festivals like this that they come back,” said Ms Chen, standing near a shrine to Confucius in central Zhangzhou.

**找主干：** said Ms Chen (倒装)。

**找修饰：**
- The younger generation, people born after 1990 — appositive
- away for their studies or work — 状语
- It’s only at festivals like this that they come back — 倒装/强调
- standing near a shrine to Confucius in central Zhangzhou — 伴随

**找从句：** that they come back 强调从句；宾语从句 inside said。

**恢复逻辑：** quote contains two sentences then attribution + setting.

**整体理解：** inverted reported speech + setting; living testimony。

### 长难句 5（原句 32）

**原句：** Zhangzhou hosts performances of folk characters on stilts; in Xi’an actors walk through the streets challenging visitors to poetry duels about the moon; the water town of Wuzhen sets up altars where people can leave food for Chang’e, the moon goddess (whose companion, the Jade Rabbit, can be spotted on the lunar landscape if you squint just right).

**找主干：** three parallel clauses semicolon-joined.

**找修饰：**
- on stilts — 介词短语
- challenging visitors to poetry duels about the moon — 分词短语
- where people can leave food for Chang’e — 定语从句
- the moon goddess — 同位
- (whose companion ... if you squint just right) — parenthetical

**找从句：** where + whose + if。

**恢复逻辑：** 三地 customs list，末尾 mythological footnote + squint joke.

**整体理解：** 物理 parallelism 与 parenthetical humor; Economist voice.

## 精读结束总结

### 1. 本文核心表达
- 中秋等 festival revival = 政治 project：CP 将自己定位为 Chineseness guarantor。
- 宣传利用“同一个月亮” symbol 融合 ethnic unity/Taiwan Strait/humanity and nation=family。
- Revival 有自下而上 dimension：festival as rhythm of life, family reunion anchor against anomie.
- Tradition deepening via regional rituals, but tourism motivates.
- State control over temples/teachings/flags vs local lived practice.
- Minority & Uyghur tension; Han-centric calendar risks alienation.
- Confucian mentors carry moral authority; loyalty conditional on benevolence.
- Party’s guardianship summons deeper forces with greater loyalty claims.

### 2. 重要语法
- `once forsaken by the government and neglected by many, have been re-established, with modern glosses, as fixtures...`
- `Not until recently did people find...`
- `In one of its more audacious about-faces, ... morphed from ... to ...`
- `It was not until 2005, however, that ...`
- `The more that..., the more that ...`
- `whose roots run much deeper than its own, and which may ...`

### 3. 写作技巧
- Sports-ish/reportage lead (glamping) then political pivot.
- Historical narrative: from anti-festival modernization to CP arbiter.
- Data/local detail (298 yuan, trains sold out) grounds abstraction.
- Third-person observation plus quoted lives (Ms Chen, Confucian mentor) create texture.
- Parenthetical wry aside (Jade Rabbit if you squint) adds Economist voice.
- Paradoxical conclusion: guardian summons deeper forces.
