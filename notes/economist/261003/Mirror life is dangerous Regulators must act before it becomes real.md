# Mirror life is dangerous. Regulators must act before it becomes real（精读分析）

## 概览

- **来源/栏目/副题**：The Economist（leaders/社论）；副题：Playing catch-up with the technology is not an option；日期：2026-10-01（URL 2026/10/01）；作者：By The Economist。
- **主题一段话**：文章警告，合成生物学与人工智能推进“镜像生命”（镜像细胞/镜像细菌）从理论走向现实，这种与自然生物手性相反的人造生命可能逃避免疫系统、不受自然捕食者/病毒控制，从而成为不可召回的生态灾难；因此各国必须在技术成形前就达成资金停供、研究禁令与监测体系，而不能等到事后补救。
- **结构列表**：
  1. 合成生物与 AI 快速推进，可能从零造细胞。
  2. 镜像生命是什么：手性反转，潜在认知/药物价值。
  3. 威胁远超收益：免疫盲区、病原性、生态失控与不可召回。
  4. 还有时间，但必须现在行动；Asilomar 类比不够。
  5. 三步 controls：资助/研究禁令、mavericks 监测、biosurveillance。
  6. 主权/恐怖组织/公司/犯罪都不会有意释放（不可控）。
  7. 但非理性行动者存在；措施齐备则可防。结语：无法预知合成细胞走向；若连利益一致时的威胁都应对不了，未来未知威胁更无从 tackling。

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：合成生物学与 AI

> **原句 1:** SYNTHETIC BIOLOGY is moving fast and artificial intelligence is turbocharging it.

**中文理解：** 合成生物学正在快速推进，而人工智能正在为它“增压加速”。

**句子结构：** 并列句：SYNTHETIC BIOLOGY is moving fast and artificial intelligence is turbocharging it；两个分句共享主语 implicit “it”。

**关键词：** synthetic biology = 合成生物学；turbocharging = 增压加速/ turbo 充能（比喻）。

**表达方式：** “turbocharging” 用汽车涡轮增压的比喻，强调 AI 给合成生物带来的加速效应，而非温和提升。

**为什么这样写：** 开篇直接 assert 技术加速，建立 “time pressure” 叙事基调，为后文 “must act now” 铺垫。

> **原句 2:** Soon scientists may be able to create whole cells from scratch.

**中文理解：** 不久，科学家可能就能从零开始创造整个细胞。

**句子结构：** 状语 Soon；主语 scientists；情态 may be able to create；宾语 whole cells from scratch。

**关键词：** whole cells from scratch = 从零开始的完整细胞。

**表达方式：** “from scratch” 强调 de novo（全新创造），而非修改现有生物。

**为什么这样写：** 明确 capability milestone，引出 unnatural biochemistry 的可能性，标示风险前沿。

> **原句 3:** This could allow the AI-assisted exploration of completely unnatural biochemistries.

**中文理解：** 这将允许借助 AI 探索完全非自然的生物化学。

**句子结构：** 主语 This；谓语 could allow；宾语 the AI-assisted exploration of completely unnatural biochemistries。

**关键词：** AI-assisted exploration = AI 辅助探索；completely unnatural biochemistries = 完全非自然/前所未有的生物化学。

**表达方式：** “completely unnatural” 强调 unprecedented，为 “mirror life” 提供语境。

**为什么这样写：** 将 capability 与“探索”绑定，引出下一段 “one such is mirror life”。

### 第 2 段：镜像生命的概念

> **原句 4:** One such is mirror life.

**中文理解：** 其中一种就是镜像生命。

**句子结构：** 主语 One such；系动词 is；表语 mirror life。

**关键词：** one such = 这样的一个/其中一种；mirror life = 镜像生命。

**表达方式：** 简句，聚焦 new concept。

**为什么这样写：** 从 unnatural biochemistries 具体落脚到 “mirror life”，启动定义段。

> **原句 5:** Many organic molecules come in two asymmetrical forms, like your hands.

**中文理解：** 许多有机分子存在两种不对称形式，就像你的双手。

**句子结构：** 主语 Many organic molecules；谓语 come in；宾语 two asymmetrical forms；比较 like your hands。

**关键词：** asymmetrical forms = 不对称形式（手性/对映体）；like your hands = 像左右手（不可重叠）。

**表达方式：** 用手做类比，让手性概念直观可感。

**为什么这样写：** 以 everyday image 解释 chirality，降低 reader 理解门槛。

> **原句 6:** Amino acids and sugars can in theory take on both a left- and right-handed form which cannot be superimposed.

**中文理解：** 氨基酸和糖在理论上可以采取左旋与右旋两种形式，且这两种形式不能重叠。

**句子结构：** 主语 Amino acids and sugars；谓语 can in theory take on；宾语 both a left- and right-handed form；定语从句 which cannot be superimposed。

**关键词：** left- and right-handed form = 左/右手性形式（对映体）；superimposed = 重叠/叠合。

**表达方式：** “left- and right-handed” 缩写并列，简洁；“cannot be superimposed” 精准定义非全同。

**为什么这样写：** 给出 biochemical basis，清楚说明 hand analogy 的分子含义。

> **原句 7:** In nature, organisms generally contain only one of those forms for each molecule.

**中文理解：** 在自然界，生物体通常每种分子只含其中一种形式。

**句子结构：** 介词短语 In nature；主语 organisms；副词 generally contain；宾语 only one of those forms for each molecule。

**关键词：** generally contain only one = 通常只含一种。

**表达方式：** 平句，强调 natural homochirality。

**为什么这样写：** 对比“镜像”可能性，强调 mirror life 的 unnatural 性。

> **原句 8:** However, an artificial cell could have its molecules’ handedness reversed.

**中文理解：** 然而，一个人工细胞的分子手性可以被反转。

**句子结构：** 转折 However；主语 an artificial cell；情态 could have；宾语 its molecules’ handedness reversed（过去分词作宾语补足语）。

**关键词：** handedness reversed = 手性反转；molecules’ handedness = 分子的手性。

**表达方式：** “could have ... reversed” 表 possibility，显示人为 intervention。

**为什么这样写：** 明确 mirror cell 的定义：手性 fully reversed。

> **原句 9:** That could shed light on reasons behind handedness.

**中文理解：** 这可能有助于揭示手性背后的原因。

**句子结构：** 主语 That；情态 could shed light on；宾语 reasons behind handedness。

**关键词：** shed light on = 揭示/照亮。

**表达方式：** 简句，先 concession：research value。

**为什么这样写：** 提供 “potential benefits” 的第一点（科学理解）。

> **原句 10:** Perhaps the anti-molecules may turn out to be valuable as drugs, say.

**中文理解：** 或许这些“反分子”可能最终被证明可作为有价值的药物，举例说。

**句子结构：** 副词 Perhaps；主语 the anti-molecules；情态 may turn out to be；表语 valuable as drugs；句末 “, say”（for example）。

**关键词：** anti-molecules = 反分子（镜像分子）；turn out to be = 最终被证明是。

**表达方式：** “say” 口语式注明举例，soft hedges。

**为什么这样写：** 提供潜在药物价值，完成 benefit 列举，为下段 “pale beside threats” 对比。

### 第 3 段：威胁评估

> **原句 11:** Yet any potential benefits pale beside the threats.

**中文理解：** 然而，任何潜在益处与威胁相比都相形见绌。

**句子结构：** 转折 Yet；主语 any potential benefits；谓语 pale beside；宾语 the threats。

**关键词：** pale beside = 与...相比相形见绌。

**表达方式：** “pale beside” 对比修辞，明确 outweighing。

**为什么这样写：** 核心 valuation：threats dominate benefits，启动 threat 列表。

> **原句 12:** Immune systems may not recognise mirror bacteria, in the way that a glove will not fit the wrong hand.

**中文理解：** 免疫系统可能识别不了镜像细菌，就像手套戴不上另一只手。

**句子结构：** 主语 Immune systems；情态 may not recognise；宾语 mirror bacteria；比较 in the way that a glove will not fit the wrong hand。

**关键词：** recognise = 识别；glove will not fit the wrong hand = 手套戴不上另一只手（手性 mismatch）。

**表达方式：** glove analogy 具象化免疫识别失败，strong metaphor。

**为什么这样写：** 提供 threat 的 anchor 机制：immune evasion  due to handedness mismatch。

> **原句 13:** Mirror bacteria may therefore become wildly pathogenic.

**中文理解：** 因此，镜像细菌可能变得极度致病。

**句子结构：** 主语 Mirror bacteria；情态 may therefore become；表语 wildly pathogenic。

**关键词：** wildly pathogenic = 极度致病的。

**表达方式：** “may therefore become” 因果链：immune evasion → pathogenicity。

**为什么这样写：** 直接 threat consequence，与 immune 不识别 causal link。

> **原句 14:** Because viruses and predatory amoebas that keep natural pathogens in check may be unable to control them, they could ravage ecosystems.

**中文理解：** 因为那些把自然病原体控制在 check 的病毒和捕食性变形虫可能无法控制它们，镜像细菌可能肆虐生态系统。

**句子结构：** 因果从句 Because [viruses and predatory amoebas that keep natural pathogens in check] may be unable to control them；主句 they could ravage ecosystems。

**关键词：** predatory amoebas = 捕食性变形虫；keep natural pathogens in check = 控制/遏制自然病原；ravage ecosystems = 肆虐生态系统。

**表达方式：** 长因果句，强调 ecological control failure，扩大 threat scope beyond humans。

**为什么这样写：** 说明 pathogens 无天敌/无控制，导致生态灾难，升级 severity。

> **原句 15:** If mirror life escaped from the lab, it would be impossible to recall.

**中文理解：** 如果镜像生命逃出实验室，将无法召回。

**句子结构：** 条件从句 If mirror life escaped from the lab；主句 it would be impossible to recall。

**关键词：** escaped from the lab = 逃出实验室；impossible to recall = 无法召回/收回。

**表达方式：** 条件虚拟句表达不可逆 consequence。

**为什么这样写：** 强化 irreversible risk，支持 “pre-emptive controls”。

### 第 4 段：紧迫与历史类比

> **原句 16:** Luckily, unlike many past technologies, there is still time to put controls in place.

**中文理解：** 幸运的是，与许多过去的技术不同，现在还有时间把控制措施 put in place。

**句子结构：** 状语 Luckily, unlike many past technologies；主句 there is still time to put controls in place。

**关键词：** put controls in place = 建立控制措施/管控。

**表达方式：** “Luckily” + 对比 past tech, establishes opportunity window。

**为什么这样写：** 转向 action possibility，avoid despair, encourage policy action。

> **原句 17:** Governments and scientists must act now.

**中文理解：** 各国政府与科学家现在必须采取行动。

**句子结构：** 主语 Governments and scientists；模态 must act；状语 now。

**关键词：** must act now = 必须现在行动。

**表达方式：** 简句，imperative urgency。

**为什么这样写：** 直接 call to action，无 hedging。

> **原句 18:** Some biologists are already calling for pre-emptive measures.

**中文理解：** 一些生物学家已经在呼吁采取先发制人的措施。

**句子结构：** 主语 Some biologists；谓语 are already calling for；宾语 pre-emptive measures。

**关键词：** pre-emptive measures = 先发制人的措施/先期应对。

**表达方式：** “already calling” signals emerging consensus。

**为什么这样写：** 引入 community endorsement，强化 necessity。

> **原句 19:** This has echoes of the Asilomar meeting of (mostly Western) biotechnologists 51 years ago that set voluntary safety guidelines for genetic engineering.

**中文理解：** 这让人回想起 51 年前（多数是西方的）生物技术专家召开的阿西洛马会议，该会议为基因工程设定了自愿安全准则。

**句子结构：** 主语 This；谓语 has echoes of；宾语 the Asilomar meeting of ... that set voluntary safety guidelines for genetic engineering。

**关键词：** Asilomar meeting = 阿西洛马会议（1975 年基因工程安全会议）；voluntary safety guidelines = 自愿安全准则；genetic engineering = 基因工程。

**表达方式：** 历史类比，提供 precedent。

**为什么这样写：** 借 precedent 建议 voluntary guidelines，但后面说不够。

> **原句 20:** But today’s practitioners recognise that would not be enough.

**中文理解：** 但今天的从业者认识到，仅靠自愿准则还不够。

**句子结构：** 转折 But；主语 today’s practitioners；动词 recognise；宾语从句 that would not be enough（that refers to Asilomar-style voluntary guidelines）。

**关键词：** today’s practitioners recognise = 当今从业者认识到。

**表达方式：** “would not be enough” direct negation of voluntary approach。

**为什么这样写：** 为 enforceable/ban emphasis 铺垫。

> **原句 21:** Biotech is now vastly larger, more capable, more profitable and more international than it was then.

**中文理解：** 生物技术如今比那时规模大得多、能力强得多、利润丰厚得多，也更国际化。

**句子结构：** 主语 Biotech；谓语 is now vastly larger, more capable, more profitable and more international than it was then。

**关键词：** vastly larger/more capable/more profitable/more international = 规模更大/能力更强/利润更高/更国际化。

**表达方式：** 四联比较，量化 “then vs now”，justify stricter controls。

**为什么这样写：** 解释 Asilomar insufficient rationale。

> **原句 22:** China is a force.

**中文理解：** 中国是一股力量。

**句子结构：** 主语 China；系动词 is；表语 a force。

**关键词：** a force = 一股力量/重要力量。

**表达方式：** 简句，strong emphasise China’s importance，隐含 “international coordination must include China”。

**为什么这样写：** Point out geopolitical necessity for broad participation。

### 第 5 段：控制措施

> **原句 23:** The first necessity is for all countries capable of the research to agree not to fund it, and to ban public and private work leading in this direction—and to do both today, well before synthetic cells are possible.

**中文理解：** 首要之务是，所有有能力开展此项研究的国家要同意不资助它，并禁止通向该方向的公共与私人研究——而且今天就要做，远远早于合成细胞还成为可能之前。

**句子结构：** 主语 The first necessity；系动词 is for [all countries capable of the research to agree not to fund it, and to ban public and private work leading in this direction] —and to do both today, well before synthetic cells are possible。

**关键词：** agree not to fund = 同意不资助；ban public and private work = 禁止公共与私人研究；well before synthetic cells are possible = 远早于合成细胞可能实现之前。

**表达方式：** “first necessity” 强调优先序；破折号 + and to do both today emphasise immediacy。

**为什么这样写：** 最 stringent measure first：funding/research ban upfront。

> **原句 24:** Next comes detecting and stopping mavericks who try to create mirror life furtively.

**中文理解：** 接下来是侦测并阻止那些试图偷偷创造镜像生命的“独行侠”。

**句子结构：** 主语 Next；谓语 comes；动名词 detecting and stopping mavericks；定语从句 who try to create mirror life furtively。

**关键词：** detecting and stopping mavericks = 侦测并阻止独行侠/离经叛道者；furtively = 偷偷地/秘密地。

**表达方式：** “Next comes” sequential policy, turns to enforcement。

**为什么这样写：** 从 ban 到 detection of rogue actors，complete control chain。

> **原句 25:** This requires bolstering the system of monitoring and reporting fishy orders placed with biochemicals suppliers.

**中文理解：** 这需要加强监测并报告向生化试剂供应商下的“可疑订单”的体系。

**句子结构：** 主语 This；谓语 requires；宾语 bolstering the system of monitoring and reporting fishy orders placed with biochemicals suppliers。

**关键词：** bolstering the system = 加强该体系；fishy orders = 可疑订单；biochemicals suppliers = 生化试剂供应商。

**表达方式：** “fishy orders” colloquial, ties to supply-chain screening。

**为什么这样写：** 具体监管 mechanism: supplier order monitoring。

> **原句 26:** Requests for opposite-handed molecules must be reported alongside the current screening for DNA sequences that might form parts of pathogens.

**中文理解：** 对反手性分子的请求必须与当前筛选可能构成病原体组分的 DNA 序列一起被报告。

**句子结构：** 主语 Requests for opposite-handed molecules；模态 must be reported；状语 alongside the current screening for DNA sequences that might form parts of pathogens。

**关键词：** opposite-handed molecules = 反手性分子/异手性分子；reported alongside = 与...一并报告；DNA sequences that might form parts of pathogens = 可能构成病原体组分的 DNA 序列。

**表达方式：** “alongside” 并行 screening, expands surveillance scope。

**为什么这样写：** 提供 operational detail，显示 enforceable.

> **原句 27:** Publicly available AI models should have guardrails to stop them being used for mirror biochemistry.

**中文理解：** 公开可用的 AI 模型应具备“护栏”，防止它们被用于镜像生物化学。

**句子结构：** 主语 Publicly available AI models；模态 should have；宾语 guardrails；不定式 to stop them being used for mirror biochemistry。

**关键词：** publicly available AI models = 公开可用的 AI 模型；guardrails = 护栏/安全约束；mirror biochemistry = 镜像生物化学。

**表达方式：** “guardrails” 抽象安全 controls，响应前文 AI turbocharging。

**为什么这样写：** Address AI as dual-use: prevent misuse by AI assistance。

> **原句 28:** Last, in case mirror life does ever escape containment, biosurveillance tests must be developed to pick out wrong-handed molecules in the wild and sound a warning.

**中文理解：** 最后，为防镜像生命真的逃脱收容，必须开发生物监测测试，以便在野外识别出“错误手性”分子并发出警报。

**句子结构：** 副词 Last；状语 in case mirror life does ever escape containment；主语 biosurveillance tests；模态 must be developed；不定式 to pick out wrong-handed molecules in the wild and sound a warning。

**关键词：** in case = 以防；escape containment = 脱离收容/逃逸；biosurveillance tests = 生物监测测试；pick out wrong-handed molecules in the wild = 在野外挑出错误手性分子；sound a warning = 发出警报。

**表达方式：** “last” 作为 backup layer, completes defense in depth。

**为什么这样写：** 提供 contingency surveillance, acknowledge residual risk。

### 第 6 段：动机分析

> **原句 29:** Rational state actors, or even terrorists with earthly objectives, are unlikely to create mirror life deliberately as a weapon, because once it had been released it would be uncontrollable.

**中文理解：** 理性的国家行为体，甚至有世俗目标的恐怖分子，也不太可能故意把镜像生命用作武器，因为一旦它被释放就会不可控。

**句子结构：** 主语 Rational state actors, or even terrorists with earthly objectives；谓语 are unlikely to create mirror life deliberately as a weapon；原因 because once it had been released it would be uncontrollable。

**关键词：** rational state actors = 理性的国家行为体；terrorists with earthly objectives = 有世俗目标的恐怖分子；deliberately as a weapon = 故意用作武器；uncontrollable = 不可控的。

**表达方式：** “unlikely ... because ...” cost-benefit reasoning; reduces deliberate weapon scenario。

**为什么这样写：** 排除 state/terrorist deliberate release (rationale: uncontrollability)。

> **原句 30:** Companies have even less reason to dabble in an outlawed market.

**中文理解：** 企业更没有理由去试探（涉足）一个非法市场。

**句子结构：** 主语 Companies；谓语 have even less reason to dabble in；宾语 an outlawed market。

**关键词：** dabble in = 试探/涉足；outlawed market = 非法市场/被禁市场。

**表达方式：** 简句，commercial disincentive。

**为什么这样写：** 从 intentional weapon to market actors，降低 likely misuse by profit entities。

> **原句 31:** And if some opposite-handed molecule had black-market value as a recreational drug, criminal enterprises have no interest in letting loose something that could wipe out their customers.

**中文理解：** 而且如果某种反手性分子作为娱乐性药物有黑市价值，犯罪团伙也没有兴趣释放会 wiped out 其客户的物质。

**句子结构：** 条件从句 And if some opposite-handed molecule had black-market value as a recreational drug；主句 criminal enterprises have no interest in letting loose something that could wipe out their customers。

**关键词：** black-market value = 黑市价值；recreational drug = 娱乐性药物（毒品）；criminal enterprises = 犯罪企业/团伙；letting loose = 放出/释放；wipe out their customers = 摧毁/消灭他们的客户。

**表达方式：** 黑市 drug angle: self-interest discourages release (customer wipeout)。

**为什么这样写：** 排除 criminal deliberate misuse due to self-interest, further reduce intentional risk narrative。

### 第 7 段：结语

> **原句 32:** Irrational actors exist, as the Japanese found out when Aum Shinrikyo, a doomsday cult, tried to build bioweapons.

**中文理解：** 非理性行动者确实存在，正如日本人当年在奥姆真理教（一个末日崇拜组织）试图制造生物武器时所发现的那样。

**句子结构：** 主语 Irrational actors；谓语 exist；状语 as the Japanese found out when [Aum Shinrikyo, a doomsday cult, tried to build bioweapons]。

**关键词：** irrational actors = 非理性行动者；Aum Shinrikyo = 奥姆真理教；doomsday cult = 末日崇拜组织；bioweapons = 生物武器。

**表达方式：** 历史 cautionary example，acknowledge non-rational risk。

**为什么这样写：** 反驳过度乐观：irrational actors 仍可能 pursue dangerous research。

> **原句 33:** But the more countermeasures are in place, the easier such psychopaths are to stop.

**中文理解：** 但措施越到位，这些 психopath 就越容易被阻止。

**句子结构：** The more countermeasures are in place, the easier such psychopaths are to stop（the more...the easier... 比较结构）。

**关键词：** countermeasures are in place = 应对措施到位；psychopaths = 精神病态者/ psychopaths。

**表达方式：** “the more... the easier...” 性价比，支持 multipronged controls。

**为什么这样写：** 正面结论：preparedness reduces rogue risk。

> **原句 34:** No one can know where the advent of synthetic cells will lead.

**中文理解：** 没人知道合成细胞的兴起将把我们引向何方。

**句子结构：** 主语 No one；谓语 can know；宾语从句 where the advent of synthetic cells will lead。

**关键词：** advent of = 降临/兴起；lead = 导向/导致。

**表达方式：** humility about unknown futures。

**为什么这样写：** 强调 uncertainty beyond known (mirror life) threat。

> **原句 35:** If humanity cannot deal in advance with a threat when everyone’s interests are aligned, it cannot hope to tackle the unknown unknowns of the future.■

**中文理解：** 如果人类连在所有人利益一致时的一个威胁都无法提前应对，那么它就别奢望 tackle 未来那些“未知的未知数”。■

**句子结构：** 条件从句 If humanity cannot deal in advance with a threat when everyone’s interests are aligned；主句 it cannot hope to tackle the unknown unknowns of the future。■

**关键词：** deal in advance = 提前应对/处理；when everyone’s interests are aligned = 当所有人利益一致时；unknown unknowns = 未知的未知数（Rumsfeld phrase）。

**表达方式：** 强 closing maxim，connects mirror life to governance capacity thesis；■ ending marker.

**为什么这样写：** 全篇落脚到 governance capacity，■ 结束符号。

## 长难句专项

1. **原句 12**：Immune systems may not recognise mirror bacteria, in the way that a glove will not fit the wrong hand.
   - **结构**：主句 + 比较分句 in the way that...
   - **难点**：隐喻映射 handedness mismatch to immunological recognition。
   - **解剖**：[Immune systems may not recognise mirror bacteria], [in the way that a glove will not fit the wrong hand].
   - **译法**：免疫系统可能不识别镜像细菌，就像手套戴不上另一只手。

2. **原句 14**：Because viruses and predatory amoebas that keep natural pathogens in check may be unable to control them, they could ravage ecosystems.
   - **结构**：因为从句 + 主句；定语从句 modifies viruses and amoebas。
   - **难点**：causal chain: natural predators fail → ecological ravage。
   - **解剖**：Because [viruses and predatory amoebas that keep natural pathogens in check] may be unable to control them, [they could ravage ecosystems].
   - **译法**：因为控制自然病原的病毒/捕食者可能无法控制镜像菌，它们可能肆虐生态系统。

3. **原句 23**：The first necessity is for all countries capable of the research to agree not to fund it, and to ban public and private work leading in this direction—and to do both today, well before synthetic cells are possible.
   - **结构**：主句 + 不定式短语 + 破折号强调。
   - **难点**：双重 obligation（fund + ban）与 timing clause。
   - **解剖**：The first necessity is for [all countries capable of the research to agree not to fund it, and to ban ... work] —and to do both today, well before synthetic cells are possible.
   - **译法**：首要之务是所有能研究的国家同意不资助，并禁止公共/私人研究——而且今天就做，远早于合成细胞可能实现。

4. **原句 28**：Last, in case mirror life does ever escape containment, biosurveillance tests must be developed to pick out wrong-handed molecules in the wild and sound a warning.
   - **结构**：副词 Last + 状语 in case + 主语 + 模态 + 不定式链。
   - **难点**：contingency clause + dual purpose of tests。
   - **解剖**：Last, in case [mirror life does ever escape containment], [biosurveillance tests] must be developed [to pick out wrong-handed molecules in the wild] and [sound a warning].
   - **译法**：最后，为防镜像生命逃逸，必须开发生物监测测试，以在野外识别错误手性分子并发出警报。

5. **原句 35**：If humanity cannot deal in advance with a threat when everyone’s interests are aligned, it cannot hope to tackle the unknown unknowns of the future.■
   - **结构**：条件从句（内含 when 子句）+ 主句。
   - **难点**：governance capacity thesis 的嵌套条件。
   - **解剖**：If [humanity cannot deal in advance with a threat when everyone’s interests are aligned], [it cannot hope to tackle the unknown unknowns of the future].■
   - **译法**：如果人类连利益一致时的威胁都应对不了，就别指望 tackle 未来未知的未知。

## 精读结束总结

1. **本文核心表达**：镜像生命手性反转将导致免疫盲区、生态失控与不可召回；威胁大于收益；必须在技术成熟前建立全链条 controls；利益一致时的可应对性决定未来 unknown unknowns 的处理能力。
2. **重要语法**：
   - 虚拟条件：would be impossible to recall；may not recognise...
   - 比较结构：the more countermeasures are in place, the easier...
   - 让步/因果：Because ... may be unable to control them, they could ravage...
   - 不定式链：to pick out ... and sound a warning.
3. **写作技巧**：
   - analogy：glove / hands / hurricane-free?（手性类比）。
   - 历史类比：Asilomar（1975）。
   - case-by-case 排除 deliberate misuse（state/terrorist/company/criminal）。
   - risk layering（ban → monitor → biosurveillance）。
