# There is too little money in British politics, not too much（精读分析）

## 概览

**来源：** The Economist  
**栏目：** 时事评论  
**副题：** 英国政治不是钱太多，而是钱太少——为 Reform 两笔 £36m 巨额捐赠辩护，同时主张放开政治献金

**主题：** 通过 Reform 党两笔每笔 £36m 的加密货币捐赠引发的 Westminster 争议，作者指出担忧者的三项指控中只有一项站得住脚；更深层的问题是英国政治长期缺钱——党派缺资金、影子内阁缺人手、捐赠者 pool 太窄——因此限制合法献金（更遑论溯及既往）只会适得其反，伤害法治。

**结构列表：**

1. 引入：Ben Delo 与 Christopher Harborne 两位加密货币富翁向 Reform 各捐 £36m；
2. 捐赠引发 Westminster 震动，其他党派呼之欲-block；
3. 担忧者的三项交织指控，只有一项站得住脚；
4. Reform 的道德污点是真实无疑的；
5. 但不应限制合法钱进入政治；
6. 钱在一定点 beyond 无法 Simpson 胜选举；支出上限 leveling effect；
7. 英国政治需要钱，英国政党连慈善机构都不如；
8. impoverished 党无法 prepare for power；
9. 公民 cynicism 不 warranted，需要更多 public-spirited donors；
10. 溯及既往将 discredit rule of law；
11. 对 Reform 的对手：别动 statute book，拿 chequebook。

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：两位加密货币富翁向 Reform 各捐 £36m

> **原句 1:** Nobody could mistake Ben Delo or Christopher Harborne for saints.

**中文理解：** 没人会把 Ben Delo 与 Christopher Harborne 当成圣人。

**句子结构：** 主句 "Nobody could mistake A or B for saints"——must not mistake A for B 的 "nobody can/could" 版，强调"He's definitely not one."

**关键词：** mistake (误认为)；saints (圣人)

**表达方式：** 用 "nobody could mistake X for saints" 先给 controversial donor 定调——承认 flaws 以建立 credibility，避免 being 其他 party 的 naive defender。

**为什么这样写：** colocate ing two men with "saints" rhetorical baseline sets readers's expectation: we know they're problematic, yet 他们 still did something useful。

> **原句 2:** Both made fortunes in cryptocurrencies.

**中文理解：** 两人都靠加密货币发了财。

**句子结构：** both + V (动词短语)

**关键词：** fortunes ( fortunes; substantially rich ); cryptocurrencies (加密货币)

**表达方式：** 用 "Both made fortunes" 而 "Both are wealthy"——更强 economic framing，paving the road for "money" 句神。

**为什么这样写：** 暗示 donor base 不 noble（crypto money has regulatory taint），yet still argues structurally that money 贡献 political participation via other than ideal origins。

> **原句 3:** In 2022 Mr Delo was convicted in America of failing to implement money-laundering controls—he was later pardoned by President Donald Trump.

**中文理解：** 2022 年 Mr Delo 因未实现反洗钱控制在美国被定罪——他后来被川普总统赦免。

**句子结构：** "subject + was convicted of + -ing/noun"；"— he was later pardoned by..." 补充句 appositive 用 em dash.

**关键词：** convicted (被判有罪); failing to implement money-laundering controls; pardoned (特赦)

**表达方式：** em dash em dash 连结 related information：前面败诉，后面 pardon—pointing out redemption/pardon nuance，不 tally clean sweep。

**为什么这样写：** author shows both sides（conviction + pardon）to forestall "too quick to judge" opponent yet preserves moral nuance without overstating exculpation。

> **原句 4:** Both men are keener on Reform uk, a populist right-wing party led by Nigel Farage, than any sensible person should be.

**中文理解：** 两人都比任何 sensible 人都更迷恋 Reform UK——这个 Nigel Farage 领导的 populist right-wing party。

**句子结构：** "A is keener on X than any B should be" + 同位 phrase + relative clause

**关键词：** keener (更着迷); populist (民粹); right-wing (右翼); sensible person (明智的人)

**表达方式：** "than any sensible person should be" 自我隔绝 political preference—underscoring author's centrist/est establishment's stand.

**为什么这样写：** 读者 explicitly told author doesn't endorse Reform, yet defends donor rights; establishes political positioning as neutral-ish observer。

> **原句 5:** Yet, by promising unheard-of quantities of money to Reform, both have done something useful.

**中文理解：** 而 yet，通过发给 Reform unprecedented money, both men 做了一件 useful 的事。

**句子结构：** "Yet by + -ing，主 + have done something useful"

**关键词：** unheard-of ( unprecedented ); quantities (数量); useful (有用)

**表达方式：** "Yet" 转折 framing contrast（他们 flaws + Reform-sympathy vs something useful they caused），sets thesis of article early();

**为什么这样写：** 确立 counter-intuitive thesis—<|constrain|> money to a populist party inadvertently improves British politics）。

**段落逻辑：** 介绍 donor duo（加密货币 fortunes + conviction/pardon + Reform enthusiasm）→ yet argues their donations "useful" → 开篇 thesis。

### 第 2 段：捐赠引发 Westminster tizzy 与 cap 提议

> **原句 6:** Their donations, each of £36m ($48m), have caused a tizzy in Westminster.

**中文理解：** 他们 each donate £36m ($48m) 引发 Westminster 一片 panic/tizzy。

**句子结构：** 主语 They/donations + non-restrictive appositive "each of £36m($48m)" + have caused a tizzy in Westminster.

**关键词：** tizzy (慌乱/混乱); Westminster ( Westminster)

**表达方式：** 用 in Westminster (而非 in politics broadly) 体现 institutional/elite alarm。

**为什么这样写：** 语言 economy; establishes scale by pairing £36m with $48m for international readers。

> **原句 7:** Other parties are crying corruption and threatening to block the gifts.

**中文理解：** 其他 Parties 呼 corruption 且威胁 blocked the gifts。

**句子结构：** "Other parties are crying corruption" (accusative = "claim corruption") and threatening to block X.

**关键词：** crying corruption ( charge corruption ); threatening to block ( threaten to block).

**表达方式：** "crying corruption" 将 opponent response framed as reactive moralizing, not rigorous analysis。

**为什么这样写：** narrates controversy from other parties' perspective to set up "only one claim holds water".

> **原句 8:** Parliament had already been considering proposals to cap donations at £100,000 per person per year.

**中文理解：** Parliament already was considering proposals to cap individual donation to £100k/year.

**句子结构：** past perfect continuous; proposals to cap X; ellipsis.

**关键词：** proposals; cap donations at £100,000 per person per year.

**表达方式：** irony of threshold £100k vs £36m → dramatizes scale gap, primes reader with "would force Reform return most money".

**为什么这样写：** creates counterfactual path; indicates future new rules could retrospectively apply.

> **原句 9:** Any new rules could be made retroactive, forcing Reform to return most of the dosh.

**中文理解：** New rules could be made retroactive, forcing Reform to return most of the dosh ( slang for money ).

**句子结构：** "Any new rules could be made retroactive, forcing X to V."

**关键词：** retroactive ( retrospectively effective ); dosh (cash/money slang).

**表达方式：** 用 "dosh" informal air within serious political article → mild comic relief; follows "tizzy" informal tone.

**为什么这样写：** keeps prose light while forewarning of consequence; stakes are real.

**段落逻辑：** 说明 donation scale X Westminster panic X counter-proposals X risk of retroactive return X comic register.

### 第 3 段：担忧者的三项交织指控

> **原句 10:** The worriers make three intertwined arguments.

**中文理解：** 担忧者 weave three intertwined arguments。

**句子结构：** subject + make three intertwined arguments。

**关键词：** worriers ( worrying ones ); intertwined ( intertwine).

**表达方式：** "three intertwined arguments" -> combination is harder to untangle than individual issues.

**为什么这样写：** enumeration signals upcoming argumentative architecture; lets author dismantle point by point.

> **原句 11:** They claim that Reform habitually bends and even breaks the rules; that the cash gives the party an unfair advantage; and that big money harms politics.

**中文理解：** 他们 claim that Reform habitually bends—and even breaks—the rules; that the cash gives party unfair advantage; and that ⬇️ money harms politics.

**句子结构：** "They claim that A; that B; and that C." (parallelism).

**关键词：** habitually (习惯性); bends and breaks rules; unfair advantage; harms politics.

**表达方式：** "; and" tripartite claim; semicolon separates clauses in compound list. Appositive-type enumeration.

**为什么这样写：** lays out full prosecutor-case before agreeing only 1 claim holds water.

> **原句 12:** Only one of these claims holds water.

**中文理解：** only one of those claims holds water.

**句子结构：** "Only [one of these claims]" verb holds water.

**关键词：** holds water ( stand/scoring in argument sense; credible).

**表达方式：** idiom "holds water" = credible/evidence-backed. Chos ing colloquial idiom, anchoring article's magazine flair.

**为什么这样写：** brief and decisive; announces which single worry is genuine (likely claim 1: habitual rule bending), then follows with concession.

**段落逻辑：** enum three intertwined worries → only one 具 validity,牌子也 other two deeper rejected later.

### 第 5 段：不应限制 legal money

> **原句 18:** Rules must be followed, including those on donations from abroad.

**中文理解：** Rules must be followed, including those on foreign donations.

**句子结构：** 命题 “Rules must be followed” + including phrase (relative clause?).

**关键词：** Rules must be followed; donations from abroad.

**表达方式：** drops the "must be followed" core credo; self-soothing (?) pre-argue against own conclusion (not restrict money).

**为什么这样写：** immunizes conclusion from "why are you ignoring rules?" counter by establishing rule-fidelity first.

> **原句 19:** But people who care about the health of British politics should not try to restrict the flow of legal money into it.

**中文理解：** But those who care about health of UK politics shouldn't try to restrict flow of legal money into it.

**句子结构：** "But X who Y should not Z the flow of legal money into it."

**关键词：** health of British politics; restrict (限制); flow of legal money (合法 money 流入).

**表达方式：** adversative but; broad policy thesis.

**为什么这样写：** Core thesis—explicit call to action; set up next paragraph reasons.

> **原句 20:** Britain suffers not from an excess of political donations, but from a severe lack of them.

**中文理解：** Britain suffered not from an excess but from a severe lack of political donations.

**句子结构:** "Britain suffers not from A but from B." (not...but).

**关键词:** suffers; excess of political donations; severe lack.

**表达方式:** concessive-not-but named immediately becomes memorable slogan.

**为什么这样写：** turns conventional wisdom inside out: "too much money in politics" is false British context; setter for evidence.

**段落逻辑:** rule fidelity concession → principal thesis → reframing scarcity vs excess.

### 第 6 段：钱不能 simply buy 胜利 + spending ceiling leveling effects

> **原句 21:** One reason not to block money is that, after a certain point, cash cannot simply buy victory in elections (see Britain section).

**中文理解：** One reason not to block money is that beyond some point, cash can't simply buy election victories (see Britain section).

**句子结构：** "One reason … is that …" + "judge (see Britain section)" parenthetical指指南.

**关键词/directions:** buy victory; see Britain section; after a certain point.

**表达方式:** em dash-ish parenthetical source reference; “a certain point” hedge prevents absolutism.

**为什么这样写:** pragmatic re defensive point: even with money, elections are too complex for purchase； hedging "simply" maintains nuance (possibly influence).

> **原句 22:** And even if it could, restrictions on campaign spending in Britain are a leveller.

**中文理解:** even if money could buy victories, UK's campaign spending restrictions (including campaign expenditure caps) leveller.

**句子结构:** "And even if it could, restrictions on X are a leveller."

**关键词:** restrictions on campaign spending; leveller.

**表达方式:**  conditional on "could" prior hyperbole; "leveller" = bring equal candidates footing.

**为什么这样写:** second prong—argues spending restrictions already address any potential imbalance, so banning money not needed.

> **原句 23:** In the case of Reform, the windfall could grow poisonous as factions already at war within the party struggle for a share of the spoils.

**中文理解:** In Reform case, the windfall could become poisonous as internal warring factions fight over spoils—may poison party dynamics internally.

**句子结构:** "In the case of X, Y could grow poisonous as Z."

**关键词:** windfall; poisonous; factions at war within party; spoils.

**表达方式:** metaphor "poisonous" + "spoils" (war/s spoils) foreshadows internal cannibalism.

**为什么这样写:** warn money has internal risks (factionalism) not purely electoral—subtly acknowledges money can hurt, but hurt Reform internally, not electorate.

**段落逻辑:** argues money can't simply purchase elections; even if, spending caps level; warns Reform money may fragment internally.

## 长难句专项

长句 1（paragraph sentence 3):

> "In 2022 Mr Delo was convicted in America of failing to implement money-laundering controls—he was later pardoned by President Donald Trump."

**句法拆解：**

- 主语："Mr Delo" —— 人物 singular
- 谓语："was convicted" + "of + V-ing clause"
- 句号："of failing to implement money-laundering controls"
- 额外信息 em dash: "he was later pardoned by President Donald Trump."

**理解：** 定罪 vs赦免 的 narrative duality。

长句 2（paragraph sentence 11):

> "They claim that Reform habitually bends and even breaks the rules; that the cash gives the party an unfair advantage; and that big money harms politics."

**句法拆解：**

- 主动起句："They claim that + three parallel complement clauses."
- clauses: [ that Reform habitually bends and even breaks the rules ]; [ that the cash gives the party an unfair advantage ]; [ that big money harms politics ].

**理解：** 枚举 three intertwined critiques.

长句 3（paragraph sentence 30):

> "The victor of a general election wins control of more than £1.3trn in public spending; those vying for this prize do so on a shoestring."

**句法拆解：**

- semicolon compound: first half: victor + wins control of more than £1.3trn public spending.
- second half: those vying for this prize + "do so" (指 wins control) "on a shoestring."

**理解：** Prize magnitude vs campaign thin resourcing.
