# China’s tutoring crackdown has created a black market（精读分析）

## 概览

**来源与栏目：** The Economist，2026-09-19。Chaguan 专栏，题为 “China’s tutoring crackdown has created a black market”。

**主题：** 中国“双减”政策压低了校外辅导供给，却没有消灭需求；家长、学生与教师被推入准地下市场，反而推高价格、加剧不平等，政策的善意被深嵌的社会竞争模式反噬。

**全文结构：**
- 第 1 段：执法突查——突查课外辅导中心，把“禁补”政策的 enforcement 推上前台
- 第 2 段：全国效仿——匿名举报与伪装，形成 quasi-criminal underground
- 第 3 段：后果定性—— striving students / anxious parents / earnest tutors 都成“非法人”
- 第 4 段：政策起点——2021 年“双减”，非 blanket ban，仅封营利性核心科
- 第 5 段：成效——巨头崩盘，需求地下化
- 第 6 段：北京实地——隐蔽辅导、跳课教学
- 第 7 段：价格效应——供给受限、需求强劲，价格翻涨与课外军备竞赛
- 第 8 段：学术军备竞赛——海淀“虎妈虎爸”与低收入家庭压力
- 第 9 段：政府替代方案——5+2 模式与资金缺位
- 第 10 段：根源——超竞争社会与可得性的丧失

**段落脉络表格：**

| 段 | 主题 |
|---|---|
| 1 |執法突查视频化，标榜“ Dangerous scourge” |
| 2 | 全国围剿与伪装、113 名非法 tutor 曝光 |

| 8 | 虎爸 tiger parents 与 inequality loop |
| 9 | 5+2 替代方案与 supervised homework |
| 10 | 根源： ultra-competitive life 与 accessible cram schools 之失 |

**核心金句：**
- “Heaven forfend children study even more maths!”
- “In this way, striving students, anxious parents and earnest tutors have become unlikely members of a quasi-criminal underworld, one devoted to exam preparation.”
- “But the results have been a lesson in unintended consequences.”
- “What is left is more unequal, and it makes scofflaws of many who just want the best for their children.”

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段： enforcement 秀场 —— 一次 after-school tutoring 突查

> **原句 1:** THE INSPECTION team barged in as business was in full swing.

**中文理解：** 执法检查组在生意最为繁忙的时候冲了进来。

**句子结构：** `THE INSPECTION team [barged in] [as business was in full swing]`——主谓 + 时间从句。

**关键词：**
- `barged in` ⭐⭐⭐ — 冲进来、闯入。
- `in full swing` ⭐⭐⭐ — 正在进行、最为繁忙。

**表达方式：** `THE INSPECTION team barged in`：以 THE INSPECTION team（检查组）+ barged in（冲入）——“执法闯入”画面感。

**为什么这样写：** 开幕式般开场：把日常补课写成“犯罪现场”，为后文 quasi-criminal 定调。

> **原句 2:** An official confronted the front-desk staff, a cop snapped photos of incriminating evidence, an investigator questioned customers.

**中文理解：** 一名官员与前台员工对峙，一名警察给“罪证”拍照，一名调查员询问顾客。

**句子结构：** 三个并列分句（`An official confronted…, a cop snapped…, an investigator questioned…`）。

**关键词：**
- `front-desk staff` ⭐⭐ — 前台员工。
- `incriminating evidence` ⭐⭐⭐ — 定罪证据。
- `questioned customers` ⭐⭐ — 质询顾客。

**表达方式：** 三短句并列，像电影分镜；“cop / investigator” 强化执法阵仗。

**为什么这样写：** 通过执法细节夸张化 ordinary tutoring，突出“过度执法”。

> **原句 3:** Next, they got inside the computer system, scoping out prices paid and services rendered.

**中文理解：** 接着，他们进入计算机系统，查探所付价格与所提供的服务。

**句子结构：** `Next, they got inside the computer system, [scoping out prices paid and services rendered]`——主谓 + -ing 分词短语。

**关键词：**
- `scoping out` ⭐⭐⭐ — 查探、打探。
- `services rendered` ⭐⭐⭐ — 提供的服务。

**表达方式：** “got inside … scoping out …”——技术化 surveillance，把追查写到数据层。

**为什么这样写：** 说明执法深入系统，显示“ tutors as quasi-criminals”的真实感。

> **原句 4:** Finally, when it was all done, the authorities in Huiyang, a southern city, posted a video of the sting, set to stirring music.

**中文理解：** 最终，当一切结束后，南方城市惠阳的当局发布了一段配以激昂音乐的“卧底执法”视频。

**句子结构：** `Finally, when it was all done, the authorities in Huiyang, a southern city, posted a video of the sting, [set to stirring music]`。

**关键词：**
- `the sting` ⭐⭐⭐ — 卧底/诱捕执法。
- `stirring music` ⭐⭐⭐ — 激昂音乐。

**表达方式：** `set to stirring music`——把 enforcement 做成 propaganda video。

**为什么这样写：** 突出 performative governance：执法变成宣传，显示“以震慑代引导”。

> **原句 5:** For this had been a raid on a most dangerous scourge: an after-school tutoring centre.

**中文理解：** 因为这次突查的对象是“最危险的祸害”之一：课后辅导中心。

**句子结构：** `For this had been a raid on a most dangerous scourge: an after-school tutoring centre`——解释性同位/冒号。

**关键词：**
- `most dangerous scourge` ⭐⭐⭐ — 最危险的祸害。
- `after-school tutoring centre` ⭐⭐ — 课后辅导中心。

**表达方式：** 冒号揭示“祸害”实质是 tutoring centre，制造 satirical gap。

**为什么这样写：** 讽刺性揭示：把辅导中心比作“scourge”，批判 rhetoric overreach。

> **原句 6:** Heaven forfend children study even more maths!

**中文理解：** 上天保佑，别让孩子们学更多数学！

**句子结构：** 感叹/祝福祈使句。

**关键词：**
- `Heaven forfend` ⭐⭐⭐ — 上天禁止/ forbid hope。
- `even more maths` ⭐⭐ — 更多数学。

**表达方式：** `Heaven forfend…`：讽刺 tone，反话 criticise ban mentality。

**为什么这样写：** 用反讽收束段落，点出“ban 对学习本身”的荒诞。

**段落逻辑：** 突查视频化（原句 1–4）→ 揭示对象（5）→ 反讽（6）：执法秀的荒唐感先行，引出主题“ unintended policy”。

### 第 2 段：全国化与伪装 —— 命名羞辱与 underground tactics

> **原句 7:** Across China, authorities have staged similar raids.

**中文理解：** 在中国各地，当局也在 staging 类似突查。

**句子结构：** `Across China, authorities have staged similar raids`。

**关键词：**
- `staged similar raids` ⭐⭐⭐ — staging 类似突查。

**表达方式：** `Across China`——全国化，显示 systematic campaign。

**为什么这样写：** 承接段落 1，把个案扩展 nationwide。

> **原句 8:** Inspection teams in Inner Mongolia and Hubei have posted their own videos.

**中文理解：** 内蒙古与湖北的检查组也发布了各自的视频。

**句子结构：** `Inspection teams in Inner Mongolia and Hubei have posted their own videos`。

**关键词：**
- `Inspection teams` ⭐⭐⭐ — 检查组。
- `posted their own videos` ⭐⭐ — 发布视频。

**表达方式：** 列地名 reinforcing performative enforcement。

**为什么这样写：** 例证“ raids as spectacle”扩大化。

> **原句 9:** Others have taken a different tack, naming and shaming: in Changsha they released the addresses of 113 illicit tutors.

**中文理解：** 其他地方采取了不同策略—— naming and shaming：长沙发布了 113 名非法家教的地址。

**句子结构：** `Others have taken a different tack, naming and shaming: [in Changsha they released…]`。

**关键词：**
- `taken a different tack` ⭐⭐⭐ — 采取不同策略。
- `naming and shaming` ⭐⭐⭐ — 公布点名羞辱。
- `illicit tutors` ⭐⭐⭐ — 非法家教。

**表达方式：** `naming and shaming:`——冒号引出具体后果。

**为什么这样写：** 显示“ naming/shaming” become enforcement tool, pushing tutors underground。

> **原句 10:** Many have adopted disguises.

**中文理解：** 许多人开始伪装。

**句子结构：** `Many have adopted disguises`。

**关键词：**
- `adopted disguises` ⭐⭐⭐ — 采取伪装。

**表达方式：** 简洁句，过渡到 below.

**为什么这样写：** 设问式过渡，引出 father example。

> **原句 11:** One father in Beijing pretends his son attends a calligraphy school (permitted) when in fact he is studying for the maths olympiad (discouraged).

**中文理解：** 北京一名父亲谎称儿子上书法学校（允许），实际上却在进行数学奥林匹克备考（被 discouraged）。

**句子结构：** `One father in Beijing pretends his son attends a calligraphy school (permitted) [when in fact he is studying for the maths olympiad (discouraged)]`。

**关键词：**
- `pretends` ⭐⭐ — 假装。
- `calligraphy school (permitted)` ⭐⭐⭐ — 书法学校（被允许）。
- `maths olympiad (discouraged)` ⭐⭐⭐ — 数学奥赛（被 discouraged）。

**表达方式：** 括号对照 permitted/discouraged， reveal regulatory asymmetry。

**为什么这样写：** 用 personnale anecdote 说明 policy creates dishonesty。

> **原句 12:** Wealthy families now hire “guidance counsellors” to aid their children, an expensive and illegal workaround.

**中文理解：** 有钱家庭现在雇“升学指导顾问”帮助孩子，这是一种昂贵而非法的 workaround。

**句子结构：** `Wealthy families now hire “guidance counsellors” to aid their children, [an expensive and illegal workaround]`——主从 + 同位。

**关键词：**
- `guidance counsellors` ⭐⭐⭐ — 指导顾问/升学顾问。
- `workaround` ⭐⭐⭐ — 绕行方案、权宜之计。

**表达方式：** `workaround`——把 evasion 包装为 “service innovation”。

**为什么这样写：** 引出 inequality： wealthy can buy loopholes。

**段落逻辑：** 全国 raids（7–8）→ naming/shaming（9）→ disguise (10–11) → wealthy workaround (12)：enforcement begets evasion and inequality。

### 第 3 段：quasi-criminal underworld —— policy 的 unintended consequences

> **原句 13:** In this way, striving students, anxious parents and earnest tutors have become unlikely members of a quasi-criminal underworld, one devoted to exam preparation.

**中文理解：** 这样一来， striving 的学生、焦虑的家长与 earnest 的家教，都成了“准犯罪地下世界”中意想不到的成员，而这一地下世界的唯一目标就是考试备考。

**句子结构：** `In this way, [striving students, anxious parents and earnest tutors] have become unlikely members of [a quasi-criminal underworld, one devoted to exam preparation]`。

**关键词：**
- `striving students` ⭐⭐⭐ — 努力/ striving 的学生。
- `anxious parents` ⭐⭐ — 焦虑家长。
- `earnest tutors` ⭐⭐⭐ — earnest 家教。
- `quasi-criminal underworld` ⭐⭐⭐ — 准犯罪地下世界。

**表达方式：** 三重修饰名词（striving / anxious / earnest）+ `unlikely members`——讽刺性 elevation of normal people to criminals。

**为什么这样写：** 核心 framing：policy criminalises ordinary striving。

> **原句 14:** It is a revealing—and for many in China maddening—saga about the shortcomings of public policy, especially against deep-seated social patterns.

**中文理解：** 这是一段 revelatory——对许多中国人来说也是 maddening——的故事，关于公共政策在 deep-seated social patterns 面前的 shortcomings。

**句子结构：** `It is a [revealing—and …—saga] about [the shortcomings of public policy, especially against deep-seated social patterns]`。

**关键词：**
- `revealing saga` ⭐⭐⭐ — 揭示性 saga。
- `maddening` ⭐⭐⭐ — 让人抓狂的。
- `deep-seated social patterns` ⭐⭐⭐ — 深根蒂固的社会模式。

**表达方式：** 两个破折号插入 `revealing—and maddening—`，加强 emotional tone。

**为什么这样写：** 直接结论：policy shortcoming vs entrenched culture。

> **原句 15:** The government may mean well in trying to ease academic pressure.

**中文理解：** 政府试图缓解学术压力，可能是善意的。

**句子结构：** `The government may mean well in [trying to ease academic pressure]`。

**关键词：**
- `may mean well` ⭐⭐⭐ — 或许出于善意。
- `ease academic pressure` ⭐⭐⭐ — 缓解学业压力。

**表达方式：** `may mean well`——concession，balance tone。

**为什么这样写：** fair-minded hedging，避免 polemical。

> **原句 16:** But the results have been a lesson in unintended consequences.

**中文理解：** 但结果却成了 unintended consequences 的一课。

**句子结构：** `But the results have been a lesson in unintended consequences`。

**关键词：**
- `unintended consequences` ⭐⭐⭐ —  unintended consequences。

**表达方式：** `a lesson in`——经典 economics phrase。

**为什么这样写：** 句点式主题总结，引出全文 analytical arc。

**段落逻辑：** quasi-criminal framing (13) → policy shortcoming (14) → concession (15) → central thesis (16)：unintended consequences。

### 第 4 段：政策起点 —— “双减”与学术劳动 12 小时

> **原句 17:** The crackdown began in 2021 when the government launched its “double reduction” policy, aiming to reduce both off-campus tutoring and homework for long-suffering students.

**中文理解：** crackdown 始于 2021 年，政府推出“双减”政策， aiming to reduce off-campus tutoring and homework for long-suffering students。

**句子结构：** `The crackdown began in 2021 when the government launched its “double reduction” policy, [aiming to reduce both A and B for C]`。

**关键词：**
- `double reduction` ⭐⭐⭐ — 双减。
- `long-suffering students` ⭐⭐⭐ — 长期受苦的学生。

**表达方式：** `launched its … policy, aiming to…`—— policy intent description。

**为什么这样写：** 建立 context 与 legitimacy。

> **原句 18:** Something had to be done: Chinese children can spend 12 hours a day on academic work, cutting into sleep and exercise, not to mention fun.

**中文理解：** 不得不做点什么：中国孩子每天可能花 12 小时在学业上， cutting into sleep and exercise, not to mention fun。

**句子结构：** `Something had to be done: [Chinese children can spend 12 hours a day on academic work, cutting into sleep and exercise, not to mention fun]`。

**关键词：**
- `12 hours a day` ⭐⭐ — 12 小时一天。
- `cutting into sleep and exercise` ⭐⭐⭐ — 侵占睡眠与运动。
- `not to mention fun` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更别说乐趣。

**表达方式：** `not to mention`——递进 rhetorical emphasis。

**为什么这样写：** 用数据与生活代价说明 “why crackdown”。

> **原句 19:** Burnouts are common as students approach entrance exams for both secondary school and university.

**中文理解：** 当学生接近中考与高考时， burnouts 很常见。

**句子结构：** `Burnouts are common as students approach [entrance exams for both secondary school and university]`。

**关键词：**
- `Burnouts` ⭐⭐⭐ — 烧尽、大崩溃。
- `entrance exams` ⭐⭐ — 入学考试。

**表达方式：** `as students approach`——时间递进。

**为什么这样写：** 补充“pressure”的 human cost。

> **原句 20:** The government's restriction on off-campus training is not a blanket ban.

**中文理解：** 政府对校外培训的限制并不是 blanket ban。

**句子结构：** `The government's restriction on off-campus training is not a blanket ban`。

**关键词：**
- `blanket ban` ⭐⭐⭐ — 一刀切禁令。

**表达方式：** 否定句，界定 policy scope。

**为什么这样写：** 澄清“非 blanket”为后文 “ballet/coding”做铺垫。

> **原句 21:** Parents can continue to send their children to ballet or coding classes.

**中文理解：** 家长仍可送孩子上芭蕾或编程课。

**句子结构：** `Parents can continue to send their children to ballet or coding classes`。

**关键词：**
- `ballet or coding classes` ⭐⭐ — 芭蕾或编程课。

**表达方式：** 具体 counterexamples。

**为什么这样写：** 显示 policy carve-outs， explain substitution effects。

> **原句 22:** But for-profit tutoring of core subjects, from science to history, is prohibited.

**中文理解：** 但是 for-profit 的核心科辅导——从 science 到 history——被禁止。

**句子结构：** `But for-profit tutoring of core subjects, from science to history, is prohibited`。

**关键词：**
- `for-profit tutoring` ⭐⭐⭐ — 营利性辅导。
- `core subjects` ⭐⭐ — 核心科目。

**表达方式：** “from science to history”——举例 span，显示 broad restriction。

**为什么这样写：** policy baseline set，为 “supply restriction” 奠基。

**段落逻辑：** 起点 (17) → 原因 (18–19) → scope (20–22)：policy legitimacy but limited instrument。

### 第 5 段：五年成效 —— 崩盘与地下化

> **原句 23:** Five years on, the results are visible.

**中文理解：** 五年过去，结果已 visible。

**句子结构：** `Five years on, the results are visible`。

**关键词：**
- `Five years on` ⭐⭐ — 五年后。

**表达方式：** 时间提示，过渡到 evidence。

**为什么这样写：** 结构句，过渡 evidence。

> **原句 24:** China's academic industrial complex has been wrecked.

**中文理解：** 中国的“学术工业复合体”已经被 wrecked。

- `wrecked` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被摧毁。

**表达方式：** strong verb “wrecked”， emphasize market collapse。

**为什么这样写：** Dramatic summary。

> **原句 25:** Listed companies such as New Oriental and TAL—the biggest cram-school brands—lost nearly 90% of their market value, wiping out about \$100bn.

**中文理解：** 像 New Oriental 与 TAL 这样——最大的 cram-school brands——的上市公司跌掉近 90% 市值， wiped out 约 1000 亿美元。

**句子结构：** `Listed companies such as New Oriental and TAL—the biggest cram-school brands—lost nearly 90% of their market value, [wiping out about \$100bn]`。

**关键词：**
- `cram-school brands` ⭐⭐⭐ — 补习班品牌。
- `market value` ⭐⭐ — 市值。
- `wiping out` ⭐⭐⭐ — 一举抹掉。

**表达方式：** 破折号 insert brands； `wiping out` 伴随后果。

**为什么这样写：** quantify policy impact (financial damage)。

> **原句 26:** They have since pivoted to adult education and non-academic tutoring.

**中文理解：** 此后它们 pivoted 到成人教育与 non-academic tutoring。

**句子结构：** `They have since pivoted to adult education and non-academic tutoring`。

**关键词：**
- `pivoted` ⭐⭐⭐ — 转型/ pivot。
- `non-academic tutoring` ⭐⭐⭐ — 非学术辅导。

**表达方式：** `pivoted`—— corporate adaptation。

**为什么这样写：** show partial substitution, evasion of ban.

> **原句 27:** Nevertheless, as shown by the raids on smaller tutoring businesses, and even individual instructors, the demand for extra studying has not vanished.

**中文理解：** 不过， raids on smaller businesses and even individual instructors show，额外学习的 demand hasn't vanished。

**句子结构：** `Nevertheless, as shown by [the raids on smaller tutoring businesses, and even individual instructors], the demand for extra studying has not vanished`。

**关键词：**
- `raids on smaller tutoring businesses` ⭐⭐⭐ — 对小补习机构的突查。
- `individual instructors` ⭐⭐ — 个人教师。
- `has not vanished` ⭐⭐ — 并未消失。

**表达方式：** “as shown by …”——把 enforcement itself as evidence of persistent demand。

**为什么这样写：** key paradox: ban fails eliminate demand。

> **原句 28:** It has been pushed underground and into new channels.

**中文理解：** 它被 pushed underground 和 into new channels。

**句子结构：** `It has been pushed underground and into new channels`。

**关键词：**
- `pushed underground` ⭐⭐⭐ — 被推向地下。
- `new channels` ⭐⭐⭐ — 新渠道。

**表达方式：** passive + paired destinations (underground/new channels)。

**为什么这样写：** state-market displacement thesis。

**段落逻辑：** 成效 (23–24) → quant (25) → adaptation (26) → demand persistence (27–28)：market destruction but demand underground。

“Alas, the belief that extra education is the best way up—or the way to avoid falling back—is entirely reasonable: plenty of the parents now sending children to tutors went through the same grind and saw the impact.”

- 主语：the belief that extra education is the best way up—or the way to avoid falling back
- 系表：is entirely reasonable
- 冒号解释：plenty of the parents now sending children to tutors went through the same grind and saw the impact
- 要点：验证家长rationale（rational, not panic）。

## 精读结束总结

### 1. 本文核心表达
- “双减” intend to reduce pressure but criminalises ordinary striving and displaces demand underground.
- Supply restriction + buoyant demand → higher prices + unequal access.
- Government alternative (5+2) underfunded → homework extension → more tutoring pressure.
- Root cause: ultra-competitive life; parental investment rational.

### 2. 重要语法
- as/when 从句 marking time/cause
- passive: has been pushed underground
- less by A than by B comparative
- colon for explanation

### 3. 写作技巧
- 讽刺与数字paradox（90% collapse vs persisting demand）
- 现场细节与直接引语提升 realism
- 断句短句制造 rhythm/impact
