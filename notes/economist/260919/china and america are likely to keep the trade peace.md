# China and America are likely to keep the trade peace（精读分析）

## 概览

**来源/栏目：** The Economist，Politics / Leaders 社论。发表日期：2026-09-19（时事评论）。副题隐含：中美在华盛顿峰会前竭力避免破坏性动作，围绕" Constructive strategic stability "（建设性战略稳定）的模糊停火能维持多久。

**主题：** 本文论述中美贸易停火的脆弱稳定逻辑——华盛顿峰会在即，双方因不愿破坏会谈而暂时压制了彼此的贸易纠纷；双方都希望维持贸易和平， magnets 等出口管制暴露了美国的脆弱性，而中国的出口增长也依赖稳定贸易环境。因此，休战可能持续，但具体谈判进展微小，双方共同的"让步"与"克制"才是会面的真正价值。

**全文结构：**

- 第 1 段：峰会前的"不 rash 原则"—— anticipation 比 outcome 更重要
- 第 2 段：休战可能就是本次会议最重要的成果
- 第 3 段：Busan 停火与北京峰会的具体让步
- 第 4 段：北京峰会成果： modest outcomes + 共享口号
- 第 5 段：Xi 访美前的不确定性：摩擦点不断、停火定义不清
- 第 6 段：美国测试停火： tariffs 替代 legal pretexts、humanoid robots 禁令

## 长难句专项

### 长难句 1（原句 8）

**原句：** What will matter most is not what is done at the get-together, but what was not done in advance of it.

**找主干：** What will matter most is ... (主系表)。

**找修饰：** 主语从句 What will matter most + is + 对比短语 not what is done at the get-together, but what was not done in advance of it.

**找从句：** What will matter most 主动从句; what is done at the get-together / what was not done in advance of it 宾语从句。

**恢复逻辑：** 用否定-肯定对比强调“会晤的真正价值 = 提前 built-up restraint”；是 thesis 核心。

**整体理解：** 高阶对比句，难在于 phrase embedding 两层 “what”; 读成 “most important = not actions at meeting but missing pre-meeting actions.”

### 长难句 2（原句 9）

**原句：** The meeting follows Donald Trump's visit to Beijing in May and the leaders' talks in Busan in South Korea, last October, where the two sides tried to formalise the stalemate the countries had reached after a furious few months of tit-for-tat tariffs and export controls (see timeline).

**找主干：** The meeting follows A and B (follows parallel NPs).

**找修饰：** Donald Trump's visit to Beijing in May (NP1); leaders' talks in Busan in South Korea, last October, where clause modifying noun phrase (NP2); nested relative clause: the stalemate the countries had reached; after...clause.

**找从句：** where 引导 relative clause modifying talks; the stalemate the countries had reached 强调 relative clause; after a furious few months... prepositional phrase.

**恢复逻辑：** temporal context: Washington meeting builds on two earlier events; Busan attempted formalise post-tariff/export-control stalemate.

**整体理解：** layered relative clauses compress chronology; "where" shifts from place to event, "the stalemate countries had reached" is dense but can be unrolled as “stalemate reached by countries after...”.

### 长难句 3（原句 34）

**原句：** But China's exports have provided a vital prop to its growth this year (even if direct sales to America make up a diminishing share).

**找主干：** China's exports have provided a vital prop to its growth (subject + verb + indirect object)。

**找修饰：** this year 时间状语; parenthetical concession: even if direct sales to America make up a diminishing share.

**找从句：** even if 条件让步从句。

**恢复逻辑：** Chinese exports globally support growth despite US sales shrinking, so China needs global stability; allows US-side retrenchment without direct high-US dependency.

**整体理解：** concessive parenthetical contrast maps global export dependency with US share decline; not complicated grammatically but subtle economically.

## 精读结束总结

### 本文核心表达
1. “Expectation > event itself”： anticipation creates pre-event restraint.
2. “Meeting value = restraint, not breakthrough”: reevaluation framework given no decisive settlement。
3. “Truce vagueness” causes mutual uncertainty about what counts as breach.
4. “Mutual vulnerability”: US fears magnets; China depends on exports but direct US share shrinking, plus global stability incentives。
5. Water polo metaphor: visible sportsmanship hides underwater kicking; both sides willing/able to push back if provoked.
6. Closing circle: “important meetings in the offing generate restraint,” making meetings truly important。

### 重要语法
- “what will matter most is not what is done ..., but what was not done ...” (cleft + contrast)
- “With luck” + future verb + paired location: “will with luck prevail.”
- “As long as ... in the offing, each side will show restraint” (conditional future from temporal offing).
- “can and will retaliate” (modality pairing)
- “provided (that) it could ...” (conditional proviso)
- “has compared with ... points out ...” attribution trailing.

### 长难句
- 最值得模仿 cleft/contrast "What will matter most is ... but ..." 作为 thesis condenser.
- 定语从句 layer: “the leaders’ talks ... where the two sides tried ... stalemate the countries had reached after ...”.
- parenthetical concession “(even if direct sales ... diminishing share)” shows cauting qualifier that shifts inference.

### 写作技巧
- Open with grounded metaphor (sex/Christmas) to make abstract “anticipation” vivid.
- Lower expectations explicitly (“No one expects breakthrough”), then pivot to value through absence.
- Use historical institutional chronology (Busan/Beijing) to establish context before new uncertainty.
- Enumerate breaching tests (US replacement tariff, regulator robot ban, China rare-earth drop), allowing reader weigh ambiguity.
- Explain stability via mutual vulnerability rather than treaty text.
- Use metaphor (water polo) for bilateral roughness and diplomatic quote for human voice.
- Close with “important meetings in the offing → restraint → truly important” echoes opening anticipation.
