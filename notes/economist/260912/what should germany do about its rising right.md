# What should Germany do about its rising right?（精读分析）

## 概览

- **来源/栏目**：The Economist（online, 2026-09-12）
- **副题/摘帽**：无独立副题；标题本身就是提问式
- **主题**：AfD 在萨克森-安哈尔特州拿下 44% 选票后，德国主流政党长期靠 *Brandmauer*（防火墙）与好政策压制极右的策略宣告失败。文章认为政局已形成亲/反 AfD 两大阵营，提出了三项应对：(1) 政府不要因 AfD 而 paralysis，反而要继续推进改革；(2) CDU 应重新审视 rigid *Brandmauer*，为未来可能的内化与合作打开条件空间；(3) 必要时代替领导层——默茨若无法 articulating 出 compelling vision，就该下台。
- **结构**：
  1. 事件冲击（AfD 44% 选票的冲击与红色警报）
  2. AfD 仍可能败北，但危险已成规模（ richer western states 也破纪录）
  3. 全国层面 AfD 领先 CDU/CSU 八个百分点，政局开始“curdle”
  4. Brandmauer 与主流策略失败，AfD 反而激进化
  5. AfD 成功 resting on 动员往年不投票者；Centre 不能 business as usual
  6. 第一策：改革不可因 AfD 而停
  7. 改革 unpopular 也非 abandon 的理由；经济 signs of recovery
  8. 第二策：CDU 应重新审视刚性 Brandmauer
  9. 第三策：必要时看高层——默茨的 communications failure 与 replaceability
- **段落脉络**：

| 段落 | 主题 | 核心变化 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 事件冲击 | “不应发生” → 红色警报 |
| 2 | 危险规模 | 穷州/富州 BOTH 破纪录 |
| 3 | 全国地位 | AfD 领先 8 个百分点，politics curdles |
| 4 | Brandmauer 失灵 | 希望 good policy curb，结果 opposite |
| 5 | 动员与进程 | 不投票者被动员，centre 无 luxury |
| 6 | 第一策 | 不让 AfD stun into inaction；改革 pressures |
| 7 | 责任与恢复 | unpopular ≠ abdication；lean into recovery |
| 8 | 第二策 | rigid Brandmauer 重思；有利 moderate |
| 9 | 第三策 | look at the top；Merz 13%；可能下台 |

- **核心金句**：
  > "But the political centre cannot afford to pursue business as usual."

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：事件冲击

> **原句 1:** IT WAS NOT supposed to happen.

中文理解：这是“意外”，背离了德国战后身份的预期——本不该发生。

句子结构：主语“IT”、谓语“WAS”、表语“NOT supposed to happen”；be supposed to 结构。

关键词："supposed to"（本应 / 预期）、"happen"（发生）。

表达方式："IT WAS NOT supposed to happen" 以全大写下结论作 hook。

为什么这样写：开篇用全大写短句造成 shock，先定调整篇语气。

> **原句 2:** For the radicals of the Alternative for Germany (AfD) to secure 44% of the vote in Saxony-Anhalt, one of Germany’s 16 states, has shocked a country that built its post-war identity on the rejection of extremes.

中文理解：AfD 极端派在萨克森-安哈尔特州拿下 44% 选票，震撼了战后以“拒绝极端”为身份根基的德国。

句子结构：不定式短语 “For … to secure” 作主语；谓语“has shocked”；宾语“a country that built …”；关系从句“that built its post-war identity on the rejection of extremes”。

关键词："radicals"（激进派）、"secure … of the vote"（拿下 … 选票）、"post-war identity"（战后身份）、"rejection of extremes"（拒绝极端）。

表达方式：不定式表惊骇事实；“one of Germany’s 16 states”插入语。

为什么这样写：把州内数字升级为 national identity 的危及，放大 stakes。

> **原句 3:** A party that employs unrepentant former neo-Nazis and has been formally decreed “extremist” by Germany’s intelligence service is on the cusp of becoming the first populist-right group to run a state since the second world war.

中文理解：一个雇用毫无悔意的前新纳粹、且被情报机构正式裁定为“极端”的政党，正要把成为二战以来第一个执政某州的右翼民粹组织。

句子结构：主句 “A party … is on the cusp of becoming”；who 定语从句 “that employs … and has been formally decreed”；宾语不定式 “the first populist-right group to run a state since the second world war”。

关键词："unrepentant"（无悔意的）、"decreed"（裁定）、"on the cusp of"（即将）、"populist-right"（右翼民粹）。

表达方式：定语从句叠加强调 party legitimacy deficit；“on the cusp of” 预示 imminent threat。

为什么这样写：把极右 party 和“新纳粹/极权”相连， framed 为 unprecedented。

> **原句 4:** For Germany, it is a red alert.

中文理解：这对德国来说是红色警报。

句子结构：形式主语“For Germany” + 主谓宾“it is a red alert”。

关键词："red alert"（红色警报）。

表达方式：短句收束成警号。

为什么这样写：paraphrase 全篇 stakes。

**段落逻辑：** 事件冲击 → 历史意义 classesi → EMERGENCY 定调。

### 第 2 段：危险规模

> **原句 5:** The AfD may yet fall short.

中文理解：AfD 还可能失手。

句子结构：主语 The AfD + 助动词 may yet + 谓语短语 fall short。

关键词："may yet"（仍可能）、"fall short"（功亏一篑）。

表达方式：may yet 表 uncertainty。

为什么这样写：先给失败可能性以 balanced tone。

> **原句 6:** Its only paths to power in Saxony-Anhalt rest on shaky alliances or defections from opponents.

中文理解：它在该州的唯一上台路径 resting on 不稳定联盟或对手倒戈。

句子结构：主语 “Its only paths to power”；谓语 rest on；宾语 “shaky alliances or defections from opponents”。

关键词："rest on"（依赖于）、"shaky"（不牢的）、"defections"（倒戈）。

表达方式：rest on 弱基础比喻 fragility。

为什么这样写：说明上台“ routes shaky，避免 overstated certainty。

> **原句 7:** If it does take office, it may be laid low by its own incompetence and in-fighting.

中文理解：若上台，也可能被自身 incompetent 与内斗拖垮。

句子结构：条件从句 “If it does take office” + 主句 “it may be laid low by its own incompetence and in-fighting”（may be laid low by... 被动）。

关键词："laid low"（打垮）、"incompetence"（ incompetence）、"in-fighting"（内斗）。

表达方式：被 động + by 强調 internal vulnerabilities。

为什么这样写：说明 even if succeeded，不可持续。

> **原句 8:** But that should not blind Germany to the scale of the danger.

中文理解：但不能因这些可能性而让德国 blindness 危险规模。

句子结构：否定主语 + 宾语 should not blind Germany to the scale of the danger。

关键词："blind … to"（使对… blindness）、"scale"（规模）。

表达方式：blind to idiomatic inability to perceive。

为什么这样写： pivot from complacency。

> **原句 9:** The AfD was always going to outperform in a state like Saxony-Anhalt: depopulated, deprived and in the east, where party loyalties are weaker.

中文理解：AfD 本就 likely 在萨克森-安哈尔特这样的州超常发挥：人口外流、贫困、东部，party loyalties weaker。

句子结构：主语 The AfD was always going to outperform；“in a state like …” 介宾；冒号后的 apposition “depopulated, deprived …”。

关键词："outperform"（超常发挥）、"depopulated"（人口流失的）、"deprived"（贫困的）。

表达方式：冒号提供 explanatory list；where led 是 non-restrictive relative。

为什么这样写：解释 AfD 契机 structural factors，降低 attribution to luck。

> **原句 10:** But it often breaks records in elections in richer western states such as Baden-Württemberg, too.

中文理解：但 richer 西德州 such as 巴登-符腾堡的选举中，它也往往刷新纪录。

句子结构：主语 it + 谓语 often breaks records；“in elections in richer western states such as Baden-Württemberg”；too。

关键词："breaks records"（创纪录）、"western states"（西部各州）。

表达方式：but + also 提到 spread beyond east。

为什么这样写：防止 east-only framing，shows nationalization danger。

**段落逻辑：** 可能败北 → 但危险不可估计 → 东西 both 破纪录。

### 第 3 段：全国地位

> **原句 11:** Nationally, the AfD now enjoys an eight-point polling lead over the ailing centre-right CDU/CSU, led by Friedrich Merz, the chancellor.

中文理解：全国看，AfD  now enjoyed 对 ailing centre-right CDU/CSU（Fraiedrich Merz 为 chancellor）八个百分点的 polling lead。

句子结构：副词 Nationally；主句 “the AfD now enjoys …”；宾语 an eight-point polling lead over ...；非限定 apposition “led by Friedrich Merz, the chancellor”。

关键词："polling lead"（polling 领先）、"eight-point"（8 个百分点）、"ailing"（困境的）、"centre-right"（中间偏右）。

表达方式：apposition for quick ID of leader.

为什么这样写：给 national-level evidence supporting urgency。

> **原句 12:** An election today would probably require a coalition of every centrist party to keep the AfD out of office.

中文理解：如果今天投票，可能须要 all centrist party 组 coalition 才能阻止 AfD into office。

句子结构：主语 “An election today”；“would probably require a coalition of every centrist party”；infinitive purpose “to keep the AfD out of office”。

关键词："every centrist party"（所有中间党）、"out of office"（离岗/不得参政）。

表达方式：would probably 条件 projection.

为什么这样写：展示 institutional squeeze；blocking AfD requires unusual unity。

> **原句 13:** German politics is starting to curdle into pro-AfD and anti-AfD blocs, as has long been the case across much of the east.

中文理解：德国政治正 tend to curdle 成 pro-AfD 与 anti-AfD 两 bloc，如多年来 eastern widespread pattern。

句子结构：主语 German politics；谓语 is starting to curdle；“into … blocs”；时间从句 “as has long been the case …”（so-called eastward parallel）。

关键词："curdle"（凝结/变质）、"blocs"（阵营）、"pro-AfD / anti-AfD"。

表达方式：curdle 不寻常食品 metaphor for political degradation。

为什么这样写：eastward normalisation of polarised blocs risk。

**段落逻辑：** 全国 lead large → institutional exclusion fragile → blocs formation。

### 第 4 段：Brandmauer 失灵

> **原句 14:** Germany’s centrists long thought they could keep AfD at bay.

中文理解：德国 centrist 们长时间 thought themselves can keep AfD at bay（压制）。

句子结构：主语 Germany’s centrists；谓语 long thought；宾语从句 “they could keep AfD at bay”。

关键词："at bay"（压制/周旋）。

表达方式：long thought 表 strategy belief。

为什么这样写：set up failed 抗御 hypothesis。

> **原句 15:** Their *Brandmauer* (firewall) policy excluded it from coalitions.

中文理解：Brandmauer（防火墙） policy excluded AfD from coalition governments。

句子结构：主语 Their policy；谓语 excluded；宾语 it；from coalitions。

关键词："Brandmauer"（防火墙政策）、"excluded … from coalitions"（排除 coalition）。

表达方式：定义 in-line parenthetical firewall confirms policy meaning。

为什么这样写： introducing key term.

> **原句 16:** In the meantime, they hoped, good policy would curb its appeal.

中文理解：与此同时，他们 hoping good policy would restrain/curb its appeal。

句子结构：插入语 In the meantime they hoped + expectation clause good policy would curb its appeal。

关键词："curb"（抑制）、"appeal"（conduct appeal）。

表达方式： insertion “they hoped” hints wishful thinking。

为什么这样写：show second bet that governance would solve problem。

> **原句 17:** But the opposite has happened.

中文理解：但 opposite occurs。

句子结构：But + 主谓 the opposite has happened。

关键词："the opposite"（反面）。

表达方式：三词 decisive reversal.

为什么这样写： pivot structuring critique of centrist strategy。

> **原句 18:** Behind the *Brandmauer* the AfD has radicalised as it has grown, pursuing racist fantasies of “remigration”, rapprochement with Russia and exit from the European Union and euro.

中文理解：在防火墙 protected space（behind Brandmauer）中，AfD as grows radicalized，pursuing racist fantasies like “remigration”、与 Russia rapprochement、退出欧盟与 euro。

句子结构：adverbial “Behind the Brandmauer”；主句 “the AfD has radicalised as it has grown”；participle “pursuing racist fantasies of …”（列举三个）。

关键词："radicalised"（激进化）、"remigration"（再移民）、"rapprochement"（缓和/和解）。

表达方式：pursuing multi-parallel civic alternatives.

为什么这样写： argues firewall inadvertently nurtured radicalization in protected space.

> **原句 19:** Its manifesto for Saxony-Anhalt, which called for a radical reshaping of the state’s education, culture, media and civil service, was a fantasy.

中文理解：它为萨克森-安哈尔特写的 manifesto，calling for radical reshaping of education culture media civil service，本身就是 fantasy。

句子结构：主语 “Its manifesto … was a fantasy”；非限定关系从句 “which called for a radical reshaping of …”。

关键词："manifesto"（宣言）、"radical reshaping"（彻底改造）、"civil service"（公务员体系）。

表达方式：which-call clause adds content; "fantasy" top-level judgement.

为什么这样写：undercuts governance capability even where popular.

> **原句 20:** Still, close to half the state’s voters were drawn to it.

中文理解：尽管如此， close to half of voters drawn to it。

句子结构： conjunction Still + subject close to half the state’s voters + were drawn to it.

关键词："were drawn to"（被吸引/ drawn to）。

表达方式：Still 不顾前面 Fantasy，highlight resonance.

为什么这样写：emphasise emotional resonance over realistic manifesto.

**段落逻辑：** centristst hope → opposite happens → radicalisation behind firewall → fantasy but still popular.

### 第 5 段：动员与进程

> **原句 21:** The AfD’s success in Saxony-Anhalt rested in large part on mobilising people who did not vote in the previous election.

中文理解：AfD in 的 bracket successes resting largely on mobilising previous non-voters.

句子结构：主语 success rested in large part on mobilising people；宾语从句/小句 “who did not vote in the previous election”。

关键词："rested … on"（依赖于）、"mobilising"（动员）。

表达方式：large-part hedge.

为什么这样写：structural explanation of rise, not just policy failure.

> **原句 22:** Many of them are lost to mainstream politics.

中文理解：many of these mobilized become lost to mainstream politics。

句子结构：Many of them are lost to mainstream politics.

关键词："lost to"（ irretrievable from）。

表达方式：lost to 表 irreversibly captured.

为什么这样写：warns electoral recovery difficult.

> **原句 23:** This is not to say that, nationally, Germany is fated to fall to the extremes.

中文理解：但这 does not mean nationwide Germany destined to fall extremes。

句子结构：主句 This is not to say + 从句 Germany is fated to fall to the extremes（with globally comma adverb nationally）。

关键词："fated"（注定）、"fall to extremes"（fall 极端）。

表达方式：not to say qualifications guard against fatalism。

为什么这样写：avoids despair, maintain agency。

> **原句 24:** But the political centre cannot afford to pursue business as usual.

中文理解：但政冷中心 cannot afford to continue business as usual。

句子结构：But 主语 cannot afford to pursue business as usual.

关键词："cannot afford"（承担不起）、"business as usual"（照常/保持惯性）。

表达方式：cannot afford 使 abstract stakes economic.

为什么这样写：transitional thesis to recommendations。

**段落逻辑：** mobilising non-voters → lost to mainstream → not inevitable nationally → but must change approach。

### 第 6 段：第一策

> **原句 25:** What should it do instead?

中文理解：那应该 instead do什么？

句子结构：疑问句 What should it do instead?（代词 it 指 political centre）。

关键词："instead"（反而）。

表达方式：直接设问 transitioning to recommendations.

为什么这样写： rhetorical pivot。

> **原句 26:** First, it must not let the AfD stun it into inaction.

中文理解：第一，must not let AfD stun into inaction（惊吓致 immobilize）。

句子结构：First, 主句 “it must not let the AfD stun it into inaction”（state-of-being after let）。

关键词："stun … into inaction"（惊吓到不作为）。

表达方式：stun into inaction vivid paralysis metaphor.

为什么这样写： warns against defeatism psychology。

> **原句 27:** Before the summer, Mr Merz’s dysfunctional coalition with the Social Democrats (SPD) approved an ambitious set of reforms covering everything from income tax to pensions.

中文理解：夏天前，Frau Merz 的 dysfunctional coalition with SPD approved ambitious reform package spanning income tax to pensions.

句子结构：时间主语 Before the summer + 主句 “Mr Merz’s dysfunctional coalition … approved …”；宾语 an ambitious set of reforms covering everything from income tax to pensions.

关键词："dysfunctional"（功能失调的）、"ambitious"（大胆/ ambitious）、"income tax / pensions"。

表达方式：from X to Y 范围修辞.

为什么这样写： gives concrete policy actions at risk。

> **原句 28:** But the SPD, which increasingly appears to find governing a burden rather than a blessing, is now considering reversing sensible policies such as ending early retirement.

中文理解：But SPD，appears increasingly find governing a burden rather than a blessing，现在 considerations reversing sensible policies such as ending early retirement.

句子结构：But 主语 “the SPD” + 非限定 which-clause；谓语 is now considering reversing；宾语 sensible policies such as …

关键词："governing a burden rather than a blessing"（执政 burden not blessing）、"reversing sensible policies"。

表达方式：which 定语 + rather than 衬 opposed evaluations.

为什么这样写： warning SPD backtracking would undo reform.

> **原句 29:** Such was the delicacy of the compromise that unravelling one proposal could imperil others.

中文理解： Such was the delicate compromise 使得 unmaking one proposal could endanger others。

句子结构：extraposition “Such was the delicacy” + that-clause “that unravelling one proposal could imperil others” (resultative).

关键词："delicacy"（脆弱/精细）、"unravelling"（拆解/ unravel）、"imperil"（危及）。

表达方式：such was … that 结果链。

为什么这样写： stresses policy interdependence.

**段落逻辑：** 设问 → AfD-induced paralysis 必免 → SPD reforms → delicate compromise risk of unraveling。

### 第 7 段：改革 unpopular 也非 abandon

> **原句 30:** True, many of the reforms are unpopular and AfD voters in particular dislike change, but this would be an abdication of responsibility.

中文理解：确实 reforms unpopular, AfD voters especially dislike change, but doing so abdication responsibility。

句子结构：concession True, … , but this would be + 名词 phrase。

关键词："unpopular"（不受人欢迎的）、"abdication"（abdication）、"responsibility".

表达方式：True, … but rhetorical concession.

为什么这样写： anticipate objections.

> **原句 31:** That is not only because Germany’s creaking economy and welfare state require urgent upgrade.

中文理解：Not only because Germany’s creaking economy and welfare state need urgent upgrade。

句子结构：主语 That is not only because + 宾从句。

关键词："creaking"（老态/吃力）、"urgent upgrade"。

表达方式：not only sets up fuller reason next.

为什么这样写：enumerates reasons to persist。

> **原句 32:** For the coalition to relapse into do-nothing bickering would also bolster the AfD’s claim that the old parties are tired and useless.

中文理解：For coalition to relapse into do-nothing bickering would also bolster AfD’s claim old parties 老累无用。

句子结构：for-phrase main subject + would bolster + object claim；宾从句 that old parties are tired and useless.

关键词："do-nothing bickering"（ inactive bickering）、"bolster"（ bolster）、"tired and useless"（ tired useless）。

表达方式：for-phrase nominalizes hypothetical behaviour.

为什么这样写：risk of reform reversal - narrative opportunity for AfD。

> **原句 33:** Germany’s economy is at last showing signs of recovery.

中文理解：German economy finally signs of recovery emerge。

句子结构：主语 Germany’s economy；谓语 is at last showing signs of recovery.

关键词："at last"（ finally）、"showing signs of"（ signs）。

表达方式：at last 兴奋 timing.

为什么这样写： encourage leaning into momentum.

> **原句 34:** The government should lean into it.

中文理解：政府 should lean into recovery（ capitalized 借势）。

句子结构：主语 The government，谓语 should lean into it.

关键词："lean into"（ capitalize/embracing）。

表达方式：lean into metaphor.

为什么这样写： framework positive opportunity not fear.

**段落逻辑：** unpopular reforms justified by responsibility/economy → do-nothing reinforces AfD attack → recovery should be embraced.

### 第 8 段：第二策

> **原句 35:** Second, it is past time for the CDU to reconsider the rigid *Brandmauer*.

中文理解：其次， CDU past time to reconsider rigid Brandmauer。

句子结构：Second，主句 it is past time for the CDU to reconsider + appositive rigid Brandmauer。

关键词："past time"（ overdue）、"reconsider"（ reconsider）。

表达方式：past time recipe for overdue discussion.

为什么这样写： introduces second major recommendation.

> **原句 36:** That does not mean kneeling before extremists; the AfD’s stated goal is to destroy the CDU, not to work with it.

中文理解：这不 implies kneeling extremists；AfD expressly goal destroy CDU not working with it。

句子结构：first clause That does not mean kneeling before extremists；semicolon second independent clause.

关键词："kneeling before extremists"（ appeasement）、"stated goal".

表达方式：semicolon distinguishes bold-but not-kneeling.

为什么这样写： balances scrutiny vs appeasement concerns.

> **原句 37:** And the CDU should not enable the entry to government of a party that opposes so much of what it stands for.

中文理解： and CDU should not enable entry government of party that opposes so many of its core stances。

句子结构：主句 “the CDU should not enable” + object the entry to government of a party that opposes so much … (relative clause)。

关键词："enable the entry to government"（ let into government）、"opposes so much of what it stands for".

## 长难句专项

### 1. 不定式作主语长句

> The sentence: (Quote above #2)
> For the radicals of the Alternative for Germany (AfD) to secure 44% of the vote in Saxony-Anhalt, one of Germany’s 16 states, has shocked a country that built its post-war identity on the rejection of extremes.

解剖：主语为不定式短语 “For … to secure …”（表达事实冲击）；主句谓语 “has shocked”；宾语 “a country that built its post-war identity on the rejection of extremes”。
要点：不定式 here 不表达未来，而是把“事件”名词化；“that built” provides causal-historical explanation；“on the rejection of extremes” 介词短语 signifies foundational value.
可学 pattern: For X to do Y has shocked a country that Z.

### 2. 定语从句中宾语 tagged with "that" + "its intelligence"

> Quote #3: A party that employs unrepentant former neo-Nazis and has been formally decreed “extremist” by Germany’s intelligence service is on the cusp of becoming the first populist-right group to run a state since the second world war.

解剖：主语 “A party that employs ... and has been decreed ...”；谓语 “is on the cusp of becoming”; 宾语 “the first populist-right group to run a state since ...”。
Grammar: 非限制? Actually restricts party definition. Coupling “employs neo-Nazis” + “intelligence decreed extremist” compounds disqualification.
Takeaway: legislation in quotes to signal official negative evaluation.

### 3. complex conditional in second policy

> Quote #38: But if the CDU signalled that it would not dogmatically exclude future co-operation with a party that shed its most radical ideas and politicians, it could eventually help boost the (relative) moderates within the AfD, some of whom are uneasy about their extreme comrades.

解剖：条件从句内含 that-clause and relative that; 结果主句 infinitive help + boost.  “some of whom” nonrestrictive further nuance.
Key: conditional diplomacy proposing internal party evolution; the 'if' is hypothetical and hedged.

### 4. Short awareness sentence

> Quote #45: His approval rating stands at just 13%, and the 70-year-old chancellor shows no sign of understanding why.

解剖：paratactic conjunction; first numeric estimate, second attribution of leader's blindness; age mention confirms temporal tenure/stasis.
Pattern: figure + psychological judgement.
