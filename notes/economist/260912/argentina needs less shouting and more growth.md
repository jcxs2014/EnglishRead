# Argentina needs less shouting and more growth（精读分析）

## 概览

**来源**：The Economist, Leaders, 2026-09-12

**栏目**：Leaders（社评）

**副题**：无（Economist 领袖社评无副题）

**主题**：本文为米莱三年执政评功论过。先承认他压低通胀（161%→34%）与恢复增长、贫困率下降三分之一；继而指出其工作未竟：2027 年 10 月大选、支持率低迷、增长缓慢且不均、实际工资低于上任时、正式就业消失、民众仍苦。文章认为米莱多数经济政策方向正确，但应把重心从继续压通胀转向促增长；更自由派的措施（废除资本管制、央行停止汇率干预、适度宽松货币支持企业信贷）可形成良性循环。又警告 Falklands 炒作、粗暴攻击记者、党内腐败等政治短板，要求严管 ranks 并以同理心回应改革阵痛。结尾以"平衡难于 bombast"收束。

**结构列表**：
1. 成绩单：通胀、增长、贫困
2. 未竟：支持率、增长质量、就业与工资、Perón 回潮风险
3. 方向正确但重心应转向增长
4. 更自由派的药方：资本管制、汇率、货币、税收与基建
5. 政治短板：Falklands、攻击记者、党内腐败
6. 同理心与平衡收尾

**段落脉络表格**：

| 段 | 主题 | 核心内容 |
|----|------|---------|
| P1 | 成绩单 | 高 inflation 压至 34%；第二年增长；贫困降三分之一 |

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：成绩单

> **原句 1:** JAVIER MILEI swept to power three years ago as a libertarian tub-thumper, promising brutal cuts to public spending in order to tame inflation and free the Argentine economy.

**中文理解**：哈维尔·米莱三年前以自由主义 tub-thumper（半桶响）之姿横扫上台，承诺以残暴的公共支出削减压低通胀、解放阿根廷经济。

**句子结构**：主语 JAVIER MILEI + swept to power（上台）+ as 本语 + 伴随式 promising... + in order to 目的状语。

**关键词**：swept to power（横扫上台）、libertarian tub-thumper（自由主义响炮手）、brutal cuts（残暴削减）、tame inflation（压低通胀）

**表达方式**：tub-thumper 的贬义色彩把米莱描绘成煽动型人物，与之后"proved them wrong"形成落差。

**为什么这样写**：以人物素描开场，先承认其最初形象激进而不被看好。

> **原句 2:** Many analysts doubted that this aggressive, wild-haired man could possibly succeed.

**中文理解**：许多分析师怀疑这个好斗、满头乱发的人究竟能不能成功。

**句子结构**：Many analysts doubted + that 从句；could possibly succeed。

**关键词**：wild-haired（满头乱发）、possibly succeed（可能成功）

**表达方式**：wild-haired 的身体化描述把个人外表 tied to 争议。

**为什么这样写**：设下怀疑靶子，为下句"proved them wrong"蓄势。

> **原句 3:** Mr Milei proved them wrong: annual inflation has fallen from 161% in the month before he took office to 34% in July; the economy is on track to grow for a second year in a row, a rarity in Argentina since the end of the commodity boom in 2008.

**中文理解**：米莱证明他们错了：年通胀已从上任前一个月的 161% 降到 7 月的 34%；经济有望连续第二年增长——这是自 2008 年大宗商品 boom 结束以来阿根廷的罕见之事。

**句子结构**：proved them wrong: 冒号引出两组证据；has fallen from...to...；is on track to grow；a rarity in Argentina since... 同位语。

**关键词**：proved them wrong（证明他们错了）、annual inflation（年通胀）、on track to grow（有望增长）、a rarity（罕见之事）、commodity boom（大宗商品 boom）

**表达方式**：冒号后的两组数据以 from...to... 的量化 rhetoric 占据主动。

**为什么这样写**：用身分数据回击怀疑论，奠定"功"半边。

> **原句 4:** With inflation curbed, the share of Argentines who are poor has fallen by a third.

**中文理解**：通胀得到抑制后，贫困阿根廷人的比例下降了三分之一。

**句子结构**：With inflation curbed（with 绝对结构/条件）、the share...has fallen by a third。

**关键词**：inflation curbed（通胀受抑）、fallen by a third（下降三分之一）

**表达方式**：With 开头的绝对结构把"通胀压低"作为发放贫困改善的前奏。

**为什么这样写**：completing 成绩单金句，引出"work not complete"的转折。

**段落逻辑：** 横扫上台的 libertarian tub-thumper + 怀疑论者 → proved them wrong: 通胀 161%→34% + 连续增长 + 贫困降三分之一

### 第 2 段：未竟

> **原句 5:** But his work is not complete.

**中文理解**：但他的工作并未完成。

**句子结构**：But 转折 + is not complete。

**关键词**：not complete（未完成）

**表达方式**：短句断言式进入"过"半邊。

**为什么这样写**：节奏反转，引出反对面。

> **原句 6:** Argentines will vote in a general election in October 2027.

**中文理解**：阿根廷人将在 2027 年 10 月进行大选投票。

**句子结构**：will vote in a general election in...

**关键词**：general election（大选）、October 2027

**表达方式**：具体日期的硬性锚点把 abstract 风险具象化。

**为什么这样写**：提出选举时间表，把支持率问题 tied to 选战。

> **原句 7:** The president’s approval ratings are near their lowest point of his term.

**中文理解**：总统的支持率已接近其本届任期内的最低点。

**句子结构**：are near their lowest point of...

**关键词**：approval ratings（支持率）、lowest point（最低点）

**表达方式**：lowest point 的湖底修辞强调危境。

**为什么这样写**：把政治资本的 depleted 状态抛出。

> **原句 8:** Although the economy is growing, that growth is slow and uneven.

**中文理解**：虽然经济在增长，但那种增长缓慢且不均衡。

**句子结构**：Although 让步从句 + 主句。

**关键词**：slow and uneven（缓慢且不均）

**表达方式**：slow and uneven 的双形容词节奏。

**为什么这样写**：成績单与现实落差的 reconciling 句。

> **原句 9:** Real wages are still lower than when he took office; formal jobs have been disappearing.

**中文理解**：实际工资仍低于他上任时的水平；正式就业一直在消失。

**句子结构**：分号并列：are lower than + have been disappearing。

**关键词**：Real wages（实际工资）、formal jobs（正式就业）、disappearing（正在消失）

**表达方式**：分号的并列 syntaxis 把两組 hardship 数据並置。

**为什么这样写**：指出民眾痛感的 material basis。

> **原句 10:** Many people are still struggling.

**中文理解**：许多人仍在挣扎度日。

**句子结构**：Many people are still struggling。

**关键词**：still struggling（仍在挣扎）

**表达方式**：still 的时间拖拽感强化持续性 pain。

**为什么这样写**：把數據 humanize 为 lived experience。

> **原句 11:** Polls suggest that voters have a worse view of Mr Milei than of several champions of Peronism, the left-wing movement that favours state meddling over markets and created the economic disaster that he inherited.

**中文理解**：民调显示，选民对米莱的看法比对几位 Peronism champion（旗手）还要差——Peronism 是那个重国营干预轻市场、并制造了他继承的经济灾难的左翼运动。

**句子结构**：Polls suggest that + voters have a worse view of A than of B + 同位语 the left-wing movement that... which created...。

**关键词**：champions of Peronism（Peronism 的旗手）、state meddling over markets（国营干预压过市场）、created the economic disaster he inherited（制造他继承之灾难）

**表达方式**：worse view than... 的 surprising 比较把对手优势具象化；"state meddling" 的 pejorative 词表露出 editing position。

**为什么这样写**：强调 Peronism 回潮的真实可能性而非 strawman。

> **原句 12:** The prospect of a return to the past is alarming for Argentina, and for the fate of the world’s most important experiment in economic liberalism.

**中文理解**：重返过去的前景令人警觉——不仅为了阿根廷，也为了全世界最重要这一 economic liberalism 实验的命运。

**句子结构**：The prospect of A is alarming for X, and for Y.

**关键词**：prospect of a return to the past（重返过去之 prospect）、economic liberalism（经济自由主义）、most important experiment（最重要实验）

**表达方式**：global framing 把单国政治 saga 抬升为 ideology test case。

**为什么这样写**：结尾危言，解释 stakes。

**段落逻辑：** work not complete → 2027 大选 → 支持率谷底 + growing but slow/uneven → wages lower + jobs disappearing + still struggling → 民调：米萊比 Peronists 更不受歡迎 → 重返过去的 prospect alarming Argentina and world liberalism experiment

### 第 3 段：重心转向增长

> **原句 13:** Most of Mr Milei’s economic policies are the right ones: his zealous commitment to budget surpluses remains crucial, he is correct that outright money-printing is the root of Argentina’s woes and he should continue slashing enterprise-shackling regulations.

**中文理解**：米莱的多数经济政策都是对的：他对财政盈余的热切 commitment 仍关键；他指出 outright money-printing 是阿根廷 woes 的 root，是对的；他还应继续 slashing enterprise-shackling regulations。

**句子结构**：Most...are the right ones: 三重 appositive claim（commitment...; he is correct that...; he should continue...）。

**关键词**：zealous commitment（热切 commitment）、budget surpluses（财政盈余）、outright money-printing（直接印钞）、root of woes（woes 之 root）、enterprise-shackling regulations（束缚企业的 regulation）

**表达方式**：enterprise-shackling 的连字符复合形容词加速 editor's lexicon。

**为什么这样写**：先授勋再 pivot，建立 credibility for pivot。

> **原句 14:** But today Argentines worry more about jobs than inflation.

**中文理解**：但如今阿根廷人更担心工作而非通胀。

**句子结构**：But today...worry more about A than B.

**关键词**：worry more about jobs than inflation（更忧就业而非通胀）

**表达方式**：今昔 concern 的 reversal 定义 policy priority shift。

**为什么这样写**：全文 pivot 句。

> **原句 15:** Now is the time to build on the foundations he has laid and focus more on growth, even if that means a slightly slower decline in inflation.

**中文理解**：现在是在他已建 foundation 上更进一步、把重心转向 growth 的时候，即便这意味着通胀下降略慢一些。

**句子结构**：Now is the time to X and Y, even if that means Z.

**关键词**：build on the foundations（在 foundation 上加建）、slightly slower decline in inflation（通胀下降略慢）

**表达方式**：even if that means 的 concessive framing 把 trade-off explicit。

**为什么这样写**：把 growth-over-inflation 的 trade-off 公开化，Economist 的 signposting 习惯。

**段落逻辑：** policies right: surpluses crucial + money-printing is root + slash regs → But today worry jobs > inflation → Now time to build foundations + focus growth even slower inflation decline

### 第 4 段：药方

> **原句 16:** Happily, the most obvious way to fix this is to be more libertarian, not less.

**中文理解**：好消息是，最明显的 fix 途径是"更自由派"而非"少一点"。

**句子结构**：Happily, the most obvious way to fix this is to be more X, not less.

**关键词**：Happily（可喜的是）、more libertarian, not less（更自由派而非更少）

**表达方式**：Happily 的 editorial reassurance + not less 的 antithesis 强调 directionality。

**为什么这样写**：把 growth pivot 跑回到 ideology mission statement。

> **原句 17:** Mr Milei should ditch Argentina’s remaining capital controls and the central bank should stop intervening in financial markets to try surreptitiously to prop up the peso.

**中文理解**：米莱应 ditch 阿根廷 residual capital controls；央行也应停止在 financial markets intervention 以 surreptitiously prop up the peso。

**句子结构**：and 连接 two should clauses：ditch X + stop intervening...to try surreptitiously to prop up...

**关键词**：ditch capital controls（废除 capital controls）、intervening in financial markets（干预金融市场）、surreptitiously prop up the peso（偷偷托举 peso）

**表达方式**：surreptitiously 的 loaded adverb 把 intervention 描成 dishonest manipulation。

**为什么这样写**：政策 recipe 之首；把 honest market 价格发现作为 goal。

> **原句 18:** That would imply marginally looser monetary policy, which would help boost lending to business.

**中文理解**：这将 imply marginally looser monetary policy，进而 help boost 对企业的 lending。

**句子结构**：That would imply... , which would help boost...

**关键词**：marginally looser monetary policy（边际宽松货政）、boost lending to business（ boosting 企业 lending）

**表达方式**：marginally 的 cautiou qualifiers 平衡 certitude。

**为什么这样写**：把 deregulation 与 credit supply 的 transmission mechanism spell out。

> **原句 19:** Faster growth would improve the tax take, enabling Mr Milei to further cut Argentina’s absurd export taxes, and perhaps even other taxes, without endangering his prized budget surplus.

**中文理解**：更快的增长会 improve the tax take，使米莱 pouvoir further cut 阿根廷 absurd export taxes—and perhaps even other taxes—without endangering its prized budget surplus。

**句子结构**：Faster growth would improve... , enabling..., ... , without...。

**关键词**：tax take（税收总量）、absurd export taxes（荒唐的出口税）、prized budget surplus（珍视的 fiscal surplus）

**表达方式**：prized 的 possessive adjective 提升 surplus 为 trophy。

**为什么这样写**：拼出 virtuous cycle 的 arithmetic。

> **原句 20:** That could set off a virtuous cycle.

**中文理解**：这可能触发一个 virtuous cycle。

**句子结构**：That could set off a virtuous cycle.

**关键词**：virtuous cycle（良性循环）

**表达方式**：virtuous cycle 经济学 formula 作政策 telos。

**为什么这样写**：把 package 以 macro concept 封装。

> **原句 21:** So would further cutting subsidies, especially for transport, and instead reviving investment in roads and rail.

**中文理解**：进一步削减 subsidies—尤其是交通补贴—并 instead reviving 对道路与铁路的投资，也会如此（trigger virtuous cycle）。

**句子结构**：So would [further cutting subsidies...and instead reviving investment...]。

**关键词**：cutting subsidies（削减补贴）、reviving investment in roads and rail（恢复道铁投资）

**表达方式**：So would 的倒装 rhetorics 回溯上句 cycle claim。

**为什么这样写**：把 fiscal reallocation 作为 second cycle lever。

**段落逻辑：** happier fix = more libertarian not less → ditch capital controls + stop propping peso → looser monetary → business lending → faster growth → tax take → cut export taxes → virtuous cycle; plus cut subsidies → roads/rail

### 第 5 段：政治短板

> **原句 22:** There are signs his government is edging in this direction.

**中文理解**：有迹象表明其政府正在 edged in this direction。

**句子结构**：There are signs + 主语 + is edging。

**关键词**：edging in this direction（正朝此方向 edged）

**表达方式**：edging 的 cautious verb 配之前 marginally。

**为什么这样写**：给 credit to partial progress。

> **原句 23:** Yet in an interview with *The Economist* Mr Milei was unwilling to accept that his efforts to curb inflation have come at any cost to growth.

**中文理解**：然而在与《经济学家》的访談中，米萊不愿承认其 anti-inflation efforts 已经以 any cost to growth 为代价。

**句子结构**：Yet + in interview with... + was unwilling to accept that...

**关键词**：unwilling to accept（不愿承认）、came at any cost to growth（以增长为代价）

**表达方式**：Economist 自引的 interview 作 sources upgrade。

**为什么这样写**：指出 blind spot: denial of trade-off。

> **原句 24:** He is posturing over the Falkland Islands to fire up Argentine voters, a distraction from economic problems for them and, more troublingly, for him.

**中文理解**：他 over Falkland Islands posturing to fire up 阿根廷选民——这对他们是 economic problems 的 distraction，更 troublingly for him too。

**句子结构**：He is posturing over X to Y, a distraction from Z for them and, more troublingly, for him.

**关键词**：posturing（摆姿态）、Falkland Islands（福克兰群岛）、fire up voters（煽动选民）、a distraction（ distraction）、more troublingly（更 trouble）

**表达方式**：Falklands 作 rhetorical weapon 冷却 economic focus risk。

**为什么这样写**：把 populist diversion 打 into policy critique。

> **原句 25:** And he is raging ever more crudely at his perceived critics, above all Argentine journalists.

**中文理解**：他还对 perceived critics—尤其是阿根廷 journalists—raging ever more crudely。

**句子结构**：And he is raging ever more crudely at X, above all Y.

**关键词**：raging crudely（粗暴 rant）、perceived critics（perceived critics）、above all（尤其）

**表达方式**：raging 的 animal register 刻画 anger management failure。

**为什么这样写**：把 rhetoric coarsening 提入 leadership critique。

> **原句 26:** He need not abandon the arresting style that helped get him elected.

**中文理解**：他 need not abandon the arresting style that helped get him elected。

**句子结构**：He need not abandon A that...

**关键词**：arresting style（抓人 style/夺目风格）、helped get him elected（助其当选）

**表达方式**：arresting 的 police-word double entendre（arresting = 引人注意也含逮捕 flavor）。

**为什么这样写**：concede style value，avoid total dismissal。

> **原句 27:** But he should focus on his political rivals, many of whom might take Argentina back to wild spending and loopy red tape.

**中文理解**：但应 focus on political rivals，many of whom might take Argentina back to wild spending and loopy red tape。

**句子结构**：But he should focus on X, many of whom might...

**关键词**：political rivals（政治对手）、wild spending（疯狂 spending）、loopy red tape（荒谬 red tape）

**表达方式**：wild spending / loopy red tape 的漫画化 phrase 为对手勾勒 villain frame。

**为什么这样写**：把 aggressiveness target pivot — from journalist to rivals。

**段落逻辑：** edging in direction; but unwilling to accept cost; Falklands posturing; raging at critics (journalists); need not abandon style; but focus rivals who might wild spending & loopy red tape

### 第 6 段：党内腐败

> **原句 28:** The difficulty is that the Peronists are not in power, and Mr Milei’s government is vulnerable to allegations of corruption.

**中文理解**：麻烦在于 Peronists not in power，而米莱 government vulnerable to allegations of corruption。

**句子结构**：The difficulty is that A, and B.

**关键词**：Peronists not in power、vulnerable to allegations of corruption（易受腐败指控）

**表达方式**：The difficulty is that 的 editorial diagnosis framing。

**为什么这样写**：解释 caste rhetorics 的 handicap。

> **原句 29:** It is hard to rail against the rotten “caste” when your allies are facing charges of graft of their own.

**中文理解**：当你的 allies 自己也 facing charges of graft，再 rail against rotten "caste" 就 difficult。

**句子结构**：It is hard to X when Y.

**关键词**：rotten "caste"（rotten "caste"：米莱惯用词，指建制 elitist）、charges of graft（graft charges）

**表达方式**：caste 上引号显示 borrowed rhetoric，反向 haunt 自己人。

**为什么这样写**：把 anti-caste credibility 管 tied to team cleanliness。

> **原句 30:** So Mr Milei must take the conduct within his ranks more seriously.

**中文理解**：所以米莱必须 take the conduct within ranks more seriously。

**句子结构**：So + must + take...more seriously.

**关键词**：conduct within his ranks（ranks 内 conduct）、more seriously（更 seriousness）

**表达方式**：must 的 obligation line。

**为什么这样写**：prescribe remedy with clarity。

> **原句 31:** That will make it easier to warn against the dangers of a return to self-dealing politics.

**中文理解**：这样更 easy to warn against dangers of a return to self-dealing politics。

**句子结构**：That will make it easier to warn against...

**关键词**：self-dealing politics（self-dealing politics）

**表达方式**：self-dealing 的 legalistic frame for corruption risks。

**为什么这样写**：link cleanliness → credible warnings → anti-Peronist stance.

**段落逻辑：** Peronists not in power → Milei gov vulnerable to corruption allegations → hard to rail caste if allies face graft → must take ranks conduct seriously → easier to warn self-dealing politics

### 第 7 段：同理心

> **原句 32:** And instead of implying that Argentines who complain about the pain of his reforms are feeble, Mr Milei should try a little empathy.

**中文理解**：并且 instead of implying 改革阵痛的阿根廷人 feeble，米莱应 try a little empathy。

**句子结构**：And instead of implying that..., Mr Milei should try...

**关键词**：implying...are feeble（暗示他们弱）、try a little empathy（试试 empathy）

**表达方式**：feeble 的 disparagement 对比 empathy 的 medicine.

**为什么这样写**：把 political empathy as complement not surrender.

> **原句 33:** Carrying on with a much-needed economic overhaul does not preclude showing that he understands the difficulties that his policies are imposing upon ordinary Argentines in the short term.

**中文理解**：Continuing much-needed economic overhaul does not preclude showing that 他 understands his policies impose 于 ordinary Argentines 的 difficulties (short term)。

**句子结构**：Carrying on with... does not preclude showing that he understands...

**关键词**：much-needed economic overhaul（必要 economic overhaul）、does not preclude（不 preclude）、short term（短期）

**表达方式**：does not preclude 的 logical frame把 cohesion 强调（reform continuity & empathy coexist）。

**为什么这样写**：closing political prescription before final synthesis.

**段落逻辑：** instead of implying complainers feeble → try empathy; carrying on overhaul does not preclude understanding short-term difficulties

### 第 8 段：平衡

> **原句 34:** Balance, between pulverising inflation and boosting growth, and between empathy and aggression, is harder than the bombast which won Mr Milei election.

**中文理解**：Balance—between pulverising inflation and boosting growth, and between empathy and aggression—比 won Mr Milei election 的 bombast 难得多。

**句子结构**：Balance, [between A and B, and between C and D], is harder than E which...

**关键词**：Balance（平衡）、pulverising inflation（pulverising inflation）、bombast（bombast）

**表达方式**：appositive of paired contrasts 把 dossier 全文 themes reconcile; bombast 回呼 opening tub-thumper。

**为什么这样写**：把 entire editorial thesis 压成 one aphorism。

> **原句 35:** But the fate of his project, which has inspired free-marketers worldwide, may depend on finding it.

**中文理解**：但是 his project 的 fate—which has inspired free-marketers worldwide—may depend on finding it（平衡）。

**句子结构**：But the fate of X, which has inspired Y, may depend on finding it.

**关键词**：inspired free-marketers worldwide（inspire worldwide free-marketers）、may depend on finding it（取決於 finding it）

**表达方式**：finding it 的 pronoun callback 收束全文 motif。

**为什么这样写**：global stakes + conditional uncertainty 的 final note。

**段落逻辑：** Balance (inflation/growth, empathy/aggression) harder than bombast that won election; project fate has inspired free-marketers worldwide may depend on finding it

## 长难句专项

### 长难句 1

> Polls suggest that voters have a worse view of Mr Milei than of several champions of Peronism, the left-wing movement that favours state meddling over markets and created the economic disaster that he inherited.

**句子结构**：Polls suggest that + voters have a worse view of A than of B, + 同位语 B 定义 + that 从句。

### 长难句 2

> Mr Milei should ditch Argentina’s remaining capital controls and the central bank should stop intervening in financial markets to try surreptitiously to prop up the peso.

**句子结构**：and 连接两 should clauses：ditch X + stop intervening...to try surreptitiously to prop up...。

**解剖**：并列义务句把 fiscal-monetary package 压成 one legislative prescription；surreptitiously 的 moral framing 对干预的(policy) dishonesty。

### 长难句 3

> Carrying on with a much-needed economic overhaul does not preclude showing that he understands the difficulties that his policies are imposing upon ordinary Argentines in the short term.

**句子结构**：Carrying on with... does not preclude showing that he understands...
