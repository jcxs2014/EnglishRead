## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：实验室就是厨房——生活与科研的边界消融

> **原句 1:** Whenever in later life a fresh idea struck him, Cyril Hilsum would leave the dinner table and wander to his lab.

**中文理解**：希尔斯姆在晚年的生活中，每有新想法袭来，就会离开饭桌，径直走向他的实验室。

**句子结构**：`Whenever` 引导时间状语从句（whenever + 从句）+ 主句 `Cyril Hilsum would leave... and wander...`；`would leave... and wander...` 用 would + 动词原形表达习惯性动作（过去习惯）；`to his lab` 是 wander 的终点宾语。

**关键词**：whenever（每次 whenever 引导时间/让步狀语），fresh idea（新想法），struck him（袭来 / 涌上心头），wander to（走向，漫步行）。

**表达方式**：`a fresh idea struck him` —— 把 idea 当作"打击"强行使能动性；`would + 动词` 表示习惯性过去，比 used to 更自然。

**为什么这样写**：开篇第一句即立人物特性：一个" ideas 驱动，吃饭到一半就跑去做实验"的科学家。动词 wander 有漫不经心又理所当然之意，暗示科研对他像呼吸一样自然。

> **原句 2:** He didn't have far to go.

**中文理解**：他不必走多远。

**句子结构**：简单句 `S + didn't have + far to go`，have + to-doing 远动表达 "不必走远""路程不长"；far 作宾语补足。

**关键词**：far to go（还要走的路/距离）。

**表达方式**：极短句承接上句，制造悬念——"不远" implying "就在家"。

**为什么这样写**：短句做过渡，引出下句"实验室 literal ists 在家的各个角落"的惊异 reveal。

> **原句 3:** Part of it was in the garage, part in the scullery, of his house in Pinner, north-west of London.

**中文理解**：他的实验室一部分在车库，一部分在 Scullery（洗碗间/炊事间），位于伦敦西北郊 Pinner 的房子里。

**句子结构**：`Part of it was in X, part in Y`——Part 的省略主语 it，第二分句省略 `was`；`of his house in...` 是 in Pinner 的后置修饰，修饰 part；`north-west of London` 是 Pinner 的方位。

**关键词**：scullery（洗碗间/炊事间，罕见词），Pinner（伦敦西北郊地名），north-west of London。

**表达方式**：`part of it was in..., part in...` 的 parallel ellipsis（省略）；方位短语 `north-west of London` 替代 "a suburb of London"。

**为什么这样写**：列举车库+洗碗间使荒诞感具象——科学家把整屋当实验室。Pinner 作 London 副都，写 geographic grounding，avoid generic "in London"。

> **原句 4:** The rest was in the kitchen.

**中文理解**：剩下的在厨房。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（`The rest was in the kitchen`），the rest = the remainder，was in = be + 位置。

**关键词**：the rest（其余部分），kitchen（厨房）。

**表达方式**：极短句做 climax，把 laboratory 与 kitchen 重合。

**为什么这样写**：在 garage/scullery 后仍 escalate 到 kitchen——最高撞色； sentence economy 也 mirroring his "everything had its place" ethos.

> **原句 5:** One counter was his electronics bench, with an oscilloscope, a pair of waveform generators, a spectrum analyser and a tray of voltage dividers looped to each other and to a soldering station with a maze of cables.

**中文理解**：一个台面是他的电子实验台，上面有示波器、一对波形发生器、频谱分析仪，以及一托盘彼此互连并连到焊台的电压分压器，缠绕着迷宫般的线缆。

**句子结构**：主 `One counter was his electronics bench` + `with...` 伴随成分；`a tray of voltage dividers looped to each other and to a soldering station with a maze of cables` 是后置修饰 tray。

**关键词**：oscilloscope（示波器），waveform generator（波形发生器），spectrum analyser（频谱分析仪），voltage divider（电压分压器），soldering station（焊台），maze of cables（电缆迷宫）。

**表达方式**：enumerate 4 categories of instruments; stack prepositional chains (`looped to each other and to a soldering station with...`) to convey cluttered complexity; "maze of cables" metaphoric.

**为什么这样写**：工程师细节累积——具体仪器名建立权威，heap of nouns conveys the lived-in clutter; parallel to last sentence where "everything had its place."

> **原句 6:** On the facing counter stood a three-roll mill for working conductive powders, a precision balance, a small vacuum chamber and many labelled jars of carbon black and nickel powder.

**中文理解**：对面的台面上立着一台三辊研磨机，用于加工导电粉末，还有一台精密天平、一个小真空室，以及许多装有炭黑和镍粉的贴标签罐瓶。

**句子结构**：倒装句 `On the facing counter stood A, B, C and D`；`for working conductive powders` 修饰 three-roll mill；`labelled jars of...` 是名词短语。

**关键词**：three-roll mill（三辊研磨机），conductive powders（导电粉末），precision balance（精密天平），vacuum chamber（真空室），carbon black（炭黑），nickel powder（镍粉）。

**表达方式**：fronted location (`On the facing counter`) + inversion for scene-setting; list sealed with "many labelled jars" showing meticulousness.

**为什么这样写**：与上句 electronics bench 对照——conducting vs measuring; "labelled jars" 落脚到 his meticulous order. He's as much a materials scientist as an electronics man, stated by inventory.

> **原句 7:** Everything had its place here apart from kitchen stuff; but he dined, and lunched too, mostly on red wine, ready meals and chocolate.

**中文理解**：这里一切东西都在其位，除了厨具；但他吃饭（含午饭）主要吃红酒、方便食品和巧克力。

**句子结构**：`Everything had its place here apart from kitchen stuff` + `; but` adversative + clause `he dined, and lunched too, mostly on X, Y and Z`；`dined` 与 `lunched` 由 and 连接，too 强调 inclusive diet.

**关键词**：had its place（各归其位），apart from（除...之外），ready meals（方便食品/速食）。

**表达方式**：`dined, and lunched too` —— 逗号+and 提升时间轻重；`mostly on` 共用三宾； kitchen stuff vs red wine, ready meals —— anticlimax of a healthy-scientist trope.

**为什么这样写**：幽默解构"秩序科学家=健康生活" cliché。 kitchen's absence from its own counter is the comic point; his diet is anti-conventional to mirror his curiosity-driven life.

> **原句 8:** Fruit and veg he disdained.

**中文理解**：蔬果他都 disdain（鄙视、不屑）。

**句子结构**：OSV 倒装：`Fruit and veg` 是宾语前置，`he` 主语，`disdained` 谓语。动词 disdain = verb of treating with contempt.

**关键词**：disdained（鄙视/不屑）。

**表达方式**：宾语前置（fronting）强调 "Fruit and veg" 这个 hate对象，动词 disdain elevates colloquialism "veg" — 一种 deadpan British humor.

**为什么这样写**：一行短语定义 eccentricity; OSV rhythm staccato. 文章 thus sketching a character consistent with his practical, no-frills self.

> **原句 9:** He lived very healthily on a diet of curiosity, hard work and endless ideas.

**中文理解**：他依靠好奇心、勤奋与永不枯竭的想法"活得很健康"——一种精神食粮的"健康生活"。

**句子结构**：主 `He lived very healthily` + `on a diet of X, Y and Z`（diet of = 以...为食/日常）。 parallel 三名词。

**关键词**：diet of X：（以...构成的日常/ aliment），curiosity, hard work, endless ideas。

**表达方式**：literal "diet" 与上句 "red wine, ready meals and chocolate" 互文； healthily shifted from physical to intellectual. 三名词 parallel climax.

**为什么这样写**：Closing sentence of paragraph redefines "healthy"; punchline resolves potential concern (he ate junk food yet "lived healthily"). Sets thematic motif: curiosity as sustenance.

### 第 2 段：70 多项专利，液晶显示让他举世闻名

> **原句 10:** In all he had over 70 patents to his name.

**中文理解**：总共他名下有 70 多项专利。

**句子结构**：`In all` = altogether/ in total；`he had over 70 patents to his name` = 他名下 patented。`to one's name` 作 have 的宾语补足补语.

**关键词**：In all（总计/共），to one's name（以某人名义/某人拥有的）。

**表达方式**：`over 70 patents` (<|constrain|> 70) vs exact figure deliberately vague; `to his name` idiom gives human possession.

**为什么这样写**：Quantified credibility 引入 careers. "over 70" avoids pedantry; opens tally before pivoting to headline achievement.

> **原句 11:** The last, filed when he was 97, was for ink with sensors that conducted electricity.

**中文理解**：最后一项，是他 97 岁时申请的，是一种带导电传感器的油墨。

**句子结构**：`The last, filed when he was 97, was for X` — `filed when he was 97` 是 The last 的非限制性定从缩写；`was for` 表用作/针对；`ink with sensors that conducted electricity` 后置 attrib.

**关键词**：filed（已申请/已递交），97（ Paddington... aged 97）。

**表达方式**：dash flourish `(filed when he was 97)` inserted mid-sentence creates awe at longevity. "ink with sensors" compact phrase showing range.

**为什么这样写**：穿插 "97 岁" 让人物 vitality 与 longevity concrete; reinforces "endless ideas" motif. Last patent as synecdoche for lifelong innovating.

> **原句 12:** His lasting fame, though, came from liquid-crystal displays.

**中文理解**：然而，他 enduring 的 fame 来自液晶显示。

**句子结构**：主 `His lasting fame came from liquid-crystal displays`；`though` 句中插入，对抗 over-70-patents 的 breadth with the singular fame. `liquid-crystal displays` as noun phrase (also abbrev LCD).

**关键词**：lasting fame（ enduring fame），though（然而），liquid-crystal display（液晶显示，LCD）。

**表达方式**：`lasting` vs. instantaneous; `though` conjunction adverb shift; "came from" verb of origin not "was about."

**为什么这样写**：Pivot from quantity (70 patents) to quality (one fame). `though` marks humility: patents many, renown one. Foregrounds LCD for the article's thesis.

> **原句 13:** In the 1970s he realised that these could replace the bulky, power-hungry TVs of old, with their bulging cathode-ray tubes and curved screens, with slim flat-screen models that could be hung on a wall.

**中文理解**：1970 年代他意识到，液晶技术可以取代过去笨重高耗的电视——那些有着凸出的阴极射线管和弧面屏幕的电视——代之以可以挂在墙上的纤薄平板屏机型。

**句子结构**：`In the 1970s he realised that ...` +宾语从句；`replace A, with B, with C`：第一 with = with their..., 第二 with = with slim...（A 替换中保留/去除 old parts，B 新装入）。`that could be hung on a wall` 定从。

**关键词**：realised that（意识到），bulky（笨重），power-hungry（高耗电），cathode-ray tube/CRT（阴极射线管），curved screens（曲面屏幕），slim flat-screen models（纤薄平板屏）。

**表达方式**：dual `with`: first describes old TV's ugliness, second new thinness → balanced contrast; "hung on a wall" domestic, evocative.

**为什么这样写**：Historical turning point statement: the core invention claim. Dual "with" constructs before/after elegantly. "Hung on a wall" makes technical leap visceral to lay reader.

> **原句 14:** He and his collaborators started small, with digital watches in 1974; but in no time flat screens were everywhere, in computer monitors, cockpit displays, departure boards and, especially, mobile phones.

**中文理解**：他与合作者从小处起步，1974 年是数字手表；但转眼间平板屏无处不在——电脑显示器、座舱显示、出发牌、尤其是手机。

**句子结构**：复合句 `He and collaborators started small, with X; but in no time Y were everywhere, in A, B, C and, especially, D`。`in no time` = very quickly; 列举 4 物品 + especially.

**关键词**：collaborators（合作者），started small（从小起步），in no time（转眼间），departure boards（航班/出发屏），especially（尤其）。

**表达方式**：`started small ... in no time` narrative acceleration; "especially" elevates mobile phones as ubiquity peak; semicolon joins.

**为什么这样写**：Timeline of diffusion; emphasis on phones signals his relevance to reader's own life. "Especially" nudges modern reader to look at pocket.

> **原句 15:** Sharper images took longer; but in the modern screen-bound age, he changed the world's way of seeing.

**中文理解**：清晰影像要更久才来；但在当今被屏幕捆绑的时代，他改变了全世界观看的方式。

**句子结构**：`Sharper images took longer` + `; but` + `in the modern screen-bound age, he changed the world's way of seeing.` 句中 `screen-bound age` 作时间状语名词短语。

**关键词**：Sharper images（更清晰的影像），screen-bound age（被屏幕捆绑的时代），way of seeing（观看的方式）。

**表达方式**：break with semicolon for temporal shift; "took longer" concessive; "changed the world's way of seeing" has Berenson-esque grandeur (John Berger echo).

**为什么这样写**：Closes paragraph with thesis: his impact transcends display hardware into perception itself. "Screen-bound age" ironically nails modern condition he engendered.

### 第 3 段：液晶稳定与可控的攻关

> **原句 16:** It had been difficult work.

**中文理解**：那是艰难的工作。

**句子结构**：`It had been + adj complement`，it 远指上段 LCD 的研究；过去完成时 had been 确认 retrospective.

**关键词**：difficult work（艰难的工作）。

**表达方式**：three-word sentence, past perfect for gravitas.

**为什么这样写**：Paragraph transitions from triumph to toil; minimal sentence slows pace before technical explanation.

> **原句 17:** Liquid crystals certainly behaved like liquids, because they flowed, but their molecules could keep a structure for a very brief time.

**中文理解**：液晶显然表现得像液体，因为它们会流动，但其分子却只能短暂保持结构。

**句子结构**：compound `A, because B, but C`；`because they flowed` causal; `could keep a structure for a very brief time` modal + duration.

**关键词**：liquid crystals（液晶），flowed（流动），molecules（分子），structure（结构），brief time（短暂）。

**表达方式**：because clause explains liquid likeness; but introduces paradox. Colloquial "certainly" softens technical claim.

**为什么这样写**：Define technical problem for lay audience without jargon. The paradox (liquid + structure) is the challenge—sets up next sentence.

> **原句 18:** It was up to him to stabilise them and induce them to work at room temperature.

**中文理解**：稳定它们并诱使它们在室温工作，这是他的责任/任务。

**句子结构**：`It was up to him to V` = it fell to him to...; `stabilise... and induce... to work at...` 两个并列不定式。

**关键词**：up to him（落到了他身上/他的责任），stabilise（稳定），induce（诱导/促成），room temperature（室温）。

**表达方式**：`It was up to him` agency; `induce them to work` formal verb choice.

**为什么这样写**：Personalizes the problem—he chose the path. "Room temperature" is crucial applicability criterion; dramatic tension.

> **原句 19:** In two years he and two colleagues, George Gray and Peter Raynes, produced and patented a whole new controllable family of them.

**中文理解**：两年内，他与两位同事 George Gray 与 Peter Raynes 研发并申请了专利，创造出一整个全新的、可控的液晶家族。

**句子结构**：`In two years S produced and patented X`；apposition `two colleagues, George Gray and Peter Raynes` (insert names); `a whole new controllable family of them` = family of liquid crystals.

**关键词**：colleagues（同事），produced and patented（研发并申请专利），controllable family（可控家族）。

**表达方式**："In two years" fronted duration as achievement marker; apposition names two collaborators; "whole new... family" grand scale.

**为什么这样写**：Resolution of problem in two sentences; naming Gray & Raynes gives credit (fairness ethos in para 9 foreshadowed). "Two years" contrasts with "took longer" images.

### 第 4 段：1962 年英国首枚半导体激光器

> **原句 20:** His second-proudest achievement was to build in 1962 Britain's first semiconductor laser, which involved getting gallium arsenide to behave.

**中文理解**：他第二引以为傲的成就是 1962 年建造英国第一枚半导体激光器，这需要让砷化镓" behave（ behaving）"。

**句子结构**：主 `His second-proudest achievement was to build X, which involved Y`；`in 1962` temporal within infinitive; `Britain's first` ranking; `which involved getting Z to behave`: which refers to achievement/act of building; getting A to behave = causing behaving.

**关键词**：second-proudest（第二引以为傲），semiconductor laser（半导体激光器），gallium arsenide（砷化镓），behave（ behaving/服帖）。

**表达方式**：`second-proudest` ranking implies there is a first (LCD); "getting gallium arsenide to behave" personifies material.

**为什么这样写**：Orders taxonomy of his achievements, making LCD #1; material personification conveys handling difficulty humorously.

> **原句 21:** This was extremely hard.

**中文理解**：这极其困难。

**句子结构**：demonstrative `This` + copula `was` + adj `extremely hard`.

**关键词**：extremely hard（极其困难）。

**表达方式**：three-word intensification after setup; "extremely" rather than "very."

**为什么这样写**：Short punch after long sentence; sets up anecdote to prove "extremely hard" wasn't hyperbole.

> **原句 22:** To make it he had to mix gallium with arsenic in a silica tube, heat it furiously, stick it in a shed and wait til morning.

**中文理解**：为了制作它，他必须把镓与砷在 silica 管中混合，猛烈加热，然后塞进小棚里等到早上。

**句子结构**：`To make it` infinitive of purpose; `he had to V1, V2, V3 and V4` series of verbs; last clause `wait til morning`.

**关键词**：silica tube（ silica/石英管），heat furiously（猛加热），shed（小棚/棚子），til morning（等到早上）。

**表达方式**：four-verb chain with escalating roughness (mix → heat → stick → wait); "furiously" anthropomorphizes heating; "stick it in a shed" informal.

**为什么这样写**：Conjures jury-rigged hack; language domestic ("shed") against sophisticated physics — theme of practical engineer persona.

> **原句 23:** By then it would have burst the tube asunder, and he would have to pick out the pieces.

**中文理解**：到那时 silica 管就会 burst asunder（炸得四分五裂），而他得把碎片挑出来。

**句子结构**：`By then it would have burst X asunder, and he would have to V`；would have burst = conditional future-in-past (inference/result); second clause would have to = obligation; `pick out the pieces`.

**关键词**：burst asunder（炸裂/裂开），pick out the pieces（挑出碎片）。

**表达方式**：`would have` not "had" — speculative/reported result; "asunder" archaic/poetic intensifier; "pick out the pieces" understated.

**为什么这样写**：Narrates iterative failure → success; "would have" signals repeated trials ("it" each time). "Pick out pieces" British understatement — deflates drama, builds character.

> **原句 24:** But now he had a compound whose electrons were finely tuned to convert electrical energy efficiently into light, making a laser so compact that it could be used with ordinary batteries in everyday devices: CD players, burglar alarms, speed-detection devices, LEDs, printers and scanners.

**中文理解**：但现在他得到了一种化合物，其电子被 finely tuned（精细调谐）以高效把电能转为 light，由此制成的激光器 so compact（如此 compact），能在日常设备中用普通电池：CD 播放器、防盗警报、测速器、LED、打印机和扫描仪。

**句子结构**：`But now he had a compound whose electrons were...` （whose 定从修饰 compound）；`to convert... into light` 不定时短语表 purpose；`making a laser so compact that...` 结果分词；`devices: list` 冒号引出 6 例。

**为什么这样写**： Reveals family-centered values; "rock" epitomizes partnership; memorializing daughter's promise (engineer) honors her. Quiet emotional close before pragmatic gender commentary.

> **原句 53:** She had been a promising engineer.

**中文理解**：她曾是 a promising engineer.

**句子结构**：`She had been + adj + noun`，past perfect indicating before death.

**关键词**：promising engineer（前途无量的工程师）。

**表达方式**：Simple eulogy; "promising" unfulfilled potential; adjectival placement.

**为什么这样写**：Memorial sentence; "promising" honors unfulfilled potential; supports para 9's respect for women engineers.

> **原句 54:** All through his career he had hired or promoted women equally with men.

**中文理解**：他 entire career 都 hire 或 promote 女性与 men equal（平等地）。

**句子结构**：`All through his career he had hired or promoted women equally with men.` — `All through` time phrase; `equally with men` comparative.

**关键词**：hired or promoted（雇佣或提拔），equally with men（与男性同样）。

**表达方式**：`All through` underscores consistency; `equally with` precise legal-ethical tone.

**为什么这样写**：Establishes egalitarian practice cred; sets up preference statement. Reinforces accountability ethos.

> **原句 55:** In fact, as scientists he preferred them.

**中文理解**：fact 上，作为 scientist，他 preferred them（women）.

**句子结构**：`In fact, as scientists he preferred them.` — `as scientists` scope; `In fact` intensifies.

**关键词**：In fact（事实上），as scientists（作为科学家），preferred them（更偏好他们）。

**表达方式**：`In fact` reversal/intensification; "as scientists" scope prevents reading as generic.

**为什么这样写**：Turns equality into positive preference; sets dichotomy in next sentence. Risky claim (era's gender norms) but delivered flatly.

> **原句 56:** Men cared about learning more; women cared about using science for good.

**中文理解**：men cared about learning more；women cared about using science for good.

**句子结构**：semicolon compound with parallel clauses; each subject+verb+about+gerund; contrasting objects: "learning more" vs "using science for good."

**关键词**：cared about learning more（关注学习更多），using science for good（运用科学做好事）。

**表达方式**：Semicolon balance; "using science for good" Saxon-ish phrasing; gender essentialism stated as empirical observation, not polemic.

**为什么这样写**： Epigrammatic moral: purpose > knowledge. Completes characterization as consequentialist scientist. The article lets the claim stand without editorial distancing—obit convention trusts subject's voice.

> **原句 57:** That was much more crucial, for him, than any wonders they might do in the kitchen.

**中文理解**：那是 far more crucial for him（对他而言）than any wonders they might do in the kitchen.

**句子结构**：`That was much more crucial, for him, than any wonders they might do in the kitchen.` — comma parenthetical `for him`; `any wonders they might do` relative.

**关键词**：much more crucial（至关重要得多），wonders they might do in the kitchen（他们可能在厨房中创造的奇迹）。

**表达方式**："for him" inserted emphasizes perspective-dependence; "wonders... in the kitchen" echoes para 1 lab-in-kitchen and chauvinist trope inverted; "might do" conditional.

**为什么这样写**：Feints at kitchen trope (women in kitchen) then subverts: "wonders in kitchen" < science for good. But also loops back to para 1: his own kitchen-as-lab, not cooking. Multi-layered wordplay.

> **原句 58:** And anyway, they could hardly get into his. ■

**中文理解**：anyway，她们 almost 进不了他 (的厨房/实验室). ■

**句子结构**：`And anyway, they could hardly get into his.` — "his" = his kitchen/lab (ellipsis of "kitchen/lab"); "hardly" negation; ending ■ end marker.

**关键词**：and anyway（anyway/ anyway），could hardly get into（几乎进不去）, ■（Economist 结束符）。

**表达方式**：sarcastic closer; "they" women, "his" kitchen (chock-full of lab). Ends on private joke—his lab-kitchen is impenetrable. ■ artifact.

**为什么这样写**：Closed with dry wit; recalls kitchen scenes. Reinforces that his defining space is laboratory, not domesticity. ■ is original marker—preserve but don't add at file end beyond this quote.

## 长难句专项

1. **原句 13**（难在 dual "with"）：`In the 1970s he realised that these could replace the bulky, power-hungry TVs of old, with their bulging cathode-ray tubes and curved screens, with slim flat-screen models that could be hung on a wall.`
   - 解析：主 `he realised that` + 宾语从句；第一 with + 宾（old TV 的部位）；第二 with + 新模式；"that could be hung..." 定从修饰 slim flat-screen models。
   - 翻译要点：把两个 with 分别译成"其..."与"代以..."，保持 before/after 平行。

2. **原句 24**（难在 nested relative + result）：`...a compound whose electrons were finely tuned to convert electrical energy efficiently into light, making a laser so compact that it could be used with ordinary batteries in everyday devices: ...`
   - 主 `he had a compound`；`whose electrons were finely tuned to V`； participial `making a laser so compact that...`；冒号列举。
   - 翻译要点："whose..." 定从别断；"making" 是结果而非目的；冒号 lists.

3. **原句 28**（难在长 compound）：`As for him he was stopped too, by a policeman with a speed-gun, as he roared down a motorway in his usual mode; but got away with it by saying he was its inventor, and that one was wrongly calibrated.`
   - 第二分句 "got away with it by saying..." 双宾（he was its inventor + that one was wrongly calibrated）；"its" 指 speed-gun。
   - 翻译要点：its = speed gun；末句 "that one was wrongly calibrated" 并列 complement.

4. **原句 36**（难在 parataxis + Parentheses）： parenthetical `chamois leather on Portobello Road, flat caps on Petticoat Lane` 告诉 what stalls；主 father concealing phrase。
   - 解析： two temporal clauses (At weekends / in the evenings) joined by and; parentheses identify goods; "in order to sell them for more" purpose.
   - 翻译要点： parentheses maybe reline as " namely...".

5. **原句 42**（难在 double because + parenthetical）：`He went willingly, because every promotion was a step further from the East End, and because his quick mind (he beat Alan Turing at chess, once) always won him a measure of independence.`
   - 解析： dual because; parenthetical boast as subject complement; "won him" = earned for him.
   - 翻译要点： parenthetical 可作插入语；step further spatial metaphor.

## 精读结束总结

### 1. 本文核心表达
- 科学家的 daily life 可以 literally in kitchen —— 好奇心 as fuel.
- LCD 革命: 把 CRT 转为挂墙 flat displays，changing world's way of seeing.
- 液晶稳定 + 半导体激光：two breakthroughs.

### 2. 重要语法
- `would leave... and wander...` 习惯性过去。
- `It was up to him to...` 责任归属。
- `so...that...` 结果状语。
- Semicolon compound for balanced contrast.
- OSV fronting (`Fruit and veg he disdained`).

### 3. 写作技巧
- 开头 scene-setting (kitchen-lab) 建立 eccentric character.
- 具体仪器枚举建立 credibility.
- Anecdotes (father painting prices; police speed-gun; Alan Turing chess) humanize.
- Dry British humor & litotes ("didn't lose many arguments").
- Epigrammatic closing gender observation.
- Number cadence (70 patents, 97 filing, 2 years, 1962, £100m, 42, 101).
