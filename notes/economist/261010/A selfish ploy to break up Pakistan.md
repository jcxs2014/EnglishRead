## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段： “分而治之”与军方统治

> **原句 1:** IT WAS Philip of Macedon who reputedly coined the phrase “divide and rule”, more than 2,000 years ago.

**中文理解：** 据传是马其顿的腓力创造了一句“分而治之”的说法，距今已逾两千多年。

**句子结构：** it-cleft 强调句：IT WAS Philip of Macedon who...；主语从句 Philip of Macedon；谓语 coined；宾语 the phrase “divide and rule”；时间状语 more than 2,000 years ago。

**关键词：** IT WAS ... who = 强调句型（だったのは...）；reputedly = 据传/据说；coined = 创造（短语/词语）；divide and rule = 分而治之。

**表达方式：** 引用历史典故 opener，将现代政治与古典权谋直接并置，建立 article 的道德批判基调。

**为什么这样写：** 用“reputedly”弱化历史断言的绝对性，同时让读者接受“divide and rule 是强人共同的 playbook”这一 premise。

> **原句 2:** Strongmen ever since have employed the tactic to cement their power.

**中文理解：** 自此以后的强人们都运用这一招来巩固权力。

**句子结构：** 主语 Strongmen ever since；谓语 have employed；宾语 the tactic；目的状语 to cement their power。

**关键词：** Strongmen = 强人/独裁者；ever since = 自此以后；cement = 巩固/ cement（比喻）。

**表达方式：** “cement their power” 用 concrete metaphor 把抽象权力具象为可施工的对象。

**为什么这样写：** 明确前句与当下巴基斯坦的跳跃桥梁，指出 divide-and-rule 不是逸闻而是 recurrent power strategy。

> **原句 3:** The military man who really rules Pakistan—despite the existence of a president and a prime minister who are both nominally his superiors—seems to have taken the lesson literally.

**中文理解：** 尽管巴基斯坦存在一名总理与一名总统—— nominalles 均在其之上——真正统治巴基斯坦的武人似乎字面性地把這一课 取 了。

**句子结构：** 主语 The military man who really rules Pakistan；插入破折号 despite the existence of a president and a prime minister who are both nominally his superiors；谓语 seems to have taken；宾语 the lesson literally。

**关键词：** nominally = 名义上；superiors = 上级/上司；seems to have taken = 似乎已将...取之；literally = 字面地。

**表达方式：** 破折号和 “nominally his superiors” 的弱化，修辞性地暴露Pakistan军方 over the civilian leadership；“literally” 为 ironic 修辞（ divider into provinces as literal division）。

**为什么这样写：** 通过 constitutional form vs real power 的反差，先立 article 的 political premise——Pakistan Pakistan 是一个 under praetorium of military rule。

**段落逻辑：** 以历史典故为引 → 强人 recurrent tactic 提出 → 巴基军方是真实 ruler，成历史课堂literal 采纳者；为下文拆省方案铺垫“divide and rule”之源。

### 第 2 段：拆省方案

> **原句 4:** Field Marshal Asim Munir, the Trump-flattering head of Pakistan’s armed forces, reportedly wants to do away with his country’s four provinces, which date to the creation of Pakistan in 1947.

**中文理解：** 巴基斯坦武装部队的 Trump-flattering 统帅阿西姆·穆尼尔 field marshal， reportedly 想废除本国自 1947 年建国以来就有的四省。

**句子结构：** 主语 Field Marshal Asim Munir（同位语 the Trump-flattering head...）；谓语 reportedly wants to do away with；宾语 his country’s four provinces；非限制定语从句 which date to the creation of Pakistan in 1947。

**关键词：** Field Marshal = 元帅；Trump-flattering = 奉承特朗普的；armed forces = 武装力量；reportedly = 据报道；do away with = 废除/取消；date to = 追溯到。

**表达方式：** “Trump-flattering” adjectival fore-phrase 以道德 off-handedly 附上，预设 persona 为 opportunistic 强人；nonrestrictive which 强调四省就是建国同龄 institutions。

**为什么这样写：** 把 leader’s plan link 建国以来的 provincial map，暗示 plan 的radical self-servingness。

> **原句 5:** They may be divided into 12 new ones, or perhaps 15, or perhaps even 160 much smaller and less powerful units.

**中文理解：** 它们可能会被分为 12 个新省，或许 15 个，甚至甚至的甚至更高是 160 个更小、更无力的单位。

**句子结构：** 主语 They；被动 may be divided into；宾语 12 new ones, or perhaps 15, or perhaps even 160 much smaller and less powerful units。

**关键词：** may be divided = 可能被分割；much smaller and less powerful units = 更小且更无力的单位。

**表达方式：** 三个 escalating 的 “or perhaps” 推 progression 从 administrative reform 的 plausible to extreme 160 单位，ridiculing “logic” 的 arbitrary caprice。

**为什么这样写：** 相应 uncertain about plan 细节，却强调 whichever number，特点 is maximal fragmentation - goalile is goal，sizes are instrumentation.

> **原句 6:** These new mini-polities might be ruled by elected chiefs, or by provincial or district assemblies, or by nominees of “the government”: ie, Field Marshal Munir.

**中文理解：** 这些新的迷你政治体可能由 elected chiefs 管理，或由省/地区议会管理，或 by nominees of “the government”：即穆尼尔元帅。

**句子结构：** 主语 These new mini-polities；助动词 might be ruled by (三选) elected chiefs / provincial or district assemblies / nominees of “the government”；解压 nominative “the government”: ie, Field Marshal Munir。

**关键词：** mini-polities = 迷你邦/小政权；elected chiefs = elected chiefs；nominees = 提名人/指派人物；ie = i.e.（—that is）。

**表达方式：** quota 引号 “the government” adds scathing rhetorical scare-quotes，随后 i.e. collapse fictive civilian government into person（Munir），是article 的signature irony。

**为什么这样写：** 以 nominal/democratic option of elected chiefs 作 rhetoric counterfoil，突出 real option of Munir's nominees。

> **原句 7:** Although the details have yet to be revealed, it is abundantly clear who will benefit.

**中文理解：** 虽然细节尚未对外披露， beneficiary 显而易见。

**句子结构：** 让步状语 although the details have yet to be revealed；主句 it is abundantly clear；宾语从句 who will benefit。

**关键词：** have yet to be revealed = 尚未披露；abundantly clear = 十分明白/显而易见。

**表达方式：** 典型 Economist dual clause：epistemic modesty（details unknown） followed by confident evaluative conclusion（benefit transparent）。

**为什么这样写：** 将 political truth 与 informational uncertainty 拉开，reader 诊断受益者为 Munir。

**段落逻辑：** 方案提出 → 分裂数量 escalate → 治理名义 vs 一人 benefactor → 不详DETAILS 但受益 obvious，定调 power grab。

### 第 3 段：现有四省的 mixed bag

> **原句 8:** In principle the plan has some logic.

**中文理解：** 原则上，这个计划有一些逻辑。

**句子结构：** 介词短语 In principle；主语 the plan；谓语 has；宾语 some logic。

**关键词：** In principle = 原则上；some logic = 某些道理。

**表达方式：** concessionary first clause，mitigates absolutism，展示 fairest reviewer pose。

**为什么这样写：** 提前承认 argument，弱化 strawman，使后续 debunking 更有 credibility。

> **原句 9:** The existing four provinces are a mixed bag.

**中文理解：** 现有四省参差不齐。

**句子结构：** 主语 The existing four provinces；系动词 are；表语 a mixed bag。

**关键词：** mixed bag = 大杂烩/参差不齐（原多义）。

**表达方式：** idiomatic compression， colloquial tone reduces tedium。

**为什么这样写：** 为 details 展开设 contrast ratio，qualifying prior sentence。

> **原句 10:** The most populous, Punjab, has 128m inhabitants, slightly over half the national population.

**中文理解：** 人口最多的旁遮普有 1.28 亿居民，略过全国人口的一半。

**句子结构：** 主语 The most populous, Punjab（同位语/Punjab）；谓语 has；宾语 128m inhabitants（postmod slightly over half the national population）。

**关键词：** populous = 人口多的；inhabitants = 居民；slightly over half = 略过半数。

**表达方式：** statistical illustration reveals scale imbalance；“128m” as factual anchor.

**为什么这样写：** demonstrates governance scale problem， legitimate concern about sizeable Punjab。

> **原句 11:** (For comparison, only 12% of Americans live in California, America’s most populous state.)

**中文理解：** （作比较，只有 12% 的美国人住在国内人口最多 California。）

**句子结构：** 括号句 for comparison；主语 only 12% of Americans；谓语 live；介词短语 in California（同位语 America’s most populous state）。

**关键词：** For comparison = 为作比较；most populous state = 人口最多的州。

**表达方式：** parenthesis provides cross-national baseline showing Pakistan's unusual provincial scale.

**为什么这样写：** 与 Punjab 50%+ 比较， aids reader intuition on abnormal concentration。

> **原句 12:** Balochistan, the least populous province, has but 15m people, some 6% of the total.

**中文理解：** Balochistan，人口最少的省，却只有 1500 万人口，约占 全 6%。

**句子结构：** 主语 Balochistan（同位语 the least populous province）；谓语 has but 15m people；解释 some 6% of the total。

**关键词：** least populous = 人口最少；has but = 只有……（but 强语义）；some 6% = 大约 6%。

**表达方式：** “has but” archaic-ish emphasis on low count; complements Punjab scale to show extremes.

**为什么这样写：** 说明 4-province heterogeneity and Balochistan underrepresentation， adds complexity to reform narrative。

> **原句 13:** Governance in gargantuan Punjab is suboptimal.

**中文理解：** 庞大 Punjab 的治理效果 suboptimal。

**句子结构：** 主语 Governance in gargantuan Punjab；系动词 is；表语 suboptimal。

**关键词：** gargantuan = 巨大的/庞大的；suboptimal = 低于最优的。

**表达方式：** “gargantuan... suboptimal” ironic euphemism understates corruption；short sentence punch.

**为什么这样写：** transitions from factual scale to judgmental governance assessment。

> **原句 14:** This is not merely a polite word for corrupt.

**中文理解：** 这个词不只是为了委婉表达“腐败”。

**句子结构：** 主语 This；系动词 is not merely；表语 a polite word for corrupt。

**关键词：** merely = 仅仅；polite word for = euphemism for。

**表达方式：** clarifies “suboptimal” as diagnostic not neutral; sharpens critique.

**为什么这样写：** 避inguish policy failure from rhetorical softening， signals directness。

> **原句 15:** People in rural areas must travel for many hours to interact with officialdom and lack basic services: less than a quarter of Punjabis have a reliable supply of safe drinking water at home.

**中文理解：** rural residents must travel many hours to access officialdom and lack basic services：Punjabis 不足四分之一 家中拥有可靠安全饮用水。

**句子结构：** 主句 People in rural areas；谓语 must travel... to interact with officialdom and lack basic services；冒号引 explanatory clause less than a quarter of Punjabis have a reliable supply of safe drinking water at home。

**关键词：** officialdom = 官府/官僚机构；basic services = 基本服务；less than a quarter = 不足四分之一；reliable supply of safe drinking water = 可靠安全饮用水供应。

**表达方式：** dramatic colon linking travel burden to concrete service deficit; micro-level proof.

**为什么这样写：** 提供 ordinary Pakistani hardship evidence， grounding reform's legitimate motive。

**段落逻辑：** concession 先予logic → mixed bag → details concrete extremes → governance fail given specifics; 然而 for Munir 只 constitute excuse， set up counter.

### 第 4 段：军队的真正责任

> **原句 16:** The field marshal says the provinces are too big to be well-run, and that this is a crucial reason why the country is in such a dismal state.

**中文理解：** field marshal claimed 各省太庞大难管理，是国家处于 such dismal state 的 crucial reason。

**句子结构：** 主语 The field marshal；谓语 says；宾语从句 the provinces are too big to be well-run, and that this is a crucial reason why the country is in such a dismal state (two complement clauses).

**关键词：** field marshal = 元帅；too big to be well-run = 太大而难以管好；crucial reason = 重要原因；dismal state = 糟糕状态。

**表达方式：** indirectly quotes justification as premise for rebuttal next sentences。

**为什么这样写：** 展现 opponent's reasoning frontally， enable evidence-based attack。

> **原句 17:** He is right that Pakistan is in poor shape, with a growth rate less than half that of next-door India, inflation in double figures and debt-service costs excruciating.

**中文理解：** 他说对了 Pakistan in poor shape：增长 less than half of next-door India, double-digit inflation, and debt-service costs excruciating.

**句子结构：** 主句 He is right that Pakistan is in poor shape；with 扩展宾语 phrase growth rate...，inflation... and debt-service costs...

**关键词：** in poor shape = 状态不好；next-door India = next door 的 India（邻国）；double figures = 两位数；debt-service costs excruciating = 债务偿债成本极其难受。

**表达方式：** uses “right that...” moral accuracy concession while pivoting to deeper causes；“excruciating” hyperbolic adjective.

**为什么这样写：** avoid ad hominem; establish factual common ground to then blame attribution。

> **原句 18:** But the reasons are complex, and a big share of blame belongs to the army itself.

**中文理解：** 但原因复杂，并且 a big share of blames 属于军队自己。

**句子结构：** 并列主语/谓语：But the reasons are complex, and a big share of blame belongs to the army itself.

**关键词：** big share of blame = 大量 blames；army itself = 军队本身。

**表达方式：** turning from concessive to accusatory by pivot “But”。

**为什么这样写：** 重定向 causal attribution from scale to institutional culprit。

> **原句 19:** It gorges on taxpayers’ cash, owns huge tracts of the country’s most valuable land and controls businesses that intimidate their competitors.

**中文理解：** 它（军队） extracts taxpayers’ cash、own huge tracts of country’s most valuable land、controls businesses that intimidate competitors。

**句子结构：** 主语 It；并列谓语 gorges on taxpayers’ cash, owns huge tracts... and controls businesses（that intimidate their competitors）。

**关键词：** gorges on = 大量 greed consume；taxpayers’ cash = 纳税人现金；tracts = 地块/面积；intimidate = 恐吓。

**表达方式：** 三fold parallel indictment; “gorges” animalistic metaphor; escalating institutional capture.

**为什么这样写：** enumerates army's economic entrenchment as conflict-of-interest basis。

> **原句 20:** Pakistan has spent more than two-fifths of its existence under direct military rule, and much of the remainder under civilian governments terrified of upsetting the men with guns.

**中文理解：** 巴基斯坦在其存在中超过五分之二时间 under direct military rule，其余大部分 under civilian governments terrified of upsetting men with guns。

**句子结构：** 主语 Pakistan；谓语 has spent；宾语 more than two-fifths of its existence under direct military rule（并列） and much of the remainder under civilian governments terrified of upsetting the men with guns。

**关键词：** two-fifths = 五分之二；direct military rule = 直接军事统治；remainder = 剩余时间；terrified of upsetting = 害怕得罪。

**表达方式：** statistical + categorical juxtaposition emphasizes military dominance both overt and covert。

**为什么这样写：** provides historical bedrock for current army supremacy narrative。

> **原句 21:** The army also has an interest in keeping relations with India hostile, which is good for its budget but terrible for Pakistan.

**中文理解：** 军队 also 有兴趣 keep India relations hostile， which 对其 budget 好 but 对巴基斯坦 terrible。

**句子结构：** 主语 The army；谓语 has an interest in keeping relations with India hostile；非限制定语从句 which is good for its budget but terrible for Pakistan。

**关键词：** has an interest in = 有利益在于；hostile = 敌对的；budget = 预算。

**表达方式：** “good for its budget but terrible for Pakistan” ironic antithesis exposing misaligned incentives。

**为什么这样写：** adds geostrategic self-interest evidence undermining army's reform credibility。

> **原句 22:** As was said of Freudian psychoanalysis, Pakistan’s armed forces are the problem to which they pretend to be the solution.

**中文理解：** 正如对 Fraudian psychoanalysis 所说道，巴基斯坦的武装力量 themselves 是他们 pretend 是 solution 的 problem。

**句子结构：** 原始 sayings 引入 As was said of Freudian psychoanalysis；主语 Pakistan’s armed forces；谓语 are；宾语 the problem to which they pretend to be the solution。

**关键词：** Freudian psychoanalysis = 弗洛伊德精神分析；pretend to be = 假装是/自居为；solution = 解决方案。

**表达方式：** allusion to joke about psychoanalysis (“problem to which they pretend to be solution”) summarizes army's parasitic reform role elegantly。

**为什么这样写：** quoting witty epigram elevates editorial gravitas and provides kicker for section。

**段落逻辑：** 承认 facts → 重新 assign blame → enumerates army parasitism → historical/ geostrategic self-interest → epigrammatic summary of perverse power。

### 第 5 段：第一 wary：大省制衡

> **原句 23:** Today’s provincial system needs reform.

**中文理解：** 当今的省制需要改革。

**句子结构：** 主语 Today’s provincial system；谓语 needs；宾语 reform。

**关键词：** provincial system = 省制; needs reform = 需要改革。

**表达方式：** flat concession, permits critique's next structure.

**为什么这样写：** pre-empts “reject reform” misreading; keeps nuance。

> **原句 24:** But there are two reasons to be wary of the field marshal’s plans.

**中文理解：** 但有多 two reasons 要 wary 该 plan。

**句子结构：** 主句 But there are two reasons；宾语 to be wary of the field marshal’s plans。

**关键词：** wary = 谨慎/警惕；plans = 计划。

**表达方式：** explicit roadmap signals organized argument ahead。

**为什么这样写：** anchors reader expectations for two-part caveat。

> **原句 25:** The first is that in a country with very little pluralism, the big provinces are an essential counterweight to the centre.

**中文理解：** first，in a country with 很 little pluralism，big provinces 作 essential counterweight 对 centre。

**句子结构：** 主语 The first；系动词 is；表语从句 that in a country with very little pluralism, the big provinces are an essential counterweight to the centre。

**关键词：** very little pluralism = 几乎没有多元主义；essential counterweight = 必不可少的制衡/制衡力量；the centre = 中央政府。

**表达方式：** political-science framing; balance as normative claim。

**为什么这样写:** moves from policy assessment to governance theory to argue large province preservation。

> **原句 26:** Punjab and Sindh, a southern province centred on the country’s commercial capital, Karachi, are the fiefs of two long-lived parties, the PML(N) and the PPP, respectively.

**中文理解：** Punjab 和 Sindh（a southern province centred on Karachi）是两长期党派 PML(N) 与 PPP 的 fiefs，respectively。

**句子结构：** 主语 Punjab and Sindh（同位语 a southern province...）；系动词 are；表语 the fiefs of two long-lived parties, the PML(N) and the PPP; respectively mapping。

**关键词：** fiefs = 封地/强权领地（比喻）；long-lived parties = 长寿党派；respectively = 分别地。

**表达方式：** “fiefs” fealty metaphor signals bossism, but positive as pluralist bulwark.

**为什么这样写：** identifies existing power centers with historical party organizations, legitimizing them as counterweights.

> **原句 27:** Even under military dictators, they have preserved alternative centres of power, up to a point.

**中文理解：** even under 군事独裁 Tor，they 已 preserved alternative power centres, up to a point。

**句子结构:** 状语 Even under military dictators；主语 they；谓语 have preserved；宾语 alternative centres of power；限定 up to a point.

**关键词:** military dictators = 军事独裁者；alternative centres of power = 替代权力中心；up to a point = 有限度地.

**表达方式:** acknowledges limits with “up to a point” tempers idealization.

**为什么这样写:** bolsters case that provincial parties have been resilient pluralism nodes.

> **原句 28:** No doubt that is why the field marshal has them in his sights.

**中文理解:** 无疑，that is why field marshal has them in his sights.

**句子结构:** 主语 It（here “that”）actually No doubt that is why...；主语 none? 替: “that is why” clause: the field marshal has them in his sights。

### 第 7 段：结语： should retreat

> **原句 35:** So it seems unlikely that the plan to chop up Pakistan into smaller slices is about making provincial government more responsive to the needs of ordinary Pakistanis.

**中文理解:** 因此，把巴基斯坦 chop up into smaller slices 的计划似乎 unlikely 是 about making provincial government more responsive to ordinary Pakistanis' needs.

**句子结构:** So 引导主句 it seems unlikely；表语 从句 that the plan... is about making provincial government more responsive to the needs of ordinary Pakistanis.

**关键词:** chop up = 碎切; smaller slices = 更小片; responsive = 响应的; ordinary Pakistanis = 普通巴基斯坦人.

> **原句 36:** Rather, it is about making it more malleable to the demands of one man.

**表达方式:** antithesis “responsive to ordinary Pakistanis” vs “malleable to demands of one man”; moral climax.

**为什么这样写:** exposes core objective as personal control, summarizing article thesis.

> **原句 37:** For the good of his country, Field Marshal Munir should retreat. ■

**中文理解:** 为了他国家的利益，穆尼尔 field marshal should retreat. ■

**句子结构:** 介词短语 For the good of his country；主语 Field Marshal Munir；谓语 should retreat；■ sentence terminator marker.

**关键词:** For the good of = 为了...利益; should retreat = 应该 retreat.

**表达方式:** normative closing; direct imperative-ish “should retreat.”；■ Economist end symbol remains.

**为什么这样写:** calls explicitly on leader to step back, closes with normative force and ■.

**段落逻辑:** public justification rejected → actual motive exposed (malleability) → normative retreat call + ■. Synthesis.

## 长难句专项

1. **原句 3**：The military man who really rules Pakistan—despite the existence of a president and a prime minister who are both nominally his superiors—seems to have taken the lesson literally.
   - **结构**：主句 “The military man ... seems to have taken the lesson literally” + 破折号插入 despite... 名义 superior。
   - **难点**：破折号长插入打乱 subject-predicate 流动；“who are both nominally his superiors” 弱化 constitutional hierarchy。
   - **解剖**：The military man who really rules Pakistan — despite the existence of a president and a prime minister who are both nominally his superiors — seems to have taken the lesson literally.
   - **译法**：尽管存在名义上在其之上的总理与总统，真正统治巴基斯坦的武人似乎已将“分而治之”字面实践。

2. **原句 4**：Field Marshal Asim Munir, the Trump-flattering head of Pakistan’s armed forces, reportedly wants to do away with his country’s four provinces, which date to the creation of Pakistan in 1947.
   - **结构**：appositive 同位语 + 主句 + nonrestrictive which 从句。
   - **难点**：非限制性从句 tells founding-era provenance of the four provinces.
   - **解剖**：Field Marshal Asim Munir (the Trump-flattering head...) reportedly wants to do away with his country’s four provinces (which date to the creation of Pakistan in 1947).
   - **译法**：据报道，穆尼尔想废除与巴基斯坦建国同龄的四省。

3. **原句 15**：People in rural areas must travel for many hours to interact with officialdom and lack basic services: less than a quarter of Punjabis have a reliable supply of safe drinking water at home.
   - **结构**：and 并列动词 + 冒号 explanatory clause。
   - **难点**： travel for “many hours” 句 plus colon example supplying quantified service deficit.
   - **解剖**：People in rural areas [must travel for many hours to interact with officialdom] and [lack basic services]: [less than a quarter of Punjabis have a reliable supply of safe drinking water at home].
   - **译法**：农村居民必须长途前去政府办事且缺乏基本服务：旁遮普不到四分之一的人家中有可靠安全饮用水。

4. **原句 20**：Pakistan has spent more than two-fifths of its existence under direct military rule, and much of the remainder under civilian governments terrified of upsetting the men with guns.
   - **结构**：has spent ... under A, and ... under B（分词 phrase terrified...）。
   - **难点**：parallel prepositional phrases under differing regimes; “terrified of upsetting the men with guns” encodes fear.
   - **解剖**：Pakistan has spent [more than two-fifths of its existence under direct military rule], and [much of the remainder under civilian governments terrified of upsetting the men with guns].
   - **译法**：巴基斯坦在其存在史中五分之二以上处于直接军管，其余多在害怕 offend 持枪者的文官政府下度过。

## 精读结束总结

1. **本文核心表达**：article 指责巴基斯坦军方实际统治者以 administrative efficiency 作 pretext 拆除四省，实为 fragment opposition and consolidating personal power。Army itself 为国家困境根源，province scale argument 不 sufficient；big Provinces serve as essential pluralist counterweights.

2. **重要语法**：
   - it-cleft emphasis: “IT WAS Philip of Macedon who...”
   - concession: “In principle the plan has some logic.”
   - nonrestrictive relative: “which date to the creation...”
   - reported speech + that-clause: “says the provinces are too big...”
   - negative superlative-ish phrasing: “it seems unlikely that...”

3. **写作技巧**：
   - historical allusion (Philip of Macedon) 引出 motif
   - escalation rhetoric (“12... or perhaps 15... or 160”)
   - scare quotes “the government” + ie. identity reveal
   - concession-rebuttal 段落 (principle → roots of blame)
   - quoted epigram (psychoanalysis joke) 作 moral kicker
   - stark normative close “should retreat. ■”
