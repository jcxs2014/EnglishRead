# The Federal Reserve must soon give Donald Trump bad news

**文章概览：** 《经济学人》Leaders 社论，2026-06-09。主人公凯文·沃什（Kevin Warsh），特朗普新任美联储主席，本以"鹰派"著称，却因为被特朗普任命而转向支持降息。然而经济数据（就业强劲、通胀攀升）把他的降息理由逐一击碎。社论结论：美联储很快必须给特朗普送去坏消息——利率更可能上调而非下调。

**文体特征：** 金融评论。标题用 must soon + bad news 制造"不可避免的坏消息"悬念。副标题用 "the unlucky new chairman" + "his case ... disintegrate" 直接给出全文判决。

**全文结构：**
- 第 1 段：沃什的反讽——鹰派底色 vs 降息处境
- 第 2 段：任命背景——特朗普要降息，沃什配合
- 第 3 段：转折——"快乐的巧合结束了"
- 第 4 段：就业市场走强，降息理由崩塌
- 第 5 段：油价推高通胀（5 月 4.2%，三年新高）
- 第 6 段：沃什的"AI 灭通胀"论摇摇欲坠
- 第 7 段：同事反对 AI 通缩论
- 第 8 段：QT（量化紧缩）效应"微不足道"
- 第 9 段（副标题 "Are you Jay Powell in disguise?"）：结论——6 月 16 日首次会议，降息坚决下桌

## 一、逐句精读

### 第 1 段：鹰派底色 vs 降息处境

> **原句 1:** FOR MOST of Kevin Warsh's career, becoming chair of the Federal Reserve with the American economy hot and in need of higher interest rates would have been the stuff of professional nirvana.

**中文理解：** 在凯文·沃什职业生涯的大部分时间里，如果美国经济火热、需要提高利率时出任美联储主席，那将是职业上的"极乐净土"。

**句子结构：** `[FOR MOST of Kevin Warsh's career], [becoming chair of the Federal Reserve [with the American economy hot and in need of higher interest rates]] would have been the stuff of professional nirvana`——时间状语 + 动名词主语 + with 复合结构 + 虚拟式 would have been + 表语。

**关键词：**
- `nirvana` ⭐⭐⭐ — 极乐、涅槃（此处：职业理想国）。例句：*Becoming chair would be professional nirvana.*
- `in need of` ⭐⭐⭐ — 需要。
- `stuff of` ⭐⭐ — ……的构成材料。

**表达方式：** **would have been（虚拟式） + 动名词主语**：`becoming chair ... would have been the stuff of professional nirvana`——用 would have been 虚构理想处境——"本应如此"的反讽铺垫。

**为什么这样写：** 开篇立"反讽靶子"：鹰派当主席本该是天堂，可现实恰恰相反——为后文"becoming miserable"埋雷。

> **原句 2:** Few central bankers had staked out so hawkish a reputation.

**中文理解：** 几乎没有央行行长树立过如此鹰派的名声。

**句子结构：** `Few central bankers had staked out so hawkish a reputation`——Few + 主谓 + so + adj + a + n（倒装性强调结构）。

**关键词：**
- `staked out` ⭐⭐⭐ — 划定、声明立场。例句：*Stake out a reputation.*
- `hawkish` ⭐⭐⭐ — 鹰派的（倾向于高利率/紧缩）。反义：dovish 鸽派。
- `so ... a reputation` ⭐⭐⭐ — 如此……的名声。

**表达方式：** **so + 形容词 + a + 名词（正式倒装）**：`so hawkish a reputation`——把 so hawkish 提前造成强调——"如此之鹰"。

**为什么这样写：** Few ... had staked out so hawkish 用"极少数"+ 现在完成时强调沃什的鹰派声望——与后文他的鸽派转向形成强烈反差。

> **原句 3:** So it is ironic that this scenario has come to pass, yet it seems likely to make Mr Warsh's life miserable as he starts at the Fed.

**中文理解：** 所以，具有讽刺意味的是，这个场景竟然成真了，然而它似乎很可能让沃什先生刚上任美联储时的日子难过起来。

**句子结构：** `So it is ironic that [this scenario has come to pass], yet it seems likely to make Mr Warsh's life miserable [as he starts at the Fed]`——So + it is ironic that + 主句 + yet 转折 + seems likely to + as 时间。

**关键词：**
- `ironic` ⭐⭐⭐ — 讽刺的。
- `come to pass` ⭐⭐⭐ — 发生、成真。例句：*The scenario has come to pass.*
- `miserable` ⭐⭐⭐ — 糟糕的、难熬的。

**表达方式：** **it is ironic that ... yet ...**：`So it is ironic that this scenario has come to pass, yet ...`——it is ironic（讽刺的是）+ come to pass（竟然成真）+ yet（然而）——反讽递进。

**为什么这样写：** 点破第 1 段埋下的反讽：理想场景成真（come to pass）却让沃什日子难熬（miserable）——"实现即灾难"的戏剧性。

**段落逻辑：** 鹰派理想（would have been nirvana）→ 声望铺垫（Few ... so hawkish）→ 反讽成真（ironic that ... come to pass, yet ... miserable）。第一段用"理想↔现实"对照立起全文张力。---

### 第 2 段：任命背景 —— 特朗普要降息，沃什配合

> **原句 4:** For that, he can thank the circumstances of his appointment.

**中文理解：** 为此，他要感谢自己获任时的境况。

**句子结构：** `For that, he can thank the circumstances of his appointment`——For that（为此）+ 主谓 + 宾。

**关键词：**
- `For that` ⭐⭐⭐ — 为此、就此事。
- `thank ... for` ⭐⭐ — 感谢（反语：责怪）。

**表达方式：** **can thank ... for（反语）**：`For that, he can thank the circumstances of his appointment`——用"感谢"（thank）表达"该怪"——辛辣反讽。

**为什么这样写：** 承上启下：沃什的麻烦"要感谢"任命时的境况——把责任指向政治任命。

> **原句 5:** President Donald Trump wants lower interest rates, and appointed Mr Warsh in January because he, too, favoured them.

**中文理解：** 特朗普总统想要更低的利率，并在 1 月任命沃什先生，因为沃什（他也）赞成降息。

**句子结构：** `President Donald Trump wants lower interest rates, and appointed Mr Warsh in January [because he, too, favoured them]`——并列 + because 原因 + he, too（强调同位）。

**关键词：**
- `appointed` ⭐⭐⭐ — 任命。
- `favoured` ⭐⭐⭐ — 赞成、偏好。
- `too` ⭐⭐ — 也（插入强调）。

**表达方式：** **because he, too, favoured them**：`appointed ... because he, too, favoured them`——he, too（他也）——强调沃什与特朗普在降息上的默契。

**为什么这样写：** 点明任命逻辑：特朗普要降息 → 沃什也赞成 → 任命顺理成章——为后文"降息理由崩塌"埋线。

> **原句 6:** Back then, the economic case for looser money was respectable: the post-pandemic inflation surge had been all but killed, and the jobs market looked like it was wobbling.

**中文理解：** 那时，宽松货币的经济理由还算站得住脚：疫情后的通胀潮几乎已被扼杀，就业市场看起来摇摇欲坠。

**句子结构：** `Back then, the economic case for looser money was respectable: [the post-pandemic inflation surge had been all but killed], and [the jobs market looked like it was wobbling]`——时间 + 主系表 + 冒号 + 并列两分句。

**关键词：**
- `case for` ⭐⭐⭐ — ……的理由。
- `looser money` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更宽松的货币（政策）。
- `respectable` ⭐⭐⭐ — 体面的、站得住的。
- `all but killed` ⭐⭐⭐ — 几乎扼杀。
- `wobbling` ⭐⭐⭐ — 摇摆、不稳。

**表达方式：** **was respectable + 冒号 + 双证据**：`the case was respectable: inflation all but killed, jobs wobbling`——respectable（能站住脚）+ 冒号列举两个理由。

**为什么这样写：** 先承认降息理由当时"勉强成立"（respectable）——客观铺垫——为后文"crumbled"做对照。

> **原句 7:** Mr Warsh's out-of-character doveishness provoked wry smiles but not scorn from other central bankers, most of whom were glad that Mr Trump had picked someone sane for the job.

**中文理解：** 沃什先生一反常态的鸽派姿态引起了其他央行行长的苦笑，但并没有招来嘲笑，他们中的大多数很高兴特朗普选了个正常人来干这份工作。

**句子结构：** `Mr Warsh's out-of-character doveishness provoked wry smiles but not scorn [from other central bankers], [most of whom were glad that Mr Trump had picked someone sane for the job]`——主谓 + but not + 非限制性定语从句（most of whom）。

**关键词：**
- `out-of-character` ⭐⭐⭐ — 一反常态的。
- `doveishness` ⭐⭐⭐ — 鸽派倾向（dove + -ish + -ness 造词）。
- `wry smiles` ⭐⭐⭐ — 苦笑。
- `scorn` ⭐⭐⭐ — 轻蔑。
- `sane` ⭐⭐⭐ — 理智的、正常的。

**表达方式：** **wry smiles but not scorn**：`provoked wry smiles but not scorn`——苦笑（轻度讽刺）但不嘲笑——"圈内人半认可半无奈"。

**为什么这样写：** out-of-character（反常态）+ wry smiles（苦笑）——刻画同行对沃什转鸽的复杂态度，且 "someone sane" 反衬特朗普选人标准之低。

**段落逻辑：** For that 承上（任命背景）→ Trump wants cuts, appointed him (favoured too) → case respectable (inflation killed / jobs wobbling) → doveishness wry smiles but not scorn, most glad picked someone sane。第 2 段完成"任命合理性的生前史"。---

### 第 3 段：转折 —— "快乐的巧合结束了"

> **原句 8:** Alas, the happy coincidence is over.

**中文理解：** 唉，这快乐的巧合结束了。

**句子结构：** `Alas, the happy coincidence is over`——感叹语 + 主系表。

**关键词：**
- `Alas` ⭐⭐⭐ — 唉（感叹词）。
- `coincidence` ⭐⭐⭐ — 巧合。
- `is over` ⭐⭐ — 结束。

**表达方式：** **Alas + 简短断言**：`Alas, the happy coincidence is over`——Alas（一声叹息）+ 三个词断言——转折信号。

**为什么这样写：** 极短一句独立成段式，用 Alas 划出前后对比的"断崖"——经济判断的转向。

> **原句 9:** The case for lower interest rates has crumbled.

**中文理解：** 降息的理由已经瓦解。

**句子结构：** `The case for lower interest rates has crumbled`——主谓 + for 补语。

**关键词：**
- `crumbled` ⭐⭐⭐ — 崩塌、瓦解。

**表达方式：** **has crumbled（现在完成）**：`The case ... has crumbled`——crumble（崩塌）呼应第 2 段 respectable——"理由从站得住到瓦解"。

**为什么这样写：** crumble 与前文 respectable 形成明对照——经济理由的判词。

> **原句 10:** Mr Trump still wants rate cuts but, if anything, today's economic conditions demand tighter money.

**中文理解：** 特朗普先生仍想要降息，但是，如果要说什么的话，今天的经济状况反而要求更紧的货币。

**句子结构：** `Mr Trump still wants rate cuts but, if anything, [today's economic conditions demand tighter money]`——but 转折 + if anything 插入语 + 主谓。

**关键词：**
- `if anything` ⭐⭐⭐ — 甚至可以说、如果要说什么。
- `demand` ⭐⭐⭐ — 要求。
- `tighter money` ⭐⭐⭐ — 更紧的货币。

**表达方式：** **but, if anything, + demand**：`but, if anything, ... demand tighter money`——if anything（恰恰相反）——把让步转向反推。

**为什么这样写：** still wants 与 if anything ... demand tighter 对撞——政治意愿 vs 经济现实的分裂点明。

**段落逻辑：** Alas（转折信号）→ case crumbled → Trump still wants but if anything demand tighter。第 3 段是全文转向枢纽。

### 第 4 段：就业市场走强，降息理由崩塌

> **原句 11:** Since Mr Warsh's appointment America's labour market has firmed up.

**中文理解：** 自沃什先生获任以来，美国的劳动力市场已经变得强劲。

**句子结构：** `Since Mr Warsh's appointment [America's labour market has firmed up]`——Since 时间 + 主谓。

**关键词：**
- `firmed up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 变坚实、走强。例句：*The labour market firmed up.*
- `labour market` ⭐⭐⭐ — 劳动力市场。

**表达方式：** **Since + has firmed up**：`Since appointment ... has firmed up`——用 Since 连接人事变化与市场转强——"人事与市场的呼应"。

**为什么这样写：** 时间锚定（Since appointment）——暗示沃什上任恰逢市场转强，降息前提消失。> **原句 12:** From March to May payrolls swelled by an average of 188,000 per month, far above estimates of growth in the labour force at a time when migration is low or negative.

**中文理解：** 从 3 月到 5 月，非农就业人数平均每月增加 18.8 万人，远超在移民为负或很低时期对劳动力增长的估计。

**句子结构：** `From March to May payrolls swelled by an average of 188,000 per month, [far above estimates of growth in the labour force] [at a time when migration is low or negative]`——时间 + 主谓 + 数量 + 比较状语 + 时间定语。

**关键词：**
- `payrolls` ⭐⭐⭐ — 就业人数（非农）。
- `swelled` ⭐⭐⭐ — 膨胀、猛增。
- `far above estimates` ⭐⭐⭐ — 远超预期。
- `migration` ⭐⭐⭐ — 移民（人口流动）。

**表达方式：** **swelled + far above + at a time when**：`payrolls swelled ... far above estimates ... at a time when migration is low`——swelled（猛增）+ far above（远超）+"移民走弱时"——双重佐证。

**为什么这样写：** 数字（188,000/月）+ 背景（移民低）——劳动力紧缺下就业仍旺，说明经济过热。

> **原句 13:** Until November the unemployment rate had been gently rising; it has since fallen and held steady at 4.3%.

**中文理解：** 直到 11 月，失业率一直在缓慢上升；此后它已回落并稳在 4.3%。

**句子结构：** `Until November the unemployment rate had been gently rising; [it has since fallen and held steady at 4.3%]`——Until 时间 + 过去完成进行 + 分号 + 现在完成 + and 并列。

**关键词：**
- `gently rising` ⭐⭐⭐ — 缓慢上升。
- `held steady at` ⭐⭐⭐ — 稳定在。
- `since` ⭐⭐ — 此后。

**表达方式：** **had been rising; has since fallen**：`had been gently rising; it has since fallen and held steady`——过去完成（曾缓升）vs 现在完成（已回落）——时间轴反转。

**为什么这样写：** 用两个时态对切换展示"失业率从缓升转稳定"——就业市场好转的证据链。

> **原句 14:** The economy is exuberant.

**中文理解：** 经济十分繁荣。

**句子结构：** `The economy is exuberant`——主系表。

**关键词：**
- `exuberant` ⭐⭐⭐ — 繁荣的、充满活力的。例句：*An exuberant economy.*

**表达方式：** **一句话断言**：`The economy is exuberant`——极短断言收束。

**为什么这样写：** 数据铺垫后一句话总结（exuberant）——把就业、失业率结论凝练成判词。

**段落逻辑：** labour market firmed up → payrolls swelled far above estimates at a time migration low → unemployment fell to 4.3% → economy exuberant。第 4 段用"就业三证据"完成"经济过热"判断。---

### 第 5 段：油价推高通胀

> **原句 15:** Higher oil prices, the result of Mr Trump's war with Iran, have pushed up annual inflation, to 4.2% in May, a three-year high.

**中文理解：** 更高的油价——特朗普与伊朗战争的产物——推高了年通胀率，5 月达到 4.2%，为三年新高。

**句子结构：** `Higher oil prices, [the result of Mr Trump's war with Iran], have pushed up annual inflation, [to 4.2% in May, a three-year high]`——主谓 + 同位语（the result of）+ 补语（to + 数字 + 同位）。

**关键词：**
- `annual inflation` ⭐⭐⭐ — 年通胀率。
- `a three-year high` ⭐⭐⭐ — 三年高点。
- `pushed up` ⭐⭐⭐ — 推高。

**表达方式：** **同位语 + 数字 + 同位语**：`Higher oil prices, the result of ... , have pushed up inflation, to 4.2% in May, a three-year high`——多层同位嵌入"原因-结果-数据"。

**为什么这样写：** 一句话装下因果（war→oil）、数据（4.2%）、参照（three-year high）——信息密度极高。

> **原句 16:** Often central bankers ignore inflation that comes from oil prices.

**中文理解：** 央行行长们通常忽略源于油价的通胀。

**句子结构：** `Often central bankers ignore inflation [that comes from oil prices]`——频率副词 + 主谓 + 定语从句。

**关键词：**
- `ignore` ⭐⭐⭐ — 忽略。
- `comes from` ⭐⭐ — 源于。

**表达方式：** **Often + 一般现在时**：`Often central bankers ignore inflation that comes from oil`——Often（通常）+ ignore（忽略）——行业惯例陈述。

**为什么这样写：** 先立"行业惯例"（通常忽略油价通胀）——为后文"today difficult"做铺垫。

> **原句 17:** That is difficult today because inflation has exceeded the Fed's target for more than five years.

**中文理解：** 今天这很难做到，因为通胀超过美联储目标已有五年多。

**句子结构：** `That is difficult today [because inflation has exceeded the Fed's target for more than five years]`——主系表 + because 原因。

**关键词：**
- `exceeded the target` ⭐⭐⭐ — 超过目标。
- `more than five years` ⭐⭐⭐ — 五年多。

**表达方式：** **That is difficult because + 现在完成时长**：`That is difficult ... because inflation has exceeded the target for more than five years`——difficult（难）+ for + 时长（五年）——"常态性超纲"。

**为什么这样写：** 用时间长度（for more than five years）把"例外"（油价通胀）升级为"常态"——不可再忽略。

> **原句 18:** Overshoots could get baked into the public's expectations.

**中文理解：** 超目标的情形可能会被"烘进"公众的预期里。

**句子结构：** `Overshoots could get baked into the public's expectations`——主谓 + 被动（get baked into）。

**关键词：**
- `Overshoots` ⭐⭐⭐ — 超过目标值。
- `baked into` ⭐⭐⭐ — 融入、固化进。
- `expectations` ⭐⭐⭐ — 预期。

**表达方式：** **could get baked into（被动比喻）**：`Overshoots could get baked into expectations`——baked（烘焙/固化）——预期锚定比喻。

**为什么这样写：** baked into 生动传达"通胀预期固化"——央行最怕的"脱锚"风险。

> **原句 19:** Inflation that started with oil could take on a life of its own.

**中文理解：** 始于油价的通胀可能会拥有自己的生命。

**句子结构：** `Inflation [that started with oil] could take on a life of its own`——主谓 + 定语从句 + 固定搭配。

**关键词：**
- `take on a life of its own` ⭐⭐⭐ — 有了自己的生命、自我延续。

**表达方式：** **take on a life of its own（习语）**：`Inflation ... could take on a life of its own`——把通胀拟人化为"有独立生命"——失控警告。

**为什么这样写：** 习语收束第 5 段：油价通胀可能自我强化——降息更不可行。

**段落逻辑：** oil pushed inflation to 4.2% (three-year high) → usually ignored (oil inflation) → difficult today (exceeded target for five years) → baked into expectations → could take on a life of its own。第 5 段完成"通胀失控风险"论证。

### 第 6 段：沃什的"AI 灭通胀"论摇摇欲坠

> **长难句 1（原句 1）：**
> *FOR MOST of Kevin Warsh's career, becoming chair of the Federal Reserve with the American economy hot and in need of higher interest rates would have been the stuff of professional nirvana.*

**① 找主干：** 主语 `becoming chair of the Federal Reserve`（动名词短语），谓语 `would have been`（虚拟式），表语 `the stuff of professional nirvana`。

**② 找修饰：** 句首 `FOR MOST of Kevin Warsh's career` 是时间状语；`with the American economy hot and in need of higher interest rates` 是 with 复合结构作伴随状语（with + 名词 + 形容词短语）。

**③ 找从句：** 无从句，但 `with ... hot and in need of` 是核心难点（with 后接宾语 + 两个并列补语）。

**④ 恢复逻辑：** 在他的职业生涯大部分时候（FOR MOST of ... career），如果他当上美联储主席（becoming chair），且当时美国经济火热、需要加息（with economy hot and in need of higher rates），那本该是职业极乐（would have been the stuff of nirvana）。

**⑤ 整体理解：** 用虚拟式 would have been 构建"理想场景"——反讽即将登场。

> **长难句 2（原句 7）：**
> *Mr Warsh's out-of-character doveishness provoked wry smiles but not scorn from other central bankers, most of whom were glad that Mr Trump had picked someone sane for the job.*

**① 找主干：** 主语 `Mr Warsh's out-of-character doveishness`，谓语 `provoked`，宾语 `wry smiles but not scorn`。

**② 找修饰：** `from other central bankers` 为来源状语；`most of whom were glad ...` 是非限制性定语从句修饰 central bankers。

**③ 找从句：** whom 定语从句中嵌套 `that Mr Trump had picked someone sane` 作 glad 的宾语从句。

**④ 恢复逻辑：** 沃什反常的鸽派（out-of-character doveishness）引来同行苦笑（wry smiles）而非嘲笑（not scorn）；大多数同行（most of whom）很高兴特朗普选了个正常人（someone sane）。

**⑤ 整体理解：** 双重态度——"不嘲笑但苦笑"，并借 "someone sane" 反衬特朗普用人尺度。

> **长难句 3（原句 25）：**
> *And Mr Warsh's new colleagues have lined up to remind the incoming chair that if AI lifts productivity growth, economic theory suggests interest rates would need to go up, not down, thanks to stronger appetites for spending and investment.*

**① 找主干：** 主语 `Mr Warsh's new colleagues`，谓语 `have lined up`，不定式 `to remind`，宾语 `the incoming chair`。

**② 找修饰：** `that ...` 宾语从句是 remind 的间接宾语内容；`thanks to stronger appetites` 为原因状语。

**③ 找从句：** that 从句中嵌套 `if AI lifts productivity growth` 条件句；主句 `economic theory suggests interest rates would need to go up, not down`。

**④ 恢复逻辑：** 同事们排队提醒（lined up to remind）新主席：若 AI 提升生产率（if AI lifts productivity），理论上是利率该升不该降（go up, not down），因为支出与投资意愿更强（thanks to appetites）。

**⑤ 整体理解：** 同事集体反对沃什"AI 通缩论"——若 AI 真有效，利率反而该升。

> **长难句 4（原句 35）：**
> *Under Mr Warsh's scheme, by contrast, the balance-sheet and rates would pull in opposite directions.*

**① 找主干：** 主语 `the balance-sheet and rates`，谓语 `would pull`，状语 `in opposite directions`。

**② 找修饰：** `Under Mr Warsh's scheme` 条件状语；`by contrast` 插入语表示与前文对比。

**③ 找从句：** 无从句。难点在 by contrast 与 Under 两个介词短语的语义功能。

**④ 恢复逻辑：** 在沃什方案下（Under ... scheme），与之相对（by contrast），资产负债表和利率会往相反方向拉扯（pull in opposite directions）。

**⑤ 整体理解：** 历史案例中缩表与利率同向，而沃什方案让两者反向——信号机制失效，QT 效果存疑。

## 三、精读结束总结

### 1. 重要表达
- `the stuff of ... nirvana`（职业极乐的构成材料）
- `can thank ... for`（反语：该怪……）
- `all but killed`（几乎被扼杀）
- `take on a life of its own`（有了自己的生命）
- `off the table` / `on the table`（排除 / 摆上桌面）
- `play for time`（拖延）
- `pull in opposite directions`（朝相反方向拉扯）
- `rules of thumb`（经验法则）

### 2. 核心语法
1. **虚拟式 would have been**：构建"本该如此"的反讽（原句 1）。
2. **with 复合结构**：with + 宾语 + 形容词补语（原句 1）。
3. **非限制性定语从句 most of whom**：补充说明人群态度（原句 7）。
4. **现在完成时态对比**：had been rising vs has since fallen（原句 13）。
5. **such ... that 结构**：因果强调链（原句 22）。
6. **省略关系代词**：a list of nerdy reforms he wants to make（原句 38）。

### 3. 长难句要点
1. 长句的骨架常是"主语 + 动名词/不定式 + 虚拟或条件"，先拆出主干再处理修饰。
2. `with + 复合结构` 与 `by contrast` 这类介词短语承载大量对比信息。
3. `not ... but`、`up, not down` 类"对举"结构是经济评论强调对比的惯用手法。

### 3. 写作技巧
1. **标题即结论**：标题 "must soon give Trump bad news" 直接给出最终判断，正文逐层论证。
2. **反讽开篇**：用虚拟式（would have been nirvana）+ "ironic that ... come to pass" 制造理想落空的戏剧性。
3. **数字证据链**：188,000/月、4.3%、4.2%、5% GDP、四分之一基点——用精确数据支撑判断。
4. **比喻说理**：baked into expectations、take on a life of its own、暖气和空调同开——把抽象概念具象化。
5. **段落转折枢纽**：Alas, the happy coincidence is over——一个词完成立场切换。
6. **首尾呼应**：标题 bad news → 末句 give Trump bad news，闭环收束。

### 5. 可迁移表达
- 表达"本该却未能"：*would have been the stuff of ...*
- 表达"站不住脚"：*the case for ... has crumbled / look shakier than ever*
- 表达"固化预期"：*get baked into expectations*
- 表达"自我延续"：*take on a life of its own*
- 表达"排除选项"：*should be firmly off the table*
- 表达"对比反驳"：*by contrast, ... would pull in opposite directions*
- 表达"不得不送坏消息"：*will have to give ... bad news*
