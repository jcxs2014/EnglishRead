# America makes waves in Cuba（精读分析）

## 概览

本文为 *The Economist* 2026-10-15 的 Leaders 社论（标题 "America makes waves in Cuba"，副标题 "But it shouldn't undermine the growth of the island's private sector"），论析美国封锁古巴燃料供应所意外打开的私营部门机会，以及特朗普政府应如何善用这一工具。核心论点：封锁虽造成人道苦难却无意间松动了古巴政府对燃料贸易的垄断；应继续助私企成长，甚至利用间谍-style（应作 enforceable）制裁筛掉政权前台，而非恢复以银行冻结为代表的企业惩罚。结尾警告：别走向美式优先的委内瑞拉模式，私营力量需要自由竞争；封锁无终点即是人道灾难。

## 全文结构

- 第 1 段：特朗普封锁古巴燃料的目标与现实——苦难来了，政权没倒，却意外创造机会
- 第 2 段：委内瑞拉石油断供 + 古巴政府放权私营企业进口燃料 + 美国授权出口
- 第 3 段：燃料出口增长，部分苦难缓解；重油未到，但汽车与发电机回来
- 第 4 段：杠杆增强；私营企业扩展，法律开放石油业；古巴观察家期待发电厂私有化
- 第 5 段：弱化 Gaesa 军事财阀； Rubio 定位私营企业；特朗普却冻结企业银行账户
- 第 6 段：主张回撤；回应" façade/寡头"质疑； tighter sanctions 执法是 welcomed sign
- 第 7 段： worry：美式优先的委内瑞拉式安排风险
- 第 8 段：委内瑞拉油转售古巴私营部门的坏激励
- 第 9 段：封锁无终点 = 人道灾难； conclusion：加强私营部门是最有效工具

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：封锁的意图与意外机会

> **原句 1:** SINCE HIS return to power, President Donald Trump has made clear that he hopes to bring down Cuba's repressive regime.

**中文理解：** 自从重回权力以来，总统唐纳德·特朗普已经表明他希望推翻古巴的压制性政权。

**句子结构：** `SINCE HIS return to power, [President Donald Trump has made clear [that he hopes to bring down Cuba's repressive regime]]`——时间状语从句 + 主谓宾 + that 宾语从句。

**关键词：**
- `return to power` ⭐⭐⭐ — 重返权力
- `repressive regime` ⭐⭐⭐ — 压制性政权

**表达方式：** 全大写 "SINCE HIS return to power" 作句首时间背景，强调政权更迭的宿命感；"bring down" 直指特朗普的真实目标。

**为什么这样写：** 先把目标说清，再写手段，为后面"封锁未达目的卻造成机会"的转折做铺垫。

> **原句 2:** This year he struck upon a cruel approach: blockading the impoverished island's fuel supply.

**中文理解：** 今年他想出了一种残酷的做法：封锁这个贫困岛屿的燃料供应。

**句子结构：** `This year he struck upon a cruel approach: blockading ...`——主谓宾 + 冒号引出同位语动名词短语。

**关键词：**
- `struck upon` ⭐⭐⭐ — 想出、 hit upon
- `cruel approach` ⭐⭐ — 残酷做法
- `impoverished` ⭐⭐⭐ — 贫困的
- `blockading` ⭐⭐⭐ — 封锁

**表达方式：** 冒号把"手段"具体化为"封锁燃料"；形容词 "impoverished" 预设了人道代价。

**为什么这样写：** 用 "cruel" 明确道德立场，避免装中立；同时揭示燃料是 Cuba 的 Achilles heel。

> **原句 3:** The idea was that growing hardship would spur Cubans to protest, and then to topple their vile leaders.

**中文理解：** 目的是让日益增长的困苦刺激古巴人抗议，进而推翻他们狠毒的领导人。

**句子结构：** `The idea was [that growing hardship would spur Cubans to protest, and then to topple their vile leaders]`——主系表 + that 宾语从句；不定式并列。

**关键词：**
- `spur` ⭐⭐⭐ — 刺激、推动
- `vile` ⭐⭐⭐ — 邪恶的、狠毒的
- `topple` ⭐⭐⭐ — 推翻

**表达方式：** `spur ... to protest, and then to topple` 双不定式串起"抗议→推翻"的因果链。

**为什么这样写：** 摘取封锁的逻辑，为后面的"Misery duly followed, political change did not come"埋伏笔——Means vs. ends 不对称。

> **原句 4:** Misery duly followed, as Cubans suffered more blackouts, buses stopped running and food got more expensive.

**中文理解：** 苦难如期而至：古巴人遭受更多停电，公交车停运，食物也更贵。

**句子结构：** `Misery duly followed, [as ...]`——主句 + as 固定时态作结论性观测。

**关键词：**
- `duly` ⭐⭐ — 按预期、如期
- `blackouts` ⭐⭐ — 停电

**表达方式：** 逗号串起三项民生成本，辅以 "duly" 强化"预料之中"。

**为什么这样写：** 用具体成本展示封锁后果，强调 results are already visible。

> **原句 5:** Political change did not come.

**中文理解：** 但政治变革并未来临。

**句子结构：** 主谓宾简单句。

**关键词：** `political change` ⭐⭐ — 政治变革

**表达方式：** 单句短句，与前面 misery 的拖长形成张力；宣告封锁策略失败。

**为什么这样写：** 把 " misery came; change didn't" 放在紧邻段落，制造 policy gap.

> **原句 6:** Yet by accident or design, the blockade has still created an opportunity.

**中文理解：** 然而有意或无意，封锁仍制造了一个机会。

**句子结构：** `Yet by accident or design, [the blockade has still created an opportunity]`——让步/方式状语 + 主谓宾。

**关键词：**
- `by accident or design` ⭐⭐ — 有意或无意
- `created an opportunity` ⭐⭐ — 创造机会

**表达方式：** "Yet ... has still created" 把苦役叙事转为 opportunity narrative； ambiguity intentional。

**为什么这样写：** 为第二段 explaining mechanism 的落脚点——opportunity 是"unintended consequence"。

### 第 2 段：燃料 monopoly 与 regime 放权

> **原句 7:** For decades Cuba got most of its imported fuel from Venezuela and, as with most of the economy, Cuba's government had a monopoly over the trade.

**中文理解：** 几十年来，古巴大部分进口燃料来自委内瑞拉；与经济的大部分一样，古巴政府对该贸易拥有垄断权。

**句子结构：** `For decades Cuba got most of its imported fuel from Venezuela [and, as with most of the economy, Cuba's government had a monopoly over the trade]`——并列句，中间插入 as with...

**关键词：**
- `monopoly` ⭐⭐ — 垄断
- `imported fuel` ⭐⭐ — 进口燃料

**表达方式：** "as with most of the economy" 把燃料贸易嵌入 state-monopoly norm；M monopoly is systemic.

**为什么这样写：** 铺垫后续"松手是为了生存"的 policy shift。

> **原句 8:** That arrangement collapsed in January when American forces captured Nicolas Maduro in Venezuela and threatened sanctions on anyone who sent oil to Cuba's regime.

**中文理解：** 那一安排在一月崩塌：美国军队在委内瑞拉捕获尼古拉斯·马杜罗，并威胁对任何向古巴政权输油的人实施制裁。

**句子结构：** 主句 + when 时间从句（captured ... and threatened ...）。

**关键词：**
- `collapsed` ⭐⭐⭐ — 崩塌
- `captured` ⭐⭐ — 捕获
- `threatened sanctions` ⭐⭐⭐ — 威胁制裁

**表达方式：** "captured ... and threatened" 双动词把行动与 appended consequence 串起。

**为什么这样写：** 打破 monopoly 之因是 US pressure; sets leverage narrative。

> **原句 9:** Facing paralysis, the island's government loosened its grip on private businesses, letting them import fuel for the first time since the revolution in 1959.

**中文理解：** 面对瘫痪，该岛政府放松对私营企业的控制，允许它们自1959年革命以来第一次进口燃料。

**句子结构：** `Facing paralysis, [the island's government loosened its grip on private businesses], [letting private businesses import fuel ...]`——分词并发主句 + 让步/伴随结果。

**关键词：**
- `Facing paralysis` ⭐⭐⭐ — 面对瘫痪
- `loosened its grip` ⭐⭐⭐ — 放松控制
- `for the first time since` ⭐⭐ — 自……以来首次

**表达方式：** 省略号 linking Action to consequence; "first time since 1959" 是历史突破。

**为什么这样写：** Reveals leverage real driver of liberalization — not ideology.

> **原句 10:** America's administration, in turn, authorised exporters to sell it to them.

**中文理解：** 美国行政当局随即授权出口商把燃料卖给它们。

**句子结构：** `America's administration, in turn, authorised exporters [to sell it to them]`。

**关键词：**
- `authorised` ⭐⭐ — 授权
- `exporters` ⭐⭐ — 出口商

**表达方式：** "in turn" 表示 reciprocal concession; 美方 acknowledge thaw.

**为什么这样写：** 为" rising swiftly" leg.

### 第 3 段：局部缓解与重油未到

> **原句 11:** American fuel exports to Cuba's private sector are now rising swiftly.

**中文理解：** 美国对古巴私营部门的燃料出口现在正在迅速增长。

**句子结构：** 主谓宾+副词

**关键词：**
- `rising swiftly` ⭐⭐ — 迅速增长

**表达方式：** 短句, positive momentum.

**为什么这样写：** 确认 opportunity policy is working in practice.

> **原句 12:** This, happily, is relieving a portion of the suffering that the sudden blockade has caused.

**中文理解：** 幸好，这正在缓解突然封锁所造成的部分苦难。

**句子结构：** `This, happily, is relieving a portion [that the sudden blockade has caused]`.

**关键词：**
- `relieving` ⭐⭐⭐ — 缓解
- `a portion of` ⭐⭐ — 一部分

**表达方式：** "happily" 表 editor approval; "a portion" avoids overclaim.

**为什么这样写：** Keep moral balance: provide Good but not enough.

> **原句 13:** The heavy fuel that is necessary to keep Cuba's power stations running is not yet flowing.

**中文理解：** 使古巴发电厂运转所需的重油尚未开始流入。

**句子结构：** `The heavy fuel [necessary to...] is not yet flowing`.

**关键词：**
- `heavy fuel` ⭐⭐ — 重油
- `power stations` ⭐ — 发电站

**表达方式：** break in supply cep provides continuity problem.

**为什么这样写：** Distinguishes small generators from grid reality.

> **原句 14:** But cars are returning to the streets and you can hear the whirring of small generators.

**中文理解：** 但汽车正回到街道上，而且你能听到小型发电机的嗡嗡声。

**句子结构：** 并列句 (cars are returning and you can hear...)

**关键词：**
- `whirring` ⭐⭐⭐ — 嗡嗡声

**表达方式：** Sensory detail evokes everyday relief.

**为什么这样写：** Ground abstract fuel exports in street life.

### 第 4 段：杠杆与私有化趋势

> **原句 15:** As a result Mr Trump has gained leverage over a country that depends on America for food and remittances.

**中文理解：** 因此特朗普先生获得了对一个依赖美国粮食和汇款的国家的杠杆。

> **原句 16:** Private business is growing, and not just in importing fuel.

**中文理解：** 私营企业正在增长，而不仅仅是在进口燃料。

**句子结构：** `Private business is growing, and not just in importing fuel`.

**关键词：**
- `not just` ⭐ — 不只是

**表达方式：** 短句 confirms broadening.

**为什么这样写：** Undermine impression process narrow; expanding.

> **原句 17:** The state has leased some petrol stations to entrepreneurs.

**中文理解：** 国家把一些加油站租给了企业家。

- `entrepreneurs` ⭐⭐ — 企业家

**表达方式：** Specific institutional change.

> **原句 18:** On October 12th it enacted a law to encourage private participation in the oil industry, from exploring and refining to selling.

**中文理解：** 十月十二日，它颁布了一部法律以鼓励私营企业在石油工业中的参与，从勘探、炼制到销售。

**关键词：**

**表达方式：** Scope spanning entire chain emphasizes breadth.

**为什么这样写：** Provides legal proof of policy direction.

> **原句 19:** Cuba-watchers hope the government may next be forced to privatise power plants—and even the state's oil-and-gas arm, Cupet.

**中文理解：** 古巴观察家希望政府接下来可能被迫把发电厂而且甚至国有油气公司 Cupet 私有化。

**句子结构：** 异常者希望 + `may next be forced to privatise` + em-dash 扩展.

**关键词：**
- `privatise` ⭐⭐⭐ — 私有化
- `Cupet` ⭐⭐ — 古巴国有油气公司

**表达方式：** Dash escalation from plants to Cupet.

**为什么这样写：** Shows possible cascading reform; but cautions with hope, not certainty.

### 第 5 段：Gaesa 与政策 contradictory

> **原句 20:** A stronger private sector would in turn loosen the grip of Gaesa, a sprawling and corrupt military conglomerate.

**中文理解：** 更强的私营部门反过来会放松 Gaesa——一个 sprawling and corrupt military conglomerate ——的控制。

**句子结构：** 主谓宾+非限制性同位语.

**关键词：**
- `loosen the grip` ⭐⭐⭐ — 放松控制
- `sprawling` ⭐⭐⭐ — 蔓延的
- `conglomerate` ⭐⭐⭐ — 企业集团

**表达方式：** Appositive clarifies Gaesa.

**为什么这样写：** Links economic reform to anti-corruption/military depowering.

> **原句 21:** Marco Rubio, America's secretary of state, says the deal on fuel is intended to put the private sector in a "privileged position".

**中文理解：** 马可·卢比奥，美国国务卿，说燃料协议的目的是让私营部门处于"特权地位"。

**句子结构：** `Marco Rubio, ..., says [the deal ... is intended to put ...]`.

**关键词：**
- `privileged position` ⭐⭐⭐ — 特权地位

**表达方式：** 引号 marks policy rationale.

**为什么这样写：** Clarifies US intent; "privileged" signals tiered policy.

> **原句 22:** Unfortunately, his boss has not got the memo.

**中文理解：** 不幸的是，他的老板没有领会这个信息。

**句子结构：** 简单主谓宾

**关键词：**
- `has not got the memo` ⭐⭐ — 没领会、没跟上

**表达方式：** Idiom introduces jarring policy reversal.

**为什么这样写：** Tiny sentence amplifies contradiction before details.

> **原句 23:** On September 30th, without explanation, Mr Trump's administration reversed a 2024 rule that had let Cuban entrepreneurs bank with American institutions, forcing banks to freeze their accounts.

**中文理解：** 九月三十日，没有任何解释，特朗普先生的政府推翻了 2024 年一项允许古巴企业家与美国机构银行往来的规则，迫使银行冻结了它们的账户。

**句子结构：** 时间状语 + `reversed a 2024 rule [that had let...]`, 伴随状语 `forcing banks...`.

**关键词：**
- `without explanation` ⭐⭐ — 没有解释
- `reversed` ⭐⭐ — 反转
- `entrepreneurs` ⭐⭐ — 企业家
- `forcing ... to freeze` ⭐⭐ — 迫使冻结

**表达方式：** "without explanation" emphasises arbitrary damage; reversal + forcing creates harm.

**为什么这样写：** Central internal contradiction of US policy.

### 第 6 段：回撤与 fenced-directory

> **原句 24:** Mr Trump should backtrack and return to helping private firms grow.

**中文理解：** 特朗普先生应撤回到并恢复帮助私营企业增长。

**句子结构：** `should backtrack and return to helping...`.

**关键词：** `backtrack` ⭐⭐⭐ — 退让/回头

**表达方式：** Direct normative command.

**为什么这样写：** Explicit recommendation.

> **原句 25:** Many object that Cuba's private businesses are a facade for the regime, or that America's policies will create a new oligarchy.

**中文理解：** 许多人反对说，古巴私营企业只是政权的门面，或美国政策会造就一个新的寡头统治。

**句子结构：** `Many object [that ... or that ...]`.

**关键词：**
- `facade` ⭐⭐⭐ — 门面、假象
- `oligarchy` ⭐⭐⭐ — 寡头政治

**表达方式：** "that ... or that ..." captures two objection streams.

**为什么这样写：** Air objections fairly to answer them.

> **原句 26:** But the American administration can do much to weed out importers that are merely fronts for Cuba's government.

**中文理解：**  but 美国政府能够大量清除那些只是古巴政府前台的进口商。

**句子结构：** `can do much to weed out importers [that are merely fronts...]`.

**关键词：**
- `weed out` ⭐⭐⭐ — 清除、剔除
- `fronts` ⭐⭐ — 前台组织

**表达方式：** "merely fronts" narrows accusation scope.

**为什么这样写：** Reframe policy solvable via enforcement rather than abandonment.

> **原句 27:** The announcement on October 7th that America had seized some 90 fuel shipments headed not for private buyers but for a state firm under sanctions is a welcome sign.

**中文理解：** 十月七日的宣布——美国查押了约 90 批燃料装货，它们的目的地不是私营买家而是受制裁的国有公司——是个可喜的迹象。

**句子结构：** `The announcement [that America had seized...] is a welcome sign` with apposition.

**关键词：**
- `seized` ⭐⭐⭐ — 查押、没收
- `shipments` ⭐⭐ — 装货、运输批次
- `under sanctions` ⭐⭐ — 受制裁

**表达方式：** "headed not for private buyers but for a state firm" contrast enforcement target.

**为什么这样写：** Evidence enforcement exists, so facade worry manageable.

> **原句 28:** On balance, it is better for Cubans if the children of the ruling class focus on getting rich rather than repressing them.

**中文理解：** 总体而言，对古巴人更好，如果统治阶级的孩子专注于致富而不是压迫他们。

**句子结构：** `On balance, it is better for Cubans if...`.

**关键词：**
- `On balance` ⭐⭐⭐ — 总体而言
- `getting rich rather than repressing` ⭐⭐ — 致富而非镇压

**表达方式：** Stark binary frames incentive realignment.

**为什么这样写：** Provocative but utilitarian conclusion; editor acknowledges second-best.

### 第 8 段：委内瑞拉油转售的坏激励

> **原句 31:** A new licence allows some Venezuelan oil to be resold to Cuba's private sector.

**中文理解：** 一项新的许可证允许一些委内瑞拉石油转售给古巴私营部门。

**关键词：** `resold` ⭐⭐ — 转售

**中文理解：** 这可能复活加拉加斯—哈瓦那航线，但这次由美国政府和美国公司抽取它们的提成。

> **原句 33:** Mr Trump would then be invested in keeping the oil flowing, whatever the situation in Cuba.

**中文理解：** 特朗普先生那时就会 vested in 保持石油流动，无论古巴局势如何。

**关键词：**

> **原句 34:** Instead, private actors need the freedom to vie for business competitively.

**中文理解：** 相反，私营行动者需要自由地为了业务竞技竞争。

**句子结构：** `private actors need the freedom to vie for business competitively`.

## 长难句专项

### 原句 1（时态长句）

> SINCE HIS return to power, President Donald Trump has made clear that he hopes to bring down Cuba's repressive regime.

**主干：** President Donald Trump has made clear ...  
**从句：** that he hopes to bring down ...  
**状语：** Since his return to power  

**拆解技巧：** 先抓 "has made clear"，再看冒号/that 宾语。

### 原句 19（带 Appositive 与破折号）

> Cuba-watchers hope the government may next be forced to privatise power plants—and even the state's oil-and-gas arm, Cupet.

**主干：** Cuba-watchers hope (the government may ... be forced to privatise ...)  
**扩展：** —and even the state's oil-and-gas arm, Cupet  

**技巧：** 破折号扩展 scope，不用 complicates base meaning.

### 原句 27（嵌入 Appositive）

> The announcement on October 7th that America had seized some 90 fuel shipments headed not for private buyers but for a state firm under sanctions is a welcome sign.

**主干：** The announcement ... is a welcome sign  
**同位/从句：** that America had seized ... shipments ...  
**短语：** headed not for private buyers but for a state firm under sanctions.

**技巧：** 先拿主谓表 is a welcome sign，再还原 that-clause.

## 精读结束总结

1. 本文核心表达：战略与道德两难 — 封锁造成苦难但无意放松 regime 垄断，应助私部门而非拖后腿。
2. 重要语法：since-his-return 状语、冒号“approach: blockading”、破折号 scope。
3. 写作技巧：简短结论句、stark binary（getting rich rather than repressing）、numbers for credibility.
