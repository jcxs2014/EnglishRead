# 5 On the Rise and Fall of Publishing's "Make Book". — 精读分析

## 概览
- **来源**：Literary Hub｜2026-10-29｜URL: https://lithub.com/on-the-rise-and-fall-of-publishings-make-book/
- **副题**：在注重优化的时代，“make book”（编辑用热情打造一本书）为什么死了；它为什么活着
- **主题**：在社交媒体数字意义不大的时代，出版社仍要冒险投资新作家；“make book”——编辑对一本书格外热爱，试图通过高质量的执行和销售、市场团队的投入，把一本书写成畅销书——是乐观的但艰难的企图；公司合并和小型出版社的消失，使它更难出现。
- **结构**：引言（直觉与数字的对抗）→ 处女登场难题 → “make book”概念 → 使它成功的条件 → 案例与一个额外要求 → 7位数预付费与2026年的现实 → 公司合并的压力 → 小型出版社的消亡（Catapult、Graywolf、Tin House）→ 结尾呼吁
- **核心金句**：
> gut feelings are more valuable than statistics
> the process of making art was never a numbers game
> you know them when you see them
> a coordinated labor of love
> the belief that the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career for a new author
> We need healthy small presses with small budgets and big ideas to look out for writers and create a sustainable publishing ecosystem

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：引言与处女登场难题
> In this optimization-obsessed age, it can feel downright subversive to say that gut feelings are more valuable than statistics. But the process of making art was never a numbers game. It's the unquantifiable qualities that make the great books great: you know them when you see them. Commerce, of course, is different.
> Such discord illustrates the book industry's debut novel conundrum. You're asking a publisher to take a chance on a writer with no track record at a time when social media numbers mean almost nothing. Maybe if the author is well-connected their work will come with a few fancy blurbs from other writers vouching for this new talent, but beyond that there's not much else to go on except the work. All you've got is the hope of introducing a new writer to the world and hoping readers will like the way that writer arranges the words on a page.
- **原句 1**：In this optimization-obsessed age, it can feel downright subversive to say that gut feelings are more valuable than statistics。But the process of making art was never a numbers game。It's the unquantifiable qualities that make the great books great: you know them when you see them。Commerce, of course, is different。
- **中文理解**：在这个注重优化的时代，说直觉比统计更有价值，可能会让人觉得非常反叛。但艺术创作的过程从来就不是数字游戏。是不可量化的品质造就了真正优秀的书：你知道它们何时出现。当然，商业不同。

- **中文理解**：这种不一致性说明了出版行业处女登场小说的难题。你是在要求出版社冒险投资一个没有过往记录的作家，而社交媒体数字在这个时候几乎没有意义。如果作者有关系，他们的作品可能会附带几条来自其他作家的精美推荐语，证明这个新人才，但除此之外，除了作品本身，没有太多可依赖的信息。你所能拥有的，只有将新作家介绍给世界的希望，并希望读者会喜欢作家排列页面上文字的方式。

- **关键词**：optimization-obsessed age；gut feelings are more valuable than statistics；the process of making art was never a numbers game；unquantifiable qualities；you know them when you see them；debut novel conundrum；take a chance on a writer with no track record；social media numbers mean almost nothing；fancy blurbs；the way that writer arranges the words on a page。
- **表达方式**：用 `it can feel downright subversive to say that gut feelings are more valuable than statistics`——**“直觉比统计更有价值”**，在注重优化的时代说起来“稍显颠覆”；用 `It's the unquantifiable qualities that make the great books great`——**强调句**，把“不可量化的品质”写成伟大书籍的真正成分；用 `you know them when you see them`——**“你看见了就知道”**，把“质量”写成一种直觉判断，而非指标；用 `the book industry's debut novel conundrum`——**“处女登场小说难题”**，把“投资新人”写成一个固有的“难题”。

### 第 2 段：“Make book”概念
> Back when I was an assistant at Simon & Schuster in the early aughts I was introduced to the concept of the "make book," a particular book that an editor or publisher felt extra passionate about and wanted to try to "make" a success. A make book did not depend on the recognizability of the author's name or the sellability of their idea; it was all in the execution. The job of the editor was to spread their enthusiasm to sales and marketing and other departments, with the hopes of building momentum for the book that would eventually penetrate the outside world.
- **原句 3**：Back when I was an assistant at Simon & Schuster in the early aughts I was introduced to the concept of the "make book," a particular book that an editor or publisher felt extra passionate about and wanted to try to "make" a success。A make book did not depend on the recognizability of the author's name or the sellability of their idea; it was all in the execution。The job of the editor was to spread their enthusiasm to sales and marketing and other departments, with the hopes of building momentum for the book that would eventually penetrate the outside world。
- **中文理解**：回到我在西蒙＆ Schuster 担任助理的早期（2000年代初），我被介绍到“make book”这个概念，指的是编辑或出版者对某本书特别有热情，并想尝试“make”成功。Make book 不依赖于作者姓名的可识别性或其观点的可销售性；一切都在执行上。编辑的工作是把他们的热情传播到销售、市场和其他部门，希望为最终渗透外部世界的书建立 momentum（势头）。
- **句子结构**：(1) `Back when I was an assistant at Simon & Schuster in the early aughts I was introduced to the concept of the "make book," a particular book that an editor or publisher felt extra passionate about and wanted to try to "make" a success`——`Back when I was an assistant ... I was introduced to ...` 时间状语；`a particular book that an editor or publisher felt extra passionate about and wanted to try to "make" a success` 后置定语，`that` 限制性定语从句；(2) `A make book did not depend on the recognizability of the author's name or the sellability of their idea; it was all in the execution`——并列句；`it was all in the execution` 强调“一切都在执行”；(3) `The job of the editor was to spread their enthusiasm to sales and marketing and other departments, with the hopes of building momentum for the book that would eventually penetrate the outside world`——`to spread ...` 不定式作表语；`with the hopes of building momentum for the book that would eventually penetrate the outside world` 介词短语，`that would eventually penetrate the outside world` 定语从句。
- **关键词**：the concept of the "make book"；extra passionate about；make a success；the recognizability of the author's name；the sellability of their idea；it was all in the execution；spread their enthusiasm to sales and marketing；building momentum；penetrate the outside world。
- **表达方式**：用 `Back when I was an assistant at Simon & Schuster in the early aughts`——**“回到2000年代初，我在西蒙＆ Schuster 担任助理时”**，用个人经历把概念引入文章；用 `a particular book that an editor or publisher felt extra passionate about and wanted to try to "make" a success`——**“一个编辑或出版者对某本书特别热情，并想尝试使其成功”**，用“背叛”这个词定义 make book；用 `it was all in the execution`——**“一切都在执行上”**，三连音节的短句，一针见血；用 `with the hopes of building momentum for the book that would eventually penetrate the outside world`——**“建立势头”**，把“让一本书成功”写成“推动一本书穿越出版大门”。
- **为什么这样写**：这一段把“make book”从一个商业术语，定义为“编辑的热情 + 执行 + 团队的势头”。
- **段落逻辑**：Back when I was an assistant at Simon & Schuster in the early aughts → introduced to the concept of the "make book" → a particular book → extra passionate about → make a success → did not depend on the recognizability of the author's name → sellability of their idea → it was all in the execution → the job of the editor → spread their enthusiasm to sales and marketing → building momentum → penetrate the outside world

### 第 3 段：make book 的条件
> The idea of the make book is essentially optimistic: that a publisher, through a coordinated labor of love, could turn a book into a bestseller. The make book requires a number of conditions in order to work: a publisher willing to risk the time and effort of their staff on a book that is not a slam dunk, a wider company ethos that values the making of art, the ability of publishing workers to trust their instincts to determine if a book is special, and, most importantly, a trust in the market to elevate worthy literary material.
> The idea of the make book is essentially optimistic: that a publisher, through a coordinated labor of love, could turn a book into a bestseller.
> Viet Than Nguyen's The Sympathizer is a great example of a make book that worked, as is Curtis Sittenfeld's Prep. It's in looking at these authors bodies of work that I add one more requirement for a make book's success: the belief that the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career for a new author.
- **原句 4**：The idea of the make book is essentially optimistic: that a publisher, through a coordinated labor of love, could turn a book into a bestseller。The make book requires a number of conditions in order to work: a publisher willing to risk the time and effort of their staff on a book that is not a slam dunk, a wider company ethos that values the making of art, the ability of publishing workers to trust their instincts to determine if a book is special, and, most importantly, a trust in the market to elevate worthy literary material。
- **中文理解**：make book 的理念本质上是乐观的：出版社通过协调的爱的劳动，可以把一本书变成畅销书。make book 要运作，需要满足许多条件：一家愿意把员工的时间和精力冒险投入到一本“不是击鼓传奇”的书上的出版社，一个重视艺术创作的更广泛的公司文化，出版工作者相信自己的直觉，判断一本书是否特别的能力，以及最重要的是，相信市场会抬升有价值的文学材料。
- **原句 5**：The idea of the make book is essentially optimistic: that a publisher, through a coordinated labor of love, could turn a book into a bestseller。
- **中文理解**：make book 的理念本质上是乐观的：出版社通过协调的爱的劳动，可以把一本书变成畅销书。
- **原句 6**：Viet Than Nguyen's The Sympathizer is a great example of a make book that worked, as is Curtis Sittenfeld's Prep。It's in looking at these authors bodies of work that I add one more requirement for a make book's success: the belief that the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career for a new author。
- **中文理解**：越泰作家（原文拼写 "Viet Than Nguyen"，应为 Viet Thanh Nguyen）《The Sympathizer》是成功的 make book 的一个很好的例子，Curtis Sittenfeld 的《Prep》也是如此。我在看这些作者的作品时，我补充了一个 make book 成功的额外要求：相信处女登场的突破不是终点，而是新作者可持续职业生涯的开始。
- **句子结构**：(1) `The idea of the make book is essentially optimistic: that a publisher, through a coordinated labor of love, could turn a book into a bestseller`——`The idea ... is essentially optimistic:` 系表结构；冒号后是同位语从句 `that a publisher ... could turn a book into a bestseller`；(2) `The make book requires a number of conditions in order to work: a publisher willing to risk ..., a wider company ethos ..., the ability of publishing workers to trust ..., and ... a trust in the market to elevate worthy literary material`——动词 `requires` 后接 `a number of conditions` 宾语，冒号后是具体条件列表（四项并列）；`willing to risk ...` 过去分词短语作后置定语；`in order to work` 不定式作目的；(3) `Viet Than Nguyen's The Sympathizer is a great example of a make book that worked, as is Curtis Sittenfeld's Prep`——`as is ...` 比较结构；`that worked` 限定性定语从句；(4) `It's in looking at these authors bodies of work that I add one more requirement for a make book's success: the belief that the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career for a new author`——强调句 `It's ... that ...`；`the belief that ...` 宾语从句；`that the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career for a new author` 双重 `that` 从句。
- **关键词**：essentially optimistic；coordinated labor of love；turn a book into a bestseller；in order to work；a publisher willing to risk the time and effort of their staff；a wider company ethos that values the making of art；a trust in the market to elevate worthy literary material；the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career。
- **表达方式**：用 `essentially optimistic: that a publisher, through a coordinated labor of love, could turn a book into a bestseller`——**“协调的爱的劳动”**，把“赚钱”写成“爱的劳动”；用 `a publisher willing to risk the time and effort of their staff on a book that is not a slam dunk`——**“不是击鼓传奇”**，把“不确定性”写成“不敢押注”；用 `the ability of publishing workers to trust their instincts to determine if a book is special`——**“相信直觉判断一本书是否特别”**，把“专业判断”写成信任；用 `the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career for a new author`——**“处女登场的突破不是终点，而是新作者可持续职业生涯的开始”**，强调“可持续的人才培养”。
- **为什么这样写**：这段把“make book”从一个单纯的商业策略，扩展为一种“对新人才的培养伦理”。
- **段落逻辑**：The idea of the make book is essentially optimistic → coordinated labor of love → turn a book into a bestseller → requires a number of conditions → a publisher willing to risk → not a slam dunk → a wider company ethos → a trust in the market → Viet Than Nguyen's The Sympathizer → Curtis Sittenfeld's Prep → breakout of the debut is not the end goal → the beginning of a sustainable career

### 第 4 段：7位数预付费与2026年的现实
> Make books often aren't successful–the stars still have to align in a particular way to create a hit and even the most dedicated publishers can't bend the markets to their will. But there are also plenty of flashy million-dollar debuts whose titles I can no longer remember. Remember: the debut that lands a $1 million advance (think Yesteryear) is not a make book, the debut with a 7-figure advance is one that must achieve some success in order not to be a total flop.
> What's still true in 2026 is that debut novels with modest advances just about always count as make books. Debuts need the care and attention of a staff that's invested in breaking them out simply because publishers believe in the importance of developing new voices.
- **原句 7**：Make books often aren't successful–the stars still have to align in a particular way to create a hit and even the most dedicated publishers can't bend the markets to their will。But there are also plenty of flashy million-dollar debuts whose titles I can no longer remember。Remember: the debut that lands a $1 million advance (think Yesteryear) is not a make book, the debut with a 7-figure advance is one that must achieve some success in order not to be a total flop。
- **中文理解**：make book 经常不成功——要制造一场热门，星星仍然必须以某种特定方式排列，而且即使是最投入的出版商也不能改变市场。然而，还有许多花哨的百万美元处女登场，其标题我已经不记得了。记住：以100万美元预付费（想想 Yesteryear）的处女登场不是 make book；以7位数预付费的处女登场是必须取得一定成功的，否则就是彻底失败。
- **原句 8**：What's still true in 2026 is that debut novels with modest advances just about always count as make books。Debuts need the care and attention of a staff that's invested in breaking them out simply because publishers believe in the importance of developing new voices。
- **中文理解**：2026 年仍然成立的是，处女登场小说在适度预付费情况下几乎总是构成 make book。处女登场需要被投入精力的员工的关怀和关注，因为出版者相信培养新声音的重要性。
- **句子结构**：(1) `Make books often aren't successful–the stars still have to align in a particular way to create a hit and even the most dedicated publishers can't bend the markets to their will`——并列句；`the stars still have to align in a particular way to create a hit` 比喻；`can't bend the markets to their will` 虚拟隐喻；(2) `But there are also plenty of flashy million-dollar debuts whose titles I can no longer remember`——`plenty of ... debuts whose titles ...`；(3) `Remember: the debut that lands a $1 million advance (think Yesteryear) is not a make book, the debut with a 7-figure advance is one that must achieve some success in order not to be a total flop`——两个 `that` 限定性定语从句；`in order not to be a total flop` 不定式目的；(4) `What's still true in 2026 is that debut novels with modest advances just about always count as make books`——强调句 `What's still true in 2026`；(5) `Debuts need the care and attention of a staff that's invested in breaking them out simply because publishers believe in the importance of developing new voices`——`that's invested in breaking them out` 现在完成式定语从句；`simply because publishers believe in the importance of developing new voices` 原因状语从句。
- **关键词**：the stars still have to align；can't bend the markets to their will；flashy million-dollar debuts；lands a $1 million advance；in order not to be a total flop；debut novels with modest advances；just about always count as make books；invested in breaking them out。
- **表达方式**：用 `the stars still have to align in a particular way to create a hit`——**“星星必须以某种特定方式排列”**，把“畅销”写成一种命运的偶然；用 `can't bend the markets to their will`——**“不能把市场弯到他们的意志上”**，把市场写成一个强大的对手；用 `$1 million advance (think Yesteryear)` 与 `7-figure advance`——**“100万美元预付费”** 与 **“7位数预付费”**，用数字建立两档标准；用 `debut novels with modest advances just about always count as make books`——**“适度预付费的处女登场小说几乎总是构成 make book”**，把“小预付费”与“make book”重新绑定。

### 第 5 段：公司合并的压力
> Corporate consolidation has made the conditions much more difficult for make books to break out. At conglomerate publishers today, employees might get to pick one book across a variety of imprints for the company's sales and marketing teams to really focus on. These teams are selling hundreds of books per season and there simply isn't time enough to make deep personal pitches for every book they love (here is where I remind you that for the most part these teams are overwhelmed, not disinterested).
> More and more, it falls to smaller presses to take chances on new voices and to nurture them. Which is why last week's news that Catapult Books would be shutting down at the end of the month is particularly devastating. In its decade of operation, Catapult has helped a lot of prominent writers get their starts.Catapult took chances on writers like Lauren Oyler, Nicole Chung, Chibundu Onuzo, and Chelsea Bieker, who later sold their subsequent books to larger presses.
> Before Percival Everett received 7-figures from Random House for James, the indie press Graywolf gave him plenty of room to experiment and grow as a writer. Annie Hartnett, whose latest novel The Road to Tender Hearts is a consistent seller, got her start at Tin House (an indie that was purchased by Zando in 2025)。We need healthy small presses with small budgets and big ideas to look out for writers and create a sustainable publishing ecosystem。But the outlook for developing new voices looks a little bit bleaker as another small creative press bites the dust。

## 长难句专项

**原句**：The make book requires a number of conditions in order to work: a publisher willing to risk the time and effort of their staff on a book that is not a slam dunk, a wider company ethos that values the making of art, the ability of publishing workers to trust their instincts to determine if a book is special, and, most importantly, a trust in the market to elevate worthy literary material.

**解剖**：
- 结构：`The make book requires a number of conditions in order to work:`——动词 `requires` 后接 `a number of conditions` 宾语，冒号后是条件列表。
- 列表共四项：(1) `a publisher willing to risk the time and effort of their staff on a book that is not a slam dunk`；(2) `a wider company ethos that values the making of art`；(3) `the ability of publishing workers to trust their instincts to determine if a book is special`；(4) `and, most importantly, a trust in the market to elevate worthy literary material`。
- 语法要点：`willing to risk ...` 过去分词短语作后置定语；`that is not a slam dunk` 限定性定语从句；`the ability of publishing workers to trust their instincts` 其中 `to trust their instincts to determine if a book is special` 是不定式短语作后置定语；`a trust in the market to elevate worthy literary material` 中 `to elevate ...` 是不定式作后置定语。
**难点**：四项并列条件与大量嵌套的不定式/定语从句混在一起，阅读时需逐项拆分；`in order to work` 是“为了让它运作”。
**翻译要点**：Make book 需要很多条件才能运作：一家愿意把员工的时间和精力冒险投入到一本“不是击鼓传奇”的书上的出版社；一个重视艺术创作的更广泛的公司文化；出版工作者相信自己的直觉，判断一本书是否特别的能力；以及最重要的是，相信市场会抬升有价值的文学材料。

**原句**：It's in looking at these authors bodies of work that I add one more requirement for a make book's success: the belief that the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career for a new author.

**解剖**：
- 强调句：`It's in looking at these authors bodies of work that I add one more requirement for a make book's success`——强调句，将 `in looking at these authors bodies of work` 提前强调。
- 冒号后是同位语：`the belief that the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career for a new author`。
- 宾语从句：`that the break out of the debut is not the end goal, but rather the beginning of a sustainable career for a new author`，其中 `but rather` 并列结构。
**难点**：`It's ... that ...` 强调句的强调成分是 `in looking at these authors bodies of work`；`authors bodies of work` 应为 `authors' bodies of work`（缺少所有格，明显笔误）；`but rather` 是“而不是”的固定用法。
**翻译要点**：正是在看这些作者的作品时，我为 make book 的成功增加了一个额外要求：相信处女登场的突破不是终点，而是新作者可持续职业生涯的开始。

**原句**：Remember: the debut that lands a $1 million advance (think Yesteryear) is not a make book, the debut with a 7-figure advance is one that must achieve some success in order not to be a total flop.

**解剖**：
- `Remember:` 为提示句，后接两个并列的 `that` 限定性定语从句。
- `the debut that lands a $1 million advance (think Yesteryear) is not a make book`——`lands a $1 million advance` 现在时；`(think Yesteryear)` 插入语，表示举例；`is not a make book` 系表结构。
- `the debut with a 7-figure advance is one that must achieve some success in order not to be a total flop`——`with a 7-figure advance` 介词短语作后置定语；`is one that must achieve some success in order not to be a total flop`——`one` 代指 `the debut`。
**难点**：两个并列的 `that` 限定性定语从句；`(think Yesteryear)` 是插入的例证；`in order not to be a total flop` 是目的状语。
**翻译要点**：记住：以100万美元预付费（想想 Yesteryear）的处女登场不是 make book；以7位数预付费的处女登场是必须取得一定成功的，否则就是彻底失败。

## 精读结束总结
1. **本文核心表达**：在社交媒体数字价值不大的时代，出版社仍要冒险投资新作家；“make book”——编辑对一本书格外热情，通过高质量的执行和销售、市场团队的投入，把一本书写成畅销书——是乐观的，也是艰难的；公司合并和小型出版社的消失，使它更难出现。
2. **结构特点**：以“直觉 vs. 数字”的引言开场，用“make book”概念、条件、案例、进阶、现实和小型出版社的链条，完成一篇从个人到行业的完整论述。
3. **写作策略**：用数字（$1 million / 7-figure / 2026）做边界，用“星星必须排列”“不能把市场弯到自己的意志上”做隐喻，用 Catapult、Graywolf、Tin House 三个具体案例把宏观的“公司合并”写成可感知的“损失”。
4. **读者提醒**：文中 "israeli" 全部小写、"Resiman" 拼写错误等原文笔误，需逐字核对引用。
