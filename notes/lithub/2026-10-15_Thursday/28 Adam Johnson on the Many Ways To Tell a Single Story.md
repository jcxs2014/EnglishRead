# Adam Johnson on the Many Ways To Tell a Single Story（Adam Johnson 谈讲述一个故事的多种方式）— 精读分析

## 概览
- **来源**：Literary Hub｜作家对谈（The Center for Fiction 活动，与谈者 Amitav Ghosh）｜出版 Tue, 13 Oct 2026｜URL: https://lithub.com/adam-johnson-on-the-many-ways-to-tell-a-single-story/
- **副题**：叙事形式与创伤经验
- **主题**：普利策奖得主 Adam Johnson 谈新作《The Wayfinder》以及他如何讲述"一个故事"。核心论点是"叙事是一台制造意义的机器"（narrative is a meaning-making machine），而讲述创伤故事时，这台机器会"把痛苦带回来"。他讲述自己采访朝鲜脱北者的经历——他们的故事"碎片化、乱序、视角转换"，因此《The Orphan Master’s Son》必须碎片化、必须变换视角、必须让读者自己拼合。他还谈到"忘掉小说这个词"的便签，以及口头传统与现代小说形式之间的张力。
- **结构**：A 活动导言 → B 提问（口头传统 vs. 书面小说）→ C 叙事是意义制造机（自传→小说）→ D 脱北者的碎片叙事与痛苦 → E 故事的片段与视角转换 → F 忘掉"小说"一词 → G 作者简介
- **核心金句**："I do believe that narrative is a meaning-making machine, and that it works on all these levels of meaning of, linguistically, of memory, of emotion, of chronology, of causality, of structure."（我确实相信，叙事是一台制造意义的机器，它作用于所有这些意义层面——语言、记忆、情感、时间顺序、因果、结构。）

### 第 2 段：提问

> **原句 3:** [Audience Member]: As you said, you’re writing, from or into this culture that has a great oral tradition. Obviously, with writing a novel, authorship is very modern, with the Eastern European or European idea that there’s the story and then there’s a story as your uncle tells it.

**中文理解**：[观众]：正如你说的，你正在写，从或进入这个有伟大口头传统的文化。显然，写小说时，作者是非常现代的，带着东欧或欧洲的观念：有一个故事，然后有一个你叔叔讲的故事。
**句子结构**：`[Audience Member]: As you said, you’re writing, from or into this culture that has a great oral tradition. Obviously, with writing a novel, authorship is very modern, with the Eastern European or European idea that there’s the story and then there’s a story as your uncle tells it.` 引语（含 As 从句 + that 定语从句 + with 复合结构 + that 同位语从句）。

**段落逻辑：** As you said → culture with oral tradition → authorship very modern → the story vs. uncle's story → 核心问题

### 第 3 段：叙事是意义制造机

### 第 5 段：片段与视角

> **原句 17:** And I don’t know if you have a grandma who tells you a bit of a story about the old country every holiday. And it’s your job to put them all together.

**中文理解**：我不知道你是否有一个奶奶，每个节日都给你讲一点关于故国的故事。把它们全部拼起来是你的工作。
**句子结构**：`And I don’t know if you have a grandma who tells you a bit of a story about the old country every holiday. And it’s your job to put them all together.` 主谓宾（含 if 从句 + who 定语从句）+ 主系表。

## 长难句专项

**原句**：I do believe that narrative is a meaning-making machine, and that it works on all these levels of meaning of, linguistically, of memory, of emotion, of chronology, of causality, of structure.

**解剖**：
- 主句：I do believe that narrative is a meaning-making machine, and that it works on all these levels of meaning ...。
- 两个并列 that 宾语从句：① that narrative is a meaning-making machine；② that it works on all these levels of meaning。
- 六个并列 of 短语：of, linguistically, of memory, of emotion, of chronology, of causality, of structure。
**难点**：`of, linguistically, of memory` 中第一个 `of` 后直接接副词 linguistically，结构松散（口语），读者容易误判并列关系。
**翻译要点**：按"我确实相信，叙事是一台制造意义的机器，它作用于所有这些意义层面——语言、记忆、情感、时间顺序、因果、结构"翻译，把 of 短语处理为列举。

---

**原句**：You write stories, right? You write about your family. You write about your journey, your culture, right? And my first two books were very much like that.

**解剖**：
- 四个短句：You write stories, right? / You write about your family. / You write about your journey, your culture, right? / And my first two books were very much like that.
- `right?` 是附加疑问（tag question），口语中表确认。
**难点**：多个短句连续，读者容易忽略其节奏功能；`right?` 的语用功能是邀请认同，而非真正提问。
**翻译要点**：保留短句与反问的口语节奏——"你写故事，对吧？你写你的家庭。你写你的旅程，你的文化，对吧？"

---

**原句**：When I tried to write about North Korea—and why I did that is another question or mystery. Still a mystery—I just knew it could never be like a "beginning, middle, end" western sensibility.

**解剖**：
- 主句：When I tried to write about North Korea ..., I just knew it could never be like a "beginning, middle, end" western sensibility。
- 破折号插入：and why I did that is another question or mystery. Still a mystery。
**难点**：插入语用破折号包裹，且内部含两个句子，读者容易丢失主句的 When 从句骨架；`western sensibility` 是抽象名词短语。
**翻译要点**：按"当我试图写朝鲜时——而我为什么那样做是另一个问题或谜，至今仍是一个谜——我只是知道它永远不可能像……的西方感性"翻译。

## 精读结束总结
1. **本文核心表达**：叙事是一台"意义制造机"，它既是解药也是复发——讲述创伤时，它会把痛苦带回来。因此，面对脱北者碎片化、乱序、视角漂移的叙事，小说也必须碎片化、必须变换视角、必须让读者自己拼合。作者甚至贴了一张"忘掉小说"的便签，因为"小说"是一个有传统、有惯例、有历史的词，命名会背叛材料。
2. **重要语法**：
   - `deploy that machine` / `do it on ourselves`：机器隐喻贯穿全段
   - 破折号插入：`—and why I did that is another question or mystery. Still a mystery—`
   - `make + 宾语 + 宾补`：`made the story less accessible`
   - `leave it to the reader to do`：`leave it to the reader to put the story together`
   - 直接引语被主句插入：`He said, oh, we were scared.`
3. **写作技巧**：
   - **用亲历的引语立论**：脱北者"我们害怕/有守卫/河水很急"的碎片，比任何论证都有力。
   - **自我打断制造幽默**：`listen to that, "reading" oral histories`。
   - **便签作为结构装置**：`forget the word novel` 把抽象反思落成一个可触摸的动作。
   - **承认读者**：`I get that, but it just felt true to me` 把分歧写成两种真实。
4. **可迁移表达**：
   - "narrative is a meaning-making machine"
   - "it works on all these levels of meaning"
   - "when writers first deploy that machine, we do it on ourselves"
   - "Who am I? What does my life mean?"
   - "the book gives you the answers"
   - "I’ve got to go write about something else, which is fiction"
   - "his story came out of order in fragments"
   - "achieve the catharsis of externalizing it"
   - "the meaning-making machine brings the pain back"
   - "the dilemma of the hurt person"
   - "How do you tell the story without your story re-hurting you?"
   - "it’s your job to put them all together"
   - "he was in the first person plural"
   - "I put her on my back. I was swimming, but then he lost her."
   - "it could never be like a 'beginning, middle, end' western sensibility"
   - "the book had to fragment"
   - "leave it to the reader to put the story together"
   - "if I ever categorize it or name it, it will never have a chance to become the closest thing it could be to the material that’s informing it"
