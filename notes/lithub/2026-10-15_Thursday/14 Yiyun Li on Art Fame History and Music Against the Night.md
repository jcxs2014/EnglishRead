# Yiyun Li on Art, Fame, History, and Music Against the Night（李翊云谈艺术、声名、历史与《夜之音乐》）— 精读分析

## 概览
- **来源**：Literary Hub｜Fiction/Non/Fiction 播客访谈｜出版 Thu, 15 Oct 2026｜URL: https://lithub.com/yiyun-li-on-art-fame-history-and-music-against-the-night/
- **副题**：作家访谈（对谈者 V.V. Ganeshananthan、Whitney Terrell）
- **主题**：李翊云谈新作《Music Against the Night》——一对已婚音乐家 John Field 与 Adelaide Percheron（Percherette）的生平。她讲自己如何在托尔斯泰自传里"撞见"John Field、为什么 Percherette 只在传记里出现五次却足以撑起一部小说、她做历史研究的边界（学三年钢琴，但不去本地重走 Pondicherry→Moscow）、三卷本结构（童年/青年/成年与死亡），以及她对"成功与失败"的定义。
- **结构**：A 播客导言 → B 历史人物的缘起（托尔斯泰自传 → 五段提及 → 三卷本）→ C 研究边界（Edward P. Jones 更正 + 只读没人读的旧书）→ D 现场引读
- **核心金句**："Just to think about the trajectory of her life coming from Pondicherry, even just as a young woman traveling from Pondicherry all the way to Moscow. That's an epic novel."（只要想想她的人生轨迹——一个年轻女子从 Pondicherry 一路走到莫斯科。那就是一部史诗小说。）

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：播客导言

> **原句 1:** Acclaimed writer Yiyun Li returns to the show to talk about her new novel, Music Against the Night, with co-hosts V.V. Ganeshananthan and Whitney Terrell.

**中文理解**：备受赞誉的作家李翊云再次来到节目，与联合主持 V.V. Ganeshananthan、Whitney Terrell 谈她的新小说《Music Against the Night》。
**句子结构**：`Acclaimed writer Yiyun Li [returns to the show to talk about her new novel, Music Against the Night, with co-hosts ...]`。主谓宾（含 with 介词短语作状语）。
**关键词**：*Acclaimed* 备受赞誉的；*co-hosts* 联合主持。
**表达方式**：用 *Acclaimed*（受赞誉的）而非 *famous*——**学术性的褒扬而非娱乐性的名气**，为访谈定调。
**为什么这样写**：一句话交代人物、作品与对话者，是播客导言的标准开场。

**段落逻辑：** acclaimed writer → returns to the show → new novel + co-hosts → 访谈定调

---

> **原句 2:** She also shares how she chose to structure two wide-ranging life stories within one novel, and how her characters illustrate disparities in opportunity among equally talented peers.

**中文理解**：她也分享了如何把两个跨度极大的人生故事结构进一部小说，以及她的角色如何呈现同等才华的同侪之间机会的悬殊。
**句子结构**：`She [also shares how she chose to structure two wide-ranging life stories within one novel, and how her characters illustrate disparities in opportunity among equally talented peers]`。主谓宾（含两个并列 how 宾语从句）。
**关键词**：*wide-ranging* 跨度极大的；*disparities* 悬殊；*equally talented peers* 同等才华的同侪。
**表达方式**：用 *disparities in opportunity among equally talented peers*——**把"才华相等但机会不等"写成全书的社会命题**。
**为什么这样写**：预告访谈的深层议题（机会不均），把话题从"写作技巧"抬到"社会观察"。

**段落逻辑：** shares → how structure two life stories in one novel → how characters show opportunity disparities → 社会命题

### 第 2 段：John Field 的缘起

> **原句 3:** So I did meet John Field in Tolstoy’s autobiography, and John Field was Tolstoy’s mother’s music master.

**中文理解**：所以我是从托尔斯泰自传里认识 John Field 的，John Field 是托尔斯泰母亲的音乐教师。
**句子结构**：`So I [did meet John Field in Tolstoy’s autobiography], and John Field [was Tolstoy’s mother’s music master]`。并列句（did 表强调）。
**关键词**：*autobiography* 自传；*music master* 音乐教师。
**表达方式**：用 `did meet` 的强调式——**把"偶然相遇"写成命运的确认**，与前文 *run into an Anglicized name* 呼应。
**为什么这样写**：交代人物出处，说明这个历史人物是"撞见"而非"寻找"来的，呼应她的研究哲学。

**段落逻辑：** So → meet John Field in Tolstoy's autobiography → he was music master → 缘起

---

> **原句 4:** It’s very odd when you read a Russian novel, a Russian book, and all of a sudden, you run into an Anglicized name: John Field.

**中文理解**：当你读一本俄国小说、一本俄文书，突然撞见一个英化的名字：John Field，这非常奇怪。
**句子结构**：`It [is very odd when you read a Russian novel, a Russian book, and all of a sudden, you run into an Anglicized name: John Field]`。形式主语 + when 从句（含冒号同位语）。
**关键词**：*odd* 奇怪的；*all of a sudden* 突然；*run into* 撞见；*Anglicized* 英化的。
**表达方式**：用 `run into`（撞见）与冒号引出 *John Field*——**把学术发现写成一次"邂逅"**，口语化、有画面。
**为什么这样写**：让读者亲历她的困惑（俄文书里的爱尔兰名字），为后文"Percherette 只出现五次"的悬念铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** odd → read Russian book → run into Anglicized name → 学术邂逅的困惑

---

> **原句 5:** So I did a little research, read a very slim biography, half of which was just musical scores.

**中文理解**：所以我做了一点研究，读了一本非常薄的小传，其中一半只是乐谱。
**句子结构**：`So I [did a little research, read a very slim biography, half of which was just musical scores]`。并列谓语（含 half of which 定语从句）。
**关键词**：*slim biography* 薄小传；*musical scores* 乐谱。
**表达方式**：用 `half of which was just musical scores`——**用"一半是乐谱"的荒诞细节消解传记的权威**，显出史料之稀薄。
**为什么这样写**：解释为什么 Percherette 的资料"查无"——因为连 John Field 的传记都薄到只剩乐谱，为后文"全是虚构"做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** So → did a little research → slim biography half musical scores → 史料稀薄

---

> **原句 6:** So Percherette, she’s the wife. She’s an important artist, but she was barely mentioned in the biography.

**中文理解**：所以 Percherette 是他的妻子。她是一位重要的艺术家，但在传记里几乎没被提及。
**句子结构**：`So Percherette, she’s the wife. She’s an important artist, but she was barely mentioned in the biography.` 三个短句，but 转折。
**关键词**：*barely mentioned* 几乎未被提及。
**表达方式**：用 `barely mentioned`——**用"缺席"本身作为写作的起点**，被历史抹去的人正是小说要打捞的人。
**为什么这样写**：点出全书的方法论：史料越少，虚构的空间越大；历史沉默处正是小说的入口。

**段落逻辑：** So → Percherette the wife → important artist but barely mentioned → 缺席即入口

---

> **原句 7:** And clearly one novel is not long enough to put everything in, so we just said, "Well, three novels. Why not?"

**中文理解**：显然一部小说装不下所有东西，所以我们干脆说，"好吧，三部小说。为什么不呢？"
**句子结构**：`And clearly [one novel is not long enough to put everything in], so we just said, "Well, three novels. Why not?"` 因果 + 直接引语。
**关键词**：*not long enough to put everything in* 装不下一切；*Why not?* 为什么不呢。
**表达方式**：用 `Well, three novels. Why not?` 的口语引语——**把严肃的结构决策写成一句玩笑式的自我对话**，幽默而自信。
**为什么这样写**：交代三卷本的由来，语气轻松，反衬前文"一部装不下"的严肃判断。

**段落逻辑：** clearly one novel not long enough → so three novels → Why not → 结构决策

### 第 3 段：Percherette 的空白

> **原句 8:** There’s no information to be found about her. It’s all in my head.

**中文理解**：关于她找不到任何信息。全在我脑子里。
**句子结构**：`There’s no information to be found about her. It’s all in my head.` 两个短句（There be + 主系表）。
**关键词**：*no information to be found* 找不到任何信息；*in my head* 在我脑子里。
**表达方式**：用 `It’s all in my head`——**把"虚构"坦然承认，反而成为创作的诚实**，与访谈的轻松语气一致。
**为什么这样写**：正面回答主持人的提问（你如何找到她的信息？），答案是没有——虚构就是她面对历史空白的方式。

**段落逻辑：** no information to be found → it's all in my head → 虚构即诚实

---

> **原句 9:** There was one funny mention saying ‘she is a flirtatious young woman.’

**中文理解**：有一处有趣的提及说"她是个爱调情的年轻女子"。
**句子结构**：`There was one funny mention saying ‘she is a flirtatious young woman.’` There be + 现在分词 saying + 直接引语。
**关键词**：*funny mention* 有趣的提及；*flirtatious* 爱调情的。
**表达方式**：用 `funny mention` 与直引——**把史料里唯一一句评价当作笑话讲**，既尊重又调侃。
**为什么这样写**：给 Percherette 一个"历史里的唯一声音"，并立刻引出下一句更生动的比喻。

**段落逻辑：** one funny mention → flirtatious young woman → 史料唯一声音

---

> **原句 10:** Now if you set a top hat over a stick, that’s enough for this young woman to start flirting.

**中文理解**：你要是把一顶礼帽架在一根棍子上，就足够让这个年轻女子开始调情了。
**句子结构**：`Now [if you set a top hat over a stick], that’s enough for this young woman to start flirting.` If 条件 + 主句（enough for ... to do）。
**关键词**：*top hat* 礼帽；*start flirting* 开始调情。
**表达方式**：用 `a top hat over a stick`（礼帽架在棍子上）的画面——**把"爱调情"的抽象评价落成一个滑稽的视觉形象**，是全场最生动的引语。
**为什么这样写**：用同时代人的刻薄比喻，既展示史料的语气，也让读者看见 Percherette 在历史里被怎样看待。

**段落逻辑：** if top hat over a stick → enough to start flirting → 滑稽画面

### 第 4 段：从 Pondicherry 到莫斯科

> **原句 11:** Just to think about the trajectory of her life coming from Pondicherry, even just as a young woman traveling from Pondicherry all the way to Moscow. That’s an epic novel.

**中文理解**：只要想想她的人生轨迹——从 Pondicherry 出发，哪怕只是作为一个年轻女子从 Pondicherry 一路走到莫斯科。那就是一部史诗小说。
**句子结构**：`Just to think about the trajectory of her life coming from Pondicherry, even just as a young woman traveling from Pondicherry all the way to Moscow. That’s an epic novel.` 两个独立句（含动名词短语 + 独立主格式判断）。
**关键词**：*trajectory* 轨迹；*Pondicherry* 本地治里；*epic novel* 史诗小说。
**表达方式**：用 `trajectory`（轨迹）这一数学/物理词——**把人生写成一条可测算的曲线**，学术词与"史诗"并置，反差强烈。
**为什么这样写**：点出 Percherette 的人生为何"史诗"——不是因为她伟大，而是因为她的移动本身超出了同时代女性的全部可能。

**段落逻辑：** think about trajectory → Pondicherry to Moscow → That's an epic novel → 移动即史诗

---

> **原句 12:** Percherette’s contemporaries are the characters in Jane Austen’s novels, and those girls just live in their home and sometimes go to the nextdoor neighbor’s home.

**中文理解**：Percherette 的同时代人是简·奥斯汀小说里的人物，那些女孩只住在自己家里，有时去隔壁邻居家。
**句子结构**：`Percherette’s contemporaries [are the characters in Jane Austen’s novels], and those girls [just live in their home and sometimes go to the nextdoor neighbor’s home]`。并列句。
**关键词**：*contemporaries* 同时代人；*nextdoor neighbor’s home* 隔壁邻居家。
**表达方式**：用 `live in their home and sometimes go to the nextdoor neighbor’s home`——**用空间之小反衬 Percherette 旅程之大**。
**为什么这样写**：用奥斯汀小说里的女性作参照系，让读者瞬间明白 Percherette 的"越界"有多远。

**段落逻辑：** contemporaries = Austen characters → live at home / nextdoor → 空间对照

---

> **原句 13:** So I just thought Pesharet is epic in that way—that she’s living this life that’s entirely foreign to most women her age. And I think that’s enough for a novel.

**中文理解**：所以我只是觉得 Pesharet（Percherette）在那种意义上是史诗的——她过着一种对大多数同龄女性完全陌生的生活。我觉得那就够写一部小说了。
**句子结构**：`So I just thought [Pesharet is epic in that way—that she’s living this life that’s entirely foreign to most women her age]. And I think [that’s enough for a novel].` 宾语从句 + 破折号同位语 + that 定语从句。
**关键词**：*epic* 史诗的；*entirely foreign* 完全陌生；*enough for a novel* 够写一部小说。
**表达方式**：用 `enough for a novel`——**把写作的门槛降到"够不够一部小说"**，朴素而坚定。
**为什么这样写**：给上一段的"史诗"下定义：不是宏大，而是"陌生"；陌生本身就是小说的充分条件。

**段落逻辑：** epic in that way → life foreign to women her age → enough for a novel → 陌生即充分

### 第 5 段：研究的边界

> **原句 14:** I’m fairly certain I was quoting Edward P. Jones.

**中文理解**：我相当确定我当时是在引用 Edward P. Jones。
**句子结构**：`I [am fairly certain (that) I was quoting Edward P. Jones]`。主系表 + 省略 that 的宾语从句。
**关键词**：*fairly certain* 相当确定；*quoting* 引用。
**表达方式**：用 `fairly certain`——**用"相当确定"而非"确定"，诚实面对记忆的不精确**，把更正写成风度。
**为什么这样写**：主动纠正主持人（学生引错的话），既是学术诚实，也是把功劳归还原作者。

**段落逻辑：** fairly certain → was quoting Edward P. Jones → 更正归还功劳

---

> **原句 15:** Because I remember Edward P. Jones, when he talked about researching for The Known World, he said he checked out all these books from the library, but he did not read a single book because he realized everything was in his brain.

**中文理解**：因为我记得 Edward P. Jones 在谈为《The Known World》做研究时说，他从图书馆借了所有这些书，但一本都没读，因为他意识到一切都在他脑子里。
**句子结构**：`Because I remember [Edward P. Jones, when he talked about researching for The Known World, said he checked out all these books from the library, but he did not read a single book because he realized everything was in his brain]`。Because 从句（含嵌套 when/because）。
**关键词**：*checked out* 借出；*a single book* 一本书；*in his brain* 在他脑子里。
**表达方式**：用 `did not read a single book`——**把"不读书"写成研究的高阶形态**，反常识却有力。
**为什么这样写**：借 Jones 的例子解释她的研究哲学：阅读是为了激发，而不是为了照搬。

**段落逻辑：** remember Edward P. Jones → checked out books but read none → everything in his brain → 研究即激发

---

> **原句 16:** He said, "If you write one thing wrong about this county, everybody will come to say you got the history wrong." And he said, "Why not just make up a county myself?"

**中文理解**：他说，"如果你把关于这个县的一件事写错，所有人都会来说你历史搞错了。"他又说，"为什么不干脆自己编一个县呢？"
**句子结构**：直接引语 + 直接引语（两句并列引语，含 if 条件句）。
**关键词**：*write one thing wrong* 写错一件事；*got the history wrong* 历史搞错；*make up a county* 编一个县。
**表达方式**：用 `Why not just make up a county myself?` 的反问——**把"规避史实风险"写成一种解放**，幽默而锋利。
**为什么这样写**：给出 Jones 的解决之道——与其被史实绑架，不如自己创造一个地方，正面回应"历史小说怎么写"的问题。

**段落逻辑：** if write one thing wrong → everybody says history wrong → Why not make up a county → 规避史实风险

---

> **原句 17:** Even if I go to Pondicherry today, I would not have seen Pondicherry in Percherette’s time.

**中文理解**：即使我今天去 Pondicherry，我也不会看见 Percherette 时代的 Pondicherry。
**句子结构**：`Even if I go to Pondicherry today, I would not have seen Pondicherry in Percherette’s time.` Even if 让步 + 主句（would have done 表虚拟）。
**关键词**：*Even if* 即使；*in Percherette’s time* 在 Percherette 的时代。
**表达方式**：用 `would not have seen`——**用虚拟语气把"实地考察"的幻觉戳破**：地理还在，时间已逝。
**为什么这样写**：回答主持人的核心问题（你划的线在哪？）——她不去 Pondicherry，因为实地无法抵达过去。

**段落逻辑：** Even if go to Pondicherry today → would not have seen Percherette's time → 实地无法抵达过去

### 第 6 段：只读没人读的旧书

> **原句 18:** When I do research, I rarely read contemporaries’ writing about the past because those are digested information and summarized, and I usually just go for books nobody reads.

**中文理解**：我做研究时，很少读同时代人关于过去的写作，因为那些是被消化过的信息和总结，我通常只找没人读的书。
**句子结构**：`When I do research, I [rarely read contemporaries’ writing about the past because those are digested information and summarized], and I [usually just go for books nobody reads]`。When 从句 + 并列主句（含 because + 定语从句）。
**关键词**：*digested information* 被消化的信息；*summarized* 总结的；*books nobody reads* 没人读的书。
**表达方式**：用 `books nobody reads`——**把"冷门"写成研究方法的优势**，反主流、反学术综述。
**为什么这样写**：给出她的研究配方：避开二手综述，直取无人问津的一手材料。

**段落逻辑：** When do research → rarely read contemporaries' writing → digested/summarized → go for books nobody reads → 反综述

---

> **原句 19:** I have gone through many books nobody has ever heard of.

**中文理解**：我读过许多从未有人听说过的书。
**句子结构**：`I [have gone through many books nobody has ever heard of]`。主谓宾（含 nobody has ever heard of 定语从句）。
**关键词**：*gone through* 通读；*nobody has ever heard of* 从未有人听说过。
**表达方式**：用 `gone through`（通读）而非 *read*——**强调"啃完"的动作量**，与她"做了很多研究"的辩白呼应。
**为什么这样写**：用一句朴素的自白支撑前文"I’ve done so much research"，把抽象说法落成具体经验。

**段落逻辑：** have gone through many books → nobody has ever heard of → 具体经验

---

> **原句 20:** I must—I must!—correct the record because I’ve done so much research.

**中文理解**：我必须——我必须！——更正这个记录，因为我做了这么多研究。
**句子结构**：`I must—I must!—correct the record because I’ve done so much research.` 主谓宾（含破折号重复强调 + because 从句）。
**关键词**：*correct the record* 更正记录；*done so much research* 做了这么多研究。
**表达方式**：用 `I must—I must!—` 的破折号重复——**把自我辩白写成一种急切、近乎可爱的强调**，节奏感强。
**为什么这样写**：在轻松更正 Jones 之后，突然认真申明"我确实做了研究"，一松一紧，语气转换见个性。

**段落逻辑：** I must—I must!—correct the record → done so much research → 自我辩白

## 长难句专项

**原句**：She also shares how she chose to structure two wide-ranging life stories within one novel, and how her characters illustrate disparities in opportunity among equally talented peers.

**解剖**：
- 主句：She also shares [how she chose to structure two wide-ranging life stories within one novel, and how her characters illustrate disparities in opportunity among equally talented peers]。
- 两个并列 how 宾语从句：① how she chose to structure ... within one novel；② how her characters illustrate disparities in opportunity among equally talented peers。
- 第二从句内：illustrate 的宾语是 disparities in opportunity，among equally talented peers 是介词短语修饰 disparities。
**难点**：两个 how 从句由 and 连接，读者容易把第二个 how 误读为修饰第一个；实际是 shares 的两个并列宾语。
**翻译要点**：拆成两句译——"她也分享了如何把两个跨度极大的人生故事结构进一部小说"，"以及她的角色如何呈现同等才华同侪之间的机会悬殊"。

---

**原句**：Just to think about the trajectory of her life coming from Pondicherry, even just as a young woman traveling from Pondicherry all the way to Moscow. That’s an epic novel.

**解剖**：
- 第一句：Just to think about the trajectory of her life coming from Pondicherry, even just as a young woman traveling from Pondicherry all the way to Moscow.（动名词短语 + even 补充 + 现在分词短语作定语）。
- 第二句：That’s an epic novel.（主系表）。
- 两个句子之间用句号断开，是口语里的"停顿式强调"。
**难点**：第一句没有谓语动词，是名词短语独立成句；读者需要把它理解为一个被感叹的念头。
**翻译要点**：保留两句断开的形式，第一句译为"只要想想……"，第二句独立成句"那就是一部史诗小说"。

---

**原句**：Because I remember Edward P. Jones, when he talked about researching for The Known World, he said he checked out all these books from the library, but he did not read a single book because he realized everything was in his brain.

**解剖**：
- 主句：Because I remember [Edward P. Jones, when he talked about researching for The Known World, said he checked out all these books from the library, but he did not read a single book because he realized everything was in his brain]。
- 嵌套：when 时间从句 + said + 宾语从句（checked out ... but did not read ... because ...）。
**难点**：remember 的宾语是 "Edward P. Jones said ..."，中间插入了 when 从句，读者容易把 when 从句当成 remember 的时间状语。
**翻译要点**：按"我记得 Edward P. Jones 说……（他谈到为《The Known World》做研究时）"调整语序，把 when 从句前置。

## 精读结束总结
1. **本文核心表达**：历史沉默处是小说的入口；史料越薄，虚构的空间越大。李翊云以"Percherette 只出现五次"为起点，把"查无此人"转成"全靠想象"。
2. **重要语法**：
   - 破折号重复强调：`I must—I must!—correct the record`
   - 虚拟语气：`would not have seen Pondicherry in Percherette’s time`
   - 形式主语 + when 从句：`It’s very odd when you read a Russian novel...`
   - 并列 how 宾语从句：`shares how she chose ... and how her characters ...`
   - 独立名词短语成句：`Just to think about ... That’s an epic novel.`
3. **写作技巧**：
   - **口语引语**：`Well, three novels. Why not?` / `Why not just make up a county myself?`——把严肃决定写成玩笑。
   - **用缺席立论**：`barely mentioned` / `no information to be found`——把史料的空白写成创作的正当性。
   - **更正的风度**：`I’m fairly certain I was quoting Edward P. Jones.`——不护短，把功劳归还原作者。
   - **自我辩白的节奏**：先更正，再 `I must—I must!—correct the record`，一松一紧。
