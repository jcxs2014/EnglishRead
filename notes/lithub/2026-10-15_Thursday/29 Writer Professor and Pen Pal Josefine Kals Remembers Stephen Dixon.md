# Writer, Professor and Pen Pal: Josefine Kals Remembers Stephen Dixon（作家、教授与笔友：Josefine Kals 回忆 Stephen Dixon）— 精读分析

## 概览
- **来源**：Literary Hub｜文学回忆录（节选自 Stephen Dixon 作品集的序言，Josefine Kals 撰）｜出版 Tue, 13 Oct 2026｜URL: https://lithub.com/writer-professor-and-pen-pal-josefine-kals-remembers-stephen-dixon/
- **副题**：师生、笔友与文学遗产
- **主题**：作者回忆她与作家 Stephen Dixon 的关系——从约翰·霍普金斯写作研讨班的学生，到差点"pre-unemployment"（失业预科的）毕业生，到企鹅兰登书屋的公关助理，最终成为他的笔友。文章核心是 Dixon 的散文风格："像读某人的购物清单一样平凡而亲密"，以及他如何把人类生活中最微小的细节（在喜欢的餐馆吃饭、照顾病亲、为人父母的恐惧、生活的突然中断）当作"精致而值得关注的东西"来照亮。结尾以他 2019 年进临终关怀、以及 McSweeney’s 将出版他未发表作品的讣闻收束。
- **结构**：A 初遇（JHU 学生 → 签名 → 退休）→ B 从"pre-unemployment"到出版社 → C 成为笔友 → D Dixon 的散文风格（购物清单）→ E 电子邮件与未发表作品 → F 最爱的故事"Flying" → G 临终与文学遗产
- **核心金句**："His stream of consciousness spotlights, as if something exquisite and worthy of attention, every minor detail in a human’s life."（他的意识流把人类生活中每一个微小的细节，都当作某种精致而值得关注的东西来照亮。）

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：初遇

> **原句 1:** I was a sophomore at the Johns Hopkins Writing Seminars undergraduate program when I first encountered Stephen Dixon.

**中文理解**：我第一次遇见 Stephen Dixon 时，是约翰·霍普金斯写作研讨班本科项目的二年级学生。
**句子结构**：`I [was a sophomore at the Johns Hopkins Writing Seminars undergraduate program when I first encountered Stephen Dixon]`。主系表（含 when 从句）。
**关键词**：*a sophomore* 二年级学生；*the Johns Hopkins Writing Seminars undergraduate program* 约翰·霍普金斯写作研讨班本科项目；*when I first encountered Stephen Dixon* 当我第一次遇见 Stephen Dixon。
**表达方式**：用 `encountered`（遇见）而非 *met*——**用"相遇"写初遇的偶然性**，与后文"我给他留下了印象"（反讽）呼应。
**为什么这样写**：交代作者的身份（学生）与初遇场景，为后文"我提醒他我的名字"的幽默做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** I was a sophomore → at Johns Hopkins Writing Seminars → when I first encountered Stephen Dixon → 初遇

---

> **原句 2:** Some details I recall from his small and intimate class in the basement of Gilman Hall were his ink-stained pants pockets, and how he paced around the classroom teaching us about fiction writing in his soporific voice.

**中文理解**：我回忆起他在 Gilman Hall 地下室那间小而亲密的课堂的一些细节：他墨迹斑斑的裤子口袋，以及他如何在教室里踱步，用他那催眠般的声音教我们小说写作。
**句子结构**：`Some details I recall from his small and intimate class in the basement of Gilman Hall [were his ink-stained pants pockets, and how he paced around the classroom teaching us about fiction writing in his soporific voice]`。主谓（含 I recall 定语从句 + how 表语从句）。
**关键词**：*small and intimate class* 小而亲密的课堂；*in the basement of Gilman Hall* 在 Gilman Hall 地下室；*ink-stained pants pockets* 墨迹斑斑的裤子口袋；*paced around the classroom* 在教室里踱步；*in his soporific voice* 用催眠般的声音。
**表达方式**：用 `ink-stained pants pockets` 与 `soporific voice`——**用两个具体细节（墨迹、催眠声音）写记忆的选择性**，把课堂写成一组感官碎片。
**为什么这样写**：用感官细节（墨迹、踱步、催眠声音）重建课堂场景，为下一句"他安静地催促我们"做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** Some details I recall → ink-stained pants pockets → paced around the classroom → in his soporific voice → 感官碎片

---

> **原句 3:** In his kind and understated manner, he quietly urged us to find the poignancy in the everyday.

**中文理解**：以他善良而低调的方式，他安静地催促我们去找出日常中的辛酸。
**句子结构**：`In his kind and understated manner, he [quietly urged us to find the poignancy in the everyday]`。状语 + 主谓宾（含 urge ... to do + in the everyday）。
**关键词**：*kind and understated manner* 善良而低调的方式；*quietly urged us* 安静地催促我们；*find the poignancy in the everyday* 找出日常中的辛酸。
**表达方式**：用 `the poignancy in the everyday`——**用"日常中的辛酸"写 Dixon 的核心教导**，与全文的主题（购物清单式的平凡）呼应。
**为什么这样写**：给出 Dixon 作为教师的核心教导（日常中的 poignancy），为全文的"平凡即美"主题定调。

**段落逻辑：** In his kind and understated manner → quietly urged us → find the poignancy in the everyday → 核心教导

### 第 2 段：签名与退休

> **原句 4:** Later that year, I stood in line for a signed copy of his new book—End of I, published by McSweeney’s—at the Barnes & Noble that had recently opened in the newly built dorm across the street from the campus’s main entrance.

**中文理解**：那年晚些时候，我排队买他的新书《End of I》的签名版——由 McSweeney’s 出版——在校园主入口对面新建宿舍里最近开张的 Barnes & Noble。
**句子结构**：`Later that year, I [stood in line for a signed copy of his new book—End of I, published by McSweeney’s—at the Barnes & Noble that had recently opened in the newly built dorm across the street from the campus’s main entrance]`。状语 + 主谓（含 stood in line for + 破折号同位语 + that 定语从句）。
**关键词**：*stood in line for a signed copy* 排队买签名版；*End of I, published by McSweeney’s* 《End of I》，McSweeney’s 出版；*the Barnes & Noble that had recently opened* 最近开张的 Barnes & Noble；*the newly built dorm across the street from the campus’s main entrance* 校园主入口对面新建的宿舍。
**表达方式**：用 `stood in line for a signed copy`——**用"排队"写学生的虔诚**，与后文"我提醒他我的名字"的自嘲呼应。
**为什么这样写**：描写学生时代的虔诚（排队买签名书），为下一句"我提醒他我的名字"的尴尬做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** stood in line for a signed copy → End of I → at the Barnes & Noble → 学生的虔诚

> **原句 5:** I reminded him of my name so he could personalize my book (I hadn’t made such an impression on him as his student).

**中文理解**：我提醒他我的名字，好让他为我的书个性化题签（我作为他的学生并没有给他留下这样的印象）。
**句子结构**：`I [reminded him of my name so he could personalize my book (I hadn’t made such an impression on him as his student)]`。主谓宾（含 so 目的从句 + 括号同位语）。
**关键词**：*reminded him of my name* 提醒他我的名字；*personalize my book* 为我的书个性化题签；*I hadn’t made such an impression on him* 我没有给他留下这样的印象；*as his student* 作为他的学生。
**表达方式**：用括号插入 `(I hadn’t made such an impression on him as his student)`——**用括号写自嘲**，把"提醒名字"写成一种温和的尴尬。
**为什么这样写**：用自嘲（没给他留下印象）写出学生的谦卑，为下一句"一两年后他退休"做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** reminded him of my name → personalize my book → I hadn't made such an impression → 自嘲

---

> **原句 6:** A year or so later, Professor Dixon retired from his nearly 30 year teaching tenure at JHU.

**中文理解**：大约一年后，Dixon 教授从他在 JHU 近 30 年的教学生涯中退休。
**句子结构**：`A year or so later, Professor Dixon [retired from his nearly 30 year teaching tenure at JHU]`。状语 + 主谓（含 retire from + at JHU）。
**关键词**：*A year or so later* 大约一年后；*retired from* 从……退休；*nearly 30 year teaching tenure* 近 30 年的教学生涯；*at JHU* 在 JHU。
**表达方式**：用 `teaching tenure`（教学生涯/终身教职）——**用学术术语写教师的职业生涯**，与全文的学院语境一致。
**为什么这样写**：交代 Dixon 的退休，为下一段"我闯入诗歌教授办公室"的转折做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** A year or so later → Professor Dixon retired → from nearly 30 year tenure at JHU → 退休

### 第 3 段：从"pre-unemployment"到出版社

> **原句 7:** Soon after that, teary-eyed and panicked, a second semester senior on the brink of graduation, I barged into the office of my poetry professor, Mary Jo Salter, begging her to tell me what to do with a degree in what my pre-med friends affectionately dubbed "pre-unemployment".

**中文理解**：此后不久，含泪而惊慌，一个处于毕业边缘的第二学期大四学生，我闯进我的诗歌教授 Mary Jo Salter 的办公室，求她告诉我该拿一个我预科医生朋友亲切地戏称为"失业预科"的学位怎么办。
**句子结构**：`Soon after that, teary-eyed and panicked, a second semester senior on the brink of graduation, I [barged into the office of my poetry professor, Mary Jo Salter, begging her to tell me what to do with a degree in what my pre-med friends affectionately dubbed "pre-unemployment"]`。状语（含两个同位语）+ 主谓宾（含 begging + what 从句 + what 从句）。
**关键词**：*teary-eyed and panicked* 含泪而惊慌；*a second semester senior on the brink of graduation* 处于毕业边缘的第二学期大四学生；*barged into the office* 闯进办公室；*my poetry professor, Mary Jo Salter* 诗歌教授 Mary Jo Salter；*begging her to tell me what to do* 求她告诉我该做什么；*what my pre-med friends affectionately dubbed "pre-unemployment"* 预科医生朋友戏称为"失业预科"的东西。
**表达方式**：用 `teary-eyed and panicked` 与 `barged into`——**用身体状态与动作写毕业生的焦虑**，把"失业预科"的玩笑写成一种自嘲的智慧。
**为什么这样写**：用"失业预科"的玩笑写人文学科的就业焦虑，为下一句"找一家出版社"的建议做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** teary-eyed and panicked → barged into poetry professor's office → what to do with "pre-unemployment" degree → 就业焦虑

---

> **原句 8:** She said: "Find a publishing house." I’m pretty sure I responded with: "What’s that?"

**中文理解**：她说："找一家出版社。"我很确定我回答的是："那是什么？"
**句子结构**：`She said: "Find a publishing house." I’m pretty sure I responded with: "What’s that?"` 直接引语 + 主谓宾（含直接引语）。
**关键词**：*Find a publishing house* 找一家出版社；*I’m pretty sure I responded with* 我很确定我回答的是；*What’s that?* 那是什么。
**表达方式**：用 `What’s that?` 的直接引语——**用一句天真无知的回答写学生的天真**，幽默而真实。
**为什么这样写**：用"那是什么？"写作者对出版业的陌生，为下一段"奇迹般挤进公关助理的格子间"做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** She said "Find a publishing house" → I responded "What's that?" → 天真无知

---

> **原句 9:** Miraculously, a year after graduation I managed to eke my way into a publicity assistant cubicle at the Knopf Doubleday group of Random House (now Penguin Random House).

**中文理解**：奇迹般地，毕业一年后，我设法挤进了兰登书屋旗下 Knopf Doubleday 集团的一个公关助理格子间（现为企鹅兰登书屋）。
**句子结构**：`Miraculously, a year after graduation I [managed to eke my way into a publicity assistant cubicle at the Knopf Doubleday group of Random House (now Penguin Random House)]`。状语 + 主谓（含 manage to do + eke one's way into + 括号同位语）。
**关键词**：*Miraculously* 奇迹般地；*a year after graduation* 毕业一年后；*managed to eke my way into* 设法挤进；*a publicity assistant cubicle* 公关助理格子间；*the Knopf Doubleday group of Random House (now Penguin Random House)* 兰登书屋旗下 Knopf Doubleday 集团（现企鹅兰登书屋）。
**表达方式**：用 `managed to eke my way into`——**用"挤进"写进入出版业的艰难**，与 `Miraculously`（奇迹般地）呼应。
**为什么这样写**：用"奇迹般地挤进格子间"写职业起点的卑微，为下一句"几乎同样令人惊讶地成为笔友"做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** Miraculously → eke my way into a publicity assistant cubicle → at Knopf Doubleday → 职业起点

> **原句 10:** Some years later, almost just as astonishingly, Stephen Dixon and I became pen pals.

**中文理解**：几年后，几乎同样令人惊讶地，Stephen Dixon 和我成了笔友。
**句子结构**：`Some years later, almost just as astonishingly, Stephen Dixon and I [became pen pals]`。状语 + 主谓。
**关键词**：*Some years later* 几年后；*almost just as astonishingly* 几乎同样令人惊讶地；*became pen pals* 成了笔友。
**表达方式**：用 `almost just as astonishingly`——**用"几乎同样令人惊讶"把"成为笔友"与"挤进格子间"并列**，把师生关系的逆转写成一种奇迹。
**为什么这样写**：给出全文的转折（成为笔友），并用"令人惊讶"强调这段关系的反常与珍贵。

**段落逻辑：** Some years later → almost as astonishingly → became pen pals → 关系逆转

### 第 4 段：笔友往来

> **原句 11:** I don’t remember how it started—I think I may have asked another of my JHU professors for his email address so I could tell him how much his work and that class had always meant to me—but, for years, we were in regular email touch about life, work, and his writing, which continued to pour out of him until the very end.

**中文理解**：我不记得它是怎么开始的——我想我可能曾向我的另一位 JHU 教授要了他的电子邮件地址，好告诉他他的作品和那门课一直对我有多重要——但是，多年来，我们保持定期的电子邮件联系，谈论生活、工作和他的作品，而他的作品一直不断地从他身上涌出，直到最后。
**句子结构**：`I don’t remember how it started—I think I may have asked another of my JHU professors for his email address so I could tell him how much his work and that class had always meant to me—but, for years, we were in regular email touch about life, work, and his writing, which continued to pour out of him until the very end.` 主谓宾（含破折号插入 + so 目的从句 + which 定语从句）。
**关键词**：*I don’t remember how it started* 我不记得怎么开始的；*I think I may have asked another of my JHU professors for his email address* 我想我可能向另一位 JHU 教授要了他的邮箱；*so I could tell him how much his work and that class had always meant to me* 好告诉他他的作品和课对我有多重要；*we were in regular email touch about life, work, and his writing* 我们定期邮件联系；*which continued to pour out of him until the very end* 一直不断从他身上涌出直到最后。
**表达方式**：用 `continued to pour out of him until the very end`——**用"不断涌出"写 Dixon 的创作活力**，与"直到最后"并置，写出未完成的丰产。
**为什么这样写**：交代笔友关系的开始（要邮箱）与持续（定期联系），并用"不断涌出直到最后"写 Dixon 的创作状态。

**段落逻辑：** I don't remember how it started → asked for his email → for years in regular email touch → which continued to pour out until the end → 笔友关系

**中文理解**：Stephen Dixon 的散文像读某人的购物清单一样平凡而亲密。对我来说，那就是其中的美。
**句子结构**：`Stephen Dixon’s prose [is as ordinary and intimate as reading someone’s shopping list]. To me, that’s the beauty in it.` 主系表（含 as ... as ...）+ 主系表。
**关键词**：*as ordinary and intimate as reading someone’s shopping list* 像读购物清单一样平凡而亲密；*To me, that’s the beauty in it* 对我来说，那就是其中的美。
**表达方式**：用 `as ordinary and intimate as reading someone’s shopping list`——**用"购物清单"写散文的平凡**，把"美"定义在最低处，是全文的核心美学判断。
**为什么这样写**：给出全文的核心美学命题（购物清单式的美），为下一段"他的电子邮件就像他的小说"做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** Stephen Dixon's prose → as ordinary and intimate as reading someone's shopping list → that's the beauty → 核心美学

> **原句 13:** Professor Dixon’s emails were just like his fiction writing—intimate and full of quotidian details.

**中文理解**：Dixon 教授的电子邮件就像他的小说写作——亲密而充满日常细节。
**句子结构**：`Professor Dixon’s emails [were just like his fiction writing—intimate and full of quotidian details]`。主系表（含 just like + 破折号同位语）。
**关键词**：*just like his fiction writing* 就像他的小说写作；*intimate and full of quotidian details* 亲密而充满日常细节。
**表达方式**：用 `just like his fiction writing` 与 `quotidian details`——**用"就像小说"写邮件与作品的连续性**，把日常通信写成文学的延伸。
**为什么这样写**：把"购物清单式的美"落到具体载体（电子邮件），为下一句三个具体例子做铺垫。

**段落逻辑：** Professor Dixon's emails → just like his fiction writing → intimate and full of quotidian details → 连续性

> **原句 14:** He referred to himself as "a fussbudget worrywart," he gleefully told me that his grandson’s sonogram photo looked "like a bust carved from marble by Michelangelo," and he signed off messages with sentences like this: "The plumber just arrived so I’ve got to go."

**中文理解**：他把自己称为"一个爱挑剔的忧患者"，他兴高采烈地告诉我他孙子的超声波照片看起来"像米开朗基罗用大理石雕刻的胸像"，他用这样的句子结束信息："水管工刚到，所以我得走了。"
**句子结构**：`He [referred to himself as "a fussbudget worrywart," he gleefully told me that his grandson’s sonogram photo looked "like a bust carved from marble by Michelangelo," and he signed off messages with sentences like this: "The plumber just arrived so I’ve got to go."]`。三个并列谓语（含直接引语 ×2 + like this 引出引语）。
**关键词**：*referred to himself as "a fussbudget worrywart"* 把自己称为"爱挑剔的忧患者"；*his grandson’s sonogram photo looked "like a bust carved from marble by Michelangelo"* 孙子的超声波照片像米开朗基罗的胸像；*signed off messages with sentences like this* 用这样的句子结束信息；*"The plumber just arrived so I’ve got to go."* "水管工刚到，所以我得走了。"
**表达方式**：用 `a bust carved from marble by Michelangelo` 与 `The plumber just arrived`——**用"米开朗基罗的胸像"与"水管工来了"的极端反差写日常与崇高的并置**，是 Dixon 风格的精髓。
**为什么这样写**：用三个具体例子（自我称呼、超声波照片、结束语）证明"邮件=小说"，把抽象美学落成可感的细节。

**段落逻辑：** referred to himself as fussbudget worrywart → grandson's sonogram like Michelangelo bust → signed off "The plumber just arrived" → 日常与崇高并置

### 第 5 段：未发表的作品

> **原句 15:** And every few months, a bursting FedEx envelope from Baltimore would appear at my desk at PRH containing Professor Dixon’s typewritten chunks of new and unpublished writing.

**中文理解**：每隔几个月，一个鼓鼓囊囊的来自巴尔的摩的 FedEx 信封就会出现在我在 PRH 的桌子上，里面装着 Dixon 教授用打字机打的新而未曾发表的作品的块块。
**句子结构**：`And every few months, a bursting FedEx envelope from Baltimore [would appear at my desk at PRH containing Professor Dixon’s typewritten chunks of new and unpublished writing]`。状语 + 主谓（含 would 表过去习惯 + containing 现在分词作定语）。

## 长难句专项

**原句**：In his kind and understated manner, he quietly urged us to find the poignancy in the everyday.

**解剖**：
- 状语：In his kind and understated manner。
- 主句：he quietly urged us to find the poignancy in the everyday。
- `urge + 宾语 + 不定式`：urged us to find。
**难点**：`the poignancy in the everyday` 中 `everyday` 是名词化形容词，作介词宾语，容易被误读为形容词。
**翻译要点**：按"以他善良而低调的方式，他安静地催促我们去找出日常中的辛酸"翻译。

---

**原句**：Soon after that, teary-eyed and panicked, a second semester senior on the brink of graduation, I barged into the office of my poetry professor, Mary Jo Salter, begging her to tell me what to do with a degree in what my pre-med friends affectionately dubbed "pre-unemployment".

**解剖**：
- 状语：Soon after that。
- 两个同位语：teary-eyed and panicked / a second semester senior on the brink of graduation。
- 主句：I barged into the office of my poetry professor, Mary Jo Salter。
- 现在分词状语：begging her to tell me what to do with a degree in what my pre-med friends affectionately dubbed "pre-unemployment"。
**难点**：主语前有两个同位语，且 `what to do with a degree in what ...` 是双层 what 结构，读者容易迷失。
**翻译要点**：把同位语处理为"含泪而惊慌、一个处于毕业边缘的第二学期大四学生"，把双层 what 译为"该拿一个……的学位怎么办"。

---

**原句**：His stream of consciousness spotlights, as if something exquisite and worthy of attention, every minor detail in a human’s life, from the simple pleasure of having dinner at a favorite local restaurant, to the pangs of caring for an ailing loved one, the constant fear of being a parent, and life’s sudden interruptions.

**解剖**：
- 主句：His stream of consciousness spotlights ... every minor detail in a human’s life。
- 插入语：as if something exquisite and worthy of attention。
- 介词短语列举：from the simple pleasure ..., to the pangs ..., the constant fear ..., and life’s sudden interruptions。
**难点**：主语与宾语之间插入了 as if 短语；四个并列的名词短语中，后两个（the constant fear / life's sudden interruptions）省略了介词，读者容易丢失并列关系。
**翻译要点**：按"他的意识流把……每一个微小细节当作……照亮，从……到……、……以及……"翻译，把四个名词短语处理为列举。

## 精读结束总结
1. **本文核心表达**：Dixon 的散文"像读某人的购物清单一样平凡而亲密"，而他的意识流把人类生活中最微小的细节（晚餐、病亲、父母、中断）当作"精致而值得关注的东西"来照亮。文章用"师生→笔友→被允许参与死亡"的关系弧线，把一位作家的一生写成一段关于"平凡"的证词。
2. **重要语法**：
   - 同位语列举：`teary-eyed and panicked, a second semester senior on the brink of graduation, I barged into ...`
   - 括号插入自嘲：`(I hadn’t made such an impression on him as his student)`
   - 非限定定语从句：`which continued to pour out of him until the very end` / `which was an honor`
   - `from ... to ..., ..., and ...` 的省略并列：`from the simple pleasure ..., to the pangs ..., the constant fear ..., and life’s sudden interruptions`
   - 方括号标记：`[literally]`
3. **写作技巧**：
   - **用物质细节写人**：`ink-stained pants pockets` / `a bursting FedEx envelope` / `typewritten chunks`。
   - **用自嘲写谦卑**：`I reminded him of my name` / `What’s that?` / `a lowly publicist`。
   - **日常与崇高的并置**：`a bust carved from marble by Michelangelo` vs. `The plumber just arrived`。
