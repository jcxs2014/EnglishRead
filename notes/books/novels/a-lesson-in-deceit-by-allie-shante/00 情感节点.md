# A Lesson in Deceit by Allie Shante — 情感节点

## 核心情感节点

### 1. Riley 转学进入 Mystic Riegan
**叙事概括**：Riley 独自来到这所教授魔法的特殊院校，带着追查父亲死亡真相的使命。
**关键引语**：
- "I tapped my foot against the floor, way too antsy to sit in my chair."
- "You transferred specifically to find the truth about your dad?"

---

### 2. 四人关系的形成
**叙事概括**：Riley 与 River、Grayson、Asher 发展出复杂的多角亲密关系，从对抗到接纳到深入连接。
**关键引语**：
- "We are a millimeter closer than we were before."
- "My brother came first."

---

### 3. Corrin 的巫会秘密
**叙事概括**：Riley 发现室友 Corrin 来自一个巫会，这个巫会的成员正在神秘失踪。
**关键引语**：
- "I know both of you have had a hard semester so far with everything going on with your coven."

---

### 4. 记忆提取行动
**叙事概括**：Riley 服用 Corrin 制作的记忆提取药水，回到过去亲眼目睹父亲被杀的场景。
**关键引语**：
- "I wanted to reach my hand out and save him from falling."
- "You don't get to take things that don't belong to you."

---

### 5. Oliver St. James 的嫌疑
**叙事概括**：Riley 发现 Oliver St. James 认识她的父亲 Thomas，这个线索让嫌疑人浮出水面。
**关键引语**：
- "You're Thomas's daughter?"
- "I spoke to your father a couple of times."

---

### 6. Asher 的觉醒
**叙事概括**：Asher 从敌意转向接纳，承认对 Riley 有超越肉体的感情，但兄弟情谊始终优先。
**关键引语**：
- "I may have fucked Riley and felt something for her that needed to be thoroughly investigated."
- "River's mysterious little girlfriend."

---

### 7. 力量觉醒——ruby 项链的摧毁
**叙事概括**：在全校集会上，Riley 的 ruby 项链被摧毁，她隐藏多年的 telekinesis 力量爆发。
**关键引语**：
- "It rattled and shook as if it was being overtaken with so much energy. It was lifted from the ground and then exploded into a million tiny little pieces."
- "Your powers have been hiding in that ruby around your neck since the moment they manifested!"

---

### 8. Grayson 的背叛揭露
**叙事概括**：Riley 发现 Grayson 一直被 Chancellor Fowler 威胁，被迫成为帮凶。
**关键引语**：
- "Because we told him to. Unless he wanted to explain to his parents why his scholarship money just went away."
- "You always have a fucking choice! Why did you do it?"

---

### 9. Marianne 的死亡
**叙事概括**：Chancellor Fowler 在 Riley 面前用玻璃碎片杀害了 Marianne，这是全书最黑暗的时刻。
**关键引语**：
- "Blood immediately gushed from the deep wounds."
- "Her eyes that always had so much life, descended into pure nothingness."

---

### 10. 终极身份揭露——Fowler 是 Riley 的父亲
**叙事概括**：Chancellor Fowler 揭示自己是 Riley 的亲生父亲，Thomas 不是她的生父。
**关键引语**：
- "I am."
- "Thomas is not your father!"
- "You were not born to two commonplace humans, Riley!"
