# 《The Red Rider》情感节点

## 节点一：酒馆宣战（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 十六岁的红斗篷猎手单骑闯进 La Maison de Touraine，在店主低声递来的情报里锁定角落的大个子——Lycanthru 新主 Luc Grenault。对方把死亡威胁讲成对红斗篷的赞美，她当众接下宣战，转身把狼群引出店门。

**关键引语：**

> "“Don’t look yet, but there’s a big man in the corner who seems to be one of them. He’s been talking about the recent wolf attacks all night with his cronies there. I assume they’re part of it, too, but he must be one, don’t you think?”"（ch01）

> "“I like the color of your cloak. The color of blood. If anything bad happened to you, it would be difficult to sort out the cloak from the girl, shrouded in all that bloody mess.”"（ch01）

> "“Unlike the rest of the Lycanthru, I don’t take orders from a little girl. This is a new order, Mademoiselle. You’re dealing with me now.”"（ch01）

## 节点二：山野首杀与空弩（ch02）

**叙事概括：** 她以马和斗篷作饵跳出七狼围猎，在月夜的山丘上连杀三狼；最后一箭打空后，靠空弩与"Better to bluff"逼退狼群。Grenault 缓慢退走、反复回望——他赢走了她的底牌。

**关键引语：**

> "I pulled my feet from the stirrups and shouted, “Hyah!” to spur Crimson onward. Then I leaped off, diving to the ground and tumbling away. I rolled aside and lay flat and still, as the wolves crested the hill and continued after Crimson and the flapping red cloak."（ch02）

> "I might have enough time to reload before he realized my predicament and rushed down the hill to devour me. Or I might not. Better to bluff."（ch02）

> "We faced each other a moment longer, before we both slowly turned to depart, preparing to battle another day. His slow retreat unsettled me, as he continued to scrutinize me. … As if he knew I couldn’t kill him."（ch02）

## 节点三：名字、银箭与 Lisette（ch03）

**叙事概括：** 镇民把 Red Rider 当传奇仰望，Celia 当街喊出"Helena Basque"；铁匠铺里 Pierre 交出二十二支淬银箭，Madame Leóne 的拥抱温暖如常——直到两句独立成段的硬揭示落地：她就是 Grenault 瞄准的女人。

**关键引语：**

> "“Look, Marie, there she is,” she said, staring down her nose at me. “Helena Basque. The girl who dresses like a man.”"（ch03）

> "“Anytime. I dipped all the tips in silver. You said it only takes a trace, right? I keep telling Papa I made mistakes and had to scrap some metal and silver pieces, so he doesn't wonder why we're short on some material.”"（ch03）

> "Madame Lisette Leóne. The woman Grenault said he was targeting."（ch03）

## 节点四：童话噩梦与火枪清晨（ch04）

**叙事概括：** 七岁的梦里，会说话的狼先她一步赶到外婆家，爪如银刀挥出她一生的疤；九岁的清晨，Papa 提枪站在床边，把她从噩梦里领进了打猎的森林。

**关键引语：**

> "I could swear it was smiling. “Where are you off to in such a hurry, Mademoiselle?” it asked. Its voice was cold and coarse, but the beast clearly spoke."（ch04）

> "The wolf chortled, then drew closer and raised its paw. Its claws glinted like silver knives in the window's sunlight. It swiped at my face and the entire world turned into a fiery red agony."（ch04）

> "My bedroom suddenly felt as black and cold as a tomb. I had to embrace that darkness, or I would forever cower from it. “Yes, Papa,” I said."（ch04）

## 节点五：泥潭、兔子与小鹿（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 狩猎课三连：鞋陷泥潭父亲不帮，兔子面前犹豫脱靶，荆棘割脚只换来一句"那就流血吧"——她撕碎心爱的裙子自行裹伤，再一箭放倒小鹿，回家用钢铁般的声音答母亲："我把血止住了。"

**关键引语：**

> "You can't be afraid to shoot. You've got to shove your fear aside. That was only a rabbit."（ch05）

> "Glancing up, I saw Papa's eyes bulge, as I gathered some leaves to press them against the wound and stop the bleeding. Then I wrapped the cotton strip around my foot four times and tied it off. I looked up again to see the hint of Papa's smile."（ch05）

> "Then I lay down on the cot, blew out the candle, and forced my eyes shut, still trembling at the memory of Grenault's grip."（ch05）

## 节点六：屋顶扼喉（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 成年线的同一章里，她伏击 Grenault 未中，反被他徒手跃墙扼喉夺弩。他把处刑方案当登基演讲念完，镇民的闲话救了她——逃回地堡的那夜，她的手还在抖。

**关键引语：**

> "I tried to rise and retrieve it, but Grenault's fist closed around my throat like an iron manacle. He held me in place, one mighty arm supporting him, the other choking me."（ch05）

> "“I'm going to squeeze until you suffocate,” he said. “After you pass out, I'll string you up like a piece of meat, to show the Lycanthru how fragile you really are. Then I'll kill you—slowly—in front of the entire order."（ch05）

> "Then I lay down on the cot, blew out the candle, and forced my eyes shut, still trembling at the memory of Grenault's grip."（ch05）

## 节点七：裁缝店踩点（ch07）

**叙事概括：** 她忍着九年前被拒之门外的旧创跟进裁缝店，撞见 Grenault 以店员身份收集女性地址、当面品评 Lisette。他扬言今夜偷走地址纸条——她的守夜计划由敌人的台词直接下达。

**关键引语：**

> "But I reasoned—working at the mine, I would only meet other men. Here I have constant visits from beautiful young women, sometimes bringing small children on the hip. They often leave us their addresses, to contact them or deliver clothing to their door."（ch07）

> "Assuming, of course, she knows nothing of my private activities. I trust you haven't shared anything with her about our secret relationship. That might force me to stop any such rumors from leaving this room."（ch07）

> "And I would be waiting. I had no idea what I would do when we met again in the darkness, but I would be waiting."（ch07）

## 节点八：棺前誓言与圣堂祝福（ch10）

**叙事概括：** 又一名小女孩遇害。她坚持开棺直面撕碎的蓝裙，在圣堂里重续葬礼誓言；Father Vestille 不赞成她的杀戮却承诺一直为她祝福——大卫的狮与熊给她的恐惧找到了原型。

**关键引语：**

> "I opened the lid and fought the nausea building in my throat. Torn shreds of a pale blue dress made a mockery of an innocent child's life. The small body was mostly skeleton, with a few limbs missing and very little flesh."（ch10）

> "Could still hear the coffin lid echo as I declared to everyone at the funeral mass that this would end. … That I would end it."（ch10）

> "Before David fought Goliath, he was a shepherd. The scriptures say he fought wild animals that tried to steal his sheep, including a lion and a bear. He even rescued a lamb from the lion's mouth."（ch10）

## 节点九：倒数到零（ch11）

**叙事概括：** 铁匠铺里 Grenault 以炉栅挡尽十箭、口头倒数 Nine 到 None；Lisette 织针刺狼换来一记反手。她被夺弩、扣喉、钉上远墙——"It's over, little Helena." 全书最低点在终章门口落锁。

**关键引语：**

> "My blood surged, my breath coming in short gasps. His countdown of my remaining bolts felt like a death knell."（ch11）

> "“No!" Madame Leóne shrieked, seizing two large knitting needles and lunging at his back. She stabbed deep into him, her teeth bared with shocking savagery. "Run, Helena!""（ch11）

> "Then he rushed at the far wall, ramming me against it. I screamed at the impact, which drove his claws a little deeper. I squeezed my eyes tight against the agony, against the purr of his voice. … "It's over, little Helena.""（ch11）

## 节点十：火与炉膛（ch12）

**叙事概括：** 毒打与祈祷之后，她抬头看见墙上那支火把，又一眼认出 Grenault 的进退站姿正是 Francois 教的虚张声势。火焰窜上狼毛，苏醒的 Lisette 掷来草叉，两人把狼王推进炉膛封死烧尽——"血止住了，这场战争她会亲手终结。"

**关键引语：**

> "He crept toward me with a broad grin, sliding one paw forward, holding one paw back. The same fighting stance Francois had taught me, keeping myself ready to advance, or, if necessary—to retreat."（ch12）

> "He growled and spread his claws, lunging at me. I bowed my head low and shoved the torch at his chest, hearing the hiss of sizzling flesh as the fire raced across his fur."（ch12）

> "I had stopped the bleeding. … I would end this."（ch12）
