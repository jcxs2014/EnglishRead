# 18. Chapter Eighteen

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Tigglestide Eve 当天上午，Jessica 揣着两桩任务进城——替市长的"无情"讨个说法、把 Greg 押回家过 Tigglestide——却在节庆集市上被 pantomime 与 Round Robin 一路贴身，最后硬着头皮冲进 civic center。
- **情感弧线位置**：从清晨的 new sense of purpose 起跳，先被 Molly 缺席带来的 sick, sinking feeling 压进胃里；pantomime 的荤笑话砸在身上、Round Robin 呕糖与那张脸又把她推入 a rare mélange of fear and arousal，直到 "I'm not here for Round Robin, she reminded herself. I'm here for Greg." 才把航向强行扳回。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：ch13 埋下的兔耳计划兑现成 bunny ears 头箍加大衣下的 negligee；ch16 的盛装兽群以 foxes in top hats and rabbits in waistcoats 的姿态回归集市；pantomime 的 "women lying with women" 笑点正落在 Jessica 自己身上——节庆喜剧当场翻成公开点名。
- **人物弧线**：Jessica 从被动等待转为主动出击，但她的修复清单里没有 Molly（"She needed to stop thinking about Molly"）；Morgan McKinley 的旧事被她拿来度量孤独；Sandy 闭门不出（they/them），Greg 缺席——她把全家的缺席一起记在 lord mayor 与丈夫两人头上。
- **叙事手法**：章首 epigraph 借民谣《The Visitor on Tigglestide》的飞行药膏段（precious ointment / a secret balm in a carafe）给节日垫上异教底色；正文三层递进——集市风情画 → pantomime 戏中戏 → Round Robin 登场——戏台上的笑闹一层比一层更贴她的私事；Princess Kayla 与 Dargonna 的消失只给现象、不给解释。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "It was the first time in days that she didn’t find Molly already cooking breakfast, and Molly’s absence gave her a sick, sinking feeling in the pit of her stomach. She had never felt so alone, not since Morgan McKinley told her to go back to her parents."

- **中文理解**：连着好些天，她没再一进门就看见 Molly 在做早餐；Molly 的缺席让她胃底泛起一阵病态的下坠感。她从没这么孤独过——上一回还是 Morgan McKinley 叫她回娘家去。
- **关键词**：Molly’s absence / sick, sinking feeling / alone / Morgan McKinley
- **为什么这样写**：把孤单压进身体里写——absence 先落成 pit of her stomach 里的生理症状，再搬出 Morgan McKinley 的旧事当标尺，量出这次缺席的分量；早餐没人做，空缺先疼在胃上，然后才轮到心里。
- **读者视角提示**：注意她的痛感来自那张空着的早餐桌而非别的——这个人名与这个体感都值得记住，是本章情绪的底座。

> **原句 2:** "She thought that the lord mayor was being a right proper Ebenezer Scrooge, and if she couldn’t count on three ghosts showing up to explain the error of his ways, then she would have to take matters into her own hands. She was going to march down to the civic center and give that lord mayor a piece of her mind."

- **中文理解**：她认定市长活脱是 Ebenezer Scrooge 再世，既然等不来三只鬼点醒他，就只好自己动手；她要一路 march 到 civic center，当面"奉送"自己的一番意见。
- **关键词**：Ebenezer Scrooge / three ghosts / lord mayor / civic center / a piece of her mind
- **为什么这样写**：借狄更斯的框架给私怨套上正义外衣——市长被派进 Scrooge 的剧本，three ghosts 缺席，替补出场的就得是她本人；a piece of her mind 这种客套短语让兴师问罪听起来像送礼。
- **读者视角提示**：注意她顺手把自己排进了圣诞故事的救场角色——援引的经典越正经，这份计划越显出一厢情愿。

> **原句 3:** "There were foxes in top hats and rabbits in waistcoats, clowns and harlequins, and other costumes that Jessica did not recognize but that she assumed represented popular characters from British folklore. At the corner before the jam monger, she paused suddenly and did a double take—she was certain, for a brief moment, that she had spotted revelers in the crowd dressed as Princess Kayla and Dargonna the Warrior."

- **中文理解**：满街是戴礼帽的狐狸、穿马甲的兔子、小丑与丑角，Jessica 把认不出的装束都归为英国民俗角色；走到 jam monger 摊前，她猛地一怔——人群里有人扮成 Princess Kayla 和 Dargonna the Warrior。
- **关键词**：foxes in top hats / rabbits in waistcoats / British folklore / jam monger / Princess Kayla / Dargonna the Warrior
- **为什么这样写**：先让她用 assumed 把一切盛装收编进民俗的安全解释，再用 did a double take 撕开一道口子——她熟识的两个角色混进了真人的节日队伍，惊疑只维持 a brief moment 就被她自己按下。
- **读者视角提示**：注意 jam monger 在此只是路标式的摊位名——记住这个转角的位置，人群与戏台都要从这里展开。

> **原句 4:** "But then the crowd surged momentarily, blocking her view of the couple. When it receded, they were gone. Jessica wasn’t entirely sure that she hadn’t imagined them."

- **中文理解**：人群一涌，挡住那对装扮者；人潮退去，两人已不见踪影。Jessica 拿不准自己是不是凭空想象出了他们。
- **关键词**：crowd surged / receded / gone / imagined
- **为什么这样写**：退场只交给一次 crowd surged 的物理遮挡，不给任何解释；随后 wasn’t entirely sure that she hadn’t imagined them 把判断权原样推给读者——见过的与想象过的，从此对不上账。
- **读者视角提示**：注意这段没有一个动词替那两位作证——叙述承认的只有 they were gone 这一个事实。

> **原句 5:** "Jessica was quite proud of her own Tigglestide costume, despite the fact that she was bundled into a thick peacoat and scarf, so that the only part that was visible to the public was the bunny ears, now affixed to a headband. Beneath her coat, she wore just her negligee. When she arrived at Greg’s work, she would get him alone and then she would open her coat to surprise him."

- **中文理解**：她对自己的 Tigglestide 装束相当得意：厚大衣围巾裹紧，旁人能看见的只剩头箍上的 bunny ears；大衣底下只穿 negligee。等到了 Greg 工作的地方，她要把他拉到没人的地方，敞开大衣给他一个惊喜。
- **关键词**：Tigglestide costume / peacoat and scarf / bunny ears / negligee / open her coat / surprise
- **为什么这样写**：得意与荒寒并置——quite proud 先立起她的自我评估，peacoat、scarf 的严实再补上寒冬的物理现实，negligee 才从大衣底下抖出来；她把当众 open her coat 当成分内的功课，步骤排得一板一眼。
- **读者视角提示**：注意视线的分配——公众只许看见 bunny ears，正主才看得到大衣以内，这套分层本身就是她的台词。

> **原句 6:** "Several dewy-eyed lambs milled around in a nearby pen, bleating, and passersby paused to inspect them like lobsters in a tank. Laid out on the counter of his stall, which was stained red and laced with deep scars, Rowan had an array of metal pokers and prodders and knives and cleavers and all the tools of the butcher’s trade."

- **中文理解**：Rowan 摊边的围栏里，几只眼神水润的羊羔咩咩叫着打转，过路人停下脚步，像打量水箱里的龙虾那样端详它们；染红、结满深疤的台面上，摆开 metal pokers、prodders、knives、cleavers——屠夫行的全套家什。
- **关键词**：dewy-eyed lambs / bleating / inspect them like lobsters in a tank / stained red / deep scars / butcher’s trade
- **为什么这样写**：让 dewy-eyed lambs 与 butcher 的 metal pokers 挤进同一画面，刀具以 array 的货架口吻排开；lobsters in a tank 一笔把围观者换算成挑食材的顾客，血腥于是有了日常的定价。
- **读者视角提示**：注意镜头从活物平移到案板——中间没有任何转场，买卖双方都把这条动线当作理所当然。

> **原句 7:** "A crowd of children was gathered in front of a stage, where Postman Pete, his face slathered with white pancake makeup and explosions of bright red rouge on his cheeks, was screeching in an alarming high-pitched voice. He wore a blond fright wig and a baggy black dress that gathered around his feet."

- **中文理解**：一群孩子聚在戏台前；台上的 Postman Pete 满脸白粉妆、两颊炸开大红的 rouge，用刺耳的高嗓尖叫，头戴金色乱发假发，一件肥大的 black dress 堆在脚边。
- **关键词**：Postman Pete / white pancake makeup / bright red rouge / alarming high-pitched / blond fright wig / baggy black dress
- **为什么这样写**：五官与衣着一样样加码——pancake makeup、rouge、fright wig、baggy black dress，把丑角行头堆到过载；alarming high-pitched voice 先声夺人，孩子与读者同时被钉在台前。
- **读者视角提示**：注意丑扮先于台词——他开口之前，嗓音、妆容与裙子已经把"这不对劲"演完了一半。

> **原句 8:** "“Welcome to the All Souls’ Priory, I’m the abbess,” he said in a lisping falsetto. “We’re a simple order of Discalced Silurian nuns here, nothing strange to see, just a bunch of women living together, no men at all, merely enjoying one another’s company. We just do normal nun things here, like idolatry, blasphemy, devil worship, and, of course, fornication!”"

- **中文理解**：他用咬舌的 falsetto 自我介绍是 All Souls’ Priory 的 abbess，手下的 Discalced Silurian 修女会"一切正常"：一群女人同住、没有男人、彼此作伴；日常功课是 idolatry、blasphemy、devil worship，外加 fornication。
- **关键词**：abbess / lisping falsetto / Discalced Silurian nuns / idolatry / blasphemy / devil worship / fornication
- **为什么这样写**：台词把"此处无事"与罪目清单缝在同一口气里——nothing strange to see 紧接着一串重罪，of course, fornication 的随手一加让亵渎成了报菜名；修道院的外壳包着荤段子。
- **读者视角提示**：注意笑点的构造——nuns、inspector 与 normal 三个说法互相拆台，台下笑的是台词，台上的人笑的是台下。

> **原句 9:** "Jessica flinched inside. … But she still felt like this pantomime was calculated to put her on the spot."

- **中文理解**：Jessica 心里一缩。……但她仍觉得这场 pantomime 存心要把她架在火上。
- **关键词**：flinched inside / pantomime / calculated / put her on the spot
- **为什么这样写**：flinch 先于任何宽解落地，身体先投了票；省略号跳过的辩护一句也没能立住，calculated 一词径直把这场戏定性为有意瞄准——而被瞄准的人正是她自己。
- **读者视角提示**：注意这里没有旁观者替她作保——感受全部记在她名下，连"是不是针对她"也只能由她自己判定。

> **原句 10:** "It was draped entirely beneath a trailing white sheet, so that only the head, a good ten feet above the floor, was visible. It glided across the floor with a strange lumbering gait, as if there were two people under the sheet both trying unsuccessfully to move in time."

- **中文理解**：这生物整个裹在拖地的 white sheet 里，只有约 ten feet 高的头露在外面；它以奇怪的蹒跚步态滑行，仿佛床单底下有 two people，怎么也合不上拍。
- **关键词**：trailing white sheet / ten feet / lumbering gait / two people under the sheet / in time
- **为什么这样写**：先给尺寸（a good ten feet）再给步态（strange lumbering gait），谜面全押在运动方式上——two people under the sheet both trying unsuccessfully to move in time，用同步的失败写出了床单下的拥挤；它越想走齐，越不像一个生物。
- **读者视角提示**：注意叙述只交出可观察项——高度、布料、步态，一个动机都不给；不安来自这些核对不了的细节。

> **原句 11:** "Round Robin’s head bowed forward awkwardly, its jaw unhinged, and it vomited a deluge of candy upon the children, who shrieked even louder as they scrambled for their reward. CRUNCH CRUNCH CRUNCH, the harsh sound of hard candy splintering under eager kiddie feet and between gapped kiddie teeth made Jessica cringe."

- **中文理解**：Round Robin 的头别扭地前倾，jaw unhinged，朝孩子们 vomit 出糖果的洪流，孩子们叫得更响、争抢这份 reward；CRUNCH CRUNCH CRUNCH——硬糖在急切的 kiddie feet 与豁牙的齿缝间碎裂，吵得 Jessica cringe。
- **关键词**：jaw unhinged / vomited a deluge of candy / reward / CRUNCH CRUNCH CRUNCH / kiddie feet / cringe
- **为什么这样写**：把发糖写成 vomited a deluge——馈赠与呕吐共用一个动词；CRUNCH CRUNCH CRUNCH 的拟声把领奖现场压成咀嚼现场，欢腾与反胃之间只隔一层糖纸。
- **读者视角提示**：注意孩子们的快乐是真的——这段的可怕不靠他们的恐惧，靠 Jessica 的 cringe 单独供电。

> **原句 12:** "But that strange face reminded her of the creature from her dream, and she started to feel faint, a strange fluttering in her gut, a rare mélange of fear and arousal that made her feel physically sick, and she was forced to turn away and hurry into the civic center before she lost her nerve."

- **中文理解**：那张 strange face 让她想起 creature from her dream，她开始发晕，胃里一阵陌生的扑腾——fear and arousal 稀罕地调在一起，恶心得她浑身发虚——最后她只得扭头，赶在 lost her nerve 之前快步冲进 civic center。
- **关键词**：strange face / creature from her dream / faint / fluttering in her gut / fear and arousal / physically sick / civic center
- **为什么这样写**：感觉像调酒一样逐味加注——faint、fluttering in her gut、fear and arousal——rare mélange 承认这杯配方见不得人；行进方向随之定死：turn away、hurry、进 civic center，用移动把眩晕压下去。
- **读者视角提示**：注意 her dream 这个回环——梦不在本章现场，身体的反应却作不了假。

> **原句 13:** "I’m not here for Round Robin, she reminded herself. I’m here for Greg."

- **中文理解**：她提醒自己：我不是为 Round Robin 来的。我是为 Greg 来的。
- **关键词**：Round Robin / reminded herself / Greg
- **为什么这样写**：两个短句同构对仗，宾语从 Round Robin 换成 Greg，就把跑偏的注意力手动扳回正题；reminded herself 说明这个念头需要反复按住——偏离的引力比意志更省力。
- **读者视角提示**：注意"提醒自己"这个动作本身——需要提醒的事，往往已经快要守不住。

## 一句话总结

Tigglestide Eve 当天，Jessica 带着兔耳头箍与 negligee 的"挽回计划"挤进节庆集市，pantomime 的荤笑话与 Round Robin 的白床单、vomited a deluge of candy 把她逼到 a rare mélange of fear and arousal 的边缘，她甩下一句 "I'm not here for Round Robin, she reminded herself. I'm here for Greg."，赶在失去勇气之前快步冲进 civic center。
