# Quaint Folk 情感节点

## 节点一：不空的空宅

全书从一座无人的宅子开始：庭园衰败、设施完好、无人入住，本地人都知道房子并不空。威胁不登场，只被当作常识顺口带出。

> "A good family would do well here, so it was a shame, the locals said, that such a beautiful house should stand empty—although they all understood, even if they were loath to say it aloud, that the house was not empty." （ch00）

> "And, sometimes, there were dreams." （ch00）

## 节点二：渡过不亮的灯塔

一家三口渡海赴岛。灯塔多年未亮，理由是"没人走这条路"；甲板上只有他们一辆巨大的租来的美国车。进港前手机信号永远消失，Sandy 被剪断与现代世界的最后一条线。

> " 'Tain't been lit in years, ma'am. 'Tain't necessary. No one comes this way." （ch02）

> "They had passed beyond the reach of the modern world." （ch02）

## 节点三：入住与画像

bobby 反问"为什么要去那栋空了多年的房子"，Bunnyfluff Bridge 的铭牌把兔子和宅子钉在一起。市长与 vicar 登门送欢迎礼篮，第一次面对紫头发的 Sandy，客气底下已经露出了嫌恶。门厅里，Helena Hollyoak 等身画像挂着 stern and sorrowful 的表情，膝上一只面目含混的动物——这个形象将贯穿全书并最终吞噬 Jessica。

> "'Ollyoak 'ouse 'as been empty for years." （ch03）

> "'It's purple,' said the vicar, raising his eyebrow. He looked positively sick at the idea, as sick as you could look while still smiling. 'He has purple hair?'" （ch04）

> "She clutched a furry animal in her lap with one hand and a thick book against her chest with the other." （ch03）

## 节点四：Sandy 的变身之夜

独睡的第一夜：鬼压床、胸口的重物、不得不念出的古老咒文。Sandy 被"剥"出身体化作野兔，飞过荒原、潜入地下安息日，与长耳黄眼的庞然之物对视——醒来时吐了一地，知道自己"被看见了"。

> "With sighing and sorrow and meckle care, I go forth in the form of a hare, and I go forth in the devil's name, e'er that I come back again . . ." （ch05）

> "They had seen something they weren't supposed to see, but, worse, they felt like it had seen them back." （ch05）

## 节点五：雾中的 George

Sandy 在花园设坛"召唤"来的不是精灵，而是农场少女 George。她嘴上最硬（"only little babies believe in Round Robin"），却主动提出带 Sandy 去看真的——石阵下她用捡来的垃圾搭起自己的"den o' sin"，连标本 ferret 都换上了人眼。跨出这一步时，两个"不对的那种"少年第一次结成同盟。

> "Finally, George said, 'If I wanted to give someone a real fright, I should say something like I curse you in Round Robin's name!'" （ch11）

> "'That's Jocko,' said George, noticing Sandy's attention as she leafed through a box of old vinyl records." （ch15）

> "'How would you like to see him for real?'" （ch11）

## 节点六：Jessica 梦游画像

Jessica 的梦一遍遍把她引向宅邸深处与那幅画像；凌晨三点她穿着睡裙梦游到画前，被 Sandy 撞见。晨起的羞耻与自我检查（"smooth and unblemished skin"）让恐惧第一次长在她自己的身体上。

> "It was the same painting as in the foyer of Hollyoak House. Well, almost the same." （ch13）

> "But she still couldn't help but run her fingers over her flanks as she showered, reassuring herself that where last night were mangled bite marks, today, in the harsh light of morning, there was only smooth and unblemished skin." （ch13）

## 节点七：机器说出她的名字

Greg 的"项目"原来是把 Round Robin 做成聊天机器人；原型机自己亮起，说出 Jessica 的初恋、她的梦，命令她今夜"supplicate"。她砸碎机器，也砸碎了婚姻最后一块遮羞布。

> "LET ME TOUCH YOU IN ALL THE PLACES THAT MORGAN MCKINLEY TOUCHED YOU. EVENTS HAVE BEEN SET IN MOTION. TONIGHT, YOU WILL SUPPLICATE ROUND ROBIN." （ch19）

> "She wasn't domesticated; she was merely caged." （ch19）

## 节点八：Molly 回来

崩盘之夜，众叛亲离的 Jessica 烂醉在床上，是"绝交"过的 Molly 摸黑回来了。拥抱与眼泪之后，Molly 说出那句全书最冷的话——她一直都知道。

> "'I had to, luv. I couldn't leave you like this.'" （ch20）

> "'I do know, luv. Round Robin can fix anything. You go to him and you give supplication to him and he'll sort it out, jiggery jam.'" （ch20）

## 节点九：造神史

临终的 Lady Tuppence 讲出全部真相：Round Robin 是 Helena Hollyoak 战后的"写作计划"——画像、节日、石阵、口碑，几年之内无中生有。她对"造假"指控的辩护只有一句反问；而她递出的那本《The Story of Round Robin》以儿童读物的口吻问读者：为什么不让它进门？

> "'Do you think that just because something is made up, it isn't real?' said the Lady Tuppence. 'Are you so foolish? Look at Hasenhurst and tell me that Round Robin isn't real.'" （ch23）

> "'Why don't you let Round Robin in?'" （ch23）

## 节点十：三场死亡与一场逃亡

同一夜：Jessica 在暗室签约、被咬穿喉咙，账目式短句落款；Greg 在酒馆被重生的神割喉；全村提灯上门，Sandy 和 George 在 carol 催眠下把死神请进屋。Sandy 摔碎设备、高呼代词，从混战中杀上塔顶，以百分之百的信念念完变兔咒，纵身一跃。

> "'But the deed was done and she now belonged to Round Robin.'" （ch24）

> "'He blinked in surprise but said nothing. He didn't even reach for his throat as he went down.'" （ch25）

> "The trapdoor burst open and the villagers erupted up onto the widow's walk. Sandy jumped from the tower and took flight." （ch27）

## 节点十一：第二回合的清晨

终章抹平一切：Jessica 在培根香气中醒来，噩梦内容不敢说出口；死者在厨房谈笑，Sandy 的紫发变成棕色，童话人物走在大街上。窗外兔群里蹲着一只体格过大、直立而坐的野兔，黄眼回望后跑开。园子恢复了盛景——这一次是"under the family's watchful eye"。

> "She remembered that it was the worst possible dream that a mother could have, so terrible that she dared not even speak it aloud: the destruction of her family, the loss of her child." （ch28）

> "It's so good, she thought as she gazed out the window, at the lush grounds of Hollyoak House, at the sunken garden now regaining its former splendor under the family's watchful eye, … to have a second chance." （ch28）
