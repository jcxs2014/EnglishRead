# 《Public Access Afterworld》概述

## 全书梗概

**这是一场招魂，转述者只是代笔。** 匿名叙事者开篇即交出作者权："This book is not my own. I wrote the words down on the page, but the stories did not originate with me. I want you to understand this up front."（ch01） 圣托马斯康复中心是一座没有棱角的甜甜圈建筑，以痛苦为燃料；他请读者把这本书记作仪式："Maybe think of this text as a séance. I’m hoping, probably foolishly, that by putting my friend’s TV show down on the page as best as I can remember it, I’ll help them find a safe path back to this world from wherever they’ve been hiding."（ch01） 而这个世界的规则只有一句："They put us in hospitals. They send us to hell."（ch01）

**第一夜：模拟电视的死亡。** 2009 年 6 月 12 日关断夜，David 与 Erin 在地下室办双人派对、醉酒初吻；周一 Erin 失踪，而 David 的人生如约变成电视剧："You see, ever since he was a child, David Sawyer had wished his life could feel more like a TV show. After the night of the analog-to-digital switch-off, it finally became one."（ch02） Erin 在静电底下看见的第一个广播是唱着《Amazing Grace》的锅铲女："Her face was pale, her eyes hollow little ant holes, as if she hadn’t slept in days. “I saw something underneath the static. A transmission. A broadcast.”"（ch03） Jim 的林中木屋里，那盘录像带的标签写好了她的命运："“It had my name on it. My full name: Erin Morrison. And a date: February fifth, 1983.”"（ch07）

**第二季：审核员 Bethany。** 二十七岁的跨性别女孩 Bethany Peters 在 GlobalVill 做夜班内容审核，她要的很小："All she wants—all Bethany Peters has ever wanted—is for her life to finally, actually start."（ch10） 同事 Kyle 教她的第一课是化粪池理论与告别委托："“Hey, let me ask you a favor,” Kyle said. “Don’t let them grind you into pea dust, yeah? Don’t let them make you disappear.”"（ch11） 她在队列里遇见吞药的 Jules、报出 911，代价是被开除；Abel 的单膝求婚成了压垮她的最后一根稻草："Because really there was no such thing as freedom. Not for people like her. There was just this same cycle, stuck on a loop. Prison and escape."（ch17）

**静电子追上来。** 她逃亡、撞车、被 Jules 收留又被逐出；夜里来接她的不是传说，而是那张脸："“I heard a rumor you’ve been looking for me,” David Sawyer told them, and Jules Estrada stopped struggling."（ch25）

**插播 1945：Chicago 项目。** Los Alamos 的主妇 Willa Fischer 从丈夫 Elliot 的公文包里掉出一台「无页之书」；她本人就是实验，而丈夫的告别语是全书最冷的家训："“You’ve never been happy here,” he said. “We both know it.” He stared at her. “Everything I’ve done at Los Alamos, I did for the safety of our country. For the future of our family. And I promise—I promise you, Willa, your sacrifice won’t be in vain.”"（ch26）

**A/B 测试的账单。** 回到奥斯汀，Bethany 唯一能救她的人 Abel 在晚餐桌上被灰静电闪死——公司出面承认这是家务："“It’s done,” Shailene told her boss tonight. “The boyfriend has been removed.”"（ch28） 围城之夜她押着 Kyle 上 HQ 对质，得到的答复是死亡通知的冷笑话："“Nope,” Kyle told her. “He was mostly telling the truth up there, but he left out the part where, as of a few hours ago, we actually do want you dead. Sorry, Bethany, you should have just eaten the stew.”"（ch33） 枪响处倒下的是 Kyle；逆天登场救走她的是 Jules 与 David Sawyer。

**季中停播：叙事者现身。** 转述者回到 Saint Thomas 的熄灯夜——幽灵们在地毯上互相讲述自己的假电视剧："A shared dream after lights-out, once the glow at the center of the donut had dimmed and it was possible for all of us to disappear once again, to escape our confines and become ghosts on the carpet in the basement, together and alone, listening to a fake television show."（ch34）

**第六季：Script Girl 之死。** Erin 的最后一个八月、伪维基里的「Air Ball Blues」、被父亲「修好」的磁带——她看穿 David 的欲望本质："“You don’t love me,” Erin said suddenly. “You want to be me.”"（ch38） Captain Video 在操场成形把她卷走，她留下的最后一句是："“David,” she said. Her voice was calm. “It’s okay. I get it now. I’m not really here. I never was.”"（ch39） 而 Jim Davis 原来是 Fisch 的亲哥哥——两个家族用两条产业链追捕同一批「crooked」。

**第七季：Detox 与百年口供。** 粉色牧场的 detox 里，1945 年的第一位受害者 Rachel 交出全部宇宙论——PAA 的本名："My father always called it simply ‘the septic tank.’ A place to put all that we must keep below."（ch45） 以及百年账单："“She’s the one I lost,” Rachel says. “And David has his. But there are so many of them. A century of screens, and so many women lost.”"（ch46） 出逃被新闻通缉后，Bethany 点单最后一场狂欢："“One last bender," Bethany says. "So let's make it count.""（ch49）

**第八季：五部虚构电影。** 第二人称放映的怪物 Wilhelm（Chimotera）为 Fisch 当了半个世纪的 informant；蜡笔论是全部宇宙论的通俗版："And you know what else, Wilhelm? No matter how many other crayons you add to the mix after that to try and change it, that brown isn’t going anywhere. Once you get the color brown, there’s no going back."（ch52） 被逼到认怂的怪物交出了 receiver 的名字："“I’ll tell you who I saw. Because I am weak. I’ll tell you, so that things might go on.”"（ch54）

**第九季：一年一年。** David 的活埋思想实验是他的人生判词："They say youth is wasted on the young. Well, in David's morbid little thought experiment, life is wasted on the living. And how has David spent his? Staring at screens. Sending girls to hell."（ch55） GlobalVill 围剿、Jules 的 unforming、Bethany 的镜中宣言："“I made it to the other side of the mirror," Bethany says. "I made you a TV show.""（ch63） 而无标题 coda 交出全书的身世之谜："Had she created him or had he created her?"（ch65）

**Postscript：视频店。** Bethany 携空白带走进 Captain Video，沿圆柱下行、分辨率与身体一同降解："Now she becomes part of it. She checks to make sure her VHS tape is still tucked safely in the waistband of her jeans. And, finally, she arrives."（ch66）

**终季：Self-Titled。** 她在治疗室醒来（ch68）、看清病房的假窗（ch71）、在门牌 NOAH PETERS 面前把这座监狱重新命名并以念力崩锁（ch73）、牵 Sunday 探长穿过幕布（ch74）、唤醒 Weaver Street 里的 Willa（ch75）——最后在面向全地球的直播里交出本书最大声的宣言："I’m leaving this place now. I’m coming back to your broken little world. And when I get there, everything is going to change."（ch77）

**五年后：三具身体爬回地球。** Willa 从巨屏爬出（"“Is this hell?” she asks him in a soft, scared voice."（ch78））、David 手臂穿屏接生 Erin（"David pulls his best friend out through the static, delivering her back to earth in one confident tug. A home birth through a sea of television."（ch78））、Bethany 独自爬出——红色跟着她们渗回地球。终幕画格交给叙事者："We hold on to the image, because it’s the final image of my TV show’s first season. Bethany Peters, a silhouette against a glowing crimson plague, hunched and sick on a calm morning back on earth. Born again—yet again—into the desert, through a screen."（ch78）

## 三个主题

**一、电视不是娱乐，是关押的隐喻。** PAA 的本名是化粪池（ch45），病人的广播是燃料，治疗的产线把创伤「打包成可消化份额」（ch70）；Erin 的「very special episode」把这座监狱的运作说尽："All that hurt and anger stewing while she was stuck so long in her own vacancy. In that “very special episode” that could never actually make sense of her violation, could only become a rerun of it, a prison dedicated to its endless re-creation."（ch77） 全书的一切恐怖都从「看」里长出来——屏幕是墙、是产道、是单向镜。

**二、被选中＝被猎捕。** 「crooked」是燃料条款里的猎物：审核员岗位就是感染管道（ch28 的算法投喂）、连环杀手与上市公司共用同一份名单、家族三代传承同一门「追线法」（ch40）。Rachel 的账单与 David 的自白互为正反面："A century of screens, and so many women lost."（ch46） "Sending girls to hell."（ch55）

**三、命名与重生：名字是唯一带得走的行李。** Noah→Bethany 的两次出生、Foreword 的「make it real」、coda 的换位童话、终季的直播宣言——本书把跨性别叙事写成媒介叙事："I was born on April nineteenth, 1999, in Harlingen, Texas, and then I was born again the summer of 2017 in a corner booth at Cheer Up Charlies, where I cried all night into my best friend Lily’s shoulder and said my real name out loud for the very first time."（ch77） 而终幕的 "Born again—yet again—into the desert, through a screen."（ch78） 是这条线的第三次、也是未完成的一次。
