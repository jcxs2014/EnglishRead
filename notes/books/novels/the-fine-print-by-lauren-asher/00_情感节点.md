## 节点一 · 遗书（ch01，Rowan）

祖父的葬礼上，Rowan 用冷淡把所有人挡在三步之外，直到律师开始念：三兄弟各得一份股份与一份任务，
他的那一份是 18%，条件是当董事、半年内交改造方案、交独立第三方表决，不通过就永久转给父亲。
全书从这一刻开始——他不是在追一个女孩，他是在守一道会被判不及格的题。

> ① "the dreamer who stopped dreaming, I ask you one thing…"（ch01）
> ② "An independent party will be contacted and asked to vote on your changes. If they are not approved, then your percentage of the company will be given to your father permanently. No second tries. No buying him out."（ch01）
> ③ "I will never allow our father to obtain our shares of the company."（ch01）

## 节点二 · 第一次赢（ch06，Zahra）

她把十亿美金设施写成废铁，换来的不是一顿斥责，而是一份新合同。她进他办公室前备好了无辜的姿态，
出来时已经是他的人了——而她当时并不知道的是，这份合约也是祖父早就写好的两份任务之一。

> ① "Not everyone can survive off Dreamland’s dismal wages."（ch23）
> ② "This is your chance to prove to yourself that nothing anyone says defines who you are. Only your actions do."（ch05）

## 节点三 · 提案会上的吻（ch09，Zahra）

她带着自己也知道撑不住的草图走进会议室，计时器按停的最后一秒被他叫回上一页。
本章是全书第一次身体接触发生的地方，也是两个人第一次同时明白：这不是玩。

> ① "Can I really present this to the group? My skills seem on par with a two-year-old child learning how to hold a crayon for the first time."（ch09）
> ② "“Let me guess. You’ll fire me. It’s a bit predictable for my taste but I respect it since you’re the man in charge.”"（ch09）

## 节点四 · 泡沫雪（ch26，Rowan）

圣诞村落的泡沫雪里他吻了她，脱口而出要约一次会——然后紧接着把她形容成自己最讨厌的那类人。
她转身走掉，他站在原地摸出一只空药瓶。这是全书最甜也最伤的一次转折。

> ① "She lets out a sad excuse for a laugh, and I realize I never want to hear that version of her laugh again."（ch26）
> ② "Her words throw me back into the past—of the boy who was rejected until he learned to stop caring."（ch26）
> ③ "I’ll prove it to Zahra. I’m willing to put myself out there and be rejected if it shows her I don’t need to hide behind a mask."（ch28）

## 节点五 · 对账（ch29，Zahra）

一顿道歉晚餐把童年、父亲、被偷拍、校园勒索、以及「我最初接近你只是为了验证你是个骗子」
一次倒完。Rowan 与 Scott 在这一夜合成同一个人，他们之间所有误会一次性结清——
但结清之后，两个人仍然没有在一起。

> ① "“If apologizing was easy, everyone would do it.”"（ch29）
> ② "So, instead of giving myself up, I found ways to spend time with you as Rowan while purposefully stealing the rest of your attention as Scott."（ch29）
> ③ "“While I’m sorry he hurt you, I’m not sorry he let you go.”"（ch29）

## 节点六 · 她交出自己的来处（ch38，Zahra）

纽约的顶层公寓，他们吃着披萨聊到深夜，她说出两年前的深度抑郁，以及抑郁的起点是前任出轨。
Rowan 在这一夜第一次说出喜欢，而她第一次写下「我可能爱他」，却按住不肯先开口。

> ① "“About two years ago, I fell into a deep depression.”"（ch38）
> ② "“This is me now. But who I was before, when everything happened—I was a broken shell. I forgot to believe in myself when it mattered most.”"（ch38）
> ③ "“Because I like you. It’s scary as hell because you make me feel everything."（ch39）

## 节点七 · 跨年夜与病毒性肺炎（ch40–ch42）

洛克菲勒中心的圣诞树、溜冰、中央公园的雪人，他第一次想把这些「第一次」一件件从她手里拿走；
同一天下午一通电话把一切压了回去。随后她冻病、恶化、瘫在他床上，醒来时记忆整段断了，
而救她的人坐在床边，是她认识的那个冷版本。

> ① "I want to steal more of Zahra’s firsts. Anything to recreate that smile she has while looking at a pile of rocks and a lopsided snowman. I want to own her smile just as much as I want to own every other part of her."（ch40）
> ② "How could I not? He’s thoughtful, reserved, and so damn sweet to me that I completely forget how he hates the general population. He drives me wild in the best kind of way and he makes my heart race whenever he’s in the same room as me."（ch41）
> ③ "Is this how my dad felt when my mother was rushed to the hospital time and time again? This burning desperation to do something yet the inability to fix anything?"（ch42）

## 节点八 · 「那我们之间还有『我们』吗」（ch44，Zahra）

病床上她第一次主动逼问答案，他用一串「这本来只是玩玩」「我不想当你男朋友」把话说到最伤人的地方，
又补上一颗炸弹——一月底 Dreamland 要换新 Director。这是她弧线的最低点。

> ① "“Is there still an us?” I voice the one question I’ve been avoiding since I woke up in this place."（ch44）
> ② "I would never subject you to that kind of misbelief when I’m moving back to Chicago soon."（ch44）
> ③ "I’ve resorted to stalking my buddy at her workplace because I hate the fact that she’s mad at me."（ch45）

## 节点九 · 表决（ch49–ch51，Zahra / Rowan）

她带着「他是骗子」的判定走进会议室，才发现祖父把自己放进了表决名单，而他毫不知情；
他展示的新方案不是扩建乐园，而是给最底层的员工涨薪、恢复福利、设立首个感官时段。
委员会通过，父亲说出那句从没打算说的话。门廊的秋千上，他把拆掉又装回去的东西一件件说清楚。

> ① "No matter how much Rowan has personally hurt me, there’s no question in my mind anymore that he’s the right man for the job. I would be stupid and petty to vote against him."（ch49）
> ② "No. I want to tell you that I’m proud of you."（ch50）
> ③ "Would you be willing to try something new with me?"（ch51）

## 节点十 · 开园（ch52，Zahra）

Nebula Land 正式开园，两家人剪断红绸带。她借致辞说明这片园区对已故祖父的意义，
并宣布自己怀孕。全书停在与第一章同一个情感坐标上：一个人从静止开始，走完了整个弧线，
再把终点交到一个还没出生的孩子手里。

> ① "I imagine Brady is smiling down on us today."（ch52）
> ② "He hasn’t stopped calling me that since we got married last winter in the snow."（ch52）
> ③ "There’s nothing I want more in life than to share Rowan’s love with our child."（ch52）
