# 《A Scholar's Guide to Demonology & Other Dark Arts》情感节点

## 节点一：围墙上的碎玻璃朝里（ch01-ch02）

**叙事概括：** 全书从一封措辞优雅的聘书开始，落在一条荒原公路的尽头。出租车司机见惯了在这所学校门口下车、出来时额头上抽动着惊恐的人；围墙顶上的玻璃不是为了挡外人，尖端全部朝内。Phil 此刻还只是一名找不到教职的拉丁文学者，她看见的是一所学校，读出的却是一座把学生关在里面的建筑。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I for one would far rather have an enthusiast who can learn the minutiae of teaching than some pallid disciplinarian who has a dozen certificates in teaching but no true burning desire to share their enthusiasm."（ch01）

> ② "The broken glass atop the walls sparkled in the afternoon sun as Phil’s taxi approached the school grounds. It seemed rather more jagged than Phil remembered from past experiences or pictures of schools – or prisons, or even madhouses."（ch02）

> ③ "It was then she realized that the jagged glass edges on top of the surrounding walls were pointing inwards. They weren’t to keep intruders out. They were to keep the pupils inside."（ch02）

## 节点二：教工室里的驯狮课（ch03-ch05）

**叙事概括：** 校长用宣读成绩单的腔调念出她的全名，随即告诉她简历换不来职位。教工室里坐着一排各怀心思的同事：有人告诉她「希望你比布伦登撑得久」，有人把小手枪塞回手提包，有人在她还没签字时就催她趁早跑。窗玻璃被抓钩砸碎的那一刻，Phil 第一次还手，也第一次听校长把管学生说成「驯狮」。

**关键引语：**

> ① "‘My dear, your CV got you this interview, but it will hardly secure you a post here at Grayne’s. Anyone can write a CV. That doesn’t necessarily make them a good teacher."（ch03）

> ② "‘That’s why it’s always very important to keep a firm but friendly air of authority, and never turn your back on the dear little things. Something that Miss Fellson has yet to learn, I fear.’"（ch05）

> ③ "‘Why are you still here?’ she hissed. ‘I told you to run. You haven’t signed a contract yet, have you? She can’t hold you to it just by your word if that’s all she’s got on you. This is your chance to get away.’"（ch05）

## 节点三：窗框里的密信与一张黑斑（ch06-ch08）

**叙事概括：** 她住进前任拉丁文教师安妮·布伦登的房间，从窗框里震出一张没写完的拉丁文密信，笔迹与教案上的相同。几天后，作业本里掉出一张只画着大黑圆的纸——黑斑。Tregennis 说这是个警告，Jane 催她趁还没签字赶紧走。被反锁在塔楼房间、面对六支冒烟的雪茄时，把她放出来的却是校长那只柯基。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Si fuerit in triangulo, mentitur in cunctis, et fallit in aliis negociis, it started. The handwriting was that of Anne Bredon – Phil could recognize it from the lesson plans."（ch06）

> ② "‘Tiny Tim does not die!’ Dr Tregennis barked, nearly rising from his chair in his vehemence. He subsided again. ‘Anyhow. This possibly richly deserved Black Spot is a warning. Have you done anything to annoy the girls recently, Miss Fen?’"（ch08）

> ③ "One round to her. One round to the girls. For she could hardly claim victory in this round, merely an assisted lack of penalties. It remained to be seen what they’d try next. And what other plausible, possible or impossible surprises the school might have for her."（ch08）

## 节点四：雾夜十字路口的那笔交易（ch09-ch10）

**叙事概括：** 玛格特连夜离校，Phil 冒雾去追。她在一处本不该存在的十字路口撞上安妮·布伦登，对方开出条件：午夜到地下室破坏学校的 wards，价钱随她填。Phil 没有说教，也没有转身逃走，而是追问对方究竟能从中得到什么。雾散之后，路口与人都消失了，只剩一只火焰豹子带话来。

**关键引语：**

> ① "My dear Philomela, you must be wondering why these women promenade the world with such careless abandon, or how the school still exists. I wonder myself! I can only conclude that over the years they have amassed a significant store of secrets and that they practise BLACKMAIL."（ch09）

> ② "‘Anne Bredon,’ the other woman said. ‘Pleased to meet you. It’s always interesting to see the people who try to fill your shoes. Do you ever have the urge to go back to your university and find out how well they’re managing without you?’"（ch10）

> ③ "‘No,’ Phil said. ‘I’m just trying to work out exactly what you gain from this. Because, and I hate to be a party pooper here, what you’re suggesting sounds like it might not be to the school’s ultimate benefit, and before I get into the water I like to know how deep it is, and whether or not it’s boiling.’"（ch10）

## 节点五：地下室，与那只终于开口的猫（ch11-ch13）

**叙事概括：** 从文献综述转向实地调查，她提着手电走进地下室：嵌在石地里的金属圆环、围成一圈的陌生文字、塞满链锁羊皮书的书架，还有一盒开了封、饼干却仍然新鲜的铁盒。读经台上摊开的是她自己的活页夹，页面上是一段召唤文，底部用红墨水写着「不要以那种形态降临」。她醒来时，身边卧着一只巨大的黑色缅因猫，随后那声音直接滑进了她的脑子。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Since apparently you need me to spell it out, the Head made me. Blackmail is such an ugly word, but it’s a very effective employment method. I’m sure she’s convinced herself she’s acting for the best."（ch11）

> ② "At the bottom was a sentence written in large capitals. The author had put exclamation marks around it and added arrows in red ink. It was like the desperate notes one saw attached to office printers to remind the user to check that paper is present and the printer is turned on before panicking and calling for help. It simply said: COME NOT IN THAT FORM."（ch13）

> ③ "At her involuntary squeak, the cat – for it was a cat, a huge black Maine Coon of the sort she’d dreamed about, just as gorgeous, magnificent and majestic as she’d always known it would be – yawned, looking vaguely disgusted."（ch13）

## 节点六：一位公爵，与一份可撤销的契约（ch14-ch15）

**叙事概括：** 猫自报头衔：Alastor，地狱的公爵、路西法的行刑者、地狱公共工程专员。它随即提醒她「你答应过给我吃的」。随后校长摊牌：学校替王座培养召唤者，契约是她自己念出来的；但她若真不愿意，可以退出，连记忆一并抹去。Phil 选了留下——从此她的房间里多了一只不肯承认自己是宠物的猫。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I am Alastor, Duke of Hell, Executioner of Lucifer, Hell’s Commissioner of Public Works. While I have numerous other titles and signs of distinction, I shall not ask you to remember them at this moment, though I will of course expect proper respect on all occasions. May I ask the name of my new mistress?"（ch14）

> ② "Trust me when I say that if you truly don’t want to go ahead with this, then you won’t have to. You won’t even remember it. But don’t make any hasty decisions. You have all sorts of possibilities open to you now."（ch14）

> ③ "You promised me food, Alastor said inexorably."（ch15）

## 节点七：检查团、假驾照与一场爆炸（ch17-ch19）

**叙事概括：** 教育部的检查团进校，校长把带路的差事塞给 Phil。她在走廊尽头看见倒地的检查员，口袋里是一张写着 Tom Abernathy 的驾照，而真正的司机早已溜进校内。检查团带走一肚子不满离校之后，小恶魔把伏特加蒸馏器接上中央供暖系统，一声爆炸把六个低年级生困在阁楼里。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The school has wards around it, which stop demons inside from going out, or demons outside from coming in, and also prevent communication, so you can’t talk to me from a distance if we’re on opposite sides.’"（ch17）

> ② "‘Driving licence in the name of Tom Abernathy,’ Phil said, inspecting it then putting it back again. ‘Wallet with a load of cards in that name too, so it’s probably genuine. No incriminating letters or typed instructions, how annoying."（ch18）

> ③ "There has been an explosion, Alastor reported. I believe the little imps tried to connect their vodka still to the school central heating system. Both have ruptured. He sounded moderately approving of their enterprise."（ch19）

## 节点八：一夜之间，学校成了战场（ch21-ch24）

**叙事概括：** 校门以东一英里的爆炸震得窗玻璃发颤，Alastor 坐起来说 wards 已倒。Phil 在楼梯间打断众人「是女生干的」的推断，指出有人正在攻击学校。十名女生对十名持械的雇工，武器是台球、曲棍球棒和一具灭火器；而在这一切之中，她还要听玛格特·汉密尔顿承认，是自己炸开了 wards、把安妮放了进来。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Alastor sat up abruptly. Mistress, he said. The wards are down."（ch21）

> ② "‘It’s not them,’ Phil cut in. ‘It’s not the girls. Someone’s attacking the school. The phone’s been cut off.’"（ch22）

> ③ "‘I blew up the school’s wards and let Miss Bredon in. Did any of the others get to do that? No, they did not. And she’s said she’ll be wanting my help again. She said she can recognize potential when she sees it. That she’ll teach me the big secrets, the “arts”, demon summoning and everything, all the stuff that everyone thinks I’m too young or too stupid for."（ch24）

## 节点九：乌鸦飞走，雪落在募捐的歌声里（ch25-ch27）

**叙事概括：** 地下室最后一战，安妮·布伦登自称「我是恶魔学家」，要的是那些书；她最终在被击溃时化形为一只乌鸦，向南飞去。学期结束，捐给 RNLI 的合唱队在雪里唱着歌，Phil 背着装了猫的包，听家人算她该回家的账，也知道自己会回来。

**关键引语：**

> ① "No. I am not a witchfinder, or an exorcist, or anything like that. I’m a demonologist! A demonologist, do you hear me! And I want those books so I can summon up exactly whichever demons I need to, whenever I want!"（ch25）

> ② "She seemed to crumple in on herself, losing mass like a sandcastle dissolving in the tide. A raven burst from her discarded trench coat, spreading its wings as it launched into a near-vertical climb, and cawed bitterly as it flew southwards."（ch26）

> ③ "Phil felt the snow brush against her cheeks and smiled, looking at the singing schoolgirls and watching teachers haloed by dancing whiteness on this December evening. ‘Perhaps sometimes one does get a school story ending after all,’ she said to herself."（ch27）
