# 《A Scholar's Guide to Demonology & Other Dark Arts》概述

一位古典学者在女校教书，却因为半夜念错一份召唤文书，把地狱公爵请进了自己的宿舍——这部小说的趣味，正在于它把学术考据、校园政治与恶魔契约套进了同一套语法里。

## 全书梗概

**一封正经信，和一地朝内的玻璃（ch01–ch04）**

Philomela Fen 应聘 Grayne's 的拉丁文教师，校长 Miss Jagerson 不用邮件而用亲笔信联系她；她父亲破产、母亲掌了家里账本，她因此不得不接受这份工作。出租车驶近校地时，她看见围墙顶上的碎玻璃朝里长；守在门口的学生头领是 Head Girl Elizabeth James，一句「old Jaggers」就把这所学校的旧账撂在她面前。这所学校请人容易，却从不轻易放人走。

""It was then she realized that the jagged glass edges on top of the surrounding walls were pointing inwards. They weren’t to keep intruders out. They were to keep the pupils inside."（ch02）"

Jane Worth 在参观校舍时连声劝她别签合同、趁早走人，但 Phil 最终还是接下那本 1970 年初版的《剑桥拉丁课程》第三册，签了字，把一位姑婆的告诫和自己的骄傲一起锁进了校门里。

""‘Oh, absolutely.’ The corgi scuffling along behind them gave a little bark, which almost sounded like a snort of laughter. ‘And you did quite creditably. I think you have potential, my dear. Let’s go back to my office. I’d like to get your signature on a contract before we go any further.’"（ch04）"

**教工室的第一次交手（ch05–ch08）**

Phil 在教工室认识 Miss Grending（历史）、Dr Tregennis（英文）、Miss Day（校长秘书）、Mrs Vaughan（科学）、Miss Fellson（体育）与 Jeffrey Smithson（现代语言），也第一次领教了 Miss Grending 的尖刻。学生用 grapnels 从窗户砸向新老师，Miss Jagerson 在旁边教她「永远不要背对那些小东西」。同一时期，因试图组织工会并罢工而被开除的 Margot Hamilton 开始纠集高年级生反抗，Phil 的黑斑纸片也在这时出现在她门下。

""Something – some things – came spinning towards the window, and Phil took an involuntary step back. As her perspective came into focus, she realized they were grapnels, and they’d hooked themselves onto the ornate stone carvings (wolves and robed skeletons) on the windowsill."（ch05）"

Dr Tregennis 那句「大概完全应得的 Black Spot 是个警告」把校内的敌意挑明了：学生并非恶作剧，她们在结党，而校长显然乐见其成。

""‘Tiny Tim does not die!’ Dr Tregennis barked, nearly rising from his chair in his vehemence. He subsided again. ‘Anyhow. This possibly richly deserved Black Spot is a warning. Have you done anything to annoy the girls recently, Miss Fen?’"（ch08）"

**雾夜十字路口，与一封勒索信（ch09–ch12）**

Phil 的姑婆 Belinda 写信数落她的生活方式，信里那个全大写的「BLACKMAIL」把学校靠秘密运转的底色点破；早餐桌上 Jane Worth 用一句「generous」盘问她，而 Margot 离校失踪。Phil 冒雾走到一个本不该有横路的十字路口，Anne Bredon 现身开价：午夜动手毁掉学校的 wards，就给她一大笔钱。Phil 拒绝了。

""‘I do have a duty of care,’ Phil pointed out. ‘In loco parentis, which I hope you’re able to translate as well as I am. And given the way the Fourth and Fifth Forms were slacking off on their work, they’d never have passed their GCSEs. I know you’re offering me a bucketload of money, but please. I have my pride.’"（ch10）"

夜里锁门、Jane 承认自己是被校长用同样手段弄来的教师，而暴风雨夜巡逻时 Miss Grending 承认那场针对她的局面是自己设的局。

""‘But then I’d have to kill you.’ Miss Grending put out her cigarette on one of the tables. ‘I’ll be going back to my rooms now. Don’t wait up. Oh, and if you run across any little secret meetings on your way, do try not to disturb the girls."（ch12）"

**召唤，与地狱公爵的头衔（ch13–ch16）**

地下室的密室里，召唤文书上用红墨水画着箭头写着一句警告。Phil 终究念出了它，得到一只巨大的黑色缅因猫；那猫随即自报头衔，说明这所学校源自女修院，并摊开校长与教师们知情共谋的真相。Anne Bredon 带着她的宠物蛇来上课，Phil 想要一只会呼噜的普通猫，而 Alastor 开口讨吃的姿态，像极了一个交上伙食费的主人。

""At the bottom was a sentence written in large capitals. The author had put exclamation marks around it and added arrows in red ink. It was like the desperate notes one saw attached to office printers to remind the user to check that paper is present and the printer is turned on before panicking and calling for help. It simply said: COME NOT IN THAT FORM."（ch13）"

枪、纵火与后门的动静接连出现，Jane 的左轮第一次出鞘；校长在校门口的雨伞下等着她们，只留下一句「听你那只猫的话」。

""You promised me food, Alastor said inexorably."（ch15）"

**检查团、蒸馏器与一张藏宝图（ch17–ch20）**

教育部检查团进校，Phil 跟着自称 Richard 的检查员一路盯梢，才知道那人的驾照名字是 Tom Abernathy；同一天，低年级生把伏特加蒸馏器接上中央供暖系统，阁楼里六个孩子险些丧命。晚自习时 Casey Nevins 召来恶魔 Valac，用一小时的「精力」换走约克郡荒原上最近一处埋藏宝物的位置。

""There has been an explosion, Alastor reported. I believe the little imps tried to connect their vodka still to the school central heating system. Both have ruptured. He sounded moderately approving of their enterprise."（ch19）"

""‘You will accurately and scrupulously mark the location of the nearest buried treasure on a map of these Yorkshire moors,’ Casey said. ‘In return, you shall have a gift of my energy equal to an hour of my life.’"（ch20）"

**结界失效，与十名雇来的打手（ch21–ch24）**

早餐时传来 Anne Bredon 被捕的消息，紧接着一声远处的爆炸炸掉了学校周围的 wards；Phil 带着十名女生应对校门外袭击，对手带着毒气弹、电击棒和防弹背心，她自己则靠一枚扔出去的台球把一名打手引开。对讲机里 Anne 亲口下令「All shift to contingency plan A」，而 Phil 拒绝了 Alastor 提供的恶魔力量，改用灭火器。

""Alastor sat up abruptly. Mistress, he said. The wards are down."（ch21）"

Margot 承认是自己炸了 wards、把 Anne 放进校里，理由是 Anne 说她「看得出潜力」，要教她召唤与法术。

""‘I blew up the school’s wards and let Miss Bredon in. Did any of the others get to do that? No, they did not. And she’s said she’ll be wanting my help again. She said she can recognize potential when she sees it. That she’ll teach me the big secrets, the “arts”, demon summoning and everything, all the stuff that everyone thinks I’m too young or too stupid for."（ch24）"

**地窖、拔剑与雪中的募捐（ch25–ch27）**

地窖里 Anne 自称「我是恶魔学家」，她要的是图书馆，好随时召出她需要的恶魔；她抽出一柄黑钢长剑，甩飞时剑刃在 Phil 的手腕上划开一道口子，血滴到地板上。Jane 抱着笔记本走到校门口，宣布 Anne 名下的账户已被清空并报到税务与警方；Anne 最终失去实体，化作一只向南飞去的乌鸦。

""When she withdrew her hand, there was a long blade shining in it. It was black steel, so deeply ominous it almost seemed to glisten with bloodlust, and the hilt was ornamented with rubies and runes. The air hissed when the sword sliced through it, like radio static but a hundred times worse."（ch26）"

广场上女生们唱歌募捐，校长把保险不赔、缺钱说成常事；雪落下来时，Phil 背着装猫的背包，看着被白色镶边的老师们。

""‘I just wanted to tell you that all of those names have had their bank accounts drained.’ Jane smiled, and it was a dreadful thing to see. ‘And on top of that, reported to the Inland Revenue and various police services, and you don’t want to know who else."（ch26）"

## 三个主题

### 主题一：契约、责任与共居

Phil 想要的从来不是力量，而是一份不掺杂任何东西的亲昵；可既然契约是她自己在半夜念出来的，责任就只能由她承担。她把 Alastor 当成需要被教导的对象，也因此让自己同时成了恶魔与女生的道德榜样。

""And if Alastor was her responsibility now, due to some utter stupidity when it came to reading demonic contracts aloud at midnight in hidden cellars (Phil wasn’t going to forgive herself for that any time soon), then after all, it was her duty and her job to teach, enlighten and provide a better way forward in life to everyone she was associated with. Including her cat. Including her demon."（ch15）"

### 主题二：教学就是站到学生那一边

Phil 拒绝照本宣科地赶进度，宁可另编课文、改口试形式；她清楚地知道自己在争的从来不只是课程表，而是「谁有资格定义学会」这件事。学生最后开始替她说话，而她要的回报，不过是她们真的学到东西。

""‘Excellent,’ she said. ‘I look forward to telling you a great deal about Roman culture and history. But we are also going to cover our set texts and improve our grasp of Latin. And when I say we, I mean you.’"（ch07）"

### 主题三：一所靠秘密运转的学校

Miss Jagerson 经营这所学校的手段包括勒索、告密与共谋；这地方原本是女修院，黑魔法一路写进了校史与课程。她挑中 Phil 的理由坦白得近乎体面：有伦理、够好奇、够鲁莽。

""My dear Philomela, you must be wondering why these women promenade the world with such careless abandon, or how the school still exists. I wonder myself! I can only conclude that over the years they have amassed a significant store of secrets and that they practise BLACKMAIL."（ch09）"

## 人物弧光

### Philomela Fen

- 起点："More than that, there was the feeling of something vast, unexplored and interesting here. She wasn’t quite sure what it was, but it had the smell of a secret – and Phil was only human. She liked discovering secrets."（ch05）
- 转折："Just because you could solve problems with demons, Phil decided, didn’t necessarily mean that you should. There had to be something that she could do. ‘Fire extinguishers,’ she said, the words coming out like a mingled inspiration and prayer. ‘We have fire extinguishers in the school, don’t we?"（ch23）
- 落点："Phil felt the snow brush against her cheeks and smiled, looking at the singing schoolgirls and watching teachers haloed by dancing whiteness on this December evening. ‘Perhaps sometimes one does get a school story ending after all,’ she said to herself."（ch27）

Phil 从一个把新工作当谜题解的拉丁文教师，变成明知危险仍不肯把恶魔之力当默认解法的人：她宁可拿灭火器、曲棍球棍和学校里的桌椅去对付 Flauros 与那些亡者，也不肯让 Alastor 替她出手。结局不是她赢了，而是她还在原地，背着猫，看着雪落在唱歌的女生身上。

### Harriet Jagerson

- 起点："May I first say what a pleasure it was to receive a formal letter rather than an email in your application for a position as Latin teacher here at Grayne’s? I had begun to think that much of the modern world has forgotten how to apply pen to paper. It’s good to know that some people still remember how it’s done."（ch01）
- 转折："I see in front of me someone ethical enough to say no to Anne Bredon, inquisitive enough to find their way down to the summoning room, and – how shall I put this – reckless enough to read the contract which I admit I left there. Three qualities necessary to any good demonologist."（ch14）
- 落点："‘Of course, dear. My door is always open.’ She smiled at them both. ‘I’ll leave you to get on with things. Do keep up with your studies, Phil. And listen to your cat. I’m sure he has a great deal to share with you.’ She turned and followed the juniors into the school, Faust padding at her heels. It might have been Phil’s imagination, but the rain seemed to avoid them both."（ch16）

她以亲笔信、称呼与程序感建立权威，再用勒索把教师与学生一并锁在校内；学生叫她 old Jaggers，教师叫她 Miss Jagerson。她承认召唤文书是她故意留下的，也知道自己在拿一所学校赌。雾夜与枪声之后，她仍在校门口的伞下等她们回来，把关键的一句托付给一只猫。

### Anne Bredon

- 起点："A serpent’s head glided out from the cuff of Anne’s sleeve. It was scarlet. It was vibrantly obvious against the dark wood of the desk and ink-spotted paper. There was no way it could be a hallucination."（ch08）
- 转折："No. I am not a witchfinder, or an exorcist, or anything like that. I’m a demonologist! A demonologist, do you hear me! And I want those books so I can summon up exactly whichever demons I need to, whenever I want!"（ch25）
- 落点："She seemed to crumple in on herself, losing mass like a sandcastle dissolving in the tide. A raven burst from her discarded trench coat, spreading its wings as it launched into a near-vertical climb, and cawed bitterly as it flew southwards."（ch26）

她以袖中滑出的猩红蛇头登场，以一封未写完的拉丁文密信为标志；她要的从来不是钱，而是学校地下的那座图书馆。她提供价钱、地图、恶魔与政客，把别人的贪心当杠杆；被逼到地窖深处时她仍然自认是学者而非猎人，最终以一只乌鸦的形态向南逃去。

### Alastor

- 起点："I am Alastor, Duke of Hell, Executioner of Lucifer, Hell’s Commissioner of Public Works. While I have numerous other titles and signs of distinction, I shall not ask you to remember them at this moment, though I will of course expect proper respect on all occasions. May I ask the name of my new mistress?"（ch14）
- 转折："Alastor lifted his head to give her a very direct stare. Just because you can depart and return at will, Mistress, you think it a small matter. How do you think I feel, confined within this pitiful square mile? I, who am a Duke of Hell?"（ch19）
- 落点："‘There will be shortly,’ Jane said. She was in a heavy dolman-sleeved black jacket and thick wool trousers, and looked rather more diabolical than angelic. ‘Once people start coming out of work and heading for the pubs.’ She peered over Phil’s shoulder. ‘Is your cat all right? I still can’t see why you felt the need to bring him with us.’"（ch27）

他自报头衔时是地狱公爵与公共工程专员，把人当契约关系的一端来谈；被关在 wards 之内时，他把这份不自由当成一种不公平。他既替 Phil 追踪敌人、也替她向学生通风报信，最终被装进背包带出校门，成为队伍里最不通人情的那一个。
