# 《The Labyrinthine Rule of Lavina Theron》情感节点

## 节点一：Dranderon 黑港——冷血买主的救援（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 化名 Opal 的女主与线人 Casimir 潜入禁港，从独眼人贩 Gremlar 手中「买」回三个孩子。验货、压价、砍半价——每一个字都在把人折算成损耗品，而每一个字都是演的。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“No. Half is what I require for the girl. We don’t know if she can even see through that eye. I’ve got years of feeding her before I can even work her—if that’s her destiny, anyway."（ch01）

> ② "“Because those with the power to help have the responsibility to do so—and you are now my responsibility.”"（ch01）

## 节点二：驻营与首场试炼的暗过（ch02）

**叙事概括：** 侦察确认敌营、遭袭反杀、掷匕救将——她在战场上的每一秒都在为将来铺路。而将军帐篷里的评估报告早已交上去：第一场 Brinden Trial 在不知情中已经通过。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I entered this world by way of my mother’s death. You will find I am no stranger to the dead,” I snap. The time-worn general whose service I’ve been assigned to demands that I scout the Dranderon movement while he remains protected atop his own steed, concealed by the forest."（ch02）

> ② "“You know, Commander, when I heard I’d be getting Theron’s kid, I expected a lady—and you are no lady.” I frown at the words. I’m both."（ch02）

## 节点三：宫廷两张面孔与宴席惊变（ch03-05）

**叙事概括：** Mildred 把 Commander 妆成 Lady；Marked 臂的石刃与家世的全景图同章铺开。宴会狂人抓住 Eirene 的手腕——袖刀见血、国王散席、她抢下审讯权：「This is personal。」

**关键引语：**

> ① "True marks are somewhat rare but there are countless imitations. The worst are on children whose own parents scar them in hopes of binding them to a fortunate match. The cruel practice—illegal here and supposedly in Dranderon as well—happens everywhere."（ch04）

> ② "Entering the Brinden Trials required my family’s approval, as participation forfeits claims to noble holdings. The law exists to balance power between those who rule and those who wield force. Without other lines of succession to the titles, nobody would be left to rule."（ch05）

## 节点四：地牢与「Little Fire」的第一声（ch06）

**叙事概括：** 疯将军 Tenvelo 把她错认成 Brea 王后，吐出大分裂秘辛。「Little fire」未说完即被父亲割喉——全国第一个知道她代号的人死了。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Hayden was already experimenting with using Death Benefits to communicate all those years ago? I thought it was only after Reynolds left. The timeline doesn’t matter—Tenvelo’s revelation about the wicked king wouldn’t change my opinions about Dranderon. My hate solidified long ago."（ch06）

> ② "Before he can continue, my father steps forward, grabbing Tenvelo by the hair and swiftly slicing through his neck with his small dagger. Tenvelo’s eyes flare and close just as quickly. He barely gurgles before settling into a slump in his chair, the ghosts living inside his body for all these long years finally resting."（ch06）

## 节点五：童年闪回——Little Fire 的诞生（ch07）

**叙事概括：** 十三岁的马车遇袭：卫兵战死、她跳车引敌、冰河逃生——刺客在河沿喊出「Come back, Little Fire!」。噩梦持续十三年，直到地牢的话渗进梦里。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I have to be the one to jump.” I have always handled the cold winters better than her, and in this weather, Eirene’s lungs will have no chance against the freezing wind."（ch07）

> ② "Drenched in sweat, I wake with my blankets twisted about my legs and my pulse unnaturally slowed by my amulet, fighting the memory of the Dranderons. I have had the nightmare for almost thirteen years, and it has always unfolded the same way—until now. My pursuer spoke this time at the river’s edge."（ch07）

## 节点六：Rowena 初遇与「You will be mine」（ch08-12）

**叙事概括：** 黑袍骑士策马而过、审讯被一句耳语反杀、醉汉被她秒放倒——Draven 报出名字。从 lint 相遇到同床共枕的口误「You will be mine」，两人的权力游戏五局三胜。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“You will be mine.” I know how that sounds. Great choice of words, Lavina. “I’m not going to let you out of my sight now that you have seen my mark,” I clarify."（ch12）

> ② "It’s the emblem for Cadmar—from when the Killer King ruled. I’ve seen it before on some old kildron my father has from there.” Alden’s blood gift of Memory often pulls out random facts."（ch09）

## 节点七：验臂与「He's not my Marked」（ch13）

**叙事概括：** 她亲手摘他的手套验臂——臂上无印。Lucan 同夜撞见那只 Marked 手、摔门而去。情感线最高点之一：她以为输了，其实还没开奖。

**关键引语：**

> ① "My heart endures a line of fracture I’m struggling to hide. “Well, that settles that,” I say, my voice attempting to betray me."（ch13）

> ② "Under his gaze, I feel seen; not watched or judged like I do with everyone else, and it forces my body to hold a heaviness I’ve not carried before."（ch13）

## 节点八：Lavintia 沉没大陆与初吻（ch17-18）

**叙事概括：** 互信开箱、Vision Stone 验印、森林画撕页相赠——初夜前的所有铺垫。黑暗中他凭听觉擒住她：「I won't be second to fate.」她的初吻在 24 岁交出。

**关键引语：**

> ① "It’s part of you!” He’s a bit louder now as he glowers. “You don’t get to hide behind your snakes, Lavina. I won’t be second to fate. When you choose me, it won’t be for any reason other than desire."（ch18）

> ② "People are desperate for better lives. Sometimes the marks are so convincing—defined by whatever they use for pigment—that they still show through a little."（ch17）

## 节点九：Brinden Ball 弃衔与「My fire」（ch19-20）

**叙事概括：** Brinden Ball 弃衔夜——黑装亮相、蓟花授勋、当众宣誓放弃 Lady of Estrold。Vine 渡河初捷后，第三试遭遇红羽箭伏击：护符炸裂、森林熔化、King Reynolds 死于乱局。

**关键引语：**

> ① "My fire."（ch33）

> ② "Look around. You. Are. Intimidating. Like a dark princess.”"（ch19）

## 节点十：身世核弹与放逐日（ch34-41）

**叙事概括：** Stone Sickness 病理课揭开她的伪装史、Hayden 的 Death Benefit 全揭、父亲与 Draven 的对话在她病榻旁进行——「Love her」的双声部与「她不知道 Lavina 的父亲是谁」的半句悬崖。Tribunal 判流放：四场告别、部下全员跟随、临别威胁。

**关键引语：**

> ① "She would have one tempering the other, hoping to one day take back Cadmar."（ch35）

> ② "it’s all of us or none of us now."（ch41）

## 节点十一：Seeker 起航与「crown her queen」（ch42-43）

**叙事概括：** 兄妹告别、Eirene 怀孕、Lumi 托孤——Seeker 的蛇首与四年前 Drighton 的 skiff 夜遇终于对上。Orris 一句「my prince」揭出 Draven 的王储身份——终句「I would take my exiled wicked princess to Cadmar and crown her queen.」为全书收尾。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I was there. I’m sure.”"（ch42）

> ② "Then I would take my exiled wicked princess to Cadmar and crown her queen.”"（ch43）
