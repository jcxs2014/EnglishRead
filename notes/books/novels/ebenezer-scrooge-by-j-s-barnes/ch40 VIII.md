# 40. VIII

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：晚餐后的餐桌是本章的舞台。Fell 太太先说「I don’t believe that Mr Scrooge can be joining us tonight after all」，作者立刻点明这句是以「a kind of forced lightness」说出，好像那件事无关紧要；上尉用「an approximation of the same, dishonest tone」附和，女士补一句「Perhaps something has delayed him」，上尉连说「Oh no doubt. No doubt.」——三个人共同演出一场轻描淡写的戏，而作者称之为 dishonest。餐桌上的残羹「looked like one of happy domesticity」，Fell 太太却觉得「I don’t believe… that I expected so warm a welcome somehow」，上尉也惊讶；一切「Pleasant, yes, but ordinary too.」轮到 Bethlem 的规矩，女士被晚餐与时间软化，承认「I do think we should follow them」，但替 Bethlem 辩护：她独居此地太久，「it would hardly be unusual if all manner of queer beliefs and superstitions should spring up in her」。上尉想追问 Bethlem 与她在门外台阶上谈了什么，先「paused then, to make sure that he did not phrase what followed as a question」。Fell 太太答：她问了舅舅的事——因为他看上去生前与 Bethlem 熟识，她那时只是个孩子，还问了关于他的传闻是否属实。Bethlem 的回答是：他不是那种能被真正了解的人；她从未与任何称得上亲近他的人相识；她只能据实而言，但舅舅待她「a very fair – no, more than fair – a very generous employer」，死后仍对她与丈夫「considerable material help」。Fell 太太补充：「I had the strong sense that there was more that she might have said then. But she seemed so eager to depart that I let her.」于是上尉说「So, we’re on our own」，女士答「Only until Mr Scrooge gets here」，而他「Without meaning to, Ambrose made the word sound full of doubt. ‘Only until then.’」随即提议就寝。话一出口他就「stopped short then, for he realised that he had asked a question aloud」——Fell 太太也意识到了，脸上显出「distinct nervousness」。之后只有小镇的寂静。两人在楼梯平台上道别，站得「a goodly distance apart」；Fell 太太说「in spite of it all, I should not have wished to have come here alone」，上尉答「Very welcome, ma'am. Very welcome indeed.」各自持烛回房，紧张张望，却「discovered that there was nothing at all to be worried about」，床铺舒适，「drifting off very easily and lightly to sleep」。作者给出时间坐标：约夜里十点，「for the following four hours」两人睡得又香又沉——末句：「This was to be the last time that they would do so in that house.」

**情感弧线位置**：本章弧线为「伪装的轻松—家常的表象—惊异与琐碎—为规矩辩护—交出底牌（舅舅）—承认有话没说完—「我们只能靠自己」—失言成问—紧张的沉默—克制的道别—安稳入睡—最后一句的判决」。真正的动作发生在最后一句：前面十四块全部是「一切正常」的铺垫，读者被训练成相信今晚无恙，然后作者用一句预告把这份安稳全部收回。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现了「暴风雨前的平静」与「最后的安眠」两个 trope。反转在于：这份平静不是被外力打破的，而是**本来就不该存在**——所有人都遵守了规矩、都睡得沉甜，问题恰恰出在这里。另一个精妙反转是上尉的「失言」：他小心避开问句，结果还是问了出来；Bethlem 禁止的是「提问」，而提问可以是句子的语气。

**人物弧线**：Fell 太太是本章唯一发生位移的人：她从怀疑 Bethlem 的规矩（「But Mrs Bethlem's rules…」）转为替她辩护（「I do think we should follow them」），理由是人性化的——一个独居太久的老人长出古怪信念并不奇怪。同时她也交出了自己最在意的东西（舅舅的真相），却因为「she seemed so eager to depart that I let her」而错过了追问。上尉则从保护者滑向焦躁者：他用「honest tone」的假面维持镇定，最后一句「Only until then」暴露了不信。Bethlem 太太只以转述在场，而那句转述里藏着关键留白。

**叙事手法**：一是**作者不断标注「不真诚」**：「with a kind of forced lightness」「an approximation of the same, dishonest tone」「Without meaning to」——叙述者不断提醒读者：这些人嘴上说的和心里想的不是一回事。二是**小标题式的清单段落**：「Now, there is no need to trouble you with what followed. With the setting aside of… With Captain Ambrose's careful locking of the front door…」——用一串 With 开头把准备就寝的琐事写成仪式清单，读者自动意识到「作者在拖延」。三是**时间锁定**：「By now it was around ten o'clock at night. And for the following four hours…」——把「安全」精确到四小时，而 Bethlem 的规则正是「十点后不开门」，规则在夜里生效。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** I don’t believe that Mr Scrooge can be joining us tonight after all.” This was said by Mrs Fell and it was said with a kind of forced lightness, as though the matter of which she spoke was somehow of very little moment. “I fear you may be right,” replied the captain, in an approximation of the same, dishonest tone. “Perhaps something has delayed him,” suggested the lady.

**中文理解**：「我不认为 Scrooge 先生今晚还能来得了。」这句话是 Fell 太太说的，语气带着一种刻意的轻松，好像她说的那件事无关紧要。「恐怕你说得对，」上尉答道，用的是同一个腔调——那同样是一种不诚实的调子。「也许有什么事耽搁了他，」那位女士说。

**关键词**：I don’t believe that Mr Scrooge can be joining us tonight after all / with a kind of forced lightness / as though the matter of which she spoke were somehow of very little moment / in an approximation of the same, dishonest tone / Perhaps something has delayed him

**为什么这样写**：作者在第一块就把话术拆穿：forced lightness（勉强的轻快）与 dishonest tone（不诚实的腔调）。三个人用三种说法表达同一件事——不抱希望，却都假装这不重要。读者由此知道：他们已经等了一整天，Scrooge 根本没来。

**读者视角提示**：作者用「同样不诚实的调子」把上尉和 Fell 太太捆在一起。

> **原句 2:** “Oh no doubt. No doubt. We can only hope it’s nothing serious. I’m sure he will be here tomorrow. Perhaps even in the morning.” They were sitting in the dining room together with the remnants of their supper spread out on the table before them. The scene looked like one of happy domesticity. “I don’t believe,” said the lady thoughtfully, “that I expected so warm a welcome somehow.

**中文理解**：「哦，那当然。那当然。我们只能希望不是什么严重的事。我肯定他明天会来。也许明天早上。」两人坐在饭厅里，晚饭剩下的东西摊在桌上。这场景看上去像一幅家庭幸福图景。「我不觉得，」那位女士若有所思地说，「我原本会料到会得到这样热情的欢迎。」

**关键词**：Oh no doubt. No doubt / The scene looked like one of happy domesticity / I don’t believe / that I expected so warm a welcome somehow

**为什么这样写**：上尉的重复（No doubt. No doubt.）是焦虑的信号——重复即心虚。而作者用「The scene looked like one of happy domesticity」把餐桌写成一张明信片：看上去一切正常。紧接着 Fell 太太的话拆穿这份正常：她没料到会这么舒适、这么受欢迎——对一个带着鬼屋秘密来的人而言，「太舒服」本身就是可疑的。

**读者视角提示**：越舒适越不对劲。

> **原句 3:** Or for things to have been made so comfortable for us.” The captain remarked that he shared her surprise. “It’s just all so very ordinary. Pleasant, yes, but ordinary too.” “But Mrs Bethlem’s rules…” Captain Ambrose began. Supper and the time that had passed since the departure of that lady seemed to have softened Mrs Fell in her opinion of it all.

**中文理解**：「或者说，为我们把这里安排得这么舒适。」上尉说他和她一样感到意外。「这一切实在太普通了。是啊，愉快，但也就是普通。」「可是 Bethlem 太太的规矩……」Ambrose 上尉开口。晚饭以及 Bethlem 太太离开后这段时间，似乎软化了 Fell 太太对这一切的看法。

**关键词**：Or for things to have been made so comfortable for us / the captain remarked that he shared her surprise / It’s just all so very ordinary / Pleasant, yes, but ordinary too / But Mrs Bethlem’s rules / seemed to have softened Mrs Fell in her opinion of it all

**为什么这样写**：作者把「普通」这个词放在最显眼处：普通＝不合逻辑地妥帖，而妥帖本身是 Bethlem 早就布置好的伪装。而 Bethlem 名字一出现，Fell 太太立刻接上「But Mrs Bethlem's rules…」，却被叙述者插入的一句（seemed to have softened…）抢白——作者代替角色宣布：她已经软化了。

**读者视角提示**：「ordinary」在这个故事里从来不是褒义。

> **原句 4:** “I do think we should follow them,” she admitted. “Yet she has been here a very long time, in this little place, and it would hardly be unusual if all manner of queer beliefs and superstitions should spring up in her.” “These things are quite likely,” said the captain.

**中文理解**：「我确实觉得我们该遵守它们，」她承认道，「不过她在这个小地方待了很久很久，就算她心里长出各种古怪的信念和忌讳，也不算稀奇。」「这些东西很可能有，」上尉说。

**关键词**：I do think we should follow them / she admitted / she has been here a very long time, in this little place / it would hardly be unusual if all manner of queer beliefs and superstitions should spring up in her / These things are quite likely

**为什么这样写**：作者借 Fell 太太之口给了 Bethlem 太太一份同情性的辩护：孤独会催生迷信。这是全章最有人情味的一句推理，也是一个精妙的转移——把「Bethlem 知道什么」换成「Bethlem 只是个怕鬼的老太太」。上尉的「These things are quite likely」是附议，但下一句他就要问个究竟。

**读者视角提示**：这是 Bethlem 太太在本章唯一的辩护词。

> **原句 5:** “But I should like to know…” He paused then, to make sure that he did not phrase what followed as a question. “I should like to know what it was that you discussed with Mrs Bethlem on the steps outside.” “I asked her about my uncle,” the lady said simply. “For it sounded to me as though she had known him well in life.

**中文理解**：「不过我想知道……」他停了一下，确保接下来的话不被说成一个疑问句。「我想知道，你在门外台阶上究竟和 Bethlem 太太谈了什么。」「我问了她关于我舅舅的事，」那位女士平静地说，「因为听起来她生前似乎很了解他。我问过他是个什么样的人，因为我见到他时还是个孩子；我还问，那些关于他的传闻是否属实。」

**关键词**：He paused then, to make sure that he did not phrase what follows as a question / I should like to know what it was that you discussed with Mrs Bethlem on the steps outside / I asked her about my uncle / it sounded to me as though she had known him well in life / I asked if any of the rumours about him were true

**为什么这样写**：上尉的小心很反常：他要把疑问句改成陈述句才敢问——可见 Bethlem 的规矩在他心里已经生效。而 Fell 太太的回答用词讲究：她没有问「我舅舅是不是恶人」，只问「他是个什么样的人」和「传闻是否属实」。她要的不是答案，是描述。

**读者视角提示**：上尉的措辞是本章最耐人寻味的一处。

> **原句 6:** I asked what he was like, for I was just a child when I met him, and I asked if any of the rumours about him were true.” She took a sip from the glass that lay before her. “Her replies were as follows. Firstly, that she wasn’t sure if my uncle was the kind of man that anyone ever really knew at all well.

**中文理解**：她问他是个什么样的人——因为她见到他时还是个孩子；她还问，那些关于他的传闻是否属实。她端起面前杯中的酒抿了一口。「她的回答如下：第一，她不确定我舅舅是不是那种真的能被谁彻底了解的人。」

**关键词**：I asked what he was like / for I was just a child when I met him / I asked if any of the rumours about him were true / She took a sip from the glass / Her replies were as follows. Firstly / the kind of man that anyone ever really knew at all well

**为什么这样写**：作者把她的提问写得很克制：问的是「他是什么样的人」和「传闻是否属实」，而不是「他是好人吗」。而「Her replies were as follows. Firstly」这个清点口吻，暗示后面还有第二项、第三项——本章却只给了第一项。Bethlem 的证词以「Firstly」开场，读者会等一个「Secondly」直到本章结束也没等到。

**读者视角提示**：Bethlem 的证词是一份清单，本章只给了第一条。

> **原句 7:** She had never met anybody, she said, who might be said to be close to the man. But that she could only speak as she found and that my uncle had been to her a very fair – no, more than fair – a very generous employer and that he had been considerable material help to her and her husband, even after his death.

**中文理解**：「她从未遇见过任何人，」她说，「可以说与那个人亲近。但是她只能据实而言，而我的舅舅待她算是十分厚道——不，比厚道更多——他是个非常慷慨的雇主，而且在她丈夫死后仍对她与丈夫有相当大的实际帮助。」

**关键词**：She had never met anybody, she said / who might be said to be close to the man / But that she could only speak as she found / a very fair – no, more than fair – / a very generous employer / considerable material help to her and her husband, even after his death

**为什么这样写**：这段话在 Fell 太太的转述中层层套着「她说道」，读者听到的是转述的转述。而「但她只能据实而言」是 Bethlem 的免责声明——她拒绝说更多，正是这份克制让她可疑。「死后仍有实际帮助」是整段证词里唯一的硬信息：Gowing 死后仍在供养她们。

**读者视角提示**：「她只能据实而言」＝她不肯多说。

> **原句 8:** I had the strong sense that there was more that she might have said then. But she seemed so eager to depart that I let her.” “So, we’re on our own,” stated the captain. “Only until Mr Scrooge gets here.” “Yes.” Without meaning to, Ambrose made the word sound full of doubt. “Only until then.” He rose abruptly to his feet. “Now, I shall tidy this away.

**中文理解**：「我有一种强烈的感觉：她当时本来还可能说更多。但她那么急着要走，我就由她去了。」「那么，我们只能靠自己了，」上尉说。「至少在 Scrooge 先生到来之前。」「是啊。」Ambrose 不由自主地把那个「是」字说得满怀疑。「只到那时候为止。」他蓦地站起身来。「现在，我来收拾这些。

**关键词**：the strong sense that there was more that she might have said / But she seemed so eager to depart that I let her / So, we’re on our own / Only until Mr Scrooge gets here / Without meaning to, Ambrose made the word sound full of doubt / He rose abruptly to his feet

**为什么这样写**：「I let her」三个字是 Fell 太太的自我责备：她本可以追问，却让 Bethlem 走了。而上尉那句「Only until then」的疑虑，作者特别注明「Without meaning to」——他自己也不想这样怀疑，但身体先于理智说出了真心话。他「rose abruptly」——站起来的动作替代了情绪的描写。

**读者视角提示**：Fell 太太在自责，她自己知道错过了什么。

> **原句 9:** We have the girl visiting with us first thing tomorrow to keep things nice and shipshape. And it’s been a long day. So, please, might I suggest that we go to our beds and see if we can’t both of us have a good night’s sleep?” He stopped short then, for he realised that he had asked a question aloud.

**中文理解**：「我们明天一早会有那位姑娘来帮忙，把这里收拾得井井有条。而且今天很长了。所以，请容我提议：我们去睡吧，看看我们俩是不是都能睡个好觉？」他随即停住，因为他意识到自己刚才把一个问题问出了口。

**关键词**：We have the girl visiting with us first thing tomorrow to keep things nice and shipshape / And it’s been a long day / might I suggest that we go to our beds / good night’s sleep / He stopped short then / he had asked a question aloud

**为什么这样写**：这是本章最漂亮的机关：上尉为了遵守「不许提问」的规矩，一路小心地把请求写成陈述句，却在最后一刻用「might I suggest that…」的问句收尾——他自己当场愣住。作者把 Bethlem 的禁令执行得如此彻底，以至于一条无害的提议也变成了违规。

**读者视角提示**：问句的禁令已经进入了本能层面。

> **原句 10:** Mrs Fell must have realised the same, for she looked at him with an expression of distinct nervousness upon her face. After that? Nothing. Nothing at all. But only the quietness of the little town. Mrs Fell smiled at the captain, and it was a smile of tentative but growing relief. “Yes,” she said. “I quite agree. And then we can see what everything looks like in the daytime.”

**中文理解**：Fell 太太显然也意识到了这一点，因为她带着一种明显紧张的神情看着他。此后呢？什么也没有。什么也没有。只有小镇的寂静。Fell 太太朝上尉笑了笑，那笑容里有试探，却渐渐舒展开来。「好的，」她说，「我完全同意。然后我们就可以看看白天这一切是什么样子。」

**关键词**：she looked at him with an expression of distinct nervousness / After that? Nothing. Nothing at all. / But only the quietness of the little town / a smile of tentative but growing relief / I quite agree. And then we can see what everything looks like in the daytime

**为什么这样写**：作者用两个问句片段（After that? Nothing. Nothing at all.）制造停顿，像录音机被按下暂停。而「tentative but growing relief」写表情的渐变：从试探到安定——她把「我们一起去睡」当成了安全感的证据。

**读者视角提示**：这份安全感是本章最贵的一句铺垫。

> **原句 11:** Now, there is no need to trouble you with what followed. With the setting aside of the dinner and the remainder of the food. With Captain Ambrose’s careful locking of the front door, with his almost reverential hanging up of the key upon a hook which had been placed in the hallway for that purpose, and with his making sure that the house was as secure as it could possibly be.

**中文理解**：现在，没必要用接下来的事来烦扰你了。撤走晚餐与剩下的食物。上尉 Ambrose 小心地锁上前门，几乎是虔敬地把钥匙挂到门厅里为此专设的钩子上，又确保这所房子尽可能地安全。

**关键词**：there is no need to trouble you with what followed / With the setting aside of the dinner / Captain Ambrose’s careful locking of the front door / his almost reverential hanging up of the key upon a hook / making sure that the house was as secure as it could possibly be

**为什么这样写**：这段「没有必要告诉你」的清单，是叙述者第一次公然把读者推开——而它列出的全是琐碎的安保动作。「almost reverential」（近乎虔敬）形容挂钥匙的动作，把一个日常习惯写成了仪式：他在向这房子表示服从。

**读者视角提示**：虔敬地挂钥匙＝已经把自己交给了这栋房子。

> **原句 12:** With their making ready for bed, with all their nervous preparations. They said good night to one another upon the landing, standing a goodly distance apart. “I do hope that you sleep well, ma’am,” said the captain. “And I wish the same for you,” said the lady. “Thank you for all that you have done today.

**中文理解**：他们做着睡前的准备，带着各自神经紧绷的忙乱。他们在楼梯平台上互道晚安，彼此站得相当远。「我希望你睡得好，夫人，」上尉说。「我也祝你如此，」那位女士说。「谢谢你今天所做的一切。因为不管怎样，我原本也不愿意一个人来这里。」

**关键词**：With their making ready for bed, with all their nervous preparations / They said good night to one another upon the landing / standing a goodly distance apart / I do hope that you sleep well, ma’am / Thank you for all that you have done today / I should not have wished to have come here alone

**为什么这样写**：「standing a goodly distance apart」——睡前道晚安却隔着一段距离，这个空间细节比任何心理描写都更能说明二人的不安。而 Fell 太太那句「我本不愿意一个人来」是全章最坦诚的示弱：她需要他。

**读者视角提示**：他们之间的距离是心理距离的度量。

> **原句 13:** For, in spite of it all, I should not have wished to have come here alone.” “You’re very welcome, ma’am. Very welcome indeed.” They exchanged a small smile. Then they said good night once again and they went to their separate rooms. Both bore with them a candle.

**中文理解**：「不敢当，夫人。非常不敢当。」两人交换了一个小小的微笑。随后他们又道了一次晚安，各自回到自己的房间。两人都带着一支蜡烛。

**关键词**：You’re very welcome, ma’am. Very welcome indeed. / They exchanged a small smile / they said good night once again / they went to their separate rooms / Both bore with them a candle

**为什么这样写**：作者连用两次「good night」，写出一个多余的、几乎不肯散去的告别。而「Both bore with them a candle」用复数起句把两人捆成一体——同一个动作、同一样东西，说明他们此刻是同盟，也是同谋。

**读者视角提示**：两支蜡烛，两间房，同一栋楼。

> **原句 14:** Both looked rather nervously about their chambers, but both also discovered that there was nothing at all to be worried about, even as the candlelight threw capering shadows upon the walls. Both discovered that the beds which Mrs Bethlem had made up for them were comfortable indeed. And, to their surprise, both of them found themselves drifting off very easily and lightly to sleep.

**中文理解**：两人都有些紧张地打量自己的房间，却也都发现根本没有什么可担心的——即便烛光在墙上投下跳跃的影子。两人还发现，Mrs Bethlem 为他们铺好的床确实舒适。而且，出乎他们意料，两人都很快就、轻轻松松地睡了过去。

**关键词**：Both looked rather nervously about their chambers / there was nothing at all to be worried about / the candlelight threw capering shadows upon the walls / the beds which Mrs Bethlem had made up for them were comfortable indeed / they found themselves drifting off very easily and lightly to sleep

**为什么这样写**：作者在这里密集使用「Both… both… both…」，像三连拍，构造出一种整齐的、无恙的、几乎过于完美的睡前画面。而「capering shadows」（跳跃嬉闹的影子）是唯一的异样笔触——读者在文本里看到了不安，人物却没有。

**读者视角提示**：影子在跳，墙上却“没什么好担心”。

> **原句 15:** By now it was around ten o’clock at night. And for the following four hours Captain Ambrose and Mrs Louisa Fell slept well and deeply in their beds. This was to be the last time that they would do so in that house.

**中文理解**：此时大约已是夜里十点。在接下来的四个小时里，Ambrose 上尉与 Louisa Fell 太太在各自的床上睡得又香又沉。这将是他们在那栋房子里最后一次这样安睡。

**关键词**：By now it was around ten o’clock at night / for the following four hours / slept well and deeply in their beds / This was to be the last time that they would do so in that house

**为什么这样写**：作者把时间钉在十点——正是 Bethlem 规定的「十点后不开门」的起点；四小时的睡眠，是规则生效的窗口期。而末句以否定式的将来（would do so）宣告：他们睡得越好，损失越大。作者在读者最安心的一刻收回安全感。

**读者视角提示**：这四小时正是那栋房子开始工作的时段。

## 一句话总结

本章的舞台是 Starningham 那栋老宅的饭厅：Fell 太太以「with a kind of forced lightness」说「I don’t believe that Mr Scrooge can be joining us tonight after all」，上尉用「an approximation of the same, dishonest tone」附和，女士补「Perhaps something has delayed him」，上尉连说「Oh no doubt. No doubt.」；残羹在桌上，「The scene looked like one of happy domesticity」，可她觉得「I don’t believe… that I expected so warm a welcome somehow」，一切「Pleasant, yes, but ordinary too」；说到 Bethlem 的规矩，她已被晚餐软化：「I do think we should follow them」，只替对方开脱——独居太久，「all manner of queer beliefs and superstitions should spring up in her」；上尉想问 Bethlem 在台阶上与她谈了什么，先「paused then, to make sure that he did not phrase what followed as a question」；她答：问的是舅舅，「it sounded to me as though she had known him well in life」，还问了传闻是否属实；Bethlem 的转述是：舅舅不是能被真正了解的人，她从未与任何「close to the man」的人相识，「she could only speak as she found」，但待她「a very fair – no, more than fair – a very generous employer」，死后仍「considerable material help」；Fell 太太承认「the strong sense that there was more that she might have said then. But she seemed so eager to depart that I let her.」于是上尉说「So, we’re on our own」「Only until Mr Scrooge gets here」，而他「Without meaning to, Ambrose made the word sound full of doubt. ‘Only until then.’」他提议就寝，却「stopped short then, for he realised that he had asked a question aloud」，Fell 太太脸上显出「distinct nervousness」；此后只有小镇的寂静，她露出「tentative but growing relief」的笑；作者列出一串琐事清单（撤走晚餐、锁门、「almost reverential hanging up of the key」、确保安全），两人在平台上「standing a goodly distance apart」道晚安，她说「I should not have wished to have come here alone」，他答「Very welcome, ma'am. Very welcome indeed.」；各自持烛回房，紧张张望却「there was nothing at all to be worried about」，床舒适，「drifting off very easily and lightly to sleep」；时间锁定：「By now it was around ten o'clock at night」，接下来的四个小时两人「slept well and deeply in their beds」——而末句是判决：「This was to be the last time that they would do so in that house.」
