# 13. XIII

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：At precise moment Archie Beck reveals name of first communicant, Captain Ambrose steps with care and trepidation through graveyard; Edwin Cadge reveals father found in churchyard of Chapel of St Peter and St Simeon the Wise, "That is where he is to be found every night now"; Ambrose finds Cadge sitting beside gravestone of Harriet Cadge (1799–1847, "She waits in eternity"); Cadge rises, "mercy for her in the end"; Ambrose reveals sent by Scrooge; Cadge says "Mr Scrooge's creature?"; Ambrose asks about will of Herbert Gowing, beneficiary Louisa Fell; Cadge agrees to go to The Jolly Boaters.

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 séance（Beck's Marley contact）过渡到 Ambrose's investigation（graveyard, Cadge's grief），把 narrative 从 spiritualist performance 转回 investigation；Cadge's grief 与 willingness to talk sets up Gowing will revelation。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：graveyard trope 兑现（dismal place, death and shadows, damp air）；grieving husband trope 兑现（Cadge at wife's grave, tattered blanket, disrepair）；informant trope 兑现（Cadge agrees to talk at Jolly Boaters）。

**人物弧线**：Ambrose——从 military discipline 到 gentle persuasion ("I've met your boy"), 显示 his growing empathy；Cadge——从 grieving widower 到 reluctant informant, 显示 his shame and need for help；Edwin——从 nervous clerk 到 forlorn informant, 显示 his family's hidden grief。

**叙事手法**：third-person close on Ambrose; parallel timing (Beck's revelation vs Ambrose's graveyard); sensory gothic (damp air, dew, rainwater, shadows); dialogue-driven investigation; chapter ends with Cadge's agreement to Jolly Boaters (cliffhanger)。
