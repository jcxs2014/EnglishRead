# 08. VIII

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Mrs Fell 与 Scrooge/Ambrose 在 Scrooge's chamber 会面；Scrooge 透露 Giles 信息——Brightwell Road house "unquiet"，resident ghost；Archibald Beck（psychic medium）曾为 Gowing's paying guest；Scrooge 要出席 Beck 的 séance（9pm）；Mrs Fell 准备 port wine 后讲述 Brightwell Road 经历。

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 Mrs Fell 的 vulnerability（nightmare）过渡到 information exchange（Giles's intelligence），把 narrative 从 gothic encounter 转回 investigation；Scrooge 的 "positively beaming" 引入 new plot thread（Beck séance）。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：gothic medium trope 兑现（Archibald Beck as "most lauded and successful psychic medium"）；haunted house trope 兑现（"unquiet" house, "resident" ghost）；port wine as courage trope 兑现（Mrs Fell's "small glass of port wine"）。

**人物弧线**：Scrooge——从 comforting smile 到 "positively beaming"，显示 his intellectual excitement；Mrs Fell——从 vulnerable dreamer 到 composed narrator，显示 her resilience；Ambrose——confidential ally to Mrs Fell，leaps to aid with port。

**叙事手法**：third-person close on Mrs Fell；dialogue-driven information exchange；rain as atmosphere（"pattering insistently"、"fingertips drumming"）；chapter ends with Mrs Fell's promise to tell everything。
