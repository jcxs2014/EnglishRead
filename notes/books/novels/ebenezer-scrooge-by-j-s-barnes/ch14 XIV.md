# 14. XIV

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：The silence in séance room after Beck spoke name of first communicant was not so very far removed from silence of graveyard; Beck wore smile of quiet triumph; Scrooge looked pale and drawn; Beck asked "Scrooge? Is name of Marley known to you?"; Scrooge said "It was... A long time ago in life. And once – just once – afterwards, in death."; Beck contorted, "He says he's proud"; Scrooge gave Louisa look of thin-lipped scepticism "That hardly sounds like Jacob"; Beck said "Marley has for you a warning"; Scrooge asked querulously; Beck refused "I won't besmirch memory"; then extraordinary incident: Beck slammed against table, blood from nose, mannequin mouth, "Ebenezer... pay me heed"; Scrooge "Jacob?"; voice said "Do not go to house on Brightwell Road. The place is a trap, Ebenezer, set and baited."; Beck fell headfirst; wind sensation of departure; Beck sat up "But what has happened?"; no one could answer.

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 séance（Beck's Marley contact）过渡到 possession（Marley speaks through Beck），把 narrative 从 spiritualist performance 转向 genuine supernatural event；Marley's warning "Do not go to house on Brightwell Road" 设置 next plot thread。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：séance trope 兑现（medium, circle, spirit contact）；possession trope 兑现（Beck slammed, mannequin mouth, different voice）；ghost-warning trope 兑现（Marley's "The place is a trap"）。

**人物弧线**：Scrooge——从 pale and drawn 到 scepticism 到 astonishment, 显示 his hidden fear of Marley; Beck——从 quiet triumph 到 recalcitrant refusal 到 possessed vessel, 显示 his loss of control; Mrs Fell——从 watching smile 到 uncertainty, 显示 her growing concern。

**叙事手法**：third-person close on Scrooge and Beck; theatrical séance description; possession scene as climax; chapter ends with Beck's bewildered "But what has happened?" (no answer)。

第十四章：The silence in séance room after Beck spoke name of first communicant was not so very far removed from silence of graveyard; Beck wore smile of quiet triumph; Scrooge looked pale and drawn; Beck asked "Scrooge? Is name of Marley known to you?"; Scrooge said "It was... A long time ago in life. And once – just once – afterwards, in death."; Beck contorted, "He says he's proud"; Scrooge gave Louisa look of thin-lipped scepticism "That hardly sounds like Jacob"; Beck said "Marley has for you a warning"; Scrooge asked querulously; Beck refused "I won't besmirch memory"; then extraordinary incident: Beck slammed against table, blood from nose, mannequin mouth, "Ebenezer... pay me heed"; Scrooge "Jacob?"; voice said "Do not go to house on Brightwell Road. The place is a trap, Ebenezer, set and baited."; Beck fell headfirst; wind sensation of departure; Beck sat up "But what has happened?"; no one could answer.本章省略的细节（Beck's "He has watched you", "He has seen much", Talbot's interruption, Beck's "The connection is but a thin thread", "I won't besmirch", convulsive cry, flung arms, sleeper awakened）均在读者视角提示中提及。
