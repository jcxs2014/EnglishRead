# 12. XII

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Back in darkened drawing room, Archie Beck claps hands, says "Let us begin", takes place at table; circle holds hands; Beck says "Let us see who will be first to speak"; Mrs Seabrook asks "Is it my sister?"; Beck says "No, not Emmeline"; Beck asks "Is there a Mr Scrooge present?"; Scrooge says "I am Scrooge, though I do not recognise that description any longer"; Beck says "language is not of my choosing"; Scrooge asks "what is the name of this dead person?"; Beck says "His name is Jacob Marley."

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 séance（Beck's spiritualist performance）过渡到 Marley's return（Jacob Marley contact），把 narrative 从 Ambrose's investigation（ch11）转回 Scrooge's haunting；Marley's name as cliffhanger。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：séance trope 兑现（hand-holding, circle, spirit guide）；ghost-return trope 兑现（Marley's name）；spiritualist-as-fraud trope 兑现（Beck's theatrical performance, plausibly distracted）。

**叙事手法**：third-person close on Beck and Scrooge; theatrical séance description; dialogue-driven contact; chapter ends with Marley's name（cliffhanger）。
