# 10. X

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Scrooge 与 Mrs Fell 乘 hansom cab 至 Kensington Shepherd's Gardens 的 Talbot 宅邸，出席 Beck 的 séance；Scrooge 途中询问 Mrs Fell 是否曾出席 séance、是否相信；butler Mr Barwood 透露其父亲病重，Scrooge 给予金钱；Talbot 热情迎接；Beck 抵达——高大、健壮、"half-officer and half-infantry"。

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 journey（cab, rain stopped）过渡到 séance 前的 social gathering，把 narrative 从 investigation 转向 gothic performance；Beck 的 arrival 为下一章的 séance 做 setup。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：séance trope 兑现（circular table, dimmed lights）；medium as celebrity trope 兑现（Beck "captain of the winning team"）；butler-as-informant trope 兑现（Barwood's father）。

**人物弧线**：Scrooge——从 cautious escort 到 generous benefactor（Barwood's father），显示 his hidden kindness；Mrs Fell——从 determined participant 到 skeptical observer（"not credulous of all claims"）；Beck——introduced as physical, charismatic medium, contrasting with Giles's disembodied madness。

**叙事手法**：third-person with narrator intrusion（"No doubt you have attended séances yourself"）；sensory（rain stopped, dark wet unwelcoming, gas-lit wealth）；physical description of Beck as gothic anti-Giles。
