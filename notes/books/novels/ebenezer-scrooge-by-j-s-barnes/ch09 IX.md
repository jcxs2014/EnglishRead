# 09. IX

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Mrs Fell 向 Scrooge 与 Ambrose 讲述 1832 年 September 与父亲赴 Starningham Brightwell Road 25号 Uncle Herbert Gowing 家的童年经历：house "unquiet"、hallway "wrongness"、first night leaves in bed、second night voices shouting、young woman's voice calling "Louisa"、living "patch of darkness" attacking；Scrooge 提出三步方案；Mrs Fell 决定陪同 Scrooge 出席 séance。

**叙事手法**：first-person testimony framed by third-person narration；sensory gothic；chapter ends with Mrs Fell's defiance。
