# 05. V

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Mrs Fell 被 maid Charlotte 送入 guest chamber；Charlotte 透露 Scrooge 曾是 "byword for meanness" 但 "done a lot to help folks"；Mrs Fell 询问 Scrooge 是否 good man，Charlotte 说 "plenty who tell you he wasn't always so"；Mrs Fell drifted to window，sensed the life growing within her，fell asleep；dreamed of Brightwell Road house，turned pale and writhed；narrator 用 "We must leave her there awhile longer" 压缩；Scrooge 与 Ambrose 步行去 visit Giles，Ambrose 不情愿（"odd man... queer company... something broken in him"）；Scrooge 坚持 "see him we must"；jostled by swells；雨势渐强。

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 Mrs Fell 的 private moment（与 Charlotte 的对话、window-gazing、sleep、dream）过渡到 Scrooge/Ambrose 的 quest（visit Giles），把 narrative 从 guest chamber 的 intimacy 推向 street 的 adventure；dream sequence 是 supernatural foreshadowing。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：haunted house dream trope 兑现（Mrs Fell dreamed of Brightwell Road house，turned pale and writhed）；"servant as chorus" trope（Charlotte 揭露 Scrooge's past）；"odd specialist" trope（Giles 作为 knowledge source）。

**人物弧线**：Mrs Fell——从 anxiety 到 rest to nightmare，显示 her fear 的 subconscious activation；Charlotte——servant chorus，揭露 Scrooge's past 与 present contrast；Scrooge——从 promise 到 action（visit Giles），显示 his mission's urgency；Ambrose——reluctant companion，skepticism 与 loyalty 并存。

**叙事手法**：third-person with narrator intrusion（"We must leave her there awhile longer"）；dream sequence as supernatural foreshadowing；parallel window-gazing（Mrs Fell upstairs, Ambrose downstairs in ch04）；weather as mood（"day was growing gloomier... rain intensified"）。
