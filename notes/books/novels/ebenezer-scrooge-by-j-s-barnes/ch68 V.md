# 68. V

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：顶楼对话继续。Scrooge 逼近那年轻女子——「doing so gingerly, as one might approach a tiger behind bars at the zoo」——追问她用房子与那份古怪遗嘱设下陷阱、是为了夺取某个活人的身体；镜下那人笑答「You are. And you ask more questions than have been asked for many long years in this house.」，Scrooge 回敬「Mrs Fell might surprise you yet」，对方斥他「more sentimental than I was expecting」，他答「I used to think the same… No longer.」，对方骂「Then you’re a fool.」，他轻描淡写地说「You’re not the first to have said so」并转入正题：这地方被打开了一扇通往彼岸的门，「an awful moral taint has crept through the whole of this house」，「Mrs Fell, she saw it as a child」，「none escaped unchanged」，「Mr Beck」亦未能幸免；那东西承认 Beck「was stronger」但「still sufficient for my purposes」，Scrooge 反问「do I not stand here before you?」，对方答「You had help. … Help from some… other source.」，他则说「I have learned the value of friends.」；末了那东西说「Your adventures have made you provincial, Ebenezer.」并自夸「The scope and scale of my achievement!」，煤气灯忽然窜亮、女孩身后浮起一个「something like a man」的暗影又沉下去；Scrooge 厉声道「Tell me what you are, sir! … Tell me why you wanted your niece to have this house!」，继而以圣名下令「In the name of Lord Jesus Christ, tell me what all of this has been for!」——「the dead thing – Mr Gowing – told him everything」。

**情感弧线位置**：弧线为「审问—互相讥刺—揭底—对方的骄傲—受挫—圣名压服—全盘托出」。本章是正面对峙的高潮前半段：Scrooge 从被质询者变成发问者，最后以信仰语言取得审问权。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现「附身者自陈身份」与「以真名压制邪祟」两个母题。反转有三处：其一，Gowing 自称 Scrooge「made you provincial」——它把他的善行视为小打小闹；其二，那道暗影「something like a man」升起又落下，说明它连自己的形体都撑不住；其三，最终的屈服不是被打败，而是「told him everything」——作者干脆把揭秘交给对手自己说。

**人物弧线**：Scrooge 在本章完成身份总汇：他说「I have learned the value of friends.」，却又在命令里用了「the name of Lord Jesus Christ」——前者是人间学到的，后者是他骨子里带来的；这两句并置，划出他这个人物的两条根。

**叙事手法**：一是**对话推进＋旁白评点交替**，Scrooge 的评点随时校正台词；二是**旧章回声**（黑狗、Mr Beck、Mrs Fell 的童年）；三是**以灯与影替代解释**——暗影一起一伏，不作任何说明；四是以一句极短的收尾完成揭秘的转场。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** I’m right, then?” said Mr Scrooge, stepping a little closer to the young woman, but doing so gingerly, as one might approach a tiger behind bars at the zoo. “Your intention with this house – and with that most peculiar will – was to create a trap? One which would allow you to take possession of a certain living body?”

**中文理解**：「我猜得没错，」Scrooge 说着，朝那年轻女子走近了一些，但走得很谨慎，像在动物园里靠近隔着铁栏的老虎。「你打这所房子——和那份最古怪的遗嘱——的主意，是为了设一个陷阱？一个能让你占据某个活人身体的陷阱？」

**关键词**：stepping a little closer to the young woman / doing so gingerly, as one might approach a tiger behind bars / Your intention with this house / to create a trap? / take possession of a certain living body

**为什么这样写**：作者用动物园铁栏的比喻给出一幅「危险却被展示」的画面，把顶楼写成展笼；而 Scrooge 的靠近是「谨慎」的——他已认出对手，却不敢轻举妄动。问句以「我猜得没错」开头，显示他一直占着上风。

**读者视角提示**：他像靠近笼中老虎那样走近。

> **原句 2:** There came a chuckle from the person who stood beneath the mirror. “You are. And you ask more questions than have been asked for many long years in this house.” “I rather think,” said Scrooge, “that Mrs Fell might surprise you yet.”

**中文理解**：镜子底下那人发出一声窃笑。「你说对了。而且你问的问题，比这所房子里许多许多年都没人问过。」「我倒觉得，」Scrooge 说，「Fell 太太也许还会让你吃惊。」

**关键词**：a chuckle from the person who stood beneath the mirror / you ask more questions than have been asked for many long years in this house / I rather think / Mrs Fell might surprise you yet

**为什么这样写**：作者让对手先确认「你说对了」——他并不否认陷阱，反而享受被识破；随后用「许多许多年没人问过」把 Scrooge 的提问框成一桩久违的盛事，暗示这里的规则一向是只许服从。Scrooge 随即把赌注押在一个「已经被占据」的人身上，表面自相矛盾，读者要等下一章才明白他的理由。

**读者视角提示**：它对被识破毫不惊慌；他押宝在 Fell 太太身上。

> **原句 3:** “Then you are more sentimental than I was expecting. It is a great weakness in one of your profession.” “I used to think the same,” said Scrooge, adding, with a quick flash of a smile: “No longer.”

**中文理解**：「那你就比我料想的更感情用事。这是你这行当的一大弱点。」「我从前也这么想，」Scrooge 说，闪念之间露出一个笑容，「现在不了。」

**关键词**：Then you are more sentimental than I was expecting / It is a great weakness in one of your profession / I used to think the same / with a quick flash of a smile / No longer

**为什么这样写**：对手的反驳立刻切中职业判断：驱鬼人不该用感情，措辞里带着居高临下的轻蔑。而「现在不了」四个字是全书人物弧线的浓缩：从前与此刻的 Scrooge 用同一句话完成对照；中间那「一闪而过的笑」则暴露他并没有十足把握——他只是在气势上占先。

**读者视角提示**：它笑他感情用事；他承认自己变了。

> **原句 4:** “Then you’re a fool.” “You’re not the first to have said so,” Scrooge said airily. “But we should get to business. It seems plain to me that you somehow succeeded in opening a door in this place to one of those realms which lie beyond. And that an awful moral taint has crept through the whole of this house. Mrs Fell, she saw it as a child. But all who have lived here have sensed it and none escaped unchanged. Mr Beck, for instance.”

**中文理解**：「那你就是个傻瓜。」「你不是第一个这么说我的人，」Scrooge 轻快地说，「不过我们该谈正事了。依我看很清楚，你设法在这地方打开了一扇通往那些彼岸领域的门。而一种可怕的精神污秽已经渗入这整所房子。Fell 太太小时候就看见了它。但所有住过这里的人都感觉到它，没有一个能全身而退。比如贝克先生。」

**关键词**：Then you’re a fool / You’re not the first to have said so / But we should get to business / opening a door in this place to one of those realms which lie beyond / an awful moral taint has crept through the whole of this house / none escaped unchanged / Mr Beck

**为什么这样写**：作者用「你不是第一个这么说的」一句化解侮辱——这是全书里他最成熟的表现：他不辩解、不动气，直接越过人身攻击转入议题。随后用「门」这一意象把鬼屋传统与此处接上，又用「道德污秽」把超自然与伦理绑在一起：这不是闹鬼，是败坏。末句点名 Mr Beck，把旧章碎片一次缝回主线。

**读者视角提示**：他不接羞辱，直接谈正事，并点名 Beck。

> **原句 5:** “He was stronger,” the girl – Susan Naylor – admitted, “than I had expected. Yet he was still sufficient for my purposes.” “Do you know,” said Scrooge, “I’m not so sure that he was. For do I not stand here before you? In spite of all your efforts to keep me from Starningham?”

**中文理解**：「他比我料想的更强，」那女孩——苏珊·奈勒——承认道，「但仍然足够我使用。」「你知道吗，」Scrooge 说，「我可不那么确定。我此刻不是正站在你面前吗？尽管你费尽心机不让我来到斯塔宁厄姆。」

**关键词**：the girl – Susan Naylor – admitted / He was stronger / yet he was still sufficient for my purposes / I’m not so sure that he was / do I not stand here before you? / your efforts to keep me from Starningham

**为什么这样写**：作者在正文中直接点出容器身份是 Susan Naylor，并让对手用「足够我使用」这种器物语言评价一个活人——既显其残酷，也透出其自大。Scrooge 随后以存在本身为论据反问：既然 Beck 强到能撑住计划，为什么我还站在这里？答案留到下一章。

**读者视角提示**：容器是 Susan Naylor；他以「我站在这里」为论据。

> **原句 6:** “You had help,” said the creature with the girl’s voice. “That is all. Help from some… other source.” “I have learned the value of friends,” Scrooge said.

**中文理解**：「你有人相助，」那用着女孩声音的东西说，「仅此而已。某种……别的来源的帮助。」「我明白了朋友的价值，」Scrooge 说。

**关键词**：the creature with the girl’s voice / That is all / Help from some… other source / I have learned the value of friends

**为什么这样写**：作者让对手承认失败却归因于外力——「某种别的来源」，并配上省略号与迟疑。这一笔把 Scrooge 的胜利削掉一半，也把 Marley、朋友、旧日善行这些线索在暗处接上。而他随后那一句六词的对白承担了人物转变的宣言功能。

**读者视角提示**：它承认他有人相助；他确认了朋友的价值。

> **原句 7:** “The conversation grows tedious. Such a shame, for I have been looking forward to it. I have heard your name, Ebenezer, in the furthest reaches of the underworld. There are some who are afraid of you there. And there are others who feel that they have been cheated out of a soul. All that sin, and then the slate wiped clean after just a night of visitations.”

**中文理解**：「这谈话越来越乏味。真可惜，我本来很盼着它的。我听过你的名字，老埃比尼泽，在冥界最偏远的地方。那里有些人是怕你的。还有些人则觉得，他们被人骗走了一个灵魂。那么多罪孽，然后只消一夜造访就被抹得干干净净。」

**关键词**：The conversation grows tedious / I have heard your name, Ebenezer, in the furthest reaches of the underworld / There are some who are afraid of you there / they have been cheated out of a soul / the slate wiped clean after just a night of visitations

**为什么这样写**：作者让对手从私人层面升级到宇宙层面：它的势力直达冥界最远之处，并把 Scrooge 的救赎说成「一了百了」——罪孽与抹除之间只隔一夜。这是全书对「圣诞一夜改命」最锋利的质疑。

**读者视角提示**：它质疑他救赎的分量。

> **原句 8:** “No, no. You are misinformed. No slate has been wiped clean for me. Rather, I work every day to erase the mistakes of my past. And to help others from falling into that same abyss.” “All a great pity,” said the maidservant in a voice that was closer to a growl. “Your adventures have made you provincial, Ebenezer. For you still do not appreciate what I am. The scope and scale of my achievement!”

**中文理解**：「不，不。你搞错了。我这块石板从来没有被抹干净过。相反，我每天都在擦去我过去的错误。也在帮助别人不跌进同一个深渊。」「真是大可惋惜，」那女佣以更接近低吼的声音说，「你的冒险让你变得 provincial 了，老埃比尼泽。你仍未看清我是什么。我这番成就的规模与分量！」

**关键词**：No slate has been wiped clean for me / I work every day to erase the mistakes of my past / to help others from falling into that same abyss / in a voice that was closer to a growl / Your adventures have made you provincial / The scope and scale of my achievement

**为什么这样写**：作者用「每天都在」把一次性的奇迹改写成持续的劳动——这是本书最重要的伦理主张：救赎不是一次抵免，而是持续的擦除与扶助。而对方的反驳也以咆哮代替窃笑，语调骤变，并用「provincial」这一个大词把 Scrooge 毕生的功德贬为地方性小成就。

**读者视角提示**：他说救赎是每天的持续工作；它咆哮着贬低他的成就。

> **原句 9:** Inexplicably, the gas lamp flared at these words, suddenly illuminating the room. Behind the girl, there seemed to rise a darker shadow, something like a man. Then it sank back down again behind her. “Then tell me!” said Scrooge. “Tell me what you are, sir! And tell me what it is that has been planning all this long while from beyond the grave! Tell me how you knew Captain Fell! Tell me why you wanted your niece to have this house!”

**中文理解**：奇怪的是，煤气灯在这几句话上窜起火焰，忽然把整间房照亮。在女孩身后，似乎升起一个更浓的暗影，形状像个男人。随后它又沉了回去，藏在她身后。「那你告诉我！」Scrooge 说，「告诉我你是什么，先生！告诉我这一切在坟墓那边谋划了这么久究竟是什么！告诉我你怎么认识 Fell 上尉的！告诉我你为什么想要你外甥女继承这所房子！」

**关键词**：the gas lamp flared at these words / suddenly illuminating the room / a darker shadow, something like a man / Then it sank back down again behind her / Tell me what you are, sir! / what has been planning all this long while from beyond the grave / why you wanted your niece to have this house

**为什么这样写**：作者用煤气灯的物理反应为它的自夸作注——话说到自高自大处，灯就窜亮；随后那「像个人形」的暗影升起又落下，不作任何解释，恐怖感全在形状的失败上。而 Scrooge 连珠炮式的四问每一问都比前一问更私密，最后一问把谜面从怪谈推到家族。

**读者视角提示**：灯窜亮，女孩身后浮起人形暗影；他连问四问，最后问到外甥女。

> **原句 10:** At this, the thing just giggled to itself, though Scrooge thought that he detected in the timbre of the laughter a hint of disquiet, as though he had not quite prepared himself for the force of the ghost hunter’s conviction, nor for those forces which stood at his back.

**中文理解**：这时，那东西只是自顾自地咯咯笑，但 Scrooge 觉得，他在笑声的音色里听出了一丝不安，仿佛它并未完全准备好承受这位幽灵猎手的笃信，也承受不了站在他背后的那些力量。

**关键词**：the thing just giggled to itself / he detected in the timbre of the laughter a hint of disquiet / he had not quite prepared himself for the force of the ghost hunter’s conviction / nor for those forces which stood at his back

**为什么这样写**：作者只让 Scrooge「觉得」，不证实不安；而「站在他背后的那些力量」一句话把前文的 Marley、朋友、信念一次收拢——读者与它同时意识到，对手是有靠山的。

**读者视角提示**：笑声里有一丝不安。

> **原句 11:** When Scrooge spoke again, it was as he had to the black dog on the road outside, and his words emerged with the force not of a request but of holy command. “In the name of Lord Jesus Christ, tell me what all of this has been for!” And, after that, in the space of a few short minutes which were to haunt Ebenezer Scrooge for the rest of his days, the dead thing – Mr Gowing – told him everything.

**中文理解**：当 Scrooge 再开口时，他的语气与先前对门外那条黑狗说话时一样，字句带着的不是请求而是神圣的命令：「以主耶稣基督之名，告诉我这一切是为了什么！」而在那之后——在那短短几分钟里，它们将以余生的全部日子纠缠 Ebenezer Scrooge——那个死物——戈文先生——把一切都告诉了他。

**关键词**：it was as he had to the black dog on the road outside / the force not of a request but of holy command / In the name of Lord Jesus Christ / in the space of a few short minutes which were to haunt Ebenezer Scrooge / the dead thing – Mr Gowing – told him everything

**为什么这样写**：作者让读者看到 Scrooge 的两套手法是同一套——对黑狗与对鬼都用命令与圣名，区别只在对象；回指黑狗一句把此前的伏笔接成本章的武器，也显出他一路走来的积累。而结尾把「讲述全部真相」压缩进几分钟，又提前声明那几分钟会困扰他余生——信息量与消化量之间的落差，正是接下来几十章的引擎。

**读者视角提示**：他以圣名下令，如对黑狗时一样；几分钟后 Gowing 全盘托出。
