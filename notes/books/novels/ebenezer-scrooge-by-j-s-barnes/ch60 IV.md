# 60. IV

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：接第 57、58 章——Scrooge 在 Starningham 滩上被 Marley 牵进雾中，本章幻视先让他以「the naturalist inspecting a new specimen」的目光细看那具尸体，问出 Gowing 的死法，Marley 答「by drowning. Though he did so upon dry land with no drop of water present」，并留下「All will become plain」「Hush now, Ebenezer, and pay good heed」；幻视随即回退到 The Jolly Boaters 酒馆包间，律师 Cadge 颤着问遗嘱与事务的「some urgency」，Gowing 冷冷回一句「You’re very kind」，小舌舔干唇「like a vole returning to its burrow」，随即说「I am about to make a deal. A bargain, if you will」；Cadge 只往钱上猜（「Some matter of finance?」），Gowing 答「My business is knowledge and power and…」「Blood」；Cadge 仓皇喝干酒、撞倒椅子逃走，被一句「Do cherish your wife, won’t you?… While you can.」钉住；另一头挪来一个「bruised good looks and a swagger」的年轻人，Gowing 点名「Captain Fell」，说「Very nearly… it only remains for you to do your part」；Scrooge 认出「pieces have been coming together」，Marley 笑说「You are but a step away」；幻视随即像水流过玻璃一般褪尽；Scrooge 独自醒在 Starningham 沙上，Marley 已无踪影，风里传来 carol singers——素来提振心情，此刻却「a rather eerie, ominous thing」；他整衣向小城走去，知道自己「close to a terrible but necessary confrontation」。

**情感弧线位置**：弧线为「冷眼审视—追问死因—旁观谈判—目睹惊走—拼图成形—幻视褪去—孤身赴镇」。前半段是旁观者的震惊与寒意，后半段收束成一种被迫承担的行动感；Scrooge 从「看标本」走到「整衣赴镇」，中间只隔着一次幻视的褪色。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现「死者引路」与「幻视重演」两重母题，但反转在于幻视的内容不是给他答案，而是给他拼图的边角——Gowing 的「Blood」与 Fell 的名字都在，Marley 却始终不肯开口（Marley said nothing），只留一句「All will become plain」。另一处反转在结尾的圣诞颂歌：素来温暖的 carol singers 在此夜被作者写成「a rather eerie, ominous thing」，熟悉之物被染上不祥。

**人物弧线**：Gowing 在这一段完成正面亮相——他不怕、不急、也不否认，他说自己身体无恙、要活很多年，同时准备好了「for all contingencies」。Cadge 则被写成他自己的方法论的囚徒：只信「precedent and recorded fact and hard, unyielding numbers」，所以他读不懂 Gowing 的生意，只能以「business deal」去猜。而 Scrooge 的变化在于他不再只当旁观者：他开始把碎片拼起来（The pieces have been coming together），并追问「What else must I know?」——学习与承担同时发生。

**叙事手法**：一是**以生物分类写鬼**（博物学家审视标本、粉红小舌像田鼠回洞），让恐怖落在冷观察的语域里；二是**用体态代替心理**（干唇、舔舌、不安地挪动、撞得椅子 clattering），把恐惧写在身体上；三是**对话承担全部情节**，作者几乎不写心理评注，只在每句台词后补一句冷判断（such as surely indecorous / not so very far away from panic）；四是**以褪色作转场**——颜色像溪流沿玻璃流下、all washed away，随后海声与凉风接管叙述，把幻视与现实缝在同一套水与玻璃的比喻里。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Now that Mr Scrooge knew better who the cadaverous man had been in life, he took a closer look at him, peering at the fellow in the vision with something in his attitude of the naturalist inspecting a new specimen.

**中文理解**：既然已经知道那个形容枯槁的男人生前是谁，Scrooge 便凑近了看幻视里的他，那副态度里带着博物学家端详一件新标本的意味。

**关键词**：the cadaverous man had been in life / took a closer look at him / the naturalist inspecting a new specimen

**为什么这样写**：作者把「看清一个人」写成一件职业动作——不是辨认，而是检视，Scrooge 此刻的观看方式像分类学而不像人情。作者还先给这具身体一个定语（cadaverous，形容枯槁），再让他凑近，让恐怖由细节累积而非由形容词宣告。

**读者视角提示**：他看 Gowing 的眼神，像在看一件标本。

> **原句 2:** “Hmm. So this is Mr Gowing,” he said. “About whom we have heard so much. He has a fearsome aspect. Tell me, Jacob, how did he die? I don’t believe the lady ever told us.” “He died,” said Jacob Marley, his voice very low and very close, “by drowning. Though he did so upon dry land with no drop of water present. All will become plain,” said the spirit. “Hush now, Ebenezer, and pay good heed.”

**中文理解**：「嗯。这就是 Gowing 先生，」他说，「我们听说过他这么多。他的相貌可怕得很。告诉我，Jacob，他是怎么死的？那位女士可从没跟我们说过。」「他死了，」Jacob Marley 说，声音极低极近，「死于溺水。可他是在干燥的陆地上、眼前一滴水也没有的情况下溺死的。一切自会明白，」那灵体说。「嘘，Ebenezer，好好听着。」

**关键词**：Tell me, Jacob, how did he die / said Jacob Marley, his voice very low / dry land with no drop of water present / All will become plain / pay good heed

**为什么这样写**：作者让 Marley 用一句自相矛盾的死法把悬念拧紧：溺毙，却死在滴水不着的旱地。Marley 不作解释，只丢下「一切自会明白」，紧接着又用命令的语气（pay good heed）堵住追问——读者拿到的是谜面加一个不许追问的暗示。

**读者视角提示**：死法本身就是个矛盾，Marley 却不解释。

> **原句 3:** A silence of the awkward sort had lain between the lawyer and the magician in the snug of The Jolly Boaters, until it was broken by Mr Cadge, who remarked, after a further, nervous sip of ale: “You said in your letter that there was some urgency in the preparation of the will and in the settling of your affairs in general. I do hope… that is… I trust that all is well and that you are only a very cautious man. You seem, I have to say, very well.”

**中文理解**：在 The Jolly Boaters 的雅座里，律师与魔术师之间横着一段尴尬的沉默，直到 Cadge 抿了又抿一口麦酒、神经质地开口才打破：您在信里说遗嘱的准备和事务的料理「有些紧迫」，我盼望……也就是说，我信任一切安好，您只是格外谨慎。您看上去，实在很好。

**关键词**：A silence of the awkward sort / the snug of The Jolly Boaters / some urgency in the preparation of the will / a further, nervous sip of ale / a very cautious man

**为什么这样写**：作者先用一个专有名词把幻视的地点钉牢，再让沉默本身成为叙事事件。真正说话的是那些自我打断——「I do hope… that is… I trust」，读者从结巴里听出这位律师其实已经起了疑，只是不敢直说。

**读者视角提示**：他嘴上道贺，句子却一直在改口。

> **原句 4:** Again, Mr Gowing favoured Mr Cadge with a look of chilly disdain. “You’re very kind,” he said. His small pink tongue emerged to lick his dry lips, then retreated into his mouth, like a vole returning to its burrow. “Very kind indeed. And yes, there is nothing in the least bit wrong with me. I hope to live for many more years to come, a full, rich and satisfying life. “Yet I wish also to make preparations for all contingencies. You see, Mr Cadge, I am about to make a deal. A bargain, if you will.”

**中文理解**：Gowing 又以一种冷冷的轻蔑瞥了 Cadge 一眼。「您太客气了，」他说。那条粉红的小舌探出来舔了舔干燥的嘴唇，又缩回嘴里，像一只田鼠回到洞中。「真是太客气了。是啊，我身上没有半点不对的地方。我希望还能再活许多年，过完整、丰足而满足的一生。」「不过我也要为一切可能的情况做准备。您看，Cadge 先生，我要做一笔交易。如果您愿意，就叫它一桩买卖吧。」

**关键词**：a look of chilly disdain / His small pink tongue emerged / like a vole returning to its burrow / make preparations for all contingencies / I am about to make a deal

**为什么这样写**：作者让 Gowing 说漂亮话时配上小动作——粉红的小舌舔干唇，正是蛇的信子，而田鼠回洞的比喻把这个动作滑向地下、藏起意图。作者同时用「nothing in the least bit wrong with me」给出一句过于周全的自我否认，让读者听出这份从容是排练过的。

**读者视角提示**：他每句客气话后面都跟着一个小动作。

> **原句 5:** “Oh?” Cadge looked to Scrooge as though he were struggling (and not altogether succeeding) to make sense of the words of his client. “You must mean a business deal? Some matter of finance?” “I suppose one might call it a question of business,” said Mr Gowing, smiling to himself as if at some private joke, “though it is not quite so cut-and-dried. And there is a very great deal more at stake than mere money. A very great deal more than that.”

**中文理解**：「哦？」Cadge 望向 Scrooge，仿佛在努力（而且并不十分成功）理解客户的话。「您指的该是一笔生意上的交易？某件财务上的事？」「我想也可以说这是生意上的事，」Gowing 笑着说，像是在独享一则私下的笑话，「不过并不那么直截了当。而且押上的东西远比金钱多得多。远比那多得多。」

**关键词**：Cadge looked to Scrooge / as though he were struggling / not altogether succeeding / You must mean a business deal? / more at stake than mere money

**为什么这样写**：作者让 Cadge 在最需要听懂的时候把求助的目光投向幻视里的 Scrooge，读者因此被拉进这间包间，成了被咨询的人。Gowing 的答话先顺着对方的「business」框架点头，再补一句「远不止钱」——先让步、后收紧，正是他对付这位律师的方式。

**读者视角提示**：律师听不懂，只好回头看 Scrooge。

> **原句 6:** Mr Cadge – who always preferred to work with precedent and recorded fact and with hard, unyielding numbers – looked thoroughly bemused. “And what precisely is your business, Mr Gowing? What is the nature of it? I confess I could not discern it from any of the papers relating to your estate.” Mr Gowing’s smile grew wider and still more unfriendly even than before. “My business, Mr Cadge? My business is knowledge and power and…” He paused then, searching for the apposite phrase. “Blood,” he said finally and with an odd cheerfulness that did not in the least suit him.

**中文理解**：Cadge ——凡是只愿凭先例、凭记录在案的事实、凭坚硬而不让的数字办事——此刻彻底困惑。「那么请问，您的生意究竟是什么？它是什么性质？我坦白说，从所有与您的遗产有关的文件里都看不出。」「我的生意？」Gowing 的笑容更宽，也比先前更不友善。「我的生意是知识，是权势，还有……」他停了一下，像在寻找恰当的说法。「血，」他终于说，语气带着一种与他本人毫不相称的古怪轻快。

**关键词**：precedent and recorded fact / hard, unyielding numbers / looked thoroughly bemused / could not discern it / knowledge and power and

**为什么这样写**：作者先亮出 Cadge 的世界观（先例、实录、硬数字），再用他读不出答案这件事反衬 Gowing 的生意无法归类。那个词被拆成两次说出：先有停顿与搜寻（原文写他在寻找恰当的说法），最后才落地，而落地时那种「古怪的轻快」与词义形成刺目的反差。

**读者视角提示**：这位律师只信文件，而文件里没有答案。

> **原句 7:** Mr Cadge shifted uncomfortably in his chair, looking like a man who had waded unheedingly into waters that were far deeper than he had imagined. Too hastily, he reached for his drink and finished it at a speed that was surely indecorous. “Well, Mr Gowing,” he said. “It has been a pleasure, but now I must away. My wife is waiting for me and I should not wish to leave her by herself for too long.”

**中文理解**：Cadge 在椅子上不安地挪动，像个没留意就趟进了远超预想之深的水里的人。他太急，伸手抓起酒杯，以一种肯定不得体的速度喝干。「那么，Gowing 先生，」他说，「很荣幸，不过我得走了。我妻子在等我，我不该让她一个人待太久。」

**关键词**：shifted uncomfortably in his chair / waded unheedingly into waters / a speed that was surely indecorous / now I must away / My wife is waiting for me

**为什么这样写**：作者把律师的处境写成涉水过深，用一个比喻解释他的失态：不是被吓到，是自己走进了深水。他逃跑时找的借口是体面的（妻子在等），而作者偏偏点破那杯酒喝得「surely indecorous」——失态正在于他想维持体面。

**读者视角提示**：他逃走时还不忘给一个体面的理由。

> **原句 8:** With this, he rose in an ungainly manner to his feet, moving with such speed and force that he sent the chair clattering across the tavern floor and earned himself a few disgruntled looks from other drinkers there. He had the look on his face of one who would do almost anything to leave behind the company of the man whom he was with.

**中文理解**：说着，他以一种笨拙的姿态站起身来，又急又猛，撞得椅子哐啷一声滑过酒馆地板，也给自己招来旁侧酒客几道不满的目光。他脸上那种神情，是一个几乎愿意做任何事、只求离开眼前这个人的家伙才有的。

**关键词**：rose in an ungainly manner / sent the chair clattering across / a few disgruntled looks / would do almost anything to leave behind

**为什么这样写**：作者把市井喜剧与恐怖叠写在同一段：撞椅子、惹侧目是喜剧的质感，而「愿意做任何事只为离开」却直指恐惧。心理只以一句 look on his face 呈现——这种恐惧在一位体面律师的举止里没有别的出口。

**读者视角提示**：他撞翻了椅子，全场都看见了。

> **原句 9:** “Of course,” said Gowing, still smiling. “I understand entirely. Thank you so much for your help, Mr Cadge. And…” He raised his left hand in a gesture meant to keep the lawyer from scuttling away without a further word. “Mr Cadge?” “Yes, Mr Gowing?” said the legal man reluctantly. “Do cherish your wife, won’t you?” “Cherish her?” “Yes, Mr Cadge, sir.” The smile was now a grin and wider than ever. “While you can.”

**中文理解**：「当然，」Gowing 仍在笑，「我完全明白。非常感谢您的帮忙，Cadge 先生。而且……」他抬起左手，做了个手势，要把这个律师留下来再说一句。「Cadge 先生？」「什么事，Gowing 先生？」这位法律人勉强应道。「要好好爱惜您的妻子，好吗？」「爱惜她？」「是的，Cadge 先生。」那笑容此刻已成咧嘴，比先前更宽。「趁还来得及的时候。」

**关键词**：He raised his left hand / keep the lawyer from scuttling away / said the legal man reluctantly / Do cherish your wife, won’t you? / While you can

**为什么这样写**：作者用抬手这个动作强行制造停顿，破坏律师离场的节奏，也把读者的注意力钉在即将出口的那句叮嘱上。叮嘱偏偏用疑问句说出，表面是关心，语用上却是预告；而最后两个字「While you can」把威胁说完，力量全在省略。

**读者视角提示**：那一抬手，是不让走的意思。

> **原句 10:** Cadge said nothing more, but only gave a single look at his client with an expression in his eyes not so very far away from panic before he turned and fled from the establishment. At the moment that he did so, a man who had been drinking in another part of the inn moved discreetly over to the snug and, without asking for permission, sat himself down opposite the magician. This was a younger man, in possession of bruised good looks and a swagger. “Jacob?” Scrooge asked. “Who is this? And what is he to Gowing?”

**中文理解**：Cadge 再没多说，只朝委托人投去一眼，那眼神离恐慌不远，随后转身逃出了这间酒馆。就在他离开的当口，一个在酒馆另一头喝酒的男人悄悄挪到雅座，不打招呼，便在魔术师对面坐下。那是个年轻人，长着一副揍过的好相貌，带着一股张扬。「Jacob？」Scrooge 问，「这是谁？他与 Gowing 是什么关系？」

**关键词**：not so very far away from panic / turned and fled from the establishment / sat himself down opposite the magician / bruised good looks and a swagger / what is he to Gowing?

**为什么这样写**：作者用「一进一出」完成镜头交接：Cad 的逃出与新来者的入座紧挨着，中间一句「At the moment that he did so」把两桩动作焊在一起。而 bruised good looks 里的 bruised 一语双关，既指外貌上的瘀伤，也指被生活打过的痕迹；Scrooge 立刻追问「他与 Gowing 是什么关系」，说明他尚未认出此人。

**读者视角提示**：有人刚逃走，另一个人已经坐下了。

> **原句 11:** Marley said nothing. “How much of that,” asked Gowing to the newcomer, “did you overhear?” “Enough of it, I think,” said the younger man. “Sounds as though everything is in place.” “Very nearly, Captain Fell. Very nearly.” The smile again. “Now it only remains for you to do your part.” Scrooge whirled about to face the spirit of his old friend. “Captain Fell!” cried he. “Yes. Yes, I thought it must be him. The pieces have been coming together for a long while now. The shape of the plot grows ever more distinct in my mind.”

**中文理解**：Marley 一言不发。「你偷听到了多少？」Gowing 问新来者。「够多了，我想，」年轻人说，「听起来一切都各就各位了。」「差不多，Fell 上尉。差不多。」又是那笑容。「如今只剩你要尽你自己的那份了。」Scrooge 猛地转身，正对老友的鬼魂。「Fell 上尉！」他喊道。「对，对，我就知道一定是他。这些碎片拼了老长时间了。那桩阴谋的形状在我心里越来越分明。」

**关键词**：Marley said nothing / did you overhear? / Sounds as though everything is in place / Very nearly, Captain Fell / for you to do your part / pieces have been coming together

**为什么这样写**：作者让 Marley 的沉默与 Gowing 的点名形成对照：一个拒答，一个点破。年轻人那句「一切都各就各位」把阴谋说成事务性的进度汇报，而 Gowing 把「差不多」重复两遍，把真正的不安留在了自己心里。Scrooge 则在此真正动脑——他用 pieces 把此前零散的怪事当成拼图。

**读者视角提示**：Marley 不答；Gowing 替他把话说破。

> **原句 12:** Jacob smiled. “Well done, Ebenezer. You are but a step away.” “Go on,” said Scrooge. “What else must I know?” But it was too late. The vision began to fade, the colours of the scene running like rivulets of water down a pane of glass. In seconds all was washed away – the surroundings of The Jolly Boaters, all its customers, Mr Gowing and Captain Fell, all drained suddenly into grey.

**中文理解**：Jacob 笑了。「干得好，Ebenezer。你离那一步只有一步之遥。」「说吧，」Scrooge 说，「我还必须知道什么？」但已经太迟了。幻视开始褪色，场景里的颜色像玻璃上淌下的水珠。几秒之间一切都被冲走——The Jolly Boaters 的四壁、所有的客人、Gowing 与 Fell 上尉，统统骤然褪成灰色。

**关键词**：You are but a step away / What else must I know? / But it was too late / running like rivulets of water / all drained suddenly into grey

**为什么这样写**：Marley 的「You are but a step away」是全书最长的一次预告，作者随即用只有五个词的独立段落把它掐断。转场则交给水：颜色像溪流沿玻璃滑下，接着是 all was washed away——幻视被冲走的方式与场景里的海同源，为下一段海声的回归埋下接口。

**读者视角提示**：他刚刚想通，幻视却先一步结束了。

> **原句 13:** Cool air blew against Ebenezer’s face and, like a dreamer awaking, he became aware of his true surroundings. He stood still upon the edge of the sands of Starningham. Behind him came the sound of the sea. The mist had cleared a little, at least enough to see that he stood all alone in that place. Of Jacob Marley there was no sign at all. “Jacob!” he called out, though only once, for he expected no reply. He felt utterly certain that he was all alone.

**中文理解**：凉风拂上 Ebenezer 的脸，他像做梦人醒来那样意识到真实的所在：他静静站在 Starningham 的沙边，身后是海的声音。雾散开了一些，至少足够让他看清这地方只有他一个人。至于 Jacob Marley，则一点踪影也没有。「Jacob！」他喊了一声，只喊这一声，因为他本就不指望回应。他完全确定这地方只有自己一个人。

**关键词**：like a dreamer awaking / upon the edge of the sands / The mist had cleared a little / no sign at all / he expected no reply

**为什么这样写**：作者让凉风与海声来接替幻视，而「像做梦人醒来」这一句把整章的梦感收束到一次身体感知上。末段用最平静的语气写失落——Marley 的缺席因此更冷；Scrooge 只喊一声就作罢，因为他已经知道没有回答。

**读者视角提示**：颜色被冲走，只剩他自己站在沙上。

> **原句 14:** Looking ahead of him, he saw a few glimmering lights which seemed to speak of Starningham itself, the spire of the church, and then the noise of it drifting on the breeze, the unmistakable sound of carol singers. It was a thing which generally was known to lift his spirits, though it sounded on this occasion a rather eerie, ominous thing. Still, without hesitation, Scrooge squared his shoulders, drew his gown tight about him and began to walk towards the little town, knowing now that he was close to a terrible but necessary confrontation.

**中文理解**：望向前方，他看见几点微光，像是在说 Starningham 本镇、教堂的尖顶；接着那声音顺着风飘来——确凿无疑是颂歌者的歌声。这声音素来能提振他的心情，可这一回听起来却是一件相当阴森、不祥的东西。尽管如此，Scrooge 仍不犹豫，耸紧双肩，把长袍裹紧在身上，朝那座小城走去——他此刻知道，自己离一场可怕却必要的对峙已经很近了。

**关键词**：a few glimmering lights / the spire of the church / the unmistakable sound of carol singers / a rather eerie, ominous thing / close to a terrible but necessary confrontation

**为什么这样写**：作者让颂歌承担反讽：同一段旋律，一度是圣诞夜的安慰，今夜却成了不祥之物。他让 Scrooge 在怯意与行动之间做选择——「毫不犹豫」与「耸紧双肩」都是身体语言，而结尾「可怕却必要」这组形容词把恐惧与义务绑在一起。

**读者视角提示**：熟悉的圣诞颂歌，今夜听起来像警告。

## 一句话总结
第四卷第四段，Scrooge 以「the naturalist inspecting a new specimen」的目光细看幻视中的 Gowing，问他究竟怎么死的；Marley 压低声音答「by drowning. Though he did so upon dry land with no drop of water present」，留下「All will become plain」「Hush now, Ebenezer, and pay good heed」；幻视回退到 The Jolly Boaters 酒馆包间，Cadge 颤着问遗嘱与事务的「some urgency」，Gowing 冷冷一瞥「a look of chilly disdain」，小舌舔干唇「like a vole returning to its burrow」，随即说「I am about to make a deal. A bargain, if you will」；Cadge 只往钱上猜「Some matter of finance?」，Gowing 答「My business is knowledge and power and…」「Blood」；Cadge 像「waded unheedingly into waters that were far deeper」的人，「a speed that was surely indecorous」，撞得椅子 clattering 逃出酒馆，被「Do cherish your wife, won’t you?… While you can.」钉住，最后带着「not so very far away from panic」的眼神逃走；另一头挪来一个「bruised good looks and a swagger」的年轻人，Gowing 问「did you overhear?」，对方答「Sounds as though everything is in place」，Gowing 点头「Very nearly, Captain Fell. Very nearly.」「it only remains for you to do your part」；Scrooge 转身认出 Fell 上尉，「pieces have been coming together」，Marley 笑说「You are but a step away」，Scrooge 追问「What else must I know?」，但「But it was too late」；幻视开始褪色，颜色像玻璃上的水流一样「all was washed away」，「all drained suddenly into grey」；他像 dreamer awaking 般站在 Starningham 的沙边，「no sign at all」；前方微光指着小镇与教堂尖顶，风里传来「the unmistakable sound of carol singers」，素来提振心情的声音此刻却是「a rather eerie, ominous thing」；Scrooge 仍旧「without hesitation」，squared his shoulders、drew his gown tight，向小城走去，知道自己「close to a terrible but necessary confrontation」。
