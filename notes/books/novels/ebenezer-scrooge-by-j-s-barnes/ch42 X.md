# 42. X

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：镜头切回伦敦的 The Jolly Boaters 酒馆，时在清晨、刚过黎明。雪还在下，作者先把这地方在冬夜里的样子写足：即使隔着麦酒与烈酒带来的「pleasantly dishonest mist」，也「far from being an attractive place」；而清晨的薄光下，它是「a dreary and dilapidated building indeed」——「reeks of the night before」，带着「an air of quiet and stubborn misery」。女工 Emilia Hare 走向店门，比平日更显沮丧；她的靴子踩过水坑，「with the petulance of the child she had once been」，姿态里只有「a kind of irritated resignation」；开工前有一堆活要干，而老板 Bill Crickmore「would doubtless be of very little use indeed」帮不上什么忙。招牌在风中「swung… screeching on its hinges, the noise of it like a wounded cat」；就在她想着这些苦差时，她看见酒馆门洞里站着一个人。远处看只知是个穿深色衣服的男人，「much the worse for the wear」，同时在「slouching and swaying against the door」。她犹豫了一下——她是「a careful young woman who knew all the dangers of the streets」，身上带着一把「small but very sharp knife」以备自卫；她本已断定那多半是喝多了睡在店门口的常客，走近才发现认得此人，却「not as well, in some respects, as she might have wished」。认出之后她几乎跑过去：「Archie, what are you doing here?」Beck 用「plaintive eyes」看她——Hare 小姐心里想，那是「the eyes of a whipped cur」，或「those of a child close to tears from hunger」。他摇着头，说不出话。她说「I was worried about you… when you ran away yesterday」，看到他一身「streaked and grimy」，问「Archie, is that blood? Is that blood upon you?」他的手伸向她，「His hands were shaking」；他说「I did something… I did something dreadful I should never have done.」她问「What have you done?」，他答「Whatever it was, I don't believe it can be so very bad.」他答的却是「They'll be hunting me by now」「And I dare say I'll swing for it」，并强调「please believe me – you have to believe me! – when I tell you that I wasn't working under my own… control…」她说「Come inside… Let's clean you up and get some liquor in you」，语气尽量平稳。他似乎听见了，看她时「for an odd moment it seemed to Miss Hare that something shifted in the eyes of her friend and – somehow, impossibly – a different intelligence entirely peeped out at her」；他「grunted」，那声音「animalistic」；她的「Who…」没能说完。片刻过去，人又是他自己：「I can't!」他挤过她，走下台阶，进入街中；她喊「But Archie! Please stay. I can help you.」「I shouldn't… I shouldn't have come here.」「Whatever it is, we can solve it together.」他迟疑了，终于心软，转身走回一步。Emilia 笑着说「Come into the house. All will yet be well.」作者说：他原本像是能被劝进屋、先安定下来，再去跟 authorities 说话——「A good many things would have happened differently if Miss Hare had been successful in this endeavour.」然而就在这一刻，The Jolly Boaters 的门被猛地推开，房东走了出来，满脸「beady-eyed suspicion」：「What's all this? What's going on out here?」不必再多说一个字——灵媒转身踉跄离去；Hare 小姐追上去，仍想护他周全。老板喝道：「Leave him! Leave the poor man be. And get inside, my girl. We've a deal of work to be done before we can open this morning.」她本想追上去、把他拖回来、跟着他、把他带到安全的地方，因为「she suddenly felt certain that he was in some considerable danger」；可老板一声「Miss Hare! There was no kindness in his voice」把她钉住，她只得叹气说这就进来。再看一眼，Beck 已「gone entirely」，她转身去干活。整个漫长的白天，她都被一个「scarcely even made sense to her」的问题困扰：Who looked out from Archie's eyes? Who had taken him over?

**情感弧线位置**：本章弧线为「阴冷破败的清晨—苦差—门口的陌生人—认出 Archie 的惊与怜—血与罪—被附身的瞬间—挣脱—短暂的回心转意—房东一搅—追不上—独自疑问」。情绪从「日常的压抑」经「认出」急升到「血与绞刑」的高压，中段以「另一个心智」的瞬间跌入灵异，再被老板的出现拉回冷酷的日常，末段不落在事件而落在问题上。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现了「被恶魔附身的可怜人」与「清晨酒馆」两个 trope。反转有三处：其一，附身不是持续状态，而是**一闪而过**（a different intelligence entirely peeped out，随后又「the man was himself again」）——这使它更像借住者而非寄生者；其二，Emilia 是**唯一试图救他的人**，而阻断救援的是老板 Crickmore——恶不是鬼，是日常利益；其三，结尾把悬念从「他做了什么」移交给「**谁在看着他**」——问题不再是犯罪，而是主体归属。

**人物弧线**：Emilia 在本章从第 26 章那个「imperishable scorn」的女工，变成一个会为朋友撒谎、会带刀防身、会在最后一刻还想追上去的人；她的关怀是具体的（擦干净、灌酒、稳住声音），因此读者信她。Bill Crickmore 本章首次正面出场，是纯功能性的阻力：只关心开张前的活计。Archie Beck 则从第 30 章那个举枪的人彻底崩解：如今他只求一扇门、一杯酒，却连自己的眼睛都保不住。

**叙事手法**：一是**场景对照**：第 26 章写的是午后暖光里的吧台，本章写的是清晨薄光里的同一地点，作者用「which we have visited before」提醒读者这是同一扇门；二是**生理性细节密集**（水坑、靴子、铰链、招牌、刀、抖的手），让超自然发生在可信的肉身里；三是**叙述者的冷幽默**：「pleasantly dishonest mist」「like a wounded cat」「of very little use indeed」；四是**以问句收尾**：「Who looked out from Archie's eyes? Who had taken him over?」——把整章变成一个开放谜题。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Morning. Just after dawn. London, again. Our setting is that public house – which we have visited before – called The Jolly Boaters. It is far from being an attractive place even in the midst of a winter’s evening, with the snow falling outside and with the fire banked up high, and seen through that pleasantly dishonest mist which the consumption of ale and spirits can often cause to descend.

**中文理解**：清晨，刚过黎明，伦敦。我们的场景是那家我们此前拜访过的酒馆，叫 The Jolly Boaters。即便在冬夜里、外面落着雪、炉火烧得很旺的时候，隔着喝麦酒和烈酒常常会降下的那层「令人愉快的、不诚实的雾」，这地方也远远谈不上吸引人。

**关键词**：Morning. Just after dawn. London, again / Our setting is that public house – which we have visited before / called The Jolly Boaters / far from being an attractive place / the fire banked up high / that pleasantly dishonest mist

**为什么这样写**：作者开门先交代坐标（清晨、伦敦、那家我们已经来过的店），用「which we have visited before」把读者从 Starningham 一夜拉回伦敦，场景转换干净利落。而「pleasantly dishonest mist」是全章的文眼：这层雾既是酒带来的生理效果，也是叙述者对「这地方如何被叙述」的自觉——作者承认自己也在用一层不诚实的雾把它美化。炉火「banked up high」（封得高高的）是雪夜里的写法，为后文的「清晨薄光下破败」做铺垫。

**读者视角提示**：作者自认这层雾是"不诚实的"。

> **原句 2:** But by the thin light of morning it is a dreary and dilapidated building indeed, one which reeks of the night before and which has about it an air of quiet and stubborn misery. Miss Emilia Hare, the young and put-upon barmaid, approached the place with a greater than usual degree of glumness.

**中文理解**：但在清晨的稀薄光线里，它确实是一栋阴沉而破败的房子，散发着前一夜的气味，周身带着一种安静而顽固的悲惨气息。年轻的、任劳任怨的女招待 Emilia Hare 走向这地方，比平常更显阴郁。

**关键词**：by the thin light of morning / a dreary and dilapidated building indeed / reeks of the night before / an air of quiet and stubborn misery / Miss Emilia Hare, the young and put-upon barmaid / a greater than usual degree of glumness

**为什么这样写**：作者用两个夜晚的对照：第一块写雪夜炉火下那层不诚实的雾，本块写晨光下剥掉雾之后的真相。「quiet and stubborn misery」（安静而顽固的悲惨）是关键——它不动、不吵，只是赖着不走，正像这栋房子本身。而「put-upon」（受够了的）一个词就写尽女招待的处境。

**读者视角提示**：晨光是本章的照妖镜。

> **原句 3:** Her boots stamped through puddles with the petulance of the child she had once been, while her posture spoke only of a kind of irritated resignation. There were a good deal of labours ahead of her before the working day could begin in earnest, and the landlord, Bill Crickmore, would doubtless be of very little use indeed in such necessary arrangements, the business of cleaning and tidying and making ready.

**中文理解**：她的靴子踩过水坑，带着她小时候那种孩子气的执拗；而她的姿态只透出一种受烦扰的认命感。正式开工之前还有一大堆活在等着她，而房东 Bill Crickmore 在这些必要的安排上「的确不会有太大用处」——清洁、整理、备好一切。

**关键词**：Her boots stamped through puddles / with the petulance of the child she had once been / her posture spoke only of a kind of irritated resignation / a good deal of labours ahead of her / the landlord, Bill Crickmore, would doubtless be of very little use indeed / cleaning and tidying and making ready

**为什么这样写**：作者用「the child she had once been」做一次时间折叠——她早年的娇气（petulance）今天变成了她踩水坑的节奏，脾气没变，只是变老了一岁。而对 Crickmore 的评价用「of very little use indeed」轻描淡写，却信息量极大：他不是恶人，他只是没用——这正是本章真正的阻力所在。

**读者视角提示**：本章的敌人不是鬼，是"没什么用"的老板。

> **原句 4:** Emilia was just thinking of her onerous duties as the sign of the inn swung in the breeze above her, screeching on its hinges, the noise of it like a wounded cat, when she saw that someone was waiting for her in the tavern doorway.

**中文理解**：Emilia 正在想着那些繁重的活计，这时酒馆的招牌在头顶的微风里晃动，铰链发出尖啸，那声音「像一只受伤的猫」；就在此时，她看见有人正等在酒馆的门洞里。

**关键词**：Emilia was just thinking of her onerous duties / the sign of the inn swung in the breeze above her / screeching on its hinges / the noise of it like a wounded cat / she saw that someone was waiting for her in the tavern doorway

**为什么这样写**：作者用招牌的尖啸当转场音，把她的心思与场景的异常同时推到读者耳边。那声「like a wounded cat」不是写招牌，是写整栋房子的处境——它也像那只猫。而「someone was waiting for her in the tavern doorway」用了「was waiting」而非「stood」，一个进行体的过去时，暗示他早就在那儿等。

**读者视角提示**：他在等她，不是刚到。

> **原句 5:** From a distance, she could not make out much about the figure, save that it was a man, clad in dark clothes, and who seemed much the worse for the wear, somehow succeeding in both slouching and swaying against the door of the public house. Briefly, she hesitated, for she was a careful young woman who knew all the dangers of the streets.

**中文理解**：远处看，她辨不出这身影多少，只知是个男人，穿着深色衣服，并且似乎「糟蹋得厉害」，同时在酒馆门边「slouching and swaying」（又驼又晃）。她短暂地犹豫了一下——她是个谨慎的年轻女人，深知街上的种种危险。

**关键词**：From a distance, she could not make out much about the figure / a man, clad in dark clothes / much the worse for the wear / succeeding in both slouching and swaying against the door / Briefly, she hesitated / a careful young woman who knew all the dangers of the streets

**为什么这样写**：作者先制造视觉的模糊（could not make out much），再给三个精准的判断（男人、深色衣服、状态极差）。而「slouching and swaying」两个动名词并列，把「颓丧」写成身体的两种失重方式。Emilia 的「careful young woman」与「all the dangers of the streets」把她从第 26 章那个嘲讽世界的女工，写成一个在街头需要自我保护的人。

**读者视角提示**：她带刀；她先假定对方是醉汉。

> **原句 6:** She carried about her person a small but very sharp knife in case she ever had to resort to defence of her own person. Walking forward, she was about to reach for this blade, even though she had by then deduced that the gentleman was most likely a patron who, having overindulged, had inadvertently slept beside the premises. Drawing closer, she realised that she knew the man, though not as well, in some respects, as she might have wished.

**中文理解**：她随身带一把又小又锋利的刀，以防万一需要自卫。她向前走，几乎已经伸手去摸那把刀，尽管这时她已推断出，这位先生多半是位常客，喝多了，不小心在店门口睡了一觉。走近些，她意识到自己认得这个人——虽然在某些方面，她并不那么想认得他。

**关键词**：She carried about her person a small but very sharp knife / in case she ever had to resort to defence of her own person / she was about to reach for this blade / a patron who, having overindulged / had inadvertently slept beside the premises / she realised that she knew the man, though not as well, in some respects, as she might have wished

**为什么这样写**：作者用两句话完成紧张与松绑：刀与伸手的动作是紧张，「having overindulged」的推断是松绑。但最后半句「though not as well, in some respects, as she might have wished」极其阴险——她认得他，而且**认得不够清楚**。读者此刻尚不知道「某些方面」指什么，只感到一阵不祥。

**读者视角提示**：她认得他，但"在某些方面不想认得"。

> **原句 7:** Now that she understood, she all but ran to his side. “Archie!” she called out. “Archie, what are you doing here?” Mr Beck looked at her with plaintive eyes. The eyes of a whipped cur, thought Miss Hare, or those of a child close to tears from hunger. “What’s happened?” she asked. He shook his head, seemingly unable to speak.

**中文理解**：弄明白之后，她几乎跑到了他身边。「Archie！」她喊道，「Archie，你在这里干什么？」Beck 小姐……Mr Beck 用一种哀求的眼神看着她。Hare 小姐心想，那是「一条挨了打的狗的眼睛」，或者「一个饿得快要哭出来的孩子的眼睛」。「出什么事了？」她问。他摇了摇头，像是说不出口。

**关键词**：she all but ran to his side / Archie, what are you doing here? / Mr Beck looked at her with plaintive eyes / The eyes of a whipped cur / those of a child close to tears from hunger / He shook his head, seemingly unable to speak

**为什么这样写**：作者用两个比喻把 Beck 的眼睛写到底：一条被打的狗（挨过打、怕人、可怜），一个饿哭的孩子（无辜、无助）。而「he shook his head, seemingly unable to speak」——不是不能，是不敢说。这一笔把「他杀了人」的信息推到读者眼前，又不给任何事实。

**读者视角提示**：他的沉默本身就是内容。

> **原句 8:** “I was worried about you,” said the lady softly, “when you ran away yesterday.” She looked him up and down, and saw how streaked and grimy his clothes were. “Archie, is that blood?” she asked. “Is that blood upon you?” He reached out to her then. His hands were shaking. “I did something…” he said faintly. “I did something dreadful I should never have done.” “What have you done?” she asked.

**中文理解**：「昨天你跑掉的时候，我很担心你。」那位女士轻声说。她上下打量他，看见他一身又脏又污。「Archie，那是血吗？」她问，「那是你身上的血吗？」这时他向她伸出手来——他的手在抖。「我做了件事……」他微弱地说，「我做了件可怕的事，我本不该做。」「你做了什么？」她问。

**关键词**：I was worried about you / when you ran away yesterday / how streaked and grimy his clothes were / Archie, is that blood? / His hands were shaking / I did something dreadful I should never have done / What have you done?

**为什么这样写**：Emilia 的问话是线性推进的：担心→打量→问血→追问，节奏像急救。作者把「streaked and grimy」（一道道脏污）写成视觉的条痕，说明他不是简单弄脏，而是有拖拽与奔逃的痕迹。而他的自白用省略号「I did something…」开场——话没说完就先泄了底。

**读者视角提示**：他身上的"一道道"脏污说明发生过拖拽。

> **原句 9:** “Whatever it was, I don’t believe it can be so very bad.” “They’ll be hunting me by now,” said Beck, showing no indication that he had heard the question at all. “And I dare say I’ll swing for it. Though, please believe me – you have to believe me! – when I tell you that I wasn’t working under my own… control…” “Come inside,” said Emilia, keeping her voice as calm as she was able.

**中文理解**：「不管是什么，我不相信会有那么糟。」「他们这会儿肯定已经在追我了，」Beck 说着，根本没显出听见那个问题。「我敢说，我大概要为它被吊死。不过——请相信我，你必须相信我！——我告诉你，我不是在自己……不受控制的……状态下做事的。」「进来吧，」Emilia 说，努力让自己的声音保持平静。

**关键词**：I don’t believe it can be so very bad / They’ll be hunting me by now / I dare say I’ll swing for it / please believe me – you have to believe me! / I wasn’t working under my own… control / Come inside / keeping her voice as calm as she was able

**为什么这样写**：作者让 Beck 完全不接她的话——「showing no indication that he had heard the question at all」，因为他此刻在讲另一件事：他有罪，而罪不在自己。而「not working under my own… control」用两处省略号把最要命的那半句卡在喉咙里。Emilia 的「keeping her voice as calm as she was able」是本块唯一的技巧：她其实在抖。

**读者视角提示**：他已经在预告自己的死法。

> **原句 10:** “Let’s clean you up and get some liquor in you. And then we’ll have a decent conversation and see if we can’t straighten this all out.” These words he seemed to hear. He looked at her carefully, and for an odd moment it seemed to Miss Hare that something shifted in the eyes of her friend and – somehow, impossibly – a different intelligence entirely peeped out at her. He grunted. There was something animalistic in the sound.

**中文理解**：「我们把你收拾干净，再给你灌点酒。然后我们好好谈一次，看能不能把这事捋顺。」这些话他似乎听见了。他仔细地看着她——有那么一瞬间，Hare 小姐觉得朋友的眼睛里似乎有什么东西移了位，而某种——不知怎么的，不可能地——完全不同的智慧从里面探了出来。他「咕哝」了一声。那声音里带着某种动物性。

**关键词**：These words he seemed to hear / he looked at her carefully / for an odd moment / something shifted in the eyes of her friend / a different intelligence entirely peeped out at her / He grunted / There was something animalistic in the sound

**为什么这样写**：这是全书「被附身」母题最精微的一次呈现：作者没有让它现身，只写「something shifted」与「a different intelligence peeped out at her」——peep（探出）这个词选得极好，是窥视，不是占据。而「animalistic」的一声咕哝，紧接着她那句未完成的「Who…」，构成灵异一闪而过。

**读者视角提示**：另一个心智只露了一秒。

> **原句 11:** “Who…” she began, then found herself unable to complete the sentence. And then the moment passed, and the man was himself again. “I can’t!” said Archibald Beck, and he pushed past her, away from the steps of The Jolly Boaters and out into the street beyond. “But Archie!” the lady cried out. “Please stay. I can help you.” “I shouldn’t…” he said. “I shouldn’t have come here.” “Whatever it is,” said Emilia Hare, “we can solve it together.”

**中文理解**：「谁……」她开了个头，却发现自己没能把话说完。然后那片刻过去了，男人又是他自己。「我不能！」Archibald Beck 说着，从她身边挤过去，走下 The Jolly Boaters 的台阶，走进外面的街里。「可是 Archie！」那位女士喊道，「留下来。我能帮你。」「我不该……」他说，「我不该来这里。」「无论是什么事，」Emilia Hare 说，「我们一起解决。」

**关键词**：Who… / then found herself unable to complete the sentence / the moment passed, and the man was himself again / I can’t! / pushed past her, away from the steps of The Jolly Boaters / out into the street beyond / we can solve it together

**为什么这样写**：作者用「I can't!」替代她的「Who…」——他抢在她问出那个问题之前先封了口。而后那句「I shouldn't have come here」把她的善意变成他的指控：来找你是错的。作者让 Emilia 说出全书最漂亮的一句台词「we can solve it together」，正因为他随即软化。

**读者视角提示**：她这句承诺他会信，所以他会回头。

> **原句 12:** He seemed to hesitate then, and to relent. He turned back, took a step towards the tavern. “Come into the house,” Emilia smiled. “All will yet be well.” It seemed as though he might be coaxed inside to calm himself before speaking to the authorities. A good many things would have happened differently if Miss Hare had been successful in this endeavour.

**中文理解**：他似乎犹豫了一下，终于心软。他转过身，朝酒馆走了一步。「进屋来，」Emilia 笑道，「一切都会好起来的。」看起来他像是能被劝进屋、先冷静下来，然后去跟 authorities 说话。假如 Hare 小姐在这件事上成功了，许多事情本来都会变得不一样。

**关键词**：He seemed to hesitate then, and to relent / He turned back, took a step towards the tavern / All will yet be well / to calm himself before speaking to the authorities / A good many things would have happened differently / if Miss Hare had been successful in this endeavour

**为什么这样写**：作者在这里第一次启用**反事实叙述**——「假如她成功了，许多事会不一样」。这不是威胁读者，而是预告：她差一点点就成了。而「speaking to the authorities」用公文式的抽象词，与前文他具体的「They'll be hunting me by now」形成对照：她想的是流程，他想的是绞刑。

**读者视角提示**：作者在此处开了反事实的口子。

> **原句 13:** Yet at that very moment the door to The Jolly Boaters was flung open and the landlord emerged, looking about him with beady-eyed suspicion. “What’s all this?” he said. “What’s going on out here?” No more words needed to be spoken. At this new vision, the medium turned again and stumbled away. Miss Hare started after him, still hoping that she might keep him safe. “Leave him!” said Bill Crickmore. “Leave the poor man be.

**中文理解**：然而恰在那一刻，The Jolly Boaters 的门被猛地推开，房东走了出来，满脸「贼眉鼠眼般的猜疑」。「这是怎么回事？」他说，「你们在外面搞什么？」不必再多说一个字。看见这番景象，那灵媒又转过身，踉跄着走开了。Hare 小姐追了上去，仍希望能护他周全。「别管他！」Bill Crickmore 说道，「别去管那可怜人。

**关键词**：the door to The Jolly Boaters was flung open / the landlord emerged, looking about him with beady-eyed suspicion / What’s going on out here? / No more words needed to be spoken / the medium turned again and stumbled away / Miss Hare started after him / Leave him! Leave the poor man be

**为什么这样写**：作者用「No more words needed to be spoken」一句话完成全部谈判：房东一露面，救援就结束了。而「the medium」这个冷静的名词在惊惶中显得格外刺眼——他刚刚还在否认一切。「beady-eyed suspicion」（贼眉鼠眼般的猜疑）既写老板，也顺带写这世界的眼光。

**读者视角提示**：救人的唯一障碍，是一个只想开张的房东。

> **原句 14:** And get inside, my girl. We’ve a deal of work to be done before we can open this morning.” Emilia thought of running after Archie, of dragging him back or following him or taking him to a place of safety, for she suddenly felt certain that he was in some considerable danger. “Miss Hare!” shouted her employer. There was no kindness in his voice. And so she sighed and said that she was coming in now.

**中文理解**：「进屋吧，我的姑娘。开门营业之前我们还有一大堆活要干。」Emilia 想过追上去把 Archie 拽回来，或者跟着他，或者把他带到安全的地方去，因为她忽然真切地感到他正处于某种相当大的危险之中。「Hare 小姐！」她的雇主大声喝道。他的声音里没有一丝善意。于是她叹了口气，说自己这就进来。

**关键词**：And get inside, my girl / We’ve a deal of work to be done / Emilia thought of running after Archie / of dragging him back or following him / or taking him to a place of safety / she suddenly felt certain that he was in some considerable danger / There was no kindness in his voice / And so she sighed and said that she was coming in now

**为什么这样写**：作者用四个「or」把她的心念排成一列，句式本身像挣扎。而「she suddenly felt certain」是她第一次获得超常的直觉——她确知他有危险，却无法救他。而老板的声音「There was no kindness in his voice」是本块的判词：不是命令，是漠然。

**读者视角提示**：她的直觉比老板的判断更接近真相。

> **原句 15:** She looked again, saw that Mr Beck was now gone entirely, and went to go about her business. For the rest of that long day she was troubled by a particular question, one which scarcely even made sense to her. Who looked out from Archie’s eyes? Who had taken him over?

**中文理解**：她又看了一眼，看见 Mr Beck 已经彻底不见了，便去忙自己的活。整个漫长的白天，她都被一个特定的问题困扰着——那问题甚至几乎说不通。究竟是谁从 Archie 的眼睛里往外看？是谁接管了他？

**关键词**：saw that Mr Beck was now gone entirely / went to go about her business / For the rest of that long day / troubled by a particular question / one which scarcely even made sense to her / Who looked out from Archie’s eyes? / Who had taken him over?

**为什么这样写**：作者让本章在一问一答之间收束：第一问是物证式的（谁的眼睛），第二问是主体性的（谁接管了谁）。而「one which scarcely even made sense to her」承认这个问题超出她的经验框架——她不是灵媒，她只是酒馆女工。整章的谜被留在这个最没有资格问的人心里。

**读者视角提示**：全书的核心问题第一次以人称问出。

## 一句话总结

镜头切回伦敦 The Jolly Boaters 酒馆：雪夜炉火与「that pleasantly dishonest mist」之下这地方已「far from being an attractive place」，清晨薄光里更是「a dreary and dilapidated building indeed… an air of quiet and stubborn misery」；女工 Emilia Hare 带着比平日更深的阴郁走向店门，靴子「stamped through puddles with the petulance of the child she had once been」，姿态只有「a kind of irritated resignation」，而老板 Bill Crickmore 在清洁整理上「would doubtless be of very little use indeed」；招牌在风中「screeching on its hinges, the noise of it like a wounded cat」，她抬头看见门洞里等着一个人——「much the worse for the wear」，「succeeding in both slouching and swaying against the door」；她因「a careful young woman who knew all the dangers of the streets」而短暂犹豫，「She carried about her person a small but very sharp knife」，走近才认出是 Archie Beck，「though not as well, in some respects, as she might have wished」；她「all but ran to his side」喊「Archie, what are you doing here?」，他以「plaintive eyes」相看——「The eyes of a whipped cur」或「those of a child close to tears from hunger」——摇着头说不出话；她说「I was worried about you… when you ran away yesterday」，见他一身「streaked and grimy」便问「Archie, is that blood?」，他手抖着说「I did something… I did something dreadful I should never have done」，她答「I don’t believe it can be so very bad」，他却说「They’ll be hunting me by now」「I dare say I’ll swing for it」「please believe me – you have to believe me! – … I wasn’t working under my own… control…」；她把他拉进屋、想「clean you up and get some liquor in you」，就在这一瞬「something shifted in the eyes of her friend and – somehow, impossibly – a different intelligence entirely peeped out at her」，他一声 animalistic 的 grunt，她的「Who…」没能说完，而「the man was himself again」；他挣脱道「I can’t!」「I shouldn’t have come here」，她答「we can solve it together」，他心软转身（作者提示：若她成功，「A good many things would have happened differently」）；然而门被猛地推开，房东带着「beady-eyed suspicion」现身：「What’s going on out here?」「No more words needed to be spoken.」灵媒踉跄而去，她追上去想护他，老板喝道「Leave him!」「Leave the poor man be. And get inside, my girl.」，而她本已「felt certain that he was in some considerable danger」，只能叹气进店；这一天她始终被一个问题困扰：「Who looked out from Archie’s eyes? Who had taken him over?」
