# 37. V

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：本章进屋，是全书「规则」章。作者先交代房屋格局：底层四间、二层四间，三层「for reasons which seemed obscure」只有一间——而且谁也没敢上去。Bethlem 太太点起灯、把两人领进那间「the nearest thing that the building possessed to a space of comfort」的起居室，随即发表一段她自陈「a good deal of thought and consideration」的训话。她的第一条事实是：这房子完全可能住得安全，「even, I dare say, to raise a family here」；她承认这房子「what country folk would think of as ‘haunted’」，但把「what forces may or may not be at work here」推给「men of the cloth and men of learning」，只给结论：若守三条规则，你在这里可以幸福。规则一：晚上十点后无论门外是谁都不开门。Ambrose 打趣说「But it might be you, Mrs Bethlem」，她却正色道：连我也不开，「Or – I should say – even to what seems to you to resemble me」，直看得老兵低下目光。规则二：忽略顶层的声音——「banging、running feet、a kind of rustling sound, like the wind through dry leaves」，「You do not investigate and you leave well alone」，噪音最终会停，「They always do」。Fell 太太当即要那间房，理由是整层空着太可惜；Bethlem 断言她会后悔，并给出更实际的理由：这里不会有仆人，村里也没人肯来，她已安排村里姑娘 Susan Naylor 每天早上来、待到午餐，「I have promised the girl nothing.」规则三最要紧：尽量别在这房子里提问——「the asking of questions of any sort」；上尉表示不解，她重复「I mean exactly what I say」；Fell 太太问若无意间问了会怎样，Bethlem 不答，只四下张望、侧耳听上面或隔壁，「There was only silence」，然后说：这房子有倾向「to… answer」，「Not always. But often.」而且「the answers that it gives may not be welcome ones」。上尉与 Fell 太太交换了一个眼神。Bethlem 如释重负，说实质内容就到此，接着要带他们看屋子；Fell 太太请她留下吃饭并提到她已备好食物，Bethlem 婉拒——「But I have no wish to eat here.」她不等回答便走出房间，两人跟上去。末句：屋外某处的狗叫了三声，随即静下来。

**情感弧线位置**：本章是「立规」段，弧线为「格局（未敢上去）—点灯迎客—训话开场—承认闹鬼却给出出路—规则一（严肃压场）—规则二（具体可怖）—规则三（最不祥）—沉默试探—承诺会回答—如释重负—离席—狗吠」。整体是一条收紧的绳：Bethlem 的语气越来越像教师，而听者的空间越来越小；结尾狗叫三声，把刚刚被安抚的空气再次戳破。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现了「闹鬼老宅的入住须知」这一经典设定（管家/长者交代三条禁令），但作者做了三处反转：其一，禁令不是迷信禁忌而是**操作规程**（十点后不开门、顶层不查、不提问），像一份租约条款；其二，最反常的一条是「不提问」——鬼宅的规矩通常是别做某事，这里却是**别问**，理由是「房子会回答」；其三，Bethlem 表面是善意的引路人，实则她的台词里藏着一层自利：她要走了，而这三条规矩谁也不许违反。上一章的「coming home」在本章得到局部解释——这屋子确实可以住人，代价是服从。

**人物弧线**：Bethlem 太太在本章完成了从「看房人」到「传讯者」的转变——她不再只是交接钥匙，而是交出一整套生存法。她的姿态是教师（schoolmistress insisting upon the attention of some recalcitrant），而她心里真正盘算的是「a pair of sceptics or scoffers would be placing themselves in the path of considerable peril」——她要的是听话的住客，不是清醒的住客。Ambrose 上尉在规则一被调侃后立刻被压回去（lowered his gaze），显示他在这屋里已经是学生而非保护者。Fell 太太则当场挑战规则二（要那间房），是三人中唯一敢质疑的——但她也被 Bethlem 用「你一定会后悔」挡回。

**叙事手法**：一是**房屋清单式开场**（四间、四间、一间），作者用数字把读者的空间感固定下来，而「第三层只有一间房」与「谁也不敢上去」两句连起来，读者自动把注意力引向那间房；二是**「我以……之名断言」式的法律口吻**（it is my considered opinion / it is my certain belief），Bethlem 每下一条结论都加上限定词，模拟证词；三是**以沉默代替回答**：第三条规则被追问「what happens」时，叙述者不写答案，只写她张望、倾听，「There was only silence」——然后由她自己说出「the house has a tendency to… answer」，这一答本身就是最坏的答案；四是**结尾的狗吠**：与第 21 章墓园那声狗叫遥相呼应，把「静」的意象再翻一次。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** The house, although it had seemed big to Louisa in childhood, was not a terribly large one. There were four rooms on the ground floor and as many again on the second. The third appeared to be comprised, for reasons which seemed obscure, of a single chamber. Not, of course, that any of them ventured so far upstairs in the first instance.

**中文理解**：这栋房子在 Louisa 童年时曾显得很大，其实并不算太大。一层有四间房，二层同样有四间。第三层似乎只由一间屋子构成，出于某些说不清的理由。当然，一开始他们谁也没有冒险上到那么高的地方去。

**关键词**：it had seemed big to Louisa in childhood / not a terribly large one / four rooms on the ground floor / The third appeared to be comprised, for reasons which seemed obscure, of a single chamber / any of them ventured so far upstairs

**为什么这样写**：作者先用「童年时显得很大」纠正一个旧印象——她不是怕这房子大，而是记得它大。这一纠正让第 36 章那句「像是回家」更危险：她记得的正是这样一所小宅。随后作者用「for reasons which seemed obscure」把「三层只有一间」写成谜面，而末句「谁也没敢上去」直接把谜底的一半交了出来：不需要解释，因为他们连试都不试。

**读者视角提示**：第三层那间房，是全书的物理核心。

> **原句 2:** While Mrs Fell and the captain stood in the hallway, Mrs Bethlem bustled past them both. For what she suspected would surely be the final time, she set about lighting the lamps in the house before ushering the two new arrivals into the drawing room, a snug little space with table and chairs, which was the nearest thing that the building possessed to a space of comfort.

**中文理解**：就在 Fell 太太和上尉站在门厅里的时候，Bethlem 太太从他们身边一阵风似的走过。她料想这必定是最后一次了，于是动手把这屋子的灯一盏盏点起，再把两位新来的客人引进起居室——一个安着桌椅的小小舒适空间，是这栋建筑所拥有的、最接近「舒适」二字的地方。

**关键词**：Mrs Bethlem bustled past them both / what she suspected would surely be the final time / set about lighting the lamps / ushering the two new arrivals into the drawing room / a snug little space with table and chairs / the nearest thing that the building possessed to a space of comfort

**为什么这样写**：作者把「点灯」写成一个仪式动作——她来的目的是让这屋子在自己手里最后一次亮起来。而「大概是最后一次」这个心理动作用一个状语从句挂在动作上，使点灯与告别同时发生。最锋利的是末句：连一间有桌椅的小客厅都「the nearest thing… to a space of comfort」——这栋房子的舒适上限已经被作者预先标定。

**读者视角提示**：她点灯是在告别这所房子。

> **原句 3:** She addressed the lady and the gentlemen then, giving them a speech into which she had poured a good deal of thought and consideration. “Now, I dare say you both have a deal of questions. I will answer them all as best I can. But before then, I want you to understand one important fact.” Her tone was that of the schoolmistress insisting upon the attention of some recalcitrant. “Of course.”

**中文理解**：她于是对那位女士和那位绅士说话，发表一番她倾注了大量心思与斟酌的讲话。「现在，我敢说你们两位都有不少问题。我会尽我所能一一回答。但在此之前，我要你们先理解一件重要的事。」她的语气，就像一位女教师坚持要某个不听话的学生集中注意力。「当然。」

**关键词**：giving them a speech into which she had poured a good deal of thought and consideration / you both have a deal of questions / I will answer them all as best I can / before then, I want you to understand one important fact / Her tone was that of the schoolmistress / insisting upon the attention of some recalcitrant

**为什么这样写**：作者先宣布这是一篇「讲稿」——准备已久、一字一句想过，这让读者预期规则会成体系。随后立刻用「女教师 vs 不听话的学生」这个比喻定调，并把听众降为学生。末句那句「当然。」只有两个词，却是全章的服从测试：Fell 太太没有顶嘴。

**读者视角提示**：Bethlem 要的是听话的住客，不是清醒的住客。

> **原句 4:** “Well, then. The fact is this: it is quite possible to spend time in this house and to live here – even, I dare say, to raise a family here – without being in the slightest danger or being in the least unsettled. I have no doubt that you know the house’s reputation and even that of its owner, your uncle. And, having worked in and around the place for a number of years now, it is my considered opinion that this house is indeed what country folk would think of as ‘haunted’.

**中文理解**：「那么，事实是这样：在这房子里住一段时间、在这里生活——甚至，我敢说，在这里养家——而不受丝毫危险、不受半分困扰，是完全可能的。我毫不怀疑你们知道这房子的名声，甚至知道它的主人、你们那位叔叔的名声。我在屋里屋外忙活这些年，我的深思熟虑的结论是：这房子确实是乡下人所谓『闹鬼』的那种。」

**关键词**：it is quite possible to spend time in this house / even, I dare say, to raise a family here / the house’s reputation and even that of its owner, your uncle / my considered opinion / what country folk would think of as ‘haunted’

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章的思想核心，结构是「承认—让步—再承认」：先承认危险可以不存在（甚至可以养家），再承认闹鬼之名（以及叔叔的名声），最后落在「我的深思熟虑的结论」上。作者让 Bethlem 用「even, I dare say, to raise a family here」来收买人心——这正对着 Fell 太太腹中的孩子。而引用「乡下人所谓」则把自己的判断外包给民俗，姿态谦逊，立场却毫不含糊。

**读者视角提示**：「养家」那句是冲着 Fell 太太的身孕来的。

> **原句 5:** As to what forces may or may not be at work here, I leave that to men of the cloth and to men of learning. Yet it is my certain belief that you may be happy here, Mrs Fell, if only you follow these three rules.” She paused at this point, not only for reasons of dramatic effect but also to catch her breath and to take a swift look at the faces of her audience.

**中文理解**：「至于这里究竟有什么力量在起作用、是否在起作用，我留给神职人员和学者去判断。但我确信，只要你们遵守三条规则，Fell 太太，你在这里可以过得幸福。」她在这里停了一下，不只是为了戏剧效果，也是为了喘口气，并飞快地看一眼听众的脸。

**关键词**：As to what forces may or may not be at work here / I leave that to men of the cloth and to men of learning / it is my certain belief / you may be happy here, Mrs Fell / if only you follow these three rules / not only for reasons of dramatic effect / to catch her breath / a swift look at the faces of her audience

**为什么这样写**：作者用「推给神职人员和学者」这一手把最核心的问题（是什么在作祟）悬置起来，同时完成两件事：保住自己的专家身份，又把读者引向第 35 章那位神父——他知道答案，却只说了「你不该来」。随后她「停了一下」，作者立刻点明这一停有双重原因（戏剧效果与实际需要），把「她在演」这件事摆到台面上。

**读者视角提示**：神父知道答案却没说完——两条线在这里交叉。

> **原句 6:** To her great relief, they seemed to be listening attentively and to be receiving her words with all necessary seriousness. It was a good thing, thought Mrs Bethlem, that both had open minds; a pair of sceptics or scoffers would be placing themselves in the path of considerable peril simply by setting foot into the house at all. “Rule one,” said the lady, “is do not open the front door to anybody after ten o’clock at night, no matter who you think is waiting for you there.”

**中文理解**：令她大为宽慰的是，他们似乎在专心听着，并以一切必要的严肃态度接收她的话。Bethlem 心想，这很好：两人都心胸开放；换成一对怀疑者或嘲弄者，光是踏进这屋子，就已经把自己置于相当的危险之中了。「第一条规则，」那位女士说，「是晚上十点以后，不要给任何人开前门，无论你以为门外站着的是谁。」

**关键词**：to her great relief / to be receiving her words with all necessary seriousness / both had open minds / a pair of sceptics or scoffers / placing themselves in the path of considerable peril / Rule one / do not open the front door to anybody after ten o’clock at night / no matter who you think is waiting for you there

**为什么这样写**：作者又一次把「戏剧效果」和「实际需要」并列——Bethlem 自己的算计也承认自己在演，但同时这确实是她需要时间平复。她在心里给听众分类：开放心灵者 vs 怀疑者、嘲弄者；后者是「considerable peril」的来源。这一分类也悄悄把嘲笑者预先排除在读者阵营之外。紧接着规则一登场，而它的措辞里埋着钩子：「无论你以为门外站着的是谁」——危险的不是「人」。

**读者视角提示**：规则一真正禁止的是「你以为」。

> **原句 7:** “But it might be you, Mrs Bethlem,” said the man, trying to be jocular. “We would so hate to leave you shivering on the doorstep.” The woman’s voice was grave. “You do not open the door even to me, Captain Ambrose. Or – I should say – even to what seems to you to resemble me.” She looked at him then till the old soldier lowered his gaze.

**中文理解**：「但那可能是我啊，Bethlem 太太，」那位先生试图用玩笑的口气说，「我们可舍不得让你在门口冻得发抖。」那女人的声音变得庄重。「连我也不开，Ambrose 上尉。或者说——即便门外那个看起来像我的东西，我也不开。」她这样看着他，直到那位老兵低下了目光。

**关键词**：But it might be you, Mrs Bethlem / trying to be jocular / We would so hate to leave you shivering on the doorstep / The woman’s voice was grave / You do not open the door even to me / even to what seems to you to resemble me / till the old soldier lowered his gaze

**为什么这样写**：Ambrose 的玩笑正戳在规则的漏洞上——如果门外是你呢？作者让 Bethlem 用一句补语把规则的边界推到极限：连「像她的东西」也不开——这就把「鬼」的存在正式写进了规则条文里。而「直到老兵低下目光」是全章唯一一次身体性的服从展示：这位一路护航的老上尉，在这条规矩面前移开了视线。

**读者视角提示**：规则一其实已承认门外可能是「像她的东西」。

> **原句 8:** “The second rule,” she went on, “is to ignore any sounds you hear from the uppermost floor of this house. Indeed, my advice is to avoid venturing up there altogether unless it is absolutely necessary. You may from time to time hear sounds which might resemble… banging. Or running feet. Or a kind of rustling sound, like the wind through dry leaves. Now, you leave these things alone. In this, I wish to make myself clear. You do not investigate and you leave well alone. The noises will stop in the end. They always do.”

**中文理解**：「第二条规则，」她接着说，「是忽略你在本屋最高一层听到的任何声音。事实上我的建议是：除非绝对必要，否则根本别上去。你可能会不时听到一些声音，也许像是……砰砰的敲打声。或是奔跑的脚步。或是一种沙沙声，像风吹过干枯的落叶。现在，你要放任这些不管。在这一点上，我要说清楚：不要去查，暂且不管。噪音最终会停下来。总是会停。」

**关键词**：The second rule / to ignore any sounds you hear from the uppermost floor / to avoid venturing up there altogether / sounds which might resemble… banging / Or running feet / a kind of rustling sound, like the wind through dry leaves / You do not investigate and you leave well alone / The noises will stop in the end. They always do.

**为什么这样写**：作者把恐怖写成了「声音清单」，三个拟声短语里最妙的是第三个——「像风吹过干枯的落叶」，因为第 9 章那个女孩床上的落叶正与此呼应，却没有人点破。而「不要去查」被作者重复成两句短命令，句号加重。末尾「总是会停」是安慰，也是威胁的另一半：它会停，但会停多久？没说。

**读者视角提示**：落叶的声音——第 9 章的旧账在这里被悄悄提起。

> **原句 9:** “But I might need that room,” said Mrs Fell stoutly. “It seems an awful pity to leave a whole floor of a house like this entirely empty.” “You can take it from me,” said Mrs Bethlem, “that you will regret any decision you might take to make of that upper floor anything useful or decorative. Leave it be, Mrs Fell, that’s my strong advice to you. This will never be a large household. You have no room for servants. And even if you did, you would find no one local who would accept the position.

**中文理解**：「可是我也许需要那间房，」Fell 太太坚决地说，「把这样一栋房子的整整一层完全空着，实在太可惜了。」「你尽可以相信我，」Bethlem 太太说，「你若打算拿最高那一层做点什么有用的或好看的，都会后悔。听我的劝，Fell 太太，那个楼层就别去动。这户人家永远不会太大。你没有用佣人的房间；就算有，你也找不到本地人肯来干这活。」

**关键词**：But I might need that room / said Mrs Fell stoutly / It seems an awful pity / to leave a whole floor of a house like this entirely empty / you will regret any decision you might take / Leave it be / This will never be a large household / You have no room for servants / no one local who would accept the position

**为什么这样写**：这是全章唯一一次有人挑战规则，而挑战的理由极其正当、极其日常——实用。这正是规则的可怕之处：它要你为了一件合理的需要，付出不可测的代价。Bethlem 的回击不直接说她害怕，而是给出三条现实理由：人少、没房间、村里没人肯来——让「顶层不能住人」从一个鬼故事变成后勤问题。

**读者视角提示**：正当理由最容易让人违规。

> **原句 10:** I have made arrangements for a young girl from the village – one Susan Naylor – to come into the house every morning and to stay until luncheon, so as to make sure that you have everything you need. If the arrangement is not to your taste, then you are quite at liberty to make your own. I have promised the girl nothing.” “I am obliged to you,” said Louisa. “Not at all. I wanted your first days here to be as… easy as possible.”

**中文理解**：「我已经安排了一个村里的姑娘——Susan Naylor——每天早上来这屋子，一直待到午餐时分，好保证你需要的都有。若这安排不合你的心意，你尽管另请他人。我可没答应过那姑娘什么。」「我很承你的情，」Louisa 说。「不客气。我只希望你们在这里最初的日子能尽量……容易些。」

**关键词**：a young girl from the village – one Susan Naylor / to stay until luncheon / so as to make sure that you have everything you need / you are quite at liberty to make your own / I have promised the girl nothing / I am obliged to you / I wanted your first days here to be as… easy as possible

**为什么这样写**：「我没有答应过那姑娘什么」是全段最冷的一笔——她不欠 Susan Naylor 什么，暗示这份差事是她施舍的，也暗示那姑娘随时可以被撤换。而「让最初的日子尽量容易些」这句体恤话，紧接着就是三条要命的规则——她的体贴与她的警告是同一张嘴说出来的。

**读者视角提示**：Susan Naylor 是全书唯一一个未来的普通人，她被写进来又被打发掉。

> **原句 11:** “That was thoughtful of you.” Mrs Fell smiled at the older woman, a gesture which was not then returned in kind. “The third and final rule,” Bethlem went on, “is the most important of all. I would do everything in your power to avoid the asking of questions when you are together or alone in this house.” “I’m not sure I understand what you mean by that.” A note of bemusement had entered the voice of Captain Ambrose. “I mean exactly what I say, sir. I mean the asking of questions of any sort.”

**中文理解**：「你真体贴。」Fell 太太朝这位年长的女人笑了笑，但那笑意并未以同样的方式得到回应。「第三条，也是最后一条规则，」Bethlem 接着说，「是最重要的一条。我会竭尽你们力所能及的一切，避免在这房子里——无论是你们在一起时，还是独自一人时——提出问题。」「我不太明白你的意思。」Ambrose 上尉的声音里带上了困惑。「先生，我说的正是字面意思。我说的是任何种类的问题。」

**关键词**：Mrs Fell smiled at the older woman / a gesture which was not then returned in kind / The third and final rule / is the most important of all / to avoid the asking of questions / when you are together or alone in this house / A note of bemusement had entered the voice / I mean exactly what I say, sir / the asking of questions of any sort

**为什么这样写**：作者用一处未被回应的微笑写尽两人关系：Fell 太太给出善意，得到的却是冷的。这一细节比任何评论都省力。第三条规则的表述本身令人困惑——「避免提问」不是「别问某事」，是「别提问」这种行为本身。作者显然感觉到了这份古怪，因为紧跟着让上尉替读者问一次，而 Bethlem 那句近乎粗暴的「我说的正是字面意思」把读者和她一起推开。

**读者视角提示**：第三条反常到连上尉都听不明白。

> **原句 12:** “But what happens,” asked Louisa Fell, “if any questions are inadvertently asked?” The other woman did not reply to this at first. Instead, her eyes darted quickly around the room and she seemed to be listening out for something either above them or in the adjacent rooms. There was only silence. “Well,” she said, “the house has a tendency to… answer. Not always. But often. And the answers that it gives may not be welcome ones.” The captain and Mrs Fell exchanged a glance at this.

**中文理解**：「可是，」Louisa Fell 问道，「如果无意间问了问题，会怎么样？」那女人起初没有回答这句。她的目光飞快地扫过房间四周，她像是在听头顶上方或隔壁房间里的什么动静。只有一片寂静。「好吧，」她终于说，「这房子有倾向……回答。不是每次都会。但常常会。而它给出的那些回答，可能不是受欢迎的回答。」上尉与 Fell 太太听到这话交换了一个眼神。

**关键词**：if any questions are inadvertently asked / did not reply to this at first / her eyes darted quickly around the room / to be listening out for something either above them or in the adjacent rooms / There was only silence / the house has a tendency to… answer / Not always. But often / the answers that it gives may not be welcome ones / exchanged a glance at this

**为什么这样写**：作者用三步制造恐惧：先拒绝回答（把答案悬空），再让她四下张望、侧耳倾听（读者也开始听），然后用「There was only silence」让沉默本身成为回答——仿佛房子刚才是真的在答话。而那句「the house has a tendency to… answer」的省略号在此处像这房子在开口前的停顿。末句的「交换一个眼神」没有台词，只有一瞥。

**读者视角提示**：这一处的沉默是「房子已经答了」。

> **原句 13:** Mrs Bethlem, meanwhile, seemed relieved to be coming to the end of her speech. “And that, I think, is everything of substance. Now, let me show you everything you ought to see before I take my leave of you.” “You are most welcome to stay with us,” said Mrs Fell. “Since you were kind enough to have arranged food for our larder, it’s really the least we can do to share it with you.” “Thank you, that is most kind. But I have no wish to eat here.” “I see.”

**中文理解**：与此同时，Bethlem 太太似乎如释重负，终于快讲完了。「我想，实质的内容就是这些。现在，让我带你们看看在离开之前该看的所有地方。」「非常欢迎你留下来，」Fell 太太说，「既然你这么好心替我们的食品储藏室备了东西，我们最起码也该和你一起吃一点。」「谢谢，这太客气了。但我一点也不想在这里吃饭。」「我明白了。」

**关键词**：seemed relieved to be coming to the end of her speech / everything of substance / before I take my leave of you / You are most welcome to stay with us / you were kind enough to have arranged food for our larder / it’s really the least we can do to share it with you / But I have no wish to eat here / I see.

**为什么这样写**：作者在规则讲完后立刻给两个人物反应位：上尉与 Fell 太太交换眼神（消化），Bethlem 则是「如释重负」（收工）。Fell 太太那句留饭既是客气，也是试探——她给了一个可以推心置腹的场合；而 Bethlem 拒绝得干脆：「我一点也不想在这里吃饭」——她刚讲完「这房子会回答问题」，自然不想在问题最多的地方久留。

**读者视角提示**：Bethlem 急着走，理由她没说。

> **原句 14:** “Now, let me show you the rest of the house.” Without waiting for any response, Bethlem swept from the room, in the evident expectation that the others would follow, which indeed they did. From somewhere outside, a dog barked three times, then fell silent.

**中文理解**：「现在，我带你们看看这屋子的其余部分。」不等任何人回答，Bethlem 便径自走出房间，显然认定别人会跟上来——他们确实跟上了。从屋外某处传来一声狗吠，叫了三下，随即归于寂静。

**关键词**：Without waiting for any response / Bethlem swept from the room / in the evident expectation that the others would follow / which indeed they did / a dog barked three times, then fell silent

**为什么这样写**：Bethlem 走出房间的方式是「swept」——她不等、不留余地，认定两人会跟上来。末句的狗吠三声是全章的最后一个音：不是回答，是打断；而第 21 章墓园那声狗叫正是同一只狗，它曾让 Scrooge 在说出 Starningham 之前闭嘴。狗吠之后「fell silent」，房子重新安静下来——安静，正是这栋房子的默认状态。

**读者视角提示**：狗叫三声就静了——房子在听。

## 一句话总结

进屋之后，Bethlem 太太点亮各屋的灯——她料想这将是「what she suspected would surely be the final time」——把两人带进那间「the nearest thing that the building possessed to a space of comfort」的起居室，随即发表一番「a good deal of thought and consideration」的训话，语气「like the schoolmistress insisting upon the attention of some recalcitrant」；她先讲一个事实：住在这里、甚至「to raise a family here」而无危险是可能的，房子确是乡下人所谓「‘haunted’」，但「what forces may or may not be at work here」她「leave that to men of the cloth and to men of learning」，只给一句保证：守三条规则就能幸福；规则一是十点后不给任何人开门，哪怕门外「what seems to you to resemble me」，说得那位老上尉「lowered his gaze」；规则二是忽略顶层的声音——「banging. Or running feet. Or a kind of rustling sound, like the wind through dry leaves」——「You do not investigate and you leave well alone」，「The noises will stop in the end. They always do.」；Fell 太太当即要那间房（理由是整层空着太可惜），Bethlem 断言她会后悔，给出三条现实理由（这里不会有仆人、村里没人肯来、顶层不能动），并说已安排村里姑娘 Susan Naylor 每天早上来待到午餐，「I have promised the girl nothing.」；规则三最要紧：尽量别在这房子里提问——「the asking of questions of any sort」，上尉表示不解，她答「I mean exactly what I say, sir」，Fell 太太问若无意间问了会怎样，她不答，只四下张望、侧耳听楼上与隔壁，「There was only silence」，然后说「the house has a tendency to… answer. Not always. But often.」且「the answers that it gives may not be welcome ones」；上尉与 Fell 太太交换一个眼神，Bethlem 如释重负地说实质内容到此，Fell 太太留她吃饭，她却答「But I have no wish to eat here」，径自出门，末了屋外某处的狗叫了三声，随即归于寂静。
