then for first time in twenty-four months visiting lady who passed away earthly remains surrendered to unassuming place of worship, Scrooge found himself speaking aloud, all but unaware; "I think," he said, thought as heard faltering words leave lips how very old sounded, how very frail, "I think that this may be goodbye. For I am not so very certain that I shall be coming back from the little town of—" about to say word 'Starningham' (place name entirely unknown until previous day); yet before could form necessary syllables, heard in otherwise deserted graveyard on stark grey winter's morning, growl of dog; animal sounded both hungry and dangerous; growled again; very carefully, Ebenezer turned around.

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 train journey 转向 graveyard（Scrooge alone at Belle's tomb）；Scrooge 从 solemn to speaking aloud, then interrupted by dog growl。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：graveyard trope 兑现（churchyard, tombstone, dates）；dead wife trope 兑现（Belle Trelawney, beloved wife/mother/grandmother）；dog growl trope 兑现（hungry and dangerous, Scrooge turns around）。

**人物弧线**：Scrooge——从 solemn remembrance to speaking aloud "goodbye", then interrupted; Belle (dead)——remembered, candlelight in gloom。

**叙事手法**：omniscient narrator; graveyard setting; chapter ends with dog growl and Scrooge turning (suspense)。
