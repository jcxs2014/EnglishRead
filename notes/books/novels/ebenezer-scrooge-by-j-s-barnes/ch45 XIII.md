# 45. XIII

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：正午，The Club of Decent-Hearted Men 迎来一批访客。先是医生再度上门，随后是前一日下午那两位绅士来探问是否无恙；再有俱乐部的其他会员，关心他们最负盛名的一位会员的安危。午餐前不久，一名警察"好心地"抽出片刻，溜达到 St James’s Square，以一种懒洋洋的方式讯问秘书与 Charlotte。他被带到 Mr Scrooge 熟睡的形体前，于是一阵摇头、一阵咂嘴、一阵对世界的种种不义指指点点。Charlotte 询问警察，警队能做点什么去找到那个企图杀死 Mr Scrooge 的人；警察的回答听上去极为受伤，他搬出两重理由：自己囊中羞涩，以及这座城市大得惊人——因此找到袭击者恐怕极难。这番小演说毕，他打听起俱乐部厨房里有什么东西，随即被 Charlotte 与 Bentinck 毫不客气地挡了回去。Scrooge 本人把这一切统统睡了过去：他不再说话，也不再动弹，仿佛完全不知道有人来看他。下午早些时候，一名记者闻警方的通报赶来，对 Mr Scrooge 的健康状况提出不得体的问题；这位先生被客气地请了出去，问题被顶了回来。他极不情愿地离开，一路留下不少关于"一家挂着如此名号的机构竟缺乏体恤"之类的酸话。等这一切平息下来已是两点；Charlotte——上午她休息了几个钟头——回到老人身边坐下，在他旁边祈祷，极其专心地听着他每一次呼吸。

**情感弧线位置**：本章弧线为「探病的访客—警察的懒散与推诿—记者的冒犯—一切被 Charlotte 与 Bentinck 挡回—Scrooge 全程沉睡—两点钟的守候」。情绪几乎全在"外部世界的嘈杂"与"内部世界的静默"之间来回：外面是一群人对名人的围观、盘问与消遣，里面只有一个沉睡的老人和听呼吸的女仆。本章是全书节奏上罕见的"过场章"，却用讽刺写尽了社会对一个人的围观式关怀。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现了「被名人光环包围的当事人」这一 trope。反转有三处：其一，来访者中真正关心 Scrooge 的是俱乐部会员与医生，其余皆为消费他的处境者；其二，警察被写成**笑话**——他想找的不是凶手，而是"俱乐部厨房里有什么东西"；其三，本章最锋利的一句在读者预期之外：Scrooge 睡过了所有这一切，"seemed wholly oblivious"——他的生死大事成了别人的谈资，他连旁观的资格都没有。

**人物弧线**：本章 Scrooge 只以"熟睡的形体"在场，作者反复强调他的缺席（「wholly oblivious」）。Charlotte 与 Bentinck 在这一章成为事实上的守门人：他们替一个不能应酬的人挡下所有不对的提问，这正是第 1 章"Scrooge 不会应酬"的另一种形态——如今他连挡的力气都没有。警察是本章唯一的喜剧角色，一句「citing both personal poverty and the alarming size of the city」把伦敦警察的惰性与怨气一并写出。

**叙事手法**：一是**清单式罗列**（医生、两位绅士、会员、警察、记者），作者让访客像潮水一样一波波来，用「short shrift indeed」「rebuffed」「went only reluctantly」这类小评语形成连击；二是**以细节收束全章**：满篇喧哗之后，最后一句只写 Charlotte 坐在旁边祈祷、听每一次呼吸——由喧到寂，由多到一；三是**冷幽默**：「much sucking of teeth」「a lackadaisical fashion」「such a name」。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Noon now, and there have been visitors to The Club of Decent-Hearted Men.

**中文理解**：此刻是正午，而 The Club of Decent-Hearted Men 今日已有访客。

**关键词**：Noon now / there have been visitors / to The Club of Decent-Hearted Men

**为什么这样写**：作者用最短的一句把时间与事件并置：正午、有人来。前一句是钟点，后一句是清单的开始，读者的期待立刻被吊起——"谁来了？来干什么？"——而答案要等下一段才给。

**读者视角提示**：这一章靠"来客名单"推进。

> **原句 2:** First came the doctor again, then the two gentlemen from the afternoon before to see if all was well. Then more members of the Club, concerned about the well-being of one of their most celebrated members. Shortly before lunch, a policeman kindly found a moment to saunter down to St James’s Square, where he interviewed the secretary and Charlotte in a lackadaisical fashion. He was shown the sleeping form of Mr Scrooge, at which there was much shaking of head, much sucking of teeth and much wagging of finger at the iniquities of the world.

**中文理解**：先是医生又来了，然后是前一日下午的那两位绅士，来看看是否一切安好；接着是更多俱乐部成员，关心他们最负盛名的一位会员的安危。午餐前不久，一名警察好心地抽出片刻，溜达到 St James’s Square，在那里以懒洋洋的姿态讯问秘书与 Charlotte。他被带到 Mr Scrooge 熟睡的形体面前，于是有了大把摇头、大把咂嘴，以及大把对世间种种不义的指指点点。

**关键词**：First came the doctor again / then the two gentlemen from the afternoon before / to see if all was well / Then more members of the Club / concerned about the well-being of one of their most celebrated members / Shortly before lunch, a policeman / kindly found a moment to saunter down to St James’s Square / interviewed the secretary and Charlotte in a lackadaisical fashion / He was shown the sleeping form of Mr Scrooge / much shaking of head, much sucking of teeth and much wagging of finger

**为什么这样写**：作者把访客按"由真到假"排成一条轴：医生（真关心）、老友（真关心）、会员（集体关心）、警察（执行公务）。而警察那段写得最有戏——「kindly found a moment to saunter down」暗示他是不情不愿被派来的；「much shaking of head, much sucking of teeth and much wagging of finger at the iniquities of the world」用三个重复的 "much" 把官僚反应写成一段机械动作。

**读者视角提示**：访客按"真心程度"排队，最末是警察。

> **原句 3:** When the maidservant enquired as to whether there was anything which the constabulary might do to find the man who had tried to kill Mr Scrooge, the policeman sounded most affronted in his response, citing both personal poverty and the alarming size of the city as reasons why locating the attacker might prove very difficult. This little speech completed, he made enquiries as to the contents of the Club’s kitchens, only to be given short shrift indeed by Charlotte and Mr Bentinck.

**中文理解**：当女仆询问警察，警队能做些什么来找出那个企图杀死 Mr Scrooge 的人时，警察的回答听上去极为受辱，他援引两重理由：自己囊中羞涩，以及这座城市大得惊人——因此要找到那个袭击者恐怕会非常困难。这番小演说毕，他转而打听起俱乐部厨房里的东西，只得到 Charlotte 与 Bentinck 毫不客气的对待。

**关键词**：When the maidservant enquired / whether there was anything which the constabulary might do / to find the man who had tried to kill Mr Scrooge / the policeman sounded most affronted / citing both personal poverty and the alarming size of the city / reasons why locating the attacker might prove very difficult / he made enquiries as to the contents of the Club’s kitchens / only to be given short shrift indeed by Charlotte and Mr Bentinck

**为什么这样写**：作者让警察的"委屈"成为笑点：他为自己办不了案子而义愤填膺，理由却是没钱加城市太大。而结尾的反转是全段最漂亮的一击——这位追查凶手的警官，在无人能回答时转而去问厨房里有什么菜。两个守门人的反应只有四个字「short shrift indeed」（不予好脸色）。

**读者视角提示**：警察最后关心的是厨房的饭菜。

> **原句 4:** Scrooge himself slept through all of this. He did not speak or stir again and seemed wholly oblivious to all who came to see him. In the early afternoon a journalist arrived, asking impertinent questions about the health of Mr Scrooge, having been informed of the situation by the policeman. This gentleman was asked politely to leave, and his questions were rebuffed. He went only reluctantly with a good deal of rather sour commentary on the lack of decent-hearted treatment in an establishment which bore such a name.

**中文理解**：Scrooge 本人把这一切统统睡了过去。他不再说话，也不再动弹，似乎对所有前来看他的人完全无知。下午早些时候，一名记者到了——他是从警察那里听说此事的——对 Mr Scrooge 的健康状况提出不得体的问题。这位先生被客气地请了出去，他的提问被顶了回去。他极不情愿地走了，一路留下不少关于"一家挂着如此名号的机构，在体恤方面竟如此欠缺"的酸话。

**关键词**：Scrooge himself slept through all of this / did not speak or stir again / seemed wholly oblivious to all who came to see him / In the early afternoon a journalist arrived / asking impertinent questions about the health of Mr Scrooge / having been informed of the situation by the policeman / This gentleman was asked politely to leave / and his questions were rebuffed / He went only reluctantly / a good deal of rather sour commentary / on the lack of decent-hearted treatment in an establishment which bore such a name

**为什么这样写**：作者用记者的名字（"decent-hearted treatment… which bore such a name"）做了一次冷峻的反讽——这机构以"心肠正直"为名，而它正在被媒体指责"不够心肠正直"。而「wholly oblivious」把 Scrooge 从本章的主角位置上抹去：全章都在谈他，他却在被谈论之外，读者由此感到一种被全世界消费却无人真正认识他的孤独。

**读者视角提示**：记者讽刺"心肠正直"的招牌，本身就在打脸的机构脸上。

> **原句 5:** It was two o’clock by the time that all of this had died down, and Charlotte – who had taken a few hours’ rest that morning – went back to the side of the old man. She sat and prayed beside him, listening, with terrible care, to his every breath.

**中文理解**：等这一切平息下来已经是两点钟；Charlotte——那天上午她休息了几个钟头——回到老人身边坐下，在他旁边祈祷，极其专心地听着他每一次呼吸。

**关键词**：It was two o’clock by the time that all of this had died down / Charlotte – who had taken a few hours’ rest that morning / went back to the side of the old man / She sat and prayed beside him / listening, with terrible care, to his every breath

**为什么这样写**：作者用「all of this had died down」——潮水退去的比喻——收掉整章的喧闹，再把镜头推回到一个人身上。而「with terrible care」四个字是全章的分量所在：她听的是每一次呼吸，不是心跳。作者把第 43 章那个"听呼吸辨死兆"的场景原样重演，形成跨章的呼应。

**读者视角提示**：全章喧哗之后，只剩她听一个人的呼吸。

## 一句话总结**：正午，The Club of Decent-Hearted Men 已有访客：先是医生再来，然后是前一日下午那两位绅士「to see if all was well」，再是俱乐部会员「concerned about the well-being of one of their most celebrated members」；午餐前不久一名警察「kindly found a moment to saunter down to St James’s Square」，「interviewed the secretary and Charlotte in a lackadaisical fashion」，看到 Mr Scrooge 熟睡的形体便「much shaking of head, much sucking of teeth and much wagging of finger at the iniquities of the world」；Charlotte 问警队能否找出那个「had tried to kill Mr Scrooge」的人，警察「sounded most affronted」，搬出「both personal poverty and the alarming size of the city」，说完便去打听「the contents of the Club’s kitchens」，只「given short shrift indeed by Charlotte and Mr Bentinck」；Scrooge 本人「slept through all of this」「seemed wholly oblivious to all who came to see him」；下午早些时候一名记者（消息来自警察）就其健康状况提出「impertinent questions」，被「asked politely to leave」「questions were rebuffed」，临走留下「rather sour commentary on the lack of decent-hearted treatment in an establishment which bore such a name」；待「all of this had died down」已是两点，Charlotte 上午歇过几个钟头，此时回到老人身边「sat and prayed beside him, listening, with terrible care, to his every breath」。
