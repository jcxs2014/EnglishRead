# 04. IV

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Scrooge 承诺尽力协助 Mrs Fell；追问 house 位置与 childhood experience，Mrs Fell 给出 Brightwell Road 25, Starningham（East Anglia 海岸）但以 tired 为由 deferred childhood experience 的讲述；Scrooge 说需至 morning 才能 determine best way forward，并 mention specialist "Giles"；Mrs Fell 询问 Scrooge 是否会 accompany 她去 house，Scrooge 说 first conduct researches；Ambrose 对 "Giles" snorted in irritation；Scrooge 提供 Club guest room 与 kitchens/cellars；Mrs Fell 休息；Scrooge 与 Ambrose 独处，Ambrose 问 "What do you make of her?"，Scrooge 说 "She is afraid... tangled business"，并 sense "A presence... growing steadily closer. The unmistakeable presence of evil."

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 Scrooge 的 brisk determination（man of business）过渡到 Mrs Fell 的 anxiety 与 deferral，再到 Scrooge 与 Ambrose 的 private assessment——Scrooge 从 reassurance 转为 sensing evil 的 pivot，把 narrative 从 social realism 推向 supernatural thriller。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：haunted house investigation trope 兑现（research specialist Giles、location named Brightwell Road/Starningham）；"evil presence" trope 在章末 revelation 兑现；Scrooge 的 "door forever open"（ch02）在此具象化为 Club guest room。

**人物弧线**：Scrooge——从 brisk determination 到 sensing evil 的 pivot，显示 his mission 从 charity 转向 supernatural confrontation；Ambrose——对 Giles 的 snort 与 "What do you make of her?" 的 question 显示 his skepticism 与 concern；Mrs Fell——从 anxiety 到 rest，deferred childhood experience 为后文 revelation 做 setup。

**叙事手法**：third-person frame with embedded dialogue；"we need not linger" 的 narrative ellipsis 压缩 Mrs Fell's departure；Scrooge/Ambrose 的 private exchange 是 chapter 的 thematic core（assessment + evil presence）；"presence... growing steadily closer" 是 supernatural foreshadowing。
