# 06. VI

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Scrooge 与 Ambrose 步行至 Euston Station 附近的一座 railway bridge，去拜访 Giles；bridge 附近 "thick gloom and thicker cobwebs"；Giles 的声音 "thin and rather wheedling... taint of madness"；Scrooge 以 small bag of coins 交换信息；Scrooge 提及 "young lady and a will and a house in Starningham upon Brightwell Road" 与 haunting；Giles 说 "bring me my money and let me tell you what I can"；chapter 以 Scrooge 与 Ambrose 走入黑暗收尾。

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 street journey（rain, bridge）过渡到 gothic encounter（darkness, Giles's mad voice），把 narrative 从 social realism 推向 supernatural thriller；Giles 作为 "lion brought low" 是 Scrooge's past contact 的 redemption mirror。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：gothic bridge/underworld trope 兑现（"thick gloom and thicker cobwebs"、rats、vermin）；mad oracle trope（Giles's "taint of madness"）；coin-for-information transaction trope（Scrooge's bag of coins）。

**人物弧线**：Scrooge——从 "lion brought low" 的 characterization of Giles 到 coin transaction，显示 his resourcefulness；Ambrose——reluctant companion，"I know just what this Giles man is"，显示 his skepticism 与 concern；Giles——mad oracle，"thin and rather wheedling" voice，把 knowledge 重新定义为 commodity。

**叙事手法**：third-person with narrator intrusion（"there we shall, for the time being, leave them"）；Shakespeare quotation（"What a noble mind is here o'erthrown"）；sensory gothic（darkness, cobwebs, rats, slime）；chapter ends with characters entering darkness（cliffhanger）。
