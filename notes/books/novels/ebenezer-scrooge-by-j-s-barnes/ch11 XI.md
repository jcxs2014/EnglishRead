# 11. XI

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Captain Ambrose 于 well beyond business hours 拜访 Cleeford, Crawlin and Cadge 律师事务所（109 Chancery Lane），发现办公室 low and dingy；derelict woman 警告 "things ain't what they seem in there"；young Edwin Cadge 开门，透露其父 Walter Cadge 与母亲同住，Edwin 表现 shame 并警告 Ambrose "you'll think you've stumbled into bedlam"。

**情感弧线位置**：chapter 从 Ambrose 的 solo investigation（lawyer's office）过渡到 Edwin Cadge 的 cryptic warning，把 narrative 从 séance（ch10）转回 investigation；Edwin 的 shame 与 "bedlam" 预告 Cadge 家庭的异常。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：gothic office trope 兑现（low, dingy, dereliction）；madwoman-as-prophet trope 兑现（derelict woman's warning）；young clerk as informant trope 兑现（Edwin Cadge）。

**人物弧线**：Ambrose——从 military discipline 到 accepting cryptic warnings, 显示 his growing openness to the uncanny；Edwin Cadge——从 nervous clerk 到 shame-faced informant, 显示 his family's hidden secret。

**叙事手法**：third-person close on Ambrose；sensory gothic（soot-streamed window, candle, mice, cobwebs, shadows）；dialogue-driven investigation；chapter ends with Edwin's "bedlam" warning（cliffhanger）。

第十一章：Captain Ambrose 于 well beyond business hours 拜访 Cleeford, Crawlin and Cadge 律师事务所（109 Chancery Lane），发现办公室 low and dingy；derelict woman 警告 "things ain't what they seem in there"；young Edwin Cadge 开门，透露其父 Walter Cadge 与母亲同住，Edwin 表现 shame 并警告 Ambrose "you'll think you've stumbled into bedlam"。本章省略的细节（Ambrose's military courtesy, Edwin's "All but me have gone home", "Just me and the mice and the cobwebs") 均在读者视角提示中提及。
