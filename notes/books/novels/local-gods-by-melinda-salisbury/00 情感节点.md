# Local Gods 情感节点

## 节点 1：白鸦降临（ch01）

Sylvie 带着父亲的承诺和滑雪卡来到 Pine Ridge Hollow。她重遇 Chantry，重新建立社交圈。但第一只白鸦落在她肩上的瞬间，她意识到小镇的异常不是想象——West Woods 的动物会进城，而每当它们这样做，就会有坏事发生。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 1:** "Bad things happen when West Woods animals come into town. It means something's coming."

> **原句 2:** "It's not 'just a crow' though, is it? It's a West Woods crow. They're not supposed to come into town."

---

## 节点 2：Divine Interventions 的诱惑（ch05-ch08）

Zelda Munro——一个经营灵性商店的女人——成为 Sylvia 的 mentor 和庇护者。Zelda 的异象、盐灯、烟雾熏黄的玻璃和"神圣干预"的哲学为 Sylvia 打开了一扇通往神秘学的大门。但同一时间，Kenny Johnson 向她透露了自己的恐惧：他相信 Illican 是真实的，而 West Woods 正在变得活跃。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 3:** "Bad things happen when West Woods animals come into town. It means something's coming."

> **原句 4:** "The store is tenebrous, lit entirely by Himalayan salt lamps and candles flickering behind smoke-stained glass."

---

## 节点 3：头发变白（ch15-ch18）

Sylvie 开始经历异常——她的头发变白，她能在梦中进入森林，她看到 Illican 的身影。她以为这是成长的阵痛，是小镇的传说在应验。她不知道的是，West Woods 正在选择她。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 5:** "Her hair has gone white."

> **原句 6:** "Sylvie bites back a laugh, because it's entirely sinister; it's the West Woods claiming her, sucking her life force out or whatever it is they do to the things that live in there."

---

## 节点 4：建镇真相（ch25）

Sylvie 在逃离前偶遇 Chantry，被带去见 Mayor James。Mayor James 在止痛药的作用下吐露了建镇真相：Miller 家族用 blood ritual 召唤并控制 Illican，每次 Illican 醒来都需要 human sacrifice 来安抚。Pine Ridge Hollow 的繁荣建立在谋杀之上——James Johnson 被冤枉入狱，他的女儿 Alicia 被 Miller women 作为祭品杀死。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 7:** "I mean, in the story they're called the Fishers, but it's obviously supposed to be them."

> **原句 8:** "They think they have a god. Their own personal god who smooths the way for them whenever they call it, like a familiar sent to do their bidding."

---

## 节点 5：读到 The Illykan（ch26）

Sylvie 冲去 Kenny 的熟食店读到了 James Johnson 用生命写下的真相——Illican 是被 Miller women 用 blood ritual 召唤的，每次醒来都需要 human sacrifice 来安抚。她还没来得及消化这些，Zelda 就在 Kenny 的厨房里给她注射了镇静剂。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 9:** "It tastes like ash to her."

> **原句 10:** "What she brought forth was the Illykan."

> **原句 11:** "There is a sharp sting in her arm—something cold entering her."

---

## 节点 6：Zelda 身份揭露（ch27）

Sylvie 醒来发现自己被绑在 Kenny 的客厅里，Zelda 暴露了她的 Miller-Munro 身份和 sacrifice 计划——整个镇都被 Abigail 和 Zelda 说服参与明天的 sacrifice 来让 Illican 重新沉睡，而 Sylvia 就是祭品。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 12:** "My full name is Zelda Miller-Munro. I may not have been born and raised in Pine Ridge Hollow, but I'm still a Miller."

> **原句 13:** "We needed you to stay. You said you were going to leave."

---

## 节点 7：Mill Memorial Park（ch29）

Sylvie 被推向 Mill Memorial Park——全镇戴着动物面具围观。Abigail 说 "Thank you for coming"，Haydn Miller 拒绝执行 sacrifice，但 Zelda 动手刺向 Sylvia 的喉咙。Sylvie dies。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 14:** "Thank you for coming."

> **原句 15:** "They hate her that much."

> **原句 16:** "She dies."

---

## 节点 8：重生为森林（ch30）

Sylvie 从献祭之死中重生为西森林本体。她感觉爆炸式铺陈把"变形"外化为感官体验——她能感受到每只鹿、每只猫头鹰、每棵树。她以"森林新主人"的平静语气烧遍小镇，Zelda 亲口揭露献祭是她"真实计划"。Sylvie 选择不亲手杀 Zelda 而让鹿完成处决。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 17:** "A deer is trying to eat Sylvie when she wakes up."

> **原句 18:** "She's all wrong."

> **原句 19:** "She's not done burning things down."

---

## 节点 9：Illican 的告白（ch31）

Illican 在站点用第一人称告白"我曾经是无时无刻不存在的森林本体，被绑定成单个形态让我愤怒"。Sylvie 意识到森林从一年前就选了她。Illican 用 "sylvan"（意为森林的）这个词向她告别并化作一千朵白花。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 20:** "I tried to warn you," he says. "I told you to leave."

> **原句 21:** "Now you can hear me."

> **原句 22:** "If you'd left town, then I'd still be bound. I needed you to die so I could die too."

> **原句 23:** "Goodbye, sylvan Sylvie."

---

## 节点 10：雪在森林里开始下（ch31）

几周后 Chantry 用 "Highlander: there can be only one" 的流行文化梗解构整场悲剧。Sylvie 长出鹿角，把 Haydn 的项链送给 Chantry 作为"森林的钥匙"，最后她笑起来——雪就在她的森林里开始下，全书以最反常理的温柔句号结束。

**关键引语：**

> **原句 24:** "Here she is home. Here she is always welcome. Here she is adored and cherished, and the wood will always take care of her."

> **原句 25:** "The woods are calling Sylvie home."
