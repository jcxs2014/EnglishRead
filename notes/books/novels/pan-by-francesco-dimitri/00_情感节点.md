# Pan by Francesco Dimitri — 情感节点（10 个）

> 节点按全书时间顺序排列，跨章合并；每节点引语 2–3 句，逐字取自 `text/chNN_*.txt`。

## 节点一：罗马的夜与孩子的梦（ch01）

> "At night in Rome the children sleep, and they dream. They dream of things that exist, of things that don’t and never will, and of other things, whose nature is more ambiguous."（ch01）

> "“Tell them he’s coming back,” they whisper. Nothing else. Then they turn away, followed by the children."（ch01）

**情感定位**：全书从睡眠开始：孩子在梦里去往别的 Aspects，醒来什么都不记得；同一夜，街上有人低声传话「告诉他们他要回来了」。两条线并置，把「神话」与「日常」焊在一起。

## 节点二：家道、父亲的病与最后一次晚餐（ch02–ch04）

> "And the triumph is swept away by guilt, as he thinks how this morning has cost Mama even that little token of love, and all he can do is brag that he’s right, Papa doesn’t recognise him, yay. Michele would like to apologise, but doesn’t, because you have to be strong around parents, never show weakness."（ch02）

> "Bit by bit as Michele grew, Stefano got worse. The illness had begun with a series of small strokes that no one recognised for what they were: the only symptom had been a few headaches. Then the strokes got bigger, along with Michele, destroying larger and larger portions of Stefano’s brain—as if his youngest son was a monster feeding on his father’s life."（ch04）

> "It’s the last evening of their old life."（ch04）

**情感定位**：Renzo 一家在贫困里挣扎，Michele 的父亲 Stefano 一天天坏下去，母亲 Angela 撑着一个正在塌的家。第四章末，一家人在旧生活最后一顿晚饭前坐着——此后一切都不再是原来的样子。

## 节点三：神归来的征兆（ch05–ch10）

> "The truth is in the children. Literally. Augusto Dal Mare has opened up two of them, but he needs the third to obtain the answers he seeks. He lines up the bodies to read their innards, blessing the wisdom of the ancient soothsayers. The pale flesh is parchment, the tracks within it, words. Today they speak of war and revenge."（ch05）

> "His signs appear on every wall; every street is talking of him. He was gone, far off, exiled by asphalt and disenchantment, but that’s the beauty of the world: what is ancient is reborn, created afresh in new forms. The old stories return to Flesh."（ch10）

> "“He’s coming back,” says Irene."（ch10）

**情感定位**：Dal Mare 用孩子的内脏占卜，寻找最强的「血肉锚点」；城里墙上到处是角神的记号；Irene 说「他要回来了」。恐怖与期待同时升高：读者和角色都不知道该盼哪一边赢。

## 节点四：父亲的死（ch11–ch12）

> "“I’ve stopped fighting, Michele. I’m dying.”"（ch11）

> "The dead lose gestures, expressions, the constant movement of the ribcage, lose themselves, lose their souls, you’d have to say."（ch12）

**情感定位**：Michele 在 Enchantment 的岛上与父亲告别，Stefano 说自己不再抵抗；回到 Flesh，父亲死在床上。Michele 从岛上带回来的不只是一个答案，还有一个必须去杀的人。

## 节点五：Angela 的暴力与重生（ch13–ch14）

> "This boy, who is at least ten years younger than her, is raping her. That is all there is to say."（ch14）

> "And something snaps. The world has gone crazy, not her. She is not broken, she is very much herself; and what she is, is angry."（ch14）

**情感定位**：Silvia 死后，Angela 被 Peter 强暴，家被彻底摧毁。她没有碎：她把「疯」判给世界，自己站起来。全书从这一章起，母亲这一线不再是受害者，而是行动者。

## 节点六：间章：十七年的信（ch15）

> "Augusto Dal Mare, my best friend, is Captain Hook. And Captain Hook is not a man, nor a mysterious entity. He is a god."（ch15）

> "It wasn’t any noble sentiment that stopped me. It was lack of courage. How could I kill a man who had been at my side for twenty years?"（ch15）

**情感定位**：1975 到 2012 的十七封信，Stefano 写给未来的自己：他如何在 Nemi 遇到 Irene、如何发现最好的朋友就是 Hook、如何在岩边放过他。全书最大的一次信息倾泻，也是最痛的一次自白。

## 节点七：Lupercalia：全城的那一夜（ch16–ch20）

> "They are the Lost, and they drink to a man they once knew."（ch16）

> "Luisa feels the metal pierce her right eye, scrape against bone."（ch19）

> "“Michele,” it says, “aren’t you going to say hello to your grandfather?”"（ch20）

**情感定位**：鲜花广场的狂欢把整座城卷进去：迷失之子痛饮，Luisa 被箭射穿眼睛而死，Irene 被五支箭钉在座位上。Michele 在同一个夜里失去了母亲这一辈最后的保护者。

## 节点八：Nemi 与「它必须被毁掉」（ch18–ch22）

> "Tradition is like love: when it becomes habit, it means it’s dead."（ch18）

> "It needs to be destroyed."（ch18）

**情感定位**：Irene 带 Michele 看荒废的 Diana 神庙，讲象征、讲传统、讲神如何靠人的体验活着；他立誓杀神。同一趟路上，他还知道了父亲当年为何放过 Dal Mare。

## 节点九：革命前夜与真相（ch25–ch27）

> "And so the Lupercalia ends, with a party still raging and a stinging defeat. The seeds of the end have been planted; it won’t be long until they germinate."（ch25）

> "Papa’s Alzheimer’s began as a series of small strokes. That’s how Dal Mare must have killed him: he tortured him night after night, every time that Papa didn’t have lucid dreams. Night after night he stole his brain away, he harvested it one cluster of neurons at a time."（ch27）

**情感定位**：Lupercalia 的失败没有终止战争，只把它推进到「革命」：贴纸、连锁邮件、全城动员。Michele 在 Dream 里亲眼看见父亲被一点点偷走脑子的过程——复仇有了画面。

## 节点十：结局：神死、人被关、日子继续（ch29–ch30）

> "“I don’t believe in your weapons, Greyface,” says Michele. “I don’t believe in your strength.” Dal Mare sees the hooked stick disappear too, dissolving into nothing. “I don’t believe in you.” The red coat, the antiquated clothes dissolve too, and the god, in his strong, ancient body, is left naked in front of the boy. “You’re Greyface. You’re like these cars: a cast-off of humanity. My father was sorry for you.” He pauses. “I’m not.”"（ch29）

> "“By the way,” the satyr says, “I invited a few friends over tonight.”"（ch30）

**情感定位**：Michele 用「不信」在 Dream 里剥掉 Hook 的神形，把他关进血肉；Pan 一度被杀，又被海盗的恐惧重新聚形。尾声是一杯蛋酒和一句「我请了几个朋友今晚过来」——神话收场，生活开局。
