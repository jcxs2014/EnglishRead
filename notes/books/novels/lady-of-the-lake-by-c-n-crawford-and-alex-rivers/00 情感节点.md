# Lady of the Lake 情感节点

## 节点一：婚礼——假婚礼的真心

**叙事概括：** Griflet 的巫师小屋，Nia 穿上 periwinkle 婚纱，在森林中与 Talan 交换戒指。戒指的力量激活她的 Fey 血脉，Talan 的意识短暂流入她脑海。婚礼是假的，戒指的力量是真的。

**关键引语：**
> "Two hearts entwined, two fates aligned, with these words, to thee I bind."

> "I will bring you back to the castle. I will make sure that you are safe. But then, my princess, I'm afraid I have a traitor to find."

## 节点二：蛇怪追杀——身体先于理智

**叙事概括：** 森林中蛇怪追杀，Talan 抱着 Nia 跳入冰河逃生。在生死关头，Nia 第一次承认她在他身边感到安全，也第一次问自己"为什么我要救这个我要杀的人"。

**关键引语：**
> "I almost feel safe with him, but the basilisk shrieks again, and my muscles freeze."

> "Why do I want to save this man I'm trying to kill?"

## 节点三：You didn't leave——双向确认

**叙事概括：** 狩猎小屋外，Nia 违背 Talan 的命令冲出去用 Sentinel 之力击碎蛇怪的魔法屏障，Talan 趁机击杀蛇怪。战后他看着她说"You didn't leave"——这是他们第一次确认彼此的在意。

**关键引语：**
> "You didn't leave."

> "Don't fucking ask me why. I don't know, either."

## 节点四：梦境真相——他替父背锅

**叙事概括：** Nia 在 Talan 的梦境中看到他的核心创伤：被活埋、被囚禁、替父亲背下所有罪名。她发现他根本不是屠夫，而是被误解的保护者。她对他的理解从此不同。

**关键引语：**
> "My father has arranged stone slabs for disemboweling prisoners, racks for stretching them, horses for pulling them apart. But it's my name above them all…Prince Talan."

> "In the hollows of my mind, I hear a voice, the one that has kept me company these past few years. The voice I crave for companionship."

## 节点五：暗杀之夜——被自己人背叛

**叙事概括：** 暗杀之夜，Nia 用开窗当信号让刺客进入，却在最后时刻决定救 Talan。爆炸揭示真相：窗户根本不是入口，是"她在房间"的引爆信号。Avalon Tower 从未打算让她活着。她选择叛逃。

**关键引语：**
> "Right now, it's clear as the moon in the sky that I'm not going to let them kill Talan."

> "The assassins never needed me to open the window to let them in. The window was a signal that I was in the room with him."

## 节点六：Stop fucking lying——真相揭露

**叙事概括：** 在 Kahedin 的梦境中，Nia 的幻术失效，Talan 第一次看到她的真实模样——人类。他识破她是 demi-Fey。"Stop fucking lying"是谎言的终结，也是他们关系的转折。

**关键引语：**
> "When entering a dream, we reflexively shape our appearance in the way we see ourselves."

> "It would be nice if you stopped fucking lying."

## 节点七：肉搏战——身份大揭露

**叙事概括：** 真相败露后，Nia 与 Talan 展开残酷肉搏。战斗中她宣布自己是 Morgan 的后裔、Lady of the Lake、王位继承人。他最终用藤蔓绑住她——但放她离开。

**关键引语：**
> "I'm the real heir to the fucking Fey throne. It's not yours. It's mine. I'm the Lady of the Lake. I'm the heir of Morgan."

> "I was the voice. All those years. The voice in your head."

## 节点八：放她走——最苦涩的告白

**叙事概括：** Talan 用"赦免一条命"的承诺放 Nia 离开，让她骑 Tarasque 回 Camelot。他宁愿在战场相遇，也不愿看她死。Nia 在飞行中明白：他从未打算杀她。

**关键引语：**
> "And truthfully, Nia, I'd rather meet you on the battlefield someday than watch you die here."

> "I was the voice," I call out. "All those years. The voice in your head."

## 节点九：重逢与和解——英语告白

**叙事概括：** Nia 从传送门回到 Brocéliande，发现 Talan 没有设防、没有揭发她。两人重逢，她用英语说出全部真相，他回应"Speak to me in English. I want to hear how you sound."——谎言的尽头是真爱。

**关键引语：**
> "I will never stop thinking about you. Even when the stars burn out, you'll still be in my head because you were the only thing that ever made me feel like I wasn't completely alone."

> "I don't just want you. I need you. Without you, I'm not whole."

## 节点十：Serana 之死——胜利的代价

**叙事概括：** 内战中，Serana 为团队断后被 Tarquin 的弩箭射穿喉咙。Nia 怒杀 Tarquin 复仇。Serana 的死提醒所有人：和平的代价是牺牲。

**关键引语：**
> "A bolt shatters everything in the next second. A thud, sharp and sickening, and a crossbow bolt slams into Serana's throat."

> "Don't let her die for nothing. End this."

## 节点十一：和平与永恒——琥珀中的完美

**叙事概括：** 终章，Nia 在 Avalon 庆祝生日，为 Serana 和 Viviane 举杯。Talan 放弃王位选择她。漂泊者终于找到家园——"A perfection encased in amber."

**关键引语：**
> "I've been rootless my whole life... But here? Here, I'm finally given the gift of stillness."

> "I don't need the throne. Just you and Tarasque."
