# 10. Chapter Ten

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Emma 在办公室处理 Arthur 的 Guardian 危机（Linda 不接电话、Hayley 要 statement），回家撞见 Miles 与 Poppy 吵架（Poppy quit Dance、"Don't tell?"），Linda 回电 "will not bend to this wokery"，Miles 躺床上 upset，Emma 去 committee meeting，Dan 在水槽问 "Didn't you hear me call you?"。
- **情感弧线位置**：present-day 危机的 deepening（professional crisis + family crisis + marriage crisis 三线并进；Miles 的 "Don't tell?" 是 loop 的 hidden thread）
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：Miles's anger as anomaly (he's "the most placid child"); "Don't tell?" as secret hook; committee meeting as Emma's escape from family
- **人物弧线**：Emma 从 "I desperately needed to get back to work" 到 "making promises to his back"; Miles 从 "most placid" to yelling; Dan 从 "called me down for tea" to "Didn't you hear me call you?"
- **叙事手法**：close third (Emma); intercut office/home/committee; "Don't tell?" as the chapter's unanswered question; the committee meeting as Emma's avoidance mechanism

## 精读

> **原句 1:** “By the time I’d left the room Hattie had rung off. Settling into the office on the first floor, I fired up my laptop, wishing I’d thought to make myself a cup of tea.”
**中文理解**：Emma 离开 Miles 房间时 Hattie 已挂电话；她坐进一楼办公室，打开 laptop， wishing 她想到了泡茶。
**关键词**：fired up my laptop / wishing I’d thought to make myself a cup of tea
**为什么这样写**：fired up my laptop = Emma's immediate pivot to work—she's already leaving Miles's crisis behind. "wishing I'd thought to make myself a cup of tea" is her self-care deferral—she'll tend to herself later (she never does). The transition from Miles's yelling to office work shows Emma's compartmentalization: family crisis → professional crisis, no pause. This is the "dragging herself back from another place" pattern in action.
**读者视角提示**：fired up =「打开、启动」；Hattie rung off = Emma missed another connection; the tea wish is never fulfilled.

> **原句 2:** “Another agent was asking if I was OK, if I’d seen Twitter. I immediately abandoned the drawer and logged in, searching for the agency handle.”
**中文理解**：另一个 agent 问 Emma 是否 OK、是否看过 Twitter；她立刻放弃抽屉、登录、搜索 agency handle。
**关键词**：immediately abandoned / searching for the agency handle
**为什么这样写**：immediately abandoned shows Emma's priority: professional crisis over personal (she was looking for paper for Dan's letter). "searching for the agency handle" = she's monitoring her public reputation. "A stream of angry responses, many retweeting the incredibly damaging Guardian piece" = the crisis is escalating. "I had to sort this, Arthur was Linda's client but this was our agency and we were, rightly, getting dragged for our lack of response" = Emma's moral stance (they deserve criticism) vs her professional obligation (sort it).
**读者视角提示**：abandoned =「放弃」；agency handle =「机构账号」；"rightly getting dragged" = Emma's self-awareness about the agency's complicity.

> **原句 3:** “Opening my office door, I realized they were coming from his room next door, the wood decorated with remnants of some rocket stickers he removed last year as being 'too babyish' (I cried). I stepped forward, craning to listen. My eyebrows shot up—Miles was actually yelling.”
**中文理解**：Emma 开门，意识到声音来自 Miles 房间；她 stepped forward, craning to listen；眉毛扬起——Miles 真的在 yelling。
**关键词**：craning to listen / My eyebrows shot up
**为什么这样写**：craning to listen = Emma eavesdropping on her own children—she's spying, not parenting. "My eyebrows shot up" = surprise; Miles yelling is an anomaly ("Miles didn't get angry; he was the most placid child I'd ever known"). The fire doors ("Why did we install fire doors? They made it so much harder to hear things.") are Emma's complaint about barriers—she wants to hear, to know, but the doors (and Miles) block her. This is the novel's theme: Emma wants to know but can't reach.
**读者视角提示**：craning =「伸长脖子」；"most placid child" = Miles's baseline; the fire doors are both literal and metaphorical barriers.

> **原句 4:** “Don’t tell?”
**中文理解**："别告诉？"
**关键词**：Don’t tell?
**为什么这样写**：Poppy's "Don't tell, OK?" is the chapter's unanswered question—what secret are the children hiding? "Don't tell?" (Emma's repetition, with question mark) shows her registering the phrase but not pursuing it. This is the novel's hidden thread: Miles's secret, the "something wrong" that Emma senses but doesn't investigate. The question mark transforms Poppy's command ("Don't tell") into Emma's uncertainty. This will become the loop's central mystery.
**读者视角提示**：Don't tell? =「别告诉？」；this is the chapter's hook—what are the children hiding? Emma's failure to pursue it is her pattern: she notes but doesn't act.

> **原句 5:** “'Arthur did not appreciate being blind-sided in that manner. And he will not bend to this wokery.'”
**中文理解**：Linda："Arthur 不欣赏被这样 blind-sided。他不会屈服于这种 wokery。"
**关键词**：blind-sided / wokery
**为什么这样写**：blind-sided = Linda's framing of Emma's actions (she told Melissa about the bursary without Arthur's consent). "wokery" = Linda's dismissive term for accountability—she frames diversity initiatives as ideological extremism. "And he will not bend" = Arthur's (and Linda's) refusal to adapt. "We will discuss your disappointing response to all of this in our meeting on Friday" = Linda's threat—Emma is in trouble for trying to help. This is the professional crisis's escalation: Emma's lie is not just unraveling, it's being weaponized against her.
**读者视角提示**：blind-sided =「 blindsided」；wokery =「觉醒主义」（蔑称）；Linda's "disappointing response" = Emma is blamed for her own lie.

> **原句 6:** “'OK. Well . . .' I said, aware of the time I simply hadn’t got, the committee meeting any minute, Dan who I needed to talk to too, 'we can talk about it at breakfast, OK? We’ll sort it.'”
**中文理解**："OK. Well . . ." Emma 说，意识到自己 simply hadn't got 的时间，committee meeting any minute，Dan 也得谈，"我们早餐时谈，OK？我们 sorted。"
**关键词**：aware of the time I simply hadn’t got / We’ll sort it
**为什么这样写**："aware of the time I simply hadn't got" = Emma's time-anxiety—she's always running out of time (the loop's temporal pressure). "the committee meeting any minute" = her escape hatch—she'll leave. "Dan who I needed to talk to too" = she knows she should talk to Dan but defers. "We'll sort it" = her promise to Miles, which she'll break (she goes to the committee meeting instead). "He didn't reply" = Miles's silence is his answer—he doesn't believe her.
**读者视角提示**：sort it =「解决了」；Emma's "We'll sort it" is another unfulfilled promise; Miles's silence = his distrust.

> **原句 7:** “Rushing to my bedroom I tried to shrug off my unease, my instinct whispering in my ear that something was really wrong as I rummaged for a Band-Aid to stick over the rub on my heel.”
**中文理解**：Emma 冲向卧室，试图 shrug off unease，instinct whispering something really wrong，翻找 Band-Aid 贴在 heel 的 rub 上。
**关键词**：shrug off my unease / instinct whispering / something was really wrong
**为什么这样写**：shrug off my unease = Emma dismissing her own instinct—she knows something is wrong but chooses to ignore it. "instinct whispering in my ear" = her body knows before her mind does. "something was really wrong" = the novel's central tension—Miles's secret, the loop's hidden thread. "rummaged for a Band-Aid to stick over the rub on my heel" = Emma's physical self-care (Band-Aid on heel) while ignoring the deeper wound (Miles). The Band-Aid is the chapter's metaphor: surface fixes for deep problems.
**读者视角提示**：shrug off =「摆脱、甩掉」；unease =「不安」；rummaged =「翻找」；the heel rub is from ch04 (the cyclist incident)—Emma's body carries the day's damage.

> **原句 8:** “Didn't you hear me call you?” he said, not turning around.
**中文理解**："你没听见我叫你吗？"他没转身。
**关键词**：Didn't you hear me call you?
**为什么这样写**：Dan's question is the chapter's final line—not "How was work?" or "How are the kids?" but "Didn't you hear me call you?" = he called, she didn't hear (or pretended not to). "not turning around" = Dan's back is to her—he's withdrawing. This is the marriage's emotional temperature: Dan is present (washing up, calling) but Emma is elsewhere (phone, committee, "I need to deal with this"). The question is both literal (did you hear me?) and metaphorical (do you hear me at all?).
**读者视角提示**：Didn't you hear me call you? = Dan's question about Emma's presence; "not turning around" = his withdrawal; this mirrors ch02's "Dan . . . ?" (Emma reaching, Dan not responding) but reversed—now Dan reaches, Emma doesn't hear.

## 读者视角提示

- **"Don't tell?"**：Poppy 的 "Don't tell, OK?" 是本章的 unanswered question—Miles 和 Poppy 隐藏着什么？Emma 的 failure to pursue it is her pattern: she notes but doesn't act. This will become the loop's central mystery.
- **"You said you would quit"** (ch08) + "will not bend to this wokery" (ch10)：Emma's professional crisis is escalating—her lie is unraveling, Linda is blaming her, Arthur refuses to commit. The professional thread is tightening.
- **Miles's secret**："something was really wrong" (Emma's instinct) + "Don't tell?" + Miles lying on bed = the novel's hidden thread. Emma promises "We'll sort it at breakfast" but goes to the committee meeting instead—her absence from Miles's crisis is the loop's engine.
- **"Didn't you hear me call you?"**：Dan's question is the chapter's final line—literal (did you hear?) and metaphorical (do you hear me at all?). This mirrors ch02's "Dan . . . ?" (Emma reaching, Dan not responding) but reversed: now Dan reaches, Emma doesn't hear. The loop's emotional throughline.
- **committee meeting as escape**：Emma goes to the committee meeting instead of staying with Miles/Dan—her pattern of escaping into work. "I'll be five minutes, literally" = she's always minimizing her absence.
