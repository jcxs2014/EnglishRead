**读者视角提示**：What. The. Hell. = Emma's shock at the loop's repetition; three separate sentences = staccato shock; this is the loop's second iteration.

**读者视角提示**：Mum's being weird = the family's misreading of Emma's loop-awareness; "She just loves us" = Dan's interpretation; Emma's weirdness is her trying to prevent the loop's tragedy.

## 读者视角提示

- **reality-testing**：Emma's reality tests (heel rub, Miles's room, Sound of Music) are the loop's diagnostic—she's checking if the day has reset. The smooth skin (no heel rub) = the loop has reset Emma's body. "The Sound of Music" was Sunday (not today) = the loop has reset the timeline. This is Emma's first systematic test of the loop.
- **"Please don't go anywhere"**：Emma's first correction attempt—she's trying to prevent Dan's death by keeping him home. But Dan doesn't understand (he's not in the loop). Emma can't tell him why (she can't explain the loop without sounding insane). This is the loop's central tension: Emma knows, Dan doesn't.
- **"Mum's being weird." / "She just loves us."**：the family's misreading of Emma's loop-awareness. Emma's "weirdness" is her love—she's trying to hold onto them because she knows what's coming. Dan reframes her weirdness as love, which is both true and insufficient.
- **committee chat interlude**：same as ch03/ch17—the loop's reset content. Emma is reliving the same day, and the same messages appear. This confirms the loop: same day, same messages, same events.
- **"I do love you"**：Emma's "I do love you" (ch18) echoes ch17's "I love you" (her correction of ch15's "I had ruined it"). The loop is giving Emma a second chance to say the right thing. But she'll forget again (the loop resets).
- **loop structure**：ch17 (loop reset, Emma wakes, "Again.") → ch18 (loop iteration #2, Emma tests reality, tries to prevent Dan's death). The loop is giving Emma another chance. But she can't prevent it (she goes to work, Dan goes out). The loop will repeat.
