"Despite barely looking at my mobile I couldn't help glancing at the messages and missed calls." = Emma's compulsion—she checks her phone. "Any mention of her name from the kids, or Dan, sent a stab of pain through my stomach that never lessened." = Emma's pain—Hattie's name causes pain. "The questions didn't go away either; in fact, they seemed to multiply over time." = Emma's questions—she has more questions. "When had she got in her car? Why was she coming to our house? How had she not seen him?" = Emma's questions—she questions Hattie's actions. "The dreaded bang in the street still marked the moment Dan died and some days I would feel a red-hot anger fire through me at the sound." = Emma's anger—she feels anger at the sound. "She had done this. She had killed him. She had run him down repeatedly." = Emma's accusation—she blames Hattie. "On other nights the urge to leave the house, to go to her, to check on her, snuck in and I forced those thoughts away." = Emma's conflict—she wants to check on Hattie but forces the thought away. This is the chapter's opening: Emma has rediscovered joy but Hattie still casts a shadow. The "gift" mindset hasn't fully resolved her anger at Hattie.
