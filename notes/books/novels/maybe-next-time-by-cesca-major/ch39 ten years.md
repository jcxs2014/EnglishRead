## 精读

> **原句 1:** “I'd been really good at keeping it a secret—I wanted to surprise you so much because I'm always the avid planner, the slow decision-maker.”
**中文理解**："I'd been really good at keeping it a secret—I wanted to surprise you so much because I'm always the avid planner, the slow decision-maker."
**关键词**：I'd been really good at keeping it a secret / I'm always the avid planner, the slow decision-maker
**为什么这样写**：I'd been really good at keeping it a secret = Dan's pride—he kept the proposal a secret. "I wanted to surprise you so much" = his motivation—he wanted to surprise Emma. "I'm always the avid planner, the slow decision-maker" = Dan's self-description—he's a planner, a slow decision-maker. This is Dan's voice: self-deprecating, warm, honest. "avid planner" = he plans everything. "slow decision-maker" = he takes his time. The proposal was a departure from his usual slowness—he decided and acted.
**读者视角提示**：I'd been really good at keeping it a secret = Dan's pride; "I'm always the avid planner, the slow decision-maker" = his self-description; the proposal was a departure from his usual slowness.

> **原句 2:** “Then I was nervous because you looked particularly amazing, even in the insane sandals with all the ribbony bits up the leg, like a sexy gladiator, and your bright white cotton sundress really showed off your tan.”
**中文理解**：Dan was nervous because Emma looked particularly amazing—even in the insane sandals with all the ribbony bits up the leg, like a sexy gladiator, and her bright white cotton sundress really showed off her tan。
**关键词**：like a sexy gladiator / bright white cotton sundress / really showed off your tan
**为什么这样写**：like a sexy gladiator = Dan's simile—Emma's sandals with ribbony bits look like a gladiator's. "bright white cotton sundress" = Emma's outfit—white, cotton, sundress. "really showed off your tan" = Dan's observation—Emma's tan is visible. This is Dan's gaze: he's admiring Emma's appearance. "I thought my throat would close up with nerves" = Dan's nervousness—he's about to propose. The contrast between his nervousness and Emma's beauty is the scene's tension.
**读者视角提示**：like a sexy gladiator = Dan's simile; "bright white cotton sundress" = Emma's outfit; "I thought my throat would close up with nerves" = Dan's nervousness; the contrast between his nervousness and Emma's beauty.

> **原句 3:** “I really thought the whole thing had been ruined when I saw the rucksack go under. But thank God for my habit of putting things in bags, inside bigger bags—it literally saved the day and I'm so pleased you loved the book.”
**中文理解**：Dan really thought the whole thing had been ruined when he saw the rucksack go under. But thank God for his habit of putting things in bags, inside bigger bags—it literally saved the day and he's so pleased Emma loved the book。
**关键词**：the rucksack go under / putting things in bags, inside bigger bags / it literally saved the day
**为什么这样写**：the rucksack go under = the proposal's crisis—Dan's rucksack (with the ring, the book) fell in the water. "putting things in bags, inside bigger bags" = Dan's habit—he bags things inside bags. "it literally saved the day" = the habit saved the proposal—the ring and book survived. "I'm so pleased you loved the book" = Dan's satisfaction—Emma loved the book (Wind in the Willows, decorated by the kids). This is Dan's humor: the proposal nearly failed because of a rucksack, but his bagging habit saved it.
**读者视角提示**：the rucksack go under = the proposal's crisis; "putting things in bags, inside bigger bags" = Dan's habit saved the proposal; "I'm so pleased you loved the book" = his satisfaction; Dan's humor about the near-disaster.

> **原句 4:** “I know she would have LOVED that you wear it now.”

**关键词**：I know she would have LOVED that you wear it now

"LOVED" (capitalized) = Dan's emphasis—his mother would have been thrilled. "you wear it now" = Emma now wears Mum's ring. This is the letter's emotional core: Mum's ring, passed to Emma.
