# 《Our Wives Under the Sea》情感节点

## 节点一：凌晨三点的电话（ch01-03）

**叙事概括：** 全书的起点不是一场相遇，而是一次接人。中心在凌晨三点打来电话，说 Leah 在停车场；Miri 开车过去，看见她站在那儿，没带行李，像被人匆忙推出来。三个星期之后，这个家已经换了一套日常：早上她吃饭、Leah 不吃；血从牙龈渗出来，用纸巾塞住；楼上邻居的电视声从天花板渗下来；书桌上是写不完的基金申请。Leah 说海是闹钟响着的房子，Miri 听着，听久了明白这些话不是对她说的。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I’m aware, by now, that this kind of talk isn’t really meant for me, but is simply a conversation she can’t help having, the result of questions asked in some closed-off part of her head. “What you have to understand,” she says, “is that things can thrive in unimaginable conditions. All they need is the right sort of skin.”"（ch01）

> ② "Sometimes, if I listen very closely (sometimes, if I stand on a chair), I can make out the show that’s playing upstairs and tune our television to the same channel, which negates the irritation a little. They seem to favour game shows and programmes about people tasked with falling in love with each other in exotic locations for money."（ch03）

> ③ "“I can’t stand this,” Leah says suddenly. She’s been sitting in the corner of the room for upwards of an hour, chewing on the collar of her jumper in an odd, reflexive gesture, like one might gnaw at a hangnail."（ch03）

## 节点二：三周变成六个月（ch05-06）

**叙事概括：** 说好三周，结果消失六个月。中心先说延长期，再说情况复杂，最后不接电话；Miri 在希望与确认死亡之间住了半年——喝酒、做梦、查论坛、把工作搬回家做。她也学会反过来用中心那套话对付中心：被「authorised personnel」挡回的时候，她提议挂断重拨，换一个声音冒充 Leah。

**关键引语：**

> ① "In the fifth month, I began to assume she was dead, which made things both easier and harder. I felt nothing and then utterly kneecapped by it, wanted so desperately to know what her last thought had been and whether it had been about me."（ch13）

> ② "I could put the phone down and call you back and do a different voice, if you liked? I’ll say it’s Leah from the start, that way you won’t get into trouble. You just tell anyone who asks that I’m her and it’s fine, I swear."（ch05）

> ③ "Would you look after me, I found myself wanting to ask and unable to do so, the request tangling back on itself, coming out as would you pass the gravy, would you change the channel, would you look at this. For a long time, it failed to occur to me that I was not, in fact, the only person this could happen to."（ch13）

## 节点三：潜艇开始下沉（ch04-08）

**叙事概括：** 深海那条线从一个失灵的瞬间开始：系统离线，压载水舱不受控制，CO₂ 吸收器偏偏完好无损，于是除了继续往下沉没有别的选择。Leah 用训练压住恐惧——恐慌是氧气的浪费；Matteo 挨个查过引擎、接线盒、压力表，说一切正常；灯灭了，窗外没有地平线，只有自己那扇舷窗的一条边框。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Panic is a misuse of oxygen. The first thing anyone learns in diving is how to breathe. When the console lights went off, I held my breath for a full sixty seconds, considered the wet wing shapes of my lungs."（ch04）

> ② "It was like the dispassionate realisation that one has left the house without first turning a light out – unfortunate but hardly a disaster. I don’t remember thinking we would die, so much as noting that we wouldn’t be able to come back up again. I don’t remember thinking we could fix things, only wondering what would happen next."（ch08）

> ③ "The deep sea is dark, particularly when the lights on your submersible craft have cut out for reasons unknown. I did my best to keep my gaze away from the windows, thought of strange-shaped ocean creatures peering in at the three of us and smiling with all of their teeth."（ch06）

## 节点四：深处的声音与圣徒（ch16-30）

**叙事概括：** 三个人在黑暗里各自找一套东西安放恐惧。Jelka 摆出一尊圣布伦丹的塑料小像，说祈祷就像潜水时有同伴；Matteo 用一圈火把把自己围起来，不许 Leah 进；Leah 什么也不做，只听见。气味先来——烧焦的人肉；然后是贴着船壳碾过去的噪声；再然后是噪声里那个说话的东西。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Praying to a saint would be a gesture of idolatry, Jelka had said, seeming to smirk at herself but carrying on regardless. You pray so that a saint will make an intercession to God on your behalf. Spiritually speaking, it’s like diving with a buddy."（ch16）

> ② "“I read a story once,” he told me, “about a paranormal detective who spent a night in a haunted room, and as long as he was inside this ring he’d created, this ring of protective objects, nothing could get at him.” I asked him if I could come inside the circle and he shook his head at me."（ch28）

> ③ "It’s like there’s something on the inside,” she pressed her thumbs upwards into his temples, “like water condensed on the inside of my brain and I can’t wipe it away. Ghosts don’t speak,” she said, again, the way she had before, “but something is speaking to me.”"（ch30）

## 节点五：母亲的海边房子（ch07-19-33）

**叙事概括：** 母亲最后那段日子住在那栋海边悬崖上的房子里。Miri 想把她搬到别处，理由是天太多，人会被天看疯；母亲拒绝，她也就没有再争。后来母亲被送进临终关怀院，她一个人在那栋房子里收拾了一个周末，把家具一件件搬进自己的公寓，墙上只剩相框留下的深色印子。

**关键引语：**

> ① "When my mother was dying I wanted her moved somewhere other than where she was, wished her away from the wide bright vista that spilled into her room every morning, sending her – as I felt at the time – undeniably mad."（ch07）

> ② "When my mother used to smile, a rarity, the skin at the sides of her mouth rippled back like a stone thrown into water. In physical terms, there is nothing to a smile: twelve to thirteen muscles, give or take. Teeth only optional."（ch19）

> ③ "Later on, I sat in my mother’s chair near the windows and thought about the curious way she sometimes had of speaking to me more freely in bad weather, as though the rattle of sleet against the window panes might have served as a cover for her confidences. I used to think of it this way: with the rain, conversation."（ch33）

## 节点六：婚礼、章鱼与《大白鲨》（ch11-23）

**叙事概括：** 这条线是全书唯一明亮的段落。市政厅停电，二十分钟婚礼，桌上是电子蜡烛；认识是在放《I've Got a Feeling》的酒吧（那首歌后来换成了《Horny》）；第一次过夜看《大白鲨》，为 Hooper 和 Brody 显然相爱争到忘了做爱；还有她十七岁照顾过的巨型太平洋章鱼帕梅拉，和三十岁生日收到的旧水族馆宣传卡。

**关键引语：**

> ① "In the candle-dark, then, we got married, and came out afterwards to gentle rain and no plans for the rest of the day, which felt like a miracle. Leah suggested we find something to eat and walked us over the road to a place that sold burgers, her hand in my hand like something obvious, something grown from the fabric of my own body and pressing out."（ch23）

> ② "When I returned to this story later, I would superimpose an eighteen-year-old me over the top of the girlfriend, scribbling her out and sketching my lines in more permanent ink. In this edited version, we would kiss – eighteen-year-old Leah and me – and then afterwards she’d take me to the octopus room, rap her fingertips on the glass cylinder, and Pamela wouldn’t be dead."（ch11）

> ③ "In one photograph, a teenaged Leah crouches by a tall cylindrical tank containing what she identified to me as a giant Pacific octopus named Pamela. We were pals, she said, in a voice that I thought seemed to strive for offhandedness, did you know they taste with their skin?"（ch11）

## 节点七：Juna 的电话（ch27-29）

**叙事概括：** Jelka 的姐姐打来电话，用姐姐的身份说话。她们在咖啡馆见面，她说妹妹死了：中心先给她互相矛盾的信息，再开始筛她的电话，最后像关了门，只有一封信告诉她 Jelka 在潜水过程中意外死亡、遗体按规程在水下就被排出舱外。她还带着一叠 Leah 写的纸——正是那些纸让她找到这间公寓。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I answer the phone and the caller identifies herself as the sister of Jelka, who was on the craft with Leah when it went down. “I’d love to speak,” she says, “if that wouldn’t be too much trouble. There are some things I think we ought to discuss.”"（ch27）

> ② "Her sister was dead, she told me, and the Centre had at first given her contradictory information, then seemed to start screening her calls, then appeared to close up shop altogether."（ch29）

> ③ "She tells me that the Centre informed her via letter of her sister’s accidental death during the course of the dive and explained that, as was regulation, the body had been expelled from the craft while still submerged."（ch39）

## 节点八：那只眼睛与放手（ch35-40）

**叙事概括：** 全书的高潮不在怪物出场，而在 Miri 承认自己留不住她：眼睛化成水球、腿一条条失去动作、皮肤薄到能看见肺在收放。最后一程是把 Leah 送回海里——母亲那栋空房、加了盐的浴缸、走私者用过的小径、水到胸口，然后手里的重量开始变成水。

**关键引语：**

> ① "In the second before I drop her, I half expect her to sluice through my fingers. She goes down again – a sharp splash, water slopping forwards over the edge of the bathtub and soaking my feet – drops back, her eye on me, her long-drenched bandage slipping sideways, and I realise as I watch her sink that I cannot keep her here."（ch35）

> ② "I remember I saw it – the eye, and then the face beyond the eye, the way it rose up from the chasm below us and kept rising, the way it filled the windows, filled my field of vision, seemed to fill the whole ocean by itself."（ch38）

> ③ "She is looking at me – this now, the last of her – and she is still looking at me when I move my arms to release her, when she melts between my hands and into water, twisting down into the rolling tide."（ch39）
