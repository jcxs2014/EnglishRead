# The Art of Charming a Changeling · 情感节点（10 节点）

> 引语全部逐字取自原书，说话人与章节已逐一回原文核对。

## 节点一：第五十七种损伤（Ch.1）

Florrie 用最温和的溶剂清洗镇馆之作，却在几秒之内溶掉了画中唯一的拱门。她的第一反应不是求救，而是"重新以得体的疏离审视画布"——因为承认失手比失手本身更可怕。整本书的情感线从这个最难堪的时刻开始：一个把规程当成护身符的人，发现护身符没有生效。

- 「As a royal conservator well versed in the fifty-six varieties of painting damage, Florrie Hyverfell felt quite confident she’d just discovered the fifty-seventh.」（叙述，Ch.1）
- 「Not intentionally, mind you, but that wouldn’t make much difference once her blunder was revealed.」（叙述，Ch.1）
- 「“Oh, please don’t cry!” exclaimed a faint, ethereal voice. “If it’s any consolation, that was my least favourite arch in the painting.”」（Vern，Ch.1）

## 节点二：一声安慰与一句谎（Ch.2）

画中人替她卸下了毁画的重担，坦白自己是画家落笔失误生出的换生灵、住在真迹被偷走后留下的赝品里。也正因为在这样一场对话之后，她必须在 Fairy 礼节的压力下编出一个可信的假话，于是脱口说出自己是亲王的妻子——那个她此后要用整本书来拆掉的谎言。

- 「“Nobody notices me unless I allow it,” said Vern. “There’s an entire world in here, and I can hide within it whenever I like. I’ve done that for a lifetime – until now.”」（Vern，Ch.2）
- 「"You know, I might inhabit a forgery, but I make it a habit to remind myself that I’m authentically me, at least."」（Vern，Ch.2）
- 「The best protective lies were those that suggested some false invulnerability in the liar.」（叙述，Ch.2）

## 节点三：没有第三条路（Ch.3）

Mim Glossmith 的小屋里，最坏的消息被平静地说出来：家传珍藏是一幅 Fairy 伪造者留下的替身画，而真迹在没人再去的 Fairy 领地。回家之后，父母对这份差事的骄傲与表姐 Chary 的盘问各夹了她一下，她只能交代半个恋爱谎言。真正让她转向的，是破败街道上一个人待着的那一刻。

- 「“Yes. The Resolute Portrait of Vibrant Lord Vernal Mauve Among the Ruins is a forgery.”」（Mim，Ch.3）
- 「“And that is why I prefer the bookshop,” said Mim. “If I may offer some advice, Florrie – do spend some time in the company of your living acquaintances, rather than personifying paintings.”」（Mim，Ch.3）
- 「“But it’s not truly gone, is it?” asked Florrie. “If it’s simply in the Fairy realms, as you say…”」（Florrie，Ch.3）

## 节点四：从来没有人认真看过（Ch.4）

她重新掀开护布，与 Vern 完成最初几次真正的长谈；也第一次听见一个被关了十年的存在说出最孤独的一句话。与此同时，Prince Ardant 带她走进藏着 My Chorus of Isolation 的秘密书房，把地图与 Fairy token 放在她手心里——她接下时清楚自己是在拿雇主的善意当路费。

- 「“The fact of the matter is that no one else has ever looked at my painting.”」（Vern，Ch.4）
- 「“Even Prince Ardant, who claims to love my painting, can barely give me the briefest glance.”」（Vern，Ch.4）
- 「“Out there, I overhear people; in here, I converse with you. One of those is far more engaging than the other.”」（Vern，Ch.4）

## 节点五：被看见的那张脸（Ch.5）

在放大的镜片下，她第一次看清他的脸、他的酒窝和那双十年没停过的手；他用最轻快的语气讲完了最沉重的事——被光损伤的画、偷听来的社交技巧、把礼貌当成手艺从头学起的一生。然后他说出那间小屋里还有一整个房间，并问她能不能带他一起走。

- 「“By the time I worked up the nerve – and by the time I learned how to move and speak, which did take awfully long – I was already packed up.”」（Vern，Ch.5）
- 「“When I first heard it sung, I took that refrain as a guide. I assumed I, too, should want to live again. At the very least, I suppose I’d like to know if it’s possible.”」（Vern，Ch.5）
- 「“I’ve studied four hundred and thirty-eight produce-based still-lives in my lifetime,” Florrie admitted, “and I have never seen the likes of that.”」（Florrie，Ch.5）

## 节点六：边界上的两个人（Ch.7–8）

独自带着画作上路之后，她在水晶树前交出平生、对花的执念和对触觉的依赖；夜里睡不惯地面，靠一件旧物和自我劝解硬撑。次日她被从山与树中涌出的两道"活颜色"逐滴穿过雨点追上，跪倒在画袋旁昏过去——醒来时已被 Vern 主动放进画中，第一次以真人尺度见到他，也第一次亲手拆掉那句"我是亲王的妻子"。

- 「“I remember every one I’ve heard. For years, they’ve kept me company – all, what…? Three hundred and fifty-one of them, at my most recent estimate.”」（Vern，Ch.7）
- 「“I’m afraid you’ll find there’s nothing worth learning about me,” she warned.」（Florrie，Ch.7）
- 「“I’m the funniest Fairy to say something like this, but here goes: that sounds lonely, Florrie.”」（Vern，Ch.8）

## 节点七：一次迟到的自我介绍（Ch.8–9）

在画中的厨房里，两人交换面包与坦白：她说出亲王并不知道 Vern 的存在，也第一次主动伸手完成一次正式自我介绍——两只手握了很久。回到真实的夜色里，她发现世界刺眼地真实，而自己的手空得令人心碎。此后山路上她收服了一只人形大的蝴蝶，又险些被伪装成画架的巨鸭吞掉，被蝴蝶救下后躲进画中，第一次把手放在他肩上。

- 「His hand didn’t shake when it took hers, though; Vern’s grip was surprisingly firm. “It’s a pleasure to meet you too, Florrie.”」（叙述/Vern，Ch.8）
- 「“Is this correct?” Vern asked, as he raised his hand to her shoulder. … “And yet is good enough for me,” he murmured.」（Vern，Ch.9）
- 「“I feel utterly useless.” … Before she knew what she was doing, Florrie placed her hand on his shoulder.」（Vern，Ch.9）

## 节点八：崩塌与准许（Ch.12）

峡谷石柱下，Lord Mauve 用一句失礼的追问逼她面对自己对 Vern 的心意；回到宫殿后，Vern 的欢快第一次塌了——他承认快乐是自己维系存在的工程，一旦停下，一切散架。她笨拙地准许他难过，两人在耳语中彼此对齐；而她转身时发现鲛丝袋里亲王的那枚 token 不见了，只剩几点颜料。

- 「I must stay busy and keep my head up, because if I stop being cheerful for a moment, everything will fall apart.」（Vern，Ch.12）
- 「“I want you to be miserable,” she said. “That is to say, I would prefer it if you weren’t, obviously. But if you truly are upset, you needn’t hide it.”」（Florrie，Ch.12）
- 「“You were?” Vern whispered. “So was I.”」（Vern，Ch.12）

## 节点九：把那条规则作废（Ch.15）

在童年卧房的画中树冠下，她把 Cousin Chary 的善意如何变成代管、自己如何在一次失手后感到彻底无能全部说出；他则承认自己一直以为他是她的负担。两人最终当场废掉"最脆弱者需被最不脆弱者照料"那条她奉行了半生的规则。

- 「“Meeting your family made me realise what burden I am,” he said.」（Vern，Ch.15）
- 「“The most vulnerable requires careful handling by the least vulnerable, to address the imbalance.”」（Florrie，Ch.15）
- 「Rested. Settled. Restored, as though she were a fixed-up painting herself.」（叙述，Ch.15）

## 节点十：不必离开的理由（Ch.23–24）

画中的第一个清晨从散步走到亲吻，她第一次承认那种"舒适甚至满足"的感觉；而画框之外，Ceru 带着解法剪开一幅伪造静物画取出那枚蛋，蛋落地碎裂。画中随之起了第一场风暴，蜷在破窗里的 Vern 说出那声只有他能听见的尖叫，并承认他"就是这幅画"。她用一句"我爱这幅画"和"它感觉像一个庇护所"给了他不必离开的理由。

- 「Once again, she felt herself struck by the vaguely unfamiliar feeling that might be, Florrie suspected, categorised as comfort, or even contentment.」（叙述，Ch.23）
- 「“The reason why I can’t leave the painting is because it’s part of me.”」（Vern，Ch.24）
- 「“It feels like a refuge.”」（Florrie，Ch.24）
