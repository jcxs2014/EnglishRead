# 情感节点 · A Lie So Pretty and Poisonous

> 16 个节点，按时间线排列。引语逐字取自 text/ 原文并核对说话人。

## 节点一 · 花窗前的服从测试（prologue）

被 Micah 抛弃的 Aurora 抱着六个月大的 Cora 回到母亲的庄园求收留。仆人伸手抱走孩子，母亲抬手示意——这是「条件之爱」的第一次具象化：要庇护，先交出孩子、交出骄傲。

> "If you want my help, Aurora, you will become the proper lady I raised you to be. That starts now."
——母亲（ch00）

> "I slowly and hesitantly placed Cora in the servant's arms before sinking back to the sofa."
——Aurora（ch00）

> "The conditions of her motherly love."
——Aurora 的内心判词（ch00）

## 节点二 · 三天通牒（ch01）

四年的平静被 Terren 的耳语撕碎：监护权判归、银鞘鞭般的期限、还有 Jairus 之名的预告。她在药铺里第一次明白，躲藏是一种租金随时上涨的租约。

> "I will be back in three days' time to collect you and that bastard child."
——Terren（ch01）

> "By the way, Jairus wants you to know he looks forward to having you back."
——Terren（ch01）

## 节点三 · 告别与「选择的母亲」（ch03）

出逃前夜，Greta 把最后的钱、拥抱与身份一起交给她：你不是被抛弃的，你是被选择的。

> "You are the daughter I always wanted. You and Cora are my family. Never forget that a family is not always forged by blood."
——Greta（ch03）

> "Thank you for being the mother to me I always needed and for giving me the skills and knowledge to better myself and provide for Cora."
——Aurora（ch03）

## 节点四 · 屏障吞没母女（ch03 末）

Terren 的马队破林而出，Aurora 决定跳车赴死——货车翻覆、自由落体，落地却永远没有到来。白雾、挤压、听不懂的人声：第一卷的类型换轨在此完成。

> "I love you, sweet girl. I promise I will find you."
——Aurora 对 Cora（ch03）

> "A temporary burst of pain quickly blazed through me as the forest and wagon dissolved into a cloudy haze of whites and greys."
——Aurora（ch03）

## 节点五 · 神殿初见与白光救场（ch04）

穿越后的密林步步耳语，敌意 seraph 把母女当「谈判筹码」——千钧一发时一支来路不明的箭钉穿他心口。Gabriel 的登场定义了「善意」的初始语法。

> "His wide eyes blinked before casting down at the arrow protruding from his heart."
——叙述（ch04）

> "I'm Gabriel. I'm here to help."
——Gabriel（ch04）

## 节点六 · 测谎之痛（ch09）

王座厅里，veracitos 的魔法把「说谎」变成刑罚——她奉命说谎、当场腹痛如绞。真相的价格被公开标出，而她的幸存本身成了疑点。

> "I am thirty," I lied. I doubled over and cried out as intense pain shot through my stomach.（Aurora，ch09）

> "I wonder why you were spared."
——King Torrish（ch09）

## 节点七 · 白焰破面具（ch11）

花园里 Gabriel 误读邀请、把她拽进怀里——积压一生的怒气挣脱贵族面具，她用「自己都不知道的力气」推开王弟。第一次反抗，第一次心惊。

> "The mask of nobility I thought I had fastened tightly chipped and cracked."
——Aurora（ch11）

> "A white flame formed from years of being a target of entitlement."
——Aurora（ch11）

## 节点八 · 银匕首与「我为什么不信你」（ch17–18）

西行查证变成遭遇战：Darragh 被绑树受辱、她违誓来救、一匕首钉穿民兵大腿；重逢的对骂以他生平第一次道歉收场。信任在这个夜晚完成了从「递刀」到「相信」的升级。

> "I had never once had a man genuinely apologize to me. Or thank me. Never in the same breath."
——Aurora（ch17）

> "Why wouldn't I believe you?"
——Darragh（ch18）

## 节点九 · 「At no point is this your fault」（ch19）

Relystone 溪边的袭击与三杀救场之后，Darragh 捧起她的脸，第一次唤她「Rory」，把她一生的受害者有罪论原路退还——并以「教你打架」把恐惧换算成课程。

> "Look at me, sweetheart." My focus snapped to the chestnut eyes mere inches from my face. "They can't hurt you anymore."（Darragh，ch19）

> "At no point is this your fault. It could never be your fault."
——Darragh（ch19）

> "I would consider it an honor. You are not weak, Rory."
——Darragh（ch19）

## 节点十 · 蜂蜜蛋糕（ch14）

衣坊与面包街的「平常日子」插曲：她把奶油酥皮分给衣衫褴褛的 daemonite 幼崽，把蜂蜜蛋糕递给全城最危险的 seraph——他迟疑、收下、搂紧。善意第一次在两个方向同时流动。

> "I hope you like honey cake. You seem like a honey cake kind of guy,"
——Aurora（ch14）

> "I reached for the box, and he quickly pulled it close to him. “Uh…thank you. My lady.”"
——Darragh（ch14）

## 节点十一 · 父亲的戒指（ch26）

刀匠桌上一枚鹿衔叶家纹金戒，把「失踪潮=seraph 掠人」的猜想钉成物证。Gabriel 的「hysterical」与 Darragh 的「Why wouldn't I believe you」在同一章完成信任的重新分配。

> "This was my father's ring, and now it is in Tironaill!"
——Aurora（ch26）

> "Gabriel wants to believe in things that only align with his preconceived beliefs and thoughts."
——Darragh（ch26）

## 节点十二 · 告白被拒（ch29）

深夜间她交出全部脆弱：「I feel a pull to you, damn it!」——他退了一步、留下一句「Goodnight, my lady」走进黑暗。她配了无梦药，梦却照样来了。

> "I need you in my life. Not to get back home or collect information, but because…because I feel a pull to you, damn it! Don’t you feel it?"
——Aurora（ch29）

> "Goodnight, my lady."
——Darragh（ch29）

> "Dreams of heartache, betrayal, lies, and longing."
——Aurora（ch29）

## 节点十三 · 鞭刑与溶解（ch31）

神殿公开赎罪礼上，国王把行刑「荣誉」赐给 Darragh——十记银梢鞭下，她以为看透的人「溶解」了。信任的账面被清空，她决定当晚独自动手。

> "Darragh didn't hesitate and cracked the whip, slicing open Russtoph's tender, exposed flesh."
——叙述（ch31）

> "A reminder that my heart could only ever be a sunken chamber and nothing more."
——Aurora（ch31）

## 节点十四 · 契约与偷书之夜（ch32–33）

王后的滴血契约把沉默标价为「三天无痕之死」；她潜入书房躲进桌底，听到 compeller、elixir 与育种清单——所有伏笔在一夜闭环。真相到手，自由归零。

> "You are not a stupid woman. You have been quite busy wooing my brother-in-law, or should I say, using him."
——Queen Mollah（ch32）

> "I'm a compeller. I can make anyone do anything I want."
——Gabriel（ch33）

## 节点十五 · 地牢的最后恳求（ch33）

Cora 被抱走、自己被扛进地牢——她用最后的尊严换一句口信。Darragh 头一次正眼看她，点头，锁门。

> "Don't let them hurt Cora. Have Anya watch over her. Cora loves Anya."
——Aurora（ch33）

> "Tell Cora I love her more than life itself."
——Aurora（ch33）

## 节点十六 · 越狱与点火（ch34）

地牢四日、狱友替她挨打、越狱爆炸掀开铁门——革命者抱起她，「欢迎加入革命」。第一卷在纵火宣言里收束：她不再逃了。

> "Welcome to the revolution, love."
——Khaitos（ch34）

> "The angheals built the kindling to their own destruction, and now it was time to light a spark and let it all burn."
——Aurora（ch34）
