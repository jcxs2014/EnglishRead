# 《The Splintered Sun》情感节点

## 节点一：客栈的栏杆，仓库的横梁（ch01–ch04）

**叙事概括：** 全书的起点是两次坠落。十岁的 Daley 在首都 Hernysadharc 的客栈屋顶上听见母亲 Ardis 与班主 Yuval 摊牌，接着是栏杆断裂、一声闷哼，母亲摊在院子石板上；他没有哭给谁看，放火烧了剧团的两辆大车，又折回火里把困住的醉矮子 Beppe 拖出来，然后躲进城南的芦苇丛。几十里外的 Garnerhouse，年轻占卜师 Vedra 在富商家里当众看镜失败，换上一面小黑镜后看见六角塔与扑来的蝙蝠，昏倒在地；她被送回破仓库，在横梁上发现一张惨白无声的男孩的脸。她把蛋糕分给他，指尖相触的一刻被拖进大火，醒来后男孩说自己叫 Daley，母亲已经死了。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The balcony railing had broken away, parts of it still hanging in the air, held only by the splintered remains of a few posts, as if it had been destroyed by a single savage kick. Much farther below, splayed unnaturally on the courtyard cobbles like a discarded toy, lay Daley’s mother Ardis."（ch01）

> ② "It was as though some vast hand, some godly grip, had flung her face-first into a bonfire. For a moment she could feel heat, terrible heat, and billowing flames roared up all around her, consuming her, and she plunged into black, burning emptiness."（ch04）

## 节点二：一封信、一枚戒指、一支朝圣队（ch05–ch10）

**叙事概括：** 一封无名信被塞进十五岁王子 Ewain 的门缝，劝他去 Dun Ard 向流放的父亲讨要一枚戒指；他先怀疑送信的神父 Rumon，随后答应暂不做计划，却说自己不会永远等下去。另一头，Vedra 收留了 Daley，带他进第一领主 Phelan 的宅邸；Daley 从厨房徒手爬上塔墙，在窗外听见 Phelan 与黑袍祭司 Lochrin 共读密文信、听见他咒骂 Bellicent，又认出窗内那枚像母亲遗物的红宝石戒指，趁楼下的尖叫把两个男人引走时，把戒指连信一起抽出铁栅，往下爬时又把戒指扔进了墙外的黑夜。两人被卫兵从市场追到屋顶，靠 Pegeen 的三天之诺在一杯热酒里落脚。出城那天，一件不合身的朝圣袍把两个孩子藏进了近百人的队伍；Wormwood 深处，两道火墙夹住队伍，跳火而来的 Flann Alderwood 射倒藏在「修士」车里的弓手，揭出里夫 Hagan 借朝圣队偷运国库银子的机关，把一箱银子送给神庙放众人上路——他转身走开时，Daley 不见了。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The letter was written in ordinary Hernystiri letters, but something about them looked odd to Rumon, though he could not immediately say why that was. “You are in danger if you remain where you are,” he read aloud. “It is time to go to Dun Ard and take the ring from your father.”"（ch05）

> ② "“Not to you,” said Flann, laughing. “No, I fear that Hagan, the First Lord’s reeve in Crannhyr, has used your pilgrimage to cloak a delivery of riches being sent to him from First Lord Phelan’s treasury in Hernysadharc. You see, we have relieved Hagan on several occasions of the money needed to pay his troops, causing him much frustration."（ch10）

## 节点三：一份死刑名单上的收留（ch11–ch14）

**叙事概括：** Daley 被带回了亡命徒的营地。竖琴手 Diarban 在树下捡起男孩掉落的鞋，鞋里那片发白的羊皮纸是别人写给别人的密信，戒指上的盘蛇纹把这封信认到了 Lord Scaeth 名下；一场抢劫因此变成一次押送，两个孩子被扣在队伍里。High Oak Camp 的草地上，Vedra 一边吃下这一路最饱的一顿，一边看清这群「乌鸦」的底细：他们是失了地、背着 First Lord 死刑判决的流亡者，营地的真身藏在巨橡之上。夜里 Mearna 说下一站是 Crannhyr，Tika 说那地方满是女巫，要为她唱护佑的歌。Vedra 也看清自己与 Daley 名义上是客、实际上是等那封信有了处置才走得脱的囚徒——可 Daley 像小狗一样跟着三个水手，比从前任何时候都快活。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Of course,” said Conal, frowning. “We are all of us under the First Lord’s sentence of death. Flann has to know what is going on around us—we all do. Also, we are close to the lands his family once owned before Phelan and Hagan stole them. Flann has hunted in this forest and the marshes beside them all his life, and he also knows the common folk in every town, village, and crofter’s hut for leagues.”"（ch12）

> ② "At least for now, she realized, they were as good as prisoners, since Flann had not decided what to do about the letter Daley had stolen and certainly would not let them go until he had. But Daley himself was happier than she had ever seen him as he followed the three sailors around like a puppy. Vedra knew she could only make the best of the situation."（ch14）

## 节点四：破衫厅的镜子（ch15–ch22）

**叙事概括：** 队伍翻过隘口下到海边，进了城门自开的宅子 Threadbare Hall，Vedra 看着这个一路发号施令的人单膝跪下。房主 Xaniko 把老宅的转手与亡妻的旧事讲了一遍，戒指被认出是 Scaeth 的，密文被解开——信里写着要在 Ewain 东行的路上取他性命。Daley 溜进 Crannhyr，Vedra 瞒着 Flann 与 Xaniko 下山找人，最后在 Mircha 神庙的屋顶下找到捧着两枚死蛋的男孩；Pegeen 提出带她回家，她拒绝了。宅子里，Xaniko 把「留下」定成两个人的条件，Vedra 在走廊里替两人做了决定；Daley 想到独自一人的可怕，拖着脚步走回书房，像被牵往绞架。随后是两堂课：Xaniko 揭开黑布下的无柄凸面镜，说这是巨虫鳞片磨成的 Witness，严禁她擅自触碰；又带她在铜镜里第一次望见海边一个银发黄眼的女子——那是他死了数百年的妻子 Ona。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“You have a greater gift than you know,” he said. “That was my wife Ona that you saw, who has been dead for hundreds of mortal years.”"（ch20）

> ② "“Never touch the Witness without my permission. That is the first lesson and perhaps the most important. You are not ready to use such a powerful, perilous thing. It has many uses, all of them important, and all of them capable of leading the unwary or the overconfident into unhappiness, madness, or death.”"（ch22）

## 节点五：火里的共感与「别告诉 Vedra」（ch23–ch30）

**叙事概括：** Flann 把计划摊开：借 Hagan 引来 Phelan 的兵，让 Phelan 与 Scaeth 彼此缠住，再把 Ewain 偷送回 Hernystir；Diarban 当着全队质问凭什么开始下命令，Flann 只回一句「你还是自由的」，然后自己带队翻山。Daley 在幻视里站在燃烧的院子中央，先听见母亲求救，又听见那声音变成 Vedra；Vedra 在同一场灰烬与焦骨的景象中隔火看见他痛哭，祷告没有得到回答。两人在 Xaniko 书房醒来，主人只肯说男孩碰了她、也许误触了盖着布的 Witness。随后灰袍的 Rumon 与揭下头巾的「priestess」抵达——那是 Prince Ewain。临行前 Xaniko 把一块琥珀交到 Daley 手里，让孩子自己决定要不要用。船离了 Hernystir 的海岸往南走，夜里 Xaniko 在山坡上带 Vedra 用 Witness 联络那个男孩，她听见他断断续续地请求「别告诉 Vedra」；老人只肯说一句：有东西在跟着她、在猎她。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Help us! She prayed without praying to any single thing, the many gods and goddesses she had encountered in her short, strange life now indistinguishable, a compendium of priest’s promises, none of them fulfilled. If you’re ever going to help, help me now!"（ch24）

> ② ". . . Don’t . . . tell Vedra . . . ! There was a note of dismay in Daley’s voice, at least in those words she could catch. It made her heart start to pound again."（ch30）

## 节点六：王座厅里的儿子（ch31–ch38）

**叙事概括：** 南边，Phelan 用产权、亲兵与地窖里的「那些人」把 Hagan 变成手上的一件工具；Hagan 献上一条既能表忠又能恶心 Scaeth 的计策。Scaeth 从一个占卜师含糊的幻象里抠出「Crannhyr 的纹章」四个字，就把整件事读成 Phelan 的插手，读成战争。北边，Diarban 把一队假乐手带进 Dun Ard 的王座厅，趁 Gwynn 那张自己写的邀请函和一场「家乡小曲」，替 Ewain 争一次当面认父的机会；国王 Tethtain 已经胖得走了形，句句要看白鸫 Uatha 的脸色，听完只回一句「你说的不可能，陌生人」「我妻子死了，孩子也死了」。同一段日子里，Flann 把 Vedra 带到营地外的坡地上，摊开自己押上一切的缘由：祖产被夺，妻子 Moirin 死在 Hagan 手里；Xaniko 也在坡地上第一次说起自己的悔——他唯一的儿子死前几年不跟他说话，世上某处有个他不认识的孙女。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“My mother was your wife, Cria of Newyth, who died giving birth to me. Some say that grief over her was the reason you left Hernystir and came here to Dun Ard. Majesty, I am your son!”"（ch36）

> ② "“I was not much of a father, I fear. My only child did not speak to me for years before he died, because we disagreed about . . . about how he chose to live his life. And somewhere in this world I have a grandchild, but I do not know her, nor where she might be.”"（ch37）

## 节点七：断腿的占卜师与六天的囚徒（ch39–ch44）

**叙事概括：** Scaeth 命人砸断占卜师 Vespillis 的双腿，再逼他走梦道去寻 Xaniko；洞里那个自称 Uruzemai 的黑影答应帮忙，条件是他日后随叫随到。Bruch Thir 的火堆旁，Rumon 不劝也不替，只把选择、后果和孤独一并交到 Daley 手里；Daley 承认有东西在找 Vedra，Diarban 却用「Vedra 也会落进 Scaeth 手里」逼他答应日落再试一次。另一边，Mearna 被 Hagan 关在空村舍里六天，用一枚铜币磨出一根木刺准备自救；Hagan 来逼问仙镜的下落，又许她一条活路换 Flann 的住处——她把话顶回去、啐在地上。当夜营地被攻破，挑开她草铺的却是一身红蛇外袍、染黑胡子的 Flann；他拽她出门，穿过火与影，把她从被砍开的栅栏缺口扔出去。

**关键引语：**

> ① "And may those same gods forgive my cruelty in convincing you, he prayed. Great Oymos gave us words. Sometimes we must use them as weapons to save our own lives."（ch41）

> ② "I want to know the whereabouts of Flann Alderwood. I care little about you one way or the other, so if you will tell me where Flann makes his home—homes, I should say, since I hear he moves his band about from place to place—then I will arrange for you to ‘escape’ during the trip to Hernysadharc."（ch44）

## 节点八：银溪边的弓（ch45–ch50）

**叙事概括：** 男孩把琥珀里传来的话带到山上：穿 Glennafar，去 Greenwrack。Rumon 一伙逼出 Gwynn 的私藏——一枚国王封印和一笔银币——凑出一封假公文，租下一条旧平底船，在 Diarban 的歌声里斜着漂过 River Baraillean，钻进别名「蛇谷」的 Glennafar。战事在银溪边烧起来：Flann 与 Tynon 爬上俯瞰大路的高处，他把弓拉满对准走进射程的 Scaeth，却在最后一刻松了手，改射草原兵队长 Hrinvart Firemouth，让失去雇主的雇佣兵自己冲起来。坡上，Daley 从石头上跳下去救 Ewain，一头白色巨犬冲进雇佣兵中间撕开一条血路，Ewain 却被一支来路不明的箭射倒；Flann 顶着假胡子赶到，靠坡上高处接连射来的三支箭才把巨犬放倒。营地一侧，Tika 用石刀剜开 Xaniko 的伤口取出箭头，Mearna 从作呕撑到缝完最后一针，才发现自己满身是血。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“You never ran from anything,” Flann said, holding his bow steady despite the string being stretched to its full tension. But his face was full of doubt. At last, he loosened the draw on the bow a little."（ch48）

> ② "“Oh, no, no. I saw this on the Dream Road.” Vedra was finally able to control her voice. It was as if the dream world and the waking world had suddenly flowed together, as if something she had half-believed to be a nightmare had now taken shape right before her eyes. “I did—I saw it! It came for Daley and the prince.”"（ch49）

## 节点九：山谷、沼泽与「不是小荣誉」（ch51–ch56）

**叙事概括：** 夜里下山这一程靠 Flann 与 Tynon 在最险的崖边扶人；Ach Uafas 的废墟里，Flann 刮掉染色的胡子，只带 Diarban 摸进敌营中央的帐篷，把刀架在年轻骑士 Aidan 的喉上，让 Diarban 弹琴盖住声音，劝他送人回去护住父亲。Greenwrack 的旧栈道上，追兵只剩不到半里格的差距，Flann 在泥丘上把话挑明：打起来只能死得体面。Vedra 借 Amber 与龙晶镜走上梦道，被 Xaniko 的幽影认作孙女 Ela，她顺着这个身份问出过沼泽的走法，出来却觉得自己更像个贼。Liam 抱着一棵三人高的树连根拔起、砸断栈道末端；Flann 又把竖琴手诓上 Chimney Ridge，让他在崖边对谷里的草原雇佣兵唱《Cormach the Lame and the Valley of Death》，用一支老歌把 Sir Aidan 唱了回去，再借岩油与地精把谷中的追兵吞掉。回到 Threadbare Hall 时，内院挤满举火把的家仆，他们不欢呼，而是围上来；Xaniko 活了下来，留下龙鳞镜；王子和 Rumon 扮装回 Cashelrigg；Scaeth 把母亲 Bellicent 赶出队伍。最后，Flann 说两个孩子当然跟他们走，Vedra 问 Daley 是不是真想去，他点头——她说那我们就当 Crows，Mearna 一把抱住她。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I tricked him. And no matter how gratefully Flann and the others congratulated her when she related what Xaniko’s shade had told her, Vedra could not help feeling more like a thief than a savior."（ch52）

> ② "“Scaeth forced my hand,” said Flann, shaking his head. “But I do not like plain murder, even of sell-sword dogs like those. I will add it to Lord Scaeth’s reckoning and pray I may bring him to account one day.”"（ch54）

> ③ "“And as Mearna pointed out, you two can come back to Threadbare Hall whenever you wish. We still have many things we must do with Lord Xaniko in the time ahead. We have had a lucky victory, but our poor land is not any safer than it was, and perhaps is even less so now, though we gave Scaeth, Phelan, and that dog Hagan a shameful drubbing.”"（ch56）
