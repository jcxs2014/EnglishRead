# 《Good Good Loving》金句精选

① Ellen didn’t have the energy to open her eyes, but wasn’t worried about that, because she didn’t want to. What she did mind was not being able to close her ears. It felt very unfair to be so completely mentally alert while she was lying there on her hospital bed trying to await a peaceful passing.（ch01）

**中文**：艾伦连睁开眼的力气都没有了，可她不在意——她本来就不想睁眼。真正让她难受的是耳朵关不上。
**上下文**：ch01 开篇，二〇二〇年一月，她躺在病床上等一个平静的离场。
**为什么重要**：全书第一句就定了她的位置：她从来不是这个家的主角，她只是那个**听得见所有人说话的人**。而她临终前唯一失去不了的能力，就是继续听。
**呼应关系**：与 CJ walked up the path past the people gathered in the garden and towards the front door. On the invitations, the family had asked everyone attending to wear something green, and the request had been embraced to such an extent that, at a quick glance, it was difficult for him to distinguish the mourners from the foliage around them.（ch10）她死后的葬礼同构——活着的时候她听，死了以后所有人为她穿绿色。

② ‘CJ, I want to stay with you,’ he said. Then, because CJ didn’t answer straight away and because he was afraid his son might refuse him in front of everyone, he added, ‘Please,’ and this time, as he spoke, his voice broke.（ch02）

**中文**：「CJ，我想跟你住。」他说。因为儿子没有立刻回答、又怕他当着所有人的面拒绝自己，他补了一句「求你了」——说到这儿，他的声音破了。
**上下文**：ch02 客厅会议，一家人正在决定患四期癌症的父亲该住哪儿。
**为什么重要**：全书唯一一次克莱德开口求人，而他要的不是原谅，是一个住处。作者让他把最重要的那句话说得最不像话——**求一个儿子收留自己，是他这辈子最接近道歉的动作**。
**呼应关系**：与 ‘Just gimme one good reason why I should go see him?’ Leah asked, dropping her spoon into the mug she’d been stirring, her interest in the cup of hot chocolate she was making having evaporated.（ch04）莉亚当桌拒绝去看他形成一组：一个在求，一个在拒绝。

③ ‘People call you Ellen,’ her mother said. ‘You name is Virgie.’（ch04）

**中文**：「人家都叫你艾伦，」她母亲说，「你的名字是 Virgie。」
**上下文**：ch04 大餐之后，艾伦回忆自己名字的来历。
**为什么重要**：全书最短的一句、也是最重的一句。**连名字都不是自己的**——她这一生的身份从第一天起就是别人给的、别人改的。作者把这句话放在一场以「清账」为主题的晚餐之后，用意很直白：她算了一辈子别人的账，自己的账从名字开始就欠着。
**呼应关系**：与 Leah took a step closer to the rose bushes, inhaled deeply, closed her eyes a moment then opened them again when she heard the sound of a burp. She shifted the baby from her shoulder and cradled her in her arms. The baby gazed up as Leah began to rock her.（ch10）「小艾伦」形成闭环——同一个名字，一次是被夺走，一次是被交给。

④ She said, ‘Plenty man might sex you, but none a’them will marry you.’（ch05）

**中文**：她说：「多少男人都愿意跟你上床，可他们一个都不会娶你。」
**上下文**：ch05 Dumpling 二十一岁生日，她已经怀孕。
**为什么重要**：全书写得最不留情的一句真话，出自一个不被爱的女人之口。作者不评判她说得对不对——她说的就是那个年代那个地方的事实，而**说这句话的人自己也是受害者**。
**呼应关系**：与 Then Wilhelmina laughed and said, as if she was speaking to Ellen but loud and directly to the room, ‘But Ellen, me never realise is three big woman ah live here.’（ch06）Wilhelmina 在牌桌上当众羞辱人的那句同源：这个家里的女人习惯用「说难听的真话」来表达关心。

⑤ Then Wilhelmina laughed and said, as if she was speaking to Ellen but loud and directly to the room, ‘But Ellen, me never realise is three big woman ah live here.’（ch06）

**中文**：然后 Wilhelmina 笑了，像是说给艾伦听、其实又响又直地冲着整个屋子说：「可是艾伦，我都没看出来这屋里住着三个大女人呢。」
**上下文**：ch06 一九八一年，两个女儿刚穿着新买的紧身裙下楼。
**为什么重要**：全书最锋利的一句社交谋杀。她当着满屋子人否定了两个女孩的成年身份，而开口的还是一个客人。作者让艾伦只用**一个眼神**就把女儿们赶回楼上——**这个家对外人的失礼，从来是无声地咽下去。**
**呼应关系**：与 ‘You said it was time you had fun. You wanted somebody to make you laugh. Me never learn no new joke after you gone. You sure is back here you want to come?’ Ellen asked.（ch03）艾伦自己那句「你确定要回来？」同类：这里是别人刺她，那里是她刺别人。

⑥ When she thought about it now, she realised it was no coincidence CJ had opened the front door and let his father in, and it must have been why her son had been in the front room on his own to begin with; he’d been waiting for his dad to return home with the new baby so he could let them both in. Then she realised the new baby was why Claudette and Joycelyn had been sweetening her up by getting on with cooking the dinner without her having to ask them first, and for a moment after she’d had that revelation she stopped walking because the feelings that accompanied that realisation momentarily overwhelmed her.（ch07）

**中文**：现在回想起来，她才明白 CJ 去开前门放他父亲进来绝不是巧合……他是特意等在那儿，好让他们两个一起进来。接着她又想到，那个新生的孩子，正是 Claudette 和 Joycelyn 没等她说就自己动手把晚饭做好的原因——想到这里她停下了脚步，因为这个发现带来的情绪一下子把她淹没了。
**上下文**：ch07 一九七九年，Wilhelmina 把婴儿抱进这个家。
**为什么重要**：全书唯一一处写艾伦**被理解**。她一辈子都在察觉别人需要什么，只有这一次她发现别人也在替她准备——而作者让她为此停下脚步。**这是全书给她的、唯一一次不欠任何人的温柔。**
**呼应关系**：与 Dumpling was sitting on Ellen’s bench in the back garden staring at what remained of the rose bushes, breastfeeding the new baby. She’d only discovered what Ellen had seen in the garden after she’d died and it was one of the many things she’d had to add to the lengthy list of things she felt guilty about. She’d been switched off, thinking about her biological mother all those years Ellen had loved her, been there for her. She didn’t notice Leah’s approach till she sat down next to her, leaned against her and put her arm around Dumpling’s back so she was cuddling her side-on.（ch10）Dumpling 在花园里想通那些没来得及说的感谢，是同一种「迟到的看见」。

⑦ Then Cassius grabbed her, held her and kissed her, and she was powerless to resist his magnetism and submitted, kissing him back and allowing him to lead her to his room, the spare room where all her children would be sleeping soon and onto Wilhelmina’s old bed, and somehow all her clothes were off and he was possessing her and she was mindless, going along with it, lost and overcome by hurt. That was how she’d tried her hardest to remember and frame it. But in reality it was she who had kissed him and he’d said, no. Then she’d kissed him again and he’d said you’re my brother’s wife. And she’d replied, no, I am not, then physically dragged him by the arm to the spare room where she closed the door and began to undress and he’d said, you have to go back to him. She’d answered, after, then began to undress him. Then he’d said, I don’t know if I can, and she’d said, we’ll see about that.（ch09）

**中文**：然后卡西乌斯抓住她、抱住她、吻了她，她无力抵抗他的吸引力，顺从了……她一直尽力这样回忆、这样描述。可事实上，是她吻了他，而他说「不」。然后她又吻了他，他说「你是我哥哥的妻子」。她回答「我不是」，接着拽着他的手臂进了那间备用房。
**上下文**：ch09 一九七二年产房，宫缩把她推回九个月前。
**为什么重要**：全书最大的一次翻转，而作者只用 `But in reality` 五个词完成——**她怪了一辈子的那件事，从头到尾是她自己做的，而她把这个故事讲给自己听了几十年。** 这本书里所有人都在撒同一个谎：用一个自己编的版本活下去。
**呼应关系**：与 Leah took a step closer to the rose bushes, inhaled deeply, closed her eyes a moment then opened them again when she heard the sound of a burp. She shifted the baby from her shoulder and cradled her in her arms. The baby gazed up as Leah began to rock her.（ch10）她死后的名字同构：她的一生有官方版本和真实版本，而两个版本最后都被交到了一个婴儿手里。

⑧ CJ walked up the path past the people gathered in the garden and towards the front door. On the invitations, the family had asked everyone attending to wear something green, and the request had been embraced to such an extent that, at a quick glance, it was difficult for him to distinguish the mourners from the foliage around them.（ch10）

**中文**：CJ 沿着小路走过聚在花园里的人群，朝前门走去。请柬上，这家人要求所有出席的人都穿点绿色，而这个要求被响应得如此彻底，以至于一眼望去，他很难把吊客和四周的草木区分开来。
**上下文**：ch10 艾伦的葬礼。请柬上是家人要求来宾穿绿色。
**为什么重要**：全书的最后一块拼图放在最后一章的第一句：她要求所有人穿绿色，于是**人和植物混成了一片**。她一辈子是这个家的环境而不是人物，死后她把所有来送她的人都变成了环境的一部分。
**呼应关系**：与 Ellen didn’t have the energy to open her eyes, but wasn’t worried about that, because she didn’t want to. What she did mind was not being able to close her ears. It felt very unfair to be so completely mentally alert while she was lying there on her hospital bed trying to await a peaceful passing.（ch01）她躺着听的第一句互为终点：开头她什么都听得见，结尾她什么都看不见了，只剩一片绿色。
