# 《Good Good Loving》概述

## 全书梗概

**二〇二〇年一月，先给一个躺着等待的人。** 艾伦在医院的病床上等着最后的时刻，她唯一还想保住的是耳朵——因为她什么都听得见：Ellen didn’t have the energy to open her eyes, but wasn’t worried about that, because she didn’t want to. What she did mind was not being able to close her ears. It felt very unfair to be so completely mentally alert while she was lying there on her hospital bed trying to await a peaceful passing.（ch01） 她的孩子们围着她吵，谁都不肯先闭嘴；而她心里想的是：这一屋子人，全都是我一个人拉扯大的。

**真正的开始在三年前。** 二〇一七年，四期癌症来了，克莱德不肯吃饭、不肯洗澡，哭着一遍遍喊的是妻子的名字。一家人开会决定他住哪儿，开场白是：
- ‘We are gathered here today to talk about Daddy and where he should live. As you know, he’s staying with me at the moment, but he’s not happy,’ she said.（ch02）
- 艾伦在会上开了三次口，每次都是拒绝（`For the first time, Ellen spoke. ‘No.’` 到后来的 `I said no and I mean no`）——而克莱德自己要的答案早就有了：‘CJ, I want to stay with you,’ he said. Then, because CJ didn’t answer straight away and because he was afraid his son might refuse him in front of everyone, he added, ‘Please,’ and this time, as he spoke, his voice broke.（ch02）

**再往前是那场篝火夜的聚会。** 二〇一五年十一月，全家聚齐，克莱德当着艾伦的面承认了自己得的是癌症。那一晚散场后艾伦泡在浴缸里，孙女在门外把她的声音听成了哭——推门才听出是笑；聚会的热闹底下，这个家已经开始了它的最后一段。

**倒着走，是那顿把桌子掀翻的大餐。** 二〇一一年二月，卡西乌斯夫妇来做客，莉亚在饭桌上拒绝去看父亲。克莱德在心里把跟 Janet 有关的几条不该做的事编号数了一遍，而饭桌上卡西乌斯一句「像我这样的男人是稀缺品」把整桌炸散，珍妮特尖叫着离席。而这一章真正的刀口在最后——艾伦不叫艾伦：‘People call you Ellen,’ her mother said. ‘You name is Virgie.’（ch04）

**再往前，是那个被叫作「金童」的孩子。** 二〇〇〇年，Dumpling 二十一岁生日是她过得最糟的一个——她第一次比生母弗朗西斯卡去世时的年纪还大，而全家只有一个人愿意说难听的真话：She said, ‘Plenty man might sex you, but none a’them will marry you.’（ch05）

**一九八一年那个多米诺的夜晚。** 牌桌上，Wilhelmina 当众问 Gloria「你怀孕了？」；两个女儿穿着新买的紧身裙下楼，被母亲一个眼神赶回了楼上：Then Wilhelmina laughed and said, as if she was speaking to Ellen but loud and directly to the room, ‘But Ellen, me never realise is three big woman ah live here.’（ch06） 也是这一夜，CJ 把 Roxanne 带上了楼，全屋由此给她判了刑。

**一九七九年，那个婴儿是怎么进的家门。** Wilhelmina 把婴儿放到艾伦怀里，说这是「上帝不会给我们承受不住的」。艾伦后来才想通：When she thought about it now, she realised it was no coincidence CJ had opened the front door and let his father in, and it must have been why her son had been in the front room on his own to begin with; he’d been waiting for his dad to return home with the new baby so he could let them both in. Then she realised the new baby was why Claudette and Joycelyn had been sweetening her up by getting on with cooking the dinner without her having to ask them first, and for a moment after she’d had that revelation she stopped walking because the feelings that accompanied that realisation momentarily overwhelmed her.（ch07） ——原来那扇门是有人特意留着等他们回来的。

**一九七八年十月，Clay 拿着那辆绿色的保时捷。** 一个星期六的下午，他把玩具车举给母亲看，看她涂指甲油。同一天下午，他的父亲在球场边想着另一个女人、另一把刀、另一个孩子；然后是一脚油门、一声「爸爸，小心」，和两个躺在车里的儿子。这一章的最后一句，克莱德是在喊大儿子的名字。

**一九七二年五月，最底下的一层。** 产房里，艾伦的第四次生产被高血压催产。宫缩把她推回九个月前——丈夫躺在同一家医院里，而她叫来了丈夫的哥哥。她多年来一直说是他抓住她、她无力抵抗；真相是：Then Cassius grabbed her, held her and kissed her, and she was powerless to resist his magnetism and submitted, kissing him back and allowing him to lead her to his room, the spare room where all her children would be sleeping soon and onto Wilhelmina’s old bed, and somehow all her clothes were off and he was possessing her and she was mindless, going along with it, lost and overcome by hurt. That was how she’d tried her hardest to remember and frame it. But in reality it was she who had kissed him and he’d said, no. Then she’d kissed him again and he’d said you’re my brother’s wife. And she’d replied, no, I am not, then physically dragged him by the arm to the spare room where she closed the door and began to undress and he’d said, you have to go back to him. She’d answered, after, then began to undress him. Then he’d said, I don’t know if I can, and she’d said, we’ll see about that.（ch09） 这个生下来的孩子，血缘是哥哥的。

**最后一章回到葬礼。** 艾伦去世了，请柬上是家人要求每个来的人都穿绿色：CJ walked up the path past the people gathered in the garden and towards the front door. On the invitations, the family had asked everyone attending to wear something green, and the request had been embraced to such an extent that, at a quick glance, it was difficult for him to distinguish the mourners from the foliage around them.（ch10） 厨房里莉亚用拳头依次抵住姨母、母亲、自己的胸口说「她还在这里」；后花园里 Dumpling 抱着自己的孩子坐在她的凳子上，为那些没来得及说的感谢坐着。最后，莉亚把女婴抱到玫瑰丛前深吸一口气——她叫她「小艾伦」。

## 三个主题

**一、一个女人的一生，被家里人当成了天气。** 全书倒着写了九章，每向前一章就少知道一点结局。艾伦从年轻到老一直站在同一个位置上：她是这个家的地板、屋顶和煤气灶，没有人问过她想要什么。到了最后一章，所有人穿绿色来送她，作者才第一次让读者看见——Dumpling was sitting on Ellen’s bench in the back garden staring at what remained of the rose bushes, breastfeeding the new baby. She’d only discovered what Ellen had seen in the garden after she’d died and it was one of the many things she’d had to add to the lengthy list of things she felt guilty about. She’d been switched off, thinking about her biological mother all those years Ellen had loved her, been there for her. She didn’t notice Leah’s approach till she sat down next to her, leaned against her and put her arm around Dumpling’s back so she was cuddling her side-on.（ch10） 那些被她照亮过的人里，有人知道自己一直是被照亮的。

**二、原谅不是一次决定，是一件一件做完的事。** 这本书里没有一个坏人被清算。克莱德没被清算，卡西乌斯没被清算，Wilhelmina 也没被清算——作者甚至让 Shani 替她问出那个没人愿意回答的问题：为什么我们对她，比对那个真正娶了母亲的人还严？答案不在书里，只在这个家里。

**三、秘密是会遗传的。** 艾伦生下的第四个孩子是哥哥的；她把这件事压了一辈子，直到 1972 年那个产房里才想起自己当年是主动的。而书里所有人都在重复同一件事：用一种不能说出口的方式爱一个人，再用一辈子掩饰。到了最后，那个秘密没有毁掉谁——它变成了一个名字，被交给了一个刚出生的女婴。

## 人物弧光

**Ellen**——

- 起点：Ellen didn’t have the energy to open her eyes, but wasn’t worried about that, because she didn’t want to. What she did mind was not being able to close her ears. It felt very unfair to be so completely mentally alert while she was lying there on her hospital bed trying to await a peaceful passing.（ch01）
- 转向：Then Cassius grabbed her, held her and kissed her, and she was powerless to resist his magnetism and submitted, kissing him back and allowing him to lead her to his room, the spare room where all her children would be sleeping soon and onto Wilhelmina’s old bed, and somehow all her clothes were off and he was possessing her and she was mindless, going along with it, lost and overcome by hurt. That was how she’d tried her hardest to remember and frame it. But in reality it was she who had kissed him and he’d said, no. Then she’d kissed him again and he’d said you’re my brother’s wife. And she’d replied, no, I am not, then physically dragged him by the arm to the spare room where she closed the door and began to undress and he’d said, you have to go back to him. She’d answered, after, then began to undress him. Then he’d said, I don’t know if I can, and she’d said, we’ll see about that.（ch09）
- 落点：Leah took a step closer to the rose bushes, inhaled deeply, closed her eyes a moment then opened them again when she heard the sound of a burp. She shifted the baby from her shoulder and cradled her in her arms. The baby gazed up as Leah began to rock her.（ch10）
- 她一生都在替别人收拾残局，唯一一次替自己说话是在产房里想起真相的那一刻——而她把这件事也带进了坟墓。

**Clyde**——

- 起点：‘CJ, I want to stay with you,’ he said. Then, because CJ didn’t answer straight away and because he was afraid his son might refuse him in front of everyone, he added, ‘Please,’ and this time, as he spoke, his voice broke.（ch02）
- 中段：In complete desperation, he’d told her he was a married man and there was nothing he could do for her. That was when she pulled out the knife and for a moment Clyde thought he’d had it, then she began to force the knife into his hand, and he realised the weapon hadn’t been brought along to kill him. She told him he might as well kill her and the baby there and then because it would be quicker and less painful than allowing their deaths to drag out. Francesca had pulled her blouse up, exposing her belly so that he could plainly see that what should’ve been a flat stomach on a small-framed woman of her size was now softly rounded. She told him she would lose her job and she wouldn’t be able to pay her rent. Then she said there was probably no need for her to worry about rent anyway, as once the landlady found out she’d be out on the streets. She’d asked him what she was supposed to do, and for the first time since entering the office, Clyde answered with absolute honesty when he said he did not know. She’d given him a piece of paper with her address on it and told him he needed to come and see her at the weekend, otherwise she would kill herself and both she and the baby would haunt his backside for the rest of eternity. She swore it on her baby’s life. Clyde had been raised on an indulgent diet of stories about ghosts and hauntings, about jumbies, duppies and Jack Lantern, about revelations, death and madness, and although he didn’t generally consider himself to be a superstitious man, the threat terrified the living daylights out of him. Which meant he had to do something, whatever that something was, and he needed to do it fast as he was already on day one of the weekend.（ch08）
- 回声：‘You said it was time you had fun. You wanted somebody to make you laugh. Me never learn no new joke after you gone. You sure is back here you want to come?’ Ellen asked.（ch03）——注意这句是艾伦说的，不是他自己说的；作者让他的结局由妻子的反问来收。
- 他这辈子最像样的一件事，是老了以后要住在儿子家；他最不像样的一件事，是让儿子坐在那辆车里。

**Leah**——

- 起点：‘Just gimme one good reason why I should go see him?’ Leah asked, dropping her spoon into the mug she’d been stirring, her interest in the cup of hot chocolate she was making having evaporated.（ch04）
- 转向：Leah walked towards the kitchen which she’d purposely been avoiding because her mum and aunt were in hostess overdrive. She got that it was related to the day, but her own grief was still raw which had left her feeling far too easily triggered. She found them wiping down the already clean surfaces around the biscuits, johnnycakes and cocktail patties set out in meticulous lines on Granny’s best plates.（ch10）——她在葬礼那天主动走进一直躲着的厨房，用一只拳头回答「她在不在」。
- 落点：Leah took a step closer to the rose bushes, inhaled deeply, closed her eyes a moment then opened them again when she heard the sound of a burp. She shifted the baby from her shoulder and cradled her in her arms. The baby gazed up as Leah began to rock her.（ch10）
- 她从那个当桌拒绝去看父亲的女孩，变成最后抱着孩子念出祖母名字的人——作者把全书的收尾交给她，而不是交给任何一个姓艾伦的人。
