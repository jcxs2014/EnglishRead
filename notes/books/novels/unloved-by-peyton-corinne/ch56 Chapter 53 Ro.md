## 精读

### 原句 1
> "Will you be my girlfriend? —Matty"

**中文理解**：你愿意做我的女朋友吗？——Matty

**关键词**：`girlfriend`——Matt 正式 ask Ro

**为什么这样写**：Matt asks Ro to be his girlfriend with a note on the flowers。他 choose "Matty" not "Freddy"——Matty 是他的 real self。This is a formal, official ask。

---

### 原句 2
> "To everyone else he might be Freddy, smiling and joking at his own expense. To me, he is Matty, or Matt—walls down, the real him."

**中文理解**：对其他人他是 Freddy，微笑、自嘲地开玩笑。对我来说，他是 Matty，或者 Matt——卸下防备的，真正的他。

**关键词**：`Matty`——Freddy's true self

**为什么这样写**：Ro recognizes the difference between Freddy（public persona）和 Matt/Matty（true self）。Matty 是他在亲密关系中的真实身份。

---

### 原句 3
> "Tyler made me feel ashamed of the fact that I wasn't wanted when I thought that I was."

**中文理解**：Tyler 让我觉得自己不被珍惜，当我以为被珍惜的时候。

**关键词**：`not wanted`——Ro 的 past trauma

**为什么这样写**：Ro's past relationship with Tyler left her with insecurities about being wanted。她的 past trauma makes this moment more significant。

---

### 原句 4
> "So then, we promise not to ever make each other feel that way." He hesitates for a moment, searching for the words. "To be… careful with each other?"

**中文理解**：我们承诺永远不让对方有那种感觉。要……小心翼翼地对待彼此？

**关键词**：`careful with each other`——他们 mutual commitment

**为什么这样写**：Matt asks them to be careful with each other。这说明他的 emotional intelligence and his desire to protect their relationship。

---

### 原句 5
> "I think it would be almost too easy to fall in love with you."

**中文理解**：我觉得爱上你这件事几乎太容易了。

**关键词**：`fall in love`——Ro 的 love confession

**为什么这样写**：Ro says she's falling in love with Matt。这是她的 love confession。
