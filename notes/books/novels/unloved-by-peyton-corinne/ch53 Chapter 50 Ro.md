## 精读

### 原句 1
> "She was my biology professor freshman year."

**中文理解**：她是我大一的生物学教授。

**关键词**：`professor`——Freddy 与 Carmen 的权力不对等关系

**为什么这样写**：Carmen 是 Freddy 的 professor——这是一个 power imbalance 关系。老师有权力 over 学生。这使得这段关系 inherently problematic。

---

### 原句 2
> "I was so depressed, and I was drinking all the time."

**中文理解**：我当时很抑郁，一直在喝酒。

**关键词**：`depressed`——Freddy 的 mental state

**为什么这样写**：Freddy 在母亲去世后用 drinking 来 cope。他的 depression 和 grief 让他 vulnerable to manipulation。

---

### 原句 3
> "She was married."

**中文理解**：她已婚。

**关键词**：`married`——Carmen 的 betrayal

**为什么这样写**：Carmen 已婚——这使得整个 affair 更复杂。Carmen's husband 是她利用 Freddy 的见证人。

---

### 原句 4
> "But I thought it was real. I thought I was in love with her," he laughs, but there's no humor in it. "And I thought she loved me."

**中文理解**：我以为我爱上她了。我以为她爱我。

**关键词**：`thought`——Freddy 的 illusions

**为什么这样写**：Freddy thought the relationship was real。他 was manipulated into believing Carmen loved him。Carmel later told him she "didn't mean for him to get the wrong impression"。

---

### 原句 5
> "My mom would be so disappointed in me."

**中文理解**：我妈妈会对我非常失望的。

**关键词**：`disappointed`——Freddy 的 shame

**为什么这样写**：Freddy 的 shame about his past behavior。He feels like he's disappointed his deceased mother。这是他的 guilt 和 self-hatred 的表现。

---

### 原句 6
> "I know I won't sleep. I'll spend the night watching over him."

**中文理解**：我知道我睡不着。我会整夜守着他。

**关键词**：`watching over him`——Ro 的 nurturing instinct

**为什么这样写**：Ro 选择照顾 Freddy——这是她想要保护他的表现。她想要 be the person who finally loves him for who he is, not what he can do。

## 一句话总结

Freddy 带着伤出现在 Ro 的门口，在感恩节前夜向她坦白了自己与 Dr. Tinley 的往事——她在母亲去世后趁他最脆弱时利用了他的 grief 和 depression，他曾以为这段关系是真实的、以至于 thought he was in love，直到发现她已婚才明白一切都是 manipulation；他为自己的过去感到羞耻觉得"我妈妈会失望的"，而 Ro 选择不评判、只是 holding space for him 并整夜守护着他。
