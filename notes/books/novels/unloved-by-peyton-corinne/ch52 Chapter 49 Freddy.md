## 精读

### 原句 1
> "Do I look all right?"

**中文理解**：我看起来没事吗？

**关键词**：`all right`——Freddy 的 deflection

**为什么这样写**：Freddy 用"smile and nod"来 deflect。他的真实感受是"pain in my head"和 struggling。他 use his usual mask to hide his pain。

---

### 原句 2
> "It was strange, how brutal she wanted me to be with her, and I aimed to please her."

**中文理解**：她想要我对她粗暴，这很奇怪，而我的目标就是让她满意。

**关键词**：`brutal`——Carmen 的性偏好

**为什么这样写**：Carmen wanted Freddy to be brutal。这是她的 desire，而 Freddy 的目标是"please her"。这种 dynamic 是 problematic——Freddy 在迎合别人的 desires 而不是 his own comfort。

---

### 原句 3
> "I didn't like it. But I did like the way she soothed me afterward."

**中文理解**：我不喜欢。但我喜欢事后她安慰我的方式。

**关键词**：`soothed me`——Freddy 对 aftercare 的渴望

**为什么这样写**：Freddy didn't like the sexual act itself but craved the aftercare。这是 attachment behavior——他在寻找 emotional comfort through physical intimacy。他的 trauma 是 sought comfort in all the wrong places。

---

### 原句 4
> "I wanted her praise, which I only really received when I fucked her hard and fast, furious."

**中文理解**：我想要她的表扬，而我只有在狠狠、快速、愤怒地和她做爱时才能得到。

**关键词**：`praise`——Freddy 的 conditioned response

**为什么这样写**：Freddy learned that praise was earned through sexual performance。这是 conditioning——he had to earn love through sex。这影响了他后来与 Ro 的关系：he's afraid he's only valuable for what he can do sexually。

## 一句话总结

Freddy 宿醉后在校园里迷路被 Carmen Tinley 发现并送回家，揭露了他与 Carmen 的 backstory——Carmen 是他的老师、两人在她任教期间就开始了性关系，Carmen 要求他在床上粗暴对待她、只在他愤怒地狠狠做时给予表扬，Freddy 其实并不喜欢这种性但渴望事后的安慰——这解释了为什么他在昨晚的约会崩溃后会说"You'd hate me"以及为什么他如此害怕 Ro 会发现他过去的一切。
