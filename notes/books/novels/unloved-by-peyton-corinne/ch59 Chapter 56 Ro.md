## 精读

### 原句 1
> "You dress like a kid and act like one. I don't know why I ever thought I could take you seriously as a girlfriend."

**中文理解**：你穿得像个小孩，举止也像个小孩。我不知道我为什么会认真把你当女朋友。

**关键词**：`dress like a kid`——Tyler 的侮辱

**为什么这样写**：Tyler attacks Ro's appearance and behavior。这是典型的 narcissistic abuse——he tries to make her feel small。

---

### 原句 2
> "Considering I wrote your entire research paper that got you into this program in the first place for you, I think it's you who should be thanking me."

**中文理解**：考虑到我帮你写了让你进入这个项目的整篇研究论文，我觉得应该是你感谢我。

**关键词**：`research paper`——Ro 的反击

**为什么这样写**：Ro reveals she wrote Tyler's research paper for him。这是 powerful comeback——Tyler 一直靠她，却还在羞辱她。

---

### 原句 3
> "My dad is just like you. A narcissistic, abusive asshole."

**中文理解**：我爸爸和你一样。一个自恋的、有虐待倾向的混蛋。

**关键词**：`narcissistic`——Matt 对 Tyler's 性格的精准描述

**为什么这样写**：Matt 直接把 Tyler 和他自己的父亲相提并论——both are narcissistic and abusive。这是 protective Matt defending his girlfriend。

---

### 原句 4
> "That's the best thing I've heard all year."

**中文理解**：这是我今年听到的最好的事。

**关键词**：`best thing`——Matt 对 Ro 从大一就暗恋他的反应

**为什么这样写**：Matt is happy that Ro has had a crush on him since freshman year——而不是 upset 或觉得被 stalk。这是 romantic Matt。

---

### 原句 5
> "Thanks for the info. You can't imagine how good it feels to know that all five times you slept with my girlfriend, she was thinking about me."

**中文理解**：谢谢你的信息。你无法想象知道和我女朋友睡的五次里她都在想着我，感觉有多好。

**关键词**：`she was thinking about me`——Matt 对 Tyler 的最后一击

**为什么这样写**：Matt uses Tyler's own revelation against him——he turns it into a compliment to Ro and a dig at Tyler。
