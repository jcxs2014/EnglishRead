## 精读

### 原句 1
> "Being a team player is bullshit—I practice the phrase over and over as if that will be my response to anything Dr. Tinley says to me next week. I don't want to play on their team anymore."

**中文理解**：我是个团队合作的人，但我不想再在他们的队里打球了。

**关键词**：`team player`——Ro 对 Dr. Tinley 团队的 disillusionment

**为什么这样写**：Ro 用"team player"来描述她对学术团队的感受。她一直是 team player——但 Tyler 的设局让她看清这个 team 在保护她方面失败了。她决定"不玩了"：向 dean 报告 Tyler 的不当行为。

---

### 原句 2
> "Matt is a better person than you have a chance of ever being."

**中文理解**：Matt 是一个比你有可能成为的更好的人。

**关键词**：`better person`——Ro 对 Freddy 的公开维护

**为什么这样写**：Ro 公开为 Freddy 辩护——这很 significant。她 defend Freddy against Tyler's insult（"pathetic"）。Tyler 用 Freddy 来贬低她，但 Ro 的反应是 elevate Freddy。

---

### 原句 3
> "You sat here for seven hours waiting for me?"

**中文理解**：我等了七个小时。

**关键词**：`seven hours`——Freddy 的 devotion

**为什么这样写**：Freddy 从下午 2 点等到晚上 9 点——7 个小时。他一直在各种地方找她：图书馆、她的办公室、最后在她的宿舍门口。他"couldn't reach her"——Ro's phone died。Freddy 的等待说明他的 devotion 和 fear of losing her。

---

### 原句 4
> "My dad would rather I didn't exist."

**中文理解**：我爸爸宁愿我不存在。

**关键词**：`would rather I didn't exist`——Freddy 的父亲创伤

**为什么这样写**：Freddy 的 frank statement about his father 是一个 major revelation。他的父亲 not just absent——他 actively wishes Freddy didn't exist。这是 narcissistic abuse 的核心 dynamic。

---

### 原句 5
> "He pushes me to be better, to work harder, constantly there to remind me of every mistake I make along the way. But he doesn’t want me to succeed—not if it means I’ll be seen by anyone as the better Fredderic."

**中文理解**：他督促我更好、更努力，时刻提醒我沿途犯的每个错。但他不想让我成功——除非那意味着有人会把我看成"更强的 Fredderic"。

**关键词**：`doesn't want me to succeed`——Freddy 父亲的 sabotaging behavior

**为什么这样写**：Freddy 描述他父亲的 paradoxical behavior：push him to be better BUT sabotage his success。他不想 Freddy 超越他。这是 narcissistic injury：父亲 view Freddy 的 success as a threat。

---

### 原句 6
> "They gave her six months after the diagnosis." My heart squeezes, but I stay still and silent, letting him speak without interruption. "It was only four, though."

**中文理解**：医生给了她六个月的诊断……但实际只有四个月。

**关键词**：`four months`——Freddy 母亲的 death timeline

**为什么这样写**：Freddy 的母亲 only lived 4 months after diagnosis despite 6-month prognosis。这对 16 岁的 Freddy 是 traumatic loss。Freddy 对死亡 timeline 很敏感——他在奶奶的葬礼上提到过"three years"的 grieving process。
