## 精读

### 原句 1
> "Everything feels lighter now, instead of the weight of some unknown responsibility that being Tyler's girlfriend always came with."

**中文理解**：现在一切都感觉轻松多了，而不是 Tyler's girlfriend 那种无形的责任压力。

**关键词**：`weight`——Ro 的过去与现在对比

**为什么这样写**：Ro 对比了她和 Tyler 在一起时的感受（anxiety, pressure）和现在与 Matt 在一起的感受（lighter, no games）。这是 healing。

---

### 原句 2
> "I don't want to be a disappointment."

**中文理解**：我不想让人失望。

**关键词**：`disappointment`——Ro 对自己性表现的不安全感

**为什么这样写**：Tyler 让她觉得自己"不行"。这是 internalize shame——Ro believes she's bad at sex because Tyler told her so。

---

### 原句 3
> "I actually thought I couldn't hate anyone more than my father, but I think Tyler takes the cake."

**中文理解**：我原以为我不会比恨我父亲更恨谁了，但我觉得 Tyler 独占鳌头。

**关键词**：`Tyler takes the cake`——Matt 对 Tyler 的愤怒

**为什么这样写**：Matt 表达对 Tyler 的 hatred——Tyler damaged Ro's confidence about sex。这是 protective Matt。

---

### 原句 4
> "You are so perfect, so real and raw and enthusiastic. So responsive."

**中文理解**：你是如此完美，如此真实、如此有热情、如此有反应。

**关键词**：`perfect`——Matt 对 Ro 的 reassurance

**为什么这样写**：Matt reassurance Ro——she is perfect, responsive, real。这是他在 rewire 她的 insecurity。

---

### 原句 5
> "I want to tell him that. I want to thank him for making me feel so cherished and wanted and free. Tell him that I'm falling in love with him. But the words catch in my throat."

**中文理解**：我想告诉他。我想感谢他让我感到被珍惜、被需要和自由。告诉他我正在爱上他。但话卡在喉咙里说不出来。

**关键词**：`falling in love`——Ro 的 love confession 卡住了

**为什么这样写**：Ro wants to say "I love you" but can't。这是 vulnerable moment——她 still has trouble expressing her feelings openly。
