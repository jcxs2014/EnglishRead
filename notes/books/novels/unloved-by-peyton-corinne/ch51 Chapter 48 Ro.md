## 精读

### 原句 1
> "Something is wrong."

**中文理解**：有什么不对劲。

**关键词**：`wrong`——Ro's instinct

**为什么这样写**：Ro immediately sense 有什么不对。她 recognize Freddy's body language change 和 emotional disconnect。这是他们的 emotional connection 够强——她能 read him。

---

### 原句 2
> "Let me make you feel good," Matt says, hands tightening on my hips as he licks his lips and the vulnerability that was just there slips away behind the Freddy mask. "I’m good at that, at least, right?"

**中文理解**：让我让你感觉好——他的手收紧我的胯，舔着唇，刚才的脆弱已滑回 Freddy 面具之后——我至少擅长这个，对吧？

**关键词**：`good at that`——Freddy 的 people-pleaser 模式

**为什么这样写**：Freddy 在 emotional distress 时 fallback to his people-pleaser 模式——using sex 来 make others feel good。他问"I'm good at that, at least"说明他的 insecurity：he's afraid he's only valuable for what he can do sexually。

---

### 原句 3
> "Stop."

**中文理解**：停下。

**关键词**：`stop`——Ro 的 boundary

**为什么这样写**：Ro 说"stop"——这是她的 consent 和 boundary。她 recognize Freddy isn't in a good place for this。她优先考虑他的 emotional wellbeing over physical pleasure。

---

### 原句 4
> "You'd hate me."

**中文理解**：你会恨我的。

**关键词**：`you'd hate me`——Freddy 的 fear

**为什么这样写**：Freddy 的这句话说明 his fear of abandonment。他觉得如果 Ro知道 Carmen 的 confrontation，她就会 hate him。

---

### 原句 5
> "Sometimes the people we love most hurt us the easiest, even if they don't mean to."

**中文理解**：有时候我们最爱的人伤害我们最深，即使他们并非有意。

**关键词**：`hurt us easiest`——Bennett 的 wisdom

**为什么这样写**：Bennett 分享他的 emotional insight。这说明 Bennett 有自己的 hurt 和 relationships。他 also struggling with something in his own life。

---

### 原句 6
> "Everyone who has touched Matt Fredderic has only fed that monster in his head, the one that says he isn't good enough beyond what he can do for them."

**中文理解**：每一个接触过 Matt Fredderic 的人都只是在喂养他脑海里的那只怪物——那个说他除了能为他们做的事之外都不够好的声音。

**关键词**：`monster in his head`——Ro 对 Freddy 心理的理解

**为什么这样写**：Ro recognize Freddy 的 insecurity is a "monster" that has been fed by everyone who's used him for his body/sex appeal。Ro 想成为第一个"love him for who he is, not what he can do"的人。
