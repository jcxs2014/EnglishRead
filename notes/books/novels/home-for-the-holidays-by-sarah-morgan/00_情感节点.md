# 《Home for the Holidays》情感节点

## 节点一 · 一封寄走的信（ch01）

立冬睡前，Lizzie 宣布信已寄往拉普兰；Bryony 以为最坏不过是买不起，直到女儿轻声说出愿望——而信已经追不回来。

"‘But I know what I want, so I thought I might as well write to him now.’ Lizzie reached for the stuffed mermaid that she always slept with. ‘And anyway, this present is special so he might need some time to find exactly the right one.’"（ch01）

"‘A daddy,’ she breathed happily. ‘For Christmas this year, I really, really want a daddy. And I know that Santa is going to bring me one.’"（ch01）

## 节点二 · 愿望当众宣读（ch03）

生日酒馆夜，她为「开始约会」给出理由，把女儿的信当众读出——前几条还惹人发笑，最后一条让满屋安静。

"‘I want a daddy who is strong so that he can swing me in the garden. I want a daddy who is funny and makes jokes. I want a daddy who lets me watch television before school and who won’t make me eat sprouts because I hate them and I want a daddy who will meet me at the school gate and give me a hug like the other daddies sometimes do.’"（ch03）

"Bryony broke off at that point and swallowed hard, aware of the stunned silence around her. ‘But most of all I want a daddy who will hug my mummy and stay with us for ever.’"（ch03）

## 节点三 · 搅局的开始（ch04）

初次约会被他从更衣室搅到餐桌：裙子、折花、外套、门禁、三通电话；篝火派对的强行随行换来一句当众点破。

"Jack was already pressing the keys. ‘Trust me, this is an emergency,’ he assured her, his mind still mentally on Bryony’s creamy breasts. His mouth tightened. ‘A big emergency. Her baby girl wants to say goodnight.’"（ch04）

"‘You’re not her father, Jack!’ Suddenly remembering that Sean was still in the room, Bryony coloured with embarrassment and shot them an apologetic look."（ch04）

## 节点四 · 篝火与拥抱（ch05）

Annie 裙子着火，他扑火封神、Lizzie 传令立功；深夜他开口要一个拥抱，又在临界点收手。

"‘What I really need is a hug.’ Without waiting for a response, he hauled her against him and she went into his arms, feeling the softness of his jumper covering the hard muscle of his chest and the strength of his arms as he held her. He gave a groan and tightened his hold, burying his face in her hair."（ch05）

"‘We should probably get some sleep, Blondie.’ His tone was light and he glanced at the clock on the wall. ‘It’s getting late.’"（ch05）

## 节点五 · 车吻（ch06）

第三次约会被当众截胡；车里一句怨气话引来惩罚式的吻——他定性「教学」，她道谢「陪练」下车不回头。

"‘You want a man to kiss you?’ Jack’s voice was a deep growl and without waiting for her answer he slid a hand round her head and brought his mouth down on hers with punishing force. His long fingers bit into her scalp and he lifted his other hand and curved it around her cheek, holding her face still for his kiss."（ch06）

"‘Well, thanks for the practice,’ she said lightly, leaning forward and kissing him on the cheek, resisting the almost overwhelming temptation to trace a route to his mouth with the tip of her tongue. ‘I’d forgotten how to do it, but you reminded me. Now I know I’ll get it right next time I go out with David.’"（ch06）

## 节点六 · 雪山（ch07）

她滑坠悬崖、冰镐急制动；他失声恐慌、亲手把她从坡上压住拉回，脱险后一言不发地抱紧她。

"‘Don’t even say it,’ he said harshly. ‘I’ve got you and there’s no way I’m letting you go.’ He glanced up the slope and shouted something to Sean, who was holding the other end of the rope."（ch07）

"‘Any time,’ Bryony said lightly, but she was shaking badly now that the danger had passed, and Jack must have known that because he pulled her into his arms and held her until his warmth and strength gradually calmed her."（ch07）

## 节点七 · 圣诞树下（ch08）

挑树日 Lizzie 当面许愿「让你当我的爸爸」；他温柔拒绝并否认「那种爱」，当晚却做出一顿后备箱里早备好食材的晚饭。

"‘Lizzie…’ Jack’s voice sounded strangely thick and his hard jaw was tense as he struggled to find the right words. ‘I can’t be your daddy, sweetheart. But I’ll always be here for you.’"（ch08）

"He stooped to pick up Lizzie. ‘I just picked up a few things on my way home, in case I was hungry later.’"（ch08）

## 节点八 · 蜜月套房（ch09）

大雪封路只剩最后一间房——「取暖」的借口成全了一个吻；他头一回唤她的名字，她笃定他爱她。

"His mouth was warm against hers, his kiss maddeningly seductive. ‘But I like making mistakes. It’s the only thing that prevents me from being completely perfect.’"（ch09）

"It was the first time she could ever remember him calling her by her name and she stared into his eyes, overwhelmed by emotion and sensation, every part of her body feeling every part of his."（ch09）

## 节点九 · 决裂与坦白（ch10）

清晨告白撞上「只是性」的决裂句，他人间蒸发；她向女儿坦白愿望落空，女儿却笑着让她「等着看」。

"I think you’ve gone mad. For me it was just sex, Blondie—great sex, but just sex.’"（ch10）

"‘And a daddy isn’t really something that Santa can bring you.’ Tears spilled down her cheeks and she scrubbed them away quickly, not wanting her daughter to see her cry. ‘It’s up to me to find you a daddy, and so far I haven’t managed it.’"（ch10）

"‘This is going to be the best Christmas ever.’ She hugged Bryony and snuggled down, her eyes squeezed tightly shut. ‘Santa won’t come while I’m awake so I’m going straight to sleep.’"（ch10）

## 节点十 · 树下的求婚（ch11）

圣诞清晨，他在圣诞树下自任「Lizzie 的礼物」：伴娘裙、钻戒、迟到的告白——愿望成真，而 Lizzie 已写好明年的信。

"‘Because that’s where Christmas presents are supposed to be.’ His voice was husky and he gave her a lopsided smile. ‘And I’m Lizzie’s Christmas present.’"（ch11）

"He bent his head and his mouth hovered close to hers. ‘I’m marrying you because I love you,’ he said softly. ‘And why it’s taken me so long to work that out I really don’t know.’"（ch11）

"‘For Christmas next year, I really really want a baby sister. And I know that Santa is going to bring me one.’"（ch11）
