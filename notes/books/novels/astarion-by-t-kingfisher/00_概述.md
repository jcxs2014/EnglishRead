# 《Astarion》概述

> 说明：这本书**没有章节**。正文是出版方用两级分隔符（20 处装饰花饰 + 68 处破折号）
> 切开的连续文本，本精读按这两级信号切成 29 节，`ch01`–`ch29` 对应「装饰花饰／破折号」
> 的出现次序。**书内章号一栏写的就是本节的级别**，不写第几章。

## 全书梗概

**一、一场轻判，和一句「代价是永生」。** 全书从一个被打趴在地的治安法官开始：
- 他躺在巷子里，先注意到的是自己流出来的血「是一种相当好看的粉色」"Good shade of pink, he thought absently. You wouldn’t want too much of it in an outfit, of course, but it would make a splendid accent. Perhaps as the hidden color in a set of slashed sleeves? Black fabric overall, and then flickers of color as the wearer moved…no, the pink would still be too much. Embroidery in a waistcoat, perhaps? Silver on black, and then a trace of pink here and there, just enough to provide a flicker of warmth…yes, that might work. He should probably write that down."（ch01）
- 他受过的是「年轻治安法官不会蠢到老」那套训练{p:1:5}（ch01）
- ，所以第一反应是替自己辩护——「明明是很轻的刑罚！很轻！」"It was a light sentence! Light! What did they want me to do, pat them on the head and say, “Good job, here’s a copper, go buy some candy”?"（ch01）
- 然后一个吸血鬼蹲下来，把价码念给他听："“The price,” the vampire said gently, “is immortality.”"（ch01）

**二、两百年后，那条丝绸还在。** 从「代价」到「账本」，中间是两百年。Astarion 在 Szarr Palace 做了两百年的子嗣，吃饭变成了一件不得不做、做完就想吐的差事{p:5:4}（ch05） 这套表演有它的判准——「只要你拒绝，或者看起来不够热切，主人的意志就会像一只戴手套的拳头收紧」"The problem, of course, was that if you refused or even didn’t seem sufficiently enthusiastic, Cazador’s will would tighten around you like a gloved fist. And then you would want to do it. You’d be eager. You’d beg him for the privilege and weep with gratitude when he granted it, even as the taste of rancid fish and old piss made you gag."（ch04） 属于他自己的只剩一样东西：
- 一条丝巾，锁在一间堆杂物的空屋里，配一把只有他自己知道怎么开的锁{p:5:2}（ch05）
- 他自己也说得很清楚："But Cazador did not know about this. A single sensual pleasure still belonged to Astarion: that silk was still a cool whisper and velvet a warm glide against his fingertips. This solitary thing remained untainted…as long as the old man never found out."（ch05）

**三、现在线的引信，是一件会唱歌的石头。** 一个自称来自「银月之下的精灵」的女人找到他，说地底有一样东西{p:12:8}（ch12） 与此同时，主人开始张罗「朝圣」——所有子嗣都在议论这次差事的油水{p:12:2}（ch12） Astarion 想的是另一件事：五年里学下的本事，最能换钱的那一样是开锁{p:11:5}（ch11）

**四、过去线：
- 一个葡萄园、一场集会、一件丝绸，和一个肯收留他的地方。** 七岁那年他跟着父母去 Silverymoon 赶 Shieldmeet，看不清Lady of Silverymoon，只看见旁边那位人类市民的袖口{p:3:6}（ch03）
- 他伸手去摸，那个词在他指尖留了一辈子{p:3:7}（ch03）
- 十二岁那年使者上门，他被带去 Lady Erikinia 的庄园，路上那四天是他「截至当时人生里最好的四天」"They were the best four days of Astarion’s life to date. The air was crisp, and the innkeepers were friendly, and he was on an adventure. He fell even more madly in love with Bajia than he had with his teacher back home. The teacher had been kind and beautiful and good, but Bajia carried a sword, and goodness simply could not compete. Granted, she was a good bit older than he was, but he was an elf, and she was a human, which meant that in twenty years or so, he would have caught up."（ch06）
- 十七岁他进城读法律；他说过自己不属于那种「脑回路像开瓶器」的人，自己是锁匠型的{p:15:2}（ch15）
- 十年后他成了「最后的 Ancunín 一脉」"“And the gods willing, that will be far enough.” Lazlo shoved more papers at him. “You are now the last scion of the obscure Baldur’s Gate branch of the Ancuníns, just returned from legal work abroad.”"（ch17）

**五、转机：
- 一个被误判成背誓者的圣骑士。** 他在地底遇见了那个圣骑士。先是一句套近乎，接着是要一个承诺："“Now, before I set you free,” Astarion said, tapping the lockpick against his fingertips, “I am going to need you to promise me something.”"（ch18）
- 然后是整本书最难看也最扎实的一场戏——他把对方按在地上，一边问一边数："“Probably.” He lifted his head until they were almost nose to nose. “Of course, you did swear to protect me with your life and soul.”"（ch19）
- 圣骑士要的是「那就证明我的誓言没有白费」"“That’s why I want to live. It’s been a very long time now. If I simply drop dead, what was the point of all that agony?” He shook his head. “For a decade or two, yes. I only wanted it to stop. But it never did. Now I want to outlive the miserable old bastard.” He drew a meaningless pattern in the dust with a fingertip, then wiped it away. “Don’t you see? If I die now, then everything I’ve endured for the last hundred and fifty-odd years was wasted. I might as well have died in that alley. But if I live…” He spread his hands. “He can’t last forever. And you’d be amazed at how long spite can sustain me.”"（ch19）
- 而 Astarion 转身就笑："Astarion laughed out loud. He bounced to his feet. “Come on, then. You help me steal the blood of a god, and then you get to take a stab at killing me…and who knows what else might happen in between?”"（ch19）

**六、代价：
- 他从不问那位老太太还能不能等到。** 去临终床那一路是靠怒气撑完的，而且——这是全书最冷的一句——他自己都注意到了{p:20:1}（ch20）
- 老人只来得及说一句："“Always been an Ancunín here…Promise me. Take care of it.”"（ch20）
- 剩下的话他咽下去了，因为旁边还站着 Bajia 和 Wendin{p:20:6}（ch20）

**七、终局两段：
- 一句奖赏，和一次背叛自己的告别。** 主人当众许诺："Dry lips brushed his forehead. “For a full vial, I would have turned you this very night. But when I finally do make a vampire,” Cazador whispered against his skin, “you will be the first, my boy.”"（ch27）
- 而两百年后回望，他走下坡路时心里想的是："Astarion Ancunín touched the letter of introduction still in his pocket, took a deep breath, and started down the road to his new life in Baldur’s Gate. And this time, he vowed, no one would take it from him."（ch28）
- 同一段里还有一句——「大约再过十二年，命运就会跟他开这个玩笑」"It would be another dozen years before the irony of that vow caught up with him, and more than two hundred before he was finally able to keep it."（ch28）

**八、尾声：
- 两百年过去，他还是那个样子。** 他回到旧住处取衣服，排在第三件的是一件荒唐的粉色丝质睡袍{p:29:1}（ch29）
- 最后来的那位客人是当年的宿敌，她把一枚棋子滑进棋盘{p:29:5}（ch29）
- 她给的那句评价是："“I don’t have to lie about this.” She moved her hand over his back, slow and soothing, as if he were a child and not the one person who had suffered at Cazador’s hands nearly as long as she had. “This will end. I don’t know how or when, but someday, this life of ours will end.” She made a soft sound, half serious, half amused. “You and I have already outlived several immortal gods, have we not? And if they can die, surely we can as well.”"（ch29）

## 三个主题

**一、「至少还可以走」这句话，需要别人替他担保。** 全书所有转折都不是他自己算出来的，而是有人替他做完了担保：
- Yousen 指给他看入口{p:14:8}（ch14）
- 、Lazlo 把假出生证明塞进他手里{p:17:4}（ch17）
- 、圣骑士在最后一刻仍然信他{p:24:8}（ch24）
- 。他自己给这套逻辑写过一条冷评语："I thought I had found a loophole. I was required to bring him back to the palace. So long as I didn’t go back, it didn’t count. I told him to leave the city, that it wasn’t safe, but he thought he was in love with me.” Astarion shook his head. “It turns out that vampire lords are even less fond of loopholes than gods."（ch21）

**二、欲望有形状：
- 丝绸、城市、法律、一尊小像。** 全书反复把「想要」写成一样可以摸的东西——丝巾{p:5:3}（ch05）
- 、十七岁看窗外那座城{p:13:7}（ch13）
- 、关在地下墓穴里反复听的那块石头{p:12:8}（ch12）
- 。而他给的尺度始终是同一个："“Trust the bat when you need to fly,” the druid said, looking directly at Astarion. “But don’t become too comfortable, or you are likely to forget your purpose. And don’t get into a fight with any real flying creatures, because they’ll make a meal out of you.”"（ch22）

- **三、承诺是最结实的绳子。** 他一生里最珍视的，是「最好的谎言有时候是真的」"Sometimes the best lies were true. There was a brief moment in which things could have gone either way, and Astarion waited, trying not to look like he was waiting. Either he laughs and walks away or he tells me he’s disappointed, and Dalyria and I spend a fortnight in the kennels with Godey dropping rats through the bars…"（ch12）
- ；他最恨的，是被人拿承诺套住——所以他开口第一句就是「亲爱的，我是个贼」"“Darling, I’m a thief who came to steal something you came to destroy. I don’t think a little extra insurance is a bad idea.”"（ch18）
。
可到最后，他自己也变成了别人的绳子："“I’ve outlived everyone in my family,” he said. “I saw my youngest sister’s children die of old age. They were all human, and I was the cuckoo in the nest. I haven’t actually seen another member of my order in decades. For all I know, I’m the last one.”"（ch27）

## 人物弧光

**Astarion**——

- 起点："Astarion Ancunín was in a position to observe all this because he was lying in an alley with his right eye about six inches from the turnip in question. Also, it was his blood."（ch01）
- 转向："Astarion’s entire goal in life was to get the hell out of the Silver Marches, get as far from nature as possible, and go south to a city full of colored silk."（ch03）
- 最低点："He had friends and a living and a life. How dare someone take those things away? And not even because of something he’d done, but because his great-grandmother was going to die and was so far gone that she’d made him her heir."（ch17）
- 落点："He did not ask any more questions. He did not dare. She must think he was dead, and that was almost certainly for the best. But the knowledge quivered under his heart like a blade, and he did not know whether it was cutting him open or lancing a wound."（ch29）
- 他的弧线不是从受害者变成加害者，而是从「逃」变成了「守住」。全书最后一件被交还给他的东西，是他自己把那封信按在了桌上{p:29:6}（ch29）。

**den Suriel（圣骑士）**——

- 起点："“Escape seems like a fine idea. Would you care to join me?”"（ch16）
- 中段："The paladin flushed and looked away. “Believe me,” he said in that low golden gravel voice, “there is nothing I want mo…mm…” He choked then, as if the words were being dragged out of him: “Killing you feels like such a goddamn waste.”"（ch19）
- 落点："It was a last desperate kiss, the kiss of a soldier going to battle or a prisoner to the gallows. The aasimar’s lips were hard, and Astarion could almost taste the anguish on them. It mingled with the triumph growing in his chest, sweet as poison, bitter as truth."（ch27）
- 他是这本书里唯一从头到尾没有撒过一次谎的人，而他学会撒谎的那一课，恰恰是 Astarion 教的。

**Bajia（波夏）**——

- 起点："They were the best four days of Astarion’s life to date. The air was crisp, and the innkeepers were friendly, and he was on an adventure. He fell even more madly in love with Bajia than he had with his teacher back home. The teacher had been kind and beautiful and good, but Bajia carried a sword, and goodness simply could not compete. Granted, she was a good bit older than he was, but he was an elf, and she was a human, which meant that in twenty years or so, he would have caught up."（ch06）
- 转折："“Otherwise they’ll seize everything I own to pay her debts,” Astarion said grimly. “Possibly including me.” He had learned how these things worked in his years as both a lawyer and a Guild member. Debts were things that could be bought and sold, and the respectable creditors who had loaned money to Lady Erikinia had likely been dissolved and their debts sold to other, less respectable individuals who would try to collect. In Silverymoon, of course, it would be entirely illegal for someone to grab Astarion off the street and sell him, but that would be cold comfort if he found himself hog-tied and bound for an auction block in Skullport."（ch17）
- 落点："“Thank you for the escort,” he said. “It was appreciated.” He recognized his own voice as the one he used when speaking before a magistrate: calm and measured and remote."（ch28）
- 她是唯一一条从过去贯穿到现在的活线：他两次说「我没事」，而她两次都听出那是假的。

**Cazador（卡扎度）**——

- 起点："Cazador’s proscription was clear: Thou shalt not drink the blood of intelligent beings. Astarion’s victims belonged to the master vampire alone."（ch01）
- 落点："Cazador Szarr, ancient vampire lord, servant of Mephistopheles, infernalist and ruler of the night, was weeping."（ch27）
- 全书他只真正开口说过两段话，两段都在推进他的计划：第一段是定价，第二段是发奖。

**Aurelia**——

- 起点："She had been that way as long as he had known her. He wondered if it was how she had always been or if she had learned it under Cazador, retreating from her flesh like a soldier retreating from a burning keep. Occasionally, when Cazador took it into his head to torture her, she would react, but Astarion always suspected that it was an act, that she chose to scream and bare her teeth so that the old vampire would be satisfied at wringing a response from her and turn his attention elsewhere."（ch09）
- 落点："“Your strategies are too complicated.” Aurelia picked up a rook that he had sacrificed early on. “You make these elaborate plans that would work on someone who was also making elaborate plans, but they’re useless against straightforward assaults.”"（ch29）
- 她是唯一跨越两百年仍与主角对峙的对手；她的出现方式也从「打招呼」变成了下棋。
