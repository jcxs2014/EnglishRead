# 情感节点

本书共选 10 个情感节点，每节点含叙事概括 + 2-3 句关键引语。

---

## 节点 1：相遇（约第 2-3 章）

**叙事概括**：Mag 偷了 UWG 的赃物，发现了火绒盒，在 widow Mirchella 的屋檐下过夜。他本想偷走火绒盒，却被 widow 发现，展开了一场关于"承诺"的对话。

**关键引语**：
> "I don't deny there are some chores hereabouts that I've put off too long, and perhaps you're the man to take them in hand. Perhaps, I say, but I make no promises."

> "What would you have me do? I'll make no promises until I know."

---

## 节点 2：魔鬼现身（约第 5-7 章）

**叙事概括**：Mag 在树林里尝试使用火绒盒，第一次召唤出了 Keal。Keal 告诉他三个愿望的交易，并直接说出了魔鬼的立场："There is nothing we give of our own free will."

**关键引语**：
> "There is nothing we give of our own free will. There is nothing you can ask, you can demand, you can compel."

> "A thousand, Herr Devil. Do as I bid you."

---

## 节点 3：战场噩梦（约第 10-12 章）

**叙事概括**：Mag 用第一个愿望要了一千金币，却发现这引发了追捕。他被迫逃亡，开始意识到魔鬼的交易不是简单的许愿。他在 Ketowik 的创伤记忆开始浮现。

**关键引语**：
> "The dead are never very far from the living. But they have nothing left to tell us."

> "He who would pun about the name of his Maker shall have his wit about him in Hell."

---

## 节点 4：魔鬼军团（约第 22-24 章）

**叙事概括**：Mag 意识到自己被裁缝和女仆背叛，发现 Gluck 的人在追捕他。他开始用第二个愿望组建一支小军队，Keal、Dorsa 和 Borrigor 都开始以人形出现。这是魔鬼们第一次以完整形象现身。

**关键引语**：
> "You see the world through a dark glass, Herr Devil!"

> "Discretion it is, then. No throat-cutting, at least for now. No hunting, and no devouring."

---

## 节点 5：Jannae 的真相（约第 29-31 章）

**叙事概括**：Mag 被 Gluck 抓获，Jannae 试图营救他。她的姐姐 Bruna 被 Gluck 害死，她隐姓埋名十年，现在第一次暴露自己的 witch 身份来救 Mag。

**关键引语**：
> "I could have had you killed a hundred times over. But I need something from you, and the dead give nothing."

> "The army surgeon wanted to take the arm. I told him no."

---

## 节点 6：分裂的抉择（约第 36-38 章）

**叙事概括**：Mag 被困在 Gluck 的牢房里，Gluck 试图用魔法强迫他说出火绒盒的秘密。Keal 出现并告诉他只有自愿给予才能转移所有权——这成为了后来击败 Gluck 的关键。

**关键引语**：
> "You make these promises very easily, commander."

> "There is nothing we give of our own free will."

---

## 节点 7：最后战场（约第 54-56 章）

**叙事概括**：Heldenplatz 的大营救。Audacious Sunderer 撞塌了宫殿围墙，Borrigor 救出了囚车里的孩子们，Keal 和 Dorsa 攻击了 Gluck。Jannae 宁死不屈，拒绝告诉 Gluck 火绒盒的秘密。

**关键引语**：
> "Know me and kneel to me! I am the Audacious Sunderer! I am your doom!"

> "If I must die here, it brings me some joy at least to deny you the thing you desire most."

> "How does it work?" he demanded. "In the normal way? Let us say it is in the normal way."

---

## 节点 8：Gluck 的死亡（约第 59 章）

**叙事概括**：Gluck 被 Jannae 从死亡中拉回，却发现自己的灵魂被困在她的魔法中。他的所有护盾都失效了，他只能作为旁观者看着自己的王国崩塌。他看到了 Borrigor 救孩子们的场景——曾经的"巨人刽子手"变成了"Bergmönch"。

**关键引语**：
> "Ah, you're here. It worked then. I've never woven such a spell before, so I couldn't be sure. Good day to you, Meister Gluck."

> "Your wards didn't last. They died in the same moment you did."

> "He lifted the children down from the wagon as gently as if they were kittens and he their mother."

---

## 节点 9：告别（约第 61 章前半）

**叙事概括**：Mag 在病榻中醒来，得知 Gluck 已死。Borrigor 选择留在人间守护孤儿；Keal 和 Dorsa 要返回地狱继续"用词语止战"的使命。四人在草地上共进最后的盛宴，气氛从轻松到感慨。

**关键引语**：
> "To enterprises successfully concluded," he said, "and to those still in train."

> "To Mag, who kept his word in the uttermost need and liberated us from the tinderbox."

---

## 节点 10：远行（约第 61 章后半）

**叙事概括**：Mag 接受 Keal 和 Dorsa 的邀请，决定与他们同往地狱。Jannae 会照顾他的身体（如果她能造出新手臂的话）。Borrigor 已经先一步离开了——他用花瓣消失了。Borrigor 留在人间成为了 Bergmönch，而 Mag 走向了地狱去帮助结束另一场战争。

**关键引语**：
> "I am the Bergmönch. The good giant. I protect the children."

> "Your testimony might be useful. In your world wars end. Treaties are signed… Soldiers beat their swords into back-scratchers."

> "It will not be a perfect match to the one you have. But it will serve. And it will likely be stronger."
