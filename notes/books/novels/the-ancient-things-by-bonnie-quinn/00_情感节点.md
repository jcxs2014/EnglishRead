# 情感节点

**节点 1：规则背后的真相**
叙事概括：Kate 列出十条露营规则，每一条都暗藏超自然威胁——规则是保护，也是诅咒。
- "Cheap tents and pop-ups are not designed for weather."
- "If you're approached by a man offering you a drink from a cup made out of a humanoid skull, accept."

**节点 2：父母的遗产**
叙事概括：Kate 回忆父亲杀怪、母亲保护家人——两种遗产在她体内冲突。
- "My mother, who was so quick to kill anything she perceived as a threat to the people she loved."
- "It was my father who taught me to love the land and to have compassion for the creatures within it."

**节点 3：Children 的马车**
叙事概括：Children 用马车冲撞——Dancers 的脆弱显露，Beau 身份危机。
- "Those aren't normal children. And it wasn't just water—it was holy water."
- "Does it… hurt to act like a human?"

**节点 4：Gummy Bear 遭遇战**
叙事概括：Kate 被 Gummy Bear 覆盖——恶心、恐惧、崩溃后的呕吐。
- "Its hands wrapped around my chest, pinning my arms to my sides."
- "It covered my face, my chest. It soaked my hair. And this is the worst part—some of it got in my mouth."

**节点 5：Tyler 被马蛊惑**
叙事概括：马耳语蛊惑 Tyler 弑姐——Kate 的恐惧成真。
- "He looked like Dad."
- "The dapple-gray stallion had finally come home."

**节点 6：Beast 出逃**
叙事概括：万圣节守墓夜——Beast 追杀 Kate 至大门，门被撕碎。
- "Keep a close eye on your possessions—especially alcohol and cigarettes—when in the presence of children."
- "My blood ran cold. I felt frozen in place."

**节点 7：Perchta 的 Wild Hunt**
叙事概括：Perchta 化身为 Kate 母亲之形邀她入 Hunt——母亲形象被亵渎。
- "They tied me to a tree branch by my ponytail."
- "I guess because they look like dogs I thought they were just… dogs."

**节点 8：Musician 之死**
叙事概括：Fomorian 杀了 Musician——小提琴碎了，Kate 的盟友付出代价。
- "Campground manager! You took my allies from me!"
- "I kill everything I love. Everything that gets close to me."

**节点 9：狗变花瓣**
叙事概括：狗死了——变成花瓣飘散。Kate 失去最忠诚的伙伴。
- "Their eyes were open, staring out at the field beyond."
- "Flower petals. Their bodies drifted away on the cold morning breeze as flower petals."

**节点 10：Bryan 离开**
叙事概括：Bryan 带走 sealkin coat 消失——不是抛弃，是去找 selkie。Kate 独自守夜。
- "He's gone."
- "Where else would he go but to the campground, where everything he loved was?"
