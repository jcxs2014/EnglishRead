# 《The Life Cycle of the Common Octopus》概述

## 全书梗概

**开场是一位母亲在黑夜里抱着婴儿。** 三十一岁的叙述者奶着孩子，感到祖母们失去的选择、受伤的心；她说生育大多由雌性承担，而这可能是致命的工作。全书的母职之问第一句就摆出来：I know there are exceptions—pregnant male seahorses with their brood pouches and so on—but mostly, it’s females who carry the future. It can be deadly work.（ch01）

**真正的开始在十几年前。** 十八九岁的佩内洛普到爱丁堡读大一，父亲泰德沉默多年。她绕过出版社的接待员，给父亲的旧同学、推理小说家伦诺克斯写信，试探父辈的往事：I believe you went to school with my dad, Edward (Ted) Winters. He is not the most forthcoming of conversationalists, as you may know, but I have pieced together some clues over the years, and they all lead to you.（ch02）

**她被邀去塔尔莫拉赫庄园。** 伦诺克斯夫妇当家，克里斯蒂娜把庄园经营成社区；儿子萨沙、弗雷迪，表亲乔治带着小丹尼。一次骑马，凯文受惊冲进树林，佩内洛普落马，萨沙跳下马背来救：\"Pen!\" Sasha was sliding from Cindy's back, crouching beside her, his face contorted with anger and concern. He gently lay a hand on her arm. His touch cut through her shock. \"Those bloody lunatics. Are you all right?\" Pen's wrists felt loose from hitting the ground and her spine had a jerked-sideways feel, but she'd landed well. She had always been good at falling. Although of course she had not fallen; she had jumped. Shame rose in her chest and pricked at her eyes. She blinked it away and sat up in the underbrush. Chet returned before long, still mounted, and pulling along a heaving, wild-eyed Kevin. \"I'm so sorry. He could have been killed,\" Pen said, reaching out to touch Kevin's sweat-darkened neck.（ch12）

**双线在爱丁堡展开。** 佩内洛普与萨沙在聊天窗口里彻夜长谈，爱丽丝一眼看穿她动了心；与此同时，爱丽丝自己与辅导老师朱利安走得太近："You're in love," Alice accused. They were debriefing in Pen's room over a convenience store supper of marble cheese and melba toast. "With Sasha?" Pen had wanted to say his name. "With all of them." Alice sat on Pen's bed, her legs outstretched, her feet bare. Her toenails needed trimming and were half-painted with cracked coral polish. Her heels were white with dead skin.（ch14）

**电影室事件之后，三个女孩取证。** 她们把朱利安的越界行为报到系主任彼得·麦卡沃伊那里。二月寒晨发给全系学生的邮件这样写：The official story, sent by email to every student in the School of Philosophy, Psychology, and Language Sciences by Department Head Peter McAvoy on a cold morning in February, once the board of faculty members and administrators called upon to decide the matter had reached its conclusion, was that Julian Sachs would take six months' parental leave to support his growing family, effective immediately.（ch38）

**身世在伦敦水族馆揭开。** 玛戈告诉佩内洛普：当年泰德爱的是玛戈，是玛戈拒绝了婚姻、拒绝搬去加拿大；佩内洛普是那场旧事的涟漪："When it came down to it, Elliot said your father had a right to know, and I didn't argue.（ch45）

**收束回到产后的房间。** 五月庄园种花和解，八月看一场年轻人的婚礼，第一年在李楼收箱结束。叙述者抱着孩子明白：她做过孩子的家，还要做很久的港湾，但不是永远：I was your home for a while, I think.（ch52）

## 三个主题

**一、母职的两极。** 忘记带零食被吃掉的母亲是极少数，更常见的是自己吞噬自己：Rare is the mother who forgets the Goldfish crackers and is cannibalized in her minivan. More common is the one who devours herself.（ch01） 玛戈把"自私的怪物还是被吃掉"推成二选一，克里斯蒂娜则用章鱼的答案松开它。

**二、沉默的父亲。** 泰德把往事守成一座孤岛，圣诞餐桌对面近在咫尺却无法抵达：The man across the table, handling boxes of Thai takeout, was as impossible to reach as the miniature people on the sidewalk below doing last-minute Christmas shopping.（ch33） 佩内洛普整本书都在做一件事：把父亲不肯说的话，从别人那里一句一句问回来。

**三、退场。** 雌章鱼死于不与孩子争食；克里斯蒂娜说父母尽责到最后是会变得多余的："So you don't believe a mother has to choose between being a selfish monster and being eaten alive?（ch50） 庄园托付信托，故事留给下一代，母亲把旧故事放进床头抽屉。

## 人物弧光

**佩内洛普·温特斯**——

- 起点：As nights smudge into looping days, I return to that year. I remember who we were, and what we thought we knew. I turn it over in my mind deciding what to let sink, and what to preserve for a future that I can hardly presume to imagine, because it will be yours.（ch01）
- 转向："When it came down to it, Elliot said your father had a right to know, and I didn't argue.（ch45）
- 落点：I was your home for a while, I think.（ch52）
- 她真正的转折不是查清身世，而是查清之后仍然选择把故事写下来，留给孩子。

**爱丽丝**——

- 起点："You're in love," Alice accused. They were debriefing in Pen's room over a convenience store supper of marble cheese and melba toast. "With Sasha?" Pen had wanted to say his name. "With all of them." Alice sat on Pen's bed, her legs outstretched, her feet bare. Her toenails needed trimming and were half-painted with cracked coral polish. Her heels were white with dead skin.（ch14）
- 转向：Alice had never seen a picture of Emily Sachs before, nor had she allowed herself much curiosity about what she might look like. It turned out she was petite and smiley, with round cheeks—and a round belly. In the picture, Julian was beside her, his arm protectively circling her shoulders, the two of them standing under a cheesy banner that said “It's a girl!”（ch34）
- 落点：She needed Pen. No one else in the city of Edinburgh, maybe no one else in the world, could be trusted with knowing how vile she was. Alice would sooner have shaved her head and called it a fashion statement than asked Jo or Neville, whom she had known for a mere six months, to comb vermin from her hair. And she could not call her mother. Even if she had not been five thousand kilometers away, Nicola would only have folded Alice's panic into her own larger and more general panic. It had to be Pen. But Pen's phone was off. Alice had listened to her friend's polite-to-strangers voice a dozen times. “You've reached the voicemail of Penelope Winters. I'm not available right now, so please leave a message and I will return your call as soon as I can. Thanks!”（ch44）
- 她从操控局面的人，变成敢把最不堪的一面交给朋友的人；舞台上的克吕泰墨涅斯特拉是她的回声。

**克里斯蒂娜**——

- 起点：Christina was, at eight thirty on Saturday morning, inside the white cloud of a duvet cover, grasping for two ties that should have been in the corners.（ch22）
- 落点："Funny," said Christina, resuming her previous position amid the white and pink buds that looked like roses, but without the thorns. "I used to hate gardening.（ch50）
- 她从为证明自己而经营庄园，到自由选择留下；讨厌园艺的人，最后在花坛里和解。
