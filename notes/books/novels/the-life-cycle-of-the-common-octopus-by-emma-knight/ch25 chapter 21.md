# The Life Cycle of the Common Octopus

## 第二十五章：Chapter 21

## 本章导航

- **标题**：Chapter 21（编号 21）
- **字数**：约 31 个自然段
- **核心主题**：佩内洛普从 Talmòrach 坐火车回爱丁堡；火车上的骚扰；在 Potato Arms 酒吧的思考
- **场景**：火车上（靠近爱丁堡的车厢）；Potato Arms 酒吧

## 概览

佩内洛普坐火车从 Talmòrach 回爱丁堡。一个粗壮的男人坐在她旁边，开始对她进行骚扰。佩内洛普被迫换车厢。在 Potato Arms 酒吧，她开始反思自己在 Talmòrach 的经历，以及她对 Sasha 的情感。

## 逐句精读

### 第1段：火车上的男人

> **原句 1:** The train was getting busier as they neared Edinburgh. A few stops south of Stonehaven, a burly man stood in the aisle next to where Pen was sitting, eyeing the empty seat beside her.

**中文理解**：随着接近爱丁堡，火车越来越挤。在斯通赫文以南几站，一个粗壮的男人站在佩内洛普座位旁边的过道上，盯着她旁边的空座位。

**关键词**：
- `neared v. 接近`
- `burly adj. 粗壮的`
- `boarded v. 上车`

**表达方式**："stood in the aisle next to where Pen was sitting"——站在佩内洛普座位旁边的过道上，暗示男人的意图不轨。

### 第2段：男人的挑衅

> **原句 2:** "Afternoon," said the man once he had sprawled into his seat and commandeered the middle armrest. He gestured at the book in her lap. "What are you reading?"

**中文理解**："下午好，"男人 sprawl 在座位里，霸占了中间扶手，说道。他指着她腿上的书。"你在读什么？"

**关键词**：
- `sprawled adj. 舒展地躺着`
- `commandeered v. 霸占`
- `armrest n. 扶手`

**表达方式**："sprawled into his seat and commandeered the middle armrest"——舒展地躺着并霸占扶手，暗示男人的霸道。

### 第3段：佩内洛普的回应

> **原句 3:** She nodded in his direction, smiling tightly, and returned to her book.

**中文理解**：她朝他的方向点点头，勉强微笑，然后继续看她的书。

**关键词**：
- `nod v. 点头`
- `smile v. 微笑`
- `tightly adv. 勉强地`
- `return v. 返回`

**表达方式**："smiling tightly"——勉强微笑，暗示佩内洛普的不快。

### 第4段：男人的进一步骚扰

> **原句 4:** "I reckon you like it in bed, too, don't you," he added in a wet whisper.

**中文理解**："我猜你在床上也喜欢这样，对吧，"他低声说。

**关键词**：
- `reckon v. 猜想、认为`

- `whisper v. 低声说`

**表达方式**："in a wet whisper"——低声说，暗示男人的猥亵意图。

---

### 第5段：佩内洛普的逃离

> **原句 5:** Just after Pen crossed into the next car, a bend in the tracks sent her careening into the legs of another passenger.

**中文理解**：佩内洛普刚走进下一节车厢，铁轨上的一个弯道就把她甩进另一名乘客腿上。

**关键词**：
- `cross v. 穿过`
- `bend n. 弯道`
- `tracks n. 铁轨`
- `careen v. 倾斜地移动`

**表达方式**："a bend in the tracks sent her careening"——铁轨的弯道让她倾斜移动，暗示火车的颠簸。

### 第6段：白发妇女的关心

> **原句 6:** "Are you all right, hen?" the white-haired woman beside him asked as Pen picked herself up.

**中文理解**："你还好吗，姑娘？"旁边的白发妇女问道。

**关键词**：
- `white-haired adj. 白发的`
- `beside prep. 在……旁边`
- `picked herself up phr. 站起身来`

**表达方式**："Are you all right, hen?"——白发妇女关心地问佩内洛普，暗示她在火车上遇到了麻烦。

### 第7段：酒吧的思考

> **原句 7:** Pen had worked hard to acquire her protective coating, and she did not like to feel it crack. After her father had moved out and her mother had confirmed what Pen already knew, while they were packing her childhood illusions into boxes along with the contents of the house, Pen had decided that organized religion was not for her. She still believed in something, but it was not her grandmother's God. She had abruptly stopped praying. Going to church with Tilda had furnished her with a lifelong respect for the power of stories, and a physical response to certain hymns when sung by a choir, but it was time to stop asking imaginary forces for favors and start functioning as a self-contained unit, at least emotionally.

**中文理解**：佩内洛普花了很大力气获得她的保护壳，她不喜欢感到它裂开。

**关键词**：
- `acquire v. 获得`
- `protective adj. 保护的`
- `coating n. 涂层、壳`
- `crack v. 裂开`

**表达方式**："protective coating"——保护壳，暗示佩内洛普用冷漠来保护自己。

---

### 第8段：痛苦的根源

> **原句 8:** Pen had never needed to go far to find suffering. She could feel it in the drive from Talmòrach to the station.

**中文理解**：佩内洛普从未需要走远就能找到痛苦。她能感觉到从 Talmòrach 到车站一路上弥漫的痛苦。

**关键词**：
- `far adv. 远`
- `find v. 找到`
- `suffering n. 痛苦`
- `feel v. 感觉到`

**表达方式**："feel it in the drive"——在驾驶途中感觉到痛苦，暗示痛苦是无处不在的。
