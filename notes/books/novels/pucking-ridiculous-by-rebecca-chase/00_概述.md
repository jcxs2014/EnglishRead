# 《Pucking Ridiculous》概述

## 全书梗概

**两份人生在同一个九月同时触底。** Flora 拼命合群、拼命自我惩罚，心里循环的仍是父母的判词："I silently ask the two questions I've bullied myself with after hearing my parents ask them all my life. … Why are you like this? What is wrong with you?"（ch01） 她寄住在 Clouds 家，评估排不上、基金取不出、连独处的空间都要靠谎称住朋友家来偷。

**同一周，Max 从美国降落到小镇冰场。** 前世界级球星被会计卷走几百万，只能在这支英格兰二线球队打一年过渡："A former world-class player playing for a tiny English team that isn't even in the Elite Ice Hockey League, the top UK league. They're not even in the first half of the National Ice Hockey League, and I'm not surprised, seeing as they go to the pub every night. I don't know how they can afford to socialise daily. I don't get paid much more than a busboy here."（ch02） 他唯一的目的是修补与继子 Bailey 的关系——而孩子坚信「Hockey always comes first」，把父亲与曾经的施虐者 Ted 归为同类。

**两人在一堂亲子烹饪课上相撞。** Flora 认出他是自己看过片子的「boss」，Bailey 要求她整晚说脏话助兴——第一次见面以一场「谋杀计划书」收尾。真相在随后几天层层出土：她自我怀疑的根源、他缺席七年的缘由（Ted 藏掉了所有信件："“Every month I put money into a trust fund for you. You can't access it until you're sixteen, but we can adjust that if we want to. I need you to know that I never stopped loving you, not for a second. I thought about you every day. Ted told me that I wasn't allowed to be in your life. That's why I moved here as soon as the divorce proceedings started.”"（ch36）），两颗破碎的心在面包与糖霜之间慢慢靠近。

**她先交出脆弱，他先交出接纳。** 深夜头痛把他引到她的房门外，一段「一秒就好」的拥抱、一份「不用解释」的保密协议：
> "“Will you…lie on the bed with me?” I ask, my voice barely above a whisper. “Migraines are a lonely experience, and when I'm coming out of them, I need something or someone to help me feel less empty.”"（ch19）
- 「你被训练得相信自己的感受不正当——但它们正当」成了全书的枢纽句："“Look at me, Flour.” I study the colours in his cerulean eyes. “From what I can tell, for years you've been led to believe that your feelings, experiences, and ways of thinking aren't valid. But they are. And how you experience the world is important. Please don't force yourself to be something you're not to make others happy. Those that make you do that don't deserve the real you.”"（ch19）
而她也第一次说出「跟你在一起，大脑停了」。

**关系在规则中升温，在规则中失控。** 餐巾纸合同（Max's Fanny Contract）把同居合法化（ch23）；茶课升级成「就一次」的越界提议被一场 panic 打断（ch32）；她在 ch60 的生日派对上当众断亲、宣告 "My family has never accepted me, and I always thought it was because I was too much. But the Clouds are proof that you can be a lot and still be loved."（ch60） 二十四小时后诊断落地：autism 与 ADHD 正式盖章。

**Ted 的威胁把 Max 逼上飞机。** 「不滚回美国就争监护权」——他在机场告别时听见她脑内循环的 I love you（ch57），自己却在纽约的洗手间里崩溃：她一周不回消息、Nate 拿「You owe me」逼签。至暗时刻，英格兰这边一张大网正在收口。

**全家设局，骗子落网。** Amber 化名「Cleo」钓了 Ted 一周，车牌钓鱼、五名受害女性的证词、Kath 的一把椅子——
""By the time you get to the station, you'll find five women giving statements about money he took from them to pay for his 'sick son'," Kalen informs the officer hauling Ted to his feet.""（ch66）

监护权威胁终结。他飞回来，用一盏「十分茶」宣布
""Not everything. Not you and Bailey. I was about to sign, but then I remembered everything I truly want is right here. I asked for a minute, and that's when I heard from Els. I tried to call you, but you didn't answer." … "The future is scary, and labels are terrifying, but I want you. We'll take it however you need to, but I can't be without you. I've tried, and it was hell. I cried during televised interviews. I cried while playing the best hockey of my life. I even cried while jerking off.""（ch67）

而她终于说出那句全书最难的三个字：
"I take a breath and force my body to let me say the words that I've been too scared to say. They've always meant rejection and pain, but I can't fight them any longer. "I love you." … "Hell fucking yeah!" He beams down at me, pride and joy mingling on his face. "And I love you too. You're it for me, and I can't wait to spend the next months, years, or however long you'll give me arguing and bantering with you.""（ch67）

**七个月后，冰场中央。** Bailey 举牌「他真是我爸」、kiss-cam 转向看台、字母牌拼出「Will you marry me, Maximus?」——她的应答自带婚礼方案：""Yes, and I want a small wedding with a few people and then a bigger version, maybe in Vegas. I've got to feed both my autism and ADHD sides.""（ch68） 全场为一对「求关注的内向者」沸腾。

## 三个主题

**一、做自己不是宣言，是连续的日常练习。** 她的自我接纳不是一场演讲，而是由「禁用惩罚性发圈」（ch05）、「每日查岗协议」（ch44）、「三人大小的社交」（ch62）等一串小决定累积而成。诊断日的顿悟是：
> ""Good. Flora, you have a lot you can be angry about. You were let down by many people. But eventually you need to own this and decide how it will impact your future. You don't have to tell people, but you also can't expect people to understand how you need things to be different if you don't. What would you tell Gina if she were struggling?""（ch61）
- 最终答案是那句 "I shake my head. "No, you arrogant arse. I'm my passion. Accepting myself and asking for what I want is the passion I'm embracing." … "Damn, that's a much better answer.""（ch67）

**二、父亲不是血缘，是「出现」这个动词。** Bailey 的「典型爸爸」字典被 Max 用七年的缺席账单一页页撕掉：
- "“Every month I put money into a trust fund for you. You can't access it until you're sixteen, but we can adjust that if we want to. I need you to know that I never stopped loving you, not for a second. I thought about you every day. Ted told me that I wasn't allowed to be in your life. That's why I moved here as soon as the divorce proceedings started.”"（ch36）
- 孩子在 Santa Run 起跑线上说出的 "“I don't need money. I just need Max.” Bailey's declaration makes my heart sing, but the quiver in his voice breaks it."（ch55）
- 与 epilogue 的举牌「他真是我爸」（ch68）
首尾相扣：真正的爸爸身份，由孩子签发。

**三、好意可以是温柔的暴力。** Clouds 的爱与她父母的爱是同一种语言的两种口音——都以「为你好」开头。她最终学会的不是推开关心，而是给关心设计接口：
- "Flora: I could have coffee with you, maybe next week? Everyone all at once can be a bit much, but three of you at first would be great. Would it be okay if we did it that way?"（ch62）
- 而 Max 的版本从不索要解释——「永远别为开口要而道歉」（ch38）
。

## 人物弧光

**Flora Macdonald**——

- 起点："I silently ask the two questions I've bullied myself with after hearing my parents ask them all my life. … Why are you like this? What is wrong with you?"（ch01）——把父母的判决内化成自我审讯
- 转向："“Look at me, Flour.” I study the colours in his cerulean eyes. “From what I can tell, for years you've been led to believe that your feelings, experiences, and ways of thinking aren't valid. But they are. And how you experience the world is important. Please don't force yourself to be something you're not to make others happy. Those that make you do that don't deserve the real you.”"（ch19）——「你的感受正当」第一次有人担保
- 最低点："“Flour, I'm sorry,” he calls after me. But I keep running up the stairs. I hate myself because even though I freaked out, I still want him. I can't tell him what was wrong because even though he says it's okay to be weird, I know it's not okay to be like me. Flashbacks of my teenage years snap at my heels as I run to my room."（ch32）——距离最近的一夜，她被十五岁的闪回追着跑上楼
- 落点："I take a breath and force my body to let me say the words that I've been too scared to say. They've always meant rejection and pain, but I can't fight them any longer. "I love you." … "Hell fucking yeah!" He beams down at me, pride and joy mingling on his face. "And I love you too. You're it for me, and I can't wait to spend the next months, years, or however long you'll give me arguing and bantering with you.""（ch67）——把三个字说出口，把求婚的反向提案说出口

**Max Morin**——

- 起点："Bad. I don't know how to be a dad to a teenager. I miss how he was when he was seven, before I moved to Boston and Els moved back to England."（ch07）——不会给青春期孩子当爸，只会想念七岁的版本
- 转向："I nudge her gently. “I like that you overexplain everything. It means people know where they are with you. When I was married to Bailey's mom, we sucked at communication because I couldn't read her. And now I live with a teenager who doesn't tell me anything. So keep being you, and be as honest as you like. I won't judge. I've had a hell of a life, and I'm not in the position to judge anyone.”"（ch13）——「喜欢你事事过解释」，无条件接纳书签署
- 最低点："But she still hasn't told me she loves me. I know she does, but she hasn't said it, and I can't help but wonder if part of that is because she wants to have an out in case being so far apart is too much."（ch65）——在纽约的至暗时刻把她不回消息读成告别
- 落点：""Not everything. Not you and Bailey. I was about to sign, but then I remembered everything I truly want is right here. I asked for a minute, and that's when I heard from Els. I tried to call you, but you didn't answer." … "The future is scary, and labels are terrifying, but I want you. We'll take it however you need to, but I can't be without you. I've tried, and it was hell. I cried during televised interviews. I cried while playing the best hockey of my life. I even cried while jerking off.""（ch67）——拒签梦想球队，「我真正想要的都在这儿」

**Bailey Jackson/Morin**——

- 起点："“No, you don't. You want me to pretend to love you until you return to America. You didn't care before, and you won't care when you leave. Hockey always comes first.”"（ch10）——「你要我假装爱你直到你回美国」的判决书
- 转向："“Gina saw you two kiss before Christmas.” … Bailey beams. “No. I like that you're both really happy, and you haven't changed how you are with me. But don't keep any more secrets.” He waggles his finger between us. “And, Dad, if you hurt Flora, I'll kick your ass.”"（ch53）——Gina 的眼睛让他提前解密了 Flora 与 Max
- 落点：""No really. He's actually my dad." … She's still struggling with everything that happened with Ted, although both she and Els cheered in the courtroom when he was given a jail sentence. … Already, Flora's made the place a safe space for neurodivergent students, and she's battled several teachers in the process. The students love her because she doesn't bullshit them. She's embraced her true self, and I couldn't be prouder."（ch68）——「No really. He's actually my dad.」举牌认证
