**叙事概括：** Flora 的独处计划被 Clouds 一家连环击穿（耳机外放六十秒社死），Max 的美国梦只剩退休金窟窿——两人各自带着「不能更糟」的九月开场。她的自我审讯手册与他的父职判词在同一天被翻到第一页。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I silently ask the two questions I've bullied myself with after hearing my parents ask them all my life. … Why are you like this? What is wrong with you?"（ch01）

> ② "A former world-class player playing for a tiny English team that isn't even in the Elite Ice Hockey League, the top UK league.
