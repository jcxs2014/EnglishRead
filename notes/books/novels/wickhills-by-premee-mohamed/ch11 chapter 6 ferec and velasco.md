# Chapter 6

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Ferec 在 St. Everard Park 与 Watch 管理员 Velasco 会面，获取 Tourtile murder (case #18923) 的调查细节——子弹击中日历 ，在 wrecks 上发现 expensive pen cap，case 已被 Department of Justice 接管。
- **情感弧线位置**：investigation —— Ferec 主动 gather information；collateral death 的 guilt（）。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：informant meeting (Velasco)；bullet-in-calendar coincidence；case jurisdiction transfer = conspiracy signal。
- **人物弧线**：Ferec 从 passive to active investigator；Velasco 作为 nervous informant；Sgt. Morecombe as case officer。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称 limited（Ferec POV）；dialogue-heavy exposition; Velasco's testimony as info dump.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Ferec felt flushed himself, even nauseated for a moment. One more collateral death? How those numbers would dance and tumble if he ever sat down one day and reckoned them up. He had not been thinking, only shooting, and only meaning to strike the rotten supports under the water tank; and now a man was dead."

**中文理解**：Ferec felt flushed, even nauseated for a moment. One more collateral death? How those numbers would dance and tumble if he ever sat down one day and reckoned them up. He had not been thinking, only shooting, and only meaning to strike the rotten supports under the water tank; and now a man was dead。

**关键词**：collateral death（附带死亡）；reckoned them up（算账/ tally）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's guilt — "One more collateral death?" echoes ch07's "of no account" killing history; collateral deaths accumulate. "had not been thinking, only shooting" = loss of control in combat. "numbers would dance and tumble" = quantification of violence as moral weight.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's shot killed an innocent (the man in the flat, line 22 of ch03 police report). This connects Ferec's mission to Tourtile murder.

---

> **原句 2:** "The soft lead shot, already deformed from its passage through the unfortunate man’s skull, might be entirely useless after being stepped on; but he did not put too much stock in fortunate coincidences. 'Go on,' he said."

**中文理解**：The soft lead shot, already deformed from its passage through the unfortunate man's skull, might be entirely useless after being stepped on; but he did not put too much stock in fortunate coincidences. "Go on," he said。

**关键词**：deformed（变形的）；fortunate coincidences（幸运的巧合）

**为什么这样写**：Velasco's accidental bullet find — Ferec is skeptical ("did not put too much stock in fortunate coincidences"). Professional suspicion: "fortunate coincidences" may be planted. Ferec's investigative instinct.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec suspects the bullet find is staged; "fortunate coincidences" = possible trap. Establishes Ferec's paranoia.

---

> **原句 3:** "The really strange thing is, I was present for that investigation. So when you contacted me, I thought … well, does it mean there’s a connection? And of course I really can’t say."

**中文理解**：Velasco: "The really strange thing is, I was present for that investigation. So when you contacted me, I thought … well, does it mean there's a connection? And of course I really can't say."

**关键词**：connection（联系）

**为什么这样写**：Velasco's observation that he was present for the Tourtile investigation — suggests the case is connected to Ferec's mission. "does it mean there's a connection?" = reader's question too. "I really can't say" = Velasco's uncertainty/avoidance.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec summoned Velasco (Watch administrator) — the connection between Tourtile murder and Ferec's mission is central plot thread.

---

> **原句 4:** "The only odd thing was a pen cap—" / "What?" / "A fountain pen cap, an expensive one—some kind of blue stone, maybe ashquesite, with either a sapphire or a very convincingly cut piece of blue glass in the finial, and a gold clip."

**中文理解**：Velasco: "The only odd thing was a pen cap—" Ferec: "What?" Velasco: "A fountain pen cap, an expensive one—some kind of blue stone, maybe ashquesite, with either a sapphire or a very convincingly cut piece of blue glass in the finial, and a gold clip."

**关键词**：fountain pen cap（钢笔笔帽）；finial（笔帽顶端装饰）；ashquesite（虚构宝石名）

**为什么这样写**：pen cap as potential clue — expensive, out of place on shipwreck. Ferec's "What?" shows surprise/recognition (Alabeus said "key" — translation error? or pen cap is the key?). Ferec's racing thoughts: "Not once that night had Ferec heard the word pen."

**读者视角提示**：pen cap may be the "key" Alabeus mentioned; or a planted clue; or connected to Alabeus's proof. Case #18923's transfer to DOJ (below) reinforces conspiracy.

---

> **原句 5:** "It's no longer with the Watch," Velasco said, his voice rising anxiously. "The Department of Justice sent a memo, and they said if anyone tries to get access to the file, they'll be sorted right out. Everything's been transferred to Central. I've never seen that happen before with a case of homicide."

**中文理解**：Velasco: "It's no longer with the Watch. The Department of Justice sent a memo, and they said if anyone tries to get access to the file, they'll be sorted right out. Everything's been transferred to Central. I've never seen that happen before with a case of homicide."

**关键词**：sorted right out（处理/警告）；transferred to Central（移交中央）

**为什么这样写**：case #18923 transferred to DOJ/Central — institutional cover-up signal. "if anyone tries to get access... sorted right out" = threat. Velasco's "I've never seen that happen before" emphasizes anomaly.

**读者视角提示**：case #18923 (Tourtile murder) is connected to Ferec's mission and being suppressed at high level. Pen cap in Evidence also gone.

---

> **原句 6:** "Ferec stood still for a minute, thinking. It might be nothing; it might be everything. He had the sense again of being in the restaurant, nudging things around with his fork, imagining hooks—baited, unbaited, he could not tell, dangling at the end of fishing lines nearly invisible in water whose depth had never been measured. Some must connect. Some would not. But he was certain he felt a tug on one of the lines, and he disliked not knowing which one it was."

**中文理解**：Ferec stood still for a minute, thinking. It might be nothing; it might be everything. He had the sense again of being in the restaurant, nudging things around with his fork, imagining hooks—baited, unbaited, he could not tell, dangling at the end of fishing lines nearly invisible in water whose depth had never been measured. Some must connect. Some would not. But he was certain he felt a tug on one of the lines, and he disliked not knowing which one it was。

**关键词**：baited, unbaited（有饵的/无饵的）；fishing lines（钓鱼线）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's metaphor for investigation — hooks in deep water, some connect, some don't. "tug on one of the lines" = partial certainty without knowing which. Reuses restaurant metaphor (ch04) — Ferec's thinking pattern. "disliked not knowing which one" = his driving motivation.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec is now actively investigating, not just executing missions. The "tug" likely = pen cap / case transfer connection.
