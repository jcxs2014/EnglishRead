## 精读

> **原句 1:** "I find I will have to speak of such things as seem tediously obvious. But if we do not proceed from the known, I cannot lead you into the unknown. I suppose we can begin as children begin: the sea is salt; the sun makes the plants grow; rain falls from sky to land; the walls of our great city are about six feet high and cannot keep out, or in, anything larger than a fairly determined hedgehog."

**中文理解**：Cazenave: "I find I will have to speak of such things as seem tediously obvious. But if we do not proceed from the known, I cannot lead you into the unknown. I suppose we can begin as children begin: the sea is salt; the sun makes the plants grow; rain falls from sky to land; the walls of our great city are about six feet high and cannot keep out, or in, anything larger than a fairly determined hedgehog."

**关键词**：tediously obvious（tediously obvious）；fairly determined hedgehog（相当坚决的刺猬）

**为什么这样写**：Cazenave's opening — wry, pedagogical, establishing tone. "walls are about six feet high and cannot keep out anything larger than a fairly determined hedgehog" = walls are ineffective barriers (against external threats). Sets up revelation: walls aren't for external defense.

**读者视角提示**：Cazenave's voice — ancient, sardonic, direct address. "fairly determined hedgehog" = humor.

---

> **原句 2:** "What keeps us imprisoned in the city is not out there. It is in here. With us. Below us, around us. Yes, the scientist is nodding: see."

**中文理解**：Cazenave: "What keeps us imprisoned in the city is not out there. It is in here. With us. Below us, around us. Yes, the scientist is nodding: see."

**关键词**：imprisoned（囚禁的）；below us, around us（在我们下方、周围）

**为什么这样写**：core revelation — the threat is internal, not external. "What keeps us imprisoned... is in here" = walls themselves (or what's below/around). "Yes, the scientist is nodding" = Alabeus understands.

**读者视角提示**：walls are the prison, not the protection. This flips the conventional city-walls-as-defense trope.

---

> **原句 3:** "And you did not build them. / That is what is known. / Here is what is not known: no one did."

**中文理解**：Cazenave: "And you did not build them. That is what is known. Here is what is not known: no one did."

**关键词**：no one did（没人建造它们）

**为什么这样写**：walls were not built by anyone — they appeared (like the North/South Wexford wall that appeared overnight). "no one did" = walls are self-generated/sentient.

**读者视角提示**：walls are living entities that generate themselves. This is the novel's central fantasy twist.

---

> **原句 4:** "And then we stopped doing the ceremonies. We were too modern; it was too silly. No one mandated it, nor insisted on the preservation of its tools—the stone markings, the sacred groves, the monoliths, the voice-cones—so it ceased. Except in the Low—and the Holloway and the Synfolde—and their persistence is why Wickhills still stands and the city still grows. Do you understand now?"

**中文理解**：Cazenave: "And then we stopped doing the ceremonies. We were too modern; it was too silly... Except in the Low—and the Holloway and the Synfolde—and their persistence is why Wickhills still stands and the city still grows. Do you understand now?"

**关键词**：ceremonies（仪式）；their persistence is why Wickhills still stands（它们的存续是 Wickhills 仍然屹立的原因）

**为什么这样写**：ceremonies fuel the walls — when surface stopped, Low continued. "their persistence is why Wickhills still stands" = Low's ceremonies sustain the city. Walls need "fuel from the ceremonies."

**读者视角提示**：Low's ceremonies are essential to city's survival; surface's abandonment of ceremonies weakened walls.

---

> **原句 5:** "So, angry little foreigner: that is what you found, have you not? That thing that the oldest races knew and would never speak of. The cities keep us in because that is what cities do when confronted with a threat. You are not talking to walls. You are talking to the things that make the walls. And you finally made sense of what you were hearing … / Your “device” cannot be a weapon of great import in St. Barothain. They do not have what we have. We have … fuel from the ceremonies. More of it, certainly, than any other city. Here, your invention could end it all. Like a match dropped on a pile of dry leaves."

**中文理解**：Cazenave: "So, angry little foreigner: that is what you found, have you not? That thing that the oldest races knew and would never speak of. The cities keep us in because that is what cities do when confronted with a threat. You are not talking to walls. You are talking to the things that make the walls. And you finally made sense of what you were hearing … Your 'device' cannot be a weapon of great import in St. Barothain. They do not have what we have. We have … fuel from the ceremonies. More of it, certainly, than any other city. Here, your invention could end it all. Like a match dropped on a pile of dry leaves."

**关键词**：the things that make the walls（制造墙壁的东西）；fuel from the ceremonies（仪式的燃料）

**为什么这样写**：Cazenave's accusation — Alabeus's "device" (key) could end Wickhills. "You are not talking to walls. You are talking to the things that make the walls." = walls are made by sentient entities. "Like a match dropped on a pile of dry leaves" = Alabeus's device could destroy the city.

**读者视角提示**：Alabeus's key is a weapon against the city itself; Cazenave blames Alabeus ("murderous, world-ending fool").

---

> **原句 6:** "You fool. You … murderous, world-ending fool."

**中文理解**：Cazenave: "You fool. You … murderous, world-ending fool."

**关键词**：murderous, world-ending fool（ murderous, world-ending fool）

**为什么这样写**：Cazenave's condemnation of Alabeus. "murderous, world-ending" = Alabeus's discovery could end the world (or at least the city). Final line of interlude.

**读者视角提示**：Cazenave's anger at Alabeus; the key's true danger revealed.
