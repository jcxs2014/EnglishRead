## 精读

> **原句 1:** "“Why did the secret police kill Alabeus’ father?” Ferec said, half to himself."

**中文理解**：Ferec: "Why did the secret police kill Alabeus' father?" Hoepsachett: "They said he was spreading seditious ideas." Ferec: "Pardon me?" Hoepsachett: "I talked to him. To Alabeus. The 'official' reason for the arrest and execution was 'spreading seditious ideas.' That is, he said something in public, or he made something publicly available, that the government didn't like, and someone reported him."

**关键词**：seditious ideas（煽动性思想）；spreading（散布）

**为什么这样写**：Alabeus's father's death (Ssura) parallels Seaborne's methods. "spreading seditious ideas" = government suppressing dissent. Hoepsachett's revelation sets up moral debate.

**读者视角提示**：Alabeus's father killed for "seditious ideas" — connects to Alabeus's own defection (scientific discovery = seditious).

**中文理解**：Hoepsachett: "The system is lost to us... Bardanes has it in hand." "But he shouldn't have to... What do you really think he was trying to tell us?" Ferec: "He told us what he was trying to tell us. You're reading too much into it."

**为什么这样写**：Hoepsachett's suspicion of Bardanes's motives (treason laws mention). Ferec's dismissal ("reading too much into it") vs. Hoepsachett's concern. Sets up moral debate.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes's mention of treason laws (ch05) is significant; Hoepsachett reads it as warning about Seaborne's own methods.

**中文理解**：Ferec: "Don't exaggerate. How melodramatic. It's not the same at all. St. Barothain is a dictatorship... Wickhills has a mayor and a council... Everything is quite open. And we're a counter-terrorism agency. You could have told him that without fear of reprisal."

**关键词**：melodramatic（ melodramatic）；counter-terrorism agency（反恐机构）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's rebuttal — distinguishes Wickhills (democratic, open) from St. Barothain (dictatorship). "counter-terrorism agency" = Seaborne's self-justification. "Everything is quite open" = Ferec's faith in system.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's ideological defense of Seaborne; his faith may be tested later.

> **原句 5:** "“It was self-defense, Ferec.”"

**中文理解**：Hoepsachett: "It was self-defense, Ferec." Ferec: "You panicked. You weren't thinking. Attempting to justify it in retrospect isn't much improving my opinion of Bardanes' choice. Up. March. Five minutes, knees high. I'll take the lead."

**关键词**：self-defense（自卫）；panicked（惊慌的）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's harsh judgment of Hoepsachett's shooting of Stangl. "You panicked" = Ferec's professional assessment. "Five minutes, knees high" = physical exertion to keep blood circulating (cold surveillance). Ferec's cold leadership.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's leadership style — demanding, unsympathetic; Hoepsachett's guilt about Stangl's death.
