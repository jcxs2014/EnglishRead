# Chapter 13

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Ferec 在 Low 的 餐厅与 Cazenave (vampyr) 和 Gus (15-year-old thief) 会面，组建新团队；Alabeus 作为 asset；Cazenave/Gus 接受 ground rules（no feeding, no communication, hunted）。
- **情感弧线位置**：team assembly —— Ferec 从 lone operative 转为 team leader; new allies.
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：team assembly scene; ground rules; Newbinding Hold ceremony (old Wickhills ritual).
- **人物弧线**：Ferec 作为 leader (hiring Cazenave/Gus); Alabeus's grudging presence; Hoepsachett/Velasco as existing team.
- **叙事手法**：第三人称 limited（Ferec POV）；dialogue-heavy; team dynamics.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "“A sweet little doe-eyed boy,” someone cooed, stroking Velasco’s arm. “Come dance with me … first one is free…”"

**中文理解**：Someone cooed: "A sweet little doe-eyed boy. Come dance with me … first one is free…"

**关键词**：doe-eyed（ doe-eyed, doe-eyed）；first one is free（第一支舞免费）

**为什么这样写**：Low 的 restaurant 作为 social space —— Velasco 被 solicited。"doe-eyed" = Velasco's innocent appearance. Establishes Low's culture (commerce, dance, solicitation).

**读者视角提示**：Low 的 social norms differ from surface; Ferec's team is conspicuous (begrime, foreign).

---

> **原句 2:** "“Gus,” Ferec said, waving the waiter over with his good arm. “How many commissions have you performed to date?” / “Counting the one last week, uh, a hundred and fifty-eight, Mr. Ferec.”"

**中文理解**：Ferec: "Gus, how many commissions have you performed to date?" Gus: "Counting the one last week, uh, a hundred and fifty-eight, Mr. Ferec."

**关键词**：commissions（委托/任务）

**为什么这样写**：Gus's competence established — 158 commissions at age 15. Ferec's practical assessment (experience over age). Hoepsachett's objection ("We cannot possibly drag a child into this") vs. Ferec's pragmatism.

**读者视角提示**：Gus as child prodigy thief; her experience validates Ferec's hiring despite ethical concerns.

---

> **原句 3:** "“You are clearly coppers of some variety,” Cazenave said mildly, tilting his chin at Velasco and Hoepsachett. “But you, ‘Ferec,’ I am not sure, and that is … unusual. I mislike it.”"

**中文理解**：Cazenave: "You are clearly coppers of some variety. But you, 'Ferec,' I am not sure, and that is … unusual. I mislike it."

**关键词**：coppers（警察）；mislike（不喜欢，古语）

**为什么这样写**：Cazenave's perception — Velasco/Hoepsachett read as police; Ferec's identity ambiguous. "mislike" = vampyr's archaic diction. Cazenave's suspicion of Ferec's true nature.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's identity is deliberately ambiguous; Cazenave (vampyr, old) senses something off.

---

> **原句 4:** "“I think of myself,” Ferec said, “as something of a detective. We are attempting to solve a mystery. The law has nothing to do with it.”"

**中文理解**：Ferec: "I think of myself as something of a detective. We are attempting to solve a mystery. The law has nothing to do with it."

**关键词**：detective（侦探）；mystery（谜团）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec self-identifies as "detective" — connects to in-world detective fiction (Percival Alnwyck, ch04). "The law has nothing to do with it" = operation is off-book, justice-oriented not legal.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's self-image as detective vs. spy; "law has nothing to do with it" = moral mission.

---

> **原句 5:** "“First: we do not feed on our employer or his associates.”"

**中文理解**：Ferec's ground rules: "First: we do not feed on our employer or his associates." Cazenave: "Very well." "Second: no communication whatsoever with anyone on the outside..." "Third: We are being hunted. Act accordingly."

**关键词**：ground rules（基本规则）；feed on（吸血）

**为什么这样写**：team assembly scene — Ferec establishes authority. "we do not feed on our employer" = vampyr restraint rule. "no communication" = secrecy. "We are being hunted" = stakes.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's leadership; Cazenave's compliance ("Very well") = respect. Ground rules establish team dynamics.

---

> **原句 6:** "The band cut off with a squeak of strings; the dancers stopped their whirling. Alabeus looked up, suddenly alert as a bird. / Cazenave and Gus pushed back from the table and walked to the centre of the dance floor, where the waltzing couples had begun to separate, raising their arms in the ritual motion. Ferec knew this one, felt the ache of memory tugging at him to rise, walk over, join them: the Newbinding Hold, one of the city's oldest ceremonies."

**中文理解**：The band cut off with a squeak of strings; the dancers stopped their whirling. Alabeus looked up, suddenly alert as a bird. Cazenave and Gus pushed back from the table and walked to the centre of the dance floor, where the waltzing couples had begun to separate, raising their arms in the ritual motion. Ferec knew this one, felt the ache of memory tugging at him to rise, walk over, join them: the Newbinding Hold, one of the city's oldest ceremonies。

**关键词**：Newbinding Hold（新绑定仪式，城市最古老的仪式之一）

**为什么这样写**：Newbinding Hold ceremony — Low/surface shared ritual. Ferec's "ache of memory" = his past in the Low. Cazenave/Gus participate (Low residents); Ferec's hesitation = his divided identity.

**读者视角提示**：Newbinding Hold as shared cultural practice between surface and Low; Ferec's nostalgia for his Low past.
