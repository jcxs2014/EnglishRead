## 精读

> **原句 1:** "“My fellow Wickhillers,” the mayor began in his thin, sincere voice, unsullied by the dust and smoke still clouding the neighbourhood. “I will make this brief. This is not the time for speeches; this is the time for work, and I will not impede it.” He glanced at Ferec, flinched, and turned away."

**中文理解**：Mayor: "My fellow Wickhillers, I will make this brief. This is not the time for speeches; this is the time for work, and I will not impede it." He glanced at Ferec, flinched, and turned away。

**关键词**：unsullied（未被污染的）；flinched（畏缩）

**为什么这样写**：mayor's speech — thin, sincere voice; "not the time for speeches" = ironically a speech. Glanced at Ferec, flinched = recognition/guilt. Mayor knows Ferec's role.

**读者视角提示**：mayor's flinch suggests awareness of Ferec's involvement; "state of municipal emergency" = City's response to disaster.

> **原句 2:** "How their eyes shone, looking at their hero, Ferec thought, and the thought was almost kindly underneath the rage that still burned in him, a resurgent fire. How … how beautiful you could say their eyes looked, as they drank him in."

**中文理解**：How their eyes shone, looking at their hero, Ferec thought, and the thought was almost kindly underneath the rage that still burned in him, a resurgent fire. How … how beautiful you could say their eyes looked, as they drank him in。

**关键词**：resurgent fire（死灰复燃的火）；drank him in（如饥似渴地看他）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's observation — citizens' eyes "shone, looking at their hero" (mayor). "almost kindly underneath the rage" = Ferec's complex emotion. "drank him in" = citizens' adoration. Irony: mayor is the conspiracy's face.

**为什么这样写**：Alabeus's possible death — "blood was blooming, like roses" = visceral imagery. "faint cursing still coming from the split and oozing lips" = Alabeus still alive, still defiant. "Maybe he would die; maybe he would be killed" = Ferec's expectation.

**读者视角提示**：Alabeus's fate uncertain; "maybe he would be killed, as Ferec fully expected to be" = Ferec's expectation of execution.

> **原句 4:** "Was that a small girl with red hair lurking just behind the remains of a half-fallen pub? Ferec shook his head minutely, hearing his neck creak, and she melted away. Good. Good. Run, and keep running, and head down, back to the safety of the Synfolde."

**中文理解**：Was that a small girl with red hair lurking just behind the remains of a half-fallen pub? Ferec shook his head minutely, hearing his neck creak, and she melted away. Good. Good. Run, and keep running, and head down, back to the safety of the Synfolde。

> **原句 5:** "“The beast,” he said in Anglis, and repeated it in Barain. “The beast! Someone go claim the reward!” / For it was real, it had been up there all along, he suspected—living on the rooftops, its long fur blending in with the colour of the ornamented stone. The beast! Like and unlike a gargoyle, the single glimpse Ferec had had of it loping away that of a baboon, only very large, maned, with a long black-leather face and white fangs in disconcertingly red gums. Anyone might have mistaken it for a gargoyle. In a city where everyone watched one another, people had seen it and never known what they had seen. Long may it run from us, evading our snatching hands, seeking to imprison. Long may it run. Long may it."

**中文理解**：Ferec: "The beast! Someone go claim the reward!" For it was real, it had been up there all along, he suspected—living on the rooftops, its long fur blending in with the colour of the ornamented stone. The beast! Like and unlike a gargoyle, the single glimpse Ferec had had of it loping away that of a baboon, only very large, maned, with a long black-leather face and white fangs in disconcertingly red gums. Anyone might have mistaken it for a gargoyle. In a city where everyone watched one another, people had seen it and never known what they had seen. Long may it run from us, evading our snatching hands, seeking to imprison. Long may it run. Long may it。

**关键词**：Long may it run（愿它长跑）；black-leather face（黑皮革脸）；disconcertingly red gums（令人不安的红色牙龈）

**为什么这样写**：the beast is real — lived on rooftops, fur blending with stone. "Like and unlike a gargoyle" = its camouflage. "people had seen it and never known what they had seen" = Wickhills's collective blindness. "Long may it run" = Ferec's final wish — freedom for the wild thing.

**读者视角提示**：the beast's reality = Wickhills's hidden wildness; "Long may it run" = Ferec's final act of defiance (wishing freedom for the wild).
