> **原句 1:** "Very pleased to meet you," someone said from behind him—a thin, fluting voice, as if from a mechanical music box. The professor stepped aside uncertainly, swinging the cane till it cracked against the edge of his desk, then groping his way over and sitting gratefully in the padded leather chair. "Mr. Ferec, is it?"

**中文理解**："Very pleased to meet you," someone said from behind him—a thin, fluting voice, as if from a mechanical music box. The professor stepped aside uncertainly, swinging the cane till it cracked against the edge of his desk, then groping his way over and sitting gratefully in the padded leather chair. "Mr. Ferec, is it?"

**关键词**：mechanical music box（机械音乐盒）；gratefully（感激地）

**为什么这样写**：Eldebald's entrance — snake-voiced, blind, cane-swinging. "thin, fluting voice, as if from a mechanical music box" = uncanny. "gratefully" in padded chair = relief at sitting. Establishes Eldebald's character.

**读者视角提示**：Eldebald is a blind scientist with snake-voice; Wickhills's academic culture.

> **原句 2:** "“There’s something in there,” Ferec said. “We think.”"

**中文理解**：Ferec: "There's something in there. We think." Eldebald: "Superb. Just a moment … dear oh dear. The size of it. Well well." Eldebald twiddled knobs, lengthened/shortened tubes, muttering softly; the snake seeming to supervise.

**关键词**：；dear oh dear（哎呀呀）

**为什么这样写**：scientific examination scene — Eldebald examining pen cap under microscope. "dear oh dear. The size of it" = something found. "snake seeming to supervise" = Eldebald's disassociation from his own body.

**读者视角提示**：pen cap contains something hidden (hexagon under finial).

> **原句 3:** "Ferec’s first instinct was to pull out the cap and step on it, like a cockroach; he resisted the urge, and went back to the field glasses, as if to convince himself. Yes: there underneath the bloody finial (knew it!), hidden in the central facet, a hexagon camouflaged underneath the hexagon of glass. At this magnification, the lower one was not featureless but exhibited a small, regular pattern of wavy lines, like the moiré effect of certain kinds of fabric."

**中文理解**：Ferec's first instinct was to pull out the cap and step on it, like a cockroach; he resisted the urge, and went back to the field glasses. Yes: there underneath the bloody finial (knew it!), hidden in the central facet, a hexagon camouflaged underneath the hexagon of glass. At this magnification, the lower one was not featureless but exhibited a small, regular pattern of wavy lines, like the moiré effect of certain kinds of fabric。

**关键词**：moiré effect（莫尔条纹效应）；camouflaged（伪装的）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's instinct (destroy it) vs. restraint (examine first). "bloody finial (knew it!)" = Ferec suspected finial hid something. "moiré effect" = pattern suggests encoded message/signal.

**读者视角提示**：hexagon under hexagon = hidden layer; pattern = signal/encoded message.

> **原句 4:** "“Oh, yes. You are a man of cutting-edge science, I see!” The snake flicked its tongue again, perhaps laughing. “Yes, there are a fair few projects at the moment—collaborating with Public Health, of course—studying vibrational waves of sound.”"

**中文理解**：Eldebald: "Oh, yes. You are a man of cutting-edge science, I see! Yes, there are a fair few projects at the moment—collaborating with Public Health, of course—studying vibrational waves of sound." Ferec: "Public Health?" Eldebald: "Bats. And streetlights. Also, apparently, the lesser teacup-beetle. If you can … blast and damn it, really. I wish I had my head back … shall we try to remove it and take another look?"

**关键词**：vibrational waves of sound（声波振动）；cutting-edge science（前沿科学）

**为什么这样写**：Eldebald connects pen cap to "vibrational waves of sound" research (Public Health studying bats, streetlights, teacup-beetle). Confirms tracking device hypothesis: cap reflects signal back to sender.

**读者视角提示**：pen cap is a tracking device (reflects vibrational signal); St. Barothain's scientific advance. "blast and damn it, really. I wish I had my head back" = Eldebald's frustration.

> **原句 5:** "“It was never their bloody craft,” Ferec muttered as he whistled for a cab. “There I was, having existential crises in the middle of the night … am I losing my touch? Am I losing my vision? Am I simply getting old?”"

**中文理解**：Ferec muttered: "It was never their bloody craft. There I was, having existential crises in the middle of the night … am I losing my touch? Am I losing my vision? Am I simply getting old?"

**关键词**：existential crises（存在主义危机）；losing my touch（失去手感）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's frustration — he'd been carrying a tracking device, thinking he was being followed. "am I losing my touch?" = self-doubt. "It was never their bloody craft" = the tracking was mechanical, not skill-based.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's professional pride wounded; "losing my touch" echoes his earlier competence.
