## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Now he remembered. They had been ambushed by another set of sewer-sailors; he had warned them off, in the Low cant he had learned in his youth; he had demanded immunity long enough to speak to his former employer; he had insisted upon a meeting; and then he was quite sure he had fainted."

**中文理解**：Now he remembered。They had been ambushed by another set of sewer-sailors；he had warned them off, in the Low cant he had learned in his youth；he had demanded immunity long enough to speak to his former employer；he had insisted upon a meeting；and then he was quite sure he had fainted。

**关键词**：sewer-sailors（地下船夫）；Low cant（地下黑话）；fainted（昏厥）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's memory returns — he was ambushed, used Low cant, demanded meeting with former employer. "fainted" = injury severity. Establishes Ferec's past in the Low.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec has a past identity in the Low ("Vy Ferec"); sewer-sailors are a faction; meeting with "former employer" suggests Ferec's pre-Seaborne connections.

**中文理解**：Below the known was the Low, where those who did not wish to be seen made their homes。Velasco should not have been so surprised that they would speak the languages used most commonly both above and below; there was a fair bit of traffic between surface and subsurface, since most Low residents kept at least a toe-hold aboveground。

**为什么这样写**：worldbuilding — Low as hidden city beneath Wickhills. "those who did not wish to be seen" = Low's purpose. "toe-hold aboveground" = Low residents maintain surface connections.

**读者视角提示**：Low is a parallel society; Ferec's past there gives him access/protection.

> **原句 4:** "We're not safe down here," Ferec said. "We need protection. And down here there's only one person who can offer that. Which is why I asked for the meeting. I'm hoping he'll let you live."

**中文理解**：Ferec: "We're not safe down here. We need protection. And down here there's only one person who can offer that. Which is why I asked for the meeting. I'm hoping he'll let you live."

**关键词**：let you live（让你们活）；protection（保护）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's admission — Low is dangerous; "he" (unnamed) controls access. "I'm hoping he'll let you live" = Ferec's companions' lives depend on his standing with this figure. High stakes.

**读者视角提示**："he" = likely a Low leader (Bardanes? or someone else?). Ferec's past connection is key.

> **原句 5:** "Ahoy there, Vy Ferec! Is that you, lad?"

**读者视角提示**：Ferec has a past in the Low under name "Vy Ferec"; this identity may be significant.
