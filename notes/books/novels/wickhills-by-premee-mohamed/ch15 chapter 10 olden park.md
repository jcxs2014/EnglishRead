**读者视角提示**：Ferec's adherence to protocol even when rogue = his character core; "operating outside of agency sanction" = Bardanes's operation is off-book.

**读者视角提示**：insider threat within Watch; someone at Garsley Block or Central Office is complicit. Blonde woman may be Watch or City Hall.
