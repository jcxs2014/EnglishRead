> **原句 2:** "If you have pickle beets, use those (at least one jar per large pot, plus the brine). **关键词**：pickle beets（腌甜菜）；brine（盐水）；if you can spare it（如果能省出来）

**为什么这样写**：recipe's conditional instructions ("if you have", "if you can spare it") = resource scarcity. "Let them cool in the brine, then take them out and put them in the soup at dinner" = timing detail. Establishes St. Barothain's domestic economy.
