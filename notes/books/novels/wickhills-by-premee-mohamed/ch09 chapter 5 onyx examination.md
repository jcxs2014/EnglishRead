## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bardanes was frightened, Ferec thought—deeply frightened, and unused to the feeling, and still trying to hide it not out of ego but out of a desire to maintain the tone of the room, to receive knowledge as if in its pure state without distraction. But there was no such thing as knowledge without the fear of its consequence."

**中文理解**：Bardanes was frightened, Ferec thought—deeply frightened, and unused to the feeling, and still trying to hide it not out of ego but out of a desire to maintain the tone of the room, to receive knowledge as if in its pure state without distraction. But there was no such thing as knowledge without the fear of its consequence。

**关键词**：unused to the feeling（不习惯这种感觉）；tone of the room（房间的氛围）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec reads Bardanes — handler's fear is unprecedented (Bardanes "unused to" fear). "not out of ego but out of a desire to maintain the tone of the room" = Bardanes's fear is managed for operational reasons, not pride. "no such thing as knowledge without the fear of its consequence" — thematic statement: knowledge = danger.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes's fear signals the mission's true stakes; Ferec's ability to read his handler is professional skill.

> **原句 2:** "“It is immaterial,” Bardanes said. “Because unfortunately, we are not able, as I said, to avail ourselves of Seaborne’s normal analytical facilities… what about this one?” / “Even less,” Hoepsachett went on, moving to the table with the dead ōg."

**中文理解**：Bardanes: "It is immaterial. Because unfortunately, we are not able, as I said, to avail ourselves of Seaborne's normal analytical facilities... what about this one?" Hoepsachett: "Even less," moving to the dead ōg table。

**关键词**：immaterial（无关紧要的）；avail ourselves of（利用/使用）

**为什么这样写**：Bardanes's "not able to avail ourselves of Seaborne's normal analytical facilities" = operation is off-book, no institutional support. Secrecy constraint. Hoepsachett's "Even less" = ōg even harder to analyze (lithodermous species resist marking).

**读者视角提示**：operation is clandestine — no Seaborne lab access. This explains why examination is manual/field-based.

---

> **原句 3:** "“No dealing with the guilds,” Bardanes said."

**中文理解**：Bardanes: "No dealing with the guilds."

**关键词**：guilds（行会）

**为什么这样写**：Bardanes's constraint — no guild involvement, meaning operation stays fully off-book. Contrast with Ferec's suggestion to check blacksmith guild registry. Bardanes rejects institutional channels.

**读者视角提示**："no dealing with the guilds" = maximum secrecy; even the Watch (line "No Watch") is excluded. Operation is fully rogue.

> **原句 4:** "Ferec made himself look at this one too: a human. Did that make a difference? He felt around inside himself and decided: Not really. No. No, no difference."

**中文理解**：Ferec made himself look at this one too: a human. Did that make a difference? He felt around inside himself and decided: Not really. No. No, no difference。

**关键词**：felt around inside himself（内省/自我检查）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's moral self-examination — does killing a human vs. non-human matter? "felt around inside himself" = searching for feeling. "No, no difference" = desensitization complete. Mirrors ch07's killing history ("of no account").

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's numbness is both professional asset and moral cost. "No, no difference" echoes "of no account" — his emotional flattening.

---

> **原句 5:** "“No one else knew about this operation,” Ferec finally said, putting the bag down again. His hands were shaking, making the casings jingle against one another. “You told myself and Tanczel that; and you’ve just said it again. So no one on our side revealed our movements.” / “It seems not.” / “So what does that leave us with? These pursuers: Alabeus made some kind of arrangement. We’d get him off the ship, unseen, and get him past Customs—with your help—and then these people would snatch him from us. He’s got his own agenda coming here.”"

**中文理解**：Ferec: "No one else knew about this operation... So no one on our side revealed our movements." Bardanes: "It seems not." Ferec: "So what does that leave us with? These pursuers: Alabeus made some kind of arrangement... He's got his own agenda coming here."

**关键词**：revealed our movements（泄露行踪）；agenda（议程/目的）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's deductive reasoning — eliminating internal leak, concluding Alabeus's own arrangement. "He's got his own agenda" = Ferec's suspicion of asset. Bardanes's "It seems not" is noncommittal.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec suspects Alabeus; Bardanes neither confirms nor denies. Handler's opacity persists.

---

> **原句 6:** "“It wasn’t Tanczel,” he said."

**中文理解**：Ferec: "It wasn't Tanczel." Bardanes: "No? Why not?" Ferec: "It wasn't Tanczel." Bardanes demands evidence (unaccounted times, messages). Then: "For what it's worth, I too don't feel that it was him. It's why I chose him. Why I chose you. But we cannot rely on what we feel, Ferec. What have I taught you all these years? Was it for nothing?"

**关键词**：unaccounted for（无法解释的/失踪的）；

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's loyalty to Tanczel vs. Bardanes's demand for evidence. Bardanes's "I too don't feel that it was him" — handler shares Ferec's intuition but insists on evidence. "What have I taught you all these years?" = training emphasis on evidence over feeling. The "I chose him. Why I chose you." — Bardanes's personal investment in his agents.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes suspects Tanczel too but won't say so directly; he tests Ferec. "I chose him. Why I chose you." reveals handler's personal bonds. Ferec's "It wasn't Tanczel" (repeated, no evidence) = loyalty over logic.
