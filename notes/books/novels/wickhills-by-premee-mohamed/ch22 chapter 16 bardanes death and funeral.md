## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The City was working with St. Barothain," he said, keeping his voice low. "I was about to say all along, but in fact I think only since Alabeus and the key were separated, because it's only the key they could track … Where is it, by the way? Please don't tell me it's in the canal."

**中文理解**：Ferec: "The City was working with St. Barothain. I was about to say all along, but in fact I think only since Alabeus and the key were separated, because it's only the key they could track … Where is it, by the way? Please don't tell me it's in the canal."

**关键词**：City was working with St. Barothain（City 与 St. Barothain 合作）；track（追踪）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's revelation — City (Wickhills government) is working with St. Barothain to recover Alabeus's key. "only since Alabeus and the key were separated, because it's only the key they could track" = key is trackable. Establishes conspiracy.

**读者视角提示**：City + St. Barothain conspiracy; key's trackability is plot device.

"sailor he had killed by accident, whose name he did not even know" = Ferec's accumulated collateral deaths.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's professional isolation — he doesn't know Bardanes personally; handler-agent relationship is functional, not intimate.

> **原句 3:** "He made the arrangements himself, calling again upon the Indigo King for discretion—out of pity, out of respect."

**中文理解**：Ferec made the arrangements himself, calling again upon the Indigo King for discretion—out of pity, out of respect. Please, he'd said. The man was … the man was a mountain. Was he now. I don't know what else to call him. He rose above all others, and he never believed he did. He was the last thing of value I had。

**关键词**：the last thing of value I had（我所拥有的最后有价值的东西）；a mountain（一座山）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's grief for Bardanes — "the last thing of value I had" = Ferec's emotional core. "a mountain" = Bardanes's stature. "He rose above all others, and he never believed he did" = Bardanes's humility.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes's death is Ferec's deepest loss; "last thing of value I had" reveals Ferec's emotional poverty.

> **原句 4:** "Us. Our own City. My city. It can't be. I know it is, but it can't be. They have been corrupted by this thing. This stolen thing. How, why?"

**中文理解**：Ferec's internal monologue: "Us. Our own City. My city. It can't be. I know it is, but it can't be. They have been corrupted by this thing. This stolen thing. How, why?"

**关键词**：corrupted by this thing（被这个东西腐蚀了）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's disbelief — City's corruption by Alabeus's key. "This stolen thing" = the key/proof. "How, why?" = Ferec's investigative drive.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's loyalty to Wickhills is being tested; City's complicity in conspiracy.

**为什么这样写**：funeral scene — King wouldn't provide clergy (surface-world). Ferec didn't mind. "This graveyard, to feed the natural hungers" = Wickhills's burial customs (feeding the earth). "long golden sun through the evergreens" = peaceful imagery.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes's funeral is quiet, private; no City presence (no mayor/councillors). Contrast with Tanczel's funeral.
