# 情感节点

> 10 个节点，每节点叙事概括 + 2-3 句关键引语。所有英文引语一律单独成行引用块（AGENTS 第 9 条 d2）。

## 节点一：Ferec 与 Tanczel 的 partnership（ch01-02）

**叙事概括**：Ferec 与 Tanczel 在雪中的 Wickhills 港口执行任务，尾随目标船 Caranton Hill。Tanczel 是 Ferec 的搭档，两人 banter、分工、cover 为 dockworkers。Tanczel 的 death（ch02）是 Ferec 的 first major loss， establishes Ferec's professional composure 与 underlying grief。

**关键引语**：

> "There she is," he said.（ch01）

> "Tanczel was dead not two paces from where Ferec had left him; the snow told a brief, confused story of attack and retreat, of hits both shallow and deep, of someone or something that had shed bright red blood, like a human."（ch02）

> "“Welcome,” he replied, “to Wickhills.”"（ch01）

---

## 节点二：Bardanes 的 reveal 与 handler-agent bond（ch03, ch08）

**叙事概括**：Bardanes（manticore handler）在 Walking Office 中 debrief Ferec，揭示 asset 无 clearance level。Bardanes 的 monologue（ch08）揭示 Seaborne 的起源、Civil War 历史、manticore 迁徙史。Bardanes "saved my life" — Ferec 的 personal debt。

**关键引语**：

> "In the far corner, what appeared to be a heap of discarded furniture rumbled and stood, revealing itself as a silvery-grey manticore dressed in a plum tweed suit with a deep green silk tie."（ch5）

> "He saved my life."（ch15）

> "The last thing of value I had."（ch22）

---

## 节点三：Tanczel 的 death 与 Ferec 的 guilt（ch02, ch04, ch07）

**叙事概括**：Tanczel 死于 ōg 伏击；Ferec 的 "One more collateral death?" guilt；Ferec 在 Tanczel 葬礼后 dining，反思杀人历史。Tanczel 的 wife receives money (Ferec forged cheque)。

**关键引语**：

> "One more collateral death? How those numbers would dance and tumble if he ever sat down one day and reckoned them up. He had not been thinking, only shooting, and only meaning to strike the rotten supports under the water tank; and now a man was dead."（ch11）

> "It was of no account, really of no account. The man he had killed on Thursday was not his first kill, nor his fifth or dozenth or twentieth."（ch07）

> "Tanczel's wife has money now, Ferec. He was insured through us, and she will shortly begin to receive those payments as well as those from his pension."（ch13）

## 节点四：Alabeus 的 reveal 与 asset 的 mystery（ch03, ch05, ch16）

**叙事概括**：Alabeus（St. Barothain 的科学家）交付 asset (snail-stone keepsake)；Bardanes 揭示 asset 无 clearance；Alabeus 的 monologue（ch16）揭示 St. Barothain 的生活、walls project (Project Horseshoe)、Tecuric 的 hostage-taking、Alabeus 的 discovery (walls are listening)。asset 是 "key"（incomplete, dangerous in enemies' hands）。

**关键引语**：

> "“So if you are not Alabeus of the Dossvern family, long known to my own, then you have gone to a lot of trouble coming here to fool me and my employer,” he said. “And they would not be pleased to hear it; but I have told them nothing, both because you asked and because I judged it to be unwise. Are you he?”"（ch05）

> "The walls are listening. Through all the centuries they have listened to one another, spoken to one another. This is a gross oversimplification. But what do you think happens if we can speak to them? If we can mimic their voices, give them orders? What do you think they would do?"（ch16）

> "You think we know nothing of what you're doing up there," the king hissed, leaning even closer to Ferec's face.（ch18）
> "I was already wearying of the world when Bardanes' great-great-grandfather was still pecking his way through his shell. Yes, Bardanes is known to me, Ferec. He does not know about me, and we will keep it that way. I am adding it to your oath."（ch18）

## 节点五：Bardanes 的 death 与 Ferec 的 grief（ch16, ch22）

**叙事概括**：Bardanes 死于 City attack（shot）；Ferec 在 Cazenave's safe house 醒来，发现 Bardanes 死，Alabeus 哭泣；Ferec 搜 Bardanes 衣服找到 luck-stone 半块；Bardanes 的葬礼 at Ashforest Memorial（blank marker）。Ferec 的 grief（"the last thing of value I had"）与 City 的 corruption 之问。

**关键引语**：

> "The old man was dying."（ch22）

> "The man was … the man was a mountain. / Was he now. / I don't know what else to call him. He rose above all others, and he never believed he did. He was the last thing of value I had."（ch22）

> "Bardanes' marker was blank: a neatly beveled semicircle of black granite at the head of the grave. In death he could have no name. Later, when it was all over, maybe."（ch22）

## 节点六：Hoepsachett 的 moral challenge 与 Ferec 的 identity（ch22-23）

**叙事概括**：Ferec 与 Hoepsachett 对话 —— 他怀疑她为 leak；Hoepsachett 的 moral challenge (Seaborne vs Ssura, "We as much as killed his father")；Ferec 揭露 "Vycol" (real name) and takes pen cap from Alabeus by force。Hoepsachett: "No. Definitely not. But go."

**关键引语**：

> "“You thought it was me.” / “What was?” / “You know.” The steady breath, the smell of herbs and blood. He almost spoke again, then she said, “You thought whoever was giving us away to the enemy might have been me. The leak.” / “Yes.” / “Thank you,” Hoepsachett said."（ch23）

> "“How do you know it’s for that? And not for the greater good of the city?” / “Because they all lost their minds with the wanting of it. Because it’s unhealthy to look at the descent into madness that this … this thing has caused. Not even the thing itself.”"（ch23）

> "“My real name isn't Ferec,” he said. / “Of course it isn't,” Hoepsachett said, not opening her eyes. “Are you going to tell us what it is?” / “No,” he said. “But I can tell you some of the rest.”"（ch22）

## 节点七：Cazenave 的 monologue (city as living entity)（ch24）

**叙事概括**：Cazenave (vampyr) 的 monologue —— Wickhills 的 walls 是 sentient entities（城市自我建造），Six Cities 是 prison（walls keep out something），ceremonies fuel walls; Cazenave's love for the city; Alabeus's device could "end it all. Like a match dropped on a pile of dry leaves."

**关键引语**：

> "What keeps us imprisoned in the city is not out there. It is in here. With us. Below us, around us. Yes, the scientist is nodding: see."（ch24）

> "And you did not build them. / That is what is known. / Here is what is not known: no one did."（ch24）

> "You are not talking to walls. You are talking to the things that make the walls. And you finally made sense of what you were hearing … Your 'device' cannot be a weapon of great import in St. Barothain. They do not have what we have. We have … fuel from the ceremonies. More of it, certainly, than any other city. Here, your invention could end it all. Like a match dropped on a pile of dry leaves."（ch24）

## 节点八：Ferec 与 Hoepsachett 的 moral debate（ch14, ch23）

**叙事概括**：Ferec 与 Hoepsachett 辩论 Seaborne vs Ssura（moral equivalence）；Hoepsachett 提问 "What have we done, as an agency, that's so different from the Ssura?"；Ferec 的 rebuttal（Wickhills 有 mayor/council, counter-terrorism agency）；Ferec's moral uncertainty ("What if we're wrong?").

**关键引语**：

> "“If I had told Alabeus what Seaborne’s mission is, he would have stopped talking to me at once. I do believe he would have … he might have tried to physically attack me. He still thinks Bardanes is the head of some kind of … publicly accountable government department, like the Ministry of Trade, not the kind of invisible where we have to give husbands and wives cover stories and we’re disavowed at a moment’s notice. What have we done, I mean, as an agency, as an organization, that’s so different from the Ssura? We as much as killed his father.”"（ch20）

> "“Don’t exaggerate,” Ferec said. “How melodramatic. It’s not the same at all. St. Barothain is a dictatorship, its people are suffering under hereditary tyrants who refuse to give up rule to the common man, and Alabeus himself was blackmailed into working on his project by people who literally kidnapped his family. Wickhills has a mayor and a council and publicly posted policies and a consultation process. Everything is quite open. And we’re a counter-terrorism agency. You could have told him that without fear of reprisal.”"（ch20）

> "“What if we’re wrong? Wrong about everything? What if the old man took us in entirely, and Bardanes, and Tecuric and her scientists? What if we’re the … the dangerous terrorists who are aiding and abetting the thief who stole fire from the gods for himself, and we’re preventing everyone from benefiting from it by keeping it secret? Simply because he’s asked us to?”"（ch31）

## 节点九：Ferec 的 identity & backstory（ch07, ch30）

**叙事概括**：Ferec 的 backstory —— 孤儿，8 岁杀人，杀死名为 "Ferec" 的伪造者并接管其身份，被 Bardanes 招募进入 Seaborne；Ferec 的 moral crisis（"We are the secret police. It is us."）；Ferec's survival philosophy ("Always a lock, always a key... Just away").

**关键引语**：

> "Almost the first thing I remember learning was that you should always have a door no one can open but you. Always a lock, always a key. Always a hiding place no one would ever, ever look. And a way out. It doesn’t have to lead anywhere better. Just away."（ch30）

> "My father left; my mother killed herself. In my district—no, I’m not telling you which one it was—that was about, on average, statistically normal. I was five years old. No one took me in."（ch30）

> "I killed a man named Ferec. Why him? He was older than me, with unusual looks—black hair, blue eyes, tall. He looked like me. And he too was a forger. He didn’t leave his house very often. And that was important too—he had a house."（ch30）

> "We are, as you said, not only no better than Alabeus’ secret police. We are the secret police. It is us. What will we do? What choices are left to us?"（ch30）

## 节点十：Epilogue — Ferec in Sidi Oksaoud（ch35）

**叙事概括**：Ferec（now "Erak"）在 Sidi Oksaoud 的 exile life —— 市场购物、与 Grandmother Rima 的互动、收到神秘信件（sealed, from Wickhills?）；Empress offers secret police role, Ferec refuses; final line "His hands had never been clean and never would be again."

**关键引语**：

> "Then it was exile?” the Empress said, leaning back in her throne. “They exiled you to die? I did not know they did that in your city.” Ferec smiled, not showing his teeth. They didn’t like that here. “My options were removed from me, Celestial One,” he said. The lizard cocked its head. Definitely a hostile. “If I stayed, I was told my life would always be in danger. For those in power remained in power, and I had shown myself to be an enemy of theirs. They did not believe I would cease to be their enemy. But I had no evil in my heart. What I did, I did for the good of my city. Because, in the end, I was unable to … to change it.”（ch35）

> "I suggest you do not do this. If you create a secret police, you will create at the same time many, many enemies of the secret police, as if out of thin air. They will not keep the peace; they will terrify people and make them mistrustful of one another and of your very rule. You think you will be removing thorns from your foot; but I promise you, the thorns will not exist before you seek to pull them out. I speak from experience."（ch35）

> "He had been discovered; the sky had opened upon him, proving itself not to be a mere celestial phenomenon but the skin covering an eye, closed till now. He was seen, known. They had written his name in his own hand: a message meant as a test, to see what he would do."（ch35）

> "His hands had never been clean and never would be again."（ch35）
