## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The first rule of a sale was to have it take place in public, and in daytime. And to insist on it however you had to—laughing about how paranoid your boss or father or broker or mastermind was, sighing, shrugging, acting grim, acting meek, whatever. Absolutely insist. No flexibility on that one."

**中文理解**：交易的第一条规则，是把交换安排在公共场合、大白天进行。并且无论用什么方式都要坚持这一点——拿你老板或父亲或经纪人的偏执当笑谈，叹气、耸肩、装凶、装卑微，怎么都行。绝对坚持，这条没得商量。

**关键词**：sale（交易/交换）；paranoid（偏执的）

**为什么这样写**：这是 Ferec 的交换操作规则——公开、白天。确立了 Ferec 的职业操守。掩护说法是拿老板或父亲或经纪人的偏执当笑谈。公开白天交换是为了保证双方礼貌与文明，Ferec 以此专业的姿态压制 Gillain。

**读者视角提示**：sale 即囚犯交换；这里展现 Ferec 在谈判中的专业经验与职业素养。

---

> **原句 2:** "“We’ll both be happier,” Ferec said. “And I don’t want to stand out here all morning. Do you? The damp is getting right through my coat. We can finish our business, go our separate ways, and get a nice pint and a hot lamb pie for lunch.” Ferec tipped his chin at one of the nearer buildings, adorned with triangular blue flags along its upper level; one of the Watchmen hastily pulled her head back from an upper window. “Zarpiya’s does a good one. With mashed parsnip and goat cheese. And the longer we stand out here, the more likely someone is to recognize you and wonder what you’re doing here. Have you prepared a story to tell them?”"

**中文理解**：Ferec 说："我们俩都会更高兴。我可不想在这儿站一上午——你愿意吗？潮气都渗进外套了。办完事，各走各的，中午喝杯啤酒吃个热羊肉派。"他下巴朝附近一栋挂三角蓝旗的房子一点；一个 Watchmen 匆匆把头缩回楼上窗口。"Zarpiya's 的不错，配捣碎的防风草和山羊奶酪。我们在这儿站得越久，越可能有人认出你，猜你在这儿干什么。你准备好说法了吗？"

**关键词**：a nice pint and a hot lamb pie（一杯啤酒和一个热羊肉派）；mashed parsnip and goat cheese（捣碎的防风草和山羊奶酪）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec 的随和式威胁——"喝杯啤酒吃个热羊肉派"是以友好语气掩藏威胁；"站得越久越可能被认出"是施压。确立 Ferec 的谈判风格：表面随和、实则带刺。

**读者视角提示**：Ferec 把友好当作武器；Gillain 此刻已经失去平衡。

---

> **原句 3:** "Ferec wondered how long it would take him to remember it, and watched casually, not moving his head much, as people filed out of the buildings on either side of the bridge, and the two on the island somewhat sheepishly unshipped a tiny rowboat and paddled back to shore. Ferec didn't speak, only let the silence build, letting Gillain become more and more uncomfortable with their simple proximity. It wasn't so much the fact that he was taller than the councillor, or stronger, or heavier; it was that he was there, and the entire structure of prestige, salary, name-plates, correct forms of address, deferential secretaries, and decision-making ability was not."

**中文理解**：Ferec 想着 Gillain 要多久才能想起来这一点，漫不经心地观察——不怎么动头——人们从桥两侧的楼里出来，岛上两人略带羞愧地卸下一条小划艇、划回岸边。Ferec 不说话，只让沉默累积，让 Gillain 因他们简单的 proximity 越来越不自在。倒不是因为他比议员高、壮、重；而是他在这儿，而声望、薪水、名牌、正确称呼、恭顺的秘书、决策权那些整套东西，都不在这儿。

**关键词**：sheepishly（羞愧地）；deferential secretaries（恭顺的秘书）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec 的心理战——沉默与在场。Ferec 的在场否定了 Gillain 的制度性权力——声望、薪水、名牌、正确称呼、恭顺的秘书、决策权那些整套东西都不在这儿。确立 Ferec 的心理优势。

**读者视角提示**：Ferec 把沉默当作武器；Gillain 的权力是制度性的，而非个人的。

---

> **原句 4:** "“I promise,” Ferec said, “to play exactly as fair as you do.”"

**中文理解**：Ferec："我保证，我和你玩得一样公平。"

**关键词**：play exactly as fair as you do（和你玩得一样公平）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec 的威胁——"和你玩得一样公平" = Gillain 一直在作弊（City 与 St. Barothain 合作）。Ferec 将以 Gillain 的公平程度回报（即毫无公平）。确立 Ferec 的道德弹性。

**读者视角提示**：Ferec 的"公平"是有条件的；Gillain 作弊，Ferec 也一样。

---

> **原句 5:** "Krztevski was allowed to come quietly—clean and scrubbed, in a borrowed gown, emanating the kind of silent, crystallized fury that seems to solidify in the air like snow. Alabeus, who had had to be gagged, and had wet himself—possibly in petty revenge—was not."

**中文理解**：Krztevski 被允许安静地来——洗净、穿上借来的长袍，散发出那种在空气里凝固像雪一样的结晶化愤怒。Alabeus，被堵嘴、并且尿了自己——可能是 petty revenge——却不是。

**关键词**：crystallized fury（结晶化的愤怒）；petty revenge（ petty revenge）

**为什么这样写**：Krztevski 的尊严 vs. Alabeus 的羞辱。结晶化的愤怒 = Krztevski 受控的愤怒。Alabeus 尿了自己——可能是 petty revenge = 他的反抗。

**读者视角提示**：Krztevski 的镇定 vs. Alabeus 的羞辱；Ferec 对 asset 的处置。

---

> **原句 6:** "“And the key,” Gillain said, almost but not quite pushing past Krztevski. / “Here you are.” Ferec handed Gillain the white envelope and waited as he checked it: both body and cap intact. And no proof of authenticity, of course, except that Alabeus’ struggles grew frantic as he saw Gillain handle the pen."

**中文理解**：Gillain："还有 key。" Ferec："给你。" Ferec 把白色信封递给 Gillain，等他检查：body and cap intact。当然无 proof of authenticity，只是 Alabeus 看到 Gillain 拿起 pen 时，变得疯狂地挣扎。

**关键词**：no proof of authenticity（无真实性证明）；frantic（疯狂的）

**为什么这样写**：key 交换——Ferec 把 pen cap 交给 Gillain。无真实性证明 = Ferec 的欺骗（cap 可能是假的或被禁用的）。Alabeus 的疯狂挣扎 = 他对 key 的依恋。

**读者视角提示**：Ferec 的计谋——交出的 pen cap 可能是被禁用的/假的（"Terence... He's clean. They got rid of the … thing."）。Ferec 的计划涉及欺骗。
