# Chapter 8

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Ferec 被 Bardanes 召唤到 Merovec Square，发现 someone impersonated Ferec via old code to lure Bardanes out of his house (trap)；Bardanes 揭示 mayor/councillors at Tanczel's funeral 询问 mission；Alabeus 从 Bardanes 家中 emerge，Ferec 接手 asset。
- **情感弧线位置**：escalation —— trap revealed; Ferec's team (Stangl/Hoepsachett/Maaz) established; Bardanes's anger at infiltration.
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：impersonation/trap; handler vulnerability (private machine hacked); Ferec's team assembled.
- **人物弧线**：Ferec 从 investigator 转为 team leader; Bardanes's fear/anger; Alabeus's grumpy impatience.
- **叙事手法**：第三人称 limited（Ferec POV）；dialogue-heavy; Merovec Square as meeting point.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "I received a cable on my private machine," Bardanes went on quietly. "From you."

**中文理解**：Bardanes: "I received a cable on my private machine. From you." He rose, dressed, rang for Tompkins, began to compose reply. Fortunately, that was the moment he discovered the mistake in the message。

**关键词**：private machine（私人电报机）；mistake in the message（信息中的错误）

**为什么这样写**：Bardanes's private machine compromised — impersonation trap. "mistake in the message" = old code (changed last month) used by impersonator; Bardanes caught it. "You did not know that Alabeus was staying at my house" — trap aimed to remove Bardanes from home (where Alabeus was).

**读者视角提示**：impersonator used old code; knows Ferec's name and timing. "The gap was too small to have been a random guess" = insider knowledge.

---

> **原句 2:** "The sender had known the precise time period at which Ferec would be unreachable by ordinary means—a note in a drop, a messenger, a cable at one of the Seaborne check-in stations—and therefore unaware that anything might be happening with his name on it. The gap was too small to have been a random guess."

**中文理解**：The sender had known the precise time period at which Ferec would be unreachable by ordinary means—a note in a drop, a messenger, a cable at one of the Seaborne check-in stations—and therefore unaware that anything might be happening with his name on it. The gap was too small to have been a random guess。

**关键词**：unreachable by ordinary means（无法通过普通方式联系）；gap was too small（时间差太小）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's analysis — impersonator knew his schedule (when he'd be unreachable). "too small to have been a random guess" = insider with schedule knowledge. Establishes threat level.

**读者视角提示**：impersonator has Seaborne-level intelligence; insider threat.

---

> **原句 3:** "At Tanczel's funeral. The mayor himself came, and two of the councillors."

**中文理解**：Bardanes: "At Tanczel's funeral. The mayor himself came, and two of the councillors." Imitating mayor's saccharine voice: "'We didn't see anything about his operation in Committee.'" Bardanes instantly thought not of Tanczel but the asset。

**关键词**：；Committee（委员会）

**为什么这样写**：mayor/councillors at funeral = City's awareness of Tanczel's death (despite Seaborne's secrecy). Mayor's question ("We didn't see anything about his operation in Committee") = City suspects Seaborne operation. Bardanes lied (heart attack) to protect Alabeus's concealment.

**读者视角提示**：City is aware of Seaborne's operations despite compartmentalization; mayor is "the City" that Alabeus feared.

---

> **原句 4:** "My private machine, Ferec. Mine. At my home. I will hunt them down."

**中文理解**：Bardanes's anger — "My private machine, Ferec. Mine. At my home. I will hunt them down."

**关键词**：hunt them down（追捕他们）

**为什么这样写**：Bardanes's personal violation — private machine at home compromised. Anger palpable ("fur standing on end", "like standing next to a stove"). Personal stakes for handler.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes takes this personally; "I will hunt them down" = declaration of war on impersonators.

---

> **原句 5:** "Tanczel's wife has money now, Ferec. He was insured through us, and she will shortly begin to receive those payments as well as those from his pension. And how in the hells did you know that account existed in the first place, let alone forge a cheque to access it?"

**中文理解**：Bardanes: "Tanczel's wife has money now, Ferec. He was insured through us, and she will shortly begin to receive those payments as well as those from his pension. And how in the hells did you know that account existed in the first place, let alone forge a cheque to access it?"

**关键词**：insured through us（通过我们投保）；forge a cheque（伪造支票）

**为什么这样写**：reveals Ferec's off-book action — forging cheque to give Tanczel's wife money. Bardanes's "how in the hells did you know..." = Ferec's hidden competence. Establishes Ferec's moral compass (cares for Tanczel's family beyond duty).

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's secret action (forging cheque) shows his character; Bardanes's "You are a thorn of prodigious length in my side" = grudging respect.

---

> **原句 6:** "You are a thorn of prodigious length in my side," he said. "Everything you do when I am not watching you is unsanctioned."

**中文理解**：Bardanes: "You are a thorn of prodigious length in my side. Everything you do when I am not watching you is unsanctioned." Ferec: "I'll put the money back by then." Bardanes: "What possessed you to do it in the first place? Did you think you wouldn't be caught?"

**关键词**：thorn of prodigious length（极长的刺）；unsanctioned（未授权的）

**为什么这样写**：Bardanes-Ferec dynamic — handler exasperated by agent's independence. "thorn of prodigious length" = backhanded affection. Ferec's "I'll put the money back" = his pragmatic morality. "What possessed you..." = Bardanes's genuine (if frustrated) care.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's unsanctioned actions are character-defining; Bardanes's frustration masks concern. "I know greater things are moving all around us than ever before" (Ferec's unspoken) = his awareness of stakes.
