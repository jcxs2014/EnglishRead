# 概述

## 全书梗概

Premee Mohamed 的 *Wickhills* 是一部奇幻/间谍长篇，设定在一个被高墙围困的古老城市 Wickhills。城市由六座幸存城市之一构成，墙壁具有某种感知能力（"The walls are listening"），城市的繁荣与古老仪式（ceremonies）相关。故事围绕 Seaborne（秘密情报机构）agent Ferec 的任务展开：护送叛逃者 Alabeus（St. Barothain 的科学家）及其"钥匙"（key）——一枚能影响城市墙壁的装置——到 Wickhills。

Ferec（真名 Vycol）与搭档 Tanczel 在港口执行任务时遭到伏击，Tanczel 死亡。Ferec 将 Alabeus 交付 handler Bardanes（manticore），但 Bardanes 发现 asset 无 clearance level，且 operation 无 Seaborne 正式支持。随后 Ferec 被召集到 Onyx 的隐藏房间，Hoepsachett 检验 asset 容器、ōg（石皮生物）与死者；Bardanes 暗示 Tanczel 可能是内鬼。Ferec 坚决否认，认为是 Alabeus 自己安排了 pursuers。

Ferec 与 Watch 管理员 Velasco 会面，获取 Tourtile murder (case #18923) 的调查细节：子弹击中日历 "Dread Portal"，wrecks 上发现 expensive pen cap，case 已被 Department of Justice 接管。Ferec 推测 "another trap, just like the one we almost walked into"，deduces insider (Watch) involvement。

Ferec 带新团队（Stangl/Hoepsachett/Maaz）进入 Wellip Alley（黑市街区）， encountering Yhata ground-falcon（追踪生物）。Bardanes 召唤 Ferec 到 Merovec Square，发现 impersonator 用 old code 诱 Bardanes 出家（trap）；mayor/councillors at Tanczel's funeral 询问 mission。Alabeus 从 Bardanes 家中 emerge，Ferec 接手 asset。

Bardanes 通过 cable 召集 Ferec 到 hidden courtyard，揭示 City 已开始调查 Seaborne（Stangl/Maaz 死亡），被 pressed in Committee。Ferec 报告 Stangl 为 traitor，Hoepsachett 杀了他。Bardanes 的哲学演讲（chaos vs meaning）；glass-wren signal = hostiles approaching。

City 攻击，Ferec 与 Bardanes 受伤。Ferec 在 Cazenave's safe house 醒来，发现 Bardanes 死，Alabeus 哭泣。Ferec 搜 Bardanes 衣服找到 luck-stone 半块。Bardanes 的葬礼 at Ashforest Memorial（blank marker）。Ferec 的 grief（"the last thing of value I had"）与 City 的 corruption 之问。

Ferec 与 Hoepsachett 对话——他怀疑她为 leak；Hoepsachett 的 moral challenge (Seaborne vs Ssura, "We as much as killed his father")；Ferec 揭露 "Vycol" (real name) and takes pen cap from Alabeus by force。Hoepsachett: "No. Definitely not. But go."

Ferec 与 Bardanes 在 hidden courtyard 会面（before death）；Bardanes 揭示 City 已开始调查 Seaborne；Bardanes 的哲学演讲（chaos vs meaning）；glass-wren signal = hostiles approaching。

Ferec 与 Hoepsachett surveil 建筑，Gus 进入偷取 "key"；对话揭示 Hoepsachett 的 past (Marie Perghins, first kill)；Ferec 与 Hoepsachett 辩论 Seaborne vs Ssura（moral equivalence）；Gus 发送 distress signal（3-3-3）。

Ferec 与 Velasco 在 Olden Sculpture Park 会面；Velasco 报告 pen cap 已被 DOJ 转移、Evidence Vault 中 box 空；一名 blonde woman（purple hat/coat, spectacles）进入 Evidence Vault —— Ferec 推测 "another trap, just like the one we almost walked into"；deduces insider (Watch) involvement。

Ferec 带 Cazenave/Alabeus 进入 Mirrormere（Mer） district，穿过 wall 进入 Mirror Grove（封闭的 district），witness "Proclamation of the Stones" ceremony；Alabeus 用 pen (key) 测试 walls' intelligence; Alabeus's defiance — he knows Ferec/Cazenave need him (the only one who can build and break the device)。

Ferec 与 Hoepsachett 在 Cardston's（百货商店）设 bait 陷阱，引出 Seaborne agents（Zeri, Samsy, Conrade Le Fer）；Ferec 用 knife 威胁 Zeri 获取信息；Hoepsachett 作为 "Margrit" 伪装。

Ferec 在 Museum of Dynamic Curiosities 设 trap，引出 Krztevski (City official)；Cazenave's glamour conceals; Krztevski 被捕，带入 Low tunnels；taiq (St. Barothain agent) 追踪出现。

Ferec 与 Councillor Gillain 在 Menwaki Bridge 进行 "sale"（prisoner exchange）—— Krztevski 归还，Alabeus 交付，key (pen) 交给 Gillain；Ferec 获得 immunity/amnesty/resettlement for team + Velasco；Ferec 的 trick (pen cap may be disabled) + "I promise to play exactly as fair as you do."

Ferec 被捕，mayor 宣布 state of municipal emergency；Ferec 在 infirmary queue 看到 Velasco (injured) 和 Morecombe；mayor's speech；Ferec sees "the beast" (real, running on rooftops) — "Long may it run" — chapter ends with Ferec's final defiance.

Epilogue — Ferec（now "Erak"）在 Sidi Oksaoud 的 exile life：市场购物、与 Grandmother Rima 的互动、收到神秘信件（sealed, from Wickhills?）；Empress offers secret police role, Ferec refuses; final line "His hands had never been clean and never would be again."

## 主题

1. **城市作为活体（City as Living Entity）**：Wickhills 的 walls 是 sentient entities，ceremonies fuel them；Six Cities 是 prison（walls keep out something）；Alabeus's discovery (walls are listening) 是 novel 的 core fantasy twist。

2. **秘密警察与道德困境（Secret Police & Moral Dilemma）**：Seaborne 的 "public health" 自我正当化；Ferec 的 moral crisis（"We are the secret police. It is us."）；Hoepsachett 的 moral challenge（"What have we done, as an agency, that's so different from the Ssura?"）；Ferec 最终拒绝在 Sidi Oksaoud 重建 secret police。

3. **身份与流放（Identity & Exile）**：Ferec 的 stolen identity (Vycol → Ferec)；Bardanes 的 death（"the last thing of value I had"）；Ferec 的 exile to Sidi Oksaoud；"His hands had never been clean and never would be again."

## 人物弧光

- **Ferec/Vycol**：orphan → killer → forger → Seaborne agent → investigator → exile. His arc is defined by moral reckoning and the search for purpose ("until you understand that you live in a city, not just your own head").
- **Bardanes** (manticore handler)：ancient, wise, transactional; dies protecting Alabeus/Ferec. His monologue (ch08) reveals Seaborne's origins.
- **Alabeus** (scientist)：defector from St. Barothain; his discovery (walls are listening) is the novel's core twist; his "key" is dual-use (weapon/defense).
- **Hoepsachett** (archivist/field agent)：moral conscience; first kill (Marie Perghins); questions Seaborne's legitimacy; refuses exile.
- **Tanczel** (Ferec's partner)：killed in ch02; his death haunts Ferec.
- **Cazenave** (vampyr)：ancient, loves the city; provides safe house; ground rules (no feeding).
- **Gus** (15-year-old thief)：child prodigy; 158 commissions; escapes to Synfolde.
- **Velasco** (Watch administrator)：nervous informant; provides intelligence on Tourtile murder.

## 叙事结构

- **Part 1 (ch01-02)**：Ferec 与 Tanczel 的 surveillance → infiltration → Tanczel's death.
- **Part 2 (ch03-08)**：Debrief (Bardanes) → Onyx examination → Wellip Alley → Merovec Square (trap) → Bardanes's monologue (Seaborne origins).
- **Part 3 (ch09-15)**：Carriage journey → safe house → Mirror Grove → Indigo King → Ferec/Bardanes meeting → glass-wren signal.
- **Part 4 (ch16-22)**：Alabeus's monologue (walls are listening) → Cazenave's monologue (city as living) → recovery → Ferec's identity revelation → Bardanes's death → funeral.
- **Part 5 (ch23-33)**：Ferec's moral crisis → confrontation with Hoepsachett → Cardston trap → Eldebald (tracking device) → Museum trap → Menwaki Bridge sale → arrest → beast revelation.
- **Epilogue**：Ferec in Sidi Oksaoud; Empress offers secret police role (refused); mysterious letter; "His hands had never been clean and never would be again."

## 城市 Wickhills

- **结构**：slice of pie — broad landward border tapering to point where river Weck meets ocean.
- **特征**：windmills, canals, Market Street Wyrm, dragon shrine, aerial wildlife (house swallows, pigeons, angels, streetlights).
- **社会**：guilds (Dockworkers', etc.), Watch, Seaborne (secret), City Council, mayor.
- **非人类种族**：manticore (Bardanes), vampyr (Cazenave), ōg (stone-skinned), ground-falcon (Yhata), silkens, grem, ikeli, faune, griffig, etc.
- **地下城市**：Low, Holloway, Synfolde — parallel society with eye-stones, funicular, aquarium, Indigo King's realm.
- ** ceremonies**：Newbinding Hold, Feast of Embers, Proclamation of the Stones — fuel the walls.
- **Six Cities**：Wickhills + 5 others, isolated by "whatever is out there" (the vanishings).
