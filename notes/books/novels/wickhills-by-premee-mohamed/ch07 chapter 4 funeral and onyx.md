## 精读

> **原句 1:** "After the funeral Ferec turned down various offers of lifts—there was no wake—and walked through the cemetery to the Grossaert Street gate, feeling Bardanes’ eyes on his back the entire way."

**中文理解**：葬礼后 Ferec 拒绝各种 lift offers（no wake），walked through cemetery to Grossaert Street gate，feeling Bardanes' eyes on his back the entire way。

**关键词**：turned down（拒绝）；wake（守灵）；Grossaert Street gate（街道门）

**为什么这样写**：opening establishes Ferec's isolation (no wake, walks alone) and Bardanes's surveillance (eyes on his back). "feeling Bardanes' eyes" = handler's watchful presence even off-duty.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes attends the funeral — handler's personal involvement beyond protocol. Tanczel's death has handler-level significance.

---

> **原句 2:** "It was of no account, really of no account. The man he had killed on Thursday was not his first kill, nor his fifth or dozenth or twentieth. He had killed a man long before his first Prosperity Day, traditionally held when a child was ten; now, he remembered little about the death except that he had stood over the body for a long time, too long, waiting to feel something other than overwhelming relief. Relief and gratitude and awe that it had been so easy."

**中文理解**：It was of no account, really of no account。The man he had killed on Thursday was not his first kill, nor his fifth or dozenth or twentieth。He had killed a man long before his first Prosperity Day (traditionally held when a child was ten)；now he remembered little about the death except that he had stood over the body for a long time, too long, waiting to feel something other than overwhelming relief。Relief and gratitude and awe that it had been so easy。

**关键词**：Prosperity Day（Wickhills 传统，孩子十岁时的仪式）；overwhelming relief（压倒性的解脱）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's killing history — child killer (before age 10), desensitized. "waiting to feel something other than overwhelming relief" = emotional numbness as survival mechanism. "Relief and gratitude and awe that it had been so easy" — disturbing calm about violence, establishes Ferec's moral complexity.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec is a trained killer since childhood; his "no account" framing is self-protective. Tanczel's death = "collateral damage" he "could not count... and stay sane."

> **原句 3:** "No we weren’t. We were not. In my bones I know it. I know what it’s like to be followed, dammit."

**中文理解**：Ferec's internal monologue: "No we weren't. We were not. In my bones I know it. I know what it's like to be followed, dammit."

**关键词**：In my bones（凭本能/直觉）

**为什么这样写**：Ferec's instinct vs. logic — he's certain they weren't followed, yet Bardanes implies they were. "In my bones I know it" = professional intuition. The "dammit" shows frustration at being doubted.

**读者视角提示**：Ferec's certainty conflicts with Bardanes's implication; reader must decide whom to trust. This tension drives investigation.

> **原句 4:** "The room smelled of tar, canal scum, and mint-scented disinfectant. Bardanes tugged down a lever set into the wall and hidden fans began to spin."

**中文理解**：room smelled of tar, canal scum, mint-scented disinfectant。Bardanes tugged down a lever, hidden fans began to spin。

**关键词**：canal scum（运河浮渣）；mint-scented disinfectant（薄荷味消毒剂）

**为什么这样写**：hidden room reveal — Onyx's secret space (six tables, corpses). Hidden fans = ventilation for corpse examination. Ferec's discomfort in the space.

**读者视角提示**：Onyx has hidden facilities; Bardanes is investigating something beyond the mission. "for something to say" = Ferec nervous.

---

> **原句 5:** "“I apologize,” Bardanes said. “I have spoiled your dinner.” / “Not at all.” Ferec had a sense of approaching some large, dangerous beast as yet asleep, wondering what would wake it."

**中文理解**：Bardanes: "I apologize. I have spoiled your dinner." Ferec: "Not at all." Ferec had a sense of approaching some large, dangerous beast as yet asleep, wondering what would wake it。

**关键词**：large, dangerous beast as yet asleep（尚未醒来的大型危险野兽）

**为什么这样写**：Bardanes's apology is formal/ironic; Ferec's "large dangerous beast as yet asleep" metaphor captures Bardanes's latent power and Ferec's wariness. The hidden room + corpses suggest Bardanes is pursuing something bigger.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes's "ten years" comment + "Impressive milestone" + Committee mention = Ferec's career evaluation; mission's hidden stakes.
