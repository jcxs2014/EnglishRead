# ch08. Interlude — Bardanes Tells a Story

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Bardanes（manticore handler）向 Ferec 与 Hoepsachett 讲述 Seaborne 的起源、Civil War 历史、manticore 的迁徙史，以及 Alabeus 的身世与 mission 的真正 stakes。
- **情感弧线位置**：exposition/revelation —— Bardanes 的 monologue 揭示 backstory 与 stakes；Ferec 的 直觉被 confirmed。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：handler monologue as exposition；origin story of agency (Seaborne); Civil War history; manticore migration.
- **人物弧线**：Bardanes 从 handler 转为 narrator/historian；Ferec 作为 listener；Alabeus 的 identity fully revealed (Dossvern lineage, Carm Cadir).
- **叙事手法**：first-person monologue (Bardanes)；direct address to Ferec (); historical exposition; meta-commentary on history/revisionism.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "I’ve heard humans say that manticores cannot lie. I’ve also heard them say, less often, that we can see in the dark."

**中文理解**：Bardanes 开篇："I've heard humans say that manticores cannot lie. I've also heard them say, less often, that we can see in the dark."

**关键词**：manticores cannot lie（狮身蝎尾兽不能撒谎）；see in the dark（在黑暗中视物）

**为什么这样写**：opening establishes Bardanes's voice — wry, self-aware, addressing audience directly. "manticores cannot lie" is a human myth he neither confirms nor denies; sets up his theme of truth/lie. "see in the dark" = manticore capability + metaphor for seeing hidden things.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes is an unreliable narrator by design — he admits to lying ("I have lied for him"). The "cannot lie" myth is his ironic frame.

---

> **原句 2:** "Today I wield liars by proxy. Bullets in Seaborne’s gun. It is much easier to lie from afar. Yes, that’s both of you. My bullets, loaded into my gun. To be aimed as accurately as I can with the information I have."

**中文理解**：Bardanes: "Today I wield liars by proxy. Bullets in Seaborne's gun. It is much easier to lie from afar. Yes, that's both of you. My bullets, loaded into my gun. To be aimed as accurately as I can with the information I have."

**关键词**：wield liars by proxy（借他人之手撒谎）；bullets in Seaborne's gun（Seaborne 的子弹）

**为什么这样写**：Bardanes's metaphor for agents as his "bullets"/"liars by proxy" — dehumanizing but honest about handler-agent relationship. "It is much easier to lie from afar" = handler's distance from field. "Both of you" addresses Ferec and Hoepsachett.

**读者视角提示**：Bardanes views agents instrumentally; his honesty about this is itself a form of manipulation/trust-building.

---

> **原句 3:** "Seventy-five years. You never get to the end of this work. There is always more. There is always the need for us to do what we do, to calculate new ways to do it—to raise the walls between the innocent, or at least ignorant, public, and the conspirators who would set a match under their quiet little world and burn it all to cinders."

**中文理解**：Seventy-five years. You never get to the end of this work. There is always more. There is always the need for us to do what we do, to calculate new ways to do it — to raise the walls between the innocent, or at least ignorant, public, and the conspirators who would set a match under their quiet little world and burn it all to cinders.

**关键词**：raise the walls（筑墙）；conspirators（ conspirators）；burn it all to cinders（烧成灰烬）

**为什么这样写**：Seaborne's mission statement — protect innocent public from conspirators. "raise the walls" = information barrier as protection. "set a match under their quiet little world" = threat to social order. Establishes agency's self-justification.

**读者视角提示**："innocent, or at least ignorant" — Bardanes's cynicism about public; walls between public and conspirators mirror agency's internal compartmentalization.

---

> **原句 4:** "I was there, and even at that time I saw things I was not meant to see."

**中文理解**：Bardanes on Civil War: "I was there, and even at that time I saw things I was not meant to see." The "four" majors; Coalition of Unified Citizens were "awful traitors" only in retrospect.

**关键词**：；

**为什么这样写**：Bardanes as eyewitness to history, but admits to seeing "things I was not meant to see" — privileged knowledge. "They weren't traitors just yet" = history is written by victors; traitor status is retrospective. Establishes Bardanes's age (~124+ years) and deep historical memory.

**读者视角提示**：Civil War history is contested/revisionist; Bardanes's account may be biased. "I was there" claims authority but also implies he's a survivor with perspective.

---

> **原句 5:** "Alabeus, the man you removed from the Caranton Hill, belongs to a lineage, not a family, a great amorphous branching shrub, I might say--to which my own lineage also belongs."

**中文理解**：Alabeus belongs to a lineage (not family), a great amorphous branching shrub; his people and Bardanes's knew each other in Carm Cadir, a mountain city with humans, manticores, goblins, dwarfen-folk, etc.

**关键词**：lineage（血统/世系）；amorphous branching shrub（无定形分枝灌木）；Carm Cadir（山城名）

**为什么这样写**：establishes Alabeus's identity and his connection to Bardanes (Dossvern lineage). "great amorphous branching shrub" = loose, extended kinship network. Carm Cadir as multi-species city establishes worldbuilding (Wickhills is not unique in diversity).

**读者视角提示**：Alabeus's "piece" (snail-stone) connects the two lineages — a mutual-aid token. His defection from St. Barothain is the mission's core.

---

> **原句 6:** "He says he has proof of what he speaks; he says he stole this proof, and brought it with him. What he has stolen, I do not rightly know. I do know that in the single message I received before he came, he requested that I keep his arrival concealed from the City itself, until he could be certain he was safe."

**中文理解**：Alabeus has proof (stolen) of what he speaks; Bardanes doesn't know what. Alabeus requested concealment from the City until safe. Bardanes has lied for him, falsified documents, pretended to be elsewhere.

**关键词**：proof（证据）；concealed（隐藏）；falsified documents（伪造文件）

**为什么这样写**：establishes the mission's stakes — unknown proof, concealment from City. Bardanes's "I have lied for him, I have falsified documents for him" = his personal risk. "Do not make me regret it" = trust extended to Ferec/Hoepsachett.

**读者视角提示**：the object (snail-stone? or something else) is the proof; Bardanes's reaction to it (ch05) suggests it's more dangerous than expected. "A door remains open. Things will come through it."
