# 《Unspeakable Things》情感节点

## 节点一 · 石棚下的搏斗与相认（ch02）

初遇以袭击开场：石棚下的女孩把男装的 Olivia 扑倒锁喉，直到帽子落地才认出「都是女孩」。搏斗、误认、分食一枚煮蛋——敌意与亲昵在十分钟内走完，欲望以疼痛的形式第一次报到。

"Before I could move, she’d knocked me to the ground. I fell on my back, tried to shout, but she’d already locked an arm around my throat. … With all my remaining strength, I flung myself backward."（ch02）

"I stared at her, cap in hand. Then I understood. My hair. She rubbed her chin, wincing. “You’re a girl. You right knocked me out.”"（ch02）

"I smiled, peeled my egg, and ate it, watching her do the same. She ate slowly, catching crumbs on her fingertip and touching it to her tongue. Watching her, I felt a shiver of diffuse pleasure, a sense of coming adrift from my mind but not my body."（ch02）

## 节点二 · 彼得·潘之夜与双重自我（ch03）

母亲带她看父亲演虎克；父亲打破第四堵墙直视小 Olivia，她第一次自觉「既是奥利维娅又是奥利弗」。性别在这本书里从不是转变，而是与生俱来的两腔室。

"I saw Peter Pan only once, when I was very young. My mother still lived with us then, and she took me to the matinee to see my father playing Hook. The experience cleaved my life in two, just as my meeting Tamsin did years later."（ch03）

"And I wasn’t frightened—not until late in the play, when my father broke the fourth wall to stare directly at me. In that terrifying moment, I glimpsed the desperate fear behind Hook’s bravado, his knowledge that the crocodile with the ticking clock in its belly was never far away. When I grew older, I understood where that fear had come from: To him, the crocodile was real."（ch03）

"That day marked the first time I thought of myself as both Olivia and Oliver. My heart was never divided; my self flowed between its chambers, now a girl, now a boy. When I was young, my bohemian parents indulged my penchant for boys’ clothing."（ch03）

## 节点三 · 厨房里的身世炸弹（ch09）

一支 Abdulla 香烟换来的不只是 Tamsin 的姓氏——Olivia 得知自己是臀位难产、由 Tamsin 之母 Anna Griggs 从鬼门关拉回来的孩子。两家的债在出生夜就已立据。

"Mrs. Fletcher balanced her cigarette on the edge of the bowl. “That would be Tamsin Griggs. Her mother was Anna Griggs—Anna Eudy. I knew Anna all my life; we were at the village school together. Yes, Tamsin’s her girl. She had only the one child who lived. You met her on the moor?”"（ch09）

"We didn’t realize what the problem was till Anna put her hand in there and felt your little foot. You were a breech birth. Anna could feel where the cord was wrapped around your little foot like a snagged line."（ch09）

"“Accidents happen, bird. You come to me. Not a doctor, not some girl tells you she can help. You come to me. Do you understand?” Mrs. Fletcher turned and grabbed a ladle. “Don’t you ever trust a man. You go on, now. And keep your tongue in your mouth.”"（ch09）

## 节点四 · 窗台上的初吻（ch16）

一个清晨的窗座、一次「同时看见你们两个人」的对视，初吻把未名饥渴第一次落到实处；Tamsin 的恐惧换来 Olivia 的三重誓言——保护、留住、占有。

"Before I could, Tamsin closed her hand around mine. She kissed my fingers, her lips cool against my skin; pressed my fingers, hard, against her mouth. She let my hand drop and leaned closer until her lips touched mine."（ch16）

"We stared at each other in silence. I saw my terror mirrored in her eyes—fear that I would flee, that I would demand she leave Kerrith, that whatever had just happened had changed the world irrevocably."（ch16）

"Most of all, to keep her mine. My solitude had always seemed as natural and impregnable as Gurnard’s Head. I realized now that I was bound to her by what we had just shared, not merely a kiss, but a glimpse of our future."（ch16）

## 节点五 · 浓雾夜与「他摔下去了」（ch23）

Kelville 的悬崖之死是第一桩不可言说之事：Tamsin 湿着头发回房讲述，Olivia 在听懂的同时拿起导演筒——口径、谎言、守夜，全部当场排定。

"So I did. He grabbed me and tried to kiss me, tried to stick his hand in my coat. I pushed him away and…"（ch23）

"I stared at her empty black eyes, mouthed the word No as she whispered, “He fell.”"（ch23）

"“They’ll believe me,” I said. “Martin and my father know that Kelville goes out to smoke and do his stupid perambulations. Just don’t tell them you saw him or went with him.”"（ch23）

## 节点六 · 母亲的旧案与口径令（ch26）

Lundy 上门之前，Tamsin 交出自己的底档：母亲被勒死、凶手逍遥、死于火灾、她被盘问过。两人练好同一套「照常睡觉」，警车碾上车道时秘密已成为制度。

"“They found her on the moor. She’d been strangled. Everyone knew the guy who’d done it."（ch26）

"The police arrested him, but they couldn’t prove anything, so they let him go. Then he died in a fire. That’s when Lundy questioned me.”"（ch26）

"Her expression silenced me. “No matter who comes from the police, don’t say a word more than you have to when they ask questions. Tell them we went to bed same as usual, that’s all."（ch26）

## 节点七 · 请柬与四张梅花（ch29）

Ledges 的请柬把 Olivia 照亮、把 Tamsin 烧伤——「找男人关笼子」「假装我是玩具」之后，她以凶牌应下同行。爱的裂缝第一次以占卜的形式出示。

““You’re just like them.” Her hatred was focused on me, not my cousin. “Looking for a man to keep you in a cage. You’ve been pretending with me, like I’m a bloody toy.””（ch29）

“She was breathing too fast, her chest moving up and down like a bird’s. Behind her rage was something I hadn’t seen before—fear. No, not fear but terror.”（ch29）

““Not for me.” Tamsin slipped the card back into the deck. “But maybe for you.””（ch29）

## 节点八 · 毒芹与「如果是错的呢」（ch54→ch57）

溪边采毒是教学也是立项；午餐桌上一口假噎送走 Dickie。毒发之前 Olivia 就问出了那个无法撤回的问题——怀疑迟到，是共谋者独有的刑期。

"“That’s right. Water hemlock, they call it sometimes. It fools you. But it’ll kill you in a few hours. Sometimes less.”"（ch54）

"Tamsin smiled. She lifted her face to the sun so that her freckles stood out against her pale skin. “Dickie Soames’s lunch.”"（ch54）

"What if we were wrong? What if he had no intention of writing about us, what if he’d torn up the French postcards, what if he had far more interesting, well-known names and faces to pursue?"（ch57）

## 节点九 · 楼梯栏杆与封口令（ch69–ch70）

Simon 摊出三桩命案与苏格兰场的电话；栏杆断裂后，黑暗里的 Rex 捡走了全部证词。「你说的每一个字我都听见了」——这句话把 Olivia 交给了第四个知情人。

"A thread of gray moonlight spilled from a window to the floor. My cousin lay on his side, one arm behind him, the other curled toward his chest. His neck was bent as though he’d fallen asleep, chin resting on his chest."（ch70）

"“I know. I heard everything you said.”"（ch70）

"“You have to. Wait by the main door with the others. Don’t tell anyone about this. Do you understand?” His fingers tightened around my chin, hurting me. I tried to wrest free but he was far too strong. “Do not say a thing to anyone. About Simon or about seeing me.”"（ch70）

## 节点十 · 纵火、求婚与「All right」（ch81）

舞会的火是 Tamsin 的哭声写成的；Rex 在初遇的画廊里开出价码——三桩死刑换一场婚姻。Olivia 的回答不为爱情，只为名单上那些还不能死的人。

"“You didn’t have to stay. I saw you dancing with him. You wanted him. Like you want me.”"（ch81）

"“I’m not asking you,” he went on. “Your friend murdered three men. You were complicit in one of those murders.""（ch81）

"My fear was gone, replaced by a cold clarity, the knowledge that I had power still—to make a choice, if nothing else. I wouldn’t doom Tamsin to execution, or myself. I wouldn’t let this man destroy Martin or my father."（ch81）

## 节点十一 · 尾声：婚礼与「他们已经在这儿」（ch82）

婚礼按意外销案的节奏举行；Martin 的拥抱是全书最后一次无条件的爱。海湾边幸福被说过两次，蓝胡子被 Tamsin 说了三次——然后她们手牵手走向大海。

"I never saw Kerrith again."（ch82）

"Ever since I’d told her about the hairbrushes I’d found in Rex’s nightstand, Bluebeard’s Castle no longer seemed a joke to Tamsin."（ch82）

"“It’s all ours now. We’re going to be so happy here,” I assured her. “So very, very happy.” And we continued walking, hand in hand, down to the sea."（ch82）
