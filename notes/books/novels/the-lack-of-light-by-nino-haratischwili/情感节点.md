# 情感节点

## 节点一：翻越栏杆（1987）

四个女孩翻越栏杆潜入植物园——这是"童年的最后庆典"，也是"友谊的起点"。Dina 是第一个翻越的人，Keti 是第二个，Ira 和 Nene 紧随其后。

> "The evening light was tangled in her hair."

> "She was nearly there; at any moment she would overcome this barrier, too, would press her body against the railing with all her strength until, putting up only a feeble resistance to her weight, it succumbed with a soft groan."

> "We're fourteen again; no bars, no locked gate can stop us making our way to freedom."

---

## 节点二：动物园的谋杀（1991）

Keti 与 Dina 为救 Rati 筹集资金，在示威中目睹谋杀。Dina 拍摄了 Keti 跪在血与呕吐物中的照片——"动物园"。Keti 选择了逃跑而非见证，用"指南针交换了生存"。

> "I choose Dina's life and mine, and my brother's future, which I am carrying in my coat pocket. I choose us over the red-haired man."

> "Everything is now; everything dissolves; all that was, all that will be, is erased."

> "I ran through the lurking, lengthening shadows like a predator, toward the gully that was to be our salvation."

---

## 节点三：Nene 与 Saba 的初吻（1990）

Nene 在庭院的秋千上吻了 Saba Iashvili——这是她的"初恋"，也是她的"叛逆"。

> "I kissed him," she cried, burying her face in the back of my neck.

> "You kissed Saba Iashvili?" "Yes. He's such a chicken, I could have waited years before he made any kind of move himself."

> "We left the room for a minute, and he went into the bathroom, so I just followed him. The way he stood there with this baffled look on his face—it was all too much for him, he went bright red, God, he was so sweet!"

---

## 节点四：Saba 之死（1993）

Otto Tatishvili 在 Zqneti 的房子里枪杀了 Saba——Nene 目睹了一切。

> "He shot Saba in the heart. Right in front of Nene."

> "I was her alibi! Keto, you have to understand, I was my excuse!"

> "That love cost him his life." His tone was cold, dismissive.

---

## 节点五：Dina 与 Rati 的决裂（1993）

Rati 发现 Dina 与 Zotne 的关系后，称她为"whore"。Dina 用刀刺伤了 Rati 的大腿。

> "Did you enjoy it?" he asked, and looked up, an unfamiliar expression on his face. He had never looked at her like this before: with revulsion.

> "Fucked me." She looked me in the eye as she said it, as if savoring the full extent of the destruction she was wreaking in me.

> "I'll kill you, both of you, I'll . . ." he screamed. But at the same time, something happened: his expression changed; her proximity disarmed him.

---

## 节点六：Ira 的"背叛"（1997）

Ira 作为检察官揭露了 Zotne 的毒品帝国——Nene 视之为"背叛"。

> "I loved you, Nene," Ira begins, carefully. "That's possibly the only truth you'll accept. I loved you as a friend, as a person, as a woman."

> "I wanted to set you free—I wanted you to be free. When will you finally understand that?" says Ira.

> "If, now, I could go back in time, I would spare you my love."

---

## 节点七：Rati 之死（1998）

Rati 死于海洛因过量——Keti 发现了他的尸体。

> "He had died at home, in his bed, the tourniquet still on his arm, eyes rolled back, neck stretched too far, a soot-blackened teaspoon at his feet."

> "He must have stolen from someone and bought himself a double dose. It was quick."

> "After Rati's funeral, she was completely beside herself."

---

## 节点八：Dina 之死（1999）

Dina 在工作室用体操环的绳子自杀——Keti 发现了她的尸体。

> "She, whose life would end in the last year of this leaden, sick, suffocating century, in a noose improvised with the rope of a gymnastics ring."

> "I didn't find Dina! Whatever gives you that idea?"

> "I scream. I scream with all my might, then and now. I scream until all the air has been squeezed from my lungs."

---

## 节点九：Keti 的逃离（1999）

Keti 逃离格鲁吉亚，在 Dresden 与 Reso 生下儿子 Rati。

> "I went to Kyiv. I stayed in that huge, empty hotel. I followed Reso everywhere he went, and carried out his instructions to the letter."

> "And then there was the pregnancy. I was convinced I wasn't going to keep the baby."

> "It was Reso who gave me the strength to believe that I could cope."

---

## 节点十：布鲁塞尔的重聚（2019）

三个女人在布鲁塞尔重聚，面对 Dina 的遗产与彼此的创伤。

> "She'd be happy today, here," says Ira, and we want to believe her. I want to believe her.

> "All the beautiful things were there too, Keto. We had all of that as well," she says, and her eyes are so full of kindness, of reconciliation.

> "My pen races across the paper; I capture us in fragile lines."
