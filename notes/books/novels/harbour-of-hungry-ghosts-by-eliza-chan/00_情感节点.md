# Harbour of Hungry Ghosts by Eliza Chan — 情感节点（10 个）

## 情感弧线总览

这本书的情感曲线不是「相恋—误会—和解」，而是一条**学会分辨**的曲线：Kiamling 从「杀得干净」的学徒，被一场火灾夺走祖母，再被一位京官、一位妓院老板娘、一个洋人文官轮番逼着去看清——哪一刀该落、哪一句该咽、哪一个人值得她把路改掉。全书最陡的落差在最后三章：她在除夕夜从阴间把祖母拽回来，紧接着就在箭号的甲板上，用一把铜钱剑把那个说「我爱你」的人捅到没柄。收尾不给胜利，只给一笼点心、一张照片、三炷香。

### 1. 抿嘴的判词与供上神龛的父母（ch01–ch02）

**节点叙事**：全书从「错」开始。她杀得干净，却杀错了分寸，祖母不说话，只把嘴唇抿成一条线。回到家，那句「你母亲会仔细得多」把死去的父母请上神龛，也把她按在标准之下。她对标尺的反应是顶嘴，顶完又偷偷把白骨用脚蹭土盖住——这个细节把她整个人交代完了：要强、心虚、嘴硬。

**关键引语**：

> "Too late to consider the ways she could have contained the geongsi rather than dispatching it. Too late to investigate what had gone wrong. Por Por did not voice her disapproval; the thin line of her pursed lips was enough."（ch01）

> "Two years her junior, Jingling was day to Kiamling’s night. Other families had gold and jade, rice fields and orchards, learned scholars and herbalists. The Au family had Jingling. Even in their darkest moments, Jingling was a spring breeze on a sweltering day."（ch02）

### 2. 茶楼里骂回去，鬼节上被否认（ch03–ch06）

**节点叙事**：她在华人街与洋人世界之间来回试手：茶楼里用粤语把辱骂原样奉还，还泼了一壶滚水；盂兰节上，总督拍拍祖母的手背说「你们的鬼不存在」，顺手安排她进教会学校。她第一次意识到，这个城里最不讲理的怪物穿着礼服、说一口过得去的粤语。

**关键引语**：

> "“I was just wondering if all your bastards were as ugly as you,” she replied in Cantonese, cushioning her words with a cheery smile. She paused but had judged correctly. She had learnt English. Absorbed it on the docks, in the teahouses and from half a dozen other casual jobs she’d done over the years."（ch03）

> "Bowring turned to Por Por and patted her on the hand like she’d come begging for coin. He spoke in passable Cantonese. “My dear lady, don’t you worry. I can assure you, your ghosts aren’t real. Haven’t we talked about this? Why don’t you enrol your granddaughters in the church school?”"（ch06）

### 3. 屋顶跃下的祖母，与空下来的家（ch07–ch09）

**节点叙事**：火灾那一夜是全书的断点。祖母从屋顶跃下、骑上兽背，用一叠符截断火路——那是她第一次亲眼看见「最强」是什么样子；紧接着一枪打穿祖母的肩膀，怪物把她叼走，Kiamling 赤脚追到裂口前，听见一句「放手」，就真的站住了。此后她独自撑家：编出「闭关」的说法稳住客户，用一壶真阳酒揭穿白蛇精，用「祖母会怎么做」把自己从床上拽起来。

**关键引语**：

> "Por Por leapt down from a rooftop, a handful of fu talismans in her hands. She landed on the monster’s back, riding it like a warhorse, throwing fu down around her. Kiamling had never seen her move so fast, blurring as she rocked."（ch08）

> "What would Por Por have done in her place? The old woman would have rolled her sleeves up and got on with it. Food did not buy itself. There was no point wallowing while there was work to be done."（ch09）

### 4. 一个会念错名字的盾（ch12–ch14）

**节点叙事**：被门卫轰出总督府之后，她撞上 Archie，用「教中文」换到走进去的资格——两人的关系从第一秒就是交易。她在鸦片馆里吞符开天眼，看见昏迷的 Ng Choy 被一圈深蓝描边，满屋找不到该驱的东西；跌倒时被他接住，出门只用一句「只是工作关系」把那一抱收起来。这段感情的第一个动作，就是她把温度重新塞回公事里。

**关键引语**：

> "“Yes, yes, perfect.” Kiamling tried to ignore how he had mangled her name. Her mind schemed as she glanced behind her furtively. Archie would provide excellent cover for her to snoop around Government House. And perhaps he could be persuaded to set up a meeting with Governor Bowring, too."（ch12）

> "Opening her third eye felt like staring into the midday sun. The heat seared the skin of her forehead, concentrated into one spot like a stone pressed there. She could only hold it open for so long before the overwhelming urge to blink and look away could not be denied."（ch14）

### 5. 一次失败的远征：她其实没有立刻追（ch17–ch19）

**节点叙事**：广州之行把她的自尊拆到最里面。先是对妹妹坦白「那道门把婆婆带走了」，却在妹妹一句体贴的安慰里把最要命的一半咽回去——门当时并没有立刻关上，是她自己没敢追。到了广州，同行一个个推脱、退休、把门摔上，她竟因此松了一口气：这样就不必戳破自己的谎。最后乞丐 So 摊开浑仪，给出三个月的期限，主时钟才开始走。

**关键引语**：

> "Tears filled Kiamling’s eyes too. In that moment of panic – of resentment also, truth be told – alone and out of qi, she had done nothing. The yiugwai hunter who said she was ready to step up had failed at the final hurdle."（ch17）

> "Three months. Kiamling had only three months to prepare for the veil to thin, for Por Por to survive in a realm of Western ghosts and monsters. She hardly knew if it was possible, or if her grandmother would be the same person at the end of it."（ch19）

### 6. 妹妹的自由与姐妹的裂缝（ch22–ch24）

**节点叙事**：她认出台上那个歌手是妹妹，先是骄傲，随即被邻桌的调笑点着，冲上去把场子砸成闹剧，也砸掉了妹妹的差事。门外妹妹哭到跪地，把「我不要做被关起来的鸟」说完。此后是出走、空饭桌、拉不下的脸；直到赛马日，Archie 站在她身旁，听她一句「一个字也别说」——姐妹之间的默契不需要解释，只需要有人先闭嘴。

**关键引语**：

> "Jingling sang of the goddess’s exile not as punishment, but as breaking away from restraints and judgement. The world looking up at the full moon with wistful envy, wishing that they too could be brave enough to seize the chance. The refrain was sung in English: To be free."（ch22）

> "“Do not say a word,” Miss Kim said when she returned to Archie. He had seen the outfit in a shop window in the European district and had been quite taken by it. It was not the latest fashion by any means, but it had reminded him of her."（ch24）

### 7. 钟馗的妹妹叫什么名字（ch27–ch30）

**节点叙事**：雨里她站在教堂门外那座「活人与死者的门」下，第一次想道歉；进了春芽阁却用「我是姐姐，我一个人也行」把自己按回暗处。Madam Yau 一把一把撑开纸伞，把被男人虐待、杀害、抛弃的女人一个个摆在她面前，最后一句问话把她钉在原地：钟馗的妹妹叫什么名字——她答不出。回家打开祖母的箱子，她看见的不是法器，是一册册账本和一笔还不完的债。

**关键引语**：

> "Abused, murdered, abandoned by men. These are women who did not get to live their lives. Who fear it will be no better the next time. I’m giving them that chance now. To live. To experience, safely, before passing over. When do women ever get the chance to be anything other than prey?”"（ch29）

> "Yet Por Por had taken on insurmountable debt to care for these displaced strangers. It would be years yet before it was paid off; the interest was eye-wateringly high. Her grandmother was no bamboo shoot, soft and foolish in the ways of the world."（ch30）

### 8. 从「盾」到「软肋」（ch35–ch45）

**节点叙事**：她在他身上第一次单独划出一格：他和那些有乡间宅子兜底的少爷不一样，他也替家里扛。此后两人一起办案：他学着报出「祭坛」、学着把她的名字念对、学着在杀完老虎之后蹲在地上干呕；她在墓园之前一直以为他只是笨拙的好人。感情升温的每一步，都踩在她对「洋人」这个类别的重新分类上。

**关键引语**：

> "Archie was not one of these men. He did not have a country house and rich parents to bail him out. Had he not told her – many times – how he sent money home for his brother and sisters?"（ch41）

> "Others, she was sure, would brag about the kill, take the tiger’s pelt back as a trophy. Archie was not one of those. She linked her arm through his, guiding him back down the slope, holding on for longer than strictly necessary."（ch45）

### 9. 牧师口中的「巫婆的儿子」（ch47–ch51）

**节点叙事**：教堂里，牧师把全港的怪事按成集体癔症，转头把「你求我救你，可你救不了」掷在 Archie 脸上。她这才明白那些低声的谈话不是安慰。此后僵尸围堂、钟楼敲钟、鸡血写符，她一路把他护在身后；直到墓园里，她用桃木棍抵住他的喉咙问出「一直都是你」，他当场变成影兽，咬破她的皮，掉头跑了。

**关键引语**：

> "“Claiming to be devout, cavorting with this wicked woman, coming to me with talk of sin and transgressions. You asked me to save you, but you cannot be saved. You are nothing more than a filthy ruffian, no matter how you dress it up with a gentleman’s education. The son of a witch!”"（ch47）

> "His face contorted into a snout, bones cracking as they shifted position. Sharp fangs protruded from his mouth and his eyes turned red as flame. Kiamling looked for a trace of humanity in him, for a mote of the Archie she knew. There was nothing."（ch51）

### 10. 空城、游行，与巷子里的三炷香（ch55–ch63）

**节点叙事**：决战前夜，全城关张，她们蹲在门外给父母与祖父烧纸钱——这是全书唯一一次把「守门」写成「过日子」。年节游行里，牛头马面开道，她们舞着狮混进警署；除夕夜，她从阴间把祖母拽回来。箭号的甲板上，他说这里有了繁荣，她说街上有妖正在杀人；空膛的枪响了三声，她的剑捅进去没到柄。尾声里她带一笼点心到巷子里，把照片立在蒸笼后，点三炷香，说「希望你不再饿了」。

**关键引语**：

> "At the head of the parade, Ox-Head and Horse-Face – the two guardians of the underworld – marched in their full ceremonial uniforms, setting a slow, even pace. Behind them, ten spider demons as big as horses, webs trailing from their abdomens like fine veils. Their mandibles clacked together, adding to the drumbeat."（ch57）

> "Words that she did not know how to articulate and probably never would: the colour of unbridled joy, the smell of his collar against her nose and the feeling of her hands running across the back of his shirt. Those three words in English were still three words in Cantonese. They were not enough, but they were all she had left to offer."（ch62）

> "People said it was the empire on which the sun never set. Well, those people had not met Au Kiamling. If she had to, she would shoot down the sun itself."（ch63）
