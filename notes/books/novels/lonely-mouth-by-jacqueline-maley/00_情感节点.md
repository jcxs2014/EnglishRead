# 《Lonely Mouth》情感节点

> 全书十个情感节点，按叙事顺序排列。每节点含叙事概括与 2-3 句关键引语（逐字取自原文，text/ 提取件已 grep 验证）。

## 节点一：巨羊下的遗弃（ch01，2002）

**叙事概括**：Barbara 的崩溃视角：被监视的妄想、被女儿的需求围猎的耗竭、堪培拉买地毯的公路旅行——最终她在 Goulburn 巨羊卡车站的厕所里补好妆，把两个女儿留在身后，"果断地"驶向不知何处。全书以她的内部视图开场，不给判决。

**关键引语**：

- "In the months before she abandoned her children in the toilets of a Goulburn truck stop – a building in the shadow of a giant sheep – Barbara became convinced she was being watched."
- "She got in, turned the key in the ignition, reversed out and drove away, firmly, with a sense of resolution, even though she didn't know where she was going or what, really, she was doing."
- "To herself, to her girls, or at all."

## 节点二：秘密仪式的揭底（ch02）

**叙事概括**：十七年后的悉尼。餐厅经理 Matilda 的体面人生下藏着 Id Weekend——每月第一个周末的大麻、暴食与催吐。作者先让读者喜欢上这个克制的人，再交出清单末尾那句零修饰的话。同一章，Angus Dante 的疯传视频把私人耻辱推上公共屏幕。

**关键引语**：

- "I tried to eat just up to the point of satiation but this was not always possible. And then I threw it all up."
- "Angus Dante: Lara's father, my former and estranged not-quite-stepfather."

## 节点三：接力交棒（ch04，1999）

**叙事概括**：Lara 出生后 Barbara 产后崩塌：病房里看电视、以床单罩头拒绝哺乳。Marion 把婴儿抱起、护士调好奶瓶，十岁的 Matilda 接过喂食——一场没有签约的母职过户，期限"第一次，也是永远"。

**关键引语**：

- "She was resolute, like a restaurateur putting up a Closed sign."
- "Later, when I would think about that afternoon, the baton pass between adults that landed my sister with me for the first time, and for all time, I recalled my mother's rejection of Lara with equal parts pain and bafflement."

## 节点四：黄裙子之夜（ch05，2002）

**叙事概括**：Barbara 的生日派对上，十三岁的 Matilda 遭客人性侵——她靠装睡撑过，靠妹妹的啼哭解围。事后她把裙子叠进慈善袋、把记忆"像软罐头一样压平"。床脚那只啤酒罐成为她确认"真的发生过"的终身物证。

**关键引语**：

- "The man left his half-drunk beer can at the foot of my bed. The image of it there was what I held onto in the years afterwards. That's how I knew it had really happened."
- "I took the memory of what had happened and flattened it like a soft can under a strong heel."

## 节点五：地毯与 Big Merino（ch04-ch05、ch01 回环，2002）

**叙事概括**：Angus 以"排练"为由离开，卷走他当初带来定情的波斯地毯；Barbara 坠入"desolate pit"，厨房起火、接到驱逐通知，却坚持"先有新地毯才有新家"——那趟去堪培拉的旅行没有抵达，她把女儿们留在了大美利奴羊脚下。Part 1 的谜团在此全部签收。

**关键引语**：

- "He leaves and he takes the rug with him when he goes, leaving a bare floor and a broken mummy."
- "That was when she took us to Canberra to buy one, but we never made it to Canberra because she left us at the Big Merino."

## 节点六：Butler 家的两年与散场（ch08-ch10，2003-04）

**叙事概括**：寄养生涯最温柔的段落：Will 的篮球与 Kinder Surprise 对半分、carbonara 之夜、她在那栋房子里的第一夜没有催吐。然后是派对上的越界、怀孕的确认、"为名义天主教徒破例"的堕胎、Will 不敢抬头的告别——她烧掉了那张字条，也烧掉了自己唯一一次被"正常"收录的机会。

**关键引语**：

- "I didn't make myself throw up that night."
- "The Butlers were nominal Catholics, and they didn't believe in abortion. But they made an exception for me."
- "I screwed it up in my fist. I borrowed a cigarette lighter from my new roommate and I burned it."

## 节点七：家庭理事会与 Phil Malansky（ch16-ch17，2019）

**叙事概括**：山间疗养院的 Rosemary Room：Barbara 的十页控诉稿与拒不给出的原谅、Angus 的神灯宣言——直到第八步名单念出"Phil Malansky"之名，十七年前的手指第一次有了主人。她当庭否认、索钥离场，与追出来的 Lara 在 gravel 路上完成全书最痛的对账："我为你做过一切""我从没要你做"。

**关键引语**：

- "'There was an incident,' he said. 'At a party. When we were all living at the flat. I . . . came across my friend Phil. In your room.'"
- "'I've done everything for you,' I said quietly. / 'I never asked you to,' said Lara."

## 节点八：青酱与辞职（ch19，2020）

**叙事概括**：Will 的投喂被她吐掉，他只说了一句餐桌礼仪的话，然后抱了她很久。同月她在备餐室撞见 Colson 与 Natalie——"一罐番茄的贬值无关紧要"。第二天她辞去 Bocca，秘密接受大学录取；宜家书桌前，仙人掌从电视机上搬到了新人生上。

**关键引语**：

- "'If I feed you, Matilda,' Will said quietly, 'it's polite to digest it.'"
- "And then I realised that a spell had been broken, that something dangerous had been defanged, and against that, the devaluation of a tomato seemed inconsequential."

## 节点九：Angus 之死与母亲的复仇（ch20，2020）

**叙事概括**：复发的 Angus 醉闯公寓，讨酒、翻走 Trev 误留的药粉——组合杀死了他。Matilda 选择不告诉妹妹他最后那晚来过。葬礼的《Satisfaction》与守灵会上，Barbara 亮出她的脸书私刑：她给 Phil Malansky 的妻子发去私信——"她会一直怀疑他，不管她自己承认不承认"，然后是全书最迟的母爱："没有人可以伤害我的孩子。"

**关键引语**：

- "The powder was, it turned out, a potent mixture of cocaine and ketamine, powerful enough to penetrate a person's bloodstream with alacrity. Mixed with alcohol and prescription opiates, such a combination can kill. It did."
- "'I have sorted out Phil Malansky,' she told me. 'Just wanted you to know.' She smiled a triumphant smile, and then she winked at me."
- "'Nobody hurts my children,' she said."

## 节点十：狼之厅的牙（ch21，2022）

**叙事概括**：巴黎尾声。姐妹在狩猎与自然博物馆交换对亡父讣告的判词（"wolfish"/"狼是优雅的野兽"），在 Diana 女神像前接受"complex"这个最终判词；一个陌生小女孩在斜线游戏里的大笑，让 Matilda 说出全书的最后誓言——把三代女性的牙，翻译成给未来女儿的祝福。

**关键引语**：

- "'My father was described as wolfish,' she says. 'In one of the obits.' / 'Well,' I reply. 'A wolf is an elegant beast.'"
- "I look at her and think: if I have a girl, I will tell her to take up all the space in the world that she wants. I will tell her: tear through life like a wolf. Tear through it with your teeth."
