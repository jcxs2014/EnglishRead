# 情感节点

### ① Song 潜入 Isari 战舰（ch02）

Song 潜入 Isari 战舰寻找妹妹 Lila，被守卫发现后喊出"Don't kill me"。这是全书的起点——Song 从"Shardraku 的战士"变成"为家人而战的逃兵"。

> **原句 1 (ch02):** "Don't kill me."

### ② Valik 面对 hagaron 的恐惧（ch26）

Valik 在 hagaron 洞穴中直面童年的恐惧——Einar 被 hagaron 杀死的记忆。他选择不再逃离，而是以爱战胜恐惧："The fear was strong; his love was stronger."

> **原句 2 (ch26):** "Einar and Sagr, Rolf, and Lara. Memories flashed before his eyes. He felt an undeniable tug deep inside, and the fire in his belly ignited. The fear was strong; his love was stronger."

### ③ Fallon 向 conclave 汇报 Shahin 的死（ch25）

Fallon 用军事语言保护自己，但"his ship"泄露了真实感受。她第一次质疑自己的情感是否正常——"Has something inside of me broken?"

> **原句 3 (ch25):** "She spoke about the annihilation of the ship . . . his ship . . . as if everybody onboard had been perfect strangers—faceless casualties of war."

### ④ TOM 首次宣告活着（ch27）

TOM 在沙漠中触摸花朵后第一次宣告"我活着"。他不再是观察 android，而是活着的存在。

> **原句 4 (ch27):** "I am alive."

### ⑤ Koji 领导 Avarix 起义（ch28）

Koji 在蓝色火焰中召唤 Ryos 力量，击败 Tarquin。他不再是奴隶，而是 Reborn Phoenix——"for the first time in Koji's life, he had hope. His people had hope."

> **原句 5 (ch28):** "for the first time in Koji's life, he had hope. His people had hope."

### ⑥ Valik 率众冲锋（ch30）

Valik 在最后的战斗前喊出"We are the stone!"——他不再逃避，而是直面死亡。

> **原句 6 (ch30):** "We are the stone!"

### ⑦ Fallon 发现父亲是叛徒（ch31）

Fallon 发现父亲 King Seran 策划了一切——包括 Shahin 的死。她不再哭泣，而是销毁 hologram——"she would never weep again. That part of her was gone."

> **原句 7 (ch31):** "It was you all along, wasn't it? Even the attacker outside of my room. You sent him. Why?"

### ⑧ TOM 毁灭 Programmer（ch32）

TOM 拒绝 Programmer，将能量注入通讯塔——"这是我的反抗，我的反叛，我的生命宣言"。他消亡但信息永存。

> **原句 8 (ch32):** "This is my defiance, my rebellion, my declaration of life."

### ⑨ Valik 与 Gregor 相遇（ch33）

Valik 在 Kasborg Castle 醒来，遇见觉醒的 android Gregor。他看到胸口的 hagaron 标志——"那是对未来战斗的勇气召唤"。

> **原句 9 (ch33):** "It was a hagaron."

### ⑩ Song 独自飞向星空（ch29）

Song 送 Lila 回 Rojin 后，独自飞向星空——"但她仍然感受到内心的 pull。目前，这就够了"。

> **原句 10 (ch29):** "But she still felt the pull inside her. For now, that was enough."
