# 《Florescentia (The Luminary)》情感节点

## 节点一：魔时刻的收割（ch01）

午夜，Lucia 离开熟睡的丈夫，下楼到书房把两只 Peculiar 摊成液面，念出 Florescentia。仪式以情色完成，收束在「以欲换运」的自我定价上。

> "The best time to harvest luck was none other than the witching hour. … As my husband lay beside me, tucked away in the night’s hold, the dangling dreams behind his eyelids presented the opportunity to take my leave."（ch01）
> "“Florescentia.” The welcoming of the void with a single word. Once the deed was done, you sent the void back whence it had come with Obscuria. Words older than our religion, the power behind them more ancient than the words themselves."（ch01）

## 节点二：替他锁定的职位（ch02–ch03）

早餐桌上她送 Dorian 出门，心里清楚那个天文台职位早已被她的仪式锁定；同一段叙事里她承认，自己的第一项专利也是靠一场仪式换来的。

> "What he didn’t know was that the position was already his. He would interview, he would compete with others just as qualified, but ultimately, that position had been his the minute my hand dipped through to the Otherside last night."（ch02）
> "There were many ways to farm luck from the Otherside. The easiest was to be intimate with the Peculiar. … Sex worked especially well since larger Peculiars suffered from a rather intense gulosity from their isolation and inability to cross over to our world. … As long as I conducted a controlled release in a strict environment, the process was foolproof."（ch03）

## 节点三：空街与停尸房（ch06–ch07）

酸雨夜她看见一条空无一人的街，横穿过去，马车连环相撞；凌晨她在停尸房拒认丈夫，从警方口中听见「一个女人跑进马路」。

> "The streets had been empty. I’d seen it with my own eyes! I’d swear it, not that I would be believed. … no street was ever empty here. Nobody ever slept."（ch06）
> "What remained of my husband was stored in the morgue of the county. But Dorian lived on, in me, as guilt. A final declaration of my luck running dry."（ch07）

## 节点四：它借他的脸（ch08–ch10）

她强行再开门，招来一只会模仿人声的巨兽；几天后它穿着丧服出现在葬礼上，又戴着 Dorian 的脸回到她家谈判。

> "“Beast,” it mocked in my ear, with my own voice. The word disintegrated into an array of chittering sounds, then underlying whisperings of nothing."（ch08）
> "“I am what holds the stars at arm’s length,” he said."（ch10）

## 节点五：手记的警告（ch15）

父亲的皮面手记不是日记，而是逃命笔记：水是门、关门比开门重要、不要放「欺骗者」进来。她照着做，第一次靠知识召来一只手，也第一次靠知识把它逼退。

> "Heed me, reader. Whether this be my future self or an unfortunate stranger—hear my warning, you will never outrun death twice. Take all that I know and run with what you understand."（ch15）
> "The deceiver lies alone, cursed with knowing all, and possessing none. Do not let him through."（ch15）

## 节点六：海葬（ch18）

Maxim 半夜把她带到东海悬崖，揭开「下葬的是空棺」的秘密；兄妹把遗体送入海中，她念完悼词，听见海里有声音唤她同去，答了一句「今天不行」。

> "“A lot of people see things that are different, and they grimace,” I started … “But not Dorian. Never him. He ran headfirst into any enigma.” … “His only regret would be not exploring them all.”"（ch18）
> "A small, whispering voice called for me to join him. It took only thirty seconds to make a secession, three seconds to act on it. … Not today, I decided. … I would see him soon."（ch18）

## 节点七：从日常到坦白（ch23–ch26）

父亲在电话里只要她读那本日记；她崩溃时，Altair 笨拙地抱住她，又问能不能带他去看更多吃的。几天后它承认自己来这里是为了「死」，又说因为她改变了主意。

> "“If you don’t want to talk,” he began, his weight shifting on his heels, “can you show me more food?”"（ch23）
> "“Because you changed my mind,” he said—and suddenly his hands were holding my face, and his shoulders shrugged forward for our lips to meet again."（ch26）
> "“Do not take it as an insult when I say this—but I’ve always wanted to be insignificant.”"（ch26）

## 节点八：父亲的债（ch34）

彼界的水里映出父亲的交易：用「第一个孩子」换取与母亲的生存。Altair 承认自己只是接单的人，又用一座幻象宫殿求她留下。

> "“Since your union will be bound by your world, as well as mine,” the shadow said, “your firstborn will bear your debt.”"（ch34）
> "Collesano. … “Papà?” I leaned closer to the water, hoping my eyes were betraying me."（ch34）

## 节点九：她终于到了彼界（ch25–ch31）

她在海里下沉时被它接住，获救后仍说自己想去找人；它说不再期待死亡，只想为她去发现；而她终于从一张嘴里落到彼界的岸边，发现满地的「花」都是活的。

> "He could let go, but he didn’t . . . and neither did I."（ch25）
> "It wasn’t death I looked forward to anymore. A new momentum was growing inside me, an urge to discover. … But not for me, for her."（ch27）
> "Peculiars waiting idly by, not flowers at all. … Finally, I was on the Otherside. But where to go from here was still a mystery."（ch31）

## 节点十：铃（ch37）

终章她与父母在幻象中无声告别，与 Altair 摊牌，说完「Humanity」后念出 Obscuria 跳下悬崖；最后在一口安全棺里醒来，一直拉铃，直到听见掘墓人的铁锹。

> "“Goodbye, Altair,” I said with a smile. “You were a life of lessons.” … Then I whispered against his lips. … “Obscuria.”"（ch37）
> "And the ringing would go on for hours before anyone noticed, even longer before the digging would start, and all the while I wondered if I really was returning to the world I knew. … Until I heard the undertakers’ shovels knock on my casket door."（ch37）
