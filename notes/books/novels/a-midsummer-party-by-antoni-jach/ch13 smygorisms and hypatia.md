# 13. Chapter 13

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Smyg 把 Julian 当哲学点唱机——时间四惑、赫拉克利特、维特根斯坦猜谜——随后交出 Olivetti 打印的 SMYGORISMS；Gloria 赶来对撞一场着装战争，午夜前一分钟，Kierkegaard 的假面论给派对盖上哲学邮戳。
- **情感弧线位置**：战场的喜剧间歇：哲学游戏是 Smyg 的求偶替代品；Gloria 登场后， jealousy 的旧火（ch01 的「诱走」公案）由她亲口补全。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现 mentor-apprentice flirtation；反转：自封「laughing philosopher」被Democritus 抢注——Smyg 的每个名号都早有人用。
- **人物弧线**：Smyg：从被骂 jejune 到被 Julian 判「on to something」——唯一被正名的时刻；Gloria：补完二十年前「我甩了他」的版本；Julian：哲学问答机＋金句鉴定师。
- **叙事手法**：格言页直接嵌入（SMYGORISMS 八连）；哲学名词喜剧化（Mr Aris of Totle／to wis 变位）；午夜假面论作章末格言。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "I particularly liked gazing out of the MoMA windows at the street. Having looked at art for two hours, you get attuned to seeing everything as a work of art – even a humble streetside stall selling hot dogs takes on a mystical-luminous appearance when seen through MoMA’s glazed windows. The world appears to be different after looking at art; it becomes suddenly aestheticised"

**中文理解**：回忆一个月前的 Met＋MoMA 一日：看画两小时后，隔着 MoMA 的玻璃望街，连卖热狗的路边摊都带上「神秘发光」的外观——「看过艺术之后，世界看起来不一样了；它突然被审美化了。」
**关键词**：gazing out of the MoMA windows、a humble streetside stall、a mystical-luminous appearance、suddenly aestheticised
**为什么这样写**：把美术馆效应推到街景——「mystical-luminous」的自造词给热狗摊镀上圣像光；「aestheticised」是本章的动词钥匙：艺术训练改变的不是眼睛而是世界。 Julian 与叙述者的友谊建立在共看之上。
**读者视角提示**：记住这个「看后世界」——本书的派对就是一场 MoMA：热狗摊对应喷泉与鲸尸，人人都在给眼前之物加滤镜。

> **原句 2:** "Here goes. Listen to this. This will impress you:
>
>
> Less is more.
>
>
> More is more.
>
>
> But more than more becomes a bore.
>
>
> She looked up expectantly at Julian, waiting for his approval. You like it?
>
>
> I do."

**中文理解**：Smyg 的哲学首演：「听着——这句会镇住你：少即是多。多即是多。但多过头就无聊。」她抬头期待 Julian 的认可：「喜欢吗？」「喜欢。」
**关键词**：Less is more、More is more、more than more becomes a bore、waiting for his approval、I do
**为什么这样写**：以押韵三段论模仿格言——前两句都是名句，第三句用「bore」押韵泄气：她对智慧的模仿止步于节奏；「waiting for his approval」把哲学降格为才艺表演，而 Julian 的「I do」是全场最客气的判词。
**读者视角提示**：对照她的 aphorisms 正业——节奏天赋是真的，思想供给不足。记住「More is more」：它与 ch10 的「I want everything, and I want it all at once」押同一韵。

> **原句 3:** "I often feel I am stuck in the middle of time, and yet at other times I feel like I am outside of time and looking in, then again, thirdly, I feel that time doesn’t exist – I’m standing on an absolute precipice of disbelief – and then again, fourthly, I am having trouble believing that the so-called present actually exists"

**中文理解**：Smyg 的时间四惑：「我常觉得自己卡在时间中间；有时又像站在时间外面往里看；第三，我觉得时间根本不存在——我站在怀疑的绝对悬崖边；第四，我很难相信所谓的『现在』真的存在。」
**关键词**：stuck in the middle of time、outside of time and looking in、an absolute precipice of disbelief、the so-called present
**为什么这样写**：把哲学困惑装进「firstly/then again」的购物清单句式——悬崖（precipice）的崇高比喻接上「so-called present」的市井口气；四惑层层内收：从位置到存在论，最后落在「现在」这个最小的怀疑对象。
**读者视角提示**：这是全章真问题的入口。与 ch06 的「two kinds of time」（clock-time vs time-as-consciousness）连读：Smyg 的谜语终于等来了专业的听众。

> **原句 4:** "Philosophers over the centuries have had a lot to say about time. Aristotle wrote that while the past and the future are imaginable, the present is infinitesimally small.
>
>
> But how does he know that?
>
>
> He doesn’t – he conceptualises it thus. It’s a theory.
>
>
> Because time itself is a slippery concept. It’s often difficult to get a sense of what it really is. We know we are part of time, but it’s difficult to get a grasp of being in the moment, as we are often slipping between states of anteriority and futurity"

**中文理解**：Julian 授课：亚里士多德说过去与未来皆可想象，「现在」却小到无穷。Smyg 追问「他怎么知道？」——「他不知道——他如此概念化它。这是个理论。」结论：时间本身是滑溜的概念——我们总在「先在性」与「后在性」之间打滑（她要白话：之前与之后）。
**关键词**：the present is infinitesimally small、he conceptualises it thus、a slippery concept、being in the moment、states of anteriority and futurity
**为什么这样写**：哲学对话的三级跳——权威（亚里士多德）→祛魅（he doesn't know）→修辞性同情（slippery）；「anteriority/futurity」的术语马上被要求翻译成「before and after」，学术语言的电梯在章内自降一层。
**读者视角提示**：记住「现在小到无穷」——午夜倒计时（章末）正是对「present」的派对测试。Smyg 的直觉（present 不存在）与亚里士多德只差一层窗户纸。

> **原句 5:** "I know one thing about change: The only constant in life is change. I saw that on a desk calendar recently.
>
>
> You are quoting Heraclitus.
>
>
> Who?
>
>
> A pre-Socratic philosopher: Heraclitus of Ephesus, born in the sixth century BC. He’s best known for his statement, No man ever steps in the same river twice. He believed that reality is in a constant state of flux. He was called the weeping philosopher"

**中文理解**：Smyg 献宝：「关于变化我只懂一件事：生活中唯一不变的是变化——最近在一本台历上看到的。」「你在引用赫拉克利特。」「谁？」「前苏格拉底哲学家，以弗所的赫拉克利特，生于公元前六世纪。他最有名的话：没有人能两次踏入同一条河流。他认为现实永远处于流变之中。人称流泪的哲学家。」
**关键词**：The only constant in life is change、a desk calendar、Heraclitus of Ephesus、the same river twice、the weeping philosopher
**为什么这样写**：哲学传播的食物链在一段里走完——台历→经典→无名的提问者；「weeping philosopher」与 Smyg 想当的「laughing philosopher」是古希腊的现成对子，她的自我命名欲望撞上历史编制表。
**读者视角提示**：记住「台历哲学」——本书里 Smyg 的所有智慧都来自这种二手渠道（Seinfeld、Friends、台历）。二手不等于假：她只是没拿到出处。

> **原句 6:** "Here’s a real dinky-di clue: I am ignorant about the weather when I see that it is either raining or not raining. Who said that?
>
>
> You’re misquoting Wittgenstein from his Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus.
>
>
> Correct, you win the chocolates, the slab of beer and the meattray … Don’t you just love good ol’ language games?"

**中文理解**：猜书终题：「给个地道线索：当我看到天在下雨或没下雨时，我对天气一无所知。谁说的？」「你在误引维特根斯坦的《逻辑哲学论》。」「答对了——你赢得巧克力、一板啤酒和一托盘肉……你就是爱这种老派的语言游戏吧？」
**关键词**：a real dinky-di clue、ignorant about the weather、misquoting Wittgenstein、Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus、good ol’ language games
**为什么这样写**：把《逻辑哲学论》的命题改造成澳洲谜语——误引被识别为「misquoting」，知识的口径之争比知识本身更好笑；奖品三连（巧克力/啤酒/肉托盘）是议会选举式的澳式幽默，哲学在这里被请进酒馆。
**读者视角提示**：与 ch06 的「infinite monkey theorem」连读：Smyg 的哲学趣味全靠游戏化存活。记住「language games」——它即将变成她的出版计划。

> **原句 7:** "I’m a writer, with the help of your friend here, Mr Printer, who saved me then discovered me then shamelessly exploited me.
>
>
> Commercialised you, I said, is a better way to express it.
>
>
> But who wants to be a commercial? she said. I, for one, certainly do not"

**中文理解**：Smyg 给 Julian 报履历：「我是作家——多亏你这位朋友，Mr Printer：他救了我、发现了我、又无耻地剥削了我。」「『商业化了你』——这么说更准确，」我说。「可谁想当广告呢？我反正不想。」
**关键词**：shamelessly exploited me、Commercialised you、who wants to be a commercial、I, for one, certainly do not
**为什么这样写**：三动词递进（saved→discovered→exploited）把恩人史写成案例——叙述者的更正（commercialised）只换一个词，语义却从压榨滑向工业；「who wants to be a commercial」的双关（广告/商业品）让她连反驳都像口号。
**读者视角提示**：与 ch11 的「一切冒险都要付账」并读：恩情也有会计科目。记住这组动词——它会在 Smyg 的退场独白里再次变奏。

> **原句 8:** "No thesis, it’s only a gift book – just random thoughts and cute animal photos. I’m supposed to be writing a sequel, but I’m feeling uninspired. Readers love the photos in my book, and some told me that it changed their lives.
>
>
> However, many of the published book reviews are either negative – one reviewer called it jejune fluffery – or patronising. I don’t even know what jejune means"

**中文理解**：Smyg 招认书的内容：「没有论点，只是礼物书——随想加可爱动物照片。续集本该写，但我没灵感。读者爱照片，有人说它改变了他们的人生。可多数书评要么负面——有位评论家骂它是『幼稚的絮叨』——要么居高临下。我连 jejune 什么意思都不知道。」
**关键词**：only a gift book、random thoughts and cute animal photos、changed their lives、jejune fluffery、I don’t even know what jejune means
**为什么这样写**：畅销书的自白结构：先亮底牌（no thesis），再报战果（changed lives），最后引骂评自嘲——「不知道 jejune 什么意思」是全段最诚实的句子：她与自己的差评共用一套不懂。JIan 的义务翻译（simplistic and naïve）让她再次接触自己的失败。
**读者视角提示**：与 ch10 的「bestselling fraud」对账——「骗」的证据链如今包括一篇她读不懂的差评。记住「cute animal photos」：Smygworld 的国民美术。

> **原句 9:** "Oh, and I like to go out with firemen – I’m choosy when it comes to men. They like me at first – they call me a real live wire and a go-getter – but then they sour on me when they get to know me; and to tell the truth, they diminish in my affection too, when they won’t do what they’re told to do. I like a fireman who’s trainable"

**中文理解**：恋爱偏好自白：「我喜欢跟消防员约会——选男人我是挑剔的。起初他们喜欢我，说我是个活泼的电线、女强人；可了解之后他们就对我凉了。说实话，他们不听指挥的时候，我对他们的感情也会缩水。我喜欢可训练的消防员。」
**关键词**：go out with firemen、a real live wire and a go-getter、they sour on me、diminish in my affection、a fireman who’s trainable
**为什么这样写**：把择偶写成驯兽术语（trainable）——双向降温的机制被她坦然摊牌：新鲜感是双向消耗品；「when they won't do what they're told」把浪漫降格为指令系统。与 ch08 的 Lyle 三晚战役互为注脚。
**读者视角提示**：这是 Smyg 全书最不设防的一句——她的爱情理论从不承认对等。记住「trainable」：她的格言、她的狗、她的男人，共用同一本训练手册。

> **原句 10:** "SMYGORISMS – BY SMYG Of BRUNSWICK
>
>
> Life gets in the way of all the best intentions.
>
>
> Am I living the dream or am I dreaming the life?
>
>
> I amuse myself more than I amuse others.
>
>
> Sunshine is better than rain unless you are thirsty.
>
>
> My raison d’être has turned out to be more raisin than being.
>
>
> Benevolence is all well and good, but malevolence gets all the attention.
>
>
> Hypocrisy never goes out of style.
>
>
> Life is temporary but death is forever."

**中文理解**：SMYGORISMS 首页八条（Smyg 制，Brunswick 出品）：生活挡在所有好意图前面。／我在过梦想的生活，还是在梦想我的生活？／我逗自己胜过逗别人。／阳光比雨好，除非你渴。／我的存在理由（raison d'être），结果更像葡萄干（raisin）而非存在（being）。／善行固然好，恶行更吸睛。／虚伪永不过时。／生命是暂时的，死亡是永远的。
**关键词**：SMYGORISMS – BY SMYG Of BRUNSWICK、Am I living the dream、more raisin than being、Hypocrisy never goes out of style、Life is temporary but death is forever
**为什么这样写**：八条里混着真格言（Benevolence 条袭罗什富科的冷）与谐音残次品（raisin/raison）——作者把成色差异原样摊给读者；「Life is temporary but death is forever」是最重的一条：轻浮文体里突然出现墓志铭。Olivetti 打字机与「不给你看」的傲慢给这页纸加了出土文物感。
**读者视角提示**：Julian 的判词在下一块——「写一百条，删到五十」。记住 raisin/raison 这条：它是 Smyg 全书的自嘲压缩包（自以为 raison，活成 raisin）。

> **原句 11:** "But there are female philosophers; one was the neoplatonist, Hypatia of Alexandria.
>
>
> She lived in the fourth and fifth century AD. Hypatia was a mathematician and an astronomer; she was also the leading public intellectual of her day.
>
>
> To teach superstitions as truths is a most terrible thing. She was murdered for her beliefs and for her influence. She challenged accepted belief structures, especially to do with religion"

**中文理解**：Julian 接住「女哲学家都在哪」的性别控诉：「有一位——新柏拉图主义者、亚历山大的希帕提娅。」四至五世纪人物：数学家、天文学家、「当时最领先的公共知识分子」。名言：「把迷信当真理来教，是极可怕的事。」她因信念与影响被杀，「她挑战了被接受的信仰结构，尤其是宗教」。
**关键词**：the neoplatonist, Hypatia of Alexandria、a mathematician and an astronomer、the leading public intellectual、To teach superstitions as truths、murdered for her beliefs
**为什么这样写**：女哲学史以个案形式补课——希帕提娅的三重身份（数学/天文/公共知识分子）正是 Smyg 想要的全部头衔；「superstitions as truths」回指她自己那句「宗教是伪装的迷信」——两千年前的死者替她完成了学校开除之战的论证，代价是被杀。
**读者视角提示**：这是本章最庄重的一块：笑话章节里嵌进一桩殉教案。记住 Hypatia——Smyg 的「笑」与她的「泪」（weeping philosopher）在历史里都有位置。

> **原句 12:** "My favourite ploy was to quote Thomas Aquinas to the teacher and ask, How many angels can dance on the head of a pin? That was always a good one to spice up a dull afternoon. I would add, And why are they dancing? Why aren’t they merely standing on a pin?"

**中文理解**：Smyg 回忆宗教课战术：「我最爱引托马斯·阿奎那问老师：多少个天使能在针尖上跳舞？给无聊的下午提提神。我还会加一句：他们为什么要跳舞？为什么不只是站在针上？」
**关键词**：Thomas Aquinas、How many angels can dance、on the head of a pin、spice up a dull afternoon、And why are they dancing、merely standing on a pin
**为什么这样写**：把中世纪神学的经典难题改造成课堂游击——第二问（为什么跳舞）才是她的签名：给形而上问题追加「动机审查」；「spice up a dull afternoon」把神学降为下午茶的调味料。被开除的代价在此兑换成叙事资本。
**读者视角提示**：对照 Julian 的考据（阿奎那＝angelic doctor）——她拿天使问题砸老师，他拿天使问题致敬大师。同一根针尖，两种扎法。

> **原句 13:** "Well, your friend and I used to go out together, but he was mean to me. He became more interested in my housemate, Katy, than in me; an unforgiveable sin in my book, so I dropped him. We have remained good friends because he has been perfectly well behaved since then – he’s a good listener, and we are simpatico; we like the same books"

**中文理解**：Gloria 向 Julian 交代旧史：「你朋友和我交往过，但他对我不好——他更迷我室友 Katy，那是不可饶恕之罪，所以我甩了他。我们仍是好朋友，因为此后他一直规规矩矩——他是好听众，我们相契，喜欢同样的书。」
**关键词**：used to go out together、he was mean to me、my housemate, Katy、an unforgiveable sin、we are simpatico
**为什么这样写**：三角往事第一次由当事人完整陈述——「mean to me」与 ch01 Katy 版本（I seduced you away）是同一事件的两个版本；「unforgiveable sin」的宗教措辞接住本章的天使与迷信话题：爱情罪亦需教义。Smygworld 之外终于有了第二份证词。
**读者视角提示**：记住这版叙事的所有权：ch01 里「被诱走」的是他，这里「甩人」的是她——两版都对，都由利益方签发。与 ch07 的「You left me」（Miranda 版）并置：每段旧情都有两本账。

> **原句 14:** "Nothing changes after midnight, said Gloria. We will all still be the same people as now.
>
>
> Kierkegaard thought midnight leads to changes, said Julian. He wrote that after midnight, everyone throws off their masks to reveal who they really are – a beguiling idea"

**中文理解**：午夜前一分钟：Gloria 说「午夜什么都不改变——我们还是现在这些人」。Julian 递上克尔凯郭尔：「他认为午夜带来改变——午夜之后，人人摘下面具，露出真面目。一个迷人的想法。」
**关键词**：Nothing changes after midnight、the same people as now、midnight leads to changes、throws off their masks、a beguiling idea
**为什么这样写**：用一分钟倒计时给全书上半场装上哲学闹钟——两个命题（不变论/变面论）正好对应两位说话者：Gloria 自认已无面具，Julian 留着哲学的余地；「beguiling」回收本章第一个词（Gloria beguiling）。
**读者视角提示**：这是 Part One 尾声的主题句：午夜之后，面具还摘不摘？记住这句——Part Two 的火车、酒店与站台，都是这个问题的实验场。

## 一句话总结
Smyg 用时间四惑、维特根斯坦谜语与一页 SMYGORISMS 完成她的哲学成人礼；Gloria 补交二十年前「我甩了他」的版本——午夜前一分钟，摘面具的时辰到了，而火车已经停在门外。
