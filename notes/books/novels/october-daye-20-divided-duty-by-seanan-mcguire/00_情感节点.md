# 《A Divided Duty》情感节点

## 节点一：正午的哭声，与三个倒计时（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书的第一个情感单位是这半日安宁本身：Miranda 四个月大，正午的哭声把 October 叫醒，喂奶、补觉、与 Tybalt 交班照看女儿——读者在这半日里被交代这个家的来路与亏欠：May、Quentin、Raysel，每一段关系都带着旧账。叙述者也在这里交出了她最重的一笔：Gillian，她曾绝望地爱着的女儿，被教着去恨她这个抛下她的人。安宁之下，三个计时器已经上好发条——Raysel 的服罪期还剩三天，女王的庭两天后才开，而唯一的对策只能再买来四个月。本章不制造危机，只负责让读者先爱上这份安宁。

**关键引语：**

> ① "ACCORDING TO THE CLOCK on the bedside table, it was almost noon, the solid middle of the day, when all good fae should be asleep and dreaming of better worlds. According to the high, undulating wail coming from the corner of the room, it was time for everyone in earshot to be awake. Especially me."（ch01）

> ② "I loved Gillian desperately. I still do, and I always will. She’s my daughter. But our time together was cut short by forces beyond our control, and she grew up being taught to hate me for leaving her."（ch01）

> ③ "Unfortunately for all of us, but most unfortunately for Raysel, her term of offense was going to end in another three days, and the only way we’d been able to come up with to get out of it involved convincing the Queen we should be allowed to extend our claim due to Titania’s interference. It was an unlikely maneuver, and even if it somehow worked, it would only buy us four months."（ch01）

## 节点二：四点的厨房，与她在怕什么（ch02）

**叙事概括：** 四点起床的「清晨」从厨房开始：May 抱走 Miranda 去换尿布，把「自带保姆」吹嘘成家族优势。随后消息到达——Raysel 怕的不是被送走本身，而是「太麻烦」会让 October 意识到代价、从而把她交还回去；她怕的也不是父亲会做什么，而是他不会。真正的坏消息来自地理：Luna 能走 Rose Roads 直达她父亲的 skerry，女王裁决挡不住这条血亲之路。这一章让 October 决定当晚去 Shadowed Hills 摊牌，而 Tybalt 坚持同行。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“She’s afraid that if she’s too much trouble, you’ll realize this could cost you your oaths and decide to give her back.” Chelsea put her hands up defensively when I narrowed my eyes. “Messenger! Do not shoot! I know you would never. But Raysel … well. Raysel worries.”"（ch02）

> ② "Sylvester had already shown that he couldn’t stand up to Luna, not where Raysel was concerned. Raysel wasn’t afraid he’d do something. She was afraid he wouldn’t."（ch02）

## 节点三：书房里那句「我不能独身一人」（ch03）

**叙事概括：** 书房里没有谈判的余地：October 拒绝对「可以回家」背书，警告强行接人将失去女儿，并声明若被命令就请求解除效忠——「逼你做选择的不是我。也不是 Raysel。」而 Sylvester 的回击不是愤怒，是一句自白：他的女儿选了别人的家，如果 Luna 也离开，他就只剩下孤身一人；「我不能孤身一人。我一个人待着，过不好。」这场对话把「爱」与「控制」的界线第一次摆上桌面。话未谈完，门外已传来一男一女的对吼。

**关键引语：**

> ① "And my daughter—my only daughter—chooses your company and your household over my own. If Luna leaves me, I will be alone.” His voice dropped, and he looked down at his hands. “I can’t be alone. I don’t do well when I’m alone.”"（ch03）

> ② "“Yes, I do,” I said, voice going soft. “I owe you my fealty. But if you order me to bring her back to you when I’ve already told you it will hurt her, I will counter by asking you to release me. You haven’t been acting like my liege for a while now, and this will be the last straw. You can lose Luna for the hope of keeping your daughter, and me, or you can keep her and lose us both. I’m not the one forcing your hand here. Neither is Raysel.”"（ch03）

## 节点四：诅咒，与「你只要开口」（ch04）

**叙事概括：** Luna 上门兴师问罪，指控 October 没能救她们、把她们丢在黑暗里自己走开；被顶回后，她留下当众的诅咒——刀子和箭你都能甩开，那我们就看这道伤口你要怎么痊愈。Sylvester 公开站到 October 一边、拒绝强迫女儿回来，随后崩溃自责：他抛下过弟弟 Simon，没有资格再认这个兄弟。October 于是叫人去 Saltmist 请 Simon——兄弟重逢，Simon 用一句「你只要开口叫我」把多年的亏欠轻轻放下。这一轮冲突的收束点不在和解，而在这句话的对比里：一个怕独处的人，有一个从不让人独处的兄弟。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I hope you understand what you’ve done,” she said. “I hope you learn what it feels like when someone turns your mother-love against you. You shrug off knives and arrows like they’re nothing. Let’s see you recover from this wound.”"（ch04）

> ② "“He wouldn’t come,” said Sylvester, voice muffled by his hands. “He would never come for me. I abandoned him when I met Luna, I left him to fall into his liege’s clutches, and I didn’t even see that it was happening. I have no right to claim him as my brother.”"（ch04）

> ③ "“Of course I came,” replied Simon. “All you ever had to do was call. I’ll admit, having one of Dean’s friends suddenly appear in the central nursery and start begging me to come away with her was a little bit surprising, but it wasn’t the strangest thing that’s ever happened, and it worked.”"（ch04）

## 节点五：凭根与枝（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 回家之后的夜晚被喂奶、闲谈与访客填满；October 向 Raysel 立下守护誓言——凭根与枝、凭叶与藤，不让任何人违逆她的意愿把她带走，魔法在空气里噼啪作响。这是全书最安宁的一晚：所有人都安全、都完整、都在家。而叙述者在收尾处亲手拆掉这份安宁——「我愚蠢地允许自己去相信：这样的日子会一直下去」。灾难前的最后一个刻度被标在这里。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I took a deep breath. “By root and branch, by leaf and vine, on rowan and oak and ash and thorn I swear that I will keep you safe and here within my halls, that I will not allow anyone to remove you from my keeping against your will. My blood to the defense of the task I am set, my heart to the keeping of the promise to which I am bound.” My magic rose around me, crackling in the air and filling it with the scent of cut grass and copper."（ch05）

> ② "Everyone was safe, and whole, and home. And in that moment, I foolishly allowed myself to believe this state of things would last."（ch05）

## 节点六：听证会上的那声「不」（ch06）

**叙事概括：** 法庭设在 Muir Woods 红杉深处的树屋宫廷。Dianda 的开场白把账目摊开：四个月服役因迷咒白白损失，不服务满整整一年不算结清；Luna 中途闯入施压，要求把女儿带回家。而当事人自己站了出来——声音在那一个音节上劈了叉，却把「不」说了两遍：她要服满刑期，因为不把代价付完，她没法独自成为任何人。女王裁定再延长四个月；Luna 留下诅咒离席，Saltmist 一方回以威慑。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“The term of offense had barely begun when the Lady of the Flowers enchanted the land, and Rayseline Torquill was pulled from the home where she had been placed to serve and thrown into a skerry with no way to fulfill her obligations,” said Dianda. “Time passed normally during that enchantment. Four months of service were lost. We will not be satisfied that the girl has paid properly for her actions until she has served a full year.”"（ch06）

> ② "“No,” said Raysel. Her voice cracked on that single syllable, unsteady and unable to hold its tone. She paused, swallowing heavily, and moved a little closer to me as she repeated, “No. No, Mother, I’m not ready. I already said I wanted to finish my term of service. That I need to finish my term of service. Until I do, I’m not going to feel like I really tried to get better. I did so many things wrong. Some of them were … I don’t see how anybody forgives me for some of the things I did. And if I’m not done paying for what I did, I have to finish getting better before I can leave and be anyone on my own. Please, Mother. Please don’t try to make me.”"（ch06）

## 节点七：一滴血里的玫瑰与酒（ch07）

**叙事概括：** 散场之后 Chelsea 破空而至——Raysel 从会客厅凭空消失了。October 咬血施术，从桌沿一滴未干的血里尝出糖渍黑刺李酒的味道，当众给出结论：Luna 来过，是 Luna 带走了自己的女儿，而且人很可能已被带出 the Mists 的管辖范围。追捕从这里正式起步：证据是一滴血，方向是管辖权之外，而下一步只能去敲 Luidaeg 的门。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Arden and Nolan blinked, both of them looking stunned by this new arrival. Chelsea ignored them, clinging to my arms as she babbled, “Can’t find her I tried I went looking and she’s not there not anywhere Toby she’s gone she’s gone she’s gone…”"（ch07）

> ② "“What you’re tasting is wine,” I said. “And what it is, is that we didn’t double-check to make sure Luna would still bleed in a way we could recognize after she lost her Kitsune skin. Luna was here. Luna took Raysel. Chelsea, you need to go and notify Queen Windermere, because Rayseline Torquill has been abducted by her own mother, and I’m pretty sure that she’s been taken outside the authority of the Mists.”"（ch07）

## 节点八：海巫婆开价（ch08）

**叙事概括：** 要走到 Blind Michael 的旧地，只能请 Luidaeg 开门。她的价不是钱，是「每人一次的叫停权」：她说停就得停，哪怕女儿身陷危险也必须忍住不动——不过同一笔交易里紧接着谈定了例外：若停手会让她的教女 Miranda 遭到无法挽回的伤害，她保证不强迫他们停；她还顺手敲了 October 一句——「应该」是个好大的词，而你「应该」待在那个池塘里。价钱付清，一双蜡烛亮起，歌谣给出的保证是「只凭一支蜡烛的光，你就能到了那里、再回到这里」。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Such a big word, ‘supposed.’ Everything I can see about the last several years tells me that you were ‘supposed’ to be in that pond, but I bet it didn’t feel like that at the time. Maybe Raysel’s ‘supposed’ to be exactly where she is right now. Did you consider that?”"（ch08）

> ② "“I can get you there and back by the light of a candle, so I’ll only ask once, but for the both of you: for each of you, one time, I will be able to tell you not to do something, and you’ll obey me, no matter how dear the danger might be. If I say no, you will stop. Even if your daughter is in danger, you will refrain from taking action. Do you understand what I’m asking of you?”"（ch08）

## 节点九：荆棘尽头，两位母亲（ch09）

**叙事概括：** 他们走过 Maeve 的荆棘之路，路过玻璃棺里的 Eira，抵达 Blind Michael 旧日的辖地：Acacia 用幻象遮着一条被剜至见骨的左臂，Luna 也倒在袭击现场、由 October 喂血疗伤。Acacia 交出的是一段很冷的自陈——七个孩子一个个离开，而这个孩子身上「没剩下她/我的任何东西」。转醒的 Luna 第一句话是问女儿下落：「现在没空说那些。」她把所有寒暄、所有立场，都让位给了同一个问题。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Blood was splashed across the front of her dress and torso, painted up her chest and neck like some sort of attempt at abstract art. It was very red, very mammalian, and much of it appeared to have come from her left arm, which was a ruin, gouged down to the bone in places, the flesh hanging red and raw where it remained at all."（ch09）

> ② "“Seven children I had with Michael, seven babes at my breast and cradles by the fire,” she said. “And one by one they left me, turned away for the sunlit world where they could thrive. My Dryads were more loyal than their rose-colored siblings, but they left me long before Michael wooed and won me.”"（ch09）

> ③ "“No time for that,” Luna rasped. “October, where is my daughter?”"（ch09）

## 节点十：借来的痛，与「我会回来」（ch10）

**叙事概括：** 僵局里 October 用一场「讨债」谈判换来 Firstborn 的一口血：三段借来的记忆让她看清袭击者的来路，也借到不属于她的痛——那只是伤口的记忆，皮肤没有裂开、骨头没有断，「它只是感觉像断了一样」。她在记忆里同时看清了 Acacia 对 Raysel 的冷意，并给出自己的判词：血在 Faerie 被抬得太高，家建立在比血多得多的东西上。出发前她与 Tybalt 谈成那个残忍的约定——若败，你先走；他的回答是：我会走，但我会回来。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I screamed as I stumbled back from Acacia, borrowed pain lancing through my body. It was only the memory of injury: no gashes opened in my skin, no bones broke. It just felt like they did."（ch10）

> ② "Why did blood matter so much to everyone in Faerie? Raysel was still her grandchild, still born of her youngest daughter, whether she was Blodynbryd or not. Quentin was still my son, and we weren’t related even that tenuously. Family was built on so much more than blood."（ch10）

> ③ "“Yes, October. For you, for our child, I will run if the need arises. I will leave you to fight on alone, and I will go. But I will come back.”"（ch10）

## 节点十一：空堡，与挤压伤（ch11）

**叙事概括：** 荒废的 keep 里没有守卫、没有埋伏，只有一摊血迹——线索之所以笔直好跟，是因为绑走 Raysel 的人知道会有人追来。October 回头与 Luna 当面对质，随后给出全书最精确的一个比喻：Raysel 正在从一场心理上的「挤压伤」里往外恢复，而治疗的办法恰恰是别再把重量压回去。归途的篝火旁，当年同被囚禁的半羊人认出了她——「本可以当我们的 Lady，却选择做了我们的救主」。

**关键引语：**

> ① "A small, despairing sound escaped my lips. I couldn’t help it. The people who’d taken Raysel had clearly known that they would be followed, if not who would be following them; their trail had been so straight and easily followed because they’d known that they were laying it."（ch11）

> ② "There’s a thing the humans call a crush injury, where someone has been pressed under something so heavy for so long that their body adjusts and forgets how to exist under normal pressure. When you take the weight away, their tissues start to swell, and it can kill them. Raysel has been trying to recover from a psychological crush injury, and you’ve been trying to heal it by crushing her back down."（ch11）

> ③ "“He might as well have been, to us,” said the Satyr. “I know you. Sir October Daye, who could have been our Lady, but chose to be our savior instead. Even those of us who loved Him know what you did for us. Even those of us who weren’t able to go home are grateful.”"（ch11）

## 节点十二：一百五十八（ch12）

**叙事概括：** Tybalt 在篝火边交出这个家的账单：从他记事起，仅他的宫廷就有一百五十八只幼崽消失在 Blind Michael 手里；他记不住他们的名字，但记得他们的数目。May 的电话确认家里有人接手；马厩里的骑手 Athan 用两个谜语般的身份供出「消息」的来路——一个女人、一个曾在烛光下不再是兄弟的男人。嫌疑被钉向 Shadowed Hills 的仆役层，而她与 Tybalt 议定了分工：她独行，他回家。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I don’t remember their names, but I remember their numbers,” he said. “One hundred and fifty-eight kittens have vanished from my court since I grew old enough to become aware of the threat posed by Blind Michael. One hundred and fifty-eight Cait Sidhe from my court alone, tortured and transformed and finally wiped clean by that monster’s magic. I haven’t seen nearly enough Riders here to account for that number, which means most of them have long since stopped their dancing.”"（ch12）

> ② "“Sister mine, by this point, you owe me so many that you should be grateful I’m not charging you interest,” she said. “Get home soon and safe, okay? I am not explaining to your kid how Mommy and Daddy couldn’t stop messing around with the Firstborn and got themselves sucked into some sort of messed-up shadow realm for a thousand years.”"（ch12）

> ③ "“The news was carried by a woman gone invisible with place and position, who has been kind to us ever since her eldest daughter came to join our number, and by a man who ceased to be a brother once, by candlelight. They thought we deserved to know that the girl would be vulnerable one more time, before she was lost to us forever.” Athan shrugged. “As to where Blind Michael’s granddaughter belongs, you know that answer. She should have been here from the beginning. She should be here still.”"（ch12）

## 节点十三：储藏室的门（ch13）

**叙事概括：** 回到 Shadowed Hills 的仆役厨房，October 从老友 Melly 听说 Raysel 失踪时的那一瞥里读出心虚——她在盘算出厨房的退路。储藏室里 Melly 交出全部真相：九十年前被掳走的女儿 Nara，醒来只见空气里悬着蜡与蜡烛烟的味道；以及她把 Riders 引向 Raysel 的那笔「交易」——他们需要一个新领袖，而他们保证过不再带走孩子。她一刀刺穿 October，October 收走刀、把她锁进储藏室，随后用一段不留退路的实话，从 Sylvester 那里换回「在 Raysel 回来之前不动手」的承诺。

**关键引语：**

> ① "She paused, but not long enough: I’d known her for most of my life, and I saw the way she glanced to the side, assessing her routes out of the kitchen. My heart sank. There was only one reason I could think of for her to look so suspicious when I’d just told her that our mutual liege’s daughter was missing, and that was if she wasn’t surprised."（ch13）

> ② "“I put her to bed on a Monday morning, and all was right with my world. Then, when I woke, it was to find her gone and the smell of wax and candle smoke hanging in the air. I never felt him coming."（ch14）

> ③ "“Because what they needed was a new leader for their Hunt. And who better to lead them than the granddaughter of the man who made them? They’ve promised to take no more children."（ch14）

## 节点十四：浮雕里的第二个人（ch15）

**叙事概括：** 回到 Muir Woods，October 在前厅的浮雕里认出新的嫌疑人——那个站在 Riders 面前、胸前护着 Arden 纹章的男人。追进满是盆栽的套间，她用一把角刀把藏进树里的 Eion 逼出来：从这里切下去，树就会死，而他会带着「本来有机会救它」的念头活下去。他于是把复仇的逻辑说完整——弟弟本该永生，如今该轮到他们尝尝没人保护是什么滋味；他的答案也是下一站：他们叫它「the Children's Hall」。

**关键引语：**

> ② "“If I cut this here, it’s going to die,” I said. “It won’t kill you immediately, but it’ll hurt, and you’ll have to live with the knowledge that you could have saved this tree. If you’re all right with that, well. I won’t judge. But I’ll be sure to tell your wife that in addition to everything else, you were willing to sacrifice a tree to save yourself. Not a great look, Eion."（ch16）

> ③ "“It wasn’t fair,” said Eion. “He was supposed to live forever. He deserved the chance to live forever, not to die because no one was willing to protect us. Well, now it’s time for them to learn what it feels like not to be protected.”"（ch16）

> ④ "“The Rider I spoke with said that they would get her away from her mother when they came to the skerry, and that from there, they’d sound the horn and carry her back into their territory. There’s a place they call ‘the Children’s Hall,’ where new captives are kept until they change enough to Ride. They’re going to put her there, and keep her until she’s ready.”"（ch16）

## 节点十五：生根，与拆网（ch17）

**叙事概括：** 雾散后回到荆棘小径，猎号由远及近三度响起；Riders 追上，把 October 定在原地生根——「现在你留下来了，永远留下……你不想休息吗？」。她的意识沉入树的梦，又从树形里挣脱；在 Children's Hall 里，Raysel 正在被咒语「开花」——咒语从血肉里织出来，等织完就几乎分不清哪里是她、哪里是咒语。October 一寸寸拆掉那张网，也听 Raysel 说出了幕后推手：是那些骑手，然后她低头看着膝盖：我妈妈。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I would be less afraid. I would be less afraid. And maybe if I kept telling myself that, it would be true."（ch17）

> ② "“Now you get to stay,” said another. “Stay forever, as you should have all along. Stay silent and strong and not putting your little fingers where they aren’t desired, where they don’t belong. Now you get to rest. Don’t you want to rest?”"（ch17）

> ③ "“The Riders,” she said. Her head drooped, and she looked down at her knees as she said, “My mother.”"（ch18）

> ④ "Here, though, the spell was worked through Raysel’s flesh, like some sort of elaborate fishhook piercing. By the time it was finished, it would be almost impossible to tell where she stopped and the spell began."（ch18）

## 节点十六：夜岛对峙（ch19）

**叙事概括：** 在 Blind Michael 的夜岛上，Raysel 从恐惧里站出来当面质问母亲——「你把我从我的家里带走」，那个地方不是 Shadowed Hills，却是她终于感到安全的地方。Luna 的逻辑是「让一家人不分开，永远不算错」；October 割掌放血，拆掉 Rider Athan 身上的法术披肩，被长矛从背后贯穿仍不罢手。最后 Acacia 现身接管 Luna，把空气像画布那样拉开，送三人回家——礁岛与它永恒的黑夜被留在身后。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“You took me from my home,” she snapped. “It wasn’t Shadowed Hills, but it didn’t need to be, because it was a place where I was finally home. Where I was finally safe."（ch19）

> ② "“Keeping a family together is never wrong,” she countered. “You may not like my methods, but you would do the same if you were backed into a corner. You would fight the world to save your child from being lost to it.”"（ch19）

## 节点十七：两周后（ch20）

**叙事概括：** 两周后，家中的秩序缓慢回正：Raysel 开始出门——只要她肯出门，就说明她开始重新觉得安全了，「这才是真正要紧的」。Eion 被流放、Melly 降职留用、Luna 仍留在她父亲的岛上。October 带着 Tybalt 与 Quentin 面见女王 Arden，宣告要带蜡烛重返 Blind Michael 的国土——岛上还有曾经是孩子的树，也许更容易救一些。三人一起走进正在成形的雾里，去把失落与失踪的孩子们带回家。

**关键引语：**

> ① "That had really been the first sign that things might eventually get back to normal, or what passed for normal around our place. If Raysel was willing to go out at all, she was starting to feel safe again. That was really what mattered."（ch20）

> ② "I don’t know how many more of them could still be saved, at least enough to return them to the state they were in before they Rode. I may not be able to get most of them any closer to who they used to be. But there are also trees who used to be children. They might be easier to rescue."（ch20）

> ③ "“We’ll be back soon,” I said, and tapped my candle with my thumb. The wick promptly lit itself, as did Tybalt and Quentin’s, and together, the three of us walked into the forming fog, back into the land of nightmares, to bring the lost and missing children home."（ch20）

## 节点十八：番外的清晨（ch21）

**叙事概括：** 番外把镜头交给被留下的人。Quentin 从失眠的早晨出走：Luidaeg 按着他哭完，告诉他「你还是你，只是混进了一点乱糟糟的额外碎片」；Raj 拆掉他的逻辑，Dean 把 Titania 赶出他们的卧室。一天之内把真相说出口的他，终于在梦中的假卧室里对另一个自己宣胜——「我不把这份权力给你……现在我要回家了」。这是整个故事最安静的一次收束：不是复仇，不是自证，是回家。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Hey. None of that,” she scolded lightly, taking a step away without removing her hand from my back. “You needed to cry or you wouldn’t have been crying. Don’t you go feeling rotten about something you couldn’t possibly have helped.”"（ch21）

> ② "“Only someone who’d learned most of their morality from Toby would still think Faerie gave a single damn about being fair. You’re still you, Quentin. You’re just you with a few messy extra bits mixed in and making everything harder for you to understand right now. Breathe. That jumped-up old florist doesn’t get to undo everything you’ve worked for with a little sorcery.”"（ch21）

> ③ "“Quentin.” Dean gripped my upper arms, firmly, pulling me slightly toward him. “I don’t care who you were while she was in control, you understand me? He doesn’t matter. And after this, right now, she doesn’t get to matter in our bed. Our time together is limited because of who you are. I refuse to let her steal a single minute of it away from us.”"（ch21）

> ④ "I looked at my reflection for the first time with pity, and bowed my head slightly as I said, “You have no power over me anymore. I refuse to give it to you. You thought you were better than anyone, but I’m the you who keeps company with people you would revile, and I beat you. Just me. No one else. And now I’m going home.”"（ch21）
