# 《A Divided Duty》概述

## 全书梗概

**一、正午的哭声，与三个倒计时。** 全章是喂奶、补觉、与 Tybalt 交接班照看女儿的寻常半日，也是这个家的来路与亏欠的交代——叙述者自报家门：
- 她不是人类，丈夫不是，女儿也不是。这一天的开场由两只表决定，一只摆在床头柜上，一只在角落里哭："ACCORDING TO THE CLOCK on the bedside table, it was almost noon, the solid middle of the day, when all good fae should be asleep and dreaming of better worlds. According to the high, undulating wail coming from the corner of the room, it was time for everyone in earshot to be awake. Especially me."（ch01）
- 而在安宁底下，三个倒计时已经上好发条——Raysel 的服罪期再过三天结束，女王的庭还要两天才开，而他们想出的唯一对策就算走通，也只能再买来四个月："Unfortunately for all of us, but most unfortunately for Raysel, her term of offense was going to end in another three days, and the only way we’d been able to come up with to get out of it involved convincing the Queen we should be allowed to extend our claim due to Titania’s interference. It was an unlikely maneuver, and even if it somehow worked, it would only buy us four months."（ch01）

**二、条款的漏洞，比条款本身更长。** 厨房里的对话点破了一件事：
- Dianda Lorden 讨来的条款写的是「她与 Daye 爵士双方都同意才算还清」，而两个人并没有都同意——根本不必申请延期："“You shouldn’t have to extend the term. ‘She shall return to her parents’ halls only if she and Sir Daye both agree her debt is fulfilled,’ that’s what Duchess Lorden asked for, isn’t it? That’s what Queen Windermere granted. None of this matters, because you don’t both agree.”"（ch02）
- 可真正的危险来自另一条路：Luna 是 Blodynbryd，她能走 Rose Roads 直达她父亲的 skerry，在那里 Acacia 的话就是法律："Luna was Blodynbryd. Luna had access to the Rose Roads, which would take her straight to her father’s skerry, where Acacia’s word was law. And if Luna showed up there with Raysel in tow, I wasn’t sure Acacia would be willing to listen to reason long enough to let us take her granddaughter away."（ch02）
女王裁决挡不住母亲——October 决定当晚去 Shadowed Hills 与 Sylvester 摊牌。

**三、一场没有主角的庆典。** 进门才知道 Shadowed Hills 正在筹办庆典——「公爵大人正在筹办一场庆祝他独生女归来的庆典，她的服罪期就要结束了」：
- "“His Grace is arranging a celebration of his only daughter’s return, now that her term of offense is coming to an end,” she said. “If you’re here as well, we might rightly be celebrating the both of you. It’s been too long since you walked with us on any reliable sort of basis. We miss you awfully.”"（ch03）
- 书房里 October 拒绝对「可以回家」背书，并当面把最重的话说完：若被命令把 Raysel 送回，她将请求解除效忠；逼 Sylvester 做选择的不是她，也不是 Raysel——"“Yes, I do,” I said, voice going soft. “I owe you my fealty. But if you order me to bring her back to you when I’ve already told you it will hurt her, I will counter by asking you to release me. You haven’t been acting like my liege for a while now, and this will be the last straw. You can lose Luna for the hope of keeping your daughter, and me, or you can keep her and lose us both. I’m not the one forcing your hand here. Neither is Raysel.”"（ch03）
- 而 Sylvester 的回答是一句自白：他的女儿选了别人的家，如果 Luna 也离开，他就只剩下孤身一人——"And my daughter—my only daughter—chooses your company and your household over my own. If Luna leaves me, I will be alone.” His voice dropped, and he looked down at his hands. “I can’t be alone. I don’t do well when I’m alone.”"（ch03）

**四、诅咒，与一个兄弟的回答。** Luna 上门兴师问罪，留下一句当众的诅咒——刀箭你都甩得开，那我们就看这道伤口你怎么痊愈：
- "“I hope you understand what you’ve done,” she said. “I hope you learn what it feels like when someone turns your mother-love against you. You shrug off knives and arrows like they’re nothing. Let’s see you recover from this wound.”"（ch04）
- Sylvester 公开站到 October 一边、拒绝强迫女儿回来，又在事后崩溃自责：他抛下过弟弟 Simon，没有资格再认这个兄弟。于是 October 派人去 Saltmist 把 Simon 请来——兄弟重逢，Simon 的回答是全书最轻也最重的一句「你只要开口叫我」："“Of course I came,” replied Simon. “All you ever had to do was call. I’ll admit, having one of Dean’s friends suddenly appear in the central nursery and start begging me to come away with her was a little bit surprising, but it wasn’t the strangest thing that’s ever happened, and it worked.”"（ch04）

**五、一句誓言，和一句自我告警。** 回家之后的夜晚被喂奶、闲谈与访客填满；October 向 Raysel 立下守护誓言——凭根与枝、凭叶与藤，不让任何人违逆她的意愿把她带走，魔法在空气里噼啪作响：
- "I took a deep breath. “By root and branch, by leaf and vine, on rowan and oak and ash and thorn I swear that I will keep you safe and here within my halls, that I will not allow anyone to remove you from my keeping against your will. My blood to the defense of the task I am set, my heart to the keeping of the promise to which I am bound.” My magic rose around me, crackling in the air and filling it with the scent of cut grass and copper."（ch05）
- 全章以一句不祥的自我告警收束——就在那一刻，「我愚蠢地允许自己去相信：这样的日子会一直下去」："Everyone was safe, and whole, and home. And in that moment, I foolishly allowed myself to believe this state of things would last."（ch05）

**六、听证会上的那声「不」。** 法庭设在 Muir Woods 的树屋宫廷里；Dianda 的开场白把账目摊开——四个月服役因迷咒白白损失，不服务满整整一年不算结清：
- "“The term of offense had barely begun when the Lady of the Flowers enchanted the land, and Rayseline Torquill was pulled from the home where she had been placed to serve and thrown into a skerry with no way to fulfill her obligations,” said Dianda. “Time passed normally during that enchantment. Four months of service were lost. We will not be satisfied that the girl has paid properly for her actions until she has served a full year.”"（ch06）
- 而当事人自己站了出来，声音在那一个音节上劈了叉——「不，我还没准备好」，她要服满刑期，因为不把代价付完，她没法独自成为任何人："“No,” said Raysel. Her voice cracked on that single syllable, unsteady and unable to hold its tone. She paused, swallowing heavily, and moved a little closer to me as she repeated, “No. No, Mother, I’m not ready. I already said I wanted to finish my term of service. That I need to finish my term of service. Until I do, I’m not going to feel like I really tried to get better. I did so many things wrong. Some of them were … I don’t see how anybody forgives me for some of the things I did. And if I’m not done paying for what I did, I have to finish getting better before I can leave and be anyone on my own. Please, Mother. Please don’t try to make me.”"（ch06）
- Saltmist 一方回以威慑："“I said, you should be afraid. We don’t want war. We’ve done everything in our power to avoid it. I’m from the land, originally, as is our husband; our son and my stepdaughter both live in Queen Windermere’s demesne. But our bonds don’t make us harmless. If anything, they make us more dangerous, since we’d have to be truly antagonized before we were willing to attack. And you’re treading very close to that line, Duchess Torquill. You’re standing on the shore, looking at the sea, and saying, ‘I don’t see the depths, so they must not exist.’ They exist. We don’t want war because we don’t want to wash you all away.”"（ch06）
女王裁定再延长四个月，Luna 留下诅咒离席。

**七、一滴血里的玫瑰与酒。** 散场之后 Chelsea 破空而至——Raysel 从会客厅凭空消失了。October 咬血施术，从桌沿一滴未干的血里尝出糖渍黑刺李酒的味道，当众说出结论：
- Luna 来过，是 Luna 带走了自己的女儿，而且人很可能已被带出 the Mists 的管辖范围——"“What you’re tasting is wine,” I said. “And what it is, is that we didn’t double-check to make sure Luna would still bleed in a way we could recognize after she lost her Kitsune skin. Luna was here. Luna took Raysel. Chelsea, you need to go and notify Queen Windermere, because Rayseline Torquill has been abducted by her own mother, and I’m pretty sure that she’s been taken outside the authority of the Mists.”"（ch07）
- 她接下来的决定只有一句："“I mean, she’s probably in Blind Michael’s lands by now,” I said. “And we’re going to have to go and get her back.”"（ch07）

**八、海巫婆的开价。** 追查把方向锁死在 Blind Michael 的旧地；要走到那里，只能去敲 Luidaeg 的门。开的价不是钱——是「每人一次的叫停权」：
- 她说停就得停，哪怕女儿身陷危险也必须忍住不动；不过同一笔交易里紧接着谈定了例外——若停手会让她的教女 Miranda 遭到无法挽回的伤害，她保证不强迫他们停："“I can get you there and back by the light of a candle, so I’ll only ask once, but for the both of you: for each of you, one time, I will be able to tell you not to do something, and you’ll obey me, no matter how dear the danger might be. If I say no, you will stop. Even if your daughter is in danger, you will refrain from taking action. Do you understand what I’m asking of you?”"（ch08）
- 价钱付清，一双蜡烛亮起，歌谣给出的保证是「只凭一支蜡烛的光，你就能到了那里、再回到这里」："“Long you’ll go and far you’ll walk, and never see the sun, but when you hear the night bird cry, you’ll know your journey’s done. Hear me weary wanderer, and know I tell you right: you’ll make it there and back again by just one candle’s light.”"（ch08）

**九、荆棘尽头，两位母亲。** 他们走过 Maeve 的荆棘之路，路过玻璃棺里的 Eira，抵达 Blind Michael 旧日的辖地：
- Acacia 用幻象遮着一条被剜至见骨的左臂，Luna 也倒在袭击现场、由 October 喂血疗伤。Acacia 交出的是一段很冷的自陈——七个孩子一个个离开，而这个孩子身上「没剩下她/我的任何东西」："“Seven children I had with Michael, seven babes at my breast and cradles by the fire,” she said. “And one by one they left me, turned away for the sunlit world where they could thrive. My Dryads were more loyal than their rose-colored siblings, but they left me long before Michael wooed and won me.”"（ch09）
- 转醒的 Luna 第一句话是问女儿下落："“No time for that,” Luna rasped. “October, where is my daughter?”"（ch09）

**十、借来的痛，与「我会回来」。** October 用一场「讨债」谈判换来 Firstborn 的一口血——三段借来的记忆让她看清袭击者的来路：
- "Acacia’s eyebrows lowered, and then, to my deep surprise, she laughed. “Oh, you have been bargaining with my sister for all these years! She’d teach a mountain how to split a hair in the pursuit of what it desires, and no mistaking that. Yes, it’s true, you could easily argue that you’ve the right to call upon the debts I owe you, and anyone who has a heart would listen. Here.”"（ch10）
- 血里也带来不属于她的痛：那只是伤口的记忆，皮肤没有裂开、骨头没有断，「它只是感觉像断了一样」："I screamed as I stumbled back from Acacia, borrowed pain lancing through my body. It was only the memory of injury: no gashes opened in my skin, no bones broke. It just felt like they did."（ch10）
- 出发前她与 Tybalt 谈成那个残忍的约定——若败，你先走；他的回答是：我会走，但我会回来——"“Yes, October. For you, for our child, I will run if the need arises. I will leave you to fight on alone, and I will go. But I will come back.”"（ch10）

**十一、一座空堡，一个诊断。** 荒废的 keep 里没有守卫、没有埋伏，只有一摊血迹——线索之所以笔直好跟，是因为绑走 Raysel 的人知道会有人追来：
- "A small, despairing sound escaped my lips. I couldn’t help it. The people who’d taken Raysel had clearly known that they would be followed, if not who would be following them; their trail had been so straight and easily followed because they’d known that they were laying it."（ch11）
- 回头与 Luna 当面对质之后，这一章给出了全书最精确的一个比喻：Raysel 正在从一场心理上的「挤压伤」里往外恢复，而把她重新压回去，不是治疗："There’s a thing the humans call a crush injury, where someone has been pressed under something so heavy for so long that their body adjusts and forgets how to exist under normal pressure. When you take the weight away, their tissues start to swell, and it can kill them. Raysel has been trying to recover from a psychological crush injury, and you’ve been trying to heal it by crushing her back down."（ch11）

**十二、一百五十八。** Tybalt 交出的是数字，不是名字——从他记事起，仅他的宫廷就有一百五十八只幼崽消失在 Blind Michael 手里，而他在这里见到的骑手远不够对上这个数目：
- "“I don’t remember their names, but I remember their numbers,” he said. “One hundred and fifty-eight kittens have vanished from my court since I grew old enough to become aware of the threat posed by Blind Michael. One hundred and fifty-eight Cait Sidhe from my court alone, tortured and transformed and finally wiped clean by that monster’s magic. I haven’t seen nearly enough Riders here to account for that number, which means most of them have long since stopped their dancing.”"（ch12）
- 马厩里的骑手 Athan 供出两条身份的谜语——一个女人、一个「曾在烛光下不再是兄弟的男人」，并说他们带来消息：那女孩还会再脆弱一次："“The news was carried by a woman gone invisible with place and position, who has been kind to us ever since her eldest daughter came to join our number, and by a man who ceased to be a brother once, by candlelight. They thought we deserved to know that the girl would be vulnerable one more time, before she was lost to us forever.” Athan shrugged. “As to where Blind Michael’s granddaughter belongs, you know that answer. She should have been here from the beginning. She should be here still.”"（ch12）
这两句话把嫌疑钉向 Shadowed Hills 的仆役层。

**十三、储藏室的门。** 在 Shadowed Hills 的仆役厨房，October 从老友 Melly 听说 Raysel 失踪时的那一瞥里读出心虚——她在盘算出厨房的退路：
- "She paused, but not long enough: I’d known her for most of my life, and I saw the way she glanced to the side, assessing her routes out of the kitchen. My heart sank. There was only one reason I could think of for her to look so suspicious when I’d just told her that our mutual liege’s daughter was missing, and that was if she wasn’t surprised."（ch13）
- 储藏室里 Melly 交出全部真相：九十年前被掳走的女儿 Nara，醒来只见空气里悬着蜡与蜡烛烟的味道："“I put her to bed on a Monday morning, and all was right with my world. Then, when I woke, it was to find her gone and the smell of wax and candle smoke hanging in the air. I never felt him coming."（ch14）
- 以及她把 Riders 引向 Raysel 的那笔「交易」——因为他们需要一个新领袖，而他们保证过不再带走孩子："“Because what they needed was a new leader for their Hunt. And who better to lead them than the granddaughter of the man who made them? They’ve promised to take no more children."（ch14）
她用一刀刺穿 October，October 反而收走刀、把她锁进储藏室——随后用一段不留退路的实话，从 Sylvester 那里换回「在 Raysel 回来之前不动手」的承诺。

**十四、浮雕里的第二个人。** 回到 Muir Woods，October 在前厅的浮雕里认出新的嫌疑人——那个站在 Riders 面前、胸前护着 Arden 纹章的男人：
- "The fourth panel was the one that gave me pause. A man stood in front of three of Blind Michael’s Riders, carved so precisely that I could easily make out Arden’s arms at his breast. He looked grimly determined, not afraid, and I knew him. I could pretend I didn’t, but I did, and after confronting Melly, I wasn’t going to let her accomplice go free."（ch15）
- 追进满是盆栽的套间，她用一把角刀把藏进树里的 Eion 逼出来，也让他把「不公平」的逻辑说完整——他弟弟本该永生，如今该轮到他们尝尝没人保护是什么滋味："“It wasn’t fair,” said Eion. “He was supposed to live forever. He deserved the chance to live forever, not to die because no one was willing to protect us. Well, now it’s time for them to learn what it feels like not to be protected.”"（ch16）
- 以及他供出的下一站——「the Children's Hall」，新抓去的人会被留在那里，直到变得足以参与 Ride："“The Rider I spoke with said that they would get her away from her mother when they came to the skerry, and that from there, they’d sound the horn and carry her back into their territory. There’s a place they call ‘the Children’s Hall,’ where new captives are kept until they change enough to Ride. They’re going to put her there, and keep her until she’s ready.”"（ch16）

**十五、生根，与拆网。** 雾散后回到荆棘小径，猎号三度响起，Riders 追上并把 October 定在原地生根——「现在你留下来了，永远留下……你不想休息吗？」：
- "“Now you get to stay,” said another. “Stay forever, as you should have all along. Stay silent and strong and not putting your little fingers where they aren’t desired, where they don’t belong. Now you get to rest. Don’t you want to rest?”"（ch17）
- 她的意识沉入树的梦："But there wasn’t any more time. The numbness surged again, and the last of the air was crushed out of me, and my thoughts tumbled down into the dark, into the soft, silent place where the dreams of trees are dreamt."（ch17）
- 在 Children's Hall 里，Raysel 正在被咒语「开花」——咒语从血肉里织出来，等织完就几乎分不清哪里是她、哪里是咒语："Here, though, the spell was worked through Raysel’s flesh, like some sort of elaborate fishhook piercing. By the time it was finished, it would be almost impossible to tell where she stopped and the spell began."（ch18）
- October 一寸寸拆掉那张网，也听 Raysel 说出了幕后推手——是那些骑手，然后她低头看着膝盖：我妈妈："“The Riders,” she said. Her head drooped, and she looked down at her knees as she said, “My mother.”"（ch18）

**十六、夜岛对峙。** 在 Blind Michael 的夜岛上，Raysel 从恐惧里站出来当面质问母亲——「你把我从我的家里带走」，那个地方不是 Shadowed Hills，却是她终于感到安全的地方：
- "“You took me from my home,” she snapped. “It wasn’t Shadowed Hills, but it didn’t need to be, because it was a place where I was finally home. Where I was finally safe."（ch19）
- Luna 的逻辑是「让一家人不分开，永远不算错」："“Keeping a family together is never wrong,” she countered. “You may not like my methods, but you would do the same if you were backed into a corner. You would fight the world to save your child from being lost to it.”"（ch19）
- October 割掌放血，拆掉 Rider Athan 身上的法术披肩，被长矛从背后贯穿仍不罢手；最后 Acacia 现身接管 Luna，把空气像画布那样拉开，送三人回家："She pulled the air between us open like a sheet of painted canvas, revealing the Luidaeg’s living room. I reached over and took Raysel’s arm, and together, the three of us stepped through, leaving Blind Michael’s skerry and its eternal night behind."（ch19）

**十七、两周，与一次宣告。** 家中的秩序缓慢回正：
- Raysel 开始出门——只要她肯出门，就说明她开始重新觉得安全了，「这才是真正要紧的」："That had really been the first sign that things might eventually get back to normal, or what passed for normal around our place. If Raysel was willing to go out at all, she was starting to feel safe again. That was really what mattered."（ch20）
- Eion 被流放、Melly 降职留用、Luna 仍留在她父亲的岛上。October 带着 Tybalt 与 Quentin 面见女王 Arden，宣告要带蜡烛重返 Blind Michael 的国土——岛上还有曾经是孩子的树，也许更容易救一些："I don’t know how many more of them could still be saved, at least enough to return them to the state they were in before they Rode. I may not be able to get most of them any closer to who they used to be. But there are also trees who used to be children. They might be easier to rescue."（ch20）
- 出发时三人一起走进正在成形的雾里，去把失落与失踪的孩子们带回家："“We’ll be back soon,” I said, and tapped my candle with my thumb. The wick promptly lit itself, as did Tybalt and Quentin’s, and together, the three of us walked into the forming fog, back into the land of nightmares, to bring the lost and missing children home."（ch20）

**十八、番外：
- 被留下的人。** 被骑士「留下」的 Quentin 从失眠的早晨出走：海巫婆按着他哭完，告诉他「你还是你，只是混进了一点乱糟糟的额外碎片」："“Only someone who’d learned most of their morality from Toby would still think Faerie gave a single damn about being fair. You’re still you, Quentin. You’re just you with a few messy extra bits mixed in and making everything harder for you to understand right now. Breathe. That jumped-up old florist doesn’t get to undo everything you’ve worked for with a little sorcery.”"（ch21）
- Raj 拆掉他的逻辑，Dean 把 Titania 赶出他们的卧室；一天之内把真相说出口的他，终于在梦中的假卧室里对另一个自己宣胜——「你再也不能奈何我了。我不把这份权力给你」："I looked at my reflection for the first time with pity, and bowed my head slightly as I said, “You have no power over me anymore. I refuse to give it to you. You thought you were better than anyone, but I’m the you who keeps company with people you would revile, and I beat you. Just me. No one else. And now I’m going home.”"（ch21）
然后他闭上眼，再睁开时，人已经在家。

## 三个主题

**一、判决只能管到它的边界，而血缘越过一切边界。** 这本书的所有冲突都发生在「判决书覆盖不到的地方」：
- Dianda 的索赔让 Raysel 的刑期成立，却挡不住 Luna 走 Rose Roads 直达她父亲的 skerry——"Luna was Blodynbryd. Luna had access to the Rose Roads, which would take her straight to her father’s skerry, where Acacia’s word was law. And if Luna showed up there with Raysel in tow, I wasn’t sure Acacia would be willing to listen to reason long enough to let us take her granddaughter away."（ch02）
- Etienne 把 Luna 的账单算得很清楚：她不能原谅 Raysel 离开、不能原谅 October 帮她离开、不能原谅 Sylvester 放她走，甚至不能原谅女王依章办事——在 Luna 眼里，你们所有人都串通起来对付她："“Luna can’t forgive her for leaving, you for helping her leave, or Sylvester for allowing her to go. Luna can’t even forgive Queen Windermere for allowing the claim of offense, when she had no fair way of refusing it. In Luna’s eyes, you’ve all conspired against her.” Etienne looked at me, clearly miserable. “Luna is furious and in pain, and in her pain, she lashes out and hurts her husband in ways which then reflect out onto all of us. I don’t think she’ll know peace until her girl comes home again.”"（ch04）
法律能把刑期延长四个月，却没有任何条款能让一个母亲接受判决。

**二、「为你好」的两种执行方式，而它们都会伤人。** 一次是「把重量重新压回去」——Raysel 正在从一场心理上的挤压伤里恢复，而治疗的办法恰恰是别再把重量压回去：
- "There’s a thing the humans call a crush injury, where someone has been pressed under something so heavy for so long that their body adjusts and forgets how to exist under normal pressure. When you take the weight away, their tissues start to swell, and it can kill them. Raysel has been trying to recover from a psychological crush injury, and you’ve been trying to heal it by crushing her back down."（ch11）
- 另一次是 Luna 的自辩——「让一家人不分开，永远不算错」："“Keeping a family together is never wrong,” she countered. “You may not like my methods, but you would do the same if you were backed into a corner. You would fight the world to save your child from being lost to it.”"（ch19）
两种执行都出自爱，也都在对方身上留下伤口。

**三、家不是血。** October 在借来的记忆里看清 Acacia 对 Raysel 的冷意之后，说出了全书最干净的一句：
- Raysel 是不是 Blodynbryd 都不影响她是她的孙辈，血在 Faerie 被抬得太高了——「家，建立在比血多得多的东西上」："Why did blood matter so much to everyone in Faerie? Raysel was still her grandchild, still born of her youngest daughter, whether she was Blodynbryd or not. Quentin was still my son, and we weren’t related even that tenuously. Family was built on so much more than blood."（ch10）
- 而同一本书用整章来兑现这句话：真正认识 Raysel 的、在意她安全与清醒的，不是 Shadowed Hills 的人，而是这个非血亲的家："I had almost said “with her family,” because more and more, it felt like that was what we were, more than the people at Shadowed Hills were: we were her family. We were the people who actually knew her, not just the idea of her, the ones who cared about keeping her safe and sane rather than trying to force her into a box she was never going to fit."（ch13）

## 人物弧光

**October Daye（第一人称叙述者）** 她的弧线不在「变强」，而在「停止追讨认可」：
- 她承认自己一辈子都在追着 Amandine 的认可跑，同时向自己保证 Miranda 永远不必体会这种感觉："We grow up, but we never outgrow the people we were as children. Not really. Amandine wasn’t even my mother anymore, and I was going to be chasing her approval for the rest of my life. Not for the first time, I promised myself that Miranda was never going to need to feel like this."（ch08）
- 全书结束时她没有变成征服者——她带着蜡烛回到噩梦之地，去把还能救回来的孩子带回家："“We’ll be back soon,” I said, and tapped my candle with my thumb. The wick promptly lit itself, as did Tybalt and Quentin’s, and together, the three of us walked into the forming fog, back into the land of nightmares, to bring the lost and missing children home."（ch20）

**Tybalt（丈夫，前猫之王）** 退位换来的丈夫身份被反复证实：
- 他是会在危难前谈判、把「我会回来」说成承诺的人——"“Yes, October. For you, for our child, I will run if the need arises. I will leave you to fight on alone, and I will go. But I will come back.”"（ch10）
- 也是把分工安排得最实际的那一个：一起回家，你继续上路，我留下等 May 接手照看孩子——"“Little fish, I adore you,” he said. “And that may be the only reason I can survive you. We’ll return to the house together, and I’ll remain while you journey onward. Just call before leaving Shadowed Hills. I want to be able to rejoin you when May is prepared to resume sitting for our child.”"（ch12）

**Sylvester（Shadowed Hills 领主）** 他在这本书里的位置是一道逐渐清晰的裂缝：
- 一边是「我不能孤身一人」的恐惧——如果 Luna 离开，他就只剩自己："And my daughter—my only daughter—chooses your company and your household over my own. If Luna leaves me, I will be alone.” His voice dropped, and he looked down at his hands. “I can’t be alone. I don’t do well when I’m alone.”"（ch03）
- 另一边是他在冲突当口守住骑士的那一次——她要留在这里，这不是 Luna 能否决的事："“Luna,” said Sylvester again, more quietly this time, almost resigned. “Sir Daye is a knight of my household, and she has never done anything but honor her oaths, not even when it would have been easier for her to set them aside. Her service has been impeccable. It doesn’t matter whether you want her here. I want her here. She is my knight. You have no right to deny her place.”"（ch04）

**Luna（Blodynbryd）** 她的敌意有完整的来路：
- 她不能原谅 October 没能救她们，也不能原谅她把她们丢在黑暗里、自己走开——「你失败了。你把我们丢在黑暗里囚着」："“I’ve never been able to forgive you for not being able to save us, when you pranced around pretending to be a hero like you understood the meaning of the word! My husband is your liege. You had no dearer duty than our salvation, and what did you do? You failed. You left us imprisoned in the dark and went off to your own imprisonment like we were nothing to you.”"（ch04）
- 到夜岛上她仍在自我辩护，而对 October 的评价已经缩成一句「你不归我管」："“I wasn’t talking to you,” she said dismissively. “You’re none of my concern, little changeling who didn’t have the sense to let your mother save you from your fate. I’m here for my own child, the one who can still be saved.”"（ch19）

**Raysel** 她的弧线是从「不敢要」到「敢拒绝」：
- 听证会上她抽着气说出「不，我还没准备好」："“No,” said Raysel. Her voice cracked on that single syllable, unsteady and unable to hold its tone. She paused, swallowing heavily, and moved a little closer to me as she repeated, “No. No, Mother, I’m not ready. I already said I wanted to finish my term of service. That I need to finish my term of service. Until I do, I’m not going to feel like I really tried to get better. I did so many things wrong. Some of them were … I don’t see how anybody forgives me for some of the things I did. And if I’m not done paying for what I did, I have to finish getting better before I can leave and be anyone on my own. Please, Mother. Please don’t try to make me.”"（ch06）
- 回家后的第一周她把自己锁进房间，只有噩梦太严重时才爬进 October 与 Tybalt 的被窝——而「肯出门」成了这个家恢复正常的第一信号："That had really been the first sign that things might eventually get back to normal, or what passed for normal around our place. If Raysel was willing to go out at all, she was starting to feel safe again. That was really what mattered."（ch20）

**Melly 与 Eion** 两个被 Hunt 夺走至亲的人，长出了两种形状：
- Melly 把女儿的名字封存起来——「我们不说她」："“We do not speak of her,” she said, voice sharp and clipped. “Kerry is my only child yet living, and she deserves that distinction, not to be standing in the shadow of a girl she’ll never be nor know.”"（ch14）
- Eion 则把丧亲之痛延伸成一份复仇清单——「现在该轮到他们尝尝没人保护是什么滋味」："“It wasn’t fair,” said Eion. “He was supposed to live forever. He deserved the chance to live forever, not to die because no one was willing to protect us. Well, now it’s time for them to learn what it feels like not to be protected.”"（ch16）
两个人都是悲剧的受害者，也都是加害链上的一环。

**Quentin（squire）** 正传里他始终守在「我是你的 squire，我本来就该待在你所在的地方」的位置上：
- "Quentin looked at me, mulish. “I’m your squire. I’m supposed to be where you are.”"（ch08）
- 番外则把他的伤口摊开——他是那个被留下的人；而他要学的功课写在别人对他说的话里：「你还是你」："“Only someone who’d learned most of their morality from Toby would still think Faerie gave a single damn about being fair. You’re still you, Quentin. You’re just you with a few messy extra bits mixed in and making everything harder for you to understand right now. Breathe. That jumped-up old florist doesn’t get to undo everything you’ve worked for with a little sorcery.”"（ch21）
