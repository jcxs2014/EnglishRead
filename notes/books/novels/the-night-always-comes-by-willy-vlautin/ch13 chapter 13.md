# Chapter 13: Abandoning Cody

**一句话概括**: Lynette escapes the mechanic's shop, confronts Cody for betraying her, and tricks him into taking a worthless car key while she keeps the money and cocaine.

**情感弧线位置**: Falling action — Lynette transitions from survival mode to calculated revenge

**Tropes 兑现/反转**: The "double-cross" trope—Cody attempts to steal from Lynette but is outsmarted; the "useless car" reveal subverts the promised Mercedes payoff

**人物弧线**: Lynette demonstrates cold cunning learned from years of caregiving manipulation; Cody's desperation makes him predictable and vulnerable

---

> **原句 1:** "The field ended at a paved road and a row of houses. There were no sidewalks and dogs barked from every home she passed." … "A floodlight was now on and she saw Cody sitting on the trunk of her car in the steadily falling rain."

**中文理解**: Lynette 翻越围栏逃回修车厂，看见 Cody 坐在她车尾箱上淋雨——Kansas 已开车送助手去医院。

**关键词**: floodlight / trunk / steadily falling rain

**为什么这样写**: 雨水贯穿全章成为情绪载体；Cody 的被动等待显示他的无助和依赖。

**读者视角提示**: 灯光照亮现场，象征秘密即将曝光——Lynette 必须决定如何处置这个背叛者。

> **原句 2:** "Get out of my car," she told Cody. … "That's bullshit. I ain't getting out." … "The buses have quit and it's raining like a motherfucker. No way I'm getting out. Just give me my cut and a ride back to the Dutchman."

**中文理解**: Lynette 命令 Cody 下车，他拒绝——公交停运、大雨倾盆，他要求分赃后送他回餐厅。

**关键词**: get out / buses have quit / my cut

**为什么这样写**: Cody 的耍赖暴露他的无赖本质；提及公交呼应前文他对通勤的抱怨。

**读者视角提示**: 权力动态开始逆转——Lynette 掌握车和钱，Cody 从合作者沦为乞求者。

> **原句 3:** "She looked at the Chinese restaurant. It was closed, but the inside lights were on. The walls and tables were gold. There were two women vacuuming and Lynette leaned over and opened the glove box. She grabbed a pint of Jägermeister from it, took a long drink, and handed it to Cody."

**中文理解**: Lynette 在路边停车，从手套箱拿出野格酒猛喝一口后递给 Cody——中餐馆内灯亮着，两名女性在吸尘。

**关键词**: Jägermeister / gold / vacuuming

**为什么这样写**: 酒精作为临时镇定剂；金色内饰与外部黑暗形成对照，象征正常生活的遥不可及。

**读者视角提示**: 分享酒瓶是短暂和解的姿态，但 Lynette 已在策划下一步。

> **原句 4:** "What was I supposed to do?" … "I don't know." Lynette sighed. … "Well, there was nothing. I mean I wasn't going to fight him. I told you he was psycho. And remember you're the one who wanted to steal the safe, not me."

**中文理解**: Cody 为自己不帮忙辩护——Kansas 是疯子，他不会打架；强调是 Lynette 主动策划盗窃。

**关键词**: psycho / wasn't going to fight / you wanted

**为什么这样写**: 推卸责任显示 Cody 的道德懦弱；"you wanted" 将罪责转嫁给 Lynette。

**读者视角提示**: 这段话揭示两人关系的本质——Cody 只愿享受收益，不愿承担风险。

> **原句 5:** "Cody lit a cigarette. 'What are you going to do with the drugs?' … 'I could probably sell the package for you.' … 'You told Kansas you didn't want them.'" … "I don't, I can't even have the shit near my house, but I know some people who could get rid of it pretty easy."

**中文理解**: Cody 询问毒品处理方式，提出代为销售——尽管之前声称不要毒品，现在却改变主意。

**关键词**: drugs / sell the package / get rid of it

**为什么这样写**: Cody 的反复无常显示他的机会主义；想卖毒品既为赚钱也为控制 Lynette。

**读者视角提示**: 这段对话预示 Cody 的背叛——他会偷走毒品并试图骗取汽车。

> **原句 6:** "But Cody held on to it and snorted more off his hand. When Lynette turned west on Division Street, he put the package in his hoodie pocket and shut the glove box."

**中文理解**: Cody 抓住毒品不放，又吸了一些后藏进连帽衫口袋——公然盗窃行为。

**关键词**: held on to it / snorted / hoodie pocket

**为什么这样写**: 动作描写展示 Cody 的胆大妄为；吸食动作增加他的不可靠性。

**读者视角提示**: 这是第一次背叛，但 Lynette 假装不知——她在观察 Cody 能走多远。

> **原句 7:** "The stuff in the safe is mine as much as it is yours," he said and wiped the condensation on the windshield with his arm. "You couldn't have stole it without my help."

**中文理解**: Cody 声称保险箱财物也有他的一份——没有他的帮助 Lynette 无法得手。

**关键词**: mine as much as yours / without my help

**为什么这样写**:  entitlement（应得感）暴露 Cody 的贪婪；擦车窗的动作显示他的焦虑。

**读者视角提示**: 这句话成为 Lynette 报复的正当理由——Cody 已证明自己不可信任。

> **原句 8:** "Look," he said and took a drag from his cigarette. "I'll be honest. I'm not good when shit gets weird. But I wasn't gonna let you die. I had a plan. You just jumped in before I could do anything."

**中文理解**: Cody 辩解称自己有救援计划，只是 Lynette 行动太快——典型的马后炮式自我辩护。

**关键词**: not good when shit gets weird / had a plan / jumped in

**为什么这样写**: "had a plan" 明显是谎言，增加 Cody 的可笑性；"shit gets weird" 轻描淡写生死危机。

**读者视角提示**: Lynette 识破谎言但不拆穿——沉默比反驳更有力量。

> **原句 9:** "When I got out of prison, my mother gave me my college fund. Ten grand. I was broke and she knew by then I was never going to go to college, so she gave it to me so I wouldn't move in with her." … "And worse than that she probably won't even give me the house when she dies. She's crazy for my cousins, sends them to science camp and shit like that."

**中文理解**: Cody 透露出狱后母亲给他一万美金大学基金，以免他搬回家住；但他抱怨母亲偏爱表亲，房产也不会留给他。

**关键词**: college fund / ten grand / cousins

**为什么这样写**: 家庭背景解释 Cody 的绝望——他被亲人边缘化，金钱成为唯一安全感来源。

**读者视角提示**: 七千美元的说法可疑（实际只剩五千），为后续欺骗埋下伏笔。

> **原句 10:** "At the edge of Gresham was a 1970s single-story ranch house at the end of a cul-de-sac. Two cars and a truck were parked in the driveway and no lights were on." … "Five minutes passed, but he didn't come back. Lynette grew more and more nervous and pulled onto the street."

**中文理解**: Cody 进入死胡同尽头的一栋平房取钱，五分钟后仍未返回——Lynette 紧张加剧。

**关键词**: cul-de-sac / no lights / five minutes

**为什么这样写**: 黑暗房屋营造不安氛围；时间延迟暗示 Cody 可能在撒谎或逃跑。

**读者视角提示**: Lynette 的等待显示她的天真——她仍希望 Cody 会遵守承诺。

> **原句 11:** "There's only five thousand here." … "I told you I bought a bike and an Xbox. And I needed to keep some to cover my rent. I mean I have to keep my room. My parole will get fucked up if I don't have a real place to stay."

**中文理解**: Lynette 发现信封里只有五千而非七千；Cody 辩称买了自行车和游戏机，还需付房租保住假释住所。

**关键词**: five thousand / Xbox / parole

**为什么这样写**: 金额缩水揭露 Cody 的谎言；提及假释增加他的脆弱性（失去住所=违反假释）。

**读者视角提示**: Cody 的借口合理但不可信——他从未打算给足七千。

> **原句 12:** "Cody kept his eyes on the Mercedes. He took the envelope out of his hoodie pocket. 'It better open the door.'" … "She did and across the street the car lit up." … "Goddamn," he said and then suddenly lunged for the key and grabbed it."

**中文理解**: Cody 盯着奔驰车，拿到钥匙后突然扑过去抢夺——贪婪压倒理智。

**关键词**: lunged / grabbed / Goddamn

**为什么这样写**: 暴力动作显示 Cody 的真实意图；"Goddamn" 表达他对车辆的渴望。

**读者视角提示**: 这一扑是转折点——从欺骗升级为抢劫，Lynette 有权自卫。

> **原句 13:** "He put his hands around her throat and pressed so hard she couldn't breathe. He was strangling her. She tried to pull his hands off her neck, but he was too strong and she was jammed against the driver's-side door unable to move."

**中文理解**: Cody 掐住 Lynette 的脖子使她窒息，她被卡在驾驶座门边无法动弹。

**关键词**: strangling / couldn't breathe / jammed

**为什么这样写**: 窒息场景重现第十章 Kansas 的威胁——Lynette 再次面临死亡，但这次来自"盟友"。

**读者视角提示**: Cody 的暴力证明他的软弱只是表象，绝望使人危险。

> **原句 14:** "With her left hand she grabbed his hair and pulled on it as hard as she could. He screamed and took his hands from her throat and hit her in the stomach, but by then she got her feet up from under the steering wheel and began kicking him."

**中文理解**: Lynette 猛扯 Cody 的头发迫使他松手，随后用脚踢他脸部——从防御转为反击。

**关键词**: grabbed his hair / kicking / steering wheel

**为什么这样写**: 头发是 Cody 的弱点（前文描述其卷曲长发）；踢击动作显示 Lynette 的求生本能。

**读者视角提示**: 这场搏斗是 Lynette 三年压抑的爆发——她对所有剥削者的愤怒集中在这一刻。

> **原句 15:** "As he staggered back into the street, Lynette found Kenny's old Portland Beavers mini-bat on the floor of the back seat, grabbed it, got out of the car, and ran to him. 'Give me the fucking key back,' she yelled. But Cody was holding Kansas's Buck knife."

**中文理解**: Lynette 拿起 Kenny 的迷你棒球棒追打 Cody，但他掏出 Kansas 的刀对峙。

**关键词**: mini-bat / Buck knife / Give me the key back

**为什么这样写**: Kenny 的球棒成为武器，象征弟弟间接保护姐姐；刀与棒的对峙升级冲突。

**读者视角提示**: 这把刀是第十一章 Kansas 交给金发男子的，Cody 偷走它预示后续悲剧。

> **原句 16:** "Okay. I don't want to get stabbed. I'll leave, but just so you know, you have the wrong key. It's a key to a Saab. I have the right key in my pocket. I hit that one at the same time I hit the one I gave you."

**中文理解**: Lynette 谎称给的是萨博车钥匙，真钥匙在她口袋里——心理战术使 Cody 分心查看。

**关键词**: wrong key / Saab / right key

**为什么这样写**: 谎言利用 Cody 的不安全感；具体品牌（Saab）增加可信度。

**读者视角提示**: 这招源自她多年照顾 Kenny 时学会的转移注意力技巧——操纵弱者的方法反过来用于对付 Cody。

> **原句 17:** "A panic fell across Cody's face and he took the key from his pocket and glanced at it for just a moment, and when he did, Lynette hit him on the side of the neck with the bat. He dropped the knife and the key and fell to the ground and she began to beat him."

**中文理解**: Cody 惊慌查看钥匙时，Lynette 用球棒猛击他颈部，将其打倒在地并持续殴打。

**关键词**: panic / hit / beat him

**为什么这样写**: 暴力描写不加修饰，显示 Lynette 的愤怒积累；颈部打击可能致命，但她已不在乎。

**读者视角提示**: 这次殴打是对所有剥削者的清算——Cody 代表了一连串利用她的人。

> **原句 18:** "By the end he was sobbing. His mouth and nose were pouring out blood and he was curled into a ball. When she quit, he was still breathing, but he wasn't moving."

**中文理解**: Cody 被打到蜷缩成一团哭泣，口鼻流血不止——Lynette 停手时他仍有呼吸但不动弹。

**关键词**: sobbing / pouring out blood / curled into a ball

**为什么这样写**: 受害者姿态唤起同情，但也是 Cody 罪有应得；"still breathing" 确认未致死。

**读者视角提示**: Lynette 的克制显示她仍有底线——她想要惩罚而非谋杀。

> **原句 19:** "Minutes passed and she stared at Cody on the pavement, under the street lamp, not moving. She was about to call for an ambulance when he sat up and slowly stood. He spit on the ground and felt his face and looked around until he found the Mercedes key."

**中文理解**: Lynette 犹豫是否叫救护车时，Cody 自行起身找到奔驰钥匙，吐了一口痰后准备离开。

**关键词**: street lamp / ambulance / Mercedes key

**为什么这样写**: Cody 的坚韧令人惊讶；吐痰动作显示他的屈辱和愤怒。

**读者视角提示**: 他找到钥匙意味着 Lynette 的谎言失败——但她已有新计划。

> **原句 20:** "He opened the driver's-side door, got in, and started it. The headlights came on and she watched him drive off."

**中文理解**: Cody 启动奔驰车驶离，Lynette 目送他离开——她记下车牌号，准备报警陷害他。

**关键词**: started it / headlights / drive off

**为什么这样写**: 车灯亮起象征 Cody 短暂的胜利；Lynette 的静默观察预示她的复仇。

**读者视角提示**: 这辆车将成为 Cody 的陷阱——Lynette 已拨打匿名电话举报盗窃。
