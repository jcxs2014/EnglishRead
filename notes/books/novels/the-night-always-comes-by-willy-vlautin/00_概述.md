# 《夜夜来临》概述

## 全书梗概

**一、一天从凌晨三点开始，而她能谈下的条件只有十五分钟。** Kenny 摇着她的脚踝把她拖下床，智力停在三岁的哥哥要的是陪，她想要的是睡："I get to sleep for fifteen more minutes. So please don't touch me or say anything until then."（ch01） 这一天接下来要把面包房的班、酒吧的夜班和一节课串起来；她们住的那栋老宅，地基是 1922 年用劣质水泥浇的，雨季从约六处往里渗。

**二、她全部的筹划压在一栋漏水的房子上。** 七十五岁的房东愿意让她们优先买下，价格是他主动让出来的；可 Lynette 一个人贷不下款来，母亲的回应是一声粗粝的笑："Then you buy it," their mother said and let out a rough laugh.（ch03）

**三、同一天，车先到了。** 母亲未经商量买了一台白色 Toyota Avalon Limited，没有牌照、只有临时行驶牌，理由是那些销售「一点都不油腻」："I didn't tell you because I knew you'd get upset. But I've been looking to get a new car for a long time, you know that, and those guys at Toyota of Portland are nice. They're not slimy at all."（ch03）  Lynette 当场问出的只有一句：这会不会把贷款搞砸。

**四、崩溃是从一杯打翻的咖啡开始的。** Kenny 尖叫着在客厅来回踱步，她道歉、把他带回房间、关上卧室门，坐在他身边哭出来："She shut the bedroom door, sat down next to him, and began to sob."（ch04） 她既是照护者也是那个吼了的人，这两件事她自己分得清。

**五、夜里她是另一个人。** 父亲来酒吧时带着酒精刻出来的脸：
- "He was a handsome man with tired blue eyes and a veined alcoholic's face."（ch05）
- 她去 Hotel deLuxe 见一位已婚男人，房间是他先开好的，而他惊讶的是她竟然主动打了电话："'I already got us a room. You know, I was surprised you called. You've never called me before.'"（ch06）
- 她还去一处旧相识家门口讨钱，得到的回答是「什么钱」："'Then I just need my money.' 'What money?'"（ch07）

**六、买房计划在一天之内归零。** 她原本已经排好了刷墙的（父亲的旧班底）、画队徽的（9th Street 面包房里那位会画画的同事）、装电的（一名有执照的电工，用一天工换一个月免费酒水），等着搬进那栋房子；现在这些安排一起作废："In one day, years of her planning and struggle and sacrifice came to mean nothing. There would be no house; she wouldn't be able to bring that to Kenny or her mother. She had failed."（ch08）

**七、要账的人，在衣柜里看见一只保险箱。** 她带着七个月前那笔以酒驾为由借出的八千美元去找 Gloria，开口要还："And I know you're in a hurry, but I was hoping I could get back the money I loaned you." "The money?" "The eight thousand dollars I gave you." Gloria looked at her. "For the DUI? You told me you were gonna give me the money back within a week no problem. But it's been seven months."（ch09）  Gloria 躲开了账，也躲不开那只箱子——它成了 Lynette 唯一的补救方案。

**八、她需要一双手。** 她找上有前科的 Cody，说得很诚实：搬不动、也不会开："There's a safe I want, but I'm not strong enough to carry it out and I don't know how to open it."（ch10） 而 Cody 肯来的理由与道德无关——他坐了一年公交车，太累了。

**九、三分钟，和一场摊在案台上的谋杀。** 进 Gloria 公寓到带着箱子出来：
- "They were inside Gloria's apartment less than three minutes."（ch11）
- 修车厂里 Kansas 把东西一件件摆上台面，也说清了她的下场："We'll bleed her in the utility sink and put the rest of her in the acid drum."（ch12）
她靠一句谎（一辆八万美元的奔驰）和一次未必能通的 911 撑到那个发作的助手倒地，抢回钱物翻过铁丝网逃走。

**十、往回追，她的账本比一晚更长。** 凌晨的 Hotcake House 里她想起祖父——他总把她介绍成「史上最好的小孩」，也是唯一让她短暂脱离 Kenny 的人：
- "He was proud of her and always introduced her as 'the greatest kid of all time.' He had been good to her, had always been kind, and he gave Lynette her only real break from Kenny."（ch14）
- 她带着可卡因回到 JJ Benada 家，那个在她高一就盯上她的旧货店老板；十一个月里她在那栋房子学会喝酒、吸毒，也第一次彻底垮掉："For eleven months she learned to drink, smoke weed, and do cocaine in that house. And it was there that she lost her virginity to JJ and slept with other men, and a woman, too. It was also where she first fell apart, where her anger finally erupted, where she lost ten pounds, had hives on her back, and spiraled into her first depression."（ch15）
- 最后一步是修车厂的拖车，Rodney 一句话揭穿这不是交易："The deal is you can get out of my trailer now."（ch17）

**十一、母亲替她取背上的玻璃，也在同一夜说出真话。** 她终于把问题问出口："I'm not that smart, but I think I'm beginning to figure out what you're doing. . . . You don't want to live with me, do you? This is about me. That's what's going on?"（ch18） 答案是承认——母亲爱她，但不想再和她住在一起。

**十二、Gloria 上门兴师问罪，被一句「同一种人」顶回去。** 她把自己和 Gloria 放在同一个词里，然后把威胁说得很具体："You're a prostitute, I'm a prostitute. But I've tried to be your friend, your real friend, and you fucked me. You basically stole from me. So please just leave me alone, and if you keep bothering me, I'll call Terry and I'll tell him to look for your Catlin Gabel yearbook and I'll tell him you didn't go Berkeley."（ch19）

**十三、卖车、寄钱，母亲的算盘。** 她在拆车场交出所有权证和钥匙，换到两百美元现钞，然后和 Kenny 在游戏机旁吃一块中号意大利辣香肠披萨：
- At B & R Auto Wrecking she gave them the title and the keys and they gave her two hundred dollars cash for the car. It was four fifty p.m. and raining and already dark. From there they took a Radio Cab to Stark Street Pizza. Lynette ordered a medium pepperoni and two Cokes and they sat by the video games and ate.（ch20）
- 母亲搬去 Mona 那里的理由是一整套福利安排，谁都不多拿、谁都不吃亏；而 Lynette 只回了一句——我带 Kenny 走，那才是说好的。真正落地的是母亲那套自认命定的哲学："I'm starting to think that some people are just born to sink. Born to fail. And I'm beginning to realize that I'm one of those people, and you have no idea what that's like. How truly awful it is to know that about yourself. But that doesn't mean I want things as hard as they've been. And it doesn't mean I want it to stay that way. It doesn't mean I like it either. But I've got to live, too, don't I?"（ch20）

**十四、Shirley 给了她一条出路和一台车。** 报纸上说该去中西部的老城市，那里便宜、人正在回流，也许你真能开一家面包店："You know, I read in the paper a while ago that the places to move to are in the Midwest. That it's cheap in the old cities like St. Louis or Kansas City or Detroit or Cleveland. Everyone left them for a while, but now people are going back. Maybe you could start a bakery out there. In some city like that. How much money you got?"（ch21） 那台 2003 年的 Buick LeSabre 本是 Shirley 父亲的遗物，Lynette 打电话想买它；车有洗不掉的雪茄味、只跑过三万英里，却在 Craigslist 挂了整整一个月没人要。Shirley 最后不收钱，只说不许在她邮箱里塞钞票。

**十五、凌晨的州际公路。** 她对 Kenny 的保证里塞着一句球队嘱托，也塞着一句誓言：
- "Remember to say hello to the Trail Blazers for me. Tell them not to trade CJ or Damian. And remember no matter where you end up, I'll come and get you. I swear on my life I will, and when you see me, I'll be good, I'll be doing good."（ch22）
- 她给母亲留下便条，锁门后把钥匙从邮件槽塞回屋里，倒了杯咖啡，向东开进雨里：At the kitchen table, Lynette wrote a note to her mother saying goodbye and that she loved her. When she'd locked the front door, she put her key through the mail slot and got into the Buick. She poured a cup of coffee and started the car. It was still raining and past midnight when she got on the interstate and headed east.（ch22）

## 三个主题

**一、运气是一种货币，而穷人只有一张。** 这本书里最贵的一样东西不是房子，是「第一次交上好运」这件事本身。Lynette 数得清自己为那栋房子省下的每一笔：请朋友刷墙、找电工给全天工、给 Kenny 挑房间；而母亲那句「那你买啊」之所以刺耳，是因为她比谁都清楚这笔账的结构——她只有靠母亲才贷得下款："I'm really confused. . . . We're supposed to sign the papers on the house next week. Is it going to screw up the loan? Did you think about the loan?"（ch03） 一次冲动购车就把多年归零，这不是运气不好，是本来就没有缓冲。

**二、城市在换人，而换掉的方式很客气。** 她工作的面包房卖掉员工停车场，原地盖起半栋十层公寓；二十年前的空仓库现在是 loft、餐厅和 condo："Twenty years ago the area was mostly deserted warehouses; now high-end lofts and stores, restaurants, and condominiums stood in their place."（ch02） 母亲在浴缸边说的那句话把这件事拉到自家门口——这座城市变得太快，快到她认不出自己童年走过的街。

**三、爱与「一起生活」不是同一件事。** 全书最狠的一刀不是 Kansas 的撬棍，是母亲那句「我太累了，不想再谈这件事」；而最软的一句也来自同一个人：她说她爱 Lynette，只是不想和她住。这条裂缝让 Lynette 在 Shirley 家的客厅里对她复述时，第一次把原因说得很干净："She doesn't want to live with me, Shirley. That's why she doesn't want the house. That's the reason. She doesn't want to be near me anymore."（ch21）

## 人物弧光

**Lynette（第一人称叙述者）** 她从「再给我一次运气」走到「我自己上路」。前半本她所有计算都围着房子转：贷款、房东、装修、Kenny 的房间；后半本她的计算单位换成了一趟趟具体的事——要账、偷箱、卖车、寄东西、被人赶出拖车。她的反抗不是英雄式的：在 Rodney 的拖车里她用的是一瓶防狼喷雾，在修车厂她用的是谎话和一部没有把握的手机。真正的转折是母亲的拒绝——她失去的不是房子，是「我们三个人一起」这个前提。

**Kenny（哥哥）** 他想要的东西一直很简单：手机、散步、Superman 这个称呼、开拓者队别交易球员。全书第一个动作是他做的——他双手抱住姐姐的脚踝，把她从床上往外拖："Kenny had his hands around her ankle and began pulling her from the bed."（ch01） 他从被照护者变成了 Lynette 唯一不敢弄砸的那件事；告别的场景发生在母亲的房间里，他坐在床上，被她一遍遍亲脸，直到他把头扭开、把她推开。

**母亲** 她不是反派，她是一个把宿命论想通了的人。她爱女儿、会熟练地取玻璃碎片、会把房子的事记在心上，但她拒绝和女儿共同生活，并用一套「有人生来就会沉」的说法给自己放行：
- "I'm starting to think that some people are just born to sink. Born to fail. And I'm beginning to realize that I'm one of those people, and you have no idea what that's like. How truly awful it is to know that about yourself. But that doesn't mean I want things as hard as they've been. And it doesn't mean I want it to stay that way. It doesn't mean I like it either. But I've got to live, too, don't I?"（ch20）
- 最后她把这套说法直接传给 Lynette："Well, fine then. If they're gonna do what they want to do, I'll do what I have to do, too. Screw them. That's what I believe in now. So this is my advice to you, Lynette, at the end of the day just look out for yourself and screw everyone else."（ch22）

**Gloria Milligan** 她把自己重造成了 Catlin Gabel 和 Berkeley 出来的女人，而 Lynette 是唯一记得 Clatskanie 拖车的人。「警察」是她一上门就先搬出来的筹码（还箱子就不报警）；被 Lynette 拆穿学历之后她先是加钱（连一千块「利息」一起给），最后那句话才骂到 Kenny 和母亲身上——而辱骂与「报警把你们全送进监狱」是同一口气里说完的，先骂人、后报警。她上门的第一句就是指控："I know you stole my safe."（ch19）

**Cody 与 Kansas** Cody 是那种「事情变糟时我不行」的人，事后仍坚持自己本来有方案："Look," he said and took a drag from his cigarette. "I'll be honest. I'm not good when shit gets weird. But I wasn't gonna let you die. I had a plan. You just jumped in before I could do anything."（ch13） Kansas 则把谋杀当成报价单来讲。两个人都不是天降的恶，而是同一套「谁都只为自己」的逻辑里长出来的两种浓度。

**Shirley** 全书唯一给出无条件东西的人。她早就知道 Lynette 在做什么，也一直没有说出去，理由很简单——她明白她为什么做。她的判断落在同一章她替母亲记的那本账上：上百杯免费的 Lemon Drop，一次都没有小费："And I will say this, too, I'm relieved that your mom backed out on the house. I'm really happy for you because you would have been handcuffed to her for the rest of her life. And you don't deserve that. I've known your mom a lot of years. As you know, I knew her when she was married to your dad. And in the three years you've worked at the Dutchman, she's never once come in while you're working, but she's come in while I'm working because she knows I'll give her free drinks. After a while I started writing down the number of free drinks. I've given her over a hundred free Lemon Drops and she's never once tipped or asked to pay. And all she's ever done when she's there is bitch. Just complains about things, about her job, you, your brother, your dad. That says enough to me about who she is."（ch21）

**Jack Burns 与祖父** 两个已经不在场的人构成她的标尺。Jack 是叉车司机，是家里除祖父母、母亲、哥哥之外唯一爱过她的人：
- "Outside of her grandparents, her brother, and mother, Jack was the only person she had ever really loved, and he was the only person, outside of her family, who had ever loved her. The only person in the world who had chosen to live with her, chosen to care for her, and took the chance to love her."（ch16）
- 祖父给过她的称号，是她此后每一次自我审问时的那个听众："Tears leaked down her face as she ate. What would her grandfather think of her now? After all the horrible things she'd been a part of, the horrible things that she had done herself or let be done to her."（ch14）
