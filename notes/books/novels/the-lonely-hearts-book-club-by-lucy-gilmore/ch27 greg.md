# 27. Greg（两天后 · 「每一个字我都坚持」与「你姐姐的声音是什么样的」）

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：两条线并行。祖父这边：Greg 拒绝拿道歉当筹码，祖父却自己把话继续说完——梅赛那条、Mateo 那条、斯隆那条，三条全部成立；说到斯隆那条时他把书摔了，然后整个人塌进 Greg 的怀里，问了一句「真是她让你来的吗」。厨房这边：Greg 数着步子，斯隆走进来，四个问题交换两个死人的声音、气味、回忆。车开进了院子。

**情感弧线位置**：弧线的**双向坦白**：这一章的两个人做了同一件事——用最具体的细节讲一个已经不在的人。Greg 问的四句全是诊断式的（声音／气味／回忆／你会对她说什么），斯隆一句一句全答了。作者让这组问答变成两个人交换遗物的仪式。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：「毒舌孤老 × 闯入的关心者」的兑现形态在本章是**他自己塌下来**：他没有道歉，他先声明「每一个字我都坚持」，然后——用 Greg 母亲的死——把全部依据摊开。指责是外壳，底牌是真的。

**人物弧线**：Greg 的弧线在本章给出他最想要的东西，而且是被问出来的：他真正想知道的不是原谅，是那个死掉的人的声音。他讲完母亲之后立刻反问「你失去了谁」——这一问把他自己也交了出去。

**叙事手法**：第一人称 Greg；三场（客厅 → 窗前 → 厨房）。**全章的对称是两个「我有／你有」**：祖父说「她是我女儿」，Greg 回「你浪费了那段时间」；Greg 问她母亲的声音，她答，然后他问她姐姐的。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** “I know we told you to take as long as you need, but we lied. The situation is getting desperate. We’re drowning without you.”

**中文理解**：第一句就承认在骗他。

**关键词**：“I know we told you to take as long as you need, but we lied. The situation is getting desperate. We’re drowning without you.”

**为什么这样写**：全章第一句就是一个自揭骗局，而作者用 we're drowning（我们在溺水）把一个项目延期说成一场生存危机。而这句里 they had said you could take as long as you need（他们说过你尽管慢慢来）这个承诺，是全章真正的主题。

**读者视角提示**：留意前一句说「我们知道跟你说过你尽管慢慢来」——作者让撒谎的一方先把谎的内容复述一遍，读者才听得出那是假的。

---

> **原句 2:** “That makes two of us,” I said grimly. “I’m thinking about cutting my losses and putting him in a home. There are some nice ones around here with bingo game nights and group trips to the children’s theater.”

**中文理解**：他要送他去养老院。

**关键词**：“I’m thinking about cutting my losses and putting him in a home. There are some nice ones around here with bingo game nights and group trips to the children’s theater.”

**为什么这样写**：全章最好笑的一次威胁，而它的形式是一份福利项目清单。作者让他用「儿童剧院」这四个字去攻击一个八十二岁的人，而这份清单是精确计算过的。

**读者视角提示**：留意 cutting my losses（及时止损）——这个说法带着商人的语气，而说的人是这本书里最没心机的人。

---

> **原句 3:** “Take it back,” my grandfather said. He’d risen to his feet with the aid of his cane and was hobbling toward me. “Tell whoever you’re talking to that I’m not going anywhere near a children’s theater. I’d rather be chained up and bricked behind a basement wall.”

**中文理解**：他击穿了。

**关键词**：“I’d rather be chained up and bricked behind a basement wall.”

**为什么这样写**：全章最快的一次反转。一个自称快溺水的人，一个自称准备止损的人，被「儿童剧院」四个字逼出了他对全书的唯一恐惧。而他两天来说的第一句话，就是这句。

**读者视角提示**：留意这句话是 Poe 的典故，下一块专门讲它。

---

> **原句 4:** I couldn’t help laughing. Wayne might not have caught the Poe reference, but I did.

**中文理解**：作者替读者说破。

**关键词**：Wayne might not have caught the Poe reference, but I did.

**为什么这样写**：全章唯一一处作者出面解释。chained up and bricked（锁起来并砌起来）指的就是 Poe 那个把人活埋的故事。作者让书里学历最高的那个人听出了这个出处，而电话那头的同事没有。

**读者视角提示**：留意这是 Greg 第一次在这本书里显示出他读过的东西——而他用的地方是拆自己祖父的梗。

---

> **原句 5:** “Don’t worry.

**中文理解**：他还在装。

**关键词**：“Don’t worry.（别担心。）

**为什么这样写**：全章最短的一块，而它只有三个词。作者让一个刚说完「我在这儿什么都 accomplish 不成，至少这一件我能做到」的人，接着说别担心——而这句话是反讽，因为他正在做的那一件是瞒着他。

**读者视角提示**：留意 紧跟着的下一块才是他真正的意图：他会活下去。

---

> **原句 6:** He’s going to live.” I locked eyes with my grandfather’s. They were like a reflection of my mom’s—the same gray color, the same steely flash of determination—but without a fraction of her warmth.

**中文理解**：他真正的计划。

**关键词**：He’s going to live.” I locked eyes with my grandfather’s. They were like a reflection of my mom’s—the same gray color, the same steely flash of determination—but without a fraction of her warmth.

**为什么这样写**：全章最重要的一次交代，而他把这句计划和他眼睛里的东西写在了同一句里。作者让一个说他「什么都不做」的人宣布他要保住一个人的命，而那双眼是全书他最接近母亲的时刻。

**读者视角提示**：留意 without a fraction of her warmth（连她温暖的万分之一都没有）——这半句是这一章唯一一处他承认自己不如她。

---

> **原句 7:** And then…this. A late-night call from the hospital. The burden of a promise I’d made my mom weighing heavily on my shoulders. A group of unexpectedly friendly strangers offering to share that burden with me.

**中文理解**：他突然看见的东西。

**关键词**：A group of unexpectedly friendly strangers offering to share that burden with me.

**为什么这样写**：全章最温和的一次总结，而 unexpected（出人意料）这三个字是它的前提：这些人本来一个都不认识。作者让「非亲非故」这四个字成了一句赞美。

**读者视角提示**：留意这句话紧跟在他母亲去世的那一段后面——作者用这个顺序说明：先有那份重量，然后有这四个人。

---

> **原句 8:** “That depends. Are you going to apologize for what you said yesterday?”

**中文理解**：他的条件。

**关键词**：“Are you going to apologize for what you said yesterday?”

**为什么这样写**：全章最重要的一次谈判，而它的形式是一个反问。他不是在要道歉（他知道老人不会给），他是在把道歉换成别的东西。

**读者视角提示**：留意这是那场爆发之后两人说的第一句话，而它是一句条件，不是回应。

---

> **原句 9:** He set his jaw. “I don’t have to explain myself to you. Hannah was my daughter long before she became your mother.”

**中文理解**：祖父的挡箭牌。

**关键词**：“Hannah was my daughter long before she became your mother.”

**为什么这样写**：全章最准的一次反击。注意 the long before（很久以前）——这是一个所有权声明，用的是时间顺序。他不给道歉，他给优先权。

**读者视角提示**：留意 Greg 母亲的名字是 Hannah。他下一句的回应里也用这个名字。

---

> **原句 10:** “Yes, and we both know how well you used the time you had together.” I felt proud of the way my voice didn’t waver. “But I wasn’t talking about an apology for me. I meant for them.”

**中文理解**：Greg 的澄清。

**关键词**：“But I wasn’t talking about an apology for me. I meant for them.”

**为什么这样写**：全章最短也最关键的一次转向。作者让他在争取了一个不属于他的东西之后立刻让出去——这既是他第二十二章里那笔交易的用法，也说明他知道自己在干什么。

**读者视角提示**：留意 them（他们）这个词把 Greg 从受害者的位置上挪到了另一个位置。

---

> **原句 11:** “I stand by all of it, you hear? Every single word.

**中文理解**：他守住了。

**关键词**：“I stand by all of it, you hear? Every single word.

**为什么这样写**：全章最响亮的一次表态，而作者用 you hear（你听到没有）给他一个当庭陈述的口气。然后他开始解释——而解释的第一句就比刚才的挑衅更有说服力。

**读者视角提示**：留意 Every single word（每一个字）——这四个字把上一章那场爆发的定性从「失态」改成了「立场」。

---

> **原句 12:** He drew an irritated breath and kept going. “I stand by all of it, you hear? Every single word. Maisey is suffering from the same growing pains every mother feels at that age, only she’s so bored and lonely over there that she can’t see a way out of it. Mateo is shuffling through careers he doesn’t give two licks about, and all because he doesn’t want to give his mother the satisfaction of seeing him follow in her footsteps. And Sloane…”

**中文理解**：他对 Mateo 的那条。

**关键词**：Mateo is shuffling through careers he doesn’t give two licks about, and all because he doesn’t want to give his mother the satisfaction of seeing him follow in her footsteps.

**为什么这样写**：全章最伤人的一次诊断，而它出自这个上一章刚替 Mateo 挡过刀的老人。他给的理由不对——那不是怨恨，那是秀。而他给出理由这件事本身，比理由更让 Mateo 无法反驳。

**读者视角提示**：留意这一章里第一次有人替 Mateo 说话，而说的是他最不想听的人。

---

> **原句 13:** “She’s only marrying that man because he’s offering her the easy way out. He has money and a roof over his head and a built-in family she doesn’t have to work to earn.”

**中文理解**：他对斯隆的那条。

**关键词**：“She’s only marrying that man because he’s offering her the easy way out. He has money and a roof over his head and a built-in family she doesn’t have to work to earn.”

**为什么这样写**：全章最详细的一次指控——也是最真实的一次。作者让老人的观察力在这里精确得可怕，而那份精确是 Greg 刚刚才承认的。

**读者视角提示**：留意他列举的全是实物：钱、屋顶、一整套不用挣就有的家庭。这不是心灵分析，这是清点财产。

---

> **原句 14:** “She doesn’t know what she’s worth. She doesn’t think she can do anything better with her life.” He threw his copy of The Joy Luck Club across the room. It hit the opposite wall with a thud. “But she can, by God. She can and she will.”

**中文理解**：他承认她可以。

**关键词**：“She doesn’t know what she’s worth. She doesn’t think she can do anything better with her life.”

**为什么这样写**：全章最重要的一次翻转——而且是老人自己完成的。作者让一个刚刚用最恶毒的话贬低斯隆的人，接着说出全书对她最准确的一句评价。而他把这本书摔到对面墙上才说出口。

**读者视角提示**：留意紧跟着的那半句 But she can, by God. She can and she will（但她能，看在老天的份上。她能，而且她会的）——这句才是这一章真正的落点。

---

> **原句 15:** To my surprise, my grandfather suddenly started crumpling into a heap. Leaping up from the bed, I had my arms around him before either of us knew what was happening. The fact that he didn’t fight me was bad enough—that he actually let out a sound almost like a sob as I held him was even worse. He felt much more fragile than his size seemed to indicate, as though his body was just a projection to distract from the real man inside.

**中文理解**：他塌了。

**关键词**：he actually let out a sound almost like a sob as I held him

**为什么这样写**：全章最短也最重的一次描写。作者用了 almost（几乎）——他并没有真的哭出来。

**读者视角提示**：留意 And then they left（然后他们就走了）那句在第 21 块：他抱住祖父之后屋里其他三个人立刻站成了一道弧线。

---

> **原句 16:** “Did she really ask you to come here?” he asked, and in so quiet a voice I almost had to put my ear to his lips to hear him. “It was her idea?”

**中文理解**：他问的第一句。

**关键词**：“Did she really ask you to come here?”

**为什么这样写**：全章最重要的一个问题，而它的内容不是「她为什么让我来」，是「她有没有求你」。

**读者视角提示**：留意这一章里「她」指过三个人：他母亲（他不在场）、Hannah（他刚刚吵过架）、Sloane（她最后问出口的那句）。这三次指代连起来，就是 Greg 这五个月走过的路。

---

> **原句 17:** “Yes,” I said, my own voice low and tight. I didn’t have to ask him to explain. I knew very well that we weren’t talking about Sloane anymore. “It’s the only reason I came, and the only reason I’m still here now. If it had been left up to me, I’d have buried you alongside her.”

**中文理解**：Greg 的答案。

**关键词**：“If it had been left up to me, I’d have buried you alongside her.”

**为什么这样写**：全章最诚实的一句威胁，而它之所以诚实，是因为它不是真的。作者让 Greg 说出他最想说的话，而下一段祖父的回应只是点了点头。

**读者视角提示**：留意这是全章唯一一次 Greg 说出「你」。这一章他最想砸的就是那一个人。

---

> **原句 18:** That was all he said—just two words, two syllables—but it was enough to lay my fears to temporary rest. He was fine. I was fine. We weren’t good and we weren’t whole, but we were getting by.

**中文理解**：他们的现状。

**关键词**：We weren’t good and we weren’t whole, but we were getting by.

**为什么这样写**：全章最短也最准的诊断，而它是复数主语（we）。两个分句全是「不」，最后才落到一个正面的词上。

**读者视角提示**：留意作者连一句安慰都不肯给到底——getting by（撑下去）之后他还要再补一句 For now（眼下而已）。

---

> **原句 19:** A few days later, I paced the length of my grandfather’s kitchen, my long steps eating up the linoleum and spitting it back out again.

**中文理解**：他在数步子。

**关键词**：My long steps eating up the linoleum and spitting it back out again.

**为什么这样写**：全章最好笑的一次身体描写，而作者用 eating up（吃掉）和 spitting back out（吐回来）来写踱步——这是一个人在原地消化什么东西的隐喻。

**读者视角提示**：留意四—三—二—一。他踱到窗前就倒着数回去。这一段是全章唯一一处他不在别人身边。

---

> **原句 20:** Four-three-two-one. I was back at the pantry, where we’d stored enough foodstuffs to carry my grandfather through the apocalypse and back again. And not just dried beans and boxes of powdered eggs, either. This was the good stuff. I’d never even heard of half the food being kept in here. What were you supposed to do with hearts of palm?

**中文理解**：他窗前站着时在想什么。

**关键词**：What were you supposed to do with hearts of palm?

**为什么这样写**：全章最莫名其妙的一句问话，也正是这一章笑点的核心：一个儿子在替父亲清点储藏室时，思考一种蔬菜该怎么处理。

**读者视角提示**：留意这一句之所以成立，是因为前一段先写了足够的存粮。而斯隆在第二十三章刚把这栋房子收拾成了一个家。

---

> **原句 21:** “What did her voice sound like?” I asked, since it seemed like the only way I could repay her.

**中文理解**：他的第一个问题。

**关键词**：“What did her voice sound like?”

**为什么这样写**：全章最重要的一句转折。作者让一个刚跟祖父吵完架的人，走到自己母亲的记忆里去问一个具体到不能再具体的问题。

**读者视角提示**：留意这四句问题的顺序：声音、气味、回忆、你会对她说什么。前面三句是取证，第四句才是那个真正的请求。

---

> **原句 22:** “She had a congenital heart defect,” she added before I could ask. “She was born with an early expiration date.”

**中文理解**：她的答案。

**关键词**：“She was born with an early expiration date.”

**为什么这样写**：全章最能被记住的一句，因为它把死亡写成出厂设置而不是意外。作者用 expiration date（有效期）替代了死，而这个替换是那条线的全部逻辑。

**读者视角提示**：留意 expired 这个词在本章之前没有出现过。而它解释了这个人物身上几乎所有的事。

---

> **原句 23:** She must have known this one was coming, because she fingered the old Victorian-looking pin she always wore. “She made this for me. It’s from our favorite book. Once things got bad, we didn’t get outside a lot, so reading together was our only way of experiencing the world. Even though our surroundings were confined, we went so many places together, shared so many adventures. And after she was gone, well…” Her voice trailed off and she looked out the window toward the backyard I’d been examining for myself less than an hour ago. When she glanced back at me, it was with a look of self-deprecation that brought out a small dimple I’d never noticed before. “I’m a disgraced former librarian so desperate to find that feeling again that I forced a book club on an unwilling old man. Clearly, I haven’t changed much.”

**中文理解**：她为什么在这里。

**关键词**：“I’m a disgraced former librarian so desperate to find that feeling again that I forced a book club on an unwilling old man.

**为什么这样写**：全章最重要的一次自白，而它的形式是自嘲。作者让她用一个最不体面的理由解释一件最体贴的行为，而 Greg 发现自己一点也高兴不起来。

**读者视角提示**：留意 Here（在这里）和 again（再次）这两个词同时出现——她要找回的不是那种感觉本身，是那种感觉曾经存在过这件事。

---

> **原句 24:** “What would you say to her?” I asked, my next line just as clear as all the rest. “If she was with us right now?”

**中文理解**：他换了个方向问。

**关键词**：“What would you say to her?” I asked, my next line just as clear as all the rest.

**为什么这样写**：全章唯一一次两个问句被写进同一个分析块。作者让他在同一口气里问完「你失去了谁」和「你会对她说什么」——而这正是这一章的交换结构。

**读者视角提示**：留意这一段没被引用，因为它是全书最安静的一刻：作者在这里没让任何一个人讲道理。

---

> **原句 25:** “I can’t believe we’re actually doing this,” Sloane said as her hand shot out and sought mine. I recognized her sweaty, tenacious grip for what it was—a friendly gesture meant to soothe and be soothed—but that didn’t stop my stomach from doing an actual somersault. I liked how hot she felt, how real. “Are we sure this is a good idea?”

**中文理解**：她的最后一句。

**关键词**：“Are we sure this is a good idea?”

**为什么这样写**：全章最后一个问句，而它出现在全章最甜蜜的时刻之后三秒钟。作者让一个刚刚交出自己最深的记忆的人，先问一句风险。

**读者视角提示**：留意这一句是唯一一次她用问句。而在之前那些提问里她一次都没有为自己问过什么。

---

> **原句 26:** “Not even remotely,” I said, not daring to move until she did. “But there’s nothing we can do to stop this train now.”

**中文理解**：他的答案。

**关键词**：“Not even remotely,” I said, not daring to move until she did. “But there’s nothing we can do to stop this train now.”

**为什么这样写**：全章的最后一句，也是这本书前半部分的最后一个节奏。作者让一个人在被握住手的时候不敢动，而最后一句把整件事定性成一列停不下来的车。

**读者视角提示**：这一章结束在一个门口：奈杰尔已经到了，而他还坐着没动。

## 一句话总结

祖父拒绝道歉，却继续把话说下去——他的三条判断全对，直到斯隆那一条把他自己说塌了。他抱住 Greg，问「真是她让你来的吗」，得到一个带威胁的答案之后闭上眼睛。几天后，Greg 在厨房里数步子，斯隆走进来——然后他们开始交换各自死掉的那个人的声音、气味和回忆。车开进院子了。
