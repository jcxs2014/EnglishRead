# 《Sleepers in the Snow》情感节点

十个节点按全书推进排列；每节点为叙事概括加两三句关键引语（引语均为已过门禁的章节原句）。

## 节点一：买下它的人（ch01）

他因「太便宜」买下 Kirkhill，第一夜就看见窗玻璃上的白发老汉——逃离婚姻的人以为买到了庇护，买到的是一座伏击。荒原的第一件赠礼（门口的崭新网球）已经等在那里。

> "He bought the house because it was cheap. Too cheap, perhaps, for that striking view; too cheap for the swatch of purple moor and the reservoir in the distance."（ch01）

> "And through his reflection in the glass, he thought he saw someone looking in. A man with white hair and open mouth, staring eyes in the yellow light."（ch01）

## 节点二：前主人的日记盒（ch02–05）

衣柜抽屉里的丝带日记盒打开了两条时间线：Caroline 的生日被忘、爱犬 Bailey 的死、以及 Arthur 提着死狗的牵绳走进雪里。他替素不相识的人开始心疼。

> "The wardrobe had a lingering scent of lavender and mothballs. There was a mirror inside the door, showing him his reflection at eighty, ninety, a hundred years old."（ch02）

> "More snow today. Arthur's gone out with Bailey's lead and a tennis ball in his pocket."（ch05）

## 节点三：楼梯上的弹跳声（ch09–10）

他第一次以第一人称写日记，从「Arthur 与 Caroline 只是名册上的名字」到楼梯上滚下来的球——荒原第一次把东西送到他脚边。

> "I haven't kept a diary since I was in high school. But this is a new beginning, and keeping a record seems to make sense."（ch09）

> "He awoke to the sudden, rhythmic sound of something on the stairs. Bump-bump. The stairs were wooden, uncarpeted, and the sound was both familiar and alarmingly out of place: the sound of a ball bouncing down the stairs, a longer interval between each bounce as gravity took over."（ch10）

## 节点四：为 Caroline 摆的餐具（ch16–20）

他用 Caroline 的浴油洗澡、认领小屋原名 Firefleet、在她的生日摆两副餐具对空椅说话——哀悼越过了调查，变成了同居。万圣夜，威胁短信抵达。

> "He put down the pen, remembering Arthur's journal. Deer are good animals. What an odd phrase."（ch16）

> "And it was pleasant, cooking for two; setting two plates at the table; pouring two glasses of wine at the meal and seeing her coat on the back of the chair."（ch20）

> "He dropped the phone as if it had delivered a burst of current. Then, he picked it up again and turned it back on, and read it once more, before dropping it for a second time."（ch20）

## 节点五：Benn 的信（ch21–22）

第一封信警告「雪落之前就太迟」；Arthur 的日记停在「我想我要躺一会儿」——此后再无一字。两条时间线在此并轨：警告与结局都已在纸上。

> "You need to get out now. I know what we said, but you're in worse danger than I thought. Grab a bag and leave."（ch21）

> "Thunderstorm and snow last night. Looked out of the window, and saw the sky walk by on stilts of lightning. The lines are all down. The walkers are here."（ch22）

## 节点六：衣柜里的哀求（ch27）

包裹里的四十多张照片让他第一次看清 Arthur 与 Caroline 的脸——窗玻璃上的鬼影有了名字。他穿上 Arthur 的冬装，衣柜里 Caroline 的声音叫的却是 Arthur。

> "Please, Arthur. Don't do this. The scent from the wardrobe intensified. Green lawn; warm skin; summertime. Cocktails on the terrace. Cashmere sweaters, fretted by moths. The raspberry taste of her lipstick. We used to be so happy, before. Please, Arthur, let me in—"（ch27）

> "It was the old man he'd seen that first night through the kitchen window."（ch27）

## 节点七：雪人与雪球（ch30–31）

雪带来了他自以为的平静；他花一个多小时堆了个穿 Caroline 大衣的雪人，自认是雪球里的王。然后雪人转身，又消失——他为自己筑的温柔第一次反对他。

> "I am alone in the snow globe, he thought, here in my own private world. No one to trouble my solitude."（ch30）

> "One day he built a snowman by the untrodden path to the pines. He worked on it for over an hour, until his hands were chapped and red"（ch30）

> "Caroline says she wants to leave. But she just stands out there on the moor. She smiles. She knocks and knocks. She walks, but she doesn't get anywhere."（ch31）

## 节点八：电塔脚下（ch33）

Arthur 活着的消息确认了，他反而走向电塔——Caroline 与 Benn 的声音同时劝阻，他摔倒在大塔脚下，狂笑，喊「这里什么都没有」。真相落空，人已站在祭坛上。

> "It's asleep, whispered Caroline's voice from the wires overhead. Why would you want to wake it now?"（ch33）

> "He tilted his head back, saw the world spinning like the spokes of a wheel, lost his balance and fell backwards into the snow, seeing the awful symmetries of the pylon over him shift and turn like the Eye of God."（ch33）

## 节点九：路人的版本（ch36–37）

徒步者说出最冷的版本：Arthur 把妻子关在门外冻死，她的手全是敲门敲烂的血口；而他转身补了一句——这里附近没有叫 Lyke 的地方。回家路上，门口的雪人被拆成了一堆衣服。

> "'No one lives there,' he said again. 'No one since the tragedy. Man killed his wife of thirty years. No one wants to live there now.'"（ch36）

> "'Shut her out of the house to freeze to death in the middle of winter. Made out it was an accident, but folk round here knew better. Heard her hands were all bloody and torn from knocking and knocking at the door.'"（ch36）

> "the distorted voice went on sobbing and pleading. Oh, my Caroline. My lovely girl. This number has been disconnected. I'm sorry. Their words had forked no lightning, no. I'm sorry. Do not. Do not go. Do not go gentle. Caroline."（ch37）

## 节点十：最长的夜（ch40–41）

冬至夜电缆崩断，Lucy 端着「World's Best Dad」的杯子讲完一切：Arthur 献祭记忆换取悬停，所有异象都是他的潜意识在敲门。他在日记里写下与 Arthur 末条几乎逐字重演的最后一句（唯结尾措辞稍异）——然后，病历接管了叙事。

> "'Let me tell you a story,' she said. 'This is, after all, the longest night. The night when spirits walk, and the dead rise cold and hungry from their howes. The night when the old gods walk the Earth, and Christ is just a newborn.'"（ch40）

> "And so Arthur gave up his memory – the memory of the life he had led, the woman he had loved, their sorrows, their joys, the children they raised together. He even gave up his remorse for the terrible act he'd committed."（ch40）

> "He picked up his diary and wrote: I think I'm going to lie down for a while."（ch40）

> "Notes: Patient remains largely non-verbal, with brief intervals of lucidity. No response to visitors. Requires two-person assist to stand. Sleeps 18–22 hours a day."（ch41）
