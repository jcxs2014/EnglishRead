# 35. Plunder

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Storm 后 Calvin 加入 raft 里的 sexual encounter，和 Zane、Easton 一起和 Haley 发生四人性行为，之后 Easton 发现一艘船。

**情感弧线位置**：高潮 → 转折，从 sexual abundance 到 rescue hope。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：Ménage（四个人的性）+ Post-Storm Sex（storm 后的性）+ Rescue Fantasy（第二次看到船）。

**人物弧线**：Calvin 从 exclusion (在雨中守火) 到 inclusion (加入性活动) 到 protective (拿着flare gun保护大家)。他的 sexual ownership 欲望和 practical survival instinct 在最后冲突。

**叙事手法**：Calvin POV，第一次从这个角度叙述他的 desires 和 insecurities。"She's the kind you keep"揭示他 emotional vulnerability。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** The fire's out. I tried the best I could to keep it going, but the rain poured through the flimsy fire protection we threw together at the last minute.

**中文理解**：火灭了。我尽最大努力保持它燃烧，但雨从我们最后时刻匆忙搭建的脆弱防火罩倾泻而下。

**关键词**：fire's out / tried the best I could / flimsy fire protection / rain poured

**为什么这样写**：Calvin 的 opening focus on failure (lost fire) 即使他 tried his best。这和他之后在 sexual encounter 中的"taking his turn"形成 parallel——他被 Zane 和 Easton 抢先，他也要 claim his share。

**读者视角提示**：Calvin 作为 protector 和 provider 的角色——他的价值建立在 usefulness 上，火灭了是他的 failure。

---

> **原句 2:** The only thing that was keeping me outside in the rain was the noise coming from the raft. Fuck me. I told her to have fun. This was the plan. But still.

**中文理解**：唯一让我待在雨里的是从木筏里传来的声音。操。我告诉她去找乐子。这是计划。但还是。

**关键词**：noise coming from the raft / fuck me / this was the plan / But still

**为什么这样写**：Calvin 的 jealousy 和 desire 的冲突——他 rationalizes (it was the plan) 但 emotional response (fuck me) 暴露真实感受。他在给自己找借口留下/进去。

**读者视角提示**：这是言情小说中"planned seduction"的 male perspective——original plan 是让 Zane 和 Easton 和 Haley 发生性行为，但 Calvin 作为 creator/leader 反而被 excluded。

---

> **原句 3:** I grab her around her waist because the raft is shaking and I don't want her to tip over. Or at least, that's the story I'm going to go with, because it can't be the softness of her skin or the way she smells of coconut and rainwater.

**中文理解**：我抓住她的腰因为木筏在晃，我不想让她摔倒。至少，这是我要用的借口，因为原因不可能是她皮肤柔软或她闻起来像椰子和雨水。

**关键词**：grab her around her waist / raft is shaking / that's the story I'm going to go with / coconut and rainwater

**为什么这样写**：Calvin 的 self-deception 是 comic relief——他找借口 physical contact 但明确知道真正原因。他的 rationalization 展示他对自己 desire 的防御。

**读者视角提示**：这是 male gaze 的反转——Calvin 通常是 in control 但在这里他在给自己找理由去 touch her。

---

> **原句 4:** "We're going to die, we might as well have some fun before we go."

**中文理解**：「我们反正要死了，不如在死前找点乐子。」

**关键词**：we're going to die / might as well have some fun / before we go

**为什么这样写**：这是 existential hedonism 的陈述——Calvin 用"dying"as justification for sexual abandon。这个 line 也揭示他对 survival 的 resignation (即使他们有 rescue hope 他仍然用"we're going to die"作为 sexual motivation)。

**读者视角提示**：这是言情小说常见的"we might die tomorrow"trope——作为无保护性行为的借口。

---

> **原句 5:** Easton screams. Zane isn't far behind. We're one mass of arms and legs shaking.

**中文理解**：Easton 尖叫。Zane 紧随其后。我们是一个由手臂和腿组成的身体，一起颤抖。

**关键词**：Easton screams / Zane isn't far behind / one mass of arms and legs / shaking

**为什么这样写**：这是四人共同高潮的 imagery——physical unity 是 sexual satisfaction 的 metaphor。"One mass"强调他们不再是四个独立个体而是一个整体。

**读者视角提示**：这是言情小说中"group orgasm"的高潮场景描写。

---

> **原句 6:** "Where's the flare gun?" Easton yells. "Where's the flare gun? There's a boat. There's a fucking boat. Get out here."

**中文理解**：「信号枪在哪？」Easton 喊道。「信号枪在哪？有船。有一艘该死的船。出来。」

**关键词**：flare gun / There's a boat / Get out here

**为什么这样写**：这是 chapter 的 cliffhanger——从 sexual afterglow 立即切换到 rescue emergency。Easton 的 urgency 和其他人的怀疑形成对比。

**读者视角提示**：这是第二章看到船的情节重演——但这次 Easton 更确定，而且有四发信号弹了。
