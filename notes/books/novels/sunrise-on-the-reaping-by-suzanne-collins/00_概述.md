# 《Sunrise on the Reaping》概述

## 全书梗概

**一、生日与抽签日撞在同一个早晨。** 十二区的男孩 Haymitch Abernathy 在十六岁生日这天睡了个懒觉，因为生日恰好是抽签日；而这一届不是普通年份，是第二届大旬祭，名额翻倍。"The upside of being born on reaping day is that you can sleep late on your birthday. It's pretty much downhill from there."（ch01）

**他先去墓地，再去讨酒。** 矿工墓地里父亲埋在一块替全家代用的石灰岩下；Hattie Meeney 的私酒坊给了他生日酒；回程经过草甸，Lenore Dove 塞来一只蛇与鸟纹样的燧石打火器。"The second Quarter Quell. Twice as many kids. No point in worrying, I tell myself, there's nothing you can do about it."（ch01）

**二、广场上的枪声把这一年的规则提前改写了。** 四个名字被念出之后，Woodbine Chance 转身逃跑，司法大楼屋顶上一枪打爆了他的后脑。"Just when I'm thinking he might make it — all those Chance kids run like greased lightning — a shot rings out from the Justice Building rooftop, and the back of Woodbine's head explodes."（ch01）

**直播没有播出这一段。** 首都的五分钟延时留出了剪辑与重拍的空间；Drusilla 嫌那具尸体不能用，扑上去护住 Lenore Dove 的 Haymitch 于是补上了第二个男名额。"For the first time, I understand that when they show the reaping live, it isn’t really live. There must be a five-minute hold on the broadcast in case something like this happens."（ch02）

**那个穿紫色连体衣的男人。** Plutarch Heavensbee 用一场含泪告别换下了 Lenore Dove 的命，却在 McCoy 一家和他自己的母亲与弟弟身上反复索要眼泪，直到 Sid 真的哭出来——而他没有得到 Lenore Dove 的眼泪。"My face fills the screen over the stage. My feet begin to move. I see them cut to Lenore Dove, who has a hand pressed against her mouth. She isn’t crying, so Plutarch won’t get his tearful good-bye. Not from her and not from me. They will not use our tears for their entertainment."（ch02）

**三、火车往首都开，展示就开始了。** 母亲不做生日蛋糕，把力气留给元旦的堆叠蛋糕。"We don’t do birthday cakes in my house. Seems wrong on reaping day, and Ma thinks it’s unfair for her and Sid to have a cake if I don’t. Instead, she makes something nice for breakfast, like the corn bread and sauce, and saves all of her cake energy for New Year’s Day."（ch04）

**蛋糕、生日歌，和一台摄影机。** 首都把这些一起送上，他转身去看窗外，用它拒绝入镜；开幕游行上，四个孩子被打扮、被剃、被摆布成展品。"I feel like a wild animal on display, cuffed and mute, dragged in from the hills for their fun. All of us shrink a bit. Maysilee keeps her head up, but her cheeks burn with embarrassment."（ch05）

**他做了一件没有台词的事。** 抱着一路死去的 Louella 冲进总统府阳台下，他用一次鼓掌逼 Snow 不得不看那具小小的尸体。"Something steels inside me, and I think, You are on a high horse, mister. And someday someone will knock you off it straight into your grave. I dismount the chariot and lay Louella down, taking a step back so Snow can’t pretend he doesn’t see her broken little bird body. Then I gesture to him and begin to applaud, giving credit where credit is due."（ch06）

**四、这届游戏第一次有了联盟。** 训练场上，Plutarch 当众问出一个没人愿意回答的问题：既然绞刑、枪杀、饥饿与饥饿游戏每天都在眼前，为什么所有人还是照旧同意这套安排。"“No, I do. I see the hangings and the shootings and the starvation and the Hunger Games. I do,” Plutarch says. “And yet, I still don’t think the fear they inspire justifies this arrangement we’ve all entered into. Do you?” We stare at him. He’s not taunting or mocking us, he’s genuinely asking. “Why do you agree to it? Why do I? For that matter, why have people always agreed to it?” When we don’t respond, he shrugs. “Well, it’s something to think about.”"（ch08）

**十二岁的人给出了答案。** Ampert 用一道算术题把局面翻了过来——职业选手只占四分之一，剩下的人只要联合起来，就能反过来猎杀他们。"I like how even though he’s only twelve, he jumps right in. “It’s like this. A disproportionate amount of the time, the Careers win. But they’re only one quarter of the tributes. We’ve got three times their numbers. So the idea is, we get the rest of us together and, for a change, we hunt them down instead of letting them hunt us.”"（ch08）

**四十八个贡品在开场前就结成了空前规模的联盟。**

**五、总统的威胁。** 他被单独带进 Heavensbee 家族的宅邸，在藏书室与温室之后见到抱病呕吐的 Snow，并且喝光了本该端给总统的那罐奶。"This is probably the best chance I will ever have to fight back against Snow directly. Here’s to you, Louella. I tip open the eagle lid, down the milk, and wipe the moustache from my lip."（ch09）

**一条量身定做的死法。** Snow 认出他脖子上的燧石打火器，追问出 Lenore Dove 的全名，然后告诉他从今往后每件事都会被用来编排他的死。"“No, but I do. And I will orchestrate your death based on your behavior from here on out. You decide what you want Lenore Dove and your mother and that dear little brother of yours to see. You can die clean and fair, or we can open the Games with the slowest, most agonizing death ever to befall a tribute. And yes, you should be thanking me for giving you the option.”"（ch09）

**六、竞技场。** Beetee 把完整方案交给他：向北走，跟着回巢的变异兽找到通道，进入储罐区。"As the Peacekeepers redirect toward my stylist, Beetee plucks off a minuscule radish and speaks rapidly under his breath. “Head north. Ampert will do the same after he has the explosive. Do your best to locate a mutt portal by tracking returning mutts after an attack. After you and Ampert meet up, take one to access Sub-A, where the tank is located. We’ve replaced the black cord in Ampert’s token with fuse, the blasting cap’s hidden in the weave.”"（ch13）

**一座好得不像话的园子。** 竞技场的太阳与外界同步，草地平整到令人怀疑，头顶的彩鸟与脚下的鲜花、与贡品背上的服装配套。"A scowl contracts my face as suspicion sets in. It’s just too attractive to be good. The smooth green meadow stretching for miles in either direction. The array of colorful songbirds overhead that match the tufts of cheerful flowers underfoot that match the outfits on the tributes’ backs. Sky so blue it hurts your eyes, clouds so fluffy you want to bounce on them. And the smell! Like they bottled the best day of spring and uncorked it just for us."（ch15）

**炸开地下水箱之后。** 洪水灌满 Sub-A；等他爬上地面，守在梯顶替他扶住土堤的 Ampert 已经被一群只认他气味的变异松鼠啃成一具白骨。"A small white skeleton, stripped clean to the bone. No flesh or clothing remains, only an ax at its right side, my knife at its left. My lips move, but no sound comes out."（ch18）

**七、死亡一名接一名。** Panache 的剑落地时，Maysilee Donner 从树后现身，吹镖还挂在脖子上——她救了他，两人就此结盟。"Panache’s sword thuds to the earth and he collapses, senseless. I whip around to see Maysilee emerge from behind a tree. A blowgun balances delicately in her fingers, the mouthpiece attached to a braided vine around her neck. Her latest necklace. Emotionless, she watches Panache expire."（ch20）

**也是在这具尸体旁边。** 他第一次意识到自己已经杀了两个人。"I take in the dead bodies around us, for the first time recognizing that I’ve killed someone. Two someones. Brutally. It was self-defense, no question, but I know I can never go back to five minutes ago. Having taken their lives . . . in that way . . . it’s undoable. I pick up my weapons. “Let’s get out of here.”"（ch20）

**一群被专门设定追踪她的粉色水鸟。** 鸟喙扎进她的喉咙，他在她最后的时刻握住了她的手。"A bird swoops down at a sharp angle, driving its beak through her throat. As it withdraws, I decapitate it, slicing through the skinny neck. I realize Maysilee’s beyond recovery when the flock clears out. Falling to my knees beside her, I reach for her sound hand, which grasps mine like a vise. Her wounded one curls up and rests in her nest of necklaces, which lays in a pool of blood. Through the rasping of her breath, she attempts to speak, but the last mutt silenced her voice with its wicked beak. Mine seems silenced as well, as no words of comfort or hope or apology make it out. I just stare into those burning blue eyes, letting her know she’s not dying alone. She’s with family. She’s with me."（ch23）

**他还剩最后一张海报要画。** Wellie 被 Silka 斩首之后，他用一把刀反手刺瞎 Silka 的眼、再让她自己掷出的斧头落回她头上；最后他把自己当作最后一张海报，连同向日葵炸弹一起掷向力场。

**八、胜利之后才是真正的判决。** 他没死。首都把他修好、关起来，又送他去看一部删掉自己全部反抗痕迹的纪录片，最后在加冕礼上给他戴上那顶沉重而算计的冠冕。"Some victors bow, some kneel, but I just stand there trying to read his expression as he approaches and places the crown on my head. Heavy. Entrapping. “I guess Snow lands on top,” I say under the applause. Utterly guilty on all possible counts, I await his sentence."（ch25）

**回十二区的那条路上。** 远远的亮光他先当成了日出，走近才知道是家门口的火——母亲和 Sid 死在里面。"I take the glow up ahead for the sunrise, until I realize it’s too local, too bright. A whiff of smoke drifts through the heavy, humid air. Fire. But not coal fire. I break into the closest thing I can manage to a run. Withered muscles, screaming scars, swollen feet reduce my efforts to a wild hobble. Maybe I am wrong. Any house can catch fire. What with rusted stoves and unwatched hobs. Maybe it’s not mine."（ch26）

**几周之后，在草甸上。** 他从袋子里拈起一颗软糖喂进 Lenore Dove 嘴里，低头才发现袋中剩下的糖全是血红。"“But . . .” I look down at the bag. It’s a normal bag, with the Donners’ label. Then I notice the gumdrops. Not a variety of colors. Not a rainbow. They’re all a deep bloodred. I remember Snow’s rose, his final words to me, and the pieces fall into place."（ch26）

**他终于承认自己没有计划、没有希望，却不能说没有未来。** 每年生日，他都会得到一对新的贡品。"I make no plans, have no hopes, keep no company, speak to no human being except old Bascom Pie when my nepenthe runs low. But I can’t say I have no future, because I know that every year for my birthday, I will get a new pair of tributes, one girl and one boy, to mentor to their deaths. Another sunrise on the reaping."（ch27）

## 三个主题

**一、服从不是天生的，而是被一次次默许出来的。** Plutarch 在训练场上问的那个问题贯穿全书：为什么所有人还是照旧走上广场。"“No, I do. I see the hangings and the shootings and the starvation and the Hunger Games. I do,” Plutarch says. “And yet, I still don’t think the fear they inspire justifies this arrangement we’ve all entered into. Do you?” We stare at him. He’s not taunting or mocking us, he’s genuinely asking. “Why do you agree to it? Why do I? For that matter, why have people always agreed to it?” When we don’t respond, he shrugs. “Well, it’s something to think about.”"（ch08）

**而他的回答不是豪言。** 是一个动作：他不肯再默认顺从。"Plutarch grasps my shoulders and gives me a little shake. “No more implicit submission for you, Haymitch Abernathy. Blow that water tank sky high. The entire country needs you to.”"（ch14）

**结盟、炸水箱、把每一次死亡都变成首都不得不播出的画面。** 这些都是同一句话在不同阶段的形态；而游戏最终没有停下，说明默许一旦松开，代价并不会更小。

**二、一切都在镜头里，包括眼泪。** 抽签不是直播，五分钟的延时留出了剪辑与重拍的空间。"For the first time, I understand that when they show the reaping live, it isn’t really live. There must be a five-minute hold on the broadcast in case something like this happens."（ch02）

**有人拒绝表演。** McCoy 一家用一脸木然把自己变成了第三种答案。"If anything, the McCoys are more stony-faced than before. It’s not confusion; it’s a blanket refusal to put on a show for the Capitol."（ch02）

**在竞技场里，他同样在扮演。** 扮演邋遢、扮演自私、扮演友善，好让观众相信一个连贯的角色。"Cocky. Out for myself. Sarcastic. Nice to the other Newcomers. I’m channeling all these things so I can present a consistent character to the audience, but when Ampert arrives, he throws his arms around me and I just hug him back and say, “Hey, buddy.”"（ch17）

**唯一没有被播出的告别。** 是 Lenore Dove 在山脊上对着风嚎哭的那一场。"That’s when I see Lenore Dove. She’s up on a ridge, her red dress plastered to her body, one hand clutching the bag of gumdrops. As the train passes, she tilts her head back and wails her loss and rage into the wind. And even though it guts me, even though I smash my fists into the glass until they bruise, I’m grateful for her final gift. That she’s denied Plutarch the chance to broadcast our farewell."（ch02）

**三、纪念是唯一带得走的东西。** Maysilee 死后他取下她的挂坠与吹箭筒，把「姐妹」这个词重新定义了一遍。"Good-bye, Maysilee Donner, who I loathed, then grudgingly respected, then loved. Not as a sweetheart or even a friend. A sister, I’d said. But what is that exactly? I think about our journey — everything from sniping with her in those early days after the reaping to battling those pink birds. I guess that’s my answer. A sister is someone you fight with and fight for. Tooth and nail."（ch23）

**战后他把那本纪念册推开了很久。** 直到要写 Burdock 那一页，所有名字才一起涌出来。"I didn’t want to have anything to do with their memorial book after the war. What use? What point? To relive all the loss. But when Burdock’s page came up, I had to mention him showing me the grave. And I felt compelled to tell them about Maysilee Donner, former owner of the mockingjay pin. And how Sid loved the stars. Before I knew it, they all came tumbling out: family, tributes, friends, comrades in arms, everybody, even my love. I finally told our story."（ch28）

**全书的落点因此不是复仇，也不是胜利。** 而是一句判断——能从他身上拿走的东西，本来就不值得留下。"I know one thing, though: The Capitol can never take Lenore Dove from me again. They never really did in the first place. Nothing you can take from me was ever worth keeping, and she is the most precious thing I’ve ever known."（ch28）

## 人物弧光

**Haymitch Abernathy——**

- 起点："The upside of being born on reaping day is that you can sleep late on your birthday. It's pretty much downhill from there."（ch01）
- 转向："Plutarch grasps my shoulders and gives me a little shake. “No more implicit submission for you, Haymitch Abernathy. Blow that water tank sky high. The entire country needs you to.”"（ch14）
- 最低点："I am living proof that the Capitol always wins. I tried to keep that sun from rising on another reaping day, I tried to change things, and now everybody’s dead. You don’t want me."（ch27）
- 落点："I know one thing, though: The Capitol can never take Lenore Dove from me again. They never really did in the first place. Nothing you can take from me was ever worth keeping, and she is the most precious thing I’ve ever known."（ch28）
- 他的成长不是变强，而是从「没有人可指望」变成「宁可被憎恨也不要被忽视」，最后又走回彻底的自我封闭；中间那段时间，他替整个十二区挡住了镜头。

**Maysilee Donner——**

- 起点："Maysilee’s lips twitch. “Well, you can’t wear it like that. It’s not the least bit ornamental. You look like a weasel caught in chicken wire.”"（ch08）
- 转向："“One of us has to be the worst victor in history. Tear up their scripts, tear down their celebrations, set fire to the Victor’s Village. Refuse to play their game.”"（ch21）
- 落点："Good-bye, Maysilee Donner, who I loathed, then grudgingly respected, then loved. Not as a sweetheart or even a friend. A sister, I’d said. But what is that exactly? I think about our journey — everything from sniping with her in those early days after the reaping to battling those pink birds. I guess that’s my answer. A sister is someone you fight with and fight for. Tooth and nail."（ch23）
- 她从头到尾都在得罪人，包括对她最亲近的人；她的政治判断比 Haymitch 更早成熟，而她没能活到用它。

**Lenore Dove——**

- 起点："Less interested in pretty melodies, more in dangerous words. The kind that lead to rebel acts. The kind that got her arrested twice."（ch01）
- 转向："“We will be together always,” I say with conviction. “I don’t know how, and I don’t know where, I don’t know anything, but I feel that in my heart. You and me, we will find each other, as many times as it takes.”"（ch14）
- 落点："When I tell her that, she always says, “I love you like all-fire.”"（ch28）
- 她是全书唯一一个从不需要首都给意义的人：她唱歌、传话、被抓、被威胁，最后死在爱人手里的一颗软糖上，却始终是那个说出「像烈火一样爱你」的人。

**Ampert——**

- 起点："I like how even though he’s only twelve, he jumps right in. “It’s like this. A disproportionate amount of the time, the Careers win. But they’re only one quarter of the tributes. We’ve got three times their numbers. So the idea is, we get the rest of us together and, for a change, we hunt them down instead of letting them hunt us.”"（ch08）
- 落点："A small white skeleton, stripped clean to the bone. No flesh or clothing remains, only an ax at its right side, my knife at its left. My lips move, but no sound comes out."（ch18）
- 他十二岁就把联盟的算术算清楚了，也第一个兑现了它；留下的只有一具被变异松鼠啃净的白骨和一把斧头。

**Plutarch Heavensbee——**

- 起点："There’s a long pause, then Plutarch says, “You may find Drusilla ridiculous, but be smart. You four don’t have your own district mentor. Your stylist’s job begins and ends with your appearance. It might not be fair, but Drusilla may be the best advocate you have in the Capitol. Think about it before you burn that bridge entirely.” He leaves, quietly closing the door behind him."（ch04）
- 转向："Plutarch grasps my shoulders and gives me a little shake. “No more implicit submission for you, Haymitch Abernathy. Blow that water tank sky high. The entire country needs you to.”"（ch14）
- 落点："I am a man on a mission. For weeks, I wander through the trees, circle the lake, examine the soil under the apple trees, looking for any sign of her."（ch27）
- 他看人比谁都准，救人也比谁都算计；他给过 Haymitch 一个可以拒绝的邀请，也从此背着那条被拒绝的答案。
