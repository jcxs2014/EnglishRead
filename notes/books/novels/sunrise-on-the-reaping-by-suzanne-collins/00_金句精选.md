# 《Sunrise on the Reaping》金句精选

① "The upside of being born on reaping day is that you can sleep late on your birthday. It's pretty much downhill from there."（ch01）

**中文**：生在抽签日唯一的好处，是生日那天能睡个懒觉。从那儿往后基本全是下坡路。
**上下文**：ch01 开篇，十六岁生日的早晨，他还不知道自己会在这一天被抽中。
**为什么重要**：全书用一句自嘲把生日与死亡绑在一起，而这本书的名字正是「抽签日的日出」。
**呼应关系**：
- 与 "I make no plans, have no hopes, keep no company, speak to no human being except old Bascom Pie when my nepenthe runs low. But I can’t say I have no future, because I know that every year for my birthday, I will get a new pair of tributes, one girl and one boy, to mentor to their deaths. Another sunrise on the reaping."（ch27）
- 「每年我都会得到一对新的贡品」构成首尾；"Give me one good reason why it should keep happening just because it's your birthday."（ch01）
那句「凭什么因为是你生日」是它的展开。

② "Give me one good reason why it should keep happening just because it's your birthday."（ch01）

**中文**：给我一个像样的理由，凭什么就因为是你的生日，这件事就该一直发生下去。
**上下文**：ch01 草甸上，Lenore Dove 对他说「至少你今天该得到点什么」，他这样反问。
**为什么重要**：这是全书反抗意识最早的一次成形，而且它出自一个完全私人的场合。
**呼应关系**：
- "“No, I do. I see the hangings and the shootings and the starvation and the Hunger Games. I do,” Plutarch says. “And yet, I still don’t think the fear they inspire justifies this arrangement we’ve all entered into. Do you?” We stare at him. He’s not taunting or mocking us, he’s genuinely asking. “Why do you agree to it? Why do I? For that matter, why have people always agreed to it?” When we don’t respond, he shrugs. “Well, it’s something to think about.”"（ch08）
- Plutarch 在训练场问的是同一件事的公共版本；"Full of surprises. Full of secrets, even from me. But Maysilee had put it together. Orange paint on her fingernails. This is Lenore Dove’s work. Her sign. Her message to me now. Her reminder that I must prevent another sunrise on the reaping."（ch27）
那句喷漆标语是它的回音。

③ "Less interested in pretty melodies, more in dangerous words. The kind that lead to rebel acts. The kind that got her arrested twice."（ch01）

**中文**：她对漂亮旋律没什么兴趣，更爱危险的词句——那种会引出反抗行动、也让她被捕过两次的词句。
**上下文**：ch01 介绍 Lenore Dove 的音乐与她被和平卫队抓走的往事。
**为什么重要**：全书把「歌」定义成武器，也预告了这句话最后会以她之死兑现。
**呼应关系**：
- "“No. Just hauled me in. Less about what I played, more about how it drew people. Everybody’s real upset this year, so many kids. They needed a place to be together, to raise their voices. Sometimes the hurt’s too bad to bear alone.”"（ch14）
- 「大家需要一个地方聚在一起、把声音抬高」是它的实践；"“But . . .” I look down at the bag. It’s a normal bag, with the Donners’ label. Then I notice the gumdrops. Not a variety of colors. Not a rainbow. They’re all a deep bloodred. I remember Snow’s rose, his final words to me, and the pieces fall into place."（ch26）
那颗血红的软糖是它的代价。

④ "Just when I'm thinking he might make it — all those Chance kids run like greased lightning — a shot rings out from the Justice Building rooftop, and the back of Woodbine's head explodes."（ch01）

**中文**：就在我以为他能跑掉的时候——Chance 家的孩子跑得像抹了油的闪电——司法大楼屋顶响起一枪，Woodbine 的后脑炸开了。
**上下文**：ch01 抽签仪式结尾，转身逃跑的那个贡品被司法大楼屋顶的枪当场击毙。
**为什么重要**：全书第一场死亡发生在故事开始之前，它把「规则」的真面目一次亮完。
**呼应关系**：
- "For the first time, I understand that when they show the reaping live, it isn’t really live. There must be a five-minute hold on the broadcast in case something like this happens."（ch02）
- 的五分钟延时正是为了掩盖这一幕；"The two dozen waterbirds remind me of ones I’ve seen at the lake. Long-legged. Beaks like sword blades — thin, narrow, and deadly. Not cool blue gray, not paper white, but the color of the bubblegum sold at the Donners’ sweetshop. They dive again and again at Maysilee, who’s kneeling on the ground, trying to use a tarp as protection while she vehemently slices at them with her dagger. A couple of dead birds lie on the ground, but they have taken their toll. Blood blossoms from her cheek, her chest, the palm of her hand. Like Ampert’s squirrels, they have no interest in me. Programmed to target Maysilee in a very personal punishment. I hack away at the mutts with my ax, piling up a collection of rosy wings and legs like cattail stems, but they badly outnumber us."（ch23）
那些被程序点名攻击的水鸟是同一套逻辑的竞技场版本。

⑤ "For the first time, I understand that when they show the reaping live, it isn’t really live. There must be a five-minute hold on the broadcast in case something like this happens."（ch02）

**中文**：我这才第一次明白，他们所谓的「直播抽签」根本不是直播。广电里必定有五分钟的延迟，以防这类事情发生。
**上下文**：ch02 枪响之后，屏幕转黑又恢复，他当场算出了这套机制的运行方式。
**为什么重要**：一句话把「真实」从首都的叙事里抽走，全书此后所有镜头都建立在这条认识上。
**呼应关系**：
- "If anything, the McCoys are more stony-faced than before. It’s not confusion; it’s a blanket refusal to put on a show for the Capitol."（ch02）
- McCoys 拒绝表演是它对个体的反作用；"Their lack of discernment transforms the recap, validating it as truth. I hope those in the districts can still see it as the piece of propaganda it is, but no telling what they’ve been fed."（ch25）
那部删改过的纪录片是它的成品。

⑥ "If anything, the McCoys are more stony-faced than before. It’s not confusion; it’s a blanket refusal to put on a show for the Capitol."（ch02）

**中文**：要说有什么不同，McCoy 一家比之前更面无表情了。那不是困惑，而是一种彻底的拒绝——拒绝为首都表演。
**上下文**：ch02 直播里家属的悲痛镜头被反复重拍——McCoys 一家始终木着脸不肯表演，Plutarch 只好转而向 Haymitch 的母亲与弟弟一次次索要更激烈的反应。
**为什么重要**：全书第一次写出「不表演」也是一种反抗，而且它发生在最没有权力的人身上。
**呼应关系**：
- "My face fills the screen over the stage. My feet begin to move. I see them cut to Lenore Dove, who has a hand pressed against her mouth. She isn’t crying, so Plutarch won’t get his tearful good-bye. Not from her and not from me. They will not use our tears for their entertainment."（ch02）
- 「他们别想用我们的眼泪娱乐自己」是它的宣言；"“One of us has to be the worst victor in history. Tear up their scripts, tear down their celebrations, set fire to the Victor’s Village. Refuse to play their game.”"（ch21）
Maysilee 的「撕掉他们的剧本」是它的竞技场版本。

⑦ "That’s when I see Lenore Dove. She’s up on a ridge, her red dress plastered to her body, one hand clutching the bag of gumdrops. As the train passes, she tilts her head back and wails her loss and rage into the wind. And even though it guts me, even though I smash my fists into the glass until they bruise, I’m grateful for her final gift. That she’s denied Plutarch the chance to broadcast our farewell."（ch02）

**中文**：就在那时我看见了 Lenore Dove。她站在山脊上，红裙贴在身上，一只手攥着那袋软糖。火车经过时，她仰起头，把自己的失去与愤怒嚎进风里。而尽管这一幕把我剖开，尽管我把拳头砸向车窗直到淤青，我却感激她这最后一份礼物——她让 Plutarch 没能播出我们的告别。
**上下文**：ch02 结尾，他被押上火车离开十二区。
**为什么重要**：全书唯一一场没有被拍下来的告别，正因如此才属于他们自己。
**呼应关系**：
- "“We will be together always,” I say with conviction. “I don’t know how, and I don’t know where, I don’t know anything, but I feel that in my heart. You and me, we will find each other, as many times as it takes.”"（ch14）
- 「我们会找到彼此，不管要多少次」是它的续写；"When Lenore Dove comes to me now, she’s not angry or dying, so I think she’s forgiven me. She’s grown older with me, her face etched with fine lines, her hair touched with gray."（ch28）
她老去的幻象是它的兑现。

⑧ "I will never see Lenore Dove again. Never hear her laugh coming from high above me in the branches. Never feel the warmth of her in my arms as we lay on a bed of pine needles, my lips pressed into the hollow of her neck."（ch03）

**中文**：我再也见不到 Lenore Dove 了。再也听不到她在我头顶枝叶间传来的笑声。再也不能在松针铺成的床上把她搂在怀里，感受她的温度，把嘴唇贴进她颈窝。
**上下文**：ch03 车厢里，他第一次把「再也」说成一句一句。
**为什么重要**：全书的哀悼写作以排比起手，而每一次重复都在删掉一样具体的东西。
**呼应关系**："Never see her face brighten at a bag of gumdrops or a full moon or the sound of me whispering, “I love you like all-fire.”"（ch03）「再也看不到她因为一袋软糖、一轮满月而亮起脸」是它的延续。

⑨ "We don’t do birthday cakes in my house. Seems wrong on reaping day, and Ma thinks it’s unfair for her and Sid to have a cake if I don’t. Instead, she makes something nice for breakfast, like the corn bread and sauce, and saves all of her cake energy for New Year’s Day."（ch04）

**中文**：我们家不过生日蛋糕。在抽签日这天显得不合适，而且妈觉得，既然我没有蛋糕，她和 Sid 有也不公平。所以她改做一顿像样的早餐，比如玉米面包配酱汁，把所有的蛋糕力气都留给元旦。
**上下文**：ch04 首都送上生日蛋糕与生日歌，他想起自己家是怎么过生日的。
**为什么重要**：一分钟就写完了一个家庭在匮乏中维持体面的方式，也解释了后来他为什么要拒绝那辆蛋糕车。
**呼应关系**：
- "So the cake in front of me, with its fancy frosted flowers, is all wrong. The candles smack of the Capitol. And the song Tibby leads the Peacekeepers in, while common in 12, is never sung in my house because it would be as unsuitable as a birthday cake."（ch04）
- 「眼前这个蛋糕完全是错的」是它的结论；"Inside we’re told to strip, which is easy from the waist down but undoable above the belt with our cuffed hands. Peacekeepers come around and cut away our shirts with knives. If anyone objects, they laugh and say it’s all the same to the incinerator. It hurts watching them slice through Ma’s careful stitches. I remember her painstakingly laying out those handkerchiefs to make every inch of material count. Now it sits in shreds at my feet."（ch05）
母亲缝的手帕被剪碎是它的下场。

⑩ "I feel like a wild animal on display, cuffed and mute, dragged in from the hills for their fun. All of us shrink a bit. Maysilee keeps her head up, but her cheeks burn with embarrassment."（ch05）

**中文**：我觉得自己像一头被展示的野兽，戴着手铐、说不出话，被人从山里拖出来供他们取乐。我们全都缩了一点。Maysilee 抬着头，但她的脸因羞耻而发烫。
**上下文**：ch05 首都车站与开幕式之间，四个贡品被剃、被洗、被摆布。
**为什么重要**：它把「贡品」这个词从荣誉还原成展品，且用的是被展者自己的视角。
**呼应关系**："“We collect all of them from the tributes, dead or alive,” says the woman. “They’re reusable. Of course, this year we needed twenty-four extra.”"（ch15）「这些牌子都从贡品身上收集，死人或活人都收」是同一物化逻辑的实物形态。

⑪ "Something steels inside me, and I think, You are on a high horse, mister. And someday someone will knock you off it straight into your grave. I dismount the chariot and lay Louella down, taking a step back so Snow can’t pretend he doesn’t see her broken little bird body. Then I gesture to him and begin to applaud, giving credit where credit is due."（ch06）

**中文**：有什么东西在我心里硬了起来，我想：你骑在高头大马上，先生。总有一天会有人把你从上面直接打进坟墓。我下了战车，把 Louella 放下，往后退一步，让 Snow 没法假装看不见她那具小小的破碎鸟身。然后我朝他比了个手势，开始鼓掌——功劳归功于人。
**上下文**：ch06 开幕式车祸之后，他驾着马车停在总统府阳台下，后退一步、抬手鼓掌，逼已经站在那里的 Snow 无法假装看不见 Louella 的尸体。
**为什么重要**：全书第一个真正的政治动作，它没有台词，只有一次鼓掌。
**呼应关系**："Some victors bow, some kneel, but I just stand there trying to read his expression as he approaches and places the crown on my head. Heavy. Entrapping. “I guess Snow lands on top,” I say under the applause. Utterly guilty on all possible counts, I await his sentence."（ch25）Snow 在颁奖礼上把王冠按到他头上，是这句话的延迟回答。

⑫ "I don’t cry much in general. Only when people die, and then I cry hard and fast and ugly, which is what I do now. Because Louella is dead and I was supposed to look out for her and I didn’t."（ch07）

**中文**：我一般不哭。只在有人死的时候哭，而那时候我哭得又狠又快又难看，就像现在这样。因为 Louella 死了，而本该照看她的人是我，我没做到。
**上下文**：ch07 首都为贡品租下的临时公寓里，他终于把那道闸口放下来。
**为什么重要**：全书写这个角色只承认过一次软弱，而他把这次软弱直接写成了失职。
**呼应关系**：
- "She follows Wyatt to the van, leaving me to ponder the possibility that I’ve out-meaned Maysilee. Not something to be proud of. But neither is factoring Louella’s death into our odds. Her body’s not even cold, and he’s reduced her to a number. But she was not a number, she was a little girl I met on the day she was born when Mr. McCoy, his face alight with joy, held her up at the window for all us kids to see. A terrible, dark grief begins to well up inside me, threatening to drown me, but I force it back down. Swallow the sadness, clamp a lid on it, dam it up. They will not use my tears for their entertainment."（ch06）
- 「把悲伤吞下去、盖上盖子、筑坝拦住」是它的前置；"I shift positions, but something seems unfinished. Like I never really thanked her for today. No, I did. With the juice. But that doesn’t begin to cover it. What do you say to the meanest girl in town who’s become your friend? No, more than a friend, really. A Newcomer. Being tributes and not killing each other . . . looking out for each other with no questions asked . . . that’s family, I guess."（ch20）
「我大概还没真正谢过她」是它的延长。

⑬ "“No, I do. I see the hangings and the shootings and the starvation and the Hunger Games. I do,” Plutarch says. “And yet, I still don’t think the fear they inspire justifies this arrangement we’ve all entered into. Do you?” We stare at him. He’s not taunting or mocking us, he’s genuinely asking. “Why do you agree to it? Why do I? For that matter, why have people always agreed to it?” When we don’t respond, he shrugs. “Well, it’s something to think about.”"（ch08）

**中文**：不，我看见了。绞刑、枪杀、饥饿和饥饿游戏，我都看见了。可是，我依然不认为他们靠这些激起的恐惧，能证明我们所有人都默认的这套安排是正当的。你呢？……你为什么同意它？我为什么？说到底，人们为什么一直同意它？
**上下文**：ch08 训练场上，Plutarch 当着一群贡品的面问出这个问题。
**为什么重要**：全书的政治核心问题被一次性说完，而它问的不是首都，是所有人。
**呼应关系**：
- "Plutarch grasps my shoulders and gives me a little shake. “No more implicit submission for you, Haymitch Abernathy. Blow that water tank sky high. The entire country needs you to.”"（ch14）
- 「你别再默认顺从了」是它对单个个体的判决；"The full force of my failure hits me. Who do I think I am? Why did I think I could change anything? That I could take on the Capitol, with all its might, and bring the Hunger Games to a standstill? Me, a sixteen-year-old kid from the trashiest district in Panem with little schooling and no outstanding skills. I’ve got nothing but a big mouth and an inflated sense of my own self-importance. All foam, no beer, that’s me. Near beer."（ch19）
「我凭什么以为我能改变什么」是它最黑暗的回声。

⑭ "“I am. And no doubt you’re wondering why I’m here, Haymitch.” Beetee removes his glasses and polishes them on his shirt. “It’s because I’m being punished for coming up with a plan to sabotage the Capitol’s communication system. I’m too valuable to kill, but my son is disposable.”"（ch08）

**中文**：我是。你八成在想我为什么会在这儿，Haymitch。因为我正在为提出过一套破坏首都通讯系统的方案受罚。我太有价值，杀不得，但我的儿子是可以牺牲的。
**上下文**：ch08 训练场的导师展位旁，Beetee 第一次向他说出自己参赛的原因。
**为什么重要**：全书把「技术人才」与「人质」的关系写得最冷的一句，也解释了他后来为什么愿意交出全部方案。
**呼应关系**：
- "As the Peacekeepers redirect toward my stylist, Beetee plucks off a minuscule radish and speaks rapidly under his breath. “Head north. Ampert will do the same after he has the explosive. Do your best to locate a mutt portal by tracking returning mutts after an attack. After you and Ampert meet up, take one to access Sub-A, where the tank is located. We’ve replaced the black cord in Ampert’s token with fuse, the blasting cap’s hidden in the weave.”"（ch13）
- 蔬菜拼盘边交出的那套完整计划是它的兑现；"A small white skeleton, stripped clean to the bone. No flesh or clothing remains, only an ax at its right side, my knife at its left. My lips move, but no sound comes out."（ch18）
梯顶那具白骨是它的代价。

⑮ "“No, but I do. And I will orchestrate your death based on your behavior from here on out. You decide what you want Lenore Dove and your mother and that dear little brother of yours to see. You can die clean and fair, or we can open the Games with the slowest, most agonizing death ever to befall a tribute. And yes, you should be thanking me for giving you the option.”"（ch09）

**中文**：不，但我确实会。从今往后，我会根据你的表现来编排你的死法。由你决定，你希望 Lenore Dove、你母亲和你那个亲爱的小弟弟看到什么。你可以死得干净、公平，或者我们可以用史上最缓慢、最痛苦的方式开幕本届游戏。是的，你该谢谢我给了你选择。
**上下文**：ch09 Heavensbee 家宅邸的藏书室里，他刚刚喝光了本该端给总统的那罐奶。
**为什么重要**：全书最完整的一次权力宣言，它的可怕之处在于它把暴行包装成一个选项。
**呼应关系**："Burdock’s hand clamps over my mouth. “It’s too late, Haymitch. We tried. It’s too late.”"（ch26）Burdock 捂住他的嘴说「太迟了」是这句话的最终执行。

⑯ "As the Peacekeepers redirect toward my stylist, Beetee plucks off a minuscule radish and speaks rapidly under his breath. “Head north. Ampert will do the same after he has the explosive. Do your best to locate a mutt portal by tracking returning mutts after an attack. After you and Ampert meet up, take one to access Sub-A, where the tank is located. We’ve replaced the black cord in Ampert’s token with fuse, the blasting cap’s hidden in the weave.”"（ch13）

**中文**：往北走。Ampert 拿到炸药之后也会去。尽你所能，跟着攻击之后回巢的变异兽找到通道。你和 Ampert 会合之后，带一只进去进 Sub-A，水箱就在那里。我们已经把 Ampert 信物里的黑绳换成了导火索，雷管藏在编织纹里。
**上下文**：ch13 采访前，Beetee 一边挑蔬菜一边把完整方案说完。
**为什么重要**：从这一句起，整届游戏在他眼里变成了一张施工图。
**呼应关系**：
- "This must be the side of the water tank, and it’s indeed massive, running from the concrete floor twenty feet up to the ceiling. The droplets stretch as far as I can see in either direction. What could they possibly need this much water for? Do they intend to turn the entire arena into a lake?"（ch18）
- 他终于摸到水箱侧壁是它的抵达；"I have failed. The arena has been damaged, but not incapacitated. The Games continue."（ch19）
「竞技场受损，但未瘫痪」是它的结果。

⑰ "“We will be together always,” I say with conviction. “I don’t know how, and I don’t know where, I don’t know anything, but I feel that in my heart. You and me, we will find each other, as many times as it takes.”"（ch14）

**中文**：我们会永远在一起。我不知道怎么做到，不知道在哪，我什么都不知道，但我心里就是这么觉得。你和我，我们会找到彼此，不管要多少次。
**上下文**：ch14 竞技场开放前的最后通话，两个人都在抢着认错。
**为什么重要**：全书唯一的誓言，说完之后再也没有第二次通话。
**呼应关系**：
- "Oh, Lenore Dove. Oh, love of my life. I am with you before, now, and always. And I will find you. I will find you."（ch24）
