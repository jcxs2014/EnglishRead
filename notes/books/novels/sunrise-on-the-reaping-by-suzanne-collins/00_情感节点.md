# 《Sunrise on the Reaping》情感节点

## 节点一：十六岁生日的早晨（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书的情感起点是一个反讽：他生在抽签日，所以生日那天能睡懒觉。这一天从矿工墓地开始，经过 Hattie 的私酒坊，停在草甸上与 Lenore Dove 的那场争执——她说至少今天该得到点什么，他反问凭什么因为是生日这件事就该一直发生。这句话在当时只是一次少年人的倔强，读完全书才知道它是全书政治意识的第一次成形。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The upside of being born on reaping day is that you can sleep late on your birthday. It's pretty much downhill from there."（ch01）

> ② "Give me one good reason why it should keep happening just because it's your birthday."（ch01）

> ③ "Less interested in pretty melodies, more in dangerous words. The kind that lead to rebel acts. The kind that got her arrested twice."（ch01）

## 节点二：广场上的那一枪与山脊上的嚎哭（ch02）

**叙事概括：** Woodbine 转身逃跑，司法大楼屋顶一枪打爆他的后脑，而这条命在直播里根本没出现过——屏幕转黑又恢复，他当场算出了五分钟延时。Drusilla 嫌尸体不能用，于是扑上去护住 Lenore Dove 的他补上了第二个名额；McCoy 一家用一脸木然拒绝为首都表演。这一章以全片唯一没被播出的告别收尾：Lenore Dove 站在山脊上，把失去与愤怒嚎进风里。

**关键引语：**

> ① "For the first time, I understand that when they show the reaping live, it isn’t really live. There must be a five-minute hold on the broadcast in case something like this happens."（ch02）

> ② "If anything, the McCoys are more stony-faced than before. It’s not confusion; it’s a blanket refusal to put on a show for the Capitol."（ch02）

> ③ "That’s when I see Lenore Dove. She’s up on a ridge, her red dress plastered to her body, one hand clutching the bag of gumdrops. As the train passes, she tilts her head back and wails her loss and rage into the wind. And even though it guts me, even though I smash my fists into the glass until they bruise, I’m grateful for her final gift. That she’s denied Plutarch the chance to broadcast our farewell."（ch02）

## 节点三：火车开走之后，家变成了过去式（ch03-ch04）

**叙事概括：** 哀悼在这一章变成排比，一句一句删掉具体的东西——笑声、松针床上的温度、她为一袋软糖亮起的脸。而母亲在车外所做的事，是把家里唯一能给的体面守住：不做生日蛋糕，把力气留给元旦；首都送来的蛋糕和生日歌是错的，因为它不属于任何一个活着的人。母亲临走时重复了父亲当年对另一个孩子说过的话。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I will never see Lenore Dove again. Never hear her laugh coming from high above me in the branches. Never feel the warmth of her in my arms as we lay on a bed of pine needles, my lips pressed into the hollow of her neck."（ch03）

> ② "We don’t do birthday cakes in my house. Seems wrong on reaping day, and Ma thinks it’s unfair for her and Sid to have a cake if I don’t. Instead, she makes something nice for breakfast, like the corn bread and sauce, and saves all of her cake energy for New Year’s Day."（ch04）

> ③ "There’s a long pause, then Plutarch says, “You may find Drusilla ridiculous, but be smart. You four don’t have your own district mentor. Your stylist’s job begins and ends with your appearance. It might not be fair, but Drusilla may be the best advocate you have in the Capitol. Think about it before you burn that bridge entirely.” He leaves, quietly closing the door behind him."（ch04）

## 节点四：被展示的野兽，与一次鼓掌（ch05-ch06）

**叙事概括：** 首都先让他们变成展品——被剃、被洗、被摆布，连母亲缝的手帕都被剪碎。然后是 Louella 的死：他抱着那具小小的尸体冲出开幕式的车队，把马车停在总统府阳台下，用一次鼓掌逼 Snow 在镜头前看见她。这是全书第一个真正的政治动作，而它没有台词。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I feel like a wild animal on display, cuffed and mute, dragged in from the hills for their fun. All of us shrink a bit. Maysilee keeps her head up, but her cheeks burn with embarrassment."（ch05）

> ② "“She doesn’t belong to them,” snaps Maysilee. “Don’t just hand her over. Make them fight for her. Run!”"（ch06）

> ③ "Something steels inside me, and I think, You are on a high horse, mister. And someday someone will knock you off it straight into your grave. I dismount the chariot and lay Louella down, taking a step back so Snow can’t pretend he doesn’t see her broken little bird body. Then I gesture to him and begin to applaud, giving credit where credit is due."（ch06）

## 节点五：第一次哭，也是第一次有人问为什么（ch07-ch08）

**叙事概括：** 在 Mags 的公寓里，他终于把筑了五十年的坝放下，哭得又狠又快又难看，因为他本该照看 Louella。同一天，Plutarch 在训练场当着一群贡品问出一个所有人都在回避的问题：既然恐惧不能证明这套安排正当，你为什么同意它。而十二岁的 Ampert 用一道算术题给出了答案——职业选手只占四分之一，剩下的人联合起来就能反过来猎杀他们。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I don’t cry much in general. Only when people die, and then I cry hard and fast and ugly, which is what I do now. Because Louella is dead and I was supposed to look out for her and I didn’t."（ch07）

> ② "“No, I do. I see the hangings and the shootings and the starvation and the Hunger Games. I do,” Plutarch says. “And yet, I still don’t think the fear they inspire justifies this arrangement we’ve all entered into. Do you?” We stare at him. He’s not taunting or mocking us, he’s genuinely asking. “Why do you agree to it? Why do I? For that matter, why have people always agreed to it?” When we don’t respond, he shrugs. “Well, it’s something to think about.”"（ch08）

> ③ "I like how even though he’s only twelve, he jumps right in. “It’s like this. A disproportionate amount of the time, the Careers win. But they’re only one quarter of the tributes. We’ve got three times their numbers. So the idea is, we get the rest of us together and, for a change, we hunt them down instead of letting them hunt us.”"（ch08）

## 节点六：书房里的死法与门口的姑娘（ch09-ch10）

**叙事概括：** 他被带进 Heavensbee 家的宅邸，在猪笼草与藏书室之后见到抱病呕吐的 Snow，并且喝光了本该端给总统的那罐奶。Snow 认出他脖子上的燧石打火器，问出 Lenore Dove 的全名，然后给出一条量身定做的死法。第二天早上，门口站着本该已经死掉的 Louella McCoy——而他一眼就看出那不是她。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“No, but I do. And I will orchestrate your death based on your behavior from here on out. You decide what you want Lenore Dove and your mother and that dear little brother of yours to see. You can die clean and fair, or we can open the Games with the slowest, most agonizing death ever to befall a tribute. And yes, you should be thanking me for giving you the option.”"（ch09）

> ② "But this isn’t Louella. In the same way you instinctively know the waxed pears on the table lack juice, this girl lacks Louella’s essence."（ch10）

> ③ "She’s right. While Louella’s wrists were lean, Fake Louella’s tend toward bony. No wonder they had to plump up her face. The irrational anger I’ve held against this girl dissolves into pity as she lifts her bowl to lick it clean like a dog."（ch10）

## 节点七：施工图、誓言与一座诱人的园子（ch13-ch15）

**叙事概括：** Beetee 一边挑蔬菜一边把整套方案交给他，从那一刻起竞技场变成了一张施工图。开放前的最后一次通话里，两个人抢着认错，然后做出全书唯一的誓言：不管要多少次，我们会找到彼此。而当他真的跑进那座园子，第一反应不是恐惧——它美得太不正常。

**关键引语：**

> ① "As the Peacekeepers redirect toward my stylist, Beetee plucks off a minuscule radish and speaks rapidly under his breath. “Head north. Ampert will do the same after he has the explosive. Do your best to locate a mutt portal by tracking returning mutts after an attack. After you and Ampert meet up, take one to access Sub-A, where the tank is located. We’ve replaced the black cord in Ampert’s token with fuse, the blasting cap’s hidden in the weave.”"（ch13）

> ② "“We will be together always,” I say with conviction. “I don’t know how, and I don’t know where, I don’t know anything, but I feel that in my heart. You and me, we will find each other, as many times as it takes.”"（ch14）

> ③ "A scowl contracts my face as suspicion sets in. It’s just too attractive to be good. The smooth green meadow stretching for miles in either direction. The array of colorful songbirds overhead that match the tufts of cheerful flowers underfoot that match the outfits on the tributes’ backs. Sky so blue it hurts your eyes, clouds so fluffy you want to bounce on them. And the smell! Like they bottled the best day of spring and uncorked it just for us."（ch15）

## 节点八：洪水、白骨与第一次杀人（ch18-ch20）

**叙事概括：** 他炸开地下水箱，洪水冲垮了半个竞技场，也冲走了留守梯顶的 Ampert——只留下一具被啃净的白骨和一把斧头。他对着土堤吼叫，让全帕纳姆听见，却在一天之后承认：竞技场受损，但游戏继续。接下来的伏击中，Maysilee 用吹镖救了他，而他第一次意识到自己亲手杀了两个人。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I dance around the berm, bellowing the first thing that comes to mind for all of Panem to hear. A song too dangerous to sing —"（ch18）

> ② "I have failed. The arena has been damaged, but not incapacitated. The Games continue."（ch19）

> ③ "I take in the dead bodies around us, for the first time recognizing that I’ve killed someone. Two someones. Brutally. It was self-defense, no question, but I know I can never go back to five minutes ago. Having taken their lives . . . in that way . . . it’s undoable. I pick up my weapons. “Let’s get out of here.”"（ch20）

## 节点九：姐妹这个词的最终定义（ch21-ch23）

**叙事概括：** 火堆边他们定下唯一的约定：总得有人去做史上最糟的胜利者，撕掉剧本、拒绝玩这场游戏。然后是崖边的分手，那群粉色的水鸟以针对个人的方式攻向她。他斩下那只鸟，把她抱在怀里看着呼吸停下来，然后用全书最长的一段告别，把「姐妹」从名词改写成动词。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“One of us has to be the worst victor in history. Tear up their scripts, tear down their celebrations, set fire to the Victor’s Village. Refuse to play their game.”"（ch21）

> ② "A bird swoops down at a sharp angle, driving its beak through her throat. As it withdraws, I decapitate it, slicing through the skinny neck. I realize Maysilee’s beyond recovery when the flock clears out. Falling to my knees beside her, I reach for her sound hand, which grasps mine like a vise. Her wounded one curls up and rests in her nest of necklaces, which lays in a pool of blood. Through the rasping of her breath, she attempts to speak, but the last mutt silenced her voice with its wicked beak. Mine seems silenced as well, as no words of comfort or hope or apology make it out. I just stare into those burning blue eyes, letting her know she’s not dying alone. She’s with family. She’s with me."（ch23）

> ③ "Good-bye, Maysilee Donner, who I loathed, then grudgingly respected, then loved. Not as a sweetheart or even a friend. A sister, I’d said. But what is that exactly? I think about our journey — everything from sniping with her in those early days after the reaping to battling those pink birds. I guess that’s my answer. A sister is someone you fight with and fight for. Tooth and nail."（ch23）

## 节点十：王冠、灰烬与另一种日出（ch25-ch28）

**叙事概括：** 他没死。他被首都修好、监禁、送上一部删掉全部反抗痕迹的纪录片，然后在加冕礼上站着不动，等一句宣判。回到十二区，家已成灰；在废墟前，Lenore Dove 从袋子里取出一颗血红的软糖。他此后每年生日都会得到一对新的贡品——而许多年后，她以老去的模样回来告诉他，能从他身上拿走的东西本来就不值得留下。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Some victors bow, some kneel, but I just stand there trying to read his expression as he approaches and places the crown on my head. Heavy. Entrapping. “I guess Snow lands on top,” I say under the applause. Utterly guilty on all possible counts, I await his sentence."（ch25）

> ② "“But . . .” I look down at the bag. It’s a normal bag, with the Donners’ label. Then I notice the gumdrops. Not a variety of colors. Not a rainbow. They’re all a deep bloodred. I remember Snow’s rose, his final words to me, and the pieces fall into place."（ch26）

> ③ "I make no plans, have no hopes, keep no company, speak to no human being except old Bascom Pie when my nepenthe runs low. But I can’t say I have no future, because I know that every year for my birthday, I will get a new pair of tributes, one girl and one boy, to mentor to their deaths. Another sunrise on the reaping."（ch27）

> ④ "I know one thing, though: The Capitol can never take Lenore Dove from me again. They never really did in the first place. Nothing you can take from me was ever worth keeping, and she is the most precious thing I’ve ever known."（ch28）
