# 《Affairs of State》情感节点

## 节点一：舞会重逢——错认中的复活（ch01-05）

**叙事概括：** Levar 以使团军需官身份赴禧庆，发现皇帝 Astridur 就是十年前失散的恋人，而她的档案里他早已"阵亡且已婚"。舞会上一句脱口而出的问候重启旧情，舞池里他把拆散真相说破——误会第一次松动。

**关键引语：**

> Which might be what prompted my mouth to say, “Hello, beautiful,” to the most fearsome warlord in the galaxy.

> He took you from me. I wish I’d killed him myself.

> So there’s hope for the day after,”

## 节点二：错位的刀与反向求婚（ch07-11）

**叙事概括：** 夜宴上她用"你妻子还好吗"试探，痛苦全部来自信息差；枕边争论中她给出最短的自白，却在求婚摊牌夜暴露当年真相——开口求嫁的是她这个皇帝，而法统拆散了这场婚事。

**关键引语：**

> “How is your wife?” the Emperor asked. “Has she come with you?”

> “I don’t love any of the others.”

> “I wasn’t asking you to be my consort. I was asking you to be my wife!”

## 节点三：星空条件（ch16）

**叙事概括：** 北阿尔卑斯星空下旧情重燃，他把关系风险摆上桌面，并为 Tilly 与她针锋相对——十年分离的定义权之争：被留下的人才有资格谈忠诚。

**关键引语：**

> That depends on whether or not you plan to make love to me or rip my heart from my chest.”

> Tilly has been with me longer than you were, and she never left me.”

## 节点四：第一次死亡与换心（ch18-19）

**叙事概括：** 暗杀夜他为护她重伤濒死，帝国医术给他装上魔族心脏。醒来后他当众宣布效忠——身体成为身份合并的物理注脚，而她"不要再失去你一次"的失言泄密了十年的伤。

**关键引语：**

> “Stay with me, Levar,” I heard her say as my mind went to black. “I don’t want to lose you again.”

> I had no choice. I’m not willing to watch you die, not after you came back to life.

> I already have. To die again would be wasteful. Next time, I intend to kill for her.”

## 节点五：皇帝的囚笼（ch14/27）

**叙事概括：** 战争的起源是一场的"短期战争"失控；枕边争吵升级到"我就是帝国"，深夜低谷里她说出想逃去外国、与爱人一事无成的梦想——而那恰是 Levar 的祖国与他们的小屋。

**关键引语：**

> I have power, yes. But no control.

> “I am the Empire. In saying that, you say you want me to lose.”

> I hate this. I hate being Emperor. I hate the games, the fights, the assassinations. I want to go back to being a nobody, to live in a foreign land, and learn things that are forbidden in the Voidskies and spend my life pleasantly doing nothing of consequence with the human I love, but I can’t. I have responsibilities now. I can change things for the better.”

> “Am I a monster to you?”

## 节点六：亡国夜与背叛（ch29-30）

**叙事概括：** 亡国夜宴上他盛装赴会，随后政变爆发、皇帝被废、他被己方拘押收买。敌人的"皇帝已死"反而激他反杀——葬礼最终没有举行。

**关键引语：**

> If tonight be the funeral of my nation, I wanted to look my best to see the old girl off,”

> The Emperor is dead, her fate consigned whether her heart still beats or not.

> No one ever said it would be easy or painless or popular. That doesn’t mean it’s not right.”

## 节点七：重整旗鼓（ch31-33）

**叙事概括：** Tilly 越狱救主并在逃亡路上与他辩论生死；重聚盟友、立下写给马的遗嘱、军帐点兵——决战前的最后一次集结，忠诚从仆人、马、王妃一路结账到皇帝身边。

**关键引语：**

> But you would be alive.

> There’s more to life than living, Tilly.

> A servant chooses little in this life, but the one thing they may choose is their master.”

> if the worst should happen, I want you to promise me you’ll take care of Tilly.

## 节点八：真相与清算（ch34）

**叙事概括：** 亚轨道站审讯揭开全部底牌：联邦递送激活码、战争为清除异见者、以及十年前那句"提前说出的真话"。Zukov 死于自己教给 Levar 的箴言。

**关键引语：**

> You were a distraction we could not allow.

> “I’m not going to stop you. I’m going to speak, you will listen, and when I am done speaking, you will stop yourself.”

## 节点九：救驾与代价（ch35-37）

**叙事概括：** 他用学院旧词唤醒她，她用军令宣告醒来；平流层坠落、Tilly 空中拦截、骑兵冲锋——大胜与 Tilly 坠毁同时到账，皇帝越过枪口承认"她是你的一部分"。

**关键引语：**

> I know, darling, that’s the drugs they used to knock you out. It’s just a bad trip,”

> Consort, you saved the Emperor’s life.”

> “Tilly, no.” It was stupid, but all I could think to say.

> No, but I know now she is a part of you, and I could never harm you.”

## 节点十：终局——我要的就会得到（ch38-39）

**叙事概括：** 复位、审判、登座演说之后，她把 Levar 写进停战条约——大使、特赦、行省。两难的选择被一支笔作废，全书终于停在微笑着的"活着"上。

**关键引语：**

> Come on. Let’s go kill some people.”

> “I am the Emperor, my dear. I get what I want.”

> For the first time in years, I found myself looking towards the future with joy, excited to have the chance to finally live.
