# 情感节点

> 每节点叙事概括 + 2-3 句关键引语

## 1. 无家可归（ch01）

**叙事概括：** Skotch — 一只失业的浣熊 — 在 Franz-Ferdinand 餐厅外等活。他被 HengZeico 的线人 Springer 找到，开始了调查。

**关键引语：**
> "They're sensible about things and talked, and talked. Until they came back with the next course. And then there'd be nothing to do but look. Look and smell, and not have. Look at the wealth of the world you weren't part of, the world that had thrown you away."

## 2. 初次接触（ch02-ch03）

**叙事概括：** Skotch 进入 Gehirner 社会的底层 — 公司城、黑市、下水道。他遇到了 Lulu（一只鸽子记者），并第一次听说了"圣人"圣弗朗西斯。

**关键引语：**
> "But Gehirner who make a habit of it get kicked out, find themselves sans work or friends and, soon after, get dumb, and get dead, in that order."

## 3.  Franz-Ferdinand 大战（ch05-ch07）

**叙事概括：** Skotch 在 Franz-Ferdinand 餐厅找到了 Meece — 一只逃亡的老鼠科学家。Szerky（农场的杀手白鼬）追踪而至，引发了一场餐厅混战。Meece 被 Tybelle（一只猫）杀死，但他的发明（伟大均衡剂）已经通过猫的消化系统释放。

**关键引语：**
> "Meece was," says Tybelle, "delicious."
> "She left!" Lulu bursts out. "Skotch, we were all sitting here waiting..."

## 4. 圣弗朗西斯的教堂（ch06-ch07）

**叙事概括：** Skotch 带受伤的 Lulu 到圣弗朗西斯的教堂求治。圣弗朗西斯是一个人类修女 — 她的存在本身就是对 Rule One 的违背。她收留了所有物种的难民。

**关键引语：**
> "Because there should be a huge figure at his back right now, forbidding by its very presence the possibility of violence. Instead of which there's an absence as big as the Chapel itself."

## 5. Ratlabs 的揭秘（ch16-ch17）

**叙事概括：** Skotch 通过 Nimoy（一只 Ratlabs 的科技鼠）了解到 Meece 的伟大均衡剂 — 一种可以人工合成 Plangent 的公式。农场的"大秘密"是：他们通过控制 Plangent 供应来压迫老鼠劳工，让老鼠保持"接近退化"的状态。

**关键引语：**
> "The human received a call from the sect she belongs to," Szerky announces proudly. "They are most displeased with her and have called her to an accounting."

## 6. 圣弗朗西斯被召回（ch18-ch19）

**叙事概括：** 圣弗朗西斯被她的教团召回了 — 这是农场通过人类体制施压的结果。在最需要保护的时候，唯一的保护者离开了。

**关键引语：**
> "Saint Frances just up and left them in their hour of need and that's mighty inconvenient, because that need is just ramping up the more soldiers filter in."
> "She left! She left, Skotch. Just walked away. Walked up."

## 7. 电车偷渡（ch17）

**叙事概括：** Skotch 和 Lulu 一起乘地铁（电车）横跨城市去见圣弗朗西斯 — 但他们发现她已经不在了。在电车底部，Skotch 与乘客近在咫尺，意识到自己的"牵绊"正在拖累他。

**关键引语：**
> "The rat gondoliers usually plying their trade here have fled, and so it's wade or swim."

## 8. Chapel 被围困（ch21-ch22）

**叙事概括：** Szerky 率领红灰联军围攻 Chapel。Meece 揭示了农场控制 Plangent 供应的真相。Nimoy 带来了声波武器，制造了"隐形敌人"的假象，逼退了军队。

**关键引语：**
> "The Reds and the Grays have at least as many eyes on each other as they do on anyone else. No love lost nor trust given, over that way."
> "I'm giving you this one chance, Meinen Herren and Damen," she shouts. "Give up Meece or float his body over..."

## 9. 主人现身（ch23）

**叙事概括：** Schreiber（Lulu 的主人）出现在 Chapel — 这是 Rule One 的彻底终结。Lulu 把 Meece 的设备交给了 Schreiber，让他把公式发布到人类互联网上。Plangent 从此成为"像水一样自由"的商品。

**关键引语：**
> "A human has intruded into the Chapel. Not Saint Frances, that creature of two worlds."
> "And the greatest of these is Rule One."
> "The human takes a thunderous step forwards. Animals scatter..."

## 10. 尾声：新的开始（ch24）

**叙事概括：** Plangent 已在街头自由流通，Gehirner 社会发生了不可逆转的变化。Skotch 获得了 HengZeico 的工作，与 Benson 再次会面。Lulu 在等着听他的故事。

**关键引语：**
> "You are a goddamn pain in the ass, Skotch," Benson says, hooking his claws over the edge of his bowl to haul himself up.
> "We adapt, Skotch. Better than the local guilds. Better than the Country Clubs and their aristocratic nonsense."
> "He heads off, feeling that extra security in every step. Lulu's waiting to buy him lunch and hear him talk. He owes her a story, and he has a damn good one to tell."

## 11. Sly 的幽灵（贯穿全书）

**叙事概括：** Skotch 的调查间接导致了 Sly 的死亡 — 这是他道德觉醒的起点。Sly 的幽灵贯穿全书，提醒 Skotch：他的行为可以导致死亡。

**关键引语：**
> "I had something …" He'd had those scraps of notes he retrieved from Franz-Ferdinand's, the first brief time the pair of them met. The notes he copied for Sly, that got the fox killed purely because of what he might or might not have worked out.

## 12. Tybelle 的复杂性（ch19, ch22）

**叙事概括：** Tybelle 是一只复杂的反派 — 她吃了 Meece（这是谋杀），但她也帮助 Skotch 保护了 Meece 的设备。她的"忠诚"不是对农场的，而是对 Mother Murnau（一只负鼠革命者）的。最终她杀死了 Szerky，为 Meece 报了仇。

**关键引语：**
> "That weasel," she says, "is fast." Confirming his fears. (Tybelle 在与 Szerky 的对决中受伤)
