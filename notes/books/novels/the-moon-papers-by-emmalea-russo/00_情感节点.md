# 《Two Wars and a Wedding》情感节点

## 节点一：离坦帕两英里的道床边（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书的情感起点不是一个相遇，而是一个决定。一八九八年六月二日，Betsy 穿着被呕吐物弄脏的裙子站在铁轨边，目的是把最好的朋友从古巴的战场拉回来；她遇见的第一个人是旧日同学 Paul，第二个是那位不由分说把她塞回车厢的军官。她此时并不知道，那位军官这辈子最想救的人就是她。

**关键引语：**

> ① He took a deep breath, pushed his secret phone, the phone he used only to connect with Vesta, deep into the pocket of his white jumpsuit, and walked into the office.（ch01）

> ② In these meetings, Dean often felt he was melting, voice and body disintegrating into desert anonymity.（ch01）

> ③ Bobby: Dean, dude, just calm down. I know you two were close, but she wasn’t doing her job. She wandered off. It’s as simple as that.（ch03）

## 节点二：德尔斐，柱旁的那个吻（ch06-08）

**叙事概括：** 她在德尔斐向一个已婚法国人交出了自己；被拒绝的方式不是冷酷而是温柔——他蹲下来替她把领带系好，然后说出那段时间「在世界之外」。她没有哭也没有闹，只把怒气层层裹起来当盔甲，然后一个人爬上了赫里孔山。

**关键引语：**

> ① Radko: Velour…the mothers just left to feed!（ch08）

> ② Velour smiled. With the mothers gone, the babies were in the attic all alone, tucked into blind little clusters. If she were to get rid of them, evacuate her house of the pups, would the moms kill Velour, scouring the house, then the earth, searching for what they birthed?（ch08）

> ③ Lars: is Vesta with you?（ch08）

## 节点三：营火边的七把空椅子（ch09-11）

**叙事概括：** 拉古斯马斯的火线上，一个叫 Ham 的中尉当场死去，一个叫 Thomas 的中尉在血泊里下令「弟兄们得看见指挥官站在前面」。当夜七个人合葬一处蓝毯之下，剩下两个人在营火边为「该不该报仇」争到天亮。

**关键引语：**

> ① How was it made? How had it gotten there? Who brought it? Who would press the button to begin the lighting and inflation process? Was there a button? He’d been swimming in language about Moon2, but as far as he could tell, the Collective had spent very little time fleshing out technical questions. What Dean knew was this: The trial balloon would launch tonight at sunset from the Center’s huge concrete patio. Dean and the rest of the Collective would watch as it ascended three hundred yards into the sky.（ch11）

> ② “I like talking to you before the sun rises in the desert,” she said. “It makes me feel like time can reverse, like it’s possible to go backward.”（ch11）

> ③ “Who the hell’s there?” Ziggy screamed. “Is that you, Darlene?”（ch11）

## 节点四：桑塞卡号（ch13-15）

**叙事概括：** 她违抗命令接下一船伤员，指挥官用一句话把整船人的命压给她。船在高热中病倒。她在那间被当作野战病房改装的船上第一次感到自己有用，也第一次被人怀疑是不是在演一场戏。

**关键引语：**

> ① Dean stood there, utterly disconnected from the six entities before him, praying he would never, ever be born again so that he might stay connected forever to Vesta, his distant placenta.（ch13）

> ② Mac: R u ever gonna respond?（ch14）

> ③ When he looked up, he realized Tulip had been staring at him, her head cocked like a spaniel’s. He forced a smile and she half-smiled back, eyes narrowing. Dean was leaning against the coffee table, slumped. Tulip’s posture, however, was impeccable: spine straight, shoulders back, neck long. Her hair was dark and pulled back and her body lithe and vascular. She resembled neither a man nor a woman. Instead, her presence evoked a bird imitating a human: disturbing but oddly astonishing.（ch15）

## 节点五：她以为自己毁了所有人（ch22-25）

**叙事概括：** 希腊在战火里塌了，她怀着孩子回到美国，躲进一间伤兵收容所，把负罪一条条数给自己听：那个没机会出生的孩子、那个她救不回来的同事、那个她以为死在自己手上的法国人。这一段的最低点是她写下的那句——我毁掉了我碰到的一切。

**关键引语：**

> ① I’m around the age of Lars, her husband. I think my generation struggles more with Moon2 than Vesta’s generation. She seemed unperturbed. We spoke for a little while. She came over to the window of my car with those three dogs. The intersection near the church. I told her to be careful because cars really zip around that curve, the one she lives off. She just laughed. She was carrying a plastic bag from the store, but I don’t know what was in it. She was in a good mood. I regret not speaking to her more.（ch25）

## 节点六：桑塞卡号的听证会（ch26-27）

**叙事概括：** 战争结束了，但没有人愿意听她说话。报纸称她是「歇斯底里的女人」，军方把她的话当作不着边际的胡言。她在会上只做了一件事：把「该负责的人」和「该称赞的人」分开，于是规程来了，调查委员会也来了。

**关键引语：**

> ① When Lars put his hand on her shoulder, she became aware of herself again: skin, muscles, bones, outfit (long white shirt with big pockets over her usual jean shorts), hair. The wetness between her legs reminded her of the slick road she’d driven a few nights ago on her way to the bus station to pick up Lars. Over the soaked road, bullfrogs jumped, making arches in front of her car, their emerald skin glowing in her high beams. She had run over one, and when she stopped to see if it had died or lived, she was struck by how the frog resembled a human man, splayed out on soaking macadam, as dead as her grandparents. She whispered I’m sorry and ran back to the car. In rain, hidden critters emerge, get wet, and die.（ch27）

> ② She showed him. He moved closer, staring for a long time as she sweated. When she closed her eyes and leaned back, everything was chartreuse and yellow for a delicious instant, and the taste of candy, its quick chemical bliss, filled her mouth when her husband gave her a kiss. An endless circle, recursive and doubling back on itself. She was in the dry desert and she was here. With him and with him. When he licked the paint off her hand, they both smiled.（ch27）

## 节点七：中央公园，报童旁边的那个人（ch28）

**叙事概括：** 她拿着那篇把自己写进头版的报道来堵他，却在公园里看见一个穿得一丝不苟、正在用拐杖从水里捞小船的男人。他为她做了一件他自己这辈子最难的事：给父亲打了电话。

**关键引语：**

> ① I remember a raccoon too. It was already dead, like so many things. I ran over it again. And it felt terribly good. To further flatten a body already flat. I remember a lack of air. And pink flowers on trees in the later part of the season, ones I’d never seen. The flowers did not have petals, but thin strands of spiked hair. She’s not dead. Vesta will turn up when and if she wants. If my daughter were dead, I’d know it in my bones and heart.（ch28）

## 节点八：混杂档案（ch29）

**叙事概括：** 全书以一叠文件收尾：一封宣布婚讯并讨债的信、一份调查委员会报告的节选、一封礼貌到关门的老相识来信、一则大学教师与军医的婚讯剪报。没有一句总结，只有她写下的最后一条计划——婚礼一过就回希腊。

**关键引语：**

> ① Her office, which Rad had painted Bluebird Blue, sat at the back of the house with its own separate entrance. In the afternoons, when the sun filled the room, she felt she was inside a melting ice cube. The perfect environment for listening. Chloe considered herself a renegade therapist, as she had no formal training but desired to give the world her undivided attention.（ch29）

> ② I just know there is something wrong. I got those horses for therapy, and I think they need therapy. You should see how lethargic they are! Depressed, even. Something’s taking their energy.（ch29）

> ③ Lisa: Whoever’s behind Moon2 has us all in his mouth and he’s about to bite down.（ch29）
