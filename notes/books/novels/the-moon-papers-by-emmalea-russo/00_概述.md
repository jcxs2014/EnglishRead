# 《Two Wars and a Wedding》概述

## 全书梗概

**一八九八年的坦帕，先给一个被掏空的人。** 火车在离坦帕两英里的野外停住，二十四岁的 Betsy Hayes 站在烟与煤渣里，裙子上沾着一个三岁小孩的呕吐物。她刚从希腊来，为的是拦住最好的朋友 Ava 跟她一起去古巴当护士。而她带回来的经验只有一句话：He took a deep breath, pushed his secret phone, the phone he used only to connect with Vesta, deep into the pocket of his white jumpsuit, and walked into the office.（ch01）

**真正的开始在三年之前。** 时间倒回一八九六年：贝茜刚在雅典的美国学校拿到两个学位中的头一个，成为该校仅有的两名女生之一，却被所长挡在发掘现场之外——理由不是能力，是性别。她把所长三言两语绕成了指路人，转头又在梯林斯的城墙上想通了自己这一生要挖什么：“There he goes,” she said. She didn’t know who or what she was speaking to, but she felt a presence all around her. She said it again.（ch04）

**她在那里遇见了那位法国考古学家。** 他已婚，对她的评价是「你有一种独特而令人耳目一新的思考方式」。他替她说出了她自己那句话：“Not this. I’m keeping this little one. I mean, do you want the real one, the big one? It’s a genuine Windsor bench. Do you know what that is? It’s worth quite a lot. But I’d like to give it to you. I want you to have it. I think it should fit in your truck. It’s over there. Near the Sheraton Cylinder desk.”（ch06） 一八九六年十一月的德尔斐，两人在一根神庙柱旁接了吻，也在那里听见了「我娶她的时候我已经有妻子了」这个事实。她拒绝了做情人，也拒绝了做朋友，只答应重新开始——而他给她的回报是把她气得爬上了赫利孔山。

**与此同时，另一场战争已经开打。** 巴顿女士带着一群女护士在古巴上岸，征用民船转运伤员；而被称作「响尾蛇」的一支骑兵团里，站着一个把化名当掩护的哈佛法学院退学生。他脸上的伤不是枪伤，是家暴；母亲死在他面前，而官方的结论是悲伤让人神志不清。营火边他终于说出口的是：“I like talking to you before the sun rises in the desert,” she said. “It makes me feel like time can reverse, like it’s possible to go backward.”（ch11）

**两人在桑塞卡号上相遇。** 她为救一船伤员违抗命令，他奉命押送伤员转移，双方都把对方当作麻烦。船在高热中病倒，指挥官拒绝听命，她当众用一句话把这道命令顶了回去：When I think about the Collective and Moon2, and what they did, the amazing spectacle of it all, I mean, shit, I wonder what any of the other stuff is even for. To come back to intern in this space after watching the moon launch a few nights ago feels impossible.（ch21） 而那位男爵夫人的儿子并没有死——他就是霍尔特。

**她以为他死了，而她自己的希腊也塌了。** 一八九七到一八九八年间，希腊已经烧起来，克里特在反、她在发掘现场失去了那位法国同行；她怀着孩子回到美国，被当作伤兵收容所的麻烦。等她终于拿到桑塞卡号的听证机会、准备重新开口时，她听见的第一件事是——她认错了人。真正的霍尔特站在报童旁边，替她把一场丑闻变成了一次约见。

**收尾在一叠混杂档案里。** 婚礼、调查委员会、一份发往古巴与波多黎各的规程、一封礼貌到关门的信、一则大学教师与军医的婚讯剪报。她在信里只写了一句自己的计划：Lisa: Whoever’s behind Moon2 has us all in his mouth and he’s about to bite down.（ch29）

## 三个主题

**一、起因不重要。** 这本书里每个人都在追问「这件事为什么值得做」，而 Betsy 的答案在第一章就说完了：He took a deep breath, pushed his secret phone, the phone he used only to connect with Vesta, deep into the pocket of his white jumpsuit, and walked into the office.（ch01） 她的哥哥把她的方向说成「不过是父亲没去成」，她则把同一件事拆成两半：重要的是谁来做，不是为什么。

**二、做事与做人之间的裂缝。** 霍尔特背着一条他永远带不走的行军包：Some of the artists had come to the program because they were already unconscious and not likely to wake up. This was the case with Vesta’s grandmother, Vivienne Volker. Velour had signed her into the Collective’s care all those years ago after an accident had rendered her brain-dead.（ch05） 而 Betsy 的裂缝是另一种形状——她先毁了希腊的一切，又在桑塞卡号上救了一船人。两个人各自的罪，最后都在同一个位置上会合：他选择「用杀还杀」还是「那不是正义，那是复仇」；她选择「我毁掉一切才能救下这些人」还是「我不能因为过去就放弃」。

**三、说出真相的代价与权利。** 从 Kit 被军报拒之门外，到 Betsy 被当作「歇斯底里的女人」，到最后一章那份规程里的一句「一支经过挑选与训练的后备女护士队」：Lisa: I heard this woman on the street answer her phone. The first thing she said was “Is everything okay?” There’s an edge to every conversation, a panic. Tendency to jump to the worst possible thing. Don’t you think?（ch29） 全书的转折从来不是某个人变勇敢，而是某个制度终于承认了她们本来就会做的事。

## 人物弧光

**Betsy Hayes / Ambrose Rutherford IV**——

- 起点：In these meetings, Dean often felt he was melting, voice and body disintegrating into desert anonymity.（ch01）
- 转向：The sound of her name, which rose from a throat coated in bat shit and her own blood, circled her head like a filthy halo.（ch04）
- 最低点：I’m around the age of Lars, her husband. I think my generation struggles more with Moon2 than Vesta’s generation. She seemed unperturbed. We spoke for a little while. She came over to the window of my car with those three dogs. The intersection near the church. I told her to be careful because cars really zip around that curve, the one she lives off. She just laughed. She was carrying a plastic bag from the store, but I don’t know what was in it. She was in a good mood. I regret not speaking to her more.（ch25）
- 落点：I just know there is something wrong. I got those horses for therapy, and I think they need therapy. You should see how lethargic they are! Depressed, even. Something’s taking their energy.（ch29）
- 她真正的转折不是任何一次胜利，而是说出那句最低点之后，仍然选择去开那场听证会。

**Holt / Hold 'Em Holt**——

- 起点：Dean walked Vesta around the balloon before tilting the screen up to the real moon, then moved around in a circle, showing her the perimeter—desert hills, outlines of cacti, the studio, the office. He would have to go to the sunrise meeting soon. Before Vesta hung up, before she left her secret studio to check on the old man, she painted or sketched something. Dean couldn’t tell what. He only saw her shoulders and arms, veins and bones, moving. He inhaled, taking in her skin, which he could almost smell through the screen.（ch11）
- 中段：She was in her secret studio at Ziggy’s place, her face very close to the screen, so that Dean could see her constellation of birthmarks enlarged, her hair messed into loops and clumps around her face and falling on her white shirt, which was covered in green grass stains and paint. Her skin color matched the test moon, and he wondered, as he held his small rectangular screen filled with her face up to the ginormous glistening tarp, if Moon2 would look like her, like this.（ch11）
- 落点：I remember a raccoon too.（ch28）
- 他一生只做过一件难事：向那个男人开口。

**Ava Saltonstall**——

- 起点：He hung up and stood under the rising moon, feeling utterly human.（ch01）
- 她与贝茜的关系从不靠表白维持，而靠「你不必解释，我也不问」；结局是拿到医学学位、与军医结婚、并在婚礼上做了唯一出席的宾客。
