# 《Paradise》情感节点

**节点一 · 天堂的欢迎词（ch01–ch02）**
Simone 的欢迎广播立起全书框架：孩子与父母分区而住，"你的体验由你决定"既是许诺也是圈套。三个孩子在滑翔伞上完成全书第一次彼此托举，吐沫立誓要把这个假期过成最好的一个。

> “In fact, there are several adventures to explore right now at Iguana Beach, just north of the guest villas. You can go tubing along the coastline, play vintage video games on the pontoon boat arcade, and go parasailing to see the best views of this majestic island.”（ch01）

> “I’m just worried this is our last hurrah, you know? Vancouver is so freaking far away. I doubt we’re going to be seeing each other much now.”（ch02）

**节点二 · 信件惊雷（ch05）**
一封银字黑信把 Jordan 的假期炸出裂缝：Nan 要来、预科学校坐实、父母"没得选"的争吵全被她隔窗偷听。被爱得最周全的孩子，第一次看清自己活在所有人的决定之下。

> I took the liberty of making a reservation for myself, and will be joining you and your cousins in a couple of days! Look forward to seeing you end hearing all about Vancouver.（ch05）

> But prep school? Her parents had enrolled her in PREP SCHOOL? She bolted off the couch so fast that Lily scrambled to follow.（ch05）

> “No, not yet. He already hates Mom enough. I don’t want to give him another reason.”（ch05）

**节点三 · 占星师的警告（ch06–ch08）**
Estrella 在帐篷帘后避开 Lookout 说出全书题句；两天后它的"兑现版"在崖顶风暴里显形——Lily 的预言玩笑（和 Milo 会亲近）以一场失控夜探的方式应验。

> They got as close as they could and she softly said, “Things aren’t what they seem on the island. Your curiosity is admirable, but it could get you in big trouble. Be careful. Both of you,” Estrella whispered, with a piercing stare.（ch06）

> “She said Milo and I are going to get very close on this trip, and a mysterious event is going to bring us together,” said Lily. Jordan’s eyes widened with delight. “Whoa, maybe that event is happening right now!”（ch08）

**节点四 · 风暴夜的分岔（ch08）**
崖顶月光下的"I will be okay."话音未落，风暴即至：Milo 摔伤、手电熄灭、Lily 听到那声不属于 Paradise 的咆哮后独自狂奔。三个人在同一个夜里被拆散，各自欠下一笔账。

> As she took in the expanse of the sea, her troubles suddenly seemed tiny and insignificant. Jordan breathed in deeply and willed these words to be true: I will be okay.（ch08）

> Just in time for Jordan to see Lily breaking into a run without looking back.（ch08）

**节点五 · Silverback 与被抛弃（ch09、ch12）**
Sam 在特权少年们的讥讽里被活门吞进半球最大滑梯；一夜之后"他们就这么把他甩了"成为定论。复仇同盟碰拳成立——那是他从食物链底层借来的第一次主动出击。

> But Sam wouldn’t get the chance. Suddenly the floor beneath him vanished. Then all he could hear was his own screams and the roar of the Silverback as it swallowed him whole.（ch09）

> They’d just … ditched him.（ch12）

**节点六 · 没人信的怪物报告（ch11）**
Lily 的 growl 证词被系统礼貌驳回，"进了系统"的怪物就此蒸发；而客厅里兄弟为 Nan 摊牌——"她还没挣到原谅"。孩子学会的第一课：说出真相的成本由说出的人支付。

> “Yes, there might be some … thing … roaming around that area,” Lily confessed. “Excuse me?” Rebecca said, startled.（ch11）

> “Listen, I’ve entered your statement into the system, but I’m sure whatever you heard was related to the weather,” Rebecca explained. “Being caught in a storm can be very disorienting.”（ch11）

> Her father threw his hands up, appalled. “Because she hasn’t earned one! Mom still isn’t sorry for what she did.”（ch11）

**节点七 · 调查组建档（ch16、ch19）**
Milo 的第二双耳朵让孤证变双证；爪痕、防水布下的电话与"25% 的订房崩塌"把秘密拼成账本。证据半路遗失，孩子们却换来更硬的筹码——业主子女决定亲自去看。

> Milo nodded. “There’s a good chance that sound we heard was a cry for help.”（ch16）

> “Look, we threw the security team off the scent, but we’re running out of time,” British Man said. “The jaguar escaped because of your carelessness during the transport, and you will face the consequences if something serious happens.”（ch19）

**节点八 · Feeding Frenzy 揭幕（ch22）**
灯亮的一刻，"捕食者对猎物"的记分牌与一到五千美元的价目表把耳语变成罪证；Avery Max 对父母的信仰当场破产，断电与兽舍门震开把恐怖推到顶点。

> Lily looked at it again. Instead of Home and Away, it said something different. And sinister. “Predator and Prey.” “If you put a jaguar in an arena with its prey, audiences aren’t going to see a fight,” Milo said. “They’re going to witness a—” “Feeding Frenzy,” Lily said, finishing his sentence.（ch22）

> Exclusive, once-in-a-lifetime event. Elite guests will experience the primal instincts of the jungle’s most dangerous predator. NDAs mandatory. All bookings made through a secure portal. Ticket prices range from one to five thousand dollars. Project nearly completed, awaiting final security sweep.（ch22）

**节点九 · 与豹对峙（ch23–ch24）**
"Don't. Run."接管全队、一步一退的慢舞对死亡生效；而 Max 的足球闹钟把"许愿规则"兑现成致命的 distraction。暴雨里的推车、修车与和解，让"我们做到了"第一次取代"我做到了"。

> He wanted to sprint away the second he saw the animal, but Lily grabbed him by the arm so tightly, it hurt. “Don’t. Run.” Lily’s voice was low but commanding. This girl got anxious when it was her turn to order food at a restaurant! How could she be so chill?（ch23）

> A loud alarm went off and tore through the room like an air-raid siren. It was the obnoxious football timer Max had set on his phone back at the tree house. This was a distraction all right, but a lethal one. The jaguar was charging right toward them.（ch23）

**节点十 · Nan 登场与最长飞行（ch26–ch27）**
green room 的拥抱还带着余温，Nan 已宣布"我来修好一切"；机舱颠簸掉出的"Angel Investor"文件夹让装睡成为 Lily 的成人礼。"未完待续"把三个问号与一颗红宝石一起带上续作的航程。

> “I have another idea,” said a voice coming from behind Sam. Sam saw a woman in the doorframe, in wide-legged black pants, a tan waffle-knit T-shirt, and a striped headband. “It’s me. Nan,” she said. “And don’t you worry. I’m going to fix everything, just like I always do.”（ch26）

> Nan shoved the handkerchief back in her pocket and then wrapped an arm around her granddaughter. “Lily, my dear. Everyone is going to be fine, including the animals. I’ll make sure of it.” “But … how?” she asked. “Have you ever heard the phrase, Money talks?” Nan asked in return. “Yes,” Lily said. “Of course.” There was a devious sparkle in their grandmother’s eyes as she delivered her punchline. “Well, my money speaks every language there is,” she said with a wink. “And just like you, I have friends. In very high places.”（ch26）

> Then Lily noticed her grandmother’s bag had tipped over from the turbulence. A bunch of items had slipped out. One of them caught Lily’s attention as Nan tried to scoop everything back up. A yellow folder with the Paradise logo printed on it and a label at the top that read: Penelope Sprague, Angel Investor. As soon she realized what she was looking at, Lily quickly closed her eyes and pretended to be asleep. She couldn’t let her grandmother know what she’d seen.（ch27）
