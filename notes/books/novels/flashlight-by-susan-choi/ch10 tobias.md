# 10. Tobias

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1978 年 10 月，一个二十岁的青年以「妹妹的丈夫」的身份回到母亲的空公寓，做一场无从取舍的收拾，从掀起的榻榻米下摸出 Anne 女儿的那页梦，最后带着一句 `We already are brave` 起飞。

- **情感弧线位置**：从一进门就把姿态摆正（`the way it was supposed to be done`），到与人周旋时还得自己撑住场面（`the atmosphere of uncanny absence`），再到独处时 `Tobias’s last strength went also`——这是全章的低点，之后只剩飞机上极短的一下回升。

- **人物弧线**：从 `a helplessly terrible student` 变成替人收拾遗物、并最终违逆母亲一次的那个人；而他对自己那套「迹象」的信仰并不否认来路——那是他 `inherited` 的。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角贴紧 Tobias；作者几乎不用心理动词，改用身体与物件——`The torso levered smoothly from the hips`、`as if tenderly laying each discarded cloth husk in a grave`；收束靠一页手写梦境和一只握过来的手，不靠叙述者评断。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Tobias bowed in return, the way it was supposed to be done. His skin may have been white, but the volume of his being, he had come to believe, was Asian, most obviously in these graceful displacements. The torso levered smoothly from the hips, the even magnetism of the palms. … He could still recall, but with remote wonderment, his unbalanced, twitchy teenage former self, his abhorrence of confinement whether it be the elastic band of his jock shorts or his father’s expectation of reverent attention. … He had been a helplessly terrible student, a late and agonized reader, almost certainly an undiagnosed dyslexic.

管理员还记得他，却明显被这副改头换面惊到；作者随即把这个「像样了」的 Tobias 拆成两半——皮肤或许还是白的，但「体积」被他自己判成了亚洲的。而另一个 Tobias（拘谨、抽动、被校规与父亲的要求勒住）只以「从前的」身份被回忆，并且只配上一句诊断。

**关键词**：was Asian / remote wonderment / undiagnosed dyslexic

**为什么这样写**：段落第一句就把「得体」写成一套动作规范——`the way it was supposed to be done`，照着规矩做，而不是照着自己做。紧接着的错位靠一个量词完成：`the volume of his being … was Asian`——他不是「变得像」，而是把自己的「体积」判给了另一套身体记忆。否定自己那一句也被量化了：`He could still recall, but with remote wonderment` 把回忆推成远景；`almost certainly an undiagnosed dyslexic` 更把少年时代的自己一次性归档成一份诊断书，通篇没给一句解释。

**读者视角提示**：他随后向管理员自称是 `my sister’s husband`。本段的价值在于：这套说法要说服的不是管理员，是他自己——身体先承认了一个不属于自己的尺寸。

> **原句 2:** Tobias wasn’t aware of deciding upon this imposture. He’d arrived at it by instinct, which belated consideration only ratified further. For one thing, it was a form of tact. Anne was understood here in exclusive relation to her late husband and to the child of their marriage, Louisa. Revealing himself as her son would have only exposed Anne to unkind speculation.

他没有「决定」要冒充，这个做法是事后追认的；作者随即给它一个不为己利的理由：在这位管理员眼里，Anne 只以两种身份存在——亡妻与那个孩子的母亲。说出自己是她儿子，等于把无关的揣测引到她身上。

**关键词**：wasn’t aware of deciding / form of tact / unkind speculation

**为什么这样写**：本块的写法是先把意图从行为里抽掉：`wasn’t aware of deciding` 说做这件事的是本能；`belated consideration only ratified further` 再说事后那点思考也只是补盖一个章——两层都取消了「盘算」。可紧跟着的 `For one thing` 之后给出的偏偏是道德理由：`a form of tact`。于是本段的结构是：他嘴上声称的动机与真实的动机并不冲突，被放在同一次交代里。

**读者视角提示**：`Anne was understood here` 里的「这里」限定得很死：这个版本只在管理员面前成立。此后每换一个日本人，这个冒充都得重新成立一次。

> **原句 3:** In the corridor they passed through the rich odor of the lavatories like passing through a cloud. Drenched rust, algal darkness, hardy microscopic flora requiring little human contribution to thrive. It was oddly an odor of human absence, of the living world indifferent to the comings and goings of people. … These were Tobias’s favorite sorts of places, abandoned and vibrant with invisible life. These were where he felt It, where he caught the fleeting Clue. His father’s dogged insistence that every such clue had been dropped by a fatherlike God had done much to drive Tobias away from him—both the father and the fatherlike God—a circumstance Tobias would have liked to discuss with Anne but had not, and not just because the unspoken rule of their relations seemed to be that they didn’t discuss Adrian. … “They’re disgusting, but they’re all ours,” Anne had remarked of the toilets. This was the sort of blunt and funny thing Adrian and Norma had never said to him. They’d never joked in that or any other way.

走廊里的味道被写成一场云，作者说那是「人的缺席」，是活着的世界对人来得去去无所谓。而 Tobias 偏偏最喜欢这类地方——在这里他感觉到 It，捕捉到 Clue：父亲坚持这些迹象都是一位父性的神丢下的，而这份坚持恰恰把他推离父亲。

**关键词**：favorite sorts of places / fatherlike God / all ours

**为什么这样写**：本块把「怪异」拆成两股互不相干的来源。一股是感觉上的：`These were where he felt It, where he caught the fleeting Clue`——叙述者不给心理动词，只给两个被命名的大写名词（It / Clue），这套信念于是始终是私人语言。另一股是记忆上的：同一处空间又连通到 Anne 说过的一句 `They’re disgusting, but they’re all ours`，而这句玩笑紧接着就被 `Adrian and Norma had never said to him` 判为父母绝不会讲的话——记住母亲，靠的就是这一句不合家风的玩笑。

**读者视角提示**：`the unspoken rule of their relations seemed to be that they didn’t discuss Adrian`：本章里他和 Anne 之间被写下来的「不谈」只有这个人。迹象与来路因此是同一条线。

> **原句 4:** The scuffed oxfords with ragged laces were Louisa’s school uniform shoes, abandoned for a month at the shore. By the fall she would have outgrown them. A pair of black rubber overshoes were obviously Serk’s. Leaning in the corner, a child’s clear plastic umbrella with a bright yellow handle. On the opposite wall from a series of hooks hung the deflated corpses of their winter coats. … Anne and Louisa had jettisoned three households in less than a year, winnowing their things to a pair of suitcases. Tobias admired this, even as he worked to counteract it, briefly studying the yellow-handled umbrella—not merely counteract it, but countermand Anne’s one request to him. “I don’t want anything,” she’d said. “Throw it all away.” Even when he’d promised to do this he’d known that he wouldn’t, despite having left everything of his own past behind. … For Tobias, it was the shock of unexpected life that had compelled him, or inspired him, to be rid of the past.

玄关里每样东西都当场报出主人：Louisa 的校鞋、Serk 的橡胶套鞋、一把黄柄儿童伞、塌掉的冬衣。Anne 要他「全扔掉」，他答应了却事先就知道自己做不到；而真正违逆她的动作不是留下什么，是那只伞——他停下来看了它。

**关键词**：jettisoned three households / known that he wouldn’t / shock of unexpected life

**为什么这样写**：作者把「要不要留东西」改写成两个人动机的差。Anne 扔东西是 `under duress`，是被 `the shock of unexpected death` 逼的；他自己扔东西是 `the shock of unexpected life`——同一个动作，一边是失去，一边是多出来的生命。`Even when he’d promised to do this he’d known that he wouldn’t` 用一个早就知道的将来时承认自己会违约：承诺与行动从一开始就不在同一层。

**读者视角提示**：`briefly studying the yellow-handled umbrella—not merely counteract it, but countermand Anne’s one request to him`：破折号把「抵消」升级为「违抗」，而被点名违抗的请求就是那一句「扔掉」。

> **原句 5:** Here lay the challenge, the layered sediment of family life, six months’ worth no less daunting than six years’. In a crammed bookshelf next to the window were stacked shoeboxes full of cassette tapes labeled in Anne’s hand with dates from March, April, and May. There was no cassette tape player to be found, it must have gone with them. All the tapes would have to come along, there was no way to pick and choose between them, no way to rule them out as a whole. … Tobias couldn’t find a basis for picking and choosing, he’d lost sight of the goal or perhaps never had a clear goal, in a blind trance he placed item atop item in a mounting heap, straightening with small yanks and caresses, as if tenderly laying each discarded cloth husk in a grave.

公寓里真正堆着的不是家具，是生活的沉积：六个月的量并不比六年少。磁带没有播放器，作业不能挑，换季的衣服也不能挑；作者给出的动作只是「把一件摞到另一件上」，并把它写成一次安葬。

**关键词**：layered sediment / no way to pick and choose / laying each discarded cloth husk in a grave

**为什么这样写**：一个判断词 `unsortable` 就把取舍取消了：书、磁带、作业都一样，既不能整体否决，就只能整体带走。写法落在动作的语法里——`he’d lost sight of the goal or perhaps never had a clear goal`，两个并列项一个承认失焦、一个否认曾有过目标；于是 `in a blind trance` 里那堆东西既不是完成也不是放弃，而是一串 `straightening with small yanks and caresses`：用力气和抚摸做完的埋葬。

**读者视角提示**：本块开头是 `“You honor me,” he told the man`：管理员正因这一句彻底放下戒心。人物关系的松动与劳动的加重发生在同一个动作里。

> **原句 6:** I had a weird dream. I was at the beach with my dad but it was really pretty, not like beaches here. We were standing on the beach but at the same time we were standing on top of a hill with the water below us. … there was something sticking up out there, small like the size of a mushroom. But it was getting closer until I could see that it looked like a tower of water. The water in the tower rushed up like a waterfall turned upside down. The tower moved closer and closer, gliding the way people walk if they’re wearing a skirt that’s too long that you can’t see their feet. I’d never seen anything like that before. The tower got louder the closer it got, making a noise like a storm. Then I was in the water and I could feel how the tower was sucking me in. I tried to scream but I couldn’t make noise, and tried to swim but couldn’t move. The ocean was roaring and shaking like when a train passes. … Then my mom woke me up and said, There was just an earthquake, did you feel it? It felt like when a train passes by. And that’s how I remembered the dream. The first second I woke up, I couldn’t remember. Now I remember it all as if I had been there.

一个孩子的梦中，海滩高踞而不是平铺；水从水面下冲上来，越来越近，最后把她吸进去、连声音也吞掉。叫醒她的不是她自己，是「妈妈摇醒」加一句「刚才地震，你感觉到了吗」——梦的入口是一句别人递过来的理由。

**关键词**：a weird dream / the tower was sucking me in / as if I had been there

**为什么这样写**：这一页被当作整段来写，句子短，比喻全是孩子调得动的现成件：`like a wall coming up from the water`、`gliding the way people walk if they’re wearing a skirt that’s too long`。最要紧的是 `like when a train passes`——它先出现在梦里，醒后又原样出现在那句解释里，于是「地震」在文本层面被证明是事后贴上去的标签。结尾用两拍收：`The first second I woke up, I couldn’t remember.` 接 `Now I remember it all as if I had been there.`

**读者视角提示**：紧接着的一段说 `Nothing about the account suggested why it had been removed from its notebook and hidden.`——这页梦解释了「把东西搬回去」这件事的性质：他搬的不是遗产，是一段还在被人接着写的东西。

> **原句 7:** By the time Tobias’s flight lifted off it was October 11, 1978, one week into his twentieth year, and more than four weeks into the time he’d intended to spend helping Anne in Los Angeles. One thing after another had seemed to conspire to delay his departure, people and places he’d been unable to part from without visiting one last time; things, most often insignificant, to which he’d paid tender tribute. The weathered shrines by the roadside; the sample soups, grown congealed and buzzing with flies, in the noodle house windows; the tattered plastic tents within which wizened men tended wizened bonsai. He’d found himself suffering the sensation of leaving a homeland, he who’d felt no such thing for the actual homeland he’d left. … That harrowing day he’d seen Louisa inert on the cottony slab of her hospital bed, he’d thought of the wisdom-struck faces of the young Egyptian dead, painted, as if to serve as a wordless unspeakable label, on the face-ends of their mummies. He had stood in line the previous year with Adrian to see King Tut’s tomb contents at the Field Museum. Louisa in her hospital swaddling had not even been as expressive as an Egyptian memorial portrait. Louisa, who had defied death by drowning, had not seemed alive. … As the plane continued to struggle upward, like a senseless fish butting its head through one hostile current after another, the man peered thoughtfully in Tobias’s face, and then took his hand.

起飞的日期是 1978 年 10 月 11 日，二十岁的头一个星期。他把这次离开感觉成「离开故土」，而对真正的故土他从来没有过这种感觉；同段时间里最重的一笔记忆是躺在医院床上的 Louisa——他想的是埃及木乃伊的脸，因为活过来的那个孩子「看上去不像活着」。

**关键词**：October 11, 1978 / felt no such thing for the actual homeland / had not seemed alive

**为什么这样写**：段落情绪靠一个错位完成：`He’d found himself suffering the sensation of leaving a homeland, he who’d felt no such thing for the actual homeland he’d left.`——同一个 homeland 前后所指不同，中间隔着一个更长的定语，于是故乡落在了不属于他的地方。医院那段的比法被压平了：他想到木乃伊，不是因为苍白，而是因为 `Louisa in her hospital swaddling had not even been as expressive as an Egyptian memorial portrait`——不是太没有血色，是不如一张纪念肖像有表情。

**读者视角提示**：本块止于一只握过来的手（`the man peered thoughtfully in Tobias’s face, and then took his hand`）。此后两人反复说的那句 `We already are brave` 把这段收拾东西的路收了尾。

## 一句话总结

他以冒充的身份进入母亲的公寓，把「全部扔掉」执行成一次无从挑选的堆积；榻榻米下那页梦让他知道，这份清单已经被人接着写下去；等到飞机还在爬升，邻座老人握住他的手并跟着重复 `We already are brave`，这一章于是结束在一句用外国话说出来的「我们已经是勇敢的人」。
