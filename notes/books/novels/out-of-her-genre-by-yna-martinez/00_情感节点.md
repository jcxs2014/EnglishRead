# 《Out of Her Genre》情感节点

## 节点一：厨房战役与毙掉的咖啡馆（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 四十岁生日早晨，Yna 在三个孩子、一只失踪球鞋与冷咖啡里完成「送学遭遇战」，又在 Tim Hortons 排队时亲手毙掉凌晨两点写下的「咖啡馆相遇」开场——把女主角空投进阴影森林。全书的故事规则由此立下：安全网都要被剥掉。

**关键引语：**

> ① "No. Absolutely not. … She stared at the screen in pure writer's horror. She had written her protagonist and Thaerin meeting across a polished table in a quaint village café. … “A café table?” she muttered under her breath, shaking her head. “That's from her world, Martinez. What were you thinking?”"（ch01）

> ② "They couldn't meet in some civilized tavern over tea. She had to strip away the safety nets. Her protagonist needed to be thrown straight into a hostile, shadowed forest on her very first night in this foreign world—freezing, disoriented, and about to get eaten by roaming predators when Thaerin finds her."（ch01）

## 节点二：四十层的机器与街角的噪音（ch02）

**叙事概括：** Kai Rhodes 的世界按分钟计费：毙掉「安全」的提案、拒绝备车、步行两个街区——他在银行与干洗店之间的 Tim Hortons 遇见了一个喊血神战号、敲桌如鼓点的乱发女人。他二十年的商务会议里没有过这种条目，而她推门离开时，他漏了一拍。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Kai Rhodes’s life was a carefully engineered machine, and he had built himself to be the only person capable of maintaining it. … At thirty-eight, he was the founder of Rhodes Creative Group, a powerful branding and publishing firm that occupied the top floors of a downtown tower. In his world, noise was a sign of incompetence, chaos was a failure of planning, and emotions were a waste of billable hours."（ch02）

> ② "“Time to find out if caffeine can legally resurrect the dead,” she muttered. The words were low, almost lost to the ambient noise of the shop, but Kai caught them. His eyes flicked toward her, barely a heartbeat—a momentary lapse in his usual composure—before he pushed through the glass door and stepped out into the street. … The cool morning air hit him, crisp and clean. He took a sip of his coffee. … Black. Bitter. Predictable. … Exactly the way he liked everything."（ch02）

**关键引语：**

> ① "A short while later, after ordering a round of spicy Caesars, Yna stood up from the booth. “Excuse me, ladies. Washroom run.” … At the exact same moment, Kai stood up from his bar stool, intending to head down the same narrow hallway. … They turned the corner at the same time. … Thump."（ch03）

> ③ "“It's loud, isn't it?” Yna asked over the noise, her eyes sparkling with lighthearted amusement. … Kai turned his head slowly, his dark eyes locking onto hers, visibly caught off guard by her sudden proximity. … “It's... unstructured,” Kai admitted softly, his low voice barely carrying over the laughter at the table. … Yna let out a light chuckle. “Unstructured is my specialty. > ④ "And before he could even form a response, Yna had already turned right back to the table, grabbing another spicy wing and diving headfirst into Jaemee’s story about her gym trainer—completely unaware of the fact that Kai Rhodes was sitting right beside her, completely unable to take his eyes off her."（ch03）

**关键引语：**
