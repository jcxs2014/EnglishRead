# 情感节点

## 节点一：灯泡闪（ch01）——求神记号

Cyrus 向上帝求一个眨眼，灯泡闪了一下：神迹还是烂电路？全书的问从此开始：没见过神迹的人怎么信。

> **①** "Maybe it was that Cyrus had done the wrong drugs in the right order, or the right drugs in the wrong order, but when God finally spoke back to him after twenty-seven years of silence, what Cyrus wanted more than anything else was a do-over. Clarification."

> **②** "How was it fair to celebrate those guys for faith that wasn't faith at all, that was just obedience to what they plainly observed to be true?"

## 节点二：martyr 命名（ch03）——AA 之夜说出想死

AA 分享变檄文，Gabe 逼问"人物要什么"剥出"I want to die"，再剥出"rounding error"。"martyr"一词落地。

> **③** "My mom died for nothing. A rounding error. She had to share her death with three hundred other people."

> **④** "I'm not that certain of anything. I'm not that certain of gravity."

## 节点三：655 档案（ch04–ch05）——尘与鹅

Roya 的死从比喻变成档案：Like a goose，turned into dust，66/67。Cyrus 的幸存者内疚确诊为数学。

> **⑤** "Shortly after takeoff, Roya's plane was destroyed by a U.S. Navy boat. Just shot out of the sky. Like a goose."

> **⑥** "There were sixty-six children killed on board Iran Air Flight 655. It would have been sixty-seven."

## 节点四：book 立项（ch09）——周二之夜

290/289 actuarial，bounty 机票，Zee 檄文，Orkideh 展讯（推文）。后视镜结束，纽约在前。

> **⑦** "The difference between 290 dead and 289. It's actuarial. Not even tragic, you know?"

> **⑧** "He called the money the U.S. had paid his father for his mother's death her "bounty.""

## 节点五：earth martyrs（ch12）——Day 1 受洗命名

Cyrus 带 one good death 来，Orkideh 回 another cliché + earth martyrs。出馆开水浇头：烫、懵、活着。

> **⑨** "You're talking about earth martyrs."

> **⑩** "Another death-obsessed Iranian man?"

## 节点六：此刻（ch20/ch24）——Day 2–3 交心

Won't grow old + mattering now（按手），grief diamond + token 公式（美国式 grief）。Cyrus 感到一切，除了说出来。

> **⑪** "We won't grow old together, Cyrus. But can't you feel this mattering? Right now?"

> **⑫** "It seems very American to expect grief to change something. Like a token you cash in."

## 节点七：决裂（ch29）——Day 3 夜与 Zee 吵翻

pizza 喜剧→床→烟悲剧。cum on chest 处刑 vacuum，mattering 宣言赢辩论输人，hallway light 照不认识。

> **⑬** "You know I have your cum on my chest, right? Like, right now this second? While you opine about how nobody will care if you kill yourself?"

> **⑭** "I actually want to make a difference. I want my having-been-alive to matter."

## 节点八：死讯（ch35）——Day 4 风车停

尿床、$40 Sorry、Miserere 进城、velvet rope（Art survives）、pills 八卦、collapse。Cyrus 从地上起来，Sang 电话进来。

> **⑮** "Art is where what we survive survives."

> **⑯** "A meaningless life meant a meaningless death."

## 节点九：相认与重逢（ch40–ch45）——妈与爱

Was Orkideh my mother？（子弹穿瓷）→Roya 自白（三名一生）→讣告 demand→重逢 room→wilding（黑骑金池）。爱是 step 现的房。

> **⑰** "Was Orkideh my mother? It came out of his mouth like a bullet shredding through porcelain."

> **⑱** "I missed Leila in my body. I missed Cyrus in time."

> **⑲** "Love was a room that appeared when you stepped into it."
