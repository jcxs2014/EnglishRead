# 《The Hexing Miss Helena》情感节点

## 节点一：地毯上的血泊（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书的情感起点不是相遇，而是一具尸体和一口不体面的气。辛西娅推开书房的门，看见丈夫面朝下趴在奥比松地毯上，血积成一汪深红；她攒了十年的怒火刚找到对象，人却已经死了。警察问的不是丧亲的话，而是她是不是希望丈夫死——而她真的答了。

**关键引语：**

> ① "He was sprawled face down on the thick Aubusson rug. Blood pooled around him like a large crimson lake."（ch01）

> ② "If she did feel a breath of liberation at his demise, it was only because she no longer had to pretend her society marriage was whole and happy. Or that she was whole and happy, for that matter."（ch01）

> ③ "‘Lady Burrows, it gives me no pleasure to ask, but I must. Is there any reason you might want your husband dead?’"（ch01）

## 节点二：读不到的心思（ch02-04）

**叙事概括：** 布莱尔家的海伦娜奉警司之命上门，本该靠读心术直接读出凶手；结果她伸出去的手碰到辛西娅，只摸到一片刺骨的冷和一道窜进来的电流。这是全书第一次交手，也是她们此后所有纠缠的起点——越读不到，越放不下。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Which is why she heeded her uncle’s request. If Clio couldn’t speak to the ghost and determine who killed him, then Helena would steal the thoughts from Cynthia’s head and learn if Lady Burrows was a murderess."（ch02）

> ② "But that same breathless anxiety set in the moment Helena reached out with her powers to read Cynthia’s thoughts and heard… nothing."（ch03）

> ③ "Taking Cynthia’s hand was like plunging her own into a frigid lake and holding onto an electric eel. A current of power tore through Helena, but she refused to let go. Not until she broke through Cynthia’s barrier."（ch04）

## 节点三：守寡的第一夜（ch05-06）

**叙事概括：** 丈夫死了，礼数还把她按在原位；她却在守寡的第一个夜里算了一笔账：既然社会要把她从高台上拉下来，她宁愿自己跳下来摔个痛快。而布莱尔家那张祖传餐桌上，另一个女人正把水搅成漩涡，法力在她身上一次也没能像她预期的那样起作用。

**关键引语：**

> ① "‘Not any more. If society wants to pull me from the pedestal they forced me to stand upon, then I’m going to bloody well enjoy the fall. I’m not the perfect lady my parents raised, nor am I the innocent bride Hubert married.’"（ch05）

> ② "But on the first night of her widowhood, soaking up the silver moonlight, summer’s midnight song serenading her soul as her perfectly ordered life fantastically fractured, she started to remember."（ch06）

> ③ "She was brought back to the present as Helena reached for the cup and saucer. Their fingers brushed together. Fireflies erupted in Cynthia’s bloodstream, racing through her body and leaving trails of light behind."（ch06）

## 节点四：魔鬼的俱乐部（ch09-11）

**叙事概括：** 一封催债信把辛西娅推到七针区那家俱乐部的门前；赌厅里的镜面墙、烟雾与筹码之间，她第一次直面把杀人当生意算账的辛克莱。而海伦娜在那一瞬间只闪过一个词：我的。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Mine."（ch09）

> ② "‘But know this, your friend might be dangerous, but I am death incarnate. Do not fuck with me. You will not survive the experience.’"（ch10）

> ③ "‘If I killed everyone ’oo fell behind in their payments, London’s ruling class would be culled, as would me coffers. A wise businessman doesn’t eliminate an avenue of income without due cause. And your ’usband was a very lucrative avenue of income. … Only a fool would kill a cash cow like ’ubert Burrows.’"（ch11）

## 节点五：底片、坦白与亚麻壁橱（ch12-13）

**叙事概括：** 辛克莱要的价码是照片和随叫随到；辛西娅在暗处先把话说出口，然后照他的要求摆出姿态，再把底片泡烂。可真正让她崩掉的不是这些，而是同车回家的那段路：她讲出七岁那年和小女孩一起躲进亚麻壁橱的往事，讲到「我里面的一切都活了过来」。

**关键引语：**

> ① "‘I don’t find your attraction to women twisted in the slightest. And the only desires I fear are my own. I’ve never wanted anyone the way I want you.’ She stepped back. Helena ached at the loss of her warmth."（ch12）

> ② "But when Cynthia made a promise, she did not renege. ‘I was thinking that your hair is like a blazing sunset. Or the glory of autumn. And I wanted to taste you.’"（ch13）

> ③ "Clara and I hid together in a linen cupboard. I remember the smell of starch and pears. She pressed her body against mine in the small space and everything in me came alive. It was then I knew."（ch13）

## 节点六：客厅里的那个吻（ch14-15）

**叙事概括：** 晚宴上，两句「我来保护你」撞在一起，辛西娅用一声「停」把满桌人冻住。几天后的客厅里，话题从「重新做人」滑到「你很特别、很神秘、非常非常美」，再滑到那个被她抢先说出口的请求；而两个名字第一次被并排叫出来。

**关键引语：**

> ① "‘I am sick to death of men telling me what I can and cannot do. I have spent my entire life following other people’s orders. My husband is dead. I am finally free. I won’t be dictated to by anyone.’"（ch14）

> ② "‘I would very much like to kiss you, Helena Blair. If you would allow me such liberties.’ It was glorious to take the lead, even if she wasn’t quite sure where they were going."（ch15）

> ③ "‘Cyn and Hel. What a pair we make.’"（ch15）

## 节点七：利珀特家与公爵夫人的邀约（ch16-19）

**叙事概括：** 探到的地址把她们带到切普赛德：一条扑向哈姆雷特的野狗，逼出辛西娅掌心里自己跑出来的电；随后才是那间逼仄的屋子——一个父亲、一个女儿、一桩说不出口的旧事。海伦娜的杀意两次顶到「不做伤害」那条誓的边上，辛西娅却当场变成了另一个人：她提出补偿，掏出钱。几天后公爵夫人把话说得更直白：来我的舞会，带上你的情人，做一次出格的事。

**关键引语：**

> ① "‘He wants the riches your potions bring. He needs your power. That is not love. You are making love spells. I saw the ingredients. I know what that means. It isn’t right to steal someone’s will. You are breaking our most sacred oath for him. We do no harm.’"（ch16）

> ② "‘Stop!’ Cynthia screamed, throwing her hand out towards the dog. A snap of energy flew from her palm to the dog. He jerked back, caught on an invisible tether. His muscles quivered, and his lip snarled, showing sharp white teeth. He couldn’t break free as he strained against what held him. An invisible leash she controlled by some miracle."（ch17）

> ③ "Bring your paramour with you to my party. I dare you. Do something inappropriate, for once. You might find you quite like rebellion."（ch19）

## 节点八：灵魂交织，与「我是诅咒」（ch20-21）

**叙事概括：** 魔宠选了两个人，罗温姨妈给出的解释是灵魂被织在一起，分不开；海伦娜被吓退，辛西娅却在一个夜里把界限推到了尽头。欢愉之后是坦白：母亲与铁匠被村民锁在屋里烧死的那一年，她七岁，她的水变成了雾——她说自己是个诅咒。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Those witches are bound to each other. Separate souls woven together as one. It’s possible Hamlet chose both you and Cynthia because your spirits are intertwined and he can’t devote himself to one without also committing to the other."（ch20）

> ② "‘I cannot be your spirit match. We can be friends. Even friends who sometimes seek pleasure in each other’s company. But our souls cannot be intertwined. It’s impossible.’"（ch20）

> ③ "Love twisted her into something cold and cruel. I can’t risk becoming like that, Cynthia. I won’t."（ch21）

## 节点九：被推开的门，与被抽干水的房间（ch22-23）

**叙事概括：** 辛西娅在这场谈话结束之后看清了自己：她爱上一个不肯爱她的人，而她是个傻瓜；她没有争辩，转身推门走了出去。但她没有塌掉——她把守寡的宅子改成收容需要帮助的女人的地方，把力量练到能挪动一整只橡木衣柜。同一时间，海伦娜把自己埋进药房的班表里，而辛克莱的一只手按在了她后脑勺上。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I’ve fallen in love. With a woman who refuses to love me back. I’m such a fool!"（ch22）

> ② "So, I am going to put my dowager house to good use. I am turning it into a home for women in need. I hope to offer education and training for those who wish to improve their circumstances."（ch22）

> ③ "Her element was water. It was where she drew her strength and fuelled her magic. And the Devil of Seven Dials had somehow constructed a room that stole every last drop."（ch23）

## 节点十：浪尖上的女神，与红裙舞会（ch24-27）

**叙事概括：** 抽干水的房间里，她在濒死时终于承认自己早已爱上对方；而另一个女人在两件裙子之间挑了那件旅行装，坐马车赶到五铃街，用自己的血解开那道封住力量的咒，把三个打手举到半空。子弹出膛的一瞬，海伦娜踏着一道浪升起来。醒来之后是月牙形的伤疤、当着警司撒下的谎、一个被放走的真凶，以及最后那首只放给两个人听的曲子。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Because as she faced the stark reality of death, she could no longer hide from the truth. She had fallen in love. … Despite seeing the devastation love left in its wake."（ch24）

> ② "The last thing she saw before the world went black was Helena, rising on a wave. A goddess. Her goddess. Come to take her away."（ch25）

> ③ "Helena rested her hands on Cynthia’s hips. Cynthia wrapped her arms around Helena’s shoulders, and they swayed to a melody playing only for them as Hamlet stood sentinel, protecting the two women whose hearts beat as one."（ch27）
