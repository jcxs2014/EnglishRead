# 《The Hexing Miss Helena》概述

## 全书梗概

**一具尸体，和一口不体面的气。** 辛西娅·布尔洛斯推开书房的门，看见丈夫休伯特面朝下趴在奥比松地毯上，血积成一汪深红。她攒了十年的怒火刚找到对象，人却已经死了；同一天，警司拉克兰·麦克杜格尔就站在书房里，问的不是丧亲的话，而是她是不是希望丈夫死。十年里她唯一敢承认的那点快意，就藏在这句里："If she did feel a breath of liberation at his demise, it was only because she no longer had to pretend her society marriage was whole and happy. Or that she was whole and happy, for that matter."（ch01）

**查案的人是她丈夫的债主。** 休伯特生前欠下七针区那位“魔鬼”的债，催债信上的措辞不留情面；达米恩·辛克莱把杀人当生意算账，理由冷静得让人发冷："‘If I killed everyone ’oo fell behind in their payments, London’s ruling class would be culled, as would me coffers. A wise businessman doesn’t eliminate an avenue of income without due cause. And your ’usband was a very lucrative avenue of income. … Only a fool would kill a cash cow like ’ubert Burrows.’"（ch11） 而真凶不是流窜在外的陌生人——她一直在这个家里，端茶倒水，看着女主人一步步查到自己头上。

**她们相遇的方式，是一次失败的读心。** 布莱尔家的海伦娜奉警司之命上门，本该靠读心术直接读出凶手；结果她伸出去的手碰到辛西娅，只摸到一片刺骨的冷和一道窜进来的电流："Taking Cynthia’s hand was like plunging her own into a frigid lake and holding onto an electric eel. A current of power tore through Helena, but she refused to let go. Not until she broke through Cynthia’s barrier."（ch04） 这是全书第一次交手，也是她们此后所有纠缠的起点——越读不到，越放不下。

**守寡第一夜，她决定不再当那尊雕塑。** 丈夫死了，礼数还把她按在原位；她却在月光下算了一笔账：既然社会要把她从高台上拉下来，她宁愿自己跳下来摔个痛快："‘Not any more. If society wants to pull me from the pedestal they forced me to stand upon, then I’m going to bloody well enjoy the fall. I’m not the perfect lady my parents raised, nor am I the innocent bride Hubert married.’"（ch05） 从这一夜起，她做的每一件事都不再是为了守规矩，而是为了活得像个活人。

**价码是照片。** 辛克莱要的从来不是钱，是把柄：几张照片，再加上一根随时能勒住她叔叔的绳。而辛西娅给出的回答是“我唯一怕的是自己的欲望”："‘I don’t find your attraction to women twisted in the slightest. And the only desires I fear are my own. I’ve never wanted anyone the way I want you.’ She stepped back. Helena ached at the loss of her warmth."（ch12） 她当场毁掉了底片，也把自己交到了这个人手里。

**探到一个父亲，也探到自己身上的东西。** 地址是希金斯太太给的：切普赛德一间又挤又脏的屋子，住着被休伯特伤害过的女佣和她的父亲。马车里两人还在说笑，一条扑向哈姆雷特的野狗却让辛西娅的手自己放出了电："‘Stop!’ Cynthia screamed, throwing her hand out towards the dog. A snap of energy flew from her palm to the dog. He jerked back, caught on an invisible tether. His muscles quivered, and his lip snarled, showing sharp white teeth. He couldn’t break free as he strained against what held him. An invisible leash she controlled by some miracle."（ch17） 在此之前她以为自己只是个被封住力量的普通人；这一下之后，两个人看彼此的眼神都变了。

**两个人原来是同一种人。** 魔宠哈姆雷特选了两个女主人，罗温姨妈给出的解释是：灵魂被织在一起，分不开。"Those witches are bound to each other. Separate souls woven together as one. It’s possible Hamlet chose both you and Cynthia because your spirits are intertwined and he can’t devote himself to one without also committing to the other."（ch20） 到这一步，她们要的已经不只是一桩案子的答案，而是一份不被允许的关系。

**母亲、炉火，和那句自我判刑。** 海伦娜七岁那年，母亲与铁匠被村民锁在屋里烧死；她一直认定那把火是自己招来的，认定自己的天赋是女神落下的惩罚："I’m the reason she died. It’s because of me the villagers swarmed my mother’s cottage, locked her inside with the blacksmith, and burned them to cinders. A firewitch consumed by mortal flames."（ch21） 她把爱说得那样危险，正因为她亲眼见过爱会把人变成什么样子。

**最低点是那扇被推开的门。** 辛西娅在这场谈话结束之后看清了自己：她已经爱上那个不肯爱她的人，而她是个傻瓜。"I’ve fallen in love. With a woman who refuses to love me back. I’m such a fool!"（ch22） 她没有多说，转身推门走了出去。但她没有塌掉：她把守寡的宅子改成收容需要帮助的女人的地方，把自己的力量练到能挪动一整只橡木衣柜。

**然后是那间专门为水造出来的牢房。** 辛克莱掳走海伦娜，关进一个把每一滴水都抽干的房间："Her element was water. It was where she drew her strength and fuelled her magic. And the Devil of Seven Dials had somehow constructed a room that stole every last drop."（ch23） 她在濒死时才承认自己早已爱上对方；而救她的人用自己的血解开了那道封住力量的咒，把三个打手举到了半空。

**最后是红裙与新的誓约。** 中枪之后，辛西娅当着警司的面撒了一个谎，把真凶从绞架上护了下来；海伦娜则用一句全新的誓约替这段关系重新命名："‘Cynthia, I will love you with my body. Seek you with my mind. Honour you with my spirit. And provide your heart sanctuary for the rest of my days.’"（ch27） 上流社会的舞厅里，煤气灯、钻石与一首只放给两个人听的曲子，把这一整本书收在她们终于不必再向谁请示的位置上。

## 三个主题

**一、誓约与破誓。** 布莱尔家的女人都立过不做伤害的誓——那是海伦娜身上最贵的东西："She was a light witch. She never used her magic for violence. She had taken an oath with her coven to do no harm."（ch18） 同样的誓也被人破过：海伦娜的母亲为了留住铁匠做了让人爱上他的药；海伦娜自己又加了一道更重的誓——永远不要爱上任何人。全书真正的问题不是「谁破了誓」，而是「什么值得破」——她最后用一句新的誓约，把旧的那道换掉了。

**二、读心与坦白。** 海伦娜这一行的本事是读心，而全书最重要的一次读心，恰恰是空的："But that same breathless anxiety set in the moment Helena reached out with her powers to read Cynthia’s thoughts and heard… nothing."（ch03） 于是故事只能靠另一种方式往前推——两个人一遍遍把心里的话说出来：说出欲望，说出母亲的火，说出「我是诅咒」，说出新的誓词。全书越往后，魔法越让位于开口。

**三、头衔与真名。** 她从「布尔洛斯夫人」变成「辛西娅」，从一尊被摆好的家具变成一个会自己决定去留的人：
- "She was a sapphist. A witch. And a powerful woman who would no longer be subject to the whims of others."（ch22）
- 到最后，她既不做某个人的夫人，也不只做某个人的朋友；她要的是一个从来没人给过她的东西——自己说了算。"Cynthia shook her head. ‘I’m not Lady Burrows any more. And you are no longer a housekeeper. We are both free, Mrs Higgins.’"（ch27）

## 人物弧光

**辛西娅·布尔洛斯（肯特米尔侯爵夫人）**——

- 起点："If she did feel a breath of liberation at his demise, it was only because she no longer had to pretend her society marriage was whole and happy. Or that she was whole and happy, for that matter."（ch01）
- 转向："‘Not any more. If society wants to pull me from the pedestal they forced me to stand upon, then I’m going to bloody well enjoy the fall. I’m not the perfect lady my parents raised, nor am I the innocent bride Hubert married.’"（ch05）
- 最低点："Pushing open the door, Cynthia stepped out of the room and into her future. One she would face alone."（ch22）
- 落点："Helena rested her hands on Cynthia’s hips. Cynthia wrapped her arms around Helena’s shoulders, and they swayed to a melody playing only for them as Hamlet stood sentinel, protecting the two women whose hearts beat as one."（ch27）
- 她真正的转折不是查清了案子，而是发现“被允许”从来都不是问题；她要的从来不是许可，是不必再问。

**海伦娜·布莱尔**——

- 起点："Which is why she heeded her uncle’s request. If Clio couldn’t speak to the ghost and determine who killed him, then Helena would steal the thoughts from Cynthia’s head and learn if Lady Burrows was a murderess."（ch02）
- 转向："She was a light witch. She never used her magic for violence. She had taken an oath with her coven to do no harm."（ch18）
- 最低点："Because as she faced the stark reality of death, she could no longer hide from the truth. She had fallen in love. … Despite seeing the devastation love left in its wake."（ch24）
- 落点："‘Cynthia, I will love you with my body. Seek you with my mind. Honour you with my spirit. And provide your heart sanctuary for the rest of my days.’"（ch27）
- 她的整条弧线是“从不伤害”到“以伤害护人”，而最后用一句新誓约替两者同时收尾——她并没有放弃不做伤害，只是重新定义了被保护的人是谁。

**希金斯太太**——

- 起点："Mrs Higgins looked from Cynthia to Helena and back again. ‘Of course Lily’s father is George Lippert. I’ve known Georgie since we were little mites in leading strings. His mother, God rest her, was a dear friend to my own mother. It was my doing that got Lily here in the first place.’"（ch16）
- 中段："‘She knew what Hubert had been doing to the maids. She caught him strangling one of the poor girls…’ Her throat tightened and the tears she had kept at bay broke free. ‘I couldn’t tell your uncle. She doesn’t deserve to hang for doing what I should have done all along. She stopped him, Helena.’"（ch26）
- 落点："Cynthia shook her head. ‘I’m not Lady Burrows any more. And you are no longer a housekeeper. We are both free, Mrs Higgins.’"（ch27）
- 她是这个家里唯一从头到尾都在看的人；她杀人不为利，只为让那个掐住女仆脖子的手停下来，而最后放她走的人，是那个她服务过的女主人。

**哈姆雷特**——

- 起点："Hamlet, her familiar and constant companion, twitched his fluffy copper tail. His left ear flicked back, and he tipped up his head, his black nose twitching. The strange clicking growl, indicative of foxes, never ceased to charm Helena."（ch02）
- 转折："Those witches are bound to each other. Separate souls woven together as one. It’s possible Hamlet chose both you and Cynthia because your spirits are intertwined and he can’t devote himself to one without also committing to the other."（ch20）
- 落点："Helena rested her hands on Cynthia’s hips. Cynthia wrapped her arms around Helena’s shoulders, and they swayed to a melody playing only for them as Hamlet stood sentinel, protecting the two women whose hearts beat as one."（ch27）
- 全书最短也最笃定的一个角色：起手他只属于海伦娜一个人，后来发现自己的心分给了两个，于是从此站在两个人中间——结尾处他仍在放哨。
