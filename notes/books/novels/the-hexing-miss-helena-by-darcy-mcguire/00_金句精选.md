# 《The Hexing Miss Helena》金句精选

① "If she did feel a breath of liberation at his demise, it was only because she no longer had to pretend her society marriage was whole and happy. Or that she was whole and happy, for that matter."（ch01）

**中文**：如果她在他死时确实松了一口气，那也只是因为她不必再假装这桩门当户对的婚姻圆满如意，也不必再假装自己圆满如意。
**上下文**：ch01 尸体刚被发现，她对着警察承认了自己那点见不得人的如释重负。
**为什么重要**：全书的起点不是悲伤，而是一次承认——读者由此知道这桩婚姻里真正发生过什么。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘Hubert’s death.’ Cynthia didn’t realise the truth until she spoke it. ‘That’s when things started happening. I think being released from that vow altered something within me. I’ve always been trapped by someone else’s rules. When Hubert died, that changed. A widow is beholden to no one but herself.’"（ch20）「丈夫一死，她就不再欠任何人」同源。

② "Cynthia might have stolen Clio’s time and Ellie’s goodwill, but Helena would invite the fires of hell to burn every drop of water within her before she would see the damnable woman steal her familiar’s affections."（ch03）

**中文**：辛西娅也许抢走了克莱奥的时间、埃莉的好感，但海伦娜宁可让地狱之火烧干身上每一滴水，也不肯看那个女人夺走自己魔宠的宠爱。
**上下文**：ch03 辛西娅第一次登门，海伦娜在门厅里就已经在嫉妒。
**为什么重要**：嫉妒写在这里不是小姐脾气，而是全书唯一一次把「占有」写在「保护」前面。
**呼应关系**：与 "Her mother’s love had been a desperate thing. An all-consuming whirlpool sucking everything into its depths. And the blacksmith’s desire for Willow had twisted, warped, and destroyed her."（ch23）母亲那场吞噬一切的爱，是同一形状的反面。

③ "Taking Cynthia’s hand was like plunging her own into a frigid lake and holding onto an electric eel. A current of power tore through Helena, but she refused to let go. Not until she broke through Cynthia’s barrier."（ch04）

**中文**：握住辛西娅的手，像把自己按进冰冷的湖里、还攥着一条电鳗；一道电流撕过海伦娜全身，但她不肯松手，直到冲破辛西娅的屏障。
**上下文**：ch04 她第一次尝试读心，用的是最直接的办法。
**为什么重要**：全书所有第一次接触都写成了这一句的变体——危险、疼痛、却不肯放手。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘I would very much like to kiss you, Helena Blair. If you would allow me such liberties.’ It was glorious to take the lead, even if she wasn’t quite sure where they were going."（ch15）客厅里那句「我很想吻你」是同一条温度曲线。

④ "‘Not any more. If society wants to pull me from the pedestal they forced me to stand upon, then I’m going to bloody well enjoy the fall. I’m not the perfect lady my parents raised, nor am I the innocent bride Hubert married.’"（ch05）

**中文**：不再是了。既然社会要把我从他们逼我站上的那座基座上拉下来，那我干脆痛痛快快地享受摔下来：我不是父母教出来的完美夫人，也不是休伯特娶回家的纯洁新娘。
**上下文**：ch05 守寡第一夜，火光里她把这句话说了出来。
**为什么重要**：全书唯一一次由她自己宣布不再演那尊雕像，此后她所有的越界都从这里出发。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘I am sick to death of men telling me what I can and cannot do. I have spent my entire life following other people’s orders. My husband is dead. I am finally free. I won’t be dictated to by anyone.’"（ch14）「我受够了男人告诉我能做什么不能做什么」是同一句话的两副嗓子。

⑤ "But on the first night of her widowhood, soaking up the silver moonlight, summer’s midnight song serenading her soul as her perfectly ordered life fantastically fractured, she started to remember."（ch06）

**中文**：守寡的第一个夜晚，她泡在银色月光里，听着夏夜的歌哄她的灵魂，而她那秩序井然的人生正以一种夸张的方式碎裂开来，她开始回忆。
**上下文**：ch06 守寡的第一个夜晚，她在银色月光下开始回想这十年。
**为什么重要**：全书唯一一次把「回忆」放在叙述的正中央——出事之后先想起来的东西，才是这本书真正关心的东西。
**呼应关系**：与 "Clara and I hid together in a linen cupboard. I remember the smell of starch and pears. She pressed her body against mine in the small space and everything in me came alive. It was then I knew."（ch13）亚麻壁橱里那个七岁的孩子同源。

⑥ "If she was destined to lose everything, what harm might come from grabbing a live wire and letting the electricity run through her until she was nothing but light? Because whenever she touched Helena, that is how it felt. Like currents of power rushing through her, making her feel things she only ever imagined."（ch07）

**中文**：如果注定要失去一切，那么抓住一根带电的线、让电流穿过自己直到只剩一片光，又有什么损害？每次碰到海伦娜，她就是这种感觉。
**上下文**：ch07 她独处面对债务与旧账，中段第一次承认自己身上的异状。
**为什么重要**：全书最直白的一次自毁倾向，而「像电流一样」正是女巫之间才有的生理事实。
**呼应关系**：与 "Taking Cynthia’s hand was like plunging her own into a frigid lake and holding onto an electric eel. A current of power tore through Helena, but she refused to let go. Not until she broke through Cynthia’s barrier."（ch04）那一次电流是同一个比喻的两次出现。

⑦ "The deadline has passed. Fulfil your bargain or pay your debt in full. Breaking a deal with the Devil carries profound consequences."（ch07）

**中文**：期限已过，履行你的交易，或者连本带利地付清。与魔鬼做买卖，失约的后果深重。
**上下文**：ch07 一封没有署名的催债信落到她手里。
**为什么重要**：全书的时限从此开始计时——它把一桩本来没有期限的悬案变成了有价格的买卖。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘If I killed everyone ’oo fell behind in their payments, London’s ruling class would be culled, as would me coffers. A wise businessman doesn’t eliminate an avenue of income without due cause. And your ’usband was a very lucrative avenue of income. … Only a fool would kill a cash cow like ’ubert Burrows.’"（ch11）他承认休伯特是「一头现金奶牛」，是同一笔账的两面。

⑧ "Her job was to be beautiful, obedient, and fertile. She failed in the last aspect, and if she did not excel in the first two obligations, he would divorce her. It was a fate worse than death for any lady of the beau monde."（ch08）

**中文**：她的本分是漂亮、顺从和多生。她在最后一项上失败，而前两项若做不好，他就会和她离婚——那是上流社会的女人宁可去死也不愿面对的命运。
**上下文**：ch08 她被迫回忆这桩婚姻给自己定下的规格。
**为什么重要**：全书对「体面」最冷的一份说明书，后面她推翻的每一条都记在这张清单上。
**呼应关系**：与 "She was a sapphist. A witch. And a powerful woman who would no longer be subject to the whims of others."（ch22）「她不再受制于任何人的任性」正面相对。

⑨ "Mine."（ch09）

**中文**：我的。
**上下文**：ch09 俱乐部的人请辛西娅上楼，海伦娜在楼梯口只闪过这一个词。
**为什么重要**：全书最短的一句引语，也是占有欲第一次抢在理智前面开口；说出口的不是辛西娅，是海伦娜。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘I don’t find your attraction to women twisted in the slightest. And the only desires I fear are my own. I’ve never wanted anyone the way I want you.’ She stepped back. Helena ached at the loss of her warmth."（ch12）「我唯一怕的是自己的欲望」是同一件事的两面——一个是占有，一个是自知。

⑩ "‘I don’t find your attraction to women twisted in the slightest. And the only desires I fear are my own. I’ve never wanted anyone the way I want you.’ She stepped back. Helena ached at the loss of her warmth."（ch12）

**中文**：我不觉得你爱女人这件事有任何扭曲，我唯一怕的是我自己的欲望——我从没有像想要你这样想要过任何人。
**上下文**：ch12 照相之前，两人在暗处单独说话。
**为什么重要**：全书最坦白的一次示爱，也是唯一一次两人都没有逃开的地方。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘I cannot be your spirit match. We can be friends. Even friends who sometimes seek pleasure in each other’s company. But our souls cannot be intertwined. It’s impossible.’"（ch20）「灵魂不能交织」构成全书最锋利的一组正反。

⑪ "Clara and I hid together in a linen cupboard. I remember the smell of starch and pears. She pressed her body against mine in the small space and everything in me came alive. It was then I knew."（ch13）

**中文**：克拉拉和我一起躲在亚麻壁橱里。我记得淀粉和梨子的气味。她在那个小空间里紧贴着我的身体，我里面的一切都活了过来。就是那一刻我知道了。
**上下文**：ch13 马车里的坦白，她第一次讲出七岁那年的事。
**为什么重要**：全书唯一一段童年记忆，而「我知道了」三个字已经预告了她此刻的欲望。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘I was seven. Old enough to have done something. And I tried, but my powers weren’t enough to stop the fire.’"（ch21）七岁那年她没能救下火里的人，是同一个年纪的两件事。

⑫ "‘I am sick to death of men telling me what I can and cannot do. I have spent my entire life following other people’s orders. My husband is dead. I am finally free. I won’t be dictated to by anyone.’"（ch14）

**中文**：我受够了男人告诉我能做什么、不能做什么。我一辈子都在听别人的吩咐。我丈夫死了，我自由了，谁也别想再支配我。
**上下文**：ch14 晚宴桌上，她把这句话甩了出去。
**为什么重要**：全书她第一次公开说「我自由了」，而说这句话的人马上要学会自由并不等于能说出口。
**呼应关系**：与 "Pushing open the door, Cynthia stepped out of the room and into her future. One she would face alone."（ch22）她推开门独自走出去，是同一句话的实践。

⑬ "‘Cyn and Hel. What a pair we make.’"（ch15）

**中文**：辛西娅与海伦娜，我们这一对可真绝配。
**上下文**：ch15 客厅里的那个吻之后，两人用一个只有彼此听得懂的称呼自居。
**为什么重要**：全书第一次把「我们」说出口，而它出现在一个谁都没有许诺以后的时刻。
**呼应关系**：与 "Helena rested her hands on Cynthia’s hips. Cynthia wrapped her arms around Helena’s shoulders, and they swayed to a melody playing only for them as Hamlet stood sentinel, protecting the two women whose hearts beat as one."（ch27）全书最后一句两人并排起舞，是同一个称呼的收尾。

⑭ "She was a light witch. She never used her magic for violence. She had taken an oath with her coven to do no harm."（ch18）

**中文**：她是光之女巫，从不用魔法伤人；她曾与自己的女巫团立下不做伤害的誓。
**上下文**：ch18 她看着辛西娅被利珀特的话刺痛，杀意第一次顶到了誓约边上。
**为什么重要**：全书最深的一次「不动手」，它让后来的破誓有了分量。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘We do no harm, Helena. Even now.’ Aunt Rowan’s words resonated along her nerve endings as her screams of vengeance became wails of grief. No longer fighting against her, Helena gripped her aunt in a desperate plea. ‘He shot Cynthia. He shot her. He shot Cynthia.’ She couldn’t stop the haunting chant."（ch26）姨妈那句「我们不做伤害，海伦娜，即使现在」是同一条誓的两端。

⑮ "But as Helena sank deeper into his mind, it was hatred for himself that eclipsed all else. Not because he had taken a man’s life. But because he hadn’t."（ch18）

**中文**：当海伦娜越往他心里沉，越是被对自己的恨意盖过一切——不是因为他取了一个人的性命，而是因为他没有取。
**上下文**：ch18 她读利珀特的脑子，看见的不是杀意而是悔恨。
**为什么重要**：全书用一句反向的否定写尽了复仇者的资格：一个父亲动手的机会，被他自己错过了。
**呼应关系**：与 "Sinclair nodded. His cruel smile widened as his finger tightened on the trigger. ‘That’s right, Lady Burrows. I was there the night ’e died. Mrs ’iggins got to him before Lippert or I could – a real pity. Ironic, really. Two men intent on destroying ’im, and in the end, a woman took care of it far more efficiently.’"（ch25）辛克莱笑称两人「都想毁掉他」，是同一件事的两种说法。

⑯ "Those witches are bound to each other. Separate souls woven together as one. It’s possible Hamlet chose both you and Cynthia because your spirits are intertwined and he can’t devote himself to one without also committing to the other."（ch20）

**中文**：那些女巫是彼此绑定的，各自的灵魂被织成一块；哈姆雷特也许同时选了你们两个，就是因为你们的灵魂交织在一起，他不能只忠于一个而不忠于另一个。
**上下文**：ch20 罗温姨妈解释魔宠为什么会同时认两个女主人。
**为什么重要**：这一句把「被选中」从运气改写成命运，也把全书推向不可回头的境地。
**呼应关系**：与 "Cynthia would never try to possess Helena’s soul. She didn’t need to. Because their spirits were intertwined. They were laced together in a tapestry woven before time began. And the love she felt for Cynthia could never warp or twist into something monstrous."（ch24）「你们的灵魂被织进一幅早于时间织好的挂毯」是同一句的重述。

⑰ "I’m the reason she died. It’s because of me the villagers swarmed my mother’s cottage, locked her inside with the blacksmith, and burned them to cinders. A firewitch consumed by mortal flames."（ch21）

**中文**：她死是因为我。村民们是因为我围住了母亲的屋子，把她和铁匠锁在里面，烧成了灰。一个被凡人的火烧死的火巫。
**上下文**：ch21 她在告白之后的夜里向辛西娅交代自己的过去。
**为什么重要**：全书的最低点之一，也是海伦娜所有逃避的源头。
**呼应关系**：与 "I’ve fallen in love. With a woman who refuses to love me back. I’m such a fool!"（ch22）她被爱吓退的真正理由就在这段往事里。

⑱ "‘I always thought my gift as a waterwitch was a curse from the goddess. A reminder of how I failed. I can douse any flame with no more than a flick of my fingers, and yet when my mother screamed inside the inferno that had once been our home, my water turned to mist."（ch21）

**中文**：我一直以为水巫的天赋是女神落下的诅咒，提醒我当年失败过；我能一挥手就浇灭任何火焰，可母亲在我们曾经的家里尖叫时，我的水却变成了雾。
**上下文**：ch21 她解释自己为什么从不敢承认那是天赋。
**为什么重要**：全书最精确的一次自我认识——她的力量从来没有缺席，缺席的是她的勇气。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘Only I can set my powers free.’ She had bound her magic. So, she could release it. She only needed blood. Pulling a pin from her hair, she pressed the edge to her finger, ignoring the pain as crimson welled. … Words formed on her lips like a spell. ‘With this blood, I set my powers free.’ The ground quaked beneath her feet, cracking the tile."（ch25）她用自己的血解开封印，是这一段的反面。

⑲ "I’ve fallen in love. With a woman who refuses to love me back. I’m such a fool!"（ch22）

**中文**：我恋爱了。爱上一个不肯爱我的人。我真是个傻瓜！
**上下文**：ch22 辛西娅在这场谈话之后独自想通了自己爱上了一个不肯爱她的人。
**为什么重要**：全书最坦白也最狼狈的一次自我承认；说出口的不是爱，是认输。
**呼应关系**：与 "Because as she faced the stark reality of death, she could no longer hide from the truth. She had fallen in love. … Despite seeing the devastation love left in its wake."（ch24）濒死时海伦娜终于承认自己也早已爱上对方，是同一个词在两个人身上的两次落地。

⑳ "She was a sapphist. A witch. And a powerful woman who would no longer be subject to the whims of others."（ch22）

**中文**：她是萨福式的女人，是女巫，是一个再也不必受制于他人任性的人。
**上下文**：ch22 她在新宅子里给自己下的定义。
**为什么重要**：全书对她的一次收束式命名，三样身份并列，没有一样是她出生时带来的。
**呼应关系**：与 "Her job was to be beautiful, obedient, and fertile. She failed in the last aspect, and if she did not excel in the first two obligations, he would divorce her. It was a fate worse than death for any lady of the beau monde."（ch08）那张「漂亮、顺从、多生」的清单正好相反。

㉑ "Her mother’s love had been a desperate thing. An all-consuming whirlpool sucking everything into its depths. And the blacksmith’s desire for Willow had twisted, warped, and destroyed her."（ch23）

**中文**：母亲的爱是一种绝望的东西，一个把所有东西都卷进深处的漩涡；铁匠对薇洛的欲望扭曲了、压坏了、毁掉了她。
**上下文**：ch23 她终于看清自己身上那条危险的谱系。
**为什么重要**：全书对「爱会变成什么」最冷静的一次分析，也是辛西娅日后能做出选择的原因。
**呼应关系**：与 "‘I don’t find your attraction to women twisted in the slightest. And the only desires I fear are my own. I’ve never wanted anyone the way I want you.’ She stepped back. Helena ached at the loss of her warmth."（ch12）「我唯一怕的是自己的欲望」是同一个担心在两代人身上的形状。

㉒ "Because as she faced the stark reality of death, she could no longer hide from the truth. She had fallen in love. … Despite seeing the devastation love left in its wake."（ch24）

**中文**：面对死亡这个赤裸的现实，她再也没法对真相撒谎了：她已经爱上了对方——尽管她亲眼见过爱留下的满目疮痍。
**上下文**：ch24 她被关在抽干了水的房间里，第一次承认自己的感情。
**为什么重要**：全书唯一一次把「我爱」放在生死之间说的地方。
**呼应关系**：与 "Her mother’s love had been a desperate thing. An all-consuming whirlpool sucking everything into its depths. And the blacksmith’s desire for Willow had twisted, warped, and destroyed her."（ch23）母亲的结局是同一个警告的两面。

㉓ "The last thing she saw before the world went black was Helena, rising on a wave. A goddess. Her goddess. Come to take her away."（ch25）

**中文**：世界黑下去之前，她看见的最后一样东西是海伦娜踏着一道浪升起来——一位女神，她的神女，来接她走。
**上下文**：ch25 子弹出膛的一瞬。
**为什么重要**：全书最漂亮的一个收束镜头，把复仇、救援与背叛压进同一幅画里。
**呼应关系**：与 "When I die here, goddess, let my soul linger. Let me watch over my love until her days are done. I will gladly suffer the hollow loneliness of a spectre’s existence if only I can be near my lady."（ch24）她请求死后留在世上守望爱人，是同一个愿望的两种说法。

㉔ "‘Cynthia, I will love you with my body. Seek you with my mind. Honour you with my spirit. And provide your heart sanctuary for the rest of my days.’"（ch27）

**中文**：辛西娅，我愿以我的身体爱你，以我的心念寻你，以我的灵魂敬你，并把你的心安放为我的庇护所，直到我的岁月尽头。
**上下文**：ch27 卧室里，她在新月形的伤疤上许下新的誓。
**为什么重要**：全书唯一一处用誓词格式写爱情，而它是给「不做伤害」那条旧誓让位的替代品。
**呼应关系**：与 "She was a light witch. She never used her magic for violence. She had taken an oath with her coven to do no harm."（ch18）旧誓与新誓在这里完成交接。

㉕ "Helena rested her hands on Cynthia’s hips. Cynthia wrapped her arms around Helena’s shoulders, and they swayed to a melody playing only for them as Hamlet stood sentinel, protecting the two women whose hearts beat as one."（ch27）

**中文**：海伦娜把手搭在辛西娅的腰上，辛西娅环住她的肩，两人随着一首只放给她们听的曲子摇动；哈姆雷特站在一旁放哨，守着两颗心跳如同一个的人。
**上下文**：ch27 舞会散场后的最后一段。
**为什么重要**：全书最后一句；魔宠在这里从「被争夺的对象」变回「守卫」。
**呼应关系**：与 "Mine."（ch09）那句「我的」首尾相扣，只是这一次他守的是两个人。
