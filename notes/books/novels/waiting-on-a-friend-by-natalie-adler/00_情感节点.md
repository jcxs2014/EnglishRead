# 《Waiting on a Friend》情感节点

## 节点一 · 十一小时与葬礼前夜（ch01）

开篇把死亡写成两条时间线的错位：Mark 死于 6 月 5 日深夜，她翌日早晨才接到通知——一个本该在场的人缺席了十一个小时。那一周她按十年养成的惯例满城找他：餐馆、影院、舞池、「我们的歌」；而葬礼前夜泡澡时真正显形的，是四月惨死的 François。全书的第一记重拳来自一个否定句：来的不是她等的那个人。

"Mark died of complications. Don’t we all, he would have said. June 5, 1984, 11:42 p.m., Bellevue Hospital. That night I was home."（ch01）

"I walked river to river and sat at our table at the Ukrainian place and went to the movies and sat in the back row, where we used to laugh through the whole film, and went dancing and expected him to show whenever I heard one of our songs."（ch01）

"His eyes were milky, though they were set on me. He opened his mouth wide enough for me to see his bleeding gums and he started to scream. When he vanished a moment later, the sound stayed behind."（ch01）

## 节点二 · 葬礼：无味的教堂与后排的受吊线（ch03）

Mark 的母亲把仪式办在一间「没有气味」的教堂，全程不哭；姐姐们带着防传染式的洁癖和一群被留在家的金发孩子；父亲缺席；而「我」在仪式中只想把悲伤当水泼给每一个失职的人。仪式结束后，Patrick 在后排自设吊唁线——他解开的衬衫与那句「为什么不打电话」的对峙，把葬礼变成两个人抢夺 Mark 的战场。

"She was flanked by his older sisters, who each had a brood of blond children who must have been left at home in case the dead man was contagious. The sisters hadn’t stopped talking to Mark, but Mark stopped talking to them when they tried to get him to understand that really, the whole gay thing was hard on their mother, and he should be a little more compassionate."（ch03）

"Patrick operated his own receiving line in the back of the church, like a Florida widower before a line of old ladies with a casserole and a wink. His silk shirt was unbuttoned to the last button, and even though I was pissed at him, I hoped Mark’s mother saw his bare chest and knew her son had lain his head there."（ch03）

"For a minute, I thought I saw his outline in the doorway, but then I saw what it really was: my own want, filling up the empty space."（ch03）

## 节点三 · 第一次完整显形：Mary Ellen 与守夜夜（ch14、ch21）

大学公路夜她交出「我看见了 Mary Ellen Bishop 的鬼」；多年后回看，那是她唯一一次在校园里看见全形。守夜礼的雪地上，Mary Ellen 不湿的裙摆与不留的脚印走过两派人马之间，唯一另一个看见她的活人——她的前男友 Rick——一遍遍念「你不是真的」直到把自己摔进雪里。她学到的第一课不是恐惧鬼，而是「目睹别人的痛苦太私密」。

"I said it quickly. “I saw Mary Ellen Bishop’s ghost.”"（ch14）

"He must have tried to grab her, but he landed face down in the snow. Some of the Catholics tried to help him up, but he thrashed away from everyone and stalked off away from the protest. Even in the dark, I could see she was gone."（ch21）

"It scared me. I can see that now. It wasn’t Mary Ellen herself, or even the sadness of suicide. It was witnessing her pain. It was too intimate. I felt like I had seen something I shouldn’t have, like a window had opened, and I was scared of what might crawl in."（ch21）

## 节点四 · François 的尖叫与送行（ch09、ch17）

从 ch01 章末的那声尖叫，到撒尿之夜她终于说出「你知道你已经死了吗」——François 的鬼是全书第一场完整的哀悼实习：她先是惊恐、后是愤怒（「Rest in piss」），再是给鬼递出选择权。冷库里剩下的最后一课是认罪：她当着他说出「我确实想让你走」。送别因此不是慈悲的施舍，而是两个人结算一笔账。

"“Do you know that you’re dead, François? You don’t have to hurt like this anymore. I don’t know if it’s your choice, but can you rest now? It’s okay if you want to.”"（ch09）

"I wanted to hold him but that wasn’t possible, so I whispered without stopping, as if reciting a spell, “It’s okay, honey, you were sick, you were in a coma, you didn’t know what was happening, and you died, honey, I’m so sorry, I’m sorry we couldn’t save you, I’m sorry I’m so mad at you, it’s not your fault, I’m sorry, François, I’m so sorry.”"（ch09）

"“I did, I did mean it. I’m sorry, I did want you to go. I wanted you to leave me alone.” The sentence settled around us. Terrible and true."（ch17）

## 节点五 · Patrick 之鬼：Mark 生日的夜谈（ch31）

Mark 本该三十岁的那天，Patrick 以过量的尸体重回门口——没有重量、只剩情绪、借旅行零钱。两个失去同一个男人的人在 Mark 的床上坐到天黑：她第一次听完「他为什么爱你」，也第一次说出「我确实气他离开，在他死之前」。分手时的那句「我也为你的损失难过」来自她的仇人——全书唯一一次有人对她说这句话。

"He started to cry. “Mark isn’t here.”"（ch31）

"It was. It was October 21. He should have been thirty."（ch31）

"Renata would have her lovers and I’d have mine at the other end of our cave house and we’d all be in the mix together. Is it sad that my island fantasy has my whole regular life in it? But why would I fantasize about paradise and not bring all my friends?”"（ch31）

## 节点六 · 边界与重逢：Claude（ch18、ch35）

河岸的争吵把两人拆开：Claude 划下职业边界（「我帮活人，下班不做这个」），她则想拉她一起救鬼。直到三十岁生日的病房，她终于说出「我一直没能面对自己有多需要人」——「认真对待我们」。Claude 的回答只有四个字：「我在这儿」，随后用「我带 712 去做 CT」的谎把庆祝带出医院。这段关系的全部承诺都不在誓言里，而在到场的动作里。

"“Uh-uh. You’re not listening. I don’t know about ghosts. I’m telling you that I can’t deal with the dead. I can’t help them. I don’t know how to do that. I help the living. That’s what I do.”"（ch18）

"“Well,” Claude said, “here I am.”"（ch35）

"“I haven’t been able to deal with how much I need people,” I said. “I’m sorry. I’d like to take us seriously.”"（ch35）

## 节点七 · Star 失踪与七楼（ch25、ch27）

空屋的漂白水味、失踪的一周、第九分局的笑声——最后答案在 St. Vincent's 的七楼：乌青占掉半张脸、耳旁一枚硬币大的 KS 斑解释了男友为何下手。病床边 Bianca 说出最黑的一句辩护（「她说我是嫉妒她有人爱她爱到那样抓她」），而她们替昏迷的 Star 筹划了一场永远没能办的 bat mitzvah。警察始终没来。

"It was oddly clean, a whiff of bleach left behind. No trace of Star."（ch25）

"Star did not look like Star. She did not look like anyone. Her black eye was truly black, not purple or mauve or green, and it took up half her face. She had neon bruises along her neck running up to her ear, where there was, small as a dime, a KS spot. The boyfriend must have seen it and known what it was."（ch27）

"“She was all black and blue on her wrist one time. She said she bruised easy, but when I kept asking her, she was such a cunt about it.” She choked up. “She said I was jealous she had someone who loved her enough to grab her like that.”"（ch27）

## 节点八 · 仓库之夜：释放（ch32、ch34）

万圣夜的第二幕：Mucous Membrane 在冷库门口开演，TZZT 之后鬼笼短路，全城的死者归位；Rick 在一首《Good Vibrations》里崩溃，被当作「今晚第三个」送往 Bellevue。她在敞开的冷库里读遍每一格地址，把「Mark 在哪」的清单读成自己人生的地图——而他不在任何一格里。这一夜她交出全书的定理：彼此依赖是生存条件；并第一次祈愿：不要回来。

"In that moment, with the bat in my hands, I hoped I wouldn’t see Mark again, I hoped that he wouldn’t come back to this awful place."（ch32）

"There was a hush you could hear, if you knew what you were listening for, like the kind before someone yells surprise."（ch34）

"And then I was just me. My body was only mine. The ghosts were released, because they were everywhere, as they should be. I couldn’t hold them—no one could."（ch34）

## 节点九 · 游行里的「我们」（ch36）

终章把整本书收进一场游行：鬼与人挤到分不出区别，过去与未来一起涌入村庄。她在人潮里看见三年前的自己与 Mark——他托住跳起来的她，她穿着他的牛仔夹克——那不是鬼、不是抓不住的爱，而是自由的过去一层。全书最后一行用两个动词收束：先抓紧，然后松开。

"It was us. He wore the knit hat he used to love before it got swiped in a bar last year, the one that made him look like Jacques Cousteau, and I was wearing his denim jacket. I was standing on my tiptoes to see the parade and he put his hands on my waist and I leapt up and he held me there for a moment. I was watching us watch the parade, maybe three years ago."（ch36）

"All I’d ever have of him is what we already had. It wasn’t enough, but it was so much."（ch36）

"I watched Mark take me by the hand and we disappeared into the crowd. I held on, and I let go."（ch36）
