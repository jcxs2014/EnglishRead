# 16. Raff

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Raff 的内心世界——他对鲸鱼歌声的痴迷、与 Alex 的亲密关系、对母亲缺席的愤怒；他用拳击袋发泄情绪，用音乐填补沉默。
- **情感弧线位置**：建置——Raff 的"理性"外表下是"沸腾的愤怒"：对母亲死亡的愤怒、对 Alex 离开的愤怒。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The quiet one——Raff 的"沉默"不是"平静"而是"压抑"；First love——Raff 与 Alex 的关系是"未说出口的"。
- **人物弧线**：Raff 从"保护者"到"被保护者"——他的"男性气质"正在被"脆弱"侵蚀。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角，Raff 的"内心独白"穿插对 Alex 的回忆。

## 精读

① "The heart of a whale is the heaviest on the planet. It is big enough for a human to swim through. And it is very slow. These are the things that occupy Raff's thoughts. He thinks of that weight and feels overwhelmed by it."

- 中文理解：鲸鱼的心脏是地球上最重的。它大到足以让一个人游过去。而且它很慢。这些是占据 Raff 思想的事情。
- 关键词：heaviest——最重的；overwhelmed——被压垮。
- 为什么这样写：用"heaviest"建立 Raff 的"重量"主题——他的"悲伤"是"有重量的"。
- 读者视角提示：Raff 的"鲸鱼心脏"是全书的"重量"主题。

② "In the communications building on the island he keeps a hydrophone, a prized possession second only to his violin, which was a gift from his mother and which, at the time, was too big for his little arms and hands to wield but which he has grown into like a fifth limb. The hydrophone was from his dad for his fourteenth birthday, and he is aware that it cost a lot of money and that his dad had to save up for it, he is aware that he loves it almost as much as the instrument but without the entangled pain."

- 中文理解：在岛上的通信楼里，他保存着一个水听器，一个仅次于他小提琴的珍贵财产。小提琴是母亲的礼物，当时对他的小手臂来说太大了，但他已经像第五个肢体一样长大了。水听器是父亲在他十四岁生日时送的。
- 关键词：hydrophone——水听器；prized possession——珍贵财产；entangled pain——纠缠的痛苦。
- 为什么这样写：用"hydrophone"建立 Raff 的"科学家"身份；"entangled pain"暗示 Raff 的"矛盾"——他爱小提琴，但它让他想起母亲。
- 读者视角提示：Raff 的"水听器"是全书的"声音"主题。

③ "When he can find enough time among chores and schoolwork, Raff sets up his waterproofing and dives beneath the surface of the ocean to capture whatever sounds he comes across. It is best in deep water because there is less noise pollution, and sound transmits better in cold water. The sounds aren't what he first imagined they'd be, they aren't what you hear in movies. There are no friction-based sounds, which can occur only when you add wind or sand or plants into the equation. But for Raff this is the thrill. The discovery of the unexpected. And while he enjoys it all, from the bubbles of air popping on the surface to the swish of a nearby seal fin, what he wants more than anything, what gets him out here at the crack of dawn, even in freezing temperatures and miserably windy conditions that skew the sound results, is the possibility of hearing the song of a whale. He was born the day he first heard the song of a whale, and he has been finding ways to record them ever since. In one moment high and squeaking like the top note of a violin, in the next guttural, echoing, some space between the moan of a cow and the trumpet of an elephant. Sometimes joyous and playful, sometimes mournful and searching. Sometimes with a trill or a creak or a rumble. A question, a call, a love letter."

- 中文理解：当他在家务和功课中找到足够的时间时，Raff 会潜入海洋表面，捕捉他遇到的任何声音。在深水中最好，因为噪音污染较少。声音不是他最初想象的那样。但对 Raff 来说，这就是刺激。发现意外的喜悦。他最想要的是听到鲸鱼歌声的可能性。他在第一次听到鲸鱼歌声的那天出生了。
- 关键词：thrill——刺激；mournful——悲伤的；love letter——情书。
- 为什么这样写：用"thrill"建立 Raff 的"好奇心"；"love letter"的隐喻暗示 Raff 的"浪漫"——他把"鲸鱼歌声"当作"情书"。
- 读者视角提示：Raff 的"鲸鱼歌声"是全书的"声音"主题。

④ "After these lucky moments—and there have been only a few—he inevitably finds his way to the boxing bag, where he punches his body empty of the white-hot rage he feels at the thought of his mother not being here to experience the sound with him. What he is left with when that is punched out is loneliness."

- 中文理解：在这些幸运的时刻之后——只有少数几个——他不可避免地找到拳击袋，在那里他把身体打空，打空他想到母亲不在这里和他一起体验声音时感到的白热愤怒。当那被打空时，他剩下的是孤独。
- 关键词：white-hot rage——白热愤怒；punching out——打空；loneliness——孤独。
- 为什么这样写：用"white-hot rage"建立 Raff 的"愤怒"——他不是"悲伤"而是"愤怒"；"punching out"的暴力暗示 Raff 的"无力"。
- 读者视角提示：Raff 的"拳击袋"是全书的"男性气质"主题——他用"身体"处理情感。

⑤ "Which is how he's feeling on the day he first meets Alex. He is in the comms building, up at the peak, and he is playing his fiddle over the top of the humpback music he recorded last month. The audio file floats from the shithouse laptop speaker; it's all he has. He has been trying to compose something that will complement the whale song, that will translate and magnify it, but everything he adds only distracts from it. He is growing frustrated by his ineptitude. A stray G sounds discordant to his ears and he drops the bow, closing his eyes, letting the last notes reach their crescendo and fade out. He needs something more ambient, something to create atmosphere, not battle for the lead."

- 中文理解：这就是他第一次见到 Alex 那天的感受。他在通信楼里，在山顶，他在上个月录制的座头鲸音乐上拉小提琴。他一直在尝试创作一些能补充鲸鱼歌声的东西，但他添加的一切都只会分散注意力。他正为自己的无能而越来越沮丧。
- 关键词：ineptitude——无能；discordant——不和谐的；ambient——氛围的。
- 为什么这样写：用"ineptitude"建立 Raff 的"自我怀疑"；"ambient"的音乐术语暗示 Raff 的"专业"。
- 读者视角提示：Raff 的"小提琴"是全书的"艺术"主题——他用"音乐"表达无法用语言表达的情感。

⑥ "Those days are long past. Alex is gone and there is no slow. Raff can't touch his violin, hasn't reached for his hydrophone. Now there is only the bag for when he needs calm, and such moments are coming too often."

- 中文理解：那些日子早已过去。Alex 走了，没有慢。Raff 无法触摸他的小提琴，没有伸手去拿他的水听器。现在只有在他需要冷静时的袋子，而这样的时刻来得太频繁了。
- 关键词：long past——早已过去；no slow——没有慢。
- 为什么这样写：用"long past"建立 Raff 的"失去"——他失去了"快乐"；"no slow"与"鲸鱼心脏"形成对比——Raff 的"生活"是"快的"。
- 读者视角提示：Raff 的"无法触摸小提琴"是全书的"创伤"主题——他失去了"音乐"。

## 一句话总结

ch16 用 Raff 的"鲸鱼之歌"建立全书的"声音"主题——Raff 用"水听器"捕捉鲸鱼的歌声，用"小提琴"为它们伴奏；但 Alex 的离开让他失去了"音乐"，现在他只有"拳击袋"来发泄"白热愤怒"；他的"心"是"慢的"（像鲸鱼心脏），但他的"生活"是"快的"（像拳击）。
