# 61. Rowan

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Rowan 发现 Fen 的月经问题，陪伴她；Fen 透露自己曾以为怀孕；Rowan 意识到 Hank 是 narcissist；他们进行最后一次种子运输，Dom 决定去救 Hank。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Rowan 的"同情"与"愤怒"冲突：她"恨" Hank 但"不能"让他死。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The monster——Hank 的" narcissist"本质被揭示；The protector——Rowan 的"保护"是"阻止" Dom 杀人。
- **人物弧线**：Rowan 从"愤怒"到"同情"——她"阻止" Dom 杀 Hank。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角，Rowan 的"内心独白"贯穿全章。

## 精读

① "The water in the vault was knee-high when we last left—it will be higher again now. Several of the lower shelves are submerged, there are containers floating listlessly. I wonder what this family intends to do with my husband. If they have decided his cell shall become his grave, too."

- 中文理解：我们上次离开时仓库里的水已经齐膝高——现在会更高。几个较低的架子被淹没，有容器无精打采地漂浮着。我想知道这个家庭打算怎么处理我的丈夫。如果他们决定他的牢房也将成为他的坟墓。
- 关键词：knee-high——齐膝；submerged——淹没；listlessly——无精打采；grave——坟墓。
- 为什么这样写：用"grave"建立 Rowan 的"恐惧"——她"害怕" Hank 会死。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"想知道"是全书的"真相"主题——她"思考" Hank 的命运。

② "The lower storage area will start to fill up soon," I say. Orly frowns. What do I mean? "Down the air shaft. Water will start getting in there. It'll fill up. So if there's anything down there, you and your dad should think about moving it now." He flushes a little, then nods. He is bound to be confused by my pointedness, but I don't think he will read the subtext behind the words; he is far too trusting to think I wouldn't be up-front about what I know. Orly jumps off my bed and I hear his feet slapping down the stairs."

- 中文理解："下部储藏区很快就会开始积水，"我说。Orly 皱眉。我是什么意思？"沿着通风井。水会开始渗进去。它会满的。所以如果下面有什么东西，你和你爸爸现在应该考虑把它移走。"他微微脸红，然后点头。他一定会对我的直白感到困惑，但我认为他不会读懂话背后的潜台词；他太信任了，不会认为我知道什么却不直说。Orly 从我的床上跳下来，我听到他的脚啪嗒啪嗒跑下楼梯。
- 关键词：fill up——积满；air shaft——通风井；pointedness——直白；subtext——潜台词；up-front——直说。
- 为什么这样写：用"pointedness"建立 Rowan 的"暗示"——她"暗示" Orly。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"太信任"是全书的"真相"主题——Orly"相信"她。

③ "I dress warmly, protectively. Mentally I am arming myself. I don't know what I am going to do today, but I am going to do something."

- 中文理解：我穿得温暖，有保护性。精神上我在武装自己。我不知道今天要做什么，但我要做些事。
- 关键词：protectively——有保护性；arming——武装。
- 为什么这样写：用"arming"建立 Rowan 的"决心"——她"准备"行动。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"做些事"是全书的"行动"主题——她"决定"行动。

④ "Fen lets her head drop onto her knees and she cries hard and long. I stroke her shaved hair gently, letting her get it out. There is trauma here, I can hear it, and I can hear relief, too, maybe that it's over, maybe also that I finally know. When she is spent, when all the tangles within her have unspooled into the porcelain tub beneath her, she rests her head wearily against the wall. I sit crammed in between the sink and the bath, watching her. She says, 'I'm so sorry.' I just shake my head. 'You don't need to be.' He is the one who will be sorry. I could kill him for it. For hurting her. For the affair too, I suppose, but far more for choosing to have it with a child. I could watch him drown. Maybe I will. It is clear, now, why he's in a cell."

- 中文理解：Fen 让头靠在膝盖上，她大哭了一场。我轻轻抚摸她剃过的头发，让她发泄出来。这里有创伤，我能听到，我也能听到解脱，也许是因为结束了，也许还因为我终于知道了。当她筋疲力尽时，当她所有的纠结都在她下面的瓷盆里展开时，她疲倦地把头靠在墙上。我挤在洗脸池和浴缸之间，看着她。她说，"我很抱歉。"我只是摇头。"你不需要抱歉。"他才是那个会抱歉的人。我可以为此杀了她。为了伤害她。为了这段关系，我想，但更多的是因为选择和一个孩子在一起。我可以看着他淹死。也许我会的。现在很明显了，为什么他在牢房里。
- 关键词：trauma——创伤；relief——解脱；spent——筋疲力尽；tangles——纠结；unspooled——展开；porcelain——瓷；crammed——挤；drown——淹死；cell——牢房。
- 为什么这样写：用"cell"建立 Rowan 的"真相"——她"知道" Hank 在牢房。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"也许我会"是全书的"愤怒"主题——她"想"让 Hank 死。

⑤ "Hank is a narcissist," I tell her. "He is very good at convincing people he cares about them. But in reality his whole world is just—himself. He can't think beyond that. He can't feel beyond that. He's charismatic and clever and this allows him to collect people. A lot of people have fallen under his spell and there's no shame in it. 'Is that why you married him?' Fen asks. There is recognition in her eyes, as though this is all making sense to her. 'Yeah, I suppose so.' 'Why didn't you leave, when you realized it?' I think about this. 'I think it suited me to be with someone I knew would never look directly at me.' She breathes out. Then says, 'But you're so nice to look at.' It makes us both smile. I lean over the bath to hug her. All I can hope is that having some understanding of who Hank is will help her process this."

- 中文理解：" Hank 是个自恋者，"我告诉她。"他非常擅长说服人们他关心他们。但实际上他的整个世界只是——他自己。他无法超越这个思考。他无法超越这个感受。他有魅力、聪明，这让他能够收集人们。很多人都被他迷住了，这没什么可耻的。""这就是你嫁给他的原因？"Fen 问。她眼中有一种认领，仿佛这一切都说得通了。""是的，我想是的。""当你意识到的时候，你为什么没有离开？"我想了想。"我想和一个我知道永远不会直接看我的人在一起很适合我。"她呼出一口气。然后说，"但你看起来真好看。"让我们都笑了。我倾身拥抱她。我只希望了解 Hank 是怎样的人会帮助她处理这件事。
- 关键词：narcissist——自恋者；convincing——说服；reality——现实；charismatic——有魅力；spell——迷住；shame——可耻；recognition——认领；suited——适合；process——处理。
- 为什么这样写：用"narcissist"建立 Rowan 的"定义"——她"定义" Hank。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"不会直接看我"是全书的"真相"主题——Hank"不会"真正看人。

⑥ "As Dom and I wade down through the freezing water I think of Hank. I wonder if Dom means to drown my husband; I wonder if I might let him. I have never been so angry. All the ice on the walls of the vault has melted away. It takes a lot of effort for Dom and me to move through the freezing water; we are slow, and it feels pointless if you stop to think, so we don't think, we keep on. We carry our containers to the floating pallet we've tied with a rope to the chamber door so it doesn't float away. We don't look at labels, we just take from the pile Orly instructed us to focus on. With every container I carry, I contemplate a plant species that may survive because I forced myself to keep going, to keep moving through this freezing water. I can't feel my feet. We don't think about what's getting left behind; there will be time for that later, a lifetime for it."

- 中文理解：当 Dom 和我在冰冷的水中跋涉时，我想着 Hank。我想知道 Dom 是不是打算淹死我的丈夫；我也许会让他做。我从来没有这么生气。仓库墙上的冰都融化了。Dom 和我需要花很多力气才能在冰冷的水中移动；我们很慢，如果你停下来思考就会觉得很无意义，所以我们不思考，我们继续。我们把容器搬到我们用绳子绑在门上的浮动托盘上，这样它就不会漂走。我们不看标签，只是从 Orly 指示我们关注的堆里拿。每搬运一个容器，我都在沉思一个可能存活的植物物种，因为我强迫自己继续前进，继续在冰冷的水中移动。我感觉不到我们的脚。我们没有思考那些被留下的东西；以后会有时间的，一辈子都会有。
- 关键词：wade——跋涉；drown——淹死；melted——融化；effort——力气；pointless——无意义；pallet——托盘；labels——标签；contemplate——沉思；survive——存活；forced——强迫。
- 为什么这样写：用"contemplate"建立 Rowan 的"沉思"——她"思考"生命。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"一辈子"是全书的"遗憾"主题——她"思考"失去。

⑦ "When a second crack opens in the wall, letting a deluge pour free, Dominic shouts that it's time to go. That entire wall is about to give way and the cave we are in will crumble. We steer the floating pallet out through the chamber doors and up the tunnel. The water is around our waists now, already higher than when we entered this morning. 'Dom,' I say as we wade through the dark. Because maybe there won't be many more chances. 'Yeah?' 'You need to talk to Raff and Fen.' 'About what?' 'Whatever it is that they want to talk about.' He looks at me, understanding. This is a very weird time to have this conversation and I think he understands the why of that too. 'I don't know what to say to them,' he admits. 'Then just listen.' I let go of the barge for a moment so I can reach for him, reach for his cheek. 'Okay?' Dominic nods. 'Okay.'"

- 中文理解：当墙上出现第二道裂缝，让洪水自由倾泻时，Dominic 大喊是时候走了。整面墙即将倒塌，我们所在的洞穴会崩塌。我们把浮动托盘推出密室门，沿着隧道向上。水现在已经到我们的腰了，比今天早上我们进来的时候还高了。"Dom，"我们在黑暗中跋涉时我说。因为也许不会有很多次机会了。"嗯？""你需要和 Raff 和 Fen 谈谈。""关于什么？""无论他们想谈什么。"他看着我，明白了。这是一个非常奇怪的谈话时间，我想他也明白为什么。"我不知道该对他们说什么，"他承认。"那就只听。"我放开驳船一会儿，这样我可以够到他，够到他的脸。"好吗？"多米尼克点头。"好的。"
- 关键词：crack——裂缝；deluge——洪水；give way——倒塌；crumble——崩塌；steer——引导；weird——奇怪；barge——驳船。
- 为什么这样写：用"give way"建立 Rowan 的"紧迫"——他们"必须"离开。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"只听"是全书的"沟通"主题——她"建议" Dom 倾听。

⑧ "I can feel in it a farewell. He knows something. He might be planning something. He has done bad things. It doesn't matter. I put all of myself into this kiss, I cling to him. If this is our last, I hope he feels within it the days, hours, minutes left in my life, I hope he knows I am giving them to him, every one of them."

- 中文理解：我能感觉到其中的告别。他知道些什么。他可能在做计划。他做了坏事。这不重要。我把自己全部投入这个吻，我依偎着他。如果这是我们的最后一次，我希望他在其中感受到我生命中剩下的日子、小时、分钟，我希望他知道我把它们都给了他，每一个都给了。
- 关键词：farewell——告别；cling——依偎；giving——给。
- 为什么这样写：用"farewell"建立 Rowan 的"告别"——她"感觉"到结束。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"最后一个"是全书的"爱"主题——她"爱" Dom。

⑨ "I can't leave him to die," I say. There is no surprise in his face. He has already worked it out. "You don't have to," he says, so calm. "You just have to get in the boat." "Why did you do it?" I ask. "He and Fen—" "He tried to kill her," Dominic says. "He held her head under the water." I stare at him. "He is dangerous, Rowan. I couldn't tell you where he was because I could never be sure you wouldn't let him out. Or that Fen would be safe from him if he was free." The cold is working its way up from my numb feet to my guts, my chest, my mind. I have rage in my heart for what Hank has done, at the thought of the ways he has hurt Fen and broken apart this family. But somehow I also have pity for his sickness, which has transformed him. He needs help. He needs treatment and medicine. He needs to get away from this island. "We still can't let him die," I say. "You don't have to," Dom says. "But I can." "Dom," I say, squeezing his hands. "I've thought a lot about what I'd do if the vault flooded before the ship came. I was going to hand him over to the authorities, but nature's decided for me." "If you do this," I say clearly, "if you make me part of it, there will be nothing left for you and me. Do you understand? We won't come back from it." His eyes close as though he is in great pain. "Row," he says. He looks at me. "I'm sorry. Truly. But I have to protect my daughter."

- 中文理解："我不能让他死，"我说。他脸上没有惊讶。他已经想通了。"你不必，"他说，很平静。"你只需要上船。""你为什么这么做？"我问。"他和 Fen——""他试图杀了她，"多米尼克说。"他把她的头按在水下。"我盯着他。"他很危险，Rowan。我不能告诉你他在哪里，因为我不确定你不会把他放出来。或者如果他被释放了，Fen 会是安全的。"寒冷正从我麻木的脚上升到我的内脏、胸膛、头脑。我对 Hank 所做的一切充满愤怒，想到他伤害 Fen 和破坏这个家庭的种种方式。但不知何故，我也对他的病感到同情，这改变了他。他需要帮助。他需要治疗和药物。他需要离开这个岛。"我们还是不能让他死，"我说。"你不必，"Dom 说。"但我可以。""Dom，"我说，握着他的手。"我已经想过很多，如果船来之前仓库被淹了怎么办。我打算把他交给当局，但自然已经替我决定了。""如果你这样做，"我清楚地说，"如果你让我参与其中，你和我之间就不会再有什么了。你明白吗？我们无法从中恢复。"他的眼睛闭着，好像很痛苦。"Row，"他说。他看着我。"我很抱歉。真的。但我必须保护我的女儿。"
- 关键词：farewell——告别；worked it out——想通；dangerous——危险；let him out——把他放出来；safe——安全；numb——麻木；rage——愤怒；pity——同情；sickness——病；treatment——治疗；authorities——当局；flooded——被淹；squeezing——握；protect——保护。
- 为什么这样写：用"protect"建立 Dom 的"父性"——他"必须"保护女儿。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"无法恢复"是全书的"爱"主题——她"威胁" Dom。
