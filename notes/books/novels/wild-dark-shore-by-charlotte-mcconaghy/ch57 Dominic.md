# 57. Dominic

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Dom 讲述 Hank 事件的后续——他差点杀了 Hank；Hank 被关进地下室；通讯被破坏；Naija、Tom、Alex 死亡；他们一边挖坟墓一边维持 Hank 的生存。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Dom 的"保护"达到极端：他"关"了 Hank。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The monster——Hank 的"疯狂"是"真实"的；The father——Dom 的"保护"是"监禁"。
- **人物弧线**：Dom 从"暴力"到"监禁"——他"选择"关 Hank。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称回顾式叙事，Dom 的"现在"与"过去"交织。

## 精读

① "Something at the heart of me fractures when I see the fear in my daughter's eyes. I can't fathom how I let this happen, how I let her stray into such danger without noticing. I am astounded at the unlikelihood of bringing my children to a place so remote and still having something so terrible happen to her. Maybe it is not unlikely at all. Where men go there is harm. I have failed her, and I have to do better. I have to be better."

- 中文理解：当我看到女儿眼中的恐惧时，我内心有些东西破碎了。我无法理解我怎么让这件事发生，我怎么让她陷入这样的危险而没有注意到。我感到惊讶，把孩子们带到这样一个偏远的地方，仍然有如此可怕的事情发生在她身上。也许这根本不是不可能的。有男人的地方就有伤害。我辜负了她，我必须做得更好。我必须更好。
- 关键词：fractures——破碎；fathom——理解；stray——陷入；remote——偏远；harm——伤害；failed——辜负。
- 为什么这样写：用"fractures"建立 Dom 的"心碎"——他"感到"失败。
- 读者视角提示：Dom 的"必须更好"是全书的"父性"主题——他"承诺"保护。

② "I wish I had killed Hank, but I didn't, and now there's a problem to be dealt with. I'll need to radio the mainland, get some authorities down here to pick up the bastard. But when I walk up to the comms tower, I find every piece of equipment dead, as if it has been gone at by some kind of rabid animal. Wires have been cut, instruments have been cracked, holes have been punched. I try a few buttons but there's nothing, no response from any of it, and I can already see that this is well beyond my capacity to fix. If there is any part of this job I'm not great at, it's the electricals."

- 中文理解：我希望我杀了 Hank，但我没有，现在有一个问题需要处理。我需要无线电联系大陆，让一些当局来抓这个混蛋。但当我走到通讯塔时，我发现每台设备都死了，好像被某种疯狂的动物咬过。电线被割断，仪器被砸破，洞被砸了几个。我试了几个按钮，但没有任何反应，我已经看到这远远超出了我的修复能力。如果这份工作有什么我不擅长的，那就是电气。
- 关键词：authorities——当局；bastard——混蛋；comms——通讯；rabid——疯狂的；cut——割断；cracked——砸破；punched——砸；capacity——能力；electricals——电气。
- 为什么这样写：用"bastard"建立 Dom 的"愤怒"——他"恨" Hank。
- 读者视角提示：Dom 的"超出能力"是全书的"无力"主题——他"无法"修复。

③ "It's not difficult to piece together what's happened. Hank didn't want anyone to be able to call for help before he'd finished destroying what's in the vault. Or murdering my daughter."

- 中文理解：不难拼凑出发生了什么。Hank 不想让任何人在他完成摧毁仓库里的东西之前能够求救。或者谋杀我的女儿。
- 关键词：piece together——拼凑；destroying——摧毁；murdering——谋杀。
- 为什么这样写：用"murdering"建立 Dom 的"真相"——他"知道" Hank 想杀 Fen。
- 读者视角提示：Dom 的"拼凑"是全书的"真相"主题——他"明白"了。

④ "I go back to the lighthouse and gently try to explain it to Fen. I consider not, but I think she deserves to know. She flinches. 'So we're trapped here with him? We're just going to be stuck on this island with him for two more months?' 'I'm not letting him anywhere near you,' I promise her. 'You're safe.' But she is shaking her head and I can see she doesn't believe me. Why would she?"

- 中文理解：我回到灯塔，轻轻地试着向 Fen 解释。我考虑不说，但我觉得她应该知道。她退缩了。"所以我们被困在这里和他一起？我们要在这个岛上和他一起困两个月？""我不会让他靠近你的，"我承诺她。"你安全。"但她摇头，我能看出她不相信我。她为什么要相信？
- 关键词：deserves——值得；flinches——退缩；trapped——被困；stuck——困住；anywhere near——靠近；safe——安全。
- 为什么这样写：用"deserves"建立 Dom 的"尊重"——他"告诉" Fen 真相。
- 读者视角提示：Dom 的"她为什么要相信"是全书的"信任"主题——他"理解"她的恐惧。

⑤ "Hank gets out of the hospital twice, and twice he makes his way down to the vault and starts throwing packets of seeds into the ocean. No amount of reasoning will get through to him, and it takes it happening a third time before the others agree he needs to be contained."

- 中文理解：Hank 两次逃出医院，两次他走到仓库开始把种子包扔进海洋。任何理由都无法说服他，直到第三次发生其他人才同意他需要被关押。
- 关键词：twice——两次；reasoning——理由；get through to——说服；third time——第三次；contained——被关押。
- 为什么这样写：用"contained"建立 Dom 的"决定"——他"关押" Hank。
- 读者视角提示：Dom 的"第三次"是全书的"耐心"主题——他"等待"时机。

⑥ "It's Orly who tells us about the storage room. All he knows is that something is wrong with Hank, that he's sick and a threat to the seeds, which Orly cares about more than anything. He tells us of a room Hank showed him when they were down here together (I live in a state of rolling nausea at the thought of how much time I let that man spend alone with my children). There is a lot of arguing. Talk of it being inhumane. Worse than a prison cell. Not to mention right beneath the seeds he's trying to destroy. There is also talk of it being the only place we can be sure is secure. I don't bother getting involved, I wait it out, knowing he's going down in that cement hole whether the others agree to it or not. My mind is made up the moment I explain the idea to Fen and see relief in her eyes."

- 中文理解：是 Orly 告诉我们储藏室的事。他所知道的是 Hank 出了问题，他生病了，对种子构成威胁，Orly 关心种子胜过一切。他告诉我们 Hank 在这里时给他看的一个房间（一想到我让那个男人单独和我的孩子们待了多久，我就处于一种翻滚的恶心状态）。有很多争论。谈论它不人道。比监狱牢房更糟。更不用说就在他试图摧毁的种子下面。也有谈论它是我们能确定安全的唯一地方。我不费心参与，我等着，知道不管其他人是否同意，他都要下到那个混凝土洞里。当我向 Fen 解释这个想法并在她眼中看到解脱时，我就下定了决心。
- 关键词：storage room——储藏室；threat——威胁；arguing——争论；inhumane——不人道；prison cell——监狱牢房；secure——安全；cement hole——混凝土洞；relief——解脱。
- 为什么这样写：用"relief"建立 Dom 的"决定"——他"看到" Fen 的解脱。
- 读者视角提示：Dom 的"下定决心"是全书的"父性"主题——他"保护"女儿。

⑦ "So we come up with a regimen. He needs to be toileted and fed, he needs to be able to wash, he needs company, he needs his health and well-being checked on, he needs books and things to do. Naija is very clear on this: we will try to make him as comfortable as we can. But we will not be letting him out. Not until the ship comes."

- 中文理解：所以我们制定了一个规则。他需要上厕所和喂食，他需要能够洗澡，他需要陪伴，他需要检查他的健康和福祉，他需要书和事情做。Naija 非常清楚这一点：我们会尽量让他舒适。但我们不会让他出来。直到船来。
- 关键词：regimen——规则；toileted——上厕所；fed——喂食；company——陪伴；well-being——福祉；comfortable——舒适；letting him out——让他出来。
- 为什么这样写：用"regimen"建立 Dom 的"规则"——他"制定"规则。
- 读者视角提示：Dom 的"不会让他出来"是全书的"监禁"主题——他"关"了 Hank。

⑧ "Soon they are dead. Naija and Tom first, and then Alex. And my children and I are digging graves and keeping our prisoner alive, and we are barely holding our heads above water and that's when a woman washes ashore, seeking to find this man and set him free."

- 中文理解：很快他们死了。Naija 和 Tom 先，然后 Alex。我的孩子们和我一边挖坟墓一边维持我们的囚犯的生存，我们勉强保持头部露出水面，这时一个女人被冲上岸，寻找找到这个人并释放他。
- 关键词：digging graves——挖坟墓；prisoner——囚犯；barely——勉强；holding our heads above water——保持头部露出水面；washes ashore——被冲上岸；set him free——释放他。
- 为什么这样写：用"prisoner"建立 Dom 的"现实"——他"承认" Hank 是囚犯。
- 读者视角提示：Dom 的"释放他"是全书的"真相"主题——Rowan"来"了。
