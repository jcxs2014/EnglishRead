# 70. Rowan

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Rowan 在竖井中等待 Dom 打开舱口；她给 Orly 讲 wombats 的故事；她向 Orly 表白爱；她准备牺牲自己让 Orly 先呼吸；她最后看到的是 Hank 和 Claire。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Rowan 的"母爱"达到顶峰：她"准备"为 Orly 牺牲自己。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The found family——Rowan 的"母爱"是"牺牲"；The ghost——她看到 Hank 和 Claire。
- **人物弧线**：Rowan 从"爱"到"牺牲"——她"准备"为 Orly 死。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角，Rowan 的"内心独白"贯穿全章。

## 精读

① "The water has nearly reached us. I am appalled at how quickly the shaft has filled up. And if Dom hasn't got the hatch open yet, it means it's not as simple as grinding the rusted hinges off."

- 中文理解：水快涨到我们这里了。我对竖井这么快就满了感到震惊。如果 Dom 还没打开舱口，那就说明这并不像磨掉生锈的铰链那么简单。
- 关键词：appalled——震惊；quickly——快；hinges——铰链。
- 为什么这样写：用"appalled"建立 Rowan 的"震惊"——她"发现"情况危急。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"不简单"是全书的"困难"主题——Dom"遇到"困难。

② "We might be dying in here. I feel it come upon me with eerie certainty. We might be dying in here. If so, then what? What is my choice? My path forward? It is to protect him from fear. To help him feel only love."

- 中文理解：我们可能会死在这里。我感到一种诡异的确定感降临。我们可能会死在这里。如果是这样，那怎么办？我的选择是什么？我前进的道路是什么？是保护他免受恐惧。帮助他只感受到爱。
- 关键词：eerie——诡异的；certainty——确定；path——道路；fear——恐惧。
- 为什么这样写：用"eerie"建立 Rowan 的"确定"——她"知道"要死了。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"保护他"是全书的"母爱"主题——她"选择"保护 Orly。

③ "I forgot to tell you something," I say. I am looking up, and I see his eyes tilt from their vigil on his father to find me in the dark below. "What?" "You know how you were talking about the banksias? How they come to life after a fire, when everything else is burned?" "Yeah." "Well I didn't tell you the most important part of the story. Of my story, of the fire at my place. I said everything burned, but it didn't, Orly. Afterward, I was walking through the ash, looking for something, anything still living. And remember how I told you about the wombats and their square poos?" "Yeah." "Wombats have a thing they do in fires. They take their families underground, into their burrows. They have tunnels under the earth, and they go down there to take shelter, but they don't just take their families, they also take other animals down there. They save everyone they can. And then the mum and dad wombats stick their bums up into the entrances of the burrows to block the fire and the ash from coming down. And their bums get burned, and sometimes they die, but they protect the others." Orly meets my eyes. I smile. "They were down there. A whole group of them, huddled together. The wombats had saved a dozen little creatures, there were lizards and frogs and possums and a wallaby, and there was a koala, too, and they were all alive." He is smiling now too. "And those mum and dad wombats that stick their bums up to save their family, that's your mum and dad," I say, and we are both laughing, knowing it's true. "So I wanted to ask if you guys would like to come and live there with me?" I say. "When we're out of here." "To replant?" I nod. "Won't another fire just come?" "Maybe. But we'll make sure the whole place is covered in banksias.""

④ "I think of River, in these last minutes. I let myself remember him, and for the first time in a long time I take pleasure in the memories. He was a gorgeous, smiling boy. He made the world richer for having been in it. I look at the little boy above me. "Hey, Orly." "Yeah?" "I love you." He puts his little hand on my head, like a pat. "Love you too, Row." "Tell your dad..." I break off and swallow. The water is at my shoulders. I have already climbed as high as I can; both our heads are touching the top of the hatch. I can see how frantic Dom is up there. This might come down to seconds so I won't delay him long enough to tell him anything. I will just have to hope Orly can pass something along. Because one of us will be able to press up through that window and breathe, even when the shaft is entirely submerged. "Tell your brother and sister the same, okay? I'm not your mum, but I will always love you." "Stop," he says. "Just stop. You can tell them." "I think it carries on, even after." I breathe out slowly. Breathe in slowly. Stay calm. The water reaches my chin. "Press your face up now," I tell Orly."

- 中文理解：在最后几分钟，我想起了 River。我让自己想起他，很久以来第一次我从记忆中获得快乐。他是一个帅气的、微笑的男孩。他让这个世界因为他的存在而更丰富。我看着上面的小男孩。"嘿，Orly。""嗯？""我爱你。"他把他的小手放在我的头上，像拍一拍。"我也爱你，Row。" "告诉你爸爸……"我停下来，咽了咽。水已经到了我的肩膀。我已经爬得尽可能高；我们的头都碰到舱口的顶部。我能看到 Dom 在上面多么焦急。这可能取决于几秒钟，所以我不会耽误他足够长的时间来告诉他任何事情。我只能希望 Orly 能传达些什么。因为当我们中有一个人能从那个窗口探出头呼吸，即使竖井完全被淹没。"告诉你的兄弟姐妹同样的话好吗？我不是你的妈妈，但我会永远爱你。" "停，"他说。"就停。你可以告诉他们。""我认为它继续存在，即使在死后。"我慢慢呼气。慢慢吸气。保持平静。水到了我的下巴。"现在把脸探上去，"我告诉 Orly。
- 关键词：River——River（人名）；minutes——分钟；pleasure——快乐；gorgeous——帅气；richer——更丰富；smiling——微笑；break off——停下；swallow——吞咽；shoulders——肩膀；touching——碰到；frantic——焦急；seconds——秒；delay——耽误；pass along——传达；submerged——被淹没；brother——兄弟；sister——姐妹；carries on——继续；breathe out——呼气；breathe in——吸气；calm——平静；chin——下巴；press——探。
- 为什么这样写：用"River"建立 Rowan 的"过去"——她"想起"了弟弟。

⑤ "I have drowned once before. I thought of my mother, then. It was strange to me that I should think of her at the end, after years of trying to convince myself I hated her. Untrue of course, but armor against the way she blamed me, the way she couldn't stand to look at me. I have been so angry with her—even after she died I have held on to that anger and it has made me fearful—but being a parent is complex and it is altering and being the parent of a lost child is something no human should have to contend with. I forgive the distance she imposed between us to try to survive. I think instead of the love she had for us in the beginning, and of my sisters and our boat. I think of Hank and how grateful I am that he taught me to appreciate the things that grow in the ground. He gave me access to wildness and that is no small thing in a human life. I think of this life, of my life, of the things I built and planted. I have been lucky to know such richness. But I also think of how my husband taught me something else, something so deeply wrong I am stunned that I ever believed it: that in the face of world's end love should shrink. I think of Raff and Fen and Orly. I have spent my life loving other people's children. There is no safety in this. But what is the use of safety if it deprives you of everything else? I feel immense grief, thinking of the time I spent resenting this little boy and wishing I could have been anywhere else. I should have been treasuring every precious second with him, with them all, instead of wishing those seconds away. I realize I never told Orly what I wanted to say to you. I am underwater now. There are little lights flashing, they look like sparks from the cut glass of your Fresnel lens. I think of the whale, and her calf, and the sea embracing them. I think I finally understand your words. It's just a body. They hold on or they don't. You're right, it's nothing to be frightened of. Mine will become the salt of this water. And every time you swim it will be me upon your skin. Orly slips. He comes under. His body slides into mine and I catch him in the dark and his long pale hair is all around me. My survival instinct tells me to surge for the surface, to press my mouth to that small opening and draw breath—to do anything to end the agony in my chest—but I can feel Orly panicking, his eyes are wide, he is going to suck the water into his lungs and then he will be gone, and so instead of going for air, I cover his mouth with mine and I press into him the last of what I have in my lungs, in my body. Enough, maybe, for another second or two, and I feel him calm a little, and then I think I feel him pulled from my arms and I am falling. But there is someone here. A woman. Down here in the dark with me. She catches me and holds me so tenderly, and I know her. She is his mother, and she died so he could live. I understand it so simply now, it is a love that lives in the body but unlike the body it never dissolves. It lasts forever."

- 中文理解：我曾经淹死过一次。那时我想起了我的母亲。奇怪的是我在最后想起了她，在多年来试图说服自己我恨她之后。当然不真实，但这是对她责怪我的方式的盔甲，是她无法忍受看我的方式。我对她如此生气——即使在她死后我也抱着那份愤怒，它让我害怕——但做父母是复杂的，它是改变性的，做一个失去孩子的父母是任何人类都不必面对的。我原谅她为了生存而强加在我们之间的距离。我想起的是她一开始对我们的爱，以及我的姐妹们和我们的船。我想起 Hank，我多么感激他教我欣赏地里生长的东西。他给了我通往野性的通道，这在人的一生中不是小事。我想起这一生，我的一生，我建造和种植的东西。我很幸运知道如此的丰富。但我也想起我丈夫教我的另一件事，如此深深的错，我很震惊我曾经相信它：在世界末日面前爱应该收缩。我想起 Raff 和 Fen 和 Orly。我花了一生去爱别人的孩子。这没有安全感。但如果安全剥夺了你其他的一切，安全有什么用？我感到巨大的悲伤，想起我花在这个小男孩身上的怨恨时光，希望我能在别的地方。我应该珍惜和他在一起的每一秒，和他们所有人，而不是希望那些秒过去。我意识到我从未告诉过 Orly 我想对你说的话。我现在在水下。有小灯闪烁，它们看起来像从你菲涅耳透镜的切割玻璃发出的火花。我想起鲸鱼，她的幼崽，和拥抱它们的大海。我想我终于明白你的话。这只是身体。他们抓住或不抓住。你是对的，这没什么好害怕的。我的将成为这水的盐。每次游泳它都会在你皮肤上。Orly 滑了。他来到水下。他的身体滑进我在黑暗中抓住他，他长长的苍白头发在我周围。我的生存本能告诉我要涌向表面，把我的嘴压向那个小开口呼吸——做任何事来结束胸口的痛苦——但我能感觉到 Orly 在恐慌，他的眼睛睁得很大，他要把水吸进肺里然后他就会走了，所以我没有去呼吸，我用我的嘴覆盖他的嘴，我把我肺里、身体里的最后一点东西压给他。也许够再撑一两秒，我感到他平静了一点，然后我感到他从我的手臂中被拉走，我在坠落。但这里有人。一个女人。在黑暗中和我在一起。她抓住我，如此温柔地抱住我，我认识她。他是他的母亲，她死了所以他才能活。我现在简单地理解它，它是一种活在身体里的爱，但与身体不同，它永不溶解。它永远持续。
