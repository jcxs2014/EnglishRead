# 56. Fen

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Fen 回忆与 Hank 的关系——她十六岁认识他，十七岁被他追求，发现怀孕后告知他，却被他试图淹死；Dom 打了 Hank，把他关进地下室。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Fen 的"创伤"被完全揭示：Hank 试图淹死她。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The monster——Hank 的"好男人"面具碎裂；The father——Dom 的"保护"是"暴力"。
- **人物弧线**：Fen 从"受害者"到"幸存者"——她"游"出了 Hank 的魔爪。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角，Fen 的"回忆"贯穿全章。

## 精读

① "Fen is sixteen when she first sees Hank. He's shouting at someone driving the Frog onto land, signaling that they need to wait for a pair of gentoos who are waddling across their path. He is the new team leader of the base, here to spend a few weeks in handover with Carol. Fen is sad to see Carol go—Carol taught her how to cut open the giant kelp and cook fish inside. None of them know what to expect from the new guy, but Fen likes that he is concerned for the penguins."

- 中文理解：Fen 十六岁时第一次看到 Hank。他正对着开车上岸的人喊叫，示意他们需要等待一对正在摇摇摆摆过路的巴布亚企鹅。他是基地的新团队负责人，来这里和卡罗尔交接几周。Fen 对卡罗尔的离开感到难过——卡罗尔教她如何切开巨型海藻在里面煮鱼。他们都不知道对新来的人有什么期待，但 Fen 喜欢他关心企鹅。
- 关键词：signaling——示意；gentoos——巴布亚企鹅；waddling——摇摇摆摆；handover——交接；concerned——关心的。
- 为什么这样写：用"concerned"建立 Hank 的"伪装"——他"看起来"很好。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"喜欢"是全书的"欺骗"主题——她"被骗"了。

② "Fen is aware, after a few lessons like this, that she is developing a crush on him. She has been raised by a man so reticent his silences can last days. So all of Hank's talking, all of these conversations—they are a revelation to her. It is actually possible to know what someone is thinking, it is actually possible for them to tell you! It makes her feel grown-up, to know someone like this. He's smart and passionate, and he's funny too. Plus there aren't many choices on this island for people to fantasize about, and the fantasies come without her permission, anyway. One day he is just an adult she doesn't know, like any other, the next he is filling her thoughts."

- 中文理解：几节这样的课后，Fen 意识到她对他产生了好感。她由一个沉默寡言到可以连续几天不说话的男人抚养长大。所以 Hank 的所有谈话，所有这些对话——对她来说是一种启示。实际上有可能知道某人在想什么，实际上有可能让他们告诉你！认识这样一个人让她感到成熟。他聪明、热情，也很有趣。加上这个岛上没有太多人可以幻想，而幻想也不请自来。有一天她只是一个不认识的成年人，和其他人一样，第二天他就填满了她的思想。
- 关键词：developing——产生；crush——好感；reticent——沉默寡言；revelation——启示；fantasize——幻想；permission——允许。
- 为什么这样写：用"revelation"建立 Fen 的"天真"——她"不知道"危险。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"不请自来"是全书的"危险"主题——她"无法控制"。

③ "Fen is seventeen by the time Hank's new orders are given. They will both be staying on for another three months and then heading off island for good. Something in him shifts at this point. She can feel the nature of his interest in her changing. Everything about him takes on an urgent quality, a sort of frenzied abandon. When he says he's noticed her watching him, she flushes, she feels caught. When he says he knows she likes him, that he can feel her attraction, she realizes she has done this to herself, that it's her fault. She tells herself it's what she wants. She's old enough, it's legal, he says, and anyway they're leaving soon. Actually, his exact words are 'It will all be over soon.'"

- 中文理解：到新命令下达时 Fen 十七岁了。他们都将再待三个月，然后永远离开这个岛。在这一点上他身上发生了变化。她能感觉到他对她的兴趣的性质在改变。他的一切都呈现出一种紧迫的品质，一种疯狂的放纵。当他说他注意到她在看他时，她脸红了，感到被抓住了。当他说他知道她喜欢他，他能感觉到她的吸引力时，她意识到这是她自己造成的，是她的错。她告诉自己这是她想要的。她够大了，他说，这是合法的，反正他们很快就要走了。实际上，他的原话是"这一切很快就会结束。"
- 关键词：shifts——变化；urgent——紧迫；frenzied——疯狂的；abandon——放纵；flushes——脸红；caught——被抓住；attraction——吸引力；fault——错；legal——合法的。
- 为什么这样写：用"frenzied"建立 Hank 的"疯狂"——他"失控"了。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"是她的错"是全书的"受害者"主题——她"自责"。

④ "Each month Fen dreads her period; she marks its impending arrival on the calendar so she can be prepared for the pain of it. Which means she is aware of this day passing her by. At only one day late, she becomes convinced. She spirals. Panicking, thinking through every implication. The worst part of this is that her dad will have to be told, because he will need to take her off island so she can get an abortion."

- 中文理解：每个月 Fen 都害怕她的月经；她在日历上标记它即将到来的日子，这样她就可以为疼痛做好准备。这意味着她意识到这一天过去了。只晚了一天，她就确信了。她螺旋式下降。恐慌，思考每一个影响。最糟糕的是必须告诉她爸爸，因为他需要带她离开这个岛，这样她就可以堕胎。
- 关键词：dreads——害怕；period——月经；impending——即将；calendar——日历；passing her by——过去；convinced——确信；spirals——螺旋式下降；panicking——恐慌；implication——影响；abortion——堕胎。
- 为什么这样写：用"spirals"建立 Fen 的"恐慌"——她"崩溃"了。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"最糟糕"是全书的"秘密"主题——她"害怕"告诉爸爸。

⑤ "When Fen arrives at the vault to tell him, he seems weird. He's been pretty weird for weeks now, but today he is opening containers and pulling out seed packets and dumping them in a pile on the floor. All his meticulous categorizing out the window. Where are the others? Tom and Naija and Alex? Why aren't they stopping him from doing whatever this is? 'What are you doing?' she asks. He doesn't look at her, just carries on with his work. 'It's the end of it all.' 'What is?' 'They don't get to tell me to drown half. That is not something any bureaucracy gets to decide on.' 'So then don't,' Fen says. 'Just tell them you won't do it.' 'If I refuse, they'll send someone else down here.' 'Okay, so what…?' 'Don't worry about it, kid.' He is enraged, she realizes. This is not a good time to tell him what she suspects. But he turns to her and levels that look on her, and it seems to say why have you bothered me, and she gets flustered, wanting something important to justify her presence here. So she blurts it out. I think I'm pregnant."

- 中文理解：当 Fen 到达仓库告诉他时，他看起来很奇怪。他已经很奇怪几周了，但今天他打开容器，拉出种子包，把它们堆成一堆扔在地板上。他所有细致的分类都白费了。其他人在哪里？Tom 和 Naija 和 Alex？他们为什么不阻止他做这件事？"你在做什么？"她问。他不看她，只是继续他的工作。"这一切都结束了。""什么结束了？""他们不能告诉我淹死一半。这不是任何官僚机构可以决定的。""那就不要，"Fen 说。"就告诉他们你不会做。""如果我拒绝，他们会派别人下来。""好吧，那……？""别担心，孩子。"她意识到他怒不可遏。现在不是告诉他她怀疑的时候。但他转向她，用那种眼神看着她，似乎在说为什么你要打扰我，她慌乱起来，想要一些重要的事情来证明她在这里的合理性。所以她脱口而出。我想我怀孕了。
- 关键词：weird——奇怪；dumping——扔；pile——堆；meticulous——细致的；categorizing——分类；bureaucracy——官僚机构；enraged——怒不可遏；flustered——慌乱；justify——证明；blurts——脱口而出；pregnant——怀孕。
- 为什么这样写：用"blurts"建立 Fen 的"冲动"——她"脱口而出"。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"怀孕"是全书的"真相"主题——她"告诉"了 Hank。

⑥ "At first he doesn't react. Hank is calm as he leads her out of the vault. They walk in silence to the blue field hut. 'Where are the others?' she asks. 'Up at the base.' Her stomach sinks. Something feels so off. He's not looking at her. He is watching the ocean. 'You know what's hilarious?' he asks. 'My wife was right.' It takes a moment for that word to sink in. Wife. And now she can hardly concentrate on what he's saying. 'I've always wanted children but she kept saying it was wrong. Bad for the world, bad for the kids. I thought it was just an excuse but she was so fucking right. This world is a dumpster fire.' Fen is dissolving. He has a wife. This is bad. This is way worse than she realized. She is a party to something cruel."

- 中文理解：起初他没有反应。Hank 平静地领她走出仓库。他们沉默地走到蓝色田野小屋。"其他人在哪里？"她问。"在基地上面。"她的胃沉了下去。有些事情感觉很不对劲。他不看她。他在看海洋。"你知道什么好笑吗？"他问。"我妻子是对的。"过了一会儿这个词才沉入脑海。妻子。现在她几乎无法集中注意力听他在说什么。"我一直想要孩子，但她一直说这是错的。对世界有害，对孩子有害。我以为只是借口，但她他妈的太对了。这个世界是一个垃圾箱火灾。"Fen 在溶解。他有妻子。这很糟。这比她意识到的要糟得多。她是某种残忍事情的帮凶。
- 关键词：react——反应；sink——沉；hilarious——好笑的；wife——妻子；concentrate——集中；excuse——借口；dumpster fire——垃圾箱火灾；dissolving——溶解；party——帮凶；cruel——残忍。
- 为什么这样写：用"dissolving"建立 Fen 的"崩溃"——她"溶解"了。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"妻子"是全书的"真相"主题——她"发现"了真相。

⑦ "I've figured out how to solve the problem," Hank rambles on (he is always rambling on, how has she abided this?). "You know Orly's wind voices?" he asks her. "I thought he was really screwed up." Hank laughs bitterly. "I was the one who didn't have a clue. This place is full of them." "Of who?" "Of the dead." She is staring at him and her skin is crawling. He's always joked about how stupid her superstition is. "I think this is where things come to die," Hank says. "I'm gonna walk home. I'll see you later." "Fen." She pauses, though she very much doesn't want to. "I figured out what has to happen. It's impossible to choose. Do you understand that?" "Yes." "So I won't. Nobody will. They will all drown. Every one of them, and every one of us, and then everything will start again." She doesn't know what he's talking about and she doesn't care, she just wants to get back to her family. But he pulls her in for a hug. As he holds her he says, "I'm so sorry this happened. It's my fault and I'll pay for it in the end, I'll pay for it all." Then he pulls her down into the seawater and shoves her head under the surface."

- 中文理解："我已经想出了解决问题的办法，"Hank 继续喋喋不休（他总是喋喋不休，她怎么忍受的？）。"你知道 Orly 的风声吗？"他问她。"我以为他真的疯了。"Hank 苦涩地笑了。"我是一个没有头绪的地方。这里到处都是。""谁？""死人。"她盯着他，她的皮肤在爬行。他总是开玩笑说她的迷信多么愚蠢。"我认为这就是东西来死去的地方，"Hank 说。"我要走回家。我晚点见你。""Fen。"她停了下来，尽管她非常不想。"我已经想出必须发生什么。不可能选择。你明白吗？""是的。""所以我不会。没有人会。他们都会淹死。他们每一个人，我们每一个人，然后一切都会重新开始。"她不知道他在说什么，她也不在乎，她只想回到她的家人身边。但他把她拉进一个拥抱。当他抱着她说，"我很抱歉发生了这件事。是我的错，我最终会付出代价，我会付出一切。"然后他把她拉进海里，把她的头按在水下。
- 关键词：figured out——想出；rambling——喋喋不休；abided——忍受；screwed up——疯了；bitterly——苦涩；clue——头绪；crawling——爬行；superstition——迷信；impossible——不可能；drown——淹死；shoves——推。
- 为什么这样写：用"shoves"建立 Hank 的"暴力"——他"试图"淹死她。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"不在乎"是全书的"危险"主题——她"不知道"危险。

⑧ "First there is shock. There is disbelief. But she is strong, and she has very good lungs. Her brothers and father like to say she was born for the water and maybe that's true, she is certainly at home beneath its surface. So if he thinks this is how to kill her? He is mistaken. The secret is simple: you have to know how to stay calm. Panic—even a slightly raised pulse—is your enemy. Even when the pressure comes, even with the pain. Fen lets her mind go blank and her body go limp. She plays dead. The seconds tick past but she doesn't move, doesn't panic. Finally his grip on her head loosens a fraction and she uses the moment to twist out of his hold. Instead of surging up beside him she kicks out underwater, swimming as far into the sea as she can. She is not frightened of the burn in her lungs, she knows it well because she is always pushing to its edge, always wanting just that little longer beneath. Even so, she is all too human."

- 中文理解：首先是震惊。有难以置信。但她很强壮，她的肺很好。她的兄弟和父亲喜欢说她是为水而生的，也许这是真的，她确实在水下很自在。所以如果他认为这就是杀死她的方法？他错了。秘密很简单：你必须知道如何保持冷静。恐慌——甚至是稍微加快的脉搏——是你的敌人。即使压力来了，即使有痛苦。Fen 让她的头脑空白，她的身体软弱。她装死。几秒钟过去了，但她不动，不恐慌。最后他对她头的抓握松了一点，她利用这一刻扭出他的控制。她没有在他旁边涌上去，而是踢水游向大海，尽可能远。她不害怕肺部的灼烧，她很清楚，因为她总是推到它的边缘，总是想在水下多待一会儿。即便如此，她也太人性化了。
- 关键词：shock——震惊；disbelief——难以置信；lungs——肺；born——生；beneath——在……下方；mistaken——错了；secret——秘密；calm——平静；panic——恐慌；pulse——脉搏；enemy——敌人；pressure——压力；blank——空白；limp——软弱；plays dead——装死；tick——过去；loosens——松；fraction——一点；twist——扭；surging——涌；underwater——水下；burn——灼烧；edge——边缘；human——人类。
- 为什么这样写：用"plays dead"建立 Fen 的"智慧"——她"装死"逃脱。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"太人性化"是全书的"生存"主题——她"幸存"了。

⑨ "Fen watches her brother, who is a lot bigger than Hank, tackle the older man to the ground. Together, he and Alex drag Hank into the field hut. She watches that door; she isn't coming out of the sea until she knows it's safe. Alex returns and waves her in, but she doesn't move, she treads water, watching him. He comes down to the shoreline and shouts to her. 'Are you okay?' She says nothing. 'Your dad's on his way, okay?' he calls. 'And Raff's watching Hank. You can come out.' But she can't. She won't. She starts to shiver, her teeth chattering. She will have to move soon, she doesn't have her wetsuit, she might be in shock. 'Fen, baby,' Alex calls. 'Please come out. It's too cold.' In the end it's Raff who wades into the water and pulls her bodily to shore. 'Why did he do that to you?' he is asking. 'Why was he doing that, Fen?' But she can't talk. That was a man who said he loved her. She does not understand."

- 中文理解：Fen 看着她的哥哥，他比 Hank 大得多，把那个老人摔倒在地。他和 Alex 一起把 Hank 拖进田野小屋。她看着那扇门；在她知道安全之前她不会从海里出来。Alex 回来向她挥手，但她不动，她踩水，看着他。他走到岸边向她喊叫。"你没事吧？"她什么也没说。"你爸爸在来的路上，好吗？"他喊道。"Raff 在看 Hank。你可以出来了。"但她不能。她不会。她开始发抖，牙齿打颤。她必须很快移动，她没有穿潜水衣，她可能处于震惊状态。"Fen，宝贝，"Alex 喊道。"请出来。太冷了。"最后是 Raff 涉水到水里，把她整个人拉到岸上。"他为什么这样对你？"他问。"他为什么这样做，Fen？"但她不能说话。那是一个说爱她的男人。她不理解。
- 关键词：tackle——摔倒；drag——拖；safe——安全；treads water——踩水；shoreline——岸边；okay——好；shiver——发抖；chattering——打颤；wetsuit——潜水衣；wades——涉水；bodily——整个地；shore——岸。
- 为什么这样写：用"bodily"建立 Raff 的"保护"——他"拉"她上岸。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"不理解"是全书的"创伤"主题——她"无法"理解。

⑩ "Fen won't ever ask what happened inside, but it is obvious that Dominic has nearly killed Hank in that little kitchen. It is only Raff, with strength that almost rivals his father's, who manages to wrestle Dom away before it's too late. Tom, Alex, and Raff get Hank to the hospital on the base. Naija will treat him. Dom takes Fen home to the lighthouse, where they sit at the kitchen table and talk. When she explains that she and Hank have been in a relationship of sorts for about a month, she sees the horror that falls over him and she will never be able to unsee it. She knows exactly what he perceives her as now. Damaged. Broken. He asks, 'Has he ever forced you?' 'No.' 'Has he ever hurt you, before this?' 'No. He's not himself,' she tells him. 'He wants to drown all the seeds. So that no one can ever make the choice. I saw it in his eyes—he won't stop until he's drowned them all.' She gets her period two days later."

- 中文理解：Fen 永远不会问里面发生了什么，但很明显 Dominic 差点在那个小厨房里杀了 Hank。只有 Raff，用几乎与他父亲匹敌的力量，在太迟之前把 Dom 拉开。Tom、Alex 和 Raff 把 Hank 送到基地的医院。Naija 会治疗他。Dom 带 Fen 回到灯塔，他们坐在厨房桌子旁说话。当她解释她和 Hank 大约一个月来有一种关系时，她看到恐惧笼罩着他，她永远无法忘记。她确切地知道他现在如何看待她。受损的。破碎的。他问，"他强迫过你吗？""没有。""他以前伤害过你吗？""没有。他不是他自己，"她告诉他。"他想淹死所有的种子。这样就没有人能做出选择。我在他眼睛里看到了——他不会停下来，直到他把它们都淹死。"两天后她来月经了。
- 关键词：obvious——明显；nearly——几乎；rivals——匹敌；wrestle——拉；treat——治疗；horror——恐惧；unsee——忘记；perceives——看待；damaged——受损；broken——破碎；forced——强迫；hurt——伤害；period——月经。
