# 43. Alex

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Alex 追随哥哥 Tom 来到 Shearwater；他认识 Raff 并爱上他；Raff 十八岁生日那天，风暴卷走了绿色小屋，Tom 和 Naija 死亡；Alex 在燃料罐下上吊自杀；Dominic 和 Raff 埋葬了他。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Alex 的"失去"是全书的"镜像"：Raff 失去了爱人，Rowan 失去了弟弟，Dominic 失去了妻子。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The ghost——Alex 的"死"是 Raff 的"创伤"；The island——Shearwater 的"诅咒"是"失去"。
- **人物弧线**：Alex 从"追随"到"放手"——他"选择"了死亡。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称回顾式叙事，Alex 的"现在"（自杀）与"过去"（与 Raff 的回忆）交织。

## 精读

① "Alex came eight years after Tom did, and that's how his life has been since. Trailing behind his older brother, who is everything to him. He never probes too deeply into why Tom means everything to him, for fear of uncovering hidden pain about their father having left. He prefers to just let it be fact: all Alex really wants is to be close to his brother."

- 中文理解：Alex 在 Tom 之后八年来到这里，这就是他此后的生活。追着他的哥哥，哥哥就是他的全部。他从不深究为什么 Tom 意味着一切，害怕揭开关于父亲离开的隐藏痛苦。他宁愿把它当作事实：Alex 真正想要的只是靠近他的哥哥。
- 关键词：trailing——跟随；everything——全部；probes——探究；uncovering——揭开；hidden——隐藏的。
- 为什么这样写：用"trailing"建立 Alex 的"追随"——他不是"独立"的而是"依附"的。
- 读者视角提示：Alex 的"害怕揭开"是全书的"创伤"主题——他"害怕"面对真相。

② "Tom did swimming in school, so Alex had to do swimming too. Tom learned the guitar, so Alex learned the guitar. Tom developed a passion for mathematics and science, so Alex decided he would be passionate about the same. When Tom left university in Chicago and went chasing the worst storms to hit the country, Alex had all his fears realized: he was being left behind. It was this damn eight years between them: What had possessed his parents to leave it so long? Why couldn't they be two years apart like every other pair of siblings he knew?"

- 中文理解：Tom 在学校游泳，所以 Alex 也必须游泳。Tom 学吉他，所以 Alex 学吉他。Tom 对数学和科学产生热情，所以 Alex 决定对同样的事产生热情。当 Tom 离开芝加哥大学去追逐袭击这个国家的最糟风暴时，Alex 所有的恐惧都实现了：他正在被留下。就是这该死的八年差距：他父母怎么会让他隔这么久？为什么他们不能像他认识的其他兄弟姐妹一样只差两岁？
- 关键词：passion——热情；fears——恐惧；realized——实现；left behind——被留下；Siberia——西伯利亚。
- 为什么这样写：用"left behind"建立 Alex 的"被抛弃"——他"追随"但"被留下"。
- 读者视角提示：Alex 的"该死的八年"是全书的"命运"主题——时间"摧毁"了爱。

③ "He thinks of the weekly psych sessions they're each required to complete, check-ins to make sure they aren't suffering any mental health problems due to the nature of their isolation. 'Why does everyone here love saying the place is haunted?' he asks. Halloween is always celebrated with more gusto than Christmas or New Year's. Raff's mouth quirks. 'To laugh, instead of being disturbed.' He supposes that makes sense. Hates thinking about any of it, so turns his mind ahead. 'There's a botanic garden just outside Chicago,' Alex says. Raff looks at him and grins, knowing exactly what he's doing. 'And I'm sure he'd love it. But it's a long way from the sea.' 'Alright, where, then?' 'Vancouver Island,' Raff says without hesitation. 'The most whale sightings of any place in the world.' Alex smiles and thinks about this. 'Loads of orcas and humpbacks there.' 'And seals and sea lions.' 'Vancouver Island it is then.' They laugh, but the laughter trickles away, both aware of how unlikely this plan is to happen. Still. It is nice to lie here in the grass and the sun and daydream."

- 中文理解：他想起了他们每周都必须完成的心理检查，确保他们没有因为隔离的性质而出现任何心理健康问题。"为什么这里的人都说这个地方闹鬼？"他问。万圣节总是比圣诞节或新年更热闹地庆祝。Raff 的嘴角扭曲。"为了笑，而不是被困扰。"他想这有道理。讨厌想这些，所以把思想转向前方。"芝加哥外有一个植物园，"Alex 说。Raff 看着他笑了，完全知道他在做什么。"我相信他会喜欢的。但它离海很远。""好吧，那么哪里？""温哥华岛，"Raff 毫不犹豫地说。"世界上鲸鱼出现最多的地方。"Alex 微笑着想着这个。"那里有很多虎鲸和座头鲸。""还有海豹和海狮。""那就温哥华岛。"他们笑了，但笑声逐渐消失，两人都意识到这个计划多么不可能实现。尽管如此。躺在草地和阳光下做白日梦真好。
- 关键词：psych sessions——心理检查；check-ins——签到；isolation——隔离；haunted——闹鬼；gusto——热情；quirks——扭曲；disturbed——被困扰；daydream——白日梦。
- 为什么这样写：用"haunted"建立 Alex 的"恐惧"——他"害怕"隔离但"留下"。
- 读者视角提示：Alex 的"白日梦"是全书的"梦想"主题——他"梦想"和 Raff 在一起。

④ "On the morning of Raff's eighteenth birthday, there is a storm brewing. Things have started to get very bad on Shearwater, but Alex wants to give Raff one good day to forget all the rest, all the shit with his family and with Hank, just one. Alex, Tom, and Naija are living in the red field hut and spending most of their time in the vault. It is a strange experience, and at first Hank made them take a lot of breaks, insisting that so much time underground, specifically under this ground, with this particular task and all its complicated tragedy, could very well make them loopy. So he was on them all the time, checking they'd eaten, checking they'd slept well, checking they'd been up and out for fresh air. This task was asking a lot of them."

- 中文理解：在 Raff 十八岁生日那天早上，一场风暴正在酝酿。Shearwater 的情况开始变得非常糟糕，但 Alex 想给 Raff 一天好日子，让他忘记所有其他的，所有和爸爸和 Hank 的破事，就一天。Alex、Tom 和 Naija 住在红色田野小屋，大部分时间都在地下室里。这是一种奇怪的经历，起初 Hank 让他们多休息，坚持认为在地下花这么多时间，特别是在这片土地上，带着这个特别任务和它所有的复杂悲剧，很可能会让他们变得疯狂。所以他一直在他们身边，检查他们是否吃过，检查他们是否睡得好，检查他们是否出去呼吸新鲜空气。这个任务对他们要求很高。
- 关键词：storm brewing——风暴酝酿；vault——地下室；loopy——疯狂的；breaks——休息；complicated——复杂的；tragedy——悲剧。
- 为什么这样写：用"loopy"建立 Alex 的"压力"——他"承受"不住了。
- 读者视角提示：Alex 的"就一天"是全书的"渴望"主题——他"渴望"但"得不到"。

⑤ "The storm grows. They both spend some time watching it through the windows, noting the rise of the sea, acknowledging with wonder how high it's come, how it smashes against the walls of the huts. They can recognize that the waves perhaps should not be so high or so violent, but they are distracted. Alex wakes in the middle of the night to a mighty crash. The rain and wind are both so loud he is deafened. Tom is in their room, looming over them, and behind him is Naija. 'Come on, it's not safe.' His voice is barely audible over the sounds of the storm and the creaking of the cabin pylons."

- 中文理解：风暴加剧。他们花了一些时间透过窗户观看，注意到大海的涨起，惊奇地承认它涨得多么高，它如何砸向小屋的墙壁。他们能认识到波浪也许不应该那么高或那么猛，但他们分心了。Alex 在半夜被一声巨响惊醒。雨和风都很大，他震耳欲聋。Tom 在他们房间里，在他们上方隐约可见，身后是 Naija。"走，不安全。"他的声音在风暴和小屋吱嘎声中几乎听不见。
- 关键词：smash——砸向；distracted——分心的；mighty——巨大的；deafened——震耳欲聋；looming——隐约可见；creaking——吱嘎声；pylons——柱子；barely audible——几乎听不见。
- 为什么这样写：用"deafened"建立 Alex 的"恐惧"——他"听到"了但"来不及"。
- 读者视角提示：Alex 的"不安全"是全书的"死亡"主题——他"知道"但"来不及"。

⑥ "In Raff's Zodiac, Alex turns to look behind, to check his brother is following. Instead what he sees where the green cabin sat only moments before is something different, shapes he can't make sense of in the dark and the swell. He blinks, squinting, desperately trying to understand, and then the whole shape moves and he realizes what he is looking at: the cabin has come off its pylons and is on its side in the water. A wave dumps onto it, disappearing the entire hut. Alex screams and Raff grabs for him but Alex is swift, he is plunging into the rough ocean, mad with terror and with grief for he already knows, deep down. The waves batter him and he realizes he may have drowned himself, too, but while there is blind hope he will still try to find him, he will kick and struggle against this violent sea, reaching in the dark for Tom's body."

- 中文理解：在 Raff 的 Zodiac 里，Alex 转身回头看，检查他的哥哥是否跟着。相反，他在绿色小屋刚才所在的地方看到的是不同的东西，在黑暗和波涛中他无法理解这些形状。他眨着眼睛，眯着，拼命想理解，然后整个形状移动了，他意识到他在看什么：小屋已经从柱子上脱落，侧翻在水里。一个波浪砸向它，整个小屋消失了。Alex 尖叫，Raff 抓住他但 Alex 很敏捷，他跳入汹涌的海洋，因恐惧和悲伤而疯狂，因为他内心深处已经知道。波浪拍打着他，他意识到他可能也淹死了，但当他还有盲目的希望时，他仍然会尝试找到他，他会踢打着在这片狂暴的海中挣扎，在黑暗中伸手摸 Tom 的身体。
- 关键词：swell——波涛；squinting——眯着；desperately——拼命地；plunging——跳入；batter——拍打；blind hope——盲目的希望。
- 为什么这样写：用"plunging"建立 Alex 的"选择"——他"选择"跳海。
- 读者视角提示：Alex 的"盲目希望"是全书的"爱"主题——他"爱" Tom 胜过"自己"。

⑦ "He loses consciousness for a second or two but revives quickly as he's pulled back into the Zodiac. Raff's strong arms have plucked Alex from this maelstrom and the engine is gunning them away from the hut. What madness possessed him to bring them out here? To this flimsy matchbox house on its toothpick legs? It's been said for years the green cabin isn't to be occupied, not since the sea got so high. His brother told him this morning it wasn't safe. His brother, whom he has killed."

- 中文理解：他失去了一两秒钟的意识，但当他拉回 Zodiac 时迅速苏醒。Raff 有力的手臂把 Alex 从这场漩涡中拉出来，引擎把他们从小屋带离。是什么疯狂驱使他们来到这个？来到这个用牙签腿支撑的脆弱火柴盒房子？多年来人们都说绿色小屋不能住，自从海涨得这么高以来。他哥哥今天早上告诉他这不安全。他哥哥，他杀了的人。
- 关键词：revives——苏醒；plucked——拉出；maelstrom——漩涡；flimsy——脆弱的；matchbox——火柴盒；toothpick——牙签；possessed——驱使；killed——杀。
- 为什么这样写：用"killed"建立 Alex 的"内疚"——他"觉得"自己"杀"了哥哥。
- 读者视角提示：Alex 的"他杀了的人"是全书的"内疚"主题——他"无法原谅"自己。

⑧ "They do manage to recover the bodies. Dominic and Raff do. They go without telling Alex, and they don't ever show him what they've found, they simply invite him to help bury the sheet-wrapped figures. Alex is distressed—he and Tom have been raised firmly secular and yet he feels a need to wash his brother's body properly, to give him some sort of ceremonial burial. Instead he shovels dirt onto Tom. A grave down here at the bottom of the world, an unmarked, anonymous grave in the middle of nowhere, without any way to call for help or tell his mom. As he shovels the dirt he thinks of how he has always been trying to catch up to Tom, has spent his whole life chasing after, and how he might actually be able to do it now: in eight years Alex will have closed that gap at last. It is a disaster. It is unbearable."

- 中文理解：他们确实找到了尸体。Dominic 和 Raff 找到的。他们没有告诉 Alex，他们从未给他看他们找到的，他们只是邀请他帮忙埋葬裹在床单里的人影。Alex 很痛苦——他和 Tom 从小就被严格地世俗抚养长大，但他觉得需要给他哥哥洗身体，给他某种仪式的埋葬。相反，他把土铲到 Tom 上。这里在世界尽头的底部，一个没有标记、没有名字的荒坟，没有任何办法求救或告诉他妈妈。他铲着土，想着他总是试图追上 Tom，花了一辈子追赶，他现在可能真的能做到：八年后 Alex 终于能缩小差距。这是一个灾难。这是无法承受的。
- 关键词：recover——找到；distressed——痛苦的；secular——世俗的；ceremonial——仪式的；unmarked——没有标记；anonymous——没有名字的；disaster——灾难；unbearable——无法承受的。
- 为什么这样写：用"unbearable"建立 Alex 的"崩溃"——他"承受"不了。
- 读者视角提示：Alex 的"八年后"是全书的"命运"主题——他"永远"追不上。

⑨ "He finds himself walking north to the isthmus and sitting in that same spot, in the grass overlooking the base, he does this often, no matter the weather. He finds himself looking down at the fuel tanks."

- 中文理解：他发现自己走到北方的地峡，坐在同一个地方，在俯瞰基地的青草地上，他经常这样做，无论天气如何。他发现自己看着燃料罐。
- 关键词：isthmus——地峡；overlooking——俯瞰；fuel tanks——燃料罐。
- 为什么这样写：用"fuel tanks"建立 Alex 的"死亡"——他"选择"在燃料罐下自杀。
- 读者视角提示：Alex 的"经常"是全书的"死亡"主题——他"已经"想死了。
