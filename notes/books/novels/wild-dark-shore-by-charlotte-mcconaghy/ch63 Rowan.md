# 63. Rowan

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Rowan 在船上发现种子被 Hank 调换过；她告诉 Dom 和 Orly；Orly 不见了；Dom 去找他。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Rowan 发现 Hank 的"破坏"证据。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The truth——Hank 的"疯狂"是"预谋"的；The monster——Hank 调换种子。
- **人物弧线**：Rowan 从"同情"到"愤怒"——她"发现" Hank 的破坏。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角，Rowan 的"内心独白"贯穿全章。

## 精读

① "There is a ship on the horizon. I can see it from the kitchen window, even through the storm. It has arrived one day too late to save a great many of the species in that vault. But it may have come in time to save a man's life. It will be full of naval officers. I can tell them where he is and they can retrieve him, and I won't have to go back down there. I am shaking with the relief of it."

- 中文理解：地平线上有一艘船。即使透过风暴，我也能从厨房窗户看到它。它来得太晚，无法拯救那个仓库里的许多物种。但它可能来得正是时候，来拯救一个人的生命。它将满是海军军官。我可以告诉他们他在哪里，他们可以救他，我就不用再回去了。我因解脱而颤抖。
- 关键词：horizon——地平线；storm——风暴；species——物种；naval——海军的；officers——军官；retrieve——救；relief——解脱。
- 为什么这样写：用"relief"建立 Rowan 的"解脱"——她"感到"解脱。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"解脱"是全书的"希望"主题——她"感到"希望。

② "I need to take the quad back for Dom and Orly, but first I unpack my load of containers. Our freezer is just about full. Both Raff and Fen are there, making space for this last lot. The only way we've been able to pack them in tightly enough is to follow Fen's idea of removing them from their containers and storing the little plastic bags of seeds. They help me open my last containers and take the precious bags out, placing them gently on the piles. I look at these seeds, pausing to note that the bag I have just placed on top contains something rather extraordinary looking, and not what I was expecting. The wrinkly seed seems to sit within a hood, a great draping hood that curves around like a papery moth wing, and then at its base is a long needlelike point. The shape of the whole thing is that of a sickle, and this hood is so fine—parchment thin—that light travels through it, illuminating the delicate filaments and giving the illusion of movement. I stare at it, taken aback by its loveliness, and then I look at the label. Pterocymbium tinctorium (Malvaceae)—or the melembu, from Indonesia. I don't know what this name means; it could be any kind of plant."

- 中文理解：我需要把四轮车拿回去给 Dom 和 Orly，但首先我卸下我的那批容器。我们的冰箱几乎满了。Raff 和 Fen 都在那里，为最后一批腾出空间。我们能把它包装得足够紧的唯一方法是遵循 Fen 的想法，把它们从容器里取出，装在小塑料袋里。他们帮我打开最后的容器，把珍贵的袋子拿出来，轻轻地放在堆上。我看着这些种子，停下来注意到我刚才放在最上面的袋子里的东西看起来相当不同寻常，不是我所期望的。褶皱的种子似乎坐在一个兜帽里，一个巨大的垂坠兜帽，像纸质的蛾翅膀一样弯曲，然后在它的底部是一个长长的针状点。整个东西的形状像一个镰刀，而这个兜帽是如此精致——薄如羊皮纸——光线穿过它，照亮了精致的细丝，给人一种运动的幻觉。我盯着它被它的美丽惊呆了，然后我看着标签。Pterocymbium tinctorium（锦葵科）——或者叫 melembu，来自印尼。我不知道这个名字是什么意思；它可以是任何种类的植物。
- 关键词：containers——容器；tightly——紧；plastic——塑料；precious——珍贵的；wrinkly——褶皱的；hood——兜帽；draping——垂坠；papery——纸质的；sickle——镰刀；parchment——羊皮纸；illuminating——照亮；filaments——细丝；illusion——幻觉；extraordinary——不同寻常。
- 为什么这样写：用"extraordinary"建立 Rowan 的"惊奇"——她"发现"种子的美丽。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"不知道"是全书的"真相"主题——她"不知道"种子是什么。

③ "I look at another packet, also strange. A walnut-shaped seed is covered in long, thick, blond hairs that stand on end, a child's drawing of a golden sun. The Aulax pallasia (Proteaceae)—or the needle-leaf featherbush from South Africa. Again, no idea what this is; I have read Hank's list a few dozen times, scanning my eyes over the seemingly endless and incomprehensible Latin names in search of containers to collect and move, and neither of these is remotely familiar. Doesn't mean much really, there were too many names for me to memorize of course, but a thought starts tugging at the back of my mind."

- 中文理解：我看着另一个袋子，也很奇怪。一个核桃形状的种子覆盖着长长的、浓密的金色毛发，直立起来，像孩子画的金色太阳。Aulax pallasia（山龙眼科）——或者叫针叶羽毛丛，来自南非。同样，不知道这是什么；我读过汉克的清单几十遍了，扫视着那些看似无尽且难以理解的拉丁名字，寻找要收集和移动的东西，而这两个都不熟悉。真的没什么意义，有太多名字我记不住了，但一个想法开始在我脑海中拉扯。
- 关键词：walnut-shaped——核桃形；blond——金色的；hairs——毛发；stand on end——直立；needle-leaf——针叶；featherbush——羽毛丛；remotely——远远地；familiar——熟悉的；memorize——记住；tugging——拉扯。
- 为什么这样写：用"tugging"建立 Rowan 的"怀疑"——她"感觉"到不对。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"太多名字"是全书的"真相"主题——她"无法"记住所有名字。

④ "I look at more of the seeds, now exposed. There are the Wollemi pine seeds, and the common dandelion seeds. I know those two and I'm not surprised by them, but the next looks like a weapon. It is covered in sharp tusks or antlers, and it's called a Cullenia ceylanica from Sri Lanka. There is a seed that looks like a jellyfish, with a hood and several long tentacles. There is one with two chambers, both an impossibly vibrant and deep inky-blue color. One that is long and curling and snakelike. One that looks like a pineapple. Another like a wiry bird's nest. They are strange and otherworldly and I was not expecting their beauty. They don't look anything like seeds as I know them. When I reach a packet containing the Banksia grandis, from Australia, I know what this is—it is a giant banksia—and I know what he's done."

- 中文理解：我看着更多现在暴露出来的种子。有瓦勒迈松种子和常见的蒲公英种子。我知道这两个，对它们不感到惊讶，但下一个看起来像武器。它覆盖着尖利的长牙或角，叫做 Cullenia ceylanica，来自斯里兰卡。有一种种子看起来像水母，有一个兜帽和几根长长的触手。有一个有两个腔室，都是不可能鲜艳的深墨蓝色。有一个又长又卷曲，像蛇一样。有一个看起来像菠萝。另一个像铁丝鸟窝。它们奇怪而超凡脱俗，我没有预料到它们的美丽。它们看起来完全不像我所知道的种子。当我拿到一个来自澳大利亚的 Banksia grandis 的袋子时，我知道这是什么——它是一个巨大的班克木——我知道他做了什么。
- 关键词：Wollemi pine——瓦勒迈松；dandelion——蒲公英；weapon——武器；tusks——长牙；antlers——角；jellyfish——水母；tentacles——触手；chambers——腔室；impossibly——不可能地；vibrant——鲜艳的；inky-blue——墨蓝色；curling——卷曲；snakelike——蛇一样；pineapple——菠萝；wiry——铁丝的；otherworldly——超凡脱俗；expecting——预料；beauty——美丽。
- 为什么这样写：用"otherworldly"建立 Rowan 的"震惊"——她"看到"超凡的种子。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"知道"是全书的"真相"主题——她"发现" Hank 做了什么。

⑤ "What's wrong?" Raff asks me, because I am standing in the freezer, staring at the sea of seeds around me. "Nothing," I say."

- 中文理解："怎么了？" Raff 问我，因为我站在冰箱里，盯着周围的种子海。"没什么，"我说。
- 关键词：wrong——不对；staring——盯着；sea——海。
- 为什么这样写：用"sea"建立 Rowan 的"感受"——她"看到"种子的海洋。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"没什么"是全书的"真相"主题——她"隐藏"了真相。

⑥ "As I approach the beach, I can see my hope was in vain—that ship is too far out. It will take time for them to prepare and load their Zodiacs, to reach us, to understand. The weather might even be too rough to disembark at all. The air shaft will be starting to fill."

- 中文理解：当我接近海滩时，我看到我的希望是徒劳的——那艘船太远了。他们需要时间准备和装载他们的 Zodiac，到达我们这里，理解。天气可能甚至太恶劣而无法下船。通风井将开始积水。
- 关键词：approach——接近；in vain——徒劳；load——装载；Zodiac——黄道带；rough——恶劣；disembark——下船；shaft——井。
- 为什么这样写：用"in vain"建立 Rowan 的"失望"——她"看到"船太远。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"开始积水"是全书的"紧迫"主题——水"开始"涨了。

⑦ "Dom is loading the Frog with tools. I kill the bike's engine and jump off. Shout through the rain. "What are you doing?" "I'm going back," he yells, with a shake of his head like he can't believe he's doing it. "Where's Orly?" Dominic straightens. He frowns, and meets my eyes, and in the space of a moment I see a universe pass through him. "He was with you," he says. "He told me he was going on your boat." He sees the answer in my face. Orly told me the same thing."

- 中文理解：Dom 正在给青蛙车装工具。我关掉摩托车引擎，跳下车。透过雨喊叫。"你在做什么？""我要回去，"他喊道，摇摇头，好像他不敢相信自己正在做这件事。"Orly 在哪里？"多米尼克直起身。他皱了皱眉头，和我对视，在那一刻我看到了一个宇宙在他眼中传递。"他和你在一起，"他说。"他告诉我他要上你的船。"他从我的脸上看到了答案。Orly 也告诉我同样的事。
- 关键词：loading——装；tools——工具；kill——关；engine——引擎；jump——跳；yell——喊；straighten——直起身；frown——皱眉；universe——宇宙；space——空间；answer——答案。
- 为什么这样写：用"universe"建立 Dom 的"震惊"——他"意识到" Orly 不见了。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"同样的事"是全书的"恐惧"主题——Orly"消失"了。
