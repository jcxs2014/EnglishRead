# 58. Rowan

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Rowan 发现 Hank 后逃离地下室；Dom 察觉异样；Rowan 与 Raff 对话，确认 Dom 的暴力；Orly 爬上她的床，她意识到 Orly 一直知道 Hank 在地下室。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Rowan 的"真相"与"谎言"冲突：她"找到"了 Hank，但 Dom 是"骗子"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The liar——Dom 的"谎言"被揭穿；The ghost——Hank 的"鬼魂"是"活人"。
- **人物弧线**：Rowan 从"爱"到"怀疑"——她"质疑" Dom。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角，Rowan 的"内心独白"贯穿全章。

## 精读

① "My husband and I stare at each other. We are both in shock. He looks pale and tired enough to shrivel into a husk. His hair is brushed and tidy, but long. His clothes are clean. He doesn't look underweight or starved. There are books strewn everywhere and notepads he's been scribbling in. There is a terrible smell coming from a bucket in the corner. I take all of this in, and I take in what it means, and I am raging, my heart is raging, but I don't have much time."

- 中文理解：我丈夫和我面面相觑。我们都很震惊。他看起来苍白疲惫得足以枯萎成空壳。他的头发梳得整齐，但很长。他的衣服干净。他看起来不瘦弱也不饥饿。到处都是散落的书和他一直在涂鸦的笔记本。角落里一个桶里传来难闻的气味。我接受这一切，我接受这意味着什么，我在愤怒，我的心在愤怒，但我没有多少时间。
- 关键词：shock——震惊；pale——苍白；husk——空壳；strewn——散落；scribbling——涂鸦；raging——愤怒。
- 为什么这样写：用"raging"建立 Rowan 的"愤怒"——她"愤怒"但"冷静"。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"没有多少时间"是全书的"时间"主题——她"必须"行动。

② "What are you doing here?" he demands. Thinks better of it. "Get me out, you have to get me out, please help me." I am nodding, peering around, trying to think. "He'll be back any second." The fear that fills Hank's face is telling enough. "Don't let him know you've found me. Don't let him know, and then sneak back and let me out." He crosses the small space to me, grabbing my arms, and his grip is rough, tight, it frightens me. "They've been keeping me down here for weeks, they're fucking crazy, he nearly killed me, Rowan, you have to help me."

- 中文理解："你在这里做什么？"他要求。转念一想。"把我弄出去，你必须把我弄出去，请帮帮我。"我点头，四处张望，试着思考。"他随时会回来。" Hank 脸上充满恐惧，这足以说明一切。"别让他知道你找到我了。别让他知道，然后偷偷回来把我弄出来。"他穿过小空间走到我面前，抓住我的胳膊，他的抓握粗糙、紧，吓到我了。"他们已经把我关在这里几周了，他们他妈的疯了，他差点杀了我，Rowan，你必须帮我。"
- 关键词：demands——要求；sneak——偷偷；grip——抓握；rough——粗糙；tight——紧；keeping——关；nearly——几乎。
- 为什么这样写：用"nearly killed me"建立 Hank 的"受害者"——他"声称"被虐待。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"吓到了"是全书的"恐惧"主题——她"害怕" Hank。

③ "I will," I promise, trying to free myself from him. His eyes are frantic, darting, my god, what has he been through? He seems so small. "It's okay," I tell him. "I promise I'll get you out of here. It's why I came." "Go, quick," he hisses. "Don't let him know, and don't trust him." I climb the ladder as fast as I can, lock the door, and run. The tunnel seems darker than it has before. The splashing of my feet echoes in my ears. What the fuck is going on? Why is he being held down there, what possible reason could there be to do that to someone, it doesn't make sense, I can't make it make sense.

- 中文理解："我会的，"我承诺，试图挣脱他。他的眼睛疯狂地转动，我的天，他经历了什么？他看起来那么小。"没事，"我告诉他。"我答应我会把你弄出去。这就是我来的原因。" "快走，"他嘶嘶地说。"别让他知道，别相信他。"我尽快爬上梯子，锁上门，跑。隧道看起来比以前更暗。我的脚步溅水声在我耳边回响。到底怎么回事？为什么他被关在那里，有什么可能的理由这样对待一个人，这说不通，我无法理解。
- 关键词：frantic——疯狂；darting——转动；hisses——嘶嘶；splashing——溅水；echoes——回响。
- 为什么这样写：用"what the fuck"建立 Rowan 的"困惑"——她"无法"理解。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"别相信他"是全书的"信任"主题——她"被告知"别信 Dom。

④ "His footsteps arrive and we nearly collide in the dark. His hands are on my arms, my shoulders, there is tenderness there. Don't trust him. 'You okay?' Dom asks me, and the earth shifts off its axis. Everything he has ever said to me will need to be reevaluated in this new, ugly context. There are lies within him, and malice, and violence. At the very least he has kept a terrible thing from me, he has worked hard and elaborately to keep it from me. 'Forgot my scarf,' I say, and there is, I think, a survival instinct that is keeping my voice normal."

- 中文理解：他的脚步声到了，我们在黑暗中差点相撞。他的手在我的胳膊上，我的肩膀上，那里有温柔。别相信他。"你没事吧？" Dom 问我，地球偏离了轴心。他曾经对我说的一切都需要在这个新的、丑陋的背景下重新评估。他内心有谎言，有恶意，有暴力。至少他对我隐瞒了一件可怕的事，他努力而精心地对我隐瞒。"忘了围巾，"我说，我想，有一种生存本能让我保持声音正常。
- 关键词：collide——相撞；tenderness——温柔；reevaluated——重新评估；ugly——丑陋；malice——恶意；elaborately——精心；survival instinct——生存本能。
- 为什么这样写：用"survival instinct"建立 Rowan 的"伪装"——她"假装"正常。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"忘了围巾"是全书的"谎言"主题——她"撒谎"了。

⑤ "At some point Raff knocks on the door and pokes his head in. I am calmer now, so I gesture and he comes to sit on the bed. His heavily bandaged arm is back in the sling. He looks pale and in pain. Am I to fear these kids, too? They must know about Hank—Dom would need their help keeping him alive, which means they are complicit. The performance of it all is staggering, all that time I spent in the vault, so close to where my husband sat, and them pretending nothing was wrong. I find I can't access any feelings to go with this knowledge. Just disbelief. These kids, who are so kind and warm, all three of them, locking a man in what amounts to a dungeon. Can you blame kids for the decision of a parent? Not Orly, surely. But Raff and Fen are pretty much adults themselves."

- 中文理解：在某个时候，Raff 敲门探进头来。我现在平静了，所以我示意他过来坐在床上。他严重包扎的手臂又吊在吊带上。他看起来苍白而且很痛苦。我也要害怕这些孩子吗？他们一定知道 Hank——Dom 需要他们的帮助让他活着，这意味着他们是同谋。这一切的表演令人震惊，所有我在仓库里度过的时间，离我丈夫坐的地方那么近，他们假装没有问题。我发现我无法获取任何感受来配合这种知识。只是难以置信。这些孩子，如此善良和温暖，他们三个，把一个男人锁在相当于地牢的地方。你能责怪孩子做父母的决定吗？当然不是 Orly。但 Raff 和 Fen 几乎是成年人了。
- 关键词：poke——探进；complicit——同谋；staggering——令人震惊；pretending——假装；amounts to——相当于；dungeon——地牢。
- 为什么这样写：用"complicit"建立 Rowan 的"怀疑"——她"怀疑"孩子们。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"难以置信"是全书的"信任"主题——她"无法"相信。

⑥ "I can't escape some of the blame either. I asked Dom straight why he had Hank's passport and he said he just forgot to take it, and I believed him. The insanity of that is mind-blowing. My own stupidity, unbearable. 'You're sick?' Raff asks me. I nod. 'Your arm's bad.' He shrugs. 'I made it worse. Easy trade for their lives.' The whales. They seem a lifetime ago. I study his face as he peers out the window. 'Are you scared?' Raff doesn't look at me. Just nods once. There will be no violin without a properly working hand. 'I need to ask you some things,' I say. 'Okay.' I lick my lips, my whole mouth feels dry. 'Your dad said Hank started to become unwell, at the end.' Raff nods. 'Did Dom ever have to be violent with him?' The boy goes very still. 'You can tell me,' I say. 'Whatever it is, I'll try to understand.' Raff's head tilts and he is studying me the way I am studying him. 'You love my dad,' he says suddenly, like it is fact. 'No,' I croak. Because I can't. Not now. 'So you know,' Raff goes on as though I have said nothing, 'deep down, you know as well as we do, that everything Dom does is for his kids.'"

- 中文理解：我也无法逃脱一些责备。我直接问 Dom 为什么他有 Hank 的护照，他说他只是忘了拿，我相信了他。那种疯狂令人震惊。我自己的愚蠢，无法忍受。"你生病了？" Raff 问我。我点头。"你的手臂很糟。"他耸耸肩。"我让它更糟了。为了他们的生命，简单的交易。"鲸鱼。它们似乎是一辈子以前的事了。我研究他的脸，他望着窗外。"你害怕吗？" Raff 不看我。只是点一下头。没有正常工作的手就没有小提琴。"我需要问你一些事，"我说。"好的。"我舔了舔嘴唇，我的整个嘴巴感觉干燥。"你爸爸说 Hank 最后开始不舒服。"Raff 点头。"Dom 曾经对他使用过暴力吗？"男孩变得非常静止。"你可以告诉我，"我说。"无论是什么，我都会试着理解。"Raff 歪着头，他研究我的方式就像我研究他一样。"你爱我爸爸，"他突然说，好像这是事实。"不，"我沙哑地说。因为我不能。不是现在。"所以你知道，"Raff 继续说，好像我什么都没说，"在内心深处，你和我们一样清楚，Dom 做的一切都是为了他的孩子。"
- 关键词：blame——责备；straight——直接；insanity——疯狂；mind-blowing——令人震惊；unbearable——无法忍受；violent——暴力；croak——沙哑；deep down——内心深处。
- 为什么这样写：用"deep down"建立 Raff 的"真相"——他"知道" Dom 的动机。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"我不能"是全书的"爱"主题——她"否认"爱。

⑦ "The storm arrives. Mighty claps of thunder and a dazzling light show. The wind is wailing and as though swept in by it, Orly appears in my room. I pull back the covers for him and he scurries under. His warm little body is like a hot water bottle in the cold. 'Where will all the animals go?' he asks me. 'When the island's gone?' I think of Ari and Nikau and their egg. I think of King Brown and his harem of mother seals and their babies. I think of the thousands and thousands of penguins. 'They'll find another,' I tell him. But we both know there is no island like this one. As I hold Orly I think of how he stood at the air vent, talking to Hank. He knew. He has known all along that my husband is beneath the ground. Still, I can't bring myself to let him go."

- 中文理解：风暴来了。巨大的雷声和耀眼的灯光秀。风在呼啸，仿佛被它吹来，Orly 出现在我的房间。我掀开被子给他，他急忙钻进去。他温暖的小身体在寒冷中就像一个热水瓶。"所有的动物会去哪里？"他问我。"当岛屿消失的时候？"我想起 Ari 和 Nikau 和它们的蛋。我想起 King Brown 和它的母海豹后宫和它们的宝宝。我想起成千上万只企鹅。"它们会找到另一个，"我告诉他。但我们都知道没有像这个一样的岛屿。当我抱着 Orly 时，我想起他站在通风口，和 Hank 说话。他知道。他一直都知道我丈夫在地下。尽管如此，我还是无法让自己放开他。
- 关键词：mighty——巨大的；dazzling——耀眼的；wailing——呼啸；scurries——急忙；hot water bottle——热水瓶；harem——后宫；beneath——在……下方。
- 为什么这样写：用"hot water bottle"建立 Orly的"温暖"——他"温暖"了 Rowan。
- 读者视角提示：Rowan 的"无法放开"是全书的"爱"主题——她"爱" Orly。
