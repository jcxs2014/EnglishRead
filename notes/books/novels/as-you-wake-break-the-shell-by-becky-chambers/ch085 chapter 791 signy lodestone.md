# 85. Chapter 791: Signy, Lodestone

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy at James's repair shop——Cora's small talk (Lunar Larceny recommendation), James (bearded father of two, repurposed colony containers), Rosie (toddler, "Simmy"), Signy's reflection on Cora's gift (small talk, sticky hands, community), "Simmy" (Rosie's name for Signy)。
- **情节位置**：Cypsela 线，Chapter 791；Signy-Cora's Lodestone market visit (repair shop)。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从 "tinkerer" (work) 到 reflecting on Cora's gift—her small talk, sticky hands, community; Signy's "I never really knew what to do with kids" to "Simmy" (Rosie's name)。
- **叙事手法**：ensemble scene (James, Rosie, Cora, Signy)；Signy's internal reflection (Cora's gift); "Simmy" as Rosie's name for Signy。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Signy dug idly around bins of bits and bolts, contentedly listening to Cora chat away with the repair shop's proprietor. This was Signy's ideal form of small talk—the kind where she didn't have to talk at all."

**中文理解**：Signy 理想的闲聊——“the kind where she didn't have to talk at all”。

**关键词**：ideal form of small talk / didn't have to talk at all

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的闲聊写成“the kind where she didn't have to talk at all”——她的理想；“contentedly listening”写出她的安宁。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 理想的闲聊——听，不说。

> **原句 2:** "—I'm telling you, it's a great show. And the music is stupid good." / "Y'know, I don't usually go for old radio, but you're making an awfully good case."

**中文理解**：Cora 的推荐——“Lunar Larceny”（Saturday Spectacular）推荐给 James。

**关键词**：Lunar Larceny / Saturday Spectacular / stupid good

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的推荐写成“stupid good”——她的热情；James 的“awfully good case”写出 Cora 的说服力。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的闲聊——有说服力、热情。

> **原句 3:** "None of this was news to Signy, but she wanted to kiss Cora for it all the same."

**中文理解**：Signy 的感受——“she wanted to kiss Cora for it all the same”。

**关键词**：wanted to kiss Cora for it all the same

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的感受写成“wanted to kiss Cora”——她欣赏 Cora 的闲聊；“for it all the same”写出她的温暖。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的欣赏——Cora 的闲聊值得亲吻。

> **原句 4:** "The OCTA logos on the outside had long ago been painted over with a rough-but-heartfelt mural of Solace and its moonlets—Cypsela included, represented by a rock with a sprouting leaf."

**中文理解**：James 的店铺——OCTA logos 被壁画覆盖，画着 Solace 与其卫星（Cypsela = 一块长芽的石头）。

**关键词**：OCTA logos painted over / mural of Solace and its moonlets / Cypsela represented by a rock with a sprouting leaf

**为什么这样写**：把 James 的店铺写成“rough-but-heartfelt mural”——他的艺术；“Cypsela represented by a rock with a sprouting leaf”写出 Signy 的辨认（她的工作）。

**读者视角提示**：James 的店铺——OCTA logos 被覆盖，Cypsela 如长芽的石头。

> **原句 5:** "Simmy!" a little voice cried."

**中文理解**：Rosie 对 Signy 的称呼——“Simmy!”。

**关键词**：Simmy

**为什么这样写**：把 Rosie 的称呼写成“Simmy!”——她对 Signy 的误读；“a little voice cried”写出她的兴奋。

**读者视角提示**：Rosie 对 Signy 的称呼——“Simmy”（误读）。

> **原句 6:** "Corale swooped in, scooped the now-shrieking kid into her arms, flipped her around, and held her aloft by her ankles."

**中文理解**：Cora 带孩子——“scooped the now-shrieking kid into her arms, flipped her around, and held her aloft by her ankles”。

**关键词**：scooped / flipped her around / held her aloft by her ankles

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 带孩子写成“scooped... flipped... held her aloft”——她的身体力行；“now-shrieking”写出 Rosie 的欢喜。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 带孩子——身体力行、 playful。

> **原句 7:** "Rosie stared at Cora for a moment, then ran forward into Cora's legs, her chubby arms stretched upward in the universal gesture of Do that again."

**中文理解**：Rosie 的手势——“chubby arms stretched upward in the universal gesture of Do that again.”。

**关键词**：chubby arms stretched upward / universal gesture of Do that again

**为什么这样写**：把 Rosie 的手势写成“universal gesture of Do that again”——Signy 的观察；“chubby arms”写出她的身体。

**读者视角提示**：Rosie 的手势——通用的“Do that again”。

> **原句 8:** "Signy never really knew what to do with kids, or what to say. Each of them spoke their own private language. Each of them came at life from their own angle."

**中文理解**：Signy 对孩子的不确定——“never really knew what to do with kids” / “Each of them spoke their own private language”。

**关键词**：never really knew what to do with kids / private language

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的不确定写成“never really knew what to do with kids”——她的诚实；“Each of them spoke their own private language”写出她的观察。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 对孩子的不确定——私人的语言、各自的角度。

> **原句 9:** "Cora, on the other hand, could flip a child like a sack of flour. She could make them laugh, make them sleep, make them stop crying."

**中文理解**：Cora 带孩子——“could flip a child like a sack of flour” / “make them laugh, make them sleep, make them stop crying”。

**关键词**：flip a child like a sack of flour / make them laugh, make them sleep, make them stop crying

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的本事写成“flip a child like a sack of flour”——Signy 的隐喻；“make them laugh, make them sleep, make them stop crying”写出她的范围。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 带孩子——身体力行、情绪范围广。

> **原句 10:** "That was such a gift, in Signy's eyes. She had never been afraid of people, but she'd always felt a step apart."

**中文理解**：Signy 的评估——“That was such a gift, in Signy's eyes” / “She had never been afraid of people, but she'd always felt a step apart.”。

**关键词**：such a gift / felt a step apart

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的本事写成“such a gift”——Signy 的欣赏；“felt a step apart”写出她毕生的感受。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的自我评估——天赋（Cora 的本事）对比 a step apart（她的感受）。

> **原句 11:** "She no longer thought small talk was stupid, not if you were listening to someone who knew how to do it right."

**中文理解**：Signy 的转变——“She no longer thought small talk was stupid”。

**关键词**：no longer thought small talk was stupid

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的转变写成“no longer thought small talk was stupid”——她的成长；“if you were listening to someone who knew how to do it right”写出她的条件。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的成长——闲聊不再愚蠢（与 Cora 一起）。

> **原句 12:** "Passing through wasn't the same as living in. Cora had unlocked that door for her, and everything on the other side was so bright."

**中文理解**：Signy 的领悟——“Passing through wasn't the same as living in. Cora had unlocked that door for her, and everything on the other side was so bright.”。

**关键词**：Passing through wasn't the same as living in / unlocked that door / everything on the other side was so bright

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的领悟写成“Passing through wasn't the same as living in”——她的洞察；“unlocked that door... everything on the other side was so bright”写出 Cora 的影响。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的领悟——Cora 为她打开了社群之门。

> **原句 13:** "Hello, Rosie," she said. / "Simmy." Rosie grinned gummily, before bursting into giggles again. "Up-sye down." / "Yes, you are," Signy confirmed, returning the smile. "You are upside down."

**中文理解**：Signy 与 Rosie 的互动——“Hello, Rosie” / “Simmy” / “Up-sye down” / “Yes, you are. You are upside down.”。

**关键词**：Hello, Rosie / Simmy / Up-sye down / You are upside down

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy-Rosie 的互动写成“Simmy” / “Up-sye down”——Rosie 的语言；Signy 的“Yes, you are. You are upside down.”写出她的参与。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Rosie 的互动——“Simmy”（她的名字）、“Up-sye down”（Rosie 的用语）。
