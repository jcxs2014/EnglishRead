# 93. Chapter 44: Signy, Fortune

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy & Otto (Otto's heart attack, hospice, "Where's Henry?", Nurse Arlo's warning about flagged blood pressure meds, "You need to shut it down. Now.", "My friend is dying", Cora arrives with groceries, "I've got you")。
- **情节位置**：Fortune 线 Chapter 44，紧跟 ch092 (Chapter 43)；Otto's hospice + Arlo's warning。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从 "tinkerer" (work) 到 "My friend is dying"——her crisis (Arlo's warning vs patient needs); Otto 从 "brilliant, kind, hilarious" to hospice (confused, "Where's Henry?")。
- **叙事手法**：ensemble (Otto, Arlo, Signy, Cora, Darcy, Ramona, Sam)；Arlo's warning (flagged meds) as plot point; "I've got you" closing (Cora)。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Certain things in the natural world followed others so predictably, they never came as a surprise. Acid rain followed a volcanic eruption. Root rot followed overwatering. Tissue damage followed a severe heart attack, as did arrhythmia and organ failure. And just as nobody could do anything about acid rain other than take cover, and just as there was no cure for soggy roots more sophisticated than letting them dry out—sometimes, there was no way to tell a body that had badly malfunctioned how to keep going."

**中文理解**：Signy's opening——natural world's predictability (acid rain, root rot, tissue damage)——"sometimes, there was no way to tell a body that had badly malfunctioned how to keep going."。

**关键词**：Certain things in the natural world followed others so predictably / no way to tell a body that had badly malfunctioned how to keep going

**读者视角提示**：Signy's opening—heart attack as natural consequence。
