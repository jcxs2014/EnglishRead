# 90. Chapter 793: Signy, Cypsela

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy finds Cora crying in the nook——reading Lily Marten's essay (ch009 interstitial)——"Oh, sweetheart... Sometimes you should look." / "Well, I can't speak for you, but it helps me. Sometimes I have to look, too."。
- **情节位置**：Cypsela 线，Chapter 793；Signy-Cora's nook scene (Lily Marten's essay)。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从 "tinkerer" (work) 到 "Sometimes I have to look, too."——her care for Cora's grief; Cora 从 "Oh, it's—old shit. Stupid." to Signy's comfort。
- **叙事手法**：nook scene (Cora crying, reading Lily Marten's essay)；Signy's care (hair, nuzzle)；"Sometimes I have to look, too." as mutual grief。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The very last touch had been the nook—a snug, semispheric pocket carved right up against Cypsela's surface."

**中文理解**：nook——"snug, semispheric pocket carved right up against Cypsela's surface"。

**关键词**：snug, semispheric pocket / carved right up against Cypsela's surface

**为什么这样写**：把 nook 写成 "snug, semispheric pocket"—Signy's design; "carved right up against Cypsela's surface"写出 its location (surface window)。

**读者视角提示**：Nook—Signy's design, surface window, decorative plants。

> **原句 2:** "Signy had assumed she'd find Cora resting in there after the market, as she often did. What she hadn't planned on was finding Cora in tears."

**中文理解**：Signy's expectation——"find Cora resting" vs "finding Cora in tears"。

**关键词**：finding Cora in tears

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's expectation 写成 "finding Cora in tears"—her surprise; "What she hadn't planned on"写出 her unpreparedness。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's surprise—Cora crying。

> **原句 3:** "Oh, it's—" Cora, puffy and red, tried to dry her face on her own bare forearm. When that didn't work, she pulled up the bottom edge of her shirt and pressed it to her eyes. "—old shit. Stupid. See, this is why I shouldn't look at this stuff."

**中文理解**：Cora's "old shit. Stupid."——"this is why I shouldn't look at this stuff" (Lily Marten's essay)。

**关键词**：old shit / Stupid / this is why I shouldn't look at this stuff

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora's grief 写成 "old shit. Stupid."—her self-dismissal; "this is why I shouldn't look at this stuff"写出 her avoidance pattern。

**读者视角提示**：Cora's grief—Lily Marten's essay (ch009), "old shit" (past trauma)。

> **原句 4:** "Oh, sweetheart," Signy said. She pulled Cora into her arms. "Yes, you should look. Sometimes you should look."

**中文理解**：Signy's response——"Oh, sweetheart" / "Yes, you should look. Sometimes you should look."。

**关键词**：Oh, sweetheart / Yes, you should look / Sometimes you should look

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's response 写成 "Oh, sweetheart"—her tenderness; "Yes, you should look. Sometimes you should look."写出 her encouragement (repeated "look" for emphasis)。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's care—encouraging Cora to look (grieve)。

> **原句 5:** "It doesn't help," Cora managed to say."

**中文理解**：Cora's "It doesn't help."。

**关键词**：It doesn't help

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora's grief 写成 "It doesn't help"—her despair; "managed to say"写出 her effort。

**读者视角提示**：Cora's grief—looking doesn't help (she thinks)。

> **原句 6:** "Well, I can't speak for you," Signy said. "But it helps me." She kissed the crown of Cora's head and nuzzled her nose there. "Sometimes I have to look, too."

**中文理解**：Signy's "Well, I can't speak for you, but it helps me." / "Sometimes I have to look, too."。

**关键词**：I can't speak for you / it helps me / Sometimes I have to look, too

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's care 写成 "I can't speak for you, but it helps me"—her honesty; "Sometimes I have to look, too"写出 her shared grief (mutual)。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's care—shared grief ("Sometimes I have to look, too")。
