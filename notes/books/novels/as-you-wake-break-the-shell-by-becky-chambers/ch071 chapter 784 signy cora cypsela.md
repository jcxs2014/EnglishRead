# 71. Chapter 784: Signy & Cora, Cypsela

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy & Cora's domestic dialogue——dishes, cake, chicory, haircut, pants patch, "We've never minded the floor" / "We are too old for the floor" / "Bed? / Bed. / Yes. / Yes. / More. / Yes."。
- **情节位置**：Cypsela 线，Chapter 784；Signy-Cora's daily domestic routine。
- **人物弧线**：Signy & Cora 从 chores (dishes, cake) 到 intimacy ("Bed? / Bed.")——their domestic rhythm。
- **叙事手法**：dialogue-driven (alternating lines, no speaker tags beyond implicit)；domestic comedy；"More. / Yes." as closing.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "We're almost out of hot sauce."

**中文理解**：开场——“We're almost out of hot sauce.”——家庭存货。

**关键词**：hot sauce

**为什么这样写**：把 domestic life 写成“almost out of hot sauce”——例行；“We're”写出共同的 household。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的家庭例行——共享的存货。

> **原句 2:** "We're almost out of a lot of things. We should make a market run."

**中文理解**：Cora 的补充——“almost out of a lot of things” / “market run”。

**关键词**：almost out of a lot of things / market run

**为什么这样写**：把 domestic life 写成“market run”——他们共同的差事；“a lot of things”写出 household 的消耗。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的家庭例行——计划 market run。

> **原句 3:** "How about Saturday?" / "Saturday's good."

**中文理解**：Saturday 的 market run——“How about Saturday?” / “Saturday's good.”。

**关键词**：Saturday's good

**为什么这样写**：把 scheduling 写成“Saturday's good”——他们的轻松；“How about”写出随意的协商。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的轻松——排期随意。

> **原句 4:** "Do we have enough fuel, or should we call for a fill-up?" / "I'll check the tank. But not right now."

**中文理解**：fuel 检查——“I'll check the tank. But not right now.”。

**关键词**：check the tank / not right now

**为什么这样写**：把 fuel 写成“not right now”——Cora 的推迟；“But not right now”写出她的优先（此刻胜过 fuel）。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的优先——此刻胜过 fuel。

> **原句 5:** "This was delicious." / "I love how much you love this soup." / "I love how well you make it."

**中文理解**：soup 的欣赏——“This was delicious” / “I love how much you love this soup” / “I love how well you make it.”。

**关键词**：This was delicious / I love how much you love this soup / I love how well you make it

**为什么这样写**：把 appreciation 写成相互的“I love”——他们相互的钦佩；“how much you love this soup” / “how well you make it”写出他们的互补。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的相互钦佩——soup 作为爱的语言。

> **原句 6:** "Would you hand me your bowl?" / "Sure. Can I help you clean up?" / "No, I'm good. Would you like some cake? Cup of tea?"

**中文理解**：餐后——“Would you hand me your bowl?” / “Can I help you clean up?” / “No, I'm good. Would you like some cake? Cup of tea?”。

**关键词**：Would you hand me your bowl / Can I help you clean up / Would you like some cake

**为什么这样写**：把 post-dinner 写成“Would you hand me your bowl”——他们的例行；“No, I'm good”写出 Signy 的独立；“cake? Cup of tea?”写出她的好客。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的家庭例行——餐后编排。

> **原句 7:** "Let's have chicory. You've done enough, I'll make a pot." / "Oh, thanks."

**中文理解**：Cora 做 chicory——“You've done enough, I'll make a pot.”。

**关键词**：You've done enough, I'll make a pot

**为什么这样写**：把 chicory 写成“You've done enough, I'll make a pot”——Cora 的体贴；“You've done enough”写出她的辨认。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的体贴——接手 chicory。

> **原句 8:** "I think your haircut turned out nice." / "You always do a good job."

**中文理解**：Signy 对发型的赞美——“You always do a good job.”。

**关键词**：You always do a good job

**为什么这样写**：把 haircut 写成“You always do a good job”——Signy 信任 Cora；“always”写出她的信任。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的信任——Cora 给 Signy 剪发。

> **原句 9:** "We need to get these knives sharpened." / "We can bring them on Saturday. I need to get these pants patched, too."

**中文理解**：家务清单——“knives sharpened” / “pants patched”。

**关键词**：knives sharpened / pants patched

**为什么这样写**：把 chores 写成“knives sharpened / pants patched”——他们共同的差事；“We can bring them on Saturday”写出规划。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的家务清单——共同的差事。

> **原句 10:** "If you want clean dishes, stop doing that." / "Forget the dishes." / "Fine. Forget the chicory." / "I already did."

**中文理解**：分心——“If you want clean dishes, stop doing that.” / “Forget the dishes.” / “Fine. Forget the chicory.” / “I already did.”。

**关键词**：Forget the dishes / Forget the chicory / I already did

**为什么这样写**：把 distraction 写成升级的“forget”——Signy 的优先（亲密胜过碗碟/chicory）；“I already did”写出 Cora 的放弃。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的优先——亲密胜过家务。

> **原句 11:** "We've never minded the floor." / "We are too old for the floor."

**中文理解**：地板对比床——“We've never minded the floor.” / “We are too old for the floor.”。

**关键词**：We've never minded the floor / We are too old for the floor

**为什么这样写**：把 surface choice 写成“too old for the floor”——他们的衰老；“We've never minded”写出他们的历史。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的衰老——“too old for the floor”。

> **原句 12:** "Bed?" / "Bed." / "Yes." / "Yes." / "More." / "Yes."

**中文理解**：收尾——“Bed?” / “Bed.” / “Yes.” / “Yes.” / “More.” / “Yes.”。

**关键词**：Bed? / Bed. / Yes. / More. / Yes.

**为什么这样写**：把 closing 写成“Bed? / Bed. / Yes. / Yes. / More. / Yes.”——对话如诗；“More.”写出 Cora 的请求；最后的“Yes.”写出 Signy 的同意。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的收尾——床、是、更多、是。
