# 88. Chapter 40: Cora, The Stables

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "There were lots of tricks for dealing with comedown after leaving the saddle. Some pilots liked to do body scans... Some pilots liked to watch a movie, listen to a show, find a distraction. Some pilots swore by cold sponge baths; others just wanted to sleep until it was over."

**中文理解**：comedown 的应对——body scans、电影、cold sponge baths、睡觉。

**关键词**：body scans / movies / cold sponge baths / sleep

**为什么这样写**：把 comedown 写成“tricks”——pilots 的 coping 方法；“Some pilots liked... others just wanted”写出个体的差异。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的 comedown——pilots 的 coping 方法。

> **原句 2:** "Cora's go-to was playing five things, which was best if you played it with someone else, but you could also do it solo."

**中文理解**：Cora 的“five things”游戏——“best if you played it with someone else, but you could also do it solo”。

**关键词**：five things / best if played with someone else / solo

**为什么这样写**：把 game 写成“five things”——Cora 的 coping；“best if played with someone else”写出 Signy 的角色（ch083 的“Tell me five kinds of pie”）。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的“five things”游戏——Signy 的 grounding 技巧（ch083）。

> **原句 3:** "Cora had cheeks. Colibri didn't have cheeks, but Cora did. It was important, to remember things like that."

**中文理解**：Cora 的自我提醒——“Cora had cheeks. Colibri didn't have cheeks, but Cora did. It was important, to remember things like that.”。

**关键词**：Cora had cheeks / Colibri didn't have cheeks / It was important, to remember things like that

**为什么这样写**：把 self-reminder 写成“Cora had cheeks”——她的锚定；“It was important, to remember things like that”写出她需要区分自我与 Colibri。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的 mindbleed coping——记住自我（cheeks）对比 Colibri。

> **原句 4:** "It was odd, it was odd to eat plants, Colibri had told her. Colibri did not eat organisms, she ate gases and vapors, the inorganic, the never-alive. Does it trouble you, to eat organisms? Colibri had asked, and Cora had replied, No, otherwise I'd die."

**中文理解**：Colibri 的提问——“Does it trouble you, to eat organisms?” / Cora 的“No, otherwise I'd die.”。

**关键词**：Does it trouble you, to eat organisms / No, otherwise I'd die

**为什么这样写**：把 Colibri 的提问写成“Does it trouble you, to eat organisms?”——她的好奇；Cora 的“No, otherwise I'd die”写出她的务实。

**读者视角提示**：Colibri 的好奇——吃生物（植物）令人不安。

> **原句 5:** "I hope not but I guess I don't know. I can't communicate with them, and Colibri said, You cannot communicate with me natively, either. Would you eat me if you could? and Cora replied, No, never, I would never eat a friend."

**中文理解**：Colibri 的“Would you eat me if you could?” / Cora 的“No, never, I would never eat a friend.”。

**关键词**：Would you eat me if you could / No, never, I would never eat a friend

**为什么这样写**：把 Colibri 的提问写成“Would you eat me if you could?”——她的直接；Cora 的“No, never, I would never eat a friend”写出她与 Colibri 的羁绊。

**读者视角提示**：Colibri 的直接——吃 rorqual？Cora 的拒绝（朋友）。

> **原句 6:** "Are Signy's plants not of her love? and Cora hadn't known what to do with that, because of course Signy's plants were of her love, but—"

**中文理解**：Colibri 的提问——“Are Signy's plants not of her love?”。

**关键词**：Are Signy's plants not of her love

**为什么这样写**：把 Colibri 的提问写成“Are Signy's plants not of her love?”——她的哲学探问（ch066 的“Potted plants seem lonely” / ch072 的“I do not think plants love. / How do you know? / I suppose I don't.”）。

**读者视角提示**：Colibri 的哲学探问——植物即“of her love”（Signy 的爱）。

> **原句 7:** "I swear you are evening out a lot faster than you used to." / "It's more of a pronounced trend than I'd expect solely from ginger clearing out of your system, though."

**中文理解**：Dr. Coleman 的观察——“evening out a lot faster than you used to” / “more of a pronounced trend than I'd expect solely from ginger clearing out of your system, though.”。

**关键词**：evening out a lot faster / more of a pronounced trend / ginger clearing out of your system

**为什么这样写**：把 Dr. Coleman 的观察写成“evening out a lot faster”——她的医学评估；“more of a pronounced trend than I'd expect solely from ginger clearing out”写出她的怀疑（Signy 的药起效）。

**读者视角提示**：Dr. Coleman 的观察——Cora 好转快于预期（Signy 的药）。

> **原句 8:** "My girlfriend said it's bad for me, and she's way scarier than you when she gets mad."

**中文理解**：Cora 的掩护——“My girlfriend said it's bad for me, and she's way scarier than you when she gets mad.”。

**关键词**：My girlfriend said it's bad for me / way scarier than you when she gets mad

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的掩护写成“My girlfriend said it's bad for me”——她的幽默；“way scarier than you when she gets mad”写出 Signy 的厉害。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的掩护——Signy 的影响（停止 ginger）。

> **原句 9:** "I was taking ginger for a while last year," she said. "But then I stopped. I know, I should've told you—"

**中文理解**：Cora 的坦白——“I was taking ginger for a while last year. But then I stopped.”。

**关键词**：I was taking ginger for a while last year / But then I stopped

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的坦白写成“I was taking ginger for a while last year”——她的诚实；“But then I stopped”写出她的改变（Signy 的影响）。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的坦白——ginger（因 Signy 而停）。

> **原句 10:** "You are allowed your paradoxes, Cora. I enjoy the time we spend with them."

**中文理解**：Colibri 的收尾——“You are allowed your paradoxes, Cora. I enjoy the time we spend with them.”。

**关键词**：You are allowed your paradoxes / I enjoy the time we spend with them

**为什么这样写**：把 Colibri 的收尾写成“You are allowed your paradoxes”——她的接纳；“I enjoy the time we spend with them”写出她欣赏 Cora 的矛盾。

**读者视角提示**：Colibri 的接纳——Cora 的悖论（吃“of her love”的植物，不吃 Colibri）。

> **原句 11:** "Goddammit," Cora sighed. "Five. Five flavors of porridge powder. Come on, Cora. Be here. Stay here. Five flavors of porridge powder . . ."

**中文理解**：Cora 的收尾——“Goddammit. Five. Five flavors of porridge powder. Come on, Cora. Be here. Stay here.”。

**关键词**：Goddammit / Five flavors of porridge powder / Be here. Stay here

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的收尾写成“Goddammit. Five. Five flavors of porridge powder.”——她 grounding 的尝试；“Be here. Stay here.”写出她的自我命令。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的 grounding——五种粥粉（自我命令）。
