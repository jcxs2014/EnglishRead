# 86. Chapter 39: Signy, Fortune

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy at police station with Cora (Twig arrested, bail paid), Cora-Twig argument (Sucker's Luck, stolen a bunch of fuel), Signy leaves, 灵长类电影类比 (screaming at each other / cuddled together), "For being someone who will hide with me" (ch074) callback, Sando Press vs Hot Stuff Skewer Shack ("stuff in bread")。
- **情节位置**：Fortune 线 Chapter 39，紧跟 ch080 (Chapter 36)；Signy-Cora's police station night。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从修理铺工作到 "For being someone who will hide with me" (ch074 callback)—her care for Cora; Cora from "I'm going to kill her" to bail payment + anger (Twig's stolen a bunch of fuel)。
- **叙事手法**：ensemble (police station)；Cora-Twig argument (Sucker's Luck, stolen a bunch of fuel); Signy's 灵长类电影类比 (screaming at each other / cuddled together); "stuff in bread" as Cora's comfort food.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "I'm going to kill her," Cora said."

**中文理解**：对方的开场——我真想杀了她；因为她妹妹被捕了。

**关键词**：I'm going to kill her

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的愤怒写成“I'm going to kill her”——她的沮丧；全章反复（呼应 ch074 的“I'm going to kill her” / “For being someone who will hide with me”）。

**读者视角提示**：对方的愤怒——妹妹被捕了。

> **原句 2:** "Signy wasn't sure she should be here for this, but she'd offered to come along, once their plans for the evening abruptly changed. Cora had accepted, because she didn't like to be alone."

**中文理解**：她的不确定——她不确定自己该不该来；对方答应了，因为她不喜欢一个人待着。

**关键词**：Signy wasn't sure she should be here / Cora had accepted, because she didn't like to be alone

**为什么这样写**：把这份不确定写成“不确定自己该不该来”——她尊重姐妹俩之间的羁绊；“对方答应了，因为不喜欢一个人待着”写出对方的需要。

**读者视角提示**：她的不确定——对方其实需要陪伴。

> **原句 3:** "It would suck less with a better drink," Signy replied. / "I owe you so big," Cora said. / "You don't owe me anything." / "Bullshit," Cora said. "Name it."

**中文理解**：两人的交换——我欠你太多了；你什么都不欠我；少来，你说吧。

**关键词**：I owe you so big / You don't owe me anything / Bullshit / Name it

**为什么这样写**：把两人的相处写成“我欠你太多了”——这是对方的感激；“你什么都不欠我”写出她的拒绝；“少来，你说吧”写出对方的坚持。

**读者视角提示**：两人的相处——感激遇上拒绝。

> **原句 4:** "Well, let's see . . ." She tilted her head back, making a show of thinking. "I think, when we're done here, we should get takeout at Sando Press."

**中文理解**：她的要求——去那家老地方买点外带。

**关键词**：get takeout at Sando Press

**为什么这样写**：把这个要求写成“去那家老地方买外带”——那是她常去的地方；“装模作样地想了一会儿”写出她的俏皮。

**读者视角提示**：她的要求——去那家叫三多馆的老地方。

> **原句 5:** "You don't like Sando Press." / "I don't. They oversalt their fries." / "And you're not going to put up any fight about it?" / "I said 'name it,' and if that means subpar fries, then—"

**中文理解**：对方的接受——要是那意味着薯条一般般，那也行。

**关键词**：subpar fries

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的接受写成“subpar fries”——她的愿意；“if that means subpar fries, then—”写出她的妥协。

**读者视角提示**：对方的妥协——为了她，接受一般的薯条。

> **原句 6:** "Twig was bruised, Twig was embarrassed, and Twig was handcuffed."

**中文理解**：妹妹的状态——带着伤，一脸窘迫，还戴着手铐。

**关键词**：bruised / embarrassed / handcuffed

**为什么这样写**：把 Twig 写成“bruised, embarrassed, handcuffed”——三个形容词；“handcuffed”写出她的被捕。

**读者视角提示**：妹妹的状态——带伤、窘迫、戴着手铐。

> **原句 7:** "Did you hurt her?" she demanded."

**中文理解**：对方的质问——你们伤到她了吗？问的是警察。

**关键词**：Did you hurt her

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的提问写成“Did you hurt her?”——她的保护；“demanded”写出她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：对方的保护——在意妹妹的安全。

> **原句 8:** "I fell," Twig mumbled. "For reals. I just fell." / "Ms. Marten tried to evade arrest and tripped as she was running," the cop said."

**中文理解**：妹妹说自己摔了一跤；而警察说的是她试图逃避逮捕时绊倒了。

**关键词**：I fell / tried to evade arrest and tripped

**为什么这样写**：把 Twig 的借口写成“I fell”——她的轻描淡写；警察的“tried to evade arrest”写出真相。

**读者视角提示**：Twig 的轻描淡写对比现实（逃避逮捕）。

> **原句 9:** "Why is she here?" / "Well, y'see," Cora said as she furiously entered her information into the tablet, "we were on our way to get takeout when I got a call from you. She came with me because she's nice and wanted to help, and also because otherwise, I might've fucking murdered you in the middle of a police station."

**中文理解**：对方的解释——我们本来去买外带，你打电话来了；她跟来是因为人好、想帮忙，也因为不然我可能在警局里就把你宰了。

**关键词**：we were on our way to get takeout / She came with me because she's nice and wanted to help / I might've fucking murdered you

**为什么这样写**：把这份解释写成“我可能就把你宰了”——这是她的幽默；“她跟来是因为人好”写出对方的关心。

**读者视角提示**：对方的幽默——既说对方的关心，也放句杀人狠话。

> **原句 10:** "Are you out of your fucking mind?" Cora continued. / "Do we have to do this here?" Twig said. / "Where else?" Cora said, throwing up her hands. "Your apartment? Oh, right, you don't have one. Sorry, I forgot—when I checked there last week, I learned you got kicked out. And you didn't pick up my calls about it—"

**中文理解**：姐妹俩的争吵——你是不是疯了？非得在这儿说吗？还能在哪？你的公寓？哦对，你没有公寓。

**关键词**：Are you out of your fucking mind / Where else? Your apartment? Oh, right, you don't have one

**为什么这样写**：把这场争吵写成“还能在哪？你的公寓？哦对，你没有公寓”——这是对方的尖锐；“你是不是疯了”写出她的愤怒。

**读者视角提示**：这场争吵——对方拿妹妹无家可归这件事戳她。

> **原句 11:** "You didn't have to come," Twig said. / "Okay? Well? I did." Cora gestured broadly at herself... "I'm here. Where have you been?"

**中文理解**：妹妹说你不必来的；对方答：我来了，可你去哪儿了？

**关键词**：You didn't have to come / I'm here. Where have you been

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的回应写成“I'm here. Where have you been?”——她的在场加追问；“You didn't have to come”写出 Twig 的防御。

**读者视角提示**：两人的相处——一个在场，一个防备。

> **原句 12:** "I have a guess," Cora goaded. "Would you like me to guess? Because I am pretty sure the reason I just coughed up three hundred bucks"—Twig cringed—"was because you were caught tagging along with some idiots who'd stolen a bunch of fuel. They didn't tell me for what, but so help me god, if you're still on this Sucker's Luck kick—"

**中文理解**：对方的猜测——她刚掏了三百块钱，多半是因为妹妹跟着一帮偷燃料的蠢货混在了一起。

**关键词**：coughed up three hundred bucks / tagging along with some idiots who'd stolen a bunch of fuel / Sucker's Luck kick

**为什么这样写**：把这份猜测写成“刚掏了三百块钱”——这是她的经济负担；“跟着一帮偷燃料的蠢货”写出妹妹的牵连；“对那桩事的痴迷”写出妹妹的兴趣所在。

**读者视角提示**：对方的猜测——妹妹卷入偷燃料，还痴迷着那桩事。

> **原句 13:** "Signy picked at a bit of lint on her pant leg. The problem with this exchange happening in the hallway was there wasn't another hallway to exit through."

**中文理解**：她的不适——这场对话发生在走廊里的麻烦在于，没有第二条走廊可以退出去。

**关键词**：The problem with this exchange happening in the hallway

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的不适写成“The problem with this exchange happening in the hallway”——她被困的感觉；“there wasn't another hallway to exit through”写出她的无处可逃。

**读者视角提示**：她的不适——被夹在走廊里，看着姐妹俩争吵。

> **原句 14:** "Signy cleared her throat, once. She turned her attention to Cora. "I'm going to the ferry station," she said. "If I'm not still there when you get there, I'll be at my place." She kissed Cora on the cheek, and gave Twig a neutral nod. "I hope you have a better night.""

**中文理解**：她的退场——我要去渡口站了；希望你今晚过得好一些。

**关键词**：I'm going to the ferry station / I hope you have a better night

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的退场写成“I'm going to the ferry station”——她优雅的退让；“I hope you have a better night”写出她对 Twig 的关心。

**读者视角提示**：她的退场——退让得体，还关心了妹妹一句。

> **原句 15:** "Signy wondered what it felt like, to have that kind of family. She wondered if it was better or worse that she'd never know."

**中文理解**：她的反思——她想知道有这样一家人是什么感觉；也想知道自己永远不会知道，是好事还是坏事。

**关键词**：what it felt like, to have that kind of family / better or worse that she'd never know

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的反思写成“what it felt like, to have that kind of family”——她的好奇；“better or worse that she'd never know”写出她的不确定。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的反思——对家庭羁绊的好奇。

> **原句 16:** "Signy had once seen a Primer movie of other primate species from Earth—peculiar cousins with thick brows and tails that behaved like arms. The movie had showed a pair screaming at each other, chasing and posturing with fangs bared to the upper gums. I'm going to kill her, the entire scene said. Then minutes later, they cuddled together in the crook of a tree, picking through fur and eating each other's fleas in inseparable atonement."

**中文理解**：她借灵长类纪录片打的比方——彼此尖叫，龇着牙追逐摆姿态；几分钟后又搂在一起，翻着毛、互相捉虱子，像是在不可分割地和解。

**关键词**：screaming at each other / chasing and posturing with fangs bared / cuddled together / picking through fur and eating each other's fleas / inseparable atonement

**为什么这样写**：把这个比方写成“尖叫……然后搂在一起……不可分割地和解”——这是她对姐妹羁绊的理解；“整场戏都在说‘我真想杀了她’”写出两者的平行。

**读者视角提示**：她的灵长类比方——姐妹俩的羁绊，先叫骂后依偎。

> **原句 17:** "Not skewers and flatbread specifically, but rather, anything from the category that Cora referred to as stuff in bread. Signy had learned that if you wanted to cheer Cora up, there was no going wrong with stuff in bread."

**中文理解**：她选的晚餐——夹馅面包，用的是对方那套分类，当作安慰食物。

**关键词**：stuff in bread

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的选择写成“stuff in bread”——她了解 Cora 的安慰食物；“there was no going wrong with stuff in bread”写出她的关心。

**读者视角提示**：她的关心——选了对方的安慰食物，夹馅面包。
