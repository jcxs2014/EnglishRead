# 75. Chapter 786: Cora, Lodestone

## 一句话总结

Cora 睡不着时用 tablet 学习新知识——这次学了人类进化树，发现 Lodestone 居民的"burrow deep"与 genetic ancestors 的相似性。

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Cora 睡不着时用 tablet 学习新知识——这次学了人类进化树，发现 Lodestone 居民的"burrow deep"与 genetic ancestors 的相似性。
- **情节位置**：Cypsela 线 Chapter 786，紧跟 ch073；Cora 的 insomnia and learning——establishes 她的性格 and foreshadows Signy 和 Cora 的 deeper bond。
- **人物弧线**：Cora 从"fireworks / waterfalls / roller coasters"到"tiny skittery guys"——her intellectual curiosity; roller coasters "had been a mistake"暗示 her regret。
- **叙事手法**：internal monologue; human evolution as metaphor for Ring society; humor ("that had been a mistake")。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Sometimes, when Cora was lounging around or if she couldn't sleep, she'd get her tablet and learn about something she'd never seen before. One time, she'd learned about fireworks. Another time, she'd learned about waterfalls. Another time, she'd learned about roller coasters, and that had been a mistake, because those sounded super fun, and she couldn't stop reading about them, and it hadn't helped her get back to sleep at all."

**中文理解**：对方的失眠——她随手学各种杂七杂八的东西；学云霄飞车那次是个错误，因为听起来太好玩了。

**关键词**：lounging around / couldn't sleep / that had been a mistake

**为什么这样写**：用一串列举写出她那份按捺不住的好奇心；而“那次是个错误”是一句自嘲——睡不着的时候去学好玩的东西，只会适得其反。

**读者视角提示**：她的性格——好奇、坐不住，容易被有趣的东西带跑。

> **原句 2:** "This other time, though, she'd learned about what kinds of animals humans were descended from. Mammals and primates were concepts she'd learned in school, because everybody learned that, but she'd never had cause to walk through the family tree step by step. She found out that if you went back past apes and monkeys, and then back further, then back even further still, you eventually wound up with these tiny skittery guys—tiny, tiny skittery guys that burrowed underground and didn't come out until after dark, when all the big scary guys had gone to sleep."

**中文理解**：人类的演化——从猿到猴，再到那些四处乱窜的小东西；而那位躲进地下的祖先，正为石岩站的居民埋下伏笔。

**关键词**：descended from / family tree / tiny skittery guys / burrowed underground

**为什么这样写**：用“四处乱窜的小东西”这样随意的说法讲科学发现——写出她与知识之间那种不拘礼节的关系；“躲进地下”是个关键隐喻，石岩站的居民也是这样生活的。

**读者视角提示**：她的隐喻——她后来会把姐妹俩的关系比作这些灵长类。

> **原句 3:** "Cora thought of the tiny skittery guys sometimes, when she considered the places people lived in the Ring. Some folks lived in moonlets, like she did. A single rock carved out for a family or two. The majority of their community, however, lived on Lodestone, the biggest little moon on the block. And just like their genetic ancestors, the people there had burrowed deep. Way deep. Lodestone didn't have an atmosphere, and its flimsy magnetic field wasn't enough to shield people from radiation. The only thing to do was dig down."

**中文理解**：环系的居住模式——小卫星很小，而石岩站是最大的那一颗；那里的居民往深处挖，这也为她和对方的关系埋下伏笔。

**关键词**：moonlets / biggest little moon / burrowed deep / dig down

**为什么这样写**：从那些乱窜的小东西过渡到石岩站——“往深处挖”这个隐喻把进化史与环系社会连了起来；“挖得很深”用重复加以强调；最后那句“唯一能做的就是往下挖”既是结论，也是伏笔。

**读者视角提示**：她的内省——她在想家、想归属，以及“往深处挖”在一段关系里意味着什么。
