# 91. Chapter 42: Signy, Fortune

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "There had to be a way to make this work."

**中文理解**：Signy's opening——"There had to be a way to make this work." (apartment search)。

**关键词**：There had to be a way to make this work

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's determination 写成 "There had to be a way"—her problem-solving; "to make this work"写出 her goal (living together)。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's determination—apartment search。

> **原句 2:** "There are three different floor plans available, depending on your needs," he said, displaying the options on one of his computer monitors. "Do either of you have any extended family you want to have room for? Elderly relatives?" He glanced at Cora. "Live-in siblings?"

**中文理解**：Red's floor plans——"three different floor plans" + "Elderly relatives? Live-in siblings?"。

**关键词**：three different floor plans / Elderly relatives / Live-in siblings

**为什么这样写**：把 Red's options 写成 "three different floor plans"—his efficiency; "Elderly relatives? Live-in siblings?"写出 his thoroughness。

**读者视角提示**：Red's efficiency—floor plans, family questions。

> **原句 3:** "No," Cora and Signy replied at the same time."

**中文理解**：Cora & Signy's simultaneous "No" (no extended family, no kids)。

**关键词**：No / at the same time

**为什么这样写**：把 their answer 写成 "at the same time"—their synchronization; "No"写出 their shared decision。

**读者视角提示**：Cora-Signy's synchronization—shared "No" (no kids)。

> **原句 4:** "No kitchen?" she asked. / "No, all cooking spaces here at the dorms are communal," Red said. "There are kitchens available if you want to cook, but most folks just use the cafeteria or go out to eat."

**中文理解**：no kitchen——"all cooking spaces here at the dorms are communal"。

**关键词**：No kitchen / communal

**为什么这样写**：把 no-kitchen 写成 "all cooking spaces are communal"—Flytown's design; "most folks just use the cafeteria"写出 the norm。

**读者视角提示**：Flytown's design—communal kitchens, no private kitchen。

> **原句 5:** "It's not like you cook anyway," Cora said to Signy with a somewhat nervous smile."

**中文理解**：Cora's "It's not like you cook anyway"——nervous smile。

**关键词**：It's not like you cook anyway / nervous smile

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora's reassurance 写成 "It's not like you cook anyway"—her attempt to ease; "nervous smile"写出 her anxiety。

**读者视角提示**：Cora's reassurance—nervous smile (anxious about Signy's comfort)。

> **原句 6:** "Signy didn't really know her neighbors on raft sixteen. She kept to herself, didn't chat at the taps, didn't chat in the showers, just used her apartment as the point A mundanely destined for a point B."

**中文理解**：Signy's raft sixteen life——"kept to herself" / "point A mundanely destined for a point B"。

**关键词**：kept to herself / point A mundanely destined for a point B

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's life 写成 "point A mundanely destined for a point B"—her routine; "kept to herself"写出 her introversion。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's introversion—raft sixteen as point A to point B。

> **原句 7:** "Pilots, as she'd learned, were gossipy as hell in one another's company. Signy had neither patience for nor interest in that, but she was also afraid that if she did keep to herself, it would reflect badly on Cora."

**中文理解**：Signy's fear——"Pilots... gossipy as hell" / "afraid that if she did keep to herself, it would reflect badly on Cora"。

**关键词**：gossipy as hell / afraid that if she did keep to herself, it would reflect badly on Cora

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's fear 写成 "gossipy as hell"—her assessment; "afraid that if she did keep to herself, it would reflect badly on Cora"写出 her concern for Cora's reputation。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's fear—Flytown gossip reflecting on Cora。

> **原句 8:** "But they both knew it would be hard tacking a commute onto a pilot's complicated schedule, and Signy's apartment was already starting to feel cramped for any shared use longer than an overnight stay."

**中文理解**：Signy's reasoning——"hard tacking a commute onto a pilot's complicated schedule" / "Signy's apartment was already starting to feel cramped"。

**关键词**：hard tacking a commute / Signy's apartment was already starting to feel cramped

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's reasoning 写成 "hard tacking a commute"—her practicality; "Signy's apartment was already starting to feel cramped"写出 her space limitation。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's reasoning—commute hard for Cora, apartment cramped。

> **原句 9:** "Lots of people was not a claim supported by good apartments standing empty, but Signy didn't say anything further."

**中文理解**：Signy's observation——"Lots of people was not a claim supported by good apartments standing empty"。

**关键词**：Lots of people was not a claim supported by good apartments standing empty

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's observation 写成 "Lots of people was not a claim supported by good apartments standing empty"—her skepticism; "didn't say anything further"写出 her restraint。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's skepticism—Red's "lots of people" unsupported by empty apartments。

> **原句 10:** "It's an emergency." / "What is it?" / "It's Otto," Ramona replied, her voice cracking. "It's his heart."

**中文理解**：Ramona's call——"It's an emergency" / "It's Otto" / "It's his heart."。

**关键词**：It's an emergency / It's Otto / It's his heart

**为什么这样写**：把 Ramona's call 写成 "It's his heart"—her urgency; "her voice cracking"写出 her distress; "It's Otto" (not "Otto is") writes the personal connection。

**读者视角提示**：Ramona's call—Otto's heart emergency。
