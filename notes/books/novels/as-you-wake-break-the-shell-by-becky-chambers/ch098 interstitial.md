# 98. Interstitial: Twig's Letter to Cora (forcora.rd)

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Twig's letter to Cora (forcora.rd)——Sucker's Luck expedition, "I'm going to see you out in space", "I love you, sis. You are the best one in the whole house. See you soon."。
- **情节位置**：全书间插文本，Twig's letter found in her computer (ch097's "forcora.rd")。
- **人物弧线**：Twig 从 "fuckup" (ch092) 到 Sucker's Luck crew member——her escape from Fortune; Cora's fear ("I'm going to kill her" / "I'm this close to calling the cops")。
- **叙事手法**：epistolary (letter format)；Twig's voice (apologetic, excited, hopeful)；"You are the best one in the whole house" (callback to ch033's "the best one in the whole house")。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Please don't be mad at me. You probably are mad at me and I'm really sorry."

**中文理解**：Twig's opening——"Please don't be mad at me. You probably are mad at me and I'm really sorry."。

**关键词**：Please don't be mad at me / You probably are mad at me / I'm really sorry

**为什么这样写**：把 Twig's letter 写成 "Please don't be mad at me"—her apology; "You probably are mad at me"写出 her awareness; "I'm really sorry"写出 her regret。

**读者视角提示**：Twig's letter—apologetic opening。

> **原句 2:** "I know you won't understand why I've done this but you'll see, I'll prove you wrong this time. This time you'll be the dipshit, ha ha."

**中文理解**：Twig's justification——"I'll prove you wrong this time. This time you'll be the dipshit, ha ha."。

**关键词**：I'll prove you wrong this time / This time you'll be the dipshit

**为什么这样写**：把 Twig's justification 写成 "I'll prove you wrong this time"—her defiance; "This time you'll be the dipshit, ha ha"写出 her humor (callback to Cora's "dipshit")。

**读者视角提示**：Twig's justification—defiance + humor (callback to Cora's "dipshit")。

> **原句 3:** "I'm going to see you out in space. I know you said rorquals can't hear the ships that leave from the surface, but I know that's not true."

**中文理解**：Twig's claim——"I'm going to see you out in space" / "rorquals can't hear the ships that leave from the surface, but I know that's not true."。

**关键词**：I'm going to see you out in space / rorquals can't hear the ships that leave from the surface, but I know that's not true

**为什么这样写**：把 Twig's claim 写成 "I'm going to see you out in space"—her hope; "rorquals can't hear the ships... but I know that's not true"写出 her belief (ch038's "Colibri can feel the Mothership")。

**读者视角提示**：Twig's claim—rorquals can hear ships (belief, not fact)。

**为什么这样写**：把 Twig's reassurance 写成 "these guys are legit"—her trust in Chapter 252; "really good at math" / "computer models"写出 her naivety (trusting amateurs)。

**读者视角提示**：Twig's reassurance—naive trust in Chapter 252 (ch038's "Sucker's Luck" discussion)。

**中文理解**：Twig's honesty——"I lied when I said it pays good because it doesn't actually pay anything" / "I get to be part of something actually good for once."。
