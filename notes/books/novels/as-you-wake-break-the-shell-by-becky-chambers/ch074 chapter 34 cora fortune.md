# 74. Chapter 33: Signy, Fortune

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy & Cora's raft 2 date——police checkpoint (Signy's pill bottle "Vitamin C"), "It's very important that you turn around and kiss me right now", bathroom hiding, "Oh, good. It's just us." (Signy's selfish thought), "Stuff to do with Signy" list, "For being someone who will hide with me"。
- **情节位置**：Fortune 线 Chapter 33，紧跟 ch068 (Chapter 31)；Signy-Cora's date + police checkpoint。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从 "tinkerer" (work) 到 "Oh, good. It's just us." (selfish relief)——her growing attachment to Cora; Cora from confident to "I have some ginger on me, but, um..." (mindbleed fear)。
- **叙事手法**：ensemble (Signy-Cora dialogue)；police checkpoint as plot device (forced proximity); "Stuff to do with Signy" list as Cora's vulnerability; "For being someone who will hide with me" as emotional close。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Signy hadn't ever had much reason (or desire) to explore rafts she didn't live or work on. Cora, on the other hand, seemed to always have the pulse of something going on somewhere."

**中文理解**：Signy 的探索——Cora “always had the pulse of something going on somewhere”。

**关键词**：pulse of something going on somewhere

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 写成“pulse of something going on”——她的社交能量；“Signy hadn't ever had much reason (or desire)”写出 Signy 的内向。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 与 Cora 的对比——内向对比社交联结者。

> **原句 2:** "She spotted a jacket that was not adorned with aspirational pastiche, and it was a sight, like so many others, that had started to give her a little flutter when she noticed it."

**中文理解**：Signy 的“flutter”——Cora 的夹克（非 aspirational pastiche）。

**关键词**：flutter / aspirational pastiche

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的吸引写成“flutter”——她滋长的感觉；“aspirational pastiche”写出 raft 2 的做作。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的吸引——Cora 作为“flutter”的触发。

> **原句 3:** "I know it's silly," Cora said with a smirk, a shrug, a blush. "But I saw a lady selling them, and—"

**中文理解**：Cora 的纸花——“I know it's silly”加“I saw a lady selling them”。

**关键词**：I know it's silly / saw a lady selling them

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的礼物写成“silly”——她的自贬；“saw a lady selling them”写出她的即兴。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的礼物——纸花（即兴、傻气）。

> **原句 4:** "No, it's lovely," Signy said, accepting the bouquet of folded paper flowers. Sure, it was silly, too, but wasn't it awfully nice, to have somebody who wanted to do silly things for you?"

**中文理解**：Signy 的接受——“No, it's lovely” / “wasn't it awfully nice, to have somebody who wanted to do silly things for you?”。

**关键词**：No, it's lovely / awfully nice / somebody who wanted to do silly things for you

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的接受写成“No, it's lovely”——她的温暖；“awfully nice, to have somebody who wanted to do silly things for you”写出她的欣赏。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的欣赏——Cora 傻气的礼物。

> **原句 5:** "In her left pocket, beneath her barrier pants, she carried a bottle labeled Vitamin C. / The bottle was full. / The bottle was for Cora."

**中文理解**：Signy 的药瓶——“Vitamin C”标签，满的，给 Cora。

**关键词**：Vitamin C / full / for Cora

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的药写成“Vitamin C”（euphemism）——她的秘密；“The bottle was full. / The bottle was for Cora.”写出它的重要。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的药——给 Cora 的 Vitamin C 瓶。

> **原句 6:** "It's very important that you turn around and kiss me right now."

**中文理解**：Signy 的命令——“It's very important that you turn around and kiss me right now.”。

**关键词**：It's very important that you turn around and kiss me right now

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的命令写成“It's very important”——她的急切（警察在看）；“turn around and kiss me right now”写出她的策略。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的策略——亲吻作为掩护（警察在看）。

> **原句 7:** "Thank goodness for a lover who didn't argue."

**中文理解**：Signy 的释然——“Thank goodness for a lover who didn't argue.”。

**关键词**：Thank goodness for a lover who didn't argue

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的释然写成“Thank goodness for a lover who didn't argue”——她欣赏 Cora 的顺从；“didn't argue”写出 Cora 的信任。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的释然——Cora 的顺从（不多问）。

> **原句 8:** "Oh, good. It's just us."

**中文理解**：Signy 自私的念头——“Oh, good. It's just us.”。

**关键词**：Oh, good. It's just us

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的念头写成“Oh, good. It's just us”——她自私的解脱；“It's just us”写出她渴望 Cora 的陪伴。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 自私的念头——渴望 Cora 的陪伴胜过收容他人。

> **原句 9:** "I have some ginger on me, but, um . . ." / "Well, good news. In two days, I'll have some real medicine ready for you."

**中文理解**：Cora 的 ginger 快用完 / Signy 的“real medicine ready in two days”。

**关键词**：some ginger on me / real medicine ready

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的用药写成“some ginger on me”——她仅剩的补给；Signy 的“real medicine ready in two days”写出她的工作进展。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的药物——ginger（临时）→ real medicine（即将）。

> **原句 10:** "I don't want you to see me if it hits," she said, very quietly."

**中文理解**：Cora 的恐惧——“I don't want you to see me if it hits”。

**关键词**：I don't want you to see me if it hits

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的恐惧写成“I don't want you to see me if it hits”——她的脆弱；“very quietly”写出她的羞耻。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的 mindbleed 恐惧——脆弱、羞耻。

> **原句 11:** "I'm not a doctor," she said. "But my mother was. I have seen . . . a lot."

**中文理解**：Signy 的安抚——“I'm not a doctor, but my mother was. I have seen . . . a lot.”。

**关键词**：I'm not a doctor / my mother was / I have seen . . . a lot

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的安抚写成“my mother was”（医生）——她的医学背景；“I have seen . . . a lot”写出她的经验。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的医学背景——母亲是医生、诊所长大。

> **原句 12:** "Would you rather we just do that?" Cora asked, with heartrending earnestness. "Seriously, this is your space, we don't have to—"

**中文理解**：Cora 的提议——“Would you rather we just do that?”——给 Signy 空间。

**关键词**：Would you rather we just do that / heartrending earnestness

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的体贴写成“Would you rather we just do that”——她尊重 Signy 的空间；“heartrending earnestness”写出她的真诚。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的体贴——尊重 Signy 的空间。

> **原句 13:** "Would you still like to take that break?" she asked."

**中文理解**：Signy 的提问——“Would you still like to take that break?”——重申他们的亲密。

**关键词**：Would you still like to take that break

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的提问写成“Would you still like to take that break”——她的邀请；“still”写出她希望一切未变。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的邀请——尽管 mindbleed 恐惧仍重申亲密。

> **原句 14:** "The worry on Cora's face vanished, and all that remained was her smile. "Yes," she said."

**中文理解**：Cora 的回应——“The worry on Cora's face vanished, and all that remained was her smile.”。

**关键词**：worry vanished / all that remained was her smile

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的回应写成“worry vanished... smile remained”——她的释然；“all that remained”写出她情绪的简化。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的释然——Signy 接纳她的 mindbleed。

> **原句 15:** "Stuff to do with Signy" (list) / "I don't care at all about—what did you just say? Going out on the town?" / "I'm trying to impress you!" / "Because you're the coolest person I've ever met, and all I do and all I talk about is pilot shit, and I don't want you to get bored with me."

**中文理解**：Cora 的“Stuff to do with Signy”清单（archival movies、ceramics show、Blue Moon、Crumbs、massage/steam bath、BBQ pop-up、Leaf and Stem）加“I'm trying to impress you!”加“Because you're the coolest person I've ever met”。

**关键词**：Stuff to do with Signy / I'm trying to impress you / coolest person I've ever met

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的脆弱写成“Stuff to do with Signy”——她的努力；“I'm trying to impress you”写出她的恐惧；“coolest person I've ever met”写出她的钦佩。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的脆弱——试图打动 Signy，害怕被厌倦。

> **原句 16:** "For being someone who will hide with me" was how Signy replied, but it didn't encompass all that she felt. For being someone who wants to, she thought."

**中文理解**：Signy 的收尾——“For being someone who will hide with me” / “For being someone who wants to”。

**关键词**：For being someone who will hide with me / For being someone who wants to

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的感激写成“For being someone who will hide with me”——她的欣赏；“For being someone who wants to”（未说出口）写出她更深的感受。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的感激——Cora 愿意与她一起躲藏。
