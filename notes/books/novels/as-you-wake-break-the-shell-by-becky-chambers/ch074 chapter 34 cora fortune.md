# 74. Chapter 33: Signy, Fortune

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy & Cora's raft 2 date——police checkpoint (Signy's pill bottle "Vitamin C"), "It's very important that you turn around and kiss me right now", bathroom hiding, "Oh, good. It's just us." (Signy's selfish thought), "Stuff to do with Signy" list, "For being someone who will hide with me"。
- **情节位置**：Fortune 线 Chapter 33，紧跟 ch068 (Chapter 31)；Signy-Cora's date + police checkpoint。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从 "tinkerer" (work) 到 "Oh, good. It's just us." (selfish relief)——her growing attachment to Cora; Cora from confident to "I have some ginger on me, but, um..." (mindbleed fear)。
- **叙事手法**：ensemble (Signy-Cora dialogue)；police checkpoint as plot device (forced proximity); "Stuff to do with Signy" list as Cora's vulnerability; "For being someone who will hide with me" as emotional close。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Signy hadn't ever had much reason (or desire) to explore rafts she didn't live or work on. Cora, on the other hand, seemed to always have the pulse of something going on somewhere."

**中文理解**：Signy's exploration——Cora "always had the pulse of something going on somewhere"。

**关键词**：pulse of something going on somewhere

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 写成 "pulse of something going on"—her social energy; "Signy hadn't ever had much reason (or desire)"写出 Signy's introversion。

**读者视角提示**：Signy-Cora contrast—introvert vs social connector。

> **原句 2:** "She spotted a jacket that was not adorned with aspirational pastiche, and it was a sight, like so many others, that had started to give her a little flutter when she noticed it."

**中文理解**：Signy's "flutter"——Cora's jacket (not aspirational pastiche)。

**关键词**：flutter / aspirational pastiche

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's attraction 写成 "flutter"—her growing feeling; "aspirational pastiche"写出 raft 2's pretension。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's attraction—Cora as "flutter" trigger。

> **原句 3:** "I know it's silly," Cora said with a smirk, a shrug, a blush. "But I saw a lady selling them, and—"

**关键词**：I know it's silly / saw a lady selling them

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora's gift 写成 "silly"—her self-deprecation; "saw a lady selling them"写出 her spontaneity。

**读者视角提示**：Cora's gift—paper flowers (spontaneous, silly)。
