# 84. Chapter 38: Cora, Fortune

## 精读

> **原句 1:** ". . . I got us a bit of boar, a bit of elk, and a bit of humpback whale," Otto said as he rolled a ball of cookie dough between his palms. "Distorted them, added the air filter from my dentist's office, of all things, and turned it into a nice layer cake. Now, I don't know what you'd make of it, but we thought that made for a pretty good rorqual."

**中文理解**：Otto 的 rorqual Foley——“bit of boar, bit of elk, bit of humpback whale”加“air filter from dentist's office”等于“nice layer cake”。

**关键词**：bit of boar / bit of elk / bit of humpback whale / air filter from dentist's office / nice layer cake

**为什么这样写**：把 rorqual 声写成“layer cake”——Otto 的 Foley 手艺；“Distorted them”写出他的创作过程；“added the air filter from dentist's office”写出他的足智多谋。

**读者视角提示**：Otto 的 Foley 艺术——rorqual 作为想象力的创造（ch019 的“Lunar Larceny”）。

> **原句 2:** "Well, I'd love to give it a listen," Cora remarked on Otto's herd of wild rorquals. "You could've gone with a heartbeat, though."

**中文理解**：Cora 的回应——“I'd love to give it a listen”加“You could've gone with a heartbeat, though.”。

**关键词**：I'd love to give it a listen / You could've gone with a heartbeat

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的回应写成“I'd love to give it a listen”——她的兴趣；“You could've gone with a heartbeat”写出她的知识（Colibri 的心跳）。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的知识——Colibri 的心跳（ch039 的“once or twice a minute”）。

> **原句 3:** "They've got five hearts," Cora said. "But, they're not hearts like ours. Different structure, different tissues. Same basic idea, though. Pumps nutrients around and clears the junk out."

**中文理解**：Cora 的 rorqual 解剖——“five hearts” / “not hearts like ours” / “Same basic idea, though”。

**关键词**：five hearts / not hearts like ours / Same basic idea

**为什么这样写**：把 rorqual heart 写成“five hearts”——Cora 的知识；“not hearts like ours”写出物种差异；“Same basic idea”写出功能相似。

**读者视角提示**：Rorqual 解剖——五颗心、结构不同、功能相同。

> **原句 4:** "Accuracy isn't everything," he said. "If you were working on the news hour, then sure, you gotta be accurate. But we were telling stories. In a story, you have to make people feel something. That's your goal. You're not teaching, you're evoking."

**中文理解**：Otto 的哲学——“Accuracy isn't everything” / “You're not teaching, you're evoking.”。

**关键词**：Accuracy isn't everything / You're not teaching, you're evoking

**为什么这样写**：把 Otto 的哲学写成“Accuracy isn't everything”——他的艺术信念；“You're not teaching, you're evoking”写出他的目标（情感，非信息）。

**读者视角提示**：Otto 的 Foley 哲学——唤起胜过准确。

> **原句 5:** "Oh, I love that," Otto said approvingly. "Yes, that's it."

**中文理解**：Otto 的认可——“Oh, I love that. Yes, that's it.”（Cora 的“sometimes the wrong sound makes people feel the right thing”）。

**关键词**：Oh, I love that / Yes, that's it

**为什么这样写**：把 Otto 的认可写成“Oh, I love that”——他对 Cora 洞察的欣喜；“Yes, that's it”写出他的确认。

**读者视角提示**：Otto 的认可——Cora 的洞察（“wrong sound makes people feel the right thing”）。

> **原句 6:** "Henry was the one who got me to think about it the right way around. He was a musician, see. Musicians know all about playing tricks with people's feelings."

**中文理解**：Henry 的影响——“Henry was the one who got me to think about it the right way around” / “Musicians know all about playing tricks with people's feelings.”。

**关键词**：Henry / got me to think about it the right way around / playing tricks with people's feelings

**为什么这样写**：把 Henry 的影响写成“got me to think about it the right way around”——他的指导；“Musicians know all about playing tricks”写出他职业的技能。

**读者视角提示**：Henry 的影响（composer、Otto 的丈夫）——唤起方面的指导。

> **原句 7:** "These are his grandmother Lissa's thumbprint cookies. And he never met her, but his father taught him this recipe."

**中文理解**：指纹饼干——用外婆 Lissa 的配方；他从未见过外婆，配方却是父亲传下来的。

**关键词**：grandmother Lissa's thumbprint cookies / never met her / his father taught him this recipe

**为什么这样写**：把 recipe 写成“grandmother Lissa's”——传承；“he never met her, but his father taught him”写出代际传递。

**读者视角提示**：Thumbprint cookies——Lissa 的食谱，经 Henry 之父传下。

> **原句 8:** "Corr didn't know anyone who hadn't lost someone—a lot of someones, usually. Death in the Berth was regular as rain. But the thing that was always hard to guess at was how a person wanted to be about it."

**中文理解**：Cora 的观察——“Death in the Berth was regular as rain” / “how a person wanted to be about it”。

**关键词**：Death in the Berth was regular as rain / how a person wanted to be about it

**为什么这样写**：把 death 写成“regular as rain”——Fortune 的现实；“how a person wanted to be about it”写出个体的悲伤风格。

**读者视角提示**：Berth 的死亡——常态、个体的悲伤风格。

> **原句 9:** "It was a real compliment, to be trusted with tears. She gave him a moment, then gave him an out. "So . . . if a roar was correct, then I guess you don't want me to record Colibri's heartbeat for you.""

**中文理解**：Cora 的 tears 信任——“It was a real compliment, to be trusted with tears”加“gave him an out”（Colibri 的心跳）。

**关键词**：trusted with tears / gave him an out / record Colibri's heartbeat

**为什么这样写**：把 tears 写成“real compliment”——Cora 的理解；“gave him an out”写出她的得体；“record Colibri's heartbeat”写出 Otto 的兴趣。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的得体——在 tears 后给 Otto 台阶。

> **原句 10:** "Oh my god, would you?" / "No dessert for you, young lady," he snapped. "You spit that dough right back out."

**中文理解**：Otto 的兴奋——“Oh my god, would you?” / “No dessert for you, young lady”（打趣）。

**关键词**：Oh my god, would you / No dessert for you, young lady

**为什么这样写**：把 Otto 的兴奋写成“Oh my god, would you?”——他的热情；“No dessert for you, young lady”写出他的打趣。

**读者视角提示**：Otto 的热情——录制 Colibri 的心跳。

> **原句 11:** "Cora, at last!" Otto said jubilantly, leaning on his cane and hugging her with his free arm."

**中文理解**：Otto 的问候——“Cora, at last!”，jubilantly。

**关键词**：Cora, at last / jubilantly

**为什么这样写**：把 Otto 的问候写成“Cora, at last!”——他的热情；“jubilantly”写出他的喜悦。

**读者视角提示**：Otto 的热情——见到 Cora。

> **原句 12:** "Hey, cutie," Cora said. / "Hey, cutie," Otto echoed. / "Back off, bud, you're not her type."

**中文理解**：Cora-Otto-Signy——“Hey, cutie” / “Hey, cutie”（Otto 附和）/ “Back off, bud, you're not her type”。

**关键词**：Hey, cutie / Back off, bud, you're not her type

**为什么这样写**：把他们的 dynamic 写成“Hey, cutie”（Cora）→“Hey, cutie”（Otto 附和）→“Back off, bud, you're not her type”（Cora）——他们打趣的斗嘴。

**读者视角提示**：Cora-Otto-Signy 的 dynamic——打趣的斗嘴。

> **原句 13:** "Why does Cora get to help?" Signy demanded. "I have spent years trying to get you to sit your arthritic ass down—" / "Because you didn't want to," Otto said. "You were being polite. I could tell. You don't cook for yourself, you don't like it."

**中文理解**：Signy 的吃醋——“Why does Cora get to help?” / “I have spent years trying to get you to sit your arthritic ass down—” / Otto 的“Because you didn't want to”。

**关键词**：Why does Cora get to help / I have spent years trying to get you to sit your arthritic ass down / Because you didn't want to

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的吃醋写成“Why does Cora get to help?”——她的意外；Otto 的“Because you didn't want to”写出他的洞察（Signy 不想做饭，Cora 想）。

**读者视角提示**：Otto 的洞察——Signy 不想做饭（客气），Cora 想（愿意）。

> **原句 14:** "Her ass isn't skinny," Cora said, correcting the record in the same way Otto had about his knee. "It's just those skirts, y'know, they're so loose, it's hard to tell."

**中文理解**：Cora 的纠正——“Her ass isn't skinny” / “It's just those skirts... so loose, it's hard to tell”。

**关键词**：Her ass isn't skinny / It's just those skirts

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的纠正写成“Her ass isn't skinny”——她为 Signy 辩护；“It's just those skirts... so loose”写出她的观察。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 为 Signy 辩护——不是瘦，是裙子宽松。

> **原句 15:** "I am finding," Signy said, hitting each word hard, "some music." / "Do you think we're in trouble?" Otto whispered. / "Nah," Cora whispered back. "But if we are, you're gonna have to let me crash on the couch."

**中文理解**：Signy 的“I am finding some music” / Otto 的“Do you think we're in trouble?” / Cora 的“Nah... you're gonna have to let me crash on the couch.”。

**关键词**：I am finding some music / Do you think we're in trouble / you're gonna have to let me crash on the couch

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的退场写成“I am finding some music”——她的逃离；Otto 的“Do you think we're in trouble?”写出他的担心；Cora 的“you're gonna have to let me crash on the couch”写出她的幽默。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的退场——找音乐；Cora 的幽默——借睡沙发。
