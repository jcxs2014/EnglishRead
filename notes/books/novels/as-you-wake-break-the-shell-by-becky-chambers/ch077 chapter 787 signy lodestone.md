## 精读

> **原句 1:** "If you were to compare Lodestone City to a flower turned upside down, the elevator hub was the stem. Residential sectors stretched out from it like petals."

**中文理解**：Lodestone City 的隐喻——“flower turned upside down”，elevator hub 即 stem，residential sectors 即 petals。

**关键词**：flower turned upside down / stem / petals

**为什么这样写**：把 Lodestone 写成“flower turned upside down”——Signy 的植物视角；“stem/petals”写出它的结构。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的植物视角——Lodestone 如花。

> **原句 2:** "Hello, ladies!" It was Jeremy, cheerfully waving to them from his seat at the info desk. He was thirteen years old and took his job as weekend market greeter very seriously."

**中文理解**：Jeremy（13 岁）——weekend market greeter，对工作是“very seriously”。

**关键词**：thirteen years old / weekend market greeter / very seriously

**为什么这样写**：把 Jeremy 写成“very seriously”——他的投入；“thirteen years old”写出 Lodestone 的年轻。

**读者视角提示**：Lodestone 社群——年轻人当迎宾。

> **原句 3:** "Ooh, you picked a good day to be here," he said in a confident salesman's patter. "Big batch of snap peas just hit the grocery today, and if you stop by the bakery, they've got strawberry rhubarb pie."

**中文理解**：Jeremy 的推销话术——“snap peas just hit the grocery” / 面包房有“strawberry rhubarb pie”。

**关键词**：snap peas / strawberry rhubarb pie

**为什么这样写**：把 Lodestone 的市场写成“snap peas... strawberry rhubarb pie”——Jeremy 的热情；“confident salesman's patter”写出他的本事。

**读者视角提示**：Lodestone 市场——snap peas、strawberry rhubarb pie。

> **原句 4:** "Well, shit, we gotta get strawberry rhubarb pie." / "Before . . . pie?" Cora said. / "Before everything." Signy held Cora's hand tightly and began to hustle in the direction of the produce stalls."

**中文理解**：Signy 的优先——“Before everything”（先看 squash 再看 pie）。

**关键词**：Before everything

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的优先写成“Before everything”——她的科学好奇；“hustle”写出她的急切。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的优先——squash（未测模板）胜过 pie。

> **原句 5:** "Squash was not a crop that had been attempted in the Ring until a few months prior. It had a high water cost, and as it had never been part of anyone's diet on Fortune, it wasn't at the top of Lodestone's agricultural wish list."

**中文理解**：squash 的历史——“high water cost” / “never been part of anyone's diet on Fortune” / “not at the top of Lodestone's agricultural wish list”。

**关键词**：high water cost / never been part of anyone's diet / not at the top of agricultural wish list

**为什么这样写**：把 squash 写成“high water cost”——它的限制；“never been part of anyone's diet”写出 Fortune 的饮食文化；“not at top of wish list”写出优先排序。

**读者视角提示**：Squash——高水耗、非传统 Fortune 食物。

> **原句 6:** "But the Ring had far more ice on hand than was needed to cover the basics, and so there was plenty of opportunity for people to experiment with the Primer's untested templates."

**中文理解**：squash 实验——“far more ice on hand” / “plenty of opportunity for people to experiment with untested templates”。

**关键词**：far more ice on hand / experiment with untested templates

**为什么这样写**：把 squash 写成“experiment”——Lodestone 的农业自由；“far more ice”写出资源丰裕。

**读者视角提示**：Lodestone 的农业自由——以冰盈余做实验。

> **原句 7:** "It's spring," she said. "I thought that's a fall crop." / "Uh . . . I dunno," Jeremy shrugged. "I just know she said they've got squash."

**中文理解**：Signy 的困惑——“It's spring. I thought that's a fall crop.” / Jeremy 的“I just know she said they've got squash.”。

**关键词**：It's spring / I thought that's a fall crop

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的困惑写成“It's spring. I thought that's a fall crop.”——她的植物学知识；Jeremy 的“I just know”写出他有限的信息。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的植物学知识——季节意识。

> **原句 8:** "Well, good news for us both," Signy replied with a bounce in her step. "We're about to find out."

**中文理解**：Signy 的回应——“We're about to find out.”。

**关键词**：We're about to find out

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的好奇写成“We're about to find out”——她的热情；“bounce in her step”写出她的兴奋。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的好奇——渴望发现 squash。
