# 63. Interstitial: Drop Dumpling Soup (Recipe)

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Otto's drop dumpling soup recipe——navy beans, potatoes, carrots, celery, dill, thyme, rosemary; dumpling dough (flour, baking powder, salt, rosemary, oil, milk); "You know what dough looks like." / "Fuck vat-grown. Solace Golds all the way."。
- **情节位置**：全书间插文本，Otto's recipe (ch031's Friday dinner)。
- **人物弧线**：无具体人物；Otto's cooking voice (recipe as character)。
- **叙事手法**：recipe format (imperative, measurements)；footnotes (1: "Fuck vat-grown. Solace Golds all the way." 2: "This always turns out to be more like ten or twelve for me.")。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Important: You will need 1½ cups of cooked navy beans for this recipe. If you do not have any shelf-stable packs on hand, make sure to cook a dry batch ahead of time. You can also use any white bean instead."

**中文理解**：recipe opener——navy beans (or any white bean)；shelf-stable packs or dry batch。

**关键词**：navy beans / shelf-stable packs / white bean

**为什么这样写**：把 recipe 写成 "Important:" opener—Otto's practical voice; "any white bean instead"写出 flexibility。

**读者视角提示**：Recipe—flexible bean choice。

> **原句 2:** "If you can't get vat-grown spuds, do NOT substitute this with mash powder, it will not work. I suppose you could use cauliflower instead, but it is not going to be the same."

**中文理解**：potato note——"do NOT substitute with mash powder" (footnote 1: "Fuck vat-grown. Solace Golds all the way.")。

**关键词**：do NOT substitute / mash powder / Solace Golds

**为什么这样写**：把 potato 写成 "do NOT substitute"—Otto's emphasis; footnote 1 "Fuck vat-grown. Solace Golds all the way."写出 his preference。

**读者视角提示**：Recipe—potato preference (Solace Golds, not vat-grown)。

> **原句 3:** "Use a spatula to toss the flour in the oil and keep stirring for about four minutes, until the flour clumps are a bit toasty."

**中文理解**：roux——"toss the flour in the oil" / "until the flour clumps are a bit toasty"。

**关键词**：toss the flour / toasty

**为什么这样写**：把 roux 写成 "toasty"—visual cue; "toss"写出 technique。

**读者视角提示**：Recipe—visual cue for roux。

> **原句 4:** "Add ½ teaspoon of sweet paprika and a dash of black pepper."

**中文理解**：seasoning——sweet paprika + black pepper。

**关键词**：sweet paprika / dash of black pepper

**为什么这样写**：把 seasoning 写成 "dash"—Otto's casualness; "sweet paprika"写出 his preference。

**读者视角提示**：Recipe—seasoning with casualness。

> **原句 5:** "Mix together until it looks like dough. You know what dough looks like."

**中文理解**：dumpling dough——"You know what dough looks like."。

**关键词**：You know what dough looks like

**为什么这样写**：把 dough 写成 "You know what dough looks like"—Otto's trust in reader's knowledge; no precise description。

**读者视角提示**：Recipe—trust reader's knowledge。

> **原句 6:** "Drop spoonfuls of dough straight into the soup. You should have enough dough for fourteen dumplings, ish."

**中文理解**：drop dumplings——"fourteen dumplings, ish" (footnote 2: "This always turns out to be more like ten or twelve for me.")。

**关键词**：fourteen dumplings, ish

**为什么这样写**：把 dumpling count 写成 "ish"—Otto's casualness; footnote 2's "more like ten or twelve"写出 his honesty。

**读者视角提示**：Recipe—casual count with honest footnote。

> **原句 7:** "Serve as soon as it's cool enough to eat."

**中文理解**：serving note——"Serve as soon as it's cool enough to eat."。

**关键词**：as soon as it's cool enough to eat

**为什么这样写**：把 serving 写成 "as soon as it's cool enough"—Otto's urgency; closing note。

**读者视角提示**：Recipe—serve hot。
