# 79. Chapter 788: Cora, Lodestone

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Cora & Signy at Lodestone market——"dirt" (Lodestone's growing substrate), squash discovery (Noelle), "What the fuck" (Cora's reaction to squash guts), Signy entranced ("Oh my god"), "plants have organs" (Cora's discomfort), bread-or-cake convincing Cora ("Okay, fine, we'll bring some home")。
- **情节位置**：Cypsela 线，Chapter 788；Cora-Signy's Lodestone market visit (squash)。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "A lot of stuff in the Primer hadn't been possible to implement on Fortune. Lumber. Trains. Fossil fuels. Chicken eggs. Satellite internet."

**中文理解**：Primer 未实现的东西——Lumber、Trains、Fossil fuels、Chicken eggs、Satellite internet。

**为什么这样写**：把 Primer 未实现的东西写成清单——Signy 的枚举；“hadn't been possible to implement on Fortune”写出 Fortune 的局限。

**读者视角提示**：Fortune 的局限——Primer 的承诺未兑现。

> **原句 2:** "But the cool thing about Lodestone and some of its neighboring moonlets was that the stuff they were made out of could be ground up into a really good growing substrate. **中文理解**：Lodestone 的“dirt”——磨碎的卫星加 compost、soil bacteria、raw kincrete。

**关键词**：ground up into a really good growing substrate / compost / soil bacteria / raw kincrete / Everybody called the end result dirt

**读者视角提示**：Lodestone 的“dirt”——磨碎卫星加 compost、bacteria、kincrete。
