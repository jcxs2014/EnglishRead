# 23. Chapter 771: Signy, Cypsela

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy 与 Cora 在 vault garden 检查 pepper strain，发现 leaves limp/brown tips；Signy 排除 bacterial/fungal/underwatering，归因 "something about this strain in these conditions"；Cora 引用 Signy 的 own advice（"A failed batch doesn't mean you failed..."），Signy "annoyed"但继续。
- **情节位置**：Cypsela 线，紧跟 ch021（Chapter 770）；Signy-Cora 的 vault garden work。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从 frustration 到 Cora 的 reassurance——她的 perfectionism 与 Cora 的 encouragement；apprentices ("cubs") 建立。
- **叙事手法**：dialogue-driven；"failed batch" metaphor；Signy's grumbling vs Cora's quoting。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The vault garden was a far stricter environment than the test garden."

**中文理解**：vault garden 比 test garden 更 strict——segmented planters, transparent covers, air purifiers。

**关键词**：stricter environment / segmented / air purifiers

**为什么这样写**：把 vault garden 写成 controlled environment，与 test garden 的"overgrow"对比；"stricter"写出 Signy 的 precision。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的 work space hierarchy——test garden (experimental) vs vault garden (controlled)。

> **原句 2:** "There were no makeshift meadows, no vines crawling along the floor."

**中文理解**：vault garden 无 makeshift meadows, no vines——controlled vs wild。

**关键词**：makeshift meadows / vines crawling

**为什么这样写**：把 test garden 的 wildness（ch005）与 vault garden 的 control 对比；"no vines"写出 containment。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的 dual garden philosophy——experimentation vs control。

> **原句 3:** "She didn't tend this place alone, or solely with Cora. Little fist-sized robots trundled along the paths, taking soil measurements and misting leaves."

**中文理解**：vault garden 由 robots + apprentices（"cubs"）协助——Signy 不 solo。

**关键词**：fist-sized robots / trundled / soil measurements

**为什么这样写**：把 technology (robots) 与 human (apprentices) 并置；"trundled"写出 robots 的 humble, persistent presence。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's team——robots + apprentices（Imogen, Dylan）。

> **原句 4:** "Much as Signy liked to have her own space, her own curated peace and quiet, she did love the energy of a garden full of sweating, bustling people."

**中文理解**：Signy 喜欢 own space/peace，但 love garden full of sweating, bustling people。

**关键词**：curated peace and quiet / sweating, bustling people

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's introvert preference 与 social energy 对比；"curated"写出 her control over space。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's social battery（ch019）——introvert but enjoys communal energy。

> **原句 5:** "God, it had been so much work for such little yield, in the old days."

**中文理解**：Signy's reflection——"so much work for such little yield, in the old days"。

**关键词**：so much work / little yield / old days

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's past struggles 写成 reflection；"old days"写出 her growth from solo to team。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's arc—from solo struggle to collaborative ease。

> **原句 6:** "The leaves were limp and flimsy, crunching brown at the tips."

**中文理解**：pepper strain 的 leaves——limp, flimsy, crunching brown at tips。

**关键词**：limp / flimsy / crunching brown

**为什么这样写**：把 plant distress 写成 sensory detail；"crunching brown"写出 leaf death。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's diagnostic eye—plant symptoms as data。

> **原句 7:** "It's not your fault. And it's not the soil, and it's not the irrigation, either."

**中文理解**：Signy 排除 Cora fault, soil, irrigation——systematic diagnosis。

**关键词**：not your fault / not the soil / not the irrigation

**为什么这样写**：把 diagnosis 写成 elimination process；"not your fault"写出 Signy's care for Cora。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's diagnostic method—elimination。

> **原句 8:** "It's something about this strain in these conditions, but I cannot figure out what."

**中文理解**：Signy's admission——"something about this strain in these conditions, but I cannot figure out what"。

**关键词**：something about this strain / cannot figure out

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's uncertainty 写成 honest admission；"cannot figure out"写出 her limits。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's perfectionism—frustrated by unsolved problem。

> **原句 9:** "A failed batch doesn't mean you failed, it just means you've learned something for the next—"

**中文理解**：Signy's own advice（quoted by Cora）——failed batch = learning, not failure。

**关键词**：failed batch / learned something

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's advice 写成 self-quoted wisdom；"just means you've learned something"写出 her growth mindset。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's ethic—failure as learning。

> **原句 10:** "Are you quoting me?" Signy asked. "Are you quoting me to me?"

**中文理解**：Signy's reaction——"Are you quoting me to me?"——annoyed but touched。

**关键词**：quoting me to me

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora's support 写成 "quoting me to me"——Signy's self-aware humor；"to me"的 emphasis。

**读者视角提示**：Signy-Cora's dynamic—Cora as mirror/support。

> **原句 11:** "Oh, that's so annoying," Signy said. She turned away and continued on to the carrot patch.

**中文理解**：Signy's "so annoying" + turn to carrot patch——deflection via work。

**关键词**：so annoying / carrot patch

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's emotion 写成 "so annoying" + physical turn；"carrot patch"写出 her work-as-coping。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's coping—turn to next task。
