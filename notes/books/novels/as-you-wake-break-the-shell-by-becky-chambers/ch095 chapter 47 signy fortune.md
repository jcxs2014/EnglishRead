# 95. Chapter 46: Signy, Fortune

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy's last vigil with Otto——"You are so loved. I love you. And you are always going to be my friend."——Otto's death (kind strangers take body away)。
- **情节位置**：Fortune 线 Chapter 46，紧跟 ch094 (Chapter 45)；Otto's death。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从 "tinkerer" (work) 到 "You are so loved. I love you."——her grief; Otto 从 "brilliant, kind, hilarious" to death (kind strangers take body)。
- **叙事手法**：vigil scene (Signy alone with Otto)；"You are so loved. I love you. And you are always going to be my friend." as closing; "kind strangers came to take it away" as ending。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "They started to take shifts. Signy sat with him until midnight, listening to old episodes of Saturday Spectacular."

**中文理解**：Signy's vigil——"listening to old episodes of Saturday Spectacular" (Otto's work)。

**关键词**：take shifts / Saturday Spectacular

**为什么这样写**：把 vigil 写成 "take shifts"—community care; "listening to old episodes of Saturday Spectacular"写出 Otto's legacy (his Foley work)。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's vigil—listening to Otto's work (Saturday Spectacular)。

> **原句 2:** "I've never had many friends," she said to him, not sure of where the words were coming from. "I've never been very good at it. I can take care of people, but . . . that's not really the same."

**中文理解**：Signy's confession——"I've never had many friends" / "I can take care of people, but that's not really the same."。

**关键词**：I've never had many friends / I can take care of people, but that's not really the same

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's confession 写成 "I've never had many friends"—her vulnerability; "I can take care of people, but that's not really the same"写出 her distinction (care vs friendship)。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's confession—care vs friendship distinction。

**中文理解**：Signy's words——"You . . . are such a good person. You're such a beautiful person. And the world is going to be so stupid without you in it, but it's okay to let go. It's okay to be done."。

**关键词**：such a good person / such a beautiful person / the world is going to be so stupid without you in it / it's okay to let go / it's okay to be done

**中文理解**：Signy's frustration——"she couldn't find the fucking words" / "like Cora and her rorqual colors, maybe. The pieces of the world that wouldn't stay in your grasp, no matter how hard you tried."。

**读者视角提示**：Signy's frustration—inarticulacy, parallel to Cora's mindbleed (ch040)。

> **原句 6:** "You are so loved," Signy said, frustrated, ardent. "I love you. And you are always going to be my friend."

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy's declaration 写成 "You are so loved"—her love; "I love you" (callback to ch082's first declaration)；"you are always going to be my friend"写出 her permanence。
