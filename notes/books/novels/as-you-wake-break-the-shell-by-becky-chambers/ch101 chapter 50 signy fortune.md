# 101. Chapter 50: Signy, Fortune

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy & Cora lying together after Twig's disappearance——"What if she's still out there and I give up too early?" / "Did she take your last name, when you adopted her?" (Marten = marten babies kicked out by moms) / "You know what I've always liked the thought of? A vacation." / "Do you wanna come to the stables with me, for my preflight next week?" / "Why not."。
- **情节位置**：Fortune 线 Chapter 50，紧跟 ch097 (Chapter 48)；Signy-Cora's grief + stables invitation。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从 "tinkerer" (work) 到 "Why not." (stables vacation)—her openness to Cora's invitation; Cora 从 "What if she's still out there" to stables invitation——her hope。
- **叙事手法**：dialogue-driven (Signy-Cora)；Marten backstory (marten babies kicked out by moms); "vacation" as grief response; "Why not." as closing.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "It didn't matter that Signy's apartment was too small for two. There was a bed that would hold them both, and in that moment, that was enough."

**中文理解**：Signy 的公寓——「too small for two」但「in that moment, that was enough」。

**关键词**：too small for two / in that moment, that was enough

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的公寓写成「too small for two」——她的现实；「in that moment, that was enough」写出她的接受。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的公寓——太小，但此刻够了。

> **原句 2:** "What if she's still out there and I give up too early?" Cora said to the ceiling."

**中文理解**：Cora 的恐惧——「What if she's still out there and I give up too early?」。

**关键词**：What if she's still out there and I give up too early

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的恐惧写成「What if she's still out there and I give up too early?」——她的希望；「to the ceiling」写出她的回避（不看 Signy）。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的恐惧——放弃太早（Twig）。

> **原句 3:** "I don't think anyone could ever accuse you of giving up on her."

**中文理解**：Signy 的安慰——「I don't think anyone could ever accuse you of giving up on her.」。

**关键词**：I don't think anyone could ever accuse you of giving up on her

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的安慰写成「I don't think anyone could ever accuse you of giving up on her」——她的确信；「anyone could ever accuse」写出她的辩护。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的安慰——Cora 对 Twig 的奉献。

> **原句 4:** "Did she take your last name, when you adopted her?" Signy asked. "Or did you take hers?" / "Neither," Cora said. "We chose it together."

**中文理解**：Signy 的提问——「Did she take your last name... or did you take hers?」/ Cora 的「Neither. We chose it together.」。

**关键词**：Did she take your last name / We chose it together

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora-Signy 的命名写成「We chose it together」——他们的伙伴关系；「Neither」写出他们的平等。

**读者视角提示**：Cora-Twig 的命名——共同选择（Marten）。

> **原句 5:** "There's this mural in the movie room at Heartwise—it's still there. A forest full of animals. I don't think they're animals that even live in the same places on Earth—like, there's an elephant, but also a moose."

**中文理解**：Cora 的 Heartwise 壁画——「A forest full of animals... there's an elephant, but also a moose.」。

**关键词**：mural in the movie room at Heartwise / forest full of animals / elephant, but also a moose

**为什么这样写**：把 Heartwise 壁画写成「forest full of animals」——Cora 的记忆；「elephant, but also a moose」（不生活在一起）写出壁画的不准确。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的 Heartwise 壁画——森林里有不匹配的动物。

> **原句 6:** "We found out that marten babies get kicked out by their moms when they're really small, and they have to go it on their own. And Twig said, 'Just like us!'"

**中文理解**：Marten 背景——「marten babies get kicked out by their moms when they're really small」/ Twig 说「Just like us!」。

**关键词**：marten babies get kicked out by their moms / Just like us!

**为什么这样写**：把 Marten 写成「get kicked out by their moms」——Cora-Twig 的平行；「Just like us!」写出 Twig 的认同。

**读者视角提示**：Marten 背景——marten 幼崽被踢出（Cora-Twig 的平行）。

> **原句 7:** "So, yeah. When we became family on paper, we took Marten."

**中文理解**：Cora-Twig 的名字——「When we became family on paper, we took Marten.」。

**关键词**：When we became family on paper, we took Marten

**为什么这样写**：把命名写成「When we became family on paper」——Cora 的正式；「we took Marten」写出他们的选择。

**读者视角提示**：Cora-Twig 的命名——Marten（chosen）。

> **原句 8:** "You know what I've always liked the thought of?" Signy said. / "Hmm?" / "A vacation."

**中文理解**：Signy 的「A vacation.」。

**关键词**：A vacation

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的愿望写成「A vacation」——单词；「You know what I've always liked the thought of?」写出她的试探。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的愿望——度假（Primer 电影风格）。

> **原句 9:** "You know how in Primer movies they put some clothes in a bag and they go somewhere they've never—"

**中文理解**：Signy 的度假参照——「in Primer movies they put some clothes in a bag and they go somewhere they've never—」。

**关键词**：in Primer movies they put some clothes in a bag and they go somewhere they've never

**为什么这样写**：把 vacation 写成「in Primer movies」——Signy 的参照；「put some clothes in a bag and they go somewhere they've never」写出她的想象。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的度假——Primer 电影风格（clothes in bag, somewhere new）。

> **原句 10:** "Do you wanna come to the stables with me, for my preflight next week?" / "Sure, all right." / "No, I'm being serious." / "And do . . . what?" / "Look at stars. Say hi to Colibri. Make out in micrograv. Drink chicory from a tube." Cora stroked Signy's cheek. "Not be here."

**中文理解**：Cora 的邀请——「Do you wanna come to the stables with me, for my preflight next week?」/「Look at stars. Say hi to Colibri. Make out in micrograv. Drink chicory from a tube.」/「Not be here.」。

**关键词**：Do you wanna come to the stables with me / Look at stars / Say hi to Colibri / Make out in micrograv / Drink chicory from a tube / Not be here

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的邀请写成「Look at stars. Say hi to Colibri. Make out in micrograv. Drink chicory from a tube.」——她的清单；「Not be here」写出她的逃避愿望。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的邀请——stables 度假（stars, Colibri, micrograv, chicory）。

> **原句 11:** "The thing about grief was it threw all your hesitations and hang-ups out the airlock."

**中文理解**：Signy 的反思——「The thing about grief was it threw all your hesitations and hang-ups out the airlock.」。

**关键词**：grief threw all your hesitations and hang-ups out the airlock

**为什么这样写**：把 grief 写成「threw all your hesitations and hang-ups out the airlock」——Signy 的洞察；「out the airlock」写出太空隐喻。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的反思——grief 消除犹豫。

> **原句 12:** "A month ago, Signy would've kissed Cora amusedly over the sweet, silly suggestion. She would've said no, she had customers to look after, she had her plants to look after, she had projects to puzzle out, and besides, she'd only be in the way."

**中文理解**：Signy 的过去自我——「A month ago, Signy would've kissed Cora amusedly... She would've said no」。

**关键词**：A month ago, Signy would've kissed Cora amusedly / She would've said no

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的过去自我写成「A month ago, Signy would've... said no」——她以前的犹豫；「She would've said no」写出她以前的拒绝。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的过去自我——犹豫、拒绝（before grief）。

> **原句 13:** "And what did she care now? Her templates had been deleted. Her favored plants had been torched. She'd let her customers down, sent them off into the wind to suffer alone. And no one would wave at her from a window across the street when she got to the shop each morning. No one would make her dumpling soup on Fridays. And Cora, Cora was in pieces."

**中文理解**：Signy 的「And what did she care now?」——「Her templates had been deleted. Her favored plants had been torched. She'd let her customers down... And no one would wave at her from a window across the street... No one would make her dumpling soup on Fridays. And Cora, Cora was in pieces.」。

**关键词**：Her templates had been deleted / Her favored plants had been torched / She'd let her customers down / no one would wave at her from a window / No one would make her dumpling soup on Fridays / Cora, Cora was in pieces

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的失去写成清单——templates deleted, plants torched, customers let down, Otto gone, Cora in pieces；「And Cora, Cora was in pieces」写出她的优先（Cora）。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的失去——一切（templates, plants, customers, Otto, Cora 的悲伤）。

> **原句 14:** "All Signy wanted to do for the foreseeable future was hold Cora and be held back, and she didn't care if they did that in the Berth or floating above it."

**中文理解**：Signy 的愿望——「All Signy wanted to do for the foreseeable future was hold Cora and be held back, and she didn't care if they did that in the Berth or floating above it.」。

**关键词**：All Signy wanted to do for the foreseeable future was hold Cora and be held back / she didn't care if they did that in the Berth or floating above it

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的愿望写成「hold Cora and be held back」——她的需要；「she didn't care if they did that in the Berth or floating above it」写出她的灵活。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的愿望——hold Cora, anywhere。

> **原句 15:** "Why not," she said. "If they say yes, then . . . why not."

**中文理解**：Signy 的收尾——「Why not,」she said.「If they say yes, then . . . why not.」。

**关键词**：Why not / If they say yes, then . . . why not

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的收尾写成「Why not」——她的接受；「If they say yes, then . . . why not」写出她的开放。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的接受——stables 度假。
