# 78. Chapter 35: Signy, Fortune

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Signy brings Cora to Otto's apartment (206) for dinner——Darcy, Ramona, Sam present; Otto's insomnia (Darcy's report), Ramona's OCTA flag neighbor (blue dog charm), Cora's not-quite-cheek kiss practice, Otto teaching Cora to cook (basil, apron)。
- **情节位置**：Fortune 线 Chapter 35，紧跟 ch074 (Chapter 33)；Signy-Cora's visit to Otto's。
- **人物弧线**：Signy 从 "tinkerer" (work) 到 bringing Cora to Otto's—her social integration; Cora 从 outsider to Otto's kitchen helper—her acceptance by Signy's circle。
- **叙事手法**：ensemble scene (Otto, Darcy, Ramona, Sam, Signy, Cora)；Otto's insomnia (Darcy's report)；Ramona's OCTA flag neighbor (blue dog charm); Cora's cooking lesson (Otto's teaching)。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Okay, so . . . he's going to kiss my cheek?" Cora said. / "Sort of," she said. "He's going to put his cheek against yours and kiss the air beside it."

**中文理解**：Signy 解释 Otto 的问候——“put his cheek against yours and kiss the air beside it”。

**关键词**：put his cheek against yours / kiss the air beside it

**为什么这样写**：把 Otto 的问候写成“kiss the air beside it”——Signy 的说明；“Sort of”写出她的近似。

**读者视角提示**：Otto 的问候仪式——贴脸旁的飞吻。

> **原句 2:** "Signy rocked her head back and forth as she led Cora up the inner stairwell of Mountain Hearth Family Apartments. "Here, I'll show you." She leaned in and demonstrated Otto's not-quite-cheek kiss."

**中文理解**：Signy 的示范——“not-quite-cheek kiss”。

**关键词**：not-quite-cheek kiss

**为什么这样写**：把 Otto 的问候写成“not-quite-cheek kiss”——Signy 的用语；“demonstrated”写出她的示范。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的示范——Otto 的问候仪式。

> **原句 3:** "Signy had barely knocked on the door to apartment 206 before it was thrown open. "Cora, at last!" Otto said jubilantly, leaning on his cane and hugging her with his free arm."

**中文理解**：Otto 的问候——“Cora, at last!”，jubilantly。

**关键词**：Cora, at last / jubilantly

**为什么这样写**：把 Otto 的问候写成“Cora, at last!”——他的热情；“jubilantly”写出他的喜悦。

**读者视角提示**：Otto 的热情——见到 Cora。

> **原句 4:** "I thought she was going to be ugly, given how it's taken you four months to bring her by the old folks' home—" / "But she's a catch, is where I was going with that!"

**中文理解**：Darcy 的打趣——“I thought she was going to be ugly... But she's a catch”。

**关键词**：I thought she was going to be ugly / she's a catch

**为什么这样写**：把 Darcy 的打趣写成“I thought she was going to be ugly”——他的幽默；“she's a catch”写出他的认可。

**读者视角提示**：Darcy 的性格——打趣、温暖。

> **原句 5:** "Cora accepted the cheap bit of plastic as though it were worth a hundred dollars."

**中文理解**：Cora 接过 Ramona 的松鼠小像——“as though it were worth a hundred dollars”。

**关键词**：as though it were worth a hundred dollars

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的接受写成“as though it were worth a hundred dollars”——她的善意；“cheap bit of plastic”写出 Ramona 的手艺。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的善意——Ramona 的松鼠小像。

> **原句 6:** "Darcy, in civilian clothing and without a wig crowning his swoop of gray, clucked his tongue as Signy and Cora were ushered inside. "I thought she was going to be ugly, given how it's taken you four months to bring her by the old folks' home—""

**中文理解**：Darcy 的出现——“civilian clothing and without a wig”；“old folks' home”。

**关键词**：civilian clothing / without a wig / old folks' home

**为什么这样写**：把 Darcy 写成“civilian clothing and without a wig”——他台下的自我；“old folks' home”写出他的幽默。

**读者视角提示**：Darcy 的性格——台下的自我、幽默。

> **原句 7:** "I've been meaning to tell you," he said, putting his hand on her shoulder and speaking in a hush, "Otto's been doing a bit poorly lately. I don't think he's getting much sleep."

**中文理解**：Darcy 的担心——“Otto's been doing a bit poorly lately. I don't think he's getting much sleep.”。

**关键词**：doing a bit poorly / not getting much sleep

**为什么这样写**：把 Darcy 的担心写成“doing a bit poorly”——他的 euphemism；“I don't think he's getting much sleep”写出他的观察。

**读者视角提示**：Darcy 的担心——Otto 的失眠。

> **原句 8:** "Insomnia was the sort of thing a comfort care nurse could handle legally, after all. She might not need to step in."

**中文理解**：Signy 的评估——“Insomnia was the sort of thing a comfort care nurse could handle legally”。

**关键词**：Insomnia / comfort care nurse / legally

**为什么这样写**：把 Signy 的评估写成“comfort care nurse could handle legally”——她的谨慎；“She might not need to step in”写出她的希望。

**读者视角提示**：Signy 的评估——Otto 的失眠或可由 Arlo 处理。

> **原句 9:** "And for you—" Ramona said, taking Signy's hands. / "For protection," Ramona said."

**中文理解**：Ramona 的蓝色狗挂饰——“For protection”。

**关键词**：For protection

**为什么这样写**：把 Ramona 的挂饰写成“For protection”——她的理由；“taking Signy's hands”写出她的仪式。

**读者视角提示**：Ramona 的挂饰——blue dog 用于保护（OCTA flag 邻居）。

> **原句 10:** "She's bad news. She's got an OCTA flag on her door." Ramona gestured broadly through the air as though she were tracing a line around a person. "Her whole energy's off."

**中文理解**：Ramona 的评估——“OCTA flag on her door” / “Her whole energy's off.”。

**关键词**：OCTA flag / Her whole energy's off

**为什么这样写**：把 Ramona 的评估写成“OCTA flag on her door”——她的怀疑；“Her whole energy's off”写出她的直觉。

**读者视角提示**：Ramona 的怀疑——OCTA flag 邻居。
