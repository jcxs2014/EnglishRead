# 99. Chapter 49: Cora, Fortune

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Cora at police station——"I don't care what it takes, I want her back" / "Three weeks was..." (surface survival) / "I need a contact number—" (Red, university)。
- **情节位置**：Fortune 线 Chapter 49，紧跟 ch096 (Chapter 47)；Cora's police station + contact university。
- **人物弧线**：Cora 从 "I'm going to kill her" (ch096) 到 "I don't care what it takes, I want her back"—her desperation; Cora's "Three weeks was..." (surface survival)。
- **叙事手法**：dialogue-driven (Cora-cop)；"Three weeks was..." as cliffhanger; "I need a contact number—" as action。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "That's unacceptable," Cora spat. It wasn't a good idea, generally, to mouth off at cops, but right then, she couldn't care less."

**中文理解**：Cora 的开场——「That's unacceptable」（警察说不救）/「couldn't care less」。

**关键词**：That's unacceptable / couldn't care less

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的愤怒写成「That's unacceptable」——她的反抗；「couldn't care less」写出她的绝望（无克制）。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的绝望——对警察无克制。

> **原句 2:** "I'm not gonna fucking sit down until you send someone out there to bring my sister home."

**中文理解**：Cora 的要求——「I'm not gonna fucking sit down until you send someone out there to bring my sister home.」。

**关键词**：I'm not gonna fucking sit down / send someone out there to bring my sister home

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的要求写成「I'm not gonna fucking sit down」——她拒绝接受；「send someone out there to bring my sister home」写出她的绝望。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的绝望——要求救援。

> **原句 3:** "There are no restrictions on leaving the Berth, and therefore, there's no legal reason for us to go after her."

**中文理解**：警察的回应——「no restrictions on leaving the Berth... no legal reason for us to go after her」。

**关键词**：no restrictions on leaving the Berth / no legal reason for us to go after her

**为什么这样写**：把警察的回应写成「no legal reason」——官僚；「no restrictions on leaving the Berth」写出 Berth 的开放政策。

**读者视角提示**：警察的回应——官僚、无法律理由。

> **原句 4:** "What about a moral reason?" Cora said. "I get that she and her buddies have done something incredibly stupid, but they're gonna die out there. Do you not care about that?"

**中文理解**：Cora 的反驳——「What about a moral reason?」/「they're gonna die out there. Do you not care about that?」。

**关键词**：What about a moral reason / they're gonna die out there / Do you not care about that

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的反驳写成「What about a moral reason?」——她的道德挑战；「they're gonna die out there」写出她的恐惧。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的道德挑战——救援对比官僚。

> **原句 5:** "I care," said the cop, testily. "But we do not send rescue teams outside of the Berth. You're a pilot, you know that. And you know why."

**中文理解**：警察的「I care... But we do not send rescue teams outside of the Berth.」。

**关键词**：I care / we do not send rescue teams outside of the Berth / You're a pilot, you know that. And you know why.

**为什么这样写**：把警察的回应写成「I care... But」——他官僚的关心；「You're a pilot, you know that. And you know why.」写出 Cora 的认知（救援队需要被救援）。

**读者视角提示**：警察的回应——官僚、Cora 知道原因（无救援队）。

> **原句 6:** "Okay, but what about—" She pointed at the cop's desk monitor, filled with the files she'd pulled from Twig's computer. Manifestos, group chats, schematics for spacecraft that one hundred percent would not work."

**中文理解**：Cora 的尝试——指着 Twig 的文件（manifestos, group chats, schematics）——「schematics for spacecraft that one hundred percent would not work」。

**关键词**：Manifestos, group chats, schematics for spacecraft that one hundred percent would not work

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的尝试写成「schematics for spacecraft that one hundred percent would not work」——她的绝望；「Manifestos, group chats」写出 Twig 的参与。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的尝试——用 Twig 的文件对付 Chapter 252。

> **原句 7:** "The irony of trying to sic cops on these fuckwits while having spent these past weeks consoling Signy as she tried to avoid them was not lost on Cora."

**中文理解**：Cora 的讽刺——「trying to sic cops on these fuckwits while having spent these past weeks consoling Signy as she tried to avoid them」。

**关键词**：irony / trying to sic cops on these fuckwits / consoling Signy as she tried to avoid them

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的讽刺写成「trying to sic cops on these fuckwits」——她的虚伪；「consoling Signy as she tried to avoid them」写出她近来对 Signy 回避的支持。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的讽刺——一边安抚 Signy 一边举报 Chapter 252。

> **原句 8:** "I don't care what it takes, I want her back," she said. "She's a good kid. She's just—lost. She's always been a little lost, and—and looking for something. I have to find her. I can't just sit here and do nothing."

**中文理解**：Cora 的请求——「I don't care what it takes, I want her back」/「She's a good kid. She's just—lost.」/「I can't just sit here and do nothing.」。

**关键词**：I don't care what it takes, I want her back / She's a good kid / She's just—lost / I have to find her / I can't just sit here and do nothing

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的请求写成「I don't care what it takes, I want her back」——她的绝望；「She's a good kid. She's just—lost.」写出她对 Twig 的理解；「I can't just sit here and do nothing」写出她的行动强迫。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的绝望——Twig 迷失、行动强迫。

> **原句 9:** "Ms. Marten, you might have to face the possibility that there is nothing left to be done." / "Judging by what you've told us and by our examinations of her telecommunications records, she most likely left the Berth three weeks ago." / "No. No, no, no. Cora shook her head."

**中文理解**：警察的「you might have to face the possibility that there is nothing left to be done」/「she most likely left the Berth three weeks ago」/ Cora 的「No. No, no, no.」。

**关键词**：you might have to face the possibility that there is nothing left to be done / she most likely left the Berth three weeks ago / No. No, no, no

**为什么这样写**：把警察的评估写成「nothing left to be done」——他的现实主义；「three weeks ago」写出时间线；Cora 的「No. No, no, no.」写出她拒绝接受。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的拒绝——三周（地表生存）。

> **原句 10:** "Three days on the surface would be hard, even if you had great gear. Three weeks was . . ."

**中文理解**：警察的评估——「Three days on the surface would be hard, even if you had great gear. Three weeks was . . .」。

**关键词**：Three days on the surface would be hard / Three weeks was . . .

**为什么这样写**：把警察的评估写成「Three days on the surface would be hard」——他的现实主义；「Three weeks was . . .」（省略号）写出暗示（死亡）。

**读者视角提示**：警察的评估——三周在地表＝可能死亡。

> **原句 11:** "Who can I talk to about what goes on out at Sucker's Luck? There must be somebody who monitors—monitors launches." / "Uh, well, we don't monitor launches of any type, but I believe the university does" / "Oh. Shit. Yes, Cora did have an in there, maybe. Not directly, but she knew who would know."

**中文理解**：Cora 的线索——「Who can I talk to about what goes on out at Sucker's Luck?」/ 警察的「the university does」/ Cora 的「Oh. Shit. Yes, Cora did have an in there, maybe.」。

**关键词**：Who can I talk to about what goes on out at Sucker's Luck / the university does / Oh. Shit. Yes, Cora did have an in there

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的线索写成「Who can I talk to about what goes on out at Sucker's Luck?」——她的绝望；「the university does」写出她的希望；「Oh. Shit. Yes, Cora did have an in there」写出她的领悟（Red、大学联系人）。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的希望——大学联系人（Red）。

> **原句 12:** "Great," she said. "Okay, great, I can—yes. Okay, thank you." / "Wait, before you—" the cop began to say. / Cora didn't catch the rest. She was already out in the hallway, making a call on her phone. "Hey, Red, it's Cora, I need a contact number—"

**中文理解**：Cora 的退场——「Great... Okay, thank you.」/「Hey, Red, it's Cora, I need a contact number—」。

**关键词**：Great / Okay, thank you / Hey, Red, it's Cora, I need a contact number

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora 的退场写成「Great... Okay, thank you.」——她的宽慰；「Hey, Red, it's Cora, I need a contact number—」写出她的行动（给 Red 打电话）。

**读者视角提示**：Cora 的行动——给 Red 打电话找大学联系人。
