# 99. Chapter 49: Cora, Fortune

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Cora at police station——"I don't care what it takes, I want her back" / "Three weeks was..." (surface survival) / "I need a contact number—" (Red, university)。
- **情节位置**：Fortune 线 Chapter 49，紧跟 ch096 (Chapter 47)；Cora's police station + contact university。
- **人物弧线**：Cora 从 "I'm going to kill her" (ch096) 到 "I don't care what it takes, I want her back"—her desperation; Cora's "Three weeks was..." (surface survival)。
- **叙事手法**：dialogue-driven (Cora-cop)；"Three weeks was..." as cliffhanger; "I need a contact number—" as action。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "That's unacceptable," Cora spat. It wasn't a good idea, generally, to mouth off at cops, but right then, she couldn't care less."

**中文理解**：Cora's opening——"That's unacceptable" (cop says no rescue) / "couldn't care less"。

**关键词**：That's unacceptable / couldn't care less

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora's anger 写成 "That's unacceptable"—her defiance; "couldn't care less"写出 her desperation (no restraint)。

**读者视角提示**：Cora's desperation—no restraint with cops。

> **原句 2:** "I'm not gonna fucking sit down until you send someone out there to bring my sister home."

**中文理解**：Cora's demand——"I'm not gonna fucking sit down until you send someone out there to bring my sister home."。

**关键词**：I'm not gonna fucking sit down / send someone out there to bring my sister home

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora's demand 写成 "I'm not gonna fucking sit down"—her refusal to accept; "send someone out there to bring my sister home"写出 her desperation。

**读者视角提示**：Cora's desperation—demanding rescue。

> **原句 3:** "There are no restrictions on leaving the Berth, and therefore, there's no legal reason for us to go after her."

**中文理解**：cop's response——"no restrictions on leaving the Berth... no legal reason for us to go after her"。

**关键词**：no restrictions on leaving the Berth / no legal reason for us to go after her

**为什么这样写**：把 cop's response 写成 "no legal reason"—bureaucratic; "no restrictions on leaving the Berth"写出 the Berth's open policy。

**读者视角提示**：Cop's response—bureaucratic, no legal reason。

> **原句 4:** "What about a moral reason?" Cora said. "I get that she and her buddies have done something incredibly stupid, but they're gonna die out there. Do you not care about that?"

**中文理解**：Cora's counter——"What about a moral reason?" / "they're gonna die out there. Do you not care about that?"。

**关键词**：What about a moral reason / they're gonna die out there / Do you not care about that

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora's counter 写成 "What about a moral reason?"—her moral challenge; "they're gonna die out there"写出 her fear。

**读者视角提示**：Cora's moral challenge—rescue vs bureaucracy。

> **原句 5:** "I care," said the cop, testily. "But we do not send rescue teams outside of the Berth. You're a pilot, you know that. And you know why."

**中文理解**：cop's "I care... But we do not send rescue teams outside of the Berth."。

**关键词**：I care / we do not send rescue teams outside of the Berth / You're a pilot, you know that. And you know why.

**为什么这样写**：把 cop's response 写成 "I care... But"—his bureaucratic care; "You're a pilot, you know that. And you know why."写出 Cora's knowledge (rescue teams would need rescuing)。

**读者视角提示**：Cop's response—bureaucratic, Cora knows why (no rescue teams)。

> **原句 6:** "Okay, but what about—" She pointed at the cop's desk monitor, filled with the files she'd pulled from Twig's computer. Manifestos, group chats, schematics for spacecraft that one hundred percent would not work."

**中文理解**：Cora's attempt——pointing at Twig's files (manifestos, group chats, schematics)——"schematics for spacecraft that one hundred percent would not work"。

**关键词**：Manifestos, group chats, schematics for spacecraft that one hundred percent would not work

**为什么这样写**：把 Cora's attempt 写成 "schematics for spacecraft that one hundred percent would not work"—her desperation; "Manifestos, group chats"写出 Twig's involvement。

**读者视角提示**：Cora's attempt—using Twig's files against Chapter 252。

> **原句 7:** "The irony of trying to sic cops on these fuckwits while having spent these past weeks consoling Signy as she tried to avoid them was not lost on Cora."

**中文理解**：Cora's irony——"trying to sic cops on these fuckwits while having spent these past weeks consoling Signy as she tried to avoid them"。

**关键词**：irony / trying to sic cops on these fuckwits / consoling Signy as she tried to avoid them

> **原句 8:** "I don't care what it takes, I want her back," she said. "She's a good kid. She's just—lost. She's always been a little lost, and—and looking for something. I have to find her. I can't just sit here and do nothing."

**关键词**：I don't care what it takes, I want her back / She's a good kid / She's just—lost / I have to find her / I can't just sit here and do nothing

**读者视角提示**：Cora's desperation—Twig's loss, action compulsion。

> **原句 9:** "Ms. Marten, you might have to face the possibility that there is nothing left to be done." / "Judging by what you've told us and by our examinations of her telecommunications records, she most likely left the Berth three weeks ago." / "No. No, no, no. Cora shook her head."

**关键词**：you might have to face the possibility that there is nothing left to be done / she most likely left the Berth three weeks ago / No. No, no, no

**为什么这样写**：把 cop's assessment 写成 "nothing left to be done"—his realism; "three weeks ago" writes the timeline; Cora's "No. No, no, no." writes her refusal to accept。

**读者视角提示**：Cora's refusal—three weeks (surface survival)。

> **原句 10:** "Three days on the surface would be hard, even if you had great gear. Three weeks was . . ."

**关键词**：Three days on the surface would be hard / Three weeks was . . .

**为什么这样写**：把 cop's assessment 写成 "Three days on the surface would be hard"—his realism; "Three weeks was . . ." (ellipsis) writes the implication (death)。

**读者视角提示**：Cop's assessment—three weeks on surface = likely death。
