# All the Way Home · 情感节点

> 10 个节点。关键引语逐字取自已过门禁的章节块。

## 节点一 · 水瓶坠地，宇宙接住（ch01）
Hour One-10:41 a.m.，Zach 的 Hydroflask 松紧带断裂，水瓶被 teal peacoat 陌生人捡起——「You dropped this」。他为自己在对方面前出丑而失语，对方身边站着酷似的妹妹与熟稔的金发男。恐慌与心动的第一次同框。
> "The bottle goes flying, hitting the ground with a loud clang that reverberates as it skitters through the line."
> "The guy holding his water bottle is a little too striking to exist at all, let alone at an airport at 10 a.m. on a Monday."

## 节点二 · 拦下的 meet-cute 与三天倒计时（ch02）
Rena 一眼认出「像 Josiah」的男孩，把想冲过去的 Kieran 拽住——Kieran 妥协却留下一句「错过了我此生最重要的 meet-cute 都算你头上」。同时，家族的倒计时浮出：worst day 的周年就在三天后。
> "Kieran locks her into a silent battle of wills, and she glares him down until he raises both hands and relents. “All right. But if I just missed out on the meet-cute of my life, it’s on you.”"
> "Even though they’ll be at the lake house, not home where it happened, Mum will fret, and Dad won’t want to talk about it, and Kieran won’t want to talk about it, and nobody will mention that the anniversary of the worst day of their lives is only three days away."

## 节点三 · 17D 与 17E（ch03）
行李失踪的混乱里，Zach 在饮水机边与「Peacoat Boy」重逢——水瓶牵引绳的玩笑换来一个响亮的笑。互报 zone（都是 2）之后，Kieran 亮出登机牌：17D。邻座是命运写好的。
> "“Big market for water-bottle leashes,” Peacoat Boy says, and then turns to the fountain. “What zone are you in?”"
> "“You’re not.” Peacoat Boy’s whole expression changes, that disarming smile flashing across his face."

## 节点四 · 「I asked」与未完成的句子（ch06、ch14）
Rena 目送 Kieran 出手成功，从他站姿里认出 Josiah 的旧影；闪回给出 club 夏天的告白——「He's perfect. You don't understand」——和那声截断一切的关门。而当下，Kieran 对「为什么姐姐同意今天飞」只答两个字：I asked。
> "Whatever he thought, he’d never finished the sentence, because in the distance, a door slammed shut with a brittle echo. Josiah called his name from across the field."
> "A flicker like a dancing flame flashes across his face, dismay there one moment, gone the next. When he speaks, his voice is light. “I asked.”"

## 节点五 · 同校乌龙，Ouch 时刻（ch22）
A14 登机口的闲谈让 Zach 得知 Kieran 一直在 U of C——「你故意让我以为不用担心」。他脱口说出「我可能还会见到你」，把 Kieran 的善意谎言钉成伤害。Kieran 只回一句 raw 的反问，Seth 的电话在此刻尴尬地响起。
> "“Ouch, Zach.” His voice sounds a little raw, and more than a little wounded. “Jesus Christ. That would be so terrible?”"
> "Voice tight, nearly colorless, Kieran says, “You should get that.”"

## 节点六 · 镜子与 hurricane（ch24）
Zach 离场后的死寂里，Kieran 要求一场真正的架。Rena 的「playing with him」起诉换来「I fucking loved him」，她那句脱口而出的「你到底怎么了」碎在半空——而他的回击把矛头转向她：照照镜子，看得够久你会看见别的什么。全章以 hurricane 的预言收束。
> "“What is wrong with you?” It’s out before Rena realizes she’s going to say it."
> "“Look at yourself in the mirror.” Kieran shakes his head. “Maybe if you look long enough, you’ll see something other than me.”"

## 节点七 · worst day：他没能应答（ch38）
Sahar 的对质让 Rena 终于说出「Kieran almost died」。闪回回到去年圣诞夜：Uber 上那条以 Love you 结尾的短信、错放的爵士黑胶、Sully 扒门、淋浴声后无人应答——「不是不想，是不能」。
> "Rena takes a breath, and then she tells the truth. “Kieran almost died.”"
> "Somehow, right then in the wing-flicker space between heartbeats, blood like ice, Rena knew it wasn’t because he didn’t want to."

## 节点八 · 大和解：always be here（ch44）
Kieran 主动求战，Rena 的「playing with him」被「I fucking loved him」翻案——ghost 是自保（Josiah 一句 psycho 之语）。她第一次先抱住弟弟；错责互驳之后，他立誓永远在场。
> "“I’m sorry.” Rena steps forward and wraps her arms around him, hooking her chin against his shoulder."
> "“I will always,” Kieran says, eyes glittering, with an unfamiliar, solemn determination in his voice, “always be here when you need me.”"

## 节点九 · liminal inches 与双向解绑（ch41、ch47）
封锁翼的雪窗前，「总得有一个人勇敢」换来全书第一吻；Rena 召唤打断第二回合。同一夜，Rena 用 wired 的真相与 Lucas 完成分手——「我也一直在努力快乐」让这段六年感情双人签字结案，握手与最后的拥抱交在起飞广播之前。
> "“Well.” Kieran swallows, Adam’s apple bobbing. “I guess one of us is going to have to be brave now.”"
> "“I was trying to be,” Lucas tells her, and the first tear falls. He lets it. “I’m still trying to be.”"
> "“Attention all Air Canada passengers,” the attendant begins, relief flooding her voice. “Flight AC156 to Toronto has been cleared to depart, and we will begin boarding immediately.”"

## 节点十 · 烟火、拉黑与 Liftoff（ch51–53）
湖边夜：Kieran 与 Mara 的密谋用烟火替代流星雨——「第二好的」。Zach 按下拖欠数月的 Block caller，把过去关在车外。lake house 厨房里 Mum 说 It's time，Kieran begins。Epilogue 的 1 月 11 日，Room 72 门口，三个字对一个字——Liftoff。
> "“It’s not quite a meteor shower,” Kieran says, somehow suddenly at his elbow. “But I thought this might be a close second.”"
> "In a feat of incredible eloquence, he says, “You’re here.”"
> "Liftoff."
