# 《Pumpkin Spice Promises》情感节点

## 节点一：雨夜迎撞（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 五年前的九月一日，Carson 带着第一份真金白银的赞助驶向田纳西；雨里一辆皮卡甩尾直冲——挡风玻璃碎裂、气囊爆开，她担心着马与祖父母在黑暗中坠下。全书从这场黑屏开始倒计时。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I read the email three times before I finally believed it. Then a fourth. And a fifth. Just to be extra sure I wasn’t misreading anything. … My heart hammered wildly against my ribs. This is what I had been waiting for all these years. A sponsorship. … Not a discount code. Not free gear. Not a local feed store wanting a picture for Facebook. An actual sponsorship with real money."（ch01）

> ② "Meme hugged me twice before I climbed into the driver’s seat of the red Ford F-150 Pops bought for me when I turned sixteen. … “Don’t forget where home is, Sunshine.” … My chest tightened at the silly nickname, one that I had found embarrassing at one point in my life, but now loved. “You know I could never.”"（ch01）

> ③ "Then the driver overcorrected. The truck fishtailed. The headlights swung sideways. Straight toward me. … The truck filled my windshield. The glass shattered, slicing my skin as metal crunched around me, the noise so deafening I couldn’t hear my own scream. … Please let Blueberry be okay. … The impact snapped my head back, and darkness swallowed everything before I could so much as twitch."（ch01）

## 节点二：空门廊（ch02）

**叙事概括：** 五年后的送奶日：万圣节布景从满格清到零，镇子以为她只是「出了事故、不再骑」——义肢、农场濒危、母亲缺席三层秘密叠在一条牛仔裤下。开场即全书信息差的完整地图。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I loaded the last crate of milk into Pops’s truck and slammed the tailgate shut. The sound echoed across Marigold Ranch. … Five years ago, the ranch would have been covered in pumpkins by now. Orange lights would have been hanging from the fence posts. Hay bales would have lined the driveway. Pops would’ve been arguing with Meme about where to put the scarecrows while pretending not to enjoy every second of it. … Now the porch sat empty. No pumpkins. No lights. No decorations."（ch02）

> ② "Most people in Willow Creek didn’t know the full extent of my injuries, and that was exactly how Pops and Meme wanted it. > ③ "Five years ago, I’d spent half my life making fun of this truck. Now it was one of the few things I had left of Pops. … The engine sputtered. Once. Twice. … As if the morning wasn’t bad enough, the sky finally opened. Rain hammered the roof of the truck so hard I could barely hear myself think. A crack of thunder rattled the windows, making me jump as I grabbed my phone from the cupholder. … “Well,” I muttered, reaching for the door handle. “This is going to suck.”"（ch02）
