# 情感节点

## 节点一：归乡

**叙事概括**：James 驱车回到阔别二十年的 Six Rivers 森林，处理亡兄 Johnny 的遗物。她与 Johnny 的"双胞胎联结"让她"亲历"了他的死亡瞬间——子弹穿透肋骨，森林树冠在风中摇摆。

**关键引语**：
- We were made in the dark. I used to hate it when Johnny said that, but now I know it's true.
- There was no world without Johnny.

## 节点二：调查的开始

**叙事概括**：James 在 Johnny 的遗物中发现了一卷标注为"TG 11/10"的胶卷，冲洗后发现了 Autumn Fischer 的粉色背包。她开始调查 Autumn 的下落，联系了 Autumn 的室友 Maria，发现 Autumn 从未到达 Byron。

**关键引语**：
- I'd felt the white-hot heat of that bullet pierce between my ribs.
- How was it possible that Johnny, everything he was, every memory he had, every thought and feeling, was now just…dust?

## 节点三：重逢

**叙事概括**：James 与 Micah Rhodes（她的前男友）重逢。Micah 承认他与 Johnny 曾在夏天争吵（关于 Johnny 与 Autumn 的"关系"），并在 Johnny 死前几周留下了语音信箱。

**关键引语**：
- The last time I'd walked through that door, I'd been an eighteen-year-old girl with rehearsed words on my lips, only minutes from telling a lie that would change my entire life.
- You just…erased me. Like I never existed.

## 节点四：峡谷之夜

**叙事概括**：James 和 Micah 在峡谷中过夜，抽大麻后分享了各自的创伤。James 揭示了 Griffin 试图侵犯她的真相，Micah 承认他知道 Johnny 的"联结"能力。

**关键引语**：
- He was trying to…I don't know, start something with me. That day in the gorge, he tried to…touch me.
- I'd believed my whole life that there was no me without Johnny. It turns out, I was wrong.

## 节点五：真相的碎片

**叙事概括**：James 发现 Olivia Shaw 是 Instagram 账号 @firstfrostchronicle，并发现 Olivia 在 Autumn 离开那天早上与她在一起。Olivia 最终承认 Autumn 在拍照时从悬崖上摔落死亡。

**关键引语**：
- It was an accident. I honestly don't remember who was first one to say it—me or Micah. I only know that it immediately became true.
- She was just so…special.

## 节点六：追悼会

**叙事概括**：Johnny 的追悼会在 Six Rivers Diner 举行。Sadie 在悼词中说 Johnny 是"温柔的灵魂，狂野的心"。James 发现了一张 Sadie 的照片——她手持一把旧猎枪，与杀死 Johnny 的子弹相符。

**关键引语**：
- We're here tonight to say goodbye to Johnny Golden. A soft soul with a wild heart.
- It was her. I rasped.

## 节点七：告别

**叙事概括**：James 和 Micah 在峡谷中撒 Johnny 的骨灰。Johnny 的"鬼魂"最后一次出现并"离开"，暗示 James 终于"放下"了他。

**关键引语**：
- Goodbye is a lost language. A silent one.
- It had taken losing Johnny to fully know him, but there was more to it than that. I had to lose him in order to even know myself.

## 节点八：选择

**叙事概括**：James 回到旧金山参加画展，但 Micah 出现在画廊。James 选择回到 Six Rivers 与 Micah 一起生活——她怀孕了。

**关键引语**：
- The city had no soul.
- I want you to take me home.

## 节点九：新生

**叙事概括**：James 与 Micah 在 Six Rivers 的怀孕生活。James 怀孕（孩子是 Micah 的），Johnny 和 Autumn 的命运在峡谷中"联结"。James 最终理解"我们是在黑暗中创造的"。

**关键引语**：
- I'd believed my whole life that there was no me without Johnny. It turns out, I was wrong.
- Maybe we were made in the dark, like Johnny said. But we'd found a way to create our own kind of light.
