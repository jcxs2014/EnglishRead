# Twisted Lies

## 概述

Ana Huang 的《Twisted Lies》是"Twisted"系列的收官之作，以双主角交替第一人称叙事展开——单数章属于 Stella Alonso，多数章属于 Christian Harper。故事发生在华盛顿特区，把一段从"假恋爱"起步的交易关系，写成两条互相撕扯的线：一条是关于一个过于习惯隐忍的女孩如何学会开口索要；另一条是关于一个用冷血统治世界的男人如何被迫承认，他早在很久以前就已经动了心。

Stella Alonso 是 DC 小有名气的时尚博主，白天在《D.C. Style》杂志做助理，被上司 Meredith 当作随手差遣的隐形人。她真正的软肋是 Maura——那个"practically raised me"、如今住在 Greenfield 养老院、患着阿尔茨海默症的老人。当 Greenfield 通知月费将涨到六千五百美元，Stella 的全部生活立刻被一笔她负担不起的账单压住。她在暴雪夜独自走回家，路边一个声音叫住了她：

> "Ms. Alonso." … The deep, rich voice brushed my skin like black velvet and stopped me in my tracks. A shiver chased its wake, born of equal parts pleasure and warning. … I recognized that voice. I'd heard it only three times in my life, but that was enough. Like the man who owned it, it was unforgettable.（ch01）

那是 Christian Harper，她所住大楼 Mirage 的所有者。她只听过这个声音三次，却已经无法忘记。他把她塞进自己的车，而她的身体先于理智服从了：

> "Get in the car, Stella." … I got in the car. … I told myself it was because the temperature had somehow dropped twenty degrees in the space of five minutes, but I knew that was a lie. It was the sound of my name, in that voice, delivered with such calm authority my body obeyed before I could protest.（ch01）

Christian 是 Harper Security 的创始人，一个把控制当成本能的男人。他从不承认自己在意什么，却已经为 Stella 破例——他的办公室里锁着一份关于她的档案，他暗暗操纵着她身边的一切。当他看见 Stella 的老友、也是 Christian 自己第一位客户的 Dante Russo 吻上 Stella 的手背时，他给出的反应不像房东，而像一个占有者：

> "Touch Stella again, and you'll no longer have a fiancée." … I slammed the door in his face. … Dante was my first client and an old friend. I didn't provoke him often. But like I said, I didn't like people touching what was mine.（ch02）

Stella 被《D.C. Style》辞退、母亲的生日宴又近在眼前，她的经纪人 Brady 出了个馊主意：找一个假的男朋友，堵住家人的嘴。她列了一张候选人清单，最后找上的却是那个最不该找的人。Christian 答应了——但条件由他定，条款由他写。她签下这份合同时，自己心里最清楚这桩交易的性质：

> "I think I signed a deal with the devil. … Okay, that sounds a little dramatic, but you get the idea. Christian has been super nice and helpful since we met, but he didn't get to where he is today by being all warm and fuzzy."（ch06）

"假恋爱"很快溢出纸面。Christian 把她带进他的世界：慈善晚宴、画廊开幕、他朋友们的牌局。她也在他的世界里第一次被当成一件值得保护的东西对待。但阴影从未离开——那个两年前就缠上她的跟踪者卷土重来，在她公寓的门上留下字条：

> "You were supposed to wait for me, Stella. You didn't. … No direct threat, but the message was ominous enough to send my dinner rising in my throat. … Ugly memories from two years ago swamped me in a rush."（ch11）

当跟踪者终于闯进她的房间，Christian 不再给她商量的余地：

> "Everything looks clear, but you're moving into my place until we sort this out." … I should've listened to my gut and insisted she move in with me after the first note, but I hadn't wanted to push her too far, too soon. … But now that the creep had gotten into her apartment, in my building…（ch16）

Stella 搬进 Mirage 顶层公寓，两人在同一屋檐下重新丈量彼此的边界。Christian 主动提出帮她做起自己的时尚品牌，替她挡掉所有明枪暗箭；而 Stella 逐渐看清，她面对的不只是保护。这个从不说爱的男人，对自己的欲望有一套更冷酷的定义：

> "I didn't love her, but I wanted her with an intensity that left the flimsy, overused concept of love in the dust. It wasn't sweet or saccharine. There were no rainbows or unicorns, only desire edged with roughness and darkness. … Hot June days. Secret smiles. Turquoise. … I'd waited a long time. … Eventually, I would catch her, and once I did, I was never letting her go."（ch25）

夏威夷、意大利……在罗马，Christian 第一次讲出自家的过去：父亲是艺术品窃贼，母亲的遗书藏在一幅画里。他把自己最难堪的部分摊开在她面前，而 Stella 给他的回应，是一句他从未打算相信的话：

> "I love you," I whispered. … I knew he didn't believe in love. … I knew there was a strong chance he wouldn't say it back. … But I had to tell him anyway. … "The day I met you," he said. "Was the luckiest day of my life. You've always been the brightest part of my world, Butterfly. And you always will be." … "I believe in everything when it comes to you." … Including love.（ch39）

崩塌来得很轻——她在他办公室的暗格里发现了那些档案。不是只有跟踪者的资料，而是她整个人生：家人、朋友、Maura、历任男友，全部被归档、被分析、被她爱着的那个人瞒了几年。她终于明白，这段关系的地基是谎言：

> "But they didn't change the fact that our relationship had been built on a lie. He knew how much the stalker had traumatized me. How much I hated the invasion of privacy and loss of control over my own life. … Christian did what he did before the stalker showed up, but he'd sat on those files for years and never told me."（ch42）

分手之后，Christian 一天写一封信，写了整整二十封，从不寄出。而真正把他从深渊里拽出来的，是 Stella 被绑架的消息。绑架她的人是 Julian Kensler——她的大学同学，也是那篇写她的报道的作者，更是她所住 Mirage 里一位过世住户的孙子（那位老人把公寓留给了他）。在全书的至暗处，被绑在金属棚屋里的 Stella 是这样回答他的：

> "I would rather die than be with you." … I headbutted him as hard as I could. … His howl of pain ricocheted through the cabin. … Lights washed across my vision at the force of the impact, but I didn't have time to waste. I slammed my wrists down as hard as I could behind me until the frayed rope snapped against the protrusion.（ch49）

Christian 杀死了 Julian。这一枪没有悬念，作者甚至没有为它保留任何犹豫——他没有把她带回深渊，而是把她带回了家。

## 主题

**被看见的渴望**。Stella 惯于把自己缩到最小：对上司、对父母、对这个总在评价她的世界。她做博主，是为了被看见；她做助理，是为了不被看见。而 Christian 是唯一一个从第一眼起就"看见"了她的人，哪怕他的方式一开始是监视与掌控。全书最大的进展，不是她获得了多少关注，而是她终于敢于要求——要求被爱、被尊重、被当作一个平等的人。

**金钱、羞耻与尊严**。Greenfield 的账单、Delamonte 的大使合约、Christian 随手送出的珠宝，每一笔钱都在书里带着重量。Stella 的本能是拒绝被供养，因为接受意味着欠下；而 Christian 送出的每一件礼物都在试探这条线。作者最终给出的答案并不清高也不庸俗——尊严不在于不要钱，而在于清楚自己为什么收下它。

**控制与放手**。Christian 的整个世界都建立在控制之上：他筛选员工、操纵市场、把对手的公司从内部拆掉。唯一他控制不了的是 Stella。全书让他学会的，恰恰是放手——烧掉那幅困住他半生的画时，他是这样说的：

> "In hindsight, she was so inconsequential. A complicated problem of my own design, fabricated to shield me from my past. … Everyone thought she was important because she contained some big business secret or shocking revelation when the truth was so much simpler. … She represented the part of my past I'd never been able to let go of. A wound I'd covered with temporary bandaids to hide the festering disease that'd been eating me alive from the inside out for decades."（ch55）

## 结局

一年后，Stella 在米兰办成了自己的第一场时装秀；在波西塔诺的落日里，Christian 单膝跪地：

> "I'm glad you think so." Christian's velvet murmur brushed my skin, along with an odd roughness I'd never heard before. "I couldn't decide whether to do this in Hawaii or Italy, but it seems I made the right choice." … Because before me, framed by pastel-covered hills and the golden hues of sunset, was a sight I'd never anticipated. … Christian Harper on one knee, velvet box open in hand to reveal a dazzling diamond ring set with emeralds. … "Stella, will you marry me?"（ch56）

四年后的尾声里，他们结了婚，搬去纽约，两个人的公司都在曼哈顿落户。Maura 在他们的婚礼后一个月于睡梦中离世。而在一个圣诞夜，Stella 把丈夫叫进厨房，说出了最后那个秘密：

> "I didn't want to tell you in front of the others, but I also couldn't wait anymore." Stella's voice lowered. "Christian, I'm pregnant." … The words echoed in my head, too gilded with shock to sink in fully. … A fierce protectiveness gripped my chest. … Once upon a time, I hadn't believed in love. … Now, I realize that love was the last piece that'd been missing in the puzzle of my life. … With it, I was finally whole.（ch57）

Christian 曾是一个坚信自己不懂爱的人。故事的落点，是他终于承认，爱是那块一直缺失的拼图——补上它，他才算完整。
