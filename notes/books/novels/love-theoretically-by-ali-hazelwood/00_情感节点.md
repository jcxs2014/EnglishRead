## 情感弧线总览

本书的情感弧线不是"相爱—误会—和好"的经典三段，而是一条**解除武装**的曲线：Elsie 的每一段关系（Jack、Dr. L.、Cece、母亲）都经历同一个节点——"定制版本被拒绝，真版本被接住"。Jack 是第一个拒绝接收任何"定制版本"的人，Dr. L. 是第一个因她卸下版本而撕破脸的人，Cece 是第一个让她验证"真话不会赶走人"的人。全书最陡的温差在 ch23–ch24：幸福峰值与感情冰点只隔一场 Blitz Go 决赛。

---

### 1. 隔间里的第一次贴身（ch01–ch02）

**节点叙事**：寿宴洗手间，假女友被客户的哥哥护在怀里——全书在最大公约数的尴尬里开局。Go 棋盘上她与他打成平手，指尖相触有电流：她习惯了所有人给她信号，这个人给的是棋局。

**关键引语**：
- "In my life I have experienced regret, embarrassment, maybe even a touch of agony. But nothing, absolutely nothing prepared me for the ignominy of finding myself in a bathroom stall, pressed against the arrogant older brother of the guy I've been pretending to date for the past six months."
- "My fingers brush against Jack's, and something electric and unidentifiable licks up my arm."

---

### 2. 宿敌揭晓：十二年恨意的宣战（ch03–ch04）

**节点叙事**：面试的对手就是毁掉导师的人。她第一次不讨好、不建模，把恨意当面押上桌——APE 策略对这个人彻底失效，而失效本身竟让她感到轻松。

**关键引语**：
- "I hated you when I was twelve and read what you'd done in Scientific American. I've hated you harder, I've hated you longer, and I've hated you for better reasons."
- "Here's the deal: things between him and me are unsalvageably bad. I'll never conjure an Elsie able to please him, especially since he's figured out my APE strategies. And as much as I hate knowing that there's someone out there whom I cannot win over, it also lets me off the hook. With Jack, I don't need to be someone else, because I can't be someone else. It's unsettling, and disturbingly baring, and also . . . relaxing."

---

### 3. 为复仇而战的诱惑（ch05–ch06）

**节点叙事**：Dr. L. 把她的求职改写成他的战争——"For revenge"两个字为全书埋下最阴的线。她带着半真半假的话离开导师办公室，第一次用"lielet"给自己的谎言记账。

**关键引语**：
- "Now that I think about it, you winning the position over his handpicked candidate might be an opportunity in disguise." He slowly breaks into a smile. "For revenge."
- "Ja—he's the head of the Physics Institute. He's . . . barely part of the search. I might never meet him again." I wrap a hand around a green armrest. Okay, this one's a lie. But small. A lielet.

---

### 4. 被唯一之人看穿（ch06–ch08）

**节点叙事**：教学演示上她绝地翻盘，一对一面试却被逐层看穿；参观系里变成他"参观她"，而低血糖时第一个递果汁的也是他。被看见的恐惧与被照顾的暖意第一次同时出现。

**关键引语**：
- "No, I didn't." Jack's expression sharpens. He straightens and leans forward, elbows on his desk. The entire room shifts and thickens with tension. "I did know, however, that there is something about you. That you tirelessly study people. Figure out who they are, what they want, and then mold yourself into whatever shape you think will fit them."
- "You lie, Elsie. Every single one of your interactions is a lie." He crosses his arms and looms. We're supposed to be on a tour of the department. I feel like he's taking a tour of me.

---

### 5. 保护与崩塌（ch09–ch10）

**节点叙事**：Austin 在派对上把四年前被拒的旧账当众翻出，诬她是"escort"——她的职业世界与学术世界在一句话里相撞。Jack 挺身而出，而她的感受是双重崩塌：被保护，也被看穿到体无完肤。

**关键引语**：
- "I understand what I'm seeing, so listen to me, asshole." Jack's tone is chilling. Terrifyingly calm. Austin pales and takes a small step back, and I almost feel sorry for him. "You're harassing a woman who asked you to get out of her personal space while she's at a work function. Because she rejected you."
- He sees everything. Every molecule I am built of—he could list it, describe it, reproduce it in a lab. He sees the rebar structure in me, and I . . . I see nothing. I understand nothing.

### 6. "Spectacular"：敌意破产后剩下的东西（ch11–ch13）

**节点叙事**：牙科急诊的意外同处、Jack 的深夜坦白、雪夜的崩溃与追逐——他从"希望她撤回候选"讲到"没算到她是惊艳的"，再用一句"I like to see you"把看穿变成告白。假日期里的 George 真身揭晓，误会反而清空了道路。

**关键引语**：
- "I'm relieved because whatever thing I have for her, it'll go away. It won't survive knowing that she lied. Except that I didn't account for having to watch her talk about physics, or read her work. I didn't account for having to spend two days with her and finding out that she is . . ." He smiles at me. Gentle. Resigned. "Spectacular."
- "I like to see you. When you're not trying to be someone else."

---

### 7. 诚实规则（ch15–ch19）

**节点叙事**：拍立得、祖母的身世课、芝士店的 J.J. 创伤长谈、George 的合同——关系升温的每一步都由同一条规则护航："No lies, Elsie."（只要是你）她第一次有了可以说真话的对象，也开始练习审计"被照顾"与"被控制"的区别。

**关键引语**：
- "If we do this . . . when you're with me, I need you to be honest. No pretending you're someone else. No trying to be whatever you think it is that I want. You say what you think. And when you can't, at least let yourself think it. No lies, Elsie." His jaw sets. "Just you."
- "I'm thinking that two things can be true at once: you want to protect me, and also do it in a patronizing way. I'm thinking that by trying to respect me, you ended up making a decision for me—like everyone else before you."

---

### 8. 冰点（ch20–ch24）

**节点叙事**：幸福在五天内登顶（过夜、数纹身、烤芝士、Blitz Go 决赛的胜利），也在一夜之间冻结——Andrea 的一句质问撬出 Annals 文章的全部真相，她用他教的诚实赶他走。爱与背叛指向同一个男人的两副面孔。

**关键引语**：
- "That's not for you to decide! And anyway, why do you think I spent my life lying to people, Jack?" I explode. "Why do you think I never told Laurendeau that I hate teaching, or Cece that her movies are worse than a Windows screen saver, or Mom that I'm a real fucking human being? Because I'm afraid that if I hurt them with the truth, then they'll leave me. Why is it only a good excuse when it comes to you?"
- "I am." I'm starting to sob. I want him to touch me, but I cannot stand for him to be here. "You always talk about what I want, Jack. You helped me learn how to ask for it. Well." I force myself to look him squarely in the eye and show him that I mean what I say, even though I'm not sure I do. There's a burning heat in my chest, scalding, painful. "Right now, I don't want to be with you. I need you to give me some space."

### 9. 双重决裂与重新出发（ch24–ch25）

**节点叙事**：与导师的办公室对峙把六年恩情清账——施压、辱骂、谎言被戳穿、"你欠我的"浮出水面；她留下"改叫我 Elsie"的宣言离开，随后接受 George 的 offer、对 Cece 抖出全部伪装并重新握手。真话第一次在三个方向同时被证明不会赶走人（制度回函、挚友相拥、新同事的欢呼）。

**关键引语**：
- "Dr. Laurendeau," I say before turning around, "you should really start calling me Elsie."
- "I'm Elsie. And I really like cheese, particle physics, and movies with sparkly vampires."

### 10. 公开信与 "right here"（ch26–终章）

**节点叙事**：他的公开信把赎罪摆上全世界的案头，她的告白撞上散场的人潮；他用最长的独白接住她的"我恨你"，然后把节奏权交还——"take your time"。八个月后，Nature Physics 的接受函与樱桃 Jell-O 里的对撞机模型一起到场，她的卡片写下了全书的答案。

**关键引语**：
- "I want you, Elsie. All the time. I think of you. All. The. Fucking. Time. I'm distracted. I'm shit at work. And my first instinct, the very first time I saw you, was to run away. Because I knew that if we'd start doing this, we would never stop. And that's exactly how it is. There is no universe in which I'm going to let you go."
- "Dear Jack, I know I've been slow, but I just wanted you to know something: I'm right here. With you."
