# 金句精选

① *"It was the perfect morning for a duel."*
— Viscount，ch01 初遇，以讽刺语气掩饰对 Phoebe 鲁莽行为的关注



⑤ *"Sophie insisted, tying the gold filigree mask around Phoebe's hair."*
— ch16，filigree 意象：精致外表下的金属内核，暗示 Phoebe 的双重身份

⑥ *"'Well, I think Sophie would be most happy to help you in your hour of need,' Phoebe smiled."*
— ch16，Phoebe 代 Sophie 应承援助，揭示姐妹间的默契与各自的感情走向

⑦ *"'You look quite the dazzling debutante!' He smiled then, his teeth glinting in the candlelight."*
— ch24，Thomas 的讽刺赞美；glint 呼应 Viscount 的金色意象

⑧ *"'La! Does no one tell you poor country mice anything?' Aurelia purred."*
— ch24，揭露赌债婚约的真相，Aurelia 成为真相的使者

⑨ *"'You're in love with him! It's as plain as a pikestaff!'"*
— Aurelia，ch26，当面对 Phoebe 的终极戳穿，迫使她面对真心

⑩ *"She drew a steadying breath, there were still the Sydney Gardens to navigate."*
— ch26，breakneck speed 下的理性挣扎，Phoebe 拒绝承认感情的瞬间

⑪ *"Seconds later, both phaetons were bolting along the bridge at breakneck speed, their surroundings already a blur."*
— ch26，疾驰的身体体验与内心觉醒同步，外在速度与心理速度的双重高峰

⑫ *"'I have never betrayed your confidence except in frank admiration of a young woman who appeared unafraid of anything.'"*
— Viscount（经 Phoebe 回忆），ch26，frank admiration：Viscount 对 Phoebe 所有"干扰"的真实动机

⑬ *"Phoebe swallowed, memories of their many encounters fluttering through her head, like pages from a book she hadn't read carefully enough."*
— ch26，记忆的重新解读： Phoebe 开始用新的眼光看待 Viscount

⑭ *"Of all the courageous, hare-brained simpletons!' the viscount thundered."*
— ch27，disembodied voice：Viscount 总是先于视觉出现，无法回避

⑮ *"Phoebe, this is ridiculous!' Thomas expostulated. 'You are to marry the earl in a week!"*
— ch27，Thomas 的真实动机被揭穿后，他用"责任"包装愤怒

⑯ *"She'd done nothing but detest him since the very first day they met."*
— ch26，Phoebe 用 detest 压制对 Viscount 的真实感情，detest 本身是强烈情绪投入的证明

⑰ *"'Your life can continue as it always has – I will consider myself a … companion only.' His relief was palpable."*
— ch25，Captain 的 marriage of convenience：他需要的是掩护，不是爱情

⑱ *"'We're still leaving the moment Dr Kapoor has seen her.'"*
— ch27，Captain 在感情与生存之间的最后挣扎

⑲ *"Josephine's fever refused to abate for a further two days."*
— ch23，abate：疾病顽抗，Phoebe 的恐惧升级

⑳ *"'Jo … I'm here now,' she whispered hoarsely."*
— ch27，Phoebe 对 Josephine 的罪责感与照顾承诺

㉑ *"And day by day, hour by hour, ever since, it had been thawing."*
— ch28，Phoebe 回到 moorland 后冰心融化的隐喻

㉒ *"She drew to a halt, unwilling to tread further and watch the apparition melt away with the night shadows."*
— ch28，apparition：Phoebe 不敢相信 Viscount 真的出现在 moorland

㉓ *"'On the contrary,' he smiled, gold flecks glistening, 'it's just I believe you would make a much better viscountess.'"*
— ch28，Viscount 对"hoydenish"的正面定义：他爱的正是她的"野"

㉔ *"'I've never known a girl as headstrong, stubborn, reckless, and hopelessly courageous as you,' he murmured. 'You are a heroine in every way.'"*
— ch28，Viscount 对 Phoebe 的最终定义：heroism 不是社会认可的德行，而是她的本质

㉕ *"'Well then…' she replied with a gentle smile, 'it's probably the most heroic thing I can do.'"*
— ch28，Phoebe 的最终回答：嫁给 Viscount 不是"屈服"，而是又一次"英勇冒险"
