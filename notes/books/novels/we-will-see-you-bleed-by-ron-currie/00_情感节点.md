# 《We Will See You Bleed》情感节点

## 节点一：显灵与使命（ch01）

1984 年 8 月的酷暑，Mill 停摆后的 Babs 在厨房迎来死去十年母亲 Rose 的显灵——讨咖啡、点烟、宣告「更大的考验将至」。母亲的判词把她的创伤重新命名为身份：受害者只算那几秒，从刀刃进去那一刻起你就是动手的人。显灵以一只凭空被喝空的咖啡杯收束，幻觉与神迹的界线从此失效。

> "Sacha made you a killer. The only choice you have is whether to accept what you are, and what you must do. Knowing it will make you hated, and hunted.."（ch01）
>
> "Then you reached for the other mug, the one you’d poured for your mother and left on the counter, but when you turned it upside down over the sink you found that it was now impossibly, inexplicably empty, just a few drops of coffee tapping against the stainless steel of the sink.."（ch01）

## 节点二：链锯、归乡与「戒了杀人」（ch02）

持链锯的刺客在酒吧被 Rheal 反制，口供牵出 Aucoin 与毒品渗透；Rita 五年后踏下灰狗巴士，在满街 FOR LEASE 里走回那栋唯一亮灯的房子。重逢先死抱、后算账，Babs 在斗嘴的最末句承认自己「戒了杀人」——一个 Rita 听不懂、读者已经懂的承诺。

> "When the guy with the chain saw walked in, Babs’s husband, Rheal Dionne, was seated alone at the Chez Paree Bar and Nightclub, three whiskeys deep and lost in a stir of thoughts.."（ch02）
>
> "Killing ages a person,” Babs said. “That’s why I swore off it.."（ch02）

## 节点三：心碎与两颗肾（ch03）

Claude 为 Sophie 的透析费向公司递话，被 Rheal 在酒吧摊牌擒下——「你伤透了我的心」。同章 Rheal 自曝与 Babs「弄丢了彼此」：罢工话挤掉了所有别的话，还有一根说不清的杠杆在撬开他们。Clement 的「锁箱论」给这场婚姻的静默下了存在论注脚。

> "You know I’m not much of a crier. If I was, you’d understand how much this conversation has hurt me. You broke my fucking heart, Claude.."（ch03）
>
> "Laughter is much more eloquent. So’s suicide. Each one of us is trapped in our own skull, alone at the center of all creation.."（ch06）

## 节点四：女人们起立（ch04）

Jackson 的演讲余温未散，撤资的坏消息已到——体育馆里男人们瘫坐、女人们为尊严起立亮牙。Rita 断言 Babs 迟早杀人，Carmeline 骂圣保罗滚回去喂不饱的孩子；女人们的战争内阁在此集结完毕。

> "Because they want you to see each other as enemies, and the easiest way to do that is to pit black against white. But you aren’t enemies. You are all brothers and sisters.."（ch04）
>
> "The men, quick to anger at the beginning of the strike but not much for endurance, had burned out months earlier, while the women’s greater perspective and self-restraint had preserved their energy for this moment, when they had nothing left to lose but the last thing: the dignity of every person who’d ever walked through the gates of the Hollingsworth & Whitney mill to punch a clock.."（ch04）

## 节点五：十四岁的自白（ch05）

厨房圆桌逼宫，Rita 用 Mafia 本益比请求 Babs 领导「法裔黑帮」。Babs 以修女院五年的孤独交出自白书——十四岁杀人的滋味、夜夜的血、洗到脱皮的手——再用「你们将终生回头张望、永不真正入睡」把王座的代价逐条念给选民听。

> "Barely sprouted tits and I had killed a man. Most people go their whole lives without knowing what that feels like. But I was fourteen, and I knew.."（ch05）
>
> "But understand this—if you get what you’re asking for, you will spend every day, for the rest of your life, looking over your shoulder. You will never really sleep again. It will be my way in all matters, all the time, and you will hate and fear me.."（ch05）

## 节点六：两分钱桥崩塌（ch05）

Aucoin 的人混入集会，把两千名醉汉「骗成自毁」——跳水、跺桥、抛瓶，百年老桥在镜头与亡魂见证下塌进河里。Babs 在桥上认下「这是她的错」，抽出甩棍第一次行使残忍/仁慈的天职，被闺蜜们拖下桥的那一刻，桥的远端正砸进水中。

> "Before long one of the cops took a bottle to the head and went down, and that, for whatever reason, was the thing that suddenly clarified Babs’s mind: Aucoin’s men, infiltrating the march here and there, hadn’t come to strikebreak; they’d come to fool the strikers into breaking themselves.."（ch05）
>
> "Babs knew now that this was her fault. She’d thought she could shirk her duty. She’d refused, ever since returning to Little Canada, the responsibility of deciding when to be cruel and when to be merciful, for the good of all.."（ch05）

## 节点七：Sacha 真相与爬行层（ch06／ch09）

牢房里 Babs 第一次完整交待 Sacha 之夜——刚满十四岁、刚被强暴、几分钟后他死了、谁杀的谁也猜不准。数日后，同一套「看不见的手」替她清理了 Davies：便士恶作剧击溃心理，Saucier 兄弟把 Aucoin 的毒赃与磨号改械枪种进局长卧室上方的爬行层，匿名女声的电话完成最后一步。

> "He’d just finished raping me,” Babs said. “A few minutes later, he’d been stabbed to death, though by whom is anyone’s guess. I didn’t see a thing.."（ch06）
>
> "They would find, in the crawl space above Davies’s bedroom, right where the anonymous caller had told them it would be, right where the Saucier brothers had planted it, the portion of Aucoin’s stolen drugs that had been held in reserve for this purpose: one hundred grams of uncut cocaine, half a kilo of pot, and two hundred pills of various kinds, all requiring either a prescription or a watertight explanation or else it was your ass.."（ch09）

## 节点八：ghetto pass 与拇指救场（ch07／ch10）

接管之夜，Babs 拉下面罩驱逐 Charlie——「你的街区通行证已吊销」；Stella 的球棒像节拍器，快乐第一次被承认是危险的。数日后 Charlie 回镇挑衅，Babs 当众抵腮扣枪，Rheal 在最后一瞬用拇指隔开击锤与撞针——他一生最快的动作第一次用来阻止暴力。

> "Now you know who did this to you,” Babs said. “And you’ve got a lot more batting practice to look forward to if you’re still here when we come back tomorrow. You like dealing for Aucoin so much, move to Lewiston and do it there.."（ch07）
>
> "Because Rheal had, at the last moment, reached out and put his thumb between the hammer and the firing pin. Keeping his hand on the pistol, he wrapped his arms tight around Babs while she kicked and cursed.."（ch10）

## 节点九：「rest of time」与船坞（ch15／ch16）

术后清晨，Claude 被 Sophie 半推半就地「赶」出门——那个额吻成了余生的最后一面。同夜，被 Aucoin 一句「不去澄清」点燃的 Rheal 把清白的 Claude 押到船坞：承认背叛换活命。Claude 拒绝为谎言盖章，只求他照顾 Sophie 和孩子们——「你开口求这个，是一种侮辱」。

> "And then Claude kissed his wife on the forehead and walked out of their home for the rest of time.."（ch15）
>
> "No," Claude said. "I won’t tell my best friend I betrayed him when I did not.."（ch16）

## 节点十：告解、房子与两束花（ch18／ch19／ch20／ch21）

Babs 在病床上只问一句「是否还怀着孕」并得到「是」；同一条电话线里 Rheal 交出 Claude 的死讯。谷仓与船坞之后，Babs 立下「如上帝般不容指摘」的新宪法、被 Sophie 一句「这房子还是我的」逐出；尾声以未来时清点一切——儿子战死、父亲至死未宽恕却至死同床、女王的王位由每周的鸢尾花继承。

> "Either you or that kid is extraordinarily tough, because you are still with child, I’m happy to report.."（ch18）
>
> "Christ have mercy on that baby you’re carrying," Sophie said. "Now get out. You may own this neighborhood, but this is still my house.."（ch20）
>
> "every week without fail, through all their troubles and triumphs, they will come together to the cemetery and put a clutch of fresh irises on your grave, and when the great and terrible Lori is finally gone, Evangeline the Queen will come alone, with two bouquets.."（ch21）
