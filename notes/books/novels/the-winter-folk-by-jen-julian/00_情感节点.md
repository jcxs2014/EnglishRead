# 《The Winter Folk》情感节点

## 主要人物

- **Moth / Vera Stoker Whitfield**：叙述者，本名 Vera，Winter Folk 赐名 Moth。童年在 Appalachia 山区长大，母亲讲述 Winter Folk 故事 seeded 了她的 "want"。大学失败后回乡，在 Deerhaven 服务三个冬天，与 Mr. Oslin 有秘密恋情，最终被 expulsion。二十年后携家 return，与 remade Oslin 对决，救回女儿 Cass。
- **Mr. Oslin**：Deerhaven 的 Host and Master，Winter Folk， golden antlers，beautiful，powerful。对 Moth 有 fondness，后成为 lover。Homunculus（Mrs. Mandrel 揭示），由 Owner 用 millennia 塑造。最终被 Moth 用 Gilrad 刺伤，"He's awake"（Owner 醒来）。
- **Birch**：Moth 的 roommate，third-year veteran，tall, pretty, Black farmgirl。 initially rival, later friend。与 Crow 有 secret correspondence。Crow 死后 grief-stricken, then angry.
- **Crow / Ian Peretti**：shy errand boy, talented artist. Moth's friend. Stole key from Oslin's cabinet, opened Owner's Hall, burned by key's magic, died. Birch's lover.
- **Lo / Celosia**：Winter Folk, pink-faced, singsong accent, black bag. Moth's recruiter. Fierce, contemptuous of humans ("dirt people"). Maimed Miss Lys's child. Tribunal sentenced her to auction to Taskmasters of Ar; Oslin saved her temporarily, then she disappeared into bag.
- **Miss Lys / Jean Mélodie Lys**：pregnant Winter Folk guest, blood-leaking. Child was a monster (contained, not dead). Banished from Deerhaven perpetually.
- **Mrs. Mandrel**：veiled woman from tribunal, Owner's ally. Homunculus-maker. Spied via Agnes. Manipulated Crow into stealing key. Killed Crow. Wants to remake Oslin.
- **Eddy (Edina)**：Sifter, old woman at flea market. Sifter kin. Recruited Moth and Cass. Taught Cass runes through roleplaying game.
- **Martin**：Moth's husband, pharmacist, cheerful, wellness-obsessed.
- **Cass / Cassandra**：Moth's daughter, 19, gap year, teal hair, online friends, doodler. Recruited by Eddy via roleplaying game. Becomes heroine.
- **Moth's mother (Libby)**：storyteller, unstable, artist, schemes. Worked at Deerhaven before Moth (broke contract, stole key). Relationship with Dozer.
- **Moth's father**：salamander surveyor, retreated to remote cabin.

## 情感节点

1. **ch01 — Moth's "want" seeded.** Mother's Deerman story and Moth's encounter with Celosia seed her lifelong "want" for Winter Folk. "I'm cursed." Mother: "Promise me... if you ever see one of them Winter Folk, don't you even open your mouth." Moth: "But that was before."

2. **ch02 — Moth enters Deerhaven.** Lo recruits Moth from motel parking lot (lowest point). Mr. Oslin negotiates: "Five bedrooms. Moonflowers." Moth's desire becomes concrete. First entry through red door.

3. **ch03 — Power Triad forms.** Moth, Birch, Crow bond through shared suffering (Barkus's capture). Lo's witching hour speech: "your heroes, if they're lucky, grow old and mold and die." Mrs. Tataryan's "peace" wish.

4. **ch04 — Barkus's capture and Bag Room.** Birch, Moth, Crow bound by spellbound cord. Lo fights Barkus, becomes buzzard. Sifters refuse trade (contract-bound). Mr. Oslin arrives at dawn. Shared suffering creates bond.

5. **ch05 — Rumor of Host succession.** "The rumor was this: Lo didn't have the fundamental temperament of a Host." Mr. Oslin's "in the orchard" toast. Season end: Moth forgets she asked for house; Mr. Oslin reminds her. "I hope you enjoy the house."

6. **ch06 — Post-Deerhaven life.** Moth marries Martin, inherits house, moonflowers bloom year-round. Mother's relationship with Dozer; drug raid. Father: "Sell the damn house." Moth: "I fucking earned it, and I'm never selling it."

7. **ch07 — Miss Lys's birth.** Miss Lys (pregnant, blood-leaking) arrives. Lo threatens to kill baby. Moth promises to protect. Birth: child is monster (teeth, fingers, legs, curved spine). Oslin transforms into Deerman, contains child. Owner appears. Oslin wounded but survives. "CONTAINED. NOT DEAD."

8. **ch08 — Owl Tribunal.** Miss Lys banished. Lo sentenced to auction to Taskmasters of Ar. Moth's testimony ("She wasn't swayed. She would've hacked it up anyway.") seals Lo's fate. Oslin's "need assistant" request saves Lo temporarily. Lo disappears into bag. "Either way, moments later, she transformed in a sweep of black feathers..."

9. **ch09 — Present-day return.** Moth spots old house (missing moonflowers). Flea market: Cass confronts Moth about shame. Old Sifter woman: "Luna moth is a messenger from the other side." Red Boy knife. Starlight hole: "I think I might tumble into it, and that will be how I find my way back to the other side."

10. **ch10 — Mother's Deerhaven history.** Libby returns (drug raid). Revelation: Libby worked at Deerhaven before Moth was born, broke contract, stole key. Moth's third year: affair with Oslin in loft. "I love you, Mr. Oslin. Can you love?" Oslin: "You have the instincts of a Host."

11. **ch11 — Mrs. Mandrel and Crow's death.** Mrs. Mandrel reveals Oslin is homunculus. Crow steals key, opens Owner's Hall, burned by key's magic, dies. Moth's grief. Oslin: "In between. There are gaps." "I cannot retaliate against Mrs. Mandrel." Oslin burns Crow's contract.

12. **ch12 — Birch's secret and Mrs. Mandrel's threat.** Birch reveals secret correspondence with Crow (Ian Peretti). "That bitch needs to die." Mrs. Mandrel: "Consciousness is a cruel burden for such simple creatures." Dr. Moon vs. Mrs. Tataryan: control vs. acceptance. "If she's leaving, then why has she secured herself a second room?"

13. **ch13 — Exile and return.** Moth's hospital: "I didn't have a name, had never had one." Mother: "That worked, did it?" Key confrontation: "I need it... It's the only way I can save Birch." Present-day: Cass missing, Moth calls "Oslin!" in the woods. Eddy takes her to cave.

14. **ch14 — Final confrontation.** Moth enters Deerhaven. Remade Oslin (homunculus without self) holds Cass. Moth fights, stabs Oslin with Gilrad. Eddy's mark (ouroboros) activates. Moth rescues Cass. Owner awakens. Escape through cave. Eddy reveals: "Three generations of the same family whipped their hides." "Truths disguised as lies." Cass: "This is her story now, not mine."
