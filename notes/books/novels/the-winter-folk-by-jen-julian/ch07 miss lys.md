## 精读

> **原句 1:** Gone to see Elton. Be back in March.

"'Gone to see Elton. Be back in March.'（'去看 Elton 了。三月回。'）"

**关键词**：Gone to see Elton / Be back in March

**为什么这样写**：Moth's note to her mother uses her Deerhaven cover story (Elton John) as alibi; "Be back in March" mirrors her contract's three-month term. The note's brevity and deception show Moth's compartmentalization.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 note 读作 Moth's double life; "Elton" is her cover for Winter Folk service.

> **原句 2:** "She wants a woman to help her," he called to Mr. Oslin, who was overseeing his effort in the courtyard.

"'她想要一个女人来帮忙，'他向正在监督的 Mr. Oslin 喊道。"

**关键词**：wants a woman to help her

**为什么这样写**：Miss Lys's request for female help establishes the gendered nature of her crisis; "help" is ambiguous—medical, emotional, or witness. The carriage's sealed, leaking design (spiky iron tank) suggests Miss Lys is both prisoner and dangerous.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 "wants a woman" 读作 Miss Lys's vulnerability; her pregnancy is a crisis requiring female solidarity.

> **原句 3:** "I feel vulnerable here, Moth. I really do. I trust Mr. Oslin, but he's so run-down, so dusty. And you, the way you sprang up the side of that carriage—you were so kind, so brave. You didn't even ask questions. I need a friend like that. Someone to stay by my side and do what's right—when the time comes."

"'我在这里感到 vulnerable, Moth。真的。我 trust Mr. Oslin，但他 so run-down, so dusty。而你，你 spring up the side of that carriage 的 way——你 so kind, so brave。你 even didn't ask questions。我需要一个 friend like that。Someone to stay by my side and do what's right——when the time comes。'"

**关键词**：vulnerable / run-down, so dusty / kind, so brave / when the time comes

**为什么这样写**：Miss Lys's speech manipulates Moth through flattery ("kind, so brave") and vulnerability ("vulnerable"); "when the time comes" foreshadows the birth. "Run-down, so dusty" is Miss Lys's assessment of Oslin's declining power, revealing her awareness of Deerhaven's hierarchy.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 this speech 读作 Miss Lys's recruitment of Moth; "when the time comes" is the key phrase.

> **原句 4:** "You promise? Cross your heart and hope to die?"

"'你 promise? Cross your heart and hope to die?'（'你 promise 吗？cross your heart and hope to die？'）"

**关键词**：promise / cross your heart and hope to die

**为什么这样写**：Miss Lys's "cross your heart and hope to die" is a child's oath, contrasting with the deadly stakes; the oath's innocence makes Moth's promise more binding. Moth's nod seals her moral burden.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 the oath 读作 Moth's binding commitment; "hope to die" foreshadows the night's danger.

> **原句 5:** "I have always been struck by your remarkable sensitivity, Moth, which I would never have expected. You have the instincts of a Host."

"'我 always been struck by your remarkable sensitivity, Moth, which I would never have expected。You have the instincts of a Host。'"

**关键词**：remarkable sensitivity / instincts of a Host

**为什么这样写**：Oslin's "instincts of a Host" echoes the rumor (ch05) and confirms Moth's potential; "which I would never have expected" shows Oslin's own surprise, validating Moth's growth. This is the chapter's thematic climax—Moth's Host potential is officially recognized.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 "instincts of a Host" 记作 Moth's central identity; it's the reason she was recruited and the reason she returns.

> **原句 6:** YOU'VE BOTH DONE WELL, it said.

"'YOU'VE BOTH DONE WELL,' 它说。"

**关键词**：You've both done well

**为什么这样写**：The Deerman/Oslin's capitalized speech marks his non-human voice; "both" refers to Moth and Crow, acknowledging their bravery. The capitalization visually distinguishes Winter Folk speech from human dialogue.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 capitalized speech 读作 Winter Folk communication; "both" confirms Oslin sees Moth and Crow as a pair.

> **原句 7:** CONTAINED. NOT DEAD.

"'CONTAINED. NOT DEAD.'（'CONTAINED。NOT DEAD。'）"

**关键词**：Contained / not dead

**为什么这样写**：The Deerman's two-word answer to Moth's question ("Is it dead?") is ominous; "contained" implies the child is imprisoned, not destroyed, leaving future threat. "Not dead" confirms the child survives, setting up future conflict.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 "contained, not dead" 记作 a loose end; the child will return.

> **原句 8:** "You fucking fool. You oily little striver, do you have any idea what you've done?"

"'You fucking fool. You oily little striver, do you have any idea what you've done?'（'你 fucking fool。你 oily little striver，你有 idea 你've done 什么吗？'）"

**关键词**：oily little striver / what you've done

**为什么这样写**：Lo's "oily little striver" is her contempt for Moth's ambition; "striver" echoes the rumor (Moth as potential protégé). Lo's fury at Moth throwing the knife into the Bag Room reveals the knife's importance and Lo's protective role.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 "oily little striver" 读作 Lo's assessment of Moth's ambition; the knife's loss is a significant plot turn.

> **原句 9:** "Good evening, Oslin," he rasped. "Celosia signaled to me. Apparently, there has been some trouble tonight."

"'Good evening, Oslin,' 他 rasped。'Celosia signaled to me。Apparently, there has been some trouble tonight。'"

**关键词**：Good evening, Oslin / Celosia signaled / some trouble

**为什么这样写**：The Owner's "Good evening" is chillingly polite; his acknowledgment that Celosia "signaled" him reveals Lo's direct line to the Owner. "Some trouble tonight" understates the chaos, showing the Owner's detachment.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 the Owner 读作 the ultimate authority; his "trouble" euphemism shows his power to minimize.

> **原句 10:** "How many days do you think you'll be out of commission from this, Oslin? Can Celosia step up, or will we need reinforcements?"

"'How many days do you think you'll be out of commission from this, Oslin? Can Celosia step up, or will we need reinforcements?'（'你 think 你'll be out of commission from this 多少 days，Oslin？Can Celosia step up，or will we need reinforcements？'）"

**关键词**：out of commission / Celosia step up / reinforcements

**为什么这样写**：The Owner's practical questions reveal Deerhaven's operational reality; "Celosia step up" suggests Lo is the designated successor (despite her temperament); "reinforcements" implies a larger Winter Folk workforce. This is the Owner's management style—practical, unsentimental.

**读者视角提示**：读者应把 "reinforcements" 记作 a hint of the wider Winter Folk world; the Owner manages Deerhaven as a business.
