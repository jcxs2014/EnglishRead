# 《The Winter Folk》概述

## 全书梗概

**回到山里找鬼。** 叙述者 Moth（本名 Vera Stoker Whitfield）携丈夫 Martin 与女儿 Cass 回到 childhood mountains 休假，却暗中搜寻二十年前消失的 Winter Folk。本章穿插她童年与 Celosia 的初遇、母亲的 Deerman 故事、以及她与 Mr. Oslin 在 Deerhaven 的往事。

> "I've come back to the mountains to look for ghosts."（ch01）

> "Moth is the name I received while serving the Winter Folk, the only name in the world that has ever felt like it belonged to me."（ch01）

**名字与归属。** Moth 的名字链（Vera → Stoker → Whitfield → Moth）把身份拆解为 layers：家族强加的、父系继承的、婚姻契约的、Winter Folk 赐予的。"received while serving" 把 Winter Folk 的命名写成 quasi-religious 召唤。

> "Moth is the name I received while serving the Winter Folk, the only name in the world that has ever felt like it belonged to me, much more so than Vera... or Stoker... or Whitfield."（ch01）

**Deerman 的禁忌。** 母亲的 Deerman 故事以直接引语呈现，保留民间传说的"禁忌清单"：don't look / don't smile / don't speak。"catch it and keep it" 把语言写成可被掠夺的物质。

> "He can make himself like any other deer—till you get close. If you see a deer that won't run when he hears you, don't look in his eyes, and don't smile, and don't dare say a word."（ch01）

**Celosia 的初遇。** 十岁 Moth 在后院与 Celosia（Lo）相遇，被测试抓鬼；Lo 吸走她手上的血，留下"谁会信你"的沉默。这是 Moth 被 Winter Folk 选中的起源场景。

> "Did you see a ghost come through here?" she asked.（ch01）

> "I'd say not to tell anyone," Lo whispered. "But who would believe you?"（ch01）

**大学失败与 motel 打工。** Moth 的 gifted child 阅读史（Brontës、Jane Eyre）与大学崩溃；回乡后在 Lucky 9 汽车旅馆打工，遭老板 Bullion 骚扰。Lo 重返，带她穿过红门进入 Deerhaven。

> "College was no escape. Just another dead end. Another place to not belong."（ch02）

> "Acting in the interest of Mr. Oslin, Host and Master of the House," she announced with a bow.（ch02）

**Deerhaven 的第一季。** Mr. Oslin 以"五卧室、月光花露台的石屋"换取三个月服务。Moth 结识 Birch 和 Crow，经历 strange guests（Mrs. Tataryan、Old Goatface 等）的 strange arrivals，Lo 的 witching hour decay ritual。

> "A lodge," he said, gesturing vaguely. "Resting place for the timeworn and weary."（ch02）

> "Five bedrooms. Moonflowers. Complete your three months and the house is yours."（ch02）

**Barkus 的威胁。** Barkus 用 spellbound cord 捆绑 Birch、Moth、Crow，强闯 Bag Room 寻找 knife；Lo 变 buzzard 飞走；Sifters 因 contract 拒绝 trade；Mr. Oslin 在 dawn reclaim 三人。shared suffering 让三人 bond。

> "Barkus connected Birch and me on a single cord like a mule train."（ch04）

> "They are bound by a contract," said the old woman. "As trade items, unviable."（ch04）

**Miss Lys 的 arrival 与 birth。** Miss Lys（pregnant, blood-leaking Winter Folk guest）arrival；Lo 威胁杀婴；Moth 承诺保护；Oslin 变 Deerman contain 了 child；Owner 出现；Oslin wounded but survives。Moth 的 "instincts of a Host" 被 Oslin 确认。

> "I feel vulnerable here, Moth. I really do... You promise? Cross your heart and hope to die?"（ch07）

> "I have always been struck by your remarkable sensitivity, Moth... You have the instincts of a Host."（ch07）

**Owl Tribunal。** child 被 confined in Owner's Hall; Miss Lys 被 banished 永久；Lo 被 sentenced to auction to Taskmasters of Ar; Moth's testimony 导致 Lo's conviction; Oslin 通过 "need assistant" request 暂时留 Lo，最终 Lo 被装入 bag 消失。

> "Miss Celosia... sentenced to be immediately stripped of her position, removed from Deerhaven, and auctioned to the Taskmasters of Ar."（ch08）

> "Either way, moments later, she transformed in a sweep of black feathers, and Mr. Oslin held open the bag, and she disappeared inside it, never to be seen again."（ch08）

**Mrs. Mandrel 与 Crow 的死亡。** Mrs. Mandrel 揭示 Oslin 是 homunculus; Crow 偷 key、被 key 的 magic 烧死; Moth's grief; Oslin 无法 retaliate; "in between. There are gaps."

> "He is not a person. Only a series of variations, which the Owner has meticulously shaped over millennia."（ch11）

> "In between. There are gaps."（ch11）

**Exile 与 return。** Moth's expulsion: hospital (nameless); mother's visit; key confrontation; post-Deerhaven life; present-day Cass's disappearance; Moth calling "Oslin" in the woods; Eddy (Sifter) 带她重返 Deerhaven.

> "I didn't have a name, had never had one."（ch13）

> "Oslin!"（ch13）

**最终对决。** Moth 重返 Deerhaven; remade Oslin (homunculus without self) holds Cass; Moth fights, stabs Oslin with Gilrad; Eddy's mark (ouroboros) activates; Moth rescues Cass; Owner awakens; escape through cave; Eddy reveals Sifters' network and Cass's "recruitment."

> "Once upon a time, your mother loved a monster. This is not a metaphor. This is a fact."（ch14）

> "This is her story now, not mine."（ch14）
