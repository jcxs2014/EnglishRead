# Chapter Twenty-Nine

- **情感弧线位置**：**下降（rejection）**——Leigh 从"vulnerability"进入"rejection"（Hazel got fellowship）；Erica Go 在 bathroom 给 Leigh advice（"I don’t know why he rejected you at Middlebury"）；Leigh 写 poetry for herself（"love and yes and will"）；Leigh 收到 Will's letter（"I’ve always wanted to"）。

- **人物弧线**：Leigh 从"vulnerability"进入"rejection"（"I haven’t allowed myself to mourn at all"）；Erica Go 从"stranger"进入"mentor"（"You’re a poet, Leigh, don’t worry"）。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称（Leigh 视角）顺叙，大量内心独白与 therapy session，以 fellowship announcement 与 bathroom conversation 作为章节的 two emotional peaks。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "'This session is over.' I stand up. 'I’m no longer taking appointments with you. You can find another consultant if you want. And please don’t touch me ever again.'"

- **中文理解**："This session is over." I stand up（"This session is over." I stand up）。"I’m no longer taking appointments with you."（"I’m no longer taking appointments with you."）"You can find another consultant if you want."（"You can find another consultant if you want."）"And please don’t touch me ever again."（"And please don’t touch me ever again."）
- **关键词**：This session is over（This session is over）／I’m no longer taking appointments with you（I’m no longer taking appointments with you）／please don’t touch me ever again（please don’t touch me ever again）
- **为什么这样写**：用"This session is over"把 Leigh’s "assertion"具象化， she says "This session is over"；"I’m no longer taking appointments with you"把 her "refusal"具象化， she says "I’m no longer taking appointments with you"；"please don’t touch me ever again"把 her "boundary"具象化， she says "please don’t touch me ever again"； this is Leigh’s "assertion"， she asserts her boundary with Lucas。
- **读者视角提示**：读者应记住"This session is over"—— this is Leigh’s "assertion"， she asserts her boundary with Lucas； this is the chapter’s first emotional peak。
