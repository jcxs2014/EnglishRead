# Chapter Twenty-Six

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Erica Go leads workshop (Paul out of town)；Leigh 提交 "Aubade for a Stranger"（minimalist poem）；Erica critiques it（"crosses the line into actual fiction"）；Will writes "This isn’t you, and you know it" on Leigh's poem；Leigh confronts Will about plagiarism（"I know you plagiarized 'Invisible Summer'"）；Will 说"This isn’t going to work, is it?"；Leigh 与 Will breakup。
- **情感弧线位置**：**下降（breakup）**——Leigh 从"vulnerability"进入"breakup"，Will 说"This isn’t going to work, is it?"是 their second breakup。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现「**enemies-to-lovers 的 breakup**」——Leigh 与 Will 在 classroom 交锋（"This isn’t you, and you know it" → "This isn’t going to work, is it?"）；同时以「**plagiarism as betrayal**」把 Will's "secret" 推到台前。
- **人物弧线**：Leigh 从"vulnerability"进入"breakup"（"This isn’t going to work, is it?"）；Will 从"vulnerable"进入"cold"（"This isn’t going to work, is it?"）。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称（Leigh 视角）顺叙，大量内心独白与 poetry analysis，以 plagiarism confrontation 作为章节的 emotional peak。
