## 节点一：Prologue — "All style, no substance"（ch01）

> "All style, no substance," I hear once again in his low, sure voice.

**触发事件**：Leigh 在 Mrs. Lincoln 的 creative writing workshop 上读 poem "Introduction to Feminist Blogging"，Will 当众批评为"all style, no substance"，Leigh 的 crush 瞬间崩塌。

**情感弧线位置**：**开端（创伤）**——全书从十年前的羞辱性一刻切入，埋下 Leigh 对 Will 的长期敌意与自我怀疑。

**人物状态**：Leigh 从" cater to 他人"的顺从者，被一句话钉死在"只懂表面"的位置，开始对文学权威产生反抗与自卑并存的复杂情绪。

**叙事手法**：第一人称（Leigh 视角）倒叙式开场，先抛"十年后"的羞辱性一刻，再回填人物关系，制造追读欲望。

**关键引语**：
- "WHEN WILL LANGFORD DRAGS HIS pen over each line of my poem, slowly, as if to not miss a single word, I feel the movement scraped over my legs."（Prologue 开篇）
- "I don’t plan on reading them. I don’t want his feedback. Any semblance of a crush I had feels irreconcilably snapped. Evaporated. No evidence it was ever there at all."（Prologue 末尾）

## 节点二：Ch 02 — "William Langford of Cleveland"（ch02）

> "Friends, I am pleased to introduce our final poet—William Langford of Cleveland!"

**触发事件**：Leigh 在迎新 barbecue 上与 Will 重逢，Will 以"William Langford of Cleveland"的正式介绍出场，Leigh 的 stomach drops low, then lower。

**叙事手法**：第一人称（Leigh 视角）顺叙，大量内心独白与 therapy-style 自我分析，以 Will 的出现作为章节收尾钩子。

**关键引语**：

- "‘Oh! Hi!’ he says, and I cannot begin to imagine the flush my face is betraying me with. / 'Will?' I hear my voice, pitched higher with the cadence of a question. But it’s not a question. Not really."（Ch 02）
