# Chapter Twenty-Two

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Leigh 与 Will 回到 Perrin，Will 做饭（pasta），Leigh 与 Will 在 Will's apartment 发生性接触（oral sex + intercourse）；Will 说"All of them are about you"（his poems are about Leigh）。
- **情感弧线位置**：**上升（intimacy）**——Leigh 与 Will 在 Will's apartment 发生性接触，这是 their first full sexual encounter in Perrin。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现「**enemies-to-lovers 的 intimacy**」——Will's apartment 里 sexual contact；同时以「**poetry as intimacy**」把 Will's poems 推到台前（"All of them are about you"）。
- **人物弧线**：Leigh 从"coldness"进入"intimacy"（"I want him to tell me that my work is also good"）；Will 从"cold"进入"vulnerable"（"All of them are about you"）。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称（Leigh 视角）顺叙，大量内心独白与身体反应描写，以 Will's apartment sexual contact 作为章节的 emotional peak。
