# Chapter Twenty-Five

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Leigh 的 First-Year Reading Series（Leigh runs out of the bookstore due to panic）；parents reveal divorce finalized；Leigh 与 Will 在 Leigh's apartment 交锋（"I already know you’d break my heart" → "We need a break"）；Leigh 与 Will breakup。
- **情感弧线位置**：**下降（breakup）**——Leigh 从"vulnerability"进入"breakup"，Will 的 "We need a break" 是 their first breakup。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现「**enemies-to-lovers 的 breakup**」——Leigh 与 Will 在 Leigh's apartment 交锋（"I already know you’d break my heart" → "We need a break"）；同时以「**First-Year Reading Series as crisis**」把 Leigh's panic 推到台前。
- **人物弧线**：Leigh 从"vulnerability"进入"breakup"（"I already know you’d break my heart"）；Will 从"vulnerable"进入"cold"（"We need a break"）。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称（Leigh 视角）顺叙，大量内心独白与身体反应描写，以 First-Year Reading Series panic 与 breakup 作为章节的 two emotional peaks。
