# Chapter Nine

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Leigh 与 Will 开始通过 poem annotations 交流；Will 给 Leigh 他的电话号码；Leigh 缺 pumpkin，Will 带她去 Willow Organic Farms（corn maze、pumpkin patch）；两人在 farm 上 conversation，Will 透露 antidepressants（Zoloft）与父亲 backstory；near-kiss 被 toddler 打断。
- **情感弧线位置**：**上升（intimacy）**——Leigh 与 Will 的 relationship 从"hostility"进入"friendship"，farm outing 是 their first one-on-one date-like experience；near-kiss 是 their first romantic moment。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现「**slow burn / friends-to-lovers**」——poem annotations → phone number → grocery errand → corn maze → farm conversation；同时以「**antidepressants as vulnerability**」把 Will 的 mental health backstory 推到台前。
- **人物弧线**：Leigh 从"do not engage"进入"friendship"（"I don't use the number until a week later, and only when I have no choice"）；Will 从"aloof"进入"vulnerable"（"I don't like who I was off them"）。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称（Leigh 视角）顺叙，大量内心独白与身体反应描写，以 farm outing 与 near-kiss 作为章节的 two emotional peaks。
