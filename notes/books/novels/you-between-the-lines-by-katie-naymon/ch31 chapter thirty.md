# Chapter Thirty

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Leigh 写 vulnerable poem "All the Leighs I’ve Been—and One I Could Be"；workshop 中 Will 写 "This isn’t you, and you know it" on Leigh's poem；Leigh 给 Will marginal note（"I am irrevocably in love with you"）；Will 来 Leigh's apartment，confess love（"I can hardly remember a time I wasn’t in love with you"）；Leigh 与 Will sex；Leigh 与 Will 在 MFA Prom；Leigh 与 Bridget therapy session（"You’re a poet, Leigh, don’t worry"）。
- **情感弧线位置**：**上升（reconciliation）**——Leigh 从"breakup"进入"reconciliation"，Will confess love（"I can hardly remember a time I wasn’t in love with you"）；Leigh 与 Will sex；Leigh 与 Bridget therapy session。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：兑现「**enemies-to-lovers 的 reconciliation**」——Leigh 与 Will 在 Leigh's apartment 交锋（"I am irrevocably in love with you" → "I can hardly remember a time I wasn’t in love with you"）；同时以「**vulnerable poem as confession**」把 Leigh's vulnerability 推到台前。
- **人物弧线**：Leigh 从"breakup"进入"reconciliation"（"I am irrevocably in love with you"）；Will 从"cold"进入"vulnerable"（"I can hardly remember a time I wasn’t in love with you"）。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称（Leigh 视角）顺叙，大量内心独白与 poetry analysis，以 vulnerable poem 与 Will's confession 作为章节的 two emotional peaks。
