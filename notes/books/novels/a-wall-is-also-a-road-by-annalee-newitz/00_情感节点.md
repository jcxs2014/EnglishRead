# 《A Wall Is Also a Road》情感节点

## 节点一：入学日，第一千次检查（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 银河学府 Memory 的开学典礼上，伪装成 Rational People 的 Gardenpath 一边用隐藏的触手偷看同学，一边第一千次确认膜上那簇亮绿受体仍被堵着不可见； Plumline 的致辞把典礼拆穿成考试的第一格，而 Gardenpath 的研究方向（一颗会呼吸的行星）在当晚的宿舍集群里换来室友 Wainscot 的第一记嘲讽——希望与隐忧在第一章同时挂牌。

**关键引语：**

> ① "It was yet another reminder that Gardenpath’s place at the Memory was contingent on camouflage. For the thousandth time, they checked to make sure the tiny constellation of bright-green receptors in their membrane was blocked and invisible. They had to look Rational, or else."（ch01）

> ② "“I have a hunch that something might be growing on this planet.” They weren’t about to say anything about their data that showed faint signatures of molecular respiration in the planet’s atmosphere. Somebody was breathing down there, and they wanted to know who."（ch01）

> ③ "Right before full dissolution, they tasted what was certainly Wainscot’s mood, now a diminishing bubble of attenuated chemicals. Entitled. Disdainful. And something else—sharp, calcified grains of insecurity."（ch01）

## 节点二：改变一生的一眼（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 二访行星的 Gardenpath 跟着举火的 Unnamed 走进一座横平竖直的城，最后在柜台后撞见一个 Unnamed 正蘸着矿物粉末在湿墙上作画——「这将改变一生」。语言不通、来历不明，但那个「不许墙荒着」的生物，从此成了整本书的引力中心。

**关键引语：**

> ① "And that’s when they saw something they knew would change their life. An Unnamed, draped in a red-edged white textile, was using their arms to create marks on a section of damp wall behind the counter. … The creature dipped a small tool into a pot full of a mineral powder with a bright-red hue, then smeared it against the wall."（ch05）

> ② "When Gardenpath sent out more eyes, a shock dimpled their membrane. On their left loomed a structure that was unmistakably a wall, built from rocks cut into rectangular prisms. Primitive and stunted, it was nothing like the soaring, undulating walls of the Memory."（ch05）

## 节点三：庞贝与 peristyle（ch10）

**叙事概括：** 词汇课从身体动作教到文化抽象，追问「你怎么被找到的」把 Gardenpath 的降落翻译成受害者的故事；当「花长在墙里的路」被 Murtis 认成 peristyle，这个名字成了她在庞贝的一生代号——而城市名 Pompeii 的揭晓，让「外星田野」变成「古罗马的街道」。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“My city has … flowers growing in walls. My name means … road between flowers? Road in walls?” Truly, it was hard to translate the idea, especially in a city with such barren architecture. But Murtis’s mouth was elongating, eyes wide with recognition."（ch10）

> ② "Peristyle nodded. This would be the name they used in the city of Pompeii, on the planet Tentacle, in the third arm of the Spigot galaxy, where they were starting to think of a multicellular animal as a friend."（ch10）

## 节点四：我以为你死了（ch13）

**叙事概括：** 地壳扰动后的庞贝半毁，Murtis 的楼塌了——重逢以「你他妈跑哪儿去了」开场，以「我以为你死了」的拥抱收束；几个旅程对一年多，主角的时间观被当场修正，而 Murtis 的 Mactar 身世（被买、被改名、获释）把两段流亡第一次摆上同一张桌子。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Where the absolute fuck have you been? You said you were going away for a little while, but it’s been more than a year!"（ch13）

> ② "I thought you were dead." And then they were hugging Peristyle, face wet and body shivering slightly. "Don’t ever do that again."（ch13）

> ③ "I’m a liberta now, but my old master bought me when I was a little girl. He’s the one who gave me this Greek name—I don’t remember my Punic one."（ch13）

## 节点五：泥球与十五年（ch17）

**叙事概括：** 一团湿泥交出真身，一次触碰验证黏合剂——而 Murtis 的病揭开「幸运符」的真相、她的暴怒揭开「狗」的日常：Gardenpath 第一次看见自己看不见的东西；争吵的终点不是道歉，是两个说谎者互相看见了对方的谎言学。

**关键引语：**

> ① "You keep saying you’ve told me everything about who you are, and then I find out that you’re actually shaped like a handful of mud and can grow tentacles out of your cunt. I’m starting to feel like this is all a dream. Like I’m going crazy."（ch17）

> ② "Bitch, I was a slave for fifteen years, sucking my master’s cock for bread. Haven’t you noticed what these asshole Romans think of the women who work in their lupanar? They call us dogs."（ch17）

## 节点六：孢子、堆肥船与怪物（ch21）

**叙事概括：** 全书的暗室在此开启：孢子被吸走、虫洞提速、落进对跖——被叫 Wanderer、种错光感受器、硬化濒死、宁愿没被找到；「理性也许只是幻觉，也许我是怪物」的濒死一念，让此后所有的伪装都有了病历。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Gardenpath had been swept into the maw of a passing compost ship, and their first memories were of the warm stench of rotting organic matter, rich and varied in flavor."（ch21）

> ② "Finally they became sclerotic, body hardening into stone, their consciousness stilled. As the last vestiges of awareness drained from them, Gardenpath wondered if rationality was merely a hallucination. Perhaps they were a monster, doomed to die alone."（ch21）

> ③ "For a long time, Gardenpath wished they had never been found."（ch21）

## 节点七：像被谋杀（ch27）

**叙事概括：** Plumline 的单独操练把 Molecular 备课变成化学战争，Wainscot 的崩溃自白（「疼得像被谋杀」）撕开长期虐待——举报无门，两个宿敌在共同的创伤里合谋「假竞争」；book 里最敌对的关系，在这一章完成最结实的翻转。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“They’ve been making me practice for a long time. Until you came along. They always … they always say I need to work harder. But it hurts so much. It feels like being murdered.”"（ch27）

> ② "“What if we told Plumline that we want to study for the Third together? I bet they won’t pull this pain chemical shit if we’re both there.” … “We need Plumline to think this is a show for them. They would love that.”"（ch27）

## 节点八：山会吃掉城（ch29–30）

**叙事概括：** 散点图三算不改判——火山模型的判决（山会吃掉城）把 Gardenpath 推进卡珊德拉困境：说，没人信；不说，挚友会死。浴池坦白换来真身之夜与 Neapolis 计划，而第五考考官席上那句「我们用得上」把她的遭遇标了价——知识与武器在同一条流水线上只隔一道工序。

**关键引语：**

> ① "This was the temporal arc of her food web, and her hypothesis: The mountain would eat the city."（ch29）

> ② "“What good luck that the volcano is about to erupt.” Keystone was exploring their most recent measurements."（ch30）

## 节点九：灰烬与生还者（ch36）

**叙事概括：** 坐标报错即讣告——山顶炸飞、庞贝的墙沉入灰烬之海；「我真希望自己没说对」是全书唯一一次嫌弃正确，而 Milk Road 的救济厨房里，两天没睡的 Murtis 与跛脚生还的 Julia 相拥——灾难的废墟边，爱的形状比原来更多了。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“It happened just as you said! Vesuvius exploded like Aetna, only much worse.”"（ch36）

> ② "“I’m so sorry. I wish I hadn’t been right.”"（ch36）

## 节点十：第一集群，曾是家（ch44–47）

**叙事概括：** 答辩上的公开出柜（我欠一条光合者一条命）把三个秘密清零；第五考没有考试，只有一张「离开 Memory 建自己的社区」的提案——文化与科学平权的新家落成，而全书最后一页翻向地图背面：那个不是第一集群的第一集群，不 Rational，但曾是家。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I owe my life to a photosynthesizer.” Gardenpath’s words hung in the air as they revealed the photoreceptors in their membrane, green against sky-black."（ch44）

> ② "“What if we … left the Memory? All of us?” They elongated so quickly that one of their photoreceptors blinked open. “We could start our own community!”"（ch45）

> ③ "They did have a first cluster, out there. It wasn’t Rational, but for a while, it had been home."（ch47）
