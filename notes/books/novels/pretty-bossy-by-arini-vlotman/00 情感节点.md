# 情感节点

> **情感节点**收录全书 10 个最关键的情感转折点，每节点含叙事概括 + 2-3 句关键引语。按全书叙事顺序排列。

---

### 节点一：The Road to Hell（ch01）

**叙事概括：** Zarna 在公司年度战略规划周的第一天，一边忍受枯燥会议，一边盘算着自己的 Craft Gin 创业困境。

**关键引语：**
- "The road to hell is paved with boardroom tables."
- "Money made the world go round, put food on the table, and paid for her rather large crafting collection."
- "After getting fired from her previous job in spectacular fashion, Zarna had decided her end game was to be her own boss."

---

### 节点二：Nickelback（ch02）

**叙事概括：** Mayan 因 Zarna 音乐声过大闯入她的房间，意外撞见她被头发困住的尴尬场面，两人第一次以"剪掉她一撮头发"的方式完成初次见面。

**关键引语：**
- "I am calm, I am peace, I am serene. I'm going to be jailed for assault."
- "He tried his best to keep a straight face, but she must have heard the laughter bubbling in her voice."

---

### 节点三：Trapped（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 暴风雨困住两人的物理环境，催化了情感和利益的双重结盟。他们从"互相嫌弃的陌生人"变成"假婚约的同盟者"。

**关键引语：**
- "In such a short time, their fake engagement was becoming more real than he anticipated."
- "If that's a proposal, my answer is yes," she said, husky and soft before a smile lit her face.

---

### 节点四：Meeting the Parents（ch09）

**叙事概括：** Mayan 拜访 Zarna 的家人，以完美男友姿态赢得她父母的好感；Zarna 与姐姐 Riya 冲突后向 Mayan 敞开心扉，两人分享各自的创伤往事。

**关键引语：**
- "She needed him, and for the first time in years, he wanted to be needed."
- "Talking to Mayan, offloading rather, was therapeutic. His solid presence beside her wasn't invasive or judgmental."

---

### 节点五：Partners（ch14）

**叙事概括：** 停电之夜的脆弱让 Zarna 和 Mayan 的关系从"假婚约"升级为"真实伴侣"。入侵者扔砖打破窗户，Mayan 保护 Zarna 并坦诚 Noa 的威胁。

**关键引语：**
- "You don't get to send me packing like a troublesome child. I decide what happens to me, and the last time I checked, this was a partnership."
- "I know you, Zar. I know you're passionate about art, and you defend those you love."

---

### 节点六：The Dinner（ch15）

**叙事概括：** Noa Snr 的现身和录音曝光让 Zarna 从"假婚约"的幻想中彻底惊醒。她以为自己在 Mayan 心中是"特别的"，但录音证明她只是"bland, unassuming, and a little naïve"。

**关键引语：**
- "Zarna is bland, unassuming, and a little naïve, but that works in my favour."
- "You're not bland at all. I don't even know why I said that."

---

### 节点七：Aftermath（ch16）

**叙事概括：** 晚宴后的深夜，Zarna 和 Mayan 在浴室里坦诚相对——两人分享各自的创伤，Mayan 承认"bland"的评论是谎言，两人从"假婚约"升级为"真正的伴侣"。

**关键引语：**
- "I pee in the shower!"
- "I wanted to be a better man for you."

---

### 节点八：The Extraction Plan（ch20）

**叙事概括：** Damin 发送的视频揭露了"假婚约"的"提取计划"（六個月后分手），让 Zarna 意识到自己在 Mayan 眼中只是"棋子"。Mayan 虽然承认了真相，但他已经失去了 Zarna 的信任。

**关键引语：**
- "I'm not a pawn in your game, Mayan. All this time you've been playing me."
- "I'd completely forgotten about that damn plan."

---

### 节点九：The Sun Rises（ch21）

**叙事概括：** Mayan 用热气球惊喜和日出表白打动了 Zarna，两人在热气球上完成了最终的表白和和解。Zarna 坦白自己爱上了 Mayan，Mayan 也坦诚了自己的过去。

**关键引语：**
- "I love you, in whatever way you'll have me."
- "I love you more than you love me."

---

### 节点十：Epilogue（ch22）

**叙事概括：** Zarna 的 Gin 品牌发布会标志着她从"假婚约的工具人"变成了"真正的自己"。通过 Ellie 和 Mayan 的互动、Kabelo 的幽默、Esha 的"想跳舞"，作者展现了"found family"的温馨氛围。

**关键引语：**
- "You're pretty bossy when the mood strikes. I like it."
- "He's so romantic."
