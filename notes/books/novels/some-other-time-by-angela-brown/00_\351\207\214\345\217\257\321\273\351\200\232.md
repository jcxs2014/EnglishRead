# Some Other Time — 金句精选

> 本书核心金句，揭示命运、选择、婚姻与接受的主题。

## 命运与选择

### 1. "It was because of a crash."

> *It was because of a crash.* Looking back, her rose-colored view of the event long gone, that seemed to her like a more accurate way to describe it.

**出处**：ch01 Prologue

**解读**：Ellie和Jonah相遇的方式被大家称为"可爱的小追尾"（a cute little love tap），但Ellie知道真相：这是一个事故（a crash）。"Crash"和"tap"的区别是全书的核心：爱情是命运的安排还是纯粹的巧合？Ellie的答案是两者兼有。

---

### 2. "The choice is, quite literally, yours!"

> She'd read once, in a book displayed near the register during an exceptionally quiet shift, that the average adult makes something like thirty-five thousand choices in a day. Ellie had wondered how researchers had come up with that number... As she set the book down to ring up a lone customer, she couldn't decide if that stat did more to support the idea of free will *(The choice is, quite literally, yours!)* or fate *(With all those minuscule decisions, what are the chances of, well, anything?)*.

**出处**：ch01 Prologue

**解读**：Ellie在一家书店工作（这本身就是一个隐喻）。她每天都在思考"选择"和"命运"——她是书呆子，她用书本的视角来看待人生。全书的第一句话就揭示了核心问题：我们的人生是由"选择"还是"命运"决定的？

---

### 3. "An accident."

> Which was why, in those final weeks they lived together, Ellie—a lover of words and stories and happy endings, a woman who once upon a time had believed in foolish things like fate—finally mustered up the courage and began to describe their chance encounter... as something else, the thing that in all reality it had always been, even though neither of them had initially wanted to believe it. An accident.

**出处**：ch01 Prologue

**解读**：全书的第一句话和最后一句话是同一个词："An accident."这是Angela Brown的"首尾呼应"写法——Ellie用二十年的时间，终于接受了"相遇是一个事故"这个事实。

---

### 4. "some stories are best left untold"

> She considers asking him more about that memory, and others, but decides some stories are best left untold. Instead, Ellie keeps her gaze straight ahead. She wants to see what destiny awaits them. This time, whatever it is, she knows that she's ready for it.

**出处**：ch38 ch37

**解读**：全书结束前最重要的一句话。"有些故事最好不讲述"——这是Ellie的"接受"：她不需要知道"另一个世界"的一切，有些事情不需要解释。真正的智慧是知道什么时候该放下。

---

## 婚姻与家庭

### 5. "This is my home."

> Jonah leans back, closing his eyes, but he keeps holding on to her hand. "No," he corrects. "This is my home." He opens his eyes and smiles at her. "I've always felt that way."

**出处**：ch30 Thirty

**解读**：Jonah和Ellie结婚二十年。"This is my home"是Jonah对Ellie的爱的最直接表达——不是因为某个地方，而是因为Ellie在的地方就是家。

---

### 6. "I don't care if it doesn't make sense."

> She doesn't want to believe it, but she knows, deep down, that this is the realest feeling she has ever experienced. It doesn't make sense—it can't—but she's felt it for years, and she's never once tried to justify it. I don't care if it doesn't make sense. It just is.

**出处**：ch33 Thirty-Two

**解读**：这是Ellie在面对"另一个世界"时说的话。真正的爱不需要"讲道理"——它就是存在。"I don't care if it doesn't make sense"是Angela Brown对"命运"主题的直接表达。

---

### 7. "We made it."

> Maggie nods and reaches over to wipe her own tears. "We made it," she declares, as if these three words are a brand-new, original thought. But she says them with such relief, such gratitude, that Ellie feels the exact same way.

**出处**：ch36 Thirty-Five

**解读**：这是Maggie在Florida的"家庭团聚"后说的话。"We made it"——我们做到了。这是全家人在Florida的最后一刻：Ellie、Jonah、Maggie和Bunny、Frank在一起。没有"另一个世界"的诱惑，只有"真实生活"里的温暖。

---

## 告别与接受

### 8. "I'd still do it all again, too, Jo."

> "I'd still do it all again, too, Jo," she whispers, their hands laced together. "Even now. Even knowing how it all ends."

**出处**：ch31 Thirty

**解读**：Jonah在医院的最后一刻，Ellie说出了她从未说出的话："我还是会重新做一遍。即使知道结局是什么。"这是全书的"爱情宣言"——真正的爱是无悔的。

---

### 9. "I'll see you soon, Jo."

> "I'll see you soon, Jo," Ellie says as a form of goodbye. "I don't know where or when. But I know I will."

**出处**：ch31 Thirty

**解读**：这是Ellie和Jonah的告别。"我不知道在哪里或什么时候，但我知道我会再见到你"——这是Ellie的"信念"：即使在"另一个世界"里，她也会和Jonah在一起。这是Angela Brown的"命运"主题：真正的爱不受"生死"的限制。

---

### 10. "This time, whatever it is, she knows that she's ready for it."

> She wants to see what destiny awaits them. This time, whatever it is, she knows that she's ready for it.

**出处**：ch38 ch37

**解读**：全书结束语。"这一次，无论是什么，她知道她已经准备好了"——这是Ellie在"告别"后的话：她接受了Jonah的离去，她接受了"真实生活"，她准备好了迎接任何命运。真正的爱是"准备好了"——准备好了接受一切，包括失去。

---

## Jack的隐喻

### 11. "He wasn't the one."

> For the record, Ellie hadn't even really liked Jack that much. Sure, he was handsome, and he was nice, and he was easy on the eyes, but he wasn't the one.

**出处**：ch01 Prologue

**解读**：这是Ellie在回忆她年轻时遇到Jack时的想法。"He wasn't the one"——他不是那个对的人。这是全书"命运"主题的关键：Ellie选择了Jonah，不是因为他是"更好的选择"，而是因为他是"对的人"。

---

### 12. "I just want you to be okay."

> The man—whose name, Ellie realizes, is Jack—pulls out a chair from the next table and sits beside her. He slides the pastry across to her. "My treat," he says. "I just want you to be okay."

**出处**：ch11 Ten

**解读**：Jack在Florida救了Bunny，然后他遇到了Ellie（二十年后）。"I just want you to be okay"是Jack对Ellie的关心的最直接表达——这是"另一个世界"的Jack对Ellie的爱。

---

### 13. "You don't have to do anything."

> Jack pulls his seat closer to the table and rests his elbows on it. "You don't have to do anything," he says. "You don't have to talk about it or think about it or decide anything right now."

**出处**：ch11 Ten

**解读**：Jack是Ellie的"旧爱"，但他已经有了自己的生活（Kristin）。"You don't have to do anything"是Jack对Ellie的尊重——他不会强迫她做任何决定。这是"另一个世界"的Jack和Ellie的关系的隐喻。

---

## Bunny的信仰

### 14. "I miss you terribly, Mom."

> Bunny locks eyes with her daughter. "I miss you terribly," she admits.

**出处**：ch37 Thirty-Six

**解读**：Ellie在Florida和Bunny告别。"I miss you terribly"是Bunny在Florida最直接表达的爱——尽管她和Frank在Florida很幸福，但她想念Ellie。这是Angela Brown的"家庭"主题：母亲的爱不因距离而改变。

---

### 15. "Until death do you part."

> You two made a vow to each other, Ellie. Until death do you part.

**出处**：ch31 Thirty

**解读**：Bunny在Florida对Ellie说的话。"直到死亡将你们分开"——这是Bunny和Frank的婚姻观，也是Ellie和Jonah的婚姻观。真正的婚姻是"直到死亡"——但真正的爱在死亡之后仍然存在。
