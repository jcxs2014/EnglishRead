# 《The Last Lifeboat》概述

## 全书梗概

**一九三九年九月三日，一间厨房里，一位记录者打开收音机。** 她听的是张伯伦的广播，而她写下的全部反应是："War! Listened to Chamberlain’s announcement over the wireless this morning."（ch01） 她给自己定的原则是找一个藏眼泪的好理由（"Besides, it was a useful way to hide my tears."，ch01）——**而这一篇署名是 #6672；直到 ch58 我们才看到这个人自己写的字。**

**那个"谁"，是彼得·尼科尔斯。** 一九四〇年二月十四日——比英国开打早八个月——他离开家去训练营，全篇五句日记，最后一句是："Expected to feel angry, or afraid, but when I looked at her standing on the doorstep, I felt nothing but immeasurable unspeakable love."（ch58）**而他此后再没回到那张门阶前——这本书记的是"一个人一生中所有正要说什么、却始终没说完的话"。**

**一九四〇年五月到九月，另一个女人在申请一份工作。** 她的申请理由是这样的："‘I don’t want to say too much, because it might all come to nothing yet, but I applied as a volunteer with the new overseas evacuation scheme. There’s a chance I might be escorting the seavacs.’ It felt strange to say it out loud. She felt immensely proud, and more than a little nauseous."（ch11） 而她自己也知道这份工作"无关紧要"（"So, when the final acceptance letter arrived, it felt less like an obligation to play her part in the war, and more like a very welcome invitation."，ch11）——**所以这本书对"责任"的定义，从一开始就不是英雄主义，是"我没什么可做，那就做点有用的"。**

**九月十日，伦敦东区在烧。** 一位记录者把这一夜命名为：
- "Black Saturday, they’re calling it."（ch13）
- 而他写下的最后一句是："Nothing else to say. No words can ever capture what we have seen."（ch13）
**——#6385 是这本书里唯一一个从头到尾只用记录来抵抗的人。**

**九月十三日，卡莱尔号出发。** 码头上的人说了一句关于迷信的话："‘Bad luck isn’t it, to set sail on a Friday?’"（ch17） 而孩子们已经在给护送员起名字了（"It still caught Alice by surprise that escorts were called auntie and uncle by the children."，ch17）——**这一天同时是全书最热闹和最不祥的一天。**

**九月十七日夜里，船上。** 一个十七岁的女孩在跌进救生艇时说的第一句话是：
- "Alice can’t breathe. The wind snatches her breath away, leaving her gasping for air as she half-jumps, half-stumbles into the lifeboat and falls, face down, against the boards."（ch02）
- 而她做的第一件事是数孩子："Barely able to distinguish one child from another, she counts five boys and one girl. They are each bunched up into a tight ball, hands wrapped around their knees, heads bent against the wind and rain. She doesn’t recognize any of them."（ch02）
**——这本书对"母亲"的定义从这里开始：先数清楚，再抱。**

**接下来的七天，是这本书的正文。** 他们先失去了水（"Life has been reduced to fragments – a few inches of space, a dipper of water, a sliver of food."，ch40），再失去了方向（"‘Yes, they proceed unescorted.’"，ch30），再失去了"希望"这个东西本身：
- "Without that, without hope, she may as well slip into the water now and let it carry her away."（ch40）
- **而这一天她摸到了一头鲸，全船人告诉她那是幻觉："‘There wasn’t a whale, Alice.’"（ch45）
**

**真正的转折点是那个黑点。** 她先说了两次谎（"‘Smile, smile, smile.’"，ch45），第三次有人喊出来的结果是："It is impossible, incredible, and yet, there it is."（ch49）**——而这一章的标题是《五个小奇迹》（"Five little miracles."，ch49）**

**回到岸上，代价才刚刚开始。** 她在镜子里认不出自己：
- "The woman in the mirror is unrecognizable, a wild woman with thickly matted hair, cuts and bruises on her wind-reddened face, a thin crust of salt on her lips and eyelashes, her teeth thick with it."（ch52）
- 而一个孩子的诊断只有一个词：‘Pneumonia.’（ch52）
**这一章是全书最安静的一章，因为最难的部分还没有被说出来。**

**然后是追责。** 一位母亲在格拉斯哥的码头上等：
- "It is a ship of ghosts, a ship of lost souls coming back to life."（ch53）
- 而一个匿名者在信里写下："It is disappointing that a full inquiry was not considered necessary. Not necessary for the grieving parents desperate for answers, or for those in positions of authority who wished to avoid public scrutiny of their actions?"（ch59）
**——她要的不是惩罚，是"See point 1) above."（"See point 1) above."，ch59）**

**结局在十年后。** 一个孩子在花园里松开一根羽毛（"It feels as if it should take a lifetime."，ch60）**而这本书的最后一句话是三个词：‘Hello, Auntie.’（ch62）**

## 三个主题

**一、活下去的人要替不能说话的人说话。** 那封信在 ch57 被全文抄出：
- "she’s here.’"（ch48）
- 而这个要求在结尾被兑现为一个书店的女主人，她对孩子们说："If she fixed it, she would lose another part of him, and so it remains, with its perfect flaws."（ch57）
**——所以这本书里的"语言"从来不是修辞，是一种代持。**

**二、"我尽力了"是这本书唯一的和解。** 彼得在遗书里说"这不是一封告别信"（"Everything is mismatched and uneven without him."，ch48） 而爱丽丝在船上对那个孩子说的是："‘I won’t leave you, Arthur. I promise. We’ve come this far together, and we’ll go home together. You’ve been ever so brave."（ch45）**——这两句是同一个句式：一个人把自己能给的东西交出去，然后停止。**

**三、记住一个人的方式，是继续用他的方式活着。** 那个写信的孩子说：
- "I thought you might like it, to remember me while I’m away."（ch61）
- 而十年后那个人真的记住了："There are a thousand things she wants to say, but only two words are ever needed for their story to begin again, to take them back, and carry them on."（ch62）
**——所以这本书的最终形态是：一本书本身。**

## 人物弧光

**爱丽丝·金：
- ** 从一个在船上学会停止呼吸的十七岁女孩（"Alice can’t breathe. The wind snatches her breath away, leaving her gasping for air as she half-jumps, half-stumbles into the lifeboat and falls, face down, against the boards."，ch02），到那个用一封信把全船希望撑住的护送员（"‘I don’t want to say too much, because it might all come to nothing yet, but I applied as a volunteer with the new overseas evacuation scheme. There’s a chance I might be escorting the seavacs.’ It felt strange to say it out loud. She felt immensely proud, and more than a little nauseous."，ch11），再到那个拒绝被称为弥赛亚的人："‘They look at me as if I’m the Messiah. And I’m not. I’m just me. Just Alice.’"（ch56）
- **她的弧线不是"战胜创伤"，而是把创伤变成一种可以每天做的动作——那动作在 ch59 有了名字："These are the real acts of heroism. Ordinary women, holding the country together. The quiet essential backbone of the war on the home front."（ch59）
**

**莉莉·尼科尔斯：
- ** 从一个在饼干铁盒里寻找儿子痕迹的母亲（"She doesn’t need to read what’s inside the biscuit tin. She already knows every word."，ch33），到那个拒绝被安慰的人："‘You brought him back to me so that I could say goodbye. I can never thank you enough for that.’"（ch56）
- **而她最重的一句不是关于自己儿子的："It is a ship of ghosts, a ship of lost souls coming back to life."（ch53）
**

**彼得：** 一个不敢写"再见"的人（"Impossible to say goodbye to my darling Lil."，ch58）——**而他的弧线不在自己身上，在 ch61 那个孩子的信里，以及 ch60 那根被松开的羽毛上。**

**比利·富尔顿：** 全书最短的弧线——从一封信（"We have a life jacket each and the warships will keep us safe from the Germans, so don’t worry."，ch61）到一句遗言："He slipped away quietly in his mother’s arms.’"（ch56）**而这两句之间只隔了六页纸。**
