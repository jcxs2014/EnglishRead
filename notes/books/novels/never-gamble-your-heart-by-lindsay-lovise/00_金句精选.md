# Never Gamble Your Heart · 金句精选

## 数学与爱情的隐喻

> "My life has been an unsolved equation, and you, Frankie, are the final sum."

我的人生是一道未解方程，而你，Frankie，是最终的答案。（ch40）

> "She could not think when he touched her; his hands dulled the clarity of her mind, and she was left with only feelings, and Frankie was never quite sure what to do with those."

当他触碰她时她无法思考；他的双手钝化了她思维的清晰度，只剩下情感，而 Frankie 从不确定该如何处理情感。（ch20）

> "For a bloody brilliant mathematician, you are acting astoundingly stupid."

作为一个极其聪明的数学家，你的行为却惊人地愚蠢。（ch20）

> "You count cards, you are disarmingly candid, and you broke a man's nose with your forehead. You sparkle, Frankie Turner."

你会算牌，你坦诚得让人卸下防备，你用额头撞烂了一个人的鼻子。你闪闪发光，Frankie Turner。（ch25）

> "I have spent years avoiding marriage because I could not imagine my life with any woman, and then you walked into my house and now I cannot imagine living without you."

我多年逃避婚姻是因为无法想象与任何女人共度一生，直到你走进我家，现在我无法想象没有你的生活。（ch40）

## Jasper 的保护欲与克制

> "As long as I am breathing I will do anything to keep you safe."

只要我还有一口气，我会不惜一切保护你的安全。（ch35）

> "Jasper protected what was his at all costs."

Jasper 不惜一切代价保护属于他的东西。（ch25）

> "He had to get to Frankie and Cecelia. He had to protect them from Evelyn and her father."

他必须回到 Frankie 和 Cecelia 身边。他必须保护她们免受 Evelyn 父女伤害。（ch50）

> "If anyone knows about gambling with high stakes, it's me."

如果有人说了解高风险赌博，那就是我。（ch20）

> "We are all monsters in our own way; my way just happens to be more visible than theirs. But I am better at seeing you."

所有人都是以各自方式存在的怪物，只是我的方式更显眼。但我更擅长看见真正的你。（ch40）

## Frankie 的独立宣言

> "I will not sit back out of fear for my own life when I have the skills to help, even in some small way."

当我有能力帮助时，我不会因害怕自己的生命而坐视不管，哪怕只是微小的帮助。（ch20）

> "I will not be run off."

我不会被赶走。（ch35）

> "However, I cannot afford to be timid. My sister is impulsive and young, and no one knows where she is. There are women being sold into lives of miserable matrimony because they dared stand up for others. Who is defending them now? No one."

然而，我承担不起怯懦的代价。我妹妹冲动又年轻，没人知道她在哪里。有些女性因为敢于为他人发声而被卖入悲惨的婚姻生活。现在谁在保护她们？没有人。（ch20）

> "I am not working under the assumption that I am invincible."

我不是在假设自己无敌的情况下工作。（ch20）

> "I must remain unwed for our ruse to work."

我必须保持未婚以便计策奏效。（ch25）

## 社会批判

> "The Chartist movement is gaining traction—the working classes want the vote, and it is making the House of Lords nervous. It is very possible someone has taken it upon himself to quell the 'troublemaking women' in order to ease the tension."

宪章运动正在获得势头——工人阶级想要投票权，这让上议院感到紧张。很可能有人自行决定镇压"惹麻烦的女性"以缓解紧张局势。（ch30）

> "Marriage is not the only way to silence a woman."

婚姻不是让女人沉默的唯一方式。（ch30）

> "No woman should dress in disagreeable clothes for modesty's sake."

没有女人应该为了谦逊而穿令人不快的衣服。（ch10）

> "Should not everyone have a say in who he or she foolishly chains themselves to?"

难道每个人不应该对自己愚蠢地与之绑定的人有发言权吗？（ch10）

> "They were being called Fallow Fellows, Grim Grooms, and Silver Shag-bags, and they would live out the rest of their days shunned from good society."

他们被称为"Fallow Fellows"、"Grim Grooms"和"Silver Shag-bags"，将在余生被上流社会排斥。（ch51）

## 幽默讽刺

> "The only thing worse than a frightened aristocrat was a bored aristocrat."

比受惊的贵族更糟糕的是无聊的贵族。（ch35）

> "I have a difficult time comprehending the use of sarcasm. It seems to me a very lazy form of expression."

我很难理解讽刺的用法。在我看来这是一种非常懒惰的表达方式。（ch01）

> "You are mistaken, Mr. Jones. I am not a servant. I am a gov-er-ness." She said the word governess slowly, as if he were an imbecile.

"你错了，Jones 先生。我不是仆人。我是家—庭—教—师。"她缓慢地说出这个词，仿佛他是个白痴。（ch01）

> "Have some propriety, young man," Madam Margaret chided, slapping him with her fan as she tottered past.

"有点体统吧，年轻人，"Madam Margaret 责备道，用扇子拍打他，蹒跚而过。（ch10）

> "This woman, the Dove, runs an underground network of governess spies, and you were working for her."

"这个女人，The Dove，运营着一个地下家庭女教师间谍网络，而你为她工作。"（ch51）

## 浪漫告白

> "Despite decades dedicated to bachelorhood and emotionally detached affairs, he'd fallen entirely, stupidly, and undisputedly in love with his future wife."

尽管几十年奉行单身主义和情感疏离的风流韵事，他还是完全、愚蠢且无可争议地爱上了未来的妻子。（ch40）

> "You are everything that is perfect. Your little dimples, your gorgeous smile, your messy hair and your big spectacles, your tendency toward being so candid it stings, your wit and intelligence, the way you make love—everything about you is exactly perfect for me. It is as if you were made for me."

你是完美的化身。你的小酒窝、迷人的笑容、凌乱的头发和大眼镜、你那坦率到刺痛人的倾向、你的机智和智慧、你做爱的方式——你的一切都为我量身定制。仿佛你是为我而生。（ch40）

> "Husbands and wives do not keep score."

夫妻之间不计较得失。（ch40）

> "I will forever be in your debt."

我将永远欠你的人情。（ch40）

> "Do you know, Jasper, that if you keep saying things like that, we may never get out of bed?"

你知道吗，Jasper，如果你继续说这样的话，我们可能永远下不了床？（ch40）

## 智慧洞察

> "You clever fox. If it were socially acceptable to have a woman of good standing in my hell, I would woo you until you accepted a job at Rockford's."

你这狡猾的狐狸。如果在我的赌场雇佣有地位的女性在社会上可接受，我会追求你直到你接受 Rockford's 的工作。（ch30）

> "Ruining his reputation was only justice."

毁掉他的名声只是正义。（ch51）

> "I will work on believing you when you say you like my oddities—and rest assured I will remind you of your claims when you are exasperated with me—if you will work on believing that you are a good man."

我会努力相信你喜欢我的怪癖——放心，当你对我感到恼火时我会提醒你今天的承诺——条件是你要努力相信自己是个好人。（ch40）

> "Barely civilized satisfaction flashed across his face before he said, 'What do you want?' He spread his hands and gave her a wolfish smile. 'Anything I am capable of giving you. Do not be afraid to think big, Frankie. I will be.'"

近乎野蛮的满足感闪过 Jasper 的脸，他说："你想要什么？"他张开双手露出狼一般的笑容说："任何我能给你的东西。不要害怕往大处想，Frankie。我会的。"（ch20）

> "Frankie, love, I think you correspond with your governess agency more than two lovers write one another."

"Frankie，亲爱的，我觉得你和家庭女教师机构的通信比恋人之间还多。"（ch51）
