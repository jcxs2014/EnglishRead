# 《This Shattered Tempest》情感节点

## 节点一：碎玻璃与一记带着血的吻（ch01-ch04）

**叙事概括：** 伊安塔斯的假面舞会本该是一场胜利庆典：夜帝与内纳瓦伦的继承人成婚，无月之暗结束。刺客在人群里动手，塔拉辛被劫走，阿拉里克右肋挨刀，一座城的玻璃碎在地上。他带十个人从后方摸进丝兰地的狩猎小屋，用影门把整栋楼掀开，把她带出来——然后在破晓的院子里，当着所有还活着的人吻了她，嘴里全是血与战场的味道。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The fracture began in the pit of Alaric’s stomach. It rose into his chest and flowed everywhere else, pushed through his veins by the stuttering beats of his heart, growing wider and more searing until his whole being was numb. Cracked in half, the only hope of repair hinging on the moment that a girl in green and gold, with eyes like fire, would emerge from out of the darkness."（ch01）

> ② "He pulled her to him and kissed her, hard and angry, shoving his tongue past her lips that were still parted in the shape of a question. He tasted like blood and battle, like a promise of vengeance. He kissed her as the sun soared over the mountains, in front of everyone who was still alive."（ch03）

## 节点二：天穹宫的王座厅与阳台上的越线（ch08-ch10）

**叙事概括：** 回内纳瓦伦之后，她要面对的是最不擅长的一件事——以继承人的身份说话。王座厅里她替被反剪双臂的伊尚·瓦伊卡出头，当众顶撞乌尔杜佳，第一次把场面从祖母手里夺过来。但那晚在阳台上，她与丈夫的争吵一路滑到最不该碰的地方：她把贝利安那桩死人的事摔到他脸上，他露出父亲的脸，召出影门，而她的情绪召来了三条龙。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“How dare you?” Talasyn shouted at Urduja, her eyes blazing. She rushed over to Ishan and helped her to her feet, even as she continued castigating her grandmother. “Without Daya Vaikar, none of us would have made it through the Night of the World-Eater! Is this how you treat your allies, like criminals? Where is your gratitude, you cruel, evil—”"（ch08）

> ② "A muscle in his pale jaw clenched. “It was always part of the deal that we’d return to the Night Empire. I have other responsibilities and I can’t keep on neglecting them. But neither can I let you stay here when we’ve yet to neutralize the threat on your life.”"（ch10）

## 节点三：蛇窝里的那杯茶（ch11）

**叙事概括：** 乌尔杜佳登岛，一场本该是礼节性的茶喝成审讯。她先点破塔拉辛一直在服用避孕的树皮，再点破这段婚姻对这个女孩意味着什么，最后用一句「在毒蛇窝里，你也得是毒蛇」收尾。塔拉辛没有哭出声，她只是在那间沙龙里第一次明白：祖母不是要她幸福，而是要她活下来，并且活成一件趁手的工具。

**关键引语：**

> ① "She’d been taking the bark of the wisewoman’s lilac as a preventive. She’d been going down to the castle kitchens for a dose every time she and Alaric slept together."（ch11）

> ② "“In a nest of vipers, one must be a viper, too.” The Dragon Queen’s eyes were as dark as midnight, giving nothing away. “Keep this in mind, Lachis’ka, and you just might survive us all.”"（ch11）

## 节点四：城塞里的女侍叫出了她的名字（ch15-ch16）

**叙事概括：** 到了凯萨斯的城塞，她成了名义上的夜后、实际上的客人。新来的女侍自称埃丽丝，说话卑顺得像另一个物种——直到那晚在阳台上，凯德叫出「Tal」，说出自己在那场战争里生了一个儿子、把孩子留在瓦利萨，然后回来当间谍。第二天早上，那个曾经和她一起在军营里大笑的女人，端着一杯热可可站在她面前，手在抖。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“The— Your baby,” she said, and found that she couldn’t say anything more. Khaede had been pregnant when the Sardovian Allfold lost the war and now she wasn’t, obviously—almost a year had passed—and Talasyn waited for the response with trepidation."（ch15）

> ② "To her utmost horror, Talasyn’s eyes began to water in a way that had nothing to do with the soap or the steam. What was it about a familiar fragrance that could shatter her into so many tiny pieces? How could it so quickly conjure him—the moon-kissed tones of his bare skin, his drowsy half-smiles in the early morning, his arms around her—"（ch16）

## 节点五：五十个人的处决与南附楼的那只鸟（ch25-ch27）

**叙事概括：** 沉船峡打下来五十名俘虏，盖赫里斯点名要她到场。塔拉辛拒绝观刑，最后还是去了；阿拉里克一句仪式性的话都没说，放出数十把影刃。她站在原地哭到脸上全是泪，拒绝擦。同一夜，凯德带她从仆役通道钻进南附楼，在那间「闹鬼」的圆室里，她看见笼中的萨里曼——她自己的魔法，正是在这只鸟的血里被拿走的。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Without uttering a single word of the ceremonial speech that was expected of him, Alaric sent the inky knives flying in all directions. They moved toward the captive Sardovians as swiftly as swarms of crumpled black leaves in a stiff wind."（ch25）

> ② "Talasyn stumbled toward the sound, her entire being weighed down with disbelief. She pushed one door open, and her world shattered into a million pieces as she beheld the sariman."（ch26）

## 节点六：最后一枚球，与他走进暴风雨（ch28-ch29）

**叙事概括：** 马哈吉尔憩地的莲花塔里，她投出六枚球，最后一枚在手里停住，逼凯德用两国的神起誓留她丈夫一命。爆炸之后，她爬上西侧尖塔被达里乌斯围住，而阿拉里克带着战镰出现——他杀了自己人、解开她的束缚，然后一句话也没说，转身走进雨里。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Promise me,” Talasyn said as calmly as she could manage even as she also raised her voice to be heard over the din of magic. “Swear to me that, after this, the Sardovian Allfold will keep their word and spare my husband’s life.”"（ch28）

> ② "A droplet of water moving over moon-kissed skin caught her attention. She thought at first that it was the rain, which had tangled with the ends of Alaric’s black hair, but she soon realized that it was a lone tear, trickling from the corner of an eye that blazed with the starlight of aetherspace, streaming down the charcoal-etched line of his scar."（ch29）

## 节点七：界间的对质与母亲的手（ch34-ch38）

**叙事概括：** 月食把她拖进界间，两人第一次把所有账摊开：他问她当初为什么要跟他睡，她说因为她也想；他数自己付的代价，她说她付的也不少。后来她在黎明群岛的朝圣路上，看见母亲从龙群里向她走来——那是一个梦，也是一次告别。再后来，她把光链缠上祖母的脖子，听见祖母说自己快死了，最后没有落下那一千片光。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Then why sleep with me in the first place, Lachis’ka?” Alaric countered. “Why spend all that time agreeing with me that we could work together, when you and I could have continued loathing each other and it wouldn’t have altered your plans in the slightest?” He wasn’t looking at her as he said any of this."（ch34）

> ② "Hanan Ivralis smiled back and wrapped her arms around her sobbing daughter. The dragons raised their heads and flew off into the clouds, but the swirling golden radiance remained. It was coming from Talasyn’s form as she shook in her mother’s embrace."（ch37）

> ③ "“Yes, I am dying.” Urduja lowered the now crimson-streaked handkerchief. “I am dying, and when Gaheris sought me out at the Citadel, you were not ready to rule. I had to buy time—make him see you as less of a threat.”"（ch38）

## 节点八：王冠里的毒针（ch40）

**叙事概括：** 乌尔杜佳死在自己设的饵里，冰冠冲上海滩，尸体烧得认不出来。葬礼之后是加冕，巴昆在崖上盘旋，誓词一句句念完。她戴上王冠之前，用光织把它撬到空中，从缝隙里抽出几根毒针，然后转过去，把第一项正式指控交给那个一直站在祖母身侧的女人。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The sage gave a start as the crown was pried loose from her grip and sent hovering several feet in the air, suspended in currents of golden magic. The radiant threads slipped into the crown’s every crevice, searching for what Talasyn already knew would be there."（ch40）

> ② "Talasyn turned to a white-faced Lueve. “And now, my first act as the Zahiya-lachis of the Nenavar Dominion is to formally charge Lady Lueve of House Rasmey, the Daya of Cenderwas, with treason for spearheading the conspiracy that aimed to overthrow the ruling royal house."（ch40）

## 节点九：影门里的千个声音与海上的龙（ch47-ch50）

**叙事概括：** 盖赫里斯的舱室里，影门把她整个人吊起来，血管爆裂；黑暗里一个既是她又不是她的声音说「他不过是个男人」，光就从她眼睛里炸出来。同一场乱局里，影子嵌合兽吞掉了摄政，百条龙从越海下升起，而她在海滩上看见丈夫肩上插着匕首、利苏正要刺下去——她用最后一点力气把龙牙匕首送进对方的颈动脉。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Then the Shadowgate erupted into being, a sudden, jagged cloud of midnight, surrounding her and lifting her off the floor. Had she not been a Lightweaver, she would have been sliced to ribbons on the spot."（ch47）

> ② "The Lightweave spiraled out of her in the form of seven snarling dragons. Tethered to her, they lashed through the air from the bowrider’s well in all directions, disintegrating every single iron bolt in their path before their golden jaws closed around the wolf coracles. The whole squadron crumbled in the onslaught."（ch49）

> ③ "She heard Alaric utter her name in a hoarse shout. Lisu jerked back in shock but he was still holding on to the hilt, which ensured that he was still within Talasyn’s reach. She tugged the dragon-fang dagger from its sheath and—with the last, fading vestiges of strength and motor function—she drove it straight into Lisu’s jugular."（ch50）

## 节点十：病床上的七天与五年后的早晨（ch52-ch56）

**叙事概括：** 她昏迷了七天，他在床边把话说完：一切在你出现之前都是空的。此后是龙巴昆的死、和谈、以及一个早晨——她在天穹宫的走廊上跑向自己的女儿，三岁的希拉雅手心里托出一枚影门圆盘，父亲跪下来教她怎么收手。故事最后落在他要讲给孩子听的那句话上。

**关键引语：**

> ① "His confession was muffled against her robes. “Everything was so hollow until you came along. I’d been fighting my whole life, consumed by a singular purpose, and then there you were—making me laugh, making me see red, making my heart race, making me feel at peace."（ch52）

> ② "When Alaric broke the news of Bakun’s death, Talasyn had wept so hard and for so long that he’d feared she would lose consciousness once more. He’d been powerless to do anything but hold her, silently cursing himself, silently vowing to the gods that he would cut out his own tongue before he ever made her cry again."（ch54）

> ③ "“For everything I ever did that has hurt you. For every word I said while I was angry. I will spend the rest of my life making it up to you.”"（ch55）

> ④ "“Somebody loved me,” he said. “That is how I survived the war.”"（ch56）
