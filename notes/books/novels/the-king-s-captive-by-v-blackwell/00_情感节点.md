# 《The King’s Captive》情感节点

> 10 个节点；每节点叙事概括 + 2–3 句关键引语（逐字取自已过门禁的章节块；说话人见括注）。

**节点一：灭门之后的囚居（ch01）**。三个月前父母被杀，二十岁的 Kit 被留在凶手 Soren 的宅子里；他蜷在小房间里哭，唯一温和的是每天送饭的 Lorraine。第一次走进厨房，Soren 一句 Christopher 就把他的自我叫回去了。（说话人：Kit 叙述）

> "Three months; nearly three months since that night, and I still don't know if I'm crying because Mom and Dad are gone… or because I'm still here."（ch01）

> "Soren just grunts. The low sound makes me flinch hard enough that my shoulders jump. … Then his voice cuts through the kitchen again, flat and unreadable. "So, you finally left your room, Christopher.""（ch01）

---

**节点二：厨房里的照料与「允许」（ch01–ch02）**。Lorraine 顶着老板的压力替 Kit 争回名字，又劝他吃东西；Soren 从门后喝止她的搭话，却在水杯打碎、孩子划伤手时下意识抓住手腕、贴创可贴，随后才惊觉自己像看护一样捧着他的手。（说话人：ch01 为 Kit 叙述；ch02 为 Soren 叙述）

> "Lorraine lets out a soft hum. It's protective, but I can hear the tiny smile in it when she answers. "Oh, he prefers to be called Kit.""（ch01）

> "I don't usually put band-aids on people, in fact… I never have. Why the fuck did I just do that? I keep hold of his hand, frowning down at it for several long seconds, before the realisation hits me… I'm standing here cradling the kid's hand like some kind of caretaker."（ch02）

---

**节点三：第一次越界与「说 no 的机会」（ch03）**。一个月后 Kit 已能在花园帮忙，却被一句「Kit 不离开庄园」拦下出门；小客厅里 Soren 给了唯一一次拒绝的机会。Kit 说不出那个字，只能在心里用「有用好过当没处理的尾巴」换命，又在事后把对方的体贴退回给「怪物不会体贴」。（说话人：Kit 叙述）

> "Soren turns me slowly, before one large finger slides under my chin and tips my face up. His voice comes out rough, almost strained, like he's holding himself back. "I'll give you one chance to say no, Kit." … I frown, completely lost. My eyes flick up to his for half a second before I look away again, trying to make sense of the words. Chance to say no to what?"（ch03）

> "This is absurd. … This man killed my parents. … Why didn't I say no? … Why am I just letting this happen?"（ch03）

> "The way he sounds… so thoroughly satisfied, almost like he's been starved of this… makes a strange thought flicker through my exhausted mind. … Does he not usually have time for sex? Is that why he seems so affected right now?"（ch03）

---

**节点四：真相：父母与 Zero Hour（ch04）**。书房会议揭开灭门的组织背景：父母替 Zero Hour 递情报、造武器。Soren 下令所有人闭嘴，让 Kit 继续把他当杀亲仇人；Ray 当面问出「你从不保护人」，Soren 的自问第一次转向自己。（说话人：Soren 叙述）

> "I keep my voice flat and firm. "I don't want any of you fuckers bothering him, you got that? He doesn't need to know what his parents were into. He doesn't need to know they were wrapped up with Zero Hour, feeding them information and building them weapons. Let him keep thinking they were just his innocent mom and dad and that I'm the bad man that killed them." … Armen actually looks confused. "But… why on earth would you want him to believe that?" … I roll my eyes. "He's safer that way.""（ch04）

> "But underneath that relief sits something else… a quiet, confusing fear that refuses to leave. It sits there unbidden, stubborn, and unwanted. … Why the fuck am I this bothered about keeping Kit alive? About keeping him indoors, out of sight, and protected?"（ch04）

---

**节点五：卧室、计数与声音（ch05）**。三周后 Kit 心里数到第四次，Soren 只找他。醉酒的 Soren 把他抱上三楼自己的卧室，要他开口说话；这个一向以沉默自保的人，第一次在失控里放开声音喊出来。（说话人：ch05 前半为 Kit 叙述，末段为 Soren 叙述）

> "That was the fourth time. I've been unwillingly keeping count in my head. Maybe one day the numbers will blur and I'll lose track, but right now I still know. Four. And for the past three weeks he hasn't touched anyone else, only me. … He's not exactly hard to look at either… I can admit that much, even if I don't want to. … Still, choosing me every single time feels… odd."（ch05）

> "I look around and pause… this isn't another random room. This is a bedroom; a large, dark, expensive-looking bedroom. Huge bed with black sheets, heavy curtains, and sleek furniture. The faint smell of his cologne in the air. Everything about the space feels private and lived-in and completely his. … I'm standing in Soren Sinclair's actual bedroom."（ch05）

> ""Come on, Kit," I growl, teeth clenched. "Fucking talk to me. I know you can. Let me hear that pretty voice." … He only turns his face away, lips parted, breathing hard. The silence just makes me hungrier and more frustrated at the same time."（ch05）

---

**节点六：被困的一夜（ch06）**。事后 Soren 压着他睡死，留下「明天上桌吃早餐」的醉话；Kit 先说这没有意义，再自己抬起那只沉重的手钻出去，带着腕上的束缚摸黑回房。（说话人：Kit 叙述）

> "His voice comes out quiet and slurred against my skin. "You'll… have breakfast at the table tomorrow." … I frown up at the dark ceiling. Why? He's never wanted me at the table before. I'm usually allowed to eat in the kitchen with Lorraine or just take something back to my room. Why would he suddenly want me sitting there with the rest of them?"（ch06）

> "The hallway is completely dark and empty; no voices, no footsteps, nothing. The silence feels heavier than noise ever could. It presses in from both ends of the long corridor, making the back of my neck prickle. I glance left, then right, heart still hammering, and then I'm turning toward the back staircase as fast as I can without actually running. I still have to figure out a way to get my wrists free, but first, I need my room."（ch06）

---

**节点七：餐桌新规（ch07）**。Soren 把 Kit 押到餐桌右手边，宣布没人许坐他旁边、没人许碰他；他把这套隔离叫保护。Ray 一句「他最怕的是你」拆了台，Kit 从指缝里偷看的那一眼则被 Soren 当成新规律收下。（说话人：Soren 叙述）

> "Something dark and immediate snaps in my chest. "Don't touch him, Selina." The words come out hard and commanding. … "Not anymore. No one sits next to Christopher. Leave him alone." I raise my voice just enough for the whole table to hear. "That goes for the rest of you as well. Don't bother him. He doesn't need to be pressured into conversation by a bunch of people twice his size who walk around armed all day. He's fine where he is, leave him be.""（ch07）

> "I tell myself it's for his own good; the kid's already skittish enough. Crowding him, touching him, and forcing him to talk… none of that helps. He's safest when people keep their distance and let him stay under my direct watch. Isolation isn't the word I'd use; protection is."（ch07）

---

**节点八：生日爆发（ch08）**。五个月后新来的 Aria 一个拥抱就让 Kit 第一次抗命出走。被扛回卧室后，他交出第一句完整的话与生日账：二十一岁无人问津，「你可以要我的身体，但得不到我的话」。Soren 破例抱他下楼、允许他听音乐。（说话人：ch08 前半为 Kit 叙述，后半为 Soren 叙述）

> "I keep walking because I refuse to sit there and watch Aria, who's been here five minutes, smile and talk and get hugged like she already fits, while I've been trapped in this house for ten months with permission to speak to exactly one person: Lorraine. I sleep in a tiny box room, and I own three outfits and a single pair of shoes."（ch08）

> "Kit keeps talking, voice thick and heartbreaking. "I was twenty-one five days ago, on Monday. My parents would have made a fuss. They would have acted like it was Christmas Day. But here… no one cared to ask about my birthday. No one was even allowed to smile at me… just one smile would have been nice. So, no… you don't deserve my words. You can have my body, but not my words.""（ch08）

> "I swallow, suddenly unsure of myself in a way I rarely am. "Do you… want me to carry you or something?" … He only shrugs, the smallest movement, like the answer is yes but he doesn't dare actually ask. A short, surprised laugh leaves me. I hook my hands under his thighs and lift him up. The second his weight settles against me, something deep and possessive in my chest eases."（ch08）

---

**节点九：十七个月的一声名字（ch09）**。近一年后，Kit 在失控里第一次喊出 Soren 的名字，随即捂住嘴。早餐桌上 Ray 提精神状态、Lorraine 直斥「Shame on you」，Soren 的冷面具当场失灵，第一次承认自己把人当囚徒。（说话人：Soren 叙述）

> "My name. For the first time in seventeen months, my actual name leaves his mouth"（ch09）

> "Lorraine exhales a long, sad breath. Her hand settles on his shoulder, rubbing gently. She shakes her head and whispers, low enough that Kit doesn't catch it. "Shame on you, Soren Sinclair. You know how to look after everyone else under this roof; your whole family. And yet you've neglected the one person you've ever actually let close to you… all for selfish reasons.""（ch09）

---

**节点十：二十二岁生日与尾声（ch10–ch11）**。Lorraine 的贺卡与蛋糕给了 Kit 短暂的暖意，他仍先问「我被允许吗」；Soren 迟到的祝福用的是 Christopher。尾声里一张印着 Christopher Sinclair 的黑卡被锁进保险柜，Soren 只留下自己死后的应急密码，并在书房感到一道叫不出名字的裂缝。（说话人：ch10 为 Kit 叙述；ch11 为 Soren 叙述）

> "My eyes go wide; for a long moment, I just blink at it. “Is that… for me?” I whisper. … I hesitate. “Am I… allowed this?”"（ch10）

> "Then he just walks out. The door clicks shut behind him with a soft finality, leaving the room suddenly too quiet. … Alright, priorities… shower first. Horrifying mental spiralling can wait until I’m clean and locked safely in my own room."（ch10）

> "I hate not knowing. I’ve built everything on control, on seeing the board clearly before anyone else moves. This… blank, formless pressure sits wrong under my skin. … A crack I can’t locate. … A weakness I can’t name yet."（ch11）
