# Anchored to the Storm · 情感节点

> 节点编号全书唯一；引语均逐字取自已过门禁的章节精读块。

## 节点一：坠海与身世炸弹（ch01）

Maddox 呛水醒来、确认童年幻影真实存在，又在章末听到 Styx 交出第一块身世拼图——被救的狂喜与被掀翻的身世在同章完成。

> "It was all fucking real: the sparkling water, the call to the sea, the bike plunging into the ocean. And then her, there, saving me. Again. Just like she had that fateful day when I plunged into the Atlantic at age ten."

> "Styx wiped under his eyes and his nose, then told me, “And handed me my son.”"

## 节点二：掌心誓言与十岁的账（ch02/ch04）

Styx 用十指相扣把守护写成能力清单；西线的 Sarah 则在同一段时光里，把领养人的名字与噩梦里那个名字重叠。

> "“Kid, listen to me. I won’t ever let you go. Whatever you need from me, or whoever you need me to be,” he rattled my hand to hold my attention that was already glued to him, “I have it in me to be it. Understand?” His jaw locked and his brown eyes watered. “I stayed by your dad’s side until I couldn’t in order to save his life. Your life.”"

> "Styx. A name Johnny knew and would cry for in his nightmares. Please be good to my friend."

## 节点三：血誓显形（ch04–ch06）

星光水、全员牙痛、共享噩梦、梦呓密码——血誓的账单分三期寄到两线。

> "I was already shaking my head. 'No way.' My fingers reached out, warmth flooding me again where I had felt cold after Johnny left. It was the water. The sparkles. They connected me to Johnny. 'I feel so close to him, Ethan. It's… wow.' I closed my eyes, and I could picture his face, his smile. 'It's like he's right here.'"

> "As I shut the door, he mumbled, 'Zero-four-nine-nine.' Huh?"

## 节点四：神话对账与亡父的泳池（ch07/ch08）

木船蓝血与神之判决，让 Maddox 接过"守界者"的身份；The Lagoon 与隧道把神话兑换成不动产。

> "I don't know how to explain it, but I instantly knew I had made a huge mistake. It flowed through me like a wave of unknown knowledge. Something I should've known all along but didn't—a hindrance of being human. An out-of-body experience."

> "I jerked to a stop when I saw a very long underground tunnel, about four feet wide and tall. It pointed directly under my room, yet I couldn't see the end of it. 'Wait a minute. Does this lead to the ocean?'"

## 节点五：牢笼与放行（ch13）

为她而建的泳池在她的眼睛里是监狱——他抬闸放行，把"永不拒绝她"的誓言第一次改写成"让她离开"。

> "I lifted the gate. I love you, Saleeni."

## 节点六：海草王冠之夜（ch15）

掌心相扣、分子爆裂之吻与灵魂互赠——结合之后她说出完整的名字，而他发现她长出了腿。

> "Saleeni rested on top of me, also spent and breathless. 'I love you, Maddox.'"

> "My mermaid… now had legs."

## 节点七：真相与立法（ch14）

"Her father."三个词引爆 Blue 案；Harmony 用两条家规给复仇立宪——不许杀人、遇袭只躲。

> "Finn squinted. His neck strained. Then he growled, 'Her father.'"

> "'Don't take a life. Don't be like that bastard of a man.' She sobbed, 'Be the beautiful soul you are.'"

## 节点八：至暗夜（ch19）

911 已死、Harmony 被割喉、Dale 托孤——Maddox 失去第三位母亲，也在灵堂上接过整个家。

> "Two men had been stabbed in various places and tied to furniture. Another body lay on her side. No beautiful brown eyes to see. They were closed, but I knew it was her because of her greying blonde hair spread out behind her. I knew it was her because of who was holding her hand. And I knew she was dead because her blood had drained from the slice across her neck."

> "He smiled again. 'Nothing is impossible, Brass.'"

## 节点九：净化与告别（ch20）

棚屋的私刑、淋浴的血水、木棺里的 Caleb、海边重逢又分别——最后一页把两线题记合流成一句话。

> "Opening my eyes, I took the last step forward and placed a hand on his left shoulder. I whispered, 'My turn to give you the time needed.'"

> "We are life… for each other."
