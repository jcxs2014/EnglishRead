# 《Backwoods Banshee》情感节点

## 节点一：奥格登剧院的那支歌（ch01-02）

**叙事概括：** 全书的情感起点是一场被中断的娱乐：亚当强撑着陪男友看演出，歌声落下时全场昏倒、主唱惨死。他独木支撑到精灵卫队赶到，救了几百人，得到的却是一句缓刑警告。维森特以 Reaper 之姿现身撑住时间——两人的关系在这一夜从「约会」变成「并肩」。

**关键引语：**

> ① "He drew his wards around himself to shut out the crowd’s emotions—waves of excitement spiked with the occasional pink static of lust or the placid gray of boredom. All of it broke against him, eroding his calm."（ch01）

> ② "The noise went on. It wasn’t music, not exactly, but still, it was a song, something jagged and broken that scraped along Adam’s nerves. It shrieked through his bones, like a train stopping too hard."（ch01）

> ③ "Time slowed as Vic stared at the scythe in his grip, his face concerned beneath the translucent skull laid atop his features. He extended his empty hand and a wasp flew to it, landing on his open palm."（ch02）

## 节点二：睁着眼睛的空人（ch03）

**叙事概括：** Bobby 把哥哥带到病房：六名患者醒着、能吃能走，里面却没有人。Adam 打开 Sight 确认这不是 Annie 式的附体，而是灵魂被整个扯走。谋杀在此变成一种更冷的消失，全卷的主案由此立案。

**关键引语：**

> ① "A young man sat on the edge of the bed, his eyes open. A nurse was talking to him, but getting no response. He looked awake, conscious, but blank."（ch03）

> ② "This wasn’t possession. It was displacement. The man’s spirit had been ripped out of him."（ch03）

## 节点三：骷髅镍币与纸箱里的王（ch06-07）

**叙事概括：** 夜市询价灵魂，商贩说它不值钱；街头窝棚里，钱币国王 Seamus 给出更老的证词——那支歌是警告，警告之物能把全体抹除。他递给亚当一枚雕着礼帽骷髅的五分镍币：临时同盟的第一份契约。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“We’ve heard the song.” A shudder passed through Wendy’s wings, causing a momentary dip toward the ground. “Everyone has.”"（ch06）

> ② "Seamus lowered his head. After a slow shake of it, he said, “I don’t exactly know. It’s older than me, but I can feel it in my bones. I’ve heard the song. It’s a warning. I don’t know what it is, what’s down there, but I know it could unmake us all.”"（ch07）

> ③ "It wasn’t like Seamus to be so generous, but Adam couldn’t feel any malicious magic in it. It was a gift, a sign of their temporary alliance."（ch07）

## 节点四：一堵墙（ch10-12）

**叙事概括：** 家被入侵、书被偷走，亚当在愤怒与恐惧之间熬过一夜；治疗室里 Cahill 把他的麻木命名为「纯真之死」——不是缺爱，是一堵墙。处方轻得像玩笑：每天抱紧一个好时刻。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Something red and sour—anger—churned in his guts. It felt better than the icy fear lurking beneath it."（ch10）

> ② "“Is it missing or is it a wall?” He sat with that for a moment. It fit. Less like a gap and more akin to something he couldn’t break through."（ch12）

> ③ "“Grief doesn’t just mean that someone died. It can also mean that you’ve suffered a loss, and you have.” … “You’ve had a loss of innocence. Your scars aren’t just physical, they’re psychological.”"（ch12）

## 节点五：Erythraean 地下室（ch13）

**叙事概括：** 夜返旅馆取书：顶层保险柜是空局，真正的答案在当年受刑的地下室。Argent 已把刑房洗净、把书放回原位；亚当拿回家族之物，也第一次直视被种进体内的恨——它就住在 warlock wound 与爱情旁边。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The taste of it lingered and he felt an orange puff of satisfaction. At the time, he’d needed it as a distraction, for it not to get out of control. Now he wished it had kept on burning, that it had brought the hotel down."（ch13）

> ② "It was the sick feeling, the heavy thing in Adam’s chest. He’d felt hatred before, but never like this, never at such a deep, visceral level, and never so thoroughly."（ch13）

> ③ "He hated it, and he hated that this was within him, that White and Wilcox and their cult had put it there, right beside the warlock wound and his love for Vic."（ch13）

## 节点六：召唤失败的那一夜（ch14）

**叙事概括：** 三人绳阵为 Cole 定位失败，召唤却「请」来了界域的触须与歌；断阵之后，亚当决定首次独自灵行下界域。Emily 执绳为锚——这是全卷他最接近失控的一次出发。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Cole Walker Lawrence,” they said together. “We call you back. We summon you to us.”"（ch14）

> ② "“Break the circle!” Emily dropped the ends of the cord. The circle, and their summoning, dissolved, the magic dissipating."（ch14）

> ③ "“We can anchor him and pull him out of it if it goes wrong.” … in truth, he’d never attempted one like this. Sue had warned him about traveling beyond the spirit realm and getting lost in the planes. He’d gone beyond and made it back, but never by his own hand or by his own power."（ch14）

## 节点七：雪玫瑰与抽屉（ch15）

**叙事概括：** 骑士为儿子换上母亲面孔，开口只求一次探望。雪山上，North 卷出不会持久的雪玫瑰；衣柜抽屉里，一整套「人间」躺在垃圾之中。亚当许诺带书教英语——这份善意后来成了 North 记得的唯一温柔。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Real flowers don’t either, but that doesn’t mean they aren’t worth it.”"（ch15）

> ② "Inside was garbage, or what looked like it. A really old magazine, a few pieces of paper, ski lift tickets written in German, and a single wallet. Its contents had been carefully laid out like they occupied a case in a museum."（ch15）

> ③ "Then the little storm exploded, bursting across the room. Icy flakes pricked Adam’s cheeks and he understood. North’s magic was unstable."（ch15）

## 节点八：夜市之战（ch17, 20）

**叙事概括：** 空人在摊位前破体，成群 banshee 同夜袭击；亚当召回剑、负伤死战，Argent 从天而降。战后清点：保安食人魔被毒杀，Cole 的衣柜里藏着十二只储魂瓶——六只满，六只待用。案件在这一夜从追踪变成战争。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The sword settled into Adam’s hand, a familiar weight. He could feel its joy."（ch17）

> ② "“I’ve been focused on the wrong thing. It’s not about the souls. It’s about the bodies. The empty ones, the people the banshees take the spirits from. The banshees are crossing over through them. Whoever is doing this is using the empty people as weapons.”"（ch17）

> ③ "Six contained a glowing insect, each like a tiny, winged star. They had different auras. Adam handled them carefully as he lifted them out and lined them up on the floor."（ch20）

## 节点九：梦者墓穴（ch29-30）

**叙事概括：** Archive 的机械档案把「精灵地狱」改写成造物主的囚牢；Silver 在泳池之下的密室里承认抹档，也承认 Empress 是自己的母亲。他杀过父亲救人间，却不知能否对母亲第二次拔剑。亚当抱住他——PTSD 这一次没有拦路。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“It’s a prison for one being, and only one. There wasn’t a name, just a title: the Empress, but if I read it right, she’s one of the elven goddesses, or more like a primordial power.”"（ch29）

> ② "“You met my father, Adam. You know he was a terror. Did you expect my mother to be anything less?”"（ch30）

> ③ "“My world dies.” Silver deflated. “I killed my father, Adam. I did what I had to save your world. I don’t think I can do it again, not even to save mine.”"（ch30）

## 节点十：裁决与长局（ch35）

**叙事概括：** 游乐园的树上挂满丝带，每条对应一位死者。Silver 判 Adam 留任 Page、判 Dautre 百年流放；裂缝仍悬在天上。走出大厅的 Adam 笑着认下自己的路——先救世界，才谈得上救 Ian。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“You are impertinent, Adam. You did not even manage to respect the bounds of your probation for half a year, but you are my page and my page you shall remain. You killed a man in self-defense, and you have shown more than adequate remorse, though I for one am glad you saved yourself when we could not. The realms are better for having you in them.”"（ch35）

> ② "“While many chose to follow you that day, you led them by example. As you failed to understand the value of mortal life, we ostracize you from this court and elven company for one century. You are barred from Alfheimr, the Sea Upon the Land, and any of our holdings on this plane or others. Your rank within it is dissolved. You will instead be sent to the western watchtower, to assume whatever duties the King of Coins asks you to perform. Do you accept this judgment?”"（ch35）

> ③ "What choice did he have? If he wanted to play the long game and save Ian, he first had to save the world."（ch35）
