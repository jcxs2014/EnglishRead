# 34. Chapter Thirty-Four

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：灵魂像橡皮筋回弹进肉身，她栽在墓地呕吐——三连 which means 推演出流程已崩（没会合 → 没交骨 → 不该被召回）；Rook 红着眼哀求"只能是你"，把法典塞进她手里：Juliet 出岔、只有她回来了，Credence 卡在土里等咒语；墓土浸着 potion of life、散着新土嫩叶石榴香，还有一股唯有完成仪式才能餍足的饥渴；三遍咒语逐级升格（首遍 Rook 纠音磕绊 → 二三遍火花、穿胸之风、悬吊白光、异界嗓）——十帧解剖式视见把死者从骨缝缝到睁眼，破土成活（Resurrected. I did it.）；可"你做到了"是对 Rook 说的，第三具尸体数出五花大绑堵嘴的 Tolliver——Rook 向"妹妹"鞠躬，蒙太奇结账：The con was never mine. It was his. I was the mark.——末行 "Welcome back… Hades."

**情感弧线位置**：呕吐晕眩 → 慌乱接锅 → 硬着头皮的自我打气 → 仪式三段加速（抖 → 悬 → 白光）→ 复活狂喜（I did it）→ 冷却（功劳记错名）→ 数人头的恐怖 → 认知崩塌（我是标记）→ 末行核爆——单章完成"接任务—施大法—揭大骗"三幕，情绪峰谷落差全书之最。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：复活戏 = 全书魔法系统的总验收（二十年积攒一次放完，她确实 filled to brimming）；"我做的功被记在别人名下"是 con 结构的微缩预演——紧接着宏观揭露；heist 反转经典款：任务成功即陷阱合拢（数人头数出来的）；双 con 嵌套——她的假悲情局被他的真悲情局反套，grieving his sister as I was grieving my brother 是作者明示的对局镜像。

**人物弧线**：Elle 完成从"我不会魔法"到"我做的魔法"的身份跨越——落膝那刻她既是施法者也是咒语本身；Rook 的人物弧在此整章反转为操盘手——回看他的血、泪、吻、"被看见"，全部是 mark 工程的施工记录；被复活者不是 Credence——"the person who is definitely not his sister" 把身份之谜直接抛给后文（他称她 Hades）。

**叙事手法**：咒语三遍 = 标准仪式升格结构（每遍加一层感官特效，句长递减、断句变碎，拉丁词由读者被迫跟读）；反转不靠台词靠算术（Three of them. / But there should only be two.）——恐怖感是数出来的；蒙太奇闪回用 Rook…Me… 交替排比十行重演证据链，再用三行诗体断句（The con was never mine. / It was his. / I was the mark.）完成最后一锤。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** My soul hits my body like a snap of a rubber band. My eyes fly open. Rook’s face is above mine, behind him the shadow of trees and the pinpricks of stars. His hands are on my cheeks. His mouth is moving, but I can’t make out what he’s saying. I shove him away, turn to my side, and vomit into what I’m realizing is grave dirt. We’re in the cemetery. Which means it’s time to do the resurrection. Except it’s not. I never met up with Ezra and CJ, which means they shouldn’t have given Credence her finger bone, which means Tolliver shouldn’t have brought me back.

**中文理解：** 我的灵魂像橡皮筋回弹一样撞进身体。眼帘弹开——Rook 的脸在上方，背后是树影与星点。他的手捧着我的脸颊，嘴在动，我听不清他在说什么。我把他推开、侧过身，吐在……哦，这是墓土里。我们在墓地。也就是说，到了做复活仪式的时候。可又不对：我根本没跟 Ezra 和 CJ 会合，也就是说他们不该把 Credence 的指骨交出去，也就是说 Tolliver 不该把我弄回来。

**关键词：** a snap of a rubber band（橡皮筋回弹）/ grave dirt（墓土）/ which means…which means（一环扣一环的推演）

**为什么这样写：** 回魂用弹弓物理写——痛感先于视觉，睁眼只有 Rook 的嘴在动（声音失焦模拟刚上线的晕眩）；呕吐进墓土的感官粗粝把浪漫复活戏钉回泥土，浪漫必须踩着泥才有重量；三个 which means 串成逻辑链、每环一惊——她的第一反应不是"我回来了"而是"流程出事了"，职业本能永远优先于生存本能。

**读者视角提示：** 章首直接续接 ch33 末的拔线，三连 which means 替读者做前情提要（队伍散了、步骤崩了）；"Tolliver shouldn't have brought me back" 悬起本章第一钩——答案要等 Rook 开口，而 Rook 正是本章末的那颗炸弹：第一次读，没人会怀疑这个捧着她脸的人。

> **原句 2:** “Credence,” Rook says. “Her soul reunited with her body. You have to do the spell. Now.” “What?” I slur. “Please, Elle, she’s stuck in the ground. She can’t be fully resurrected without the spell.” I shake my head as another wave of nausea hits me. “Poppy…” Poppy is the one who’s supposed to do the spell. She should be back by now… But no. The backup plan. What was the backup plan? If Poppy couldn’t do it, then— “CJ,” I mumble. “There was a problem with Juliet,” Rook says, speaking so quickly he’s almost stumbling over his words. “You were the only one who came back. Tolliver’s working on it. But we can’t wait. We don’t know how long it’ll take to bring them back, and she’s stuck, Elle. Credence is stuck. You have to help her. Please.” I blink. This was never even a possibility. I can’t do magic, not like they can. I’ll mess it up. “I can’t…” I choke out. “You have to,” Rook says. “It has to be you.” I look up, finally having enough focus to fully see him. His eyes are red and wet. A fat tear runs down his face. “Please,” he begs. I shake my head, but I’m not saying no. “Do you have the—” Relief washes over his face as he shoves the codex into my hands.

**中文理解：** "Credence，"Rook 说，"她的灵魂归位了。你必须现在做那个咒语。""什——"我含混。"求你了 Elle，她卡在地里。没有咒语她没法真正复活。"又一阵恶心袭来，我摇头："Poppy…"咒该由 Poppy 来做，她早该回来了……不，备选方案——Poppy 不行的话就——"CJ，"我咕哝。"Juliet 出了问题，"Rook 说得太快，话都快绊在一起，"回来的只有你一个。Tolliver 在处理。但我们等不了——不知道要多久才能把他们带回来，而她卡着，Elle，Credence 卡着。你必须帮她。求你了。"我眨眼。这从不在选项里——我不会魔法，不像他们。我会搞砸的。"我不行……"我挤出半句。"你必须，"Rook 说，"只能是你。"我抬头，终于聚焦看清他：眼睛红而湿，一颗胖泪滚下脸。"求你。"他哀求。我摇头——但我说的不是"不"。"你有没有——"他如释重负，一把把法典塞进我手里。

**关键词：** reunited with her body（灵魂归体）/ It has to be you（只能是你）/ shoves the codex into my hands（把法典塞进我手里）

**为什么这样写：** Rook 的台词全被语速注释追着跑（speaking so quickly he's almost stumbling）——恳求的急迫不靠形容词、靠句法打结；"You were the only one who came back" 一句同时交代减员与她的特殊性；她连拒三次（I can't / I'll mess it up / I can't…）到"摇头却不是说不"的默许，转折只靠一个动作：codex 落进手里——眼泪与塞书是恳求与布置的同一个手势。

**读者视角提示：** "It has to be you" 是双关：字面上她确实只能是她（别人都没回来），暗面上 Rook 的局正需要一个不知情的施法人——两层意思到章末才合龙；红眼、落泪、"Please" 全是真演技，ch34 末的蒙太奇会给这些镜头逐条标价。

> **原句 3:** Rook helps me to my feet, hands firm under my elbows. Every instinct tells me to find Tolliver and help him bring CJ and Ezra back, but Rook all but drags me over to Credence’s grave, and I can see now why he’s so worried. The grave dirt is wet with the potion of life. The scent of fresh tilled earth and new leaves and pomegranate waft up from the grave. But there’s something else as well. Something hitting a sense I didn’t even know I had. It’s like wailing, but I don’t hear it. Like an itch, but I don’t feel it. It rattles my bones, squeezes my chest. It’s a desperation that fills me. A hunger that will only be sated by finishing the spell. I take a deep breath, trying to feel the magic inside me. I know it’s there. I’ve been going to the Afterlife since I was six years old. More times than I can count, and I’ve barely done any magic. I must be filled to brimming with it. I can do this. I can do this. I think I can do this.

**中文理解：** Rook 扶我起身，手稳稳托着肘。我每个本能都在喊：先去找 Tolliver、帮他把 CJ 和 Ezra 带回来——但 Rook 几乎是把我拖到 Credence 坟前，现在我明白他为什么急成这样了：坟土被生命药剂浸湿，翻过的新土、嫩叶和石榴的气味往上冒——但还有别的东西，敲在一个我都不知道存在的感官上：像嚎哭，我却听不见；像瘙痒，我却摸不着。它震着我的骨头、挤着我的胸——一种灌满我的绝望，一种唯有念完咒语才能餍足的饥渴。我深吸气，试着感受体内的魔法。我知道它在：我六岁起就跑来世，次数多到数不清，却几乎没用过一点魔法——我一定满得要溢出来了。我能做到。我能做到。我想我能做到。

**关键词：** potion of life（生命药剂）/ A hunger that will only be sated（唯有完成才能餍足的饥渴）/ filled to brimming（满得要溢出来）

**为什么这样写：** 坟土段逐级点名五感：湿（药剂浸土）、嗅（新土嫩叶石榴）、然后是第六感——"像嚎哭却听不见、像瘙痒却摸不着"的通感双联把仪式的外部压力直接写进骨头；自我打气三行重复却逐行加码，第三行加的 I think 不是犹豫，是把"相信"降到能执行的档位——完美自信不可用，能启动就行。

**读者视角提示：** "六岁起、次数多到数不清、几乎没用过"是她给自己算的账——brimming 之说既是鼓励也是伏笔：攒了二十年的量，她确实会在这座坟前一次放空；饥渴来自复活术本身（咒未完、死者不瞑）——魔法在全书第一次主动"要"她，攻守在这一段悄悄易位。

> **原句 4:** The grimoire automatically opens to the resurrection spell. I look at the page, trembling. Rook puts his hand over mine, holding me steady as I begin to read the incantation. “Au…dite me…spiritus ultra…velum.” My voice is thin. I stumble over the unfamiliar words. “Rompe limen—” “Rumpe,” Rook corrects me. “Rumpe…limen reforma nexum.” I let out an unsteady breath before continuing. “Magicae fluit…per med…u…llam profundam…” I shake my head at the next word. “Expergiscere,” Rook says, voice starting to lose its gentle edge. “Ex-per-gi-sce-re,” I pronounce the words carefully. “Nunc e…somno…aeternum.” “Good,” Rook says. “Two more times.”

**中文理解：** 法典自己翻到了复活咒那页。我看着页面，手在抖。Rook 的手覆上我的，稳住我，我开始念：“Au…dite me…spiritus ultra…velum。”声音是细的，生词上直绊跟头。“Rompe limen——”“Rumpe，”Rook 纠我。“Rumpe…limen reforma nexum。”我吐出一口不稳的气，接着念：“Magicae fluit…per med…u…llam profundam…”下一个词我摇摇头。“Expergiscere，”Rook 说，声音已经开始失去那点温柔的耐心。“Ex-per-gi-sce-re。”我一个音节一个音节咬准。“Nunc e…somno…aeternum。”“好，”Rook 说，"再来两遍。"

**关键词：** grimoire（魔法书）/ stumble over the unfamiliar words（在生词上绊跟头）/ Two more times（再来两遍）

**为什么这样写：** 第一遍是课堂戏：手按手托着念、逐词纠音（Rompe 与 Rumpe 一字之差也抓）、她把单词切成音节硬嚼（Ex-per-gi-sce-re）——神圣咒语被降格成外语跟读，笑点与紧张同源；"voice starting to lose its gentle edge" 用老师的耐心衰减计量进度；"Two more times" 把仪式预告成三遍循环——读者立刻知道后两遍会升级。

**读者视角提示：** 纠音细节（Rumpe 而非 Rompe——拉丁 rumper e 的祈使形，意为"打破"）是作者的考据彩蛋，也是结构需要：仪式必须先不完美，才配得上后面的成功；法典自动翻页说明这本书在等她——法典的主动性是个没解释的钩子，留给后文。

> **原句 5:** I nod, taking another deep breath, and begin again. “Audite me. Spiritus…ultra velum.” My voice is stronger this time. I try to reach inward, to feel some pull of magic, and I’m surprised when something sparks to life inside me. Something calling to the unfinished spell. Or the unfinished spell calling to me. “Rumpe limen…reforma nexum.” A shiver runs through me. A prickling sensation that comes from the inside out. Like something lives inside me and is trying to break free. “Magicae fluit per…medullam profundam.” Wind rustles my hair. An owl hoots in the distance. “Ex…per…giscere…nunc e somno…aeternum.” A sudden rush hits me. Not the wind this time but something very much like it. I feel my hair lift, then my body with it, like a string is tied to my heart and pulling me upward until my toes barely skim the ground. I barely notice as the codex falls from my hand. The spark inside me bursts to life, clawing at my chest. My vision goes white, and when I speak this time, there’s some otherworldly quality to my voice.

**中文理解：** 我点头，再深吸一口气，重新开始：“Audite me. Spiritus…ultra velum。”这次声音稳了些。我试着向内探、去够那点魔法的拉力——惊讶于体内真有什么被点燃：是某种东西在召唤这句未完的咒，或是这咒在召唤我。“Rumpe limen…reforma nexum。”一阵战栗穿过我，由内而外的刺麻——像身体里住着什么、正要破出来。“Magicae fluit per…medullam profundam。”风捋过我的头发，远处猫头鹰叫了一声。“Ex…per…giscere…nunc e somno…aeternum。”一股突来的急流击中我——这次不是风，却极像风：头发先飘起来，然后身体跟着，像有根线系在心口往上提，脚趾刚擦着地。法典从手里滑落我几乎没察觉。胸口那点火花炸开、抓挠着我的胸。视野变白——再开口时，嗓音里已经有了不属于人间的质地。

**关键词：** sparks to life（点燃）/ prickling sensation（刺麻感）/ otherworldly quality（非人间的音色）

**为什么这样写：** 第二三遍的感官 buff 按清单逐条加装：战栗（内）→ 刺麻（由内而外）→ 风与猫头鹰（外景）→ 悬吊感（线系心口）→ 法典脱手（撒手）→ 白光 → 异界嗓——每完成一遍咒语就多一层特效，句式随之从完整句滑向碎句；"Like something lives inside me and is trying to break free" 把她自己写成一台正在启用的容器。

**读者视角提示：** 三遍结构是复活戏的升格模板（与 ch29 虚空诗、ch33 DNA 段同属"句式服从仪式"的设计）；"unfinished spell calling to me" 的双向呼应她前面的账（攒而未用）——二十年积攒在这一刻过闸，法力的性质也从"她的财产"滑向"她在借用什么"。

> **原句 6:** “Audite me, spiritus ultra velum.” I am filled. I am whole. Light shoots from my toes, my fingertips, burns bright inside me. “Rumpe limen, reforma nexum.” I see Credence’s soul now in my mind’s eye. I see it struggle among the pile of her remains. I see it gasp in delight as it finds purchase. “Magicae fluit per medullam profundam.” I see bones stitched together with tissue, sinew and veins forming, stretching out along the pathways of a body. Organs, then flesh, then skin, all the crude materials that make up a body knit themselves together. Muscles contract. Lungs fill. The heart beats. Eyes flash open. “Expergiscere nunc e somno aeternum.” The final line echoes through the cemetery in a voice I don’t recognize, though it came from my own lips. As soon as I’ve finished the final syllable, my body drops to the ground. I land on my knees.

**中文理解：** “Audite me, spiritus ultra velum。”我充盈。我完整。光从脚趾、从指尖射出，在我体内烧亮。“Rumpe limen, reforma nexum。”我在心眼之景里看见了 Credence 的灵魂——在她那堆残骸里挣扎、觅到落脚点时如释重负地吸气。“Magicae fluit per medullam profundam。”我看见骨头被组织缝合，肌腱与血管沿身体的路径拉伸成形；器官、再血肉、再皮肤——组成人的一切粗料自己织到一起：肌肉收缩，肺充气，心脏起搏，双眼猛睁。“Expergiscere nunc e somno aeternum。”最后一行在墓园里回荡，那声音我不认识——尽管它出自我自己的嘴唇。最后一个音节念完，我的身体砸向地面，双膝落地。

**关键词：** I am filled. I am whole.（我充盈。我完整。）/ sinew（肌腱）/ my body drops to the ground（身体砸向地面）

**为什么这样写：** 满功率段每句咒语后接一行宣告体短祷（I am filled. I am whole.）——第一人称从"念咒的人"升格成"咒语本身"；视见段用解剖课排比（骨 → 肌腱 → 血管 → 器官 → 血肉 → 皮肤 → 肌肉 → 肺 → 心 → 眼）从里往外缝，十个名词短句就是十帧动画——复活蓝图以生物课顺序背诵，越专业越震撼；末句"声音我不认识、却出自我自己的唇"给她的声音记了别人的名字：力量全部到账的同时，叙述者的第一人称第一次松动。

**读者视角提示：** "我的声音不再是我的"与 ch34 末"我是标记"同轴——法力到账的代价是自我边界的先行让渡；落膝收式是仪式的句号：先静默、后破土，作者把爆发与安静背靠背排，节奏张力全在那一秒的空白里。

> **原句 7:** Silence falls. No more owls hooting, crickets chirping. Not even the wind dares make a noise. I wait. The grave is still. “Maybe we should—” I start, but then Rook is dragging me back, and I finally see movement. The earth is shifting and crumbling. A dull groan sounds from under the dirt. Rook still has his arm around me, and I feel both our hearts thumping in time, each breath no more than a quick inhale. Finally, a pale dirt-encrusted finger emerges from the soil. “She’s here,” Rook says, releasing me. I watch as the single finger becomes a whole hand, then two, clawing at the damp grass. Slowly, agonizingly, more of her appears. A forearm. An elbow. A second arm. Then the crown of her head emerges, pushing through the dirt. It seems an eternity before her entire head is free. She gasps in a breath, eyes wild. Her naked body is smeared in mud as she pulls herself free from the grave. Rook is already beside her, throwing a velvet robe around her shoulders and helping her stand. And she does stand. Whole and alive. Resurrected. I did it. Oh my god, I did it. Not only did I do magic, but I did the magic. I brought someone back to life. Someone who had been rotting beneath the ground. Her soul had been tortured in Tartarus, and now she’s here before me. Alive. She stands taller than her brother, brushing dirt out of her hair, and I realize it’s much lighter than I first thought. A pale blond. The robe is tight around her thin form, but she doesn’t seem cold. She seems elated. “You did it,” she says to Rook, her voice a breathy rasp. I did it, I selfishly want to say, but I don’t. I turn to give them their private reunion and try not to think about how I should be having a similar one with my brother.

**中文理解：** 寂静落下——猫头鹰不叫了，蟋蟀不响了，连风都不敢出声。我等。坟是静的。"也许我们应该——"我刚开口，Rook 把我往后拖——我终于看见动静：土在松动、在塌陷，泥下传来一声闷哼。Rook 手臂还环着我，我感觉我们俩的心跳踩在同一个拍上，每次呼吸都只是短促一吸。一只裹着泥的惨白手指终于破土。"她来了。"Rook 松开我。我看着那根手指变成整只手、变成两只，抓着湿草；前臂、手肘、第二条手臂、然后是头顶的发冠顶开泥土——像过了一世，整个头才挣出来，她猛吸一口气，眼睛是狂的。裸身糊着泥把自己从坟里拔出来——Rook 已经在她身边，把丝绒袍甩上她肩、扶她站起。她站住了。完整而活着。复活了。我做到了。天哪，我做到了——不止是用了魔法，是我做的魔法：我把人带回了生。一个在地下腐烂过的人、灵魂在塔耳塔洛斯被拷问过的人，此刻站在我面前——活着。她站得比哥哥还高，拨着发间的土——发色比我原想的浅得多，浅金。袍子裹着她纤细的身形，她却不冷。她看起来亢奋。"你做到了。"她对 Rook 说，声音是气声的嘶哑。"我做到了"——我私心想着说出口，但我没有。我转身把这场私人重逢留给他们，不去想本该也有一场属于我和哥哥的重逢在等我。

**关键词：** dirt-encrusted finger（裹着泥的手指）/ Whole and alive（完整而活着）/ a breathy rasp（气声嘶哑）

**为什么这样写：** 破土段按身体部位点名（指 → 手 → 前臂 → 肘 → 手臂 → 头）——慢镜头出土与上一 block 的缝合蓝图首尾互文：刚缝好的零件逐件亮相；静默段用三行短句（I wait. / The grave is still.）把悬念抻到极限再放 movement；庆祝句全用独词段（Resurrected. / I did it.）——而喜悦顶点被一句转述掐灭："You did it," she says to Rook：她咽回 I did it 的那一秒，是整章温度的转折点。

**读者视角提示：** "你做到了"对 Rook 说 = 下一 block 反转的预热——功劳的归属就是骗局的账本；Credence 描写里的浅金发色、亢奋、站得比哥哥高，都是冷静的外观记录，揭晓"她不是 Credence"后全部要重读；Elle 转身给他们"私人重逢"，自己失去属于她的那场重逢——同章对照的残忍对称。

> **原句 8:** I want to share what I did with the others, but they’re all still lying motionless on the forest floor. I watch the three of them breathe in and out, wondering how long until they…wake… Three of them. But there should only be two. CJ and Ezra. Poppy entered the Afterlife in some Phantoms’ moritory. And Tolliver should be working on what went wrong with Juliet. But instead, Tolliver’s lying next to CJ and Ezra. Feet and hands bound. A gag in his mouth. I turn slowly, hoping for a reasonable explanation but knowing I’ll find none. At the foot of the grave, Rook is bowing to his sister. She stands tall and pale in the moonlight, arms spread as though she’s glorying in the feel of it. “You’ve done well,” she says to Rook. I shake my head, struggling to make sense of what I’m seeing. My mind flies back to the con. Rook grieving his sister as I was grieving my brother. Rook opening the door covered in blood. Me falling prey to empathy. Rook, tears welling in his eyes. Me not feeling so alone. Rook kissing me. Rook making me feel seen. Rook offering me exactly what I wanted, and I realize… The con was never mine. It was his. And I— I was the mark.

**中文理解：** 我想把我的壮举讲给大伙听，可他们还一动不动躺在林地上。我数着三个人的呼吸，纳闷还要多久才……醒……三个人。可本该只有两个：CJ 和 Ezra——Poppy 是在 Phantoms 的停灵处进的来世，Tolliver 应该还在处理 Juliet 的岔子。但躺在 CJ 和 Ezra 旁边的，是 Tolliver：手脚绑着，嘴里塞着布。我缓缓转身，盼着有个合理解释——又知道不会有。坟脚边，Rook 正向他的"妹妹"鞠躬。她高高立在月光里、张开双臂，像在享受这清辉。"你做得很好。"她对 Rook 说。我摇头，拼不出眼前画面的意义——我的脑子飞回那场 con：Rook 为妹妹哀恸，正如我为哥哥哀恸；Rook 开门时满身是血；我中了共情的招；Rook 眼里蓄着泪；我不再孤单；Rook 吻我；Rook 让我感觉被看见——Rook 给了我 exactly 我想要的一切，于是我意识到……那场 con 从来不是我的。是他的。而我——我就是那个目标。

**关键词：** A gag in his mouth（嘴里塞着布）/ I was the mark（我就是那个目标）/ grieving his sister as I was grieving my brother（他丧妹如我丧兄）

**为什么这样写：** 反转不靠台词靠算术："Three of them. / But there should only be two." 两行数人头把恐怖感算出来，再上五花大绑的 Tolliver 与 Rook 向"妹妹"鞠躬的定格；蒙太奇用 Rook…Me… 交替排比十行重演证据链——血门、共情、泪、吻、被看见，每一拍都是她亲历过的甜，重读全变苦；三行诗体断句（The con was never mine. / It was his. / I was the mark.）完成身份反转的最后一锤——本章的最后一行（Welcome back… Hades）把锤子钉到底。

**读者视角提示：** 她的 con（假悲情接近 Rook）被他的 con（真悲情接近她）反套——grieving his sister as I was grieving my brother 的镜像句是作者明示的对局结构；ch32 章尾 CJ 的警句（叫你信他的人不能信）在此坐实全书题眼——Rook 从头到尾都在要她信任；被称"妹妹"的鞠躬对象、那句 "Welcome back… Hades" 把最大的悬念（她复活的到底是谁）直接甩给下一章。

## 一句话总结

三遍咒语把二十年的积攒一次放完、十帧缝合动画把死者拼回站立——然后"你做到了"被记在 Rook 名下、第三具尸体数出惊悚、十行蒙太奇结账：那场 con 从来不是我的，是他的，而我就是那个目标——"欢迎回来，Hades。"
