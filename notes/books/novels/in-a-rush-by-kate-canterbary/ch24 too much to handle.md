## 逐句精读

### 第1段（第23–60句）

#### 第1句："If this first week was any indication, I wasn't especially good at being married."

**中文理解**："如果这第一周有任何指示的话，那我尤其不擅长婚姻。"

**句子结构**："If this first week was any indication, I wasn't especially good at being married."（条件状语从句+主系表）。

**关键词**：If this first week was any indication / wasn't especially good at being married

**表达方式**：If this first week was any indication是"如果这第一周有任何指示的话"；wasn't especially good at being married是"不擅长婚姻"。

**为什么这样写**：这是Emme对婚后第一周的评价。她混合了过敏药和Moonshine margaritas，错过了所有事情，Ryan不得不出差。这是"糟糕"的开始。

#### 第2句："I woke up the next afternoon with hair drool-plastered to my face and sinuses swollen enough to see from space."

**中文理解**："第二天下午我醒来，头发口水粘在脸上，鼻窦肿得可以从太空看到。"

**句子结构**："I woke up the next afternoon with hair drool-plastered to my face and sinuses swollen enough to see from space."（主谓+with复合结构）。

**关键词**：hair drool-plastered to my face / sinuses swollen enough to see from space

**表达方式**：hair drool-plastered to my face是"头发口水粘在脸上"；sinuses swollen enough to see from space是"鼻窦肿得可以从太空看到"。

**为什么这样写**：这是Emme在描述她醒来的状态。她混合了过敏药和Moonshine margaritas——这是一个糟糕的决定。她的"从太空可以看到"的鼻窦是幽默的夸张。

### 第2段（第60–120句）

#### 第3句："And then there was the issue of me waiting around for him to come back to the condo on Monday night, my calls and texts going unanswered for hours, only to discover he was filming a commercial outside of Vancouver."

**中文理解**："然后就是我在周一晚上等他在公寓里出现、我的电话和短信几个小时没有得到回复的问题，最后发现他在温哥华郊外拍广告。"

**句子结构**："And then there was the issue of me waiting around for him...only to discover he was filming a commercial outside of Vancouver."（there be句型+only to do不定式作结果状语）。

**关键词**：waiting around for him / my calls and texts going unanswered / filming a commercial outside of Vancouver

**表达方式**：waiting around for him是"等他"；my calls and texts going unanswered是"电话和短信没有得到回复"；filming a commercial outside of Vancouver是"在温哥华郊外拍广告"。

**为什么这样写**：这是Emme在说她对Ryan"消失"的反应。她等了他几个小时——然后才发现他在另一个国家。这是他们沟通问题的证据。

**句子结构**："I wanted to talk to him about everything that night."（主谓宾）+ "I assigned my class a long, involved independent project that gave me enough time..."（主谓宾+定语从句）+ "...to sit at my desk and blankly stare at my computer..."（不定式作定语）。

**关键词**：I wanted to talk to him / a long, involved independent project / blankly stare at my computer / all the lines we'd crossed

**表达方式**：I wanted to talk to him是"我想和他谈"；a long, involved independent project是"一个冗长的、复杂的独立项目"；blankly stare at my computer是"茫然地盯着电脑"；all the lines we'd crossed是"我们跨过的所有界限"。

**为什么这样写**：这是Emme在说她需要和Ryan谈"所有事情"——尤其是他们跨过的那些界限。她给了自己时间来做这个决定，但最终没有和他谈。

### 难点句1：第4句（Emme的"决定"）

> I assigned my class a long, involved independent project that gave me enough time to sit at my desk and blankly stare at my computer while I decided what I was going to do about Ryan—and all the lines we'd crossed.

## 精读结束总结

**本章主题**：婚后第一周的混乱——生病、错过电话、震动棒事件、以及关于边界的深夜对话。

6. **边界讨论**：Emme试图设定规则，Ryan尊重她
