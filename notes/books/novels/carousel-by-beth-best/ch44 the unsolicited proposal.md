# 44. The Unsolicited Proposal

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：阿拉在梦境中重建维克米海滩并为死者立纪念碑，德雷克加入并写下父亲的名字；随后他跪下用家族戒指向阿拉求婚，要求她成为王后而非制衡者，阿拉断然拒绝，德雷克坦白通过羁绊能感受到她与科斯默的亲密，警告她计划不可行。
- **情感弧线位置**：梦想与现实的冲突。阿拉在梦中建立属于自己的世界，却被德雷克的求婚打破。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：命中注定的求婚被拒绝；德雷克不再是冷漠的旁观者，而是主动争取的人。
- **人物弧线**：阿拉在梦中展现了她的力量和主导权，现实中却要面对德雷克的执着和羁绊带来的痛苦真相。
- **叙事手法**：梦境与现实交替，用海滩纪念碑的庄严衬托求婚场景的突兀与冲突。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "I use no magic for it. Just a simple piece of driftwood.
>
>
> I write in the sand the names of all of those who have been lost. There may be survivors in Vikemy, I know that, but even if there are, they will be rebuilding themselves among their ghosts. And that, too, is worth mourning.
>
>
> Writing the names of the dead in sand is tradition in Hevalla on days The Assembly designates for remembrance. Hevalla has no religion – and having any religion is discouraged – but the tradition dates from hundreds of years ago, when the country was still a kingdom. The idea is that as the tide rises, swallowing the names, these people will be committed to the memory of the sea, which – unlike humankind – never forgets. The salt water from the sea, meanwhile, will flow through and strengthen those mourning them."
**中文理解**：我没有用魔法，只用了一块简单的浮木。在沙滩上写下所有逝去之人的名字。我知道维克米可能还有幸存者，但即使有，他们也会在亡灵的阴影中重建生活。这同样值得哀悼。在赫瓦拉，议会指定的悼念日有在沙滩上写下死者名字的传统。潮水上涨吞没名字时，这些人将被交托给永不遗忘的大海记忆。
**关键词**：I use no magic for it / simple piece of driftwood / Just a simple piece of driftwood
**为什么这样写**：阿拉用最朴素的方式哀悼逝者，不用魔法，用传统，用人类共有的方式表达悲伤。
**读者视角提示**：读者会感受到阿拉对家园和亲人的深情。她用梦境重建家园，用沙滩纪念碑铭记逝者。
> **原句 2:** "He drops down onto one knee.
>
>
> There is a box in his hand, open to the most ornate, beautiful ring I’ve ever seen of rubies and diamonds.
>
>
> “It is probably not the right moment, or the right place,” he starts, voice low. “But time is running out. We will be in Rhavna soon⁠—”
>
>
> I snap back into my senses. Jump to my feet. “What the hell do you think you’re doing?”
>
>
> “Asking you to be my queen,” he replies simply. With a slight shake of his head, he confirms, “Not my advisor. Not my friend. My queen.” He pauses. Nothing moves but the waves, the insects, the swans, and my beating heart. “This ring has been in my family for generations. It was always going to be yours.”
>
>
> “No,” I say. And then I’m tearing at my hair with my hands. “You know it’s a no. Sisters,” I say, my voice high but clear. I’m surprised I’m not screaming at him. “How many times can I rebuff you? I’m going around and around in circles with you. Like…” I skim my gaze across the shoreline. Point at the carousel at the far end. “Like that carousel.”
>
>
> The ring vanishes from his hand.
>
>
> He stands.
>
>
> “Your plan isn’t going to work,” he states. “We need to do this, the way the fortune foresaw.”
>
>
> “It will work,” I stress, my whole body rocking. “It already is. Dezeeves will speak to the Queen. Our power can thrive regardless of any attachment⁠—”
>
>
> He cuts through, edged. “I can feel you, when you are with him.” He adds, as an explanation, “Your rapture. Through the bond.”
>
>
> I gawk at him. “You can feel me?” I physically recoil.
>
>
> He nods. “I could not before. But now I can. And that is one of the reasons your plan won’t work. There are many. But that is one.”
>
>
> Rage bursts through me. This sounds entirely like his problem, and not a reason my plan won’t work.
>
>
> “Well, you’ll just have to get to used to feeling me,” I say, nauseous.
>
>
> He exhales thickly through his nose, something flaring in his eyes. “And you. Would you be able to get used to feeling me? If I married someone else?”
>
>
> “I’d have to.”
>
>
> He’s disappointed.
>
>
> He takes a step forwards. A crab scuttles away from him.
>
>
> “I can feel other things, Ara,” he states, almost inaudibly. As if we’re not the only ones here. “I can feel how he lets you down.”
>
>
> I reply instantly. “He doesn’t let me down.”
>
>
> “He does. Quite regularly. He loses his temper. Says the wrong things. Acts irrationally – which endangers you. He can be rude and unkind to others, and to you. He lets you down.”
>
>
> Any patience I had left is gone. I let my voice become a shriek. It sounds like how my soul feels.
>
>
> “I’m not perfect either, Drake!”
>
>
> I’m panting.
>
>
> “I’m not perfect,” I repeat, “And neither is he. But he doesn’t let me down. You think you are telling me things I don’t already know about him? I know who he is. I love him anyway.”
>
>
> He shakes his head again. As if my own mind is wrong, and only he knows it.
>
>
> “You love that he suffered for you,” he says, disappearing like a trick of the light. Then he reappears right in front of me, dropping his head towards me. “But he has suffered for you no more than I have.” He holds out his arms to me, showing me the scars by his rolled-up sleeves.
>
>
> “That’s not the same. You were made to suffer for me. No one made Cosmer⁠—”
>
>
> He’s interrupting me again. “I was made to, then. What about now?” Slowly, he sinks his head into the crook of my neck. I stand, completely frozen, as he whispers into my skin, “No one is making me now.”
>
>
> I go cold all over. Swallow nothing.
>
>
> Hesitantly, my pulse in my ears, I lift his heavy head with my fingers.
>
>
> I try to form thoughts through the haze in my brain he put there. “You’re saying you have feelings for me. Beyond just wanting my power.”
>
>
> “I am saying,” he says carefully, “I want you to marry me.”
>
>
> I separate myself from him fully. “I’m sorry,” I say, because I am. “I sympathise for you. But my answer is no. And,” I add, “I would appreciate it if you would stop coming here. Especially when there is another man in my bed⁠—”
>
>
> “There isn’t.”
>
>
> I almost laugh. “What do you mean, there isn’t?”
>
>
> “He left your room. A while ago.”
>
>
> The smile I’d half-made slips off my lips.
>
>
> “What? No, he didn’t.”
>
>
> He shrugs. “He did.”
>
>
> I say, in a breath, “But where would he go?”
>
>
> Drake flicks a crane fly off my shoulder, then dusts my sleeve with the palm of his hand. The movement is absent-minded, gentle. Almost a caress.
>
>
> But his words wound me like violence.
>
>
> “To that which he loves more than you.”
>
>
> “There isn’t anything⁠—”
>
>
> I stop myself, my eyes widening. Because there is.
>
>
> There is.
>
>
> I hear Riss’ voice, like she’s standing by my shoulder.
>
>
> You can’t even really fall asleep. You have to make sure they don’t get away from you.
>
>
> I let Cosmer get away from me.
>
>
> And he’s filling himself with something even my love can’t compete with.
>
>
> Wake up, I will myself.
>
>
> I do.
>
>
> I’m in a cold sweat, as if I’ve had a fever.
>
>
> I know without checking that Cosmer isn’t beside me. Of course he isn’t.
>
>
> I’m in a dark room, on a virtually deserted ship, somewhere in the sky…
>
>
> Alone in the world."
**中文理解**：他单膝跪下，手中打开的盒子里是一枚镶着红宝石和钻石的华美戒指。“也许时机和地点都不对，”他低声说，“但时间不多了，我们很快就要到拉夫纳了——”我猛地回过神，跳起来：“你到底在做什么？”“请你做我的王后，”他简单地回答，轻轻摇头，“不是我的顾问，不是我的朋友。是我的王后。”
**关键词**：knee / ring / queen
**为什么这样写**：德雷克的求婚在纪念碑前显得突兀而荒谬。他终于说出了自己的渴望，却完全无视阿拉的立场和此刻的庄重。
**读者视角提示**：读者会为阿拉感到愤怒。德雷克在哀悼逝者的时刻求婚，既自私又不合时宜。
> **原句 3:** "He cuts through, edged. “I can feel you, when you are with him.” He adds, as an explanation, “Your rapture. Through the bond.” I gawk at him. “You can feel me?” I physically recoil. He nods. “I could not before. But now I can. And that is one of the reasons your plan won’t work. There are many. But that is one.”"
**中文理解**：他尖锐地打断：“我能感觉到你和他在一起的时候。”他解释道，“你的欢愉。通过羁绊。”我瞪大眼睛：“你能感觉到我？”我身体不由自主地后退。他点头：“以前不能。但现在可以。这就是你的计划行不通的原因之一。还有很多原因，但这是其中之一。”
**关键词**：I can feel you, when you are with him / Your rapture. Through the bond / I could not before. But now I can.
**为什么这样写**：羁绊的真相被揭开。德雷克不仅能感知阿拉，还能感知她与科斯默亲密时的情感。这让阿拉的计划变得不可能。
**读者视角提示**：读者会意识到这对阿拉和科斯默来说是残酷的真相。即使他们在一起，德雷克也会感受到，这让阿拉的处境更加艰难。

## 一句话总结

阿拉在梦境中为逝者立纪念碑，德雷克突然跪地求婚要求她成为王后，阿拉愤怒拒绝，德雷克坦白通过羁绊能感知她与科斯默的亲密，警告她的计划注定失败，两人关系再度陷入紧张。
