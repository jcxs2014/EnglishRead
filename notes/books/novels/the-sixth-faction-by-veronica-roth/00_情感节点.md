# 《The Sixth Faction》情感节点

## 节点一：滑门后的镜子（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书的情感起点不在任何一场冲突里，而在一个不许被看见的动作里：Abnegation 禁止照镜子，她把镜子藏在楼上滑门后，用母亲的手势给自己剪头发。她想变得不一样，而这个「不一样」在她心里长成的样子，是「配得上这个家」。第一章结束时她已经把第一绺头发剪了下来——决定是在家里做出的，后果要等很久才到。

**关键引语：**

> ① "But I’m just so desperate to be different. To be the person I’m supposed to be. Desperate to be the person who fits here, in our faction, in our home, instead of the one who doesn’t."（ch01）

> ② "I close the scissors over the first lock of hair, and cut."（ch01）

## 节点二：红砖迷宫里的那个词（ch03-04）

**叙事概括：** 针扎进脖子，她在模拟里救了一个金发小女孩，在黑烟追上来时摸着凸砖爬上墙、张着双臂走过一条只有脚宽的路。Tori 出门手工录结果，回来时把「Divergent」这个词交给她，并让她登记为 Abnegation、对所有人保密。她这一天的收获是一个不能说的身份。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Some of the things you did in that test aren’t supposed to be possible,” she says, her voice quiet. “Scaling the wall, for example. Walking along the top of it. Those suggest . . . a certain level of awareness that you’re not supposed to have.”"（ch04）

> ② "“No. I’m saying you’re a Divergent,” she says. “It’s what we call it when someone’s mind is especially resistant to the faction computers. And it’s—”"（ch04）

## 节点三：玻璃、血与一次不能说的重逢（ch06-10）

**叙事概括：** 选择仪式被天窗上砸下来的玻璃打断，五面旗落地，白花绿旗升起；一枚针把袭击者从梁上打落，一枚子弹打死她的同学 Robert。她被一个 Dauntless 男孩推向侧门，在门口认出了早已「死」于第二次怀孕并发症的 Evelyn Eaton。接下来几天全是葬礼与程序：骨灰混进黏土做砖，Caleb 把血滴进 Erudite 的水碗，她留在 Abnegation，在 Memory Vault 里重看自己的测试，然后父亲当着 Marcus 的面要求抹掉她的记忆。她在最后一刻把脚放回了原处。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Shards of glass pelt us like hailstones. I duck automatically, covering my head with my arms. Beside me, Caleb is staring, wide-eyed, at a piece of glass sticking out of his forearm."（ch06）

> ② "She looks at Robert’s empty seat, and says, “Weeds grow, flowers bloom, fruit ripens.” She rests her hand on the back of the chair. “And they don’t ask us when they should.”"（ch07）

> ③ "I pause there with my heel lifted, frozen between forgetting and remembering. Then I put my foot back down. I step forward, instead, closer to the woman whose ashes I watched Marcus pack into clay."（ch10）

## 节点四：站台上的一跳与一个新名字（ch12）

**叙事概括：** 她给母亲留了字条，母亲在宵禁后把她带出总部，教她跳上行驶中的火车，然后在车厢里把她交给了 Evelyn。她没有回头，只在 Evelyn 叫她 Beatrice 时把那句话顶了回去——从这一刻起她叫 Tris。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The train is pulling up to the station. It slows as it approaches, but it’s still moving too fast, my last few minutes with my mother disappearing. She throws her arms around me and holds me so tightly I can’t breathe; then she jumps out of the train without warning."（ch12）

> ② "“Don’t call me that,” I say, and I’m not sure what gives me the audacity. Maybe it’s my anger at the people who killed my friend and upended my life, or maybe it’s the bitterness in Evelyn’s voice— it gives me permission to be bitter, too."（ch12）

## 节点五：中庭、冷汤与三条规矩（ch15-17）

**叙事概括：** 废弃教堂的长椅上睡满了人，冷汤罐头是入会测试；她在红砖楼八角玻璃顶的中庭里听见「第六派系」这个词，听见三条规矩与四个公会，也第一次看见有人把「没有派系」当成德性来守。她在这里遇见 Gretchen、Jove 和 Mae——Mender 的妹妹。她记住了三条规矩、四个公会，也记住了中庭里那盏灯。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“But there’s another kind,” she says. “Six years ago, a few of us decided we didn’t have to live that way. We came together, and started building something better, right under the five factions’ noses.”"（ch15）

> ② "I nod, but what I’m really thinking is that I’m standing in a faction headquarters—a sixth faction, hidden from the others, and the virtue it wraps itself around is “no factions.”"（ch15）

> ③ "“I was disappearing,” I say. “I realized I could do more good by leaving than by staying. And . . . I figured that if I’m going to feel this way, if I’m going to feel wrong all the time, it may as well mean something."（ch17）

## 节点六：一只眼睛与一只手（ch19-23）

**叙事概括：** Christina 的眼睛里插着一把黄油刀，医生保住了她的命、没保住眼睛，Eric 当场宣布她无法继续入门训练，她说「我不想再当 Dauntless 了」。后来 Weaver 带人闯进 Rookery 要带走 Mender 的妹妹，Mender 伸手去够枪，Tris 把他的手按了下去——那一下没有一句台词。

**关键引语：**

> ① "There’s blood spilling down the left side of her head, but the knife is holding some of it back. It turns my stomach. I keep my face as neutral as possible."（ch19）

> ② "It’s okay to be a person, to want things, to think whatever you like in your private thoughts.” He leans closer to my ear, like he’s telling me a secret. “It’s okay to be free.”"（ch22）

> ③ "“She’s my sister,” the Mender says, glaring at the Dauntless through the lock of dark hair that’s fallen in his eyes. “She doesn’t want to live with our mother; you can ask her.”"（ch23）

## 节点七：看着烟吞掉一栋楼（ch27-29）

**叙事概括：** Candor 总部起火，她在火车上看着烟把 Merchandise Mart 吞掉却要先往反方向走；她钻进气窗把 Mae 托出去，肩上留下一片烧痕；而在医院的病床上，Four 等到穿着 Amity 红裙的母亲亲口承认火是她放的。他不敢断定母亲不会为了杀一个人就烧一栋楼——想相信一件事，并不能让它变成真的。

**关键引语：**

> ① "On this route we have to go the wrong direction before we can go the right one, and watching the smoke engulfing the Merchandise Mart get farther and farther away is agonizing before the train turns and switches tracks. I bounce on my toes as we thunder back across the river; closer to the flames, to Christina, and to my father."（ch27）

> ② "“I’m going to give you a boost, and you’re going to crawl through that window frame, okay?” I say to her. “There’s a boy on the other side who will catch you. His name is Four.”"（ch28）

> ③ "I want to think my mom isn’t someone who would burn a building just to kill one man. I want to think she cares about the greater good. But I’ve learned the hard way that wanting to believe something doesn’t make it true."（ch29）

## 节点八：两种恐惧、一份契约（ch37-41）

**叙事概括：** 他带她走自己的恐惧地貌：火、桌上那支朝外的枪、拿他父亲当盾的 Weaver，她开枪打偏、击中父亲的肩。走完之后两人各自看见了对方最不愿被看见的东西，也交换了一份不谈爱情的承诺。到了她的恐惧地貌，他看见的是 Marcus 举起的皮带——而她扑上去替他挡。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I fire, but my aim is off. I hit my father in the shoulder, and blood gushes from the wound, spreading over his shirt. Weaver fires, too, right into the back of my father’s head."（ch37）

> ② "“I’ll promise not to try to get you to become factionless,” I say. “If you promise to believe me when I tell you I have my own reasons for doing things.”"（ch37）

> ③ "Sometimes you decide things in the moment, and sometimes you realize they were already decided a long time ago. Like when he hauled me to my feet at the Choosing Ceremony and shoved me toward safety. Or when I boarded a train and saw him standing in a shaft of moonlight, steady as a rock no matter how the ground moved."（ch41）

## 节点九：母亲倒下，哥哥举枪（ch53-56）

**叙事概括：** 母亲在仓库的水箱边替她挡下一枪，中弹倒下；「Go. Now.」是她把女儿推走之前说的。几个小时后，在 Dauntless 的模拟室里，Caleb 脱下夹克、抽出枪，把枪管顶在 Tobias 的头骨上，用「我跟 Jeanine 做了交易」这句话逼妹妹进入模拟。这一夜她把两个最亲的人都失去了：一个死了，一个变成了拿枪的人。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I see her fall, staggering back like she’s been punched. Her words echo in my mind, “Go. Now.” The boy is staring at my mother, lying on the ground with her arms at a strange angle."（ch53）

> ② "The sight of him makes rage surge inside me so suddenly that I feel sick. How can he stand to put on that sympathetic expression, that gentle voice, when he’s such a monster beneath them both? I still remember the way he looked when he raised a hand to his son."（ch55）

> ③ "“I don’t, that’s correct,” Caleb says evenly. “But I made a deal with Jeanine to save our father’s life, and I intend to keep my end of it.” He pushes the barrel of the gun into Tobias’s skull."（ch56）

## 节点十：一枪之后，坠盒里的罗盘（ch60-62）

**叙事概括：** 她在模拟里对着哥哥开了枪，然后被 Tobias 蒙着眼睛带出模拟室。天亮时 Evelyn 放下枪，一行人赶上开往 Amity 的火车。她在车上打开母亲的坠盒，里面不是照片，是一个只标着一颗星的罗盘——母亲早就替她把路留好了。全书最后，她数的是自己还在的部分。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Tobias closes his eyes and his head drops, and in the space he creates, I see my brother’s face, and I discover the choice is not impossible. I fire. I close my eyes, but I still hear it."（ch60）

> ② "“Mom,” I say, and it’s the first time I’ve called her that since she came back from the dead. It seems to startle her; I know it startles me. The sound of it and the feeling of it, so much bigger and heavier than it’s supposed to be."（ch61）

> ③ "I shift, and my mother’s locket slides across my sternum. Frowning, I hook my thumb under the chain and pull it out of my shirt. I open it, expecting to see a photograph of someone inside—my father, maybe, or . . ."（ch62）
