# 《The Sixth Faction》概述

## 全书梗概

**一个在无私派系里长到十六岁、却天天想着「不一样」的女孩。** Abnegation 不照镜子、不许自己出声；她躲在楼上滑门后，用母亲的手势给自己剪头发。她并不无私，也知道自己不够无私——她真正想要的，是变成配得上这个家的那个人："But I’m just so desperate to be different. To be the person I’m supposed to be. Desperate to be the person who fits here, in our faction, in our home, instead of the one who doesn’t."（ch01）

**能力测试把她的答案撕成了三份。** 测试员 Tori 让她在红砖迷宫里救一个金发小女孩，黑烟追上来时她没有跟着墙走，而是摸着凸出的砖爬上去、在上面张着双臂走过一条只有脚宽的路。Tori 出门手工录了结果，回来告诉她那个词："“No. I’m saying you’re a Divergent,” she says. “It’s what we call it when someone’s mind is especially resistant to the faction computers. And it’s—”"（ch04） 登记表上写的仍是 Abnegation，代价是假装。

**选择仪式当天的玻璃是从天窗上砸下来的。** 绿色面具的人喊「Death to factions」，五面旗被割落，一面白花绿旗升起来；一枚针打死了一个袭击者，一枚子弹打死了她的同学 Robert。混乱里，一个 Dauntless 男孩把她推向侧门，她在门口认出了本该在多年前死于难产的女人：Marcus Eaton 的妻子 Evelyn。"Shards of glass pelt us like hailstones. I duck automatically, covering my head with my arms. Beside me, Caleb is staring, wide-eyed, at a piece of glass sticking out of his forearm."（ch06）

**她被留下了，而留下来的方式是忘记。** 那年没有人转入 Abnegation，她留在家；Caleb 把血滴进 Erudite 的水碗。当晚 Marcus 让新人把手放在 Memory Vault 的石桌上「学习接受」，她在那里面重看了自己的测试，听见 Tori 将要说出那个词时，记忆被换成了屋顶上的尖叫。几天后的阅读室，她在旧相册里确认 Evelyn 还活着；父亲当着她与 Marcus 的面要求把这两段记忆抹掉："I pause there with my heel lifted, frozen between forgetting and remembering. Then I put my foot back down. I step forward, instead, closer to the woman whose ashes I watched Marcus pack into clay."（ch10）

**母亲比她早知道一切。** 后巷里，母亲说从 Evelyn 的葬礼之前就知道了，也早看出女儿是什么，并给了两条路：装作被抹净、留下来往上爬，或者走。她选了走，母亲教她跳上行驶中的火车，把她交给了 Evelyn；她在车厢里对母亲说别叫她那个名字："“Don’t call me that,” I say, and I’m not sure what gives me the audacity. Maybe it’s my anger at the people who killed my friend and upended my life, or maybe it’s the bitterness in Evelyn’s voice— it gives me permission to be bitter, too."（ch12）

**无派系者的组织叫 Hemlock，他们自称第六派系。** 三处安全屋（Rookery、Museum、Conservatory）、四种公会（Healers、Travelers、Makers、Foxes）、三条规矩（别被抓、让自己有用、没有派系）。她在这里遇见独眼的 Christina、剑拔弩张的 Gretchen、以及一个凑到她耳边说「当一个人是可以的」的缝衣人："It’s okay to be a person, to want things, to think whatever you like in your private thoughts.” He leans closer to my ear, like he’s telling me a secret. “It’s okay to be free.”"（ch22） 她要从 Foxes 开始，把自己凿出位置来。

**另一条线在两个男孩身上同时收紧。** Caleb 在 Erudite 的实验室里做面孔识别，发现自己的编号是「第四轮 B 组的对照组」；Four 在 Dauntless 的模拟室里替新人跑恐惧地貌、在 Eric 眼皮底下藏着自己的档案，后来在医院里听母亲亲口讲 Candor 总部那场火：她烧一栋楼，可能只为了杀一个人。而 Tris 在摩天轮上与他对视——他们各自认出了对方是什么："I told her she didn’t know me, and that was a lie. She knows too much, sees too much. I’ve spent two years trying to bury Tobias Eaton as deep as I could, to become someone new, and here she is, unearthing him."（ch26）

**最后一夜在模拟室里结算。** Jeanine 用 Dauntless 新兵当傀儡、用中继器把恐惧播进整座城；Evelyn 带着装子弹的枪去找 Marcus；母亲 Natalie 用身体替 Tris 挡了枪。

**Caleb 的那一半更冷。** 他为了保住父亲的命，跟 Jeanine 做了交易，把枪口抵在 Tobias 的后脑上，逼 Tris 进模拟："“I don’t, that’s correct,” Caleb says evenly. “But I made a deal with Jeanine to save our father’s life, and I intend to keep my end of it.” He pushes the barrel of the gun into Tobias’s skull."（ch56）

## 三个主题

**一、派系的德性是拿来用的，不是拿来信的。** Abnegation 教她把自己放低，Dauntless 教她跳，Erudite 教她算；每一派都有一套好听的宣言，而每个派系里的人都在用这套话做别的事。她最后站在中庭里数出那个词——第六派系，德性是「没有派系」：
- "I nod, but what I’m really thinking is that I’m standing in a faction headquarters—a sixth faction, hidden from the others, and the virtue it wraps itself around is “no factions.”"（ch15）
- 这句话既是全书的书名，也是作者对这一整套制度的判词：能约束人的从来不是德性，是「别被抓」。而 Erudite 的德性一旦被当成杠杆，就会变成一句轻描淡写的交易："“Of course. Frustration arises from curiosity, and curiosity is always to be met with satisfaction, like hunger or thirst or the need for sleep,” Jeanine says. “So would you like me to tell you more about this experiment?”"（ch31）

**二、能力与身份之间没有等号。** 测试说她三项都像，登记表说她一项都不是；父亲说抹掉就干净了，母亲说抹掉也还在。她在 Erudite 的镜子里看见自己带着火伤，在 Dauntless 的模拟里看见自己打偏，在屋顶上才想清楚："But now, with the wind dancing over my skin, I realize I haven’t given any part of myself away. I’m the whole me, and I’m free, and I belong because I’ve carved out a place for myself instead of waiting for someone else to let me into one."（ch43） 这本书里每一个「我是谁」的答案，都是先被别人写坏，才由她自己改写的。

**三、爱是可以被当作武器的。** Evelyn 用儿子对她的感情逼他回来，Jeanine 用「救父亲」这句话把一个儿子变成持枪的人，Caleb 用「守约」把枪口抵在朋友头上："Jeanine Matthews is a storyteller. I can see, so clearly, the narrative she’s set up for what happened today. I’m the twisted Dauntless boy who spends too much time alone, too much time going through his fear simulations."（ch59） 而在这一整套算计里，唯一没有被使用过的，是母亲替女儿挡下的那一枪——它不是交易，所以它不能被写进任何人的计划。

## 人物弧光

**Beatrice / Tris**——

- 起点："For most people, being looked at that way by a council leader—the final word of not only our faction but our city’s entire government—would be flattering. But it just makes me feel like a fraud."（ch02）被 Marcus 那样看一眼就让她觉得自己是个骗子——她连被称赞都要先扣掉自己一份。
- 转向："“You chose the wrong faction, or you think you did,” I say. “You don’t quite fit no matter where you are. And the more you learn about the world, about this city, about all the things you were told, the more confused you feel about where to go, what to do.”"（ch17）她第一次当面把另一个人的困局说清楚，也第一次允许自己相信「哪里都不合身」是一种共性。
- 最低点："Abnegation locked me inside myself. I think you’re trying to make yourself smaller to fit into its little box, my mother said, and she was right. A life of disappearing is no life."（ch46）母亲那句「一辈子消失不算活」被她认了下来，而那时母亲还活着。
- 落点："I shift, and my mother’s locket slides across my sternum. Frowning, I hook my thumb under the chain and pull it out of my shirt. I open it, expecting to see a photograph of someone inside—my father, maybe, or . . ."（ch62）最后她打开母亲的坠盒，里面不是照片，是替她留好的方向。

**Tobias / Four**——

- 起点："I’m a lot taller than her, and bigger, too, but I still back up. “I’m going to kill him, and that’s just the start,” she says. “Is that specific enough for you?”"（ch18）他对母亲说的第一句话是「我要杀他」。
- 中段："“I’ll promise not to try to get you to become factionless,” I say. “If you promise to believe me when I tell you I have my own reasons for doing things.”"（ch37）他把恐惧地貌给她看，换来的是一句互相约束的承诺。
- 落点："“Mom,” I say, and it’s the first time I’ve called her that since she came back from the dead. It seems to startle her; I know it startles me. The sound of it and the feeling of it, so much bigger and heavier than it’s supposed to be."（ch61）他第一次叫出「Mom」，不是为了和解，是为了让母亲放下枪。
- 他一直想做的只是「不再当 Marcus Eaton 的儿子」，而这件事他到最后也没有真正做到。

**Caleb**——

- 起点："“When I was younger, my sister and I wanted to develop a secret code so we could talk without Robert and Susan—the other kids in our apartment—knowing what we were saying. So I went to the library and did some research.” Caleb shrugs a little."（ch24）他在迷宫里认出二进制，也认出妹妹小时候和他编过的暗号。
- 中段："“I do,” Caleb says, trying to control his irritation. He knows how control groups work. “I just didn’t know anything about the greater experiment. I didn’t know that I was the control group.”"（ch31）他发现自己是对照组——他的全部努力原来是别人实验的一部分。
- 落点："“I am not angry with you,” Jeanine says. “Family loyalty is a difficult thing to shake. No matter how much we all insist ‘faction before blood,’ a mantra is not a reality.”"（ch48）Jeanine 说「派系先于血缘」只是一句口号；而 Caleb 把这句口号换成了另一句更硬的「我要守约」。
- 他不是坏人，他是那种把「我会守约」当成德性、于是可以把枪口对准任何人的人。

**Evelyn Eaton**——

- 起点："“But there’s another kind,” she says. “Six years ago, a few of us decided we didn’t have to live that way. We came together, and started building something better, right under the five factions’ noses.”"（ch15）她讲六年前几个人如何在五个派系眼皮底下建起另一个组织。
- 中段："“There’s something else,” Tris says. “Evelyn told me if I needed to persuade you, I should tell you that her ‘dream’ is about to come true. It sounded like she was referring to something specific.”"（ch49）她托女儿给儿子带话，用的是「我的梦想就要成真了」这种句子。
- 落点："“I’m not sure what you think you’ll accomplish as a goddamn puppet of Jeanine Matthews,” I say. “But what you’re not going to do is kill my father right in front of me. So stop pretending that’s what’s going to happen.”"（ch61）她被儿子当面叫作 Jeanine 的傀儡，而她确实在为别人的剧本做事。
- 她要的自由是真的，她要的复仇也是真的，两样东西她从来没有分开过。
