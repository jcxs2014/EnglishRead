# 《Pachinko》— 金句精选

> **"History has failed us, but no matter."**
> — Book I 开篇语，Min Jin Lee 的历史宣言

---

> **"You are my girl; you are my dear girl."**
> — Hansu，对 Sunja 说的话；贯穿全书，Sunja 始终无法释怀

---

> **"For a woman, the man you marry will determine the quality of your life completely. A good man is a decent life, and a bad man is a cursed life—but no matter, always expect suffering, and just keep working hard."**
> — Gombo 警告 Sunja，1932年朝鲜女性的生存哲学

---

> **"A woman's lot is to suffer."**
> — 哥伦比亚农妇，电视节目"Other Lands"，1979年；大阪病房里 Yangjin、Sunja、Kyunghee 三代女性共鸣

---

> **"Of course, the worst one is the tax on the mediocre."**
> — Kazu 对 Solomon 说；日本社会"平庸税"的哲学

---

> **"Everyone wants to die sometimes, nee?"**
> — Hana 对 Solomon 说；她对死亡的坦诚

---

> **"I don't want to live. I'm ready to die."**
> — Hana，临终前对 Solomon 说

---

> **"Name one of your little girls after me, nee? Because you see, I will not have any."**
> — Hana 请求 Solomon 用她的名字命名未来的女儿；无法生育后的遗愿

---

> **"In Japan, you're either a rich Korean or a poor Korean, and if you are a rich Korean, there's a pachinko parlor in your background somewhere."**
> — Kazu 对 Solomon 说；揭示在日朝鲜人的经济现实

---

> **"I sent you to those American schools so that no one would—no one is going to look down at my son."**
> — Mozasu，不想让 Solomon 做 pachinko；父亲的阶层焦虑

---

> **"Mozasu created spaces where grown men and women could play pinball for money. And yet we played on, because we had hope that we might be the lucky ones. Pachinko was a foolish game, but life was not."**
> — Etsuko 对 pachinko 的反思；全书主题的隐喻

---

> **"The people you loved, they were always there with you, she had learned."**
> — 叙事者评论，Sunja 在丈夫墓前的感悟

---

> **"May I get you something to drink? I have a thermos of tea in the cottage. It is not very fine tea, but it is warm."**
> — 墓地園丁，对 Sunja 说；全书最后一个对话；简单的善意

---

> **"Noa loved to read. The best. He loved to read."**
> — Sunja 回忆 Noa；母亲对儿子的最后记忆
