# The Payback Plan 情感节点（10 节点）

> 节点编号全书连续不重置。每节点：叙事概括 + 2-3 句关键引语（逐字取自 text/ 提取件）。

## 节点一：暴风雪圆桌（ch01）

奥黑尔机场，暴风雪+生日+四年旧伤的 Paige 接过粉色传单，与三个陌生女人拼桌。两小时香槟、四场惨案轮讲，Sienna 提议"去整别人家的前男友"，Bella 一条短信得到 Olly 的 "fine"——复仇同盟在举杯中成立。

> ‘To just desserts,’ Astrid declared and they tapped glasses.
> ‘Now,’ Paige said, ‘tell me more about this Olly.’

## 节点二：姜黄风暴进门（ch02）

Oliver 在《虎胆龙威》"坏蛋得到报应"的定格帧里开门，迎来迟到大半天的彩色风暴。仓鼠、标语 T 恤、一场"是不是圣诞电影/是不是爱情片"的辩论——他的暂停键人生第一次被人按了快进。

> It was as if the north wind had dumped her on his doorstep like some kind of ginger Mary Poppins.
> ‘He’s doing it for the woman he loves.’ She crossed her arms as if resting her case. ‘Romance.’

## 节点三：厨房战争（ch03-04）

"Operation kitchen chaos" 与 AC/DC 叫醒服务开场，vegan 谎言当场挂账两个月；她的Arsenal 磁贴精准命中他的足球信仰，而他的回应是洗碗、借车。复仇者的"混蛋账单"第一次对不上账。

> Paige’s payback plan was all about death by a thousand paper cuts rather than one big climatic event.
> The fact he’d been nothing but polite and accommodating, despite being supremely irritated, was exceptionally irksome.

## 节点四：传记开闸（ch05-08）

十万英镑传记委托被撕碎，Paige 的 "Welcome to the real world" 与他的 "I’m prioritising the script" 对撞后，她以 VA 全套接管工程。红围巾口述开场——机场人群里那点"彩色渐渐远去"的背影，把父子两代的爱无能摊上桌面。

> ‘He had this scarf.’ Oliver murmured eventually. ‘A red scarf.’
> ‘I loved my father but nowhere near as much as he loved himself.’

## 节点五：Oscar 落地（ch08-09）

WI 演讲局让 Oliver 交出"人人想听的版本"，也让奥斯卡第一次被放下、被触摸、被六岁孩子带去学校。夜里三杯父亲的干邑下肚，他把奖杯塞进了仓鼠笼——"His father would be pissed. And somehow that felt extra good."

> People asked politely if they could touch and hold the Oscar for their photos and he nodded amiably.
> His father would be pissed. And somehow that felt extra good.

## 节点六：楼梯吻与 Don’t（ch09）

"You’ve been a real godsend, Paige Barker" 刺中她的良心，他在她笑容关闭前吻了进去。她回应了——然后推开："Don’t. Don’t do that again. It can’t happen again." 第一次叫他 Olly，第一次两人都听到了心跳。

> So, he leaned in and kissed her, sliding in before they shut for good.
> ‘I mean it, Olly. It can’t happen again.’

## 节点七：送狗与"我们"（ch11-12）

Casper 的主人 Sheila 上门，两个假装不难过的人哭成一对。门口拥抱、鸭子路过，"our dog" 的口误让 Oliver 第一次尝到 "our" 的发音——家庭语法在失去中诞生。

> Kneeling, he nuzzled the top of Casper’s head and whispered, ‘Thanks for the beach walks. You’re a good dog.’
> ‘Maybe it’s a sign from the universe? Takes away our dog, gives us a duck.’
> Oliver liked how that sounded a little too much.

## 节点八：黑屏之夜（ch13）

Pinocchio 放完的一瞬，房间交给黑暗。她覆上他抚着空沙发垫的手，四年的自我禁欲在"下一次呼吸"的迫切里终结——"I never want to be anywhere else" 的失言，被她存进 "Future Paige" 的保险柜。

> Without thinking, Paige slid her hand across and placed it over the top of his.
> ‘Fuuuck,’ Oliver panted into her neck. ‘I never want to be anywhere else.’

## 节点九：头条爆炸与删除键（ch16-17）

沙滩之吻被狗仔拍成头条，她的四年噩梦重演。争吵升级——他骂她 passive、她删掉他两个月的心血演示"乘以一千"的感觉——摔门而出，心碎成渣，书名在章末兑现："The payback was on her."

> Redondo’s Runaway Groom Gets Sandy with Buxom Beach Babe
> ‘Take that feeling and multiply it by a thousand and you might just be in my ballpark.’
> Her heart crumbling into thousands of tiny pieces.

## 节点十：五百英里（ch18-19→Epilogue）

真相法庭开庭：charade 清单、鼓掌送客、两周黑暗沙发。她的道歉信落款 "Paige (the real one)"，宣布将以倡导者身份站出来；他连夜要来地址，把 HAMSTER FACTS 反装成 LOVE FACTS，用五百英里把她追回——Epilogue 里书稿付印、献词致谢"阴谋"、Sheila 归还 Casper。

> ‘You got what you wanted. Now you can leave.’
> To Bella, Sienna and Astrid – thank you. Without your scheming I never would have met the love of my life.
> ‘Reckon he’s found his next forever person,’ Sheila said, her eyes on Paige. ‘Don’t you?’
> Paige nodded and smiled. Casper had. And so had she.
