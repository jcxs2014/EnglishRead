## 引语块

> "I couldn't hear a word they were saying—all I could hear was the loud beating of my own heart."

Maggie 在 Carbone 看到 Cole 时的反应。创伤记忆的身体反应——听觉变窄，只听到自己的心跳。

---

> "There was a terror roaring through my body—a dizzying, red-hot alarm swirling inside my veins."

Maggie 描述 PTSD 发作的身体体验。文字本身就带有创伤的质感——混乱、沸腾、红色警报。

---

> "For five years, I had worked tirelessly in therapy to alleviate the unfounded guilt that mounted in regard to Cole."

Maggie 花了五年治疗来消除关于 Cole 的内疚感。注意"unfounded"——她知道这内疚是没有根据的，但她还是花了五年才处理它。

---

> "Did he do something to you?" "Who?"

Raini 问 Maggie，Maggie 反问"谁？"——她假装不知道 Raini 在问谁。这展示了 Maggie 仍然在隐藏这件事。

---

> "I've heard things about Cole. From my older sister's best friend."

Raini 的话。这说明 Cole Wyan 不是一个孤立的侵犯者——他有一个模式，有多个受害者。Raini 的姐姐的朋友可能也是其中之一。

---

> "She shuffled in her beautiful pumps with a fragile brokenness, as if her road hadn't been all rainbows and sunshine."

Maggie 观察 Raini 的细节。Raini 也有她的伤痛——每个在好莱坞工作的女人都有自己的故事。

---

> "We both held on to each other, acknowledging some sort of different but shared nightmare."

两个女人的相拥。不同的噩梦，但共同的体验。这是全书中关于女性团结最有力的一刻。

---

> "In Asher's hold I felt innocent, and this truth would bring me into reality."

Maggie 解释为什么她不告诉 Asher。她在 Asher 那里感到"无辜"——而 Cole 的事会把她拉回现实。她害怕的不是 Asher 会怎么看她，而是她会怎么看自己。

---

## 句型分析

**"The paparazzi were camped outside Thompson Street, and I was the other half of their target."**

"target"这个词在这句话里有双重含义——一方面是狗仔队的目标，另一方面是 Cole 的目标。Maggie 一直活在某种被猎取的状态中。
