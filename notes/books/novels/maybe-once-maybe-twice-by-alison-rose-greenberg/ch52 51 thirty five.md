## 引语块

> "Asher's face silenced me. It was darker than I'd ever seen it."

Asher 听到 Cole Wyan 的事时的反应。不是蓝光，是愤怒的黑。这是 Maggie 第一次看到 Asher 愤怒。

---

> "I don't want to hear how you ran to someone else, unless this is your way of trying to tell me that you don't want to be with me—in which case, please spare me the details."

Asher 说的话。他不需要知道 Maggie 和 Garrett 做了什么——他只需要知道她是否要和他在一起。这是 Asher 的成熟：他不占有，他只是确认。

---

> "If I'm not the arms you want to run into—if I'm not your person—this needs to end."

Asher 的话。这是他最脆弱的表态。他不是要求 Maggie 留下来，他是在确认：她是选择留下来，还是选择结束。

---

> "You're my person."

Maggie 对 Asher 说的话。简短、清晰。这是全书 Asher 等了 21 年的一句话。

---

> "I don't think I ever stopped loving you."

Asher 对 Maggie 说的话。这句话在 Maggie 说完"I really love you"之后。Casual，但意义深远。Asher 一直在等 Maggie——他从未停止过。
