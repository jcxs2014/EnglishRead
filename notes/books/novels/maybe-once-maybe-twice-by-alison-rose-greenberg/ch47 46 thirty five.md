## 引语块

> "In the fairy tale, the villain doesn't profit off the underdog until her dying day. The villain doesn't get to take credit for discovering the underdog."

Maggie 对整个情况的内心反应。Cole 作为villain，偷走了她的发现 credit，还要永远 profit。这不公平，但这是现实。

---

> "Cole Wyan had stifled my career five years ago, and he was about to do it again."

Maggie 意识到历史在重演。五年前 Cole 摧毁了她，现在他又要再做一次。

---

> "My rock was crumbling in South Florida—I was without a lifeline, crumbling in New York City."

Maggie 描述她的困境。Summer 在佛罗里达处理离婚，无法接电话，Maggie 在纽约独自崩溃。两个 Rocks 都在崩塌。

---

> "I felt my finger dial a name on my iPhone's 'Favorites.'"

Maggie 打电话给 Garrett。注意"favorites"——Garrett 是她 favorites 里的人。在最崩溃的时刻，她求助的不是 Asher，是 Garrett。这是本能反应。

---

## 结构亮点

**Cole 的手段**：Cole 利用 Maggie 五年前签的 release agreement 来控制她。他发现她要成功了，就故意发行这首歌来与 Bex 竞争。这说明他一直在监视 Maggie 的职业生涯。
