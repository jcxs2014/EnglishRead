# You Were Never Not Mine 情感节点

## 节点 1：Stroll 重逢（ch05）

Sinclair 在 Alpha Squared 派对上直面四年没见的霸凌者 August。他没认出她，她也没报名。她想走，他问为什么走——电流从第一句对白就接通了。

> "Why are you leaving?"

> "I doubt that."

## 节点 2：霸凌真相（ch08）

Flashback 揭晓高中线：14 岁的她，18 岁的他，外号"flat-chested little freak"，拽头发+言语羞辱。他说这是"enemies thing"的乐趣，她回"I hate you"后转身，他没追——"disappointed"是她当时的心情，也是全书的起点。

> "Flat-chested little freak."

> "It’s kind of fun, right? This whole enemies thing we’ve got going on?"

## 节点 3：身份揭穿（ch09）

August 从唇膏留言倒查出 Sinclair 就是当年那个 freshman，对着镜子承认"She is sin personified"。忘记比霸凌更伤人——他连恨的对象都记不住，而她记了四年。

> "She is sin personified and how did I let her slip by me in the halls of Lancaster Prep without noticing?"

## 节点 4：Bathroom 转折（ch17）

Suite 派对洗手间：他跟进去，她失去第一次（手指），哭着求他继续，他事后甩下一句"Get yourself cleaned up"走人。St. worthy 的混蛋行径，但"A saint?"的反问说明他从没装过好人。

> "Get yourself cleaned up."

> "Of course not. Who do you think I am? A saint?"

## 节点 5：初夜交付（ch40-41）

从"terrified"到"on top"，双视角写同一夜：她怕疼怕被抛弃，他怕弄疼她怕太快。从"Breathe"到"Don’t be scared"，再到"Whatever you want"——权力在此完成交接。

> "Don’t be scared, Sin. I’m going to make it good for you."

> "Whatever you want, Sin, I’ll let you do it. All you gotta do is ask."

## 节点 6：宿舍原谅（ch44-45）

August 杀到宿舍，从 family curse 告白到跪求原谅（"begging"），Sinclair 回以"I forgave you a while ago"。紧接着他 shirtless 开门赶走 Tim——原谅和宣示主权在同一张小床上完成。

> "I’m begging you to forgive me."

> "I forgave you a while ago, I just hadn’t said it out loud yet."

## 节点 7：带回家官宣（ch47-48）

"Come home with me"炸弹+ Porsche +耳语"my wife"，到 Lancaster 豪宅门口"My girlfriend"官宣，再到书房对父亲"marry her"。求婚路线图三级跳，Sinclair 却在审计"team vs love"。

> "My girlfriend."

> "I’m going to marry her,"

## 节点 8：大吵与玫瑰（ch49-51）

Iris 的"确认 intentions"警告引爆卧室大吵（"Are your intentions true?"），摔门后 Whit 早餐开导（"I hated her"平行史），玫瑰房里标题句落地："You were never not mine"。从摔门到告白只隔一章。

> "Are your intentions true? For me?"

> "You were never not mine, Sinclair Miller. You belong to me."

## 节点 9：晚宴大战与门后（ch53-55）

岳母 Jennifer 开火，August 拍桌（"marry your daughter"），岳母离席，Sinclair 选择留下。门后夜话："You belong to me"对"I love you"，再以"All I want is you"收尾。原生家庭线杀青。

> "I am the man who’s going to marry your daughter one day"

> "All I want is you,"

## 节点 10：毕业与前传（ch56-57）

五月毕业宴：Rafe/Tim 官宣，"mellowed out"盖章，镜面口红变祝福，"Forever"落版。前传倒回食堂：14 岁的她，lightning bolt，"What’s your name again?"没问出口的名字，"She’d better watch out"是全书最后一句。

> "You’ve mellowed out these last six months."

> "She’d better watch out."
