# 《Chosen by Monsters》情感节点

**一、砸断鼻子（ch01）**——Doug 拽腕索吻，Tava 把他的脸砸上吧台：
> "I grab the back of his head and slam his face into the bar. He comes up howling, blood pouring from his nose. "You broke my fucking nose!""（ch01） 她为这件事被停职、被追租、被逼到 $43.18 的地板上——全书从一次正当自卫的代价开始。

**二、黑信封（ch01）**——蓝色圆珠笔三个词「来北方吧，孩子」：
> "On the back, someone has written three words in blue ballpoint, in small, slanted handwriting. Come north, kiddo. I know that handwriting. It takes me a minute, standing there under the kitchen light with Billy Bob snoring on his bed by the fridge, but I know it. It's been on a birthday card every year of my life."（ch01） 一封没有回信地址的请柬，把退无可退的她拽向波特兰。她是被邀请的，也是没得选的。

**三、掌心亮银（ch02）**——打掉下药者的杯子之后，
> "Then pain shoots through my right palm. It’s like grabbing a pan straight out of the oven. I gasp and turn my hand over. Thin silver lines are spreading under my skin, branching from the center of my palm toward my fingers and glowing faintly."（ch02） 剧痛从右掌炸开，银线在皮下分叉——她被这栋楼识别为守誓人，而她还以为那是一家夜店。

**四、獠牙与清单作废（ch03）**——
> "“Most of the people who come to this Club aren’t human,” he says. “Neither are the owners. I’m a vampire.” I laugh. It comes out short and too loud. Lenart just looks at me. He doesn’t seem offended. If anything, he seems patient, like he’s watched people laugh at this before and knows how it ends. “Okay,” I say. “Sure. And I’m—” He smiles. His canines lengthen as I watch. They slide down past his other teeth, smooth and white and sharp, until there’s no mistaking what they are."（ch03） 她花一夜编好的「合理化清单」当场作废；条件摊牌、四男握手——她从酒保变成了有誓约在身的人。

**五、冷库的十八分钟（ch04）**——
> "The cold gets into me in stages. First it's just cold, the kind you can be annoyed about. Then it starts to hurt. My ears burn. My fingers go stiff, then clumsy, then white, and when I try to make a fist, they won't close all the way. The shivering starts somewhere in my back and spreads until my teeth are knocking together so hard I bite my tongue. I look at my phone. Ten forty-four. The interior release on a walk-in isn't supposed to fail. That's the whole point of it."（ch04） 零度、死锁、无人听见——Merek 破门
> "Merek fills the doorway, his horns nearly brushing the frame. His eyes find me, and whatever he sees on my face makes his expression go hard. "Jesus, Tara." He steps inside, and the heat rolling off him cuts through the freezing air. "Why didn't you use the emergency release?" "I tried." My teeth are chattering so hard I can barely get the words out. "It doesn't work." His gaze snaps to the red knob beside the door. He reaches over and presses it. The plunger slides inward without catching anything. He tries it again. This time, his jaw tightens."（ch04） 后膝上焐手的「Lucky me」
> "“No.” His thumb moves slowly over the back of my hand. “Lucky me.” The smile slips from my face. He's looking at my mouth, and I'm close enough to see the faint orange glow beneath the skin of his throat. One of his arms is around my waist, his other hand still covering mine against his chest. I'm sitting sideways across his thighs, with my knees tucked against the arm of the chair. All I'd have to do is lean up. And holy shit, I want to."（ch04）：她第一次被照顾，也第一次看见有人为她的安危失守。

**六、被拆穿的谎言（ch05）**——
> "“You told me The Nines,” he says. “I know what I told you.” “Did you check in?” “Not yet. I was going to get a nap first, and then I was going to drive over and...” “Tara.” He doesn't raise his voice. He just says my name, and I stop, because the rest of that sentence wasn't going to help me, and we both know it."（ch05） 「你 told 我 The Nines。」「入住办了吗？」——Merek 的温柔火力压制让她无处还手，她第一次承认需要一个睡觉的地方：
> "“You don't have to do four nights.” He spreads his hand on the roof of the car. “I have a house with four bedrooms, and I sleep in one of them. Nobody's charging you rent. Nobody's keeping a tab.” “I don't want charity.” “Call it whatever you want, Tara. The bed's empty, and you need somewhere to sleep.” “I met you yesterday.” “I know.”"（ch05）

**七、「做给我看」（ch06）**——四人齐答「我们全部」之后
> "“Okay,” I say. “Which one of you is it?” Nobody answers right away. Then Lenart says, quietly, “All of us.” Merek doesn't say anything. He just looks at me from the stove, steady, with the lines on his forearms glowing. Tav nods once, slowly, so his horns dip. William, by the door, says, “Yes.”"（ch06），她的回应是把命运翻译成绩效考核：
> "I sit back. Fate, apparently, has handed me four monsters and a membership card. My whole life, people have been handing me things and expecting me to be grateful. A bad shift. A worse manager. A bill. “Fine,” I say. “Fate thinks you're all mine. That doesn't mean I know a damn thing about any of you.” I look around the table, at each of them. “You want me? Show me.” The kitchen goes quiet. The bacon grease pops once in the pan. Merek reaches over and turns off the burner without looking at it."（ch06） 随后 Lenart 的初吻
> "“Tara,” he says. Not Ms. Iverson. “May I?” I take a fistful of his shirt and pull. He comes down to me, and his mouth is on mine. He starts out careful. One hand goes to my jaw, cool fingers along the side of my face, his thumb under my chin to tip it up. The other settles at my waist, over the knot in Merek's shirt. He kisses me slowly, as if he has all the time in the world, and he does. Then I kiss him back."（ch06）与她的「不收回」
> "I'm shaking. I'm not ashamed of it. I hold on to his shirt and look at what I've done to him, the most controlled man I've ever met, with his hair out of place and his mouth wet and his fangs out in someone else's kitchen. It's very satisfying. He lets go of my hair slowly. He straightens the knot at my hip with two fingers, as if he's the one who messed it up, and steps back. His fangs slide up again. It takes him longer than it did in his office."（ch06）——她第一次把欲望说成动作而不羞耻。

**八、石碑与松结（ch07）**——石碑六行
> "I come by my own choice. I may leave by my own choice, at any moment. A refusal is an ending, and I will honor it at once. No one who cannot choose has chosen. I will not bend another’s will by glamour, voice, scent, or blood. I will keep the rules of this house, and its keepers will hold me to them."（ch07）与 Lenart 私室的可挣脱之结
> "“See this knot?” he says. “You could slip out of it in a second. You’re choosing to stay, and I want you to remember that.” He hooks one finger through the silk between my wrists. “Do you understand?” “Yes.” “Tell me how it feels.” I look down at my hands, tied with a vampire’s necktie, and my whole body goes hot. “It feels like I’m letting you,” I say. “And I like it.”"（ch07）：同一种哲学在制度与肌肤上各写一遍——先给退路，才配谈留下。

**九、「我不再等了」（ch08）**——追猎与初咬
> "I back up one step. Then another. He comes forward exactly as many steps as I go back, no more, matching me, until my shoulders touch the wall beside the library door and there's nowhere else to go. He puts one hand flat on the wall beside my head. Not touching me. Just there. "Caught," he says."（ch08）之后，Lenart 的自白
> "“No.” His hand stops moving. “I stopped expecting you,” he says. “A hundred years ago. I'd had three hundred years of looking, and I decided I was done. That it wasn't going to happen for me, and I'd be better off not waiting for it. And I was. I was fine.” He's quiet. “And then you walked into my club and told a poisoner to stop.” I lift my head and look at him. All that calm. All that control, that perfect patience I've been teasing him about for four days. I thought it came from being sure of everything."（ch08）把四百年的耐心翻面：不是笃定，是放弃后重拾——而她的回答是「抱歉我来晚了」。

**十、212 间房（ch09）**——双订危机的裁定之夜
> "Then I go and tell the trolls they're staying right where they are, and the father of the groom bursts into tears, which I wasn't expecting. He's at least eight feet tall and has a face like a landslide, and he pulls a handkerchief the size of a pillowcase out of his jacket and blows his nose on it and tells me his son has been in love with this girl since they were forty. "Forty?" "Years old," he says, wounded. "Very young. Too young, his mother thought." "He's lucky," I say, and he cries harder."（ch09），Tav 在码头说出他的全部孤独
> "“That troll wedding,” he says. “That's two hundred and twelve.” “Two hundred and twelve what?” “Weddings I've set up a room for.” He turns the water bottle. “Anniversaries. Bachelorette parties. First dates, if they're big.” His ears tip back, then forward again. “I'm a lot better at building the room than going on the date.” He says it like a joke, and drinks his water. Two hundred and twelve rooms. I think about him carrying iron tables down the hall tonight for somebody else's party, and I don't laugh."（ch09）——她无意触到牛头人的角，第一次「先问再碰」
> "I snatch my hand back. "Oh my God. Did I hurt you? I'm sorry, there was a scrape, I wasn't thinking..." "No." His voice has gone very rough. He's breathing hard through his nose, and the brass ring trembles. "You didn't hurt me." "Then what..." "Horns," he says. He clears his throat, and it doesn't help. "They're. Sensitive.""（ch09）：这段感情以同意语法开场。

**十一、切断了（ch09）**——
> "He reaches out and touches one of the bolts holding the crossbar to the leg, and it shifts under his finger. It shouldn't. It's as thick as my thumb. He leans closer, then reaches up and turns on the work light over the frame. In the hard white light, I can see the bolt clearly. A fresh cut runs most of the way through the bolt, the exposed steel bright under the work light. He checks the next bolt. Same thing. And the next."（ch09） 吊装架顶梁螺栓的新切口：有人用了工具。浪漫章被一刀切冷——从这一刻起，每一扇门、每一根缆都可能是武器。

**十二、档案库与天空（ch10）**——第二次被反锁
> "I'm not stupid. I've been in this building a week. I've been locked in one room already, and I spent four days telling myself it was an old door and bad linkage, and last night Tav put his finger on a bolt somebody cut most of the way through. A door that won't open from the inside isn't an accident in this building anymore. Not twice. Somebody did this. To me. Again."（ch10），William 拆门救人
> "William stands in the opening. His wings are folded tight against his back, the tips brushing the ceiling, because the corridor is too narrow for him to spread them. His hands are stone, all the way up past his wrists to the middle of his forearms, gray and grainy and huge, his claws hooked and cracked at the tips where they bit into the steel. There's concrete dust on his chest and in his hair. If you were the person who did this, I think, you'd want to be very, very far away from him right now."（ch10）后带她上屋顶
> "They go out, and out. His shoulders shift, and the muscles all down his back move under the gray skin, and the wings unfold in long sections, like something being unrolled, until they're wider than the water tower, wider than any room in the Club.""（ch10）：「我从没因为想给谁看而带人上来」
> "There's no sound at all. No flapping. No strain. We just hang there, high over the Willamette, with the Steel Bridge and the Broadway Bridge and the Fremont far off to the north all lit up below us, so far down they look like jewelry laid out on dark cloth. The wind's gone quiet. I can hear his breathing. I can hear mine. I look up only to catch him watching me instead of the skyline."（ch10）——同一个夜晚，囚笼与天空。

**十三、「Specifically you, Merek」（ch11）**——冷库复盘与烤奶酪
> "Merek sets a block of cheddar on the counter. “Grilled cheese?” “Are you even listening?” “Every word.” “Because I'm so mad I could scream.” “I know, Trouble.” He puts a pan on the stove. “That's why I'm making you dinner.”"（ch11）之后，她在投毒现场说出了单选
> "“I want the guy who rescued me and my dog. Who fixed a ramp on Tuesday and didn't mention it. Who keeps making me laugh when I'd rather throw a chair through a window.” I swallow. “And I like your tail. And the horns. And that you're too hot to sleep under a blanket. I want that too. Specifically you.” The grin he's been wearing since my boot hit his couch is gone. The ember lines along his throat go gold. “Say that again,” he says. “Which part?” “Specifically.” “Specifically you, Merek.”"（ch11）：热量许可实验（你说 when 就停在哪
> "“Tell me when,” he says. “Not yet.” It climbs another degree. My pulse is going hard under his palm, and I know he can feel it. “When,” I say. The heat stops climbing. It holds right there, steady, not a degree more. He doesn't move his hand. “I'll keep it right here,” he says. “You say when, and that's where it stops. You can trust me with that.”"（ch11））把 consent 写进了体温。

**十四、Dez 的信（ch12）**——
> "If you're reading this, you came, and you're sitting at my desk, and this drawer stuck on you the way it's stuck on me for thirty years. Good. It means you're still here. I don't have time to explain all of it, and I'm not sure I'd get it right if I did. Don't trust anyone in a hurry for you to leave. And whatever they do, don't miss a night. I'm sorry I wasn't a better uncle. You'd have been a hell of a bartender here. D."（ch12） 抽屉底的遗书：「别信任何催你离开的人，无论如何别缺一晚。」——她死去的叔叔隔着死亡递来第二张邀请函，也是第二道锁。

**十五、It doesn't come（ch19）**——Lenart 视角的悬崖
> "Her happiness has my full attention. Later, I'll have to admit to the council and to myself that I'd stopped watching for danger. She has the glass raised toward the bar, and the stairs behind it, and the stone. The green dress. The silver on both arms, nearly to the elbow, catching the light every time she moves. Two hours ago she signed her name under Dez's in a hand that shook, and now two hundred members of this house have just said his name back to her, and she's smiling at all of them."（ch19）：她的银纹转黑
> "Then I see her arm. The line nearest her left wrist has gone dark. It's black. As I watch, the one beside it darkens, from the wrist upward. Then the next. I look at her right arm, and it's happening there too. Two hours. She's had them for two hours. "Odette," I say again, and I can hear the panic in my voice. I've stopped breathing. I don't need to. I don't start again."（ch19）、结界熄灭
> "I wouldn't know it if I couldn't see it. The carvings in the brick by the front doors, the ones nobody notices unless the wards are lit, flare silver the way they did on the Solstice, all at once. Then they go dark. The music is still playing. For a second, nobody understands. The brass in the nearest arch lights and dies. Then the crown molding along the left wall. Section by section, it's coming down the room toward the bar. Toward her."（ch19）、脉息停止
> "Her lips move behind the mask. Odette lifts it an inch. I lean down until my ear is almost against her mouth. "I didn't miss a single night," she says. Twenty-eight nights. The freezer, and the vault, and the fog in the suite, and every midnight after. I can't say any of it. "I know," I say. "I know you didn't.""（ch19）——全书在她「我一晚都没缺过」的履约申报里坠落，等待 Book 2。
