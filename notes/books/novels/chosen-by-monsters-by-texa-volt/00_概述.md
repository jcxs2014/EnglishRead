# 《Chosen by Monsters》概述

## 全书梗概

**弗雷斯诺的酒保 Tara Iverson 的日子已经见底。** 常客 Doug 在吧台拽住她的手腕强索吻，她把他的脸砸上了吧台——换来的不是正义，是一张停职通知：
> ""And you broke his face on my bar, in front of witnesses." Rick taps the form. "You're suspended through Sunday. When you come back, Kayla's got your Fridays." Friday is my best night. Friday is half my rent. He knows that."（ch01） 回到家，门上贴着三天搬离通知，银行余额 $43.18，狗的手术账单六千美元还没还完。就在这一夜的最底处，一封黑信封从波特兰寄到：
> "On the back, someone has written three words in blue ballpoint, in small, slanted handwriting. Come north, kiddo. I know that handwriting. It takes me a minute, standing there under the kitchen light with Billy Bob snoring on his bed by the fridge, but I know it. It's been on a birthday card every year of my life."（ch01） 而第二天早晨，一通律师电话告诉她：叔叔 Desmond Hale 两周前去世了，把「怪物俱乐部」的股份留给了她——条件是夏至之夜前亲自到场。

**俱乐部第一夜，她撞进了另一个世界。** 有角的男人弯腰过拱门、红色漆门后传出音乐——而一位炭灰西装的客人在一个翡翠裙女人的杯子里下了药：
> "His hand passes over her glass. A tiny vial flashes between his fingers, and he tips it. Then it’s back in his pocket, and he’s drifting a few steps back toward the corridor as if he’s waiting for a friend."（ch02） 她扑过去打掉杯子，掌心灼出银线印记，全场符纹应声亮起——守誓人的血在她不知情的时刻接管了这栋楼的结界。吧台尽头那个全场静止的男人接住了坠落的她：
> "A cold hand closes around my bare upper arm and pulls me back, hard, out of the aisle and against a chest. An arm comes across my front and holds me there. The poisoner goes past close enough that I feel the air move, and two security guards take him down a few feet later in a crash of chairs."（ch02）

**第二天，办公室摊牌给出规则。** Lenart 直认自己是吸血鬼、獠牙作证：
> "“Most of the people who come to this Club aren’t human,” he says. “Neither are the owners. I’m a vampire.” I laugh. It comes out short and too loud. Lenart just looks at me. He doesn’t seem offended. If anything, he seems patient, like he’s watched people laugh at this before and knows how it ends. “Okay,” I say. “Sure. And I’m—” He smiles. His canines lengthen as I watch. They slide down past his other teeth, smooth and white and sharp, until there’s no mistaking what they are."（ch03） Desmond 是守誓人，死后续着宽限期直到继承人到场——「昨晚有人违约，结界抓了最近的守誓人：你」
> "“Yes. And the wards need an Oathkeeper to anchor them. When Desmond died, they went into what the treaty calls a grace. They kept running on what he’d left, until his heir arrived to take them up.” He pauses. “Last night, a member broke his oath on our floor, and the wards reached for the nearest Oathkeeper they could find.” “Me. Even though I didn’t know any of this existed.” “It doesn’t require your knowledge. Only your blood.” He glances at my hand. “The grace ended the moment you answered.”"（ch03） 条件随之摊牌：每个营业夜必须到场，直到八月一日 Lammas 正式绑定；缺席一晚，份额归密封附录里那个连四人都不知道是谁的名字
> "“If you miss a single operating night before then, you forfeit your share.” His voice doesn’t change. “It passes to whoever is named in a sealed codicil to the will.” “Who’s that?” “None of us know.”"（ch03） 四个男人——吸血鬼 Lenart、恶魔 Merek、牛头人 Tav、石像鬼 William——逐一握手认人，每次触碰都越过人类边界。

**第一夜上岗，她用专业赢回尊严，也被送进了冷库。** 她照章要证件、揪出被调包的酒嘴、顶住巨魔逼酒——然后在数急冻果泥时被反锁在零度冷库：
> "The cold gets into me in stages. First it's just cold, the kind you can be annoyed about. Then it starts to hurt. My ears burn. My fingers go stiff, then clumsy, then white, and when I try to make a fist, they won't close all the way. The shivering starts somewhere in my back and spreads until my teeth are knocking together so hard I bite my tongue. I look at my phone. Ten forty-four. The interior release on a walk-in isn't supposed to fail. That's the whole point of it."（ch04） Merek 破门救人、膝上焐手——「Lucky me」对视差点吻上。午夜她第一次以右掌续住整栋楼：
> "Silver lines open under my skin and run out from my fingers across the stone, along the carvings, and up into the brick of the walls. I hear the building. I don't know how else to say it. The old wood, the pipes, the floor above us full of people, all of it gives a long, low creak, like a house settling for the night. Then the pressure lets go, and the light fades, and it's just a rock under my hand again."（ch04）

**被带回家、被拆穿、被逐一认识。** Merek 在车窗外敲响她睡了两夜的车：
> "“You told me The Nines,” he says. “I know what I told you.” “Did you check in?” “Not yet. I was going to get a nap first, and then I was going to drive over and...” “Tara.” He doesn't raise his voice. He just says my name, and I stop, because the rest of that sentence wasn't going to help me, and we both know it."（ch05） 谎言拆穿后他把她连人带狗抱回西 Hills 木屋——一晚、内锁、无条件。办公室里 Lenart 讲明 fated mate 的机制与她的 fae 血脉
> "“Some of our kind have what we call a fated mate,” he says. “Not all of us. Most never find one. It's a recognition. When you touch the person you're meant for, you know. It's immediate, and it's certain, and it isn't something any of us chooses or controls.”"（ch06），她的回答是一场主动的初吻
> "“Tara,” he says. Not Ms. Iverson. “May I?” I take a fistful of his shirt and pull. He comes down to me, and his mouth is on mine. He starts out careful. One hand goes to my jaw, cool fingers along the side of my face, his thumb under my chin to tip it up. The other settles at my waist, over the knot in Merek's shirt. He kisses me slowly, as if he has all the time in the world, and he does. Then I kiss him back."（ch06）——随后每人一次触碰：热、嗅、石。

**红门之下是誓词，红门之上是追逐。** 石碑六行：「我出于自愿而来」
> "I come by my own choice. I may leave by my own choice, at any moment. A refusal is an ending, and I will honor it at once. No one who cannot choose has chosen. I will not bend another’s will by glamour, voice, scent, or blood. I will keep the rules of this house, and its keepers will hold me to them."（ch07） 她与 Lenart 同声再立；私室里她练习松手——红黄安全词、可一秒挣脱的结。Lenart 的追猎
> "He's not standing like a businessman. He looks ready to chase me no matter which way I might run. His weight is forward, loose and balanced, like an animal that could go in any direction faster than I could follow it. His head is tipped very slightly to one side. His fangs are down, fully down, white in the dim light, and his eyes have gone almost black, and he's watching me the way I've seen a cat watch a moth against a window. Patient. Completely focused. Delighted."（ch08）以「Caught」收束，初咬与「I stopped expecting you」在同一夜交割。

**危机升级，信任升级。** 双订夜她用一间高顶大厅救回两场婚礼
> "Then I go and tell the trolls they're staying right where they are, and the father of the groom bursts into tears, which I wasn't expecting. He's at least eight feet tall and has a face like a landslide, and he pulls a handkerchief the size of a pillowcase out of his jacket and blows his nose on it and tells me his son has been in love with this girl since they were forty. "Forty?" "Years old," he says, wounded. "Very young. Too young, his mother thought." "He's lucky," I say, and he cries harder."（ch09），散场时撞见被工具切断的螺栓
> "He reaches out and touches one of the bolts holding the crossbar to the leg, and it shifts under his finger. It shouldn't. It's as thick as my thumb. He leans closer, then reaches up and turns on the work light over the frame. In the hard white light, I can see the bolt clearly. A fresh cut runs most of the way through the bolt, the exposed steel bright under the work light. He checks the next bolt. Same thing. And the next."（ch09）；档案库二次受困、William 徒手拆门
> "William stands in the opening. His wings are folded tight against his back, the tips brushing the ceiling, because the corridor is too narrow for him to spread them. His hands are stone, all the way up past his wrists to the middle of his forearms, gray and grainy and huge, his claws hooked and cracked at the tips where they bit into the steel. There's concrete dust on his chest and in his hair. If you were the person who did this, I think, you'd want to be very, very far away from him right now."（ch10）后带她上屋顶：「我一百三十年来第一次因为想给谁看而带人上来」
> "There's no sound at all. No flapping. No strain. We just hang there, high over the Willamette, with the Steel Bridge and the Broadway Bridge and the Fremont far off to the north all lit up below us, so far down they look like jewelry laid out on dark cloth. The wind's gone quiet. I can hear his breathing. I can hear mine. I look up only to catch him watching me instead of the skyline."（ch10） 四个触点、三名嫌疑人、Dez 的遗书——「别信任何催你离开的人，无论如何别缺一晚」
> "If you're reading this, you came, and you're sitting at my desk, and this drawer stuck on you the way it's stuck on me for thirty years. Good. It means you're still here. I don't have time to explain all of it, and I'm not sure I'd get it right if I did. Don't trust anyone in a hurry for you to leave. And whatever they do, don't miss a night. I'm sorry I wasn't a better uncle. You'd have been a hell of a bartender here. D."（ch12）

**内鬼落网，但不是答案。** Grady 六年 $81,000 的假发票被三单比对钉死
> "I asked the accountant to pull every payment we've made to them," he says. "Six years. Eighty-one thousand dollars." The payments went to an account Grady controls. The accountant traced it back to him." I look at the invoice on the screen. Kit rolling her eyes every time the ice machine broke. Grady on his knees in front of it on my first night with a flashlight in his teeth. "He's been fighting with that ice machine for a year," I say. "Everybody said so. It just kept breaking." "It kept breaking because nobody fixed it," Tav says."（ch17）——他认偷认伪证却发誓没杀人，而她也看出了破绽：
> "We caught a thief." "Yeah." I pull my knees up in Lenart's chair. "That's what I keep thinking about. He stole for six years and nobody noticed. That's the whole point of stealing. You don't want anybody to look at you." I look at him. "So why would he send me a picture of my car from Dez's window and tell me to go home?" "He says he didn't." "I want to believe him. I don't know if I do." I rub my eyes. He didn't even ask about the picture. It's like it never occurred to him.""（ch17） 小偷要安静，恐怖要观众。Lammas 夜，她在 Varga 夫人与 Tobias 的账单纠纷里做出守誓人首裁
> "“Did either of you swear an oath over this?” I ask. They both shake their heads. Tobias looks offended by the idea. "Then it's a booking," I say. "So it goes by the house rules." I turn to Mrs. Varga. "The room is yours. Mr. Reed gave it to you, and it's his to give. Dom comes with the room, because that's how we book it, so he stays." I turn to Tobias. "But a room isn't a tab. You never promised to buy thirty people drinks, and nobody gets to decide that for you after. The tab's Mrs. Varga's.""（ch18），午夜在 D. Hale 的签名序列下签下自己的名字：
> "“Sign,” Lenart says. His voice isn't steady, which I've almost never heard. He holds out a pen. The ledger's open to this year. There's a column of signatures in the same cheap black ballpoint, D. Hale, D. Hale, D. Hale, going down the page, and then nothing since spring. I sign my full name under his. Tara Iverson. My hand is shaking, and it looks terrible. Ashby writes in her notebook. "Oathkeeper," she says. "So recorded.""（ch18）

**庆祝的香槟里，夏至重来了一遍。** Lenart 视角的最后一章：
> "Tara looks down into the glass. She lifts her free hand and touches her throat, two fingers at the hollow of it, and I have seen that gesture before. On the Solstice. A woman in an emerald dress, at a high-top on the main aisle, saying I only had a sip. Tara looks up at me. Her lips are losing their color. "Lenart," she says. "It's the same.""（ch19） 结界沿大厅一段段熄灭——朝她
> "I wouldn't know it if I couldn't see it. The carvings in the brick by the front doors, the ones nobody notices unless the wards are lit, flare silver the way they did on the Solstice, all at once. Then they go dark. The music is still playing. For a second, nobody understands. The brass in the nearest arch lights and dies. Then the crown molding along the left wall. Section by section, it's coming down the room toward the bar. Toward her."（ch19） 她面罩下的最后一句话是：「我一晚都没缺过。」
> "Her lips move behind the mask. Odette lifts it an inch. I lean down until my ear is almost against her mouth. "I didn't miss a single night," she says. Twenty-eight nights. The freezer, and the vault, and the fog in the suite, and every midnight after. I can't say any of it. "I know," I say. "I know you didn't.""（ch19） 脉息再至，更弱。It doesn't come.

## 三个主题

**一、所有权的代价。** Tara 的每一份「拥有」——工作、狗、俱乐部、四个人——都被同一个敌人标价。她的回答不是退守，是把每一次失去的风险折算成继续前行的成本：
> "But it’s mine. Dez left it to me. And I’m not handing it to some name in a sealed envelope without finding out what it is. “I’ll stay,” I say. Long enough to see what I’m getting into, anyway. “When do I start?”"（ch03） 「我想要我的生活。我刚拿到它。」

**二、同意即安全。** 从石碑六行誓词到安全词、从「你说 when 就停在哪」到「不 invoke 界石而用店规」：
> "Every rule on that wall is about choosing. Coming in by choice. Leaving by choice. Saying no and having it stick. This whole floor, every room and every chain and every polished bolt, was built for people who decided for themselves what they wanted. This morning, four men told me fate had picked them for me. Down here, nobody gets picked. You walk in, and you choose."（ch07） 这本书把 consent 写成了建筑—— hosts 巡场、恢复室、结界的代价——而每一次越界（无论善恶）都要先过 Tara 的许可。

**三、家的底稿是家具。** 踏凳、狗坡道上的螺栓、四卧室里空着的那间、卸货码头的床垫：
> "Half finished. Two steps. The wood's been sanded smooth, and there's a pencil line where the top step will go. "What's this?" "Nothing," Tav says. ""It's a step stool." I glance up at the high shelf with the jars. Tav. Is this for me?" "You can't reach anything in here," he says. "You can't reach anything at the Club, either. I watched you climb the speed rack Friday to get the Bénédictine." "You're building me furniture." "It's a stool, Boss. Don't get emotional." He rubs the back of his neck. "I started it last week.""（ch15） 这本书从不写「我们是一家人」的宣言——它写谁来修坡道、谁把脚放进谁怀里、谁在床头地板收起翅膀。

## 人物弧光

**Tara Iverson**：从 $43.18 与三天搬离通知，到在 D. Hale 签名序列下签下全名。她的弧线不是变得强大——是她终于承认「有可失去的东西」并决定守住：
> "I put both hands flat on the table. "I want all four of you. I don't mean one of you, with the rest as backup. And it's not because of the bond, or because somebody keeps trying to kill me and four bodyguards is convenient. If the building were fine and nobody was cutting cables, I'd still want this. Each of you, and all of us together. I'm choosing it. That's what I wanted to say.""（ch16） 「我喜欢我现在的生活。这才是问题。」

**Lenart**：四百年的自控在两秒内失守（「我不丢两秒」
> "“All of it. Every thought I had. For about two seconds, I didn't know where I was. I didn't know there was a poisoned woman on the floor or a man with too many teeth running straight at you. I knew you, and nothing else.” He turns the glass. “I've been alive for four hundred years, Tara. I don't lose two seconds.”"（ch08）），又用四百年养成的耐心学会「不因 bond 而有权决定」
> "“Yes,” he says again. “So I let go of you. And I walked you to the door, and I asked you to come back in the morning, and I let you walk away down the sidewalk. Which was, I promise you, the hardest thing I've done in a century.” He looks at me. “The bond tells me you're mine. It doesn't tell me I have the right to decide anything for you. I had to learn the difference in about four minutes.”"（ch08）。他的弧线是学会在爱里做学生。

**Merek**：九十一年酒 barender 的热与直，从「我怕我烫到你」到「我不赶我上心的活」
> "“I want to. I really want to. But not today. And not without, like, a plan.” I put my hand on his chest. “We're going to have to work up to you.” He's quiet for a second. Then he laughs. A real laugh, from deep in his chest, so big the bed shakes. “Okay,” he says. “Okay? That's it?” “I'm a contractor, Boss.” His ears are all the way forward. He looks happier than I've ever seen him. “Every big job has phases.” “ “Oh my God.” You don't pour the foundation and frame the roof the same afternoon.”"（ch15）。他的弧线是学会等待。

**Tav**：六十年的修理工、212 间婚礼房——「我擅长造房间，不擅长去约会」
> "“That troll wedding,” he says. “That's two hundred and twelve.” “Two hundred and twelve what?” “Weddings I've set up a room for.” He turns the water bottle. “Anniversaries. Bachelorette parties. First dates, if they're big.” His ears tip back, then forward again. “I'm a lot better at building the room than going on the date.” He says it like a joke, and drinks his water. Two hundred and twelve rooms. I think about him carrying iron tables down the hall tonight for somebody else's party, and I don't laugh."（ch09）。他的弧线是走出工具间。

**William**：一百三十年只看不说，「Downstairs William 说不九个词」——直到他说出「我要无聊的年份」
> "William is the last. He's been watching me since I started talking, and I'm braced for him to say nothing at all, because sometimes that's what he does. "Tara Iverson," he says. "Yeah?" "I don't want you in between emergencies." He holds my eyes. "I want the boring years. I want you on the roof when you're fifty, complaining about the stairs.""（ch16）。他的弧线是开口。
