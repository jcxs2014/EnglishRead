# ch97. The Roof and the Choice

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：2014 年纽约，Addie 和 Luc 在八十四层屋顶酒吧——"选一个人代替他"——她指向一个人——他只是笑——"你变了。"——他把她拉入黑暗。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 —— 从"散步"到"酒吧"到"对话"到"选择"到"黑暗"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Choice（选择）—— 他让她选择 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"请求"到"选择"—— 她指向一个人 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **叙事手法**："THE LOW ROAD" = 低路 = 他的酒吧 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Luc's mood lifts a little as they walk. The night is warm, the moon barely a crescent overhead. His head falls back, and he inhales, breathing in the air as if it were not ripe with summer heat, too many people in too little space. "How long have you been here?" she asks. "I come and go," he says, but she has learned to read the space between his words, and guesses he has been in New York almost as long as she has, lurking like a shadow at her back. She doesn't know where they are going, and for the first time, she wonders if Luc does either, or if he is simply walking, trying to put space between them and the end of their meal. But as they make their way uptown, she feels time folding around them, and she does not know if it's his magic or her memory, but with each passing block, she is storming from him down the Seine. He is leading her away from the sea. She is following him in Florence. They are side by side in Boston, and arm in arm on Bourbon Street. They are here, together, in New York. And she wonders what would have happened if he hadn't said the word. If he hadn't tipped his hand. If he hadn't ruined everything.

Luc 的心情在散步时稍微轻松。夜晚温暖，头顶的月亮几乎是新月。他向后仰头，吸气，呼吸空气好像它没有充满夏天的炎热，太多人在太小的空间。"你在这里多久了？"她问。"我来来去去，"他说，但她已经学会了阅读他话语之间的空间，并猜测他几乎和她一样长在纽约，像影子一样潜伏在她背后。她不知道他们要去哪里，第一次，她想知道 Luc 是否也不知道，或者他是否只是在走，试图在他们和晚餐结束之间放一些空间。但当他们向北走时，她感觉到时间在周围折叠，她不知道是他的魔法还是她的记忆，但每一个街区，她都在塞纳河上从他身边暴风雨。他正带领她离开大海。她在佛罗伦萨跟着他。他们在波士顿并肩，在波旁街手挽手。他们在这里，一起在纽约。她想知道如果他没有说出那个词会发生什么。如果他没有亮出他的手。如果他什么都没有毁掉。

**关键词**：shadow / memory / Seine / Florence / Boston / Bourbon / word

**为什么这样写**："shadow" = 影子 = 他潜伏在她背后 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。"word" = 词 = 那个词 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"他什么都没有毁掉" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 2:** Over the years, Addie has learned many of the city's secrets. But here is one she did not know. It resides not underground, but on a roof. Eighty-four stories up, reached by a pair of elevators, the first one nondescript and rising only to the eighty-first floor. The second, a direct replica of Rodin's Gates of Hell, with its writhing bodies, clawing to escape, takes you the rest of the way. If you have a key. Luc draws the black card from his shirt pocket and slides it into a yawning mouth along the elevator's frame. "Is this one of yours?" she asks as the doors slide open. "Nothing is really mine," he says by way of answer as they step inside. It is a short ascent, three brief floors, and when it stops, the doors open onto an uninterrupted view of the city. The bar's name winds in black letters at her feet. THE LOW ROAD. Addie rolls her eyes. "Was Perdition taken?" "Perdition," he says, eyes sparkling with mischief, "is a different kind of club."

多年来，Addie 学到了城市的许多秘密。但这里有一个她不知道的。它不在地下，而在屋顶。八十四层楼高，由一对电梯到达，第一个不起眼，只到八十一楼。第二个，罗丹地狱之门的直接复制品，有它扭曲的身体，抓着逃跑，带你走完剩下的路。如果你有钥匙。Luc 从衬衫口袋里抽出黑色卡片，沿着电梯框架滑进一张打哈欠的嘴。"这是你的一个吗？"当门滑开时她问。"没有什么真正是我的，"他回答，当他们走进去。这是一个短暂的上升，三个简短的楼层，当它停下时，门打开，看到不间断的城市景色。酒吧的名字在她脚下用黑色字母蜿蜒。低路。Addie 翻白眼。"被诅咒被拿走了吗？""被诅咒，"他说，眼睛闪烁着恶作剧，"是一种不同类型的俱乐部。"

**关键词**：roof / elevators / Rodin / Gates of Hell / key / THE LOW ROAD / Perdition

**为什么这样写**："Gates of Hell" = 地狱之门 = 电梯 = 他的力量 = 她的恐惧 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。"THE LOW ROAD" = 低路 = 他的酒吧 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"被诅咒" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 3:** Renee returns with a bottle of Champagne, a visible film of dust coating the glass. "Dom Perignon, 1959," she explains, holding the bottle out for inspection. "From your private case, Mr. Green." Luc waves his hand, and she opens the bottle, pouring two flutes, the bubbles so small they look like flecks of diamond in the glass. Addie sips, savors the way it sparkles on her tongue. She scans the crowd, filled with the kinds of faces you would recognize, even though you're not sure where you've seen them. Luc points them out to her, those senators, and actors, authors and critics, and she wonders if any of them have sold their soul. If any of them are about to. Addie looks down into her glass, the bubbles still rising smoothly to the surface, and when she speaks, the words are barely more than a whisper, the sound stolen by the chattering crowd. But she knows he is listening, knows he can hear her. "Let him go, Luc."

Renee 拿着一瓶香槟回来，一层可见的灰尘覆盖着玻璃。"Dom Perignon, 1959，"她解释，把瓶子拿出来检查。"来自你的私人酒柜，格林先生。"Luc 挥手，她打开瓶子，倒了两杯，气泡如此小，看起来像玻璃中的钻石碎片。Addie 小口喝，品味它在她舌头上闪烁的方式。她扫视人群，充满你会认出的那种脸，即使你不确定你在哪里见过他们。Luc 向她指出那些参议员，演员，作家和评论家，她想知道他们中是否有人卖掉了灵魂。他们中是否有人要。Addie 低头看着她的杯子，气泡仍然平稳地上升到表面，当她说话时，这些话几乎是低语，声音被喋喋不休的人群偷走。但她知道他在倾听，知道他能听到她。"让他走，Luc。"

**关键词**：Champagne / Dom Perignon / souls / whisper

**为什么这样写**："souls" = 灵魂 = 他们中是否有人卖掉了灵魂 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"whisper" = 低语 = 她低语 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"让他走" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 4:** "Fine." He leans back against the rail and spreads his arms. "Tell me. What do you see in him, this latest human lover?" Henry Strauss is thoughtful, and kind, she wants to say. He is clever, and bright, gentle, and warm. He is everything you're not, But Addie knows she must tread lightly. "What do I see in him?" she says. "I see myself. Not who I am now, perhaps, but who I was, the night you came to rescue me." Luc scowls. "Henry Strauss wanted to die. You wanted to live. You are nothing alike." "It's not that simple." "Isn't it?" Addie shakes her head. "You see only flaws and faults, weaknesses to be exploited. But humans are messy, Luc. That is the wonder of them. They live and love and make mistakes, and they feel so much. And maybe—maybe I am no longer one of them." The words tear through her as she says them, because she knows this much is true. For better or worse. "But I remember," she presses on. "I remember what it's like, and Henry is—" "Lost." "He is searching," she counters. "And he will find his way, if you let him." "If I let him," says Luc, "he would have leapt off a roof." "You don't know that," she says. "You never will, because you intervened." "I am in the business of souls, Adeline, not second chances." "And I am begging you to let him go. You will not give me mine, so give me his, instead."

"好。"他靠在栏杆上，张开双臂。"告诉我。你在他身上看到了什么，这个最新的人类情人？"Henry Strauss 体贴，善良，她想说。他聪明，明亮，温柔，温暖。他是你的一切不是，但 Addie 知道她必须小心行事。"我在他身上看到了什么？"她说。"我看到我自己。也许不是现在的我，而是曾经的我，你来救我的那个晚上。"Luc 皱眉。"Henry Strauss 想死。你想活着。你一点也不像。""没那么简单。""不是吗？"Addie 摇头。"你只看到缺点和错误，被利用的弱点。但人类是混乱的，Luc。这就是他们的奇迹。他们生活，爱，犯错误，他们感觉那么多。也许——也许我不再是他们中的一个。"这些话在她说话时撕裂她，因为她知道这是真的。不管是好是坏。"但我记得，"她继续。"我记得那是什么感觉，Henry 是——""迷失了。""他在寻找，"她反驳道。"他会找到他的路，如果你让他。""如果我让他，"Luc 说，"他会从屋顶跳下去。""你不知道，"她说。"你永远不会，因为你干预了。""我是做灵魂生意的，Adeline，不是第二次机会。""我求你让他走。你不会给我我的，所以给我他的。"

**关键词**：flaws / faults / weaknesses / messy / wonder / searching / second chances

**为什么这样写**："messy" = 混乱 = 人类是混乱的 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"second chances" = 第二次机会 = 不是第二次机会 = 他的选择 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"我求你让他走" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 5:** Luc exhales, and sweeps his hand across the roof. "Choose someone," he says. "What?" He turns her to face the crowd. "Choose a soul to take his place. Pick a stranger. Damn one of them instead." His voice is low and smooth and certain. "There is always a cost," he says gently. "A price must be paid. Henry Strauss bartered his own soul. Would you sell someone else's to have it back?" Addie stares out at the crowded roof, the faces she recognizes and the ones she doesn't. Young and old, together and alone. Are any innocent? Are any cruel? Addie does not know if she can do it—until her hand drifts up. Until she points to a man in the crowd, heart plunging through her stomach as she waits for Luc to let go of her, to step forward, and claim his price. But Luc doesn't move. He only laughs. "My Adeline," he says, kissing her hair. "You have changed more than you think." She feels dizzy and ill as she twists to face him. "No more games," she says. "All right," he says, just before he pulls her into the dark. The roof drops away, and the void surges up around her, swallowing everything but a starless sky, an infinite, violent black. And when it withdraws again an instant later, the world is silent, and the city is gone, and she is alone in the woods.

Luc 呼气，在屋顶上挥手。"选一个人，"他说。"什么？"他转身让她面对人群。"选一个灵魂代替他。挑一个陌生人。诅咒他们中的一个。"他的声音低沉、平滑、确定。"总是有代价的，"他温柔地说。"必须付出代价。Henry Strauss 出卖了自己的灵魂。你会卖掉别人的灵魂来换回它吗？"Addie 盯着拥挤的屋顶，她认识的脸和她不认识的。年轻和年老，在一起和独自。有没有无辜的？有没有残忍的？Addie 不知道她是否能做到——直到她的手飘起。直到她指向人群中的一个人，心脏沉入她的胃，当她等待 Luc 放开她，向前走，索取他的价格。但 Luc 没有动。他只是笑。"我的 Adeline，"他说，吻她的头发。"你变得比你想象的更多。"当她转身面对他时，她感到头晕和恶心。"不要再玩游戏了，"她说。"好吧，"他说，就在他把她拉入黑暗之前。屋顶落下，虚空在她周围升起，吞噬一切除了没有星星的天空，无限的，暴力的黑色。当它再次退去一瞬间，世界沉默了，城市消失了，她独自在树林里。

**关键词**：Choose / stranger / cost / price / innocent / cruel / changed / dark / woods

**为什么这样写**："Choose" = 选择 = 他让她选择 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"changed" = 改变 = 她变了 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"你变得比你想象的更多" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。
