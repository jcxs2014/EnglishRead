> **原句 3:** Background: The subject of the photos remains a topic of intense speculation, given the author's relationship to the source material. The flash has erased all meaningful details, but the medium is what makes the pieces remarkable. In standard photography, long exposure would make it possible to achieve the desired effect of motion, but the Polaroid's fixed shutter speed makes the illusion of movement all the more impressive.

背景：鉴于作者与源材料的关系，照片的主题仍然是激烈猜测的话题。闪光灯已经抹去了所有有意义的细节，但正是媒介使这些作品引人注目。在标准摄影中，长时间曝光将使实现运动效果成为可能，但宝丽来的固定快门速度使运动的幻觉更加令人印象深刻。

**关键词**：subject / speculation / author / relationship / source / flash / erased / details / medium / remarkable / photography / exposure / motion / Polaroid / shutter / speed / illusion / movement / impressive

**为什么这样写**："erased" = 抹去 = 闪光灯已经抹去 = 她的诅咒 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。"illusion" = 幻觉 = 运动的幻觉 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
