# ch86. Three Hundred Years

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：2014 年纽约，Addie 的三百周年纪念日，Henry 用甜甜圈和蜡烛庆祝，他们去洛克威海滩——"我爱你"——"我也爱你。"
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 —— 从"恐惧"到"庆祝"到"爱"到"承诺"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Anniversary（周年纪念）—— 三百年的诅咒 = 三百年的自由；The Beach（海滩）—— 他们的约会 = 她的爱 = 他的爱。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"恐惧"到"接受"—— 她说"我也爱你" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **叙事手法**："heat vs warmth" = 热 vs 温暖 = 激情 vs 满足 = 她的爱 = 他的爱。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** It is just another day. That is what Addie tells herself. It is just a day—like all the others—but of course, it is not. It is three hundred years since she was meant to be married—a future given against her will. Three hundred years since she knelt in the woods, and summoned the darkness, and lost everything but freedom. Three hundred years. There should be a storm, an eclipse. Some way to mark the monument of it. But the day dawns perfect, and cloudless, and blue.

这只是另一天。这是 Addie 告诉自己的。这只是像其他所有日子一样的一天——当然，不是。这是她本该结婚的三百年——一个违背她意愿给予的未来。这是她跪在森林里，召唤黑暗，失去一切除了自由的三百年。三百年。应该有一场风暴，一次日食。某种方式来标记它的纪念碑。但这一天黎明完美，无云，蓝色。

**关键词**：three hundred years / freedom / monument

**为什么这样写**："three hundred years" = 三百年 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由 = 她的力量。"monument" = 纪念碑 = 她的存在 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"三百年" = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由 = 她的力量 = 她的爱。

> **原句 2:** When she opens her hand, the wooden ring falls out. She brushes it off the bed as if it were a spider, an ill omen, listens to it land, and bounce, and roll away across the hardwood floor. Addie draws up her knees, and lets her head fall forward on them, and breathes into the space between her ribs, and reminds herself it is just a ring, and it is just a day. But there is a rope inside her chest, a dull dread winding tighter, telling her to go, to put as much distance between her and Henry as possible, in case he comes.

当她张开手，木戒指掉出来。她把它从床上刷掉，好像它是一只蜘蛛，一个不祥之兆，听着它落地，弹跳，滚过硬木地板。Addie 把膝盖收起来，让头向前靠在它们上，呼吸进肋骨之间的空间，提醒自己这只是一个戒指，这只是一天。但她的胸口有一根绳子，一种沉闷的恐惧越收越紧，告诉她走开，在她和 Henry 之间放尽可能多的距离，以防他来。

**关键词**：ring / spider / ill omen / dread

**为什么这样写**："spider" = 蜘蛛 = 不祥之兆 = 她的恐惧 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。"dread" = 恐惧 = 她的恐惧 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"恐惧" = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由 = 她的爱 = 她的选择。

> **原句 3:** Henry's knuckles rap on the open door, and she looks up to see him holding a plate with a donut, three candles stuck into the top. And despite everything, she laughs. "What's this?" "Hey, it's not every day that your girlfriend turns three hundred." "It's not my birthday." "I know, but I didn't exactly know what to call it." And just like that, the voice rises like smoke inside her head. Happy anniversary, my love. "Make a wish," says Henry. Addie swallows, and blows the candles out.

Henry 的指节轻敲开着的门，她抬头看到他拿着一个盘子，上面有一个甜甜圈，三根蜡烛插在上面。尽管如此，她笑了。"这是什么？""嘿，不是每天你女朋友都满三百岁。""这不是我的生日。""我知道，但我不知道该怎么称呼它。"就这样，声音像烟一样在她脑海中升起。周年快乐，我的爱人。"许愿，"Henry 说。Addie 吞咽，吹灭蜡烛。

**关键词**：donut / candles / three hundred / wish

**为什么这样写**："donut" = 甜甜圈 = 他的爱 = 她的庆祝 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。"wish" = 愿望 = 她的愿望 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"许愿" = 她的愿望 = 她的爱 = 她的自由 = 她的选择。

> **原句 4:** "I don't think we should be together," she says. "Not today." His face falls. "Oh." Addie cups his cheek, and lies. "It's just a day, Henry." "You're right," he says. "It's a day. But how many of them has he ruined? Don't let him take it from you." He kisses her. "From us." If Luc finds them together, he will take more than that. "Come on," insists Henry, "I'll have you back long before you turn into a pumpkin. And then, if you want to spend the night apart, I understand. Worry about him in the dark, but it's hours until then, and you deserve a good day. A good memory." And he's right. She does. The dread loosens a little in her chest. "Okay," she says, one little word, and Henry's whole face lights with pleasure. "What do you have in mind?"

"我觉得我们不应该在一起，"她说。"今天不行。"他的脸沉了下来。"哦。"Addie 捧着他的脸颊，撒谎。"这只是一天，Henry。""你说得对，"他说。"这是一天。但他毁了多少天？别让他把它从你身边夺走。"他吻了她。"从我们身边。"如果 Luc 发现他们在一起，他会夺走更多。"来吧，"Henry 坚持说，"我会在你变成南瓜之前让你回来。然后，如果你想晚上分开，我理解。在黑暗中担心他，但还有几个小时，你应该有一个美好的一天。一个美好的记忆。"他是对的。她应该。恐惧在她的胸口松了一点。"好吧，"她说，一个小小的词，Henry 的整个脸都亮了起来。"你有什么想法？"

**关键词**：ruined / pumpkin / memory / okay

**为什么这样写**："ruined" = 毁掉 = 他毁了多少天 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。"pumpkin" = 南瓜 = 灰姑娘 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"好吧" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 5:** Rockaway Beach is a sea of colored towels, and flags planted in the sand. Laughter rolls in with the tide as kids make castle mounds and people lounge beneath the glaring sun. Henry stretches their towels out on a narrow patch of unclaimed sand, weights them down with shoes, and then Addie grabs his hand and they run down the beach, the soles of their feet stinging until they hit the damp line of the tide and plunge into the water. Addie gasps at the welcome brush of the waves, cool even in the heat of summer, and wades out until the ocean wraps around her waist. Henry ducks his head beside her, and comes back up, water dripping from his glasses. He pulls her to him, kisses the salt from her fingers. She slicks the hair from his face. They linger there, tangled together in the surf. "See," he says, "isn't this better?" And it is. It is.

洛克威海滩是一片彩色毛巾的海洋，旗帜插在沙滩上。笑声随着潮水而来，孩子们堆城堡，人们在刺眼的阳光下闲逛。Henry 把毛巾铺在一小块无主的沙滩上，用鞋子压住，然后 Addie 拉着他的手，他们跑下海滩，脚底刺痛直到他们碰到潮湿的潮线，跳入水中。Addie 对海浪的欢迎刷喘气，即使在夏天的炎热中也很凉爽，涉水直到海洋环绕她的腰。Henry 在她旁边把头埋进水里，又上来，水从他的眼镜上滴落。他把她拉向她，吻去她手指上的盐。她把头发从他脸上抹开。他们在那里徘徊，在浪花中纠缠在一起。"看，"他说，"这不是更好吗？"它是。它是。

**关键词**：Rockaway Beach / towels / waves / salt / tangled

**为什么这样写**："tangled" = 纠缠 = 他们的关系 = 她的爱 = 他的爱 = 她的自由。"salt" = 盐 = 海 = 她的爱 = 他的爱 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"纠缠" = 他们的关系 = 她的爱 = 他的爱 = 她的自由。

> **原句 6:** Henry takes out a book, but Addie's eyes are stinging, and she leans against him, savoring his sun-and-paper scent, and the seat is plastic and the air is stale, and she has never been so comfortable. She feels herself sinking into Henry, head lolling on his shoulder. And then he whispers three words into her hair. "I love you," he says, and Addie wonders if this is love, this gentle thing. If it is meant to be this soft, this kind. The difference between heat, and warmth. Passion, and contentment. "I love you too," she says. She wants it to be true.

Henry 拿出一本书，但 Addie 的眼睛刺痛，她靠着他，品味着他的阳光和纸张的气味，座位是塑料的，空气是陈旧的，她从未如此舒适。她感觉自己沉入 Henry，头垂在他的肩上。然后他在她头发里低语三个字。"我爱你，"他说，Addie 想知道这是否是爱，这种温柔的东西。如果它注定是这么柔软，这么善良。热和温暖之间的区别。激情和满足。"我也爱你，"她说。她希望这是真的。

**关键词**：love / gentle / warmth / contentment

**为什么这样写**："gentle" = 温柔 = 她的爱 = 他的爱 = 她的自由。"warmth" = 温暖 = 她的爱 = 他的爱 = 她的自由。"contentment" = 满足 = 她的爱 = 他的爱 = 她的自由。
