# ch100. Henry's Last Days

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：2014 年纽约，Henry 生命最后的日子——"是他生命中最快乐的日子"——北上州看星星——和 Bea、Robbie 告别——手表向午夜逼近。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 —— 从"最快乐"到"看星星"到"告别"到"午夜"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Last Days（最后的日子）—— 他的最后日子 = 他的选择 = 他的爱 = 他的自由。
- **人物弧线**：Henry 从"快乐"到"告别"—— 他选择告别 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。
- **叙事手法**："watch" = 手表 = 他的生命 = 向午夜逼近 = 他的命运 = 他的选择。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** These are the happiest days of Henry's life. It is an odd thing to say, he knows. But there is a strange freedom to it, a peculiar comfort in the knowing. The end is rushing up to meet him, and yet, he does not feel like he is falling toward it. He knows he should be scared. Every day he braces for the restless terror, waits for the storm clouds to roll in, expects the inevitable panic to climb inside his chest, pry him apart. But for the first time in months, in years, in as long as he can remember, he is not afraid. He is worried about his friends, of course, about the bookstore, and the cat. But beyond the low hum of concern is only a strange calm, a steadiness, and the incredible relief that he found Addie, that he got to know her, to love her, to have her here beside him. He is happy. He is ready. He is not afraid. That is what he tells himself. He is not afraid.

这些是 Henry 生命中最快乐的日子。这是一个奇怪的说法，他知道。但有一种奇怪的自由，一种在知道中的奇特安慰。结局冲上来迎接他，然而，他并不觉得自己正在向它坠落。他知道他应该害怕。每一天他都准备好迎接不安的恐惧，等待乌云翻滚而来，期待不可避免的恐慌爬进他的胸口，把他撕开。但几个月来，几年来，在他的记忆里，他第一次不害怕。他担心他的朋友们，当然，还有书店，和猫。但在担忧的低沉嗡嗡声之外，只有一种奇怪的平静，一种稳定，以及他找到了 Addie 的不可思议的宽慰，他认识了她，爱她，有她在她身边。他很快乐。他准备好了。他不害怕。那就是他告诉自己的。他不害怕。

**关键词**：happiest / freedom / knowing / end / falling / afraid / relief

**为什么这样写**："happiest" = 最快乐 = 他生命中最快乐 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。"relief" = 宽慰 = 他找到了 Addie = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Henry 的"他不害怕" = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由 = 他的爱。

> **原句 2:** They decide to go upstate. To get out of the city, away from the stagnant summer heat. To see the stars. He rents a car, and they drive north, and he realizes, halfway up the Hudson, that Addie has never met his family, and then he realizes, with a sudden, sinking weight, that he is not supposed to go home until Rosh Hashanah, and that he will be gone by then. That if he does not take this exit, he will never get a chance to say good-bye. And then, the clouds begin to roll in, and fear tries to climb inside his chest, because he doesn't know what he would say, he doesn't know what good it would do. And then he is past the exit, then it is too late, and he can breathe again, and Addie is pointing to a sign for fresh fruit, and they pull off the freeway and buy peaches from the stand, and sandwiches from the market, and drive an hour north to a state park, where the sun is hot but the shade beneath the trees is cool, and they spend the day wandering the woodland paths, and when night falls they make a picnic on the roof of the rented car, and stretch out between the wild, weedy grass and the stars. So many, the night doesn't seem that dark. And he is still happy. And he can still breathe.

他们决定北上州。离开城市，远离停滞的夏热。看星星。他租了一辆车，他们向北开，他意识到，在哈德逊河中途，Addie 从未见过他的家人，然后他意识到，突然的下沉重量，他不应该回家直到犹太新年，到那时他将已经离开。如果他不走这个出口，他将永远没有机会说再见。然后，乌云开始翻滚，恐惧试图爬进他的胸口，因为他不知道该说什么，他不知道这有什么好处。然后他过了出口，然后太晚了，他可以再次呼吸，Addie 指着新鲜水果的标志，他们离开高速公路，从摊位买桃子，从市场买三明治，开一小时北到一个州立公园，那里太阳很热但树荫下很凉爽，他们花一天时间在树林小径上漫步，当夜晚降临他们在租来的车顶上做野餐，伸展开在野生杂草和星星之间。如此多，夜晚似乎不那么黑。他仍然很快乐。他仍然可以呼吸。

**关键词**：upstate / stars / Hudson / Rosh Hashanah / exit / good-bye / peaches / park

**为什么这样写**："stars" = 星星 = 看星星 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。"exit" = 出口 = 错过的出口 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Henry 的"他仍然可以呼吸" = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由 = 他的爱。

> **原句 3:** "If you could do it again," he says, "would you still make the deal?" And Addie says yes. It has been a hard and lonely life, she says, and a wonderful one, too. She has lived through wars, and fought in them, witnessed revolution and rebirth. She has left her mark on a thousand works of art, like a thumbprint in the bottom of a drying bowl. She has seen marvels, and gone mad, has danced in snowbanks and frozen to death along the Seine. She fell in love with the darkness many times, fell in love with a human once. And she is tired. Unspeakably tired. But there is no question she has lived. "Nothing is all good or all bad," she says. "Life is so much messier than that." And there in the dark, he asks if it was really worth it. Were the instants of joy worth the stretches of sorrow? Were the moments of beauty worth the years of pain? And she turns her head, and looks at him, and says, "Always."

"如果你能再做一次，"他说，"你还会做交易吗？"Addie 说是。这是一个艰难和孤独的生活，她说，也是一个美好的生活。她经历过战争，在其中战斗，见证重生和重生。她在千件艺术品上留下了她的印记，像干碗底部的指纹。她见过奇迹，发疯过，在雪堆中跳舞，沿着塞纳河冻死。她多次爱上黑暗，一次爱上人类。她累了。难以言喻地累。但毫无疑问她活过。"没有什么是全好或全坏的，"她说。"生活比那混乱得多。"在黑暗中，他问这是否真的值得。欢乐的瞬间是否值得悲伤的延伸？美丽的时刻是否值得多年的痛苦？她转头看着他，说，"永远。"

**关键词**：deal / wars / revolution / rebirth / art / marvels / mad / tired / lived / messy / joy / sorrow / beauty / pain / Always

**为什么这样写**："lived" = 活过 = 毫无疑问她活过 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"Always" = 永远 = 她说是永远 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"永远" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 4:** And then the weeks turn into days. There are some good-byes he has to make. He meets Bea and Robbie at the Merchant one night. Addie sits across the bar, sipping a soda and giving him space. He wants her there, he needs her there, a silent anchor in the storm. But they both know that if she were at the table with him, Bea and Robbie might forget, and he needs them to remember. And for a little while, everything is wonderfully, painfully normal. Bea talks about her latest thesis proposal, and apparently ninth time's the charm, because it's been approved, and Robbie talks about the show's premiere next week, and Henry does not tell him that he snuck into a dress rehearsal yesterday, that he and Addie lurked in the last row of seats, slouched low so he could watch Robbie on the stage, brilliant, and beautiful, and in his element, lounging on his throne with Bowie's flare, and a devil's grin, and a magic all his own.

然后几周转成几天。有一些告别他必须做。一天晚上他在 Merchant 见 Bea 和 Robbie。Addie 坐在酒吧对面，喝苏打水，给他空间。他希望她在那里，他需要她在那里，风暴中沉默的锚。但他们都知道如果她和他一起坐在桌旁，Bea 和 Robbie 可能会忘记，他需要他们记住。有一切都非常美妙地、痛苦地正常。Bea 谈论她最新的论文提案，显然第九次是魅力，因为它被批准了，Robbie 谈论下周的首演，Henry 没有告诉他昨天他溜进了彩排，他和 Addie 潜伏在最后一排座位，低头弯腰这样他可以看到 Robbie 在舞台上，辉煌，美丽，在他的元素中，懒洋洋地坐在他的王座上，带着 Bowie 的光芒，和魔鬼的咧嘴笑，和他自己的魔法。

**关键词**：good-byes / Merchant / Bea / Robbie / space / remember / normal / thesis / premiere / stage

**为什么这样写**："remember" = 记住 = 他需要他们记住 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。"normal" = 正常 = 美妙地、痛苦地正常 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。

> **原句 5:** And at last, Henry lies, and tells them he is going out of town. Upstate, to see his parents. No, it is not time, he says, but he has cousins visiting, his mother asked. Just for the weekend, he says. He asks Bea if she can work the store. Asks Robbie if he will feed the cat. And they say yes, as simple as that, because they do not know it is good-bye. Henry pays the tab, and Robbie jokes, and Bea complains about her undergrads, and Henry tells them he'll call when he gets back. And when he gets up to go, Bea kisses his cheek, and he pulls Robbie in for a hug, and Robbie says he better not miss his show, and Henry promises he won't, and then they are going, they are gone. And this, he decides, is what a good-bye should be. Not a period, but an ellipsis, a statement trailing off, until someone is there to pick it up. It is a door left open. It is drifting off to sleep. And he tells himself he is not afraid. Tells himself it is okay, he is okay.

最后，Henry 撒谎，告诉他们他要出城。北州，见他的父母。不，不是时候，他说，但他有表亲来访，他母亲问的。只是周末，他说。他问 Bea 是否能看店。问 Robbie 是否会喂猫。他们说是，就这么简单，因为他们不知道这是告别。Henry 付账，Robbie 开玩笑，Bea 抱怨她的本科生，Henry 告诉他们他回来时会打电话。当他站起来离开时，Bea 吻他的脸颊，他把 Robbie 拉进拥抱，Robbie 说他最好别错过他的演出，Henry 答应他不会，然后他们走了，他们走了。他决定，这就是告别应该的样子。不是句号，而是省略号，一个陈述渐渐消失，直到有人在那里捡起来。这是一扇敞开的门。这是入睡。他告诉自己他不害怕。告诉自己没事，他没事。

**关键词**：lies / good-bye / town / parents / cousins / weekend / store / cat / yes / tab / kiss / hug / period / ellipsis / door / sleep / afraid / okay

**为什么这样写**："ellipsis" = 省略号 = 不是句号 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。"door" = 门 = 敞开的门 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Henry 的"他没事" = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由 = 他的爱。

> **原句 6:** And just when he begins to doubt, Addie's hand is there, soft and steady on his arm, leading him back home. And they climb into bed, and curl into each other against the storm. And sometime in the middle of the night, he feels her get up, hears her padding down the hall. But it is late, and he thinks nothing of it. He rolls over, and goes back to sleep, and when he wakes again it is still dark, and she is back beside him in the bed. And the watch on the table twitches one step closer to midnight.

就在他开始怀疑时，Addie 的手在那里，柔软而稳定在他的臂上，引导他回家。他们爬上床，在风暴中蜷缩进彼此。半夜的某个时候，他感觉到她起床，听到她走过走廊。但很晚了，他没有多想。他翻身，回去睡觉，当他再次醒来时天还黑，她回到他身边在床上。桌子上的手表向午夜抽搐了一步。
