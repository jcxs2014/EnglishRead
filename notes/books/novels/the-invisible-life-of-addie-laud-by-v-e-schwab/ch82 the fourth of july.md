# ch82. The Fourth of July

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：2014 年纽约，独立日烟火，Henry 情绪低落，Robbie 表演《浮士德》，Addie 和 Henry 在浴缸中谈论名字和真相——"他想要抹杀我。"
- **情感弧线位置**：升温 → 不安 —— 从"庆祝"到"观察"到"对话"到"沉默"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Holiday（节日）—— 独立日烟火；The Play（戏剧）—— Robbie 表演《浮士德》= 浮士德与魔鬼的交易 = 他们的关系。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"快乐"到"观察"—— 她知道 Henry 在撒谎 = 她的洞察 = 她的爱。
- **叙事手法**："name" = 名字 = 她的存在 = 她的权力 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。

灯光在城市上空爆炸。他们聚集在 Robbie 的楼顶上，和其他二十个人一起看烟花绽放，把曼哈顿的天际线染成粉红色、绿色和金色。

**关键词**：fireworks / Manhattan / pink / green / gold

> **原句 2:** He's been in a strange mood all day, but she assumes it's the heat, sitting like a weight on everything. The bookstore was closed, and they spent most of the day stretched together on the sofa in front of a box fan, Book pawing at an ice cube as they watched TV, the heat enough to temper even Henry's manic energy. She was too tired to tell him stories. He was too tired to write them down.

他一整天情绪都很奇怪，但她认为是天气炎热，像重量一样坐在一切上。书店关门了，他们大部分时间都一起躺在沙发前对着箱扇，Book 抓着冰块当他们看电视时，炎热足以抑制甚至 Henry 的狂躁能量。她太累了，不能给他讲故事。他太累了，不能把它们写下来。

**关键词**：strange mood / heat / tired

**为什么这样写**："strange mood" = 奇怪的情绪 = 他的情绪 = 他的秘密 = 他的恐惧 = 他的诅咒。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"太累了" = 她的疲惫 = 他的疲惫 = 他们的爱 = 她的诅咒。

> **原句 3:** Twelfth time's the charm, she thinks as he hands her a fruit bar, but even though he doesn't remember her, Henry's obviously said enough, or perhaps Robbie simply recognizes everyone else, and makes the deduction. One of these things is not like the others. Addie doesn't lose a second. She breaks into a sudden grin. "Oh my god, you must be Robbie." She throws her arms around his neck. "Henry's told me all about you."

第十二次是魅力，当她想当他递给她一根水果条时，但即使他不记得她，Henry 显然说得够多了，或者也许 Robbie 只是认识其他人，然后做出推断。这些东西中有一个不一样。Addie 一秒钟都没有失去。她突然露出灿烂的笑容。"天哪，你一定是 Robbie。"她搂住他的脖子。"Henry 告诉我关于你的一切。"

**关键词**：twelfth time / deduction / charm

**为什么这样写**："twelfth time" = 第十二次 = 第十二次见面 = 她的诅咒 = 她的存在 = 她的生存。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"第十二次" = 她的诅咒 = 她的存在 = 她的生存 = 她的力量。

> **原句 4:** "We're doing a spin on Faust," he says. "You know, man makes a deal with the devil…" Addie bites into the ice pop, sending a wave of shock through her teeth. It is enough to mask the grimace as Robbie goes on. "But it's going to be set against a stage that's more Labyrinth. Think Mephistopheles but by way of the Goblin King." He gestures at himself when he says it. "It's a really cool spin. The costumes are amazing. Anyway, it doesn't open until September."

"我们正在做《浮士德》的改编，"他说。"你知道，人与魔鬼做交易……"Addie 咬了一口冰棒，在她的牙齿间送出一股冲击波。这足以掩盖当 Robbie 继续时的皱眉。"但它将设置在一个更像迷宫的舞台上。想想梅菲斯特但通过妖精王的方式。"他说话时指着自己。"这是一个非常酷的改编。服装太棒了。无论如何，它要到九月才开幕。"

**关键词**：Faust / devil / Labyrinth / Goblin King

**为什么这样写**："Faust" = 浮士德 = 人与魔鬼的交易 = 他的交易 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"浮士德" = 人与魔鬼的交易 = 他的交易 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱。

> **原句 5:** He's staring out—not up, but down over the edge. "I think I finally cracked Robbie," she says, wiping her hands on her shorts. "Hm?" he says, not really listening. A bead of sweat runs down his cheek, and he closes his eyes into the faint summer breeze and sways a little on his feet. Addie pulls him away from the edge. "What's wrong?" His eyes are dark, and for a moment, he looks haunted, lost. "Nothing," he says softly. "Just thinking." Addie has lived long enough to recognize a lie. Lying is its own language, like the language of seasons, or gestures, or the shade of Luc's eyes. So she knows that Henry is lying to her now. Or at least, he's not telling her the truth.

他在向外看——不是向上，而是向下看着边缘。"我想我终于搞定了 Robbie，"她说，在短裤上擦着手。"嗯？"他说，没有真正在听。一滴汗珠顺着他的脸颊流下，他闭上眼睛感受着微弱的夏风，在脚上微微摇晃。Addie 把他从边缘拉开。"怎么了？"他的眼睛很黑，有一瞬间，他看起来很迷茫，迷失了。"没什么，"他轻声说。"只是在想。"Addie 活得够久，能认出谎言。谎言有自己的语言，像季节的语言，或手势，或 Luc 眼睛的颜色。所以她知道 Henry 现在在对她撒谎。或者至少，他没有告诉她真相。

**关键词**：edge / lie / truth / language

**为什么这样写**："edge" = 边缘 = 他在边缘 = 他的情绪 = 他的恐惧 = 他的诅咒。"lie" = 谎言 = 他在撒谎 = 他的秘密 = 他的恐惧 = 他的交易。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"她在边缘" = 他的情绪 = 他的恐惧 = 他的诅咒 = 他的交易。

> **原句 6:** That night, when they get home, it is too hot to think, to sleep, so they fill the bathtub with cold water, turn off the lights, and climb inside, shivering at the sudden, merciful relief. They lie there in the dark, bare legs intertwined beneath the water. Henry's fingers play a melody across her knee. "When we first met," he muses, "why didn't you tell me your real name?" Addie looks up at the darkened ceiling tiles, and sees Isabelle as she was, that last day, sitting at the table, her eyes gone empty. She sees Remy in the café, staring dreamily past her words, unable to hear them. "Because I didn't think I could," she says, running her fingers through the water. "When I try to tell people the truth, their faces just go blank. When I try to say my name, it always gets stuck in my throat." She smiles. "Except with you." "But why?" he asks. "If you're going to be forgotten, what does it matter if you tell the truth?" Addie closes her eyes. It's a good question, one she's asked herself a hundred times. "I think he wanted to erase me. To make sure I felt unseen, unheard, unreal. You don't really realize the power of a name until it's gone. Before you, he was the only one who could say it." The voice curls like smoke inside her head. Oh Adeline. Adeline, Adeline. My Adeline. "What an asshole," says Henry, and she chuckles, remembering the nights she screamed up at the sky, called the darkness so much worse. And then he asks, "When's the last time you saw him?" and Addie falters. For an instant, she is in a bed, black silk sheets twisted around her limbs, the New Orleans heat oppressive even in the dark. But Luc is a cool weight, wrapped around her limbs, his teeth skating along her shoulder as he whispers the word against her skin. Surrender. Addie swallows, pushes the memory down like bile in her throat. "Almost thirty years ago," she says, as if she doesn't count the days. As if the anniversary isn't rushing up to meet them. She glances sideways at the clothes piled on the bathroom floor, the indent of the wooden ring in the pocket of her shorts. "We had a falling-out," she says, and it is the barest version of the truth. Henry looks at her, clearly curious, but he doesn't ask what happened, and for that, she is grateful. There is an order to the story. She will tell him when she gets there. For now Addie reaches up, and turns the shower on, and it falls down on them like rain, soothing and steady. And this is the perfect kind of silence. Easy, and empty. They sit across from each other beneath the icy stream, and Addie closes her eyes and tips her head back against the tub, and listens to the makeshift storm.

那天晚上，当他们回到家，太热了，无法思考，无法睡眠，所以他们用冷水填满浴缸，关掉灯，爬进去，在突然的、仁慈的缓解中颤抖。他们躺在黑暗中，光裸的腿在水中交缠。Henry 的手指在她的膝盖上弹奏旋律。"当我们第一次见面时，"他若有所思，"为什么不告诉我你的真名？"Addie 抬头看着黑暗的天花板瓷砖，看到伊莎贝尔最后一天的样子，坐在桌子旁，她的眼睛变得空洞。她看到雷米在咖啡馆里，梦幻般地盯着她的话，无法听到它们。"因为我以为我不能，"她说，在水中拨动手指。"当我试图告诉人们真相时，他们的脸只是变得空白。当我试图说出我的名字时，它总是卡在喉咙里。"她微笑。"除了和你。""但为什么？"他问。"如果你要被忘记，说出真相有什么关系？"Addie 闭上眼睛。这是一个好问题，她问了自己一百次。"我想他想要抹杀我。确保我感到看不见，听不见，不真实。你真的不知道一个名字的力量直到它消失。在你之前，他是唯一能说出它的人。"声音像烟一样在她脑海中盘旋。哦 Adeline。Adeline，Adeline。我的 Adeline。"真是个混蛋，"Henry 说，她咯咯笑，记得她对天空尖叫的夜晚，称黑暗更糟。然后他问，"你最后一次见到他是什么时候？"Addie 犹豫了。有一瞬间，她在一张床上，黑色丝绸床单缠绕着她的四肢，新奥尔良的炎热即使在黑暗中也很压抑。但 Luc 是一个凉爽的重量，缠绕着她的四肢，他的牙齿沿着她的肩膀滑行，当他把这个词低语在她的皮肤上时。投降。Addie 吞咽，把记忆推下去就像喉咙里的胆汁。"差不多三十年前，"她说，好像她不数日子。好像周年纪念日没有冲上来迎接他们。她瞥了一眼堆在浴室地板上的衣服，她短裤口袋里木戒指的凹痕。"我们闹翻了，"她说，这是最简化的真相版本。Henry 看着她，显然很好奇，但他没有问发生了什么，为此，她感激。故事有它的顺序。当她到达那里时她会告诉他。现在 Addie 伸手，打开淋浴，它像雨一样落在他们身上，舒缓而稳定。这是完美的沉默。轻松，而空虚。他们坐在冰冷的水流对面，Addie 闭上眼睛，把头靠在浴缸上，听着临时风暴。

**关键词**：name / truth / erase / power / Surrender / silence

**为什么这样写**："erase" = 抹杀 = 他想要抹杀她 = 她的诅咒 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"Surrender" = 投降 = 他的要求 = 她的拒绝 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"他想要抹杀我" = 她的诅咒 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。
