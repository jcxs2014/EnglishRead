# ch48. Part Four: The Man Who Stayed Dry in the Rain

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Part Four 分隔页，以 Muriel Strauss 的雕塑作品《Open to Love》的描述呈现——关于家庭、爱情与失衡的隐喻。
- **情感弧线位置**：过渡 —— 从 Addie 的坦白转向 Henry 的故事。
- **叙事手法**：元叙事 = 书中书 = 艺术作品的描述 = 对 Henry 家庭的隐喻。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Title: Open to Love. Artist(s): Muriel Strauss (design) and Lance Harringer (manufacture). Date: 2011. Medium: Aluminum, steel, and glass sculpture.

标题：《向爱开放》。艺术家：Muriel Strauss（设计）和 Lance Harringer（制造）。日期：2011。媒介：铝、钢、玻璃雕塑。

**关键词**：Open to Love / Muriel Strauss / aluminum, steel, and glass

**为什么这样写**：艺术作品的"博物馆说明牌"格式 = 客观描述中的情感暗流。"Muriel Strauss" = Henry 的母亲 = 她的艺术 = 她对家庭的理解。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"Open to Love" = 主题预告 = Part Four 将关于 Henry 如何学会爱。

---

> **原句 2:** Originally displayed as an interactive installation, in which the aluminum heart, perforated by small holes, hung suspended over a bucket. On a table beside the metal heart, jars of varying shapes and sizes contained different-colored liquids, some water, some alcohol, some paint, and participants were encouraged to select one of the glass jars, and empty the contents into the heart. The liquid instantly began to leak out, with a speed dependent on the viscosity of the substance poured.

最初作为互动装置展出，铝心被小孔穿透，悬挂在桶上方。在金属心旁边的桌子上，各种形状和大小的罐子装着不同颜色的液体，有些是水，有些是酒精，有些是油漆，参与者被鼓励选择一个玻璃罐，把内容物倒进心里。液体立即开始泄漏，速度取决于倒入物质的粘度。

**关键词**：interactive installation / aluminum heart / perforated / leak / viscosity

**为什么这样写**："perforated heart" = 穿孔的心 = 无法容纳爱 = Henry 的家庭隐喻。"viscosity" = 粘度 = 爱的类型决定流失速度。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"instantly began to leak" = 爱的必然流失 = Henry 的家庭主题。

---

> **原句 3:** This sculpture formed the central piece of Strauss's senior portfolio, a collection of work on the theme of family. At the time, Strauss did not specify which member of her family was paired with which piece, but insisted that Open to Love was designed as "an homage to the exhaustions of serial monogamy and a testament to the dangers of unbalanced affection."

这件雕塑是 Strauss 毕业作品集的核心作品，一系列以家庭为主题的作品。当时，Strauss 没有指定她家庭的哪个成员与哪件作品配对，但坚持认为《向爱开放》被设计为"对连续一夫一妻制疲惫的致敬和对不平衡感情危险的证明"。

**关键词**：theme of family / serial monogamy / unbalanced affection

**为什么这样写**："serial monogamy" = 连续一夫一妻制 = 母亲不断结婚离婚 = Henry 的家庭背景。"unbalanced affection" = 不平衡的感情 = 多子女家庭中的偏爱。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"homage to exhaustions" = 爱的疲惫 = Henry 为何害怕爱。
