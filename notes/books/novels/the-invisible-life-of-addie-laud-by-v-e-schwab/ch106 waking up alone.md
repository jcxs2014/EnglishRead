# ch106. Waking Up Alone

## 精读

> **原句 1:** This is how it ends. A boy wakes up alone in bed. Sunlight spills through the gap in the curtains, the buildings beyond slick with the aftermath of rain. He feels sluggish, hungover, still caught within the dregs of sleep. He knows he was dreaming, but he can't for the life of him remember the details of the dream, and it must not have been very pleasant, because he feels only a deep relief at waking. Book looks over the mound of the comforter, orange eyes wide and waiting. It's late, the boy can tell by the angle of the light, the sounds of traffic on the street. He didn't mean to sleep so long.

这就是它结束的方式。一个男孩独自在床上醒来。阳光从窗帘的缝隙中洒落，建筑物在雨后的湿润中闪闪发光。他感到迟钝，宿醉，仍然困在睡眠的残渣中。他知道他在做梦，但他一辈子也记不住梦的细节，而且它一定不是很愉快，因为他在醒来时只感到深深的宽慰。Book 从被子堆上看着，橙色眼睛宽大而等待。很晚了，男孩能从光线的角度判断，街道上的交通声。他不想睡这么久。

> **原句 2:** The girl he loves is always the first to wake. Shuffling beneath the sheets, the weight of her attention, the soft touch of her fingers on his skin—they are always enough to rouse him out of sleep. Only once did he wake first, and then he had the strange pleasure of seeing her, knees curled up and face tucked against the pillows, still beneath the surface of sleep. But that was a rainy morning just after dawn, when the world was gray, and today the sun is so bright he doesn't know how either of them slept through it. He rolls over to wake her. But the other side of the bed is empty. He splays his hand over the place where she should be, but the sheets are cold and smooth. "Addie?" he calls, rising to his feet. He moves through the apartment, checks the kitchen, the bathroom, the fire escape, even though he knows, he knows, he knows, that she is not there. "Addie?"

他爱的女孩总是第一个醒来。在床单下拖着脚步，她注意重的重量，她手指在他皮肤上的柔软触感——它们总是足以把他从睡眠中唤醒。只有一次他先醒来，然后他有奇怪的快乐看到她，膝盖卷起，脸埋在枕头里，仍然在睡眠的表面下。但那是一个黎明后的雨早晨，当世界是灰色的，今天太阳如此明亮他不知道他们怎么睡过去的。他翻身去叫醒她。但床的另一边是空的。他张开手放在她应该在的地方，但床单又冷又滑。"Addie？"他喊，站起来。他穿过公寓，检查厨房，浴室，消防逃生梯，即使他知道，他知道，她知道，她不在那里。"Addie？"

> **原句 3:** And then, of course, he remembers. Not the dream, there was no dream, only the night before. The last night of his life. The damp concrete smell of the rooftop, the last tick of the watch as its hand found twelve, her smile as she looked up into his face, and made him promise to remember. And now he's here, and she's gone, and there's no trace of her left behind except the stuff in his head and— The journals.

然后，当然，他记起来了。不是梦，没有梦，只有前一晚。他生命的最后一晚。屋顶潮湿的混凝土气味，手表的滴答声当它的指针找到十二，当她抬头看进他的脸时的微笑，让他答应记住。现在他在这里，她走了，没有她的痕迹留下除了他脑子里的东西和——日记。

> **原句 4:** He's up, crossing the room to the narrow set of shelves where he kept them: red, blue, silver, black, white, green; six notebooks, all of them still there. He pulls them from the shelf, spreads them on the bed, and as he does, the Polaroids tumble out. The one he took that day of Addie, her face a blur, her back to the camera, a ghost at the edges of the frame, and he stares at them a long time, convinced that if he squints, she will come into focus. But no matter how long he looks, all he can see are the shapes, the shadows. The only thing he can make out are the seven freckles, and those are so faint he can't tell if they're really visible, or his memory is simply filling them in where they should be.

他站起来，穿过房间到那排狭窄的架子，他在那里保存它们：红、蓝、银、黑、白、绿；六个笔记本，它们都还在那里。他把它们从架子上拉出来，铺在床上，当他这样做时，宝丽来照片掉出来的。他那天拍的 Addie 的照片，她的脸一片模糊，背对着相机，一个幽灵在画面的边缘，他盯着它们很长时间，相信如果他眯起眼睛，她会变得清晰。但无论他看多久，他只能看到形状，阴影。他唯一能辨认的是七个雀斑，而那些是如此微弱他无法分辨它们是否真的可见，或者他的记忆只是在它们应该在的地方填充它们。

> **原句 5:** He sets the photograph aside and reaches for the first journal, then stops, so convinced that if and when he opens it, he will find the pages blank, the ink erased like every other mark she tried to make. But he has to look, and so he does, and there they are, page after page written in his slanting script, shielded from the curse by the fact the words themselves are his, though the story is hers. She wants to be a tree. There is nothing wrong with Roger. She simply wants to live before she dies. It will take her years to learn the language of those eyes. She claws her way up, and out, hands splayed across the bony mound of a dead man's back. This is her first. How it should have been. She feels him press three coins into her hand. Soul is such a grand word. The truth is so much smaller. It does not take her long to find her father's grave. He picks up the next journal. Paris is burning. The darkness comes undone. And the next. There is an angel above the bar. Henry sits there for hours against the side of the bed, turning through every page of every book, every story she ever told, and when he's done, he closes his eyes, and puts his head in his hands amid the open books. Because the girl he loved is gone. And he's still here. He remembers everything.

他把照片放在一边，伸手去拿第一本日记，然后停下来，如此相信如果他打开它，他会发现页面空白，墨水被抹去就像她试图做的每一个其他标记。但他必须看，所以他看了，它们在那里，页页写着他倾斜的笔迹，被诅咒屏蔽因为文字本身是她的，虽然故事是她的。她想成为一棵树。Roger 没有错。她只是想在死之前活。她将花多年时间学习那些眼睛的语言。她向上爬，出来，双手张开在一个死人背部的骨堆上。这是她的第一个。它应该是怎样的。她感觉他把三个硬币压在她手上。灵魂是一个如此宏大的词。真相是如此小。她不需要很长时间找到他父亲的坟墓。他拿起下一本日记。巴黎在燃烧。黑暗来了。下一本。酒吧上方有一个天使。Henry 在床边坐了几个小时，翻看每一页每一本书，她曾经告诉他的每一个故事，当他做完时，他闭上眼睛，把头埋在手中在打开的书中间。因为他爱的女孩走了。他还在这里。他记得一切。
