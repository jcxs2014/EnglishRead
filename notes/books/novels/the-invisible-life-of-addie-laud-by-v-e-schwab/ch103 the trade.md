# ch103. The Trade

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：2014 年纽约，Addie 在深夜的 Merchant 酒吧——旋转戒指——Luc 出现——"我接受。"
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 —— 从"决定"到"戒指"到"Luc"到"交易"到"接受"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Trade（交易）—— 她的交易 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"决定"到"接受"—— 她做交易 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Some decisions happen all at once. And others build up over time. A girl makes a deal with the darkness, after years of dreaming. A girl falls in love with a boy in a moment, and resolves to set him free. Addie doesn't know exactly when she decided. Perhaps she has known since the night Luc walked back into their lives. Or perhaps she has known since the night he wrote her name. Or perhaps she has known since he said those words: I remember you. She isn't sure. It doesn't matter. What matters is that, three nights before the end, Addie slips out of bed. Henry rolls over in his sleep, wakes enough to hear her padding down the hall, but not enough to hear her put on her shoes, or slip out into the dark.

有些决定一下子就发生了。其他的随时间积累。一个女孩与黑暗做交易，在多年梦想之后。一个女孩在瞬间爱上了一个男孩，并决心让他自由。Addie 不准确知道她何时决定的。也许自从 Luc 走回他们的生活那晚她就知道了。也许自从他写她的名字那晚她就知道了。也许自从他说那些话她就知道了：我记得你。她不确信。这无关紧要。重要的是，在结束前三晚，Addie 溜出床。Henry 在他睡梦中翻身，醒过来听到她走过走廊，但不够清醒听到她穿上鞋子，或溜进黑暗。

**关键词**：decisions / once / time / deal / darkness / dreaming / love / moment / set him free / I remember you / matters / slips / bed / rolls / wakes / padding / dark

**为什么这样写**："I remember you" = 我记得你 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。"matters" = 重要 = 重要的是 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"我记得你" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 2:** It is almost two—that time between very late, and very early—and even Brooklyn has quieted to a murmur as she walks the two blocks to the Merchant bar. It is an hour until closing, the crowd thinned to a few determined drinkers. Addie takes a stool at the bar, and orders a shot of tequila. She's never been one for hard liquor, but she downs the drink in one, feels the warmth settle in her chest as she reaches into her pocket and finds the ring. Her fingers curl around the wooden band. She draws it out, balances the ring upright on the counter. She spins it like a coin, but there are no heads or tails, no yes or no, no choice beyond the one she's already made. She decides that when it settles, she will put it on. When it falls—but as it begins to wobble and tip, a hand comes down on top of it, pressing it flat against the bar.

几乎两点——那个在很晚和很早之间的时间——甚至布鲁克林已经安静下来当她走到 Merchant 酒吧两个街区。离打烊还有一小时，人群减少到几个坚定的饮酒者。Addie 在吧台前坐下，点了一杯龙舌兰。她从来不是烈酒的人，但她一饮而尽，感到温暖在她胸口安定下来当她伸进口袋找到戒指。她的手指蜷曲在木带上。她把它拿出来，在柜台上平衡戒指直立。她像硬币一样旋转它，但没有正面或反面，没有是或否，没有超越她已经做出的选择。她决定当它安定下来时，她会戴上它。当它倒下——但当它开始摇晃和倾斜，一只手落下来，把它压平在吧台上。

> **原句 3:** The hand is smooth and strong, the fingers long, the details just as she once drew them. "Shouldn't you be with your love?" There is no humor in Luc's eyes. They are flat, and dark. "He's sleeping," she says, "and I cannot." Luc's hand has withdrawn, and Addie looks at the pale circle of the ring still on the counter. "Adeline," he says, stroking her hair. "It will hurt. And it will pass. All things do." "Except for us," she murmurs. And then she adds, as if to herself, "I am glad it was only a year." Luc sinks onto the stool beside her. "And how was it, your human love? Was it everything you dreamed of?" "No," she says, and it is the truth. It was messy. It was hard. It was wonderful, and strange, and frightening, and fragile—so fragile it hurt—and it was worth every single moment. She does not tell him any of that. Instead, she lets the "no" hang in the air between them, heavy with the weight of Luc's assumption. His eyes, such a smug shade of green.

手光滑而强壮，手指长，细节就像她曾经画的那样。"你不应该和你的爱人在一起吗？"Luc 的眼睛里没有幽默。它们平淡而黑暗。"他在睡觉，"她说，"我不能。"Luc 的手已经收回，Addie 看着柜台上仍然苍白的戒指圆圈。"Adeline，"他说，抚摸着她的头发。"它会痛。它会过去。所有事情都会。""除了我们，"她低语。然后她补充，好像对自己说，"我很高兴只是一年。"Luc 沉入她旁边的凳子上。"你的爱人怎么样？它是你梦想的一切吗？""不，"她说，这是真相。它很混乱。它很艰难。它很美好，奇怪，恐惧，脆弱——如此脆弱它很痛——它值得每一刻。她没有告诉他任何这些。相反，她让"不"悬在他们之间的空气中，沉重带着 Luc 的假设的重量。他的眼睛，如此自鸣得意的绿色。

**为什么这样写**："fragile" = 脆弱 = 如此脆弱它很痛 = 她的爱 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。"smug" = 自鸣得意 = 如此自鸣得意的绿色 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"它值得每一刻" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 4:** "But Henry doesn't deserve to die to prove your point." The arrogance flickers, cut through with anger. "A deal is a deal," he says. "It cannot be broken." "And yet, you told me once that a deal could be bent, the terms rewritten. Did you mean it? Or was it just part of the ploy to get me to surrender?" Luc's expression darkens. "There was no ploy, Adeline. But if you think I'll change the terms of his—" Addie shakes her head. "I'm not talking about Henry's deal," she says. "I'm talking about mine." She has practiced the words, but they still tumble awkwardly off her tongue. "I'm not asking for your mercy, and I know you have no charity. So I'm offering a trade. Let Henry go. Let him live. Let him remember me, and—"

"但 Henry 不值得死来证明你的观点。"狂妄闪烁，被愤怒切断。"交易就是交易，"他说。"它不能被打破。""然而，你曾经告诉我交易可以弯曲，条款可以重写。你是认真的吗？还是只是让我投降的阴谋的一部分？"Luc 的表情变暗。"没有阴谋，Adeline。但如果你以为我会改变他的条款——"Addie 摇头。"我不是在说 Henry 的交易，"她说。"我是在说我的。"她练习过这些话，但它们仍然笨拙地从她舌头上滚落。"我不是在求你的慈悲，我知道你没有慈善。所以我提供一个交易。让 Henry 走。让他活。让他记住我，和——"

> **原句 5:** "You would surrender your soul?" There is a shadow in his gaze when he says it, a hesitation in the words, less want than worry, and she knows then, she has him. "No," she says. "But only because you do not want it." And before he can protest, she continues, "You want me." Luc says nothing, but his eyes brighten, his interest piqued. "You were right," she says. "I am not one of them. Not anymore. And I am tired of losing. Tired of mourning everything I ever try to love." She reaches out to touch Luc's cheek. "But I won't lose you. And you won't lose me. So yes." She looks straight into his eyes. "Do this, and I will be yours, as long as you want me by your side."

"你会交出你的灵魂吗？"当他说时他的目光中有一个阴影，话语中有一丝犹豫，更少想要而是担心，她知道那时，她有了他。"不，"她说。"但只是因为你不想得到它。"在他能抗议之前，她继续，"你想要我。"Luc 什么也没说，但他的眼睛亮了起来，他的兴趣被激起。"你是对的，"她说。"我不是他们中的一个。不再是了。我厌倦了失去。厌倦了我曾经试图爱的每件事的哀悼。"她伸手触摸 Luc 的脸颊。"但我不会失去你。你也不会失去我。所以是的。"她直视他的眼睛。"这样做，我将属于你，只要你想让我在你身边。"

**关键词**：surrender / soul / shadow / gaze / hesitation / worry / has him / protest / want / brighten / interest / piqued / right / anymore / tired / losing / mourning / love / cheek / lose / yours / side / breathe / world / tips / falters / falling / smiles / emerald / victory / accept / fold / bows / head / relief / fingers / chin / face / kisses / night / met / swift / deep / hungry / teeth / skate / lip / copper / tongue / done

**为什么这样写**："yours" = 你的 = 我将属于你 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"accept" = 接受 = 我接受 = 他的选择 = 他的力量 = 他的自由。
