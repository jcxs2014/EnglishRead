# ch31. The First Date

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Addie 和 Henry 在布鲁克林酒吧的第一次约会——她害怕让他离开视线，因为害怕他会忘记她。
- **情感弧线位置**：转折后的不确定 —— Addie 害怕这一刻会消失。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：First Date（第一次约会）—— 但这是三百年来第一次有人记得她。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从享受当下到恐惧失去，Henry 的孤独与她的孤独呼应。
- **叙事手法**：Addie 的"afraid"三连 = 她的恐惧；Henry 的"penny for your thoughts" = 他的关心。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** She follows Henry to a bar that's too crowded, too loud. All the bars in Brooklyn are like that, too little space for too many bodies, and the Merchant is apparently no exception, even on a Thursday. Addie and Henry are crammed into a narrow patio out back, bundled together under an awning, but she still has to lean in to hear his voice over the noise.

她跟着 Henry 去了一家太拥挤、太吵的酒吧。布鲁克林的所有酒吧都是这样，空间太少，人太多，而 The Merchant 显然也不例外，即使在周四。Addie 和 Henry 被挤在后院狭窄的露台上，挤在一个雨棚下，但她仍然必须倾身才能在噪音中听到他的声音。

**关键词**：too crowded, too loud / too little space for too many bodies / crammed / narrow patio / bundled together / lean in / hear his voice over the noise

**为什么这样写**：酒吧的拥挤 = 他们的亲密。"bundled together" —— 挤在一起 = 物理上的亲密。"lean in" —— 倾身 = 她想靠近他。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"lean in" = 她想靠近他，不仅是身体上。

---

> **原句 2:** "Where are you from?" she starts. "Upstate. Newburgh. You?" "Villon-sur-Sarthe," she says. The words ache a little in her throat. "France? You don't have an accent." "I moved around."

"你从哪里来？"她开始。"上州。纽堡。你？""Villon-sur-Sarthe，"她说。这些话在她喉咙里有点痛。"法国？你没有口音。""我搬过家。"

**关键词**：Where are you from / Upstate / Newburgh / Villon-sur-Sarthe / ache a little in her throat / don't have an accent / moved around

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"Villon-sur-Sarthe"。"ache a little in her throat" —— 喉咙痛 = 她的过去。"don't have an accent" —— 没有口音 = 三百年磨去了她的法国口音。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"moved around" = 三百年搬过无数次。

---

> **原句 3:** Plus, she's afraid. Afraid to let him walk away. Afraid to let him out of sight. Whatever this is, a blip, a mistake, a beautiful dream, or a piece of impossible luck, she's afraid to let it go. Let him go. One wrong step, and she'll wake up. One wrong step, and the thread will snap, the curse will shudder back into place, and it will be over, and Henry will be gone, and she will be alone again.

而且，她害怕。害怕让他走开。害怕让他离开视线。无论这是什么，一个错误、一个失误、一个美丽的梦，或一点不可能的运气，她害怕让它走。让他走。一个错误的步骤，她就会醒来。一个错误的步骤，线就会断裂，诅咒就会颤抖回原位，它就会结束，Henry 就会离开，她就会再次孤独。

**关键词**：afraid / let him walk away / let him out of sight / blip / mistake / beautiful dream / impossible luck / let it go / let him go / One wrong step / wake up / thread will snap / curse will shudder back into place / alone again

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"恐惧"。"afraid" 重复三次 = 她的恐惧。"thread will snap" —— 线会断裂 = 连接会消失。"curse will shudder back into place" —— 诅咒会颤抖回原位 = 他会忘记她。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"alone again" = 她三百年孤独。

---

> **原句 4:** "Penny for your thoughts," he calls over the crowd. She smiles. "I can't wait for summer." It's not a lie. It has been a long, damp spring, and she is tired of being cold. Summer means hot days, and nights where the light lingers. Summer means another year alive. Another year without—

"你在想什么，"他在人群中喊。她微笑。"我等不及夏天了。"这不是谎言。这是一个漫长、潮湿的春天，她厌倦了寒冷。夏天意味着炎热的白天，和光线徘徊的夜晚。夏天意味着又一年活着。又一年没有——

**关键词**：Penny for your thoughts / can't wait for summer / long, damp spring / tired of being cold / hot days / light lingers / another year alive / Another year without—

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"summer"。"can't wait for summer" —— 等不及夏天 = 她想活下去。"another year alive" —— 又一年活着 = 她的不朽。"Another year without—" —— 又一年没有—— = 她没有说完（没有 Luc？没有 Henry？）。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"Another year without—" = 她没有说完的话 = 她的恐惧。

---

> **原句 5:** "If you could have one thing," cuts in Henry, "what would it be?" He studies her, squinting at her as if she's a book, not a person; something to be read. She stares back at him like he's a ghost. A miracle. An impossible thing. This, she thinks, but she lifts her empty glass and says, "Another beer."

"如果你能有一样东西，"Henry 插嘴，"你会要什么？"他研究她，眯着眼看她好像她是一本书，不是一个人；某种要被阅读的东西。她盯着他看好像他是一个幽灵。一个奇迹。一个不可能的东西。这个，她想，但她举起空杯子说，"再来一杯啤酒。"

**关键词**：If you could have one thing / what would it be / studies her / squinting / as if she's a book / not a person / something to be read / stares back / ghost / miracle / impossible thing / This / Another beer

**为什么这样写**：Henry 的"问题"。"If you could have one thing" —— 如果你能有一样东西 = 他的问题。"as if she's a book" —— 好像她是一本书 = 他想读懂她。"This" —— 这个 = 她的答案（他）。"Another beer" —— 再来一杯啤酒 = 她的回避。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"This" = 她的答案是他，但她不能说。

## 一句话总结

Addie 和 Henry 在布鲁克林酒吧的第一次约会——她害怕让他离开视线，因为害怕他会忘记她。但当他说"你在想什么"时，她想说"这个"（你），却只说"再来一杯啤酒"。
