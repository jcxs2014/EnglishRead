# ch87. The Speakeasy and the Ring

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1928 年芝加哥，Addie 在禁酒令时期的秘密酒吧中，十四年来第一次见到 Luc——"我赢了。我没叫你，你却来了。"——"你属于我。"——"我宁愿做幽灵。"
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 → 低谷 —— 从"快乐"到"相遇"到"冲突"到"分离"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Meeting（相遇）—— 十四年后的第一次见面；The Ring（戒指）—— 她拒绝戴上 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"快乐"到"愤怒"—— 她说"我宁愿做幽灵" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **叙事手法**："ghost" = 幽灵 = 她的存在 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由 = 她的选择。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** There is an angel over the bar. A stained-glass panel, lit from behind, with a single figure, chalice raised and hand outstretched, as if calling you to prayer. But this is no church. Speakeasies are like weeds these days, springing up between the stones of Prohibition. This one has no name, save the angel with its cup, the number XII over the door—twelve, the hour of midday, and of midnight—the velvet curtains and chaises that lounge like sleepers round the wooden floor, the masks given to the patrons at the door. It is, like most of them, only a rumor, a secret passed from mouth to liquored mouth. And Addie loves it. There is a wild fervor to this place. She dances—sometimes alone, and sometimes in the company of strangers. Loses herself in the jazz that rocks against the walls, rebounds, filling the crowded space with music. She dances, until the feathers of her mask cling to her cheeks, and Addie is breathless, and flushed, and only then does she retreat, falling into a leather chair.

吧台上方有一个天使。一块彩色玻璃面板，从后面照亮，有一个单一的形象，举起杯，伸出手，仿佛在召唤你祈祷。但这不是教堂。秘密酒吧就像杂草一样，在禁酒令的石头之间涌现。这个没有名字，除了带杯的天使，门上方的数字 XII——十二，中午和午夜的时刻——像睡觉者一样靠在木地板周围的丝绒窗帘和长椅，给顾客的门上的面具。就像大多数一样，它只是一个谣言，一个从嘴到醉酒的嘴的秘密。Addie 爱这个地方。这个地方有一种狂野的狂热。她跳舞——有时独自一人，有时在陌生人的陪伴中。在爵士乐中迷失自己，爵士乐在墙上摇晃，反弹，用音乐填满拥挤的空间。她跳舞，直到面具的羽毛贴着她的脸颊，Addie 气喘吁吁，脸红，只有然后她才退却，落入皮椅。

**关键词**：angel / speakeasy / weeds / masks / fervor

**为什么这样写**："weeds" = 杂草 = 秘密酒吧 = 她的存在 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。"masks" = 面具 = 秘密 = 她的存在 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"狂野的狂热" = 她的存在 = 她的自由 = 她的爱 = 她的力量。

> **原句 2:** It is almost midnight, and her fingers drift like the hands of a clock up to her throat, where the ring hangs on a silver cord, the wooden band warm against her skin. It is always within reach. Once, when the cord snapped, she thought it lost, only to find it safe within the pocket of her blouse. Another time, she left it on a windowsill, and found it hours later at her neck again. The only thing she doesn't lose. She toys with it, a lazy habit now, like curling a lock of hair around one finger. She skims the edge of the band with her nail, twirls it, careful to never let the ring slide over her knuckle. She has reached for it a hundred times: when she was lonely, when she was bored, when she saw a thing of beauty and thought of him. But she is too stubborn, and he is too proud, and she is determined to win this round. Fourteen years she has resisted the urge to put it on. And fourteen years he has not come. So she was right—it is a game. Another kind of forfeit, a lesser version of surrender.

快到午夜了，她的手指像钟一样飘到她的喉咙，戒指挂在银绳上，木带温暖贴着她的皮肤。它总是在触手可及的地方。有一次，绳子断了，她以为它丢了，结果发现它在她衬衫的口袋里安全。另一次，她把它留在窗台上，几小时后在她的脖子上又找到了。她唯一不会失去的东西。她玩弄它，现在是一个懒惰的习惯，像把一缕头发缠绕在一根手指上。她用指甲划过边缘，旋转它，小心地永远不要让戒指滑过她的指关节。她伸手拿了一百次：当她孤独时，当她无聊时，当她看到美丽的东西并想到他时。但她太固执，他太骄傲，她决心赢得这一轮。十四年来她抵制了戴上它的冲动。十四年他没有来。所以她是对的——这是一场游戏。另一种类型的投降，一种较小版本的投降。

**关键词**：ring / reach / stubborn / game / forfeit / surrender

**为什么这样写**："reach" = 触手可及 = 戒指 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"forfeit" = 投降 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"较小版本的投降" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 3:** "I win," she says, sinking into his booth. And even though he folded first, his eyes are bright with triumph. "How so?" "I didn't call, and yet you came." His chin lifts, a study in disdain. "You assume I'm here for you." "I forget," she says, sliding into his smooth, low cadence. "There are so many maddening humans around to swindle out of their souls." A wry smile tugs at perfect lips. "I promise, Adeline, few are as maddening as you." "Few?" she teases. "I'll have to try harder." He lifts a glass, and tips it toward the bar. "The fact remains, you have come to me. This place is mine."

"我赢了，"她说，沉入他的包厢。即使他先认输，他的眼睛也闪烁着胜利。"怎么讲？""我没叫你，你却来了。"他的下巴抬起，一种蔑视的研究。"你以为我是为你来的。""我忘了，"她说，滑入他平滑、低沉的韵律。"周围有这么多令人发狂的人类可以骗取他们的灵魂。"一个苦笑拉扯着完美的嘴唇。"我保证，Adeline，很少有人像你这样令人发狂。""很少？"她调侃。"我必须更努力。"他举起酒杯，向酒吧倾斜。"事实仍然是，你已经来找我了。这个地方是我的。"

**关键词**：win / triumph / maddening / mine

**为什么这样写**："win" = 赢 = 她的胜利 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。"maddening" = 令人发狂 = 他们的关系 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"我赢了" = 她的胜利 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 4:** Addie looks around, and suddenly, it is obvious. She sees the markings everywhere. Realizes, for the first time, that the angel above the bar has no wings. That the curls rising around his face are black. That the band she took for a halo might as well be moonlight. And she wonders what it was that drew her here the first time. Wonders if they are like magnets, she and Luc. If they have circled each other for so long that now they share an orbit. It will become a hobby of his, these kinds of clubs. He will plant them in a dozen cities, tend them like gardens, and grow them wild. As plentiful as churches, he will say, and twice as popular. And long after the days of Prohibition, they will still flourish, catering to many tastes, and she will wonder if it is the energy that stokes him, or if they are a grooming ground for souls. A place to ply, and pry, and promise. And in a way, a place to pray, albeit a different kind of worship.

Addie 环顾四周，突然，很明显了。她到处看到标记。第一次意识到，吧台上方的天使没有翅膀。他脸上卷起的卷发是黑色的。她以为是光环的带子也可能是月光。她想知道是什么第一次把她吸引到这里。想知道她和 Luc 是否像磁铁。如果他们彼此绕了这么久，现在他们共享一个轨道。这将成为他的一种爱好，这种类型的俱乐部。他将在十几个城市种植它们，像花园一样照料它们，让它们野生生长。他会说，像教堂一样多，两倍受欢迎。在禁酒令的日子过去很久后，它们仍然会繁荣，迎合许多品味，她会想知道是能量激励他，还是他们是灵魂的梳理场所。一个经营、打听、承诺的地方。在某种程度上，一个祈祷的地方，尽管是一种不同的崇拜。

**关键词**：wings / halo / magnets / orbit / souls

**为什么这样写**："magnets" = 磁铁 = 他们的关系 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。"orbit" = 轨道 = 他们的关系 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"共享一个轨道" = 他们的关系 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。

> **原句 5:** "So you see," says Luc, "perhaps I win." Addie shakes her head. "It is only chance," she says. "I did not call." He smiles, gaze falling to the ring against her skin. "I know your heart. I felt it falter." "But I didn't." "No," he says, the word nothing but a breath. "But I was tired of waiting." "So you missed me," she says with a smile, and there is the briefest glimpse in those green eyes. A fracture of light. "Life is long, and humans boring. You are better company." "You forget that I am human." "Adeline," he says, a shade of pity in his voice. "You have not been human since the night we met. You will never be human again."

"所以你看，"Luc 说，"也许我赢了。"Addie 摇头。"这只是机会，"她说。"我没有叫。"他微笑，目光落在她皮肤上的戒指。"我了解你的心。我感觉到了它的动摇。""但我没有。""不，"他说，这个词只是一口气。"但我厌倦了等待。""所以你想我了，"她微笑着说，那双绿色的眼睛里有最短暂的瞥见。一道光的生活。"很长，人类很无聊。你是更好的陪伴。""你忘了我是人类。""Adeline，"他说，声音中带着一丝怜悯。"自从我们那晚见面以来，你就不再是人类了。你再也不会是人类了。"

**关键词**：win / chance / heart / falter / human

**为什么这样写**："falter" = 动摇 = 她的心 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"human" = 人类 = 她不再人类 = 她的诅咒 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

> **原句 6:** "I am still human," she says, voice tightening around the words as if they were her name. "You move among them like a ghost," he says, his forehead bowing against hers, "because you are not one of them. You cannot live like them. You cannot love like them. You cannot belong with them." His mouth hovers over her own, his voice dropping to nothing but a breeze. "You belong to me." There is a sound like thunder in the back of his throat. "With me." And when she looks up into his eyes, she sees a new shade of green, and knows exactly what it is. The color of a man off-balance. His chest rises and falls as if it were a human thing. Here is a place to put the knife. "I would rather be a ghost." And for the first time, the darkness flinches. Draws back like shadows in the face of light. His eyes go pale with anger, and there is the god she knows, the monster she has learned to face. "Suit yourself," mutters Luc, and she waits for him to bleed into the dark, braces for the sudden, reaching void, expects to be swallowed up and spit out on the other side of the world. But Luc does not vanish, and neither does she. He nods at the club. "Go on, then," he says, "go back to them." And she would rather he had banished her. Instead, she rises, even though she's lost her taste for drinks, for dancing, for any kind of company. It is like stepping out of sunlight, the humid room gone cold against her skin, as he sits there in his velvet booth, and she goes through the motions of her night, and for the first time she feels the space between the humans and herself, and fears that he is right. In the end, she is the one to leave. And the next day, the speakeasy is boarded up, and Luc is gone. And just like that, new lines are drawn, the pieces set, the battle started. She will not see him again until the war.

"我仍然是人类，"她说，声音在词语周围收紧，好像它们是她的名字。"你像幽灵一样在他们中间移动，"他说，他的额头靠在她的额头上，"因为你不是他们中的一个。你不能像他们一样生活。你不能像他们一样爱。你不能和他们在一起。"他的嘴悬在她的上方，他的声音低到只有一口气。"你属于我。"他的喉咙里有一种像雷一样的声音。"和我。"当她抬头看着他的眼睛时，她看到一种新的绿色，知道它是什么。一个失去平衡的人的颜色的。他的胸膛起伏，好像它是一个人类的东西。这里是一个放刀的地方。"我宁愿做幽灵。"第一次，黑暗退缩。像面对光的影子一样后退。他的眼睛因愤怒而变白，有她知道的神，她学会面对的怪物。"随你便，"Luc 咕哝，她等着他融入黑暗，准备突然的、伸手可及的虚空，期望被吞没并在世界的另一端吐出来。但 Luc 没有消失，她也没有。他向俱乐部点头。"去吧，"他说，"回到他们身边。"她宁愿他放逐了她。相反，她站起来，尽管她已经失去了对饮料、跳舞、任何类型陪伴的品味。就像走出阳光，潮湿的房间在她皮肤上变冷，当他坐在他的丝绒包厢里时，她经历她夜晚的动作，第一次感到人类和自己之间的空间，并害怕他是对的。最后，她是离开的人。第二天，秘密酒吧被封起来，Luc 走了。就这样，新的线被画出来，棋子被摆放，战斗开始了。她不会再见到他直到战争。

**关键词**：ghost / belong / knife / war

**为什么这样写**："ghost" = 幽灵 = 她宁愿做幽灵 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"knife" = 刀 = 放刀的地方 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"我宁愿做幽灵" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。
