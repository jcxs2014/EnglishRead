# ch85. The Tree and the Ring

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1914 年法国维隆，Addie 发现 Estele 的树被雷劈毁，Luc 出现道歉——"对不起"——他归还她的戒指——"戴上它，我就会来"——"我们永远不可能扯平。"
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 —— 从"悲伤"到"愤怒"到"领悟"到"选择"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Loss（失去）—— 树被摧毁 = 她的悲伤；The Gift（礼物）—— 他归还戒指 = 他的爱 = 她的选择。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"悲伤"到"选择"—— 她拒绝使用戒指 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **叙事手法**："lightning" = 闪电 = 自然的力量 = 他的力量 = 她的力量。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** It is pouring in Villon. The Sarthe swells against its banks, and the rain turns footpaths into muddy rivers. It spills over doorways, fills her ears with the steady white noise of rushing water, and when Addie closes her eyes, the years dissolve, and she is ten again, she is fifteen, she is twenty, her skirts wet and hair flying behind her as she races barefoot through a countryside washed clean. But then she opens her eyes again, and it has been two hundred years, and she cannot deny that the little village of Villon has changed. She recognizes less and less, finds more and more strange. Here and there she can still make out the place that she once knew, but her memories are threadbare, those years before her deal left to weather and fade. And yet, some things are constant. The stretch of road that runs through the town. The small church sitting in the center. The low wall of the graveyard, immune to the slow procession of change.

维隆在下大雨。萨尔特河在岸边膨胀，雨水把小路变成泥泞的河流。它漫过门口，用流水的稳定白噪音充满她的耳朵，当 Addie 闭上眼睛，岁月溶解，她又十岁，十五岁，二十岁，裙子湿了，头发在身后飞舞，赤脚穿过洗净的乡村。但然后她再次睁开眼睛，已经两百年了，她不能否认维隆的小村庄已经改变。她越来越不认识，发现越来越多奇怪。在这里和那里，她仍然能认出她曾经知道的地方，但她的记忆已经破旧，那些交易之前的岁月任凭风吹雨打。然而，有些东西是不变的。穿过城镇的道路。坐在中心的小教堂。墓地低矮的墙壁，不受缓慢变化的影响。

**关键词**：pouring / dissolve / threadbare / constant

**为什么这样写**："dissolve" = 溶解 = 岁月溶解 = 她的记忆 = 她的过去 = 她的存在。"threadbare" = 破旧 = 她的记忆 = 她的过去 = 正在消失。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"破旧" = 她的记忆 = 她的过去 = 正在消失 = 她的恐惧。

> **原句 2:** But the tree is not there. The grave slumps, weary, in its plot, the stone moldering and cracked, but the beautiful tree, with its wide limbs and its deep roots, is gone. Nothing but a jagged stump remains. Addie lets out an audible gasp, sinking to her knees, runs her hands over the dead and splintered wood. No. No, not this. She has lost so much, and mourned it all before, but for the first time in years, she is struck with a loss so sharp it steals her breath, her strength, her will. Grief, deep as a well, opens inside her. What is the point in planting seeds? Why tend them? Why help them grow? Everything crumbles in the end. Everything dies. And she is all that's left, a solitary ghost hosting a vigil for forgotten things. She squeezes her eyes shut and tries to conjure Estele, tries to summon the old woman's voice, so she can tell her it will be all right, that it's just wood—but the voice is gone, lost beneath the raging storm.

但树不在那里。坟墓在它的地块中疲惫地倾斜，石头腐烂和开裂，但那棵美丽的树，有着宽阔的枝干和深深的根，不见了。只剩下一个参差不齐的树桩。Addie 发出可闻的喘息，跪下，双手抚摸着死亡和碎裂的木头。不。不，不是这个。她失去了很多，以前都哀悼过，但多年来第一次，她被如此尖锐的损失击中，它偷走了她的呼吸，她的力量，她的意志。悲伤，深如井，在她里面打开。种子的意义是什么？为什么照料它们？为什么帮助它们生长？一切最终都会崩溃。一切都死了。她是一个孤独的灵魂，为被遗忘的东西守夜。她闭上眼睛，试图召唤 Estele，试图召唤老妇人的声音，这样她就能告诉她没关系，那只是木头——但声音消失了，在暴风雨中迷失。

> **原句 3:** "I'm sorry," he says, and it is the first time she has ever heard those words in that silken voice, the only time they will ever sound honest. "Did you do this?" she whispers without looking up. And to her surprise, Luc kneels beside her on the sodden earth. His own clothes do not seem to dampen. "You cannot blame me for every loss," he says. She doesn't realize she is shivering until his arm folds around her shoulders, until she feels her limbs trembling against the steady weight of his. "I know I can be cruel," he says. "But nature can be crueler." It is obvious, now, the charred line along the center of the stump. The swift, hot shear of lightning. It doesn't ease the loss. She cannot stand to look upon the tree. She cannot bear to linger here any longer. "Come," he says, drawing her to her feet, and she does not know where they are going, and she does not care, so long as it is somewhere else. Addie turns her back on the ruined stump, the tombstone worn to nothing. Even rocks, she thinks as she follows Luc away from the graveyard, and the village, and the past. She will never go back.

"对不起，"他说，这是她第一次在那丝绸般的声音中听到这些话，也是唯一一次它们听起来诚实。"你做了这件事吗？"她低语，没有抬头。令她惊讶的是，Luc 跪在她旁边的湿地上。他自己的衣服似乎没有变湿。"你不能把每件事都怪我，"她说。她没有意识到她在颤抖，直到他的手臂环绕她的肩膀，直到她感觉到她的四肢在他稳定的重量下颤抖。"我知道我可能很残忍，"他说。"但自然可能更残忍。"现在很明显了，沿着树桩中心的烧焦线。闪电的迅速、炽热的剪切。它没有减轻损失。她无法忍受看那棵树。她无法忍受在这里再逗留。"来，"他说，把她拉起来，她不知道他们要去哪里，她不在乎，只要是在别处。Addie 转身离开毁坏的树桩，墓碑磨损得什么都没有。甚至石头，当她跟着 Luc 离开墓地，村庄，过去时她想。她永远不会回去。

**关键词**：sorry / cruel / lightning / never go back

**为什么这样写**："sorry" = 对不起 = 他的道歉 = 他的爱 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"never go back" = 永不回去 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"永不回去" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 4:** "Happy anniversary, my Adeline." She looks at him, lips parting with their usual retort, but then stops short. If she is his—then by now he must be hers as well. "Happy anniversary, my Luc," she answers, just to see the face he'll make. She is rewarded with a raised brow, the crooked upturn of his mouth, the green of his eyes shifting in surprise. Then Luc looks down, turns the glass of port between his fingers. "You told me once that we were alike," he says, almost to himself. "Both of us … lonely. I loathed you for saying it. But I suppose in some ways you were right. I suppose," he goes on slowly, "there is something to the idea of company." It is the closest he has ever come to sounding human. "Do you miss me," she asks, "when you are not here?" Those green eyes drift up, the emerald even in the dark. "I am here, with you, more often than you think." "Of course," she says, "you come and go whenever you want. I have no choice but to wait." His eyes darken with pleasure. "Do you wait for me?" And now it is Addie who looks away. "You said it yourself. We all crave company."

"周年快乐，我的 Adeline。"她看着他，嘴唇分开准备他们通常的反驳，但然后停住了。如果她是他的——那么现在他也必须是她的。"周年快乐，我的 Luc，"她回答，只是为了看看他会做出什么表情。她得到一个扬起的眼眉，嘴角的歪斜上扬，他的绿眼睛在惊讶中变化。然后 Luc 低头，在手指间转动波特酒杯。"你曾经告诉我们很像，"他说，几乎对自己说。"我们两个……孤独。我恨你这么说。但我想在某种程度上你是对的。我想，"他继续慢慢地说，"陪伴的想法有一些道理。"这是最接近他听起来像人的时候。"你想我吗，"她问，"当你不在这里的时候？"那些绿眼睛飘起来，即使在黑暗中也是翡翠。"我在这里，和你在一起，比你想象的更频繁。""当然，"她说，"你想来就来想走就走。我别无选择，只能等待。"他的眼睛因快乐而变暗。"你等我吗？"现在是 Addie 移开目光。"你自己说的。我们都渴望陪伴。"

**关键词**：anniversary / lonely / company / miss / wait

**为什么这样写**："anniversary" = 周年纪念 = 他们的交易 = 他的爱 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。"company" = 陪伴 = 他的爱 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"我们都渴望陪伴" = 他的爱 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 5:** And if you could call on me, as I call on you?" Her heart quickens a little. She does not look up, and that is why she sees it, rolling toward her on the table. A slim band, carved of pale ash wood. It is a ring. It is her ring. The gift she made to the dark that night. The gift he scorned, and turned to smoke. The image conjured in a seaside church. But if it is an illusion now, it is an exceptional one. Here, the notch where her father's chisel bit a fraction too deep. There, the curve rubbed smooth as stone by years of worrying. It is real. It must be real. And yet— "You destroyed it." "I took it," says Luc, looking over his glass. "That is not the same thing." Anger flares in her. "You said it was nothing." "I said it was not enough. But I do not ruin beauty without reason. It was mine, for a time, but it was always yours." Addie marvels at the ring. "What must I do?" "You know how to summon gods." Estele's voice, faint as a breeze. You must humble yourself before them. "Put it on, and I will come." Luc leans back in his chair, the night breeze blowing through those raven curls. "There," he says. "Now we are even." "We will never be even," she says as she turns the ring over between finger and thumb, and decides she will not use it. It is a challenge. A game, parading as a gift. Not a war so much as a wager. A battle of wills. For her to don the ring, to call on Luc, would be to fold, to admit defeat. To surrender. She slips the token into the pocket of her skirts, forces her fingers to let go of the talisman.

"如果你能呼唤我，就像我呼唤你一样？"她的心跳加速了一点。她没有抬头，这就是为什么她看到它，在桌上向她滚来。一条细带，雕刻着淡灰木。它是一个戒指。它是她的戒指。她那天晚上给黑暗的礼物。他鄙视的礼物，变成了烟雾。在海边教堂召唤的图像。但如果它现在是一个幻觉，那也是一个特殊的。这里，她父亲凿子咬得太深的缺口。那里，被多年担忧磨得像石头一样光滑的曲线。它是真实的。它一定是真实的。然而——"你毁了它。""我拿了，"Luc 说，透过他的酒杯看。"那不是一回事。"愤怒在她心中燃烧。"你说它什么都不是。""我说这还不够。但我不会无缘无故地毁掉美丽。它曾经是我的，但一直是你的。"Addie 惊叹戒指。"我必须做什么？""你知道如何召唤神。"Estele 的声音，微弱如微风。你必须在他们面前谦卑。"戴上它，我就会来。"Luc 靠在椅背上，夜风吹过那些乌鸦卷发。"好了，"他说。"现在我们扯平了。""我们永远不会扯平，"她说，在手指和拇指间翻转戒指，并决定她不会使用它。这是一个挑战。一个伪装成礼物的游戏。与其说是战争，不如说是赌博。一场意志的较量。对她来说，戴上戒指，呼唤 Luc，就是屈服，承认失败。投降。她把信物塞进裙子的口袋，强迫她的手指放开护身符。

**关键词**：ring / summon / surrender / even

**为什么这样写**："ring" = 戒指 = 她的礼物 = 他的爱 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"surrender" = 投降 = 他想要她投降 = 她拒绝 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"我们永远不会扯平" = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱 = 她的选择。

> **原句 6:** Only then does she notice the tension in the air that night. It is an energy she's felt before, but cannot place, until Luc says, "There is about to be a war." She had not heard. He tells her of the archduke's assassination, his face a mask of grim displeasure. "I hate war," he says darkly. "I would have thought you fond of conflict." "The aftermath breeds art," he says. "But war makes believers out of cynics. Sycophants desperate for salvation, everyone suddenly clinging to their souls, clutching them close like a matron with her finest pearls." Luc shakes his head. "Give me back the Belle Epoque." "Who knew gods were so nostalgic?" Luc finishes his drink, and rises. "You should leave, before it starts." Addie laughs. It sounds almost as if he cares. The ring sits, a sudden weight in her pocket. He holds out his hand. "I can take you." She should have accepted, should have said yes. Should have let him lead her through the horrible dark and out again, and saved herself an ocean, a miserable week stowing away in the belly of a ship at sea, the beauty of the water tarnished by the unending nature of it. But she has learned too well to hold her ground. Luc shakes his head. "You are still a stubborn fool." She toys with staying, but after he is gone, she cannot help but conjure the shadows in his gaze, the grim way he spoke of the coming strife. It is a sign, when even gods and devils dread a fight. A week later, Addie caves, and boards a ship for New York. By the time she docks, the world is already at war.

只有到那时她才注意到那天晚上空气中的紧张。这是一种她以前感觉过的能量，但无法放置，直到 Luc 说，"即将有一场战争。"她没有听到。他告诉她大公被暗杀的消息，他的脸是阴沉不满的面具。"我恨战争，"他阴沉地说。"我以为你喜欢冲突。""后果孕育艺术，"他说。"但战争让愤世嫉俗者成为信徒。绝望的谄媚者寻求救赎，每个人突然紧紧抓住他们的灵魂，像贵妇抱着她最好的珍珠一样紧紧抓住。"Luc 摇头。"把美好年代还给我。""谁知道神这么怀旧？"Luc 喝完酒，站起来。"你应该在它开始之前离开。"Addie 笑。听起来几乎像他在乎。戒指坐着，突然的重量在她口袋里。他伸出手。"我可以带你。"她应该接受，应该说应该让他带领她穿过可怕的黑暗再出来，拯救自己一个海洋，在船的可怕一周，水的美丽被无尽的本性玷污。但她已经学会了坚守阵地。Luc 摇头。"你仍然是个固执的傻瓜。"她犹豫着留下，但在他离开后，她不禁召唤他目光中的阴影，他谈论即将到来的冲突的严峻方式。这是一个迹象，当甚至神和魔鬼都害怕战斗时。一周后，Addie 屈服了，登上前往纽约的船。当她靠岸时，世界已经在战争中。
