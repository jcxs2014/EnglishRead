# ch101. The Last Day

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：2014 年纽约，Henry 生命的最后一天——"这是一个普通的日子"——躺在床上听故事——饥饿让时钟开始滴答——"一秒一秒地活"——"和我在一起。"
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 —— 从"普通"到"床"到"饥饿"到"散步"到"雨"到"和我在一起"。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** It is such an ordinary day. They stay in bed, curled together in the nest of sheets, head to head and hands trailing over arms, along cheeks, fingers memorizing skin. He whispers her name, over and over, as if she can save the sound, bottle it up to use when he is gone. Addie, Addie, Addie. And despite it all, Henry is happy. Or at least, he tells himself he is happy, tells himself he is ready, tells himself he isn't afraid. And he tells himself that if they just stay here, in the bed, the day will last. If he holds his breath, he can keep the seconds from moving forward, pin the minutes between their tangled fingers.

这是一个普通的日子。他们躺在床上，蜷缩在被子的巢穴里，头对头，手沿着臂膀，沿着脸颊，手指记住皮肤。他一遍又一遍地低语她的名字，好像她可以保存声音，瓶装起来当他走了时使用。Addie，Addie，Addie。尽管如此，Henry 很快乐。或者至少，他告诉自己他很快乐，告诉自己他准备好了，告诉自己他不害怕。他告诉自己，如果他们只是留在这里，在床上，这一天会持续。如果他屏住呼吸，他可以让秒针不向前走，把分钟夹在他们纠缠的手指之间。

> **原句 2:** It is an unspoken plea but Addie seems to sense it, because she makes no motion to get up. Instead, she stays with him in bed, and tells him stories. Not of anniversaries—they have run out of July 29ths—but of Septembers and Mays, of quiet days, the kind no one else would remember. She tells him of fairy pools on the Isle of Skye, and the Northern Lights in Iceland, of swimming in a lake so clear she could see the bottom ten meters down, in Portugal—or was it Spain? These are the only stories he will never write down. It is his own failing; he cannot bring himself to unfold, to let go of Addie's hands and climb out of the bed, and grab the latest notebook from the shelf—there are six of them now, the last only half-filled, and he realizes it will stay that way, those last blank pages, his cramped cursive like a wall, a false end to an ongoing story, and his heart skips a little, a tiny stutter of panic, but he can't let it start, knows it will tear through him, the way a shiver turns a momentary chill into teeth-chattering cold, and he cannot lose his hold, not yet, not yet.

这是一个无声的恳求，但 Addie 似乎能感觉到，因为她没有起床的动作。相反，她和他一起躺在床上，给他讲故事。不是周年纪念——他们已经过完了 7 月 29 日——而是九月和五月，安静的日子，那种没有人会记得的。她告诉他斯凯岛上的仙女池，冰岛的北极光，在葡萄牙游泳的湖，清澈得可以看到十米深的底部——还是西班牙？这些是他永远不会写下的唯一故事。这是他自己的失败；他无法让自己展开，放开 Addie 的手，爬下床，从架子上拿最新的笔记本——现在有六个，最后一个只填了一半，他意识到它会保持那样，那些最后的空白页，他拥挤的草书像一堵墙，一个正在进行的故事的虚假结局，他的心跳了一下，一下小小的恐慌的结巴，但他不能让它开始，知道它会撕裂他，就像一阵颤抖把一时的寒意变成牙齿打颤的寒冷，他不能失去他的把握，还没有，还没有。

> **原句 3:** Sometime in the afternoon, he is suddenly hungry. Famished. He shouldn't be. It feels frivolous, and wrong, inconsequential now, but the hunger is swift and deep, and with its arrival, the clock begins to tick. He can't hold time at bay. It is racing forward now, rushing away. And Addie looks at him as if she can read his mind, see the storm building in his head. But she is sunshine. She is clear skies. She draws him out of bed, and into the kitchen, and Henry sits on a stool and listens as she makes anomelet and tells him about the first time she flew a plane, heard a song on the radio, saw a moving picture. This is the last gift she can give him, these moments he will never have. And this is the last gift he can give her, the listening.

下午某个时候，他突然饿了。非常饿。他不应该。它感觉轻浮，错误，无关紧要，但饥饿是迅速而深刻的，随着它的到来，时钟开始滴答。他无法阻止时间。它现在正在向前冲，冲走。Addie 看着他好像她能读懂他的头脑，看到风暴在他脑海中建造。但她是阳光。她是晴朗的天空。她把他从床上拉出来，进入厨房，Henry 坐在凳子上，听她做煎蛋卷，告诉他她第一次开飞机，在收音机上听到一首歌，看到一部电影。这是她能给他的最后礼物，这些他永远不会有的时刻。这是他能给她的最后礼物，倾听。

> **原句 4:** And he wishes they could climb back into bed with Book, but they both know there's no going back. And now that he's up, he cannot bear the stillness. He is all restless energy, and urgent need, and there isn't enough time, and he knows of course that there will never be. That time always ends a second before you're ready. That life is the minutes you want minus one. And so they get dressed, and they go out, and walk, wearing circles into the block as the panic begins to win. It is a hand pressing against weakened glass, a steady pressure on spreading cracks, but Addie is there, her fingers laced through his. "Do you know how you live three hundred years?" she says. And when he asks how, she smiles. "The same way you live one. A second at a time."

他希望他们能爬回床上和 Book 一起，但他们都知道没有回头路。现在他起来了，他无法忍受静止。他充满了不安的能量，和迫切的需要，没有足够的时间，他知道当然永远不会有。时间总是在你准备好前一秒结束。生活是你想要的分钟减一。所以他们穿好衣服，出去，走，在街区走出圆圈，因为恐慌开始赢。它是一只手压在变弱的玻璃上，稳定的压力在扩散的裂缝上，但 Addie 在那里，她的手指穿过他的。"你知道你怎么活三百年吗？"她说。当他怎么问时，她微笑。"和你活一年一样。一秒一秒地。"

> **原句 5:** And eventually his legs are tired, and the restlessness recedes, doesn't vanish but dulls to a manageable degree, and they go to the Merchant, and order food they do not eat, and order beers they do not drink because he cannot bear to dull these last few hours, as frightening as it is to face them sober. And he makes some comment about his last meal, laughs at the morbid thought of it, and Addie's smile falters, for just a second, and then he is apologizing, he is sorry, and she is folding herself around him, and the panic has its claws in him. The storm is brewing in his head, churning the sky on the horizon, but he doesn't fight it. He lets it come. Only when it starts raining does he realize the storm is real. He tips his head back, and feels the drip of rain on his cheeks, and thinks of the night they went to the Fourth Rail, the downpour that caught them breathless when they reached the street. He thinks of that before he thinks of the rooftop, and that is something.

最终他的腿累了，不安消退了，没有消失但变钝到可管理的程度，他们去 Merchant，点他们不吃的食物，点他们不喝的啤酒因为他无法忍受让这最后几个小时变钝，尽管清醒地面对它们很可怕。他对他的最后晚餐发表了一些评论，嘲笑它的病态想法，Addie 的微笑动摇了，就一秒钟，然后他在道歉，他很抱歉，她在他周围折叠自己，恐慌有它的爪子在他身上。风暴在他脑海中酝酿，在地平线上搅动天空，但他不战斗。他让它来。只有当它开始下雨时他才意识到风暴是真实的。他向后仰头，感觉雨滴在他的脸颊上，想起他们去第四轨的夜晚，当他们到达街道时让他们喘不过气的倾盆大雨。他在想到屋顶之前想到那，那是一些东西。

> **原句 6:** He feels so far from the Henry who climbed up there a year ago—or perhaps he's not that far at all. It is only a matter of steps, after all, from the street to the edge. But what he would give to go back down. God, what he would give for just another day. The sun is gone now, the light going thin, and he will never see it again, and the fear crashes into him, sudden and traitorous. It is a gust of wind, cutting through a too-still scene. He fights it back, not yet, not yet, not yet, and Addie squeezes his hand, so he won't blow away. "Stay with me," she says, and he answers, "I'm here." His fingers tighten on hers. He doesn't have to ask, she doesn't have to answer. There is an unspoken agreement that she will be there, with him, until the very end. That this time, he won't be alone. And he is okay. It is okay. It will be okay.

他觉得离一年前爬上那里的 Henry 如此之远——也许他根本没有那么远。毕竟，从街道到边缘只是几步的事。但他愿意付出什么来回去。上帝，他愿意付出什么只为再多一天。太阳现在消失了，光线变薄，他再也看不到它了，恐惧冲碎他，突然和背叛。它是一阵风，切割一个太静止的场景。他把它打回去，还没有，还没有，还没有，Addie 握紧他的手，这样他就不会吹走。"和我在一起，"她说，他回答，"我在这里。"他的手指在她的手上收紧。他不必问，她不必回答。有一个无声的协议，她会在那里，和他在一起，直到最后。这一次，他不会独自一人。他没事。没关系。会没事的。
