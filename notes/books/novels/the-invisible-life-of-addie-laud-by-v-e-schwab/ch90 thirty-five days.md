# ch90. Thirty-Five Days

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：2014 年纽约，Addie 戴上戒指召唤 Luc——"和我过夜"——他提出交易，她接受，回家发现 Henry 只剩三十五天。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 —— 从"理解"到"愤怒"到"交易"到"绝望"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Deal（交易）—— Luc 提出交易 = 她的选择 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"愤怒"到"接受"—— 她接受交易 = 她的选择 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。
- **叙事手法**："thirty-five days" = 三十五天 = 他的生命 = 他的选择 = 他的爱。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Now it all makes sense. He makes sense. This boy, who could never sit still, never waste time, never put off a single thing. This boy, who writes down every word she says, so she'll have something when he's gone, who doesn't want to lose even a single day, because he doesn't have that many more. This boy she's falling in love with. This boy, who will soon be gone.

现在一切都明白了。他明白了。这个男孩，从不能静坐，从不浪费时间，从推迟任何事。这个男孩，写下她说的每一个字，这样当他走了她会有一些东西，不想失去哪怕一天，因为他没有更多了。她爱上的这个男孩。这个男孩，很快就会离开。

**关键词**：makes sense / soon be gone

**为什么这样写**："makes sense" = 明白了 = 她理解 = 他的交易 = 他的选择 = 他的爱。"soon be gone" = 即将离开 = 他的生命 = 他的选择 = 他的爱。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"即将离开" = 他的生命 = 他的选择 = 他的爱 = 她的恐惧。

> **原句 2:** "How?" she asks. "How could you give up so much for so little?" Henry looks up at her, his face hollow. "In that moment," he says, "I would have given it for less." A year. It seemed like so long, once. Now it is no time at all. A year, and it is almost up, and all she can see is the curve of Luc's smile, the triumphant color of his eyes. They were not clever, they were not lucky, they were not slipping past his notice. He knew, of course he knew, and he let it come to this. He let her fall.

"怎么？"她问。"你怎么能用这么少的代价放弃这么多？"Henry 抬头看着她，他的脸空洞。"在那一刻，"他说，"我会用更少的代价放弃。"一年。曾经看起来那么长。现在根本不算什么。一年，快结束了，她能看到的就是 Luc 微笑的曲线，他眼睛的胜利颜色。他们不聪明，他们不幸运，他们没有溜过他的注意。他知道，当然他知道，他让它走到这一步。他让她坠入。

**关键词**：hollow / triumphant / fall

**为什么这样写**："hollow" = 空洞 = 他的脸 = 他的痛苦 = 他的选择 = 他的爱。"fall" = 坠入 = 她爱上他 = 她的选择 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"她坠入" = 她爱上他 = 她的选择 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。

> **原句 3:** She shoves her hand into her pocket, and of course the ring is there. It is always there. Grains of sand flake from the smooth wooden surface as Addie slides the band over her knuckle. It's been forty years since she last wore it, but the ring slips effortlessly on. She feels the wind, like a cool breath at her back, and turns, expecting to find Luc. But the street is empty—empty, at least, of shadows and promises and gods. She twists the ring around her finger. Nothing. "Show yourself!" she shouts down the block. Heads turn, but Addie doesn't care. They'll forget her soon enough, and even if she weren't a ghost, this is New York, a place immune to the actions of a stranger in the street. "Dammit," she swears. She wrenches the ring from her finger, and hurls it down the road, hears it bounce, and roll. And then the sound suddenly drops away. The nearest streetlight flickers out, and a voice comes from the dark. "All these years, and you still have such a temper."

她把手塞进口袋，当然戒指在那里。它总是在那里。沙粒从光滑的木头表面剥落，当 Addie 把戒指滑过她的指关节。她上次戴它已经四十年了，但戒指毫不费力地滑上。她感觉到风，像凉爽的呼吸在她身后，转身，期待找到 Luc。但街道是空的——至少，没有阴影和承诺和神。她扭动手指上的戒指。没有。"现身！"她沿街大喊。人们转头，但 Addie 不在乎。他们很快就会忘记她，即使她不是幽灵，这是纽约，一个对陌生人的行为免疫的地方。"该死，"她发誓。她从手指上拧下戒指，把它扔到路上，听到它弹跳，滚动。然后声音突然消失。最近的路灯闪烁熄灭，一个声音从黑暗中传来。"这么多年了，你还是这么大的脾气。"

**关键词**：ring / temper / empty

**为什么这样写**："ring" = 戒指 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"temper" = 脾气 = 她的愤怒 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"这么大的脾气" = 她的愤怒 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 4:** "Spend the night with me," he says. "Tomorrow. Let us have a proper anniversary. Give me that, and I'll consider freeing Mr. Strauss from his obligations." His mouth twitches. "If, that is, you can persuade me." It is a lie, of course. It is a trap, but Addie has no other choice. "I accept," she says, and the darkness smiles, and then dissolves around her. She stands on the sidewalk, alone, until her heart steadies, and then walks back into the Merchant. But Henry is gone.

"和我过夜，"他说。"明天。让我们有一个正式的周年纪念。给我那个，我会考虑把 Strauss 先生从他的义务中解放出来。"他的嘴抽搐。"如果你能说服我。"当然，这是一个谎言。这是一个陷阱，但 Addie 别无选择。"我接受，"她说，黑暗微笑，然后在她周围消散。她站在人行道上，独自一人，直到她的心脏稳定，然后走回商人。但 Henry 不见了。

He stares ahead, into the distance, the way he did that summer night on the rooftop, after the fireworks. And Addie realizes that she is going to lose him, the way she has lost everyone. And she doesn't know if she can do it, not again, not this time. Hasn't she lost enough?

她发现他在家里，坐在黑暗中。他在床的边缘，毯子仍然纠缠着他们下午的小睡。他盯着前方，远处，就像他在屋顶上那个夏夜，烟火之后。Addie 意识到她要失去他，就像她失去了所有人。她不知道她是否能做到，不是再一次，不是这一次。她还没有失去够多吗？

> **原句 6:** "I'm sorry," he whispers as she crosses to him. "I'm so sorry," he says, as she runs her fingers through his hair. "Why didn't you tell me?" she pleads. Henry is quiet for a moment, and then he says, "How do you walk to the end of the world?" He looks up at her. "I wanted to hold on to every step." A soft, shuddering sigh. "My uncle had cancer, when I was still in college. It was terminal. The doctors gave him a few months, and he told everyone, and do you know what they did? They couldn't handle it. They were so caught up in their grief, they mourned him before he was even dead. There's no way to un-know the fact that someone is dying. It eats away all the normal, and leaves something wrong and rotten in its place. I'm sorry, Addie. I didn't want you to look at me that way." She climbs into bed, and pulls him down beside her. "I'm sorry," he's saying, soft and steady as a prayer. They lie there, face-to-face, their fingers intertwined. "I'm sorry." And Addie forces herself to ask, "How long do you have left?" Henry swallows. "A month." The words land like a blow on tender skin. "A little more," he says. "Thirty-six days." "It's after midnight," Addie whispers. Henry exhales. "Then thirty-five." Her grip tightens around his, and his tightens back, and they hold on until it hurts, as if any minute someone might try to pull them apart, as if the other might slip free, and disappear.

"对不起，"当她走向他时他低声说。"我很抱歉，"他说，当她用手指穿过他的头发时。"你为什么不告诉我？"她恳求。Henry 沉默了一会儿，然后他说，"你怎么走到世界的尽头？"他看着她。"我想抓住每一步。"一声轻柔的颤抖的叹息。"我叔叔得了癌症，当我还在大学时。这是晚期。医生给了他几个月，他告诉了每个人，你知道他们做了什么吗？他们无法处理。他们太沉浸在悲伤中，他们在甚至他死之前就哀悼他。没有办法不知道某人正在死去的事实。它吞噬了所有正常的东西，留下一些错误和腐烂的东西。对不起，Addie。我不想让你那样看我。"她爬上床，把他拉到自己身边。"对不起，"他说，轻柔而稳定地像祈祷。他们躺在那里，面对面，手指交缠。"对不起。"Addie 强迫自己问，"你还剩多少时间？"Henry 吞咽。"一个月。"这些话像打击嫩皮肤。"多一点，"他说。"三十六天。""已经过了午夜，"Addie 低语。Henry 呼气。"那么三十五。"她的握紧他的手，他的握紧回来，他们抓住直到它受伤，好像随时有人可能试图把他们拉开，好像另一个可能滑落，消失。
