# ch95. Dinner with the Dark

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：2014 年纽约，Addie 和 Luc 在 Le Coucou 共进晚餐——"你想我吗？"——"我想你。一直。"——"如果爱我，就放了 Henry Strauss。"
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 —— 从"观察"到"回忆"到"表白"到"请求"到"拒绝"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Dinner（晚餐）—— 他们的晚餐 = 她的选择 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。
- **人物弧线提示**：Addie 从"观察"到"表白"—— 她说"我想你" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **叙事手法**："wolf at the edge of the woods" = 树林边缘的狼 = 他 = 她的恐惧 = 她的爱。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** The city slides past beyond the window, but Addie doesn't turn her head, doesn't admire the skyline of Manhattan, the buildings soaring to every side. Instead, she studies Luc, reflected in the darkened glass, the line of his jaw, the arc of his brow, angles drawn by her hand so many, many years ago. She is watching him, the way one watches a wolf at the edge of the woods, waiting to see what it will do.

城市在窗外滑过，但 Addie 没有转头，没有欣赏曼哈顿的天际线，建筑物向每一边高耸。相反，她研究 Luc，映在黑暗的玻璃中，他下巴的线条，他眉毛的弧度，这么多年前她亲手画的角。她看着他，就像一个人看着树林边缘的狼，等待看它会做什么。

**关键词**：wolf / woods / waiting

**为什么这样写**："wolf at the edge of the woods" = 树林边缘的狼 = 他 = 她的恐惧 = 她的爱 = 她的选择。"waiting" = 等待 = 她等待 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"等待看它会做什么" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 2:** "Do you remember the opera in Munich?" "I remember everything, Luc." "The way you looked at the players on that stage, as if you'd never seen theater before." "I'd never seen theater like that." "The wonder in your eyes, at the sight of something new. I knew then I'd never win."

"你还记得慕尼黑的歌剧吗？""我记得一切，Luc。""你看舞台上演员的方式，好像你以前从未看过戏剧。""我从未看过那样的戏剧。""你眼中的惊奇，看到新事物的样子。我知道那时我永远不会赢。**

**关键词**：opera / Munich / wonder / win

**为什么这样写**："wonder" = 惊奇 = 她看到新事物的样子 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"win" = 赢 = 他知道他永远不会赢 = 他的爱 = 她的诅咒。

**读者视角提示**：Luc 的"我永远不会赢" = 他的爱 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由 = 她的选择。

> **原句 3:** But as they eat, the host and servers stand against the walls, eyes open, empty, a bland expression on their faces. She has always hated this aspect of his power, and the careless way he wields it. She tips her glass in the direction of the puppets. "Send them away," she says, and he does. A silent gesture, and the servers disappear, and they are alone in the empty restaurant. "Would you do that to me?" she asks when they are gone. Luc shakes his head. "I could not," he says, and she thinks he means because he cared for her too much, but then he says, "I have no power over promised souls. Their will is their own." It is cold comfort, she thinks, but it is something.

但当他们吃饭时，主人和服务员站在墙边，眼睛睁开，空洞，脸上带着平淡的表情。她一直讨厌他力量的这个方面，以及他挥舞它的粗心方式。她把杯子指向木偶的方向。"让他们走，"她说，他做了。一个无声的手势，服务员消失了，他们独自在空荡荡的餐厅里。"你会对我这样做吗？"当他们走后她问。Luc 摇头。"我不能，"她说，她想他的意思是因为他太在乎她了，但然后他说，"我对许诺的灵魂没有权力。他们的是他们自己的。"她想，这是冰冷的安慰，但它是一些东西。

**关键词**：puppets / power / promised souls / will

**为什么这样写**："promised souls" = 许诺的灵魂 = 他对她没有权力 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"will" = 意志 = 他们的是他们自己的 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"冰冷的安慰" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 4:** "Tell me, Adeline," he says. "Have you missed me?" Of course she has missed him. She can tell herself, as she has told him, that she only missed being seen, or missed the force of his attention, the intoxication of his presence—but it is more than that. She missed him the way someone might miss the sun in winter, though they still dread its heat. She missed the sound of his voice, the knowing in his touch, the flint-on-stone friction of their conversations, the way they fit together. He is gravity. He is three hundred years of history. He is the only constant in her life, the only one who will always, always remember. Luc is the man she dreamed of when she was young, and then the one she hated most, and the one she loved, and Addie missed him every night that he was gone from her, and he deserved none of her pain because it was his fault, it was his fault no one else remembered, it was his fault that she lost and lost and lost, and she does not say any of that because it will change nothing, and because there is still one thing she hasn't lost. One piece of her story that she can save. Henry.

"告诉我，Adeline，"他说。"你想我吗？"当然她想他。她可以告诉自己，就像她告诉他的，她只是想念被看见，或者想念他关注的强度，他存在的陶醉——但这不止。她想他的方式就像有人可能会想念冬天的太阳，尽管他们仍然害怕它的炎热。她想他的声音，他触摸中的认识，他们对话的燧石摩擦，他们结合在一起的方式。他是重力。他是三百年的历史。她生活中唯一不变的人，唯一一个会永远永远记得的人。Luc 是她年轻时梦想的男人，然后是她最恨的人，也是她爱的人，Addie 每晚都想他当他从她身边消失，她不值得她的痛苦因为这是他的错，这是他的错没有别人记得，这是她的错她失去又失去又失去，她没有说任何那些因为它不会改变什么，因为还有一样东西她没有失去。她故事中她能拯救的一块。Henry。

**关键词**：missed / gravity / constant / fault / Henry

**为什么这样写**："gravity" = 重力 = 他是重力 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"constant" = 不变 = 唯一不变的人 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"Henry" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 5:** So Addie makes her gambit. She reaches across the table and takes Luc's hand, tells him the truth. "I missed you." His green eyes shimmer and shift at the words. He brushes the ring on her finger, traces the whorls in the wood. "How many times did you almost put it on?" he asks. "How often did you think of me?" And she assumes he is baiting her—until his voice softens to a whisper, the faintest roll of thunder in the air between them. "Because I thought of you. Always." "You didn't come." "You didn't call." She looks down at their tangled hands. "Tell me, Luc," she says. "Was any of it real?" "What is real to you, Adeline? Since my love counts for nothing?" "You are not capable of love." He scowls, his eyes flashing emerald. "Because I am not human? Because I do not wither and die?" "No," she says, drawing back her hand. "You are not capable of love because you cannot understand what it is to care for someone else more than yourself. If you loved me, you would have let me go by now."

所以 Addie 做出她的策略。她伸手越过桌子，握住 Luc 的手，告诉他真相。"我想你。"他的绿眼睛在话语中闪烁变化。他抚摸她手指上的戒指，追踪木头上的螺纹。"你几乎戴上它多少次？"他问。"你多久想我一次？"她以为他在引诱她——直到他的声音软化为低语，他们之间空气中最轻柔的雷声。"因为我一直想你。""你没来。""你没打。"她低头看着他们纠缠的手。"告诉我，Luc，"她说。"其中有真实的吗？""对你来说什么是真实的，Adeline？既然我的爱不算什么？""你没有能力爱。"他皱眉，他的眼睛闪烁翡翠。"因为我不是人类？因为我不会枯萎和死亡？""不，"她说，把手抽回。"你没有能力爱因为你不能理解关心别人比自己多是什么感觉。如果你爱我，你现在就会让我走了。

**关键词**：gambit / missed / real / love

**为什么这样写**："gambit" = 策略 = 她的策略 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"real" = 真实 = 其中有真实的吗 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"我想你" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 6:** Luc flicks his fingers. "What nonsense," he says. "It is because I love you that I won't. Love is hungry. Love is selfish." "You are thinking of possession." He shrugs. "Are they so different? I have seen what humans do to things they love." "People are not things," she says. "And you will never understand them." "I understand you, Adeline. I know you, better than anyone in this world." "Because you let me have no one else." She takes a steadying breath. "I know you won't spare me, Luc, and perhaps you are right, we do belong together. So if you love me, spare Henry Strauss. If you love me, let him go." His temper flashes through his face. "This is our night, Adeline. Do not ruin it with talk of someone else." The server has just set a pear tart on the table, but it turns to ash as Luc speaks, and Addie marvels, the way she always has, at the moodiness of gods.

Luc 轻弹手指。"什么废话，"他说。"就是因为我爱你我才不会。爱是饥饿的。爱是自私的。""你在想占有。"他耸肩。"它们有那么不同吗？我见过人类对他们爱的人做什么。""人不是东西，"她说。"你永远不会理解他们。""我理解你，Adeline。我了解你，比世界上任何人都好。""因为你让我没有别人。"她深吸一口气。"我知道你不会放过我，Luc，也许你是对的，我们确实属于彼此。所以如果你爱我，就放过 Henry Strauss。如果你爱我，就让他走。"他的愤怒闪过他的脸。"这是我们的夜晚，Adeline。不要用别人的谈话毁了它。"服务员刚把梨挞放在桌上，但当 Luc 说话时它变成了灰烬，Addie 惊叹，就像她一直那样，神的喜怒无常。

**关键词**：love / hungry / selfish / possession / spare / ash / moodiness

**为什么这样写**："hungry" = 饥饿 = 爱是饥饿的 = 他的爱 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。"ash" = 灰烬 = 梨挞变灰 = 他的愤怒 = 他的力量 = 她的恐惧。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"放过 Henry" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。
