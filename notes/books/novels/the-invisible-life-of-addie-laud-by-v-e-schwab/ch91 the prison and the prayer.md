# ch91. The Prison and the Prayer

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1944 年法国，Addie 在纳粹监狱中，第一次向 Luc 祈祷——他出现，救了她——"她呼唤，他来了。她请求，他回答了。"
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 → 高潮 —— 从"绝望"到"祈祷"到"拯救"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Prison（监狱）—— 纳粹监狱 = 她的诅咒 = 她的祈祷 = 他的拯救。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"绝望"到"祈祷"—— 她第一次向 Luc 祈祷 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **叙事手法**："pray" = 祈祷 = 她第一次向黑暗祈祷 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Her back hits the rough stone wall. The cell grinds shut, and German soldiers laugh beyond the bars as Addie slumps to the floor, coughing blood. A handful of men huddle in one corner of the cell, slouched and murmuring. At least they don't seem to care that she's a woman. The Germans have noticed. Though they caught her dressed in nondescript trousers and coat, though she kept her hair pulled back, she knew by the way they scowled and leered that they could tell her sex. She told them in a dozen different tongues what she would do if they came near, and they laughed, and satisfied themselves with beating her senseless.

她的背靠在粗糙的石墙上。牢房碾轧着关闭，德国士兵在栏杆外笑，Addie 跌坐在地上，咳血。几个人蜷缩在牢房的一角，无精打采地低语。至少他们似乎不在乎她是个女人。德国人注意到了。虽然他们抓住她时穿着不起眼的裤子和外套，虽然她把头发向后扎，她知道他们从他们怒视和斜视的方式可以看出她的性别。她用十二种不同的语言告诉他们如果他们靠近她会做什么，他们笑了，满足于把她打得失去知觉。

**关键词**：cell / blood / woman / beating

**为什么这样写**："cell" = 牢房 = 她的诅咒 = 她的监狱 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。"blood" = 血 = 她的痛苦 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"咳血" = 她的痛苦 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

> **原句 2:** She is a fool for thinking it would work. For thinking that forgettable was the same as invisible, that it would protect her here. She should have stayed in Boston, where the worst she had to worry about was wartime rations and winter cold. She should never have come back. It was foolish honor, and stubborn pride. It was the last war, and the fact she ran away, fled across the Atlantic instead of facing the danger at home. Because somehow, despite it all, that's what France will always be. Home.

她是个傻瓜，以为它会起作用。以为健忘和隐形是一样的，以为它会在这里保护她。她应该留在波士顿，那里她最担心的是战时配给和寒冷。她不应该回来。这是愚蠢的荣誉，和固执的骄傲。这是上次战争，以及她逃跑的事实，逃离大西洋而不是面对家里的危险。因为不知何故，尽管一切，法国永远是家。

**关键词**：fool / forgettable / invisible / home

**为什么这样写**："forgettable" = 健忘 = 她的诅咒 = 她的存在 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"home" = 家 = 法国 = 她的家 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"法国永远是家" = 她的家 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

> **原句 3:** Three years of ferrying secrets through Occupied France. Three years, only to end up here. In a prison outside Orleans. And it does not matter that they will forget her face. It does not matter, because these soldiers do not care about remembering. Here, all the faces are strange, and foreign, and nameless, and if she doesn't get out, she is going to disappear.

三年来在占领区法国传递秘密。三年，只落得在这里。在奥尔良外的监狱。他们不会记得她的脸这没关系。没关系，因为这些士兵不在乎记住。在这里，所有的脸都是陌生的，外国的，无名的，如果她出不去，她就会消失。

**关键词**：secrets / prison / disappear

**为什么这样写**："secrets" = 秘密 = 她在战争中的工作 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"disappear" = 消失 = 她的诅咒 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"她会消失" = 她的诅咒 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

> **原句 4:** And so, for the first time in centuries, Addie prays. She slides the wooden band over her finger, and holds her breath, expects to feel something, a stirring of magic, a rush of wind. But there is nothing. Nothing, and she wonders if, after all this time, it was just another trick, a way to lift her hopes, only to drop them, in the chance they might shatter. She has a curse ready on her tongue, when she feels the breeze—not biting, but warm, cutting through the prison cell, carrying the far-off scent of summer.

所以，几个世纪以来第一次，Addie 祈祷。她把木带滑过手指，屏住呼吸，期待感觉到什么，一阵魔法的骚动，一阵风的急流。但没有。没有，她想，在这段时间之后，这不过是另一个诡计，一种提升她希望的方式，只在它们可能粉碎的机会中放下它们。当她的舌头上准备好诅咒时，她感觉到微风——不刺骨，但温暖，穿过牢房，带着遥远的夏日气味。

**关键词**：pray / wind / breeze / summer

**为什么这样写**："pray" = 祈祷 = 几个世纪以来第一次 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"breeze" = 微风 = 他的出现 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"几个世纪以来第一次祈祷" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 5:** "Oh Adeline," he says, hand drifting down the icy bars. "What a state you're in." She manages a small, pained laugh. "Immortality breeds a high tolerance for risk." "There are things worse than death," he says, as if she does not already know. He looks around at the prison, brow furrowed in disdain. "Wars," he mutters. "Tell me you are not helping them." Luc almost looks offended. "Even I have limits." "You bragged to me once about the successes of Napoleon." He shrugs. "There is ambition, and there is evil. And as much as I'd like to create a roster of my past exploits, your life is the important one right now." He leans his elbows on the bars. "How do you plan to get out of this?"

"哦 Adeline，"他说，手沿着冰冷的栏杆飘落。"你这是什么状态。"她设法发出一个小的、痛苦的微笑。"永生培养了高度的冒险容忍力。""有比死亡更糟的事情，"他说，好像她已经知道。他看着监狱周围，皱眉表示蔑视。"战争，"他咕哝。"告诉我你没有帮助他们。"Luc 几乎看起来被冒犯了。"即使我也有限制。""你曾经向我吹嘘拿破仑的成功。"他耸肩。"有野心，有邪恶。尽管我想创造一份我过去功绩的名册，你的生命现在是重要的。"他靠在栏杆上。"你打算怎么出去？"

**关键词**：state / immortality / risk / death / wars / limits / ambition / evil

**为什么这样写**："immortality" = 永生 = 她的诅咒 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。"limits" = 限制 = 他也有限制 = 他的力量 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"高度的冒险容忍力" = 她的诅咒 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 6:** "Wait," she calls desperately, pushing into the bars—only to find the lock undone, the cell door swinging open beneath her weight. Luc looks back over his shoulder, and he almost smiles, turning toward her just enough to offer up his hand. She stumbles forward, out of the cell and into freedom, into him. And for a moment, the embrace is only that, and he is solid, and warm, folded around her in the dark, and it would be easy to believe that he is real, that he is human, that he is home. But then the world cracks wide, and the shadows swallow them whole. The prison gives way to nothingness, to blackness, to the wild dark. And when it parts, she is back in Boston, the sun just beginning to set, and she could kiss the ground in sheer relief. Addie pulls the jacket close around her, and sinks onto the curb, legs shaking, the wooden band still wrapped around her finger. She called, and he came. She asked, and he answered. And she knows he will hold it over her, and but right now, she does not care. She does not want to be alone. But by the time Addie looks up to thank him, he is gone。

"等等，"她绝望地喊，推向栏杆——只发现锁未上，牢房门在她体重下摇摆。Luc 回头看他，他几乎微笑，转向她刚好伸出手。她踉跄向前，走出牢房走向自由，走向他。有一瞬间，拥抱只是那样，他坚实，温暖，在黑暗中折叠在她周围，很容易相信他是真实的，他是人类，他是家。但然后世界裂开，阴影吞噬了他们。监狱让位于虚无，黑暗，狂野的黑暗。当它分开时，她回到波士顿，太阳刚刚开始落下，她可以亲吻地面纯粹地解脱。Addie 把外套拉紧，坐到路边，双腿颤抖，木带仍然缠绕在她的手指上。她呼唤，他来了。她请求，他回答了。她知道他会把它压在她身上，但现在，她不在乎。她不想独自一人。但当 Addie 抬头感谢他时，他已经走了。

**关键词**：wait / freedom / embrace / home / called / came / alone / gone

**为什么这样写**："embrace" = 拥抱 = 他的拯救 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"home" = 家 = 他是家 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"她呼唤，他来了" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。
