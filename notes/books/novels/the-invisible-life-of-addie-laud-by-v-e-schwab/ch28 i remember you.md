# ch28. I Remember You

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Addie 回到 The Last Word 书店，试图用《奥德赛》换书，但 Henry 记得她——三百年后第一次有人记得她。
- **情感弧线位置**：转折 —— 三百年来的第一次"被记住"，Addie 的世界被颠覆。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The One Who Remembers（记住你的人）—— Henry 是第一个打破诅咒的人。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从习惯性偷窃到被"记住"的震惊，她的世界出现了裂缝。
- **叙事手法**："I remember you" 重复三次 = 三百年等待的三个字；Henry 的记忆 = 诅咒的裂缝。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** The bookshop is busier today. A kid plays hide-and-seek with his imaginary friend while his father turns through a military history. A college student crouches, scanning the different editions of Blake, and the boy she met yesterday stands behind the counter.

书店今天更忙。一个孩子和他的隐形朋友玩捉迷藏，他的父亲翻阅着军事历史。一个大学生蹲下，扫视着 Blake 的不同版本，而她昨天遇到的男孩站在柜台后面。

**关键词**：busier today / hide-and-seek / imaginary friend / military history / scanning / editions / stands behind the counter

**为什么这样写**：书店的日常。"imaginary friend" —— 隐形朋友 = Addie 也是隐形的。"stands behind the counter" —— 他站在柜台后面 = 他是店员。

**读者视角提示**：书店的"busy" = Addie 可以在人群中隐身，但 Henry 记得她。

---

> **原句 2:** His black hair tumbles forward into his eyes, unruly, untamable. He pushes it back, but in seconds it has fallen forward again, making him look younger than he is. He has the kind of face, she thinks, that can't keep secrets well.

他的黑色头发向前落入他的眼睛，不驯服、不可驯服。他把它们推回去，但几秒钟后它们又向前落下，让他看起来比实际年轻。他有一种脸，她认为，不能很好地保守秘密。

**关键词**：tumbles forward / unruly, untamable / pushes it back / fallen forward again / look younger / can't keep secrets well

**为什么这样写**：Henry 的外貌。"unruly, untamable" —— 不驯服 = 他的性格。"can't keep secrets well" —— 不能保守秘密 = 他会说出真相。

**读者视角提示**：Henry 的"can't keep secrets" = 他会记住她（不能保守她的存在秘密）。

---

> **原句 3:** He studies her. A dark brow lifts behind his glasses. "Are you serious?" "I know," she says with a laugh, "hard to believe I already own this one in Greek but—" He rocks back on his heels. "You are serious." Addie falters, thrown off by the edge in his voice. "I just thought it was worth asking…" "This isn't a library," he chides. "You can't just trade one book for another." Addie straightens. "Obviously," she says, a little indignant. "But like I said, I didn't buy it. My friend did, and I just heard you tell Ms. Kline that—" His face hardens, the flat regard of a door slammed shut. "Word of advice. Next time you try to return a book, don't return it to the same person you stole it from the first time."

他研究她。一只黑眉在眼镜后面抬起。"你是认真的？""我知道，"她笑着说，"难以相信我已经在希腊语中拥有这一本，但他向后摇晃脚跟。"你是认真的。"Addie 动摇了，被他声音中的边缘扰乱。"我只是想值得问……""这不是图书馆，"他责备。"你不能只用一本书换另一本书。"Addie 挺直身体。"显然，"她说，有点愤慨。"但就像我说的，我没买。我的朋友买了，我刚才听到你对 Kline 小姐说——"他的脸变硬，像门砰地关上的平坦注视。"一句话建议。下次你试图还书时，不要还给你第一次偷它的同一个人。"

**关键词**：studies her / dark brow lifts / Are you serious / rocks back on his heels / falters / thrown off by the edge / This isn't a library / trade one book for another / face hardens / flat regard / door slammed shut / Word of advice / return a book / stole it from the first time

**为什么这样写**：Henry 的记忆。"This isn't a library" —— 他的口头禅。"don't return it to the same person you stole it from" —— 他记得她偷了书 = 他记得她。

**读者视角提示**：Henry 的"stole it from the first time" = 他记得她昨天偷了书 = 他打破了诅咒。

---

> **原句 4:** "I remember you." Three words, large enough to tip the world.

"我记得你。"三个字，足够大得颠覆世界。

**关键词**：I remember you / Three words / large enough to tip the world

**为什么这样写**：全书最关键的时刻。"I remember you" —— 三百年后第一次。"large enough to tip the world" —— 三个字颠覆了她的世界。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"tip the world" = 她的世界被三个字颠覆了。

---

> **原句 5:** Three hundred years. Three hundred years, and no one has said those words, no one has ever, ever remembered. She wants to grab him by the sleeve, wants to pull him forward, wants to know why, how, what is so special about a boy in a bookstore—but the man with the military history is waiting to pay, the kid clinging to his leg, and the boy with the glasses is glaring at her, and this is all wrong. She grips the counter, feels like she might faint. His eyes soften, just a fraction. "Please," he says under his breath. "Just go."

三百年。三百年，没有人说过那些话，没有人曾经、曾经记得。她想抓住他的袖子，想把他拉向前，想知道为什么、怎么，书店里的男孩有什么特别的——但带着军事历史的男人在等着付钱，孩子抱着他的腿，戴眼镜的男孩在瞪着她，这一切都错了。她抓住柜台，觉得她可能要晕倒。他的眼睛变软，只是一点。"求求你，"他低声说。"走吧。"

**关键词**：Three hundred years / no one has said those words / no one has ever, ever remembered / grab him by the sleeve / pull him forward / know why, how / what is so special / boy in a bookstore / waiting to pay / clinging to his leg / glaring at her / this is all wrong / grips the counter / feels like she might faint / eyes soften / Just go

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的震惊。"Three hundred years" —— 三百年。"no one has ever, ever remembered" —— 没有人曾经记得。"this is all wrong" —— 这一切都错了 = 诅咒被打破了。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"feels like she might faint" = 三百年后第一次被记住，她的世界被颠覆。

> **原句 6:** She opens her eyes, and sees him pointing to the door. Her feet won't move. They refuse to carry her away from those three words. I remember you. Three hundred years. Three hundred years, and no one has said those words, no one has ever, ever remembered. She wants to grab him by the sleeve, wants to pull him forward, wants to know why, how, what is so special about a boy in a bookstore—but the man with the military history is waiting to pay, the kid clinging to his leg, and the boy with the glasses is glaring at her, and this is all wrong. She grips the counter, feels like she might faint. His eyes soften, just a fraction. "Please," he says under his breath. "Just go."

她睁开眼睛，看到他指着门。她的脚不会动。它们拒绝把她带离那三个字。我记得你。三百年。三百年，没有人说过那些话，没有人曾经、曾经记得。她想抓住他的袖子，想把他拉向前，想知道为什么、怎么，书店里的男孩有什么特别的——但带着军事历史的男人在等着付钱，孩子抱着他的腿，戴眼镜的男孩在瞪着她，这一切都错了。她抓住柜台，觉得她可能要晕倒。他的眼睛变软，只是一点。"求求你，"他低声说。"走吧。"

**关键词**：feet won't move / refuse to carry her away / those three words / I remember you / Three hundred years / no one has said those words / grab him by the sleeve / pull him forward / know why, how / what is so special / boy in a bookstore / grips the counter / feels like she might faint / eyes soften / Just go

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"瘫痪"。"feet won't move" —— 脚不会动 = 她无法离开。"refuse to carry her away from those three words" —— 拒绝带她离开那三个字 = 她想留在那里。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"refuse to carry her away" = 她想留在"我记得你"的时刻。

She slumps onto the lip at the top of the stairs, puts her head in her hands, feels like she might cry, or laugh, but instead, she stares back through the beveled glass insert of the shop door. She watches the boy every time he comes into the frame. She cannot tear her eyes away. I remember you. I remember you. I remember you. I remember you. I remember you. I remember you. I remember you. 她尝试。她不能。Addie 走到开着的门口，从商店到街道的四步，然后她体内的什么东西放弃了。她瘫倒在楼梯顶部的边缘，双手抱头，觉得她可能会哭，或笑，但相反，她透过商店门的斜玻璃窗凝视回来。她每次看他进入画面。她无法移开目光。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得你。我记得——

**关键词**：She tries / She can't / as far as the open door / four short steps / something in her gives / slumps / lip at the top of the stairs / puts her head in her hands / feels like she might cry, or laugh / stares back / beveled glass insert / watches the boy / comes into the frame / cannot tear her eyes away / I remember you (repeated)

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"重复"。"I remember you" 重复 15 次 = 三百年的等待。"cannot tear her eyes away" —— 无法移开目光 = 她被他吸引。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"I remember you"重复 = 她在消化三百年后第一次被记住。

> **原句 8:** "What are you doing?" She blinks, and sees him standing in the open doorway, arms crossed. The sun has shifted lower in the sky, the light going thin. "Waiting for you," she says, cringing as soon as she says it. "I wanted to apologize," she continues. "For the whole book thing." "It's fine," he says curtly. "No, it's not," she says, rising to her feet. "Let me buy you a coffee." "You don't have to do that." "I insist. As an apology." "I'm working." "Please." And it must be something in the way she says it, the sheer mix of hope and need, the obvious fact it means more than a book, more than a sorry, that makes the boy look her in the eyes, makes her realize that he hadn't really, not until now. There's something strange, searching in his gaze, but whatever he sees when he looks at her, it changes his mind. "One coffee," he says. "And you're still banned from the shop." Addie feels the air rush back into her lungs. "Deal."

"你在做什么？"她眨眼，看到他站在开着的门口，双臂交叉。太阳已经移到天空较低处，光变薄。"在等你，"她说，一说出就畏缩。"我想道歉，"她继续。"为整本书的事。""没关系，"他简短地说。"不，不是，"她站起来。"让我请你喝咖啡。""你不必那样做。""我坚持。作为道歉。""我在工作。""求求你。"一定是她说的方式中的某种东西，希望和需求的纯粹混合，明显的事实它意味着比一本书更多，比一个道歉更多，让男孩看着她的眼睛，让她意识到他真的没有，直到现在。他的目光中有某种奇怪的、搜索的东西，但无论他看她时看到什么，它都改变了他的想法。"一杯咖啡，"他说。"你仍然被禁止进入商店。"Addie 感到空气涌回她的肺里。"成交。"

**关键词**：What are you doing / Waiting for you / cringing / apologize / It's fine / rising to her feet / buy you a coffee / You don't have to / I insist / I'm working / Please / sheer mix of hope and need / means more than a book / more than a sorry / look her in the eyes / strange, searching in his gaze / changes his mind / One coffee / still banned from the shop / air rush back into her lungs / Deal

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"请求"。"Waiting for you" —— 在等你 = 她等他。"sheer mix of hope and need" —— 希望和需求的混合 = 她的真诚。"One coffee" —— 一杯咖啡 = 他的同意。"Deal" —— 成交 = 她的胜利。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"Deal" = 她赢得了与 Henry 的连接。

## 一句话总结

Addie 回到书店试图换书，但 Henry 记得她——"I remember you"——三百年后第一次有人记得她。她的世界被三个字颠覆，她无法离开，反复默念着这三个字。
