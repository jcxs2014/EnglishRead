# ch99. The Dance and The Revelation

## 本章导航

- **句话概括**：2014 年纽约，Addie 在 Central Park 醒来，Luc 出现——"和我跳舞"——他揭示："我把 Henry 放在你路上"——"我不爱你"——她回到 Henry，发现过了一周。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 —— 从"恐惧"到"跳舞"到"揭示"到"拒绝"到"回归"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Revelation（揭示）—— Luc 揭示 Henry 是他安排的 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"恐惧"到"觉醒"—— 她说"我不爱你" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **叙事手法**："dance" = 跳舞 = 他们的关系 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Addie is surrounded by trees. The mossy scent of summer in the woods. Fear winds through her, the sudden, horrible certainty that Luc has broken both rules instead of one, that he has dragged her through the dark, stolen her away from New York, abandoned her somewhere far, far from home. But then her eyes adjust, and she turns, and sees the skyline rising above the trees, and realizes she must be in Central Park. Relief sweeps through her. And then Luc's voice drifts through the dark. "Adeline, Adeline…" he says, and she cannot tell what is an echo, and what is simply him, unbound by flesh and bone and mortal shapes. "You promised," she calls. "Did I?" Luc steps out of the dark, the way he did that night, drawing together from smoke and shadow. A storm, bottled into skin. Am I the devil or the darkness? he asked her once. Am I a monster or a god? He is no longer dressed in the sleek black suit, but as he was when she first summoned him, a stranger in trousers, a pale tunic open at his throat, his black hair curling against his temples. The dream conjured so many years ago. But one thing has changed. There is no triumph in his eyes. The color has gone out of them, so pale they're almost gray. And though she's never seen the shade before, she guesses it is sadness.

Addie 被树木环绕。树林中夏天的苔藓气味。恐惧在她身上蜿蜒，突然的、可怕的确定性，Luc 打破了两条规则而不是一条，他把她拖过黑暗，从纽约偷走她，把她遗弃在离家很远很远的地方。但然后她的眼睛适应了，她转身，看到天际线在树木上升起，意识到她一定在 Central Park。宽慰涌过她。然后 Luc 的声音在黑暗中飘来。"Adeline，Adeline……"他说，她无法分辨什么是回声，什么只是他，不受血肉和骨骼和凡人形状的束缚。"你承诺了，"她喊。"我有吗？"Luc 从黑暗中走出来，就像他那个夜晚所做的那样，从烟雾和阴影中聚集。一个风暴，装在皮肤里。我曾经问过她，我是魔鬼还是黑暗？我是怪物还是神？他不再穿着光滑的黑色西装，而是像她第一次召唤他时那样，一个穿裤子的陌生人，一件苍白的束腰外衣在喉咙处敞开，他的黑卷发贴着他的太阳穴。多年前召唤的梦。但有一件事改变了。他的眼睛里没有胜利。颜色已经离开它们，如此苍白几乎是灰色。虽然她从未见过这种色调，她猜测那是悲伤。

**关键词**：trees / fear / Central Park / sadness

**为什么这样写**："sadness" = 悲伤 = 他的眼睛 = 他的爱 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。"Central Park" = 中央公园 = 她没有离开 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"宽慰" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 2:** "I will give you what you want," he says. "If you will do one thing." "What?" she asks. Luc holds out his hand. "Dance with me," he says. There is longing in his voice, and loss, and she thinks, perhaps, it is the end, of this, of them. A game finally played out. A war with no winners. And so she agrees to dance. There is no music, but it does not matter. When she takes his hand, she hears the melody, soft and soothing in her head. Not a song, exactly, but the sound of the woods in summer, the steady hush of the wind through the fields. And as he pulls her close, she hears a violin, low and mournful, along the Seine. His hand slides through hers, and there is the steady murmur of the seaside. The symphony soaring through Munich. Addie leans her head against his shoulder, and hears the rain falling in Villon, the brass band ringing in an L.A. lounge, and the ripple of a saxophone through the open windows on Bourbon. The dancing stops. The music fades. A tear slides down her cheek. "All you had to do was set me free."

"我会给你你想要的，"他说。"如果你做一件事。""什么？"她问。Luc 伸出手。"和我跳舞，"他说。他的声音里有渴望，和失去，她想，也许，这是结束，这个，他们。一场终于打完的游戏。一场没有赢家的战争。所以她同意跳舞。没有音乐，但没关系。当她握住他的手时，她听到旋律，轻柔而舒缓在她脑海里。不完全是歌，而是夏天树林的声音，风吹过田野的平稳的沙沙声。当他把她拉近时，她听到一把小提琴，低沉而悲伤，沿着塞纳河。他的手滑过她的手，有海边平稳的低语。交响乐在慕尼黑高涨。Addie 靠在他的肩上，听到雨落在维隆，铜管乐队在洛杉矶酒吧响起，萨克斯风在波旁街的开放窗户中涟漪。跳舞停止。音乐消退。一滴眼泪滑下她的脸颊。"你所要做的就是让我自由。"

> **原句 3:** "I lied." The words, a knife. "You loved me," he says. "And I loved you." "And yet," she says, "you didn't come to find me until I'd found someone else." She turns back toward him, expecting to see those eyes yellowing with envy. But instead, they have gone a weedy, arrogant green, mirrored by the expression on his face, the faint lift of a single brow, the corner of his mouth. "Oh, Adeline," he says. "You think you found each other?" The words are a missed step. A sudden drop. "Do you truly think that I would let that happen?" The ground tilts beneath her feet. "That for all the deals I do, such a thing would ever pass beneath my notice?" Addie squeezes her eyes shut, and she is lying beside Henry, their fingers laced together in the grass. She is looking up at the night sky. She is laughing at the idea that Luc finally made a mistake. "You must have thought yourselves so clever," he is saying now. "Star-crossed lovers, brought together by chance. What are the odds that you would meet, that you would both be bound to me, both have sold your souls for something only the other could provide? When the truth is so much easier than that—I put Henry in your path. I gave him to you, wrapped and ribboned like a gift."

"我撒谎了。"这些话，一把刀。"你爱我，"他说。"我也爱你。""然而，"她说，"你没有来找我直到我找到别人。"她转向他，期待看到那些眼睛因嫉妒而变黄。但相反，它们变成了一种杂草般的、傲慢的绿色的，反映在他脸上的表情，单个眉毛的微微抬起，嘴角。"哦，Adeline，"他说。"你以为你们找到了彼此？"这些话是一个错步。突然下跌。"你真的以为我会让那种事情发生？"地面在她脚下倾斜。"对于我做的一切交易，这样的事情会溜过我的注意？"Addie 紧闭双眼，她躺在 Henry 旁边，他们的手指在草丛中交缠。她看着夜空。她在笑 Luc 终于犯了个错误。"你们一定以为自己很聪明，"他现在说。"命运多舛的恋人，由机会带到一起。你们相遇的赔率是什么，你们都束缚于我，都卖掉了灵魂只为只有对方能提供的东西？当真相比那简单得多——我把 Henry 放在你的路上。我把他对你，像礼物一样包装和系缎带。"

**关键词**：lied / clever / star-crossed / odds / path / gift

**为什么这样写**："clever" = 聪明 = 你们一定以为自己很聪明 = 他的揭示 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。"gift" = 礼物 = 像礼物一样包装 = 他的安排 = 她的选择 = 她的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"你把 Henry 放在我的路上" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 4:** "Because it's what you wanted. You were so set upon your need for love, you could not see beyond it. I gave you this, I gave you him, so you could see that love was not worth the space you held for it. The space you kept from me." "But it was worth it. It is." He reaches out to brush her cheek. "It won't be, when he's gone." Addie pulls away. From his words, his touch. "This is cruel, Luc. Even for you." "No," he snarls. "Cruelty would be ten years instead of one. Cruelty would be to let you have a lifetime with him, and have to suffer more for losing." "I would choose it anyway!" She shakes her head. "You never intended to let him live, did you?" Luc inclines his head. "A deal is a deal, Adeline. And deals are binding." "That you would do all this to torment me—" "No," he snaps. "I did it to show you. To make you understand. You put them on such a pedestal, but humans are brief and pale and so is their love. It is shallow, it does not last. You long for human love, but you are not human, Adeline. You haven't been for centuries. You have no place with them. You belong with me."

"因为这是你想要的。你如此执着于对爱的需要，你看不到它之外。我给你这个，我给你他，这样你就可以看到爱不值得你为它保留的空间。你从我这里保留的空间。""但它值得。它是。"他伸手刷她的脸颊。"当他不在时，它不会值得。"Addie 拉开。从他话语，他触摸。"这很残酷，Luc。即使对你。"不，"他咆哮。"残酷会是十年而不是一年。残酷会是让你和他过一辈子，不得不为失去而受更多苦。""我还是会选择它！"她摇头。"你从未打算让他活着，是吗？"Luc 倾斜他的头。"交易就是交易，Adeline。交易是有约束力的。""你会做这一切来折磨我——""不，"他厉声说。"我这样做是为了给你看。让你明白。你把它们放在这样的基座上，但人类是短暂和苍白，他们的爱也是。它是浅的，它不持久。你渴望人类的爱，但你不是人类，Adeline。你已经几个世纪没有和它们在一起的地方了。你属于我。"

**关键词**：love / space / cruel / pedestal / belong

**为什么这样写**："pedestal" = 基座 = 你把它们放在这样的基座上 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。"belong" = 属于 = 你属于我 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

> **原句 5:** "Want?" he sneers. "Want is for children. If this were want, I would be rid of you by now. I would have forgotten you centuries ago," he says, a bitter loathing in his voice. "This is need. And need is painful but patient. Do you hear me, Adeline? I need you. As you need me. I love you, as you love me." She hears the pain in his voice. Perhaps that is why she wants to hurt him worse. He taught her well, to find the weakness in the armor. "But that's the thing, Luc," she says, "I don't love you at all." The words are soft, steady, and yet they rumble through the dark. The trees rustle, and the shadows thicken, and Luc's eyes burn a shade she's never seen before. A venomous color. And for the first time in centuries, she is afraid. "Does he mean so much to you?" he asks, voice flat and hard as river stones. "Then go. Spend time with your human love. Bury him, and mourn him, and plant a tree over his grave." His edges begin to blur into the dark. "I will still be here," he says. "And so will you."

"想要？"他冷笑。"想要是给孩子的。如果这是想要，我现在就会摆脱你。我会在几个世纪前忘记你，"他说，声音中带着苦涩的厌恶。"这是需要。需要是痛苦但耐心的。你听到了吗，Adeline？我需要你。就像你需要我。我爱你，就像你爱我。"她听到他声音中的痛苦。也许这就是为什么她想更严重地伤害他。他教得很好，找到盔甲中的弱点。"但这就是问题，Luc，"她说，"我根本不爱你。"这些话柔和、稳定，然而它们在黑暗中隆隆作响。树木沙沙作响，阴影变厚，Luc 的眼睛燃烧着一种她从未见过的色调。一种有毒的颜色。几个世纪以来第一次，她害怕了。"他对你意味着这么多？"他问，声音扁平而坚硬如河石。"然后走。花时间和你的人类爱人在一起。埋葬他，哀悼他，在他的坟墓上种一棵树。"他的边缘开始模糊成黑暗。"我会依然在这里，"他说。"你也会。"

**关键词**：need / pain / love / afraid / grave

**为什么这样写**："need" = 需要 = 我需要你 = 他的爱 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。"afraid" = 害怕 = 她害怕 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"我根本不爱你" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 6:** Addie sinks to her knees in the grass. She stays there until the first threads of light seep into the sky, and then, at last, she forces herself up again, walks to the subway in a fog, Luc's words looping through her head. You are not human, Adeline. You thought you found each other? You must have thought yourselves so clever. Spend time with your love. I will still be here. And so will you. The sun is rising by the time she gets to Brooklyn. She stops to pick up breakfast, a concession, an apology, for staying away all night. And that is when she sees the paper stacked against the newsstand. That is when she sees the date stamped in the upper corner. August 6, 2014. She left the apartment on the 30th of July. Spend time with your love, he said. But Luc has taken it. He didn't just steal a night. He took an entire week. Seven precious days, erased from her life … and Henry's. Addie runs. She stumbles through the door, and up the stairs, turns out her purse, but the key is gone, and she pounds on the door, terror surging through her that the world has changed, that Luc has somehow rewritten more than time, somehow taken more, taken everything. But then the lock slides, and the door falls open, and there is Henry, exhausted, disheveled, and she knows, by the look in his eyes, that he did not expect her to come back. That at some point, between the first morning and the next, and the next, and the next, he thought she was gone. Addie throws her arms around him now. "I'm so sorry," she says, and it is not just for the stolen week. It is for the deal, the curse, the fact it is her fault. "I'm sorry," she says, over and over, and Henry doesn't shout, doesn't rage, doesn't even say I told you so. He simply holds her tight, and says, "Enough," says, "Promise me," says, "Stay." And none of them are questions, but she knows he is asking, pleading with her to let it go, to stop fighting, stop trying to change their fates, and just be with him until the end. And Addie cannot bear the thought of giving up, of giving in, of going down without a fight. But Henry is breaking, and it is her fault, and so, in the end, she agrees.

Addie 跪在草地上。她在那里停留直到第一缕光渗入天空，然后，最后，她再次强迫自己站起来，走到地铁在雾中，Luc 的话在她脑海中循环。你不是人类，Adeline。你以为你们找到了彼此？你们一定以为自己很聪明。花时间和你爱的人在一起。我会依然在这里。你也会。当她到达布鲁克林时太阳正在升起。她停下来买早餐，一个让步，一个道歉，因为整晚都在外面。那就是她看到报纸堆在报摊上的时候。那就是她看到日期印在右上角的时候。2014 年 8 月 6 日。她7月30日离开公寓。花时间和你爱的人在一起，他说。但 Luc 拿走了它。他不仅偷走了一个晚上。他拿走了整整一周。七天珍贵的日子，从她的生活中抹去……和 Henry 的。Addie 跑。她踉跄穿过门，上楼，翻出钱包，但钥匙不见了，她敲门，恐惧涌过她世界已经改变，Luc 不知何故改写了比时间更多的东西，不知何故拿走了更多，拿走了一切。但然后锁滑动，门打开，有 Henry，疲惫，凌乱，她知道，从他眼中的神色，他没有期待她回来。在某个时刻，在第一个早晨和下一个之间，和下一个，和下一个，她以为她走了。Addie 现在用双臂抱住他。"我很抱歉，"她说，这不仅仅是被偷走的一周。这是交易，诅咒，事实是她的错。"我很抱歉，"她说，一遍又一遍，Henry 没有喊，没有怒，甚至没有告诉你早就告诉我。他只是紧紧抱住她，说，"够了，"说，"答应我，"说，"留下。"它们都不是问题，但她知道他在问，恳求她放手，停止战斗，停止试图改变他们的命运，和他在一起直到最后。Addie 无法忍受放弃的想法，屈服的想法，不战斗就倒下。但 Henry 正在破碎，这是她的错，所以，最后，她同意。
