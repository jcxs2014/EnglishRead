# 48. Then（Bregan视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：改革派的监狱突袭失败，Bakuit被揭露为叛徒（他的孙女被Stav威胁）。Addym射杀了Bakuit，Bregan意识到改革派已经变成了他们曾经反对的东西。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷——Bregan的理想主义彻底崩溃，他意识到革命已经失败。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：背叛——Bakuit的背叛是因为Stav威胁他的孙女；Addym的"正义处决"是暴民正义。
- **人物弧线**：Bregan从"理想主义改革派"转向"冷酷的幸存者"——他意识到革命不是请客吃饭。
- **叙事手法**：闪回场景——通过Bregan的内心独白和感官细节推进情节。

## 精读

> Bregan slapped a bloody handprint on the back of the safe house door. He knocked three times in quick succession, twice. The door flew open. Jaari peered out. "What happened?" the Players musician gasped.

**中文理解**：Bregan在安全屋的门上拍了一个血手印。他连续敲了三下，又敲了两下。门猛地打开了。Jaari向外窥视。"怎么了？"Players音乐家喘息着问。

**关键词**：bloody handprint / safe house door / knocked three times / quick succession / Jaari peered out

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"bloody handprint"是监狱突袭失败的证据。"knocked three times, quick succession, twice"是改革派的秘密暗号。

---

> We were betrayed, he thought, unable to speak the words. The small safe house, in the lower levels of a Spires apartment building, overflowed with people—mostly members of the Players. Tez rocked a crying Phinnin. Jaari grabbed a basket of medical supplies.

**中文理解**：我们被出卖了，他想，无法说出这些话。小小的安全屋位于Spires公寓楼的底层，挤满了人——大多是Players的成员。Tez抱着哭泣的Phinnin摇晃。Jaari抓起一篮医疗用品。

**关键词**：betrayed / safe house / overflowed with people / Players / crying / medical supplies

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"betrayed"是内心独白——他知道有人出卖了他们。"overflowed with people"是安全屋的拥挤——改革派的残余。

---

> The bullet had nearly taken his life. Several other Reformists hadn't been so lucky. They hadn't even breached the outermost wall of the prison. Even in the dark, even in the fog, the Stav knew precisely where they would approach from. They'd pounced at the exact right moment, and the Reformists on Bregan's team fell like stones. He had barely made it out.

**中文理解**：子弹差点要了他的命。其他几个改革派就没有这么幸运了。他们甚至没有突破监狱的最外层墙壁。即使在黑暗中，即使在雾中，Stav也精确地知道他们会从哪里接近。他们在正确的时刻猛扑过来，Bregan团队中的改革派像石头一样倒下。他勉强逃了出来。

**关键词**：bullet / nearly taken his life / breached the outermost wall / precisely / pounced / fell like stones / barely made it out

**为什么这样写**："fell like stones"是视觉隐喻——改革派像石头一样倒下。"barely made it out"是Bregan的幸存——他是少数逃出来的人之一。

---

> "How?" a new voice demanded. Addym wedged into the room with Thom Draifey. Addym's forearm was hastily bandaged and soaked with red, but he raised his gun anyway, pivoting from face to face. "Who snitched?"

**中文理解**："怎么做到的？"一个新的声音要求道。Addym和Thom Draifey挤进房间。Addym的前臂草草包扎，被鲜血浸透，但他仍然举起枪，从一张脸转向另一张脸。"谁告密了？"

**关键词**：wedged into the room / hastily bandaged / soaked with red / pivoting from face to face / Who snitched

**为什么这样写**：Addym的"Who snitched?"是愤怒——他想知道谁是叛徒。"pivoting from face to face"是Addym的怀疑——他看每个人都是叛徒。

---

> "It was you, wasn't it?" Addym asked, aiming at Draifey. The other man glared down his nose. "That's how it's gonna be?" Addym pressed the pistol into Draifey's chest. "You're the new guy. You show up from the factories with no story, no references. You expect us to—"

**中文理解**："是你，对吧？"Addym问，瞄准Draifey。另一个人瞪着他鼻子下方。"就是这样吗？"Addym把枪顶在Draifey的胸口。"你是新人。你从工厂冒出来，没有故事，没有推荐。你期望我们——"

**关键词**：aiming at / glared down his nose / pressed the pistol / new guy / no story, no references

**为什么这样写**：Addym的"new guy"是排外——他不信任新人。"pressed the pistol"是Addym的暴力——他准备用暴力解决问题。

---

> "Now hold on, son," Draifey said. "I'm not your fucking son," Addym shouted.

**中文理解**："等等，孩子，"Draifey说。"我不是你他妈的孙子，"Addym喊道。

**关键词**：hold on, son / not your fucking son

**为什么这样写**：Draifey的"son"是居高临下——他把Addym当孩子。Addym的"not your fucking son"是愤怒——他拒绝被轻视。

> "Did you do it?" Thom Draifey grunted, staring down Bakuit like a judge. The old man withered. "My granddaughter," he said. "She is pregnant. The Stav threatened . . . they threatened to kill her. Please, you have to know I didn't want this."

**中文理解**："是你干的吗？"Thom Draifey咕哝着，像法官一样盯着Bakuit。老人萎缩了。"我的孙女，"她说。"她怀孕了。Stav威胁……他们威胁要杀了她。请，你必须知道我不想这样。"

**关键词**：staring down / like a judge / withered / granddaughter / pregnant / threatened to kill her / didn't want this

**为什么这样写**：Bakuit的"granddaughter"是软肋——Stav威胁要杀他的孙女。"didn't want this"是Bakuit的无奈——他是被迫的。

---

> "What did you tell them?" Addym asked. "Just . . . just the dates and times I heard, something about attacking in the thick of the mist." "Did you give them names?" Draifey pressed. Bakuit fell silent. A beat passed, then another. Bregan spun and pinned the old man with a glare. "Whose names did you give?" Without hesitation, Bakuit listed almost every single Reformist. The room erupted in opinions.

**中文理解**："你告诉他们什么了？"Addym问。"只是……只是我听到的日期和时间，关于在雾中攻击的事情。""你给他们名字了吗？"Draifey追问。Bakuit沉默了。过了一拍，又一拍。Bregan转身用怒视钉住老人。"你给了谁的名字？"毫不犹豫，Bakuit列出了几乎每一个改革派。房间里爆发出一片意见。

**关键词**：dates and times / thick of the mist / names / fell silent / pinned the old man / without hesitation / listed almost every single Reformist / erupted in opinions

**为什么这样写**：Bakuit的"without hesitation"是绝望——他已经没有希望了。"listed almost every single Reformist"是Bakuit的背叛——他出卖了所有人。

---

> "Maybe we can use him," he said. Bakuit was now a direct line to the Stav. Everyone was arguing too loud to hear him. He turned around to repeat himself. "Maybe we could—" Bang. The shot rattled the apartment. Bregan nearly fell over as Addym lowered his shaking pistol. There was a hole in Bakuit's hand-embroidered tunic. Blood seeped through the beer-stained fabric, which one of his daughters must have stitched.

**中文理解**："也许我们可以利用他，"他说。Bakuit现在是通往Stav的直接线路。每个人都在大声争吵，听不见他。他转过身重复自己。"也许我们可以——"砰。枪声震动了公寓。Bregan几乎摔倒，Addym放下了他颤抖的枪。Bakuit手工刺绣的衬衫上有一个洞。血渗过了啤酒染过的布料，这一定是他女儿缝的。

**关键词**：use him / direct line / arguing too loud / Bang / rattled the apartment / shaking pistol / hand-embroidered tunic / blood seeped / beer-stained fabric / stitched

**为什么这样写**："Bang"是枪声——Addym杀了Bakuit。"hand-embroidered tunic"是细节——Bakuit的女儿缝的衣服。"blood seeped"是死亡——Bakuit死了。

> Bregan's rage exploded. "Addym," he roared. He drove his fist into the other man's jaw. Addym didn't hit back. He didn't resist as Bregan shoved him into the wall. He only raised his blood-splattered chin and gave him a cold, defiant smile. "I only did what you couldn't."

**中文理解**：Bregan的愤怒爆发了。"Addym，"他吼道。他一拳打在下巴上。Addym没有还手。当Bregan把他推到墙上时，他没有反抗。他只是抬起沾满血的下巴，给了他一个冷酷、挑衅的微笑。"我只是做了你做不到的事。"

**关键词**：rage exploded / roared / drove his fist / jaw / didn't hit back / shoved him into the wall / blood-splattered chin / cold, defiant smile / did what you couldn't

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"rage exploded"是愤怒——Addym杀了Bakuit。"did what you couldn't"是Addym的辩护——他做了Bregan不敢做的事。

---

> "He was a liability and you know it," Addym said. "If your pa was here, he'd have done the same." "Don't you fucking tell me what my pa would have done." Thom Draifey caught Bregan's arm. "Bregan, he's not wrong." "He's not the one calling the shots," Bregan snarled. "And who is?" Addym's chest heaved as he glared at the rest of the room. "I just did what none of you had the stomach to do. You're welcome." When he stepped around Bregan, no one stopped him. Every green, horrified face turned away from the body. But as Addym vanished into the night, the room almost seemed to exhale in relief. He had made their choice for them, and now they didn't have to carry the burden of it.

**中文理解**："他是个负担，你知道的，"Addym说。"如果你爸在这里，他也会这么做的。""不要他妈的告诉我我爸会怎么做。"Thom Draifey抓住Bregan的手臂。"Bregan，他没有错。""他不是发号施令的人，"Bregan咆哮道。"那是谁？"Addym的胸膛起伏，瞪着房间的其他人。"我只是做了你们都没有胆量做的事。不客气。"当他绕过Bregan时，没有人阻止他。每一张苍白的、惊恐的脸都转离了尸体。但当Addym消失在夜色中，房间几乎似乎松了一口气。他替他们做了选择，现在他们不必承担这个负担。

**关键词**：liability / calling the shots / had the stomach to do / You're welcome / green, horrified face / turned away / exhaled in relief / made their choice / carry the burden

**为什么这样写**：Addym的"had the stomach to do"是冷酷——他有胆量杀人。"exhaled in relief"是房间的集体反应——他们松了一口气，因为有人替他们做了选择。

---

> Bregan staggered outside. This wasn't a revolution. This was a group of angry, vengeful egos. They had no leadership, no path. They needed Pa. They needed the Five. But after tonight, that didn't seem possible. If the Reformists were going to survive, they needed to get their shit together. They needed leaders, a vision, a plan.

**中文理解**：Bregan踉跄着走到外面。这不是革命。这是一群愤怒、复仇的自我。他们没有领导，没有道路。他们需要Pa。他们需要五个人。但在今晚之后，这似乎不可能。如果改革派要生存，他们需要振作起来。他们需要领导、愿景、计划。

**关键词**：staggered outside / revolution / angry, vengeful egos / no leadership / no path / needed Pa / needed the Five / get their shit together / leaders, a vision, a plan

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"get their shit together"是粗口——改革派需要团结。"leaders, a vision, a plan"是Bregan的思考——革命需要组织。

---

> Looking up at the mist-shrouded moon, Bregan dug out the note he'd stashed there the day before and ran his thumb over the signature. Baron Ihan Hulei. If Pa were here, Bregan knew what he would do.

**中文理解**：Bregan抬头看着薄雾笼罩的月亮，掏出前一天藏在那里的纸条，用拇指摩挲着签名。Baron Ihan Hulei。如果Pa在这里，Bregan知道他会怎么做。

**关键词**：mist-shrouded moon / dug out the note / stashed / ran his thumb over the signature / Baron Ihan Hulei / If Pa were here

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"Baron Ihan Hulei"是选择——他决定接受Baron Hulei的帮助。"If Pa were here"是Bregan的遗憾——Pa不在他身边。
