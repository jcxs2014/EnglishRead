# 44. Now（Firin视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Firin回到Grande，与Suri讨论政府的审查令。Nodtacht男孩（Bekat）从藏身处出来，告诉Firin关于其他儿童的下落——他们被关在医院的秘密房间。Firin承诺救出他们。
- **情感弧线位置**：伏笔铺设——Firin终于找到了其他儿童的下落，但她意识到Hulei可能已经对他们进行了实验。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：审查令——Hulei要求Grande表演《From Fifthday's Ashes》来美化自己；Firin的妥协是为了保护她的计划。
- **人物弧线**：Firin从"利用Bekat"转向"真心想救他"——她看到了Bekat眼中的恐惧，她无法再忽视这些孩子。
- **叙事手法**：密闭空间对话——Firin与Suri的对话揭示政治背景，Firin与Bekat的对话揭示Nodtacht的真相。

## 精读

> "It is outrageous," Suri hissed, following me down the Grande's marble hallway. Haunting music swelled behind us as Luka led the orchestra into the final act of The Light Between Us, accompanied by the tapping rhythm of dozens of dancers' shoes.

**中文理解**："这太离谱了，"Suri在我后面低声说，跟着我走在大理石走廊里。后面，Luka带领管弦乐队演奏《我们之间的光》的最后一幕，伴随着几十个舞者鞋子的敲击节奏。

**关键词**：outrageous / marble hallway / Haunting music swelled / tapping rhythm

**为什么为什么这样写**："outrageous"是Suri对政府审查令的反应。"Haunting music"是背景音乐——《我们之间的光》是Firin和Bregan曾经一起创作的剧本。

---

> "We're free to perform but only what the government tells us to?" Suri scoffed. I climbed the back steps. "They're requesting." "Are they?" Her disgust reverberated up the stairwell. Of course they weren't. Hulei was censoring us.

**中文理解**："我们可以自由表演，但只能演政府告诉我们的？" Surl嘲笑道。我爬上后面的台阶。"他们是请求。""是吗？"她的厌恶在楼梯间回荡。当然不是。Hulei在审查我们。

**关键词**：free to perform / only what the government tells us / requesting / disgust reverberated

**为什么这样写**：Firin的讽刺——她告诉Suri政府在"请求"，但她知道这是命令。"disgust reverberated"是Suri的反应——她知道这是审查。

---

> "It's too soon to glorify Bloody Fifthday," Suri said, trailing me to Veska's office. "People are still reeling from the insurrection. We don't need to be reminded of how many of us died to get here."

**中文理解**："现在美化血腥第五天太早了，"Suri说，跟着我走到Veska的办公室。"人们还在从起义中恢复。我们不需要被提醒我们有多少人才能走到这里。"

**关键词**：glorify Bloody Fifthday / reeling from the insurrection / reminded / how many of us died

**为什么这样写**：Suri的"reeling from the insurrection"是现实——人们还没有从起义中恢复。"glorify Bloody Fifthday"是Hulei的目的——他想美化自己的英雄形象。

---

> "They're requesting." ... "Are they?" ... Of course they weren't. Hulei was censoring us. The politely worded decree was clear: We could perform what he wanted or not perform at all.

**中文理解**："他们是请求。"……"是吗？"……当然不是。Hulei在审查我们。措辞礼貌的法令很清楚：我们可以表演他想要的，或者根本不表演。

**关键词**：politely worded decree / perform what he wanted / not perform at all

**为什么这样写**：Firin的"politely worded decree"是反讽——Hulei的命令被包装成"请求"。"not perform at all"是威胁——不服从就会被关闭。

---

> "This is about more than The Light Between Us," Suri said. "It's about principle. If the government requests one play, what's to stop them from dictating our entire season?"

**中文理解**："这不仅仅是关于《我们之间的光》，"Suri说。"这是关于原则。如果政府要求一个剧本，什么能阻止他们支配我们的整个演出季？"

**关键词**：more than / principle / dictating our entire season

**为什么这样写**：Suri的担忧——审查的滑坡效应。"dictating our entire season"是Hulei的目标——控制所有艺术。

---

> When I entered Veska's office, the window was open. A ringing filled my ears. As Suri chattered behind me, I scanned the office. Nothing was out of place . . . Except the little Nodtacht boy's knife.

**中文理解**：当我进入Veska的办公室时，窗户开着。我的耳朵里充满了铃声。当Suri在我后面喋喋不休时，我扫视了一下办公室。一切都井然有序……除了那个Nodtacht男孩的刀。

**关键词**：window was open / ringing filled my ears / Nothing was out of place / Except the little Nodtacht boy's knife

**为什么这样写**：Firin的观察力——她注意到刀不在原来的位置。"Except"是转折——Bekat在办公室里。

---

> The closet door creaked. The Nodtacht boy peered out. He was wearing a Labor-issue uniform. Floppy dark hair framed a round brown face and familiar rich brown eyes. He looked about the same age Ihzi was when I first met her and she tried to feed me despite Skavka's wrath.

**中文理解**：衣柜门吱吱作响。那个Nodtacht男孩窥探出来。他穿着一套劳工制服。蓬乱的黑色头发围绕着一张圆润的棕色面孔和熟悉的深棕色眼睛。他看起来和我第一次见到Ihzi时她差不多年龄，尽管Skavka很愤怒，她还是试图给我吃东西。

**关键词**：closet door creaked / peered out / Labor-issue uniform / floppy dark hair / familiar rich brown eyes

**为什么这样写**：Bekat的"familiar rich brown eyes"是Firin的记忆——她想起了Ihzi。"Skavka's wrath"是回忆——Ihzi在监狱中对她的帮助。

---

> "You're Afyn?" he whispered. As I nodded, I pulled on my illusion cord, donning the face of a Merchant I'd once used for a set of Nodtacht missions: a face he might recognize. He bounced on his toes. "You didn't die like the rest of them?"

**中文理解**："你是Afyn？"他低声说。当我点头时，我拉下我的幻觉绳，戴上我曾经用于一系列Nodtacht任务的商人面孔：一张他可能会认出的脸。他踮起脚尖。"你没有像其他人一样死掉？"

**关键词**：Afyn / pulled on my illusion cord / donning the face / bounced on his toes / didn't die like the rest of them

**为什么这样写**：Bekat的"Afyn"是Firin在Nodtacht中的名字。"donning the face"是Firin展示她的能力。"bounced on his toes"是Bekat的兴奋——他没有想到Firin还活着。

---

> "What does he ask you to do?" I pressed. Spinning the handle of his knife, he said, "Missions, when we're not training the new children." New children. Hulei must have successfully made some new Nodtacht out of the orphans.

**中文理解**："他让你做什么？"我问。他旋转着刀柄说："任务，当我们不训练新孩子的时候。"新孩子。Hulei一定成功地从孤儿中制造了一些新的Nodtacht。

**关键词**：Missions / training the new children / New children / successfully made some new Nodtacht

**为什么这样写**：Bekat的"Missions"是Hulei对Nodtacht儿童的使用。"new children"是Hulei的实验——他已经成功制造了新的Nodtacht。

---

> "To the hospital." I startled. "He's got you all at the Enlightened Hospital?" Bekat shook his head. "There's another room they call the 'hospital.' We're not allowed to go in. They brought my friend there, and I tried to follow, but it was locked. I could hear him crying."

**中文理解**："去医院。"我吓了一跳。"他把你们都关在启蒙医院？" Bekat摇了摇头。"还有一个房间他们叫'医院'。我们不允许进去。他们把我的朋友带到那里，我试图跟着，但门锁着。我能听到他在哭。"

**关键词**：Enlightened Hospital / another room / locked / crying

**为什么这样写**：Bekat的"locked"和"crying"是恐怖——Hulei在医院里进行秘密实验。"another room"是Hulei的秘密实验室。

---

> I startled. "He's got you all at the Enlightened Hospital?" ... It sounded like Hulei was experimenting on the children, but maybe he hadn't figured out how to compel them yet.

**中文理解**：我吓了一跳。"他把你们都关在启蒙医院？"……听起来Hulei在对孩子们进行实验，但也许他还没有弄清楚如何强迫他们。

**关键词**：startled / experimenting on the children / compel them yet

**为什么这样写**：Firin的推断——Hulei在进行实验。"compel them yet"暗示Hulei还没有完全控制Nodtacht儿童。

---

> "I can rescue you. Then you won't ever have to spy for anyone but yourself again. Would you like that?" He nodded. "I just need you to tell me where they are," I said.

**中文理解**："我可以救你。然后你将永远不必再为任何人刺除了你自己。你喜欢吗？"他点了点头。"我只需要你告诉我他们在哪里，"我说。

**关键词**：rescue you / spy for anyone but yourself / tell me where they are

**为什么这样写**：Firin的承诺——她承诺救出Bekat。"tell me where they are"是她需要的信息。

---

> He swallowed. "What if they find out?" "They won't," I promised. "I won't fail you." Not this time.

**中文理解**：他吞了吞口水。"如果他们发现了呢？""他们不会，"我答应。"我不会让你失望的。"这次不会。

**关键词**：What if they find out / They won't / fail you / Not this time

**为什么这样写**：Firin的承诺——她不会让Bekat失望。"Not this time"是她的誓言——她不会再让Nodtacht儿童受到伤害。
