# 46. Then（Bregan视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：两年前，Bregan在Rambler's Tavern收到Baron Hulei的纸条，表示愿意支持改革派。Bregan怀疑这是陷阱，但Baron Hulei似乎真诚。
- **情感弧线位置**：回忆——Bregan在过去与现在的对比，展示他的成长。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：酒吧作为聚集地——Rambler's Tavern是Bregan和Firin的回忆之地，现在也是改革派的秘密会议地点。
- **人物弧线**：Bregan从"年轻改革派"转向"成熟间谍"——他学会了怀疑一切。
- **叙事手法**：回忆场景——通过过去与现在的对比，揭示Bregan的内心变化。

## 精读

> The Rambler's Tavern was full of ghosts. Bregan drained his mug of beer, and the familiar malt turned to ash on his tongue. The place hadn't changed in twenty years. A Gwyn string band played at the back. Fisherfolk of all ages drank, laughed, and danced in lines together.

**中文理解**：Rambler's Tavern充满了幽灵。Bregan喝干杯中的啤酒，熟悉的大麦芽在舌头上变成了灰烬。这个地方二十年没有变化。一个Gwyn弦乐队在后面表演。各个年龄段的渔民喝酒、大笑、排成队跳舞。

**关键词**：full of ghosts / familiar malt turned to ash / hadn't changed in twenty years

**为什么这样写**："full of ghosts"是Bregan的内心写照——他已故的家人（Firin、他的叔叔一家）。"familiar malt turned to ash"是味觉描写——Bregan的心情让一切都变得无味。

---

> If there is still an effort, the note said, I would like to lend support. The baron had signed it himself. Two years ago, on Bloody Fifthday, the Stav had shot the baron. Then he was exiled to Qunsii. No one had heard from him since.

**中文理解**："如果还有行动，"纸条上说，"我愿意提供支持。"男爵亲自签了名。两年前，在血腥第五天，Stav射杀了男爵。然后他被流放到Qunsii。从那以后没有人听到过他的消息。

关键词：still an effort / lend support / baron had signed it / exiled to Qunsii

**为什么这样写**：Baron Hulei的"lend support"是意外的——Bregan以为他已经死了。"exiled to Qunsii"是Baron Hulei的命运——他被迫离开Luisonn。

---

> Soon, he thought. Tomorrow they would break Pa and the other Reformist leaders out of the Stav's prison. Then the rebellion would be back in respectable, responsible hands.

**中文理解**：很快，他想。明天他们要把Pa和其他改革派领导人从Stav的监狱里救出来。然后起义就会回到可敬的、负责任的手中。

关键词：break Pa and the other Reformist leaders out / rebellion / respectable, responsible hands

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的乐观——他相信明天会成功。"respectable, responsible hands"是Bregan的信念——改革派是正义的。

---

> "Whatever you're up to, Breg . . . you don't need to go through with it." ... "I'm all right," Bregan lied, patting the man's veiny hand.

**中文理解**："无论你在做什么，Breg……你不需要继续下去。"……"我没事，"Bregan撒谎说，拍了拍老人青筋毕露的手。

关键词：Whatever you're up to / go through with it / lied / veiny hand

**为什么这样写**：Bakuit的警告——他知道Bregan在策划危险的事。Bregan的"lied"是他的保护——他不想让Bakuit担心。
