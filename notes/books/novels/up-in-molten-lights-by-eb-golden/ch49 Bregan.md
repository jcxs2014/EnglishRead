# 49. Now（Bregan视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Bregan与Asli在Cove区会合，发现Lockup。他们从悬崖上的洞穴进入，找到了通往Lockup的格栅窗，听到孩子们的声音。他们用蜡烛蜡标记退路，并确认Dr.Slunai在那里。他们需要炸药来炸开格栅。
- **情感弧线位置**：紧张——Bregan和Asli被迫合作，两人之间的敌意仍在，但他们有共同目标。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：Asli的解释——他帮助是因为"Thora"（Firin），但Bregan不相信他是英雄；洞穴探险是间谍/盗贼trope。
- **人物弧线**：Bregan从"独狼"转向"团队合作"——他意识到自己无法单独拯救孩子们。
- **叙事手法**——夜间观察场景——通过视觉细节（"dozen dimly lit windows"）和听觉（"children's voices"）推进情节。

## 精读

> The address in Thora's note led to the Cove, a small inlet in the Bilge that housed the city's most destitute citizens. Bregan seethed as he followed the shoreline, barely able to see his hand in front of his face. Makeshift, lean-to homes crept up on the beach, so close he had to walk in the tide to pass them. Somewhere among these slums, Hulei was torturing, manipulating, and murdering children where no one would think to look.

**中文理解**：Thora纸条上的地址通向Cove——Bilge区的一个小海湾，居住着城市最贫困的公民。Bregan沿着海岸线前行，怒火中烧，几乎看不见面前的手。临时搭建的棚屋在海滩上蔓延，如此密集以至于他不得不在潮水中走过它们。在这些贫民窟中的某个地方，Hulei正在折磨、操纵和谋杀孩子们，而没有人会想到要看这里。

**关键词**：Thora's note / the Cove / destitute citizens / makeshift, lean-to homes / walk in the tide / slums / torturing, manipulating, and murdering children

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"seethed"是愤怒——Hulei在贫民窟中隐藏的暴行。"makeshift, lean-to homes"是Cove区的贫困——最弱势的人住在最恶劣的环境中。"torturing, manipulating, and murdering children"是Hulei的罪行——三个动词的递进揭示了Hulei的残忍。

---

> The cliffs around the violent bay blocked out the moon. Brine, mold, and shit laced the night air, and Bregan tried not to think about what he was stepping in and over. People here couldn't even afford candles, so only a few flames danced in windows, isolated and weak.

**中文理解**：汹涌海湾周围的悬崖遮挡了月亮。盐、霉菌和粪便点缀着夜间的空气，Bregan尽量不去想他踩到和跨过的是什么。这里的人甚至买不起蜡烛，所以只有几扇窗户里舞动着火焰，孤立而微弱。

**关键词**：cliffs / violent bay / blocked out the moon / Brine, mold, and shit / laced the night air / couldn't afford candles / isolated and weak

**为什么这样写**："Brine, mold, and shit"是Cove区的感官描写——贫困的嗅觉。"couldn't afford candles"是细节——贫困到连光都买不起。"isolated and weak"是火焰的微弱——希望的微弱。

> Many of the dead he'd investigated over the last few months had been found here with X's carved into their arms. How many of them had died by Hulei's hand? The idea that the president was trying to control people was straight from a Kirru legend, so horrifying it didn't seem possible.

**中文理解**：他过去几个月调查的许多尸体都在这里被发现，手臂上刻有X。其中有多少是死于Hulei之手？总统试图控制人们的想法简直像是基尔鲁传说一样，可怕得似乎不可能。

**关键词**：dead / X's carved into their arms / died by Hulei's hand / control people / Kirru legend / horrifying

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的推理——X标记的尸体与Hulei有关。"Kirru legend"是Bregan的认知——Hulei的暴行超越了现实，像是神话中的怪物。

---

> "Were you followed?" A gas lamp flared. Bregan grabbed his gun as a man in a trench coat sauntered out from between row houses. The man's dark beard and weathered features weren't familiar, but his split lip caught the lamplight. It was Asli. Bregan didn't release his gun.

**中文理解**："你被跟踪了吗？"煤气灯亮起。Bregan抓起枪，一个穿着风衣的男人从排屋之间闲逛出来。男人的黑胡须和风化的面孔并不熟悉，但他的裂开的嘴唇捕捉到了灯光。是Asli。Bregan没有放下枪。

**关键词**：Were you followed / gas lamp flared / grabbed his gun / trench coat / sauntered / split lip / didn't release his gun

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的警惕——"didn't release his gun"。Asli的"split lip"是细节——Bregan上次打伤了他。"sauntered"是Asli的态度——他看起来很轻松，但Bregan不信任他。

---

> "No," he said. He'd easily lost his young constable tails tonight. "Where's Thora?" "Not coming," Asli said calmly. "Why?" This whole plan was Thora's idea. "She's making sure we're not followed," Asli said.

**中文理解**："没有，"他说。他今晚轻易地甩掉了年轻的警察尾巴。"Thora呢？""没来，"Asli平静地说。"为什么？"这个整个计划都是Thora的主意。"她在确保我们没被跟踪，"Asli说。

**关键词**：easily lost / young constable tails / Where's Thora / Not coming / making sure we're not followed

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的怀疑——他想知道Thora在哪里。"easily lost"是Bregan的技能——他擅长甩掉尾巴。

---

> Asli shrugged. "She cornered one of the Stav's old Nodtacht. It looks like my father kept the young ones alive. They work for him now. The boy told her where they were." Bregan frowned. In her cell after the gala, Veska'nora had claimed that the former commissioner was a Nodtacht turncoat.

**中文理解**：Asli耸了耸肩。"她堵住了一个Stav的老Nodtacht。看起来我父亲让孩子们活了下来。他们现在为他工作。男孩告诉了他们在哪里。"Bregan皱起眉头。在晚会后她的牢房里，Veska'nora声称前专员是Nodtacht叛徒。

**关键词**：cornered one of the Stav's old Nodtacht / kept the young ones alive / work for him now / told her where they were / cell / Nodtacht turncoat

**为什么这样写**：Asli的解释——Thora找到了Nodtacht男孩。"work for him now"是Hulei的暴行——孩子们被迫为凶手工作。

---

> "Let's climb up there," he said, pointing to a rock formation that jutted up above most of the Cove's shacks. "We can observe the Lockup's entrances. We need to figure out what room they're in; then we can plan from there."

**中文理解**："我们爬到那里去，"他说，指着一块凸起的岩石构造，它比Cove的大多数棚屋都高。"我们可以观察Lockup的入口。我们需要弄清楚他们在哪个房间；然后我们可以从那里制定计划。"

**关键词**：climb up / rock formation / jutted up / observe / Lockup's entrances / figure out / plan from there

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的策略——他们需要观察入口。"rock formation"是观察点——制高点。

---

> "Why are you doing this?" Asli was silent for a long moment, then sighed. "You think I'm a coward." "I never said that," Bregan countered. "Look, I get how it looks," Asli said carefully. "But all I've ever done is try to survive."

**中文理解**："你为什么要这样做？"Asli沉默了很长时间，然后叹了口气。"你认为我是个懦夫。""我从来没有说过，"Bregan反驳道。"好吧，我知道看起来怎样，"Asli小心地说。"但我所做的一切都是为了生存。"

**关键词**：Why are you doing this / coward / I never said that / try to survive

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的怀疑——他质疑Asli的动机。"try to survive"是Asli的辩护——他只是想活下去。

---

> "So, your selfishness is forgivable, then?" he asked. "I did try to play hero, you know," Asli said with a thread of irritation. Bregan faced him fully. "Is that right?" Only the whites of Asli's eyes shone in the dark. "I went to General Weras after I discovered my father returned to the Reform movement because I feared he was trying to get his hands on the Nodtacht's illusion stones," Asli said.

**中文理解**："那么，你的自私是可以原谅的吗？"他问。"我确实试过扮演英雄，你知道，"Asli说，带着一丝恼怒。Bregan完全面对他。"是吗？"只有Asli眼睛的白色在黑暗中闪烁。"我发现我父亲回到改革运动后，我去找了General Weras，因为我担心他试图得到Nodtacht的幻觉石，"Asli说。

**关键词**：selfishness / forgivable / play hero / thread of irritation / whites of Asli's eyes / discovered my father returned / feared / trying to get his hands on

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的讽刺——"selfishness is forgivable"。Asli的解释——他试图扮演英雄但失败了。"whites of Asli's eyes"是黑暗中的视觉细节。

---

> "Thora." "What do you mean?" "She inspires heroism," Asli said with a light thread of sarcasm. Bregan tensed. As a Reformist, he had seen people play hero for less than a lover's approval, but this didn't settle well with him.

**中文理解**："Thora。""你是什么意思？""她激发了英雄主义，"Asli说，带着一丝讽刺。Bregan紧张了。作为一个改革派，他见过人们为了不到爱人的认可而扮演英雄，但这让他感到不安。

**关键词**：Thora / inspires heroism / light thread of sarcasm / play hero / lover's approval / didn't settle well

**为什么这样写**：Asli的解释——Thora（Firin）激发了他的英雄主义。"inspires heroism"是Asli的感受——他想为她成为更好的人。

---

> "The caves." The cliffs between the Spires and the Cove were full of caverns. As children, Bregan and his cousin had enjoyed free rein of the docks of the Spires when their parents were working. His grandparents forbade them from exploring the caves in the eastern cliffs, but that only made them more seductive. Once they had found grated windows at the very back, and Bregan was willing to bet they led into the Lockup.

**中文理解**："洞穴。"Spires和Cove之间的悬崖上充满了洞穴。小时候，Bregan和他的表弟在父母工作时可以自由地在Spires码头玩耍。他的祖父母禁止他们探索东部悬崖的洞穴，但这只是让他们更具诱惑力。有一次他们在最后面找到了格栅窗，Bregan敢打赌它们通向Lockup。

**关键词**：caves / caverns / free rein / docks / grandparents forbade / exploring / eastern cliffs / seductive / grated windows / willing to bet

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的回忆——他和表弟在码头玩耍。"seductive"是禁忌的诱惑——被禁止的东西更具吸引力。"grated windows"是细节——他小时候见过。

---

> He looked at Asli's arms, exposed by his short-capped tunic, and he halted. "You don't have a scar," he said. Asli turned. Water lapped around his knees. "My stone's not in my arm, not anymore."

**中文理解**：他看着Asli的胳膊，被他短袖束腰外衣暴露出来，他停了下来。"你没有疤痕，"他说。Asli转过身。水拍打着他的膝盖。"我的石头不在我胳膊里了，不再在了。"

**关键词**：arms / exposed / short-capped tunic / halted / scar / stone's not in my arm / not anymore

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的观察——Asli没有疤痕。"stone's not in my arm, not anymore"是Asli的秘密——他的石头被移除了。

---

> Suddenly, a voice echoed, deeper in the cave. The Lockup. Bregan's boots splashed as he raced toward the sound, farther into the dark, until he found the source. On the other side of a grated window in the cave's wall, far above their heads, a couple fought like street cats. He could hear every word.

**中文理解**：突然，一个声音在洞穴深处回响。Lockup。Bregan的靴子溅起水花，他朝着声音跑去，深入黑暗，直到找到源头。在洞穴墙壁的格栅窗的另一侧，远高于他们的头顶，一对情侣像街猫一样打架。他能听到每一个字。

**关键词**：voice echoed / deeper in the cave / The Lockup / boots splashed / raced / grated window / couple fought / street cats

**为什么这样写**：Bregan听到声音——"couple fought"是意外发现。"grated window"是入口——他们找到了进入Lockup的方式。

---

> Dozens of little voices screeched and laughed. Asli hung back, dulling the candle's spreading light with his palm, but Bregan waded through to the wall. He placed his hands on the rough stone and looked up, desperately wishing he could look inside. The children sounded fine. Content. Healthy. Were they Hulei's or someone else's?

**中文理解**：几十个小声音尖叫着、笑着。Asli退缩了，用手掌遮挡蜡烛扩散的光线，但Bregan涉水走到墙边。他把双手放在粗糙的石头上，抬头看，拼命希望能看到里面。孩子们听起来很好。满足的。健康。他们是Hulei的还是别人的？

**关键词**：Dozens of little voices / screeched and laughed / hung back / dulling / spreading light / waded through / rough stone / desperately wishing / sounded fine / Content / Healthy

**为什么这样写**：Bregan听到孩子们的声音——"dozens of little voices"是希望。"desperately wishing he could look inside"是他的渴望——他想确认孩子们是否安全。

---

> Finally, an adult spoke. "All right, let's get ready for training." Asli's eyes widened. He nodded but didn't say anything. Bregan didn't dare to either. As they ran back through the caverns, Asli marked the walls with wax from the candle as high as he could.

**中文理解**：终于，一个成年人说话了。"好吧，让我们准备训练。"Asli的眼睛睁大了。他点了点头，但什么也没说。Bregan也不敢说。当他们跑回洞穴时，Asli用蜡烛的蜡在墙上标记，尽可能高。

**关键词**：adult spoke / ready for training / eyes widened / nodded / didn't say anything / didn't dare / marked the walls with wax

**为什么这样写**："ready for training"是Dr.Slunai的声音——孩子们在Lockup里。"marked the walls with wax"是Asli的谨慎——他们需要找到回去的路。

---

> "That was Dr. Slunai," Asli said quickly. "She works at the orphanage. Thora and I tried to get her to talk a few weeks ago. If she's there, that's the right place." Bregan dragged his worn-out legs through the tide, which was now waist high. "Explosives," he said, squinting against the daylight. "We blow out the metal grates, get the kids into the caves, and then sail them away."

**中文理解**："那是Slunai医生，"Asli快速说。"她在孤儿院工作。Thora和几周前试图让她开口。如果她在那里，那就是正确的地方。"Bregan拖着疲惫的双腿穿过潮水，现在已经到腰部了。"炸药，"他说，眯着眼睛看着日光。"我们炸开金属格栅，把孩子们带进洞穴，然后把他们运走。"

**关键词**：Dr. Slunai / orphanage / tried to get her to talk / right place / worn-out legs / tide / waist high / Explosives / blow out the metal grates / get the kids into the caves / sail them away

**为什么这样写**：Asli确认——Dr.Slunai在那里。"Explosives"是Bregan的计划——他们需要炸药。"blow out the metal grates"是方法——从内部突破。
