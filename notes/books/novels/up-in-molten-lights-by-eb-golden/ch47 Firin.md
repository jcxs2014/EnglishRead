# 47. Then（Firin视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：六个月前，Firin（作为Stav间谍）在Rambler's Tavern发现改革派的秘密会议。她偷听到他们计划突袭监狱，但Dustn和Skavka伏击了她。
- **情感弧线位置**：紧张——Firin被迫在Stav的职责和对Bregan的爱之间做出选择。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：间谍潜入——Firin伪装成吧台女孩，偷听改革派会议；Dustn的伏击揭示Stav一直在监视她。
- **人物弧线**：Firin从"忠诚的Stav间谍"转向"动摇的间谍"——她听到了改革派的计划，但她没有向Stav报告。
- **叙事手法**：回忆场景——通过Firin的内心独白和感官细节（"hot tea sloshed onto my hand"）推进情节。

## 精读

> "Can I get you something, miss?" the old man asked behind the Rambler's Tavern bar. Decades of kind smiles had etched permanent lines above his gray beard, but they were drawn in stiff worry as he scanned the room. One of his green eyes had the cloudy mark of age.

**中文理解**："我能给您拿点什么吗，小姐？"老人站在Rambler's Tavern吧台后面问。几十年的慈祥笑容在他的灰色胡须上方刻下了永久的皱纹，但它们被拉成僵硬的担忧，因为他扫视着房间。他的一只绿色眼睛有老年的浑浊斑点。

**关键词**：Decades of kind smiles / etched permanent lines / stiff worry / cloudy mark of age

**为什么为什么这样写**：Bakuit的"stiff worry"暗示他知道今晚有改革派的会议。"cloudy mark of age"是视觉细节——Firin记住了他的脸。

> A lifetime ago, Bregan had brought me here. He'd told me it was a formative place from his childhood and introduced me to this old man—Bakuit, if I remembered correctly. We'd danced, drunk, and laughed our way through the night, until we ended up on the roof in each other's arms.

**中文理解**：一生之前，Bregan带我来到这里。他告诉我这是他童年时代的一个形成性地方，并把我介绍给了这个老人——Bakuit，如果我没记错的话。我们跳舞、喝酒、笑着度过了一夜，直到我们在屋顶上相拥。

**关键词**：formative place / childhood / danced, drunk, and laughed / on the roof in each other's arms

**为什么这样写**：Firin的回忆——她和Bregan在这里有过美好的时光。"on the roof in each other's arms"是浪漫意象——他们的爱曾经是纯粹的。

---

> I saw Bregan everywhere—on market streets, in bars, at the docks—and it was never him. Just because this was the Rambler's bar and I'd left a piece of my heart here that night, that didn't mean he was here.

**中文理解**：我到处都看到Bregan——在集市街道、在酒吧、在码头——但那从来都不是他。仅仅因为这是Rambler的酒吧，那天晚上我把一片心留在了这里，并不意味着他在这里。

**关键词**：saw Bregan everywhere / it was never him / left a piece of my heart / didn't mean he was here

**为什么这样写**：Firin的"left a piece of my heart"是她的内心写照——她从未忘记Bregan。"it was never him"是现实——Bregan可能已经死了。

> At least one of the people in this bar was a Third Division Nodtacht, tasked with ensuring I wasn't committing treason. I suspected a blond man at the front of the room who'd entered shortly after me. I'd caught him staring at me at least twice. Whoever my tail was, I didn't want them to suspect I was here for any important reason.

**中文理解**：这个酒吧里至少有一个第三分部Nodtacht，负责确保我没有叛国。我怀疑房间前面一个金发男人在我进来后不久就进来了。我至少抓到他在盯着我两次。无论我的尾巴是谁，我不想让他们怀疑我来这里有任何重要原因。

**关键词**：Third Division Nodtacht / committing treason / my tail / suspect

**为什么这样写**：Firin的恐惧——她被Nodtacht跟踪。"my tail"是间谍术语——跟踪她的人。

---

> "This prison's got a few layers," a man with a deep voice was saying. "But I think the Five are in the tower at the center. This here is the straightest route to get there . . ." I leaped away from the wall. The Five? That's what people were calling Bregan's father and the other rebel leaders the Stav had captured on Bloody Fifthday. This was a meeting of Reformists.

**中文理解**："这个监狱有好几层，"一个声音低沉的人说。"但我认为五个人在中间的塔里。这是到达那里最直接的路线……"我从墙上跳开。五个人？这就是人们称呼Bregan的父亲和其他在血腥第五天被Stav抓获的反叛领袖。这是一次改革派的会议。

**关键词**：prison's got a few layers / the Five / tower at the center / straightest route / Reformists

**为什么这样写**：Firin偷听到改革派的计划——他们要突袭监狱。"the Five"是Pa和其他改革派领袖——这是Bregan的家人。

> "Mira?" I bumped a shelf, sending a box clattering to the floor. In the dark, the old bartender gaped at me, holding a bag of what looked like potatoes. "What are you doing down here? Who's manning the bar?"

**中文理解**："米拉？"我撞到了架子，让一个盒子咔嗒一声掉到地板上。在黑暗中，老酒吧老板目瞪口呆地看着我，拿着一袋看起来像土豆的东西。"你在这里做什么？谁在管理酒吧？"

**关键词**：bumped a shelf / clattering to the floor / gaping / potatoes / manning the bar

**为什么这样写**：Firin的失误——她撞到了架子。"manning the bar"是酒吧术语——谁在值班。

> At the end of the alley, Bakuit stood with someone familiar, a face I knew. It was one of Dustn's favorites: the pockmarked, scarred face he'd worn the day I met him. While the old man gesticulated, Dustn glanced up at me. He broke into a chilling grin. A bag came down over my head. Hands grabbed my wrists. "There's no escape now, girl," Skavka rasped in my ear.

**中文理解**：在巷子的尽头，Bakuit和一个我熟悉的人站在一起，一张我认识的脸。这是Dustn最喜欢的脸之一：我那天见到他时他戴的麻子伤疤脸。当老人做手势时，Dustn抬头看了我一眼。他露出了令人毛骨悚然的笑容。一个袋子套在我头上。手抓住了我的手腕。"现在逃不掉了，女孩，"Skavka在我耳边嘶嘶地说。

**关键词**：pockmarked, scarred face / gesticulated / chilling grin / bag came down over my head / no escape now

**为什么这样写**：Dustn的"chilling grin"是他的残酷——他一直在等Firin犯错。"bag came down over my head"是绑架——Skavka逮捕了她。
