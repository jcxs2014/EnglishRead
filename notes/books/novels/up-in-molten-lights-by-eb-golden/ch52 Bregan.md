# 52. Now（Bregan视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Bregan在Ruanti Perria的公寓拜访她。Ruanti透露她患有salt fever但幸存，父母死于该病。Bregan问她是否想当总统，Ruanti说"取决于人民是否想要我"。
- **情感弧线位置**：伏笔铺设——Bregan寻找合适的接班人。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：政治访谈——Ruanti的坦诚与Bregan的怀疑形成对比；Ruanti的"我们都是以自我为中心的生物"是对政治本质的洞察。
- **人物弧线**：Bregan从"寻找完美候选人"转向"接受不完美的现实"——Ruanti不是完美的，但她可能是最好的选择。
- **叙事手法**：对话场景——通过两人的对话推进情节，Bregan的内心独白揭示他的真实想法。

## 精读

> The shipyard docks taunted Bregan. He wanted to sail north to Nusias. He wanted to get moving on their plans, but he couldn't until he heard from Suri.

**中文理解**：造船厂码头嘲弄着Bregan。他想北上Nusias。他想继续推进计划，但在收到Suri的消息之前他不能。

**关键词**：shipyard docks / taunted / sail north / get moving / until he heard from Suri

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的焦虑——他被迫等待。"taunted"是码头在嘲笑他的无力。

---

> Unfortunately, whenever he found himself alone and aimless, he thought only of Thora. He kept replaying how she'd touched his cheek, analyzing the memory from every angle, trying to make some sense of why she'd done it and why he'd let her.

**中文理解**：不幸的是，每当他发现自己独自一人、漫无目的时，他只想到了Thora。他不断回想她是如何触摸他的脸颊，从各个角度分析这段记忆，试图弄清楚她为什么这样做，以及他为什么允许她。

**关键词**：alone and aimless / thought only of Thora / replaying / touched his cheek / analyzing / every angle / make some sense

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的痛苦——他无法停止想Thora。"analyzing from every angle"是他的理性试图理解感性。

---

> "Did you know Ruanti Perria as a child?" he asked an old woman. She was perched on a bench that looked out over the bay, her lap full of a blue knitting project.

**中文理解**："你小时候认识Ruanti Perria吗？"他问一位老妇人。她坐在长椅上，望着海湾，膝盖上满是蓝色的编织物。

**关键词**：perched on a bench / looked out over the bay / lap full of a blue knitting project

**为什么这样写**：老妇人的"perched"和"blue knitting project"是日常生活的细节——她在做手工。

---

> "She had the salt fever?" Bregan looked up from his notes. "She had the salt fever?" The sickness had recently spread through the lower districts like a summer fire, something Thora claimed Hulei was using to fake the deaths of the Nodtacht children.

**中文理解**："她得了salt fever？"Bregan从笔记本上抬起头来。"她得了salt fever？"这种病最近在低层街区蔓延，就像夏天的火灾，Thora声称Hulei用它来伪造Nodtacht儿童的死亡。

**关键词**：salt fever / lower districts / summer fire / fake the deaths

**为什么为什么这样写**：Bregan的惊讶——Ruanti患有salt fever。"fake the deaths"是Thora的理论——Hulei用salt fever来掩盖谋杀。

---

> "I heard that Saint Chyira spoke to Perria on the brink of death," the younger woman said. "Chyira told her how to help us Labor folk." Bregan hid a grimace. Why did people always want to consecrate their heroes? Ruanti was a person, just like Hulei. Believing them touched by the gods was dangerous.

**中文理解**："我听说Chyira圣人在Perria临终前对她说话，"年轻女子说。"Chyira告诉她如何帮助我们Labor人民。"Bregan隐藏了一个鬼脸。为什么人们总是想神化他们的英雄？Ruanti是人，就像Hulei一样。相信他们被神明触摸是危险的。

**关键词**：on the brink of death / consecrate their heroes / touched by the gods / dangerous

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的理性——他不相信"圣人显灵"。"consecrate their heroes"是Bregan的批评——人们需要英雄是危险的。

---

> The apartment building was newer than the one Bregan grew up in, but the layout was the same. He tried not to imagine the ghosts of his parents as he followed Ruanti down a hall on the first floor.

**中文理解**：公寓楼比Bregan长大的那座更新，但布局是一样的。他试图不去想象父母的鬼魂，当他跟着Ruanti走过一楼的走廊时。

**关键词**：newer / layout was the same / tried not to imagine / ghosts of his parents

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的记忆——布局相同但父母已逝。"ghosts of his parents"是Bregan的痛苦——他失去了他们。

---

> "Why did you decide to help the Reformists?" ... He hadn't. Bregan had resisted helping the movement for as long as possible, to protect his mother, then to protect Firin. He hadn't joined the Reformists until after Pa was arrested. He only helped Addym restart the revolution to save his father and right his mother's wrongs. He wasn't altruistic. He never had been.

**中文理解**："你为什么要帮助改革派？"……他没有。Bregan尽可能长时间地抵制帮助运动，为了保护他的母亲，然后为了保护Firin。直到Pa被捕后他才加入改革派。他帮助Addym重启革命只是为了拯救他的父亲并纠正他母亲的错误。他不是利他主义者。他从来都不是。

**关键词**：resisted helping / protect his mother / protect Firin / wasn't altruistic

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的坦诚——他不是英雄。"wasn't altruistic"是他的自知之明——他做的一切都是自私的。

---

> "We are all selfish creatures, Mr. Nimsayrth," Ruanti said, squinting out the window. "The difference between right and wrong lies in who else benefits when we help ourselves."

**中文理解**："我们都是自私的生物，Nimsayrth先生，"Ruanti说，向窗外眯起眼睛。"对与错的区别在于，当我们帮助自己时，还有谁受益。"

**关键词**：selfish creatures / difference between right and wrong / who else benefits / help ourselves

**为什么这样写**：Ruanti的智慧——她理解人性的自私。"who else benefits"是她的政治哲学——好的领导者让其他人也受益。

---

> "Don't misunderstand me," Ruanti said. "I am not saying your sacrifices were for nothing. You and the other Reformists have given the lower classes a voice. But if we do not fight tooth and nail to use it, then we will be silenced once again."

**中文理解**："别误会，"Ruanti说。"我不是说你的牺牲毫无意义。你和其他改革派给了下层阶级一个声音。但如果我们不拼命去使用它，我们就会再次被噤声。"

**关键词**：sacrifices were for nothing / given the lower classes a voice / fight tooth and nail / silenced once again

**为什么这样写**：Ruanti的激励——她感谢Bregan的牺牲。"fight tooth and nail"是她的决心——她会为Labor的权利而战。

---

> "You don't think it's dangerous to speak such things?" Her eyes flared. "It shouldn't be." Perhaps she wasn't as naive to the danger Hulei posed as Bregan had thought.

**中文理解**："你不说这些话危险吗？"她的眼睛闪烁。"不应该危险。"也许她不像Bregan想的那样对Hulei构成的危险天真。

**关键词**：dangerous / eyes flared / It shouldn't be / naive

**为什么这样写**：Ruanti的勇气——"shouldn't be"是她的信念——言论自由是理所当然的。"Perhaps she wasn't as naive"是Bregan的重新评估。

---

> Saints, her resolve was alluring. In a strange way, she reminded him of Firin the first time he'd met her on the roof of the Grande—determined, and desperate for freedom—and yet, Ruanti was pulling up the entire Labor class alongside her.

**中文理解**：天哪，她的决心很诱人。奇怪的是，她让他想起了他第一次在Grande屋顶上见到Firin时的样子——坚定，渴望自由——然而Ruanti正在把整个Labor阶级拉上来。

**关键词**：resolve was alluring / reminded him of Firin / desperate for freedom / pulling up the entire Labor class

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的比较——Ruanti让他想起Firin。"pulling up the entire Labor class"是Ruanti的领导力——她不是为自己，而是为整个阶级。

---

> He drained the whiskey. "Would you ever run for president?" "We have a president." Bregan said nothing, and understanding slowly dawned on her face. "It would depend," she said, "on if the people wanted me to."

**中文理解**：他喝干了威士忌。"你会竞选总统吗？""我们有总统。"Bregan什么也没说，理解慢慢浮现在她的脸上。"这取决于，"她说，"人民是否希望我这样做。"

**关键词**：drained the whiskey / run for president / We have a president / understanding slowly dawned / if the people wanted me to

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的试探——他在问Ruanti是否想当总统。"if the people wanted me to"是Ruanti的民主态度——她不会强求。
