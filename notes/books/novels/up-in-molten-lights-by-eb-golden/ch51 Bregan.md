# 51. Now（Bregan视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Bregan向Suri坦白了一切（关于Hulei、Nodtacht、Ma的逮捕）。Suri同意帮忙获取炸药。Bregan告诉Firin他不能爱Thora，因为他还在爱着Firin的幽灵。Firin意识到Bregan已经认出了她。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Bregan向Suri坦白，同时Firin意识到Bregan知道她是谁。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：坦白场景——Bregan向Suri解释他逮捕Ma的原因；Bregan的"tortured hero"形象在此达到顶峰。
- **人物弧线**：Bregan从"隐藏情感"转向"表达真实感受"——他告诉Firin他不能爱Thora，因为他还在爱Firin的幽灵。
- **叙事手法**：密闭空间对话——通过三人对话推进情节，Bregan的内心独白揭示他的真实想法。

## 精读

> Bregan told Suri everything. In an unfiltered stream of consciousness, he explained how he and Firin—Vota, to Suri—discovered that Hulei had bombed his own rally. Unable to meet Suri's eyes, he told her that he'd hoped to expose Hulei on opening night of Qoyn & Insei, but it all went downhill.

**中文理解**：Bregan告诉Suri一切。在一条未经过滤的意识流中，他解释了他和Firin——对Suri来说是Vota——如何发现Hulei炸毁了自己的集会。他无法与Suri对视，他告诉她他希望在Qoyn & Insei的首演之夜揭露Hulei，但一切都走下坡路了。

**关键词**：told Suri everything / unfiltered stream of consciousness / Firin—Vota / discovered / bombed his own rally / expose Hulei / opening night / went downhill

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"unfiltered stream of consciousness"是坦白——他把一切都告诉了Suri。"Firin—Vota"是Bregan的认知——他知道Thora就是Vota（Firin的化名）。

---

> He described everything from how his father was brainwashed to how Hulei made him arrest his own mother. Finally, he explained that Hulei was kidnapping orphans to create his own face-changing spies, and where the kids were in the Cove.

**中文理解**：他描述了一切，从他的父亲被洗脑到Hulei如何让他逮捕自己的母亲。最后，他解释了Hulei如何绑架孤儿来制造自己的变脸间谍，以及孩子们在Cove的哪里。

**关键词**：father was brainwashed / arrest his own mother / kidnapping orphans / create his own face-changing spies / where the kids were in the Cove

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"arrest his own mother"是痛苦——他被迫逮捕Ma。"face-changing spies"是Hulei的计划——制造Nodtacht军队。

---

> He confessed everything except Asli's identity and their plan to assassinate the president, as neither seemed critical for her to know. "Long story short," he concluded, "we need explosives to free them."

**中文理解**：他坦白了一切，除了Asli的身份和他们的暗杀总统计划，因为这两者似乎都不需要她知道。"长话短说，"他总结道，"我们需要炸药来解救他们。"

**关键词**：confessed everything / except Asli's identity / assassinate the president / not critical / Long story short / need explosives / free them

**为什么这样写**：Bregan保留了两个秘密——Asli的身份和暗杀计划。"not critical"是Bregan的判断——Suri不需要知道一切。

---

> Suri stared at him, frozen in her chair. Throughout the monologue, the color had slowly drained from her face. She either thought they were insane or was horrified by the truth. He prayed it was the latter.

**中文理解**：Suri盯着他，僵在椅子上。在整个独白过程中，她的脸上慢慢失去了血色。她要么觉得他们疯了，要么被真相吓坏了。他祈祷是后者。

**关键词**：stared at him / frozen / monologue / color had slowly drained / insane / horrified by the truth / prayed it was the latter

**为什么这样写**：Suri的"color had slowly drained"是恐惧——她被真相吓坏了。"prayed it was the latter"是Bregan的希望——他希望Suri相信他。

---

> "You trust me?" Suri asked softly. Bregan felt sick. "You protect your own." "You haven't spoken to me since the fire." "I haven't spoken to any of the Players," he said.

**中文理解**："你信任我？"Suri轻声问。Bregan感到恶心。"你保护自己人。""火灾后你就没跟我说过话。""我没跟任何Players说过话，"他说。

**关键词**：trust me / felt sick / protect your own / haven't spoken / since the fire / any of the Players

**为什么这样写**：Suri的"trust me"是质疑——她想知道Bregan是否信任她。"protect your own"是Bregan的答案——他会保护自己的人。

---

> "He's killing children," Bregan interrupted. "We can get them out, but we need explosives, and we don't have a lot of time. Can you help us?" For a long moment, she didn't say anything; she just held his gaze, brimming with unspoken things—apologies and empathy, probably—neither of which Bregan deserved.

**中文理解**："他在杀孩子，"Bregan打断道。"我们可以把他们救出来，但我们需要炸药，而且我们没有多少时间。你能帮我们吗？"她很长时间没有说话；她只是盯着他的眼睛，充满了未说出口的事情——道歉和同情，大概——Bregan都不配得到。

**关键词**：killing children / get them out / need explosives / don't have a lot of time / help us / long moment / didn't say anything / held his gaze / brimming with unspoken things / apologies and empathy / neither of which Bregan deserved

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"killing children"是道德制高点——Hulei在杀孩子。"brimming with unspoken things"是Suri的情感——她有话要说。

---

> Suri chewed her lip. She and Thora stared at one another, as if engaging in some kind of silent challenge. Finally, she nodded. "Cidd's cousin was our contact to the dealers in Gwynythaid. I can get word to them, but they won't come any closer than Nusias, which used to be good cover because no one was out there, but they're overrun with the new military training outposts now."

**中文理解**：Suri咬着嘴唇。她和Thora互相盯着，仿佛在进行某种无声的挑战。最后，她点了点头。"Cidd的堂兄是我们在Gynythaid的经销商联系人。我可以给他们传话，但他们不会比Nusias更近，那里过去是很好的掩护，因为没有人去那里，但现在被新的军事训练前哨占据了。"

**关键词**：chewed her lip / stared at one another / silent challenge / nodded / Cidd's cousin / contact / dealers / get word / Nusias / good cover / overrun / military training outposts

**为什么这样写**：Suri的"chewed her lip"是犹豫——她在权衡风险。"good cover"是Nusias的优势——偏远。"overrun"是Nusias的现状——军事训练前哨。

---

> "I can get there," Bregan said. After Ma's arrest, Pa had fled back to the military base on Nusias like a dog with his tail between his legs. At least his cowardice would give Bregan an excuse to visit.

**中文理解**："我能到那里，"Bregan说。Ma被捕后，Pa像一条夹着尾巴的狗一样逃回了Nusias的军事基地。至少他的懦弱给了Bregan一个借口去探望。

**关键词**：can get there / Pa had fled / military base / Nusias / like a dog with his tail between his legs / cowardice / excuse to visit

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"like a dog with his tail between his legs"是Pa的懦弱——他逃跑了。"excuse to visit"是Bregan的机会——他可以借机获取炸药。

---

> "I'll send a message, then," Suri said. "What will you do with the kids after?" "I'm working on that," Thora said quickly. "I think I'll take them north, slip them into the countryside where they'll be safe."

**中文理解**："那我就发个消息，"Suri说。"之后你打算怎么安置孩子们？""我正在想办法，"Thora快速说。"我想我会带他们北上，把他们送到乡下安全的地方。"

**关键词**：send a message / do with the kids after / working on that / take them north / slip them into the countryside / safe

**为什么这样写**：Thora的"take them north"是计划——她要把孩子们带到安全的地方。"slip them into the countryside"是方法——躲藏。

---

> As she left, Thora said, "If anyone asks, these two are fighting over me, and I'm trying to resolve it." When she glanced at Bregan, there was heat in her smirk. When he didn't return it, her composure flickered. Bregan glanced at his boots. As necessary as their public show of affection was, he couldn't get caught up in Thora's game. It was as agonizing as it was unfair. She wasn't who he wanted her to be; she never could be.

**中文理解**：她离开时，Thora说："如果有人问起，这两个人在为我争吵，我正在努力解决。"当Bregan瞥了她一眼时，她的 smirk 中带着热度。当Bregan没有回应时，她的镇定闪烁了一下。Bregan看向自己的靴子。尽管他们的公开展示爱意是必要的，但他不能陷入Thora的游戏。这既痛苦又不公平。她不是他想让她成为的人；她永远不可能成为。

**关键词**：fighting over me / trying to resolve it / heat in her smirk / didn't return it / composure flickered / glanced at his boots / public show of affection / caught up in Thora's game / agonizing / unfair / wasn't who he wanted her to be / never could be

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"wasn't who he wanted her to be"是心碎——Thora不是Firin。"agonizing"是Bregan的痛苦——他必须与一个不是Firin的女人表演爱意。
