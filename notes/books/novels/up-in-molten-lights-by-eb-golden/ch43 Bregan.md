# 43. Now（Bregan视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Bregan在旧Stav监狱审讯Ma，向Hulei谎称Ma知道Asli的下落，争取到4天隔离时间。他必须在这4天内杀死Hulei，否则Ma会被处决。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Bregan被迫审讯自己的母亲，同时向Hulei透露Asli还活着的信息。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：审讯场景——Bregan的"grim-but-determined captor"表演；Ma的"no fear in her eyes, just regret"揭示她的内心。
- **人物弧线**：Bregan从"寻找母亲"转向"利用母亲"——他必须牺牲Ma来换取Hulei的信任。
- **叙事手法**：密闭空间对峙——通过对话和肢体语言推进，Bregan的内心独白揭示他的真实想法。

## 精读

> In a pool of torchlight at the end of the old Stav prison's interrogation corridor, Hulei spoke with Pa in a low, heated debate. The prison fortress butted up against Luisonn's southern cliffs and was comprised of concentric half-circle, open-air corridors.

**中文理解**：在旧Stav监狱审讯走廊尽头的火炬光池中，Hulei和Pa在低声激烈地争论。这座监狱堡垒紧贴Luisonn的南部悬崖，由同心半圆形的露天走廊组成。

**关键词**：interrogation corridor / prison fortress / concentric half-circle, open-air corridors

**为什么这样写**：监狱的"concentric half-circle"结构是视觉隐喻——Bregan被困在Hulei的权力结构中。"interrogation走廊"是Ma即将被带进去的地方。

---

> The storm had finally receded, and the sky loomed ink-dark above them as Bregan approached. Briny air carried brisk sea winds and the distant calls of gulls.

**中文理解**：暴风雨终于过去了，天空在他们上方显得漆黑一片。Bregan走近时，咸涩的空气带着清新的海鸥叫声。

**关键词**：storm had finally receded / ink-dark / briny air / distant calls of gulls

**为什么这样写**：暴风雨的"receded"是隐喻——Bregan的内心风暴才刚刚开始。"ink-dark"暗示Hulei的黑暗统治。

---

> "Bullshit. Hulei wouldn't put Ma on a public stand. She knew too much, especially about Asli Hulei. There were a lot of ways for a woman to unexpectedly perish behind prison walls, and one of them would surely befall his mother before a trial could start."

**中文理解**："废话。Hulei不会把Ma放在公共审判台上。她知道得太多了，尤其是关于Asli Hulei的事。在监狱围墙后面，一个女人有很多方式意外死亡，其中一种肯定会在审判开始前降临到他母亲身上。"

**关键词**：Bullshit / public stand / unexpectedly perish / befall his mother

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的内心独白——他知道Hulei会杀死Ma。"befall"是古语词，暗示这是命运。

---

> "Has anyone questioned her yet?" he asked. "We waited for you," the president said. His scrutiny lingered on Bregan's face. "May I?" Bregan braced himself. If he wished to stand a chance of finding the Nodtacht children, he needed to convince the president, without a shred of doubt, that he was on his side.

**中文理解**："有人审讯过她吗？"他问。"我们在等你，"总统说。他的目光在Bregan的脸上停留。"我可以吗？"Bregan鼓起勇气。如果他想有机会找到Nodtacht儿童，他需要让总统毫无疑问地相信他站在他这边。

**关键词**：scrutiny lingered / without a shred of doubt / on his side

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"braced himself"是恐惧——他必须审讯自己的母亲。"without a shred of doubt"是他的目标——他必须让Hulei完全信任他。

---

> "A letter can't hold an explanation for abandoning your son," he snarled. "A letter can't make up for running away from your dying sister. Did you leave a letter for Iima too?"

**中文理解**："一封信无法解释你抛弃儿子。一封信也无法弥补你逃离垂死的姐妹。你也给Iima留了信吗？"

**关键词**：abandoning your son / make up for / running away from your dying sister

**为什么这样写**：Bregan对Ma的愤怒到达了顶点——他用Iima来攻击Ma的良知。"make up for"是道德层面的追讨——Ma的道歉永远不够。

---

> "I acted before the insurrection," Ma said, heat threading into her tone. "You can't call it treason just because it happened to the people who now happen to be in power."

**中文理解**："我在起义前就行动了，"Ma说，语气中带着热度。"你不能仅仅因为碰巧掌权的人碰巧是那些人就称之为叛国。"

**关键词**：acted before the insurrection / treason / happen to be in power

**为什么这样写**：Ma的辩护——她的行动发生在掌权者之前。"happen to be in power"是讽刺——Hulei的掌权是偶然的。

---

> "Cut the shit," Pa growled, stepping into the lamp's light, trembling with more rage than Bregan had ever seen. Ma swiveled to face him. Instantly, she blanched. Her gaze traced from the scars on Pa's head, to the wounds on his face, to the sling binding his arm. All visible signs of what her betrayal had cost him.

**中文理解**："废话少说，"Pa咆哮着走进灯光，颤抖着，带着Bregan从未见过的愤怒。Ma转向面对他。立刻，她脸色苍白。她的目光从Pa头上的疤痕，到他脸上的伤口，到他手臂上的绷带。所有这些都是她背叛的可见代价。

**关键词**：Cut the shit / blanched / scars / wounds / sling / visible signs

**为什么这样写**：Pa的"Cut the shit"是粗口——他的愤怒已经到了顶点。Ma的"blanched"是恐惧——她看到了自己的背叛给Pa带来的伤害。

---

> "How?" Pa shouted. "How could you betray everything we sacrificed for?" "I did it for you," she said. "For Bregan." "Do not blame what you did on our son."

**中文理解**："怎么能？"Pa喊道。"你怎么能背叛我们为之牺牲的一切？""我是为了你，"她说。"为了Bregan。""不要把你所做的归咎于我们的儿子。"

**关键词**：betray everything we sacrificed for / For you / For Bregan / blame what you did on our son

**为什么这样写**：Ma的辩护——她是为了保护Pa和Bregan。但Pa的"blame what you done on our son"是愤怒——他拒绝让Bregan为Ma的背叛负责。

---

> "You sold our secrets. You set the movement back four years. You sent me to fucking prison." Ma paled but squared her shoulders again. "If you are determined to make me the villain, then fine."

**中文理解**："你出卖了我们的秘密。你让运动倒退了四年。你把我送进了该死的监狱。"Ma脸色苍白，但再次挺起肩膀。"如果你决心让我成为恶棍，那就好吧。"

**关键词**：sold our secrets / set the movement back / fucking prison / villain

**为什么这样写**：Pa的指控——Ma的背叛导致了"four years"的倒退。"fucking prison"是粗口——Pa的愤怒已经到了顶点。Ma的"villain"是自我认知——她接受了自己的角色。

---

> "Isolate her for four days. Meals only." The same treatment Pa had suggested for the Reformist traitors weeks ago. To loosen their tongues, he'd said.

**中文理解**："隔离她四天。只给饭吃。"这是Pa几周前对Reformist叛徒建议的同一待遇。为了让他们开口，他说。

**关键词**：Isolate her for four days / Meals only / loosen their tongues

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"隔离四天"是他的策略——他需要时间来杀死Hulei。"loosen their tongues"是讽刺——Bregan用这个借口来争取时间。

---

> "Sir... I believe your son, Asli, is alive." Hulei's lips parted. Nusio straightened. "I wouldn't say so if I didn't believe it," Bregan pressed. "In Qunsii, I discovered my mother was asking questions about someone I later learned was Asli Hulei. She met with him a few years ago and has been trying to get in contact with him again. I believe your son faked his death and is now making treasonous claims against you. My mother is trying to help him."

**中文理解**："先生……我相信你的儿子Asli还活着。" Hulei的嘴唇张开。Nusio站直了。"如果我不相信，我不会这么说，"Bregan继续说。"在Qunsii，我发现我母亲在问关于一个人的问题，后来我得知那是Asli Hulei。她几年前见过他，并一直试图再次与他联系。我相信你的儿子伪造了死亡，现在正在对你提出叛国指控。我的母亲正在试图帮助他。"

**关键词**：Asli, is alive / faked his death / treasonous claims / trying to help him

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的谎言——他告诉Hulei Asli还活着。"treasonous claims"是Bregan的策略——他让Hulei相信Ma是威胁，从而争取到时间。

---

> Now he just had to kill the president before Ma cracked.

**中文理解**：现在他只需要在Ma崩溃之前杀死总统。

**关键词**：kill the president / before Ma cracked

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的决心——他必须杀死Hulei。"before Ma cracked"是时间压力——Ma可能无法承受审讯。

## 一句话总结
Bregan在旧Stav监狱审讯Ma，向Hulei谎称Ma知道Asli的下落，争取到4天隔离时间。他必须在这4天内杀死Hulei，否则Ma会被处决。Bregan意识到他正在重复Ma的悲剧——为了保护所爱的人，他必须牺牲所爱的人。
