# 45. Now（Bregan视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Bregan在Spires码头观察Ruanti Perria——一个正在崛起的Labor政治家。他意识到她可能是推翻Hulei后的合适接班人，但Nusio也在监视她。Bregan收到一张纸条，上面写着"我找到了他们"和一个Bilge区的地址。
- **情感弧线位置**：伏笔铺设——Bregan寻找合适的接班人，同时继续调查Nodtacht儿童的下落。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：政治观察——Ruanti的演讲让读者想起Hulei的早期；Bregan的"she reminded me of what my father stands for"揭示了他的政治理想。
- **人物弧线**：Bregan从"复仇者"转向"建设者"——他不仅想杀死Hulei，还想建立一个更好的国家。
- **叙事手法**——政治演讲场景——通过Ruanti的演讲和Bregan的内心独白揭示政治背景。

## 精读

> The humidity blanketing the docks felt thick and heavy on the back of Bregan's neck as he watched Ruanti Perria captivate a small group of fishermen in the Spires. The politician stood atop an overturned crate, her shoulder-length blonde curls glowing in the midday sunlight as she delivered a lively oratory that could rival the president's speeches. She looked like a Labor version of Ihan Hulei. Bregan didn't know how to feel about her. He didn't trust her.

**中文理解笼罩码头的潮湿感厚重地压在Bregan的颈后，他看着Ruanti Perria在Spires码头吸引了一小群渔民。这位政治家站在一个翻过来的箱子上，她齐肩的金色卷发在正午的阳光下闪闪发光，她的生动演讲可以与总统的演讲相媲美。她看起来像是Ihan Hulei的Labor版本。Bregan不知道该怎么看她。他不信任她。

**关键词**：humidity blanketing the docks / captivate / overturned crate / lively oratory / rival the president's speeches

**为什么这样写**："humidity blanketing"是触觉描写——Luisonn的潮湿。"captivate"是Ruanti的魅力——她能够吸引人群。"Labor version of Ihan Hulei"是Bregan的第一印象——她像Hulei，但来自Labor阶层。

---

> When Bregan had arrived, he'd spotted Nusio immediately, hovering near a pile of crab traps, watching the politician with hawklike focus. Ever since Bregan's performance with Ma in interrogation, he had noticed various young constables tailing him in Nusio's place. It seemed he'd won a bit of the president's trust.

**中文理解**：Bregan到达时，立刻发现了Nusio，他潜伏在一堆蟹笼附近，用鹰一样的专注盯着这位政治家。自从Bregan在审讯中与Ma的表演以来，他注意到各种年轻警察在接替Nusio跟踪他。看来他赢得了总统的一些信任。

**关键词**：spotted Nusio immediately / hovering / hawklike focus / young constables tailing him

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的观察力——他发现了Nusio。"hawklike focus"是Nusio的专注——他像猎鹰一样盯着Ruanti。

---

> "This government has been in power for three months, and what have they done for you?" Ruanti asked the group of sweating fisherfolk and net weavers. "Have they put food on your tables? No. Not unless you've signed up for their military. Have they lowered costs? No. They closed trade, pushing prices up."

**中文理解**："这个政府已经掌权三个月了，他们为你做了什么？"Ruanti问一群满头大汗的渔民和织网工。"他们把食物放在你的餐桌上？没有。除非你报名参军。他们降低成本了吗？没有。他们关闭了贸易，推高了价格。"

**关键词**：in power for three months / put food on your tables / signed up for their military / closed trade / pushing prices up

**为什么这样写**：Ruanti的演讲——她指出了Hulei政权的失败。"pushing prices up"是经济现实——Hulei的贸易限制伤害了Labor阶层。

---

> Onlookers grunted in agreement, and Bregan's skin crawled. It wasn't that he disagreed with her. He agreed, emphatically. But he wasn't about to put all his faith in another stranger, especially one so unknown, even if she was of the Labor class.

**关键词**：skin crawled / agreed, emphatically / put all his faith in another stranger

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的内心独白——他同意Ruanti，但不信任她。"put all his faith in another stranger"是他的谨慎——他不会再犯错误。

---

> "Right now there is only one Labor on a council of five. Even if Thom Draifey's seat was filled, the lower class would be outnumbered. Tell me, how can this council possibly represent you?" Hopping off the crate, Ruanti pulled a paper from a leather satchel. She brandished it high above her head. "If you agree with me, sign this petition to add two additional Labor seats to the council. One for Luisonn and one for the outer isles."

**中文理解**："现在在一个五人委员会中只有一个Labor。即使Thom Draifey的席位被填补，下层阶级也会被人数压倒。告诉我，这个委员会怎么可能代表你？"Ruanti从箱子上跳下，从皮革公文包里拿出一张纸。她把它高高举过头顶。"如果你同意我，签这份请愿书，要求在委员会中增加两个额外的Labor席位。一个给Luisonn，一个给外岛。"

**关键词**：only one Labor on a council of five / outnumbered / sign this petition / additional Labor seats

**为什么这样写**：Ruanti的行动——她不仅在说，还在做。"sign this petition"是她的策略——她需要公众支持。

---

> "Don't trust her. Don't trust any of them political types. They always want somethin' you can't see." Bregan couldn't disagree with that.

**中文理解**："不要信任她。不要信任任何政治人物。他们总是想要你看不到的东西。" Bregan不得不同意这一点。

**关键词**：Don't trust her / political types / want somethin' you can't see

**为什么这样写**：老人的警告——"political types"的不可信。"want somethin' you can't see"是政治的本质——隐藏议程。

---

> Ruanti, to her credit, was thawing through that suspicion. She beamed as people signed her petition. She spoke to each newcomer with a fierce focus, as if they were each the only person in the world. Her care appeared genuine.

**中文理解**：Ruanti值得称赞，她正在融化这种怀疑。当人们签署她的请愿书时，她笑了。她以强烈的专注与每个新人交谈，仿佛他们都是世界上唯一一个人。她的关心似乎是真诚的。

**关键词**：thawing through that suspicion / beamed / fierce focus / care appeared genuine

**为什么这样写**：Ruanti的真诚——"thawing through that suspicion"是她的能力。"care appeared genuine"是Bregan的判断——她可能是真诚的。

---

> Bregan slowed. She knew him? Blushing, she tucked a blonde curl behind her ear. "I've seen you at the president's speeches. It's an honor to meet you."

**中文理解**：Bregan放慢了脚步。她认识他？脸红着，她把一缕金色卷发塞到耳后。"我在总统的演讲中见过你。很荣幸认识你。"

**关键词**：blushing / tucked a blonde curl behind her ear / seen you at the president's speeches / honor

**为什么这样写**：Ruanti的"blushing"是紧张——她知道Bregan是谁。"honor"是她的敬意——Bregan是英雄。

---

> "You must forgive me for hovering at the back," Bregan said. "I was curious about your endeavors. Your name is all over the city right now."

**中文理解**："请原谅我在后面徘徊，"Bregan说。"我对你的努力很好能。你的名字现在全城都在谈论。"

**关键词**：hovering at the back / curious about your endeavors / name is all over the city

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的借口——"hovering at the back"是观察。"name is all over the city"是Ruanti的知名度——她已经引起了注意。

---

> "I admire you, deeply. You did the work I regret that I did not. I am merely trying to make the most of what your sacrifices gifted us. This country has a chance to become so much more than it is, and we have you to thank for that."

**中文理解**："我深深地钦佩你。你做了我后悔没有做的事。我只是想充分利用你的牺牲给我们带来的东西。这个国家有机会变得比现在更好，我们要感谢你。"

**关键词**：admire you, deeply / regret that I did not / sacrifices gifted us / have you to thank

**为什么这样写**：Ruanti的敬意——"admire you, deeply"是真诚的。"sacrifices gifted us"是感谢——Bregan的牺牲让国家变得更好。

---

> Bregan squinted toward the horizon. Her infectious optimism stung. Whatever democracy she believed he had helped build was a sham. Hulei, not the people, held the power, as surely as any dictator or king.

**中文理解**：Bregan向地平线眯起眼睛。她的传染性乐观刺痛了她。无论她相信他帮助建立了什么民主，都是假的。Hulei，而不是人民，掌握着权力，就像任何独裁者或国王一样。

**关键词**：infectious optimism stung / democracy / sham / Hulei held the power / dictator or king

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的内心——他看到了Ruanti的天真。"sham"是现实——Hulei的民主是假的。"dictator or king"是Hulei的本质。

---

> "You remind me of what my father stands for, or at least, what he used to." Her eyes widened. Bregan stepped out of her personal space.

**中文理解**："你让我想起了我父亲所代表的，或者至少，他曾经代表的。"她的眼睛睁大了。Bregan走出了她的个人空间。

**关键词**：remind me of what my father stands for / what he used to / personal space

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的评价——Ruanti像Pa。"what he used to"暗示Pa已经改变了。"personal space"是Bregan的谨慎——他不想太接近。

---

> "Elections are on hold." "For now." She smiled. In the distance, Nusio glanced over with interest. Ruanti Perria had no idea what fire she was playing with.

**中文理解**："选举被搁置了。""暂时。"她笑了。在远处，Nusio感兴趣地瞥了一眼。Ruanti Perria不知道她在玩什么火。

**关键词**：Elections are on hold / For now / what fire she was playing with

**为什么这样写**：Ruanti的"For now"是承诺——选举会恢复。"what fire she was playing with"是危险——Hulei可能杀了她。

---

> "I don't trust her," Bregan said, looking after Ruanti. It wasn't a lie. The woman seemed genuine, but everyone had skeletons. He couldn't trust or endorse her until he knew more—which meant he needed a good cover story for why he was looking into her. "Tell the president I'm going to keep an eye on her," he added. "We need to make sure she's not another Draifey."

**中文理解**："我不信任她，"Bregan说，看着Ruanti离开。这不是谎言。这个女人似乎很真诚，但每个人都有秘密。在她了解更多之前，他无法信任或支持她——这意味着他需要一个很好的掩护故事来解释他为什么在调查她。"告诉总统我会密切关注她，"他补充道。"我们需要确保她不是另一个Draifey。"

**关键词**：everyone had skeletons / couldn't trust or endorse / keep an eye on her / not another Draifey

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的谨慎——他不信任Ruanti。"skeletons"是每个人都有秘密。"not another Draifey"是Bregan的恐惧——Ruanti可能是Stav的间谍。

---

> "I found them," it said in neat, upper-class cursive. Below it was an address in the Bilge.

**中文理解**："我找到了他们，"用整洁的上层阶级草书写道。下面是一个Bilge区的地址。

**关键词**：I found them / neat, upper-class cursive / address in the Bilge

**为什么这样写**：纸条的内容——"I found them"是Firin的笔迹。"address in the Bilge"是孩子们的下落——Bilge区。
