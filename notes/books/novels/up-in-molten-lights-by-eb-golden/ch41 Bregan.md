# 41. Now（Bregan视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Nusio跟踪Bregan到Guilo家，Bregan发现"Thora"与Asli密谋。三人被迫合作：Bregan在内，Firin和Asli在外，计划先找到孩子，再用孩子引出Hulei。Bregan和Firin在雨中演了一出戏，假装三角恋，以欺骗Nusio。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Bregan被迫与"Thora"（Firin）表演三角恋，他们的痛苦如此真实，以至于Nusio相信了。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：三角恋假戏——Bregan的"heartbroken people do all sorts of irrational things"是他的自嘲；Firin引用《寡妇的眼泪》是互文性时刻。
- **人物弧线**：Bregan从"寻找母亲"转向"拯救母亲"——他意识到Ma牺牲了自己，他必须利用这一点。
- **叙事手法**：雨景——通过雨水、雷声和视觉细节推进情节，Bregan的内心独白揭示他的真实想法。

## 精读

> Nusio loitered across the street. Bregan cursed silently. When he left the Landings to find Sijyn, he hadn't been thinking straight. The guard had followed him right here.

**中文理解**：Nusio在街对面闲逛。Bregan默默咒骂。当他离开Landings去找Sijyn时，他脑子不清楚。警卫一直跟到了这里。

**关键词**：loitered / across the street / hadn't been thinking straight

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的粗心——他暴露了自己的位置。"hadn't been thinking straight"是他四天的心理崩溃的结果。

---

> "What are you doing?" Thora hissed, dragging him from the window. Citrus accosted him. Before he could stop himself, he braced her by the small of her back.

**中文理解**："你在做什么？"Thora把他从窗口拉开，低声说。柑橘的气味扑面而来。他还没来得及反应，就用手托住了她的腰。

**关键词**：hissed / Citrus accosted him / braced her by the small of her back

**为什么这样写**："Citrus"是Firin的香水——Bregan记得这个味道。"braced her by the small of her back"是他的本能——他仍然在保护她。

---

> Then Asli kneed him in the gut. Bregan grunted as he hit the wall. His pistol fell and Asli's gun bit his jaw, smooth and cold. "Nusio?" The other man leaned in with new, unyielding violence in his sneer.

**中文理解**：然后Asli用膝盖顶了他的肚子。Bregan撞上墙壁，发出咕哝声。他的手枪掉了下来，Asli的枪顶住他的下巴，冰冷而光滑。"Nusio？"另一个人凑过来，脸上带着新的、毫不掩饰的暴力。

**关键词**：kneed him in the gut / gun bit his jaw / unyielding violence

**为什么这样写**：Asli的暴力——他对Bregan的攻击是愤怒的反应。"gun bit his jaw"是触觉隐喻——枪的冰冷和光滑。

---

> "Are you going to turn me in to save your own ass, like you did your mother? Like you did your lover?"

**中文理解**："你要为了自救而把我交出去吗？就像你对你母亲那样？就像你对你的爱人那样？"

**关键词**：turn me in / save your own ass / your mother / your lover

**为什么这样写**：Asli的指控——Bregan出卖了Ma和Firin。"save your own ass"是粗口——Asli对Bregan的不信任已经到了顶点。

---

> "I didn't have proof," Bregan said. "You did." For months, Bregan had stewed in the anguish of knowing what Hulei was without any proof to back his claims.

**中文理解**："我没有证据，"Bregan说。"你有。"几个月来，Bregan一直在知道Hulei是什么却没有证据来支持他的指控的痛苦中煎熬。

**关键词**：hadn't had proof / stewed in the anguish

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的"stewed in the anguish"是他的内心写照——他几个月来一直在痛苦中煎熬。"stewed"是烹饪隐喻——痛苦像慢炖一样。

---

> He glanced to her arm—to the place where Firin's scar would have been, but wasn't. If what Asli said was true, and only children could get illusion, then Firin had been a Nodtacht since childhood. What horrors had she been through?

**中文理解**：他瞥了一眼她的手臂——Firin的疤痕应该在的地方，但没有。如果Asli说的是真的，只有儿童才能拥有幻象能力，那么Firin从小就是Nodtacht。她经历了怎样的恐怖？

**关键词**：Firin's scar / horrors / since childhood

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的推理——Firin从小就是Nodtacht。"horrors"是他的想象——他不知道Firin经历了什么。

---

> Thora wrung her fingers together. "A few days after you left, he and I... started courting." "Courting?" Bregan asked. Asli smiled smugly.

**中文理解**：Thora扭动着手指。"你离开几天后，他和我……开始约会了。""约会？"Bregan问。Asli得意地笑了。

关键词：wrung her fingers together / started courting / smiled smugly

**为什么这样写**：Firin的"courting"是谎言——她在编造故事。Asli的"smiled smugly"是他知道真相。

---

> "You really think that Hulei will believe I went to blows over you, a woman I just met, when the entire city knows I just had my heart irrevocably broken?"

**中文理解**："你真的认为Hulei会相信我会为了你——一个我刚认识的女人——动手吗，全城的人都知道我刚刚心碎欲绝？"

**关键词**：went to blows / irrevocably broken

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的讽刺——他不相信Hulei会相信他会为一个刚认识的女人打架。"irrevocably broken"是夸张——他的心已经碎了。

---

> "She gives you too much credit," Asli said, still leaning against the bedpost. He dabbed at his split lip with a handkerchief. Right back at you, Bregan thought. Out loud, he said, "You play your part, and I'll play mine. With any luck, your father's legacy will wither before it takes root."

**中文理解**："她给了你太多信任，"Asli说，仍然靠在床柱上。他用手帕擦拭裂开的嘴唇。你也是，Bregan想。他大声说："你演你的角色，我演我的。如果运气好的话，你父亲的遗产会在扎根之前就枯萎。"

**关键词**：gives you too much credit / split lip / legacy will wither

**为什么这样写**：Asli的"legacy will wither"是预言——Hulei的遗产会消失。"split lip"是细节——Bregan打伤了他。

---

> "The Widow's Tears was one of his mother's favorites, full of grief and heartbreak, but also love." ... "Lemon coated his tongue, conjuring memories of Firin dancing and laughing, holding and loving him, trusting him."

**中文理解**："《寡妇的眼泪》是他母亲最爱的一出戏，满溢悲伤与心碎，但也有爱。"……"柠檬味裹上他的舌头，唤起了Firin跳舞、大笑、拥抱他、爱他、信任他的记忆。"

**关键词**：The Widow's Tears / grief and heartbreak / Lemon coated his tongue

**为什么这样写**：Thora提议重演母亲生前最爱的那场戏，把诱饵直接挂在Bregan最柔软的家庭记忆上。"Lemon"是Firin的气味——身体比头脑更早认出假戏里的真心，也更容易被操纵。
