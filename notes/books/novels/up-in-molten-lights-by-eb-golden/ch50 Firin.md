# 50. Now（Firin视角）

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Bregan来到Grande找Suri要炸药。Firin在排练厅与Bregan重逢，Suri出现。Bregan告诉他们找到了Lockup的位置，需要炸药炸开金属格栅。Suri同意帮忙联系她在Gynythaid的黑市联系人。
- **情感弧线位置**：紧张——Firin和Bregan被迫继续他们的"三角恋"表演，Suri在场让气氛更加微妙。
- **Tropes兑现/反转**：Suri的怀疑——Suri知道Bregan和Firin之间的关系不简单；Bregan的"tortured hero"形象让剧团成员着迷。
- **人物弧线**：Firin从"独狼"转向"团队合作"——她开始信任Suri和Bregan。
- **叙事手法**：密闭空间对话——通过三人对话推进情节，Firin的内心独白揭示她的真实想法。

## 精读

> In an armchair in the Grande's green room, I drummed my fingers on my thigh. A dozen of the troupe's key members were reading through copies of From Fifthday's Ashes, but I couldn't quite make myself care about their reactions. My attention kept wandering to the door.

**中文理解**：在Grande绿色房间的扶手椅上，我用手指敲打着大腿。十几个剧团的关键成员正在阅读《从第五天的灰烬》的副本，但我无法让自己关心他们的反应。我的注意力一直在门上游荡。

**关键词**：Grande's green room / drummed my fingers / wandering to the door

**为什么这样写**：Firin的"drummed my fingers"是紧张——她在等Bregan。"wandering to the door"是她的注意力——她心不在焉。

---

> I couldn't stand sitting here, knowing the kids were out there. I kept replaying Bekat's fearful whispers, picturing Ihzi slung over the shoulder of a Nodtacht soldier, and imagining the toddlers in my bedroom on a surgical table, bleeding out like Ihzi's brother.

**中文理解**：我无法忍受坐在这里，知道孩子们在外面。我不断回想Bekat的低声恐惧，想象Ihzi被扛在Nodtacht士兵的肩膀上，想象我卧室里的孩子们在手术台上流血，就像Ihzi的兄弟一样。

**关键词**：couldn't stand / replaying Bekat's fearful whispers / picturing Ihzi slung over the shoulder / imagining the toddlers / surgical table / bleeding out

**为什么这样写**：Firin的内心痛苦——她无法忘记孩子们。"bleeding out like Ihzi's brother"是回忆——Ihzi的兄弟死于手术。

---

> That morning, Asli had sent me a note that I should apologize to Bregan for our "fight" at his townhome. I assumed Bregan needed an excuse to come visit me, so I complied. I desperately hoped this meant they'd found the kids.

**中文理解**：那天早上，Asli给我发了一张纸条，让我为我们在Bregan联排别墅的"争吵"向他道歉。我猜Bregan需要一个借口来拜访我，所以我照做了。我拼命希望这意味着他们找到了孩子们。

**关键词**：sent me a note / apologize / "fight" / townhome / complied / desperately hoped

**为什么这样写**：Firin的"desperate hope"是希望——她想知道孩子们是否安全。"complied"是她的配合——她愿意为Bregan做任何事。

---

> Bregan appeared in the doorway. He leaned against the frame in a ruggedly handsome, tortured-hero kind of way. Every troupe member inhaled at once, like a joint prayer—and it stabbed me as surely as a knife.

**中文理解**：Bregan出现在门口。他靠在门框上，以一种粗犷英俊、受难英雄的方式。每个剧团成员同时吸气，就像一次共同的祈祷——它像刀子一样刺痛了我。

**关键词**：appeared in the doorway / leaned against the frame / ruggedly handsome, tortured-hero / inhaled at once / joint prayer / stabbed me as surely as a knife

**为什么这样写**：Firin的痛苦——其他人崇拜Bregan，但她知道他的真相。"stabbed me as surely as a knife"是Firin的内心——她仍然爱他。

---

> "They're definitely in the Lockup. There are grated windows at the back of the building that lead into the Spires' western caves. I think we can get them out through there with some explosives."

**中文理解**："他们肯定在Lockup里。大楼后面有通向Spires西部洞穴的格栅窗。我想我们可以用一些炸药把他们从那里救出来。"

**关键词**：definitely in the Lockup / grated windows / Spires' western caves / explosives

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的消息——他们找到了Lockup的位置。"explosives"是计划——他们需要炸开格栅。

---

> He squeezed my upper arm. "We just need Suri." I stared at him. "Suri?" "Yes. She and her old Reformist partner, Cidd, used to get the Reformists weapons. They had connections to black-market traders out of Gwynythaid."

**中文理解**：他挤压着我的上臂。"我们只需要Suri。"我盯着他。"Suri？""是的。她和她的老改革派搭档Cidd过去常常为改革派获取武器。他们有来自Gwynythaid的黑市商人的人脉。"

**关键词**：squeezed my upper arm / just need Suri / old Reformist partner / weapons / connections / black-market traders / Gwynythaid

**为什么这样写**：Bregan的计划——他们需要Suri的帮助。"connections to black-market traders"是Suri的资源——她有武器来源。

---

> "You trust her?" a new voice asked. Asli materialized beside me out of nowhere. My hand flew to my chest. He was wearing his Guilo the Gallant illusion again, complete with the red magician's coat.

**中文理解**："你信任她？"一个新的声音问道。Asli不知从哪里出现在我身旁。我的手飞到胸口。他再次戴着Guilo the Gallant的幻觉，配上红色魔术师外套。

**关键词**：trust her / materialized beside me / out of nowhere / hand flew to my chest / Guilo the Gallant illusion / red magician's coat

**为什么这样写**：Asli的"materialized"是突然——他像魔术师一样出现。"hand flew to my chest"是Firin的反应——她被吓了一跳。

---

> Asli sauntered inside, scanning the shelves of expensive violins and flutes, but Bregan watched me. My stomach tried to climb up my throat. I forced it down and nodded. Bregan pulled out one of the rehearsal chairs. "You might want to sit down," he said. Suri did.

**中文理解**：Asli闲逛进去，扫视着昂贵的小提琴和长笛的架子，但Bregan看着我。我的胃试图爬到喉咙里。我强迫它下来并点了点头。Bregan拉出一把排练椅。"你可能想坐下，"他说。Suri坐下了。

**关键词**：sauntered inside / scanning / expensive violins and flutes / stomach tried to climb up / forced it down / rehearsal chairs / sit down

**为什么这样写**：Firin的"stomach tried to climb up"是紧张——Bregan的目光让她不安。"rehearsal chairs"是场景——音乐室。
