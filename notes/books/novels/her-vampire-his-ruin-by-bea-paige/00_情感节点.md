# Her Vampire, His Ruin 情感节点

> 10 个节点；每节点叙事概括 + 2–3 句关键引语（逐字取自 text/；说话人见括注）。

**节点一：母亲、离婚与送鞋人（ch01）**。母亲咽气当晚，Robert 用一句「抱歉时机不对」宣布离婚；两小时后 Edie 在酒店吧台用假名「Maud Buchanan」把前夫锁进箱子，也把自己交给一个送鞋回来的陌生男人。伤口与新可能出现在同一夜。

> "The first words out of my husband’s mouth were not ‘I’m sorry. Let me comfort you,’ but ‘I’m sorry if this is bad timing. I’m leaving you.’ Out of everything he’d said in that one cruelly delivered sentence, that was what hurt me the most. Not he was leaving me, but that he chose that very moment to tell me he was."（ch01）

> "“Maud Buchanan,” I lie. It’s actually Edie Valenti now that I’m back to using my maiden name, but I’m not about to tell him that."（ch01）

> "“My husband used to call me his strange little duck. When we were first together, it was an expression of endearment. I think he liked that I was different, that I wasn’t interested in his wealth or his title."（ch02）

---

**节点二：黑卡与视频通话（ch03–ch04）**。黑金名片在 UV 灯下显出荆棘缠镜、乌鸦与血滴——不是夜店门票，而是 Dorian 的视频通话。他自白「想要就夺」的权力，又露出罕见的恳求；她拆穿自己的战甲，立下「必须掌权」的宪法，合上电脑。

> "Sure enough, the light reveals some kind of emblem. It’s the image of a crow perched on an ornate mirror, droplets of blood dripping from the thick thorny vine wrapped around it. Beneath it is a row of numbers."（ch03）

> "His demand unleashes something in me, a stubbornness, yes, but beneath that, a hunger for control. I was powerless in my previous marriage. My happiness was at the mercy of a man who barely noticed me. Love made me fragile, it weakened me. I never want to go back there again. I need to be in control."（ch04）

---

**节点三：规则与「请」（ch05–ch07）**。他承认 Aegis 禁食令，她在酒吧逼他说「请」才上车；晚餐的「惩罚」让她第一次尝到被权力反噬，也让她在门外说出十五年婚姻的旧账。他承诺为她探索新关系，她第一次被放在需求之前。

> "It would also break my second rule: never feed from an Aegis. Yet here I stand, knowing if I can’t control my bloodlust, I could bite her and reveal what I am, with no way to glamour her memories."（ch05）

> "“If you want me to get into the car with you, Dorian Crow, say please.” A muscle in his jaw flickers. “Please, Edie. Would you get in the car?”"（ch06）

> "“I’m willing to explore a new dynamic. I can’t promise I won’t slip up and revert to what I’m used to, but I’m going to try for you."（ch07）

---

**节点四：血与失控（ch08）**。车内血腥气逼出他的犬齿——她在经期；他一边警告自己「别下口」，一边已经吮出淤痕。她离开后，他独自尝到一滴血，眼底炸开星系，从这一刻起他认定她属于他。

> "“Never take a bite unless you’re prepared to finish the meal,” I mutter, my eyes flicking to the pulse beating in Edie’s neck. Her skin is slightly bruised there from where I sucked on her skin earlier."（ch08）

> "I drop my gaze to my hand to find a slick trail of crimson covering my fingers. She’s menstruating."（ch08）

> "Every human carries their own distinct flavour, but none has ever come close to this. No other blood has ever painted galaxies behind my eyes. In that instant, I know Edie belongs to me, irrevocably and without question."（ch08）

---

**节点五：Basilia 的画（ch13–ch17）**。美术馆展签写着 Basilia Hallward（1702–1728），Grayhaven 庄园的捐赠；画中人与 Dorian 一模一样。第十七章他讲出婚姻史：她 18 岁嫁、他 22 岁娶、他带女人回家、她自杀——他承认自己用同样的方式毁了她，也害怕毁掉 Edie。

> "Basilia Hallward (1702-1728) Untitled, 1727. This work is one of several pieces donated to Scottsdale Gallery by the Grayhaven Estate. It is rumoured that Basilia’s short life was one filled with emotional instability until her tragic death in 1728. Little else is known about Basilia, but her work is certainly as inspiring as many of her peers during that period."（ch13）

> "I hurt Basilia in the same way. I was the cause of her pain. My actions forced her hand. My cruelty and selfishness twisted her sweetness into something bitter and vengeful. I destroyed every last bit of goodness in her. And if I don’t end this, I will destroy Edie, too."（ch17）

---

**节点六：情感抽离的判决（ch18）**。Edie 撞见 Robert 在俱乐部的包厢，也拼出 Dorian 早知情的隐瞒；Dorian 以抽离应对，她当场判他「和 Robert 一样」。Sybil 补刀「Dorian 没有爱人的能力」，她转身离开。

> "The way you just behaved, withdrawing emotionally, that’s exactly what he used to do to me. I won’t put myself through that again.”"（ch18）

> "“I’m afraid I can’t tell you, but what I will say is this; Dorian is incapable of love,” she warns, the look in her eyes softening just a little, just enough for me to see a shred of sympathy hidden within them. “I should know.”"（ch18）

---

**节点七：雨夜谈判与吸血鬼揭晓（ch21–ch22）**。他翻窗进来，被刀抵着脖子，只争一句「你没有给我同意」；她摸到激素贴，他用自己的残缺作答；她宣布掌控、逼他说「请」——然后他挣脱，说「你最好跑」。花园里他咬了她，次日她找不到伤口，却说出「我是吸血鬼」的答案。

> "“If I’m being brutally honest, I wanted to do far more than I did.” “Then why didn’t you?” she asks, her gaze flicking to the knife. “Because you didn’t give me your consent."（ch21）

> "“I’m a cursed man. A monster.” “What is that supposed to mean?” He lifts his gaze to meet mine, his eyes swimming with regret. “I’m a vampire,” he replies, before falling to the side and passing out."（ch22）

---

**节点八：绑架与银环（ch24–ch25）**。族群逼他「转化或吸干」，他两个都不选，决定绑架；他抹掉 Henrietta 的记忆，却无法 glamour Edie。囚禁中他讲出 Basilia 是他的妻子、一个女巫，并用獠牙为她穿上银环，向全族宣告她属于他。

> "Striding towards Henrietta’s home, I brace myself for what’s to come. Edie will put up a fight, but I have no choice but to do what I must. I’m not here to drain Edie, and I’m not here to change her either, despite what Logan thinks."（ch24）

> "Dorian reaches into the pocket of his jeans, pulling out a delicate silver chain, two silver loops attached to each end. “This is my gift to you, Edie. What I’m about to do isn’t just a piercing, it is a declaration to all of my clan that you are mine. Humans slip on rings, we choose intimate piercings. ”"（ch25）

---

**节点九：真爱即死、殉情与复活（ch29–ch30）**。Basilia 揭出真诅咒：一旦真爱，他就会死。Dorian 承认「我爱你」，诅咒当场拆解他的身体；Edie 喝下他的血、拒绝被 Basilia 转化，说「我爱你」后死去——她的爱又把他带了回来，也把她自己变成吸血鬼。

> "“You silly, foolish man. Within the original curse, hides another. If you ever found true love, it would kill you, permanently, just like my love for you killed me. That is the true curse. The rest is just a pretty package. Rather clever, don’t you think?”"（ch29）

> "“Now I will die with you,” she whispers. “I don’t want to be reborn as a vampire sired by a woman filled with so much hatred and cruelty.” “No,” I cry, tugging at the thread that still connects to my body, trying in vain to get back to Edie. “I love you, Dorian.” Then she takes her last breath, her body collapsing next to mine."（ch29）

> "“Is broken,” Dorian says. “Somehow, someway, Edie’s love brought me back. It brought us both back.”"（ch30）

---

**节点十：尾声：家与钩子（ch31–ch32）**。两周后，他们在没有诅咒的夜里第一次「make love」，她说爱他爱到不再是原来的自己。一个月后的家宴上，Edie 向妹妹承认「我选择成为吸血鬼，而且我死过一次」；Dorian 把她的家人认作自己的家人，最后一句却预告「几个月后我就要食言」。

> "And this time, we don’t fuck. We make love. Free from the curse that threatened to destroy us, we make love without pain, without restraint, with all the emotions a human heart can feel. And though we are vampires, that humanity lives in us still. It will live within us for all eternity from this day forward."（ch31）

> "“He’s that too. His blood brought me back to life.” “What do you mean, he brought you back to life?” Edie reaches up and cups Elizabeth’s face. “Because I died,” she whispers."（ch32）

> "“It’s been over a century since I’ve heard of a sighting. It’s no threat to you,” I say, not knowing in a few short months I’d be eating my words."（ch32）
