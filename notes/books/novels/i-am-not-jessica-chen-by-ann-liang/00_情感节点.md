# 《I Am Not Jessica Chen》情感节点

## 节点一：四点五十九分的流星与一句「我想当杰西卡」（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书的起点不是一个相遇，是一个决定。十七岁的杰娜·陈在锁着的卧室里等一封邮件，收到的是拒信；同一晚她在后院看见一颗流星，说出口的愿望是成为自己的表姐。而在这同一个夜里，杰西卡·陈也说了自己的愿望——只是没有人知道。

**关键引语：**

> ① I’ve always had this theory that if I want something badly enough, the universe will make sure to keep it just out of my reach—either out of boredom or cruelty, like an invisible hand dangling stars on a string.（ch01）

> ② I wish I was Jessica Chen.（ch01）

## 节点二：睁眼在别人的床上，第一次看见自己的胎记（ch02）

**叙事概括：** 她不是在照镜子时知道的，是在看到手指之间多出一枚硬币大小的花形胎记时知道的。这一章她花了整整一个上午说服自己那只是一场梦：梦、昏迷、颜料中毒。而这一章的最后一句，她不再确定自己还相信那句安慰。

**关键引语：**

> ① I am her.（ch02）

> ② But I’m no longer sure I believe that.（ch02）

## 节点三：第一次被人当成威胁，而这件事让她高兴（ch02）

**叙事概括：** 车上两个朋友在聊奖学金和谁更适合哪门课，顺口说了句「当然是你赢啦」。杰娜意识到自己从来没有人当成过对手——而被人当成对手的感觉，好得不应该。全书她第一次发现，嫉妒之外还有另一种匮乏。

**关键引语：**

> ① “I didn’t even realize they gave out full scholarships before Jessica,” Celine says, in a tone caught between admiration and envy, her smile sharp as cut glass. Nobody’s ever spoken to me like this before. Nobody’s ever looked at me as a threat. It feels better than it should. Then she adds, “Guess they make exceptions for the best.”（ch02）

## 节点四：被要求为帝国主义辩护，而辩题就是她的这一生（ch07）

**叙事概括：** 午休的模拟辩论抽到一个辩题：帝国主义是向弱国传播知识与技术的正当手段。她坐在杰西卡那一侧，必须为一套她痛恨了一辈子的世界观找论据，而她一开口对方就说了一句她整晚没忘的话——他们俩都这样，这最难受。

**关键引语：**

> ① But here I am, trying to verbalize my own pain, to justify my own existence, breaking it down into digestible points. Every word comes out a double-edged knife. This isn’t just a debate for me. This is my history, my life.（ch07）

> ② “It’s debating, you know?” he continues. “You can argue things you don’t believe in. That’s what makes a good debater.”（ch07）

## 节点五：螃蟹、疹子，和一句「你不为不完美反省吗」（ch08）

**叙事概括：** 姨妈家饭桌上，政治测验的九十一分被追问；她只顾着躲那封不祥的纸条，吃了一口蟹肉，三分钟后满脸起疹子。她在那一章才第一次真正明白：换来的不只是一个名字，是一整套没人告诉过她的规矩。而当晚饭后她拨通 Aaron 的电话，才知道那天是他母亲去世的周年。

**关键引语：**

> ① “I don’t care about the score,” Auntie says, shaking him away. “I care about how Jessica is reacting to the score. She doesn’t even look upset. How can she improve if she’s not reflecting on what she’s done?”（ch08）

> ② It’s his mother’s death anniversary.（ch08）

## 节点六：第一次被认出——「我太了解你了」（ch11）

**叙事概括：** 骑马摔下来之后，Aaron 送她走两英里回家。暮色、雾气、虫声，两个人聊童年和理想。走到半路他停下来问：是你吧。这一次她没有否认——这是全书她第一次不演了。而她第一次允许自己说想养一只哈士奇、想在乡下种草莓。

**关键引语：**

> ① “I couldn’t stop thinking about it,” he says, walking ahead again, and I make myself catch up. “After that day at the lake . . . I wanted to assume you were joking, but then—it wasn’t logical. Why would you joke about that? It would require a truly terrible, warped sense of humor. And then everything started to make sense. I started to comb through all my memories from before I left for Paris and comparing them to the after. I thought at first it was because I’d been gone for so long. People can change, right? But people can’t transform so drastically. I couldn’t sleep,” he continues, shaking his head. “I just kept replaying our conversations, searching for the differences. Of course, there’s also the fact that I haven’t seen you . . . Jenna . . . I mean, the person you were—” He breaks off. “God, this is absolutely bizarre.”（ch11）

> ② I grimace. Kick at the grass beneath my feet. “If this is a nightmare, we’re dreaming the same dream. You know what? Maybe that’s what it means,” I say, recalling the proverb slowly. “To dream of becoming a butterfly. I didn’t understand it when we were studying it in Chinese school, but I think I do now. Maybe it’s impossible to tell which is the dream and which is reality.”（ch11）

## 节点七：七年的沉默被一次性说完（ch13）

**叙事概括：** 他说他早就喜欢她，从她还是个小女孩的时候；他走，是因为受不了。而他给她的答案比任何表白都更狠——你不是不够好，你是把所有人都想得比实际更好，把自己想得比实际更差，于是我对你来说只是一个够不到的目标。

**关键引语：**

> ① You think everyone’s so much better than they really are, and you think you’re so much worse than you really are.（ch13）

> ② “If I’d kissed you,” he goes on, “you would have wanted me for an afternoon, and I would have wanted you for the rest of my life. But even though I knew it wouldn’t work, I also knew that if I stayed, I wouldn’t be able to stop myself from kissing you anyway.” The bitter crack of a smile. “I only have so much self-control.”（ch13）

## 节点八：勒索者摘下面具，而她说「想要和痛一直分不清」（ch14）

**叙事概括：** 图书馆的小隔间里，匿名者原来是 Cathy Liu——而她要杰娜坦白的罪，是杰西卡抄了她的论文。杰娜准备了十二章来应对最坏的消息，消息轻得像一张纸；让她双腿发软的是对方随后那几句：对我来说，想要和痛一直分不清。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Did you think I wouldn’t recognize my own thesis?（ch14）

> ② Because to me, wanting has always been indistinguishable from pain.（ch14）

> ③ I always had a clear view of you; you were always first, and I was always second.（ch14）

## 节点九：她第一次说「我不想换回去」，而他没有生气（ch15）

**叙事概括：** 他说他们想通了怎么撤销——闭上眼，再许一次愿，和第一次一样。她许了，什么也没发生。然后他问她：你其实并不想换回去，对吧。这一章是她十六章演技的终点：她承认自己贪恋那副身体、贪恋被认可，而她最羞于承认的是第三件。

**关键引语：**

> ① I can’t deny it, because it’s true.（ch15）

> ② “Of course you don’t understand,” I tell him. “But you know, someone like Cathy Liu would. Can you believe that?” My laughter tastes bitter on my tongue. “I have so much in common with the girl who’s been blackmailing my own cousin.”（ch15）

## 节点十：日记最后一页，和一句与第一章对称的愿望（ch16）

**叙事概括：** 美术馆上她告诉 Aaron 艺术不是有用的技能，而他只承诺了一件事：永远不把她的画撤下来。当晚她读到日记的最后几页——署名的不是她，是那个她羡慕了十六年的人：什么都很重，我只求有人替我过完这一生，我不想再当杰西卡·陈了。

**关键引语：**

> ① “I would never move on,” Aaron says softly. “I would never take your paintings down.”（ch16）

> ② Everything gets so heavy. I wish somebody else would just come and take over my life. Live it for me. Please. If the universe is listening; if the stars could grant me any impossible wish, then all I ask for is this:（ch16）

## 节点十一：他眼里的雾清了半秒，然后被抹掉（ch18）

**叙事概括：** 她从颁奖现场逃到校门口，被 Aaron 叫住。她试着解释、试着撤销那个愿望、问「你觉得我是谁」，而他答得理所当然：你当然是杰西卡·陈。她说「你不能忘」——她要回来的不是这个人，是别人脑子里的自己。

**关键引语：**

> ① I’m already at the gates when somebody calls me by the wrong name.（ch18）

> ② “You’re Jessica Chen, of course.”（ch18）

> ③ “Aaron, please,” I choke out. I really am begging him now, desperate. Dread threatens to strangle me. “You can’t do this. You can’t forget.”（ch18）

## 节点十二：山顶的清单——「其实什么都有，只是当时不知道」（ch19）

**叙事概括：** 全书最短也最重的一章。她抱着自画像爬上去，画不出自己的脸，等到流星，一口气把想念的东西全说出来：房间、花园、阳台上的星星、妈妈喊她下楼吃饭。而清单上没有一样是成就——全是拿不走的。光消失的时候，她对着天空说了句「我懂了」，而天空没有回答。

**关键引语：**

> ① My fingers—Jessica’s fingers—still won’t obey me.（ch19）

> ② I miss my room, our garden that never grew anything but was still lovely to gaze at, the view of the stars from the balcony.（ch19）

> ③ I miss it all. I miss my life, because even when I felt like I had nothing, I had everything. I just didn’t know it at the time. You never do, until it’s in hindsight.（ch19）

## 节点十三：醒来，手指上有茧——「我又存在了」（ch20）

**叙事概括：** 她检查自己的手，掌心有二十年画画留下的茧。而杰西卡告诉她一件把全书焊死的事：那也是我的愿望。两个人都不是被随机交换的，她们都想要对方身上自己最想脱掉的那部分。

**关键引语：**

> ① I exist again.（ch20）

> ② “It was my wish too,” she says.（ch20）

> ③ “I don’t think so,” I say slowly. “You know, I used to have this theory that if I wanted something badly enough, the universe would make sure to keep it just out of my reach. Like a cruel joke, or a trick. But . . . maybe the cruelest trick the universe can play on us is to give us exactly what we wish for.”（ch20）

## 节点十四：画室里那张不一样的自画像，和「Hi, Mom」（ch21）

**叙事概括：** 她回到自己的画桌前，画了一张新的自画像——这次画的是她身后的某个人，而且她在笑。而后她又在姨妈家条件反射地喊了一声「妈」，立刻后悔。全书最后一句是：除了眼前这一切，她什么都不想要了。

**关键引语：**

> ① But this self-portrait is different.（ch21）

> ② We bring braised beef this time, and a bag of fresh mangoes. When the door opens, I smile up at my aunt and greet her without thinking, “Hi, Mom.” Then immediately wince at my mistake. Some habits really are too easy to form.（ch21）

> ③ But right now, there’s nothing else I’d wish for except this.（ch21）
