# 金句精选

> She will never be beautiful.
> One day, this will be her ruin.
> — Gaia 的诅咒（ch03）

---

> my father crafted a hurricane
> and blessed it with my name.
> — Oceanus 以风暴为 Styx 命名（ch03）

---

> I defied this prophecy.
> In the end, I defied everything
> my family expected of me.
> — Styx 的反抗宣言（ch03）

---

> But power is a slow poison.
> — 全章核心论点（ch04）

---

> In his quest for solitary dominance,
> Ouranos committed a terrible outrage
> against Gaia and her children.
> — Ouranos 的背叛（ch04）

---

> Kronos, the God of Time,
> usurped his father's throne
> to teach him a lesson.
> — 讽刺性的"正义"（ch04）

---

> Still, Gaia wept for her consort.
> She wept for what was lost
> inside the mouth of vengeance.
> — 复仇之口的意象（ch04）

---

> It was grief which led her
> to speak her words about me.
> Grief that curdled
> her premonition
> into my curse.
> — 诅咒的真正来源（ch04）

---

> 'Styx is loved by many,'
> Coeus told him, 'but no one
> needs to be loved by all.'
> — 生存哲学（ch05）

---

> A child is no place to put misspent grief.
> — Oceanus 对 Gaia 行为的定性（ch05）

---

> I would like to think he wanted to protect me.
> I would like to think that he did not know
> that some promises are an undoing
> in and of themselves.
> That they are not meant for even Gods to keep.
> — 关于承诺的警示（ch05）

---

> I wish I could say I missed company
> but you do not miss
> what you do not know.
> — 孤独的哲学性接受（ch06）

---

> It was only when they met
> their nieces and nephews
> that they realized
> they had made
> a terrible mistake
> with me.
> — 全章最震撼的反转（ch06）

---

> The children of Gods
> were meant to be Gods.
> This law was established
> by Kronos in the infancy of his rule,
> a reign that was born
> after my own birth.
> — 制度性排斥的根源（ch07）

---

> I had received no such blessing,
> so the name for what I was did not yet exist.
> — 身份危机的核心陈述（ch07）

---

> Her hand left mine in that moment.
> It seems so strange
> to think back to it now.
> The way her hand left mine.
> The way she never held it
> with that kind of love again.
> — 命名仪式上的情感断裂（ch08）

---

> When you name something,
> are you naming it because you love it?
> Or are you naming it to build it a golden cage
> of your understanding?
> — 命名行为的哲学质疑（ch09）

---

> Names can do that.
> They can become a drowning.
> They can grow around your wrists
> and shackle you to their meaning.
> — 名字作为束缚的意象（ch09）

---

> I did not want to be an Oceanid,
> one of countless sisters.
> I wanted to be like my parents.
> I wanted powers of my very own.
> — Styx 的核心渴望（ch09）

---

> Although my father was the oldest
> of all the Titan Gods,
> it was Kronos,
> the youngest of the Titan brothers,
> who had overthrown Ouranos.
> — 年龄与权力的倒置（ch10）

---

> But the prophecy from Ouranos
> lingers long on Kronos's mind and crown.
> — 预言笼罩王权（ch10）

---

> A question wormed its way into my mind.
> What prophecy were they talking about?
> — 悬念结尾（ch10）
