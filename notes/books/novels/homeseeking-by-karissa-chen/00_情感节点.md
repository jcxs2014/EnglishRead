# 《Homeseeking》情感节点

## 节点一：一整条弄堂醒来（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书从一个梦开始：十六岁的苏祖贞在后窗哭，海承威从街上回家，两人都没有说出对方等的那句话。叙述者同时提前告诉我们，这个男人此后一生都在翻检这个地方的记忆——这是全书唯一一次泄露结局，而泄露的不是事件，是一种方法。

**关键引语：**

> ① In the last violet minutes of the disappearing night, the longtang wakes.（ch01）

> ② For years afterward, he will riffle through his memories of this place he considers home, layering them on top of one another like stacks of rice paper, trying to remember what was when and never quite seeing the full picture.（ch01）

> ③ But for now: she can feel the warm heft of Haiwen’s presence encircling hers, the tender touch of his hand cupping her face, and she believes he has forgiven her. Her body unclenches. Right before a deep, untroubled sleep claims her, she hears his voice in her ear, kind, reassuring. Soon, he promises her, the plum rains are almost here.（ch01）

## 节点二：逃难的人和孩子（ch03）

**叙事概括：** 一九三八年，七岁的 Suji 就住在这条弄堂里；母亲是乡下人，有一双小脚，父亲 Li'oe 在商务印书馆做事，家里把一间屋子改成书店。这一章把「家」从房子换成了三个人讲道理的方式，也顺手把全书的政治问题第一次摆上桌。

**关键引语：**

> ① There’s a difference between something that is old-fashioned and something that belongs to your heritage. An instrument like the guzheng is timeless; what makes it old-fashioned or contemporary is how you use it.（ch03）

> ② Suji cracked the tin open. He had eaten everything.（ch03）

> ③ Who will teach you how to live in this world if not for me, daughters?（ch03）

## 节点三：被战争改写的声音（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 一九四五年上海，父亲当众斥责一首日伪宣传曲，苏祖贞在雨里第一次看清自己爱的人长什么样；同一个四月，先低头的是苏祖贞。全章最重的一次不是这个吻，而是一个人决定把恐惧变成背景音——那是他后来在战场上活下来的方法。

**关键引语：**

> ① Love crashed upon her in a pastel splash. Sulan was right, Suchi thought as they trudged home through the rain together. She loved his stupid hair. She loved his tedious aloofness. She loved the mystery of the music she couldn’t hear behind his eyes, even more than the music that came from his fingers. She loved him, and she felt dumb that Sulan had known before her.（ch05）

> ② Think, daughter, think! Why would this government want all of you youngsters to learn this song that glorifies this idea of a Japanese-controlled Asian state? Why do you think this song is all over the radio, presented by popular celebrities? That precious Bai Guang of yours is nothing more than a Japanese-loving traitor, using that pretty voice of hers for Chen Gongbo’s propaganda so she can become rich and famous!（ch05）

> ③ Haiwen turned, his eyes narrow and stormy. That was when she kissed him.（ch05）

## 节点四：一九四七年，两条线画成（ch07 + ch18）

**叙事概括：** 同一年里有两件事同时发生：十六岁的海承威在哥哥身后决定「如果非得有一个人去，那应该是他」；十六岁的苏祖贞在黄浦江边跟他说政治立场，然后说「我们结婚吧」。他答不上来的那一道题和他说出口的拒绝，是同一个动作的两面。

**关键引语：**

> ① “You can be so much more than what you allow yourself to be,” Apa said, his voice returning to an even volume. “I don’t understand why you hold yourself back. I don’t know where I’ve failed. But I want you to be more than another pretty face.” He looked at Sulan. “Both of you. Do you understand?”（ch07）

> ② But he shook his head and looked at her sadly. “No,” he said, “I don’t. Which is why I can’t marry you.”（ch07）

> ③ If someone had to go, it should be him.（ch18）

## 节点五：九龙站没有人来（ch09）

**叙事概括：** 一九四八年逃港那一场戏的残酷不在路上，在终点：十七岁的苏祖贞在九龙站等一个从来没出现过的人。三年后姐妹俩算开销、把余下的钱寄回上海，母亲的回信仍没到；后来房东收回寄宿屋，姐妹俩哭着收拾行李——那封信被藏进琴盒夹层，藏在全书写得最不吉利的位置上。

**关键引语：**

> ① “My daughters,” he shouted. “Don’t forget to eat!”（ch09）

> ② “The only thing that separates us from those people in the shantytown is luck. It’s luck we found this boardinghouse, it’s luck I found a job. It’s luck the money and jewelry we had weren’t stolen or discovered. It’s luck we left Shanghai before the Communists won the war. It’s all luck! All of it!”（ch09）

> ③ Mrs. Chan’s landlord gave the sisters one week to move out. They wept as they packed their things. “This is all my fault,” Sulan murmured. Suchi hid Siau Zi’s letter inside a small tear in the lining of Haiwen’s violin case. She promised herself she would tell her sister about it eventually, once they were in a better place.（ch09）

## 节点六：基隆的一枚硬币（ch16）

**叙事概括：** 一九五九年，李潜游不回大陆，海承威在竹石小屋里数着他与上海之间的六百八十公里。全章没有一句抒情，只有一个老兵在基隆街边掷骰子的酒摊前用一句「宁可错杀一千个无辜人」讲完这几十年的荒谬；而他自己在跟蚵仔煎摊主的女儿说话时，忽然发现自己有一会儿没想起难过。

**关键引语：**

> ① Hope and despair were two sides of the same coin, Haiwen now thought. Li Tsin, like Haiwen, hadn’t given up hope of returning home. It was what got the boy through the bullying, the harsh work he was ordered to do, the beatings from officers. He had nothing else—because he was a forced conscript, the military never fully trusted him, and he was stuck at the bottom of the totem pole.（ch16）

> ② Haiwen tried to picture the map he had stashed somewhere in his hut, one on which he had taken a ruler and drawn a straight line from Keelung to Shanghai. Six hundred and eighty kilometers, he had calculated.（ch16）

> ③ Haiwen rode toward his house in the dark, turning the conversation over in his mind. Briefly, while he’d been talking to Tsai Linyee, he’d forgotten his grief, and this alarmed him. What if it leaked out of his brain like the music?（ch16）

## 节点七：名字与那七天（ch13 + ch14）

**叙事概括：** 一九六六年三月，姐姐终于拿到「多发性硬化症」这个名字；同一个月，苏祖贞在天星小轮上遇见一个背影，以为是鬼。他们只有七天：茶楼、粥店、面店、一张床、四个清晨。他到最后才说出自己已婚，她到这时才问；最后他把手伸进琴盒夹层找一枚戒指，摸空了；而她进门时喊的那声「王海承威」，他其实一直听得见。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Being a self-made man means I get to marry whomever I want!”（ch13）

> ② “I knew it,” Sulan whispered, “I knew I wasn’t crazy. I knew I wasn’t making it up. The monster. It finally has a name.”（ch13）

> ③ She felt the same in his arms. Her head still met him right at the armpit, her ribs still felt thin enough he was afraid they might break if he held her too tightly. Even the light touch of her fingers on his spine felt the same. The only thing different was the floral perfume she now wore, and even then, he was certain if he buried his head deeply enough, he would still find her sweet, salty, powdery scent.（ch14）

## 节点八：台北，一句不是我的将军（ch12）

**叙事概括：** 一九七五年蒋介石去世，三个退伍老兵把「回家」吵散了：一个说自己无所谓，一个说自己只想自杀，一个摔门而去。回到家，台湾妻子只问他饿不饿。这一章的最低点是他一个人守着电视上的葬礼重播，哭的不是蒋介石，是这辈子丢掉的每一样东西；小女儿后来抱着那只旧琴盒来哄他。

**关键引语：**

> ① “I envy Li Tsin, I really do,” Zenpo said. Haiwen realized he was crying. “He was the smartest one of us all. I’m a sniveling coward compared to him.”（ch12）

> ② She didn’t immediately respond. Finally she said, almost in a whisper. “He’s not my general.”（ch12）

> ③ She was such a good girl, his younger daughter. So sensitive, so caring. But what she could never understand, what her sister and Linyee could never understand, was this complicated grief. He didn’t give a shit about Chiang Kai-shek. It was true, everything Linyee had said. It was because of President Chiang he had been forced to join the army, because of President Chiang he had seen his friends’ brains blown out, because of President Chiang he’d shot and killed his fellow countrymen. It was because of President Chiang they had lost a winning war and retreated to this jungle. It was because of President Chiang he was separated from his family, from Suchi, forbidden to reach out to them. And yes, it was because of President Chiang so many locals hated him, because of President Chiang so many innocents had suffered and died for simply trying to live a just and fair existence.（ch12）

## 节点九：纽约，雪和一枚戒指（ch10）

**叙事概括：** 一九八一年霍华德去下城的设计公司开会，在暴风雪里终于问出那一句「她也住在纽约吗」。这一章把全书最重的一枚戒指交出来：修琴的师傅在琴盒夹层的裂缝里摸到一枚金戒指，妻子把这件事带回家说给他听；他自己认出来——那是母亲临走前塞给他的，本就要转交苏祖贞。同一天，姐姐苏兰追到旅馆，把香港的号码塞给他；他回家把信原样锁进抽屉，一个字也没拆。

**关键引语：**

> ① He didn’t want to dwell on that now. He was inside, warm, safe. From the haven of the taxi, he could appreciate the beauty of the snowflake, its miniature geometry. Like much in this world, what was beauty and what was torment depended upon the context.（ch10）

> ② She stopped rubbing and looked up at him. “She’s never forgotten you. I know she hasn’t. She never says anything, she never talks about the past, but I know you live somewhere in her secret heart.”（ch10）

> ③ Howard ripped the envelope into fourths, then into progressively smaller pieces. When the shreds were too numerous for him to continue, he cupped them and carried them to the toilet. He watched them swirl away. He would appreciate what was in front of him. His children. His wife.（ch10）

## 节点十：祖母签下另一张机票（ch17）

**叙事概括：** 一九八五年香港到纽约，母亲替儿子挨打、被儿子吼「你为什么这么爱当烈士」，却在凌晨四点改签了机票，因为儿子皱眉的样子像极了她父亲。走之前她交代佣人别告诉丈夫——这是全书唯一一个女性主动抛下生活而不是被生活抛下的动作。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Yeah, when I was like eleven. Those rides are all for little kids. And the dolphins all look miserable. You know that’s animal cruelty? Katie says they get depressed and try to drown themselves if they’re caged for too long.”（ch17）

> ② “And do what?” Soukei asked. “Without him, I have nothing. Nothing I have is my own. My clothes, this apartment, every nice thing I have—it all belongs to Saikeung. I am a useless fifty-four-year-old woman. It’s too late for me to start over.”（ch17）

> ③ At four in the morning, Soukei realized why it had unnerved her so—Samson had looked just like her father.（ch17）

## 节点十一：回上海，找不到那面墙（ch08）

**叙事概括：** 一九九三年霍华德回沪探亲，弄堂变成了公寓，没有人知道那户姓张的人家。饭桌上他问出那句关于共产主义的问题，饭后弟弟掀开旧事：那封检举书是姐姐自己写、自己签的，还把当兵的哥哥一起告了。这一章把「谁在什么时候出卖了我们」这个问题交给下一代，而下一代选择把它说出口。

**关键引语：**

> ① But Howard wasn’t listening anymore. He was peering down the lane, toward the direction of Suchi’s shikumen. He thought of the many times he had been down that path, to walk her home or meet her before school or tell her a bit of news. He suddenly recalled the day after their first kiss, how nervous he’d been as he’d walked toward her house, how his heart had thrummed against his lungs. He’d been sure the kiss had been a dream, that he’d imagined the slight chap of her lips, the soft tease of her tongue, that when she opened the door, she’d act as if nothing had happened. But when she stepped through the frame, soft and shy and radiant in a blush-colored skirt he’d never seen before, he knew instantly things between them would never be the same, that he’d never want them to be the same again.（ch08）

> ② “How does this fall in line with the idea of Communism?”（ch08）

> ③ “It’s true. She wrote a denouncement, signed it herself. Labeling Ba as a bourgeois, capitalist, foreign-loving dog. We’re lucky they dragged only Ba off. She denounced you, too, saying she had severed ties with her traitorous Nationalist brother, though she’ll never tell you that.”（ch08）

## 节点十二：洛杉矶，两代人用同一句话和解（ch19）

**叙事概括：** 二〇〇八年的洛杉矶，苏祖贞几次拿起电话要给海承威打，又都没打，直到听说自己母亲还活着；餐馆里她先认错，他也认错。整章的关键不是和解本身，而是她讲出那段雨伞记忆：六十几年前他雨中追上来把伞塞给她，她在窗口看见一排伸向无穷的伞。八十年后，是她把它还给他。

**关键引语：**

> ① “I mean,” Howard struggled to explain, “we don’t talk about anything that matters.”（ch06）

> ② “I think it’s harder to be brave when you’re young and have little control over anything,” Momo said.（ch19）

> ③ “I thought to myself, This is someone who will do this for me for the rest of our lives. And I could see all the umbrellas you would bring me, a line of them stretching into infinity, the skin of your palm on their handles becoming more creased over time, your hair turning thinner and grayer.”（ch19）

## 节点十三：一九八二年，六月，石库门（ch20）

**叙事概括：** 全书结束在一九八二年，比第二条线的开始还早二十六年。母亲李玉萍躺在住了陌生人的石库门里，幻觉把四个名字一个一个变回两个孩子。她把当年在当铺看见、觉得正合女儿心意的那枚金戒指按进唯一旧友洗玉英的掌心，托她带话「这是你女儿的」；又托人把一封由她自己折好、亲手贴上邮票的信带给海承威；最后她糊涂了，把朋友的安慰听成儿子的琴声。

**关键引语：**

> ① Come home. We are waiting. Nothing is your fault, if anything it’s our fault, for not having protected you, for not having held you, for missing your entire life. Come home.（ch20）

> ② But that can’t be possible, because the letter appears before her, written in beautiful calligraphy, careful neat characters in perfect columns down the page. She watches her own hands fold the letter, inserting it in the envelope. She puts the stamps on herself.（ch20）

> ③ But what is Sieu’in talking about? He is almost here. She can feel it. She can hear the long, plaintive note of a violin, and it is growing louder.（ch20）
