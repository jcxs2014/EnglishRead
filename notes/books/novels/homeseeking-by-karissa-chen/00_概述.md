# 《Homeseeking》概述

> **关于姓名的一行说明**：这本书几乎不用汉字，名字一律用拼音或方言罗马化拼出，章节标题也因此不用人名（全书唯一一处汉字是第 11 章夜总会的招牌「百樂門」，英文写作 Gate of a Hundred Delights）。本笔记为了阅读方便起了中文读法（苏祖贞、海承威、李玉萍、林耀顺等），这些读法**不是原文**，凡涉及名字的地方以原文写法为准。

## 全书梗概

**先说这本书怎么排的。** 两条线交替出现：一条从 1938 年的上海走到 2008 年的洛杉矶，写祖母李玉萍的儿子海承威；另一条固定在 2008 年 4 月到 6 月的洛杉矶，写孙女安妮和从没有见过面的婆家人。同一个年份会在两章里各写一次，一次从上往下看，一次从下往上看，直到最后它们在同一个人身上合上。

**1938 年，七岁的 Suji 就住在这条弄堂里。** 母亲是乡下人，有一双小脚，同学因此在背后议论她；父亲 Li'oe 在商务印书馆做事，家里把一间屋子改成书店。这个家后来的一切——两个女儿、一间后门、一只琴盒——都是从这一间开始的。There’s a difference between something that is old-fashioned and something that belongs to your heritage. An instrument like the guzheng is timeless; what makes it old-fashioned or contemporary is how you use it.（ch03）

- **1947 年 4 月的某个夜里，弄堂醒来。** 十六岁的苏祖贞在后窗哭，海承威从街上回家，谁都没有说话；同一个春天，她哥哥被强征入伍，父亲因为一位当编辑的朋友被从工厂里抓去审问、母亲当天就把他捞了回来；她提出结婚，他拒绝了。In the last violet minutes of the disappearing night, the longtang wakes.（ch01）
- But he shook his head and looked at her sadly. “No,” he said, “I don’t. Which is why I can’t marry you.”（ch07）

**同一年，战争把家切成两半。** 他自己在那张征兵纸上签了名，她押上共产党，两人在政治上分了家。这场分裂不是后来才有的裂痕，是从一开始就画好的线：If someone had to go, it should be him.（ch18）

**1948 年，十七岁的苏祖贞和姐姐在九龙站下车，没有人来接。** 临走前母亲把首饰缝进她的衣裳，父亲在站台上朝她们喊了一句「别忘了吃饭」；三年后一场棚屋大火把陈家母女烧没了，房东又给了姐妹俩一周时间收拾行李：“My daughters,” he shouted. “Don’t forget to eat!”（ch09）

**1959 年到 1966 年，两份人生隔着一道海峡各自往下走。** 海承威在基隆当兵，娶了台湾的 Tsai Linyee；苏祖贞在香港的百乐门唱歌，姐姐得了多发性硬化症。1966 年初姐姐终于拿到那个名字，同一个春天两个人在天星小轮上重逢：
- “I knew it,” Sulan whispered, “I knew I wasn’t crazy. I knew I wasn’t making it up. The monster. It finally has a name.”（ch13）
- “Wang Haiwen?” The voice, a woman’s, calling his name in halting Mandarin.（ch14）

**1972 年到 1982 年，各自有各自的代价。** 苏祖贞在 1966 年 5 月嫁给林耀顺，儿子在 1972 年正是五岁；海承威留在台北，蒋介石去世那几天他连着几晚守在电视前看重播，看着那些排起长队的人群——他自己在队伍里认出了同袍 Zenpo。1982 年 6 月，母亲李玉萍躺在上海的石库门里，用一封托人带回给儿子的信把话说完：Come home. We are waiting. Nothing is your fault, if anything it’s our fault, for not having protected you, for not having held you, for missing your entire life. Come home.（ch20）

**1985 年，祖母 Soukei 五十四岁，还住在香港，儿子山孙就在她身边。** 她第一次算清楚：这一辈子没有一件东西是自己的：“And do what?” Soukei asked. “Without him, I have nothing. Nothing I have is my own. My clothes, this apartment, every nice thing I have—it all belongs to Saikeung. I am a useless fifty-four-year-old woman. It’s too late for me to start over.”（ch17）

**1993 年，儿子回上海探亲。** 他站在已经变成公寓的弄堂口，再也找不到那面掉漆的兔子墙；饭桌上他问「这跟共产主义怎么对得上」，然后才知道母亲当年自己写了一份揭发书、亲笔签名，把父亲说成走资派：“It’s true. She wrote a denouncement, signed it herself. Labeling Ba as a bourgeois, capitalist, foreign-loving dog. We’re lucky they dragged only Ba off. She denounced you, too, saying she had severed ties with her traitorous Nationalist brother, though she’ll never tell you that.”（ch08）

**2008 年 4 月到 6 月，两条线在洛杉矶合上。** 安妮找上了婆家，苏祖贞在餐馆里对六十一年没见的海承威认错，海承威隔着太平洋讲出一段她从来不知道的雨伞记忆。到最后苏祖贞坐上了飞往上海的飞机，讲起飞走之后母亲在石库门外年年点着的那一盏红灯笼：“I was thinking about my mother,” she said quietly. “A few months after you joined the army, she began lighting a red paper lantern every evening and hanging it in front of the shikumen without explanation. After a week, I asked her why she did this and she told me, It’s what mothers used to do back in my old village. For the boys who went to fight the Japanese. They didn’t know if or when they’d be back, but this way, if they were on the road late at night, they could find their way home.”（ch19）

## 三个主题

**一、家不是一个地方，是一个反复练习的动作。** 全书没有一个角色真正「回到」过家：
- Hope and despair were two sides of the same coin, Haiwen now thought. Li Tsin, like Haiwen, hadn’t given up hope of returning home. It was what got the boy through the bullying, the harsh work he was ordered to do, the beatings from officers. He had nothing else—because he was a forced conscript, the military never fully trusted him, and he was stuck at the bottom of the totem pole.（ch16）
- 而让家散掉的从来不是距离，是不谈——他们会谈童年、会谈邻居、会谈天气，就是不碰那件事：“I mean,” Howard struggled to explain, “we don’t talk about anything that matters.”（ch06）

**二、话在母女之间传不下去。** 母亲替孩子做的那个决定，长成了女儿第一个要驳的东西：
- “The only thing that separates us from those people in the shantytown is luck. It’s luck we found this boardinghouse, it’s luck I found a job. It’s luck the money and jewelry we had weren’t stolen or discovered. It’s luck we left Shanghai before the Communists won the war. It’s all luck! All of it!”（ch09）
- 同一个「不」她也对着一整个男人的世代说过：She didn’t immediately respond. Finally she said, almost in a whisper. “He’s not my general.”（ch12）
- 到最后，连说出「勇敢更难」这句的，都不是她的女儿，而是住在同一个屋檐下的朋友：“I think it’s harder to be brave when you’re young and have little control over anything,” Momo said.（ch19）

**三、琴盒是全书的第二个主角。** 它在第 01 章被留在后门口，The five plain back doors until the painted bunny comes into view, its flaked white outline wringing a pang in Haiwen’s chest. He will leave his violin here: he sees himself setting it down, laying it against the chipped paint as tenderly as he imagines a mother abandons a beloved baby.（ch01）在第 10 章的纽约夹层里让人摸到一枚戒指，同一章他还把姐姐转交的旧信撕成越来越小的碎片冲进马桶，Howard ripped the envelope into fourths, then into progressively smaller pieces. When the shreds were too numerous for him to continue, he cupped them and carried them to the toilet. He watched them swirl away. He would appreciate what was in front of him. His children. His wife.（ch10）又在第 20 章变成一段越来越响的声音：But what is Sieu’in talking about? He is almost here. She can feel it. She can hear the long, plaintive note of a violin, and it is growing louder.（ch20）

## 人物弧光

**海承威**——

- 起点：But he shook his head and looked at her sadly. “No,” he said, “I don’t. Which is why I can’t marry you.”（ch07）
- 最低点：Haiwen rode toward his house in the dark, turning the conversation over in his mind. Briefly, while he’d been talking to Tsai Linyee, he’d forgotten his grief, and this alarmed him. What if it leaked out of his brain like the music?（ch16）
- 落点：“I thought to myself, This is someone who will do this for me for the rest of our lives. And I could see all the umbrellas you would bring me, a line of them stretching into infinity, the skin of your palm on their handles becoming more creased over time, your hair turning thinner and grayer.”（ch19）
- 他一生做的每一个决定都在证明那一句政治理由是错的，却一直没有人告诉他。

**苏祖贞**——

- 起点：Love crashed upon her in a pastel splash. Sulan was right, Suchi thought as they trudged home through the rain together. She loved his stupid hair. She loved his tedious aloofness. She loved the mystery of the music she couldn’t hear behind his eyes, even more than the music that came from his fingers. She loved him, and she felt dumb that Sulan had known before her.（ch05）
- 转向：“Being a self-made man means I get to marry whomever I want!”（ch13）
- 落点：“I was thinking about my mother,” she said quietly. “A few months after you joined the army, she began lighting a red paper lantern every evening and hanging it in front of the shikumen without explanation. After a week, I asked her why she did this and she told me, It’s what mothers used to do back in my old village. For the boys who went to fight the Japanese. They didn’t know if or when they’d be back, but this way, if they were on the road late at night, they could find their way home.”（ch19）
- 她的转折不是离开谁，而是终于肯把当年的事说出来；说完之后她仍然是那个「你认识的那个苏祖贞已经蒸发了几十年」的人。

**李玉萍 / 苏家两代母亲**——

- 起点：“My daughters,” he shouted. “Don’t forget to eat!”（ch09）
- 落点：Come home. We are waiting. Nothing is your fault, if anything it’s our fault, for not having protected you, for not having held you, for missing your entire life. Come home.（ch20）
- 这一对母亲一生都在做同一个动作：把东西缝进女儿的衣裳、把名字留给自己等的人，而她们自己一件也没留下。

**祖母 Soukei**——

- 起点：“Yeah, when I was like eleven. Those rides are all for little kids. And the dolphins all look miserable. You know that’s animal cruelty? Katie says they get depressed and try to drown themselves if they’re caged for too long.”（ch17）
- 落点：At four in the morning, Soukei realized why it had unnerved her so—Samson had looked just like her father.（ch17）
- 她是全书唯一一个自己拿主意改主意的人——一九八五年，她把回香港的班机改签成去纽约，让儿子先进大学，自己在美国多留一阵。
