# 《Beg, Borrow, or Steal》概述

## 书籍信息

- **作者**：Sarah Adams
- **出版**：Dell（Random House Publishing Group），2025-01-07；电子版 ISBN 9780593723708
- **体裁**：长篇成人言情（rom-com），第一人称双 POV
- **篇幅**：作者序 + 正文 37 章 + 尾声，共 39 件；正文 POV 分布为 Emily 23 章 / Jack 14 章
- **结构特点**：Emily 与 Jackson Bennett 交替出场；正文之间夹有多封两人十年前、十三年前的往来邮件（Email interlude），以及 Jack 与母亲、与教师同事的短信往来
- **开篇与书名的回声**：作者序之后附上全书最早的一组邮件——她写信让即将搬走的 Jack 别忘了把休息室里的咖啡豆搬走，他四分钟后回信让她想哭就哭；书名在 ch31 有一次字面回声，Madison 打趣她是否以为 Jack 偷走了她的心，她答他大概只是借走了。

## 全书梗概

**一桩在发廊里被听说取消的婚礼，和一个搬来的邻居。** 肯塔基州罗马镇的小学二年级教师 Emily Walker 每周去发廊做头发，那天从两个邻居的闲话里听说一件与自己无关的事：她讨厌了十年的同事 Jack Bennett 原本今天结婚，婚礼却在他搬走几个月后取消了（ch02）。这座镇子的尺寸由她自己给出："I don’t care who you are, when you live in a town the size of your thumb, if you don’t like the way your hair turns out at the salon, you stuff it deep down and never acknowledge it."（ch02）她嘴上说与自己再无关系，心里放不下的却是另一件事——"She isn’t wrong, but most important (or less depending on how you look at it), I just want to get moving so I can stop thinking about Jack Bennett and wondering if he’s okay after his failed engagement—even though I’ll never see him again. Even though I often would have chosen to pluck my eyelashes out one by one instead of interacting with him. Even though he didn’t say goodbye to me."（ch02）Emily 每个周六上午都去镇上的咖啡馆写她的小说，用 Noah 派店里那张算不平的账本换一段固定的安静；这一次她在自己常坐的角落桌前看见了 Jack，停车场上停着一辆熄了火的黑色 Land Rover，就停在她的红白皮卡旁边（ch03）。两人为那张桌子争了半章，最后达成一份荒唐的合约：同一张桌子、不同桌角、谁也不跟谁说话。紧接着切到 Jack 那一侧：他签下新家的合同，而那栋房子正是 Emily 的隔壁（ch04）。

**十年敌意的两笔旧账。** 两条插叙交代这段宿怨的起点与代价。Jack 那一侧：与未婚妻 Zoe 一起搬去内布拉斯加之后他才看清真相——"It took moving with her to Nebraska where she got a new job to realize I felt lonelier with her than I ever felt by myself. It took being stripped of my work and…Emily…to see the truth. Zoe didn’t love me—she loved the lifestyle I could provide because of my writing career, but not me."（ch04）Emily 那一侧更早：高三毕业年的 5 月 23 日，Liam 拿着一封外州大学的录取通知书来到她家（ch05）；而她八岁丧亲这件事，直到 ch24 才第一次说出口——二年级那年，父母在科罗拉多登山露营时遇上风暴，医生怀疑是雷击击中了他们的帐篷。多年后她用一只水槽刷给自己做了定位："Each of those guys were looking for women like Annie and Madison. But me? I’m more akin to a Brillo pad you keep under your sink for when you need to scrub out those really tough grease stains."（ch05）

**邻居这件事，被写成了物理冲突。** Emily 的应对是把 Jack 的消息当八卦撒遍全镇、孤立他（ch05），Jack 的应对是埋头翻修房子。真正的转折发生在夜里：她提着一根水管上门报复，撞见没穿上衣的 Jack（ch07），他接住了这场对峙，把她挡在门外，塞给她一副耳塞（ch08）。到这一章末尾，两人的对抗已经降级成每天互发的挑衅邮件；而 Jack 自己承认，那场对峙里有一样东西是新的："We are standing in front of each other as a man and a woman—not teachers. This is…new. And the surge of attraction racing through my system is also new."（ch08）

**一封发错收件人的邮件。** 妹妹 Madison 临时爽约，Emily 独自喝醉，把写完的八万字手稿投给文学经纪人，一小时后才发现邮件发到了校长 Bart Killick 的邮箱（ch11）。她认定这世上唯一能解决它的人正是那个她讨厌了十年的 Jack：

> "The only thought that crosses my mind is: Help. And there’s only one person that I really trust to fix it. For our feuds and faults through the years, there’s one thing I know without a doubt about Jackson—he is the most competent person I’ve ever met. That’s why he’s my greatest rival. If anyone can find a way to get my email back into my computer, it’s him."（ch11）

这一夜他们第一次共谋——她交代了邮件里装的是什么：

> "“I wrote…” She pauses, looks at me, presses her lips together, and then I see the moment she decides to trust me. “I wrote a romance novel. A sexually explicit one that no one in the entire world knows I was writing. And after a terrible day and feeling sorry for myself that Madison didn’t come home when I really wanted her to, I had some wine and then thought it would be a great idea to send the book to an agent. But I accidentally sent it to Bart instead.”"（ch12）

并用一条交易换他翻进校长办公室删信（第 12 至 14 章）。这条线上还有一句更早的坦白，是他第一次听她说起自己为什么要写：

> "Emily (11:25 AM): The simple reason: I’ve always wanted to try it. The complicated reason: Because real life started hurting too much."（ch12）

**两支笔。** Emily 把手稿发给了 Jack。翌日清晨六点四十五分，他抱着一叠打印稿走进她的厨房，告诉她那本书他读了两遍（ch16）。此后两人的秘密同时摊开：他承认自己是畅销作家、笔名 AJ Ranger，而 AJ Ranger 的真身是父亲 Fredrick Bennett——镇上人人都认识的那位作家（ch25）。他把守密的理由说全，Emily 的第一反应不是愤怒而是敬畏（ch26）。

**壁橱、暗下来的酒吧，与一个退开两步的人。** 两人在 Bart 家走廊那只塞满大衣和清洁用品的小壁橱里困了很久，Jack 在黑暗里说清了当年为什么不肯说再见（ch20）；又是在 Hank's 酒吧打烊后、屋里只剩零星灯火的黑暗里，两人第一次把童年交出来——她讲雷雨夜替弟妹当镇定人的角色，他讲父亲与那间他此后再没踏进过的酒吧（ch24）。她吻了他；灯随即亮起，他退开两步，承认自己一直不肯再吻，是因为答应过在能坦白某件事之前不碰她。

**拒稿、姐妹，和 James 家的储藏室。** 真相刚落地就被一封拒稿打断：Colette Menton 直白地否了 Emily 的书，Jack 给出他一次拒稿都没收过的诚实答案，她当场反弹：

> "Emily blinks back fast and furious tears. “Exactly. You don’t know what this feels like—so don’t pretend you do.”"（ch27）

Madison 忽然从纽约回来宣布要辞掉一切回家，Emily 答应她，又反过来劝她把学位念完（ch28）。她真正的解冻发生在 James 家的步入式储藏室里：她为了躲开 Jack 说了那句「我要你的全部」而躲进去，四个妹妹一个接一个挤进来，最后进来的是 Noah——他撵走所有人，翻出当年那场她一直对外宣称是双方都很难受的分手，把她当年劝他的话原样还给了她（ch31）。

**两张餐桌，与尾声。** Jack 先在父亲家吃完了那顿他做了许多年的乖儿子式的饭，第一次站着跟父亲对望，并当场宣布自己不会再回来（ch32）。不久之后父亲在 Instagram 的一段直播里替他公开了 AJ Ranger 的身份（第 35 至 36 章）。他的反制不是隐退，而是接受采访，并且选择继续教书：

> "“Some things are going to change for me. Like having to get used to doing interviews and book tours. But not that. I love teaching—and I feel lucky to be at a school that makes it easy to love. I’m not ready to give that up yet. Also without me the teachers would have to drink your shit coffee every day and I can’t do that to them.” She smacks the back of her hand playfully against my chest. “Besides, I think I’m one of those authors who need a day job. If all I did was focus on my writing, I’m pretty sure my creativity would shrivel up and die.”"（ch38）

同时他卖掉了那辆摩托，理由是自己不能再成为她生活里的又一次丧失（ch37）。尾声在十一个月后的 5 月 23 日：Emily 的小说以笔名 Goldie 出版，她按捺不住把消息发过去，而他的第一反应不是恭喜，是追问笔名（ch39）。

## 主题

### 主题一：敌意是读人训练出来的铠甲

两个主角的本事是同一件：极快地读出对方想要什么，然后把自己变成对方能接受的那一个。Emily 把这门本事用在妹妹们身上，八岁那年起她就一直在做这件事：

> "I laugh once. “I’m the oldest daughter, I’m not scared of anything.” I pause as memories hit me wave after wave. And for the first time in my life, I say them out loud. “Someone has to hold it together. Someone had to lift the blanket on her bed and let her sisters climb in when the thunder would shake the house. Someone had to assure them that her bed was the safest place in the world.” Even when my own hands were trembling. “I was always promising them that I would never go anywhere, and my door would always be open for them.”"（ch24）

Jack 把同一门本事用在父亲身上，那段自白是全书最冷的一次自我诊断：

> "He looks away, sighs, and meets my eyes again. “My dad is a narcissist. I don’t mean it in a hyperbolic way either. I’m pretty certain that he is an actual narcissist. My life growing up…it was painful a lot. And I quickly learned that saying what I truly thought or felt could get turned on me in an instant. Honesty, vulnerability…those things were what I got rid of first. And then I learned the art of reading him. Reading his moods and his energy and becoming what I needed to be to get through the day and to protect my mom from his shit. That mechanism bled into the rest of my life too…mostly by accident. I read people and adapt before I even realize I’m doing it. It’s usually not until later when I’m sitting alone, and I feel hollow and used and upset that I realize I betrayed myself in some way. And so often…I’m scared that all this reading and maneuvering people—even if it’s with the right intent—is somehow going to turn me into him. So when you made that comment—”"（ch15）

全书真正的进展不是他们停止了这门本事，而是他们第一次把本事用在了承认自己需要对方上面。

### 主题二：写作是唯一没被驯服的那部分

两个写作者、两个笔名、同一套家庭阴影。Jack 用 AJ Ranger 躲开父亲，理由他自己数过：

> "And then I thought of my dad finding out. I thought of all the ways I would either fade into his shadow as Fredrick’s son who also writes, or he’d suck every last drop of joy from my writing process by insisting he was the reason for my success, or he wouldn’t be able to handle the competition and it would send him spiraling back to alcohol, which would in turn make my mom’s life miserable. Most likely all of the above."（ch04）

Emily 用一个直到尾声才揭晓的笔名躲开全世界；他们各自在对方身上认出的，恰恰是那个终于不肯再被驯服的人。写作在这本书里不是爱好，是他们唯一不设防的地方——所以拒稿（第 27 章）与曝光（第 36 章）才成为各自最重的危机。

### 主题三：家是「一直有人在」这件事

祖母是一个相当私密的女人，她最私密的秘密只有开旅馆的 Mabel 知道；她留下的习惯是难过的时候冲一杯热巧克力（第 28 章）：

> "My heart squeezes that I never knew these existed. That she never pulled them out for us to play dress-up in where she could see us laughing and enjoying each other’s company. My grandma was a very private lady. Kind and sweet—but reserved. The only person who likely knew all her secrets was Mabel—her best friend. Maybe she was open with her husband, but he had died before I was born, so I don’t know what sort of relationship they had."（ch13）

镇上的老人把这四个没有血缘的孩子认作自家孩子，说出口时用的是同样笃定的语气：

> "Unmistakable affection enters his eyes. “Emily was born and raised in this town. Her and each of her siblings. To those of us who have been here since their birth, those Walker kids are ours. Just not by blood.”"（ch06）

Mabel 讲出亡夫的故事时，给出的处方只有一个——学会在爱里休息，而不是在爱里害怕：

> "“It took some time and lots of small moments of building trust between us, but eventually, I learned to rest in my love for him instead of fear it.” She stares out at the memories floating through her mind for another moment, and I don’t dare interrupt. A few moments later, her smile changes to something lighter and she looks at me. “I have a feeling you’re a sorry fool just like my sweet husband. And I’m willing to bet all my hidden money that you got possessive of Emily yesterday because you love her, but you have your own wounds you’re bringing to the table.”"（ch29）

Emily 对妹妹 Madison 说的那句，是同一个承诺的姐妹版本：

> "I nudge her knee under the table with my foot. “We would never think that. And you didn’t fail. You just realized it wasn’t what you thought it would be—and that’s okay. You can always come home, Maddie. New York doesn’t have to be for you. But hey, at least you tried it, you know?”"（ch28）

## 人物弧光

### Emily Walker（笔名 Goldie）

她从一个把一切提前堵住、绝不让局面走到那一步的完美主义者，走到肯把手稿交出去、肯在那间暗下来的酒吧里开口的人。起点是一个八岁就学会当镇定人的大姐，而她对爱情的全部判决书写在这里：

> "People I love die. Or they hide things from me and leave. Or they simply outgrow me and move on. The one constant in my life has been me, at the end of the day, alone in my bedroom. And if I let myself love Jack fully with arms outstretched wide and he leaves me, it will break me."（ch30）

弧光的转轴在第 27 章——她第一次拒绝从一个一次拒稿都没收过的畅销作家手里接安慰，因为那意味着她的痛不成立；再往前一步是第 31 章，那天在 James 家的储藏室里，她才允许自己承认另一件事：

> "“Well, you’re right about one thing—I was afraid to leave them. And you.” I pause and refuse to cry this time. “But the truth is, I would have. I loved him enough that I absolutely would have. But…he didn’t ask me to go.”"（ch31）

终点的标志不是一句我爱你，而是十一个月后她能把出版的消息立刻发出去——她终于相信有人会为她高兴。

### Jack Bennett（笔名 AJ Ranger）

他从一个绝不在还没坦白某件事之前碰你的极端自控者，走到站着跟父亲对望、然后决定继续教书。他的弧光是一再把控制权交出去，头一件是为躲开父亲而隐瞒写作这件事，理由他自己一条条数过：

> "“It started as a secret because of my dad. I didn’t want him to know I was a writer and turn it into something about him. Writing—it brings me so much comfort and happiness. I didn’t want him to take that from me. And then when I submitted my first manuscript and got an agent, I made sure it all happened under a pen name. Zoe knew, but I asked her to sign an NDA first. I didn’t want any traces to come back to me, because then I’d be connected to my dad professionally. People wouldn’t be discussing my book as a debut author; it would all be about how Fredrick Bennett’s son wrote a novel and how it compared to his.” I shut my eyes tight and breathe out the words “I didn’t want that.”"（ch25）

然而同一门本事也让他在撞上 Zoe 出轨的真相之后，仍然选择瞒着她搬去内布拉斯加：

> "It took moving with her to Nebraska where she got a new job to realize I felt lonelier with her than I ever felt by myself. It took being stripped of my work and…Emily…to see the truth. Zoe didn’t love me—she loved the lifestyle I could provide because of my writing career, but not me."（ch04）

全书让他学会的是同一件事的反面——有些话只有说了才不会失去。第 32 章里他在父亲家做的最要紧的一件事，就是停止下那盘棋：

> "But tonight, it’s feeling too difficult to sit at this table and move pieces around an imaginary board game again. As long as we keep playing, my dad will always win."（ch32）

第 36 章被公开之后他选择反击而不是消失；第 37 章他选择卖掉那辆留了多年的摩托。他的笔名来历到第 38 章才说破：Ranger 是他童年抱过的填充玩具熊的名字。

### Noah Walker

他最早出现在 Emily 的叙述里，是一扇卧室门外：Liam 上门提分手那天，他从头听到尾却始终没有推门进去，而她只对他说过一句她一直感激的话——她从没告诉过他，当年她告诉全家这段感情是双方都很难受的。中间他还做过一件很像他的事：第 22 章他宣布要随巡演的妻子 Amelia 离开，第一反应不是自己的事，而是先把店安排好、免得 Emily 把自己累垮：

> "Noah’s smile has a touch of pity to it when he looks at me. “Actually, Em, I won’t need you to do that. Jeanine needed a change. She’s going to quit at The Diner and work for me at the Pie Shop full time instead. Since it’s a manager position she’ll get paid more than she did at The Diner and you won’t have to work yourself to the bone between school and the shop. It’s a win-win.” He pauses to assess my expression. I don’t know what he sees but whatever it is, it brings him to add, “I thought this would be easier on you.”"（ch22）

他的弧光在第 31 章完成：家庭聚会之后他不再躲在餐桌上，而是走进那间储藏室、撵走所有人、翻出当年的真相——

> "“We’re going to—because it’s important to this moment. Emily, you were always so tough and independent after Mom and Dad died, but never so closed off as you are now. It’s like that day froze a layer of ice over your heart so thick it stopped beating normally. And you know why I think it was? Because you’ve regretted not going with him.”"（ch31）

那是他第一次不以让妹妹好过为目的，而是把话说到底。
