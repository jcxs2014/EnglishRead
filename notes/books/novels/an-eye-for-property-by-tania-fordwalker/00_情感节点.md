# 00. 情感节点 · An Eye for Property（Tania Fordwalker）

**说明**：九个节点；每节点＝叙事概括＋关键引语（逐字取自已过门禁的章节块，说话人已对照 text/ 复核）。

## 节点一 · 手术台上多出一个人（ch01）
Mabel 全程清醒地做完脑部手术；合同规定乘客术后休眠，但 Rose 的芯片醒着——面对技术员的例行提问，她选择撒谎，把多出来的时间私吞下来。
> "You are awake throughout the brain surgery, and by the end of it, so am I—and that means something went wrong. Or right, depending on your point of view."
> "My lawyer and my accountant walked me through every line of that contract, so I know this for certain: I’m not supposed to be awake at the same time as you. The extra time I had—if only as a silent watcher—was an error. A bug. Unintentional, but very much to my benefit, especially if it continues."

## 节点二 · 穷住处与富地皮（ch02）
Mabel 搬进抵薪的海滩棚屋，边还债边拆箱；她不知道自己住的是 Rose 地皮近两百万的早期投资，也不知道自己被看得到梦境。
> "The seaside shack was one of my earliest investments, built in the seventies, and I held on to it all these years out of sentimentality, I suppose. … While the house itself is fit only for a bulldozer, this land, this dream location, is worth almost two million. I always had an eye for a good investment."
> "My chip must have tendrils in the image processing part of your mind, because I can see what you’re seeing even now, though it lacks the clarity of real sight. The speed is all wrong, like an old film from a hand-cranked camera. I glimpse faces, snatches of arguments, shifting landscapes."

## 节点三 · 全知的边界（ch02 末）
站在大宅铁门内，Rose 确认了自己的能力范围：感受全部漏得进来，想法一条也进不来——这条边界是此后所有误判的尺寸。
> "Your gut curdles. Envy? Anxiety? Both? I cannot say. I feel the physical effects of your thoughts, but the thoughts themselves are the only part of you unavailable to—"

## 节点四 · 盘点日（ch03）
Rose 的第一个周末：给肖像估价、读 Mabel 的讨债短信、定义「脑在身体里」、从眉缘开始按周改造，最后打出不能写明原因的四星。
> "Let me tell you, child, when you’re lying in a hospital bed befouling yourself as your body falls apart around you, you realise what matters. You are a brain in a body long before any other concern enters the picture, and you are a brain in a body after all else falls away. Currently, I am a brain in your body, and I mean to craft that body into a worthy vessel."
> "I have the beautician pluck a few stray hairs from the edges of your eyebrows—only subtly, for now. Arguably what I’m doing could fall under the contractual body modification exclusion, but the changes I’m planning are hardly along the lines of tattoos or blue hair. I can do all this piecemeal over weeks, for a gradual transformation you won’t notice."

## 节点五 · 评分战争（ch04）
数周拉锯：拔眉被抓包、麦当劳的「麦头痛」、社交圈的羞辱；调解会上 Mabel 差点说出「被盯着」，制度替 Rose 谈走了多出的一天。
> "You don’t notice the gradual removal of your brows until the third week, when I go too far and you, squinting at your reflection in a magnifying mirror, spot the puckered pores where hair used to be. You leave me two stars, and type and retype three progressively more polite versions of the same review before you lodge it."
> "Through the Tec, I talk you into quitting your job at McDonald’s and selling more time to me instead. I ask for the full five days—forty hours—per week, the legal maximum time a Passenger can use their Driver. Reluctantly, you agree to just one extra day. … Bully for you. Now you’ll have more time to not paint."

## 节点六 · 身体第一次反抗（ch05）
Prisha 的越洋劝退让 Rose 暴怒——Mabel 的手随她的怒火攥成拳；两天后，Rose 对镜砸碎「她的脸」。控制第一次越过了情绪的边界。
> "No wonder your life is a mess, with friends like these. How dare this girl undermine you just as you’ve begun making good choices? How dare she show up now and try to drag you away from the sensible path I’ve laid out for you? In the warm hollow of your mind, I rage and curse, and with a convulsive jerk, your hand twitches into a fist."
> "At eight thirty PM on Sunday, I stand in front of the mirror, breathing hard, glaring at your face in the glass. I lash out. You splinter into a thousand glimmering shards. A trickle of blood runs down your knuckle, and I absently brush it away."

## 节点七 · 两封信（ch05）
一封提案信要买十年，一封回信只想赎回自己：Mabel 被粉色床单留住一周，最后礼貌地预约了取消——她始终以为这只是份能辞职的工作。
> "Who knows what they’ll have invented by then—android bodies, or clones—or, simpler still, as politicians begin to age out and stare down the barrel of their own mortality, they’ll reverse the blanket ban on transfer of whole-life ownership. Ten years, and I’ll be able to buy a body; I’d bet my life on it. No more handovers, no ticking clock. No more negotiations with silly little girls. Just a body of my own: my property."
> "Thank you for your kind offer. It helped me figure out what I want, and I’m so sorry to tell you this, but we need to make an appointment for cancellation on Monday—being a Driver isn’t working out for me. I’ll happily serve out my one-month notice, and I’m grateful for the amazing opportunity. Thanks again for everything."

## 节点八 · 夺体与肉搏（ch06）
周一凌晨 Rose 顺睡眠接管身体；早晨 Mabel 从浏览器历史拼出真相，砸镜片、抢手机、扑向菜刀；Ben 破门听见的是疯话——八点五十九分，一切结束。
> "“If you’re … if you’re listening—” Your voice cracks. “I don’t want your fucking house. I don’t want your fucking money. I want you out of my head, and I’m going to get you out.”"
> "“Get her out!” you scream, waving the shard at him. “She’s in my head, she’s awake! The chip, the chip, cut it out of me!” … “There’s no time! She takes over at nine! She won’t give me back! PLEASE!”"

## 节点九 · 幽灵与坍缩（ch07）
现金收买、假合同、那不勒斯：接管完成的那一刻，世界开始收缩——评分换了主人，市集上总有什么在背后，最后连厨房的刀都转向了自己的太阳穴。
> "She goes quiet at last—though I know better than most that quiet doesn’t mean gone, and on the weekends, when I leave the house to go to the bustling market, I find myself looking behind— … Awake, asleep, you’re always there, behind me, no matter how fast I turn around."
> "It’s Saturday. My head pounds. I wince in the syrupy afternoon light. Where am I? Kitchen. Yes, of course. My lovely slate-tiled kitchen with its merry brass fittings and polished oak beams. Now, what was I…? Oh, yes. Chopping a parsnip, fresh from the market. It sits half sliced and abandoned on the cutting board, gnarled and bloodless as an old woman’s finger. The knife is in my raised hand, the blade turned inwards. The point brushes my temple."
