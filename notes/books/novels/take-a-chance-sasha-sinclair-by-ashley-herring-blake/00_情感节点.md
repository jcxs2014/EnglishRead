# 《Take a Chance, Sasha Sinclair》情感节点

## 节点一：回到克莱尔山的第一夜——飞镖、砖墙与「操，我真服了」（ch01-05）

**叙事概括：** 萨莎·辛克莱为了替父母完成遗愿回到克莱尔山，拍 Bishop 家族「玫瑰女巫」的纪录片。她驶过城镇界线时就预感到什么，而第一天晚上她就把预感具体化成一场错误：她不知道和她玩飞镖、接吻的女人就是 Margot Bishop。等她发现真相时，只能灌下一大口酒。而 Margot 得知真相后，用一句「把 Bishop 家的人弄上床就能得到你想要的一切」把那个吻变成指控——Sasha 没有否认，只回了一句「而且那真的他妈的棒极了」。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I want to kiss,” DW said matter-of-factly."（ch03）

> ② "Sasha glanced at the red peacoat on the stool next to her, then took a big gulp of her drink, slurping a jalapeño into her mouth as she did. “Well, fuck me.”"（ch03）

> ③ "Margot’s gorgeous eyes went dark, and she lifted her chin. “Get a Bishop into bed, get whatever you want. Was that how it went?”"（ch05）

> ④ "“Yes,” Sasha said. She sent both hands through her hair, exasperated. She let her palms slap back down to her sides, meeting Margot’s eyes again. “And it was fucking amazing.”"（ch05）

## 节点二：阁楼上的日记——「我绝无可能在笼中幸福」（ch08）

**叙事概括：** Margot 把 Sasha 带上阁楼看 Alice 的日记与遗物。两个都不习惯信任别人的人，在布满灰尘的旧物之间第一次交换了童年：Sasha 说起被人喊「小女同」，Margot 想起这个家里每个女人都有故事、唯独她像是被遗漏的那一个。Alice 一百年前写下的那句「我绝无可能在笼中幸福，即使是为了她」，像一句预言悬在她们头顶。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I could never be happy in a cage. Even for her."（ch08）

> ② "Friends trust each other. And I have no clue how to do that."（ch08）

> ③ "Everyone’s story except Margot’s."（ch08）

## 节点三：森林露营——一夜盛开的血玫瑰与篝火旁的吻（ch10-11）

**叙事概括：** Georgie 怂恿下的露营夜变成了一场猎鬼之旅。风里传来低语，冷风不合时宜地刮起，而 Sasha 在篝火旁问出了那句改变一切的话：「你上一次允许自己拥有真正想要的东西，是什么时候？」她们在帐篷里接吻，Margot 一边吻一边说「我甚至都不喜欢你」。次日清晨，帐篷四周一夜之间开出二十多朵血红的玫瑰，根扎得牢牢的——像 Alice 的鬼魂，也像这段不肯松手的感情。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“When was the last time you let yourself have something you really wanted?” Sasha finally asked."（ch10）

> ② "Roses. At least twenty of them, spread out around their tents, sprouting up from the dirt, from the pine straw, single flowers in full bloom, their petals a deep blood red."（ch11）

> ③ "“I don’t think anyone planted this thing overnight,” she said, holding it up and peering at the roots. “It was pretty locked in there.”"（ch11）

> ④ "“Fucking Bishops,” Sasha said under her breath, then finished packing up the tent, threw her backpack over her shoulder, and walked out of the woods by herself."（ch11）

## 节点四：塞勒姆——被退回的信与「屏住呼吸的三十五年」（ch14-15）

**叙事概括：** 在女巫审判纪念地，Margot 第一次讲出 Prudence 的故事——「超自然的，非尘世的，所以他们绞死了她」——也第一次承认自己「屏住呼吸了三十五年」。随后她们在 Alice 的后人 Allie 家里读到 Rose 写给 Alice、却被原封退回几十年的信：Rose 在嫁给别人后仍每年写信，Alice 一封也没有拆。最后一封写于 1934 年：「我不会再写了。」Margot 望着信纸说，那一定很可怕——同时又可怕又美好又令人恐惧又美妙。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I feel like I’ve been holding my breath for thirty-five years, stomping through my days with my eyes closed, my heart locked up tight."（ch14）

> ② "“My last letter came back to me. I suppose I understand why, though part of me still hopes I inscribed the address incorrectly, that you didn’t see my name and decide against me. I know what you’d say. That I am the one who decided. My love, it didn’t feel like a decision. It never has. Not for me.”"（ch15）

> ③ "“I won’t write again. My heart can no longer withstand the silence, the dread I feel nearly every day when I gather the post. I pray for your happiness, Alice. I really do.”"（ch15）

> ④ "“It must be horrible,” she finally said. “Horrible and beautiful and terrifying and wonderful all at once.”"（ch15）

## 节点五：阁楼之夜——Slut Witch 与被说出口的童年（ch22）

**叙事概括：** 塞勒姆回来后的温存清晨，Sasha 醒来便预感到「搞砸了」——不是身体，而是整间房间的空气变了。Margot 兑现了阁楼之夜欠下的「轮到我说」，讲出作为 Bishop 家的孩子在小镇长大的每一天：被闺蜜欺凌、从未正式出柜、储物柜上的「Slut Witch」。而 Sasha 没有安慰她，只是翻身把她罩在身下，宣布「Slut Witch」从此是她最喜欢的新短语——把羞辱改写成只属于两个人的暗语。

**关键引语：**

> ① "True, that word was never used, nor should it be, because it was completely preposterous, but Sasha felt the word nonetheless. Every single letter, curling around her, sliding over her skin, enveloping her until Sasha had felt safe."（ch22）

> ② "“Growing up as a Bishop was hard,” Margot said, her voice quiet and soft. “Clare Mountain is insular and narrow-minded and homophobic, and I think I felt all of that every single day growing up.”"（ch22）

> ③ "“A lot of people said they saw them write Slut Witch on my locker in permanent marker. I never even officially came out.”"（ch22）

> ④ "“You know,” Sasha said, rolling them so that Margot was underneath, Sasha’s arms caging around her head. “I think Slut Witch might be my new favorite phrase.”"（ch22）

## 节点六：万圣节派对——「我不想再躲藏了」（ch23-24）

**叙事概括：** 拍摄日那天，Margot 在镜头前说「有时我觉得也许我才是那个鬼魂」，又说 Bishop 家的坚强并不能消除伤痛与孤独——然后她擦掉眼泪，笑了。当晚她拉着 Sasha 去万圣节派对，在凉亭里第一次当众握住她的手：「我想握住你的手，我想吻你，想和你一起笑……我不想再躲藏了。」全镇人都在看，而她没有松手。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“But sometimes I feel like maybe I’m the ghost,” she said."（ch23）

> ② "And she smiled. It was small. It was a little sad. But it was there. And it was real."（ch23）

> ③ "“I want to hold your hand,” Margot said, her fingers cool on Sasha’s neck. “I want to kiss you and laugh with you. And I know it’s not as simple as just wanting something. That the world is complicated and scary, but I don’t want to hide anymore. Not because of who I am, or who my family is, or what people in this town think of me, and certainly not because of who I love.”"（ch24）

> ④ "She just wanted to hold Margot Bishop in her arms and dance."（ch24）

## 节点七：森林徒步——「她想让家人看见她」（ch25）

**叙事概括：** 真相在徒步中摊开：Margot 一直觉得自己被家人爱着却不被理解，被接纳却从未被庆祝。Adeline 承认「我把她的坚强误当成刀枪不入」，Margot 则承认自己很擅长掩饰感受——因为她从来不知道该怎么去感受。她把日记读给母亲和妹妹听，不是要展示，只是想让家人真正看见她。

**关键引语：**

> ① "She wanted her family to see her."（ch25）

> ② "“I confused that strength for invincibility.”"（ch25）

> ③ "“I think I’m really good at masking what I feel, and…yeah, a lot of that is because I’ve never known how to feel it.”"（ch25）

> ④ "“I think that’s what I’m trying to figure out.”"（ch25）

## 节点八：批准撤回与森林失踪——「你还好吗？」（ch26-27）

**叙事概括：** 拍摄日当天，Adeline 收回了批准——她不再相信 Sasha 是讲述她们故事的人。Sasha 的世界瞬间崩塌；而 Margot 与 Georgie 随后在森林里失联，所有短信都显示未送达，把她拖回三年前父母车祸那晚的恐惧。Sasha 的「你还好吗」一遍又一遍地发出去，像溺水的人抓住最后一根浮木。Margot 最后开着卡车冲出车道，只留下一句话：「对不起，妈妈，但我现在就得走。」

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I’m pulling my approval,” she finally said."（ch26）

> ② "She and her sister would end up a headline. The idiots who went off the trail, when everyone knew the easiest way to get lost and die in the woods was to go off the trail. … Bishop Witches Vanish in Thornrose."（ch27）

> ③ "And the rest were from Sasha. … Did you know? … Margot, answer me. … Margot, where are you? … Are you okay? … Are you okay? … Are you okay? … The last text was sent more than an hour ago."（ch27）

> ④ "“I’m sorry, Mama, but I have to go right now,” Margot said, opening the front door and hurrying down the porch steps. She screeched out of the driveway in the truck, peeling down the street in a cloud of burnt rubber and exhaust."（ch27）

## 节点九：推开与坦白——「我他妈爱上她了」（ch28-29）

**叙事概括：** Margot 追到 Sasha 门外，发誓要在门外活下去、吃饭、呼吸、睡觉，直到她开门。但 Sasha 已经被恐惧控制，说出「我一直只在乎这部片子」这种最安全的谎，然后逃到 April 与 Daphne 家。在车道上的崩溃里，她第一次把爱说出口：「我他妈爱上她了。」她还想改变——想成为一个能够去爱、也能够被爱的人。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Sasha, I swear to god. I will live, eat, breathe, and sleep outside this door until you open it.”"（ch28）

> ② "“I’ve always only cared about the film,” she said."（ch28）

> ③ "“I’m fucking in love with her,” she finally said, shaking her head."（ch29）

> ④ "And she wanted to change. She wanted to be a person who could love and be loved."（ch29）

## 情感节点十：十四个小时、加州罂粟与首映夜（ch31-34）

**叙事概括：** Margot 开车十四个小时追来，说出「从来没有人追过你，而你值得这样的对待」；Sasha 捧着母亲最爱的加州罂粟花登门，向 Adeline 剖白真心，得到的回应是「萨莎只是来和我们一起吃晚饭」。门阶旁并生出玫瑰与加州罂粟。一年后，首映夜的银幕亮起，Sasha 在 Margot 的目光里确认了自己的家——Margot Bishop 就是家。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“This is about me coming after you. Because I don’t think anyone ever has. And you deserve that.”"（ch32）

> ② "“Truest words I know,” Sasha said, tucking a lock of hair behind her ear. “You make me want to be scared with you, Margot Bishop.”"（ch32）

> ③ "“Everything’s fine, sweetheart,” Adeline said, standing and picking up the flowers. “Sasha was just joining us for dinner.”"（ch33）

> ④ "Margot Bishop was home."（ch34）
