# 《Take a Chance, Sasha Sinclair》（Ashley Herring Blake）金句精选

① "Even in the dead of winter, Sash, her father had told her more than once, roses, like tiny miracles. Tiny stories that need to be told."（ch01）

**中文**：即使在隆冬，玫瑰也像小小的奇迹，是需要被讲述的小小故事。
**上下文**：ch01 萨莎驶过克莱尔山镇界线后，想起父亲生前不止一次对她说的话。
**为什么重要**：书名意象的第一处落点，也是她拍这部纪录片的深层动机——替父母讲述没来得及讲完的故事。
**呼应关系**：与 "“They’d be so proud of you,” Margot said now, turning to look up at the film title on the marquee with her, their faces tilted toward the lights. “They are proud of you.”"（ch34）「她们正在为你骄傲」形成首尾闭环。

② "The woman smiled without her teeth. “How did you know?” Her voice was like butter, smooth and calm and devastating. “Do I look like a witch?”"（ch01）

**中文**：那女人没露齿地笑了笑。「你怎么知道的？」她的声音像黄油，顺滑、平静，又令人倾倒。「我看起来像女巫吗？」
**上下文**：ch01 玫瑰叶咖啡馆，Adeline 第一次见 Sasha 时主动调侃「女巫」标签。
**为什么重要**：女族长第一次露面就把恐怖的传说变成幽默与掌控——Bishop 家的女人从不让别人定义她们。
**呼应关系**：与 "Margot flinched slightly at that word—witch—but regained her composure quickly. “I understand weight and trajectory. That’s all.”"（ch02）Margot 听到「女巫」时的退缩形成对照——同一顶帽子，Adeline 戴着是王冠，Margot 穿着是旧伤。

③ "“The Bishops,” he said slowly, carefully, as though navigating a land mine. “You just played darts with Margot for the last hour?”"（ch03）

**中文**：吧台后的侍者 Callum 放慢语速、小心翼翼地说：「Bishop 家。」——仿佛在雷区里挪步——「你刚才跟 Margot 玩了一小时飞镖？」
**上下文**：ch03 萨莎刚和「DW」在酒吧玩了飞镖、接了吻，转头才知道她的真名。
**为什么重要**：全书第一个戏剧性反讽——她要说服的人，就是她刚刚吻过的人。
**呼应关系**：与 "Margot’s gorgeous eyes went dark, and she lifted her chin. “Get a Bishop into bed, get whatever you want. Was that how it went?”"（ch05）「把 Bishop 家的人弄上床，就能得到你想要的一切」构成同一件事的两面解读。

④ "“Destroy what, exactly?” Adeline asked, lifting her arms and gesturing around her. “The walls this family has surrounded itself with for generations like a curse? If so, then I’m all for it.”"（ch04）

**中文**：「到底会毁掉什么？」阿德琳抬起手臂，指着四周问道。「这个家族像被诅咒一样围了祖祖辈辈的高墙吗？如果真是这样，那我举双手赞成。」
**上下文**：ch04 Margot 质疑纪录片会毁掉家族时，Adeline 的回应。
**为什么重要**：女族长把「拍纪录片」从威胁重新定义为拆墙——家族的控制权第一次松动的瞬间。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I no longer believe you’re the right person to tell our story.”"（ch26）「我不再相信你是讲述我们故事的人」构成批准与撤回的两极。

⑤ "Margot’s gorgeous eyes went dark, and she lifted her chin. “Get a Bishop into bed, get whatever you want. Was that how it went?”"（ch05）

**中文**：Margot 那双漂亮的眼睛暗了下去，她抬起下巴。「把 Bishop 家的人弄上床，就能得到你想要的一切。事情就是这么回事吗？」
**上下文**：ch05 Margot 得知 Sasha 就是纪录片导演后，认定自己被利用了。
**为什么重要**：Margot 的防御机制第一次完整现身——她把亲近等同于算计，因为小镇一直这样对待她们家。
**呼应关系**：与 "“Yes,” Sasha said. She sent both hands through her hair, exasperated. She let her palms slap back down to her sides, meeting Margot’s eyes again. “And it was fucking amazing.”"（ch05）Sasha 那句「而且那真的他妈的棒极了」正面相撞。

⑥ "“Yes,” Sasha said. She sent both hands through her hair, exasperated. She let her palms slap back down to her sides, meeting Margot’s eyes again. “And it was fucking amazing.”"（ch05）

**中文**：「对，」萨莎说。她烦躁地把双手插进头发里，又让手掌啪地落回身侧，重新迎上 Margot 的目光。「而且那真的他妈的棒极了。」
**上下文**：ch05 面对 Margot「你只是想利用我」的指控，Sasha 拒绝否认那个吻。
**为什么重要**：Sasha 第一次在 Margot 面前选择坦诚而不是自保——也是全书她们之间极少见的诚实时刻。
**呼应关系**：与 "Margot’s gorgeous eyes went dark, and she lifted her chin. “Get a Bishop into bed, get whatever you want. Was that how it went?”"（ch05）构成同一场对峙的两句台词。

⑦ "But I will never," Margot said, then leaned a little closer, her voice that kind of deadly whisper she’d inherited from her mother, "never approve this film for production."（ch06）

**中文**：Margot 说：「但我永远不会——」她凑近了一点，声音是她从母亲那里继承来的致命低语，「——永远不会批准这部影片投入制作。」
**上下文**：ch06 过山车之后，两人之间气氛正暧昧，Margot 却把禁令说得像挑逗。
**为什么重要**：全书最「口是心非」的一句禁令——她越靠近，越要把对方推开。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I don’t even like you.”"（ch10）「我甚至都不喜欢你」是同一套防御的变体。

⑧ "I could never be happy in a cage. Even for her."（ch08）

**中文**：这是 Alice 于 1901 年 9 月 9 日写下的日记结尾——她绝无可能在笼中幸福，即使是为了心爱的 Rose。
**上下文**：ch08 Bishop 家阁楼，Margot 第一次读 Alice 的日记。
**为什么重要**：一百年前的 Alice 已经替 Margot 说出了她不敢说的话——不自由的爱不叫爱。
**呼应关系**：与 "I hiked through the woods today to the top of a mountain. I don’t know what this peak is called. I never had a head for geography, but I find I like not knowing. I enjoy feeling like the only person in the world sometimes. The only woman in all of Appalachia. If I’m the only one, there is no one to lose, no sting of leaving or being left, no reason to ever go back into the valley if I so choose. I am my own, and my own is mine. This sunset is mine. The colors spreading across the sky, tangerine bleeding into lavender into the softest pink. Mine and me and my own."（ch16）「我是我自己的，我的归我所有」同属 Alice 的独立宣言。

⑨ "“When was the last time you let yourself have something you really wanted?” Sasha finally asked."（ch10）

**中文**：「你上一次允许自己拥有真正想要的东西，是什么时候？」Sasha 终于问道。
**上下文**：ch10 森林露营之夜，篝火旁，Sasha 问 Margot。
**为什么重要**：全书爱情线的题眼——Margot 三十五年来的生存方式就是从不允许自己要。
**呼应关系**：与 "Take a chance, Sasha Sinclair."（ch13）「冒险吧，Sasha Sinclair」分别指向两个人的同一课。

⑩ "Looking Margot Bishop in the eye was like Sasha’s own personal kryptonite—she turned into a powerless, desperate mess, and that was not who Sasha Sinclair was. She was the kryptonite."（ch11）

**中文**：直视玛戈特·毕晓普的眼睛，就像面对萨莎自己的氪石——她会变成一个无力、绝望的烂摊子，而那不是萨莎·辛克莱该有的样子。她才是那块氪石。
**上下文**：ch11 露营次日清晨，Sasha 看着熟睡的 Margot 时意识到自己已经陷进去了。
**为什么重要**：全书最生动的爱情比喻——「她是我的氪石」随即反转成「我才是那块氪石」，自我认知第一次松动。
**呼应关系**：与 "One thing she knew for sure—Margot Bishop was going to be the total, complete, absolute death of her."（ch13）「Margot Bishop 终将成为她的致命伤」同义反复。

⑪ "I feel like I’ve been holding my breath for thirty-five years, stomping through my days with my eyes closed, my heart locked up tight."（ch14）

**中文**：「我感觉自己像是屏住呼吸了三十五年，闭着眼睛、把心锁得紧紧的，一天天踩过去。」
**上下文**：ch14 塞勒姆女巫审判纪念地，Margot 在 Prudence 的碑前对 Sasha 坦白。
**为什么重要**：Margot 全书最重要的一句自我陈述——她把自己三十五年的压抑压缩进一句话。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I think I’m really good at masking what I feel, and…yeah, a lot of that is because I’ve never known how to feel it.”"（ch25）「我很擅长掩饰自己的感受」是同一件事的两次表达。

⑫ "“I won’t write again. My heart can no longer withstand the silence, the dread I feel nearly every day when I gather the post. I pray for your happiness, Alice. I really do.”"（ch15）

**中文**：最后一封信写于 1934 年 1 月，比第一封晚了三十多年。Rose 说：我不会再写了，我的心再也承受不住这份沉默，也承受不住每天取信时那种恐惧。我为你祈祷幸福，Alice，真的。
**上下文**：ch15 Allie 家的壁炉边，Margot 读 Rose 写给 Alice 的最后一封信。
**为什么重要**：一段持续三十多年的酷儿爱情以「不再写信」收尾——比任何一次争吵都更沉默、更重。
**呼应关系**：与 "“My last letter came back to me. I suppose I understand why, though part of me still hopes I inscribed the address incorrectly, that you didn’t see my name and decide against me. I know what you’d say. That I am the one who decided. My love, it didn’t feel like a decision. It never has. Not for me.”"（ch15）第一封信构成时间的两端。

⑬ "I hiked through the woods today to the top of a mountain. I don’t know what this peak is called. I never had a head for geography, but I find I like not knowing. I enjoy feeling like the only person in the world sometimes. The only woman in all of Appalachia. If I’m the only one, there is no one to lose, no sting of leaving or being left, no reason to ever go back into the valley if I so choose. I am my own, and my own is mine. This sunset is mine. The colors spreading across the sky, tangerine bleeding into lavender into the softest pink. Mine and me and my own."（ch16）

**中文**：这是 Alice 日记里的一段——她独自爬上山顶，不知道山的名字，却享受这种「不知道」；她说如果世上只有自己，就没有失去、没有离别，她就是自己的主人，晚霞的颜色也只属于自己。
**上下文**：ch16 塞勒姆旅馆，Margot 与 Sasha 在唯一一张床的两侧各自读 Alice 的日记。
**为什么重要**：Alice 的孤独哲学既安慰了 Margot，也映照出她「不敢拥有」的根源——怕失去，所以宁可不要。
**呼应关系**：与 "I could never be happy in a cage. Even for her."（ch08）「我绝无可能在笼中幸福」互为表里。

⑭ "I don’t even have anyone to blame," Sasha said. "It was no one’s fault. Just weather and roads, the slip of a tire around a sharp curve. It would be so much easier if I could be angry with someone."（ch21）

**中文**：Sasha 说这场夺走父母的事故甚至没有可归咎的对象——只是天气、道路，轮胎在急弯处一滑；如果世上有一个可以恨的人，反而会容易得多。
**上下文**：ch21 Clover Lake，Ramona 与 Dylan 家客房之夜，Sasha 第一次向 Margot 完整讲出父母车祸的经过。
**为什么重要**：Sasha 的创伤核心——她连「怪谁」都做不到，于是只能怪自己。
**呼应关系**：与 "“Trauma,” she said, enunciating every consonant, elongating every vowel. “And the fact that you don’t even want to deal with the word itself is proof enough that it’s relevant.”"（ch29）April 逼她面对「trauma」这个词直接接续。

⑮ "True, that word was never used, nor should it be, because it was completely preposterous, but Sasha felt the word nonetheless. Every single letter, curling around her, sliding over her skin, enveloping her until Sasha had felt safe."（ch22）

**中文**：「爱」这个字从未被说出口，也不该说出口——因为那实在太荒谬了——但萨莎仍然感觉到了那个字。每一个字母都环绕着她，滑过她的皮肤，将她包裹起来，直到她感到安全，感到自己像是回了家。
**上下文**：ch22 温存后的清晨，Sasha 醒来发现「整间房间的空气变了」。
**为什么重要**：全书第一次正面书写「爱」——虽然没人说出口，但每一个字母都已经在了。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I’m fucking in love with her,” she finally said, shaking her head."（ch29）「我他妈爱上她了」从无声到有声。

⑯ "“But sometimes I feel like maybe I’m the ghost,” she said."（ch23）

**中文**：「但有时候我觉得，也许我才是那个鬼魂。」玛戈特低着头，看着日记本封面上凸起的玫瑰，说出了这句话。
**上下文**：ch23 拍摄日，Margot 在镜头前谈及自己在这个家的位置。
**为什么重要**：全书最痛的一句自我定义——Bishop 家的女人都有故事，唯独她觉得自己像不存在。
**呼应关系**：与 "Everyone’s story except Margot’s."（ch08）「每个人的故事，除了玛戈特的」同一句话的两层写法。

⑰ "“It’s perseverance. It’s continuing even when it’s hard. It’s what every single person in my family has been doing for centuries. But that perseverance doesn’t remove the hurt or the fear or the loneliness.”"（ch23）

**中文**：「那是坚持。是在艰难的时候仍然继续。这是我家每一代人做了几个世纪的事。但这份坚持并不能消除伤痛、恐惧，或孤独。」
**上下文**：ch23 镜头前，Margot 回答「坚强是什么」。
**为什么重要**：Margot 把 Bishop 家族的「坚强」神话拆开——坚持是真的，但伤痛也是真的。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I confused that strength for invincibility.”"（ch25）Adeline「我把坚强误当成刀枪不入」互相印证。

⑱ "“I want to hold your hand,” Margot said, her fingers cool on Sasha’s neck. “I want to kiss you and laugh with you. And I know it’s not as simple as just wanting something. That the world is complicated and scary, but I don’t want to hide anymore. Not because of who I am, or who my family is, or what people in this town think of me, and certainly not because of who I love.”"（ch24）

**中文**：「我想握住你的手，」玛戈特说，她的手指凉凉地贴在萨莎的颈侧。「我想吻你，想和你一起笑。我知道事情没有那么简单，世界是复杂而可怕的，但我不想再躲藏了。不是因为我是谁，或我的家人是谁，或这个镇上的人怎么看我，更不是因为——我爱的是谁。」
**上下文**：ch24 万圣节派对的凉亭里，Margot 第一次当众向 Sasha 表白。
**为什么重要**：Margot 决定不再躲藏——这是她全书弧光的转折点。
**呼应关系**：与 "I feel like I’ve been holding my breath for thirty-five years, stomping through my days with my eyes closed, my heart locked up tight."（ch14）「屏住呼吸三十五年」形成因果：那个不敢呼吸的人，在这里决定呼气。

⑲ "“I confused that strength for invincibility.”"（ch25）

**中文**：Adeline 承认，自己一直把 Margot 的坚强误当成刀枪不入。
**上下文**：ch25 家中深夜，Adeline 向 Margot 承认自己一直把她的坚强误当成刀枪不入。
**为什么重要**：母女关系里最温柔的一句道歉——爱是真的，看错也是真的。
**呼应关系**：与 "She wanted her family to see her."（ch25）「她想让家人看见她」是同一个和解的两面。

⑳ "“I think I’m really good at masking what I feel, and…yeah, a lot of that is because I’ve never known how to feel it.”"（ch25）

**中文**：Margot 承认自己很擅长掩饰感受，而这种掩饰的根源，是她从来不知道该怎么去感受。
**上下文**：ch25 家中深夜，Margot 向母亲 Adeline 承认自己「很擅长掩饰感受」。
**为什么重要**：Margot 全书最坦诚的自我剖析——不会感受，所以只能假装没事。
**呼应关系**：与 "I feel like I’ve been holding my breath for thirty-five years, stomping through my days with my eyes closed, my heart locked up tight."（ch14）「把心锁得紧紧的」同源。

㉑ "“I’m fucking in love with her,” she finally said, shaking her head."（ch29）

**中文**：「我他妈爱上她了，」她终于说出口，摇了摇头。
**上下文**：ch29 克莱尔湖，April 与 Daphne 家的车道上，Sasha 崩溃大哭后第一次把爱说出口。
**为什么重要**：Sasha 全书唯一一次不带任何防御地承认爱情。
**呼应关系**：与 "True, that word was never used, nor should it be, because it was completely preposterous, but Sasha felt the word nonetheless. Every single letter, curling around her, sliding over her skin, enveloping her until Sasha had felt safe."（ch22）那个从未被说出口的字，在这里终于有了声音。

㉒ "Nearly thirty-five years have I loved you, my dearest, and I do fear, hope, dread, desire that I will love you for thirty-five more."（ch30）

**中文**：「我爱了你将近三十五年，我最亲爱的，而我的确恐惧、盼望、害怕、渴望能再爱你三十五年。」
**上下文**：ch30 Margot 与 Georgie 重读 Alice 写给 Rose 的信（信最初在 ch27 森林小屋发现），其中一封这样开头。
**为什么重要**：Alice 对 Rose 的爱跨越了整整一生——这是历史线对现代线的最终启示。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I won’t write again. My heart can no longer withstand the silence, the dread I feel nearly every day when I gather the post. I pray for your happiness, Alice. I really do.”"（ch15）Rose 的最后一封信互为回声：一个写了三十五年，一个退了三十五年。

㉓ "“This is about me coming after you. Because I don’t think anyone ever has. And you deserve that.”"（ch32）

**中文**：「这跟你逃跑没关系，而是我来追你了。因为我觉得从来没有人追过你。而你应该得到这样的对待。」
**上下文**：ch32 Margot 开车十四个小时来到 Sasha 面前。
**为什么重要**：全书爱情线最重要的行动——不是表白，是「追来」。
**呼应关系**：与 "Take a chance, Sasha Sinclair."（ch13）「冒险吧，Sasha Sinclair」——最终冒险的是 Margot。

㉔ "“Truest words I know,” Sasha said, tucking a lock of hair behind her ear. “You make me want to be scared with you, Margot Bishop.”"（ch32）

**中文**：「这是我所知道的最真的话，」萨莎说着，把自己的一缕头发别到耳后，「你让我想和你一起害怕，玛戈特·毕晓普。」
**上下文**：ch32 和解之夜，Sasha 对 Margot 说出这句话。
**为什么重要**：Sasha 的「被爱恐惧」在这里被改写——不是不怕了，而是愿意一起怕。
**呼应关系**：与 "“Trauma,” she said, enunciating every consonant, elongating every vowel. “And the fact that you don’t even want to deal with the word itself is proof enough that it’s relevant.”"（ch29）从「创伤」一词的对抗，到「一起害怕」的同行。

㉕ "“They’d be so proud of you,” Margot said now, turning to look up at the film title on the marquee with her, their faces tilted toward the lights. “They are proud of you.”"（ch34）

**中文**：玛戈与萨莎并肩仰望着剧院招牌上的片名，说：「她们会为你骄傲的……她们正在为你骄傲。」
**上下文**：ch34 一年后，圣莫尼卡 Aero 剧院首映夜，Margot 对 Sasha 说。
**为什么重要**：全书最后一课的落点——Sasha 终于从「追赶父母梦想的人」变成「拥有自己生活的人」。
**呼应关系**：与 "Even in the dead of winter, Sash, her father had told her more than once, roses, like tiny miracles. Tiny stories that need to be told."（ch01）父亲那句「玫瑰是小小奇迹」首尾相合。
