# Blacktail · 情感节点（10 节点）

> 引语全部逐字取自原书，说话人与出处已按原文上下文核验。

## 节点一：谋杀与加冕（Prologue: Hate）

公猞猁 Hate 上山去杀那头"耳朵和脚掌大得滑稽"的幼狼，却发现自己被逆光剪影骗了——对方是头巨兽。他被咬断脖子，临死前默念了一个只有猫才懂的词。母猞猁 Guile 半路迎到的不是丈夫，而是站在石头上的新王。

- 「As my prey have fulfilled their duty, so let me fulfill mine. I would be the tooth and claw of Your will. Use me as You see fit.」（Hate 的父亲临终祈祷，Prologue）
- 「Death would come for her one day. She would be remembered for a time. Then she would be forgotten. The only thing that really mattered were the tracks she left in the snow.」（叙述者写 Guile 的决意，Prologue）
- 「I am Blacktail who is the death of Hate. This is wolf land. None other may hunt here.」（Blacktail 留在石头上的气味诗，Prologue）

## 节点二：铁链上的爱（Chapter 1: Love）

循着一缕气味越过"黑路"的 Blacktail，遇见了拴在铁链上的看门狗 Renren。他们交配、定情、计划逃跑；Renren 却要先给主人家的孩子一个告别吻。第二天她被送进"打金属棍的巢穴"，回来时肚子空了，伤口无人过问，死在原地。当夜他杀进人宅。

- 「They are strange and sometimes cruel, true enough. But in their essence, their nature, they are not so different from us. They breathe. They mate. I think they may even love. I owe them this much.」（Renren，Chapter 1: Love）
- 「She can run from this end to that, he realized. Then, if she likes, she can run back again. Beyond that circle of stony earth lay all of the world, bounded by the lush grass of summer.」（叙述者写 Renren 的链条，Chapter 1: Love）
- 「God may forgive them for this but if so I will kill Him, too.」（Blacktail 留在门口的誓言，Chapter 1: Love）

## 节点三：白骨森林与逃亡（Chapter 2: The Son of Snow）

花栗鼠 Apple 用一句关于"白骨森林"的谎话吓退灰熊，也救了 Blacktail 一命——可这句谎话招来了三代人以来最大的一场围猎。老狐狸 Trap-Slipper 带他穿过暴雪、跳下悬崖，最后自己跳出藏身的洞穴把猎犬引走；两条追猎的狗闻出了他的味道，却说他"什么都没闻到"。

- 「You thought yourself above us, with your reason and your gunshots and your lights…but the hunt is eternal. It can afford to be patient.」（Snooky-Ookums 对被诅咒的死人头，Chapter 2）
- 「“My mother was called Snow,” Trap-Slipper said. “If you were to kill a man in her name, just one, I would consider all debts repaid.”」（Trap-Slipper，Chapter 2）
- 「“I smelled nothing,” he said after a long moment. “Did you?” “No,” Rex said, meeting his eyes. “Nothing at all.”」（Rex 与 Cephus，Chapter 2）

## 节点四：沉睡的神与卡拉克（Chapter 3: Dominion）

旧猫妈妈带着两只美洲狮找到 Blacktail：众神不是被杀就是逃走，只有最伟大的那一位仍在沉睡。她用一段被封存的记忆给他看乌鸦 Karakaa——那位守教者在祈祷得不到回应之后，向地狱请愿、换来对人类的生杀之权，抹掉了一座镇子，随后在流放的路上被降临的月亮带走。

- 「Only the greatest of them remains. The God of Gods is not dead, only sleeping. I am sure of it.」（旧猫妈妈，Chapter 3）
- 「There, in the sight of the moon, this devout beast who had lived many lifetimes in the service of the Forest God petitioned hell.」（旧猫妈妈转述 Karakaa 的叛教，Chapter 3）
- 「The moon came down to Karakaa.」（旧猫妈妈，Chapter 3）

## 节点五：断腿、洞穴与失落之神（Chapter 4: The God of the Lost）

Blacktail 横穿那条永不间断的车河时被撞断后腿，靠爬行和高烧撑到濒死。救他的是一个吃人的人：那人会说狩猎的语言，信奉一位"失落之神"，替他抢来会治伤的兽医，又带他走进满是头骨的洞穴献祭。神借尸体开口，告诉他下一步该往哪里去，并把那人变成一条郊狼。

- 「My god is of the lost things.」（吃人者对 Blacktail，Chapter 4）
- 「Words may bind the eyes to a single sight, the mind to a single thought, blinding you to all else.」（失落之神，Chapter 4）
- 「You will find what you seek in the Fire of God, I think.」（失落之神，Chapter 4）

## 节点六：混血儿的信仰与"变大"（Chapter 5: Fatberry）

饥饿的狼群领主 Fang 被告知"旅行者"即将过境——猫族信使说他是"死亡之神"，希望重燃。Blacktail 真的来了，比传说更大，答应替他们除掉独占猎场的大灰熊 Fatberry。围猎失败，Fang 被咬死；只有混血的 Little One 死战不退，Blacktail 才在那一刻撕碎了那头熊，并把 Little One 立为领主。

- 「“Perhaps a creature may have more than one nature, and each of them be true,” Little One suggested in his small, soft voice.」（Little One，Chapter 5）
- 「But where she had struck down a wolf, something else now rose up.」（叙述者写 Blacktail 的再起，Chapter 5）
- 「He is bigger now.」（Quickfoot 的内心，Chapter 5）

## 节点七：慈悲的教团与以命换命（Chapter 6: Mercy）

一群家畜崇拜每天投喂它们的女人 Mercy，视她为神；Blacktail 撕碎教团的战犬 Sarge 与整支信众，却在最后被巫师猫 Larkspur 告知：她的法术是以命换命，Mercy 的病三年前就已转到他自己身上。他闯进内室，看见 Mercy 用身体护住那只小狗——想起母亲临死前同样护着幼崽，于是撞破墙逃走。

- 「The hunt is broken here. The thought cut to the heart of him. The hunt is broken here.」（Blacktail 的内心，Chapter 6）
- 「Doing? I am doing nothing. It was done three winters ago, not long after you killed Hate. You are murdered, wolf.」（Larkspur，Chapter 6）
- 「He remembered looking on this with a terrible species of wonder, unable to comprehend the mind of the monster that would destroy such love.」（叙述者写 Blacktail 的记忆，Chapter 6）

## 节点八：饥饿岩与神之火（Chapter 7: The Fire of God）

在被称作"饥饿岩"的沙漠里，被主人遗弃的混血狗 Chester 靠一段祷文打发等死的时间。Blacktail 教他狩猎，逼他亲手杀掉一对人类男女。随后一群"圣者"升起巨火，火里浮出天使 Uriel——人类之神的使者：它用 Renren 的鬼魂与死去的五只幼崽换他罢手；Renren 的鬼魂只回了一句话：杀光他们。

- 「Hell, he had discovered, is hunger.」（叙述者写 Chester 的处境，Chapter 7）
- 「Your wrath is a terrible thing, Blacktail, perhaps the worst thing. All who hear it must tremble. The god of man would ransom your peace.」（Uriel，Chapter 7）
- 「Then kill them, she barked, spitting the words out with such venomous rage that even Blacktail drew back a step. Kill for me, who was faithful and loved them without reservation.」（Renren 的鬼魂，Chapter 7）

## 节点九：骷髅之地与血咒（Chapter 8: The Place of the Skull）

兽群从各地赶来列队目送他。Blacktail 走进那座关满"狩猎之神造物"的实验楼，撕开铁栏，用自己的血治愈猫狗与猴子，答应母狗 Joy-Hop 在远方山丘与死去的伴侣和幼崽重逢。天亮后他等来了当年给 Renren 做手术的兽医——把血咒交到他手上。

- 「You must carry on—even past the outer edge of your courage.」（Blacktail 对 Joy-Hop，Chapter 8）
- 「I have granted them their wish. They will learn more of it than they ever wished to know.」（Blacktail 对白猫 Silence，Chapter 8）
- 「For you, Snow, who was the mother of Slipper, he said, laying it before her like a quilt. God remembers you. Sleep on this tonight, that it may protect you from the chill of the ground. This one dies in your name. The wolf turned back to Doctor. The rest die in Mine.」（Doctor 梦中的神谕，Chapter 8）

## 节点十：远方山丘与赦免（Chapter 9: The Far Hill）

人的时代结束后，旧猫妈妈在沙漠里被一支狗群追上——群首正是当年那只被遗弃的狗 Chester，如今自称 Raze。她不抵抗，坐下来等着被吃，却听他说起两个梦：神攻上天庭唤出 Karakaa 抹掉天军；神在长满花与蝴蝶的山丘上托他带话。她听完，不再需要退回那片紫罗兰田。

- 「It didn’t have to be him, you know, the Old Kitty Mother said. In the beginning he was nothing more than a wolf.」（旧猫妈妈，Chapter 9）
- 「He said that I was to tell you that the violets grow also on his Far Hill.」（Chester 转述神的口信，Chapter 9）
- 「There is a little breath still in my chest, she thought, a little hunger still in my belly. I think now is the time for food, and for naps in the warm sun and for scheming my little schemes.」（旧猫妈妈，Chapter 9 末段）
