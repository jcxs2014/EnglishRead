# 53. You Can Start Over as Many Times as It Takes.（一面墙 · 4,917 字节 · 42 段）

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：**这是全书的高潮，而它的技术是**把一个「我」钉成一堵墙**——`Carefully, I tack them to the walls in chronological order and then stand back and look at them.`（我小心地把它们按时间顺序钉在墙上，然后退后一步看着它们。）——**而那一堵墙的三站是**：从外婆的一台相机（① `Her large-format 8x10" camera.`）到她自己对着镜头的那一次（④ `"I'm an alcoholic."`）到**一个陌生人说的四个字**（⑤ `I'm not inexperienced with addiction myself.`）；**而全章的最后一个动作是**把镜头对准自己**（⑥ `Because I don't want to be a watercolor. I don't want to wash off, or away.` / `Click.`）

**情感弧线位置**：**弧线的顶点，也是全书唯一一个「好」的结局**。弧线的位置因此是**ch52 那张纸之后的下一个上午**——**而它的技术是**把 ch50 ④ `Dawn` 说的那句 `You have to reset the clock. Start over.`（你得把钟重置。重新开始。）变成一整章的标题**（`You Can Start Over as Many Times as It Takes.`）。**注意本章的标题本身就是一个从句的主语**（`as many times as it takes`——需要多少次就是多少次）——**也就是说这本书的最后一个标题不是一句断言，是一句让步。**

**人物弧线**：① **第一次把自己的照片钉成一幅有时间顺序的东西**（② `There's the story of me.`）——**而这一句的技术是五个词，而它是全书她对自己下的唯一一次定义**；② **第一次把 `I'm an alcoholic.` 说给一个不认识她的人**（④）——**而 `It sounds weird, saying that out loud, for the first time, really, to anyone.`（把那句话真地说出口、对任何一个人说，是第一次，说起来很怪。）这一句是全书那句诊断的第四次出现，而这一次它是**被别人听见**的**；③ **第一次拒绝变成一种会消失的东西**（⑥ `I don't want to be a watercolor.`）——**而这一句接的是 ch51 ① `José` 背的那两句诗**。

**叙事手法**：单视角（Bella）＋ **0 处 `—` 分节**＋ 0 处 U+00AD ＋ **4,917 字节 / 42 段**。**结构是「四站」**：① 阁楼里的外婆的箱子 ② 墙上那面 `Polaroid` ③ 作业的构想 ④ `Clara Comstock` 与那四个词 ⑤ 外婆的二十岁 ⑥ 最后一张。**而本章的形式上有一个特点：全章只有一个人称的「我」的自我陈述是完整的两句**（⑤ `Each one contributes to the whole story of me as written so far.` / `I am too much, too little, too bruised, starry-eyed, scared, wren sparrow roadrunner quail,`）——**而这第二句里嵌着全书那四个鸟名。**

## 故事梗概

**阁楼（①）** —— `The shed in our backyard is cluttered, and the ceiling is quilted with webs.` —— `Old bicycles, gardening equipment, roller skates, boxes and boxes and boxes labeled Mom, as in Laurel. My mother's mother.` —— `I wedge myself between dusty suitcases to reach Grandma's dented green trunk.` —— `I lift out cartons of unused film, albums of black-and-white images, notebooks in Laurel's messy handwriting.` —— `She told me once she always took notes about what she wanted to portray before she would do a shoot, like she was drafting a story before bringing it to life.` —— **`At the bottom of the trunk, I found it, wrapped in a thick blue wool blanket. Her large-format 8x10" camera.`** —— `I'm going to learn to use it someday. I think Laurel would like that.` —— **`But for now, I put it away.`**

**那面墙（②）** —— `I reach into my backpack and take out the baggies of Polaroids from rehab and the ones I've been taking here with the camera Tracy gave me.` —— **`Carefully, I tack them to the walls in chronological order and then stand back and look at them.`** —— **`There's the story of me.`** —— `Puffy and bruised and beaten down in the beginning, and then, gradually, a lightening, shades of something else, or someone else, trying to break through.`

**作业（③）** —— `I still haven't finished last semester's final project for Ms. Green.` —— `I think I'm going to use these instead of the tree, where I tried to hide myself behind branches.` —— **`I think I could make a whole diary of the last three months in these Polaroids. I'm sorry if Ms. Green won't like it.`** —— `what better than these images of a certain girl who is me? Who started out one way and ended another?` —— `I understand now what Tracy meant when she said they were telling a story, one that I was writing about myself without fully realizing it.`

**那位客人（④⑤）** —— `The door to the shed opens suddenly, startling me. I jump.` —— **`You must be Bella,` she says warmly. `Your mother said you were out here. I'm Clara Comstock. I'm an old friend of your grandmother's.`** —— `Are you really storing Laurel's work out here? I'm worried about the temperature.` —— `Very interesting,` she says. `Little bit of a rough start, I see.`** —— **`I was in rehab,` I say softly. `I got really hurt.`** —— **`I'm an alcoholic.`** —— `It sounds weird, saying that out loud, for the first time, really, to anyone.` —— **`I'm not inexperienced with addiction myself,` she murmurs. `Good for you. Very nice eye.`**

**外婆的二十岁（⑤）** —— `You look like her,` she says finally, turning back to me. `We met at Brearley. Pretending to be perfect little girls in our A-line skirts during the day and then coating our faces with powder and false eyelashes and ratting our hair and pulling on fishnets and boots at night to go to impossibly exciting and dangerous parties with impossibly exciting and dangerous people.` —— `And she recorded all of it. Like she did everything. She never wanted to forget a thing.` —— `And I want to make sure no one forgets her.` —— `There was a period in her twenties when she did some captivating self-portraits. Mostly nudes in abandoned buildings. I'm very interested in those. The exposed self.`** —— **`You're the curator,` I say. `The one who's been calling for months.`** —— `Okay, but can you come back? I'm kind of thinking. I have some homework to finish. I missed a lot of school and I have an art project.` —— `I think about what she said, "the exposed self."`

**最后（⑤⑥）** —— `I look at my photos again, walk along them, touching each image, one by one, with a finger.` —— **`Each one contributes to the whole story of me as written so far. I am too much, too little, too bruised, starry-eyed, scared, wren sparrow roadrunner quail, and one day I hope I look back at all these photographs and think I was beautiful in my brokenness, in all the pieces I keep gathering up and trying to suture back together.`** —— `One day.` —— **`But for now, I reach into my backpack and slip out my Polaroid, position it in front of me.`** —— **`Because I don't want to be a watercolor. I don't want to wash off, or away.`** —— **`Click.`**

## 精读

### ①

> "The shed in our backyard is cluttered, and the ceiling is quilted with webs. Old bicycles, gardening equipment, roller skates, boxes and boxes and boxes labeled Mom, as in Laurel. My mother's mother. … I wedge myself between dusty suitcases to reach Grandma's dented green trunk. … I lift out cartons of unused film, albums of black-and-white images, notebooks in Laurel's messy handwriting. She told me once she always took notes about what she wanted to portray before she would do a shoot, like she was drafting a story before bringing it to life. … At the bottom of the trunk, I found it, wrapped in a thick blue wool blanket. Her large-format 8x10" camera. It's heavy in my hands, boxy and big. I'm going to learn to use it someday. I think Laurel would like that. … But for now, I put it away."

**中文理解**：——**我们后院那间小屋堆满了杂物，天花板织满了蛛网。**——**旧自行车、园艺工具、旱冰鞋，还有箱子、箱子、箱子，标着「妈妈」，也就是 `Laurel`。我妈妈的妈妈。**——**我挤在落满灰的行李箱之间，去够外婆那个有凹痕的绿色箱子。**——**我搬出成盒的没用过的胶片、成册的黑白照片、还有 `Laurel` 那手潦草字迹写的笔记本。**——**她跟我说过一次，她总是在拍之前先记下她想呈现什么，就像她是在把一个故事打好了草稿，再把它变成现实。**——**在箱子最底下，我找到了它，包在一床厚厚的蓝色羊毛毯里。她那台大画幅的 8x10 相机。在我手里很重，方方正正，很大。我总有一天要学会用它。我想 `Laurel` 会喜欢那样。**——**但现在，我先把它放回去。**

**关键词**：The shed in our backyard is cluttered / the ceiling is quilted with webs / Old bicycles, gardening equipment, roller skates / boxes and boxes and boxes labeled Mom / My mother's mother / I wedge myself between dusty suitcases / Grandma's dented green trunk / cartons of unused film / albums of black-and-white images / notebooks in Laurel's messy handwriting / notes about what she wanted to portray before she would do a shoot / like she was drafting a story before bringing it to life / At the bottom of the trunk, I found it / wrapped in a thick blue wool blanket / Her large-format 8x10" camera / It's heavy in my hands, boxy and big / I'm going to learn to use it someday / I think Laurel would like that / But for now, I put it away

**为什么这样写**：**这一段的技术是**用一句自我校正把「外婆」和「妈妈」打通，而这一段真正的发现被放在最后三句（①）。** —— `boxes and boxes and boxes labeled Mom, as in Laurel. My mother's mother.`（**还有箱子、箱子、箱子，标着「妈妈」，也就是 `Laurel`。我妈妈的妈妈。**）—— **而它的技术是**三次 `boxes`（箱子）加一个 `as in`（也就是），而最后那一句 `My mother's mother.`（我妈妈的妈妈。）独立成句**；**——而 `Mom`（妈妈）这个标签在全书写的是**死者**（ch12 `Mom, this is Franny.`／ch34 `Mommy's taking me to Agnes's.`），而本章这一处它第一次被用来指**一个活人的母亲**。

**而外婆的那个工作方法是这一章的题眼（①）** —— `She told me once she always took notes about what she wanted to portray before she would do a shoot, like she was drafting a story before bringing it to life.`（**她跟我说过一次，她总是在拍之前先记下她想呈现什么，就像她是在把一个故事打好了草稿，再把它变成现实。**）—— **而它的技术是**那个 `like` 从句把「记笔记」翻译成了「写草稿」，而 `bringing it to life`（把它变成现实）把这个比喻收了尾**；**——而这一句与 ② `There's the story of me.`（这就是我的故事。）是同一件事的两代版本**——**也就是说这一章的真正内容是：她发现她外婆做摄影的方式，就是她这一个月在做的事。**

**而那台相机被找到又被放回去，而这两句是本章的转折（①）** —— `At the bottom of the trunk, I found it, wrapped in a thick blue wool blanket. Her large-format 8x10" camera.`（**在箱子最底下，我找到了它，包在一床厚厚的蓝色羊毛毯里。她那台大画幅的 8x10 相机。**）→ `I'm going to learn to use it someday. I think Laurel would like that.`（**我总有一天要学会用它。我想 `Laurel` 会喜欢那样。**）→ **`But for now, I put it away.`**（**但现在，我先把它放回去。**）—— **而 `But for now`（但现在）这三个词是本章最重要的三个词**，**——因为它意味着她知道自己还会回来拿，而她此刻要用的不是那台，而是她自己的那台。**

**而那台相机的外形在 ch51 ① 已经出现过一次（①）** —— ch51 ① `a boxy-looking camera strapped around her chest`（胸前斜挂着一台方方正正的相机）——**而本章这一处是实物，而它还是 `boxy and big`（方方正正，很大）**；**——也就是说这本书用了一张照片把一件实物预告了两章。**

**读者视角提示**：**`notebooks in Laurel's messy handwriting`（`Laurel` 那手潦草字迹写的笔记本）是全书唯一一次有人说外婆的字「潦草」（①）** ——**而 ch53 ⑤ 那个来访者 `Clara Comstock` 说的那句关于她的话（`And she recorded all of it. Like she did everything.`）（她把一切都记下来。像她做的每一件事一样。）——**也就是说外婆的「记」这件事在 ch53 由两个不同的人各说了一次：一次用「潦草」，一次用「一切」。**

### ②

> "I reach into my backpack and take out the baggies of Polaroids from rehab and the ones I've been taking here with the camera Tracy gave me. … Carefully, I tack them to the walls in chronological order and then stand back and look at them. … There's the story of me. … Puffy and bruised and beaten down in the beginning, and then, gradually, a lightening, shades of something else, or someone else, trying to break through."

**中文理解**：——**我把背包伸进去，掏出那些从戒治所带回来的拍立得（装在自封袋里），还有我在这儿用 `Tracy` 给我的那台相机拍的。**——**我小心地把它们按时间顺序钉在墙上，然后退后一步看着它们。**——**这就是我的故事。**——**一开始是浮肿的、有淤青的、被打下去的；然后，渐渐地，一片亮起来，某种别的东西、或者别的什么人，正试着破出来。**

**关键词**：I reach into my backpack / the baggies of Polaroids from rehab / the ones I've been taking here with the camera Tracy gave me / Carefully, I tack them to the walls in chronological order / and then stand back and look at them / There's the story of me / Puffy and bruised and beaten down in the beginning / and then, gradually, a lightening / shades of something else, or someone else / trying to break through

**为什么这样写**：**这一段是全书最长的一次「不说话」的技术，而它的核心动作是**退后一步**（②）。** —— `Carefully, I tack them to the walls in chronological order and then stand back and look at them.`（**我小心地把它们按时间顺序钉在墙上，然后退后一步看着它们。**）—— **而这一句的技术是**三个副词式成分（`Carefully`／`in chronological order`／`then stand back`）走完了「钉 → 排 → 退」三步**；**——而 `stand back`（退后一步）的技术是**它让叙述者与那面墙之间第一次有了距离**——**也就是说这一句是这本书里她第一次作为「观众」看自己。**

**而 `There's the story of me.`（**这就是我的故事。**）是全书她对自己下的唯一一次定义，而它的技术是**五个词、零个形容词（②）** —— **——而这一句与 ① 那句 `like she was drafting a story before bringing it to life` 是一组**：**外婆在拍之前先起草一个故事，而她在这里发现自己的照片已经是一个写好的故事**；**——而 `Tracy` 那句被她在此刻"理解"了的话（③ `I understand now what Tracy meant when she said they were telling a story, one that I was writing about myself without fully realizing it.`）是这一句的机构版。**

**而那一句的最后一个分词是本章最要紧的动词（②）** —— `Puffy and bruised and beaten down in the beginning, and then, gradually, a lightening, shades of something else, or someone else, trying to break through.`（**一开始是浮肿的、有淤青的、被打下去的；然后，渐渐地，一片亮起来，某种别的东西、或者别的什么人，正试着破出来。**）—— **而它的技术是**前三个形容词全在描述**被打**（`beaten down`——被打下去），而后三个成分全在描述**出去**（`a lightening`——一片亮／`shades of something else`——某种别的东西／`trying to break through`——试着破出来）**；**——而 `or someone else`（或者别的什么人）这个 `or` 是这一句里最要紧的一个词**：**它把「变好」这件事从「感觉」挪到了「人」上，而这一挪正是这一章那位客人的到来。**

**读者视角提示**：**那面墙的两批照片来源不同，而这一句把它们并列了（②）** ——`the baggies of Polaroids from rehab`（从戒治所带回来的那些，装在自封袋里）／`the ones I've been taking here with the camera Tracy gave me`（还有我在这儿用 `Tracy` 给我的那台相机拍的）——**而这两批的载体不同（自封袋 vs 一台相机）、拍摄者不同（`Tracy` vs 她自己）、地点不同（沙漠 vs 这个后院）**；**也就是说这一面墙是她把「被拍的三个月」和「自拍的这些天」接在了一起——而这一步正是 ch40 ⑥ 那句 `I look right into the lens and I do not blink.`（我直直看进镜头里，而我没有眨眼。）的意义所在。**

### ③

> "I still haven't finished last semester's final project for Ms. Green. I think I'm going to use these instead of the tree, where I tried to hide myself behind branches. … I think I could make a whole diary of the last three months in these Polaroids. I'm sorry if Ms. Green won't like it. I don't mind whatever grade I get. But if she wants a self-portrait project, what better than these images of a certain girl who is me? Who started out one way and ended another? I understand now what Tracy meant when she said they were telling a story, one that I was writing about myself without fully realizing it."

**中文理解**：——**我还没做完上学期给 `Ms. Green` 的那个期末作业。我想我要用这些来代替那棵树，那棵我曾经试图躲在树枝后面的树。**——**我想我可以用这些拍立得做出这三个月的一整本日记。如果 `Ms. Green` 不喜欢，我道歉。我不在乎我拿什么分数。但如果她要的是一个自画像的作业，还有什么比这些「那个就是我」的女孩的照片更好呢？一个开头是一个样子、结尾是另一个样子的？**——**我现在明白了 `Tracy` 那句话是什么意思——她说它们在讲一个故事，一个我在自己都没完全意识到的情况下就在写的故事。**

**关键词**：I still haven't finished last semester's final project for Ms. Green / I think I'm going to use these instead of the tree / where I tried to hide myself behind branches / I think I could make a whole diary of the last three months in these Polaroids / I'm sorry if Ms. Green won't like it / I don't mind whatever grade I get / if she wants a self-portrait project / what better than these images of a certain girl who is me / Who started out one way and ended another? / I understand now what Tracy meant / they were telling a story / one that I was writing about myself without fully realizing it

**为什么这样写**：**这一段的技术是**把一件作业换掉，而换掉它的理由是一个她曾经躲过的地方（③）。** —— `I think I'm going to use these instead of the tree, where I tried to hide myself behind branches.`（**我想我要用这些来代替那棵树，那棵我曾经试图躲在树枝后面的树。**）—— **而它的技术是**`instead of`（代替）这个介词**——**而 `the tree, where I tried to hide myself behind branches`（那棵我曾经试图躲在树枝后面的树）这一项把一件美术作业变成了一次关于躲藏的记录**；**——而这与 ch53 ① 那句外婆的 `like she was drafting a story before bringing it to life`（就像她是在把一个故事打好了草稿，再把它变成现实）是一组**：**树是躲，相册是写。**

**而 `I don't mind whatever grade I get.`（我不在乎我拿什么分数。）是这一段里最硬的一句，而它的技术是**它紧跟在一句道歉后面（③）** —— `"I'm sorry if Ms. Green won't like it. I don't mind whatever grade I get."`（**如果 `Ms. Green` 不喜欢，我道歉。我不在乎我拿什么分数。**）—— **而这一组的技术是**一句为对方着想的话 + 一句为自己豁免的话，而它们的主语都是「我」**；**——而紧接着的反问 `what better than these images of a certain girl who is me? Who started out one way and ended another?`（**还有什么比这些「那个就是我」的女孩的照片更好呢？一个开头是一个样子、结尾是另一个样子的？**）是全书她第一次为自己的证据辩护**，而它用了两个问句。**

**而最后一句是全书对 `Tracy` 那面墙的最终解释（③）** —— `I understand now what Tracy meant when she said they were telling a story, one that I was writing about myself without fully realizing it.`（**我现在明白了 `Tracy` 那句话是什么意思——她说它们在讲一个故事，一个我在自己都没完全意识到的情况下就在写的故事。**）—— **而它的技术是**`what Tracy meant`（`Tracy` 那句话的意思）这个从句把整章的机构线收了尾**，而 `without fully realizing it`（在自己都没完全意识到的情况下）这半句是全书对「事后理解」这个动作的第四次使用**（ch38 ⑦ 喝完之后才懂 `Fran`／ch42 ② 学会用「防护力场」／ch49 ⑥ 喝之前就认出了那个比喻／**本章：终于在事后之外理解了事中**）。

**读者视角提示**：**`Ms. Green` 是她的美术老师，而 `Ms. Ferris` 是 ch43 ③ 那个拿错档案的辅导老师（③）** ——**而 ch53 这一句是 `Ms. Green` 在全书里的第二次出现**（第一次是 ch43 ⑨ `on the way to Ms. Green's`）——**也就是说这一章里被提到的那位老师，是那个在两章前还只是一个走廊名字的人。**

### ④

> "The door to the shed opens suddenly, startling me. I jump. … There's a smartly dressed woman staring at me, an elegant black bag slung over one shoulder. … You must be Bella, she says warmly. Your mother said you were out here. I'm Clara Comstock. I'm an old friend of your grandmother's. … Oh, dear, she says. Are you really storing Laurel's work out here? I'm worried about the temperature. … Her eyes land on my Polaroids. She peers closer at them. … Very interesting, she says. Little bit of a rough start, I see. … She motions to the first set of pictures. … I was in rehab, I say softly. **而那句自我陈述是全书最长的一句，而它的技术是**一串形容词里嵌着四个鸟名（⑤）** —— `I am too much, too little, too bruised, starry-eyed, scared, wren sparrow roadrunner quail,`（**我太多、太少、太有淤青、眼含星光、害怕、鹪鹑麻雀走鹃鹌鹑，**）—— **而它的技术是**前五个成分是形容词（`too much`／`too little`／`too bruised`／`starry-eyed`／`scared`），而最后那个不是形容词，是**四个鸟名**（小写、无逗号）——**也就是说那四个词在这里不是比喻，是一个**名字**；**——而这一处的形态与 ch41 ⑥ 那句 `no more` 清单里的同一串完全相同**（`wren sparrow roadrunner quail, daily Polaroids,`）**——也就是说这本书在两处让这四个词出现在一张**失去**的清单上，而这一处它出现在一张**正在写**的清单上。**

**而 `suture`（缝合）这个词是全书最好的一次自我定位（⑤）** —— `in all the pieces I keep gathering up and trying to suture back together.`（**在我一直捡起来、一直试着缝回去的那些碎片里。**）—— **而它的技术是**`gathering up`（捡起来）与 `suture back together`（缝回去）是一组方向相反的动作，而 `keep`（一直）这个副词在两个动作上都有**——**也就是说这一句的技术是**把「破碎」写成一个持续的、她自己参与的过程**；**——而它与 ch49 ⑧ 那句 `And pick up all the broken parts of me`（然后捡起「我」所有的碎片）是同一条线**，**而本章这一句是那条线的完成态：`suture back together`。**

**而最后三句是全书最重要的一次「不」（⑤）** —— `One day.`（**总有一天。**）→ `But for now, I reach into my backpack and slip out my Polaroid, position it in front of me.`（**但现在，我把手伸进背包，掏出我的拍立得，把它摆在我面前。**）→ **`Because I don't want to be a watercolor. I don't want to wash off, or away.`**（**因为我不想变成一张水彩画。我不想晕掉，也不想被洗掉。**）→ **`Click.`**（**咔哒。**）—— **而 `One day.` 独立成段，而它的技术是**它接住了前面那句 `and one day I hope…`（而我希望有一天…）里的时间词，把它单独拎出来放在段末**；**——而 `But for now`（但现在）这三个词与 ① 那句 `But for now, I put it away.`（但现在，我先把它放回去。）是同一组词，而两次的方向相反**：**一次是放回外婆的相机，一次是掏出自己的。**

**而 `Because I don't want to be a watercolor.` 这一句是 ch51 ① 那两句诗的回声（⑤）** —— ch51 ① `I am a watercolor / I wash off.`（我是一张水彩画／我会晕开。）——**而本章这一句的技术是**把那两个陈述句变成一个否定句**，**并且把 `I wash off`（我会晕开）拆成 `wash off`（晕掉）与 `wash away`（被洗掉）两个**；**——而 `Click.` 这一个词是全书最后一个字，而它的技术是**它是一个拟声词、一个不及物动词、零个主语、零个宾语、句号**——**也就是说这本书以一个声音结束，而不是以一句话结束。**

**读者视角提示**：**`Click.` 与 ch40 ⑥ 那句 `I do not blink.`（而我没有眨眼。）是一组（⑤）** ——ch40 那是她第一次**正面看镜头**（`I look right into the lens`），而本章这一个字是她第一次**自己按下快门**；**——而 ch44 ⑬ 那句 `Aim it toward myself and click.`（把它对准我自己，然后按下快门。）是本章这一句的预告**，**——也就是说这本书里那台相机被送出去（ch44）→ 被写进日程（ch52）→ 在这一章第一次自己用（本章），一共用了四章。**

## 一句话总结

**这一章是全书的高潮，而它的技术是**把一个「我」钉成一堵墙**（② `Carefully, I tack them to the walls in chronological order and then stand back and look at them.` / `There's the story of me.`）——**而那堵墙的最后一格是一个陌生人说的四个字**（④ `I'm not inexperienced with addiction myself,` she murmurs.）；**而全章最后三句是这本书对 ch51 ① 那两句诗的回答**（⑤ `Because I don't want to be a watercolor. I don't want to wash off, or away.` / `Click.`）——**也就是说这本书没有用一句话结束，它用了一个声音结束。**
