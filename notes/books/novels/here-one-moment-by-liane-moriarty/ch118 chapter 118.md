## 精读

> **原句 1:** "His name was not Thor. He introduced himself as ‘Ben’ but I got the feeling it wasn’t his real name, so let’s call him Thor."

**中文理解**：他不叫 Thor；他自我介绍叫「Ben」，但她觉得那不是真名，那就叫他 Thor 吧。
**关键词**：His name was not Thor, introduced himself as ‘Ben’, wasn’t his real name, let’s call him Thor
**为什么这样写**：句子一开口就把名字否掉，再用 so let’s call him Thor 把它现场发明出来；叙述者不追问，自己给读者一个可用的称呼——这份随意正是她信任对方的起点。
**读者视角提示**：她开门时先撞见的是 I opened the door and found myself face to face with a man’s torso.；这个 Thor 称呼来自 He resembled an older version of ‘Thor’, the fictional superhero portrayed by the astonishingly attractive Australian actor, Chris Hemsworth.——她先在他脸上找的是「银幕上的雷神」，找到的其实是机上帮她提过行李的那个人。

> **原句 2:** "He said he’d been following the story and was becoming increasingly concerned. He said I’d correctly predicted three deaths and now there had been a fourth."

**中文理解**：他说他一直在跟这件事，而且越来越担心；他说她准确预言了三起死亡，现在出现了第四起。
**关键词**：following the story, increasingly concerned, correctly predicted three deaths, now there had been a fourth
**为什么这样写**：计数被放在句子的两头——先说「三」这个已经既成的事实，再用一个 now 把「四」推进当下；读者跟着的是时间的方向，而不是数字的大小。
**读者视角提示**：One November morning, seven months after the flight, and about a week before the anniversary of Ned’s death, as well as the deaths of Jill and Bert, someone knocked on my door. 把这一天钉在忌日前一周：来访的时点不是随机的：叙述者把敲门这一天与忌日的距离写得很精确。

> **原句 3:** "It was frightening and distressing to hear they were now dead and that people thought it proved I had supernatural abilities. I felt that dreadful sense of responsibility I’d experienced when I learned about the two people falling off the rooftop terrace."

**中文理解**：听说那些人已经死了、而人们认为这证明她有超自然能力，这让她又怕又难受；她又一次感到了当初得知有人从屋顶露台坠落时那种可怕的责任感。
**关键词**：frightening and distressing, people thought it proved, supernatural abilities, that dreadful sense of responsibility
**为什么这样写**：第二句用「当初那种」把两件事扣在一起：她早就背过一次责任，现在被告知要再背一次；回忆不是插叙，是加重。
**读者视角提示**：He gently told me what I’d done on the plane and seemed unsurprised when I said I had no memory of it. 交代她为什么当时就信了——对方语气平常，还提到 who else but me would talk of ‘cause of death’ and ‘age of death’?；I remembered how the little boy had been staring back at me, and children rarely show interest in me. 与 there was something so eerily familiar about what Thor described – not that I suddenly remembered my actions, but as if I could remember once dreaming them, 是同一次搜索里的两条旁证，It was the same sick shame I used to feel when people told me about my drunken behaviour at those rooftop parties. 那种熟悉的丢脸感也跟着回来了。

> **原句 4:** "(Look. He didn’t really say ‘safe house’. He said ‘investment property’. It’s just that he really did have the rather exciting manner of someone working in international espionage.)"

**中文理解**：（你看。他其实并没有说「安全屋」，他说的是「投资房产」。只是他身上确实带着一种在跨国情报工作的人才会有的相当 exciting 的做派。）
**关键词**：He didn’t really say ‘safe house’, He said ‘investment property’, international espionage
**为什么这样写**：整块被括号括起来，是叙述者事后向读者交底：她当时被说服的正是这两种说法之间的落差；「房产」是这个世界能报销的说法，「安全屋」是另一个世界的。
**读者视角提示**：同一段里 He also said he had a safe house where I could stay until the story blew over. 才是他当时的原话，括号把「他真说了什么」和「我们怎么理解」分成两层；紧接着的 He said he believed my identity was about to be exposed, probably any day now, and that people were looking for me, and he didn’t want me to walk out my front door 才是他真正的顾虑，而 He said it was up to me how I worded it. 把措辞权整个交回给她。

> **原句 5:** "His cape didn’t swirl, as he wasn’t wearing one and he’s not really a superhero. He’s just one of those heroically helpful people. He certainly rescued me."

**中文理解**：他的斗篷没有飘起来，因为他根本没穿斗篷，他也并不是真正的超级英雄；他只是那种英勇地提供帮助的人。他确实救了我。
**关键词**：His cape didn’t swirl, wasn’t wearing one, not really a superhero, heroically helpful, He certainly rescued me
**为什么这样写**：先拆掉一个意象（斗篷不飘），再降级一个身份（不是超级英雄），最后才给出 He certainly rescued me 这个承认；降级之后那个承认才有分量。
**读者视角提示**：他自称 I believe he is a retired intelligence officer of some sort, although he is vague about the details.，叙述者的评价只有「他没说清楚细节」；Well, my head spun after Thor left. I literally spun in circles for a while, I was distraught, confused and incredibly embarrassed, and I very badly needed Ned, 则交代了他走后她那段时间的状态——她的信任从头到尾不是靠身份，而是靠那几处降级。

> **原句 6:** "It was like coming face to face with another resident in the stairwell of the Perth apartment block, and thinking, Oh my goodness, I hope I didn’t kiss you last night."

**中文理解**：那种感觉就像在珀斯公寓楼的楼梯间里迎面撞见另一个住户，然后心想：天哪，我昨晚没亲你吧。
**关键词**：coming face to face, the stairwell of the Perth apartment block, I hope I didn’t kiss you last night
**为什么这样写**：用一个自嘲的类比把「对别人可能造成的后果」缩小到可承受的尺度；笑点越轻，越能盖住底下真实的羞耻。
**读者视角提示**：笑点的底色在上一段：Colour flooded my face. I was mortified, horrified. I wanted the ground to swallow me up.——她当时是宁愿地上裂开一条缝。

> **原句 7:** "I’m so glad I remembered that his name was Leo and I wasn’t too shy to use it."

**中文理解**：她很庆幸自己还记得他叫 Leo，而且当时没有太害羞喊不出这个名字。
**关键词**：so glad, remembered that his name was Leo, wasn’t too shy to use it
**为什么这样写**：整句只有一句，却把「记性」和「胆量」并列为功劳——她一向在陌生人面前开不了口，所以这一喊才被当成成绩。
**读者视角提示**：她喊的是 I shouted, ‘LEO, WATCH OUT!’ and pointed.，他随即 He jumped nimbly to the other side of the muddy trench just in the nick of time. 跳开；It was going to hit him. 之前那一眼来自 He looked like he’d seen a ghost. We stared at each other for an endless second and then I caught a movement out of the corner of my eye. 里的「caught a movement」，而认出这个人的依据是 It was the man who had sat across the aisle from me on the flight, the one with the same impatient-tapping fancy shoes as Ned.——他坐在过走道对面；她先前还以为 I believed she’d been scammed. ## 一句话总结

这一章先用一位来客把「你在飞机上说过什么」这件事讲得她无法反驳，再在后院把这份「无法反驳」变成一场实祸——她差点成了又一次死亡的原因，最后靠喊出一个自己一向喊不出的名字把人拉开。
