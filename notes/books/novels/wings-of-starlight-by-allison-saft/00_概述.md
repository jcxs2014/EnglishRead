# 00. 概述

## 书目信息

- **书名**：Wings of Starlight
- **作者**：Allison Saft
- **类型**：YA 奇幻长篇（Peter Pan 前传 / Pixie Hollow 王国）
- **章节数**：28（Prologue + Chapter 1–27）
- **精读 md 数**：28 + 总览 3 = 31

## 一句话梗概

在 Pixie Hollow 王国，准女王 Clarion 为一只蜜蜂爬下树，却因此踏入 Winter Woods 与 Warden Milori 的世界——在那里，她发现 Elvina 隐藏的真相：Nightmares 囚禁在 Winter 的冰湖 beneath，而 Elvina 计划永远切断 Winter 与暖季的联系。Clarion 必须在 coronation 前封印 Nightmares，同时面对她与 Milori 之间不可能的爱情。

## 人物弧光

### Clarion（主角）
- **起点**：被 Elvina 教育成 "cold and remote" 的准女王，却天性 compassionate，感到被崇拜而非被认识的孤独。
- **转折**：在 Winter 遇到 Milori，发现 Elvina 隐藏的 truth，第一次 challenge Elvina 的 authority。
- **高潮**：在 crisis 中首次 wield governing-talent magic（serpent attack），发现 magic = emotion, not control。
- **结局**：defeat Queen Nightmare (dragon)，被 crowned Queen of Pixie Hollow on border，与 Milori 分离但 maintaining connection in spirit。

### Milori（Warden of Winter Woods / Lord of Winter）
- **起点**：孤独的 Warden，守护 Nightmares' prison，guilt-ridden。
- **转折**：Clarion 到来，他第一次感到 hope。
- **高潮**：carries Clarion across border (sacrifices his wings)。
- **结局**：broken wing, but recognized as Lord of Winter, serves on Clarion's council.

### Elvina（Queen of Pixie Hollow）
- **起点**：cold, remote mentor，information control。
- **转折**：Clarion's defiance forces her to reveal truth.
- **高潮**：power waning, admits "You were right."
- **结局**：returns to stars after coronation, but gives Clarion blessing.

### Petra（tinker, Clarion's best friend）
- **起点**：anxious, risk-averse, brilliant.
- **转折**：helps Clarion cross into Winter (coat, T-bow).
- **高潮**：lies to Elvina about sword, crosses into Winter to fight.
- **结局**：reconciles with Clarion, kisses Artemis.

### Artemis（scout, Clarion's guard）
- **起点**：stoic, duty-bound, "second chance" backstory.
- **转折**：lets Clarion go to border ("I trust you").
- **高潮**：crosses into Winter, fights Nightmares, injured.
- **结局**：chooses to stay as Clarion's guard, kisses Petra.

## 主题

1. **Love vs. Duty**：Clarion loves Milori but chooses duty (separation). "A love like theirs was ruinous."
2. **Hope vs. Fear**：Nightmares are "a child's fear, given shape." Clarion's magic = hope.
3. **Connection vs. Isolation**：Elvina's "cold and remote" vs. Clarion's "warmth." Winter is not cold; it's warm.
4. **Information Control**：Elvina filters information; Clarion demands truth. "You cannot decide what I should and should not know."
5. **Belonging**：Clarion finds belonging in Winter, with Milori, with friends. "It suits you."
