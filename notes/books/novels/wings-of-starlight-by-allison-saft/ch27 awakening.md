# 26. Chapter Twenty-Six

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "You did it," Artemis said against her hair."

**中文理解：** "You did it," Artemis said against her hair。Artemis 对 Clarion 说 "You did it"。

**关键词：** did it · against · hair

> **原句 2:** "'I believe in you,' Artemis said. 'Your strength. Your kindness. Your vision. Protecting someone like you is a worthy use of my talent.'"

**中文理解：** "I believe in you," Artemis said。「Your strength。Your kindness。Your vision。Protecting someone like you is a worthy use of my talent。」（Artemis 的话）——Artemis 对 Clarion 的信任与信念。

**关键词：** believe · strength · kindness · vision · worthy

**为什么这样写：** 用 Petra's "You did this to me" 作 anger—she blames Clarion for getting hurt. "You refused to listen to me when it mattered the most" 作 Petra's hurt—Clarion ignored her advice. "You are so selfish" 作 Petra's judgment—she sees Clarion as self-centered. "I'm sorry" (both) 作 mutual apology—both acknowledge fault. "I'm not going anywhere" 作 Petra's commitment—she'll stay.

**读者视角提示：** 注意 "I'm sorry" (both)——this is the book's argument: reconciliation requires mutual acknowledgment. Neither is entirely right or wrong. Both made mistakes.

> **原句 3:** "I believe in you," Artemis said."

**中文理解：** "I believe in you," Artemis said。Artemis 对 Clarion 说她相信她。

**关键词：** believe · in · you

> **原句 4:** "Your Majesty?" "Not yet," said Clarion softly."

**中文理解：** "Your Majesty?" "Not yet," said Clarion softly。Rowan 叫 Clarion "Your Majesty"，Clarion 说 "Not yet"。

**关键词：** Your Majesty · not yet · softly
