# 《What We Give Away》（Paulette Stout）情感节点

## 节点一：Leslie 的 relapse 与 Tasha session（ch35）

**叙事概括：** Leslie 在 Tasha session 中坦白 restrict relapse——她开始 sneaking peeks in mirrors, skipping meals, increasing dog walks，weight gain slowed。Tasha 帮她 talked things through，Leslie agreed to go back to using meal planner app and promised to stay away from scales。但 network 要求 meeting，Leslie 的 clothing crisis（"I have nothing to wear to meet them. My old clothes will be too small"）与 network hypocrisy（"I was. Before. They never cared when I was starving myself"）共同推进 anti-diet awakening。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I began sneaking peeks in mirrors, skipping meals, and increasing the length of my dog walks with Pepper."（ch35）

> ② "I was. Before. They never cared when I was starving myself."（ch35）

Leslie 的 "But if they thought I'd go away quietly, they were sadly mistaken" 宣布 resistance。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Curvy people are better off somewhere else."（ch36）

> ② "But if they thought I'd go away quietly, they were sadly mistaken."（ch36）
