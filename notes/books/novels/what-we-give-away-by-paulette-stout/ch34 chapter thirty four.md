## 精读

> "Little Diana drowned today too."

**中文理解**："Little Diana 今天也淹死了。"

**关键词**：Little Diana（Little Diana）/ drowned today too（今天也淹死了）

**为什么这样写**："Little Diana drowned today too"将 Mom's voice 定义 as "drowned"——not "silenced" but "drowned" (killed)。"today too"将 Mom's drowning 定义 as "alongside"——not "alone" but "too" (alongside Mom's toxic beliefs)。This "Little Diana drowned today too" is Leslie's anti-diet awakening's breakthrough: Mom's voice (Little Diana) is "drowned" (killed) alongside Mom's toxic beliefs. Leslie's anti-diet awakening kills both Mom's external voice (Little Diana) and Mom's internalized voice (diet dogma)。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "Little Diana drowned today too" 与 "My mom was a fucking cult leader" (ch16) 形成 arc: from "cult leader" (naming) to "drowned" (killing)。This arc is the anti-diet theme's resolution: Leslie kills Little Diana's voice, not just names it。

---

> "Heart, mind, and soul."

**中文理解**："心、思想和灵魂。"

**关键词**：Heart（心）/ mind（思想）/ soul（灵魂）

**为什么这样写**："Heart, mind, and soul"将 Leslie's nourishment 定义 as "holistic"——not "physical" but "heart, mind, and soul" (three dimensions)。This "Heart, mind, and soul" is Leslie's anti-diet awakening's resolution: food nourishes "heart, mind, and soul" not just "body"。Leslie's "Heart, mind, and soul" parallels Risto's "love in a dish" (ch09)——both define food as "love" (heart, mind, soul)。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "Heart, mind, and soul" 与 "Food was medicine, joy, pleasure, and life all rolled into one" (ch31) 形成 parallel——both define food as "multi-dimensional nourishment"。This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's resolution: food nourishes "heart, mind, soul" not just "body"。
