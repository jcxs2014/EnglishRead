## 精读

> "I'm more afraid of eating than not eating."

**中文理解**："我更害怕吃而不是不吃。"

**关键词**：more afraid of eating（更害怕吃）/ than not eating（而不是不吃）

**为什么这样写**："more afraid of eating than not eating"将 Leslie 的 food anxiety 定义为 "fear reversal"——not "afraid of not eating" but "afraid of eating"。"more... than..."的比较结构揭示 Leslie's fear hierarchy: eating > not eating. This "fear reversal" is the anti-diet theme's core insight: diet culture inverts natural hunger cues—eating becomes "threat", not eating becomes "safety"。"I'm more afraid of eating than not eating"的 "I'm"（I am）将 fear 定义为 "identity"——not "I feel afraid" but "I am afraid"，暗示 Leslie's fear has become part of her identity.

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "more afraid of eating than not eating" 与 Dot's "You could be biologically starving and malnourished at any size" (ch08) 形成 confrontation——Dot reveals that "not eating" is the real danger, but Leslie fears "eating" more. This fear inversion is diet culture's core psychological damage.

---

> "I'm hungry every damn minute of every day."

**中文理解**："我每时每刻都他娘的饿。"

**关键词**：hungry every damn minute（每时每刻都饿）/ of every day（每一天）

**为什么这样写**："hungry every damn minute of every day"将 Leslie's hunger 定义为 "chronic"——not "occasional" but "constant"。"every damn minute"用"damn"（该死的）强调 hunger 的 "relentlessness"——not "sometimes" but "every minute"。"of every day"将 hunger 定义为 "all-consuming"——not "during meals" but "all day"。This "chronic hunger" is Leslie's body's protest against food restriction—her body is "screaming for help" (Dot's words, ch13) but Leslie has learned to ignore it.

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "hungry every damn minute" 与 "I'd eaten enough" (Little Diana's whisper, same chapter) 形成 internal conflict——Leslie's body screams "hungry" but Mom's voice says "enough"。This conflict is diet culture's core psychological damage: the body's natural hunger cues are overridden by external rules.

---

> "I went to your mom's meeting last night. Let's say I'm curious to learn more."

**中文理解**："我昨晚去了你妈妈的会议。就说我很好奇想了解更多。"

**关键词**：curious to learn more（好奇想了解更多）/ your mom's meeting（你妈妈的会议）

**为什么这样写**："I went to your mom's meeting"将 Leslie's attendance 定义为 "action"——not "accidental" but "deliberate"。"Let's say I'm curious to learn more"用 "Let's say"（姑且说）定义 Leslie's curiosity as "understatement"——not "I'm convinced" but "I'm curious"，suggesting Leslie is still in the "exploration" phase, not "conversion"。This "curiosity" is Leslie's anti-diet awakening's first step: from "skeptic" to "curious"。"I went to your mom's meeting" reveals Leslie's willingness to engage with body acceptance——not "reject" but "attend"。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "curious to learn more" 与 "I'm more afraid of eating than not eating" (same chapter) 形成 tension——Leslie is "curious" about body acceptance but still "afraid" of eating. This tension is the anti-diet theme's core: intellectual curiosity vs. embodied fear。
