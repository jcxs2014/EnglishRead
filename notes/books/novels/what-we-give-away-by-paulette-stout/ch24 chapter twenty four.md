## 精读

> "Dieting is a waste of time because we are hardwired to fight attempts to shift our weight too far away from our set point."

**中文理解**："节食是浪费时间，因为我们被硬连线来对抗试图将体重移离设定点太远的尝试。"

**关键词**：hardwired（硬连线）/ set point（设定点）/ fight attempts（对抗尝试）

**为什么这样写**："hardwired"将 body's response 定义为 "biological"——not "choice" but "hardwired" (innate)。"set point"将 body's natural weight 定义 as "set"——not "variable" but "set" (fixed)。"fight attempts to shift our weight too far away"将 dieting 定义 as "fighting biology"——not "healthy" but "fighting"。This "hardwired to fight" is Leslie's anti-diet thesis: dieting is not "willpower" but "fighting biology"。Leslie uses scientific language to legitimize anti-diet position。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "hardwired to fight" 与 Mom's "Nothing tastes as good as thin feels" (ch04) 形成 direct confrontation——Mom defines thinness as "willpower" (thin feels good)，Leslie defines thinness as "biology" (hardwired)。This confrontation is the anti-diet theme's core tension: willpower vs. biology。

---

> "Shit, people! Don't you see? Dieting is all such fucking bullshit!"

**中文理解**："Shit，人们！你们没看到吗？节食全是 fucking bullshit！"

**关键词**：Shit, people!（Shit，人们！）/ Don't you see?（你们没看到吗？）/ Dieting is all such fucking bullshit!（节食全是 fucking bullshit！）

**为什么这样写**："Shit, people!"将 Leslie's outburst 定义 as "profanity-laced"——not "polished" but "raw"。"Don't you see?"将 Leslie's frustration 定义 as "urgency"——not "calm" but "don't you see?" (desperate). "Dieting is all such fucking bullshit!"将 Leslie's position 定义 as "absolute rejection"——not "questioning" but "bullshit" (complete dismissal)。This "Dieting is all such fucking bullshit!" is Leslie's anti-diet manifesto: she rejects diet culture entirely, not just questions it。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "Dieting is all such fucking bullshit!" 与 "my mom was a fucking cult leader" (ch16) 形成 parallel——both use profanity to reject diet culture. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's emotional peak: Leslie's rejection of diet culture is not intellectual but visceral/emotional。

---

> "We eat."

**中文理解**："我们吃。"

**关键词**：We eat.（我们吃。）

**为什么这样写**："We eat."将 Leslie's answer 定义 as "simple"——not "complex strategy" but "we eat" (one word)。This "We eat." is Leslie's anti-diet manifesto's conclusion: the answer to diet culture is not "another diet" but "eating"。"We eat."的 simplicity is its power: one word, two letters, complete rejection of diet culture's complexity。Dot's question "So what do we do now?" (same chapter) is the practical prompt that leads to Leslie's "We eat."——the answer to diet culture is not "another diet" but "eating"。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "We eat." 与 "But first, food. Suddenly I felt very hungry." (ch16) 形成 parallel——both define food as "answer" not "problem"。This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's core message: food is not the enemy but the solution。
