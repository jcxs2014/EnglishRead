## 精读

> "I want us to be together. I've wanted it for years, but have been too afraid, or proud, or stubborn to try again."

**中文理解**："我想要我们在一起。我多年来一直想要，但太害怕、太骄傲、或太 stubborn 才没再试。"

**关键词**：I want us to be together（我想要我们在一起）/ I've wanted it for years（我多年来一直想要）/ afraid, proud, stubborn（害怕、骄傲、stubborn）

**为什么这样写**："I want us to be together"将 Leslie's desire 定义为 "direct"——not "hinting" but "direct"。"I've wanted it for years"将 Leslie's desire 定义 as "long-standing"——not "recent" but "years"。"afraid, proud, or stubborn"将 Leslie's barriers 定义 as "three-fold"——not "one reason" but "afraid, proud, stubborn"。This "I want us to be together... afraid, proud, stubborn" is Leslie's commitment confession: she acknowledges her own barriers (fear, pride, stubbornness) and chooses to overcome them。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "I want us to be together" 与 Risto's "I still love you" (ch19) 形成 mutual commitment——both partners commit to being together. This mutual commitment is the romance arc's turning point: from "avoidance" to "commitment"。

> "My career may take off a bit. I won't lie and say I'm not thrilled about it. But it's no different than you being on TV or streaming to millions on YouTube. Me pursuing my dreams doesn't mean you can't be a part of it. You are as much a dream of mine as growing the restaurant."

**中文理解**："我的事业可能会起飞一点。我不会撒谎说我不 thrilled about it。但这与你在电视上或在 YouTube 上直播给数百万人看没什么不同。我追求我的梦想并不意味着你不能成为其中的一部分。你和扩大餐厅一样，都是我的梦想。"

**关键词**：career may take off（事业可能会起飞）/ You are as much a dream of mine as growing the restaurant（你和扩大餐厅一样，都是我的梦想）

**为什么这样写**："My career may take off"将 Risto's ambition 定义 as "possible"——not "certain" but "may"。"You are as much a dream of mine as growing the restaurant"将 Leslie 定义 as "dream"——not "obstacle" but "dream" (equal to career)。"as much a dream... as growing the restaurant"将 Leslie 与 career 定义为 "equal dreams"——not "career first" but "equal"。This "You are as much a dream of mine as growing the restaurant" is Risto's commitment confession: Leslie is not "obstacle to career" but "dream equal to career"。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "You are as much a dream of mine as growing the restaurant" 与 Leslie's "I want us to be together" (same chapter) 形成 mutual commitment——both partners commit to each other AND career. This mutual commitment resolves the romance arc's core tension: career vs. love. Risto chooses both。

---

> "Right where she belonged. And where she'd always stay."

**中文理解**："就在她属于的地方。而且她永远都会待在那里。"

**关键词**：Right where she belonged（就在她属于的地方）/ where she'd always stay（她永远都会待在那里）

**为什么这样写**："Right where she belonged"将 Leslie 定义 as "belonging"——not "visiting" but "belonging" (permanent)。"where she'd always stay"将 Leslie's presence 定义 as "permanent"——not "temporary" but "always"。This "Right where she belonged. And where she'd always stay." is the romance arc's resolution: Leslie belongs with Risto, permanently. The "always stay" is the romance genre's "happily ever after" promise。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "Right where she belonged. And where she'd always stay." 与 Leslie's "I wanted so much more than what I'd been allowing myself" (ch23) 形成 mutual fulfillment——Leslie wants "more" (ch23)，Risto provides "belonging" (ch25)。This mutual fulfillment is the romance arc's resolution: both partners get what they want (Leslie gets love, Risto gets Leslie)。
