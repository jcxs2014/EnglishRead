## 精读

> "Count me in. I'll start tomorrow."

**中文理解**："算我一个。我明天开始。"

**关键词**：Count me in（算我一个）/ I'll start tomorrow（我明天开始）

**为什么这样写**："Count me in"将 Leslie's commitment 定义为 "joining"——not "observing" but "joining" (participating)。"I'll start tomorrow"将 Leslie's action 定义 as "deferred"——not "now" but "tomorrow"，suggesting Leslie is ready but needs time to prepare. This "Count me in. I'll start tomorrow." is Leslie's career arc's action turn: from "researcher" to "activist"。Leslie commits to sharing her personal food journey with her 44,000 YouTube followers。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "I'll start tomorrow" 与 "I'll start tomorrow." (ch04, Mom's voice) 形成 echo——Mom's "I'll start tomorrow" was about dieting, Leslie's "I'll start tomorrow" is about activism. This echo reveals the anti-diet theme's reversal: Leslie repurposes Mom's "tomorrow" (diet) for her own "tomorrow" (activism)。
