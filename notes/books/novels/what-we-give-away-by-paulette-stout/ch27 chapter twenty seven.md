## 精读

> "I'm proud of me too."

**中文理解**："我也为我自己骄傲。"

**关键词**：proud of me too（也为我自己骄傲）

**为什么这样写**："I'm proud of me too"将 Leslie's self-affirmation 定义 as "self-directed"——not "proud of others" but "proud of me"。"too"用 "too" 强调 Leslie's pride is "additional"——not "only Dot's pride" but "also my own"。This "I'm proud of me too" is Leslie's anti-diet awakening's self-affirmation: from "I'm proud of me too" (self-directed) to "I'm proud of me too" (self-acceptance)。Leslie affirms her own progress, not just Dot's approval。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "I'm proud of me too" 与 "I'm proud of you" (Dot, same chapter) 形成 mutual pride——Dot is "proud of you"，Leslie is "proud of me too"。This mutual pride is the anti-diet theme's emotional peak: Leslie internalizes self-affirmation, not just external validation。

---

> "Food shopping can be scary. For some, it's akin to being let loose in a candy shop. But the point is to build a new relationship with food, one where you take back the control and make decisions for yourself without guilt or fear."

**中文理解**："食品购物可能很可怕。对有些人来说，就像被放进了糖果店。但关键是建立与食物的新关系，一个你夺回控制权、为自己做决定、没有内疚或恐惧的关系。"

**关键词**：Food shopping can be scary（食品购物可能很可怕）/ akin to being let loose in a candy shop（就像被放进了糖果店）/ take back the control（夺回控制权）/ without guilt or fear（没有内疚或恐惧）

**为什么这样写**："Food shopping can be scary"将 grocery shopping 定义 as "fearful"——not "routine" but "scary"。"akin to being let loose in a candy shop"将 grocery shopping 定义 as "overwhelming"——not "simple" but "candy shop" (temptation overload)。"take back the control"将 food relationship 定义 as "control"——not "restriction" but "control" (agency)。"without guilt or fear"将 food relationship 定义 as "liberated"——not "anxious" but "without guilt or fear"。This "take back the control... without guilt or fear" is Dot's core advice: food relationship should be "empowered" not "anxious"。

**读者视角提示**：Dot's "take back the control... without guilt or fear" 与 Mom's "Nothing tastes as good as thin feels" (ch04) 形成 direct confrontation——Mom defines food as "guilt" (thin feels good)，Dot defines food as "control without guilt"。This confrontation is the anti-diet theme's core tension: food as guilt vs. food as control without guilt。

---

> "I was screwed. We were only standing in the produce section. What would happen when we got to the cookie aisle?"

**中文理解**："我完了。我们才站在农产品区。当我们到达饼干通道时会发生什么？"

**关键词**：I was screwed（我完了）/ produce section（农产品区）/ cookie aisle（饼干通道）

**为什么这样写**："I was screwed"将 Leslie's anxiety 定义 as "doomed"——not "nervous" but "screwed" (defeated)。"We were only standing in the produce section"将 grocery shopping 定义 as "progressive"——not "simple" but "only... produce section" (beginning)。"What would happen when we got to the cookie aisle?"将 Leslie's anxiety 定义 as "escalating"——not "constant" but "when we got to" (future fear)。This "I was screwed... produce section... cookie aisle" is Leslie's anti-diet awakening's anxiety arc: from "produce section" (safe) to "cookie aisle" (danger)。Leslie's anxiety escalates as she moves through the store, revealing her food anxiety's spatial dimension。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "produce section → cookie aisle" anxiety 与 "I'm proud of me too" (same chapter) 形成 arc: from "screwed" (anxiety) to "proud" (self-affirmation)。This arc is the anti-diet theme's progression: from "fear" to "self-affirmation"。
