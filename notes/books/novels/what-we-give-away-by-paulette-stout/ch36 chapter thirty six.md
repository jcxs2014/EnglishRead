## 精读

> "Curvy people are better off somewhere else."

**中文理解**："曲线型的人最好去别的地方。"

**关键词**：Curvy people（曲线型的人）/ better off somewhere else（最好去别的地方）

**为什么这样写**："Curvy people are better off somewhere else"将 salesperson 的 discrimination 定义 as "exclusion"——not "inclusion" but "better off somewhere else" (exclusion)。"Curvy people"用 "curvy" 定义 Leslie's body as "curvy"——not "fat" but "curvy" (euphemism)。This "Curvy people are better off somewhere else" is the anti-diet theme's institutional turn: anti-fat bias exists not just in "personal" (Mom) but in "institutional" (retail, network)。Leslie's shopping trip reveals anti-fat bias is systemic, not individual。

**读者视角提示**：Salesperson's "Curvy people are better off somewhere else" 与 Mom's "Nothing tastes as good as thin feels" (ch04) 形成 parallel——both exclude "curvy" bodies: Mom through "mantra"，salesperson through "store policy"。This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's broader application: body size regulation exists in both "personal" (family) and "institutional" (retail, network) domains。

---

> "But if they thought I'd go away quietly, they were sadly mistaken."

**中文理解**："但如果他们以为我会安静地离开，那就大错特错了。"

**关键词**：But if they thought（但如果他们以为）/ go away quietly（安静地离开）/ sadly mistaken（大错特错）

**为什么这样写**："But if they thought"将 Leslie's resistance 定义 as "conditional"——not "automatic" but "if they thought" (conditional on their assumption)。"go away quietly"将 network's expectation 定义 as "silent departure"——not "fighting" but "quietly" (silent)。"sadly mistaken"将 Leslie's resistance 定义 as "definitive"——not "maybe" but "sadly mistaken" (definitive)。This "But if they thought I'd go away quietly, they were sadly mistaken" is Leslie's anti-diet activism's institutional turn: she refuses to silently accept institutional body size regulation。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "But if they thought I'd go away quietly" 与 "I'm not going away quietly" (ch35) 形成 parallel——both declare resistance to institutional body size regulation. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's institutional turn: Leslie resists both personal (Mom) and institutional (network) body size regulation。
