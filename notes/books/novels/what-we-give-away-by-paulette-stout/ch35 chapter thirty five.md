## 精读

> "I began sneaking peeks in mirrors, skipping meals, and increasing the length of my dog walks with Pepper."

**中文理解**："我开始偷偷窥视镜子，跳过餐点，增加和 Pepper 的遛狗时长。"

**关键词**：sneaking peeks in mirrors（偷偷窥视镜子）/ skipping meals（跳过餐点）/ increasing the length of my dog walks（增加遛狗时长）

**为什么这样写**："sneaking peeks in mirrors"将 Leslie's relapse 定义 as "secret"——not "open" but "sneaking" (hidden)。"skipping meals"将 Leslie's relapse 定义 as "restriction"——not "eating" but "skipping" (avoiding)。"increasing the length of my dog walks"将 Leslie's relapse 定义 as "compensatory"——not "exercise for health" but "increasing" (more to burn calories)。This "sneaking peeks in mirrors, skipping meals, increasing dog walks" is Leslie's relapse pattern: mirror-checking (body monitoring) + meal-skipping (restriction) + exercise-increasing (compensation)。Leslie's relapse reveals her anti-diet awakening's fragility: even after progress, old patterns resurface under stress。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's relapse 与 Tasha's "How have you been since the ordeal with your mother?" (same chapter) 形成 confrontation——Tasha sees through Leslie's "Okay. Good." (denial)。This confrontation reveals the anti-diet theme's recovery reality: relapse is part of recovery, not failure。

> "I have nothing to wear to meet them. My old clothes will be too small."

**中文理解**："我没有衣服可以穿去见他们。我的旧衣服会太小。"

**关键词**：nothing to wear（没有衣服可以穿）/ My old clothes will be too small（我的旧衣服会太小）

**为什么这样写**："nothing to wear"将 Leslie's clothing crisis 定义 as "crisis"——not "inconvenience" but "nothing to wear" (crisis)。"My old clothes will be too small"将 Leslie's body change 定义 as "growth"——not "gain" but "too small" (her old clothes no longer fit because her body has grown)。This "I have nothing to wear... My old clothes will be too small" is Leslie's anti-diet awakening's material evidence: her body has changed (grown) and her old clothes (thin body) no longer fit. Leslie's clothing crisis reveals her body's transformation: from "thin" to "curvy"。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "My old clothes will be too small" 与 "I'd been the same weight for years" (ch36) 形成 parallel——Leslie's body has changed (grown) and her old clothes (thin body) no longer fit. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's material dimension: body change is visible in clothing, not just in mirrors。

> "I was. Before. They never cared when I was starving myself."

**中文理解**："我以前是。在我饿死自己的时候，他们从不关心。"

**关键词**：I was. Before.（我以前是。）/ They never cared（他们从不关心）/ when I was starving myself（在我饿死自己的时候）

**为什么这样写**："I was. Before."将 Leslie's prior state 定义 as "past"——not "current" but "before" (past)。"They never cared when I was starving myself"将 network's hypocrisy 定义 as "indifferent"——not "concerned" but "never cared" (indifferent to her starvation)。This "I was. Before. They never cared when I was starving myself" is Leslie's anti-diet activism's institutional critique: the network only cares about her weight gain (not her starvation)。Leslie exposes institutional hypocrisy: the network cared about her "weight gain" (not her "starvation")。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "They never cared when I was starving myself" 与 "They sat there, insulting me to my face like it's a perfectly normal activity" (ch36) 形成 parallel——both expose institutional hypocrisy: the network only cares about "weight gain" (not "starvation")。This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's institutional turn: Leslie exposes institutional body size regulation as hypocritical。
