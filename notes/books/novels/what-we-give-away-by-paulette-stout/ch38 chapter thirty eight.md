## 精读

> "They screwed with the wrong gal."

**中文理解**："他们惹错了人。"

**关键词**：screwed with the wrong gal（惹错了人）

**为什么这样写**："They screwed with the wrong gal"将 Leslie's activism 定义 as "defiant"——not "passive" but "screwed with the wrong" (defiant)。"wrong gal"将 Leslie 定义 as "wrong target"——not "vulnerable" but "wrong gal" (they picked the wrong person to mess with)。This "They screwed with the wrong gal" is Leslie's anti-diet activism's defiant turn: the network picked the wrong person to fire. Leslie's defiance parallels her "But if they thought I'd go away quietly, they were sadly mistaken" (ch36)——both declare resistance to institutional body size regulation。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "They screwed with the wrong gal" 与 "But if they thought I'd go away quietly, they were sadly mistaken" (ch36) 形成 parallel——both declare defiance against institutional body size regulation. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's activist turn: Leslie refuses to accept institutional discrimination quietly。

> "Operation Fat Justice."

**中文理解**："Fat Justice 行动。"

**关键词**：Operation Fat Justice（Fat Justice 行动）

**为什么这样写**："Operation Fat Justice"将 Leslie's movement 定义 as "military operation"——not "protest" but "Operation" (military framing)。"Fat Justice"将 movement 定义 as "justice"——not "complaint" but "justice" (moral claim)。This "Operation Fat Justice" is Leslie's anti-diet activism's naming: she frames anti-fat bias as injustice requiring justice. Leslie's naming parallels her "If people only knew. This nastiness festers because it's kept secret" (ch37)——both frame anti-fat bias as systemic injustice requiring exposure。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "Operation Fat Justice" 与 "If people only knew. This nastiness festers because it's kept secret" (ch37) 形成 parallel——both frame anti-fat bias as systemic injustice. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's public turn: Leslie mobilizes public action against institutional body size regulation。

> "I wanted to apologize. It's long overdue, and I've been a coward for not coming to you sooner."

**中文理解**："我想道歉。这早就该做了，我 cowardly 地没有早点来找你。"

**关键词**：I wanted to apologize（我想道歉）/ It's long overdue（这早就该做了）/ I've been a coward（我 cowardly 地）

**为什么这样写**："I wanted to apologize"将 Dad 定义 as "accountable"——not "defensive" but "apologize" (taking responsibility)。"It's long overdue"将 Dad's apology 定义 as "delayed"——not "timely" but "long overdue" (too late)。"I've been a coward"将 Dad 定义 as "cowardly"——not "brave" but "coward" (self-critique)。This "I wanted to apologize" is Dad's parenting accountability turn: he finally takes responsibility for not protecting Leslie. Dad's apology parallels Leslie's "They screwed with the wrong gal"——both take agency (Dad takes responsibility, Leslie takes action)。

**读者视角提示**：Dad's "I wanted to apologize" 与 Leslie's "They screwed with the wrong gal" 形成 parallel——both take agency (Dad takes responsibility for past inaction, Leslie takes action for future justice). This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's generational turn: both father and daughter reject passive acceptance (Dad's past cowardice / network's discrimination)。

> "I'd been learning about the medical industry and their twisted habit of praising patients exhibiting disordered systems because the clinicians relied on BMI charts."

**中文理解**："我一直在学习医疗行业及其 twisted 习惯——因临床医生依赖 BMI 图表而 praising 展现 disordered systems 的患者。"

**关键词**：twisted habit（twisted 习惯）/ praising patients（praising 患者）/ disordered systems（disordered systems）/ BMI charts（BMI 图表）

**为什么这样写**："twisted habit"将 medical industry 定义 as "twisted"——not "neutral" but "twisted" (corrupt)。"praising patients"将 medical industry 定义 as "praising"——not "treating" but "praising" (encouraging disordered eating)。"because the clinicians relied on BMI charts"将 cause 定义 as "BMI reliance"——not "science" but "BMI charts" (flawed metric)。This "twisted habit of praising patients exhibiting disordered systems" is Leslie's medical critique: the medical industry enables disordered eating by praising thinness (BMI charts)。Leslie's critique parallels her "Thin was good, fat was bad" (ch38)——both expose medical industry's fatphobia。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "twisted habit of praising patients exhibiting disordered systems" 与 "Thin was good, fat was bad" 形成 parallel——both expose medical industry's fatphobia. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's medical turn: Leslie exposes how BMI charts enable disordered eating。
