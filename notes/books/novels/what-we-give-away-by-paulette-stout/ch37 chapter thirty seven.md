## 精读

> "I'd become too hungry to cook."

**中文理解**："我变得太饿了，无法烹饪。"

**关键词**：too hungry to cook（太饿了，无法烹饪）

**为什么这样写**："too hungry to cook"将 Risto's diet 定义 as "incapacitating"——not "uncomfortable" but "too hungry to cook" (unable to perform)。"too... to"结构将 Risto's hunger 定义 as "limiting"——not "manageable" but "too" (excessive)。This "too hungry to cook" is Risto's anti-diet realization: Brock's diet plan has incapacitated him as a chef. Risto's "too hungry to cook" parallels Leslie's "I'm more afraid of eating than not eating" (ch11)——both partners' relationship with food is "damaged" by diet culture (Brock's diet / Mom's dogma)。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "too hungry to cook" 与 Leslie's "I'm more afraid of eating than not eating" (ch11) 形成 parallel——both partners' relationship with food is "damaged" by diet culture. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's couple-level resolution: both partners reject body size regulation (Risto rejects Brock's BMI clause, Leslie rejects Mom's diet dogma)。

---

> "Food is love. Food is life. Food is family."

**中文理解**："食物是爱。食物是生命。食物是家庭。"

**关键词**：Food is love（食物是爱）/ Food is life（食物是生命）/ Food is family（食物是家庭）

**为什么这样写**："Food is love"将 food 定义 as "love"——not "nutrition" but "love"。"Food is life"将 food 定义 as "life"——not "survival" but "life" (thriving)。"Food is family"将 food 定义 as "family"——not "individual" but "family" (community)。This "Food is love. Food is life. Food is family." is Ruben's anti-diet wisdom: food is not "threat" but "love, life, family"。Ruben's wisdom parallels Risto's "love in a dish" (ch09) and Leslie's "Food was medicine, joy, pleasure, and life" (ch31)——all define food as "love"。

**读者视角提示**：Ruben's "Food is love. Food is life. Food is family." 与 Leslie's "Food was medicine, joy, pleasure, and life all rolled into one" (ch31) 形成 parallel——both define food as "love/life" not "threat"。This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's mutual adoption: both partners define food as "love" (Risto through cooking, Leslie through eating)。

---

> "If people only knew. This nastiness festers because it's kept secret."

**中文理解**："如果人们只知道。这种 nastiness 因为被保密而 festers。"

**关键词**：If people only knew（如果人们只知道）/ This nastiness festers（这种 nastiness festers）/ because it's kept secret（因为它被保密）

**为什么这样写**："If people only knew"将 Leslie's activism 定义 as "revelation"——not "hidden" but "if people only knew" (public knowledge)。"This nastiness festers"将 anti-fat bias 定义 as "festering"——not "occasional" but "festers" (growing in darkness)。"because it's kept secret"将 anti-fat bias 定义 as "secret"——not "public" but "kept secret" (hidden)。This "If people only knew. This nastiness festers because it's kept secret." is Leslie's anti-diet activism's public turn: she wants to expose anti-fat bias (kept secret) to public knowledge。Leslie's activism parallels Risto's "Food is love. Food is life. Food is family." (Ruben's wisdom)——both reject secrecy (anti-fat bias hidden in contracts)。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "If people only knew" 与 Risto's "I'd become too hungry to cook" (same chapter) 形成 parallel——both partners reject secrecy (Brock's BMI clause / network's weight clause) through public exposure (Leslie's activism / Risto's rejection of Brock's diet)。This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's couple-level resolution: both partners expose anti-fat bias publicly。
