## 精读

> "You and me, Mom, we're sick. We have eating disorders."

**中文理解**："你和我，妈妈，我们病了。我们有饮食失调。"

**关键词**：You and me, Mom（你和我，妈妈）/ we're sick（我们病了）/ We have eating disorders（我们有饮食失调）

**为什么这样写**："You and me, Mom"将 Leslie's confrontation 定义 as "inclusive"——not "you" (accusatory) but "you and me" (shared condition)。"we're sick"将 Leslie's confrontation 定义 as "diagnosis"——not "accusation" but "sick" (medical condition)。"We have eating disorders"将 Leslie's confrontation 定义 as "shared diagnosis"——not "your problem" but "our problem" (shared)。This "You and me, Mom, we're sick. We have eating disorders." is Leslie's anti-diet awakening's confrontational turn: she names Mom's eating disorder alongside her own, revealing the intergenerational transmission of diet culture。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "You and me, Mom, we're sick" 与 Mom's "I just wanted you to be healthy" (same chapter) 形成 direct confrontation——Mom defines her actions as "health"，Leslie defines them as "sickness" (eating disorder)。This confrontation is the anti-diet theme's intergenerational turn: Leslie names Mom's eating disorder, not just her own。

---

> "My mother stood alone, clinging to her toxic beliefs as if they had the power to save her."

**中文理解**："我的母亲独自站着，紧抓着她的 toxic beliefs，仿佛它们有拯救她的力量。"

**关键词**：stood alone（独自站着）/ clinging to her toxic beliefs（紧抓着她的 toxic beliefs）/ as if they had the power to save her（仿佛它们有拯救她的力量）

**为什么这样写**："stood alone"将 Mom 定义 as "isolated"——not "supported" but "alone"。"clinging to her toxic beliefs"将 Mom 定义 as "desperate"——not "confident" but "clinging" (desperate hold)。"as if they had the power to save her"将 Mom's beliefs 定义 as "false salvation"——not "true" but "as if" (false)。This "stood alone, clinging to her toxic beliefs" is Leslie's anti-diet awakening's resolution: she sees Mom's diet beliefs as "toxic" not "salvation"。Leslie's "as if they had the power to save her" reveals Leslie's insight: Mom's diet beliefs are "false salvation" (toxic) not "true health"。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "clinging to her toxic beliefs" 与 "I'll expect you to send money to replace my broken phone and torn clothes" (same chapter) 形成 contrast——Leslie confronts Mom's toxic beliefs but also asserts practical demands (phone replacement, groceries)。This contrast reveals Leslie's transformation: from "victim" to "assertive"。
