## 精读

> "I think I have an eating disorder. Anorexia."

**中文理解**："我觉得我有饮食失调。厌食症。"

**关键词**：eating disorder（饮食失调）/ Anorexia（厌食症）

**为什么这样写**："I think I have an eating disorder"将 Leslie's self-diagnosis 定义为 "tentative"——not "I know" but "I think"，suggesting Leslie is still processing, not certain. "Anorexia"用 single word 将 condition 定义为 "named"——not "vague problem" but "specific diagnosis"。This "I think I have an eating disorder. Anorexia."的 two-sentence structure mirrors Leslie's cognitive process: first "I think I have" (tentative), then "Anorexia" (specific). This is Leslie's anti-diet awakening's personal turn: from "researcher" to "patient"。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "I think I have an eating disorder. Anorexia." 与 "I'm more afraid of eating than not eating" (ch11) 形成 arc: from "fear" (ch11) to "diagnosis" (ch18)。This arc is the anti-diet theme's progression: from "food anxiety" to "medical condition"。

---

> "Untreated anorexia nervosa has the highest mortality rate of any mental illness."

**中文理解**："未经治疗的神经性厌食症是所有精神疾病中死亡率最高的。"

**关键词**：Untreated anorexia nervosa（未经治疗的神经性厌食症）/ highest mortality rate（最高死亡率）/ any mental illness（任何精神疾病）

**为什么这样写**："Untreated"将 condition 定义为 "treatable"——not "fatal" but "untreated" (implies treatment exists)。"highest mortality rate of any mental illness"将 anorexia 定义为 "most dangerous" mental illness——not "one of many" but "highest"。This "highest mortality rate" is Tasha's warning: Leslie's condition is life-threatening, not just "personal issue"。"Untreated anorexia nervosa has the highest mortality rate of any mental illness"的 "any mental illness" 将 anorexia 定义为 "worse than all others"——not "comparable" but "highest"。

**读者视角提示**：Tasha's "highest mortality rate" 与 Leslie's "I don't want to die. I promise" (same chapter) 形成 tension——Tasha warns of death, Leslie promises to live. This tension is the anti-diet theme's personal stakes: Leslie's condition is life-threatening, not just "food issue"。

---

> "There was only one person I could talk to. And his name was Risto."

**中文理解**："只有一个人我可以倾诉。他的名字叫 Risto。"

**关键词**：only one person（只有一个人）/ his name was Risto（他的名字叫 Risto）

**为什么这样写**："only one person"将 Leslie's support system 定义为 "singular"——not "many" but "one"。"his name was Risto"用 "his name was"（他的名字叫）定义 Risto as "identified"——not "some person" but "Risto" (named)。This "only one person... Risto" is Leslie's personal crisis resolution: despite food anxiety and relationship conflict, Risto is Leslie's "only" support. This reveals Leslie's true feeling: Risto is not "ex-boyfriend" but "only person" she can talk to。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "only one person... Risto" 与 Risto's "I will be here, by your side, for as long as you want me" (ch19) 形成 mutual commitment——both partners commit to being there for each other. This mutual commitment is the romance arc's turning point: from "avoidance" to "support"。
