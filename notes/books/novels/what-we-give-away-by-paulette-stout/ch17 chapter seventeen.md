## 精读

> "Someone like him? What did my size have to do with my cooking?"

**中文理解**："Someone like him?我的体型与我的烹饪有什么关系？"

**关键词**：Someone like him（Someone like him）/ What did my size have to do with my cooking?（我的体型与我的烹饪有什么关系？）

**为什么这样写**："Someone like him?"将 investor 的 comment 定义为 "coded racism"——not "direct insult" but "someone like him" (euphemism for "fat/brown man")。"What did my size have to do with my cooking?"将 Risto's reaction 定义为 "logical rebuttal"——not "emotional" but "logical"，Risto challenges the irrelevance of body size to culinary skill。This "size ≠ cooking skill" is Risto's core belief: his body (size/race) should not determine his professional worth. But the investor's comment reveals that in the culinary industry, body size IS a factor in "relatability" and "marketability"。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "What did my size have to do with my cooking?" 与 Tasha's "There is no litmus test of worthiness to enjoy it, least of all weight" (ch14) 形成 parallel——both challenge the notion that body size determines professional worth. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's broader application: body size should not determine access to nature, health, OR professional opportunity。

> "Love was everywhere. But not for me."

**中文理解**："爱无处不在。但不属于我。"

**关键词**：Love was everywhere（爱无处不在）/ But not for me（但不属于我）

**为什么这样写**："Love was everywhere"将 Risto's observation 定义为 "ubiquitous"——not "occasional" but "everywhere"。"But not for me"将 Risto's exclusion 定义为 "personal"——not "universal" but "personal"。This "love everywhere but not for me" is Risto's emotional state: he sees love (couple feeding each other, kissing) but feels excluded from it. "Love was everywhere. But not for me."的 "But"（但是）将 Risto's observation 定义为 "contrast"——not "love doesn't exist" but "love exists but not for me"。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "Love was everywhere. But not for me." 与 Leslie's "I cared for someone who wanted nothing to do with me" (ch11) 形成 mutual exclusion——both feel excluded from love despite evidence of mutual desire. This mutual "exclusion" is the romance genre's "denial" setup: both feel unloved despite mutual desire。

> "Why did she have to come back? I'd finally patched my broken heart, a sloppy job to be sure with duct tape and gum."

**中文理解**："她为什么非得回来？我终于修补好了我破碎的心，虽然修补得 sloppy，用 duct tape 和 gum。"

**关键词**：patched my broken heart（修补好了我破碎的心）/ sloppy job（sloppy 修补）/ duct tape and gum（duct tape 和 gum）

**为什么这样写**："patched my broken heart"将 Risto's healing 定义为 "patch"——not "healed" but "patched" (temporary fix)。"a sloppy job to be sure"将 Risto's healing 定义 as "imperfect"——not "complete" but "sloppy"。"with duct tape and gum"将 Risto's healing 定义 as "makeshift"——not "professional" but "duct tape and gum" (improvised materials)。This "sloppy patch" reveals Risto's true state: he hasn't healed, just "patched" his broken heart with makeshift materials. Leslie's return "unpatches" the wound。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "patched my broken heart with duct tape and gum" 与 Leslie's "I cared for someone who wanted nothing to do with me" (ch11) 形成 mutual vulnerability——both have "patched" hearts that are easily "unpatched" by proximity. This mutual "patched heart" is the romance genre's "vulnerability" setup: both partners have fragile emotional defenses that proximity threatens。
