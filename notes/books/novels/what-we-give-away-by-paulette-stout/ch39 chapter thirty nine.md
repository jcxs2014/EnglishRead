## 精读

> "What we give away is power. What we give away is agency. What we give away is the freedom to live life on our own terms."

**中文理解**："我们 give away 的是 power。我们 give away 的是 agency。我们 give away 的是 on our own terms 生活的 freedom。"

**关键词**：What we give away is power（我们 give away 的是 power）/ What we give away is agency（我们 give away 的是 agency）/ What we give away is the freedom to live life on our own terms（我们 give away 的是 on our own terms 生活的 freedom）

**为什么这样写**："What we give away is power"将 diet culture 定义 as "power loss"——not "health" but "power" (agency taken away)。"What we give away is agency"将 diet culture 定义 as "agency loss"——not "discipline" but "agency" (autonomy taken away)。"What we give away is the freedom to live life on our own terms"将 diet culture 定义 as "freedom loss"——not "control" but "freedom" (autonomy taken away)。This "What we give away is power. What we give away is agency. What we give away is the freedom to live life on our own terms" is Leslie's anti-diet manifesto: diet culture takes away power, agency, freedom. Leslie's manifesto parallels her "They screwed with the wrong gal" (ch38)——both declare resistance to institutional body size regulation。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "What we give away is power" 与 "They screwed with the wrong gal" (ch38) 形成 parallel——both declare resistance to institutional body size regulation. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's public turn: Leslie mobilizes public action against institutional body size regulation。

> "Us curvy gals gotta stick together."

**中文理解**："我们 curvy gals gotta stick together。"

**关键词**：Us curvy gals（我们 curvy gals）/ gotta stick together（gotta stick together）

**为什么这样写**："Us curvy gals"将 Mo'nique 定义 as "community"——not "individual" but "us" (collective)。"gotta stick together"将 Mo'nique 定义 as "solidarity"——not "alone" but "stick together" (solidarity)。This "Us curvy gals gotta stick together" is Mo'nique's anti-diet solidarity: curvy people must support each other. Mo'nique's solidarity parallels Leslie's "What we give away is power"——both frame anti-fat bias as collective struggle requiring solidarity。

**读者视角提示**：Mo'nique's "Us curvy gals gotta stick together" 与 Leslie's "What we give away is power" 形成 parallel——both frame anti-fat bias as collective struggle. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's solidarity turn: both partners (Leslie/Mo'nique) reject individual shame for collective action。

> "I challenge him to an on-air debate."

**中文理解**："我 challenge 他 to an on-air debate。"

**关键词**：I challenge him（我 challenge 他）/ to an on-air debate（to an on-air debate）

**为什么这样写**："I challenge him"将 Leslie 定义 as "challenger"——not "victim" but "challenge" (active)。"to an on-air debate"将 Leslie's demand 定义 as "public"——not "private" but "on-air" (public accountability)。This "I challenge him to an on-air debate" is Leslie's anti-diet activism's public demand: she demands Reed publicly account for his behavior. **读者视角提示**：Leslie's "I challenge him to an on-air debate" 与 "I challenge him to an on-air debate" 形成 parallel——both demand public accountability. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's accountability turn: Leslie demands public reckoning for anti-fat bias。
