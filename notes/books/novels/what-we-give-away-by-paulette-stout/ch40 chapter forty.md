## 精读

> "I no longer recognized myself."

**中文理解**："我 no longer recognized myself。"

**关键词**：I no longer recognized myself（我 no longer recognized myself）

**为什么这样写**："I no longer recognized myself"将 Risto 定义 as "unrecognizable"——not "same" but "no longer recognized" (lost identity)。"myself"将 Risto 定义 as "self"——not "other" but "myself" (lost self)。This "I no longer recognized myself" is Risto's self-recognition crisis: Brock's diet has made him unrecognizable to himself. Risto's crisis parallels Leslie's "I'd been the same person they hired, only more in every way" (ch39)——both partners lose themselves to diet culture and recover。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "I no longer recognized myself" 与 Leslie's "I'd been the same person they hired, only more in every way" (ch39) 形成 parallel——both partners lose themselves to diet culture and recover. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's self-recognition turn: both partners reject diet culture's erasure of self。

> "Why was I risking it all to starve myself?"

**中文理解**："为什么 I was risking it all to starve myself?"

**关键词**：Why was I risking it all（为什么 I was risking it all）/ to starve myself（to starve myself）

**为什么这样写**："Why was I risking it all"将 Risto 定义 as "questioning"——not "accepting" but "why" (questioning)。"to starve myself"将 Risto 定义 as "starving"——not "nourishing" but "starve" (self-deprivation)。This "Why was I risking it all to starve myself?" is Risto's self-questioning: he questions why he's risking his career to starve himself. Risto's questioning parallels Leslie's "I was no longer starving myself" (ch39)——both partners question diet culture's cost。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "Why was I risking it all to starve myself?" 与 Leslie's "I was no longer starving myself" (ch39) 形成 parallel——both partners question diet culture's cost. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's questioning turn: both partners reject diet culture's self-deprivation。

> "I didn't have to choose."

**中文理解**："I didn't have to choose。"

**关键词**：I didn't have to choose（I didn't have to choose）

**为什么这样写**："I didn't have to choose"将 Risto 定义 as "free"——not "limited" but "didn't have to" (freedom from binary)。"choose"将 Risto 定义 as "choosing"——not "forced" but "choose" (agency)。This "I didn't have to choose" is Risto's menu realization: he can be both sophisticated and humble, innovative and traditional. Risto's realization parallels Leslie's "What we give away is power. What we give away is agency" (ch39)——both reclaim agency (Risto reclaims menu agency, Leslie reclaims body agency)。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "I didn't have to choose" 与 Leslie's "What we give away is power. What we give away is agency" (ch39) 形成 parallel——both reclaim agency. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's agency turn: both partners reclaim agency from diet culture (Risto reclaims menu agency, Leslie reclaims body agency)。

> "All of me would go into this restaurant."

**中文理解**："All of me would go into this restaurant。"

**关键词**：All of me（All of me）/ would go into this restaurant（would go into this restaurant）

**为什么这样写**："All of me"将 Risto 定义 as "whole"——not "partial" but "all" (whole self)。"would go into this restaurant"将 Risto 定义 as "authentic"——not "performed" but "go into" (authentic self)。This "All of me would go into this restaurant" is Risto's authentic self declaration: he commits to being his whole self in his restaurant. Risto's declaration parallels Leslie's "I was no longer starving myself" (ch39)——both commit to being their whole selves (Risto commits to authentic cooking, Leslie commits to authentic body)。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "All of me would go into this restaurant" 与 Leslie's "I was no longer starving myself" (ch39) 形成 parallel——both commit to being their whole selves. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's authenticity turn: both partners reject diet culture's erasure of self for authentic living。
