**为什么这样写**："We've all been fed"将 Leslie 定义 as "collective"——not "individual" but "we've all" (collective)。"a steady diet of lies"将 diet culture 定义 as "lies"——not "truth" but "lies" (deception)。This "We've all been fed a steady diet of lies" is Leslie's closing statement: diet culture is lies fed to everyone. Leslie's closing parallels her "What we give away is power" (ch39)——both frame diet culture as harmful (lies, power loss)。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "We've all been fed a steady diet of lies" 与 "What we give away is power" (ch39) 形成 parallel——both frame diet culture as harmful. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's harm turn: Leslie exposes diet culture's harm (lies, power loss)。
