## 精读

> "we're not just selling food. We're selling you."

**中文理解**："你卖的不只是食物。我们卖的是你。"

**关键词**：not just selling food（不只是卖食物）/ We're selling you（我们卖的是你）

**为什么这样写**："not just selling food"将 Risto's career 定义 as "beyond food"——not "restaurant" but "personality"。"We're selling you"将 Risto 定义 as "product"——not "chef" but "you" (personal brand)。This "selling you" is Brock's core advice: Risto's career ambition requires personal branding, not just culinary skill. "You're not just selling food. We're selling you."的 "you"（你）将 Risto 定义为 "commodity"——not "artist" but "product"。

**读者视角提示**：Brock's "selling you" 与 Risto's "I'd be lying if I said my interest in opening a New York location had nothing to do with getting closer to Leslie" (ch10) 形成 tension——Risto's career ambition is "personal branding" (Brock) but also "proximity to Leslie" (Risto). This tension reveals the career arc's complexity: Risto's career is both "professional ambition" and "romantic strategy"。

---

> "There's also a physical appearance clause. He wants you to maintain a certain BMI, and you are way over what he's suggesting. If you sign this, you'd need to lose weight."

**中文理解**："还有一条关于外貌的条款。他希望你保持一定的 BMI，而你远远超过了他建议的范围。如果你签了这个，你需要减肥。"

**关键词**：physical appearance clause（外貌条款）/ maintain a certain BMI（保持一定的 BMI）/ you'd need to lose weight（你需要减肥）

**为什么这样写**："physical appearance clause"将 contract 定义 as "body regulation"——not "professional" but "physical appearance"。"maintain a certain BMI"将 BMI 定义 as "requirement"——not "health metric" but "contractual obligation"。"you'd need to lose weight"将 BMI clause 定义 as "weight loss mandate"——not "health recommendation" but "requirement"。This "BMI weight clause" is the anti-diet theme's career dimension: Risto's body (size) is regulated by contract, not just by Mom's diet dogma. The anti-diet theme extends from "personal" (Mom) to "professional" (Brock's contract)。

**读者视角提示**：Brock's BMI weight clause 与 Mom's "Nothing tastes as good as thin feels" (ch04) 形成 parallel——both regulate body size: Mom through "mantra"，Brock through "contract"。This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's broader application: body size regulation exists in both "personal" (family) and "professional" (career) domains。

---

> "No fucking way. That has to come out."

**中文理解**："没 fucking 门。那必须删掉。"

**关键词**：No fucking way（没 fucking 门）/ That has to come out（那必须删掉）

**为什么这样写**："No fucking way"将 Risto's rejection 定义 as "absolute"——not "negotiable" but "no fucking way"。"That has to come out"将 BMI clause 定义 as "removable"——not "acceptable" but "must be removed"。This "No fucking way" is Risto's anti-diet stance: he rejects body size regulation in his career, just as Leslie rejects it in her personal life. Risto's rejection of the BMI clause parallels Leslie's rejection of Mom's diet dogma——both partners reject body size regulation。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "No fucking way" 与 Leslie's "my mom was a fucking cult leader" (ch16) 形成 parallel——both partners use profanity to reject body size regulation. This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's mutual adoption: both partners reject diet culture, Risto in career (BMI clause) and Leslie in personal life (Mom's dogma)。
