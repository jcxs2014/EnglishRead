## 精读

> "I can't see anything else but your rolls of flab."

**中文理解**："我除了你的赘肉卷什么都看不到。"

**关键词**：rolls of flab（赘肉卷）/ can't see anything else（什么都看不到）

**为什么这样写**："rolls of flab"将 Risto's body 定义 as "fat"——not "chef's body" but "rolls of flab" (disgusting)。"can't see anything else"将 Brock's gaze 定义 as "fixated"——not "balanced" but "fixated" (only sees fat)。This "rolls of flab... can't see anything else" is Brock's body shaming: he reduces Risto to "fat" not "chef"。Brock's body shaming parallels Reed's body shaming of Victoria (ch02)：both use body size to undermine professional worth。

**读者视角提示**：Brock's "rolls of flab" 与 Mom's "Nothing tastes as good as thin feels" (ch04) 形成 parallel——both define body size as "problem"。But Brock's body shaming is "professional" (career) while Mom's is "personal" (family)。This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's broader application: body size regulation exists in both "personal" and "professional" domains。

---

> "This was how Leslie starved herself."

**中文理解**："这就是 Leslie 饿死自己的方式。"

**关键词**：This was how（这就是...的方式）/ Leslie starved herself（Leslie 饿死自己）

**为什么这样写**："This was how"将 Brock's diet plan 定义 as "starvation"——not "healthy diet" but "how Leslie starved herself" (connecting to Leslie's anorexia)。"Leslie starved herself"将 Risto's realization 定义 as "empathy"——not "self-concern" but "Leslie" (empathy for Leslie)。This "This was how Leslie starved herself" is Risto's anti-diet realization: he sees Brock's diet plan as "starvation" (same as Leslie's anorexia)。Risto's empathy for Leslie reveals his anti-diet values: he rejects body size regulation because he sees its harm to Leslie。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "This was how Leslie starved herself" 与 Leslie's "I'm more afraid of eating than not eating" (ch11) 形成 mutual understanding——both partners now understand starvation's harm. This mutual understanding is the anti-diet theme's couple-level resolution: both partners reject body size regulation。

---

> "Without taking a bite, I knew my new breakfast was barely better than not eating at all."

**中文理解**："没吃一口，我就知道我的新早餐几乎和完全不吃一样。"

**关键词**：Without taking a bite（没吃一口）/ barely better than not eating at all（几乎和完全不吃一样）

**为什么这样写**："Without taking a bite"将 Risto's breakfast 定义 as "unappetizing"——not "tempting" but "without taking a bite" (no desire to eat)。"barely better than not eating at all"将 Risto's breakfast 定义 as "starvation"——not "diet" but "barely better than not eating" (near-starvation)。This "barely better than not eating at all" is Risto's anti-diet realization: Brock's diet plan is "starvation" not "health"。Risto's realization parallels Leslie's "not to eat at all" (ch04)——both see diet as "starvation"。

**读者视角提示**：Risto's "barely better than not eating at all" 与 Leslie's "not to eat at all" (ch04) 形成 parallel——both see diet as "starvation"。This parallel reveals the anti-diet theme's mutual adoption: both partners see diet as "starvation"。Risto's diet dilemma (follow Brock vs. reject starvation) parallels Leslie's eating disorder recovery (reject starvation)。
