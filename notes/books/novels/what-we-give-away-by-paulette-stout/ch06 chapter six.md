# 06. Chapter Six

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Leslie 驱车前往宾夕法尼亚照料 Aunt Dot，途中回忆与 Risto 的分手与余情，最终在 Dot 家的露台上与 Risto 不期而遇——"Living next door to him was going to be torture"的预言在章节末尾成真。

**情感弧线位置**：升级（压力升级），情绪基调：焦虑 → 怀旧 → 惊喜 → 紧张。Leslie 从"城市独立女性"身份向"家族成员"与"旧情人"双重身份过渡。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**： forced proximity（被迫接近——Dot 安排 Leslie 住在 Risto 隔壁）+ second-chance romance setup（旧情重燃——"Risto still impact me this way"）+ body image（身体形象——"Being the sole trim person in a family of larger bodies"）。

**人物弧线**：Leslie 从"城市 girl"向"家族成员"过渡——Dot 的 embrace 与 Risto 的出现让 Leslie 的"wall of curls"成为防御武器。Leslie 对 Risto 的思念从"suppressed"向"undeniable"过渡。

**叙事手法**：第一人称现在时（Leslie POV），以驾驶为起点，经回忆与 Dot 的 embrace 到 Risto 的 surprise appearance，以"Living next door to him was going to be torture"收尾——物理接近（neighbor）与情感距离（ex）的矛盾在章节末尾固化。

## 精读

> "Risto. I gulped hard at the idea of seeing him."

**中文理解**："Risto。一想到要见他，我便使劲咽了口唾沫。"

**关键词**：gulped hard（使劲咽唾沫）/ at the idea of（一想到）

**为什么这样写**："Risto."单字成句——不是"Risto, I gulped"而是先 naming then reacting，将 Risto 定义为"trigger"而非"person"。"gulped hard"用生理反应（吞咽）来表达心理焦虑——Leslie 的身体比她的思想更诚实。"at the idea of seeing him"将"seeing"定义为"idea"而非"reality"——Leslie 尚未见到 Risto，但"idea" already causes physiological response，暗示 Risto 对她的影响是"anticipatory"而非"reactive"。

**读者视角提示**：Leslie 的 "gulped hard" 与 Risto 的 "I'm screwed"（ch05）形成 mutual anxiety——both fear reunion, but neither has communicated this fear. This mutual anxiety drives the romance arc: both want reunion but both fear it.

---

> "That delicious man was mostly why I'd been scarce at Aunt Dot's."

**中文理解**："那个美味的男人主要就是我为什么很少来 Aunt Dot 家的原因。"

**关键词**：delicious man（美味的男人）/ scarce（稀少的/少来的）/ mostly why（主要就是为什么）

**为什么这样写**："delicious man"用食物修辞（delicious）来描述 Risto——Leslie 用 culinary metaphor 将 Risto 定义为"temptation"而非"person"。"mostly why"将 Leslie 的 absence 定义为"primarily due to Risto"——不是"work busy"而是"avoiding Risto"。"scarce"用"稀少"定义 Leslie 的 visits——不是"never" but "rarely"，暗示 Leslie 仍然 visit Dot，只是 less frequently. This "partial avoidance" reveals Leslie's emotional state: she can't fully cut ties with Dot (family loyalty) but she can minimize exposure to Risto.

**读者视角提示**：Leslie 的 "delicious man" 与 Risto 的 "The forever love that wasn't" (ch03) 形成 mutual longing——both use food metaphors to describe each other (Risto = "delicious", Leslie = "invaded my every waking moment"). This mutual food-metaphor longing is the romance genre's "shared language" setup.

---

> "Hey, Risto. Been awhile." I hid behind my wall of curls.

**中文理解**："嘿，Risto。好久不见。"我躲在卷发的墙后。

**关键词**：wall of curls（卷发的墙）/ hid behind（躲在...后面）/ Been awhile（好久不见）

**为什么这样写**："wall of curls"将 Leslie 的头发定义为"defense mechanism"——不是"hairstyle" but "shield"。"hid behind"用"hide"而非"look through"——Leslie 用头发作为 physical barrier between herself and Risto. "Been awhile"是 understatement——"a while" downplays the four-year separation, revealing Leslie's emotional avoidance: she doesn't want to acknowledge the time gap because acknowledging it means acknowledging the pain.

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "wall of curls" 与 Risto's "I better head back to the restaurant" (immediate retreat) 形成 mutual avoidance——both use physical barriers (hair / leaving) to manage emotional proximity. This mutual avoidance is the romance genre's "reunion anxiety" setup.

---

> "Being the sole trim person in a family of larger bodies meant a lot of teasing came my way."

**中文理解**："在一个全是大体型家人的家族中，我是唯一瘦的人，这意味着我受到了很多取笑。"

**关键词**：sole trim person（唯一瘦的人）/ family of larger bodies（大体型家人）/ teasing（取笑）

**为什么这样写**："sole trim person"将 Leslie 定义为"family outlier"——不是"healthy" but "different from family". "family of larger bodies"将 family 定义为"larger bodies"——不是"family members" but "bodies"，暗示 Leslie's family identity is defined by body size. "teasing came my way"将 teasing 定义为"inevitable"——not "sometimes" but "a lot", revealing Leslie's experience of body-based teasing as pervasive. This "trim person in larger family" dynamic is the anti-diet theme's social dimension: Leslie's body anxiety is not just "media-driven" but "family-driven".

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "sole trim person" 与 Dot's "body acceptance classes" (ch04) 形成 family tension——Leslie is "trim" but anxious, Dot is "larger" but accepting. This contrast reveals the anti-diet theme's complexity: body size ≠ body acceptance. Leslie is "trim" but not "accepting"; Dot is "larger" but "accepting".

---

> "Living next door to him was going to be torture."

**中文理解**："和他住隔壁将会是折磨。"

**关键词**：Living next door（住隔壁）/ torture（折磨）

**为什么这样写**："Living next door"将 proximity 定义为"living arrangement"——not "visiting" but "living"，暗示 permanent proximity. "torture"用"折磨"定义 proximity——not "challenging" but "torture"，揭示 Leslie's emotional state: proximity to Risto is not "difficult" but "unbearable". This "torture" framing is Leslie's defense mechanism: by defining proximity as "torture", she avoids acknowledging that proximity is also "desirable".

**读者视角提示**：Leslie's "torture" 与 Risto's "I'm screwed" (ch05) 形成 mutual dread——both define proximity as negative ("torture" / "screwed") but neither can articulate the positive desire underneath. This mutual negative-framing is the romance genre's "denial" setup: both want reunion but both frame it as suffering.
