**关键词**：I was grateful（I was grateful）/ to be me（to be me）

**为什么这样写**："I was grateful"将 Leslie 定义 as "grateful"——not "ungrateful" but "grateful" (grateful)。"to be me"将 Leslie 定义 as "self-accepting"——not "self-rejecting" but "to be me" (self-accepting)。This "I was grateful to be me" is Leslie's self-acceptance: she's grateful to be herself. Leslie's self-acceptance parallels her "I was no longer starving myself" (ch39)——both mark recovery (self-acceptance, nourishment)。
