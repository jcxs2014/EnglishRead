# 《Embarkare》情感节点

> 10 个节点，按叙事顺序；引语逐字取自已过门禁的章节精读块。

## 节点一：纪念日的双面（ch01–02）

纪念日的白天她被村庄需要，夜里是丧母忌日；篝火讲古让 Andra 第一次"看见"Edgeron，树线边缘的猎人命她"跑"。日常与追猎在同一个夜晚完成交接。

> "Andra’s attention tunneled inward as memories of that night flashed through her mind. Of her tiny body shivering in the wet grass, her small fingers still clutching the paper flowers from the remembrance celebration."（ch01）

> "“Why’s that?” she asked with a smile. She took a drink of wine, courage slowly sinking in. … “Because I want you to run.” … His grin was feral as he whispered, “Run.”"（ch02）

> "“Monsters are not always made of sharp teeth and talons, love.” His gravelly voice caressed her skin and settled deep into her bones. “Sometimes, monsters are made of beautiful faces and sultry lies.”"（ch02）


## 节点二：Bernice 的刀（ch03）

怪物捣毁家门、黑雾驱尸复活，恶魔借 Bernice 之口喊出她的身世与通牒；Bernice 夺回身体的最后一刻告白并自捅驱魔——她活着，代价刻在所有人身上。

> "The black vapor slid down Bernice’s throat. Horror rooted her to the spot as the elderly woman she loved so dearly rose from her grasp, head lolling to the side as her feet planted on the ground."（ch03）

> "“Andra of the Southern Woods.” … It was ravenous with hunger, a snarl that worked its way under Andra’s skin and festered there. … “Daughter of Cecilia the Light Witch,” she began again, “it is time you pay for your mother’s sins.”"（ch03）

> "“I have loved you like my own. It has been my greatest pleasure watching you grow,” she whispered, a single tear carving a path down her face. … With trembling hands, Bernice plunged the blade into her abdomen."（ch03）


## 节点三：黑湖真相（ch04）

Egeria 带她潜入母亲的决战：Cecilia 以怜悯作最后武器照亮 Edgeron；银发女人跪吻尸体、以翡翠收走暗影——Vasara 的爱与恨在同一晚开户。

> "Shadow and light clashed and tangled in a vicious attempt to gain control, and, gods, it was beautiful and terrifying. Short black hair hung into the bloodshot eyes of the man wielding shadows, his skin deathly pale with slithering dark lines along his neck extending from the collar of his shirt."（ch04）

> "“Surrender now or the darkness will take what it wants without permission.” … “I feel bad for you. That you never experienced the simple joys of life.” Tears spilled down her dirty face, and her arms shook. “Never content enough to relax in your lover’s arms or enjoy a warm summer day. I have lost many today, Edgeron, but it’s you who ultimately lost.”"（ch04）

> "“My love,” the woman sobbed as she cradled his face and kissed his cold lips. “My love, I will avenge you. I will not stop until dark magic has conquered all. Cecilia of the light will suffer for this. Your sacrifice will not be in vain.”"（ch04）


## 节点四：启程与血魔法（ch05–08）

小咒典、林中和解、三马启程；她学会以自身之血为薪的禁忌魔法，第一次结界让匪徒对着火光失明——"我看他妈的哪有什么火"。

> "After all, it is love that often makes people do the worst things. … Andra held tightly to Bernice’s hands, kissing her knuckles as she whispered, “Maybe love can also make people do the best things.” … With nothing more than a satchel of supplies and limited knowledge of life outside her woods, Andra set off into the night."（ch05）

> "“Andra, there’s a reason blood magic stopped being passed through the generations. There’s a reason blood magic doesn’t exist anymore. No one being is meant to hold so much power.” … “It’s entirely the right amount for what needs to be done.” … “We start small.”"（ch07）

> "“Darkness is coming,” she began again. “And we must be ready. These incantations are from my mother, and while I’ve come to realize I may not have known much about her long life, I do know that she believed in the good. She was good.” … “I’m with you, Cousin.”"（ch08）


## 节点五：Nivek 之殇（ch06）

死村、孩子的尸体、以恐惧为食的白怪——Daegal 被卷上树冠消失，Tolan 用身体挡住扑击，Lucas 的箭射进他头骨终结尖叫。队伍第一次减员，账记在她头上。

> "His voice cut off when he glimpsed the fresh corpses just inside the home, the dirty feet sticking out much too small to be that of an adult. … “We need to leave before whatever attacked this village comes back,” Daegal’s voice was deeper than usual."（ch06）

> "As if in sync with her thoughts, leaves shuffled at the edge of the clearing and a long bone-white hand crept into view. Another. Sharp crimson-coated claws sank into the dirt. The face of a monster slowly emerged from the shadows, mere inches from the ground, with protruding cheek bones and sunken pearly-white eyes that stared straight at them."（ch06）

> "The monster devoured Tolan, ripping his limbs from his body and feasting upon his flesh and blood as his piercing screams rent the air. … As soon as she was out of sight, Lucas nocked an arrow and fired true, straight into Tolan’s skull."（ch06）


## 节点六：主体性宣言（ch09–11）

Avernon 的妓院里她拒绝 Jasper 的"身体抵账"；巷中反杀未遂、被掳、获救、复仇——两兽当街处刑施暴者，她"不敢看又渴望看"。她学会了把恐惧翻译成边界。

> "Well, Hachar has been in charge since he started traveling ages ago. He arranges monster fights. … Sometimes we pick them up from merchants on our travels, other times they hang on to them in their village for when we pass through again. Hachar has even captured some in the wild."（ch09）

> "“I see your offer for what it is, Jasper.” His name left a bad taste in her mouth. “Now let me make myself very clear. My answer is no. So, stay the hell away from me.”"（ch09）

> "The beasts pounced. Andra watched, horrified, as claws and fangs tore into flesh while Jasper screamed. She wanted to look away, needed to look away, but a primal part of her craved to witness the absolute destruction of this man that tried to take so much from her."（ch11）


## 节点七：信任重建（ch12–14）

Quentin/Rayner 兄弟的身世与"生物/怪物"第一课；她交出全部身世换回一句"Together"；客栈的庇护所原则（光暗同席）为她撑起最后一段安稳。

> "If I can eliminate the amount of monsters coming for me, then maybe I would actually stand a chance … “Yes.” … “What?” … “Yes,” Rayner repeated. “We’ll find the hole and we’ll close it. Together.”"（ch12）

> "“That asshole did unspeakable things to you, and it’s okay to feel the weight of that. You may be battered and bruised. You may be cracked. Yes, you may even be a little bit broken. But it is in the process of picking ourselves up, of putting those broken pieces back together again, that we find our strength.”"（ch13）

> "“My two barmaids recently left. Sisters. Said they had to care for their dying father. You help me down here in the tavern every night until our supply runner returns, and that will cover the costs of your supplies.” … Andra didn’t hesitate. “Done.”"（ch14）


## 节点八：光球之夜（ch24）

面具、星夜庭院、十二盏水包火的光球——她把初见的"愤怒"听成了供词："我得不到你"。第一次吻与第一次不羞耻，同夜发生。

> "But the most beautiful thing about him tonight was his expression. Gone were the harsh lines between his eyes, his shoulders were relaxed in a comfortable stance, and a small smile graced his lips as he looked at her."（ch24）

> "“I was angry,” he responded. “I was angry that you’re so beautiful, so pure. I was angry at the way your face lit up when you diagnosed a villager's ailment and gave them the proper tonic for it. I was angry that you’re so perfectly you. I was angry that I couldn’t have you.”"（ch24）

> "Never before had she experienced this level of intimacy. She was no stranger to sex, but this was new. Never had there been an emotional attachment with other partners, never trust or belonging or a desire to keep that person in her life long after the mutual goal was accomplished. … Gerrick was different. … When she was with him, she felt a smolder within her very soul that she’d believed burned out long ago. He made her want to fight. He made her want to live."（ch24）


## 节点九：至暗连环（ch28–30）

长矛贯胸（"直到我最后一次呼吸"）、繁殖营的孕妇、Vasara 的审判席与三重真相——母亲没赢过、凶手是暗恋者、同行的是傀儡。真 Lucas 的遗言只有夜听见了。

> "Magic tore from her body, her skin the catalyst for the winds surrounding the entirety of the camp, for the electricity charging the very air around her. Objects lifted from the ground, sucking into the vicious whirlwind now expanding toward the heavens. Beastmen roared as they, too, were plucked from the ground, rotating in a flurry of monsters and debris."（ch28）

> "Andra withdrew her hands quickly as the gases began to fade, slowly dissipating as it was replaced with a rich viridian hue. The emerald. There it was, suspended in the center of the orb by an invisible force, and Andra’s entire body tensed."（ch29）

> "The scene cleared, and she saw the black vapor that had inhabited Bernice’s body rush into Lucas’s. His eyes opened, black and glaring before morphing slowly into the dark brown of her cousin’s. Clumsily, his body rose from the ground, bones snapping back into place as he stumbled into the darkness."（ch30）


## 节点十：怪物判词（ch31–33）

Gerrick 的坑生真相与倒戈、她的"我就是火"、骨刃入心、剖胸纳翡翠；终章地牢里她的判词落定——"你造出了一个你无法想象的怪物""也许我不是。但你是。"

> "“Like it or not, hunter, you are one of them. Born a bastard in the darkest part of the pit itself.” She straightened. “When you slay one of your own, a tattoo appears, correct? This physical manifestation marks your betrayal and claims you as a traitor, a beacon of sorts for your monster brethren to hunt you down and make an example out of you.”"（ch31）

> "“Damn it, witch,” he said through clenched teeth. “Why did you do that?” … The room blurred and she struggled to focus on his beautiful face. … “Until my last breath,” she choked."（ch32）

> "“You have no idea the monster you’ve created, Gerrick,” she whispered in the small space between them. … “You’re not a monster,” he growled. … Emotion swelled in his chest as she looked directly into his eyes. … “Perhaps not. But you are.”"（ch33）
