# The Last Thing 情感节点

## 节点一：酒吧初遇（Chapter 1）

- **叙事概括**：Hallie 在法庭脱身后与朋友聚会，邂逅了 Deck。两人一拍即合，达成协议：只做一晚的玩伴。
- **关键引语**：
  - "Love is not coming for me," I say pointedly, looking around the booth at our favorite little bar, McGills Tavern.
  - "As long as you're good with this being one night."
  - "Thank God. I was worried you were going to ask for my number—or my full name. I'm here for fun, and that's all."

## 节点二：嘉年华之夜（Chapter 2-3）

- **叙事概括**：Hallie 带 Deck 去嘉年华，两人在摩天轮上初吻。Deck 为她赢了绒毛兔子，她给了他"true love"项链。
- **关键引语**：
  - "Now you'll always remember the night you met your true love."
  - Walking in is a deliciously hot man with dark hair and a face full of dark stubble.
  - Target acquired.

## 节点三：清晨离别（Chapter 4）

- **叙事概括**：Deck 在清晨悄悄离开，只留下纸条和绒毛兔子。他保留了"true"那半颗项链。
- **关键引语**：
  - My little hellion, Last night was one of the best I've had in a long time. Might sound cheesy, but this will be a fond memory for me—maybe my only one of the city. Take care of yourself and keep the bunny. I got it for you. Deck
  - One souvenir from an amazing night won't hurt, right?

## 节点四：发现怀孕（Chapter 5）

- **叙事概括**：Hallie 发现自己怀孕，决定留下孩子。Frannie 提供了一份在 Ida 的工作。
- **关键引语**：
  - Condoms are useless.
  - Why am I so easy to leave?
  - I don't mind being a Lorelai Gilmore—with better parental boundaries—and raising a kid alone.

## 节点五：命运重逢（Chapter 8）

- **叙事概括**：Hallie 第一次以"保姆应聘者"身份去见 Deck，两人发现对方就是那一夜的人。Sophia 的出场让一切更复杂。
- **关键引语**：
  - "Deck," I breathe, my heart stuttering as I take in the devil of a man who I was only supposed to have one night with.
  - "Hellion?" he rasps, eyes locked on mine.
  - My baby daddy.

## 节点六：Deck 的告白（Chapter 11）

- **叙事概括**：Deck 为逃离道歉，向 Hallie 承诺会照顾她和孩子。Hallie 再次逃跑。
- **关键引语**：
  - I will be here for every step. I will take care of you. I'll be at every doctor's appointment. I'll make middle of the night food runs.
  - Because I felt something for you the moment I saw you. I said one night because I thought that's all I could have.

## 节点七：深夜照顾（Chapter 13）

- **叙事概括**：Deck 半夜听到 Hallie 呕吐，拿着备用钥匙冲去她家，为她买冰淇淋和薯条。
- **关键引语**：
  - I'm moving before I realize what I'm doing. I grab her spare key off the side of my fridge and the video monitor for Soph's room and bolt out the door.
  - I ache to reach for her, cup her cheek, comfort her. But I'm not sure she finds me comforting.

## 节点八：Sophia 的过敏（Chapter 14-15）

- **叙事概括**：Sophia 对护手霜过敏，Hallie 冷静处理并送她去医院。Deck 的感激让 Hallie 意识到"in so much trouble"。
- **关键引语**：
  - Wilson Decker has ruined me.
  - Falling for Wilson Decker is a recipe for heartbreak.
  - He wants to take care of me. And there's a part of me that's soft for him and wants to let him.

## 节点九：Hallie 的恐惧起源（Chapter 23）

- **叙事概括**：Hallie 揭示她害怕爱情的根源——14 岁时目睹祖父去世后祖母的崩溃。
- **关键引语**：
  - Growing up, I was obsessed with my grandparents' love story. I'd beg my gran to tell me about it over and over.
  - I will never forget Gran screaming at the top of her lungs that she was supposed to die first because she couldn't live without him. That was the moment I knew I never wanted to fall in love because I didn't want to endure that type of pain.

## 节点十：Hallie 说"我爱你"（Chapter 30-31）

- **叙事概括**：Hallie 在加州婚礼上向朋友们承认她爱 Deck，最终在酒店对 Deck 说出"我爱你"。
- **关键引语**：
  - I… am." I throw my hands up. "I'm in love with him. The love bug bit me hard. Okay? Are you all happy now?
  - I realized if you left this planet tomorrow, I could never regret loving you, but I'd forever regret not telling you how much I love you. And Sophia. And our beautiful little family." Tears well in her eyes and her voice breaks. "I love you."

## 节点十一：Hope 的诞生（Epilogue）

- **叙事概括**：Hallie 分娩，Sophia 请求叫 Hallie"妈妈"，他们给女儿取名 Hope。
- **关键引语**：
  - "Hope," Hallie says with a smile. "Hope Baker-Decker."
  - Now that Hope is here, and she'll call you Mommy, I was wondering if I could call you Mommy too.
  - I used to think the last thing I'd ever want to do was fall in love. Life, or maybe fate, proved me wrong. Thank God I broke my rules—got tangled up with the man I was nannying for, and more importantly, let myself get lost in love. Best decisions ever.
