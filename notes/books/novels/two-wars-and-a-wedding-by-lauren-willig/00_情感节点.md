# 《Two Wars and a Wedding》情感节点

## 节点一：离坦帕两英里的道床边（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书的情感起点不是一个相遇，而是一个决定。一八九八年六月二日，Betsy 穿着被呕吐物弄脏的裙子站在铁轨边，目的是把最好的朋友从古巴的战场拉回来；她遇见的第一个人是旧日同学 Paul，第二个是那位不由分说把她塞回车厢的军官。她此时并不知道，那位军官这辈子最想救的人就是她。

**关键引语：**

> ① "She'd learned in Greece that it wasn't the cause that mattered; even the best of intentions wouldn't stop wounds from festering and disease from spreading."（ch01）

> ② "Ha. She hadn't been a lady in years. Possibly ever, if her brother was to be believed."（ch01）

> ③ "There's no winning, don't you understand? Even if you win you lose."（ch03）

## 节点二：德尔斐，柱旁的那个吻（ch06-08）

**叙事概括：** 她在德尔斐向一个已婚法国人交出了自己；被拒绝的方式不是冷酷而是温柔——他蹲下来替她把领带系好，然后说出那段时间「在世界之外」。她没有哭也没有闹，只把怒气层层裹起来当盔甲，然后一个人爬上了赫里孔山。

**关键引语：**

> ① "They have been a time outside time. They don't belong to the rest of the world—the part of the world that has my wife in it."（ch08）

> ② "I knew, eventually, a reckoning would come. But was it so wrong to dream a little?"（ch08）

> ③ "Just because a man knows he'll be burned doesn't mean he won't reach out to the flames."（ch08）

## 节点三：营火边的七把空椅子（ch09-11）

**叙事概括：** 拉古斯马斯的火线上，一个叫 Ham 的中尉当场死去，一个叫 Thomas 的中尉在血泊里下令「弟兄们得看见指挥官站在前面」。当夜七个人合葬一处蓝毯之下，剩下两个人在营火边为「该不该报仇」争到天亮。

**关键引语：**

> ① "There wasn't one vacant chair but seven. Seven men laid in a common grave, wrapped in blue blankets to thwart the vultures that circled above."（ch11）

> ② "I'm not shooting the Spanish because I want to shoot them. I'm shooting them because I want to stop them doing what they're doing. There's a difference."（ch11）

> ③ "He couldn't right murder with murder, even if his soul cried for justice. That wasn't justice. That was revenge."（ch11）

## 节点四：桑塞卡号（ch13-15）

**叙事概括：** 她违抗命令接下一船伤员，指挥官用一句话把整船人的命压给她。船在高热中病倒。她在那间被当作野战病房改装的船上第一次感到自己有用，也第一次被人怀疑是不是在演一场戏。

**关键引语：**

> ① "These men’s lives depend on you. You cannot fail."（ch13）

> ② "“You’ve been given everything. You’ve been given everything and you’re throwing it away.”"（ch14）

> ③ "“Nurse Hayes,” Betsy said matter-of-factly. “We’ve met. You have a nasty abscess in your leg and I need to drain it. I think you’d be better off going to sleep again. This is going to hurt.”"（ch15）

## 节点五：她以为自己毁了所有人（ch22-25）

**叙事概括：** 希腊在战火里塌了，她怀着孩子回到美国，躲进一间伤兵收容所，把负罪一条条数给自己听：那个没机会出生的孩子、那个她救不回来的同事、那个她以为死在自己手上的法国人。这一段的最低点是她写下的那句——我毁掉了我碰到的一切。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I had a lover—and I killed him. I had a baby—and I gave him away—"（ch25）

> ② "Because I destroy everything I touch."（ch25）

> ③ "There should be—there should be a warning on me. Caveat . . . emptor . . . Beware. Lasciate ogni speranza . . ."（ch25）

## 节点六：桑塞卡号的听证会（ch26-27）

**叙事概括：** 战争结束了，但没有人愿意听她说话。报纸称她是「歇斯底里的女人」，军方把她的话当作不着边际的胡言。她在会上只做了一件事：把「该负责的人」和「该称赞的人」分开，于是规程来了，调查委员会也来了。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Half of what we call heroics are just people making the best of a bad situation. But we shouldn’t have been in that situation in the first place!"（ch27）

> ② "Don’t look for someone to praise—look for someone to hold accountable!"（ch27）

> ③ "If it is hysteria to speak the truth and speak it plainly, then I am, indeed, what they say."（ch27）

## 节点七：中央公园，报童旁边的那个人（ch28）

**叙事概括：** 她拿着那篇把自己写进头版的报道来堵他，却在公园里看见一个穿得一丝不苟、正在用拐杖从水里捞小船的男人。他为她做了一件他自己这辈子最难的事：给父亲打了电话。

**关键引语：**

> ① "And just like that, Betsy couldn't remember what she'd meant to say, never mind that she'd been rehearsing it for the entire walk over."（ch28）

> ② "'Because you were right,' Holt said quietly. 'Would I rather crawl naked over ground glass than ask my father for anything?"（ch28）

> ③ "'You got everyone through alive,' said Holt quietly."（ch28）

## 节点八：混杂档案（ch29）

**叙事概括：** 全书以一叠文件收尾：一封宣布婚讯并讨债的信、一份调查委员会报告的节选、一封礼貌到关门的老相识来信、一则大学教师与军医的婚讯剪报。没有一句总结，只有她写下的最后一条计划——婚礼一过就回希腊。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Don't think I've forgiven you for that nauseating article; I mean to make you pay for that for the next decade at least and possibly longer. You can start by being my bridesmaid."（ch29）

> ② "I did nothing of the kind. I simply, clearly laid out the mistakes that were made and the abuses that needed to be corrected and then allowed him to reach his own conclusions."（ch29）

> ③ "I mean to return to Greece directly after the wedding."（ch29）
