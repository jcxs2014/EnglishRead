# 19. Yvette

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：四十八岁的数学教师伊薇特在诊室里当场**重演**了被捕获：骨头在溶解、身体被拆成碎片又重组、频率被改写；她说他们来过三次，取走她的卵、用自己的精子受精、三个月后用一根长针从她肚脐下方取出胎儿，放进飞船的保育管——最后她像喂养雏鸟一样喂窗台上的黑birds，说：我是个养育者，这就是他们选中我的原因。

**信念弧线位置**：马瑟判断力的**崩塌点**。他明确地、公开地厌恶上了这位当事人——「我把她归为癔症型自恋者」——而这一章同时是全书对「捕获」最完整的一次**实证性陈述**（有人被取走了胎儿）。怀疑与证据在这一章里第一次同时到了顶格。

**母题兑现/反转**：母题的最终形态在这里成形：捕获不是一次事件，而是**一套程序**——取卵、受精、取胎、保育。而它同时是保险、也是警告、也是延续种族的工程。而被捕获者对此的回应是：「我是个养育者，这就是他们选中我的原因。」

**人物弧线**：伊薇特是全书唯一一位**主动认领**自己身份的人（养育者、传讯者、正在执行一项使命的人），因此也是唯一一个让马瑟失去客观性的对象。这份失衡最终以一个**梦**结算：一间没有窗的屋子，他在墙上挖洞，一层塑料挖掉又长出一层。

**叙事手法**：全章的高潮是**身体剧场**：颤抖、过度换气、尖叫、要求握手；而第五章那个关于黑birds 的承诺在此兑现——「只有一次，鸟显出了意义」。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Yvette: my piece de resistance."

**中文理解**：伊薇特：我的压轴之作。

**关键词**：Yvette: my piece de resistance.

**为什么这样写**：作者用一句法语借词点题——「我的拿手好戏」。而这个说法在读完本章之后会变得极其刺耳：马瑟在**开始之前**就把一个人称为自己的作品。

**读者视角提示**：请记住这句开场。它是马瑟视角的一次自我暴露，而全书后面要清算的正是这一点。

---

> **原句 2:** "There is something about Yvette, a compact muscularity that suggests physical strength, a woman grounded, down-to-earth, and yet when we shake hands it’s as if an electric current is coursing along her skin. She tosses her head back and gazes around the room with an air that is both distracted and proprietorial. I indicate the chair opposite us and she walks towards it as if she intends to go past it, then stops abruptly. When she is seated she bends forward and for a few minutes or so she rummages in her handbag, as if searching for something she has lost, then snaps it shut, looks up and gazes unblinkingly into my eyes."

**中文理解**：伊薇特身上有种东西：一种紧凑的结实感，暗示着体力；一个踏实的、落地的女人。然而我们握手时，却像有一股电流沿着她的皮肤流过。她把头往后一甩，打量着房间，那神态既心不在焉又像是这里的主人。我指了指对面的椅子，她朝那走去，像是要从旁边走过去，然后突然停住。坐下后她前倾身子，在手提包里翻找了几分钟，像在找什么丢了的东西，然后啪地合上包，抬头直直地看进我的眼睛。

**关键词**：a compact muscularity that suggests physical strength / a woman grounded, down-to-earth / it’s as if an electric current is coursing along her skin / an air that is both distracted and proprietorial / she walks towards it as if she intends to go past it / she rummages in her handbag / then snaps it shut / gazes unblinkingly into my eyes

**为什么这样写**：作者用一系列**行为细节**（甩头、走过椅子、翻包、猛地合上、直视）替代任何性格描写——这正是马瑟的方法：不判断，只记录。而「电流沿着皮肤流过」是唯一一处感受性描写，它把这位当事人身上那种「带电」的特质落在了生理上。

**读者视角提示**：请注意「她朝椅子走去，像是要从旁边走过去」——她在这个空间里不服从空间的安排。这一切在几分钟后就会变成临床信号。

> **原句 3:** "I ask Yvette if she is comfortable, and she nods. I then attempt to initiate my usual introductory chitchat by asking about her work. As with my other experiencers, I need to gain some sense of the normal, everyday Yvette, but when she opens her mouth, all that comes out is a hoarse sound that seems to issue from the back of her throat, and then a few inchoate words, halting and mumbled until, with a startling directness, her face composed and her voice clear, she says, ‘I know why I’m here, Dr Mather.’"

**中文理解**：我问伊薇特是否自在，她点了点头。于是我照惯例开始闲聊，问她的工作——和面对其他体验者一样，我需要先了解日常的伊薇特。可她一张嘴，出来的却是一声沙哑的响，仿佛从喉咙深处发出，然后是几个未成形的词，断断续续、含糊不清；接着，出人意料地直接，她的脸平静下来，声音清晰了，说：「我知道我为什么来这儿，马瑟医生。」

**关键词**：I need to gain some sense of the normal, everyday Yvette / all that comes out is a hoarse sound that seems to issue from the back of her throat / a few inchoate words, halting and mumbled / with a startling directness, her face composed and her voice clear / I know why I’m here, Dr Mather

**为什么这样写**：作者用**声音**的三段变化写出这次会谈的异常：沙哑的喉音 → 未成形的词 → 清晰而直接。而「出人意料地直接」（with a startling directness）这半句，把她的转变写成了一次**开关**。马瑟的整套开场程序（先问工作）在第一分钟就被跳过。

**读者视角提示**：请注意马瑟的即时反应——他不高兴（见下一段）。他的方法被打断了，而打断本身成了第一个数据点。

---

> **原句 4:** "I am not altogether happy with what I sense is her desire to go straight to it, as if it is a matter of urgency. Without the usual small talk I have no way of establishing a context that, among other considerations, offers me an insight into the way in which an experiencer uses language, so revealing and yet so underrated an aspect of any psychological profile. On the other hand, I have determined on allowing the experiencer, as far as possible, to control their session. I want them, again as much as possible, to feel equal and not a mere object of investigation. I do not want them to feel used."

**中文理解**：她想直奔主题、仿佛事关紧迫，这让我并不完全高兴。没有了惯常的闲聊，我就无法建立一个语境——而这个语境除了别的考虑之外，还能让我洞察一位体验者**使用语言的方式**，那是任何心理侧写中最有启发、却又最被低估的一个方面。另一方面，我已经决定，尽可能让体验者掌控他们自己的会谈。我希望他们尽可能感到平等，而不是仅仅是一件被研究的物品。我不希望他们觉得自己被**使用**了。

**关键词**：her desire to go straight to it, as if it is a matter of urgency / no way of establishing a context / the way in which an experiencer uses language, so revealing and yet so underrated / allowing the experiencer…to control their session / to feel equal and not a mere object of investigation / I do not want them to feel used

**为什么这样写**：这一段是马瑟方法论的**伦理声明**，而且写得非常漂亮：他承认语言方式本身就是数据（所以失去闲聊是实质损失），同时坚持让当事人掌握节奏。而「我不希望他们觉得自己被使用」这半句，在本章结尾会变成一句**自我指认**——他确实在把人当材料用。

**读者视角提示**：请把这句「我不希望他们觉得自己被使用」与本章结尾的「我可能失去了客观性」并读。

---

> **原句 5:** "Still, I am unprepared for the suddenness with which Yvette conjures her own reality and her disconnect from mine. Immediately after she says, ‘I know why I’m here,’ she gives a convulsive shudder and her body begins to vibrate. Nerves, I think, but no, it is more than that, a mild but persistent all-over tremor. I begin to suspect an oncoming fit but the seizure, if seizure it be, is uncharacteristic, and there is no mention of epilepsy in the preliminary report on her health, or of her being a histrionic subject in her therapy sessions with a counsellor."

**中文理解**：尽管如此，我仍没准备好面对她召唤出自己那个现实的突兀，以及她与我之间那种断裂。紧接着那句「我知道我为什么来这儿」，她猛烈地打了个寒战，身体开始颤动。是紧张，我想——不，不止如此，那是一种轻微却持续的全身震颤。我开始怀疑癫痫发作；但如果那真是抽搐，它并不典型，而且初步健康报告里没有提到癫痫，也没有提到她在咨询师那里被归为癔症人格。

**关键词**：the suddenness with which Yvette conjures her own reality and her disconnect from mine / she gives a convulsive shudder and her body begins to vibrate / a mild but persistent all-over tremor / I begin to suspect an oncoming fit / the seizure, if seizure it be, is uncharacteristic / no mention of epilepsy in the preliminary report / of her being a histrionic subject in her therapy sessions with a counsellor

**为什么这样写**：作者在这里把**临床判断**写成了实时演算：紧张？不对，是全身震颤；癫痫？不像，报告里没有；癔症人格？她的咨询师没这么写。而最后那句「她在咨询师那里**并没有**被归为癔症人格」是关键：马瑟正在为一种分类找依据，而这份依据不存在。

**读者视角提示**：请注意「癔症人格」（histrionic）这个词在本章结尾会回来——马瑟在当晚把伊薇特归为「癔症型自恋者」。

---

> **原句 6:** "She begins then to shake her head violently and cries out: ‘No, they’re here, in my bedroom.’"

**中文理解**：于是她猛烈地摇头，喊道：「不，它们在这里，在我的卧室里。」

**关键词**：shake her head violently / cries out / No, they’re here, in my bedroom

**为什么这样写**：一句七十字母的短句，是全书节奏最猛的一次转折。而「在我的卧室里」这半句把场景从诊室拽进了她的家——她重演的不是事件，是**那件事发生的地方**。

**读者视角提示**：请注意「我的卧室」——这个空间在第十四章（伯纳德）也是发生地。每一个案例都发生在卧室里。

---

> **原句 7:** "It’s with some excitement that I hear this. It tells me that, without hypnosis, Yvette is not going to describe her experience, she is going to relive it. I have some concern that this might occasion anxiety in Lucy, for without looking in her direction I can sense the intensity of her focus. I have experience of patients coming apart in therapy sessions, but this is new to Lucy and I consider how I might devise some signal to reassure her. I cannot, however, afford to take my gaze away from Yvette, who is hyperventilating."

**中文理解**：听到这句，我竟然有些兴奋。它告诉我：不用催眠，伊薇特不是要**描述**她的经历，她是要**重演**它。我有点担心这会让露西焦虑，因为不用朝她那边看，我也能感觉到她专注的强度。我有病人在治疗会谈中崩溃的经验，可这对露西是新的，我盘算着是不是该设计个什么信号让她安心。然而我不能把目光从伊薇特身上移开——她正在过度换气。

**关键词**：It’s with some excitement that I hear this / Yvette is not going to describe her experience, she is going to relive it / I have some concern that this might occasion anxiety in Lucy / I can sense the intensity of her focus / I have experience of patients coming apart in therapy sessions / I cannot, however, afford to take my gaze away from Yvette / who is hyperventilating

**为什么这样写**：作者让马瑟的**兴奋**成为一个问题——这是全书最尖锐的一次专业性失守的开始：他不是在观察创伤，他是在**等待数据**。而作者同时给出露西的位置（她坐在后面，承受着被忽略的强度），并让马瑟意识到自己顾不上她。

**读者视角提示**：请注意「我无法把目光移开」这半句。它在第十一章格里芬那场戏里也出现过一次（马瑟无法停止观察）——这里它让一个医生在护士需要帮助时选择了病人。

> **原句 8:** "‘There’s so much light here,’ she cries, ‘I don’t know if I can stand this, it’s flooding me, a pale-blue light, it’s flooding into my bones…my bones are dissolving.’ She groans and flings her head back."

**中文理解**：「这里的光太多了，」她喊道，「我不知道自己能不能承受，它在淹没我，一片苍白的蓝光，涌进我的骨头……我的骨头在溶解。」她呻吟一声，把头猛地后仰。

**关键词**：There’s so much light here / I don’t know if I can stand this / it’s flooding me, a pale-blue light / it’s flooding into my bones…my bones are dissolving / She groans and flings her head back

**为什么这样写**：「苍白的蓝光」（a pale-blue light）与第二章那个白化病女孩的「近乎半透明的苍白」、以及伯纳德的「蓝白色的光」同属一个色域——本书对外来之光的一致想象。而「涌进骨头」「骨头在溶解」把光写成了**物理性的侵入**。

**读者视角提示**：请记住「苍白的蓝」。这本书里所有来自外面的光都是冷色；而所有来自人的光（炉火、烛光）都是暖的。

---

> **原句 9:** "‘They’re turning me inside out…I’ve lost my body…I’m in pieces.’ She wraps her arms across her chest as if she might hold herself together and begins to describe a kind of physical disaggregation in which all the cells in her body are coming apart, all the molecules separating out, on and on in a terrifying cascade of formlessness until she screams, ‘I’m melting, I’m melting away!’"

**中文理解**：「它们在把我从里到外翻过来……我失去了身体……我成了碎片。」她把双臂环在胸前，仿佛这样就能把自己抱住，然后开始描述一种身体上的解离：全身的细胞正在散开，分子一个一个分离出去，在一场恐怖的无形之流的连锁里不断延续，直到她尖叫：「我在融化，我在融化掉！」

**关键词**：They’re turning me inside out / I’ve lost my body / I’m in pieces / She wraps her arms across her chest as if she might hold herself together / a kind of physical disaggregation / all the cells in her body are coming apart / all the molecules separating out / a terrifying cascade of formlessness / I’m melting, I’m melting away

**为什么这样写**：作者给「解体」写了一条**物理学路线**：细胞→分子→无形。而「抱着自己以免散架」这个姿势，让整个过程在身体上可见。更冷的一层是那个词——解离（disaggregation）：这不是崩溃，这是**有序的分解**。

**读者视角提示**：请把这句与第十一章卢卡的「像我没有身体，可我在那里」并读。同一件事的两极：一边是被彻底拆散，一边是完全没有身体却还在。

> **原句 10:** "‘Yes. Yes…now I’m coming back together again. Yes, coming back, coming back. But there’s a wind, a big wind, it’s taking me down a tunnel.’ And she throws her arms out and her head back against the high rim of her chair, her mouth open, her eyes wide and staring. ‘I’m flying, flying.’ Then, gasping for air, she folds her arms back in across her chest and holds them there, her head bent, her words coming in a hoarse whisper. ‘It’s all right, it’s all right,’ and she is rocking back and forth in her chair, slowly, as if measuring out the distance between her body and what lies in front of her, something only she can see. ‘It’s going to be okay. I’m inside the ship now, and it’s okay. They recognise me, and they’re telling me it’s okay, they just had to reprogram me. They had to program me on a different frequency so they could communicate with me.’"

**中文理解**：「是的，是的……现在我又聚回来了。是的，聚回来，聚回来。可有风，一阵大风，它正把我带进一条隧道。」她把双臂甩开，头后仰抵在椅子的高边上，嘴张着，眼睛大睁、呆滞。「我在飞，在飞。」然后她大口喘气，把双臂重新环回胸前抱紧，头低下来，词句变成沙哑的低语。「没事了，没事了。」她在椅子里缓慢地前后摇晃，像在丈量自己的身体与她面前那东西之间的距离——那东西只有她看得见。「会没事的。我现在在飞船里面了，没事。他们认得我，他们在告诉我没事，他们只是不得不重新设定我。他们必须把我编程到另一个频率上，才能与我沟通。」

**关键词**：now I’m coming back together again / there’s a wind, a big wind, it’s taking me down a tunnel / I’m flying, flying / rocking back and forth in her chair, slowly / as if measuring out the distance between her body and what lies in front of her / They recognise me / they just had to reprogram me / They had to program me on a different frequency so they could communicate with me

**为什么这样写**：这一整段是**表演性**的：甩开的手臂、后仰的头、缓慢摇晃——读者看到的是一具身体在演出一个场景。而「重新设定」「编程到另一个频率」这些词是**技术词汇**，却被用在一段宗教体验的语境内（与伯纳德的「神性的光」正好相反）。

**读者视角提示**：请注意「他们认得我」（They recognise me）——这句话后来解释成「他们来过两次，所以认得我」。马瑟当时**错过了这个线索**，而这正是全书关于「马瑟有多容易错过」的关键一处。

---

> **原句 11:** "‘No, they don’t speak, not like us. But I’m on another frequency now and I can receive their thoughts.’"

**中文理解**：「它们不说话，不像我们那样。可我现在在另一个频率上，能接收它们的思想。」

**关键词**：No, they don’t speak, not like us / I’m on another frequency now and I can receive their thoughts

**为什么这样写**：与安东尼、玛丽一样，伊薇特也给出了「心灵感应」，但她的版本是**技术性的**（频率、接收）而非神秘性的。通信方式被换成了无线电的语言，而这套语言恰好抹掉了「谁在说话」这个问题。

**读者视角提示**：请把这句与第七章玛丽那句「就像那个想法自己进入了我的脑海」并读：同一件事，一个用无线电比喻，一个用心灵感应比喻。

---

> **原句 12:** "‘They’re very tall. They’re bluish. A bit fuzzy. I can’t see any limbs. I don’t know. It doesn’t matter. I’m different now. I’m vibrating on a different frequency.’"

**中文理解**：「它们非常高。偏蓝。有点模糊。我看不见任何肢体。我不知道。无所谓。我现在不一样了。我在另一个频率上振动。」

**关键词**：They’re very tall. They’re bluish. A bit fuzzy / I can’t see any limbs / I don’t know. It doesn’t matter / I’m different now / I’m vibrating on a different frequency

**为什么这样写**：「我看不见任何肢体」与「我无所谓」这组并置极其关键：她对那些存在**毫无形象感**，却毫不怀疑——这正是一个有信念的人的描述方式。而「无所谓」这个回应，把「我描述不出来」与「我不在乎」混成了一句。

**读者视角提示**：请把「我看不出任何肢体」与第十四章伯纳德的「高大、充满蓝白色的光」并读：两个人看见的完全不是同一个东西，却都称之为「他们」。

---

> **原句 13:** "‘But I’m not—no, I’m not the same—they didn’t put me back together again—not like I was before—that’s the problem, I’m not the same.’"

**中文理解**：「但我不再——不，我不是原来的我了——他们没有把我重新拼回原来的样子——不像之前那样——问题就在这里，我不是原来的我了。」

**关键词**：they didn’t put me back together again / not like I was before / that’s the problem, I’m not the same

**为什么这样写**：全章的核心句，而这**不是关于飞船的**，是关于**身体**的：重组没有把她变回原样。而这一句被放在她最平静的时刻——闭着眼、缓慢呼吸之后——所以它不是歇斯底里，而是一个结论。

**读者视角提示**：这一句是马瑟后来整个研究问题的核心：「被捕获之后发生了什么」——而答案不是「见到了外星人」，是「回不来了」。

---

> **原句 14:** "‘I’m sitting in front of the TV, and I feel my feet start to burn, like they’re on fire. Then the fire moves up into my legs and into my body and it’s scary. I feel my heart burning in my chest, like burning glass, burning up into my head until it reaches here.’ She pats the crown of her head. ‘I feel it here, and I’m on fire. > **原句 24:** "‘For a long time, Dr Mather, I was traumatised. **关键词**：I was traumatised / I began to see that for some reason I had been chosen / helping to save our genes / maybe dozens of us. Maybe hundreds / I just wish they had left me as I was before / when all the bits of me came apart so they could communicate with me / I’m not the same. I have these episodes

**为什么这样写**：这一段把**信念的成立过程**完整写了出来：先是创伤，然后是**重新诠释**（我被选中），然后是对代价的接受（希望别改我），最后是后遗症。而「也许几十个，也许几百个」这半句把个人的遭遇变成了一场运动。

> **原句 26:** "And, glancing down at her screen, I see she has written the words avatar syndrome."

**中文理解**：我低头瞥了一眼她的屏幕，看见她写下了「avatar syndrome」（化身症候群）这几个字。

**关键词**：glancing down at her screen / I see she has written the words avatar syndrome

**为什么这样写**：全章最冷的一击，藏在露西的速记里。她没有写「癔症」，也没有写「妄想」，她写的是**一个术语**——化身症候群：一个人把自己的化身（替代形象）当成了真身。而这个词精准地描述了伊薇特的状态：她回不来了，她成了自己在飞船上的那个版本。

**读者视角提示**：请注意这三个词是由**在场却一言未发**的见证人写下的。全书所有重要的判断，都出自她那份不引人注目的速记。

> **原句 27:** "I reproach myself for what seems to be something new, a deficit in my compassion. The moment she began so confidently to feed the birds I pegged her as a histrionic narcissist."

**中文理解**：我责备自己，因为那似乎是某种新出现的东西：**共情力的亏空**。我原本是怀着对体验者的喜爱开始的，喜欢他们的诚实、他们的敞开、他们的信任；可我对伊薇特产生了反感。就在她那么自信地开始喂鸟的那一刻，我把她归为**癔症型自恋者**。

**关键词**：I reproach myself / a deficit in my compassion / a feeling of fondness for my experiencers / a delight in their honesty, their openness, their trust / but I took a dislike to Yvette / The moment she began so confidently to feed the birds / I pegged her as a histrionic narcissist

**为什么这样写**：作者让马瑟**当场**承认自己判断失误的可能——「共情力的亏空」这个词极准：他失去的不是判断力，是**共情**。而「就在她开始喂鸟的那一刻」这个时间点，把他的反感钉在了全书最不该发生误判的那一幕上。

**读者视角提示**：这一段是全书对**研究者本人**最不留情的一笔，也是「以共情为方法」这条路线第一次出现裂口。

> **原句 28:** "I dream that I am in a windowless room, crowded with dishevelled figures pressed shoulder to shoulder, jostling and anxious, the women crying out, the children with the fearful stare of the bewildered. I have climbed onto a rickety chair set against one wall, and with a small, blunt pocketknife I am trying to cut a window out into the world through layers of thick black plastic. But as I tear away each layer, another appears, and another, and another…"

**中文理解**：我梦见自己在一间没有窗的屋子里，挤满了衣衫不整的人，肩挨着肩，拥挤、焦虑；女人在喊叫，孩子带着迷惘者那种惊惧的目光。我爬上一把靠着墙的摇摇晃晃的椅子，手里拿一把小而钝的折刀，试图在一层又一层的厚黑塑料上割开一扇通向外界的窗。可每当我撕掉一层，另一层就冒出来，又一层，又一层……

**关键词**：I dream that I am in a windowless room / crowded with dishevelled figures pressed shoulder to shoulder / jostling and anxious / the children with the fearful stare of the bewildered / I have climbed onto a rickety chair set against one wall / with a small, blunt pocketknife I am trying to cut a window out into the world / through layers of thick black plastic / as I tear away each layer, another appears

**为什么这样写**：全书的**最后镜像**：马瑟被关进一间没有窗的屋子——而伊薇特刚刚在梦里被关进一个不断溶解的身体。他爬上一把椅子（与「站在箱子上」的露西同一个姿势），用一把钝刀在塑料上挖窗。而最后那个「撕掉一层又长出一层」的结构，就是**捕获**这个母题的最终定义：你无法从里面出来。

**读者视角提示**：请注意那把刀是**钝的**（blunt）——他挖不穿。而结尾的省略号说明：他也出不去。

## 一句话总结

全书最长的一场会谈：一个数学教师在诊室里把自己的捕获重演了一遍——骨头溶解、身体散成碎片又被拼回、频率被改写——然后平静地交代了一套程序：三次来访，取卵、受精、三个月后用长针取出胎儿放进飞船的保育管，肚脐上留下黑淤青；她喂了窗台上的黑birds，说「我是个养育者，这就是他们选中我的原因」；而三个小时之后，那个一直以共情为方法的研究者，发现自己对她产生了反感——露西在速记里写下的判词是：化身症候群。
