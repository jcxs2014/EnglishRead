# 《Don't Leave Her Alone》金句精选

## 一、先看见身体，再看见人（ch00-ch02）

**①**

> "She counted nine hoops, all different sizes, five in one ear and four in the other. They appeared to be silver, but she wasn't sure, since everything was scabbed over, brown and black. The appointment she couldn't remember must have been long, unhygienic, and cruel."（ch00）

**中文**：她数出九只耳环，大小各不相同，一只耳朵五只，另一只四只。它们看上去是银的，但她不敢确定，因为一切都结了痂，棕的、黑的。她想不起来的那场「预约」，一定又长、又不卫生、又残忍。

**上下文**：序章，她还没有名字，脸糊着黑红的东西，耳朵上多出一圈新孔。

**为什么重要**：全书的谜面浓缩在这一句里——「预约」是别人给她安排的，戒指是别人给她戴的，连她自己都不记得发生过什么。

**呼应关系**：与 "If Alba had taken refuge for too long in the memory of La Tortuga Ligera, it was because that was where she had last felt completely free, at ease, and self-sufficient. The only place she’d experienced anything similar since then—sporadically, for minutes at a time—was at the mall."（ch12）是同一夜的另一视角：那边记不得的预约，这边被写成童年营地里的一次「被关住」。

**②**

> "Then, hearing someone humming, she turned and saw the girl."（ch00）

**中文**：这时，听见有人哼着歌，她转过头，看见了那个女孩。

**上下文**：序章最后一句。店里的灯一段段亮起来，末尾她转身。

**为什么重要**：全书用最轻的一句收掉最重的一夜，也把「谁在哼歌」这个钩子留到第十二章才兑现。

**呼应关系**：与 "Her face was round, framed by two symmetrical locks of hair, a stunning explosion of freckles covered her nose, and she was wearing silver hoop earrings. Alba’s blood went cold."（ch11）里那个戴银圈耳环的女孩是同一个人。

**③**

> "She longed for a harmless flame, something small she could easily put out, to prove to herself that she wasn't ridiculous; sure, she had missed the start of the play, but she'd also averted a total catastrophe."（ch01）

**中文**：她甚至盼着有一场无害的小火，某种她随手就能扑灭的东西，好向自己证明自己并不荒唐——是啊，她错过了演出开头，但她也避免了一场大祸。

**上下文**：第一章，戴安娜在 Balmes 街折返、最终错过女儿的圣诞汇演之后。

**为什么重要**：这是全书的「公共症状」：一件小小的失控（忘了关直发夹）被她自动换算成一场灾难，而她需要的只是一个小到可以自己扑灭的火星来当证据。

**呼应关系**：与 "“I’m not strong, I’m resistant,” she would say to herself. Deep down, she wanted to blow up one day, so everyone could see that she wasn’t lying, she said she was exhausted because she was exhausted."（ch04）里那句「我并不强，我只是更能扛」是同一副心理的两面。

**④**

> "The grandmother always a bodily extension of the mother, a large and obedient appendage. The three parts forming a single organism, a many-tentacled jellyfish, a socially accepted and fully integrated creature that multiplied across the neighborhood. You’d catch sight of them at the playground, the grocery store, the school gates, the clinic."（ch02）

**中文**：祖母永远是母亲身体的延伸，一个巨大而顺从的附肢。三者组成同一个生命体——一只多触手的水母，一个被社会完全接纳、彻底融入其中的生物，在整片街区里不断繁殖。游乐场、杂货店、校门口、诊所，你随时会撞见它们。

**上下文**：第二章，戴安娜清点家族里那些「祖母—母亲—孩子」的链条。

**为什么重要**：这本书最惊人的一个比喻，把女性血缘写成了一只会分蘖的触手生物，也让后面「多出来的触手」不再是奇幻设定，而是家族遗传的具象化。

**呼应关系**：与 "She, who had long forgotten what a party was, who hadn’t danced since her wedding day, had a disco ball in her belly. The dancing tentacles hypnotized her, then she felt a spasm of pain as they tightened."（ch11）里肚子里那颗由触手组成的迪斯科球遥相呼应。

**⑤**

> "Once she had freed Brisa of it, Diana, in complete shock, contemplated the clot that stretched from her palm to her elbow. She leaned against the table and carefully flipped her arm, letting the clot fall to the surface. She found it strange that even in that movement, it didn’t break, didn’t explode."（ch02）

**中文**：把女儿鼻子里那块东西弄出来之后，戴安娜完全懵了，盯着那块从自己掌心一直长到肘部的血块。她靠着桌沿，小心地把手臂一翻，让血块落到桌面上。她觉得奇怪：这么翻一下，它居然没有断，也没有炸开。

**上下文**：第二章，两个女孩在同一分钟流鼻血之后。

**为什么重要**：全书第一次出现「身体不服从常识」的描写，而叙述者的第一反应不是害怕，是想验证它到底算不算普通的一团血。

**呼应关系**：与 "But I’m bleeding. It doesn’t hurt, but I’m bleeding. I unbuttoned my pants because they were too tight and it was starting to get uncomfortable, and I noticed my stomach was warm and wet."（ch08）里那份不疼的出血属于同一类提问：血都流成这样了，为什么不疼。

## 二、这个家的怪癖与会说的话（ch03-ch06）

**⑥**

> "“Come on. I already told the kids you were coming for lunch.” “But we’re coming on Christmas Eve, we can really celebrate then.” “You can’t celebrate two things at once. It’s bad luck. And you know it.”"（ch03）

**中文**：妹妹催人：孩子那边我已经说了你要来吃午饭。戴安娜接话：可我们是平安夜来，到时候可以好好庆祝。妹妹挡回去：你不能一件事同时庆祝两件，不吉利，你知道的。

**上下文**：第三章，整章都是这两姐妹通电话。

**为什么重要**：一句迷信就把两个成年人的时间表错开——这个家的亲密不是靠说出来的，是靠一串不容质疑的小规矩维系的。

**呼应关系**：与 "“In case he did swallow it. It’s too dangerous. They’re full of germs, they’re toxic.” She started crying harder. “It was the evil eye. I knew it. Those damn old women.”"（ch05）里那句「这是邪眼」同源：她们不害怕鬼，只害怕不吉利。

**⑦**

> "“I’m not strong, I’m resistant,” she would say to herself. Deep down, she wanted to blow up one day, so everyone could see that she wasn’t lying, she said she was exhausted because she was exhausted."（ch04）

**中文**：「我并不强，我只是更能扛。」她总在心里对自己这么说。心底里，她希望哪一天能爆发一次，好让所有人都看见她没有撒谎：她说累，因为她是真的累。

**上下文**：第四章，丈夫提醒她该回去看心理医生之后。

**为什么重要**：一句话把「能扛」和「强」拆开，也把她的爆发欲、疲惫感和隐约的自欺同时交代清楚。

**呼应关系**：与 "She needed someone—most of all, Carmen—to confirm she was still around, with their presence, their shouting, their affection. Lying on the floor of Splau on Christmas Eve, her body broken and covered in monstrous babies, she longed to reclaim what was hers: her time, her body, her privacy, her eyes, her desire to look at herself in shop mirrors."（ch13）里那句「她需要有人证明自己还在」是同一件需求的两种说法。

**⑧**

> "“In case he did swallow it. It’s too dangerous. They’re full of germs, they’re toxic.” She started crying harder. “It was the evil eye. I knew it. Those damn old women.”"（ch05）

**中文**：以防万一他真的吞下去了。太危险了，硬币上全是细菌、全都有毒。妹妹哭得更厉害，然后说：这是邪眼，她早就知道了，都怪那些该死的老太婆。

**上下文**：第五章，生日饭吃到一半，孩子吞下一枚一欧硬币，全家翻箱倒柜地找。

**为什么重要**：恐惧、寻物、迷信三件事在同一个动作里同时发生——找硬币是理性的，怪老太婆是荒谬的，而两种解释居然能同时成立。

**呼应关系**：与 "“Come on. I already told the kids you were coming for lunch.” “But we’re coming on Christmas Eve, we can really celebrate then.” “You can’t celebrate two things at once. It’s bad luck. And you know it.”"（ch03）那句「不吉利，你知道的」构成这个家的世界观底色。

**⑨**

> "Your sister is never alone. If she’s not with your mom or the kids, she’s with your cousin or someone else, but she’s never alone. I don’t think I’ve ever seen her alone."（ch05）

**中文**：丈夫对她说：你妹妹从不独处——不是跟母亲和孩子们在一起，就是跟亲戚或别人在一起，反正总有人陪着；他甚至说，认识她这么多年，从没见她一个人待过。

**上下文**：第五章，丈夫试图解释姐姐为什么永远有人陪。

**为什么重要**：全书最要紧的安全条款被这样平平淡淡说出来：谁都不许一个人待着——因为一旦落单，那边就会来收。

**呼应关系**：与 "Out of inertia, or because they had to. Because doing otherwise had never crossed their minds. They did it out of a deep affection she hadn’t recognized, and since she hadn’t recognized it, she had taken it for granted."（ch16）里那种「被当成理所当然」的深情互为表里。

**⑩**

> "She couldn’t wrap her head around the brutality of children, the way you could detect, in creatures that weighed less than fifty pounds, the behaviors of aggressive, devastated, lost adults. Her blood went cold in the park because it was such an obvious simulacrum—a fenced-in, miniature depiction—of an angry, hostile society."（ch06）

**中文**：她始终活在一种恐惧里：孩子会不会出事。这种恐惧早就有，只是随着压力陡增而加剧。她想不通孩子的残忍——在体重不到五十磅的小小身体里，她能认出成年人才有的那种侵略、崩溃和迷失。她在公园里会觉得血都凉了，因为那座围栏围起的小天地，简直就是一个充满敌意的社会的缩影。

**上下文**：第六章，戴安娜讲女儿学校里的恐惧，讲到那座有围栏的儿童乐园。

**为什么重要**：她第一次把「养孩子」翻译成「在一份微缩的敌意社会里值夜班」，也第一次意识到恐惧已经越过家门。

**呼应关系**：与 "The grandmother always a bodily extension of the mother, a large and obedient appendage. The three parts forming a single organism, a many-tentacled jellyfish, a socially accepted and fully integrated creature that multiplied across the neighborhood. You’d catch sight of them at the playground, the grocery store, the school gates, the clinic."（ch02）的水母比喻同源：家变成一个整体，就意味着它整体地害怕。

## 三、身体先说话（ch07-ch09）

**⑪**

> "When she tried to turn her around by the shoulders, Brisa collapsed. She collapsed as if she’d been deactivated, as if the energy that had kept her standing had suddenly been cut off."（ch07）

**中文**：母亲伸手想把她转过来，女儿却一下子瘫了下去，像被关掉了开关一样，撑着她站立的那股劲儿忽然被切断了。

**上下文**：第七章，深夜，戴安娜拿着手电进女儿的房间。

**为什么重要**：一个母亲用「被关掉开关」来形容自己女儿的身体——既精确又残忍，也是全书最让人不敢往下读的一刻。

**呼应关系**：与 "Her stomach then clenched as soon as she realized she didn’t know quite what she was touching. It had the warmth of Brisa, but it felt nothing like her back or even her chest."（ch07）里摸到陌生背脊的下一行相连。

**⑫**

> "Her stomach then clenched as soon as she realized she didn’t know quite what she was touching. It had the warmth of Brisa, but it felt nothing like her back or even her chest."（ch07）

**中文**：她伸手去安抚女儿，手一碰，胃就抽紧了——她说不清自己摸到的到底是什么；那是女儿身上的温度，可既不像背，也不像胸。

**上下文**：同一夜，她把手放在趴着的女儿背上。

**为什么重要**：恐怖不靠描写，靠触觉的失灵：温度是对的，形状是错的，于是母亲的胃先于眼睛做出了反应。

**呼应关系**：与 "The lights everywhere went out: in the mall, out on the street. Splau became the mouth of a wolf, and the only thing still lit was the tree with three women inside. For a while, all Alba could see was her sister’s pallid face and her own face, twice duplicated, broken in two different ways"（ch16）里两张以不同方式破碎的脸，属于同一条「身体开始变形」的线。

**⑬**

> "But I’m bleeding. It doesn’t hurt, but I’m bleeding. I unbuttoned my pants because they were too tight and it was starting to get uncomfortable, and I noticed my stomach was warm and wet."（ch08）

**中文**：可她在流血。不疼，可她在流血。裤子勒得难受，她解开扣子，然后发现小腹又热又湿。

**上下文**：第八章，平安夜出租车上，她隔着裤子按住小腹。

**为什么重要**：句子和内容一样反常：先承认事实，再补充最不合理的那部分——不疼。她后来一路把这个伤口带到斯普劳，带到那间圣诞树里。

**呼应关系**：与 "Once she had freed Brisa of it, Diana, in complete shock, contemplated the clot that stretched from her palm to her elbow. She leaned against the table and carefully flipped her arm, letting the clot fall to the surface. She found it strange that even in that movement, it didn’t break, didn’t explode."（ch02）血块不碎不炸是同一种「不遵守常识」。

**⑭**

> "Running her fingertips over the injury, Diana realized that it was an open wound, about four fingers wide and wrapping around her waist like a belt made of pulp and blood. The top of her pants and the bottom of her shirt were stained, but the wound wasn’t actively bleeding."（ch08）

**中文**：她用指尖沿着伤口摸过去，才认出那是一道敞开的裂口，约四指宽，像一条由纸浆和血做成的腰带，绕过她的腰。裤腰和衬衫下摆都染上了颜色，可伤口并没有在大量出血。

**上下文**：同一章，出门前她终于看清自己的腰。

**为什么重要**：一个精确的宽度（大约四指）加一个拙劣的比喻（纸浆和血做的腰带），写出了既恐怖又家常的质感——像有人替她缝了一圈歪歪扭扭的边。

**呼应关系**：与 "Carmen and Diana opened their coats and lifted their sweaters. Where there had been a sash of flesh and blood—an open wound that oozed, howled; a gash that sometimes hurt and sometimes didn’t; a gaping hole that housed tentacles—a delicate scar had now formed."（ch14）三姐妹身上那道同样宽度的银色疤痕，构成了同一条身体线索的首尾。

**⑮**

> "“Mom just said Alba is missing.”"（ch09）

**中文**：对方说，妈妈刚说阿尔巴不见了。

**上下文**：第九章，全章只有这一通电话、六个自然段；戴安娜手上全是血。

**为什么重要**：全书最短的一章给出一句最短的坏消息，句子越短，世界越塌。

**呼应关系**：与 "Then, I don’t know why, she suddenly got very calm. One second to the next. I almost lost it. It was so weird, like she’d been injected with morphine or something."（ch10）里母亲忽然平静下来的描述连在一起看，才知道那一刻坏消息已经传到了每个人那里。

## 四、斯普劳那一夜（ch10-ch13）

**⑯**

> "The yellow M on its red background liquefied, trickling downward and leaving behind just a white billboard with red text and a running turtle—the billboard for La Tortuga Ligera. Alba closed her eyes for a moment, and when she opened them, the ad for the campground was still there. She went inside and shut the curtains."（ch10）

**中文**：那块麦当劳广告牌上的黄色标志化开了，顺着牌面往下淌，淌完只剩一块白底红字的牌子，上面画着一只奔跑的海龟——那是露营地的广告牌。她闭上眼再睁开，露营地的广告还挂在那里。她转身回屋，把窗帘拉上了。

**上下文**：第十章，一家人散在商场外的草地上。

**为什么重要**：最日常的公共广告忽然换成人人现身的地方，恐怖不是从天上来的，是从一块已经签好合约的牌子上淌出来的。

**呼应关系**：与 "One was for the Finestrelles mall and the other for Splau. Her apartment was right in the middle, two exits away from each shopping center. Alba laughed and went back inside."（ch17）里两块夹住她家的商场广告牌，是同一手法的两次出现。

**⑰**

> "Her face was round, framed by two symmetrical locks of hair, a stunning explosion of freckles covered her nose, and she was wearing silver hoop earrings. Alba’s blood went cold."（ch11）

**中文**：她看清了那张脸：圆脸，两缕对称的头发，雀斑铺满鼻梁，耳朵上是一对银圈耳环；她的血一下子凉了。她知道这是谁——她找了这个人很多年，却从没想过会在商场隔间的地上这样见到她。

**上下文**：第十一章，阿尔巴隔着卫生间隔间的门缝往外看。

**为什么重要**：全书最重的一次认领：不是重逢，是认出——她的血凉了，因为终于见到了，也因为对方根本不是活人。

**呼应关系**：与 "She counted nine hoops, all different sizes, five in one ear and four in the other. They appeared to be silver, but she wasn't sure, since everything was scabbed over, brown and black. The appointment she couldn't remember must have been long, unhygienic, and cruel."（ch00）那九只银圈耳环是同一套记号。

**⑱**

> "She, who had long forgotten what a party was, who hadn’t danced since her wedding day, had a disco ball in her belly. The dancing tentacles hypnotized her, then she felt a spasm of pain as they tightened."（ch11）

**中文**：她撩起衣服，看见自己被从中间剖开，肚子上一个圆洞能看见内脏；灯灭再亮时，银色的触手缠在她的肠子上，像一颗迪斯科球。

**上下文**：同一章，卫生间隔间里。

**为什么重要**：一个从来不敢独处的人，肚子里长出了舞会——全书把「团聚」的渴望直接写成了怪物。

**呼应关系**：与 "The grandmother always a bodily extension of the mother, a large and obedient appendage. The three parts forming a single organism, a many-tentacled jellyfish, a socially accepted and fully integrated creature that multiplied across the neighborhood. You’d catch sight of them at the playground, the grocery store, the school gates, the clinic."（ch02）的多触手水母是同一意象的生理版本。

**⑲**

> "As the years went by, the ghosts of La Tortuga got used to the place changing suddenly, even coming to see the disappearances as normal. Some vanished when their originals lost their memories or their minds, or when they died. The end came for them when they were swimming in the ocean, celebrating a goal, putting on makeup in secret in the shared bathrooms."（ch12）

**中文**：这些年过去，营地的那些幽灵已经习惯了这里忽然变样，甚至把消失当成寻常。有的在「原型」失去记忆或心智时跟着散了，有的死在某个具体瞬间——正在海里庆祝进球，或者正在公用浴室里偷偷化妆。

**上下文**：第十二章，女孩一边拔自己的耳环一边讲营地的规矩。

**为什么重要**：规则解释了一切为什么可怕：真正的规则是「不要被忘记」，而不是「不要死」。

**呼应关系**：与 "If Alba had taken refuge for too long in the memory of La Tortuga Ligera, it was because that was where she had last felt completely free, at ease, and self-sufficient. The only place she’d experienced anything similar since then—sporadically, for minutes at a time—was at the mall."（ch12）里阿尔巴二十年来在营地记忆里躲藏，是同一件事的两面。

**⑳**

> "At one point the silvery, shining appendages also joined in the embrace. They were much less gentle. They squeezed and squeezed and squeezed the woman’s waist, until the woman split in half."（ch13）

**中文**：拥抱进行到一半时，那几根银亮的触手也挤了进来；它们一点也不温柔，而是一下、一下、一下地勒紧那个女人的腰，一直勒到她整个人从中间裂成两半。

**上下文**：第十三章，平安夜的商场二楼，阿尔巴与一个穿脏睡袍的女人相拥。

**为什么重要**：全书最暴力也最亲昵的一个动作：拥抱中途被截断，而动手的正是「想留住你」的那部分身体。

**呼应关系**：与 "She needed someone—most of all, Carmen—to confirm she was still around, with their presence, their shouting, their affection. Lying on the floor of Splau on Christmas Eve, her body broken and covered in monstrous babies, she longed to reclaim what was hers: her time, her body, her privacy, her eyes, her desire to look at herself in shop mirrors."（ch13）里「她需要有人证明自己还在」同场对照，一句说需求，一句给出代价。

## 五、我们家从来没有一个人（ch14-ch17）

**㉑**

> "Carmen and Diana opened their coats and lifted their sweaters. Where there had been a sash of flesh and blood—an open wound that oozed, howled; a gash that sometimes hurt and sometimes didn’t; a gaping hole that housed tentacles—a delicate scar had now formed."（ch14）

**中文**：卡门和戴安娜脱下外套、掀起毛衣，露出同一道疤——那曾经是一条血肉做的腰带，是一道渗着血、痛一阵不痛一阵的裂口，是一个曾经住着触手的洞；如今那里结成了一道精致的疤。

**上下文**：第十四章，平安夜的草地上，两个女人互看彼此的腹部。

**为什么重要**：三个人身上一模一样的银疤，把「营地」从传说变成身体档案：她母亲不是听说过，她经历过。

**呼应关系**：与 "Running her fingertips over the injury, Diana realized that it was an open wound, about four fingers wide and wrapping around her waist like a belt made of pulp and blood. The top of her pants and the bottom of her shirt were stained, but the wound wasn’t actively bleeding."（ch08）那道绕过腰身的裂口对读，可知伤口的形状一直是被「做」出来的。

**㉒**

> "I had it in my stomach all that summer. I noticed it almost as soon as we got there, but I didn’t let it out. That kind of thing takes courage."（ch15）

**中文**：母亲承认：那个夏天它就一直待在我肚子里。几乎是一到营地我就发现了，可我忍着没放出来——这种事需要勇气。

**上下文**：第十五章，草地上一边吃披萨一边谈这件事。

**为什么重要**：整本书最重的一次坦白，而它是用谈食物的口吻说出来的——家族史从此不再是传说，是家规。

**呼应关系**：与 "There was a tail coming out from inside me, a little tail that wagged back and forth. And blood was streaming out, down my legs, there was blood in the water, too. It was horrific, absolutely horrific."（ch17）里母亲讲肚脐里那条会摆的小尾巴，是同一段记忆的两次版本。

**㉓**

> "Out of inertia, or because they had to. Because doing otherwise had never crossed their minds. They did it out of a deep affection she hadn’t recognized, and since she hadn’t recognized it, she had taken it for granted."（ch16）

**中文**：尽管如此，她们还是做了：没有抱怨，出于惯性，或者因为非做不可——因为她们压根没想过要不做。她们这样陪着她，是出于一种她从未认出过的深情；而正因为从未认出，她才把这一切都当成了理所当然。

**上下文**：第十六章，圣诞树里几百个女人和她们的同伴。

**为什么重要**：全书最痛的一处反讽：深情之所以是深情，正因为它没有被认出来；而被当成理所当然的那一刻，也是它开始崩塌的那一刻。

**呼应关系**：与 "Your sister is never alone. If she’s not with your mom or the kids, she’s with your cousin or someone else, but she’s never alone. I don’t think I’ve ever seen her alone."（ch05）里「你妹妹从不独处」是同一份亲情的正反两面。

**㉔**

> "I just wanted the controllers."（ch16）

**中文**：全章最后一句只有五个词：我想要的不过是那两只手柄。

**上下文**：第十六章末尾，她从楼上走下来，脚下是死寂的触手，眼前是几百个女人。

**为什么重要**：整本书的所有光怪陆离，最后被一件最不浪漫的商品收尾；这既是荒诞，也是慈悲——她的欲望小到可怜，也因此还能被叫作欲望。

**呼应关系**：与 "I don’t know. How could I have explained it? I just told you, thirty years later, and still you don’t believe me. Imagine if I’d tried to tell you back then."（ch10）里「三十年后讲出来你还是不信」同属那种过于日常的收束。

**㉕**

> "There was a tail coming out from inside me, a little tail that wagged back and forth. And blood was streaming out, down my legs, there was blood in the water, too. It was horrific, absolutely horrific."（ch17）

**中文**：母亲讲自己当年那个夏天：肚脐里钻出一条小尾巴，还左右摆动；血顺着腿往下流，海里也被染了血。她说那场面可怕得不得了。

**上下文**：第十七章，平安夜的餐桌边，一家人用 Alexa 放歌。

**为什么重要**：全书最后一段恐怖记忆被包在节日噪音里讲出来，而三个女人笑到断气——这本书对「寻常」的处理才是它真正的主题。

**呼应关系**：与 "I had it in my stomach all that summer. I noticed it almost as soon as we got there, but I didn’t let it out. That kind of thing takes courage."（ch15）里母亲那句「我肚子里也有一只章鱼」，是两代人对同一件事的讲法对照。
