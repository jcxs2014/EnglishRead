# 《Don't Leave Her Alone》概述

> 作者：Desirée de Fez ｜ 出版社：（西班牙语原作，英译本）｜ 人物译名：本册一律保留英文原名
> 叙述视角：第三人称限制视角，主跟 Alba/Diana/Carmen；部分章节贴紧 Alba 单独行动。

## 全书梗概

**序章（Prologue）：从血污中醒来。** Alba 在一家店里从昏迷中醒来：灯光分阶段亮起，像有人在有节奏地拨动开关。她想起上一次带女儿 Brigitte 来这里试戴塑料皇冠。睁眼后她看见成排的芭蕾裙溅满红色，蝴蝶翅膀与熊形背包覆着一层干硬的血痂——那正是她和 Brigitte 曾试戴自拍的东西。那是"她女儿最爱的店"，在这里 Brigitte 会把廉价首饰误当成真货。她的头在跳痛，脸上是一大片暗色血污，耳朵上多出九个从未见过的耳环（五只一耳、四只另一耳），全是新穿孔、结着褐黑色的痂。那场她完全不记得的「预约」必定漫长、不卫生而残忍。章节最后，她听见有人哼歌，转头看见了那个女孩。**本章 establishes the supernatural premise: Alba 在血污与童年记忆中醒来，那个女孩第一次现身。**

> "She came to in the dark, sprawled across the floor."（ch00）

**ch01–ch04：圣诞前的家庭日常与身体警报。** ch01：平安夜前一天的圣诞演出，Diana 因强迫性怀疑（直发器是否未关）折返家中查证而迟到，onstage 的女儿 Brisa 因母亲缺席，成了"唯一不唱歌的孩子"。ch02：Alba 与母亲 Carmen 为换游戏手柄在 Sant Ildefons 与 Finestrelles 商场奔走，Alba 在商场 dissociate、遭遇"寡妇"二人组、被施"邪眼"并流鼻血；章节后半回溯姐妹的女儿同日流鼻血（Brigitte 体操课全身血染、Brisa 睡中抽出 30 厘米血凝块）。ch03：Alba 与 Diana 通电话，Alba 要 Carmen 代看孩子并放弃圣诞聚会，姐妹就"孩子能否离开母亲"争执。ch04：Diana 与 Carlos 讨论 Alba 对她的控制，地铁上 Diana 看见持刀青年并拖家带口逃下车。**本阶段 establishes family dynamics and the somatic alarms (bleeding, the evil eye) that the supernatural later explains.**

> "Jesus, Alba, couldn't you have gone another time?"（ch03）

**ch05–ch07：Christmas Eve 前的紧张。** ch05：TV conflict（电视之争——Carmen 与 Alba 嘲笑 Diana 舍不得换掉那台老三星），urgent care panic（急诊恐慌——baby 吞了 euro），Alba autophobia（独处恐惧——Diana 查证到姐姐自十几岁起就无法独处），Diana father ghost（父亲 ghost——半年前某个清晨，浴室里只有烟味）；ch06：Carlos 与 Diana 讨论给 Tomás 吃褪黑素，Diana 坚持 Brisa 会梦游，Carlos 斥为胡思乱想（Carlos rationalizes）；ch07：Brisa 真的在睡梦中起身，醒来时脸朝下、膝盖与脚尖朝天，Diana 摸到"不知道自己在摸什么"的东西（uncanny touch），随后在女儿房里的垫子上握着她手躺了一夜。**本阶段 builds tension: the ghost appears, the family starts to rationalize.**

> "Diana lived in terror of the possibility that something would happen to her children."（ch06）

> "Her stomach then clenched as soon as she realized she didn't know quite what she was touching."（ch07）

**ch08–ch09：Christmas Eve，Alba missing。** Christmas Eve taxi chaos（出租车混乱），earring motif（耳环意象），Diana wound（Diana 的伤口——waist, no pain, oozing, feeding on itself），Carlos horror（Carlos 的恐惧）。Alba goes missing（Alba 失踪）——cliffhanger（悬念）：Diana wound still bleeding when Carmen calls. **本阶段 is the inciting incident: Alba disappears on Christmas Eve, Diana is wounded.**

> "Mom just said Alba is missing."（ch09）

**ch10：Splau 商场外，Christmas Eve。** Family scattered (autonomous individuals)（家人分散——自主个体），car blood-streaked + Carmen wounded (same as Diana)（车血迹斑斑 + Carmen 受伤——与 Diana 相同），Camp La Tortuga Ligera backstory（营地 backstory），Diana ghost story (bingo women, pink light, axe)（Diana 讲鬼故事——bingo 女人、粉光、斧头），father ghost (cigarette smell)（父亲 ghost），the family waits outside Splau for news, and Juan reports that Alba is calm now and that he is more worried about their mother than about her（家人在 Splau 外等消息，Juan 说 Alba 看起来很平静，自己更担心的是母亲）。**本阶段 reveals the scope: multiple women wounded, the camp backstory, the bingo women.**

> "She's calm now. Too calm, I think."（ch10）

> "But I heard something like an axe, or like when they're cutting meat at the butcher"（ch10）

**ch11–ch13：Alba alone in Splau。** Alba's POV bathroom at Splau（Alba 视角——Splau 卫生间），wound with tentacles/sequins（伤口有触手/亮片），girl in red swimsuit with hoop earrings（红泳衣女孩戴圈形耳环——你忘了我），Claire's store（Claire 的店），song（歌曲），tentacles attack（触手攻击），faints（昏厥）。Alba wakes in Claire's, 9 hoop piercings（Alba 在 Claire's 醒来，9 个圈形穿孔）；girl reveals Camp La Tortuga Ligera ghost backstory (camp swallowed by airport expansion, ghosts of happy campers)（女孩揭示营地 ghost backstory——营地被机场扩建吞没，快乐露营者的 ghost）；Alba's autonomy loss（Alba 的自主性丧失）；girl leaves, Alba chases, tentacles emerge and drag the girl back, and Alba devours her in her sleep（女孩离开，Alba 追，触手出现并把女孩拖回，Alba 在睡梦中吞噬了她）。Alba on second floor sees doppelgänger woman (her own face) with blood-covered children; birth ritual（Alba 在二楼看到 doppelgänger 女人——她自己的脸——带着血淋淋的孩子；分娩仪式）；Alba screams, children attack (tiny red points)（Alba 尖叫，孩子攻击——红点）；doppelgänger wears Alba's wedding dress (Rosa Clará)（doppelgänger 穿 Alba 的婚纱——Rosa Clará）；the doppelgänger tells Alba that she doesn't know how to do anything（doppelgänger 告诉 Alba 她什么都不会）；Alba claims autonomy（Alba 声称自主权）；tentacles destroy doppelgänger (split in half, snap)（触手摧毁 doppelgänger——劈成两半，断裂）；Alba alone, picks up Switch controllers, finds ballet flats, hears scream, sees silhouettes（Alba 独自，捡起 Switch 控制器，找到芭蕾平底鞋，听到尖叫声，看到剪影）。**本阶段 is the novel's horror centerpiece: Alba confronts the girl's ghost, the doppelgänger, and claims her autonomy.**

> "She looked down at her stomach and saw she had a spectacular wound, from one side to the other."（ch11）

> "Because you forgot about me."（ch11）

> "She, who had long forgotten what a party was, who hadn't danced since her wedding day, had a disco ball in her belly."（ch11）

> "The earrings are just the start."（ch12）

> "Alba, who unknowingly devoured the girl as she slept."（ch12）

> "You don't know how to do anything."（ch13）

> "I don't want another life. I want mine. I want to go home."（ch13）

**ch14–ch15：Family reunites, revelations.** Carlos/Diana find Alba; family reunites; wounds healed (silvery scars, all three women identical, traced with glitter)（Carlos/Diana 找到 Alba；家人团聚；伤口愈合——银色疤痕，三个女人相同，用亮片描摹）；Alba calm; Juan's account (found Alba naked, hole in belly)（Alba 平静；Juan 的叙述——找到赤裸的 Alba，腹部有洞）；children saw pink lights in sky; pizza delivery; everyone eats Domino's（孩子看到天空粉光；披萨配送；所有人吃 Domino's）。Alba/Diana/Carmen eat pizza outside Splau; discuss the octopus, i.e. the tentacles（三人吃披萨；讨论 octopus——触手）；Carmen confesses she had octopus at camp (the summer she fainted)（Carmen 承认营地有 octopus——她昏厥的夏天）；Diana mentions telepathy（Diana 提及心灵感应）；Alba stays silent about full Splau experience（Alba 对 Splau 全部经历保持沉默）；key revelation: Diana, without knowing it, was with Alba in Splau that night（关键揭示：Diana 不知情的情况下，与 Alba 一起在 Splau 那夜）。**本阶段 reveals the family's hidden connections: Carmen had octopus, Diana was in Splau.**

> "She was completely naked from the waist up, covered in blood, with a hole in her belly."（ch14）

> "I had an octopus in my stomach, too"（ch15）

> "Diana, without knowing it, had been with her in Splau that night."（ch15）

**ch16–ch17：Final revelations and homecoming.** Alba at Splau's giant Christmas tree; sees women like her (doppelgängers, all mothers) with companions (frail, skeletal); collective scream; tentacles emerge as light show; women have scars/stumps (tentacles cut off); Alba realizes why the women scream; tentacles revive; women try to trap Alba; tentacles free her; Alba walks through them; chapter ends with a line about the controllers（Alba 在 Splau 巨大圣诞树下；看到像她一样的女人——doppelgängers，全是母亲——带着伴侣（虚弱、骨骼）；集体尖叫；触手作为灯光秀出现；女人有疤痕/残桩（触手被切掉）；Alba 意识到她们尖叫的原因；触手复活；女人试图困住 Alba；触手释放她；Alba 走过它们；章节以"我只想拿控制器"结束）。Family returns home; Alba hides Switch controllers on patio; family eats/drinks champagne; Carmen reveals she had octopus at camp (bingo lady helped); Alba reveals tentacles (glitter, dancing); family laughs and dances to Camela; chapter ends with Carmen asking about air fryer（家人回家；Alba 把 Switch 控制器藏在露台；家人吃喝香槟；Carmen 揭示营地有 octopus——bingo 女人帮助了她；Alba 揭示触手——亮片、跳舞；家人笑、跳 Camela；章节以 Carmen 问空气炸锅结束）。**本阶段 is the novel's ending: family reunited, supernatural accepted, normalcy returns.**

> "That's why they scream, she thought."（ch16）

> "I just wanted the controllers."（ch16）

> "Why don't you get an air fryer like the one your sister has?"（ch17）

## 主题

1. **母性与自主性（Motherhood and Autonomy）**：Alba 的 tentacles 是她 suppressed identity（被压抑的身份）的具象——它们是 glitter/sequins（亮片），是她早已忘了派对是什么的那部分人生。doppelgänger 穿着她的婚纱（Rosa Clará）说她什么都不会做——这是她 inner critic（内心批评者）。她 claim autonomy（声称自主权）时 tentacles destroy doppelgänger。**tentacles = her suppressed self; doppelgänger = her self-criticism; autonomy = destroying the critic.**

2. **家庭的隐藏联系（Family's Hidden Connections）**：Carmen had octopus at camp (bingo lady helped)（Carmen 在营地有 octopus——bingo 女人帮助）；Diana was with Alba in Splau that night (without knowing)（Diana 在 Splau 那夜与 Alba 一起——不知情）；three women have identical scars（三个女人有相同疤痕）。**The supernatural connects them across time and space.**

3. **身体恐怖（Body Horror）**：wound with tentacles/sequins（伤口有触手/亮片），肚子里的迪斯科球，birth ritual with blood-covered children（血淋淋孩子的分娩仪式），women with scars/stumps (tentacles cut off)（女人有疤痕/残桩——触手被切掉）。**Body horror as metaphor for suppressed identity.**

4. **西班牙/加泰罗尼亚背景（Spanish/Catalan Setting）**：Barcelona (Balmes, Splau, El Prat airport, Finestrelles mall, Camp La Tortuga Ligera)。**Setting is integral to the story.**

## 人物弧光

### Alba｜从 exhausted 到 revealing tentacles
ch01–ch04 Alba 为换游戏手柄在商场 dissociate、被施"邪眼"、卷入"两个女儿同日流血"之谜. ch05–ch07 家庭聚会（baby 吞 euro 送急诊）、父亲 ghost、Brisa 梦游；Alba 的 autophobia 起源在此揭出（十三岁起把床垫拖到客厅地板睡）。 ch08–ch09 Christmas Eve, missing. ch10–ch13 alone in Splau: bathroom, girl, Claire's, doppelgänger, autonomy. ch14–ch17 family reunites, reveals tentacles, laughs and dances. **Alba's arc: from exhausted mother to woman who claims her suppressed self.**

### Diana｜从 child-terror 到 part of mystery
ch01–ch04 Diana 强迫性检查（直发器）、错过 Brisa 的圣诞演出、地铁持刀事件. ch05–ch07 child-terror、nighttime checking Brisa、Brisa 梦游、留在垫子上、uncanny touch. ch08–ch09 wound, missing. ch10 讲罢 bingo 女鬼故事（bingo 女人、粉光、斧头）。ch14–ch15 与 Carlos 一起找到 Alba、得知她曾与自己同在 Splau；ch16–ch17 octopus revelation. **Diana's arc: from traumatized sister to participant in the mystery.**

### Carmen｜从 stoic 到 confessing
ch01–ch04 mother, practical, dismissive of supernatural（电视之争上批 Diana 不换新机）。ch05–ch07 当着家人把 Diana 的恐惧一律压下去。ch08–ch09 自己也受了同样的伤口。 ch10 与 Diana 共享伤口、听罢鬼故事. ch11–ch13 absent. ch14–ch17 reveals octopus, bingo lady, dances to Camela. **Carmen's arc: from stoic mother to woman who confesses her hidden supernatural experience.**

### The Girl Ghost｜从 dead girl 到 released
Prologue: Alba 在血污中醒来，听见有人哼歌、转头看见她. ch11–ch12: appears to Alba, reveals camp backstory, tells her she forgot about me, and is devoured by the tentacles. ch13: doppelgänger destroyed. ch16: women with scars/stumps, the reason they scream, tentacles revive. **The girl's arc: from waiting ghost to released (tentacles free her).**

### Carlos｜从 skeptic 到 witness
ch04 critical of Alba's control. ch05–ch07 置身事外（Brisa 梦游那一夜他一路睡过去）。ch08–ch09 horrified. ch10 随 Diana 赶到 Splau、冲岳母提高声音. ch11–ch13 absent. ch14–ch17 witness to scars, silent. **Carlos's arc: from skeptic to silent witness.**

### Juan｜从 practical to witness
ch01–ch04 practical father. ch05–ch07 practical. ch08–ch09 practical. ch10 被转述（他说 Alba 看起来很平静，自己更担心母亲）。 ch11–ch13 absent. ch14–ch17 finds Alba, sees scars, silent champagne. **Juan's arc: from practical father to silent witness.**
