# 《Don't Leave Her Alone》概述

> 作者：Desirée de Fez ｜ 出版社：（西班牙语原作，英译本）｜ 人物译名：本册一律保留英文原名
> 叙述视角：第三人称限制视角，主跟 Alba/Diana/Carmen；部分章节贴紧 Alba 单独行动。

## 全书梗概

**序章（Prologue）：Splau 商场，圣诞节前夜，一个女孩的死。** 一个女孩在 Splau 商场的卫生间里死去——她的母亲在商场的停车场被车撞死，女孩被留在商场里，独自一人。她身上有「octopus」（触手）——她把它们叫做「friends」——它们从她身体里长出来，像闪光的亮片。女孩死前把 earrings（耳环）留给 Alba（Alba 是商场的常客，女孩认识她）。女孩的 ghost 从那以后就留在 Splau，等待 Alba 回来。她死前在卫生间里，用指甲在墙上刻下「you forgot about me」。**本章 establishes the supernatural premise: a dead girl with tentacles, waiting for Alba.**

**ch01–ch04：Alba 的日常与家庭。** Alba 是一个母亲（三个孩子：Juanito, Brigitte, Tomás, 还有个 baby），与丈夫 Juan 住在一起，妹妹 Diana 与 Diana 的丈夫 Carlos 及他们的孩子 Brisa、Brigitte 经常来往。Alba 的母亲 Carmen 也住附近。Alba 有 autophobia（独处恐惧）——她小时候就睡在客厅地板上避免独处。Diana 有 childhood terror（童年创伤）——她总是夜里检查 Brisa。Alba 的父亲（已去世）的 ghost 时常出现——cigarette smell（烟味）。Carlos 是 family 的 skeptical voice（怀疑论者），认为一切都可以 rationalize。Alba 的 wound（伤口）开始出现——waist（腰部），no pain（无痛），oozing（渗液），feeding on itself（自我喂养）。**本阶段 establishes family dynamics and the first supernatural signs (wound, ghost).**

**ch05–ch07：Christmas Eve 前的紧张。** TV conflict（电视冲突），urgent care panic（急诊恐慌——baby 吞了 euro），Alba autophobia（独处恐惧），Diana father ghost（父亲 ghost），Carlos critique（Carlos 的批判）。Brisa sleepwalking（Brisa 梦游——站在床脚面对窗户），Diana stays on mat（Diana 留在垫子上），uncanny touch（不祥的触碰）。**本阶段 builds tension: the wound grows, the ghost appears, Carlos rationalizes.**

**ch08–ch09：Christmas Eve，Alba missing。** Christmas Eve taxi chaos（出租车混乱），earring motif（耳环意象），Diana wound（ Diana 的伤口——waist, no pain, oozing, feeding on itself），Carlos horror（Carlos 的恐惧）。Alba goes missing（Alba 失踪）——cliffhanger（悬念）：Diana wound still bleeding when Carmen calls. **本阶段 is the inciting incident: Alba disappears on Christmas Eve, Diana is wounded.**

**ch10：Splau 商场，Christmas Eve。** Family scattered (autonomous individuals)（家人分散——自主个体），car blood-streaked + Carmen wounded (same as Diana)（车血迹斑斑 + Carmen 受伤——与 Diana 相同），Camp La Tortuga Ligera backstory（营地 backstory），Diana ghost story (bingo women, pink light, axe)（Diana 的鬼故事——bingo 女人、粉光、斧头），father ghost (cigarette smell)（父亲 ghost），Alba found, "try not to be scared when we see her"（找到 Alba，"我们看到她时尽量别害怕"）。**本阶段 reveals the scope: multiple women wounded, the camp backstory, the bingo women.**

**ch11–ch13：Alba alone in Splau。** Alba's POV bathroom at Splau（Alba 视角——Splau 卫生间），wound with tentacles/sequins（伤口有触手/亮片），girl in red swimsuit with hoop earrings ("you forgot about me")（红泳衣女孩戴圈形耳环——"你忘了我"），Claire's store（Claire 的店），song（歌曲），tentacles attack（触手攻击），faints（昏厥）。Alba wakes in Claire's, 9 hoop piercings（Alba 在 Claire's 醒来，9 个圈形穿孔）；girl reveals Camp La Tortuga Ligera ghost backstory (camp swallowed by airport expansion, ghosts of happy campers)（女孩揭示营地 ghost backstory——营地被机场扩建吞没，快乐露营者的 ghost）；Alba's autonomy loss（Alba 的自主性丧失）；girl leaves, Alba chases, tentacles emerge and "devour" girl（女孩离开，Alba 追，触手出现并"吞噬"女孩）。Alba on second floor sees doppelgänger woman (her own face) with blood-covered children; birth ritual（Alba 在二楼看到 doppelgänger 女人——她自己的脸——带着血淋淋的孩子；分娩仪式）；Alba screams, children attack (red pinpricks)（Alba 尖叫，孩子攻击——红点）；doppelgänger wears Alba's wedding dress (Rosa Clará)（doppelgänger 穿 Alba 的婚纱——Rosa Clará）；"You don't know how to do anything"（"你什么都不会"）；Alba claims autonomy（Alba 声称自主权）；tentacles destroy doppelgänger (split in half, snap)（触手摧毁 doppelgänger——劈成两半，断裂）；Alba alone, picks up Switch controllers, finds ballet flats, hears scream, sees silhouettes（Alba 独自，捡起 Switch 控制器，找到芭蕾平底鞋，听到尖叫声，看到剪影）。**本阶段 is the novel's horror centerpiece: Alba confronts the girl's ghost, the doppelgänger, and claims her autonomy.**

**ch14–ch15：Family reunites, revelations.** Carlos/Diana find Alba; family reunites; wounds healed (silvery scars, all three women identical, traced with glitter)（Carlos/Diana 找到 Alba；家人团聚；伤口愈合——银色疤痕，三个女人相同，用亮片描摹）；Alba calm; Juan's account (found Alba naked, hole in belly)（Alba 平静；Juan 的叙述——找到赤裸的 Alba，腹部有洞）；children saw pink lights in sky; pizza delivery; everyone eats Domino's（孩子看到天空粉光；披萨配送；所有人吃 Domino's）。Alba/Diana/Carmen eat pizza outside Splau; discuss "octopus" (tentacles)（三人吃披萨；讨论"octopus"——触手）；Carmen confesses she had octopus at camp (the summer she fainted)（Carmen 承认营地有 octopus——她昏厥的夏天）；Diana mentions telepathy（Diana 提及心灵感应）；Alba stays silent about full Splau experience（Alba 对 Splau 全部经历保持沉默）；key revelation: Diana, without knowing it, was with Alba in Splau that night（关键揭示：Diana 不知情的情况下，与 Alba 一起在 Splau 那夜）。**本阶段 reveals the family's hidden connections: Carmen had octopus, Diana was in Splau.**

**ch16–ch17：Final revelations and homecoming.** Alba at Splau's giant Christmas tree; sees women like her (doppelgängers, all mothers) with companions (frail, skeletal); collective scream; tentacles emerge as light show; women have scars/stumps (tentacles cut off); Alba realizes "That's why they scream"; tentacles revive; women try to trap Alba; tentacles free her; Alba walks through them; chapter ends "I just wanted the controllers."（Alba 在 Splau 巨大圣诞树下；看到像她一样的女人——doppelgängers，全是母亲——带着伴侣（虚弱、骨骼）；集体尖叫；触手作为灯光秀出现；女人有疤痕/残桩（触手被切掉）；Alba 意识到"这就是她们尖叫的原因"；触手复活；女人试图困住 Alba；触手释放她；Alba 走过它们；章节以"我只想拿控制器"结束）。Family returns home; Alba hides Switch controllers on patio; family eats/drinks champagne; Carmen reveals she had octopus at camp (bingo lady helped); Alba reveals tentacles (glitter, dancing); family laughs and dances to Camela; chapter ends with Carmen asking about air fryer（家人回家；Alba 把 Switch 控制器藏在露台；家人吃喝香槟；Carmen 揭示营地有 octopus——bingo 女人帮助了她；Alba 揭示触手——亮片、跳舞；家人笑、跳 Camela；章节以 Carmen 问空气炸锅结束）。**本阶段 is the novel's ending: family reunited, supernatural accepted, normalcy returns.**

## 主题

1. **母性与自主性（Motherhood and Autonomy）**：Alba 的 tentacles 是她 suppressed identity（被压抑的身份）的具象——它们是 glitter/sequins（亮片），是她"forgotten party life"（被遗忘的派对生活）。doppelgänger 穿着她的婚纱（Rosa Clará）说"You don't know how to do anything"——这是她 inner critic（内心批评者）。她 claim autonomy（声自主权）时 tentacles destroy doppelgänger。**tentacles = her suppressed self; doppelgänger = her self-criticism; autonomy = destroying the critic.**

2. **家庭的隐藏联系（Family's Hidden Connections）**：Carmen had octopus at camp (bingo lady helped)（Carmen 在营地有 octopus——bingo 女人帮助）；Diana was with Alba in Splau that night (without knowing)（Diana 在 Splau 那夜与 Alba 一起——不知情）；three women have identical scars（三个女人有相同疤痕）。**The supernatural connects them across time and space.**

3. **身体恐怖（Body Horror）**：wound with tentacles/sequins（伤口有触手/亮片），"disco ball in her belly"（肚子里的迪斯科球），birth ritual with blood-covered children（血淋淋孩子的分娩仪式），women with scars/stumps (tentacles cut off)（女人有疤痕/残桩——触手被切掉）。**Body horror as metaphor for suppressed identity.**

4. **西班牙/加泰罗尼亚背景（Spanish/Catalan Setting）**：Barcelona (Balmes, Splau, El Prat airport, Finestrelles mall, Camp La Tortuga Ligera)。**Setting is integral to the story.**

## 人物弧光

### Alba｜从 exhausted 到 revealing tentacles
ch01–ch04 Alba 的日常：autophobia, father ghost, wound beginning. ch05–ch07 TV conflict, urgent care, Brisa sleepwalking. ch08–ch09 Christmas Eve, missing. ch10–ch13 alone in Splau: bathroom, girl, Claire's, doppelgänger, autonomy. ch14–ch17 family reunites, reveals tentacles, laughs and dances. **Alba's arc: from exhausted mother to woman who claims her suppressed self.**

### Diana｜从 child-terror 到 part of mystery
ch01–ch04 Diana's child-terror, nighttime checking Brisa. ch05–ch07 stays on mat, uncanny touch. ch08–ch09 wound, missing. ch10–ch13 ghost story, finds Alba. ch14–ch17 reunites, learns she was in Splau, octopus revelation. **Diana's arc: from traumatized sister to participant in the mystery.**

### Carmen｜从 stoic 到 confessing
ch01–ch04 mother, practical, dismissive of supernatural. ch05–ch07 critical of Carlos. ch08–ch09 wounded. ch10–ch13 absent (in car). ch14–ch17 reveals octopus, bingo lady, dances to Camela. **Carmen's arc: from stoic mother to woman who confesses her hidden supernatural experience.**

### The Girl Ghost｜从 dead girl 到 released
Prologue: dead girl at Splau, tentacles, waiting for Alba. ch11–ch12: appears to Alba, reveals camp backstory, "you forgot about me", devoured by tentacles. ch13: doppelgänger destroyed. ch16: women with scars/stumps, "That's why they scream", tentacles revive. **The girl's arc: from waiting ghost to released (tentacles free her).**

### Carlos｜从 skeptic 到 witness
ch01–ch04 critical, rationalizing. ch05–ch07 embarrassed. ch08–ch09 horrified. ch10–ch13 absent. ch14–ch17 witness to scars, silent. **Carlos's arc: from skeptic to silent witness.**

### Juan｜从 practical to witness
ch01–ch04 practical father. ch05–ch07 practical. ch08–ch09 practical. ch10–ch13 absent. ch14–ch17 finds Alba, sees scars, silent champagne. **Juan's arc: from practical father to silent witness.**
