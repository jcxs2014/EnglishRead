> **原句 1:** “She found them at the giant Christmas tree at the mall. Some were closer than others. None of them were alone. Most of them were milling around just a few meters from the tree, each lost in their own world. Others stood before it and looked up, completely still, seemingly hypnotized. It was a fake tree, a towering cone-shaped structure, metallic, with a door that led into it, visible from the outside. It wasn’t really a door; it was just the frame. Everything was lit: the string lights covering the tree, and the glowing ornaments—glass globes that, oddly, were inside the branches rather than hanging from them. You could see the heart of the tree from the outside, since its structure was hollow. It was made of metal bars joined together at the top, like a bride’s bouquet turned upside down and strung with tiny LED lights. Inside were two benches spray-painted gold. The days leading up to Christmas, at peak hours, Santa Claus would welcome visitors there, asking if they’d been good and what gifts they wanted. The days and times when Santa Claus wasn’t present, customers could enter the tree freely, take photos, and contemplate the season while looking out at Splau. Alba walked toward the tree, afraid, staring up at the star that crowned the silvery structure. She was hoping if she didn’t look at them, they wouldn’t realize she was there. Like when she covered her eyes playing hide-and-seek as a kid. She wanted to believe that it would work, at least while the tentacles were at bay. As she approached, she caught sight of dark stains at the edge of her vision, all different sizes, shifting, edging one another away. She didn’t know who they were, but she didn’t intend to look and find out.”

## 一句话总结

第十六章：surreal chapter. Alba at Splau's giant Christmas tree: sees women like her (doppelgängers, all mothers) with companions (frail, skeletal); collective scream; tentacles emerge as light show; women have scars/stumps (tentacles cut off); Alba realizes "That's why they scream"; tentacles revive; women try to trap Alba; tentacles free her; Alba walks through them. Chapter ends with "I just wanted the controllers." Novel's surreal climax: Alba encounters all mothers, recognizes her own condition, and walks through them with tentacles as guardians. Key revelation: women scream because their tentacles were cut (suppressed), Alba's tentacles are intact (she hasn't suppressed them).
