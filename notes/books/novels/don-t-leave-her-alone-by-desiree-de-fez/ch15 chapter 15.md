# Chapter 15

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Alba/Diana/Carmen eat pizza outside Splau; discuss "octopus" (tentacles); Carmen confesses she had octopus in stomach at camp (the summer she fainted); Diana mentions telepathy; Alba stays silent about Splau experience. Chapter ends with Diana's clumsy encouragement ("Both of you are so brave").

- **情感弧线位置**：全书第十五章——aftermath dialogue; family's first honest conversation about supernatural; Carmen's confession (she had octopus too); Alba's silence (she doesn't tell full story); Diana's clumsy support.

- **人物弧线**：Alba 从“exhausted”到“silent”; Diana 从“confused”到“clumsy support”; Carmen 从“terrific”到“confessing octopus”。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角主跟 Diana; dialogue-heavy; family dynamics; supernatural confession; emotional intimacy.
