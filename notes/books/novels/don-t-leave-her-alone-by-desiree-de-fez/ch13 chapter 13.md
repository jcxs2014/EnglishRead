# Chapter 13

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Alba on second floor of Splau: sees doppelgänger woman (her own face) with blood-covered children; woman gives birth repeatedly; Alba screams, children attack (red pinpricks); Alba recognizes herself in woman; tentacles emerge, babies fall; woman splits in half and vanishes; Alba alone, picks up Switch controllers, hears scream, sees silhouettes.

- **情感弧线位置**：全书第十三章——supernatural mythology expands: Alba's doppelgänger (her own face) + children (monstrous babies); birth ritual; Alba's realization she wants autonomy (not more children); tentacles destroy doppelgänger; chapter ends with Alba hearing scream and seeing silhouettes.

- **人物弧线**：Alba 从“voyeur”到“screamer”到“recognizer”到“autonomy claimer”到“alone”; doppelgänger woman 从“birthing”到“recognizing”到“splitting in half”; children from “newborns” to “attackers” to “vanishing”。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角主跟 Alba; horror imagery (children, birth, doppelgänger); supernatural mythology (doppelgänger as Alba's suppressed mother-identity); lyrical ending (Alba alone, hears scream)。

**为什么这样写**：作者用分身的死亡（从中间裂开、清脆断裂）显示 Alba 的触手摧毁了她自己的母亲身份；"infinite grief" 显示 Alba 的哀悼（她哀悼被压抑的自我）；"clapped at the sky" 显示她重演孩子们在卫生间断电时的游戏（灯光恢复）；"alien planet" 显示 Splau 的怪异特质仍在；"tentacles coiled back gently" 显示伤口的新状态（抚慰而非攻击）；"ballet flats" 显示 Alba 夺回自己的身体（为自己而非他人穿鞋）；"scream suspended in time" + "silhouettes" 为第14章铺垫（新的超自然遭遇）；本章弧线：Alba 认出分身，选择自主，摧毁母亲身份，哀悼，然后转向新的遭遇。

**读者视角提示**："split in half" = doppelgänger 被摧毁（Alba 的母亲身份被杀死）；"infinite grief" = 哀悼；"tentacles coiled gently" = 伤口如今为 Alba 服务（而非攻击）；"ballet flats" = 自我照料；"scream + silhouettes" = ch14 铺垫；本章是小说的心理高潮与转折点。
