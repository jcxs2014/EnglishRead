# Chapter 12

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Alba wakes in Claire's with 9 hoop piercings; narrative flashback to Camp La Tortuga Ligera's ghost backstory (camp swallowed by airport expansion, ghosts of happy campers); Alba's autonomy loss (married, kids, no self); girl leaves, Alba chases, tentacles emerge and "devour" girl. Chapter ends with Alba unknowingly devouring the girl as she sleeps.

- **情感弧线位置**：全书第十二章——Alba's POV continues; reveals supernatural mythology (camp ghosts, Alba's forgotten self); body horror climax (tentacles devour girl); chapter ends with Alba "devouring" girl (absorbing her?).

- **人物弧线**：Alba 从“amnesiac”到“memory recovery”到“chasing girl”到“devouring”; girl 从“piercing”到“camp backstory”到“leaving”到“being devoured”。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角主跟 Alba; long chapter with embedded backstory (camp history); supernatural mythology expansion; body horror climax; lyrical ending。

## 一句话总结

第十二章：Alba wakes in Claire's with 9 hoop piercings; girl reveals Camp La Tortuga Ligera ghost backstory (camp swallowed by airport expansion, ghosts of happy campers feeding on memory); Alba's autonomy loss (married, kids, no self); girl leaves, Alba chases, tentacles emerge and "devour" girl. Chapter ends with Alba "devouring" girl (absorbing her younger self). Novel's core supernatural revelation: girl is Alba's 12-year-old camp self, and Alba's autophobia (fear of being alone) = fear of forgetting (forgetting = death).
