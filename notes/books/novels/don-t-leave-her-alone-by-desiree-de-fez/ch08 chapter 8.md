# Chapter 8

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Christmas Eve: Diana 发现 laundry tub 里第二个 hoop earring（supernatural）；Carmen 反复来电；Diana 强行让 taxi 停车（谎称 Brisa 恶心）； reveal “stomach bleeding”——腰间 open wound，无痛，oozing; Carlos 崩溃。

- **情感弧线位置**：全书第八章——把 body horror 引入：Diana 腰间 spontaneous wound；earring motif（supernatural object）与 wound 形成 supernatural thread；Diana 的 calm（“I’m not in pain”）与 Carlos 的 horror 对比。

- **人物弧线**：Diana 从“anxious mother”到“serene despite wound”；Carlos 从“dozing”到“sobbing, near fainting”; Brisa suspicious observer。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角主跟 Diana；long build-up (taxi chaos) → sudden reveal (wound); Diana’s calm is uncanny; Carlos’s reaction is reader surrogate。

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "belt made of pulp and blood" 把 wound 写成 garment (wrapping around waist like belt)—supernatural body modification; "oozing, yet still at the same time" 显示 unnatural stasis (wound is alive but not flowing); "feeding on itself" 是 key uncanny detail—wound is autonomous, self-sustaining; this is body horror (Desirée de Fez’s genre); Diana’s lack of pain confirms supernatural; Carlos’s sobbing is reader surrogate (horror at impossible wound)。

**读者视角提示**："four fingers wide" gives measurement; "wrapping around her waist" shows circumferential wound; "just below Diana’s abdomen" locates wound; "not actively bleeding" distinguishes from normal wound; "feeding on itself" is the chapter’s horror climax; this wound parallels Brisa’s sleepwalking (both are nighttime/uncanny bodily events)。
