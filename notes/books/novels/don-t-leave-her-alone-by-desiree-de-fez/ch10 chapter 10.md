# Chapter 10

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Splau Christmas Eve: Alba missing；Diana 与 Carlos 抵达，发现 family scattered（autonomous individuals）；car blood-streaked + Carmen wounded (stomach like Diana's)；Camp La Tortuga Ligera backstory (Alba happy there, never alone since); Diana's ghost story (bingo women, pink light, axe); father's ghost (cigarette smell); Alba found, "try not to be scared when we see her"。

- **情感弧线位置**：全书第十章——把 supernatural thread 扩展：Diana 与 Carmen 共享 wound; Alba missing; Camp La Tortuga Ligera 作为 Alba's happy place; Diana's ghost story (bingo night) 是 supernatural origin story; father's ghost; chapter 以 Alba found + "don't be scared" cliffhanger 收尾。

- **人物弧线**：Diana 从“examining wound”到“ghost story teller”; Carmen 从“Candy Crush relaxed”到“wounded but calm”; Carlos 从“anxious”到“raising voice at mother-in-law”; Alba absent then found。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角主跟 Diana；long chapter with multiple embedded narratives (camp backstory, ghost story); dialogue-heavy; supernatural elements accumulate。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** “When the taxi parked, they quickly spotted Juan and Alba’s car. … All the van’s doors were open, and the interior lights were on. Several figures of different sizes were moving around outside on the grass. Something about it made Diana think of a haphazard manger, one a child had assembled. This image of her family—incomplete—shocked her. … There were five of them, not six. Alba was missing. And, probably because she was missing, the rest of them were more spread out than usual. She couldn’t remember ever having seen her family standing so far apart. … Nor, for that matter, could she recall standing before them without the sense that they belonged to one single mass—uniform, if conflicted.”

出租车停好后，他们很快便发现了Juan和Alba的车。……面包车的所有车门都敞开着，车内灯也亮着。几道不同大小的身影在草地上移动。这一幕让Diana想起一个草草搭成的马槽，像孩子拼装出来似的。这个不完整的家庭影像让她震惊。……他们共有五个人，不是六个。Alba不见了。大概正因为Alba不见了，其余人站得比平时更散。她不记得曾见过家人站得如此疏离。……而且，她也想不起曾站在他们面前、却没有觉得他们属于同一个整体——统一的，却也冲突着的。

**关键词**：haphazard manger / incomplete / autonomous / uniform

**句子结构**：长段——"When the taxi parked, they quickly spotted Juan and Alba’s car."；"All the van’s doors were open, and the interior lights were on."；"Several figures of different sizes were moving around outside on the grass."；"Something about it made Diana think of a haphazard manger, one a child had assembled."；"This image of her family—incomplete—shocked her."；"There were five of them, not six."；"Alba was missing."；"And, probably because she was missing, the rest of them were more spread out than usual."；"She couldn’t remember ever having seen her family standing so far apart."；"Nor, for that matter, could she recall standing before them without the sense that they belonged to one single mass—uniform, if conflicted."

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "haphazard manger" 把家人写成耶稣降生场景（圣诞夜背景）；"incomplete" 显示 Alba 的缺席让家庭碎裂；"more spread out than usual" 显示 Alba 的引力（在场时家人聚拢）；"autonomous individuals" 显示 Alba 的缺席解放了他人；"uniform, if conflicted" 显示家庭的常态（整体而非个体）；本段开篇确立了本章主题：没有 Alba 的家庭是不同配置。

**读者视角提示**："The streetlights cast a violent light" + "grass extraordinarily green, almost phosphorescent" 显示 supernatural atmosphere; "fountain changing shape like a small tornado" 显示 uncanny setting; Diana's "blinders with her own hands—still bloodstained" 显示 her wound persists but she focuses on vision。

> **原句 2:** "She’s calm now. Too calm, I think. But something you won’t believe happened. I’m still reeling, if I’m being honest. I’m more worried about your mom than I am about Alba." … "She was bleeding all over me. I asked Juanito to watch the littler ones and told him I had to tell Grandma something important, then I took her over behind the car so they wouldn’t see her that way. I opened the trunk and had her sit down back there, and when I unzipped her coat and lifted up her sweater to see what was going on, I almost passed out. Her stomach is destroyed. She has a terrible injury. It’s all bits of skin and blood clots. Juan累得完全没了力气，靠在车侧，然后沿着车身滑坐到地上。……“她现在很平静。平静得过头了，我觉得。但发生了一件你不会相信的事。老实说，我到现在还晕乎乎的。我比担心Alba更担心你妈。”……“她全身都是血，流到我身上。我让Juanito看着小的几个，说我要去告诉奶奶一件重要的事，然后把她带到了车后头，免得他们看到她那副样子。我打开后备厢，让她坐在里面，等我拉开她的外套、掀开毛衣看看到底怎么回事时，我差点晕过去。她的肚子毁了。她受了重伤。全是皮肉和血块。就像有人用immersion blender攻击她、要把她切两半似的。”……“她说不疼，说她没事，几乎是在拍打着让我别管她。”

**关键词**：immersion blender / destroyed / too calm / swatting

**句子结构**：长段——"Juan, completely spent, leaned against the side of the car then slid down it until he was sitting on the ground."；"She’s calm now. Too calm, I think. But something you won’t believe happened. I’m still reeling, if I’m being honest. I’m more worried about your mom than I am about Alba."；"She was bleeding all over me. I asked Juanito to watch the littler ones and told him I had to tell Grandma something important, then I took her over behind the car so they wouldn’t see her that way. I opened the trunk and had her sit down back there, and when I unzipped her coat and lifted up her sweater to see what was going on, I almost passed out. Her stomach is destroyed. She has a terrible injury. It’s all bits of skin and blood clots. Like someone attacked her with an immersion blender and tried to cut her in half."；"She says it doesn’t hurt, that she’s fine, and she’s practically swatting me away to get me to leave her alone."

**为什么这样写**：作者用 Juan 的证词显示 Carmen 也受伤（与 Diana 相同）；"too calm" 显示她的解离（类似 Diana 的平静）；"immersion blender" 显示伤势严重（非正常创伤）；"swatting me away" 显示她拒绝帮助；这确立了创伤传染（Diana 的伤 → Carmen 的伤），暗示超自然传播；Juan 作为见证者（非家人）提供创伤真实性的外部验证。

**读者视角提示**："blood-streaked car" 与 "handprint, fingers spread wide" 显示 wound 的严重性；"sheet soaked with blood" 在车底；Juan 的 sneaker 留有血渍；Carmen 的 "gushing blood"；这个 wound 与 Diana 的 (waist, no pain, oozing) 平行。

> **原句 3:** "Carlos couldn’t make sense of it, he didn’t understand why they would avoid doing something so normal. He found it bizarre that they were so close and so mutually dependent, while also completely lacking deeper knowledge about one another. What they did know was conjecture or intuition, not the result of something shared." … "Diana maintained it hadn’t always been that way—she said when she and Alba lived at their parents’ house, they would tell each other everything. Carlos想不明白，他不懂他们为什么回避做这么正常的事。他觉得奇怪：他们如此亲近、如此相互依赖，却完全缺乏对彼此更深层的了解。他们所知道的，是推测或直觉，不是分享而来的。……Diana坚持说并非一直如此——她说她和Alba住在父母家时，什么都会告诉对方。有了孩子之前，她们三人也常常见面叙旧。但事实是，她得把时间推得更远，才能找到真正亲密的时刻。

**关键词**：intimacy / conjecture / mutually dependent / bizarre

**句子结构**：长段——"Diana maintained it hadn’t always been that way—she said when she and Alba lived at their parents’ house, they would tell each other everything."；"And before they had kids, the three of them would meet all the time to catch up."；"But the truth was, she had to go further back in time to reach a moment of actual intimacy."；"Carlos couldn’t make sense of it, he didn’t understand why they would avoid doing something so normal."；"He found it bizarre that they were so close and so mutually dependent, while also completely lacking deeper knowledge about one another."；"What they did know was conjecture or intuition, not the result of something shared."

**为什么这样写**：作者用 Carlos 的观察作为家庭批判——Diana/Carmen/Alba 亲近但不亲密；"conjecture or intuition" 显示他们的了解是猜测而非共享；这解释了为什么 Carmen 能说 "Alba is never alone" 却不处理它；Carlos 作为局外人看到内部人忽略的；本段具有主题性（家庭亲近 ≠ 亲密）。

**读者视角提示**："She had never met up with just Alba because Alba didn’t go anywhere alone" 显示 Alba's autophobia's social cost; "visualizing her sister getting off the metro by herself... couldn't do it" 显示 Diana's inability to imagine Alba alone。

> **原句 4:** “‘She hasn’t been alone in years,’ Carmen said after a long pause. Diana sat up, astonished, and gave her mother a long look. **关键词**：astonished / satisfied smile / finally on her own

**句子结构**：长段——"‘She hasn’t been alone in years,’ Carmen said after a long pause."；"Diana sat up, astonished, and gave her mother a long look."；"Then she stretched back out, a satisfied smile on her face."；"‘Wow. **为什么这样写**：作者用 Carmen 的承认显示母亲的觉察（她知道 Alba 的独处恐惧）；"finally on her own" 是双刃剑（Alba 的独立 = 潜在危险）；Diana 的 "satisfied smile" 显示她在 Carmen 命名它时的释然；这次交流是家庭罕见的诚实时刻；"finally on her own" 预示 Alba 的消失（她第一次独自一人，事情发生）。

**读者视角提示**："Alba was happy at the campground. She wasn't afraid" 显示 Camp La Tortuga as Alba's origin of happiness (and possibly supernatural origin); "She never has any fun" 显示 Diana's diagnosis of Alba's adult life。
