# Chapter 10

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Splau Christmas Eve: Alba missing；Diana 与 Carlos 抵达，发现 family scattered（autonomous individuals）；car blood-streaked + Carmen wounded (stomach like Diana's)；Camp La Tortuga Ligera backstory (Alba happy there, never alone since); Diana's ghost story (bingo women, pink light, axe); father's ghost (cigarette smell); Alba found, "try not to be scared when we see her"。

- **情感弧线位置**：全书第十章——把 supernatural thread 扩展：Diana 与 Carmen 共享 wound; Alba missing; Camp La Tortuga Ligera 作为 Alba's happy place; Diana's ghost story (bingo night) 是 supernatural origin story; father's ghost; chapter 以 Alba found + "don't be scared" cliffhanger 收尾。

- **人物弧线**：Diana 从“examining wound”到“ghost story teller”; Carmen 从“Candy Crush relaxed”到“wounded but calm”; Carlos 从“anxious”到“raising voice at mother-in-law”; Alba absent then found。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角主跟 Diana；long chapter with multiple embedded narratives (camp backstory, ghost story); dialogue-heavy; supernatural elements accumulate。
