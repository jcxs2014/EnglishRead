**关键词**：vibrate / unconcerned / missing

**句子结构**：长段——"One of Diana’s back pockets started to vibrate."；"She stopped examining herself and picked up, unconcerned that her hand was covered in blood."；"She said, 'Hello,' listened, didn’t respond."；"'I have to call you back,' she said, almost to herself, sliding the iPhone into her pocket."；"'They can’t find Alba.'"；"'What?' Carlos asked, still covering Diana with the coat."；"'They can’t find Alba.'"；"'What do you mean they can’t find Alba?'"；"'Mom just said Alba is missing.'"

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "unconcerned that her hand was covered in blood" 显示 Diana 的 dissociation——she picks up phone despite bleeding hand; "almost to herself" 显示 her internal processing; "They can’t find Alba" repeated three times 显示 shock; Carmen calling (not Alba) shows Alba’s absence; "still covering Diana with the coat" 显示 Carlos’s protective gesture persists despite news; this chapter connects Diana’s wound to Alba’s disappearance (supernatural link?)。

**读者视角提示**："Mom just said Alba is missing" 显示 Carmen is at Alba’s house (or urgent care?) and Alba left; "missing" is ominous (not "gone to store"); this is shortest chapter, pure cliffhanger; Diana’s wound + Alba’s disappearance suggest supernatural causation (Alba’s evil eye? wound as curse?)。
