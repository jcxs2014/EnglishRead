# Chapter 9

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Diana 腰间 wound 仍 bleeding 时电话响起——Carmen 报 Alba missing；Diana 与 Carlos 仍 behind parked car, wound 未处理。

- **情感弧线位置**：全书第九章——极短，Alba missing 的消息把 Diana 的 wound 与 Alba 的 disappearance 连接；章节是 cliffhanger。

- **人物弧线**：Diana 从“examining wound”到“Alba missing”; Carlos 从“covering Diana”到“shocked”。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角主跟 Diana；极短章节；dialogue-driven; 无 description。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "One of Diana's back pockets started to vibrate. She stopped examining herself and picked up, unconcerned that her hand was covered in blood. She said, 'Hello,' listened, didn't respond."

**中文理解**：戴安娜的一个后口袋开始震动。她停止检查自己，接起电话，毫不在意手上沾满鲜血。她说："喂。"听了一会儿，没有回应。

**关键词**：vibrate / unconcerned / Hello

**句子结构**：三句——"One of Diana's back pockets started to vibrate." 短句，震动打断 Diana 的 wound 检查；"She stopped examining herself and picked up, unconcerned that her hand was covered in blood." 两个分词短语 (stopped examining / picked up) + unconcerned that 从句；"She said, 'Hello,' listened, didn't respond." 三动词短句，显示 Diana 的 dissociation（她接电话时手还沾满血）。

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "unconcerned that her hand was covered in blood" 显示 Diana 的解离状态——她接电话时手还沾满血，却毫不在意；"stopped examining herself" 显示伤口检查被外部事件打断；"didn't respond" 显示她在听却未处理（震惊）。这一开场把 Diana 的身体伤口与外部危机连接。

**读者视角提示**：Diana 的 bleeding hand 与 "unconcerned" 显示她的 dissociation——wound 对她来说已不是 priority；"Hello" + "didn't respond" 显示 she is in shock mode; this opening establishes the chapter's horror beat: bodily crisis interrupted by family crisis。

> **原句 2:** "'I have to call you back,' she said, almost to herself, sliding the iPhone into her pocket. 'They can't find Alba.'"

**中文理解**："我得回个电话。"她几乎是对着自己说，把 iPhone 滑进口袋。"他们找不到阿尔芭了。"

**关键词**：call you back / almost to herself / sliding / can't find

**句子结构**：两句——"'I have to call you back,' she said, almost to herself, sliding the iPhone into her pocket." 两个分词短语 (almost to herself / sliding)；"'They can't find Alba.'" 短句，直接引语，显示 Diana 的 first response to Carmen's call。

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "almost to herself" 显示 Diana 的内在处理——她未完全在场；"sliding the iPhone into her pocket" 显示机械性动作（她处于自动驾驶状态）；"They can't find Alba" 是本章首次揭示，Carmen 打来（而非 Alba）表明 Alba 不在；"can't find"（而非 "gone"）不祥。

**读者视角提示**："They can't find Alba" 显示 Alba is missing (not just "away"); Carmen is the caller (not Alba), which is alarming; "almost to herself" 显示 Diana is dissociating between wound and family crisis; this beat connects Diana's wound to Alba's disappearance (supernatural link?)。

> **原句 3:** "'What?' Carlos asked, still covering Diana with the coat. 'They can't find Alba.' 'What do you mean they can't find Alba?' 'Mom just said Alba is missing.'"

**中文理解**："什么？"卡洛斯问，仍用外套裹着戴安娜。"他们找不到阿尔芭了。""你说他们找不到阿尔芭是什么意思？""妈妈刚说阿尔芭失踪了。"

**关键词**：still covering / can't find / missing / Mom just said

**句子结构**：四句——"'What?' Carlos asked, still covering Diana with the coat." 短疑问句 + 分词短语 (still covering)；"'They can't find Alba.'" 重复，显示 Diana's shock; "'What do you mean they can't find Alba?'" Carlos 的追问，显示 disbelief; "'Mom just said Alba is missing.'" Diana's final statement，"missing" (not "gone") is the ominous word。

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "still covering Diana with the coat" 显示 Carlos 的保护性姿态在消息传来后仍持续（他没有松开外套）；"They can't find Alba" 重复三次显示震惊与难以置信；"Mom just said Alba is missing" 显示 Carmen 是信息来源（而非 Alba），且 "missing"（而非 "away"）不祥；本章是纯粹的悬念收尾，把 Diana 的伤口与 Alba 的失踪连接（超自然因果？）。

**读者视角提示**："Mom just said Alba is missing" 显示 Carmen is at Alba's house (or urgent care?) and Alba left; "missing" is ominous (not "gone to store"); this is shortest chapter, pure cliffhanger; Diana's wound + Alba's disappearance suggest supernatural causation (Alba's evil eye? wound as curse?); "still covering Diana with the coat" 显示 Carlos's care persists despite crisis。
