# 《Don't Leave Her Alone》情感节点

## 节点一：Prologue——从血污中醒来，一觉醒来满身是血（Prologue）

**叙事概括：** Alba 在一家店里从昏迷中醒来：灯光分阶段亮起，像有人在有节奏地拨动开关。她想起上一次带女儿 Brigitte 来这里试戴塑料皇冠。睁眼后她看见成排的芭蕾裙溅满红色，蝴蝶翅膀与熊形背包覆着一层干硬的血痂——那正是她和 Brigitte 曾试戴自拍的东西。她的头在跳痛，脸上是一大片暗色血污，耳朵上多出九个从未见过的耳环（五只一耳、四只另一耳），全是新穿孔、结着褐黑色的痂。那场她完全不记得的「预约」必定漫长、不卫生而残忍。章节最后，她听见有人哼歌，转头看见了那个女孩。**本章 establishes the supernatural premise: Alba 在血污与童年记忆中醒来，那个女孩第一次现身。**

**关键引语：**

> "She came to in the dark, sprawled across the floor."（ch00）

> "Because you forgot about me."（ch11）

## 节点二：ch01–ch04——圣诞前的家庭日常与身体警报（ch01–ch04）

**叙事概括：** ch01：平安夜前一天的圣诞演出，Diana 因强迫性怀疑（直发器是否未关）折返家中查证而迟到，onstage 的女儿 Brisa 因母亲缺席，成了"唯一不唱歌的孩子"。ch02：Alba 与母亲 Carmen 为换游戏手柄在 Sant Ildefons 与 Finestrelles 商场奔走，Alba 在商场 dissociate、遭遇"寡妇"二人组、被施"邪眼"并流鼻血；章节后半回溯姐妹的女儿同日流鼻血。ch03：Alba 与 Diana 通电话，Alba 要 Carmen 代看孩子，姐妹就"孩子能否离开母亲"争执。ch04：Diana 与 Carlos 讨论 Alba 对她的控制，地铁上 Diana 看见持刀青年并拖家带口逃下车。**本阶段 establishes family dynamics and the somatic alarms (bleeding, the evil eye) that the supernatural later explains.**

**关键引语：**

> "Jesus, Alba, couldn't you have gone another time?"（ch03）

> "It was a knife. The young man was searching for his reflection."（ch04）

## 节点三：ch05–ch07——Christmas Eve 前的紧张与 Brisa 梦游（ch05–ch07）

**叙事概括：** TV conflict（电视冲突），urgent care panic（急诊恐慌——baby 吞了 euro），Alba autophobia（独处恐惧），Diana father ghost（父亲 ghost），Carlos critique（Carlos 的批判）。Brisa sleepwalking（Brisa 梦游——站在床脚面对窗户），Diana stays on mat（Diana 留在垫子上），uncanny touch（不祥的触碰）。**本阶段 builds tension: the wound grows, the ghost appears, Carlos rationalizes.**

**关键引语：**

> "Diana lived in terror of the possibility that something would happen to her children."（ch06）

> "Her stomach then clenched as soon as she realized she didn't know quite what she was touching."（ch07）

## 节点四：ch08–ch09——Christmas Eve，Alba missing（ch08–ch09）

**叙事概括：** Christmas Eve taxi chaos（出租车混乱），earring motif（耳环意象），Diana wound（Diana 的伤口——waist, no pain, oozing, feeding on itself），Carlos horror（Carlos 的恐惧）。Alba goes missing（Alba 失踪）——cliffhanger（悬念）：Diana wound still bleeding when Carmen calls. **本阶段 is the inciting incident: Alba disappears on Christmas Eve, Diana is wounded.**

**关键引语：**

> "Mom just said Alba is missing."（ch09）

## 节点五：ch10——Splau 商场外，Christmas Eve（ch10）

**叙事概括：** Family scattered (autonomous individuals)（家人分散——自主个体），car blood-streaked + Carmen wounded (same as Diana)（车血迹斑斑 + Carmen 受伤——与 Diana 相同），Camp La Tortuga Ligera backstory（营地 backstory），Diana ghost story (bingo women, pink light, axe)（Diana 讲鬼故事——bingo 女人、粉光、斧头），father ghost (cigarette smell)（父亲 ghost），the family waits outside Splau for news, and Juan reports that Alba is calm now and that he is more worried about their mother than about her（找到 Alba，Juan 说她看起来很平静，自己更担心的是母亲）。**本阶段 reveals the scope: multiple women wounded, the camp backstory, the bingo women.**

**关键引语：**

> "She's calm now. Too calm, I think."（ch10）

> "But I heard something like an axe, or like when they're cutting meat at the butcher"（ch10）

## 节点六：ch11–ch13——Alba alone in Splau（ch11–ch13）

**叙事概括：** Alba's POV bathroom at Splau（Alba 视角——Splau 卫生间），wound with tentacles/sequins（伤口有触手/亮片），girl in red swimsuit with hoop earrings（红泳衣女孩戴圈形耳环——你忘了我），Claire's store（Claire 的店），song（歌曲），tentacles attack（触手攻击），faints（昏厥）。Alba wakes in Claire's, 9 hoop piercings（Alba 在 Claire's 醒来，9 个圈形穿孔）；girl reveals Camp La Tortuga Ligera ghost backstory (camp swallowed by airport expansion, ghosts of happy campers)（女孩揭示营地 ghost backstory——营地被机场扩建吞没，快乐露营者的 ghost）；Alba's autonomy loss（Alba 的自主性丧失）；girl leaves, Alba chases, tentacles emerge and drag the girl back, and Alba devours her in her sleep（女孩离开，Alba 追，触手把女孩拖回，Alba 在睡梦中吞噬了她）。Alba on second floor sees doppelgänger woman (her own face) with blood-covered children; birth ritual（Alba 在二楼看到 doppelgänger 女人——她自己的脸——带着血淋淋的孩子；分娩仪式）；Alba screams, children attack (tiny red points)（Alba 尖叫，孩子攻击——红点）；doppelgänger wears Alba's wedding dress (Rosa Clará)（doppelgänger 穿 Alba 的婚纱——Rosa Clará）；the doppelgänger tells Alba that she doesn't know how to do anything（doppelgänger 告诉 Alba 她什么都不会）；Alba claims autonomy（Alba 声称自主权）；tentacles destroy doppelgänger (split in half, snap)（触手摧毁 doppelgänger——劈成两半，断裂）；Alba alone, picks up Switch controllers, finds ballet flats, hears scream, sees silhouettes（Alba 独自，捡起 Switch 控制器，找到芭蕾平底鞋，听到尖叫声，看到剪影）。**本阶段 is the novel's horror centerpiece: Alba confronts the girl's ghost, the doppelgänger, and claims her autonomy.**

**关键引语：**

> "She looked down at her stomach and saw she had a spectacular wound, from one side to the other."（ch11）

> "Because you forgot about me."（ch11）

> "She, who had long forgotten what a party was, who hadn't danced since her wedding day, had a disco ball in her belly."（ch11）

> "The earrings are just the start."（ch12）

> "You don't know how to do anything."（ch13）

> "How to do anything at all."（ch13）

> "I don't want another life. I want mine. I want to go home."（ch13）

## 节点七：ch14–ch15——Family reunites, revelations（ch14–ch15）

**叙事概括：** Carlos/Diana find Alba; family reunites; wounds healed (silvery scars, all three women identical, traced with glitter)（Carlos/Diana 找到 Alba；家人团聚；伤口愈合——银色疤痕，三个女人相同，用亮片描摹）；Alba calm; Juan's account (found Alba naked, hole in belly)（Alba 平静；Juan 的叙述——找到赤裸的 Alba，腹部有洞）；children saw pink lights in sky; pizza delivery; everyone eats Domino's（孩子看到天空粉光；披萨配送；所有人吃 Domino's）。Alba/Diana/Carmen eat pizza outside Splau; discuss the octopus, i.e. the tentacles（三人吃披萨；讨论 octopus——触手）；Carmen confesses she had octopus at camp (the summer she fainted)（Carmen 承认营地有 octopus——她昏厥的夏天）；Diana mentions telepathy（Diana 提及心灵感应）；Alba stays silent about full Splau experience（Alba 对 Splau 全部经历保持沉默）；key revelation: Diana, without knowing it, was with Alba in Splau that night（关键揭示：Diana 不知情的情况下，与 Alba 一起在 Splau 那夜）。**本阶段 reveals the family's hidden connections: Carmen had octopus, Diana was in Splau.**

**关键引语：**

> "She was completely naked from the waist up, covered in blood, with a hole in her belly."（ch14）

> "I had an octopus in my stomach, too"（ch15）

> "Diana, without knowing it, had been with her in Splau that night."（ch15）

## 节点八：ch16–ch17——Final revelations and homecoming（ch16–ch17）

**叙事概括：** Alba at Splau's giant Christmas tree; sees women like her (doppelgängers, all mothers) with companions (frail, skeletal); collective scream; tentacles emerge as light show; women have scars/stumps (tentacles cut off); Alba realizes why the women scream; tentacles revive; women try to trap Alba; tentacles free her; Alba walks through them; chapter ends with a line about the controllers（Alba 在 Splau 巨大圣诞树下；看到像她一样的女人——doppelgängers，全是母亲——带着伴侣（虚弱、骨骼）；集体尖叫；触手作为灯光秀出现；女人有疤痕/残桩（触手被切掉）；Alba 意识到她们尖叫的原因；触手复活；女人试图困住 Alba；触手释放她；Alba 走过它们；章节以"我只想拿控制器"结束）。Family returns home; Alba hides Switch controllers on patio; family eats/drinks champagne; Carmen reveals she had octopus at camp (bingo lady helped); Alba reveals tentacles (glitter, dancing); family laughs and dances to Camela; chapter ends with Carmen asking about air fryer（家人回家；Alba 把 Switch 控制器藏在露台；家人吃喝香槟；Carmen 揭示营地有 octopus——bingo 女人帮助了她；Alba 揭示触手——亮片、跳舞；家人笑、跳 Camela；章节以 Carmen 问空气炸锅结束）。**本阶段 is the novel's ending: family reunited, supernatural accepted, normalcy returns.**

**关键引语：**

> "That's why they scream, she thought."（ch16）

> "I just wanted the controllers."（ch16）

> "The bingo lady and her friends."（ch17）

> "Why don't you get an air fryer like the one your sister has?"（ch17）
