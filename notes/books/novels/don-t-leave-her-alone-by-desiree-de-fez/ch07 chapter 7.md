# Chapter 7

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Diana 夜晚发现 Brisa sleepwalking（standing at foot of bed facing window）；Brisa collapse then wake for water; Diana stays on mat beside her;在半暗中 Diana 触到"not Brisa's back"的错觉；Diana 全夜无眠。

- **情感弧线位置**：全书第七章——Brisa sleepwalking 事件把 Diana 的 nighttime fear 具象化；章节短，是 Diana 单独与 Brisa 的 intimacy moment；Diana 触到的并非 Brisa 的背部，而是"某种温热却不对劲的东西"——uncanny moment。

- **人物弧线**：Diana 从“checking on Brisa”到“staying all night on mat”; Brisa 从“sleepwalking”到“thirsty, asleep”; Carlos absent (deep sleeper)。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角主跟 Diana；短章节（single scene）；uncanny touch moment 是本章 climax。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** “Diana heard something in Brisa’s room. She grabbed her iPhone from the nightstand, got up, and went to her daughter, phone flashlight on. They always left the mushroom lamp on, but she’d forgotten to charge it during the day, so that night, it had turned off a bit early. From the door, she could see the girl was standing at the foot of the bed, her feet on the little mat, facing the window. Diana went over to her. Since they’d only moved in recently, they still hadn’t put up curtains. And the mental image of her daughter from the other side of the window, seen from the apartment across the way, unsettled her as much as the sight in front of her.”

Diana 听到 Brisa 房间里有动静。她从床头柜抓起 iPhone，起身走向女儿，手机手电筒开着。他们一向开着那盏蘑菇灯，但她白天忘了充电，所以那天夜里灯提早灭了。从门口她就能看见，女孩站在床脚，脚踩在小垫子上，面朝窗户。Diana 走到她身边。因为她们搬家不久，窗帘还没挂上。而她脑中浮现出女儿在窗的另一侧、被对面公寓的人看见的画面，这种想象让她感到的不安，与眼前所见不相上下。

**关键词**：sleepwalking / mushroom lamp / curtains / unsettled

**句子结构**：长段——"Diana heard something in Brisa’s room."；"She grabbed her iPhone from the nightstand, got up, and went to her daughter, phone flashlight on."；"They always left the mushroom lamp on, but she’d forgotten to charge it during the day, so that night, it had turned off a bit early."；"From the door, she could see the girl was standing at the foot of the bed, her feet on the little mat, facing the window."；"Diana went over to her."；"Since they’d only moved in recently, they still hadn’t put up curtains."；"And the mental image of her daughter from the other side of the window, seen from the apartment across the way, unsettled her as much as the sight in front of her."

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "standing at the foot of the bed, facing the window" 把梦游写成面向外部的朝向——Brisa 面向窗口，显示她的梦游有方向性（朝向外部世界）; "no curtains" 显示暴露（Brisa 可被对面公寓看见）; "mental image of her daughter from the other side of the window" 显示 Diana 的偷窥式恐惧（她想象别人看见 Brisa）; "unsettled her as much as the sight in front of her" 显示 Diana 的焦虑双重（真实 + 想象）; 蘑菇灯未充电是情节装置（黑暗促成诡异）。

**读者视角提示**："sleepwalking" 确认（ch06 Diana 猜测）；Brisa 的 sleepwalking 与 Alba 的 autophobia 形成家族模式（两者都涉及夜晚，都涉及害怕独自一人/在外）；Diana 的 "phone flashlight on" 显示她临时凑合的照明工具（没有灯）。

> **原句 2:** “‘Honey, what’s going on? Get in bed.’ Brisa didn’t answer. ‘Do you have to go to the bathroom?’ When she tried to turn her around by the shoulders, Brisa collapsed. She collapsed as if she’d been deactivated, as if the energy that had kept her standing had suddenly been cut off. Diana quickly bent to pick her up. ‘Are you OK, sweetie? You fell,’ she said, kneeling on the floor. Brisa got back on her feet and looked at her worried mother, but she didn’t seem to comprehend her words. ‘Brisa, are you OK? Did you hurt yourself?’ Diana could tell that although her eyes were open, she was fast asleep. Unsettled, she helped her lie back in bed and covered her with the duvet. It was then that Brisa truly woke up. ‘I’m thirsty.’”

“亲爱的，怎么了？回床上去。”Brisa 没答。“你要上厕所吗？”她试着从肩膀把女儿转过来，Brisa 却倒下了。她像是被关机一样倒下，像是支撑她站立的能量突然切断了一样。Diana 赶紧弯腰把她抱起。“你没事吧，宝贝？你摔倒了。”她说着，跪在地板上。Brisa 站起身，望着担心的母亲，却似乎没听懂她的话。“Brisa，你没事吗？伤到自己了吗？”Diana 能看出，尽管她睁着眼，其实已熟睡。不安中，她扶女儿躺回床上，用羽绒被盖好。这时 Brisa 才真正醒来。“我渴了。”

**关键词**：deactivated / comprehend / truly woke up

**句子结构**：长段——"‘Honey, what’s going on? Get in bed.’ Brisa didn’t answer."；"‘Do you have to go to the bathroom?’"；"When she tried to turn her around by the shoulders, Brisa collapsed."；"She collapsed as if she’d been deactivated, as if the energy that had kept her standing had suddenly been cut off."；"Diana quickly bent to pick her up."；"‘Are you OK, sweetie? You fell,’ she said, kneeling on the floor."；"Brisa got back on her feet and looked at her worried mother, but she didn’t seem to comprehend her words."；"‘Brisa, are you OK? Did you hurt yourself?’ Diana could tell that although her eyes were open, she was fast asleep."；"Unsettled, she helped her lie back in bed and covered her with the duvet."；"It was then that Brisa truly woke up."；"‘I’m thirsty.’"

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "deactivated" 把 Brisa 的倒下写成机器般的——能量切断; "eyes open but fast asleep" 是梦游的经典标志; "didn’t seem to comprehend" 显示 Brisa 的解离; "truly woke up" 显示存在两种状态（梦游 vs 清醒）; Brisa 的 "I’m thirsty" 是打破诡异氛围的平凡请求; Diana 的 "unsettled" 显示她的恐惧; 本场景确认了 Diana 在 ch06 的怀疑。

**读者视角提示**："eyes open but fast asleep" 是 sleepwalking 的诊断标志；Diana 试图按 Brisa 肩膀转身触发 collapse（身体接触会打断 sleepwalking）；Brisa 醒后口渴显示正常儿童行为；Diana 留在垫子上显示她的警惕。

> **原句 3:** “She took one of the kids’ camp sleeping bags from the closet, stretched it out on the little mat, and reached up with one hand to hold Brisa’s; her daughter had now rolled onto her stomach. Diana was on edge and wide awake. She let go of Brisa’s hand and covered her eyes with her forearm. She lay like that for a few seconds. The girl shifted, and Diana reached back out to soothe her, gently placing a hand on her back. Her stomach then clenched as soon as she realized she didn’t know quite what she was touching. It had the warmth of Brisa, but it felt nothing like her back or even her chest. In the half darkness, with her heart in her throat, she looked. Her daughter was face down from the waist up, but her knees and the tips of her toes pointed toward the ceiling. Diana jumped up and rushed to turn on the light. She looked at Brisa again. She was in an uncomfortable position, but not an unnatural one, nothing like what she thought she’d seen. Standing at the door to her child’s bedroom, one hand covering her mouth, legs trembling, she had no idea what to do. She went to the office for her AirPods, put on a podcast, and proceeded to lie awake all night right next to Brisa, who didn’t move again.”

她从衣柜里拿出一个孩子的野营睡袋，铺在小垫子上，又伸出一只手去握 Brisa 的手；女儿这会儿已翻成趴着睡。Diana 紧张得完全清醒。她松开 Brisa 的手，用手臂遮住眼睛，那样躺了几秒。女孩动了动，Diana 又伸出手去安抚她，轻轻把手放在她背上。可她立刻胃一紧——她意识到自己并不确定自己摸到的是什么。那有 Brisa 的温度，却完全不像她的后背，甚至不像她的胸口。半暗中，她心提到嗓子眼，看了过去。女儿从腰部以上是俯卧的，可膝盖和脚趾尖却冲向天花板。Diana 跳起来，冲过去打开灯。她再看 Brisa，她只是姿势不舒服，并非不自然，完全不像她以为自己看到的那样。站在孩子卧室门口，一只手捂着嘴，双腿发颤，她不知道该做什么。她去书房拿了 AirPods，戴上播客，就在 Brisa 身边躺了一整夜没睡，而 Brisa 再也没动过。

**关键词**：on edge / heart in her throat / AirPods / podcast

**句子结构**：长段——"She took one of the kids’ camp sleeping bags from the closet, stretched it out on the little mat, and reached up with one hand to hold Brisa’s; her daughter had now rolled onto her stomach."；"Diana was on edge and wide awake."；"She let go of Brisa’s hand and covered her eyes with her forearm."；"She lay like that for a few seconds."；"The girl shifted, and Diana reached back out to soothe her, gently placing a hand on her back."；"Her stomach then clenched as soon as she realized she didn’t know quite what she was touching."；"It had the warmth of Brisa, but it felt nothing like her back or even her chest."；"In the half darkness, with her heart in her throat, she looked."；"Her daughter was face down from the waist up, but her knees and the tips of her toes pointed toward the ceiling."；"Diana jumped up and rushed to turn on the light."；"She looked at Brisa again."；"She was in an uncomfortable position, but not an unnatural one, nothing like what she thought she’d seen."；"Standing at the door to her child’s bedroom, one hand covering her mouth, legs trembling, she had no idea what to do."；"She went to the office for her AirPods, put on a podcast, and proceeded to lie awake all night right next to Brisa, who didn’t move again."

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "didn’t know quite what she was touching" 把 Diana 的诡异时刻写成触觉误认——Brisa 的温度但质感不对; "heart in her throat" 显示恐惧; "knees and tips of toes pointed toward ceiling" 是怪异姿势; "nothing like what she thought she’d seen" 显示感知与现实的差距; Diana 的回应（AirPods + podcast 以保持清醒）显示她的应对机制（音频分散注意力）; "who didn’t move again" 收尾显示 Brisa 安定下来; 本章的诡异触碰时刻是本章的恐怖节拍——可能是超自然的（Brisa 床上还有别的东西？）或焦虑性误认。

**读者视角提示**：Diana 触到的并非 Brisa 的背部——"某种温热却不对劲的东西"——是本章关键的 uncanny 细节；"Brisa 的体温，却完全不像她的后背"暗示那是别的东西；Brisa 的姿势（膝盖/脚趾冲向天花板）不自然；Diana 全夜无眠 = 警惕；本章短，单场景，无家族混乱——只有母女亲密 + uncanny。
