# Chapter 2

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：圣诞前的某天，Alba 与母亲 Carmen 为换游戏手柄奔走 Sant Ildefons 与 Finestrelles 商场；Alba 在商场 dissociate，并遭遇"寡妇"二人组、被施"邪眼"、流鼻血；章节后半回溯姐妹（Alba 与 Diana）女儿同日流鼻血的恐怖事件（Brigitte 体操课全身血染、Brisa 睡中抽出 30 厘米血凝块）。

- **情感弧线位置**：全书第二章——从 ch01 的 Diana 转向 Alba，扩展"母女/姐妹"的家族图谱；把恐怖从"社会性焦虑"推进到"身体恐怖与超自然暗示"；邪眼与血凝块把书的惊悚落点从心理 onto 身体。

- **人物弧线**：Alba 从"控制欲强、 compulsive 的母亲"滑向 dissociation 与邪眼恐惧；Carmen 从"牺牲型祖母"到"被动目击者"；Diana/Alba 姐妹共同陷入"女儿同日流血"的不可解释恐怖；章节末把家族女性的"身体警报"写成遗传诅咒。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限制视角主跟 Alba，后半切 Diana；长段落模拟 Alba 的 dissociation；"jellyfish" 隐喻把家族结构写成寄生有机体；结尾以"exactly the same time"收束，把巧合推向宿命。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** “Alba planned her summers in January, buying cheap plane tickets as soon as she’d rung in the New Year, before she even knew if she could afford a vacation or what her husband’s schedule would look like. In August, she got ready for Halloween, ordering every decoration she could find on AliExpress. And in September, she did her first round of Christmas shopping. Just like that, year after year.”

Alba 一月就规划夏天，新年后立刻买便宜机票，甚至还没确定是否负担得起假期或丈夫的行程；八月就为万圣节准备，AliExpress 上能订的装饰全订；九月就进行第一轮圣诞购物。就这样，年复一年。

**关键词**：planned / rung in / ordering / year after year

**句子结构**：三句——第一句 "Alba planned her summers in January, buying … before she even knew …" 现在分词短语作方式状语 + before 引导时间从句；第二句 "In August, she got ready for Halloween, ordering …" 现在分词；第三句 "And in September, she did her first round of Christmas shopping." 短句；"Just like that, year after year." 独立短句收尾。

**为什么这样写**：作者用时间错位（一月规划夏天、八月准备万圣节、九月 Christmas 购物）把 Alba 写成"永远提前、无法活在当下"的人；"Just like that, year after year." 的平淡语气把 compulsive 行为正常化，暗示这是家族女性的共同模式；与后文 "never anywhere just to be there" 呼应，Alba 无法"无目的"存在。

**读者视角提示**：本章开头定义 Alba 的时间焦虑，与 ch01 Diana 的"强迫性怀疑"形成姐妹对照；两人共享 compulsive 特质但表现不同。

> **原句 2:** “She was the first in her household to open her eyes that morning. It was seven thirty a.m. but still pitch-dark. She looked around. Only a point of light lit the room. Going to superhuman lengths to sit up in bed without waking the baby stretched out next to her, she slid her legs toward the floor, felt the cold of the parquet on her feet, and moved like a modern dancer, careful to stand without rustling anything. Barefoot, she crept toward the hall, sidestepping the clutter. When she was almost at the door, she kicked a toy car, which crashed against the wall. She couldn’t believe an object so small could make such a giant noise. It wasn’t an accident. She didn’t trip. She had done it on purpose. She’d seen the toy perfectly well and used all her strength to send it flying. The result: Everyone opened their eyes, two minutes after her. All of them now awake, tense, and jumpy.”

她是家中第一个睁眼的人。七点半但天还漆黑。她环顾四周，只有一点光照明房间。她使出超人般的努力坐起，不吵醒身边伸展的婴儿，腿滑向地板、感到木地板的冷、像现代舞者般移动，小心站立不发出声响。赤脚，她向走廊 creep，避开杂物。快到门时，她踢了一辆玩具车，撞到墙上。她不敢相信这么小的东西能发出这么大的噪音。这不是意外。她没绊倒。她是故意的。她明明看见玩具，用全力把它踢飞。结果：两分钟后，所有人都睁眼了。全部醒来，紧绷、神经质。

**关键词**：superhuman lengths / pitch-dark / on purpose / tense and jumpy

**句子结构**：十二句——短句 "She looked around. Only a point of light lit the room." 制造紧张；"Going to superhuman lengths to …, she slid …, felt …, and moved …" 三并列动词 + 分词短语；"Barefoot, she crept …" 副词 + 主谓；"When she was almost at the door, she kicked a toy car, which crashed …" which 引导非限制性定语从句；"She couldn’t believe …"；"It wasn’t an accident. She didn’t trip. She had done it on purpose." 三短句递进，从否定到主动；"She’d seen the toy perfectly well and used all her strength to send it flying."；"The result: Everyone opened their eyes, two minutes after her." 冒号引出结果；"All of them now awake, tense, and jumpy." 分词短语。

**为什么这样写**：作者把"踢玩具车"写成 deliberate act——Alba 故意制造噪音让全家醒来，显示她的控制欲与破坏欲并存；"She couldn’t believe an object so small could make such a giant noise" 的 disbelief 是自我合理化；"tense, and jumpy" 与后文"Everyone opened their eyes"的 symmetry（她先醒，全家两分钟后醒）把 Alba 写成"家族唤醒者"；这个场景 establishes 她无法独处、必须让全家与她同步的 compulsive 模式。

**读者视角提示**："modern dancer" 的比喻把日常动作仪式化，暗示 Alba 的身体 always 在"表演"; "on purpose" 三字是全章恐怖的种子——她故意制造混乱。

> **原句 3:** “There were endless possible combinations of people in Sant Ildefons, but one had come to dominate the rest: grandmother, daughter, and grandchildren, always together. The grandmother always a bodily extension of the mother, a large and obedient appendage. The three parts forming a single organism, a many-tentacled jellyfish, a socially accepted and fully integrated creature that multiplied across the neighborhood. You’d catch sight of them at the playground, the grocery store, the school gates, the clinic. The infestation wasn’t unique to this area, nor even to the outskirts of Barcelona. But it was more pronounced at the periphery, since deeper in the city, the grandmothers were often supplanted by nannies. And with paid help, the bonds weren’t so tight, because that’s precisely what you were paying for—to delegate, to kill the jellyfish. In Sant Ildefons, though, it was an epidemic, though nobody really saw it as such.”

Sant Ildefons 的人组成千变万化，但一种模式压倒其余：祖母、女儿、孙辈，永远在一起。祖母永远是母亲的身体延伸、巨大而顺从的附肢。三部分形成单一有机体、多触手的水母、被社会接受并完全融入的生物，在街坊繁殖。你会在操场、杂货店、校门、诊所看见它们。这种侵扰不独此地，甚至不独巴塞罗那郊区。但在边缘更显著，因为城市深处祖母常被保姆取代；有偿帮助时纽带不那么紧——那正是你付费的目的：委派、杀死水母。但在 Sant Ildefons，这是流行病，却无人视之为流行病。

**关键词**：bodily extension / appendage / jellyfish / infestation / epidemic

**句子结构**：长段——"There were endless possible combinations … but one had come to dominate the rest: …" 冒号引出 apposition；"The grandmother always a bodily extension …" 无动词的名词短语；"The three parts forming a single organism, a many-tentacled jellyfish, …" 分词短语作主语补足；"You’d catch sight of them at …"；"The infestation wasn’t unique …"；"But it was more pronounced …, since …"；"And with paid help, …"；"In Sant Ildefons, though, it was an epidemic, though nobody really saw it as such." 双 though 强调。

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "jellyfish" 隐喻把家族三代女性写成寄生有机体——"bodily extension / appendage" 去人格化，显示 Carmens 们的自我消解；"to delegate, to kill the jellyfish" 把"请保姆"写成唯一解放路径，讽刺家族结构；"epidemic / infestation" 的病理化修辞把"正常"家庭模式写成疾病，与后文"邪眼/血凝块"的超自然恐怖形成社会恐怖的平行线。

**读者视角提示**："symbiotic vs parasitic" 是本章主题句——家族女性关系"more parasitic than symbiotic"；这句话是 Diana 后文"遗传诅咒"叙事的理论基础。

> **原句 4:** “Whenever she felt obsessive or anxious, the places she moved through shed all objects and life; they became thresholds that she alone crossed. In that moment, all she could see was the shirt collar of the associate. Everything else was blurry or dark, depending on the intensity of the light. For seconds, she lost herself in the details of the fabric’s edge, stretched out and frayed. Then the fabric blurred, became a grayish mole that slipped through the darkness. Alba followed that mark for a good while, until the weight of her son became intolerable: Since when did the child weigh thirty pounds? Her legs had started aching, like they did when she drank wine. It was always like that when she felt completely alone, and that could happen anywhere, on the street, at home, or at the mall. Her body would detach from reality. Then it hurt.”

每当她感到 obsessive 或 anxious，她穿行的空间就 Shed 所有物体与生命，变成她独自穿越的 threshold。此刻她只能看见店员的衬衫领子。其余一切模糊或暗，取决于光线强度。几秒内她迷失在布料边缘的细节里—— stretched out and frayed。然后布料模糊、变成滑过黑暗的灰色痣。Alba 跟随那标记良久，直到儿子的重量变得无法忍受：孩子什么时候重三十磅了？她的腿开始酸痛，像她喝酒时那样。每次感到完全孤独时总是这样，可能发生在任何地方——街上、家、商场。她的身体脱离现实。然后痛。

**关键词**：threshold / dissociate (detach) / obsessive / anxious

**句子结构**：长段——"Whenever she felt obsessive or anxious, the places she moved through shed all objects and life; they became thresholds that she alone crossed." when 引导时间从句 + 分号并列；"In that moment, all she could see was …"；"Everything else was blurry or dark, depending on …"；"For seconds, she lost herself in the details of the fabric’s edge, stretched out and frayed." 分词短语作后置定语；"Then the fabric blurred, became a grayish mole that slipped through the darkness." 两动词 + that 从句；"Alba followed that mark for a good while, until …"；"Since when did the child weigh thirty pounds?" 疑问句；"Her legs had started aching, like they did when she drank wine."；"It was always like that when she felt completely alone, and that could happen anywhere, on the street, at home, or at the mall."；"Her body would detach from reality. Then it hurt." 两短句。

**为什么这样写**：作者把 dissociation 写成"空间 Shed 物体"——不是 Alba 看不见，而是世界主动消失，显示她的感知被焦虑劫持；"grayish mole" 把店员领子变成追踪目标，显示 compulsive 的"追着一个细节"模式；"Since when did the child weigh thirty pounds?" 是 dissociation 中的身体扭曲（ proprioception 失真）；"Her body would detach from reality. Then it hurt." 两短句是本章最冷的句子——身体脱离现实是 relief，痛是回归代价，显示 Alba 的 dissociation 是"逃避"而非"休息"。

**读者视角提示**：Camp La Tortuga Ligera 的 sign 是 Alba 的"安全意象"，暗示她 dissociation 时会逃往童年记忆；后文 likely 复现此 motif。

> **原句 5:** “Alba had just stepped out of MediaMarkt—for the second time—when she saw them approaching. The same two women she always saw there. They were a few meters away now, and they terrified her. She couldn’t understand why they always turned up, no matter what time they went to the mall. Were they really always there? They looked to be around seventy and were practically identical. They had hairstyles from another era—stiff waves framing two hard, faintly mustached faces, not a single trace of makeup. They dressed in uncompromising black, as if they’d been mourning someone for years, out of inertia, duty, or boredom. And they were always roaming arm in arm. With their free arms, they clung to their bags so tightly that stealing them was outside the realm of possibility. Not a day went by without Alba and Carmen seeing them stroll through the mall, same attitude, same expression, as if they were just passing time in the village square. … Alba simply called them “the widows,” and every time she saw them, she’d make Carmen turn abruptly so they wouldn’t cross paths.”

Alba 刚走出 MediaMarkt——第二次——就看见她们逼近。那两个她总在那儿看见的女人。她们现在几米远，吓坏她了。她不明白为什么她们总是出现，不管她什么时候去商场。她们真的总在吗？看起来七十岁左右，几乎一模一样：过时发型——僵硬波浪框住两张硬朗、微髭的脸，一丝妆都没有。她们穿毫不妥协的黑，像为某人哀悼多年，出于惯性、义务或无聊。她们总是挽臂而行，空着的手紧抱包包，偷都偷不走。Alba 和 Carmen 没有一天不看见她们在商场 stroll，同样态度、同样表情，像在村广场消磨时光。……Alba 只叫她们"寡妇"，每次看见，她都让 Carmen 突然转身避免相撞。

**关键词**：terrified / mourning / inertia / roaming arm in arm

**句子结构**：多句——"Alba had just stepped out … when she saw them approaching." when 引导时间从句；"The same two women she always saw there." 同位语；"They were a few meters away now, and they terrified her."；"She couldn’t understand why …"；"Were they really always there?" 疑问；"They looked to be around seventy and were practically identical."；"They had hairstyles from another era—stiff waves framing two hard, faintly mustached faces, not a single trace of makeup." em dash + 分词短语；"They dressed in uncompromising black, as if they’d been mourning someone for years, out of inertia, duty, or boredom." as if + 介词短语；"And they were always roaming arm in arm."；"With their free arms, they clung to their bags so tightly that stealing them was outside the realm of possibility." so … that 结果从句；"Not a day went by without Alba and Carmen seeing them stroll through the mall, same attitude, same expression, as if they were just passing time in the village square."；"Alba simply called them 'the widows,' and every time she saw them, she’d make Carmen turn abruptly so they wouldn’t cross paths." so 引导目的从句。

**为什么这样写**：作者把"寡妇"写成商场的固定 fixture——"Not a day went by" 显示 Alba 的 obsessive 注意力，她们可能是真实人物或 Alba 投射；"uncompromising black … mourning someone for years, out of inertia, duty, or boredom" 三选一的动机显示 Alba 的 uncertainty；"as if they were just passing time in the village square" 把商场写成村庄广场，显示 Sant Ildefons 的 provincial 感；"the widows" 命名是 Alba 的防御性控制——命名即控制。

**读者视角提示**：Diana 的描述（"blind medium and her sister" from her favorite movie）把寡妇写成 gothic archetype；Alba 的邪眼恐惧与 Diana 的电影式 fascination 形成姐妹对照；"evil eye" 是本章超自然元素的引入。

> **原句 6:** “Diana and Alba both barely slept that night. They were overwhelmingly disturbed, and the guilt seeped through their insides as quickly as the blood through Brigitte’s leotard, with the same unbreakable consistency as the clot Diana had pulled from Brisa’s nose. None of the explanations from pediatricians, gymnastics coaches, Carmen, Carlos, or Juan convinced them. They could find no solace. Both situations were horrible, appalling, and incomprehensible. Even more disturbingly: It had happened to their daughters, not other women’s, and at the exact same time.”

Alba 和 Diana 那晚几乎没睡。她们被压倒性地 disturb，罪疚感 seep through 她们内脏，像血 seep through Brigitte 的体操服，像 Diana 从 Brisa 鼻子抽出的血凝块一样 unbreakable。儿科医生、体操教练、Carmen、Carlos、Juan 的解释都没说服她们。她们找不到 solace。两种情况都 horrible、appalling、incomprehensible。更 disturbing：发生在她们女儿身上，不是别的女人的，且在同一时刻。

**关键词**：overwhelmingly / seeped / solace / appalling / incomprehensible

**句子结构**：六句——"Diana and Alba both barely slept that night."；"They were overwhelmingly disturbed, and the guilt seeped through their insides as quickly as the blood through Brigitte’s leotard, with the same unbreakable consistency as the clot Diana had pulled from Brisa’s nose." 两个 as 引导的比较 + with 短语；"None of the explanations from … convinced them."；"They could find no solace." 短句；"Both situations were horrible, appalling, and incomprehensible." 三形容词；"Even more disturbingly: It had happened to their daughters, not other women’s, and at the exact same time." 冒号引出强调。

**为什么这样写**：作者用 "guilt seeped … as quickly as the blood … with the same unbreakable consistency as the clot" 把心理罪疚与身体血迹直接等同——母亲的 guilt 是"不可断"的，与血凝块的 physical consistency 一致；"None of the explanations … convinced them" 显示医学/理性解释失败，把恐怖留作不可解释；"at the exact same time" 是本章最终升级——巧合被写成宿命，暗示家族女性被某种 collective force 联结。

**读者视角提示**："not other women’s" 显示姐妹的 narcissistic 保护欲（恐怖只针对我们），也是家族诅咒叙事；本章结束在"不可解释"而非解释，把悬念留给后续章节。

## 一句话总结

第二章把 Alba 写成 compulsive 的家族女性：她故意踢玩具车吵醒全家、在商场 dissociate、遭遇"寡妇"邪眼、流鼻血；章节后半回溯姐妹女儿同日流血（Brigitte 体操课全身血染、Brisa 睡中抽出 30 厘米血凝块），把"家族女性身体警报"写成不可解释的遗传诅咒，恐怖从社会焦虑推进到身体恐怖。
