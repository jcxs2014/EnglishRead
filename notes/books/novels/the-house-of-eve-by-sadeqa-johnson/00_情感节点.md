# 《The House of Eve》情感节点

## 节点一：第一节课之前

ch01 那个下午把三件事挤在一起：表姐迟到、母亲的钱不见、母亲的男友用一个吻换路费。门开时 Inez 认定是女儿勾引了对方，把她推出门外——而她仍然按时赶去上课，迟到四十五分钟。这一章给全书定下了一条规矩：伤害已经发生，但日子仍要照走。

> "The only thing standing between me and getting to the program on time was a kiss. A measly little kiss. I could do this."（ch01）

> "It was times like this I wished there was a button that would erase me. Not to die or nothing. Just so I wouldn’t exist."（ch01）

> "Although you have great spirit, we had a strong pool of applicants and cannot offer you a place on line this year. Please keep up your community presence, study hard, and we invite you to try again next year."（ch02）


## 节点二：一封客气的拒信

ch02 Eleanor 拆开 Alpha Beta Chi 的回信，理由是『申请者太多』；室友 Nadine 当面说破这家人挑的是发质直得像尺子、皮肤比纸袋还白的女孩。Eleanor 自己给过两条反驳，而叙述者用一句话把它们收掉：Not in the mirror, you aren’t.

> "Although you have great spirit, we had a strong pool of applicants and cannot offer you a place on line this year. Please keep up your community presence, study hard, and we invite you to try again next year."（ch02）

> "Not in the mirror, you aren’t."（ch02）


## 节点三：把一整章写在别人的柜台上

ch04/ch05 两条线在同一天里各挨一次逐客。Eleanor 在 William 家看穿一屋子『白面孔的黑人』，随即被 Greta 当面泼了饮料；Ruby 在糖果店先被女客警告『当心你交的朋友』，再被店主一句『别落得像你爸那样』撵出门。前者让她看清自己站得最靠边，后者让她第一次听见父亲的判决。

> "Greta’s skin was so white the only thing that identified her as a Negro was that she attended Howard with the rest of them."（ch04）

> "Be careful of the company you keep, Shimmy. They are a danger to good Jewish boys like you."（ch05）

> "You can’t be friends with the likes of her. I thought you had more sense, boy. Don’t end up like your father."（ch05）


## 节点四：公车上的一堂课

ch11 姑姑换上演出的戏装、带她进城买第一双长袜，走到门口被一个白人女人用种族辱骂叫住。姑姑回骂一句『我也纳税』把她拽走，回程公交车上 Ruby 得出全书题眼：连本该免费的东西都得去争。同一天她把要放开 Shimmy 的念头落了地。

> "And this trip downtown had shown me that we even had to fight for what should have been free: our dignity."（ch11）

> "You don’t care because you don’t have to, Shimmy. You live in a beautiful safe bubble, and the world says I don’t belong there."（ch13）


## 节点五：母亲在台上的翻译

ch15 姑姑的 Kiki's 舞厅是 Ruby 第一次把『我想』说出口的地方，也是第一次听见 Inez把这句话原样还给她——『不合法的事多的是』。这一天之后，Ruby 学会的不是争取，是记账：哪一句话说出去会被换成什么。

> "Lots of things illegal. Don’t make it wrong. You should know—sneaking around with a damn Jewish boy for Christ’s sake."（ch15）

> "Our time is for eternity,"（ch16）


## 节点六：一栏宣读式的清单

ch27–ch29 Ruby 进了 Shapiro 家的门，用一次交出去换一个进去的资格。分娩那天她被按上一个不属于她的名字，婴儿被带走，护士用『你唯一的权利是二十四小时的羞辱室』把她关进去。这一段是全书最冷的一段，因为它把羞辱做成了场所、把人生做成了可宣读的清单。

> "This is the shaming room. It’s where the bad girls go.” She looked at me pointedly, freezing me in place with her gaze."（ch25）

> "You are unfit to raise a child, Clara. You have no job, no husband and cannot support this baby."（ch29）


## 节点七：门缝里的那句话

ch33 隔着门缝听见对方说出的那一句。前半句是四段碎句——他们、把、他、带走了；后半句忽然接上一个完整的、属于自己的名字。Ruby 在这一章第一次在别人给她的称呼之外，给自己留了一个词。

> "They. Took. Him. Away. They took my baby. My David."（ch33）

> "It didn’t take long before my fingers were stiff from the cold, but that didn’t stop my brushes from flying with yellow and orange across the blank canvas. It had been ages since I had gotten lost in Ruby Red’s World, and my mind was so deep inside my imagination that I didn’t feel the cold or hear the door open."（ch37）


## 节点八：回来的那个孩子

ch32–ch42 两条线在这里并排：Eleanor 抱着女儿闭眼谢了 Eve 一句，而 Ruby 那边的 Georgia Mae 正在学一件她从没学过的事——把孩子交出去。Eleanor 讲的那句『for her second chance』被原样传到了 Ruby 耳朵里，接收的人却不知道它从哪来。

> "But on Expectant Mother’s long list of pregnancy symptoms, there were two that Eleanor did not need to fake: insomnia and restlessness."（ch32）

> "They were all liars. These people who were supposed to be her family had played her like a fiddle."（ch42）


## 节点九：十三年后的握手

ch48 尾声。电视下方的滚动字幕是哈莱姆骚乱的第五天，屋里在争的是女儿穿哪条裙子。Eleanor 去医院，在丈夫的办公室见到新来的验光师——对方自报『请叫我 Ruby』，胸前挂着 Pearsall。握手时她很难把手抽回来。全书写到这里没有一句点破，而书名早已替她说完了。

> "Wilhelmina Rose Lorraine Pride, come out of that closet right now!"（ch48）

> "No boy had ever called me beautiful before."（ch09）
