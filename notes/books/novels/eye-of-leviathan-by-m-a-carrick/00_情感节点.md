# 情感节点

## 节点一：树丛之约（ch01）

Teresa 抱着濒死的新生儿穿过葡萄园赴精灵之约：精灵 Castaña 亲自顶替女婴、以"儿子"Estevan 的身份进入人类之家，女婴则被带去彼界由精灵抚养。换子契约不是贪念而是母爱的越权——孩子活了，代价是两家的孩子一生都被放错了位置。

关键引语：
- "Too young, too young. Blessed Virgin, I only want my child to live. If that’s a sin, then every mother is a sinner."（ch01，Teresa 心中的自辩）
- "The weight of that trade would haunt Teresa for the rest of her life."（ch01，叙述者给全书定下的债务）

## 节点二：洗礼铁疤（ch02）

曾被唤作 Castaña 的精灵以人类的躯壳领受圣餐，他亲眼看清洗礼夺走了什么：不是罪，而是精灵本性里最珍贵的东西。这道记忆此后成为他的警告与马刺。

关键引语：
- "Estevan, seeing it firsthand, knew the truth: Whatever else baptism might do, it tore something away. Something equally priceless, which that proud faerie would never get back."（ch02，Estevan 的第一手认知）
- "The memory remained in his mind like an iron scar, warning and spur alike."（ch02，铁疤意象定型）

## 节点三：海上弃儿（ch03）

女妖领主把饥饿女孩逐出巢穴，用"不属于这里"攻击她的出身；而饥饿女孩的回敬冷静得可怕——她有的是时间，被放逐者反而活得最久。

关键引语：
- "You don’t even belong here, you—you ugly, human lump. Better for everyone if your mother had drowned you at birth."（ch03，鹰身女妖的驱逐）
- "I may have had the ill-luck to be born mortal… but I’ll still be here when you’re nothing more than a stinking harpy. I have time, my lady. You don’t."（ch03，饥饿女孩的反击）

## 节点四：友谊与誓言（ch04 / ch06 / ch11）

Alejandro 为庇护 Estevan 甘愿冒宗教裁判所之怒，理由朴素到令人眩晕；Estevan 如告解般说出潜入人间的初衷；而 Diego 以失落的真名起誓——三条线都是"把自己交出去"。

关键引语：
- "The answer was simple, artless, and dizzying. “Because you’re my friend.”"（ch04，Alejandro 说）
- "I came to learn how the islands of the Sea Beyond are brought under control, so that what happened to my people in this world cannot keep happening there."（ch06，Estevan 对 Alejandro 说）
- "I swear on my lost true name—I will keep my word."（ch11，Diego 立誓）

## 节点五：三问碎魂（ch20）

Estevan 对走私船长 Beneyto 摆出精灵古礼：三问三答，答真一项留一份馈赠。拒绝回答的代价是心智本身——魔法收割的不是命，而是"能思考的那部分人"。

关键引语：
- "I will pose you three questions, and for every answer true, I will leave you a boon. Your life. Your heart. Your mind."（ch20，Estevan 对 Beneyto 说）
- "For your mind. Beneyto had refused. And magic had taken its toll."（ch20，拒绝的结局）

## 节点六：命名真言（ch23）

垂死的无翼者被饥饿女孩以真名仪式重新命名——真名是祝福与愿望的合金，命名即是造人。她把他从毁灭边缘唤回为一个新存在，名为 Morn。

关键引语：
- "A true name was an amalgam of blessings and wishes, hopes and dreams, shaping each silvery dewdrop sprung from Leviathan’s consciousness into something uniquely itself."（ch23，真名的定义）
- "She named him—and she brought him into being."（ch23，命名的瞬间）
- "It is a good name. A guide to shape my becoming."（ch23，Morn 受名）

## 节点七：铁栏棚前的崩塌（ch24）

Estevan 走进铁镣囚棚，烧焦血肉般的铁腥气把他所有"为了更大目的"的自我说服击成灰烬。十年共谋的高调辞令在一个嗅觉瞬间里全部作废。

关键引语：
- "Then the smell hit him like a blow, the burnt-meat stink of iron shackles chafing faerie flesh."（ch24，气味如拳）
- "In the face of that, all his high-minded words crumbled like ash."（ch24，辞令成灰）
- "He couldn’t do it anymore."（ch24，临界点）

## 节点八：养母的责备与不可能的答案（ch25 / ch26）

饥饿女孩第一次确认"有人守在身边"对她是多大的事；而 Teresa 面对隐瞒女儿消息二十年的养子，说出那句责备。摆在 Estevan 面前的每个答案都是错的——除非把所有人救下来。

关键引语：
- "After so much abandonment in her life, did he know what it meant to her, to have someone stay at her side?"（ch25，饥饿女孩的内心）
- "I did not think you had become that sort of man."（ch26，Teresa 对 Estevan 说）
- "An impossible choice. Every answer was wrong. Every answer except one: Rescue everyone."（ch26，不可能的选择）

## 节点九：雨塔相认（ch27 / ch28）

饥饿女孩踏上西班牙东岸的第一捧故土，在雨中的塔楼外与养兄对峙；而她看着那个操盘一切的人，发现他普通得惊人—— 陌生人与手足是同一个人。

关键引语：
- "The Hungry Girl stumbled over the wale and into the sand, digging her fingers deep into her first touch of her native soil in many long years."（ch27，初踏故土）
- "He looked so ordinary."（ch28，饥饿女孩眼中）
- "He was a stranger; he was her brother. The son her mother had raised in place of a daughter. He wasn’t family, and yet he was."（ch28，陌生与手足同体）

## 节点十：拒救的钟声（ch29）

救赎跪在饥饿女孩面前，代价是奴役所有同类——她拒绝。Morn 在崩溃中推开神父退开两步；城市的钟声缓缓敲响，国王死了。

关键引语：
- "I can’t save myself if it means damning everyone else,"（ch29，饥饿女孩的拒绝）
- "It broke Morn. He shoved the priest to the ground and backed away, one step, two."（ch29，Morn 崩断）
- "Sometime after his arrival, the city’s bells began to toll a slow, mournful peal."（ch29，终章钟声）
