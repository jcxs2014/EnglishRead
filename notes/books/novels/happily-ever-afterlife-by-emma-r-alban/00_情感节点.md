## 节点一

**叙事概括：** Frannie 二十七岁，做 Henry L. Olsen 的第三年助理兼代笔；她的「办公室」是达科尼亚第九层那间没窗的储藏室。本章第一页又被退回，怨气堆到顶——她对着空房间喊出的那句「不好」撞在护墙板上散进地毯；而她自己心里那条「早该发生却没发生」的单子也一笔笔数得出来。

**关键引语：**

> "Her life and literary career have dead-ended at twenty-seven, on the ninth floor of the Dakota Building, and she wants to scream. But no one would hear her anyway."（ch01）

> "She’s supposed to be succeeding under Henry’s tutelage. The first year as his assistant was all grunt work, but the second two—she should be a client of his literary agent by now. She should have a book deal. A short story in an anthology. Something."（ch02）

## 节点二

**叙事概括：** 一个穿维多利亚裙的小女孩抱着旧红皮球拦住她，开口不是吓唬，而是替她把孤独说破——她真的陪这女孩接了半小时球。闹钟一响，她立刻把整件事判成「不可能」；可到了 ch02，她自己承认该怕的没怕，压过恐惧的是好奇。

**关键引语：**

> "You’re always alone up here, and today you seemed more alone than normal. So, I thought I’d scare the shit out of you."（ch01）

> "She didn’t play catch with a ghost. Impossible."（ch01）

> "She should be scared. She should probably be terrified. This isn’t a fairy tale. This is her life. She should be concerned that she’s being haunted. But all she really feels is … curious."（ch02）

## 节点三

**叙事概括：** 那位被她在心里叫作 Hot Ghost Mom 的鬼第一次现身就把她的呼吸打乱；此后她想给对方一个称呼都说不出口。ch07 那场为稿子铺路的等待末尾，她伸手去抓对方的手腕——静电窜上手臂，那截手腕却是凉的、细的、实实在在的；对方这才报出名字。

**关键引语：**

> "Frannie can hardly breathe. She’s stop you on the street, drop your glass, lose your tongue kind of gorgeous. Maybe early thirties, with eyes like frosted ice, and glaring at Frannie like she’s the most exasperating person on the planet—this was not the Ghost Mom Frannie was expecting. The fact that she’s a ghost barely even registers."（ch02）

> "Static charges up Frannie’s arm, sharp and furiously tingling. Her wrist is cool to the touch, delicate and smooth, and perfectly solid."（ch07）

> "“It’s Elsa,” Hot Ghost Mom says softly, her hand flexing down by her side."（ch07）

## 节点四

**叙事概括：** ch11 雪夜，Elsa 第一次把底交出来：她生前在为 Joan 处理一只台灯时触电；她与 Joan 分手，是因为 Joan 觉得外面的人会怎么看；后来她想起 Bridgette 和 Michael 这两个和她一样走丢的孩子——三个人从此在一起，不落下任何一个。同一晚 Frannie 自己转到了同一个念头上：她得想办法做一个留下来的人——因为没有别的地方比此刻更想待。

**关键引语：**

> "Elsa’s face lifts in a true smile. “And then I had Bridgette and Michael to think about. They were lost, like me. They needed someone. And I so desperately needed to be needed. We’ve been the three of us ever since. Together forever, no one left behind.”"（ch11）

> "“Having someone to talk to. It’s been so long since Joan left—I’d forgotten. Watching the snow with you, watching movies—it’s lovely. I understand why Bridgette risks it now. It’s worth the end to have the middle.”"（ch11）

## 节点五

**叙事概括：** ch05 那通电话里，他连排四个「不信任」；ch15 同一条师徒线上，他改为承认她的编辑「不可思议」，真正不解的是她为什么不用同样的 excellence 攻击自己的写作。ch18 的年终餐桌上，这层关系被彻底翻过来：他说自己本想留她一辈子，但那不公平——你有这个天赋，去写一本让他日后能说「我早就认识你」的书。

**关键引语：**

> "He doesn’t trust her creative process. Doesn’t trust that she could have an idea complicated enough to need a few weeks’ thought. Doesn’t trust that she knows her own methods. Doesn’t trust that she’s writing the book he asked for."（ch05）

> "Henry sighs and looks down at his preorder materials. “I think your edit is incredible,” he says, tapping his fingers on the table. “So, I’m really baffled as to why you’re not attacking your own writing with the same excellence. Especially when you had so much free time,” he adds, looking up at her, face pensive."（ch15）

> "“You’re an excellent assistant, and a damn good editor. If I had my druthers, you’d be my assistant for the rest of my career. But I know that’s not fair to you. You have the talent. That’s why I hired you. So, prove me right and write me a book that’ll let me say I knew you when.”"（ch18）

## 节点六

**叙事概括：** 她给纸上的 Charlie 写的正是自己求而不得的那份童话：翻一个开关，鬼孩子就能过街，happily ever afterlife。可她自己先把这个童话拆了——她给不了他们一个幸福结局，也写不出自己知道该写的那个；ch15 一句「那又不是我的错」把责任推回来。ch18 的阁楼里，这三个字终于出口，而作者特地交代：它是不假思索出来的。

**关键引语：**

> "Romance is a pact you make with the reader: Pick up this book, get a happily ever after, or at worst, a happy for now. No ifs, no ands, no equivocations. Happily Ever After, Guaranteed."（ch13）

> "“It’s not my fault you wrote it sad.”"（ch15）

> "It comes out without any thought at all: “I love you. Of course I want this with you.”"（ch18）

## 节点七

**叙事概括：** Elsa 读了那一页，打印、转给 Henry，全程没有问过她。Frannie 撞破的那一秒不是被认可，而是被夺走——这个结果她想要过，可促成这件事的人一个也没问过她。ch21 的争吵里两边同时说出「为你好」：Henry 觉得她配得上这一切，Elsa 则不许她为了留下来把自己变小。

**关键引语：**

> "The book that Elsa read, and gave to Henry, without telling her. Without asking her. Without asking what she wanted."（ch20）

> "“How could you do that? I didn’t—you read my book, you printed my book, you gave it to Henry.”"（ch21）

> "“But you can’t give up for us. This is the one, Frannie, and I won’t let you throw that away to stay here and be less than you are.”"（ch21）

## 节点八

**叙事概括：** ch22 那封邮件会当场改变她的人生。真正拿到报价、签下合约之后，她做的第一件事不是庆功，而是把当初以为「唯一」的那个结局换掉——她得说服鬼女友相信他们还有未来。ch29 她第一次以自己的名义站到作者之夜：一个人去，一个人应付下来。

**关键引语：**

> "This email will actually change her life. Right now. This minute."（ch22）

> "When she wrote the original ending, she thought it was the only way for the story to end. The only way for her life to end. But now she has a $750,000 book deal, and she needs to convince her ghost girlfriend to believe in their future."（ch26）

> "She went there alone. She impressed people, alone. She succeeded, all on her own."（ch29）

## 节点九

**叙事概括：** 她把一套在老电网上的公寓、一次推闸、四个人的新生活排到 Elsa 面前，被当场退回——「我不是选择被困在这里，是没有别的路。」她没停手：查建筑图纸、找通灵猎人、把条件摊在桌面上，只要求把这场戏演完。ch30 地下室那面墙后面是一只挂着 1883 年铜牌的配电箱，这一次，对方点了头。

**关键引语：**

> "Elsa slowly drags her eyes up to meet Frannie’s. “I’m not choosing to be trapped here. There isn’t an alternative. That’s the difference.” She looks so calm and collected about it."（ch24）

> "“Look, if we can’t find them, then … it’s all moot anyway. But if we can, then we have a conversation, right? I know you looked. I know Michael looked. But I’ve got building plans and a ghost hunter. I’m bringing something to the table here. Can you meet me halfway? At least let us play this out?”"（ch27）

> "“I want that happiness for me, for us. It’s worth the risk. Something permanent,” Elsa whispers."（ch30）

## 节点十

**叙事概括：** ch32 告别夜，两个孩子举起写着 Happy Last Night 的横幅；Elsa 却说如果结局是幸福，那停在这里也是值得的。ch33 她送走三个鬼：Michael 先走进墙里，伸手把 Bridgette 拉过去；闸合上之后没有声响、没有光，她数到三十又把闸推了回去——本章不给结果。三十年后那一节换了视角，Charlie 推开门看见三个鬼的轮廓扑上来，却只真正碰得到其中一个，那位叫 Betty；随后镜头交回 Frannie，她正站在台上朗读这本书的尾声。

**关键引语：**

> "“It’s more than enough,” Elsa says gently. “It’s everything. We did so good, Frannie. We’re happy. And if we end with happiness, then it’s all worthwhile. It would be a kindness for things to end here.”"（ch32）

> "But her arms go right through Lucy. She feels a static pulse, but they go right through Marvin as well. It’s just Betty she can truly touch, but her shoulders, her torso, her beautiful face feel like gossamer."（ch34）

> "“I would. Look, real life is seldom so fantastic. Real people don’t go around meeting ghosts, and falling in love with them, and saving them. But isn’t it wonderful to live in a world where they do, just for a few pages?”"（ch34）
