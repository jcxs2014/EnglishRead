# 金句精选

> "Grief didn't go away—it became memories."
> — Reed Howland，ch48。全书 grief 主题的核心表达。

> "I walk through the Village, and mothers are wheeling baby carriages through Washington Square, and couples are having coffee at Caffe Reggio, and it's as though I'm locked in a glass cage. I see the living, but I'm not part of them."
> — Caitlin 向 Reed 倾诉，ch50。最动人的 grief 描写。

> "The dead do love you forever. You can't lose them."
> — Jack Kennedy，ch73。对 Caitlin 说的关于 grief 的话，也是全书的情感核心。

> "I'm hungry all the time. And I can't—I can't get full."
> — Jack Kennedy，ch73。Kennedy 罕见的自我剖析，关于他永无止境的欲望。

> "People working in intelligence neither think nor behave as everyone else, so discerning motives is dicey."
> — Allen Dulles，ch46。情报机构的标准语言游戏。

> "That's beyond the pale, wouldn't you say?"
> — Dulles 对 Jack 威胁的回应，ch46。Jack 随即做了历史注解——爱尔兰农民起义的暗喻。

> "It becomes easier, Caitlin. I promise."
> — Reed，ch50。他对 grief 的安慰，最终证明是对的——但他自己却为 Caitlin 而死。

> "Caitlin packed the diaphragm and jelly anyway, an act of wishful thinking."
> — ch48。Caitlin 收拾行装去 Belmar 前的最后一个动作，wishful thinking 是全章的点睛之笔。

> "The polls have me trailing Nixon. But the second you read a poll, it's out of date. I'm going to win, Allen."
> — Jack，ch46。竞选自信与政治计算的完美结合。

> "The beginning was my husband dying with his head in my lap!"
> — Caitlin，ch52。对 Danny 要求"从头说"的爆发式回应。

> "Very."
> — Jack Kennedy，ch46。一个字回应 Jackie 想要十八世纪古董的愿望，涵盖上流社会消费观与经济现实。
