# 53. The Investigation Stalls

## 概述

袭击事件 72 小时后，Danny 在警局陷入僵局，无法找到目击者。Julian 来电，两人深夜驱车前往 Park Central Hotel 的 Mermaid Room 直接质问 Trafficante。Matt Calvetti 嘲讽 Danny 被一拳打倒。Trafficante 表示他也在找 Clara，并否认派人袭击 Caitlin——Julian 以"两个问题"相胁，Trafficante 认账。

## 精读

> "After fifteen years of questioning people about shootings in busy subway stations and barrooms, he concluded you could machine-gun half the city and no one would pay attention unless you were shooting at them."

**句子结构**：After + 动名词短语作状语 + 主句（he concluded + that 宾语从句）。从句本身是否定条件句unless 触发。

**关键词**：machine-gun / no one would pay attention

**为什么这样写**：Danny 十五年刑侦经验的总结——Golden 用黑色幽默表达对城市人际冷漠的无奈，也是对 ch51 中"无目击者"的直接解释。

---

> "Julian lived large on his bloodstained fortune while decent people—like Danny's late father, who spent forty years pushing racks in the Garment Center—worked their asses off and pinched pennies till the day they died."

**句子结构**：while 引导的对比从句；破折号插入语是 Danny 父亲的具体化描述；work their asses off / pinched pennies 并列。

**关键词**：bloodstained fortune / decent people

**为什么这样写**：Julian 的慈善名声与黑金背景的对比，揭示 Danny 对他复杂的情感——既厌恶又依赖，也解释了为什么 Julian 能对 Trafficante 形成威胁。

---

> "If I ain't finding her, Caitlin won't. I don't got no ill will for her."

**句子结构**：两个并列条件句，双重否定（don't got no = 没有没有 = 有）形成口语强调。

**关键词**：don't got no ill will

**为什么这样写**：Trafficante 这句话里的双重否定是口语真实感，但"我找不到她 Caitlin 更找不到"同时透露他的自信与对 Clara 线索价值的判断。

## 词汇

**machine-gun** ⭐ 进阶｜v. 用机枪扫射；连续追问｜原文例句：you could machine-gun half the city and no one would pay attention｜Danny 十五年经验总结：城市居民对他人悲剧的冷漠是刑事侦查的最大障碍。
