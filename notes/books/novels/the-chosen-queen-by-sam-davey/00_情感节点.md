# The Chosen Queen · 情感节点（10 节点）

> 引语全部逐字取自原书，说话人已经原文上下文核验。

## 节点一：床笫之暖与王的目光（Chapter One）

晨间的 Tintagel 夫妻还拥有彼此的全部：她指尖熟稔他身体的每一寸，他唯一的毛病是"如冬日风暴的脾气"。庆功宴上，Uther 的目光落在她身上——Gorlois 察觉了，Igraine 选择撒谎维稳。婚姻的第一道测试里，她押了"息事宁人"。

- 「But I would rather you presented yourself modestly before the court this morning. I did not like the way the King's gaze rested on you last night.」（Gorlois，Chapter One）
- 「My husband had but one fault, a temper as high and harsh as the winter storms that lash the cliffs of Tintagel, our castle by the sea, and although he was not quick to anger, once his wrath had been unleashed it was a mighty task to calm him.」（Igraine 叙述，Chapter One）

## 节点二：月光裙与尊位之辱（Chapter Two–Three）

国王的贺礼是一条"月光织成"的裙子——美到无法拒绝，穿上即是共谋。丈夫说"You look like a Queen"时神情古怪；宴席上 Vivian 当众召她上座，吻手时舌抵指缝，"shamelessly yet secretly"；暗廊里她被堵在墙上强吻，挣脱时撕碎了裙。夫妻连夜拔营。

- 「It was truly the most beautiful garment I had ever seen. The cloth looked like woven moonbeams.」（Igraine，Chapter Two）
- 「All I ask is that you stay beside me and give Uther no reason to believe his feelings are encouraged.」（Gorlois，Chapter Two）
- 「I had no need to see his face, to see the wounds upon his much-loved body to know that I was looking at my husband.」（镜占中，Igraine，Chapter One）

## 节点三：掬土誓师（Chapter Four）

Whiddon Down 的篝火边，Gorlois 掬起康沃尔的土当众宣告妻子遭到的侵犯，举剑立誓：不道歉，就开战。Igraine 藏在人群里羞耻欲绝——她的身体成了丈夫演说的证物。这也是她第一次看清：爱与所有物在丈夫的语法里只有一步之遥。

- 「This is the soil of Cornwall. Our soil. My soil.」（Gorlois，Chapter Four）
- 「"He tried to rape his wife."」（Gorlois，Chapter Four）
- 「I felt as if Gorlois had hit me. I hadn't known that this was his intention, to share my shame with everyone, to expose me to their curiosity.」（Igraine，Chapter Four）

## 节点四：预言点名（Chapter Five–Six）

Merlin 摊牌：Caliburn 在等 Igraine 的独子。Gorlois 听懂了所有未曾言明的事，拒绝以妻换和平；威尔士的哥哥在 Vivian 的祝福下倒戈。同章，四岁的 Morgan 镜占照出湖畔迎接——蓝眼睛与夫人一模一样。预言、血统、背叛，三条线拧死。

- 「Caliburn is waiting for a warrior. And the warrior it waits for shall be Igraine's only son.」（Merlin，Chapter Five）
- 「I will no longer allow Her worship in my lands. When my son is born, we will raise him in the new ways.」（Gorlois，Chapter Five）
- 「Why had I never seen it before? Morgan, my winter child with her long dark hair, delicate bones and pale, pale skin, looked more like Vivian's daughter than my own.」（Igraine，Chapter Six）

## 节点五：长女出海（Chapter Nine）

Yseult 的预言（必有一战、只活一个）与妇人们的诅咒围逼之下，Igraine 送六岁的 Morgause 上船。她用"公主与白帆船"的童话哄女儿、以"明年花开前我就来"承诺归期——而叙述者亲口作证：这个承诺她永远兑现不了。启程当晨，被附身的 Elaine 唱出判词。

- 「But I will come soon, my love, I will come next summer, before the blossom turns to fruit on the trees in Orkney.」（Igraine，Chapter Nine）
- 「You will never hold your daughter in your arms again.」（借 Elaine 之口的声音，Chapter Nine）

## 节点六：魂影之夜与凶象兑现（Chapter Eleven）

为部署 Morgause 的祭仪，Igraine 召出自己的 taibhse 飞往海上——却不知她的灵体早已是 Vivian 的"资产"。归途中魂影被垂死的尖叫拽到 Demelihoc：战马中箭、战辫骑者被扑倒——第一章镜占的画面逐帧上演。她两次中断了丈夫的死亡画面，第三次仍没能看到名字。

- 「I had listened and obeyed, bound by the magics to serve the Lady of the Lake.」（taibhse，Chapter Eleven）
- 「I saw three soldiers pull the rider down and engulf him as he continued to scream out his war cry.」（taibhse 目击，Chapter Eleven）

## 节点七：假团圆与测谎的孩子（Chapter Twelve–Thirteen）

"Gorlois"带伤破门，第一幅镜占兑现，夫妻在恐惧失而复得中重修于好——甚至约定当众宣誓求和。但疑点累积：孤身无军、忘了 Morgause 已北送、来路不明的草药袋。全家的成年人选择相信，只有一个四岁的孩子拒绝盖章。

- 「I don't know who he is, but that man is not my father.」（Morgan，Chapter Twelve）
- 「All that happened was a squalid little fumble in the castle corridor. And because of this, we went to war.」（Igraine，Chapter Twelve）
- 「Listen Igraine, I will heed your words. There will be peace. I will make my right and proper oaths, and you must, too. Will you swear loyalty to Uther? Recognise the Pendragon as your liege, over-lord of Cornwall and rightful king of all the Britons?」（"Gorlois"，Chapter Twelve）

## 节点八：Solstice——加冕与揭穿（Chapter Fourteen）

三段仪式连轴：Yseult 向 Morgan 下跪、权杖易手；手缚礼中"Gorlois"面容闪变一瞬；效忠宣誓之后，Bennath 浴血驰归喊出三个名字即中箭。卧室里，护身囊摊开——月光裙的碎布。那个男人说出全书最轻的抱歉。

- 「I knelt before a magic greater than my own. The power of the Goddess, working through your daughter, commanded me. I had no choice but to submit.」（Yseult，Chapter Fourteen）
- 「They are all dead: Gorlois, Lowen, Erec…」（Bennath，Chapter Fourteen）
- 「I'm sorry, Igraine," he said.」（Uther，Chapter Fourteen）

## 节点九：女儿们的离别与复仇的种子（Chapter Nine、Sixteen–Seventeen）

Morgause 在痛哭中被哄上船；Morgan 在冷静中自己走向船。姐姐的诅咒落在未出世的弟弟身上——"他不是我的弟弟"；话音落处，婴儿第一次胎动。母亲的告别叮咛换来一句冷笑的承诺："我会永远忠于自己的心。"

- 「I hate this baby; he is not my brother. He is a bastard that never should have been created.」（Morgan，Chapter Sixteen）
- 「Oh, don't worry, Mother; I will always be true to my heart.」（Morgan，Chapter Sixteen）
- 「I have been told that I must stay here and you will go to Avalon without me. But you are not to be frightened, Morgan. Elaine will be with you and there will be soldiers to guard you on the road.」（Igraine，Chapter Sixteen）

## 节点十：不，与白墙（Chapter Eighteen）

蛇形项链勒住她的喉咙，替她回答了一切问题。她召女神见证"我不同意"，以跳窗相胁；Merlin 开价——孩子换 Carbonek。项链反咬 Uther，Vivian 被放逐，孩子出生即被送走：她违诺哺乳了一整夜，天亮时摇篮已空。登船，白墙，一个陌生的声音说"Welcome to Carbonek"。被偷走一切的女人，最后一次自己迈步——回到 Prologue 的那间屋子里，把这本书讲完。

- 「I do not consent to marry Uther Pendragon. I will not become his wife freely and I call down Her curse upon the match if you force me to go through with it.」（Igraine，Chapter Eighteen）
- 「I couldn't help it. I placed him to my breast and felt his tiny mouth latch on.」（Igraine，Chapter Eighteen）
- 「Reaching out, I took her hand and together we walked across the sand toward the beautiful white walls, and the promise of peace.」（Igraine，Chapter Eighteen）
