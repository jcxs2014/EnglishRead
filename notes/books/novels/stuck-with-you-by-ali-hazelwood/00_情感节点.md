# 《Stuck with You》情感节点

## 节点一：两次"世界终结"与最后一个可颂（ch01–ch02）

**叙事概括：** 全书的情感起点是双线并置的两次开端。周五夜 10:43，Sadie 被困进一部电梯，第二个"世界终结"随即到来——十三楼开门，进来的是 Erik Nowak，三周前与她有过一夜、此后再无音信的人；她一边被他的照顾击中，一边咬牙提醒自己他"本不该温柔"。而在三周前的那个早晨，她在 Faye 的咖啡店遭遇可颂断供，排在前面的陌生男人把最后一个可颂让给了她，又在她比手画脚道谢时不动声色地回了她一句——那时她还不知道他是谁。

**关键引语：**

> ① "My world comes to an end at 10:43 on a Friday night, when the elevator lurches to a stop between the eighth and seventh floor of the building that houses the engineering firm where I work."（ch01）

> ② "Why is he so kind? He was never supposed to be kind. After what happened between us, I decided to torture myself by asking around about him, and the word kind never came up. Not once."（ch01）

> ③ "He shrugs. His eyes are calm and serious. The span of his shoulders could easily moonlight as a plateau in Eurasia. “You didn’t ask a question.” His grammar is better than mine and I am withering inside."（ch02）

## 节点二：电梯在变小（ch03）

**叙事概括：** 幽闭恐惧发作，Sadie 的恐慌与心动同时失控。她一边把三周来的怨恨当宠物一样喂大，一边在脑内给 Erik 留了最大的房间；他递水、教她呼吸、点破她的迷信，而她只求安静，却停在了那一夜"从不想停下来"的记忆里。

**关键引语：**

> ① "My resentment for Erik Nowak, the feeling I've been cradling in my pocket like a little mouse for the past three weeks, the one I've been feeding bile and scraps, stirs awake. And honestly, it feels nice. Familiar. It reminds me that Erik doesn't really care whether I'm cold. I bet he has ulterior motives for asking. Maybe he wants to sell my organs. Or he's planning on establishing a pee corner on my rotting corpse."（ch03）

> ② "I'm wearing my usual work outfit of chinos and a nice blouse. Solid, neutral colors. Professional enough to be taken seriously; modest enough to convince the dudes I meet through work that my presence at any given meeting is to assess the efficacy of the biofiltration system design and not to provide them with "something cute to look at." Being a woman in engineering can be tons and tons of fun."（ch03）

> ③ "When I never wanted to stop talking, not even for a second."（ch03）

## 节点三：长椅上的那声"可爱"（ch04）

**叙事概括：** 口袋公园的长椅是全书松动得最彻底的一场戏：他们从洗钱理论聊到迷信，从幸运色内衣聊到彼此都是工程师，交换了名字与学位。她说到薰衣草色内衣时，他只回了一个字——没有索取的赞美，让她第一次无法用"男人都这样"来解释自己。章末的她，一整章都在心里喊"问我"。

**关键引语：**

> ① He nods slowly, like he's trying to picture it. "Cute," he says, and his tone is as simple and straightforward as it's been in the last few minutes. There is absolutely no creepy lasciviousness, as though he's complimenting a flower or a puppy. My heart skips a beat nonetheless.（ch04）

> ② Ask me, I think at him. Because I want to try that French bistro that you know. With you. And talk more about money laundering and a whole-systems approach to environmental engineering and purple underwear that is actually lavender.（ch04）

> ③ "I always thought of plasma coolant. Point is, she was here ten years ago, when I started working in that building, and she'll be here long after you and I are gone. Despite that." He points at the croissant I'm still clutching. Honestly, I should just bite the bullet and choke it down. My hand sweat is not going to make it any tastier. "There is no valid entrepreneurial reason for her to still be in business."（ch04）

## 节点四：紧急出口之前（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 电梯里的第一次正面冲突。Sadie 决定从紧急出口爬出去，Erik 说"不"，她质问他凭什么——他说他不会让她这么做。她宁愿冒险也不愿和他多待，而这句话终于逼出了他的真实恐惧：他怕的不是她受伤，是她宁可冒生命危险也要离开他。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Neither can you, because I'm not going to let you."（ch05）

> ② "Would you really rather do something that idiotic and reckless than be in here with me for a few more minutes?" he asks, turning back to me, voice icy and calm again.（ch05）

> ③ "You think I would be okay with you putting yourself in danger?" At baseline, Erik is not exactly a warm, convivial guy, but I had no idea he could sound like this. Deceptively calm, but furiously, icily livid. He leans forward as if to better glare at me, and his hand reaches up to close around the handrail, knuckles stretched white. I have a brief vision of him snapping it in two.（ch05）

## 节点五：这是约会吗（ch06）

**叙事概括：** 法国小酒馆之夜，两人聊 Oscar、聊足球、聊她被挖角的事。她发现自己重新开始在意"我好看吗"这个她以为早已戒掉的问题。离开时她先问出那句"这是约会吗"——Erik 答得谨慎而完整：如果不是，你愿意和我去约会吗？而她用一句"跳过程序"作答，这是全书唯一一次她主动跨出那一步。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Mostly, I'm wondering whether he likes what he sees, which is a bit different. I feel, sometimes, like I've lost the habit to wonder whether I'm pretty in favor of agonizing over other qualities. Do I look professional? Smart? Organized? Someone who should be taken seriously, whatever the hell that means? I generally find the idea of men commenting on my attractiveness, favorably or otherwise, repulsive. But tonight, right now . . . the possibility that Erik might find me beautiful uncurls warmly at the base of my stomach."（ch06）

> ② "Actually, if you don't mind, I'd rather skip Staten Island and just go to your place."（ch06）

> ③ "This is now my lucky-date outfit."（ch06）

## 节点六：一场错位的道歉（ch07）

**叙事概括：** 电梯里 Erik 终于开口请求对话，他为"那一夜"道歉——而他说的理由与 Sadie 愤怒的原因完全错位。他以为自己让她失望了，她却在气 pitch meeting 的背叛。两个人共用一段对话，却从头到尾不在同一个故事里，章末停在他那句茫然的确认上。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Because of the way I had sex with you. I was on you all night. Asked for too much. You didn't enjoy it." Suddenly, he looks as confused as I feel. Like we're both in the middle of a story that doesn't quite make narrative sense. "Sadie. Isn't that the reason?"（ch07）

> ② "I take full responsibility for what happened. It was my fault. I . . . I liked it, Sadie. A lot. So much so that I completely misread your signals and didn't realize that you didn't."（ch07）

> ③ "I think that's exactly it. I think that's why I hated Erik's I'm sorry, and why I hate that he hasn't looked at me in several minutes. It makes me wonder if he's aware that he ruined something that could have been great out of greed. And if that's the case, then I didn't imagine it: the night we spent together was as special as I remember, and he still threw it into the garbage chute—A New Hope Princess Leia style."（ch07）

## 节点七：不必成为任何样子（ch08）

**叙事概括：** 在他公寓里，从尴尬的沉默到第一个吻，再到 Sadie 的恐慌发作。他停下，道歉，说出全书最重的一句许可——她不必为任何人表演。两人最终相视而笑，她第一次允许自己想象将来；而这一夜正是背叛信息浮出水面前的最后一夜。

**关键引语：**

> ① "You don't have to be any way. I didn't bring you here so you could perform for me."（ch08）

> ② "I cannot help but wonder if this is the first day of the rest of my life."（ch08）

> ③ "Did I . . . Did I misunderstand?"（ch08）

## 节点八：真正的指控与合伙人（ch09–ch10）

**叙事概括：** 同一个电梯，话题却彻底换了题目。Sadie 先问出"你真的觉得性不好吗"，Erik 否认，转而讲出三周前的心动与当夜——他害怕搞砸，也害怕她从此消失；她用"我鄙视你""你是个骗子"回应，最后才点破真正的指控：你利用我从 GreenFrame 偷客户。下一章 Gianna 的声音把她从恍惚里叫醒：Erik 是 ProBld 的创始合伙人之一，四字一句让前九章的所有温柔被重新定价。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Did you really think the sex wasn't good?"（ch09）

> ② "Fine. Let's talk about how you used me to steal clients from GreenFrame."（ch09）

> ③ "Erik is one of the partners."（ch10）

## 节点九：五分钟之后就会爱上你（ch11）

**叙事概括：** 清算发生在同一部电梯里。Erik 交代了那篇文章与论文是怎么被团队看到的，说愿意承担全部责任；然后说出全书唯一一次正面告白——而这段告白是用"害怕"的语法说的：他怕伤害她，怕无法弥补，怕自己离爱上一个人只有五分钟。她点头说好，随即趁他转身溜走。

**关键引语：**

> ① "And I've been scared, scared like never before, that I'd hurt you." He lifts his hand. Curves it around my cheek. "That I'd left you in some—any kind of pain. That I couldn't make amends. Which, let me tell you, is no fun when you know in your lizard brain that you're about five minutes from falling in love with someone." He closes his eyes. "Maybe past. Can't really tell."（ch11）

> ② "The paper was on Google Scholar. For the thesis . . ." He presses his lips together. "I had a librarian from Caltech send me a download link."（ch11）

> ③ "I rack my brain, trying to remember whether during our dinner Erik ever mentioned taking acting classes. I want to say no, and let's be honest, it would seem a tiny bit out of character. And yet, if I didn't know what he did, I could almost buy it. I could almost believe, from the way he's blinking confusedly at me, that he has no idea what I'm talking about."（ch11）

## 节点十：你为什么要离开，与余生第一天的兑现（ch12–ch13）

**叙事概括：** 好友三人 Zoom 通话，Mara 与 Hannah 替全书做了一次外部归因：她遇到他、和他上床、以为他是混蛋、哭足三周，然后发现一切都是误会——那她为什么要走？Sadie 被这个问题击穿，Erik 随即出现在她敞开的门口，第一句话只有三个词。一个月后，她从一个吃可颂的人变成做可颂的人，成品灾难性地失败（她把盐和糖搞混了，可能还加了醋），Erik 委婉而诚实地给出判决，而她第三次说"我恨你"，他用全书的最后一句作答。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Well," Hannah says, "here's the deal. You meet this guy. And you boink him. And it's really good boinking—yay. The day after, you find out that he's a dick, which sends you on a three-week downward curlicue of tears and Talenti gelato that's about twelve times more intense than the time you broke up with a dude you'd been dating for years. But then you find out that it was all a misunderstanding, that things might be fixable, and . . . you leave? You said he wanted to talk more, and it's obvious that you're interested in hearing what he's saying. So why did you leave, Sadie?"（ch12）

> ② "I missed you," he breathes against my temple when he's reached a full seat, sounding like he's under great strain. "I only knew you for twenty-four hours, but I've never missed anyone so much."（ch12）

> ③ "Nah." When I open my eyes, Erik's smile is warm and soft. "I don't think you do, Sadie."（ch13）
