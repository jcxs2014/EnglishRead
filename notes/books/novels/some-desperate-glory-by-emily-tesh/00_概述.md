# 《Some Desperate Glory》概述

## 全书梗概

**先给一个正在反复死掉的女孩。** 地球战争之后，最后的人类据点 Gaea 站用一台训练模拟器 agoge 反复播放那一天：一颗反物质炸弹落在云层上，十四亿人死去。十九岁的 Kyr 已经在里面跑了几百遍，而这一遍她终于走到了离胜利四十秒的地方，被「智慧」的声音挡了回来：“You act in opposition to the Wisdom. Desist.”（ch01） 退出模拟器，指挥官 Jole 给了她一句「干得好」；而她的同班 Cleo 在门外骂她，然后消失了——没人知道她去了哪儿。

**这个站的全部内容，是被分配的人生。** Gaea 站不到两千人，靠着四艘无畏舰的残骸维持：
- 机库、通风、温室、育儿翼。每一个舱段对应一支翼，而每一支翼对应一种被留下来的理由。Kyr 所在的 Sparrow 七个人，全部用战争与神话命名，全部即将被分配——她自己的那一张写着 Nursery，因为产房需要她：“I would rather stand three times in the battle line than give birth to one child.”（ch02）
- 而她心里已经有一条不能出声的公式：“Do you think crying would save you from the majo?” she said. “I’m waiting.”（ch02）

**她唯一的出口是一个她看不起的人。** agoge 机房的技术员 Avicenna 用一台机器造出过一座白石城和一座悬崖花园；他让 Kyr 在里面找到了行踪不明的弟弟 Magnus。而 Avi 要她记住的第一句完整的话是：“Not everything has to have a point!” said Avi. “Are you sure you’re Magnus’s sister?”（ch05） 半小时之后，她在档案里看见弟弟的名字被写进了 Strike——一支 Gaea 否认存在的单位。

**Strike 是真的，而且是 Jole 的。** Avi 骗出了真相：
- “They sent him there to kill,” said Avi. “And to die.”（ch06）
- Jole 不但签发派工，还故意放人逃走——每过一段时间就让宇宙相信人类还在、还很愤怒：“No. But they let it happen, because every once in a while they want to remind the rest of the universe that we’re still here and we’re still angry, and every once in a while there’s a defector who’s not really a defector at all.”（ch06）
- 于是 Kyr 决定去追弟弟。她带走了被 Gaea 关着的 majo Yiso，在机库里撞见了 Cleo；两个人为了一把刀打了起来，而那一刀扎进了 Kyr 的大腿：She hesitated a fraction too long.（ch08）

**她在一颗有天空的星球上学会了另一套语法。** Chrysothemis 是人类通敌者聚居的世界：
- 海、帆船、白色的建筑、街上一个把黄色雨衣硬塞给陌生人的陌生人。而这里的智慧节点被一个不叫 Avicenna、却一模一样的少年接管了；那只 majo 站起来说：This place is mine. My home.（ch24）
- 她的手在十二个月前就已经按不住了——而对面那个人对她的评价只有一句：“Who the hell are you?” he said. “Where did you come from?” A beat. “How the hell are you breathing?”（ch19）

**这一切是为了让她看清他。** 智慧把她送回了故事开始的那一天，让她当着一屋子人的面看着 Jole 宣布：Their time is over. Our time has come.（ch25） 而她在那之后的每一天里，唯一没有做的事就是承认：她一生被训练出来保护的那个东西，是这个人的作品。

**最后是一场十二分钟的赛跑。** 他把 Jole 和那只 majo 一起关进了站心——那里有三台满载运转的无屏蔽影引擎。她跳下去，把 Yiso 接住，把 Jole 踢进了引擎：
- She saw Jole’s body slam into the maw of Ferox engine, a man-shape outlined in green subreal light for a fraction of a second. And then the shadow engine let out its next pulse of dimensional distortion and the shape became a dark blur, a white line, and then nothing at all, as her uncle Jole died smeared across fifteen dimensions.（ch32）
- 隧道塌了，无畏舰走了，虚空里剩下两个人和一块不存在的东西：I think maybe purpose is overrated.（ch32）

## 三个主题

**一、训练不能制造意义，只能制造服从。** 这句话由那个设计训练的人说出：
- Jole sends the best of the best through a Doomsday scenario he’s set up to be impossible. That always annoyed me. What’s it for, except to break you down?（ch14）
- 而它的另一半由被训练的人说出——她试过造一间 agoge 没准备过的东西：How strange, that the agoge didn’t prepare you for being on your own, when Earth’s children needed to be prepared for anything.（ch13）
- 全书对 Gaea 最锋利的一次审问，是它自己的教员问的：We didn’t lose because the majo cheated. We lost because we didn’t cheat fast enough.（ch14）

**二、无畏是一种可以拆掉的建筑。** 她一生靠「我比所有人都强」活着，而这一件东西在一天之内失效：
- After all that. Somehow she could not move.（ch29）
- 她真正学会的是另一件事——她不怕了，所以她能看见别人：Not Russell, and not his picked men either.（ch31）

**三、忠诚是谁欠谁的。** 她一辈子被告知她欠人类一个后代，而她从来没问过这句话是谁说的：
- I am not the first person who wanted to fight and got betrayed into Nursery.（ch30）
- 本书的答案是：那条链子的每一环都被同一个人捏着：She did not think it was present company excepted.（ch27）

## 人物弧光

**Kyr（Valkyr / Val Marston）**——

- 起点：She had two charges of her jump hook left, but using it would set off the majo ship’s alarms. Her mask was fractured after the last melee skirmish and held together only by rep gel and hope. If it cracked again, here above the clouds where the battle raged in Earth’s outer atmosphere, she would asphyxiate.（ch01）
- 转向：All it would cost her was her war. All it would cost was the memory of the dead, and the service she’d been born for, and the knowledge that out in deep space, clinging to a cold rock orbiting an unfriendly star, the last soldiers of humanity were still refusing to surrender, and Kyr was not among them.（ch12）
- 最低点：Hard on its heels came self-disgust: weak, weak, weak.（ch13）
- 落点：I think maybe purpose is overrated.（ch32）
- 她真正的转折不是任何一次胜利，而是开始把「服务人类」从一句话读成一份家谱。

**Avicenna（Avi）**——

- 起点：“I wonder who,” Avi said. “I don’t have many friends built like tanks. Let me guess, you must be Vallie.”（ch05）
- 转向：“To hell with that,” Avi said. “I’m not planning to make a point or sacrifice myself or whatever it is you saints of the cause think is worth dying for. I’m here to win the war.”（ch14）
- 最低点：“Justice,” Avi said. “It’s pretty simple, really. You killed my world. I’m killing yours. All of them. Or almost.”（ch18）
- 落点：Gaea Station had made them both what they were. But Kyr was determined to be different.（ch31）
- 他一生做同一件事（造东西），只是造的东西从城市和花园，一路变成了一整套判断。

**Aulus Jole**——

- 起点：“Well done, Valkyr,” said Uncle Jole.（ch01）
- 转向：Because he had a wing of his own.（ch06）
- 最低点：“No one expects perfection from you, Valkyr,” said Jole. “It’s enough that you serve.”（ch25）
- 落点：And here he was. But he didn’t matter.（ch32）
- 他从头到尾只有一句话，而那句话的分量在这三十二章里不断更换持有者。
