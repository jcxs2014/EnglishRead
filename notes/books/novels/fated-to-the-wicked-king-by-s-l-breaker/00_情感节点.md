# 《Fated to the Wicked King》情感节点

## 节点一：停摆的钟（ch01）

荒漠钟塔里，机械师 Allie 修得好一切，唯独那面大钟停了，原因不明；猫带来 Duchess 的口信，泪河隔断了来路。同一段独白里她第一次完整想起换心的旧事：他救了她的心，她也救了他的。
> "Although amidst the creaks and groans filling the ancient structure, the once never-ending, rhythmic clinking of massive metal gears and mechanical workings of the tower’s clock face, which used to be a constant, was no longer."（ch01）
> "When we’d first met, he was still a mere assassin, harvesting human hearts for the Red Queen. But when he was ordered to extract my heart and kill me, he’d found a way to resist her control."（ch01）

## 节点二：掷靴救驾（ch03）

选妻大典上刺客掷刀、卫兵恰好被引开；她脱下靴子破窗相救，翻墙逃进夜色。全书在此交底：她救他，因为爱过。
> "I slipped one off without looking down, fingers moving with precise calm even as my heart tried to tear free of my ribs. I weighed it in my hand. Leather. Heels. Enough mass."（ch03）
> "And somewhere in the Great Hall, a king who should have died tonight stood very much alive—because I had loved him once."（ch03）

## 节点三：镣铐与三条件（ch05–ch06）

他被靴子的主人认出，召她入宫；她戴着镣铐走上大殿，眼看他下令 "Kill them all."，索性不再求情，开出三条件换一条过泪河的路。
> "Those steel-blue eyes slid to meet mine again. He lifted his hand, then gave a slight wave. “Kill them all.”"（ch05）
> "“Second, in exchange for my—my help—” I faltered since he had emphasized that these were not ‘requests.’ I steeled myself and went on. “You must take me across the River of Tears to search for the Hatter.”"（ch06）

## 节点四：棋局与失控（ch07–ch09）

她被按到棋盘前，一盘慢棋烧到两人都坐不住；她逃进走廊，天亮照旧去"评估"囚犯。她问能否调用沉睡的魔法，得到的回答把两人的关系钉在"有用"上。
> "My entire body thrummed with a restless, electric heat, every nerve alive and sparking with a fierce, aching hunger that coiled low and deep, a pulse of want so consuming it felt as though it might burn me from the inside out."（ch07）
> "“The only reason you have any use at all is that you wear my mystical heart.”"（ch09）

## 节点五：影子处决与名字（ch10）

她在西翼撞见国王用影子撕碎求饶的囚犯；逃出城堡的路上被罩头掳走。同一天清晨，她握着发夹喊出他的名字——"Rabb did remember"。
> "Rabb did remember. He remembered his life before he was crowned king. He remembered having been with me. He remembered us."（ch10）
> "The man barely had time to suck in a breath before the entire room flooded with darkness for the blink of an eye. The Wicked King’s shadows, like swift whispering blades, ripped through the man, followed by dull thumps as hunks of flesh fell to the floor."（ch10）

## 节点六：过河与城前血债（ch12–ch13）

泪河的异常锁定了一台过河机器；她请缨同赴 Lucerne。城门前 Gregory 冷面相迎，影子攥着全城军队逼开城门，而国王用一句平淡的旧事回答血债——她把门摔在他脸上。
> "He shot me a glare. “If you think I’m risking going through that abomination separately, you are mad.”"（ch12）
> "The king’s response was flat. “I killed his sister. You remember. You were there.”"（ch13）

## 节点七：茶会上的真相（ch20–ch21）

Duchess 的乡间别墅先收留了决斗未遂的众人，又在茶会上抖出真相：解咒的办法早就有——国王瞒了七个月，还抹掉了知情人的记忆。她的判词只有一句。
> "The king had known about this solution for seven whole months. He hadn’t told me. Not only that, he had lied to me when I’d explicitly asked him about it. He had even erased Chez’s and the Duchess’s memories so I would never find out."（ch21）
> "The Wicked King was content to keep me imprisoned, to keep me cursed. He’d said it over and over. I belonged to him. He owned me."（ch21）

## 节点八：玉戒与一吻（ch22）

深夜花园里他交出全部答案：一对阻隔感受的法器解释了她的读心、那阵剧痛、和他所有的冷。戒指摘下的瞬间，积压数月的感情决堤。
> "“They are enchanted as a type of magical impediment. Mine hides my feelings from you. Yours from me.”"（ch22）
> "With a desperate groan, his hand cupped the back of my head, fingers digging into my hair, as he took my mouth—owned it. His tongue pushed between my lips to deepen our kiss right away, and ecstasy exploded inside me."（ch22）

## 节点九：旧我点名与兔笼（ch24–ch25）

自称旧上司的 General 当众喊出 "Dr. Carter"，她最怕的旧我应声归位；Hatter 与敌方结盟，把护她的国王变成一只白兔。她抱起兔子、推走爆药箱，跳进透着红光的树心。
> "“She works for me,” he supplied, his eyes bright. “Rather, she used to work for me.” He gave me a wink. “Dr. Carter is a world-renowned scientist where we come from.”"（ch24）
> "With the roar of molten earth rising ever louder behind me, and the rabbit clutched against my shoulder, I leaped into the hole hidden within the heart of the tree."（ch25）

## 节点十：my queen 与纵身一跃（ch26–ch27）

咒碎人归，雨落钟楼；Eleanor 的罪被两人各自认领，换过的心换了回来。终章她装上最后一枚零件、把机器设成过载，独自走进光球替两个世界还债——国王攥着护身符，追着她跳进了尾流。
> "“Yes, my queen.” Bracing against one elbow, he sank beside me. He swept his hand down to slip between my thighs, easily finding my need. His fingers caressed my core before sliding inside."（ch26）
> "“I love you, Rabb, but I can’t be selfish again. I’ve caused so much suffering across so many other worlds. I can’t let this world be one of them. I must save Wünder. I have to do this.”"（ch27）
> "The king’s grip tightened around the talisman in his hand before he dove headfirst into the fading wake of the maelstrom."（ch27）
