# 30. Breakfast of Shame

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Naomi 和 Knox 去 Liza 家吃早餐，Naomi 担心被看出刚过夜，晚上 Knox 陪她和 Waylay 看 TV。
- **情感弧线位置**：aftermath — 从尴尬到温馨
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：一夜情 → 家庭早餐 → 夜晚陪伴
- **人物弧线**：Naomi 从尴尬到放松，Knox 展现温柔和陪伴
- **叙事手法**：Naomi 视角 — 从早晨的焦虑到夜晚的温暖

## 精读

① **"You don't have to come along, you know," I pointed out. "You didn't get much sleep in the last forty-eight hours." "Neither did you," Knox said, making a show of locking up the cabin before we left. I knew he was making a point.**

> **原句 1:** "You don't have to come along, you know," I pointed out. "You didn't get much sleep in the last forty-eight hours." "Neither did you," Knox said, making a show of locking up the cabin before we left.

**中文理解**：Naomi 说 Knox 不必跟着，他没睡多少，Knox 说他也是。
**关键字**：don't have to come along（不必跟着）；forty-eight hours（四十八小时）；making a show of（故意展示）
**为什么这样写**：用对话展现两人的关心和 Knox 的坚持。
**读者视角提示**：从尴尬到温暖的转折。

② **We'd slept together. As in fell asleep in the same bed without having sex together. Not only that, but I'd woken up with Knox "Viking" Morgan spooning me. I didn't know much about no strings. Hell, I had so many strings attached to so many things, I'd been tied up in knots for most of my adult life. But even I knew that sharing a bed and cuddling was way too intimate for what we'd both agreed to.**

> **原句 2:** "We'd slept together. As in fell asleep in the same bed without having sex together. Not only that, but I'd woken up with Knox 'Viking' Morgan spooning me."

**中文理解**：Naomi 反思他们共眠不性，Knox 从背后 spoon 她，这太亲密了。
**关键字**：slept together（共眠）；spooning（从背后抱）；no strings（无关系）；tied up in knots（纠结）
**为什么这样写**：用"spooning" 和"no strings" 展现 Naomi 的焦虑和关系的复杂。
**读者视角提示**：从生理亲密到情感纠结。

③ **"Men got tired and didn't want to walk women home or let women walk home alone only to be eaten by wildlife. The man had gone through a traumatic twenty-four hours. He probably wasn't making the most rational decisions, I decided. Maybe Knox was just a restless sleeper. Maybe he spooned his dog in bed every night."**

> **原句 3:** "Men got tired and didn't want to walk women home or let women walk home alone only to be eaten by wildlife. The man had gone through a traumatic twenty-four hours. He probably wasn't making the most rational decisions, I decided."

**中文理解**：Naomi 为 Knox 的行为找借口，说他只是累了。
**关键字**：walk women home（送女人回家）；eaten by wildlife（被野生动物吃）；traumatic（创伤的）；rational decisions（理性决定）
**为什么这样写**：展现 Naomi 的防御机制和自我欺骗。
**读者视角提示**：从焦虑到自我安慰。

④ **"Of course, that didn't explain why he'd volunteered to run next door and grab a bunch of my stuff while I showered. Why he'd put actual thought into an outfit for me. I glanced down at the high-waisted green and white shorts, the cute lacy top. He'd even grabbed underwear for me. Sure, it was a thong and didn't match my bra. But still."**

> **原句 4:** "Of course, that didn't explain why he'd volunteered to run next door and grab a bunch of my stuff while I showered. Why he'd put actual thought into an outfit for me."

**中文理解**：Naomi 承认 Knox 为她拿衣服、搭配 outfit，甚至拿了内衣。
**关键字**：volunteered（主动）；run next door（跑隔壁）；actual thought（实际想法）；outfit（服装）
**为什么这样写**：用具体细节展现 Knox 的温柔和细心。
**读者视角提示**：从自我安慰到被温柔打动。

⑤ **"'Bout done thinking everything to death?" I shook myself from my reverie to find Knox shooting me one of those almost smiles. "I was just running through my to do list," I fibbed haughtily.**

> **原句 5:** "'Bout done thinking everything to death?" I shook myself from my reverie to find Knox shooting me one of those almost smiles. "I was just running through my to do list," I fibbed haughtily.

**中文理解**：Knox 问她是否想完了，Naomi 从沉思中醒来，撒谎说在想待办事项。
**关键字**：thinking everything to death（想死）；reverie（沉思）；almost smiles（几乎微笑）；fibbed haughtily（高傲地撒谎）
**为什么这样写**：用幽默展现 Naomi 的防御和 Knox 的了解。
**读者视角提示**：从沉思到被拉回现实。

⑥ **"Does Knox know you have his dog?" I asked. "Dunno." Waylay shrugged. "So are you and Knox a thing?" I stumbled over my own feet. Stef snickered unsympathetically next to me.**

> **原句 6:** "Does Knox know you have his dog?" I asked. "Dunno." Waylay shrugged. "So are you and Knox a thing?" I stumbled over my own feet.

**中文理解**：Waylay 问 Naomi 和 Knox 是否是一对，Naomi 被自己的脚绊倒。
**关键字**：have his dog（有他的狗）；a thing（一对）；stumbled over my own feet（被自己的脚绊倒）；snickered（窃笑）
**为什么这样写**：用 Waylay 的直接问题和 Naomi 的尴尬展现关系的模糊。
**读者视角提示**：从尴尬到幽默。

⑦ **"Honestly, Way. I have no idea. I don't know what we are or what I want from him or what he wants from me. So we probably won't be a thing forever. But we might be spending more time with him for a while. If it's okay with you."**

> **原句 7:** "Honestly, Way. I have no idea. I don't know what we are or what I want from him or what he wants from me. So we probably won't be a thing forever. But we might be spending more time with him for a while. If it's okay with you."

**中文理解**：Naomi 坦诚她不知道关系是什么，但可能多花时间在一起，如果 Waylay 同意。
**关键字**：no idea（不知道）；a thing（一对）；spending more time（多花时间）；okay with you（你同意）
**为什么这样写**：展现 Naomi 的坦诚和对 Waylay 的尊重。
**读者视角提示**：从模糊到坦诚。

⑧ **"He makes you smile." "What?" "Knox. He makes you smile. A lot. And he looks at you like he likes you a lot." I felt my cheeks flush. "Oh, yeah?" She nodded. "Yeah. It's cool."**

> **原句 8:** "He makes you smile." "What?" "Knox. He makes you smile. A lot. And he looks at you like he likes you a lot." I felt my cheeks flush. "Oh, yeah?"

**中文理解**：Waylay 说 Knox 让 Naomi 笑，Naomi 脸红了。
**关键字**：makes you smile（让你笑）；cheeks flush（脸红）；likes you a lot（很喜欢你）
**为什么这样写**：用 Waylay 的观察展现 Knox 对 Naomi 的影响。
**读者视角提示**：从尴尬到温暖的确认。
