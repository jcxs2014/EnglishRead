# 51. Author's Note to the Reader

## 概览

- **出处**：Lucy Score
- **定位**：全书后记，作者向读者解释写作背景
- **主题**：爱、勇气、失去

## 精读

① **I finished this book at 11:03 p.m. on November 4, 2021, and promptly burst into tears. I started it five months earlier. Just a few days before David, Claire Kingsley's beloved husband and my sweet friend, passed away suddenly. Not only was I leveled by his death, but he was the third friend's husband to leave this world far too soon in two months.**

> **原句 1:** "I finished this book at 11:03 p.m. on November 4, 2021, and promptly burst into tears. I started it five months earlier. Just a few days before David, Claire Kingsley's beloved husband and my sweet friend, passed away suddenly. Not only was I leveled by his death, but he was the third friend's husband to leave this world far too soon in two months."

**中文理解**：Lucy Score 说她于2021年11月4日晚上11:03完成了这本书，然后立刻泪流满面。她五个月前开始写的。就在 David——Claire Kingsley 的丈夫和她的好朋友——突然去世前几天。她不仅被他的击垮了，而且他是两个月内第三位过早离开这个世界的朋友的丈夫。
**关键字**：finished this book at 11:03 p.m. on November 4, 2021（于2021年11月4日晚上11:03完成了这本书）；promptly burst into tears（立刻泪流满面）；five months earlier（五个月前）；David, Claire Kingsley's beloved husband and my sweet friend（David，Claire Kingsley 的丈夫和我的好朋友）；passed away suddenly（突然去世）；leveled by his death（被他的死亡击垮）；the third friend's husband to leave this world far too soon in two months（两个月内第三位过早离开这个世界的朋友的丈夫）
**为什么这样写**：用具体的时间和情感展现作者的真诚和脆弱。
**读者视角提示**：了解作者的写作背景和情感。

② **I didn't know what this book was about when I started it. Even when I was halfway through it, I still wasn't sure. But now that I've finished it, I finally get it. This book is about the bravery it takes for us to love someone when we all know how every love story ends. It's about choosing love over fear again and again. It's about showing up and being brave even when we know it's going to hurt like hell.**

> **原句 2:** "I didn't know what this book was about when I started it. Even when I was halfway through it, I still wasn't sure. But now that I've finished it, I finally get it. This book is about the bravery it takes for us to love someone when we all know how every love story ends. It's about choosing love over fear again and again. It's about showing up and being brave even when we know it's going to hurt like hell."

**中文理解**：Lucy Score 说她开始写时不知道这本书是关于什么。甚至写了一半时，她仍然不确定。但现在她完成了，她终于明白了。这本书是关于当我们都知道每个爱情故事如何结局时，爱一个人需要多大的勇气。这是关于一次又一次选择爱而不是恐惧。这是关于出现并勇敢，即使我们知道它会像地狱一样痛苦。
**关键字**：I didn't know what this book was about when I started it（我开始写时不知道这本书是关于什么）；halfway through it（写了一半）；I finally get it（我终于明白了）；the bravery it takes for us to love someone when we all know how every love story ends（当我们都知道每个爱情故事如何结局时，爱一个人需要多大的勇气）；choosing love over fear again and again（一次又一次选择爱而不是恐惧）；showing up and being brave even when we know it's going to hurt like hell（出现并勇敢，即使我们知道它会像地狱一样痛苦）
**为什么这样写**：用直接的语言展现作者对爱和勇气的理解。
**读者视角提示**：理解本书的核心主题。

③ **Knox's fear of losing someone he loved and falling apart was so real for me. I ended up working through a lot of my own grief and fear while I wrote this story. At times I couldn't get past the loss my friends were suffering. Other times I had the presence of mind to remember how lucky we all are to love someone so much their loss is devastating.**

> **原句 3:** "Knox's fear of losing someone he loved and falling apart was so real for me. I ended up working through a lot of my own grief and fear while I wrote this story. At times I couldn't get past the loss my friends were suffering. Other times I had the presence of mind to remember how lucky we all are to love someone so much their loss is devastating."

**中文理解**：Knox 对失去所爱的人并崩溃的恐惧对她来说非常真实。她在写这个故事的过程中，最终处理了很多自己的悲伤和恐惧。有时她无法克服朋友们正在遭受的损失。其他时候她有头脑记住我们是多么幸运，能如此爱一个人，以至于他们的失去是毁灭性的。
**关键字**：Knox's fear of losing someone he loved and falling apart was so real for me（Knox 对失去所爱的人并崩溃的恐惧对我来说非常真实）；working through a lot of my own grief and fear while I wrote this story（在写这个故事的过程中，最终处理了很多自己的悲伤和恐惧）；At times I couldn't get past the loss my friends were suffering（有时我无法克服朋友们正在遭受的损失）；Other times I had the presence of mind to remember how lucky we all are to love someone so much their loss is devastating（其他时候我有头脑记住我们是多么幸运，能如此爱一个人，以至于他们的失去是毁灭性的）
**为什么这样写**：用直接的语言展现作者将个人情感融入角色。
**读者视角提示**：理解 Knox 的角色深度。

④ **My wish for all of us is that we love whole-heartedly and are present enough in our relationships that when we part our only regret is quantity not quality. That we all understand that the pain of loss is what gives the rest of our life color and flavor and texture.**

> **原句 4:** "My wish for all of us is that we love whole-heartedly and are present enough in our relationships that when we part our only regret is quantity not quality. That we all understand that the pain of loss is what gives the rest of our life color and flavor and texture."

**中文理解**：Lucy Score 说她对我们所有人的祝愿是，我们全心全意地爱，在我们的关系中足够在场，以至于当我们分离时，我们唯一的遗憾是数量而不是质量。我们都能理解，失去的痛苦正是赋予我们余生色彩、风味和质感的东西。
**关键字**：My wish for all of us is that we love whole-heartedly（我对我们所有人的祝愿是，我们全心全意地爱）；are present enough in our relationships that when we part our only regret is quantity not quality（在我们的关系中足够在场，以至于当我们分离时，我们唯一的遗憾是数量而不是质量）；the pain of loss is what gives the rest of our life color and flavor and texture（失去的痛苦正是赋予我们余生色彩、风味和质感的东西）
**为什么这样写**：用诗意的语言展现作者对爱和失去的深刻理解。
**读者视角提示**：理解本书的哲学深度。
