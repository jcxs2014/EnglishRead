# 27. Field Mice Revenge

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Knox 到酒吧发现 Naomi 不在，得知她去学校；他赶到学校，看到 Naomi 对峙老师，Waylay 用老鼠报复老师。
- **情感弧线位置**：冲突升级 — 从酒吧轻松到学校紧张
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：英雄救美 → 英雄到场时美已自救 → 英雄收尾
- **人物弧线**：Knox 从轻松到严肃，展现对 Naomi 和 Waylay 的保护
- **叙事手法**：Knox 视角 — 从酒吧到学校的追踪

## 精读

① **I walked into Honky Tonk through the kitchen, twirling my keys around my finger and whistling. "Someone's in a good mood," Milford, the line cook, observed. I wondered exactly how big of a dick I usually was that made my good mood breaking news, then decided I didn't really give a shit.**

> **原句 1:** "I walked into Honky Tonk through the kitchen, twirling my keys around my finger and whistling. Someone's in a good mood."

**中文理解**：Knox 吹着口哨走进酒吧，厨师注意到他心情好。
**关键字**：twirling my keys（转钥匙）；whistling（吹口哨）；good mood（好心情）
**为什么这样写**：用轻松的动作展现 Knox 的满足感，与平时形成对比。
**读者视角提示**：从 Knox 的轻松到发现 Naomi 不在的转折。

② **"Where's Naomi?" I asked. "Don't you take that tone with me today, Knoxy. I will break your face." I had taken no tone with her, but I knew better than to point that out.**

> **原句 2:** "Where's Naomi?" I asked. "Don't you take that tone with me today, Knoxy. I will break your face."

**中文理解**：Knox 问 Naomi 在哪，Fi 警告他不要那种语气。
**关键字**：tone（语气）；break your face（打烂你的脸）；Knoxy（Knox 的昵称）
**为什么这样写**：用 Fi 的暴躁展现 PMS 夜的紧张气氛。
**读者视角提示**：从轻松到紧张的转折。

③ **"She went to take care of it and asked us to cover for her." "She's bringing us pretzels afterward," Max said around the brownie she held between her teeth as she shuffled by with two fresh beers.**

> **原句 3:** "She went to take care of it and asked us to cover for her."

**中文理解**：Naomi 去处理 Waylay 的事，让同事帮忙顶班。
**关键字**：take care of it（处理）；cover for me（帮我顶班）；pretzels（椒盐卷饼）
**为什么这样写**：用简短句展现 Naomi 的行动力。
**读者视角提示**：从酒吧到学校的追踪开始。

④ **"I'm here because Naomi was walking into a situation she didn't know anything about. Felch has had a hard-on for Tina ever since her husband banged Tina this summer. The whole town was talking about it. Now, once again, Naomi's cleaning up a mess that Tina left behind. Something I get the feeling she's spent her whole life doing. So maybe you can cut her some slack or, better yet, help her out with the clean-up this time around."**

> **原句 4:** "I'm here because Naomi was walking into a situation she didn't know anything about. Felch has had a hard-on for Tina ever since her husband banged Tina this summer."

**中文理解**：Knox 向 Lou 解释 Naomi 在清理 Tina 留下的烂摊子，希望他帮忙而非指责。
**关键字**：hard-on for（对...有执念）；banged（上床）；cleaning up a mess（清理烂摊子）；cut her some slack（放她一马）
**为什么这样写**：展现 Knox 对 Naomi 的理解和保护，以及他对 Lou 的直言。
**读者视角提示**：从冲突到理解的转变。

⑤ **"I didn't do anything wrong. I showed up to school on the first day, and she was mean to me. Like really mean. She yelled at me in front of everyone in the cafeteria when I spilled my chocolate milk. She took recess time away from everyone and said it was my fault for not respecting what belonged to other people. Then, when she was handing out papers about some dumb bake sale to take home to our parents, she said I didn't need one since my mom was too busy in the bedroom to find the kitchen."**

> **原句 5:** "I showed up to school on the first day, and she was mean to me. Like really mean."

**中文理解**：Waylay 解释老师如何虐待她，包括当众羞辱和讽刺她的母亲。
**关键字**：mean to me（对我刻薄）；yelled at me（对我吼叫）；recess time（课间休息）；bake sale（烘焙义卖）
**为什么这样写**：用具体细节展现老师的恶毒，为 Naomi 的愤怒提供正当性。
**读者视角提示**：从 Waylay 的视角理解她的报复行为。

⑥ **"Look, kid. I think we all get that you're not used to havin' an adult around who has your back. But you need to get used to it. Naomi's not goin' anywhere. You've got your grandparents too. And you've got me and Liza J and Nash. But you scared the shit out of all of us running off like that."**

> **原句 6:** "Look, kid. I think we all get that you're not used to havin' an adult around who has your back. But you need to get used to it."

**中文理解**：Knox 告诉 Waylay 她现在有大人支持，不该独自行动。
**关键字**：has your back（支持你）；not goin' anywhere（不会离开）；scared the shit out of（吓坏了）
**为什么这样写**：展现 Knox 对 Waylay 的关心和承诺。
**读者视角提示**：从 Knox 的视角展现他对这个家庭的投入。

⑦ **"I was also gonna give her computer a virus. One of those annoying ones that adds extra letters and numbers when you're typing," she said, her cheeks pink with indignation. I hid my grin by biting the inside of my cheek. "Okay. That's pretty good," I admitted.**

> **原句 7:** "I was also gonna give her computer a virus. One of those annoying ones that adds extra letters and numbers when you're typing."

**中文理解**：Waylay 说她还想给老师的电脑装病毒，Knox 忍不住笑了。
**关键字**：computer virus（电脑病毒）；extra letters and numbers（多余的字母和数字）；indignation（愤怒）
**为什么这样写**：用幽默的方式展现 Waylay 的可爱和 Knox 的包容。
**读者视角提示**：从紧张到轻松的转折。

⑧ **"Mrs. Felch is in way more trouble than you are, Waylay. But Knox is right. You have to come to me with this stuff. Don't tell me everything's fine when it's not fine. I'm here to help. You can't be sneaking out and exacting revenge on everyone who wrongs you. Especially not with innocent little field mice."**

> **原句 8:** "Mrs. Felch is in way more trouble than you are, Waylay. But Knox is right. You have to come to me with this stuff."

**中文理解**：Naomi 告诉 Waylay 她应该信任大人，而不是独自报复。
**关键字**：in way more trouble（麻烦大得多）；come to me with this stuff（告诉我这些）；exacting revenge（报复）；innocent little field mice（无辜的小田鼠）
**为什么这样写**：展现 Naomi 作为监护人的责任感和对 Waylay 的关爱。
**读者视角提示**：从冲突到和解的温暖时刻。
