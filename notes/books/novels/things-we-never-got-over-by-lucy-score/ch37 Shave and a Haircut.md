# 37. Shave and a Haircut

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Knox 带 Naomi 和 Waylay 去无家可归者收容所志愿服务，Knox 和 Jeremiah 为收容所居民理发。
- **情感弧线位置**：温馨 — 从日常到给予
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：日常 → 志愿服务 → 给予的喜悦
- **人物弧线**：Naomi 从紧张到感动，Knox 展现温柔和慷慨
- **叙事手法**：Naomi 视角 — 从出发到感动

## 精读

① **"Where are we going?" I asked Knox as we left Knockemout in the rearview mirror. "Are we going shopping?" Waylay asked hopefully from the backseat. She'd taken the news that we were temporarily moving into Liza J's well. Of course, I'd flat-out lied to her, telling her there was a bug problem at the cottage and that we'd be staying with everyone at Liza's for a few days.**

> **原句 1:** "Where are we going?" I asked Knox as we left Knockemout in the rearview mirror. "Are we going shopping?" Waylay asked hopefully from the backseat. She'd taken the news that we were temporarily moving into Liza J's well. Of course, I'd flat-out lied to her, telling her there was a bug problem at the cottage and that we'd be staying with everyone at Liza's for a few days.

**中文理解**：Naomi 问去哪，Waylay 希望是购物，她接受了暂时搬去 Liza 家，Naomi 撒谎说有虫害。
**关键字**：rearview mirror（后视镜）；hopefully（希望地）；temporarily moving into（暂时搬进）；flat-out lied（直截了当地撒谎）；bug problem（虫害）
**为什么这样写**：用对话展现 Waylay 的期待和 Naomi 的保护性谎言。
**读者视角提示**：从日常到秘密的揭示。

② **My parents, on the other hand, were struggling. Not with having us all under one roof. That part had them in near ecstasy. But Knox had insisted I spill the truth. The whole truth, beginning with why I'd run out on Warner.**

> **原句 2:** "My parents, on the other hand, were struggling. Not with having us all under one roof. That part had them in near ecstasy. But Knox had insisted I spill the truth. The whole truth, beginning with why I'd run out on Warner."

**中文理解**：父母在挣扎，不是为住在一起，而是 Knox 坚持要我说出真相，关于为什么逃婚。
**关键字**：struggling（挣扎）；under one roof（同住）；near ecstasy（近乎狂喜）；spill the truth（说出真相）；run out on Warner（逃离 Warner）
**为什么这样写**：展现父母的复杂情绪和 Knox 的坚持。
**读者视角提示**：从日常到真相的揭示。

Dad calmed down considerably after Lucian assured him that Knox had not only mopped the floor with Warner, he'd also broken the man's nose.**

> **原句 3:** "While my mother wrote a strongly worded message to Warner's mother on Facebook at four a.m., Knox had to physically restrain my father from leaving to go after Warner. Dad calmed down considerably after Lucian assured him that Knox had not only mopped the floor with Warner, he'd also broken the man's nose."

**中文理解**：母亲在 Facebook 上给 Warner 母亲写警告，Knox 阻止父亲去找 Warner，父亲在 Lucian 说 Knox 打了 Warner 后平静下来。
**关键字**：strongly worded message（措辞强烈的消息）；physically restrain（身体阻止）；go after Warner（去找 Warner）；mopped the floor with（打败）；broken the man's nose（打断了鼻子）
**为什么这样写**：用具体细节展现父母的反应和 Knox 的暴力。
**读者视角提示**：从愤怒到平静。

④ **Over Mom's anxiety pancakes, we'd talked until nearly five a.m. before I'd fallen into bed with Knox in his childhood bedroom. I was certain I'd never be able to sleep, but with his heavy arm anchoring me to his side, I'd fallen into a dreamless oblivion and stayed there until ten.**

> **原句 4:** "Over Mom's anxiety pancakes, we'd talked until nearly five a.m. before I'd fallen into bed with Knox in his childhood bedroom. I was certain I'd never be able to sleep, but with his heavy arm anchoring me to his side, I'd fallen into a dreamless oblivion and stayed there until ten."

**中文理解**：在母亲的焦虑煎饼旁，我们谈到凌晨五点，然后睡在 Knox 的童年卧室，他的手臂让我安睡。
**关键字**：anxiety pancakes（焦虑煎饼）；fallen into bed（上床）；childhood bedroom（童年卧室）；heavy arm anchoring me（沉重的手臂固定我）；dreamless oblivion（无梦的遗忘）
**为什么这样写**：用细节展现两人的亲密和 Knox 的守护。
**读者视角提示**：从紧张到安睡。

⑤ **I realized just how blurry those lines in my heart were getting. I was in trouble. And it had nothing to do with a break-in or a criminal sister or an ex-fiancé. I was falling for the man I'd sworn I wouldn't. But Knox made it impossible not to. He made it inevitable.**

> **原句 5:** "I realized just how blurry those lines in my heart were getting. I was in trouble. And it had nothing to do with a break-in or a criminal sister or an ex-fiancé. I was falling for the man I'd sworn I wouldn't. But Knox made it impossible not to. He made it inevitable."

**中文理解**：Naomi 意识到心中的界限变得模糊，她陷入了麻烦，与闯入或罪犯无关，她爱上了她发誓不会爱的人。
**关键字**：blurry（模糊）；in trouble（陷入麻烦）；break-in（闯入）；criminal sister（罪犯姐姐）；falling for（爱上）；sworn I wouldn't（发誓不会）；inevitable（不可避免）
**为什么这样写**：用内心独白展现 Naomi 的感情转变。
**读者视角提示**：从理性到情感的转变。

⑥ **"We're not going shopping," Knox told Waylay as he took the on-ramp for the highway. "What's all the stuff in the back for?" Waylay asked. Between freaking out over what our caseworker thought of me allowing multiple break-ins to happen, I was curious too. Before he'd closed the cover over the truck bed, we'd spotted more than a dozen shopping bags. "Supplies," he said mysteriously.**

> **原句 6:** "We're not going shopping," Knox told Waylay as he took the on-ramp for the highway. "What's all the stuff in the back for?" Waylay asked. Between freaking out over what our caseworker thought of me allowing multiple break-ins to happen, I was curious too. Before he'd closed the cover over the truck bed, we'd spotted more than a dozen shopping bags. "Supplies," he said mysteriously.

**中文理解**：Knox 说不是去购物，Waylay 问后面的东西是什么，Naomi 也好奇，Knox 神秘地说"用品"。
**关键字**：on-ramp for the highway（高速公路入口）；freaking out（崩溃）；multiple break-ins（多次闯入）；caseworker（社工）；truck bed（卡车床）；supplies（用品）；mysteriously（神秘地）
**为什么这样写**：用对话展现 Knox 的秘密和两人的好奇。
**读者视角提示**：从日常到神秘的转折。

⑦ **"There" turned out to be Hannah's Place, a homeless shelter on the outskirts of Washington, D.C. It was a newer brick building on a large fenced lot. Knox pulled the truck through the gate and swung it around toward the entrance, where I saw Jeremiah standing under an awning.**

> **原句 7:** "There" turned out to be Hannah's Place, a homeless shelter on the outskirts of Washington, D.C. It was a newer brick building on a large fenced lot. Knox pulled the truck through the gate and swung it around toward the entrance, where I saw Jeremiah standing under an awning.

**中文理解**："那里"是 Hannah's Place，华盛顿特区郊外的无家可归者收容所，Knox 开车进去，Jeremiah 在门廊下等。
**关键字**：Hannah's Place（汉娜之地）；homeless shelter（无家可归者收容所）；outskirts of Washington, D.C.（华盛顿特区郊区）；newer brick building（较新的砖建筑）；large fenced lot（大围栏地块）；awning（门廊）
**为什么这样写**：用具体地点展现 Knox 的慷慨行为。
**读者视角提示**：从神秘到揭示。

> **原句 8:** "The second string has arrived," Jeremiah said with a grin as we piled out. "Great 'do, Way." Waylay proudly patted a hand to the little French braid she'd worked around her head like a crown. "Thanks."

**中文理解**：Jeremiah 说第二拨人到了，夸 Waylay 的发型，Waylay 骄傲地摸了摸法式辫子。
**关键字**：second string（第二拨）；grin（咧嘴笑）；piled out（下车）；Great 'do（好发型）；French braid（法式辫子）；crown（皇冠）
**为什么这样写**：用对话展现 Jeremiah 的幽默和 Waylay 的骄傲。

> **原句 9:** "Shirley, meet Naomi and Waylay," Knox said. "Shirley left a seven-figure corporate gig to run this shelter." "Who needs boardrooms and corner offices when you can spend your days doing good?" Shirley said, shaking my hand and then Waylay's.

**中文理解**：Knox 介绍 Shirley，她放弃了七位数的工作来经营收容所，她说做善事不需要董事会和角落办公室。
**关键字**：seven-figure corporate gig（七位数的企业工作）；run this shelter（经营这个收容所）；boardrooms（董事会）；corner offices（角落办公室）；doing good（做善事）
**为什么这样写**：用 Shirley 的话展现收容所的精神和 Knox 的慷慨。

While we had spent two hours sorting food and clothing donations with other volunteers, Knox and Jeremiah had entertained an endless stream of shelter residents in their chairs under the awning on the sidewalk.**

> **原句 10:** "I guess it's kinda cool they do this," Waylay said as we watched Knox and Jeremiah run their makeshift outdoor salon through the tall windows. While we had spent two hours sorting food and clothing donations with other volunteers, Knox and Jeremiah had entertained an endless stream of shelter residents in their chairs under the awning on the sidewalk.

**中文理解**：Waylay 说他们这样做很酷，Naomi 和 Waylay 分拣食物和衣物，Knox 和 Jeremiah 为收容所居民理发。
**关键字**：kinda cool（有点酷）；makeshift outdoor salon（临时户外沙龙）；sorting food and clothing donations（分拣食物和衣物捐赠）；endless stream of shelter residents（源源不断的收容所居民）；under the awning on the sidewalk（在门廊下的人行道上）
**为什么这样写**：用具体细节展现志愿服务的温馨。

⑪ **It was a beautiful day edging toward fall, and the mood was festive. The staff, volunteers, and residents had formed a kind of large, unruly family making something as bleak as homelessness feel like a challenge to be conquered. Not a stigma to be reinforced. Together, Knox and Jeremiah transformed ignored, unruly, disheveled hair into sleek, stylish looks. And in doing so, I realized they were also changing the way each client saw themselves.**

> **原句 11:** "It was a beautiful day edging toward fall, and the mood was festive. **关键字**：edging toward fall（临近秋天）；festive（欢乐的）；unruly family（不守规矩的家庭）；bleak（凄凉的）；homelessness（无家可归）；stigma（污名）；reinforced（强化）；disheveled hair（乱发）；sleek, stylish looks（光滑、时尚的造型）
