# 36. The Break-In

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Knox 和 Nash、Lucian 讨论枪击案，Naomi 打电话说家里被闯入，他们赶去发现 Tina 破坏了房子。
- **情感弧线位置**：冲突 — 从讨论到紧急
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：日常讨论 → 紧急入侵 → 愤怒和守护
- **人物弧线**：Knox 从冷静到愤怒，Naomi 从害怕到被保护
- **叙事手法**：Knox 视角 — 从讨论到追踪

## 精读

① **Nash yawned and scraped a hand over his face. He was sitting at his dining table in sweat pants. His usually clean-shaven mug had the beginnings of a beard. "Look, I told you. I don't remember jack shit from the shooting. I don't even remember pulling the car over."**

> **原句 1:** "Nash yawned and scraped a hand over his face. He was sitting at his dining table in sweat pants. His usually clean-shaven mug had the beginnings of a beard. 'Look, I told you. I don't remember jack shit from the shooting. I don't even remember pulling the car over.'"

**中文理解**：Nash 坐在餐桌旁，穿着运动服，胡子拉碴，说他不记得枪击的事。
