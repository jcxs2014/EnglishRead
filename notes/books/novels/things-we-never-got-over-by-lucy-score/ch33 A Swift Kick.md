# 33. A Swift Kick

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Waylay 的足球比赛，Naomi 和 Stef 观赛，Knox 到场支持；Waylay 进球，全家庆祝。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 — 从比赛紧张到进球的喜悦
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：足球比赛 → 全家支持 → Waylay 进球
- **人物弧线**：Naomi 从紧张到骄傲，Waylay 从孤独到被爱
- **叙事手法**：Naomi 视角 — 从比赛到庆祝

## 精读

① **"Why do kids' sports start at such ungodly hours? And why is the grass so wet? Look at these shoes. They'll never recover," Stef complained as we set up our folding chairs on the sideline of the soccer field.**

> **原句 1:** "Why do kids' sports start at such ungodly hours? And why is the grass so wet? Look at these shoes. They'll never recover," Stef complained as we set up our folding chairs on the sideline of the soccer field.

**中文理解**：Stef 抱怨孩子运动时间太早，草地太湿，鞋子毁了。
**关键字**：ungodly hours（不吉利的时间）；grass so wet（草地湿）；folding chairs（折叠椅）；sideline（边线）
**为什么这样写**：用 Stef 的抱怨营造轻松幽默的气氛。
**读者视角提示**：从日常到比赛的过渡。

② **"It's nine in the morning, not four a.m.," I said dryly. "Maybe if you and Liza hadn't made and then drunk an entire pitcher of margaritas last night, you wouldn't be cringing like a vampire at the light of day."**

> **原句 2:** "It's nine in the morning, not four a.m.," I said dryly. "Maybe if you and Liza hadn't made and then drunk an entire pitcher of margaritas last night, you wouldn't be cringing like a vampire at the light of day."

**中文理解**：Naomi 说 Stef 和 Liza 昨晚喝了一整壶玛格丽特，现在像吸血鬼一样怕光。
**关键字**：nine in the morning（早上九点）；pitcher of margaritas（一壶玛格丽特）；cringing like a vampire（像吸血鬼一样畏光）
**为什么这样写**：用幽默的方式展现 Stef 的宿醉和两人的友谊。
**读者视角提示**：从抱怨到幽默的转折。

③ **"It was my last night in town before my trip to Paris. I couldn't say no to margaritas. Besides, it's easy to be Suzy Sunshine when you're getting laid regularly."**

> **原句 3:** "It was my last night in town before my trip to Paris. I couldn't say no to margaritas. Besides, it's easy to be Suzy Sunshine when you're getting laid regularly."

**中文理解**：Stef 说这是他离开前最后一晚，不能拒绝玛格丽特，而且经常做爱容易心情好。
**关键字**：last night in town（在城里的最后一晚）；trip to Paris（巴黎之行）；Suzy Sunshine（阳光女孩）；getting laid regularly（经常做爱）
**为什么这样写**：用幽默的方式展现 Stef 的性格和对 Naomi 的关心。
**读者视角提示**：从抱怨到自我辩护。

④ **"Zip it, Betty Big Mouth," I said, shooting a look at the rest of Waylay's cheering section. My parents were sitting with Liza, who didn't seem any the worse for wear for her half of the margaritas.**

> **原句 4:** "Zip it, Betty Big Mouth," I said, shooting a look at the rest of Waylay's cheering section. My parents were sitting with Liza, who didn't seem any the worse for wear for her half of the margaritas.

**中文理解**：Naomi 让 Stef 闭嘴，父母和 Liza 坐在一起，Liza 看起来没受玛格丽特影响。
**关键字**：Zip it（闭嘴）；Betty Big Mouth（大嘴 Betty）；cheering section（啦啦队）；worse for wear（受损）
**为什么这样写**：用昵称和细节展现家庭的温馨。
**读者视角提示**：从幽默到家庭温暖。

⑤ **"That guy looks like a probation violation, not a girls soccer coach," Stef observed. "That's Wraith. His granddaughter Delilah is the one with the pigtails. She plays forward. She's unbelievably fast," I told him.**

> **原句 5:** "That guy looks like a probation violation, not a girls soccer coach," Stef observed. "That's Wraith. His granddaughter Delilah is the one with the pigtails. She plays forward. She's unbelievably fast," I told him.

**中文理解**：Stef 说 Wraith 像缓刑犯不像教练，Naomi 解释他是 Delilah 的祖父，很快。
**关键字**：probation violation（缓刑违反）；girls soccer coach（女子足球教练）；pigtails（辫子）；forward（前锋）；unbelievably fast（难以置信地快）
**为什么这样写**：用幽默的方式展现 Wraith 的外貌和 Naomi 的了解。
**读者视角提示**：从外貌到能力的转折。

⑥ **"She's quick, she's sneaky, she's a team player. There's just one or two little kinks that need working out." "What kind of kinks?" Stef asked. "What did I miss?" Sloane appeared next to me in jeans and a Nirvana tank top under a soft gray cardigan.**

> **原句 6:** "She's quick, she's sneaky, she's a team player. There's just one or two little kinks that need working out." "What kind of kinks?" Stef asked. "What did I miss?" Sloane appeared next to me in jeans and a Nirvana tank top under a soft gray cardigan.

**中文理解**：Naomi 说 Waylay 快、狡猾、团队球员，但有一两个小问题需要解决。
**关键字**：quick（快）；sneaky（狡猾）；team player（团队球员）；kinks（小问题）；working out（解决）
**为什么这样写**：用具体描述展现 Waylay 的性格和成长空间。
**读者视角提示**：从赞美到问题的转折。

⑦ **"So where's your hot soccer daddy?" Stef asked me. "Thank God someone asked," Sloane said, shifting in her chair. "Here are all the questions I've stored up. How good is the sex? Is he as grumpy immediately after orgasm as he is the rest of the time, or are there cracks in the stony facade that reveal the soft, teddy bear heart beating beneath?"**

> **原句 7:** "So where's your hot soccer daddy?" Stef asked me. "Thank God someone asked," Sloane said, shifting in her chair. "Here are all the questions I've stored up. How good is the sex?"

**中文理解**：Stef 问 Naomi 的足球爸爸在哪，Sloane 说她有很多问题，包括性是否好。
**关键字**：hot soccer daddy（热足球爸爸）；stored up（储存）；how good is the sex（性有多好）
**为什么这样写**：用幽默的方式展现朋友的好奇和 Naomi 的尴尬。
**读者视角提示**：从尴尬到幽默。

⑧ Stef 抱怨 Naomi 会说他们不合适

> **原句 8:** "She's going to tell us how inappropriate we're being."

**中文理解**：Stef 说每个对话都是关于性的，Naomi 否认，Sloane 说那些爸爸看起来在谈工具，其实在谈输精管结扎。
**关键字**：inappropriate（不合适）；every conversation（每个对话）；power tools（电动工具）；lawn mowers（割草机）；vasectomies（输精管结扎）
**为什么这样写**：用幽默的方式展现朋友间的对话和足球场的文化。
**读者视角提示**：从严肃到幽默的转折。

⑨ **"I get it, kid. I do. But you can't say that shit on the field or in school." "Why not? You say it. My mom says it." "We're adults and we don't have a bunch of other adults breathin' down our necks, telling us what not to do."**

> **原句 9:** "I get it, kid. I do. But you can't say that shit on the field or in school."

**中文理解**：Knox 告诉 Waylay 不能在球场或学校说脏话，Waylay 说他也说，Knox 说大人不一样。
**关键字**：say that shit（说脏话）；on the field（在球场）；in school（在学校）；breathin' down our necks（盯着我们）
**为什么这样写**：用对话展现 Knox 对 Waylay 的教导和关心。
**读者视角提示**：从叛逆到被理解。

⑩ **"You're fuckin' better than that, Way. You've got a temper, but there's a hell of a lot more power in controlling it than letting it fly. Use it, or it'll use you. You get me?" She nodded solemnly. "I think so. When can I swear?" "When you and me are watching football."**

> **原句 10:** "You're fuckin' better than that, Way. You've got a temper, but there's a hell of a lot more power in controlling it than letting it fly. Use it, or it'll use you. You get me?" She nodded solemnly. "I think so. When can I swear?" "When you and me are watching football."

**中文理解**：Knox 告诉 Waylay 控制脾气比发泄更有力量，说一起看足球时可以说脏话。
**关键字**：better than that（比那更好）；temper（脾气）；controlling it（控制）；letting it fly（发泄）；use it or it'll use you（用它，否则它用你）
**为什么这样写**：用直接的语言展现 Knox 对 Waylay 的教导和承诺。
**读者视角提示**：从严肃到温暖的转折。

⑪ **"This is the best day of my life." I was trying to blink back tears when someone pulled her from my arms. It was Knox, and he was settling Waylay on his shoulders as the rest of the players and parents gathered around to congratulate her. Knox shot me one of his rare, full-on grins that made me dizzy.**

> **原句 11:** "This is the best day of my life." I was trying to blink back tears when someone pulled her from my arms. It was Knox, and he was settling Waylay on his shoulders as the rest of the players and parents gathered around to congratulate her. Knox shot me one of his rare, full-on grins that made me dizzy.

**中文理解**：Waylay 说这是她人生中最棒的一天，Knox 把她举过肩膀，对 Naomi 露出罕见的笑容。
**关键字**：best day of my life（人生中最棒的一天）；blink back tears（忍住眼泪）；settling Waylay on his shoulders（把 Waylay 放在肩膀上）；full-on grins（灿烂的笑容）；made me dizzy（让我头晕）
**为什么这样写**：用 Waylay 的话和 Knox 的笑容展现家庭的温暖和幸福。
**读者视角提示**：从比赛到庆祝的高潮。
