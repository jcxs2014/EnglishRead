# 25. Family Fuss

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Knox 回医院看 Nash，Stef 劝他追 Naomi；晚上家庭聚餐，Naomi 被 Knox 逗弄。
- **情感弧线位置**：evening — 日常生活恢复，感情愈演愈烈
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：家庭聚餐 → 公开撩拨 → Knox 主动声明
- **人物弧线**：Knox 在病房保持礼节，晚上控制局势；Naomi 被撩拨欲火
- **叆事手法**：Knox/Naomi 双视角 — 病房冷静 vs 晚上的火热

## 精读

① **"Let me know what you find from the office and the storage unit," Nash said from the bed. His color was a bit better.**

> **原句 1:** "Let me know what you find from the office and the storage unit," Nash said from the bed.

**中文理解**：Knox 来看望 Nash，Nash 指示他调查办公室和储藏室的入侵。
**关键字**：storage unit（储物间）；color was a bit better（气色稍好）；let me know（告诉我）
**为什么这样写**：将调查指令融入医院场景，显示 Nash 对犯罪的关注未减。
**读者视角提示**：从医疗关怀到案件调查，建立 Knox 的多重任务。

② **"Gotta head out. Some shit to take care of before the big family dinner tonight celebrating Way's first day and Naomi's parents coming to town."**

> **原句 2:** "Gotta head out. Some shit to take care of before the big family dinner tonight celebrating Way's first day and Naomi's parents coming to town."

**中文理解**：Knox 向 Nash 说明要离开，准备应付 Waylay 上学和 Naomi 父母的聚餐。
**关键字**：family dinner（家庭聚餐）；Way's first day（Waylay 上学）；parents coming to town（父母来城）
**为什么这样写**：建立 Knox 对 Naomi 生活的融入，以及家庭聚会的悬念。
**读者视角提示**：将个人关系与家庭社交交织。

③ **Liza held up her empty glass. "I could do another round." "I'm still working on mine, sweetie," Amanda told him.**

> **原句 3:** "I could do another round." "I'm still working on mine, sweetie," Amanda told him.

**中文理解**：Liza 想再喝一轮，Amanda 还在喝她的饮料。
**关键字**：another round（再来一轮）；working on（正在品尝）；sweetie（亲爱的）
**为什么这样写**：通过对话展现不同人物的饮酒习惯，建立气氛。
**读者视角提示**：家庭成员间的互动与不同节奏。

④ **"I always dreamed of having a big family in a big house. All these generations and dogs all tangled up in each other's lives. I guess sometimes we're just not meant for certain things." She said it wistfully.**

> **原句 4:** "I always dreamed of having a big family in a big house. All these generations and dogs all tangled up in each other's lives."

**中文理解**：Amanda 怀念大家族生活，认为人和狗都纠缠在一起。
**关键字**：dreamed of（梦想）；big family（大家庭）；tangled up（纠缠）；wistfully（怀旧地）
**为什么这样写**：用"dogs" 比喻家人之间的纠缠，展现温情。
**读者视角提示**：建立母亲对家庭的感情归属。

⑤ **"I need some Advil or something," Dad muttered, rubbing his forehead. "You shouldn't have a headache, Lou. Our daughter is fine."**

> **原句 5:** "I need some Advil or something," Dad muttered, rubbing his forehead.

**中文理解**：Naomi 父亲头疼，用 Advil 作为止痛药。
**关键字**：Advil（止痛药）；muttered（咕咕称）；rubbing his forehead（揉前额）
**为什么这样写**：通过药物描绘父亲的不适，建立紧张基调。
**读者视角提示**：从健康关切到关系困扰。
