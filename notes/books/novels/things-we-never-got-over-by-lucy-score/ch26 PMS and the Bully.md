# 26. PMS and the Bully

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Naomi 到酒吧上班，同事发现她的性满足脸，她得知老师欺负 Waylay，冲去学校对峙。
- **情感弧线位置**：冲突 — 从轻松的早晨到愤怒的对峙
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：假寐一夜情 → 同事八卦 → 保护欲爆发
- **人物弧线**：Naomi 从尴尬到愤怒，为 Waylay 不惜一切
- **叙事手法**：Naomi 视角 — 从酒吧轻松到学校紧张

## 精读

① **I arrived at Honky Tonk early for my shift in my Dad's pristine Ford Explorer. A bonus to having my parents in town. Another bonus was the fact that they were having a movie night sleepover with Waylay at Liza's.**

> **原句 1:** "I arrived at Honky Tonk early for my shift in my Dad's pristine Ford Explorer."

**中文理解**：Naomi 开着父亲的福特探险者早到酒吧上班。
**关键字**：pristine Ford Explorer（崭新的福特探险者）；bonus（额外好处）；movie night sleepover（电影夜借宿）
**为什么这样写**：用"pristine" 暗示父亲的爱车，同时展现 Naomi 的家庭支持。
**读者视角提示**：从轻松的早晨开始，为后续冲突铺垫。

> **原句 2:** "She's got orgasm face. She's not one of us."

**中文理解**：Silver 注意到 Naomi 脸上的性满足表情，说她"不是我们的人了"。
**关键字**：orgasm face（性满足脸）；not one of us（不是我们中的一员）；flipped the stools（翻凳子）
**为什么这样写**：用幽默的方式揭示 Naomi 的秘密，同时展现同事间的亲密。
**读者视角提示**：从轻松八卦到秘密暴露的尴尬。

③ **"Knox's monthly care package," Silver said. "Who gave you the O face?" "Care package for what?" I asked, ignoring the question. "All our cycles synced up. Stasia's too," Fi explained. "Every month, the boss puts together a period survival kit and is nice to us for a day or two."**

> **原句 3:** "All our cycles synced up. Stasia's too."

**中文理解**：Fi 解释 Knox 每月为女员工准备经期生存包，对她们好一两天。
**关键字**：cycles synced up（经期同步）；period survival kit（经期生存包）；nice to us（对我们好）
**为什么这样写**：展现 Knox 的体贴一面，与平时的粗暴形成对比。
**读者视角提示**：揭示 Knox 对员工的关怀，增加人物层次。

④ **"On a scale of Meh to My Vagina is Forever Ruined, how good was he?" Fi asked.**

> **原句 4:** "On a scale of Meh to My Vagina is Forever Ruined, how good was he?"

**中文理解**：Fi 用从"一般"到"我的阴道被毁"的等级问 Knox 有多好。
**关键字**：Meh（一般）；My Vagina is Forever Ruined（我的阴道被毁）；scale（等级）
**为什么这样写**：用夸张幽默的方式展现同事的好奇和 Naomi 的尴尬。
**读者视角提示**：从轻松八卦到 Naomi 的秘密即将暴露。

⑤ **"Mrs. Felch is about to be. Mind covering for me until I get back?" Silver looked up from the heating pad she was taping to her stomach. "I'll cover for you if you bring me back one of those pretzels with caramel dip from the place next to the school."**

> **原句 5:** "Mrs. Felch is about to be. Mind covering for me until I get back?"

**中文理解**：Naomi 说老师要有麻烦了，让同事帮忙顶班。
**关键字**：about to be（就要倒霉了）；cover for me（帮我顶班）；heating pad（加热垫）
**为什么这样写**：用简短句展现 Naomi 的愤怒和决心。
**读者视角提示**：从轻松到愤怒的转折。

⑥ **""No, but Mrs. Felch is about to be. Mind covering for me until I get back?" The lines on her face carved deeper.**

> **原句 6:** ""No, but Mrs. Felch is about to be. Mind covering for me until I get back?"

**中文理解**：Felch 老师恶毒地说她按"婊子女儿应得的方式"对待 Waylay。
**关键字**：daughter of a whore（婊子女儿）；snarled（咆哮）；lines carved deeper（皱纹更深）
**为什么这样写**：直接引语展现老师的恶毒，为 Naomi 的愤怒提供正当性。
**读者视角提示**：从冲突升级到道德审判。

⑦ **""I'm not Tina. I'm Naomi Witt. My niece, Waylay, is in your class, and I'd like to talk to you about how you've been treating her." "But neither does Waylay. And I'm not going to let anyone make her feel like she's responsible for the decisions adults make."**

> **原句 7:** "A movement out of the corner of my eye caught my attention, and I realized that I had a much bigger problem than a horrible sixth-grade teacher."

**中文理解**：Naomi 同情老师的遭遇，但坚持 Waylay 不该为成年人的决定受罚。
**关键字**：didn't deserve（不值得）；responsible for（为...负责）；decisions adults make（成年人的决定）
**为什么这样写**：展现 Naomi 的道德高度——既同情又坚持原则。
**读者视角提示**：从愤怒到同情的复杂情感转变。

⑧ **"I'd never been good at confrontations. Hell, I'd squeezed my ass out of a church basement window to run away from a wedding rather than tell the groom I wasn't going to marry him. But in that moment, I felt a fire burning in my belly. Backing down wasn't an option. Neither was retreat."**

> **原句 8:** "I'd never been good at confrontations."

**中文理解**：Naomi 承认自己不擅长对峙，但此刻她感到怒火中烧，绝不退让。
**关键字**：confrontations（对峙）；fire burning in my belly（怒火中烧）；backing down（退让）；retreat（撤退）
**为什么这样写**：用过去的逃婚对比现在的坚守，展现 Naomi 的成长。
**读者视角提示**：从逃避到面对的人物弧光。
