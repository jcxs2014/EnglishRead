# 《Never Thought I'd End Up Here》情感节点

## 节点一：婚礼的儿童桌（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书的情感起点不是一个相遇，而是一次被安排。母亲替她挑好衣服、催她进场，她被排进儿童桌，邻座正是两年前把她赶出旧学校的赛勒斯·隋。她整个人靠「我不想引人注目」撑着，而他一句「憔悴」就戳穿了她退出模特圈后强撑的体面。这一章的情绪不是低谷，是僵局：她退出了那个让她流泪的行业，却既没换来解脱，也没换来家人的认可。

**关键引语：**

> ① The last thing I want is to make a dramatic entrance at my cousin’s wedding.（ch01）

> ② I release an internal sigh of relief. It doesn’t matter how often I hear it—I still want the confirmation that I can be beautiful. Crave it, chase it, like an adrenaline junkie seeking their next high.（ch01）

> ③ I left it all behind me, and now, somehow, I’ve ended up here: at a wedding I never wanted to attend, next to the old nemesis I never wanted to be reunited with, who’s retained the uncanny ability to get under my skin after two years.（ch01）

## 节点二：一趟她自己不想去的旅行（ch02-03）

**叙事概括：** 母亲替她报名了研学团，她几乎是被推上飞机的。但就在旅行的开头，她第一次说出对自己最诚实的定义——「我很漂亮，而我不知道除了漂亮我还能是什么」。也是在这几天里，她意识到自己的恨与在意是同一件事的两面。

**关键引语：**

> ① So I cut my hair at a salon recommended by all the prettiest influencers. Learned how to do my makeup. Blasted songs about loving yourself as you are while working out to remold my body into something else. Poured my savings into completely revamping my wardrobe—out went the comfortable sweatpants and basic, faded shirts, and in came the suffocatingly tight dresses and crop tops that always left my stomach cold.（ch02）

> ② Instead, when I woke up, I felt an exhaustion so heavy and bone-deep I feared it would crush me. And as it turns out, I don’t have any hobbies. I never had a chance or reason to discover other interests, because modeling was an all-consuming force, coloring in every single aspect of my life, and now that it’s gone, there’s only blank white space.（ch03）

> ③ “This is it. This is my chance to get Cyrus Sui back at last. He’s ruined the better part of my life, so what’s stopping me from ruining this trip for him? If I’m going to be stuck with him in another country either way, I might as well get something out of it. Take karma into my own hands.”（ch04）

## 节点三：竹林里的迷路（ch07）

**叙事概括：** 两人在桂林的竹林里走丢，那是他们第一次真正对话。她第一次把「不确定」写成一件可以接受的事——她不再要求知道自己在哪，只承认自己不介意和他一起迷路。这是全书第一处松动，也是她后来那句「不问去哪」的最早版本。

**关键引语：**

> ① He blinks in surprise. Then he high-fives me back, and for only a moment, I see an alternative history sprawled out between us, where we might have been childhood friends instead of enemies, playing made-up games under the shade of an old oak. Where he hadn’t devoted his life to making mine miserable. Where he hadn’t lied and destroyed everything.（ch07）

> ② Ignore Cyrus, don’t trust him, don’t let yourself laugh at his remarks. Only one person is going to get their heart broken at the end of this trip, and it’s not going to be you.（ch09）

> ③ “You know what?” Cyrus says abruptly, turning toward their honeymoon suite with more eagerness than even a real newlywed. “I’ve decided that I don’t mind sleeping next to your feet, Oliver. Let’s just go back inside.”（ch10）

## 节点四：她自己定下的复仇计划（ch11）

**叙事概括：** 她在山里写下那张字条之前，先在心里设计了一场单方面的取用：让他爱上她，然后当面毁掉他。这一章是她主动性的顶点，也是全书最危险的地方——她第一次把「关系」当成一件可以规划的东西，而这份规划里没有她自己的感情。

**关键引语：**

> ① He scoffs. “I don’t need to wish for that. I’m going to make it happen.”（ch11）

> ② Cyrus grins all of a sudden, like he can’t help himself. “Hey, my sunglasses look good on you.”（ch12）

> ③ My last remaining comfort is Cyrus’s words, echoing through my thoughts. I’ll come find you. It shouldn’t bring me much comfort at all, given his record of getting me in trouble versus getting me out of it. But somehow, he sounded like he meant what he said.（ch13）

## 节点五：他是第一个替她担心的人（ch14）

**叙事概括：** 竹林夜行，她在黑暗里僵住、心跳得像要撞出胸腔；他察觉了她的不安，却没有追问原因，只是停下来待在她身边。她第一次经历「被照顾而不必解释」，这与她那套「必须解释自己才对得起别人」的习惯正面相撞。

**关键引语：**

> ① “I forget to hate you a lot of the time,” I whisper. It slips too easily from my tongue, without warning, turned by the darkness into a confession.（ch14）

> ② “I have to admit, you’re kind of different from what I thought.” He peers over at me. “Like, you’re hot, obviously, but you’re also thoughtful and brave and mature and nice even when you don’t need to be, which is, like, actually really rare these days. And I swear I’m not just saying that. Cyrus is a lucky guy.”（ch15）

> ③ “I never meant to lie about you pushing me,” he says, his voice very soft, just audible over the calls of birds in the distance. “Much less get you kicked out of the school. It was all my fault. I was concussed and everything happened so fast, I wasn’t thinking properly—when the teacher asked if someone had hurt me, my first instinct was to say that you did, because I was hurt, but it had nothing to do with the fall itself.”（ch16）

## 节点六：村巷里的坦白与那个没说出的话（ch16）

**叙事概括：** 全书感情线的顶点。他把两年来的一切讲开：误认是脑震荡下的一句错答，他写过一封从未送达的信，他爱她七年。而她原本要说出那句致命的话，结果什么也没说出来——作者不给她一个体面的理由：不是她想通了，是她没说出来。随后 Oliver 从巷口撞见，把这场告白切成了一个赌局。

**关键引语：**

> ① “I thought you were the most beautiful girl I’d ever seen,” he says, somehow without an ounce of sarcasm. “You’ve always been beautiful—beautiful like the stars are, like Shanghai is. I could never get sick of looking at you.”（ch16）

> ② He’s at my mercy, just like I always wanted. If I reject him right now, tell him that I hate him, that I’ll never forgive him for what he did, the consequences will be devastating. … but when I open my mouth to deliver the fatal blow, nothing comes out. / Nothing comes out, because his lips are on mine.（ch16）

> ③ “It’s kind of disgustingly obvious.” … “Thanks for that,” Cyrus says, but despite the self-consciousness creeping into his voice, he doesn’t deny any of it.（ch16）

## 节点七：在镜头里重新被看见（ch17）

**叙事概括：** 她被摄影师拉去拍汉服照，走进那间她熟悉的化妆 studio。这一次她没有逃，而镜子里的自己让她第一次承认「从没这么好看过，也从没这么像自己」。这一章是全书对「被看见」给出的正式答案：好的妆不是把脸换成另一种标准，而是把原来的五官讲清楚。

**关键引语：**

> ① I nod fast, the amber beads in my headpiece rattling like music. I’ve never looked this way before, but I also look more like myself than I ever have in the past; the tiny gems glued below my lash line make my eyes that much brighter, more alive, the rose blush dusted along my nose and cheekbones blending naturally into my skin, the vermilion tint enhancing the bow shape of my lips. It’s makeup that doesn’t try to alter my features or hide them or exaggerate them to near-satirical proportions.（ch17）

> ② In the mirror, I lock eyes with Cyrus, who stands patiently off to the side, hands in his pockets. He offers me a small, encouraging smile, like he can detect the shakiness spreading through my muscles, and I breathe in. Remind myself that it won’t be the same as last time. If I need to, I can always leave.（ch17）

> ③ A few tourists stop to stare as they pass us, but I don’t get the same self-conscious, ready-to-hide feeling I used to. It’s like my brain has undergone a makeover too, those dark spots of doubt brushed away and replaced by almost obnoxiously optimistic thoughts. Maybe they’re staring because they also think this style suits me. Maybe they’re admiring how the pins glow in my hair.（ch17）

## 节点八：她第一次把退圈史讲出来（ch18）

**叙事概括：** 拍完照出来的夜里，他问她既然喜欢为什么放弃，她第一次把整段经历讲了出来：批评必然人身、要被卖的是她本人、被当成「亚洲女孩里的例外」的示好、Amalia 那次把古中国做成布景的拍摄。讲到一半她开始犹豫——这是她对他最后一道防线。而他听完只说了一句：不，你一点也不弱。

**关键引语：**

> ① “It’s kind of hard to explain, but modeling is different from, like, gardening or knitting,” I say, rubbing my arms. “It’s not this skill that you can separate from yourself. It’s you; you’re the product. It’s your appearance that they’re selling.”（ch18）

> ② I never even told my parents exactly why I left. I didn’t tell anyone, because I thought, foolishly, that I could digest the shard of glass in my stomach given enough time.（ch18）

> ③ “No,” Cyrus says, his fingers threading through my hair. “No, you’re not weak at all. If something costs more than it’s worth, you let it go. If anybody dares make you feel bad for it, then screw them.”（ch18）

## 节点九：夜演看台上的旧照片（ch19）

**叙事概括：** 全书最低点。夜演《印象·刘三姐》看到一半，有人翻出她退圈前那场灾难性的拍摄照，全团当众围观、逼她解释。她本能地质问唯一知情的人，然后跑出剧场，在稻田边把「我失败了」说给一头牛和一位陌生老太太听。老太太只让她抬头看天——那道新鲜的记忆切痕没有消失，但它只是许多星星中的一颗。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Everything,” I say tearily, not even bothering to hold together the last cracks in my composure anymore. I fumble for the right words in Chinese. “My life. I—I have no idea what to do or where to go.”（ch19）

> ② The fresh gash of the memory doesn’t disappear from my mind, but it’s just one star of many, glistening in the night.（ch19）

> ③ The wasps in my head finally go still. It had been instinct to distrust him. Instinct to assume the worst. If you were to suffer a blow to your stomach every time you walked down a road, you would automatically start tensing before you’ve even taken the first step, bracing for the pain.（ch19）

## 节点十：追出来的是 Daisy（ch19）

**叙事概括：** 她整本书的核心信念是「认识我的人越多，越不喜欢我」；而追到稻田边的是 Daisy，不是爱人。她第一次承认自己从没有过真正的朋友，Daisy 的回答是「好，那现在你有了」，并在得知真相后说照片是 Sean 找的、赛勒斯从头到尾没参与。她第一次相信了「我会永远站在你这边」这句话。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Okay, well, now you do,” Daisy says. She sounds more sure of herself than I’ve ever heard her, and I jerk my head up in surprise.（ch19）

> ② “I need to talk to him,” I say, but not before I pull Daisy into a tight hug. She’s so much shorter than I am that I’m scared of crushing her, but she hugs me back just as hard, pressing her head to my shoulder. “Thank you,” I whisper. “For coming after me. For being on my side.”（ch19）

> ③ “I’ll always be on your side,” she says, and I believe it.（ch19）

## 节点十一：道歉与一次被拒绝的赦免（ch20）

**叙事概括：** 她跟着他回房道歉，承认自己做了「仓促而错误的假设」。而他没有接这个道歉——他说她把他当敌人当了很多年，她当时想的任何人都会想，他也不指望她一下子完全信他，只要一个慢慢赢得信任的机会。窗外婚礼的烟花让全旅馆的人涌上阳台，她由此想到：人看见美的东西，第一反应就是拿给所爱的人看。

**关键引语：**

> ① “You’ve considered me your enemy for years, and I’ll never be able to make up for what happened. You thought what any person would have thought. I wouldn’t expect you to go from hating me to trusting me completely right away—I would want the chance to win your trust slowly, bit by bit, if I had the honor.”（ch20）

> ② “I’m honestly shocked that you came back. Shocked, and very, very glad. I didn’t think you ever would.”（ch20）

> ③ And my heart swells at the silly, simple, human fact that when we stumble upon something beautiful, our first instinct is to show it to the people we love. It’s what we do with pretty seashells on a beach, a radiant sunset, a rare bird flitting through the trees, a herd of wild horses grazing in the countryside. Look, we say, saving these little pieces of beauty for each other. Do you see it too? Isn’t the world such a strange, lovely, breathtaking place?（ch20）

## 节点十二：石桥上的「这里就很好」（ch20）

**叙事概括：** 天没亮她就溜出房间，在石桥上找到等她的他。他说「你可以随时回来，我们可以随时再来」，她则在心里排出一串未来的画面——上海外滩、古镇、洛杉矶的海岸。而她说出全书的正面回答：她唯一想象不出的，是不再想要他。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Where do you want to go?” he asks as he makes his way toward me. / “Here is perfect,” I say. Right now, I can’t think of a single place on earth I would prefer to this.（ch20）

> ② I could love him anywhere, in any city, any season, I think to myself. I can easily envision us coming back together, maybe a year or two from now, strolling down the Bund at night, lost in Shanghai’s brilliant array of lights.（ch20）

> ③ The only thing I can’t envision is no longer wanting him.（ch20）

## 节点十三：飞机上他把一切说开（ch21）

**叙事概括：** 比赛她与他并列第一，回程的飞机上他坦白两件事：去上海的座位是他花钱跟人换的，婚礼那天他「有个人必须见」指的就是她。她整本书的起点设定（偶然重逢）被改写成一次寻找。而她终究没能说出准备好的那段话，只能靠着他、把他的手握紧。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Also … I was hoping you would be at your cousin’s wedding. I did plan to find Dr. Linda Shen, of course—that letter of recommendation is really important to me. But when I said there was someone I needed to see, I was talking about you.”（ch21）

> ② Maybe, another day, I’ll find the right words in either English or Chinese to tell him everything I’m feeling, how grateful I am that we came here together, how happy I am whenever I’m with him, how close I’d been to losing hope before he found his way to me. But a lump fills my throat, and for now I can only lean against him, squeeze his hand tight as the plane begins slowly backing away from the gate.（ch21）

> ③ I’m still not sure where I’ll end up a month from now, or a year, or half a life, whether I’ll find something I love as much as I love this moment, and whether it’ll last. But all I have to do is look out at the sky, that deep, lovely, endless blue, and remember that no matter where I end up, joy will never be too far out of reach.（ch21）

## 节点十四：台下的那个微笑（ch22）

**叙事概括：** 三个月后，她站在九音河体育馆的麦架前，用曾经听不全的中文对一屋子家长与老师致辞。她开口第一句就说实话——她当初并不想去。而全场最要紧的一刻落在台下一个人的脸上：一直是她全书伤害起点的姑姑，第一次给了她一个淡淡的、赞许的微笑。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Welcome, parents, students, and teachers,” I begin in slow, careful Chinese, hoping they can’t detect the shakiness in my voice. “I have to be honest—when my mom first signed me up for this trip, I didn’t particularly want to go …”（ch22）

> ② I brace myself as I glance in my aunt’s direction. It strikes me suddenly how much she resembles my own mother; something about the structure of her jaw, the intelligent arch of her brows.（ch22）

> ③ The resemblance is stronger than ever when her severe features finally soften, and she offers me a faint, approving smile.（ch22）

## 节点十五：她终于把「他爱我」当成事实（ch22）

**叙事概括：** 散场后两人在洛杉矶走了几英里。她的脚在凉鞋里冻僵，他没问也没说，就把外套披在她肩上。她问他怎么知道，他说「因为我知道你」——而她说出这句话时，已经不再是一个问题。全书的最后一句落在一件小事上：她没有问他要去哪里，她甚至不在意。

**关键引语：**

> ① Nothing’s perfect, but everything’s wonderful. / We walk for miles, content in each other’s company, stopping to point out a pretty flower or a squirrel in the trees. Soon, the evening chill creeps in. I try my best not to notice it, but my feet feel frozen in my sandals.（ch22）

> ② “Because I know you,” he says simply. / “You love me,” I say. I can’t pinpoint when it stopped being a question, and when it started to feel like a simple fact. There are 6,479 miles between Shanghai and Los Angeles. The sun will come up tomorrow. You can never go wrong with a well-fitted black dress. And he loves me.（ch22）

> ③ We keep walking, and I don’t ask him where we’re going, and I don’t even care, as long as I’m with him.（ch22）
