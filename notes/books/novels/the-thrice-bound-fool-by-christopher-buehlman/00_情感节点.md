# The Thrice-Bound Fool · 情感节点

① **买驴与微笑的嘴（ch01）**
Kinch 在剥熊女人的村子里花四枚银币买了驴、半夜被书的杀令惊醒、用铁帐篷钉钉穿墙上长出的嘴——而书页归位时嘴在微笑。「我杀了一张嘴，但书的意志没有死。」
> "Fine. Four scales. I'll take the donkey. But only if he knows jokes. I hate a boring companion."
> "I noticed the mouth illustration had returned to the page. Lighter because it had lost some ink. But now it was smiling."

② **猫头鹰坠落与拥抱（ch05）**
六尺白枭夜袭营地把两人逼上死树——死树翻倒、两人坠落。他在坠落里抱住她，「她没有揍我」——三个「没有」完成一段关系的无声换挡，然后 Galva 在坠落里微笑。
> "I held her, and she didn't punch me. It hit us."
> "Galva smiling—the mad thing was actually smiling—and a final look up as one of the monsters looked down at us."

③ **跷跷板上的松手（ch06）**
峡谷底醒来，死树成了跷跷板——Galva 闭眼呼唤死亡女神、Kinch 在一颗心跳里决定松手救她。「Did I let go because I was willing to sacrifice myself? … I don't either.」
> "Did I let go because I was willing to sacrifice myself to save that grumpus? If you don't know by now, don't feel bad. I don't either."

④ **Norrigal 借尸探望（ch14）**
莓毒幻觉的死亡平原上，Norrigal 的影子借死人的身体来看他——只为「再看看你的好脸」。她说了「这不是复合」，然后闭嘴、看着、走了。
> "Meaning I just wanted to look at you again," she said with such a tender sadness that I would have cried, had I water for a tear.
> "Just fucking look at me if you're going to." "S'what I'm doing."

⑤ **三神审判与三体分裂（ch11）**
神殿的灯光暗下来逼他们签下空白合同——「We accept」——然后一道火把两人各分裂成三体；两对被处决（一对烧死、一对穿墙送回），留下的一对领走「ONE GOES ON」。
> "I now felt an intense sensation under my breastbone, like fire, painful but almost pleasant. The feeling drove me to my knees, only it was four knees I went to, and then six."
> "ONE GOES ON."

⑥ **Filaine 的材质自白（ch09）**
书的梦把 Kinch 变成亡友 Malk、与「自己」摔跤——蟹爪临身时 Girleen 拉他出梦：书是用她的皮做的革、骨粉做的纸、油做的胶。他讨价还价「先帮我找秘密再烧书」。
> "It uses my skin for leather and the powder of my bones is in the paper and my fat is in the glue that binds it. My blood and the ash of my burned hair is in the ink."
> "PLEASE!" she said, desperate, on the point of tears.

⑦ **狗身与 Hasp 的课（ch08）**
Kinch 在狗身里醒来——Norrigal 的藏魂术把他寄进一条狗：狗鼻子的感官轰炸（义肢的产地、羊腿的饮食履历）与「Sit!」的条件反射把奇观降维成日常。
> "You're in a dog, s'the only thing I could find to harbor you for this singsong."
> "And oh, the smile that wrestler smiled at me."

⑧ **Galva 的三词誓言（ch28）**
角斗士群战——Galva 是全场唯一的类人族与唯一的女性。她一剑贯心 Praxeter 队的女巨、跨过鸦尸与人的血，赛后向 Kinch 比出三个手势：带我们回家。
> "Galva's three canted words would become my reason for staying alive in this karkhouse: Get. Us. Home."

⑨ **Hasp 的蓝莓与死亡（ch30–31）**
Hasp 装死归来后，把「珍惜一本好书」当作最后的遗言交给他，然后真死了——Kinch 回到房间发现「火花熄了就缩了」的躯体，在盗书的夜里听说这个消息。
> "You selfish ganny bastard," I told him in our shared tongue. "You went home without me."
> "If you take no other wine from the ewer of my mouth, Kinch, I hope you learn to treasure a good book."

⑩ **Jubel 的死与「You must kill them」（ch36）**
设局的擂台上 Jubel 被地精从侧面杀死——熊与地精同归于尽。Galva 弃剑用手、硬贯自己左手在敌刃上换一秒破防，断臂断膝杀死了 Apia——然后被 slakesword 刺穿腹部，血手比出三个词。
> "So Galva didn't use the spadín. She used her hand. She slapped out hard, impaling her own left hand on the blade."
> "You must kill them." Then she fell and was carried out.

⑪ **弑城（ch38）**
启示日——Kinch 用 Murder Alphabet 的杀句涂改神词，满场识字者应声倒毙。国王鼻血抓胸死、卫兵倒下、云船无人驾驶相撞。他杀了一座城——「I did. Not all of them. But most of them.」
> "I killed a city. I did. Not all of them. But most of them."
> "Guilty."

⑫ **Galva 的死**
Glassfall 的黑glass雨里，两人从崩塌的王宫抢出云船——Dalgatha 被 Moth Knights 双矛贯腹，Galva 被他挥舞的黑glass剑误贯。她趴在船舷看完「伟大城市的死」，听他讲 Pulhu 救 Quiggles 的奇迹，合上眼睛。
> "The point went into her. … I ripped the sword free, pushed the filthy karking thing down, and stabbed. And stabbed. And stabbed."
> "Look down there, blacktongue. It is something to see, the death of a great city."

⑬ **假死与归一**
坠机落回 Ylda 匪巢——死去的 other-Kinch 留下一封拼错连篇的绝笔信，死去的 other-Galva 在雪堆里留着三具暗杀者的尸体。然后「Jilnaedu?」——她从雪山里走出来。假死布置、讲故事之请、告白——最后她开讲。
> "Then two. And now one again."
> "I love you better than I did."
> "And I you, jilnaedu."
> "I saw my first goblin the same day I saw my first shipwreck. I was under sail, on my way to war. On my way to fall in love with Death, and with a queen."
