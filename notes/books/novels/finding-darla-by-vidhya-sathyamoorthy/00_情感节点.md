# Finding Darla by Vidhya Sathyamoorthy — 情感节点

## 节点一（ch01）：家族羞辱的起点——"Damn. Look at this car, it's filthy!"

> "Damn. Look at this car, it's filthy!"（ch01）

Nandhini 在医院探视 Uncle Ramesh，被 Aunty Mishra 与 Pooja 反复羞辱其未婚与事业状态，停车场发现废弃 Proton Saga SKA5783，埋下悬疑钩子。情感：压抑 → 惊愕 → 追问。

---

## 节点二（ch02）：命运之车——"It was destiny, she could feel it."

> "It was destiny, she could feel it."（ch02）

Darla 试镜失败被 Manny 劝慰，买下 Proton Saga SKA5783（车牌 = 生日），视之为 destiny。情感：挫败 → 希望。

---

## 节点三（ch03）：家族谎言与 paati 之死——"That virus had taken too much away from her."

> "That virus had taken too much away from her. Her job, her paati …"（ch03）

Nandhini freelance 追款、向母亲编造白领生活、裁员创伤（疫情）、paati 之死；梦到"那辆车……她的 paati"。情感：焦虑 → 恐惧 → 未愈的哀伤。

---

## 节点四（ch04）：父亲之死与 Zoomie——"You were born to be an actress!"

> "You were born to be an actress!"（ch04）

Darla 驾驶 Zoomie 兴奋，母亲 Mrs Fernandez 激烈反对（父亲死于赛车事故），Darla 在父亲遗照前许下 "make father proud"。情感：兴奋 → 家族创伤 → 决意。

---

## 节点五（ch05）：Follow the light——"Follow the light, my dear. Let it show you the way."

> "Follow the light, my dear. Let it show you the way."（ch05）

Nandhini soju 夜、drunk kiss Michael、nightmare（Paati + car SKA5783 + "Follow the light"）、hangover 与 Michael awkward repair（"it never happened"）。情感：逃避 → symbolic turn → tentative acceptance。

---

## 节点六（ch06）：荧幕之吻——"Yes, a man from my dreams. Have you heard of something so absurd?"

> "Yes, a man from my dreams. Have you heard of something so absurd?"（ch06）

Darla 在 Ms Farah crime flick 首次 acting credit，Jaga 电话（audition today），Donny "happy dance caught" 喜剧，Teresa first audition，chemistry test（on-screen kiss with Donny）。情感：兴奋 → crush。

---

## 节点七（ch07）：I've had enough——"I've had enough. You want me to follow the damn light? Fine, I'll do it."

> "I've had enough. You want me to follow the damn light? Fine, I'll do it."（ch07）

Nandhini recurring nightmare，决定 "follow the light"，eureka（light = hospital parking lot 的 Proton Saga），Akmal mamak shop meeting，vehicle ownership report 揭示 Darla Roland Fernandez（Sabah）。情感：恐惧 → 决心。

---

## 节点八（ch08）：离开 Sabah——"I love you too, you foolish girl,"

> "I love you too, you foolish girl,"（ch08）

Darla 获得 Ariyah 角色（shoot 在 KL），母亲激烈反对（"cheap whore"、"bringing shame"），Darla 在父亲遗照与话语指引下决定赴 KL，母亲 "I love you too, you foolish girl"，Darla 带着父亲遗照离开 Sabah。情感：希望 → anguish → resolve。

---

## 节点九（ch09）：Darla 的 film career——"Her portrayal of a woman in a twisted, confusing romance was nothing short of mesmerizing."

> "Her portrayal of a woman in a twisted, confusing romance was nothing short of mesmerizing."（ch09）

Nandhini Google 搜索 Darla（Scarlet Revenge + The Man from My Dreams），致电 Pooja（Pooja 同意帮忙查 HKL），与 Michael 吃鸡饭，Mrs Choi living room 清理（3kg mutton 换 pirated DVD），Nandhini 观看 The Man from My Dreams（Darla mesmerizing，teary-eyed）。情感：frustration → fascination。

---

## 节点十（ch10）：KL 首拍与 Zoomie 到达——"I am Ariyah. Ariyah is me."

> "I am Ariyah. Ariyah is me."（ch10）

Darla KL 首日拍摄 Ariyah（picnic scene + dream world scene with Jack/Donny），Donny 给 Darla 搭车（发梢轻触、心跳加速），Manny 把 Zoomie（SKA5783）从 Sabah 运到 KL。情感：nervous → exhilarated → crush deepening。

---

## 节点十一（ch11）：生日与吻——"I'm not crazy, Michael!"

> "I'm not crazy, Michael!"（ch11）

Nandhini 生日（20 Dec 2023），Michael mutton gift + birthday cake，Michael 走调 "Happy Birthday"，两人共看 The Man from My Dreams（Michael 评 "macam tak masuk akal"，Nandhini 激动辩护 "I'm not crazy, Michael!"），Nandhini 主动吻 Michael，Michael pull away（"I don't want you to do anything you'll regret"），cake comfort + 重看电影。情感：birthday isolation → emotional outburst → self-loathing。

---

## 节点十二（ch12）：怀孕与 raw scene——"Why? Why did it have to be him?"

> "Why? Why did it have to be him?"（ch12）

Darla 发现怀孕（flashback first night with Donny），Donny rejection（"Get rid of it"、"whore like you"），Darla raw final scene（mirror smashing、glass to wrist、"I want Jack … forever"），wrap。情感：excitement → betrayal → despair。

---

## 节点十三（ch14）：家庭破裂与 adoption——"Get rid of it. Then, come back here. Forget about acting."

> "Get rid of it. Then, come back here. Forget about acting."（ch14）

Darla 致电 Mrs Fernandez（"whore"、"get rid of it"、"bastard child"），Manny 发 nephew Joel 照片（Darla 断 SIM），premiere invitation + Donny email（"Did you get rid of it? If you didn't, don't come"，RSVP no），OBGYN Dr Jaspreet 帮助 Darla 决定 adoption，birth of baby girl，giving up baby，Instax photo。情感：anguish → isolation → grief。

---

## 节点十四（ch16）：产后抑郁与 recovery——"You know, your pregnancy."

> "You know, your pregnancy."（ch16）

Darla 产后抑郁，Dr Chong 治疗 + journaling，reviews of The Man from My Dreams → yearning to act，audition for Lily Matius，Donny 通过 David 以 "pregnancy scandal" 拒绝她，Darla breakdown（wailing, banging steering wheel）。情感：recovery → betrayal → despair。

---

## 节点十五（ch18）：Jimmy 救主——"With the paring knife safely tucked away in the kitchen drawer, Darla accepted that not being alone wasn't so bad after all."

> "With the paring knife safely tucked away in the kitchen drawer, Darla accepted that not being alone wasn't so bad after all."（ch18）

Darla 2014 reclusive life，near suicide（paring knife），Jimmy arrival（injured mongrel），Jimmy wormed his way into her heart，paring knife tucked away，"not being alone wasn't so bad after all"。情感：despair → tentative hope。

---

## 节点十六（ch15）：Darla & Paati 在 Covid 病房——"Unfortunately, no. She died two days before Paati did. Respiratory failure. She was only forty years old,"

> "Unfortunately, no. She died two days before Paati did. Respiratory failure. She was only forty years old,"（ch15）

Darla 2021 Covid，drives to hospital，collapses，admitted to Covid ward，meets Lakshmi/Paati，writes note（"Jasmine … no … I need to meet her"），dies of respiratory failure；Lakshmi prays，calls 'Nandhini'。情感：fear → resignation。

---

## 节点十七（ch17）：break-in 与 Michael's love confession——"Because I love you, damn it!"

> "Because I love you, damn it!"（ch17）

Nandhini break into car（unlocked），glove compartment 发现 Darla's KL address + annotated script；break into house，shoe box（photos, adoption papers, diary/Jasmine letter）；Michael 反对（"you have no right to tell Jasmine she's adopted"），confesses love（"Because I love you, damn it!"），Nandhini "Thank you"（worst response），Michael 踢她下车。情感：determination → shock → guilt。

---

## 节点十八（ch19）：Darla's dog Jimmy——"Poor boy, have you been alone all this time?"

> "Poor boy, have you been alone all this time?"（ch19）

Nandhini 回到 Darla's house，cleaning windows，发现 Jimmy（Darla's dog，collar tag "J I M M Y"），"What is it, buddy?"。情感：guilt → empathy。

---

## 节点十九（ch22）：Nandhini 带 Jimmy 回家——"Darla shouldn't be forgotten."

> "Darla shouldn't be forgotten."（ch22）

Nandhini 把 Jimmy 带回家（bath, bed, sleeping through night without dreams），与 Michael 和解（kiss, sex, "I like you. A lot."），nightmare → dream of Paati/Lakshmi（"Darla shouldn't be forgotten"），package 到达（Darla's belongings: beige cardigan, car keys, note "I'm sorry Jasmine. I love you. Forever and always."），Nandhini 开始写下这个故事。情感：guilt → peace。

---

## 节点二十（ch23）：Epilogue——Darla remembered——"Now I know where I get it from. My nose, my complexion, my love for acting. I guess the apple doesn't fall far from the tree, right?"

> "Now I know where I get it from. My nose, my complexion, my love for acting. I guess the apple doesn't fall far from the tree, right?"（ch23）

两年后，Nandhini 的书《Finding Darla》登上 MPH bestsellers list，film screening event，Jasmine（Darla's biological daughter, 18 岁）致电 Nandhini，Nandhini 将 Darla's journal 交给 Jasmine。情感：joy → resolution。
