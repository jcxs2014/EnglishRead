# My Husband's Wife — 金句集

## 六类主题金句（引文均来自 epub 原文）

### 1. 身份与欺骗（Identity & Deception）

**"My husband's wife is an unforgiving woman. It seems strange to talk about myself in third person like that, but it makes it easier for me to come to terms with what happened."**
— Jane 自白，Ch71（书名现身，第三人称自述暗示身份分裂）

**"They think I'm a pushover; so did Eden, so I pushed her right over the edge of the cliff."**
— Jane 自白，Ch71（"pushover"的狠双关，Jane 才是真正的凶手）

---

### 2. 婚姻的阴暗面（The Dark Side of Marriage）

**"I always thought it looked old and creepy built into the cliff—but Eden loves it. Sometimes I wonder if she loves this house more than she loves me."**
— Harrison 对 Spyglass 的感知，Ch20（房子比人重要，婚姻的裂缝）

**"Have you found the car? Have you found her?"**
— Carter 的追问，Ch71（他关心的是"她"还是证据？）

---

### 3. 阶级与权力（Class & Power）

**"A woman matching your wife's description was seen running up to the cliffs this morning."**
— 警官对 Carter，Ch20（身份描述的荒诞性，被看见的"描述"）

---

### 4. 死亡与悬崖（Death & The Cliff）

**"I pushed her off the cliff."**
— Jane 自白，Ch71（全书最直接的告白）

**"It seems strange to talk about myself in third person like that."**
— Jane，Ch71（身份分裂的终极表达）

---

### 5. 真相与调查（Truth & Investigation）

**"Have you found the woman pretending to be Eden?"**
— Harrison 对 Carter，Ch20（谁是冒充者？谁是真实的？）

---

### 6. 受害者与凶手（Victim & Perpetrator）

**"I took care of that bitch when I bumped into her at the waterfall."**
— Jane 自白，Ch71（"took care of"是谋杀的语言，瀑布=作案地点）

**"Sometimes I wonder if she loves this house more than she loves me."**
— Harrison 对 Spyglass 的感知，Ch20（爱房子=爱权力）
