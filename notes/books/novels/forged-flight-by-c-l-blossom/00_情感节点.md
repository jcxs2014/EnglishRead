# 《Forged Flight》情感节点

## 节点一：隔街的注视（ch01–02）

**叙事概括：** 全书的起点不是相遇而是单恋。兽医 Micah 隔着街道偷看铁匠 Lucan 一整年，自认无人知晓；而 Lucan 早把他反复出现的脸看在眼里，只觉得「怪」。两人第一次交谈，要等到一只被遗弃的幼龙登场。

**关键引语：**

> ① "We’ve never actually spoken, but I’ve admired him from afar ever since he moved here a few years ago."（ch01）

> ② "The veterinarian across the street won’t stop staring at me. It’s weird. He’s weird."（ch02）

> ③ "They don’t wonder why the town veterinarian is thirty-five years old and still hopelessly single."（ch01）

## 节点二：枫树下的拾龙（ch01–02）

**叙事概括：** 下班路上，Micah 踩到一片尚有余温的蛋壳，顺着碎片找到一只先天翼膜发育不全的幼龙。他用斗篷把它裹回诊所，取名 Maple，并做出了全书最大胆的决定——为它造一副义翅。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Frowning, I glance down to see a shard of crimson eggshell beneath my boot. I crouch and pick it up, surprised to find it’s still warm."（ch01）

> ② "The membranes never fully formed, leaving behind two undersized, misshapen limbs tucked tightly against its body. I’ve seen the mutation before—in birds, griffins, even wyverns—but never in dragons."（ch01）

> ③ "“Goddess.” The word escapes before I can stop it. “She’s beautiful.” Beside me, Micah smiles. “Yes, she is.” He keeps his gaze on the sleeping dragon. “I want to try prosthetic wings.”"（ch02）

## 节点三：锻造课与铁匠铺的心跳（ch03）

**叙事概括：** Micah 带 X 光片到铁匠铺共商义翅结构，Lucan 提出以残存骨骼为锚点的设计；随后手把手教他抡锤。第一次敲出角度的欢呼，和炉前贴近的体温，让专业合作开始升温。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“What if we anchor the frame here?” He points to the place where the remaining bone structure meets the joint. “If we build the frame around this section, she could use the movement she already has to control it.”"（ch03）

> ② "“No. I know medicine. Not… this.” I gesture toward the forge. “Metal. Mechanics. I could never do what you do.”"（ch03）

> ③ "I strike it a second time. The softened metal gives beneath the blow, bending exactly where Lucan indicated. My eyes widen. “Oh.” I stare at the newly formed angle. “I did it!”"（ch03）

## 节点四：广场上的「他是我的事」（ch04）

**叙事概括：** 偷药兽人把 Micah 提离地面，Lucan 横穿街道护人，脱口说出「他就是我的事」又慌忙找补。冲突平息后的谈话第一次触到国王与龙山的政治阴影。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I suggest you put Dr. Wolfhart down,” I say evenly. “Then return the herbs you stole.”"（ch04）

> ② "“He is my business.” Silence settles over the square. Micah’s eyes widen, surprise flashing across his face as he looks up at me."（ch04）

> ③ "“The ancient treaties exist for a reason, but all he sees is land to conquer. He’s never forgiven me for getting in his way.”"（ch04）

## 节点五：第一次离地与第一次烧毁（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 第一副钢架义翅让 Maple 短暂离地，也随即被它挣脱烧毁。两位照料者在失望里达成共识：概念可行，回去改进。

**关键引语：**

> ① "For one glorious heartbeat, all four of her feet leave the ground. She’s airborne. Only by a few inches, but she’s flying."（ch05）

> ② "She throws herself onto the grass, rolling from side to side in an obvious attempt to take the wings off."（ch05）

> ③ "“It means,” he says thoughtfully. “The concept works.” He studies the damaged wing again. “Now I just need to make it better.”"（ch05）

## 节点六：茶谈与告白（ch06）

**叙事概括：** 一壶茶里交换了母亲的瘟疫、孤儿院的弃婴、被尺寸困住的半生；Maple 的晚饭叫声打断了几乎更进一步的夜晚。Lucan 离开前留下铝架计划与那句「我早就为你神魂颠倒」。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Yes. My mother passed away when I was twelve during the plague.” His expression dims. “All three of us got sick, but only my father and I recovered.”"（ch06）

> ② "“Because nobody else is hiring half-giants. I’m too big for the human world and too small for the giants.”"（ch06）

> ③ "“I’ll get started on the next set of prosthetics tomorrow. I think replacing the steel frame with aluminum will make it much lighter for her.”"（ch06）

## 节点七：酒馆之夜（ch07）

**叙事概括：** 火貂之死击碎了 Micah 的自信，他在酒馆借酒消愁；Lucan 拦酒、扶人、把摔倒的他抱回楼上的家，让出自己的床。这件带着对方气味的旧上衣，成了下一章的定情信物。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Even when the logical part of my brain tells me I did everything I could—that I tried my hardest, that I gave them the best care I possibly could—there’s still a voice in the back of my mind whispering that I could’ve done more."（ch07）

> ② "And despite everything we’d done, I felt it take its final breath beneath my blood-soaked, gloved hands."（ch07）

> ③ "“You can take my bed,” he says as we reach the landing. “I’ll sleep on the sofa tonight.”"（ch07）

## 节点八：真正的飞行（ch08）

**叙事概括：** 铝架义翅加培根训练，Maple 第二次试飞一飞冲天又狼狈滑停；庆祝的拥抱里，两人的身体距离第一次变成相拥。

**关键引语：**

> ① "And suddenly, she rises."（ch08）

> ② "She clears the fence, wobbles for half a heartbeat, then catches the wind and surges forward. With a delighted chirp, she makes a beeline for the woods, her prosthetic wings flashing in the autumn sunlight as she disappears among the orange-topped trees."（ch08）

> ③ "Then I wrap my arms around him, pulling him into my embrace. He fits perfectly against me, his slender body settling into mine like the final piece of a puzzle."（ch08）

## 节点九：山顶的异象与山洞的吻（ch11）

**叙事概括：** 放归变成震惊——几十只畸形幼龙在树下吞食蓝蘑菇；暴风雪把两人逼进山洞，火光里 Lucan 表白、两人初吻。放手 Maple 的计划就此搁置。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Dozens of them. All juveniles or hatchlings with malformed wings."（ch11）

> ② "I was wrong,” I whisper, shaking my head. “I thought Maple was an isolated mutation, but…"（ch11）

> ③ "“In case you haven’t noticed, I’m quite smitten with you.”"（ch11）

## 节点十：农场的闲谈与王的孢子（ch12）

**叙事概括：** 蘑菇农场主的一句「国王买走过一整批孢子」把山上异象变成指向王座的投毒案。同一天，Micah 第一次以「伴侣」介绍 Lucan。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I grow them for culinary use. They aren’t native to this region.”"（ch12）

> ② "“Actually, the king is particularly fond of them. He bought an entire shipment of spores from me last year.”"（ch12）

> ③ "Someone planted those mushrooms on Mount Embercrest with one goal in mind—to wipe out every last dragon."（ch12）

## 节点十一：舞会前夜的藏龙与邀请（ch13）

**叙事概括：** 藏在皮包里的 Maple 撞了吊灯、学了如厕；窗前出神的 Micah 被一句正式邀请接住——去王宫，一起赴那个最危险也最盛大的舞会。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Hush, little one. You have to stay out of sight.”"（ch13）

> ② "“This is where you go potty. Do you understand?”"（ch13）

> ③ "“Micah,” I say softly. “Will you go to the ball with me?”"（ch13）

## 节点十二：书房窃证（ch15–16）

**叙事概括：** 舞会的金碧辉煌与武装守卫之下，两人潜入国王书房撬锁取证；一卷轴、一瓶蓝色粉末，和一场用来脱身的走廊之吻。

**关键引语：**

> ① "accused the king of something so horrible without proper evidence"（ch15）

> ② "“The king’s office.”"（ch16）

> ③ "a glass corked vial full of an blue powder rests inside"（ch16）

## 节点十三：南瓜大会的公审（ch18–19）

**叙事概括：** 当众放出 Maple，一句「我从没提过蘑菇」让国王自曝；卫兵援引古约废黜并逮捕 Castor，南瓜与嘘声送他离场。Micah 说出「我爱你」，两人转身投入下一副义翅。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Guards! Arrest Dr. Wolfhart!”"（ch18）

> ② "“I never said anything about mushrooms, Your Highness.”"（ch19）

> ③ "I love you."（ch19）

## 节点十四：春天与戒指（ch20）

**叙事概括：** 蘑菇清尽、草场开花，戴义翅的幼龙群重新起舞，Maple 飞回扑倒两人。搬进同一屋檐的 Lucan 在锻炉前打下生金——一枚给 Micah 的戒指，全书以 The End 收束。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Maple comes soaring over the trees, her ruby-red scales gleaming in the sunlight."（ch20）

> ② "It was difficult to release her months ago, but we both knew it was the right thing to do."（ch20）

> ③ "A ring for Micah."（ch20）
