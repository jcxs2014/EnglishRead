# Make or Break 情感节点

## 节点一：一个喷嚏打碎浪漫（Chapter One）

**叙事概括：** Steph 在和 Laura 拍亲密戏时一个喷嚏笑料百出，休息时 Laura 抛出"你该去追 Harper"的提议，唤醒了 Steph 压抑多年的暗恋。

**关键引语：**
- "This thing you have with Harper. You should see if you can turn years of flirting into years of…fucking."
- "I wanted that someone to be Harper."

## 节点二：Harper 的"完美高度"（Chapter Two）

**叙事概括：** Harper 回家与 Per 和 Ben 看《North Precinct》，在朋友追问下隐藏对 Steph 的暗恋。她幻想了"完美高度"——适合拥抱、接吻、被抱起来的高度。

**关键引语：**
- "The perfect height for a solid, comforting hug. The perfect height for me to pull her face down a little for a kiss."
- "But I really, really hated being in bed alone."

## 节点三：网球赛——红线事件（Chapter Three）

**叙事概括：** Gabby 宣布慈善网球赛、Harper 将担任裁判。Stepp 和 Harper 在片场有了第一次"非调情"的真正对话，Harper 主动提出陪 Stepp 练球。

**关键引语：**
- "You're the drawcard. The only person fans will be coming to see."
- "Lucky for me, I now had just the person to help me."

## 节点四：Runyon 的"约会"与初吻（Chapter Eleven）

**叙事概括：** Stepp 和 Harper 去 Runyon"跑步"（实际是徒步），Harper 承认自己不会跑步。途中遇到被蛇咬伤的狗，Stepp 扛狗下山。Harper 说"你看起来很辣"，两人第一次接吻。

**关键引语：**
- "It was pretty heroic," Harper said, and it sounded like a correction to what I'd said.
- "I want it to be something that happens again. More than just once again."

## 节点五：沙发上的"朋友"命名（Chapter Twelve）

**叙事概括：** 第二次网球练习后两人再次接吻，回到 Harper 家做三明治、在沙发上热吻。Stepp 离开后 Harper 意识到自己可能爱上了她。

**关键引语：**
- "I'm really hoping you might kiss me again," she murmured.
- "I adored her, because maybe…maybe, I was actually a little bit in love with her."

## 节点六：出柜与创伤（Chapter Fourteen）

**叙事概括：** Harper 的姐姐因吻照来电，Harper 被迫向母亲出柜——母亲说"你父亲在天堂会心碎"。Stepp 赶来安慰，两人相拥而眠，Harper 第一次做小 spoon。

**关键引语：**
- "Mama, with all respect, I don't give a flyin' fuck about what the neighbors and people at your church think of me."
- "I hardly ever got to be the little spoon."

## 节点七：关系确认与恐慌发作（Chapter Sixteen）

**叙事概括：** Stepp 和 Harper 的"关系谈话"——Stepp 听到"relationship"时慌了，但随后承认"我想和你在一起"。两人正式成为情侣。

**关键引语：**
- "Relationship?" Her voice cracked up hilariously on the last syllable of the word.
- "Maybe what I deserve is you trying your best with me," I pointed out.

## 节点八：误会与和解（Chapter Nineteen-Twenty）

**叙事概括：** Laura 在停车场无意中说破"baton"玩笑，Harper 误以为 Stepp 在利用她做 PR。Stepp 带着晚餐和 apple fritter 赶来解释——"baton"只是推动她表白的契机，不是"利用"。两人和解。

**关键引语：**
- "It sounds like you're only dating me to keep your management team happy."
- "Yourself. All of you. The ability to know you, to share your life, all of your life. As it is."

## 节点九：球场上的"baby"（Chapter Twenty-Three）

**叙事概括：** 慈善网球赛。Harper 被球意外击中，Stepp 冲过去喊"baby"通过喇叭公开了恋情。他们赢了比赛，Stepp 的新电影角色确认。

**关键引语：**
- "Oh my god. Baby, are you all right? Are you hurt?"
- "You just told the whole world who you're dating." / "I sure did."

## 节点十：从 love-all 到余生（Epilogue）

**叙事概括：** 四个月后，Stepp 向 Harper 求婚（"搬来和我一起住"），Harper 找 Per 谈话（Per 说"去吧，我等你六年了"）。两人决定同居，Stepp 用网球双关语结尾。

**关键引语：**
- "Move in with me. Officially. Please."
- "As long as you mean love, all day and all night, for the rest of our lives, I'm all for it…"
