# Eat Post Like 情感节点

## 节点 1：求婚前夜（ch01）——最后的晚餐

Capitale 晚宴，James 温柔如常（numbers 论、video 论），Cassie 撒娇如常（running away 的诊断悬而未决）。读者不知道，这是倒计时 24 小时。pang（ch01 原句 6）是唯一的预告：回头一看，心口一刺。

**关键引语**：
- "One can't live on numbers alone," he said.
- "She kissed him goodbye and walked out of the party. Looking back over her shoulder, she felt a pang deep in her chest."

## 节点 2：走廊里的死讯（ch04）——世界断电

红唇高跟的早晨，警察的走廊宣判（collision 全文）。她 kneel 在 polyester 地毯上，rough and plasticky 的触感是世界最后的质地。"I was just with him yesterday"的不可置信，是哀悼的第一课：否认。

**关键引语**：
- "We have some unfortunate news for you, Ms. Brooks. Your fiancé was in a car accident this morning and has died. His taxi was involved in a collision."
- "I was just with him yesterday, she thought. This can't be real."

## 节点 3：公寓开箱（ch05–07）——第二重伤

十几个假身份、John Davis 驾照、267k 粉丝的 NYSD。Olivia 后厨揭盅：间谍剧变美食剧。deceived（ch07）是 grief 之外的第二重伤：丧夫是失去，deceived 是归零——"never knew him at all"。

**关键引语**：
- "The worst part about all of this is that I feel so deceived. It's one thing to lose your fiancé—it's another to realize you never knew him at all."
- "He used disguises so he could dine without special treatment; it was the only way he could fairly judge a restaurant."

## 节点 4：Ruby 点火（ch08）+ 日记立规（ch10）——双发动机

Ruby 的"start posting"是 plot 发动机，James 日记的"One lie"是道德发动机。一推一拉，Cassie 上路：推的是欲望（taste），拉的是恐惧（corrupt）。124→267,569 的解锁是烟花，One lie 是烟花下的引线。

**关键引语**：
- "If you really want a job 'like that,' why couldn't you just start posting? Take yourself out to dinner and copy his technique."
- "One lie can completely corrupt a relationship—how can you build trust after that?"

## 节点 5：巴黎入场（ch12–16）——新世界的门

"Get in, loser"的 Modern 首秀（🔪🔪🔪处女作），凌晨三点的 Viand 来电（European tour），sabbatical 的 HR 假条（"career doesn't love you back"），黑车的安全带咔哒（irony 的 pang），TWA 的"make James proud"。五步上路，每步都是告别（律所/纽约/旧我）与迎接（团队/巴黎/新我）。

**关键引语**：
- "We will be embarking on an all-expenses-paid European tour of a selection of the world's best restaurants, starting in Paris, France."
- "I just want to make James proud."

## 节点 6：Crayères 升温（ch24–28）——糖里有光

火车 wine 宣言、酒窖手背 lingering、patio 三瓶 art 论、Les Crayères 正餐（virgin palate 认证）、清晨双评论（EPL+NYSD 体）、Splenda 早餐、Eamon 当面 hunting。糖度最高的四章，也是秘密最险的四章（David 👀、DM 差评潮）。"virgin palate"是味觉的认证，也是情爱的隐喻。

**关键引语**：
- "Wine tells a story—it speaks to a place, a specific time, and the people who made it."
- "It's almost as though you're coming at it with a virgin palate. Each bite is an epiphany—a whole new world opening in your mind."

## 节点 7：Rome 动摇（ch35–37）——蜜月与裂缝

Vespa 半日游的 slalom 惊魂（James 闪回）与手心吻（hold tighter）和解，Pantheon/Forum/gelato 三吻把感情推上顶峰；但 Ben 的 fling 论与 David 的 LAST WARNING 同时杀到，Il Pagliaccio 之夜她第一次问：是爱情，还是旅途幻觉？"my story is being written"与"I too have been reborn"是动摇中的锚。

**关键引语**：
- "I just wanted you to hold on tighter to me."
- "That version of the story was never meant to be, and instead another story, my story, is being written."

## 节点 8：Venice 处刑（ch39）+ 纽约辞职（ch40）——死与生

红裙 gala 的 clarity（不想回 law、第一次高兴）被 David 当众处刑（错号 clipper、"stole"定罪）粉碎；她跑路回 NY，缩回 navy suit，却在 Ruby 三问、Kelly 召回（deserve）、David 和解（proud）中复活，并在 Bryce 的 9am 考卷前说出"I quit. Right now!"。死的是旧我（lawyer），生的是新我（critic）。

**关键引语**：
- "Or should I put it this way—you stole his fucking identity."
- "Yes. I quit. Right now!"
- "You deserve to be here."

## 节点 9：Barcelona 归位（ch41–42）——自首与启程

Kelly 听证（taste 自首+untrained 赦免+one last review）、浴场洗礼（Time 不存在+move forward）、Moments 自首（forbidden fruit+burnout+same thing 判语+alive 告白）、清晨 offer（Portugal）、机场 Stop 系（unapologetically）、换票（不回家）。"seeing all of me"（被看见）与"see you on the internet"（线上见）是终点的两扇门：爱有处安放，人有处可去。

**关键引语**：
- "It was time to move forward."
- "Thank you for seeing all of me."
- "I'll see you on the internet."
