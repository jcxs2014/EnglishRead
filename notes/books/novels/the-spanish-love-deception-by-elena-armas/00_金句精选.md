# 金句精选

> 1. "We weren't friends. We barely tolerated each other, Aaron Blackford and I. We were spiteful to each other, pointed out each other's mistakes, criticized how differently we worked, thought, and lived. We condemned our differences."
>
> 我们不是朋友。我们几乎互相忍不了， Aaron Blackford 和我。我们对彼此充满恶意，互相挑刺，批评对方的工作方式、思维方式和生活方式。我们憎恶彼此的差异。

---

> 2. "Then, take me. I will be your date to the wedding, Catalina."
>
> 那就带上我吧。我会做你姐姐婚礼的男伴， Catalina 。

---

> 3. "So, you want me"—I pointed at myself—"to pretend to be your date?"
>
> 所以你是想让我——假扮你的约会对象？

---

> 4. "All right. Okay. Good. So, we have a deal."
>
> 好吧。好。成交。

---

> 5. "No, she was wrong. Everybody thought they understood. Nobody did. They didn't realize that all those pobrecita, poor little Linas, accompanied by all those pitiful looks and nods… were the reasons why I had lied to my family. Why I wanted to crawl out of my skin at the prospect of showing up alone."
>
> 不，妈妈错了。每个人都以为他们理解。没有人真的理解。他们不知道那些"可怜的 Lina"、那些怜悯的眼神和点头——正是我向家人撒谎的原因。正是那种独自出席、恨不得钻进地缝的感觉，让我做出了那个决定。

---

> 6. "You fainted, Catalina. That's really far from being fine. Eat this."
>
> 你晕倒了， Catalina 。这可完全算不上"没事"。把这个吃了。

---

> 7. "Because when I finally take those lips in mine, it will be the furthest thing from pretending. I will not be showing you what it would be like if you were mine. I'll show you what it is."
>
> 因为当我最终吻上你的双唇，那将是最不像假装的事。我不会在演示"如果你属于我"会是什么感觉。我会直接让你知道那是什么感觉。

---

> 8. "Is this … still pretending? Is this just for practice?"
>
> 这……还是在假装吗？这只是在练习吗？

---

> 9. "I want your heart, Catalina. I want it for myself, just how I have given you mine."
>
> 我想要你的心， Catalina 。就像我把我的心给你一样，我想要你的心只属于我。

---

> 10. "What are we doing?… Is this … still pretending?… Is this just for practice?"
>
> 我们在做什么？……这……还是在假装吗？……这只是在练习吗？

---

> 11. "If you were really mine, I'd do this all the time."
>
> 如果你真的属于我，我会一直这样。

---

> 12. "You feel and taste like you are mine."
>
> 你的感觉、你的味道，都像是属于我的。

---

> 13. "I'm happy you did… It's too bad you didn't think of throwing in more."
>
> 我很高兴你这么做了……可惜你没再多来几次。

---

> 14. "I love seeing your involvement."
>
> 我很喜欢看到你这么投入。

---

> 15. "I need you to trust in us, in me. Can you do that?"
>
> 我需要你相信我们，相信我。你能做到吗？

---

> 16. "I can't make you love me enough to give us a chance."
>
> 我没法让你爱得足够深，给我们一个机会。

---

> 17. "I trust you. I love you."
>
> 我相信你。我爱你。

---

> 18. "You are stuck with me, Catalina."
>
> 你甩不掉我了， Catalina 。

---

> 19. "I love you, Aaron. You shouldn't be alone—ever. And I am the one meant to be with you. Here. Holding your hand."
>
> 我爱你， Aaron 。你永远不该独自一人。而我是那个注定要陪在你身边的人。在这里。握着你的手。

---

> 20. "I was so stupid to let you walk away. So, so dumb. But I got lost in my head. I was so scared, Aaron."
>
> 我让你走开，真是太蠢了。太傻了。但我迷失在自己的脑子里了。我太害怕了， Aaron 。

---

> 21. "If you want to talk about whatever happened, then we'll do that. And if you don't, then we'll talk about something else. But I want you to relax. Just for a few minutes."
>
> 如果你想谈发生了什么，我们就谈。如果不想，我们就聊别的。但我想让你放松。就放松几分钟。

---

> 22. "I'll fight for that. I'd fight for him like I hadn't when he asked me to. I wouldn't take no for an answer. He was stuck with me."
>
> 我会为此而战。我会像他当初要求我那样，为他奋力争取。我不会接受否定答案。他跑不掉了。

---

> 23. "You are very close to driving me out of my mind."
>
> 你快要让我彻底疯狂了。

---

> 24. "When I finally kiss you, there won't be any doubt in your mind that it is real."
>
> 当我最终吻你的时候，你心里不会有任何怀疑——那是真的。
