# 《Yellow Wife》概述

## 全书梗概

**一八五〇年的弗吉尼亚，贝尔种植园。** 十七岁的 Pheby Delores Brown 是女奴 Ruth 的女儿——一个认得满月、识得百草的药娘。妈妈信月亮，也信手里的草能救人：Mama believed that the full moon was the most fertile night of the month, and that everything she touched held God’s power.（ch01） 母女俩的活路，一半缝在针线里，一半长在林子里。主人 Jacob Bell 恩威并施：让女主人 Delphina 咬定她是笨丫头，又许下生日后送她去北方上学——这一句承诺，她攥了整整十八年。

**妈妈用身体给女儿铺路，女儿用沉默保住自己。** 每个满月夜提灯采药之外，妈妈与主人的交易从不明说，只留下窗台上的一支蜡烛作信号：
- After that night, we came up with a signal. Candle in the window meant clear to come up. No candle, keep walking.（ch02）
- 她把「我做的一切都是为了你」说得像遗嘱：“Ain’t many choices for a slave woman. Just know everything I do is for you. I’ma die a slave. I knows that. But you, baby, you are meant to see freedom. I’s makin’ sure.”（ch02）
- 而 Pheby 学会的第一课是妈妈的处世法——「随叫随到，又视而不见」：Mama always said the way to keep peace with white folks was to be available and invisible at the same time.（ch01）
十八岁生日前，她只许自己等。

**等待在一场葬礼上被拦腰斩断。** 主人南下查尔斯顿后，女主人强占了 Pheby 的恋人 Essex，又以诬告相挟；妈妈归来的马车翻在 Jamestown，坏疽夺走了她——Pheby 在同一天替女主人接生下一个浅肤色的男婴，又在河边看女主人亲手溺死他。母亲咽气前没能说完的话成了她的遗训：
- She lifted her mouth and breathed into my ear. “’Member who you are Pheby… Delores Brown.”（ch07）
- 而她在葬礼当天被 Snitch 拖上囚车，女主人把她当 fancy girl 卖掉。囚车上她以女王的血脉下咒：“I curse you and all of your unborn children in the name of my grandmother, Queen Vinnie Brown. May all your worst fears come to pass, and all the evil you do come back on you tenfold. This plantation will be your living hell. Mark my words.”（ch09）

**里士满的监狱，先给她地狱，再给她一只手。** Lapier 监狱的地窖里她踩着粪便、结识了 Matilda；拍卖台上她拒绝脱衣——Something shifted inside of me. I had never been whipped in my life, but I had been snatched from my home, lost my mama and my truest love, traveled on foot in ropes for days, starved, slept in the equivalent of a hog pen with feces up to my ankles. Nothing else scared me. I would not take off my dress in front of these men. I would not follow Matilda into that back room. I would not sink further into degradation than I already had. They would have to kill me first, and I stood with my feet grounded, preparing to put up a good fight.（ch11） 一声「I said she is mine」把她从砧板上救下。买主 Rubin Lapier 就是监狱的主人：银顶针、白裙子、客厅的钢琴，一样样递到她面前。她渐渐看清这份「恩宠」的定价——它要求的不是感激，是床与身体：We would only be separated in the evening. The split had been ordered to give the Jailer full access to my body at night.（ch17）

**她在地狱里建起自己的档案与药房。** 白天替 fancy girls 上妆（「像她这样的穆拉托女孩，是上等货」——“Mulatto girls like her are a fancy breed.” He eyed me up and down and then it hit me.（ch15）），夜里把每个过手女孩的名字、来历、说过的话写进 Miss Sally 送的日记；这本册子先记着妈妈的药方：
- “I traced the route north for you last night. Traveling by dark and hiding during the day would be your best defense against the slave catchers.”（ch05）
- 后记着女孩们的血。身孕显形时她问自己怎么办，亡灵般的声音替妈妈回答：I was going to be a mother whether I liked it or not. But how? In this foreign place? Alone without my mama to guide me?（ch12）
- 儿子 Monroe 出生，母职让她做出全书最冷的决定——It was time for me to become my own savior. As much as the very thought of letting the Jailer touch me repulsed me, as shattered as my heart would be to give up on Essex, the need to protect my child outweighed everything else. My days as a girl were gone. Now I had to think like a woman.（ch16）

**七年之间，孩子被逐个夺走，自由的票价一路上涨。** 儿子被逐出大宅那天，她把妈妈的遗训原句渡给下一代：
- “You his slave in name only, never in your mind, boy. You are meant to see freedom. It is my solemn promise to you.”（ch23）
- 婴儿 Bin 没能熬过产褥热——God did not show him favor.（ch24）
- 妹妹般的 July 因她的善心被抵债卖掉——“There is no such thing as family, only business.”（ch35）
- 而 Essex 的消息从北方的风里传来：他被捕，将押回弗吉尼亚，公开展刑。她终于说出那句判决：With Basil having run, the Jailer would be ruthless. He had been merciless in his punishment before. But now, there was no telling what he would do. God help us.（ch26）

**营救是四个女人与一个男孩的合谋。** 她用主人的贪婪当杠杆（把「病秧子卖不出钱」递到他耳边——“Elsie said he stopped eating. Cannot make money off a sickly nigger.” I leaned in and kissed him.（ch39）），用 Abbie 传信、Tommy 挖洞、干草放火调虎离山；河上小船接应，珍珠项链买下第四张船票。父亲与儿子在岸边相认，而她转身走回监狱——「总得有人留下来，当那只羔羊。」桥上迎面而来的另一支锁链队，走着她七年前的来路：I strolled back toward the Lapier jail as another coffle was led across the bridge. They came the same way I had come. The same way it would always be, until enough hearts had the courage to change. As long as there was breath, there was hope.（ch40）

**尾声是两封信。** 战火烧了里士满，也烧塌了牢笼：监狱改名 God's Half Acre，女儿们以白人的身份生活在马萨诸塞，小女儿 Birdie 留在岸上教自由儿童认字；Essex 死在加拿大的自由里，Monroe 在安大略有了自己的家。她留给女儿的最后一句，仍是那套传了三代的话：Remember you are free. Free to choose. Free to live. Free to love.（ch41）

## 三个主题

**一、「你是我的」的两种版本。** 同一句占有宣言，在马厩里是情话，在监狱里是地契：“I will be here the whole time. No one will lay a finger on you. You are mine.” He smiled.（ch19） 这本书的全部张力，都在教读者分辨「被爱」与「被持有」的价差——前者住暗号与项链，后者住账本与鞭子。

**二、教育是违禁品，也是武器。** 妈妈警告识字会被剁手指，她偏把药方抄进日记、把课开进暗处；被鞭打到流产的识字者的血，没能吓住她，只让她更懂「把学问藏好」的工艺。这条线在女儿 Birdie 身上完成 daylight 化：教自由儿童认字成了「上帝派给她的使命」。

**三、留下的人，也是行动的一部分。** 全书最痛的一步棋是把自己算出局外：她放走儿子、爱人、妹妹与盟友，自己转身回监——因为总得有人当那只羔羊。这不是牺牲的修辞，是冷算术：四个人的命对一条命，她替所有人做了这道题。而结尾的「只要还有一口气，就有希望」说明她从未把留下当作终点。

## 人物弧光

**Pheby Delores Brown**——

- 起点：Mama always said the way to keep peace with white folks was to be available and invisible at the same time.（ch01）——十七岁，学会了把自己调成「隐形」。
- 转向：Something shifted inside of me. I had never been whipped in my life, but I had been snatched from my home, lost my mama and my truest love, traveled on foot in ropes for days, starved, slept in the equivalent of a hog pen with feces up to my ankles. Nothing else scared me. I would not take off my dress in front of these men. I would not follow Matilda into that back room. I would not sink further into degradation than I already had. They would have to kill me first, and I stood with my feet grounded, preparing to put up a good fight.（ch11）——拍卖台上第一次以「不肯」立身。
- 最低点：I felt worse than I did when I was sold, when I had lost Essex, worse even than how I felt when mama died. This one pierced me down in a place deep, and the pain kept hemorrhaging.（ch24）——丧子，把毕生的苦难排了序。
- 落点：“Someone has to stay behind and be the lamb.”（ch40）——「总得有人留下来，当那只羔羊。」
- 她的觉醒不是一次顿悟，而是一串连锁反应：每一次「something shifted」都由他人的灾难或出走来推动。

**Essex Henry**——

- 起点：“You mine, Pheby. If I gotta run to be with you, then so be it.”（ch02）——「你是我的。要逃就逃。」
- 中段：I replayed his hard body moving against mine in the stables until I could smell him in front of me. Pretending to be in his arms proved to be the only thing that eased my anxiety and allowed me a little peace.（ch10）——他是她失眠夜里唯一的镇痛剂。
- 落点：“I love you, Pheby. You still mine.” He hummed a slow ballad in my ear. Our hips thrust in unison until we could no longer hold the tempo. I rested my head against his mouth and bit my lip to keep our secret between us.（ch33）——铁链哗啦声里的重逢与誓言。
- 他一生只做对一件事：把自己变成她找得到的坐标——学校、街角、马厩的名字。

**Rubin Lapier / the Jailer**——

- 起点：“I said she is mine.” The man’s eyes dared the speaker to question him again, and he did not.（ch11）——三句话买下一个人，从不解释。
- 中段：Moments ago, I’d been the mother of his firstborn, mistress of the jail; now I was reduced to the woman across the hall while he bedded another. I placed a chair against my door to bar him from entering, then searched for sleep.（ch20）——宴会上的「女主人」与隔墙的床声。
- 落点：Officially, he died of an attack of cholera, which held on to him for ten days. Though I think he died of heartbreak, as a result of the war.（ch41）——「官方说法是霍乱；而我认为他死于心碎。」

**Mama / Ruth Brown**——

- 起点：“Ain’t many choices for a slave woman. Just know everything I do is for you. I’ma die a slave. I knows that. But you, baby, you are meant to see freedom. I’s makin’ sure.”（ch02）——用身体垫付女儿的明天。
- 落点：She lifted her mouth and breathed into my ear. “’Member who you are Pheby… Delores Brown.”（ch07）——遗言断在「你不属于任何人」的半截上，这句话被传了三代。

## 叙事与技法

第一人称回顾体；双线「承诺的日落」（Master 之诺）与「肉体的过户」（Jailer 之得）交替收紧；口传方言（Mama/Elsie/July 的语法）承担全书的抒情与档案功能；物件系统（蜡烛、半颗心、日记、红裙、银顶针、赞美诗）串起十一年的记忆链——每一件遗物的转手，都是一次身份的交接。
