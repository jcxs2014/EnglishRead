# ch49 Epilogue

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：Saddle Street, October 23, 1888；Sam 以 thew 观察
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Mamá (Camille) / Cris / Clem Quinn / Roy Marcel / Wister Amsel
- **核心事件**：Sam 写 memoir；Clem visits；new portal discovered in Lawrence；Sam & Clem go to Lawrence；Sam & Clem's romance
- **悬念钩子**：Hornsby's return? Cisneros Society's regroup? Sam & Clem's future?

---

> **原句 1:** "It was late October, and the mornings had dawned with frost for three days in a row. The window of my room on Saddle Street was laced with ice, so much that it obscured my view of the street."

**中文理解**：那是十月末，接连三个早晨都结着霜；Saddle Street 上他那间屋子的窗子爬满冰花，厚得看不清街面。

**句子结构**：首句主系表是 It was late October，and the mornings had dawned with frost for three days in a row 并列；第二句主系表是 The window of my room on Saddle Street was laced with ice，so much that it obscured my view of the street 是结果状语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一窗冰花写回到起点的安静。故事从这条街开始，最后也停在这里，季节的转换替全书收尾。

---

> **原句 2:** "I hadn't slept, of course; a spirit never does. I used the nights for writing. While the house was quiet, while Saddle Street was quiet, I penned page after page."

**中文理解**：他当然没睡——灵体从不睡；他把夜用来写字。趁着屋里静、街上也静，他一页一页地写。

**句子结构**：首句用分号连接 I hadn't slept, of course 与 a spirit never does 两个主谓句；第二句主谓宾是 I used the nights for writing；末句用两个 While 引导的时间状语从句（While the house was quiet, while Saddle Street was quiet），主句是 I penned page after page。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一页一页写交代这本回忆录的来历。读者至此才知道手上的故事是谁、在什么时候写下的。

---

> **原句 3:** "It was pleasing to give order to the past in this way, to see the circumstances take shape in a manner that would exist outside my own head. Perhaps, even, in a manner that would interest a reader."

**中文理解**：这样把往事理出头绪是件愉快的事：看着那些经过成形，落到自己脑子以外去；也许，还能让某个读者觉得有意思。

**句子结构**：主系表结构（It was pleasing to give order to the past in this way），it 是形式主语；随后两个不定式短语（to see the circumstances take shape in a manner that would exist outside my own head 与 Perhaps, even, in a manner that would interest a reader）作真正的成分，各带 that 定语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用也许还能让某个读者觉得有意思写叙述者的私心。前面那些沉重的场面，此刻被一句话轻轻收进写字的乐趣里。

---

> **原句 4:** "I doubt you wish to hear of yet a third tearful reunion, so I will only tell you that it was on the day following our duel with the Cisneros Society, and Clementine entered the shops on Saddle Street before me to explain the circumstances."

**中文理解**：他说料想读者不愿再听第三场哭哭啼啼的重逢，所以就只说：那是在他们与 Cisneros Society 决斗之后的第二天，Clementine 先他一步走进 Saddle Street 的店里，把事情解释清楚。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I doubt you wish to hear of yet a third tearful reunion，you wish to hear 是省略 that 的宾语从句；第二句用 so I will only tell you that… 引出宾语从句，从句里 it was on the day following our duel with the Cisneros Society 是主系表，and Clementine entered the shops on Saddle Street before me to explain the circumstances 并列。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句替读者省去把重逢略过。叙述者懂得什么时候不该写，这份节制让结尾不腻。

---

> **原句 5:** "I put down my pen and drifted down the stairs. There was Clem, leaning against the doorframe, wearing her enchantment trousers, as she called them. She smiled at me as I drifted into the room. I gave her a wink and the smile broadened."

**中文理解**：他搁下笔，飘下楼去。Clem 靠门框站着，穿着她自己叫附魔裤的那条裤子；他飘进屋时她朝他笑，他眨了下眼，那笑就更开了。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I put down my pen and drifted down the stairs；第二句是 There was 结构，leaning against the doorframe 与 wearing her enchantment trousers 是两个分词短语，as she called them 是插入的定语从句；末段用 She smiled at me as I drifted into the room 起头，I gave her a wink and the smile broadened 并列。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一条裤子的名字写日子已经平常。前面她为穿裤子冒过被逐出校门的险，此刻它只是个家常称呼。

---

> **原句 6:** "It was wondrous to see the change in her now that her mother had returned. She frowned less; she rested more; sometimes she even laughed."

**中文理解**：母亲回来之后她的变化看着叫人高兴：皱眉少了，歇得多了，有时还会笑。

**句子结构**：主系表结构（It was wondrous to see the change in her），now that her mother had returned 是原因状语从句；末句用分号连接三个并列主谓句（She frowned less; she rested more; sometimes she even laughed）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用三个越来越轻的动作写复原。没有大转折，只有一点点松开，这样的结局比喜讯更可信。

---

> **原句 7:** "Our work for the Sor Juana Alliance had many parts. In his guise as constable, Roy continued tracking down members of the Cisneros Society who had participated in what all of the commonwealth now called 'the séance crimes,' so named for their exposure by Clem at the Grand Séance."

**中文理解**：他们替 Sor Juana Alliance 做的事有好几桩：Roy 仍以警官的身份追查那些参与过「降神会罪案」的 Cisneros Society 成员——这名字是因为 Clem 在那场大降神会上把它们揭出来才有的。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 Our work for the Sor Juana Alliance had many parts；第二句主谓宾是 Roy continued tracking down members of the Cisneros Society，In his guise as constable 是方式状语，who had participated in what all of the commonwealth now called 'the séance crimes' 是定语从句加 what 宾语从句，so named for their exposure by Clem at the Grand Séance 是过去分词短语作补充。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一桩罪案得了个名字写舆论的转向。Clem 那天当众做的事，如今成了这桩案子的正式称呼。

---

> **原句 8:** "I was not sure their disgrace would last. Already Roy had told us of a certain reluctance among his fellow officers of criminal enchantment to pursue those members of Cisneros who had not personally acquired thews from the stoning deaths."

**中文理解**：Sam 不敢说他们的失势能撑多久：Roy 已经说过，犯罪附魔科的同行里有些人不愿去追那些没有亲手从石击命案里得到灵体的 Cisneros 成员。

**句子结构**：首句主系表是 I was not sure their disgrace would last，their disgrace would last 是宾语从句；第二句主谓宾是 Roy had told us of a certain reluctance，among his fellow officers of criminal enchantment 是后置定语，to pursue those members of Cisneros 是同位说明，who had not personally acquired thews from the stoning deaths 是定语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句不确定把胜利的成色说实。坏人并未全倒，读者由此明白这只是一次挫折而不是终局。

---

> **原句 9:** "It means we must ensure that the portal is secure, or Cisneros will try to seize it."

**中文理解**：Sam 说：这意味着他们得确保那个 portal 安全，否则 Cisneros 会来抢。

**句子结构**：主谓宾是 It means，that 引导宾语从句；从句里用 or 引出否定结果，Cisneros will try to seize it 是主谓宾。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句判断把新任务说出来。故事没有停在团圆上，还有一扇门要守，结尾因此留了余地。

---

> **原句 10:** "A portal has been discovered."

**中文理解**：Clem 说：发现了一个 portal。

**句子结构**：被动语态的主谓句，用现在完成时表结果，全句五词。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用五个词开启尾声的新事。前面刚收束了旧案，此处一句话又把故事往前推。

---

> **原句 11:** "I had been meaning to address the matter for some time, and now, with two hours ahead and then a potential confrontation with Cisneros men, there seemed to be no better moment. 'Clementine,' I began gently, 'I think you know that I will forever be glad to remain by your side, but I feel that I must remind you of the difference between us, and…'"

**中文理解**：这件事他早就想提，如今前面有两个钟头、之后可能还要对上 Cisneros 的人，再没有比这更合适的时机；他温和地开口，说 Clem 想必知道他会永远乐意待在她身边，但他觉得必须提醒两人之间的差别，还有……

**句子结构**：首句用过去完成进行时（I had been meaning to address the matter for some time）；第二句用 and now 起头，with two hours ahead and then a potential confrontation with Cisneros men 是 with 复合结构，there seemed to be no better moment 是 there be 句型；末段引语用 Clementine 作称呼，I began gently 是插入语，I think you know that… 后接宾语从句，but I feel that I must remind you of the difference between us, and… 以省略号留白。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句说不完的话写他的犹疑。他想替对方留退路，却把自己逼到尴尬处，人物的厚道全在这段结结巴巴里。

---

> **原句 12:** "Clem cut me off. 'You think I'm not aware that you are a spirit?' She looked up at me, her gaze as direct and unflinching as ever, full of corazón. / 'Of course I know that you're aware…' I said helplessly. / 'Then trust that I act with that awareness.' / 'But Clem…' I reached out with my other hand and ran a thumb across her cheek. 'As a spirit there is only so much I can give you.' / She smiled up at me. 'I know. And that is what I want. Only what you can give, nothing more.' She held my gaze, and I tried to find in the depths of her eyes some reason to pull back, to deny myself and her the bond that tethered us. I couldn't find it. I dipped my head to kiss her."

**中文理解**：Clem 打断他，问他难道以为她不知道他是个灵体；她要他相信她是在清楚这一点的情况下做的。Sam 仍说作为灵体他能给的只有那么些；她抬头笑着说她知道，而那正是她要的——只给他能给的那些，不多要。她迎着他的目光，他想在她眼里找一条退回去的理由，好让他和她都断开这根系着的线，却找不到。于是他低下头去吻她。

**句子结构**：引语 You think I'm not aware that you are a spirit? 是反问句，后接 She looked up at me 与同位语 her gaze as direct and unflinching as ever, full of corazón；随后引语 Of course I know that you're aware… 与 Then trust that I act with that awareness 交替，I said helplessly 是说话标签；再接引语 But Clem… 与 As a spirit there is only so much I can give you，I reached out with my other hand and ran a thumb across her cheek 是伴随动作；末段引语由 I know、And that is what I want、Only what you can give, nothing more 三句构成，随后 She held my gaze 与 I tried to find in the depths of her eyes some reason to pull back, to deny myself and her the bond that tethered us 并列，末句 I couldn't find it. I dipped my head to kiss her 收束。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用找不到退回去的理由写他的放手。他一直是替别人着想的那个，此刻第一次为自己做决定，全书的情感线在这里落地。

---

> **原句 13:** "I had the strangest sensation, as I felt her pressed against me, that the warmth I felt from her hands and lips was not hers alone, but shared: that somehow, her touch brought life to me, and that between us there was, in fact, no difference at all."

**中文理解**：他感到一种最奇异的触觉：她贴着他时，从她的手和唇传来的温度不单是她的，而是两人共有的；仿佛她的触碰给了他生机，而他们之间其实毫无分别。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I had the strangest sensation，as I felt her pressed against me 是插入的时间状语从句，that the warmth was not hers alone, but shared 是同位从句，the warmth I felt from her hands and lips 中 I felt 是省略 that 的定语从句；末句用两个 that 从句并列（that somehow, her touch brought life to me 与 that between us there was, in fact, no difference at all）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用毫无分别回答全书关于差别的争论。人、灵体、男人、女人，最后都被一句触觉抹平。

---

> **原句 14:** "'Ah!' she exclaimed, leaning forward with excitement. 'We've reached North Street. Excellent. Now we can speed up. Girar con prisa,' she called happily to the barouche, and the wheels obligingly turned faster, carrying us onward along the smooth thoroughfare toward our destination."

**中文理解**：她兴奋地前倾，喊了一声到了 North Street，说可以快起来了，便高高兴兴地朝那辆车念出咒词；轮子听话地转得更快，载着他们沿着平坦的大道往目的地去。

**句子结构**：引语 Ah! 是感叹句，后接 she exclaimed, leaning forward with excitement 的说话标签；第二段引语由 We've reached North Street、Excellent、Now we can speed up、Girar con prisa 四个短句构成，后接 she called happily to the barouche；末句用 and the wheels obligingly turned faster 起头，carrying us onward along the smooth thoroughfare toward our destination 是分词短语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句兴冲冲的咒语收尾。全书从一辆马车上的死亡开始，最后落在一辆马车上向前的速度里，首尾就这样接上。
