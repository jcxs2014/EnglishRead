# ch44 Sam: The September Séance

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 29 日，Salem Lyceum；Sam 以 thew 观察
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn / Corbin (spirit) / 17 ropers / Cassandra / Principa / Juno / Cisneros Steel (Urco)
- **核心事件**：Clem & Sam at Grand Séance；Sam summons Corbin & 17 ropers who name enchanters; Cisneros Steel creates Urco (horned wolf)
- **悬念钩子**：Urco's threat？Cisneros Society's response？Clem's plan?

---

> **原句 1:** "The Salem Lyceum has an upper balcony and a ground floor. Seats on the ground floor are reserved for enchanters and their spirit thews."

**中文理解**：Sam 说 Salem Lyceum 有楼上包厢和底层；底层的座位留给附魔师和他们的灵体奴仆。

**句子结构**：两个主谓宾并列（The Salem Lyceum has an upper balcony and a ground floor 与 Seats on the ground floor are reserved for enchanters and their spirit thews），后者用被动语态。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用座位安排交代等级。读者还没见到人，就已经知道谁坐哪儿，这一场降神会的门第规矩由此立住。

---

> **原句 2:** "Since we were new to the Lyceum and unfamiliar to the manager, we'd been slated to summon last."

**中文理解**：因为他们初来 Lyceum，经理也不认识他们，所以被排在最后一场召唤。

**句子结构**：Since we were new to the Lyceum and unfamiliar to the manager 是原因状语从句，主句用被动语态 we'd been slated to summon last。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用排在最后写他们的处境。压轴既是劣势也是优势——全场的注意力都会落到最后那一场上。

---

> **原句 3:** "The wait seemed interminable. Finally, a little past two, a reedy, bewhiskered man with ink marks on his fingers opened the door and announced our turn."

**中文理解**：等待漫长得像没有尽头；两点刚过，一个瘦削、蓄着胡须、手指上沾着墨迹的男人推门进来，通知他们上场。

**句子结构**：首句主系表（The wait seemed interminable）；第二句用 Finally 起头，a little past two 是时间状语，主谓宾是 a reedy, bewhiskered man opened the door and announced our turn，with ink marks on his fingers 是介词短语作后置定语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用漫长得像没有尽头写候场的煎熬。随后一个沾着墨迹的小人物推门进来，紧张被一个具体的动作打断。

---

> **原句 4:** "Clementine and I were given a small room backstage in which to prepare for the séance. Clementine was outfitted as she had been at Commonwealth: as a trim young man with a tidy beard. I was disguised as a female thew of middle age, unremarkable in appearance, with respectable clothes, a loose bun, and pudgy cheeks."

**中文理解**：他们被安排在后台一间小屋里准备。Clementine 又扮成在 Commonwealth 时的样子——一个收拾得干净、留着整齐胡须的年轻男子；Sam 则扮成一个中年的女灵体奴仆，相貌平平，衣着体面，挽着松髻，两颊圆胖。

**句子结构**：首句用被动语态加 in which to prepare for the séance 的定语从句；第二句主系表是 Clementine was outfitted as she had been at Commonwealth，as a trim young man with a tidy beard 是同位补充；末句主系表是 I was disguised as a female thew of middle age，unremarkable in appearance 与 with respectable clothes, a loose bun, and pudgy cheeks 是并列补充。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一副最不起眼的样子写 Sam 的伪装。前面 Clem 的男装已经够冒险，此处他选的是最平庸的中年妇人，藏拙的心思全在这份描写里。

---

> **原句 5:** "I found myself nervous as we waited in the cramped room backstage."

**中文理解**：在后台那间逼仄的小屋里等候时，他发现自己紧张起来。

**句子结构**：主谓宾是 I found myself nervous，as we waited in the cramped room backstage 是时间状语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用发现自己紧张写他的自觉。灵体已不必呼吸，却仍会怯场，这一句把他与活人的距离拉近。

---

> **原句 6:** "Clementine stood beside the table and raised her arms. 'In the Salem courthouse,' she said, 'only a few miles from here, the Honorable Brice Richardson issued an invitation to a young woman named Clementine Quinn that was also a challenge.'"

**中文理解**：Clementine 站在桌旁举起双臂，说就在几英里外的 Salem 法庭上，受人敬重的 Brice Richardson 法官向一个名叫 Clementine Quinn 的年轻女子发出了一份邀请——那同时也是一道挑战。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 Clementine stood beside the table and raised her arms；引语内用 In the Salem courthouse 作地点状语，only a few miles from here 是补充，主谓宾是 the Honorable Brice Richardson issued an invitation，to a young woman named Clementine Quinn 是间接宾语，that was also a challenge 是定语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用当众复述判决把私人恩怨变成公共事件。她要的不是同情，而是让满场的人一起作证。

---

> **原句 7:** "I allowed my eyes to close. We had perfected the summoning over the last several days, and Clementine's strength had grown apace. I found the way open before me at once, and I reached with my consciousness into the mist, plunging forward with the knowledge that I was expected on the other side."

**中文理解**：Sam 闭上眼。他们把召唤练熟了，Clementine 的气力也日渐长进；他立刻发现面前的路开着，便以意识探入雾中，向前扎进去，心里知道那边有人在等他。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I allowed my eyes to close；第二句用过去完成时写练习与长进（We had perfected the summoning over the last several days, and Clementine's strength had grown apace）；末句用 and 连接 I found the way open before me at once 与 I reached with my consciousness into the mist，plunging forward 是分词短语，with the knowledge that I was expected on the other side 是介词短语加同位从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用有人在等他写这次召唤的性质。它不是施法而是赴约，前面那些练习都为了这一刻不迟到。

---

> **原句 8:** "Corbin, I thought. The mist cleared, and I found myself plummeting through the higher reaches of the spirit realm, plunging toward an assembly of spirits who had gathered there to await my arrival."

**中文理解**：Sam 在心里叫了一声 Corbin。雾散了，他发现自己正穿过灵界的高处急速下坠，扑向一群聚在那里等他到来的灵体。

**句子结构**：首句 Corbin, I thought 是主谓宾倒置；第二句用 and 连接 The mist cleared 与 I found myself plummeting，through the higher reaches of the spirit realm 是地点状语，plunging toward an assembly of spirits 是分词短语，who had gathered there to await my arrival 是定语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一个名字召来整群人。他要找的是一个旧识，来的却是一支队伍，这一章的规模由此撑开。

---

> **原句 9:** "'Do you have anything else to tell us, Mr. Corbin?' / 'I do,' Corbin said with a grave bow. 'I wish the people of Salem to know that in the last several months, five women have been murdered so that five enchanters could take them as thews.'"

**中文理解**：Clementine 问 Corbin 还有没有别的话要讲；Corbin 郑重地一鞠躬，说他希望 Salem 的人知道：近几个月里有五个女人被杀害，只为让五个附魔师把她们收作灵体奴仆。

**句子结构**：引语 Do you have anything else to tell us, Mr. Corbin? 是疑问句；随后 I do 后接 Corbin said 与介词短语 with a grave bow；末段用 I wish the people of Salem to know 加 that 宾语从句，so that five enchanters could take them as thews 是目的状语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句平铺直叙的陈述说出全书的要害。凶手不是某个人，而是制度，这句话把账算到了整个 Society 头上。

---

> **原句 10:** "'Which enchanters?' / Rapidly, the spirits around him called out five names: 'Henry Lyman, Theodore Paine, Edward Morton, Philip Knowles, John Perkins.'"

**中文理解**：她问是哪些附魔师；他周围的灵体随即一口气报出五个名字。

**句子结构**：引语 Which enchanters? 是省略式疑问句；末句主谓宾是 the spirits around him called out five names，Rapidly 是方式状语，冒号后五个名字是同位语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用五个名字把指控落到人头。前面还是抽象的罪行，此刻每一个名字都能对上城里的一位体面人。

---

> **原句 11:** "I felt myself yanked upward, away from Corbin and the other spirits, who diminished into mere specks and then disappeared entirely. Something had happened; my connection to the realm had been broken."

**中文理解**：他感到自己被猛地往上拽，离 Corbin 和那些灵体而去；他们缩成小点，随后完全消失。出了事，他与灵界的连接断了。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I felt myself yanked upward，away from Corbin and the other spirits 是补充，who diminished into mere specks and then disappeared entirely 是定语从句；末句用分号连接 Something had happened 与 my connection to the realm had been broken 两个主谓句，后者用被动语态。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用连接断了写危险的突然。前一秒还在说话，后一秒就被扯开，这一断也为随后台上出现的十二个灵体腾出了位置。

---

> **原句 12:** "The twelve thews made a semicircle around me and Clementine, hemming us in toward the audience. They raised their arms to link hands. Then they spoke together in a slow, measured chant: 'The prophecy of Reynaldo Murcia de Quintanilla is fulfilled: the destruction wrought by a woman enchanter. She has unleashed the five terrors: the Urco, the Rabeno, the Carpia, the En Micó, the Drago. Who will defend you from them?'"

**中文理解**：十二个灵体奴仆围成半圆，把 Sam 和 Clementine 向观众那边逼住；他们举臂交握，随后一齐用缓慢而有节拍的调子念诵：Reynaldo Murcia de Quintanilla 的预言应验了——一个女附魔师带来了毁灭，她放出了五样凶物；谁来替你们抵挡它们？

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 The twelve thews made a semicircle around me and Clementine，hemming us in toward the audience 是分词短语表结果；第二句主谓宾是 They raised their arms to link hands；末句用 Then they spoke together in a slow, measured chant 引出长引语，引语内先给预言应验的判断，再用 She has unleashed the five terrors 加冒号列举五个名字，末以 Who will defend you from them? 的疑问句收尾。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句预言把罪名扣在她头上。对手不出手，只借古老的传说让众人自己去怕，这一手比任何法术都阴。

---

> **原句 13:** "Then, with a sense of horror, I understood. It was just as Draken had boasted at the Cygnets gathering. The Cisneros Steel: the shining scale armor engraved with swans, the moths turned into fairies, the fish turned into mermaids. Creatures of the imagination could, regrettably, be all too real."

**中文理解**：随后 Sam 带着恐惧明白了：正是 Draken 在 Cygnets 聚会上夸口过的那件事。Cisneros Steel——那件刻着天鹅的亮鳞甲，那些变成仙女的蛾，那些变成美人鱼。想象出来的造物，说来遗憾，可以真得不能再真。

**句子结构**：Then, with a sense of horror 是状语，主句 I understood 是主谓句；第二句用 It was just as 引出方式状语从句；第三句 The Cisneros Steel 后用冒号列出三个并列同位语（the shining scale armor engraved with swans、the moths turned into fairies、the fish turned into mermaids）；末句主系表是 Creatures of the imagination could be all too real，regrettably 是插入语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句回想把前面所有的怪事串成一条线。读者至此才明白那件盔甲的真正用处，也就明白 Clem 为什么会被栽赃。
