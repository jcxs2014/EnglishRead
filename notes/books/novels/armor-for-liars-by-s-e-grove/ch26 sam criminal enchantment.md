# ch26 Sam: Criminal Enchantment

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 13 日，Salem/Oliver Street；Sam 以 thew 观察 Maxwell 与 Clem 的 interview
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Constable Roy Maxwell / Clem Quinn / Rosetta Quinn / Simeon Trench
- **核心事件**：Maxwell 询问 Clem about courthouse paper attack；Maxwell 揭露 stoning deaths 的 bruises 写 letters；Clem 报告 mother killed by enchanter, no police attention
- **悬念钩子**：Clem's "stones" warning？Maxwell's investigation？Clem's mother's death？

> **原句 5:** "I was incensed! I wanted to shake her. Since I couldn't, I sent her a burst of furious indignation, which she responded to by frowning. It was the most outrageous lie by omission!"

**句子结构**：主系表（I was incensed!）+ 主谓宾（I wanted to shake her）+ 主谓宾（Since I couldn't, I sent her a burst of furious indignation, which she responded to by frowning）+ 主系表（It was the most outrageous lie by omission!）。
