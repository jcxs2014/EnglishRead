# ch39 Sam: Scree

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 17 日，spirit realm Weer；Sam 以 thew 观察
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn
- **核心事件**：Sam 与 Clem 跨越 Weer；Clem 遭 scree（spirit eater）；Sam 被 scree 的 "sweet, soporific embrace" 吸引；Clem 救 Sam；Sam 与 Clem 在 portal 前分离 shroud；Clem takes Sam as thew
- **悬念钩子**：Sam's transformation? Clem's return to living world?

---

> **原句 1:** "Every spirit knows that it's folly to go through the Weer without the company of a living soul. I held on to Clem's rope tightly."

**中文理解**：Sam 说，凡灵体都知道，没有活人作伴就穿过 Weer 是犯傻；他紧紧攥住 Clem 的绳子。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 Every spirit knows that it's folly…，从句里 it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to go through the Weer，without the company of a living soul 是条件状语；末句主谓宾是 I held on to Clem's rope tightly。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句常识立规矩，再用攥紧绳子把规矩变成动作。读者的紧张从第一句就被拉起来。

---

> **原句 2:** "Clem," I said loudly. I couldn't turn to shake her without dislodging her hands. Her eyes sought mine. "You must stay alert," I said, almost shouting. "Don't lose sight of where we are. Only a little more than a hundred paces left. We're three-quarters of the way there."

**中文理解**：Sam 大声喊 Clem——他不能转身去摇她，那会把她的手甩开；她的眼睛找上他。他几乎是喊出来：要保持警觉，别丢了方位，只剩一百来步，已经走了四分之三。

**句子结构**：引语 Clem 后接 I said loudly 的说话标签；第二句主谓宾是 I couldn't turn to shake her，without dislodging her hands 是条件状语；第三句主谓宾是 Her eyes sought mine；末段引语由 You must stay alert、Don't lose sight of where we are、Only a little more than a hundred paces left、We're three-quarters of the way there 四个短句构成。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用报数写同行。数字既是路线也是安心的法子，读者跟着一起数步子，危险也就有了刻度。

---

> **原句 3:** "Clem!" I called desperately, now as terrified for myself as for her. The sudden gusts of wind lifted the fog, and I saw her thirty paces away, crumpled to the ground beside the mooring line. "Get up, Clem," I shouted at her. She raised her head—she was still alive. But I couldn't close the distance fast enough."

**中文理解**：Sam 绝望地喊她，此时为自己害怕和为她也一样多；他要她起来。她抬起头——还活着。可他赶不及过去。

**句子结构**：引语 Clem! 后接 I called desperately，now as terrified for myself as for her 是同级比较作补充；第二句用 The sudden gusts of wind lifted the fog 起头，and I saw her thirty paces away 并列，crumpled to the ground beside the mooring line 是过去分词短语作宾语补足语；末段引语 Get up, Clem 后接说话标签 I shouted at her，再用 She raised her head 与 she was still alive 及 But I couldn't close the distance fast enough 递进。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用为自己害怕和为她也一样多写恐惧的诚实。他不再是单纯的旁观者，这份自私让叙述更可信。

---

> **原句 4:** "Just before it came upon me, the scree fell silent. I felt a blow, but nothing like my collision with the carriage as a living man. No—this blow was an embrace."

**中文理解**：就在那东西扑上他之前，碎石坡忽然静了下来。他感到一记撞击，却不像活着时撞上马车那样；不，这一记是拥抱。

**句子结构**：Just before it came upon me 是时间状语从句，主句是 the scree fell silent；第二句主谓宾是 I felt a blow，but nothing like my collision with the carriage as a living man 是补充对照；末句用 No 起头，this blow was an embrace 是主系表。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用撞击与拥抱的对照写致命的温柔。死亡被写成怀抱，比任何撕咬都更叫人发冷。

---

> **原句 5:** "The scree wasn't a physical being, I realized dimly; it was a spirit—pure spirit, with no flesh or casing to encumber it. It tugged on my consciousness, saying, Come, come this way, without any need for words."

**中文理解**：他恍惚地意识到，碎石坡不是有形的活物，而是一个灵体——纯粹的灵，没有皮肉或躯壳拖累；它拉扯他的意识，不必用话就在说：来，往这边来。

**句子结构**：首句用 The scree wasn't a physical being 起头，I realized dimly 是插入语，分号后 it was a spirit—pure spirit 是同位补充，with no flesh or casing to encumber it 是 with 复合结构；末句主谓宾是 It tugged on my consciousness，saying 引出引语，without any need for words 是补充。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用不必用话写诱惑的方式。它靠的是意识本身，这也解释了 Sam 为何明知危险还想去。

---

> **原句 6:** "I felt a surge of desire to comply, and then delight at giving in. What delight! I had such a sense of calm and contentment that I hadn't experienced since arriving in the realm—no, since well before that."

**中文理解**：他涌起一股顺从的欲望，随即因屈从而欢喜。那是何等的欢喜！他感到一种安宁与满足，自打进了灵界就没尝过——不，还要早得多。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I felt a surge of desire to comply，and then delight at giving in 并列；第二句 What delight! 是感叹式短句；第三句主谓宾是 I had such a sense of calm and contentment，that I hadn't experienced since arriving in the realm 是结果状语从句，破折号后 no, since well before that 作自我修正。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用安宁写沉沦。危险被写成渴望已久的东西，读者由此明白这一关靠意志是过不去的。

---

> **原句 7:** "Then it was broken. Something appeared in my line of vision, blocking the lovely pale light. A gruesome thing of metal and flesh and floating hair. 'SAM!' it shouted."

**中文理解**：随后那一切被打断。有个东西闯进他视野，挡住那片可爱的淡光——一团由金属、皮肉和飘浮的头发构成的狰狞之物，冲他大喊他的名字。

**句子结构**：首句主系表（Then it was broken）；第二句主谓宾是 Something appeared in my line of vision，blocking the lovely pale light 是分词短语；第三句 A gruesome thing of metal and flesh and floating hair 是名词短语独立成句；末句引语 SAM! 后接 it shouted 的说话标签。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用狰狞之物写救命的人。Clem 出现时像个怪物，这份颠倒正是灵界里的实情。

---

> **原句 8:** "Clem, I realized. Clem was my sense of unease. I could not leave her here alone."

**中文理解**：Sam 认出来了——那正是他心里的不安；他不能把她一个人留在这里。

**句子结构**：前两句是主谓宾与主系表（Clem, I realized 与 Clem was my sense of unease）；末句主谓宾是 I could not leave her here alone，用否定加地点状语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用不安来解释他为何挣脱。救他的其实是他对人的牵挂，而不是什么意志力。

---

> **原句 9:** "I need the case." Her eyes closed, but she slid her hand upward toward her trouser pocket. Then her fingers fell away. "It's lead," I said to her. "I can't touch it." She shook her head slightly, the barest shift from side to side. What did she mean? She couldn't do it? She wouldn't? Gingerly, I pulled at the opening of her trouser pocket. I could see the rectangular lump in the satin lining. What would happen if I touched lead? Would it burn me? Turn me to dust? Or would my fingers simply fail to grasp it, sliding through like mere air? I thrust my hand into the pocket.

**中文理解**：她说需要那只盒子，闭着眼把手往裤袋挪，随后手指垂了下去。Sam 说那是铅，他碰不得；他自问碰上去会怎样——烧起来？化成灰？还是手指像穿过空气一样抓不住？然后他把手伸进了口袋。

**句子结构**：引语 I need the case 后接 Her eyes closed, but she slid her hand upward toward her trouser pocket 的并列句；随后 Then her fingers fell away 是主谓句；引语 It's lead 与 I can't touch it 连用；She shook her head slightly 后接 the barest shift from side to side 的同位补充；再连用三个疑问句（What did she mean? She couldn't do it? She wouldn't?）；Gingerly, I pulled at the opening of her trouser pocket 是主谓宾，I could see the rectangular lump in the satin lining 并列；末段用三个疑问句自问（Would it burn me? Turn me to dust? Or would my fingers simply fail to grasp it…?），末句 I thrust my hand into the pocket 是主谓宾。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用三个自问写伸手前的犹豫。规矩说灵体动不了铅，他仍赌了一把，人物的胆量由此有了具体的代价。

---

> **原句 10:** "The case lay in my hand: a small, dainty box made of rosewood. I blinked, not comprehending. Then I realized—it was Clem's jewelry box. Wood, not lead! Of course! Clem would never keep her sister's shroud in a lead box, denying her access to her own liberty."

**中文理解**：盒子落在他手里：一只小巧的、玫瑰木做的匣子。他眨着眼没明白，随后恍然——这是 Clem 的首饰盒，是木头的，不是铅。当然，Clem 绝不会把妹妹的外壳锁进铅盒里，断了她拿回自由的路。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 The case lay in my hand，冒号后 a small, dainty box made of rosewood 是同位语，made of 是过去分词短语作后置定语；第二句 I blinked, not comprehending 是主谓宾加分词状语；第三句用 Then I realized 起头，it was Clem's jewelry box 是宾语从句，Wood, not lead! 是省略式感叹；末句主谓宾是 Clem would never keep her sister's shroud in a lead box，denying her access to her own liberty 是分词短语表结果。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一个木盒写 Clem 的为人。前面所有关于铅的规矩在此被一个选择推翻，人物的性格比任何评语都清楚。

---

> **原句 11:** "'I have it,' I said. 'But this part you must do.' I leaned down close. 'You must take the shroud. I can't.' Her eyes flickered. 'Do this and we are done. We open the portal, and this will all be over. Only one more step, Clementine.' I lifted her hand and put it against my heart. 'Pull. Pull off the shroud.'"

**中文理解**：他说拿到了，但这一步得由她来。他把她的手按在自己心口，让她拉下那层外壳：做完这件事就结束了，打开 portal，一切都会过去，只剩最后一步。

**句子结构**：引语 I have it 与 But this part you must do 连用；随后 I leaned down close 是主谓句；引语 You must take the shroud. I can't 与 Her eyes flickered 交替；再接 Do this and we are done、We open the portal, and this will all be over、Only one more step, Clementine 三句；末段主谓宾是 I lifted her hand and put it against my heart，引语 Pull. Pull off the shroud 用两个祈使句收尾。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用把她的手按在自己心口写交付。这一步既是他被束缚的开始，也是两人关系的成形，动作比台词更重。

---

> **原句 12:** "I could not let her down."

**中文理解**：他说他不能让她失望。

**句子结构**：主谓宾，五个词。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用五个词给全章收束。前面那一连串险境都压在这一句上，也解释了他在最想放弃时为何还能抓住绳子。
