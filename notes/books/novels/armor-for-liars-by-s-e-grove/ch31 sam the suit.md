# ch31 Sam: The Suit

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 14 日，Oliver Street/Salem；Sam 以 thew 观察 Clem
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn / Rosie Quinn / Wister Amsel
- **核心事件**：Clem 遇 stones attack（lobster trap）；Rosie 看到 "Father" apparition；Clem 用 bone + "Armar" 创建 armored creature "Cuidar"
- **悬念钩子**：Clem's bone creature？"Cuidar" 的 purpose？Sam's distrust？

> **原句 1:** "I saw the stones before she did. I had not imagined that they would return again so soon, but there they were: pursuing her quite openly, their clattering inaudible in the falling rain."

**中文理解**：Sam 的 opening——"I saw the stones before she did"、"pursuing her quite openly, their clattering inaudible in the falling rain"。Sam's foresight。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（I saw the stones before she did）+ 主谓宾（I had not imagined that they would return again so soon）+ 主谓宾（but there they were: pursuing her quite openly, their clattering inaudible in the falling rain）。

**关键词**：
- `I saw the stones before she did`：I saw the stones before she did——Sam's foresight
- `pursuing her quite openly`：pursuing her quite openly——stones' aggression
- `their clattering inaudible in the falling rain`：their clattering inaudible——stones' stealth

**为什么这样写**：用 Sam's foresight 把 stones' return 具象化，"pursuing her quite openly"把 stones' aggression 具象化，"their clattering inaudible"把 stones' stealth 具象化，为后文 "How many more times?" 做铺垫。

**句子结构**：引语（Morder）+ 主谓宾（The trap leaped away from her, the broken splints of wood flapping madly like long, sharp teeth）+ 主谓宾（It lunged at the stones and snapped them up hungrily）。

**关键词**：
- `Morder`：Morder——Clem's enchantment spell
- `broken splints of wood flapping madly like long, sharp teeth`：broken splints flapping madly——trap's animation

**为什么这样写**：用 "Morder"把 Clem's enchantment 具象化，"flapping madly like long, sharp teeth"把 trap's animation 具象化，"snapped them up hungrily"把 trap's action 具象化，为后文 "It seemed too easy" 做铺垫。
