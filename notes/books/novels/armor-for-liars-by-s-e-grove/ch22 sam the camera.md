# ch22 Sam: The Camera

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：Saddle Street 1877-1887；Sam 的 backstory：how he was recruited by Cisneros Society
- **人物**：Sam (Samuel/Byron) / Cris (brother) / Mamá (Camille) / Professor Orin Hornsby / Juno (thew) / Karl Draken (Embalmer) / Professor Selwyn Mead / Pelham
- **核心事件**：Sam 回忆 his bargain with Cisneros Society：Hornsby/Juno lure him to Camera；Mead reveals Cisneros Society；Sam forced to secure thews for Society；Sam's first spirit realm journey
- **悬念钩子**：Sam's death? Cisneros Society's recruitment method? Sam's "mother's lullaby" in spirit realm?

> **原句 1:** "I have no wish to describe more of what happened to me, of how I was tricked into doing the Society's bidding. But I fear I must."

**中文理解**：Sam 的 opening——"I have no wish to describe more... But I fear I must"。Sam 的 reluctance 与 necessity。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（I have no wish to describe more of what happened to me）+ 主谓宾（of how I was tricked into doing the Society's bidding）+ 转折（But I fear I must）。

**关键词**：
- `no wish to describe more`：no wish to describe more——Sam's reluctance
- `tricked into doing the Society's bidding`：tricked into doing——Sam's exploitation
- `I fear I must`：I fear I must——Sam's necessity

**为什么这样写**：用 Sam's reluctance 把 his trauma 具象化，"tricked into doing the Society's bidding"把 his exploitation 具象化，为后文 "I thought I'd gained the upper hand" 做铺垫。
