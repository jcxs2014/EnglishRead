# ch21 Sam: A Verdict

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 11 日，Salem courthouse；Sam 以 thew 观察 Clem 的 trial
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn / Mr. Arthur Pombaline / Mr. Wilbur Smythe / Judge Brice Richardson / Simeon Trench / Rosetta Quinn
- **核心事件**：Pombaline 与 Smythe 的 arguments；paper attack；Judge Richardson 判 fraud meritless 但设 task：Clem 必须 procuring a thew in 19 days
- **悬念钩子**：Clem 如何 procure a thew？Boston portal 的 location？Simeon 的 response？

---

> **原句 1:** "Their case was scheduled for ten thirty, and by the time the sisters arrived, the Salem courthouse was packed."

**中文理解**：案子排在十点半，两姐妹到场时，Salem 的法庭已经挤满了人。

**句子结构**：首句是被动语态（Their case was scheduled for ten thirty）；第二句用 by the time 引导时间状语从句，主句是 the Salem courthouse was packed。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用挤满二字写旁听者的兴致。一场关于一名女生能否入学的官司被当成戏看，这一章的敌意从一开始就不在法官身上。

---

> **原句 2:** "I considered him, trying to ascertain his inclinations. Until now, the Honorable Brice Richardson had seemed neither hostile nor friendly to Clem's case."

**中文理解**：Sam 端详法官，想摸清他的倾向。在此之前，这位 Brice Richardson 法官对 Clem 的案子既不显敌意也不显友善。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾（I considered him），trying to ascertain his inclinations 是分词短语作伴随状语；第二句用 Until now 作时间状语，主系表是 the Honorable Brice Richardson had seemed neither hostile nor friendly to Clem's case。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：先写一位不表态的法官，把结果留到最后一刻。中立的假象越久，判决公布时的偏袒越刺眼。

---

> **原句 3:** "'Your Honor,' Mr. Pombaline said, continuing into the final leg of his statement, 'Clementine Quinn has presented not only a threat to the revered traditions of the esteemed university, as I have aptly demonstrated, but indeed a threat to the standards of education more generally.'"

**中文理解**：校方律师 Pombaline 在陈述的最后一程说，Clementine Quinn 威胁的不只是这所受人敬重的大学的传统，他本人已充分证明，她威胁的是更广泛的教育标准。

**句子结构**：引语内主谓宾是 Clementine Quinn has presented，用 not only… but indeed… 连接两个并列宾语 a threat to the revered traditions 与 a threat to the standards of education；continuing into the final leg of his statement 是分词短语作伴随状语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用不只是、而且是把一桩入学纠纷抬成公共危机。对方要的不是她退学，而是让她的行为显得危险。

---

> **原句 4:** "'Second,' he said, sweeping an arm aloft with two fingers raised, 'both the rules of the university and the laws of the commonwealth mention 'men' many times, but they never explicitly bar women from applying or enrolling.'"

**中文理解**：辩方律师举起两根手指说，第二点：学校的规章和本邦的法律都多次提到男人，却从没有明文禁止女性报考或注册。

**句子结构**：引语内用 both… and… 连接 the rules of the university 与 the laws of the commonwealth 作主语，mention 'men' many times 是谓语部分；后半句用 but 转折，they never explicitly bar women from applying or enrolling 是主谓宾加动名词短语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用条文里的用词做文章，把案子从道德拉回文本。律师的这一点也替 Clem 后面那句反问铺好了台阶。

---

> **原句 5:** "Abruptly, one spear of paper peeled away from the rest, diving rapidly, and the others followed until they made a single, pointed cone, shuttling downward. With dreadful familiarity, they narrowed and sharpened, descending toward Clementine at full speed."

**中文理解**：忽然，一支纸矛脱离整叠急速下坠，其余随即跟上，合成一个尖锥向下穿行。那种熟悉得可怕的收束与锐化，正全速朝 Clementine 落下来。

**句子结构**：首句用 Abruptly 起头，主谓宾是 one spear of paper peeled away from the rest，diving rapidly 是分词短语；第二句用 and the others followed 后接 until 时间状语从句；末句以 With dreadful familiarity 作状语前置，主句是 they narrowed and sharpened，descending toward Clementine at full speed 是分词短语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用熟悉得可怕把两次袭击接起来。读者立刻明白这是同一伙人的手法，法庭里的暴力也就与校内的排挤连成一气。

---

> **原句 6:** "'I have reached a verdict,' Richardson said. 'I have set a task commensurate to the situation. If you succeed in this task, Clementine Quinn, I will ask Commonwealth to modify its admissions policy. If you fail, the university will remain closed to women. Go to the spirit realm. Find a spirit, bind it to yourself, and return. You have until the end of the month.' He consulted a piece of paper on his desk. 'Nineteen days.'"

**中文理解**：Richardson 宣布判决：他定下一项与情形相称的考验。若 Clem 通过，他会请 Commonwealth 修改招生规定；若失败，这所学校将继续对女性关闭。他命她去 spirit realm，找到灵体、与它缔结束缚，再回来——期限到月底。他看了一眼桌上的纸：十九天。

**句子结构**：引语内先用两句主谓宾交代判决与考验（I have reached a verdict 与 I have set a task commensurate to the situation）；随后用两个 If 引导的并列条件句给出赏罚；再用三个祈使句连排（Go to the spirit realm. Find a spirit, bind it to yourself, and return.）；末以 You have until the end of the month 与独立成句的 Nineteen days 收尾。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一项看似公允的考验把难题交回给她。条件开得冠冕堂皇，实质是让一个人去完成多数成年附魔师也做不到的事，而最后那三个字把善意彻底抹掉。

---

> **原句 7:** "'All I need is the location of the Boston portal.' / Judge Richardson eyed her narrowly. 'I cannot disclose that. The Boston portal is proprietary, and its location is secret. You will have to find another portal for your errand of doom.'"

**中文理解**：Clem 说，他只需要 Boston 那个 portal 的位置。Richardson 眯眼看着他，说自己不能透露：那是私产，位置保密；他得另找一个 portal 去跑这趟送死的差事。

**句子结构**：首句引语是主系表（All I need is the location of the Boston portal）；随后主谓宾 Judge Richardson eyed her narrowly；引语内先用 I cannot disclose that 表态，再用两个主系表说明理由（The Boston portal is proprietary 与 its location is secret）；末句主谓宾是 You will have to find another portal，for your errand of doom 是目的状语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用不能透露补上最后一道障碍。路既被指定又被封死，Clem 只剩下自己去找线索，主线的下一步由此确定。
