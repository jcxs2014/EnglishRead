# ch19 Sam: The Trenches

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 8 日，Salem/Commonwealth/Oliver Street；Sam 以 thew 观察 Maxwell 与 Trench 家族
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Constable Roy Maxwell / Clem Quinn / Rosie Quinn / Norbert Trench / Simeon Trench / Principa (Norbert's thew)
- **核心事件**：Maxwell 用 spiritoscope 与 Sam 交流；Maxwell 怀疑 Cisneros Society；Maxwell 跟踪 Norbert/Simeon 到 Quinn 家；Norbert 施压 Clem；Sam 确认 Cisneros Society
- **悬念钩子**：Maxwell 的 investigation？Norbert 的 "distance" 策略？Cisneros Society 的 response？

---

> **原句 1:** "The knowledge of Livia's identity had overturned my understanding of Clementine's purpose."

**中文理解**：Sam 的 opening——"The knowledge of Livia's identity had overturned my understanding of Clementine's purpose"。Sam 的 realization。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（The knowledge of Livia's identity had overturned my understanding of Clementine's purpose）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用 Sam's realization 把 his understanding 具象化，"overturned"把 Sam's paradigm shift 具象化，为后文 "I had been misled by her innocent appearance" 做铺垫。
