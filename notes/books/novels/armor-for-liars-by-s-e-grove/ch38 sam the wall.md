# ch38 Sam: The Wall

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 17 日，spirit realm；Sam 以 thew 观察 Clem 进入 realm
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn
- **核心事件**：Sam 等待 Clem at wall；Oaring 危机；Sam 与 Clem 相遇；Sam 揭露 Rosetta 已被 taken as thew；Sam 提议 Clem take him as thew
- **悬念钩子**：Clem 取 Sam as thew？Sam's role as thew？

---

> **原句 1:** "I waited for Clementine at the wall."

**中文理解**：Sam 的 opening——"I waited for Clementine at the wall"。简短而 decisive，把 Sam's waiting 具象化。

**句子结构**：主谓宾。极简。

**关键词**：
- `I waited for Clementine at the wall`：I waited for Clementine at the wall——Sam's waiting

**为什么这样写**：用极简句式把 Sam's waiting 具象化，"at the wall"把 spirit realm location 具象化，为后文 "She would come. I knew she would come" 做铺垫。

---

> **原句 2:** "She would come. I knew she would come. If anyone had the strength and tenacity to come this far, my Clementine did."

**中文理解**：Sam 的 confidence——"She would come. I knew she would come"、"If anyone had the strength and tenacity to come this far, my Clementine did"。Sam's faith in Clem。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（She would come）+ 主谓宾（I knew she would come）+ 条件句（If anyone had the strength and tenacity to come this far, my Clementine did）。

**关键词**：
- `She would come. I knew she would come`：She would come. I knew she would come——Sam's confidence
- `strength and tenacity`：strength and tenacity——Sam's faith in Clem

**为什么这样写**：用 "She would come. I knew she would come"把 Sam's confidence 具象化，"strength and tenacity"把 Clem's character 具象化，为后文 "I hoped it would be enough" 做铺垫。

> **原句 3:** "No, she wouldn't make it. In that moment I didn't think about the scree; Clementine was in danger, that was all that mattered. I needed to reach her. I vaulted the wall and closed the distance between us."

**中文理解**：Sam 的 action——"No, she wouldn't make it"、"I vaulted the wall and closed the distance between us"。Sam's determination。

**句子结构**：主系表（No, she wouldn't make it）+ 时间状语从句（In that moment I didn't think about the scree; Clementine was in danger, that was all that mattered）+ 主谓宾（I needed to reach her）+ 主谓宾（I vaulted the wall and closed the distance between us）。

**关键词**：
- `No, she wouldn't make it`：No, she wouldn't make it——Sam's assessment
- `I vaulted the wall and closed the distance between us`：I vaulted the wall and closed the distance——Sam's action

**为什么这样写**：用 "No, she wouldn't make it"把 Sam's assessment 具象化，"I vaulted the wall and closed the distance between us"把 Sam's action 具象化，为后文 "Hold on," I cried" 做铺垫。
