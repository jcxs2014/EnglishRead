# ch45 Sam: Murcia's Five

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 29 日，Salem Lyceum；Sam 以 thew 观察
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn / Cisneros Steel (Urco) / Principa (Norbert's thew)
- **核心事件**：Urco appears at Lyceum；enchanters "defeat" Urco；Clem & Sam flee；Rabeno (pale giant) appears；Clem uses "Remendar" and "Girar" to escape in coach
- **悬念钩子**：Urco's "defeat"？Rabeno's threat？Clem's escape？

---

> **原句 1:** "The mad scramble for the exits had begun, but the narrow doorways would allow only so many people at once, and the Lyceum was still mostly full as the wolf took a step forward, its teeth bared, spittle dripping from its chin."

**中文理解**：人群开始疯抢出口，可窄门一次只容那么些人通过；狼向前迈了一步，牙关张开，口水从下巴滴下来，场子里仍旧坐满大半。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 The mad scramble for the exits had begun；第二句用 but 转折，the narrow doorways would allow only so many people at once 是主系表；末句用 and the Lyceum was still mostly full 起头，as the wolf took a step forward 是时间状语从句，its teeth bared 与 spittle dripping from its chin 是两个独立主格结构。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用挤不出去写恐慌的实际形状。逃不掉的人比跑掉的人多，恐惧由此从抽象变成挤在门口的一堆身体。

---

> **原句 2:** "I'd already placed myself between Clementine and the wolf—it would have to contend with me first—but the creature ignored us. It took several steps toward the apron of the stage and gazed out at the scrambling audience."

**中文理解**：Sam 早已把自己挡在 Clementine 和狼之间——它得先过他这一关；可那东西根本没理他们，反倒朝台口走了几步，望着下面乱作一团的观众。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I'd already placed myself between Clementine and the wolf，破折号后 it would have to contend with me first 是插入的说明；第二句用 but 转折，the creature ignored us 是主谓宾；末句主谓宾是 It took several steps toward the apron of the stage and gazed out at the scrambling audience。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用被无视写危险的指向。狼不要他们的命，要的是台下那些人的眼睛，这一场戏的性质由此露出来。

---

> **原句 3:** "This, of course, was the performance the enchanters wanted the audience to see: a play in two acts, in which the commonwealth's finest enchanters defeated the monster created by the malicious enchantress."

**中文理解**：这当然是那些附魔师想让观众看到的一场戏：两幕剧，头一幕由本邦最出色的附魔师打败那个恶毒女附魔师造出来的怪物。

**句子结构**：主系表结构。This 作主语，was the performance 是谓语部分，the enchanters wanted the audience to see 是省略 that 的定语从句；冒号后 a play in two acts 是同位语，in which 引导定语从句，the monster created by the malicious enchantress 中 created 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一场戏点破对方的算盘。凶物是他们自己放出来的，观众看见的英勇也是他们排好的，读者由此明白 Clem 面对的不是法术而是舆论。

---

> **原句 4:** "The wolf lay prone, its limbs kicking uselessly in ungainly death throes. Some people in the crowd were looking past the wolf at us, and their expressions were not sympathetic."

**中文理解**：狼伏在地上，四肢徒劳地抽动，死得难看；人群里有些人越过狼看向他们，脸上没有一点同情。

**句子结构**：首句主谓句是 The wolf lay prone，its limbs kicking uselessly in ungainly death throes 是独立主格结构；末句主谓宾是 Some people in the crowd were looking past the wolf at us，and their expressions were not sympathetic 并列，用主系表作否定判断。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用没有同情写栽赃已经生效。凶物倒下本是好事，观众的眼神却转向了他们，这一章真正的败局从这里开始。

---

> **原句 5:** "'SO!' a piercing voice cried. We paused and looked out toward the audience. 'The malefactors flee from the destruction they have wrought!' It was Principa, who floated some distance above the swarming crowd and pointed at us gleefully. 'Look at them, running to save themselves from the consequences of their actions.'"

**中文理解**：一声尖利的嗓音喊出来，说作恶的人正从自己造成的灾祸前逃走；那是 Principa，她浮在涌动的人群上方指着他们，得意地叫众人看——看他们为了躲开自己行为的后果而奔逃。

**句子结构**：引语 SO! 是感叹式短句，后接 a piercing voice cried 的说话标签；随后主谓宾是 We paused and looked out toward the audience；再接引语 The malefactors flee from the destruction they have wrought!；随后主系表是 It was Principa，who floated some distance above the swarming crowd and pointed at us gleefully 是非限定定语从句；末段引语 Look at them, running to save themselves from the consequences of their actions 是祈使句加分词短语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句指控把逃跑变成罪证。逃命本是本能，被这样一说就成了心虚，舆论的转向在几句话之间完成。

---

> **原句 6:** "A shadow fell upon the cobblestone. It moved slowly, awkwardly, with a lumbering gait. Then all at once it appeared, fully as tall as the Lyceum that had until then obstructed it from view: a pale, hairless, naked giant, its skin cadaverously white, showing the branching of blue veins below the surface. The Rabeno."

**中文理解**：一道影子落在石板路上，缓缓地、笨拙地挪动；随后它一下子现出身形——足有先前挡住它的那座 Lyceum 那么高：一个苍白、无毛、赤裸的巨人，皮肤白得像尸体，皮下透出分叉的青筋。那是 Rabeno。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 A shadow fell upon the cobblestone；第二句主谓句是 It moved slowly, awkwardly, with a lumbering gait；第三句用 Then all at once it appeared 起头，fully as tall as the Lyceum 是同级比较，that had until then obstructed it from view 是定语从句；冒号后 a pale, hairless, naked giant 是同位语，its skin cadaverously white 是独立主格结构，showing the branching of blue veins below the surface 是分词短语；末句 The Rabeno 是名词短语独立成句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用影子的慢与出现的突然写巨物的体量。先给影子再给全貌，读者的想象被拉长，恐惧因此更实在。

---

> **原句 7:** "I swallowed my reluctance—it was the first coach I'd touched since my death—and climbed up onto the seat. It was very odd, sitting in the barouche with no horses before us. Clementine made a circular motion in the air and said, 'Girar.' The barouche jolted into motion obligingly, rolling over the cobblestones in the most natural manner, as if there were indeed a pair of horses diligently pulling us forward."

**中文理解**：他压下心里的不愿——这是他死后第一次碰马车——爬上座位。没有马的四轮车坐上去很怪。Clementine 在空中画了个圈，念出咒词，车子便听话地一动，沿着石板路滚得再自然不过，像真有一对马在使劲拉。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I swallowed my reluctance and climbed up onto the seat，破折号后 it was the first coach I'd touched since my death 是插入的原因说明；第二句主系表是 It was very odd，sitting in the barouche with no horses before us 是真正的主语；第三句主谓宾是 Clementine made a circular motion in the air and said, 'Girar'；末句主谓宾是 The barouche jolted into motion obligingly，rolling over the cobblestones in the most natural manner 是分词短语，as if there were indeed a pair of horses diligently pulling us forward 是虚拟比较从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用第一次碰马车写死的痕迹。逃亡的工具恰好撞上他生前的死因，这一处不写悲情，读者却会自己想起那辆烧着的车。

---

> **原句 8:** "Here on Essex Street, farther from the path of the Rabeno, some people still dared to open shutters and windows to look down on the street below, though their doors remained firmly closed."

**中文理解**：到了 Essex 街，离 Rabeno 的路线远了些，还有人敢推开百叶窗和窗子往下看街上，可他们的门仍旧关得死紧。

**句子结构**：Here on Essex Street 与 farther from the path of the Rabeno 是两个状语前置；主谓宾是 some people still dared to open shutters and windows to look down on the street below；末句用 though 引导让步状语从句，their doors remained firmly closed 是主系表。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用窗开而门不开写旁观者的分寸。看热闹可以，收留不行，这座城里的人心在两句里写完了。

---

> **原句 9:** "A young man in a second-story window stared at us, mouth agape, as we bounced by. I gave him a cheerful wave before resuming my grip on the handrail."

**中文理解**：他们颠着经过时，二楼窗口一个年轻人张着嘴瞪着他们；Sam 朝他高高兴兴地挥了挥手，才重新抓住扶手。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 A young man in a second-story window stared at us，mouth agape 是独立主格结构，as we bounced by 是时间状语从句；末句主谓宾是 I gave him a cheerful wave，before resuming my grip on the handrail 是时间状语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一个挥手写 Sam 的性子。前面他刚被满城的人指为恶人，此刻仍不忘逗一下看客，人物的底色在此透出来。

---

> **原句 10:** "'Con prisa,' Clem said again, and the carriage, with an air of gleeful liberation, burst into furious speed."

**中文理解**：Clem 又说了一次咒词，马车带着一副欢快的、被放开了的神气，猛地冲快起来。

**句子结构**：引语 Con prisa 后接 Clem said again；末句主谓宾是 the carriage burst into furious speed，with an air of gleeful liberation 是介词短语作状语，把车拟人化。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用欢快的神气写这辆车。逃命的路被写成一次撒野，紧绷了一整章的节奏在此松一下，也为下一场更重的场面留出呼吸。
