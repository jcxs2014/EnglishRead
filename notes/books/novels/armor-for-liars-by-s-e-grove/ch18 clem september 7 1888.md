# ch18 Clem: September 7, 1888

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 7 日，Clem 致信母亲；被 Cassandra 揭露后
- **人物**：Clem / Mother（Lydia）/ Cassandra (Mead's thew)
- **核心事件**：Clem 报告 dejected by reversal；Cassandra 给 human bone；Clem 对 Cassandra 的 sympathy；"an enchanter... is a genuine torturer"
- **悬念钩子**：Cassandra 与 Lydia 的关系？"For your armor" 的 bone 意味什么？

---

> **原句 1:** "I will not tell Rosie how dejected I am by the reversal. But I'm sure you could sense my disappointment. So many years of planning. The entrance exam. The tokens. All of it wasted."

**中文理解**：Clem 的 disappointment——、。Clem 对 her plans 的 loss。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（I will not tell Rosie how dejected I am by the reversal）+ 主系表（But I'm sure you could sense my disappointment）+ 并列名词短语（So many years of planning. The entrance exam. The tokens. All of it wasted.）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用 Clem's "dejected"把 her disappointment 具象化，"All of it wasted"把 her loss 具象化，为后文 "I have faith that something will become clear" 做铺垫。

---

> **原句 2:** "Mother, have you confided in Cassandra? Mead's thew? She came to me as I was leaving. I wonder if you saw us, since we were at the edge of campus, near the outer limits of your perception."

**中文理解**：Clem 问母亲是否 、。Clem 对 Cassandra 与母亲关系的 suspicion。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（Mother, have you confided in Cassandra?）+ 同位语（Mead's thew?）+ 主谓宾（She came to me as I was leaving）+ 主谓宾（I wonder if you saw us, since we were at the edge of campus, near the outer limits of your perception）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用 "confided in Cassandra"把 Clem's suspicion 具象化，"outer limits of your perception"把 Lydia's thew perception 具象化，为后文 "Mother showed her the locket" 做铺垫。

---

> **原句 3:** "How easy it would be for her to tell Mead about you, and in so doing overthrow all our efforts. But she hasn't done that. Instead, she's given me this bone. Why? What does she intend with it?"

**中文理解**：Clem 对 Cassandra's gift 的 puzzlement——、、。

**句子结构**：主系表（How easy it would be for her to tell Mead about you）+ 并列分句（and in so doing overthrow all our efforts）+ 转折（But she hasn't done that）+ 主谓宾（Instead, she's given me this bone）+ 两个问句（Why? What does she intend with it?）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用 "How easy it would be"把 Clem's fear 具象化，"she's given me this bone"把 Cassandra's gift 具象化，"What does she intend"把 Clem's puzzlement 具象化，为后文 "For your armor" 做铺垫。

---

> **原句 4:** "She said: 'For your armor…' Does she anticipate such injuries to my person that I could only repair with enchantment of bone? What kind of armor would that require?"

**中文理解**：Clem 对 的 interpretation——、。Clem 的 puzzlement。

**句子结构**：引语（For your armor…）+ 问句（Does she anticipate such injuries to my person that I could only repair with enchantment of bone?）+ 问句（What kind of armor would that require?）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用 "For your armor"把 Cassandra's gift 具象化，"enchantment of bone"把 bone magic 具象化，"What kind of armor"把 Clem's puzzlement 具象化，为后文 "human bone" 做铺垫。

---

> **原句 5:** "I think she meant to explain more, but she was drawn away by Mead. Mother, I have so little experience of thews that I have never seen one coerced in that way. The pain on her face was excruciating. She was truly a soul in torment."

**中文理解**：Clem 对 Cassandra's coercion 的 observation——、、。Clem 的 sympathy。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（I think she meant to explain more, but she was drawn away by Mead）+ 主谓宾（Mother, I have so little experience of thews that I have never seen one coerced in that way）+ 主系表（The pain on her face was excruciating）+ 主系表（She was truly a soul in torment）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用 "coerced"与 "excruciating"把 Cassandra's coercion 具象化，"soul in torment"把 Cassandra's suffering 具象化，为后文 "an enchanter... is a genuine torturer" 做铺垫。

---

> **原句 6:** "I hadn't expected to feel sympathy for someone who had just exposed me, not in the least, but if in that moment I could have ended her pain somehow, even if by striking Mead with my own hand, I would have done it."

**中文理解**：Clem 的 sympathy——、。Clem 的 moral response。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（I hadn't expected to feel sympathy for someone who had just exposed me, not in the least）+ 条件句（but if in that moment I could have ended her pain somehow, even if by striking Mead with my own hand, I would have done it）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用 "hadn't expected to feel sympathy"把 Clem's unexpected sympathy 具象化，"striking Mead with my own hand"把 Clem's moral response 具象化，为后文 "an enchanter... is a genuine torturer" 做铺垫。

---

> **原句 7:** "I realized then that an enchanter, when he pressures the shroud, is a genuine torturer. And I understood why a thew does whatever she must to abide and comply. Even a small disobedience, as Cassandra undertook, is a tremendous act of courage."

**中文理解**：Clem 的 realization——、、。这是 Clem 的 moral insight。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（I realized then that an enchanter, when he pressures the shroud, is a genuine torturer）+ 主谓宾（And I understood why a thew does whatever she must to abide and comply）+ 主系表（Even a small disobedience, as Cassandra undertook, is a tremendous act of courage）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用 "genuine torturer"把 enchanter's moral status 具象化，"tremendous act of courage"把 Cassandra's disobedience 具象化，为后文 "Please tell me that you have never been so treated by him" 做铺垫。

---

> **原句 8:** "Please tell me that you have never been so treated by him."

**中文理解**：Clem 的 plea——。这是 Clem 对母亲的 concern。

**句子结构**：祈使句（Please tell me that you have never been so treated by him）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用 "Please tell me"把 Clem's plea 具象化，"never been so treated by him"把 Clem's concern for Lydia 具象化，为后文 Sam's discovery of Livia=Lydia 做铺垫。
