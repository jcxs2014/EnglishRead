# ch40 Sam: Residues

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 18 日，Keel House；Sam 以 thew 观察
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn / Wister Amsel / Constable Roy Maxwell (Marcel)
- **核心事件**：Sam 与 Clem 返回 Keel House；Sam 的 thew powers (thought reading)；Sam-Wister dialogue about Sor Juana Alliance；Maxwell reveals identity as Marcel
- **悬念钩子**：Sam's thew powers？Sor Juana Alliance？Maxwell's identity？

---

> **原句 1:** "We tumbled into Keel House, and I closed the door to the spirit realm behind us: shutting out the Oarings and the scree; the aimless, endless drifting; the vast, yawning sense that my soul was starving for sustenance it would never receive. They were all behind me now."

**中文理解**：他们跌进 Keel House，Sam 在身后关上了通往灵界的那扇门：把划桨船与碎石坡关在外面，把那无目的、无休止的漂流关在外面，也把那巨大的、张着口的、灵魂在渴望一种永远得不到的滋养的感觉关在外面。那些都留在他身后了。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 We tumbled into Keel House；第二句主谓宾是 I closed the door to the spirit realm behind us，冒号后用 shutting out 的分词短语引出三个并列宾语（the Oarings and the scree、the aimless, endless drifting、the vast, yawning sense），第三个宾语带 that my soul was starving for sustenance 的同位从句；末句主系表是 They were all behind me now。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一连串名词列出门后的东西。危险不是被消灭而是被关起来，读者由此知道灵界仍在那儿。

---

> **原句 2:** "What a wonder, to feel thoughts! As I looked at Clem, a swirl of impressions appeared to me effortlessly, as clearly as if she were speaking them aloud: stones, more stones; a terrible sense of cold; desperation and horror."

**中文理解**：能感到念头是何等奇事！他看向 Clem，一片印象毫不费力地浮出来，清楚得像她当面说了出来：石头，更多的石头；可怕的寒冷；绝望与恐惧。

**句子结构**：首句是感叹式主系表（What a wonder, to feel thoughts!），to feel thoughts 是真正的主语；第二句用 As I looked at Clem 作时间状语从句，主句是 a swirl of impressions appeared to me effortlessly，as clearly as if she were speaking them aloud 是虚拟比较从句；末句 stones, more stones; a terrible sense of cold; desperation and horror 是一串并列名词作同位补充。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用毫不费力写新得到的本事。前面他只能推情绪、听不见回应，此刻突然能读心，这份落差正是他成为灵体奴仆的代价与报偿。

---

> **原句 3:** "I found that I was kneeling by the side of the bed, and my hand rested on Clem's tousled hair. The others had drawn back and were watching me."

**中文理解**：他发现自己跪在床边，手搭在 Clem 凌乱的头发上；旁人都退开了，正看着他。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I found that I was kneeling by the side of the bed，and my hand rested on Clem's tousled hair 并列；末句主谓宾是 The others had drawn back and were watching me，用两个并列谓语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用他发现自己写无意识的动作。他并未决定这样做，身体先动了，灵体的行为方式由此显出几分不祥。

---

> **原句 4:** "As I turned to look at them, I realized that their thoughts were also there, utterly plain to me, if I only focused my attention purposefully upon them."

**中文理解**：他转头看他们，发现他们的念头也在那儿，只要他有意把注意力聚过去，就一清二楚。

**句子结构**：As I turned to look at them 是时间状语从句，主句主谓宾是 I realized that their thoughts were also there，utterly plain to me 是补充，if I only focused my attention purposefully upon them 是条件状语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用只要聚过去写这门本事的开关。读心不是无时不在的骚扰，而是一种可以调用的能力，规矩由此立住。

---

> **原句 5:** "Their conversation in Clem's apartment had been a discussion between confederates, not a dispute between a constable and an illegal practitioner of enchantment."

**中文理解**：原来他们在 Clem 屋里那场对话是两个同伙在商量，而不是一个巡警在盘问一个非法行医的附魔师。

**句子结构**：主系表结构。主语是 Their conversation in Clem's apartment，表语用 be a discussion between confederates 加 not a dispute between a constable and an illegal practitioner of enchantment 的正反对照。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句重读推翻前面的印象。Sam 一直把 Maxwell 与 Wister 看成对立的两方，此处才发现自己看错了人。

---

> **原句 6:** "I felt no exhaustion or physical strain, no hunger or sleepiness; I didn't even feel the discomfort in my knees that would naturally arise from kneeling beside the bed for long stretches of time."

**中文理解**：他感觉不到疲惫或身体的吃力，不饿也不困；连久跪床边本该有的膝痛也一点没有。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I felt no exhaustion or physical strain，no hunger or sleepiness 是并列宾语；第二句主谓宾是 I didn't even feel the discomfort in my knees，that would naturally arise from kneeling beside the bed 是定语从句，for long stretches of time 是时间状语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一连串感觉不到写灵体之躯的轻盈。他得到了不受肉体拖累的自由，代价是连做人的感觉也一并失去。

---

> **原句 7:** "The Sor Juana Alliance was created centuries ago, not by Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz herself, but by others who practiced with her and wished to honor her memory."

**中文理解**：Wister 说，Sor Juana Alliance 是几百年前成立的，创办者不是 Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz 本人，而是与她一同修行、想以此纪念她的人。

**句子结构**：主系表结构。The Sor Juana Alliance 作主语，was created centuries ago 是谓语部分，后用 not… but… 结构引出两个并列施动者（Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz herself 与 others who practiced with her and wished to honor her memory），who 引导定语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句考据给这个团体立谱系。一个由纪念而起、以公正为名的组织，与 Cisneros 那种靠血脉与钱权结成的团伙正好相对。

---

> **原句 8:** "I've learned a great deal about the realm during my travels. I've learned about the Weer and the wall, the Oarings that travel overhead, and the scree. But all the spirits who inhabit the outer ring between the wall and the first isle know almost nothing of what exists beyond the first isle."

**中文理解**：Wister 说她游历中摸清了灵界的许多事：Weer、那堵墙、从头顶经过的划桨船、碎石坡；可住在墙与第一座岛之间外环的那些灵体，对第一座岛以外的东西几乎一无所知。

**句子结构**：前两句都是主谓宾（I've learned a great deal about the realm during my travels 与 I've learned about the Weer and the wall, the Oarings that travel overhead, and the scree），后一句用 that travel overhead 的定语从句修饰 Oarings；末句用 But 转折，主谓宾是 all the spirits know almost nothing，who inhabit the outer ring between the wall and the first isle 是定语从句，of what exists beyond the first isle 是介词短语作后置定语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一无所知把世界的边界推开。读者以为已经见识过灵界，这句话告诉他们那只是最外的一圈。

---

> **原句 9:** "Roy Marcel, of Port-au-Prince, known in Massachusetts as Constable Roy Maxwell. I've served the alliance since I was sixteen, and I've been an agent in Greater Boston for the last three years. I answer to Wister Amsel, the head of the Massachusetts chapter."

**中文理解**：他说自己叫 Roy Marcel，来自 Port-au-Prince，在 Massachusetts 以 Maxwell 警官的名字示人；十六岁起就替这个联盟做事，近三年在大波士顿一带当密探，上头是 Massachusetts 分部的负责人 Wister Amsel。

**句子结构**：首句是名词短语独立成句（Roy Marcel, of Port-au-Prince），known in Massachusetts as Constable Roy Maxwell 是过去分词短语作后置定语；随后三句都是主谓宾或主谓结构（I've served the alliance since I was sixteen、I've been an agent in Greater Boston for the last three years、I answer to Wister Amsel），the head of the Massachusetts chapter 是同位语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一个真名把前面所有误会结清。他从头到尾都是联盟的人，读者由此重新看待他此前每一次出现。

---

> **原句 10:** "Perhaps I can still be of assistance in my new guise."

**中文理解**：Sam 说，也许换了这副模样，他还能帮上忙。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（I can still be of assistance），Perhaps 表推测，in my new guise 是方式状语，全句十一词。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用这副模样把新身份说定。他不为失去身体哀叹，只想着还能做什么，人物的分量在这句轻描淡写里。
