# ch48 Sam: The Arbor

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 29 日，The Arbor (Ainsley residence)；Sam 以 thew 观察
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn / Rosetta Quinn / Lydia Quinn (Livia) / Walter Ainsley / Iris Ainsley / Bernie Ainsley / George Ainsley / Erauso
- **核心事件**：Clem/Rosie/Lydia reunite at Arbor；Rosie reveals she was disguised as Lydia；Sam learns Clem went to spirit realm for him
- **悬念钩子**：Clem's motivation？Sam's transformation? Clem & Sam's future?

---

> **原句 1:** "A short time later, we were once again aboard the horseless carriage, now with rather less space on the driver's seat. But Clem needed to see the road to drive, she and her mother wouldn't be parted, and I certainly wasn't going to ride inside."

**中文理解**：不久他们又坐上那辆没有马的车，这回驾驶座上的地方更挤了；可 Clem 得看着路才能驾车，她与母亲不肯分开，而 Sam 当然不会坐到车厢里去。

**句子结构**：首句主系表是 we were once again aboard the horseless carriage，now with rather less space on the driver's seat 是补充；末句用 But 转折，由 Clem needed to see the road to drive、she and her mother wouldn't be parted、I certainly wasn't going to ride inside 三个并列句构成。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用座位不够写一家人的不肯分离。一个挤字把团圆写成具体的位置安排，比任何抒情都实在。

---

> **原句 2:** "'I've been so worried for you,' Lydia said to Clem. 'That last note you sent me…' / 'I was unwell,' Clem admitted. 'Very unwell. The realm did something to me. You saw, Sam,' she said to me."

**中文理解**：Lydia 对 Clem 说她一直很担心，尤其是最后一封信；Clem 承认自己当时病了，病得很重——灵域在他身上留下了些什么，他说 Sam 看见了。

**句子结构**：引语 I've been so worried for you 是主系表，That last note you sent me… 是名词短语省略句；随后引语 I was unwell 与 Very unwell 递进，The realm did something to me 是主谓宾，You saw, Sam 是主谓句加称呼语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句承认把上一章的崩溃补上。当时只写她消沉，此刻由她本人说出原因，读者的疑问得以结清。

---

> **原句 3:** "'But now you seem better,' Lydia observed. 'What changed?' / 'It was Sam.' Clem smiled. 'Sam reminded me of who I was. None of this could have happened without him.'"

**中文理解**：Lydia 说他现在看着好些了，问是什么变了；Clem 笑着说，是 Sam——Sam 让他想起自己是谁，没有他这一切都做不成。

**句子结构**：引语 But now you seem better 后接 Lydia observed，再接 What changed? 的疑问句；随后引语 It was Sam 用强调句型，Clem smiled 是说话标签；末段引语由 Sam reminded me of who I was 与 None of this could have happened without him 两个主谓句构成。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句没有他这一切都做不成把功劳给人。前面读者只看到 Sam 暗中帮忙，此刻由 Clem 亲口点明，两人的关系也就有了对等的分量。

---

> **原句 4:** "'Thank the spirits,' she said softly. 'Especially you, Sam.' I was touched by this, but I could not say anything in reply, given the speed of Clementine's driving."

**中文理解**：Lydia 轻声说感谢灵体，尤其是 Sam；Sam 被这话触动，却答不上来——因为 Clem 把车赶得太快。

**句子结构**：引语 Thank the spirits 后接 she said softly 的说话标签，再接引语 Especially you, Sam；末句用 but 转折，I could not say anything in reply 是主谓宾，given the speed of Clementine's driving 是过去分词短语作原因状语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用答不上来写感动的笨拙。最动情的一刻被一个驾车的细节打断，这份不合时宜正是本书的调子。

---

> **原句 5:** "I was so confounded by the sight of Erauso that I was momentarily distracted from all other considerations. Erauso? But why was Erauso here, of all places? How had he arrived? When? And whom was he protecting?"

**中文理解**：看见 Erauso 让 Sam 一时把别的事全忘了：Erauso？它怎么偏偏在这儿？怎么来的？什么时候来的？它在护着谁？

**句子结构**：首句用 so… that 结构（I was so confounded by the sight of Erauso that I was momentarily distracted from all other considerations）；随后五个短问句连用（Erauso? / But why was Erauso here, of all places? / How had he arrived? / When? / And whom was he protecting?）。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一串自问把造物重新摆上台。那个骨制的东西一路跟着他们，读者的疑问也被提起来，为后文 Rosie 的真相做引子。

---

> **原句 6:** "I realized with shock why the place around me looked familiar. I had seen it in Clem's thoughts. I had seen Rosetta here, holding hands with Walter, in Clem's thoughts."

**中文理解**：Sam 吃惊地明白这地方为什么眼熟：他在 Clem 的念头里见过——Rosetta 与 Walter 手牵手站在这儿。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I realized why the place around me looked familiar，with shock 是插入状语；随后两句都是主谓宾（I had seen it in Clem's thoughts 与 I had seen Rosetta here in Clem's thoughts），holding hands with Walter 是分词短语作宾语补足语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一个似曾相识的画面把伏笔收回。前面那句被当成安慰的幻想，此刻落到了真实的院子里。

---

> **原句 7:** "Clem took my hand and looked at me earnestly. 'Sam. I'm so sorry I couldn't tell you; Mother swore me to secrecy. Do not tell a soul, were her words. I had to comply. I did almost tell you, by accident.'"

**中文理解**：Clem 握住他的手，认真地说对不起没能告诉他，因为母亲让她发过誓、不许告诉任何人，她只得照办；她还说差一点就无意中说漏了。

**句子结构**：引语内先用 Sam 作称呼，再用 I'm so sorry I couldn't tell you 与 Mother swore me to secrecy 两个并列句；随后 Do not tell a soul, were her words 用倒装引述原话；末段 I had to comply 与 I did almost tell you, by accident 并列，did 表强调。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一句道歉把此前的隐瞒说清。读者回头再看那些含糊的地方，都会得到解释，人物的沉默也就不是欺骗。

---

> **原句 8:** "'I understand,' I said, slowly reconciling myself to the deception. 'But then if you didn't go to the spirit realm for Rosetta, why did you go?' / Clem searched my face. 'You don't know?' / 'No.' I truly didn't. And then she showed me her thoughts: a strange jumble of impressions, all of them about me."

**中文理解**：Sam 说他明白，慢慢接受了这桩隐瞒，接着问：既然不是为 Rosetta 去灵域，那她去做什么？Clem 端详他的脸，问他真的不知道？他说真不知道。于是她把念头给他看——一团乱糟糟的印象，全都与他有关。

**句子结构**：引语 I understand 后接 I said 与分词短语 slowly reconciling myself to the deception；随后引语用 But then if you didn't go to the spirit realm for Rosetta, why did you go? 的条件问句；再接 Clem searched my face 与 You don't know? 的问句；末段引语由 No 与 I truly didn't 两个短句加 And then she showed me her thoughts 构成，a strange jumble of impressions, all of them about me 是同位补充。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一个反问把全书的动机翻过来。读者此前以为她只为救母亲与妹妹，此处才知道还有一个人。

---

> **原句 9:** "'Sam,' Clem said quietly. 'When I was in danger, you warned me; when I was alone, you comforted me; when the entire might of Cisneros was arrayed against me, you gave me strength.' She smiled, a tender smile that I didn't recognize and that made my stomach drop. 'I didn't know who you were, but I could feel who you were. So I went there to find you.'"

**中文理解**：Clem 轻声说，他遇险时 Sam 提醒他，他孤单时 Sam 安慰他，整个 Cisneros 压过来时 Sam 给他力气；她露出一个 Sam 从未见过的、温柔的笑，那笑让他心里一沉。她说她不知道他是谁，却能感到他是谁，所以去那边找他。

**句子结构**：引语用三个并列的 when 时间状语从句加主句（you warned me / you comforted me / you gave me strength）构成排比；随后主谓宾是 She smiled，a tender smile that I didn't recognize and that made my stomach drop 是同位语加两个 that 定语从句；末段引语用 I didn't know who you were, but I could feel who you were 与 So I went there to find you 并列。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用三个并列句把一路上的陪伴数一遍。此前那些被当作旁白的示警与推情绪，在此全部被重新认领为情意。

---

> **原句 10:** "There was a strange sensation in my chest, a peculiar feeling similar to what I'd felt when Clementine took the shroud: a sudden lightness, as if I'd just given a part of myself away. I couldn't think how to reply. I took Clem's face in my hands, and she didn't recoil at the coldness of my fingers. Her eyes met mine; she smiled. I leaned forward and pressed my forehead to hers."

**中文理解**：Sam 胸口涌起一种奇异的感觉，像 Clementine 取下那层外壳时的那一次：忽然一轻，仿佛刚把身上的一部分交了出去。他想不出怎么回答，只捧住 Clem 的脸——她没有因为他手指的冰冷而退缩；她的眼睛对上他的，笑了；他倾身，把额头抵上她的。

**句子结构**：首句是 There was 结构，a peculiar feeling similar to what I'd felt when Clementine took the shroud 是同位语，冒号后 a sudden lightness 是补充，as if I'd just given a part of myself away 是虚拟比较从句；随后 I couldn't think how to reply 是主谓宾；再接主谓宾 I took Clem's face in my hands，and she didn't recoil at the coldness of my fingers 并列；末段用 Her eyes met mine; she smiled 与 I leaned forward and pressed my forehead to hers 两个短句收束。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一个额头的接触代替回答。他给不出话，只能给出动作，这份笨拙比任何告白都更像 Sam。

---

> **原句 11:** "There we were: two souls, standing together, watching the sun set over the autumn garden."

**中文理解**：Sam 说：就这样，两个灵魂并排站着，看太阳落在那片秋日的园子上。

**句子结构**：主系表结构。There we were 是倒装句，冒号后 two souls 是同位语，standing together 与 watching the sun set over the autumn garden 是两个分词短语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用两个灵魂收束全章。前面写了那么多身份的差别，最后一句把它抹平，读者由此得到片刻的安宁。
