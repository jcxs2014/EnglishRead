# ch43 Sam: Summoning

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：Late September 1888，Keel House；Sam 以 thew 观察
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn / Wister Amsel / Gertie / Meg / Roy Marcel (Maxwell)
- **核心事件**：Sam 介绍 spirit realm outer reaches；Clem's depression；Sam-Wister dialogue about spirits' attachment to living world；successful summoning of Bess (Gertie's friend)；Clem's recovery begins
- **悬念钩子**：Sam's summoning power？Clem's recovery？Grand Séance preparation？

---

> **原句 1:** "A curved, wavering line marked the stone wall that separated the inner realm from the Weer."

**中文理解**：Sam 说，一道弯弯曲曲的线标出了那道石墙的位置——它把灵界内层与 Weer 隔开。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（A curved, wavering line marked the stone wall），that separated the inner realm from the Weer 是定语从句，用 separate… from… 结构。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一条线交代地形。灵界的方位此前只是感觉，此处第一次有了可指认的界线，读者也随之能画出那张图。

---

> **原句 2:** "The spirits who dwell between the wall and the first isle are still, in some ways, attached to the living world. They watch the living, they think about the living, and they wait in hope of being contacted by the living."

**中文理解**：引文说，住在墙与第一座岛之间的那些灵体，在某些方面仍与活人的世界相连：他们看着活人，想着活人，等着被活人联络。

**句子结构**：首句主系表（The spirits… are still attached to the living world），who dwell between the wall and the first isle 是定语从句，in some ways 是插入的状语；末句用三个并列主谓宾（They watch the living、they think about the living、they wait in hope of being contacted by the living）构成排比。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用看着、想着、等着三个动词把灵体写成执念的化身。这一章要写的正是这种挂念，读者由此理解 Clem 为何能把人唤回来。

---

> **原句 3:** "At Keel House, Clementine seemed still mired in a state of impassivity. Often when I considered her thoughts, I saw the vast, stony expanse of the realm."

**中文理解**：在 Keel House，Clementine 似乎仍陷在一片无动于衷里；Sam 每次去看她的念头，看到的都是灵界那片辽阔的石头地。

**句子结构**：首句主系表（Clementine seemed still mired in a state of impassivity），At Keel House 是地点状语；第二句用 Often when I considered her thoughts 作时间状语从句，主句是 I saw the vast, stony expanse of the realm。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用看到的都是石头地把她的消沉写成一种留驻。她没有哭闹，只是心还留在那边，这份沉默比哀哭更沉。

---

> **原句 4:** "And so the act of summoning was mostly an exercise in judicious selection: of finding a spirit who wished to speak."

**中文理解**：所以召唤这件事，说到底是一场审慎的挑选——挑出一个愿意开口的灵体。

**句子结构**：主系表（the act of summoning was mostly an exercise in judicious selection），冒号后用 of finding a spirit 的介词短语作同位解释，who wished to speak 是定语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用挑一个愿意开口的把召唤的法门说破。读者由此明白这门本事靠的不是法力，而是找到肯说话的人。

---

> **原句 5:** "Once I was able to reach the realm, we found that Clementine's stamina did not last long enough for me to venture far. She was, after all, still recovering."

**中文理解**：Sam 说，等他终于能进入灵界，他们发现 Clem 的体力撑不了多久，他走不了太远；毕竟她还在恢复。

**句子结构**：Once I was able to reach the realm 是时间状语从句，主句主谓宾是 we found that Clementine's stamina did not last long enough，for me to venture far 是结果状语；末句主系表是 She was, after all, still recovering，after all 是插入语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用撑不了多久把两个人的合作写成互相牵制。他能走多远取决于她的气力，这份依赖为后文她的崩溃埋下伏笔。

---

> **原句 6:** "Success occurred at last on the day the residues vanished. Clem's mood hadn't lifted; she still moved through the day as if forcing herself to complete each task. But with the residues gone she seemed less burdened, and she proposed that we attempt a summoning in the front parlor for the benefit of Gertie, Meg, and Wister."

**中文理解**：成功终于出现在那些残影消失的那天。Clem 的情绪并没有好转，她仍旧像在逼自己把每件事做完；但残影一去，她像是轻了些，于是提议在前厅给 Gertie、Meg 和 Wister 试一次召唤。

**句子结构**：首句主谓句是 Success occurred at last，on the day the residues vanished 是时间状语，the residues vanished 是省略 that 的定语从句；第二句用分号连接 Clem's mood hadn't lifted 与 she still moved through the day，as if forcing herself to complete each task 是虚拟比较；末句用 But 转折，with the residues gone 是 with 复合结构，she seemed less burdened 与 she proposed that… 并列。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用轻了些写好转的开端。她没有振作，只是少背了一层东西，这份细微的差别正是后面一切转机的起点。

---

> **原句 7:** "I sat with my eyes closed at the table and Clementine stood behind me, her hands resting on my shoulders, a gentle pressure I did not mind in the least."

**中文理解**：Sam 闭眼坐在桌旁，Clementine 站在他身后，两手搭在他肩上——那点轻柔的按压，他一点也不介意。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I sat with my eyes closed at the table；第二句用 and Clementine stood behind me 并列，her hands resting on my shoulders 是独立主格结构，a gentle pressure I did not mind in the least 是同位语加省略 that 的定语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一点也不介意写两人之间已经不必言明的东西。灵体没有触觉可言，他仍说介意与否，读者由此看出他把自己当人看。

---

> **原句 8:** "I had the uncomfortable sensation, as the spirit of Bess looked over my shoulder, that I was serving as a kind of window between the spirit realm and Keel House. Bess could see me, but she could also see through me into the living world."

**中文理解**：Sam 有种不舒服的感觉：当 Bess 的灵从他肩头望过去时，他像是成了一扇窗——一边是灵界，一边是 Keel House。Bess 能看见他，也能穿过他看见活人的世界。

**句子结构**：首句主谓宾是 I had the uncomfortable sensation that I was serving as a kind of window，as the spirit of Bess looked over my shoulder 是插入的时间状语从句，between the spirit realm and Keel House 是后置定语；末句用 but 连接 Bess could see me 与 she could also see through me into the living world 两个并列句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一扇窗写他的位置。他既不属于这边也不属于那边，这份夹在中间的处境是他此后一切行动的底子。

---

> **原句 9:** "We did it, Sam," she whispered, closing her eyes. / "We did it," I agreed. We'd accomplished a summoning. Now we had only to find the spirit we sought before the next Grand Séance at the Salem Lyceum."

**中文理解**：她闭着眼低声说他们做到了；Sam 应了一声，也说是。他们完成了一次召唤，接下来只要在下一次 Salem Lyceum 的大降神会之前找到他们要的那个灵体。

**句子结构**：引语 We did it, Sam 后接 she whispered 与分词短语 closing her eyes；第二段引语 We did it 后接 I agreed；随后 We'd accomplished a summoning 是主谓宾，Now we had only to find the spirit we sought before the next Grand Séance at the Salem Lyceum 用 only to 表唯一要务，we sought 是省略 that 的定语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用同一句话的两次重复收束全章。两个人的合声把这一场的意义定下来，也顺手把下一幕的时限交代清楚。
