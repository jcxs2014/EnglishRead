# ch14 Sam: Spirit Casting

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：1888 年 9 月 7 日，Commonwealth Pebbles；Sam 以 thew 观察 Spirit Casting 课
- **人物**：Sam（叙述者）/ Clem Quinn / Professor Selwyn Mead / Cassandra（Mead 的 thew）/ Simeon Trench
- **核心事件**：Mead 介绍 spirit casting 的历史与 categories；Cassandra 演示 shape-shifting 与 reading students；Cassandra 揭露 Clem Quinn 是 woman
- **悬念钩子**：Clem 被揭露为 woman 的后果？Mead 的 lesson 的目的？

---

> **原句 1:** "It is possible to guard one's thoughts from the uninvited gaze of a spirit thew, and no enchantment is required to do so. One requires only deliberate focus on a neutral object or subject."

**中文理解**：引文说，人可以把心思护住，不让灵体奴仆不请自来地窥看，而且不必借助附魔：只需刻意把注意力放在一个无关的人或物上。

**句子结构**：It is possible to guard… 是形式主语结构，真正的主语是 to guard one's thoughts；第二句 One requires only deliberate focus on a neutral object or subject 是主谓宾，only 限定焦点。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：先立一条防御规则，读者才知道 Clem 为何能瞒过 Cassandra，也知道这条规则有多脆——一个走神就够了。

---

> **原句 2:** "It was clear to me that the words scored onto Quinn's back were only a first warning. Simeon, and the larger Society around him, would not stop at a few malicious scratches."

**中文理解**：Sam 清楚，刻在 Quinn 背上的字只是第一次警告；Simeon 以及他背后更大的 Society 不会止于几道恶意的划痕。

**句子结构**：It was clear to me that… 是形式主语结构，从句是 the words scored onto Quinn's back were only a first warning，其中 scored 是过去分词作后置定语；第二句以 Simeon 与同位语 the larger Society around him 作主语，would not stop at a few malicious scratches 作谓语部分。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用只是第一次把伤害写成序章。读者由此知道后面的冲突会更重，也把 Simeon 从个人恶少升格为组织的一员。

---

> **原句 3:** "Whatever the reason, her assistance made it possible for Quinn to pay the warning less attention than he should have."

**中文理解**：不管出于什么原因，Livia 的帮忙让 Quinn 能够比应当的更不把那次警告放在心上。

**句子结构**：Whatever the reason 作让步状语；主句用 make it possible for sb to do 结构（her assistance made it possible for Quinn to pay the warning less attention than he should have），末带比较从句 than he should have。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用因果不明的让步句写善意带来的坏处。Livia 帮他治伤，却让他松懈，这一层反讽为下一章他被揭穿做了铺垫。

---

> **原句 4:** "Spirit Casting is taught by the venerable Professor Selwyn Mead. His spirit thew, an ancient one by the name of Cassandra, was said to be as lovely as she was powerful."

**中文理解**：这门课由德高望重的 Selwyn Mead 教授；他的灵体奴仆是个名叫 Cassandra 的老灵，据说既美又强。

**句子结构**：首句是被动语态（Spirit Casting is taught by…）；第二句用插入语 an ancient one by the name of Cassandra 补充身份，was said to be as lovely as she was powerful 用同级比较作表语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用据说二字先给人物镀一层传闻的光泽。传闻越盛，后文她在课堂上随手揭穿 Clem 才越显得轻易。

---

> **原句 5:** "'Spirit Casting,' Mead said, looking at his papers again as if to confirm the veracity of the statement, 'is the practice of casting power through a spirit thew. It is the foundation of advanced enchantment, which is, needless to say, impossible to practice without a spirit thew.'"

**中文理解**：Mead 说，所谓灵体施法，就是借灵体奴仆施展力量；它是高级附魔的基础，而高级附魔离了灵体奴仆自然无从谈起。

**句子结构**：引语内先给定义（is the practice of casting power through a spirit thew），再用 It is the foundation of advanced enchantment 作判断，末尾用 which is impossible to practice without a spirit thew 的非限定定语从句补充，needless to say 是插入语。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：借教授之口把课程的重要性说足，也顺手为 Clem 立下目标：她若真想证明女人能行，就得先弄到一个灵体。

---

> **原句 6:** "Cassandra began turning away slowly from the room, as if she had finally lost all interest in its occupants, until her back was to the students. And then she continued turning, slowly, as her aspect altered gradually from head to toe. When she had completed the slow twirl, Laurence Fishbane, founder of Commonwealth University, stood in her place."

**中文理解**：Cassandra 慢慢转过身去，仿佛终于对满室的人失去兴趣，直到背对学生；随后她继续转，形貌自头至脚一点点改变。转完这一圈，站在原处的已是大学的创办人 Laurence Fishbane。

**句子结构**：主句 Cassandra began turning away… 后接 as if 引导的方式状语从句与 until 时间状语从句；第二句用 And then 起头，as her aspect altered gradually from head to toe 作时间状语；末句以 When 引导时间状语从句，主句是 Laurence Fishbane… stood in her place。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：把变形写成一个缓慢的过程而不是一瞬间的把戏。转得越慢，读者越看清这不是障眼法，也更能体会台下学生的错愕。

---

> **原句 7:** "Mead indulged in a quiet chuckle. 'Very apropos, Cassandra. And very convincing,' he said to the students, 'as you can see. A talented and experienced thew, such as Cassandra, can effect a transformation so persuasive that it even deceives other thews.'"

**中文理解**：Mead 轻声笑了，说这个扮相十分贴切，也很有说服力；他告诉学生，像 Cassandra 这样有天赋、有年资的灵体奴仆，做出的变形足以连别的灵体都骗过。

**句子结构**：主句 Mead indulged in a quiet chuckle；引语 Very apropos, Cassandra. And very convincing 后接说话标签 he said to the students；末句以 A talented and experienced thew 作主语，such as Cassandra 作插入语，can effect a transformation 后接 so… that 结果状语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用连别的灵体都骗过把她的能力推到极处。这句话是给读者的提醒：Cassandra 的眼睛比谁都准，她在课堂上开口，Clem 就藏不住。

---

> **原句 8:** "She lifted her arm slowly and held her hand out, palm open, facing downward; she might have been taking the pulse of some invisible being who lay before her."

**中文理解**：她缓缓抬手前伸，掌心向下张开，像在替一个看不见的、躺在她面前的人把脉。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（She lifted her arm slowly and held her hand out）后接两个独立结构 palm open 与 facing downward 作伴随状语；分号后用 might have been taking the pulse of… 的虚拟语气作比，who lay before her 是定语从句。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用一个医疗式的动作写感知。把读心写成把脉，既减轻了侵犯感，也让随后报出的隐私听起来像诊断结果。

---

> **原句 9:** "Her voice was slow, ponderous. 'There is one person in this room who spent the whole of last night at his father's sickbed.' Her hand shifted slightly in a balletic gesture, and her eyes flickered. 'There is another who dove, last spring, into the cold waters of the Charles to save a drowning man.' She paused. The room was utterly silent. 'Another has a stain upon his heart; he wishes the death of his brother.'"

**中文理解**：她的声音缓慢而沉：这屋里有人昨夜整夜守在父亲的病榻旁；有人去年春天跳进 Charles 河冰冷的河水里去救一个溺水的人；还有人心里有块污痕，他盼着自己的兄弟死掉。

**句子结构**：首句主系表（Her voice was slow, ponderous）；随后三个 There is 句型引出三条爆料，中间插入分词短语 Her hand shifted slightly in a balletic gesture, and her eyes flickered 与短句 She paused；末句 The room was utterly silent 是主系表。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：用三条由轻到重的爆料排成梯级。前两条无害，第三条近乎指控，读者刚放松就被击中，最后那句不属于这里才有落点。

---

> **原句 10:** "Cassandra's gaze shifted again, and she seemed to look toward the back of the room. 'And yet another does not belong here.' Her lovely bare arm and delicate finger pointed at Clem Quinn. 'That one, at the very back,' she said, her voice cool, imperious, and certain, 'is not a man at all. She is a woman.'"

**中文理解**：Cassandra 的目光再次移动，似乎看向教室后排：还有一个人不属于这里。她那只光洁的手臂与纤细的手指指向 Clem Quinn，语气冷淡、专断而笃定地说，最后面那个根本不是男人，是个女人。

**句子结构**：主谓宾（Cassandra's gaze shifted again）后接 and she seemed to look toward the back of the room；引语 And yet another does not belong here 独立成句；随后主谓宾写 Her lovely bare arm and delicate finger pointed at Clem Quinn；末句引语中插入 her voice cool, imperious, and certain 作独立主格，主句是 That one… is not a man at all，再用 She is a woman 收尾。

**关键词**：

**为什么这样写**：把揭穿放在一串爆料之后，用平淡的语气宣布最重的事。冷、专断、笃定三个词写的是她的态度，也让 Clem 无从辩解。
