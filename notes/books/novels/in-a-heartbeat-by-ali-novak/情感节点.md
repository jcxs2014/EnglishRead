# In a Heartbeat（Ali Novak）· 情感节点

> 10 个节点，引语逐字取自 epub 原文。

## 节点 1｜被机器与家人同时"评判"的一天（ch01）

沙发、Netflix、一年多的康复期。姐姐带着明星男友进门，她顶着油腻头发和痘痘贴迎接自己的偶像——五年暗恋的人第一次站在客厅里。

> "I don't know whether to be impressed or alarmed."

> There was so much life to be lived, but what was the point of making plans for someone like me?

## 节点 2｜清单出土：十五岁的自己来接班（ch02）

被逼清理衣柜，翻出埋而未埋的时间胶囊。16 条愿望、一条没完成，除了"见 Heartbreakers"——而偶像正在楼下做饭。

> I quickly learned that you couldn't make plans for cancer.

> Maybe, just maybe, if I completed this list, I could break free from the standstill I was trapped in and stop squandering the remaining sand in my hourglass.

## 节点 3｜床底相遇：匿名是礼物（ch03）

派对无聊到逃跑，她藏进 Drew 的床底衣堆，撞破 JJ 的一通电话。被认出、被调侃、被邀请下楼——第一次被名人当普通人对待。

> "No one down there knows who you are, which means you can be whoever you want tonight. Take my word for it. That's a gift."

> "Fine," I said and placed my hand in his. "Let's do this."

## 节点 4｜假女友契约成立（ch07）

独木舟划进红树林，Layla 的前史浮出水面。他用"条件交换"提出请求，她用"我不评判"接住他的软肋。

> "I need you to pretend to be my girlfriend."

> "Until I have enough time to dedicate to another person, I'm going to continue choosing me."

## 节点 5｜冰湖与察看期：把朋友赢回来（ch05）

清单第十三条极地冬泳，报名桌后坐着被她推开两年的 Trixy。愤怒退成漠然后，一湖水换回一条毛巾。

> Anger meant she still cared. Seeing the indifference on her face though? That hurt beyond words, even though I knew I deserved it.

> "It means you're on probation," she answered.

## 节点 6｜码头真吻：誓言破产（ch15）

生日烟花散场后的木码头上，玻璃鳄鱼之后是真吻。裸泳时她"从不骂人"的秘密被看见——那本临终审计的账，当晚被两人一起喊掉的 f-bomb 作废。

> "You," I admitted.

> "Every little bit counts, right?"

> Of all the burdens I carried, I didn't know why I'd held on to this one for so long.

## 节点 7｜"昨晚只是清单第十一项"：亲手推开（ch18）

初夜清晨变成分手现场。他提出把假变真，她用谎话回答；他看穿一切，把"不为自己而战"的判词留在门口。

> "The rest of the world already thinks we're dating, Cara. I'd really like that to be true."

> "Last night was number eleven."

> "If you think I don't know what you're doing, you're wrong. But I won't fight for someone who won't fight for herself."

## 节点 8｜Trixy 的病床：最后一课（ch19）

拒信、姐姐的判词、挚友车祸——一周内三连击。病床上的 Trixy 拒绝被当悲情符号，用截肢者的身体讲完"活着"的算术。

> "But, Cara, that's life."

> "Being afraid of what might happen seems like a terrible way to spend your life."

> "Good. It's about damn time you stopped waiting for permission to live."

## 节点 9｜埋胶囊与 Always：把过去放下，把结果一起看（ch20）

她同一天完成三件不敢的事：预约 screening、与姐姐埋下时间胶囊、飞去 LA 道歉。他早看穿了谎言，原谅只要一个"That's it"；化验结果，两人一起点开。

> two sisters burying the past not to forget it, but to mark where we were brave enough to start again.

> "I was afraid," I confessed. "I'm still afraid."

> "Hey. Whatever that says, I'm right here."

## 节点 10｜EKG 与雨：心跳成为鼓点（Epilogue）

她把心电线纹在旧疤上，永久备忘"别停止活着"；暴雨里他修订第八条，她跟着走进雨里——朝他走每一步都像走进未来。

> My tattoo would be a permanent reminder to myself to never stop living.

> "From this day forth, number eight of your bucket list will be as follows: Kiss a stranger or your boyfriend in the rain."

> I was drenched in a heartbeat, but that was okay. I'd never felt more alive.
