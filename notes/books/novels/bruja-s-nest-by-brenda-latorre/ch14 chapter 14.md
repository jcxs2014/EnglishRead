## 一句话总结

本章 Panchito hatches (beak-faced baby); Yolanda brings baby to Alta (shocked); Odette reveals 12 miscarriages and Javier’s fetal collection; Don Julián accepts Panchito as Valentín’s son (with skepticism); funeral in garden—Yolanda’s scheme succeeds (Don Julián promises "Yolanda and your son will live on hacienda grounds") but Panchito’s deformity threatens acceptance.

## 读者视角提示

- Panchito’s deformity (beak, black eyes, no ears) is central horror—Yolanda must hide/accept
- Odette’s 12 miscarriages + Javier’s fetal collection shows hacienda’s horror beneath surface
- Don Julián’s "Is that really my son’s son? Or is it someone else’s?" questions paternity
- Yolanda’s "Nothing is wrong with him" shows maternal defense despite deformity
- Odette’s "orphanages aren’t so bad" threat shows family’s power over Panchito
