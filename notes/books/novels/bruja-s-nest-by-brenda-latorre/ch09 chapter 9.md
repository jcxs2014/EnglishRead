# ch09 Chapter 9

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：Afontla 街道 → doctor’s house → Hacienda Cantera；Valentín 受伤后
- **人物**：Yolanda；doctor；Alta；Valentín；Don Julián；Javier；Odette
- **核心事件**：Yolanda 去 doctor’s house 被拒（需 upfront payment）；Alta 派 Yolanda 照料 Valentín；Javier 自称 "his doctor"（medical school）；Don Julián 信任 Javier；Yolanda 与 Itzco 计划让 Valentín "pretend baby’s his"
- **悬念钩子**：Javier 是否真的能医 Valentín？Yolanda/Itzco 的 scheme 是否会成功？Valentín 是否会发现 truth？

---

> **原句 1:** "It must be eleven in the morning because the sun is high in the sky. The temperature keeps rising, but it’s not yet at its peak."

**中文理解**：Yolanda 去 doctor’s house——时间/温度细节显示 urgency 与 heat. "Not yet at its peak" foreshadows worse to come.

**句子结构**：两句话：It must be eleven... + The temperature keeps rising...。

**关键词**：
- `temperature keeps rising`：温度持续上升——urgency/heat
- `not yet at its peak`：尚未达峰值——worse coming

**为什么这样写**：Heat as backdrop for Valentín’s injury—Yolanda’s journey under sun mirrors his suffering。

---

> **原句 2:** "“Hey.” I smile at the one with the red shirt. He doesn’t return the gesture."

**中文理解**：Yolanda 问路——kid 不回应. Shows town’s hostility to hacienda staff. "I drop the antics then" shows her frustration。

**句子结构**：引语 + 叙述者评注（He doesn’t return the gesture）。

**关键词**：
- `doesn’t return the gesture`：不回应——hostility

**为什么这样写**：Town’s hostility to hacienda staff isolates Yolanda—she represents the family she serves。

---

> **原句 3:** "“I told you,” says the kid to his friend in the red shirt. “It’s those ugly dresses they give them. You can tell right away.” He scrunches his nose. “They smell so bad too. Like a cow or something.”"

**中文理解**：Kid’s insult—“ugly dresses,” "smell so bad... like a cow"—shows town’s contempt for hacienda staff. Yolanda’s "I shake my head" shows her shame。

**句子结构**：引语 + 叙述者评注 + 引语。

**关键词**：
- `ugly dresses they give them`：给他们的丑衣服——town’s contempt
- `smell so bad too. Like a cow`：闻起来像牛——dehumanization

**为什么这样写**：Town’s contempt for staff mirrors hacienda’s class hierarchy—Yolanda is despised by both。

---

> **原句 4:** "“Hey! Is it true then?” ... “What?” ... “That the hacienda is embrujada.”"

**中文理解**：Kid asks if hacienda is "embrujada" (bewitched). Yolanda’s "I stick out my tongue at them" shows defiance. " embrujada" connects to bruja/Cesarea。

**句子结构**：三问：Hey! Is it true then? + What? + That the hacienda is embrujada。

**关键词**：
- `embrujada`：被施魔法的——hacienda’s supernatural reputation
- `stick out my tongue`：吐舌头——defiance

**为什么这样写**：Hacienda’s "embrujada" reputation connects to bruja—town fears hacienda as much as hacienda fears bruja。

---

> **原句 5:** "One of them says, “The doctor’s house is five blocks to your left. It’s blue and impossible to miss.”"

**中文理解**：Kid gives directions—blue house, impossible to miss. Yolanda’s "I smile but don’t turn around" shows her relief and caution。

**句子结构**：引语。

**关键词**：
- `blue and impossible to miss`：蓝色且不可能错过——direction

**为什么这样写**：Kid’s help shows town’s complexity—contempt but also kindness. Yolanda navigates both。

---

> **原句 6:** "“Yes?” The doctor crosses his arms. He doesn’t open the metallic fence that keeps him safe from the people out here."

**中文理解**：Doctor’s "Yes?" + crossed arms + not opening fence—shows his defensiveness and suspicion. "Keeps him safe from the people out here" shows town’s danger。

**句子结构**：引语 + 两句话：He crosses his arms. He doesn’t open...

**关键词**：
- `crosses his arms`：交叉双臂——defensiveness
- `keeps him safe from the people out here`：保护他不受外面的人伤害——town’s danger

**为什么这样写**：Doctor’s defensiveness mirrors hacienda’s—both are fortresses against town’s hostility。

---

> **原句 7:** "“But doctor! Doctors make a pledge, don’t they? They’ll save anyone if they can. That’s what you’ve promised.”"

**中文理解**：Yolanda appeals to doctor’s oath—"Doctors make a pledge... They’ll save anyone." Doctor rejects: "Lots of people have accidents."

**句子结构**：引语 + 从句（don’t they?）+ 陈述（That’s what you’ve promised）。

**关键词**：
- `Doctors make a pledge`：医生发誓——hippocratic oath appeal
- `They’ll save anyone if they can`：他们能救任何人就救——oath’s promise

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s appeal to medical ethics fails—doctor prioritizes payment over oath, showing town’s transactional nature。

---

> **原句 8:** "“Come back with the money, or not at all,” he says and shuts the door behind him."

**中文理解**：Doctor’s ultimatum—"Come back with the money, or not at all." Shows town’s economy: money first, life second。

**句子结构**：引语 + 动作（shuts the door）。

**关键词**：
- `Come back with the money, or not at all`：带上钱回来，否则别来——ultimatum

**为什么这样写**：Doctor’s door-shutting mirrors Cesarea’s gate—both are barriers Yolanda must cross with payment。

---

> **原句 9:** "Alta is already waiting for me at the front door, her lips pursed."

**中文理解**：Alta’s "lips pursed"—shows anger/anxiety. She grabs silver cross with left hand—"Half of me expects her to raise it as if I were a vampire." Shows Alta’s religious authority。

**句子结构**：简单陈述句 + 叙述者评注。

**关键词**：
- `lips pursed`：嘴唇紧闭——anger/anxiety
- `raise it as if I were a vampire`：像对吸血鬼一样举起——religious authority

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s cross as weapon—she uses religion to control Yolanda。

---

> **原句 10:** "“He won’t come, Alta. He said we have to pay upfront. We owe money to too many people. He doesn’t trust the family.”"

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s report—doctor won’t come, needs upfront payment, family owes money. Shows hacienda’s financial precarity。

**句子结构**：三句话：He won’t come... + He said we have to pay upfront + We owe money... + He doesn’t trust...

**关键词**：
- `pay upfront`：预付——financial demand
- `We owe money to too many people`：我们欠太多人钱——financial precarity

**为什么这样写**：Hacienda’s debt is town’s open secret—Yolanda’s report exposes family’s vulnerability。

---

> **原句 11:** "“We could sell those,” I tell Alta. “And maybe the silver as well? Or perhaps we wouldn’t even have to sell them. We could give them directly to the doctor.”"

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s suggestion—sell taxidermied birds/silver to pay doctor. Shows her pragmatism. Alta rejects: "Those decisions aren’t up to us."

**句子结构**：三句话：We could sell those + And maybe the silver as well + Or perhaps we wouldn’t even have to sell them...

**关键词**：
- `sell those`：卖那些——pragmatic suggestion
- `give them directly to the doctor`：直接给医生——alternative payment

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s pragmatism vs Alta’s class boundary—staff cannot make financial decisions for family。

---

> **原句 12:** "“Those decisions aren’t up to us.” Her jaw tightens even more. “We are not family. All we can do is care for him now.”"

**中文理解**：Alta’s "Those decisions aren’t up to us" + "We are not family"—shows class boundary. "All we can do is care for him now" is Yolanda’s opportunity。

**句子结构**：两句话：Those decisions aren’t up to us + We are not family. All we can do...

**关键词**：
- `Those decisions aren’t up to us`：这些决定不由我们——class boundary
- `We are not family`：我们不是家人——exclusion
- `All we can do is care for him now`：我们所能做的就是照顾他——Yolanda’s opportunity

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s "We are not family" is ironic—she later says "She’s like a daughter to me" about Odette, but Yolanda is excluded。

---

> **原句 13:** "“You’ll be in charge of him,” says Alta."

**中文理解**：Alta assigns Yolanda to care for Valentín—"You’ll be in charge of him." Yolanda: "Me? This is my opportunity." Shows her calculation。

**句子结构**：引语 + 叙述者评注（says Alta）。

**关键词**：
- `You’ll be in charge of him`：你负责他——assignment
- `This is my opportunity`：这是我的机会——Yolanda’s calculation

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s assignment gives Yolanda access to Valentín—her scheme can proceed under cover of caregiving。

---

> **原句 14:** "“Me?” This is my opportunity. To be close to him. Everything will have to be done on a whim, before my stomach swells, revealing it as impossible for the baby to be his."

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s "Me? This is my opportunity"—she sees caregiving as seduction opportunity. "Before my stomach swells, revealing it as impossible for the baby to be his" shows her timeline anxiety。

**句子结构**：引语 + 两句话：This is my opportunity... + Everything will have to be done on a whim...

**关键词**：
- `This is my opportunity`：这是我的机会——calculation
- `before my stomach swells, revealing it as impossible for the baby to be his`：在肚子 swelling 前——timeline anxiety

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s "everything on a whim" shows her urgency—she must seduce Valentín before pregnancy is visible。

---

> **原句 15:** "“Señor Valentín,” I say, and slowly push his good arm to wake him."

**中文理解**：Yolanda wakes Valentín—"Señor Valentín" formal address. "Slowly push his good arm" shows her gentleness (performative)。

**句子结构**：引语 + 动作（and slowly push his good arm to wake him）。

**关键词**：
- `Señor Valentín`：Valentín 先生——formal address
- `slowly push his good arm to wake him`：慢慢推他的好手臂——gentleness

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s gentleness with Valentín contrasts with her ruthlessness with Itzco—she performs care for seduction。

---

> **原句 16:** "“Don’t worry. I’m Itzco’s friend,” I say."

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s "I’m Itzco’s friend"—lie to gain Valentín’s trust. "He relaxes back into the bed" shows it works。

**句子结构**：引语。

**关键词**：
- `I’m Itzco’s friend`：我是 Itzco 的朋友——lie for trust

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s lie (Itzco’s friend) is strategic—she uses Itzco’s name to gain Valentín’s comfort, while simultaneously using Itzco for scheme。

---

> **原句 17:** "“I’m not a visitor. I’m his doctor.” I’m grateful his back is turned to me, so he doesn’t detect the shock on my face."

**中文理解**：Javier’s "I’m his doctor"—reveals he went to medical school. Yolanda’s "shock" shows her surprise. "Grateful his back is turned" shows her hiding reaction。

**句子结构**：引语 + 叙述者评注（I’m grateful his back is turned...）。

**关键词**：
- `I’m his doctor`：我是他的医生——Javier’s credential
- `shock on my face`：脸上的震惊——Yolanda’s surprise

**为什么这样写**：Javier’s medical credential complicates Yolanda’s view of him—not just taxidermist/predator, but doctor. Shows hacienda’s resourcefulness。

---

> **原句 18:** "“It’s very easy,” says Don Julián. “Our Javier here”—he extends his hand as if I didn’t know who Javier is—“he went to medical school."

**中文理解**：Don Julián’s "It’s very easy"—Javier went to medical school. "As if I didn’t know who Javier is" shows Don Julián’s condescension to Yolanda。

**句子结构**：引语 + 叙述者评注。

**关键词**：
- `Our Javier here`：我们的 Javier——possessive
- `he went to medical school`：他上过医学院——credential

**为什么这样写**：Don Julián’s "Our Javier" shows Javier’s integration into family—unlike Itzco (gay, outsider), Javier is accepted (straight, married to Odette)。

---

## 一句话总结

本章 Yolanda 被拒于 doctor（需 upfront payment）；Alta 派她照料 Valentín， giving her access; Javier reveals medical credentials (medical school) and becomes Valentín’s doctor; Don Julián trusts Javier; Yolanda proposes Itzco ask Valentín to "pretend baby’s his"—scheme continues despite Valentín’s injury。

## 读者视角提示

- Doctor’s refusal shows town’s transactional nature—money before life
- Alta’s "We are not family" excludes Yolanda from decision-making, but assigns her caregiving (access)
- Javier’s medical credentials complicate his character—he’s not just predator but resource
- Yolanda’s "everything on a whim, before my stomach swells" shows timeline anxiety
- Yolanda/Itzco scheme (Valentín pretend father) shows her desperation and Itzco’s reluctant complicity
