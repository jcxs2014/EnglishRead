## 一句话总结

本章 Panchito desecrates Valentín’s grave; Don Julián confronts Yolanda ("unsustainable"); Yolanda makes hot chocolate with toloache, serves to Don Julián; Don Julián dies (convulsions); Panchito pecks out Don Julián’s eyes; Odette banishes Yolanda; Yolanda packs and prepares to leave with Panchito—her scheme fails catastrophically。

## 读者视角提示

- Panchito’s grave desecration shows his (animalistic) behavior—Yolanda must defend him
- Don Julián’s "unsustainable" shows his (limited) rejection—Yolanda must leave, but he offers money
- Toloache (love potion) backfires—Don Julián dies from convulsions, not love
- Panchito pecks out Don Julián’s eyes—shows his (supernatural) hunger for flesh/eyes
- Odette’s banishment ("You get out of here... hand you over to the police/people") shows family’s (limited) rejection
