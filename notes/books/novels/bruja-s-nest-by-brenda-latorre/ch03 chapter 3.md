# ch03 Chapter 3

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：Hacienda Cantera 室内（图书馆、餐厅、供应室、宿舍）；Yolanda 第一日到一个月后
- **人物**：Yolanda；Altagracia/Alta；Itzco；Inés；Berenice；Don Julián；Odette；Javier；Valentín；Josefa
- **核心事件**：Alta 带 Yolanda 参观 hacienda；Yolanda 询问工资被拒；晚餐观察中迷上 Valentín；Alta 警告 staff 不得与 family 混淆，并称 Valentín "crooked"；一个月后 Alta 因地毯责骂 Yolanda，Itzco 告诫 beware Alta 与 Javier
- **悬念钩子**：Valentín "crooked" 的诅咒？Alta 的 "clean your sins" 与 Mamá 的 demonization 呼应；Javier 的 "deal" 与 Odette 的 open relationship 暗示 hacienda 的性政治

---

> **原句 1:** "Altagracia walks so fast that her black dress billows behind her. We go through an arched hallway, the floor black-and-white marble."

**中文理解**：Alta 的黑裙在身后飘动，Yolanda 被迫追上——管家的 authority 从身体节奏开始。黑白大理石地板暗示 hacienda 的二元性：秩序与阴影并存。

**句子结构**：主从复合句："Altagracia walks so fast that..."（结果状语从句）+ "We go through..."（并列主句）。

**关键词**：
- `billows behind her`：裙裾飘动——Alta 的 authoritative presence
- `black-and-white marble`：黑白大理石——hacienda 的秩序与阴影

**为什么这样写**：以 Alta 的步伐设定 power dynamic——Yolanda 始终在追，从未 lead。

---

> **原句 2:** "“Here is the library, all the way down the hallway is the game room, up front is the dining room where the Fernández De la Rosa family dines every night. You, of course, can see the courtyard and guesthouse from here. Do you have any questions?” says Altagracia."

**中文理解**：Alta 的 tour 是 rehearsed speech——"You, of course, can see..." 显示 hacienda 的 class hierarchy：family 在 dining room，staff 在 peripheral spaces。

**句子结构**：一长句 tour + 问句。"all the way down the hallway is..." 倒装强调空间布局。

**关键词**：
- `Fernández De la Rosa family`：家族名——hacienda 的拥有者
- `You, of course, can see`：当然你能看到——staff 的 limited perspective

**为什么这样写**：Alta 的 tour 从不问 Yolanda 想看什么，只展示 family 的空间，显示 Yolanda 的 invisibility。

---

> **原句 3:** "“Cantera used to be twenty times its size in land. The Fernández De la Rosa family even had their own cemetery. They’ve sold most of it now,” she says as if disappointed."

**中文理解**：hacienda 的衰落——从二十倍土地到 "casco"，家族 cemetery 也卖了。Alta "as if disappointed" 显示她将 hacienda 的命运视为 personal loss，暗示她与家族的 deep identification。

**句子结构**：引语 + "she says as if disappointed"（叙述者评注）。

**关键词**：
- `twenty times its size`：二十倍—— past grandeur
- `their own cemetery`：家族墓地——hacienda 作为 mini-empire
- `as if disappointed`：仿佛失望——Alta 的 emotional investment

**为什么这样写**：hacienda 的衰落与 Yolanda 的"新开始"形成对照——她进入的是一个正在 shrink 的世界，而非 expanding 的机会。

---

> **原句 4:** "“What’s left now is what they call ‘casco de hacienda,’ the living section of the hacienda. The rest of the fields were used to grow crops and tend to horses.”"

**中文理解**： "casco" 是 hacienda 的 living section——Yolanda 的 world 被限定在 "living section"，而 fields/crops/horses 都已失去。她的 " Señora de Cantera" 梦想缺乏 material basis。

**句子结构**：定义句（What’s left now is...）+ 补充（The rest...）。

**关键词**：
- `casco de hacienda`：庄园生活区——Yolanda 的 bounded world
- `grow crops and tend to horses`：农业功能——已失去

**为什么这样写**： "casco" 的限定显示 Yolanda 的 aspirational fantasy（ owning hacienda）与 reality（ merely living section）的 gap。

---

> **原句 5:** "“Ah.” She flattens her lips. “No, no one hunts. Those are Javier’s. He is Don Julián’s son-in-law. One day Javier appeared. He was young and somehow convinced my Odettecita to marry him.”"

**中文理解**：Javier "appeared" and "convinced" Odette to marry——Alta 的叙述显示 Javier 的 opportunism 与 Odette 的 vulnerability。Taxidermy birds 作为 Javier 的 hobby 暗示 his controlling nature。

**句子结构**：引语内：反应（Ah）→ 否认（No, no one hunts）→ 归属（Those are Javier’s）→ 叙事（One day Javier appeared...）。

**关键词**：
- `appeared`：出现——Javier 的 sudden arrival
- `convinced my Odettecita to marry him`：说服 Odette 嫁给他——Alta 的 disapproval

**为什么这样写**：Javier 的 "appearance" 与 "convincing" 预示 hacienda 的 male figures（Don Julián, Javier, Valentín）各自以不同方式 control women——Yolanda 将进入这个 sexual economy。

---

> **原句 6:** "“She’s like a daughter to me. No one would ever be good enough for Odette in my eyes.”"

**中文理解**：Alta 将 Odette 视为 "daughter"——显示其 maternal attachment 与 possessiveness。"No one would ever be good enough" 预示 Alta 对 Javier 的 hostility，也暗示 Alta 的 own maternal failure（Mamá）的 mirror。

**句子结构**：两句话：宣称（She’s like a daughter to me）→ 评判（No one would ever be good enough）。

**关键词**：
- `like a daughter to me`：像女儿一样——Alta 的 maternal claim
- `No one would ever be good enough`：没人配得上—— possessive love

**为什么这样写**：Alta 的 "daughter" claim 与 Mamá 的 abandonment 形成对照——两种 maternal failure： possessiveness vs absence。

---

> **原句 7:** "“Gracias.” To my surprise, her smile is genuine. “Valentincito gave it to me on my birthday. He always remembers.”"

**中文理解**：Valentín 给 Alta 手表——"Valentincito" 的 diminutive 显示 Alta 对 Valentín 的 affection，与对 Javier/Odette 的 hostility 对比。"He always remembers" 暗示 Valentín 的 thoughtfulness。

**句子结构**：引语 + 叙述者评注（To my surprise, her smile is genuine）+ 引语。

**关键词**：
- `Valentincito`：小 Valentín——diminutive，显示 affection
- `He always remembers`：他总是记得——Valentín 的 reliability

**为什么这样写**：Valentín 作为 "good" male figure 的建立，为 Yolanda 的 fantasy 提供 target——"the right woman" 的 candidate。

---

> **原句 8:** "“Isn’t that why I hired you? Your mom said you could clean. Now, if you can’t, you’re of no use to me. You might as well tell me now.”"

**中文理解**：Alta 以 "use" 评估 Yolanda—— labor as value，Yolanda 的 worth 被 tied to cleaning skill。"Your mom said you could clean" 显示 Mamá 的 misrepresentation。

**句子结构**：反问（Isn’t that why I hired you?）→ 陈述（Your mom said...）→ 条件（Now, if you can’t...）→ 结论（You might as well tell me now）。

**关键词**：
- `of no use to me`：对我无用——labor value
- `Your mom said you could clean`：你妈说你会打扫——Mamá 的 lie

**为什么这样写**：Alta 的 "use" 语言显示 hacienda 的 economy：people are valued by utility, not humanity.

---

> **原句 9:** "“Please pay attention to how things are done here. I hate to repeat myself.”"

**中文理解**：Alta 的 "hate to repeat myself" 显示其 impatience 与 control——Yolanda 必须 absorb rules quickly 或 face consequences。

**句子结构**：祈使句 + 声明（I hate to repeat myself）。

**关键词**：
- `pay attention`：注意——Alta 的 demand
- `hate to repeat myself`：讨厌重复——authority

**为什么这样写**：Alta 的 rules 是 unspoken until violated——Yolanda 的 "I don’t know" 是 insufficient excuse。

---

> **原句 10:** "“How much will you pay me? And where’s my bank account? How do I access it?”"

**中文理解**：Yolanda 询问工资与 bank account——显示其 pragmatic awareness despite fantasy. "How do I access it?" 显示其 financial naivety.

**句子结构**：三问连发，节奏急促，显示 Yolanda 的 anxiety.

**关键词**：
- `bank account`：银行账户——Mamá 的 control mechanism
- `How do I access it?`：如何取用——Yolanda 的 financial dependence

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda 的 questions 显示 she is not entirely fantasy-driven; she has practical concerns, but Alta’s response reveals the trap.

---

> **原句 11:** "“How much we’ll be paying is none of your business. You’re here to work, not to get paid.”"

**中文理解**：Alta’s response—wage is "none of your business"— reveals the labor exploitation: Yolanda works for "cleaning sins," not money. "not to get paid" directly contradicts "work."

**句子结构**：两句话：第一句否定（none of your business）→ 第二句重新定义（here to work, not to get paid）。

**关键词**：
- `none of your business`：不关你的事——financial control
- `not to get paid`：不是为了赚钱——labor as punishment/penance

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s logic mirrors Mamá’s: Yolanda’s labor is penance for "evil," not employment. This is the hacienda’s economy of sin.

---

> **原句 12:** "“You have to clean your sins. Your mamá will save your money until you’ve learned your lesson.”"

**中文理解**：Alta frames Yolanda’s labor as "cleaning sins"—spiritual punishment disguised as employment. Mamá "saving money" echoes Mamá’s control over Yolanda’s wages.

**句子结构**：两句话：命令（You have to clean your sins）→ 解释（Your mamá will save your money...）。

**关键词**：
- `clean your sins`：清洗罪孽——labor as penance
- `until you’ve learned your lesson`：直到你学会教训——indefinite punishment

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s "clean your sins" connects to Mamá’s "something evil lives inside you"—both women frame Yolanda’s body/desire as sin requiring labor-penance.

---

> **原句 13:** "“I saw you looking at that boy, Yolanda.”"

**中文理解**：Alta’s warning—Yolanda’s gaze at Valentín is policed. "That boy" diminishes Valentín, but also reveals Alta’s surveillance.

**句子结构**：简单陈述句，"I saw" 强调 Alta’s watchfulness.

**关键词**：
- `saw you looking`：看到你在看——surveillance
- `that boy`：那个男孩——diminutive, disapproving

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s gaze mirrors Mamá’s—women policing women’s desire, especially toward men.

---

> **原句 14:** "“Don Julián doesn’t like the staff mingling with the family,” she continues. “If such a thing were to happen, I’d have to let you go. You understand that, don’t you?”"

**中文理解**：Alta threatens Yolanda with job loss if she "mingles" with family—class boundary enforcement. "You understand that, don’t you?" is rhetorical coercion.

**句子结构**：引语 + "she continues" + 引语（条件句 + 问句）。

**关键词**：
- `mingling with the family`：与家人混在一起——class transgression
- `I’d have to let you go`：我得解雇你——economic threat

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s threat establishes the hacienda’s sexual economy: staff cannot access family, but Yolanda’s fantasy depends on transgressing this boundary.

---

> **原句 15:** "“Not that he’d ever look at you that way. My poor Valentincito is crooked.”"

**中文理解**：Alta reveals Valentín is "crooked" (gay)—"poor Valentincito" shows maternal pity. This is Yolanda’s first obstacle: Valentín "prefers men."

**句子结构**：两句话：否定（Not that he’d ever look at you that way）+ 解释（My poor Valentincito is crooked）。

**关键词**：
- `crooked`：歪的，不正的——homophobic slur (está torcido)
- `poor Valentincito`：可怜的 Valentín——Alta’s pity

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s "crooked" frames homosexuality as curse/illness, revealing hacienda’s conservative sexual politics—and Yolanda’s opportunity: "the right woman" can "cure" him.

---

> **原句 16:** "“It’s a curse that will wear off soon. When the right woman approaches him. Not a maid, of course.”"

**中文理解**：Alta’s "curse that will wear off" frames homosexuality as temporary illness curable by "the right woman"—and "Not a maid" excludes Yolanda. But Yolanda interprets this as challenge: "It could happen."

**句子结构**：三句：诊断（It’s a curse...）→ 条件（When the right woman approaches him）→ 排除（Not a maid, of course）。

**关键词**：
- `curse that will wear off`：会消退的诅咒——homophobic framing
- `the right woman`：合适的女人——Yolanda’s aspirational target
- `Not a maid, of course`：当然不是女仆——class exclusion

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s exclusion ("Not a maid") becomes Yolanda’s motivation—she will "play her cards right" to become "the right woman," repeating Rubí’s strategy.

---

> **原句 17:** "“¡Suata!” she yells at me. Once she is close, she snatches the broom from my hands. I stare at her, not understanding what I did wrong. “What do you think you’re doing? These are Persian carpets from Iran.”"

**中文理解**：Alta’s outburst—"¡Suata!" (shut up/fool)—over carpet mistake shows her rage and Yolanda’s ignorance of hacienda’s material value. "Persian carpets from Iran" emphasizes luxury beyond Yolanda’s comprehension.

**句子结构**：引语 + 引语。"¡Suata!" 是 colloquial insult; "What do you think you’re doing?" 是 rhetorical accusation.

**关键词**：
- `¡Suata!`：（西班牙语俚语）闭嘴/蠢货
- `Persian carpets from Iran`：伊朗波斯地毯——hacienda 的 luxury

**为什么这样写**：Alta’s rage over carpet (vs Yolanda’s survival concerns) shows class disconnect—staff cannot comprehend family’s material world.

---

> **原句 18:** "“Perra,” says Itzco once she’s out. “Alta is such a bitch.”"

**中文理解**：Itzco’s "Perra" (bitch) echoes Alta’s "¡Suata!"—both use gendered insults. Itzco’s solidarity with Yolanda against Alta establishes potential alliance.

**句子结构**：引语 + 引语。"once she’s out" 时间从句显示 Itzco’s caution.

**关键词**：
- `Perra`：母狗，婊子——gendered insult
- `such a bitch`：十足的婊子——Itzco’s contempt for Alta

**为什么这样写**：Itzco’s insult mirrors Alta’s, showing hacienda’s culture of verbal violence; also establishes Itzco as Yolanda’s potential ally/adversary.

---

> **原句 19:** "“Beware of that woman, Yolanda.”"

**中文理解**：Itzco warns Yolanda about Alta and Javier—Javier "always tries to sleep with the newbies" as part of open relationship with Odette. This reveals hacienda’s sexual economy and Yolanda’s precarity.

**句子结构**：两句话：警告（Beware of that woman）+ 警告（And beware of Javier）。"Of Javier? Why?" 显示 Yolanda’s curiosity.

**关键词**：
- `Beware`：小心，提防

**为什么这样写**：Itzco’s warnings establish hacienda’s dangers: Alta (control) and Javier (sexual predation). Yolanda’s "I’m not sure what to make of his words" shows her naivety.

---

> **原句 20:** "I’ll do my best not to look at him while Alta is around. She doesn’t know that her words have inspired me. The right woman. It could happen. I only have to play my cards right."

**中文理解**：Yolanda 将 Alta’s exclusion ("Not a maid") 转化为 motivation—"play my cards right" echoes Rubí’s strategy. "The right woman. It could happen." 显示其 self-delusion.

**句子结构**：四句：决心（I’ll do my best...）→ 揭示（She doesn’t know...）→ 重复（The right woman. It could happen.）→ 策略（I only have to play my cards right.）。

**关键词**：
- `play my cards right`：打好牌，策略正确——Yolanda’s Rubí-inspired strategy
- `The right woman. It could happen.`：合适的女人。可能的。——self-delusion

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda 将 structural exclusion (class, sexuality) 重新解释为 personal challenge—her coping mechanism is to convert oppression into opportunity, repeating Rubí’s "no-guilt" logic.

## 一句话总结

本章完成 Yolanda 在 hacienda 的 first month：Alta 以 "clean your sins" 框架 her labor，Yolanda 发现 Valentín 并将其 "crooked" 诅咒解读为 opportunity，同时 Javier 的 predatory behavior 与 Odette 的 open relationship 揭示 hacienda 的 sexual economy；Yolanda 的 "play my cards right" 显示其 Rubí-inspired strategy 的危险性。

## 读者视角提示

- Alta 的 "clean your sins" 与 Mamá 的 "something evil lives inside you" 形成 maternal demonization 的 mirror——两种 women policing Yolanda’s body
- Valentín "crooked" (está torcido) 是 homophobic framing, Yolanda 将其 reinterpret 为 "curable"——显示 her internalized homophobia 与 opportunism
- Javier 的 "deal" with Odette (open relationship) 与 "sleep with newbies" 显示 hacienda 的 sexual economy—Yolanda 将进入 this system
- "Persian carpets from Iran" vs Yolanda’s "mercado rugs" 显示 class gap—her "Señora" fantasy lacks material basis
- Itzco as potential ally/adversary: his warnings about Alta & Javier, but his "Perra" insult mirrors Alta’s gendered violence
