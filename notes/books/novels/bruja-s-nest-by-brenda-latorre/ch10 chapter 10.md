# ch10 Chapter 10

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：Hacienda Cantera（shed/bathroom/Valentín room）；Yolanda 给 birth to egg；pregnancy reveal to family
- **人物**：Yolanda；Santa Muerte（vision）；Valentín；Don Julián；Odette；Javier；Alta；Itzco
- **核心事件**：Yolanda 在 shed bathroom 给 birth to a white egg；用 gauze 伪装 pregnancy；向 family 宣布 "I’m pregnant, Valentín is the father"；Don Julián 接受 "Yolanda and your son will live on hacienda grounds"；Valentín 宣布 "getting rid of the arm" + will
- **悬念钩子**：Egg 何时 hatches？Family 是否会发现 truth？Valentín 是否 survive surgery？

---

> **原句 1:** "I extend the sheets, which I change weekly. No matter that no one ever sleeps in here."

**中文理解**：Yolanda 在 shed 清洁——"No matter that no one ever sleeps in here" 显示 her diligence masking anxiety. Pregnancy symptoms begin。

**句子结构**：两句话：I extend the sheets... + No matter that...

**关键词**：
- `No matter that no one ever sleeps in here`：尽管没人睡——diligence
- `extend the sheets`：铺床单——labor

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s labor continues despite pregnancy—she cannot stop working, showing her trapped status。

---

> **原句 2:** "A contraction. I know it is."

**中文理解**：Yolanda 认识 contraction—"A contraction. I know it is." Simple, direct. Shows her body knowledge despite no medical training。

**句子结构**：两句话。

**关键词**：
- `A contraction`：宫缩——Yolanda’s recognition
- `I know it is`：我知道是——body knowledge

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s "I know it is" shows her bodily intuition—she doesn’t need doctor to tell her what’s happening。

---

> **原句 3:** "Cold water won’t be good for the baby. It could die."

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s concern—"Cold water won’t be good for the baby. It could die." Shows her maternal instinct emerging, despite dissociation。

**句子结构**：两句话。

**关键词**：
- `Cold water won’t be good for the baby`：冷水对 baby 不好——maternal concern
- `It could die`：它可能死——fear

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s concern for "the baby" contrasts with her earlier dissociation—pregnancy is becoming real to her。

---

> **原句 4:** "The screeching yell that reaches my ears from afar, but that I somehow know has come from my own throat, confirms it."

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s screeching yell—"confirms it." Pain is real, labor is happening. "From afar, but that I somehow know has come from my own throat" shows dissociation。

**句子结构**：主从复合句。

**关键词**：
- `screeching yell`：尖叫——pain vocalization
- `from afar, but that I somehow know has come from my own throat`：从 afar 传来但我知道是自己的喉咙——dissociation

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s dissociation (hearing her own scream as if from afar) shows trauma response—she is not fully present in her body。

---

> **原句 5:** "“Muerte?” I ask."

**中文理解**：Yolanda sees Santa Muerte—"Muerte?" she asks. Death saint appears during labor. Shows supernatural framing of birth。

**句子结构**：简单问句。

**关键词**：
- `Muerte?`：死神？——supernatural appearance

**为什么这样写**：Santa Muerte’s appearance during labor connects bruja’s ritual to birth—death and life intertwined。

---

> **原句 6:** "And then I split in half."

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s "And then I split in half."—body horror. "The pain is unbearable." Shows labor’s extremity。

**句子结构**：简单陈述句。

**关键词**：
- `split in half`：裂成两半——body horror

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s "split in half" echoes cesarea’s cut—body is being opened, not just for birth but for bruja’s ritual completion。

---

> **原句 7:** "I’ve given birth to a white oval that I can’t call anything other than an egg."

**中文理解**：Yolanda gives birth to egg—"white oval that I can’t call anything other than an egg." Horror reveal: baby is egg. "My baby is an egg." Shows her shock。

**句子结构**：一句话：I’ve given birth to a white oval that I can’t call anything other than an egg。

**关键词**：
- `white oval... egg`：白色卵形... 蛋——horror reveal
- `My baby is an egg`：我的 baby 是蛋——shock

**为什么这样写**：Egg birth is the novel’s central horror—bruja’s ritual worked but produced something inhuman. Yolanda must hide it。

---

> **原句 8:** "When I caress the heavy oval, it moves."

**中文理解**：Egg moves—"When I caress the heavy oval, it moves." Shows it’s alive. "A slight shake in my arms to let me know it’s there." maternal connection despite horror。

**句子结构**：一句话。

**关键词**：
- `caress the heavy oval, it moves`：抚摸沉重的卵形，它移动——alive
- `slight shake... to let me know it’s there`：轻微晃动——maternal connection

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s "caress" shows maternal instinct—she touches egg with tenderness despite its inhumanity。

---

> **原句 9:** "That’s when I realize I can’t tell them about this."

**中文理解**：Yolanda realizes she must hide egg—"can’t tell them about this." Shows her awareness of danger. "The family must never know I’ve birthed something other than a child."

**句子结构**：简单陈述句。

**关键词**：
- `can’t tell them about this`：不能告诉他们——secrecy
- `birthed something other than a child`：生下了不是孩子的东西——horror

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s secrecy is survival—family would reject egg/baby, destroying her scheme。

---

> **原句 10:** "Only until the egg hatches. When it does, I can pretend I’ve given birth."

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s plan—hide egg until hatching, then "pretend I’ve given birth." Shows her deception strategy. "Otherwise, they’ll never accept my baby as part of the Fernández De la Rosa family."

**句子结构**：两句话。

**关键词**：
- `Only until the egg hatches`：直到蛋孵化——timeline
- `pretend I’ve given birth`：假装生了——deception

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s plan is elaborate deception—she will fake birth to legitimize egg-child. Shows her resourcefulness。

## 一句话总结

本章 Yolanda 在 shed bathroom 给 birth to a white egg；用 gauze 伪装 pregnancy；向 family 宣布 "I’m pregnant, Valentín is the father"；Don Julián 接受 "Yolanda and your son will live on hacienda grounds"；Valentín 宣布 "getting rid of the arm" + will—Yolanda’s scheme succeeds, but egg secret remains。

## 读者视角提示

- Egg birth is central horror—Yolanda must hide it to maintain scheme
- Yolanda’s announcement is performance—Valentín "acts surprised," family suspicious
- Don Julián’s acceptance shows pragmatism—he uses pregnancy for family benefit
- Odette’s "lagartona" (lizard/social climber) shows town’s view of Yolanda
- Valentín’s will ("Yolanda and your son will live on hacienda grounds") is Yolanda’s triumph
