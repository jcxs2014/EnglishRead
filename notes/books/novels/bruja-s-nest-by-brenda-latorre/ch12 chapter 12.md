# ch12 Chapter 12

## 本章导航

- **时间地点**：Shed (guesthouse) → hacienda → Valentín’s room
- **人物**：Yolanda；Javier；Valentín；Itzco；Alta
- **核心事件**：Yolanda moved to shed; Valentín’s stump infected; Javier proposes marriage (Valentín asks Yolanda); Yolanda accepts "indifferently"; Javier mocks "How romantic"
- **悬念钩子**：Valentín’s infection worsening; marriage proposal—genuine or strategic? Javier’s motives?

---

> **原句 1:** "The change from the bunkhouse to the shed is worse than I expected. It’s lucky I don’t own too many clothes or things, for they’d be tarnished by the conditions of the shed."

**中文理解**：Yolanda 搬到 shed——"worse than I expected." Shows downgrade. "Lucky I don’t own too many clothes" shows poverty。

**句子结构**：两句话。

**关键词**：
- `worse than I expected`：比预期更糟——downgrade
- `tarnished by the conditions`：被条件 tarnish——poverty

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s "lucky" is ironic—her poverty is now her advantage. Shows her adaptation to hardship。

---

> **原句 2:** "It reminds me of the Silbón. A man who murdered his father."

**中文理解**：Silbón legend—man who murdered father, condemned to carry rattling bones. Wind in shed sounds like Silbón’s whistle. Shows Yolanda’s supernatural awareness。

**句子结构**：两句话。

**关键词**：
- `Silbón`：Silbón (venezuelan legend)—whistling ghost
- `murdered his father`：谋杀父亲——patricide

**为什么这样写**：Silbón legend connects to Yolanda’s situation—she has "murdered" her own innocence (Brother, Javier, bruja). Whistling wind = bad omen。

---

> **原句 3:** "It’s cold in here and smells of death, courtesy of Javier."

**中文理解**：Shed smells of death—Javier’s taxidermy. "Courtesy of Javier" is sarcastic. Shows Yolanda’s growing awareness of Javier’s darkness。

**句子结构**：简单陈述句。

**关键词**：
- `smells of death, courtesy of Javier`：死味， courtesy of Javier——sarcasm

**为什么这样写**：Javier’s "courtesy" frames his taxidermy as gift—Yolanda is surrounded by death even in her new home。

---

> **原句 4:** "I do have a bathroom to myself, but the water seldom arrives hot in this part of the hacienda."

**中文理解**：Yolanda has bathroom but cold water—small privilege, big discomfort. "Seldom arrives hot" shows hacienda’s neglect of staff quarters。

**句子结构**：主从复合句。

**关键词**：
- `bathroom to myself`：自己的浴室——small privilege
- `water seldom arrives hot`：热水很少到——neglect

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s "small privilege" (bathroom) is undercut by cold water—hacienda provides minimum for staff。

---

> **原句 5:** "Itzco built a box to serve as a crib when the baby comes."

**中文理解**：Itzco built crib box—shows his care for Yolanda despite betrayal. "It’s not the crib I thought Don Julián would buy for his grandson, but it is not nothing." Shows Yolanda’s gratitude and lowered expectations。

**句子结构**：简单陈述句。

**关键词**：
- `Itzco built a box to serve as a crib`：Itzco 做了箱子当摇篮——care
- `not the crib I thought Don Julián would buy`：不是 Don Julián 会买的摇篮——lowered expectations

**为什么这样写**：Itzco’s crib box shows his continued support—Yolanda’s "not nothing" shows her lowered expectations。

---

> **原句 6:** "I drift toward the lower level, where Javier displays his jars on the floating shelves."

**中文理解**：Yolanda drifts to Javier’s jars—human fetuses, cat eyes. Shows her curiosity and unease. "I think of visiting Valentín, but he may be sleeping."

**句子结构**：一句话。

**关键词**：
- `jars on the floating shelves`：浮动搁板上的罐子——Javier’s specimens
- `human fetuses float within different jars`：人类胎儿浮在不同罐子里——horror

**为什么这样写**：Javier’s jars show his obsession with death/preservation—Yolanda is surrounded by his "art."

---

> **原句 7:** "Life in Cantera has become strange."

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s observation—"Life in Cantera has become strange." Shows her disorientation. "I caress the tools Javier left on the table. Inviting me to experiment on one of the specimens he keeps in the freezer."

**句子结构**：简单陈述句。

**关键词**：
- `Life in Cantera has become strange`：Cantera 的生活变得奇怪——disorientation

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s "strange" shows her awareness of supernatural/horror elements—egg, bruja, Javier’s specimens。

---

> **原句 8:** "It’s been two nights since Don Julián started canceling family dinners."

**中文理解**：Don Julián cancels family dinners—shows family’s distress over Valentín. Yolanda: "I’m unsure whether it has to do with my presence."

**句子结构**：简单陈述句。

**关键词**：
- `canceling family dinners`：取消家庭晚餐——family distress

**为什么这样写**：Don Julián’s cancellation shows family’s grief—Valentín’s injury disrupts normal life。

---

> **原句 9:** "When I open the door to Valentín’s room, I see a terrible sight. Valentín’s stump is infected."

**中文理解**：Valentín’s stump infected—shows surgical failure. "The muscle, forcibly stitched together with black thread, is now purple at the center." Shows horror.

**句子结构**：两句话。

**关键词**：
- `Valentín’s stump is infected`：Valentín 的残肢感染了——surgical failure
- `purple at the center... reminiscent of a crème brûlée`：中心紫色...像 crème brûlée——horror

**为什么这样写**：Valentín’s infected stump shows Javier’s incompetence—surgery without proper medical knowledge leads to infection。

---

> **原句 10:** "“What in the devil are you talking about? ¿Ahora qué?”"

**中文理解**：Valentín’s "What in the devil are you talking about? ¿Ahora qué?" (What now?)—shows his anger at Javier’s marriage proposal. Javier: "You two should get married if you don’t want me to punch that woman in the face."

**句子结构**：引语。

**关键词**：
- `What in the devil are you talking about?`：你他妈在说什么？——anger
- `¿Ahora qué?`：现在又怎样？——frustration

**为什么这样写**：Valentín’s anger at Javier’s proposal shows his protectiveness of Yolanda—despite scheme, he cares。

---

> **原句 11:** "“What are you doing, Javier?” Valentín asks. He is enraged."

**中文理解**：Valentín’s "What are you doing, Javier?"—shows his anger at Javier’s proximity to Yolanda. "He is enraged. Even if our union is for pretend, Javier is disrespecting him."

**句子结构**：引语 + 叙述者评注。

**关键词**：
- `He is enraged`：他 enraged——anger
- `disrespecting him`：不尊重他——Valentín’s perception

**为什么这样写**：Valentín’s anger at Javier shows his possessiveness—even in fake relationship, he wants respect。

---

> **原句 12:** "“Do you want to marry me?” asks Valentín."

**中文理解**：Valentín’s proposal—"Do you want to marry me?" Shows his desperation to protect Yolanda/baby. Yolanda: "Yes. My tone is almost indifferent."

**句子结构**：问句。

**关键词**：
- `Do you want to marry me?`：你想嫁给我吗？——proposal

**为什么这样写**：Valentín’s proposal is strategic (protect Yolanda/baby) but also genuine—he cares about her. Yolanda’s "indifferent" tone shows her emotional distance。

---

> **原句 13:** "Some people are lucky."

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s "Some people are lucky."—contrast with her own life. "That’s not my life. Luck hasn’t wanted to touch me for as long as I can remember."

**句子结构**：简单陈述句。

**关键词**：
- `Some people are lucky`：有些人幸运——contrast
- `Luck hasn’t wanted to touch me`：运气不想触碰我——Yolanda’s fatalism

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s "Some people are lucky" shows her self-pity—she compares herself to romantic proposal recipients, feels deprived。

---

> **原句 14:** "Yes,” I say. My tone is almost indifferent."

**中文理解**：Yolanda’s "Yes" with "almost indifferent" tone—shows her emotional distance from marriage. "For I already see what awaits me at the end of the arrangement."

**句子结构**：引语 + 叙述者评注。

**关键词**：
- `almost indifferent`：几乎冷漠——emotional distance

**为什么这样写**：Yolanda’s "indifferent" tone shows marriage is transaction, not romance. She sees "what awaits me at the end of the arrangement."

---

> **原句 15:** "Javier smiles at us. “How romantic.”"

**中文理解**：Javier’s "How romantic."—sarcastic. "If I didn’t know him, I’d say he is jealous." Shows Javier’s feelings about Yolanda/Valentín。

**句子结构**：引语 + 叙述者评注。

**关键词**：
- `How romantic`：多浪漫——sarcasm
- `If I didn’t know him, I’d say he is jealous`：如果我不了解他，我会说他嫉妒——Javier’s feelings

**为什么这样写**：Javier’s sarcasm shows his awareness of fake relationship—he sees through scheme。

---

> **原句 16:** "Valentín presses his hand in mine."

**中文理解**：Valentín presses hand in Yolanda’s—physical connection. Shows Yolanda’s yearning for genuine connection。

**句子结构**：简单陈述句。

**关键词**：
- `Valentín presses his hand in mine`：Valentín 将手按在我手中——physical connection

**为什么这样写**：Valentín’s hand-pressing shows his genuine care—Yolanda’s "yearning" shows she wants real connection, not just scheme。

---

## 一句话总结

本章 Yolanda moved to shed (guesthouse); Valentín’s stump infected; Javier proposes marriage (Valentín asks Yolanda); Yolanda accepts "indifferently"; Javier mocks "How romantic"—marriage is strategic protection, not romance. Yolanda’s "Some people are lucky" shows her fatalism。

## 读者视角提示

- Yolanda’s "Some people are lucky" shows her fatalism—she compares herself to romantic proposal recipients
- Valentín’s proposal is strategic (protect Yolanda/baby) but also genuine—he cares
- Javier’s "How romantic" sarcasm shows he sees through scheme
- "Silbón" legend connects to Yolanda’s "murdered" innocence
- Yolanda’s "indifferent" tone shows marriage is transaction, not romance
