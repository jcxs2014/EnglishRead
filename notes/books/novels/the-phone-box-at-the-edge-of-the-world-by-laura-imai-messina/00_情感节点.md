# The Phone Box at the Edge of the World（情感节点）

> 十个节点，全书情感弧线的骨架；每节点附 2–3 句关键引语（逐字取自原文，已核验）。

## 节点一：台风前夜的包扎（Prologue）

黎明前，Yui 独自用防水布包裹整座花园——五十米塑料布、电工胶带、大号铁钉。风暴将至，她把守护做成一场战场急救，而叙述者冷冷指出她拒绝看见的真相。

> "It looked fragile, as if it were made of candy canes and crumbling meringue."
> "Yui was untouched by the most basic truth: that fragility does not reside in things so much as in flesh."
> "Yui would risk her hollow existence to ensure nothing bad happened to that thing, or to the place that had made it real."

## 节点二：一通把她击中的来电（Chapter 1）

电台夜，青森的厨娘、爱知的女孩、东京学法语的主妇依次讲述各自"活下来的小动作"，最后一位岩手听众说出风之电话——以打断 caller 为绝技的 Yui 整章失语。

> "The phone isn’t connected to anything, but your voice is carried away with the wind."
> "we’re trying to get our shadows back."
> "If not yet happiness, at the very least relief, was about to become a thing."

## 节点三：等待者与知情者（Chapter 3）

海啸后一个月：体育馆的一百二十人、信息中心的两份描述、从山坡上看海吞城的"剧院包厢"。Yui 的身份在等待与知情之间被强行推进。

> "Two names and two descriptions: the pigtails, the mid-length grey hair, the colour of a skirt, the mole on a stomach."
> "That’s what happened to her daughter and her mother, who, when they got to the shelter, found only death awaiting them."
> "Eventually, the bodies were found and Yui stopped looking at the sea."

## 节点四：后视镜里的同类（Chapter 5）

上鲸山的路上，Yui 在翼镜里认出一个灰发男人面容上的"暗角"——她认得那种交出一切情绪的黑暗。风推着她的背，一句 sumimasen 让两座孤岛互相喊出声音。

> "But then the figure of a man with a youthful face and grey hair passed through the reflection in the wing mirror, and Yui knew that he was like her. A survivor."
> "He smiled. In one glance he knew that she was like him, that they were there for the same reason."

## 节点五：第一声笑（Chapter 7）

双梦对位之夜：她梦见重新怀上女儿，他梦见教失语的 Hana 说规矩。当 Yui 脱口说出"没人知道那个！"并笑出声时——她甚至被自己喉咙里的声音吓了一跳。

> "No, it was at the sound coming from her own throat that first threw, then dumbfounded, her: laughter. She was laughing."
> "We need to possess joy in abundance before we can bestow it upon somebody else."

## 节点六：花园首日与布满裂纹的天空（Chapter 11）

Yui 留在花园里旁观：Suzuki-san 谈"死亡是私事"，她悟出电话的广播本质——死者或许在那边手拉手、交新朋友、继续变老。一个穿校服的少年来打电话，她抬头看见了裂纹里的天空。

> "Then it struck Yui that the function of the telephone, rather than to channel and guide voices into a single ear, was to broadcast them out onto this wind."
> "There must be an expiry date on the soul like there is on the body."
> "From that angle, the sky appeared full of cracks."

## 节点七：相拥而亡的照片与迟来三年的呕吐（Chapter 31）

两周年烧烤日的欢乐给了 Yui 开口的力气：母亲手册上划掉的斜线、 urn 里的相拥姿态、那张"像闭合贝壳"的照片。归途上她第一次不再压制恶心——三年的海水一次吐空，然后两人笑到无法呼吸。

> "They had found them locked together, like a closed clamshell."
> "She had been wrong. It isn’t just the best things that come to an end, but also the worst."
> "They laughed so much they couldn’t breathe."

## 节点八：嘴唇在动（Chapter 39 / Chapter 41）

Hana 第一次甩开父亲的手，独自走进电话亭——亭外无人知道她在不在说话，风声替她拉上窗帘。数月后的那一天，通话归来的她抓住 Yui 的衣角，把她拽进拥抱，随即开口说起平常话。

> "He was stunned, unable to grasp a clearer emotion. Were her lips just beating the air or were they saying something?"
> "she began to speak again, and she said only normal things."

## 节点九：求婚与台风（Chapter 55–58）

洗碗池边，Takeshi 说出"为什么不搬来一起住"——没有吻，只有"明天再谈"。Yui 满心是被爱的确认，相信这份爱会保护她，于是连夜奔向台风里的 Bell Gardia。雷击、停电、挂钩松脱——她的身体像空纸箱交给了风。

> "‘You belong with us. We are a family: you, me, Hana, Tora too. All that’s left is to make it official.’"
> "she believed this love would protect her."
> "From then on she was at the mercy of the wind, her body tossed around like an empty cardboard box."

## 节点十：第一次开口与头衔复职（Chapter 71 / Epilogue）

婚期前的自我流放结束后，Yui 在雨中走进电话亭："She lifted the receiver. / For the first time, she spoke."——她说了什么，全书保密。尾声揭晓末页实录：她说"妈妈，我是 Yui"，而横滨站里，儿子的一声"Mama"让头衔物归原主。

> "For the first time, she spoke."
> "That day, without warning, Yui had been reinstated with the title."
> "‘I’m here, it’s Mummy.’"
