# 13. Epilogue

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Veris 在康复中度过秋冬，春天来临时 Eleonor 回到村庄，请求学习魔法。

**情节位置**：结局，创伤与希望。

**核心冲突**：Veris 在失去与重建之间寻找意义。

**叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角，平静的叙事中蕴含深度情感。

---

## 精读

### ①

> **原句 1:** What remained of autumn wore away in long convalescence, and Veris spent much of it in a bed that her aunt and their neighbor built in front of the fireplace on the ground floor of the little house. The wound in her hip healed into an ugly purple scar like a rotten fruit; the one in her shoulder went bad, and Dr. Ervun came and cut it open and washed it out and bandaged it up again.

**中文理解**：秋天剩下的时间在漫长的康复中消逝，Veris 大部分时间都躺在她的姑姑和邻居在客厅壁炉前为她搭的床上。她髋部的伤口愈合成了一个丑陋的紫色疤痕，像一个腐烂的水果；肩膀上的那个恶化了，Ervun 医生来了，把它切开，清洗了它，又重新包扎。

**句子结构**：第一句用 "What remained of autumn wore away in long convalescence" 的动作，"and Veris spent much of it in a bed that her aunt and their neighbor built in front of the fireplace on the ground floor of the little house" 是并列，"that her aunt and their neighbor built in front of the fireplace on the ground floor of the little house" 是定语从句修饰 bed。第二句用 "The wound in her hip healed into an ugly purple scar like a rotten fruit" 的明喻，"the one in her shoulder went bad" 是并列，"and Dr. Ervun came and cut it open and washed it out and bandaged it up again" 是并列的动作。

**关键词**：convalescence（康复）、healed（愈合）、ugly（丑陋的）、scar（疤痕）、rotten（腐烂的）

**表达方式**：身体创伤的具体化。"ugly purple scar like a rotten fruit" 将疤痕比作腐烂的水果——它不是光荣的标记，而是丑陋的遗迹。"went bad" 的双关既指伤口感染，也暗示了 Veris 的心理状态。

**为什么这样写**：这段文本建立了创伤的永久性——Veris 的身体和心灵都留下了疤痕。这种创伤不会消失，只会"愈合"。

### ②

> **原句 2:** Without warning, and without attribution, food appeared at irregular intervals on their back doorstep, and those of her neighbors—unmarked sacks of wheat and rice, dried field peas, once a wicker basket of ham and sausage. Twice there were metal canisters of raisins and hazelnuts, and her aunt made spice-cakes for the solstice with them, and it seemed very strange to Veris to taste something so sweet in mid-winter. Clearly they were from the castle; and just as clearly, nothing could be said about it.

**中文理解**：毫无预兆，毫无来源，食物不规则地出现在她们的后门台阶上，还有邻居们的——没有标记的小麦和大米袋子，干扁豆，有一次是一篮火腿和香肠。两次出现的是装有葡萄干和榛子的金属罐，她的姑姑用它们做了冬至香料蛋糕，Veris 在隆冬时节尝到如此甜的东西觉得很奇怪。显然它们来自城堡；同样明显的是，对此什么也不能说。

**句子结构**：第一句用 "Without warning, and without attribution, food appeared at irregular intervals on their back doorstep, and those of her neighbors" 的倒装结构，破折号后引出具体食物（"unmarked sacks of wheat and rice, dried field peas, once a wicker basket of ham and sausage"）。第二句用 "Twice there were metal canisters of raisins and hazelnuts" 的存在句，"and her aunt made spice-cakes for the solstice with them" 是并列，"and it seemed very strange to Veris to taste something so sweet in mid-winter" 是并列。最后一句用 "Clearly they were from the castle; and just as clearly, nothing could be said about it" 的并列。

**关键词**：attribution（来源）、irregular（不规则的）、canisters（金属罐）、solstice（冬至）

**表达方式**：沉默的善意。"nothing could be said about it" 暗示了暴君的沉默——他通过食物来表达感激，但不愿公开承认。这种沉默的善意展示了暴君的复杂性：他不是完全的邪恶，但他也无法公开表达善意。

**为什么这样写**：这段文本建立了暴君的沉默赎罪——他通过食物来表达感激，但不愿公开承认。这种沉默的善意比公开的宽恕更真实。

### ③

> **原句 3:** And in the spring, on the first fine day, she left Grandfather sleeping late and went outside to let the rabbits out of their hutches, because there was grass now poking through the snow and some of them would want to stay in their cozily-made home and others would want the air and the green.

**中文理解**：春天里，在一个晴朗的日子，她让祖父继续睡觉，自己出去把兔子从笼子里放出来，因为现在有草从雪地里钻出来了，有些兔子想待在它们舒适的家中，而另一些则想要空气和绿色。

**句子结构**：第一句用 "And in the spring, on the first fine day, she left Grandfather sleeping late and went outside to let the rabbits out of their hutches" 的动作，"because there was grass now poking through the snow" 是原因状语从句，"and some of them would want to stay in their cozily-made home and others would want the air and the green" 是并列。

**关键词**：hutches（兔笼）、poking（钻出）、cozily（舒适地）

**表达方式**：新生的意象。"grass now poking through the snow" 暗示了新生的开始——冬天结束了，生命重新开始。"some of them would want to stay in their cozily-made home and others would want the air and the green" 展示了选择的多样性——不是所有兔子都想要同样的东西。

**为什么这样写**：这段文本建立了春天的象征意义——它不仅是季节的变化，也是 Veris 心理状态的转变。

### ④

> **原句 4:** "Good morning, Veris," said Eleonor. "Good morning," Veris said. The girl wore what she probably thought of as a plain or unremarkable gown, thick brown velvet with a few tangles of golden leaves embroidered at wrist and throat, under a luxurious gray fur; her black hair was loose, and longer than the previous fall.

**中文理解**："早上好，Veris，" Eleonor 说。"早上好，" Veris 说。这个女孩穿着她可能认为朴素或不起眼的礼服，厚实的棕色天鹅绒，手腕和喉咙处绣着几缕金色的叶子，外面披着奢华的灰色毛皮；她的黑发松散的，比去年秋天更长。

**句子结构**：第一句用 "Good morning, Veris" 的问候。第二句用 "Good morning" 的回应。第三句用 "The girl wore what she probably thought of as a plain or unremarkable gown" 的定语从句，"thick brown velvet with a few tangles of golden leaves embroidered at wrist and throat" 是同位语，"under a luxurious gray fur" 是修饰，"her black hair was loose, and longer than the previous fall" 是并列。

**关键词**：plain（朴素的）、unremarkable（不起眼的）、velvet（天鹅绒）、tangles（缠绕）、embroidered（刺绣）、luxurious（奢华的）

**表达方式**：阶级差异的细节。"what she probably thought of as a plain or unremarkable gown" 暗示了 Eleonor 的自我认知——她试图穿得朴素，但她的"朴素"在 Veris 眼中仍然是奢华的。"thick brown velvet...luxurious gray fur" 展示了阶级差异——即使在试图融入时，她仍然是公主。

**为什么这样写**：这段文本建立了 Eleonor 的矛盾——她试图融入村庄生活，但她的衣着暴露了她的身份。

### ⑤

> **原句 5:** Veris frowned; the girl seemed genuinely confused, and that confused Veris too. Who else could send it down without consequence so many times? There had been at least a dozen deliveries. Couldn't be one of the local boys recruited as a guard.

**中文理解**：Veris 皱起了眉头；女孩似乎真的很困惑，这也让 Veris 很困惑。还有谁能这么多次送东西而不受惩罚？至少送过十几次了。不可能是被招募为守卫的当地男孩。

**句子结构**：第一句用 "Veris frowned" 的动作，"the girl seemed genuinely confused" 是并列，"and that confused Veris too" 是结果。第二句用 "Who else could send it down without consequence so many times?" 的反问。第三句用 "There had been at least a dozen deliveries" 的完成时。最后一句用 "Couldn't be one of the local boys recruited as a guard" 的推测。

**关键词**：genuinely（真正地）、consequence（后果）、deliveries（递送）

**表达方式**：谜团与沉默。"Who else could send it down without consequence so many times?" 的反问暗示了唯一的答案——暴君。但 "nothing could be said about it" 的沉默意味着这个秘密不能被公开讨论。

**为什么这样写**：这段文本建立了暴君的沉默存在——他不在场，但他的影响无处不在。

### ⑥

> **原句 6:** It would have been hard enough as a child of the Tyrant with only one true friend in the castle; Veris felt a great pity for her that she now had none. Her brother had surely been her shadow: ever-present, the most familiar thing in all that pile of gray stone, his face better known to her than her own. She would think about him at night and weep; and one day, Veris realized, she had wept her last, and made a decision.

**中文理解**：作为暴君的孩子，在城堡里只有一个真正的朋友已经够难了；Veris 为她现在一个也没有感到极大的遗憾。她的弟弟肯定是她的影子：无处不在，在那堆灰色的石头中他最熟悉，他的脸比她自己的还熟悉。她晚上会想他而哭泣；有一天，Veris 意识到，她最后一次哭泣，做了一个决定。

**句子结构**：第一句用 "It would have been hard enough as a child of the Tyrant with only one true friend in the castle" 的让步，"Veris felt a great pity for her that she now had none" 是主句。第二句用 "Her brother had surely been her shadow" 的比喻，"ever-present, the most familiar thing in all that pile of gray stone, his face better known to her than her own" 是同位语。第三句用 "She would think about him at night and weep" 的过去时，"and one day, Veris realized, she had wept her last, and made a decision" 是并列。

**关键词**：shadow（影子）、ever-present（无处不在）、pity（遗憾）、wept her last（最后一次哭泣）、decision（决定）

**表达方式**：失去与决心。"she had wept her last, and made a decision" 暗示了 Eleonor 的转变——她不再为弟弟哭泣，而是决定行动。这种转变暗示了 Eleonor 的成长——她从悲伤中走出来，走向了行动。

**为什么这样写**：这段文本建立了 Eleonor 的成长——她不再是那个需要被保护的孩子，而是一个有决心的人。

### ⑦

> **原句 7:** "I'm ready to go back to... to the woods. That place in the woods. I spent all winter learning how to fence and ride with the guards; and to tie knots, and to throw knives, and all sorts of stuff. But now I want to learn from you," she said. "Magic. I want to learn how to do what you can do."

**中文理解**："我准备回到……树林里。树林里的那个地方。我整个冬天都在学习如何与守卫们击剑和骑马；还有系绳结，扔刀，各种各样的东西。但现在我想向你学习，"她说。"魔法。我想学习如何做你能做的事。"

**句子结构**：第一句用 "I'm ready to go back to... to the woods" 的省略。第二句用 "That place in the woods" 的重复。第三句用 "I spent all winter learning how to fence and ride with the guards" 的完成时，"and to tie knots, and to throw knives, and all sorts of stuff" 是并列。最后一句用 "But now I want to learn from you" 的转折，"Magic" 的省略，"I want to learn how to do what you can do" 的强调。

**关键词**：fence（击剑）、ride（骑马）、tie knots（系绳结）、throw knives（扔刀）

**表达方式**：决心与请求。"I spent all winter learning" 展示了 Eleonor 的决心——她不是空谈，而是已经准备了整个冬天。"Magic" 的省略暗示了她对 Veris 能力的理解——不是巫术，而是某种更深层的东西。

**为什么这样写**：这段对话展示了 Eleonor 的成长——她不再是那个需要被保护的孩子，而是一个有决心、有能力的人。

### ⑧

> **原句 8:** "Well, I don't know that it can be taught." "But you don't know that it can't," the girl said, sensibly enough. "Please? At least I want to... make my own magical things. Like yours."

**中文理解**："嗯，我不知道它是否能被教。""但你不知道它不能被教，"女孩说，很有道理。"求求你？至少我想……做我自己的魔法物品。像你的一样。"

**句子结构**：对话的交替，用 "sensibly enough" 修饰。

**关键词**：taught（教）、sensibly（有道理地）

**表达方式**：逻辑与恳求。"But you don't know that it can't" 展示了 Eleonor 的逻辑——她用 Veris 自己的逻辑来反驳她。"Like yours" 暗示了她对 Veris 的敬佩——她不是想成为战士，而是想成为像 Veris 一样的人。

**为什么这样写**：这段对话展示了 Eleonor 的性格——她不仅有决心，还有逻辑。

### ⑨

> **原句 9:** "I don't have mine anymore," Veris said. "I didn't get them back from Aram." "All the more reason then," Eleonor said. "Don't you think? You can make new ones, maybe. We can make new ones together. And you can teach me. Will you?"

**中文理解**："我不再有我的了，" Veris 说。"我没有从 Aram 那里拿回它们。""那更有理由了，" Eleonor 说。"你不觉得吗？你可以做新的，也许。我们可以一起做新的。你可以教我。你愿意吗？"

**句子结构**：对话的交替，用 "All the more reason then" 的省略。

**关键词**：anymore（不再）、together（一起）

**表达方式**：失去与重建。"I didn't get them back from Aram" 暗示了 Veris 的损失——她不仅失去了护符，也失去了 Aram。"We can make new ones together" 暗示了 Eleonor 的提议——她不仅想学习，也想帮助 Veris 重建。

**为什么这样写**：这段对话展示了 Eleonor 的善意——她不是只想学习，也想帮助 Veris。

### ⑩

> **原句 10:** Veris pondered for a moment, then reached down and thumbed the latch of the garden gate. "I will think about it," she said. "Shut that tight behind you, please." "You have so many rabbits!" Eleonor came in and stooped to pet them, laughing as they leapt away from her, careless of her fur; Veris grabbed it before it slid off her shoulders. "Do they all have names?" "Not all. Just these two, because they are old, and they don't have kits anymore, and Grandfather won't let us eat them." Veris took the girl's elbow and raised her up, smiling for the first time in what felt like months. "Come in. I will tell you their names." "And then you'll teach me magic?" "No. Then we'll have a cup of tea. And then we will discuss what may and may not be learned."

**中文理解**：Veris 沉思了一会儿，然后伸手用拇指拨开了花园门的门闩。"我会考虑的，"她说。"请在身后把它关紧。""你有这么多兔子！" Eleonor 走进来，弯下腰抚摸它们，当它们从她身边跳开时哈哈大笑，不在意她的毛皮；Veris 在它从她肩膀上滑下来之前抓住了它。"它们都有名字吗？""不是全部。只是这两只，因为它们老了，不再有小兔子了，祖父也不让我们吃它们。" Veris 扶着女孩的胳膊，把她扶起来，在感觉像几个月来第一次微笑。"进来。我会告诉你它们的名字。""然后你会教我魔法？""不。然后我们喝杯茶。然后我们会讨论什么可以学，什么不可以学。"

**句子结构**：第一句用 "Veris pondered for a moment" 的动作，"then reached down and thumbed the latch of the garden gate" 是并列。第二句用 "I will think about it" 的将来时。第三句用 "Shut that tight behind you, please" 的命令。后续对话用引号分隔，最后一句用 "No. Then we'll have a cup of tea. And then we will discuss what may and may not be learned" 的并列。

**关键词**：pondered（沉思）、thumbed（用拇指拨）、latch（门闩）、stooped（弯腰）、elbow（胳膊）

**表达方式**：开放结局。"smiling for the first time in what felt like months" 暗示了 Veris 的转变——她开始从创伤中恢复。"I will think about it" 的开放性暗示了未来的可能性——故事没有结束，只是暂停。

**为什么这样写**：这段文本建立了开放性结局——Veris 是否愿意教 Eleonor？她能否重建生活？这些问题没有答案，但希望是存在的。
