# 情感节点

## 节点一：开篇算账（ch01）

母女战争的总动员：Antara 宣布母亲的 misery 是自己的 pleasure，而母亲的遗忘让 tally 再也算不清。诊室脑云图（cloud/plaque）与裸体窗前（"for that moment I am no one"）是恨与空的双开场。

关键引语：
- "I would be lying if I said my mother’s misery has never given me pleasure."（ch01）
- "For a moment she did not know who I am and for that moment I am no one."（ch01）

## 节点二：婚姻与身世（ch02–ch03）

German Bakery 相识、manglik 神婚（"Married and divorced"）、"But I’m not a widow"的身世引爆，餐桌"professional liar"互指。Dilip（saving me）与艺术（losing count）是 Antara 的两条逃生梯，全都通向同一个深渊。

关键引语：
- "‘But I’m not a widow,’ Ma said."（ch02，Ma 说）
- "If On Kawara is about counting, I am about losing count."（ch03）

## 节点三：Ashram 深渊（ch09/ch12）

最早记忆（giant/pyramid）、Baba 影像神学（"They decide history"）、Sita 之罪、1986 饥饿与殴打（"independent from wanting and happiness"）。Antara 在此学会 trick（掏空耳朵）与吞食（swallowed whole），余生受用。

关键引语：
- "‘Pictures,’ Baba said, ‘do not record history. They decide history.'"（ch09，Baba 说）
- "Being together or apart was independent from wanting and happiness."（ch12）

## 节点四：纵火与旧情人（ch14）

试住一夜烧掉十年画稿，灰烬里浮出旧情人照片（"They used to be lovers"），"Maybe she hoped to incinerate my marriage"。毁灭成了启示：Antara 第一次知道自己在画谁。

关键引语：
- "‘They used to be lovers.’"（ch14，Antara 说）
- "Maybe she hoped to incinerate my marriage. Maybe my life."（ch14）

## 节点五：1989 出走（ch15）

"I hate it here"夜奔、Club 行乞（"only as far as you can see"）、Vandana 耳光（"Your own mother barely looks at you"）、父家 KPI（"Make him love you"）后被弃。"I disown so I can never be disowned"写完一生关系算法。

关键引语：
- "I disown so I can never be disowned."（ch15）
- "‘You won’t remember this hardship one day,’ she whispered in my ear. ‘When you’re older, all these moments will cease to exist.’"（ch15，Ma 说）

## 节点六：Reza 真相（ch20–ch21/ch24–ch25）

Ayodhya 三词婚姻（"So he married her"）、1996 同居（"They could be each other, but I was only myself"）、Bombay 重逢与偷照片、"I took it"（Ma 拍的）、"devour the self"。母女共用同一个男人（先后），临摹即情书，偷即供词。

关键引语：
- "So he married her."（ch20）
- "Autophagy, from the Greek, means to devour the self."（ch24）

## 节点七：Baby-trap（ch22）

保险箱估价、"irrevocably"（baby 换婚姻）、经期谎言（"come inside me…even though I am not"）、co-authored（现实合著）。Antara 从取证者变为设局者，神圣（sacred）变手铐。

关键引语：
- "At night I come to bed without my clothes on, and while we have sex I whisper in his ear that he can come inside me because I’m expecting my period, even though I am not."（ch22）
- "‘Reality is something that is co-authored,’ the woman says."（ch22，life coach 说）

## 节点八：生产与命名（ch27–ch30）

Crime scene 分娩、"I’ll call my baby Antara"（三代压成一代）、Kali 密名（breathing fire）、assembly line 厌婴、开窗杀意（"small body can fall"）、Un-Tara 词源。Tara→Antara→Kali，三代名字即三代命运。

关键引语：
- "‘Antara,’ she says. ‘I’ll call my baby Antara.’"（ch27，Ma 说）
- "For a moment, my daughter is breathing fire, and I decide to call her Kali when no one is around."（ch30）

## 节点九：糖与意图（ch31）

喂糖的法理学（murder/manslaughter）、"love her to death"、"get away with it"（公开投毒实验）、Ma 反记忆（"You used to make me feel like shit"）。投毒者在纸上自首，世界无人收监。

关键引语：
- "Sometimes I think I am doing this just to see if I can get away with it."（ch31）
- "‘You used to make me feel like shit.’"（ch31，Ma 说）

## 节点十：聚会与门铃（ch32）

十一人聚会、"Kaushal is a creep"翻案（十七章延迟引爆）、"Antara is my baby"抢夺、摇篮曲合唱（Dilip 会唱 lullaby）、出走（barefoot）又回来按门铃。"No plaque"与"not sick at all"双悬置，marrow 永不免疫。

关键引语：
- "‘No, it’s my baby. Antara is my baby.’"（ch32，Ma 说）
- "I will never be free of her. She’s in my marrow and I’ll never be immune."（ch32）
