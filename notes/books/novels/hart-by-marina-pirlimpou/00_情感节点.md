# 《Hart: An MM, Omegaverse Romance》情感节点

**节点一：酒店酒吧的一夜（ch02）**。商务会议最后一夜，快进入 heat 的 Sam 在酒店酒吧被 Devon 搭讪。两人从打哈欠的玩笑问到名字，从「你在跟我调情吗」问到「你愿意吗」，然后上楼。Sam 在天亮前独自离开，只带走一身雪松与佛手柑的气味。

> "Leaning against the counter with a glass in hand. Impeccable suit, broad shoulders, dark hair, and eyes twinkling with obvious amusement. Trained on me."（ch02）
> "I want to go with him, even if it’s just for a few hours, even knowing I’m flying out tomorrow morning, and I’ll never see him again."（ch02）
> "Swallowing thickly, I make sure I don’t jostle the bed as I slowly try to get up. For just a second, I freeze when his hand flattens on my belly, holding me tightly, but he lets me go as I slip out of his arms without waking him up."（ch02）

**节点二：一年后的面试（ch03）**。一年后，Sam 到 King Security 应聘私人助理，发现老板就是那一夜的 alpha。Devon 先闻到他的气味才转身；他从履历上读到 Sam 刚生了孩子，误以为他已有伴侣。他替 Sam 说出被辞退的真正原因，然后把职位给了他，并对自己说「It’ll pass」。

> "It’s the scent that hits me before anything else. That roots me in place, and tells me who it is before I’ve heard his voice or looked at his face. That makes every muscle in my body tighten with awareness, with familiarity."（ch03）
> "“I see. And I’m sure the difference of opinion had nothing to do with your pregnancy and the fact that you’d have to be off work for several months but still required to be paid for those months?”"（ch03）
> "It’ll pass, and he’ll work here and be here, and I’ll be able to take care of him even indirectly. Keep an eye on him, and make sure he’s alright. Like an employer would do."（ch03）

**节点三：办公室的日常与第一次越界（ch04–ch05）**。Sam 上班第一周就赢得 Devon 的欣赏，第二周 Devon 已经离不开这套日常：等他进门、喝他泡的咖啡、问 Eli 的睡眠。Sam 带来自己烤的玛芬，Devon 脱口叫他「Sammy」，又因为 Sam 提议分担日程而发火，随后道歉、邀他吃午饭。公司这边，Colin 用前任助理 Daniel 泄密的旧事划出「上下级只能专业」的红线。

> "“Not Mr. Kingston.” His tone brooks no argument, his voice dropping lower, some of the earlier softness in his eyes morphing into something sharper. “In here, when it’s you and me, you call me Devon. Not sir, not Mr. Kingston. Devon.”"（ch04）
> "“You’re right, I’m sorry. Let me rephrase that.” There’s nothing I can do about the hoarseness of my voice when I look at him from across half my office, wishing I was whispering the words in his ear. “Have lunch with me, Sammy.”"（ch05）

**节点四：涨奶、没有标记与「只有我和 Eli」（ch07）**。加班到夜里，Sam 因为没时间泵奶而渗湿了衬衫。Devon 安慰他、给他自己的衬衫换，然后在替他整理衣领时要求看他的后颈——那里没有 mating mark。Sam 说「只有我和 Eli」，Devon 吻了那个本该有标记的位置，两人重新开始。

> "His breath hitches audibly, his eyes snapping up to meet mine in a look so embarrassed and mortified I know for a fact I never want to see it on his face ever again."（ch07）
> "“It’s just me and Eli.”"（ch07）
> "“I want to feel you again, Devon.” My name in that soft murmur stirs something in me that has been slumbering for more than a year now. That has only ever stirred for him, for those liquid brown eyes peering up at me. “Will you make me feel good again?”"（ch07）

**节点五：产后身体的交付（ch08）**。在上了锁的办公室里，Sam 先因产后的身体不肯脱衣，说「我不想让你看见我裸着」。Devon 跪下替他脱鞋、隔着衬衫只吻能看见的地方，直到 Sam 主动请他帮忙脱下衬衫；Devon 吻过剖腹产疤痕与妊娠纹，把每一道痕迹都收进「被看见」的范围。

> "“I don’t want you to see me naked.” The admission slips out in a voice so thin and fragile, I instantly hate it. But I can’t help what is true. “I’m not the same. My body, it’s—” Different. Changed. Worn out."（ch08）
> "I hold my breath as the shirt falls open, because I know what he sees apart from how much I want him. My pink, C-section scar, mostly healed but still raised even after almost four months."（ch08）
> "He doesn’t. He opens button after button, mapping with his mouth every reddish-brown line, every stretch mark on my hips, nuzzling my soft belly, touching reverently every part he uncovers."（ch08）

**节点六：嫉妒、失神与「我又来晚了吗」（ch11–ch14）**。新客户 Marcus Cross 对 Sam 表现出明显兴趣，一句「lovely」让 Devon 的占有欲当场炸开。随后公司响起入侵警报（后来查明只是传感器故障），Devon 违抗规程冲上楼，跪在 Sam 腿间。Devon 的失控也让 Sam 第一次看见他会突然「走神」；而 Devon 自己知道，那声音来自十年前没能及时赶到 Angel 身边的旧事。

> "I will do anything for him. I will keep him safe. Because he’s mine, mine, mine, and I want to keep him."（ch12）
> "“Hey.” An imperceptible tremble bleeds into my voice before I can stop it. “Where did you go?”"（ch13）
> "What if I’m too late again?"（ch14）

**节点七：Angel 的旧事与「你想吗」（ch15）**。警报解除后，Devon 终于讲出埋了十年的旧事：创业第一年他拒绝了一桩生意，对方报复性地伤害了他的弟弟 Angel，导致 Angel 失明；他赶到学校时只看见救护车。Sam 说「那不是你的错」，Devon 却说自己该放 Sam 走、连看他都不配。Sam 只问一句「你想吗」，得到一声「No」；随后 Sam 邀他周末回家、见 Eli。

> "“They hurt him, Sam,” he rasps, his previous anger morphing into a quiet, seething rage, fueled by his memories. “They hurt him so bad, he can’t see anymore. They hurt a fucking kid, just because they wanted to send a fucking message.”"（ch15）
> "“I should be letting you go. I should be peeling my fucking hands off you, and staying the fuck away from you.” He clutches me so tightly, I feel him branding my skin. “God knows, I don’t fucking deserve to look at you, let alone touch you.”"（ch15）
> "“Do you want that?” I whisper. “Do you want to let me go? To never see me, never touch me again?”"（ch15）

**节点八：周末与第一次「我爱你」（ch16）**。Sam 邀请 Devon 周末回家。Devon 第一次见到 Eli，看着这个婴儿时疼得莫名其妙——「以最好也最坏的方式」。他在 Sam 的小房子里放下「必须坚强」，在厨房里说出「I love you」，Sam 回答「I love you too」。

> "Eli looks at me with his big, light eyes, wide and curious, his cheeks puffy and rosy, his small fists clenching and unclenching, and it hurts. Looking at this tiny ball of innocence hurts in a way nothing has ever hurt before. In the best and worst way imaginable."（ch16）
> "“I love you,” I whisper into his hair."（ch16）
> "“I love you too.”"（ch16）

**节点九：秘密揭开与 mating（ch18）**。第二天清晨，Sam 醒来发现 Devon 抱着 Eli 在沙发上守到天亮。两人亲密之后，Devon 问他愿不愿意做自己的 mate，Sam 说 Yes。随后 Sam 终于说出 Eli 是他的孩子——「He’s yours, Devon. Ours.」Devon 用一个吻接住这句话，问他是否可以拥有他们两个。

> "“Will you?” he whispers. “Will you be my mate?”"（ch18）
> "“He’s yours, Devon,” I say, setting free the words I’ve been keeping for so long. “Ours.”"（ch18）
> "“You’re both mine?” His eyes are shining, and the guarded hope I witness in them makes my own eyes burn too. “My Sammy? My Eli?”"（ch18）

**节点十：六个月后的尾声（ch20）**。六个月后，Devon 已经搬进 Sam 的房子，公司的规则为 Sam 与 Eli 改写，危险业务被逐步淘汰。他在厨房里雕一只「站着的熊」（实际看起来像兔子），准备送给 Eli；Sam 脖子上有新鲜的 mating mark，heat 还没结束。Devon 送给 Sam 一块刻着小雏菊的木片，说 Sam 给了他「一个家、一家人、一个可以休息的安全地方」。

> "He stayed over the next weekend. And the next. Until it was no longer weekends. Until he brought his life here, threaded it with mine, and made it ours."（ch20）
> "It’s nothing compared to what you give me every day. The kind of happiness I never knew was possible. The privilege to call you mine.” He rubs his thumb across my freckles, his eyes suspiciously shiny, and my throat burns. “A home. A family. A safe place to rest.”"（ch20）
> "After all, his heart and mine are one and the same."（ch20）
