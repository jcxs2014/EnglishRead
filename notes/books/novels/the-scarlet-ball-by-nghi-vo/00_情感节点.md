# The Scarlet Ball · 情感节点

> 节点编号全书唯一；引语均逐字取自已过门禁的章节精读块。

## 节点一：送盒之夜（ch01）

火狗袭街、空宅无人，Judith 吃下不属于她的樱桃——全书的第一口"选择"，以一声炸雷定价。

> "Eating the last cherry in the bowl was sweeter than all the rest because it was a choice, and as I ate it, chewed, swallowed with my lips tingling from the sweetness, I knew I had never tasted anything better, and the lights went out with a clap of thunder."
> "“Ten dollars,” she said, and when I countered with twenty, she agreed so fast I knew I should have asked for more."

## 节点二：换装与怪物现身（ch02）

礼服、镜中现形的獠牙、缝进头皮的脸——灰姑娘的夜在第一晚就交了税；晚宴的童游里，紫水晶女孩死在离"洞"半步处。

> "In the mirror, Mr. Howard’s face wrinkled, a fast ripple of flesh over unyielding bone, revealing a mouth full of teeth that could never fit in a human skull. Then it was gone again, leaving behind a placid face with an amiable expression in its wake; I realized it was a smile."
> "“We are going to play Rabbit Rabbit,” said Mrs. de Lange in tones of great satisfaction. “You remember this one from when you were children, don’t you, girls? Rabbits move when they can see. They are still in the dark. They are only safe when they come home to their burrows, and for the sake of our little game, we shall say that this armchair here beside me is your burrow. Now, Violet, will you take the door?”"

## 节点三：卸脸与食物锁链（ch03）

脸"不是造的、是长的"；她想走，被一顿没有魔法的饭拴住——驯化的合同第一次全文公示。

> "The word stuck in my throat as the realization slithered through my brain: It wasn’t some artisan’s work with thin leather or paper. In Italy, there are masters who will make you a new face to your exact specifications from skin-thin ceramic. What Mrs. Howard had given me was not made, but grown, and the girl who had worn it had been beautiful before the knife and cuts and the blood."
> "This I can say, though: The food that Mrs. Howard gave me that night had no magic save that which food always has. It is the only cure for hunger we have ever found, and in enough quantity and quality, given regularly and enjoyed, it is a binding as powerful as any Renaissance warlock ever imagined."

## 节点四：谈判与火室（ch04）

砸房间换来第一次平视；火室里她亲验"祖父"的真身，以脸作押谈成价格——房子、薪水、解释权一样不退。

> "You did not burn the room around you because it would send for the firehouse. You did not attack the portraits because you know people run mad when even the semblance of their dear ones are attacked. You did not break the glass because those windows face the streets. You are aware, in short, that the rich will suffer many things so long as those things cannot be seen."
> "“She was sweet,” she said, almost to herself. “She told me before I did it that there were people who had loved this face, and that I should take care of it. This is valuable in more ways than one. I should not like to lose it, and if at the end you will not return it to me, it will be a loss that I cannot smooth over with money or friends. It is not replaceable, and it cannot be returned to me without your consent. Will that suit for your collateral?”"

## 节点五：驯化课与初出门（ch05）

记忆入火、针脚封口、课程表接管身体；两周后她顶着 Howard 脸第一次出门——发现假身份竟是她此生最好的铠甲。

> "“Ruined is ruined, and it goes all the way down, no matter who you have sent away or what revenge you have taken. Once ruined, always ruined. I would think one such as you would know that.”"
> "No one wanted to hear what a debutante thought about the fighting in Mexico; she only needed to know that there was fighting in Mexico and to validate the opinion of the man who had brought it up. It was, as Mrs. Howard said with heavy irony, the difference between a true education and simply knowing many things."

## 节点六：第二次白球：血与雷（ch06）

血入金碗喊出"到我这里来"，带翅的领主落地成富绅；她被风暴选中、被下绊摔倒、被雷击般迟到的饥饿搅局者救下——规则全数上场，也全数失灵。

> "“Everything I say to you has been written in blood with pens carved from the bones of women like you. To you, everything is deadly, and if you wish to survive it, let alone walk away with that pretty house you dream of, you would do well to remember that. Here, when I offer you my hand, you simply rest your fingertips on my palm, no weight. There. That’s right. Your hand goes on top, you stand to the lord’s right.”"
> "“I am Mrs. Henry Howard, and I rule here,” she said clearly, and before her words alone, the door split along an invisible center seam and opened to the stone hall beyond, as primitive as the door itself."
> "I couldn’t take my eyes away from the blood, how there was more of it than there should be even for how gleefully the bitch had bled us. The cuts on my wrist throbbed through the tight binding, and it seemed as if the beat of it, following the beat of my heart, became words: Come to me."

## 节点七：花园之死（ch07）

接吻的情人当场裂开死去；她学会锁住喉咙，也在附眼魔与银壶烈酒之后问出全书的问题——"这样的世界怎么活？"

> "I should have been screaming, but something trapped the scream in my throat. I wanted to blame the Howards and their glittering, brutal society, but it might have come from my time in the brothels. It might have come from my years at the convent. Wherever it came from, it said, Do not make a sound, or you will be next."
> "“As you always have,” she said with a peculiar gentleness. “They have always been here. They will be here long after the rest of us are gone, sitting on the tallest ruins of what once were your tallest cities, waiting for what comes next. They have always been with you, only now, you can see them.”"

## 节点八：冰宫与三条家规（ch10）

风暴应她的命令现出本体：把她抛上天、以闪电讨她服气、织出她童年偷读小说时梦见的冰宫——她定下不跑、定量、要服气，并以真名与 Alex 的记忆两度付账。

> "Perhaps the magic was that there was no magic. It wasn’t cheap, but anyone could gain entry to the Glaciarium if they had the money for a ticket. How delightful it had been, to fly over the ice in my furs, the cold of the rink a relief from the sweltering city outside. I missed it, I missed Paris and I missed—"
> "He gestured, and I looked back and up at the palace he had crafted. It wasn’t the old aristocratic manors in France or the villas in Italy. It looked more like the palaces I’d imagined as a little girl sneaking novels at the convent. The fluted spires pierced the blue sky, and the ice, shaped into window casements and thick walls and even gargoyles on the roofs, sparkled in the sun."

## 节点九：撕脸与火葬（ch17）

餐桌手术台上她装昏、反杀、撕下自己的脸掷还主妇；夫人死于火中"输"的表情，Mielle 剥下 Howard 脸喂给风暴——雷说：胜利。

> "“There,” I said, bubbling over with triumph and horror. “You may have that back, and I shall send you the rest as soon as I get around to it.”"
> "Then the thunder spoke, and what it said was victory."

## 节点十：肋骨笼的求婚与署名（ch18–19）

骨马跨过天体，她在巨兽肋骨笼里以血裙素颜讨回舞位；以喉血完成召唤，在寒气里谈成"更多的我们"——白金钻戒落指，纽波特舞厅她以真名落款。

> "I shaped the word in my mouth, forced it past my lips, and as it hung in the air between us, I knew that this wasn’t going to work. He loved me, and for him, this was what that meant. Love was consumption, love was a devouring, and when I was gone—"
> "What a stupid time to be honest, but all right. No, not forever, but more. More than this. More of me. More of us."
> "“Welcome,” I said. “I am Judith Ban, and I rule here.”"
