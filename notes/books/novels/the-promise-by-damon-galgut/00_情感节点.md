# 情感节点

> 全书 10 节点，每节点叙事概括 + 2-3 句关键引语

---

## 节点一：死亡告知（ch02）

**叙事概括**：Amor 在学校广播中听到母亲 Rachel 已死，无法相信。姨妈 Marina 用 Afrikaans 语言层包裹真相，Amor 用视觉回避（盯手）对抗听觉痛苦。

> "The moment the metal box speaks her name, Amor knows it's happened."
> "Nobody is dead. It's a word, that's all."
> "The only thing that is true is the hands, and me looking at the hands."

## 节点二：驶向房子（ch02）

**叙事概括**：Marina 和 Ockie 开车接 Amor 回家。Marina 在 Afrikaans 中议论 Ma 的信仰背叛，Amor 盯着父亲的手。Wessel 在集邮，Anton 尚未回家。

> "To my house, her aunt says. To get Uncle Ockie."
> "I'm sorry about your mother, he says."
> "Ag, she'll come back."

## 节点三：Salome 的 invisible（ch02）

**叙事概括**：Amor 通过窗户看到 Salome 在打扫母亲卧室。Salome 是 invisible 的——她照顾临终的 Ma 但无人看见她。Amor 想起父母曾忘记她在角落。

> "They forgot I was there, in the corner. They didn't see me, I was like a black woman to them."
> "Clean up here, wash the sheets, and she obeys."

## 节点四：Anton 接电话（ch03）

**叙事概括**：Anton 在约翰内斯堡接到 Astrid 的电话，说父亲在 ICU。他内心有预感——"like a funnel down which he can see a bright, tiny picture of the future." 他决定回家。

> "Hello? he says. What's wrong?"
> "Death." / "Not yet."
> "It's time." / "I don't know. I have to think."

## 节点五：ICU 对峙（ch03）

**叙事概括**：Anton 在 ICU 看到昏迷的父亲。他对着昏迷的父亲说出积压多年的愤怒——"I will never say sorry to you." 父亲毫无反应，退入"地下隧道"。

> "Something in me cares, he's astonished to discover."
> "I was crazy when Ma died. For a while I actually believed I killed her."
> "Picture a tunnel underground where no light has ever shone."

## 节点六：Manie 之死（ch03）

**叙事概括**：Herman Albertus Swart 于 1995 年 6 月 16 日凌晨 3:22 死亡。候诊室空无一人，家人已回家。唯一在场的是穆斯林护士 Waheeda。

> "Herman Albertus Swart dies at 3:22 in the morning on 16 June 1995 and the waiting room is empty."
> "Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un"
> "whether this intervention has any effect on his soul is impossible to say"

## 节点七：Astrid 的告解（ch04）

**叙事概括**：Astrid 在教堂告解室向 Father Batty 坦白婚外情。Batty 的谴责虚伪至极——"No penance for you today。" Astrid 离开时比进来时更不安。

> "Adultery with anybody is a mortal sin!"
> "No penance for you today."
> "She emerges from the confessional in a state of unease, far worse than when she went in."

## 节点八：Astrid 被绑架（ch04）

**叙事概括**：Astrid 在停车场被 Lindile 绑架。他要的是她的宝马车，不是她。她在后备箱里蜷缩，像婴儿。Lindile 唯一的同情是"Poor Astrid!"

> "Howzit, he says to her by way of greeting, showing a gun."
> "Disappointing."
> "Poor Astrid!"

## 节点九：Desirée 的控诉（ch05）

**叙事概括**：Anton 醉回家，Desirée 揭露她与 Moti 的关系。她说"I have wasted my life"——五十岁，一事无成。Anton 用"sperm count"反击。婚姻已死。

> "I have wasted my life."
> "And I'd shut up about waste if my sperm count was as low as yours."
> "She means to hurt him, because the recent revelation is deeply bitter"

## 节点十：酒店敲门（ch05）

**叙事概括**：Anton 在酒店房间，保险箱打不开，有人敲门。"Blam blam!"他吓得僵住。"Where will I hide?"——全书在此戛然而止。

> "Later, Anton in a hotel room, trying to get money out of a safe, but the bloody thing won't open."
> "Blam blam! He freezes in fear"
> "Where will I hide?"
