# 《Love You to Death》情感节点

## 节点一：没有时钟的白房间（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 一场爆炸把四个人同时送进死后那间全白的空房间。没有窗，没有钟，没有出口——一个灰袍人坐下来跟他们谈条件：机构眼下缺人手，代价是永恒地替它收魂，换来的是一间舒适的房间和定时三餐。四个人里有一个没有问"这里是什么地方"，他问的是"那么作为交换，我们究竟要做什么"。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“You’re likely wondering why you’re here,” the man in the robe continues. He takes the seat opposite me at the low wooden table. The foul stench of long-dead rotting things fills my nose. My stomach turns. “You’ve been specially invited by the Powers That Be”—he pauses, gesturing vaguely to the ceiling—“to make a choice.”"（ch01）

> ② "“You see, we find ourselves short-staffed at the moment.” The man grins at us, showing a mouth full of too-white, perfectly straight teeth. … It would have been a less terrifying smile if his teeth were jagged and rotten. … “Employment,” the man confirms. He gestures to the pages on the table. “As compensation, a comfortable room and regular meals will be provided for you for eternity. And you will be allowed to skip all that Hell nastiness.”"（ch01）

> ③ "“And in return, we agree to…do what, exactly?” Under the circumstances, it’s probably impertinent for me to ask. But if there was one thing I was good at while living, it was being impertinent. And using explosives, too, I suppose. Fat lot of good that did me in the end."（ch01）

## 节点二：一张会消失的卡片（ch02-03）

**叙事概括：** 公司退休宴上，她为了躲开忘记带铭牌的上司，藏到一个陌生男人身后，却反被那双冰蓝色的眼睛攫住。他连自己的名字都说不准，只留下一张银色字迹的卡片——姓名、年龄、日期，一行行在现实里时隐时现。字迹会自行消失，徒手去读还会被烫掉指尖；她用餐巾裹着手把它夹走，逃出酒店给妹妹发消息求助，而一辆巴士就在这时从路口冲了出来。

**关键引语：**

> ① "That pair of ice-blue eyes snaps to mine. They widen a little, as though the question startles him. “My name?” … His eyes go distant, as if he’s trying to answer a difficult question. “As for my name, it’s…Daniel?” His sentence turns up at the end, like he’s not sure about it."（ch02）

> ② "The silver-ink lettering shimmers, drifting in and out of reality beneath the elegant ballroom lighting. I only make out the first three lines before the writing blurs, winks out, then vanishes altogether. … What I do manage to see turns my blood to ice. … Name: Lucy McKenzie … Age: 28 years, 1 month, 3 days … Date: September 3"（ch02）

> ③ "It’s as plain as the disappearing ink on this notecard. My name, the date, and my exact freaking age were there. … He must have put some kind of defensive poison or acid or something on the card so anyone who tries to read it gets their fingertips singed off."（ch03）

## 节点三：车轮底下的几十秒（ch04-05）

**叙事概括：** 他到一场公交车撞死人的现场报到，嫌围观者碍事，却在 Lucy McKenzie 那束亮得惊人的人生里没能移开视线。而在她这一边，巴士底下的一切被痛感撕碎：一张悬在上方的脸，五官模糊，只有一双亮蓝的眼睛；一个声音在她意识里说话，说"你真亮"，然后把"构成她的东西"往外拽。她抵抗了一阵，最后因为那声惊叹松了手。

**关键引语：**

> ① "If it weren’t for this bus, she would have lived to a very old age. Or so my supervisor told me when they assigned me her file. … But I can’t think about that now. … I have work to do. … Only I’m finding it difficult to focus. … But Ms. McKenzie’s soul is so brilliant I could have found her anywhere. Not just a bright flash against the gloom, but more like a kaleidoscope with all its swirling colors."（ch04）

> ② "But now there is a face, hovering just above mine. Almost within reach. The features are blurry, except for bright blue eyes. … Eyes that are both foreign and familiar. They pierce through the haze that has taken my mind. … You are…so bright. They sound stunned. The person’s mouth doesn’t move. Am I hearing the words inside my head?"（ch05）

> ③ "I feel, rather than hear, the surprised gasp. … So bright, it says again with what might be genuine awe. … I stop fighting, focusing on the awe in that voice for the span of half a heartbeat. … That’s all the time they need. … They give one final, sharp tug, and now I float weightless above my body, outside the pain. … The vehicle that holds my body lurches forward as the rest of me is tugged out and away."（ch05）

## 节点四：候车室、死亡档案与一份合同（ch06-07）

**叙事概括：** 她在纯白房间醒来，对方正在念一份死亡档案：姓名、年龄、十七分钟十四秒前的那辆巴士，还有一句"你死了"。这不是天堂，是候车室。她摸着不存在的脉搏哀求，只求能去参加妹妹六周后的婚礼。他替她上去争取——求到的不是复活，是一纸八周延寿、到期照原样死去的合同，外加一份"派他去盯着她"的差事。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Lucy McKenzie, age twenty-eight. You were hit by a city bus seventeen minutes and fourteen seconds ago. You are dead.” … He says it so matter-of-factly, never taking his eyes off the page, that it takes a moment for what he’s said to sink in."（ch06）

> ② "“Let me make a deal with you,” I beg. … “Please,” I beg. “I just need a little more time.” … “I need to be there for my sister’s wedding in six weeks. Our mom walked out on us. I’m her only family. … Now I’m crying again in huge, racking sobs that feel torn from my soul. “I’m not finished yet. With what I have to do.”"（ch06）

> ③ "At the end of this eight-week extension, Lucy McKenzie’s soul must return to us by the original method of death. … In exchange for the invaluable information Lucy McKenzie will provide, upon her death, her soul will be given an Upgrade and permitted to reside in Mid-Level Two for eternity."（ch07）

## 节点五：停住的手机，与开始倒计时的清晨（ch08-10）

**叙事概括：** 在那间等不到天亮的白色房间里，她发现自己的手机永远定格在 8:44 p.m.，与妹妹的聊天记录永远停在三个点。回到同一个街口重新醒来之后，她终于摸到自己的脉搏，也终于把"八周"换算成一份具体的清单：纸、猫、妹妹。而他回到 Agency，摊在面前的是一张盖着红印的观察表；把她那间又破又热闹的公寓和 Agency 的房间摆在一起一比，他发现自己已经不想回到那种日子里去。

**关键引语：**

> ① "There’s no way to mark the passage of time here. There’s no window I can look out of and no clock. … My phone is frozen at 8:44 p.m. That must be when I died."（ch08）

> ② "In eight weeks I have to leave Wilf behind. He’s been the only constant in my life other than Jess, and now I’m leaving him. I’m the only home he’s ever had. … “Eight weeks won’t be enough time,” I whimper."（ch09）

> ③ "The contrast between her home and the room I now stand in couldn’t be more drastic. The Agency—where I have dwelled for more than a century—is all blank walls and sterile, featureless rooms. Ms. McKenzie’s home—Ms. McKenzie herself—is life and color and sound. … And it all pales in comparison to what it was like to hold her."（ch10）

## 节点六：复活后的第一个早晨，与图书馆里的表单（ch11-12）

**叙事概括：** 她给自己定下"接下来八周，做 Jess 一个可靠的姐姐"的目标，穿着闪亮粉色牛仔靴去赴一杯自己提起的咖啡之约；杯子的奶泡上浮着一副骷髅和两根交叉骨。而在大学图书馆的自习室里，一个刚从她公寓离开的死神正一边查"为什么会想碰不该碰的人"，一边填一份"用五句话描述 Lucy 受惊时的反应"的表单——写出来全是划掉的心事。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I need to get up, though. Being a reliable sister to Jess is my primary goal for the next eight weeks. Showing up late to the coffee date I suggested would mean I’m already off to a bad start."（ch11）

> ② "When I hold the mug up to my lips to take my first sip and see what’s inside, I nearly drop it to the floor in surprise. … Two crossed bones beneath a sightless skull. … “I’m so sorry! I knew the machine had been acting weird, but…How did that even happen?”"（ch11）

> ③ "In no more than five sentences, please describe how Lucy McKenzie reacts to surprises. … Due: Six hours after receipt. … Upon completion, please return to the Agency via interoffice mail. … Under normal circumstances, I could complete this inside a handful of minutes. Now, though, my mind is just butterflies and static."（ch12）

## 节点七：周一的两难，和一杯越界的酒（ch13）

**叙事概括：** 闹钟响起的周一早上，她在"要不要为了再撑八周继续做这份吸灵魂的工作"上纠结了半小时。一边是随叫随到地帮妹妹筹备婚礼，一边是那个履历糟糕的教授和聊得来的同事；最后妹妹赢了——她辞职，抱着纸箱走出办公楼，在校园里遇见了那个不该碰的人。他替她躲过一辆自行车，然后问她要不要去喝一杯；"按规定，我不该跟你套近乎"，他这样说完，还是答应了。

**关键引语：**

> ① "When my alarm goes off for work on Monday morning, I spend thirty minutes locked in indecision … Should I keep my soul-sucking job for eight more weeks? Or should I quit now, since I’ll be dead soon anyway and nothing matters? … “It’s a dilemma,” I tell Wilfred as I brush my teeth."（ch13）

> ② "“I’m quitting, El,” I say around the sudden lump in my throat. … “No shit?” … “Today is my last day.” … “That’s amazing!” she says, laughing. “Oh my god, Skellar is such a nightmare. I’m so proud of you for taking the plunge.”"（ch13）

> ③ "“Want to get a drink?” … “Rooster’s is a bar near here where I sometimes go to unwind. And I just thought…you know.” … “It’s against protocol to fraternize with you.” … “All right, Ms. McKenzie. Let’s get a drink.”"（ch13）

## 节点八：契约、地狱，与"我也害怕"（ch14-15）

**叙事概括：** 九月闷热的田纳西，路边那家破酒吧里，他终于说出自己为什么退不掉：为了不去地狱，他签下的就是永世做收魂人的契约。临别时她终于说出"我害怕"，他回她"我在这儿"，然后补上三个字——"我也害怕"。可第二天的高管会议上他还在走神，因为昨夜那个醉着独自开车走的女孩；等他把她的差评推过桌面，地狱的首相就推门而入，用一声"Daniel Ivanov"把一百三十五年前的旧账重新翻开。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“It’s fine.” The way his eyes have shuttered, though, says otherwise. “No. I cannot quit. This isn’t a job the way you think of jobs. Becoming a Reaper for eternity was the deal I struck to avoid being sent to Hell.”"（ch14）

> ② "“I’m scared,” I finally admit on a whisper. … “Don’t be,” he says. His voice is low and steady at my ear. “I’m here.” … Tentatively, as if he knows he shouldn’t, he reaches up and tucks a lock of hair that has fallen into my eyes behind my ear. … Daniel’s expression is pure torment. “I’m scared, too.”"（ch14）

> ③ "“Nice to see you again, Daniel Ivanov.” … Lucifer might only be Hell’s Prime Minister and not the Devil himself, but it’s hard to remember that with the proprietary way he says my name. His two-foot-long horns and forked tail also don’t help. … “Sir,” I manage, inclining my head in deference."（ch15）

## 节点九："这怎么可能"（ch16-17）

**叙事概括：** 婚宴的伴手礼、闪粉、番茄酱和一只猫，中间只剩他指腹描过她下唇的那一下——"这怎么可能……已经……太久了。"而当她追问自己为什么能碰到他，他给出的是一份一百多年的触感匮乏："自从我成为收魂人以来，没有人碰过我。除了你。"紧接着他承认，没有她的手在身上，每一分钟都像在慢慢地死去，一次又一次，却没有死亡带来的解脱。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“How is this possible,” he murmurs, sounding at least as dazed as I feel. “It’s…been so long.” … He reaches out and, with a featherlight touch, traces my full bottom lip with the pad of a finger."（ch16）

> ② "“You have done nothing wrong. Devil take me—but touching you like this…it’s all I dream about.” He leans forward, elbows resting on his knees. “No one has touched me since I became a Reaper. Not until you.”"（ch17）

> ③ "“But it doesn’t matter to me why you can touch me,” he says. “All I know is that you can. Your touch has been all I’ve thought about since that tedious reception, … Now, every moment spent without your hands on me feels like it did at the beginning. Like I’m dying slowly, again and again, but without the relief of death."（ch17）

## 节点十：第 417 条（ch18-19）

**叙事概括：** 他被 Agency 强行召回，坐在 Inhuman Resources 的候诊室里挨训：规章说死神不得对自己的灵魂产生依恋，而罪证竟是他自己两天前交上去的那份观察备忘；真正让他不安的不是违规，而是自己撒谎越来越熟练。而在咖啡馆里，他对她说的是另一套话——"你是我见过的最恶劣的那种人"：怪物，然后是"所以我说你说你好的时候，你该信我"。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Consider this a warning, Friend. You will maintain professional boundaries with your assigned souls at all times.” … But holding Lucy, kissing her, awoke something in me that had been dormant so long I’d forgotten what longing was. … “I will,” I lie. … I already care too much about her. … I should be concerned, really, about how easy lying to the Agency has become."（ch18）

> ② "“As a Reaper, I have met the worst kinds of people. … Monsters, Lucy.” … I was a monster once, too. So when I tell you that you are good, trust me. You are.” … It’s been very easy to forget that he was originally supposed to go to Hell. … “I’m not sure I could stay away if I tried.”"（ch19）

## 节点十一：玩具店、脚踏酒吧车，与一次旧身份的坦白（ch20-23）

**叙事概括：** 她把他拉进玩具店挑毛绒玩具；妹妹单身派对的脚踏酒吧车上，他是那个本不该出现的人——他查了那趟车的路线，沿路一家家酒吧找过来。回到 Agency，他警告三个同事一个字都别往外说，心里盘算的却是"也许该告诉她我到底是谁"。深夜的卧室里，他把俄罗斯小镇、码头那票炸掉三个人的活儿和 Nicholas 一五一十交代出来；她的回答是："你告诉我的这些，不会改变什么。"

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I’m getting Jess and Liam a matching set of stuffies as a gift but can’t decide what to get.” … She holds up the toy she’s holding in her left hand (a pink thing with white felt eyes) and then the one in her right (a green thing with blue felt eyes). “I thought you could help me choose.”"（ch20）

> ② "I do a double take when I see Daniel sitting at the bar in his usual suit and tie, nursing a cocktail, giving the singer his full attention. … “Let me guess,” I say. “You just happened to be in the neighborhood?” … “There was a classical music performance at the Ryman tonight,” he says without missing a beat. “Mendelssohn violin concertos. My favorites.”"（ch21）

> ③ "I shake my head. “What you told me doesn’t change anything.” … “So you’re no saint,” I quip. “I already guessed that much. You were doing the best you could with the shitty hand you were dealt. It also sounds like you’ve spent every day since you died regretting what you did. Including things that weren’t your fault.”"（ch23）

## 节点十二：湖边小城，与"我不想死"（ch24-25）

**叙事概括：** 他带她回童年那片湖边，一家假疗养院旧址、一家太贵的餐厅、湖边地上的一幅粉笔画，最后是一个吻——吻到她贴着他的嘴唇改口说"带我回家"。而在卧室里，她说出了那句改变一切的话："我不想死"；他吻着她的头顶答应陪她到最后，只是那句"之后"，他不能给。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Devil take what I said last night,” he breathes. … “You are just so…vibrantly alive. Everything about you. I can’t fight it anymore. I don’t even want to try.” … “Take me home,” I breathe against his lips. … He chuckles, gives me one final, fierce kiss that steals my breath—and the world disappears."（ch24）

> ② "“I don’t want to die,” I confess, completely changing the tone of our pillow talk with one short sentence. … “I know, sweetheart.” He kisses the top of my head. The apple of each cheek. … “I know. I’ll be with you the whole way, right until the end.” … He doesn’t say he’ll be with me after the end, of course. … Because he can’t."（ch25）

## 节点十三：传唤、撤销，与她自己开出的条件（ch26-30）

**叙事概括：** 一纸传唤把 Daniel 叫进 Executive Suite 的接待台，等来的是 CEO 当面撤销 Lucy 的八周延寿、限她四天内交回灵魂，以及他自己下周一与 Lucifer 的降级面谈。当晚他只带来四个字："他们改主意了。"于是她亲自去教那帮人怎么做人——她要替两个人一起定价："这件事必须我来做。"而在三个高管面前，她把那张 Arkansas 的自拍照摊开："你们对人类的了解少得可怜……少到不明白你们自己人中的一个为什么会爱上一个人。"

**关键引语：**

> ① "“You’ve grown attached to a human soul.” He shakes his head ruefully. … “Next Monday, we will meet with you and Lucifer to discuss your demotion. … My room is spare and depressing, but it’s the only home I’ve had for 135 years. … “You may go, Daniel,” he says, softly. … I flinch. … The last time he said my name was the day I died."（ch26）

> ② "“They changed their minds.” He murmurs the words, as if saying them quietly will dull the blow. … “About you. They’re rescinding their offer.” He swallows. “You have to return to them sooner than you’d hoped.”"（ch27）

> ③ "He crushes me to him. “Please let me send you home so I can do this for you.” … “I have to be the one to do this, Daniel.” … Daniel swallows audibly. “Here we go.” But he doesn’t move a muscle. … I put my hand on the bag carrying the photographs and my phone. “Here goes nothing,” I say."（ch29）

> ④ "I turn it around so everyone in the room can see the selfie of the two of us on our date in Arkansas. Daniel has his arms wrapped around my waist. … “You know so little about humans,” I say, refusing to cry in front of these monsters, “that you don’t even understand why one of your own fell for one.”"（ch30）

## 节点十四：一张床、一张桌子、一把椅子（ch31-32）

**叙事概括：** 她走进他住的地方，问"这是你的家？"，他答"这不是我的家"——只有一张床、一张桌子、一把椅子，不过是他落脚的地方；真话"你才是我的家"被他咽了回去。同一夜，她开出的条件是"既往服役折算年限、放他走"，作为交换，她换来一份工作、一条期限不明的余生，和一张一碰就碎成灰的死亡卡，以及"我们有整夜"。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“This is your home?” She’s dismayed, and wants to hide it from me but can’t. I would know even without our bond. She’s brimming with it. … No, I want to tell her. You’re my home. … “It’s not my home,” I say instead. “Only a bed, a table, a chair. It’s just where I stay.”"（ch31）

> ② "“I said I’d only do this if they treat your service to date as a Reaper as time served and let you go.” … “They think you’ll get to try again.” … “At living,” I clarify. … I see my Death Card lying on my beside table as if it’s been waiting for me. … It crumbles into dust on contact. … And we have all night."（ch32）

## 节点十五：绕回原点的那间空房（ch33）

**叙事概括：** 仪式办在当年改变他命运的那间毫无特征的空房里，几个同批成为收魂人的旧识站在旁边——连脸都没有；他只能"几乎肯定"他们是谁。CEO 清了清嗓子，为擅自改动客座名单致歉，然后合上那本翻开的古书，抬起手说"我们开始吧"。咒语一个音都没被写出来，被写下来的只有他闭眼时的心跳，和脑子里那个唯一的名字。

**关键引语：**

> ① "It feels like a full-circle moment, holding this ceremony in the same featureless room where my destiny was changed all those years ago. … In fact, these Reapers—who I am almost positive are the murderer, the politician, and the bedraggled woman whose former name I never managed to learn—have no faces at all."（ch33）

> ② "The CEO clears his throat. “Apologies for taking liberties with the guest list,” he says to me, not sounding apologetic at all. … At length, the CEO closes his book with a definitive thump. He must have found the incantation he was looking for. … “All right,” he says, raising his hands. “Let us begin.” … My long-dormant heart thunders. … I close my eyes and think only of Lucy."（ch33）

## 节点十六：三周之后（ch34-35）

**叙事概括**：等待的日子里她把日子维持成日常：失眠、投简历、给妹妹一个藏了很久的礼物、替 Agency 备第一份作业。而在咖啡店里，一个心跳是真人、心却还在慢慢学做人的人出现在她面前——他不再问"我是不是人了"，他问"这会不会是个问题"；她回答"你是我的"，他松了肩膀："你的……永远。"三周之后，他站在浴室镜子前端详自己新长出来的二十根灰白头发，跟她走进 Jess 的婚礼，在满屋宾客面前紧张得指尖发抖。舞池上，他补上当年逃走的那支舞；全书最后一句是：说到底，值了。

**关键引语：**

> ① "I freeze. That’s Daniel’s voice. Or is my sanity going?
