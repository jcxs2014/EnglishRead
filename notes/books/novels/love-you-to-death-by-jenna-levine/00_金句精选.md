# 《Love You to Death》金句精选

① "That pair of ice-blue eyes snaps to mine. They widen a little, as though the question startles him. “My name?” … His eyes go distant, as if he’s trying to answer a difficult question. “As for my name, it’s…Daniel?” His sentence turns up at the end, like he’s not sure about it."（ch02）

**中文**：那双冰蓝色的眼睛一下转向我，微微睁大，好像这个问题把他吓了一跳。"我的名字？"他的目光飘向远处，像在回答一道很难的题。"至于我的名字，它是……Daniel？"他这句话的尾音往上扬，像自己也不确定。
**上下文**：ch02 退休宴上，她随口问了一句"你叫什么"，他给出的却是一个尾音上扬的疑问句。
**为什么重要**：全书最要紧的一次自我介绍是残缺的一句——一个活了 135 年的死神，说不出自己的名字；这份"没有名字"后来会变成他对 Lucy 说"你是我的家"时不敢出口的理由。
**呼应关系**：与 "“Nice to see you again, Daniel Ivanov.” … Lucifer might only be Hell’s Prime Minister and not the Devil himself, but it’s hard to remember that with the proprietary way he says my name. His two-foot-long horns and forked tail also don’t help. … “Sir,” I manage, inclining my head in deference."（ch15）"Daniel Ivanov"被 Lucifer 连名带姓叫出来，是同一根线的两端。

② "It’s as plain as the disappearing ink on this notecard. My name, the date, and my exact freaking age were there. … He must have put some kind of defensive poison or acid or something on the card so anyone who tries to read it gets their fingertips singed off."（ch03）

**中文**：这就像卡片上用的是消失墨水一样一目了然。我的名字、日期，还有我那精确到吓人的年龄，都印在上面。他一定在卡片上涂了毒或酸之类的东西，好让任何试图去读它的人把指尖都烧掉。
**上下文**：ch03 她用餐巾裹着手把卡片夹走，一路推断这张卡的材质与机关。
**为什么重要**：超自然在这里被写成一道**材料题**——她怕的不是命运，是字迹消失和指尖被烫；恐惧被转写成了可推理的工程问题。
**呼应关系**：与 "The silver-ink lettering shimmers, drifting in and out of reality beneath the elegant ballroom lighting. I only make out the first three lines before the writing blurs, winks out, then vanishes altogether. … What I do manage to see turns my blood to ice. … Name: Lucy McKenzie … Age: 28 years, 1 month, 3 days … Date: September 3"（ch02）卡片上会自行消失的银色字迹同源。

③ "I feel, rather than hear, the surprised gasp. … So bright, it says again with what might be genuine awe. … I stop fighting, focusing on the awe in that voice for the span of half a heartbeat. … That’s all the time they need. … They give one final, sharp tug, and now I float weightless above my body, outside the pain. … The vehicle that holds my body lurches forward as the rest of me is tugged out and away."（ch05）

**中文**：我感觉到了——而不是听见——那声惊讶的吸气。……"真亮啊，"那声音又说了一遍，语气里带着也许是真正的惊叹。……我停止抵抗，把注意力放在那声音里的惊叹上，停了半次心跳的时间。……他们需要的就只有这么点时间。
**上下文**：ch05 巴士底下，收魂的手正在把她的灵魂从身体里拽出来。
**为什么重要**：决定她生死的是半次心跳的走神——不是因为她软弱，而是因为有人在那一刻惊讶于她的亮。这半拍让她从一个数字变成一个人。
**呼应关系**：与 "If it weren’t for this bus, she would have lived to a very old age. Or so my supervisor told me when they assigned me her file. … But I can’t think about that now. … I have work to do. … Only I’m finding it difficult to focus. … But Ms. McKenzie’s soul is so brilliant I could have found her anywhere. Not just a bright flash against the gloom, but more like a kaleidoscope with all its swirling colors."（ch04）"McKenzie 女士的灵魂亮得惊人"是同一句惊艳的两次出现。

④ "“Lucy McKenzie, age twenty-eight. You were hit by a city bus seventeen minutes and fourteen seconds ago. You are dead.” … He says it so matter-of-factly, never taking his eyes off the page, that it takes a moment for what he’s said to sink in."（ch06）

**中文**：「Lucy McKenzie，二十八岁。十七分钟十四秒前，你被一辆城市巴士撞了。你死了。」他说得那么就事论事，眼睛始终没有离开那一页，以至于这句话过了一会儿才沉进我心里。
**上下文**：ch06 她在纯白房间醒来，对方正在念一份死亡档案。
**为什么重要**：全书用一份**公文**宣布一个人的死亡：姓名、年龄、精确到秒的时间差——最冷的形式承载最重的消息。
**呼应关系**：与 "The silver-ink lettering shimmers, drifting in and out of reality beneath the elegant ballroom lighting. I only make out the first three lines before the writing blurs, winks out, then vanishes altogether. … What I do manage to see turns my blood to ice. … Name: Lucy McKenzie … Age: 28 years, 1 month, 3 days … Date: September 3"（ch02）卡片上那三行字段，是同一套机构格式的两处出现。

⑤ "“Let me make a deal with you,” I beg. … “Please,” I beg. “I just need a little more time.” … “I need to be there for my sister’s wedding in six weeks. Our mom walked out on us. I’m her only family. … Now I’m crying again in huge, racking sobs that feel torn from my soul. “I’m not finished yet. With what I have to do.”"（ch06）

**中文**：「让我跟你做个交易，」我哀求道。……「求你了，」我哀求。「我只是需要多一点时间。」……「我需要在六周后的妹妹婚礼上陪着她。妈妈抛弃过我们。我是她唯一的家人。」
**上下文**：ch06 她刚被告知自己已死，第一反应不是追问这是哪里，而是开价。
**为什么重要**：这本书的第一个"谈判"由死者发起——她没有哭诉，只是把"我还没做完"变成了一个可以交换的条件。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I said I’d only do this if they treat your service to date as a Reaper as time served and let you go.” … “They think you’ll get to try again.” … “At living,” I clarify. … I see my Death Card lying on my beside table as if it’s been waiting for me. … It crumbles into dust on contact. … And we have all night."（ch32）她把同一个动作再做一次，是同一套语言隔了二十多章的回声。

⑥ "In eight weeks I have to leave Wilf behind. He’s been the only constant in my life other than Jess, and now I’m leaving him. I’m the only home he’s ever had. … “Eight weeks won’t be enough time,” I whimper."（ch09）

**中文**：八周以后我就得把 Wilf 留下去了。这一直是我生活里唯一不变的东西，除了 Jess；现在我也要离开他了。他唯一待过的家就是我。……「八周不够用。」
**上下文**：ch09 她在同一个街口醒来，回家后头一次把"八周"换算成具体的失去。
**为什么重要**：倒计时的第一个单位不是天，是一只猫——她把抽象的期限换算成了清单。
**呼应关系**：与 "I need to get up, though. Being a reliable sister to Jess is my primary goal for the next eight weeks. Showing up late to the coffee date I suggested would mean I’m already off to a bad start."（ch11）"接下来八周，做 Jess 一个可靠的姐姐"是同一支笔下的两种算法。

⑦ "I need to get up, though. Being a reliable sister to Jess is my primary goal for the next eight weeks. Showing up late to the coffee date I suggested would mean I’m already off to a bad start."（ch11）

**中文**：不过我得起来。接下来八周，做 Jess 一个可靠的姐姐，就是我的首要目标。咖啡之约既然是我提的，迟到就已经是坏的开头了。
**上下文**：ch11 复活后的第一个早晨，她为赴一杯自己提起的咖啡之约而起床。
**为什么重要**：全书对"及时"这件事最轻的一次落地——她把"活着"翻译成"不迟到"这种可执行的日常尺度。
**呼应关系**：与 "“Let me make a deal with you,” I beg. … “Please,” I beg. “I just need a little more time.” … “I need to be there for my sister’s wedding in six weeks. Our mom walked out on us. I’m her only family. … Now I’m crying again in huge, racking sobs that feel torn from my soul. “I’m not finished yet. With what I have to do.”"（ch06）"我还没做完"同源。

⑧ "“I’m quitting, El,” I say around the sudden lump in my throat. … “No shit?” … “Today is my last day.” … “That’s amazing!” she says, laughing. “Oh my god, Skellar is such a nightmare. I’m so proud of you for taking the plunge.”"（ch13）

**中文**："我要辞职了，El，"我喉咙里突然一哽，把这句话挤了出来。……"不会吧？"……"今天是我最后一天。"……"太棒了！"她笑着说。"天哪，Skellar 简直是个噩梦。我为你敢跳出这一步骄傲死了。"
**上下文**：ch13 她把那份"吸灵魂的工作"辞了，理由是妹妹的婚礼。
**为什么重要**：全书最轻的一次革命——辞职的理由不是受不了，是要给一个人腾出时间；而她的同事把这件事当成"勇敢"。
**呼应关系**：与 "“Let me make a deal with you,” I beg. … “Please,” I beg. “I just need a little more time.” … “I need to be there for my sister’s wedding in six weeks. Our mom walked out on us. I’m her only family. … Now I’m crying again in huge, racking sobs that feel torn from my soul. “I’m not finished yet. With what I have to do.”"（ch06）在候车室里说"我还没做完"是同一条线的两次落点。

⑨ "“It’s fine.” The way his eyes have shuttered, though, says otherwise. “No. I cannot quit. This isn’t a job the way you think of jobs. Becoming a Reaper for eternity was the deal I struck to avoid being sent to Hell.”"（ch14）

**中文**："没事。"可他的眼神已经合上了，那样子分明不是没事。"不。我退不掉。这不是你以为的那种工作。为了不去地狱，我签下的就是永世做收魂人的契约。"
**上下文**：ch14 破酒吧里，她问他能不能离开这份差事。
**为什么重要**：全书最重要的合同条款在这一句里说完——他不是自愿留下，是先签了字才活到今天；后面所有的违规，都是在还一笔旧债。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I said I’d only do this if they treat your service to date as a Reaper as time served and let you go.” … “They think you’ll get to try again.” … “At living,” I clarify. … I see my Death Card lying on my beside table as if it’s been waiting for me. … It crumbles into dust on contact. … And we have all night."（ch32）"既往服役折算年限、放他走"是同一份契约的解约条款。

⑩ "“I’m scared,” I finally admit on a whisper. … “Don’t be,” he says. His voice is low and steady at my ear. “I’m here.” … Tentatively, as if he knows he shouldn’t, he reaches up and tucks a lock of hair that has fallen into my eyes behind my ear. … Daniel’s expression is pure torment. “I’m scared, too.”"（ch14）

**中文**："我害怕。"我终于小声承认。……"别怕，"他说。他的声音低而稳，就在我耳边。"我在这儿。"……他迟疑着，仿佛知道自己不该这么做，伸手把我垂到眼前的头发别到耳后。他的表情全是折磨。"我也害怕。"
**上下文**：ch14 酒吧门口分别前，他终于没有再叫她"McKenzie 小姐"。
**为什么重要**：全书最短的一次坦白只有四个字——他没有比她勇敢，他只是先说"我在这儿"，才敢说"我也害怕"。
**呼应关系**：与 "“You have done nothing wrong. Devil take me—but touching you like this…it’s all I dream about.” He leans forward, elbows resting on his knees. “No one has touched me since I became a Reaper. Not until you.”"（ch17）"自从我成为收魂人以来，没有人碰过我。除了你"是同一条孤独线的两端。

⑪ "“Nice to see you again, Daniel Ivanov.” … Lucifer might only be Hell’s Prime Minister and not the Devil himself, but it’s hard to remember that with the proprietary way he says my name. His two-foot-long horns and forked tail also don’t help. … “Sir,” I manage, inclining my head in deference."（ch15）

**中文**："很高兴再见到你，Daniel Ivanov。"……Lucifer 也许只是地狱的首相、而不是撒旦本人，可他说我名字时那种据为己有的口气，让人很难记住这件事。他那两英尺长的角和分叉的尾巴也不帮忙。
**上下文**：ch15 高管会议开到一半，地狱的首相推门而入。
**为什么重要**：一百三十五年前那场爆炸的旧账，就是被这声全名叫醒的；作者让"被叫出全名"这件事同时是重逢和清算。
**呼应关系**：与 "That pair of ice-blue eyes snaps to mine. They widen a little, as though the question startles him. “My name?” … His eyes go distant, as if he’s trying to answer a difficult question. “As for my name, it’s…Daniel?” His sentence turns up at the end, like he’s not sure about it."（ch02）他连自己的名字都说不准，是同一根线上的两端。

⑫ "“You have done nothing wrong. Devil take me—but touching you like this…it’s all I dream about.” He leans forward, elbows resting on his knees. “No one has touched me since I became a Reaper. Not until you.”"（ch17）

**中文**："你没有做错任何事。要我说，接你这样碰我……那就是我全部的梦了。"他俯下身，手肘支在膝盖上。"自从我成为收魂人以来，没有人碰过我。除了你。"
**上下文**：ch17 她问自己为什么能碰到他，他解释了原因，也解释了那份禁忌。
**为什么重要**：这本书最动人的坦白不是"我喜欢你"，而是一份一百多年的触感匮乏；她的特殊不在于例外，而在于唯一。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I don’t want to die,” I confess, completely changing the tone of our pillow talk with one short sentence. … “I know, sweetheart.” He kisses the top of my head. The apple of each cheek. … “I know. I’ll be with you the whole way, right until the end.” … He doesn’t say he’ll be with me after the end, of course. … Because he can’t."（ch25）"直到最后"、与 "“This is your home?” She’s dismayed, and wants to hide it from me but can’t. I would know even without our bond. She’s brimming with it. … No, I want to tell her. You’re my home. … “It’s not my home,” I say instead. “Only a bed, a table, a chair. It’s just where I stay.”"（ch31）"你才是我的家"构成同一条线。

⑬ "“But it doesn’t matter to me why you can touch me,” he says. “All I know is that you can. Your touch has been all I’ve thought about since that tedious reception, … Now, every moment spent without your hands on me feels like it did at the beginning. Like I’m dying slowly, again and again, but without the relief of death."（ch17）

**中文**："但你为什么能碰到我，对我来说不重要，"他说。"我只知道你能。你的触碰从那场无聊的招待会之后，就一直是我想的全部。"……"现在，每一个没有你的手在我身上的时刻，都跟当初一样。像是慢慢地死去，一次又一次，却没有死亡带来的解脱。"
**上下文**：ch17 她还问了一句"为什么是我"，他把问题推了回去。
**为什么重要**：作者让死神用"没有你的手在身上"的写法来形容相思——不是渴望，而是**症状**；死过一次的人把相思说成了复发。
**呼应关系**：与 "“I said I’d only do this if they treat your service to date as a Reaper as time served and let you go.” … “They think you’ll get to try again.” … “At living,” I clarify. … I see my Death Card lying on my beside table as if it’s been waiting for me. … It crumbles into dust on contact. … And we have all night."（ch32）"我们有整夜"是同一种时间的两种定价。

⑭ "“Consider this a warning, Friend. **中文**："把这当成一次警告，朋友。你必须始终与你的对象灵魂保持职业界线。"……可是抱着 Lucy、吻着她，唤醒了我身上那样沉睡了太久、久到我连"渴望"是什么都忘了的东西。……"我会的，"我说谎。……我真的应该担心的，是自己对着 Agency 撒谎竟然变得这么容易。
**上下文**：ch18 他被召回，罪证是自己两天前交上去的那份观察备忘。
**为什么重要**：规章把"感情"列为违规项，而这一段把违规写得毫无戏剧性——真正让作者不安的是**他撒谎越来越熟练**。
**呼应关系**：与 "“You’ve grown attached to a human soul.” He shakes his head ruefully. … “Next Monday, we will meet with you and Lucifer to discuss your demotion. … My room is spare and depressing, but it’s the only home I’ve had for 135 years. … “You may go, Daniel,” he says, softly. … I flinch. … The last time he said my name was the day I died."（ch26）CEO 当面指出"你对一个凡人的灵魂产生了依恋"，是同一份卷宗的下一次开封。

**中文**："作为一个收魂人，我见过最恶劣的那类人。……怪物，Lucy。……我从前也是怪物。所以我告诉你你是好人时，你要信我。你是。"……"我不确定自己能忍住不来看她。"
**上下文**：ch19 试衣日之后的咖啡馆里，他终于把"我曾经也是怪物"这件事说出口。
**为什么重要**：他给出的不是安慰，是**资格**——"所以我说你很好的时候，你该信我"；一个说自己曾是怪物的人来担保，这句话因此有重量。
**呼应关系**：与 "I shake my head. “What you told me doesn’t change anything.” … “So you’re no saint,” I quip. “I already guessed that much. Including things that weren’t your fault.”"（ch23）"你告诉我的这些，不会改变什么"是同一件事的两面。

**中文**："这件事你们一个字都别往外说，"我用尽全部尊严警告他们。"否则我就告诉高管们，去年给 CEO 那些画像画小胡子的是你们。"…… 也许——只是也许——告诉她，我到底是谁。

**为什么重要**：全书对"身份"最喜剧化的一次亮相：他还在用勒索维持体面，而心里真正在盘算的是要不要向那个人坦白——威胁与柔软并排放在同一段里。
