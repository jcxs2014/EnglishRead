# 《Love You to Death》概述

## 全书梗概

**一、爆炸之后，那间没有时钟的白房间。** 一场爆炸把四个人同时送进死后那间全白的空房间；没有窗，没有钟，一个灰袍人告诉他们，这是"那些至高存在"的一次特别邀请："“You’re likely wondering why you’re here,” the man in the robe continues. He takes the seat opposite me at the low wooden table. The foul stench of long-dead rotting things fills my nose. My stomach turns. “You’ve been specially invited by the Powers That Be”—he pauses, gesturing vaguely to the ceiling—“to make a choice.”"（ch01） 条件也很简单——机构眼下缺人手，代价是永恒地替它收魂，换来一间舒适的房间和定时三餐："“You see, we find ourselves short-staffed at the moment.” The man grins at us, showing a mouth full of too-white, perfectly straight teeth. … It would have been a less terrifying smile if his teeth were jagged and rotten. … “Employment,” the man confirms. He gestures to the pages on the table. “As compensation, a comfortable room and regular meals will be provided for you for eternity. And you will be allowed to skip all that Hell nastiness.”"（ch01） 而四个人里有一个问出了那个最要紧的问题："那么作为交换，我们同意……究竟要做什么？"："“And in return, we agree to…do what, exactly?” Under the circumstances, it’s probably impertinent for me to ask. But if there was one thing I was good at while living, it was being impertinent. And using explosives, too, I suppose. Fat lot of good that did me in the end."（ch01）

**二、退休宴上，那双冰蓝色的眼睛。** 公司退休宴上，她为了躲开忘记带铭牌的上司，藏到一个陌生男人身后，却反被那双眼睛攫住："That pair of ice-blue eyes snaps to mine. They widen a little, as though the question startles him. “My name?” … His eyes go distant, as if he’s trying to answer a difficult question. “As for my name, it’s…Daniel?” His sentence turns up at the end, like he’s not sure about it."（ch02） 他连自己的名字都说不准，只在桌上留下一张银色字迹的卡片——姓名、年龄、日期，一行行在现实里时隐时现："The silver-ink lettering shimmers, drifting in and out of reality beneath the elegant ballroom lighting. I only make out the first three lines before the writing blurs, winks out, then vanishes altogether. … What I do manage to see turns my blood to ice. … Name: Lucy McKenzie … Age: 28 years, 1 month, 3 days … Date: September 3"（ch02） 卡片上的字会自行消失，徒手去读还会被烫掉指尖："It’s as plain as the disappearing ink on this notecard. My name, the date, and my exact freaking age were there. … He must have put some kind of defensive poison or acid or something on the card so anyone who tries to read it gets their fingertips singed off."（ch03）

**三、车轮底下的几十秒。** 他到一场公交车撞死人的现场报到，嫌围观者碍事，却在 Lucy McKenzie 那束亮得惊人的人生里没能移开视线："If it weren’t for this bus, she would have lived to a very old age. Or so my supervisor told me when they assigned me her file. … But I can’t think about that now. … I have work to do. … Only I’m finding it difficult to focus. … But Ms. McKenzie’s soul is so brilliant I could have found her anywhere. Not just a bright flash against the gloom, but more like a kaleidoscope with all its swirling colors."（ch04） 而在车轮底下，她被痛觉撕碎，一个有着亮蓝眼睛的陌生人在她意识里说话，把"构成她的东西"往外拽："But now there is a face, hovering just above mine. Almost within reach. The features are blurry, except for bright blue eyes. … Eyes that are both foreign and familiar. They pierce through the haze that has taken my mind. … You are…so bright. They sound stunned. The person’s mouth doesn’t move. Am I hearing the words inside my head?"（ch05） 她抵抗了一阵，最后因为那句惊叹松了手："I feel, rather than hear, the surprised gasp. … So bright, it says again with what might be genuine awe. … I stop fighting, focusing on the awe in that voice for the span of half a heartbeat. … That’s all the time they need. … They give one final, sharp tug, and now I float weightless above my body, outside the pain. … The vehicle that holds my body lurches forward as the rest of me is tugged out and away."（ch05）

**四、候车室、死亡档案与一份合同。** "Lucy McKenzie，二十八岁。十七分钟十四秒前，你被一辆城市巴士撞了。你死了。"："“Lucy McKenzie, age twenty-eight. You were hit by a city bus seventeen minutes and fourteen seconds ago. You are dead.” … He says it so matter-of-factly, never taking his eyes off the page, that it takes a moment for what he’s said to sink in."（ch06） 这不是天堂，是候车室："“You are not in Heaven. This is the Waiting Room. It’s where I—we—bring people after they die. We let them wake up and acclimate to their new reality here before we bring them out there.” … “On the other side of this door is the In-Between. As soon as you stop asking questions, I will take you there.”"（ch06） 她摸着不存在的脉搏哀求，只求能去参加妹妹六周后的婚礼："“Let me make a deal with you,” I beg. … “Please,” I beg. “I just need a little more time.” … “I need to be there for my sister’s wedding in six weeks. Our mom walked out on us. I’m her only family. … Now I’m crying again in huge, racking sobs that feel torn from my soul. “I’m not finished yet. With what I have to do.”"（ch06） 而他向上争取到的不是复活，是一纸八周延寿、到期照原样死去的合同，外加一份"派他去盯着她"的差事："At the end of this eight-week extension, Lucy McKenzie’s soul must return to us by the original method of death. … In exchange for the invaluable information Lucy McKenzie will provide, upon her death, her soul will be given an Upgrade and permitted to reside in Mid-Level Two for eternity."（ch07）

**五、停住的手机，和开始倒计时的清晨。** 在那间等不到天亮的白色房间里，她发现自己的手机永远定格在 8:44 p.m.："There’s no way to mark the passage of time here. There’s no window I can look out of and no clock. … My phone is frozen at 8:44 p.m. That must be when I died."（ch08） 同一个街口重新醒来之后，她终于把"八周"当成一件正在倒计时的事："In eight weeks I have to leave Wilf behind. He’s been the only constant in my life other than Jess, and now I’m leaving him. I’m the only home he’s ever had. … “Eight weeks won’t be enough time,” I whimper."（ch09） 而回到 Agency 的他，信箱里躺着一张盖着红印的观察表："There is a single folded sheet of parchment bearing the Executive’s bright red seal waiting for me in my mail slot when I return to the Agency. … Once inside my room, I close the door behind me and slump against it with the parchment clutched tight in my fist."（ch10） 把她的公寓和 Agency 的房间摆在一起一比，他才发现自己已经不想回到那种日子里去："The contrast between her home and the room I now stand in couldn’t be more drastic. The Agency—where I have dwelled for more than a century—is all blank walls and sterile, featureless rooms. Ms. McKenzie’s home—Ms. McKenzie herself—is life and color and sound. … And it all pales in comparison to what it was like to hold her."（ch10）

**六、复活后的第一个早晨。** 她给自己定下的首要目标，是"接下来八周，做 Jess 一个可靠的姐姐"："I need to get up, though. Being a reliable sister to Jess is my primary goal for the next eight weeks. Showing up late to the coffee date I suggested would mean I’m already off to a bad start."（ch11） 咖啡的奶泡上浮着一副骷髅和两根交叉骨："When I hold the mug up to my lips to take my first sip and see what’s inside, I nearly drop it to the floor in surprise. … Two crossed bones beneath a sightless skull. … “I’m so sorry! I knew the machine had been acting weird, but…How did that even happen?”"（ch11） 而在大学图书馆的自习室里，一个刚从她公寓离开的死神，正一边查"为什么会想碰不该碰的人"，一边填一份"用五句话描述 Lucy 受惊时的反应"的表单："In no more than five sentences, please describe how Lucy McKenzie reacts to surprises. … Due: Six hours after receipt. … Upon completion, please return to the Agency via interoffice mail. … Under normal circumstances, I could complete this inside a handful of minutes. Now, though, my mind is just butterflies and static."（ch12）

**七、周一的两难，和一杯越界的酒。** 周一早上，她为"还要不要继续做那份吸灵魂的工作"纠结了半小时："When my alarm goes off for work on Monday morning, I spend thirty minutes locked in indecision … Should I keep my soul-sucking job for eight more weeks? Or should I quit now, since I’ll be dead soon anyway and nothing matters? … “It’s a dilemma,” I tell Wilfred as I brush my teeth."（ch13） 最后她选了妹妹——辞职："“I’m quitting, El,” I say around the sudden lump in my throat. … “No shit?” … “Today is my last day.” … “That’s amazing!” she says, laughing. “Oh my god, Skellar is such a nightmare. I’m so proud of you for taking the plunge.”"（ch13） 辞职那天下午在校门口重逢，他替她躲过一辆自行车，然后问她要不要去喝一杯："“Want to get a drink?” … “Rooster’s is a bar near here where I sometimes go to unwind. And I just thought…you know.” … “It’s against protocol to fraternize with you.” … “All right, Ms. McKenzie. Let’s get a drink.”"（ch13）

**八、契约、地狱，与"我也害怕"。** 九月闷热的田纳西，路边那家破酒吧里，他终于说出自己为什么退不掉：为了不去地狱，他签下的就是永世做收魂人的契约："“It’s fine.” The way his eyes have shuttered, though, says otherwise. “No. I cannot quit. This isn’t a job the way you think of jobs. Becoming a Reaper for eternity was the deal I struck to avoid being sent to Hell.”"（ch14） 临别时她终于说出"我害怕"，他回她"我在这儿"，然后补上三个字——"我也害怕"："“I’m scared,” I finally admit on a whisper. … “Don’t be,” he says. His voice is low and steady at my ear. “I’m here.” … Tentatively, as if he knows he shouldn’t, he reaches up and tucks a lock of hair that has fallen into my eyes behind my ear. … Daniel’s expression is pure torment. “I’m scared, too.”"（ch14）

**九、一百三十五年前的旧账。** 高管会议上他还在走神，因为昨夜那个醉着独自开车走的女孩；可等他把她的差评推过桌面，地狱的首相就推门而入，用一声"Daniel Ivanov"把旧账翻开："“Nice to see you again, Daniel Ivanov.” … Lucifer might only be Hell’s Prime Minister and not the Devil himself, but it’s hard to remember that with the proprietary way he says my name. His two-foot-long horns and forked tail also don’t help. … “Sir,” I manage, inclining my head in deference."（ch15）

**十、"这怎么可能"。** 婚宴的伴手礼、闪粉、番茄酱与一只猫，中间只剩他指腹描过她下唇的那一下："这怎么可能……已经……太久了。"："“How is this possible,” he murmurs, sounding at least as dazed as I feel. “It’s…been so long.” … He reaches out and, with a featherlight touch, traces my full bottom lip with the pad of a finger."（ch16） 而当她追问自己为什么能碰到他，他给出的是一份一百多年的孤独："自从我成为收魂人以来，没有人碰过我。除了你。"："“You have done nothing wrong. Devil take me—but touching you like this…it’s all I dream about.” He leans forward, elbows resting on his knees. “No one has touched me since I became a Reaper. Not until you.”"（ch17） 紧接着他承认，没有她的手在身上，每一分钟都像在慢慢地死去："“But it doesn’t matter to me why you can touch me,” he says. “All I know is that you can. Your touch has been all I’ve thought about since that tedious reception, … Now, every moment spent without your hands on me feels like it did at the beginning. Like I’m dying slowly, again and again, but without the relief of death."（ch17）

**十一、第 417 条。** 他被 Agency 强行召回，罪证是自己两天前交上去的那份观察备忘；规章说死神不得对自己的灵魂产生依恋："“Consider this a warning, Friend. You will maintain professional boundaries with your assigned souls at all times.” … But holding Lucy, kissing her, awoke something in me that had been dormant so long I’d forgotten what longing was. … “I will,” I lie. … I already care too much about her. … I should be concerned, really, about how easy lying to the Agency has become."（ch18） 而他私下对她说的是另一套话："你是我见过的最恶劣的那种人"——怪物，然后是"所以我说你好的时候，你该信我"："“As a Reaper, I have met the worst kinds of people. … Monsters, Lucy.” … I was a monster once, too. So when I tell you that you are good, trust me. You are.” … It’s been very easy to forget that he was originally supposed to go to Hell. … “I’m not sure I could stay away if I tried.”"（ch19）

**十二、玩具店、脚踏酒吧车，和一次旧身份的坦白。** 她把他拉进玩具店挑毛绒玩具："“I’m getting Jess and Liam a matching set of stuffies as a gift but can’t decide what to get.” … She holds up the toy she’s holding in her left hand (a pink thing with white felt eyes) and then the one in her right (a green thing with blue felt eyes). “I thought you could help me choose.”"（ch20） 妹妹单身派对的脚踏酒吧车上，他是那个本不该出现的人："I do a double take when I see Daniel sitting at the bar in his usual suit and tie, nursing a cocktail, giving the singer his full attention. … “Let me guess,” I say. “You just happened to be in the neighborhood?” … “There was a classical music performance at the Ryman tonight,” he says without missing a beat. “Mendelssohn violin concertos. My favorites.”"（ch21） 回到 Agency，他警告三个同事一个字都别往外说——否则就去告发他们给 CEO 画像画胡子的事；然后他想：也许该告诉她我到底是谁："“You will say nothing of this to anyone,” I warn with as much dignity as I can muster. … “Or I will tell the Executives that you were the ones who drew mustaches on all the CEO’s portraits last year.” … And maybe—just maybe—tell her the truth about who I really am."（ch22） 深夜的卧室里，他把俄罗斯小镇、码头那票炸掉三个人的活儿和 Nicholas 一五一十交代出来；她的回答是："你告诉我的这些，不会改变什么。"："I shake my head. “What you told me doesn’t change anything.” … “So you’re no saint,” I quip. “I already guessed that much. You were doing the best you could with the shitty hand you were dealt. It also sounds like you’ve spent every day since you died regretting what you did. Including things that weren’t your fault.”"（ch23）

**十三、湖边小城，与"我不想死"。** 他带她回童年那片湖边，一间太贵的餐厅、一幅地上的粉笔画，最后是一个吻——吻到她改口说"带我回家"："“Devil take what I said last night,” he breathes. … “You are just so…vibrantly alive. Everything about you. I can’t fight it anymore. I don’t even want to try.” … “Take me home,” I breathe against his lips. … He chuckles, gives me one final, fierce kiss that steals my breath—and the world disappears."（ch24） 而在卧室里，她说出了那句改变一切的话："我不想死"；他答应陪她到最后，只是那句"之后"他不能给："“I don’t want to die,” I confess, completely changing the tone of our pillow talk with one short sentence. … “I know, sweetheart.” He kisses the top of my head. The apple of each cheek. … “I know. I’ll be with you the whole way, right until the end.” … He doesn’t say he’ll be with me after the end, of course. … Because he can’t."（ch25）

**十四、传唤、撤销，与她自己开出的条件。** 一纸传唤把他叫进 Executive Suite，等来的是 CEO 当面撤销 Lucy 的八周延寿："“You’ve grown attached to a human soul.” He shakes his head ruefully. … “Next Monday, we will meet with you and Lucifer to discuss your demotion. … My room is spare and depressing, but it’s the only home I’ve had for 135 years. … “You may go, Daniel,” he says, softly. … I flinch. … The last time he said my name was the day I died."（ch26） 当晚他只带来四个字："他们改主意了。"："“They changed their minds.” He murmurs the words, as if saying them quietly will dull the blow. … “About you. They’re rescinding their offer.” He swallows. “You have to return to them sooner than you’d hoped.”"（ch27） 于是她亲自去教那帮人怎么做人——她要替两个人一起定价："He crushes me to him. “Please let me send you home so I can do this for you.” … “I have to be the one to do this, Daniel.” … Daniel swallows audibly. “Here we go.” But he doesn’t move a muscle. … I put my hand on the bag carrying the photographs and my phone. “Here goes nothing,” I say."（ch29） 而在三个高管面前，她把那张 Arkansas 的自拍照摊开："你们对人类的了解少得可怜……少到不明白你们自己人中的一个为什么会爱上一个人。"："I turn it around so everyone in the room can see the selfie of the two of us on our date in Arkansas. Daniel has his arms wrapped around my waist. … “You know so little about humans,” I say, refusing to cry in front of these monsters, “that you don’t even understand why one of your own fell for one.”"（ch30）

**十五、一张床、一张桌子、一把椅子。** 她走进他住的地方，问"这是你的家？"，他答"这不是我的家"，把真话咽了回去："“This is your home?” She’s dismayed, and wants to hide it from me but can’t. I would know even without our bond. She’s brimming with it. … No, I want to tell her. You’re my home. … “It’s not my home,” I say instead. “Only a bed, a table, a chair. It’s just where I stay.”"（ch31） 而她开出的条件是"既往服役折算年限、放他走"；作为交换，她换来一份工作、一条期限不明的余生，和"我们有整夜"："“I said I’d only do this if they treat your service to date as a Reaper as time served and let you go.” … “They think you’ll get to try again.” … “At living,” I clarify. … I see my Death Card lying on my beside table as if it’s been waiting for me. … It crumbles into dust on contact. … And we have all night."（ch32）

**十六、绕回原点的那间空房。** 仪式办在当年改变他命运的那间毫无特征的空房里，几个同批成为收魂人的旧识站在旁边——连脸都没有："It feels like a full-circle moment, holding this ceremony in the same featureless room where my destiny was changed all those years ago. … In fact, these Reapers—who I am almost positive are the murderer, the politician, and the bedraggled woman whose former name I never managed to learn—have no faces at all."（ch33） CEO 合上书，抬起手说"我们开始吧"；他闭上眼，脑子里只剩一个名字："The CEO clears his throat. “Apologies for taking liberties with the guest list,” he says to me, not sounding apologetic at all. … At length, the CEO closes his book with a definitive thump. He must have found the incantation he was looking for. … “All right,” he says, raising his hands. “Let us begin.” … My long-dormant heart thunders. … I close my eyes and think only of Lucy."（ch33）

**十七、等待的日子，和一句"成了"。** 他被 Agency 带回去改造的那几天，她睡不安稳、联系不上、也交不起房租，只好接下每月一次的外出看房活；她替 Agency 做的那份兼职是拍照片加说明，对家人只说是某家小型机构的调研项目："The Agency, being what it is, has only the most rudimentary understanding of the concept of money. My compensation is fabulous, but it comes entirely in the form of a longer lifespan for me and—in theory, at least—freedom for Daniel. I can’t pay rent with that. What’s currently in my bank account won’t last much longer."（ch34） 直到她在咖啡店里帮妹妹写婚礼座位表，一个声音从背后传来——"成了"："I freeze. That’s Daniel’s voice. Or is my sanity going? Am I imagining voices that aren’t there? … Sure enough, when I turn around, there he is, wearing a T-shirt I’ve never seen before and a pair of slim-fitting blue jeans. … It worked. … The Agency made good on their promise. And it worked. … That’s as far as I get before his mouth crashes down on mine."（ch34） 而他问的不是"我是不是人了"，是"这会不会是个问题"；她答："因为我很清楚你是什么。你是我的。"："“I know I’m no longer a Reaper. My heart beats. My eyes are no longer the silver-blue they once were. And you and I no longer share a bond.” … “In fact, I don’t quite know exactly what I am.” He scans my face. “Will that be a problem?” … “Because I know exactly what you are. You’re mine.” … “Yours,” he agrees as he kisses me back. “Always.”"（ch34）

## 三个主题

**一、契约与条款。** 这本书里每一段关系都被写成了合同。她与机构的八周延寿是一条写清了交割日期的条款："At the end of this eight-week extension, Lucy McKenzie’s soul must return to us by the original method of death. … In exchange for the invaluable information Lucy McKenzie will provide, upon her death, her soul will be given an Upgrade and permitted to reside in Mid-Level Two for eternity."（ch07） 他与地狱的那一份更久——为了不去地狱，他签的是永世："“It’s fine.” The way his eyes have shuttered, though, says otherwise. “No. I cannot quit. This isn’t a job the way you think of jobs. Becoming a Reaper for eternity was the deal I struck to avoid being sent to Hell.”"（ch14） 而她最后把主动权拿回来，用的仍然是条款："只有他们把你至今为止作为收魂人的服役算作已服年限、并且放你走，我才做这份工作。"："“I said I’d only do this if they treat your service to date as a Reaper as time served and let you go.” … “They think you’ll get to try again.” … “At living,” I clarify. … I see my Death Card lying on my beside table as if it’s been waiting for me. … It crumbles into dust on contact. … And we have all night."（ch32） 三份契约叠在一起，正好是这本书的弧线：从被条款决定，到用条款谈判。

**二、名字。** 全书最重的一次改名，是把"Daniel"补成"Daniel Ivanov"："“Nice to see you again, Daniel Ivanov.” … Lucifer might only be Hell’s Prime Minister and not the Devil himself, but it’s hard to remember that with the proprietary way he says my name. His two-foot-long horns and forked tail also don’t help. … “Sir,” I manage, inclining my head in deference."（ch15） 那一刻他在一屋子高管面前被点名，像一件被登记的库存；而在这之前，他连自己的名字都说不准："That pair of ice-blue eyes snaps to mine. They widen a little, as though the question startles him. “My name?” … His eyes go distant, as if he’s trying to answer a difficult question. “As for my name, it’s…Daniel?” His sentence turns up at the end, like he’s not sure about it."（ch02） 与此同时，她想从这段关系里取回的也不只是一个承诺，而是一个能被承认的完整版本——"我想认识你的每一个版本"："Daniel is waiting for me on the couch when I get back from Jess’s house armed with a ton of old pictures. … “Images from a life well lived,” he murmurs. … I’m not so sure about that. “A life lived, anyway.” … “I know the current Lucy pretty well, I think.” … “I want to know every version of you.”"（ch28）

**三、八周。** 八周这个数字先是合同里的一个期限，接着变成她每天清晨的尺子："I need to get up, though. Being a reliable sister to Jess is my primary goal for the next eight weeks. Showing up late to the coffee date I suggested would mean I’m already off to a bad start."（ch11） 又变成一份清单——纸、猫、妹妹："In eight weeks I have to leave Wilf behind. He’s been the only constant in my life other than Jess, and now I’m leaving him. I’m the only home he’s ever had. … “Eight weeks won’t be enough time,” I whimper."（ch09） 最后它被换成了一笔谁也算不准的账："“I said I’d only do this if they treat your service to date as a Reaper as time served and let you go.” … “They think you’ll get to try again.” … “At living,” I clarify. … I see my Death Card lying on my beside table as if it’s been waiting for me. … It crumbles into dust on contact. … And we have all night."（ch32）

## 人物弧光

**Lucy McKenzie**——

- 起点："“Lucy McKenzie, age twenty-eight. You were hit by a city bus seventeen minutes and fourteen seconds ago. You are dead.” … He says it so matter-of-factly, never taking his eyes off the page, that it takes a moment for what he’s said to sink in."（ch06）
- 转向："“Let me make a deal with you,” I beg. … “Please,” I beg. “I just need a little more time.” … “I need to be there for my sister’s wedding in six weeks. Our mom walked out on us. I’m her only family. … Now I’m crying again in huge, racking sobs that feel torn from my soul. “I’m not finished yet. With what I have to do.”"（ch06）
- 最低点："“They changed their minds.” He murmurs the words, as if saying them quietly will dull the blow. … “About you. They’re rescinding their offer.” He swallows. “You have to return to them sooner than you’d hoped.”"（ch27）
- 落点："I turn it around so everyone in the room can see the selfie of the two of us on our date in Arkansas. Daniel has his arms wrapped around my waist. … “You know so little about humans,” I say, refusing to cry in front of these monsters, “that you don’t even understand why one of your own fell for one.”"（ch30）
- 她的弧线不是"从怕死到不怕死"，而是"从被人决定，到自己开价"——真正让她站住的不是勇气，是她学会了把"我要活下去"写成一份可以谈判的条款。

**Daniel Ivanov**——

- 起点："“And in return, we agree to…do what, exactly?” Under the circumstances, it’s probably impertinent for me to ask. But if there was one thing I was good at while living, it was being impertinent. And using explosives, too, I suppose. Fat lot of good that did me in the end."（ch01）
- 转向："“You have done nothing wrong. Devil take me—but touching you like this…it’s all I dream about.” He leans forward, elbows resting on his knees. “No one has touched me since I became a Reaper. Not until you.”"（ch17）
- 最低点："“Consider this a warning, Friend. You will maintain professional boundaries with your assigned souls at all times.” … But holding Lucy, kissing her, awoke something in me that had been dormant so long I’d forgotten what longing was. … “I will,” I lie. … I already care too much about her. … I should be concerned, really, about how easy lying to the Agency has become."（ch18）
- 落点："The CEO clears his throat. “Apologies for taking liberties with the guest list,” he says to me, not sounding apologetic at all. … At length, the CEO closes his book with a definitive thump. He must have found the incantation he was looking for. … “All right,” he says, raising his hands. “Let us begin.” … My long-dormant heart thunders. … I close my eyes and think only of Lucy."（ch33）
- 他的弧线是"从执行条款的人，变成愿意为一个条款去争的人"——135 年里他从没问过"那么作为交换，我们究竟要做什么"，直到有人替他问了这个问题。

**Jess**——

- 起点："“You’re nothing like Mom,” I assure her in the quiet voice we always use when we bring her up. … You’ve been more of a parent to me than our mother ever was.” … I can’t be there for my sister for very long after her wedding—but I’ll be the most supportive, most reassuring big sister in the history of the world in the weeks leading up to it."（ch11）
- 中段："“I’m quitting, El,” I say around the sudden lump in my throat. … “No shit?” … “Today is my last day.” … “That’s amazing!” she says, laughing. “Oh my god, Skellar is such a nightmare. I’m so proud of you for taking the plunge.”"（ch13）
- 落点："“Just because you haven’t had a job yet that values you for who you are doesn’t mean you don’t have skills,” Jess says. … “You have a huge heart. You’re fiercely loyal. If you make a mistake—” … “You also protect the people you care about. Look at this.” … In this picture I have a huge black garbage bag in my hands, stuffed full of trash."（ch28）
- 她是这本书里唯一一个从头到尾都在"记录"的人：她把姐姐的失败一笔笔记下，也第一次把姐姐的优点一条条念给她听；而最后她要做的，仍然是记录——把这个人的完整版本留下。

**机构与那位 CEO**——

- 起点："“You see, we find ourselves short-staffed at the moment.” The man grins at us, showing a mouth full of too-white, perfectly straight teeth. … It would have been a less terrifying smile if his teeth were jagged and rotten. … “Employment,” the man confirms. He gestures to the pages on the table. “As compensation, a comfortable room and regular meals will be provided for you for eternity. And you will be allowed to skip all that Hell nastiness.”"（ch01）
- 中段："“You’ve grown attached to a human soul.” He shakes his head ruefully. … “Next Monday, we will meet with you and Lucifer to discuss your demotion. … My room is spare and depressing, but it’s the only home I’ve had for 135 years. … “You may go, Daniel,” he says, softly. … I flinch. … The last time he said my name was the day I died."（ch26）
- 落点："“Apologies for the temperature,” the CEO says, noticing my discomfort. “We don’t feel the cold, you see—and the cost of utilities is outrageous these days.” … “Of course.” He sets my pathetic file to the side and fixes me with an uncanny, piercing stare. “We—that is, the other Agency Executives and I—would like to offer you a job.”"（ch32）
- 它从不咆哮，它只谈条款：缺人手就招人，附带有条件的人就撤销合同；直到最后，它第一次成了雇主——用一份工作换回一个真正的人类样本。
