# 《Isle of Teeth》情感节点

## 情感弧线总览

本书有两条交织的线：**Matiu 的「认罪线」**（从替帝国辩解 → 拆掉自己学到的每一条事实 → 走进法庭自首）与 **Calyx 的「夺回定义线」**（从被造出来的兵器 → 喊出「我只属于我自己」→ 被人承认不是怪物）。全书真正的转折不在任何一场打斗，而在 ch42 法庭上她跪在枪口下、仍然把造物主那套话说回去的那一刻。

---

## 节点一：被割开的那个孩子（ch01–ch02）

**叙事概括**：Matiu 在学校门口等父亲来接。他一辈子都听见死神在叫他，而那个声音是他母亲的："When Lady Death called to Matiu, he always heard his mother’s voice."（ch02） 然后一个归骸者破门而入，把他剖开——他低头才看见自己的内脏流出来。这是全书第一次正面展示归骸者，也是这两个人此后所有相遇的伤口原型。

**关键引语：**

> ① "When Lady Death called to Matiu, he always heard his mother’s voice."（ch02）

> ② "Matiu didn't even feel the gash in his stomach until he looked down—and saw his insides spilling out."（ch02）

## 节点二：爬进神庙，向死神讨一句「放我们走」（ch03）

**叙事概括**：Calyx 的妹妹 Sepal 开始发病。她循着气味爬进死神的神庙，按母亲教过的仪式——指尖血、月亮照在 Lady Death 之峰上、一支烟——向神祈祷。她没有别的东西可以献，只有自己的血。而在这之前，她骗过自己一次："For a moment she imagined she was simply visiting the Isle, that she had a life in the bustling city just beyond the forest, and that the Isle of Teeth was not a cage."（ch03）

**关键引语：**

> ① "For a moment she imagined she was simply visiting the Isle, that she had a life in the bustling city just beyond the forest, and that the Isle of Teeth was not a cage."（ch03）

> ② "She remembered the blank look in their brother Carpal's eyes the day that he rampaged across the Isle, as if the person she knew was somewhere far away and a stranger was wearing his face as a mask."（ch03）

## 节点三：她的第一个问题——为什么造了我们又丢掉我们（ch10–ch12）

**叙事概括**：
- Calyx 第一次走出岛。她戴上造物主送的眼镜，看见一千种绿色，听见从来不叫的鸟叫，然后在一堆被撕开喉咙的军校生尸体旁边站住。她大哥 Carpel 生前一遍遍说的是："Never forget you are human too," he would whisper. "You are not just the animal parts you were given."（ch10）
- 而在 Martinel 的街上，她第一次把那个问题问出了声："Why did the humans make us only to abandon us? Can they fix us? Calyx closed her eyes and quivered. Would they want to?"（ch12）

**关键引语：**

> ① "Never forget you are human too," he would whisper. "You are not just the animal parts you were given."（ch10）

> ② "Why did the humans make us only to abandon us? Can they fix us? Calyx closed her eyes and quivered. Would they want to?"（ch12）

## 节点四：地窖里的三天，和那两颗心脏（ch22–ch30）

**叙事概括**：
- Matiu 把 Calyx 带回 Agueda 家铺子底下的地窖。他给她端来冷掉的剩面汤、替她重缝手臂上的伤口，也第一次听她讲自己的身体：獠牙取自猴子、舌头取自蛇、耳朵换成了狗的。这段的高点是她带他去看自己留下的那块石头，以及她给那两颗心脏下的定义："'Only monsters don't have feelings, Matiu.' Calyx took both his hands in hers and squeezed. 'And I have two hearts, one for living and one for breaking.'"（ch30）
- 她已经知道自己走不远了，于是先安排了自己的死法："'If they catch me and you can't save me, put knives in my hearts, Matiu. There's no one else I can trust with this. I'd rather it be you. Please swear it. Don't let them experiment on me. I've lived through enough of it.'"（ch30）

**关键引语：**

> ① "'Only monsters don't have feelings, Matiu.' Calyx took both his hands in hers and squeezed. 'And I have two hearts, one for living and one for breaking.'"（ch30）

> ② "'If they catch me and you can't save me, put knives in my hearts, Matiu. There's no one else I can trust with this. I'd rather it be you. Please swear it. Don't let them experiment on me. I've lived through enough of it.'"（ch30）

> ③ "'You look younger when you smile. I don't think I've seen it before.' Calyx looked away, suddenly bashful, but Matiu's hand remained in hers."（ch30）

## 节点五：母亲回来了，而且她是一张脸（ch31–ch32）

**叙事概括**：
- Estrella 一哨吹倒 Calyx，然后转身用「母亲的那张脸」跟 Matiu 谈判。她开口的第一句是："The woman never took her eyes from Matiu. "You look so much like your father…""（ch31）
- 而 Calyx 醒来时，床已经凉了："When Calyx woke, the bed was cold and Matiu was gone. She rolled to her feet and ignored the familiar stab of hunger pangs. Calyx grabbed her knives and strode out the door Matiu had left unlocked."（ch32）

**关键引语：**

> ① "The woman never took her eyes from Matiu. "You look so much like your father…""（ch31）

> ② "When Calyx woke, the bed was cold and Matiu was gone. She rolled to her feet and ignored the familiar stab of hunger pangs. Calyx grabbed her knives and strode out the door Matiu had left unlocked."（ch32）

## 节点六：他拆掉自己学到的每一条事实（ch35–ch38）

**叙事概括**：
- Matiu 回到 Estrella 的山洞，只剩空荡荡的洞穴；回到 Agueda 家，只剩烧焦的废墟。他站在空城里，终于把那句话问到底："How had it taken him so long to realize that it was impossible to convince those who thought you were less than human of your humanity? / No matter what Matiu achieved, in the eyes of the Empire he would always be Songi."（ch35）
- 也想明白了怪物到底站在哪一边："Calyx was right. All the monsters were outside the Isle, dressed in fine suits and shiny shoes."（ch35）
- 全章从一个词开始："Trust. That was what Lettie had urged. Matiu did not know how much he could trust himself."（ch38）
- 而他被人拖走时是笑着的："He gasped for air, unable to contain himself. He stumbled behind the guard on a rope like a dog on a leash. To them he was just another Songi. No more, no less. He found he preferred it that way. / Trust. He walked toward his doom with clear eyes."（ch38）

**关键引语：**

> ① "Calyx was right. All the monsters were outside the Isle, dressed in fine suits and shiny shoes."（ch35）

> ② "How had it taken him so long to realize that it was impossible to convince those who thought you were less than human of your humanity? / No matter what Matiu achieved, in the eyes of the Empire he would always be Songi."（ch35）

> ③ "Trust. That was what Lettie had urged. Matiu did not know how much he could trust himself."（ch38）

## 节点七：Fort Desolation，和那件事真正的名字（ch40）

**叙事概括**：
- Songi 人被赶进 Fort Desolation。咳嗽在结冰的院子里回荡，最瘆人的是安静——除了耳语，一切都已经从 Songi 人身上被打了出去："Coughing echoed through the freezing courtyard, and despite the breeze, the entire place stank of unwashed people. The eeriest thing was the silence. All but whispers had been beaten out of the Songi."（ch40）
- 新闻传声筒在外面喊「重新安置」，而 Matiu 站在院子里看清了它真正的名字："This was not a relocation, but an extermination."（ch40）

**关键引语：**

> ① "Coughing echoed through the freezing courtyard, and despite the breeze, the entire place stank of unwashed people. The eeriest thing was the silence. All but whispers had been beaten out of the Songi."（ch40）

> ② "This was not a relocation, but an extermination."（ch40）

## 节点八：法庭——自首、镣铐落地、和那句「我不是怪物」（ch41–ch43）

**叙事概括**：
- Wailing Moon 前夜，Matiu 穿上 Delia 缝的红裙衬里与古 Orsai 锁甲，走进法庭认罪：""Captain Daniel Fagen is innocent of all charges you have against him. I've come to confess my crimes and turn myself in." / Fagen turned toward him. His expression told Matiu that he would murder him, but Matiu continued his charade. "I have been feeding information on troop movements to the Songi Revolutionary Army. I also murdered five soldiers and made it look like a wild beast was loose in the city.""（ch41）
- 他真正的筹码是自己的嗓子：""Your mother filled you up with songs. When you were a boy, you sang in your sleep and strange things would happen. Now I need your voice, boy. Without Estrella, you are the last true Chanter of Song.""（ch41）
- 而 Estrella 把 Calyx 像狗一样拖进来之后，Matiu 一转身，镣铐落地："And as Matiu turned, Calyx's shackles fell to the floor."（ch41）
- Calyx 跪在枪口下，把造物主那套定义当面摔回去："Matiu came up beside her, warm and whole. Her hearts both leapt, but she wasn't done with the Archduke yet." / ""Why make us so dangerous and then lock us away?" she asked her maker."（ch42）
- ""Because you must fight monsters with monsters. But you were a failure. What we needed were weapons, not children." / "They had made people, not monsters." / "People with guilt, with feelings, and the repulsion for killing." / "I am no monster.""（ch42）

**关键引语：**

> ① "And as Matiu turned, Calyx's shackles fell to the floor."（ch41）

> ② "Calyx slumped to her knees though the Archduke aimed a pistol at her head. Calyx was not invulnerable to bullets. At this distance she could not outrun them, but she was done bending for these humans." / ""You want a monster. Here, I've come for you!" she shouted."（ch42）

> ③ ""Because you must fight monsters with monsters. But you were a failure. What we needed were weapons, not children." / "They had made people, not monsters." / "People with guilt, with feelings, and the repulsion for killing." / "I am no monster.""（ch42）

## 节点九：宫殿塌下来之后（ch43）

**叙事概括**：
- 墙塌了，大公死了。Matiu 把 Calyx 从瓦砾里拖出来，塌下来的墙露出的是宫殿的真正样子："Thin afternoon sunlight bathed the space with a cool glow ribboned with the shadows of iron bars. Matiu gasped. The palace had not been built in the shape of a cage—it was a cage. A shiver crept up his spine."（ch43）
- Sepal 死在那里，Estrella 第一次听起来是真的：""Sepal is gone, child. I'm sorry." Estrella placed a hand on Calyx's shoulder. She sounded genuine for once. Calyx blinked back tears and nodded. "Her hearts finally gave out.""（ch43）
- 葬礼之后，Matiu 把修好的眼镜还给 Calyx，然后两个人一起走了：""Let's go." Matiu turned to Calyx, and they walked away together."（ch43）

**关键引语：**

## 节点十：山在唱，岛在沉（ch44–ch47）

**叙事概括**：
- 最后一夜，山上所有人一起唱——那是首不一样的歌：""Sing!" an old woman with a red head wrap and faded tattoos shouted behind her. "We need to sing!" / And all the Songi joined in. The old and the young, first tentative then louder. The music thrummed through their bones and stirred the air so that even Calyx felt like she might take flight. A dark shadow blotted out the moon, and the entire valley went dark, but the song went on, calling, welcoming, creating. It was a different song from Estrella's, one that filled them all up instead of using them up."（ch46）
- 山崩吞掉 Isle of Teeth，铁门开了，来的却不是帝国的人。Delia 把一包吃的塞给 Calyx，把她推向森林："There would be no going back. / "You need to go." Delia shoved a pack full of food and supplies at Calyx. "The Lyssians won't be kind if they find you. Estrella still wants to use you, and I don't think that's the future you deserve. You've done enough. Leo and Arnel will keep her distracted. Quickly, go on and take your family… live for us.""（ch46）
- 数月之后，Jaothan 回到岛上，在中央广场看见一块新涂上漆的石头："Littered across the city's central square were stones painted in brilliant colors. The brightest of all was a red and yellow stone. It glowed fresh with paint. Words were painted over the stone. / We lived. We are alive."（ch47）
- 而他最后想起的，是 Estrella 留给他的那句话："A shadow blotted out the sun and a screech of noise reduced him to his knees. Hasty prayers slipped off his tongue, along with Estrella's last warning: / Lady Death comes for us all in the end."（ch47）
