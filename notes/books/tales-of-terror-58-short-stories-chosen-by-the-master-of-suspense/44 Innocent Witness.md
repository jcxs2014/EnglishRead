# 44. Innocent Witness（无辜的证人）

## 概览

- **出处**：Tales of Terror: 58 Short Stories Chosen by the Master of Suspense，第 44 篇
- **作者**：Irving Schiffer
- **体裁**：侦探悬疑短篇，约 28,200 字符
- **一句话主题**：秘书 Julie 目睹了 Turner 先生谋杀妻子的全过程——她接听了 Turner 打给妻子的电话，听到忙音，意识到 Turner 是在确认妻子已死。她最终向侦探 Ruderman 坦白，但已无法改变 Turner 被处决的事实。

## 选择性精读

**①** "The detective was waiting for her as she emerged from the office building at five o'clock. Suddenly in the midst of the homegoers he was standing before her, very tall, a young man with a surprisingly gentle voice and considerate manner."

- 中文理解："她五点从办公楼出来时，侦探正在等她。突然，在回家的人群中，他站在她面前，非常高，一个声音出奇温柔、态度体贴的年轻人。"
- 句子结构：第一句时间状语从句 The detective was waiting for her as she emerged from the office building at five o'clock；第二句状语 Suddenly in the midst of the homegoers + 主语 he + 谓语 was standing + 地点状语 before her + 同位语 very tall + 同位语 a young man with a surprisingly gentle voice and considerate manner。
- 关键词：**homegoers**（回家的人群——平凡）；**surprisingly gentle voice**（出奇温柔的声音——反差）
- 表达方式：**侦探的温柔**。他"出奇温柔"——这是反差。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对侦探性格的描写**。他"温柔"——他不像警察。

- 句子结构：第一句主语 She + 谓语 was + 表语 twenty + 同位语 a dark-haired girl + 定语从句 who worked as a secretary in the financial district of New York；第二句主语 She + 谓语 was + 表语 one of many + 插入语 not much different at first glance from the girls + 定语从句 who sat at the desks around her + 同位语 pretty enough, not very sophisticated, a girl everyone liked, accustomed to anonymity。

- 表达方式：**Julie 的平凡**。她"习惯了默默无闻"——她平凡。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Julie 身份的描写**。她"平凡"——但她目睹了谋杀。

**③** "She was, above all things, not used to being singled out by detectives; and she looked about selfconsciously as the other girls passed, certain that some of them recognized Sergeant Ruderman from his visit to the office that morning."

- 中文理解："最重要的是，她不习惯被侦探单独挑出来；当其他女孩走过时，她不自在地四处张望，确信她们中的一些人认出了早上来办公室的鲁德曼中士。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 She + 谓语 was + 插入语 above all things + 表语 not used to being singled out by detectives；第二句连词 and + 主语 she + 谓语 looked about + 状语 selfconsciously + 时间状语从句 as the other girls passed + 宾语补足语 certain that some of them recognized Sergeant Ruderman from his visit to the office that morning。

- 表达方式：**Julie 的紧张**。她"不自在"——她紧张。

**④** "I wonder," he said, as if sensing her thoughts, "is there somewhere we can talk privately?"

- 中文理解："我想，"他说，仿佛感知到她的想法，"有没有什么地方我们可以私下谈谈？"
- 句子结构：直接引语 I wonder + 主语 he + 谓语 said + 方式状语从句 as if sensing her thoughts；直接引语 is there somewhere we can talk privately。

**⑥** "He asked me again about the Turners and I still didn't tell him." "Good!" Julie stared at her. "Good? Why do you say that?" "Because what's the point of making extra trouble for poor Mr. Turner?"

**⑦** "Because what's the point of making extra trouble for poor Mr. Turner?" She leaned forward confidentially. "Now, what about that detective? Did he ask you for a date?"

- 中文理解："因为给可怜的特纳先生制造额外的麻烦有什么意义？"她向前倾了倾身子，神秘地。"那么，那个侦探呢？他有没有约你出去？"
- 句子结构：直接引语 Because what's the point of making extra trouble for poor Mr. Turner；主语 She + 谓语 leaned forward + 状语 confidentially；直接引语 Now, what about that detective；直接引语 Did he ask you for a date。
