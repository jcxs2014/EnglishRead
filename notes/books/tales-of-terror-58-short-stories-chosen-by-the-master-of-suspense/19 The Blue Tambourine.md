# 19. The Blue Tambourine（蓝手鼓）

## 概览

- **出处**：Tales of Terror: 58 Short Stories Chosen by the Master of Suspense，第 19 篇
- **作者**：Donald Olson
- **体裁**：哥特恐怖短篇，约 22,100 字符
- **一句话主题**：瘾君子诗人 Willie 被疯狂摄影师 Rainfyre 诱骗——他声称在拍摄"天堂"，需要纯真者的大脑图像。Willie 发现"模特"是个盲女时拒绝合作，带着三张照片逃跑，最终发现手里攥着的只有一把雪。

## 选择性精读

**①** "In the shabbiest precinct of a city so long tainted by economic blight that he is even denied the companionship of his own image in the grime-crusted windows of its vacant buildings, Willie de Garde stoops to snatch a starving cat from a dark doorway, pops it into the sack he carries for this purpose, and then hurries on, for the night air is sharp, winter close at hand."

- 中文理解：在这个被经济枯萎长期污染的城市最破败的区域，他甚至在空置建筑积满污垢的窗户里都看不到自己的倒影，威利·德·加德从黑暗的门口抓起一只饥饿的猫，塞进他为此目的携带的袋子里，然后匆匆离开，因为冬夜寒风刺骨，冬天近在咫尺。
- 句子结构：介词短语 In the shabbiest precinct of a city + 定语从句 so long tainted by economic blight + 结果状语从句 that he is even denied the companionship of his own image in the grime-crusted windows of its vacant buildings + 主语 Willie de Garde + 谓语 stoops to snatch + 宾语 a starving cat + 地点状语 from a dark doorway + 谓语 pops + 宾语 it + 地点状语 into the sack + 定语从句 he carries for this purpose + and + 谓语 hurries on + 原因状语 for the night air is sharp, winter close at hand。
- 关键词：**economic blight**（经济枯萎）；**grime-crusted windows**（积满污垢的窗户——荒凉）
- 表达方式：**开篇就是世界**。整个句子都是环境描写——城市的破败、Willie 的贫穷、冬天的寒冷。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的恐怖基调**。这是一个"被诅咒"的城市——一切都不正常。

**②** "The young man cradles his furry prize tightly against his chest, as if to revive it by contact with his own strong young heart, and his steps quicken as he approaches an old brick building whose façade still bears faint traces of a gaudy elegance."

- 中文理解：年轻人把他的毛绒奖品紧紧抱在胸前，仿佛要通过自己强壮年轻的心跳来使它复活，当他走近一座旧砖房时，他的脚步加快了，它的正面仍然保留着一丝俗丽优雅的痕迹。
- 句子结构：第一句主语 The young man + 谓语 cradles + 宾语 his furry prize + 方式状语 tightly against his chest + 方式状语从句 as if to revive it by contact with his own strong young heart + and + 主语 his steps + 谓语 quicken + 时间状语从句 as he approaches an old brick building + 定语从句 whose façade still bears faint traces of a gaudy elegance。
- 关键词：**cradles**（轻轻抱住——温柔）；**furry prize**（毛绒奖品——猫）；**gaudy elegance**（俗丽优雅——衰败的美）
- 表达方式：**Willie 的温柔**。他"轻轻抱住"猫——他有爱心，不是坏人。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Willie 性格的定论**。他不是"坏人"——他是一个"瘾君子诗人"，但他有爱心。

**③** "Overhead, a similarly despoiled jet-black dragon with only one red bulb of an eye holds in its teeth a huge black and crimson Chinese lantern."

- 中文理解：头顶上方，一条同样被掠夺的漆黑巨龙，只有一只红色灯泡的眼睛，嘴里叼着一盏巨大的黑红相间中国灯笼。
- 句子结构：地点状语 Overhead + 同位语 a similarly despoiled jet-black dragon + 介词短语 with only one red bulb of an eye + 谓语 holds + 宾语 in its teeth a huge black and crimson Chinese lantern。
- 关键词：**despoiled**（被掠夺的、衰败的）；**jet-black dragon**（漆黑巨龙——怪物）
- 表达方式：**剧院的衰败**。"被掠夺的巨龙"——这是怪物的形象。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对剧院的定论**。它是"被掠夺的"——像怪物一样。

**④** "Those who still patronize the Oriental Garden seek something other than entertainment: a place to sleep, a place to hide, a place to hope for one of those exceedingly rare occasions when some black-sheep cousin of Eros might lead to a nearby seat a figure whose needs, communicated by signals as universally understood by the initiated as the Morse code is by the fraternity of wireless..."

- 中文理解：那些仍然光顾东方花园的人寻求的不是娱乐：一个睡觉的地方，一个藏身之处，一个希望能遇到那些极其罕见的机会——当爱神的某个黑羊表亲可能会把一个身影领到附近座位上，他们的需求通过信号传达，被入会者普遍理解，就像摩尔斯电码被无线电台的兄弟会理解一样……
- 句子结构：主语 Those + 定语从句 who still patronize the Oriental Garden + 谓语 seek + 宾语 something other than entertainment + 同位语 a place to sleep, a place to hide, a place to hope for one of those exceedingly rare occasions + 定语从句 when some black-sheep cousin of Eros might lead to a nearby seat a figure + 定语从句 whose needs, communicated by signals as universally understood by the initiated as the Morse code is by the fraternity of wireless。
- 关键词：**black-sheep cousin of Eros**（爱神的黑羊表亲——同性恋）；**Morse code**（摩尔斯电码——秘密信号）
- 表达方式：**剧院的秘密**。人们去剧院不是为了"看戏"——是为了"秘密约会"。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对"秘密"的定论**。这是一个充满"秘密"的世界。

**⑤** "Taking from his pocket three small pieces of stiff white paper, he lays them before the poet, who examines them gingerly, trembling."

- 中文理解：从口袋里拿出三张小白纸，把它们放在诗人面前，诗人小心翼翼地、颤抖着检查它们。
- 句子结构：分词短语 Taking from his pocket three small pieces of stiff white paper + 主语 he + 谓语 lays + 宾语 them + 地点状语 before the poet + 定语从句 who examines them gingerly, trembling。
- 关键词：**three small pieces of stiff white paper**（三张小白纸——死亡证据）；**trembling**（颤抖——恐惧）
- 表达方式：**照片的出现**。他拿出"三张小白纸"——这是死亡证据。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键物品**。三张照片——三只死猫的"最后图像"。

**⑥** "These, Mr. de Garde, are photographs of the last optical images in the brain of a dying guinea pig, one of the expendable creatures you so kindly procured for us."

- 中文理解："这些，德·加德先生，是垂死豚鼠大脑中最后光学图像的照片，是你为我们采购的那些可消耗的生物之一。"
- 句子结构：主语 These + 呼语 Mr. de Garde + 谓语 are + 表语 photographs of the last optical images in the brain of a dying guinea pig + 同位语 one of the expendable creatures + 定语从句 you so kindly procured for us。
- 关键词：**last optical images**（最后光学图像——死亡瞬间）；**expendable creatures**（可消耗的生物——冷漠）
- 表达方式：**Rainfyre 的疯狂**。他说"可消耗的生物"——他把生命当作"材料"。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Rainfyre 疯狂的定论**。他是"疯狂的科学家"——他把生命当作"材料"。

**⑦** "After the death of my beloved child, and with nothing but her deathbed cry to inspire me, I devoted myself to the exploration of that mystical borderland, and finally—yes, with this very camera!—devised a means of recording in black and white a picture of that afterworld whose radiant beauty illuminated my dying angel's face."

- 中文理解："在我心爱的孩子去世后，只有她的临终哭声激励着我，我投身于探索那个神秘的边境地带，最终——是的，用这架相机！——发明了一种黑白记录那个来世的方法，它的光辉之美照亮了我垂死天使的面庞。"
- 句子结构：介词短语 After the death of my beloved child + 方式状语 and with nothing but her deathbed cry to inspire me + 主语 I + 谓语 devoted myself to + 宾语 the exploration of that mystical borderland + and + 插入语 finally—yes, with this very camera!—谓语 devised + 宾语 a means of recording in black and white a picture of that afterworld + 定语从句 whose radiant beauty illuminated my dying angel's face。
- 关键词：**mystical borderland**（神秘边境——生死之间）；**radiant beauty**（光辉之美——天堂）
- 表达方式：**Rainfyre 的悲剧**。他"失去孩子"——这是他的疯狂动机。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Rainfyre 悲剧的揭露**。他不是纯粹的"坏人"——他是一个"悲伤的父亲"。

**⑧** "If anyone deserves heaven, that child does."

- 中文理解："如果有人配得上天堂，那就是这个孩子。"
- 句子结构：条件状语从句 If anyone deserves heaven + 主语 that child + 谓语 does。
- 关键词：**deserves heaven**（配得上天堂——纯真）
- 表达方式：**Rainfyre 的讽刺**。他说"配得上天堂"——但他在杀害她。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇最讽刺的时刻**。Rainfyre 说"她配得上天堂"——但他在杀害她。

**⑨** "As you can see, I'm somewhat handicapped by this." He waves the flipper. "And I'm also afflicted with a coronary weakness that forbids undue exertion. Nor is my wife strong in anything but spirit. We must therefore rely on you, my young friend, to dispose of the—er—remains."

- 中文理解："如你所见，我因为这个有点残疾。"他挥动鳍状手。"我还患有冠状动脉虚弱，禁止过度劳累。我的妻子除了精神以外也不强壮。因此我们必须依靠你，年轻的朋友，来处理——嗯——遗体。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 As you can see, I'm somewhat handicapped by this；主语 He + 谓语 waves + 宾语 the flipper；直接引语 And I'm also afflicted with a coronary weakness + 定语从句 that forbids undue exertion；直接引语 Nor is my wife strong in anything but spirit；直接引语 We must therefore rely on you, my young friend, to dispose of the—er—remains。
- 关键词：**flipper**（鳍状手——残疾）；**dispose of the remains**（处理遗体——谋杀）
- 表达方式：**Rainfyre 的虚伪**。他说"处理遗体"——他在谈论谋杀。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇最恐怖的时刻**。Rainfyre 用"处理遗体"来掩盖谋杀——这是魔鬼的语言。

**⑩** "Near the end of the street a gust of wind tears the small white photographs out of his hand. He claws at the air to retrieve them, and thinks he has, but when he reaches the river and opens his fist he holds nothing but a handful of snowflakes."

- 中文理解：在街道尽头，一阵风把小白照片从他手里扯走。他抓向空气去捡回它们，以为自己已经捡了，但当他到达河边张开拳头时，他手里攥着的只有一把雪花。
- 句子结构：地点状语 Near the end of the street + 主语 a gust of wind + 谓语 tears + 宾语 the small white photographs + 地点状语 out of his hand；主语 He + 谓语 claws at + 宾语 the air + 目的状语 to retrieve them + and + 谓语 thinks + 宾语从句 he has + but + 时间状语 when he reaches the river and opens his fist + 主语 he + 谓语 holds + 宾语 nothing but a handful of snowflakes。
- 关键词：**snowflakes**（雪花——虚无）
- 表达方式：**终极虚无**。Willie 攥着"一把雪花"——照片消失了。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的终极恐怖**。照片是"证据"——但它们消失了。Willie 无法证明任何事。
