# 49. Second Spring（第二个春天）

## 概览

- **出处**：Tales of Terror: 58 Short Stories Chosen by the Master of Suspense，第 49 篇
- **作者**：Theodore Mathieson
- **体裁**：犯罪心理短篇，约 33,900 字符
- **一句话主题**：记者 Caxton 18 岁时帮助 Maud Gullick 逃离精神病院——她杀死了父母。17 年后他已婚并育有子女，Maud 的丈夫 Buck 上门拜访。Caxton 意识到 Maud 的暴力倾向会再次爆发，但他选择沉默。最终 Maud 杀死了丈夫和孩子。

## 选择性精读

**①** "Maud Gullick was only eighteen on the spring morning in 1932 when she bludgeoned her mother and father to death over their breakfast."

- 中文理解："莫德·古利克在1932年春天的一个早晨，当她还只有十八岁时，在早餐时用棍棒打死了她的母亲和父亲。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 Maud Gullick + 谓语 was + 表语 only eighteen + 时间状语 on the spring morning in 1932 + 时间状语从句 when she bludgeoned her mother and father to death over their breakfast。
- 关键词：**bludgeoned**（用棍棒打——暴力）；**over their breakfast**（在早餐时——日常）
- 表达方式：**开篇就是谋杀**。"用棍棒打死"——这是暴力。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的钩子**。Maud "用棍棒打死"父母——这是暴力。

**②** "According to the police report, Maud had calmly gone down to her father's basement workshop—the Gullicks lived at the top of a set of three flats in the San Francisco Richmond District—had picked up one of her father's hammers, and carried it back up, concealed under her bathrobe."

- 中文理解："根据警方报告，莫德平静地走到她父亲的地下室工作室——古利克一家住在旧金山里士满区三层公寓的顶层——拿起她父亲的一把锤子，把它带回来，藏在浴袍下面。"
- 句子结构：状语 According to the police report + 主语 Maud + 谓语 had calmly gone down + 地点状语 to her father's basement workshop + 插入语 the Gullicks lived at the top of a set of three flats in the San Francisco Richmond District + 谓语 had picked up + 宾语 one of her father's hammers + and + 谓语 carried + 宾语 it + 方向状语 back up + 分词短语 concealed under her bathrobe。
- 关键词：**calmly**（平静——冷静）；**concealed under her bathrobe**（藏在浴袍下面——预谋）
- 表达方式：**Maud 的预谋**。她"平静地"——这是预谋。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Maud 预谋的描写**。她"平静地"——这是预谋。

**③** "Entering the kitchen, she called the attention of her parents to a roofing operation going on across the street. She hit her father first at the base of the skull, then her mother in the left temple."

- 中文理解："走进厨房，她让父母注意街对面正在进行的屋顶施工。她先击中父亲的后脑底部，然后击中母亲的左太阳穴。"
- 句子结构：第一句分词短语 Entering the kitchen + 主语 she + 谓语 called + 宾语 the attention of her parents + 目的状语 to a roofing operation going on across the street；第二句主语 She + 谓语 hit + 宾语 her father + 时间状语 first + 地点状语 at the base of the skull + then + 谓语 her mother + 地点状语 in the left temple。
- 关键词：**roofing operation**（屋顶施工——分散注意力）；**base of the skull**（后脑底部——致命）
- 表达方式：**Maud 的谋杀**。她"分散注意力"——这是谋杀。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Maud 谋杀的描写**。她"分散注意力"——这是谋杀。

**④** "One blow was sufficient for each, for Maud was a big girl, six feet one, and weighed almost two hundred pounds."

- 中文理解："每一下都足够了，因为莫德是个大女孩，六英尺一英寸高，体重近两百磅。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 One blow + 谓语 was + 表语 sufficient + 介词短语 for each + 原因状语 for Maud was a big girl + 同位语 six feet one + and + 谓语 weighed + 宾语 almost two hundred pounds。

**⑤** "Afterward she called the police, but by the time they arrived, she had lost control of herself, and they could make little of her story."

- 中文理解："事后她报了警，但当他们到达时，她已经失去了控制，他们对她的故事几乎无法理解。"
- 句子结构：第一句时间状语 Afterward + 主语 she + 谓语 called + 宾语 the police + but + 时间状语 by the time they arrived + 主语 she + 谓语 had lost control of herself + and + 主语 they + 谓语 could make little of + 宾语 her story。

**⑥** "Later she refused to tell them anything. Neighbors had given the police a story about a girl who had been acting strangely for weeks."

- 中文理解："后来她拒绝告诉他们任何事。邻居们给警察讲了一个关于一个女孩几周来行为怪异的故事。"
- 句子结构：第一句时间状语 Later + 主语 she + 谓语 refused to tell + 宾语 them + 宾语 anything；第二句主语 Neighbors + 谓语 had given + 宾语 the police + 宾语 a story + 介词短语 about a girl + 定语从句 who had been acting strangely for weeks。

**⑦** "The moment she saw me, because her face paled and she put her hand out against the door frame to support herself."

- 中文理解："她看到我的那一刻，因为她的脸色变得苍白，她把手伸向门框来支撑自己。"

**⑧** "Hello, Mr. Caxton," she said distantly. "Hello, Maud," I said. "I'm surprised to see you married and with a family and all. How long have you been married?" "Seventeen years."

- 中文理解："你好，"她遥远地说。"你好，莫德，"我说。"我惊讶地看到你结婚了，有了家庭。你结婚多久了？""十七年了。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 Hello, Mr. Caxton + 主语 she + 谓语 said + 状语 distantly；直接引语 Hello, Maud + 主语 I + 谓语 said；直接引语 I'm surprised to see you married and with a family and all；直接引语 How long have you been married；直接引语 Seventeen years。

**⑨** "All of a sudden a feeling came over me that I had no right coming here. The past was past. If Maud could go away quietly and get married and raise a family and live her own life for seventeen years, what right had I—"

- 中文理解："突然一种感觉涌上心头，我没有权利来这里。过去的就过去了。如果莫德能安静地离开，结婚，养育家庭，过自己的十七年生活，我有什么权利——"
- 句子结构：第一句时间状语 All of a sudden + 主语 a feeling + 谓语 came over + 宾语 me + 同位语从句 that I had no right coming here；第二句主语 The past + 谓语 was + 表语 past；第三句条件状语从句 If Maud could go away quietly and get married and raise a family and live her own life for seventeen years + 特殊疑问句 what right had I。

**⑩** "They broke my door down." "Did they?" I said and laughed uncomprehendingly. "Well, have your husband buy you a new one!" Buck laughed and the children laughed this time, and I left.

- 中文理解："他们把我的门砸坏了。""是吗？"我笑着，不理解。"好吧，让你丈夫给你买一个新的！"巴克笑了，孩子们这次也笑了，我离开了。
