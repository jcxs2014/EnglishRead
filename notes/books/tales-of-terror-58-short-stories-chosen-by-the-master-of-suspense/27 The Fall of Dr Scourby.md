# 27. The Fall of Dr. Scourby（斯库尔比医生的坠落）

## 概览

- **出处**：Tales of Terror: 58 Short Stories Chosen by the Master of Suspense，第 27 篇
- **作者**：Patricia Matthews
- **体裁**：校园悬疑短篇，约 24,500 字符
- **一句话主题**：大学女生 Jimmie Breen 因好友 Cindy 被 Dr. Scourby 逼死而怀恨在心。Scourby 在走廊辱骂她时，她用柔术将他摔下楼梯致死。探长 Cassidy 通过"气合"声音识破凶手，Jimmie 最终认罪。

## 选择性精读

**①** "Ms. Gladys Grumly, stout thighs pistoning powerfully, left hand sliding along the banister, purposefully pounded her way up the cement stairs of the Administration Building Tower."

- 中文理解：格拉迪斯·格拉姆利女士，粗壮的大腿有力地蹬着，左手沿着扶手滑动，有目的地沿着行政大楼塔楼的混凝土楼梯向上走去。
- 句子结构：主语 Ms. Gladys Grumly + 同位语 stout thighs pistoning powerfully + 同位语 left hand sliding along the banister + 谓语 purposefully pounded + 宾语 her way + 方向状语 up the cement stairs of the Administration Building Tower。
- 关键词：**pistoning powerfully**（有力地蹬着——强壮）；**pounded her way**（重重地走——愤怒）
- 表达方式：**Grumly 的愤怒**。她"有力地蹬着"——她在生气。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的基调建立**。Grumly 在"生气"——这是她的性格。

**②** "Her eyes fixed straight ahead, she climbed close to the left side of the stairs. She was more than a little afraid of heights, and if she walked to the right, the terrifying vortex formed by the spiral staircase seemed to suck her eyes downward, until her mind crashed against the cold, hard square of concrete at the bottom."

- 中文理解：她的眼睛直视前方，她靠近楼梯的左边爬上去。她非常恐高，如果她走到右边，螺旋楼梯形成的恐怖漩涡似乎把她的眼睛向下吸，直到她的头脑撞向底部冰冷、坚硬的混凝土方块。
- 句子结构：第一句独立主格 Her eyes fixed straight ahead + 主语 she + 谓语 climbed + 地点状语 close to the left side of the stairs；第二句主语 She + 谓语 was + 表语 more than a little afraid of heights + and + 条件状语从句 if she walked to the right + 主语 the terrifying vortex + 分词短语 formed by the spiral staircase + 谓语 seemed to suck + 宾语 her eyes + 方向状语 downward + 结果状语 until her mind crashed against the cold, hard square of concrete at the bottom。
- 关键词：**vortex**（漩涡——恐怖）；**suck her eyes downward**（把眼睛向下吸——恐高）
- 表达方式：**Grumly 的恐高**。她"恐高"——这是她的弱点。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Grumly 性格的补充**。她"恐高"——她走左边。

**③** "Before her nearsighted gaze could register what she was seeing, something plummeted past her line of vision. It took a moment for her mind to identify the 'something' as a human body."

- 中文理解：在她近视的目光能够识别出她看到的东西之前，有什么东西从她的视线中坠落。她的大脑花了一会儿才确定那个"东西"是一具人体。
- 句子结构：时间状语从句 Before her nearsighted gaze could register + 宾语从句 what she was seeing + 主语 something + 谓语 plummeted + 地点状语 past her line of vision；第二句形式主语 It + 谓语 took + 宾语 a moment + 介词短语 for her mind + 不定式 to identify the 'something' as a human body。
- 关键词：**plummeted**（坠落——死亡）；**human body**（人体——尸体）
- 表达方式：**Grumly 的目击**。她看到"人体坠落"——这是谋杀。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键时刻**。Grumly 目击了"坠落"——这是 Scourby 的死。

**④** "He asked me to call about his travel check. Accounting said that it wasn't ready. He gave me a bad time about that, as if it were my fault."

- 中文理解："他让我打电话问旅行支票的事。会计说还没准备好。他为此对我大吵大闹，好像是我的错一样。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 He asked me to call about his travel check；直接引语 Accounting said that it wasn't ready；直接引语 He gave me a bad time about that, as if it were my fault。
- 关键词：**travel check**（旅行支票——琐事）；**gave me a bad time**（大吵大闹——欺凌）
- 表达方式：**Jimmie 的回忆**。Scourby"大吵大闹"——这是欺凌。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Scourby 性格的揭露**。他"大吵大闹"——他是欺凌者。

**⑤** "He started swearing at me, and suddenly I just couldn't handle it. I ran for the stairs, but he followed me. I ran down the steps to the first landing and turned. He was coming down the steps toward me. All I could think of was how much I hated him."

- 中文理解："他开始骂我，突然我无法忍受了。我跑向楼梯，但他跟着我。我跑下楼梯到第一个平台，转过身来。他正沿着楼梯向我走来。我满脑子都是我对他的恨意。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 He started swearing at me + and + 直接引语 suddenly I just couldn't handle it；直接引语 I ran for the stairs + but + 直接引语 he followed me；直接引语 I ran down the steps to the first landing and turned；直接引语 He was coming down the steps toward me；直接引语 All I could think of was how much I hated him。
- 关键词：**couldn't handle it**（无法忍受——爆发）；**how much I hated him**（多么恨他——动机）
- 表达方式：**Jimmie 的爆发**。她说"无法忍受"——她爆发了。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键时刻**。Jimmie"无法忍受"——她杀人了。

**⑥** "In one fluid motion, she rose from the chair and twisted her upper body as she lifted her right shoulder. Cassidy had a vivid mental picture of Scourby pitching over her shoulder into the stairwell."

- 中文理解："她流畅地从椅子上站起来，当她抬起右肩时，她的上半身扭转。卡西迪脑海中浮现出一幅生动的画面：斯库尔比从她的肩膀上翻入楼梯井。"
- 句子结构：第一句方式状语 In one fluid motion + 主语 she + 谓语 rose + 地点状语 from the chair + and + 谓语 twisted + 宾语 her upper body + 时间状语从句 as she lifted her right shoulder；第二句主语 Cassidy + 谓语 had + 宾语 a vivid mental picture + 介词短语 of Scourby pitching over her shoulder into the stairwell。
- 关键词：**fluid motion**（流畅的动作——柔术）；**pitching over her shoulder**（从肩膀上翻过——摔投）
- 表达方式：**Jimmie 的柔术**。她"流畅地"站起来——她用柔术杀了 Scourby。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键时刻**。Jimmie 用柔术杀了 Scourby——这是谋杀。

**⑦** "I didn't mean to do it. I mean, I didn't plan it. After Cindy, I promised myself that I would get even with him some way. But I didn't plan it. It just happened."

- 中文理解："我不是故意的。我是说，我没有计划过。辛迪之后，我向自己承诺我会以某种方式报复他。但我没有计划过。事情就这样发生了。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 I didn't mean to do it；直接引语 I mean, I didn't plan it；直接引语 After Cindy, I promised myself that I would get even with him some way；直接引语 But I didn't plan it；直接引语 It just happened。
- 关键词：**didn't plan it**（没有计划——冲动）；**it just happened**（就这样发生了——意外）
- 表达方式：**Jimmie 的辩护**。她说"没有计划"——这是冲动杀人。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Jimmie 心理的揭露**。她"没有计划"——这是冲动。

**⑧** "We have a witness who heard a strange sound, a sound that could have been a kiai. I checked Dr. Martin's, Melissa's, Ben's and your records. Yours were the only ones that showed any experience in the martial arts—two years of judo classes."

- 中文理解："我们有一个证人听到了一种奇怪的声音，一种可能是气合的声音。我查了马丁医生、梅丽莎、本和你的记录。你的记录是唯一显示有任何武术经验的——两年柔道课程。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 We + 谓语 have + 宾语 a witness + 定语从句 who heard a strange sound + 同位语 a sound + 定语从句 that could have been a kiai；第二句主语 I + 谓语 checked + 宾语 Dr. Martin's, Melissa's, Ben's and your records；第三句主语 Yours + 谓语 were + 表语 the only ones + 定语从句 that showed any experience in the martial arts—two years of judo classes。
- 关键词：**kiai**（气合——柔道发声）；**judo classes**（柔道课程——证据）
- 表达方式：**Cassidy 的推理**。他说"气合"——这是柔道的声音。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键揭露**。Cassidy 通过"气合"找到了凶手——这是柔道的声音。

**⑨** "It was hard to hate her for it, Cassidy decided. Sitting there in the chair in his office, she looked more like a war orphan than a murderess."

- 中文理解："卡西迪决定，很难为此恨她。她坐在他办公室的椅子上，看起来更像一个战争孤儿而不是一个杀人犯。
- 句子结构：第一句形式主语 It + 谓语 was + 表语 hard to hate her for it + 插入语 Cassidy decided；第二句分词短语 Sitting there in the chair in his office + 主语 she + 谓语 looked + 表语 more like a war orphan than a murderess。
- 关键词：**war orphan**（战争孤儿——无辜）；**murderess**（杀人犯——凶手）
- 表达方式：**Cassidy 的同情**。他说"更像战争孤儿"——他同情她。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇最人性的时刻**。Cassidy 同情 Jimmie——她是受害者。

**⑩** "Cindy and I were...We were very close. He just the same as killed her, you know."

- 中文理解："辛迪和我很……我们非常亲密。他就像杀了她一样，你知道的。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 Cindy and I were；直接引语 We were very close；直接引语 He just the same as killed her, you know。
- 关键词：**very close**（非常亲密——友情）；**just the same as killed her**（就像杀了她——间接谋杀）
- 表达方式：**Jimmie 的悲伤**。她说"非常亲密"——她失去了朋友。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇最悲伤的时刻**。Jimmie 失去了朋友——Scourby 间接杀了 Cindy。
