# 46. Pocket Evidence（口袋里的证据）

## 概览

- **出处**：Tales of Terror: 58 Short Stories Chosen by the Master of Suspense，第 46 篇
- **作者**：未标注
- **体裁**：侦探悬疑短篇，约 47,700 字符
- **一句话主题**：律师 Colson 卷入贿赂法官 Bolt 案，被 Oster 勒索。Colson 杀死 Oster，但侦探 Jordan 通过口袋里的硝酸盐测试和弹道证据揭露了 Colson。Colson 在 IRS 办公室当场被捕。

## 选择性精读

**①** "There is a cynical little caveat which says: If you can't stand the time, don't commit the crime. U.S. District Judge Edward Marcus Bolt failed to heed this injunction."

- 中文理解："有一句愤世嫉俗的小格言：如果你受不了时间，就不要犯罪。美国地区法官爱德华·马库斯·博尔特没有听从这个告诫。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 There + 谓语 is + 表语 a cynical little caveat + 定语从句 which says: If you can't stand the time, don't commit the crime；第二句主语 U.S. District Judge Edward Marcus Bolt + 谓语 failed to heed + 宾语 this injunction。
- 关键词：**cynical little caveat**（愤世嫉俗的小格言——讽刺）；**stand the time**（忍受时间——坐牢）
- 表达方式：**开篇就是格言**。"愤世嫉俗"——这是讽刺。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的钩子**。法官"没有听从"——他犯罪了。

**②** "He had committed a crime and when he was found out, the prospect of serving time in a federal penitentiary unhinged him completely."

- 中文理解："他犯了罪，当他被发现时，在联邦监狱服刑的前景让他完全精神错乱。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 He + 谓语 had committed + 宾语 a crime；第二句时间状语从句 and when he was found out + 主语 the prospect of serving time in a federal penitentiary + 谓语 unhinged + 宾语 him + 状语 completely。
- 关键词：**federal penitentiary**（联邦监狱——重刑）；**unhinged him completely**（让他完全精神错乱——崩溃）
- 表达方式：**法官的崩溃**。他"完全精神错乱"——他崩溃了。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对法官心理的描写**。他"崩溃"——他自杀了。

**③** "Tossed off the bench, disbarred, disgraced, disavowed by his colleagues, ego mutilated, deprived of his sumptuous young bride, all this was more than he could stomach—so the judge put the muzzle of a gun against his temple and squeezed one off."

- 中文理解："被免去法官职务，被取消律师资格，被羞辱，被同事否认，自尊心被剥夺，被剥夺了年轻貌美的妻子，这一切超出了他的承受能力——所以法官把枪口对准太阳穴，扣动了扳机。"
- 句子结构：第一句分词短语 Tossed off the bench, disbarred, disgraced, disavowed by his colleagues, ego mutilated, deprived of his sumptuous young bride + 主语 all this + 谓语 was + 表语 more than he could stomach；第二句结果状语从句 so the judge put the muzzle of a gun against his temple and squeezed one off。
- 关键词：**disbarred**（取消律师资格——剥夺）；**sumptuous young bride**（年轻貌美的妻子——奢侈）
- 表达方式：**法官的自杀**。他"扣动扳机"——他自杀了。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对法官自杀的描写**。他"自杀"——他无法承受。

**④** "It ended the judge's problems, but created some new ones for his widow, Laura Bolt. Tall, blonde, with innocent blue eyes and teeth perfectly capped for her career as a fashion model, she suspended her work when she married the judge but resumed it after his death."

- 中文理解："这结束了法官的问题，但为他的遗孀劳拉·博尔特创造了一些新的问题。高个子，金发，有着天真的蓝眼睛和完美烤瓷牙，为了时装模特的职业，她在嫁给法官时暂停了工作，但在他死后恢复了工作。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 It + 谓语 ended + 宾语 the judge's problems + but + 谓语 created + 宾语 some new ones + 介词短语 for his widow, Laura Bolt；第二句主语 Tall, blonde, with innocent blue eyes and teeth perfectly capped for her career as a fashion model + 主语 she + 谓语 suspended + 宾语 her work + 时间状语 when she married the judge + but + 谓语 resumed + 宾语 it + 时间状语 after his death。
- 关键词：**innocent blue eyes**（天真的蓝眼睛——无辜）；**fashion model**（时装模特——美丽）
- 表达方式：**Laura 的美丽**。她"时装模特"——她美丽。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Laura 外表的描写**。她"美丽"——但她有秘密。

**⑤** "The man wants his money back," she told me. "What money?" "The money he claimed the judge had borrowed from him."

- 中文理解："那个人想要回他的钱，"她告诉我。"什么钱？""他声称法官借了他的钱。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 The man wants his money back + 主语 she + 谓语 told + 宾语 me；直接引语 What money；直接引语 The money + 定语从句 he claimed the judge had borrowed from him。
- 关键词：**wants his money back**（想要回钱——勒索）；**borrowed**（借——贿赂）
- 表达方式：**Oster 的勒索**。他说"想要回钱"——他在勒索。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键揭露**。Oster "勒索"——这是关键。

**⑥** "Now Madden is dead and when the opposition takes over you'll probably get axed. It's too late to start a new practice. So you were desperate."

- 中文理解："现在马登死了，当反对派接管时，你可能会被解雇。开始新的执业已经太晚了。所以你绝望了。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 Now Madden is dead and when the opposition takes over you'll probably get axed；直接引语 It's too late to start a new practice；直接引语 So you were desperate。
- 关键词：**get axed**（被解雇——失业）；**desperate**（绝望——走投无路）
- 表达方式：**Colson 的绝望**。他说"绝望"——他走投无路。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Colson 心理的描写**。他"绝望"——他走投无路。

**⑦** "Everyone knows you're a big spender and couldn't stomach a change in style. So you were itching to get your hands on Madden's loot in that numbered account."

- 中文理解："大家都知道你是个大手大脚的人，无法忍受风格的改变。所以你急于想拿到马登在那个编号账户里的赃款。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 Everyone knows you're a big spender and couldn't stomach a change in style；直接引语 So you were itching to get your hands on Madden's loot in that numbered account。
- 关键词：**big spender**（大手大脚——奢侈）；**itching to**（急于——渴望）
- 表达方式：**Colson 的贪婪**。他说"急于"——他贪婪。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Colson 贪婪的描写**。他"贪婪"——他想要钱。

**⑧** "You knew because Madden told you—an essential step in passing the money on to his daughter. That's the drill, a fixed procedure in transferring secret accounts."

- 中文理解："你知道是因为马登告诉了你——这是把钱传给他的女儿的关键步骤。这是程序，转移秘密账户的固定程序。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 You knew because Madden told you—an essential step in passing the money on to his daughter；直接引语 That's the drill, a fixed procedure in transferring secret accounts。
- 关键词：**essential step**（关键步骤——必要）；**fixed procedure**（固定程序——规定）
- 表达方式：**Jordan 的揭露**。他说"关键步骤"——这是揭露。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键揭露**。Jordan 揭露了"固定程序"——这是关键。

**⑨** "The police will perform a nitrate test to determine if any gunpowder particles were blown back into the skin of your palm. And if the test is positive, how will you explain it? Target practice in your office?"

- 中文理解："警方将进行硝酸盐测试，以确定是否有任何火药颗粒被吹回你手掌的皮肤。如果测试是阳性，你将如何解释？在办公室打靶？"
- 句子结构：直接引语 The police will perform a nitrate test to determine if any gunpowder particles were blown back into the skin of your palm；直接引语 And if the test is positive, how will you explain it；直接引语 Target practice in your office。
- 关键词：**nitrate test**（硝酸盐测试——科学）；**gunpowder particles**（火药颗粒——证据）
- 表达方式：**Jordan 的推理**。他说"硝酸盐测试"——这是推理。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键推理**。Jordan 推理出"硝酸盐测试"——这是关键。

**⑩** "He lifted his hand and stared at it. 'And that isn't all,' I said. 'I don't think you had the time or the foresight to drop your weapon off the Staten Island ferry. They know how to look. They'll find it and make a ballistics check.'"

- 中文理解："他抬起手，盯着它看。'这还不是全部，'我说。'我认为你没有时间或远见把你的武器扔下史泰登岛渡轮。他们知道怎么找。他们会找到它并进行弹道检查。'"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 He + 谓语 lifted + 宾语 his hand + and + 谓语 stared at + 宾语 it；直接引语 And that isn't all + 主语 I + 谓语 said；直接引语 I don't think you had the time or the foresight to drop your weapon off the Staten Island ferry；直接引语 They know how to look；直接引语 They'll find it and make a ballistics check。
- 关键词：**foresight**（远见——计划）；**ballistics check**（弹道检查——科学）
- 表达方式：**Jordan 的终极推理**。他说"弹道检查"——这是终极推理。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的终极揭露**。Jordan 推理出"弹道检查"——这是终极真相。
