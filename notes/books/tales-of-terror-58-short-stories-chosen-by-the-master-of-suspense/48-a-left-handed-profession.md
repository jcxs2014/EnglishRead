# 48. A Left-Handed Profession（左撇子行当）

## 概览

- **出处**：Tales of Terror: 58 Short Stories Chosen by the Master of Suspense，第 48 篇
- **作者**：Al Nussbaum
- **体裁**：黑色幽默短篇，约 14,700 字符
- **一句话主题**：大骗子 Thompson 向"我"购买六张连号百元美钞，想当然地认为它们是假钞——因为连号。实际上它们是真钞，只是被擦除了最后一位数字。"我"用花言巧语让他相信自己赚了便宜，最终以五万美元"卖出"了十万美元。

## 选择性精读

**①** "He was a big red-faced man with a nose that was too large and eyes that were too small, and I never heard a grown man whine so much."

- 中文理解："他是一个大红脸的男人，鼻子太大，眼睛太小，我从未听过一个成年男人抱怨这么多。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 He + 谓语 was + 表语 a big red-faced man + 介词短语 with a nose that was too large and eyes that were too small + and + 主语 I + 谓语 never heard + 宾语 a grown man + 补语 whine so much。
- 关键词：**big red-faced man**（大红脸的男人——粗俗）；**whine so much**（抱怨这么多——烦人）
- 表达方式：**Thompson 的粗俗**。他"抱怨这么多"——他烦人。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Thompson 外表的描写**。他"粗俗"——他是目标。

**②** "He sat in the bar, surrounded by flunkies, and didn't shut up for a moment. To hear him tell it, and no one in the lounge of the Buena Vista Casino heard much else that afternoon, he hadn't made a nickel's profit in years."

- 中文理解："他坐在酒吧里，被马屁精们围着，一刻也不停嘴。听他自己说，那天下午布恩维斯塔赌场的休息厅里没有人听到别的，他好几年没有赚到一分钱的利润。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 He + 谓语 sat + 地点状语 in the bar + 分词短语 surrounded by flunkies + and + 谓语 didn't shut up + 时间状语 for a moment；第二句目的状语 To hear him tell it + 插入语 and no one in the lounge of the Buena Vista Casino heard much else that afternoon + 主语 he + 谓语 hadn't made + 宾语 a nickel's profit + 时间状语 in years。
- 关键词：**flunkies**（马屁精——奉承）；**hadn't made a nickel's profit**（没有赚到一分钱——贫穷）
- 表达方式：**Thompson 的"贫穷"。他说"没有赚到一分钱"——他在哭穷。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Thompson 虚伪的描写**。他"哭穷"——但他有钱。

**③** "Taxes had left him with nothing. He might have convinced the Internal Revenue Service, but he didn't convince me."

- 中文理解："税收让他一无取有。他可能说服了国内税务局，但他说服不了我。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 Taxes + 谓语 had left + 宾语 him + 宾补 with nothing；第二句主语 He + 谓语 might have convinced + 宾语 the Internal Revenue Service + but + 主语 he + 谓语 didn't convince + 宾语 me。
- 关键词：**left him with nothing**（让他一无取有——哭穷）；**didn't convince me**（说服不了我——不信）
- 表达方式：**"我"的不信**。他说"一无取有"——"我"不信。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对"我"洞察力的描写**。"我"不信——"我"聪明。

**④** "His English leather shoes, hand-tailored suit and wafer-thin wristwatch all said he was a liar. So did the large diamond he wore on the little finger of his right hand—the hand he gestured with—and the thick roll of currency he carried."

- 中文理解："他的英国皮鞋、手工定制的超薄腕表都在说他是骗子。他右手小指上戴着的大钻石——他做手势的手——以及他随身携带的厚厚一卷现金也这样说。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 His English leather shoes, hand-tailored suit and wafer-thin wristwatch + 谓语 all said + 宾语从句 he was a liar；第二句倒装句 So did + 主语 the large diamond + 定语从句 he wore on the little finger of his right hand + 同位语 the hand he gestured with + and + 主语 the thick roll of currency + 定语从句 he carried。
- 关键词：**hand-tailored suit**（手工定制——奢侈）；**wafer-thin wristwatch**（超薄腕表——昂贵）
- 表达方式：**Thompson 的奢侈**。他"手工定制"——他奢侈。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Thompson 虚伪的描写**。他"奢侈"——他哭穷是假的。

**⑤** "I watched Benny Krotz nervously make his way across the casino floor, past the crap tables, blackjack dealers and roulette wheels."

- 中文理解："我看着本尼·克罗茨紧张地穿过赌场地板，经过掷骰子桌、二十一点发牌员和轮盘赌。"
- 句子结构：主语 I + 谓语 watched + 宾语 Benny Krotz + 补语 nervously make his way across the casino floor + 地点状语 past the crap tables, blackjack dealers and roulette wheels。
- 关键词：**nervously**（紧张——心虚）；**roulette wheels**（轮盘赌——赌博）
- 表达方式：**Benny 的紧张**。他"紧张"——他心虚。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Benny 心理的描写**。他"紧张"——他参与了骗局。

**⑥** "You said yourself, you've never seen anything like it. I've been in business for five years and not one bill has ever been questioned, let alone detected. It's not every day you get a chance to double your money."

- 中文理解："你自己说过，你从未见过这样的东西。我做了五年生意，没有一张钞票被质疑过，更不用说被检测到。你并不是每天都有机会让你的钱翻倍。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 You said yourself, you've never seen anything like it；直接引语 I've been in business for five years and not one bill has ever been questioned, let alone detected；直接引语 It's not every day you get a chance to double your money。

- 表达方式：**"我"的推销**。他说"翻倍"——他在推销。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对"我"推销术的描写**。他"推销"——他在骗人。

**⑦** "He was selling himself." I sat back and sipped my coffee. He didn't keep me waiting long. "Tell ya what, I'll take twenty-five thousand worth."

- 中文理解："他在说服自己。"我靠后坐着，喝着咖啡。他没有让我等太久。"告诉你，我要两万五千美元的。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 He was selling himself；主语 I + 谓语 sat back + and + 谓语 sipped + 宾语 my coffee；主语 He + 谓语 didn't keep + 宾语 me + 补语 waiting long；直接引语 Tell ya what, I'll take twenty-five thousand worth。
- 关键词：**selling himself**（说服自己——相信）；**twenty-five thousand worth**（两万五千美元——交易）
- 表达方式：**Thompson 的上钩**。他说"说服自己"——他上钩了。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键时刻**。Thompson "上钩"——他相信了。

**⑧** "You've seen the last samples you ever will. From now on I sell nothing smaller than hundred-thousand-dollar lots."

- 中文理解："你看到的最后样品是你以后再也见不到的。从现在起，我只卖十万美元以上的批量。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 You've seen the last samples you ever will；直接引语 From now on I sell nothing smaller than hundred-thousand-dollar lots。
- 关键词：**last samples**（最后样品——稀缺）；**hundred-thousand-dollar lots**（十万美元批量——大额）
- 表达方式：**"我"的饥饿营销**。他说"最后样品"——他在制造稀缺。

**⑨** "The hundred thousand is what you pay. In exchange, I give you two hundred thousand in crisp, new tens, twenties and fifties. Each bill with a different serial number."

- 中文理解："十万美元是你支付的。作为交换，我给你二十万美元崭新的十美元、二十美元和五十美元。每张钞票都有不同的序列号。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 The hundred thousand is what you pay；直接引语 In exchange, I give you two hundred thousand in crisp, new tens, twenties and fifties；直接引语 Each bill with a different serial number。

- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键揭露**。"不同序列号"——这是真钞。

**⑩** "It does if you take half a dozen consecutively numbered bills and erase the last digit." Margie's mouth opened in surprise. "You can do that? You can erase the numbers?" "Easier than you'd think, and without leaving a trace, either."

- 中文理解："如果你拿六张连号的钞票，擦除最后一位数字，它就会。"玛吉惊讶地张大了嘴。"你能做到吗？你能擦除数字？""比你想象的容易，而且不留痕迹。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 It does if you take half a dozen consecutively numbered bills and erase the last digit；主语 Margie's mouth + 谓语 opened + 状语 in surprise；直接引语 You can do that；直接引语 You can erase the numbers；直接引语 Easier than you'd think, and without leaving a trace, either。
