# 28. The Loose End（松散的线头）

## 概览

- **出处**：Tales of Terror: 58 Short Stories Chosen by the Master of Suspense，第 28 篇
- **作者**：Stephen Wasylyk
- **体裁**：犯罪悬疑短篇，约 19,200 字符
- **一句话主题**：独臂前侦探 Mark Stedd 策划珠宝盗窃案，利用小偷 Nipsy 和 Slow Harry 实施犯罪。警察 Barnes 早已识破他的计划——"松散的线头"就是 Mark 本人。最终珠宝被缴获，Mark 被捕。

## 选择性精读

**①** "The tall, baggy-suited, black-haired kid carrying the lightweight briefcase was making too many trips in the automatic elevators to be up to anything legitimate, especially since he was Nipsy Turko, a small-time thief with a long record of losing."

- 中文理解：那个穿着宽松西装、黑头发的瘦高男孩提着轻便公文包在自动电梯里来回走动次数太多了，不可能是做什么合法的事，尤其是因为他是尼普西·特科，一个长期失败的小偷。
- 句子结构：主语 The tall, baggy-suited, black-haired kid + 分词短语 carrying the lightweight briefcase + 谓语 was making + 宾语 too many trips + 地点状语 in the automatic elevators + 程度状语 to be up to anything legitimate + 原因状语 especially since he was Nipsy Turko + 同位语 a small-time thief with a long record of losing。
- 关键词：**too many trips**（来回走动次数太多——可疑）；**long record of losing**（长期失败——笨拙）
- 表达方式：**Nipsy 的可疑**。他"来回走动次数太多"——这是可疑的。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的钩子**。Nipsy 在"来回走动"——他在策划犯罪。

**②** "The only person in a position to notice or pay much attention was me, Mark Stedd, a one-armed ex-detective operating a newsstand in the lobby of the building where the elevators were located, and to tell the truth I don't know why I bothered."

- 中文理解：唯一有位置注意到或关注的人是我，马克·斯特德，一个独臂前侦探，在大楼大厅经营一个报摊，电梯就在里面，说实话我不知道我为什么费心。
- 句子结构：主语 The only person + 介词短语 in a position to notice or pay much attention + 谓语 was + 宾语 me + 同位语 Mark Stedd + 同位语 a one-armed ex-detective + 分词短语 operating a newsstand in the lobby of the building + 定语从句 where the elevators were located + and + 插入语 to tell the truth + 主语 I + 谓语 don't know + 宾语从句 why I bothered。
- 关键词：**one-armed ex-detective**（独臂前侦探——残疾）；**bother**（费心——关注）
- 表达方式：**Mark 的介绍**。他是"独臂前侦探"——他有经验。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Mark 身份的定论**。他是"前侦探"——他有经验。

**③** "The only reason I was inhabiting that cramped hole behind the newsstand was as a personal favor to Manny, an old friend who at the moment was living it up in Florida."

- 中文理解：我之所以住在报摊后面那个狭窄的洞里，只是为了帮老朋友曼尼一个忙，他此刻正在佛罗里达享受生活。
- 句子结构：主语 The only reason + 定语从句 I was inhabiting that cramped hole behind the newsstand + 谓语 was + 表语 as a personal favor to Manny + 同位语 an old friend + 定语从句 who at the moment was living it up in Florida。
- 关键词：**cramped hole**（狭窄的洞——贫穷）；**living it up**（享受生活——奢侈）
- 表达方式：**Mark 的贫穷**。他住在"狭窄的洞里"——他很穷。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Mark 处境的描写**。他"穷"——这是他的动机。

**④** "Manny's request had been heartily seconded as good rehabilitation by the doctors who had removed what remained of my left arm after the psycho with the shotgun had ripped it to shreds three months before."

- 中文理解：曼尼的请求得到了医生们的大力赞同，认为这是良好的康复，他们在我被精神病人用猎枪撕碎三个月后，取出了我左臂的残余部分。
- 句子结构：主语 Manny's request + 谓语 had been heartily seconded + 方式状语 as good rehabilitation + 介词短语 by the doctors + 定语从句 who had removed what remained of my left arm + 时间状语 after the psycho with the shotgun had ripped it to shreds three months before。
- 关键词：**ripped it to shreds**（撕成碎片——残忍）；**rehabilitation**（康复——恢复）
- 表达方式：**Mark 的悲剧**。他的手臂"被撕成碎片"——这是他的过去。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Mark 悲剧的揭露**。他失去了手臂——这是他的动机。

**⑤** "Two hundred thousand dollars; little enough payment for my left arm, and quite adequate payment for the three weeks it took me to plan the operation, talk Manny into taking his vacation, and browbeat Nipsy and Slow Harry into pulling the job for a small fee."

- 中文理解：二十万美元；对我左臂的补偿太少，但对我花三周时间策划这次行动、说服曼尼去度假、威逼尼普西和斯洛·哈利的报酬来说相当合适。
- 句子结构：第一句同位语 Two hundred thousand dollars；第二句主语 little enough payment for my left arm + and + 主语 quite adequate payment for the three weeks + 定语从句 it took me to plan the operation, talk Manny into taking his vacation, and browbeat Nipsy and Slow Harry into pulling the job for a small fee。
- 关键词：**browbeat**（威逼——威胁）；**pulling the job**（实施犯罪——盗窃）
- 表达方式：**Mark 的"正义"。他说"对我左臂的补偿太少"——他认为这是"正义"。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Mark 心理的揭露**。他认为这是"补偿"——他在自我合理化。

**⑥** "I owed the department this little job for passing me up for promotion twice and for sending me into that house with a rookie partner who froze instead of firing when the psycho swung the shotgun my way."

- 中文理解：我欠这个部门这笔小账，因为他们两次拒绝提拔我，还派我和一个新手搭档去那个房子，当精神病人朝我挥舞猎枪时，他吓得僵住了而不是开火。
- 句子结构：主语 I + 谓语 owed + 宾语 the department + 宾语 this little job + 原因状语 for passing me up for promotion twice + and + 原因状语 for sending me into that house with a rookie partner + 定语从句 who froze instead of firing + 时间状语从句 when the psycho swung the shotgun my way。
- 关键词：**passed me up for promotion**（拒绝提拔——不公）；**rookie partner**（新手搭档——无能）
- 表达方式：**Mark 的仇恨**。他说"两次拒绝提拔"——他恨部门。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Mark 动机的揭露**。他恨部门——这是他的动机。

**⑦** "The odds, Barnes said. 'I figured the odds of you being here in the lobby, of seeing the kid Nipsy, of seeing the briefcase switch, of knowing where to look for the briefcase. The odds were tremendous, Mark.'"

- 中文理解："概率，"巴恩斯说。"我计算了你在大厅这里的概率，看到尼普西那小子的概率，看到公文包调包的概率，知道去哪里找公文包的概率。概率太大了，马克。"
- 句子结构：主语 The odds + 插入语 Barnes said；直接引语 I figured the odds of you being here in the lobby, of seeing the kid Nipsy, of seeing the briefcase switch, of knowing where to look for the briefcase；直接引语 The odds were tremendous, Mark。
- 关键词：**odds**（概率——不可能）；**tremendous**（太大——不可能）
- 表达方式：**Barnes 的推理**。他说"概率太大了"——这是不可能的巧合。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键揭露**。Barnes 说"概率太大"——这是 Mark 的阴谋。

**⑧** "You always were a hard-luck cop, a good man to have around but no big brain, yet you were always one step ahead of me today and that just didn't figure. The percentages say I should have been one step ahead of you."

- 中文理解："你一直是个倒霉的好警察，一个值得身边的好人但没有大脑，但你今天总是比我领先一步，这根本说不通。概率说我应该比你领先一步。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 You always were a hard-luck cop, a good man to have around but no big brain + yet + 直接引语 you were always one step ahead of me today + and + 直接引语 that just didn't figure；直接引语 The percentages say I should have been one step ahead of you。
- 关键词：**hard-luck cop**（倒霉的好警察）；**no big brain**（没有大脑——笨拙）
- 表达方式：**Barnes 的分析**。他说"没有大脑"——但 Mark 比他聪明。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇最讽刺的时刻**。Barnes 说 Mark "没有大脑"——但 Mark 比他聪明。

**⑨** "As far as I was concerned, until the whole thing was wrapped up, you were a loose end and I never liked loose ends. Watching you was just something I had to do."

- 中文理解："就我而言，直到整个事情结束，你都是一个松散的线头，我从来不喜欢松散的线头。监视你只是我必须做的事情。"
- 句子结构：介词短语 As far as I was concerned + 时间状语 until the whole thing was wrapped up + 主语 you + 谓语 were + 表语 a loose end + and + 主语 I + 谓语 never liked + 宾语 loose ends；主语 Watching you + 谓语 was + 表语 just something + 定语从句 I had to do。
- 关键词：**loose end**（松散的线头——标题）；**never liked**（从来不喜欢——执着）
- 表达方式：**Barnes 的"松散的线头"。他说"松散的线头"——这是标题。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的终极揭露**。Barnes 说"松散的线头"——这是标题。

**⑩** "I started to laugh. Some detective. And they passed me over twice for promotion."

- 中文理解："我开始笑。什么侦探。他们两次拒绝提拔我。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 I + 谓语 started to laugh；第二句直接引语 Some detective；第三句直接引语 And they passed me over twice for promotion。
- 关键词：**Some detective**（什么侦探——讽刺）；**passed me over twice**（两次拒绝提拔——不公）
- 表达方式：**Mark 的讽刺**。他说"什么侦探"——他在讽刺 Barnes。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇最讽刺的时刻**。Mark 说"什么侦探"——他被"没有大脑"的侦探抓住了。
