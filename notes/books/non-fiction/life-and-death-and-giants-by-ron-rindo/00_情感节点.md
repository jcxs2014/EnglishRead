# Life, and Death, and Giants — 情感节点

> 全书 10 个情感节点。引语逐字取自 text/ 章节提取件。

## 节点一：巨人诞生（Prologue）

Rachel 难产两天，Jasper 把她拉到 Thomas 兽医的门前。兽医以接牛羊的手法，用助产套索把孩子拉出——18 磅，27 英寸。婴儿的第一声啼哭像"幼狼的嗥叫"，母亲的最后一口气紧随其后。

> "The boy weighed eighteen pounds. He was twenty-seven inches long."
> "Eighteen pounds! Thomas tapped the scale and squinted at the dial to be certain that was true. It was."
> "But on that bright September afternoon, Gabriel Fisher was born minutes before his mother drew her final breath."

## 节点二：绝罚之夜（ch05）

Rachel 拒绝供出生父，教区投票 streng meidung。Hannah 帮女儿收拾行李，看着 Charlotte 寡妇的皮卡驶过河去。此后的十年，只剩窗灯、雪夜摇篮曲，和月河边那声最平常的问候。

> "'Streng meidung,' he said, softly."
> "I sang that lullaby again and again, the song visible in the white clouds of my warm breath, until I could no longer feel my fingers, until my toes went numb, until, at last, the lone lighted window in Mrs. Chesterfield's house went dark."
> "'Good evening, Mother,' she said."

## 节点三：母爱的偷渡（ch08）

Gabriel 住进祖屋后，Hannah 偷偷赴了一场 T-ball 球赛；又借山羊 Magdalene 之病，第一次与孙子说话、被他拥抱。她给这个新身份命名——只用两个词。

> "He put his long arms around my shoulders, pulled me gently to him."
> "I would miss her dearly, but I thought only of Gabriel, the smell of his body, the song of his voice, the feel of his long arms around me. His grandmother."

## 节点四：Jasper 的绳索（ch10 末）

动物园归来之夜，Gabriel 发现父亲失踪。Thomas 拿起手电走进雨里的谷仓——光束扫过横梁。他做的第一件事是把孩子的脸按进自己怀里。

> "Thomas turned, and as the flashlight beam spread its yellow light into the darkness, it cut across Jasper's body hanging from a barn beam, a thick, greasy rope tight around his neck."
> "'Don't look, son,' Thomas said. 'Put your head down. Keep your eyes closed. I've got you. I've got you.'"

## 节点五：收养之夜（ch11）

Thomas 送来噩耗，也送来Gabriel。Hannah 把他领进 Rachel 的旧房间，烛火如"瓶中的草原大火"，讲起萤火虫，用德语摇篮曲哄他入睡。窗外是创世日般的日出。

> "'You'll have a room of your own here,' I said. 'Upstairs. It was once your mother's room.'"
> "'When your mother was your age,' I said, 'she used to go outside in the summer and catch fireflies in a mason jar...those fireflies would light up this whole room.'"

## 节点六：橡木对松木（ch41）

Josiah 烘干了橡木，却无法动手做棺。木匠 Abram 以"十英尺需要橡木的强度"接下了这份工。葬地定在 Cemetery of Peace——Rachel 与 Jasper 的矮碑之间，春天落满忍冬白瓣。

> "He'd kiln-dried two hundred board feet of quartersawn oak and planed it smooth, but each time he tried to begin construction, he could not will himself to do so."
> "'Ten foot's a long span,' he said, finally. 'We'll need the extra strength oak will give us.'"
> "At least, Josiah said, somberly, the three of them will be together."

## 节点七：截肢与神迹之辩（ch20 / ch45 对读）

Camp Randall 的完美秋日，千磅撞进右膝。数日后，发布会宣布截肢。数月后，万鸟之夜——Hannah 与 Thomas 各自保有自己的解释。

> "Gabriel Fisher's right leg had been amputated above the knee."
> "'It was a miracle.'"
> "'What I saw was the biggest hatch of fireflies I've ever seen in my life. Family Lampyridae, according to my field guide.'"
> "She nodded. 'Well, I like my conjecture, as you would say, better than yours.'"

## 节点八：绝笔信（ch32）

Absalom 自焚的当夜，一封信从门缝滑进 Hannah 家。信里是全书的谜底：Jasper 是他的儿子，Gabriel 是"他的 monstrousness 所生"。Hannah 对着 Nickel Mines 的宽恕传奇，交出诚实的答案。

> "Like Simon Peter, I denied him until one day, his last, he surmised the truth."
> "'I wish I could say such forgiveness flowed into my heart.'"

## 节点九：万鸟与萤火（ch42）

八月二日凌晨，Gabriel 在睡梦中用非人间的语言歌唱。飞鸟如暴雨抵达，走兽走出林线，十万萤火虫聚成一根光柱——随他的灵魂越过树梢。Thomas 从草地上捡回一束羽毛。

> "They arrived like a thunderstorm, first a distant rumbling, a few drops of rain, then the deluge."
> "They ascended like winged cherubim, and though my own eyes remained closed, I knew Gabriel's earthly life rose with them, up off the bed, above the house, and then over the trees."
> "When Thomas came back to us, in one hand he clutched a bouquet of feathers."

## 节点十：南瓜与萤火虫（ch40 / ch44 收束）

Bella 牵着 Raphael 站在门口——"他是 Gabriel 的儿子"。葬礼后，Hannah 回到 Josiah 的马车上，决定"把南瓜放回种子里"。尾声：她带曾孙拿着玻璃罐走进夜色，教他祖传的口诀。

> "'I'm Bella Alvarez. And this little guy'—she wiggled his hand in hers—'is Raphael. He's Gabriel's son.'"
> "'Josiah is my husband,' I said. '...I must put the pumpkin back into the seed.'"
> "'You have to sneak up behind them as they rise to heaven,' I said, 'and then you hold the jar over them, and they fly right in.'"
