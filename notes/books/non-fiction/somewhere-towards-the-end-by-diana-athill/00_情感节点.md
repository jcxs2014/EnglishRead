# 情感节点（Somewhere Towards the End）

> 全书 10 个节点。节点叙述概括为作者自述，关键引语均逐字取自 `text/` 对应章提取件，可回查精读文件。

## 节点一　两件买不到的东西（ch01）

**叙事概括**：全书的第一件东西不是人，是两样买不到的东西——窗外邻居的哈巴狗，和自己按目录订来、到手只有四片小叶的桫椤。狗不能养的理由是道德（年纪大了买幼犬不遛它，对狗不公平），而桫椤让她看见「为未来而种」这条美德当场破产：她知道自己等不到它长大。**她把这一年写成一次「我必须自己来做」的立项，而立的项是一本书。**

`I have always wanted a pug and now I can’t have one, because buying a puppy when you are too old to take it for walks is unfair.`

`It is so obvious that life works in terms of species rather than of individuals.`

`‘Why not have a go at it?’ So I shall.`

## 节点二　搁浅（ch02）

**叙事概括**：跨过七十一岁那年，她没有变老，只是不再是一个性存在——而这个失去比任何别的东西都来得快。她随即把这条线倒推到四岁（一出洗手间窗户往下吐口水的那位园丁之子）、推到十五岁（Paul）、推到五十岁后那句 `I simply started to fake`。**本章的情绪不是丧失，是清点——她一边清点一边发现自己早就把它算过一次账。**

`Being ‘over seventy’ is being old: suddenly I was aground on that fact and saw that the time had come to size it up.`

`I had ceased be to a sexual being, a condition which had gone through several stages and had not always been a happy one, but which had always seemed central to my existence.`

`That, for me, was the end of romantic love.`

## 节点三　两笔各算各的账（ch03）

**叙事概括**：Sam 是最后一个陪她越过那道边界的人。他与作者之间的关系之所以能持续七年，是因为双方在开始时就各自算清了：她给他 `I was white and well-bred` 的身份满足，他给她 `to be urgently wanted` 的补偿。**本章没有一见钟情的浪漫，只有一次清醒的双向清账——而这正是作者对「爱」这个词在晚年所能给出的最诚实的定义。**

`THE LAST MAN in my life as a sexual being, who accompanied me over the frontier between late middle-age and being old, was Sam, who was born in Grenada in the Caribbean.`

`So I was satisfying as a status symbol, agreeable as a companion in so far as he wanted one, and was able and willing to play along with him in a way he enjoyed.`

`Sam was not a man of vulgar instincts so he didn’t want to show his woman off, but it gave him private satisfaction to feel that she was worth showing.`

## 节点四　那声喊（ch05）

**叙事概括**：母亲九十二岁起病，她每周四夜驱车去诺福克。母亲的最后两天里，她神志不清仍喊了一声 `Basin!`——而她后来意识到，母亲**从头到尾都清楚自己正在承受什么**。母亲临终前想起的最后一件事是上周开车去苗圃买那棵桉树。**全章最重的一笔不是死亡，是那声喊。**

`‘I am not afraid of death.’ … I was not afraid of her being dead, but I was terrified of the process of her dying.`

`Unconscious? No. When about to vomit she gasped ‘Basin!’`

`She was aware of what she was having to endure.`

## 节点五　一个不属于任何章的细节（ch05）

**叙事概括**：她一生只见过的第一具尸体，是 André Deutsch 九十二岁的母亲。她事先准备了一套安慰自己的说法（死亡使面容安详），而实际开口时说出的是一句孩子话：**「哦，可怜的小玛丽亚！」** 这一声推翻了「安详」的全部想象——**生与死之间的墙不是想象筑的，是现场看见的。**

`I had seen only one dead person–and what a ridiculous state of affairs that was: that a woman in her seventies should have seen only one cadaver!`

`‘Oh poor little Maria!’`

`But it was not a comfort to be shown so clearly that my favourite image of floating out to sea at night was nonsense.`

## 节点六　一座园子与一次失控（ch09）

**叙事概括**：表姐出国前只托她 `‘sort of keep an eye’ on the garden（照看一下园子）。第二天她俯身看那个园子，**「我突然而且绝对意外地变成了我的母亲」**——而她的反应不是整理，是**全部铲掉重新开始**。第一株自己种下的植物开花那天，她上瘾了。**这一章是全书最像「重新活过来」的一段。**

`‘There’s only one thing for it,’ I heard myself saying. ‘I must take the whole thing out and start from scratch.’`

`as soon as the first plant I put in with my own hands actually grew and flowered, I was hooked.`

`Getting one’s hands into the earth, spreading roots, making a plant comfortable–it is a totally absorbing occupation, like painting or writing, so that you become what you are doing and are given a wonderful release from consciousness of self.`

## 节点七　不费力的那一桶（ch11）

**叙事概括**：Barry 因前列腺问题住院，护士请她来劝他去厕所。她做好了全部心理准备——**而最让她惊讶的不是不厌恶，是不必费力**：她像职业护士一样公事公办地做完了这件事。**那一刻她才意识到，自己经过这些年，已经进入了一种「妻子状态」——而这正是她与 Paul 订婚时曾以为自己永远不必承担的那份「布丁」。**

`What astonished me was that I didn’t mind doing it. There was no recoil, no feeling of disgust–I seemed to watch myself doing it in a businesslike way, without making any effort, like a professional nurse.`

`After that, I realized that I had moved, after all those years, into a state of Wifehood.`

`obligations which have grown out of love, however little they resemble what they grew out of, are also part of the same thing.`

## 节点八　还活着（ch14）

**叙事概括**：四十一岁那年她意外怀孕，三个月后流产，那次流产几乎要了她的命。担架上她已经缩到只剩听觉，却仍听得见上方的声音：有人去叫人来输血，有人说 `She’s very near collapse`。她想：多蠢的委婉说法，他指的是死。手术后，从身体深处涌上一股巨大的喜悦——**「我还活着！」——它填满了整个她。**

`That miscarriage very nearly killed me.`

`I even thought, ‘What a bloody silly euphemism,’ because what was the state I was in already if it wasn’t collapse? He meant death.`

`IAm STILL ALIVE! It filled the whole of me, nothing else mattered. It was the most intense sensation I have ever experienced.`

## 节点九　它没有起作用（ch11）

**叙事概括**：2006 年，皇家法院重演 Barry 六十年代的成名作《Skivers》。全场起立，小辈们喊「这是我看过最好的戏」，母女俩在出租车上哭。回家的路上她问他：这会不会让你重新振作起来？他平静地答：**「哦不，那不会有用的。」而事实确实如此。** ——这是全书最冷的一次落空，也是她对「被看见」这件事最后的祛魅。

`He had a flash of return to himself in the summer of 2006, when the Royal Court did a season of readings in their Theatre Upstairs of the plays which made them famous during the 1960s, which included Skivers, one of his.`

`he answered calmly, ‘Oh no, it won’t do that.’ And it didn’t.`

`It is not an admirable solution, but I don’t suppose I am the only old person to resort to it.`

## 节点十　值得买（POSTSCRIPT）

**叙事概括**：全书最后一篇只有一株植物。她在 ch01 断言自己看不到它长成树，而这一篇给出的答案是：**她没有低估植物，她低估了观看。** 后记的全部结论是三个词——**「这钱花得值。」** 而这三个字也是这本回忆录对「为看不见的未来而做」这件事的最终裁决。

`it now has nine fronds each measuring about twelve inches long, and within a few days of each frond unfurling to its full length, a little nub of green appears in the fuzzy top of the ‘trunk’`

`I was right in thinking that I will never see it being a tree, but I underestimated the pleasure of watching it being a fern.`

`It was worth buying.`
