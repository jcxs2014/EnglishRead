## 节点一·一场假宴会真等一个人（ch01）

1963 年夏，她为这对途经伦敦的美国夫妇办下晚宴，私下等的却是另一个从未见过的人。整章用一场应酬把读者带进她的期待，又在同一章末尾跳到五年后：他在她的公寓里吞下二十六片安眠药，打了一个电话，然后死去。本书的全部问题在此提出——这不是一个悲伤的故事，而是一个判断。

> `ONE EVENING IN the summer of 1963 I ran downstairs to answer the door with special pleasure. The dinner party was supposed to be for an American couple passing through London, but privately I was looking forward to it because of someone else: a man whom I had never met.`（ch01）

> `I think he was needing a witness. It is bad enough merely to collapse in grief when alone; other people’s ignorance of what is happening soon makes the tears seem foolish. How much worse to be performing what he called ‘the one authentic act of my life’ in a vacuum.`（ch01）

## 节点二·退潮之后剩下的小口袋（ch02）

她为他的名字找到一块地质学上的理由：有些地方的旧风格在别处消失之后仍被保存下来，像帝国退潮后的沉积。Didi 正是这样一个口袋——埃及乡间地主阶级保存着爱德华时代的一切，包括昵称与规矩。也正因如此，他的流亡不是选择，而是站错了边的必然结果。

> `There are places where a particular style is preserved long after it has vanished elsewhere—the sediment, usually, left by a receding empire, little pockets of the Austro-Hungarian empire in Eastern Europe, or of the Ottoman empire in Greece.`（ch02）

> `They resent the regime because they are to its right. Didi is forced to live abroad because he is to its left.`（ch02）

## 节点三·一枚 piastre 与一张诊断书（ch02）

家族的爱在他身上留下的第一道伤口，是母亲只留下一枚小钱、让他打电话去求一个过夜的地方。多年以后，他在一封长信里给自己下诊断：不是理智的疯，是情感的疯；是那种让人看得见一切、却什么也做不了的清醒。全章以这封信收尾。

> `And he gave him one piastre—one piastre—so he could find a telephone and ask someone for a place to sleep. She was always doing things like that.`（ch02）

> `‘Not mentally insane, emotionally insane. This horrible mental sanity which makes me able to see it, and know I can do nothing about it.’`（ch02）

## 节点四·与一位小说家的战争（ch03）

搬进她的公寓后，他一边喜欢着厨房与菜市场的仪式，一边对她隐瞒年龄。她很快看清自己进不了他的爱情——她不是那种穿小黑套装的、深色而苗条的女人。但她也由此获得了一重安全感：她救不了他，只能陪着他。

> `He lied to make things more like they ought to be: more amusing, sadder, more romantic, more strange, less humiliating. The lies could be as trivial as a description of a dinner’s being delicious when in fact it was bad; it was their pointlessness, even more than the masterly naturalness with which he told them, which made them hard to spot, and in the end made me stop trying to spot them: that, and the knowledge that I was as likely to be doing him an injustice by suspecting a lie as I was to be fooled by believing one.`（ch03）

> `A ‘little black suit’ on a dark slim woman was enough in itself to make Didi fall in love. I am not a ‘little black suit’ woman.`（ch03）

## 节点五·最后一晚，他没有转过头（ch03）

分离前夜的最后一餐，他求她不要介意自己撑不住这个结局，她跪下去抱住他——而他没有动，甚至没有把眼睛转过来。这一幕之后她写下对「说谎」的诊断：他不为自己获利撒谎，只为让事情更像它本该有的样子。领事馆那一幕则是这份诊断的实证：他听错了，却没有编造。

> `‘Oh god, Diana, I don’t know that I can stand losing all this.’`（ch03）

> `To get what he wanted he had not misrepresented, but had misinterpreted.`（ch03）

## 节点六·一朵随身携带的雷云（ch04）

南斯拉夫之行原本是件好事，三周后变成一场单方面的指控。他说她在国外的样子像个小丑：人们正捂着嘴笑她走下街。他递上「我爱你」当通行证，再把羞辱挂在上面。表姐替她总结了那三周的空气：「这就像随身带着一朵便携式的小雷云。」

> `‘It’s like carrying round a little portable thunder cloud,’ and it was. Didi’s power of mood-projection was at its strongest.`（ch04）

> `‘Don’t you realize,’ he said, ‘that you are making yourself a figure of fun? People are laughing at you—they are trying to hide their smiles as you go down the street, and I hate to see people laughing at you because I’m so fond of you.’`（ch04）

## 节点七·「你指的是现在！」（ch04）

临别时他说，可惜他们之间不可能有私情。她把这句话听成安慰，一直读到汽车里才反应过来——他是在拒绝一场正在发生的追求。而当夜在一间酒馆里，他对着一个美国中士嘶吼：有人住在伦敦一个叫汉普斯特德的地方，自认左派只因为那是时髦。这是全书最激烈的一次失序。

> `and suddenly, as though there had been a flash of light in my head, I gave a silent screech ‘Oh my god—he meant now!’`（ch04）

> `there are these people in England, they live in a part of London called Hampstead where she lives and they think they’re left-wing because it’s the smart thing to be, but it’s nothing but fashion with them, nothing but fashion, they’ve no knowledge, no feeling, no passion`（ch04）

## 节点八·说真话游戏，与强者的位置（ch05）

他喝到微微发软就来敲门，然后宣布：你是个可怕的伪君子。她赢下每一局，理由是她手里握着全部王牌。但她随即写下全书最锋利的一句自我诊断：强者的位置本身也可以是脆弱的——因为她有能力摧毁他，因此不能动用。

> `I always won these games because I held all the trumps.`（ch05）

> `It had never occurred to me before that the position of strength can be a weak one.`（ch05）

## 节点九·摊开在桌面上的日记（ch05）

他深夜出走又折回，最终她要求他搬走、承诺替他付房租。回应他的是一场彻底的崩溃。而在崩溃之后，他把日记本摊开在书桌上，旁边压着周日的报纸，等着她读到——其中一句写着：把这口气从她身上卸下来。而在他自己的诊断里，厌恶先于一切判断。

> `‘My last entry was getting Diana off my chest and with this entry I am off her back (figuratively I am pleased to say. Literally is too repulsive to even think about).’`（ch05）

> `I find it impossible to live in the same flat as someone whose physical body seems to provoke mine to cringe.`（ch05）

## 节点十·清算与移交（ch06）

她以为给他一个家就是治疗，其实正是那份安全让冰面终于承受不住。本章给出全书的因果：他要活下去需要的是幻觉，不是真相；而他被判定为不配被爱，于是必须受罚，唯一的办法就是一头扎到那个他会被逼着惩罚自己的地步去。最后一句把记录移交给读者。

> `to stay alive Didi needed illusion, not truth.`（ch06）

> `Being unworthy of love, he must be punished; and the only way he could secure this was by plunging out to the point where he was driven to punish himself. To be murdered would be a fate much simpler, and less sad.`（ch06）

> `This record has been written for him, and for people who are going to have children.`（ch06）
