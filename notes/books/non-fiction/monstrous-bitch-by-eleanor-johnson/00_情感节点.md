# 00. 情感节点（9 个）

## 节点一：一份清单，与它下面的深渊（ch01 · 序言）

**叙事概括：**全书从一个清单开始：投票、公立教育、契约自主、陪审资格、财产处分、不被强迫婚姻与生育。然后作者把这张清单整体前移到欧洲中世纪，得到一组几乎全部落空的对照——不识字、不上学、不能进入政府与教会、可能死于分娩。这个落差不是修辞，是全书唯一的坐标系：因为她要说的事必须先被看见——被看见的东西有多具体，后面的论证才有分量。而序言末尾那句话让这份庆幸立刻变成恐惧：眼下女性的事情正在变坏，逆着七百年的趋势，而且发生得很快。

**关键引语：**

> "I’VE ALWAYS thought of myself as lucky. I was born in New York City in 1979. That means that, relative to all other women in recorded history, I have had a chance at a full, free, independent, long life."（ch01）

> "Things for women are getting worse, not better, right now, and it’s happening fast. That deterioration bucks a trend that’s about seven hundred years old, in which things have slowly been getting better for women."（ch01）

## 节点二：开口即获罪（ch03 · 原书第 1 章：古美索不达米亚）

**叙事概括：**这一章最让人不安的地方，是它把「女人说话太多」写成了一段神话的起因：一位女神仅仅因为说出了触犯父权的话就真的被恶魔化，于是她的开口把生育暴力散布到整个人类世界。她被父亲放归人世，成为夜的恶魔、饮血者、孩童的杀手。而提亚马特的死法更冷——马尔杜克把她肢解，同时封住她的嘴，作为对她那孕育众生的子宫、她贪婪的性欲与她响亮嗓音的惩罚。恐惧、处置、封口，在同一段里完成。

**关键引语：**

> "Let me slow this down for a moment, because the point is crucial: This is an origin myth in which a female deity is literally demonized for speaking out in a way that offended Patriarchy. The result of her speaking out is to spread reproductive violence across the human world."（ch03）

> "Symbolically, then, Marduk rapes Tiamat to death. Moreover, by raping and murdering her, he has of course also silenced her; no more can Tiamat paralyze and incapacitate her enemies with her all-powerful voice, because she has been raped to death, in punishment for her teeming womb, her rapacious sexual desires, and her loud voice."（ch03）

## 节点三：怪物第一次有了观众（ch04 · 原书第 2 章：希腊）

**叙事概括：**在雅典悲剧之前，怪物只是神话残片；之后她有了两位有名有姓的男性剧作家，也第一次有了会为她心跳的观众。《俄瑞斯忒亚》里那场浴中的弑夫被安排成幕后演出、再由人物回报给观众，而作者明说场面是刻意去势化的——一把形状像阳具的刀，反复刺穿赤身裸体的丈夫。更动人的是美狄亚：所有杀婴者中，只有她为自己做的事感到矛盾，于是她成了唯一同时吸引同情与恐怖的形象。观众第一次发现，怪物可以让人难过。

**关键引语：**

> "Once Agamemnon is in his house, Clytemnestra stabs him to death, in the bath, while he’s naked. Even though it was scripted to be performed offstage, and then reported back in detail to the audience, Agamemnon’s death scene is pointedly emasculating: Clytemnestra repeatedly pierces his body with a large, phallic knife while she has him tangled up in tapestries in the bathroom."（ch04）

> "Medea is as much a lightning rod for compassion as she is for horror. That, too, is new in the Monstrous Bitch tradition: We’re not supposed to feel compassion for the Furies, nor for Clytemnestra, nor for Tiamat, nor for Lamashtu."（ch04）

## 节点四：一个名字变成了一个句式（ch05 · 原书第 3 章：罗马）

**叙事概括：**罗马做的事很聪明：把怪物母狗「人性化」——不再是半人半兽，只是恰好会巫术的普通女人。《埃涅阿斯纪》开篇那句判词冷得彻底：女人是可以被丢下的，男人才是建国与守护新父权家园的必需品。但作者不肯停在训导上，她要读者看见更锋利的一面：埃里赫托之所以比以往所有怪物化身都可怕，是因为她不怕神；等到二世纪末，lamia 已经从一个专名变成普通名词——复数的 lamiae 到处都是，她成了「每个女人」。恐惧从此不需要怪物，只需要一个形容词。

**关键引语：**

> "But what Lucan seems to find most terrifying about Erichtho is a trait she has that none of those other Monstrous Bitch incarnations have. She does not fear the gods. The Bacchae may not care about human patriarchs, or even about Zeus, but they sure do follow Dionysus."（ch05）

> "By the end of the second century CE, then, when Apuleius is writing, “lamia” had come to denote a Monstrous Bitch who was sexually predatory, baby-slaying, spellcasting, and violent. Now that Lamia has become lamiae, the threat of the Monstrous Bitch is potentially everywhere; she is an everywoman now, a witch who seduces men, casts her spells, and attacks the blood and bodies of children and young men."（ch05）

## 节点五：一台跨代的机器（ch06 · 原书第 4 章：基督教）

**叙事概括：**这一章读起来像一份验尸报告。基督教没有杀死怪物母狗，而是抽干了她身上的自主力量，把她改写成撒旦的雇员——然后把标签贴到任何一个「行为不端」的女人身上。克雷默那句精巧的话是整台机器的设计图：女巫没有真实的力量，却要负全部责任。而机器真正咬住的是身体：公开处刑不只是惩罚女巫，更是要让那些眼看就要失去母亲的无助女孩提前害怕成为母亲——怕自己的母亲太吵、太骚，或者对腹中的孩子过于暴烈。恐惧的终点是子宫。

**关键引语：**

> "Since they willingly take on his power, they must be held fully accountable; the law must act as if their borrowed malice were truly innate to them. Witches have no real power but full responsibility. A neat interpretive trick, and a lethal one, because it enabled witch-hunters to prosecute just about anyone they wanted to, and to prosecute them to the fullest extent of the law."（ch06）

> "The church and state used the public execution of accused witches to inflict abject terror and trauma on helpless young girls, on the brink of losing their own mothers, who were told that their mothers had been too loud, too sexy, or violent toward unborn children. Witch fever, by this point in history, had become a mechanism for brutally regulating and disciplining female behavior, and doing so intergenerationally."（ch06）

## 节点六：旧案被拆掉，然后父权换了目标（ch07 · 原书第 5 章：戏剧与猎巫的退潮）

**叙事概括：**这一章有一种解气。十七世纪的一批剧作家钻进怪物母狗的「深层基因代码」，把恐怖改写成对女性有利的版本：麦克白夫人拒绝女性生育行为的规范，三个女巫在剧终既没有被处决也没有被捕受审——因为她们实实在在提供了一种社会服务，而那种服务被编码成了抵抗。真正的收尾在哈钦森：古怪而无法解释的事，先予搁置，等到理解之后再说；他不与父权讲另一套语言，他用父权自己的经文与历史说话。迫害退潮了。可作者没让读者带着安慰离开：父权仍然需要找到新的方式，来恶魔化并虐待女性。

**关键引语：**

> "One of the most important claims Hutchinson makes is this innocent-seeming little one: “Things odd and unaccountable are to be respited till we understand them” (75). He is presupposing that supernatural-seeming events have natural and recoverable causes."（ch07）

> "Accusations and trials stopped happening, as did executions. People didn’t believe in witches that much anymore, or if they did, they didn’t feel the need to persecute, prosecute, or execute them anymore. But Patriarchy still needed to find ways to demonize and abuse women."（ch07）

## 节点七：控制术一次次失败之后留下的残影（ch09 · 原书第 7 章：四十至五十年代）

**叙事概括：**这一章有一种宿命感：女性拿到公开发声权与收入的同一批年代，男医生与精神分析师接手了驯服她们的工作。《德古拉的女儿》里的玛丽亚想把自己治好，把吸血鬼当成一种病，结果治疗当然失败——玛丽亚不是精神病人，她是吸血鬼。作者没有嘲笑四十年代的自信，反而把这批电影当作诚实的记录读：它们恰恰拍出了控制术的失败，于是留下了残影。而残影需要一种语法来解释「过去如何在今天反复回访」——附体。过去的怪物母狗猛地涌上来，附到今天的年轻女子身上。

**关键引语：**

> "But then, we shouldn’t be surprised, nor disappointed. Of course the treatment fails: Marya’s not mentally ill; she’s a vampire. Part of what this film seems to be saying to the fantasy of Freudian efficacy at explaining female monstrosity by recourse to psychosexual dynamics is that there’s something in the Monstrous Bitch that exceeds the capacity of medicine to treat or the talking cure to talk away."（ch09）

> "And in all cases, the fundamental mechanism that these films use to understand how the past keeps revisiting itself on the present is the mechanism of possession. The past Monstrous Bitch surges up to take possession of the young women of today. And that past Monstrous Bitch is loudmouthed, hypersexual, and once again eager to commit violence on young people in particular, be they babies or young girls."（ch09）

## 节点八：她活了下来（ch11 · 原书第 9 章：七十年代吸血鬼片）

**叙事概括：**这是全书少有的明亮处，但作者不让它便宜。七十年代银幕上的女同吸血鬼把女性欲望放在女性怀里，怪物母狗第一次可以当主角；作者也承认这是四千年来几乎一致厌女的传统开始离开厌女——但她同时把限制写清：观众被要求同情她们，同时也怕她们，而所有影片仍然要求把她们登记为性对象。真正让这一章站住的，是一个技术性的事实：在卡恩施泰因三部曲里她们被钉穿心脏，在另一部片里被烈日活活烧死；而弗兰与米丽亚姆这两样都不必承受——她们活了下来。

**关键引语：**

> "Fran and Miriam do not undergo the ultimate punishment of being staked through the heart, as happened in the Karnstein Trilogy, or burned alive in the sunlight, like Diane from The Velvet Vampire. Instead, they survive. This is an extraordinarily important innovation in the sprawling Monstrous Bitch genre: In this film, we like the vampiresses and they are allowed to survive."（ch11）

> "From a feminist standpoint, this taming process is disappointing. But at the same time as they decenter female desire and sapphic behavior, the films get more explicit that patriarchal predatoriness is the ultimate source for Monstrous Bitchery. That, of course, resonates loudly with what I’ve been arguing throughout this book—namely, that the Monstrous Bitch is an emergent property of Patriarchy."（ch11）

## 节点九：回到伊南娜（ch13 · 原书第 11 章：回到巴比伦）

**叙事概括：**收束章先用最坏的消息开场：二〇二〇年代是女性权利萎缩最严重的十年，生殖权利受威胁只会让拉玛什图以最可怕的形态回来。作者没有停在控诉，她把《遗传性》与《物质》并置，说它们给出的答案惊人相似——最需要我们恐惧与归咎的，不只是父权，还有女性自己甘愿从属的那份意愿。读到这里会不舒服。然后她把出路放到四千三百年前：恩赫杜安娜，一位终生崇拜伊南娜的女祭司，而伊南娜既是值得祈求庇护的母亲，也是令人惧怕的力量——被父权拆成三样的声音、性与生育，重新长回同一个女神身上。

**关键引语：**

> "These two films—which seem miles apart within the capacious boundaries of horror about Monstrous Bitches—are intimately linked, and they give surprisingly similar answers to what is causing the feminist backslide we’re in right now. Both films, in the end, suggest that it is not just Patriarchy but also women’s own willingness to subordinate themselves to Patriarchy that we perhaps most need to fear and blame."（ch13）

> "Indeed, throughout her tenure as priestess to the temples of An and Nanna, Enheduanna deepened her profound devotion to Inanna. In fact, she made quite clear that, in her view, Inanna outranked both An and Nanna in theological importance."（ch13）
