# 10. Chapter 9: Radical Honesty, Rational Hypocrisy（激进诚实，理性虚伪）

## 概览

- **出处**：*When Everyone Knows That Everyone Knows…*，Steven Pinker 著，Scribner 2025 初版（本书正文收束章，书内 Chapter 9）
- **作者**：Steven Pinker，本书自述身份为认知科学家
- **章节定位**：正文的收束章（目录页的章题作「Radical Hypocrisy」，正文章首作「Rational Hypocrisy」，提取件两处都照原样留着）。功能是把前八章的正面命题反过来写：先回收「共同知识如何生成协调」的整条线索，再列出人们同样费心阻止共同知识的证据，然后用卡特访谈、隐私与极权、激进诚实运动与桥水基金逐一拆解「什么都该摊开说」，最后给出机制解释（惯例是近乎虚构的理想化）并把公私两侧的虚伪收进同一个「递归心智化」主题
- **字符数**：32,620（提取件 `text/ch10_chapter_9_radical_honesty_rational_hypocrisy.txt` 全文实测）
- **章首副题**：「不是每件事都该成为共同知识」
- **一句话主旨**：既然共同知识是协调的钥匙，为什么人还拼命阻止它——因为维系关系的惯例是近乎虚构的理想化，一旦「谁都想偷懒、谁都知道别人知道」被说出口，合作就塌回冷冰冰的正式制度；于是激进诚实反成最大的虚伪，而理性虚伪是有逻辑的

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：透明并非在任何尺度上都更好。本章要证明的是：把私人知识与相互知识挡在共同知识之外，不是出于拘谨或礼貌的性格，而是协调博弈的结构性要求——社会关系靠「近乎虚构」的惯例撑着，公开反证会毒化这些惯例；因此「要求完全诚实」本身就是一种不诚实，而保留某些虚伪有理性根据。

- **证据链**：

| 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 一九七六年总统选战末期，卡特在《花花公子》长访谈里自承「心里犯过多次奸淫」，民调领先随即蒸发、那年十一月勉强胜出 | 案例（政治） | 无害的坦白照样致命——透明的代价可以被民调测出 |
| 一七九二年汉密尔顿谈制宪会议：若辩论当场公开，派系的喧腾会挡下任何令人满意的结果 | 史料引述 | 私密审议是达成协议的结构条件，不是道德缺陷 |
| 小说里的极权国家与现实中东德、中国的电子监控和匿名告密网；帕斯卡一六七〇年「若人人知道别人如何议论自己，世上就不会有四个朋友」 | 案例 + 引语 | 「没做错就不必隐藏」站不住——隐私是关系与名声存续的前提 |
| 一九九七年心理治疗师布兰顿创立的「激进诚实」运动、 精神科医生亚历山大「毫无好处地毁掉你的人生」的评语 | 现实实验 | 彻底坦白的真实后果是被开除与被甩，不是皆大欢喜 |
| 全球最大对冲基金桥水公司的「激进透明」：员工互相打分训斥、被摄像记录，而交易策略只对少数核心圈公开、离职者被保密协议封口 | 案例 | 激进诚实自身就是最大的虚伪——规则只约束别人 |
| 演化人类学家西蒙斯论隐秘幻想，霍布斯一六五一年与英国刑法史家的旧语，加上「现代调查里多数受访者报告过伤害、强迫与不忠的幻想」 | 研究 + 史料 | 私人知识与相互知识本已存在，说出口才会污染相处所依赖的假设池 |

- **论证脉络**：巴别塔回收前文（共同知识＝协调之钥）→ 反转：人也费心阻止共同知识（躲眼神、装不认识、借口、弦外之音、婉辞绕话）→ 制度层面的压制（央行行长、科学界自我审查、外交行话、既不承认也不否认）→ 提问「为什么不干脆摊开」→ 承认透明化的历史趋势与其收益 → 立论：要求完全诚实是终极的不诚实 → 卡特访谈、邮件外泄、热麦失言、汉密尔顿的旧话（私密审议的条件性）→ 隐私与极权监控、帕斯卡、年轻世代的告密热线 → 虚构与喜剧里的「不能说谎」实验都指向同一个结局：善意隐瞒被恢复才算解脱 → 激进诚实运动与桥水基金两个现实实验双双失败 → 转向机制：开放的天花板与「常识式解释太轻巧」→ 协调逻辑：人是彻底相互依赖的物种 → 惯例是近乎虚构的理想化，公开反证会毒化协调 → 三种关系模式（共有共享、权威等级、平等匹配）各自依赖的虚构 → 西蒙斯：想象里满是幻想，但私人／相互知识不等于共同知识 → 让步：社会改革恰恰始于有人当众指出房间里的大象 → 公共侧的理性虚伪（警察移开目光、民选的算术、生命定价、以色列的刻意模糊与台湾的主权现状）→ 学者的「不去做」可能是更高阶的理性 → 全书收束于递归心智化：我们不仅有思想，还有关于思想的思想

- **可质疑处**：
  1. **「数学上可证明」与本章实际给出的东西之间有落差**：机制段落把「把知识留在私下」说成有理性、乃至数学上可证明的理由，但本章没有给出任何模型或推导，只留了一个指向书内 Chapter 7 暗示博弈某个格子的括号提示（「the upper right cell in the innuendo game from chapter 7, page 247」）。强度断言由另一章的图表背书，读者在本章拿不到可检验的东西——「可证明」在此更接近修辞。
  2. **理论对「哪些秘密该破」没有给出判据**：让步段落同时承认惯例会掩盖不义、社会改革始于有人大声指出房间里的大象。但全书的判准只说「公开会毒化协调」，而既有的不平等协调同样能被这句话辩护——若一个假说既能解释「该沉默」又能解释「该说出来」，它在什么观察下会被推翻？本章没有交代。
  3. **反激进诚实的证据里有共同混淆因素**：桥水被描述成霸凌与 paranoid 的职场，但支撑材料里同时存在「达利奥自己打压对自己政策的批评」这条——真正摧毁协调的可能不是透明本身，而是透明被不对称地执行（只许上级评价下级）。本章把它当成「激进诚实失败」的证据，却没把它和「选择性透明失败」区分开。

## 选择性精读

> **原句 1:** "The story of the Tower of Babel narrates the idea that when humans coordinate their actions though common knowledge, the sky’s the limit on what they can accomplish. A shared language is the ultimate generator of common knowledge, but it’s far from the only one. As we have seen in the preceding chapters, much of human social life consists in generating and partaking of the common knowledge necessary for coordination on scales large and small."

- **中文理解**：巴别塔的故事讲的是——人一旦靠共同知识协调行动，能成就的事就没有上限；共享语言是共同知识最强的生成器，却远不是仅有的一种。正如前面几章所见，人类社会生活的大部分，就在于生成并分享规模有大有小的协调所必需的那份共同知识。
- **句子结构**：首句 The story of the Tower of Babel narrates the idea that… 挂一个 when 从句的同位语；⚠️ 原文此处作 `though common knowledge`（提取件逐字如此，语义上应读作 through，不是本项目转录走形）。第二句 A shared language is the ultimate generator…, but it’s far from the only one 为并列句；第三句 As we have seen in the preceding chapters 先立索引，主干 much of human social life consists in generating and partaking of…。
- **关键词**：`the ultimate generator of common knowledge`、`generating and partaking of the common knowledge`、`on scales large and small`、`the sky’s the limit`
- **表达方式**：巴别塔（神学语域）与 the sky’s the limit（口语成语）并置，把一个抽象术语同时写成「僭越」和「乐观」；generating／partaking 双动名词把社会生活描写成一场共同的生产与共享。
- **为什么这样写**：末章开篇不提新论点，而是用 As we have seen in the preceding chapters 一句把前八章的结论整体回述——先把正题（共同知识＝协调之钥）钉牢，下一章动作才好翻面：本章要问的恰恰是「为什么不该把一切摊开」。这也是全书写作策略的自白：结论必须读者自己复述得出来，才配被反转。

> **原句 2:** "Yet for all the ingenuity that people expend in creating common knowledge, they also rack their brains in figuring how to prevent it. Misbehaving children avoid catching the glances of their parents, a strategy they never outgrow: adults, too, avoid eye contact with onlookers when they commit a faux pas. People may try not to notice a need for action when they sense that others might step in. They may fail to recognize each other in compromising circumstances, or pretend to be asleep to enjoy an illicit kiss. They may fail to disclose an inadvertent act of disloyalty. They may try to pass as white, or straight, or slim. They donate anonymously while hoping the gift leaks, and conceal their thirst for status, approval, and authenticity as they strive for all of them. They accept pretexts, open secrets, and flimsy excuses. They disguise their imperatives as idle questions and hypotheticals. They veil their bribes, threats, and sexual come-ons in innuendo. They conceal their complicity in evil acts using euphemism and circumlocution. We all are aware of efforts to stymie awareness, and exercise our talents at seeing through them in brainteasers, mysteries, and jokes."

- **中文理解**：尽管人花尽心思去创造共同知识，也同样绞尽脑汁阻止它：不听话的孩子躲开父母的眼光，这招一辈子都不过时——成年人出洋相时也躲开旁观者的目光；感到别人可能接手，人就装作没看见有个行动的需要；在尴尬场合装作互不相认，或假装睡着好享受一个不正当的吻；不披露一次失手的背叛；设法冒充白人、异性恋或瘦子；匿名捐款却希望礼物走漏消息，一边追求地位、认可与真实，一边藏起这份渴求；接受借口、公开的秘密和站不住脚的托词；把命令伪装成闲话与假设；把贿赂、威胁和性招徕蒙进弦外之音；用婉辞与绕话遮掩自己在恶行里的共谋。人人都知道有人在设法阻止「被知道」，并在脑筋急转弯、侦探故事和笑话里练习看穿这些手法。
- **句子结构**：首句 Yet for all the ingenuity that people expend in creating common knowledge, they also rack their brains… 用 for all 让步起头，与前七段的正向清单构成对仗；随后是 They may… / They donate… and conceal… / They accept… / They disguise… / They veil… / They conceal… 的同构短句连排；末句 We all are aware of efforts to stymie awareness, and exercise our talents at seeing through them… 把前面所有行为升格为一个总判断。
- **关键词**：`rack their brains in figuring how to prevent it`、`They accept pretexts, open secrets, and flimsy excuses`、`They veil their bribes, threats, and sexual come-ons in innuendo`、`euphemism and circumlocution`、`efforts to stymie awareness`
- **表达方式**：整段是一串同构短句的排比（They… They… They…），句法模仿「遮」这个动作的重复性；stymie awareness 把「认知」写成可被阻挡的实体；句末把躲闪、暗示、婉辞一路接到 brainteasers, mysteries, and jokes——把遮掩说成一门娱乐产业。
- **为什么这样写**：这是全章的枢纽：本章不需要新证据，只需要把前几章一路建立的「共同知识=好」的基调翻一个面。作者选择用清单而非案例来做翻转，因为清单能覆盖从幼儿到政治、从捐款到犯罪的尺度跨度；最后一句更埋下后手——既然我们全都熟练于看穿遮掩，那么「共同知识」与「假装没有共同知识」其实是同一套能力的两用。

> **原句 3:** "So why don’t we cut the crap? If common knowledge is necessary for coordination, and coordination is a win–win game, why do we keep so much knowledge private? Why the fig leaves, the white lies, the elephants in the room?"

- **中文理解**：既然共同知识是协调所必需、协调又是双赢的博弈，我们为什么还把那么多知识留在私下——为什么要留着遮羞布、白色谎言、房间里的大象？
- **句子结构**：So why don’t we cut the crap? 三字俗语起头；If common knowledge is necessary for coordination, and coordination is a win–win game, why do we keep so much knowledge private? 条件句里把两个前提串成链（necessary → win–win）；末句 Why the fig leaves, the white lies, the elephants in the room? 省略谓语，只留三个名词短语。
- **关键词**：`cut the crap`、`necessary for coordination`、`a win–win game`、`keep so much knowledge private`、`the fig leaves, the white lies, the elephants in the room`
- **表达方式**：cut the crap 是粗口语域，紧接的三个名词却各自带着典故：无花果叶（《创世记》遮羞）、白色谎言（社交礼貌）、房间里的大象（作者自己在书内 Chapter 1 用过的成语，此处以问句回收章题意象）。
- **为什么这样写**：全章的问题式入口——作者先把读者会有的反驳（对啊，那何必遮着）完整喊出来，再逐条拆给读者看。三个名词并列而不展开，是因为它们已经在前八章各自被写过：末章提问的合法性正来自前文的积累。

> **原句 4:** "In reality, calling for complete honesty is the ultimate dishonesty. No one really wants it, sometimes for good reason. As we saw in the previous chapter, those disenchanting scientists are all too happy to censor ideas that threaten to disenchant their own vision of an ideal world. Editorialists condemn politicians for their dishonesty, and then report a “scandal” when they are too honest."

- **中文理解**：现实里，呼吁完全诚实本身就是终极的不诚实。没人真心想要它，有时还很有道理：上一章已经看到，那些负责给世界祛魅的科学家，一旦某个想法可能祛掉他们自己理想世界的魅，就乐于审查它；报纸评论员先谴责政客不诚实，等政客太诚实，又把它当「丑闻」来报。
- **句子结构**：In reality, calling for complete honesty is the ultimate dishonesty 用动名词短语作主语；No one really wants it, sometimes for good reason 两个短句定调；As we saw in the previous chapter… 回指前章；末句 Editorialists condemn…, and then report a “scandal” when they are too honest 用 and then 把两个动作钉成同一群人的自相矛盾。
- **关键词**：`calling for complete honesty is the ultimate dishonesty`、`No one really wants it`、`those disenchanting scientists`、`report a “scandal” when they are too honest`
- **表达方式**：dishonesty／honesty 同源词反复，靠词义反转构造悖论；disenchanting（祛魅，作者在前文借韦伯之口用过的词）被移回来打在科学家身上，形成语义回旋；“scandal” 加了引号——引号本身就是论证。
- **为什么这样写**：这是本章论点的正面陈述，位置在提问之后、案例之前：先用两条现成的自相矛盾（审查科学的启蒙者、报道太诚实的评论员）把「诚实=好」的直觉打薄，再交给卡特那种具体案例。As we saw in the previous chapter 明确回收书内 Chapter 8 的「取消本能」与学术自我审查，末章的收束感靠的就是这类跨章回指。

> **原句 5:** "A historic example took place in the closing weeks of the 1976 presidential campaign, when Jimmy Carter agreed to a long-form interview with Playboy, a feature of the magazine that was highly regarded in its day. When he was asked whether his public image of straitlaced piety might turn off voters, he replied that he was in no position to claim moral superiority. Quoting the Sermon on the Mount, Carter explained that Jesus said having an impure desire in one’s heart is tantamount to committing an actual sin, and confessed, “I’ve looked on a lot of women with lust. I’ve committed adultery in my heart many times.”3 Hardly a shocking revelation, especially from a notably monogamous politician. But his lead in the polls evaporated, and he barely eked out a victory over Gerald Ford that November."

- **中文理解**：一个历史性例子发生在一九七六年总统选战的收尾几周：卡特同意接受《花花公子》的一次长篇访谈（那档栏目当年很有分量）。被问到那种一本正经的虔诚形象会不会赶走选民时，他答说自己没立场宣称道德优越；接着引《登山宝训》，解释耶稣说过心中动了不干净的念头就等同于犯了实际的罪，并坦白：「我带着 lust 看过很多女人。我心里犯过很多次奸淫。」这算不上什么惊人的揭露——尤其出自一位出了名只忠于配偶的政客。但他在民调里的领先蒸发掉了，那年十一月勉强赢过福特。
- **句子结构**：A historic example took place…, when Jimmy Carter agreed to… 主句加同位语 a feature of the magazine that was highly regarded in its day；第三句用 he replied that… 挂间接引语；第四句 Quoting the Sermon on the Mount 分词起头、内层是 Carter explained that Jesus said having an impure desire… is tantamount to committing an actual sin 的三层嵌套，再以 confessed, “…” 交出直接引语；末两句 But his lead in the polls evaporated, and he barely eked out a victory… 用 But 短促收尾。（⚠️ 引语里 many times 后的「3」是提取件带出的尾注上标。）
- **关键词**：`straitlaced piety`、`tantamount to committing an actual sin`、`I’ve committed adultery in my heart many times`、`Hardly a shocking revelation`、`his lead in the polls evaporated`、`barely eked out a victory`
- **表达方式**：evaporated 与 eked out 两个动词一轻一重：前者的物化比喻（领先像水汽一样散掉）配上后者的「挤出来」，把政治后果写成一道物理与体力的双重衰减；Hardly a shocking revelation 是作者的评注插进叙事，语气词 rather／especially 承担让步。
- **为什么这样写**：本章的证据策略是拿「无害的真话」做实验：卡特说的内容几乎不算丑闻，惩罚却真实落地——这才能够干净地把代价归给「公开」这个形式，而不是归给「过错」这个内容。同时它在回收书内 Chapter 7 的主题（心中的欲望 vs 说出口的欲望）：同一个念头，私人知识与共同知识之间隔着选举结果。

> **原句 6:** "It also reminds us of the preciousness of privacy in the lives of individuals. Totalitarian states in fiction, like Nineteen Eighty-Four, and in reality, like communist East Germany and China, tolerate no zone of privacy, obliterating them with electronic surveillance and networks of anonymous informants. The rationale is, If you aren’t doing anything wrong, what do you have to hide? But of course even the most innocent of us has a great deal to hide. We know our relationships and reputations would lie in smithereens if the convivial conversations we enjoy with friends and intimates, with all their mordant remarks and politically incorrect wisecracks, were ever made public. Again, this would appear to be a part of the human condition. As Blaise Pascal put it in 1670, “I lay it down as a fact that if all men knew what others say of them, there would not be four friends in the world.”7 I suspect that one of the reasons that Gen Zers (born in the mid-1990s or later) suffer from such acute anxiety and loneliness, despite unprecedented opportunities to connect with each other, is that they live in a surveillance regime where peers can snitch to “bias response hotlines” about their “microaggressions,” and a corps of offense archeologists can dig through years of social media posts to unearth shards of bigotry.8"

- **中文理解**：这也提醒我们隐私在个人生活里的分量。小说里的极权国家——像《一九八四》——和现实中的极权国家——像共产主义的东德与中国——都不容许任何隐私地带，用电子监控和匿名告密网络把它们抹掉；其理由是那套「你又没做错什么，有什么可隐藏的？」。可当然，哪怕我们中最无辜的人也有很多要藏的：我们知道，倘若跟朋友和密友的那些愉快交谈——连同里面尖刻的评论和政治不正确的俏皮话——一旦公开，我们的关系与名声会碎成渣。
- **句子结构**：首句 It also reminds us of the preciousness of privacy… 承上启下；第二句 Totalitarian states in fiction, like…, and in reality, like…, tolerate no zone of privacy, obliterating them with… 用 in fiction／in reality 对称并挂现在分词结果状语；第三句 The rationale is, If you aren’t doing anything wrong, what do you have to hide? 把对方论证直接写成一句反问；But of course even the most innocent of us… 短句翻转；末句 We know our relationships and reputations would lie in smithereens if… were ever made public 是虚拟语气的主句—条件句倒装，主语之间插进 with all their… 的介词短语。
- **关键词**：`the preciousness of privacy`、`tolerate no zone of privacy`、`If you aren’t doing anything wrong, what do you have to hide?`、`even the most innocent of us has a great deal to hide`、`lie in smithereens`、`mordant remarks and politically incorrect wisecracks`
- **表达方式**：smithereens（碎片）配 lie in，把关系与名声写成摔在地上的器物；zone of privacy 用空间隐喻把隐私处理成有边界的领土；obliterating 与「抹除」同一语义强度，和小说／现实两组例证一起构成威慑感。
- **为什么这样写**：作者的论证顺序很关键：先给「公开」这个原则最彻底的政治版本（监控与告密网），再交出反驳它的直觉武器——同一套逻辑的口号「没做错就不必隐藏」听起来体面，却因为「最无辜的人也有得多要藏」而失效。虚拟语气（would lie… if… were ever made public）标明这一击靠的是反事实推理，而非统计数据；这也正是可质疑处 2 的来源。

> **原句 7:** "The historical trend toward openness has a natural limit, with radical honesty turning out to be the greatest hypocrisy of all. People need to keep many thoughts private, however illogical that might appear at first glance. It’s easy to “explain” this diffidence by appealing to common sense: we’re naturally sensitive and inhibited and polite, and want to avoid offending people or causing tension and embarrassment and awkwardness and bad feelings. All true, but too glib to be a satisfying explanation. Why did evolution equip us with these feelings, rather than a willingness to let it all hang out?"

- **中文理解**：走向公开的历史趋势有一条天然的界线，激进诚实到头来成了最大的虚伪。人需要把许多念头留在私下，无论这乍看多么不合逻辑。要「解释」这种保留很容易，搬出常识即可：我们天生敏感、拘谨、有礼貌，不想冒犯人，不想制造紧张、尴尬、别扭和坏情绪。这些都对，但太轻巧，算不上让人满意的解释——为什么演化要给我们这些感受，而不是那种把一切晾出来的意愿？
- **句子结构**：首句 The historical trend toward openness has a natural limit, with radical honesty turning out to be the greatest hypocrisy of all 用 with + 现在分词的独立结构给出结果；第二句 People need to keep many thoughts private, however illogical that might appear at first glance 让步副词 however 收在句尾；第三句 It’s easy to “explain” this diffidence by appealing to common sense: 冒号后接 we’re naturally sensitive and inhibited and polite, and want to avoid offending people or causing tension and embarrassment and awkwardness and bad feelings 的连排；All true, but too glib to be a satisfying explanation 是无主语的判断短句；末句 Why did evolution equip us with these feelings, rather than…? 用反问把解释责任交回演化。
- **关键词**：`a natural limit`、`the greatest hypocrisy of all`、`however illogical that might appear at first glance`、`this diffidence`、`too glib to be a satisfying explanation`、`rather than a willingness to let it all hang out`
- **表达方式**：给 explain 加上引号，标记它不是解释而是托词；diffidence（缺乏自信／拘谨）是古雅书面词，与 let it all hang out（把一切都晾出来）的口语成语同段相撞；tension and embarrassment and awkwardness and bad feelings 靠 and 连缀不断句，模仿「顾虑一层叠一层」的心理节奏。
- **为什么这样写**：这是本章从「现象描述」切到「机制解释」的门槛。作者先拦掉最容易的解释（人就是礼貌），理由是它会终止提问：如果礼貌只是性格，它本身还需要被解释。于是提出问题升级为「演化为何装配出这些感受」——由此进入下文那套协调博弈的机制。这是全书方法论在末章的一次公开亮相：不接受「常识」作为终点。

> **原句 8:** "That includes our informal social relationships, which are held together by a compact of conventions.17 The conventions are idealizations, verging on fictions. Yet in a strict logical sense they are necessary for the coordination equilibria we enjoy, and undermining them with contrary indications can poison that coordination. We often keep the knowledge that would undermine a social convention private, not because of some uptight fuddy-duddy inhibition, but for rational, indeed mathematically provable, reasons."

- **中文理解**：这包括我们的非正式社会关系——它们由一纸惯例的契约维系。这些惯例是理想化，近乎虚构。但严格按逻辑来说，它们正是我们享有的那些协调均衡所需要的，用反证去动摇它们会毒化那个协调。我们常常把会动摇某个社会惯例的知识留在私下，不是出于某种古板的、老学究式的压抑，而是出于理性的、确实是数学上可证明的理由。
- **句子结构**：首句 That includes our informal social relationships, which are held together by a compact of conventions 用 which 从句给出机制；第二句 The conventions are idealizations, verging on fictions 短句下定义，现在分词短语补充；第三句 Yet in a strict logical sense they are necessary for…, and undermining them with contrary indications can poison that coordination 以动名词短语作第二个主语；末句 We often keep the knowledge that would undermine a social convention private, not because of…, but for rational, indeed mathematically provable, reasons 用 not… but… 对举，indeed 插在两个形容词之间加重。（⚠️ 引语里 conventions 后的「17」是提取件带出的尾注上标。）
- **关键词**：`a compact of conventions`、`idealizations, verging on fictions`、`undermining them with contrary indications can poison that coordination`、`uptight fuddy-duddy inhibition`、`rational, indeed mathematically provable, reasons`
- **表达方式**：compact（契约）与 poison（毒化）分别来自法律与毒物语域，两个隐喻把「惯例」写成脆弱但具约束力的协议；fuddy-duddy（守旧古怪的人）是俚语，紧接 mathematically provable 的学术语域，反差本身即论点——被嘲为迂腐的克制其实有证明。
- **为什么这样写**：这是全章的承重墙：前八章反复讲共同知识如何让协调成为可能，末章需要说明它的反面为什么也理性——答案就在此：协调依赖的不是真相，而是不被反证破坏的假设。idealizations, verging on fictions 一句同时回收书内 Chapter 1 的「惯例不是虚构而是非物理现实」：那里说共同知识能造出非物理现实，这里补上「因此它也可以被拆穿摧毁」。数学可证明的说法本章并未给出，见可质疑处 1。

> **原句 9:** "We have private knowledge about our own minds: we know we have illicit desires. As soon as it dawns on us that we are not unique, we also have reciprocal knowledge: most people realize that most people have illicit desires. Yet voicing one of these desires aloud, generating the common knowledge in which each of us knows the other knows of the desire, and knows that, ad infinitum, is no small matter. You would be ill-advised to tell your boss that you have mused about killing him, or to inform either your spouse or a colleague that you daydream about sleeping with the latter, even if those possibilities may have been privately suspected. That is because the announcement would pollute the pool of assumptions that allow you to get along."

- **中文理解**：我们对自心有一份私人知识：我们知道自己有不该有的欲望。一旦意识到自己并不独特，我们也就有了相互知识：多数人意识到多数人都有不该有的欲望。然而把其中一种欲望说出口，制造出那种「人人都知道对方知道、还知道自己知道，无穷无尽」的共同知识，就不是小事了。你不该去告诉老板你琢磨过杀他，也不该告诉你配偶或某位同事你幻想过与后者睡觉——哪怕这些可能早已被私下猜到了。因为那声宣告会污染你们得以相处的那池假设。
- **句子结构**：We have private knowledge about our own minds: we know we have illicit desires 用冒号把术语与内容对接；第二句 As soon as it dawns on us that we are not unique, we also have reciprocal knowledge… 完成从私人到相互的升级；第三句是主语极长的 gerund 句 voicing one of these desires aloud, generating the common knowledge in which…, is no small matter，破折式递进 and knows that, ad infinitum 把递归压进动词链；You would be ill-advised to tell…, or to inform… 虚拟语气给两条禁令；末句 That is because the announcement would pollute the pool of assumptions that allow you to get along 收在因果句。
- **关键词**：`private knowledge about our own minds`、`reciprocal knowledge`、`each of us knows the other knows of the desire`、`ad infinitum`、`pollute the pool of assumptions that allow you to get along`
- **表达方式**：三层术语（private／reciprocal／common knowledge）在一段内依次登场，这是全书定义系统的最后一次完整演练；pollute the pool 用水源隐喻写「共同前提」，把说出口写成投毒而非陈述；ill-advised 的委婉与 have mused about killing him 的内容形成语域反差。
- **为什么这样写**：作者把前八章最抽象的概念阶梯（私人→相互→共同）放到一个极端场景（杀意与出轨幻想）里做终检：内容早已是相互知识，唯独不是共同知识——而正是这一层之差决定了关系能不能继续。句子在递归处用 ad infinitum 收掉，呼应书内 Chapter 2 那道「无限漩涡」的难题：末章用同一件武器反着切——这次无限层不是协调的条件，而是不协调的条件。

> **原句 10:** "All these trains of thought are exercises of recursive mentalizing, the cognitive talent that underlies common knowledge. The power of cognition to take its own outputs and feed them back into more cognition is a theme that has run through all my books, and it fills me with awe even after decades of pondering human intelligence. It underlies the vast expressive power of language, its ability to convey ideas from nursery rhymes to metaphysics.25 It explains how human intelligence, having evolved to reason about survival and reproduction, can be extended to reason about science, philosophy, and mathematics.26 It explains how human progress is possible, when people rethink their norms and institutions.27 And it implies that rationality itself is limitless: even when an application of rationality is flawed, we can step back, rationally analyze how we are deploying our rationality, and devise a higher-order rationality that subsumes it.28 It’s what’s most special about our kind: we not only have thoughts, but have thoughts about our thoughts, and thoughts about our thoughts about our thoughts."

- **中文理解**：所有这一条条思路都是递归心智化的操演——那正是共同知识所依托的认知才能。认知把自己的输出重新喂回更多认知的能力，是一个贯穿我全部著作的主题；即便几十年反复思量人类智能之后，它仍令我心生敬畏。它支撑着语言 vast 的 expressive power——从童谣到形而上学的表达能力；它解释了为生存与繁衍而演化出的人类智能，如何能被延展去推理科学、哲学与数学；它解释了当人重新思考自己的规范与制度时，进步何以可能；它还意味着理性本身没有边界：即便某次理性的应用出了错，我们仍能退后一步，理性地分析自己如何使用理性，并设计出能把它收编进去的高阶理性。这正是我们这一物种最特别之处：我们不仅有思想，还有关于思想的思想，以及关于思想的思想的思想。
- **句子结构**：首句 All these trains of thought are exercises of recursive mentalizing, the cognitive talent that underlies common knowledge 用同位语给术语下定义；第二句 The power of cognition to take its own outputs and feed them back into more cognition is a theme that has run through all my books… 主语极长、and it fills me with awe 承接；随后 It underlies… It explains… It explains… And it implies that… 四句同构排比作为收束的举证；rationality itself is limitless 后用冒号展开 step back, rationally analyze…, and devise a higher-order rationality that subsumes it 的三级递升；全书最后一句 we not only have thoughts, but have thoughts about our thoughts, and thoughts about our thoughts about our thoughts 用 not only…but…and… 把递归演成句法。（⚠️ 引语里 metaphysics 后的「25」、mathematics 后的「26」、institutions 后的「27」、subsumes it 后的「28」是提取件带出的尾注上标。）
- **关键词**：`recursive mentalizing`、`take its own outputs and feed them back into more cognition`、`a theme that has run through all my books`、`a higher-order rationality that subsumes it`、`thoughts about our thoughts about our thoughts`
- **表达方式**：全书终点落在一个句法自指的修辞上——末句用嵌套的 of our thoughts 直接演出它所说的能力，与书内 Chapter 1 开篇那句「他知道她知道她知道」的嵌套遥成镜像；higher-order rationality（高阶理性）是本章给「理性虚伪」的正式名分：虚伪不是理性的失败，而是理性对自身的再处理。
- **为什么这样写**：末章的收束不靠新证据，而靠把全章的具体建议（别把每句真心话说出口）升格成全书的世界观：递归心智化既解释了共同知识的力量，也解释了为什么必须限制它。作者在此明说这是「贯穿我全部著作的主题」，等于给全书一个跨书署名。最后一句把整本书的定义装置交给读者带走——这也是可质疑处 2 的位置：能力越强，「哪些该留在无限层之外」就越需要一个判据。

## 一句话总结

末章把全书的定义装置反过来用：共同知识让人能一起做成大事，也正因如此，人必须设法拦住它——卡特一句无害的真话就足以输掉选举，激进诚实到头来成了最大的虚伪，而维系关系的惯例是近乎虚构的理想化，一说破就毒化协调；于是全书落在「我们不仅有思想，还有关于思想的思想」上：理性虚伪是一种高阶理性。
