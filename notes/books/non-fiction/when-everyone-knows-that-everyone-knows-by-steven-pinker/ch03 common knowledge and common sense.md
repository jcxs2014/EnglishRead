# 03. Chapter 2: Common Knowledge and Common Sense（共同知识与常识）

## 概览

- **出处**：*When Everyone Knows That Everyone Knows…*，Steven Pinker 著，Scribner 2025 初版（本书第二正文章，书内 Chapter 2）
- **作者**：Steven Pinker，本书自述身份为认知科学家
- **章节定位**：承上一章（书内 Chapter 1，本目录 `ch02`）的定义与应用清单，本章转入「逻辑 vs 常识」：以三道谜题（三个逻辑学家进酒吧、菠菜牙缝、硬邮件博弈）与一条定理（Aumann 同意定理）证明共同知识的推论如何反直觉，再依次处理其三条冒犯常识的结论；末段回扣上一章埋下的概念隐喻线索（ARGUMENT IS WAR）
- **字符数**：59,462（提取件 `text/ch03_chapter_2_common_knowledge_and_common_sense.txt` 全文实测）
- **一句话主旨**：共同知识的逻辑三处冒犯常识——能从别人的无知推出事实、任何有限层「他知道她知道」都不够用、理性人不能达成「保留分歧」；作者主张这些不是「球形奶牛」，而是关于人类境况的洞见

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：共同知识有自己的逻辑，这套逻辑与常识相冲突；接受它，就得接受三件事——他人的无知携带信息、协调永远「差一层确认」、理性讨论的终局是唯一结论而非彼此让步。

- **证据链**：

| 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 三个逻辑学家进酒吧「人人都要啤酒吗」谜题 | 研究（逻辑谜题） | 从「某人不知道」可以推出世界的状态——反直觉推论的最小演示 |
| 菠菜牙缝晚餐谜题（十二位心理学家的派对版） | 研究（逻辑谜题） | 公开宣告即使不增信息也能触发多轮推理——无限层的必要性 |
| 硬邮件博弈（电子确认无限回环）与飞行员协同进攻的概率演算 | 研究 + 数据 | 「几乎共同知识」不够：追加确认把成功率从 90 拉低到 81 |
| 阿曼同意定理（一九七六年，诺奖数学家）与贝叶斯更新 | 研究（定理） | 共享后验为共同知识时，理性人不能保留分歧 |
| 白板上数学家互相翻转立场、诚实理财顾问「别想战胜市场」 | 案例 | 定理的两条推论在现实中确有对应场景 |
| 平等先验漏洞与考恩、汉森「人人认为自己更理性」 | 研究 + 案例 | 分歧的真正来源是自尊先验，而非证据 |
| 拉考夫与约翰逊「争论即战争」隐喻 + 作者自承「数十年来觉得牵强」 | 研究 + 自述 | 落点：理性争论应更像跳舞而非战争 |

- **论证脉络**：设问「共同知识难道不是常识吗」→ 列出三条冒犯常识的结论 → 「球形奶牛」自嘲预防反驳 → 三道谜题逐一展开（酒吧／菠菜牙缝／硬邮件）→ 概率演算给「多一层确认反而更糟」定量 → 出路：把知识降级为共同 p-信念 → 现实对应（计算机握手、道别、让行过马路）→ Aumann 定理入场（贝叶斯先验后验）→ 「不能保留分歧」→ 反事实：真实讨论从不收敛 → 漏洞：先验不平等 → 修补：分歧源于自尊 → 收束到隐喻系统（争论即战争 → 应如跳舞）

- **可质疑处**：
  1. **「球形奶牛」的自辩只给了意愿没给判据**：作者预告三条结论「都不是球形奶牛」，但每条落地时用的仍是「现实里有类似场景」的类比（白板数学、理财建议），没有给出可失败的检验——若某个结论在现实中找不到对应，论证如何收回？
  2. **谜题的改编抹掉了原版的暴力前提**：菠菜牙缝章里作者坦承原版（苏丹令丈夫杀不忠之妻）冒犯当代多数读者并改写成晚餐派对——但谜题的推理只依赖「公开宣告 + 无人先动」，与「杀死」换成「离席」的后果严重性其实无关；作者未说明为何认为改写保真。
  3. **Aumann 定理依赖「共享后验」这一几乎不存在的前提**：定理的强结论只在双方愿意报出真实数字时成立；章内后半已承认现实中人不翻转立场，但没有讨论一个显然的可能：当「不承认自己是错的」本身是更高收益策略时，定理描述的对象（理性讨论者）已被抽空。

## 选择性精读

> **原句 1:** "Isn’t common knowledge common knowledge? Doesn’t everyone know (and know that they know) that people care about who knows what? In this chapter we’ll see that in fact the logic of common knowledge is often an insult to common sense."

- **中文理解**：共同知识难道不就是常识吗？谁不知道「人们在意谁知道什么」呢——可本章要表明，共同知识的逻辑常常是对常识的一记羞辱。
- **句子结构**：两个反问句并列起头（Isn't…? Doesn't everyone know…?），第二问的括号内插入 and know that they know 是递归的两次方；第三句 In this chapter we'll see that… 才给出立场。
- **关键词**：`an insult to common sense`、`know that they know`
- **表达方式**：用读者的口头禅当靶心（章题 Common Sense 与书名 Common Knowledge 对撞），insult 一词把逻辑写成挑衅。
- **为什么这样写**：章题即整章策略——上一章让读者「懂了」概念，本章要让读者「被概念冒犯」。开场先复述读者自信的语气，才能把后三道谜题的惊讶一次攒足。

> **原句 2:** "The first is that you can deduce facts about the world from what people don’t know. The second is that two people really do need to think an infinite vortex of “I know that he knows that I know that he knows…” thoughts to be sure they will coordinate their plans; two or three, or any finite number, aren’t enough. The third is that rational agents with the same understanding of the world, after examining evidence and sharing their assessments, must arrive at the same conclusion: they cannot agree to disagree."

- **中文理解**：第一条：你能从人们不知道的东西里推出世界的状态。第二条：两个人要确信能协调计划，真得在脑中跑完「我知道他知道我知道他知道……」的无限漩涡，两三层或任何有限层都不够。第三条：对世界有同样理解、检验证据又交换了评估的理性行动者必须抵达同一结论——他们不能同意保留分歧。
- **句子结构**：The first is that… The second is that… The third is that… 三个同构主系表句排比；第二条内部用分号挂上「有限层不够」的补刀，第三条以冒号后短语收束出 must arrive at the same conclusion。
- **关键词**：`deduce facts about the world from what people don't know`、`an infinite vortex`、`they cannot agree to disagree`
- **表达方式**：vortex（漩涡）把递归写成会吸人的深渊；agree to disagree（保留分歧）是英语固定成语，被定理正面否定。
- **为什么这样写**：这是全章的合同：三条「冒犯」按从易到难排——谜题一抓第一条，谜题二三抓第二条，Aumann 定理抓第三条。序码式排比也让后文每个板块可被读者索引。

> **原句 3:** "These conclusions may sound preposterous, and to be sure, the proofs depend on idealizations of reasoners which are less than fully realistic. Scientists joke about the theoretical physicist hired by a farmer to increase milk yields who tells him, “First, assume a spherical cow…” I will suggest that while these findings about common knowledge may not literally apply to particular situations in everyday life, they are not spherical cows: each offers insight into the human condition."

- **中文理解**：这些结论听来荒唐——诚然，证明依赖对推理者的理想化，不够真实。科学界有个笑话：农场主请理论物理学家提高产奶量，对方说「先假设有一头球形奶牛……」；作者要说的是，共同知识的这些发现并非球形奶牛，每一条都关乎人类境况。
- **句子结构**：主句 These conclusions may sound preposterous, and to be sure… 先让步；笑话句用 who 从句套出物理学家的引语（内层再嵌祈使句）；I will suggest that… 长句以冒号收束出 each offers insight into the human condition。
- **关键词**：`idealizations of reasoners`、`a spherical cow`、`not spherical cows`
- **表达方式**：用一个笑话承担方法论声明——spherical cow 是理论物理「过度简化」的自嘲成语，作者先自认过度简化、再宣布自己已补足现实性。
- **为什么这样写**：这是本章的预防性反驳：三道谜题全部以理想化推理者为前提，读者必然怀疑「这跟我有什么关系」。作者抢先把最强怀疑做成笑点，再用一句 not spherical cows 把举证责任接回自己身上——也由此留下可质疑处 1。

> **原句 4:** "How did they figure it out? When the chair made her announcement, she wasn’t telling anyone anything they didn’t already know, because they could already see that at least one person had spinach in their teeth. But as with the small child and the emperor, the fact that she uttered those words in the presence of the other diners changed their knowledge."

- **中文理解**：他们怎么推断出来的？主持人开口时并没告诉任何人他们本来不知道的事——因为每人早就能看见至少有人牙塞菜叶；但正如小孩与皇帝，她在满桌人面前说出那句话这件事本身改变了众人的知识。
- **句子结构**：疑问短句起头，答句用 because 从句给理由（they could already see that…），第三句主语是 the fact that she uttered those words… 的名词性从句。
- **关键词**：`she wasn't telling anyone anything they didn't already know`、`changed their knowledge`
- **表达方式**：句式几乎照搬上一章的皇帝句——同一套「内容不增／知识改变」的两分在第二个故事里复现，形成术语的二次印证。
- **为什么这样写**：菠菜牙缝谜题是全章的枢纽：它同时演示三条「冒犯」中的第一、第二条。让读者先看到「这不是新信息」，才能体会「公开性才是变量」——作者的章节级重复是有意的教学手段。

> **原句 5:** "Crucially, the telltale announcement could not be private knowledge. Suppose the chair had walked around the table whispering into each psychologist’s ear that at least one of them had spinach in their teeth. Suppose as well that each one thought he or she was the only one being vouchsafed that fact and assumed that the others were privy to different gossip."

- **中文理解**：关键在于，那个要命的宣告绝不能是私人知识：假如主持人绕桌挨个耳语，悄悄告诉每人「你们中至少有人牙上有菜叶」，且每个人都以为自己是被单独告知的那个、别人听到的是别的——那么这条宣告就还停在私人知识层，接不成「人人知道人人知道」。
- **句子结构**：首句 Crucially, the telltale announcement could not be private knowledge 单句立判断；随后两个 Suppose… 虚拟条件句递进铺出反事实场景（耳语 → 各自以为独享），本块引语止于第二层假设，后续推演在引语之外的下文展开。
- **关键词**：`the telltale announcement could not be private knowledge`、`whispering into each psychologist's ear`、`being vouchsafed that fact`
- **表达方式**：telltale（告密的）把宣告拟作证人；vouchsafed（赐予、透露）是古雅及物动词，与前文的耳语画面构成语域反差。
- **为什么这样写**：这是全章论证最细的一刀：谜题的答案不能靠「大家本来就看见」，必须靠「宣告是公开的」。作者把私人化版本拆给读者看——先验层级的每抽走一层，推理立刻塌掉，「无限递归必要性」由此变成可触摸的东西。

> **原句 6:** "Paradoxically, their desire for an open-ended exchange of confirmations left them less likely to coordinate on their top choice rather than more likely. Without common knowledge—each of them perceiving that the other one got the message while knowing that the other knew they knew, and so on—no number of message volleys would give them the confidence they need to rule out the chance of a miscoordination."

- **中文理解**：自相矛盾的是，他们对无尽确认回环的渴求，反而让他们更难而非更可能按首选协调；没有共同知识——每人看见对方收到信息、又知道对方知道自己知道、如此无尽——发多少轮消息都给不了他们排除误配所需的信心。
- **句子结构**：第一句 Paradoxically 副词提领，主干 their desire… left them less likely… rather than more likely；第二句 Without common knowledge 起始，破折号内插入其定义（each of them perceiving… while knowing… and so on），主句 no number of message volleys would give them… 否定数量。
- **关键词**：`Paradoxically`、`no number of message volleys`、`rule out the chance of a miscoordination`
- **表达方式**：volley（齐射／回球）把消息写成往来球，rule out 是侦探成语；否定句式 no number of… 直接推翻「多发几条总没坏处」的常识。
- **为什么这样写**：硬邮件博弈的要点全在这句：不是「确认不够多」，是「再多也不够」。作者用平行结构的反衬（less likely… rather than more likely）把这一点钉进句法——这是第二条「冒犯」（有限层不够）的最强呈现。

> **原句 7:** "This dilemma, called the Electronic Mail game (after the original parable by the economist Ariel Rubinstein in 1989), is the most widely known paradox about common knowledge after the spinach-in-teeth puzzle.5 It’s another affront to our common sense about common knowledge. A frequently asked question about common knowledge is: “So you’re saying it has to be ‘He knows that she knows that he knows that she knows’… ad infinitum? Really? Isn’t a whole bunch of ‘He knows that she knows’ enough to ensure coordination between them?” The answer from the Electronic Mail game is: “Yes, really. A whole bunch—the state called ‘almost common knowledge’—isn’t enough.”"

- **中文理解**：这个两难叫「电子邮件博弈」，得名于经济学家鲁宾斯坦 1989 年的原始寓言；它是仅次于「牙上菜叶」谜题的、关于共同知识最有名的悖论，也是对常识的又一记冒犯。有人常问：照你的意思，真非得「他知道她知道他知道她知道……」无穷无尽才行吗？一大批「他知道她知道」难道还不足以保证两人协调？博弈的回答是：是的，真得无穷——一大批（即所谓「几乎共同知识」）并不够。
- **句子结构**：首句主干 This dilemma, called…（过去分词短语作插入语交代命名史与年份）, is the most widely known paradox… after… 比较级排次；随后一问一答：问句以 So you're saying… ad infinitum? 起头、内层用单层直引号套住层层 he knows that she knows，答句 Yes, really 短促收束，破折号内插入术语 the state called 'almost common knowledge'。
- **关键词**：`Electronic Mail game`、`parable`、`the most widely known paradox`、`almost common knowledge`
- **表达方式**：parable（寓言）一词把博弈论模型降格为故事、又把故事升格为模型——作者全章都在做这种双向翻译；结尾把读者的反驳压缩成一句 Yes, really. 再补 isn't enough. 两记短锤。
- **为什么这样写**：命名的交代是给读者的索引牌：想深挖的人可按 Rubinstein 1989 去查原始文献；after the spinach-in-teeth puzzle 则在给两条「冒犯」排座次。一问一答的写法是给全章最反直觉的结论装上护栏——先把读者心里的抗议完整喊出来（一大批难道不够吗），再用最短的句长否定它，形式即论点。

> **原句 8:** "We now have the vocabulary to translate Aumann’s Agreement theorem into everyday English. If two rational people start out with the same background assumptions about a hypothesis (the same priors), and they each look at different evidence and share their degree of confidence that the hypothesis is true (that is, their posteriors are common knowledge), then they cannot agree to disagree (those posteriors must be the same)."

- **中文理解**：现在我们有词汇把 Aumann 同意定理翻成日常英语：若两个理性的人对某假设带着同样的背景假设（同样先验）出发，各自查看不同的证据并分享自己对假设为真的信心程度（即后验成为共同知识），他们就不能同意保留分歧。
- **句子结构**：We now have the vocabulary to translate… into everyday English 宣告式主句；定理本体是 If two rational people start out…, and they each look… and share…, then they cannot… 的一长串条件—并列—结果句，括号两次挂注术语（same priors／posteriors are common knowledge）。
- **关键词**：`the same priors`、`posteriors are common knowledge`、`cannot agree to disagree`
- **表达方式**：translate into everyday English 是作者方法论自白——他相信形式定理可无损改写为白话；括号注是「术语—白话」双轨制的接口。
- **为什么这样写**：全章的智力高峰在此：Aumann 定理原文是测度论语言，作者用一句 if-and-then 白话把它交还给读者。两个括号不是装饰——它们标记「白话里必须锁死的技术项」：先验相同 + 后验共同知识，缺一结论就散。

> **原句 9:** "That’s right: no leaving it at that, splitting the difference, acknowledging opposing views, allowing for different perspectives, respectfully disagreeing, or letting a thousand flowers bloom. It all sounds intolerant and dogmatic and authoritarian, an insult to the ideal of civility. But as we shall see, the implications of the theorem are rather enlightened, more along the lines of: facts don’t care about your feelings, it’s bigger than all of us, and it’s not about being right, it’s about getting it right.20"

- **中文理解**：没听错：不许「就这样吧」，不许各让一步、不许承认对方观点、不许考虑视角差异、不许礼貌地保留分歧、不许「百花齐放」——这一切听来不宽容、教条、威权，是对礼让理想的羞辱；但如我们所见，定理的含义其实颇为开明，更接近这几句：事实不在乎你的感受，这事比我们所有人都大，重点不是「我对」而是「把它弄对」。
- **句子结构**：That's right 短感叹起头，随后一串 no + 动名词的排比否定（leaving／splitting／acknowledging／allowing／disagreeing／letting）；It all sounds… 三形容词连叠；But as we shall see 转折后 more along the lines of 再挂三条民间格言。
- **关键词**：`splitting the difference`、`respectfully disagreeing`、`letting a thousand flowers bloom`、`facts don't care about your feelings`
- **表达方式**：排比否定逐项拆掉现代礼貌的固定说法；`letting a thousand flowers bloom`（百花齐放）本是政治口号，被挪来形容「观点市场」；末三句全是网络时代的俗语格言。
- **为什么这样写**：定理最招人恨之处正是它的「不宽容」——作者的修辞策略是把仇恨值全部先列出来（六个不许），再一句「其实颇为开明」翻盘。三个格言的口语色彩是刻意的：把「独断」的定理讲成「常识的新外衣」。

> **原句 10:** "But since reading Aumann’s explanation of how common knowledge can guide rational discussants toward consensus, and the weaving, twisting path they take to get there, I appreciate Lakoff and Johnson’s insight: rational argument really should be more like a dance than a war."

- **中文理解**：但自从我读到 Aumann 解释共同知识如何引导理性讨论者走向共识、以及他们抵达共识所走的那条迂回扭缠的路径，我才领会拉考夫与约翰逊的洞见：理性的争论，真该更像一场跳舞，而不是一场战争。
- **句子结构**：But since reading Aumann's explanation of how… and the weaving, twisting path they take to get there 长介词短语起头（内嵌 how 从句与后置定语），主句 I appreciate… 短小，冒号后收在理性争论真的更像跳舞而非战争上。
- **关键词**：`the weaving, twisting path`、`more like a dance than a war`
- **表达方式**：weaving／twisting 双现在分词头韵；章末用一对反义隐喻（战争／跳舞）替代全章的定理语言——这是作者对上一章埋线的回收。
- **为什么这样写**：全章以概念隐喻收尾是结构上的回环：上一章（书内 Chapter 1）末段列过 `ARGUMENT IS WAR` 及其例句，本章末句让这一隐喻成为定理的落点——讨论不是击败对方而是共同试探。作者甚至先自承数十年来觉得拉考夫牵强，再宣告被定理说服：把全章论证的结果写成一次自认。
