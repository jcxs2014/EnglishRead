# 金句精选

## 关于家庭与父母

> "the change from Magic Mommy to the dragon that replaced her"

母亲从"魔法妈妈"变成了"龙"——最简洁的一句话，点明了母女关系的变质。（ch40）

---

> "You give up your individuality and are subjected to the whole because the combination of you all is far greater than any individual."

在合唱团舞蹈中，叙述者找到了在家庭中永远找不到的归属感——放弃个性、融入整体的幸福感。（ch23）

---

> "I have to carry her guilt and shame now as obviously she isn't carrying it herself."

为母亲承担羞耻——这是叙述者在整个 memoir 中反复经历的模式。（ch30）

---

> "Mother tells me that Daddy is in the hospital and we are going to visit. There's no asking me if I want to go."

母亲从不询问——叙述者的自主权从一开始就被剥夺。（ch23）

---

> "I can't cry. I can't feel anything. Or, I feel everything all at once. Loss, regret, anger, humiliation, shame—there isn't a negative emotion I don't have and they're all fighting to get out, so they trample each other in the doorway and die."

对 grief 最精确的描述——所有情绪都想出来，却相互踩踏而死。（ch26）

---

## 关于父亲

> "You're the guy with the rifle, right? You're the guy who was gonna drive through the front door."

叙述者对父亲最直接的质问——那个持枪威胁母亲的人。（ch25）

---

> "What a rotten kid to turn your face away from your father when he's trying so hard."

叙述者对自己在父亲最后时刻转向墙壁的内疚——这是全书最令人心碎的内疚感。（ch25）

---

> "Gone is the lost girl traumatized by her father's death and fearing change."

伦敦两年后，叙述者终于能够面对父亲的死亡和变化。（ch36）

---

> "My father is dead and I don't know what the fuck is going on!"

混乱中的唯一清醒——父亲去世后叙述者对世界的根本困惑。（ch26）

---

## 关于舞蹈

> "There is a flow, a vastness, a connectedness about her movement that is mind-expanding."

Michele 的舞蹈彻底打开了叙述者的眼界——这是她从未体验过的舞蹈方式。（ch37）

---

> "The whole point is the uniformity, all the dancers moving as one. It's beautiful when you see sixteen or twenty-four or thirty-two girls in complete unison."

芭蕾舞的 uniformity 是叙述者在混乱家庭中寻找的避难所。（ch23）

---

> "I can fly, I really can fly."

叙述者对自己舞蹈能力的信念——在跳跃中她超越了生活的痛苦。（ch37）

---

> "Ballet has become my refuge from the world and reality."

在父亲的死亡和母亲的疯狂中，舞蹈成了唯一的避难所。（ch27）

---

## 关于性与爱情

> "I feel like she sees through me into my soul and I am naked."

在 Michele 面前，叙述者感到被完全看穿、赤裸。（ch37）

---

> "The Earth quietly tilts on its axis and slows rotation."

与 Michele 的第一个吻——整个世界都因此改变了。（ch42）

---

> "My nervous system just got plugged in and properly connected for the first time in my life."

与 Michele 的性经验——这是叙述者第一次感到"正确连接"。（ch42）

---

> "I am not a lesbian! ... It's just about her."

叙述者对 LGBTQ 身份的挣扎——她无法接受自己是"女同性恋"，但也无法否认对 Michele 的感情。（ch42）

---

## 关于自我与成长

> "I am twenty-three years old, five-foot-six, and weigh 110 pounds. I am a solid, strong, long-muscled female with a will of iron and a determination to succeed."

全 memoir 的最后一句话——这是叙述者对自己的最终定义：坚强、有决心、不关心如何，只关心成功。（ch43）

---

> "I don't care how or even at what—I just know I will."

简洁而有力的宣言——23岁的叙述者不知道自己要什么，但知道自己会得到它。（ch43）

---

> "Keep passing the open window."

从 The World According to Garp 引用的自杀隐喻——这是叙述者处理自杀想法的方式：不断经过那扇窗户，选择活下去。（ch40）

---

> "I understand now the fear from people who have never been out of their town or their region or state."

伦敦之行让叙述者理解了偏狭的根源是无知。（ch35）

---

> "Just ride the wind when it blows."

叙述者学到的关于人生的智慧——有时候 synchronicity 会带你到你需要去的地方。（ch39）

---

## 关于大麻与逃避

> "Truth is, pot takes away my anxieties, or any feelings, really."

大麻成了叙述者处理无法承受的痛苦的方式。（ch40）

---

> "Here is where it seems relevant to reveal that I am now stoned every minute of my life and will be for the next twenty or so years."

这是全 memoir 最令人震惊的告白之一——大麻将占据叙述者接下来二十年的生活。（ch40）

---

## 关于纽约与自由

> "I know she holds a torch aloft to light my way."

自由女神像的火炬照亮了叙述者的道路。（ch43）

---

> "I yearn, I beg, I will myself to breathe free."

抵达纽约港时，叙述者对自由的渴望达到了顶峰。（ch43）

---

> "There are a lot of ways a human being needs to be fed—physically, emotionally, mentally, and spiritually."

叙述者理解到人类需要多维度的滋养——而她一直在挨饿。（ch43）
