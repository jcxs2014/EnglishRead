### ①

> **原句 1:** It is as easy to decide to be a writer as to decide to have a piece of cake, but then you have to do it. I moved into that beautiful apartment as I was in my final semester of my undergraduate education at San Francisco State University. It was an intense spring: I was working to support myself and taking nineteen units of classes, helped along by a handful of prescription speed in the form of little yellow pills that had been the only gift given me by the man I'd been dating before I moved.

**中文理解**：决定成为作家就像决定吃一块蛋糕一样容易，但然后你必须去做。我在旧金山州立大学本科最后一个学期搬进了那那是一个紧张的春天：我在工作养活自己，同时修了19个学分的课程，靠一把处方兴奋剂（黄色小药片）来帮助，那是我搬家前约会男人给我的唯一礼物。Solnit 用"as easy to decide to be a writer as to decide to have a piece of cake"将"决定"与"做"区分开来——决定容易，执行难。

**关键词**：**undergraduate**（本科生）、**prescription speed**（处方兴奋剂）。"prescription speed"是核心词——它暗示一种"化学帮助"：不是天生的才能，而是药物的支持。

### ②

> **原句 2:** I graduated as I turned twenty, and then realized that the world and I were not ready for each other. ### ③

### ⑩

> **原句 10:** One day I saw a work of art by Los Angeles artist Wallace Berman that captivated me. It was a grid repeating an image of a hand holding a transistor radio with images bursting out of the radio's speaker, a piece about pop culture and mysticism with a few Hebrew letters scattered on it. In my naïveté, I went to find the book that I assumed existed on this extraordinary creator. There wasn't one then, though there was a slender exhibition catalogue surveying his work. I didn't yet know that I would write that book, or a version of it, some years later. I picked Berman as the subject for my thesis, though it was unconventional for a journalism student to focus on something so far from the news and the realms of news. Berman had died in 1976, having destroyed the recording of the only interview known to have been conducted with him, so there was a lot to reconstruct from archives and interviews with members of his circle. The coincidences that led me to the museum that led me to the image that led me to the project make me grateful for my failure at opening wine bottles.

**中文理解**：有一天，我看到洛杉矶艺术家华莱士·伯曼的一件作品，它迷住了我。那是一幅重复图像的网格，一只手拿着晶体管收音机，图像从收音机扬声器中爆发出来，一件关于流行文化和神秘主义的作品，上面散布着几个希伯来字母。在我幼稚的时候，我去找那本我认为存在于这位非凡创作者的书。那时还没有，尽管有一本展览目录调查他的作品。我还不知道我会写那本书，或者几年后的一个版本。我选择伯曼作为我的论文主题，尽管对于新闻学学生来说，专注于远离新闻和新闻领域的东西是不合常规的。伯曼于1976年去世，破坏了对他的唯一已知采访的录音，所以有很多东西要从档案和他圈子成员的采访中重建。导致我进入博物馆、导致我看到图像、导致我进入项目的种种巧合，让我对开酒瓶的失败心存感激。Solnit 用"The coincidences that led me to the museum that led me to the image that led me to the project make me grateful for my failure at opening wine bottles"将"失败"重新定义为一种"幸运"——不是"开瓶失败导致找不到工作"，而是"开瓶失败导致找到更好的工作"。

**句子结构**：超长复合句，"One day I saw a work of art by Los Angeles artist Wallace Berman that captivated me" + "The coincidences that led me to the museum that led me to the image that led me to the project make me grateful for my failure at opening wine bottles"。

**关键词**：**captivated**（迷住）、**transistor radio**（晶体管收音机）、**mysticism**（神秘主义）、**slender**（纤细的）、**unconventional**（不合常规的）、**reconstruct**（重建）。"coincidences"是核心词——它暗示一种"偶然"：不是"计划"，而是"巧合"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"The coincidences that led me to the museum that led me to the image that led me to the project"的链式结构，将"发现"定义为一种"偶然链"——不是"单一的偶然"，而是"一连串的偶然"。"failure at opening wine bottles"的幽默表述，将"失败"重新定义为一种"幸运"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"偶然与命运"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"发现"不是"计划"的，而是"偶然"的；不是"寻找"，而是"遇见"。

## 一句话总结

Solnit 通过博物馆工作的经历，探讨了"成为作家"的实际过程——不是天才的灵光一现，而是偶然的巧合、一步一步的积累；而"失败"（开瓶失败）导致"发现"（Berman的作品），最终成为她第一本书的主题。
