# 31. Audibility, Credibility, Consequence · Chapter 5（可听性、可信度、后果·第五章）

## 概览

- **出处**：*Recollections of My Nonexistence* (Viking, 2020)，Part VIII "Audibility, Credibility, Consequence" 第五章
- **作者**：Rebecca Solnit
- **章节定位**：全书第 31 章；从#MeToo运动转向"声音"的理论——"可听性"（audibility）、"可信度"（credibility）、"后果性"（consequence）三要素
- **字符数**：5945
- **一句话主旨**：以"从我写《男人向我解释事情》那天早上起，十年过去了，几十篇女权主义散文之后，我意识到我并不是在谈论针对女性的暴力"开篇，探讨"声音"的三个维度——"可听性"、"可信度"、"后果性"——以及性别暴力如何根植于这三者的缺失

### 论证脉络

### 核心金句

> "It took me ten years and dozens of feminist essays from that morning I wrote 'Men Explain Things to Me' to realize that I was not talking and writing, after all, about violence against women, though I was reading about it incessantly. I was writing about what it means not to have a voice and making the case for a redistribution of that vital power."

> "There are three key things that matter in having a voice: audibility, credibility, and consequence."

> "Audibility means that you can be heard, that you have not been pressed into silence or kept out of the arenas in which you can speak or write..."

> "Credibility means that when you get into those arenas, people are willing to believe you..."

> "To be a person of consequence is to matter. If you matter, you have rights, and your words serve those rights and give you the power to bear witness, make agreements, set boundaries."

> "Gender violence is made possible by this lack of audibility, credibility, and consequence."

### ④

> **原句 4:** Credibility means that when you get into those arenas, people are willing to believe you, by which I don't mean that women never lie, but that stories should be measured on their own terms and context, rather than patriarchy's insistence that women are categorically unqualified to speak, emotional rather than rational, vindictive, incoherent, delusional, manipulative, unfit to be heeded—those things often shouted over a woman in the process of saying something challenging (though now death threats are used as a shortcut, and some of those threats are carried out, notably with women who leave their abusers, because silencing can be conversational or it can be premeditated murder).

**中文理解**：可信度意味着当你进入那些舞台时，人们愿意相信你，我不是说女人从不撒谎，而是故事应该按照自己的条款和背景来衡量，而不是父权制坚持认为女性绝对没有资格说话、情绪化而非理性、报复性强、语无伦次、妄想、操纵性、不值得注意——这些往往在一个女人说一些有挑战性的话时对她大喊（尽管现在死亡威胁被用作捷径，其中一些威胁被实施，特别是离开施虐者的女性，因为沉默可以是对话性的，也可以是预谋谋杀）。Solnit 用"categorically unqualified to speak"将"偏见"定义为一种"绝对"：不是"个别"，而是"绝对"；不是"相对"，而是"普遍"。

**句子结构**：超长复合句，"Credibility means that when you get into those arenas, people are willing to believe you, by which I don't mean that women never lie, but that stories should be measured on their own terms and context..."

**关键词**：**credibility**（可信度）是核心词——它暗示一种"可信"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"categorically unqualified to speak, emotional rather than rational, vindictive, incoherent, delusional, manipulative, unfit to be heeded"的列举，将"偏见"定义为一种"全面"。

### ⑤

> **原句 5:** To be a person of consequence is to matter. If you matter, you have rights, and your words serve those rights and give you the power to bear witness, make agreements, set boundaries. If you have consequence, your words possess the authority to determine what does and does not happen to you, the power that underlies the concept of consent as part of equality and self-determination.

**中文理解**：成为一个有后果的人意味着重要。如果你重要，你就有权利，你的话语服务于这些权利，并给你作证、达成协议、设定边界的力量。如果你有后果性，你的话语拥有决定什么会发生在你身上什么不会的权威，这种权力作为平等和自决的一部分构成了同意概念的基础。Solnit 用"to be a person of consequence is to matter"将"后果性"定义为一种"重要性"：不是"不重要"，而是"重要"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"To be a person of consequence is to matter" + "If you matter, you have rights, and your words serve those rights and give you the power to bear witness, make agreements, set boundaries"。

**关键词**：**consequence**（后果性）是核心词——它暗示一种"后果"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"the power to bear witness, make agreements, set boundaries"的排比，将"权力"定义为一种"能力"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"后果性即重要性"主题的缩影。

### ⑥

> **原句 6:** Even legally women's words have lacked consequence: in only a few scattered places on earth could women vote before the twentieth century, and not so many decades ago, women rarely became lawyers and judges; I met a Texas woman whose mother was among the first women in their region to serve on a jury, and I was an adult when the first woman was appointed to the U.S. Supreme Court. Until a few decades ago, wives throughout much of the world, including the United States, lacked the right to make contracts and financial decisions or even to exercise jurisdiction over their own bodies that overrode their husbands' ability to do so; in some parts of the world, a wife is still property under the law, and others choose her husband.

**中文理解**：即使是法律上，女性的话语也缺乏后果：在二十世纪之前，地球上只有几个分散的地方女性可以投票；在几十年前，女性很少成为律师和法官；我遇到一位德克萨斯女性，她的母亲是该地区首批担任陪审员的女性之一；当第一位女性被任命为美国最高法院大法官时，我已经成年。直到几十年前，包括美国在内的世界大部分地区的妻子缺乏签订合同和财务决策的权利，甚至缺乏对自己身体的管辖权，以凌驾于丈夫这样做的能力之上；在世界上的一些地方，妻子在法律上仍然是财产，其他人选择她的丈夫。Solnit 用"women's words have lacked consequence"将"法律"定义为一种"排斥"：不是"平等"，而是"排斥"；不是"公正"，而是"偏见"。

**句子结构**：超长复合句，"Even legally women's words have lacked consequence: in only a few scattered places on earth could women vote before the twentieth century..."

**关键词**：**consequence**（后果）是核心词——它暗示一种"后果"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"I met a Texas woman whose mother was among the first women in their region to serve on a jury"的具体例子，将"历史"定义为一种"个人"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"法律即排斥"主题的缩影。

### ⑦

> **原句 7:** Women have been injured on all three fronts—as have men of color and nonwhite women doubly so. Not allowed to speak or punished for speaking or excluded from the arenas—courts, universities, legislatures, newsrooms—where decisions are made. Mocked or disbelieved or threatened if they do find a place in which to speak, and routinely categorized as inherently deceitful, spiteful, delusional, confused, or just unqualified. Or they speak up and it is no different than remaining silent; they have told their stories and nothing happens, because their rights and their capacity to bear witness don't matter, so their voices are just sounds that blow away on the wind.

**中文理解**：女性在这三个方面都受到了伤害——有色人种男性和非白人女性更是如此。不被允许说话或因说话而受到惩罚，或者被排除在做出决策的舞台——法院、大学、立法机构、新闻编辑室之外。如果他们真的找到了说话的地方，就会被嘲笑或怀疑或威胁，并且通常被归类为本质上狡诈、恶意、妄想、困惑或只是不合格。或者他们说出来，和保持沉默没有什么不同；他们讲述了他们的故事，但什么也没发生，因为他们的权利和作证能力不重要，所以他们的声音只是在风中吹散的声音。Solnit 用"injured on all three fronts"将"伤害"定义为一种"三重"：不是"单一"，而是"三重"；不是"简单"，而是"复杂"。

### ⑧

> **原句 8:** Gender violence is made possible by this lack of audibility, credibility, and consequence. We live inside an enormous contradiction: a society that by law and preening self-regard insists it is against such violence has by innumerable strategies allowed that violence to continue unchecked; better and far more frequently protected perpetrators than victims; and routinely punished, humiliated, and intimidated victims for speaking up, from workplace harassment cases to campus rape cases to domestic violence cases.

**中文理解**：性别暴力是由这种可听性、可信度和后果性的缺乏造成的。我们生活在一个巨大的矛盾中：一个通过法律和自我吹嘘坚持认为反对这种暴力的社会，却通过无数策略让这种暴力继续不受检查；更好且更频繁地保护施害者而不是受害者；并且通常惩罚、羞辱和恐吓受害者，从工作场所骚扰案件到校园强奸案件到家庭暴力案件。Solnit 用"enormous contradiction"将"社会"定义为一种"矛盾"：不是"一致"，而是"矛盾"；不是"和谐"，而是"冲突"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"Gender violence is made possible by this lack of audibility, credibility, and consequence" + "We live inside an enormous contradiction: a society that by law and preening self-regard insists it is against such violence has by innumerable strategies allowed that violence to continue unchecked..."

**关键词**：**contradiction**（矛盾）是核心词——它暗示一种"矛盾"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"preening self-regard"的表述，将"社会"定义为一种"虚伪"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"社会即矛盾"主题的缩影。

### ⑨

> **原句 9:** The disregard for a woman's voice that underlies sexual violence is inseparable from the disregard afterward if a woman goes to the police, the university authorities, her family, her church, the courts, to the hospital for a rape kit, and is ignored, discredited, blamed, shamed, disbelieved. They are both assaults on the full humanity and membership of a person in her society, and the devaluation in the latter arena enables the former. Sexual assault can only thrive in situations of unequal audibility, credibility, and consequence. This, far more than any other disparity, is the precondition for epidemic gender violence.

**中文理解**：对女性声音的漠视是性暴力的基础，这与如果女性去警察、大学当局、家人、教会、法院、医院做强奸工具包检验，然后被无视、怀疑、指责、羞辱、不相信之后的漠视是不可分割的。它们都是对一个人在她的社会中的完全人性的攻击，后者的贬低使前者成为可能。性侵犯只能在不平等的可听性、可信度和后果性的情况下茁壮成长。这比其他任何不平等更是流行病性别暴力的先决条件。Solnit 用"inseparable"将"暴力"定义为一种"连续"：不是"孤立"，而是"连续"；不是"单一"，而是"双重"。

**关键词**：**inseparable**（不可分割）是核心词——它暗示一种"不可分割"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"the devaluation in the latter arena enables the former"的对比，将"暴力"定义为一种"相互"。

### ⑩

> **原句 10:** Changing who has a voice with all its power and attributes doesn't fix everything, but it changes the rules, notably the rules about what stories will be told and heard and who decides. One of the measures of this change is the many cases that were ignored, disbelieved, dismissed, or found in favor of the perpetrator years ago that have had a different outcome in the present, because the women or children who testified have more audibility, credibility, and consequence now than they did before.

**中文理解**：改变谁拥有声音及其所有力量和属性并不能解决一切，但它改变了规则，特别是关于什么故事将被讲述和听到以及谁做决定的规则。这种变化的衡量标准之一是多年前被无视、怀疑、驳回或判定有利于施害者的许多案件，现在有了不同的结果，因为作证的女性或儿童现在比以前有更多的可听性、可信度和后果性。Solnit 用"doesn't fix everything, but it changes the rules"将"改变"定义为一种"局限"：不是"完美"，而是"局限"；不是"全部"，而是"部分"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"Changing who has a voice with all its power and attributes doesn't fix everything, but it changes the rules..."

**关键词**：**rules**（规则）是核心词——它暗示一种"规则"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"One of the measures of this change"的表述，将"变化"定义为一种"可测量"。

## 一句话总结
Solnit 通过"声音"的三个维度——"可听性"、"可信度"、"后果性"——探讨了性别暴力的根源：不是"身体"的，而是"声音"的；不是"单一"的，而是"三重"的；而"改变"不仅是"改变"，也是"规则的改变"。
