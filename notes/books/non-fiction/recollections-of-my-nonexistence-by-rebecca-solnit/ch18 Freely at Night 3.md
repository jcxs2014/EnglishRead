# 18. Freely at Night · Chapter 3（自由之夜·第三章）

## 概览

- **出处**：*Recollections of My Nonexistence* (Viking, 2020)，Part V "Freely at Night" 第三章
- **作者**：Rebecca Solnit
- **章节定位**：全书第 18 章；深入阅读的世界——从罗伯特·格雷夫斯的《白色女神》到"双重意识"

### 论证脉络

Solnit 以"As a reader I roamed free"（作为读者我自由漫游）开篇，将"阅读"定义为一种"自由"——不是"拥有"自由，而是"成为"自由。核心论点：阅读不仅是"逃避"，也是"变形"——在书中，她可以"变成"男人、变成鸟、变成任何人。中间插入"双重意识"（double consciousness）的概念，将"阅读"定义为一种"意识扩展"——不是"失去自我"，而是"找到自我"。章末回到"我在书中生活"——书籍不仅是"逃避"，也是"生活"。

### 核心金句

> "As a reader I roamed free. As a would-be writer, it was more complicated."

> "The term double consciousness is sometimes used for black experience in a white culture. W. E. B. DuBois famously wrote... 'the Negro is a sort of seventh son, born with a veil, and gifted with second-sight in this American world,—a world which yields him no true self-consciousness, but only lets him see himself through the revelation of the other world.'"

> "You depend on men, and what they think of you, learn to constantly check yourself in a mirror to see how you look to men, you perform for them, and this theatrical anxiety forms or deforms or stops altogether what you do and say and sometimes think."

> "I lived inside books, and though it's often assumed that we choose books to travel through them to get to the end, there were books I took up residency in, books I read again and again and then picked up and opened anywhere just to be in that world, with those people, with that author's vision and voice."

## 选择性精读

### ①

> **原句 1:** As a reader I roamed free. As a would-be writer, it was more complicated. In my teens and well into my twenties, I mostly encountered the literature of heterosexual men, where the muse or the beloved or the city they explored or the nature they conquered was a woman. Throughout my teens, I wrestled with Robert Graves's The White Goddess, which seemed to have something valuable about trees and alphabets I could never quite extract from its erudite jumble. It's a book that seemed to assume that the poet's orientation is that of a straight man to a female goddess; it might have encouraged some young women to smile enigmatically and levy tribute, but I wanted to be a writer, not a muse.

**中文理解**：作为读者我自由漫游。作为一个准作家，情况更复杂。在我二十多岁的大部分时间里，我主要遇到的是异性恋男性的文学，其中的缪斯或他们所探索的城市或他们所征服的自然是一个女人。在整个青少年时期，我都在与罗伯特·格雷夫斯的《白色女神》作斗争，这本书似乎有一些关于树木和字母表的宝贵内容，但我永远无法从它博学的混乱中提取出来。这本书似乎假设诗人的取向是直男对女性女神的取向；它可能鼓励一些年轻女性神秘地微笑并征收贡品，但我想成为一个作家，而不是缪斯。Solnit 用"As a reader I roamed free. As a would-be writer, it was more complicated"的对比，将"阅读"与"写作"定义为两种不同的"自由"——阅读是"自由"的，写作是"复杂"的。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"As a reader I roamed free. As a would-be writer, it was more complicated" + "In my teens and well into my twenties, I mostly encountered the literature of heterosexual men..."。

**关键词**：**roamed**（漫游）、**would-be**（准/想要成为的）、**muse**（缪斯）、**erudite**（博学的）。"muse"是核心词——它暗示一种"被动"：女性是"被观看的"，不是"观看的"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"I wanted to be a writer, not a muse"的对比，将"写作"定义为一种"主动"——不是"被观看"，而是"观看"；不是"被书写"，而是"书写"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"阅读 vs. 写作"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"阅读"是"自由"的，"写作"是"斗争"的；"消费"是"容易"的，"生产"是"困难的"。

### ②

> **原句 2:** I also struggled secretly against the men around me who were convinced that they were the artists and I was the audience. Young women like me were supposed to exist as orbital figures, planets around a sun, moons around a planet. Never stars. When I was eighteen, one man was so adamant that I was his muse that he inspired in me a vivid sense that I was literally standing atop a pillar; I can still summon up a sense of being stranded in the hazy grayish atmosphere of nowhere. On a pillar there's nothing to do but stand still or fall. I was happy to listen and read, but I was mutely against being only a listener and reader, though all I could do about that was bide my time and build my work.

**中文理解**：我也秘密地与我周围的男人作斗争，他们确信自己是艺术家，而我是观众。像我这样的年轻女性应该作为轨道人物存在，是围绕恒星的行星，是围绕行星的卫星。永远不是星星。我十八岁时，一个男人如此坚信我是他的缪斯，以至于他让我产生了一种生动的感觉，即我字面意义上站在一根柱子上；我仍然能够唤起一种被困在朦胧的灰色虚无空间中的感觉。在柱子上，除了站着不动或摔倒之外，没有什么可做的。我很乐意倾听和阅读，但我默默地反对仅仅做一个倾听者和读者，尽管我对此能做的只是等待时机和建立我的作品。Solnit 用"planets around a sun, moons around a planet. Never stars"的比喻，将女性定义为一种"卫星"——不是"发光体"，而是"反射体"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"I also struggled secretly against the men around me..." + "Young women like me were supposed to exist as orbital figures..."。

**关键词**：**orbital**（轨道的）、**adamant**（坚定的）、**stranded**（被困的）、**bide my time**（等待时机）。"orbital"是核心词——它暗示一种"围绕"：不是"中心"，而是"围绕"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"On a pillar there's nothing to do but stand still or fall"的比喻，将"缪斯"定义为一种"困境"——不是"自由"，而是"限制"；不是"选择"，而是"被迫"。

### ③

> **原句 3:** There were comfortable books, and another kind of comfort in recognizing my own condition or its equivalents and analogies in others, in not being alone in my loneliness and angst. Sometimes one piece would crash into me: I still have the poem "Never Before" by Philip Levine, from the New Yorker in the fall of 1980 (I clipped Levine's columns and taped them together into a narrow strip, now yellowed, exactly as long as my arm, with a deeper amber where the tape joins the sections together. It looks like a bandage but reads like a wound).

**中文理解**：有安慰的书，另一种安慰是在他人的身上认识到我自己的状况或其等同物和类比，在我的孤独和焦虑中不孤单。有时会有一篇作品撞击我：我仍然有菲利普·莱文斯的诗《从未有过》，来自1980年秋天的《纽约客》（我把莱文斯的专栏剪下来，用胶带粘成一条窄条，现在发黄了，正好和我的手臂一样长，在胶带连接处有更深的琥珀色。它看起来像一个绷带，但读起来像一个伤口）。Solnit 用"It looks like a bandage but reads like a wound"的比喻，将"诗"定义为一种"伤口"——不是"安慰"，而是"创伤"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"There were comfortable books, and another kind of comfort in recognizing my own condition..." + "Sometimes one piece would crash into me..."。

**关键词**：**equivalents**（等同物）、**analogies**（类比）、**crash into**（撞击）、**amber**（琥珀色）。"crash into"是核心词——它暗示一种"暴力"：不是"温柔地进入"，而是"撞击"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"It looks like a bandage but reads like a wound"的悖论，将"诗"定义为一种"伤口"——不是"绷带"（保护），而是"伤口"（创伤）。

### ④

> **原句 4:** Milan Kundera's The Book of Laughter and Forgetting was published in the New Yorker in installments the same second half of 1980 and passed along to me in a stack of magazines. The chapters were, like Jorge Luis Borges's Labyrinths a few years earlier, revelatory. They gave me a sense of how you could mix things, how the personal and the political could spell each other, how a narrative could be oblique, how prose, like poetry, could jump from subject to subject or take flight. Of how the categories were optional, though it would take me another decade to find my way through their walls.

**中文理解**：米兰·昆德拉的《笑忘书》于1980年下半年在《纽约客》上连载，并在一堆杂志中传给了我。这些章节，就像豪尔赫·路易斯·博尔赫斯几年前出版的《迷宫》一样，具有启发性。它们让我感觉到你可以如何混合事物，个人和政治如何相互拼写，叙事如何可以是斜向的，散文如何可以像诗歌一样从一个主题跳到另一个主题或起飞。关于类别是可选的，尽管又花了我十年时间才找到穿过它们墙壁的路。Solnit 用"revelatory"（具有启发性的）将"阅读"定义为一种"启示"——不是"学习"，而是"觉醒"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"Milan Kundera's The Book of Laughter and Forgetting was published in the New Yorker in installments..." + "The chapters were, like Jorge Luis Borges's Labyrinths a few years earlier, revelatory"。

**关键词**：**installments**（连载）、**revelatory**（具有启发性的）、**oblique**（斜向的）、**categories**（类别）。"revelatory"是核心词——它暗示一种"揭示"：不是"隐藏"，而是"揭示"。

### ⑤

> **原句 5:** I wanted urgency, intensity, excess and extremes, prose and narrative bursting against the confines. Except when I wanted reassurances. I found both. I lived so deeply in books that I felt unanchored and adrift, not particularly part of my own time and place, always with one foot or more in other places, medieval or imaginary or Edwardian. I had in that floating world a sense that I might wake up or otherwise find myself in one of those other times and places.

**中文理解**：我想要紧迫感、强度、过度和极端，散文和叙事在界限上爆发。除了我想要安慰的时候。我两者都找到了。我如此深入地生活在书中，以至于我感到无锚和漂泊，不完全属于我自己的时间和地点，总是有一只脚或更多脚在其他地方，中世纪或想象中的或爱德华时代的。在那个漂浮的世界里，我有一种感觉，我可能会醒来或以其他方式发现自己在那些其他时间和地点之一。Solnit 用"unanchored and adrift"（无锚和漂泊）将"阅读"定义为一种"漂泊"——不是"安定"，而是"漂泊"；不是"扎根"，而是"漂浮"。

### ⑥

> **原句 6:** There's a benefit to being untethered from your own time. I think I gained a sense of how differently constituted the idea of being human, the purpose of life, the expectations and desires had been even a generation or two ago, let alone half a millennium before, of how the definitions metamorphosed, and how that meant you could step outside the assumptions of your time, or at least wear them lightly, and at least in theory not let them punish you. That being human can mean many things, in other words. At thirteen, I had read C. S. Lewis's The Allegory of Love, which describes the social construction, in twelfth-century France, of what would become our ideas of romantic love. That these expectations were the result of a particular time and place gave me a sense of liberation, like someone opening the windows in a stuffy room.

**中文理解**：脱离你自己的时代有一种好处。我认为我获得了一种感觉，即关于人类的观念、生命的期望和欲望在哪怕一两代人之前，更不用说在半个千年前之前，是如何不同的构成的，定义是如何变形的，以及这意味着你可以走出你那个时代的假设，或者至少轻松地穿着它们，至少在理论上不让它们惩罚你。换句话说，作为人类可以意味着很多东西。我十三岁时读过C.S.刘易斯的《爱的寓言》，它描述了在我们成为我们的浪漫爱观念的十二世纪法国的社会建构。这些期望是特定时间和地点的结果，这给了我一种解放感，就像有人在闷热的房间里打开了窗户一样。Solnit 用"like someone opening the windows in a stuffy room"的比喻，将"阅读"定义为一种"解放"——不是"限制"，而是"解放"；不是"封闭"，而是"开放"。

**关键词**：**untethered**（脱离的）、**metamorphosed**（变形的）、**liberation**（解放）、**stuffy**（闷热的）。"untethered"是核心词——它暗示一种"脱离"：不是"连接"，而是"脱离"。

### ⑦

> **原句 7:** The term double consciousness is sometimes used for black experience in a white culture. W. E. B. DuBois famously wrote in the last years of the nineteenth century (and wrote, as so many men did up through at least James Baldwin, as though all people were men or even one man) "the Negro is a sort of seventh son, born with a veil, and gifted with second-sight in this American world,—a world which yields him no true self-consciousness, but only lets him see himself through the revelation of the other world. It is a peculiar sensation, this double-consciousness, this sense of always looking at one's self through the eyes of others."

**中文理解**：术语"双重意识"有时被用来描述白人文化中的黑人经验。W.E.B.杜波依斯在十九世纪最后几年（以及至少到詹姆斯·鲍德温为止的许多男人一样，好像所有人都是男人甚至一个男人）写道："黑人是一种第七子，生来就有一层纱，在这个美国世界中拥有第二视力，——一个不给他真正的自我意识的世界，而只让他通过另一个世界的启示来看自己。这是一种奇特的感觉，这种双重意识，这种总是通过别人的眼睛来看自己的感觉。"Solnit 用"double consciousness"（双重意识）将"阅读"定义为一种"意识扩展"——不是"失去自我"，而是"看到自我"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"The term double consciousness is sometimes used for black experience in a white culture" + "W. E. B. DuBois famously wrote..."。

**关键词**：**double consciousness**（双重意识）、**veil**（纱）、**revelation**（启示）。"double consciousness"是核心词——它暗示一种"双重"：不是"单一"，而是"双重"；不是"统一"，而是"分裂"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"always looking at one's self through the eyes of others"的表述，将"自我"定义为一种"他者"：不是"自己看自己"，而是"通过他人看自己"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"双重意识"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"自我"不仅是"自己"，也是"他者"；不仅是"内在"，也是"外在"。

### ⑧

> **原句 8:** DuBois's framework found an echo in John Berger's 1968 Ways of Seeing, when Berger imagined, with generosity and brilliance, what it was to be something he had never been: "To be born a woman has been to be born, within an allotted and confined space, into the keeping of men. The social presence of women has developed as a result of their ingenuity in living under such tutelage with such a limited space. But this has been at the cost of a woman's self being split into two. A woman must continually watch herself. She is almost continuously accompanied by her own image of herself. She has to survey everything she is and everything she does because how she appears to others, and ultimately how she appears to men, is of crucial importance for what is normally thought of as the success of her life. Her own sense of being in herself is supplanted by a sense of being appreciated as herself by another."

**中文理解**：杜波依斯的框架在约翰·伯格1968年的《观看之道》中找到了回声，伯格以慷慨和才华想象了他从未成为的东西："生为女人就是出生在一个分配和限制的空间里，进入男人的保管之中。女性的社会存在是由于她们在如此有限的监护下生活的聪明才智而发展起来的。但这以女性的自我被分裂为代价。女人必须不断地观察自己。她几乎不断地被自己的形象所陪伴。她必须审视她所做的一切，因为她在别人面前，最终她在男人面前，如何出现，通常被认为是她生活成功的关键。她对自己的存在的自我感被被另一个人的欣赏感所取代。"Solnit 用"Her own sense of being in herself is supplanted by a sense of being appreciated as herself by another"将"女性"定义为一种"分裂"：不是"自我"，而是"他者"；不是"存在"，而是"被欣赏"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"DuBois's framework found an echo in John Berger's 1968 Ways of Seeing..."。

**关键词**：**tutelage**（监护）、**supplanted**（被取代）、**appreciated**（被欣赏）。"supplanted"是核心词——它暗示一种"取代"：不是"自我"，而是"被取代"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"continually watch herself"的表述，将"女性"定义为一种"自我审视"：不是"自由"，而是"监视"；不是"存在"，而是"被看"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"女性即分裂"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"女性"不仅是"自我"，也是"他者"；不仅是"存在"，而是"被看"。

### ⑨

> **原句 9:** In my case, this meant identifying with male protagonists, with the Jim of the almost womanless Lord Jim and Jim Carroll's self-anointing stud junkie in The Basketball Diaries and with Pip rather than Estella in Great Expectations, and all the grail seekers and ring bearers and western explorers and chasers and conquerors and haters of women and inhabitants of worlds where women were absent. And the task of finding one's own way must be immeasurably harder when all the heroes, all the protagonists, are not only another gender but another race, or another sexual orientation, and when you find that you yourself are described as the savages or the servants or the people who don't matter. There are so many forms of annihilation.

**中文理解**：在我的情况下，这意味着认同男性主人公，与几乎没有女人的《吉姆老爷》中的吉姆和《篮球日记》中自我涂油的花花公子瘾君子吉姆，以及《远大前程》中的皮皮而不是艾丝黛拉，还有所有圣杯追寻者、戒指持有者、西部探险者、追逐者、征服者和恨女人的女人以及没有女人的世界的居民。而当你发现你自己被描述为野蛮人或仆人或无关紧要的人时，所有的英雄，所有的主人公，不仅是另一个性别，而且是另一个种族，或另一个性取向，寻找自己的道路的任务必须更加难以估量。有这么多形式的毁灭。Solnit 用"identifying with male protagonists"将"阅读"定义为一种"认同"——不是"女性认同男性"，而是"人类认同人类"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"In my case, this meant identifying with male protagonists..."。

**关键词**：**protagonists**（主人公）、**anointing**（涂油）、**grail seekers**（圣杯追寻者）、**annihilation**（毁灭）。"protagonists"是核心词——它暗示一种"中心"：不是"配角"，而是"主角"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"There are so many forms of annihilation"的表述，将"毁灭"定义为一种"多元"：不是"一种"，而是"多种"；不是"单一"，而是"多样"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"认同与毁灭"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"认同"不仅是"连接"，也是"毁灭"；不仅是"找到自我"，也是"失去自我"。

### ⑩

> **原句 10:** I lived inside books, and though it's often assumed that we choose books to travel through them to get to the end, there were books I took up residency in, books I read again and again and then picked up and opened anywhere just to be in that world, with those people, with that author's vision and voice. Jane Austen's novels, but also Ursula K. Le Guin's Earthsea books, Frank Herbert's Dune, eventually E. M. Forster, Willa Cather, and Michael Ondaatje, some children's books I returned to as an adult, and early on novels that don't have much standing as literature. I roamed freely in them, knowing the territory in all directions, and familiarity was a reward as strangeness might be in a book read once just to find out what happened.

**中文理解**：我在书中生活，尽管人们通常认为我们选择书籍是为了穿过它们到达终点，但有些书我定居其中，我一遍又一遍地读，然后随便拿起打开，只是为了在那个世界里，与那些人在一起，与那个作者的眼光和声音在一起。简·奥斯汀的小说，还有厄休拉·勒古恩的地海系列，弗兰克·赫伯特的《沙丘》，最终是E.M.福斯特、威拉·卡瑟和迈克尔·昂达奇，一些我成年后重温的儿童读物，以及早期作为文学没有多大地位的小说。我在它们中自由漫游，了解各个方向的领土，而熟悉是一种奖励，就像在读一本只为了找出发生了什么的一次性小说时，陌生可能是一种奖励一样。Solnit 用"took up residency in"（定居其中）将"阅读"定义为一种"居住"——不是"访问"，而是"居住"；不是"旅行"，而是"生活"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"I lived inside books, and though it's often assumed that we choose books to travel through them to get to the end, there were books I took up residency in..."。

**关键词**：**residency**（居住）、**familiarity**（熟悉）、**strangeness**（陌生）。"residency"是核心词——它暗示一种"居住"：不是"访问"，而是"居住"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"familiarity was a reward as strangeness might be in a book read once"的对比，将"重读"定义为一种"奖励"——不是"无聊"，而是"熟悉"；不是"重复"，而是"深化"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"阅读即居住"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"阅读"不仅是"旅行"，也是"居住"；不仅是"访问"，也是"生活"。

## 一句话总结

Solnit 通过阅读的世界，探讨了"阅读"作为一种"非存在"——在书中，她可以"变成"男人、变成鸟、变成任何人；而"双重意识"不仅是"分裂"，也是"扩展"——通过阅读，她学会了"通过他人的眼睛看自己"。
