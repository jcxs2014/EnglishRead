# 27. Audibility, Credibility, Consequence · Chapter 1（可听性、可信度、后果·第一章）

## 概览

- **出处**：*Recollections of My Nonexistence* (Viking, 2020)，Part VIII "Audibility, Credibility, Consequence" 第一章
- **作者**：Rebecca Solnit
- **章节定位**：全书第 27 章；从酷儿文化转向"成长"——碎片如何被收集、模式如何被发现、以及内华达试验场如何教会她写作

### 论证脉络

Solnit 以"Growing up, we say, as though we were trees"（我们说长大，好像我们是树）开篇，将"成长"重新定义为一种"收集碎片"：不是"树"（单一方向），而是"拼贴"（多方向）。核心论点：内华达试验场（Nevada Test Site）教会她写作——不是"技巧"，而是"整合"；不是"单一声音"，而是"多种声音的融合"。中间插入对"希望"的思考——"希望不是乐观主义，而是方向"。章末回到"我自由了"——不是"没有限制"，而是"找到了自己的声音"。

## 选择性精读

### ①

> **原句 1:** Growing up, we say, as though we were trees, as though altitude was all that there was to be gained, but so much of the process is growing whole as the fragments are gathered, the patterns found. Human infants are born with craniums made up of four plates that have not yet knit together into a solid dome so that their heads can compress to fit through the birth canal, so that the brain within can then expand. The skull quadruples in size in the first few years, and if the bones knit together too soon, they restrict the growth of the brain; and if they don't knit at all the brain remains unprotected. Open enough to grow and closed enough to hold together is what a life must also be.

**中文理解**：我们说长大，好像我们是树，好像海拔是唯一可以获得的东西，但这个过程的很大一部分是随着碎片的收集、模式的发现而变得完整。人类婴儿出生时头骨由四块尚未愈合成实心穹顶的板组成，这样他们的头部可以压缩以适合产道，这样里面的大脑就可以扩张。头骨在最初几年翻了两番，如果骨头愈合太快，它们会限制大脑的生长；如果它们根本不愈合，大脑就保持不受保护。足够开放以成长，足够封闭以保持在一起，这也是生活必须的样子。Solnit 用"Open enough to grow and closed enough to hold together"的悖论，将"生活"定义为一种"平衡"：不是"完全开放"，也不是"完全封闭"；不是"无限"，也不是"有限"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"Growing up, we say, as though we were trees, as though altitude was all that there was to be gained, but so much of the process is growing whole as the fragments are gathered, the patterns found"。

**关键词**：**craniums**（头骨）、**knit together**（愈合）、**quadruples**（翻两番）、**restrict**（限制）。"knit together"是核心词——它暗示一种"愈合"：不是"固定"，而是"愈合"；不是"完成"，而是"过程"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"Open enough to grow and closed enough to hold together"的悖论，将"生活"定义为一种"平衡"：不是"极端"，而是"平衡"；不是"单一"，而是"双重"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"成长即平衡"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"成长"不仅是"收集"，也是"平衡"；不仅是"开放"，也是"封闭"。

> **原句 2:** I sometimes say that the Nevada Test Site taught me to write, because when I went there for the big spring antinuclear actions and campouts in 1988 and every year thereafter into the millennium, I met a place so stark and vast and, to me, strange, at which so many cultures and stories converged, that I had to bring together all the fragments of what I was doing into a new whole to have something that felt adequate to what I found there. Before then, I had accepted the pigeonholes into which writing fit. I had written criticism and reviews with a confident, objective-sounding tone, written journalistic reports that more or less colored within the lines of journalism. In those years, I had also written small, dense essays that were lyrical, personal, emotional, metaphorical, experimenting with form and tone, letting in what I was learning from poetry and the prophetic voice, doing all the things I was not supposed to do in criticism and journalism, letting wonder and melancholy and uncertainty in, giving rein to what language can do.

> **原句 8:** It may be hard for those who came later to understand how utterly ignorant we non-Natives were then, how much Native people had disappeared from or never entered the mainstream conversation or were talked of exclusively in the past tense as people who had vanished long ago and would never appear to speak up on their own behalf. They were also treated as people who had never existed in the first place when artists, photographers, environmentalists, poets, explorers, historians imagined and depicted North America as a place in which human beings had just arrived, or rather that white men had recently discovered.

**中文理解**：对于那些后来的人来说，可能很难理解我们非原住民当时是多么无知，原住民已经从主流对话中消失或从未进入，或者被专门用过去时谈论，作为很久以前消失的人，永远不会出现为自己说话。他们也被当作从未存在过的人对待，当艺术家、摄影师、环保主义者、诗人、探险家、历史学家想象和描绘北美时，将其描绘成人类刚刚到达的地方，或者更确切地说，白人最近发现的地方。Solnit 用"utterly ignorant"（完全无知）将"无知"定义为一种"完全"：不是"部分"，而是"完全"；不是"偶然"，而是"系统"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"It may be hard for those who came later to understand how utterly ignorant we non-Natives were then..."。

**关键词**：**utterly**（完全地）、**ignorant**（无知的）、**exclusively**（专门地）、**vanished**（消失）、**depicted**（描绘）。"utterly"是核心词——它暗示一种"完全"：不是"部分"，而是"完全"；不是"偶然"，而是"系统"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"people who had never existed in the first place"的表述，将"抹除"定义为一种"不存在"：不是"存在"，而是"不存在"；不是"可见"，而是"不可见"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"无知即系统"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"无知"不仅是"个人"，也是"系统"；不仅是"偶然"，也是"结构"。

### ⑨

> **原句 9:** Ideas that seem ordinary now overthrew whole categories of thought then. They ended—to some extent, if not enough—an era of telling stories about a North American landscape that had been, until Europeans arrived, without human contact. I sometimes thought of this as the Madonna/whore theory of landscape: human contact was imagined as inevitably violating a vulnerable, passive nature that was inevitably degraded by us. White people were imagined as discoverers of a place that lay waiting, before history, before culture. Beyond this binary lay other ways of being human, other ways of being in the natural world. Being an environmentalist was coming to mean, at last, recognizing and respecting the first dwellers in these places and that human impact—hunting, harvesting, fire management techniques—had to be factored into assessments of what had constituted the ecosystems before Euro-American arrival. That is to say, these new frameworks and voices transformed nothing less than history, nature, and culture in far-reaching ways.

**中文理解**：现在看起来很普通的思想当时推翻了整个思想类别。它们结束了一个时代——在某种程度上，如果还不够的话——讲述关于北美景观的故事，在欧洲人到来之前，这个景观没有人类接触。我有时把这看作是景观的圣母/妓女理论：人类接触被想象成不可避免地侵犯一种脆弱的、被动的自然，而这种自然不可避免地被我们退化。白人被想象成一个地方的发现者，这个地方在历史之前、在文化之前等待着。在这个二元论之外，还有其他做人的方式，其他在自然世界中的方式。成为一个环保主义者终于意味着承认和尊重这些地方的第一批居民，以及人类影响——狩猎、收获、火灾管理技术——必须被纳入对欧美到来之前构成生态系统的评估。也就是说，这些新的框架和声音在深远的方式上改变了历史、自然和文化。Solnit 用"Madonna/whore theory of landscape"（景观的圣母/妓女理论）将"景观"定义为一种"性别"：不是"中性"，而是"性别"；不是"客观"，而是"主观"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"Ideas that seem ordinary now overthrew whole categories of thought then" + "They ended—to some extent, if not enough—an era of telling stories about a North American landscape that had been, until Europeans arrived, without human contact"。

**关键词**：**overthrew**（推翻）、**categories**（类别）、**Madonna/whore**（圣母/妓女）、**vulnerable**（脆弱的）、**binary**（二元论）、**dwellers**（居民）、**factored**（纳入）。"overthrew"是核心词——它暗示一种"推翻"：不是"维持"，而是"推翻"；不是"接受"，而是"改变"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"transformed nothing less than history, nature, and culture in far-reaching ways"的表述，将"改变"定义为一种"深远"：不是"表面"，而是"深远"；不是"部分"，而是"全部"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"思想即力量"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"思想"不仅是"观念"，也是"力量"；不仅是"理论"，也是"实践"。

### ⑩

> **原句 10:** That was my golden age, not because I had escaped the evils of this world but because I had found ways to think about them and sometimes do something about them, valiant companions in the efforts, places to fall in love with, and ideas that transformed me. I had begun returning to the place in which I spent the first two years of my life, Northern New Mexico, where I had the great fortune to be befriended by the older feminist writer Lucy Lippard, whose response to the manuscript of Savage Dreams was, essentially, to give me the key to her little house (and a nice blurb). I began spending part of every summer house-sitting for her out on the prairie, enraptured by the sky, the space, the light, and the thunderstorms. Later in the 1990s, I got involved with a man who lived in southeastern California's Mojave Desert and spent part of my time there for four years.

**中文理解**：那是我的黄金时代，不是因为我逃避了世界的邪恶，而是因为我找到了思考它们的方式，有时对它们做些什么，在努力中有勇敢的伙伴，有爱上地方，有改变我的思想。我开始回到我度过生命头两年的地方，新墨西哥州北部，在那里我很幸运地被年长的女性主义作家露西·利帕德友好对待，她对《野蛮梦想》手稿的反应，本质上是给了我她小房子的钥匙（和一个很好的推荐语）。我开始每年夏天在她那里看房子，在草原上，被天空、空间、光线和雷暴所吸引。在1990年代后期，我和一个住在加州东南部莫哈韦沙漠的男人交往，并在那里度过了部分时间四年。Solnit 用"golden age"（黄金时代）将"时代"定义为一种"黄金"：不是"普通"，而是"黄金"；不是"平凡"，而是"珍贵"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"That was my golden age, not because I had escaped the evils of this world but because I had found ways to think about them and sometimes do something about them..."。

**关键词**：**golden age**（黄金时代）、**valiant**（勇敢的）、**enraptured**（被吸引的）、**thunderstorms**（雷暴）。"golden age"是核心词——它暗示一种"黄金"：不是"普通"，而是"黄金"；不是"平凡"，而是"珍贵"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"not because I had escaped the evils of this world but because I had found ways to think about them and sometimes do something about them"的对比，将"黄金时代"定义为一种"面对"：不是"逃避"，而是"面对"；不是"消极"，而是"积极"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"黄金时代即面对"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"黄金时代"不仅是"快乐"，也是"面对"；不仅是"逃避"，也是"行动"。

## 一句话总结
Solnit 通过内华达试验场的经历，探讨了"成长"作为一种"收集碎片"——不是"线性进步"，而是"整合"；不是"到达"，而是"成为"；而"写作"不仅是"技巧"，也是"整合"——将"碎片"收集成"整体"，将"多种声音"融合成"一个声音"。
