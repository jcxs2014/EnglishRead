# 05. 4 The Cannibalism Taboo（食人禁忌）

## 概览

- **出处**：*The Captain's Dinner*，Adam Cohen 著
- **作者**：Adam Cohen
- **章节定位**：全书第 4 章。在杀戮与食人之后，回溯食人禁忌的自然史、考古学、心理学与法律史，把 Dudley 等人的行为放进“食人类型学”——尤其是“海上求生性食人”这一子类别
- **字符数**：约 29,000（提取件 `text/ch05_4_the_cannibalism_taboo.txt`）
- **一句话主旨**：食人禁忌看似普遍，食人本身也普遍——本章把 Dudley 等人的行为从“道德异端”还原为“海上求生性食人”这一反复出现的模式，同时揭示抽签在实践中多被人为操纵，专挑外来者与弱者下手，预示 Dudley 选中 Parker 的“几乎不可避免”

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：食人禁忌与食人行为并存——Freud 把这一矛盾解释为“被压抑的欲望”；类型学把 Dudley 等人的行为归入“海上求生性食人”；历史案例显示抽签多被操纵以针对外来者与弱者，因此 Parker 被选中“极有可能，甚至几乎不可避免”。

- **证据链**：

  | 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 食人禁忌带来的生理性厌恶反应 | 心理学 | 解释禁忌的普遍性 |
  | 人类进化史中的食人记录（145 万年前） | 考古学 | 食人比人类更古老 |
  | Freud：食人是三种被禁止的本能愿望之一 | 理论 | 解释禁忌与吸引力的并存 |
  | 童话与维多利亚文学中的食人母题 | 文化证据 | 西方文化对食人的矛盾态度 |
  | 食人类型学：营养性对文化性；求生性食人 | 分类学 | 把 Dudley 等人归入特定子类别 |
  | 食尸与食人在道德与法律上的差异 | 法律 | 预示审判的“谋杀”定性 |
  | 历史海难案例（Nottingham Galley、Francis Mary、1641 St. Kitts、Dolphin、Peggy、Essex、Francis Spaight、Sallie M. Steelman） | 历史 | 显示抽签多被操纵以针对外来者与弱者 |
  | The Times：受害者通常是“无助的人” | 媒体与社会学 | 显示同群体偏袒 |
  | 从无人因海难食人被起诉的法律传统 | 法律 | 预示 Dudley 等人“无从怀疑”的辩词 |

- **论证脉络**：食人禁忌的普遍性 → 自然史与考古学 → Freud → 文化证据（童话与文学）→ 类型学 → 海上求生性食人 → 历史案例 → 选择模式（外来者与弱者）→ 法律传统（不诉）

- **可质疑处**：
  1. **Freud 的“被压抑的欲望”理论**属于心理分析框架而非实证，作者以“一种解释”的面目呈现，可能依赖过重。
  2. **类型学中“营养性对文化性”的二分**可能过度简化——真实的案例往往两者兼有。
  3. **“选择模式”的因果断言**（外来者与弱者被选）建立在数量有限的历史案例上，作者的“极有可能，甚至几乎不可避免”是推论而非已证事实。

## 选择性精读

> **原句 1:** "When they ate Parker's lifeless body and drank his blood, Dudley, Stephens, and Brooks crossed a line. They became cannibals. The boundary is a profound one. The taboo against eating human flesh and blood is not a product of a particular culture or religious tradition or historical era. It seems to stem from more primitive feelings. Cannibalism evokes not just shame and guilt but a more visceral, even primordial, reaction: disgust. When they ate Parker, Dudley, Stephens, and Brooks became part of a long, complex, and dark tradition of people eating people."

- **中文理解**：当他们吃了 Parker 的尸体、喝了血，Dudley 等人越过了一条线——他们成了食人者。这条界限是深刻的。食人禁忌不是特定文化、宗教传统或历史时代的产物，它似乎源于更原始的感受。食人唤起的不仅是羞耻和内疚，更是一种发自内脏的、甚至原始的反应：厌恶。当他们吃了 Parker，Dudley、Stephens 和 Brooks 就加入了人类吃人这一漫长、复杂而黑暗的传统。
- **句子结构**：七句递进：越过一条线 → 成为食人者 → 界限是深刻的 → 禁忌并非特定文化的产物 → 源于更原始的感受 → 唤起厌恶 → 融入吃人的传统。
- **关键词**：`crossed a line`、`profound`、`primordial`、`disgust`、`long, complex, and dark tradition`
- **表达方式**：用“crossed a line”把食人写成一个不可逆的门槛；用“primordial”与“disgust”把禁忌写成生物性的反应而非文化规训。
- **为什么这样写**：作者在这里把食人确立为一种普遍存在的禁忌——正因禁忌如此普遍，后文弗洛伊德的“被压抑的欲望”才能作为反题登场：越是到处都有的禁忌，越要追问食人本身为何也到处都有。

> **原句 2:** "The natural history of cannibalism is rich and variegated. It has always been a fixture of the animal world, and scientists have documented it in countless species, from polar bears to mollusks. The phrase "pecking order" derives from the bird world, where dominant chickens may peck subordinate ones, who are sometimes pecked to death and eaten. Female praying mantises eat males while they are having sex. Among the springbok mantis, who are avid practitioners of "sexual cannibalism," 60 percent of sexual encounters end with the female biting off the male's head."

- **中文理解**：食人的自然史丰富而多样。它一直是动物界的常见景象，科学家在无数物种中都记录到，从北极熊到软体动物。“pecking order”（啄食顺序）一词源自鸟类世界，占据支配地位的鸡会啄食从属的鸡，后者有时被啄死吃掉。雌螳螂在交配过程中吃掉雄性。springbok mantis 是“性食人”的狂热实践者——它们的性交有 60% 以雌性咬掉雄性脑袋告终。
- **句子结构**：五句递进：自然史丰富多样 → 动物界的常见景象 → 啄食顺序一词的来源 → 螳螂的性食人 → springbok mantis 的 60% 统计。
- **关键词**：`rich and variegated`、`fixture of the animal world`、`pecking order`、`sexual cannibalism`、`60 percent`
- **表达方式**：用“from polar bears to mollusks”把动物界的食人写成普遍现象；用“60 percent”把“性食人”写成可引用的统计事实。
- **为什么这样写**：作者要先把食人从“人类才有的道德堕落”拉回自然界，后文把它当作“自然现象”来论证才站得住——自然史这一节的作用，是拆除读者预装的道德判断。

> **原句 3:** "The history of cannibalism reveals two things: that there has been a strong taboo against cannibalism in many places and times and that there has also been a great deal of it over the years. Sigmund Freud explained this odd contradiction—the condemnation of cannibalism and the extensive presence of it—by theorizing that mankind is on some level strongly attracted to cannibalism. He regarded it as one of humanity's three great prohibited "instinctual wishes," along with incest and the lust to kill. He believed mankind's innate aggressiveness gives people a repressed desire to eat other people. Civilization had to develop strong taboos against cannibalism, he argued, not because people find it so distasteful but because deep down they have a drive to engage in it."

- **中文理解**：食人史揭示两件事：许多地方和许多时代都存在强烈的食人禁忌，同时这些年来食人行为也大量发生。Freud 用一个理论来解释这个古怪的矛盾——谴责食人与食人广泛存在并存；他认为人类在某个层面上强烈被食人吸引，把食人列为人类三种被禁止的“本能愿望”之一，与乱伦、杀人欲并列。他认为人类天生的攻击性给了人们一种被压抑的食人欲望。文明之所以必须建立强烈的食人禁忌，他的论证是：不是因为人们觉得食人如此令人厌恶，而是因为内心深处他们有实施食人的驱动力。
- **句子结构**：六句递进：史实揭示两件事 → Freud 提出解释 → 三种被禁止的本能愿望 → 攻击性产生被压抑的欲望 → 禁忌为何必须建立 → 结论落在“驱动力”上。
- **关键词**：`odd contradiction`、`instinctual wishes`、`innate aggressiveness`、`repressed desire`
- **表达方式**：用“odd contradiction”把禁忌与现实的并存写成谜题；用“instinctual wishes”把吃人从行为层面推到心理层面。
- **为什么这样写**：这一段是全书的理论支点：作者借 Freud 说明食人之所以需要禁忌，恰恰因为人本身有做它的倾向——这为后文“求生食人是否可被理解”提供心理学依据，也预告了后记里作者对功利主义与权利论的正面交锋。

> **原句 4:** "The cannibalism of Dudley, Stephens, and Brooks was nutritional cannibalism of a specific sort—a subcategory known as survival cannibalism. In extreme conditions, when no other food source is available, humans will consume human flesh to stay alive. Driven by the primal will to survive in extraordinary circumstances such as famines, military sieges, shipwrecks, and arduous journeys, people can take desperate measures. "It is as though," University of California evolutionary psychologist Lewis Petrinovich explains, "the outer coverings of society have been peeled away to reveal the basic core of human nature." The Donner Party, Alfred Packer, and the residents of Europe during the famine of 1315 to 1317 all turned to survival cannibalism. In each of these instances, there was a crisis that made food scarce, and the answer starving people came up with was eating human flesh."

- **中文理解**：Dudley、Stephens 和 Brooks 的食人属于一种特定类型的营养性食人——即所谓的“求生性食人”。在极端条件下，当没有别的食物来源时，人会靠吃人肉活下来。在饥荒、军事围困、海难和艰苦旅程等非凡处境中，求生的原始意志驱使人们采取绝望的手段。“就好像，”加州大学进化心理学家 Lewis Petrinovich 解释说，“社会的外衣被剥掉了，露出人性的基本内核。”Donner Party、Alfred Packer 以及 1315 至 1317 年大饥荒期间的欧洲居民都转向了求生性食人。每一个例子里都有一个让食物匮乏的危机，而饥饿的人们给出的答案就是吃人肉。
- **句子结构**：六句递进：归入类型 → 极端条件下的定义 → 驱动因素 → Petrinovich 的比喻 → 历史例证 → 危机与答案的因果。
- **关键词**：`nutritional cannibalism`、`survival cannibalism`、`"the outer coverings of society have been peeled away"`、`primal will to survive`
- **表达方式**：借 Petrinovich 的比喻把食人写成“剥开社会外衣看见人性内核”；用 Donner Party 等例证把这一子类别接到真实历史上。
- **为什么这样写**：把 Dudley 等人的行为放进“求生性食人”这个现成类别，是为了先削掉其“独特罪孽”的色彩——一旦它成了历史上反复出现的模式，审判要处理的就不再是“三个怪物”，而是所有海上求生者都会面对的极端处境。

> **原句 5:** "The most fundamental dichotomy in cannibalism is whether the person who was eaten was alive or dead. Russia, whose famines and wartime sieges offer an extensive history of cannibalism, has different words for the two distinct acts: Trupoyedstvo is "corpse-eating," and lyudoyedstvo is "person-eating." The distinction is critical, since killing to eat is not only cannibalism but murder, which brings it to a whole other level."

- **中文理解**：食人中最根本的分野，是被吃的那个人生前是活的还是死的。俄罗斯的饥荒与战时围困留下了大量食人史，它为这两种不同的行为准备了两个词：Trupoyedstvo 指“食尸”，lyudoyedstvo 指“食人”。这个区别是关键的——杀人而后食之，不只是食人，还是谋杀，那会把事情推到完全不同的层面上。
- **句子结构**：三句递进：提出分野 → 俄罗斯给出两个词 → 区别为何关键（杀而后食即谋杀）。
- **关键词**：`dichotomy`、`Trupoyedstvo`、`lyudoyedstvo`、`whole other level`
- **表达方式**：用俄罗斯语的两个专名，把抽象的分野写成有词可查的语言事实；用“whole other level”把法律性质的跃升一句话点破。
- **为什么这样写**：提前给出“食尸与食人”的划分，是在为后文的审判定性铺路——Dudley 等人若只是吃了尸体，罪名是食人；杀了人再吃，罪名就是谋杀。

> **原句 6:** "Many people regard corpse-eating with disgust, but people are rarely seriously condemned or punished for eating a dead body in survival conditions. It is widely accepted that the drive to stay alive should outweigh the taboo against eating human flesh. In the Alive incident, a priest told the survivors that the Catholic Church did not regard it as a sin to engage, "in extremis," in cannibalism of dead bodies. In a case of killing someone to eat, the ethical and legal considerations are more complex. Murder is both a grave moral offense in almost every religious and moral tradition and the most serious of crimes. If the men in the Mignonette lifeboat survived, they would be notorious as cannibals for eating Parker's flesh, but it would be the killing of Parker rather than the eating of him that would be the far weightier transgression—and the one that would have the most fateful consequences for them."

- **中文理解**：许多人对食尸感到厌恶，但在生存条件下吃死人尸体的人很少被严厉谴责或惩罚。社会普遍接受这样一条：求生的力量应当压过食人的禁忌。在 Alive 事件中，一位神父告诉幸存者，天主教会并不认为在“极端情况下”吃死尸是罪。而在杀人以食的情形下，伦理与法律的考量要复杂得多。谋杀在几乎所有宗教与道德传统中都是严重的道德罪行，也是最严重的罪行。假如 Mignonette 号救生艇上的这几个人活了下来，他们会因为吃 Parker 的肉而成为声名狼藉的食人者，但真正重得多的过犯——也是给他们带来最致命后果的那一桩——是杀死 Parker 这件事。
- **句子结构**：七句递进：食尸受容忍 → 生存压过禁忌 → 神父的说法 → 杀人食人的复杂性 → 谋杀的性质 → 假设他们生还 → 吃与杀的轻重对比。
- **关键词**：`"in extremis"`、`grave moral offense`、`far weightier transgression`、`fateful consequences`
- **表达方式**：用 Alive 事件与神父的话给“生存条件下食尸”提供合法性依据，再用“far weightier transgression”把轻重之别砸在“杀”上。
- **为什么这样写**：这一段把后文审判的核心区分提前摆到读者面前：吃尸体只是风俗问题，杀人吃人才是法律问题——作者先立好这把标尺，读者后面才会跟着他衡量 Dudley 他们的“必要”。

> **原句 7:** "A disturbing pattern emerges from these cases of men drawing lots at sea. The custom of the sea decreed that decisions about who should be killed should be made through a random process; ideally, it treated everyone on the ship as an equal who deserved the same chance of living or dying as anyone else. But that was not how it worked in practice. The losers in cannibalism drawings were, very disproportionately, outsiders or people at the bottom of the various hierarchies on the ship. On the Dolphin, the one Spaniard on an English crew was said to have drawn the short straw. On the Peggy, the only enslaved man was said to have lost. On the Essex whaleboat, an eighteen-year-old was selected by the older men, and on the Francis Spaight, a fourteen-year-old was chosen in a drawing that included only the boys."

- **中文理解**：从这些海上抽签的案例里浮出一个令人不安的模式。海上的旧俗规定，谁该被杀应当通过随机程序来决定；理想情况下，它把船上的每个人都当作平等的人，给予与他人相同的活下来或死去的运气。但实际运作并非如此。食人抽签中的输家极不成比例地是外来者，或是船上各种等级秩序最底层的人。在 Dolphin 号上，一名英籍船员中的唯一西班牙人据说抽中了短签。在 Peggy 号上，唯一的被奴役者据说输了。在 Essex 号的捕鲸艇上，一个十八岁的青年被年长者选中；在 Francis Spaight 号上，一个十四岁男孩是在只包含男孩的抽签中选出的。
- **句子结构**：七句递进：模式浮现 → 旧俗的规定 → 理想中的平等 → 实际并不平等 → 输家的构成 → 四个例证。
- **关键词**：`disturbing pattern`、`very disproportionately`、`outsiders or people at the bottom`
- **表达方式**：用“very disproportionately”把个案写成系统性偏向；用四个例证（西班牙人、被奴役者、少年）把“最弱者先死”写成历史规律。
- **为什么这样写**：抽签的不公是作者为后文埋的关键：一旦读者接受“抽签从来不是真的随机”，Dudley 事后“抽签”的辩解就失去了分量。

> **原句 8:** "Given these patterns, the selection of Parker was likely, perhaps even inevitable. Parker was at the bottom of every hierarchy in the Mignonette lifeboat. He was the lowest ranking, least paid, youngest, most inexperienced, least capable, last hired, and sickest. Even if Stephens and Brooks had agreed to draw straws, it is not hard to imagine that Dudley would have been inclined to arrange, as captains and crews had been doing for centuries, for the lowliest member of the group to lose the drawing—and that was Parker."

- **中文理解**：有了这些模式在前，Parker 被选中就极有可能，甚至几乎不可避免。Parker 处在 Mignonette 号救生艇上的每一层等级的最底端。他级别最低、工资最少、最年轻、经验最浅、能力最弱、最后被雇来，病得也最重。即便 Stephens 和 Brooks 都同意抽签，也很难想象 Dudley 不会像几个世纪以来船长与船员们一向做的那样，设法让小组里最卑微的那个成员抽输——而那个人就是 Parker。
- **句子结构**：四句递进：结论前置 → Parker 处在每层等级的底端 → 逐项罗列七项劣势 → 回到抽签但预判结果。
- **关键词**：`likely, perhaps even inevitable`、`bottom of every hierarchy`、`lowliest member`
- **表达方式**：用一串并列名词（最低级别、最少工资、最年轻、最没经验、最弱能力、最后雇用、最病重）把“Parker 最脆弱”写成可清点的清单。
- **为什么这样写**：作者把必然性写成清单而不是断言：让读者自己数出 Parker 有几重劣势，也就被说服这不是命运，而是结构。

> **原句 9:** "No less an authority than The Times, one of England's most respected newspapers, declared in the year of the Mignonette voyage that when lots were drawn at sea, the "choice of victim" was not "made with perfect impartiality." The Times observed that the victims usually had something in common. "The weakest will generally be singled out for death," the paper said, and "even if lots be cast, somehow the fate will not fall on the strong man able to defend himself, but on one who is helpless.""

- **中文理解**：权威到无可置疑的《泰晤士报》——英国最受尊敬的报纸之一——在 Mignonette 号航行的当年就宣称：海上抽签时对受害者的挑选“并不是完全公正地做出的”。该报观察到，受害者身上通常有共同点。“最弱者一般会被单独挑出来送死，”报纸写道，“即便抽签，命运也总不会落在那个有能力自卫的强者身上，而会落在一个无助的人身上。”
- **句子结构**：三句递进：权威报纸登场 → 对“公正”的否定 → 观察与两句直接引语。
- **关键词**：`"choice of victim"`、`"made with perfect impartiality"`、`"The weakest will generally be singled out for death"`、`"helpless"`
- **表达方式**：以“最有权威的报纸”开篇，把作者自己的判断转成时代共识；用报纸的直接引语把结论钉在原话上。
- **为什么这样写**：作者不自己下判断，而是让同时代的报纸来说——维多利亚时代的英国已经承认抽签从来不是真随机，这让后文对“必要”的辩解失去了时代庇护。

## 一句话总结

本章把食人从“道德异端”还原为“求生性食人”这一反复出现的模式：食人禁忌之所以普遍，恰恰因为食人本身也普遍（弗洛伊德的“被压抑的欲望”）；而历史上那些抽签的记录显示，抽签从来不是真的随机——出局的多是外来者与最底层的人，Parker 被选中“极有可能，甚至几乎不可避免”。
