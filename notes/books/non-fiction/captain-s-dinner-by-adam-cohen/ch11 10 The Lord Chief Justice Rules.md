# 11. 10 The Lord Chief Justice Rules（大法官的裁决）

## 概览

- **出处**：*The Captain's Dinner*，Adam Cohen 著
- **作者**：Adam Cohen
- **章节定位**：全书第 10 章。叙述 Court of Queen's Bench 审理：Lord Coleridge 主持，attorney general James 主控，Collins 辩护；Lord Coleridge 宣布 "convictions affirmed"（实际 first-time murder finding）；opinion 拒绝 necessity/self-defense，引用 Birkenhead/Christ，援引 Milton's Paradise Lost；sentence death；Harcourt weighs mercy；opinion's legacy (progressive reform, human dignity)
- **字符数**：约 47,000（提取件 `text/ch11_10_the_lord_chief_justice_rules.txt`）
- **一句话主旨**：Lord Coleridge 的 opinion 不仅 first-time found Dudley and Stephens murderers，更把 case 提升为 "progressive reform" 的 landmark——拒绝 necessity/self-defense，引用 Birkenhead drill 与 Christ 的 sacrifice，援引 Milton's Paradise Lost，把 "human dignity" 与 "equality" 写入 English law；Harcourt 与 Queen Victoria 的 mercy decision 留给 ch12

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：Lord Coleridge 的 opinion 是 Victorian progressive reform 的 landmark——拒绝 necessity defense（"awful danger"），拒绝 self-defense（Parker not threatening），引用 Birkenhead drill 与 Christ 的 sacrifice，援引 Milton's Paradise Lost，把 "human dignity" 与 "equality" 写入 English law；Harcourt 与 Queen Victoria 的 mercy decision 留给 ch12。

- **证据链**：

  | 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Lord Coleridge pedigree (Eton, Oxford, Liberal, progressive) | 人物 | 显示 his reformist credentials |
  | Harcourt: progressive reformer, opposed capital punishment | 人物 | 显示 his values (anti-death penalty but duty-bound) |
  | "convictions should be affirmed" (inaccurate wording) | 法律事实 | 显示 court's power + men's shock |

- **论证脉络**：court convenes → Collins's jurisdiction argument → attorney general's case → Collins's necessity defense → Lord Coleridge rejects → "convictions affirmed" → death sentence → Harcourt weighs mercy (guidelines, James's leniency, Harcourt's moral stance, Lewis's advocacy, Queen's harder line) → opinion's legacy (progressive reform, human dignity, equality)

- **可质疑处**：
  1. **Lord Coleridge 的 "sanctimonious" 现代批评**：作者承认现代批评者称其 "sanctimonious"，但坚持结论 "remain as valid today"——可能 overstate。
  2. **"progressive reform" thesis**：作者把 opinion 写成 "Victorian reform" 的 landmark，但 causal link 与 "reform" 是 inferential。
  3. **Harcourt 的 "moral stance"**：作者把 Harcourt 写成 "progressive reformer" but also "duty-bound"——tension 未 fully resolved。

## 一句话总结

本章把 Lord Coleridge 的 opinion 写成 Victorian progressive reform 的 landmark：拒绝 necessity defense（"awful danger" of calculations），拒绝 self-defense（Parker not threatening），引用 Birkenhead drill 与 Christ 的 sacrifice，援引 Milton's Paradise Lost，把 "human dignity" 与 "equality" 写入 English law；"convictions affirmed"（实际 first-time murder finding）；death sentence（black cap omitted = subtle leniency）；Harcourt weighs mercy（guidelines, James's leniency, Harcourt's moral stance, Lewis's advocacy, Queen's harder line）；opinion's legacy（progressive legal edifice, human dignity, equality, "fewer devilish deeds"）。所有这些为后文 mercy (ch12) 与 aftermath (ch13) 提供 "legal" 与 "moral" basis。
