# 12. 11 Fading Away（渐渐淡出）

## 概览

- **出处**：*The Captain's Dinner*，Adam Cohen 著
- **作者**：Adam Cohen
- **章节定位**：全书第 11 章。叙述 verdict 后的 aftermath：全球新闻、public opinion split（elite vs grassroots）、Harcourt's compromise (six months without hard labor)、Queen's pardon、Dudley 的 prison appeal、Lever's appeal to Queen、Dudley's death from bubonic plague in Sydney 1900
- **字符数**：约 31,500（提取件 `text/ch12_11_fading_away.txt`）
- **一句话主旨**：verdict 后，Dudley 和 Stephens 的 "fading away" 是 double：从 public eye 与 from legal memory——Harcourt 的 six-month compromise 与 Queen's pardon 结束 case，Dudley 在 Sydney 死于 bubonic plague（1900），virtually no news account mentioned his past

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：verdict 后，Dudley 和 Stephens 的 "fading away" 是 double：从 public eye 与 from legal memory——Harcourt 的 six-month compromise 与 Queen's pardon 结束 case，Dudley 在 Sydney 死于 bubonic plague（1900），virtually no news account mentioned his past。

- **证据链**：

  | 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |

  | Northern Echo: "free pardon" prediction | 媒体 | 显示 grassroots sympathy |
  | The Times: "mawkish, ill-directed sympathy" | 媒体 | 显示 elite disapproval |

  | Parker memorial tablet: "Lord lay not this sin to their charge" | 事实 | 显示 family's ambiguous stance |

  | Lever's appeal to Queen: Philippa's letter | 人物/事实 | 显示 supporters' advocacy |
  | "Captain Dudley, a sailmaker" | 事实 | 显示 erasure of past |

- **论证脉络**：verdict news → public opinion split (elite vs grassroots) → Harcourt's compromise → Queen's pardon → prison conditions → Dudley's appeal → Lever's appeal → Parker memorial → Dudley's death from plague → "fading away"

- **可质疑处**：
  1. **"public opinion" 的两极化**：作者把 public 写成 "elite vs grassroots"，可能 oversimplify。
  2. **Harcourt's "compromise"**：作者把 six months 写成 "Victorian reform compromise"，但 Harcourt 的 decision 可能是 pragmatic (avoiding outcry) 而非 reformist.
  3. **Dudley's "remorse"**：Dudley 的 "It makes my blood run cold" 可能是 strategic (appeal for pardon) 而非 genuine remorse.
