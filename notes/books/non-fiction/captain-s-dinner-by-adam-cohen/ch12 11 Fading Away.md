# 12. 11 Fading Away（渐渐淡出）

## 概览

- **出处**：*The Captain's Dinner*，Adam Cohen 著
- **作者**：Adam Cohen
- **章节定位**：全书第 11 章。叙述 verdict 后的 aftermath：全球新闻、public opinion split（elite vs grassroots）、Harcourt's compromise (six months without hard labor)、Queen's pardon、Dudley 的 prison appeal、Lever's appeal to Queen、Dudley's death from bubonic plague in Sydney 1900
- **字符数**：约 31,500（提取件 `text/ch12_11_fading_away.txt`）
- **一句话主旨**：verdict 后，Dudley 和 Stephens 的 "fading away" 是 double：从 public eye 与 from legal memory——Harcourt 的 six-month compromise 与 Queen's pardon 结束 case，Dudley 在 Sydney 死于 bubonic plague（1900），virtually no news account mentioned his past

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：verdict 后，Dudley 和 Stephens 的 "fading away" 是 double：从 public eye 与 from legal memory——Harcourt 的 six-month compromise 与 Queen's pardon 结束 case，Dudley 在 Sydney 死于 bubonic plague（1900），virtually no news account mentioned his past。

- **证据链**：

- **论证脉络**：verdict news → public opinion split (elite vs grassroots) → Harcourt's compromise → Queen's pardon → prison conditions → Dudley's appeal → Lever's appeal → Parker memorial → Dudley's death from plague → "fading away"

- **可质疑处**：
  1. **"public opinion" 的两极化**：作者把 public 写成 "elite vs grassroots"，可能 oversimplify。
  2. **Harcourt's "compromise"**：作者把 six months 写成 "Victorian reform compromise"，但 Harcourt 的 decision 可能是 pragmatic (avoiding outcry) 而非 reformist.
  3. **Dudley's "remorse"**：Dudley 的 "It makes my blood run cold" 可能是 strategic (appeal for pardon) 而非 genuine remorse.

> **原句 2:** "It was certainly possible to comb through the details of the case and conclude that some form of mercy might come. The Exeter jury had recommended it, and Baron Huddleston had as well. The lord chief justice had publicly said that he and the other justices all supported bestowing the royal prerogative of mercy. Noting that Lord Coleridge had not worn the black cap, news stories took it as a sign that Dudley and Stephens would not be executed. But all this was just guesswork and supposition. In predicting a pardon, newspapers may simply have been telling their readers what they wanted to hear. "The men were virtually pardoned by public opinion before they were put on trial," The Daily Telegraph observed. Now there was "universal agreement," The Daily News reported, that the death sentence should not be carried out."

- **中文理解**：确实有可能梳理案件细节并得出某种形式的 mercy 可能到来的结论。Exeter 陪审团推荐了 mercy，Baron Huddleston 也推荐了。大法官公开说过他和其他法官都支持授予 royal prerogative of mercy。注意到 Lord Coleridge 没戴黑帽，新闻报道把它作为 Dudley 和 Stephens 不会被处决的迹象。但所有这些只是猜测和推测。在预测 pardon 时，报纸可能只是在告诉读者他们想听的。"这些人实际上在受审前就被公众意见赦免了，"《每日电讯报》观察到。现在"universal agreement"，《每日新闻》报道，死刑不应执行。
- **句子结构**：八句：possible mercy → Exeter jury → Baron Huddleston → Lord Coleridge → black cap → guesswork → "virtually pardoned" → "universal agreement"。

> **原句 5:** "It was a compromise that embodied the Victorian reform spirit. Harcourt stuck to his conviction that Dudley and Stephens had committed a serious crime. In doing so, he was siding with the sick seventeen-year-old Parker, the lowliest person in the lifeboat, and insisting that taking his life was murder—"the highest crime known to the law." By calling for Dudley and Stephens to face serious punishment, Harcourt was sending the message that Victorian reformers hoped the case would deliver: that murder at sea in conjunction with survival cannibalism was still murder and would be punished as such—even if it was the captain doing it and the cabin boy being killed. If the queen adopted Harcourt's recommendation, a centuries-old tradition of allowing survival cannibalism would come to an end."

- **中文理解**：这是一个体现维多利亚改革精神的妥协。Harcourt 坚持认为 Dudley 和 Stephens 犯了严重罪行。这样做时，他站在生病的十七岁 Parker——救生艇上最低微的人——一边，坚持取他的命是谋杀——"法律所知的最重罪行"。通过要求 Dudley 和 Stephens 面临严重惩罚，Harcourt 正在传递维多利亚改革者希望案件传达的信息：海上生存食人结合谋杀仍然是谋杀，将受到相应惩罚——即使做的是船长，被杀的是 cabin boy。如果女王采纳 Harcourt 的建议，允许生存食人的几百年传统将告终。
- **句子结构**：五句：compromise → stuck to conviction → siding with Parker → sending message → centuries-old tradition end。
- **关键词**：`"the highest crime known to the law"`、`"centuries-old tradition of allowing survival cannibalism would come to an end"`

- **为什么这样写**：作者在此把 Harcourt 写成 "ending tradition"——这种 "ending" 为后文 "legacy" 提供 "significance" basis（Harcourt ended centuries-old tradition）。

> **原句 7:** "Dudley had the shortest and most eventful life after the Mignonette. > **原句 8:** "Dudley's was the first official death of Sydney's bubonic plague epidemic of 1900. It went on to take 103 lives in the first nine months of that year and 535 in a series of outbreaks that recurred through 1925. Dudley's body sat unattended for days, then was wrapped in sailcloth soaked in 5 percent sulfuric acid, placed in a watertight coffin with more acid, and wrapped again in acid-soaked sailcloth and a layer of asbestos cloth. The body was then taken by skiff to a cemetery in the city's remote quarantine station and unceremoniously deposited in a grave of "unusual depth.""
