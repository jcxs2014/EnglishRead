# 07. 6 A Bad Time to Be a Cannibal（当食人不再被容忍）

## 概览

- **出处**：*The Captain's Dinner*，Adam Cohen 著
- **作者**：Adam Cohen
- **章节定位**：全书第 6 章。分析 Dudley 等人面临的法律困境：necessity defense 的不确定性、Holmes 案的 precedent、Bacon/Blackstone vs Hale 的评论家分歧、Victorian reform 与 "civilizing process"、cannibalism 作为 "barbarism" 的帝国话语、Euxine 案的 near-prosecution
- **字符数**：约 29,000（提取件 `text/ch07_6_a_bad_time_to_be_a_cannibal.txt`）
- **一句话主旨**：1884 年是"最糟的食人之年"——necessity defense 无 precedent，Holmes 案要求 draw lots（Dudley 未 draw），Victorian reform 与 "civilizing process" 使 cannibalism 不再被容忍，Euxine 案显示 Board of Trade 已开始认真考虑 prosecution

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：Dudley 等人的 legal defense 面临多重不利：necessity defense 在 English law 无 precedent；Holmes 案要求 draw lots（Dudley 未 draw）；Victorian reform 与 "civilizing process" 使 cannibalism 从 "acceptable" 变为 "barbaric"；Euxine 案显示 Board of Trade 已开始认真考虑 prosecution。

- **证据链**：

  | 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |
  |---|---|---|
  | "All the elements of the crime of murder were present" | 法律事实 | 显示 prosecution case 强 |
  | Necessity defense：Glanville Williams "lesser of two evils" | 法律理论 | 解释 defense 的 legal basis |
  | United States v. Holmes (William Brown, 1841) | 先例 | 显示 necessity defense 的 strict conditions（draw lots） |
  | Francis Bacon & William Blackstone endorsement | 评论家 | 显示 necessity defense 的 influential support |
  | Mathew Hale opposition | 评论家 | 显示 opposing view（dangerous doctrine） |
  | Victorian reform：Merchant Shipping Act, child protection, "cult of the child" | 社会史 | 显示 legal landscape 变化 |
  | Norbert Elias "civilizing process" / "expanding threshold of repugnance" | 社会学 | 解释 violence 的 decline |
  | Cannibalism as "prime symbol of barbarism" | 文化话语 | 显示 1884 是 "最糟的食人之年" |
  | Euxine case (1874) near-prosecution | 历史 | 显示 Board of Trade 已开始考虑 prosecution |

- **论证脉络**：prosecution case 强 → necessity defense 的不确定性 → Holmes 案（precedent + strict conditions）→ Bacon/Blackstone vs Hale → Victorian reform（maritime laws, child protection）→ "civilizing process" → cannibalism as "barbarism" → Euxine near-prosecution → "poised to end long tradition"

- **可质疑处**：
  1. **"Victorian reform" thesis 的 causal strength**：作者把 "civilizing process" 与 prosecution 倾向 linked，但 causal chain 是 inferential。
  2. **Holmes 案的 "strict conditions"**：Justice Baldwin 的 jury instructions 是 one judge's view，not binding precedent in England。
  3. **Euxine 案的 "near-prosecution"**：Board of Trade "considered" 但 didn't prosecute；作者的 "poised to end" 是 extrapolation。

## 一句话总结

本章把 Dudley 等人的 legal defense 写成 "uphill battle"：prosecution case "straightforward"（所有 murder elements present + sworn statements + murder weapon）；necessity defense 在 English law 无 precedent，Holmes 案提供 "helpful" 但 "not helpful at all"（Dudley 未 draw lots，American court not binding）；Bacon/Blackstone endorsement vs Hale "dangerous doctrine" 的评论家分歧；Victorian reform（Merchant Shipping Act, child protection, "cult of the child"）与 "civilizing process"（Elias）使 cannibalism 从 "acceptable" 变为 "barbaric"；1884 是 "最糟的食人之年"（Seeley's empire discussion, cannibalism as "prime symbol of barbarism"）；Euxine 案（1874）near-prosecution 显示 Board of Trade 已开始认真考虑 prosecution——"poised to end that long tradition"。所有这些为后文审判的 "guilty" 提供 historical context。
