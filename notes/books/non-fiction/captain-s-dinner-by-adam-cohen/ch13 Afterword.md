# 13. Afterword（后记）

## 概览

- **出处**：*The Captain's Dinner*，Adam Cohen 著
- **作者**：Adam Cohen
- **章节定位**：全书后记。回溯 Dudley and Stephens 案的文化/法律/哲学遗产：Life of Pi 的 Richard Parker、法律影响（England & US）、philosophical significance（utilitarianism vs rights）、Victorian reformers 的 concerted efforts、Lord Coleridge 的 opinion、modern applications（medicine, torture, AI/self-driving cars）、Victorian reform ethic 的 best elements
- **字符数**：约 24,000（提取件 `text/ch13_afterword.txt`）
- **一句话主旨**：Afterword 把 Dudley and Stephens 从"食人案"提升为 "soaring legal tower at one end of a great philosophical divide"——utilitarianism vs rights-based moral theories；Lord Coleridge 的 opinion 不仅 changed law of cannibalism at sea forever，更 championed human rights 与 equality；modern applications（medicine, torture, AI）显示 case 的 ongoing relevance

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：Dudley and Stephens 不仅 changed law of cannibalism at sea，更 championed human rights 与 equality——Lord Coleridge 的 opinion 是 "soaring legal tower at one end of a great philosophical divide"，其 lessons speak to modern world (medicine, torture, AI)。

- **证据链**：

  | 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Life of Pi 的 Richard Parker（Yann Martel） | 文化 | 显示 case 的 cultural afterlife |
  | 案件影响 English law (Lord Denning 1971) | 法律 | 显示 case 的 enduring legal significance |
  | 案件影响 American law (Holmes 1881 → Cardozo 1931 → 2012 professor) | 法律 | 显示 case's broader impact |
  | Michael Sandel's "Justice" course | 哲学 | 显示 case 的 philosophical significance |
  | Three Victorian reformers (Huddleston, Harcourt, Coleridge) | 历史 | 显示 case 的 "concerted efforts" |
  | Lord Coleridge's rejection of utilitarian calculus | 哲学 | 显示 case's anti-utilitarian stance |
  | Modern applications (medicine/ventilators, torture/ticking bomb, AI/self-driving cars) | 现代 | 显示 case's ongoing relevance |
  | Victorian reform ethic: human dignity, equality, human rights | 社会/哲学 | 显示 case's broader significance |

- **论证脉络**：cultural afterlife (Life of Pi) → legal impact (England & US) → philosophical significance (utilitarianism vs rights, Sandel) → three reformers → Lord Coleridge's anti-utilitarian stance → modern applications (medicine, torture, AI) → Victorian reform ethic's best elements → final image (staring out into fog)

- **可质疑处**：
  1. **"modern applications" 的 stretch**：作者把 case 应用到 medicine/torture/AI，可能 over-extend——case 是关于 "killing to eat"，not all utilitarian calculations.
  2. **"Victorian reform ethic" 的 romanticization**：作者把 Victorian era 写成 "best" reform era，可能 overlook 其 imperialist/colonial aspects.
  3. **"human rights" 的 anachronism**：作者用 "human rights" 描述 Lord Coleridge's opinion，但 1884 年 "human rights" 不是 current terminology.

## 一句话总结

Afterword 把 Dudley and Stephens 从"食人案"提升为 "soaring legal tower at one end of a great philosophical divide"——utilitarianism vs rights-based moral theories；Lord Coleridge 的 opinion 不仅 changed law of cannibalism at sea forever，更 championed human rights 与 equality；modern applications（medicine/ventilators, torture/ticking bomb, AI/self-driving cars）显示 case 的 ongoing relevance；Victorian reform ethic 的 best elements（human dignity, equality, human rights）与 worst elements（imperialism, colonialism）并存；final image：Parker "staring out into the fog undisturbed, searching for a mast and a sail"。All this provides the book's final thesis: no matter how low people are on the hierarchy, their lives belong to them and them alone.
