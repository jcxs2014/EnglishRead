# 18. Chapter 17: The Deal

## 概览

- **出处**：Doreen Carvajal, *The Girl in Blue*（Grand Central Publishing, 2026），第 17 章 "The Deal"
- **作者**：Doreen Carvajal，记者、作家，二十余年欧洲文化报道
- **章节定位**：章头自标时间 "1947 to 1949"。主轴是 1949 年 5–10 月这五个月的成交过程（1949-06-08 Feuz 的电话与信、1949-06-11 卡片、1949-08-27 Irène 在 Cannes 待客、1949-09 画入苏黎世、1949-10-21 支票）；前段回叙 1947 年初 Wildenstein 的开价、1946 年卢浮宫的据称出价，中段插入 1945–1949 年对 Bührle 的指控与索还诉讼，旁及 1949 年 5 月 Saint-Germain-en-Laye 的纪念仪式与 1951 年 Witt 卖画的旁例。本章提取件没有 Part 页，章内也没有标示所属部分的文字——Part 归属属本章之外的推断
- **字符数**：14,073（text/ 提取件）
- **一句话主旨**：这一章写的不是"画被卖掉"，而是三方各就各位的一场合谋：卖方拒绝法国却接受一个"不装"的买主，掮客用一句客气的假话把劫掠史改写成来源，国家一边喊"national emergency"一边拿不出钱；最后画出境时，收据、出口许可、档案盒里全是空的

## 论证结构

**核心论点**：本章把 "The Deal" 拆成三方，而每一方都靠"没有文件"来完成自己的那一半。**卖方**：Irène 拒绝了 Georges Wildenstein 的五万法郎，也拒绝了（据称）卢浮宫的一千二百万法郎——本章给出的理由不是价格而是资格："But France had hunted her family"，法国看守在 Drancy 把人押进大门，卢浮宫里则留着"youtres, wood lice"这一类信件；于是反问成立："Why should she give them the portrait? She didn’t."她等到 1949 年，选了一个"没有荣誉伪装"的对手（"Bührle offered something simple: a cold transaction. A price. Privacy."），并按银行家孙女的方式算钱——知道瑞士法郎在跌、要在跌之前拿到现款。**买方**：Bührle 的身份先被坐实（向 Reich 卖防空与反潜装备、Cooper 统计的"thirteen of seventy-seven"、手里的 Matisse 出自 1941 年 Göring 与 Rochlitz 的交换、民事审判败诉后"methodically, he bought it all back"），再由 Feuz 替他完成不可见的一半——把巴伐利亚农舍、Jeu de Paume 与交换全部删掉，只留一句"always remained in the same private collection"，作者定罪为"It was a lie made to sound like provenance."。**国家**：Jaujard 称之为 national emergency，Salles 建议 Block the export，但预算表把立场戳穿——全部法国博物馆的年度收购预算 30 million francs，抵不过一幅画的 18 million francs；程序到 1949 年 8 月还在拒发许可，画却在 9 月已进了苏黎世。作者的落点是档案学意义上的：本章的终局证据不是文件而是缺席——没有开给 Irène 的收据、没有出口许可、Pierrefitte 的档案盒里连三个名字都不在，"Only absence."

**论证脉络**：起（1947 年有人开价，她说 no）→ 反直觉（她拒绝的不是价格而是国家："But France had hunted her family"）→ 换角（1949 年瑞士买主出面：掮客 Feuz、真买主 Bührle）→ 插叙（1949 年 5 月 Saint-Germain-en-Laye 的雨与纪念碑——本章所有价格都建在这一家人已经不在的事实上）→ 证据（一通电话与一封信：伪造的来源、被删的农舍与 Jeu de Paume、写信的地址是 Lutetia）→ 对抗（官方的惊慌、预算、Derain 交换、8 月仍在拒绝）→ 推论（卖方动机：怕拍卖、懂汇率、要现款，Feuz 的"礼貌版本"与 1951 年 Witt 的旁例）→ 落点（9 月画已出境、10-21 支票付给掮客、档案无痕——"Only absence."）。

**可质疑处**：
1. 同一章内的换算口径互相不能对齐，而且没有注明基年。法郎对美元的"今日等值"在本章出现四种比率："Fifty thousand francs" → "more than half a million dollars"（逾 ×10）；12 million francs → $1.9 million（约 ×0.16）；30 million francs → $1.2 million（约 ×0.04）；18 million francs → $700,000（约 ×0.039）。更直接的是同一场交易有两个今日币值：Salles 口中的 Bührle 出价（18 million francs）折成 "in today’s dollars" 是 70 万美元，而 1949-10-21 那张 240,000 瑞士法郎的支票折成 "almost $2 million in today’s dollars"——两者相差近三倍，作者未说明这是同一笔钱的不同算法、还是两次不同报价。瑞士法郎一侧同样对不齐：6 月 11 日卡片上的 200,000 折为 "roughly $1.3 million now"（×6.5），10 月 21 日的 240,000 却折成近 200 万美元（约 ×8）；而本章明写"she knew the Swiss franc was falling"——若瑞士法郎在跌，四个月后的换算比率不该更高。以上全部只用本章自己的数字，本章没有任何一处写明换算的基年与汇率口径。
2. "禁令为何失效"用动机填了证据的坑。作者的写法是先给事实（"And yet, somehow, the painting moved… There was no documentation."），再给解释（"Perhaps it was inevitable: Bührle thrived in opacity."），并举他烧毁战时记录为凭——但被烧的是"records from the war years"与"his company’s annual reports from 1939 to 1945"，这两样都解释不了**法国**1949 年的出口许可为何不存在；许可是法国行政机关发出的文件，其缺席只能由法国档案说明，而本章对法国一侧只写了"An archivist specializing in cultural affairs searched again"。把买方的毁证习惯当成国家程序失灵的成因，本章没有给出中间的链条。
3. 卖方动机取自本章已定性为撒谎者的同一批信件。Feuz 写下"The Countess de S. has decided to sell the picture of her youth"的理由，作者称之为"That was the polite version"，随即把"怕上拍卖"当作 Irène 的实际打算写入叙事；另一条"要现金"的旁证是 1951 年 Witt 的例子（时间在她之后），至于 Pussy 逼卖的说法既由"Some of Irène’s descendants speculated"承担，又被"her name isn’t anywhere in Bührle’s archive"否掉——两条动机都建立在掮客与传闻上。同段那句"before they took her"（她/它指代不明：可以指画，也可以指被带走的人）本章未作澄清。

## 选择性精读
> **原句 1:** "But France had hunted her family. It had targeted their properties, including Béatrice’s Neuilly apartment, and Villa Béatrice in Aumont. In Drancy, French guards had forced them inside its gates."

**中文理解**：可是法国追捕过她的家人。法国盯上这一家的财产，包括 Béatrice 在 Neuilly 的公寓，还有 Aumont 的 Villa Béatrice。在 Drancy，是法国看守把他们押进大门里面。

**句子结构**：三句，主语一路是 France / It / French guards，动词逐级逼近人身：hunted（追猎）、targeted（瞄准财产）、forced（动手押人）。第二句用 including 把抽象的 properties 落成两个具体门牌；第三句的时间地点状语 In Drancy 前置，主语让位给看守。

**关键词**：France had hunted / targeted their properties / Béatrice’s Neuilly apartment / Villa Béatrice / Drancy / French guards / forced them inside its gates

**表达方式**："French guards" 三个字是全段最重的定语——押送人的不是占领者而是本国警察；"inside its gates" 只写方向不写暴力，门是为里面的人关上的。

**为什么这样写**：本章从两次被拒的开价开始。画商 Georges Wildenstein 出到 "Fifty thousand francs for La Petite Irène"，"In today’s terms it would be more than half a million dollars"，后面只跟四个词——"But Irène said no."卢浮宫也没买到：归国展将尽时"the Louvre supposedly proposed 12 million francs"，而这个数字出自"one of the competing art brokers, who might have exaggerated the prices to a collector"——价格在本章同时是证据和传闻。这三句回答的正是开价之后没问出口的问题：法国凭什么要这幅画。作者随后才补上卢浮宫的另一面——"And at the Louvre, where they now pretended reverence, there had been other kinds of records—private letters, internal correspondence—of cultivated men referring to Jews with slurs and casual malice: youtres, wood lice."——"pretended reverence" 与本章前文的捐赠史（Château de Champs-sur-Marne、Camondo 博物馆，以及她儿子"had died in uniform for France"）对折。于是反问顺理成章："Why should she give them the portrait? She didn’t."本章的卖方立场在此立住：她不是没有买家，她是没有一个有资格的法国买家。

> **原句 2:** "But everyone knew who the real buyer was. Emil Bührle. He was Zurich’s arms king, the man who had sold the Reich its antiaircraft and submarine defenses, who had built his fortune out of iron, precision, and ambition."

**中文理解**：但人人都知道真正的买主是谁。Emil Bührle。他是苏黎世的军火王——把高射炮和潜艇防御系统卖给第三帝国的那个人，靠铁、精度和野心垒起家业。

**句子结构**：三句，第二句只有一个专名（Emil Bührle.），独立成句像点名。第三句由 the man who… who… 两个定语从句拉长，末尾三个名词并列 iron, precision, and ambition——把工业史写成性格剖面。

**关键词**：the real buyer / Emil Bührle / Zurich’s arms king / sold the Reich / antiaircraft and submarine defenses / iron, precision, and ambition

**表达方式**："arms king" 是给资本加冕的头衔式隐喻；"out of iron, precision, and ambition" 用造军火的三种原料写他怎么造钱，其中 precision 一词双关——既是精密加工，也是一丝不苟的自我形象。

**为什么这样写**：上一段交代出面者是谁："Werner Feuz, a landscape painter, who in reality was a middleman, a broker"；这一段揭底牌。作者在同段给出指控的形成方式——"During and after the war, the accusations came—gossip first, then investigative files"，以及指控的内容——"That he had bought paintings stolen from Jewish families, Jewish galleries, Jewish walls"：三连从家里、画廊到墙上，赃源越来越私人。Bührle 的自辩收在同一章的公文语体里（"the industrialist said, defending himself in writing to military investigators"），末句是"Because I am a man of honor."作者把这句与他的军火生意并排摆放，本章"体面／不体面"的标尺由此翻转：Irène 要的恰恰是"没有荣誉伪装"的那一个。

> **原句 3:** "Then came the restitution claims, a civil trial pressed by a victim, and the verdict. Bührle lost and was ordered to return what he had taken. And then, methodically, he bought it all back."

**中文理解**：接着来了索还请求、一位受害者提起的民事诉讼，以及判决。Bührle 败诉，被判返还他所拿走的东西。然后，有条不紊地，他把一切又买了回来。

**句子结构**：三句。第一句 Then came 倒装，把三件事排成一个清单（索还请求、民事诉讼、判决）；第二句主语换成 Bührle，lost 与 was ordered 一主动一被动连用；第三句用 And then 重起，副词 methodically 单独夹在两个逗号之间——节奏被人为放慢。

**关键词**：restitution claims / a civil trial / pressed by a victim / the verdict / lost / was ordered to return / methodically / bought it all back

**表达方式**："pressed by a victim" 一词点出原告身份：追索靠的是受害者个人的诉讼，不是国家的意志；"methodically" 是全段最冷的词——有条不紊本是褒义，这里形容把已经判还的东西再一件件买回去。

**为什么这样写**：这一节把买主的司法背景补齐：他不只是买赃，还在法庭上输过，然后用钱把判决绕回去。作者的量化支撑来自英国军事情报官员 Douglas Cooper 的调查——"purchasing thirteen of seventy-seven important paintings uncovered in Switzerland"；而 Bührle 手里那幅 Matisse 摩尔舞者出自 1941 年 Göring 与 Rochlitz 的交换，Rochlitz 正是把这幅 Renoir 藏进巴伐利亚农舍的人。这段的言外之意是：在瑞士这一侧，被起获的赃画没有回到原主手里，而是落进 Bührle 这样的买主收藏——所以 Irène 面对的并不是一位"干净的"买家，而是一位已被判过、也把判决买回去的买家。

> **原句 4:** "They had come to remember the three intellectual brothers—Joseph, Salomon, Théodore—and the son and grandsons, including Léon, the composer who had died in Auschwitz. Five years had passed since his death. The rain fell without ceremony."

**中文理解**：他们来纪念三位知识分子的兄弟——Joseph、Salomon、Théodore——以及儿子与孙辈，包括那位在 Auschwitz 去世的作曲家 Léon。他去世已经五年。雨落下来，没有任何仪式。

**句子结构**：三句。第一句主干 remember 后挂两个长宾语（three intellectual brothers 加破折号内三个名字，再 and the son and grandsons），最后由 including Léon 收到一个人身上；第二句是最短的时间陈述；第三句 The rain fell without ceremony 只五个词，把整场典礼收进天气。

**关键词**：the three intellectual brothers / Joseph / Salomon / Théodore / the son and grandsons / Léon / the composer / died in Auschwitz / Five years / without ceremony

**表达方式**："without ceremony" 让雨接管了典礼——没有致辞的规格，只有降水的规格；名字逐个念出来（Joseph, Salomon, Théodore）是纪念碑的写法，作者照抄了铭文的语序；"before they took her" 一句里 her 的指代（画还是人）本章不点明。

**为什么这样写**：这场纪念在 1949 年 5 月一个下雨的下午，地点是 Saint-Germain-en-Laye 一条已用她亡婿姓氏命名的街——"on the street that now bore a tribute to the last name of her late son-in-law—Léon Reinach—who had fought to protect her portrait"。作者紧接着写下本章最刺的一句缺席判词："There was no mention of the other hard choices—of the letters Léon had written, of the portrait of La Petite Irène he tried to save before they took her."发言者只肯谈音乐上的清苦（"In choosing the hard path, he made the right choice"），不谈他写过的那些信、不谈他没保住的画。这一段插在两组报价之间：本章所有的"价格"都建立在这一家人已经不在的事实上，而纪念仪式把这件事再次略过。

> **原句 5:** "The sales price was available upon request, and then Feuz added a line, almost courteous in its falseness: “I would like to mention that this painting has, until today, always remained in the same private collection.”"

**中文理解**：售价"索阅即供"，然后 Feuz 加了一句，假得近乎客气："我想提一句，这幅画直到今天，一直留在同一个私人收藏手里。"

**句子结构**：一句。前半 The sales price was available upon request 用被动式把定价权推给买方去问；后半 and then Feuz added a line 挂一个后置修饰 almost courteous in its falseness，冒号之后才放出直接引语——引语本身是 I would like to mention 的商务谦辞，套着 until today / always / the same 三个绝对词。

**关键词**：sales price / upon request / added a line / courteous in its falseness / until today / always remained / the same private collection

**表达方式**："almost courteous in its falseness" 是作者的判词嵌在叙述句里——假话的修辞品质被单独打分；引语用"我想提一句"这种最不惹眼的礼貌句式，包装一句彻底的隐瞒：所谓"同一个私人收藏"抹掉的是 1941 年那次抄家与转手。

**为什么这样写**：前一段是 1949 年 6 月 8 日早晨的那通电话（"picked up the phone in Paris and dialed Bührle’s Zurich office, their correspondence shows"）：Feuz 催着直接见老板，说有 Renoir 杰作可售，吹自己拿到"an exclusive one-week option"、承诺寄彩色印刷品作为"proof of life"。这一段之后，作者给这句话定罪："It was a lie made to sound like provenance."并给它归类："The kind of omission that passed for elegance in the art world, where silence was a form of good manners."被漏掉的清单本章逐项列出——巴伐利亚的农舍、"the canvas rolled tight and numbered by Nazi bureaucrats with lists"、"the Jeu de Paume, the exchange with Göring, the men who turned theft into a swap"。来源（provenance）在这里第一次露出它的真实功能：不是记录，是说谎的格式。

> **原句 6:** "Feuz wrote some of his letters to Bührle from the Hôtel Lutetia, which by then had gone back to being a hotel but still carried the weight of what it had become. A place where survivors were delivered, one by one, after the war. Feuz sat there, in that same building."

**中文理解**：Feuz 有几封信是从 Hôtel Lutetia 写给 Bührle 的。那时这栋楼已经重新做回一家旅馆，但仍压着它曾经变成的那个东西的分量。战后，幸存者被一个接一个送到这里。Feuz 就坐在这同一栋楼里。

**句子结构**：三句。第一句 which 从句做对折，gone back to being a hotel 与 still carried the weight 一轻一重；第二句是无主句的名词短语（A place where survivors were delivered），悬在前句之后当补语；第三句 Feuz sat there 极短，靠指示词 in that same building 把两件不相容的事钉进同一个坐标。

**关键词**：letters / Hôtel Lutetia / gone back to being a hotel / carried the weight / survivors / delivered / one by one / in that same building

**表达方式**："gone back to being a hotel" 用"重新做起某行"的句法写一栋建筑的履历，仿佛它也曾改过职业；"delivered" 是货运动词，用在幸存者身上；"same" 是全段的重音——同一地址、两种用途、同时存在。

**为什么这样写**：本章把道德地理压进一个门牌：替军火商收画的掮客，在幸存者被送回巴黎的那栋楼里写商务信。作者随即补上他的职业知识："As a professional art broker, he would have known the painting’s most recent provenance—that it had been exhibited in l’Orangerie’s 1946 show of recovered works from Germany."知道，然后照旧不提——同段上文还有一个谎（他写信说此画卢浮宫 1945 年展过，而真相是"not returned to France until March 1946"）。段末的落点不是愤怒而是一句冷陈述："What mattered was that a Renoir could be bought."到这里，本章的"deal"已经不由买卖双方定义，而由谁愿意不去追问定义。

> **原句 7:** "His advice: Block the export. Keep the portrait in France. But he admitted that the state’s total acquisition budget of 30 million francs—about $1.2 million in modern dollars—for all of France’s museums did not have enough to spare to compete with Bührle’s offer for the equivalent of 18 million francs—or $700,000 in today’s dollars—for just one portrait."

**中文理解**：他的建议是：挡住出口，把画留在法国。但他承认，全法国博物馆的国家收购预算总共三千万法郎（今值约一百二十万美元），拿不出余款去和 Bührle 为一幅画开出的等值一千八百万法郎（今值约七十万美元）竞争。

**句子结构**：三句。第一句只有 His advice 加两个祈使句（Block the export. Keep the portrait in France.），无主语、无动词变化；第二句由 he admitted 领起一个 that 从句，谓语落在 did not have enough to spare，中间由两对破折号各插入一个今日换算；for all of France’s museums 与 for just one portrait 一头一尾，把整句夹成一道减法。

**关键词**：His advice / Block the export / Keep the portrait in France / admitted / total acquisition budget / 30 million francs / did not have enough to spare / 18 million francs / for just one portrait

**表达方式**：两个祈使句的短与第二句的长形成对照——立场一句话，预算一句话都说不完；"for all of France’s museums… for just one portrait" 是本章最直白的一张资产负债表，国家与个人被放进同一行。

**为什么这样写**："His" 指国家博物馆馆长 Georges Salles——"Georges Salles, director of the national museums, suggested a countermove to the Ministry of Education."，他给教育部的措辞是"To avoid unnecessary maneuvers of potential buyers—in particular a Swiss amateur…"，作者写这句如何留下："The phrase hung there, diplomatic, coded. Everyone knew who it meant."本章官方一侧的另一端是 Jaujard——当年把胜利女神像与蒙娜丽莎经乡间公路撤走、把法国珍藏存进石灰岩城堡的人——他"called it a national emergency"，公文里说艺术品外流"more and more worrying"。可作者给出的结论是程序挡得住、钱挡不住：8 月教育部仍守线，卢浮宫首席策展人被告知若有人敢申请出口许可，外汇处要"issue a refusal"；Feuz 甚至提议用他的 Derain 换这幅 Renoir（"Derain for Irène. One painting to get another."），卢浮宫拒绝。于是本章的转折句只需要五个词："And yet, somehow, the painting moved."

> **原句 8:** "Feuz, the broker, was unraveling. He called Irène’s banker repeatedly. He complained to Bührle’s art adviser that “the Countess would not concede even one-sixth of the price because of her stubbornness.”"

**中文理解**：掮客 Feuz 正在散架。他一遍接一遍地打给 Irène 的银行家。他向 Bührle 的艺术顾问抱怨："伯爵夫人因为固执，连价格的六分之一都不肯让。"

**句子结构**：三句，主语一路是 Feuz / He / He，动词逐级降级：was unraveling（状态）、called（动作）、complained（诉苦）。第三句把引语压进 that 从句，one-sixth 做数量成分，stubbornness 收尾——被抱怨的一方在句法上始终是主语。

**关键词**：the broker / unraveling / Irène’s banker / complained / Bührle’s art adviser / the Countess / concede / one-sixth of the price / stubbornness

**表达方式**："was unraveling" 把谈判的崩解写成织物脱线；"the Countess" 是卖方在这批信件里剩下的全部身份——一个封号加一种性格；让步被精确到"one-sixth"，本章唯一以分数计价的词。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是卖方夺回主动权的时刻。上一段她刚加了码（"She wanted more: 260,000 Swiss francs."），而理由写得极短——"She was a banker’s daughter and granddaughter after all. She understood currencies, and she knew the Swiss franc was falling. She wanted it immediately."她要的是在货币贬值之前拿到现款。本章给她的动机有两条：一是怕画在她死后上拍卖——Feuz 的"礼貌版本"写着"The Countess de S. has decided to sell the picture of her youth"，理由是"It is to prevent the work from being sold at auction after her death"，作者补的是她没有说出口的那一半：Irène 清楚画在拍卖行会变成什么——"They became currency. They became spectacle."，而她要避开"her daughter Pussy might sell it at auction after her death"；二是现金——旁证是 1951 年 Witt 的话："We needed money. And he was the only person who had the cash in his pocket."作者的判词收尾："It wasn’t just his obsession for art. It was his liquidity."

> **原句 9:** "There is no record of a receipt made out to Irène. But on October 21, 1949, a check was issued—from the bank of Hentsch & Cie in Geneva from Bührle to Feuz. The amount: 240,000 Swiss francs, or almost $2 million in today’s dollars."

**中文理解**：没有开给 Irène 的收据的任何记录。但 1949 年 10 月 21 日，一张支票被开出——从日内瓦的 Hentsch & Cie 银行，付款人 Bührle，收款人 Feuz。金额：二十四万瑞士法郎，约合今日近两百万美元。

**句子结构**：三句。第一句是否定式的档案陈述（There is no record of a receipt made out to Irène.）；第二句 But 转折后接被动结构 a check was issued，破折号把付款路径（出票银行、付款人、收款人）连成一串不加动词的清单；第三句只有名词加冒号加两个数字。

**关键词**：no record / a receipt made out to Irène / October 21, 1949 / a check was issued / Hentsch & Cie / Bührle to Feuz / 240,000 Swiss francs / in today’s dollars

**表达方式**：收款人居然是掮客而不是卖方——作者不说破，只把"no record of a receipt made out to Irène"与"from Bührle to Feuz"放进相邻两句；句末"金额＋今日换算"的格式是本张支票的全部证词，而本章给这笔交易的定义就是这两行之间的差额。

**为什么这样写**：这张支票之前有一串：1949-06-11 Bührle 档案里那张发黄卡片写着法国银行家与伯爵夫人，价"200,000 Swiss francs—roughly $1.3 million now"；9 月画已由 Irène 的银行家径送苏黎世 Zollickerstrasse 的 Bührle 办公室，而"they still lacked an export license"，Feuz 当时保证"the matter with the Louvre will, I am assured, be resolved in the foreseeable future"。支票之后是空缺："On that autumn day, La Petite Irène slipped quietly out of France"，出口许可"no record that surfaced"，因为 Bührle 惯于不透明——"He burned his own records from the war years—the ledgers, the account books, the evidence of movement and exchange."但法国那一侧同样空：Pierrefitte-sur-Seine 的国家档案馆"offered no clarity"，一只系着 ecru 缎带的灰纸盒被推出，里面是 1949 年脆裂的出口许可申请，档案员再查一遍——"La Petite Irène did not appear. Nor did the names of Bührle, his broker, or Irène."章末三个碎片句结案："The painting had left once more. No trace in the registry. Only absence."本章的 deal 因此只剩一张绕开卖方的支票，和一份从来不存在的许可。把 6 月卡片上的 200,000、10 月她索要的 260,000 与这张 240,000 并排放在一起，整场谈判的幅度就出来了——而收款一栏始终没有她的名字。

## 一句话总结

第 17 章是一桩"成交"的完整工序：1947 年初画商 Georges Wildenstein 开价五万法郎，"But Irène said no"，卢浮宫 1946 年"supposedly proposed 12 million francs"也被搁下——理由不是价钱而是资格："But France had hunted her family"，French guards 在 Drancy 押人进门，卢浮宫的私人信件里留着 youtres、wood lice 这样的称呼，于是"Why should she give them the portrait?"；她等到 1949 年，出面的"landscape painter" Werner Feuz 其实"a middleman, a broker"，人人知道真买主是 Zurich’s arms king Emil Bührle——他"methodically, he bought it all back"（索还诉讼败诉之后），Douglas Cooper 统计他在瑞士起获的 77 幅重要画中买了 13 幅；1949-06-08 Feuz 从巴黎拨通苏黎世，吹"an exclusive one-week option"、要寄彩色印刷品作"proof of life"，再补一句假得近乎客气的话——此画"always remained in the same private collection"，作者判为"a lie made to sound like provenance"，而漏掉的是巴伐利亚农舍、编号的画布、Jeu de Paume 与和 Göring 的交换；几封信写自 Hôtel Lutetia，"A place where survivors were delivered, one by one, after the war"；法国官方一面惊慌——Jaujard "called it a national emergency"，Georges Salles 建议"Block the export"并暗示那位"a Swiss amateur"——一面承认全国博物馆 30 million francs 的收购预算抵不过一幅画的 18 million francs，Derain 换 Renoir 的提议被拒，8 月仍在指示外汇处拒发许可；而在 Saint-Germain-en-Laye 那场雨里的纪念碑前（"Five years had passed since his death"），没有人提 Léon Reinach 写过的那些信、和他"tried to save before they took her"的那幅画；卖方并非被动：她把价格加到 260,000 Swiss francs、要在瑞士法郎下跌前拿到现款，Feuz 抱怨"the Countess would not concede even one-sixth of the price because of her stubbornness"——她要避开的，是自己身后这幅画进拍卖行的命运："They became currency. They became spectacle."；1949 年 10 月 21 日，日内瓦 Hentsch & Cie 开出 240,000 瑞士法郎的支票，付款人 Bührle、收款人 Feuz，"There is no record of a receipt made out to Irène"，也没有任何出口许可浮出水面：Pierrefitte 那只系着 ecru 缎带的纸盒里没有这幅画，也没有 Bührle、他的掮客和 Irène 三个名字——"The painting had left once more. No trace in the registry. Only absence."
