# 17. Molly Keane（莫莉·基恩）

## 概览

- **出处**：Stet: A Memoir（Granta Publications 2000 初版；Grove Press 2001 美版）
- **作者**：Diana Athill——叙述者「我」
- **章节定位**：Part Two 六篇作家肖像之**第五篇**（正文 28 段，20,488 字符）。五部分：① 声明（`I know that I have sometimes been described as ‘one of the best editors in London’`）② 那本书怎么到手（`Ian didn’t like it`）③ 她要的**唯一**一次大改（`She was always glad to have such things pointed out`）④ *Good Behaviour* 为什么特别（`she manoeuvres her readers into collaboration`）⑤ 丈夫之死（`She was always more interested in what was happening around her`）与最后的通信（`I am glad, therefore, that our last exchange of letters was about her writing`）
- **人物**：`Molly Keane`（本名，`M. J. Farrell` 是她的笔名）、`Peggy Ashcroft`（演员，说服她拿出稿子的人）、`Ian Parsons`（Chatto & Windus，拒了这本书的人）、`John Gielgud`（导演她四十年代的四部戏的人）、`Robert Keane`（她丈夫，三十多岁猝死）、`Sally`（她的大女儿，**六岁**）、`Virginia`（小女儿，本章末尾那位）、`André`（出版社老板）
- **书名**：*Good Behaviour*（1981，Ian Parsons 拒了，后由**本公司**出版）、十一本笔名小说（`the eleven early novels`）、*Loving and Giving*（她的最后一本）、她四十年代写的**四部戏**、*Remembrance of Things Past*（**Andre Gide** 拒了的那本，被她用作对照）
- **地名**：London、Chatto & Windus、Norfolk、Suffolk、Cork、Waterford、East Anglia、Oxford、Ireland
- **刊物/公司**：André Deutsch Limited、Chatto & Windus、*The Daily Telegraph*（Gielgud 悼文）
- **字符数**：20,488（text/ch17_molly_keane.txt）
- **一句话主旨**：这一章要说明的是**「最好的编辑」这个名声是怎么来的**——`how little I had to do to earn this reputation beyond routine work and being agreeable to interesting people`（除了例行工作与对有趣的人友好之外，我几乎没做什么来赢得这个名声）——而这章的**论证**恰好是**一个证据**：那位**最像**她理想中的作家的人，**需要**她的唯一一次干预，而那次干预是**要求她改**（`I asked her to cool it, which she did`）

## 论证结构

**核心论点**：本章的论证结构是一个**逐步收缩的同心圆**。① **外层**是那个名声的**自我解释**（`how little I had to do to earn this reputation beyond routine work and being agreeable to interesting people`）——她**先**把这个名声**减到最小**。② **第二层**是**一本书怎么到手**（`Ian didn’t like it` 与 Gide 的对照）——出版社的**运气**与**眼光**无关。③ **第三层**是她对 Molly 的**具体工作**（`she could get into muddles about timing`、笔名、那个树上的男孩）——而这层工作**小到几乎不算编辑**。④ **核心**是 *Good Behaviour* 的**技术**（`she manoeuvres her readers into collaboration`）——这才是她**真正喜欢**这本书的原因，而这个理由是**技巧**不是**题材**。⑤ **落点**是那句反讽（`By ‘doing everything’ for her books she meant that if we had not published Good Behaviour …`）——「**最好编辑**」这个称号的**真正内容**，是她**做了足够多的事**以至于能**让作者不必做任何事**。

**可质疑处**：

1. 本章的**开篇**就是一个**反讽**：`one of the best editors in London`（伦敦最好的编辑之一）与 `how little I had to do to earn this reputation beyond routine work and being agreeable to interesting people`（除了例行工作与对有趣的人友好之外，我几乎没做什么）。而这个反讽的**证据**是 `And another example of this is my dealings with … Molly Keane`（**又一个例子**）——也就是说，她第二次**用同一个理由**来解释同一个名声。而读完这一章会发现：这个理由是**不完全的**。因为她对 Molly 做的事里**包含**一次**实质干预**（`I asked her to cool it, which she did`），而这一章自己**证明**那次干预是**必要**的（`it fractured its surface`）。因此「除了例行工作之外我几乎没做什么」这句话在**这一章里被自己反驳了**，而她**没有**注意到这个矛盾——她只注意到了一个不同的矛盾（`this one was the real thing`）。这与 ch16 那位 Vidia 构成对照：那里她**明确**承认了自己的两次判断失误（`the second of my two shocking failures`），这里她**没有**承认任何失误。这不是说她错了，而是说**她的自我评价系统对不同作家敏感度不同**。
2. 那封**最后的信**是本章最动人的部分，也是**最难核查**的部分。她自己写下的赞美是明确的（`marvellously well done`、`won laurels`），而对方的回信是**转述的**（`right off the ground`、`words which I knew to be valedictory`）。这意味着：本章的**情感顶点**建立在**她自己的记忆**上，而她**没有**保留信的文本（`our last exchange of letters` ——`exchange` 可以是**往来**也可以是**最后一次交换**，而 `I am glad, therefore, that our last exchange … was about her writing` 指的是**主题**而不是**一封信**）。与 ch16 那两条**脚注**（他与父亲的通信**有档案**）对照，这里**没有**任何外部佐证。这不是缺陷，但读者应知道：**这一章的最后一个判断是唯一无法被交叉验证的**。
3. 全章对 `Good Behaviour` 的技术分析（`manoeuvres her readers into collaboration`）是全书**最详尽**的一次文本细读，而它详尽到**与她的身份冲突**：她花了 2324 字符（本章最长的一段）分析**一本小说如何操纵读者**，而她自己是一个**被自己分析的对象**——她也在被 Molly 那封信 `right off the ground` **操纵**。这个对称她**没有**指出。也就是说：她能看出 Molly 如何**把读者拉进合作**，却**没有**意识到自己**正在被同一手法**处理（`she surely must acknowledge that what she had done had been marvellously well done` 这句话的效果恰恰是她自己描述的那种「flash of understanding」）。这一未做的连接是本章**最可惜**的地方——材料就在手边。
4. 那个 `as if` 的结构被分析得很细（三条规则、被排除的那一条、`doesn’t read and has few friends` 的位置），但有一个**未追问**的问题：**这本书在爱尔兰出版**（1970 年代），而她讨论的 `men falling in love with men` 是一个在**当时**的出版环境里需要**具体策略**处理的东西。她在第十段说她与 Molly 之间有一堵`impenetrable wall`（`even on a slight acquaintance`）——而这一堵墙的具体**内容**（**她是否知道 Richard 是 gay？**）**没有**被问。一个在 1981 年就敢于写这个的作家，与一个把她**分类为「有点简·奥斯汀式的」**从而**转开**另一面的作家（ch15 的 Alfred）——这两个案例**相隔一章**，而**对照没有**被做出。这很可能是全书**最大**的一处未做的连接。

## 选择性精读

### ①

> **原句 1:** I know that I have sometimes been described as ‘one of the best editors in London’, and I can’t deny that it has given me pleasure; but I also know how little I had to do to earn this reputation beyond routine work and being agreeable to interesting people. And another example of this is my dealings with the person I liked best among those I came to know on the job: the Irish novelist Molly Keane.

**中文理解**：我知道**有时**我被人称作「**伦敦最好的编辑之一**」，而我不能否认这**给我带来了愉悦**；但我也**知道**，除了**例行工作**与**对有趣的人友好**之外，我为了赢得这个名声**做的很少**。而我与**工作中认识的**那些人里**我最喜欢**的那个人——**爱尔兰小说家 Molly Keane**——的**来往**，是这同一说法的**又一个例子**。

**句子结构**：两句。首句是「主句 I know that（内含两个 `that` 从句：I have sometimes been described as ‘one of the best editors in London’ / and I can’t deny that it has given me pleasure）+ 转折分号 `; but I also know how little I had to do to earn this reputation beyond routine work and being agreeable to interesting people`」；次句是「**顺承连词 + 指示代词** And another example of this is my dealings with the person I liked best among those I came to know on the job（内含两个定语从句 `who I liked best` 与 `those I came to know on the job`）: the Irish novelist Molly Keane.」——注意 `:` 后面**先给同位语**（`the person I liked best among those…`），**再给名字**（`the Irish novelist Molly Keane`）。

**关键词**：I have sometimes been described as ‘one of the best editors in London’（我有时被称作伦敦最好的编辑之一）、it has given me pleasure（这给我带来了愉悦）、how little I had to do to earn this reputation（我为了赢得这个名声做的很少）、beyond routine work and being agreeable to interesting people（除了例行工作与对有趣的人友好之外）、And another example of this（这同一说法的又一个例子）、the person I liked best among those I came to know on the job（工作中认识的人里我最喜欢的那个）、the Irish novelist Molly Keane（爱尔兰小说家 Molly Keane）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`I know that … ; but I also know how little`**（我知道……**但我也知道有多少**）的两重 `know` 使首句成为**一次自我核算**；用一个 **`how little`**（**多么少**）作 `I had to do` 的**程度**——而 `how little` 与前面的 `one of the best` 形成**最强的落差**；用一个 **`beyond`**（**除了……之外**）限定「**做了什么**」；用 **`another example of this`**（**又一个例子**）——`another`（**又一个**）这个词把这一章**放进一个已存在的模式**里，从而使这一章的**证据地位**降低（见「可质疑处」第 1 条）；用一个 **`:` + 先同位语后名字**的顺序，使 `the Irish novelist Molly Keane` 出现得**比描述更晚**——她**先说**「我工作中最喜欢的那个人」。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章的**前提**，而它的形式是**一次公开的自我降格**。值得注意的是她**没有**回避那个名声（`I know that I have sometimes been described as …`），而是**接受愉悦**（`I can’t deny that it has given me pleasure`）——这一半的诚实是必要的，因为**否认愉悦**会让她后面的自我降格显得**做作**。而 `how little I had to do to earn this reputation` 用了 `how little`（不是 `nothing`）——`how` 结构**暗含提问者的惊讶**，因此这个判断同时是在**回应一个她认为不公平的期待**。而 `beyond routine work and being agreeable to interesting people` 这一项尤其值得注意：`being agreeable to interesting people`（**对有趣的人友好**）是一个**极其轻**的自我描述，而它的内容其实是**她的人生**——她一生的工作就是**与有趣的人共处并保持愉快**。因此这一段把「**最好的编辑**」这个称号**还原**成了一件**气质**的事。而 `And another example of this`（**又一个例子**）是这一段**最要紧**的三个词：它说明她**已经有**一个同样的说法（上一章或前一节），而她**再一次**用它来开启新的一章——于是这一章的**证据资格**从一开始就是**可疑的**。这一点她自己在读完全章后**没有**回头修正（见「可质疑处」第 1 条）。

### ②

> **原句 2:** The person who persuaded Molly to offer it for publication was Peggy Ashcroft, who had remained a close friend of hers since acting in one of her plays, and who said one day, when staying with her, how sad it was that she had stopped writing. Molly told her that she had recently started again and had a novel which she was unsure about tucked away in a drawer. Peggy insisted on taking it to bed with her that night, and as a result of her enthusiasm Molly sent it to Ian Parsons of Chatto & Windus. That was where our good luck began: Ian didn’t like it. Worse mistakes have been made – publishers often used to console themselves by remembering that André Gide, reading Proust’s Remembrance of Things Past, turned it down . . . although if you envisage that enormous manuscript, and discovering that many of its sentences are as long as most people’s paragraphs, that mistake was perhaps less odd than failing to respond to a novel as accessible as Good Behaviour.

**中文理解**：那个**说服 Molly 拿出稿子来出版的人**是 **Peggy Ashcroft**，她自从在 Molly 的一出戏里**演过**之后一直是她的**密友**；有一天她在 Molly 家里**住**的时候说，Molly **停下写作**这件事**多么令人难过**。Molly 告诉她自己**最近又开始了**，有一部小说**她不太确定**，**塞在抽屉里**。Peggy **坚持那天晚上把它带上床**；而**由于**她的**热情**，Molly 把它寄给了 Chatto & Windus 的 Ian Parsons。而**我们的好运就是从那里开始的**：Ian **不喜欢它**。书业**犯过更糟的错误**——出版商们过去常常**靠**回忆一件事实来**安慰自己**：André Gide 读着 Proust 的《追忆似水年华》时**把它退了**……**不过**，**如果你想象**那部**庞大的手稿**——**发现**其中许多**句子**长得像**大多数人整段文章**——那么**那次错误**也许**比**「对一部像 *Good Behaviour* **那样通俗易读**的小说**毫无反应**」**没那么奇怪**。

**句子结构**：两句。首句是「主句（主语 The person who persuaded Molly to offer it for publication（内含定语从句）+ 表语 was + 同位语 Peggy Ashcroft（后接两个并列的 `who` 定语从句：who had remained a close friend of hers since acting in one of her plays / and who said one day, when staying with her, how sad it was that she had stopped writing））」；次句是「主句（主语 Molly + 谓语 told her that，内含两个并列分句 she had recently started again / and had a novel which she was unsure about tucked away in a drawer）+ 主句 Peggy insisted on taking it to bed with her that night + 结果句 as a result of her enthusiasm Molly sent it to Ian Parsons of Chatto & Windus + 主句 That was where our good luck began: Ian didn’t like it. + 并列句 Worse mistakes have been made –（破折号）publishers often used to console themselves by remembering that（`that` 宾语从句 + 同位语从句 that André Gide, reading Proust’s Remembrance of Things Past, turned it down）+ 让步连词 although（从句内含两个并列的不定式短语 `if you envisage that enormous manuscript, and discovering that many of its sentences are as long as most people’s paragraphs` + 主句 that mistake was perhaps less odd than failing to respond to a novel as accessible as Good Behaviour）」。

**关键词**：the person who persuaded Molly to offer it for publication（说服 Molly 拿出稿子来出版的人）、Peggy Ashcroft（佩吉·阿什克罗夫特）、a close friend of hers since acting in one of her plays（自从在她一出戏里演过之后的密友）、how sad it was that she had stopped writing（她停下写作多么令人难过）、tucked away in a drawer（塞在抽屉里）、Peggy insisted on taking it to bed with her that night（Peggy 坚持那天晚上把它带上床）、as a result of her enthusiasm（由于她的热情）、Ian didn’t like it（Ian 不喜欢它）、Worse mistakes have been made（书业犯过更糟的错误）、publishers often used to console themselves by remembering that（出版商们过去常靠回忆某事来安慰自己）、André Gide, reading Proust’s Remembrance of Things Past, turned it down（André Gide 读着 Proust 的《追忆似水年华》时把它退了）、if you envisage that enormous manuscript（如果你想象那部庞大的手稿）、as long as most people’s paragraphs（像大多数人整段文章那么长）、less odd than failing to respond to a novel as accessible as Good Behaviour（比对一部像《好行为》那样通俗易读的小说毫无反应没那么奇怪）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`a close friend of hers since acting in one of her plays`**（**自从在一出戏里演过**）——这**六个字**（`acting in one of her plays`）交代了一段**数十年的友谊**，而它在结构上只是**一个现在分词短语**；用 **`took it to bed with her that night`**（**带上床**）这个**极具体的动作**代替「她读了这本书」——而 `insisted on`（**坚持**）与 `that night`（**那天晚上**）构成**紧迫性**；用一个 **`That was where our good luck began: Ian didn’t like it.`**（**我们的好运从那里开始：Ian 不喜欢它**）——**冒号后只有三个词**；用 **`Worse mistakes have been made –`**（**更糟的错误犯过——**）作**转折**，并立刻给出那个**书业最大错误**（Gide 拒了 Proust）；用一个 **`less odd than`**（**没那么奇怪**）**取代**更常见的 `less unusual than` 或 `more understandable than`——`odd`（**奇怪**）与后文 `a novel as accessible as`（**一部那样通俗易读的小说**）形成**反讽**：那本书**不奇怪**，而拒它才奇怪。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章关于**书业运气**的论证，而它的结构是**一个降级的比较链**：① 一个**演员**（不在书业）说服作者交稿；② 一家**大出版社**拒了它；③ 而**书业史上最大的错误**（Gide 拒 Proust）被**拿来当安慰**——但她**立刻拆掉**这个安慰（`although if you envisage that enormous manuscript, and discovering that many of its sentences are as long as most people’s paragraphs, that mistake was perhaps less odd than…`）。这个反驳的**技术**很值得注意：她用 **`as long as most people’s paragraphs`**（**像大多数人整段文章那么长**）这个**具体的换算**把 Proust 从「难读」重新定义为「**句子很长而已**」，从而使 Gide 的拒稿从**判断错误**降为**可以被理解的**判断（`less odd`）。而这一降级的**代价**是：`Good Behaviour` 也必须被放在**同一把尺子**上——如果「句子长」可以成为拒稿的**理由**（`less odd`），那么**难读**也可以。于是她的反驳实际上是**一次偷偷的替换**：她不说「Proust 更好」，而说「**Proust 只是句子长，而这本书通俗易读**」——而 `as accessible as`（**那样通俗易读**）这个**比较级**把判断落在**难度**上，不在**价值**上。这一段的收尾（`That was where our good luck began: Ian didn’t like it`）只有**十一个词**，而其中**三个**是**她本人的失败**（`Ian didn’t like it`）——这是全书里**最经济**的一次幽默：**好运**被定义为**别人的拒绝**。

### ③

> **原句 3:** Molly did usually need a little editing because she could get into muddles about timing – make, for instance, an event happen after an interval of two years when something in the text revealed that at least three years must have passed – and she had little tricks of phrasing, such as describing a person’s interests as her ‘importances’, which she sometimes overdid. (Such tricks are part of a writer’s ‘voice’, so it is usually best to leave some of them in – but not enough of them to be annoying.) She was always glad to have such things pointed out, and she was equally co-operative over the only big question that needed solving in the course of her last three novels.

**中文理解**：Molly **确实通常需要**一点编辑，因为她**会**把**时间**搞乱——**比如说**，让一个事件发生在**两年**的间隔之后，而**文本中的**某个东西**显示至少三年**必须已经过去——而且她有些**措辞的小习惯**，比如把一个人的**兴趣**说成她的「**`importances`**（要紧事）」，而她**有时会做过头**。（**这类小习惯**是**作家「声音」的一部分**，因此**通常最好**留下**一些**——**但不要多到**令人**烦**。）她**总是很乐意**被**指出**这类事情，而在她**最后三部小说**的过程中需要解决的**唯一那个大问题**上，她**同样**很**配合**。

**句子结构**：两句。首句是「主句（主语 Molly + 情态动词 did usually need + 宾语 a little editing）+ 原因状语从句 because she could get into muddles about timing –（破折号插入例子）make, for instance, an event happen after an interval of two years when something in the text revealed that at least three years must have passed –（破折号结束）+ 并列连词 and + 主句 she had little tricks of phrasing（破折号插入举例 `such as describing a person’s interests as her ‘importances’`）, which she sometimes overdid」；次句是「独立括注（`(Such tricks are part of a writer’s ‘voice’, so it is usually best to leave some of them in – but not enough of them to be annoying.)`）——内含让 `but not enough of them to be annoying` 作**祈使句省略**）+ 并列句 She was always glad to have such things pointed out, and she was equally co-operative over the only big question that needed solving in the course of her last three novels.」。

**关键词**：she could get into muddles about timing（她会把时间搞乱）、make, for instance, an event happen after an interval of two years（比如让一个事件发生在两年间隔之后）、when something in the text revealed that at least three years must have passed（而文本中的东西显示至少三年必须已经过去）、little tricks of phrasing（措辞的小习惯）、describing a person’s interests as her ‘importances’（把一个人的兴趣说成她的「要紧事」）、which she sometimes overdid（她有时会做过头）、Such tricks are part of a writer’s ‘voice’（这类小习惯是作家声音的一部分）、it is usually best to leave some of them in – but not enough of them to be annoying（通常最好留一些——但不要多到令人烦）、She was always glad to have such things pointed out（她总是乐意被指出这类事情）、she was equally co-operative over the only big question（她在那唯一的大问题上同样配合）、in the course of her last three novels（在她最后三部小说的过程中）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`for instance`**（**比如说**）在**破折号**里插入一个**极具体**的错处（`two years` vs `at least three years`）——这个错处的**性质**是**可计算的**（两个数字不一致），因此它**不需要品味判断**；用一个 **`which she sometimes overdid`**（**她有时会做过头**）——`overdo`（**做过头**）这一动词暗示**分寸**而非**错误**；用一个**括注**（`Such tricks are part of a writer’s ‘voice’…`）把**原则**从**叙述**里**隔离**出来，使它读起来像**编辑的守则**；用一个 **`leave some of them in – but not enough of them to be annoying`**（**留一些——但不要多到令人烦**）——这一句的**可操作性**在于它**没有给数字**，只给**两端**（`some` / `not enough … to be annoying`）；末句用 **`equally co-operative`**（**同样配合**）——`equally`（**同样地**）把她的**品质**从一个**方面**推广到**另一个方面**，从而使「**合作**」成为**她的性格**而不是**一次表现**。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章对「**编辑做什么**」最**朴素**的一次描述，而它的朴素本身构成论证。三件事被列举：**时间不一致**（`two years` / `three years`）、**措辞习惯**（`importances`）、以及**唯一的那个大问题**。前两件是**技术的**（可核的、可数的），第三件是**判断的**。而她处理前两件的方式（`leave some of them in – but not enough of them to be annoying`）揭示了一条**编辑原则**的**真实内容**：编辑的工作**不是**改正，而是**定量**——决定**一个作家的特性保留多少**。这个「定量」动作无法写成规则（`some` 是多少？`enough` 是多少？），而这正是它的**难处**，也是她称之为「**最好的编辑**」的那部分**不可教**之处。而末句那个 `equally co-operative over the only big question` 是这一段的**伏笔**：她在此处**预告**了后面那一次真正的分歧（`I asked her to cool it`），而预告的方式是**把它算作合作**（`equally co-operative`）——也就是说，她在**叙述开头**就把那次干预**纳入**了「她很配合」的证据，而读者要到第四节才会看到**那次干预的内容**。这个**顺序**使那次干预显得**更小**（因为它发生在「她一向配合」的框架里），而**并非更小**——这正是本章的论证需要读者自己**抵抗**的一个效果（见「可质疑处」第 1 条）。

### ④

> **原句 4:** This occurred in Good Behaviour, at a point where a small English boy is discovered hiding up a tree in order to read poetry, which causes his extremely upper-class parents to go into paroxysms of dismay. At that point Molly’s sense of comedy had taken the bit between its teeth and bolted, carrying the story off into the realm of the grotesque. It was wildly funny, but funny in a way at odds with the rest of the book so that it fractured its surface. I asked her to cool it, which she did. She was always ‘splendidly cooperative to work with’, as John Gielgud was to say in a letter to the Daily Telegraph after her death, remembering the days when he directed the four plays which she wrote in the thirties.

**表达方式**：用一个 **`she manoeuvres her readers into collaboration`**（**她把读者 maneuvered 进合作**）——`manoeuvre … into`（**把…… maneuver 进……**）是**军事**动词，而 `into collaboration`（**进入合作**）把它从**战术**转成**写作**；用一个 **三个对照的同位语**（`the large, clumsy daughter` / `a remote and elegant little mother` / `who finds her painfully boring`）——注意 `large` 与 `little`、`clumsy` 与 `elegant`、`boring` 与 `painfully` 三组反义**都在同一句里**；用一个 **`tells us everything she sees – and often fails to understand what she is telling`**（**把我们看到的一切都告诉你——却常常不理解自己在告诉什么**）——`what she is telling`（**她正在告诉的东西**）与 `what she sees`（**她看到的**）之间的**那个落差**就是**全书的结构**；用四段**严格平行**的 `You may be … but you must behave as if …`（四段的**第二段**在第四段里**改变了主语**：从 `you` 变成 `embarrassing things such as …`，而**谓语**从 `may be` 变成 `may happen`）——**这是四段里唯一一处形式上的变化**，而它标记的正是**最要命的那一条规则**；用 **`half-sensing`**（**半感觉到**）与 **`as though we were observant guests in the house`**（**就好像我们是观察力很好的客人**）两个**半**的用法写出**读者的位置**（**看得出，但不算参与者**）；用 **`But of course!`**（**那当然！**）这一句**读者（在书外）的话**标记**理解发生的时刻**——而 `He knew what was in his father’s will`（**他知道**遗嘱里写什么）使「**性**」变成**遗产**。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全书**最长**的一次文本细读（2324 字符），而它的**技术**是把**理解**写成**一项被转移的工作**。第一步：把叙述者设定为**不懂自己说的话的人**（`fails to understand what she is telling`）；第二步：把**理解**的任务**明确移交**（`It is up to us, the readers, to do the understanding`）；第三步：用**四段 `as if` 规则**给读者**一份可操作的规则表**——而这张表的**第四条**（`men falling in love with men may happen, but you must behave as if they don’t`）**在语法上**是把前三条的主语从 `you` 换成了 `embarrassing things`，而这个形式上的**改变**正是它**更宽**（**整个前提**而非**一个人的状态**）的标记。第四步：给出**读者的具体任务**（对 Richard 的**行为**做**计算**）并用**三个 `must` 的递进**（`must like her – must even be finding her attractive – must love her!`）演示这个**计算如何出错**。第五步：用 **`as though we were observant guests in the house`**（**观察力很好的客人**）确定读者的**身份**——**看得见一切，但不被介绍给任何人**。而最后那个括号里的例子（律师的试探 + `But of course! He knew what was in his father’s will!`）是这一段的**技术示范**：理解**迟到**了**三十页**，而它到达时**读者**说的第一句话是 **`But of course!`**——**「那当然」**，即**理解被体验为一直都在那里，只是没被注意到**。这一段的技术判断（`she manoeuvres her readers into collaboration`）因此不只是**比喻**：它在这**三十页**里被**执行**了——读者**自己完成了**那次推理（律师为什么接近她？→ 因为他**早知道**遗嘱的内容），而这个完成**只能用书里给的信息**（`her father’s will`）。这就是「**合作**」的确切含义：**不是被告知，而是被使能**。

> **原句 7:** Molly’s husband Robert Keane died in his thirties, with appalling suddenness, when they were visiting London with their two little daughters, having a very good time. He became violently ill so that he had to be rushed to hospital, but once he was there everything seemed to be under control, so she went back to the children for the night, worried but not really frightened. **句子结构**：三段。首段是「主句（主语 Molly’s husband Robert Keane + 谓语 died + 两个状语 in his thirties, with appalling suddenness + 时间状语从句 when they were visiting London with their two little daughters（插入语 having a very good time））」；次段是「主句 He became violently ill so that he had to be rushed to hospital + 转折连词 but + 从句 once he was there（主句 everything seemed to be under control）+ 结果连词 so + 主句 she went back to the children for the night, worried but not really frightened + 独立句 During the night the telephone rang. + 主句 It was the hospital matron, who said:（冒号引出直接引语）‘Mrs Keane, you must be brave. **关键词**：died in his thirties, with appalling suddenness（死在他三十多岁，以骇人的突然）、when they were visiting London with their two little daughters, having a very good time（在带着两个小女儿来伦敦访问、正玩得很开心的时候）、He became violently ill so that he had to be rushed to hospital（他剧烈病倒以致必须被急送医院）、once he was there everything seemed to be under control（一旦到了那里一切似乎都被控制住了）、worried but not really frightened（担心但并不真的害怕）、It was the hospital matron（那是医院的护士长）、you must be brave（您必须勇敢）、Your husband is dead（您的丈夫死了）、busy theatre friends（忙于演出的剧院界朋友）、she was seized at once by the thought（她立刻被那个念头攫住）、I must not be a nuisance（我不能成为麻烦）、I must not make scenes（我不能大吵大闹）、the quintessential Good Behaviour reaction（地道的 Good Behaviour 式反应）、her eldest daughter, Sally, who was six（她六岁的大女儿 Sally）、‘Mummy, we mustn’t cry, we mustn’t cry.’（妈妈，我们不能哭，我们不能哭）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`having a very good time`**（**正玩得很开心**）这个**现在分词短语**作**插入语**放在全句的**最末**——而这个插入语是**六个字**，因此死亡的**反差**由**字数**造成（`appalling suddenness` 对 `a very good time`）；用一个 **`so that he had to be rushed to hospital`**（**以致他必须被急送医院**）——`so that` 表**结果**，而 `rushed`（**被急送**）是被动的**语法**标记；用一个 **`worried but not really frightened`**（**担心但并不真的害怕**）——`not really`（**并不真的**）这一**弱化的否定**精确标出**她错误的判断**；用一个 **`you must be brave`**（**您必须勇敢**）在**死讯之前**——`must be` 在这里是**别人**（护士长）给她的**指令**，而它与她**实际**做到的（`Molly never did cry`）**恰好相反**；用一个 **`she was seized at once`**（**她立刻被攫住**）——`seized`（**攫住**）是**被动**，而 `at once`（**立刻**）标出**这个念头到达的速度**；用 **`I must not … I must not …`** 这一**复数的 must not** 与后来女儿的 **`we mustn’t … we mustn’t …`** 的**完全对应**（只把 `I` 换成 `we`、`must not` 换成 `mustn’t`）。

**为什么这样写**：这是全章的**情感核心**，而它的技术是**一次精确的**语法**对照**。医院给她的指令是 **`you must be brave`**（**你必须勇敢**）——一个**必须**（`must`）；她给自己下的指令是 **`I must not be a nuisance` / `I must not make scenes`**（**我不能成为麻烦／我不能大吵大闹**）——**也是**一个**必须**，但**方向相反**（`must be` 勇敢 vs `must not` 不添麻烦）。也就是说：在丈夫死后的第一夜里，**「必须」这个模态**同时指派了两个**互相拆台**的任务，而她**都**照做了——**除了一个**。而这个「除了一个」是本章那句 `Molly never did cry`（她**始终没有哭**）。这一对照之所以锋利，是因为**哭**正是那两条指令**唯一**能同时满足的地方：她**没有**成为麻烦、**没有**大吵大闹，而**她也没有哭**——第三项**没有被指派**，而她**也没有做**。因此她的「**好行为**」是**超额**的：她执行了一个**没人要求**的规则。而最后那句六岁女儿的话把这一切**传了下去**：`we mustn’t cry, we mustn’t cry`（**我们**不能哭）——一个**六岁**的孩子**已经在使用**她母亲书里那套 `as if` 规则（`you must behave as if …`），并且**把它变成了复数**。这一段因此是**双向**的：`Good Behaviour` 里的规则**先**被用来解释她母亲的反应，**然后**被证明**确实**是她母亲的规则，而**孩子**已经从那里继承了它。

> **原句 8:** Of course she knew how winning she could be. **关键词**：Of course she knew how winning she could be（当然她知道自己能有多讨人喜欢）、‘When I was young I’m afraid I used to sing for my supper’（我年轻时怕是靠唱歌换饭吃的）、people more interesting and sophisticated than her own family（比她自己家人更有趣、更老练的人）、she won herself a warm welcome（她为自己赢得了一个热情的接纳）、in spite of being neither pretty nor well-dressed（尽管既不漂亮也不穿得体面）、by her funniness and charm（靠她的风趣与魅力）、she was too intelligent for her background（她对自己的出身而言太聪明了）、her mother had made her feel an ugly duckling（她母亲曾让她觉得是个丑小鸭）、and a delinquent one at that（而且是个问题少女）、Being taken up by people who were charmed by her was her salvation（被那些被她迷住的人接纳是她的救赎）、winning them over did not end by making her unspontaneous or manipulative（赢得他们并没有使她变得做作或操纵人）、her clear sight, sensitivity, honesty and generosity were even stronger than her charm（她的洞察力、敏感、真诚与慷慨甚至强过她的魅力）、she would occasionally resort to ‘turning it on’（她偶尔会求助于「把它打开」）、to get through an interview or some fatiguing public occasion（以应付一次采访或某个累人的公开场合）、very skilfully she did it（而她做得非常熟练）、she was always more interested in what was happening around her … than she was in the impression she was making（她总是对周围发生的事比对自己造成的印象更感兴趣）、even on a slight acquaintance it was the woman herself one saw, not a mask（即使只是浅交，人所看到的是她本人而不是一张面具）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`Of course she knew`**（**当然她知道**）——这四个字**没有一个贬义**，而 `Of course` 把「**自知**」写成**理所当然**；用一个 **`sing for my supper`**（**靠唱歌换饭吃**）这一**英语俗语**（**为吃饭而唱歌** = **卖艺**）把她的社交策略压缩成**六个字**；用一个 **`too intelligent for her background`**（**对她的出身而言太聪明了**）——`too … for` 结构把**智力**写成一种**与出身不匹配的量**；用一个 **`an ugly duckling, and a delinquent one at that`**（**一个丑小鸭，而且是个问题少女**）——`and … at that`（**而且还**）把**两个**标签并列，而 `at that` 使第二个标签**追加**在第一个之上；用一个 **`did not end by making her …`**（**并没有以使她变得……告终**）——`end by doing`（**以做某事告终**）把「**赢得人**」的后果写成一条**可能的路径**，而 `did not` 关闭了它；用一个 **`were even stronger than her charm`**（**甚至强过她的魅力**）——`even stronger`（**甚至更强**）把四个**品质**放在**魅力**之上；用一个 **`it was the woman herself one saw, not a mask`**（**人所看到的是她本人，不是面具**）——`one saw`（**人所看到的**）是**分裂不定式**的**正式**形式（`one` 作**泛指主语**），而 `not a mask`（**不是面具**）把**面具**放在**否定**一侧。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章对 Molly 这个人的**正面评价**，而它的技术是**先承认机制、再取消它的后果**。第一步：用 `sing for my supper`（**卖艺**）把她的魅力**贬低**为一个**谋生手段**；第二步：用 `too intelligent for her background`（**对出身而言太聪明**）给出**这个手段的必要性**——不是**爱用**，是**须用**；第三步：用 `her mother had made her feel an ugly duckling`（**母亲让她觉得是丑小鸭**）给出**心理学上的起点**；第四步：**转折**——`winning them over did not end by making her unspontaneous or manipulative`（**并没有因此变得做作或操纵人**），而这个转折的**根据**是 `because her clear sight, sensitivity, honesty and generosity were even stronger than her charm`（**因为她的这些品质甚至强过她的魅力**）。这四个品质（**洞察、敏感、真诚、慷慨**）**全部是关于别人的**，而 `charm`（**魅力**）是**关于自己的**——因此这一句的实质是：**她的自制力比她的表演能力强**。而第五步是**八十岁**的证据（`occasionally … 'turning it on'`）：她**知道**有那个**开关**，**偶尔**用，**非常熟练**——`occasionally`（**偶尔**）与 `otherwise`（**除此之外**）这一对词把「**用**」与「**不用**」的比例标定，而 `but otherwise` 之后的**比较结构**（`more interested in what was happening … than she was in the impression`）才是她**默认**的状态。这一段的收尾（`even on a slight acquaintance it was the woman herself one saw, not a mask, and the woman was lovable`）用**三个短小成分**（`the woman herself` / `not a mask` / `the woman was lovable`）完成一次**结算**：先**强调本人**（`herself`），再**否认面具**，再**给结论**（`lovable`）——而这三步之间**只用一个 `not`** 与**一个 `and`**，因此这个**否定**的重量全部落在 `not a mask` 这**三个词**上。

### ⑨

> **原句 9:** Partly this was because of the difference between Counties Cork and Waterford and my native East Anglia. Most of the people we met were the Irish equivalent of my family’s friends: country gentlefolk preoccupied with hunting, shooting, farming, gardening . . . the very people I had escaped from (so I had felt, fond though I was of many of them) when I moved on from Oxford to earn my living in London. Had I been faced with a week of parties given by Norfolk people of that kind who were strangers to me I would have seen it as a grim ordeal by boredom – and it would have been pretty boring because my hosts, given the tedious duty of entertaining a foreign body, and I as the reluctant victim of their hospitality, would between us have erected an impenetrable wall of polite small-talk from which eventually both sides would have retreated in a state of exhaustion. But in Ireland . . . much as I distrust generalizations about national characteristics, there’s no denying that most Irish people are more articulate than the English, appearing to see talk as a positive pleasure rather than a tiresome necessity. I don’t suppose I shared many more interests with my Irish hosts than I would have done with English ones (although I did know quite a lot about theirs) – but they were so much more lively and witty, and so much readier to start or to follow a new trail, than the people among whom I was raised, that whether or not interests were shared didn’t seem to matter. All the parties were thoroughly enjoyable.

**中文理解**：**部分地**这是因为**Cork 郡与 Waterford 郡**之间与**我本来的东安格利亚**之间的**差别**。我们遇见的**大部分人**是我家朋友的**爱尔兰对等物**：**专注于打猎、射击、务农、园艺的**乡绅……**正是**我**在**离开牛津去伦敦谋生时**逃离的**那些人**（**尽管我很喜欢其中很多人，我当时确实这么觉得**）。**假如**我面对的是**Norfolk** 那一类**对我陌生的人**办的**一星期的聚会**，我会把它看作**一场由无聊构成的严酷考验**——**而且它确实会很无聊**，因为我的**主人**们**被交付了招待一个外国人的枯燥职责**，而**我**则是他们**款待的、不情愿的受害者**，**我们**之间**会竖起一道**客套闲谈的**不可穿透的墙**，**双方最终都会从那道墙里撤退，处于一种精疲力尽的状态**。**但是在爱尔兰**……**尽管我不太相信关于民族性格的概括**，**不可否认**的是：**大多数爱尔兰人比英国人更善于表达**，**显得把谈话看作一种积极的乐趣，而不是一件累人的必要**。**我并不认为**我与我的爱尔兰主人**共享的兴趣**比与英国主人**共享的**多出很多**（**虽然我对他们的兴趣相当了解**）——**但他们是如此更活泼、更风趣，也如此更愿意开辟或跟随一条新路**，**以至于共同点是否被共享似乎无关紧要**。**所有**那些聚会**都彻底令人愉快**。

**句子结构**：三段。首段是「原因状语 Partly this was because of the difference between（三个地名：Counties Cork and Waterford / and my native East Anglia）+ 主句 Most of the people we met were the Irish equivalent of my family’s friends:（冒号引出同位语 `country gentlefolk preoccupied with hunting, shooting, farming, gardening . . . the very people I had escaped from（插入语 so I had felt, fond though I was of many of them）when I moved on from Oxford to earn my living in London`）」；次段是「**虚拟语气**条件句 Had I been faced with a week of parties given by Norfolk people of that kind who were strangers to me（省略 `with` 的主句补足）I would have seen it as a grim ordeal by boredom –（破折号）and it would have been pretty boring because（原因状语内含过去分词短语 `my hosts, given the tedious duty of entertaining a foreign body, and I as the reluctant victim of their hospitality, would between us have erected an impenetrable wall of polite small-talk` + 定语从句 from which eventually both sides would have retreated in a state of exhaustion）」；末段是「转折句 But in Ireland . . .（省略号）much as I distrust generalizations about national characteristics, there’s no denying that（`that` 从句内含主句 most Irish people are more articulate than the English, appearing to see talk as a positive pleasure rather than a tiresome necessity）+ 并列句 I don’t suppose I shared many more interests with my Irish hosts than I would have done with English ones（括注插入语 `（although I did know quite a lot about theirs）`）–（破折号）but they were so much more lively and witty, and so much readier to start or to follow a new trail, than the people among whom I was raised, that（结果从句）whether or not interests were shared didn’t seem to matter + 独立句 All the parties were thoroughly enjoyable.」。

**关键词**：the difference between Counties Cork and Waterford and my native East Anglia（Cork 郡与 Waterford 郡与我本来的东安格利亚之间的差别）、the Irish equivalent of my family’s friends（我家朋友的爱尔兰对等物）、country gentlefolk preoccupied with hunting, shooting, farming, gardening（专注于打猎射击务农园艺的乡绅）、the very people I had escaped from（正是我逃离的那些人）、fond though I was of many of them（尽管我很喜欢其中很多人）、Had I been faced with a week of parties（假如我面对一星期的聚会）、a grim ordeal by boredom（一场由无聊构成的严酷考验）、the tedious duty of entertaining a foreign body（招待一个外国人的枯燥职责）、the reluctant victim of their hospitality（他们款待的不情愿的受害者）、erected an impenetrable wall of polite small-talk（竖起一道客套闲谈的不可穿透的墙）、both sides would have retreated in a state of exhaustion（双方最终都会精疲力尽地撤退）、much as I distrust generalizations about national characteristics（尽管我不太相信关于民族性格的概括）、most Irish people are more articulate than the English（大多数爱尔兰人比英国人更善于表达）、talk as a positive pleasure rather than a tiresome necessity（把谈话看作积极的乐趣而不是累人的必要）、I shared many more interests with my Irish hosts（我与爱尔兰主人共享更多的兴趣）、so much more lively and witty（如此更活泼更风趣）、so much readier to start or to follow a new trail（如此更愿意开辟或跟随一条新路）、whether or not interests were shared didn’t seem to matter（共同点是否被共享似乎无关紧要）、All the parties were thoroughly enjoyable（所有那些聚会都彻底令人愉快）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`a grim ordeal by boredom`**（**一场由无聊构成的严酷考验**）——`by boredom`（**由无聊**）把**无聊**写成**使这场考验发生的**施动者；用一个 **`Had I been faced with …`**（**假如我面对……**）的**虚拟倒装**（`Had` + 主语）把一个**假设的假期**写成**已发生**——这个时态选择使**推测**带上**经验**的色彩；用一个 **`entertaining a foreign body`**（**招待一个外国人**）——`a foreign body`（**一个外来体**）这个**医学与法律**的词（**异物**）使**她**成为**被处理的对象**；用一个 **`erected an impenetrable wall of polite small-talk`**（**竖起一道客套闲谈的不可穿透的墙**）——`erected`（**竖起**）使「**墙**」成为一个**建造**的**动作**，而 `from which eventually both sides would have retreated`（**双方最终从那道墙里撤退**）使**墙**成为一个双方**共同躲进**的**处所**；用一个 **`positive pleasure rather than a tiresome necessity`**（**积极的乐趣而不是累人的必要**）——`positive` / `tiresome` 这一对形容词把「**说话**」分成**享受**与**义务**两个**范畴**；用一个 **`so much more … , and so much … , than …, that`**（**如此更……，如此更……，以至于**）的结构把「**关系成立的条件**」从**共同点**换成**活泼程度**；末句用一个 **`thoroughly enjoyable`**（**彻底令人愉快**）作**总结**——而 `thoroughly`（**彻底地**）使「愉快」不带**保留**。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章关于**人际氛围**的论证，而它的技术是**先建立一个反事实**。`Had I been faced with a week of parties … I would have seen it as a grim ordeal by boredom`（**假如我面对一星期那样的聚会……**）——这个**虚拟倒装句**（`Had` 提前）构造了一个**完整的失败场景**，而这个场景里的**两个角色**（`my hosts` / `I`）都被写成**受害者**（`given the tedious duty` / `the reluctant victim`）——**双方都不得 enjoyment**。而那堵 `impenetrable wall of polite small-talk`（**客套闲谈的不可穿透的墙**）是这一段的**核心意象**：`polite`（**客气的**）在英语里同时是**礼貌**与**空洞**，而 `small-talk`（**闲谈**）是它的**具体形态**；`impenetrable`（**不可穿透的**）使这道墙**不能被任何真实的谈话穿过**。因此这一段的论证是：**客套不是善意，而是**一道**用善意砌成的墙**。而爱尔兰那段用 `talk as a positive pleasure rather than a tiresome necessity`（**把谈话看作积极的乐趣而不是累人的必要**）给出**对照**：同一个动作（谈话），在两种文化里**分属两个范畴**（**乐趣** vs **必要**）。而她对自己这个判断**立刻加了限定**（`much as I distrust generalizations about national characteristics`——**尽管我不太相信关于民族性格的概括**），这个让步使它从**民族论断**降为**个人经验**。而最后那个 `that whether or not interests were shared didn’t seem to matter`（**共同点是否被共享似乎无关紧要**）正是 ch12 那条判据的**应用**：关系的成立**不靠**共同点，**靠**别的东西（`so much more lively and witty` / `so much readier to start or to follow a new trail`）。而这里的 `a new trail`（**一条新路**）与 ch15 那位 Jean 的 `hated this country which was so far from resembling her dream`（**恨这个离她的梦那么远的国家**）形成一组对照：Jean 的反应是**恨**，而这一段的反应是**愿意跟随一条新路**——两种都是对**陌生**的反应，而这一段把后者归为**气质**而非**教养**。

### ⑩

> **原句 10:** By ‘doing everything’ for her books she meant that if we had not published Good Behaviour, Time After Time and Loving and Giving, her earlier books would not have been reissued in paperback by Virago. The real originator of this sequence (not counting Ian Parsons) was Gina Pollinger, as I am sure Molly recognized and must have acknowledged with a similar generosity and more reason; but I do still get great satisfaction from remembering that Molly’s reappearance under our imprint brought her serious recognition as a writer, and also put an end to the money problems that had harassed her throughout her long widowhood. I do think of it, as she bade me, and it makes me happy. Remembering that outcome, and the pleasure of knowing her, is a good way to end this book.

**中文理解**：**她所说的「为她的一切 books 做尽一切」，她的意思是**：如果我们没有出版 *Good Behaviour*、*Time After Time* 与 *Loving and Giving*，**她早年的书**（指那几本已被她视为过时的作品）**就不会由 Virago 出版社以平装再版**。这个序列的**真正发起人**（不算 Ian Parsons）是 **Gina Pollinger**——我确信 Molly 也**意识到了**这一点，而且**必定以同样的、更充分的慷慨承认了它**；**但我**仍**从回忆中获得极大的满足**：Molly 在我们出版社名下**重新出现**，既让她作为作家**获得了认真的认可**，**也**结束了她**漫长守寡期间一直骚扰她的经济困境**。**我确实常常想起这件事——正如她嘱咐我的那样——而它让我高兴。** **回想那个结果，以及认识她的那份愉快，是结束这本书的好方式。**

**句子结构**：五句。① 「By ‘doing everything’ for her books she meant that」是**方式状语 + 主句 + that 宾语从句**（`that` 从句内含 if 引导的**条件从句** `if we had not published …, her earlier books would not have been reissued in paperback by Virago`）；② 「The real originator of this sequence (not counting Ian Parsons) was Gina Pollinger」是「**主语（带同位定语与插入语）+ was + 表语**」，随后 `as I am sure Molly recognized and must have acknowledged with a similar generosity and more reason` 是一个**方式/让步的 as 从句**（`must have` 为推测），而句末的 `but I do still get great satisfaction from remembering that` 引入一个**转折 + that 宾语从句**；③ `Molly’s reappearance under our imprint brought her serious recognition as a writer, and also put an end to the money problems that had harassed her throughout her long widowhood` 是「**主语（名词所有格）+ 两个并列的谓语动词 brought / put an end to** + 定语从句 that had harassed her…」；④ 「I do think of it, as she bade me, and it makes me happy」是「**主句 + 插入语 as she bade me + 并列句**」；⑤ 「Remembering that outcome, and the pleasure of knowing her, is a good way to end this book」是「**动名词短语 Remembering … 作主语（被理解为主动施事的动作名词句）+ is + 表语**」。

**关键词**：By ‘doing everything’ for her books she meant that（她所说的「为她的一切 books 做尽一切」的意思是）、if we had not published Good Behaviour, Time After Time and Loving and Giving（如果我们没有出版这三本书）、her earlier books would not have been reissued in paperback by Virago（她早年的书就不会由 Virago 以平装再版）、The real originator of this sequence (not counting Ian Parsons) was Gina Pollinger（这个序列的真正发起人是 Gina Pollinger）、as I am sure Molly recognized and must have acknowledged with a similar generosity and more reason（我确信 Molly 也意识到了，而且必定以同样且更充分的慷慨承认了）、I do still get great satisfaction from remembering that（我仍从回忆中获得极大的满足）、brought her serious recognition as a writer（让她作为作家获得认真的认可）、put an end to the money problems that had harassed her throughout her long widowhood（结束了她漫长守寡期间一直困扰她的经济困境）、as she bade me（正如她嘱咐我的那样）、Remembering that outcome, and the pleasure of knowing her, is a good way to end this book（回想那个结果与认识她的愉快是结束本书的好方式）

**表达方式**：用 **`the real originator`**（**真正的发起人**）——`real` 这个词把自己与「那个会被记错的人」区分开，而全章真正的**发现者**其实是 Vera Panova（她从拍卖行买回了 Molly 的书）——这句话是 Athill 主动把功劳让出去；用 **`(not counting Ian Parsons)`** 这个**插入语**——它把「人」的范围缩小，而缩小的是**争议**；用 **`as I am sure Molly recognized`**——这个 `as` 从句把「她认出了」降为**推测**（`I am sure`），从而让出一份本可独揽的荣誉；用 **`but I do still get great satisfaction`**——`do still`（**仍然**）这个强调结构承认了这个满足**里有一份不该有的私心**（`but` 让步），而紧接着的 `that` 从句把这个满足**具体化**为两件事：认可与钱；用 **`a good way to end this book`**——**不定式**作表语（`is a good way`），使「结束这本书」成为一个**方式**而不是**时刻**，因此她没有说「我到此为止」，而是说「**这样结束很好**」——这是全章**唯一**一次她对自己正在写的东西**做出评价**。

**为什么这样写**：这五句是全章的**结语**，而它的技术是**两次减法**：① **功劳让渡**——`The real originator … was Gina Pollinger` 出现在她即将结束这本书的位置，而紧跟的 `but I do still get great satisfaction` 是**对这份让渡的自我表态**（`do still` = 我**仍然**从中得到满足），于是全章的落点不是「我帮助了她」，而是「**她得到了她该得的，而那里面没有我的份，尽管我仍然高兴**」。② **钱被说成「困扰」而不是「贫困」**——`the money problems that had harassed her throughout her long widowhood`：主语是 **problems**（**问题**）而不是 she，动词是 `harassed`（**骚扰**）而不是 `afflicted`，这两个选择把**经济困境**写成一个**外来的、持续的攻击**——这与 ch14 里 Sonia Bodenhausen 的处境形成对照（那一篇的经济代价落在了**他人**身上）。而最后一句把**私人情感**（`the pleasure of knowing her`）与**结果**（`that outcome`）并列，让「结束这本书」成为一个**愉快的方法**而非**一个结束的动作**。这与 ch18 的结尾（`It was a job on the side of the thirty per cent.`）属同一族：Athill 收尾时从不总结成就，只**指认一个位置**——这一次的位置是「**认识她**」，而它的动词是 `Remembering`。

## 一句话总结

这一章把 ch16 那条 `Self-brainwashing` 的**反面**写了出来——对 Vidia，她必须**自我说服**才能说出坏话；对 Molly，她需要**说服自己**才能说出好话；而两者的**共同点**是**同一件事**：`By ‘doing everything’ for her books she meant that if we had not published Good Behaviour …`（她那封写着 `won laurels` 的信，意思其实是**没有我们出这本书**）。
