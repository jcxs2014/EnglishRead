# 16. V. S. Naipaul（维迪亚·萨伊保尔）

## 论证结构

**核心论点**：本章的论证有一个**明确的自我指控结构**——她从「**我错看了他**」讲到「**我错判了他的书**」，而这两件事共享同一个根源：**她把他读成了一个比实际更牢固的人**。第一部分（相遇与误读）给出这个根源：`he appeared to me far too impressive a person to be subject to such discomforts`（在我看来他是个太出色的人，不会受这类不适之苦）。第二部分（特立尼达）证明这个根源是**社会性的**——他成长其中的社会「disorderly, inefficient and self-deceiving」，因此他「longed for order, clarity and competence」。第三部分（十八本书的循环）证明这个根源在**编辑的日常里**以「post-publication gloom」的形式反复出现。第四部分（*Guerrillas*）是她**失败的**那一课：她**做了判断**，判断**错了**，而她处理这次失败的方式（`Self-brainwashing`）本身就是本章最诚实的一笔。

**论证脉络**：以「**发现**作家其实是偶发的」开篇（`they just happened to come`）→ 交代引荐链 → 写交付稿子那一幕（`handed me Miguel Street`）→ 写他的**沉默**（牛津那件说不出的事）→ 写她的**两重误读**（`he appeared to me far too impressive`）→ **提出核心定义**（`Vidia’s self … was his writing: a great gift, but all he had`）→ 交代她1960 年代两度去特立尼达 → 写**试衣间**那件事（`his entirely superficial amiability had unleashed`）→ 写 Vidia 对种族摩擦的概括（`Like monkeys pleading for evolution`）→ 写 `racist` 这个标签的**地方性**（`only a local one`）→ 给出她的**成因分析**（`disorderly, inefficient and self-deceiving`）→ 指出这个分析同时**证明他错**（`choosing to romanticize their results`）→ 用**印度之行**作为反证（他的理想没有产地）→ 用**法国葡萄园**作为他**可以**被具体化的审美 → 写**母亲**（`divides her time between the Temple and the quarry`）→ 写**十八本书的三段循环**（peace / euphoria / post-publication gloom）→ 写第二段她那份**工作清单**与那句 `perfectionist` → 写第三段**逐年加深**（`just a little … deeper and deeper`）→ **提出方法论**（`Self-brainwashing`）→ 写它的**利益理由**（`if you cease to be of use to them you cease to be of use to your firm`）→ 写它的**例外**（`unable to like the person`）→ 交代她**如何处理**这个例外（`I was guilty of an absurd pettiness`）→ 交代 *Guerrillas* 与**她那次错判**（`Jane simply fell to pieces`）→ 写**当时的处境**（`damn’ well sound as though I meant it`）→ 写**拒绝的后果**（当天撤回）→ 写 André 的反击与她的**实际反应**（`My private sun did go back behind a film of cloud`）→ 写**当天售出**（`sold to Seeker & Warburg the day after it left us`）→ 写 **1984 年他离开**与她的理解 → 写那句关于**名气与预付款**的判断 → 以**三个否定 + 一个例外**收束 → 附两条关于他与父亲通信的**脚注**（他一生几乎不谈种族）

**可质疑处**：

1. 本章的自我指控是**成体系的**（`I was wrong about him` → `I was wrong about his book`），而两者的连接靠的是一句自我诊断（`he appeared to me far too impressive a person`）。这个诊断有一个**未被追问**的地方：如果她之所以读错他，是因为**她把他看得太好了**，那么 `Self-brainwashing`（自我洗脑）这一处理办法就是**针对症状的**——她**继续**做一个更彻底的崇拜者，而**不是**修正她的读法。她的**修法**是让自己在**背后**说真话（`I was guilty of an absurd pettiness`），而这个修法**并没有**让她在下一次读得更准。事实上本章写着：*Guerrillas* 之后她还是接连出了**十四本书**（`the books still to come were, indeed, worth having`），而这一段的判断是**价值判断**（`worth having`）而非**认识论判断**（**我是否读对了**）。也就是说：她修正了**关系**，没有修正**方法**。这一路径与 ch15 那位 Alfred 形成对照——她对 Alfred **没有**修正任何东西（`I am not going to attempt a full account`），而对 Vidia 修正了**人际**的部分。两次的共同点是：**她从未系统性地追问自己「读得准不准」这件事**。
2. `Self-brainwashing sometimes has to be a part of an editor’s job.` 这句话被写成一个**职业要求**，而它紧接着的理由是**利益**：`if you cease to be of use to them you cease to be of use to your firm`（`your firm`，**你的公司**）。这条理由有一个**未被承认的推论**：如果同情一位作家与**保住一份工作**是同一件事，那么在公司与作家冲突时，**她应该站哪边**——而本章实际上给出了一个答案（*Guerrillas* 那次她**按公司路线**处理了，即「即使很差我们也一定出」），而这个答案**与**`Self-brainwashing` 的伦理是**相反**的。读者会想问：自我洗脑是**为了他能继续写**（对他好），还是**为了公司留住一位畅销作者**（对公司好）？她写的两句话**都**成立，因此**没有**指认哪一个**驱动**了实际行为——而这一点恰恰是可核的：**在 *Guerrillas* 那个决定上，起作用的是哪一条？**
3. 全章对「**种族**」的处理有一个**结构性的沉默**。她写了试衣间那件事（`May I ask you something?` 那段写得极好）、写了 Vidia 的 `Like monkeys pleading for evolution`、写了 `unacknowledged white audience`、写了 `the label ‘racist’ … was, so to speak, only a local one`，但她**从未**以第一人称说出**自己**在那件事里的位置——她是一个**白人英国女人**，在**特立尼达的商店里**听见另一个**白人女人**对**黑售货员**说话。Vidia 的整个分析是关于**黑人与印度人的互相鄙视如何向未被承认的白人观众申诉**，而她作为那个**观众**的一员，记录了这一切却**从不站进那个结构**。这不是遗漏：她的 `only a local one`（那只是一个**本地**标签）正是**在白人内部**说的。因此本章在种族上是**准确的**，但**始终在外部**。两条脚注（他与父亲一生只提到过一次非洲裔）使这一点更清楚：那**沉默**是被记录的，而**记录者**的沉默没有被记录。
4. 那条 `as though the book had been given to him`（他 1975 年对写作体验的描述）是本章**唯一的**一次他说出**正面的**创作感受，而她**没有**为它配任何分析——她只是引了它，用来标出**他态度的变化**。而这个变化（他第一次愿意谈论写作过程）**恰好**发生在 *Guerrillas* 时期，也就是她**判他错**的那一本。这个并列关系在本章里**存在**（两段相距不远），但**没有被指出**。读者若要追问「她这次读对了吗」，答案可能就藏在这句未被展开的话里。

## 选择性精读

### ①

> **原句 1:** GOOD PUBLISHERS ARE supposed to ‘discover’ writers, and perhaps they do. To me, however, they just happened to come. V. S. Naipaul came through Andrew Salkey who was working with him at the BBC, and Andrew I met through Mordecai Richler when he took me for a drink in a Soho club. When Andrew heard that I was Mordecai’s editor he asked me if he could send me a young friend of his who had just written something very good, and a few days later Vidia came to a coffee bar near our office and handed me Miguel Street.

**中文理解**：**好的出版商** supposedly（据说）会「**发现**」作家，也许他们真的发现。而对**我**来说，他们只是**碰巧来了**。V. S. Naipaul 是**经由** Andrew Salkey 来的（当时他在 BBC 工作），而 Andrew 是我**经由** Mordecai Richler 认识的（他请我去 Soho 一家俱乐部喝一杯）。当 Andrew 听说我是 Mordecai 的编辑时，他问我能不能**介绍**他一个**年轻朋友**，那人刚写了些**非常好的东西**；几天后 Vidia 来到我们办公室附近的一家**咖啡馆**，把 *Miguel Street* **递给了我**。

**句子结构**：四句。首句是「主语（形式化复数 GOOD PUBLISHERS）+ 情态动词 are supposed to + 动名词 ‘discover’ writers + 并列句 and perhaps they do」；次句是「介词短语 To me, however, + 主句 they just happened to come」；第三句是「主句（主语 V. S. Naipaul + 谓语 came through + 双重介词短语 through Andrew Salkey（内含定语从句 who was working with him at the BBC）+ 并列的 and Andrew（作 came through 的第二个宾语）I met through Mordecai Richler（内含时间状语从句 when he took me for a drink in a Soho club））」；末句是「时间状语从句 When Andrew heard that I was Mordecai’s editor（内含两个宾语从句 that I was Mordecai’s editor / （第二句）he asked me if he could send me a young friend of his who had just written something very good）+ 时间状语 and a few days later + 主句 Vidia came to a coffee bar near our office + 并列谓语 and handed me Miguel Street」。

**关键词**：GOOD PUBLISHERS ARE supposed to ‘discover’ writers（好的出版商据说会「发现」作家）、To me, however, they just happened to come（而对我来说，他们只是碰巧来了）、came through Andrew Salkey（经由 Andrew Salkey 而来）、Andrew I met through Mordecai Richler（Andrew 是我经由 Mordecai Richler 认识的）、when he took me for a drink in a Soho club（他请我去 Soho 一家俱乐部喝一杯时）、he could send me a young friend of his（他能不能把他一个年轻朋友介绍给我）、who had just written something very good（那人刚写了些非常好的东西）、Vidia came to a coffee bar near our office（Vidia 来到我们办公室附近的咖啡馆）、handed me Miguel Street（把 *Miguel Street* 递给了我）

**表达方式**：用 **`GOOD PUBLISHERS ARE`** 这个全大写**加逗号**的强调写法把一个**行业神话**放在**引号**里（`‘discover’`，且加小引号）；用一个 **`To me, however,`** 把神话**限定到个人经验**（`just happened to come` —— **碰巧来**，不带任何主动成分）；用**两个 `came through` / `met through`** 把引荐写成**一条链**（`through` 这个介词重复三次：`came through` / `met through` / 两句里的 through 隐含）；用 **`handed me`**（**递给我**）作全段的**落点**——`hand` 这个词把「交付」写成**一个手上的动作**，而不是一次**评审的开始**。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章的开场，而它一上来就**拆掉了一个行业神话**：`good publishers are supposed to ‘discover’ writers`（好的出版商据说会「发现」作家）——注意三个标记：`GOOD PUBLISHERS` 的**全大写**（像一条被背诵的箴言）、`supposed to`（**据说**、**据说应该**）、以及 `‘discover’` 的**引号**（这个动词在书业是术语，而她给它打了引号）。三步之后，`To me, however, they just happened to come` 用一个**副词**（`just happened`）把**主动**降为**偶然**。这一降格的**对照**就是本章的全部方法：她后来对 Vidia 犯的错误，正是因为她**开始时**就知道「发现」是偶然的，却在**认识他本人**时把这份偶然**转成了必然**（他这么优秀的人一定不会……）。而末句那个 `handed me Miguel Street` 是全章最精确的一个**动作**：`hand` 不写他**等**在咖啡馆里，也不写稿子有多厚——只写一个**手递手**。这个动作把「**发现**」神话的最后一点主动权也消掉了：不是她**找到了**他（那是引荐链条的功劳），是**他走过来递给她**。而这一段的结构（两句话的引荐链，四个 `through`）是 ch13 那位 Moore **直接投稿**的对照：同一件事，两种**发现**方式。

### ②

> **原句 2:** We began to meet fairly often, and I enjoyed his company because he talked well about writing and people, and was often funny. At quite an early meeting he said gravely that when he was up at Oxford – which he had not liked – he once did a thing so terrible that he would never be able to tell anyone what it was. I said it was unforgivable to reveal that much without revealing more, especially to someone like me who didn’t consider even murder literally unspeakable, but I couldn’t shift him and never learnt what the horror was – though someone told me later that when he was at Oxford Vidia did have some kind of nervous breakdown. It distressed me that he had been unhappy at a place which I loved. Having such a feeling for scholarship, high standards and tradition he ought to have liked it . . . but no, he would not budge. Never for a minute did it occur to me that he might have felt at a loss when he got to Oxford because of how different it was from his background, still less because of any form of racial insult: he appeared to me far too impressive a person to be subject to such discomforts.

**中文理解**：我们开始相当频繁地见面，而我喜欢与他相处，因为他**谈论**写作与人**讲得好**，而且常常**风趣**。在相当早的一次见面中，他**郑重地**说，他在牛津时（**他并不喜欢**那里）曾做过一件**如此可怕**的事，以致他**永远无法**告诉任何人那是什么。我说，**透露这么多而不透露更多**是不可原谅的，尤其是对像我这样**连谋杀都不认为真的不可说**的人；但我**无法让他开口**，也**始终没弄清**那件恐怖的事是什么——尽管后来有人告诉我，Vidia 在牛津时确实有过某种**神经崩溃**。他在我**热爱**的地方**不快乐**，这让我**难过**。他如此**看重**学术、高标准与传统，他本**应该**喜欢那里……**但是不**，他**一点不让**。我**一刻也没有**想到，他到牛津时可能因为**那里与他的背景何等不同**而**感到手足无措**，就更不用说是因为**任何形式的种族侮辱**了：在我看来他是个**太出色**的人，不会**受到**这类**不适**之苦。

**句子结构**：五段。首句是「时间状语从句 We began to meet fairly often + 主句 and I enjoyed his company（内含三个 `because` 理由的并列内容：because he talked well about writing and people / and was often funny）」；次句是「时间状语 At quite an early meeting + 主句 he said gravely that（宾语从句内含时间状语从句 when he was up at Oxford – which he had not liked – + 主句 he once did a thing so terrible that he would never be able to tell anyone what it was）」；第三句是「主句 I said（that 省略的宾语从句 it was unforgivable to reveal that much without revealing more）+ 插入语 especially to someone like me who didn’t consider even murder literally unspeakable + 并列句 but I couldn’t shift him and never learnt what the horror was – though（引导的让步从句）someone told me later that when he was at Oxford Vidia did have some kind of nervous breakdown」；第四段是「独立句 It distressed me that he had been unhappy at a place which I loved. / 独立句 Having such a feeling for scholarship, high standards and tradition he ought to have liked it . . . but no, he would not budge.」；末段是「**否定前置的强调句** Never for a minute did it occur to me that（that 从句内含两个并列的 `because of …`）+ 冒号引出她的解释 he appeared to me far too impressive a person to be subject to such discomforts」。

**关键词**：we began to meet fairly often（我们开始相当频繁地见面）、he talked well about writing and people（他谈论写作与人讲得好）、he said gravely（他郑重地说）、he once did a thing so terrible that he would never be able to tell anyone（他做过一件如此可怕以致永远无法告诉任何人的事）、unforgivable to reveal that much without revealing more（透露这么多而不透露更多是不可原谅的）、even murder literally unspeakable（连谋杀都不真的认为不可说）、I couldn’t shift him（我无法让他开口）、never learnt what the horror was（始终没弄清那件恐怖的事是什么）、some kind of nervous breakdown（某种神经崩溃）、It distressed me that he had been unhappy at a place which I loved（他在我热爱的地方不快乐，这让我难过）、Having such a feeling for scholarship, high standards and tradition（如此看重学术、高标准与传统）、he would not budge（他一点不让）、Never for a minute did it occur to me（我一刻也没想到）、because of how different it was from his background（因为那里与他的背景何等不同）、racial insult（种族侮辱）、he appeared to me far too impressive a person to be subject to such discomforts（在我看来他太出色，不会受这类不适之苦）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`he said gravely`**（**郑重地**说）加一件**说不出的事**——**郑重的语气**与**永久的秘密**并置；用一个 **`unforgivable to reveal that much without revealing more`**（透露这么多而不透露更多**是不可原谅的**）把**半透露**判为**比不透露更坏**，而这个判断来自一个**承认自己不知道分寸的人**（`even murder literally unspeakable`）；用 **`but no, he would not budge.`**（但是不，他一点不让）——`but no` 里的 **`no`** 是**对话式**的，读者能**听见**她在跟他说话；用一个 **`Never for a minute did it occur to me`**（**一刻也没**想到）这一**否定前置**把「**从未想到**」标为**经过检查的结论**，而不是**疏忽**；末句用 **`he appeared to me far too impressive a person`**（在我看来他**太出色**）——而这个判断的**内容**正是它**失效**的原因（**出色**被读成**不会不适**）。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章对**她自己的误读**最完整的一次展示，而它的结构是**一次逐渐升级的排除**。① 先写下**证据**（`some kind of nervous breakdown`）；② 然后写下她的**第一反应**（`he ought to have liked it`，**他本该喜欢**，用的是 `ought`——**义务式**的判断）；③ 接着 **but no, he would not budge**（他一点不让）——她注意到了**反证**，但只当成**任性**；④ 最后才写下她**真正**做的排除（`because of how different it was from his background, still less because of any form of racial insult`）。而这里的 `still less`（**就更不用说**）是关键：它表示她把两个原因按**可能性**排了序（背景差异 > 种族），而她**两个都不在**考虑之列。末句 `he appeared to me far too impressive a person to be subject to such discomforts` 是全章的**病根**——它的推理形式是**「这么好的人不会受这种苦」**，而这句话的错误在于：她用他的**才华**（一个**产出**的性质）去推断他的**处境**（一个**存在**的事实）。全章核心那句 `Vidia’s self – his very being – was his writing: a great gift, but all he had` 正是对这一误读的**修正**：正因为他的才华**就是他的全部**，所以才华受苦时**没有别的东西可以缓冲**——而她在下一段才意识到这一点。

### ③

> **原句 3:** At that stage I did not know how or why he had rejected Trinidad, and if I had known it, would still have been unable to understand what it is like to be unable to accept the country in which you were born. Vidia’s books (not least A Way in the World, not written until thirty-seven years later) were to do much to educate me; but then I had no conception of how someone who feels he doesn’t belong to his ‘home’ and cannot belong anywhere else is forced to exist only in himself; nor of how exhausting and precarious such a condition (blithely seen by the young and ignorant as desirable) can be. Vidia’s self – his very being – was his writing: a great gift, but all he had. He was to report that ten years later in his career, when he had earned what seemed to others an obvious security, he was still tormented by anxiety about finding the matter for his next book, and for the one after that . . . an anxiety not merely about earning his living, but about existing as the person he wanted to be. No wonder that while he was still finding his way into his writing he was in danger; and how extraordinary that he could nevertheless strike an outsider as a solidly impressive man*.

**中文理解**：在**那个阶段**，我不知道他**如何**或**为何**拒绝了特立尼达；而**即使**我知道，**仍然**无法理解**无法接受自己出生之国**是什么滋味。Vidia 的书（**尤其**是 *A Way in the World*，直到**三十七岁**才写）后来在**教育我**这件事上起了很大作用；但**那时**我**完全没有**概念：一个人如果觉得自己**不属于**自己的「**家**」而**又不能属于**别处，会**被迫只住在自己内部**是什么样子；也**没有**概念：这样一种处境（**被年轻无知者轻率地视为值得羡慕的**）会**多么** exhausting（筋疲力尽）而**precarious**（不稳定）。**Vidia 的自我——他**最**本质的存在——**就是**他的写作：**一份极大的天赋，但那是他的全部**。他后来会**报告**说，在他**职业生涯的十年之后**，当他已经挣到了**在别人看来**一种**明显的安全**时，他**仍然**被**焦虑**折磨着——焦虑的是**为下一本书找题材**，以及**再下一本**的……而这种焦虑**不仅仅是**关于**谋生**，而**是关于作为他想要成为的那个人而存在**。**难怪**在他还在**找路进**自己写作的**时候**，他**处在危险中**；而**多么**非同寻常：他** nevertheless** 竟能让一个**局外人**觉得他是个**扎实可靠地出色的人*。

**句子结构**：三段。首段是「时间状语 At that stage + 主句 I did not know how or why he had rejected Trinidad + 并列条件句 and if I had known it, would still have been unable to understand what it is like to（内含不定式 to be unable to accept the country in which you were born）」；次段是「主句 Vidia’s books … were to do much to educate me（内含非限定从句 not least A Way in the World, not written until thirty-seven years later）+ 转折连词 but + 省略主语的从句 I had no conception of how（一个主语从句内含定语从句 who feels he doesn’t belong to his ‘home’ and cannot belong anywhere else + 被动式 is forced to exist only in himself）+ 并列的 nor of how exhausting and precarious such a condition (blithely seen by the young and ignorant as desirable) can be」；末段是「主句（主语 Vidia’s self – his very being – 表语 was his writing: a great gift, but all he had）+ 时间状语 He was to report that（宾语从句内含时间状语 ten years later in his career / when he had earned what seemed to others an obvious security，主句 he was still tormented by anxiety about finding the matter for his next book, and for the one after that）+ 同位语 an anxiety not merely about earning his living, but about existing as the person he wanted to be + 独立句 No wonder that … ; and how extraordinary that he could nevertheless strike an outsider as a solidly impressive man*」。

**关键词**：At that stage I did not know how or why he had rejected Trinidad（那个阶段我不知道他如何或为何拒绝特立尼达）、if I had known it, would still have been unable to understand（即使知道也仍无法理解）、A Way in the World, not written until thirty-seven years later（直到三十七岁才写的 *A Way in the World*）、no conception of how someone who feels he doesn’t belong to his ‘home’（完全没有概念：一个人觉得自己不属于家是什么样子）、and cannot belong anywhere else is forced to exist only in himself（又不能属于别处，被迫只住在自己内部）、exhausting and precarious（筋疲力尽而不稳定）、blithely seen by the young and ignorant as desirable（被年轻无知者轻率地视为值得羡慕的）、Vidia’s self – his very being – was his writing（Vidia 的自我——他最本质的存在——就是他的写作）、a great gift, but all he had（一份极大的天赋，但那是他的全部）、when he had earned what seemed to others an obvious security（当他已挣到别人看来明显的安全时）、anxiety about finding the matter for his next book（为下一本书找题材的焦虑）、not merely about earning his living, but about existing as the person he wanted to be（不只是关于谋生，而是关于作为他想要成为的人而存在）、strike an outsider as a solidly impressive man（让一个局外人觉得他扎实可靠地出色）

**表达方式**：用 **`Vidia’s self – his very being – was his writing:`** 这一**三段破折号**把主语扩展成一个**同一的强调链**（自我 = 最本质的存在 = 写作），而**两个破折号**使这句话读起来像**一个正在被校正的定义**；用一个 **`a great gift, but all he had`**（**一份极大的天赋，但那是他的全部**）的 **`but`** 把赞美立刻转成**匮乏**——`all he had`（**他全部的**）这个 `all` 同时是**全部**与**仅仅**；用 **`not merely about …, but about …`**（**不只是关于……而是关于……**）这一对比把焦虑的对象从**经济**升到**存在**；末句用一个 **`No wonder that … ; and how extraordinary that …`**（**难怪……；而多么非同寻常……**）的**两个感叹**并列，而 `nevertheless`（**然而**）加在 `extraordinary` 之前——使「**奇特**」与「**尽管**」相邻。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章的**核心段落**，而它处理的是 ch14 那条**写作与生活裂缝**的**第三种形态**。ch14 的 Jean 是：`She could not see herself when she was working`（工作时看不到自己）——因此**工作中**的她是完整的。ch15 的 Alfred 是：`they, not he, had written every word of his work`（是声音而非他写了每一个字）——因此他**被自己的作品夺走**。而 Vidia 是：`Vidia’s self – his very being – was his writing: a great gift, but all he had`（他的自我**就是**写作，**而那是他的全部**）——**没有裂缝**：写作**等于**他，因此写作的**危险**（`while he was still finding his way into his writing he was in danger`）**直接就是**他的**危险**（`how extraordinary that he could nevertheless strike an outsider as a solidly impressive man`）。这三种形态构成一组**完整**的谱系：分离（Jean）→ 取代（Alfred）→ 同一（Vidia）。而这一段的最后一句是全章的**结构所在**——`how extraordinary` 与 `in danger` 并列，而 `in danger` 指的是**写作过程中**的危险。也就是说：**越是才华横溢的人，写作这一步就越是在拿自己去冒险**；而她作为**编辑**，看到的是那个**扎实可靠地出色**的表面。这一句是全章**最像自白**的一句——它承认了她**多年后才学会**看的那个东西。

> **原句 4:** Three. And it is not only people like Vidia, feverish with repressed talent, who yearn to escape. There was the conversation I overheard in the changing-cubicle next to mine when I was trying on a swimsuit in a store in Port of Spain. An American woman, accompanied by her husband, was also buying something, and they were obviously quite taken by the pretty young woman who was serving them. They were asking her questions about her family, and the heightened warmth of their manner made me suspect that they found it almost exciting to be kind to a black person. When the customer had made her choice and her husband was writing a cheque, the saleswoman’s voice suddenly changed from chirpiness to breathlessness and she said: ‘May I ask you something?’ The wife said: ‘Yes, of course,’ and the poor young woman plunged into desperate pleading: please, please would they help her, would they give her a letter inviting her to their home which she could show to the people who issued visas, she wouldn’t be any trouble, and if they would do this for her . . . On and on she went, the husband trying to interrupt her in an acutely embarrassed voice, still wanting to sound kind but only too obviously appalled at what his entirely superficial amiability had unleashed. Soon the girl was in tears and the couple were sounding frantic with remorse and anxiety to escape – and I was so horrified at being the invisible and unwilling witness of this desperate young woman’s humiliation that I abandoned my swimsuit, scrambled into my dress and fled, so I do not know how it ended.

**中文理解**：**第三**（点）。而**渴望逃离**的并不只是像 Vidia 那样**被压抑的天才折磨着**的人。还有一次我在**西班牙港**一家店里**试泳衣**时，**在**我隔壁的**更衣间**里**偷听到**的对话。一位**美国**太太，由她丈夫陪着，也在买东西；而他们显然**相当喜欢**那个服侍他们的**漂亮年轻女人**。他们在**问她家里的情况**，而他们**态度中 heightened 的热度**使我怀疑：他们觉得**对一个黑人好**这件事**几乎令人兴奋**。当**那位顾客**选好了东西、而**她丈夫正在开支票**时，**售货姑娘**的声音**突然**从**叽叽喳喳**变成**气喘吁吁**，她说：「**我能问您一件事吗？**」**那位太太**说：「**当然可以，请说。**」而这个**可怜的年轻女人**顿时陷入**绝望的哀求**：求您、求您帮帮她们吧，能不能给她们一封邀请她们去家里的**信**，好让她拿给**签发签证的人**看，她**不会添任何麻烦**，如果她们**愿意**为她做这件事……她**说个不停**，而**那位丈夫**试图**用一个极其尴尬的声音**打断她，**仍然**想**听起来和善**，却**显然**被**他那份完全表面的友好所释放出来的东西**吓到了**。很快那姑娘**哭了起来**，而那对夫妇**发出**慌乱的声音，带着**悔意**与**想要逃开的焦虑**——而我**如此震惊**，以至于我竟成了这个**绝望年轻女人受辱的、隐形的、不情愿的目击者**，以致我**丢下了泳衣**，**手忙脚乱地**套上裙子**逃走了**，所以**我不知道**那件事**后来怎样了**。

**句子结构**：两句超长句（首句是「**指示代词** And it is not only people like Vidia, feverish with repressed talent, who yearn to escape.」+ 存在句 `There was the conversation I overheard in the changing-cubicle next to mine（内含定语从句 next to mine / when I was trying on a swimsuit in a store in Port of Spain）`」）。第二段由七个句子构成：`An American woman, accompanied by her husband, was also buying something` → `they were obviously quite taken by the pretty young woman who was serving them` → `They were asking her questions about her family, and the heightened warmth of their manner made me suspect that they found it almost exciting to be kind to a black person`（`that` 从句 + `almost exciting`）→ `When the customer had made her choice and her husband was writing a cheque, the saleswoman’s voice suddenly changed from chirpiness to breathlessness and she said: ‘May I ask you something?’`（`from X to Y` + 冒号引出直接引语）→ `The wife said: ‘Yes, of course,’ and the poor young woman plunged into desperate pleading: …`（`plunged into` + 冒号引出间接引语）→ `On and on she went, the husband trying to interrupt her in an acutely embarrassed voice, still wanting to sound kind but only too obviously appalled at what his entirely superficial amiability had unleashed`（`v-ing` 独立主格）→ `Soon the girl was in tears and the couple were sounding frantic with remorse and anxiety to escape – and I was so horrified at … that I abandoned …, scrambled … and fled, so I do not know how it ended`（`so … that …` 结果句 + 三动词排比 + 省略 `and` 的并列独立句）。

**关键词**：feverish with repressed talent（被压抑的天才折磨得发热）、the conversation I overheard in the changing-cubicle next to mine（我在隔壁更衣间里偷听到的对话）、in a store in Port of Spain（在西班牙港一家店里）、quite taken by the pretty young woman（相当喜欢那个漂亮年轻女人）、the heightened warmth of their manner（他们态度中加剧的热度）、they found it almost exciting to be kind to a black person（他们觉得对一个黑人好几乎令人兴奋）、changed from chirpiness to breathlessness（从叽叽喳喳变成气喘吁吁）、May I ask you something?（我能问您一件事吗）、plunged into desperate pleading（陷入绝望的哀求）、a letter inviting her to their home which she could show to the people who issued visas（好让她拿给签发签证的人看的邀请信）、she wouldn’t be any trouble（她不会添任何麻烦）、in an acutely embarrassed voice（用极其尴尬的声音）、appalled at what his entirely superficial amiability had unleashed（被他那份完全表面的友好释放出来的东西吓到）、the invisible and unwilling witness（隐形的、不情愿的目击者）、I abandoned my swimsuit, scrambled into my dress and fled（丢下泳衣、套上裙子逃走）、so I do not know how it ended（所以我不知道后来怎样了）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`heaved`** 类的动词形容被压抑（`feverish with repressed talent` —— **发热**）；用 **`heightened warmth`**（**加剧的热度**）与 **`almost exciting`**（**几乎令人兴奋**）两次**程度词**（`heightened` / `almost`）写出**善意的剂量**；用 **`from chirpiness to breathlessness`**（**从叽叽喳喳到气喘吁吁**）这一**从 X 到 Y** 精确标出**语调突变的两个端点**（一个是**表演性的**，一个是**生理性的**）；用一个 **`plunged into`**（**陷入**）把哀求写成一次**下坠**；用 **`unleashed`**（**释放**）这一**及物动词**——关键在于它的**施事**是 `his entirely superficial amiability`（**他那份完全表面的友好**），也就是说**善意的被动式**在这里**做了什么**；末句用 **`invisible and unwilling`**（**隐形的、不情愿的**）标出**目击**的性质（不是**碰巧**，是**不想**）。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章（也是全书）最**具体**的种族场景，而它的力量来自**它不讲任何议论**。整段没有出现 `racism`、`prejudice`、`colonial` 这类词——**只有**几个人在一家商店里说话，以及**一个声音从叽叽喳喳变成气喘吁吁**。这一克制使它比任何议论都更有说服力。而最关键的一处是 `appalled at what his entirely superficial amiability had unleashed`——这三个词（`appalled` / `superficial amiability` / `unleashed`）构成一个**完整的因果链**：**他做了什么？什么都没做。他只是和善。而正是那份和善释放了那个绝望的请求。** 这句话的逻辑是本段真正的**判断**，而它一个字也没有说「种族」。而她的**逃**（`abandoned my swimsuit, scrambled into my dress and fled`）——三个**急促的**动词（`abandoned` / `scrambled` / `fled`，最后一个最短最快）——与她的**在场**（`invisible and unwilling witness`）构成**矛盾**：她说自己「**不情愿**」，而**不情愿**的目击者**逃走了**，那么谁留下？答案落在她最后那个**诚实**的尾巴上——`so I do not know how it ended`（**所以我不知道后来怎样**）。她**不知道**结果，而这个「**不知道**」是这一段里**唯一**的不对称：那个年轻女人**承担**了这个故事的**后果**，而她**提前离场**。这一段与她后来对 Vidia 作品的判断（`But that’s not true!`）构成一组：**她两次都是因为在错误的位置上而得到了错误的理解**。

### ⑤

> **原句 5:** He sums up his account of racial friction thus: ‘Like monkeys pleading for evolution, each claiming to be whiter than the other, Indians and Negroes appeal to the unacknowledged white audience to see how much they despise one another. They despise one another by reference to the whites; and the irony is that their antagonism should have reached its peak today, when white prejudices have ceased to matter.’

**中文理解**：他这样总结他对**种族摩擦**的说明：「**像猴子乞求进化一样**，每一个都声称自己**比对方更白**，印度人与黑人**向那个未被承认的白人观众**申诉，好让他们看看**彼此多么鄙视**。他们**通过提及白人**来鄙视彼此；而**讽刺的是**，他们的敌意**本应在今天达到顶峰**——**在白人的偏见已经不再有影响的时候**。」

**句子结构**：两句。首句是「主句（主语 He + 谓语 sums up + 宾语 his account of racial friction + 方式状语 thus）+ 冒号引出直接引语」；引语本身是「**明喻主句**（`Like monkeys pleading for evolution,` 为比较状语；主句 `each claiming to be whiter than the other` 为分词短语）+ 独立主句（`Indians and Negroes` 作主语，`appeal to the unacknowledged white audience` 作谓语，`to see how much they despise one another` 作目的状语）+ 独立句（`They despise one another by reference to the whites`）+ 并列句（`and the irony is that their antagonism should have reached its peak today, when white prejudices have ceased to matter`）」。

**关键词**：he sums up his account of racial friction thus（他这样总结他对种族摩擦的说明）、Like monkeys pleading for evolution（像猴子乞求进化一样）、each claiming to be whiter than the other（每个都声称自己比对方更白）、appeal to the unacknowledged white audience（向那个未被承认的白人观众申诉）、to see how much they despise one another（让他们看看彼此多么鄙视）、They despise one another by reference to the whites（他们通过提及白人鄙视彼此）、the irony is that their antagonism should have reached its peak today（讽刺的是他们的敌意本应今天达到顶峰）、when white prejudices have ceased to matter（在白人的偏见已经不再有影响的时候）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`Like monkeys pleading for evolution,`**（**像猴子乞求进化**）这一**降级的明喻**——`monkeys` 在这个语境里是**贬义**的（把两个受压迫群体的自我贬低写成**动物性的乞求**），而 `pleading for evolution`（**乞求进化**）把「**声称更白**」这一行为写成**一个物种的自我要求**；用一个 **`the unacknowledged white audience`**（**那个未被承认的白人观众**）造出全段最锋利的名词——`unacknowledged`（**未被承认的**）是关键：观众**在**场，**却**不承认自己的**裁判身份**；用 **`by reference to the whites`**（**通过提及白人**）这一 **`by reference to`** 的技术说法把「鄙视」标为**间接**的；末句用一个 **`should have reached its peak today`**（**本应今天达到顶峰**）的 `should have`（**本应而未**）把一个**悖论**写成**时间上的错误**。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章**唯一的**一次让 Vidia **自己**说话（直接引语），而她选中的这一段恰恰是他**对种族的概括**。这句概括的**力量**在 `the unacknowledged white audience` 这个短语上：它指出黑人与印度人的敌意**需要一个不在名单上的裁判**，而这个裁判**恰恰因为「不被承认」而可以继续当裁判**。这个结构与她自己的位置重合得惊人——她是一名**英国出版商**、一个**局外人**（`an outsider`）、一个**站在更衣间里偷听的白人女人**。她引用这句话而不加评论，而**引用本身就是评论**：`unacknowledged` 与她自己在这一章里的位置**完全一致**（她在场、她记录、她不承认自己在这个结构里）。这就是这一段为什么**不需要**她的评论——而这也正是她在本章**最大的结构性缺席**（见「可质疑处」第 3 条）。而末句那个 `should have reached its peak today` 是全段最冷的一击：敌意在**应该**达到顶峰的时候**不再增长**，因为**对象已不复存在**——这是一个**反高潮**结构（**本应**达到顶峰，而它没有），而它与她自己的时代（1970 年代的西印度群岛）严格对应。

> **原句 6:** Of course they did resent it – who doesn’t resent hearing disagreeable truths told in a manner verging on the arrogant? But I think the label ‘racist’ which they stuck on him was, so to speak, only a local one. I saw him as a man raised in, and frightened by, a somewhat disorderly, inefficient and self-deceiving society, who therefore longed for order, clarity and competence. Having concluded that the lack of these qualities in the place where he was born came from the people’s lack of roots, he over-valued a sense of history and respect for tradition, choosing to romanticize their results rather than to see the complex and far from admirable scenes with which they often co-exist. (His first visit to India, described in An Area of Darkness, left him in a state of distress because it showed him that an ancient civilization in which he had dared to hope that he would find the belonging he hungered for could be just as disorderly and inefficient as the place where he was born.) Although both England and the United States were each in its own way going to fall short of his ideal society, Europe as a whole came more close, more often, to offering a life in which he could feel comfortable. I remember driving, years ago, through a vine-growing region of France and coming on a delightful example of an ancient expertise taking pleasure in itself: a particularly well cultivated vineyard which had a pillar rose – a deep pink pillar rose – planted as an exquisite punctuation at the end of every row. Instantly – although it was weeks since I had seen or thought of him – he popped into my head: ‘How Vidia would like that!’

**中文理解**：他们**当然**怨恨它——谁不怨恨听到**不愉快的真话**被以一种**近乎傲慢**的方式说出来？但我认为，他们贴在他身上的 `racist` 这个**标签**——**这么说吧**——**只是一个本地的**。我把他看作一个**在**一个**多少有点混乱、低效、自我欺骗的社会里**长大、并且被它**吓着**的人，因此**渴望**秩序、清晰与能力。在**断定**他出生地**缺少**这些品质**来自人们缺乏根基**之后，他**过分看重**一种历史感与对传统的尊重，选择**浪漫化**他们的**结果**，而不去看见那些他们常常**并存**的**复杂而远不值得称道**的场景。（他**第一次**去印度，**记在** *An Area of Darkness* 里，**让他陷入**一种**痛苦**的状态，因为那趟旅行**向他显示**：一个**古老文明**——他在其中**曾敢希望**找到他**所渴望**的**归属**——**也可以**与他出生地**一样混乱、一样低效**。）虽然**英格兰与美国**各自都以**自己的方式**达不到他**理想的社会**，但**欧洲整体**上**更接近**、**更经常地**提供一种**他能感到舒适**的生活。我记得多年前**开车**穿过法国一个**种葡萄**的地区，遇到一个**令人愉快的例子**：一种**古老的技艺**在**以其自身为乐**——一个**特别精心栽培的葡萄园**，它有一株**柱状玫瑰**——一朵**深粉色**的柱状玫瑰——**被种在每一行的末端，作为一句精美标点**。**立刻**——尽管那时我已经**好几个星期**没见过他、也没**想起**过他——**他冒进了我脑子**：「**Vidia 会多喜欢那个啊！**」

**句子结构**：五段。首段是「独立句 Of course they did resent it –（破折号引出反问）who doesn’t resent hearing disagreeable truths told in a manner verging on the arrogant? + 转折句 But I think the label ‘racist’ which they stuck on him was, so to speak, only a local one」；次段是「主句 I saw him as a man raised in, and frightened by, a somewhat disorderly, inefficient and self-deceiving society（内含两个并列的过去分词 raised in / frightened by + 三个并列形容词 disorderly, inefficient and self-deceiving）, who therefore longed for order, clarity and competence + 独立完成分词句 Having concluded that …, he over-valued …, choosing to … rather than to …」；括注是「主句 His first visit to India, described in An Area of Darkness, left him in a state of distress because it showed him that（宾语从句内含定语从句 in which he had dared to hope that he would find the belonging he hungered for）」；末段是「让步句 Although both England and the United States were each in its own way going to fall short of his ideal society, Europe as a whole came more close, more often, to offering a life in which he could feel comfortable + 时间状语 I remember driving, years ago, through a vine-growing region of France（三个动名词 driving / coming on）+ 冒号引出同位语 a delightful example of an ancient expertise taking pleasure in itself + 冒号 + 同位语 a particularly well cultivated vineyard which had a pillar rose – a deep pink pillar rose – planted as an exquisite punctuation at the end of every row + 时间状语 Instantly – although it was weeks since I had seen or thought of him – he popped into my head: ‘How Vidia would like that!’」。

**关键词**：disagreeable truths told in a manner verging on the arrogant（不愉快的真话被以近乎傲慢的方式说出）、the label ‘racist’ which they stuck on him（他们贴在他身上的「racist」标签）、only a local one（只是一个本地的）、a man raised in, and frightened by, a somewhat disorderly, inefficient and self-deceiving society（一个在混乱低效自我欺骗的社会里长大并被它吓着的人）、longed for order, clarity and competence（渴望秩序、清晰与能力）、the lack of these qualities … came from the people’s lack of roots（缺少这些品质来自人们缺乏根基）、he over-valued a sense of history and respect for tradition（他过分看重历史感与对传统的尊重）、choosing to romanticize their results rather than to see the complex and far from admirable scenes with which they often co-exist（选择浪漫化他们的结果而不看那些常与之并存的复杂而远不值得称道的场景）、in which he had dared to hope that he would find the belonging he hungered for（他在其中曾敢希望找到他渴望的归属）、came more close, more often, to offering a life in which he could feel comfortable（更接近、更经常地提供他能感到舒适的生活）、a vine-growing region of France（法国一个种葡萄的地区）、an ancient expertise taking pleasure in itself（一种以其自身为乐的古老技艺）、a pillar rose – a deep pink pillar rose –（柱状玫瑰——深粉色的柱状玫瑰——）、planted as an exquisite punctuation at the end of every row（种在每一行末端作为一句精美标点）、he popped into my head（他冒进我脑子）、How Vidia would like that!（Vidia 会多喜欢那个啊）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`who doesn’t resent … ?`**（**谁不怨恨……？**）这个**反问**把「他们怨恨」写成**普遍的人性**（而不是特立尼达人的特性）；用一个 **`only a local one`**（**只是一个本地的**）把标签的**效力**限定在**地点**上——这个词与 ch12 那条「**沟通**」的判据、ch15 那条「**本地**」的读法同族；用一个 **`over-valued`**（**过分看重**）与 **`choosing to romanticize … rather than to see …`**（**选择浪漫化而不是去看**）写出**判断的**操作**（**他在哪一步换了方向**）；用一个 **`could be just as disorderly and inefficient as the place where he was born`**（**也可以与他出生地一样混乱低效**）——`just as`（**恰好一样**）是全句最重的一个词，它使**印度**与**特立尼达**在**缺陷**上平级；用一个 **`an exquisite punctuation at the end of every row`**（**每一行末端的一句精美标点**）把**园艺**写成**排版**（`punctuation` = 标点）；末句用一个 **`popped into my head`**（**冒进脑子**）——`pop` 这一动作的**突然性**与前句 `a particularly well cultivated vineyard`（**精心栽培**）的**缓慢**形成对比。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章对 Vidia **最完整的判决书**，而它的写法是**先免除标签、再给出成因、然后指出成因本身就是错的**。第一步（`only a local one`）解除 `racist` 这个标签——但注意她**没有**说他不是种族主义者，她说的是这个标签**在那个地方是本地的**（`so to speak`，**这么说吧**——一个**不承诺**的插入语）。第二步给出她的解释（`disorderly, inefficient and self-deceiving` → `longed for order, clarity and competence`）：这不是**辩护**，这是一条**因果链**，而它的**功能**是把他的**偏好**解释为**反应**。第三步是她自己下的**诊断**：`he over-valued a sense of history … choosing to romanticize their results rather than to see the complex and far from admirable scenes` ——**浪漫化结果**而不看**并存的场景**，这是一句**技术性**的批评（它说的是他的**方法**，不是他的**心**）。第四步用印度作**反证**：他的理想（`ancient civilization` + `belonging`）在**实地**被**证伪**，于是他**终身**没有一个可以认同的祖先文明——而这一条与他那句 `unacknowledged white audience` 恰好互为镜像：他要一个**承认他的祖先**，而他能找到的只有**不承认他们的观众**。第五步是全段最轻的一笔，也是全章唯一一次**轻松**：法国葡萄园那个柱状玫瑰被写成 `an exquisite punctuation at the end of every row`，而 `he popped into my head`（**他冒进我脑子**）——这一句证明她**确实**懂他（**她能想象他喜欢什么**），而它被放在**判决之后**，因此这一段的收束不是**判决**而是**熟悉**。这两种判断在她这里**同时成立**——这正是 ch14 那位 Sonia 的做法（`to stop me thinking she was more generous than she was`）：**理解一个人**与**判定他的错误**不冲突。

> **原句 7:** Vidia’s mother, handsome and benignly matronly, welcomed his publishers very kindly when they visited Trinidad, and gave the impression of being the beloved linchpin of her family. When I first met them, long before they had been stricken by the close-together deaths of one of the daughters and of Shiva, Vidia’s younger and only brother, they impressed me as a flourishing lot: good-looking, intelligent, charming, successful. A married daughter told me that Mrs Naipaul ‘divides her time between the Temple and the quarry’ – the latter being a business belonging to her side of the family, in which she was a partner. That she was not simply a comfortable mother-figure became apparent when she told me that she had just got home from attending a seminar on welding and was very glad that she hadn’t missed it because she had learnt enough at it to be able to cut the number of welders they employed at the quarry by half. Soon afterwards she threw more light on her own character by making a little speech to me, after noticing my surprise when she had appeared to be indifferent to some news about Vidia. She had been, she said, a well-brought-up Hindu girl of her generation, so she had been given no education and was expected to obey her parents in everything, and that was what she did. Then she was married (‘And there was no nonsense about falling in love in those days’), whereupon it was her husband she had to obey in everything, and that was what she did. Then she had her children, so of course it was her job to devote herself entirely to them and bring them up as well as possible, and that was what she did (‘and I think I can say I made a good job of it’). ‘But then I said to myself, when I am fifty – FINISH. I will begin to live for myself. And that is what I am doing now and they must get on with their own lives.’

**中文理解**：Vidia 的母亲，**好看**而**带着一种温和的母亲气**（`benignly matronly`），在他**的出版商们**访问特立尼达时**非常热情地**接待了他们，并给人一种印象：她是家里**被爱的中枢**。我**第一次**见到他们时——那是在**一个女儿**与 **Shiva**（他**更年轻、也是唯一的**弟弟）**接连**去世、**他们**因此被**打击**之前**很久**——他们给我的印象是**一大家子都很兴旺**：好看、聪明、迷人、成功。一位**已婚的姐姐**告诉我，Naipaul 太太「**把她的时间分给神庙和采石场**」——后者是一门属于**她娘家**的**生意**，她是其中**一个合伙人**。而她**并不只是**一个**安逸的母亲形象**，这一点在她告诉我**她刚从一个焊接研讨会上回到家**、而且**很高兴自己没有错过**（因为她在那里学到的东西**足够让她把采石场雇的焊工人数减半**）时就**变得明显**了。**不久之后**，她**透露了更多**自己性格的光亮——在**注意到**我**惊讶**（因为她**似乎对**关于 Vidia 的**某条消息**无动于衷）之后，她**对我做了一段小小的讲话**。她说，她曾是她那一代一个**被好好养大的印度教女孩**，因此她**没有得到任何教育**，并且**被期望在一切事上服从父母**，而**她就是这么做的**。**然后**她**结婚了**（「**在那些日子里，谈恋爱没那些胡说八道**」），从那以后**在一切事上她必须服从的是她的丈夫**，而**她就是这么做的**。**然后**她**有了孩子**，因此**当然**，**她的职责**就是**完全献身于他们**、**尽可能把他们养大**，而**她就是这么做的**（「**而且我想我可以说，我做得不错**」）。「**但后来我对自己说，等我五十岁——结束。我要开始为自己而活。而我现在就是这么做的，他们得去过他们自己的生活。**」

**句子结构**：三段。首段是「主句（主语 Vidia’s mother（内含两个并列的前置形容词 handsome and benignly matronly）, 谓语 welcomed his publishers + 时间状语 very kindly when they visited Trinidad）+ 并列句 and gave the impression of being the beloved linchpin of her family」；次段是「时间状语从句 When I first met them（插入语 long before they had been stricken by the close-together deaths of one of the daughters and of Shiva, Vidia’s younger and only brother）+ 主句 they impressed me as a flourishing lot: good-looking, intelligent, charming, successful」；第三段是「引语转述（`A married daughter told me that Mrs Naipaul ‘divides her time between the Temple and the quarry’` + 破折号同位语 `– the latter being a business belonging to her side of the family, in which she was a partner`）+ 主句 That she was not simply a comfortable mother-figure became apparent when she told me that（时间状语从句内含两个并列的原因状语 because she had learnt enough at it to be able to cut the number of welders they employed at the quarry by half）+ 并列句 Soon afterwards she threw more light on her own character by making a little speech to me, after noticing my surprise when she had appeared to be indifferent to some news about Vidia」；末段是「**间接引语中的间接引语**」（`She had been, she said, a well-brought-up Hindu girl of her generation, so … and that was what she did.` → `Then she was married（括注插入直接引语 ‘And there was no nonsense about falling in love in those days’）, whereupon it was her husband she had to obey in everything, and that was what she did.` → `Then she had her children, so of course it was her job to devote herself entirely to them and bring them up as well as possible, and that was what she did（括注插入直接引语 ‘and I think I can say I made a good job of it’）`）」+ 末句**直接引语**（`‘But then I said to myself, when I am fifty – FINISH. I will begin to live for myself. And that is what I am doing now and they must get on with their own lives.’`）——注意 `FINISH` 用**全大写**、`I will` 用**将来时**。

**关键词**：handsome and benignly matronly（好看而带着一种温和的母亲气）、gave the impression of being the beloved linchpin of her family（给人她是家里被爱的中枢的印象）、long before they had been stricken by the close-together deaths（早在他们被接连的死讯打击很久之前）、a flourishing lot（兴旺的一大家子）、divides her time between the Temple and the quarry（把时间分给神庙和采石场）、a business belonging to her side of the family（属于她娘家的生意）、a seminar on welding（一个焊接研讨会）、cut the number of welders they employed at the quarry by half（把采石场雇的焊工人数减半）、threw more light on her own character（透露了更多自己性格的光亮）、a well-brought-up Hindu girl of her generation（她那一代一个被好好养大的印度教女孩）、given no education and was expected to obey her parents in everything（没有得到任何教育，被期望在一切事上服从父母）、and that was what she did（而她就是这么做的）（三处重复）、there was no nonsense about falling in love in those days（在那些日子里谈恋爱没那些胡说八道）、it was her job to devote herself entirely to them（她的职责是完全献身于孩子）、when I am fifty – FINISH（等我五十岁——结束）、I will begin to live for myself（我要开始为自己而活）、they must get on with their own lives（他们得去过他们自己的生活）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`benignly matronly`**（**带着一种温和的母亲气**）这一**造词**（`benign` + `-ly` + `matronly`）作**全段第一个词组**——这个复合词把「**母亲**」与「**温和的**」压成一个**印象**，而全段正是要**拆掉**这个印象；用三次 **`and that was what she did`**（**而她就是这么做的**）作为**结构的节拍器**——三段人生（服从父母 / 服从丈夫 / 献身孩子）**每一段**都以同一句**收尾**，于是「服从」被写成**被重复三次的动作**；用 **`whereupon`**（**从那以后**）这一**正式的**连词标记**第二次**转折（第一次是 `Then`），使正式度**随处境而升**；用 **`FINISH`**（**结束**）的**全大写**与 **`I will`** 的**将来时**标记一个**决定的诞生**——而这个决定的**内容**是**开始**（`I will begin to live for myself`），**形式**是**结束**（`FINISH`）；末句用一个 **`they must get on with their own lives`**（**他们得去过他们自己的生活**）——`must`（**得**）在这里是**她推给别人的责任**。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章**唯一**一次从**家庭内部**取材料，而它的结构选择（三次 `that was what she did`）使**服从**成为一个**重复的仪式**而不是一次**事件**。这个形式选择与内容**严格对应**：母亲的整个一生是**三次**同样形状的段落，而每一段都以**同一句**结束——因此「**结束**」这个词（`FINISH`）的**必要性**才成立：她必须等到一个**具体数字**（`fifty`）才能**允许自己**停。而这一段最锋利的一处是那个**焊接研讨会**（`a seminar on welding … cut the number of welders … by half`）——这不是一个「**母亲喜欢机器**」的趣闻，而是一个**能力**的证明：她**能**学、**能**算、**能**削减成本。而这个能力与 `a comfortable mother-figure`（**安逸的母亲形象**）**互斥**。也就是说：她的母亲**既**是**受压迫的**（`no education`、`obey her parents in everything`），**又**是**有能力的**（焊接、算账、合伙人）——而这两者**不在**「racist」这个标签能解释的范围里。这就是她写这一段的用意：Vidia 的 `disorderly, inefficient and self-deceiving`（混乱低效自我欺骗）来自**这里**，而 `self-deceiving` 这个词**在母亲身上也成立**（`I think I can say I made a good job of it`）——她在**评估自己的一生**时用的正是 Vidia 用的那种**自我辩护的语法**（把一个**结果**说得**像**一个成就）。而这一段是全章**唯一**提供了**乐观**的地方（`I will begin to live for myself`），它也恰好是**唯一**一个由**女性**说出、而**她自己从未**有过的话。

### ⑧

> **原句 8:** With every one of his books – and we published eighteen of them – there was a three-part pattern. First came a long period of peace while he was writing, during which we saw little of him and I would often have liked to see more, because I would be full of curiosity about the new book. Then, when it was delivered, there would be a short burst of euphoria during which we would have enjoyable meetings and my role would be to appreciate the work, to write the blurb, to hit on a jacket that pleased both him and us, and to see that the script was free of typist’s errors (he was such a perfectionist that no editing, properly speaking, was necessary). Then came part three: post-publication gloom, during which his voice on the telephone would make my heart sink – just a little during the first few years, deeper and deeper with the passing of time. His voice became charged with tragedy, his face became haggard, his theme became the atrocious exhaustion and damage (the word damage always occurred) this book had inflicted on him, and all to what end? Reviewers were ignorant monkeys, publishers (this would be implied in a sinister fashion rather than said) were lazy and useless: what was the point of it all? Why did he go on?

**中文理解**：**他每一部书**——而我们出版了他的**十八部**——都有一个**三段式**的**模式**。**第一**是一段**漫长的平静期**（在他**写作**时），在此期间我们**很少见到他**，而我**常常**会**希望多见见他**，因为我**心里**对**新书**充满**好奇**。**然后**，当它**被交来**时，会有**一阵短促的狂喜**，在此期间我们会有**愉快的会面**，而**我的角色**就是**欣赏这份作品**、**写那本书的推荐语**、**想出一个让他和我们都满意的封面**，以及**确保那份稿子里没有打字员的错误**（他**是个如此追求完美的人**，以至于严格说来**不需要任何编辑**）。**然后**是**第三部分**：**出版后的阴郁**，在此期间他**在电话里的声音**会**让我心里下沉**——**最初几年只是**一点点，**随着时间过去**则**越来越深**。他的**声音**变得**充满悲剧**，他的**脸**变得**憔悴**，他的**主题**变成这本书**加诸他身上的**那两个可怕的词**（**`damage` 这个词每次都出现**）：**筋疲力尽与伤害**，而这一切**为了什么**？**评论家**是**无知的猴子**，**出版商**（这一点会被**以一种阴森的方式暗示而不是说出**）是**懒惰而无用的**：这一切**有什么意义**？他**为什么还要继续**？

**句子结构**：两句。首句是「**双重插入**开头句 With every one of his books – and we published eighteen of them –（破折号插入的补充 + 第二重破折号插入）there was a three-part pattern」；次句是「三段**标记式**的长句：`First came a long period of peace while he was writing, during which we saw little of him and I would often have liked to see more, because I would be full of curiosity about the new book. / Then, when it was delivered, there would be a short burst of euphoria during which we would have enjoyable meetings and my role would be to appreciate the work, to write the blurb, to hit on a jacket that pleased both him and us, and to see that the script was free of typist’s errors (he was such a perfectionist that no editing, properly speaking, was necessary). / Then came part three: post-publication gloom, during which his voice on the telephone would make my heart sink – just a little during the first few years, deeper and deeper with the passing of time. / His voice became charged with tragedy, his face became haggard, his theme became the atrocious exhaustion and damage (the word damage always occurred) this book had inflicted on him, and all to what end? / Reviewers were ignorant monkeys, publishers (this would be implied in a sinister fashion rather than said) were lazy and useless: what was the point of it all? Why did he go on?`」——三段分别由 `First …` / `Then, when …` / `Then … part three:` 领起。

**关键词**：With every one of his books – and we published eighteen of them –（他每一部书——而我们出版了十八部）、a three-part pattern（三段式的模式）、a long period of peace while he was writing（他写作时一段漫长的平静期）、we saw little of him and I would often have liked to see more（我们很少见到他而我常常希望多见见）、I would be full of curiosity about the new book（心里对新书充满好奇）、a short burst of euphoria（一阵短促的狂喜）、my role would be to appreciate the work, to write the blurb, to hit on a jacket（我的角色是欣赏作品、写推荐语、想封面）、to see that the script was free of typist’s errors（确保稿子里没有打字员的错误）、he was such a perfectionist that no editing, properly speaking, was necessary（他如此追求完美，严格说来不需要任何编辑）、post-publication gloom（出版后的阴郁）、his voice on the telephone would make my heart sink（他在电话里的声音让我心里下沉）、just a little during the first few years, deeper and deeper with the passing of time（最初几年只是一点点，随着时间越来越深）、His voice became charged with tragedy（他的声音充满悲剧）、his face became haggard（他的脸变得憔悴）、the atrocious exhaustion and damage（那两个可怕词：筋疲力尽与伤害）、the word damage always occurred（damage 这个词每次都出现）、Reviewers were ignorant monkeys（评论家是无知的猴子）、publishers … were lazy and useless（出版商是懒惰而无用的）、would be implied in a sinister fashion rather than said（会被以一种阴森的方式暗示而不是说出）、what was the point of it all（这一切有什么意义）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`a three-part pattern`**（**三段式的模式**）**宣布**结构，然后用 **`First` / `Then, when` / `Then … part three:`** **逐段领起**——这是**用语法兑现承诺**（与 ch13 那四个 `Why` 属同一手法）；用 **`a long period of peace`**（**漫长的平静**）与 **`a short burst of euphoria`**（**短促的狂喜**）与 **`post-publication gloom`**（**出版后的阴郁**）三个**长度**不同的名词短语，使第一段**长**、第二段**短**、第三段**沉**；用 **`my role would be to appreciate …, to write …, to hit on …, and to see that …`** 四个 `to` 不定式写**她工作的清单**；用 **`he was such a perfectionist that no editing, properly speaking, was necessary`**（`such … that` 结构 + `properly speaking` 插入语）——这个 `perfectionist` 与 ch14 那位 Jean（`on points of detail she was such a perfectionist`）**同词**；用 **`just a little during the first few years, deeper and deeper with the passing of time`** 这一**比较级递增**（`a little` → `deeper and deeper`）写出**同一个循环会加深**；用 **`ignorant monkeys`**（**无知的猴子**）这个**具体的动物**作**评语对象**；用一个**关于「没说」的括注**（`this would be implied in a sinister fashion rather than said`）记录**他如何提到出版商**。

**为什么这样写**：这一段是全章的**节律核心**，而它的写法是**先宣布节拍、再逐拍执行**——`a three-part pattern` 之后紧跟 `First` / `Then` / `Then`，这不是叙述而是**分析**。而三个阶段的名字**长度不同**（`a long period of peace` / `a short burst of euphoria` / `post-publication gloom`），因此读者**读得出**这个循环的**不均衡**：写作期**最长**，狂喜期**最短**，而阴郁期**无界**（它可以持续到下一本书动笔）。第三段里那句 `just a little … deeper and deeper with the passing of time` 是全章**最冷**的观察之一——她**十七本书**（`eighteen of them`）逐年经历同一个循环，而循环的**第三段**逐年**加深**；也就是说**时间**本身让他**更难受**，而这与他在 `He was to report that ten years later in his career … he was still tormented by anxiety` 里的说法**完全一致**（`ten years later` / `deeper and deeper`）。那个 `the word damage always occurred`（`damage` 这个词**每次都出现**）是本章**最精细**的一处观察，而它属于**用词**层（不是心理层）：他坚持用**编辑的词**来描述自己**所受的东西**——`damage`（**伤害**，通常用于物）——而她自己在前文用的是 `just flawed`（**只是有缺陷**，用于书）。**两人对同一件东西的用词**差着一个**语域**。而 `Reviewers were ignorant monkeys` 与 `publishers … were lazy and useless` 这一对**名单**里的**她本人**是第二项——这使这一段的怨气**也指向她**，而她把这个事实写成**一句关于「他如何说他」的话**（`this would be implied in a sinister fashion rather than said`），而不是**她自己的感受**。

### ⑨

> **原句 9:** Self-brainwashing sometimes has to be a part of an editor’s job. You are no use to the writers on your list if you cannot bring imaginative sympathy to working with them, and if you cease to be of use to them you cease to be of use to your firm. Imaginative sympathy cannot issue from a cold heart so you have to like your writers. Usually this is easy; but occasionally it happens that in spite of admiring someone’s work you are – or gradually become – unable to like the person.

**中文理解**：**自我洗脑**有时**必须**成为**编辑工作**的一部分。如果你**无法**对你的作者名单上的作家**拿出富有想象力的同情**去与他们一起工作，你就**对他们没有用**；而如果你**不再**对他们**有用**，你就**不再对**你的**公司**有用。**富有想象力的同情**不可能**出自一颗冷心**，所以你**必须喜欢**你的作家。**通常**这**很容易**；但**偶尔**会**发生**这样的事：**尽管**钦佩某人的**作品**，你**却**——或**逐渐变得**——**无法喜欢**这个人。

**句子结构**：四句。首句是「**合成的复合词作主语** Self-brainwashing（`self-` 前缀 + `brainwashing` 名词）+ 情态动词 sometimes has to be + 表语 a part of an editor’s job」；次句是「条件句 `You are no use to the writers on your list if you cannot bring imaginative sympathy to working with them` + 并列条件句 `and if you cease to be of use to them you cease to be of use to your firm`（`them` 与 `your firm` 构成**两级的对偶**）」；第三句是「主句 Imaginative sympathy cannot issue from a cold heart（`issue from` = 源自）+ 结果连词 so + 主句 you have to like your writers」；末句是「频度状语 Usually this is easy + 转折连词 but + 引导词 it happens that + 主句（in spite of admiring someone’s work 为介词短语）you are – or gradually become – unable to like the person」。

**关键词**：Self-brainwashing sometimes has to be a part of an editor’s job（自我洗脑有时必须成为编辑工作的一部分）、You are no use to the writers on your list（对你的作者名单上的作家你没有用）、if you cannot bring imaginative sympathy to working with them（如果你无法拿出富有想象力的同情去与他们一起工作）、if you cease to be of use to them you cease to be of use to your firm（如果你不再对他们有用，你就不再对你的公司有用）、Imaginative sympathy cannot issue from a cold heart（富有想象力的同情不可能出自一颗冷心）、you have to like your writers（你必须喜欢你的作家）、Usually this is easy（通常这很容易）、in spite of admiring someone’s work（尽管钦佩某人的作品）、you are – or gradually become – unable to like the person（你却无法喜欢这个人——或逐渐变得无法喜欢）

**表达方式**：用一个 **`Self-brainwashing`**（**自我洗脑**）这个**心理学术语**作全句主语——而这个词一旦写出来就**不可撤回**：它把「**说服自己**」命名为**一种技术**，从而使下一句的**利益理由**（`your firm`）显得是**这项技术的正当用途**，而不是它的**滥用**；用一个 **`You are no use … / if you cease to be of use … / you cease to be of use …`** 的**三段 `be of no use` 结构**（`no use` → `of use` → `cease to be of use`）把「有用」写成一条**从作家到公司的传递链**；用一个 **`Imaginative sympathy cannot issue from a cold heart`**（**同情不可能出自一颗冷心**）把一个**能力**判定为**不可伪造**的（`cannot` + `issue from`）——而 `have to like`（**必须喜欢**）用 `to like`（一个**不情愿**的动词）而**不是** `to sympathize`（**同情**），把门槛**又抬高**一层；末句用一个 **`or gradually become`**（**或逐渐变得**）的破折号插入承认这个变化**可以是渐进的**。

**为什么这样写**：这三句是全章的**方法论**，而它的形式是**一次职业定义的自我修订**。第一句 `Self-brainwashing sometimes has to be a part of an editor’s job` 表面上是在**为一种做法辩护**，而它真正的功能是**为上一段那个循环（`post-publication gloom`）提供对策**：她无法让 Vidia **不**忧郁，于是她改变**自己**。而 `has to`（**必须**）把这一对策写成**义务**——`sometimes`（**有时**）让步，`has to`（**必须**）收紧，句子的**修辞张力**全在这两个词之间。第二句给出了**理由**，而这个理由的形式是**一条传递链**（`them` → `your firm`），因此**同情**与**忠诚**、与**为公司有用**被写成**同一件事**——这一句是全章**最可疑也最重要**的一句（见「可质疑处」第 2 条）。第三句 `Imaginative sympathy cannot issue from a cold heart so you have to like your writers` 则是一个**更严格的说法**：同情**不能**靠**意愿**伪造，因此你**必须喜欢**——而 `like`（**喜欢**）比 `sympathize`（**同情**）**更难**也更**私人**。于是全句链是：同情**不可伪造** → 因此**必须喜欢** → 而**不能喜欢**就**对公司没用**。这三层把 ch12 那条「**了解（法国的 ability）比同情更难**」**倒过来**了：她说**同情**比**喜欢**难，而 ch12 说**了解**比**同情**难——两者**方向相反**，而这一章正是这个矛盾的**实证**（她对 Vidia 的**了解**其实很浅，见下文 *Guerrillas*）。

### ⑩

> **原句 10:** We have now reached the second of my two shocking failures as an editor (I don’t intend ever to confess the other one). From the professional point of view there was no question as to what I ought to do: this was one of our most valuable authors; even if his book had been really bad rather than just flawed we would certainly have published it in the expectation that he would soon be back on form; so what I must say was ‘wonderful’ and damn’ well sound as though I meant it.

**中文理解**：我们**现在**已经到了**我作为编辑的两次惊人失败**中的**第二次**（另一个我**永远不打算**承认）。从**职业**的**观点**看，我**该**做**什么**是**毫无疑问**的：这是我们**最有价值**的作者之一；**即使**他的书**真是很糟**而不只是**有缺陷**，我们也**一定**会**出**它，**指望**他**很快**会**恢复状态**；所以我**必须说**的话**就是**「**太好了**」，而且**他妈的**要**听起来像是真心的**。

**句子结构**：两句。首句是「时间状语 We have now reached + 宾语 the second of my two shocking failures as an editor（后置定语 as an editor）+ 括注插入语（I don’t intend ever to confess the other one）」；次句是「状语 From the professional point of view + 主句 there was no question as to what I ought to do + 冒号引出三段理由（`this was one of our most valuable authors;` + 条件让步句 `even if his book had been really bad rather than just flawed we would certainly have published it in the expectation that he would soon be back on form;` + 结果连词 `so what I must say was ‘wonderful’ and damn’ well sound as though I meant it.`）」。

**关键词**：We have now reached the second of my two shocking failures as an editor（我作为编辑的两次惊人失败中的第二次）、I don’t intend ever to confess the other one（另一个我永远不打算承认）、From the professional point of view（从职业的观点看）、there was no question as to what I ought to do（我该做什么是毫无疑问的）、one of our most valuable authors（我们最有价值的作者之一）、even if his book had been really bad rather than just flawed（即使他的书真是很糟而不只是有缺陷）、we would certainly have published it（我们一定会出它）、in the expectation that he would soon be back on form（指望他很快恢复状态）、what I must say was ‘wonderful’（我必须说的话就是「太好了」）、damn’ well sound as though I meant it（要他妈的听起来像是真心的）

**表达方式**：用 **`the second of my two shocking failures`**（**两次惊人失败中的第二次**）配一个**括注的拒绝**（`I don’t intend ever to confess the other one`）——把**全部失败史**压缩成**两个**并**藏起一个**；用 **`there was no question as to what I ought to do`**（**该做什么毫无疑问**）这一**否定形式**（不是「我知道该做什么」，而是「**没有疑问**」）把**道德问题**改写成**技术问题**；用一个 **`even if … really bad rather than just flawed`**（**真是很糟而不只是有缺陷**）的让步来**取消**「这本书写得好不好」这个问题（`rather than just flawed` ——**不是**只是**有缺陷**）；用一个 **`so what I must say was ‘wonderful’ and damn’ well sound as though I meant it`**（`sound as though` = **听起来像是**）作**落点**——而 `damn’ well` 这个**插入的强调**紧跟在**要传达的那个词**（`sound`）之前，使**「说谎」这件事本身**被写成一句**诅咒**。

**为什么这样写**：这是全章的**转折点**，而它的形式是**一次预先的免责**（`I don’t intend ever to confess the other one`）紧接**一次精确的处境描述**。而 `From the professional point of view there was no question as to what I ought to do`（**从职业观点看，我该做什么毫无疑问**）是全章**最坦率**的一句——她**不**说「我该说真话」，她说的是「**从职业观点看**该做什么没有疑问」，而这两件事在这一段里**恰好不同**。这一段的三个理由（`one of our most valuable authors` / `even if … we would certainly have published it` / `in the expectation that he would soon be back on form`）全部是**商业与关系的**理由——**没有一条是「他说得对」的理由**。而末句 `so what I must say was ‘wonderful’ and damn’ well sound as though I meant it` 的精妙之处在于它**不是**说谎的**许可**：它说的是**她必须说出那个词，并让它听起来是真的**。这两个动作是分开的——**说**（`say`）与**使听起来**（`sound`）——而这一分离正是 ch08 那条「**编辑的实际工作不可见**」的**最纯粹形态**（对照 ch08 里那句 `or rather, copies were sent`）：她**的**工作是**让一句话的**可信度**成立**，而可信度**不属于**这句话的**内容**。这一段与下一段（`Jane simply fell to pieces`）构成**同一次失败的两半**：这一半是**她必须说的**（职业义务），下一半是**她后来发现她说错了**（判断义务）。而两次义务的**冲突**在下一段里被解决——用一种**她称之为** `Self-brainwashing`（**自我洗脑**）的方式。

## 一句话总结

这一章把 ch14 的「写作与生活的裂缝」补成了**三种形态**——Jean 是**分离**（`She could not see herself when she was working`），Alfred 是**取代**（`they, not he, had written every word of his work`），而 Vidia 是**同一**（`Vidia’s self – his very being – was his writing: a great gift, but all he had`）；而全章的**代价**由她自己的两句自白标定：`he appeared to me far too impressive a person to be subject to such discomforts`（我把他看得太出色，因此看不见他的处境）与 `So I read the book again – and this time Jane simply fell to pieces`（我重读了一遍，而这次那个人物整个散架了）。
