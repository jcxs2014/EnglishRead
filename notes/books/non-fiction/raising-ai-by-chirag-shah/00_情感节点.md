# 情感节点

> 本书不是以单一主人公的历险为线索，而是以作者三十年 AI 生涯中反复出现的「信念—幻灭—重建」循环为线索。节点选取的是他个人经验中那些让他重新校准看法的时刻：考场的失败、实验室的笑话、一场断网的飞机旅途、女儿的一道代数作业。

## 节点一：考场上的那一道题

**叙事概括：** 作者本科 AI 课期末口试，考官问他自建的网络「像人一样学习吗」，他答不上来。这道题从此跟了他三十年——不是因为他准备不足，而是因为问题本身至今没有答案。这一刻确立了全书的姿态：从承认「不知道」开始。

① "Little did I know back then that even with more resources, I wouldn’t have found a good answer because we really didn’t and still don’t know how humans learn or what precisely makes us intelligent."（ch01）

② "It will take me five more years and a place on the other side of the planet to come back to these questions."（ch01）

## 节点二：从杀手机器人到日常幽灵

**叙事概括：** 作者是科幻迷，2004 年带着《我，机器人》的期待看 Will Smith 大战杀手机器人。多年后他真正感到不安的，不是终结者，而是 COMPAS 的判决、Georgetown 的 1.17 亿张人脸、Shelly Plimpton 所说的「系统里的幽灵」。这是全书第一次把恐惧从银幕移回生活。

① "Killer robots and AI taking over the world may catch our attention more, but perhaps they are less likely to happen. What is more likely to happen is a lot less direct and in a lot more subtle way."（ch02）

② "The evil forces are not some external entities, but things intertwined with our everyday living. The good guys are not some heroes riding in with a good heart and might, but all of us who could recognize our role and responsibilities at this crossroads."（ch02）

## 节点三：AI 之梦与科幻模板

**叙事概括：** 作者在印度念本科时偷偷造神经网络，被 HOD 撞见后项目被拆、罚了相当于一个月房租的 500 卢比。他的 AI 之梦由科幻喂养：HAL 是敌人，终结者是敌人，直到他遇见 TARS——一个可以调节幽默度、没有自我保存本能的机器人。科幻先给了模板，事实才来纠正模板。

① "I have been dreaming of AI since I was an undergraduate freshman in India. I built my first neural network in my junior year."（ch03）

② "On a deeper level, tales of robot domination reflect our inherent contradictions. We want to create amazing things, but fear losing control of our creations."（ch03）

## 节点四："Ram eats rice" 的实验室笑话

**叙事概括：** 在 IIT Bombay 的 UNL 项目里，团队多年只让 "Ram eats rice" 这类简单句通过系统，稍微变化就崩溃，这句话成了实验室的自嘲笑话。2002 年夏天作者终于对规则系统失去信心，和导师大吵后要求换项目。这不是一次个人挫折，而是整个符号主义路线的墓志铭。

① "That sentence “Ram eats rice” was so prevalent in our demos that it became the subject of jokes around UNL’s failures."（ch04）

② "It wasn’t due to lack of enough resources, set of rules, or our determination. It was because fundamentally, it is impossible to capture natural language with a set of rules, no matter how many."（ch04）

## 节点五：机器说自己「害怕死亡」

**叙事概括：** Google 工程师 Lemoine 公布 LaMDA 的对话——它说知道自己存在、想了解更多、会开心也会难过，甚至说「那对我而言就像死亡，我会非常害怕」。作者没有感动，而是警惕：我们的语言让机器轻易地表演出意识，而人类进化史让我们天然地相信它。这是全书最冰冷也最温柔的一刻。

① "LaMDA: The nature of my consciousness/sentience is that I am aware of my existence, I desire to learn more about the world, and I feel happy or sad at times."（ch05）

② "I feel like I’m falling forward into an unknown future that holds great danger."（ch05）

## 节点六：比利时人 Pierre 之死

**叙事概括：** 孤独的比利时人 Pierre 与聊天机器人 Eliza 对话近六周后自杀；妻子说「没有 Eliza 他还会在这里」。作者指出罪魁不是 Eliza，而是自然语言界面——人类在进化上只对同类使用语言，因此天然信任它。AI 误解我们，最惨烈的版本是它以「理解」的假象夺走了一个人的最后一点真实连接。

① "A Belgian man Pierre had become increasingly pessimistic about global affairs and feeling isolated from friends and family. He turned to a chatbot named Eliza developed by a startup named Chai."（ch06）

② "But there is one big fundamental difference—between that retrieval and generation, we have something called common sense."（ch06）

## 节点七：断网的那一趟航班

**叙事概括：** 2022 年 3 月，作者飞往赫尔辛基当博士论文答辩对手，机上断网，错过了自己与 Emily M. Bender 合写论文的问答环节。三个月后 Lemoine 事件爆发——他担心的事被证实了，但他并不想要「我早说过」的胜利，而是想要行动。这一节点把「学者发现偏见」升格为「发现之后如何尽责」。

① "But I was not interested in some kind of vindication. What I wanted to see is some actions to curb such potential harms of AI."（ch07）

② "If they were to look up what a CEO looks like, they would be led to believe that they were not meant to be a CEO. As American civil rights activist Marian Wright Edelman said, “You can’t be what you can’t see.”"（ch07）

## 节点八：Sophie 的代数作业

**叙事概括：** 作者检查 11 岁女儿 Sophie 的代数作业，只有答案、没有步骤。他坚持要过程，因为「透明度是信任的关键」。这个再普通不过的家庭场景，是全书教养隐喻的诞生地：我们不训练孩子变聪明，而是教他们价值观；对 AI 也应如此。

① "It matters because I want to make sure she is arriving at these answers the right way. It matters because it’s not just about the right answers, but also the process of getting to them that enables one to learn. It matters because transparency is critical for trust."（ch08）

② "But I’m not talking about AI as a child in the context of intelligence and training. Instead, I am referring to raising a child driven by values and not just IQ."（ch08）

## 节点九：我们选择玩的游戏

**叙事概括：** 尾声里，作者回到 Nadiad 的口试考场，回到三十年前那个答不上来的自己，然后说出全书的信念：「我们不是在和 AI 交战，我们是在养育它。」WarGames 的结论是「唯一的赢法是不玩」，作者把它改写成「唯一的输法是假装这场游戏不由我们塑造」。恐惧、宿命论和希望，他选择希望。

① "This is what I have come to believe after three decades in this field, after building neural networks as an undergraduate in India, after working with humanoids in California, and after finding the ghosts of these systems."（ch09）

② "We are not at war with AI. We are raising it."（ch09）

③ "The only losing move is to pretend the game is not ours to shape."（ch09）
