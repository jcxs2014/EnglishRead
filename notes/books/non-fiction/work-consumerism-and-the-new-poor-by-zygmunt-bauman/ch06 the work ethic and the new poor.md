# The work ethic and the new poor（劳动伦理与新穷人）

## 概览

- **出处**：*Work, Consumerism and the New Poor*，第四章 The work ethic and the new poor，第 63–86 页
- **作者**：Zygmunt Bauman
- **章节定位**：全书论证从「福利国家如何塌方」转向「劳动伦理如何复活并改头换面」。上一章证明福利国家同时失去经济基础与心理基础；本章要证明劳动伦理并没有跟着福利国家一起死掉，反而被重新征召，承担起给贫困道德化、把穷人犯罪化、把社会从道德责任中释放的新功能。它是「underclass/新穷人」概念的起源章节，也是全书从制度分析转向文化/道德分析的枢纽
- **字符数**：71,853 字符（text/ 精确计数，含空白与换行；自然段 104 个，空行分隔）
- **一句话主旨**：当劳动从财富之源变成生产率的障碍，劳动伦理不再为工厂招工服务，而是转为把贫困道德化、把穷人犯罪化、把社会从道德责任中释放的装置；而「underclass」的发明不是在描述事实，而是在把异质的穷人凝聚成一个「无用且危险」的集体他者，以填满冷战结束后社会内部的恐惧空缺。

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：劳动伦理的复活不是因为工厂又需要人，而是因为它获得了新功能——把贫困从「集体造成的灾难」重新定义为「个人的道德缺陷」，从而让社会在抛弃穷人时不必感到内疚。underclass 的概念是这一工程的核心工具：它把贫困与犯罪、懒惰、选择无能捆在一起，使穷人从「道德上应被帮助的人」变成「道德上应被惩罚的人」。

- **证据链**：

| 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |
|------|------|---------|
| WIN（书中只称其为美国 welfare-to-work 计划的代号，未给全称）的修辞与实际效果长期脱节 | 引证 | 劳动伦理政策存活不是因为它有效，而是因为它对非穷人有用 |
| 「例行劳动者」好卖也最好换，没有议价权 | 论证 | 柔性劳动市场把劳动者变成可随时替换的零件 |
| 精神不随身体进入职场：柔性 workplace 里 work ethic 空心化 | 论证 | 劳动伦理的物质基础（稳定就业与职业认同）已经瓦解 |
| 游牧资本 vs 定居劳动：资本无约束移动，劳动被绑在土地上 | 论证 | 劳动伦理的「努力就有回报」前提被资本流动性打破 |
| underclass 的意象：超越阶级、无角色、不可救赎 | 定义 | underclass 不是阶级分析概念，而是恐惧管理概念 |
| 无用与危险：穷人被看成「花园里的杂草」 | 论证 | underclass 的凝聚靠的不是事实，而是价值判断 |
| 冷战结束与 underclass 的「发现」同步 | 论证 | 外部敌人消失后，恐惧需要内部替身 |
| Myrdal 1963（经济排除）vs Time 1977（道德败坏） | 对比 | 同一个词在二十年内从结构分析滑向道德谴责 |
| Auletta 的修辞：underclass 主动作恶，多数人只是审判 | 论证 | underclass 叙事把因果关系反转——穷人是主动方，社会是被动方 |
| Mead：贫困是「选择无能」 | 引证 | 把贫困解释为心理/文化缺陷，为放弃救济提供理由 |
| 消费社会的 seduction 把未被满足的消费者 criminalize | 论证 | 贫困被重新定义为个人犯罪，监狱取代福利机构 |
| adiaphorization：把道德非难从行为上拿掉 | 定义 | 劳动伦理变成道德 empathy 的准入门槛，穷人因此被逐出道德义务宇宙 |

- **论证脉络**：先写出劳动伦理在十九世纪与二十世纪末的两种命运（factories 需人 → 生产率视劳动为障碍）→ 引 WIN 证明政策存活靠对非穷人的效用 → 用 Reich 的四类雇员展示「例行劳动者」的可替换性与无团结可能 → 说明柔性劳动市场如何抽空 work ethic 的精神基础 → 再用游牧资本/定居劳动的不对称解释为什么努力不再有回报 → 进入「underclass 的发现」：定义、无用与危险、冷战同步 → 对比 Myrdal 与 Time 的语义滑坡 → Auletta 的修辞分析 → Mead 把贫困解释为选择无能 → 最后把贫困 criminalize，并用 adiaphorization 说明劳动伦理如何把穷人逐出道德义务宇宙；Kapuscinski 的三重修辞术作为全球版本的收束。

## 选择性精读

### ① WIN 的修辞与效果脱节：政策存活靠对非穷人的效用

> **原句 1:** "... from its inception and throughout its convoluted history, the rhetorics justifying WIN bore little relationship to its actual impact. All the available evidence indicates that the programme has had dismal results ... Work policies and programmes have persisted in various forms, despite the overwhelming historical evidence that they have generally failed to reduce the welfare rolls in any appreciable way or to improve the economic self-sufficiency of the poor. The reasons for their survival cannot, therefore, reside in their salutary effects on the poor and welfare, but rather in their apparent utility to the nonpoor."

**中文理解**：从 WIN 诞生之初到它曲折的整个历史，为它辩护的修辞与它的实际效果几乎没有任何关系。所有现有证据都表明这个项目结果惨淡……工作政策与项目以各种形式持续存在，尽管压倒性的历史证据表明它们通常既没能明显减少福利名册，也没能改善穷人的经济自给自足。它们存活的原因因此不可能在于对穷人与福利的有益效果，而在于对非穷人的表面效用。

**句子结构**：第一句主干为 the rhetorics justifying WIN bore little relationship to its actual impact，bore 为 bear 的过去式，little relationship 是宾语。第二句主干为 All the available evidence indicates that the programme has had dismal results，that 从句是宾语从句。第三句主干为 Work policies and programmes have persisted in various forms，despite 引导让步状语从句说明持续存在的背景；despite 后接 the overwhelming historical evidence that they have generally failed...，其中 that 从句说明证据的内容。最后一句主干为 The reasons for their survival cannot reside in their salutary effects on the poor and welfare, but rather in their apparent utility to the nonpoor，cannot reside in... but rather in... 是并列的介词短语作表语补足。

**关键词**：from its inception and throughout its convoluted history / bore little relationship to its actual impact / dismal results / despite the overwhelming historical evidence / apparent utility to the nonpoor

**表达方式**：Handler 与 Hasenfeld 先立一个反差（修辞 vs 效果），再用 despite 让步把「证据都显示失败」与「政策却继续存在」并置，最后用 cannot... but rather... 反转因果：存活的解释不在穷人身上，而在非穷人身上。rhetorics 用复数形式暗示修辞不止一套，salutary effects 与 apparent utility 两个短语形成对位——前者是真好处，后者只是表象效用。

**为什么这样写**：这是全章的论证支点之一：劳动伦理政策的存续不是功能性的，而是政治性的。他们要让读者先接受「WIN 失败了」这个事实，再追问「那它为什么还活着」，答案是它对非穷人有用——它把穷人问题从「我们该不该帮助」转成「他们配不配被帮助」。apparent utility 的 apparent 一词是整句的刀锋：效用不是真实效用，只是 appearances，却足以让政策活下去。

### ② 例行劳动者：好卖也最好换

> **原句 2:** "People of that last category apparently face least difficulty when it comes to selling their labour. Their prospective buyers are not particularly fastidious. Since neither rare and special skills difficult to master nor particular acumen in dealing with customers in face-to-face interactions are required for the kind of jobs "routine labourers' are expected to perform, they can move relatively easily from one employment to another throughout the whole spectrum of paid-for low-skill work. But for the same reason they are also eminently disposable: their jobs are chronically unstable. They may be replaced at any moment with no loss to their employers, they could be fired on the spot when business slows down, since there are many like them ready to take up jobs once business picks up again - and so they have no "nuisance power', no bargaining assets, no chance of winning in a struggle for better employment terms and conditions, were they able and willing to fight."

**中文理解**：最后一类人卖劳动力时显然面临最小的困难，买方对他们并不特别挑剔。因为例行劳动者被期待做的工作既不需要难以掌握的稀有特殊技能，也不需要面对面与顾客打交道的特别机敏，他们可以在整个有偿低技能工作的范围内相对容易地从一个雇主换到另一个雇主。但正因为这个原因，他们也极易被替换：工作长期不稳定，随时可能被替换而雇主不受损失，生意一慢就地解雇，因为生意一好就有许多人愿意接班——因此他们没有「骚扰权」、没有议价筹码、没有在争取更好就业条件的斗争中获胜的机会，即使他们有意愿有能耐去斗。

**句子结构**：前两句是简单句。第三句主干为 they can move relatively easily from one employment to another，Since 引导原因状语从句，其中 neither... nor... 并列作主语，are required for the kind of jobs... 是谓语。第四句主干为 they are also eminently disposable，their jobs are chronically unstable 作同位/补充。第五句主干为 They may be replaced... they could be fired... since there are many like them ready to take up jobs...，and so 引导结果：they have no "nuisance power', no bargaining assets, no chance of winning...，were they able and willing to fight 是条件状语倒装。

**关键词**：face least difficulty / prospective buyers are not particularly fastidious / eminently disposable / chronically unstable / nuisance power / bargaining assets

**表达方式**：作者先写「好卖」（least difficulty、move easily），再用 But for the same reason 翻转：好卖的同一原因使他们「最好换」。nuisance power 是本段的发明词，把「让雇主感到麻烦」重新定义为唯一的议价资源——没有它，劳动者就没有谈判筹码。were they able and willing to fight 的倒装把「想斗也斗不了」埋在句尾，强调结构性无力而非意志缺欠。

**为什么这样写**：这是「例行劳动者」概念的核心：他们不是失业者，而是永远在就业与失业边缘游走的人。作者要说明劳动伦理在这样的劳动市场里无法成立——work ethic 前提是「努力就有稳定回报与职业尊严」，而例行劳动者既无稳定也无尊严，只有可替换性。no nuisance power 一句把权力关系说透：劳动者的议价权不是来自技能，而是来自「让雇主感到不方便」的能力，而例行劳动者连这个能力都没有。

### ③ 柔性职场：身体在、精神不在

> **原句 3:** "As long as they stay employed, the occasional, exchangeable, and eminently disposable "routine labourers' are bodily inside the workplace, but their spirits seldom follow the bodies. Workplace is still a source of living, but not of life-meaning - and certainly not a greenhouse of human bonds sufficiently solid and trustworthy enough to support and sustain ethical convictions and standards of moral practices."

**中文理解**：只要他们还受雇，这些偶尔的、可交换的、极易被替换的「例行劳动者」身体在职场里，但精神很少跟随身体。职场仍然是谋生的来源，但不再是生命意义的来源——更不可能是足够坚实、值得信赖的人际纽带的温室，去支撑和维持伦理信念与道德实践的标准。

**句子结构**：第一句主干为 "routine labourers' are bodily inside the workplace, but their spirits seldom follow the bodies，As long as they stay employed 是时间状语从句。第二句主干为 Workplace is still a source of living, but not of life-meaning，破折号后 - and certainly not a greenhouse... 是补充说明，其中 sufficiently solid and trustworthy enough to support and sustain... 是定语从句修饰 human bonds。

**关键词**：As long as they stay employed / bodily inside the workplace / spirits seldom follow the bodies / source of living / not of life-meaning / greenhouse of human bonds

**表达方式**：bodily 与 spirits 的对比把「身在曹营心在汉」写成结构性事实而非个人态度。greenhouse 一词把职场从「生产场所」重新定义为「人伦纽带的培育温室」，而 flexible 职场恰恰毁掉了这个温室。enough... enough 的双重 enough 强调 bonds 的脆弱性。

**为什么这样写**：这是对 Veblen「workmanship instinct」的反讽：work ethic 的精神基础不是抽象道德，而是稳定的共同生活。当 employment 变成 episodic，职业认同无法沉淀，work ethic 也就失去了精神土壤。作者把「生命意义」与「谋生」拆开，说明现代职场的悲剧不是失业，而是有业无意义——而消费市场正好提供替代意义的场所（shopping excellence）。

### ④ 游牧资本 vs 定居劳动

> **原句 4:** "Capital owners seeking workers are no longer bound to rely on local labour markets and so they see no reason why they should select their camping sites by any other criteria than maximization of profit and the profusion of undemanding, docile and trouble-free labour. People fit and eager to assume the roles of "routine labourers', to accept any job on offer and for any, even the most miserable wages, can be found everywhere."

**中文理解**：寻求工人的资本所有者不再需要依赖本地劳动市场，因此他们没有理由按除利润最大化与大量不挑剔、温顺、省心的劳动力之外的任何标准来选择营地。适合并渴望承担「例行劳动者」角色的人——愿意接受任何提供的工作、哪怕是最微薄的工资——到处都能找到。

**句子结构**：第一句主干为 Capital owners... are no longer bound to rely on local labour markets，and so 引导结果：they see no reason why they should select their camping sites by any other criteria than...，than 后接 maximization of profit and the profusion of... 作 criteria 的同位。第二句主干为 People fit and eager to assume the roles... can be found everywhere，中间 to accept any job on offer and for any, even the most miserable wages 是不定式短语作后置修饰。

**关键词**：no longer bound to rely on local labour markets / camping sites / maximization of profit / undemanding, docile and trouble-free labour / can be found everywhere

**表达方式**：camping sites 把资本的选址写成「扎营」——资本是游牧者，劳动是定居者，不对称由此具象化。undemanding, docile and trouble-free 三个形容词把理想劳动者定义为「无要求、温顺、省心」，而原文那句 People fit and eager to assume the roles of "routine labourers'…can be found everywhere（`…` 省去两个同位语）把本地劳动者的议价权彻底取消：你不可替换的前提是「只有你能做」，现在「到处都是」。

**为什么这样写**：这是上一章「资本全球流动而福利仍属地」的延伸：劳动伦理的「努力就有回报」需要资本对本地劳动的依赖，一旦资本可以全球移动，本地劳动者的努力就不再有稀缺性。camping sites 一词还暗示资本的临时性——它随时可以拔营，而劳动被绑在土地上，这就是「定居 vs 游牧」的权力不对称。

### ⑤ underclass 的意象：超越阶级、无角色、不可救赎

> **原句 5:** "The term "underclass' belongs to the imagery of a society which is not all-embracing and comprehensive, which is smaller than the sum of its parts. "Underclass' evokes an image of a class of people who are beyond classes and outside hierarchy, with neither chance nor need of readmission; people without role, making no useful contribution to the lives of the rest, and in principle beyond redemption."

**中文理解**：「underclass」这个词属于这样一种社会的意象——社会不是包罗万象、全面的整体，而是小于其各部分之和。underclass 唤起的是一类超越阶级、处于等级之外的人的形象：既没有被重新接纳的机会，也没有被重新接纳的需要；没有角色，对其他人生活不作有用贡献，原则上不可救赎。

**句子结构**：第一句主干为 The term "underclass' belongs to the imagery of a society，后接两个 which 定语从句：which is not all-embracing and comprehensive / which is smaller than the sum of its parts。第二句主干为 "Underclass' evokes an image of a class of people，后接 who 定语从句：who are beyond classes and outside hierarchy，with neither chance nor need of readmission 是伴随状语，people without role... 是同位语，making no useful contribution... 和 in principle beyond redemption 是现在分词/形容词短语作后置修饰。

**关键词**：imagery of a society / smaller than the sum of its parts / beyond classes and outside hierarchy / neither chance nor need of readmission / people without role / beyond redemption

**表达方式**：smaller than the sum of its parts 反转了「整体大于部分之和」的有机论，暗示社会已经碎裂。beyond classes and outside hierarchy 把 underclass 放在阶级分析的框架之外——它不是被剥削的阶级，而是被排除在阶级体系之外的人。neither chance nor need of readmission 的 neither... nor... 把「机会」与「需要」同时取消：不是你不能回来，而是社会也不需要你回来。

**为什么这样写**：作者在给 underclass 下定义，但这个定义本身就是批判：它揭示 underclass 概念 presupposes 一个「不完整」的社会。without role 一句把 underclass 的本质说成「无功能」——在功能主义社会学里，无功能就等于无合法性。beyond redemption 则把宗教/道德词汇 secularize，说明 underclass 概念借用了「不可救赎」的神学修辞。

### ⑥ 无用且危险：穷人作为花园里的杂草

> **原句 6:** "One trait that does mark them all is that others see no good reason for their existence and may imagine themselves to be much better off if they were not around. People get cast in the underclass because they are seen as totally useless - something the rest of us could do nicely without. They are, indeed, blots on an otherwise pretty landscape, ugly yet greedy weeds, which add nothing to the harmonious beauty of the garden but suck out a lot of plant feed. Everyone would gain if they vanished."

**中文理解**：他们身上确实有一个共同特质：别人都看不出他们存在的任何好处，甚至会想象若他们不在会好得多。人们把他们归入 underclass，是因为他们被看作完全无用——我们其他人可以很好地少了他们。他们确实是原本漂亮风景上的污点，丑陋却贪婪的杂草，对花园的和谐美毫无增益，却吸走大量养分。人人都会因他们的消失而获益。

**句子结构**：第一句主干为 One trait that does mark them all is that others see no good reason for their existence，that does mark them all 是定语从句修饰 One trait，that others see... 是表语从句。第二句主干为 People get cast in the underclass，because 引导原因状语从句。第三句主干为 They are blots... weeds，which 定语从句修饰 weeds。第四句是简单句 Everyone would gain if they vanished。

**关键词**：see no good reason for their existence / totally useless / blots on an otherwise pretty landscape / ugly yet greedy weeds / add nothing... but suck out / Everyone would gain if they vanished

**表达方式**：blots 与 weeds 两个意象把穷人从「人」降格为「景观瑕疵」与「植物害虫」。ugly yet greedy 的 yet 把「丑陋」与「贪婪」并置——他们不仅没用，还抢夺资源。Everyone would gain if they vanished 是赤裸裸的消除主义修辞，作者用它暴露 underclass 话语的暴力内核。

**为什么这样写**：作者在揭示 underclass 概念的情感基础：不是理性分析，而是厌恶与恐惧。blots/weeds 的修辞把穷人从「公民」变成「环境公害」，而 Everyone would gain 把集体谋杀说成集体利益。这一段是全章道德批判的高潮——作者要读者看见，underclass 话语的真正功能是把穷人从道德共同体中除名。

### ⑦ Myrdal 与 Time 杂志：同一个词的两种命运

> **原句 7:** "The word "underclass' was first used by Gunnar Myrdal in 1963, to signal the dangers of de-industrialization, which - as he feared - was likely to make growing chunks of the population permanently unemployed and unemployable; not because of deficiencies or moral faults in the people who found themselves out of work, but purely and simply because of the lack of employment for all those who needed it and desired it. This was not the result of the work ethic failing to inspire, but of society's failure to guarantee life according to the work ethic's precepts. Members of the underclass, in Myrdal's sense, were victims of exclusion. Their new status was not the outcome of opting-out, as the exclusion was the product of economic logic, over which those earmarked for exclusion had no control and no influence."

**中文理解**：「underclass」一词最早由 Gunnar Myrdal 于 1963 年使用，用以警示去工业化带来的危险——他所担心的是，去工业化很可能使越来越多的人口永久失业、无法就业；不是因为身陷失业的人有缺陷或道德过错，而纯粹是因为缺乏所有需要并渴望工作的就业机会。这不是劳动伦理未能激励的结果，而是社会未能按劳动伦理的戒律保障生活的结果。在 Myrdal 的意义上，underclass 成员是排斥的受害者。他们的新地位不是退出的结果，因为排斥是经济逻辑的产物，而被划为排斥对象的人对这种逻辑没有控制权、没有影响力。

**句子结构**：第一句主干为 The word "underclass' was first used by Gunnar Myrdal in 1963，to signal... 是不定式短语作目的状语，which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 de-industrialization，as he feared 是插入语，not because... but purely and simply because... 是原因状语从句的否定/肯定并列。第二句主干为 This was not the result of the work ethic failing to inspire, but of society's failure to guarantee life according to the work ethic's precepts。第三句主干为 Members of the underclass... were victims of exclusion。第四句主干为 Their new status was not the outcome of opting-out，as 引导原因状语从句。

**关键词**：first used by Gunnar Myrdal in 1963 / de-industrialization / not because of deficiencies or moral faults / lack of employment / victims of exclusion / product of economic logic

**表达方式**：作者用 not because... but purely and simply because... 的句式把 Myrdal 的版本钉死在「结构原因」上，把道德/心理解释排除在外。purely and simply 两个副词强调因果链的单一性——不是道德失败，而是就业不足。victims of exclusion 与 product of economic logic 两个短语把 underclass 从「个人命运」转为「结构产物」。

**为什么这样写**：这是全章最关键的历史对比：同一个词在 1963 年指「经济排除的受害者」，到 1977 年 Time 杂志却指「不可驯服、敌对、不可达」的人群。作者要读者看见语义滑坡——underclass 从结构分析概念滑向道德谴责概念，而这种滑坡服务于新自由主义政治：把贫困从「经济问题」转为「道德问题」，就可以把救济从「社会义务」转为「个人选择」。

### ⑧ Auletta 的修辞：主动作恶的是穷人

> **原句 8:** "Note as well that the underclass rejects common values, but it only feels excluded. The underclass is the active and acting, action-generating, initiative-taking side in a two-sided relationship that has "most Americans' as the other protagonist; it is the behaviour of the underclass, and of the underclass alone, that comes under critical scrutiny and is declared aberrant. On the other hand it is "most Americans' who, of right, sit in judgment, but it is the actions of the underclass that are judged. If not for its antisocial deeds, the underclass would not be brought to court. Most importantly, however, there would then be no need for the court's session, since there would be no case to ponder, no crime to punish or negligence to repair."

**中文理解**：还要注意，underclass 被说成拒绝共同价值，但它只是「感到」被排斥。underclass 是一段双方关系中主动的、行动的、产生行动与主动发起的一方，而另一方是「大多数美国人」；受到严格审查并被宣布为反常的，是 underclass 的行为，而且只有 underclass 的行为。另一方面，「大多数美国人」按权利坐在审判席上，但被审判的是 underclass 的行动。若没有其反社会行为，underclass 不会被带上法庭。而最重要的是，那时法庭也不需要开庭，因为没有案件可审议、没有罪行可惩罚、没有过失可修复。

**句子结构**：第一句主干为 the underclass rejects common values, but it only feels excluded，Note as well 是祈使引导。第二句主干为 The underclass is the active and acting, action-generating, initiative-taking side in a two-sided relationship，that 引导定语从句修饰 relationship。第三句主干为 it is the behaviour of the underclass... that comes under critical scrutiny...，it... that 是强调句。第四句主干为 it is "most Americans' who... sit in judgment, but it is the actions of the underclass that are judged，两个 it... that 强调句并列。第五句主干为 the underclass would not be brought to court，If 引导条件状语。第六句主干为 there would then be no need for the court's session，since 引导原因状语从句。

**关键词**：rejects common values, but it only feels excluded / active and acting, action-generating, initiative-taking / comes under critical scrutiny / sit in judgment / antisocial deeds / no need for the court's session

**表达方式**：作者用 it is... that 强调句把「谁主动、谁审判」的不对称拆开：underclass 是 the active and acting, action-generating, initiative-taking side，多数人则是 it is "most Americans" who, of right, sit in judgment 的一方。rejects common values, but it only feels excluded 的 but 把「拒绝」与「感到被排斥」并置——underclass 被描述为拒绝价值，但实际上它只是感到被排斥。court 的修辞把社会关系司法化：underclass 不是公民，而是被告。

**为什么这样写**：作者在做修辞学分析：underclass 话语通过语法把穷人写成主动作恶者，把多数人写成被动受害者/审判者。active and acting 的一连串形容词把 underclass 的能动性放大到荒谬程度，而 feels excluded 一句把真实处境（被排斥）降格为「感觉」。court 修辞的终点是：underclass 话语需要犯罪来维持法庭的存在——没有 underclass 的 antisocial deeds，社会的审判机器就没有用武之地。

### ⑨ Mead：贫困是选择无能

> **原句 9:** "The issue hinges on whether the needy can be responsible for themselves and, above all, on whether they have the competence to manage their lives ..." Whatever outward causes one cites, a mystery in the heart of nowork remains - the passivity of the seriously poor in seizing the opportunities that apparently exist for them ... To explain nowork, I see no avoiding some appeal to psychology or culture. Mostly, seriously poor adults appear to avoid work, not because of their economic situation, but because of what they believe ...2o In the absence of prohibitive barriers to employment, the question of the personality of the poor emerges as the key to understanding and overcoming poverty. Psychology is the last frontier in the search for the causes of low work effort ... Why do the poor not seize [the opportunities] as assiduously as the culture assumes they will? Who exactly are they?21 The core of the culture of poverty seems to be inability to control one's life - what psychologists call inefficacy.22"

**中文理解**：问题的关键在于穷人能否对自己负责，尤其是能否有能力管理自己的生活……无论引用什么外部原因，在不工作的核心里始终有一个谜——穷人在抓住显然存在的机会时表现出的被动……要解释不工作，我看无法避免诉诸心理或文化。大多数情况下，穷的成年人之所以逃避工作，不是因为经济状况，而是因为他们的信念……在没有禁止性就业障碍的情况下，穷人的个性问题就成了理解与克服贫困的关键。心理学是寻找低工作意愿原因的最后前沿……穷人为什么不那么勤勉地抓住机会，像文化所假设的那样？他们到底是谁？贫困文化的内核似乎是无法控制自己的生活——心理学家称之为 efficacy 缺失（inefficacy）。

**句子结构**：第一句主干为 The issue hinges on whether the needy can be responsible for themselves and... on whether they have the competence to manage their lives，hinges on 引导双宾语从句。第二句主干为 a mystery in the heart of nowork remains，破折号后 the passivity of the seriously poor in seizing the opportunities... 是同位语。第三句主干为 To explain nowork, I see no avoiding some appeal to psychology or culture。第四句主干为 seriously poor adults appear to avoid work，not because of... but because of... 是原因状语。第五句主干为 the question of the personality of the poor emerges as the key to understanding and overcoming poverty，In the absence of prohibitive barriers to employment 是条件状语。第六句主干为 Psychology is the last frontier in the search for the causes of low work effort。最后两句是问句与定义句。

**关键词**：seriously poor adults appear to avoid work / not because of their economic situation, but because of what they believe / In the absence of prohibitive barriers to employment / personality of the poor / Psychology is the last frontier / inefficacy

**表达方式**：Mead 的论证链是：先设问（能否对自己负责）→ 再把不工作解释为「谜」（passivity）→ 然后把解释诉诸 psychology/culture → 最后落脚于 inefficacy。作者引用时保留了 Mead 的问号与省略号，让读者看见论证的跳跃：从「机会存在」到「穷人不抓」之间缺了「障碍是否存在」的验证。inefficacy 一词把结构性排斥心理化。

**为什么这样写**：作者引用 Mead 是为了展示劳动伦理如何被重新武器化：Mead 把贫困从「没有机会」转为「没有能力抓住机会」，而这种转译的前提是「机会存在」——作者用 In the absence of prohibitive barriers 一句把 Mead 的前提暴露出来，暗示这个前提在现实中并不成立。psychology is the last frontier 一句把社会学/经济学解释逐出，让贫困变成个人心理问题，从而为「放弃救济」提供学术合法性。

### ⑩ adiaphorization：把穷人逐出道德义务宇宙

> **原句 10:** "This being no longer the case, the ostensibly unchanged message of the work ethic has entered a new kind of relationship with public morality. It is no longer an outlet for moral sentiments; instead, it has become a powerful instrument of the late twentieth century version of "adiaphorization' - the process whereby the ethical opprobrium is taken away from morally repugnant acts."

**中文理解**：既然这种情况已不复存在，劳动伦理表面上不变的信息已进入与公共道德的一种新关系。它不再是道德情感的出口；相反，它已成为二十世纪末版本「adiaphorization」（道德中立化）的强大工具——把道德非难从道德上令人厌恶的行为中拿掉的过程。

**句子结构**：第一句主干为 the ostensibly unchanged message of the work ethic has entered a new kind of relationship with public morality，This being no longer the case 是绝对结构作条件/原因状语。第二句主干为 It is no longer an outlet for moral sentiments，instead 引导转折：it has become a powerful instrument of... "adiaphorization' - the process whereby the ethical opprobrium is taken away from morally repugnant acts，其中 whereby 引导定语从句修饰 process。

**关键词**：ostensibly unchanged message / new kind of relationship with public morality / no longer an outlet for moral sentiments / powerful instrument / adiaphorization / ethical opprobrium is taken away

**表达方式**：ostensibly unchanged 把「表面不变、实质已变」写成修辞策略——劳动伦理的话术没变，但功能已翻转。outlet 与 instrument 的对比把劳动伦理从「道德情感的释放阀」转为「道德非难的移除工具」。adiaphorization 是本章的理论贡献：它把「道德中立化」从哲学概念转为社会学诊断——劳动伦理变成把穷人从道德义务宇宙中除名的许可证。

**为什么这样写**：这是全章的理论收束：劳动伦理的新功能是 adiaphorization——把穷人从「道德应被帮助的人」重新定义为「道德上不必被帮助的人」。ostensibly unchanged 一句解释了为什么公众不觉得劳动伦理有问题：话术没变，所以以为功能没变。instrument 一词把劳动伦理从「价值观」转为「工具」，与上一章「福利国家是过度决定的合流」形成对照——劳动伦理也成了一个过度决定的工具，只是这次服务于非穷人。

## 一句话总结

Bauman 在本章把劳动伦理从「工厂的招工广告」重新定义为「社会的道德卸责装置」。劳动市场柔性化把劳动者变成可随时替换的例行劳动者，资本的全球流动性取消了本地劳动的议价权，work ethic 于是失去物质基础；与此同时，underclass 概念把异质的穷人凝聚成一个「无用且危险」的集体他者，把贫困从经济问题转为道德/心理问题，把穷人从道德义务宇宙中除名。劳动伦理的新功能不再是激励工作，而是测试谁配得上道德 empathy——而穷人注定通不过这个测试。
