# 00. 情感节点（9 个）

## 节点一：一门不肯替人算命的社会科学（ch01 · 丛书主编序）

**叙事概括：**全书的入口不是论证，而是一个立场：社会科学不替社会规定未来，因为它做不到确定地预测人的行为；但它并不因此退场，社会科学家的理解与公民身份，恰恰是他们参与公共辩论的资格。这两句话的次序很关键——先交出先知的权杖，再接过发言的话筒。读到这里，读者已经知道接下来要读的不是预测，而是一次介入。

**关键引语：**

> "For this to happen it would mean that the social sciences would be able to predict human behaviour with certainty. This would require one theory and one method applicable to all times and places."（ch01）

> "Therefore, while it may not be the province of social scientists to predict our futures, they are, given not only their understandings but also their equal position as citizens, entitled to engage in public debates concerning future prospects."（ch01）

## 节点二：「新」字被写进书名的那一刻（ch02 · 第一版引言）

**叙事概括：**Bauman 用一句对仗把整本书的问题摆上台面：穷人永远与我们同在，但「穷」意味着什么，取决于他们与之同在的那个「我们」是哪一种。这句话读起来像格言，实际是一次历史切割——需要每个成年成员参加劳动的社会，与不再需要大规模劳动的社会，是两种「我们」；而 underclass 概念的流行，正好标记出社会已经不再把自己想象成整体。序言的语气是冷的，但读者能听见那个「新」字落地的声音。

**关键引语：**

> "The poor will be always with us, but what it means to be poor depends on the kind of "us' they are "with'. It is not the same to be poor in a society which needs every single adult member to engage in productive labour as it is to be poor in a society which, thanks to the enormous powers accumulated by centuries of labour, may well produce everything needed without the participation of a large and growing section of its members."（ch02）

> "The recently fashionable concept of the "underclass' is scrutinized and found to act mainly as a tool of the "power-assisted' condensation of widely different forms and causes of deprivation into the image of one inferior category of people afflicted with faults common to them all and therefore presenting one "social problem'."（ch02）

## 节点三：戒律底下是一场权力斗争（ch03 · 书内第一章）

**叙事概括：**这一章最令人不适的地方，是它把一件被当作道德传统的东西还原成技术：为了让习惯了自主设定目标的人接受他人设定的任务，需要一条戒律，也需要一套让不服从者显得可耻的比较级——让贫民的处境足够悲惨，使最微薄的工资也显得诱人。Bauman 的句子毫不让步：这场十字军是一场争夺控制与从属的战斗，是一场除了名字之外彻头彻尾的权力斗争。不适感来自认出：那套语言今天仍在被使用。

**关键引语：**

> "For all intents and purposes, the work-ethic crusade was a battle for control and subordination. It was a power struggle in everything but name, a battle to force the working people to accept, in the name of the ethical nobility of working life, a life neither noble nor responding to their own standards of moral decency."（ch03）

> "It was hoped that the more the life of the non-working poor were degraded and the deeper they descended into destitution, the more tempting or at least the less unendurable would appear to them the lot of those working poor who had sold their labour in exchange for the most miserable of wages; and so the cause of the work ethic would be helped and its triumph brought nearer."（ch03）

## 节点四：诱惑坐上了规范的位置（ch04 · 书内第二章）

**叙事概括：**第二章的转折有一种冷静的漂亮：社会整合照旧发生，只是完成它的东西换了。市场不能满足于有购买力的人，它需要愿意并且乐于被诱惑的人；储蓄与等待被重新定义为对选择权的推迟，而这恰恰不是为多数人准备的选项。等到那句「在劳动伦理曾经统治的地方，如今统治的是消费美学」落下时，读者会意识到，穷人的失败已经被重新定义了——不再是道德上的不服从，而是审美与能力上的不合格。

**关键引语：**

> "It is often said that the consumer market seduces its customers. But in order to do so it needs customers who are ready and keen to be seduced (just as, in order to command his labourers, the factory boss needed a crew with the habits of discipline and command-following firmly entrenched). In a properly working consumer society consumers seek actively to be seduced. They live from attraction to attraction, from temptation to temptation, from swallowing one bait to fishing for another, each new attraction, temptation and bait being somewhat different and perhaps stronger than those that preceded them; just as their ancestors, the producers, lived from one turn of the conveyer belt to an identical next."（ch04）

> "A consumer society is a society of credit cards, not savings books. It is a "now' society. A wanting society, not a waiting society."（ch04）

## 节点五：一种「不值这个钱」的正义（ch05 · 书内第三章）

**叙事概括：**福利国家的兴衰在这里被写成价值排序的胜负：它的纯粹原则是按需平等压倒按支付能力的不平等，而消费者社会唯一不容置疑的价值是选择。于是支持福利国家的理由不是被反驳掉的，而是被一种「不值这个钱」的直觉顶替掉的。这一章最刺人的地方是它指出：给穷人的服务从来就是差的服务——所以问题不只在预算，也在谁值得被服务。

**关键引语：**

> "The concept of the "welfare state' conveys the idea that it is the duty and the obligation of the state to guarantee the "welfare' (that is, something more than sheer survival: survival with dignity, as understood in a given society at a given time) of all its subjects."（ch05）

> "services for the poor were always poor services"（ch05）

## 节点六：一个由社会制造、却拒绝承认的范畴（ch06 · 书内第四章）

**叙事概括：**「underclass」一词在这里被完整解剖：它一九六三年由 Myrdal 用来警示去工业化的危险，后来却被改写成一个人格低劣的形象；它被说成拒绝共同价值，但它其实只是「感到」被排斥——它是一个双边关系里被写成主动方的弱者。这一节的情感质地是冷怒：概念不是无辜的，它替社会完成了把结构性成因变成个人缺陷的工作。

**关键引语：**

> "The word "underclass' was first used by Gunnar Myrdal in 1963, to signal the dangers of de-industrialization, which - as he feared - was likely to make growing chunks of the population permanently unemployed and unemployable; not because of deficiencies or moral faults in the people who found themselves out of work, but purely and simply because of the lack of employment for all those who needed it and desired it. This was not the result of the work ethic failing to inspire, but of society's failure to guarantee life according to the work ethic's precepts. Members of the underclass, in Myrdal's sense, were victims of exclusion. Their new status was not the outcome of opting-out, as the exclusion was the product of economic logic, over which those earmarked for exclusion had no control and no influence."（ch06）

> "Note as well that the underclass rejects common values, but it only feels excluded. The underclass is the active and acting, action-generating, initiative-taking side in a two-sided relationship that has "most Americans' as the other protagonist; it is the behaviour of the underclass, and of the underclass alone, that comes under critical scrutiny and is declared aberrant. On the other hand it is "most Americans' who, of right, sit in judgment, but it is the actions of the underclass that are judged. If not for its antisocial deeds, the underclass would not be brought to court. Most importantly, however, there would then be no need for the court's session, since there would be no case to ponder, no crime to punish or negligence to repair."（ch06）

## 节点七：地球满了，倾倒场没有了（ch07 · 书内第五章）

**叙事概括：**这一章把镜头拉到空间，也把全书的悲观推到最高点：现代性曾经靠殖民地把本国的冗余人口和废弃物送到「无主之地」，如今「地球满了」，外部消失，废弃物只能留在原地。全球化为「被浪费的人」添上第三个来源，而各国政府面临的最紧迫问题不再是救济，而是发明一条新的合法性公式，让权威与纪律还能站得住。读到监狱从「回收」转向「废弃物处置」，读者会明白这已不是比喻。

**关键引语：**

> "As if to make the already troublesome state of affairs even more complex and threatening, a new powerful source of "wasted humans' has been added to the original two. Globalization has become the third (alongside the order-building and economic as well as technological progress), and currently the most prolific and least controlled, "production line' of human waste or wasted humans. It also puts a new gloss on the old problem and imbues it with an altogether new significance and an unprecedented urgency."（ch07）

> "A most urgent imperative and one which every government presiding over the dismantling and demise of the social state faces is therefore the task of finding or inventing a new "legitimation formula' on which the selfassertion of state authority and the demand of discipline may rest instead."（ch07）

## 节点八：带着罪责被排除的人（ch08 · 书内第六章）

**叙事概括：**收束章先回到最抽象的一层，也给出了全书最冷的句子之一：秩序的梦想是把人类世界变成无机世界，而对秩序的渴望同时就是对死亡的渴望。由此而来的两种排除方式——「破坏秩序」与「达不到规范」——在同一个地方合流：被排除者被预先判定为自身被排除的罪人。工业时代给穷人安排的角色（劳动力后备军）也是这样生效的，工作伦理则是盖在难吃的蛋糕上的那层糖霜。

**关键引语：**

> "a desire to turn the human world into an inorganic one, where everything would function perfectly and work on schedule, subordinated to a suprapersonal system. The longing for order is at the same time a longing for death, because life is an incessant disruption of order. Or to put it the other way around: the desire for order is a virtuous pretext, an excuse for violent misanthropy."（ch08）

> "And yet the work ethic was viewed almost universally as a useful, perhaps indispensable medicine for the triple ailment of poverty, insufficient supply of labour and the threat of revolution. It was meant and hoped to be a sort of icing hiding from view the unappetising quality of the cake on offer. Promoting drudgery to the noble rank of moral duty would perhaps sweeten the tempers of those exposed to it while at the same time catering to the moral consciences of those who exposed them."（ch08）

## 节点九：独自舔伤的人，与一条尚未落地的出路（ch08 · 书内第六章）

**叙事概括：**全书的最后一段路是从诊断走向出路，但走得很克制：苦难并没有减少，只是被私有化了——每个不合格的消费者独自承受，因此今天的受苦汇不成一项共同事业，也就没有集体行动的政治出口。于是出路只能来自制度设计：把个人收入权利与实际挣钱能力脱钩。Bauman 把这个方案交给读者时并不掩饰它的难度——它要求的不是政策微调，而是一次视角的转变。

**关键引语：**

> "These days the sufferings of the poor do not add up to a common cause."（ch08）

> "The hub of that solution is "the idea that individual income entitlement can be decoupled from actual income-earning capacity'. It can, though admittedly on condition of no lesser a feat than a change of perspective from one centred on wage labour, as dictated by the work ethic, to the assumption of basic entitlement and basic guarantee, dictated by the status and dignity of being human. Decoupling is given concrete form by the principle of financing social security through taxation, through doing away with means-testing and assessment of willingness to work, by the gradual replacement of the principle of equivalence by that of need, and finally through the principle of the individual as the basis for entitlement."（ch08）
