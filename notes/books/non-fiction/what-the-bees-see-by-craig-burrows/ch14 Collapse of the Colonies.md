# 14. Collapse of the Colonies（蜂群崩溃）

## 概览

- **出处**：*What the Bees See: A Honeybee's Eye View of the World* by Craig P. Burrows
- **章节定位**：Part One "The Gift of Life" 第十章
- **作者**：Craig P. Burrows / 含多位专家（David Pattemore / Brian McCall）
- **字符数**：8,562
- **一句话主旨**：分析蜜蜂种群衰退的四大环境威胁——气候变化（物候错配）、城市化（栖息地丧失）、农药（新烟碱类/草甘膦）、土壤退化（单一耕作）——以及蜂群崩溃综合症（CCD）的成因；指出人类是问题的根源，也是解决方案的来源。

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：蜜蜂种群衰退是多重环境压力共同作用的结果——气候变化、城市化、农药和土壤退化；这些威胁大多由人类活动引起，因此人类有责任也有能力解决。
- **证据链**：

| 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |
|------|------|----------|
| 气候变化影响昆虫生理和物候 | 生态学事实 | 气候威胁 |
| 城市化导致栖息地丧失 | 社会学事实 | 栖息地威胁 |
| 空气污染（印度研究）降低蜜蜂觅食效率 | 研究数据 | 污染威胁 |
| 新烟碱类农药在野生花卉中仍可检测到 | 化学监测 | 农药威胁 |
| 草甘膦表面活性剂致蜜蜂死亡 | 毒理学事实 | 农药威胁 |
| 蜂群崩溃综合症（CCD）与多种压力相关 | 综合性解释 | 系统性危机 |

- **论证脉络**：引入（"我们都是环境的产物"）→ 气候变化（物候错配）→ 城市化（栖息地丧失）→ 空气污染（印度研究）→ 农药（新烟碱类/草甘膦）→ 土壤退化（单一耕作）→ 蜂群崩溃综合症 → 落点（"问题是我们的，解决方案也是我们的"）
- **可质疑处**：
  1. 全文将 CCD 主要归因于农药，但 CCD 的成因仍在科学界争论中——可能还包括病原体、寄生虫、营养压力等多重因素。
  2. "80% of bees from highly polluted areas died"——此研究样本量和实验设计未详细说明，可能无法代表全球情况。
  3. 全文未讨论商业化授粉（如美国杏仁产业）对蜜蜂健康的跨国影响——长途运输蜂群的压力。

## 选择性精读

① "You've probably heard the saying, 'We're all products of our environment'. And just as much as that applies to humans, it also applies to honeybees."

**中文理解**：你可能听过这句话："我们都是环境的产物"。正如这句话适用于人类一样，它也适用于蜜蜂。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "You've probably heard the saying, 'We're all products of our environment'" + 分2 "And just as much as that applies to humans, it also applies to honeybees"。

**关键词**：products of our environment（环境的产物）、applies to（适用于）

**表达方式**：用"We're all products of our environment"这句俗语开篇——将人类与蜜蜂并列，制造"同病相怜"的共鸣。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"平等视角"——读者被告知"你和蜜蜂一样受环境影响"，打破"人类例外论"。

---

② "The most obvious change we are seeing in the environment is the changing climate, which has the potential to affect a honeybee's environment in the most large-scale way, just as it will impact every organism on earth."

**中文理解**：我们在环境中看到的最明显的变化是气候变化，它有潜力以最大规模的方式影响蜜蜂的环境，正如它将影响地球上的每个生物一样。

**句子结构**：复合句。主句 "The most obvious change we are seeing in the environment is the changing climate" + 定语从句 "which has the potential to affect a honeybee's environment in the most large-scale way" + 方式状语从句 "just as it will impact every organism on earth"。

**关键词**：obvious（明显的）、large-scale（大规模）、impact（影响）

**表达方式**：用"most large-scale way"（最大规模的方式）暗示气候变化的"全面性"——不是局部问题，而是全球性威胁。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"威胁排序"——将气候变化列为"最明显"的威胁，为后续"但人类直接威胁更大"蓄力。

---

③ "Insects are ectotherms, which means that they're reliant on the environment for their body heat. One of the concerns about climate change is we don't know what the impacts will be on the physiology, behaviour and breeding of insects as average temperatures increase, or even as we get more extreme events, whether it's heatwaves or cold snaps."

**中文理解**：昆虫是变温动物，这意味着它们依赖环境获取体温。对气候变化的担忧之一是，随着平均温度升高，甚至随着我们遭遇更多极端事件（无论是热浪还是寒流），我们不知道会对昆虫的生理、行为和繁殖产生什么影响。

**句子结构**：复合句。分1 "Insects are ectotherms, which means that they're reliant on the environment for their body heat" + 分2 "One of the concerns about climate change is we don't know what the impacts will be on the physiology, behaviour and breeding of insects as average temperatures increase, or even as we get more extreme events, whether it's heatwaves or cold snaps"。

**关键词**：ectotherms（变温动物）、reliant on（依赖）、physiology（生理）、heatwaves（热浪）、cold snaps（寒流）

**表达方式**：用"we don't know"（我们不知道）——承认科学的不确定性，反而增强了"应该谨慎"的论证。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"科学诚实"——读者开始理解气候变化对昆虫的影响不仅是"已知的"，还有"未知的"。

---

④ "The timing of flowering for most plants is governed by day length, but the timing of emergence for insects from the ground or when they hatch out is driven by temperature. For most of the last several millennia, there's been this nice relationship and ability for those insects and the plants to evolve alongside each other, but if the temperature is increasing, we know the day length isn't increasing. So there's a potential for this mismatch in the timing of when the pollinators emerge and the flowers are ready for pollination."

**中文理解**：大多数植物的开花时间由日照长度决定，但昆虫从地下羽化或孵化出来则由温度驱动。在过去大部分几千年里，这些昆虫和植物之间一直存在这种良好的关系和共同进化的能力，但如果温度在上升，我们知道日照长度并没有增加。因此，在传粉者羽化和花朵准备好授粉的时间上，存在这种错位的可能性。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "The timing of flowering for most plants is governed by day length, but the timing of emergence for insects from the ground or when they hatch out is driven by temperature" + 分2 "For most of the last several millennia, there's been this nice relationship and ability for those insects and the plants to evolve alongside each other" + 分3 "but if the temperature is increasing, we know the day length isn't increasing" + 分4 "So there's a potential for this mismatch in the timing of when the pollinators emerge and the flowers are ready for pollination"。

**关键词**：governed by（由...决定）、driven by（由...驱动）、millennia（千年）、mismatch（错配）

**表达方式**：用"nice relationship"（良好的关系）——拟人化描述植物与昆虫的协同进化，暗示气候变化正在"破坏这段关系"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"物候学"的句子——读者开始理解气候变化的"隐性"影响：不是直接杀死蜜蜂，而是"错过"授粉时间。

---

⑤ "Every city represents swathes of land that is no longer covered in plants for bees to forage from. Cities and their surrounds are inhospitable to insects like bees, looking for food, resources and shelter."

**中文理解**：每一座城市都代表着大片不再覆盖蜜蜂可觅食植物的土地。城市及其周边对蜜蜂等昆虫来说是不适宜居住的，它们在那里寻找食物、资源和庇护所。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Every city represents swathes of land that is no longer covered in plants for bees to forage from" + 分2 "Cities and their surrounds are inhospitable to insects like bees, looking for food, resources and shelter"。

**关键词**：swathes of land（大片土地）、inhospitable（不适宜居住的）、shelter（庇护所）

**表达方式**：用"inhospitable"（不适宜居住的）——将城市描述为"沙漠"，暗示城市化是"生态灭绝"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"城市化批判"——读者开始理解"城市扩张"不只是"人类进步"，还是"生态丧失"。

---

⑥ "In 2020, an Indian study showed that giant honeybees (Apis dorsata) were impaired by particulate air pollution. Bees were sent out to forage in four areas of Bangalore, designated as having rural, low, moderate and high levels of particulate air pollution. The foraging of bees in the least-polluted area amounted to double the foraging of the bees in the most-polluted area."

**中文理解**：2020 年，一项印度研究表明大蜜蜂（Apis dorsata）受到颗粒空气污染的损害。蜜蜂被派往班加罗尔四个区域觅食，这些区域被指定为具有农村、低、中和高水平的颗粒污染。在污染最轻的区域，蜜蜂的觅食量是污染最重区域的两倍。

**句子结构**：复合句。分1 "In 2020, an Indian study showed that giant honeybees (Apis dorsata) were impaired by particulate air pollution" + 分2 "Bees were sent out to forage in four areas of Bangalore, designated as having rural, low, moderate and high levels of particulate air pollution" + 分3 "The foraging of bees in the least-polluted area amounted to double the foraging of the bees in the most-polluted area"。

**关键词**：impaired（受损）、particulate air pollution（颗粒空气污染）、designated（被指定）、amounted to double（是...的两倍）

**表达方式**：用"amounted to double"（是...的两倍）——将抽象的"污染损害"转化为具体的数字对比，制造冲击。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"实证"的句子——读者开始理解空气污染不只是"对人类有害"，还"对蜜蜂有害"。

---

⑦ "Foraging is more difficult for bees in areas with high levels of air pollution. The pollution masks the scent molecules sent out by plants that bees require to find them, making foraging harder and slower. This in turn impacts rates of pollination, as bees are less effective at it."

**中文理解**：在空气污染严重的地区，蜜蜂觅食更加困难。污染掩盖了植物释放的蜜蜂用来寻找它们的气味分子，使觅食更加困难和缓慢。这反过来影响了授粉率，因为蜜蜂在这方面效率较低。

**句子结构**：复合句。分1 "Foraging is more difficult for bees in areas with high levels of air pollution" + 分2 "The pollution masks the scent molecules sent out by plants that bees require to find them, making foraging harder and slower" + 分3 "This in turn impacts rates of pollination, as bees are less effective at it"。

**关键词**：masks（掩盖）、scent molecules（气味分子）、in turn（反过来）

**表达方式**：用"masks"（掩盖）——将污染描述为"窃贼"，偷走了花朵的"信号"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"机制解释"——读者理解空气污染如何"隐形"地损害蜜蜂：不是直接毒死，而是让它们"找不到花"。

---

⑧ "Moreover, when the bees in the study were brought back to the lab, 80 per cent of those who had foraged in the highly and moderately polluted areas had died, in comparison to only 20 per cent of those who had foraged in the low-polluted and rural areas."

**中文理解**：此外，当研究中的蜜蜂被带回实验室时，在高度和中度污染区域觅食的蜜蜂中有80%已经死亡，而在低度和农村区域觅食的蜜蜂中仅有20%死亡。

**句子结构**：复合句。主句 "80 per cent of those who had foraged in the highly and moderately polluted areas had died" + 比较状语 "in comparison to only 20 per cent of those who had foraged in the low-polluted and rural areas"。

**关键词**：moreover（此外）、in comparison to（与...相比）

**表达方式**：用"80% vs 20%"——数字对比触目惊心，读者被"数据震撼"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"数据冲击"的句子——读者意识到空气污染的"代价"是"蜜蜂生命"。

---

⑨ "Industrial farming also means pesticides are another troublesome form of pollutant for honeybees. Pesticides that are frequently used include insecticides, to eliminate pest insects; and herbicides, to eliminate weeds. Pesticides can affect the air, soil and water around honeybees."

**中文理解**：工业化农业也意味着农药是蜜蜂的另一种麻烦的污染物形式。频繁使用的农药包括杀虫剂（用于消灭害虫）和除草剂（用于消灭杂草）。农药可以影响蜜蜂周围的空气、土壤和水。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Industrial farming also means pesticides are another troublesome form of pollutant for honeybees" + 分2 "Pesticides that are frequently used include insecticides, to eliminate pest insects; and herbicides, to eliminate weeds" + 分3 "Pesticides can affect the air, soil and water around honeybees"。

**关键词**：troublesome（麻烦的）、insecticides（杀虫剂）、herbicides（除草剂）

**表达方式**：用"another troublesome form of pollutant"（另一种麻烦的污染物形式）——暗示污染"源源不断"，一种接一种。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"威胁清单"——读者开始理解蜜蜂面临的"化学轰炸"不是单一物质，而是"多种农药的混合物"。

---

⑩ "There are bees that are on the verge of extinction, if not already becoming extinct. And that's because pesticides have taken over, they've lost their environment, same as any other animal."

**中文理解**：有些蜜蜂正处于灭绝的边缘，如果不是已经灭绝的话。这是因为农药已经接管了一切，它们失去了自己的环境，就像任何其他动物一样。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "There are bees that are on the verge of extinction, if not already becoming extinct" + 分2 "And that's because pesticides have taken over, they've lost their environment, same as any other animal"。

**关键词**：on the verge of extinction（处于灭绝边缘）、pesticides have taken over（农药已经接管了一切）

**表达方式**：用"pesticides have taken over"（农药已经接管了一切）——将农药描述为"侵略者"，制造紧迫感。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"道德呼吁"的句子——读者被告知"蜜蜂正在灭绝"，而原因是"人类活动"。

## 一句话总结

**蜜蜂种群衰退是多重环境压力共同作用的结果：气候变化导致物候错配，城市化剥夺栖息地，农药直接毒害，土壤退化削弱植物——而这一切的根源都是人类活动；正如 Brian McCall 所说，"农药接管了一切，它们失去了自己的环境，就像任何其他动物一样"。**
