# 21. What This Means For You and Me（这对你我和意味着什么）

## 概览

- **出处**：*What the Bees See: A Honeybee's Eye View of the World* by Craig P. Burrows
- **章节定位**：Part Three "The Pursuit of Immortality" 第一章
- **作者**：Craig P. Burrows / 含 Megan Bradley 专家解释
- **字符数**：8,804
- **一句话主旨**：详细介绍蜜蜂产品的现代应用——蜂蜜（烹饪/护肤）、蜂胶（免疫/护肤）、蜂蜡（蜡烛/食品包装）、蜂毒（护肤品）、花粉（保健品）、蜂王浆（营养补充剂/护肤品）；讨论"可持续美容"趋势和"生物基成分"的兴起。

## 选择性精读

① "Humans have been using honeybee products for literally thousands of years (see page 106), and today our obsession continues, with golden goodness and other products used in the kitchen, the beauty cabinet, the doctor's office and beyond."

**中文理解**：人类使用蜜蜂产品已有 literally（确实地）数千年（见第 106 页），今天我们的 obsession（痴迷）仍在继续——金色精华和其他产品被用于厨房、美容柜、医生办公室以及更远的地方。

**句子结构**：简单句。主句 "Humans have been using honeybee products for literally thousands of years (see page 106), and today our obsession continues, with golden goodness and other products used in the kitchen, the beauty cabinet, the doctor's office and beyond"。

**关键词**：literally（确实地）、obsession（痴迷）、golden goodness（金色精华）、beyond（更远的地方）

**表达方式**：用"obsession"（痴迷）——暗示人类对蜜蜂产品的"狂热"是"非理性的"，但也是"持久的"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"时间跨度"——从"数千年前"到"今天"，读者被告知"这不是新发现，而是持续的传统"。

---

② "Honey has been used in beauty routines for thousands of years. Ancient Egyptians used it to create make-up, and Queen Cleopatra is said to have regularly bathed in honey and milk to keep her skin smooth and firm."

**中文理解**：蜂蜜已被用于美容程序数千年。古埃及人用它制造化妆品，据说克利奥帕特拉女王经常用蜂蜜和牛奶沐浴以保持皮肤光滑紧致。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Honey has been used in beauty routines for thousands of years" + 分2 "Ancient Egyptians used it to create make-up" + 分3 "Queen Cleopatra is said to have regularly bathed in honey and milk to keep her skin smooth and firm"。

**关键词**：beauty routines（美容程序）、make-up（化妆品）、bathed in（沐浴在）、smooth and firm（光滑紧致）

**表达方式**：用"Queen Cleopatra"（克利奥帕特拉女王）——将蜂蜜美容与"最著名的美女"关联，制造"古老智慧=有效"的印象。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"名人效应"的句子——读者开始理解"蜂蜜美容"不只是"民间传说"，还是"王室秘方"。

---

③ "People want natural ingredients; they want to know where things come from, they want to know brands care about the planet. That's become important to consumers, and the beauty industry has traditionally not had a great track record with sustainability and traceability."

**中文理解**：人们想要天然成分；他们想知道东西从哪里来，他们想知道品牌关心地球。这已经变得对消费者很重要，而美容行业在可持续性和可追溯性方面 traditionally（传统上）没有良好的 track record（记录）。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "People want natural ingredients; they want to know where things come from, they want to know brands care about the planet" + 分2 "That's become important to consumers, and the beauty industry has traditionally not had a great track record with sustainability and traceability"。

**关键词**：natural ingredients（天然成分）、sustainability（可持续性）、traceability（可追溯性）、track record（记录）

**表达方式**：用"track record"（记录）——暗示美容行业的"历史"是"不可持续的"，为"生物基成分"的兴起提供"反面对比"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"市场"的句子——读者开始理解"蜜蜂产品"不只是"传统"，还是"未来趋势"。

---

④ "Free radicals can come from pollution in the air, and anything that's in the air or water is naturally on our skin as well, because we bathe in water, we wash our hands, we go outside. As women age, levels of oestrogen and progesterone, the hormones that tell the brain and the body to produce collagen, drop."

**中文理解**：自由基可以来自空气中的污染，而空气或水中的任何东西也 naturally（自然地）在我们的皮肤上，因为我们沐浴在水中，我们洗手，我们外出。随着年龄增长，女性 oestrogen（雌激素）和 progesterone（孕激素）——告诉大脑和身体产生胶原蛋白的激素——水平下降。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Free radicals can come from pollution in the air, and anything that's in the air or water is naturally on our skin as well, because we bathe in water, we wash our hands, we go outside" + 分2 "As women age, levels of oestrogen and progesterone, the hormones that tell the brain and the body to produce collagen, drop"。

**关键词**：free radicals（自由基）、collagen（胶原蛋白）、oestrogen（雌激素）、progesterone（孕激素）

**表达方式**：用"As women age"（随着年龄增长）——直接与女性读者对话，制造"这与你有关"的紧迫感。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"科学"的句子——读者开始理解"衰老"的生化机制，为"蜂蜜如何抗衰"做铺垫。

---

⑤ "Losing collagen in the skin can lead to dark spots, fine lines and wrinkles. This is where honey, with its free radical-fighting antioxidants, shines."

**中文理解**：皮肤中胶原蛋白的流失会导致黑斑、细纹和皱纹。这就是蜂蜜凭借其抗氧化剂（对抗自由基）shines（闪耀/发挥作用）的地方。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Losing collagen in the skin can lead to dark spots, fine lines and wrinkles" + 分2 "This is where honey, with its free radical-fighting antioxidants, shines"。

**关键词**：dark spots（黑斑）、fine lines（细纹）、wrinkles（皱纹）、antioxidants（抗氧化剂）、shines（闪耀/发挥作用）

**表达方式**：用"shines"（闪耀/发挥作用）——双关语，既指蜂蜜"闪耀"（字面意义），又指蜂蜜"发挥作用"（引申意义）。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"广告"的句子——读者几乎可以在任何蜂蜜护肤品广告中看到类似表述。

---

⑥ "It's a good exfoliant. It has natural enzymes and AHAs (alpha-hydroxy acids), a class of acids that you put on your skin to exfoliate away dead skin cells."

**中文理解**：它是一种好的 exfoliant（去角质剂）。它含有天然酶和 AHA（α-羟基酸），一类你涂在皮肤上去除死皮细胞的酸。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "It's a good exfoliant" + 分2 "It has natural enzymes and AHAs (alpha-hydroxy acids), a class of acids that you put on your skin to exfoliate away dead skin cells"。

**关键词**：exfoliant（去角质剂）、AHAs（α-羟基酸）、dead skin cells（死皮细胞）

**表达方式**：用"AHAs (alpha-hydroxy acids)"——括号内解释化学术语，让不熟悉化学成分的读者也能理解。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"化学"的句子——读者开始理解蜂蜜的"美容机制"不只是"传统"，还有"科学依据"。

---

⑦ "Propolis is believed to support immune health, reduce inflammation and aid oral hygiene. This could be due to the fact that bees also use propolis as a disinfectant of sorts."

**中文理解**：蜂胶被认为能支持免疫健康、减少炎症和 aid oral hygiene（辅助口腔卫生）。这可能是因为蜜蜂也将蜂胶用作一种消毒剂。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Propolis is believed to support immune health, reduce inflammation and aid oral hygiene" + 分2 "This could be due to the fact that bees also use propolis as a disinfectant of sorts"。

**关键词**：support immune health（支持免疫健康）、reduce inflammation（减少炎症）、oral hygiene（口腔卫生）、disinfectant（消毒剂）

**表达方式**：用"support"（支持）而非"boost"（增强）——科学写作中的谨慎用词，暗示"证据还在积累中"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"免疫"引子——读者开始理解蜂胶不只是"蜂巢的胶"，还是"免疫系统支持者"。

---

⑧ "When intruders such as mice and lizards breach the hive, the bees work together to destroy them. Because they're too large to be removed, they instead embalm them in a thick layer of propolis to disinfect the carcass and protect the colony from infection."

**中文理解**：当老鼠和蜥蜴等入侵者 breach（突破）蜂巢时，蜜蜂会合力摧毁它们。因为它们太大而无法移出，蜜蜂反而用一层厚厚的蜂胶 embalm（防腐处理）它们，以消毒尸体并保护蜂群免受感染。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "When intruders such as mice and lizards breach the hive, the bees work together to destroy them" + 分2 "Because they're too large to be removed, they instead embalm them in a thick layer of propolis to disinfect the carcass and protect the colony from infection"。

**关键词**：breach（突破）、embalm（防腐处理）、disinfect（消毒）、carcass（尸体）

**表达方式**：用"embalm"（防腐处理）——将蜜蜂描述为"制作木乃伊的工匠"，暗示蜂胶的"防腐"能力是"自然界的设计"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"画面感"的句子——读者几乎能"看到"蜜蜂用蜂胶包裹老鼠的场景，制造"蜂胶=自然防腐剂"的深刻印象。

---

⑨ "Honey acts as an anti-aging warrior by fighting free radicals, which break down the collagen in our skin, Megan explains."

**中文理解**：事实证明狗可以检测 AFB 孢子。我们还不能 practically（实际地）使用这个信息，但总有一天我们可能让狗跑过仓库，找出含有 AFB 蜂蜜的继箱，并隔离这些继箱，以免它们被放到蜂群上。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "It turns out dogs can detect AFB spores" + 分2 "We can't use this [information] practically yet, but at some point we might be able to run dogs through storage sheds to find honey supers that have AFB spores in them, and isolate the supers so that they are not put onto colonies"。

**关键词**：detect（检测）、storage sheds（仓库）、honey supers（继箱）、isolate（隔离）

**表达方式**：用"It turns out"（事实证明）——口语化表达，制造"意外发现"的效果。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"实用"的句子——读者开始理解"蜜蜂科学"不只是"理论研究"，还有"实际应用"。

---

⑩ "Believed to promote vitality in ancient times (see page 106), royal jelly — that milky secretion from the hypopharyngeal glands of worker bees — is today found in a variety of nutritional supplements. Rich in protein and vitamins, it's been linked to immunity, improved cholesterol levels and even fertility, with human studies showing it can improve sperm count and movement."

**中文理解**：在古代被认为能促进活力（见第 106 页），蜂王浆——工蜂咽下腺的乳白色分泌物——今天在多种营养补充剂中都能找到。富含蛋白质和维生素，它与免疫、胆固醇水平改善甚至 fertility（生育力）有关联，人体研究表明它能提高精子数量和活力。

**句子结构**：复合句。主句 "Believed to promote vitality in ancient times (see page 106), royal jelly — that milky secretion from the hypopharyngeal glands of worker bees — is today found in a variety of nutritional supplements" + 补充 "Rich in protein and vitamins, it's been linked to immunity, improved cholesterol levels and even fertility, with human studies showing it can improve sperm count and movement"。

**关键词**：promote vitality（促进活力）、nutritional supplements（营养补充剂）、fertility（生育力）、sperm count（精子数量）

**表达方式**：用"has been linked to"（与...有关联）——科学写作中的谨慎措辞，暗示"证据还在积累中"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"现代蜂王浆"总结——读者开始理解蜂王浆如何从"古代秘方"变成"现代保健品"。

## 一句话总结
**蜂蜜、蜂胶、蜂蜡、蜂毒、花粉、蜂王浆——蜜蜂的"六宝"在现代找到了新用途：蜂蜜是"天然甜味剂+抗衰老英雄"，蜂胶是"免疫支持者+护肤成分"，蜂蜡是"可持续包装材料"，蜂毒是"天然 Botox"，花粉是"超级食物"，蜂王浆是"长寿秘方"——蜜蜂创造的系统，正在被人类"重新发现"。**
