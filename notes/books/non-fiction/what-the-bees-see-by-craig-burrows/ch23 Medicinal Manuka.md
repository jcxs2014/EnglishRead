# 23. Medicinal Mānuka（药用麦卢卡）

## 概览

- **出处**：*What the Bees See: A Honeybee's Eye View of the World* by Craig P. Burrows
- **章节定位**：Part Three "The Pursuit of Immortality" 第三章
- **作者**：Craig P. Burrows / 含多位专家（Heike Krüger / Jonathan Cox）
- **字符数**：6,045
- **一句话主旨**：介绍麦卢卡蜂蜜的医学应用——从古埃及/古希腊的伤口敷料到现代的糖尿病伤口治疗、抗超级细菌（MRSA）、呼吸道感染；讨论其他具有医疗潜力的蜂蜜（Tualang蜂蜜、美国"麦卢卡"）；强调麦卢卡蜂蜜的独特优势在于其"非过氧化物活性"（non-peroxide activity）。

## 选择性精读

① "Beyond its function as a tasty accompaniment to toast, honey is a tried-and-true health remedy, with its use as such dating back centuries to the Ancient Egyptians and the Ancient Greeks."

**中文理解**：除了作为吐司的美味搭配这一功能外，蜂蜜还是一个 tried-and-true（久经考验的）健康疗法，其使用可以追溯到古埃及和古希腊。

**句子结构**：简单句。主句 "Beyond its function as a tasty accompaniment to toast, honey is a tried-and-true health remedy" + 伴随状语 "with its use as such dating back centuries to the Ancient Egyptians and the Ancient Greeks"。

**关键词**：tried-and-true（久经考验的）、dating back centuries（追溯到几个世纪前）

**表达方式**：用"Beyond its function as a tasty accompaniment to toast"（除了作为吐司的美味搭配）——轻松幽默的开场，暗示"蜂蜜不只是食物"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"历史定位"——读者被告知"蜂蜜入药"不是现代发明，而是"几千年的传统"。

---

② "I had a client with diabetes who had huge open wounds on both of his legs, from the foot up to the knee. There was a prescription from his doctor to put honey on top of the wounds — an old, traditional recipe to prevent the bandage clinging to the tissue, but it doesn't work for infection. I put mānuka honey MGO 100 [methylglyoxal strength] on the wound, instead of another [non-mānuka] honey. When I opened the bandages the next morning, nothing smelled and the wound was clean."

**中文理解**：我有一个糖尿病患者客户，他双腿从脚到膝盖都有巨大的开放性伤口。他的医生开了处方，将蜂蜜涂在伤口上——一个古老传统的配方，防止绷带粘在组织上，但它对感染不起作用。我在伤口上涂了 MGO 100 麦卢卡蜂蜜，而不是另一种（非麦卢卡）蜂蜜。第二天早上我打开绷带时，没有气味，伤口很干净。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "I had a client with diabetes who had huge open wounds on both of his legs, from the foot up to the knee" + 分2 "There was a prescription from his doctor to put honey on top of the wounds — an old, traditional recipe to prevent the bandage clinging to the tissue, but it doesn't work for infection" + 分3 "I put mānuka honey MGO 100 [methylglyoxal strength] on the wound, instead of another [non-mānuka] honey" + 分4 "When I opened the bandages the next morning, nothing smelled and the wound was clean"。

**关键词**：diabetes（糖尿病）、open wounds（开放性伤口）、prescription（处方）、clinging to the tissue（粘在组织上）

**表达方式**：用第一人称叙事——读者"亲眼见证"麦卢卡蜂蜜的"奇迹效果"，比数据更有说服力。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"故事"的句子——读者被"一个护士的真实经历"打动，开始相信"麦卢卡蜂蜜真的有效"。

---

③ "Before antibiotics reached their prime in the 1940s and 1950s, honey was a common ingredient in wound treatment. Decades later, as antibiotic-resistant pathogens began to rise, honey's health-promoting properties are making a comeback."

**中文理解**：在抗生素在 1940 年代和 1950 年代达到鼎盛时期之前，蜂蜜是伤口治疗中的常见成分。几十年后，随着抗生素耐药病原体的增加，蜂蜜的促进健康特性正在 comeback（卷土重来）。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Before antibiotics reached their prime in the 1940s and 1950s, honey was a common ingredient in wound treatment" + 分2 "Decades later, as antibiotic-resistant pathogens began to rise, honey's health-promoting properties are making a comeback"。

**关键词**：reached their prime（达到鼎盛时期）、antibiotic-resistant pathogens（抗生素耐药病原体）、making a comeback（卷土重来）

**表达方式**：用"making a comeback"（卷土重来）——将蜂蜜描述为"退隐后复出的英雄"，暗示"传统智慧在现代重新获得认可"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"历史循环"——读者开始理解"蜂蜜"从"传统药物"到"被抗生素取代"再到"重新被发现"的历史轨迹。

---

④ "While antibiotics are unable to kill superbugs — strains of viruses, bacteria and pathogens that are resistant to typical treatments — mānuka honey can."

**中文理解**：虽然抗生素无法杀死超级细菌——对典型治疗有耐药性的病毒、细菌和病原体株——但麦卢卡蜂蜜可以。

**句子结构**：复合句。让步状语从句 "While antibiotics are unable to kill superbugs — strains of viruses, bacteria and pathogens that are resistant to typical treatments" + 主句 "mānuka honey can"。

**关键词**：superbugs（超级细菌）、strains（株）、resistant to typical treatments（对典型治疗有耐药性）

**表达方式**：用破折号插入"strains of viruses, bacteria and pathogens that are resistant to typical treatments"——在句子中间解释"超级细菌"的定义，不打断主句节奏。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"战斗"的句子——读者被告知"抗生素失败了，但麦卢卡蜂蜜没有"。

---

⑤ "The mutational frequency — the rate at which bacteria becomes resistant to a compound — is very low for mānuka honey. So the emergence of bacterial resistance is very low, and I believe that's due to the fact that mānuka honey has a multifaceted mechanism of action. It works in several different ways to kill bacteria at the same time."

**中文理解**：突变频率——细菌对化合物产生耐药性的速率——对于麦卢卡蜂蜜来说非常低。所以细菌耐药性的出现非常低，我相信这是由于麦卢卡蜂蜜具有 multifaceted mechanism of action（多方位作用机制）。它同时以几种不同的方式杀死细菌。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "The mutational frequency — the rate at which bacteria becomes resistant to a compound — is very low for mānuka honey" + 分2 "So the emergence of bacterial resistance is very low, and I believe that's due to the fact that mānuka honey has a multifaceted mechanism of action" + 分3 "It works in several different ways to kill bacteria at the same time"。

**关键词**：mutational frequency（突变频率）、multifaceted mechanism of action（多方位作用机制）

**表达方式**：用"multifaceted mechanism of action"（多方位作用机制）——科学术语，暗示麦卢卡蜂蜜的"复杂性"是它"不易产生耐药性"的原因。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"机制"的句子——读者开始理解"为何细菌不易对麦卢卡蜂蜜产生耐药性"。

---

⑥ "Importantly, there have been no reported cases of bacteria developing a resistance to honey — and neither mānuka nor regular honey resistance has been generated in the laboratory."

**中文理解**：重要的是，没有报告病例显示细菌对蜂蜜产生耐药性——在实验室中也没有产生对麦卢卡或普通蜂蜜的耐药性。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Importantly, there have been no reported cases of bacteria developing a resistance to honey" + 分2 "and neither mānuka nor regular honey resistance has been generated in the laboratory"。

**关键词**：developing a resistance（产生耐药性）、generated in the laboratory（在实验室中产生）

**表达方式**：用"no reported cases"（没有报告病例）——强调"零记录"，制造"绝对安全"的印象。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"安全"的句子——读者被告知"蜂蜜不会导致耐药性"，这是抗生素无法比拟的优势。

---

⑦ "On a smaller scale, honey remains a popular home remedy for colds, sore throats and other ailments. It's not just folklore, either — a 2020 study found that honey may be a better treatment for mild coughs and colds than antibiotics or over-the-counter medicines."

**中文理解**：在较小规模上，蜂蜜仍然是治疗感冒、喉咙痛和其他疾病的 popular home remedy（流行的家庭疗法）。它不只是民间传说——2020 年的一项研究发现，蜂蜜可能比抗生素或非处方药更好地治疗轻度咳嗽和感冒。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "On a smaller scale, honey remains a popular home remedy for colds, sore throats and other ailments" + 分2 "It's not just folklore, either — a 2020 study found that honey may be a better treatment for mild coughs and colds than antibiotics or over-the-counter medicines"。

**关键词**：home remedy（家庭疗法）、folklore（民间传说）、over-the-counter medicines（非处方药）

**表达方式**：用"It's not just folklore, either"（它不只是民间传说）——直接回应读者可能的怀疑，然后用"2020 年研究"来"正名"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"正名"的句子——读者开始理解"家庭疗法"不只是"迷信"，还有"科学证据"。

---

⑧ "While other honeys don't come close to mighty mānuka in terms of quality, strength and health outcomes, there are other honeys with promising potential."

**中文理解**：虽然其他蜂蜜在质量、强度和健康结果方面无法与 mighty mānuka（强大的麦卢卡）相提并论，但其他蜂蜜也有 promising potential（广阔的前景）。

**句子结构**：复合句。让步状语从句 "While other honeys don't come close to mighty mānuka in terms of quality, strength and health outcomes" + 主句 "there are other honeys with promising potential"。

**关键词**：come close to（接近/与...相提并论）、mighty（强大的）、promising potential（广阔的前景）

**表达方式**：用"mighty mānuka"（强大的麦卢卡）——拟人化表达，将麦卢卡蜂蜜描述为"超级英雄"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"对比"引子——读者即将看到"其他蜂蜜"与"麦卢卡蜂蜜"的对比。

---

⑨ "Mānuka honey has actually been nebulized into people in the past for asthma treatment and also for the treatment of a bacterial infection called Pseudomonas aeruginosa, which can cause pneumonia. I see a future where we can use mānuka honey as part of the maintenance of our lung environment."

**中文理解**：麦卢卡蜂蜜实际上已经被 nebulized（雾化）用于哮喘治疗，也用于治疗一种叫做铜绿假单胞菌的细菌感染，这种感染可能导致肺炎。我看到一个未来，我们可以将麦卢卡蜂蜜用作维护我们肺部环境的一部分。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Mānuka honey has actually been nebulized into people in the past for asthma treatment and also for the treatment of a bacterial infection called Pseudomonas aeruginosa, which can cause pneumonia" + 分2 "I see a future where we can use mānuka honey as part of the maintenance of our lung environment"。

**关键词**：nebulized（雾化）、asthma（哮喘）、Pseudomonas aeruginosa（铜绿假单胞菌）、pneumonia（肺炎）

**表达方式**：用"I see a future"（我看到一个未来）——第一人称展望，暗示"这不是幻想，而是可预见的未来"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"未来"的句子——读者开始理解麦卢卡蜂蜜的"潜力"不只是"现在"，还有"未来"。

## 一句话总结
**麦卢卡蜂蜜从"古老伤口敷料"到"现代超级细菌杀手"——它比抗生素更不易产生耐药性（"多方位作用机制"），比非处方药更有效（2020 年牛津大学研究），从糖尿病伤口到 MRSA 感染，从哮喘雾化到肺部环境维护，麦卢卡蜂蜜正在被重新发现为"21世纪的天然药物"。**
