# 27. … with no particular place to go / "no side to fall in"

## 概览

- **出处**：*Ten Bridges I've Burnt: A Memoir in Verse*
- **体裁**：诗歌回忆录，自由诗
- **一句话主旨**：作者描述自己"with no particular place to go"——有人说"没有更快的路了"——他觉得这remark不幸——尽管他不急——而且是他自己说的；他离开太久时会想念北加州——"all of California tends to flatten in pictures"——"endless depth of ridges and valleys doesn't translate"；但另一方面——L.A.公交车门开——他不踢门——他打车——把L.A.当作map/grid时他产生某种order的幻觉——但实际他在车里坐了45分钟——"what if all maps are a bunch of little nowheres all put together"——他在nonlocality中找到某种peace；他总是飞走——"some nights I don't want any of you motherfuckers finding me"——但大多数夜晚他在去派对的路上——他是"still flesh and bone"——不再是party boy——但still haunt places——希望他的soul在每次与十个人lock eyes时留下某种cosmic imprint——"you may not be my type but I will still stop to flirt"——"because even though you are not my type / you are still easily one of the most beautiful things I've ever seen"——他pleasure于看这趟not-ill-fated cab ride——作为铅笔——connect-the-dots——所有他留下soul stuff的地方——"what infinite number of constellations could be imagined all between"。

## 主题脉络

1. **"no particular place to go"**：有人remark"没有更快的路了"——不幸——但他说了
2. **想念北加州**：California flatten in pictures——ridge/valleys不translate
3. **L.A. map幻觉**：作为grid时产生order幻觉——实际坐了45分钟
4. **"all maps are little nowheres"**：nonlocality——在nonlocality中找peace
5. **broomstick**：飞走——"some nights I don't want you finding me"——但大多数去派对
6. **"still flesh and bone"**：no longer party boy——但still haunt places
7. **cosmic imprint**：每次lock eyes——soul留下印记
8. **not my type but beautiful**：still stop to flirt——"easily one of the most beautiful things"
9. **connect-the-dots**：cab ride=铅笔——所有soul stuff的星座

## 逐段精读

### 第 1-15 行：remark unfortunate

> **原句 1:** "There is no faster way to get there" / I felt the remark unfortunate / despite the fact that I / was in no real hurry and that / I'm the one who said it

**中文理解**："没有更快的路了"——我觉得这remark不幸——尽管事实是——我不是很急——而且——是我自己说的。

**关键词**：no faster way / unfortunate / in no real hurry / I'm the one who said it

**为什么这样写**："There is no faster way to get there"——有人对他说——"there"=目的地——没有更快的方式——他felt the remark不幸——因为他不急——而且他自己说的——这是对"效率"的讽刺：有人告诉他"没有更快方式"——但他不急——这是关于"赶路"vs"在路上"的哲学。

**诗歌技法**：引用他人话——"there is no faster way"——然后否定——制造反讽。

### 第 16-35 行：California flatten

> **原句 2:** I miss Northern California when I leave it too long / you have to be there to actually notice it / all of California / tends to flatten in pictures / the endless depth / of the ridges and valleys doesn't translate / into the two-dimensionality of pictures

**中文理解**：我离开太久时会想念北加州——你必须在场才能真正notice它——所有California——tends to flatten in pictures——endless depth——of the ridges and valleys——doesn't translate——into the two-dimensionality of pictures。

**关键词**：miss / in too long / be there / notice / flatten in pictures / ridges and valleys / doesn't translate / two-dimensionality

**为什么这样写**："California flatten in pictures"——加州在照片里变平——"ridges and valleys"=山脊和峡谷——"doesn't translate into two-dimensionality"——无法翻译成二维——这是对"加州"的诗意描述：加州的美不在照片中——在于在场——山脊和峡谷的深度无法被二维照片捕捉。

**诗歌技法**：从"miss"（情感）到"flatten in pictures"（视觉）到"two-dimensionality"（数学）——术语叠加。

### 第 36-65 行：L.A. map幻觉

> **原句 3:** when confronted with Los Angeles / as a map or grid / I hallucinate / a certain order / but in practice I have sat for forty-five minutes in this car / and am thinking what if all maps / are a bunch of little nowheres / all put together

**中文理解**：当confronted with Los Angeles——作为map or grid——我hallucinate——a certain order——但in practice我坐了45分钟在车里——在thinking what if all maps——are a bunch of little nowheres——all put together。

**关键词**：Los Angeles / map or grid / hallucinate / order / forty-five minutes / a bunch of little nowheres

**为什么这样写**："hallucinate a certain order"——产生某种秩序的幻觉——把L.A.当作地图/网格——以为有秩序——但实际：45分钟堵在路上——"all maps are a bunch of little nowheres"——所有地图都是一堆小无处拼在一起——这是对"地图"的解构：地图不是"处处"——是"无处"的集合。

**诗歌技法**：从"map/grid"（秩序）到"a bunch of little nowheres"（无序）——悖论。

### 第 66-95 行：nonlocality+broomstick

> **原句 4:** and at this moment I find a certain peace in that / nonlocality / whether in a cab / or on my broomstick / I'll always fly away / 'cause some nights / I don't want any of you motherfuckers finding me

**中文理解**：此刻我在那中找到某种peace——nonlocality——whether in a cab——or on my broomstick——我always fly away——'some nights——我不想让任何motherfuckers找到我。

**关键词**：peace / nonlocality / cab / broomstick / fly away / motherfuckers finding me

**为什么这样写**："nonlocality"=非定域性——物理学术语——量子纠缠——作者用它来描述"不在任何处"的状态——"broomstick"=女巫扫帚——"I'll always fly away"——他总是飞走——女巫/酷儿意象——"motherfuckers finding me"=不想被任何人找到——"nonlocality"=无处=自由。

**诗歌技法**：从"nonlocality"（量子物理）到"broomstick"（奇幻）——两极并置。

### 第 96-130 行：haunt places

> **原句 5:** but the other nights / most nights / I am always en route to another party / I will quickly run out of early / in certain contexts I love a party / of a thousand "hellos" / and saying goodbye (it's such a sad word ) / and flinging my little soul / as many places on the city grid map / as I can in a night

**中文理解**：但其他夜晚——most nights——我always en route to another party——我会quickly run out of early——in certain contexts我love a party——of a thousand "hellos"——and saying goodbye（it's such a sad word）——and flinging my little soul——as many places on the city grid map——as I can in a night。

**关键词**：most nights / en route / party / thousand hellos / goodbye / sad word / flinging my little soul / city grid map

**为什么这样写**："en route to another party"=在去派对的路上——"thousand hellos"=一千个你好——"saying goodbye (it's such a sad word)"=告别是悲伤的词——括号注释——"flinging my little soul"=扔出我的小灵魂——"as many places on the city grid map"=在城市网格上尽可能多的地方——这是对"社交动物"的描述：他在派对间穿梭——扔出灵魂——留下印记。

**诗歌技法**：括号——"(it's such a sad word)"——插入注释——制造节奏变化。

### 第 131-175 行：cosmic imprint

> **原句 6:** I am still flesh and bone / and no longer a party boy / but still make it a practice to haunt places / hope my soul / is making some kind of cosmic imprint / every time I lock eyes / with any given ten people / you may not be my type but I will still stop / to flirt / because even though you are not my type / you are still easily one of the most beautiful things / I've ever seen.

**中文理解**：我仍然是flesh and bone——no longer a party boy——但仍然make it a practice to haunt places——hope my soul——is making some kind of cosmic imprint——every time I lock eyes——with any given ten people——you may not be my type but I will still stop——to flirt——because even though you are not my type——you are still easily one of the most beautiful things——I've ever seen。

**关键词**：still flesh and bone / no longer a party boy / haunt places / cosmic imprint / lock eyes / not my type / still stop to flirt / most beautiful things

**为什么这样写**："still flesh and bone"=仍然是血肉之躯——"no longer a party boy"=不再是派对男孩——"haunt places"=出没于各处——"cosmic imprint"=宇宙印记——"every time I lock eyes with any given ten people"=每次与十个人对视——"not my type but still stop to flirt"="you are still easily one of the most beautiful things I've ever seen"——这是对"美"的重新定义：不是你类型——但仍然是最美——"easily"=轻松地/不费力地——"most beautiful things"——对任何人的美都开放。

**诗歌技法**：从"flesh and bone"（物质）到"cosmic imprint"（宇宙）——尺度跳跃。

### 第 176-192 行：connect-the-dots

> **原句 7:** I take great pleasure / in seeing this not-ill-fated cab ride / as the pencil / making a connect-the-dots / of all the places I left soul stuff behind / and what infinite number of constellations could be imagined / all between

**中文理解**：我take great pleasure——在seeing this not-ill-fated cab ride——作为铅笔——making a connect-the-dots——of all the places I left soul stuff behind——and what infinite number of constellations could be imagined——all between。

**关键词**：not-ill-fated / cab ride / pencil / connect-the-dots / left soul stuff / constellations / all between

**为什么这样写**："not-ill-fated"=不凶兆的——"cab ride as the pencil"=出租车旅程=铅笔——"connect-the-dots"=连点成线游戏——"left soul stuff behind"=留下灵魂碎片——"constellations could be imagined all between"=想象中所有点之间的星座——这是对"旅程"的诗意描述：每次出行=铅笔画线——留下灵魂碎片——连成星座。

**诗歌技法**：从"cab ride"（日常）到"constellations"（宇宙）——尺度跳跃。

## 精读总结

### 1. 本诗核心表达
- **"all maps are a bunch of little nowheres all put together"**：地图不是"处处"——是"无处"的集合——对"地图"的解构。
- **"nonlocality"**：量子物理——无处=自由——"some nights I don't want any of you motherfuckers finding me"。
- **"you are still easily one of the most beautiful things I've ever seen"**：不是你类型——但仍然是最美——对"美"的重新定义：对任何人的美都开放。

### 2. 重要诗歌技法
- **broomstick**：女巫意象——飞走——不想被找到——"nonlocality"=无处=自由。
- **"thousand hellos / goodbye (it's such a sad word)"**：括号注释——"goodbye"是悲伤的词——制造节奏变化。

### 3. 写作学习点
- **"California flatten in pictures"**：美不在照片中——在于在场——"ridges and valleys don't translate"——可迁移到任何"不可复制的体验"。
- **"connect-the-dots / left soul stuff / constellations"**：旅程=铅笔画线——留下灵魂碎片——连成星座——可迁移到任何"日常行为的诗意化"。
