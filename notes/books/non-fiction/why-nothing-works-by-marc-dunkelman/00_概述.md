# 00. 概述

## 书目信息

- **书名**：*Why Nothing Works: Who Killed Progress—and How to Bring It Back*
- **作者**：Marc J. Dunkelman
- **出版**：PublicAffairs, 2025
- **类型**：非虚构论述（政治思想/政策史）
- **精读文件数**：10 章（ch01–ch10）+ 总览三篇

## 全书主旨

进步主义（progressivism）不是一种单一冲动，而是 Hamiltonian（向上收权、centralized authority）与 Jeffersonian（向下放权、防范 tyranny）两种叙事的 awkward amalgam。自 1960 年代末起，Jeffersonian 过度占上风，把政府绑进 vetocracy（否决政体），progressivism 因而 undermine 自己；解法是 full circle，重新平衡两种冲动。

## 三论点

1. **Progressivism = 两冲动**：Hamiltonian 与 Jeffersonian 两种 narrative 共存于 progressivism，从 founding 起就 torn perennially between build public authority up 与 tear centralized power down。
2. **两冲动 wax and wane**：自 1960s 起 Jeffersonian 过度占上风，cultural aversion to power 成为主流。
3. **Jeffersonian 过度是 liability**：把 government 绑进 vetocracy，undermine progressivism 自己；需要 full circle rebalance。

## 章节路线图

- ch01 Introduction：Moses vs Penn Station 对比，引出全书 thesis
- ch02 Servant or Master：progressivism 的 founding tension（1912 stolen plank, railroad 时代）
- ch03 The Price of Progress：TVA 为 Hamiltonian triumph，progressivism of two minds
- ch04 Bodies on the Machine：1950s–60s cultural shift（fear of chaos → fear of tyranny），community action programs
- ch05 Manacling the Octopus：rights revolution（LEOBORs, welfare depersonalization）
- ch06 More Harm Than Good：deregulation (airline/trucking), Big John, vetocracy
- ch07 No Place to Call Home：housing (UDC fiscal crisis, PACB, exclusionary zoning, NIMBY)
- ch08 The Bridge to Nowhere：infrastructure (Yarborough rule, Overton Park, freeway wars)
- ch09 A Tragedy of the Commons in Reverse：clean energy (NECEC, electricity grid history)
- ch10 Full-Circle Progressivism：solution (voice but not a veto)

## 核心人物

- **Robert Moses**：centralized power 的 paradigmatic figure（Cross Bronx Expressway）
- **George Walbridge Perkins**：Hamiltonian progressive（1912 Progressive Party）
- **Louis Brandeis**："the People's Attorney"，Jeffersonian anchor
- **Charles Francis Adams**：Hamiltonian anchor（"regulate rather than destroy"）
- **David Lilienthal**：TVA Hamiltonian（"make a different type of citizen"）
- **Arthur E. Morgan**：TVA Jeffersonian（restore traditional Southern society）
- **David Hackett**：community action programs 的 Jeffersonian advocate
- **Ed Logue**：UDC 的 "good guy's Robert Moses"
- **Anona Stoner**：Overton Park 的 David
- **Ralph Yarborough**：4(f) 条款的作者

## 关键概念

- **Hamiltonian impulse**：向上收权、centralized authority、expert bureaucracy
- **Jeffersonian impulse**：向下放权、防范 tyranny、speak truth to power
- **vetocracy**：否决政体，progressivism 的 Jeffersonian 过度产物
- **full circle**：rebalance 两种冲动
- **voice but not a veto**：解决方案框架

## 叙事结构

全书 8 章：前 4 章 detail progressivism 的 evolution（Hamiltonian → Jeffersonian），后 4 章 detail 这一 shift 在 corporate regulation / housing / public works / clean energy 的体现。每章以 story 开头（illustrate progressive schism），随后 chronological narrative。

> "the movement needs to come full circle—to rebalance its Jeffersonian and Hamiltonian impulses."（ch01）
