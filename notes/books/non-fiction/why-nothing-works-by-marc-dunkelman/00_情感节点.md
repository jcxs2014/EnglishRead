# 00. 情感节点

## 节点一：Moses 与 Penn Station（引言）

Robert Moses 轻易推过 Cross Bronx Expressway，而 Penn Station 重建 decades 僵局。权力架构从 centralized 变成 diffused——不是个性变化，而是 power architecture 本身的 transformation。

> "This wasn't a shift in personality—it was a transformation in the architecture of power itself."（ch01）

## 节点二：progressivism 的 great schism（引言/第 1 章）

progressivism 不是单一冲动，而是 Hamiltonian 与 Jeffersonian 两种 narrative 的 awkward amalgam。从 founding 起 torn perennially between build public authority up 与 tear centralized power down。

> "Progressives have, from the very beginning, been torn perennially between their desires to build public authority up and to tear centralized power down."（ch02）

## 节点三：TVA 的 Hamiltonian triumph（第 2 章）

TVA 是 Hamiltonian progressivism 的 greatest triumph—centralized public power 把 Tennessee Valley 从 free market failure 中拉出。但 FDR 的 vision 是 explicitly paternalistic："make a different type of citizen out of them."

> "is really a secondary matter. What we are doing there is taking a watershed with about three and a half million people in it, almost all of them rural, and we are trying to make a different type of citizen out of them."（ch03）
## 节点四：community action programs 的 Jeffersonian turn（第 3 章）

Kennedy/Johnson era 的 "maximum feasible participation" 是 progressivism 从 Hamiltonian 到 Jeffersonian 的 cultural shift 的 paradigmatic (failed) experiment。CAPs 被 mayors 与 power brokers 视为 threat。

> "The watchword for their strategy was 'institutional reform.'"（ch04）

## 节点五：New Left 的 "bodies on the gears"（第 3 章）

Mario Savio 的 Free Speech Movement 演讲把 New Left 的 anti-machine rhetoric 推到 climax："put your bodies upon the gears"。New Left 不是 frightened of chaos—order 本身就是 problem。

> "There's a time when the operation of the machine becomes so odious, makes you so sick at heart that you can't take part! You can't even passively take part! And you've got to put your bodies upon the gears and upon the wheels, upon the levers, upon all the apparatus—and you've got to make it stop!"（ch04）

## 节点六：rights revolution（第 4 章）

progressivism 的 Jeffersonian turn 把 "rights" 作为 manacling the octopus 的工具—LEOBORs (police) 与 welfare depersonalization 是 paradigmatic cases。Charles Reich "The New Property" 是 intellectual anchor。

> "The rights revolution broadly encompassed Jeffersonian progressivism's strategy of pushing authority down by investing individuals with new rights."（ch05）

## 节点七：deregulation 作为 progressive accomplishment（第 5 章）

airline deregulation (CAB) 是 progressive accomplishment，not conservative。Ted Kennedy + Ralph Nader led the fight。progressivism 的 Jeffersonian turn 与 conservatism 的 anti-government stance 在 1970s 开始 tugging in the same direction。

> "In what can only be described as the deepest of ironies, political movements that had been at odds when the economy was good—with progressives pushing for more government while conservatives demanded less—began tugging in the same direction during the 1970s."（ch06）

## 节点八：housing 从 growth machine 到 paralysis（第 6 章）

Moses/Logue 的 centralized power (UDC) → fiscal crisis → PACB vetoes → exclusionary zoning → NIMBY。housing 从 "growth machine" 到 "paralysis"。Amazon HQ2 是 paradigmatic case：single state senator scuttled deal.

> "Whether or not Amazon's proposal was worthwhile, no single state senator should have the authority to scuttle a deal of this magnitude on his own."（ch07）

## 节点九：infrastructure paralysis（第 7 章）

Yarborough rule (4(f)) → Marshall's "Jeffersonian earthquake" → infrastructure paralysis。environmental review + standing expansion 把 progressivism 的 aversion to power 制度化。

> "Congress clearly did not intend that cost and disruption of the community were to be ignored.… The statute indicates that protection of the parkland was to be given paramount importance."（ch08）

## 节点十：clean energy 的 "tragedy of the commons in reverse"（第 8 章）

NECEC 项目从 "bonanza" 到 referendum defeat—38 reviews, 2M documents, no watt delivered。private interests overuse public process to block collective goods。

> "The barriers thwarting construction of this one high-voltage transmission line aren't unique—they were, and are, indicative of a more typical rigmarole."（ch09）

## 节点十一：full-circle progressivism（结论）

progressivism has come full circle—Jeffersonian retrenchment (50+ years) has run its course。solution 是 "a voice but not a veto"。movement needs to rebalance。

> "The movement has been justified to erect protections against Robert Moses–era abuse—to manacle the octopus. But that Jeffersonian retrenchment, now more than fifty years old, has run its course."（ch10）
