12. Charlie 说**"Be my shark, Stephens."**——Nora 说**"Already was. Always have been."**→
13. Charlie 说**"I love you."**——Nora 说**"I love you too."**——**"I don't have to force it past a knot or through the vise of a tight throat. It's simply the truth, and it breathes out of me, a wisp of smoke, a sigh, another floating blossom on a current carrying billions of them."**→
