# Ch25 · The Library & The Collapse · "I won't let anything hurt you"

> 文件：`text/CH27_chapter_26_...txt`（Book Ch26，约 7200 字符）
> 叙事功能：**全书前半最高潮**——Nora 去图书馆找 Charlie 谈下一百页稿，Sharon 打电话来（Nora 的老板，Loggia Publishing 可能招人当编辑）——**"Loggia might be hiring a new editor soon."**——**Nora 说"Yes"然后又"I'd like to think about it"**——**"I'd be an editor right now!"**——**Nora 在街边向 Libby 爆发**——**"I wouldn't have passed on the job I actually wanted to make sure you could afford the best fucking doula in Manhattan!"**——**Libby 在书店门口晕倒**。

**场景：** 图书馆（Nora 和 Charlie 约见面）→ 书店门口 → 街边长椅。

**段落逻辑链：**
1. Nora 去图书馆找 Charlie 谈稿——Nora 说**"It needs to be in person"**——**"We need to bulldoze through the awkwardness"**——**"Charlie's like me. We need to bulldoze through the awkwardness."**→
2. Charlie 说**"I'll reserve us a room. At the library."**——**"Get your mind out of the gutter"**——**Nora 说"I've tried"**→
3. Sharon 打电话（Nora 的老板，正在休产假）——**"Loggia might be hiring a new editor soon."**——**Charlie 向 Sharon 推荐了 Nora**——**"I've been talking with Charlie, and he says you're really helping get Dusty's book into shape."**→
4. Nora 说**"Yes"**然后改口**"I'd like to think about it"**→
5. Nora 在街边等六分钟——**"I still feel like I'm inside a fishbowl"**——**Nora 回到书店**，Libby 抱怨她迟到→
6. Nora 爆发——**"Can you not do that, Libby? Not right now."**——**"I'm not the one sneaking around, disappearing, not answering her husband's texts, keeping secrets."**——**"If that were true, I'd be an editor right now!"**——**"I wouldn't have passed on the job I actually wanted to make sure you could afford the best fucking doula in Manhattan!"**→
7. Libby 说**"I don't want your help, Nora! …I don't want to be another excuse for you not to have a life"**→
8. Libby 在书店门口**晕倒**。
