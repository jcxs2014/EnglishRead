**场景：** Lily Cottage 后院，Charlie 和 Nora 一起搭帐篷。

**段落逻辑链：**
1. Nora 打电话说服 Dusty 参加线上读书会——Dusty 说**"I'd love to help, Nora"**——Dusty 解释她选 Sunshine Falls 因为**"it seemed like the kind of place that might look one way on the outside, and be something totally different once you got to know it"**→
2. Charlie 用 Buick 载 Nora 去 Target 买露营物资——书店后巷 Charlie 说**"Let's go somewhere…Anywhere that my mother and your sister aren't within eyeshot"**→
3. Charlie 熟练地搭帐篷——**"How are you such a pro at this?"**——**"I camped a lot with my dad, growing up."**→
4. Charlie 说**"He doesn't want me here."**——**"When I told him I was staying for a while, his exact words were, 'Son, just what do you think you can do?'"**——**Charlie 说父亲对他说过**"你能做什么"→
5. Charlie 承认**"I've never felt so useless in my life"**→
6. Charlie 揭示**Amaya 分手后和 Shepherd 交往**——**"I came home for the holidays, and I found out Amaya had been dating my cousin since a few weeks after we split"**→
7. Charlie 说**"I hated being a kid"**——Nora 说**"I think Libby's getting a divorce"**——**Nora 第一次向 Charlie 说出 Libby 离婚的猜测**→
8. Charlie 说**"Maybe you're already doing what she needs you to do. Just by being here with her."**——**Nora 说"Maybe that's all your dad needs from you too."**→
9. Nora 问**"Charlie, will you please be here?"**——Charlie 说**"I'll go anywhere with you."**——**"Sometimes, even when you start with the last page and you think you know everything, a book finds a way to surprise you."**——**全书最重的一段对话之一**。
