# Things We Fake — 金句集

## 六类主题金句（引文均来自 epub 原文）

### 1. 爱情与选择（Love & Choice）

**"I might as well put up an ad on a billboard: Single and desperate woman looking for hot hunk."**
— Sue, Ch01（自嘲式择偶观，嘴上说不要心里其实渴望）

**"If he were lost, I would be happy to give him a home."**
— Sue 对 Cam 的第一印象，Ch01（欲望诚实，幽默表达）

**"There's nothing I like better than snow on Christmas Eve, but it's frigging April."**
— Sue 的 New York 四月飞雪设定，Ch01（季节错位，暗示好运未到）

---

### 2. 友情与支持（Friendship & Support）

**"One of these days you'll have to trade in the spaghetti noodle spine you have for a real one and put your foot down."**
— Lily 对 Sue，Ch01（spaghetti noodle spine 是全书的性格关键词）

**"Have all four of you gone nuts?"**
— Sue 对闺蜜团的吐槽，Ch01（四人变五人，后文揭示是五个女生）

---

### 3. 背叛与信任（Betrayal & Trust）

**"When I found Neil playing hide the salami with Sally, I dumped his cheating ass using words I could never repeat."**
— Sue 撞见前任出轨，Ch01（"hide the salami"是俚语，chick-lit 幽默解构背叛）

**"Mamma mia! My breath whooshed out of my body and a tingle crept up my inner thighs."**
— Sue 对 Cam 的欲望反应，Ch01（身体诚实 vs. 情感防御）

---

### 4. 身份与面具（Identity & Masks）

**"Things We Fake."**
— 书名主题：每个人都在 fake 某种东西，只有在找到对的人之后才能 be yourself

**"I hate that kind of thing."**
— Sue 嘴上说恨盲约，后文却真去了——言行反差是她的防御机制

---

### 5. 兄弟与家庭（Brother & Family）

**"I might be a twin, but I'm nothing like Dylan."**
— Cam 对自己的定义，Ch05（与 Dylan 的对比是全书兄弟线核心）

---

### 6. 成长与勇气（Growth & Courage）

**"You'll have to trade in the spaghetti noodle spine for a real one and put your foot down."**
— Lily 对 Sue 的核心忠告，Ch01（全书 Sue 的成长靶心：学会说"不"）

**"My breath whooshed out of my body and a tingle crept up my inner thighs."**
— Sue 欲望书写，诚实直接，Ch01（性同意文化的健康表达）
