# Beware the Abbott Boys by Chelsea Ichaso — 情感节点

## 节点一（ch01）：新闻剪报——Mariana 之死

> "Nearly sixty years after Peter J. Abbott inherited a property worth millions under suspicious circumstances, a new tragedy brings this haunted history to the surface."（ch01）

Hayden 阅读新闻剪报，了解 Mariana Sanchez 之死与 Abbott 兄弟的嫌疑，以及 Abbott 家族六十年来的黑暗历史。

---

## 节点二（ch02）：Hayden 的辩护——"I know none of them could've hurt Mariana"

> "But I've been the Abbott boys' neighbor my entire life... I know none of them could've hurt Mariana."（ch02）

Hayden 在英语课后与 Sage 对话，确认自己对 Abbott 兄弟的辩护立场。

---

## 节点三（ch04）：Adam 的 plan——"The whispers said it had to be today"

> "The whispers said it had to be today."（ch04）

Adam POV 回溯，揭示他的 plan 与 Mariana 之死的时间线关联。

---

## 节点四（ch05）：Hayden 的怀疑——"I'm not sure they're wrong"

> "And unlike last time, I'm not sure they're wrong."（ch05）

Hayden 在 Kennedy 死后，首次怀疑 Abbott 兄弟可能涉案。

---

## 节点五（ch07）：Bram 的秘密——"Being close to Mariana hadn't felt quite right"

> "Being close to Mariana hadn't felt quite right in weeks. There was something he needed to take care of, something he had to do before things could go back to normal."（ch07）

Bram POV 回溯，揭示他对 Mariana 的疏离感与 secret relationship。

---

## 节点六（ch09）：Henry 的 secret project——"the thing that kept him awake"

> "In fact, while he was the only person around, he thought he might even be able to work on his project, the thing that kept him awake for hours every night."（ch09）

Henry POV 回溯，揭示他的 secret project 与 Mariana 之死的时间线相关。

---

## 节点七（ch10）：Hayden 的信念——"No one hated Kennedy Russo. And everyone hates the Abbott brothers."

> "No one hated Kennedy Russo. And everyone hates the Abbott brothers."（ch10）

Hayden 在调查后确认案件的核心困境——Kennedy 无仇人，但 Abbott 兄弟有无数敌人。

---

## 节点八（ch13）：Adam 的妖魔化——"Now, he was a monster"

> "Now, he was a monster."（ch13）

Adam POV 回溯，揭示 social media 泄露与 townspeople 的妖魔化。

---

## 节点九（ch16）：Bram 的决定——"This has to end"

> "This has to end."（ch16）

Bram POV 回溯，Bram 决定结束 secret relationship。

---

## 节点十（ch23）：Adam 的 plan 执行——"If I cannot inspire love, I will cause fear"

> "If I cannot inspire love, I will cause fear."（ch23）

Adam POV 回溯，Adam 告诉 Mariana Bram 的 cheating，Mariana 决定与 Bram 分手。

---

## 节点十一（ch27）：Mariana 之死——"The sky itself was on fire"

> "The sky itself was on fire."（ch27）

Bram POV 回溯，Bram 目睹 Mariana 之死。

---

## 节点十二（ch29）：Hayden 的恐惧——"For the first time in my life, I'm actually afraid of my best friend"

> "For the first time in my life, I'm actually afraid of my best friend."（ch29）

Hayden 对 Henry 的恐惧——从"我相信 Henry"到"我 afraid of Henry"的转变。

---

## 节点十三（ch31）：Bram 被逮捕——"Be strong"

> "Be strong."（ch31）

Bram 被警察逮捕，Hayden 的 tears 与 Bram 的"Be strong"显示他们的 bond。

---

## 节点十四（ch32）：Sage 的威胁——"You'll probably end up being the next dead girl"

> "You'll probably end up being the next dead girl."（ch32）

Sage 对 Hayden 的 threat，foreshadowing 后续的 murder attempt。

---

## 节点十五（ch33）：Adam 救 Hayden——"And she coughed"

> "And she coughed."（ch33）

Adam CPR 救 Hayden，Hayden 濒死但被救活。

---

## 节点十六（ch35）：Bram 的释放——"look awfully pretty in the sunshine"

> "But you, my dear, look awfully pretty in the sunshine."（ch35）

Epilogue，Bram 被释放，Hayden 与 Bram 重逢并亲吻，章节以 Bram 的"pretty in the sunshine"收尾。
