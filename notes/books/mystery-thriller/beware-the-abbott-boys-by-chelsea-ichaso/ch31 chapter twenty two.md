# 31. Chapter Twenty Two

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Hayden 与 Henry 对质，Henry 承认他 asked Bram to pull the fire alarm；Henry 揭露 Kennedy 杀害 Mariana 的 truth（tampered with wire）；Bram 被警察逮捕 for Kennedy's murder；Hayden 怀疑 Henry 的 truthfulness，思考"If Henry didn't kill Kennedy, who did?"
- **情感弧线位置**：调查 climax——Bram 被 arrest，Hayden 对 Henry 的怀疑达到顶点。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：不可靠叙述者（Henry 的 confession）；红鲱鱼（Bram 被 arrest）。
- **人物弧线**：Hayden：从"我相信 Henry"到怀疑 Henry；Henry：从"帮助 Hayden"到 confession；Bram：从"party host"到 arrest。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称 Hayden 视角；对话推进调查；Bram's arrest 作为章节钩子。

## 精读

### 第1处：Henry 的 confession——Kennedy's truth

> **原句 1:** "Henry steps into the room, dropping the empty bag at his feet, fist still clenched at his side."

中文理解：Henry steps into the room，dropping the empty bag at his feet，fist still clenched at his side——显示他的 anger 与 tension。

句子结构：主语 Henry + 谓语 steps + 介词短语 (into the room) + 分词短语 (dropping the empty bag at his feet, fist still clenched at his side) 作伴随状语。

关键词：steps into the room（steps into the room） / dropping the empty bag（dropping the empty bag） / fist still clenched（fist still clenched）

表达方式：Hayden 的叙述，显示 Henry 的 anger。

为什么这样写：Henry 的"fist still clenched"显示他的 anger——这种 anger 为后续剧情埋下伏笔。
