# The Color of Death（情感节点）

> 10 个节点。每节点：叙事概括 + 2-3 条关键引语（逐字取自原文）。

## 节点一：模拟审判（ch01）

Frank Hastings 在河边小屋醒来，被绑上高背椅，被迫观看自己搞砸的庭审录像，写下认罪书，枪被塞进手里。

- "It's about seeking justice for Larry Stafford and our mom."
- "I even brought my own .38-caliber gavel."
- Frank Hastings sobbed one last time.

## 节点二：墓园的兰花（ch02）

Colm 在秋雨的墓园躲开了恩人 Barnett 太太的墓——他把第二束兰花放在最近的一块碑上，逃回车里。

- "No flowers or apologies can absolve me of my guilt. They're here because of me."
- "I place the bouquet of orchids on the nearest tombstone and walk back to my car."

## 节点三：标题落地（ch04）

Rachel 的凶案现场，死者明亮的蓝眼睛正在浑浊——Colm 第一次说出那三个词。

- "Even a few hours after she stopped breathing, those eyes are already turning opaque—the color of death."
- "Memories crash into my heart, and panic rips through me. 'Where is her child?'"

## 节点四：地下室的纸箱（ch08）

Colm 被降职到无窗的地下室，打开装私物的纸箱：Jaci 在婴儿床里、Ally 在Nova Scotia的海岸线上、天平的链条缠着。

- "The first picture is of Jaci sitting up in her crib. She has Ally's eyes and her smile."
- "Fairness is beauty, Colm. Always remember that."

## 节点五：血手（ch07）

Lloyd 的电话警告、burner phone 的失踪之后，Martin 在后视镜里看清了自己的脸和手。

- Slowly, he turned his hands over and stared at his sweaty palms. They were covered in blood, too.

## 节点六：门后的名字（ch28）

Mae 的客厅里，SIDS 与自杀的双重丧亲第一次被言明；门口的信箱里躺着一颗撕成两半的红桃 K。

- "There were no signs, Colm. Ally was crushed after losing Jaci to SIDS, but you, me, everyone, thought that she was getting better."
- "Not the first suicide king I've gotten sent to me. I started getting them after Ally."

## 节点七：跑道上的六声枪响（ch40）

Colm 在初遇 Ally 的弯道全速冲刺，幻象里她转头看向别处——狙击手的子弹贴身落下，他抬起下巴等下一枪。

- "like there's a rip in the fabric of time, and if I turn my head fast enough when I round that bend, she's still there."
- "I stood there because I want to see Ally again."

## 节点八：车库的引擎（ch59）

Belle 的逃离、Bones 的被拒之后，Colm 独坐车库，发动引擎，闭上眼睛。

- "I raise my glass in mock toast to myself. 'Well done, Colm! When you fail, you fail in spectacular fashion for the whole world to see.'"
- "There's a difference between wanting to die and being tired of living. Right now—I'm tired."

## 节点九：钥匙与手（ch61）

Mae 用只有她与 Ally 才有的钥匙进门，坐在楼梯上陪他哭、陪他笑，最后伸出手。

- "Colm, you're serving a sentence for a crime you didn't commit. I'm going to help you escape this prison."
- "You bite the inside of your cheek and stop talking."
- She holds out her hand and I take it.

## 节点十：同桌的微笑（ch70）

终章的 The Junction：Colm 与 Belle 在长椅上晒太阳，对面的卡座里，JD 撕开红桃 K、给红桃 Q 画上领带绞索，然后起身点单。

- "Maybe I should have let him die," JD muttered, tearing the King of Hearts in half.
- "Belle. What a great name."
- "Jane. But my friends call me JD."
