# 概述

## 全书梗概

1981 年 9 月 12 日，一个孩子在 Malbry Lyceum 看魔术师 The Great Carovnik 的演出，整晚只记住台上那位穿银色礼服的「女助手」。她俯身在这个孩子耳边留下一句话，而这孩子用四十多年才敢承认自己一直照做。"Little girl. Make them look."（ch01）

她先声明了一段空白：
- 人生头七年什么都不记得，记忆从那个清晨六点半开始，清晰得像切割玻璃。门厅里有一张两人合影为证——同样的及颌发，同样的裙子，只是她粉、Katie 蓝——可她完全不记得当晚 Katie 在场。"The first seven years of my life are a blank. I don’t remember a thing before that. Most children remember something of their early years, but not me. No favourite toy, no lullaby, not even a fall down a flight of stairs. My memories start at six-thirty on September 12th, 1981, and I remember what happened then with cut-glass crystal clarity."（ch01）
- "There’s even a picture of us both; a slightly faded photograph taken in the foyer. In it, we look like sisters. We have the same dark, jaw-length hair, the same fringe, the same vivid faces. We are even wearing the same kind of dress, though mine is pink, and hers is blue. But although there can be no doubt that this was the night of the magic show, I don’t remember her being there at all. It’s as if she was snipped from my memory."（ch01）

母亲在回家路上把这一切降级：
- 魔法不是真的，只是镜子里的把戏；The Great Carovnik 是个男人，那位女士只是他的助手。而孩子在当场给出的判断与母亲正相反——"‘Magic’s not real. It’s just a trick with mirrors. And The Great Carovnik is a man. That lady was his assistant. The man in the suit and the black cape – he was the magician.’"（ch01）
- "But the woman was who I remembered. She was the one who deserved the name. She was the one who counted."（ch01）

四十年后，2022 年的 Bernie 正经历更年期，在一家旧书店里数客人的猫属种属与畅销书榜。她的丈夫 Martin 越来越沉默。公园里一桩谋杀把 Graham ‘Creepy’ Crawley 推上新闻，而她每天读那些报道，说不清自己为什么要读。"At last, Graham ‘Creepy’ Crawley has been charged with Jo Perry’s murder. The fact that he is an educator has already caused outrage. Protests and calls for more security in schools have joined the vigils in support of the victims of male violence."（ch37）

与她的自述并置的，是另一条完全独立的叙述：
- Kate Hemsworth 出版的《Class of ’92》节选。十五个 1992 年的片段，写的是同一批人在寄宿学校 Mulberry House 的日子。那里的霸凌有名字，叫 House；那些伤害有编号。"House was a game that only belonged to a distant childhood. And yes, of course, I was wrong about that. As the Third Incident demonstrates, the game was only beginning."（ch26）
- 那一夜的乐队列阵里有她未来的丈夫——"The band, a four-piece combo with Martin Ingram on bass, played all the hits, and we posed for the photographer, looking fantastic (or so we thought), except for Bernie Moon, whose weight had really ballooned over the summer term, and who hadn’t even touched her champagne."（ch26）

伤害留下的东西不会自动愈合。多年后她仍然在追问那个问题：
- "I know how naïve all this must sound. But you need to understand. What if I was right?"（ch07）
- 而关于身体与血的那套话术，"Women and blood have always been close. Wise blood; profane blood; forbidden blood; spilled blood. Blood that is at different times both primal and accusing; disgusting and exciting; provoking horror, shame and lust – and, sometimes, the promise of something else."（ch04）
- 是同一套说法的另一副面孔。1992 线的叙述者则在多年后写下这段话："Damage tells you it was your fault. That you did something to provoke it. It teaches you you’re worthless."（ch33）

她一生都在追一个被剪掉的人。直到三十年后，她自己站上那个舞台。

## 主题

- **聚光灯与消失**：谁被看见、谁被剪掉。全书反复出现同一组词——vanish、eclipse、invisible——而舞台是唯一让「被看见」成为可能的地方。
- **记忆作为证词**：她反复用「我记得」「我写下来」的句式，把记忆当作物证清点；而 1981 年那一晚之所以重要，正因为它被写下来了。
- **镜子与碎片**：Tennyson 的《The Lady of Shalott》按顺序铺满十二个 Track，镜子碎裂的那一行是「I am half sick of shadows」。真相不给你一面镜子，给你很多块。

## 人物弧光

### Bernie（Bernadette Ingram，1992 年名为 Bernie Moon）

从「人生头七年是空白」的孩子，到把记忆逐条编号的记录者。她一生都在追一个被剪掉的人，直到三十年后自己站上舞台。

### Martin Ingram

1992 年在乐队里弹贝斯的人（"The band, a four-piece combo with Martin Ingram on bass, played all the hits, and we posed for the photographer, looking fantastic (or so we thought), except for Bernie Moon, whose weight had really ballooned over the summer term, and who hadn’t even touched her champagne."，ch26），2022 年是她沉默的丈夫。三十年后他得到的是迟到的自知。

### Katie Hemsworth（1992 年为 Katie Malkin）

ch01 合影里那个蓝裙子的女孩。ch02 让她自己报名：**Kate Hemsworth，Sadie（15）与 Ben（19）的母亲，Lucas Hemsworth 的妻子，Pog Hill '92 届毕业生**——与 Bernie 上的是同一所预科学校（Mulberry House → Pog Hill Sixth Form College）。她的两个孩子是全书另一条时间线上的钉子：1992 那场舞会之后，她也成了母亲。

她既是当年霸凌的参与者，也是 1992 线的叙述者。⚠️ 全书有一处刻意的双叙述：ch05（2022 线，Bernie 视角）写的是 Katie 被 Adam Price 打，而 ch33（1992 线，Kate 视角）写的是叙述者自己被打，提到同一段「我们七岁」和同一位老师 Mrs White。**两处都是各自章的逐字原文，不是谁抄错**——这正是双时间线要读者自己去对的那道缝。

### Adam Price

1992 线里出现的人物，也是全书尾声里那个拿着花、被指控过跟踪、却终于被人证清白的人。2022 线里他已经是一个可被 @ 的账号。
