# 《The Sirens》情感节点

## 节点一：掐住本的喉咙，然后往北开（ch04-ch08）

**叙事概括：** 全书从一个动作开始：露西醒来时双手已经环在一个男人的喉咙上。她不是被侵犯的现场被抓，而是反过来成了被拍下、被传开、被叫去谈话的那一个。学生福利官劝她「想一想本的前途」，她的母亲从电话里给出同样的算法。她没有辩解，也没有报警——她收拾了东西，往北开两百公里，去投奔那个连她出事都不接电话的姐姐。在路上和姐姐空置的房子里，她开始用一门从没学过的语言做梦，并认出满屋子都是自己梦里见过的画。

**关键引语：**

> ① "She'd been strangling him."（ch04）

> ② "And then this morning she’d found herself with her hands wrapped around Ben’s throat, like her body had made the decision for her."（ch05）

> ③ "Waking in the night, the thin motel sheets twisted, she’d breathed the words into her iPhone, watching bleary-eyed as Siri produced a translation. Mo dheirfiúr Irish. My sister."（ch07）

## 节点二：一块石头与一条命的账（ch09-ch12）

**叙事概括：** 时间切回一八〇一年。玛丽与伊莱莎本该只在萨温节那晚去一趟溪边，却撞上了收租人伯恩；玛丽砸下的那块石头没有杀死他，却足够把姐妹俩送上流放船。在女囚舱里，玛丽第一次被迫承认母亲的缺席是「一块冷掉的地方」，也第一次学会把悲伤改写成愤怒。她替失明的妹妹描述一切：甲板、海、船头的美人鱼像。而在这条线的尽头，伊莱莎已经开始教她听——听每一个女人的声音。

**关键引语：**

> ① "They were not supposed to have been at the stream that night. It had been Samhain, when the veil between the human world and the spirits’ stretched to its thinnest point."（ch09）

> ② "Over the years, Da’s anger became Mary’s own. She locked her memories of her mother deep inside her heart. It was easier, she learned, to be angry than to be sad."（ch12）

> ③ "Mam had become only an absence, a scar on her heart. Mam was not warm arms holding her close, or the sweetness of a lullaby. She was the cold space by the hearth, the ache of watching the village women hug their children tight. She was Da, hunched on the shore, turning the cloak over and over in his hands."（ch21）

## 节点三：日记、画室与第一次召唤（ch13-ch20）

**叙事概括：** 露西打开了姐姐的日记。一九九八年，十六岁的杰丝用一堂遗传课上的卷舌实验推出「他们中有一个人不是我的父母」，又在艺术教室里被老师一步步圈住。同一本日记里，她记下了身体的第一道银色裂纹、梦里的洞穴、以及那个后来会出现在借书卡上的名字。而在画室里，亨尼西让她闭上眼睛把看见的东西画下来——她第一次画出了梦里的女人，并在课堂上说出那个名字：伊莱莎。这是全书最重要的一次「创作即召唤」。露西读完这些，开始查图书馆的旧报纸、镇上的失踪名单、奈厄德号的沉没记录；她不再等姐姐回来解释。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Mum and Dad can both do it. But I can’t. So that means one of them can’t be my parent."（ch14）

> ② "Jess’s art teacher, who’d offered to give her extra lessons. Mr Hennessey. The name is familiar somehow – and then she remembers. The library book she’d found while cleaning up her sister’s dining table – the one about surrealism – had last been checked out by someone called C. Hennessey. He must have given it to Jess when she was at school. Why has she kept it all this time?"（ch15）

> ③ "She emerged in feathery black strokes. The grim set of her mouth, the gaunt cheeks. The thin lines of her wrists. Her eyes, empty in the darkness. I’d never drawn her until today. I think I’d felt afraid to. As if giving her a physical form might conjure her into being, draw her out of the dream world and into this one."（ch19）

## 节点四：收养证与两个名字（ch26-ch28）

**叙事概括：** 露西从梅乐蒂的鞋盒里翻出旧报纸，又从家里翻出收养文件：她的母亲不是玛吉·马丁，父亲也不是迈克尔·马丁。她坐在悬崖屋的沙发上，一边认出母亲的喂鸟器与藏在屋檐下的旧杂志，一边想清楚自己认不认这个姐姐。她给出的答案不是「我们血浓于水」，而是「我们被同一批谜团养大」。与此同时，杰丝在悬崖屋等来了另一个敲门的人。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Her mother isn’t Maggie Martin, but Judith Wilson. Her father isn’t Michael Martin, but Robert Wilson. The man who found Baby Hope at Devil’s Lookout and offered her to his wife, as if she was some treasure he’d dredged up; a pearl or an abalone shell."（ch26）

> ② "She is sitting on the sagging couch in the living room of Cliff House. Cliff House, where her parents had once lived. Had her father sat at the worn kitchen table, pencils and paper spread out around him? Had her mother chopped vegetables in the tiny yellow kitchen, humming as she looked out to sea? It was her mother’s bird-feeder that hung from the tree outside, her mother’s magazines she’d found dust-choked in the eaves."（ch28）

> ③ "Tears sting at the thought and she realises that she can’t accept it. It doesn’t matter to Lucy that they didn’t come from the same womb, that they didn’t journey into the world from the same place. They might not have grown up together, but they were raised in the same home, by the same parents, with the same rules and rituals. The same mysteries."（ch28）

## 节点五：梅乐蒂讲出三十八年前那一夜（ch31）

**叙事概括：** 梅乐蒂冒雨上门，把一九八一年的整件事从头讲了一遍：她十七岁，跟着丹尼去了那个洞穴，听见歌声，看着他的身体僵住、带着一种欲望爬向洞口，然后消失。她没有报警，因为她知道镇上会怎么算这笔账——「他是本地人，而我是外来者」。她把这些年一直用来解释自己为什么还活着的那句话也说了出来：那天有什么东西保护了她。

**关键引语：**

> ① "‘He kissed me, pushed me to the ground, and I remember thinking gross at the slimy feel of the rocks, when I should have been thinking about how to get him off me, how to get away. His hands moved to my waistband and then inside my trousers. It hurt and I told him to stop, to get off me.（ch31）

> ② "Lucy thinks, but doesn’t say, how intertwined those things are. Fear and desire. How one can become the other so easily. All it takes is the tightening of a hand on your wrist, your throat.（ch31）

> ③ "‘I came back,’ Melody continues, ‘because I felt safe here. I feel safe here. I know that doesn’t make sense. I should be traumatised by what Danny almost did to me, by what happened to him. But something protected me that day, Lucy. Something – someone – kept me safe.’（ch31）

## 节点六：她把卡梅伦带进了洞里（ch40-ch42）

**叙事概括：** 深夜敲门的是被停职、被妻子赶出门的卡梅伦·亨尼西。他哭着自辩「我们当时都是孩子」，杰丝只回了那句「我是孩子，可你不是」。她给他倒了一杯没洗干净的茶，听他说完，然后开始安排：让他收拾行李、把车开进一处本地人才认得的野餐地、跟着她走下崖梯。她需要有人看见那辆车，需要证据指向他。进洞之前她在洞口停了一下，闻到二十年前的咸味与海藻味。

**关键引语：**

> ① "‘It wasn’t like that, Jessie,’ he says, pleading. He stands up, puts his mug down on the coffee table and takes a step towards her. ‘It wasn’t like with you and me. What we had – that was different. We were both young. Kids. This is just some silly girls with the wrong idea. Teenage hysterics. A witch hunt.’"（ch40）

> ② "‘I don’t think that,’ she says. ‘Of course I don’t think that.’ She keeps her voice, her breathing level; lets him hold her. Her mind works furiously. This is Comber Bay. Australia’s Bermuda Triangle, with its own catalogue of mysteries.（ch40）

> ③ "The cave is smaller than in her memory, dark and wet as a mouth. Tears burn in her eyes at the familiar smells of brine and seaweed. Below, the tide whispers. Hush, it says, hush. Like a mother’s lullaby."（ch42）

## 节点七：奈厄德号撞上礁石（ch47）

**叙事概括：** 全书的高潮在同一个夜里分两百年上演。奈厄德号在即将靠岸的前一夜撞上礁石，玛丽被甩出铺位，囚舱进水。伊莱莎把她的手按在自己喉咙上，让她记住那晚母亲说过的话；玛丽跳进海里，鳃像花一样张开，脊柱裂出鳍，指尖之间跳着脉搏。她想起母亲带她们走向水边、又把她们抱回石屋的那一夜——母亲爱她们，却不忍把她们分开。然后她看见了正在下沉的船，和里面还活着的人。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The night before they were due to make landfall at New South Wales, Mary woke to a strange sound. A violent tearing, like the rending of time itself. Then, as if clasped by great hands, she was wrenched from her berth, her skull cracking against the wall opposite."（ch47）

> ② "The sea wrapped itself around her, cold and sweet, as Eliza had promised. The gills on her neck opened like flowers, the water singing on her skin.（ch47）

> ③ "She remembered how she had cried, how she had dug her toes into the wet sand, calling out for Da. Mam had knelt, silhouetted against the moon and the white spray, and held her close. Then she had lifted them – one child on each hip – and taken them back to the stone cottage, her tears running into Mary’s hair as she told them the story. The story of the merrow who longed for the sea. Who loved her girls but could not part them from each other. Who hoped that, one day, they might choose her world for themselves."（ch47）

## 节点八：母女相认与一句「闭上眼」（ch48）

**叙事概括：** 露西沿着姐姐二十年前走过的崖梯下到洞里，找到的是皮肤干裂、脖子上有鳃的杰丝，和一个蜷在角落的卡梅伦。她问出那个问题——「他是我父亲吗」——杰丝摇头；于是她把这二十年唯一没被任何人告诉过她的事实自己补完。随后杰丝把整段经过讲了一遍：她怎么在梦里看见这个洞穴，怎么坐大巴南下，怎么在这块石头上生下她，又怎么被迫把她交给自己的父母。最后露西问，你说要带我去海里，是不是想让我们淹死。杰丝摸着她的脸说：那不会叫淹死。闭上眼。

**关键引语：**

> ① "‘Was he,’ Lucy trembles as she forms the words in her mouth, ‘my father?’（ch48）

> ② "‘I got a coach to Sydney, another bus from Batemans Bay. I wanted to see it – Cliff House, Devil’s Lookout. The place I started from. I was in the house when the pain began. That’s when I heard them for the first time, singing. Like they were calling me. And then I was here, with the rocks cutting into my hands and the tide beating in my ears, and then I pushed and pushed and then you were here, too. The most perfect thing I’d ever seen. I remember your little hands,’ she clasps one between hers now, squeezing tight, ‘they were so tiny, but they seemed to contain all of me. My whole heart. I called you Lucy. I wanted you to have the sea in your name.’"（ch48）

> ③ "‘But – I don’t understand. What do you mean, you wanted to take me to the sea? Are you saying,’ Lucy licks her cracked lips, ‘are you saying that you wanted us to drown?’（ch48）

## 节点九：她杀了他，然后听见了歌（ch50）

**叙事概括：** 在洞里，卡梅伦拿刀抵住杰丝的喉咙，杰丝咬住他的小臂、夺下刀、把它送进他的腹部，再把他翻下洞口。她一直在等那首歌——她以为会由海里的她们唱起——但它没有来。她后来才听见：歌声来自她自己。她本可以就此留在水里，把岸上的一切放下；她确实开始清点自己舍不得的东西：画笔与亚麻籽油、梅乐蒂的笑、母亲在电话里的嗓音，以及再见到露西的可能。然后海面炸开——马克斯的船翻了，而露西已经朝他游了过去。

**关键引语：**

> ① "What does she care for the world above? For green-gold light, the prickly warmth of a cat on her lap, the sweet drift of banksia on the evening air?（ch50）

> ② "After, she’d lingered in the cave, hungry for the sea. Her body yearned for it, even as her mouth soured with the last of the biscuits they’d taken from the house, nothing to drink but plastic-tainted rainwater. But she’d been frightened to let go of herself, once and for all. Frightened that she would look up at the water’s surface with its elusive glitter of sun, tortured by all she’d left behind. The feeling of a brush in her hands, the smell of paint and linseed. Melody’s laugh; the gentle timbre of her mother’s voice on the phone.（ch50）

> ③ "And then a new sound. The crack of fibreglass on rock. The motor cuts out, and there is the suck of a boat turning on its side, rolled by a wave, and then something is falling through the water, wreathed in silver and white.（ch50）

## 节点十：四月的阳台（ch51-ch52）

**叙事概括：** 结局停在四个月后的日常：马克斯从手术里活下来，警方采信了「绑架」的版本，杰丝的鳃褪了、蹼收了，像有什么被水洗掉。露西要写的那本书不是自己的故事，是那艘船里八十个没有名字的女人。她一边看着窗外那条蓝色的海，一边知道自己仍会回到水里去。而全书最后退回一九八二年的厨房餐桌：罗伯特·威尔逊试着画下洞里的那个女人，发现自己画不出人的脸——他把那解释成一种魔法，一种他抓不住的魔法。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Lucy looks in the mirror, running her fingers through the bristles of her hair. It’s getting longer, now, the blonde strands wiry. She tilts her head to the side, watching the pulse beat in her neck. The flesh there is only faintly ribbed. She touches the tender skin with its light crust of salt. Out of the window, she sees the blue thread of the ocean through the trees. Waiting for her."（ch51）

> ② "Afterwards, as the night air dries her skin, she will sit here, on the veranda, and she will write. Of all the things they see in the shifting depths, where the Naiad lies broken on the ocean floor. The rusted ribs of chains, the rotted planks of the berths. Here a rosary, there the brass disc of a coin. And all around, the green silt of bones."（ch51）

> ③ "He longed to take some piece of her home, to feel that she was with him, somehow. Once, returning from the cave with her briny scent in his nose and the memory of her scaly skin against his, he’d tried to draw her. But the furtive scribblings at the kitchen table (he was terrified, always, that Judy would wake) did not do her justice. He had never been good at drawing people. Perhaps because their faces changed so much, or because they themselves were always changing. That was their magic, a magic he could not capture. When Robert drew a human face he felt as if he’d pinned a butterfly for study. As if he’d taken something that flickered with life and beauty and killed it."（ch52）
