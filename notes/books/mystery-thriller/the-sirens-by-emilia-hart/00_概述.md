# 《The Sirens》概述

## 全书梗概

**一、二〇一九年二月的一个凌晨，露西醒来时手还掐在一个男人的喉咙上。** "It's the scream that wakes her."（ch04）她记得自己骑在他身上、记得那股从身体深处升起来的力——
"She'd been strangling him."（ch04）而更让她恐惧的是，那不是一次失控，而像是身体替她做的决定：
- "And then this morning she’d found herself with her hands wrapped around Ben’s throat, like her body had made the decision for her."（ch05）
- 视频已经在网上传开，被羞辱的人成了被追责的人；"But the worst thing was the look on her face – the soft melt of trust."（ch05）
她唯一能想到的落脚处，是那个在距悉尼两百公里、以失踪案出名的小镇——她姐姐杰丝的房子。

**二、故事的另一端在一八〇一年的爱尔兰。** 玛丽与伊莱莎是一对双胞胎，母亲是当地人传说中的 merrow——从「浪下之地」上岸、又终究要回到海里的女人。玛丽从会说话起就在用语言替失明的妹妹造世界：
- "Since babyhood, Mary had painted pictures for her sister with words, brought the world to life inside her mind."（ch06）
- 她们原本只是那个夜晚去了不该去的溪边——那天是萨温节，人与灵的界限最薄："They were not supposed to have been at the stream that night. It had been Samhain, when the veil between the human world and the spirits’ stretched to its thinnest point."（ch09）
- ——结果撞上收租人伯恩，一块石头落下，两条命被改判成流放。法官的话很轻："Only. Such a small word for it."（ch06）
于是她们被塞进奈厄德号的女囚舱，前往新南威尔士。

**三、夹在中间的是杰丝的日记，写于一九九八年。** 十六岁的杰丝在艺术教室里被老师亨尼西一步步圈住；同一时期她的皮肤开始裂出银色的纹路，睡眠开始把她带进一个不属于她的洞穴。她在日记里写下过一个孩子能想到的最冷的一句推论——"Mum and Dad can both do it. But I can’t. So that means one of them can’t be my parent."（ch14）——也写下了自己第一次在课堂上听到那个名字时的熟悉感：
- "Jess’s art teacher, who’d offered to give her extra lessons. Mr Hennessey. The name is familiar somehow – and then she remembers. The library book she’d found while cleaning up her sister’s dining table – the one about surrealism – had last been checked out by someone called C. Hennessey. He must have given it to Jess when she was at school. Why has she kept it all this time?"（ch15）
- 她自己并不知道，她所做的梦正在被别人梦见：露西在二〇一九年梦见自己用爱尔兰语说话——"Waking in the night, the thin motel sheets twisted, she’d breathed the words into her iPhone, watching bleary-eyed as Siri produced a translation. Mo dheirfiúr Irish. My sister."（ch07）
——而那门语言她从未学过。

**四、杰丝的来处要再往回退十七年。** 一九八二年，悬崖屋的主人罗伯特·威尔逊在「魔鬼瞭望台」的洞里遇见了一个从水里浮出来的女人：
- "And then she’d surfaced, the gleaming impossibility of her, resting her elbows on the side of the boat.（ch52）
- 他后来一直无法判断她究竟美在哪一头——"He could not decide if she was beautiful in spite of her monstrosity, or because of it."（ch52）
- ——而他自己也说不清为什么会一次次回去。同一时期，他抱着一个湿襁褓从洞里走出来，把它交给妻子朱迪；镇上的人给这个孩子取名「希望宝宝」。此后四十年，这个名字一直是当地的伤口："It’s funny, the way some cases are forgotten, yet others live on in the public consciousness, the victims somehow immortal."（ch05）

**五、露西到了康伯湾，才开始把两条时间线并成一条。** 姐姐的房子里挂满了她梦里见过的画面——"The proud masts, the winged sails, diaphanous in the light. The figurehead at the bow – wooden curls tumbling over pert breasts, tail curved and glimmering like the body of a snake. A mermaid. It’s the same ship from her dream."（ch08）——而她从一本破锁的日记、一块 1981 年立起的石碑（
"This cairn was erected on 20 October 1981 by the Comber Bay Historical Society to commemorate the sinking 180 years ago of the convict ship Naiad, wrecked off the coast of Comber Bay with the loss of approximately 100 lives."（ch13）
）和旧报纸里拼出了第一层事实：
- 奈厄德号在 1801 年 4 月 11 日凌晨撞上礁石沉没，"At about 3 o’clock in the morning on 11 April, a ship was sighted a mile out to sea, impaled on the reef and sinking rapidly. Later that morning, a native woman discovered five bodies washed to shore."（ch20）
- 船上「约一百条命」被记进地方史，而其中一些女人据说没有死。镇上有人声称夜里在海滩上听见过歌声："‘I’m not saying I believe it. But I know people who say they’ve heard it, standing on the beach at night.’ He pauses, looks back at her. ‘Voices, coming from the waves. Women’s voices.’"（ch16）
- 梅乐蒂后来把这件事从头讲给她听：1981 年，她十七岁，被一个叫丹尼的本地男孩带进那个洞穴，"‘He kissed me, pushed me to the ground, and I remember thinking gross at the slimy feel of the rocks, when I should have been thinking about how to get him off me, how to get away. His hands moved to my waistband and then inside my trousers. It hurt and I told him to stop, to get off me.（ch31）
男孩在她面前僵住、然后走向洞口，再没有回来。

**六、露西真正想问的不是传说，是身世。** 她从收养文件里读出两个名字：
- "Her mother isn’t Maggie Martin, but Judith Wilson. Her father isn’t Michael Martin, but Robert Wilson. The man who found Baby Hope at Devil’s Lookout and offered her to his wife, as if she was some treasure he’d dredged up; a pearl or an abalone shell."（ch26）
- 她把这两句并列摆开，等于亲手拆掉了自己二十年来的家："All these years, her family has been nothing more than a story. A collage of half-truths and lies.（ch26）
- 她打电话给母亲，第一句话就跳过所有铺垫——"‘I already know everything, Mum. I’ve been reading Jess’s diary. I know that Jess is Baby Hope.’（ch33）
- 而在悬崖屋，杰丝正在等另一个人：深夜敲门的是被学校停职、被妻子赶出门的卡梅伦·亨尼西，他说的第一句是求救，"‘Jessie, thank God,’ he murmurs through the door. ‘Please, Jess. Let me in.’（ch40）
也是自辩。

**七、故事的高潮在同一个洞里，分两百年上演。** 杰丝把卡梅伦骗进「魔鬼瞭望台」下方的洞穴，走的是二十年前自己走过的那段崖梯：
- "Her heart thuds as she approaches the sea, the lip of rock that curves around the cliff to the cave. Twenty years have passed and yet it is unchanged. The morning shimmers in the rockpools, and she imagines the creatures lurking beneath. Starfish, crabs, the furling tentacles of a blue-ringed octopus. She presses a hand to her abdomen and remembers the weight of her child inside, those final precious moments they shared together before the cut of the motor through the air, the hospital. The woman with the narrow features, the faceless horror of her words. Foster care."（ch42）
- 洞里，她第一次把一九九九年那间画室里发生的事说出了口；而在一八〇一年，玛丽与伊莱莎的奈厄德号正在同一片礁石上裂开——"The night before they were due to make landfall at New South Wales, Mary woke to a strange sound. A violent tearing, like the rending of time itself. Then, as if clasped by great hands, she was wrenched from her berth, her skull cracking against the wall opposite."（ch47）
- 玛丽在水中长出鳃与蹼，本该就此回到海里，却在转身之前看见了囚舱里还在呼吸的人："She closed her eyes, feeling the sea move around her. Waiting. Then there it was: something fluttering through the water, like a kick.（ch47）

**八、结尾把「血缘」与「归属」这两件事分开来称重。** 露西从崖梯下到洞里，见到的是二十年没见过她、也二十年不敢看她的母亲；杰丝把当年的整段经过讲了一遍，包括那个名字的来历——"‘I got a coach to Sydney, another bus from Batemans Bay. I wanted to see it – Cliff House, Devil’s Lookout. The place I started from. I was in the house when the pain began. That’s when I heard them for the first time, singing. Like they were calling me. And then I was here, with the rocks cutting into my hands and the tide beating in my ears, and then I pushed and pushed and then you were here, too. The most perfect thing I’d ever seen. I remember your little hands,’ she clasps one between hers now, squeezing tight, ‘they were so tiny, but they seemed to contain all of me. My whole heart. I called you Lucy. I wanted you to have the sea in your name.’"（ch48）——而卡梅伦死在了洞里。随后马克斯的船在风暴中撞上礁石，露西和杰丝一起把他托出水面。全书最后停在四月的日常：
- "Afterwards, as the night air dries her skin, she will sit here, on the veranda, and she will write. Of all the things they see in the shifting depths, where the Naiad lies broken on the ocean floor. The rusted ribs of chains, the rotted planks of the berths. Here a rosary, there the brass disc of a coin. And all around, the green silt of bones."（ch51）
- 露西要写的是那艘沉船里没有名字的八十个女人，而在更早的一九八二年，罗伯特·威尔逊已经替这本书写下过一句关于「占有」的注脚："He longed to take some piece of her home, to feel that she was with him, somehow. Once, returning from the cave with her briny scent in his nose and the memory of her scaly skin against his, he’d tried to draw her. But the furtive scribblings at the kitchen table (he was terrified, always, that Judy would wake) did not do her justice. He had never been good at drawing people. Perhaps because their faces changed so much, or because they themselves were always changing. That was their magic, a magic he could not capture. When Robert drew a human face he felt as if he’d pinned a butterfly for study. As if he’d taken something that flickered with life and beauty and killed it."（ch52）

## 三个主题

**一、血缘会撒谎，归属不会。** 露西的二十年被两套文件同时定义：
- 一套说她姓马丁、是杰丝的妹妹，另一套说她是「希望宝宝」、生母另有其人。她最终拒绝的正是「用血缘重新命名自己」这件事："Tears sting at the thought and she realises that she can’t accept it. It doesn’t matter to Lucy that they didn’t come from the same womb, that they didn’t journey into the world from the same place. They might not have grown up together, but they were raised in the same home, by the same parents, with the same rules and rituals. The same mysteries."（ch28）
- 本书对「家」的定义不是出生地，而是一起守过的规矩与谜团——她在悬崖屋认出母亲的喂鸟器、母亲藏在屋檐下的旧杂志，靠的不是基因，是痕迹："She is sitting on the sagging couch in the living room of Cliff House. Cliff House, where her parents had once lived. Had her father sat at the worn kitchen table, pencils and paper spread out around him? Had her mother chopped vegetables in the tiny yellow kitchen, humming as she looked out to sea? It was her mother’s bird-feeder that hung from the tree outside, her mother’s magazines she’d found dust-choked in the eaves."（ch28）

**二、愤怒是一种被继承下来的东西。** 玛丽把母亲走进海里的那一年锁进心里，换来的是一条家族公式：
- "Over the years, Da’s anger became Mary’s own. She locked her memories of her mother deep inside her heart. It was easier, she learned, to be angry than to be sad."（ch12）
- 露西这一代拿到的版本是另一种形状——她掐住本的喉咙那一下，"And then this morning she’d found herself with her hands wrapped around Ben’s throat, like her body had made the decision for her."（ch05）
- 以及杰丝在洞里对卡梅伦说的那段话。这条线的出口不是复仇，而是玛丽在一八〇一年做出的那个选择：她本来可以走，却选择留下来照看后来者——"She had to find a way to reach them. To show them that despite the new strangeness of her body – the frilled flesh of her neck, the fins – she was still Mary. Mary who had bled and cried with them, who had sung with them in the dark.（ch49）
- ——并把「守护」当成了母亲身份的新定义："But now another purpose hummed inside her. For while there were some men like Da, too many were like Aoife’s husband, like Byrne. Like the captain.（ch49）

**三、水是威胁，也是家。** 对露西，水从小就是敌人：
- "The aquagenic urticaria was diagnosed when she was still a baby; she can’t remember a time when water wasn’t a threat. Over the years she has reached a kind of acceptance, aided by strict routines and rituals. She keeps her hair short, she uses wet wipes instead of showering – though even that small amount of moisture can trigger an attack. She does not think of how it might feel to swim."（ch10）
- 她因此练出一整套绕开自己身体的生活；对杰丝，水是羞耻的见证（皮肤裂开、梦游、洞穴生产）；对玛丽与伊莱莎，水先是诅咒——村里的溪水会在皮肤上烧出白痕——后是唯一的归宿。全书把「水」的两义性写到了宗教的密度：岸上的人把失踪的男人记成纪念碑，"‘I’m not a churchgoing man,’ he says finally. ‘But I believe in God, and doing what’s right. Over a hundred lives were lost that day. Every soul deserves a proper resting place. A marker.’"（ch18）
- 海里的人则把那句「我们」说成誓言："But now another purpose hummed inside her. For while there were some men like Da, too many were like Aoife’s husband, like Byrne. Like the captain.（ch49）

## 人物弧光

**露西·马丁 / Lucy——**

- 起点："But who would believe her, especially after what happened?"（ch04）
- 转向："She traces a circle on the back of one hand. She has felt Eliza’s fingers in hers, the damp heat of her skin. Mary’s memories have become her own: the rich gold smell of farls cooking above the hearth fire, the bite of wet rock against her cheek."（ch20）
- 最低点："She does not let herself think about the man who might be her father, about the ring that shimmers on the coffee table. At least, she will not think of him in those terms."（ch44）
- 落点："Lucy looks in the mirror, running her fingers through the bristles of her hair. It’s getting longer, now, the blonde strands wiry. She tilts her head to the side, watching the pulse beat in her neck. The flesh there is only faintly ribbed. She touches the tender skin with its light crust of salt. Out of the window, she sees the blue thread of the ocean through the trees. Waiting for her."（ch51）
- 她的整条线是「从被保护的人变成去接人的人」：她先是在梦里替别人受苦，后来沿着崖梯走下去，把母亲和马克斯一个一个托出水面。

**杰丝 / Jessica Martin——**

- 起点："But another, louder part of me feels like something is wrong, like my parents are keeping something from me. And the more I try to ignore that feeling, to swallow it down, the more it grows and grows, swelling like a parasite in my gut."（ch14）
- 转向："I moved my hand so that my fingertips touched his.（ch32）
- 最低点："She began to crawl towards the lip of the cave, moving towards the women’s voices, towards the water. It was only then that she realised the singing was gone, that it had been replaced by a new sound, a wrong sound. A manmade sound, cutting through the drift of sea and sky. A motor.（ch36）
- 落点："And Jess is kicking hard, catching him around his waist and pushing him up, up, up, towards light and air.（ch50）
- 她被写过两次：一次是十六岁日记里那个被成年人圈住的女孩，一次是十九岁在洞里独自生下孩子的母亲。她这辈子做的唯一一个真正属于自己的决定，是把卡梅伦带进洞里。

**玛丽与伊莱莎 / Mary and Eliza Kissane——**

- 起点："Her sister’s hand brushed hers, the fingers warm and soft. Mary tried to clasp them in her own but was prevented by the bite of steel shackles at her wrist."（ch06）
- 中段："The soft, wet thwack of rock on bone; his body slumping over her; lips slack and drooling in her hair. She could see the moon again, and in its yellow light Eliza, reddened stone trembling in her hand.（ch30）
- 落点："The reef reminded Mary of the forest in spring, teeming with colour and movement. Bristles of pink and red coral moved gently with the current. Fish shimmered past. A dark shadow lifted itself from the seabed and floated away.（ch49）
- 两百年里她们从「被判流放的姐妹」变成「这片海岸的守护者」；玛丽的转变不是变成怪物，而是决定不去报复岸上的世界，只把力气留给后来落进这个洞穴的女孩："The moon glimmered on the waves, debris crashing against the rocks. The coast rose behind them, a dark curve. Mary felt a power humming from it. She saw eyes burning bright in a great forest, faces framed by pale trees. The land had hidden her people in her belly for thousands of years, but now the English – men like the captain and his officers – had come to split her open. Mary would have no part in it, she decided. She would not venture beyond the rocks and cave in the cliffs. She would leave the land and her people alone."（ch49）
