# Chapter 46

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：**沉船之夜。** Mary 与 Eliza 已在水下变形，**她们游回半沉的船舱去救 Bridie、Sarah 与 Annie**——**而 Bridie 以为见到的是怪物，直到听见那首民谣**。**她们把三人托上临时木筏**，**Sarah 用爱尔兰语向沉船方向道别**（"Slán abhaile"）。**姐妹二人潜入礁石间，第一次看见海底世界的丰饶**；**Mary 向 Eliza 道歉**（当年"是我不愿离开 Da"其实是她替妹妹背下的谎），**并宣告：她不回去了**。**她想着那个在洞里生产的女孩、以及将来所有会来这里的女孩——她要做她们的母亲**。**末句 "I promise."**
- **视角**：**第三人称限知（Mary），全章为 1801 线的收束章**；**中段出现一段无引号的心声对谈**（Mary 与 Eliza 在水下以心念交流，全书唯一一次）
- **情感弧线位置**：**Mary 线的终点**——**恐惧（Bridie 视她们为怪物）→ 被认出（歌声）→ 救援 → 姐妹和解 → 立志（做所有女孩的母亲）**
- **人物弧线**：**Mary 从"被救的人"变成"救人的母亲"**——**她放弃陆地、放弃回到 Da 身边，选择 Mam 的世界**；**Eliza 的旧谎被揭开**（她其实才是当年害怕离开的那个）
- **叙事手法**：**以"救援行动"的紧张节奏包裹"身份宣告"**；**水下的心念对谈用无引号排版与上面的对话区分**；**末段把 Mary 的个人选择升格为跨两百年的承诺**（直通 2019 线的 Jess 与 Lucy）

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bubbles streamed past her, whirling white.
>
> Eliza. She was there, the fins along her spine shifting and bristling, striped like a tiger in a story from the East."

- **中文理解**：**气泡从她身边流过，旋成一片白。**
  **Eliza。她在那儿，脊背上的鳍片移动、竖立，像东方故事里的老虎那样带着条纹。**
- **关键词**：Bubbles streamed past her, whirling white / the fins along her spine shifting and bristling / striped like a tiger in a story from the East
- **为什么这样写**：**开篇第一句就确认变形已完成**——**"旋成一片白"（whirling white）是水下视角**，**与陆地上的风、沙、绳索是两套感官**。**"东方故事里的老虎"（a tiger in a story from the East）是全书最奇异的一次比喻**：**它把 Eliza 写成异域的、危险的、被讲述的存在**——**而"故事里的"（in a story）提示她已进入传说**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：这一章开篇没有任何过渡**——**接 ch47 末 Mary 在水下呼救的那一刻**。

> **原句 2:** "The air bubble was quickly getting smaller: Bridie and Sarah pressed their faces against the dank wood of the hull, their fingers white as they gripped the planks. Annie curled into her mother’s chest, taking weak breaths of the dwindling air."

- **中文理解**：**气穴正在迅速变小：Bridie 和 Sarah 把脸贴在船壳潮湿的木板上，手指因紧抓木板而发白**。**Annie 蜷在母亲胸前，从越来越少的空气里虚弱地呼吸。**
- **关键词**：The air bubble was quickly getting smaller / pressed their faces against the dank wood of the hull / their fingers white as they gripped the planks / Annie curled into her mother’s chest / taking weak breaths of the dwindling air
- **为什么这样写**：**"气穴在变小"（The air bubble was quickly getting smaller）把死亡写成几何**：**一个不断缩小的空间**。**"手指发白"（fingers white）与"潮湿的木板"（dank wood）是全书囚舱段最典型的一组触觉**。**"越来越少的空气"（the dwindling air）用现在进行时**，**读者与她们同步缺氧**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请把"蜷在母亲胸前"（curled into her mother’s chest）记住**——**本章末 Mary 立志"做所有女孩的母亲"，第一个就是 Annie**。

> **原句 3:** "The women flinched as they approached, their feet treading the water in a frenzy of panic. They were afraid, Mary realised. She and Eliza appeared to them as monsters."

- **中文理解**：**她们靠近时，女人们畏缩了，双脚在水里慌乱地踩踏**。**Mary 明白了：她们害怕。她和 Eliza 在她们眼里是怪物。**
- **关键词**：The women flinched as they approached / their feet treading the water in a frenzy of panic / They were afraid, Mary realised / She and Eliza appeared to them as monsters.
- **为什么这样写**：**"怪物"（monsters）是 Mary 第一次从朋友的眼睛里看自己**——**全书的变形在此刻第一次有了社会代价**。**"Mary 明白了"（Mary realised）用一个短句完成视角内转**：**她不再描述自己，她描述别人眼中的自己**。**"慌乱地踩踏"（a frenzy of panic）是缺氧者最后的动作**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：救援的第一道障碍不是水，是身份**——**她必须先被认出来，才能被接受**。

> **原句 4:** "‘Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death …’
>
> ‘Don’t you know me, Bridie? Take my hand. You have to come. If you stay here, you’ll die.’
>
> But still Bridie’s eyes glittered with fear."

- **中文理解**：「**天主之母，请为我们罪人祈祷，现在以及我们死亡的时刻……**」
  「**你不认识我了吗，Bridie？抓住我的手。你得来。你留在这里会死的。**」
  **但 Bridie 的眼睛里仍然闪着恐惧。**
- **关键词**：Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death / Don’t you know me, Bridie? / If you stay here, you’ll die. / But still Bridie’s eyes glittered with fear.
- **为什么这样写**：**Bridie 用祷词回应 Mary**——**她把朋友当成了需要驱赶的东西**。**"你不认识我了吗"（Don’t you know me）是本章的核心问句**：**与 2019 线 Lucy 的"你为什么不要我"同构**——**都是被爱的人要求被认出**。**"眼睛闪着恐惧"（glittered with fear）重复出现，形成节奏**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请记住这句祷词**——**本章后段 Bridie 会说"我们有旧神要谢"，她放弃了这句祷词**。

> **原句 5:** "She had to find a way to reach them. To show them that despite the new strangeness of her body – the frilled flesh of her neck, the fins – she was still Mary. Mary who had bled and cried with them, who had sung with them in the dark.
>
> ‘You should have known me before,’ she sang, the water turning her voice eerie. ‘I sang you to sleep and I robbed you of wealth …’
>
> Eliza surfaced next to her, and together they sang the last line.
>
> ‘And again I’m a maid on the shore ’"

- **中文理解**：**她得想办法触到她们**。**让她们看到，尽管她的身体变得陌生——颈上起褶的肉、那些鳍——她仍然是 Mary。Mary，那个和她们一起流血、一起哭、一起在黑暗里唱歌的 Mary。**
  「**你们本该在从前就认识我，**」她唱道，**水把她的声音变得怪异**。「**我唱你入睡，我夺走你的财富……**」
  **Eliza 在她身旁浮出水面，两人一起唱出最后一行。**
  「**而我又成了岸上的少女**」
- **关键词**：she was still Mary / Mary who had bled and cried with them, who had sung with them in the dark / You should have known me before / the water turning her voice eerie / I sang you to sleep and I robbed you of wealth / together they sang the last line / And again I’m a maid on the shore
- **为什么这样写**：**"仍然是 Mary"（she was still Mary）用重复的名字完成身份辩护**——**而她的证据不是外貌，是共同的经历**。**"水把她的声音变得怪异"（the water turning her voice eerie）——**歌是她的武器，但水改变了它**。**姐妹二人合唱最后一行**：**这支民谣第一次由两个 merrow 一起唱完**（ch34 是 Eliza 独唱救人，ch36 是 Jess 唱给自己）。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：认出她的不是脸，是歌**——**全书反复确认：歌是这一族唯一的身份证明**。

> **原句 6:** "‘God help me,’ Bridie breathed. ‘It is you. Both of you.’ ‘Take a breath,’ Eliza said. ‘Take a breath and don’t let go.’"

- **中文理解**：「**上帝救我，**」Bridie 低声说，「**是你们。你们两个。**」「**吸一口气，**」Eliza 说，「**吸一口气，别松手。**」
- **关键词**：God help me / It is you. Both of you. / Take a breath / Take a breath and don’t let go.
- **为什么这样写**：**"上帝救我"与前面的祷词是同一套语汇，但用途反转**：**从驱邪变成认亲**。**Eliza 的两句指令极其简短**——**在水下，语言必须压缩到动作**。**"别松手"（don’t let go）是本章后续所有救援的动作纲领**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：说话人是 Eliza，不是 Mary**——**救援中 Mary 负责唱，Eliza 负责指挥**。

> **原句 7:** "Bridie wrapped her legs around Mary’s waist, as if she were a little child. Eliza took Sarah’s weight, Annie tucked safe between their bodies, her mother stroking her hair to soothe her whimpering. ‘You’ve just got to be my very good girl for a moment longer, a linbh,’ she gasped."

- **中文理解**：**Bridie 把双腿缠在 Mary 腰上，像个小孩子**。**Eliza 托住 Sarah，Annie 被安全地夹在她们身体之间，母亲抚着她的头发安抚她的呜咽**。「**你只要再当一会儿我的乖女孩，a linbh，**」她喘着气说。
- **关键词**：wrapped her legs around Mary’s waist, as if she were a little child / Annie tucked safe between their bodies / stroking her hair to soothe her whimpering / my very good girl for a moment longer, a linbh
- **为什么这样写**：**"像个小孩子"（as if she were a little child）——**成年女人在获救的一刻退化成孩子**，**而 Mary 成了母亲**：**这个动作预演了本章末的誓言**。**"a linbh"（我的孩子）是 Sarah 对 Annie 的称呼，与 Mam 对 Mary 的称呼同词**。**"再当一会儿乖女孩"（a moment longer）把逃生切成可承受的小块**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：这里的母亲是 Sarah**——**本章末 Mary 要接过的，正是这个位置**。

> **原句 8:** "The moon glimmered on the waves, debris crashing against the rocks. The coast rose behind them, a dark curve. Mary felt a power humming from it. She saw eyes burning bright in a great forest, faces framed by pale trees. The land had hidden her people in her belly for thousands of years, but now the English – men like the captain and his officers – had come to split her open. Mary would have no part in it, she decided. She would not venture beyond the rocks and cave in the cliffs. She would leave the land and her people alone."

- **中文理解**：**月亮在浪上闪着微光，碎片撞在岩石上**。**海岸在她们身后升起，一道黑色的弧线**。**Mary 感到一股力量从那里嗡鸣**。**她看见一片大森林里燃烧的眼睛，被苍白树木框住的脸**。**这片土地把她的族人藏在腹中数千年，但现在英国人——像船长和他那些军官的男人——来把她剖开**。**Mary 决定，她绝不参与其中**。**她不会越过礁石和崖中的洞穴**。**她要让土地和她的人各自安好。**
- **关键词**：The moon glimmered on the waves, debris crashing against the rocks / The coast rose behind them, a dark curve / Mary felt a power humming from it / eyes burning bright in a great forest, faces framed by pale trees / The land had hidden her people in her belly for thousands of years / had come to split her open / She would not venture beyond the rocks and cave in the cliffs. She would leave the land and her people alone.
- **为什么这样写**：**"土地把族人藏在腹中"（hidden her people in her belly）——**这是全书最重要的一次政治隐喻**：**土地是母体，殖民是剖腹**（对照 ch31 的 "What’s two hundred fucking years to more than sixty thousand?"）。**"藏在腹中数千年"把原住民的存在写成时间的深度，而英国人只有两百年**。**"绝不参与其中"（would have no part in it）是 Mary 的第一次政治选择**——**而她选择的是不越界，不是复仇**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：她划定的边界是"礁石与崖中的洞穴"**——**正是两百年后 Jess 生产的那个洞**。

> **原句 9:** "She hooked her arm around Bridie’s waist and swam backwards, letting her friend feel the night on her face, the cool air in her lungs. Tears shone in Bridie’s eyes as she took ragged, gulping breaths.
>
> Mary leaned down and placed a kiss on her forehead."

- **中文理解**：**她用手臂勾住 Bridie 的腰，向后游，让朋友感到脸上的夜色、肺里凉爽的空气**。**Bridie 眼里闪着泪，呼吸粗重、一口口吞咽。**
  **Mary 俯身在她额上吻了一下。**
- **关键词**：swam backwards, letting her friend feel the night on her face, the cool air in her lungs / Tears shone in Bridie’s eyes as she took ragged, gulping breaths / placed a kiss on her forehead
- **为什么这样写**：**"向后游"（swam backwards）——**她面朝朋友，背向深处**：**这是母亲抱孩子的姿势**。**"脸上的夜色"（the night on her face）——**救人先救的是感官，不是身体**。**额上的一吻（a kiss on her forehead）是本章唯一一次 Mary 主动的亲密动作**——**它在"救"之后，是确认而非安抚**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请把这一吻与 ch47 末她"转身离开 Eliza 去救人"并读**：**同一个决定的温柔与残酷两面**。

> **原句 10:** "While the others clung to a floating barrel, Mary and Eliza gathered as many planks of wood as they could find, lashing them together with seaweed and torn lengths of canvas sail. By the time the raft was finished, the horizon was beginning to pale, the stars fading."

- **中文理解**：**其他人抱着一只漂浮的木桶，Mary 和 Eliza 尽可能多地收集木板，用海草和撕开的帆布把它们绑在一起**。**等木筏做好时，地平线开始发白，星星在淡去。**
- **关键词**：clung to a floating barrel / gathered as many planks of wood as they could find / lashing them together with seaweed and torn lengths of canvas sail / the horizon was beginning to pale, the stars fading
- **为什么这样写**：**"用海草和撕开的帆布"（with seaweed and torn lengths of canvas sail）——**她们用海的材料和船的材料造筏**：**两种世界的第一次合作**。**"地平线发白、星星淡去"（the horizon was beginning to pale）标记时间的流逝**——**她们在水里干了整夜的活**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：木筏是她们唯一一次"造物"**——**此后的她们不再使用工具**。

> **原句 11:** "‘Do you think anybody else …’ Bridie started but did not finish. The question hung in the air.
>
> Mary thought of what she’d seen as she swam through the sinking ship. The limp flower of a hand; the strands of hair moving with the current."

- **中文理解**：「**你觉得还有别人……**」Bridie 开了口，但没有说完。**问题悬在空气里。**
  **Mary 想起她游过沉船时看到的：一只手的软弱的花；随水流动的发丝。**
- **关键词**：Bridie started but did not finish. The question hung in the air. / what she’d seen as she swam through the sinking ship / The limp flower of a hand; the strands of hair moving with the current.
- **为什么这样写**：**"没有说完"（did not finish）与"悬在空气里"（hung in the air）**——**作者拒绝写出那个问句，因为答案已经在 Mary 的回忆里**。**"一只手的软弱的花"（The limp flower of a hand）把尸体写成花**——**这是全书对死亡最抒情、也最克制的一次**。**"随水流动的发丝"（strands of hair moving with the current）只给局部，不给全貌**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：Mary 没有回答 Bridie**——**沉默就是回答**。

> **原句 12:** "Bridie reached forward, stroked Mary’s gills with gentle fingers. ‘I think we have the old gods to thank for this,’ she said softly. ‘And so I’m not much of one for praying, just now. Let us bid our friends farewell, instead.’"

- **中文理解**：**Bridie 向前伸手，用温柔的手指抚摸 Mary 的鳃**。「**我想我们得谢那些旧神，**」她轻声说，「**所以我现在不太想祈祷。我们还是向朋友们道别吧。**」
- **关键词**：stroked Mary’s gills with gentle fingers / we have the old gods to thank for this / I’m not much of one for praying, just now / Let us bid our friends farewell, instead.
- **为什么这样写**：**"抚摸 Mary 的鳃"（stroked Mary’s gills）——**恐惧的器官变成了被抚摸的地方**：**这是本章最完整的一次和解**。**"旧神"（the old gods）与开篇的祷词形成闭环**：**她换了信仰，因为信仰没能解释眼前的事**。**"道别"（bid our friends farewell）代替祈祷**——**爱尔兰的丧仪传统（caoineadh）在海上被简化成一句话**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：这是全书唯一一次有人以善意触碰鳃**——**2019 线 Lucy 做的是同一件事**。

> **原句 13:** "‘Slán abhaile,’ she said. ‘Slán abhaile.’
>
> As the sun rose in the sky, Bridie, Sarah and Annie rested, their bodies curled into each other on the makeshift raft."

- **中文理解**：「**Slán abhaile，**」她说。「**Slán abhaile。**」（**爱尔兰语：一路平安地回家**）
  **太阳升上天空时，Bridie、Sarah 和 Annie 歇下了，身体在临时木筏上互相蜷在一起。**
- **关键词**：Slán abhaile / As the sun rose in the sky / their bodies curled into each other on the makeshift raft
- **为什么这样写**：**"Slán abhaile"（平安回家）重复两遍，且不加翻译**——**作者让读者自己去感受这个词的重量**：**它既是道别，也是祝愿，而她们已经无家可回**。**"互相蜷在一起"（curled into each other）与开篇 Annie"蜷在母亲胸前"是同一个动作**——**恐惧的姿势变成了安睡的姿势**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：这句话是对着沉船方向说的**——**被道别的不是活着的人**。

> **原句 14:** "The reef reminded Mary of the forest in spring, teeming with colour and movement. Bristles of pink and red coral moved gently with the current. Fish shimmered past. A dark shadow lifted itself from the seabed and floated away.
>
> She had never seen so much beauty. Her eyes ached with looking and yet she hardly dared blink. It was as if, for all these years, she had been living with a great thirst, and at last was able to slake it."

- **中文理解**：**礁石让 Mary 想起春天的森林，满是色彩与动静**。**粉红与红色的珊瑚须随水流轻轻摆动**。**鱼群闪着光游过**。**一道黑影从海床上升起，飘走了。**
  **她从没见过这么多美**。**她的眼睛看得发疼，却几乎不敢眨眼**。**仿佛这些年来她一直带着巨大的渴，此刻终于得以解渴。**
- **关键词**：reminded Mary of the forest in spring, teeming with colour and movement / Bristles of pink and red coral moved gently with the current / A dark shadow lifted itself from the seabed and floated away / Her eyes ached with looking and yet she hardly dared blink / she had been living with a great thirst, and at last was able to slake it.
- **为什么这样写**：**"春天的森林"（the forest in spring）——**海底被写成陆地**：**她的两个世界终于重叠**。**"看得发疼却不敢眨眼"（ached with looking…hardly dared blink）把美写成一种疼痛**。**"巨大的渴终于解渴"（a great thirst…slake it）回收了全书所有关于"渴"的意象**（ch37 的喉渴、ch41 的"没有足够的水喝"、ch48 的"全身都在渴"）——**渴是她们的血统，而这里是解药**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：这一整段是 Mary 第一次主动描述美**——**此前她的视角只有囚舱与尸体**。

> **原句 15:** "I wish you could see this, she said.
>
> Eliza turned to her, and there was something different in her face, something certain in her eyes.
>
> I can, she answered. I have always seen this. This moment. Us, changed by the sea. I spent so much time dreaming of it, longing for it. For the tír fo thuinn. The land beneath the waves."

- **中文理解**：**真希望你能看见这个，她说。**
  **Eliza 转向她，她脸上有什么不同了，眼里有一种笃定。**
  **我能看见，她回答。我一直都看见这个。这一刻。我们，被海改变。我花了那么多时间去梦见它、渴望它。为了 tír fo thuinn。浪下的土地。**
- **关键词**：I wish you could see this / something different in her face, something certain in her eyes / I have always seen this. This moment. / Us, changed by the sea. / For the tír fo thuinn. The land beneath the waves.
- **为什么这样写**：**无引号排版（she said / she answered）把这段与水面上的对话区分开**——**心念不需要引号，因为它是直接的**。**"我一直都看见这个"（I have always seen this）把 Eliza 写成预言者**：**她渴望的不是自由，是成为**。**"tír fo thuinn"（浪下之地）是爱尔兰神话里的海底世界**——**而 Eliza 把它当成应许之地**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：这段是全书唯一一次姐妹直接以心念交谈**——**而 ch48 的母女之间也是靠"歌在骨头里嗡鸣"沟通**。

> **原句 16:** "I am sorry for the things I said. That night we were arrested. I am sorry that I didn’t want to talk about Mam. That I didn’t believe you. And that – and that I made us stay behind, all those years ago.
>
> Eliza smiled, tears glinting in her eyes.
>
> But you didn’t, she said. I was the one who was frightened, who did not want to go."

- **中文理解**：**我为我说过的话道歉。我们被抓的那晚。我为我不愿谈 Mam 道歉。为我不信你。还有——还有我为当年让我们留了下来。**
  **Eliza 笑了，眼里闪着泪。**
  **但你没有，她说。我才是那个害怕的人，那个不想走的人。**
- **关键词**：I am sorry for the things I said. That night we were arrested. / I didn’t want to talk about Mam. That I didn’t believe you. / I made us stay behind, all those years ago. / But you didn’t, she said. I was the one who was frightened, who did not want to go.
- **为什么这样写**：**Mary 的道歉由四个短句组成，每个都指向一个具体的旧账**——**这是全书对姐妹旧怨最完整的一次清算**。**"但你没有"（But you didn’t）用一句反驳推翻多年的自责**——**而反转的事实是：Mary 替妹妹背下了这个谎**（下一块说明）。**"眼里闪着泪"（tears glinting in her eyes）与 ch43 Aoife 葬礼上的泪是同一个写法**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：这段道歉与 ch11/ch12 的旧账直接对应**——**读者等了三十多章的一句和解**。

> **原句 17:** "I had told you that I didn’t want to leave Da. But you knew I would not tell Mam; you knew how much I longed to please her. And so you pretended that it was you – that you were the one who was afraid. You did that for me."

- **中文理解**：**我告诉过你我不想离开 Da。但你知道我不会告诉 Mam；你知道我多渴望让她高兴。所以你假装是你——你才是那个害怕的人。你为我做了那件事。**
- **关键词**：I had told you that I didn’t want to leave Da / you knew how much I longed to please her / you pretended that it was you – that you were the one who was afraid / You did that for me.
- **为什么这样写**：**"你为我做了那件事"（You did that for me）是全章最重的一句**——**它把 Mary 多年的自我责备重新定义为一次牺牲**。**"我多渴望让她高兴"（how much I longed to please her）解释了 Eliza 的性格**：**她一生都在为 Mam 的目光而活**（ch12/ch14 的日记线）。**"假装是你"（you pretended that it was you）——**谎言是保护性的，而保护的对象是妹妹的自尊。
- **读者视角提示**：**请把这一块与 ch14 的"卷舌遗传实验"并读**：**同一对姐妹，一个说谎保护，一个用科学求真**。

> **原句 18:** "But then she thought of Da. The comfort of his voice, his stories. The way he had tried so hard to protect them – from men like Byrne, but also from themselves.
>
> But hadn’t he taken them from the sea, from their birth right? Hadn’t he deprived them of the chance to choose?"

- **中文理解**：**但接着她想起了 Da。他声音里的安慰，他的故事**。**他那么努力地保护她们——不受 Byrne 那样的人伤害，也不受她们自己伤害。**
  **但他不也把她们从海里、从她们与生俱来的权利里带走了吗？他不也剥夺了她们选择的机会吗？**
- **关键词**：The comfort of his voice, his stories / The way he had tried so hard to protect them – from men like Byrne, but also from themselves. / But hadn’t he taken them from the sea, from their birth right? / Hadn’t he deprived them of the chance to choose?
- **为什么这样写**：**"也不受她们自己伤害"（but also from themselves）是最锋利的一刀**——**Da 的保护本身就是一种囚禁**。**两个反问句（hadn’t he…? / Hadn’t he…?）构成对父爱的审判**——**而答案由 Mary 自己给出**（下一块）。**"与生俱来的权利"（birth right）把海洋写成遗产**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：Da 是全书唯一被正面描写的男性**——**作者仍要他为"保护"付出代价**。

> **原句 19:** "She knew that she would miss Da for every day that remained to her. The currents would change and the coral would grow and she would think of him and worry. Whether he was ill, whether he was warm and fed. Who would wash his body and light the candles after he died."

- **中文理解**：**她知道，在她余下的每一天里，她都会想念 Da**。**洋流会改变，珊瑚会生长，而她会想起他、担心他**。**他是否病了，是否穿得暖、吃得饱**。**他死后，谁会为他洗身、点蜡烛。**
- **关键词**：she would miss Da for every day that remained to her / The currents would change and the coral would grow and she would think of him and worry / Whether he was ill, whether he was warm and fed / Who would wash his body and light the candles after he died.
- **为什么这样写**：**"余下的每一天"（every day that remained to her）与"洋流会改变、珊瑚会生长"并置**——**她的时间尺度已经被海改写**。**"谁会为他洗身、点蜡烛"（Who would wash his body and light the candles）回照 ch43 她们为 Aoife 做的仪式**：**她无法为父亲做这件事，这是她选择的代价**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：她没有后悔**——**她只是把代价算清楚了**。

> **原句 20:** "But now another purpose hummed inside her. For while there were some men like Da, too many were like Aoife’s husband, like Byrne. Like the captain.
>
> The captain, like Wright and the other sailors of the Naiad, was gone, lost to the waves. She thought of his bones crumbling on the seabed, becoming nothing. There would be other men like him, she knew. Whole ships of them ploughed the waves even now, bringing fear and violence to this unfamiliar land. Mary felt the strange new muscles in her body clench, felt the spines bristle on her back. Her blood sang with fury."

- **中文理解**：**但现在另一个目的在她体内嗡鸣**。**因为虽然有些男人像 Da，但太多人像 Aoife 的丈夫、像 Byrne。像船长。**
  **船长，像 Wright 和 Naiad 号其他水手一样，已经消失，被浪带走**。**她想象他的骨头在海床上碎裂、化为乌有**。**她知道，还会有像他一样的男人**。**整船整船的人此刻正在破浪前行，把恐惧与暴力带到这片陌生的土地**。**Mary 感到身体里那些陌生的新肌肉绷紧，感到背上的脊刺竖起。她的血在愤怒中歌唱。**
- **关键词**：another purpose hummed inside her / while there were some men like Da, too many were like Aoife’s husband, like Byrne. Like the captain. / She thought of his bones crumbling on the seabed, becoming nothing. / Whole ships of them ploughed the waves even now, bringing fear and violence to this unfamiliar land. / Mary felt the strange new muscles in her body clench, felt the spines bristle on her back. Her blood sang with fury.
- **为什么这样写**：**"另一个目的在她体内嗡鸣"（another purpose hummed inside her）——**"嗡鸣"（hummed）与海的力量、歌的力量用同一个动词**：**她的使命是被水写进身体的**。**"骨头碎裂、化为乌有"（bones crumbling on the seabed, becoming nothing）——**她给船长的结局是消失，不是受罚**。**"整船整船的人"（Whole ships of them）把个体的恶写成结构**——**而"陌生的土地"（this unfamiliar land）提醒读者：她说的这片土地本是别人的**。**"血在愤怒中歌唱"（Her blood sang with fury）——**愤怒与歌在此合流，这是她变身的真正完成**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请把"血在愤怒中歌唱"与 ch41 末的 "Only fury, building in her like a song." 并读**：**那句写的是 Aoife，这句写的是 Mary**。

> **原句 21:** "‘The female factory,’ Bridie said, her eyes dull. ‘They will take Annie from you. A man will pick you out of a line, to work his field or his bed. If you marry, you can get a ticket of leave. But you won’t be free. Not really.’"

- **中文理解**：「**女囚工厂，**」Bridie 说，**眼神黯淡**。「**他们会把 Annie 从你身边带走。会有男人把你从队列里挑出来，去干他的地里的活，或者上他的床。如果你结婚，可以拿到释放票。但你不会自由。不会真的自由。**」
- **关键词**：The female factory / her eyes dull / They will take Annie from you / A man will pick you out of a line, to work his field or his bed / If you marry, you can get a ticket of leave / But you won’t be free. Not really.
- **为什么这样写**：**"从队列里挑出来"（pick you out of a line）回照 ch37 的 "a man plucking her from a line as if she were a cow"**——**同一个动作，两处描写**。**"干他地里的活，或者上他的床"（to work his field or his bed）用并列结构把劳动与性剥削等同**。**"释放票"（a ticket of leave）是真实的殖民制度**——**而"不会真的自由"（Not really）是对它的判决**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：这段话解释了为什么 Bridie 宁愿不回去**——**她的"自由"在岸上比在海里更不可能**。

> **原句 22:** "Mary felt Eliza’s hand brush hers under the water, unseen by the others. The time for farewell had come."

- **中文理解**：**Mary 感到 Eliza 的手在水下擦过她的手，别人看不见**。**告别的时刻到了。**
- **关键词**：Eliza’s hand brush hers under the water, unseen by the others / The time for farewell had come.
- **为什么这样写**：**"在水下、别人看不见"（under the water, unseen by the others）——**她们的姐妹关系从此只能存在于水线以下**：**这是本章对"两种世界"最安静的一次注解**。**"告别的时刻到了"（The time for farewell had come.）用完成时收束**——**一个章节的结束，也是一个世界的告别**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请把这一句与本章末"看着木筏漂走"并读**：**告别是逐渐拉开的距离**。

> **原句 23:** "Their friends belonged to one world, and they to another.
>
> A world where sharks glided in the deep, where fish swam in glittering clouds. A world where songs swelled and rippled, travelling for miles.
>
> A female world. Mam’s world."

- **中文理解**：**她们的朋友属于一个世界，而她们属于另一个。**
  **一个鲨鱼在深处滑行、鱼群如闪光的云一样游动的世界**。**一个歌声涌起、荡开、传出数英里的世界。**
  **一个女性的世界。Mam 的世界。**
- **关键词**：belonged to one world, and they to another / A world where sharks glided in the deep, where fish swam in glittering clouds / A world where songs swelled and rippled, travelling for miles / A female world. Mam’s world.
- **为什么这样写**：**三个"A world"排比构成一个宣言式的定义**——**而这定义的最后落点是"Mam 的世界"**：**母亲不在了，但母亲的世界可以被继承**。**"女性的世界"（A female world）是全书对海底世界最直接的一次命名**。**"歌声传出数英里"（travelling for miles）解释了 2019 线 Lucy 在崖上听见的歌声**。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：全书对"海"的三种定义在此集齐**——**Ch48 是"陆与海之间"，此处是"Mam 的世界"，ch31 是"It keeps its women safe."**。

> **原句 24:** "Again, she saw the cave. The girl with her swollen body and wide, scared eyes. And all the other girls who would come, before and after her.
>
> Mary had thought that being a merrow meant she could never be a mother; that she’d have to choose between the two, the way Mam had done. But now, her mind full of the frightened girl in the cave, she felt an ache of certainty. She would be a mother. Yes, together she and Eliza would mother all the girls who came to this place, all the girls who needed protection and vengeance. She, Mary, would do this; until there was no more life in her body, whenever that time would be."

- **中文理解**：**她又看见了那个洞穴**。**那个身体肿胀、眼睛又大又怕的女孩**。**还有所有会来的女孩，在她之前和在她之后。**
  **Mary 曾以为做 merrow 就意味着她永远不能做母亲；以为她必须在两者之间选择，就像 Mam 做过的那样**。**但现在，满脑子都是洞里那个受惊的女孩，她感到一种确信的疼痛。她要做母亲**。**是的，她和 Eliza 将一起做所有来到这个地方的女孩的母亲，所有需要保护与复仇的女孩**。**她，Mary，会做这件事；直到她身体里再没有生命，无论那是什么时候。**
- **关键词**：she saw the cave. The girl with her swollen body and wide, scared eyes / all the other girls who would come, before and after her / she could never be a mother; that she’d have to choose between the two, the way Mam had done / she felt an ache of certainty / together she and Eliza would mother all the girls who came to this place / all the girls who needed protection and vengeance / until there was no more life in her body
- **为什么这样写**：**"在她之前和在她之后"（before and after her）——**那个洞里的女孩不是一个人，是一条队列**：**Jess 是其中一个，而队列还在延伸**。**"就像 Mam 做过的那样"（the way Mam had done）把母女三代的抉择连成一线**。**"确信的疼痛"（an ache of certainty）用矛盾修辞写决心**。**"保护与复仇"（protection and vengeance）并列，不加道德评判**——**这是全书对 siren 传说的最终定义**：**她们既救人也杀人**。**"直到身体里再没有生命"（until there was no more life in her body）——**她的誓约没有终点。
- **读者视角提示**：**请注意：这一块把 1801 线直接接到 2019 线**——**Jess 在洞里生产的那一刻，Mary 正在水里看着（ch47/ch49 的同一夜）**。

> **原句 25:** "She pictured them again, that line of girls waiting.
>
> We will keep you safe, Mary thought as she and Eliza watched the raft drift away, carried by the sunlit water.
>
> I promise."

- **中文理解**：**她再次想象她们，那条等待着的女孩队列。**
  **我们会护你安全，Mary 想，她和 Eliza 看着木筏漂远，被阳光照亮的带走了。**
  **我保证。**
- **关键词**：that line of girls waiting / We will keep you safe, Mary thought / watched the raft drift away, carried by the sunlit water / I promise.
- **为什么这样写**：**"我们会护你安全"（We will keep you safe）与 ch31 Melody 说的 "It keeps its women safe." 是同一句承诺的两个版本**——**相隔两百年，说话人从被救者变成守护者**。**"我保证"（I promise.）独立成段，只有两个词**——**这是全书最短的末句，也是最重的**。**"被阳光照亮的带走了"（carried by the sunlit water）——**道别是温柔的、明亮的，与沉船那夜的黑暗形成全书最大的反差。
- **读者视角提示**：**本章是 1801 线的终点**——**而它给出的不是结局，是一个承诺；这个承诺将在 2019 线被检验**。

## 一句话总结

**沉船之夜。** **气泡从 Mary 身边旋成一片白**；**Eliza 在她身旁，脊上的鳍片像东方故事里的老虎一样带条纹**。**气穴正迅速变小**——**Bridie 与 Sarah 把脸贴在潮湿的船壳木板上、手指发白**，**Annie 蜷在母亲胸前从越来越少的空气里虚弱呼吸**。**她们靠近时女人们畏缩了，双脚慌乱地踩水**——**"她们害怕。她和 Eliza 在她们眼里是怪物。"** Bridie 念着「**天主之母，请为我们罪人祈祷**」，Mary 喊「**你不认识我了吗，Bridie？……你留在这里会死的**」，**但 Bridie 眼里仍闪着恐惧**。**于是她唱起来**：「**你们本该在从前就认识我……我唱你入睡，我夺走你的财富**」——**Eliza 浮上来与她一起唱完最后一行**：「**而我又成了岸上的少女**」。「**上帝救我，是你们。你们两个。**」**Bridie 双腿缠上 Mary 的腰像个小孩子**，**Eliza 托住 Sarah，Annie 夹在她们身体之间**，Sarah 喘着说「**再当一会儿我的乖女孩，a linbh**」。**月亮在浪上闪光、碎片撞岩；海岸在身后升起一道黑弧**——**Mary 感到那股力量在嗡鸣，看见大森林里燃烧的眼睛**；**"这片土地把她的族人藏在腹中数千年，而英国人——像船长和那些军官的男人——来把她剖开"**——**她决定绝不参与，不会越过礁石与崖中的洞穴**。**她勾住 Bridie 的腰向后游，让朋友感到脸上的夜色与肺里的凉气**，**俯身在她额上一吻**。**她们用海草与撕开的帆布绑成木筏**，**天边发白、星星淡去**。「**你觉得还有别人……**」**Bridie 没说完，问题悬在空气里**——**Mary 想起那只手"软弱的花"、随水流动的发丝**。**Bridie 伸手抚摸 Mary 的鳃**：「**我想我们得谢那些旧神……我们还是向朋友们道别吧。**」「**Slán abhaile，**」Sarah 对着沉船方向说，「**Slán abhaile。**」**太阳升起时三人互相蜷在木筏上睡去**。**Mary 与 Eliza 游向礁石**——**礁石让她想起春天的森林，粉红珊瑚随流摆动，鱼群闪光掠过，一道黑影从海床升起飘走**——**"她从没见过这么多美，眼睛看得发疼却几乎不敢眨眼。仿佛这些年来她一直带着巨大的渴，此刻终于解渴。"**「**真希望你能看见这个。**」「**我能看见。我一直都看见这个。这一刻。我们，被海改变……为了 tír fo thuinn。浪下的土地。**」**Mary 为那晚的争吵、为不愿谈 Mam、为不信她、为当年让她们留下来而道歉**；**Eliza 却笑出泪**：「**但你没有。我才是那个害怕的人，那个不想走的人。**」——**原来当年是 Mary 替妹妹背下了这个谎**。**她想起 Da 的声音、他的故事、他那么努力地保护她们**——**"不受 Byrne 那样的人伤害，也不受她们自己伤害"**；**但他不也把她们从海里、从与生俱来的权利里带走了吗？** **"她会想念 Da 的每一天……他死后，谁会为他洗身、点蜡烛。"** **"但现在另一个目的在她体内嗡鸣"**——**像 Da 的男人有，但太多人像 Aoife 的丈夫、像 Byrne、像船长**；**船长的骨头正在海床上碎裂、化为乌有**，**而"整船整船的人此刻正在破浪前行，把恐惧与暴力带到这片陌生的土地"**——**她感到新生的肌肉绷紧、脊刺竖起，"她的血在愤怒中歌唱"**。**Bridie 说起女囚工厂**：「**他们会把 Annie 从你身边带走。会有男人把你从队列里挑出来，去干他地里的活，或者上他的床……但你不会自由。不会真的自由。**」**Eliza 的手在水下擦过她的，别人看不见——告别的时刻到了**。**"她们的朋友属于一个世界，而她们属于另一个"**：**鲨鱼滑行的深处、鱼群如闪光云朵、歌声荡出数英里**——**"一个女性的世界。Mam 的世界。"** **她又看见那个洞穴、那个身体肿胀眼睛又大又怕的女孩，以及所有会来的女孩，在她之前和在她之后**——**她曾以为做 merrow 就不能做母亲，必须像 Mam 那样二选一；但现在她感到"一种确信的疼痛"**：**"她要做母亲。她和 Eliza 将一起做所有来到这个地方的女孩的母亲，所有需要保护与复仇的女孩，直到她身体里再没有生命。"** **她和 Eliza 看着木筏被阳光照亮的水带远**——**"我们会护你安全，"Mary 想**——**"我保证。"**
