# Chapter 34

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：押运船下层，Mary 的世界已经缩到只剩自己的身体——饥饿、口渴，以及一种新的怪痛：**颈上像是有什么东西要顶破皮肤，手指与脚趾正互相织在一起**。船上传来还有四周就到新南威尔士的消息，女人们开始议论"female factory"（女囚工厂）——殖民者会去那里挑走她们当仆人或妻子。Lizzie 转述村里姑娘的信：她母亲左右为难，不知道该怕女儿嫁新教徒，还是怕她婚前失身。Mary 想过自己被像牛一样从队伍里挑走、从此与 Eliza 分开，也想过那个男人也许善良如 Da，也许像水手、像 Byrne——**问题在于根本没法分辨**。某个早上她抬手去揉颈上的刺痛，指下触到一层湿滑如薄膜的东西。她叫 Eliza 把头发撩开：**姐姐脖子上出现了两道细缝，正落在脉搏跳动的地方**，随呼吸一起动。Mary 先是惊恐到脑中空白，随即想起 Da 在 Ard na Caithne 捕到的那条鲱鱼、她梦见 Mam 的那一夜、那双肉翼的拍动。Eliza 说不必害怕，那不是病，"你记得的，那一夜 Mam 告诉我们的那些"。Mary 却翻身贴着船舱弧形的壁，闭上眼看见 Mam 那张可怕的脸、鳞状的皮、空眼里生肉的粉。Eliza 抚她的背低声安抚，她缩得更紧：**她不肯听 Eliza 的话，也不肯想起来——她承受不了**。
- **视角**：第三人称限知，锁定 Mary；1801 年押运船下层（与 ch34 同一舱位、同一场押运）——**Mary 线的"证据落地"章**：此前 ch30/ch34 里 Eliza 的水性只以他人之眼与梦境出现，本章第一次由 Mary 亲手确认。
- **情感弧线位置**：紧接 ch34（Mam 尸体梦与女囚合唱）；Mary 线从"抗拒梦"推进到"抗拒姐姐身上的事实"——**她的否认第一次有了具体的对象**。
- **人物弧线**：Mary 在本章从"病人"变成"见证者"：她颈上也有异感（暗示同一种变化正在她身上发生），却把全部恐惧投射到 Eliza 身上；Eliza 则第一次以**安抚者**而非被救者的姿态出现（她说的"你记得的"表明她早已接受）。
- **叙事手法**：**全章以身体感觉组织**——听觉（血在耳中呼啸）、触觉（膜一样的湿滑）、视觉（细缝随呼吸起伏），**没有一句写外部事件**（连"四周后抵达"都是转述）；姐妹俩的对话被压到最短，Mary 的拒绝用**三个否定句收尾**（would not listen / would not remember / could not bear to）——本书的章末否定式是它写抗拒的固定语法；"Lizzie 的信"是本章唯一的外部世界，**用一封转述的家书交代整个殖民制度**。
- **跨章核对（7b/第9条e）**："the things Mam told us" 指 ch30（Samhain 与 merrow 章，Mam 讲水神学与"是水保护了我"）；"the herring Da had caught back in Ard na Caithne" 与 "The beat of those fleshy wings" 指 ch34（Mam 尸体梦中的 "Fleshy wings, like the gills on the herring"）；"Mam’s face, that terrible face from her dream, with its scaled skin; the raw pink of its empty eyes" 亦为 ch34 梦象的回放；Eliza 颈上的细缝与 ch30 末 Mary 自己"脚趾发绿、带蹼"、ch34 女囚合唱中的转化属同一条身体线；"female factory" 是全书对殖民女性制度最直接的一次点名（1801 线的制度与 1999 线的 ECPO 形成两百年跨度的呼应）。本章末句（tail 复核）："And she would not remember. She could not bear to."

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "In the dark of the prison deck, there was little to distract her from the pain. From the whoosh of blood in her ears, the ache of thirst in her throat.
>
> Sometimes, Mary wondered if there was a world beyond the ship at all."

- **中文理解**：在囚舱的黑暗里，几乎没有什么能让她从痛里分心。从耳中血流呼啸、从喉中干渴的痛里分心。
  有时 Mary 会想，这艘船之外到底还有没有世界。
- **关键词**：In the dark of the prison deck / little to distract her from the pain / the whoosh of blood in her ears / the ache of thirst in her throat / wondered if there was a world beyond the ship at all
- **为什么这样写**：**开篇把空间压到只剩感官**：两处 "From…" 的排比（血声、喉渴）都不带主语，句式本身在模拟**被痛占据的意识**。"if there was a world beyond the ship at all" 用 at all 收尾，把怀疑推到极限——**本书写囚禁从不写锁链，只写"世界还在不在"这个问题**。这一问也为下一段的"另一个 Mary"做准备：她已经开始怀疑自己的记忆属于别人。
- **读者视角提示**：与 ch34 开篇（Mam 的梦）相比，本章连梦都省了——**Mary 的处境是"没有出口的清醒"**。

> **原句 2:** "It had been some other girl, some other Mary, who had seen the stars blink through the clouds at night; who had watched starlings write their messages in the sky. Even the girl who had shivered in the cart from Kilmainham to the port, passing through the green countryside still scabbed with war; even that girl was a stranger to her now.
>
> Mary’s world had shrunk to contain only her own body. Her hunger, her thirst. And the new, strange pain: the feeling that something was pushing through the soft skin on her neck, that her fingers and toes were knitting themselves together."

- **中文理解**：是另一个女孩、另一个 Mary，曾在夜里看星星在云隙间眨眼；曾看椋鸟在天上写下它们的讯息。就连那个在从 Kilmainham 到港口的车上发抖的女孩——穿过那片还结着战争血痂的绿色乡野——就连那个女孩，现在对她来说也是个陌生人。
  Mary 的世界已经缩到只装得下她自己的身体。她的饥饿，她的口渴。还有那种新的、奇怪的痛：**好像有什么正从她颈上柔软的皮肤里顶出来，好像她的手指和脚趾正在互相织在一起**。
- **关键词**：some other girl, some other Mary / watched starlings write their messages in the sky / shivered in the cart from Kilmainham to the port / the green countryside still scabbed with war / even that girl was a stranger to her now / Mary’s world had shrunk to contain only her own body / something was pushing through the soft skin on her neck / her fingers and toes were knitting themselves together
- **为什么这样写**：**"另一个 Mary" 是本书写创伤的固定语法**（对照 ch34 的 "some copy of her"）：创伤不是改变一个人，而是**把过去的人变成陌生人**。"starlings write their messages in the sky" 的椋鸟群飞是全书最明亮的一个意象，而它被放在"那不是我"的句子里——**美景在此证明的是距离**。"the green countryside still scabbed with war" 用 scabbed（结痂）写 1798 年起义后的爱尔兰：**同一章里"结痂"既是乡野的伤，也是她颈上那层"膜"的近亲**。"knitting themselves together" 的 knitting（编织）是本书写转化的核心动词——**身体在自己动手改造自己**。
- **读者视角提示**：注意"手指与脚趾"——与 ch30 末"脚趾发绿、带蹼"、ch34 的 "the flesh lifting from her greening bones—" 是同一条线；**Mary 的转化已经开始，只是她还不肯承认**。

> **原句 3:** "They had been told that they had only four weeks left, now. As hungry as Mary was, and as much as the smell of the slop buckets and the gnawing of the rats made her want to retch, fear wormed in her chest. When they had the energy to talk, the women began to wonder what awaited them in New South Wales."

- **中文理解**：她们被告知，现在只剩四周了。Mary 再饿、潲水桶的气味与老鼠的啃咬再让她想吐，**恐惧仍在她的胸腔里钻**。当她们有力气说话时，女人们开始猜新南威尔士等着她们的是什么。
- **关键词**：only four weeks left / the smell of the slop buckets / the gnawing of the rats / made her want to retch / fear wormed in her chest / the women began to wonder what awaited them in New South Wales
- **为什么这样写**：**"只剩四周"被写成恐惧的理由而非解脱**——本章的政治核心在此：**航程的终点不是自由，是另一种处置**。"fear wormed in her chest" 的 worm（虫）与上一段的 rat 同属啃噬类动物：**恐惧在本书里是一种生物**。"slop buckets" 与 "rats" 两个具体名词承担全部环境的残酷，作者不写呕吐，只写"想吐"。
- **读者视角提示**：请记住"四周"这个数字——它是全书 1801 线剩余篇幅的时间框（此后各章的处境都在这四周内展开）。

> **原句 4:** "A woman called Lizzie with a Northern accent spoke of a girl from her village who had been transported several years before. The girl had found someone in Sydney Town to write a letter home to her mother and Lizzie had read it aloud for her. ‘She wrote that she’d been taken to a place called the female factory,’ Lizzie told them, whispering the final words for effect. ‘Where settlers would come and pick out the girls they wanted for servants or wives. Her mammy was beside herself, thinking her precious girl might have to marry a Protestant, or bed someone out of wedlock. The poor woman couldn’t decide what would be worse.’"

- **中文理解**：一个带北方口音、叫 Lizzie 的女人说起她们村里几年前被流放的女孩。那女孩在悉尼镇找到人替她给母亲写了封信，Lizzie 曾为她朗读过。「她写她被带去了一个叫**女囚工厂**的地方，」Lizzie 告诉大家，最后几个字她压低了声音以制造效果。「在那里，殖民者会来挑走他们想要的女孩子，当仆人或者当老婆。她娘急得不成样子，想着她的宝贝女儿也许得嫁个新教徒，或者没结婚就跟人上床。**那可怜的女人分不清哪一样更糟。**」
- **关键词**：A woman called Lizzie with a Northern accent / a girl from her village who had been transported / a letter home to her mother / whispering the final words for effect / the female factory / pick out the girls they wanted for servants or wives / marry a Protestant, or bed someone out of wedlock / couldn’t decide what would be worse
- **为什么这样写**：**整段殖民制度由一封转述的家书承担**：作者不让叙述者评价，只让一个同舱的女人"压低声音以制造效果"——**被讲述的恐怖永远比被描写的大**。"servants or wives" 的两个选项把女性的全部未来压成**两种被占有**；"marry a Protestant, or bed someone out of wedlock" 则把母亲的焦虑写成**宗教与贞洁的双重恐惧**——**本书对"耻辱"的定义从来由女性互相传递**。"The poor woman couldn't decide what would be worse." 是本章最冷的一句反讽：**在她母亲的价值体系里，两件坏事都不可接受，而它们其实是同一件事**。
- **读者视角提示**：与 ch35/ch36 的 ECPO 对读：**两百年间，处置 Lucy 与处置 Mary 的都是一份文件**——本书的制度批判靠这种远距离同构，而非议论。

> **原句 5:** "Mary had imagined a man plucking her from a line as if she were a cow, taking her away from Eliza. She had felt for her sister’s hand and squeezed it tight. Perhaps it would not be so bad, she told herself. A man might be kind, like Da. But he might be like the sailors, or like Byrne.
>
> That was the problem. There seemed to be no way of telling the difference."

- **中文理解**：Mary 想象过一个男人像从牲口里挑牛一样把她从队伍里挑走，把她从 Eliza 身边带走。她摸索着找到姐姐的手，紧紧一捏。也许不会那么糟，她告诉自己。一个男人也许善良，像 Da。可他也可能像那些水手，或者像 Byrne。
  **问题就在这里。似乎根本没有办法分辨。**
- **关键词**：a man plucking her from a line as if she were a cow / taking her away from Eliza / felt for her sister’s hand and squeezed it tight / A man might be kind, like Da / he might be like the sailors, or like Byrne / no way of telling the difference
- **为什么这样写**：**「像挑牛一样」把女性被挑选写成畜牧业**——本章第二次用动物写人的处境（第一次是恐惧如虫）。她把 Da 与 Byrne 并列为两种可能，**而这两个名字在本书里分别代表庇护与侵害**：这是全书对"男性不可辨识"最简洁的陈述。"no way of telling the difference" 一句是对本书全部男性角色的判词——**问题不是有坏人，而是善恶外表相同**（与 ch35 里 Lucy 想的那句 "You would never know, she thinks, to look at him." 完全同构，**两百年的跨度被同一句话缝合**）。
- **读者视角提示**：注意她"摸索着找到姐姐的手"——**黑暗中靠触觉确认彼此**是姐妹俩在全书的固定动作（ch30 的互换眼睛亦同源）。

> **原句 6:** "One morning, after the rations had been delivered, Mary lifted her hand to her neck, wanting to soothe the stinging pain she felt there. She gasped as her fingers touched the flesh; the wet smoothness of it, like a membrane."

- **中文理解**：一天早上，口粮发下来之后，Mary 抬手去揉颈上那处刺痛。手指触到那层皮肉时她倒吸一口气：**湿滑的一层，像薄膜**。
- **关键词**：after the rations had been delivered / lifted her hand to her neck / wanting to soothe the stinging pain / She gasped as her fingers touched the flesh / the wet smoothness of it, like a membrane
- **为什么这样写**：**发现的方式仍是触觉**（与 ch34 醒来摸脖子、ch36 Jess 摸下颌"ridges gone"完全同构）——**本书判定身体变化从不靠镜子，只靠手指**。"the wet smoothness of it, like a membrane" 的 membrane（薄膜）是生物学词，**把奇迹写成组织学**：作者坚持让超自然事件停留在可触的层面。"after the rations had been delivered" 的日常时间标记让这一发现发生在**最平庸的时刻**（分饭之后），**恐怖不被安排在夜里**。
- **读者视角提示**：注意是 Mary 先在自己颈上摸到异样——**她与 Eliza 是同一种变化**，而她随后把注意力全转到了姐姐身上，这是本章最重要的心理转向。

> **原句 7:** "‘Eliza,’ she whispered now. ‘Move your hair aside.’
>
> ‘What? What’s wrong?’ Eliza’s fingers fluttered to her chest, brushing at her smock. ‘Is it a rat?’
>
> ‘Hush,’ Mary hissed. ‘I don’t want the others to see. Just – I need you to show me your neck. Move a bit closer to me.’ Eliza shuffled forward, to the space where light snaked from the upper decks, and held back her hair. Mary covered her mouth with her hand so that her sister would not hear her gasp."

- **中文理解**：「Eliza，」她现在低声说。「把头发撩开。」
  「什么？怎么了？」Eliza 的手指飘到胸口，拂着自己的罩衫。「是老鼠吗？」
  「嘘，」Mary 嘶声说。「我不想让别人看见。只是——我要看看你的脖子。往我这边挪一点。」Eliza 挪上前，挪到从上层甲板蜿蜒下来的那道光里，把头发拢到脑后。Mary 用手捂住嘴，**好让姐姐听不见她倒吸的那口气**。
- **关键词**：Move your hair aside / Eliza’s fingers fluttered to her chest / brushing at her smock / Is it a rat? / I don’t want the others to see / to the space where light snaked from the upper decks / held back her hair / Mary covered her mouth with her hand / so that her sister would not hear her gasp
- **为什么这样写**：**「是老鼠吗？」是本章的慈悲与残忍所在**：Eliza 担心的仍是舱里的日常苦难（鼠），而真正的事在别处。作者让**光从上层甲板"蜿蜒"下来**（snaked）——**光的路径本身是爬行的、像生物**，与颈上的"细缝"同属一个蠕动意象系统。"Mary covered her mouth … so that her sister would not hear her gasp" 一句完成了全章的伦理：**她怕的不是变化，是让姐姐知道她怕**——姐妹关系的全部张力在此。
- **读者视角提示**：注意她压低声音的理由是"不想让别人看见"——**女囚之间连身体的变化都要藏**（本章第二次写女性之间的相互观看：第一次是 Lizzie 讲故事时的"压低声音"）。

> **原句 8:** "Two slits had appeared on Eliza’s neck, at the exact spot where her pulse flickered.
>
> She watched them move in time with her sister’s breathing. At first, her mind was too blank with horror to comprehend them. But then she remembered the herring Da had caught back in Ard na Caithne, the nightmare she’d had of Mam. The beat of those fleshy wings."

- **中文理解**：**Eliza 的脖子上出现了两道细缝，正好在她脉搏跳动的地方。**
  她看着它们随姐姐的呼吸一起动。起初她的脑子因恐惧而空白，理解不了它们。可随后她想起了 Da 在 Ard na Caithne 捕到的那条**鲱鱼**，想起了她做过的关于 Mam 的噩梦。那双肉翼的拍动。
- **关键词**：Two slits had appeared on Eliza’s neck / at the exact spot where her pulse flickered / watched them move in time with her sister’s breathing / her mind was too blank with horror to comprehend them / the herring Da had caught back in Ard na Caithne / the nightmare she’d had of Mam / The beat of those fleshy wings
- **为什么这样写**：**「正好在脉搏跳动的地方」是本章的核心细节**：鳃与心跳共用一处——**水性与生命在解剖上被合为一体**。"watched them move in time with her sister's breathing" 的 in time（合拍）是音乐用词，**姐姐的呼吸在此成了一种节拍**（与全书反复出现的"歌"同源）。记忆的次序极准：**先想起鱼，再想起梦**——**她从自然史里认出了奇迹**（这也是本书的一贯逻辑：传说总先以渔获的形式出现）。
- **读者视角提示**：请把"两处细缝"与 ch30 末 Mary 自己的"脚趾发绿、带蹼"对读：**姐妹俩的身体正朝同一方向变化，只是 Eliza 更快**——这解释了 ch34 里 Eliza 为何能唱歌救她。

> **原句 9:** "‘Come, now,’ Eliza said, catching Mary’s shaking fingers in her own. ‘There’s no need to be frightened.’
>
> ‘But these wounds,’ Mary breathed. ‘This sickness—’
>
> ‘It is not a sickness,’ Eliza said. ‘But you know that, you remember. That night, the things Mam told us …’"

- **中文理解**：「来，」Eliza 说，用自己的一把握住 Mary 发抖的手指。「没必要害怕。」
  「可这些伤口，」Mary 喘着气说。「这种病——」
  「**这不是病**，」Eliza 说。「可你知道的，你记得。那一夜，Mam 告诉我们的那些……」
- **关键词**：catching Mary’s shaking fingers in her own / There’s no need to be frightened / But these wounds / This sickness— / It is not a sickness / But you know that, you remember / That night, the things Mam told us
- **为什么这样写**：**「这不是病」是本章的判决句**：Eliza 的措辞把医学范畴整个取消——**她拒绝让身体被命名成缺陷**。作者的对话写法极省：Mary 说半句被打断（"This sickness—" 用破折号截住），Eliza 用 "you know that, you remember" 两层肯定回应——**安抚的方式不是解释，而是提醒对方她已经知道**。省略号收尾（"the things Mam told us …"）把 ch30 的神学留作**读者自己的记忆作业**。
- **读者视角提示**：Eliza 在此说的话与 ch34 她唱的歌词同调（"You should have known me before"）——**姐姐的角色一贯是"记得的人"**。

> **原句 10:** "Mary rolled away from her sister, pressed her body against the curved wall of the berth, with its stink of sweat and tar.
>
> She closed her eyes, but instead of blackness, she saw a face. Mam’s face, that terrible face from her dream, with its scaled skin; the raw pink of its empty eyes. Eliza stroked her back, murmuring softly, but Mary pulled away, burrowing tighter into herself."

- **中文理解**：Mary 从姐姐身边翻过去，把身体贴住船舱弧形的壁——那里有汗和沥青的臭味。
  她闭上眼，但看见的不是黑，是一张脸。Mam 的脸，她梦里那张可怕的脸，**鳞状的皮肤**；**空眼里生肉的粉**。Eliza 抚她的背，低声喃喃，可 Mary 躲开了，把自己缩得更紧。
- **关键词**：rolled away from her sister / pressed her body against the curved wall of the berth / its stink of sweat and tar / instead of blackness, she saw a face / that terrible face from her dream, with its scaled skin / the raw pink of its empty eyes / Eliza stroked her back, murmuring softly / burrowing tighter into herself
- **为什么这样写**：**「看见的不是黑」把闭眼写成另一种视觉**——本章的恐惧从来不是外部的，是**眼睑内部的**。"the curved wall of the berth" 的弧形与"船腹"、"洞穴"同形：**Mary 一直在往容器的最里面钻**（burrowing tighter into herself 的 burrow 是动物挖洞的动词，与全书的洞穴意象收拢）。"Eliza stroked her back, murmuring softly" 与 ch34 女囚合唱的安抚功能一致：**姐姐的触摸与歌是同一种东西**。
- **读者视角提示**：注意 Mary 拒绝的姿势（翻身、贴壁、躲开）与她 ch34 拒绝醒来完全同构——**她的自我保护一直是"往更深处缩"**。

> **原句 11:** "She would not listen to Eliza’s whisperings, her imaginings borne of grief.
>
> And she would not remember. She could not bear to."

- **中文理解**：她不肯听 Eliza 的低语，不肯听那些**由悲伤生出来的臆想**。
  而她也不肯想起来。**她承受不了。**
- **关键词**：would not listen to Eliza’s whisperings / her imaginings borne of grief / And she would not remember / She could not bear to
- **为什么这样写**：**三个否定收束全章**（would not listen / would not remember / could not bear to）——**本书写抗拒从不用感叹句，只用不肯**。"her imaginings borne of grief" 是 Mary 对 Eliza 的判词（她认为姐姐的信仰是丧亲的幻觉），**而读者知道那不是**——本章全部张力在此。"And she would not remember." 以 And 开头独立成句，是作者惯用的**章末加重**（对照 ch34 末句 "The beauty they made of his prison."）；最后一句 "She could not bear to." 用 **to 悬空**（省略 remember），**句法上的截断就是心理上的截断**。
- **读者视角提示**：本章以拒绝收尾，而下一章（1801 线）将证明她终究会想起来——**本书的"不肯"从不长久**。

## 一句话总结

押运船下层，Mary 在自己颈上摸到一层膜似的湿滑，又亲眼在 Eliza 颈上看见两道随呼吸起伏的细缝——她想起 Da 捕的鲱鱼与 Mam 的噩梦，终于明白这不是病；Eliza 让她"记得那一夜 Mam 说的话"，而她翻过身贴住舱壁，闭眼看见 Mam 那张鳞皮空眼的脸，**三个否定收束全章：不肯听、不肯想起、承受不了**——**这一章把 ch30 的神学第一次变成 Mary 指尖下的实物，也让她的否认第一次有了具体的对象。**
