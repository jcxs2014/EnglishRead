# Chapter 44（精读分析）

## 概览

- **出处**：The Wrong Sister, Claire Douglas（悬疑惊悚）
- **章节定位**：Chapter 44，Jeanette 视角，回忆与 Zoë 对峙+Bonnie 出现
- **字符数：** ~8,600
- **一句话主旨**：Jeanette 回忆她在 Zoë 死那晚与她对质（关于威胁信）。Viv 带 Bonnie 来——Jeanette 认出她是 Holly（"one hundred per cent certain"）。Tasha 误以为 Bonnie 是 Lola（同事）。
- **叙事手法**：Jeanette 的回忆（与 Zoë 的对峙）；Bonnie 的出现（"one hundred per cent certain"）；误会（Tasha 以为 Bonnie 是 Lola）。

## 逐句精读

> **原句 1:** Jeanette notices another missed call from Eamonn. She has managed to avoid his calls since she's been back in Chew Norton but she knows she has to speak to him soon. He deserves an explanation. After all, he is her friend. And he's been great to her these last eighteen months. She taps out a quick text while sitting alone in the garden. Tasha has left to pick up the girls from nursery. I'm sorry I can't speak now. So much family stuff going on but I promise I'll call soon for a proper chat. She deliberates on whether to add kisses to the end of the message and decides on two.

**中文理解：** Jeanette 注意到 Eamonn 另一个未接来电。自从回到 Chew Norton 后，她一直设法避免他的通话，但她知道他很快必须和他谈谈。他解释。毕竟，他是她的朋友。在过去十八个月里他对她很好。她独自坐在花园里，发出一条快速短信。Tasha 已经离开去托儿所接女孩们。我现在不能说话。发生了这么多家庭事务但我保证很快会打电话好好聊。她考虑是否在信息结尾加上吻，然后决定加两个。

**句子结构：** 复合句，Jeanette notices... + She has managed to avoid... + but she knows... + He deserves... + She taps out... + She deliberates... + and decides...。

**关键词：** deserves（应得——值得）、deliberates（考虑——思考）

**表达方式：** "deserves an explanation" 是责任——他值得一个解释。"deliberates on whether to add kisses" 是细节——考虑是否加吻（显示 Jeanette 与 Eamonn 的关系亲密）。

**为什么这样写：** Jeanette 的秘密——她与 Eamonn 的关系。

> **原句 2:** Zoë's shocked face from the night she died swims behind Jeanette's closed eyes and she feels another stab of guilt. Jeanette isn't confrontational. She's like Tasha in that way. That night, after Aaron and Tasha had left for the pub, she was saying goodnight to the twins, and as she went to close their curtains, she saw Zoë walking near the pond – a shortcut to the Packhorse. She'd recognized the sharp profile, the long blonde hair and the confident walk from where she'd first seen her from the café window earlier that day. Asking Alice to keep an eye on the twins while she popped out, she'd raced through the garden, still wearing her slippers, to the gate at the back, which was unlocked. 'Zoë,' she'd called, as the girl was about to cut down the lane between the cottages further up that led to the high street.

**中文理解：** Zoë 死那晚震惊的脸在 Jeanette 闭着的眼睛后游动，她感到又一阵内疚的刺痛。Jeanette 不是对抗性的。她在这方面像 Tasha。那天晚上，在 Aaron 和 Tasha 离开去酒吧后，她正在向双胞胎道晚安，当她们去拉窗帘时，她看到 Zoë 在池塘边行走——去 Packhorse 的捷径。她从那天早些时候在咖啡馆窗口第一次见到她时，认出了她尖锐的轮廓、长发和自信的步态。请 Alice 照看双胞胎，她冲过花园，仍然穿着拖鞋，到后门的门，没锁。"Zoë，" 她喊道，当女孩即将穿过小屋之间通向高街的小巷时。

**句子结构：** 复合句（含回忆、动作描写、对话）。

**关键词：** confrontational（对抗性的——好争吵）、stab of guilt（内疚的刺痛）、slippers（拖鞋——匆忙）

**表达方式：** "stab of guilt" 是身体化的内疚——像被刺。"slippers" 是细节——匆忙出门的证据。

**为什么这样写：** Jeanette 的回忆——她与 Zoë 的对峙。

> **原句 3:** Jeanette had acted on impulse, which never used to be her way but was becoming more so since Jim died. Now that Zoë was there, in front of her, she didn't know where to start. 'I'm Jeanette. Jeanette Harper,' she said, talking too quickly in an effort to get her words out. 'I'm the mother of Alice and Tasha.' 'Oh, yes,' Zoë had replied coolly. 'This might sound a bit strange,' she said, walking towards Zoë. 'But do you know that my son-in-law was murdered last week?' Zoë looked uncomfortable and shifted her weight from one leg to the other. 'I'd heard about that, yes.' 'DNA was found at the scene suggesting someone else had been in the house – maybe that night. Someone who, as it turns out, is a close relation of mine.' Zoë looked puzzled. 'Right?' Jeanette was handling this badly, she knew that, but she didn't know how to explain it all. Instead she blurted out, 'Did you put a note through Tasha's front door a few days after Kyle was killed? You were seen by a neighbour.' She knew this was a bit of a lie. Arthur had only described someone tall, blonde and wearing a leather jacket and nose ring. But as Jeanette appraised the woman standing in front of her it was a match, apart from the missing nose ring.

**中文理解：** Jeanette 凭直觉行事，这从来不是她的方式但在 Jim 死后越来越是这样。现在 Zoë 在那里，在她面前，她不知道从哪里开始。"我是 Jeanette。Jeanette Harper，" 她说，说话太快努力把话说出来。"我是 Alice 和 Tasha 的妈妈。""哦，是的，" Zoë 冷冷地回答。"这听起来可能有点奇怪，" 她说，走向 Zoë。"但你知道我女婿上周被谋杀了吗？" Zoë 看起来不舒服，换了重心。"我听说了，是的。""现场发现了 DNA 表明有人在那晚进了房子。有人，结果是，是我的近亲。" Zoë 看起来很困惑。"对？" Jeanette 知道她处理得很糟糕，但她不知道如何解释一切。相反她脱口而出，"你在 Kyle 被杀几天后把纸条塞进了 Tasha 的门吗？有邻居看到了你。" 她知道这有点撒谎。Arthur 只描述了有人高、金发、穿皮夹克和鼻环。但当 Jeanette 评估站在她面前的女人时，除了缺失的鼻环，是匹配的。

**句子结构：** 对话体 + 内心独白（Jeanette was handling this badly...） + 揭示（She knew this was a bit of a lie...）。

**关键词：** impulse（冲动——直觉）、blurted out（脱口而出——不经思考）

**表达方式：** "acted on impulse" 是直觉行动——不经思考。"blurted out" 是脱口而出——不经思考地说。"a bit of a lie" 是轻微的谎言——不完全真实。

**为什么这样写：** Jeanette 的对抗——她与 Zoë 对质关于威胁信。

> **原句 4:** Zoë glared at Jeanette. 'Of course not,' she spat. 'Why on earth would I do that?' Her eyes narrowed. 'Who do you actually think I am, Jeanette? Why are you here?' Jeanette is sitting between her beautiful granddaughters as they watch Peter Rabbit on CBeebies. She's busy knitting dresses for their teddies and trying not to think about Zoë, but she can sense Tasha's nervous energy emanating from where she's perched on the armchair in the corner. She knows her daughter is worried about Aaron. She called him earlier and he was at work but told her he'd talk to her properly when he gets home.

**中文理解：** Zoë 怒视 Jeanette。"当然不是，" 她唾弃。"我到底为什么要这样做？" 她的眼睛眯起。"你到底以为我是谁，Jeanette？你为什么在这里？" Jeanette 坐在她美丽的孙女之间，看 CBeebies 的彼得兔。她忙着为她们的泰迪熊织裙子，尽量不去想 Zoë，但她能感觉到 Tasha 紧张的神经能量从她坐在角落的扶手椅上传来。她知道女儿担心 Aaron。她早些时候打电话给他，他在工作，但告诉她他回家后会好好和她谈谈。

**句子结构：** 对话体 + 动作描写（Zoë glared...） + 场景描写（Jeanette is sitting...）。

**关键词：** spat（唾弃——愤怒地说）、narrowed（眯起——怀疑）

**表达方式：** "spat" 是愤怒地说——像吐口水。"narrowed" 是眯眼——怀疑/愤怒。

**为什么这样写：** Zoë 的防御——她愤怒地否认。

> **原句 5:** She's surprised to see Viv standing there with a young woman in a long flowery dress. Viv looks nervous and there is a scratch on her cheek that has puckered and scabbed. 'Hi. Can we come in?' 'Um … sure. Are you here to see Tasha?' 'Well, both of you. This is Bonnie,' she says, introducing the woman standing with her and Jeanette assesses her properly for the first time. Bonnie smiles shyly and there is something about her face: her large blue eyes, so like Flossie's, her dimples like Jim's, her fine arched eyebrows like Alice's. So much so that Jeanette lets out a small 'ouf' sound, as though she's been winded, and the hallway seems to shrink. Because she's one hundred per cent certain that she's looking at Holly. Before she can react Tasha is pounding down the stairs in a dressing-gown. 'Viv,' she says in surprise, when she reaches them. 'Is everything all right?' And then she stares at the young woman, who hangs her head, blushing slightly. 'Lola! What are you doing here?'

**中文理解：** 她惊讶地看到 Viv 站在那里，旁边是一个穿长裙子的年轻女人。Viv 看起来很紧张，脸上有抓痕，皱缩结痂了。"嗨。我们能进来吗？" "嗯……当然。你们是来看 Tasha 的吗？" "嗯，都看。这是 Bonnie，" 她说，介绍站在她旁边的女人，Jeanette 第一次正确评估她。Bonnie 害羞地微笑，她的脸上有某种东西：她的大蓝眼睛，像 Flossie 的，她的酒窝像 Jim 的，她的细拱形眉毛像 Alice 的。以至于 Jeanette 发出一声小小的"嗷"声，好像被撞到了，走廊似乎缩小了。因为她百分之百确定她在看 Holly。在她能反应之前，Tasha 穿着睡衣冲下楼梯。"Viv，" 当她到达时她惊讶地说。"一切都好吗？" 然后她盯着那个年轻女人，她低着头，微微脸红。"Lola！你在这里做什么？"

**句子结构：** 动作描写（She's surprised to see...） + 对话体 + 评估（Jeanette assesses...） + 揭示（Because she's one hundred per cent certain...） + 动作描写（Tasha is pounding...） + 误会（'Lola!'）。

**关键词：** one hundred per cent certain（百分之百确定——确信）、winded（被撞到——喘不过气）

**表达方式：** "one hundred per cent certain" 是强烈的确信——完全确定。"ouf" 是拟声词——被撞到发出的声音。"Because she's one hundred per cent certain that she's looking at Holly" 是揭示——Jeanette 认出 Bonnie 是 Holly。

**为什么这样写：** 全书最大揭示之一——Jeanette 认出 Bonnie 是 Holly。但 Tasha 误会（以为 Bonnie 是 Lola）。
