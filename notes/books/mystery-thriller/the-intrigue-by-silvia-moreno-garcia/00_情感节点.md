# 《The Intrigue》情感节点

## 节点一：一张便宜火车票（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 1943 年的墨西哥城，父亲死后三年，半吊子骗子 Ulises 在两位通信对象之间做选择，标准只有票价。火车把他送进雾气弥漫的山镇，侄女 Inés 像市场挑鱼一样把他打量了一遍——猎手从进门起就是展品。

**关键引语：**

> ① "The widow or the spinster. Which one to pick? In the end, his decision came down to money: the ticket to Puerco Ahogado, Veracruz, was cheaper."（ch01）

> ② "Yes, she was better put together than he’d imagined, though it wouldn’t have mattered to him if she was a hideous hag. In his line of business, looks were extraneous. Money was what mattered."（ch01）

> ③ "“Fito, can you take our guest’s luggage upstairs, to the Butterfly Room?” she asked, and then she looked Ulises up and down, like a woman examining a fish at the market. She smirked."（ch01）

> ④ "Nepomuceno was opening and closing drawers when she walked in. He looked up at her and smiled. He was dressed, even on a Sunday, in his black vest, tie, and patched-up gray jacket, his white hair neatly parted in the middle, looking the same way he’d looked when he’d taught Latin and Greek and history to recalcitrant children."（ch02）

## 节点二：小摆件的升值（ch03）

**叙事概括：** Perla 等侄女出门便去探新房客的底——她原打算用这个「小摆件」逼 Cándido 摊牌，可真人比照片好看，估价体系当场失灵。闪回揭开她的全部前史：咖啡王朝、厌女家训、大流感夺走兄长、婚约本是父亲的并购案。

**关键引语：**

> ① "Ulises Linares was supposed to be a trinket to show off around town, a trinket to make Cándido see she had options and opportunities and that he should hurry and pick a ring. But now that she looked at Ulises in the flesh, Perla Inclán couldn’t help but wonder what might happen if she took this more seriously. She hadn’t realized he’d be this attractive."（ch03）

> ② "She could send her guest packing the next morning or keep him for those allotted three weeks. She could take him as a lover or rebuff any advances. She could marry or remain the spinster she’d always been."（ch03）

> ③ "“A lady must know how to manage her household, and she must be at all times a jewel that enhances the honor of her family,” he says. “A woman who disgraces her family’s name is like a decomposed piece of meat, sprouting maggots, infecting everything she touches.”"（ch03）

## 节点三：夹层里的四个化名（ch05）

**叙事概括：** Inés 换床单时摸到手提箱夹层的一叠信与红笔圈过的《Conmovedor》；深夜她进房摊牌，一巴掌把勒索谈成了五五分账。她要的不是钱——是逃出这座把她当标本压进书页的宅子的赎金。

**关键引语：**

> ① "He’ll think you’re flirting with him, and we both know it’s hopeless. You must take care of me until I pass away. There’ll be no wedding for you, not now."（ch05）

> ② "“We split the money down the middle,” she said. “You’ll need me. You don’t know her. She’s tough. Cándido’s been trying to marry her for ages and she won’t budge. She’s careful with her property, with her money.”"（ch05）

> ③ "She chose boldness and slapped him. He stared at her, open-mouthed, unable to utter a word, as if such an injury were inconceivable. Maybe it was. Maybe no one had dared to slap that pretty face before."（ch05）

## 节点四：泻药与婚戒（ch08-09）

**叙事概括：** 两人首战告捷：掺了泻药的咖啡放倒「从不生病」的 Lulú，Cándido 被迫陪妹妹远行三周；随后是清点财产（八万七千）、办公室强吻、以及「Cándido 提前三天归来」逼出的山道求婚——母亲的真戒指戴上了猎物的手指。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“An inventory. I need to know how many bank accounts she has, and with what banks. Property, safety-deposit boxes, that kind of stuff. I heard her talking to Cándido one day and he said she had ninety thousand pesos in the bank. Do you think it’s that much in cash, or was he talking about the value of the house and her accounts combined?”"（ch09）

> ② "This belongs on your hand. Put it on if you’ll take me as your husband. If not, fling it away, down the mountain. Fling my heart away too, and instead of taking a ride to Las Tunas tomorrow morning, I’ll catch the train back to Mexico City."（ch09）

> ③ "Afterward, lying in the back of the car, with Perla’s hand skimming over his chest in postcoital gratification and his brow damp with sweat, he remembered how Inés had laughed, and swallowed the bitter taste in his mouth."（ch09）

## 节点五：书桌、遗嘱与误杀（ch11-12）

**叙事概括：** 撬开书桌的那个星期日给了他们遗嘱（房子归 Inés）、被藏的父亲来信和两千金币的现钞——也给了他们同一个念头。当夜配好的毒酒阴差阳错进了 Nepomuceno 的喉咙；她一夜失声，他吐得直不起腰，而两人用一次「像抱住幽灵」的拥抱确认了共谋。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“I didn’t know. My aunt always told me he cut off all contact with us. But look at these letters,” she said, holding them up."（ch11）

> ② "He looked at her. He knew, in that moment, that separately they would have never dared to commit a murder and would have probably spent their miserable lives filled with grudges and spite. But together, homicide became not only plausible but preordained."（ch12）

> ③ "“He drank it. The old coot drank it,” he said furiously."（ch12）

## 节点六：楼梯的三重奏（ch13）

**叙事概括：** 教授之死被遗书按上「自杀」结案；现在时闪回却在同章揭底——十多年前，Perla 把要再度离家的妹妹推下楼梯，端着布娃娃的 Inés 看见了全程。凶手抱住小证人哭了，这个家的爱从此全是封口费的别名。

**关键引语：**

> ① "She saw me push her, Perla thinks."（ch13）

> ② "The girl lets go of her doll and stares at Perla with tear-filled eyes and Perla stares back at her. She embraces the child, and she cries."（ch13）

> ③ "The word “murder” chilled Perla’s blood. She felt as if a cold wind had blown into the house and banged a door shut, causing echoes to reverberate through the halls. She thought of her sister. Her headache threatened to split her skull."（ch13）

## 节点七：侦探、初吻与真话（ch14）

**叙事概括：** 绿领带的私家侦探循明信片找上门；Ulises 变装装傻脱身，Inés 用「秘密订婚三年」送走追兵。事后他吻了她——她跳开：「有人会看见。」危险把两人越拴越紧，这段关系从此在「同伙」与「情人」之间荡秋千。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Mr. Linares lied to you back there.”"（ch14）

> ② "“How did I do?” she asked."（ch14）

> ③ "The rush of adrenaline still coursing through his veins, or the way she pouted, prompted him to lean forward and kiss Inés on the mouth."（ch14）

> ④ "She bridged the distance between them and stretched out a hand. Instead of hitting him, she yanked his head down for a kiss."（ch16）

## 节点八：魔鬼椅的四声闷响（ch19）

**叙事概括：** 舞池里 Cándido 开出两万比索的价码，两人将计就约他夜里上山。曼陀罗酒壶、石头、坠崖——可「死人」中途睁眼反扑，Ulises 夺石四击毙命，车卡在离谷底一步的岩架上。两个凶手冒雨走回镇子，谁也没提那六七米。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“Kill him, of course.”"（ch19）

> ② "“My God. We really did it,” Ulises whispered."（ch19）

> ③ "It had tumbled a good six or eight meters, then wedged itself atop a narrow ledge, two wheels spinning in the air."（ch19）

## 节点九：空车位与绝笔信（ch22-23）

**叙事概括：** Ulises 把两千比索缝进箱衬、关好铁门消失在雾里；Inés 对着空车位坐在他没铺的床上。而 Perla 摊开 Cándido 的绝笔信——对骂中「你推了她」三字破封而出，姨妈举枪，夺枪混战里她中弹带伤，Perla 却被自己的狗绊下楼梯，倒在十多年前的同一格台阶下。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“You can’t leave me alone!”"（ch23）

> ② "“Then you hugged me. You murdered her and you hugged me.”"（ch23）

> ③ "“I do what I can on short notice. Next stop, Ithaca, my lady,” he said, with a lightness that almost managed to conceal any apprehension."（ch23）

## 节点十：红裙与十指相扣（ch24）

**叙事概括：** 1946 年的墨西哥城：Perla 谵妄三日吐尽真话后一概翻供，拄着父亲的手杖锁进父亲的宅子；Bartolo 与穿红裙皮草的 Inés 擦肩而过，没敢相认。餐厅卡座里，她给搭档背诵伪造的绝笔信——下一站，哈瓦那。镜子里，两个骗子十指相扣。

**关键引语：**

> ① "“You know me better than to think I wouldn’t notice someone was tailing me. I saw his reflection in a shop window. I could have shaken him off three blocks away from here.”"（ch24）

> ② "‘I cannot continue to live. I cannot silence the secret that nestles inside my heart.’"（ch24）

> ③ "She grasped his hand, lacing their fingers together."（ch24）
