# 《The Ghost of You》情感节点

## 节点一：葬礼上的初见（ch01，六月）

Caleb 在哥哥 Jack 的葬礼招待会上独自躲到后门廊，一只黑猫从树丛走近——一张看不真的脸。Tanya 出来时它已消失，什么也没看见。章尾，哀伤第一次有了形状。

- THE FIRST TIME I SAW Ghost was at my brother’s funeral reception.（ch01）
- Where his eyes, mouth, and nose should have been, there was just darkness.（ch01）
- That was when it started to sink in for me—from now on, everything would be different. Nothing could go back to the way it was before.（ch01）

## 节点二：命名与独视（ch02，十一月）

五个月后的 house show 上，闪回完成 Ghost 的命名史：从跟梢到卧室窗台。只有 Caleb 看得见它，而它对所有人都保持沉默。

- After that, I named him Ghost. That was what he had become to me—a haunting.（ch02）
- No one else could see him. No one ever mentioned a black cat following me around, let alone a faceless one. I was completely alone in seeing Ghost.（ch02）
- I looked behind me, and just as I expected, there he was—Ghost, sitting at the archway that led from the patio out to the packed driveway, the creature I couldn’t escape from.（ch02）

## 节点三：它的规则（ch03）

Caleb 逐渐摸清 Ghost 的边界：可能没有质量、能碰皱纸也能穿过世界、凝视过久会丢失时间——它不是猫，是 more creature than cat。

- I wasn’t sure if he had mass, if he was tangible or just a specter. I didn’t think he’d ever touched me, and sometimes it looked like he just passed through the world like his namesake.（ch03）
- Sometimes his body would become fuzzy, weird, and I couldn’t look at him for too long, otherwise it would start to feel like I was watching something I shouldn’t be, and then there would be a gap in my memory and time would slip away and Ghost wouldn’t be there anymore when I returned to myself, and all that was left was a pounding in my head.（ch03）
- That was when I remembered that Ghost was more creature than cat.（ch03）

## 节点四：成瘾的家族史（ch06–07）

保龄球之夜的针孔痕迹引爆 Jack 的完整编年史：十六岁开始使用、最长一年 clean、复发、海洛因。Caleb 第一次把哥哥的起点代入自己。

- Images of Jack flashed through my head—his slow, heavy steps up the stairs, the needles I saw in his bathroom trash can one time, his hoodies and long-sleeved shirts hiding his forearms.（ch06）
- JACK WAS SIXTEEN WHEN HE started using.（ch07）
- It was like the closer we were physically, the further he was from me, but the moment he had some distance, he was willing to open up.（ch07）

## 节点五：温柔的回忆与满格的恨（ch10、ch05）

车内出柜的记忆给出 Jack 最好的样子——direct, kind, supportive, present；而对 Ghost 的恨意也第一次满格。爱与恨共享同一个缺席者。

- After his death, this was a memory I fixated on a lot: his smile, the ease with which he’d accepted me, his pat on my shoulder. We didn’t always get along, and he wasn’t always a good big brother, but this was how I wanted to remember him—direct, kind, supportive, and, most of all, present.（ch10）
- For a second, anger flooded me, and I hated Ghost. I hated him more than I’d ever hated anything. I wanted to tell him to fuck off, leave me alone, stop following me, stop stop stop.（ch05）
- Something about that made me feel pierced through—seen. Because that was how it felt: like Jack was still dying. It was still June. The loss was happening over and over, in a million different ways, and each time, the grief was bitter and fresh, raw and new.（ch10）

## 节点六：浴室里的交换（ch11）

SALINE 演出夜，Caleb 逃进二楼浴室痛哭并第一次触到 Ghost——像摸一个梦。Emmett 循迹而来，两人在浴缸边交换了各自的死亡：Mallory Patton 与 Jack Stone。

- When I touched him, he wasn’t solid—not really. I could feel him, his dark fur, but it wasn’t like anything I’d ever experienced before.（ch11）
- “My ex-girlfriend killed herself a year ago,” Emmett said.（ch11）
- “Jack was a drug addict,” I said finally. “He died of a heroin overdose.”（ch11）

## 节点七：空椅子与家族真相（ch14）

感恩节的厨房里，母亲先交出「我救活过 Brooke」，再交出 Jack 幼年遭性侵的真相。第一场没有 Jack 的感恩节，空椅子成了恒量。

- “Sometimes I think that I used a miracle on her. And sometimes I think—what if I could have done that for Jack instead?”（ch14）
- “Jack . . . Jack was sexually abused.”（ch14）
- There would never be another Thanksgiving with Jack. From now on, it would always be this, always an absence, and I couldn’t stop re-realizing it—every time I thought about it again, it pierced through me, just as painful as the first time.（ch14）

## 节点八：失控之夜（ch17–18）

坏脾气的一周在演出夜点燃：醉后的质问、纯伏特加、街头的崩塌——「我想死」三个字第一次出现。深夜，他从床底拖出那瓶酒，Ghost 在窗台看着。

- “Then whose fucking fault was it?!” I didn’t even realize I was crying until now. “Why did it have to happen like this?! Why didn’t anyone do anything?!”（ch17）
- I didn’t care about that, though. I wanted to be out of my mind. I wanted to be someone else. I wanted to be dead.（ch17）
- I thought it again and again—maybe this will help—as I drank from it, the liquor burning on its way down, and it warmed me from the inside out, the blissful carelessness on its way.（ch18）

## 节点九：触底与辨认（ch20–21）

倒掉的酒、小组的首次发言、旧录像带里的棕斑猫——Caleb 在墓园里抱住 Ghost，看清它要的只是被看见。当晚，歌潮决堤。

- And I imagined, for the first time, that maybe Ghost had come to help me. Not haunt.（ch20）
- All Ghost had ever wanted from me was to be acknowledged.（ch21）
- “I’m so sorry it took me this long,” I said, hugging him tighter. “But I’m here now. I’m here.”（ch21）

## 节点十：拱门与音乐（ch22–23，次年三月）

Tanya 把 Ghost 画进了参展作品；showcase 上 Don't Let Me Disappear 唱给所有差点消失的人；终章的拱门下，那只猫又一次朝舞台转头——Caleb 靠进椅背，让音乐漫过自己。

- I know I can’t see him like you do, but sometimes . . . I swear it feels like I can tell that he’s around.（ch22）
- In a way, it felt like Jack was with us.（ch22）
- I started thinking of him as a reminder that Jack was still with me. That his life still mattered.（ch23）
- As Deck of Fools started the first song, I caught a glimpse of Ghost, sitting at the archway leading out to the patio, head turned toward the stage.（ch23）
