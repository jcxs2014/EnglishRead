# 《Mazywood》概述

## 全书梗概

**一、1926 年的夏天，一个七岁的孩子把愿望交给了一片林子。** Gracetown 的松林与沼泽草之间，Mazelle Washington 独自走到那处被叫作许愿池的水潭前面，投币，把愿望说出口；回程她被一只毛上结着泥的狗拦住，而那狗做了她从没见任何狗做过的事——低头，鞠躬，听她的命令跳舞。“I wish I had a best friend forever,” she said. “Someone to play with whenever I want. Who’ll love me better than anyone else. Who’ll keep me safe from anything bad.” She didn’t want to say in her wish that she wanted protection from her terrible older sisters, especially Doris, although it was true.（ch01）

**二、名声来得很快，而拿走的东西也来自同一张桌子。** 一家人穿着息影前的团服去 Jacksonville 见白人制片人，Mazelle 临场自封艺名 Little Mazy；几年后在 Lorenzo Studios 的办公室里，她同时收到两样东西——Charlie Chaplin 把她当成值得被全世界记住的喜剧天才，和 Mr. Lorenzo 一句关于她该怎么说话的裁定。“Because she’s not Mazelle,” Mr. Lorenzo said. “She’s Mazy. That’s how Mazy talks.”（ch04）

**三、那一夜她没有说出口的那半个字，记进了另一本账。** 假火灾的镜头她按自己的意思演完，也当着一个成年人的面把真名说了出来；可夜里 Aunt Ruby 动手之后，Scout 替她挡在前面，而她在楼梯口答了那个不必说出口的字——此后红遍好莱坞与那条不该湿的泥坑，都记在这条没有说出口的命令上。Scout looked directly down at Mazelle, and she saw the question in his eyes. She and Scout had never needed language, so Mazelle didn’t have to say the word he craved.（ch05）

**四、切回今天线，六十岁的人在洛杉矶听见了家族史的三个字母。** Johnny Washington 等一部片子等了三年，在一个只坐着两个无权者的会议室里承认自己可能等不到它拍成；然后在走回车的路上，听见自己的祖母藏在公司名字里。“The L in LDR stands for Lorenzo,” Rashad said, waiting for Johnny’s recognition. “Lorenzo Studios? Merged with Dynasty Studios back in the fifties. Then they linked up with Rialto Pictures. L-D-R. Lorenzo Studios discovered your grandmother. You know—Mazy.”（ch06）

**五、上山那个周六，每一次「认出」都像在替危险开门。** 暴雪里的私路、一辆无人白色 Jeep、门廊窗后一件红袍——他说出「一个人体模型」四个字之后膝盖才软下来；而答应照看房子的看屋人前一天一早就离了镇。“A mannequin,” he said aloud just to hear the words. His knees went weak with relief. Hollman had asked permission to create a small exhibition on Mazelle Washington from items in the house. He hadn’t sent photos, so he must have wanted to surprise Johnny with the display of her favorite bathrobe, which had been hanging in her bedroom when she died. Hollman had gotten his hands on a beige-skinned mannequin somehow.（ch12）

**六、屋子的旧账被 Tasha 一个人翻了出来，而 Johnny 的账也第一次被讲出口。** 钢琴凳里的爵士手稿、唱机架上的签名专辑、走廊尽头那幅不屈的肖像——她把这片林子认领为家族遗产；同一晚长走廊尽头那间挂上金星的小屋里，他讲出被夺走的耳机、被抓出血的耳朵、炉子上加热过的热烫梳烙下的那条疤。Bear Creek Lodge belonged to their family. History belonged to the world, but it required vigilant custodians. Mazelle Washington had been Johnny’s grandmother, but a piece of her lived in Tasha too. She raised her palm to her stomach, as if the unborn child in her generated a warm glow.（ch13）

**七、天亮后一家人弃车逃生，而「锁好门熬到天亮」在离家几十米的地方失效。** 生物在车道上撬开车门，把穿着 Mazelle 皮草袍的 Tasha 拖进雪中，Johnny 追出去昏倒在雪里；被留下的 Imani 与 Sharise 合力拖回父亲、留言、取枪，踩雪板顺足迹追进林子——决定权在这一夜交到了孩子手里。Not a fox. Not a bear. Not a snake or a bat. Somehow, the creature embodied all these things. Tasha struggled furiously, one shoe flying off as she kicked at the glove compartment.（ch20）

**八、历史线在后面补齐：许愿从来不是孩子的运气游戏，而它的账单由许愿者本人认领。** 1950 年父亲下葬之后，失了声的 Mazelle 独自走回 Wishing Pool，用一枚便士把 Scout「许」回来；1958 年 12 月，她把一封亲手写、专业印、由 Grady 穿制服递到床头的邀请函交给 Jerry Lorenzo——本章收尾几行写明，最后杀死他的不是心脏也不是 Scout。In the end, it wasn’t his heart or Scout that killed Jerry Lorenzo—it was the tree.（ch32）

**九、结尾没有证据、没有裁决。** 塌掉的洞、进不了山、无从证明的注视，全书的非凡事件在两位美国林务局巡护员的目送里退回成「不知道那是什么」；而红色 Cadillac 载着父子驶上双车道，同一章里，一只小狗得到了那个名字。“I think I’ll call you Scout.”（ch40）

## 三个主题

**一、愿望在这本书里是一笔交易，兑现即计价。** 早在七岁那年，就有同学 Priscilla Stephens 警告过她许愿的规矩——愿望会试着骗你，所以你得把要什么说清楚；这句警告在 1950 年的池边被她本人兑现了一次，也在 1941 年的恐惧里回响了一遍。Wishes always try to trick you, she warned her. So you have to say what you want without leaving nothin’ out. Down to every little itty-bitty thing.（ch01）

**二、称呼决定对待，而这本书反复换称呼。** 同一栋地方，她管它叫 Bear Creek Lodge 而不是 Mazywood；同一件东西，在「Not A Bear」与「Beast」之间来回易手，直到它咬破包装送来十几瓶完好的水。“She called it Bear Creek Lodge,” he said. “Not Mazywood.”（ch11）

**三、被记录下来与被抹掉，是这条家族线一直在争的事。** 兽巢里铺着覆膜的旧报纸，每一份写的都是 Mazelle Washington；而一个刚满六十的人，是在买他剧本的那家公司的三个字母里才找回她的名字。As she searched for other clothing, her light caught a laminated newspaper headline in the gap of a twig’s forked shadow: WHAT HAS LAZY MAZY GONE AND DONE NOW? The article’s photo, hidden in darkness before she shifted her beam, was a grinning Mazelle Washington as a young woman with a bow in her hair, wearing her Black maid’s uniform and white apron. The newspaper was faded despite the laminate, but the words and image didn’t vanish after Tasha grabbed the paper to hold it closer to her face. It couldn’t be—yet here it was.（ch33）

## 人物弧光

**Mazelle Washington**——

- 起点：一个把愿望说给林子听的孩子。She had never wanted anything as much as she wanted to dazzle her family with Scout. Gram had always said to ask what was truly in your heart, not only what you thought you deserved.（ch01）
- 中段：她学会用闭眼当盾，把不能听的挡在外面。MAZELLE WASHINGTON’S CLOSED EYES WERE her shield. Without light, Mazelle tried to filter out the sounds around her too. If she sat still, she could hear her own heart beating. Mother had taught her the trick long ago. But sounds bled past her wall of concentration.（ch23）
- 最低点：她命令那个东西把每一片痕迹清走，从保护者变成共犯。Scout killed again. Mazelle’s memory punished her with the image of Aunt Ruby’s twisted neck after she fell down the stairs, her head bent in such an unnatural way. Yes, she’d seen those sharp teeth and claws and, yes, sometimes his paws were stained from the blood of his prey. But he had seemed to be such a good listener when she told him, Stay away from people. Didn’t he know her thoughts, as before? Didn’t he understand that he couldn’t kill people?（ch31）
- 落点：病榻上她把「看护」的链条从人移交给狗，并约好回来探望时穿什么。(You never mind that worrying. You’ll be able to fend for yourself fine, just like you always did when I was working. Just listen to what I’m telling you—my grandson may never want to set foot here again. But if he does… I want you to look out for him. Don’t let anything happen to him or the people he loves. You know perfectly well what I mean, don’t you?)（ch36）
- 她一生做的事始终是同一件：把想要的东西说出口，再亲手付它的价钱。

**Johnny Washington**——

- 起点：一个把童年的东西归为幻想、靠「以后再说」活到六十岁的人。“When I was a kid, I used to think I saw a creature moving in the snow here.” It was a confession, the word creature more alarming than slithered. “I thought I saw it in my grandmother’s window once. And when I was outside alone. My uncle Ricky told me it was all in my head. But it would move under the snow and leave these weird zigzagging patterns instead of regular tracks, not like any animal I knew. Maybe I did see something back then, except now it’s bigger.”（ch19）
- 中段：秘密第一次被完整讲出口，讲出来不是解脱，是把负担分给另一个人。Later, Uncle Ricky told me this was her ‘punishment room,’ ” Johnny said. “But I hadn’t done anything except show up. It was like she was pissed at me because she hadn’t seen me in so long. But she knew damn well why Mom kept her distance.（ch15）
- 最低点：他在雪里听见两声枪响，只能朝那个东西嘶喊。A rifle shot pealed from somewhere north of him and then floated everywhere with an ethereal call-and-response. Despite the distance, the sound vibrated through him.（ch28）
- 落点：他抱回女儿、认回祖母的报纸，也认下自己打死了一个会纪念死者的生命。Johnny couldn’t help the pride that quavered his voice. The skinny kid who hadn’t wanted to shoot a rabbit—and had chickened out when he might have shot the creature when it was small—had felled the giant by remembering Uncle Ricky’s lessons. And he’d killed it in one shot, no less. He had not failed his family, either the living or the ancestors.（ch39）
- 他从「暂时还没人知道」走到「I shot it」——这本书给他的是承认，不是赦免。

**Tasha Morrison Washington**——

- 起点：一个还没动笔的传记作者，和一句她咽回去没问的追问。Later, Tasha Washington would wonder why she hadn’t pressed Johnny about what sound he thought he’d heard behind his grandmother’s lodge. Or why she never seized that moment to volunteer that she’d heard a strange noise inside too, and then under the porch steps. Why hadn’t she told him how the wall trembled ever so slightly beneath her touch? Her future self would cut off an arm to tell him, if she could have a second chance.（ch13）
- 中段：视觉被夺走后，她靠触觉与嗅觉逐项确认自己还活着。The dark was so thick that Tasha couldn’t tell if her eyes were open or closed. She fanned her face, lightly touching her eyelids, revisiting herself. As a child, she had imagined Hell as the fires foretold in the stories, but that felt foolish now. Hell was the cold, unrelenting dark.（ch33）
- 最低点：她把唯一能给的武器递出去——一根削尖的木杖。“Take this with you.” Mom held out her sharpened stick, the only thing she could offer. Her red-rimmed eyes commanded her to listen. “It’s not your friend,” she said. “It’s an animal. A killer, Imani. Do you hear me? Don’t forget that for a second.”（ch37）
- 落点：她提出警戒与着迷可以共存，替全书结尾定下不裁决的口吻。Tasha ached for them both, hoping she could be the bridge between her husband and daughter. She might be the only one who understood that caution and enchantment could coexist. She would never forget that a fearsome creature had brought her water when she was thirsty, and maybe tried to warm her up when she was cold. Was its love for Mazelle why it had died today?（ch39）
- 她是家里唯一同时拿着两样东西的人：家族史的档案，和活着走出洞口的身体。

**Imani Bernice Washington**——

- 起点：一个准备在曾祖母的真荒野里证明自己的十三岁孩子。Imani couldn’t believe they were finally going to the woods for real—not playing at going to the woods like in Big Bear, where those rental cabins were just in neighborhoods with a lot of trees. But this! Her great-grandmother’s lodge was in the actual wilderness, the kind she had dreamed of exploring since she first saw an episode of Naked and Afraid two years ago, in fifth grade. Which animals would she see? Were there any berries she could identify from her shelf of Audubon Society books? Did she have the skills to build a simple shelter near the lodge?（ch08）
- 中段：她把恐惧改写成一份能骗过姐姐的具体供词——撒谎与保护是同一个动作。“Something’s wrong,” Imani said. “And if you’re right… I don’t want to ruin everything good on this trip over a dumb deer. If it’s… the last trip… you know? As a family.” Imani was surprised to feel her throat tighten in a suppressed sob. Maybe she did believe it.（ch14）
- 最低点：她一路祈祷自己已经打死了它，同时看见树皮被凿开。Imani kept praying she had killed the Beast even as she saw tree bark chip away a foot from her target. She had missed! The Beast reared up higher.（ch27）
- 落点：她第一次为一个大人做出的选择哭给一条生命听，然后给另一条生命取名。“I think I’ll call you Scout.”（ch40）
- 她从「不许告诉爸妈」走到「我想叫它 Scout」——称谓的权柄最后落在这个孩子手上。

**Sharise**——

- 她出场就带着一串准确的观察，是那个先推出「爸妈要离婚」的孩子。Sharise drew in a long breath. Exhaled. “I think… Mom and Dad are getting a divorce.” She whispered the words.（ch08）
- 她立下的条件是「不单独出来」，而她在雪里扣住的是妹妹的冷手指。“Promise,” Imani said, locking cold fingers with her sister’s. Instead of a formal handshake, they were hanging on to each other for their lives.（ch26）
- 本章把「掳走」的过程全部交给她的目击与口述；父亲的回应是拒绝假设。“Dad—try over there!” Sharise said. She was pointing to the same spot as Uncle Ricky. They’d shared the same thought, or maybe she could hear him without knowing.（ch35）
- 她四十分钟内回到小屋、开 Jeep 把直升机引到崖顶；获救的狂喜与这份跑腿是同一件事。It was such a pure and complete joy that Sharise would live the rest of her life knowing that she should never lose faith.（ch39）

**Scout／那个东西**——

- 它的第一个动作是鞠躬：一只听懂命令、会跳舞的狗。But the dog did something she’d never seen any dog do: This dog lowered its head, let out a whimper… and collapsed its front legs, rear haunches still raised up high. The effect sounded crazy as she thought the word, but her eyes saw it plainly: This dog was bowing to her like one of King Arthur’s knights in the stories Mother taught her at the schoolhouse.（ch01）
- 1950 年它从池水里出来时已经变形，而眼睛还是那双棕色的眼睛。It was Scout. Changed, distorted, different in every way—but Scout all the same. She saw Scout in the eyes, but she also felt Scout vibrating in her thoughts, her missing piece returned. Scout reborn. Scout reimagined.（ch30）
- 它把自己写成「不是狐、不是熊、不是蛇也不是蝠」，本书从未给它一个物种结论。Not a fox. Not a bear. Not a snake or a bat. Somehow, the creature embodied all these things. Tasha struggled furiously, one shoe flying off as she kicked at the glove compartment.（ch20）
- 它朝黑暗暴怒低吼，也咬破包装送来完好的水；最后它躺在洞室的地上，翼膜垂着。When Imani opened her eyes, her first sight was Scout lying on his side, flaps limp and splayed across the floor. She touched the rough fur of its belly, but no motion signified breath. She couldn’t bear to try to look at Scout’s face. This incredible animal was dead.（ch38）
- 它的来历原文只写成 Mazelle 自己的猜想——这条悬置到最后也没有被解开。
