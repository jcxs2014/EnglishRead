# This is the Night They Come for You 金句精选

> 收录标准：① 在章节内有结构功能（不是随手可替换的漂亮话）；② 与前后章存在互文；③ 可逐字回溯至 `text/` 提取原文。共五辑，按叙事顺序排列。

## 一、起手：一个被遗忘的人与一位来访者（ch01–ch02）

> “Glancing at a ghostly reflection of himself in the dust-filmed glass, he sees a lean, heavy-browed man with thinning curly grey hair, dressed in a crumpled brown suit and a faded yellow shirt, open at the neck. He should be wearing his tie, in case anyone calls in to see him. But no one is going to call in to see him. Friends made in earlier days in the service are all either dead or long retired. He’s a forgotten man.”（ch01）

全书第一个照镜子式的出场。它同时交代了三件事：这个人年近退休、家境清寒，而且——最后一句才是重点——已经没有人需要他了。领带被特意提到，因为下一段它就会派上用场。

> “Ah, le pouvoir. Unlike hizb fransa, there’s good reason to believe, as most Algerians do, that some power over and above the republic’s constitution – some authority that is almost inhuman, despite humans being its ready and willing accomplices – has always had and always will have the final word in the nation’s disputes. And that authority is known to all simply as … le pouvoir: as tireless as it is merciless, always ready to crush any organization – any citizen – who dares to dream of a free and uncorrupt Algeria; the ultimate arbiter of all their fates, that will not be defied and cannot be defeated.”（ch01）

小说对阿尔及利亚政治现实的定义不是由谁宣布的，而是插在叙述中间的一段解释：权力机构之外还有一层控制着国家根本事务的东西，人人知道它存在，却没人能点名。这句解释为全书定调——故事里所有真正的对手都不在任何一份花名册上。

> “Taleb feels an unfamiliar lightness of heart as he stands there. He should have found some excuse to refuse the Director’s assignment. No good can come of seeking the truth about how Laloul was able to embezzle so much money over such a long period without being detected. Too many people had and still have too much to lose for such a quest ever to be allowed to succeed. And yet … and yet. He is actually keener to take on the challenge than he would ever have imagined. What is he looking for? Kudos? Redemption? Excitement? The truth – the real truth – against all the odds? He’s not sure. But something more than his existence has supplied him with for far too long. Yes, that’s what he’s looking for. A purpose, a meaning. A last shot at …”（ch01）

这段"不合时宜的轻快"是全书的动力来源：Taleb 接下的不是职务，而是终于有一件事值得做。作者让这个转变发生在楼梯的半层上，而不是在办公室里。

> “She closes her eyes for a moment. ‘This is very difficult for me, Stephen. Monsieur Saidi is asking me to say whether I think the document is genuine. It is typewritten, you see. They have sent me a photocopy. It looks like it was typed on the kind of old manual machine that Papa used, but I cannot be sure. Even so, everything in it suggests to me Papa wrote it. It’s not all about Harriet. There are … other things. I think Monsieur Saidi hopes to sell the document to the French press. You will understand why when you read it.’”（ch02）

Suzette 与 Gray 的第一次见面就在问一个她不能明说的问题：究竟谁需要她说这份文件是假的。这句话把全书前半段最要紧的那层交易说穿了。

## 二、一九六五：巴黎的旧账（ch04–ch09）

> “‘Because you are a white European, Nigel,’ said Haddad. ‘And as you see … we are not.’”（ch04）

这是 Haddad 拉 Nigel 入局时给出的理由。叙述者没有加任何评论——殖民与被殖民的关系被压缩成一句话，而 Nigel 恰好是能被这句话说服的那种人。

> “‘That,’ said Haddad, ‘is Guy Tournier, special aide to Monsieur le Président. He is our target.’”（ch05）

一句平静的宣布，读者与 Nigel 是同时间听见的：要杀的是总统身边的特别助理。此后全书所有追查都从这里的一扇门开始。

> “‘Your experience is out of date, Taleb.’ So there it is. His experience is out of date. And so, Hidouchi clearly believes, is he. It would be useless to point out to her that the past determines everything that happens in their country and that experience is one of the few protections against its ravages. She will have to learn that for herself. ‘The current leadership has no interest in protecting the reputation of former regimes,’ she continues. ‘Rather the contrary, if anything.’”（ch06）

Hidouchi 对 Taleb 说的第一句硬话。它不只是代际冲突，也是全书对"经验"这件事的态度：在这个国家，过去的办案方式本身已经过期。

> “‘There’s no such thing in Algeria, Stephen. Everything since 1962 happened yesterday.’”（ch09）

Gray 抱怨时间过去太久，得到的却是这句话。作者让一个阿尔及利亚人亲口说出"这里没有过去"——这不是修辞，是调查的规则。

## 三、自述《J'avoue》：三十年的判词（ch11–ch16）

> “But listen to me, Chevrette. Whatever you decide to do, don’t go back to Algeria. It consumes its sons and daughters. It shows them no mercy. The day I left, I swore I would never return. I walked down to Agha station for the early train to Oran on a spring morning in 1994 and gazed back across the roofs of Algiers as the sun was rising, casting a dusty golden light on the Martyrs’ Memorial that seemed to squat on the city like some giant locust. And the certainty was hard and clear in my mind. Algeria is a quicksand. If you are fortunate enough to escape its sucking grip, as you and I have been, you should never set foot there again. I never will. And you never should either.’”（ch11）

Riad Nedjar 给出的告别话，也是全书对"回阿尔及利亚"这个动作最重的一次判断：它会吞掉自己的儿女。

> “In keeping with national tradition, the past at Police HQ, though seldom visited, is never discarded. Everything is there – if you know where to look.”（ch12）

档案室里的这句话解释了整部小说的可能性：之所以 2020 年的人还能翻出 1965 年的案子，是因为这个制度从不打扫自己的过去。

> “I had to live with my part in her death. I had to live with the knowledge of my own lack of courage. I feel ashamed now that I managed to do that.”（ch13）

自述里最重的一句自我审判。他没有把责任推给那两个人，而是承认自己最大的问题是当年没有勇气——这是全书少见的、不带辩解的一份证词。

> “Because normality is only another set of lies. And I need to stop lying. Especially to myself.”（ch13）

全书最锋利的定义之一：他等了一辈子的"正常"，最后被自己认成另一套谎话。这句之后，自述只剩下走出去晒太阳的那几行。

> “His search reveals that Dalby was shot through the head on the balcony of his apartment some time on Monday 21 February 1994. His body was discovered late that afternoon by his former employee at the bookshop, Riad Nedjar, who regularly delivered food and supplies to him.”（ch16）

Dalby 的死因由一句冷冰冰的调查结果给出：1994 年 2 月 21 日，他在自家阳台上被一枪打中头部。父亲留给女儿的认罪书，与这句话构成全书最结实的一处因果。

## 四、钱、权力与量水人（ch14–ch24）

> “You can never be rid of all of them. Le pouvoir is a self-replicating organism in the body politic of Algeria.”（ch14）

一句话概括了这个国家权力的性质：它没有总部、没有法人，也可以被复制、被继承、被替换。故事的结局正是以此为条件：人可以死，结构不会。

> “‘A child is a woman’s greatest strength,’ she laments through her first puff. ‘And also her greatest weakness.’”（ch18）

Abderrahmane 谈自己的儿子时说的话。它既是一个母亲的坦白，也是全书审讯戏的钥匙——Hidouchi 正是靠她儿子的身世，把这个老太太的抵赖击穿的。

> “Ignorance is dangerous. But knowledge may be still more dangerous.”（ch21）

Taleb 的处境被写成一句递进：不知道很危险，知道得更多可能更危险。这句话针对的正是他手里那盘磁带——他越接近真相，越没有退路。

> “We have no allies from this point on.’ She holsters her gun. ‘Except each other.’”（ch23）

所有盟友都变成对手之后，她只留下这一句。全书对信任最干净的一次定义就停在这四个词上。

> “‘We gave you your country, my friends, but it came with a condition we were determined to impose. You can’t prosper. Independence can’t be allowed to work. Algeria – your Algeria – must be a failure.”（ch23）

独立是给的，代价是被安排成不能繁荣——这段话出自图尼耶被逼问时的录音，是小说对殖民遗产最直接的一次判断，而且是由法国一方的人亲口说出的。

> “‘When you’re my age, Agent Hidouchi, you’ll find a quiet life much more attractive than confronting any number of enemies – personal or national.’”（ch24）

Taleb 的自我定位：他想要的不是敌人，而是安宁。作者随后用巴黎街头的那场枪战证明这份安宁不会到来。

## 五、收束：胜利与失败，最后一页（ch27–ch31）

> “He nods for Gray to get in. ‘Zarbi says you live.’”（ch27）

Storza 替 Zarbi 传给 Gray 的赦令。四个词，却是全书最毛骨悚然的一句"没事了"——因为发布它的人正准备杀人。

> “‘Nothing,’ he replies. ‘I’m just a tourist … passing through.’”（ch30）

被烧过一次房子、被卷入两次谋杀的 Gray，对警察给出的自我说明。这也是他替所有人说的一句谎：他从来不是路过的游客。

> “‘We haven’t finished with you, Taleb. Yes, you can go home and crawl back to whatever squalid apartment you live in. But remember this. There will be a night we come for you. That’s a promise.’”（ch30）

书名出处。Ménard 在飞机场放走 Taleb，却把最后一枪留在话里——结局因此不是和解，而是一次悬置。

> “He could have sent text messages, but he prefers old-fashioned postcards, however long they may take to arrive. They’re a nod to the past he’s left behind.”（ch31）

Gray 的收束方式不是复仇成功，而是改用了最慢的通信方式。作者让他用一张明信片代替他追了五十五年的档案。

> “‘This is about your real grandfather, Élodie. This is about my father.’”（ch31）

Suzette 把 Élodie 一直不知道的那层亲属关系说破。这一句之后，故事里最后一个被蒙在鼓里的人也不再是了。

> “And still he says nothing. Because, in truth, he doesn’t need to. They both know the answer to her question. They both know what he’s going to do.”（ch31）

全书最后一句：他没有回答，因为不必回答。作者把答案留在空白里，把故事交回读者手上。
