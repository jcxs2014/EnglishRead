# 《The House on Otley Road》情感节点

> 按时间线排列的 10 个情感节点，覆盖 1999 与 2019 两条线。引语均取自章节精读中已过门禁的原文。

## ① 门口的男人（ch01）

序章只有三百多词：一个男人站在 Otley Road 的石阶上，用"我有些你可能感兴趣的消息"让一个年轻女子把门开大。全章没有名字、没有年份，只有一套熟练的话术。

> "He only ever told anyone a version of the truth. It surprised him when she pulled back the door slightly wider. He’d sort of expected her to close it in his face."（ch01）

> "He could see she remained uncertain. He must put her at her ease, but that was fine, he knew all the techniques. He smiled again, keeping his hands firmly inside his coat pockets."（ch01）

门缝变大的那一刻，全书的悲剧已经发生；剩下的四十四章都在解释这扇门为什么会被打开。

## ② 选一个不会被忘记的房间（ch02）

2019 年秋天，Olivia 与 Millie、Bella、Abi 搬进 Otley Road。分房靠抽外卖单，她抽到最离谱的那张，挑走了临街大房间。她给出的理由是采光与空间，真正的原因是门口。

> "She hoped that taking the room near the entrance would secure her place in the group. The others would have to pass it whenever they came and went. They’d find it harder to forget about her, which was what she feared most. Being overlooked, demoted from an equal to more of a hanger-on."（ch02）

> "It was surprisingly cheap, this slice of prime student real estate they had been so lucky to find. Instead of considering why, the girls had blindly taken the advice of the estate agent, to hurry and sign the contract before anyone else."（ch02）

她为"被看见"选了那间房；房租便宜得反常，却没有人问为什么。

## ③ 死讯与玻璃（ch03–ch04）

开学前夜，Millie 从精酿酒吧回来，带回一个陌生男生的话：那栋门上有半只石兽的房子，二十年前死过一个女孩，就在 Olivia 那间前卧室。四个人的第一反应不是搬走，而是算搬走要付多少代价。

> "Olivia swallowed back the saliva that had pooled in her mouth. If Millie’s story was true, a girl had been murdered in her bedroom and now she had to sleep there for a year with this knowledge. The others were avoiding eye contact with her."（ch03）

> "Bile stung the back of Olivia’s throat. While she was reading the story, she had briefly managed to forget it had unfolded where she now sat. She tried to challenge what her therapist would have called the intrusive thoughts, as she was taught during her unsuccessful treatment for anxiety."（ch03）

她选择留下，并把脚朝向床头睡下——不是勇敢，是不想被排除。

在 Dry Dock 酒吧，Elliot 又转述继兄听来的案情：死者房间的碎玻璃落在被翻乱的东西上面，而不是下面。Olivia 第一次意识到，警方的"入室盗窃"版本可能是假的。

> "‘The girl’s stuff had been messed about with. Thrown on the floor, you know, like the burglar was looking for valuables. But one of her flatmates noticed that the broken glass from the window was on top of her messed-up stuff, not underneath it.’"（ch04）

> "She would work out what had really happened in her room. Find out if Elliot was telling the truth. Tell the world something it didn’t already know and make her mark."（ch04）

这一夜她决定查案；动机里既有正义，也有"留下印记"。

## ④ Bradford 的圣诞酒（ch05）

1999 年 12 月，Aiden 开车带 Emily 去 Bradford 喝酒，用不存在的朋友"Dave"骗过妻子。她在桌边试探"如果 Jessop 教授知道了会怎样"，他的杯子停在唇边。

> "She’d been distracted from her usual pursuits since the whatever-it-was had started with Dr Berkeley, who she was now allowed to address as Aiden. She wouldn’t call it an affair, since he was the one having the affair, not her."（ch05）

> "Somehow it felt even more transgressive than perching on his desk while he unzipped his trousers. She could also hardly believe there was a child seat in the back."（ch05）

回程车里她看见儿童座椅，选择不看；这段关系里所有她不想承认的东西，都在后座。

## ⑤ 妻子知道了（ch07）

Gloria 从同事 Marie 口中确认丈夫的外遇对象是二年级学生 Emily Pierce。她没有当场发作，而是在系里的休息室替他把"额外辅导"的说法圆好，回家后再一笔一笔重算这段婚姻。

> "Professor Gloria Jessop knew about the girl, of course she did. She had not been given a chair at the early age of forty, appointed head of department at forty-one, without possessing a sharpness extending beyond the subject of modern history."（ch07）

> "So long as neither of them referred directly to the probable nature of Aiden’s relationship with the girl, then perhaps Gloria could convince herself that Marie wasn’t fully aware. But Gloria had never felt surer."（ch07）

她的骄傲让她不肯公开爆炸，也让她低估了丈夫的无赖程度。

## ⑥ 独自查案的女孩（ch08–ch09）

Olivia 在宿舍里搜索 Emily 的案子，从验尸报道读到死亡时间，又靠 Elliot 继兄给的错误拼写找到 Aiden Berkeley。她起草邮件，删掉，又在某个她无法解释的时刻被"代发"。

> "His picture showed a man in late middle-age, a thin grey-white beard covering a strong jawline. Olivia was tempted to think his eyes looked haunted. But people’s eyes couldn’t really look haunted, that was just a turn of phrase used in airport bestsellers."（ch08）

> "‘What do you think of him?’ asked Olivia, heartened by the eye-roll. It suggested Bella was an ally. ‘Bit rapey, maybe?’ ‘I know what you mean.’"（ch09）

她已经摸到危险，却把它当作一个可以讲给朋友听的笑话。

## ⑦ 记者接手（ch15）

2019 年，报社记者 Kate Marsden 被从情人 Smith 的床上叫起，派往利兹报道 Olivia Kavanagh 被刺案。她二十年前报道过同一条街上的 Emily 案——这一次，她认为这是"她的故事"。

> "Kate Marsden’s phone buzzed on the bedside table. Smith’s bedside table, because she had ended up staying at his. Again. She had to stop doing that, it was unprofessional and … actually, she couldn’t remember the other reasons, apart from that it was unlikely to end well."（ch15）

> "‘It’s Dan from the newsdesk,’ said the news editor unnecessarily. She knew his voice too well, her sleep had been ruined by it on countless occasions. He did not apologise for calling her at whatever hour this was."（ch15）

她的职业本能既是能力，也是她后来被牵着走的入口。

## ⑧ 被调虎离山（ch25–ch27）

Kate 开二百三十英里到 Cardiff 堵 Aiden Berkeley，在门外对着信箱喊话时收到 Smith 的短信：Elliot 被捕了。她意识到自己被一个学生设局调开；门随即打开，Aiden 站在门口。

> "Suddenly, she found herself tumbling forwards. Flung out her arms to keep herself from crashing to the ground as the front door swung wide open. Pulling herself back up, she saw a figure in the doorway."（ch25）

> "This was why she was alone. She pushed men away by bickering, holding them up to standards they either could not or would not meet. Standards she herself didn’t even meet."（ch27）

这一刻她失去的不只是线索，还有对自己判断力的信任。

## ⑨ 跨年夜：表白、拒绝与枕头（ch44）

真相章。Matty 在逃跑途中回忆 1999 年的那个夜晚：他表白、被拒、听到 Aiden 的名字，然后抓住她，用枕头把她按在床上。二十年后，他又用"Emily 的朋友"这个身份敲开了 Olivia 的门。

> "‘He’s called Aiden and he’s a lecturer. He’s coming to collect me at 8.30 p.m. to take me to a hotel. We’re going to spend New Year’s Eve together.’"（ch44）

> "He let go of one of her arms, grabbed a pillow, brought it to her face and pushed her down onto the bed."（ch44）

全书的暴力没有预谋的复杂，只有一个人不肯接受"不"。

## ⑩ 花园里的两颗星（ch45）

2019 年跨年夜，Kate 在朋友家的花园里复盘一切：Smith 不认罪、被还押，监控与法医证据指向他。她决定把整件事写下来，然后对并排的两颗星说晚安。

> "Kate’s gaze fell on two particular stars burning side by side. They looked only a very short distance apart but could be light years from each other. She told herself one was Emily, the other Olivia."（ch45）

> "‘Goodnight,’ she whispered to them both. Then she stepped back into the party to see in another new year with all her friends."（ch45）

结尾没有胜利，只有一个人选择继续生活，并记住两个本该活着的女孩。
