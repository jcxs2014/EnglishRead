# Venus Fly Trap — 金句集（Memorable Quotes）

> 以下句子均经 verify_quotes.py 逐字核验，出自 epub 原文。按主题分组。

## 一、关于爱与控制

- "You can't make someone love you out of fear. You can't make someone stay with you because they're afraid. And she is afraid of you." — Ch29（Louise 对 Henry）
- "Because love is pain, Louise. It hurts. The way that I love her, it hurts. But that just means it's real." — Ch29（Henry 的"爱即痛"论）
- "I'd never hurt her. I'd never raise my voice to her. She would never have to be afraid of me. I would hold her when she needed me to. I would kiss her when she was in pain." — Ch29（Louise 的反论）

## 二、关于 Cat 的本质

- "Cat eats people. That's the only way I know how to describe it. She knows what you want from her, and she becomes that. And then she eats you. She gobbles you up whole and spits you out when she's done with you." — Ch29（Henry 的判词）
- "She didn't believe in altruism. She didn't believe we ever did anything that wasn't in our own best interest." — Ch31（Louise 转述 Cat 的信条）
- "When I saw you next to her, I realized you look like Cat. You're wearing the same clothes she does. You've done your make-up like she does." — Ch28（Oliver 的 external 点破）

## 三、关于杀意与未杀

- "You have to do it." — Ch27（Cat 的冷静部署）
- "I'm the significant other. I would be the prime suspect." — Ch27（Cat 的法律算计）
- "You're a piece of shit. And you're not worth the effort of putting this knife through you." — Ch31（Louise 松手）
- "The only way to avoid that risk was to make sure someone else was convicted." — Ch31（Cat 的顶罪设计）

## 四、关于自我与迷失

- "Her eyes told me she understood now. She wasn't angry or afraid of me any more. The tattoo didn't mean I was unhinged. It just meant that I loved her. It meant that we were the same." — Ch27（刺青被重读为"我们相同"）
- "I was a mess: make-up dried and smeared all over my face, wearing nothing but my underwear." — Ch30（案发后 Louise 照镜）
- "It was a tail." — Ch30（老鼠尾巴，全书最冷的物象）

## 五、关于清醒的代价

- "I had called her Catherine Howard once, a long time ago. How silly of me. She was no Catherine Howard – she was Catherine Parr. The survivor." — Ch31（Louise 最终的读懂）
- "There was no one else who knew with absolute certainty that I hadn't done it. There was only her." — Ch31（最孤独的句子）
- "I didn't kill him. ... I didn't kill him." — Ch31 / Ch30（全书重复的否认，直至无人相信）
