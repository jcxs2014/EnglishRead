# Chapter 33

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Bea 在 Olivia 家过夜，收到 Valencia 案文件，发现 Olivia 曾是 Kelli 的 Legal Aid 代表——暗示 Olivia 与 Kelli 是同谋；Bea 与 Olivia 对峙，Olivia 摔倒撞到头部，Bea 报警并致电侦探。
- **情感弧线位置**：恐惧 → 崩溃——Bea 发现"盟友"（Olivia）是"敌人"，而她再次"伤害"了一个人。
- **人物弧线**：Bea 的"报警"是全书最"道德"的时刻——她选择"承担责任"而非"逃跑"。
- **叙事手法**："公寓"作为"陷阱"——Bea 在 Olivia 的公寓里"发现真相"，而"真相"让她"无处可逃"。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Then I find Kelli. Her name in the file is Kelli Duncan, but those big blue eyes are the exact same. Where photos of the other children show understandably sad faces, hers is defiant—chin stuck out and eyes staring straight into the photographer's lens. She was determined from the start. I read her file. / Then there are photos of drawings. Well, more than drawings; it's her art therapy portfolio. . . . Then I get to her assigned Legal Aid representative for the duration of the proceedings. / Olivia Oxford.

**中文理解**：然后我找到了 Kelli。她在文件中的名字是 Kelli Duncan，但那些大大的蓝色眼睛完全一样。其他孩子的照片显示出可理解的悲伤面孔，而她的很反抗——下巴翘起，眼睛直视摄影师的镜头。她从一开始就决心坚定。我读她的文件。/ 然后有画的照片。嗯，不止是画；这是她的艺术治疗作品集。/ 然后我看到了她在诉讼期间指定的法律援助代表。/ Olivia Oxford。

**关键词**：Kelli Duncan / defiant / art therapy portfolio / Olivia Oxford

**为什么这样写**：Bea 的"Olivia Oxford"是全书最"震惊"的发现——她的"导师"（Olivia）是 Kelli 的"法律援助代表"，暗示 Olivia 与 Kelli 是"同谋"。defiant（反抗的）是 Kelli 的"性格"——她"从不屈服"。

**读者视角提示**：Bea 的"Olivia Oxford"让读者意识到——她"信任"的人"背叛"了她。

---

> **原句 2:** A sudden memory wrenches forward in my mind. The night at Solo, when I was trying to find Olivia, I saw her talking to someone at the bar. Someone I didn't know. Until I met her. I've completely forgotten until just now that she was talking to Kelli. / Have they been in contact this whole time?

**中文理解**：一个突然的记忆在我脑海中涌现。在 Solo 那晚，当我试图找到 Olivia 时，我看到她在酒吧和某人说话。我不认识的人。直到我见到她。我完全忘记了，直到现在才想起她在和 Kelli 说话。/ 她们一直在联系吗？

**关键词**：wrenches forward / talking to Kelli / in contact this whole time

**为什么这样写**：Bea 的"wrenches forward"是全书最"记忆闪回"的时刻——她"想起"了 Olivia 和 Kelli 的"对话"，而这段"记忆"是"关键证据"。in contact this whole time 暗示 Olivia 和 Kelli 的"同谋"是"长期"的。

**读者视角提示**：Bea 的"in contact this whole time"让读者意识到——Olivia 的"帮助"是"设计"的。

---

> **原句 3:** It hits me. She knows. Because this whole time, Olivia and Kelli have been in on it together. / That's how Kelli found me. She didn't happen to run into me that day at the coffee stand. It was all orchestrated. / Of course Kelli wasn't working alone. And of course it was Olivia. Olivia would have known about my problems with Landon. If they were looking for someone to help them, I was perfect. But what if they were looking for something more? The thought washes over me like a wave, and I am left gasping for air. / They weren't looking for help; they never needed my help. They were looking for someone to frame. This was never going to end after Saul's death. / I was never going to get away with any of it. And now I'm here with Olivia—I let her lead me straight to her apartment. What can she be planning now?

**中文理解**：我突然明白了。她知道。因为一直以来，Olivia 和 Kelli 一直在一起。/ 这就是 Kelli 找到我的方式。她不是那天在咖啡摊偶然遇到我的。一切都是策划好的。/ 当然 Kelli 不是单独行动。当然是 Olivia。Olivia 会知道我和 Landon 的问题。如果他们在找一个人帮助他们，我是完美的。但如果他们在找更深层的东西呢？这个想法像波浪一样冲刷我，我喘不过气来。/ 他们不是来找帮助的；他们从来不需要我的帮助。他们是在找一个替罪羊。这不会在 Saul 的死之后结束。/ 我永远无法逃脱这一切。现在我和 Olivia 在一起——我让她直接带我去了她的公寓。她现在在计划什么？

**关键词**：in on it together / orchestrated / looking for someone to frame / never going to get away

**为什么这样写**：Bea 的"looking for someone to frame"是全书最"觉醒"的时刻——她意识到自己"被设计"了。orchestrated（策划好的）暗示一切都是"计划"的，而 Bea 是"棋子"。

**读者视角提示**：Bea 的"never going to get away"让读者意识到——她的"罪行"已经"无法挽回"。

---

> **原句 4:** She goes down. Her head hits the bathroom counter, a sharp crack like the sound of a gravestone being split open. Her damp tendrils twist around her head like vines; a small stream of red seeps out from beneath her. / Slowly backing away, I shiver. "I'm sorry, Olivia." / I reach the front door and stop. Enough, I think. It's been enough. I pull out my phone and make two calls. The first is to 911. "There's been an accident. Please send an ambulance." I give the address and hang up. The second is to Detective Branson, so she knows where to find at least one of Landon's killers.

**中文理解**："她倒了下去。她的头撞到浴室柜台，发出尖锐的裂缝声，像墓碑被劈开的声音。她潮湿的卷发像藤蔓一样缠绕在头上；一小股红色液体从她身下渗出。"/ 我慢慢后退，颤抖着。"对不起，Olivia。"/ 我走到前门，停下来。够了，我想。已经够了。我拿出手机打了两个电话。第一个是 911。"出了事故。请派救护车。"我报了地址，挂断。第二个是打给侦探 Branson，让她知道至少能找到 Landon 的一个凶手。

**关键词**：gravestone being split open / tendrils / one of Landon's killers

**为什么这样写**：Bea 的"one of Landon's killers"是全书最"自首"的时刻——她承认自己是"凶手之一"。gravestone 的比喻暗示 Olivia 可能"死了"，而 Bea 的"再次杀人"让她的"罪恶"更深。

**读者视角提示**：Bea 的"one of Landon's killers"让读者意识到——她终于"承认"了自己的"罪行"。
