# 3. In Which Wacker Reads KRAY In On Project GOOWIKI

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Wacker 把 KRAY 叫到四楼办公室，翻旧照、讲往事、递报纸，最后把「尚不存在」的 GOOWIKI 计划与二百亿美元预算一起交给 KRAY。
- **情感弧线位置**：全章由 Wacker 的怀旧独白与官僚热情主导，KRAY 表面顺从、内心审视，情绪在荒诞与克制之间摆动。
- **线索结构**：旧护照、挑战币、父亲的 Harrier 往事与失败的 NOC 计划共同为人物背景和 GOOWIKI 项目铺线。
- **人物弧线**：Wacker 显露自恋与对「势头」的迷信；KRAY 识破项目的荒唐却选择沉默，展现其生存智慧。
- **叙事手法**：全知第三人称在对话间插入讽刺评论与清单式铺陈，制造喜剧节奏。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Wacker’s Fourth Floor corner office looked out onto a gigantic Agency parking area, the Green Lot, jam-packed with cars, many of which appeared to be BMWs. The Green Lot also boasted a sleek, menacing, matte black, and very sexy A-12 reconnaissance jet on static display, looking like it was just about to rocket up into the sky once more to go engage in monkey business, probably somewhere over Russia."
**中文理解**：Wacker 的四楼角落办公室正对中央情报局巨大的格林停车场，场内挤满汽车，不少是宝马；停车场还陈列一架哑光黑的 A-12 侦察机，仿佛随时要再次升空去执行任务。
**关键词**：corner office、Green Lot、BMWs、A-12、reconnaissance jet、monkey business、Russia
**为什么这样写**：把平民消费符号（宝马）与退役战机并置，再用「monkey business」把间谍行动说成胡闹，开篇即立起嘲讽 CIA 的基调。
**读者视角提示**：A-12 的「蓄势待发」其实是反讽——它早已退役，机构雄风不再。

> **原句 2:** "Wacker reached into a desk drawer and pulled out an old passport. He opened it and handed it to KRAY. KRAY looked at the picture. It was Wacker in his late twenties, already plump and sporting a large blonde Afro-style hairdo. Wacker was goofy looking even then."
**中文理解**：Wacker 从抽屉里拿出旧护照，打开后递给 KRAY；照片里二十多岁的他已然发胖，留着金色爆炸头，那时就显得滑稽。
**关键词**：desk drawer、passport、picture、late twenties、plump、blonde、Afro-style hairdo、goofy
**为什么这样写**：让上司主动出示年轻时的笨拙照片，把中年威仪与青春傻气并置成一个自嘲形象。
**读者视角提示**：Wacker 亲手翻出旧照的举动说明他今天有意向 KRAY 交心。

> **原句 3:** "Momentum. The big Mo. You go out on a tour inside a decent station like that and get promoted, you’ve got momentum. Do that and keep your nose clean afterwards, there’s no place to go but up. Everyone out there with me has gone up and up and up. All of us together. That’s how you make a career in this business. Don’t forget that."
**中文理解**：说话人把晋升归结为「势头」：在像样的站点外派并获提拔就有了势头，此后安分守己便一路向上，同僚们全都如此。
**关键词**：Momentum、big Mo、tour、station、promoted、nose clean、up、career、business
**为什么这样写**：用俚语「the big Mo」把晋升讲成近乎迷信的玄学，讽刺 CIA 升迁靠势头而非实绩。
**读者视角提示**：留意「All of us together」的集体语气，说话人把个人际遇当成了普遍规律。

> **原句 4:** "“That’s right. The paper got almost everything wrong, of course, but it doesn’t take a SIS-4 Division Chief to read between the lines. We tried. We failed. We’re still struggling with it. The last guy in this office was handed the job of picking up the pieces, but he fumbled the ball too. Now it’s my turn.”"
**中文理解**：说话人承认报纸几乎全写错了，但不必是处长也能读懂内情：项目试过、败过，前任也没能收拾好，现在轮到自己。
**关键词**：paper、wrong、SIS-4 Division Chief、read between the lines、tried、failed、struggling、picking up the pieces、fumbled the ball、my turn
**为什么这样写**：用「We tried. We failed.」的短促排比把失败史说成履历，讽刺官僚把烂摊子当晋升跳板。
**读者视角提示**：「read between the lines」也在提醒读者，报纸报道本身不可信，真相要靠推导。

> **原句 5:** "KRAY picked up one of Wacker’s challenge coins and examined it. The obverse displayed CIA’s logo in bright cloisonné colors. The reverse showed a compass rose in brass. He slipped the coin into his jacket pocket. Even though he would never admit it to himself, KRAY was something of a kleptomaniac."
**中文理解**：KRAY 拿起 Wacker 的一枚挑战币端详，正面是中情局徽标，背面是黄铜指南玫瑰；他把它滑进夹克口袋，其实他有点偷窃癖。
**关键词**：challenge coins、examined、obverse、CIA’s logo、cloisonné、reverse、compass rose、brass、jacket pocket、kleptomaniac
**为什么这样写**：叙述者直接揭穿 KRAY「绝不承认」的偷窃癖，用全知口吻让读者看见人物的自欺。
**读者视角提示**：KRAY 偷币却自认不是小偷，这种自我欺骗正是全书喜剧的引擎之一。

> **原句 6:** "“That’s because it doesn’t exist yet,” said Wacker. “But it will, because we’re going to make it exist. It’s a good fit with the Director’s new Center on Human Resources and National Security, don’t you think? Could create some synergies, no? Produce some nice spin-offs, deliverables, and value-addeds?”"
**中文理解**：Wacker 承认「变革性人力资源」这个概念还不存在，但他们要让它存在，还问这是否与局长的新中心契合、能否产生协同效应。
**关键词**：doesn’t exist yet、make it exist、Director’s new Center、synergies、spin-offs、deliverables、value-addeds
**为什么这样写**：让 Wacker 亲口承认概念尚未存在，却已备好一串商业黑话，讽刺官僚先造词再造事实。
**读者视角提示**：注意「synergies、deliverables、value-addeds」全是空洞术语，术语越漂亮，概念本身越可疑。

> **原句 7:** "I need you to do some research, and I need you to use your brain. One month from today I want you to give me a plan for how we can use Transformational Human Resources as cover to accomplish the goals laid out in that newspaper article for a barnyard full of new NOCs."
**中文理解**：Wacker 要 KRAY 做研究并动脑，一个月内交出一份计划，用「变革性人力资源」作掩护，实现报纸文章里为大批新 NOC 设定的目标。
**关键词**：research、use your brain、One month from today、plan、Transformational Human Resources、cover、newspaper article、barnyard full、NOCs
**为什么这样写**：用「barnyard full of new NOCs」把特工说成牲口，暴露出 Wacker 对计划对象毫无敬意。
**读者视角提示**：任务所依赖的「掩护」本身尚不存在，KRAY 接下的活从一开始就荒诞。

> **原句 8:** "I want them all to have jobs as Transformational Human Resources consultants, and I want them to be able to live anywhere in the world. I want each of them to be a freestanding singleton who can continue operating irregardless of whether any of the others succeed or get sucked into the combine. … We have twenty billion dollars we need to allocate. Got it?”"
**中文理解**：Wacker 要所有 NOC 都当「变革性人力资源」顾问、可住在世界任何地方，各自独立运作互不拖累，并说有二百亿美元要分配。
**关键词**：jobs、consultants、anywhere in the world、freestanding singleton、operating、irregardless、combine、twenty billion dollars、allocate
**为什么这样写**：把「每人独立、互不拖累」的特工设想与二百亿预算并置，喜剧效果来自数字与荒诞概念的落差。
**读者视角提示**：二百亿美元是本章的关键数字，后续情节的荒唐程度将以它为标尺。

## 一句话总结

本章 Wacker 把 KRAY 叫进四楼办公室，用旧照片和非洲往事拉近距离，再把失败的 NOC 计划、虚构的「变革性人力资源」概念与二百亿美元预算一并交给他，限时一个月出计划。
