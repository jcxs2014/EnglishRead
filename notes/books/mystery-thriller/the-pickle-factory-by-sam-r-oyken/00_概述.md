# 《The Pickle Factory》概述

## 全书梗概

**一个赌咒自己绝不说谎的叙述者。** 全书以一段仿拉伯雷的毒誓开场——叙述者把自己献给十万筐魔鬼，以担保这部「历史」里没有一个字是假的："I offer myself, body and soul, tripe and bowels, to a hundred thousand basket-loads of fine devils in case I lie in so much as a single word in the whole of this History."（ch01） 随后他预告结局：一场为约旦三面间谍举办的生日庆祝，会以不锈钢滚珠炸弹和一块被汽化的柠檬海绵蛋糕收场。"The Chief of Base died too. The lemon sponge birthday cake she was carrying was vaporized. Four other CIA officers died on the spot, including one standing fifty feet away. Six more were wounded. It seems a lot of people had gathered for the Jordanian’s arrival, since it was to be a celebration."（ch02）

**故事从两个小词开始。** 叙述者说，一切都始于 KRAY 对上司 Wacker 说出的那句「Yes, sir.」："Truth be told, it all started with just two little words: “Yes, sir.” And looking back on the whole thing afterward, after everything had cratered and gone to hell, our hero KRAY, that disgraced CIA spy with the full-sentence pseudonym, he would think about those two words."（ch03） 在这之前，先要交代 CIA 的化名从哪来：1963 年被拒聘的怪人「The Pseudo Guy」潜入总部地下室，拔掉 ENIAC 的电源，从此手写那些荒诞的「Funny Names」；主角 KRAY 拥有一个整句式化名。"When they discovered John O. THORNE — The Pseudo Guy — camped out behind the Funny Names ENIAC computer down in the basement, the Analysts were awestruck. They all froze right in place, overcome by a grinding shift deep down in the fundament of their paradigms."（ch04）"KRAY had a full-sentence pseudonym. This gave him a certain automatic status with junior officers, since it was cool. But he never let it go to his head, which was probably a good thing, seeing as he eventually had to let it go."（ch04）

**Wacker 把 Project GOOWIKI 交给他。** 项目的全部内容是：把一千名 NOC 假装成「转型人力资源顾问」派往全球，用两百亿美元年度预算维持这场表演。"I want them all to have jobs as Transformational Human Resources consultants, and I want them to be able to live anywhere in the world. I want each of them to be a freestanding singleton who can continue operating irregardless of whether any of the others succeed or get sucked into the combine. … We have twenty billion dollars we need to allocate. Got it?”"（ch06） Wacker 交给 KRAY 的第一件事是「做研究、动脑子」："I need you to do some research, and I need you to use your brain. One month from today I want you to give me a plan for how we can use Transformational Human Resources as cover to accomplish the goals laid out in that newspaper article for a barnyard full of new NOCs."（ch06） 而 KRAY 的女伴 KANDINSKY 一眼看穿这是骗局。

**一场横跨欧亚的接头巡演。** KRAY 带着黄色文件夹走遍伦敦、巴黎、开罗、印度、尼泊尔、曼谷与香港；接头人常常迟到或失约（也有准时赴约的），STUCCO 被发配到 Moose Jaw，而他自己在印度火车上病倒，又把 KANDINSKY 织的毛衣落在 Varanasi 的衣柜里。"He was distracted though. Unlucky, you might say. Which is why he forgot KANDINSKY’s grey v-neck commando sweater on the top shelf in the wardrobe, where he had put it when he first got sick."（ch40） 与此同时，FOB 的生日庆祝变成自杀式爆炸：基地负责人与 Marla 等 CIA 官员死亡，众人正要唱「生日快乐」，却再也没有时间。"Six more were wounded. It seems a lot of people had gathered for the Jordanian’s big debut, since it was a celebration. Everyone was getting ready to sing “Happy Birthday,” but they never had the time."（ch20）

**追责的方向被高层调转。** 匿名官员把反间谍失误推给死去的基地负责人，站长 ARISTOTLE 被免职后自杀，而批准接待的 BLUEBELL 与 MUCHADOO 退休后去了承包商、薪水翻倍；CIA 发言人只用一句「少数低层员工的错误」把一切抹平。"CIA’s spokesman issued an official statement. It read: “While the Agency mourns the deaths of these brave officers as a result of errors made by a few low-level employees, it is important to keep in mind CIA’s many, many successes in her efforts to protect America.”"（ch02）

**EVERSHAG 倒戈。** HR 的 EVERSHAG 不请自来地闯进 KANDINSKY 的办公室，交出 GOOWIKI 的绝密真相："EVERSHAG finally flipped. She dropped by KANDINSKY’s shoebox of an office unannounced, knocking on the door and quietly slipping in without waiting to be invited. Her makeup was a disaster from what looked like the trails of tears."（ch55） 并告诉 KRAY，爆炸中死去的 Marla 是她的姐妹。"EVERSHAG stopped for a moment, then added, “Marla was my sister I owe her this.”"（ch55）

**KRAY 把证据交给国会，代价是被开除。** 他向 HPSCI 幕僚 D. B. Cooper 与主席 Skinner 证明 Wacker 对国会撒谎："“This is what you need to prove that GOOWIKI is a sham and that Wacker has been lying to Congress. Wacker’s HR Chief, Evita P. EVERSHAG, reached out with it. She’s braver than any of us — we need to protect her.”"（ch57） Wacker 随即把他送进 Penalty Box 并解职；老情报官 BUTTONFLY 在国家档案馆的《独立宣言》前招募他，带着二十万美元签约奖金去重建一家真正的「Pickle Factory」。

**结束就是开始。** KANDINSKY 第一个收到粉色解雇单，两人离开；叙述者让读者也走出那面裂开的镜子，去获得自由。"Well, Madame, that’s pretty much all the truth I’ve got for you. So now comes the part where you step away from this cracked mirror, walk out past that Memorial Wall of Stars and that Bible quote, and get yourself free. Good luck old gal."（ch69） DIGESTIF 用一份被 █ 遮黑的人事八卦收尾：谁和谁睡了、为什么，而那些档案被误运到了摩纳哥码头。

## 三个主题

**一、说谎是这份工作的第一要务。** 全书的框架不是「找出真相」，而是「承认人人都在编」。叙述者开篇赌咒自己绝不说谎，紧接着说 CIA 的每个雇员都是骗子："You’ve probably figured this one out for yourself already, Tough Guy, but I’ll tell you anyway. A whole bunch of liars work at CIA. The fact is you can’t work there if you aren’t a liar, because lying is job one."（ch25） 谎言在这里不是道德缺陷，而是入职门槛、晋升阶梯与组织文化；连叙述者自己的「真实故事」也是从拉伯雷那里借来的毒誓。

**二、官僚体制会自我保护，直到它变成僵尸。** KANDINSKY 把 CIA 的官僚体系说成有生命的东西："“Well mostly the good ones never come back to Headquarters,” said Veronica. “They stay overseas for years and years, then when they’re finally ordered home to run some useless Personnel office, they retire. Once in a while someone who wants to improve things gives upper management a try, but eventually the bureaucracy either co-opts or crushes them. The bureaucracy is a living thing that protects itself. It’s the ace bureaucrats who rise up in that structure, not the ace collectors.”"（ch09） 而 Nick 把它修正为更准确的形象——不是活的，是不死的僵尸，只能砍头，无法说服。"“Well, I agree about the ace bureaucrats,” said Nick. “But I’m not sure I would agree the bureaucracy is a living thing. It seems more like a zombie — not living, just undead. Someone ought to chop off its head. Put it out of its misery.”"（ch09） 这套体制的产出不是情报，而是评估系统、领导力课程、厨房家电监控和一份又一份无人负责的备忘录。

**三、个体的良知要付代价，但并非没有回报。** EVERSHAG 说 KRAY「比他自己知道的更勇敢」："“It’s what KRAY has been looking for he’s braver than he knows he really wants to make the place better I hope he does it’s the truth the real truth about Project GOOWIKI it’s the proof that it’s all just a big fake program that was never meant to succeed from the start it’s TOP SECRET.”"（ch55） 而 KRAY 决定把证据送进国会时说的是："I know this is risky stuff and that I’ll probably suffer consequences. But sometimes that’s how it has to be. If I don’t do this now, I’ll regret it.”"（ch57） 这本书不承诺好人赢，只承诺有人愿意在明知会输的时候仍然开口。

## 人物弧光

**KRAY（Nick / Andre B. KRAY）**——

- 起点："In a far corner a fit, dark-haired, Mediterranean-looking man in his mid-forties stood up and nodded as brief applause broke out. He looked harmless. He stole a glance at his watch, a hard-knock Timex given to him as an award during a voluntary war-zone TDY in Hell, and wondered whether anything worthwhile would happen at this meeting."（ch05）
- 中段："“Man, sometimes CIA just seems like a playground for clowns and psychopaths,” he said, his heart guarded. “It’ll be good to finish working here and get back out in the field, away from all these weirdos.”"（ch09）
- 最低点："The fact is, he was broken. Yes, broken. There’s really no other word for it. Broken. On account of he had just had his heart ripped — still beating, don’t you know — straight out of his chest, and then shown to him, even before he had a chance to hit the floor for the first time and start bawling like a little baby."（ch66）
- 落点："“My word is my bond, and you have my word. Andre B. KRAY will remain an overseas NOC operator when he joins the new Pickle Factory as Chief of Counterintelligence.”"（ch68）
- 他从一个把「Yes, sir.」当护身符的失势间谍，变成一个明知会丢掉工作仍把证据送进国会的人；他的转折不在任何一次任务成功，而在承认自己「broken」之后仍然选择重新开始。

**KANDINSKY（Veronica / Candie X. KANDINSKY）**——

- 起点："“I hate to admit it, honey, but you were right. We need to do our part to try to improve the Agency while we can. We need to try to make her what she should be.”"（ch31）
- 转向："“What you’re talking about is playing Ahab, hunting something evil that doesn’t want to be found. That’s a counterintelligence job. Are you saying you want to be a counterintelligence officer?"（ch18）
- 落点："The first to be served her pink slip was KANDINSKY. And after she got over the initial cold shock, she found the whole thing deliciously imbued with nemesis."（ch69）
- 她是全书少有的清醒旁观者：一开始就警告 Nick 别去当追猎恶物的 Ahab，最后第一个被解雇却觉得「报应」来得痛快——她从未误认过这个体制。

**BUTTONFLY（Howard W. BUTTONFLY）**——

- 起点："I guess you might say I'm the Ghost of CIA Past. And the sad fact is, the Pickle Factory hasn't been the Pickle Factory for a long time now."（ch13）
- 落点："“No,” said BUTTONFLY, “sadly, she’s not. It’s like I told you the day we met, there hasn’t been a Pickle Factory for a long time. But there will be now, because I’m going to stand it up, outside of CIA. And you’re going to help me do it if you’re game.”"（ch68）
- 他是「Ghost of CIA Past」：记得 CIA 曾经是什么样子，也接受它已经不是；他给 KRAY 的不是怀旧，而是一份带着二十万美元签约奖金的新工作。

**EVERSHAG（Evita P. EVERSHAG）**——

- 转折："“It’s hell,” said EVERSHAG, exhaling hard. Catching herself, she added, “But it’s our job HR is here to help.”"（ch12）
- 行动："“I’m sorry I’m not that good at saying things I’m sorry I never have been I’m sorry I brought you this I’m sorry,” EVERSHAG said, holding out the envelope she was carrying."（ch55）
- 她从一个被 KRAY 顺走拆信刀的 HR 官僚，变成把绝密文件交出去的人；她的动机不是意识形态，而是死去的姐妹。
