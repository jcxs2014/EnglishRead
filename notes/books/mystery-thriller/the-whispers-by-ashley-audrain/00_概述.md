# 《The Whispers》概述

## 全书梗概

**一、开场是两张脸：一个没有名字的男人，一场后院派对。** 全书第一段不给他名字：六月某个周三深夜十一点五十六，他摸黑回到自家厨房，抬起两根手指去闻另一个孩子的母亲留在皮肤上的气味，然后绕着白橡木地板走，用指节碰面包机的拉杆、冰箱的钢把手、果篮里正变软的香蕉，把自己按回地面。"He fiddles with the way the white terry cloth falls, perfecting the ripple in the fabric like he’s staging a department store display, his hands twitching with an unfamiliar fear."（ch01）
他洗了澡、躲开主卧、躺进客房，半夜被一通电话叫醒——电话那头只说了「出了可怕的事」这一句。
九月那一场后院派对则是另一种音量。Whitney 在自家后院开街区派对：男客挑了体面的鞋，女客戴了不上游乐场的首饰，说话的调子都打磨过；孩子在玩另一种游戏，战利品袋里装着各自名字的曲奇，缎带系得很厚。她在三楼绣球花那几根脆枝里抖出一架纸飞机，随后在满院客人面前撞见儿子 Xavier 满脸巧克力、正在舔一张贴着别人名字的糖纸，她当众吼出了脏话。"The shame pulls her to the ground. To the nest of discarded satin ribbons from the cookies, the ends cut like the tip of a snake’s tongue."（ch02） 随后是羞耻把她按到地上，按进那堆被丢掉的缎带中间。

**二、六月那几天，这条街上几个女人各自攥着一件说不出口的东西。** Blair 在自家丈夫 Aiden 的牛仔裤口袋里摸到一角不到一平方英寸的锡箔包装纸——她认得出包装上那道压纹，还有那种祖母绿的颜色。"But she recognized the texture of the ribs in the packaging. And the emerald jewel tone. It looked exactly like the condoms they used years ago."（ch03）
同一个深夜，Xavier 被发现昏迷，抬进急诊的是街对面那家的孩子，急诊医生 Rebecca 抬起他的眼皮就认出了他。"I know him. His name is Xavier. He lives across the street from me."（ch04）
住在街对面的 Mara 那天没有收拾自己，只坐在折叠椅里听整条街的动静；她说过，一个人差不多隐形的时候，最能说明她的恰恰是别人不想让她看见的那部分。"It’s amazing what you can learn about people when you’re more or less invisible. It’s the things they don’t want you to see that tell you the most."（ch06）

**三、Whitney 在儿子的病床边数他活过的天数，Rebecca 在地下层给自己听胎心。** 那些管子并没有让 Whitney 难受，她眼前只有一个男孩；她一边在心里把生下他这件事倒回去重做，一边在他前臂的细汗毛里一笔一笔描他的名字。"Three. Two. One. He is hers. She made him. She grew him and raised him. She is supposed to be able to protect him. And she wants him back inside her."（ch07）
同一段时间，Rebecca 交完班仍留在急诊，脑子里转的是她自己反复想过的那句假设——"But if that child doesn’t live, she will, from then on, be the person who changed that family’s life. Her words—I’m so sorry to have to tell you this—will be burned in their memory until the day they themselves die. She becomes a part of those broken people’s histories."（ch08）
而楼下的旧诊室里，她用多普勒听自己身体里那个东西跳。

**四、两把钥匙、一片锡纸，和一个被瞒了四个月的怀孕。** Whitney 给了 Blair 一把「以防万一」的备用钥匙；Blair 趁保姆带孩子出门进了那栋房子，在床头柜最里层摸到一只粉红缎面袋，袋里那串钥匙扣上压着丈夫姓名首字母 A. P.。"The keychain is Blair’s husband’s. His initials are embossed in the leather. A. P."（ch11）
与此同时，Rebecca 决定不再尝试要孩子——她对 Ben 说那句话时，用的是一种把将来办成丧事的说法。"I can’t go through it again, Rebecca. I’m grieving that future. I’m moving on. And you need to move on too."（ch19）
九月后院烧烤那天，Blair 察觉到 Aiden 用当年看她的那种眼神在看另一个女人；这一刻里的什么东西正平静地、理性地告诉她：这段婚姻要结束了。"Something about the moment is telling her, calmly, rationally: this marriage is going to end. And then she thinks about how it would feel if Aiden was dead."（ch28）

**五、Albert 倒在厨房的亚麻油地板上，Mara 说的是谎。** 那天她在楼下折衣服，手里攥着他那件发黄的旧背心，听见东西砸在厨房桌子上，接着撞上椅子的金属腿，接着是地板上的一声闷响；她没有上楼，只是一件一件继续折。"She would fold just one more thing."（ch50）
急救员问起来时，她答的那一段被叙述直接称作 lie。多年以前，她瞒着丈夫攒钱买了两张机票带儿子飞出去，她认定是自己让他上了那架飞机。"She knew she had killed him by making him get on the plane. If she’d listened to Albert, he’d be alive."（ch59）

**六、九月又回来了一回，这次是在厨房岛台上。** 派对散场后只剩两个女人在收拾，Xavier 一边舔迷你纸杯蛋糕的糖霜一边讲国际象棋走了多少步；Whitney 说「他最近有大问题、在看行为专家」，Blair 隔着一句反问回去，Whitney 挑明「我们要不要就当它没发生过」。"Blair is stunned by Whitney’s words. She can’t find a reply. Isn’t that what we do for each other? she thinks. We turn a blind eye? We protect each other’s dignity?"（ch48）
回到自己家，Blair 在心里问出了那句一直绕开的话：我真的知道 Xavier 没事吗。"Blair thinks to herself, for the first time: Do I know that? Do I know that Xavier is okay? Does Whitney really believe he’s fine?"（ch48）

**七、秘密开始换手，婚戒最后被摊回掌心。** Blair 把那串钥匙扣揣在外套口袋里带了好些天，最后把它丢进 Aiden 的健身包，又把那片祖母绿色的锡纸角丢进马桶；同一个下午，她还在 Xavier 房墙上那团被咖啡和马克笔弄花的黑色乱涂里，把一句糊掉过的话重新拼了出来。"I DON’T WANT TO BE YOUR SON ANYMORE"（ch61）
开学第一周起，Mara 每个周四一大早就绕着院子收纸飞机；她一直瞒着 Albert，怕他会对那家人说点什么，最后把一架纸飞机放在 Whitney 家门口。"She’s going to miss those paper planes."（ch62）
回家路上，Jacob 摊开手，把她的婚戒还回来，只说了一句：你周三晚上没戴着它们。"“Oh. Right. I forgot I brought these for you. To the hospital.” He opens his hand. Her wedding rings. She stares at them. “You weren’t wearing them Wednesday night.”"（ch65）

**八、最后回到那个周三夜里，再跳到两周之后的病房。** 那天夜里十点四十五，后院的灯还亮着；儿子从上面自己卧室的窗口看着她。"And she sees Xavier at his bedroom window above. He’s watching them."（ch66）
然后是一声很轻的闷响；她两级两级跑上楼，把咖啡泼上去，手忙脚乱地去够地上那支黑色马克笔。"She takes the stairs two at a time, she feels wild, knocking into the walls. In his bedroom, she slips in the coffee and scrambles to reach the black marker on the floor."（ch66）
两周后，Xavier 醒着，脸被类固醇打得肿胀，Jacob 每晚睡在长椅上替他守夜；而叙述给的收尾状态是：现在没有人再谈那天晚上的事了。

## 三个主题

**一、耳语要有人肯听才存在，而听的人往往要等事后。** 书名给的定义写在 Blair 脑子里：耳语是那些正试图告诉你这里哪里不对劲的时刻，问题在于有些女人要等事后回头才听得见。"She’d once heard them described as the whispers—the moments that are trying to tell you something isn’t right here. The problem is that some women aren’t listening to what their lives are trying to tell them. They don’t hear the whispers until they’re looking back with hindsight."（ch03）
另一位把这件事讲得更冷：一个人想要某样东西想要得够狠的时候，总会冒一些风险，也会学会对某些事视而不见。"But there are risks people take when they want something badly enough. There are things they learn to ignore."（ch39）
而临近结尾处那句「换了那么多东西」就是代价的账单：这么多东西，是在那些没说出口里换来的，在那些被护住的东西里。"So much was traded in what went unsaid. In what was protected."（ch63）

**二、母职在这本书里首先是一种自我消失的技术。** Blair 把这条街上的两个女人分成两种："Whitney was a conductor; Blair was a grinder."（ch20）
另一次是七个多月前两个人在 Blair 家里喝酒时说的：好像一个女人还想要点自己的东西就算是过分，好像她们必须先把那份无私的配额完成，才能轮得到成为别的什么人。"As if wanting anything more for oneself would be excessive. As if they must satisfy the quota for selflessness, and only then, after the accommodating and the pleasing, can they be anything else."（ch36）
而这条线上的另一个版本，是她在哄完女儿之后对自己说的那一句："No, darling, we pretend. This is how the life of a woman looks."（ch31）

**三、假装是这本书里被公开承认的那项技能。** Whitney 头也不回地走向电梯时，叙述只给了她一句最短的判语。"Pretending is what she is best at."（ch22）
而 Blair 那边，她能握在手里的全部就这些："She has nothing but a corner of foil packaging and a key. And hours and hours of assuming the worst. That is all she has."（ch37）
整条街随后都在替彼此圆谎——圆一个孩子是不是自己摔下去的、圆一个丈夫为什么不肯看手机、圆一个男人为什么半夜回家先去整理毛巾。Blair 最后给出的判词把落点从爱挪到了别处。"Marriage isn’t about love; it’s about choices."（ch61）

## 人物弧光

**Whitney**——

- 起点："Tombstones should be etched with total number of days lived instead of dates, she thinks; the dates mean nothing."（ch12）
- 转向："She doesn’t really like to play. In fact, she hates to. There is no productivity in play. She hates the plastic bins full of toys, and hates sitting on the floor."（ch17）
- 最低点："She holds her breath and whimpers, she thinks no, no, no, but she is doing it, she is waiting for the beeping to start and for someone to come in and take her hand off his air tube. To save him from his mother."（ch45）
- 落点："“Anything,” she says quietly. “We’ll keep it between us. Even if it’s about that night. About what happened.”"（ch67）
- 她的弧线不是从坏变好，而是从「把孩子做成一件作品」走到「让孩子来决定自己是谁」——而走到终点时，她给的不是道歉，是一个保密承诺。

**Blair**——

- 起点："Somewhere along the line, blaming Aiden for the way she felt about her life became convenient. He’s been a reliable depository for her anger."（ch03）
- 中段："She can’t decide what it means to have finally confronted him. To have freed a vicious rodent from a cage, and although the threat isn’t rattling in her hands anymore, it’s still alive somewhere. It will come back."（ch39）
- 最低点："Xavier, alone, leaning over the bedroom window. Not caring, either, if he died."（ch40）
- 落点："She has a good life, a blessed life. She will stop convincing herself otherwise."（ch61）
- 她一路都在替别人找说法，也替自己找；最后她不再跟自己作对说日子过得不好——她有过得好，享有福。

**Rebecca**——

- 起点："She is a doctor, and what she cares about are facts. She finds comfort in facts."（ch04）
- 中段："It is not an event, something that once happened and has ended. Miscarriage goes on and on, follows a woman through her days and her dreams"（ch27）
- 最低点："She wants to evaporate into a place where she can feel nothing. She is on the cusp of sleep, thinking of lying on an operating-room table, slicing herself open with a scalpel, splaying her insides, throwing every one of her reproductive parts on a metal cart and kicking it across the sterile room, the crash of the instruments, the steel kidney basins clanging on the floor, her womb splattering like a water balloon. The sound of the splash wakes her."（ch52）
- 落点："And then Rebecca starts to feel what she was waiting for. A growl of strength. Like she could reach inside herself and carry the sleeping baby out in the safe, warm palm of her hand. Like she is a mother."（ch64）
- 她做的事始终是同一件：把不能承受的事实从身体里清出去；到最后，她允许自己再当一次母亲。

**Mara**——

- 起点："Mara has become good at reading lips. It’s a skill of the lonely."（ch24）
- 中段："“I told you I had a son, and that he died.” Mara pauses. “Well, what I didn’t say is that I could’ve prevented it. I was responsible. And I think about that every day.”"（ch56）
- 最低点："“I’ll go upstairs now. I will,” she’d whispered eventually, when she was sure the noises had ceased from above. When she was certain it would be too late."（ch50）
- 落点："The afternoon sun has another hour until it dips below the roofline of the Loverlys’ third story."（ch59）
- 她是这条街上从头到尾都在听的那个人；她最后做的事，是把一架纸飞机放在别人家门口。

**Xavier**——

- 他在正文里被直接引到的声音很少；他自己被写下来的方式，是墙上那一行全大写。"I DON’T WANT TO BE YOUR SON ANYMORE"（ch66）
- 两周之后他问了一个问题，然后把答案推回给母亲。"“To you,” he says. “When I tell them everything.”"（ch67）
- 他不是被拯救的对象——他是那个在最后一刻把叙述权拿回来的人。
