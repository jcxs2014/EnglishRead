## 精读

> **原句 1:** “Bree looked even paler than usual. She was seated on a high stool, on a riser, in front of her audience. I worried that she might topple over.”

- **中文理解**："Bree looked even paler than usual. She was seated on a high stool, on a riser, in front of her audience. I worried that she might topple over."
- **关键词**：paler than usual—— paler than usual；topple over—— topple over
- **为什么这样写**：Cat's observation of Bree's physical state ("paler than usual," "worried that she might topple over") establishes Bree's fragility. This is the chapter's first clue to Bree's pregnancy (bedrest). "topple over" is Cat's concern.
- **读者视角提示**：Bree's physical fragility is a clue. Track whether it's grief, illness, or pregnancy.

> **原句 2:** “This is a fantastic new direction for Welcome, Goddess! We all know that women’s bodies are sacred and full of wonder, yes?”

> **原句 3:** “Bree’s expertise in chemistry is going to change the fertility game and put the power in women’s hands where it belongs!”

- **中文理解**："This is a fantastic new direction for Welcome, Goddess! We all know that women's bodies are sacred and full of wonder, yes?" ... "Bree's expertise in chemistry is going to change the fertility game and put the power in women's hands where it belongs!"
- **关键词**：fertility game—— fertility game；put the power in women's hands—— put the power in women's hands
- **为什么这样写**：Bodhi's "fertility game" pitch is his co-optation of Bree's expertise. "women's bodies are sacred and full of wonder" is his pseudo-spiritual language. "put the power in women's hands" is the feminist framing. This is Bodhi's performance—using Bree's pregnancy (unknown to audience) as business opportunity.
- **读者视角提示**：Bodhi's "fertility game" pitch is ironic given Bree's pregnancy. Track whether Bodhi knows.

> **原句 4:** “No comment.”

- **中文理解**："No comment."
- **关键词**：No comment—— No comment
- **为什么这样写**：Bree's "No comment" is her response to Cat's question about Clarity partnership. This is Bree's deflection pattern. Cat's "She glanced at Bodhi" notes Bree's consultation with Bodhi before answering.
- **读者视角提示**：Bree's "No comment" is her avoidance. Track whether Bree has something to hide.

> **原句 6:** “Two: The company employs a lot of people who are not in my fortunate financial position. So if you really believe Bliss died because of her plans to fold the business, you should be looking at our employees, not at me.”

> **原句 7:** “Three: I wasn’t climbing up to the lookout, not that night or any other. I’ve been on bedrest most of the week. I’m allowed up for a couple of hours a day to do events and that’s it. Doctor’s orders.”

- **中文理解**："One: Welcome, Goddess has been extremely good to me personally. I'm not living paycheque to paycheque. I don't have to work ever again if I don't want to. Doctor's orders."

"There were only so many reasons a doctor would put a young, healthy woman on bedrest" is Cat's medical knowledge. "hand rested lightly on her abdomen" is the physical clue. "tiny Larrys on the loose, wreaking havoc" is Cat's sardonic humor.
- **读者视角提示**：Bree's pregnancy is the chapter's key revelation. Track whether Bodhi knows and whether this connects to the murder.

- **中文理解**："Between us, I'm not sure all of my dad's decisions are good for the company." ... "Like cancelling our ad campaign because some lamebrain said we should." A blush ran up his neck. "The lamebrain is not my dad, in case anyone asks."

- **读者视角提示**：Peter Johnson influenced Flannery's ad cancellation. Track whether this connects to the murder.
