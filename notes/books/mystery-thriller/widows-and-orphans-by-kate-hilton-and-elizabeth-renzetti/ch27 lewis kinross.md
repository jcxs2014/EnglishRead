- **中文理解**："Lewis Kinross isn't writing a story for The Loft. This editor had never even heard of him. There's no story on their sked about the Welcome, Goddess conference. He seemed totally surprised when I asked him."

- **读者视角提示**：Lewis's fake assignment is his first deception. Track what else he's lying about.
