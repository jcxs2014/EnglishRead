## 精读

> **原句 1:** “I’m giving you a lead, Cat. I told Gerry’s events coordinator that I was throwing a cocktail party for Marian, and you were coming by to help me with the guest list. Come on, let’s go!”

- **中文理解**："I'm giving you a lead, Cat. I told Gerry's events coordinator that I was throwing a cocktail party for Marian, and you were coming by to help me with the guest list. Come on, let's go!"
- **关键词**：giving you a lead—— giving you a lead；cocktail party for Marian—— cocktail party for Marian
- **为什么这样写**：Debbie's "I'm giving you a lead" is her excitement. "I told Gerry's events coordinator that I was throwing a cocktail party for Marian" is her cover story. "Come on, let's go!" is her urgency. This is Debbie's investigative assistance.
- **读者视角提示**：Debbie's cover story (cocktail party) is her ingenuity. Track her contributions.

> **原句 2:** “… you got me into this, and you better get me out.”

> **原句 3:** “Are you fucking firing me?”

> **原句 4:** “I’ll calm down when you fix this!”

- **中文理解**："… you got me into this, and you better get me out." ... "Are you fucking firing me?" ... "I'll calm down when you fix this!"
- **关键词**：you got me into this—— you got me into this；Are you fucking firing me?—— Are you fucking firing me?；I'll calm down when you fix this—— I'll calm down when you fix this
- **为什么这样写**：Kyle's words (overheard) are the chapter's key evidence. "you got me into this, and you better get me out" is his accusation against Gerry. "Are you fucking firing me?" is his panic. "I'll calm down when you fix this" is his demand. This is Kyle's desperation.
- **读者视角提示**：Kyle's "you got me into this" suggests Gerry involved Kyle in something. Track what.

> **原句 5:** “It would be easier for all concerned if you told them, Cat,” Debbie said.

> **原句 6:** “Has anyone ever told you that you’re avoidant?” she asked. “I can lend you a book about attachment styles if you like. Enlightening stuff.”

- **中文理解**："It would be easier for all concerned if you told them, Cat," Debbie said. ... "Has anyone ever told you that you're avoidant?" she asked. "I can lend you a book about attachment styles if you like. Enlightening stuff."
- **关键词**：avoidant—— avoidant；attachment styles—— attachment styles
- **为什么这样写**：Debbie's "It would be easier for all concerned if you told them" is her push. "Has anyone ever told you that you're avoidant?" is her diagnosis. "attachment styles" is her self-help vocabulary. This is Debbie's character: helpful, therapeutic, slightly pushy.
- **读者视角提示**：Debbie's "avoidant" diagnosis is her assessment of Cat. Track their sisterly dynamic.

> **原句 7:** “I might be overstepping here, Debbie,” I said, “but are you and Gerry having problems? I can’t help but notice that you seem to be throwing him under the bus.”

> **原句 8:** “Hardly,” she said. “I’m saving him from himself. But this is the last time. I’m done.”

- **中文理解**："I might be overstepping here, Debbie," I said, "but are you and Gerry having problems? I can't help but notice that you seem to be throwing him under the bus." ... "Hardly," she said. "I'm saving him from himself. But this is the last time. I'm done."
- **关键词**：throwing him under the bus—— throwing him under the bus；saving him from himself—— saving him from himself；I'm done—— I'm done
- **为什么这样写**：Cat's "throwing him under the bus" is her observation. Debbie's "I'm saving him from himself" is her defense. "this is the last time. I'm done" is her decision (she's leaving Gerry). This is Debbie's turning point.
- **读者视角提示**：Debbie is leaving Gerry. Track whether this affects the investigation.

> **原句 9:** “He was acting as her ‘personal brand advisor,’ trying to persuade her to launch a syndicated talk-show that would air all over North America.”

> **原句 10:** “Gerry sent a message to Kyle the night Bliss died, telling him to come to the Pinerock. He said he was meeting Bliss and he wanted Kyle to be there.”

- **中文理解**："He was acting as her 'personal brand advisor,' trying to persuade her to launch a syndicated talk-show that would air all over North America." ... "Gerry sent a message to Kyle the night Bliss died, telling him to come to the Pinerock. He said he was meeting Bliss and he wanted Kyle to be there."
- **关键词**：personal brand advisor—— personal brand advisor；syndicated talk-show—— syndicated talk-show；meeting Bliss—— meeting Bliss
- **为什么这样写**：Debbie's revelation is the chapter's key intelligence. "personal brand advisor" + "syndicated talk-show" is Gerry's plan for Bliss. "Gerry sent a message to Kyle the night Bliss died" is the crucial fact—Gerry and Kyle were at the Pinerock that night. This connects Gerry/Kyle to the murder scene.
- **读者视角提示**：Gerry was meeting Bliss the night she died. Kyle was there too. Track whether this is opportunity for murder.

> **原句 11:** “Gerry has more than a few flaws,” she said. “He craves attention and he blows with the political wind and he’s a poor judge of character and”—Debbie was really warming up now—“he’s vain and honestly he only graduated university because I wrote all his papers. But he’s not a killer, for heaven’s sake.”

- **中文理解**："Gerry has more than a few flaws," she said. "He craves attention and he blows with the political wind and he's a poor judge of character and"—Debbie was really warming up now—"he's vain and honestly he only graduated university because I wrote all his papers. But he's not a killer, for heaven's sake."
- **关键词**：craves attention—— craves attention；blows with the political wind—— blows with the political wind；poor judge of character—— poor judge of character；not a killer—— not a killer
- **为什么这样写**：Debbie's assessment of Gerry is the chapter's character analysis. "craves attention" + "blows with the political wind" + "poor judge of character" + "vain" + "I wrote all his papers" is her indictment. "But he's not a killer, for heaven's sake" is her defense. This is Debbie's complex view of Gerry.
- **读者视角提示**：Debbie's "not a killer" is her assessment. Track whether Gerry's flaws extend to murder.
