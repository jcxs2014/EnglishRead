# 05. Chapter Five

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Cat 在 Pinerock 等 Bruce 时，Doug（保安）透露 Bliss 前晚从 lookout 坠亡；Bruce 到达，Cat 安排 interview Marian；Marian 向 Bruce/Cat 讲述她今早发现尸体的经过（sunrise proposal 情侣、cliff 底的 Bliss）；Marian 顺带要求 Cat 帮忙 promote 她的书；Inspector Bell 与 Martha Mercer 也到场，Cat 被 Martha 威胁驱逐。
- **情感弧线位置**：全书第五章——investigation officially begins；Bliss 的死从 "accident" 转向 "suspicious"（Inspector Bell 出现 implies homicide investigation）；Cat 与 Marian 的 mutual suspicion seed（"Had I detected an infinitesimal pause?"）。
- **Tropes**：detective arrival (Inspector Bell) / "accidental death?" / mother as witness / property owner (Martha) vs. journalist / investigation vs. accident.
- **人物弧线**：Cat 从 "covering a conference" 转为 "covering a murder"；Marian 从 "wobbly" to "grit and gumption"；Doug 从 security guard to gossip source；Bruce as Cat's partner/mediator.
- **叙事手法**：第一人称限知 + 对话驱动的信息 reveal + Cat 的 internal suspicion ("Spidey sense tingled") + 末段 mother-daughter transaction (book promotion for property investment).

## 精读

> **原句 1:** “Wellness doesn’t seem to have done much for her,” said Doug. “I’ll stick with my bacon sandwiches and beer, thank you very much.”

- **中文理解**："Wellness 似乎对她没起什么作用，"Doug 说。"我继续吃我的培根三明治和啤酒，非常感谢。"
- **关键词**：Wellness doesn't seem to have done much for her——Wellness 对她似乎没起作用；I'll stick with my bacon sandwiches and beer——我继续吃培根三明治和啤酒
- **为什么这样写**：Doug 的 black humor 是 Cat 的 voice 的 mirror—both use food/comfort to process death. "Wellness doesn't seem to have done much for her" is the chapter's first irony (Bliss, wellness guru, fell to her death). Doug's "bacon sandwiches and beer" rejects wellness culture entirely. This establishes Doug as Cat's ally in skepticism.
- **读者视角提示**：Doug will be a recurring source. His "side hustle" (security at Pinerock) gives him insider access. Track what he observes.

> **原句 2:** It hadn’t been me, in the end, who’d exploded his low-key work routine, but Bliss Bondar. The early word was that Bliss had fallen from the scenic lookout at some point during the night. Her twisted, crumpled body had been spotted in the bushes below, first by a couple enjoying the sunrise and again, shortly thereafter, by Marian Conway, out for her morning walk, ankle-weighted and Fitbitted.

- **中文理解**：最终炸掉他低调工作routine的不是我，而是 Bliss Bondar。早期消息是 Bliss 在夜里某个时刻从 scenic lookout 坠落。她扭曲、蜷曲的尸体在下面的灌木丛里被发现了，先是被一对享受日出的情侣，随后不久又被 Marian Conway 发现——她正在晨间散步，脚踝绑着重物，戴着 Fitbit。
- **关键词**：exploded his low-key work routine——炸掉他低调的工作routine；twisted, crumpled body——扭曲、蜷曲的尸体；spotted in the bushes below——在灌木丛里被发现；ankle-weighted and Fitbitted——脚踝绑重物，戴着 Fitbit
- **为什么这样写**：Cat's "It hadn't been me, in the end" acknowledges her reputation as trouble (Doug's "mind your manners" earlier). "twisted, crumpled body" is the chapter's first physical image of death—visceral. "ankle-weighted and Fitbitted" is Cat's dark humor: Marian's fitness tracking becomes part of the death narrative. The "first by a couple... and again by Marian" establishes the witness chain.
- **读者视角提示**：Marian was the second person to find the body (after the sunrise couple). This timing matters. "ankle-weighted and Fitbitted" emphasizes Marian's compulsive fitness tracking—she's always "on the clock" even during a murder.

> **原句 3:** “I told Martha Mercer that the lookout was a deathtrap. She wasn’t interested. ‘Put up a fence,’ I said. But no. We wouldn’t want to spoil the view. You know what spoils a view? A dead body, that’s what.”

- **中文理解**："我告诉 Martha Mercer 那个 lookout 是个死亡陷阱。她不感兴趣。'装个栅栏，'我说。但不行。我们可不想破坏景观。你知道什么会破坏景观吗？一具尸体，就是这样。"
- **关键词**：deathtrap——死亡陷阱；put up a fence——装个栅栏；spoil the view——破坏景观；A dead body, that's what——一具尸体，就是这样
- **为什么这样写**：Doug's warning (ignored by Martha) establishes the "accident vs. negligence" tension. "spoil the view" is Doug's sarcastic echo of Martha's aesthetic priority. "A dead body, that's what" is his blunt punchline. This sets up the question: was Bliss's fall an accident (negligent safety) or murder?
- **读者视角提示**：Doug's testimony about Martha ignoring safety warnings will be relevant to the investigation. Track whether Martha's negligence is a red herring or a clue.

> **原句 4:** “Absolutely not,” said Inspector Bell, crisply.

- **中文理解**："绝对不行，"Inspector Bell 简洁地说。
- **关键词**：Absolutely not——绝对不行；crisply——简洁地；冷硬地
- **为什么这样写**：Inspector Bell's two-word refusal is her character in miniature—efficient, authoritative, no-nonsense. "crisply" is Cat's adverb choice (she's sizing up Bell). Bell's presence (senior OPP officer at an "accidental" death) is the chapter's first signal that this is more than an accident. Cat's "You don't usually attend accidental deaths, do you?" is her provocation.
- **读者视角提示**：Inspector Bell's presence implies homicide investigation. Track her interactions with Cat—she's a professional rival/obstacle.

> **原句 5:** “I will not hesitate to have you thrown off my property, Ms. Conway,” she said, her blond hair helmet as fixed as her expression of disdain. “There has been a terrible accident. That is all. We do not need you sensationalizing it.”

- **中文理解**："我不会犹豫把你从我的财产上扔出去，Conway 女士，"她说，她的金发头盔和她鄙视的表情一样固定。"发生了一起可怕的事故。就这些。我们不需要你 sensationalize 它。"
- **关键词**：thrown off my property——从我的财产上扔出去；blond hair helmet——金发头盔；expression of disdain——鄙视的表情；terrible accident——可怕的事故；sensationalizing—— sensationalize；耸人听闻
- **为什么这样写**：Martha's "blond hair helmet" is Cat's physical description—her hair is as rigid as her authority. "terrible accident. That is all" is Martha's attempt to control the narrative (accident, not murder). "sensationalizing" is her accusation against Cat—she's framing Cat as a troublemaker, not a journalist. This echoes ch1's "Shill and Racket" (Cat as sensationalist).
- **读者视角提示**：Martha's "That is all" is her attempt to close the case. Track whether Martha's narrative (accident) holds or collapses. Her "blond hair helmet" suggests she's as rigid as her hair—unlikely to bend.

> **原句 6:** “I recognized her, you know. Even from so far away. She was face-up, her hair full of blood.” She exhaled slowly, as if taking it in for the first time. “It was shocking. Shocking.”

- **中文理解**："我认出她了，你知道的。即使从那么远。她是 face-up，她的头发全是血。"她慢慢呼气，好像第一次意识到。"太 shocking 了。太 shocking 了。"
- **关键词**：recognized her——认出她；face-up——仰面；hair full of blood——头发全是血；exhaled slowly——慢慢呼气；shocking, shocking—— shocking，shocking（重复）
- **为什么这样写**：Marian's witness account is the chapter's key testimony. "Even from so far away" emphasizes her fitness (she was power-walking). "face-up, her hair full of blood" is the visceral detail. "exhaled slowly, as if taking it in for the first time" suggests Marian's composure cracked slightly—she's processing the horror only now, in retelling. The repetition "Shocking. Shocking." is Marian's emotional release.
- **读者视角提示**：Marian's "I recognized her" implies she knew Bliss. Did she? From where? Track Marian's connection to Bliss/Bree (wellness world, book club). "Even from so far away" also hints at Marian's sharp eyes—she notices details others miss.

> **原句 7:** Had I detected an infinitesimal pause before she replied? Perhaps I’d imagined it. But my Spidey sense tingled. My mother’s presentation of reality was often Photoshopped to show her best side. Was she withholding something?

- **中文理解**：我是否在她回答前察觉到一个 infinitesimal pause？也许我想象的。但我的 Spidey sense tingled。我母亲对现实的呈现常常被 Photoshopped 来展示她最好的一面。她在隐瞒什么吗？
- **关键词**：infinitesimal pause——微小的停顿；Spidey sense tingled——蜘蛛感应 tingled；Photoshopped——Photoshopped（修图）；withholding something——隐瞒什么
- **为什么这样写**：Cat's internal suspicion is the chapter's turning point—she doesn't fully trust Marian's account. "infinitesimal pause" is her journalist's ear (she catches micro-pauses). "Spidey sense tingled" is her intuition marker. "Photoshopped to show her best side" is her metaphor for Marian's reality-twisting. "Was she withholding something?" is the direct question that seeds the Marian-secret thread.
- **读者视角提示**：This is the first explicit suspicion of Marian. Track every Marian statement for "infinitesimal pauses" and "Photoshopped" reality. The reader should wonder: what is Marian hiding?

> **原句 8:** “Before Debbie gets back, I wanted a word about my book.”

- **中文理解**："Debbie 回来之前，我想聊聊我的书。"
- **关键词**：Before Debbie gets back——Debbie 回来之前；a word about my book——聊聊我的书
- **为什么这样写**：Marian 用 "Before Debbie gets back" 把请求包成私密的——她知道这个请求在 Debbie 面前不合适。"a word about my book" 把 murder witness 的身份瞬间切回 author/promoter。这是 Marian 的 operating mode：一切皆可 leverage。
- **读者视角提示**：Marian 的 "Before Debbie gets back" 暗示她对 Debbie 的利用也有分寸——她知道 Debbie 不是 staffer，不能提 promotion 要求。

> **原句 9:** “I’m wondering if you can get some of your contacts to help promote the book. You must have some favours you can call in?”

- **中文理解**："我在想你能不能让你的 contacts 帮忙 promote 这本书。你总有一些 favours 可以 call in 吧？"
- **关键词**：contacts to help promote——contacts 帮忙 promote；favours you can call in——可以 call in 的 favours
- **为什么这样写**："You must have some favours you can call in?" 把 Cat 的 social capital 当成 Marian 的资源。"must have" 是 Marian 的假设——她假设 Cat 有 favours 可用。这是她的 transactional worldview：一切关系都是 favour economy。
- **读者视角提示**：Marian's book-promotion request amid a murder investigation shows her priorities. "One favour for another. Efficient, you might say. And I had some thoughts already, including a great title for my mother's next book: Strings Attached." Cat's "Strings Attached" is her final judgment—Marian's favors always have strings.

## 一句话总结

Cat 清晨撞上警车消防车，Marian 在咖啡厅 calmly 告诉她：她今早在 lookout 发现了一具尸体（Bliss Bondar），Inspector Bell 与 Martha Mercer 也到场，Cat 被 Martha 威胁驱逐；Marian 暗示 Cat 帮忙 promote 她的书，Cat 怀疑 Marian 隐瞒了什么——全章把 murder discovery 与 mother-daughter mutual suspicion 并置，"That poor girl. She certainly didn't fulfill the promise of her name" 作为 reveal 收尾。
