# 13. Chapter Thirteen

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Bree 在 ballroom 宣布 "evidence-based products"， convoy 抗议者（Brenda, Kyle, Peter Johnson）闯入；Inspector Bell 清场；Doug Jaglowski 在 security office 向 Cat/Bruce 展示 footage：Clarity 的 cape + Bliss 的 Ugg boots → killer mistook Bliss for Clarity.
- **情感弧线位置**：全书第十三章——investigation 的 central twist：Bliss's death may have been mistaken identity (killer targeted Clarity K, not Bliss).
- **Tropes**：evidence-based announcement / convoy protest in ballroom / Inspector Bell's command / security footage reveal / mistaken identity.
- **人物弧线**：Bree 从 "grieving partner" 转向 "evidence-based rebrander"; Peter Johnson (Kaydence's father) as convoy patriarch; Doug 从 security guard 转向 "PI candidate."
- **叙事手法**：第一人称限知 + ballroom chaos + Doug's security footage as chapter-ending reveal.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** “As I said, healing has been the foundation of our business. Bliss and I agreed wholeheartedly on that point. With her death, the responsibility of charting a path for the future of Welcome, Goddess falls to me and me alone. It’s a duty that I take seriously. I know that many of you rely on us for guidance about your health, and I want to be a source that you can trust. Consequently, I’d like to take this opportunity to reaffirm my belief—and my company’s commitment—to evidence-based products and services. Wellness without science isn’t healing—it’s misleading. And I want no part of it.”

- **中文理解**："As I said, healing has been the foundation of our business。Bliss and I agreed wholeheartedly on that point。With her death, the responsibility of charting a path for the future of Welcome, Goddess falls to me and me alone。It's a duty that I take seriously。I know that many of you rely on us for guidance about your health, and I want to be a source that you can trust。Consequently, I'd like to take this opportunity to reaffirm my belief—and my company's commitment—to evidence-based products and services。Wellness without science isn't healing—it's misleading。And I want no part of it。"
- **关键词**：evidence-based products and services—— evidence-based products and services；Wellness without science isn't healing—it's misleading—— Wellness without science isn't healing—it's misleading
- **为什么这样写**：Bree's speech is her rebranding moment—she's pivoting Welcome, Goddess from Bliss's anti-science rhetoric to evidence-based wellness. "Wellness without science isn't healing—it's misleading" is her thesis. This is Bree's power move: she's claiming the business and redefining its mission. The "And I want no part of it" is her rejection of Bliss's legacy.
- **读者视角提示**：Bree's "evidence-based" pivot is her taking control. Track whether this rebranding is genuine or strategic. "And I want no part of it" (of Bliss's anti-science) may be her attempt to distance herself from Bliss's murder.

> **原句 2:** “Who got to you?” yelled one woman, jumping to her feet.

- **中文理解**："Who got to you?" yelled one woman，jumping to her feet.
- **关键词**：Who got to you?—— Who got to you?
- **为什么这样写**：The woman's "Who got to you?" is the convoy's conspiracy theory—Bree has been "got to" by government/corporate interests. This is the wellness movement's standard narrative: anyone who pivots to evidence-based is "compromised." The "jumping to her feet" is the physical escalation.
- **读者视角提示**：The convoy's "Who got to you?" frames Bree's pivot as betrayal. Track whether the convoy's conspiracy theories have any basis.

> **原句 3:** “Bliss knew the truth!” The call came from a chorus of united voices.

- **中文理解**："Bliss knew the truth!" The call came from a chorus of united voices.
- **关键词**：Bliss knew the truth—— Bliss knew the truth
- **为什么这样写**：The convoy's "Bliss knew the truth" is their martyrdom narrative—Bliss was killed because she knew the truth (about vaccines, etc.). This is the chapter's key irony: Bliss's "truth" (anti-science conspiracy) is what the convoy believes, and her death is being weaponized. "chorus of united voices" is Cat's observation of their coordination.
- **读者视角提示**：The convoy's "Bliss knew the truth" narrative is their justification for protest. Track whether this narrative affects the murder investigation.

> **原句 4:** “This is Inspector Cheryl Bell of the Ontario Provincial Police. Everyone will take a seat immediately.”

- **中文理解**："This is Inspector Cheryl Bell of the Ontario Provincial Police。Everyone will take a seat immediately。"
- **关键词**：Inspector Cheryl Bell—— Inspector Cheryl Bell；Everyone will take a seat immediately—— Everyone will take a seat immediately
- **为什么这样写**：Inspector Bell's command is the chapter's authority moment—she takes control of the ballroom. "Everyone will take a seat immediately" is her no-nonsense order. The police presence (Bell + officers) confirms the murder investigation's seriousness.
- **读者视角提示**：Inspector Bell's command shows her authority. Track her investigation progress.

> **原句 5:** “I will say this once,” she said. “Put the signs on the ground. Now.” Half of the protesters dropped their placards. Several officers advanced down the middle and side aisles. “Last chance. Those signs are weapons and will be treated as such.” Lumber crashed to the ground, leaving two spindly signs aloft like rejected trees in a clear-cut zone.

- **中文理解**："I will say this once，"she said。"Put the signs on the ground。Now。"Half of the protesters dropped their placards。Several officers advanced down the middle and side aisles。"Last chance。Those signs are weapons and will be treated as such。"Lumber crashed to the ground，leaving two spindly signs aloft like rejected trees in a clear-cut zone。
- **关键词**：Put the signs on the ground—— Put the signs on the ground；Those signs are weapons—— Those signs are weapons；rejected trees in a clear-cut zone—— rejected trees in a clear-cut zone
- **为什么这样写**：Inspector Bell's escalation ("Those signs are weapons") is her threat. "rejected trees in a clear-cut zone" is Cat's simile for the remaining signs. The protesters' gradual compliance (half drop, then most, then holdouts) shows Bell's authority. Kyle and Peter Johnson as holdouts establishes their defiance.
- **读者视角提示**：Kyle and Peter Johnson as holdouts show their extremism. Track their role in the investigation.

> **原句 6:** “Hold your horses, I’m getting there,” he said. “Look at her feet.”

- **中文理解**："Hold your horses，I'm getting there，"he said。"Look at her feet。"
- **关键词**：Hold your horses—— Hold your horses；Look at her feet—— Look at her feet
- **为什么这样写**：Doug's "Hold your horses" is his pacing of the reveal—he's savoring his discovery. "Look at her feet" is his key instruction (the Ugg boots). This is the chapter's central clue: the hooded figure (Clarity's cape) has Bliss's Ugg boots → the killer mistook Bliss for Clarity.
- **读者视角提示**：Doug's "Look at her feet" is the chapter's turning point. The Ugg boots are the key evidence.

> **原句 7:** “Uggs! Doug, you genius!”

- **中文理解**："Uggs! Doug, you genius!"
- **关键词**：Uggs—— Uggs；Doug, you genius—— Doug, you genius
- **为什么这样写**：Cat's "Uggs! Doug, you genius!" is her excitement at the discovery. The Ugg boots (Bliss's signature footwear) on the hooded figure (Clarity's cape) = mistaken identity. "Doug, you genius" is Cat's praise. This is the chapter's central revelation.
- **读者视角提示**：The Ugg boots + Clarity's cape = the killer targeted Clarity but killed Bliss (who was wearing Clarity's cape). Track how this changes the investigation.

> **原句 8:** “Why was Bliss wearing Clarity’s cape?”

> **原句 9:** “Did the killer mistake Bliss for Clarity?”

- **中文理解**："Why was Bliss wearing Clarity's cape?" ... "Did the killer mistake Bliss for Clarity?"
- **关键词**：Why was Bliss wearing Clarity's cape?—— Why was Bliss wearing Clarity's cape?；Did the killer mistake Bliss for Clarity?—— Did the killer mistake Bliss for Clarity?
- **为什么这样写**：Cat and Bruce's questions are the chapter's conclusion. "Why was Bliss wearing Clarity's cape?" is the first question (Bliss had Clarity's cape). "Did the killer mistake Bliss for Clarity?" is the second (the killer targeted Clarity). These two questions reframe the entire investigation.
- **读者视角提示**：The mistaken-identity theory is the chapter's central revelation. Track how this changes the suspect list (now Clarity's enemies, not Bliss's).
