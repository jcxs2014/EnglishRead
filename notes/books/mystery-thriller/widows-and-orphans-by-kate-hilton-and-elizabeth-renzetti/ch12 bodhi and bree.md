## 精读

> **原句 1:** “When was the last time you were struck by wonder?” Bodhi asked, bouncing on the tips of his vintage Air Max sneakers. “When was the last time you inhaled the mineral scent of your mate, tasted his elemental nature, felt the protective warmth of his flesh?”

- **中文理解**："When was the last time you were struck by wonder?" Bodhi asked，bouncing on the tips of his vintage Air Max sneakers。"When was the last time you inhaled the mineral scent of your mate，tasted his elemental nature，felt the protective warmth of his flesh？"
- **关键词**：struck by wonder—— struck by wonder；mineral scent of your mate—— mineral scent of your mate；elemental nature—— elemental nature；protective warmth of his flesh—— protective warmth of his flesh
- **为什么这样写**：Bodhi's workshop rhetoric is the chapter's satire target. "mineral scent of your mate" and "elemental nature" are his pseudo-spiritual language. "bouncing on the tips of his vintage Air Max sneakers" is Cat's physical observation—his energy is performative. The "mate" language (heteronormative) contrasts with his "wonder" brand.
- **读者视角提示**：Bodhi's rhetoric is his scam. Track how his "wonder" language masks his affair with Bliss and his threat to Cat.

> **原句 2:** “Bliss once stopped me when I was getting into my car,” Bodhi continued, “and she pointed out there was a ladybug on the windshield. She didn’t want it to blow away.”

> **原句 3:** “So she took it on her finger and carried it over to the grass.”

- **中文理解**："Bliss once stopped me when I was getting into my car，"Bodhi continued，"and she pointed out there was a ladybug on the windshield。She didn't want it to blow away。"His voice tightened，became strained。"So she took it on her finger and carried it over to the grass。"He paused and lifted one arm to wipe his eyes，a movement which also displayed a lovingly tended triceps。
- **关键词**：ladybug on the windshield—— ladybug on the windshield；didn't want it to blow away—— didn't want it to blow away；lovingly tended triceps—— lovingly tended triceps
- **为什么这样写**：Bodhi's ladybug anecdote is his "poignant" Bliss tribute. "His voice tightened, became strained" is his performance of grief. "a movement which also displayed a lovingly tended triceps" is Cat's sardonic observation—his grief is also self-display. The triceps detail undercuts the sincerity.
- **读者视角提示**：Bodhi's "lovingly tended triceps" reveals his vanity. His grief performance is suspect. Track whether his ladybug story is true or fabricated.

> **原句 4:** “I think you’d better get out of here, and take your absurd accusations with you. And if I see one word of this in print—one word—you’ll be slapped with a libel suit so fast your hair will catch fire.”

- **中文理解**："I think you'd better get out of here，and take your absurd accusations with you。And if I see one word of this in print—one word—you'll be slapped with a libel suit so fast your hair will catch fire。"
- **关键词**：absurd accusations—— absurd accusations；libel suit—— libel suit；hair will catch fire—— hair will catch fire
- **为什么这样写**：Bodhi's threat is the chapter's confrontation peak. "absurd accusations" is his dismissal of Cat's (accurate) claim about his affair with Bliss. "libel suit so fast your hair will catch fire" is his legal threat (hyperbolic). This establishes Bodhi as a suspect who's willing to threaten journalists.
- **读者视角提示**：Bodhi's libel threat is his defensive reaction. Track whether his threat is bluff or genuine. "hair will catch fire" is ironic (he's a wellness guru).

> **原句 5:** “Not to mention,” he said through gritted teeth, “I know where your mother was when Bliss died. You might want to check your glass house before you start throwing stones. Now get the hell out.”

- **中文理解**："Not to mention，"he said through gritted teeth，"I know where your mother was when Bliss died。You might want to check your glass house before you start throwing stones。Now get the hell out。"
- **关键词**：glass house—— glass house；throwing stones—— throwing stones；gritted teeth—— gritted teeth
- **为什么这样写**：Bodhi's counter-threat is the chapter's key revelation—he knows about Marian's midnight smoking (he's the one who ratted her to cops). "glass house" is his metaphor (Cat's mother is also compromised). "Now get the hell out" is his final expulsion. This establishes Bodhi as someone with information about Marian.
- **读者视角提示**：Bodhi ratted Marian to cops. Why? Track whether Bodhi is protecting himself or attacking Marian.

> **原句 6:** “I think you all know about my mourning journey,” she said, and her voice was barely above a whisper. “When I lost my husband, I thought the sun had gone out. The only one who understood was Bliss, because she’d gone through the same thing.”

- **中文理解**："I think you all know about my mourning journey，"she said，and her voice was barely above a whisper。"When I lost my husband，I thought the sun had gone out。The only one who understood was Bliss，because she'd gone through the same thing。"
- **关键词**：mourning journey—— mourning journey；sun had gone out—— sun had gone out；the only one who understood—— the only one who understood
- **为什么这样写**：Bree's "mourning journey" speech is the chapter's performance. "barely above a whisper" is her grief voice. "the sun had gone out" is her metaphor for widowhood. "The only one who understood was Bliss" is her claim of special bond—ironic given their hatred. Cat's "auditioning for the role of Grieving Best Friend" frames this as performance.
- **读者视角提示**：Bree's "mourning journey" is performance. Cat's "Nowhere better to hide than under a cloak of pity" (narration) frames Bree as potential killer hiding behind grief.

> **原句 7:** “They haaaated each other.”

- **中文理解**："They haaaated each other。"
- **关键词**：haaaated—— haaaated
- **为什么这样写**：Janet Genovese's text is the chapter's final revelation. "haaaated" (with extra a's) is Janet's emphasis—she's confirming the hostility. This is the chapter's last line (after "They haaaated each other."). The extra a's convey Janet's certainty and drama. This confirms Bruce's "support person" theory: Bliss and Bree hated each other.
- **读者视角提示**："They haaaated each other" is the chapter's bombshell. Track whether this hatred led to murder.
