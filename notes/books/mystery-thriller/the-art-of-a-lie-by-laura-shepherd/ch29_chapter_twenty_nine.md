# 29. Chapter Twenty-Nine

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Hannah 对 Billy 产生怀疑，她跟踪调查 David Thewson，发现 Billy 曾用假名 William Everhart，并涉嫌盗窃和过失。
- **情感弧线位置**：怀疑 → 恐惧。Hannah 开始调查 Billy 的过去，发现他可能不是他声称的那个人。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：调查（investigation）——Hannah 开始调查 Billy 的过去；假名（false name）——Billy 曾用假名 William Everhart；过去追来（past catches up）——Penelope Felton 也在寻找 Billy。
- **人物弧线**：Hannah 从"深陷情网的女人"转为"怀疑者"——她开始质疑 Billy 的真实身份。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称 Hannah 视角，通过 Hannah 的调查展现她对 Billy 的怀疑。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** They say everyone has a double. All night I thought about that encounter, even while I coupled with William at his rooms. He hadn't mentioned the incident again, as if it was entirely inconsequential. And yet I kept remembering the man's expression when he'd looked back and shaken his head. He hadn't believed William. I was not sure I believed William.

**中文理解**：人们说每个人都有一个分身。整晚我都在想那次遭遇，即使当我和威廉在他的房间里做爱时。他没有再提起那次事件，好像它完全无关紧要。但我不断记住那个男人回头看并摇头时的表情。他不相信威廉。我不确定我相信威廉。

**关键词**：double（分身）/ coupled with William（和威廉做爱）/ entirely inconsequential（完全无关紧要）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 的"怀疑"——"entirely inconsequential"暗示 Hannah 的"怀疑"。"coupled with William"暗示 Hannah 的"矛盾"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"I was not sure I believed William"（我不确定我相信威廉）——这种措辞暗示 Hannah 的"怀疑"是"真实"的。

> **原句 2:** That flicker in his eye, so fleeting I might have imagined it. Had he changed his name at some point in the past? And if so, why? I recalled Felix's coarse laughter. It's not me she needs to be worrying about. He'd called me a flat. Was it true? Could I be a flat?

**关键词**：flicker in his eye（眼中的闪光）/ changed his name（改名）/ flat（傻瓜）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 的"自我怀疑"——"flat"暗示 Hannah 的"自我怀疑"。"changed his name"暗示 Hannah 的"调查"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 的说法"Could I be a flat"（我会是个傻瓜吗）——这种措辞暗示 Hannah 的"自我怀疑"是"真实"的。

> **原句 3:** I'd known William just over a month and I was offering to give him all of my money. If he failed to pay me back, then I wouldn't be able to pay off my loans - and even with the profits I was making now, the extortionate interest would cripple me.

**关键词**：offering to give him all of my money（提出把我所有的钱都给他）/ extortionate interest（高利贷利息）/ cripple me（让我瘫痪）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 的"风险"——"extortionate interest"暗示 Hannah 的"风险"。"cripple me"暗示 Hannah 的"恐惧"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 的说法"If he failed to pay me back"（如果他不还我钱）——这种措辞暗示 Hannah 的"恐惧"是"真实"的。

> **原句 4:** I'd anticipated having to make an appointment and return, but as luck would have had it, whilst I was discussing the matter with the clerk, Mr Thewson himself walked into the lobby in the company of another man.

**关键词**：anticipated having to make an appointment（预料必须预约）/ as luck would have had it（幸运的是）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 的"运气"——"as luck would have had it"暗示 Hannah 的"运气"。"anticipated having to make an appointment"暗示 Hannah 的"期待"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 的说法"Mr Thewson himself walked into the lobby"（特森先生自己走进大厅）——这种措辞暗示 Hannah 的"运气"。

> **原句 5:** 'Everhart had half the girls in Wandsworth in love with him. I don't like to call a man a liar, and I cannot swear for certain that it was him, but if it isn't, then they are as alike as twins.'

**关键词**：half the girls in Wandsworth in love with him（旺兹沃思有一半女孩爱上了他）/ as alike as twins（像双胞胎一样）

**为什么这样写**：Thewson 的"确认"——"as alike as twins"暗示 Thewson 的"确认"。"half the girls in Wandsworth in love with him"暗示 Billy 的"魅力"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 的说法"if it isn't, then they are as alike as twins"（如果不是，那么他们就像双胞胎一样）——这种措辞暗示 Thewson 的"确认"是"真实"的。

> **原句 6:** I stared into the sun. William had a scar upon his knee. He'd told me he'd got it in India, when he'd pushed a child out of the path of a runaway cart. Surely that couldn't be another coincidence, on top of his likeness and the same first name and that flicker of panic? William had changed his name. I was sure of it now.

**关键词**：scar upon his knee（膝盖上的伤疤）/ pushed a child out of the path of a runaway cart（把一个孩子推出失控马车的路径）/ coincidence（巧合）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 的"确认"——"William had changed his name. I was sure of it now"暗示 Hannah 的"确认"。"scar upon his knee"暗示 Hannah 的"证据"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 的说法"Surely that couldn't be another coincidence"（这肯定不是另一个巧合）——这种措辞暗示 Hannah 的"确认"是"真实"的。

> **原句 7:** But perhaps he'd had a good reason for doing it? Hadn't Mrs Parmenter said that his father had been a brute? And alongside that scandal involving his mother, maybe he'd wanted to distance himself from his childhood?

**关键词**：good reason for doing it（这样做的充分理由）/ distance himself from his childhood（远离他的童年）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 的"自我安慰"——"good reason for doing it"暗示 Hannah 的"自我安慰"。"distance himself from his childhood"暗示 Hannah 的"理解"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 的说法"But perhaps he'd had a good reason for doing it"（但也许他这样做的充分理由）——这种措辞暗示 Hannah 的"自我安慰"。

> **原句 8:** 'She asked me that, too.' 'She?' 'A lady called on me last month. She was looking for Everhart. Had a list of names of men who'd been enrolled with him at Wandsworth, and I was on it. I told her I hadn't seen him in an age, but that the last I'd heard he was headed for the fort at Tilbury.'

**关键词**：looking for Everhart（寻找埃弗哈特）/ list of names of men who'd been enrolled with him（和他一起入学的男人名单）/ fort at Tilbury（蒂尔伯里堡）

**为什么这样写**：Penelope Felton 的"调查"——"looking for Everhart"暗示 Penelope Felton 的"调查"。"list of names of men who'd been enrolled with him"暗示 Penelope Felton 的"坚持"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 的说法"She was looking for Everhart"（她在寻找埃弗哈特）——这种措辞暗示 Penelope Felton 的"调查"是"真实"的。

> **原句 9:** Thewson fished a little book from his pocket and turned a few pages. 'Mrs Penelope Felton.' 'Do you have an address for her?' I'm afraid not. She seemed disappointed by the little I knew, and didn't leave a card.'

**关键词**：fished a little book from his pocket（从口袋里掏出一个小本子）/ disappointed by the little I knew（对我所知道的很少感到失望）

**为什么这样写**：Penelope Felton 的"调查"——"disappointed by the little I knew"暗示 Penelope Felton 的"调查"。"didn't leave a card"暗示 Penelope Felton 的"秘密"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 的说法"She seemed disappointed by the little I knew"（她对我所知道的很少感到失望）——这种措辞暗示 Penelope Felton 的"调查"是"持续"的。

> **原句 10:** 'Not in so many words, but she was very eager to find him. My impression was that a sum of money might be involved.'

**关键词**：very eager to find him（非常渴望找到他）/ a sum of money might be involved（可能涉及一笔钱）

**为什么这样写**：Penelope Felton 的"动机"——"a sum of money might be involved"暗示 Penelope Felton 的"动机"。"very eager to find him"暗示 Penelope Felton 的"坚持"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 的说法"My impression was that a sum of money might be involved"（我的印象是可能涉及一笔钱）——这种措辞暗示 Penelope Felton 的"动机"是"金钱"。
