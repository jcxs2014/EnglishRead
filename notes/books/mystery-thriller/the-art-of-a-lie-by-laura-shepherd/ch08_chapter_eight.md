# 8. Chapter Eight

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Hannah 与 Devereux、Mrs Parmenter 一同前往 Hickford's Long Room 观看化学表演，期间遇到 Lord Richard，确认瓷鱼来自"The Goldfish"赌场，并得知其地址就在 Punchbowl and Pineapple 附近。
- **情感弧线位置**：紧张 → 兴奋。Hannah 在社交场合中保持警惕，但赌场线索的出现让她看到了希望。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：社交场合（social occasion）——Hannah 在上流社会中感到不适；赌场确认（casino confirmed）——瓷鱼被确认为"The Goldfish"的筹码；Devereux 的真面目（Devereux's true nature）——Mrs Parmenter 暗示 Devereux 对 Hannah 的兴趣不纯。
- **人物弧线**：Hannah 从"被动参与者"转为主动调查者——她开始利用 Devereux 获取信息，同时保持警惕。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称限知视角，通过 Hannah 的内心独白展现她对 Devereux 和 Mrs Parmenter 的观察。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** I knew right away that I did not like Mrs Parmenter. She was all smiles, patting me with a lace-gloved hand, when Mr Devereux handed me into his chaise and made the introductions. But I caught a glitter of judgement in her little blue eyes.

**中文理解**：我立刻知道我不喜欢 Mrs Parmenter。当 Devereux 先生把我扶上马车并介绍我们认识时，她满脸笑容，用戴蕾丝手套的手拍我。但我捕捉到了她蓝色小眼睛里的评判光芒。

**关键词**：right away（立刻）/ all smiles（满脸笑容）/ glitter of judgement（评判的光芒）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 对 Mrs Parmenter 的第一印象——"all smiles"（满脸笑容）是表面友好，"glitter of judgement"（评判的光芒）暗示 Mrs Parmenter 在评估 Hannah 的价值。这种"表面友好+内心评判"的组合暗示 Mrs Parmenter 的虚伪。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"I caught a glitter"（我捕捉到了）——这种措辞暗示 Hannah 对他人情绪的敏锐观察力。

> **原句 2:** 'William has told me about your loss,' Mrs Parmenter said. 'How brave of you to venture out so soon. And in purple already. How times change.'

**中文理解**："William 告诉过你的损失，"Mrs Parmenter 说。"你这么早就敢出来，真勇敢。而且已经穿上紫色了。时代真是变了。"

**关键词**：venture out（出来）/ purple（紫色）/ How times change（时代变了）

**为什么这样写**：Mrs Parmenter 的"brave"（勇敢）是反语，暗示 Hannah 的行为不妥。"purple"（紫色）是丧服的颜色，但 Hannah 过早穿上紫色暗示她对丈夫的死并不悲伤。"How times change"（时代变了）是讽刺，暗示当代女性不再遵守传统的丧服规范。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"And in purple already"（而且已经穿上紫色了）——这种措辞暗示 Mrs Parmenter 认为 Hannah 穿得太早，暗示她不忠。

> **原句 3:** 'I have heard all about your little shop.' Mrs Parmenter smiled. 'You must be terribly sure of yourself to even think of running it alone.'

**中文理解**："我听说过你的小店。"Mrs Parmenter 笑了。"你一定非常自信，才敢想一个人经营它。"

**关键词**：little shop（小店）/ terribly sure of yourself（非常自信）/ alone（一个人）

**为什么这样写**：Mrs Parmenter 的"little shop"（小店）是轻蔑的说法，暗示她认为 Hannah 的店铺微不足道。"terribly sure of yourself"（非常自信）是讽刺，暗示 Hannah 过于自信。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"alone"（一个人）——这种措辞暗示 Mrs Parmenter 认为女性不应独立经营，需要男性帮助。

> **原句 4:** I put her age at about seventy, her neat grey curls secured with a blue silk ribbon and a diamond brooch. Tiny in stature, she was wizened as a currant, to my mind hollowed out by an excess of malice. Yet what did I care for her good opinion? Neither she, nor her precious Mr Devereux, meant anything to me. Their company was merely to be endured in the interest of my inquiry.

**中文理解**：我估计她大约七十岁，整齐的灰色卷发用蓝色丝绸丝带和钻石胸针固定。她身材矮小，像葡萄干一样干瘪，在我看来被过度的恶意掏空了。但我干嘛在乎她的好评？她和她珍贵的 Devereux 先生对我来说都不算什么。他们的陪伴只是出于我的调查需要而忍受。

**关键词**：wizened（干瘪的）/ currant（葡萄干）/ hollowed out（掏空）/ malice（恶意）/ endured（忍受）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 对 Mrs Parmenter 的评价——"wizened as a currant"（像葡萄干一样干瘪）是生动的比喻，暗示 Mrs Parmenter 的老态。"hollowed out by an excess of malice"（被过度的恶意掏空）暗示 Mrs Parmenter 的内心已被恶意吞噬。"merely to be endured"（只是忍受）暗示 Hannah 对这段关系的功利态度。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"their company was merely to be endured"（他们的陪伴只是忍受）——这种措辞暗示她在利用 Devereux 和 Mrs Parmenter。

> **原句 5:** Mr Devereux sat back upon the carriage seat, his face golden in the evening sun. His emerald-green silk coat was unbuttoned to display a waistcoat embroidered with humming-birds, a black silk solitaire tied around his neck. Had he made an effort for me? I rather hoped he had. It gave me a frisson of pleasure to imagine his face later tonight once he discovered his elaborate plan of seduction had all been in vain.

**中文理解**：Devereux 先生靠在马车座位上，脸在晚阳光下泛着金光。他的翡翠绿丝绸外套敞开着，展示着绣有蜂鸟的背心，脖子上系着黑色丝质领结。他为我打扮过吗？我相当希望他打扮了。想象今晚稍后当他发现他精心设计的诱惑计划全部落空时的脸，我感到一阵愉悦。

**关键词**：emerald-green（翡翠绿）/ waistcoat（背心）/ humming-birds（蜂鸟）/ elaborate plan（精心设计的计划）/ in vain（落空）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 对 Devereux 的观察——"emerald-green silk coat"（翡翠绿丝绸外套）和"waistcoat embroidered with humming-birds"（绣有蜂鸟的背心）暗示 Devereux 的财富和品味。"elaborate plan of seduction"（精心设计的诱惑计划）暗示 Hannah 看穿了 Devereux 的意图。"frisson of pleasure"（一阵愉悦）暗示 Hannah 享受这种"游戏"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"I rather hoped he had"（我相当希望他打扮了）——这种措辞暗示她在享受 Devereux 的关注，尽管她知道这是"游戏"。

> **原句 6:** 'I am glad to see you smiling,' he said. 'You look very well tonight, Mrs Cole.' I inclined my head, though inwardly I deplored his lies. The gown I was wearing, the finest I had in purple, was a velvet affair more suited for winter than a hot summer's evening.

**中文理解**："我很高兴看到你微笑，"他说。"你今晚看起来很好，Mrs Cole。"我点了点头，尽管内心谴责他的谎言。我穿的礼服，我最好的紫色丝绒款，更适合冬天而不是炎热的夏夜。

**关键词**：inclined my head（点头）/ deplored（谴责）/ velvet（丝绒）/ suited for winter（适合冬天）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 对 Devereux 恭维的反应——"inwardly I deplored his lies"（内心谴责他的谎言）暗示她知道 Devereux 的恭维是假的。"velvet affair more suited for winter"（更适合冬天的丝绒款）暗示 Hannah 为了"得体"而牺牲舒适。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"the finest I had in purple"（我最好的紫色款）——这种措辞暗示她的丧服有限，也暗示她在经济上并不宽裕。

> **原句 7:** 'Thank you, Tom,' Devereux called up to his coachman. 'Bring the carriage around at nine, will you? I promised Mrs Cole that I would not have her home too late.' I'm sure he thought I'd change my mind, once subject to the full force of his charm and persuasion. Pretending not to notice the arm he offered, I made my own way into the hall, where I attempted to pay for my own ticket. Upon discovering, to my astonishment, that entrance cost a half-guinea, I fell silent and allowed Mr Devereux to pay.

**中文理解**："谢谢你，Tom，"Devereux 对车夫喊道。"九点把马车带过来，好吗？我答应过 Mrs Cole 不会让她回家太晚。"我确信他以为我会改变主意，一旦受到他魅力和说服力的全部影响。假装没有注意到他伸出的手臂，我自己走进大厅，试图自己付票。当发现门票价格是半基尼时，我震惊得说不出话来，让 Devereux 先生付了钱。

**关键词**：persuasion（说服力）/ half-guinea（半基尼）/ fell silent（说不出话来）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 对 Devereux 的警惕——"Pretending not to notice the arm he offered"（假装没有注意到他伸出的手臂）暗示她在拒绝 Devereux 的"绅士"姿态。"half-guinea"（半基尼）是昂贵的门票价格，暗示 Hannah 的经济状况不允许她独立参加这种活动。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"I attempted to pay for my own ticket"（试图自己付票）——这种措辞暗示她不想欠 Devereux 的人情，但昂贵的门票价格让她不得不接受。

> **原句 8:** 'The Goldfish,' he exclaimed. 'Best golden hell in town. I lost three thousand acres there once. Don't tell my father.' 'Where is this place?' I asked. 'St James's Square,' he said. 'Lucky number seven.' A stone's throw from the Punchbowl and Pineapple. I wondered how often Jonas had frequented the place.

**中文理解**："金鱼，"他惊呼。"城里最好的金色地狱。我在那里输了三千英亩。别告诉我父亲。""这地方在哪里？"我问。"St James's 广场，"他说。"幸运七号。"离 Punchbowl and Pineapple 一箭之遥。我想知道 Jonas 多久去那里一次。

**关键词**：golden hell（金色地狱）/ three thousand acres（三千英亩）/ stone's throw（一箭之遥）/ frequented（常去）

**为什么这样写**：Lord Richard 确认赌场——"The Goldfish"（金鱼）是赌场名字，"three thousand acres"（三千英亩）是夸张的说法，暗示 Lord Richard 输了很多钱。"stone's throw"（一箭之遥）暗示赌场离 Hannah 的店很近，Jonas 常去那里并不奇怪。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"I wondered how often Jonas had frequented the place"（我想知道 Jonas 多久去那里一次）——这种措辞暗示她对 Jonas 的不了解，也暗示她开始怀疑 Jonas 的赌场活动。

> **原句 9:** Mrs Parmenter ushered me to a vacant table, where she summoned a waiter to bring wine. When Devereux joined us, she gave him a reproving look. 'Giving your money away again, William?' 'There's more to life than money,' he said. 'Brereton's work is shockingly underfunded and I am proud to support it.' 'It is underfunded because it is an affront to right-thinking people.'

**中文理解**：Mrs Parmenter 带我到一张空桌，她叫来服务员拿酒。当 Devereux 加入我们时，她给了他一个责备的眼神。"又把你的钱送人了，威廉？""生活中还有比金钱更重要的东西，"他说。"Brereton 的工作资金不足，我很自豪地支持它。""资金不足是因为它是对正派人的侮辱。"

**关键词**：reproving look（责备的眼神）/ giving your money away（送钱）/ underfunded（资金不足）/ affront（侮辱）

**为什么这样写**：Mrs Parmenter 与 Devereux 的对话——"reproving look"（责备的眼神）暗示 Mrs Parmenter 不赞成 Devereux 的慈善行为。"affront to right-thinking people"（对正派人的侮辱）暗示 Mrs Parmenter 的保守观点。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"right-thinking people"（正派人）——这种措辞暗示 Mrs Parmenter 的"正派"是狭隘的，与 Hannah 的观点形成对比。

> **原句 10:** Beneath Devereux's smile, I detected a trace of irritation. 'Right-thinking people should be more affronted by the poverty and desperation that drive women to such extremes.' Mrs Parmenter turned to me. 'That tatterdemalion in black has established a home for fallen women,' she explained. 'All well and good, one might think, but he seeks to reintroduce them to polite society, where they might be employed by or even marry unsuspecting men. You are surely with me on this, Mrs Cole?'

**中文理解**：在 Devereux 的微笑下，我察觉到一丝恼怒。"正派的人应该对驱使女性走向极端贫穷和绝望更加感到震惊。"Mrs Parmenter 转向我。"那个穿破衣服的黑衣人建立了一个堕落女性的家，"她解释说。"人们可能认为这很好，但他试图让她们重新进入上流社会，她们可能被甚至嫁给毫无防备的男人。你肯定站在我这边，Mrs Cole？"

**关键词**：irritation（恼怒）/ tatterdemalion（穿破衣服的人）/ fallen women（堕落女性）/ unsuspecting（毫无防备的）

**为什么这样写**：Devereux 为 Brereton 辩护——"poverty and desperation"（贫穷和绝望）暗示 Devereux 对底层人民的同情。"fallen women"（堕落女性）是 18 世纪对"失足女性"的称呼，暗示社会对女性的偏见。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"You are surely with me on this"（你肯定站在我这边）——这种措辞暗示 Mrs Parmenter 试图拉拢 Hannah，但 Hannah 并不认同她的观点。

> **原句 11:** Though I considered both parties my adversaries, in this particular instance I knew whose side I was on. 'On the contrary,' I said, 'it sounds a very worthy cause. I commend your generosity, Mr Devereux.'

**中文理解**：虽然我认为双方都是我的对手，但在这件事上我知道我站在谁那边。"相反，"我说，"这听起来是一个非常值得的事业。我赞扬您的慷慨，Devereux 先生。"

**关键词**：adversaries（对手）/ on the contrary（相反）/ worthy cause（值得的事业）/ commend（赞扬）

**为什么这样写**：Hannah 选择支持 Devereux——"both parties my adversaries"（双方都是我的对手）暗示 Hannah 并不信任任何人。"on the contrary"（相反）表明她选择站在 Devereux 一边，但这更多是为了对抗 Mrs Parmenter，而非真正的认同。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"I knew whose side I was on"（我知道我站在谁那边）——这种措辞暗示她的选择是策略性的，而非情感性的。

> **原句 12:** Any further discussion upon the subject was curtailed by the ringing of a bell. Everyone hastily found their seats, and the Master of Ceremonies walked onto the stage. He announced that Professor Becker of the university at Leiden was to demonstrate a series of chemical marvels, after which there would be an interval, followed by a concert of vocal and instrumental music.

**中文理解**：铃声打断了关于这个话题的讨论。大家匆忙就座，司仪走上舞台。他宣布莱顿大学的 Becker 教授将展示一系列化学奇迹，然后是休息时间，接着是声乐和器乐音乐会。

**关键词**：curtailed（打断）/ Master of Ceremonies（司仪）/ chemical marvels（化学奇迹）

**为什么这样写**：Becker 教授的表演——"chemical marvels"（化学奇迹）暗示表演的性质是"科学展示"，但在当时被视为"奇迹"。"vocal and instrumental music"（声乐和器乐音乐）暗示这是一个综合娱乐活动。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"a series of chemical marvels"（一系列化学奇迹）——这种措辞暗示 Hannah 对科学的好奇，也暗示她对 Becker 教授的期待。

> **原句 13:** Becker made his entrance onto the stage to much applause. A portly, well-dressed man in a brown periwig, he appeared quite different to the gaunt alchemist in singed robes that I'd imagined. 'All that you will see tonight,' he informed us in a heavy German accent, 'will appear beyond nature. My experiments will excite, they will surprise, and they will astonish. Yet all are the product of natural causes: no tricks, no magic, only the wonders of chemistry.'

**中文理解**：Becker 教授在热烈的掌声中登场。一个穿着棕色假发的丰满、穿着得体的人，他看起来与我脑海中穿着烧焦长袍的消瘦炼金术士截然不同。"你们今晚看到的一切，"他用浓重的德语口音告诉我们，"将超越自然。我的实验会激发，会惊讶，会震惊。但所有都是自然原因的结果：没有诡计，没有魔法，只有化学的奇迹。"

**关键词**：portly（丰满的）/ gaunt（消瘦的）/ singed robes（烧焦的长袍）/ beyond nature（超越自然）/ wonders of chemistry（化学的奇迹）

**为什么这样写**：Becker 教授的形象——"portly, well-dressed"（丰满、穿着得体）与"gaunt alchemist in singed robes"（穿着烧焦长袍的消瘦炼金术士）形成反差，暗示 Hannah 对科学家的刻板印象。"wonders of chemistry"（化学的奇迹）暗示 Becker 教授将科学视为"魔法"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"no tricks, no magic"（没有诡计，没有魔法）——这种措辞暗示 Hannah 对"欺骗"敏感，她也担心自己被 Becker 教授"欺骗"。

> **原句 14:** 'This is the recipe we used,' I said, taking out Devereux's paper. 'I followed it to the letter, but the cream wouldn't freeze. It simply grew cold.' The professor studied the recipe a moment. 'Little wonder,' he said. 'You have neglected to unlock the hidden heat within your liquid.' I frowned, confused. 'But the cream is cold. There is no heat.' Becker smiled. 'All liquids contain a latent heat concealed within themselves. Even when they feel cold, they are secretly hot. You must withdraw that fire by means of the frigorific method.'

**中文理解**："这是我们用的食谱，"我说，拿出 Devereux 的纸。"我逐字遵循，但奶油就是不冻。它只是变冷。"教授研究了一会儿食谱。"难怪，"他说。"你忽略了释放液体中隐藏的热。"我皱起眉头，感到困惑。"但奶油是冷的。没有热。"Becker 笑了。"所有液体都包含着潜热，隐藏在它们自己内部。即使它们感觉冷，它们也秘密地热。你必须用冷冻法提取那火。"

**关键词**：to the letter（逐字）/ latent heat（潜热）/ frigorific method（冷冻法）/ withdraw that fire（提取那火）

**为什么这样写**：Becker 教授解释冰淇淋制作——"latent heat"（潜热）是科学术语，暗示 Hannah 对科学的无知。"frigorific method"（冷冻法）是专业术语，暗示 Becker 教授的专业性。"withdraw that fire"（提取那火）是形象化的说法，将"热"比喻为"火"。

**读者视角提示**：注意 Hannah 说的是"I followed it to the letter"（我逐字遵循）——这种措辞暗示她的认真，但也暗示她缺乏灵活性。

## 一句话总结

本章是调查的关键转折点——Hannah 在 Hickford's Long Room 确认瓷鱼来自"The Goldfish"赌场，并得知其地址就在 Punchbowl and Pineapple 附近。这条线索将调查方向从"街头抢劫"转向"赌场内幕"，同时也让 Hannah 意识到 Devereux 对她的兴趣可能不仅仅是"投资顾问"。
