# 46. Chapter 46

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Tabitha 逃跑后，Bell 声称人偶被"诅咒"了，Binfire 是杀死 Jack Braxton 的凶手；Kit 感到"a stone plunge into the bottom of her stomach"（一块石头坠入胃底）。

**情感弧线位置**：震惊与不安——Bell 的"dark magic"（黑魔法）和"retribution"（报应）暗示了他的"疯狂"，Kit 的"stone plunge"（石头坠入）暗示了她的"恐惧"。

**叙事手法**：对话场景（图书馆外→图书馆）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"half-listening"（半听半不听）和"surreal"（超现实）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Kit 的"TOO obvious"

> **原句 1:** Kit was only half-listening to them. It all seemed a bit surreal. Tabitha's the obvious suspect. She did it and it's as simple as that. No ghost of Furley House. Nothing to do with the mysterious room full of freshly bought suits and the unused toothbrush and the jar labelled NATE. Nothing to do with Jack Braxton's death. Every piece of evidence pointed right to her and I didn't want to know. Perhaps because it was TOO obvious. Or perhaps it's simpler than that. I just didn't want it to be her.

**中文理解**：Kit 只是半听半不听。一切似乎有点超现实。Tabitha 是明显的嫌疑人。她做了，就这么简单。与 Furley House 的鬼魂无关。与装满新买的西装、未使用的牙刷和标有 NATE 的罐子的神秘房间无关。与 Jack Braxton 的死亡无关。每一条证据都指向她，而我不想知道。也许是因为太明显了。或者也许更简单。我只是不想是她。

**关键词** | surreal / TOO obvious / didn't want it to be her

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"TOO obvious"（太明显）暗示了她的"怀疑"——她"不相信"这么"简单"。"didn't want it to be her"（不想是她）暗示了 Kit 的"情感"——她"希望" Tabitha 是"无辜"的。

---

### 第 2 段：Bell 的"cursed"

> **原句 2:** "They're not just action figures, you know?" "No?" "No." "What are they?" "They're alive." "Alive?" "Alive with dark magic. They're cursed." "Is that something a man of the cloth should be saying?" "You don't work in my profession without acknowledging the presence of evil. And they are evil. Don't you see?" "Not really," said Kit gently. "Why don't you explain it to me?" "Isn't it obvious? I broke the first rule of toy collection: I touched the mint-in-box figures. You're not supposed to do that. I opened their boxes and I ran the tip of my finger along their little plastic heads. I disturbed their slumber, like Howard Carter did when he entered Tutankhamun's tomb. And now they visit retribution on the sinners. Graham, Matty, Fenton… And now the daughter of Roland Pendragon. His sins are passed down to her. That's how it works."

**中文理解**："它们不仅仅是人偶，你知道吗？""不是吗？""不是。""它们是什么？""它们活着。""活着？""充满黑魔法地活着。它们被诅咒了。""这是神职人员应该说的话吗？""你不从事我的职业而不承认邪恶的存在。它们是邪恶的。你不明白吗？"不完全是，"Kit 温和地说。"你为什么不向我解释？""不是很明显吗？我打破了人偶收藏的第一条规则：我触摸了盒内全新的人偶。你不应该那样做。我打开了它们的盒子，我用指尖沿着它们的小塑料头抚摸。我打扰了他们的沉睡，就像霍华德·卡特进入图坦卡蒙的坟墓时那样。现在它们向罪人施以报复。Graham、Matty、Fenton……还有 Roland Pendragon 的女儿。他的罪传给了她。就是这样运作的。"

**关键词** | dark magic / cursed / man of the cloth / profession / evil / Howard Carter / Tutankhamun's tomb / retribution / sinners

**为什么这样写**：Bell 的"Howard Carter"（霍华德·卡特）和"Tutankhamun's tomb"（图坦卡蒙的坟墓）暗示了他的"知识"——他用"历史"来"解释""诅咒"。"retribution"（报应）和"sinners"（罪人）暗示了 Bell 的"宗教"思维——他认为这是"神的惩罚"。

### 第 3 段：Bell 的"Binfire killed Jack Braxton"

> **原句 3:** His eyes glittered with joy and madness. "… and the retribution won't end there. Worse things are to come. They will lose more of their loved ones…" He gave a sly grin. "And Binfire. He will be the worst affected. He killed Jack Braxton. He confessed to me, in the back of his van, when he was concussed. He said Jack Braxton deserved to die." Kit felt a stone plunge into the bottom of her stomach. "He'll lose everything now. Watch yourself. Watch out, Kit. Watch out for yourself. It's coming. Retribution is coming…" Kit almost ran back into the house.

**中文理解**：他的眼睛闪烁着喜悦和疯狂的光芒。"……而且报应不会就此结束。更糟的事情还会发生。他们会失去更多他们所爱的人……"他露出狡猾的笑容。"还有 Binfire。他将受到最严重的影响。他杀了 Jack Braxton。他在他的货车后面向我承认了，当时他脑震荡。他说 Jack Braxton 该死。"Kit 感到一块石头坠入胃底。"他现在会失去一切。小心。小心，Kit。小心你自己。它来了。报应来了……"Kit 几乎跑回房子。

**关键词** | glittered with joy and madness / sly grin / stone plunge / deserved to die

**为什么这样写**：Bell 的"glittered with joy and madness"（闪烁着喜悦和疯狂）暗示了他的"疯狂"——他从"别人的痛苦"中获得"快乐"。"stone plunge"（石头坠入）这个比喻暗示了 Kit 的"恐惧"——她"知道"了"真相"。"deserved to die"（该死）这个短语暗示了 Binfire 的"道德模糊"——他认为 Jack"该死"。
