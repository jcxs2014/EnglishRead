# 10. Chapter 10

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：晚宴继续，Goldingay 通过机器人雇佣 Kit 调查人偶破坏案，Kit 勉强接受；Tabitha 的交换提议被接受。

**情感弧线位置**：兴奋与犹豫——Kit 对侦探工作的渴望 vs 她对 Goldingay 的不信任，形成"机会与风险并存"的张力。

**叙事手法**：群戏聚焦（Goldingay 的提议 → Kit 的拒绝 → Binfire 的劝说 → Kit 的勉强接受）；单 POV（Kit）。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Goldingay 的提议

> **原句 1:** "Kit Pelham," he said. "You are here. I thought you might be here." "Hello, Graham." "I am glad to see you here." "Really?" "I have a proposal for you. I wish to employ you in your capacity as a detective. I want you to investigate the destruction of my action figure. I will pay you two hundred pounds a day, plus expenses."

**中文理解**："Kit Pelham，"他说。"你在这里。我猜你可能会在。""你好，Graham。""我很高兴在这里见到你。""真的吗？""我有个提议。我想以侦探的身份雇佣你。我要你调查我的人偶被破坏一案。我每天付你两百英镑，外加费用。"

**关键词**：in your capacity as a detective / investigate the destruction / two hundred pounds a day

**为什么这样写**：Goldingay 的"雇佣侦探"提议是全书的转折点——Kit 从"被动卷入"变为"主动调查"。"two hundred pounds a day"的报价既显示了他的财力，也暗示了案件的严重性。

---

### 第 2 段：Kit 的拒绝

> **原句 2:** "What? No chance." "You cannot say no," he grated. "No one says no to me." "Come on, pilgrim." Binfire was holding his arms out imploringly, clasping his hands together in prayer. "We've got to do this! It's detective work! You and me, doing our thing!" "Fine," sighed Kit. "I'll think about it."

**中文理解**："什么？没门。""你不能拒绝，"他咬牙切齿地说。"没人敢拒绝我。""拜托，朝圣者。"Binfire 伸出双臂恳求，双手合十祈祷。"我们必须做这是！这是侦探工作！你和我，做我们的事！""好吧，"Kit 叹了口气。"我考虑一下。"

**关键词**：No chance / grated / No one says no to me / imploringly / I'll think about it

**为什么这样写**：Goldingay 的"No one says no to me"是典型的自恋型人格宣言。Binfire 的"imploringly"（恳求地）和"doing our thing"形成对比——一个强势，一个软磨硬泡。Kit 的"Fine... I'll think about it"是典型的 Kit 式妥协——表面犹豫，内心已经决定。

---

### 第 3 段：Goldingay 的接受

> **原句 3:** Eventually, he said, "Nothing can replace my Arkadia figure. Nothing! But Lady Tabitha has the Byzantia action figure in her possession. As Byzantia is the twin sister of Arkadia, and thus those figures are identical save for a slightly different-coloured cloak, I find the substitution acceptable. I will accept the offer."

**中文理解**：最终，他说："什么都无法替代我的 Arkadia 人偶。什么都无法替代！但 Lady Tabitha 拥有 Byzantia 人偶。由于 Byzantia 是 Arkadia 的双胞胎姐妹，因此这些人偶除了斗篷颜色略有不同外完全相同，我认为这个替代方案可以接受。我接受这个提议。"

**关键词**：Nothing can replace / twin sister / identical save for / substitution acceptable

**为什么这样写**：Goldingay 的"Nothing can replace... Nothing!"的排比强调了他的执念。"identical save for a slightly different-coloured cloak"的精确描述暗示了这些人偶的稀有性——只有专家才能分辨。"substitution acceptable"的正式用语暗示了这是一笔"商业交易"而非情感妥协。
