# 64. Chapter 64

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Kit 在凶手实验室中醒来，看到被肢解的尸体部件，用气球和氦气逃脱。

**情感弧线位置**：恐怖与求生——Kit 的"blister pack"（吸塑包装）和"cauterised"（烧灼止血）暗示了"恐怖"，她的"Tesseract beam"（Tesseract 光束）则暗示了"希望"。

**叙事手法**：觉醒+逃跑场景（实验室→停车场）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"bonelessly"（无力地）和"furnace of brilliance"（光芒的熔炉）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Kit 的"blister pack"

> **原句 1:** Kit woke again, swimming towards consciousness. She could feel leather straps holding her in place. The buckles were fastened as tight as they would go, but they weren't pressing on her wrists and she found she could slip out of them easily. She stretched her arms, rubbed her wrists and gave silent thanks to her extreme dieting habits. Now all she had to do was to unfasten her legs. She unbuckled the right one, just the right, and she was suddenly free. She collapsed bonelessly onto the floor. I wasn't expecting that. She pulled herself up, but something was wrong. She plunged back onto the floor. It's the anaesthetic. It must be the anaesthetic in my system. She lay there, her cheek pressed against the cool floor, extremely tempted to go back to sleep. But no. She had to leave. Slowly and clumsily, she crept from under the table, pulling herself up and leaning heavily on the sheet of plastic – and came face to face with a nightmare. She gasped in horror. On the far wall was a piece of moulded plastic in the shape of a person, the kind of blister pack used to keep action figures held in place. Pushed inside the plastic form were body parts. Arms, legs and a torso. The only piece missing, Kit noted with a shudder, was a head. She moved a little closer. There was no blood; each chunk of person had been neatly severed, cauterised and covered in a stretchy fabric that approximated the costume of a Vixens from the Void character. This is not just a pile of limbs stacked in the charnel house of a butcher, she thought. This is a sculpture lovingly erected in the home of an utter maniac. The limbs had obviously come from Moni, Saskia and Penny. The torso? Was that really Lady Tabitha? The area around the monstrosity was shiny, smooth metal. She could see her reflection in the mirror. Her make-up was smashed across her face. Her hair was a tousled mess. She recoiled from her reflection like Frankenstein's monster. Then she saw what was on her neck. A dotted line drawn in marker pen. Cut here.

**中文理解**：Kit 再次醒来，游向意识。她能感觉到皮革带子把她固定在原地。带扣扣得尽可能紧，但它们没有压在她的手腕上，她发现她可以轻松地滑出来。她伸开手臂，揉了揉手腕，默默感谢她的极端节食习惯。现在她所要做的就是解开她的腿。她解开了右边的，只是右边的，她突然自由了。她无力地倒在地板上。我没想到会这样。她把自己拉起来，但有些不对劲。她再次倒在地板上。是麻醉剂。一定是我体内的麻醉剂。她躺在那里，脸颊压在冰冷的地板上，非常想回去睡觉。但不。她必须离开。她缓慢而笨拙地从桌子下面爬出来，把自己拉起来，重重地靠在塑料布上——然后面对一个噩梦。她惊恐地喘息。在远处墙上有一块人形塑料，那种用来固定人偶的吸塑包装。推进塑料模型的是身体部件。手臂、腿和躯干。Kit 颤抖着注意到，唯一缺少的部分是一个头。她走近了一点。没有血；每块人体部位都被整齐地切断，烧灼止血，并覆盖着一种近似 Vixens from the Void 角色服装的弹性织物。她想，这不仅仅是屠夫停尸房里堆叠的四肢。这是一个彻头彻尾的疯子家里精心竖立的雕塑。这些四肢显然来自 Moni、Saskia 和 Penny。躯干？那真的是 Lady Tabitha 吗？怪物周围区域是闪亮的、光滑的金属。她能在镜子里看到她的倒影。她的化妆品在她脸上砸得稀烂。她的头发乱成一团。她像弗兰肯斯坦的怪物一样从自己的倒影中退缩。然后她看到她脖子上有什么。用记号笔画的一条虚线。从这里剪开。

**关键词** | bonelessly / anaesthetic / blister pack / cauterised / charnel house / sculpture / utter maniac / monstrosity / tousled / recoiled / Frankenstein's monster / dotted line

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"blister pack"（吸塑包装）和"cauterised"（烧灼止血）暗示了"恐怖"——凶手"像处理人偶一样"处理受害者。"charnel house"（停尸房）和"utter maniac"（彻头彻尾的疯子）暗示了凶手的"疯狂"——他把"尸体"当作"艺术品"。"Frankenstein's monster"（弗兰肯斯坦的怪物）这个比喻暗示了 Kit 的"自我认知"——她"看起来"像"怪物"。"dotted line"（虚线）和"Cut here"（从这里剪开）暗示了凶手的"残忍"——他"标记"了 Kit。

### 第 2 段：Kit 的"Tesseract beam"

> **原句 2:** In the middle of the gloom there was a golden thread of daylight stretching up from floor to ceiling, like a Tesseract beam (Avengers Assemble, 2012). She rolled herself along the floor towards it, over and over until her stomach lurched, until she came into contact with the wall with a dull clang. Her hands felt like they belonged to someone else, and she mushed them against the light. It felt as though she was trying to crack open a safe by throwing sausages at it, but eventually she realised it was a door, a heavy sliding square of metal, and her efforts were slowly nudging it open, widening the shaft of daylight until it became a glaring furnace of brilliance. She rolled out into the sunshine and sprawled into a car park. Then there were voices. "Are you alright?" "Is she drunk?" Her eyes tried to open, but saw there were lots of people, black silhouettes against the sky. She wanted to tell them to go away. Leave her alone. Normally, she wouldn't have the strength to say anything, but the drugs in her system emboldened her. "G'wuugh." "She's definitely drunk," said a woman. "What was she doing in the warehouse, do you think?" said a man. "Sleeping it off, most likely," said the woman. "Where's her leg gone?" Another voice further away was shouting. "Holy shit! Don't look in there! Shit! It's the Braxtons Butcher!" "Don't you mean the Doll Dismemberer?" "Whatever! Someone call the police!" And then there was an ear-piercing scream.

**中文理解**：在黑暗中间，有一缕金色的光线从地板延伸到天花板，像一道 Tesseract 光束（《复仇者联盟》，2012）。她沿着地面向它滚去，一遍又一遍，直到她的胃翻腾，直到她碰到墙壁发出沉闷的叮当声。她的手感觉像是属于别人，她把她们怼在感觉上。感觉就像她在试图通过向它扔香肠来撬开一个保险箱，但最终她意识到那是一扇门，一块沉重的滑动金属方块，她的努力正在慢慢把它推开，扩大日光的轴，直到它变成一个刺眼的光芒熔炉。她滚到阳光下，瘫倒在停车场。然后有声音。"你还好吗？""她醉了吗？"她的眼睛试图睁开，但看到有很多人，天空下的黑色剪影。她想告诉他们走开。别管我。通常，她没有力气说任何话，但她体内的药物给了她勇气。"咕咕。""她肯定醉了，"一个女人说。"你认为她在仓库里做什么？"一个男人说。"很可能在醒酒，"女人说。"她的腿去哪了？"远处另一个声音在喊。"天哪！别往里面看！该死！是 Braxtons 屠夫！""你不是说 Doll 肢解者吗？""管它呢！有人报警！"然后传来一声刺耳的尖叫。

**关键词** | Tesseract beam / mushed / sausages / furnace of brilliance / sprawled / emboldened / g'wuugh / sleeping it off / Braxtons Butcher / Doll Dismemberer / ear-piercing scream

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"Tesseract beam"（Tesseract 光束）暗示了"希望"——她"看到"了"光"。"sausages"（香肠）这个比喻暗示了 Kit 的"幽默"——她"试图"用"手"撬门。"furnace of brilliance"（光芒的熔炉）暗示了"自由"——她"逃出"了。"Braxtons Butcher"（Braxtons 屠夫）和"Doll Dismemberer"（Doll 肢解者）暗示了媒体的"嗜血"——他们"起"了"绰号"。
