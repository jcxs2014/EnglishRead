# 54. Chapter 54

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Kit 和 Penny 抵达 Braxton Hall，警察正在挖掘 Jack Braxton 的坟墓；他们用警用胶带伪装机器人，混入警察队伍。

**情感弧线位置**：紧张与冒险——Bell 的"digging up his grave"（挖他的坟墓）和"Ben Gunn"（本·冈恩）暗示了"疯狂"，Kit 的"disguise ourselves"（伪装自己）则暗示了她的"冒险"精神。

**叙事手法**：调查场景（Braxton Hall）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"sepulchral voice"（阴沉的声音）和"Belts and braces"（万无一失）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Jack Braxton 的坟墓

> **原句 1:** "They're digging up Jack Braxton's grave," came a sepulchral voice. Penny and Kit both screamed in shock. Reverend Bell was right outside Kit's passenger window, leering in at them with a mad glint in his eyes. Penny slammed her hands against the steering wheel, gasping for air. "FUCK! Reverend! You scared the shit out of me!" Kit wound down her window. "Digging up his grave? Why are they doing that?" Reverend Bell pouted. "I don't know. How should I know? I came over here to tell the family I know who murdered Jack Braxton. I thought if I assembled them all in a drawing room, I could recount the events of that night and reveal the murderer to be Ben 'Binfire' Ferry. I'm sure they'd appreciate that. Perhaps that would bring closure and break the curse."

**中文理解**："他们在挖 Jack Braxton 的坟墓，"一个阴沉的声音传来。Penny 和 Kit 都惊恐地尖叫起来。Reverend Bell 就在 Kit 的副驾驶窗外，用疯狂的眼神盯着她们。Penny 猛拍方向盘，喘着气。"该死！牧师！你吓死我了！"Kit 摇下车窗。"挖他的坟墓？他们为什么这样做？"Reverend Bell 撅嘴。"我不知道。我怎么知道？我来这里是为了告诉家人我知道谁杀了 Jack Braxton。我想如果我把他们召集在客厅里，我可以讲述那天晚上的事件，揭示凶手是 Ben 'Binfire' Ferry。我相信他们会感激的。也许这会带来了结并打破诅咒。"

**关键词** | sepulchral voice / leering / mad glint / recount / closure / break the curse

**为什么这样写**：Bell 的"sepulchral voice"（阴沉的声音）暗示了他的"疯狂"——他看起来像"鬼"。"leering"（斜视）这个动词暗示了 Bell 的"威胁"——他在"盯着"她们。"closure"（了结）和"break the curse"（打破诅咒）暗示了 Bell 的"执念"——他想"结束"这一切。

### 第 2 段：Bell 的"Ben Gunn"

> **原句 2:** Reverend Bell looked like he'd been sleeping under a hedge. His grey coat was smeared with grass stains and there were things living in his beard and moustache. He reminded Kit of Ben Gunn in the Robert Newton version of Treasure Island (1950). He looked just like the kind of mad conspiracy theorist that came out of the woodwork when there was a major murder investigation. It wasn't a surprise he'd been ignored. Thank heavens.

**中文理解**：Reverend Bell 看起来像一直睡在树篱下。他的灰色外套沾满了胡子上和胡子里有东西活着。他让 Kit 想起了罗伯特·牛顿版《金银岛》（1950）中的本·冈恩。他看起来就像一个疯狂的阴谋论者，每当有重大谋杀调查时就会出现。他被忽视并不奇怪。谢天谢地。

**关键词** | sleeping under a hedge / grass stains / Ben Gunn / Treasure Island / conspiracy theorist / out of the woodwork

**为什么这样写**：Bell 的"sleeping under a hedge"（睡在树篱下）暗示了他的"落魄"——他"无家可归"。"Ben Gunn"（本·冈恩）和"Treasure Island"（《金银岛》）暗示了 Bell 的"疯狂"——他像"野人"。"conspiracy theorist"（阴谋论者）和"out of the woodwork"（从木制品中出来）暗示了 Bell 的"不可信"——他是"疯子"。

### 第 3 段：Kit 的"disguise"

> **原句 3:** Kit fell silent. The only sound was Reverend Bell's tuneless humming. Finally, she said, "No. I can't waltz in. But perhaps we can disguise ourselves as part of the investigating team." "We hardly look like coppers, Kit." "No, we don't. But we don't have to." She turned to Reverend Bell. "Could you steal a few metres of that POLICE – DO NOT CROSS tape for us?" Bell gave a loopy grin. "Simplicity itself, my lady."

**中文理解**：Kit 沉默了。唯一的声音是 Reverend Bell 的走调哼唱。最后，她说，"不。我不能踱步进去。但也许我们可以伪装成调查团队的一部分。""我们看起来不像警察，Kit。""不，我们不像。但我们不必。"她转向 Reverend Bell。"你能为我们偷几米那个警察——禁止通行的胶带吗？"Bell 露出傻傻的笑容。"简单得很，夫人。"

**关键词** | waltz in / disguise / coppers / loopy grin / Simplicity itself

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"waltz in"（踱步进去）暗示了她的"谨慎"——她不能"直接"进去。"disguise"（伪装）这个动词暗示了 Kit 的"聪明"——她想"混入"。"coppers"（警察）这个俚语暗示了 Kit 的"非正式"——她"不是"警察。"loopy grin"（傻傻的笑容）和"Simplicity itself"（简单得很）暗示了 Bell 的"疯狂"——他"愿意"帮忙。

### 第 4 段：PCs Howard 和 Kelly 的"Belts and braces"

> **原句 4:** PC Howard and PC Kelly were standing by the big tent erected around Jack Braxton's grave. They were on their fourteenth round of I spy when the robot chugged around the side of the house and lurched towards them. "Not seen one of those before," said Howard, scratching his sideburns. "Haven't you?" Kelly grinned. "Well, on the telly, obviously. Not in real life." "Right." "There was one in last night's episode of Wistful on ITV. Terrorist bomb blew it to pieces." Howard watched as it chugged past, noting the POLICE – DO NOT CROSS ribbons threaded across its carapace. "I didn't know we were getting one of those things." "Why should the SOCOs tell us anything? We're just the doormen." "You don't think the coffin's dangerous, do you? You don't think they put a bomb in it?" "Belts and braces, Mike. Belts and braces. We're dealing with a grade-one psycho. No telling what he's capable of." "Yeah." They watched as the robot disappeared inside the tent.

**中文理解**：PC Howard 和 PC Kelly 站在为 Jack Braxton 坟墓搭起的大帐篷旁。他们在玩第十四轮"我发现"时，机器人绕过房子一侧，向他们驶来。"以前没见过那种东西，"Howard 说，挠着他的连鬓胡子。""你没见过？"Kelly 笑了。"嗯，在电视上，显然。不是在生活中。""对。""昨晚 ITV 的《Wistful》剧集里有一个。恐怖分子炸弹把它炸成了碎片。"Howard 看着它驶过，注意到警察——禁止通行的 ribbons 穿过它的甲壳。""我不知道我们要来那种东西。""为什么 SOCO 要告诉我们什么？我们只是门卫。""你不认为棺材危险吗？你不认为他们在里面放了炸弹吗？""万无一失，迈克。万无一失。我们面对的是一个一级精神病。不知道他能做什么。""是的。"他们看着机器人消失在帐篷里。

**关键词** | I spy / sideburns / Wistful / carapace / SOCOs / doormen / Belts and braces / grade-one psycho

**为什么这样写**：PCs Howard 和 Kelly 的"I spy"（我发现）暗示了他们的"无聊"——他们在"玩游戏"。"Wistful"（《Wistful》）这个电视剧暗示了他们的"文化"——他们"看"电视。"SOCOs"（犯罪现场调查员）这个缩写暗示了"专业"分工——"我们只是门卫"。"Belts and braces"（万无一失）这个成语暗示了他们的"谨慎"——他们"准备""充分"。
