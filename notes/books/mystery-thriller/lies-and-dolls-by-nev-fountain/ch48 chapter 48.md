# 48. Chapter 48

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Tabitha 被绑架的照片发来，Archie 认为是"another stunt"（另一个噱头）；Kit 承诺留下直到 Tabitha 证明清白，Archie 提供免费住宿。

**情感弧线位置**：不安与决心——Kit 的"No"（不）和"sooo romantic"（太浪漫了）暗示了她的"决心"，Archie 的"fool's errand"（徒劳的使命）则暗示了他的"怀疑"。

**叙事手法**：清晨场景（庭院）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"monogrammed dressing gown"（绣有字母的浴袍）和"indebted to you"（欠你人情）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Tabitha 的绑架照片

> **原句 1:** "Come on, pilgrim, wake up!" Kit struggled into her Furley House monogrammed dressing gown and opened the door. Binfire and Freya were there, wearing identical dressing gowns. "Sorry to wake you," Freya trilled helplessly. "But…" "But I just got a WhatsApp message," Binfire said grimly. "You'd better look at it, Kit." Kit. He never calls me Kit. It's always pilgrim or Captain Kit, or something like that. Has Reverend Bell gone to the police? Is Binfire getting arrested? He held up his phone. The photo was of an unconscious woman in a steampunk space highwayman costume. She'd been gagged and tied up. Her knees had been pulled up to her chin as she had been stuffed inside a cramped space; from what little Kit could see it was probably the boot of a car. And there was a rhyme. Five little figures / Knocking on my door. / One knocked herself cold / And then there were four.

**中文理解**："起来，朝圣者，醒来！"Kit 挣扎着穿上她的 Furley House 绣有字母的浴袍，打开门。Binfire 和 Freya 穿着相同的浴袍。"抱歉吵醒你，"Freya 无助地尖声说。"但是……""但我刚收到一条 WhatsApp 消息，"Binfire 严肃地说。"你最好看看，Kit。"Kit。他从不叫我 Kit。总是朝圣者或 Kit 船长之类的。Reverend Bell 去警察局了吗？Binfire 要被逮捕了吗？他举起手机。照片是一个穿着蒸汽朋克太空公路大盗服装的失去知觉的女人。她被堵住嘴绑了起来。她的膝盖被拉到下巴，因为她被塞进了一个狭窄的空间；从 Kit 能看到的那点来看，可能是汽车后备箱。还有一首打油诗。五个小人偶 / 敲我的门。 / 一个敲晕了自己 / 然后还剩四个。

**关键词** | monogrammed dressing gown / identical / Kit (not pilgrim) / gagged / cramped / boot of a car

**为什么这样写**：Binfire 的"Kit"（不是朝圣者）暗示了"严肃"——他"不是"在"开玩笑"。"monogrammed dressing gown"（绣有字母的浴袍）暗示了 Furley House 的"奢华"——连"浴袍"都"定制"。"gagged"（堵住嘴）和"cramped"（狭窄的）暗示了 Tabitha 的"危险"——她被"绑架"了。

### 第 2 段：Archie 的"another stunt"

> **原句 2:** "Shouldn't we phone the police or something?" said Freya. "I don't think so, my dear." Archie sounded grim. "I'm sorry, but this photo is obviously posed. My dear sister is playing silly buggers again." Kit frowned. "Are you sure?" "How can it possibly be anything else? Thanks to you we know for a fact that she was behind it all. Ergo, she was the one sending the photos and the ridiculous rhymes. No, this is yet another stunt to get attention and remind us how she fooled us all good and proper. I'm sure this is not sufficient to concern the police, certainly not after the last few times we've troubled them. I doubt they'd take this as anything other than a waste of time."

**中文理解**："我们不应该打电话报警什么的吗？"Freya 说。"我不认为，亲爱的。"Archie 听起来很严肃。"我很抱歉，但这张照片显然是摆拍的。我亲爱的妹妹又在玩愚蠢的把戏了。"Kit 皱眉。"你确定？""还能是什么？多亏了你，我们知道她是这一切的幕后黑手。因此，她就是发送照片和荒谬打油诗的人。不，这是另一个噱头来获得注意力并提醒我们她是如何好好愚弄我们的。我确信这不足以让警察担心，特别是在我们上次打扰他们之后。我怀疑他们不会把这当作浪费时间。"

**关键词** | posed / playing silly buggers / Ergo / stunt / good and proper

**为什么这样写**：Archie 的"playing silly buggers"（玩愚蠢的把戏）暗示了他的"愤怒"——他"不再"相信 Tabitha。"Ergo"（因此）这个拉丁语暗示了 Archie 的"逻辑"——他用"推理"来"证明"。"good and proper"（好好）这个副词暗示了 Archie 的"愤怒"——他被"好好"愚弄了。

### 第 3 段：Kit 的"No"

> **原句 3:** "Yes," said Penny. "Yes," said Binfire. "Yes," said Freya. "No," said Kit. They all looked at her. She felt very uncomfortable. "I'm s-sorry. But… Tabitha phoned me this evening before I went to bed." Penny looked shocked. "What?" "Really." Archie's expression was inscrutable. "She said she was innocent." Archie raised his eyebrow. "I know, I know, weight of evidence and all that. But she was adamant and she wanted me to believe her. Anyway, she made me promise that I wouldn't leave until she cleared her name." "Woah," said Binfire. Freya sighed. "That's sooo romantic." "So, I'm staying for a bit longer. You two don't have to stay here. You can go back to Brighton. I made the promise, not you." "All for one and one for all, pilgrim." "As I just said, that's just sooo romantic. I'm definitely staying." "Fair enough." She turned to Archie. "If you know of any good B&Bs around here, then can you point us in the right direction?" Archie shook his head, smiling. "Alas, I think you're on a fool's errand. If you're committed to staying until my big sister proves her innocence, I fear you'll be here when Furley House crumbles to dust. But, if you wish to stay for her sake, I wouldn't dream of letting you stay anywhere else but here as long as you like, gratis. And that goes for any time you wish to come to Furley House. As I say, I am indebted to you and your friends."

**中文理解**："是的，"Penny 说。"是的，"Binfire 说。"是的，"Freya 说。"不，"Kit 说。他们都看着她。她感到非常不舒服。"我很抱歉。但是……Tabitha 今晚在我睡觉前给我打了电话。"Penny 看起来很震惊。"什么？""真的。"Archie 的表情难以捉摸。"她说她是无辜的。"Archie 扬起眉毛。"我知道，我知道，证据确凿之类的。但她很坚决，她想让我相信她。无论如何，她让我承诺在她洗清名字之前我不会离开。""哇，"Binfire 说。Freya 叹了口气。"太浪漫了。""所以，我会再待一阵子。你们两个不必留在这里。你们可以回 Brighton。我做的承诺，不是你们。""人人为我，我为人人，朝圣者。""正如我刚才说的，那太浪漫了。我肯定留下。""好吧。"她转向 Archies。"如果你知道这附近有什么好的 B&B，你能给我们指个方向吗？"Archie 摇头，微笑。"唉，我认为你在做徒劳的使命。如果你致力于留下直到我姐姐证明她的清白，我担心当 Furley House 化为尘土时你还在这里。但是，如果你愿意为她留下，我不会梦想让你留在其他地方，只要你喜欢，免费。这适用于你想来 Furley House 的任何时候。正如我所说，我欠你和你朋友的人情。"

**关键词** | inscrutable / adamant / fool's errand / crumbles to dust / gratis / indebted

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"No"（不）暗示了她的"决心"——她"选择"留下。"fool's errand"（徒劳的使命）这个成语暗示了 Archie 的"怀疑"——他"不认为" Tabitha 能"证明"。"gratis"（免费）和"indebted"（欠人情）暗示了 Archie的"感激"——他"感谢" Kit。
