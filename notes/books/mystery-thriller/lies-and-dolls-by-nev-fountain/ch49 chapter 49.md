# 49. Chapter 49

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：闪回——Saskia 回忆起"Babes in Toyland"恶作剧的起源和与 Matty 的分手；Kit 在镇上被戴口罩的人跟踪。

**情感弧线位置**：回忆与恐惧——Saskia 的"prank"（恶作剧）和"accomplice"（同谋）暗示了她的"内疚"，Kit 的"protective COVID mask"（防护口罩）则暗示了"危险"。

**叙事手法**：闪回+现实场景（Airbnb→镇上）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"Rorschach blobs"（罗夏墨迹）和"pungent stench"（刺鼻气味）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Saskia 的"prank"

> **原句 1:** It was only meant to be a prank! That's what Lady Tabitha had said! As soon as she'd surveyed the wrecked toy museum that first night, she knew Lady Tabitha was taking things too far. She should have said something right then, pointed the finger, but, hey, it was Lady Tabitha's family home. She was entitled to do a bit of self-vandalism, right? But she'd said nothing, and she'd been happy to train her camera phone on the watercooler, so Tabitha knew when to set off the explosives. And, after that, she'd been a willing accomplice after the fact. No going back. And the worst thing? It had given Matty the moral high ground.

**中文理解**：这只是一个恶作剧！Lady Tabitha 是这么说的！当她第一晚查看被毁坏的玩具博物馆时，她知道 Lady Tabitha 做得太过分了。她本应该说点什么，指指点点，但嘿，那是 Lady Tabitha 的家。她有权进行一些自我破坏，对吧？但她什么也没说，她很高兴地把摄像机对准饮水机，这样 Tabitha 就知道什么时候引爆炸药。从那以后，她成了一个事后自愿的同谋。无法回头。最糟糕的是？这给了 Matty 道德高地。

**关键词** | prank / taking things too far / self-vandalism / accomplice / moral high ground

**为什么这样写**：Saskia 的"prank"（恶作剧）暗示了她的"初衷"——她"不是"故意的。"accomplice"（同谋）这个法律术语暗示了 Saskia 的"罪责"——她"参与"了。"moral high ground"（道德高地）这个成语暗示了 Matty的"优势"——他"站在""正义"一边。

### 第 2 段：Saskia 与 Matty 的分手

> **原句 2:** "So, this is it, yeah? You cook up this plan to humiliate me, destroy my property…" "It wasn't my plan—" Matty had shouted over her. "You cook up this plan to destroy my property… And now you've had your fun, you just dump me in the middle of nowhere? Wham, bam, thank you, mister?" "There's a train to London in fifteen minutes. I can see the platform sign from here." "You dump me on a train, can't even drive me home, after all that you've done?" "I don't want to be in this car with you for two hours. You get that, don't you?" "Oh, I get that. Guilt is a terrible thing." "Get out."

**中文理解**："所以，就是这样，对吧？你策划了这个计划来羞辱我，摧毁我的财产……""这不是我的计划——"Matty 对她大喊。"你策划了这个计划来摧毁我的财产……现在你玩够了，就把我扔在荒郊野外？嘭，嘭，谢谢你先生？""十五分钟后有去伦敦的火车。我能从这里看到站台标志。""你把我扔在火车上，甚至不能开车送我回家，在你做了这一切之后？""我不想和你在车里待两个小时。你明白吗？""哦，我明白。内疚是件可怕的事。""出去。"

**关键词** | Wham, bam, thank you, mister / dump me / guilt

**为什么这样写**：Matty 的"Wham, bam, thank you, mister"（嘭，嘭，谢谢你先生）暗示了他的"愤怒"——他"感觉"被"利用"了。"dump me"（扔了我）这个动词暗示了 Matty 的"委屈"——他"被抛弃"了。

### 第 3 段：Kit 的"stalker"

> **原句 3:** She stared in the windows of an art gallery that specialised in local artists. She looked at her shadowy reflection, peeking between the insipid watercolours – and she saw another face, superimposed on her own. Staring at her. A face wearing a protective COVID mask. She turned. There was no one behind her. She looked around. The street was very quiet, as were most high streets these days. Just a few buskers howling into the abyss, and a multi-coloured stall selling artisanal cheeses. Who was it? Lady Tabitha of course. Archie's right. That photo was staged. Lady Tabitha is playing silly buggers. She's using me and abusing my trust. Now she's stalking me, so she can laugh at me. Revel in my confusion.

**中文理解**：她凝视着一家专门从事当地艺术家作品的画廊的窗户。她看着自己朦胧的倒影，在平淡的水彩画之间偷看——她看到了另一张脸，叠加在她的上面。盯着她。一张戴着防护口罩的脸。她转过身。她身后没有人。她环顾四周。街道非常安静，就像现在大多数商业街一样。只有几个街头艺人对着深渊嚎叫，还有一个五彩缤纷的摊位出售手工奶酪。是谁？当然是 Lady Tabitha。Archie 说得对。那张照片是摆拍的。Lady Tabitha 在玩愚蠢的把戏。她在利用我，滥用我的信任。现在她在跟踪我，这样她就可以嘲笑我。在我的困惑中狂欢。

**关键词** | insipid / superimposed / protective COVID mask / buskers / howling into the abyss / artisanal / staged / revel in my confusion

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"insipid"（平淡的）暗示了她对"艺术"的"不屑"——她"不欣赏"当地艺术。"superimposed"（叠加的）这个动词暗示了"超现实"——两张脸"重合"了。"howling into the abyss"（对着深渊嚎叫）这个夸张说法暗示了 Kit 的"文化批判"——街头艺人在"制造噪音"。

### 第 4 段：Lush 的"pungent stench"

> **原句 4:** Kit dodged into a doorway and peered around the corner. The watcher walked further down the high street and Kit pressed herself further back into the doorway. Then she became aware of a sickly, pungent stench in her nostrils. And she started to gag. "What are you doing?" There was someone behind her. She jumped. It was Binfire. "Why are you skulking in the doorway of Lush, pilgrim?" Kit realised that she was indeed standing outside a branch of Lush, and the smell was the choking aroma of ten thousand smelly soaps. She'd often wondered how the staff coped without gas marks.

**中文理解**：Kit 闪进门角，从拐角处往外看。跟踪者沿着商业街走得更远，Kit 把自己进一步压进门角。然后她意识到鼻孔里有一股刺鼻的气味。她开始作呕。"你在做什么？"她身后有人。她跳了起来。是 Binfire。"你为什么躲在 Lush 的门口，朝圣者？"Kit 意识到她确实站在 Lush 分店外面，气味是万块香皂的窒息香气。她经常想知道没有防毒面具的员工是怎么应付的。

**关键词** | pungent stench / gag / skulking / choking aroma / gas marks

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"pungent stench"（刺鼻气味）和"gag"（作呕）暗示了她的"不适"——气味"太强烈"了。"skulking"（躲藏）这个动词暗示了 Kit 的"恐惧"——她在"躲避"。"gas marks"（防毒面具）这个军事用语暗示了 Binfire 的"军事化"思维——他用"战争"语言来"描述"气味。
