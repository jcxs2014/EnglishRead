# 40. Chapter 40

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Tabitha 再次潜入 Kit 的房间，两人讨论 Nate 和明天的会面；Kit 的"uncertainty"（不确定性）暗示了她对 Tabitha 的"期待"。

**情感弧线位置**：紧张与期待——Kit 的"skittering in her chest"（在胸口乱跳）和"stalemate"（僵局）暗示了"欲望"与"准备不足"的冲突。

**叙事手法**：夜间场景（Kit 的房间）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"compartmentalise"（隔离）和"uncertainty"（不确定性）揭示内心。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Kit 的"compartmentalise"

> **原句 1:** Kit lay in her little room that night, duvet tucked under her chin, eyes open, staring at the blackness. She started off being furious about Matty's video, but then she was able to compartmentalise it, thrust it into the back of her mind. That was what she was good at. It was just a stupid piece of nonsense. The only people who would click on it were saddoes like him. Forget about it. But she still wasn't able to sleep. And she knew why. She knew she was waiting for the click of the door, and the soft breath on her ear, and the warmth of Lady Tabitha's body against hers.

**中文理解**：Kit 躺在她的小床上，被子塞到下巴，眼睛睁着，盯着黑暗。她一开始对 Matty 的视频感到愤怒，但随后她能够把它隔离起来，推到脑后。这是她擅长的。这只是一堆愚蠢的废话。唯一会点击它的人是他那样的可怜虫。忘了它。但她仍然无法入睡。她知道为什么。她在等待门的咔嗒声，耳朵上的轻柔呼吸，以及 Lady Tabitha 的身体贴在她身上的温暖。

**关键词** | compartmentalise / thrust it into the back of her mind / saddoes / click of the door

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"compartmentalise"（隔离）暗示了她的"心理防御机制"——她用"隔离"来"处理"情绪。"saddoes"（可怜虫）这个俚语暗示了 Kit 的"不屑"——她"看不起"Matty。"click of the door"（门的咔嗒声）暗示了 Kit 的"期待"——她在"等待"Tabitha。

### 第 2 段：Kit 的"uncertainty"

> **原句 2:** But she couldn't be certain. Uncertainty usually irritated her, but this, this was a new feeling. The tension of not knowing what was going to happen sent her heart skittering in her chest. It reminded her of Milo's paws when he charged across her kitchen floor.

**中文理解**：但她不能确定。不确定性通常让她烦躁，但这是一个新的感觉。不知道将要发生的事情的紧张让她的心在胸口乱跳。这让她想起了 Milo 的爪子在厨房地板上奔跑的声音。

**关键词** | irritated / skittering / Milo's paws

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"skittering"（乱跳）暗示了她的"紧张"——她"期待"但"害怕"。"Milo's paws"（Milo 的爪子）这个比喻暗示了 Kit 的"纯真"——她用"宠物"来描述"心跳"。

---

### 第 3 段：Kit 的"mother figure"

> **原句 3:** She hated herself for it, but she couldn't help comparing the smoothness of Tabitha's skin with Jackie's wrinkly edges. Kit never hid from the facts, and it was a fact that her recently estranged girlfriend had been nearly twenty years older than her, and she was facing up to the truth that maybe – just maybe – she'd got together with Jackie because she was subconsciously seeking a mother figure.

**中文理解**：她讨厌自己这样，但她忍不住把 Tabitha 皮肤的柔滑和 Jackie 的皱纹边缘进行比较。Kit 从不逃避事实，事实是她最近疏远的女朋友比她大近二十岁，她正在面对这个事实——也许——只是也许——她和 Jackie 在一起是因为她在潜意识里寻找一个母亲形象。

**关键词** | wrinkly edges / estranged / subconsciously / mother figure

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"wrinkly edges"（皱纹边缘）暗示了她的"真实"——她在"比较"两个女人。"mother figure"（母亲形象）这个短语暗示了 Kit 的"自我认知"——她"意识到"自己的"依恋模式"。

### 第 4 段：Tabitha 的"bored"

> **原句 4:** "I split up with Nate because he bored me. That's it. That's all. I got bored with him. I was looking for something different." "Am I different?" "I should say so." "Different enough not to get bored with me?" The arm around Kit's waist pulled her tight. "I think so."

**中文理解**："我和 Nate 分手是因为他让我无聊。就这样。仅此而已。我厌倦了他。我在寻找不同的东西。""我不同吗？""我应该说不同。""不同到不会对我感到无聊？"环绕 Kit 腰部的手臂把她拉紧。"我想是的。"

**关键词** | bored me / something different / pulled her tight

**为什么这样写**：Tabitha 的"bored me"（让我无聊）暗示了她的"标准"——她"需要""刺激"。"pulled her tight"（拉紧）这个动作暗示了 Tabitha 的"占有欲"——她"控制"着"亲密"的节奏。

---

### 第 5 段："Stalemate"

> **原句 5:** "Are you sure you don't want to do more than cuddle?" "If you switch the lights on, then maybe." There was a hiss from Tabitha, as she pushed breath through her teeth. Stalemate. "No can do." "Okay." "I'll go." "Okay. Perhaps next time." But Lady Tabitha didn't answer.

**中文理解**："你确定你不想做比拥抱更多的事吗？""如果你开灯，也许。"Tabitha 发出一声嘶嘶声，把气从牙缝中推出。僵局。""不行。""好的。""我走了。""好的。也许下次。"但 Lady Tabitha 没有回答。

**关键词** | cuddle / hiss / Stalemate / No can do

**为什么这样写**："Stalemate"（僵局）这个国际象棋术语暗示了"僵局"——双方都不愿"让步"。"No can do"（不行）这个短语暗示了 Tabitha的"坚持"——她"不会"在"黑暗"中"做爱"。
