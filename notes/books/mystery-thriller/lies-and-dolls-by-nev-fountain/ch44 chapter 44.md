# 44. Chapter 44

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Moni、Ruth、Saskia 和 Penny 被质询——她们承认参与了"Babes in Toyland" WhatsApp 群，但声称只是"玩笑"；Penny 承认偷了 Graham 的人偶。

**情感弧线位置**：冲突与揭露——Moni 的"skid marks"（内裤污渍）和"warm feeling inside"（内心温暖）暗示了她的"恶意"，Penny 的"extremely fired"（极度被解雇）则暗示了她的"愤怒"。

**叙事手法**：对质场景（图书馆）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"read some posts"（读一些帖子）和"baseball bat"（棒球棒）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段："Babes in Toyland"

> **原句 1:** Kit held up the mobile. "As you probably know, we encountered an actor who was employed by our mysterious antagonist. We phoned the number on the actor's phone, and imagine our surprise when Archie answered it." Archie raised his eyebrows. "Imagine my surprise, when I heard the phone ringing in the house. I found it hidden in a pot plant upstairs." "We managed to open it, accessed the WhatsApp and we found a chat group labelled 'Babes in Toyland'."

**中文理解**：Kit 举起手机。"你们可能知道，我们遇到了一个被我们神秘对手雇佣的演员。我们打了演员手机上的号码，想象一下我们的惊讶，当 Archie 接电话时。"Archie 扬起眉毛。"想象一下我的惊讶，当我在房子里听到电话响时。我在楼上的一盆植物里找到了它。""我们设法打开了它，访问了 WhatsApp，我们发现了一个标记为'玩具乐园宝贝'的聊天群。"

**关键词** | Babes in Toyland / pot plant

**为什么这样写**："Babes in Toyland"（玩具乐园宝贝）这个群名暗示了"讽刺"——她们把"犯罪"当作"游戏"。"pot plant"（盆栽植物）这个细节暗示了"隐藏"——手机被"藏"在"植物"里。

### 第 2 段：Moni 的"skid marks"

> **原句 2:** "His pants are not filthy!" said Vixy. "They are. And you should know!" snarled Moni. "It's only the skid marks that hold his filthy pants together!" "Be quiet, both of you!" retorted Archie.

**中文理解**："他的裤子不脏！"Vixy 说。"它们就是。你应该知道！"Moni 咆哮道。"只有污渍把它们粘在一起！""你们两个都安静！"Archie 反驳道。

**关键词** | skid marks / filthy pants

**为什么这样写**：Moni 的"skid marks"（内裤污渍）暗示了她的"粗俗"——她"攻击" Fenton 的"卫生"。"filthy pants"（脏裤子）这个细节暗示了 Moni 的"恶意"——她"恨" Fenton。

### 第 3 段：Penny 的"extremely fired"

> **原句 3:** "Did you help them?" he grated. "Of course I did! Who did you think stole your doll in the first place? Of course I helped her!" "You are fired, Penny Lane." "Do you think I care?" "I want to know why." "Why? Don't you know?" "No." "Of course you don't. It was your Furley Fanboys who helped burn down the Braxtons factory and made my mum lose her job! She never recovered from it. Yes, she got another job, but she loved working there." "The fire was an accident." Penny spat, "As Saskia just said, 'The fuck it was.' It was the same as it ever was: boys with their toys playing stupid games. And it's always the girls that get hurt." "You are extremely fired, Penny Lane." Penny gave a harsh cackle. "Good luck getting your robot home." She flicked a switch on the neck of the robot and it went dark. "I've wanted to do that for ages."

**中文理解**："你帮他们了吗？"他咬牙切齿地说。"当然我帮了！你以为一开始是谁偷了你的人偶？当然我帮了她！""你被解雇了，Penny Lane。""你觉得我在乎吗？""我想知道为什么。""为什么？你不知道吗？""不。"当然你不知道。是你的 Furley Fanboys 帮助烧毁了 Braxtons 工厂，让我妈妈失去了工作！她从未从中恢复过来。是的，她找到了另一份工作，但她热爱在那里工作。""火灾是个意外。"Penny 吐了口唾沫，"就像 Saskia 刚才说的，'去他妈的意外。'和以前一样：男孩们玩着他们的玩具，玩愚蠢的游戏。总是女孩们受伤。""你被极度解雇了，Penny Lane。"Penny 发出一声刺耳的笑。"祝你好运把你的机器人带回家。"她拨动了机器人脖子上的开关，它变暗了。"我想这么做已经很久了。"

**关键词** | extremely fired / harsh cackle / Good luck getting your robot home

**为什么这样写**：Penny 的"extremely fired"（极度被解雇）暗示了她的"愤怒"——她"不在乎"失去工作。"harsh cackle"（刺耳的笑）暗示了 Penny 的"解脱"——她"终于"做到了。"Good luck getting your robot home"（祝你好运把你的机器人带回家）暗示了 Penny 的"报复"——她"摧毁"了 Graham 的"控制"。

### 第 4 段：Archie 的"baseball bat"

> **原句 4:** There was a terrific thud which echoed around the room. Archie had pulled out a baseball bat from the umbrella stand and brought it down on his desk with incredible force, embedding it in the leather top and splintering the mahogany frame. "Be QUIET!" he roared. "I order you all to BE QUIET! I want to know how this escapade transpired, and we will get nowhere by arguing like this!"

**中文理解**：一声巨响在房间里回荡。Archie 从伞架里拿出一根棒球棒，以巨大的力量砸在桌子上，嵌入皮革顶部，劈裂了红木框架。"安静！"他咆哮道。"我命令你们都安静！我想知道这件事是怎么发生的，这样争吵我们不会有任何进展！"

**关键词** | terrific thud / baseball bat / incredible force / embedding / splintering / escapade transpired

**为什么这样写**：Archie 的"baseball bat"（棒球棒）暗示了他的"愤怒"——他"失去"了"绅士"风度。"embedding"（嵌入）和"splintering"（劈裂）这两个动词暗示了 Archie 的"力量"——他"真的"生气了。"escapade transpired"（事情是怎么发生的）这个正式用语暗示了 Archie 的"教养"——即使在"愤怒"中，他也用"礼貌"的语言。
