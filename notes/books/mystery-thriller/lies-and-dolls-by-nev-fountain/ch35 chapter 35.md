# 35. Chapter 35

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Kit 和 Penny 驱车前往 Braxton Hall，Penny 透露 Nate Braxton 是 Tabitha 的前男友，也是 Jack Braxton 的孙子；Kit 怀疑 Nate 是 Roland 的私生子。

**情感弧线位置**：震惊与推理——Kit 的"Spooky huh？"（很诡异吧？）和"Is it my imagination"（是我的想象吗）暗示了她的"侦探直觉"正在"觉醒"。

**叙事手法**：对话场景（车内）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"mind swirling"（思绪纷乱）和"bloomed into a theory"（绽放成理论）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Penny 的"coincidence"

> **原句 1:** "There's no such thing as a coincidence. I mean, there are a lot of things that seem like coincidences but which aren't really coincidences. I mean, take who we're going to see? Nate Braxton, son of Sylvia, nephew of Jack, and grandson of Albert and Vera. Did you know he dated Lady Tabitha until recently?" "No!" "Yes! Spooky huh?"

**中文理解**："没有所谓的巧合。我的意思是，有很多事情看起来像巧合，但并不是真正的巧合。我的意思是，看看我们要去见谁？Nate Braxton，Sylvia 的儿子，Jack 的侄子，Albert 和 Vera 的孙子。你知道他最近一直在和 Lady Tabitha 约会吗？""不知道！""知道！很诡异吧？"

**关键词** | no such thing as a coincidence / Spooky huh

**为什么这样写**：Penny 的"no such thing as a coincidence"（没有所谓的巧合）暗示了她的"阴谋论"思维——她相信一切都是"有联系的"。"Spooky huh？"（很诡异吧？）这个口语暗示了 Penny 的"轻松"——她把"重大发现"当作"八卦"来讲。

---

### 第 2 段：Nate 的身份

> **原句 2:** "Sylvia Braxton and the seventh Marquess stayed in touch after Jack's death. Call it a guilty conscience if you like. Nate and Tabitha knew each other as kids, and they both went to adjoining posh schools. I think Roland Pendragon hoped they'd get married, the old romantic. I mean, she didn't have to marry into the nobility, because she wasn't going to inherit the estate… You've gone quiet," said Penny. "Don't you believe me?"

**中文理解**："Sylvia Braxton 和第七任侯爵在 Jack 死后保持联系。你可以称之为内疚。Nate 和 Tabitha 从小就认识，他们都去了相邻的私立学校。我认为 Roland Pendragon 希望他们结婚，这个老浪漫。我的意思是，她不必嫁入贵族，因为她不会继承遗产……你安静了，"Penny 说。"你不相信我吗？"

**关键词** | guilty conscience / adjoining posh schools / old romantic / inherit the estate

**为什么这样写**：Roland 的"guilty conscience"（内疚）暗示了他的"良心"——他对 Jack Braxton 的死亡感到"内疚"。"adjoining posh schools"（相邻的私立学校）暗示了 Nate 和 Tabitha 的"阶级"——他们都是"上层阶级"的孩子。

---

### 第 3 段：分手视频

> **原句 3:** "Yes, about three months ago. They put out a break-up video on Instagram. It's all the rage with dysfunctional celebrity couples." Her mind still swirling with the implications, Kit took out her phone and went to Instagram. There it was, clearly labelled 'our final message'. It looked like it was in Lady Tabitha's bedroom. Tabitha was sitting in a chair. A man loomed over her, young, handsome, late twenties, with a smooth round face and angry eyebrows. He was wearing a blazer and tie. It looked a bit like a hostage video.

**中文理解**："是的，大约三个月前。他们在 Instagram 上发布了一个分手视频。这在功能失调的名人夫妇中很流行。"Kit 的思绪仍在纷乱中，她拿出手机去了 Instagram。在那里，清楚地标记为"我们的最后信息"。看起来是在 Lady Tabitha 的卧室里。Tabitha 坐在椅子上。一个男人笼罩着她，年轻，英俊，二十多岁，有着光滑的圆脸和愤怒的眉毛。他穿着西装外套和领带。看起来有点像人质视频。

**关键词** | all the rage / dysfunctional / loomed over her / hostage video

**为什么这样写**："all the rage"（很流行）这个短语暗示了"分手视频"的"流行文化"本质——即使是"私人"事件也要"公开"。"hostage video"（人质视频）这个比喻暗示了 Nate 的"威胁性"——他看起来像"绑架者"而不是"男朋友"。

---

### 第 4 段：Kit 的推理

> **原句 4:** Is it my imagination, or does Nate look a bit like Roland Pendragon? There was certainly some similarity, particularly those angry, bushy eyebrows. That round face. Kit's thought was so big it bloomed into a theory. I'm sure Roland Pendragon must have felt pretty guilty over Jack Braxton's death. I bet he visited the Braxton family to give his condolences. Perhaps his roving eye rested on Sylvia Braxton, Jack's sister? Have I found the mysterious bastard son of Roland? The occupant of that room?

**中文理解**：是我的想象吗，还是 Nate 看起来有点像 Roland Pendragon？确实有一些相似之处，尤其是那些愤怒的浓密眉毛。那张圆脸。Kit 的想法如此之大，它绽放成了一个理论。我确信 Roland Pendragon 一定对 Jack Braxton 的死亡感到非常内疚。我打赌他去拜访了 Braxton 家族表达他的哀悼。也许他游移的目光停留在 Sylvia Braxton 身上，Jack 的妹妹？我找到了 Roland 的神秘私生子吗？那个房间的居住者？

**关键词** | bloomed into a theory / condolences / roving eye / bastard son / occupant

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"bloomed into a theory"（绽放成理论）暗示了她的"侦探直觉"——她从"细节"中"推断"出"真相"。"roving eye"（游移的目光）这个成语暗示了 Roland 的"好色"——他"看"上了朋友的妹妹。"bastard son"（私生子）这个短语暗示了 Kit 的"突破"——她找到了"George Jamieson Pendragon"的真实身份。

---

### 第 5 段：Kit 的"brain getting silly"

> **原句 5:** Kit realised her brain was getting silly. It had a habit of throwing absurd and fanciful narratives in the air, because, as the brain of a Cult TV fan, it was stuffed full of absurd and fanciful narratives. It was a fun notion, but it didn't even begin to address the mystery. Why the rack of new clothes? Why the pens with 'George Jamieson Pendragon' on them? "I'm worse than Freya. I might as well blame it all on ghosts." "What?" said Penny. Kit realised she'd muttered her last thought out loud. "Nothing. I was just—"

**中文理解**：Kit 意识到她的脑子变得愚蠢了。它有一个习惯，把荒谬和奇思妙想的故事抛向空中，因为作为一个邪典电视迷的脑袋，它塞满了荒谬和奇思妙想的故事。这是一个有趣的想法，但它甚至没有开始解决这个谜题。为什么有一排新衣服？为什么有写着"George Jamieson Pendragon"的钢笔？"我比 Freya 还糟糕。我不如把一切都归咎于鬼魂。""什么？"Penny 说。Kit 意识到她大声说出了最后的想法。"没什么。我只是——"

**关键词** | absurd and fanciful narratives / Cult TV fan / stuffed full / address the mystery

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"Cult TV fan"（邪典电视迷）自嘲暗示了她的"自我意识"——她知道自己的"推理"可能"太疯狂"。"stuffed full"（塞满）这个动词暗示了 Kit 的"媒体素养"——她的"知识"来自"电视剧"而不是"现实"。
