# 45. Chapter 45

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Tabitha 被指控为幕后主使，她用面部识别解锁的手机作为证据；Tabitha 拒绝认罪，冲破彩色玻璃窗逃跑。

**情感弧线位置**：绝望与反抗——Tabitha 的"I'm being framed"（我被陷害了）和"launched herself through"（冲进）暗示了她的"绝望"——她"选择"了"逃跑"而不是"面对"。

**叙事手法**：逃跑场景（图书馆）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"frozen in Kit's mind"（冻结在 Kit 的脑海中）和"ghost"（鬼魂）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Tabitha 的否认

> **原句 1:** "What's going on?" she said. "Malcolm said you wanted to talk to me." "Ah, here she is!" Matty gave a savage grin. "The dandy highwayman behind the robbery. The mastermind behind it all." "What?" "Don't play dumb, girlie. We found your phone, there's no point denying it." Tabitha looked confused. "What are you talking about? What phone?" "This is your phone," Kit said, holding it up. "The one from which you texted Penny and told her to steal Graham's doll from his collection and mail it to a PO Box." "Woah! Back up! That is NOT my phone! You are not pinning this on me!"

**中文理解**："怎么回事？"她说。"Malcolm 说你想和我谈谈。""啊，她来了！"Matty 露出残忍的笑容。"抢劫背后的花花公子。一切的幕后主使。""什么？""别装傻了，姑娘。我们找到了你的手机，否认没有意义。"Tabitha 看起来很困惑。"你在说什么？什么手机？""这是你的手机，"Kit 说，举起它。"你用它给 Penny 发短信，让她从 Graham 的收藏中偷走人偶并邮寄到 PO 箱。""哇！倒回去！那不是我的手机！你不能把这个栽赃给我！"

**关键词** | dandy highwayman / mastermind / pinning this on me

**为什么这样写**：Matty 的"dandy highwayman"（花花公子）暗示了 Tabitha 的"形象"——她是"公路大盗"。"pinning this on me"（栽赃给我）这个短语暗示了 Tabitha 的"否认"——她"声称"被"陷害"。

### 第 2 段：Kit 的证据

> **原句 2:** "It is your phone," sighed Kit. "I opened it using the facial recognition system. I opened it using the big photo of you hanging in your bedroom." Archie sighed. "Oh, big sis. I can take almost anything from you, but not this…" "I didn't DO anything, Archie!" "The game's up, Tabitha," said Saskia. "They found our WhatsApp messages." "There's no point playing dumb. We've all admitted it," added Ruth. "Now it's your turn." Moni rolled her eyes. "Come on, lady." "I swear. I don't know what you're talking about." "You sent us all WhatsApp messages," said Penny. "Telling me to steal Graham's doll." "I didn't send ANY WhatsApp messages. Can't you see I'm being framed?" "By who? Who would want to frame you?" "Lots of people!" She angrily pointed at nothing in particular. "Nate! Nate Braxton! He's still hurting from when I dumped him! It could be him!"

**中文理解**："这是你的手机，"Kit 叹了口气。"我用面部识别系统打开了它。我用挂在你卧室里的大照片打开了它。"Archie 叹了口气。"哦，大姐。我几乎可以忍受你的一切，除了这个……""我什么都没做，Archie！""游戏结束了，Tabitha，"Saskia 说。"他们找到了我们的 WhatsApp 消息。""装傻没有意义。我们都承认了，"Ruth 说。"现在轮到你了。"Moni 翻白眼。"来吧，女士。""我发誓。我不知道你在说什么。""你给我们所有人发了 WhatsApp 消息，"Penny 说。"让我偷 Graham 的人偶。""我没有发任何 WhatsApp 消息。你们看不出我被陷害了吗？""被谁？谁想陷害你？""很多人！"她愤怒地指着某个地方。"Nate！Nate Braxton！他还在为我和他分手而伤心！可能是他！"

**关键词** | facial recognition / game's up / framed / dumped him

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"facial recognition"（面部识别）暗示了她的"证据"——她用"科学"来"证明"。"framed"（陷害）这个动词暗示了 Tabitha 的"否认"——她"声称"被"栽赃"。"dumped him"（和他分手）这个口语暗示了 Tabitha 的"过去"——她"甩"了 Nate。

### 第 3 段：Tabitha 的逃跑

> **原句 3:** Tabitha started to run, but not away, not to the door. Towards them. They didn't know what she was doing until she sprinted past them, jumping into the air and planting her boots firmly on the library ladder. The momentum sent it skittering along the shelf and, as it glided to the rear of the library, Tabitha was already clambering up the steps and she vaulted herself onto the gallery, landing on her feet like a cat. She elbowed Freya to one side, who slammed into a bookshelf with a cry, then she started running again, arms and legs pumping, boots thudding above their heads and straight towards the arch of the pretty stained-glass window. "No! Please, no, not another window," groaned Archie. Tabitha held her hands in front of her face and launched herself through. The image was frozen in Kit's mind. Tabitha spread her arms wide as she floated up into the night, looking like Adam Ant in his 'Prince Charming' video. No. Not like that. No, floating in the air, in her antiquated highwayman clothing, she looked more like a ghost. And then she was gone.

**中文理解**：Tabitha 开始跑，但不是远离，不是向门口。向他们。他们不知道她在做什么，直到她从他们身边跳过，跳到空中，把靴子牢牢地踩在图书馆梯子上。动量使它沿着书架滑行，当它滑到图书馆后部时，Tabitha 已经在爬上台阶，她跳上画廊，像猫一样用脚着地。她把 Freya 推到一边，Freya 撞到书架上叫了一声，然后她又开始跑，手臂和腿抽动，靴子在他们头顶上砰砰作响，直接冲向漂亮的彩色玻璃窗拱门。"不！求你了，不，不要另一扇窗，"Archie 呻吟道。Tabitha 双手捂住脸，冲了过去。这个画面冻结在 Kit 的脑海中。Tabitha 张开双臂，飘向夜空，看起来像 Adam Ant 在他的《Prince Charming》视频中。不。不是那样。不，在空中漂浮，穿着她过时的公路大盗服装，她看起来更像一个鬼魂。然后她消失了。

**关键词** | momentum / skittering / vaulted / antiquated / frozen in Kit's mind / Adam Ant / ghost

**为什么这样写**：Tabitha 的"momentum"（动量）和"skittering"（滑行）暗示了她的"速度"——她"很快"。"vaulted"（跳跃）这个动词暗示了 Tabitha的"敏捷"——她"像猫一样"。"frozen in Kit's mind"（冻结在 Kit 的脑海中）暗示了 Kit 的"震惊"——她"记住"了这个"画面"。"Adam Ant"（亚当·安特）和"ghost"（鬼魂）的比喻暗示了 Tabitha 的"超现实"——她"看起来"像"鬼魂"。
