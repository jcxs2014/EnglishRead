# 56. Chapter 56

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Graham 和 Penny 和解，新闻揭示 Jack Braxton 可能还活着，警察带走了 Sylvia 和 Vera 问话。

**情感弧线位置**：震惊与释然——Graham 的"duped"（被欺骗）和"twenty-eight years"（二十八年）暗示了他的"愤怒"，Kit 的"common aim"（共同目标）则暗示了"和解"。

**叙事手法**：对话场景（货车内→车内）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"news alert"（新闻提醒）和"creeping feeling of dread"（逐渐蔓延的恐惧感）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Graham 和 Penny 的和解

> **原句 1:** Penny's phone rang. "It's Graham," she said to Kit. "He's finished his lunch and he's now back in his cell. He wants to join our meeting. If that's okay with you." "If that's okay with you," corrected Kit. "He fired you for stealing his action figure and passing it to a maniac." Penny sighed. "Yeah, well, me and Graham, we've talked a bit. We've come to the conclusion that whoever's behind all this is not Lady Tabitha Pendragon, what with her being in little bits now." She grimaced. "I think we both have a common aim to find out who's doing this. Obviously, he wants to find out who broke his doll, and I want to not get murdered and cut into little pieces." She shrugged. "But essentially we're on the same side." "Then that's fine with me," said Kit.

**中文理解**：Penny 的电话响了。"是 Graham，"她对 Kit 说。"他吃完午饭，现在回到牢房了。他想加入我们的会议。如果你没问题的话。""如果你没问题的话，"Kit 纠正道。"他因为你偷了你的人偶并把它交给一个疯子而解雇了你。"Penny 叹了口气。"是的，嗯，我和 Graham，我们谈了谈。我们得出结论，无论这一切的幕后黑手是谁，都不是 Lady Tabitha Pendragon，因为她现在已经变成小块了。"她做了个鬼脸。"我认为我们都有一个共同的目标来找出是谁在做这件事。显然，他想找出是谁弄坏了他的人偶，而我想不被谋杀和切成小块。"她耸了耸肩。"但本质上我们在同一边。""那我没问题，"Kit 说。

**关键词** | maniac / in little bits / common aim / essentially

**为什么这样写**：Penny 的"in little bits"（变成小块）暗示了 Tabitha 的"死亡"——她被"肢解"了。"common aim"（共同目标）这个短语暗示了"和解"——他们"合作"了。"essentially"（本质上）这个副词暗示了 Penny 的"实用主义"——她"不在乎"过去。

### 第 2 段：Graham 的"duped"

> **原句 2:** "It looks like a mannequin," said Penny. "No." Graham shook his huge head. "That isn't just any mannequin." "I agree," said Kit. "It's one of those giant-sized versions of the dolls Braxtons made to promote the first range. There's a bunch of them in the toy museum." Reverend Bell clicked his fingers and let loose a squeal. "Of course! That's right. They made them for a trade fair, didn't they? And they all got loaned out to vendors? Didn't Fenton get one?" "Yes," said Kit. "I remember that one very well. It was outside the door of his shop for ages. I'd have loved to have one in my flat." Graham continued, "They were recalled after the promotion had finished and Braxtons auctioned them for a new charity called Reach for children who'd lost limbs in accidents. They went for a great deal of money. I bought nearly all of them. That particular mannequin never came up at auction. It's the mannequin of Professor Daxatar, which I was not overly concerned about." The mannequin had the vague look of Professor Daxatar, played by actor Brian Crowbridge, but a beard and wig had been added, and the body had been clad in a three-piece suit. It was an interesting attempt to make the figure look like Jack Braxton.

**中文理解**："它看起来像一个人偶，"Penny 说。"不。"Graham 摇着他巨大的头。"那不只是任何人偶。""我同意，"Kit 说。"那是 Braxtons 为推广第一批产品制作的巨型人偶之一。玩具博物馆里有一堆。"Reverend Bell 打了个响指，发出一声尖叫。"当然！没错。他们是为贸易展览做的，对吧？它们都借给了供应商？Fenton 没有得到一个吗？""是的，"Kit 说。"我记得很清楚。它在他的商店门外放了很久。我很想在我的公寓里放一个。"Graham 继续说，"促销结束后他们被召回了，Braxtons 为一个名为 Reach 的新慈善机构拍卖了它们，该机构帮助在事故中失去四肢的孩子。它们卖了很多钱。我几乎买下了所有。那个特定的人偶从未在拍卖会上出现过。它是 Daxatar 教授的人偶，我并不太在意。"那个人偶有着 Daxatar 教授模糊的样子，由演员 Brian Crowbridge 扮演，但添加了胡子和假发，身体穿着三件套西装。这是一个有趣的尝试，让人偶看起来像 Jack Braxton。

**关键词** | mannequin / giant-sized / trade fair / loaned out / recalled / auctioned / Reach / limbs / Professor Daxatar / wig

**为什么这样写**：Graham 的"mannequin"（人偶）和"giant-sized"（巨型）暗示了"真相"——棺材里是"人偶"不是"尸体"。"Reach"（Reach）这个慈善机构暗示了"讽刺"——它帮助"失去四肢"的孩子，而受害者被"肢解"了。"Professor Daxatar"（Daxatar 教授）这个虚构角色暗示了"细节"——人偶是"特定角色"。

### 第 3 段：BBC 的"exhumation"

> **原句 3:** "It's been twenty-eight years since toy baron Jack Braxton apparently died during a fire at his factory in Lincolnshire. Jack, son of the founder of Braxtons Models, Albert Braxton, had only been in control of the company for three years before it all ended in tragedy. There has been a lot of speculation through the years about the incident, but that was usually about whether the fire was an accident or arson. There was rather less speculation as to whether Jack Braxton died in the fire, as a body was found in the remains of the factory and an inquest pronounced him dead at the scene. But now, following a series of murders in the area, the investigation has apparently led the police to Braxton Hall and his final resting place in the grounds. An exhumation of Braxton's grave has found no body present, leading to many questions. Has he been alive all this time? What does the lack of a body have to do with the ongoing investigation into the deaths of Moni Worth and Lady Tabitha Pendragon? And, if Jack Braxton is alive, where is he now? Does Vera Braxton know where her son is? Does Sylvia know what happened to her brother?"

**中文理解**："自从玩具大亨 Jack Braxton 显然在林肯郡工厂火灾中去世以来，已经过去了二十八年。Jack，Braxtons Models 创始人 Albert Braxton 的儿子，在悲剧发生前只控制了公司三年。多年来对这一事件有很多猜测，但通常是关于火灾是意外还是纵火。对于 Jack Braxton 是否在火灾中死亡，猜测较少，因为在工厂废墟中发现了一具尸体，验尸官宣布他当场死亡。但现在，随着该地区发生一系列谋杀案，调查显然已将警察引向 Braxton Hall 和他最后的安息之地。对 Braxton 坟墓的挖掘发现没有尸体，导致了许多问题。他一直活着吗？缺乏尸体与对 Moni Worth 和 Lady Tabitha Pendragon 死亡的持续调查有什么关系？如果 Jack Braxton 还活着，他现在在哪里？Vera Braxton 知道她儿子在哪里吗？Sylvia 知道她哥哥发生了什么事吗？"

**关键词** | toy baron / speculation / arson / inquest / exhumation / final resting place

**为什么这样写**："toy baron"（玩具大亨）这个称号暗示了 Jack 的"身份"——他是"重要"人物。"exhumation"（挖掘）这个正式用语暗示了"法律"程序——警察"正式"挖了坟墓。"final resting place"（最后的安息之地）这个委婉语暗示了"死亡"——这是"坟墓"的"礼貌"说法。
