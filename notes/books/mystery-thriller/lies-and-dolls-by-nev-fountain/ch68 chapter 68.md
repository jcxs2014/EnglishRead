# 68. Chapter 68

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Archie 揭示自己是凶手，Angelina 帮助 Kit 逃脱，Binfire 制服 Archie。

**情感弧线位置**：恐怖与解脱——Archie 的"I am"（我是）和"fall guy"（替罪羊）暗示了"真相"，Angelina 的"electromagnet"（电磁铁）则暗示了"救赎"。

**叙事手法**：揭示+逃跑场景（博物馆）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"bark"（吠叫）和"icing on the cake"（锦上添花）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Archie 的"fall guy"

> **原句 1:** "Mr Binfire cannot go to the police and tell them he's Jack Braxton." He waved the gun. "Shuffle over there and join your friends. I've seen you do several handy rugby tackles this past week. I have no desire to become a victim of another one." Binfire reluctantly got to his feet and stood near Kit, Jackie and Angelina. Archie smiled. "It was I who 'tipped off' the police – as the criminal fraternity put it – to the fact that Jack Braxton had not died in the fire, that his coffin was empty and that he was at large. My father knew that Jack was alive, of course. And, of course, he didn't feel obliged to tell me. He told his beloved eldest, and it was only many years later, in a moment of weakness, that Tabitha told me of Father's scheme. I kept it filed in the back of my noggin. It seemed an opportunity too good to miss – to use a vanished escaped lunatic as a very handy fall guy. Very Agatha Christie."

**中文理解**："Binfire 先生不能去警察局告诉他们他是 Jack Braxton。"他挥了挥手。"那边去，加入你的朋友们。我看到你在过去一周做了几次方便的擒抱。我不想成为另一个的受害者。"Binfire 不情愿地站起来，站在 Kit、Jackie 和 Angelina 附近。Archie 笑了。"是我'举报'了警察——正如犯罪团伙所说——Jack Braxton 没有在火灾中死亡，他的棺材是空的，他在逃。当然，我父亲知道 Jack 还活着。当然，他觉得没有义务告诉我。他告诉了他最心爱的长女，直到多年后，在软弱的那一刻，Tabitha 才告诉我父亲的计划。我把它归档在我的脑后。这似乎是一个太好而不能错过的机会——用一个消失的逃跑疯子作为非常方便的替罪羊。非常阿加莎·克里斯蒂。"

**关键词** | tipped off / criminal fraternity / at large / obliged / beloved eldest / moment of weakness / scheme / noggin / vanished escaped lunatic / fall guy / Agatha Christie

**为什么这样写**：Archie 的"tipped off"（举报）和"criminal fraternity"（犯罪团伙）暗示了他的"狡猾"——他"利用"了警察。"fall guy"（替罪羊）这个俚语暗示了 Archie 的"计划"——他"嫁祸"给 Jack。"Agatha Christie"（阿加莎·克里斯蒂）这个引用暗示了 Archie的"自恋"——他认为自己"聪明"。

### 第 2 段：Archie 的"transactional arrangement"

> **原句 2:** "I met the lovely Angelina at the George Club last year. A friend of a friend of a friend. The moment I saw her, I could see she was amazing. Poise. Breeding. Blistering intelligence. The only drawback was the lesbian thing. After meeting a few more times, at the parties of mutual friends, we decided it wasn't a drawback after all. Didn't we?" Angelina didn't respond. "We were very fond of each other, don't get me wrong, but it was, when all's said and done, a truly transactional arrangement. For my part, I'd get the perfect wife, a woman who was able to carry herself in the social circles in which I operate, a woman who would not panic about 'love' or 'compatibility' or other such nonsense, and who was more than capable of running the estate. For her part, she would get a very comfortable lifestyle and give me issue. That was part of the deal. I don't like children, myself. Horrible little snotty things, running about knocking over the Meissen vases and leaving jammy fingerprints on the Gainsboroughs. But when one is in my position, one is expected to have offspring."

**中文理解**："我去年在乔治俱乐部遇到了可爱的 Angelina。一个朋友的朋友的朋友。我一看到她，我就知道她很棒。优雅。教养。灼人的智慧。唯一的缺点是女同性恋这件事。在共同朋友的聚会上又见面几次后，我们决定这毕竟不是缺点。不是吗？"Angelina 没有回应。"我们非常喜欢对方，别误会，但说到底，这确实是一个真正的交易安排。就我而言，我会得到一个完美的女人，一个能够在我的社交圈子里周旋的女人，一个不会对'爱'或'兼容性'或其他此类废话感到恐慌的女人，一个完全有能力管理庄园的女人。对她来说，她会得到非常舒适的生活方式并给我生个孩子。这是交易的一部分。我自己不喜欢孩子。讨厌的小鼻涕虫，到处乱跑，撞倒迈森花瓶，在庚斯博罗画作上留下果酱指纹。但是当一个人处于我的位置时，人们期望他有后代。"

**关键词** | George Club / poise / breeding / blistering intelligence / drawback / transactional arrangement / social circles / compatibility / issue / offspring / Meissen vases / Gainsboroughs / jammy fingerprints

**为什么这样写**：Archie 的"transactional arrangement"（交易安排）暗示了他的"冷漠"——他把"婚姻"当作"交易"。"Meissen vases"（迈森花瓶）和"Gainsboroughs"（庚斯博罗画作）暗示了 Archie 的"财富"——他拥有"艺术品"。"jammy fingerprints"（果酱指纹）这个细节暗示了 Archie 的"厌恶"——他"讨厌"孩子。

### 第 3 段：Angelina 的"electromagnet"

> **原句 3:** "Of course," said Kit, when she saw it. She looked up. "Obvious. Magnetism. It's so simple. Is there a false compartment in the ceiling? I bet there is. How can you steal four rare action figures and leave the others behind? There's a perfect way, when the rare set of figures all happen to be wearing die-cast metal armour." Archie's face wrinkled in confusion. "What are you…?" Then he noticed the device in Angelina's hand. "No! You little—" Archie swung his shotgun to give her both barrels, but it was too late. She pressed the button and the gun was plucked out of his hand. Angelina's gun did likewise. Jackie gave a sudden piercing scream, as her earrings were ripped from her ears by the powerful electromagnet secreted in the chapel roof. Kit was suddenly flying above everyone's heads, hurtling upwards. Her wheelchair fastened itself to the top of the chapel and Kit found herself dangling by the seat belt. Something whizzed past her and she saw that Binfire's belt-buckle had embedded itself into the ceiling, just inches from her nose. She was nose-to-nose with the repaired window. It had stayed intact. That was how they got it to smash inwards so spontaneously, with no one else around, she thought madly. They attached a lump of metal to it and let the magnet pull it into the room. Binfire didn't miss his chance. The moment the shotgun was plucked from Archie's hand, he hurtled towards the Marquess and performed his classic rugby tackle. He threw himself on top of Archie, who struggled and twisted, but the Queensbury Rules were useless when his arms were pinioned, and he had a raging ex-CEO of a bankrupt toy company on top of him, turning his face to pulp with his fists.

**中文理解**："当然，"Kit 看到时说。她抬头看。"明显。磁力。这很简单。天花板上有假隔层吗？我打赌有。你怎么能偷走四个人偶而留下其他人？当这套人偶恰好都穿着压铸金属盔甲时，有一个完美的方法。"Archie 的脸因困惑而皱起。"你在……？"然后他注意到 Angelina 手中的装置。"不！你这个小——"Archie 摆动霰弹枪给她两个枪管，但太晚了。她按下按钮，枪从他手中被拔出。Angelina 的枪也一样。Jackie 突然发出刺耳的尖叫，她的耳环被隐藏在教堂屋顶的强大电磁铁从耳朵上扯下。Kit 突然飞到每个人头顶上，向上猛冲。她的轮椅固定在教堂顶部，Kit 发现自己被安全带吊着。有什么东西呼啸而过，她看到 Binfire 的皮带扣嵌入了天花板，离她的鼻子只有几英寸。她与修复后的窗户面对面。它保持完好。他们就是让它如此自发地向内破碎的，没有其他人在附近，她疯狂地想。他们在上面绑了一块金属，让磁铁把它拉进房间。Binfire 没有错过机会。霰弹枪从 Archie 手中被拔出的那一刻，他冲向侯爵，表演了他经典的擒抱。他扑到 Archie 身上，Archie 挣扎着扭动，但当他的手臂被按住时，昆斯伯里规则毫无用处，他身上有一个愤怒的破产玩具公司前首席执行官，用拳头把他的脸打成了肉泥。

**关键词** | magnetism / false compartment / die-cast metal armour / plucked / likewise / piercing scream / electromagnet / secreted / hurtling / dangling / whizzed / belt-buckle / embedded / nose-to-nose / intact / spontaneously / rugby tackle / Queensbury Rules / pinioned / pulp

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"magnetism"（磁力）和"die-cast metal armour"（压铸金属盔甲）暗示了她的"推理"——她"理解"了"方法"。"electromagnet"（电磁铁）这个科学术语暗示了 Angelina 的"聪明"——她用"科学"来"拯救"。"Queensbury Rules"（昆斯伯里规则）这个拳击术语暗示了 Binfire 的"野性"——他"不遵守"规则。"pulp"（肉泥）这个动词暗示了 Binfire 的"愤怒"——他"打"Archie。
