# 55. Chapter 55

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Kit、Penny 和 Bell 用机器人监视 Jack Braxton 坟墓的挖掘，发现棺材里没有尸体，只有一个人偶。

**情感弧线位置**：紧张与震惊——Penny's"excruciating slowness"（令人痛苦的缓慢）和"There was no body"（没有尸体）暗示了"悬念"，Bell 的"unboxing video"（开箱视频）则暗示了他的"疯狂"幽默。

**叙事手法**：监视场景（货车内）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"panicked glances"（恐慌的目光）和"sickening crash"（令人作呕的撞击声）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：机器人的"eye view"

> **原句 1:** Kit, Penny and Reverend Bell were sitting in the transit van, watching the visuals on Penny's laptop. The screen showed them the robot's eye view as it wobbled inside the tent. It revealed an elaborate gravestone, a pile of soil, a body-shaped hole, a dirt-encrusted coffin that had been disgorged from the hole, and two police officers covered head-to-toe in protective clothing. One officer was rather portly, one slender. The officers looked at each other and then directly at the camera. "What?" said the portly one. The slender one shrugged. "Ooooh dear," muttered Penny.

**中文理解**：Kit、Penny 和 Reverend Bell 坐在货车里，看着 Penny 笔记本电脑上的画面。屏幕向他们展示了机器人进入帐篷时的视野。它显示了一个精致的墓碑、一堆泥土、一个身体形状的洞、一个从洞里取出的沾满泥土的棺材，以及两名从头到脚穿着防护服的警察。一个警察相当胖，一个苗条。警察们互相看着，然后直接对着镜头。"怎么了？"胖的那个说。苗条的耸了耸肩。"哦，天哪，"Penny 低声说。

**关键词** | visuals / wobbled / elaborate gravestone / dirt-encrusted / disgorged / portly / slender

**为什么这样写**："wobbled"（摇晃）这个动词暗示了机器人的"不稳定"——它"移动"得很"笨拙"。"dirt-encrusted"（沾满泥土的）和"disgorged"（吐出）暗示了棺材的"恐怖"——它"被""挖"出来了。"portly"（肥胖的）和"slender"（苗条的）这两个形容词暗示了警察的"对比"——他们"体型"不同。

### 第 2 段：Bell 的"unboxing video"

> **原句 2:** Reverend Bell gave another insane grin. "Ladies, we are about to have the unboxing video to end all unboxing videos." Penny extended one claw until two of its appendages slid beneath the lid. "Here we have the traditional premium casket with brass accessories, handle and nameplate," Reverend Bell intoned in a sing-song voice. "Note the design detailing on the lid which gives extra definition to the top of the box. The packaging is surprisingly intact. So, the figure inside should be mint, with any luck." "Shut up," snapped Kit. "She's concentrating."

**中文理解**：Reverend Bell 露出另一个疯狂的笑容。"女士们，我们将要拥有终结所有开箱视频的开箱视频。"Penny 伸出一只爪子，直到它的两个附肢滑到盖子下面。"这是传统的优质棺材，配有黄铜配件、把手和名牌，"Reverend Bell 用唱歌般的声音吟唱。"注意盖子上的设计细节，它为盒子顶部增添了额外的定义。包装出奇地完好。所以，里面的人偶应该是全新的，如果幸运的话。""闭嘴，"Kit 厉声说。"她在集中注意力。"

**关键词** | unboxing video / appendages / casket / brass accessories / intoned / mint

**为什么这样写**：Bell 的"unboxing video"（开箱视频）暗示了他的"疯狂"幽默——他把"挖坟墓"比作"开箱"。"intoned"（吟唱）这个动词暗示了 Bell 的"表演"——他在"模仿""YouTuber"。"mint"（全新的）这个收藏品术语暗示了 Bell 的"专业"——他"懂"人偶。

### 第 3 段："There was no body"

> **原句 3:** Penny concentrated on popping the last few nails. Finally, the last corner was free, and the lid wobbled in place. She manoeuvred one of the arms lower and got it to push the lid aside. It slid off and landed in the hole with a sickening crash. "Quicklyquicklyquickly," muttered Penny under her breath. She extended the robot's viewscreen, so they could see inside the coffin. There was no body. But it wasn't empty. There was an action figure inside. It was six feet tall, with a beard, and it was dressed in the remains of Jack Braxton's clothes, but it was definitely an action figure.

**中文理解**：Penny 专注于撬开最后几个钉子。最后，最后一个角松动了，盖子原地摇晃。她把一只手臂操纵到更低的位置，让它把盖子推到一边。它滑落了，带着令人作呕的撞击声掉进洞里。"快快快，"Penny 低声说。她延伸了机器人的观察屏幕，这样他们就能看到棺材里面。没有尸体。但它不是空的。里面有一个动作人偶。它有六英尺高，有胡子，穿着 Jack Braxton 衣服的残骸，但它绝对是一个动作人偶。

**关键词** | sickening crash / viewscreen / no body / six feet tall / action figure

**为什么这样写**："sickening crash"（令人作呕的撞击声）暗示了"恐怖"——盖子"掉"下去了。"no body"（没有尸体）这个简单的句子暗示了"真相"——Jack Braxton"没有"死。"six feet tall"（六英尺高）暗示了人偶的"巨大"——它是"真人大小"的。
