# 63. Chapter 63

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：素描师努力根据 Binfire 的模糊描述画嫌疑人，但 Binfire 的描述自相矛盾。

**情感弧线位置**：挫败与焦虑——Binfire 的"vague and contradictory"（模糊和矛盾）和"frokking bad"（该死的糟糕）暗示了他的"挫败"，素描师的"forced a smile"（强颜欢笑）则暗示了他的"无奈"。

**叙事手法**：对话场景（警察局）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"struggled to the end of the task"（艰难完成任务）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Binfire 的"Back to the Future"

> **原句 1:** The sketch artist wasn't doing as well as the police had hoped. It didn't help that Binfire was offering vague and contradictory opinions. "He had a moustache, a bit like that guy in that film. You know, the one with the cowboys and the time-travelling car." The sketch artist frowned. "You mean Back to the Future Part Three?" "Not that one, the other one." The sketch artist forced a smile on his face. "Can you pick a movie that I have seen?" They struggled to the end of the task, and the sketch artist showed Binfire the face he'd drawn. Binfire frowned. "Nah, I don't think that's it. It's a bit… wrong. Well, it's just frokking bad, man. This is hopeless." He threw his arm up over his head, showing his hairy pits. "We're never gonna find Kit at this rate. Frokk it."

**中文理解**：素描师没有警察希望的那么成功。Binfire 提供模糊和矛盾的意见并没有帮助。"他有胡子，有点像那部电影里的那个家伙。你知道，就是那个有牛仔和时间旅行车的那部。"素描师皱眉。"你是说《回到未来第三部》？""不是那个，另一个。"素描师强颜欢笑。"你能选一部我看过的电影吗？"他们艰难地完成了任务，素描师给 Binfire 看他画的脸。Binfire 皱眉。"不，我觉得不是。有点……不对。嗯，就是该死的糟糕，伙计。这没用。"他把手臂举过头顶，露出他毛茸茸的腋窝。"这样我们永远也找不到 Kit。该死。"

**关键词** | vague and contradictory / moustache / cowboys / time-travelling car / Back to the Future / forced a smile / frokking bad / hairy pits

**为什么这样写**：Binfire 的"vague and contradictory"（模糊和矛盾）暗示了他的"不可靠"——他"不善于"描述。"Back to the Future"（《回到未来》）这个电影引用暗示了 Binfire的"极客"文化——他用"电影"来"描述"。"frokking bad"（该死的糟糕）这个粗口暗示了 Binfire 的"挫败"——他"生气"了。"hairy pits"（毛茸茸的腋窝）这个细节暗示了 Binfire 的"不修边幅"——他"不在乎"形象。
