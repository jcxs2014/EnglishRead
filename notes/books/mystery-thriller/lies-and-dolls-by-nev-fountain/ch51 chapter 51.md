# 51. Chapter 51

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Fenton 被警察带走；Kit 拒绝离开 Furley House，尽管 Freya 和 Binfire 警告她有危险。

**情感弧线位置**：决心与恐惧——Kit 的"I made a promise"（我承诺过）和"Lego sets"（乐高套装）暗示了她的"坚定"，Binfire 的"scarper"（逃跑）则暗示了"危险"。

**叙事手法**：对话场景（车内）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"the difference is I'm staying"（区别是我留下）和"that was that"（就这样了）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Vixy 的证词

> **原句 1:** They led Fenton away. Vixy trotted after them and made a beeline for DC Butcher, who, of the six police officers, looked the youngest and most approachable. "Excuse me, sir! Excuse me!" The young DC turned his head. "Hi. It was me who phoned the police, you know, to try and stop Fenton setting fire to the shop." "Oh right. And what's your relationship to Mr Worth?" "I'm a lodger. My room was up there." She pointed to a patch of empty sky where the upper floor of The Battlestore Galactica used to be. "Oh. I'm sorry." "That's not important. I can find a place to stay. I usually manage, you know. But I just want to tell you that I've been with Fenton all last night and this morning – I didn't let him out of my sight." "All through last night?" She blushed. "Yes. His wife left him after a thing that had happened, in Furley House? So I've been comforting him. So, I know for definite he didn't kill his wife." "Oh. Are you prepared to make a statement to that effect?" "Yes." "Would you like to come with us?" "Absolutely, yeah. I better tell my friends I'm going to the station." She turned, but there was no one there. Kit, Binfire and Freya had all slipped away.

**中文理解**：他们带走了 Fenton。Vixy 小跑着跟上他们，直奔 DC Butcher，在六名警察中，他看起来最年轻、最容易接近。"打扰一下，先生！打扰一下！"年轻的 DC 转过头。"嗨。是我打电话报警的，你知道，试图阻止 Fenton 放火烧店。""哦，好的。你和 Worth 先生是什么关系？""我是个租客。我的房间在上面。"她指着一片空旷的天空，那里曾经是 The Battlestore Galactica 的二楼。""哦。我很抱歉。""那不重要。我能找到住的地方。我通常能应付，你知道。但我只想告诉你，我昨晚和今天早上一直和 Fenton 在一起——我没有让他离开我的视线。""整个昨晚？"她脸红了。"是的。他的妻子在 Furley House 发生事后离开了他？所以我一直在安慰他。所以，我肯定他没有杀他的妻子。""哦。你愿意就此作证吗？""是的。""你愿意和我们一起去吗？""当然，是的。我最好告诉我的朋友我要去警察局。"她转过身，但那里没有人。Kit、Binfire 和 Freya 都溜走了。

**关键词** | beeline / lodger / comforting / for definite / slipped away

**为什么这样写**：Vixy 的"beeline"（直奔）暗示了她的"急切"——她想"帮助" Fenton。"lodger"（租客）这个法律术语暗示了 Vixy 的"身份"——她"住"在 Fenton 的"店里"。"for definite"（肯定）这个口语暗示了 Vixy 的"确定"——她"知道" Fenton 没有杀人。

### 第 2 段：Kit 的"No"

> **原句 2:** "Frokk. Shit just got frokking real." Binfire was frowning extremely hard. His wild eyebrows were knitted together. His knuckles were pure white, hands clamped tightly on the steering wheel. "You said it," agreed Kit, looking out of the window at the endless flat fields. She didn't feel like saying anything at that point, so she settled on the bare minimum. "This is sooo awful. I can't believe this is happening," said Freya, sitting between them. "Me neither," said Kit. Freya made a decision. "We should go back to Furley House, say a quick 'Hi' to Archie, see if he's okay, then we should go straight back to Brighton." She flicked Binfire's (purely ornamental) dashboard switch. "Warp nine, okay?" "No," said Kit. "Kit, we've got to go!" "I made a promise." "To a dead woman!" "We don't know for certain she's dead." Freya shook her head disbelievingly. "Kit, they found a hand." "Lots of people live quite happily with only one hand." Binfire chipped in, "She's got a point, Frey. Bucky Barnes. Captain Hook. Luke Skywalker lost a hand and he still beat the Empire single-handed. Literally." Freya slapped Binfire's knee, and the motorhome wobbled. "Be sensible. You can't talk like daft Binfire now. You have to be sensible Binfire. You have to convince Kit to leave." "Yeah, right. Okay." He glanced at Kit. "We should all scarper. This insane person is turning people into Lego sets. He's killing people we know." "More than that," snapped Kit. "This insane person is targeting women. A woman who owned a doll, and a woman married to someone who owned a doll." "Exactly." "Therefore, you should leave now, Freya. Binfire owned a doll, and you're Binfire's friend…" "But so are you! You're Binfire's friend too!" "But there's a difference." "What difference?" "The difference is I'm staying." And that was that.

**中文理解**："该死。事情变得该死的真实。"Binfire 皱着眉头。他的浓密眉毛拧在一起。他的指关节发白，双手紧紧握住方向盘。"你说得对，"Kit 同意，看着窗外无尽的平坦田野。她那时不想说什么，所以她选择了最少的话。"这太可怕了。我不敢相信这正在发生，"Freya 坐在他们之间说。"我也不敢相信，"Kit 说。Freya 做了个决定。"我们应该回 Furley House，跟 Archie 快速打个招呼，看看他是否没事，然后我们应该直接回 Brighton。"她拨动 Binfire 的（纯装饰性）仪表盘开关。"曲速九，好吗？""不，"Kit 说。"Kit，我们必须走！""我承诺过。""对一个死去的女人！""我们不确定她是否死了。"Freya 难以置信地摇头。"Kit，他们发现了一只手。""很多人只有一只手也过得很好。"Binfire 插话说，"她说的有道理，Frey。巴奇·巴恩斯。虎克船长。卢克·天行者失去了一只手，但他仍然单手打败了帝国。字面意思。"Freya 拍了 Binfire 的膝盖，房车摇晃。"理智点。你现在不能像傻 Binfire 那样说话。你必须做理智的 Binfire。你必须说服 Kit 离开。""是的，好吧。"他瞥了 Kit 一眼。"我们都应该逃跑。这个疯子正在把人变成乐高套装。他在杀我们认识的人。""不止于此，"Kit 厉声说。"这个疯子正在针对女人。一个人偶主人的女人，和一个嫁给了人偶主人的女人。""没错。""所以，你现在应该离开，Freya。Binfire 拥有一个人偶，你是 Binfire 的朋友……""但你也是！你也是 Binfire 的朋友！""但有区别。""什么区别？""区别是我留下。"就这样了。

**关键词** | frokking real / knitted together / knuckles / ornamental / Warp nine / scarper / Lego sets / single-handed / the difference is I'm staying

**为什么这样写**：Binfire 的"frokking real"（该死的真实）暗示了他的"恐惧"——事情"变得""严重"。"knuckles"（指关节）这个细节暗示了 Binfire 的"紧张"——他"握紧"了方向盘。"scarper"（逃跑）这个俚语暗示了 Binfire 的"恐惧"——他想"逃跑"。"Lego sets"（乐高套装）这个比喻暗示了凶手的"残忍"——他"拆解"了人。"the difference is I'm staying"（区别是我留下）暗示了 Kit 的"决心"——她"选择"留下。
