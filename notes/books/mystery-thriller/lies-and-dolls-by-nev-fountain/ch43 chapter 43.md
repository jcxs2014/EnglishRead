# 43. Chapter 43

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：众人回到 Furley House，用面部识别尝试解锁神秘手机；Kit 意识到需要用 Tabitha 的照片来解锁。

**情感弧线位置**：紧张与不安——Kit 的"close to tears"（接近眼泪）和"contagious"（传染的）暗示了她的"恐惧"——她"知道"真相但"害怕"面对。

**叙事手法**：推理场景（图书馆）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"facial recognition"（面部识别）和"pale"（苍白）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：The Deer Hunter

> **原句 1:** "One by one, they went around the room. Freya came down from the gallery to show her face. Nothing happened. Binfire helpfully pressed the phone against the robot's computer monitor, so that Graham could lean forward and allow his face to register on the screen. Nothing happened. The phone stayed stubbornly locked. "So, it doesn't belong to any of us," mused Reverend Bell, scratching his beard. "Then we're fucked," growled Matty. "Unless our ace detective can rub her little grey cells together and find a way to open it." Kit sighed. "I do have an idea. Give it to me."

**中文理解**："他们一个接一个地在房间里轮流转。Freya 从画廊下来给他们看脸。什么也没发生。Binfire 贴心地把手机压在机器人的显示器上，让 Graham 可以向前倾，让他的脸在屏幕上注册。什么什么也没发生。手机顽固地锁着。""所以，它不属于我们中的任何一个，"Reverend Bell 若有所思地说，挠着胡子。"那我们就完了，"Matty 咆哮道。"除非我们的小侦探能开动她的小灰细胞，找到打开它的方法。"Kit 叹了口气。"我确实有个主意。把它给我。"

**关键词** | stubbornly locked / mused / ace detective / rub her little grey cells

**为什么这样写**："rub her little grey cells"（开动她的小灰细胞）是波洛（Hercule Poirot）的经典台词，暗示了 Matty 的"讽刺"——他在"嘲笑" Kit 的"侦探"身份。"stubbornly locked"（顽固地锁着）这个形容词暗示了手机的"抵抗"——它"拒绝"被"打开"。

### 第 2 段：Kit 的"idea"

> **原句 2:** Binfire handed the phone to Kit, who left the room. They could hear her boots clumping down the corridor and up the stairs. "What the fuck? What the fuck is she doing?" "She knows what she's doing," sighed Freya. "She always knows what she's doing." After a few minutes, they could hear her boots clumping down the stairs, back along the corridor, getting slowly louder until she stood in the doorway. Her face was even paler than usual, and she looked close to tears. The phone was in her hand and she stretched her arm out as far as it would go, as if it was contagious. "It's open," she said, in a very tiny voice.

**中文理解**：Binfire 把手机递给 Kit，她离开了房间。他们能听到她的靴子沿着走廊和楼梯咔嗒作响。"搞什么？搞什么？""她在做什么她知道，"Freya 叹了口气。"她总是知道她在做什么。"几分钟后，他们能听到她的靴子沿着楼梯咔嗒作响，回到走廊，声音慢慢变大，直到她站在门口。她的脸比平时更苍白，看起来快要流泪了。手机在她手里，她把手臂伸得尽可能远，好像它是传染性的。"它开了，"她用非常小的声音说。

**关键词** | clumping / close to tears / contagious / very tiny voice

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"contagious"（传染性的）暗示了她的"恐惧"——她把手机当作"危险物品"。"very tiny voice"（非常小的声音）暗示了 Kit 的"震惊"——她"知道"真相但"害怕"说出。

### 第 3 段：Matty 的"outrage"

> **原句 3:** Matty snatched the phone out of her hand. "Good work, Kitty Kat. How do we find out who it belongs to?" "I've opened the WhatsApp. Just look at the messages." "Of course, yeah." He scrolled through the messages, outrage spreading across his face. "What the actual fuck?"

**中文理解**：Matty 从她手中抢过手机。"干得好，小猫咪。我们怎么找出它属于谁？""我打开了 WhatsApp。看看消息就行了。""当然，是的。"他滚动消息，愤怒在他脸上蔓延。"到底搞什么？"

**关键词** | snatched / Kitty Kat / outrage / What the actual fuck?

**为什么这样写**：Matty 的"Kitty Kat"（小猫咪）这个昵称暗示了他的"轻蔑"——他"不认真"对待 Kit。"What the actual fuck?"（到底搞什么？）这个粗口暗示了 Matty 的"愤怒"——他"发现"了"真相"。
