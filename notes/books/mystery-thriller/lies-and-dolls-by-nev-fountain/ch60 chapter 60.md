# 60. Chapter 60

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Kit 和 Nate 成功逃脱，Nate 吸引媒体注意，Kit 乘 Archie 派来的车前往警察局。

**情感弧线位置**：解脱与紧张——Kit 的"panic badge"（恐慌徽章）和"Binfire is long gone"（Binfire 早已离开）暗示了她的"孤独"，Nate 的"friendly wink"（友好的眨眼）则暗示了他的"感谢"。

**叙事手法**：逃跑场景（Braxton Hall→警车）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"press"（记者）和"shoal of fish"（鱼群）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Kit 的"panic badge"

> **原句 1:** This was the ultimate test for Kit. To get past the cordon of journalists and photographers. They surged forward when the gate glided open, almost pushing it shut with the weight of their bodies. I can see why they're called 'press', she thought. They're very good at pressing against people. The noise was unbearable. Everyone was shouting questions at her, but they weren't nice questions, like What's your favourite episode? or How do you get your ideas? or Would you return to the series if asked? They were questions like Do you know where Jack Braxton is? Are you hiding him? Did you help him commit the murders? Her hand instinctively reached for the panic badge on her lapel, ready to turn it upside down and send a message to… Who? Who is here to rescue me? Binfire is long gone. She took several steps backwards, stumbling over the stones that separated the lawn from the bright purple driveway. But then, a miracle. Like a startled shoal of fish, the journalists and photographers all swerved away from her and moved in another direction, towards Nate Braxton, who was dressed and standing in the driveway, making a big show of climbing into a beautiful silver-blue E-Type Jaguar. They converged on him, only to part like the Red Sea as the car roared through them, out of the gates and onto the open road. As he passed her, he slowed down and gave Kit a friendly wink. This is your chance, Kit! Kit took her opportunity and ran down the road towards the transit van. A moustachioed man wearing thick tweeds and driving gloves was standing outside the van, and he doffed his cap at her as she approached. He gestured to the side door. Kit hopped into the back seat and sighed with relief. "Where to, miss?" said the man. "The police station, please."

**中文理解**：这对 Kit 来说是终极测试。要越过记者和摄影师的警戒线。当大门滑开时，他们向前涌去，几乎用身体的重量把它关上。我明白为什么他们叫"媒体"了，她想。他们非常擅长压迫人。噪音令人难以忍受。每个人都在对她大喊大叫，但不是好的问题，比如你最喜欢哪一集？或者你怎么获得想法？或者如果被要求你会回归系列吗？他们的问题是你知道 Jack Braxton 在哪里吗？你把他藏起来了吗？你帮他犯谋杀罪了吗？她的手本能地伸向翻领上的恐慌徽章，准备把它翻过来发送消息给……谁？谁来救我？Binfire 早已离开。她后退了几步，在分隔草坪和明亮紫色车道的石头上绊倒了。但是，一个奇迹。像一群受惊的鱼，记者和摄影师都从她身边转向另一个方向，走向 Nate Braxton，他穿着整齐，站在车道上，大张旗鼓地爬进一辆美丽的银蓝色 E-Type 捷豹。他们向他聚拢，结果却像红海一样分开，汽车呼啸着穿过他们，驶出大门，驶上开阔的道路。当他经过她时，他放慢速度，给了 Kit 一个友好的眨眼。这是你的机会，Kit！Kit 抓住机会，沿着路跑向货车。一个留着小胡子、穿着粗花呢和驾驶手套的男人站在货车外面，当她走近时，他向她脱帽致意。他示意侧门。Kit 跳进后座，松了一口气。"去哪里，小姐？"男人说。"警察局，请。"

**关键词** | cordon / surged / press / panic badge / shoal of fish / E-Type Jaguar / Red Sea / converged / doffed his cap

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"press"（媒体）这个双关语暗示了"语言游戏"——"press"既是"记者"也是"压迫"。"panic badge"（恐慌徽章）暗示了 Kit 的"孤独"——她"没有人"可以求助。"shoal of fish"（鱼群）这个比喻暗示了记者的"盲目"——他们"转向"了新目标。"E-Type Jaguar"（E-Type 捷豹）暗示了 Nate 的"贵族"身份——他开"豪车"。"Red Sea"（红海）这个圣经比喻暗示了 Nate 的"勇敢"——他"分开"了人群。
