# 62. Chapter 62

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Kit 在蒙面人的"实验室"中醒来，听到最后的威胁诗句。

**情感弧线位置**：恐怖与无助——Kit 的"goo had stuck her eyelashes together"（分泌物把她的睫毛粘在一起）和"gurgling noise"（咕噜声）暗示了她的"无助"，蒙面人的"mad scientist's laboratory"（疯狂科学家的实验室）则暗示了"恐怖"。

**叙事手法**：觉醒场景（实验室）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"forced herself to open her eyes"（强迫自己睁开眼睛）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Kit 的"mad scientist's laboratory"

> **原句 1:** Kit was awake, but she wasn't ready to open her eyes yet. She lay there, safe in the dark, listening to the gentle tick-tock of her pocket watch. There was something wrong, she could feel it, but she wasn't ready for that either. You can't just lie here forever. You're in danger. You know you're in danger. She forced herself to open her eyes, which wasn't easy as goo had stuck her eyelashes together, but she managed it. There was more darkness, and then there was grey, and then there were fuzzy shapes. She could feel a cool breeze and assumed she was lying on a table. She moved her arm slightly and the plastic sheeting beneath her body popped and crackled with terrifying volume. She craned her head up and could see medical machines, hospital beds and the kind of overhead light you found in operating theatres. A mad scientist's laboratory, she thought madly. I'm in a mad scientist's laboratory.

**中文理解**：Kit 醒了，但她还没准备好睁开眼睛。她躺在那里，在黑暗中感到安全，听着她怀表的轻柔滴答声。有些不对劲，她能感觉到，但她也没准备好面对那个。你不能只是永远躺在这里。你有危险。你知道你有危险。她强迫自己睁开眼睛，这并不容易，因为分泌物把她的睫毛粘在一起，但她做到了。有更多的黑暗，然后是灰色，然后是模糊的形状。她能感觉到一阵凉风，以为她躺在桌子上。她微微移动了一下手臂，身下的塑料布发出令人恐惧的爆裂声。她抬起头，能看到医疗机器、医院病床和手术室里的那种头顶灯。一个疯狂科学家的实验室，她疯狂地想。我在一个疯狂科学家的实验室里。

**关键词** | tick-tock / goo / fuzzy shapes / plastic sheeting / crackled / craned / operating theatres / mad scientist's laboratory

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"goo"（分泌物）和"fuzzy shapes"（模糊形状）暗示了她的"虚弱"——她"看不清楚"。"plastic sheeting"（塑料布）和"crackled"（爆裂声）暗示了"恐怖"——她"躺"在"塑料布"上。"mad scientist's laboratory"（疯狂科学家的实验室）这个比喻暗示了 Kit 的"恐惧"——她"以为"自己在"电影"里。

---

### 第 2 段：蒙面人的威胁

> **原句 2:** "Hello, Kit." The voice was distorted because it was behind a blank unsmiling mask, but it was also artificial, machine-like. It crackled. She tried to speak but didn't have the muscles in her face to respond. She just made a low gurgling noise. "I'm so glad you could join us, Kit," the voice grated. "I've been waiting for you. You're the last." The masked figure dipped its head low. "One little Kit. Left all alone. Then they lost the instructions. And then there were none."

**中文理解**："你好，Kit。"声音失真是因为它在一个空白无笑的面具后面，但它也是人工的，机器般的。它发出噼啪声。她试图说话，但脸上没有肌肉可以回应。她只是发出低沉的咕噜声。"我很高兴你能加入我们，Kit，"声音刺耳地说。"我一直在等你。你是最后一个。"蒙面人低下头。"一个小 Kit。独自留下。然后他们失去了说明书。然后就没有了。"

**关键词** | distorted / blank unsmiling mask / artificial / machine-like / gurgling / grated / dipped / instructions

**为什么这样写**：蒙面人的"blank unsmiling mask"（空白无笑的面具）暗示了"恐怖"——他"没有"表情。"artificial"（人工的）和"machine-like"（机器般的）暗示了"非人"——他的声音"不像"人类。"instructions"（说明书）这个词暗示了"讽刺"——他"引用"了《无人生还》的"说明书"。
