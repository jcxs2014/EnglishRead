# 61. Chapter 61

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Binfire 在警察局接受关于货车司机的询问，发现 Archie 并没有派人去接 Kit。

**情感弧线位置**：紧张与不安——Binfire 的"worried"（担心）和"Are you sure he wasn't the man who was sent to drive Kit?"（你确定他不是被派去开车接 Kit 的人吗？）暗示了"危险"。

**叙事手法**：对话场景（警察局）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"interview"（询问）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Binfire 的"worried"

> **原句 1:** They were in a small office at the police station. DCI Smallgreen and DC Butcher had decided to conduct the interview. DCI Smallgreen asked the same question again. "So can you describe the man who was driving the van?" Binfire frowned. "I've told you. I'm not sure. I was quite far away. My motorhome was parked way across the other side of the crossroads, and my night-vision binoculars don't work as well in the day." "Okay, well… If we brought in a sketch artist, do you think it would help?" "Yeah, I'll have a go." "Good. Thank you for your assistance, Mr Ferry." Binfire looked worried. "Are you sure he wasn't the man who was sent to drive Kit?" "The Marquess of Furley didn't send anyone to drive Kit. He got delayed."

**中文理解**：他们在警察局的一间小办公室里。DCI Smallgreen 和 DC Butcher 决定进行询问。DCI Smallgreen 再次问了同样的问题。"所以你能描述一下开货车的那个男人吗？"Binfire 皱眉。"我告诉过你了。我不确定。我离得很远。我的房车停在十字路口的另一边，而我的夜视镜在白天效果不好。""好吧……如果我们请一个素描师来，你觉得会有帮助吗？""是的，我愿意试试。""好。谢谢你的帮助，Ferry 先生。"Binfire 看起来很担心。"你确定他不是被派去开车接 Kit 的人吗？""Furley 侯爵没有派人去开车接 Kit。他被耽搁了。"

**关键词** | crossroads / night-vision binoculars / sketch artist / worried / delayed

**为什么这样写**：Binfire 的"night-vision binoculars"（夜视镜）暗示了他的"军事化"思维——他用"装备"来"观察"。"worried"（担心）这个形容词暗示了 Binfire 的"恐惧"——他"担心" Kit 的安全。"delayed"（耽搁了）这个动词暗示了"阴谋"——有人在"假装"是 Archie 派来的人。
