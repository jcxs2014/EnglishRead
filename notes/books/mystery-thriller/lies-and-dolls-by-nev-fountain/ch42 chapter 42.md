# 42. Chapter 42

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：风车磨坊会面——Kenneth Willoughby 出现但声称自己是"演员"，被"Sue Denham"雇佣来"表演"；真凶打来电话，声音是 Archie Pendragon。

**情感弧线位置**：震惊与真相——Kenneth 的"I'm an actor"（我是演员）和"word for word"（逐字逐句）暗示了"表演"本质，Archie 的"Marquess of Furley"（Furley 侯爵）则暗示了"真凶"身份。

**叙事手法**：对决场景（风车磨坊）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"this doesn't fit"（这不合适）和"word for word"（逐字逐句）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：出发

> **原句 1:** It was 11.30 a.m. when they assembled outside Penny's transit van. Penny, Matty, Fenton and Binfire were waiting to board. Archie, Reverend Bell, Vixy and Freya were there to wave them off. "Is Graham accompanying us today?" asked Kit. "Alas, no." A smile twitched on Penny's face. "He's still got his privileges revoked. Twenty-four hours. He'll get his phone – and his remote control – back tonight."

**中文理解**：他们上午 11:30 在 Penny 的货车外集合。Penny、Matty、Fenton 和 Binfire 在等待上车。Archie、Reverend Bell、Vixy 和 Freya 在那里为他们送行。"Graham 今天陪我们去吗？"Kit 问。"唉，不行。"Penny 脸上抽动了一下。"他的特权还被剥夺着。二十四小时。他今晚会拿回他的手机——和他的遥控器。"

**关键词** | assembled / privileges revoked / remote control

**为什么这样写**：Graham 的"privileges revoked"（特权被剥夺）暗示了他的"监狱"身份——他"失去"了"自由"。"remote control"（遥控器）这个细节暗示了 Graham 的"控制欲"——他"需要"遥控器来"控制"机器人。

### 第 2 段：Kit 的"Don Quixote"

> **原句 2:** "So, this place is a derelict windmill on the edge of Spalding." Kit frowned. "Perhaps it's some kind of joke at our expense. How he keeps making us tilt at windmills like Don Quixote."

**中文理解**："所以，这个地方是 Spalding 边缘的一个废弃风车。"Kit 皱眉。"也许这是某种嘲笑我们的玩笑。他怎么一直让我们像堂吉诃德一样冲向风车。"

**关键词** | derelict windmill / tilt at windmills / Don Quixote

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"Don Quixote"（堂吉诃德）比喻暗示了她的"文学素养"——她用"经典小说"来理解"现实"。"tilt at windmills"（冲向风车）这个成语暗示了"徒劳"——他们在"追逐"一个"不真实"的敌人。

---

### 第 3 段：Kenneth 的"confession"

> **原句 3:** "I know he did," said Kit. "He just confessed using exactly the same words as he did on his recording. Exactly the same. Word for word." Fenton frowned. "So what?" Binfire looked up from the dirt. "Are you saying he's a robot?" "I'm saying he's an actor." "He is an actor," said Fenton. "We know that." "I know he's an actor. That's the point. Don't you see?" "No." "It's a performance. That's what I'm saying. He's saying lines. And when I confronted him about the interview he did with me, he didn't know what to say. Like he was trying to improvise but couldn't."

**中文理解**："我知道他做了，"Kit 说。"他刚刚用的话和他的录音完全一样。完全一样。逐字逐句。"Fenton 皱眉。"那又怎样？"Binfire 从泥土中抬头。"你是说他是机器人？""我是说他是演员。""他是演员，"Fenton 说。"我们知道。""我知道他是演员。这就是重点。你们不明白吗？""不。""这是一场表演。这就是我要说的。他在说台词。当我质问他关于他接受我采访的事时，他不知道该说什么。就像他在试图即兴发挥但做不到。"

**关键词** | Word for word / robot / actor / performance / improvise

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"word for word"（逐字逐句）暗示了她的"观察力"——她"注意到"了"重复"。"improvise"（即兴发挥）这个动词暗示了 Kenneth 的"不自然"——他"只会"说"台词"。

### 第 4 段：Kenneth 的"script"

> **原句 4:** Kenneth frowned. "The last time? Only a few hours ago. She called to make sure I got the instructions through the post. I told her that I had, and it was fine." "What's the number this Sue Denham called you on?" asked Kit. Kenneth handed over his phone and pointed at the screen. "That one. I labelled it 'Client'." "You're wasting your time," huffed Matty. "You know that we were all sent texts from a phone that didn't take incoming messages. We tried to call the number, remember?" Fenton's eyes widened. "Perhaps this number takes incoming calls?" Kit didn't answer. She was already calling the number on Kenneth's phone. She held it to her ear. "It's ringing." "Frokk!" They were all frozen in a tableau, on a muddy track by a ditch and a burnt-out windmill. Everyone was focused on Kit with Kenneth's phone pressed against her ear. "It's still ringing." She activated the speaker to prove her point. The brrrr-brrrr crackled out of the phone and kept on going. Kit looked at Kenneth. "Does she take a lot of time to answer?" "I don't know." Kenneth shrugged. "She only rang me. And even then she only called me twice." The brrrr-brrrr continued. Fenton sighed. "It was a nice try. But I don't think she'd be stupid enough to answer her phone now. She must know that we've—" "Brrrr-Yes?" It was a male voice. Slightly out of breath. Kit nearly dropped the phone. "Hello?" she said cautiously. "Yes? Hello?" The voice sounded familiar. "Um…" said Kit. "Who is this?" The voice sounded annoyed. Matty lunged forward and grabbed the phone out of Kit's hand. "Never mind who we are! You're not the one asking the questions! We're asking the questions! Who the fuck are you?" "I beg your pardon?" "You heard me, you sick fuck. Tell me who the fuck you are or I'll find you and I'll pull your head off, just like you did to my Velhellan." There was a long pause. Finally, the voice said: "Well really! That's not the way to talk to anyone. Let alone the Marquess of Furley!"

**中文理解**：Kenneth 皱眉。"最后一次？就在几个小时前。她打电话确认我是否收到了邮寄的指示。我告诉她我收到了，没问题。""这个 Sue Denham 是用哪个号码打给你的？"Kit 问。Kenneth 递过手机，指着屏幕。"那个。我把它标记为'客户'。""你在浪费时间，"Matty 哼了一声。"你知道我们都是从一部不接受来电的手机上收到短信的。我们试着打过那个号码，记得吗？"Fenton 睁大了眼睛。"也许这个号码接受来电？"Kit 没有回答。她已经在打 Kenneth 手机上的号码了。她把它贴在耳边。"它在响。""该死！"他们都僵在一个画面中，在沟渠和烧毁的泥泞小路上。每个人都专注地看着 Kit，Kenneth 的手机贴在她耳边。"它还在响。"她打开扬声器来证明她的观点。电话里传出嗡嗡声，持续不断。Kit 看着 Kenneth。"她接电话需要很长时间吗？""我不知道。"Kenneth 耸肩。"她只给我打过电话。而且只打了两次。"嗡嗡声继续。Fenton 叹了口气。"这是个好尝试。但我不认为她会蠢到现在接她的电话。她一定知道我们已经——""嗡嗡——喂？"是一个男性的声音。稍微有点喘气。Kit 差点掉了手机。"喂？"她谨慎地说。"是的？你好？"声音听起来很熟悉。"嗯……"Kit 说。"你是谁？"声音听起来很生气。Matty 冲上前，从 Kit 手中抢过手机。"别管我们是谁！你不是问问题的人！我们在问问题！你他妈是谁？""你说什么？""你听到了，你这个变态。告诉我你是谁，否则我会找到你，把你的头拧下来，就像你对我的 Velhellan 做的那样。"长长的停顿。最后，声音说："真的！这不是跟人说话的方式。更不用说跟 Furley 侯爵说话了！"

**关键词** | Sue Denham / Client / tableau / Marquess of Furley

**为什么这样写**：Kenneth 的"Client"（客户）标签暗示了他的"职业"身份——他"不知道"雇主是谁。"tableau"（画面）这个法语词暗示了"静止"——所有人都"僵住"了。"Marquess of Furley"（Furley 侯爵）这个称呼暗示了"真凶"——是 Archie。
