# 65. Chapter 65

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Kit 在医院醒来，Binfire 告诉她失去了腿；Kit 讲述她的名字来源。

**情感弧线位置**：黑色幽默与接受——Kit 的"My boots won't fit now"（我的靴子现在不合脚了）和"phantom-limb syndrome"（幻肢综合征）暗示了她的"幽默"，Binfire 的"bodyguard duty"（保镖职责）则暗示了他的"忠诚"。

**叙事手法**：对话场景（医院）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"high-pitched woozy laugh"（高音头晕的笑声）和"kit forms"（套件）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Kit 的"celebrity"

> **原句 1:** "Hi there, pilgrim." The last person she expected to see when she opened her eyes was Binfire, but there he was, looming over her. "Hello. What are you doing here?" "I thought I'd pop in and see the local celebrity." "Celebrity?" "Yeah, pilgrim. You're famous. The only one of the Braxtons Butcher victims who got out in one piece." He frowned. "Unfortunate choice of words, but you know what I mean." "Where am I?" "Hospital of course," grunted Binfire. "Where else smells like this?" Kit flipped her gaze around the room. It was indeed a hospital room. Pale blue, with a window looking out onto the countryside. There was a bedside table straining under the weight of a huge fruit basket and flowers on the windowsill. "This is nice. Is this a private room? Am I on BUPA?" "You are," said Binfire. "Don't worry about it. Archie took care of it. You've been unconscious for two days." "Wow. How long have you been here?" "All day and night," said Binfire. "That murdering frokker is still at large. I'm on bodyguard duty. That's why I'm here. He could strike again at any time and take your other leg."

**中文理解**："嗨，朝圣者。"她睁开眼睛时，她最不想看到的人就是 Binfire，但他在那里，笼罩着她。"你好。你在这里做什么？""我想来看看当地的名人。""名人？""是的，朝圣者。你出名了。你是 Braxtons 屠夫受害者中唯一一个完整逃脱的人。"他皱眉。"用词不幸，但你知道我的意思。""我在哪里？""当然是医院，"Binfire 咕哝道。"还有哪里闻起来像这样？"Kit 环顾四周。这确实是一间医院病房。淡蓝色，窗户俯瞰乡村。床头柜在沉重的水果篮下压得喘不过气来，窗台上放着花。"这很好。这是私人房间吗？我有 BUPA 吗？""你有，"Binfire 说。"别担心。Archie 照顾的。你已经昏迷两天了。""哇。你在这里多久了？""日夜都在，"Binfire 说。"那个杀人犯还在逃。我在值班保镖。这就是我在这里的原因。他随时可能再次袭击，拿走你的另一条腿。"

**关键词** | celebrity / in one piece / at large / bodyguard duty

**为什么这样写**：Binfire 的"celebrity"（名人）和"in one piece"（完整）暗示了"讽刺"——Kit"出名"了，因为她是"唯一"幸存的人。"at large"（在逃）这个短语暗示了"危险"——凶手"还没有"被抓。"bodyguard duty"（保镖职责）暗示了 Binfire 的"忠诚"——他"保护" Kit。

### 第 2 段：Kit 的"kit forms"

> **原句 2:** Kit looked down at her legs. Leg. Her left one was missing. There was her thigh, her knee… But below that, nothing. "My leg's gone," she said stupidly. "I know, pilgrim. I'm sure it's a shock, but you were lucky. It looked like he was after your head as well." She allowed her head to fall back on her pillow. "This is bad. My boots won't fit now." She gave a high-pitched woozy laugh. "Hey. This is funny. Did I tell you why my parents called me Kit?" "No." "Go on, guess. It's not short for Katherine, or anything like that." "Sorry." "No, I won't let you guess. I don't know why I'm asking you to guess. I hate it when people ask you to guess. My parents were huge Vixens from the Void fans. I got born during the broadcast of the last episode of the series and those buggers still blame me for missing it." She cackled again, her head wobbling like it was attached to a spring. "Mum was passing the time being heavily pregnant by making kit forms of Vixens from the Void spaceships. She'd just finished one of the Hydra when she went into labour. I say 'just finished'. She couldn't finish it because there was a piece missing. My grandparents on my mother's side really wanted a grandson. They weren't subtle about it, so when I came out, my parents joked that I'd come out with a piece missing, just like the kit. So that's what I ended up being called. Kit. It was either that or Arkadia, so I think I dodged a raygun there." She grinned and pointed down. "Looks like I've got another piece missing."

**中文理解**：Kit 低头看着她的腿。腿。她的左腿不见了。有她的大腿，她的膝盖……但在那下面，什么都没有。"我的腿没了，"她傻傻地说。"我知道，朝圣者。我肯定这很震惊，但你很幸运。看起来他也想要你的头。"她让头倒在枕头上。"这太糟糕了。我的靴子现在不合脚了。"她发出高音头晕的笑声。"嘿。这很有趣。我有没有告诉你我父母为什么叫我 Kit？""没有。""来，猜猜。它不是 Katherine 的缩写，或任何类似的东西。""抱歉。""不，我不会让你猜。我不知道我为什么要你猜。我讨厌人们让你猜。我父母是 Vixens from the Void 的铁杆粉丝。我出生在该系列最后一集的播出期间，那些混蛋仍然责怪我错过了它。"她又尖笑，头摇晃着像弹簧连接着。"妈妈通过制作 Vixens from 宇宙飞船的套件形式来度过怀孕的时间。她正要完成 Hydra 时，她分娩了。我说'正要完成'。她无法完成它，因为缺少一件。我母亲的祖父母真的很想要一个孙子。他们不委婉，所以我出来的时候，我父母开玩笑说我出来时少了一件，就像套件一样。所以这就是我最终被叫做的。 Kit。要么是那个，要么是 Arkadia，所以我认为我躲过了一劫。"她笑着指向下方。"看来我又少了一件。"

**关键词** | kit forms / Hydra / labour / subtle / dodged a raygun

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"kit forms"（套件形式）暗示了她的"名字来源"——她"叫" Kit，因为她是"套件"。"dodged a raygun"（躲过一劫）这个科幻用语暗示了 Kit 的"幽默"——她"差点"叫"Arkadia"。
