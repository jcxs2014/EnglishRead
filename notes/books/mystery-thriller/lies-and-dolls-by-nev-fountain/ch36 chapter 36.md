# 36. Chapter 36

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Binfire 和 Freya 用信用卡撬开 Kit 的门，Jackie 来电质问，Binfire 用"前女友"和"新女友"的讽刺挂断电话。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Binfire 的闯入

> **原句 1:** Kit's little room in the Furley stables was still, quiet and empty. Then the silence was broken by a tiny click and the front door swung open. "Never fails," said Binfire, stuffing his credit card back into his pocket and peering into the gloom. "Where has she got to?" said Freya, poking her head around Binfire's shoulder. "I told you, pilgrim. She's gone off on a little Thelma and Louise jaunt with the Cosplay Countess."

**中文理解**：Kit 在 Furley 马厩的小房间安静而空荡。然后寂静被一声微小的咔嗒声打破，前门打开了。"从不失败，"Binfire 说，把信用卡塞回口袋，凝视着黑暗。"她去哪了？"Freya 说，从 Binfire 的肩后探出头。"我告诉过你，朝圣者。她和变装伯爵夫人一起去了个小小的《末路狂花》式短途旅行。"

**关键词** | tiny click / Never fails / Thelma and Louise jaunt / Cosplay Countess

**为什么这样写**：Binfire 的"Thelma and Louise"（《末路狂花》）比喻暗示了他的"电影知识"——他用"经典电影"来描述 Kit 和 Tabitha 的"出逃"。"Cosplay Countess"（变装伯爵夫人）这个昵称暗示了 Binfire 的"幽默感"——他给 Tabitha 起了个"外号"。

### 第 2 段：Binfire 的"manoeuvres"

> **原句 2:** "It's true, Frey, scout's honour." He held up his three fingers in a salute. "I passed Lady Tabitha yesterday and told her Kit had been dumped. And I just happened to be on manoeuvres about three hundred hours this morning, crawling through the undergrowth, and hey presto! I spy Lady Tabitha leaving this very room."

**中文理解**："这是真的，Frey，童子军荣誉。"他举起三根手指敬礼。"我昨天遇到 Lady Tabitha 并告诉她 Kit 被甩了。而我今天早上三点正好在演习，爬过灌木丛，嘿！我看见 Lady Tabitha 从这个房间出来。"

**关键词** | scout's honour / manoeuvres / three hundred hours / crawling through the undergrowth / hey presto

**为什么这样写**：Binfire 的"scout's honour"（童子军荣誉）和"manoeuvres"（演习）暗示了他的"军事化"思维——他把"监视"当作"任务"。"three hundred hours"（三点钟）这个军事时间暗示了 Binfire 的"大男孩"性格——他用"军队"语言来"炫耀"。

### 第 3 段：Jackie 的来电

> **原句 3:** They were leaving when the room phone started ringing. Binfire dived across the bed and flicked the switch on the bottom that said 'speaker'. "Kit's room, can I take a message?" "Hello? Who is this?" Binfire and Freya looked at each other in alarm. Jackie Hillier's voice. "Hello, is that Binfire?" Binfire shrugged. "Yep. It's me." "What are you doing in Kit's room?" "Oh, just hanging out, you know, being detectives. 'Cos that's what we do, me and Kit. Holmes and Watson, that's us."

**中文理解**：他们正要离开时，房间电话响了。Binfire 扑到床上，拨动底部标着"扬声器"的开关。"Kit 的房间，要留言吗？""你好？你是谁？"Binfire 和 Freya 惊恐地对视。Jackie Hillier 的声音。"你好，是 Binfire 吗？"Binfire 耸肩。"是的，是我。""你在 Kit 的房间做什么？""哦，只是闲逛，你知道，做侦探。因为这就是我和 Kit 做的事。福尔摩斯和华生，就是我们。"

**关键词** | speaker / Holmes and Watson

**为什么这样写**：Binfire 的"Holmes and Watson"（福尔摩斯和华生）暗示了他的"自我认知"——他把自己和 Kit 比作"经典侦探搭档"。"being detectives"（做侦探）这个短语暗示了 Binfire的"认真"——他把"玩笑"当成了"事业"。

### 第 4 段：Binfire 的"computer-says-no"

> **原句 4:** "Where's Kit? I tried to ring her but she's not answering." "Really? That's a surprise." A pause from Jackie. When she replied there was menace in her voice. "Where is Kit?" "Oh, around. Who wants to know?" "Me." "Right." "So put her on now. Please." "Sorry, pilgrim, as I understand it, phone access is reserved only for current girlfriends. It says here in my database that you're only an ex-girlfriend." He made a computer-says-no noise. "God's sake, you bald twat, put her on the phone!" "No can do. She's out with her sexy new girlfriend having a whale of a time, doing all that stuff lesbians do. You know. Holding hands. Going to Pilates. Making sweet love to the sounds of k.d. lang… Perhaps you should call back later? How about never? Does never sound good?" He slammed his hand on the top of the phone, cutting off the call. He looked up at Freya, who was frozen in horror. "Eek," she squeaked. "Well, that's one problem solved." He beamed.

**中文理解**："Kit 在哪？我试着打给她但她没接。""真的吗？真是个惊喜。"Jackie 停顿了一下。当她回答时，她的声音中带着威胁。"Kit 在哪？""哦，在附近。谁想知道？""我。""好的。""那现在让她接电话。拜托。""抱歉，朝圣者，据我所知，电话接入权只保留给现任女朋友。我的数据库里说你现在只是前女友。"他发出"电脑说不行"的声音。"天哪，你个秃头混蛋，让她接电话！""不行。她正和她性感的新女朋友玩得很开心，做那些女同性恋做的事。你知道。牵手。去普拉提。在 k.d. lang 的声音中做爱……也许你应该晚点再打？怎么样，永远不打？永远听起来好吗？"他猛拍电话顶部，挂断电话。他抬头看着 Freya，她吓呆了。"哎呀，"她尖叫。"好吧，一个问题解决了。"他笑了。

**关键词** | menace / computer-says-no / ex-girlfriend / sexy new girlfriend / frozen in horror

**为什么这样写**：Binfire 的"computer-says-no"（电脑说不行）暗示了他的"幽默"——他用"英国喜剧"（Little Britain）的经典台词来"拒绝"Jackie。"ex-girlfriend"（前女友）和"sexy new girlfriend"（性感新女友）的对比暗示了 Binfire 的"保护欲"——他在"保护"Kit 免受 Jackie 的"骚扰"。"frozen in horror"（吓呆了）暗示了 Freya 的"震惊"——她没想到 Binfire 会"这么过分"。
