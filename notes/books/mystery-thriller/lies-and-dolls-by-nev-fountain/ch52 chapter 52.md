# 52. Chapter 52

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Kit 得知 Tabitha 被肢解的细节（身体部分被冷冻保存），Angelina 到来安慰 Archie。

**情感弧线位置**：震惊与悲痛——Kit 的"blindsided by these emotions"（被这些情绪打得措手不及）和"soft landing"（软着陆）暗示了她的"失落"，Archie 的"terrible business"（可怕的事情）则暗示了他的"绝望"。

**叙事手法**：对话场景（图书馆→入口）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"compartmentalising"（隔离）和"parp-parp"（嘟嘟声）推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Tabitha 的"frozen"

> **原句 1:** "Yes, well. It's not just the hand. There were other parts found downstream." He shuddered. "Her legs too. They're looking for her torso, and her arms. The police told me that the body parts showed signs of having been frozen. As were Mrs Worth's parts—" "Frozen?" "Yes, they think some of the remains of both women are being stored somewhere, in a refrigerated container." He shuddered. "For what ghastly purpose, I shudder to think…" Archie's words slammed into her chest. She's dead. It affected Kit far more than she expected. She was usually excellent at compartmentalising shocking events. Talking about them was tough on her stammer but actually feeling something. That was someone else's problem. Tabitha is dead. Why do I feel so awful? All I did was cuddle her for a few nights. That was all. Why do I feel so blindsided by these emotions? You know why. It's because you saw her as a way out from Jackie. A soft landing. And now she's gone.

**中文理解**："是的，嗯。不只是手。还发现了其他部分顺流而下。"他颤抖着。"她的腿也是。他们在找她的躯干和手臂。警察告诉我，身体部位显示出被冷冻的迹象。Worth 太太的部分也是——""冷冻？""是的，他们认为两名女性的一些遗骸被储存在某个地方，在一个冷藏容器里。"他颤抖着。"为了什么可怕的目的，我颤抖着不敢想……"Archie 的话撞击着她的胸口。她死了。这对 Kit 的影响远超她的预期。她通常很擅长隔离令人震惊的事件。谈论它们对她的口吃来说很困难，但真正感受到某些东西。那是别人的问题。Tabitha 死了。为什么我感觉这么糟？我所做的只是和她依偎了几个晚上。仅此而已。为什么我被这些情绪打得措手不及？你知道为什么。因为你把她看作是摆脱 Jackie 的出路。一个软着陆。现在她走了。

**关键词** | downstream / torso / frozen / refrigerated container / ghastly / compartmentalising / blindsided / soft landing

**为什么这样写**："frozen"（冷冻）和"refrigerated container"（冷藏容器）暗示了凶手的"残忍"——他"保存"了受害者。"compartmentalising"（隔离）暗示了 Kit 的"防御机制"——她用"隔离"来"处理"情绪。"blindsided"（措手不及）和"soft landing"（软着陆）暗示了 Kit 的"失落"——她"失去"了"出路"。

### 第 2 段：Angelina 的"Archipoos"

> **原句 2:** A comical parp-parp came from outside, shredding her gloomy thoughts. She and Archie looked out of the window and saw a tiny green Fiat 500 chugging up the drive, veering from one verge to the other like a rocket-powered tortoise. It wobbled to a halt outside Furley House and a woman in a shapeless red sweater, glasses and bob-cut emerged, carrying a suitcase and a rucksack. "Angelina!" exclaimed Archie. Archie hurried out to meet his fiancée. Angelina ran up the steps and surged towards him, wrapping her arms around his chest, compressing his ribcage and knocking the air out of his lungs. "Oh, Archie! Archie! I'm so sorry!" "I know, darling." "Your poor sister!" "I know." "How could this be happening, munchkin? I don't believe it!" She gave him one last extra-vigorous hug, disengaged and looked earnestly up at her fiancé. "I know Tabitha never liked me, but I hoped in time, after the wedding, we would become friends." "Yes. I hoped that too, Angel." Angelina noticed Kit standing by the entrance and rushed towards her, the huge collar of her jumper concertinaing around her neck. She stopped short of hugging Kit, much to Kit's relief, and gave her a sunny wave. "Hey, Miss Sleuth! So, we meet in person at last!" "So we do." "But not in the best of circumstances, hey?" "No." "Do you have any idea about who's doing these awful things?" "I have a few ideas." "Fab! I'd love to hear them. And I'd love to see more pictures of your dog. But later. I'm going to look after Archipoos – he's had a terrible shock." Archie looked embarrassed. "I'm just fine, button." "No, you're not, big bear. You need me to give you lashings of TLC on toast." She threaded her arm through his and marched him through the entrance to Furley House.

**中文理解**：外面传来一声滑稽的嘟嘟声，打断了她的忧郁思绪。她和 Archie 向窗外望去，看到一辆小小的绿色菲亚特 500 沿着车道嘎吱嘎吱地驶来，像一只火箭驱动的乌龟一样在两侧边缘之间摇晃。它在 Furley House 外摇晃着停了下来，一个穿着无形红色毛衣、眼镜和波波头的女人出现了，提着一个手提箱和一个背包。"Angelina！"Archie 惊呼。Archie 急忙出去迎接他的未婚妻。Angelina 跑上台阶，冲向他，双臂环绕着他的胸部，压缩他的胸腔，把空气从他肺里挤出来。"哦，Archie！Archie！我很抱歉！""我知道，亲爱的。""你可怜的妹妹！""我知道。""这怎么会发生，小甜心？我不相信！"她给了他最后一个格外有力的拥抱，分开并认真地抬头看着她的未婚夫。"我知道 Tabitha 从来不喜欢我，但我希望随着时间推移，婚后我们会成为朋友。""是的。我也希望如此，Angel。"Angelina 注意到 Kit 站在入口附近，向她冲过来，她毛衣的大领子在她脖子周围风琴般折叠。她在拥抱 Kit 之前停了下来，这让 Kit 松了一口气，并向她阳光地挥手。"嘿，小姐侦探！我们终于见面了！""确实。""但不是在最好的情况下，对吧？""不是。""你对谁在做这些可怕的事情有什么想法吗？""我有一些想法。"太好了！我很想听。我还想看看你狗的更多照片。但以后。我要照顾 Archipoos——他受了很大的惊吓。"Archie 看起来很尴尬。"我很好，小纽扣。""不，你不是，大熊。你需要我在吐司上给你大量的温柔呵护。"她把手臂穿过他的手臂，带他穿过 Furley House 的入口。

**关键词** | parp-parp / rocket-powered tortoise / munchkin / Archipoos / button / big bear / lashings of TLC on toast

**为什么这样写**：Angelina 的"rocket-powered tortoise"（火箭驱动的乌龟）暗示了她的"可爱"——她"开车"很"慢"。"munchkin"（小甜心）、"Archipoos"（小阿奇）、"button"（小纽扣）、"big bear"（大熊）这些昵称暗示了 Angelina 的"甜蜜"——她有很多"爱称"。"lashings of TLC on toast"（在吐司上大量的温柔呵护）暗示了 Angelina 的"关怀"——她用"食物"来"安慰"。
