# 39. Chapter 39

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Kenneth Willoughby 的 DVD 自白——他承认破坏人偶，因为童年拍广告的"痛苦经历"；他要求明天中午交赎金，否则销毁最后一个人偶。

**情感弧线位置**：荒诞与威胁——Kenneth 的"asthma inhaler"（哮喘吸入器）和"Murder Mystery weekends"（谋杀之谜周末）暗示了他的"疯狂"与"可怜"，Kit 的"completist"（完美主义者）自嘲则暗示了她的"职业习惯"。

**叙事手法**：DVD 播放场景（博物馆）；单 POV（Kit），通过她的"eyed him warily"（警惕地注视他）和"nervous breakdown"（精神崩溃）观察推进剧情。

## 精读

### 第 1 段：Kenneth Willoughby

> **原句 1:** The picture went into a split screen with a man sliding onto the right-hand side, a moon-faced man in his forties, wearing a red long-sleeved T-shirt. He had a crewcut, which was his only distinguishing feature as his face was bland to the point of annoyance. "My name is Kenneth Willoughby. I thought I'd introduce myself. Though some of you might know my name already." They all looked at each other, mystified. "Any clue?" said Archie. "Nope," said Fenton. "Not one," said Matty. Kenneth Willoughby continued, "You're probably all discussing whether you know me, and most of you are shrugging now. I hate that! I can just imagine you in my mind's eye, shrugging at one another with your blank looks. It drives me mad! I hate you all!"

**中文理解**：画面切入分屏，一个男人滑入右侧，一个四十多岁的圆脸男人，穿着红色长袖 T 恤。他有一个平头，这是他唯一的特征，因为他的脸平淡到令人烦恼。"我叫 Kenneth Willoughby。我想自我介绍一下。虽然你们中的一些人可能已经知道我的名字。"他们困惑地面面相觑。"有什么线索吗？"Archie 说。"没有，"Fenton 说。"一个都没有，"Matty 说。Kenneth Willoughby 继续说，"你们可能都在讨论是否认识我，你们大多数人现在都在耸肩。我讨厌那样！我能在脑海中想象你们，带着茫然的表情互相耸肩。这让我发疯！我恨你们所有人！"

**关键词** | split screen / moon-faced / crewcut / bland / mystified / mind's eye / drives me mad

**为什么这样写**：Kenneth 的"bland to the point of annoyance"（平淡到令人烦恼）暗示了他的"无趣"——他是"隐形人"。"mind's eye"（脑海中的眼睛）这个成语暗示了 Kenneth 的"偏执"——他在"想象"别人的"反应"。"drives me mad"（让我发疯）暗示了 Kenneth 的"不稳定"——他"恨"所有人。

### 第 2 段：Kenneth 的"confession"

> **原句 2:** "I destroyed your dolls because of this – thing – playing beside me. You see this advert? You see the boy! The one who says, 'Not so fast, Medula! We have our laser cannon ready, and it can pulse with a death ray!'? That was me! I was that boy! I may have lost my hair and my rosy cheeks but that's me! That happy smiling child with the full head of blond hair. That advert – that was the last time I was happy!" His face crumpled with rage. "That advert was my introduction to showbiz. It encouraged me to become an actor. And guess what? Spoilers! My life has been miserable ever since! I've spent my whole life working in pubs and doing terrible magic tricks at children's birthday parties, and the only acting jobs…" He threw air quotes into the sky. "… the only 'acting jobs' I've ever got in all those years have been Murder Mystery weekends, corporate training videos and 'ironic' re-enactments of that bloody Vixens from the Void advert!"

**中文理解**："我毁了你们的人偶，因为这个——东西——在我旁边播放。你们看到这个广告了吗？你们看到那个男孩！那个说'别急，Medula！我们的激光炮已经准备好了，它可以发射死亡射线！'的男孩？那是我！我就是那个男孩！我可能失去了我的头发和红润的脸颊，但那就是我！那个有着一头金发的快乐微笑的孩子。那个广告——那是我最后一次快乐！"他的脸因愤怒而扭曲。"那是我进入演艺圈的开始。它鼓励我成为一名演员。你猜怎么着？剧透！从那以后我的生活一直很悲惨！我一生都在酒吧工作，在儿童生日派对上表演糟糕的魔术，而唯一的表演工作……"他向空中抛出引号。"……这些年来我得到的唯一'表演工作'就是谋杀之谜周末、企业培训视频和那个该死的 Vixens from 广告的'讽刺'重演！"

**关键词** | crumpled with rage / showbiz / Spoilers / miserable / Murder Mystery weekends / air quotes / re-enactments

**为什么这样写**：Kenneth 的"Spoilers!"（剧透！）暗示了他的"现代"思维——他用"网络语言"来"表达"愤怒。"Murder Mystery weekends"（谋杀之谜周末）暗示了 Kenneth 的"讽刺"命运——他"表演"谋杀但"真正"谋杀。"air quotes"（引号手势）暗示了 Kenneth 的"嘲讽"——他在"嘲笑"自己的"表演工作"。

### 第 3 段：Kenneth 的威胁

> **原句 3:** "But I'm not without compassion. I'm going to give you a fighting chance to save the last doll. I'm going to send you instructions later. A place to go to, tomorrow. You'd better be there, or the last doll gets it, and the Ceremonial Range will be no more. Gone forever. You hear me? Gone! And I will finally be famous!"

**中文理解**："但我并非没有同情心。我要给你们一个拯救最后一个人偶的机会。我稍后会给你们发指示。明天要去的地方。你们最好在那里，否则最后一个人偶就完了，Ceremonial Range 将不复存在。永远消失。听到了吗？消失！我终于会出名了！"

**关键词** | compassion / fighting chance / Gone forever / famous

**为什么这样写**：Kenneth 的"compassion"（同情心）和"fighting chance"（机会）暗示了他的"扭曲"——他认为自己"仁慈"。"Gone forever"（永远消失）和"famous"（出名）暗示了 Kenneth 的"动机"——他想要"名声"。

### 第 4 段：Kit 的"completist"

> **原句 4:** "I did interview him once." Everyone looked at her. "You interviewed him?" spluttered Saskia. "I interviewed everyone involved in the making of the Braxtons adverts for my podcast, The First Cult is the Deepest." "Really?" Tabitha sounded amused. "You interviewed child actors who appeared in a thirty-second advert, because…?" "I'm a bit of a completist," said Kit simply. "Understatement of the millennium," said Binfire. "They appeared in a terrestrial television broadcast related to the programme, ergo, they're in the extended universe of Vixens from the Void." She frowned. "Though, as far as I remember, Kenneth talked very fondly about recording the advert. He didn't seem angry about doing it."

**中文理解**："我确实采访过他一次。"所有人都看着她。"你采访过他？"Saskia 气急败坏地说。"我采访了所有参与制作 Braxtons 广告的人，为我的播客 The First Cult is the Deepest。""真的吗？"Tabitha 听起来很 amused。"你采访了出现在三十秒广告中的儿童演员，因为……？""我有点完美主义者，"Kit 简单地说。"千年来的轻描淡写，"Binfire 说。"他们出现在与节目相关的地面电视广播中，因此，他们在 Vixens from the Void 的扩展宇宙中。"她皱眉。"不过，据我所知，Kenneth 谈起录音广告时非常深情。他似乎并不生气。"

**关键词** | completist / Understatement of the millennium / terrestrial television broadcast / extended universe / fondly

**为什么这样写**：Kit 的"completist"（完美主义者）暗示了她的"职业习惯"——她"采访"了"所有人"。"Understatement of the millennium"（千年来的轻描淡写）这个夸张说法暗示了 Binfire 的"幽默"——他在"嘲笑" Kit 的"谦虚"。"extended universe"（扩展宇宙）这个科幻术语暗示了 Kit 的"极客"思维——她把"现实"当作"虚构"来理解。

### 第 5 段：Bell 的"breakdown"

> **原句 5:** Reverend Bell struggled to his feet. He drew himself up to his full height and tapped his cane against his boot. In the last few hours, Bell had become something of a caricature of himself, tutting and fussing and adjusting his spectacles like an Edwardian patriarch. Kit eyed him warily. I think he's heading towards a nervous breakdown. "None of this is relevant, is it?" he retorted. "I think the most important matter to address is how to get hold of five thousand pounds by midday tomorrow."

**中文理解**：Reverend Bell 挣扎着站起来。他挺直身子，用拐杖敲打着靴子。在过去的几个小时里，Bell 变成了自己的讽刺画，像爱德华时代的家长一样发出啧啧声、大惊小怪地调整眼镜。Kit 警惕地注视着他。我认为他正走向精神崩溃。"这些都不相关，对吧？"他反驳道。"我认为最重要的事情是如何在明天中午之前拿到五千英镑。"

**关键词** | caricature / tutting / fussing / Edwardian patriarch / eyed him warily / nervous breakdown

**为什么这样写**：Bell 的"caricature"（讽刺画）和"Edwardian patriarch"（爱德华时代的家长）暗示了他的"退化"——他变成了"更夸张"的自己。"nervous breakdown"（精神崩溃）暗示了 Bell 的"状态"——他正在"崩溃"。
