# 32. Chapter Thirty-One

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1948年那不勒斯，Billie 独自寻找 Francesca，走访手套店，最终得到线索指向 Rione Sanità。
- **情感弧线位置**——过渡：Billie 从寻找（Francesca）到接近（找到线索）——她即将找到父亲的情人。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转"——"调查"——Billie 走访手套店；"线索"——指向 Rione Sanità。
- **人物弧线**：Billie 从寻找到接近——她即将找到 Francesca。
- **叙事手法**：调查过程——Billie 走访店铺，寻找线索。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Billie, Ella and Alma were greeted by an unseasonably cloudy and unsettled day in Naples, whitecaps scattering in lines across the harbour. The moored boats outside the Excelsior released rapid, discordant chimes from below their windows and the sense of a brewing storm was in the air.

**中文理解**：Billie、Ella 和 Alma 在那不勒斯迎来了一个异常多云和不平静的日子，白色的浪花在港口散成一线。停泊在 Excelsior 外的船只在他们的窗户下方发出快速、不和谐的钟声，空气中弥漫着即将来临的风暴的感觉。

**关键词**：unseasonably（异常地）；whitecaps（白色浪花）；discordant（不和谐的）；brewing storm（即将来临的风暴）

**为什么这样写**："brewing storm"= Billie 的紧张——风暴即将来临。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"不和谐"=她的心情——她感到不安。

> **原句 2:** After their sightseeing the previous day, Ella and Alma were happy to be left in their hotel room, having, as Ella put it, taken 'more steps in a day than Fred Astaire in all of his movies'. This was certainly not true, but they had covered a lot of ground. This need for her mother to rest suited Billie well as she was keen to return to the search for the mysterious woman and child in that photograph with her father—his lover and his child, she had come to accept—and that was not something she could do with her mother, his widow.

**中文理解**：在昨天的观光之后，Ella 和 Alma 很高兴被留在酒店房间里，正如 Ella 所说，"一天走的步数比弗雷德·阿斯泰尔在他所有电影里走的都多"。这当然不是真的，但他们走了很多路。她母亲需要休息这对 Billie 很合适，因为她渴望继续寻找照片中与她父亲在一起的神秘女人和情人——他的爱人和她的孩子，她已经接受了——这不是她可以与他寡妇母亲一起做的事情。

**关键词**：covered a lot of ground（走了很多路）；widow（寡妇）

**为什么这样写**："widow"= Billie 的考虑——她不想让母亲参与。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"接受"=她的成熟——她接受了父亲的秘密。

> **原句 3:** 'I will step out to make some inquiries with the police today,' Billie told them after sharing a light breakfast. 'You rest, Ella. I want you two to be careful today, promise me. Don't wander too far. Perhaps try another of those wonderful restaurants in the shadow of the castle for lunch,' she suggested.

**中文理解**："我今天要出去向警察打听一下，"Billie 在他们分享了一顿轻松的早餐后告诉他们。"你休息，Ella。我希望你们今天要小心，答应我。不要走得太远。也许可以在城堡阴影下的那些美妙餐厅中再吃一顿午餐，"她建议。

**关键词**：inquiries（打听）；wander（走得太远）

**为什么这样写**："wander"= Billie 的担心——她不想让母亲冒险。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"警察"=她的计划——她想找到 Moretti。

> **原句 4:** Billie paused. 'I'll try not to, but I should look into things,' she said vaguely. 'I'll see if I can get somewhere,' she said, quite sure that she would not get anywhere with the local constabulary until Cooper arrived. She had her left hand in her pocket, fingers crossed. She did dislike misleading them, though lies came increasingly easily these days. She didn't know how she felt about that.

**中文理解**：Billie 停顿了一下。"我会尽量不，但我应该调查一下，"她含糊地说。"我会看看我能不能有所进展，"她说，很确定在 Cooper 到达之前她不会在本地警察局取得任何进展。她的左手在口袋里，手指交叉。她确实不喜欢误导他们，尽管这些天谎言越来越容易了。她不知道她对此有什么感觉。

**关键词**：vaguely（含糊地）；constabulary（警察局）；misleading（误导）

**为什么这样写**："misleading"= Billie 的内疚——她在对母亲撒谎。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"谎言越来越容易"=她的变化——她变得不诚实。

> **原句 5:** Moretti was out there somewhere, and despite how foolish and illogical it would be for him to come after Billie or her family, she couldn't quite rest until she knew he was apprehended. Could he really have come all this way at the behest of someone like Mr Elliott, to shut her up? It seemed like something more. Something personal. It always had felt personal with him, somehow. If his hate could take him this far, she did not know how much further he could go.

**中文理解**：Moretti 在某个地方，尽管他来追 Billie 或她的家人是多么愚蠢和不合逻辑，但在她知道他被捕之前她无法完全休息。他真的可能应 Elliott 先生这样的人的要求走了这么远，来让她闭嘴吗？这似乎更像是别的什么。个人的东西。不知何故，与他有关的事情总是感觉是个人化的。如果他的恨能把他带到这么远，她不知道他能走多远。

**关键词**：apprehended（被捕）；behest（要求）；personal（个人的）

**为什么这样写**："personal"= Billie 的直觉——Moretti 的仇恨是个人化的。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"能走多远"=她的担忧——Moretti 可能更危险。

> **原句 6:** Away from the windswept waterfront and its chiming boats, Naples was quieter, darker, the skies above threatening rain again. Billie bought an umbrella and made her way to the curved shop frontage of Rubinacci, which Peppino assured her was one of Naples' finest tailors. They would surely sell gloves, and perhaps this Francesca had sold them her gloves, and they knew of her there?

**中文理解**：远离风吹过的海滨和它的钟声船只，那不勒斯更安静，更黑暗，天空再次威胁要下雨。Billie 买了一把伞，走向 Rubinacci 的弧形店面，Peppino 向她保证这是那不勒斯最好的裁缝之一。他们肯定卖手套，也许这个 Francesca 把她的手套卖给了他们，他们知道她？

**关键词**：windswept（风吹过的）；frontage（店面）

**为什么这样写**："frontage"=店铺的外观——Billie 在寻找线索。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"手套"=她的线索——Francesca 是手套制造商。

> **原句 7:** Store after store proved a washout. Two hours later, Billie was frowning as she made her way further up the hill. Thus far her conversations had resulted in several dinner invitations from attentive male shopkeepers, but no one recognised the woman and child in the photograph, or much cared to, it seemed. It could sometimes take weeks to get a hit, she knew, but she didn't have that kind of time in Naples.

**中文理解**：店铺接一家店铺被证明是失败的。两小时后，Billie 皱着眉头向山上走去。到目前为止，她的谈话导致了几个细心的男性店主的晚餐邀请，但似乎没有人认出照片中的女人和孩子，或者很在意。她知道有时可能需要几周才能得到线索，但她在那不勒斯没有那种时间。

**关键词**：washout（失败）；hit（线索）

**为什么这样写**："washout"= Billie 的挫败——她找不到线索。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"几周"=她的紧迫——她没有时间。

> **原句 8:** Billie almost walked past the next shop, practically a hole in the wall at the end of the strip, but something made her pause. Inside, a bespectacled and fastidiously coiffed gentleman, wearing a shirt so finely pressed its creases were a knife edge, welcomed her. She handed him the picture of the two women, and he regarded it with interest before returning it to Billie. Unlike the others, he was not hurried, nor hoping to dine with her. There was something in the intensity of his gaze that gave Billie a glimmer of hope.

**中文理解**：Billie 差点走过下一家店铺，几乎是街道尽头墙上的一个洞，但有些东西让她停了下来。里面，一个戴着眼镜、精心梳理的绅士，穿着一件熨烫得如此精细的衬衫，它的折痕是刀锋，欢迎她。她把两个女人的照片递给他，他感兴趣地看了看，然后还给 Billie。与其他人不同，他不匆忙，也不希望和她共进晚餐。他目光中的某种强度给了 Billie 一丝希望。

**关键词**：hole in the wall（墙上的洞）；bespectacled（戴眼镜的）；fastidiously coiffed（精心梳理的）；glimmer of hope（一丝希望）

**为什么这样写**："glimmer of hope"= Billie 的希望——她终于找到了线索。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"刀锋"=店主的精细——他很专业。

> **原句 9:** 'American?' he asked her. 'No, Australian.' She pointed at her father, then the woman and the child. 'Naples. Glove makers.' He nodded. 'I learn English from Americans.' 'Your English is very good,' she remarked, and smiled. He seemed to grow taller. 'The glove-making, Naples is famous for it,' the man pointed out. 'Sì. There is a factory, I believe?' He looked towards the ceiling of his little shop and made a face. 'Omega, yes. Also, kind of a factory, but in homes. Women of Naples make the gloves. They have the finest eye for the work in all the world.'

**中文理解**："美国人？"他问她。"不，澳大利亚人。"她指着她的父亲，然后指着女人和孩子。"那不勒斯。手套制造商。"他点了点头。"我向美国人学英语。""你的英语很好，"她说着，笑了。他似乎长高了。"手套制造，那不勒斯以此闻名，"那人指出。"是的。有一个工厂，我相信？"他看着他的小店的天花板，做了个表情。"Omega，是的。也是一种工厂，但在家里。那不勒斯的女人做手套。她们有世界上最精细的眼光。"

**关键词**：glove-making（手套制造）；finest eye（最精细的眼光）

**为什么这样写**："finest eye"=那不勒斯手套的声誉——质量上乘。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"Omega"=手套工厂——Francesca 可能在那里工作。

> **原句 10:** 'I see. Yes, that is what I am looking for, I think. I think she works from a house.' 'Sì. Yes. The women in their homes. You go to Rione Sanità, maybe you find them there.' He handed the photograph back. 'I think you find them there, yes.' Back to Rione Sanità. There was nothing else for it.

**中文理解**："我明白了。是的，我想这就是我要找的。我想她在家里工作。""是的。是的。女人在家里。你去 Rione Sanità，也许你在那里找到她们。"他把照片还给我。"我想你在那里找到她们，是的。"回到 Rione Sanità。没有别的办法了。

**关键词**：Rione Sanità（Rione Sanità）

**为什么这样写**："Rione Sanità"=线索的指向——Billie 找到了方向。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"没有别的办法"=她的决心——她必须去 Rione Sanità。
