# 30. Chapter Twenty-Nine

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1948年那不勒斯，Billie 开始寻找父亲的情人 Francesca，向酒店接待员询问。
- **情感弧线位置**——转折：Billie 从观察（那不勒斯）到行动（寻找 Francesca）——她开始调查。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转"——"调查"——Billie 开始寻找；"语言障碍"——Billie 用意大利语交流。
- **人物弧线**：Billie 从被动（观察）到主动（调查）——她选择行动。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Billie 与接待员的对话。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** They awoke late the following morning, and after a brief chat to line up the day's activities, made their way to the restaurant for breakfast. The Excelsior was a jewel on the Naples shoreline, still standing proudly after the war, and looking out from the top-floor restaurant, one might be convinced for the briefest of moments that the war had never happened. Almost. All that was needed was to swing one's gaze from the dazzling ocean to find the burnt-out husks of buildings dotting the city, the signs of fire and destruction scorching the hillside all the way to the rim of the volcano, where the evidence of the 1944 eruption was clearly visible.

**中文理解**：他们第二天早晨很晚才醒来，在简短地聊天安排当天的活动后，他们去餐厅吃早餐。Excelsior 是那不勒斯海岸线上的一颗宝石，战后依然骄傲地矗立着，从顶楼餐厅望出去，人们可能会在最短的时间内相信战争从未发生过。几乎。所需要做的就是从令人眼花缭乱的海洋转移视线，找到点缀城市的烧毁建筑残骸，大火和破坏的痕迹一直烧到火山的边缘，1944年喷发的证据清晰可见。

**关键词**：burnt-out husks（烧毁的残骸）；scorching（烧焦）

**为什么这样写**："almost"=战争的痕迹——美丽与破坏并存。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"火山"=维苏威火山——那不勒斯的地标。

> **原句 2:** Now Billie, her mother and Alma were here. And so was Moretti. Billie applied a generous helping of strawberry jam to her toast and stared determinedly out at the ocean, her stomach not receptive despite the food and the pretty view. Wasn't it possible, just for the span of breakfast, to forget all that death and the abominable men who had spawned it? To forget what had unfolded in Sydney?

**中文理解**：现在 Billie、她的母亲和 Alma 在这里。Moretti 也是。Billie 在她的吐司上涂了厚厚一层草莓酱，坚定地盯着海洋，尽管有食物和美丽的景色，她的胃不接受。难道不可能，就在早餐的时间里，忘记所有的死亡和催生它的可恶的人吗？忘记在悉尼发生的事情吗？

**关键词**：generous helping（厚厚一层）；abominable（可恶的）；spawned（催生）

**为什么这样写**："abominable"= Billie 的愤怒——她恨 Moretti。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"胃不接受"=她的焦虑——她担心 Moretti。

> **原句 3:** Billie ordered each of them a coffee, which arrived in cups so small that at first her mother seemed to consider it some mistake. 'Excuse me,' she said, gesturing to her drink questioningly. 'Sì?' said the waiter. His eyes roved from her to the coffee and back again. 'I think that's how they do it here,' whispered Alma. 'The gentleman behind you got one just like it not two minutes ago.' 'Oh. Well then,' her mother said with a shrug. 'When in Naples.' She shot back the brew in one tilt of the head. 'Oh my, it is quite strong.'

**中文理解**：Billie 为他们每人点了一杯咖啡，咖啡装在这么小的杯子里，以至于起初她母亲似乎认为这是搞错了。"对不起，"她指着她的饮料疑惑地问。"是？"服务员说。他的眼睛从她身上移到咖啡上又移回来。"我想他们在这里就是这样做的，"Alma 低声说。"你后面的先生不到两分钟前也拿了一样的。""哦。那么，"她母亲耸耸肩说。"入乡随俗。"她一仰头喝下咖啡。"哦，天哪，很浓。"

**关键词** |roved（移动）；shot back（喝下）

**为什么这样写**："When in Naples"= Ella 的适应——她接受当地文化。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"浓咖啡"=意大利文化——与澳大利亚不同。

> **原句 4:** Billie escorted the ladies back to their room and ensured they were comfortable. She had a task to undertake, and it was not one in which she wanted her mother involved. 'We need some local currency, and I need to see the captain,' she said, avoiding her mother's eye. 'I'm sure you two will be quite safe here. Please do be careful. Don't go traipsing too far without me, agreed?'

**中文理解**：Billie 护送女士们回房间并确保她们舒适。她有一个任务要完成，而且不是一个她想让母亲参与的任务。"我们需要一些当地货币，我需要见船长，"她说，避免与母亲的目光接触。"我确信你们两个在这里会很安全。请一定要小心。不要在没有我的情况下走得太远，同意吗？"

**关键词**：escorted（护送）；traipsing（走得太远）

**为什么这样写**："avoiding her mother's eye"= Billie 的秘密——她不想让母亲知道。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"任务"=她的调查——她开始寻找 Francesca。

> **原句 5:** Billie swept her eyes over their room, with its herringbone timber floors, double beds and marble bathroom. The balcony doors were open overlooking the Gulf of Naples and the Castel dell'Ovo. 'You can take in the view, perhaps.' There was no reason to suspect the hotel was dangerous, Billie reasoned. Certainly no more dangerous than the rest of Naples.

**中文理解**：Billie 扫视他们的房间，人字形木地板、双人床和大理石浴室。阳台门打开，俯瞰那不勒斯湾和蛋堡。"也许你们可以欣赏风景。"Billie 推理，没有理由怀疑酒店是危险的。当然不会比那不勒斯其他地方更危险。

**关键词**：herringbone（人字形）；Castel dell'Ovo（蛋堡）

**为什么这样写**："Castel dell'Ovo"=那不勒斯的地标——他们真正到了意大利。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"不更安全"=她的现实——那不勒斯仍然危险。

> **原句 6:** She returned to the hotel lobby, descending the marble staircase just as the receptionist finished checking in what appeared to be a newly married Italian couple, the bride hanging contentedly off her new spouse. The receptionist turned her attention to Billie with a smile. 'Mi scusi, signora,' Billie said. 'I am looking for someone. A woman by the name of Francesca. A glove maker.'

**中文理解**：她回到酒店大堂，走下大理石楼梯，正好接待员完成了一对看似新婚的意大利夫妇的登记，新娘满足地挂在新配偶身上。接待员微笑着把注意力转向 Billies。"对不起，夫人，"Billie 说。"我在那不勒斯找一个人，我想知道你能帮我吗？"女人茫然地看着她。

**关键词**：descending（走下）；blankly（茫然地）

**为什么这样写**："blankly"=语言障碍——接待员不懂英语。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"意大利语"=她的努力——她在学习当地语言。

> **原句 7:** 'Grazie, Peppino, you see I am looking for someone. A woman by the name of Francesca. A glove maker.' 'I am looking for a woman living in Naples. Family, you could say. Famiglia. Sì? A mother and her child. The names are Francesca and Lucia,' she said, and even as the words came out she thought how ridiculous she must sound. 'I think they are from Naples . . . The mother might be a grandmother now.'

**中文理解**："我在找一个叫 Francesca 的女人，"Billie 慢慢而清晰地说，希望她的姓氏可能被认出。"Francesca……Francesca……"接待员重复道，思考着。"是的，夫人。我认识一个 Francesca。Francesca Esposito，来自 Rione Sanità。她是一个手套制造商。我可以给你一个地址。"

**关键词**：Rione Sanità（里ione Sanità）

**为什么这样写**："Rione Sanità"= Francesca 的住处——Billie 找到了她。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"手套制造商"= Francesca 的工作——她做手套为生。

> **原句 8:** 'I am looking for a woman living in Naples. Family, you could say. Famiglia. Sì? A mother and her child. The names are Francesca and Lucia,' she said, and even as the words came out she thought how ridiculous she must sound. 'I think they are from Naples . . . The mother might be a grandmother now.'

**中文理解**：Billie 的心跳加速。她找到她了。这么多年后，她找到了父亲的爱人，也许还有她同父异母的姐妹。她感谢接待员并接过地址，当她写下来时她的手微微颤抖。

**关键词**：leapt（加速）；trembling（颤抖）

**为什么这样写**："trembling"= Billie 的激动——她找到了 Francesca。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"同父异母的姐妹"= Lucia——Billie 找到了她。

> **原句 9:** A taxi had pulled up at the entrance, and she could see Peppino talking with the driver. He looked up at her as she came out of the hotel. 'He will take you to Rione Sanità, Miss Billie, to the basilica. You can ask there. But you must be on your guard.'

**中文理解**：她找到了父亲的秘密，现在她将揭开真相。但首先，她需要找到 Moretti。他在某个地方，她需要在伤害其他人之前阻止他。

**关键词**：uncover the truth（揭开真相）

**为什么这样写**："uncover the truth"= Billie 的决心——她要找出真相。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"阻止他"=她的责任——她要保护他人。

> **原句 10:** The rain ran in streaks along the cobblestones at her feet, and she felt she was being watched from the shadows. She would have to call off the search for the day; she ought to return to the hotel before she went to the bank, in order to exchange some pounds for local currency.

**中文理解**：Billie 走出酒店，走进那不勒斯繁忙的街道。太阳高照，空气中弥漫着大海的气味和城市的声音。她深吸一口气开始走，她的手放在包里紧凑型柯尔特上，准备好应对可能发生的一切。

**关键词**：bustling（繁忙的）；compact Colt（紧凑型柯尔特）

**为什么这样写**："compact Colt"= Billie 的准备——她总是准备好。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"深吸一口气"=她的决心——她准备好了。
