# 24. Chapter Twenty-Three

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1948年锡兰，Billie 与母亲因社交冲突冷战，随后发现 Lord Fettiplace 因病缺席，向船长询问真相。
- **情感弧线位置**——低谷：Billie 与母亲的关系紧张——她因社交冲突让母亲失望。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："社交冲突"——Billie 与 Lord Fettiplace 的冲突；"疾病"——Lord Fettiplace 的病情。
- **人物弧线**：Billie 从内疚（让母亲失望）到释然（Fettiplace 因病缺席）——她修复了与母亲的关系。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Billie 与船长的对话揭示 Fettiplace 的病情。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** The weather turned the following morning as they were departing the hotel. For her part, Ella maintained her own cold front with Billie as they reboarded the Luxor amid a tropical downpour, blustering winds landing fat drops of rain, splatting on the foliage and returning passengers alike.

**中文理解**：第二天早上他们离开酒店时天气变了。就她而言，Ella 在与 Billie 的冷战中维持着自己的前线，当他们在热带倾盆大雨中重新登上 Luxor 号时，狂风落下大滴的雨水，溅在植物和返回的乘客身上。

**关键词**：cold front（冷战）；downpour（倾盆大雨）；blustering（狂风）

**为什么这样写**："cold front"=母女关系的紧张——Ella 对 Billie 生气。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"冷战"=她的内疚——她让母亲失望。

> **原句 2:** She had ruined her mother's dream evening with her new aristocratic friends in their exotic surrounds, and for that Billie did feel a touch of guilt. She kept her eyes out for Lord and Lady Fettiplace—wanting to show that, for her at least, there were no hard feelings, and to placate Ella in her worries about their plummeting social position.

**中文理解**：她破坏了母亲与她的新贵族朋友在异国情调环境中的梦想夜晚，为此 Billie 确实感到一丝内疚。她留意着 Lord 和 Lady Fettiplace——想表明，至少对她来说，没有恶意，并安抚 Ella 对他们社会地位下降的担忧。

**关键词**：aristocratic（贵族的）；placate（安抚）；plummeting（下降的）

**为什么这样写**："placate"= Billie 的内疚——她想弥补。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"社会地位"= Ella 的焦虑——她担心他们的地位下降。

> **原句 3:** After all, she reasoned, if Lord Fettiplace wanted to behave like a bloated old toad, that was his right, just as it was hers to put him in his place. But as they reboarded, the couple steered clear of them, Lady Fettiplace gripping her silent husband, and with the rain and squalling winds playing havoc with umbrellas and hairstyles alike, it was all Billie could do to hang on to her hat.

**中文理解**：毕竟，她推理，如果 Lord Fettiplace 想表现得像个臃肿的老蟾蜍，那是他的权利，就像她有权让他认清自己的位置一样。但当他们重新登船时，这对夫妇避开了他们，Lady Fettiplace 抓着她沉默的丈夫，随着雨水和呼啸的大风对雨伞和发型造成严重破坏，Billie 只能紧紧抓住她的帽子。

**关键词**：bloated old toad（臃肿的老蟾蜍）；steered clear（避开）；playing havoc（造成严重破坏）

**为什么这样写**："bloated old toad"= Lord Fettiplace 的形象——Billie 不喜欢他。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"避开"= Fettiplace 夫妇的回避——他们不想见 Billie。

> **原句 4:** This ritual was repeated at every mealtime for several days. Lord Fettiplace she could live with offending, if that's what she had achieved at the Mount Lavinia Hotel, but Billie simply could not stand watching her mother survey the room for them at each meal, like a pet-shop puppy waiting for adoption. It had become intolerable.

**中文理解**：这个仪式在接下来几天的每餐时间重复。Lord Fettiplace 她可以冒犯，如果那是她在 Mount Lavinia 酒店取得的成就，但 Billie 简直无法忍受看着她的母亲在每餐时扫视房间寻找她们，就像等待被收养的宠物店小狗一样。这已经变得无法忍受。

**关键词**：ritual（仪式）；intolerable（无法忍受的）

**为什么这样写**："pet-shop puppy"= Ella 的可怜——她在等待被接受。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"无法忍受"=她的内疚——她想让母亲快乐。

> **原句 5:** And so, mustering her courage some five days after they had steamed out of Ceylon, she followed Captain Romano down a narrow corridor that led from the dining hall towards the ship's 'staff only' areas, determined to see if an explanation existed for their absence beyond her own inability to 'know her place'.

**中文理解**：于是，在他们从锡兰出发大约五天后，她鼓起勇气，跟着 Romano 船长沿着一条狭窄的走廊，从餐厅通向船的"仅限员工"区域，决心看看她们的缺席是否有解释，除了她自己无法"认清自己的位置"。

**关键词**：mustering her courage（鼓起勇气）；know her place（认清自己的位置）

**为什么这样写**："know her place"= Billie 的反抗——她拒绝顺从。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"鼓起勇气"=她的决心——她想找到答案。

> **原句 6:** 'Captain Romano! Mi scusi,' she called, trying out one of her new Italian phrases. The captain spun about, a smile spreading over his face. 'What can I do for the lovely Ms Walker?' She had been worried that she had offended him in Ceylon by refusing his offered cocktail, but his clear delight in seeing her put her at ease. He had even remembered her preferred title.

**中文理解**："Romano 船长！对不起，"她喊道，试着她的新意大利语短语之一。船长转过身来，脸上绽开笑容。"我能为可爱的 Walker 小姐做什么？"她曾担心她在锡兰拒绝他提供的鸡尾酒冒犯了他，但他见到她显然很高兴，让她放松了。他甚至记得她喜欢的称呼。

**关键词**：spun about（转过身来）；put her at ease（让她放松）

**为什么这样写**："put her at ease"=船长的善意——他让 Billie 放松。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"意大利语"=她的努力——她在学习当地语言。

> **原句 7:** 'I am sorry to bother you. I know you are a busy man,' she said, smiling. 'I am never too busy for you, signorina. Have you enjoyed the voyage so far?' 'Why yes, very much. Grazie.' Her hand lingered over her chest, and she was suddenly acutely aware of the neckline of her gown. She moved her hand. 'I wanted to ask you, have you seen Lord and Lady Fettiplace, by chance? I haven't laid eyes on them since we reboarded in Ceylon. They have been absent at meals, and my mother in particular has been missing their company.'

**中文理解**："我很抱歉打扰你。我知道你是个忙人，"她笑着说。"我永远不会对你太忙，小姐。到目前为止你享受这次航行吗？""为什么，是的，非常。谢谢。"她的手停留在胸前，她突然意识到自己礼服的领口。她移开了手。"我想问你，你见过 Lord 和 Lady Fettiplace 吗，偶然？自从我们在锡兰重新登船以来，我就没有见过她们。她们一直缺席用餐，尤其是我的母亲一直想念她们的陪伴。"

**关键词**：lingered（停留）；acutely aware（突然意识到）

**为什么这样写**："acutely aware"= Billie 的不自在——她意识到自己的领口。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"缺席"=她的疑问——Fettiplace 夫妇去哪了？

> **原句 8:** The captain's charming smile fell, and he nodded gravely. 'Yes, Ms Walker. I'm afraid Lord Fettiplace has been incapacitated. He is being seen by Doctor Fontana, the ship's doctor. His wife has been attending him.' 'Oh dear. I'm very sorry to hear that. It's nothing serious, I hope?' 'Lord Fettiplace is in capable hands, Ms Walker,' he said, clearly not willing to divulge personal information about one of his most important passengers. 'There's nothing passengers need be concerned about. No one else is sick and there is no cause for alarm. It was a pleasure to see you, as always. Now if you'll excuse me, I am needed on the bridge. Buona giornata.'

**中文理解**：船长迷人的笑容消失了，他严肃地点了点头。"是的，Walker 小姐。恐怕 Lord Fettiplace 已经丧失了能力。他正在被船医 Fontana 医生诊治。他的妻子一直在照顾他。""哦，亲爱的。听到这个我很难过。我希望不严重？""Lord Fettiplace 在能干的人手中，Walker 小姐，"他说，显然不愿意透露关于他最重要的乘客之一的个人信息。"乘客们没有什么需要担心的。没有其他人病了，也没有理由感到恐慌。像往常一样很高兴见到你。现在如果你能原谅我，我需要到驾驶舱去。祝你有美好的一天。"

**关键词**：incapacitated（丧失了能力）；divulge（透露）；cause for alarm（理由感到恐慌）

**为什么这样写**："incapacitated"= Fettiplace 的病情——他病得很重。

**读者视角提示**：船长的"不愿意透露"=他的专业——他保护乘客隐私。

> **原句 9:** He strode off, and Billie was left biting her lip as she watched him disappear up the passageway. Billie knocked on the door of her mother and Alma's cabin, mulling over the captain's news. That would explain the Fettiplaces' absence at least, and her mother could stop her silent treatment. It was an illness, not Billie's tongue.

**中文理解**：他大步走开，Billie 咬着她嘴唇看着他消失在通道里。Billie 敲了敲她母亲和 Alma 的舱门，思考着船长的消息。这至少解释了 Fettiplace 夫妇的缺席，她的母亲可以停止她的冷遇。这是一种疾病，不是 Billie 的舌头。

**关键词**：mulling over（思考）；silent treatment（冷遇）

**为什么这样写**："silent treatment"= Ella 的冷战——她对 Billie 生气。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"疾病"=她的释然——不是她的错。

> **原句 10:** 'That looks like nice reading,' Billie remarked. 'How is everyone feeling?' she asked, trying to keep the note of worry from her voice. 'Her cough is improving,' Alma divulged in a low voice. 'The book is one of mine,' she added. 'Gale Gallagher reminds me of you.' 'Should I be flattered?' 'Oh, why yes,' Alma said cheerfully. 'It's about a woman detective. She's very clever.' 'In that case I am flattered.' And Billie was more than a touch surprised, as well. She hadn't come across a single book that delved into the combination of her occupation and her sex.

**中文理解**："那看起来是好读物，"Billie 说。"大家感觉怎么样？"她问，试图不让担忧的语气出现在她的声音中。"她的咳嗽在好转，"Alma 低声透露。"这本书是我的，"她补充道。"Gale Gallagher 让我想起你。""我应该感到受宠若惊吗？""哦，为什么是的，"Alma 高兴地说。"这是关于一个女侦探的。她很聪明。""那样的话我受宠若惊了。"Billie 也有点惊讶。她从未遇到过一本深入探讨她的职业和性别结合的书。

**关键词**：flattered（受宠若惊）；delved into（深入探讨）

**为什么这样写**："delved into"= Billie 的惊讶——很少有书写女侦探。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"女侦探"=她的身份——她很少在文学中看到自己。
