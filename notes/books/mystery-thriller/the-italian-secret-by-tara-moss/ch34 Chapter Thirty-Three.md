# 34. Chapter Thirty-Three

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Billie 在那不勒斯的地下隧道中追逐 Moretti，最终与他面对面持枪对峙。
- **情感弧线位置**——高潮：Billie 与 Moretti 的追逐——她终于抓住了他。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转"——"追逐"——Billie 追逐 Moretti；"对峙"——两人持枪对峙。
- **人物弧线**：Billie 从被动（被追杀）到主动（追杀 Moretti）——她选择面对。
- **叙事手法**：动作场景——Billie 在地下隧道中追逐 Moretti，气氛紧张。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Billie Walker ran deeper, ever deeper into the tight darkness of the tunnel network beneath Naples. The light from Cooper's torch jumped along the jagged walls in her path, Vincenzo Moretti's footfalls audible in fits and spurts, but she seemed mostly to move on instinct from one place to the other, the occasional flash of torchlight confirming she was still on his tail. Where was he going? Somewhere to hide until he was ready to attack them again, no doubt. She would not let him. She could not. Making her pursuit more difficult, the sound here was muffled, the limestone porous and dank.

**中文理解**：Billie Walker 跑得更深，更深那不勒斯下方的隧道网络中。Cooper 的火炬的光芒在她路上崎岖的墙壁上跳跃，Vincenzo Moretti 的脚步声断断续续地可听见，但她似乎大多凭直觉从一个地方移动到另一个地方，偶尔的火炬闪光证实她仍在他的尾巴上。他要去某个地方躲藏直到他准备再次攻击他们，无疑。她不会让他。她不能。使她的追击更加困难，这里的声音被淹没，石灰石多孔而潮湿。

**关键词**：footfalls（脚步声）；audible in fits and spurts（断断续续地可听见）；muffled（被淹没）；porous（多孔的）

**为什么这样写**："muffled"=隧道的声学——声音被吸收，难以定位。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"直觉"=她的侦探本能——她在追踪。

> **原句 2:** She had not lost him, would not lose him. The tunnel opened up suddenly then, the stone ceiling now high and crossed with supports as Billie passed the rusted husks of cars, and a partly burnt military motorcycle. A string of unlit electric globes hung from a cable stretching along the wall, running down through this underworld on hooks hammered into the rock. Billie's eyes followed it, willed it to spring to life, but the lights remained dark, offering her no assistance.

**中文理解**：她没有失去他，不会失去他。隧道突然打开了，现在石天花板很高，交叉着支撑物，当 Billie 经过汽车的生锈残骸和部分烧毁的军事摩托车时。一串未点燃的灯泡挂在沿墙延伸的电缆上，通过锤入岩石的钩子在这个地下世界下行。Billie 的眼睛跟着它，希望它能活过来，但灯仍然黑暗，没有为她提供任何帮助。

**关键词**：husks（残骸）；underworld（地下世界）；willed（希望）

**为什么这样写**："underworld"=隧道的黑暗——字面意义和隐喻意义。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"希望"=她的绝望——她需要光。

> **原句 3:** To one side she noticed upturned furniture, the torchlight showing glimpses of collapsed hovels that had doubtless once been makeshift homes. Petrol drums were stacked beside a wall made of hand-hewn stone blocks, and she crossed a small bridge, perhaps twelve feet wide, over a dark chasm. Billie shone her torch over the edge to reveal a glint of water far below. On the other side of the bridge in the middle of the path sat a small toy car, about the right size for a toddler. It was brown with rust and one of the wheels was broken off. The sight of it tugged at Billie's heart. Yes, people had lived here only a few years ago. Families. Children. Her sister.

**中文理解**：在一边她注意到翻倒的家具，手电筒光照出摇摇欲坠的小屋的轮廓，这些小屋无疑是曾经的临时家园。汽油桶堆放在一面用手凿石块砌成的墙边，她穿过一座大约十二英尺宽的小桥，越过一个黑暗的深渊。Billie 把手电筒照向边缘，露出下面远处的一丝水光。在桥的另一边，路径中央有一辆小型玩具车，大小适合一个蹒跚学步的孩子。它是棕色的，生锈了，一个轮子断了。这景象揪着 Billie 的心。是的，人们几年前还住在这里。家庭。孩子。她的妹妹。

**关键词**：hovels（小屋）；hand-hewn（手凿的）；chasm（深渊）；toddler（蹒跚学步的孩子）

**为什么这样写**："toddler"= Billie 的心痛——她的妹妹曾住在这里。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"妹妹"= Lucia——她曾住在这黑暗的隧道里。

> **原句 4:** There were openings everywhere around her, and she stopped in her tracks, switching off the torch, the shadowy world plunging instantly into pitch black. She listened, as alert as an owl on the hunt. Her eyes adjusted slowly, but her ears were keen, capturing a muffled grunt. In the tangle of tunnels it was hard to determine its location, but then a glimmer of light flashed from an opening to her right, and she dived in through the limestone opening, back at a run.

**中文理解**：她周围到处都是开口，她停下脚步，关掉了手电筒，黑暗的世界瞬间陷入漆黑。她听着，像狩猎的猫头鹰一样警觉。她的眼睛慢慢调整，但她的耳朵很灵敏，捕捉到一声闷哼。在隧道的混乱中很难确定它的位置，但然后一丝光从她右边的开口闪过，她从石灰石开口冲进去，重新跑起来。

**关键词**：pitch black（漆黑）；keen（灵敏的）；grunt（闷哼）

**为什么这样写**："pitch black"=隧道的黑暗——没有光。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"猫头鹰"=她的警觉——她在追踪。

> **原句 5:** This was another dank tunnel, one that narrowed again but quickly opened up into a cistern of eerie, still water, the smell pungent and mixed with that of human waste. She had switched on the torch again in time to stop herself tumbling into a dark pool. Along one side, to her right, were stinking toilets dug into the rock, and filth. She shivered and was already running again, carefully skirting the edge of the cistern and making chase after that light she had seen, as Moretti forged deeper into the tangle of tunnels, through a series of tighter and tighter cavities, walls damp as if sweating here beneath the earth.

**中文理解**：这是另一个潮湿的隧道，再次变窄但很快打开成一个诡异、静止的水蓄水池，气味刺鼻，与人类垃圾的气味混合。她再次及时打开手电筒，阻止自己掉进黑暗的水池。在她的一边，右边，有挖进岩石的臭厕所和污秽。她发抖了，已经重新跑起来，小心地绕过蓄水池的边缘，追逐她看到的那道光，因为 Moretti 更深地推进到隧道的混乱中，穿过一系列越来越窄的洞穴，墙壁潮湿，仿佛在地下出汗。

**关键词**：cistern（蓄水池）；pungent（刺鼻的）；filth（污秽）；skirting（绕过）

**为什么这样写**："sweating"=隧道的潮湿——墙壁在滴水。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"发抖"=她的恐惧——但她继续追踪。

> **原句 6:** He's not getting away . . . This was another dank tunnel, one that narrowed again but quickly opened up into a cistern of eerie, still water. He's not getting away. There. Finally she could see him ahead. Billie lunged forward, her Colt outstretched. He stopped abruptly and Billie nearly ran into him at the edge of a low railing, blackness beyond and their footfalls still echoing. She stopped so suddenly that the torch fell from between her teeth, hitting the ground at her feet.

**中文理解**：他逃不掉的……他逃不掉的。那里。终于她可以看到他就在前面。Billie 向前冲，她的柯尔特伸出。他突然停了下来，Billie 差点在低栏杆的边缘撞上他，外面是黑暗，他们的脚步声仍在回荡。她突然停了下来，以至于手电筒从她牙齿间掉落，击中了她脚下的地面。

**关键词**：lunged（冲）；abruptly（突然）

**为什么这样写**："lunged"= Billie 的决心——她不让他逃走。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"终于"=她的欣慰——她找到了他。

> **原句 7:** Her Colt was pointed right at his face, and his at hers. After all that pursuit, barrel to barrel they stood, the carved rock surrounding them and Billie's upended torch shining eerily from below, transforming Moretti's face into that of a cartoon devil, his eyebrows stretched up in sharp lines to the top of his receding hairline. Here was a face she had so often seen in the shadows, always just at the edge of her vision.

**中文理解**：她的柯尔特直指他的脸，他的直指她的。在所有这些追逐之后，枪口对枪口他们站着，雕刻的岩石围绕着他们，Billie 倒置的手电筒从下面发出怪异的光芒，把 Moretti 的脸变成了卡通魔鬼的脸，他的眉毛以尖锐的线条向上延伸到他的发际线。这是一个她经常在阴影中看到的脸，总是在她视野的边缘。

**关键词**：barrel to barrel（枪口对枪口）；receding hairline（后退的发际线）

**为什么这样写**："barrel to barrel"=对峙的紧张——两人随时可能开枪。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"卡通魔鬼"= Moretti 的邪恶——他的脸在光线下扭曲。

> **原句 8:** 'You killed her. You killed Darlene Elliott,' Billie spat, breathless. He cocked his head, oddly at ease now despite his panting, pointing the weapon at her casually, as though it didn't matter to him one way or another the death that awaited should one sudden movement see them both squeeze those triggers. There would be no recovery from a shot to the head at this range.

**中文理解**："你杀了她。你杀了 Darlene Elliott，"Billie 唾弃道，气喘吁吁。他歪着头，奇怪地放松，尽管他喘着气，随意地用武器指着她，好像无论哪种方式等待他的死亡都无所谓，如果他们中的任何一个突然动作看到他们两个都扣动扳机。在这个范围内，头部中弹是无法恢复的。

**关键词**：cocked his head（歪着头）；casually（随意地）

**为什么这样写**："casually"= Moretti 的危险——他不在乎死亡。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"无法恢复"=对峙的致命性——他们可能都会死。

> **原句 9:** 'It wasn't easy, that one,' Moretti said, as if it were nothing to him. 'She had friends. She knew people.' He smiled, a slow, terrible smile. 'But you, you I will enjoy. You have caused me so much trouble. And now, here you are, all alone in the dark with me. No detective inspector to save you this time.'

**中文理解**："那不容易，那个，"Moretti 说，好像对他来说没什么。"她有朋友。她认识人。"他笑了，一个缓慢、可怕的笑容。"但是你，我会享受你的。你给我带来了这么多麻烦。现在，你在这里，独自在黑暗中与我在。这次没有侦探总监来救你。"

**关键词**：terrible smile（可怕的笑容）

**为什么这样写**："terrible smile"= Moretti 的残忍——他享受杀戮。

**读者视角提示**：Moretti 的"享受"=他的疯狂——他想杀 Billie。

> **原句 10:** 'So you poisoned that young woman, on behalf of her husband, and then you ran off like a coward into the night, too afraid to face her, face any of us.'

**中文理解**："所以你替她丈夫毒死了那个年轻女人，然后你像懦夫一样逃进夜色，太害怕面对她，面对我们任何人。"

**关键词**：on behalf of（替）；coward（懦夫）

**为什么这样写**："coward"= Billie 的指控——Moretti 是懦夫。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"懦夫"=她的勇气——她面对面指控他。
