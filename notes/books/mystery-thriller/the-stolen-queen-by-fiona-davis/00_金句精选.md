# 《The Stolen Queen》金句精选

① The staff meeting of the Metropolitan Museum’s Department of Egyptian Art was supposed to start at ten, which meant associate curator Charlotte Cross arrived at nine to prepare her colleagues for battle.（ch01）

**中文**：埃及部员工会议定在十点开，这意味着副策展人 Charlotte Cross 九点就到了，好让同事们做好战斗准备。
**上下文**：ch01 周一闭馆日，埃及部的会议定在十点，她九点就到。
**为什么重要**：开篇第一句就把人物钉在她的功能上——不是职位，是"替所有人打前站"。全书的权力关系从这一句开始运转。
**呼应关系**：与 You don’t have proof. Your theory is all well and good, but it’s just a theory.（ch04）她拿证据去讲道理、却被一句"不过是个理论"挡回，正好一进一退。

② The curse of Hathorkare hadn’t ended with the death of the earl’s widow. Charlotte had fallen under its spell as well.（ch01）

**中文**：Hathorkare 的诅咒并没有随着伯爵遗孀之死而终结。Charlotte 同样落入了它的魔力之下。
**上下文**：ch01 站在 Cerulean Queen 前，想起伯爵夫妇的离奇死法。
**为什么重要**：把"诅咒"从传说续写到当下——叙述者亲口承认自己也中了咒。全书往后所有的"不能回去"都由这一句担保。
**呼应关系**：She carried the curse with her, and simply couldn’t risk it.（ch07）她不敢答应的那场婚事，是同一句咒语的第二次收账。

③ Charlotte was certain Leon wouldn’t have made the request if she had been a man.（ch02）

**为什么重要**：全书对性别壁垒最不客气的一句判断，而且它出自叙述者的旁白，不是人物的抱怨——不需要争辩，只需要陈述。
**呼应关系**：与 “Exactly. Saukemet II was worried about his son being trounced by a female rival, not angry at his long-dead stepmother. And to think it was right under my nose all along, locked away in storage, long forgotten.（ch30）Saukemet II 忌惮的"女性对手"，隔着三千年说的是同一件事。

④ You don’t have proof. Your theory is all well and good, but it’s just a theory.（ch04）

**呼应关系**：It only took her a few seconds to make up her mind. “I’m going to Egypt,” she said. The words came out gravelly, like she had sand in her throat.（ch20）她终于订下机票，是对这一句的正面回答。

⑤ Fashion must be the most intoxicating release from the banality of the world.（ch06）
