# 《The Stolen Queen》概述

## 一、故事梗概

**一九七八年的周一，一个提前一小时到岗的人。** 大都会博物馆埃及部的员工会议定在十点，副策展人 Charlotte Cross 九点就到了——不是勤奋，是替所有人打前站。The staff meeting of the Metropolitan Museum’s Department of Egyptian Art was supposed to start at ten, which meant associate curator Charlotte Cross arrived at nine to prepare her colleagues for battle.（ch01）

**同一天，库房里出现了一件不可能出现的东西。** 一件匿名借展的金玻璃宽领项链被抬上工作台，Charlotte 一眼认出它，甚至不用走近就知道右下角缺了一枚护符——因为她 1936 年在埃及亲眼看着它出土，一年后又亲眼看着它沉入尼罗河底。And she’d last seen it a year later, right before it was lost at the bottom of the Nile.（ch01）

**倒回一九三六年。** 她为被眼镜蛇咬伤的贝都因人打了一针抗蛇毒血清，换来部族的庇护誓言；被同事 Leon 抢走发掘位后，她自己在帝王谷入口附近一锄头挖出了此前无人知晓的墓门台阶。A door to a previously undiscovered tomb.（ch02）

**一九三七年的尼罗河。** 婚后的 Charlotte 带着三个月大的女儿 Layla 随 Henry 北上，风暴夜里两船相撞；海水漫上甲板前，她把孩子递到丈夫怀里，自己被倒下的躺椅困住——那是她最后一次看见女儿的手。her child’s chubby, sweet hand stretched out over his shoulder, palm open wide, reaching for her mother.（ch11）

**另一条线从纽约的地下室开始。** 十九岁的 Annie Jenkins 白天替房东太太打扫、晚上端盘子，养着把自己当少女的母亲；房东太太派她连夜把一条羽毛围巾送进大都会博物馆，指名交给 Diana。Back downstairs, Mrs. H placed the boa into a shopping bag. “Now take it to the Met Museum.”（ch03）

**晚宴之夜，青金女王失窃。** Annie 负责的蝴蝶环节被换成了一箱飞蛾，扑向灯光与宾客；等 Charlotte 循着尖叫下到地下展厅，Hathorkare 的残像已经不在底座上了。The Cerulean Queen had been plucked right off its stand.（ch16）

**开罗与卢克索：追查、反转、再反转。** 两人追着走私链条穿过帝王谷与开罗市集，在画廊后堂撬开一尊分量不对的石膏像——青金石与女王的弧形嘴唇重新出现。A large chunk dropped onto the table, and Annie spied a section of shiny blue stone. As another came loose, the curved lips of the queen emerged.（ch27）

**幕后的人就在眼皮底下。** 主谋是顶着考古学者身份的 Mona，隶属以归还埃及文物为名的地下组织 Ma'at；她在被捕时丢下的话，把 Charlotte 引向四十一年前那场船难。I’ll never tell. You go after my mother? This is what you get in return. And to think she was right under your nose.（ch29）

**收束在一九七九年的卢克索河边。** 一间渔夫小屋旁，Winter Palace Hotel 的职员 Fatima 正是当年被渔夫救起、抚养长大的 Layla。Charlotte rose and crossed the space between them, softly touching Fatima’s hair. “I’m sorry we lost you for so long. I’ve missed you terribly.”（ch34）

## 二、三个主题

**一、被抹去的女人。** Hathorkare 的雕像被从神庙墙上凿去，Charlotte 的翻案理论被上司用一句"不过是个理论"挡回，Annie 被母亲、Vreeland 与 Charlotte 轮流用完就丢——三个不同时代的女人，遭遇的是同一种删除。You don’t have proof. Your theory is all well and good, but it’s just a theory.（ch04）

**二、诅咒的两种用法。** 小说给了诅咒一个世俗的版本：它不是神罚，而是一个人不敢再靠近某块土地的理由。Charlotte 把 1936 年那场事故活成了随身的行李。The curse of Hathorkare hadn’t ended with the death of the earl’s widow. Charlotte had fallen under its spell as well.（ch01）

**三、母亲的两种写法。** 全书有两对母女互为镜像：Joyce 用尽办法把 Annie 从自己的生活中删掉，Charlotte 用尽办法把失踪的女儿找回来；而真相揭晓时，重逢并没有写下母女相认的圆满。What an awful reunion, if that was true. A mother and daughter staring at each other, an intractable void between them. A far cry from the tear-filled hugs that Charlotte had envisioned the few times she’d allowed herself to imagine such a thing.（ch28）

## 三、人物弧光

### Charlotte Cross

全书的第一视角之一，也是那个替所有人收拾残局的人。她的弧线从守护别人的遗产，走到认领自己的过去：

- 起点：The staff meeting of the Metropolitan Museum’s Department of Egyptian Art was supposed to start at ten, which meant associate curator Charlotte Cross arrived at nine to prepare her colleagues for battle.（ch01）
- 转向：It only took her a few seconds to make up her mind. “I’m going to Egypt,” she said. The words came out gravelly, like she had sand in her throat.（ch20）
- 最低点：Not that the pain eased in any way, but instead of eating away at her insides like an ulcer, it had hardened into a cyst.（ch14）
- 落点：Charlotte rose and crossed the space between them, softly touching Fatima’s hair. “I’m sorry we lost you for so long. I’ve missed you terribly.”（ch34）

### Annie Jenkins

另一条线的视角人物，十九岁，被三个女人轮流需要、也轮流丢下。她的弧线是从"被需要才有位置"走到"自己决定站在哪里"：

- 起点：Back downstairs, Mrs. H placed the boa into a shopping bag. “Now take it to the Met Museum.”（ch03）
- 转向：Well done, girl. Show up tomorrow at noon in my office.（ch09）
- 最低点：She was a tired, confused, and unemployable nineteen-year-old, without a home or a job, who had just fallen over a mummified dead goose in a scary hole in the ground.（ch21）
- 落点：By the way, Mrs. Vreeland called me a little while ago. She’s trying to track you down. Looks like you have a second chance.（ch31）

### Henry Smith

缺席了四十一年的人。他既是走私案的当事人，也是唯一能回答"女儿去哪了"的人：

- “What are you doing here?” Charlotte asked Henry. “After forty-one years. Forty-one years. Now you show up?”（ch32）
- I held on to her for as long as I could, but she was sucked out of my arms by the current. I promise you, I tried.（ch33）

### Hathorkare

从未出场，却是全书的题眼。她的残像只留下嘴唇，而 Charlotte 的研究要还给她的是名字：

- And what lips they were: beautifully curved and utterly sensuous.（ch01）
- “Exactly. Saukemet II was worried about his son being trounced by a female rival, not angry at his long-dead stepmother. And to think it was right under my nose all along, locked away in storage, long forgotten.（ch30）

## 四、叙事结构

- **双线双视角交替**：Charlotte 线（1978 纽约与 1936–1938 埃及）与 Annie 线（1978 纽约与 1979 埃及）逐章轮换，两条线在 Met Gala 失窃夜合流。
- **一条物证链贯穿全书**：宽领项链（1936 出土、1937 沉没、1978 匿名借展）与 Cerulean Queen（残片、失窃、石膏像里重现）互为镜像，两件 Hathorkare 遗物各自走完一圈。
- **两个时间锚**：1936–1938 的埃及与 1978–1979 的纽约和开罗，中间隔着四十一年与一场船难。
- **一桩案子的两层解法**：表层是谁偷了青金女王，里层是四十一年前那个夜里谁活了下来。
