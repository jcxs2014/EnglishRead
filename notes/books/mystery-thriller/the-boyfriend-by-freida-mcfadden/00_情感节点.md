## 节点一：序章的最后一句（ch01）

Tom 从四岁起爱着 Daisy。序章用整页近乎纯真的初恋独白换来最后一句的翻面——他怕的不是被拒绝，是她答应。

> "I am desperately, painfully, completely, and stupidly in love."（ch01）

> "What I am most scared of is that if I lean in to kiss Daisy, she will let me do it."（ch01）

## 节点二：砖墙边的陌生人（ch03–ch04）

第一次约会被堵进砖墙，一个路人喊停了他；Sydney 追出去道谢，对方却连名字都没留。这是全书唯一一次她得到"意外的好运"。

> "God, why is he so strong?"（ch04）

> "You know you’ve got it bad when even looking at a girl’s name makes your heart skip a beat."（ch64）

> "Daisy would be so easy to kill. It would barely even be a challenge."（ch14）

## 节点四：第一次动手（ch27）

父亲酒后扬言要去"问候"Daisy。他顺手抓起切牛排的刀，扎进去，**转了一下**，割开喉咙。

> "I have never hated him quite as much as I do when I plunge the blade of the knife deep into his gut."（ch27）

> "You need to get out of there right now!!!"（ch59）
