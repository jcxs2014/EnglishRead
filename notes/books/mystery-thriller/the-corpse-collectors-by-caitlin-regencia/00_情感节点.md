# 《The Corpse Collectors》情感节点

## 节点一：林边阳光与战时（ch01-ch02）

**叙事概括：** 全书从Alliera与Elora的日常开始，李源在阳光下散心，Elora告知加入Travelers，Alliera和解于送别。

**关键引语：**

> "Warm sunlight dappled through the trees of Mindanforth Forest, spattering luminous patterns upon the great gray rocks that hung above a roaring waterfall."（ch01）

> "“It’s a good opportunity, Vix. You can’t tell me that it isn’t. I’ll go.”"（ch02）

## 节点二：深情与紧而不散（ch03-ch04）

**叙事概括：** 全书从Alliera与Dallin相遇的瞬间开始，李源眼眸薰衣草色的时间暂停变成了两人羁绊的第一页。几个月后，她回家发现Vix把屋子翻了个底朝天，藤蔓绑了屋里的东西。

**关键引语：**

> "Her world fell away, and she was trapped in the vampire’s lavender-tinged eyes. Planets aligned and time stood still. Her heart may have even ceased to beat."（ch03）

> "Having not been home in the five days she’d been assigned to the witch’s care, it took Alliera by surprise that her front door was wide open."（ch04）

## 节点三：田间偷听与围合（ch05-ch06）

**叙事概括：** 全书从Vix在田里偷听田间声开始，Kastral与Nij被指认，权臣们商议之，无路可退。

**关键引语：**

> "We have discovered what is happening to the vampires. It was us all along."（ch05）

> "I think I know who did it—is doing it. Abducting and killing the vampires."（ch05）

## 节点四：斗篷山洞与血祭（ch07-ch08）

**叙事概括：** 全书从洞里层层嵌纳的仪式开始，在阳光的水下时隔里呼吸。隧道无灯，信任是最后的小火种。

**关键引语：**

> "There was a woman hanging upside down from thick vines knotted around what looked like a butcher’s hook embedded into the ceiling."（ch07）

> "She would never see it coming. The man leading them could drop his bag, turn, and murder them both before she had time to react."（ch08）

## 节点五：水下八小时（ch08）

**叙事概括：** 全书从水下起浪开始，风把剑吹动了那双分不清边界的手。

**关键引语：**

> "Should take about eight hours now to reach the city. Rest while you can."（ch08）

> "He yanked his hood from his head, panting to catch his breath, and Alliera gaped. Solimin Cresta. A fisherman."（ch08）

## 节点六：酒会与夜空（ch10-ch11）

**叙事概括：** 全书从地下酒会开始，Kastral给所有人添血，夜空在门外阴暗。

**关键引语：**

> "As soon as he did, Lomarda was there, handing her a shot glass full of Vanyazara’s blood and kissing her cheek."（ch10）

> "Pain surged through Alliera’s veins, and she cried out, clutching her stomach as she crumpled to the floor."（ch10）

## 节点七：奖牌与手背（ch11）

**叙事概括：** 全书从回到家（Vix放走）的狭小开始，国王派人回来装常态。

**关键引语：**

> "The King sent us back to put up a front of normalcy. To—"（ch11）

> "Vampire. They drain their victims and keep their blood."（ch11）

## 节点八：书写失败与颠倒（ch12-ch13）

**叙事概括：** 全书从满地被洗白的纸开始，重口吸血鬼血渍啜取一举上光。

**关键引语：**

> "Fear, doubt, shame, distrust, lack of confidence, jealousy, fear of failure—Alliera painted the words and let herself feel each emotion as she did so, picturing the feelings running down her arm, through the brush, and into the paint."（ch12）

> "I will go. I will deliver our salvation, Kastral."（ch13）

## 节点九：血池与地牢（ch15-ch17）

**叙事概括：** 全书从池子与帘子绕成的昏黄开始，地牢钥匙空巢里一句有距离感的hello换回了生还的承诺，黑夜的哭声渐渐越来越响。

**关键引语：**

> "Her friend, his skin a sickly dusty color and dark curls floating around him in a halo, bobbed in the stone bath full of even more thick crimson."（ch15）

> "Dallin and Elora are missing."（ch17）

## 节点十：决战与归途（ch18-ch22）

**叙事概括：** 全书从决战开始的羊毛斗篷在水下化成风开始，决战打响了，Elora重伤不治。

**关键引语：**

> "Sprinting toward her, her camel-colored trousers and white shirt covered in blood, was Elora."（ch18）

> "Standing on the bow of the ship with the commander and Kyrienot but a few minutes later, the cool wind whipping Alliera’s dark curls from where they lay against Dallin’s leather-clad shoulder, she thought that maybe someday saving their people would feel worth it."（ch22）
