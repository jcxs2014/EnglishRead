# 情感节点

## 节点一：Prologue——童年创伤的揭露

**叙事概括**：十三岁的 Nova 躲在钢琴下躲避暴怒的父亲，揭露了一个虐待家庭中孩子的恐惧与无助。父亲的暴力与性侵、母亲的共谋与利用、以及一个孩子在绝境中萌生的愤怒与求生意志——"bide my time" 是全书人物弧线的种子。

**关键引语**：
- "I'm curled up in a corner, trying to stay as small as possible as my father rages at my mother."
- "I don't like how my father looks at me, it reminds me of how he comes into my room at night, and how much it hurts."
- "I hate them. It's a growing anger that exists because I know there's no one to save me."

---

## 节点二：Chapter One——Hollis 被绑架

**叙事概括**：Hollis 在光天化日下被绑架，Malcolm 与她的朋友们在雪地中寻找线索，同时 Nova 首次得知自己是 Hollis 的双胞胎妹妹。"抓错人"的设定制造了核心悬念：Hollis 能否在身份暴露前获救？

**关键引语**：
- "She's gone."
- "My omega fought. It looks like she put up a big fight, yet no one witnessed it, because she's gone."
- "It should have been me."

---

## 节点三：Chapter Four——Hollis 的复仇

**叙事概括**：Hollis 在 Domino 的宅邸中审讯并虐杀 Turbis 和 Louisa，最后与 pack 重聚。从"幸存者"变为"审判者"——她不仅复仇，还策划摧毁 Domino 家族的遗产。

**关键引语**：
- "Class is now in session," I murmur.
- "Say hello to the devil for me," I murmur, my knife ending his life with a slow slice through his carotid artery.

---

## 节点四：Chapter Seven——Nova 的发热期

**叙事概括**：Nova 独自在营地经历发热期，Lars 和 Caleb 找到她并将她送往 Tyde 的住处。这是 Nova 第一次被 alpha 照顾，也是她开始接受"我需要他人"的转折点。

**关键引语**：
- "I can do this," I gasp, bending forward as I writhe on the bed. I'm a fucking mess.
- "Tell me where you are, Princess," he growls. "Caleb and I have been driving for hours, hoping that we'll be able to find you."

---

## 节点五：Chapter Thirteen——Nova 的创伤揭露

**叙事概括**：Tyde 在 Nova 的噩梦中体验了她的记忆，发现她童年被性侵的真相，以及她试图自杀的经历。Tyde 决心用一生来治愈她的创伤。

**关键引语**：
- "You're so fucking strong," I say, kissing her hard. "No, I'm not," she says, grabbing my hand and gently rubbing my fingers over her inner wrist. There's an old scar, and it goes across her wrists.

---

## 节点六：Chapter Twenty-Five——Bond 拒绝的诊断

**叙事概括**：Nova 前往医院检查 bond 咬痕，Dr. Royal 诊断她为"拒绝 bond"，需要与 alpha 们同住来修复关系。Nova 决定面对自己的恐惧。

**关键引语**：
- "You're rejecting your bite, Nova. While that's the bad news, this can be turned around."
- "It feels like you're torn apart," he says, wincing. "I don't know why there aren't more records of it."

---

## 节点七：Chapter Thirty-Three——Nova 的自卫

**叙事概括**：Nova 为 Adira 接生后遭遇袭击，她成功自卫并杀死了袭击者。这是 Nova 从"受害者"变为"幸存者"的关键时刻。

**关键引语**：
- "Point and shoot, Little Warrior. Even if you miss by a mile. It'll hopefully make him leave," Lars says.
- "I think he's dead," says an accented voice.

---

## 节点八：Chapter Thirty-Four——找到归属

**叙事概括**：Hollis 与 Nova 讨论搬家问题，Pack Ledger 提出让他们在隔壁建房。四人成为 Pack Finnegan，Nova 找到了真正的"家"。

**关键引语**：
- "I don't want to move but my little house is too small for my pack," she groans.
- "Our name," Lars decides. "It has power in both the community and the mafia families. We're Pack Finnegan, baby."
- "Life will never be boring at least. Sometimes, a lifetime of adventures with people you can trust is all you can ask for."
