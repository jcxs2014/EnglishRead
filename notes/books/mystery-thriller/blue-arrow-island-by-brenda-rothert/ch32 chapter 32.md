# 32. Chapter 32

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：Team meeting 后大家 commit to searching for McClain；Marcus reveals McClain sank the scientists' boat and the aromium flower secret；pairs 分配后，Marcus 向 Briar 坦白他的 attraction。

**情感弧线位置**：情感高潮——Marcus 的 explicit confession of attraction（"I think about you constantly. You're the most beautiful woman I've ever seen."）；两人关系从 tentative partners 变成 mutual confession。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**：Fated Partners（mutual confession）；Villain Backstory 揭示（McClain 是 saboteur）。

**人物弧线**：Marcus 展示了 vulnerability——他对 Briar 的 attraction 是真实的，不只是 aromium。他担心 Briar 会 judge the version of him with aromium on。

**叙事手法**：单 POV（Briar）；Team meeting scene；Private confession scene；两段式结构（public commitment → private vulnerability）。

## 精读

### 第 N 段：Team Commitment

> "I'm still in, brother. Go big or go home, right? And I guess going big is our only choice by default, because we sure as hell aren't going home." Adele speaks up next... One by one, everyone in the room tells Marcus they're with him.

**中文理解**："我还是加入了，兄弟。要么大干一场要么回家，对吧？我猜大干一场是我们唯一的选择，因为我们肯定不会回家。"Adele 下一个发言……一个接一个，房间里的每个人都告诉 Marcus 他们站在他这边。

**关键词**：Go big or go home / one by one / we're with him

**为什么这样写**：这是团队凝聚力的时刻——每个人都在危机中 commit to Marcus 和 mission。"One by one"强调了团结的 gradual nature，不是强迫而是自愿。

---

### 第 N 段：Aromium 的真相

> "The aromium is what's dangerous... You don't start back at zero when it's reactivated; you pick up right where you left off. And if you were feeling volatile when it was turned off, you'll be right back there. You'll only want to fuck and fight."

**中文理解**："aromium 才是危险的……当你重新激活它时，你不会从零开始；你会从你停止的地方继续。如果你之前感觉 volatile，当它重新开启时你会回到那种状态。你只会想要性和斗争。"

**关键词**：pick up right where you left off / volatile / only want to fuck and fight

**为什么这样写**：这是 Marcus 对 aromium 的 honesty warning——reactivating 不是简单的 restart，而是从之前的状态 continue。"Only want to fuck and fight"是 stark description of aromium's effects。

---

### 第 N 段：McClain 的 Sabotage

> "He sank their boat." Marcus says. "It was a submarine, and he was able to sabotage it from here." McClain knew that would happen if they made it back, so ... they didn't.

**中文理解**："他把他们的船弄沉了。"Marcus 说。"那是一艘潜水艇，他能够从这里 sabotage 它。"McClain 知道如果他们回到大陆就会发生那种事，所以……他们没有。

**关键词**：He sank their boat / sabotage / they didn't

**为什么这样写**：这是 McClain 作为 villain 的最终揭示——他不只是逃离，他是 murder。"They didn't"是 euphemism for murder：McClain 杀了那些科学家来 prevent them from alerting Whitman。

---

### 第 N 段：Marcus 坦白 Attraction

> "Aromium amplifies attraction. That's gonna be an issue." I can't help it—my smile widens. This massive, muscled, scowling man is wrecked over this conversation, of all things.

**中文理解**："Aromium 会放大 attraction。这会是个问题。"我忍不住——我的笑容扩大了。这个高大肌肉发达、总是皱眉的男人被这个对话毁了。

**关键词**：amplifies attraction / gonna be an issue / wrecked over this conversation

**为什么这样写**：Marcus 的 worry 揭示了他的 emotional intelligence——他担心 aromium 的 effects on their relationship。他 not just physical attraction，他真的 care about her opinion of him。

---

### 第 N 段：Marcus 的 Love Confession

> "I think about you constantly. You're the most beautiful woman I've ever seen. Sometimes I forget to breathe when I'm looking at you. And it's not just that. You're also strong and smart and ... fuck. You're so blindly brave I want to tie you up sometimes to keep you out of trouble."

**中文理解**："我不断想着你。你是我见过最美的女人。有时候我看着你会忘记呼吸。还不止如此。你也很强壮和聪明……操。你太盲目勇敢了，我有时候想把你绑起来让你不要惹麻烦。"

**关键词**：think about you constantly / most beautiful woman / strong and smart / blindly brave

**为什么这样写**：这是 Marcus 的 explicit confession——不只是 physical attraction，他爱她的一切。"Blindly brave"是 Briar 的 perfect summary：她 brave 到不顾危险，这既是她的 best quality 也是最让他担心的地方。
