# 49. The Masterpiece, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Mike Lunde 在标本店为 Patterson 市长一家展示他们宠物狗 Quentin 的标本；他拿起步枪，揭示了真相——他要用市长自己的枪杀死他，就像市长的枪支政策害死了他的家人一样。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 对决——Lunde 控制了市长一家，准备"揭幕"他的"杰作"（杀死市长）。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的独白"——Lunde 揭示了市长的枪支政策如何间接导致了他家人的死亡；"宠物标本"——被标本化的宠物狗是 Lunde 的"艺术品"，也是他复仇的象征。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角——Jill Patterson 的视角，通过她的眼睛看到 Lunde 的变化。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "‘It's lovely,’ said Jill Patterson as she stroked the dog. ‘Absolutely lovely.’ ‘Thank you,’ said Mike Lunde. The two of them and the children, Siri and Simon, sat in the store in a little circle around Quentin."

关键词：lovely / stroked / dog / Siri / Simon / store / circle / Quentin

为什么这样写：Jill 的"lovely"是可爱——她说狗可爱。"Quentin"是狗的名字——他们的宠物狗。

> **原句 2:** "Jill hadn't replied. After all, this might be the last time they would ever see this nice taxidermist, whom everyone in the family had become so fond of. Mike had been to their home in Dellwood and listened as she and Kevin and the children talked about Quentin."

关键词：last time / taxidermist / fond of / Dellwood / Quentin

为什么这样写：Jill 的"fond of"是喜欢——家人都喜欢 Lunde。"Dellwood"是社区名——市长住的社区。

> **原句 3:** "Of course, neither Jill nor the children had been present when the grave was opened. After a mere fourteen days the fur was still pretty much unchanged, and Mike had said it wouldn't be a problem to repair any damage. They agreed to use as much of Quentin as possible, not just the teeth but the whole skull. That way she felt she could tell the children it wasn't just a copy of Quentin, it really was Quentin."

关键词：present / grave / opened / fourteen days / fur / unchanged / repair / damage / as much as possible / teeth / whole skull / copy

为什么这样写：Jill 的"skull"是头骨——他们用了狗的头骨。"copy"是复制品——不是复制品，是真的。

> **原句 4:** "Simon stood in front of the chair Mike was sitting in. ‘I want a living Quentin!’ Mike cocked his head to one side. ‘You know what, Simon, it's actually completely impossible to get back someone who's dead, no matter how much you loved them. You see, death …’"

关键词：living / cocked his head / completely impossible / get back / dead / loved / death

为什么这样写：Simon 的"living"是活的——他要活的。Mike 的"cocked his head"是歪头——他在思考。

> **原句 5:** "‘Death …’ said the taxidermist, ‘death is a door with a spring lock.’"

关键词：death / door / spring lock

为什么这样写：Mike 的"spring lock"是弹簧锁——死亡的比喻。

> **原句 6:** "Jill felt Siri take hold of her hand, and Simon stopped wriggling about in her lap. Jill's mouth was dry and when she spoke her voice sounded hoarse and strange: ‘What would that be, Mike?’"

关键词：take hold / hand / stopped wriggling / lap / dry / hoarse / strange

为什么这样写：Jill 的"hoarse"是沙哑的——她的声音沙哑。

> **原句 7:** "‘Since it's your husband who makes all this possible, as I said, I'm going to do for him exactly what politicians like him have done for families such as my own.’ Jill felt Siri take hold of her hand, and Simon stopped wriggling about in her lap. Jill's mouth was dry and when she spoke her voice sounded hoarse and strange: ‘What would that be, Mike?’"

关键词：husband / makes all this possible / politicians / families / dry / hoarse / strange

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"makes all this possible"是一切成为可能——市长让一切成为可能。

> **原句 8:** "‘What he and people like him did for me,’ said Mike Lunde as he looked down at the rifle, ‘was to put weapons in the hands of those who took from me everything I loved.’ He raised the rifle to his cheek. ‘So now it's my turn.’"

关键词：weapons / hands / took from me / everything I loved / raised / rifle / cheek / my turn

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"my turn"是轮到我了——他的复仇时刻。
