# 23. Wheel of Fortune, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 停职后孤独绝望，翻通讯录找女人陪——他想起 Alice 的新男友 Stan，打电话被拒，又找到 Dory（曾因其打架入院的女人），但发现她已有男友；他最终意识到自己"在麻烦中"。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷——Oz 的孤独和自毁倾向达到顶点，他甚至在广播中听到市长关于枪支的辩论。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："酗酒侦探的深夜"——Oz 在车里翻通讯录，像"幸运转盘"一样随机选择女人；"广播中的政治"——市长为枪支辩护，与 Oz 的处境形成对照。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"愤怒"变为"自怜"——他承认"我是那个在麻烦中的人"。
- **叙事手法**：独白 + 对话——Oz 的内心独白与零星对话交织，节奏从焦躁到疲惫。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bob stopped the Volvo by the kerb outside Bernie's Bar. The Happy Hour sign wasn't up. He drummed on the steering wheel as he looked toward the yellow light behind the blinds. So what would that make it in there now? Unhappy Hour?"

关键词：kerb / Bernie's Bar / Happy Hour / drummed / steering wheel / blinds / Unhappy Hour

为什么这样写：Oz 的"drummed"是焦虑——他在酒吧外等待。"Unhappy Hour"是幽默——"不快乐时光"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"Unhappy Hour"暗示他的情绪状态——他不快乐。

> **原句 2:** "Bob breathed through his open mouth. He could feel the rushing start up. He laid his police ID on the bar. ‘You want to answer my questions here or down at the station?’"

关键词：breathed through his open mouth / rushing / police ID / down at the station

为什么这样写：Oz 的"rushing"是 ch10 的"rashing sound"——他的暴力前兆。"down at the station"是威胁——警察用语。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"laid his police ID"是展示权威——即使停职，他仍用警察身份。

> **原句 3:** "‘The kid's sick, so she's at home,’ he said. ‘Is she in trouble?’ No, thought Bob. He grabbed up his ID and walked out again."

关键词：kid / sick / in trouble / grabbed up / walked out

为什么这样写：Oz 的"kid"是误会——酒保以为是孩子生病。"in trouble"是双关——Oz 才是"在麻烦中"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"No, thought Bob"是内心独白——他在纠正酒保。

> **原句 4:** "He tapped in an A. Then an L. Looked at the I and the C in surprise, and then remembered he had deleted her from his Contacts last night. Alas, he could still remember the number."

关键词：tapped in / A / L / I / C / deleted / Contacts / Alas

为什么这样写：Oz 的"tapped in"是动作——他在拨号。"Alas"是悲哀——他删了号码但仍然记得。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"deleted her from his Contacts"是过去——他试图忘记 Alice。

> **原句 5:** "The fact that Stan answered Alice's phone without saying anything other than his name told him at least two things. That Alice trusted Stan with her phone, which was something she'd never done with him. And that Stan knew it was Bob ringing and he was ready for a confrontation."

关键词：Stan / trusted / phone / confrontation

为什么这样写：Oz 的"trusted"是痛苦——Alice 信任 Stan 超过他。"confrontation"是准备——Stan 准备"对峙"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"at least two things"是分析——他像侦探一样分析。

> **原句 6:** "‘Good evening, meathead. Is Alice there?’"

关键词：meathead

为什么这样写：Oz 的"meathead"是侮辱——他在骂 Stan。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"meathead"是挑衅——他想打架。

> **原句 7:** "Bob howled into the phone. He didn't know what had happened, for a moment he was lost, and when he came back his phone was gone. He located it and saw a rose-shaped shatter in a corner of the screen."

关键词：howled / lost / located / rose-shaped shatter / screen

为什么这样写：Oz 的"howled"是动物性的——他像狼一样嚎叫。"rose-shaped shatter"是破坏——他摔了手机。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"located"是寻找——他在找手机，象征"失控"。

> **原句 8:** "He scrolled down. Spun through the names with his index finger the way you spin a wheel of fortune. The list stopped and his eye fell on a name. Dory Anvil."

关键词：scrolled down / spun / index finger / wheel of fortune / Dory Anvil

为什么这样写：Oz 的"wheel of fortune"是比喻——他在"转幸运转盘"选择女人。"index finger"是随机——他随便选。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"eye fell on"是偶然——他没有刻意选择。

> **原句 9:** "‘Have you missed me?’ Her voice had a false, trilling quality, like a grown woman pretending to be a child. ‘Hugely,’ said Bob, noting as he did so the way he had unconsciously imitated Mike Lunde's cautious irony."

关键词：false, trilling quality / grown woman pretending to be a child / hugely / unconsciously imitated / cautious irony

为什么这样写：Oz 的"false, trilling quality"是观察——他在分析 Dory 的声音。"unconsciously imitated"是模仿——他在学 Lunde。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"cautious irony"是罕见的——他在用"谨慎的讽刺"。

> **原句 10:** "‘To be honest, Dory, and that's not something I usually am, I'm lying because it's so much more fucking pleasant. And I think you should regard the obvious lie as a kind of lifebelt. Grab hold of it, and you'll avoid the humiliation of having me tell you that it's because you just weren't interesting enough.’"

关键词：to be honest / not something I usually am / lying / fucking pleasant / obvious lie / lifebelt / humiliation / weren't interesting enough

为什么这样写：Oz 的"to be honest"是反讽——他经常不诚实。"lifebelt"是比喻——"救生圈"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"weren't interesting enough"是残忍——他在伤害 Dory。

> **原句 11:** "‘Why are you telling me that you're at home and you're alone?’ ‘Well, you should know the answer to that.’ ‘I should?’ ‘You're the one who put Tony in hospital.’"

关键词：at home / alone / put Tony in hospital

为什么这样写：Dory 的"you're the one who put Tony in hospital"是事实——Oz 打了 Tony。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"swallowed"是紧张——他意识到 Dory 是 Tony 的女友。

> **原句 12:** "Bob searched his memory. It was his habit to use a system of associations to store information. Sometimes it worked, sometimes it didn't, like with Dory. An actor who plays the part of an insane captain, plus a man who really is insane. Gregory Dupont. Simple."

关键词：habit / system of associations / store information / insane captain / Gregory Dupont

为什么这样写：Oz 的"system of associations"是记忆术——他用关联记忆。"insane captain"是线索——他在想房东的名字。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"Simple"是轻松——他终于想起来了。
