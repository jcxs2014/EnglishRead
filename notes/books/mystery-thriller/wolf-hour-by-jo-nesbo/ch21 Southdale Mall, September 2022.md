# 21. Southdale Mall, September 2022

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Holger 与出租车司机 Gabriel 交谈，透露了案件与自己的关系——被害人是他的表兄；他来到 Southdale Mall 寻找童年的记忆，但发现一切已变。
- **情感弧线位置**：过渡 / 回忆——Holger 从"调查者"变为"讲述者"，他分享了自己的过去。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："元小说"——Holger 直接与读者/司机讨论他的写作方法；"童年记忆"——Southdale Mall 是 Holger 童年的象征。
- **人物弧线**：Holger 从"旁观者"变为"参与者"——他承认案件与他有关（表兄是受害者）。
- **叙事手法**：对话 + 回忆——Holger 与 Gabriel 的对话中嵌套着对 Southdale Mall 的历史回忆。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "We're waiting for a red light in Edina, which is technically speaking another town. The cab driver, whose name I have discovered is Gabriel, tells me that he thinks the mayor of Edina is of Norwegian descent. I'm more preoccupied by the fact that I don't recognise my surroundings. What's happened to my Southdale Mall?"

关键词：technically speaking / Norwegian descent / preoccupied / recognise my surroundings

为什么这样写：Holger 的"technically speaking"是学究气的——他在纠正地理。"Norwegian descent"是移民主题——明尼苏达的挪威裔社区。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"preoccupied"暗示他的注意力在"过去"而非"现在"。

> **原句 2:** "‘What made you choose this particular story?’ he asks. ‘I'm a crime writer,’ I answer. ‘Well, I'm a cab driver,’ he says, ‘but I don't go to New York and drive around a lot of streets I don't know.’"

关键词：choose this particular story / crime writer / cab driver

为什么这样写：Gabriel 的"cab driver"是幽默——他在类比。"choose this particular story"是问题——Holger 为什么选择这个案件？

读者视角提示：Holger 的"crime writer"是表面身份——他的真实身份更复杂。

> **原句 3:** "‘The hero of the story – if you can call him that – was my cousin. I guess I just want somebody to tell his story.’ ‘Was? You mean he passed away?’ I don't answer."

关键词：hero of the story / if you can call him that / cousin / passed away

为什么这样写：Holger 的"if you can call him that"是犹豫——他不确定表兄是"英雄"。"passed away"是委婉语——死亡。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"don't answer"是默认——表兄已经死了。

> **原句 4:** "‘No. I trained as a priest.’ ‘Really? Isn't that pretty unusual? For a priest to be writing about gruesome murders?’ ‘Not as unusual as you might think. Maybe you've heard of Ronald Knox? He was a Catholic priest. He's the one who laid down the ten commandments of crime fiction.’"

关键词：trained as a priest / unusual / gruesome murders / Ronald Knox / Catholic priest / ten commandments / crime fiction

为什么这样写：Holger 的"trained as a priest"是意外——他的背景出人意料。"Ronald Knox"是真实人物——英国天主教神父，侦探小说规则制定者。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"ten commandments"是幽默——他把"十诫"用于犯罪小说。

> **原句 5:** "‘As in, the killer has to be introduced early on in the story, but we're not allowed access to his thoughts.’ ‘And you follow that commandment?’ ‘No, not at all. I let the reader follow the murderer's thoughts as I think he must have thought them at the time.’"

关键词：introduced early on / not allowed access to his thoughts / follow the murderer's thoughts

为什么这样写：Holger 的"not allowed access"是规则——Knox 的规则禁止进入凶手内心。"follow the murderer's thoughts"是违反——Holger 故意违反规则。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"as I think he must have thought them"是想象——他在"成为"凶手。

> **原句 6:** "‘But then, I'm writing true crime, not a detective novel. And Knox's commandments aren't meant to be taken seriously. The fifth commandment states that there shouldn't be a Chinaman in the story.’"

关键词：true crime / detective novel / Chinaman

为什么这样写：Holger 的"true crime"是体裁——非虚构犯罪。"Chinaman"是种族歧视——Knox 时代的偏见。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"aren't meant to be taken seriously"是讽刺——规则本身就可笑。

> **原句 7:** "When Southdale opened in 1956 it turned out to be the start of an urban development that would soon characterise the whole country and, in due course, the whole Western world. Victor Gruen, the architect who designed Southdale and fifty other shopping malls, fled Austria when Hitler annexed it in 1938."

关键词：Southdale / 1956 / urban development / characterise / Victor Gruen / architect / fled Austria / annexed

为什么这样写：Holger 的"Victor Gruen"是真实人物——购物中心设计者。"fled Austria"是历史——纳粹吞并奥地利。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"in due course"是不可避免的——购物中心文化蔓延全球。

> **原句 8:** "He arrived in New York with eight dollars, no English, a training in architecture and the idea of building small urban centres where people would live with every facility a country town could need right next to them: post offices, bakeries, police stations, schools. But as the old saying goes, the road to hell is paved with good intentions, and there's an obvious irony in the fact that a dedicated socialist and urbanist like Gruen should be the architect responsible – in my uncle's view – for the gradual destruction of the Minneapolis he had grown up in, a vibrant and living centre with a thriving business, cultural and social life."

关键词：eight dollars / no English / training in architecture / urban centres / the road to hell is paved with good intentions / irony / dedicated socialist / urbanist / gradual destruction / vibrant and living centre / thriving

为什么这样写：Holger 的"road to hell is paved with good intentions"是谚语——好意导致坏结果。"irony"是讽刺——社会主义者摧毁了城市。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"vibrant and living centre"是怀旧——他怀念过去的明尼阿波利斯。

> **原句 9:** "To him Gruen's shopping malls were parasites that sucked the life out of the towns and left nothing behind but a dying organism choked by exhaust fumes and crime, a lack of public transport and ordinary, everyday humanity, an accretion of cold stone-and-glass castles containing offices where people worked but from which they fled as soon as the working day was over."

关键词：parasites / sucked the life out / dying organism / choked / exhaust fumes / lack / humanity / accretion / stone-and-glass castles / fled

为什么这样写：Holger 的"parasites"是比喻——购物中心是"寄生虫"。"dying organism"是比喻——城市在死亡。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"fled"是逃离——人们下班后逃离办公楼。

> **原句 10:** "I remember my uncle once saying to my father that shopping malls like Southdale created psychopaths, whatever that was supposed to mean. Anyway, my uncle must have been mollified to learn that Gruen repented of his sins and atoned for them by returning to Austria to live and to work on the pedestrian precincts in the centre of Vienna, and that two years before his death he publicly expressed his regret for what his shopping malls had turned into."

关键词：created psychopaths / mollified / repented of his sins / atoned / pedestrian precincts / publicly expressed his regret

为什么这样写：Holger 的"psychopaths"是极端观点——购物中心创造精神病态。"repented of his sins"是宗教语言——Gruen 的"忏悔"。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"mollified"是安慰——Gruen 的后悔让叔叔感到安慰。

> **原句 11:** "Me, I love shopping malls. I don't tell anyone, but I can still feel some of the elation of my childhood as I enter those riotously coloured jungles where everything screeches at you, everyone hunts you, and the ant-like columns of humans move up the escalators as they head for new worlds."

关键词：elation / riotously coloured jungles / screeches / hunts / ant-like columns / escalators / new worlds

为什么这样写：Holger 的"riotously coloured jungles"是比喻——购物中心是"丛林"。"ant-like columns"是比喻——人群像蚂蚁。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"new worlds"是双关——每层楼都是"新世界"。

> **原句 12:** "It's like something out of a computer game. Where my uncle and my father saw vulgar commercialism I feel the joy of sinking into a warm cacophony of sight and sound, walking through an Eden of temptations and sinful invitations, feeling how your life might have been had you owned this thing or that, the sensation and excitement of a possible Fall of biblical dimensions, even if you don't have a krone or a dollar to spend."

关键词：computer game / vulgar commercialism / joy / sinking into / warm cacophony / Eden / temptations / sinful invitations / Fall of biblical dimensions / krone

为什么这样写：Holger 的"Eden"是比喻——购物中心是"伊甸园"。"Fall of biblical dimensions"是比喻——"堕落"（消费主义）。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"krone"是挪威货币——他仍然用挪威人的视角看世界。

> **原句 13:** "Six women in red T-shirts carrying placards are standing under some trees at the end of the lot. I walk toward them. They're on strike, nurses from the women's hospital on the other side of the road, they explain."

关键词：red T-shirts / placards / on strike / nurses

为什么这样写：Holger 的"placards"是标语——护士在罢工。"on strike"是抗议——医疗系统问题。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"women's hospital"是地点——与停车库事件相关。

> **原句 14:** "I explain, but they've never heard of the incident. Six years is a long time, they say. Before the pandemic, before Floyd, it was another time."

关键词：incident / six years / pandemic / Floyd / another time

为什么这样写：Holger 的"six years"是时间——案件发生在 2016 年。"before the pandemic, before Floyd"是历史标记——2020 年。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"another time"是怀旧——过去是"另一个时代"。

> **原句 15:** "Close my eyes behind the sunglasses and breathe in deeply, maybe hoping to breathe in the air of my childhood. Open car windows with the smell of Minnesota's sun-scorched fields and the smoke from Dad's cigar in the driving seat. But above all, the smell of freshly baked doughnuts from Southdale Mall."

关键词：breathe in deeply / sun-scorched fields / smoke / cigar / freshly baked doughnuts

为什么这样写：Holger 的"breathe in deeply"是感官——他试图"吸入"过去。"freshly baked doughnuts"是嗅觉——童年的味道。

读者视角提示：Holger 的"above all"是强调—— doughnuts 是最重要的记忆。
