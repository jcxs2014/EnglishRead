# 38. The Rage of Abandonment, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 回家与 Alice 谈话，请求她解释"孤独"；Alice 区分了"存在性孤独"和"人际孤独"，并解释了"遗弃之怒"（rage of abandonment）；Alice 宣布自己怀孕了，Oz 在车里崩溃大哭。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 低谷——Oz 与 Alice 的对话揭示了 Goya 的内心世界，但 Alice 的怀孕消息让 Oz 彻底崩溃。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："心理学的对话"——Alice 用心理学解释 Goya 的动机；"怀孕的消息"——Alice 怀孕了，Oz 意识到她真的"翻篇"了。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"愤怒"变为"悲伤"——他在车里崩溃大哭；从"追踪记忆"变为"接受现实"——他意识到 Alice 已经"翻篇"了。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Oz 与 Alice 的对话揭示了 Goya 的心理和 Oz 的内心世界；结尾的独白是全书最感人的段落之一。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "It was dark but the rain had stopped by the time Bob reached the house. He rang the bell. Heard the footsteps inside, recognised them as Alice's, knew which slippers she was wearing, also that she would be wearing the white lamb's wool sweater she always wore when it was cold."

为什么这样写：Oz 的"recognised"是认出——他认出了 Alice 的脚步声。"slippers"是拖鞋——他知道她穿哪双拖鞋。

> **原句 2:** "She opened the door. Smiled. And it seemed to him that she was still the same, beautiful Alice, with her hair tied up in a knot and stray honey-blonde locks of hair tapping at the corners of her mouth, though the wrinkles around her eyes were now more pronounced."

为什么这样写：Oz 的"honey-blonde locks"是蜜金色的头发——Alice 的头发。"pronounced"是明显——皱纹更明显了。

> **原句 3:** "‘Thanks,’ he said, and tried not to think how odd it was to be invited to enter his own house. ‘And thank you for seeing me.’ He removed his coat and hung it on one of the vacant hooks. Tried not to wonder whether she might have removed one of Stan's jackets from the same hook just before he rang the bell."

关键词：odd / invited / enter his own house / vacant hooks / Stan's jackets

为什么这样写：Oz 的"odd"是奇怪——被邀请进自己的房子很奇怪。"vacant hooks"是空挂钩——没有 Stan 的外套。

> **原句 4:** "She led the way into the kitchen. He registered that she was back to her familiar Alice size, the curves had returned, there was a little more flesh on the bones, all of which suggested to him that she was doing well. Immediately following Frankie's death she had lost weight dramatically, and then gained it so swiftly she became a sort of inflated version of herself. And then lost it again. It was as though she had gone through the whole repertoire of eating disorders familiar to her from her patients. Or maybe it was the pills."

关键词：led the way / kitchen / registered / familiar Alice size / curves / flesh on the bones / doing well / lost weight dramatically / swiftly / inflated version / repertoire / eating disorders / patients / pills

> **原句 5:** "They sat in their usual seats on opposite sides of the kitchen table. She laced her fingers around a large teacup. How many times had he seen her do that, warming her hands, with her shoulders slightly hunched? He noticed that the picture of Frankie on the refrigerator was still there. And next to it, one of Frankie, Bob and Alice together."

关键词：usual seats / opposite sides / kitchen table / laced her fingers / teacup / warming her hands / hunched / refrigerator / picture / Frankie / Bob and Alice together

为什么这样写：Oz 的"usual seats"是平时的座位——他们坐在平时的位置。"hunched"是弓着的——她的肩膀微微弓起。

> **原句 6:** "‘I'm sorry for having behaved like an idiot. I want to sign those papers as soon as possible, so that you're also the formal owner of the house, you don't just live here.’ ‘What?’ she said, as though the running water had drowned out what he was saying. Bob turned off the tap, took the glass and sat back down opposite her. ‘On one condition.’ She gave him a cautious look. ‘And that is?’ ‘That we lower the price.’ ‘Lower? Don't you mean raise?’ ‘No, lower. Even you won't be able to pay off the loan if we stick to the current valuation.’"

关键词：behaved like an idiot / sign those papers / formal owner / running water / drowned out / On one condition / cautious look / lower the price / raise / pay off the loan / stick to / valuation

为什么这样写：Oz 的"lower the price"是降价——他要降价。"valuation"是估价——当前估价。

> **原句 7:** "Bob looked at the disbelieving face before emptying the glass in a single long swallow. As he put the glass down he could see that she believed him. Her eyes were shining. A slight shiver passed between her shoulders, as though she wanted to put her hand onto his."

关键词：disbelieving face / emptying the glass / single long swallow / shining / slight shiver / put her hand onto his

为什么这样写：Oz 的"single long swallow"是一口闷——他一口气喝完。"shiver"是颤抖——她的肩膀颤抖。

> **原句 8:** "‘And there's one more thing I want from you,’ he said. ‘What's that?’ ‘Explain loneliness to me.’ ‘Loneliness?’ ‘In technical terms.’ ‘Are you lonely?’ ‘I'm asking you to explain it, not me.’"

关键词：one more thing / Explain loneliness / technical terms

为什么这样写：Oz 的"technical terms"是术语——他要术语解释。

> **原句 9:** "She folded her arms, breathed deeply and calmly and fixed her gaze some place just above his head the way she did when she was concentrating. He waited. Waited the way he had waited outside her flat before those first, comically old-fashioned dates. Outside her workplace after they became a couple. Outside the bathroom after they started living together and she was getting ready to go out to a party. Outside the delivery room when Frankie was born. Waiting for Alice was something he associated with happiness, because he was waiting for something good. But there would be no more waiting. He knew that now. There was nothing left to wait for."

关键词：folded her arms / breathed deeply / calmly / fixed her gaze / concentrating / waited / flat / comically old-fashioned dates / couple / bathroom / party / delivery room / Frankie / associated with happiness / no more waiting

为什么这样写：Alice 的"comically old-fashioned dates"是滑稽的老式约会。"delivery room"是产房——Frankie 出生的地方。

> **原句 10:** "‘The language that describes loneliness is limited,’ she began slowly, as though testing her way. ‘But first you have existential loneliness. The knowledge that you have been thrown into this world, and that you, me and everybody else are all, in the final analysis, alone. And then you have interpersonal loneliness. A lack of belonging, the feeling that you are alone even when with friends. You feel as though you're inside a bubble, the others seem far, far away because you are, emotionally speaking, somewhere else.’"

关键词：language / describes loneliness / limited / testing her way / existential loneliness / thrown into this world / in the final analysis / alone / interpersonal loneliness / lack of belonging / inside a bubble / emotionally speaking / somewhere else

为什么这样写：Alice 的"existential loneliness"是存在性孤独——存在主义概念。"interpersonal loneliness"是人际孤独——缺乏归属感。

> **原句 11:** "‘Talk about loneliness when the most important people in your life are gone,’ said Bob. ‘Someone you love. And children.’ It was as though he had pressed a button. Her lips twisted and tears at once sprang to her eyes. ‘Bob, please don't start again …’ Her voice was hoarse."

关键词：most important people / gone / love / children / pressed a button / lips twisted / tears / sprang to her eyes / hoarse

为什么这样写：Oz 的"pressed a button"是按下按钮——他触动了 Alice。"hoarse"是沙哑的——她的声音沙哑。

> **原句 12:** "‘That's trauma,’ she said. ‘Trauma, not loneliness. Trauma arises when you lose someone you thought you would be spending the rest of your life with. When it was more than an expectation, it was a conviction. Something you based your whole life on. Something that was everything.’"

关键词：trauma / arises / lose / spending the rest of your life / expectation / conviction / based your whole life on / everything

为什么这样写：Alice 的"conviction"是信念——不是期望，是信念。"based your whole life on"是建立一生——你建立一生的事情。

> **原句 13:** "‘The trauma is the wound. But the loneliness that comes with it locks you to your trauma. Sometimes there are physical manifestations. Often intolerable pains that follow the spine down toward the stomach.’ She put a hand to her own stomach. ‘You feel you want to disappear, but the body is frozen up, and you become simply incapable of drawing warmth from those around you.’"

关键词：wound / locks you to / physical manifestations / intolerable pains / spine / stomach / disappear / frozen up / incapable / drawing warmth

为什么这样写：Alice 的"physical manifestations"是身体表现——疼痛。"frozen up"是冻结——身体冻结。

> **原句 14:** "‘Or raging. Everyone reacts differently. But we often share the feeling that something drastic has to be done. Traumatic memory is circular. Meaning that when something happens that reminds us of a previous trauma it can awaken rage, in this case the rage of abandonment. Everything that has happened before happens again. The whole weight of those previous experiences invades the present. The grief that has up to that point been frozen explodes in a vengeful rage. The violence of trauma is often extreme. People stab in a frenzy, they molest the body, not uncommonly there are elements of sadism.’"

关键词：raging / reacts differently / drastic / circular / previous trauma / awaken rage / rage of abandonment / invades / frozen / explodes / vengeful rage / extreme / stab / frenzy / molest / not uncommonly / sadism

为什么这样写：Alice 的"rage of abandonment"是遗弃之怒——术语。"circular"是循环——创伤记忆是循环的。

> **原句 15:** "‘Have you ever felt afraid of me?’ Alice tilted her head. ‘No. But as a psychologist I know that as a rule people overestimate their ability to predict how those closest to them will react, especially if the person concerned has been traumatised. Maybe that's exactly the mistake I'm making now. Given your sometimes aggressive behaviour, it is definitely not doing things by the book for me to meet you here alone, like this, where all the memories are.’"

关键词：afraid / tilted her head / overestimate / predict / traumatised / mistake / aggressive behaviour / by the book / meet you here alone / memories

为什么这样写：Alice 的"by the book"是照规矩——她不应该单独见他。"traumatised"是受创伤的——Oz 受创伤了。

> **原句 16:"‘I'm over the worst. I accept that life goes on. I remember you saying that the rational mind forgets things it has no use for. That's true. I can feel how I'm thinking less about both you and Frankie with each day that passes. And you'll see, they'll be even better now that I'm getting rid of the house. It'll be as though none of this –' he gestured toward the photos on the refrigerator – 'ever happened. Don't you think?’ He was smiling so hard the corners of his mouth ached and through the tears her face grew fluid and indistinct. It felt as though his skin was on fire. ‘But then, there's a part of my brain that isn't rational and smart, and that can't forget, even though it knows it should.’"

> **原句 17:"Alice nodded. ‘Maybe we don't have to forget, Bob. Maybe it's more about treasuring the good memories and learning to live with the not so good ones. And suddenly he understood what it was. ‘Carrying on?’ he said. And steeled himself as he waited for what he knew must come. Because of course he'd seen it as soon as he arrived, the way she looked just the way she had done back then. Alice wrapped her fingers around her teacup and looked down into it. ‘Yes, I'm …’ She seemed to ready herself, then looked up and directly into Bob's eyes. ‘I'm pregnant.’"

> **原句 18:"Bob nodded and nodded, his head going up and down like that dog on the parcel shelf in the back of his parents' car. ‘Congratulations,’ he said, his voice thick and hoarse. ‘Thanks,’ she said quietly. ‘No, I really mean it,’ he said. ‘I'm … happy for you.’ ‘I know you are.’ ‘You do?’ ‘Of course,’ she said. They looked at each other. He smiled. She gave a cautious smile in return. ‘You've been dreading giving me the news?’ he asked. ‘A bit,’ she said. ‘So it's OK?’ ‘Yes, it's OK.’ He thought about it. It really was. More than OK. It felt … yes, like a relief. Alice was pregnant again, and in some strange way it felt as though he now had one life less on his conscience.
