# 18. Milkman, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Hanson 在密西西比河边钓鱼时接到神秘电话——"Die Man"（钻石之男）威胁他杀死 Lobo（Gomez），否则揭露他 30 年前的罪行。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 威胁——Hanson 的过去追上他，他被迫面对自己的罪恶。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："过去的幽灵"——Hanson 30 年前的罪行被揭露，他不再是"反派"，而是"被威胁者"。
- **人物弧线**：Hanson 从"霸凌者"变为"猎物"——他曾经为黑帮工作，现在黑帮要他杀人。
- **叙事手法**：纯 Hanson 视角——读者第一次进入他的内心，发现他的"恶"背后是恐惧。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Olav Hanson pulled his fishing rod sideways, against the current. Stared out into the night-time darkness that descended over the Mississippi before it took the rest of the city. Sometimes it even felt as though the darkness rose up from the Mississippi and not the other way round. Because it was a river with a lot of darkness in it. A lot of dirt and devilment people dumped there in hopes the river would take it all away, far from where they were."

关键词：fishing rod / sideways / current / night-time darkness / descended / Mississippi / darkness rose up / dirt / devilment / dumped

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"darkness rose up"是比喻——密西西比河是"黑暗"的源头。"devilment"（邪恶）暗示人们向河中倾倒罪恶。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"dumped"是字面意义——人们向河中扔垃圾，也是隐喻——他们"倾倒"罪恶。

> **原句 2:** "He'd told Violet not to call him when he was out, but she was as unpredictable as Sean. This, however, was a caller unknown."

关键词：unpredictable / Sean / caller unknown

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"unpredictable"是抱怨——他的妻子和儿子都不可预测。"caller unknown"是悬念——电话来自未知号码。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"Sean"是他的软肋——他爱儿子，但儿子让他失望。

> **原句 3:** "The voice at the other end breathed in before speaking in a low voice: ‘Milkman?’ Olav Hanson felt his heart stop beating in his chest. Thirty years. And it took him only a second and two syllables to recognise the voice."

关键词：breathed in / low voice / Milkman / heart stop beating / thirty years / two syllables

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"heart stop beating"是恐惧——他认出了这个声音。"thirty years"是时间跨度——30 年前的罪恶被唤醒。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"Milkman"是黑帮给他的绰号——他曾经为黑帮工作。

> **原句 4:** "‘I can hear that you know who this is, Milkman. And that you're afraid. That's good. It means you'll listen extra close. One of my guys tells me you're looking for Lobo.’"

关键词：afraid / listen extra close / Lobo

为什么这样写：Die Man 的"afraid"是威胁——他知道 Hanson 害怕。"listen extra close"是命令——Hanson 必须听。

读者视角提示：Die Man 的"Lobo"是关键——他在说 Gomez。

> **原句 5:** "‘Evidently not,’ said the voice. ‘MPD are looking for him. Suspicion of attempted murder. Which can only mean he's got a bit rusty. Anyway, if Lobo really has shown up again then neither you nor I want the MPD to find him. We don't want him sitting in some interrogation where they suggest a deal that involves him telling them everything he knows. About me. And about you, Milkman. Do you get my drift?’"

关键词：evidently / rusty / interrogation / deal / telling them everything / drift

为什么这样写：Die Man 的"rusty"是讽刺——Gomez 的枪法退化了。"interrogation"是威胁——如果 Gomez 被逮捕，他会供出所有人。

读者视角提示：Die Man 的"drift"是黑帮术语——"你明白我的意思吗？"

> **原句 6:** "Olav got it. He understood the nightmare was back. The man at the top, the one they called Die Man, and not just because of the diamonds in his teeth. ‘You want me to …?’"

关键词：nightmare / Die Man / diamonds in his teeth

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"nightmare"是真实的——他的过去回来了。"Die Man"是黑帮老大——他的名字暗示死亡。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"diamonds in his teeth"是细节——Die Man 的牙齿上镶着钻石。

> **原句 7:** "‘Yes, Milkman, I want you to make sure Lobo never gets as far as that interrogation.’"

关键词：make sure / never gets as far as / interrogation

为什么这样写：Die Man 的"make sure"是命令——Hanson 必须杀死 Goz。"interrogation"是目标——在 Gomez 被审讯前杀死他。

读者视角提示：Die Man 的"never gets as far as"是威胁——Hanson 必须行动。

> **原句 8:** "He had never asked Die why he called him Milkman. It could of course be because Olav was pale and blond or because people like him were typical of the Scandinavian farmer class. Or because he milked the gang for money. But it might also have been ironically meant, giving a milk-white name to a dirty cop who had done what was necessary each time investigations of some gangland killing made things hot for Die Man and his people."

关键词：Milkman / pale / blond / Scandinavian farmer class / milked the gang / ironically / milk-white name / dirty cop / gangland killing / made things hot

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"milked the gang"是双关——他"挤"黑帮的钱。"dirty cop"是自我认知——他知道自己是腐败的。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"made things hot"是黑帮术语——当调查变得"热"时，他让事情"冷"下来。

> **原句 9:** "It started with the triple homicide that evening thirty years ago. The girl in the wheelchair, the little boy and the mother. It hadn't been one of Olav's cases, but he'd managed to send it off in the wrong direction, and yes, he'd had trouble sleeping after that."

关键词：triple homicide / girl in the wheelchair / little boy / mother / send it off in the wrong direction / trouble sleeping

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"triple homicide"是 Gomez 的家人——他 30 年前谋杀了 Gomez 的妻儿。"wrong direction"是误导——他让调查走错方向。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"trouble sleeping"是良心——他并非完全冷血。

> **原句 10:** "‘Sure, but we have to talk about the price.’ Word for word that was what he had said the first time, when he made his choice and let the genie out of the bottle. He spat in the direction of the river and saw the foamy white ball carried off into the darkness. All right then. But this time, he wouldn't be the one going under."

关键词：price / word for word / genie out of the bottle / spat / foamy white ball / going under

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"genie out of the bottle"是比喻——他 30 年前释放了"精灵"（罪恶），现在无法收回。"going under"是黑帮术语——被杀死。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"spat"是愤怒——他对自己感到愤怒。
