# 53. Hunting Trophy, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Lunde 向 Oz 解释了他的复仇哲学——Karlstad、Dante、Hanson 都是"艺术品"的一部分；Lunde 请求 Oz 帮他扣扳机，Oz 拒绝；SWAT 冲进来，Lunde 用步枪自杀。
- **情感弧线位置：高潮 / 死亡——Lunde 用步枪自杀，Oz 走出标本店，Kay 拥抱了他。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的自杀"——Lunde 选择自杀而非被 SWAT 杀死；"艺术的完成"——Lunde 说"眼睛必须对"。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Oz 与 Lunde 的对话是本章的核心；结尾的第三人称视角展示了 Kay 的观察。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "‘I made a hunting trophy for Karlstad a couple of years ago,’ said Mike as he folded his hands behind his head. Like someone who's finished a piece of work, thought Bob."

关键词：hunting trophy / Karlstad / folded his hands / finished / piece of work

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"hunting trophy"是狩猎标本——他为 Karlstad 做了标本。"folded his hands"是交叉双手——他像完成工作的人。

> **原句 2:** "‘Which is?’ ‘That one day the gun you make is going to be aimed at you.’"

关键词：gun / aimed at you

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"aimed at you"是对准你——枪最终会对准你。

> **原句 3:** "‘So now the head is …?’ Mike nodded toward his workshop. ‘The leather beetles are busy.’"

关键词：head / workshop / leather beetles / busy

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"leather beetles"是皮蠹——甲虫在清理头骨。

> **原句 4:** "‘You don't need to do this, Mike. There are people who can help. Treat your depression. Not just give you pills.’ ‘Please, Bob.’ ‘I can't, Mike. I haven't carried a gun, haven't touched one, since … since …’"

关键词：depression / treat / pills / can't / carried a gun / touched

为什么这样写：Oz 的"depression"是抑郁——Lunde 的抑郁。"pills"是药片——治疗抑郁。

> **原句 5:** "Bob Oz closed his eyes. Then he whispered her name, the name of his greatest joy. Frankie."

关键词：closed his eyes / whispered / name / greatest joy / Frankie

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Frankie"是女儿的名字——他低声说。

> **原句 6:** "Kay walked toward Bob. It struck her that he looked very tired. And very lonely. Without thinking about it she found herself putting her arms around him."

关键词：walked toward / struck / tired / lonely / arms around him

为什么这样写：Kay 的"struck"是打动——她被打动了。"lonely"是孤独的——Oz 看起来很孤独。

> **原句 7:** "The odd thing was that the signal was given not to O'Rourke but to Springer. ‘Can you tell them what they need to know?’ whispered Bob. ‘Me?’ said Kay. ‘Where are you going?’ ‘Let's see if I can give you an answer to that some other day.’"

关键词：odd / signal / O'Rourke / Springer / need to know / answer / some other day

为什么这样写：Kay 的"odd"是奇怪的——信号是给 Springer 的。

> **原句 8:** "Bob Oz carefully extricated himself from her embrace and crossed the street to where Walker stood waiting. Kay headed to the store doorway. Pushed it open and went in. The SWAT team was obviously now going through the other rooms on the premises, because the body was still in the chair. The shot had entered under the chin. The top of the head was gone, like a breakfast egg. But the face was intact."

关键词：extricated / embrace / crossed / Walker / SWAT team / premises / body / chair / shot / chin / top of the head / intact

为什么这样写：Kay 的"premises"是场所——SWAT 在搜查场所。"intact"是完好无损——脸完好。
