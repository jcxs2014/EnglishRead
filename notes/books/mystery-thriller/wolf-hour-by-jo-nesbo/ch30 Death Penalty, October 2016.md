# 30. Death Penalty, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 在 Lunde 的标本店喝咖啡，两人深入交谈——关于死刑、孤独、Oz 的女儿 Frankie 之死（她玩手枪走火身亡），以及 Oz 与 Alice 的分手真相。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 / 袒露——Oz 第一次向他人倾诉女儿之死，这是全书最感人的段落。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："哲学对话"——Oz 和 Lunde 讨论死刑、悲伤、孤独；"杀手的镜像"——Oz 意识到他和 Gomez 都是"失去女儿的父亲"。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"愤怒的侦探"变为"悲伤的父亲"——他终于敞开心扉；Lunde 从"标本师"变为"倾听者"——他提供了罕见的理解。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——几乎全为 Oz 与 Lunde 的对话，节奏缓慢而沉思。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "‘So now he's officially a killer,’ Mike Lunde said with an unhappy shake of the head. They were sitting in the smaller of the two workshops as Bob sipped at the strong black coffee which Mike told him he needed."

关键词：officially / unhappy shake of the head / sipped / strong black coffee

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"unhappy shake"是遗憾——他喜欢 Gomez。"strong black咖啡"是细节——Oz 需要清醒。

> **原句 2:** "Bob leaned back in his chair. He was already beginning to sober up. And not feeling too bad either. ‘Where do you stand on that? Do you think we should be executing people too?’"

关键词：leaned back / sober up / Where do you stand / executing people

为什么这样写：Oz 的"sober up"是清醒——他从醉酒中恢复。"where do you stand"是立场——他在问 Lunde 的立场。

> **原句 3:** "Mike paused his cutting and peered up into the air. ‘It's a difficult one. I'm against capital punishment because I believe that as a society we should be taking a lead in the whole civilisation project, and that means not taking human life.’"

关键词：paused / peered up / capital punishment / taking a lead / civilisation project

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"peered up"是思考——他在看天花板。"capital punishment"是死刑——正式用语。

读者视角提示：Lunde 的"civilisation project"是理想——他认为社会应该"文明"。

> **原句 4:** "‘I believe Tomás Gomez has introduced the death penalty and appointed himself judge, jury and executioner.’"

关键词：introduced / appointed / judge, jury and executioner

为什么这样写：Oz 的"appointed"是任命——Gomez 自封为法官。"judge, jury and executioner"是成语——集审判者和执行者于一身。

> **原句 5:** "‘Mike?’ ‘Yeah?’ ‘You ever had woman trouble?’ ‘No.’ ‘Never?’ ‘No. Or rather yes. The summer I was twenty-two.’"

关键词：woman trouble / never / summer / twenty-two

为什么这样写：Oz 的"woman trouble"是口语——"女人麻烦"。"summer I was twenty-two"是回忆——年轻时的事。

> **原句 6:** "‘This Stan …’ ‘A guy who works with Alice. Psychologist. I talked to someone I'd got to know a bit there and he reckoned Stan had been interested in her for a long time. I guess he was just waiting for his chance.’"

关键词：psychologist / reckoned / interested / waiting for his chance

为什么这样写：Oz 的"reckoned"是估计——他听别人说。"waiting for his chance"是机会——Stan 在等机会。

> **原句 7:** "‘What's her name?’ Bob lowered his head. Maybe it hadn't been such a good idea to sober up so quickly after all. ‘Alice,’ he said. ‘What happened?’ ‘Same story as yours. She met someone else.’"

关键词：sober up / Alice / same story / someone else

为什么这样写：Oz 的"sober up too quickly"是后悔——他不想清醒。"same story"是重复——同样的故事。

> **原句 8:** "Bob stood up and walked over to a white hare that looked as if it had frozen in mid-hop. He gently stroked the fur. ‘Before I met her, I didn't know what loneliness was. Or maybe I just covered it over with other women. She opened me up like a clam, and I discovered there was another Bob in there, a sensitive, tender guy who could love, cry, ask for help … yeah, all that kind of stuff.’"

关键词：frozen in mid-hop / gently stroked / covered it over / opened me up like a clam / sensitive / tender

为什么这样写：Oz 的"opened me up like a clam"是比喻——像蛤蜊一样被打开。"sensitive, tender guy"是柔软——他发现了自己的另一面。

> **原句 9:** "‘But when she left me, I found out she had nullified the effect of my antidote to loneliness. Women. Casual sex. Alcohol. Work. I try, and for a short while it's OK, but I know it can't last. I'm like that open clam, the sphincter is gone. I stand there gaping, defenceless, and all the time I'm drying out inside and smelling worse as each day goes by.’"

关键词：nullified / antidote / casual sex / sphincter / gaping / defenceless / drying out

为什么这样写：Oz 的"antidote to loneliness"是解药——女人、酒精、工作是"解药"。"sphincter"是括约肌——他无法"关闭"自己。

> **原句 10:** "‘We lost our daughter,’ said Bob. ‘Frankie. She was three years old.’ Lunde stopped working. Briefly wiped his hands together and let his arms drop to his sides. The look he gave Bob was open, naked, direct. What Bob saw wasn't a look that asked for something, some further explanation. And Mike Lunde didn't say anything either, it was as though he was someone who understood that no words added to those just spoken could give them meaning. Daughter. Lost. Three years old."

关键词：lost / Frankie / three years old / open, naked, direct / no words added / give them meaning

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Frankie"是名字——他第一次说出女儿的名字。"open, naked, direct"是目光——Lunde 的眼神毫无保留。

> **原句 11:** "‘She found my service pistol in our bedroom drawer,’ said Bob. ‘She was playing with it. Alice was home and heard the shot. An hour later our daughter died at the hospital.’"

关键词：service pistol / bedroom drawer / playing with it / shot / died at the hospital

为什么这样写：Oz 的"service pistol"是配枪——他的警用手枪。"playing with it"是玩耍——女儿在玩枪。

> **原句 12:** "‘Alice is a psychologist and she persuaded me to see various professionals who specialised in grief management. They all said the same thing; that experience shows that grief like this often leads to divorce; that it was important to give each other space and not apportion any blame.’"

关键词：psychologist / persuaded / grief management / leads to divorce / give each other space / apportion any blame

为什么这样写：Oz 的"grief management"是悲伤管理——专业术语。"apportion any blame"是归咎——不要互相指责。

> **原句 13:** "‘We completely forgot that we loved each other, we dragged ourselves along with this millstone of grief around our necks that was pulling us both down. Just the sight of each other at the breakfast table was a reminder of what had happened. Neither one of us would let the other forget, because forgetting, escape from the pain the other felt, would be a betrayal. Until in the end we just couldn't take it any more.’"

关键词：millstone of grief / pulling us both down / reminder / escape from the pain / betrayal / couldn't take it any more

为什么这样写：Oz 的"millstone"是磨石——沉重的负担。"betrayal"是背叛——忘记是背叛。

> **原句 14:** "‘So the reason wasn't that she found someone else?’ ‘Oh yes. But … she threw me out first.’"

关键词：reason / someone else / threw me out

为什么这样写：Oz 的"threw me out"是抛弃——她先赶他走。

> **原句 15:** "‘Maybe she didn't say it in so many words, but she froze me out. Wouldn't talk to me, wouldn't touch me. So I accepted the consequences. I packed my bag and I left.’"

关键词：in so many words / froze me out / wouldn't talk to me / wouldn't touch me / packed my bag

为什么这样写：Oz 的"froze me out"是冷暴力——她不说话、不碰他。"packed my bag"是离开——他收拾行李走了。

> **原句 16:** "‘So maybe you're not really angry because she found someone else but because she's happy. And since your daughter's death you feel she has no right to be.’"

关键词：angry / happy / no right to be

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"no right to be"是没有权利——Oz 认为 Alice 没有权利幸福。

> **原句 17:** "‘Then why are you so angry with her? You were the one who left, and I'm guessing you weren't exactly celibate once you were gone.’"

关键词：celibate

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"celibate"是禁欲——Oz 离开后没有禁欲。

> **原句 18:** "‘But the way it looks, you work night and day.’ ‘That's just because I want to be the one who brings Tomás Gomez down, not Olav Hanson or one of those other idiots.’ ‘Is that why you haven't told any of them about a taxidermist where Tomás Gomez has an order waiting to be picked up?’"

关键词：brings Tomás Gomez down / order waiting to be picked up

为什么这样写：Oz 的"brings down"是打倒——他想亲手抓到 Gomez。"order waiting to be picked up"是订单——Gomez 的猫还在标本店。

> **原句 19:** "‘Apart from work?’ ‘Not even that.’ ‘But the way it looks, you work night and day.’ ‘That's just because I want to be the one who brings Tomás Gomez down, not Olav Hanson or one of those other idiots.’"

关键词：apart from / brings Tomás Gomez down / idiots

为什么这样写：Oz 的"apart from"是除了——除了工作没有什么。"brings down"是打倒——他想亲手抓到 Gomez。

> **原句 20:** "Bob could see he meant it. There was empathy in his eyes, a mute pain like an echo of Bob's own. Bob could only wonder at the arbitrary way empathy was distributed among humans."

关键词：empathy / mute pain / echo / arbitrary / distributed

为什么这样写：Oz 的"empathy"是共情——Lunde 能感受到他的痛苦。"arbitrary"是随机——共情的分配是随机的。

> **原句 21:** "‘I'm done here for today. Where do you live?’ ‘Phillips.’ ‘I can drive you.’ Bob had protested, but Mike pointed out that Phillips was just down the road, and that anyway it was more or less on his route. His car was a Chevrolet Caprice station wagon, 1995 model, with the characteristic imitation wood-panelling on the sides."

关键词：done here for today / drive you / protested / just down the road / Chevrolet Caprice / station wagon / imitation wood-panelling

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"done here for today"是结束——他做完了。"imitation wood-panelling"是仿木纹——车的装饰。

> **原句 22:** "‘He seemed lonely. You a fan?’ Bob nodded. ‘Alice and I kissed the first time to "Purple Rain".’"

关键词：lonely / fan / kissed the first time / Purple Rain

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"lonely"是孤独——Prince 看起来很孤独。"Purple Rain"是歌曲——Prince 的经典。

> **原句 23:** "‘If you really could get Alice back, are you so sure that's what you want?’ ‘What are you talking about? It's all I ever think about.’ ‘I get that. But as it says in one of Aesop's fables, be careful what you wish for. Nothing's changed, Bob. That millstone, it's still there.’"

关键词：get Alice back / Aesop's fables / be careful what you wish for / millstone

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"Aesop's fables"是伊索寓言——"小心你许愿的东西"。"millstone"是磨石——悲伤的负担。

> **原句 24:** "Mike shrugged. ‘You've seen those animals in my store. They fade a little, but they don't disappear. Just ask Tomás Gomez. Sometimes I wonder whether I'm really doing my customers a favour by stuffing the things they love. My job is to freeze memories, preserve them in solid form. But there's something unhealthy about it. You don't move on. I can see it in my customers, they're frozen themselves, they're stuffed themselves, you know?’"

关键词：fade a little / don't disappear / doing my customers a favour / freeze memories / preserve them / solid form / unhealthy / move on / frozen themselves / stuffed themselves

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"freeze memories"是冻结记忆——标本是"冻结"的记忆。"stuffed themselves"是填充——客户也被"填充"了。
