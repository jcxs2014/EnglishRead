# 27. Skyways II, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Gomez 在珠宝店前驻足，想起亡妻 Monica，看到 Hanson 在追踪自己；他躲进洗手间分解步枪，而 Hanson 和 Myers 等人也赶到，在洗手间外形成对峙。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 追捕——Gomez 的内心独白（对妻子的回忆）与外部追捕形成强烈对比。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的柔情"——Gomez 看着珠宝店戒指思念妻子，揭示他"有人性"的一面；"猫鼠游戏"——Hanson 和 Myers 追踪 Gomez 到洗手间，但不敢轻易进入。
- **人物弧线**：Gomez 从"冷血杀手"变为"悲伤的丈夫"——他的动机再次得到人性化解释；Hanson 从"追捕者"变为"暗杀者"——他计划在洗手间里杀人。
- **叙事手法**：多视角交叉——Gomez、Hanson、Myers/Oz 三视角快速切换，节奏紧张。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "It was a Harry Winston diamond ring. It lay cushioned on black velvet in the jewellery-store window and twinkled more brightly than any of the others. Tomorrow would have been our wedding anniversary."

关键词：Harry Winston / diamond ring / cushioned / black velvet / twinkled / wedding anniversary

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"Harry Winston"是奢侈品牌——他买得起但妻子不要。"wedding anniversary"是回忆——他想起亡妻。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"would have been"是虚拟——妻子已死，纪念日不再。

> **原句 2:** "Monica hadn't wanted a ring like that, though, and she didn't get one either, but I knew she would have loved it. And I would have loved it. Loved to have been standing in the kitchen making our breakfast, knowing that right then she was waking up and discovering the box on the pillow."

关键词：would have loved / making breakfast / discovering the box / pillow

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"would have loved"是虚拟语气——他想象一个不可能的未来。"box on the pillow"是浪漫——戒指放在枕头边。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"making breakfast"是日常——他怀念平凡的夫妻生活。

> **原句 3:** "No, it wasn't correct to say our anniversary would have been tomorrow, it was tomorrow. You can't stop the days from coming. Time just keeps rolling along, no matter how meaninglessly."

关键词：correct / anniversary / can't stop the days / rolling along / meaninglessly

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"can't stop the days"是哲学——时间不因人的悲伤而停止。"meaninglessly"是虚无——时间没有意义。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"rolling along"是比喻——时间像轮子一样滚动。

> **原句 4:** "The security guard inside the jeweller's stood with his arms folded, swaying back and forth on his toes. There was no music to sway to, the stores at Tracks quit playing canned music after research showed that it distracted people from their shopping."

关键词：security guard / arms folded / swaying / canned music / distracted

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"canned music"是细节——商场不再播放音乐。"distracted"是原因——音乐让人分心。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"swaying"是观察——他在分析保安的行为。

> **原句 5:** "The guard could see me out here. From the scarred Latino face and the moth-eaten hoodie he guessed I couldn't afford their jewels. Maybe he was wondering what was inside the bubble wrap. When you work security I guess you see potential threats everywhere. You don't ask yourself, ‘Is that thing there a rifle?’ You ask, ‘Can I exclude completely the possibility that that thing there is a rifle?’"

关键词：scarred / Latino / moth-eaten / afford / potential threats / exclude completely

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"moth-eaten"是比喻——连帽衫像被虫蛀过。"exclude completely"是逻辑——保安用排除法判断威胁。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"potential threats"是职业习惯——保安看谁都像威胁。

> **原句 6:** "Well, anyway, I guess that's a question people in this country must have been asking themselves ever since the birth of the nation: does that person intend to shoot me?"

关键词：birth of the nation / intend to shoot me

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"birth of the nation"是历史——美国建国以来的问题。"intend to shoot me"是核心——每个人都可能开枪。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"this country"是美国——枪支文化的根源。

> **原句 7:** "I raised my gaze. I wasn't looking at the ring now but at the reflection in the glass. Behind me, on the other side of the shopping mall where people were hurrying along, a man was standing talking into his phone as he studied the interior of the pizza restaurant I had been sitting in just a few minutes ago. There was something familiar about him. Could that really be Olav Hanson, the Milkman?"

关键词：raised my gaze / reflection / familiar / Olav Hanson / Milkman

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"reflection"是视觉——他通过玻璃反射看到身后。"Milkman"是绰号——Hanson 的代号。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"familiar"是直觉——他认出了 Hanson。

> **原句 8:** "I looked up into the camera above the skyway I stepped onto. Looked down again. A cop car glided out into the street below me. Then another. Both had blue lights but no sirens. Like something was urgent, but they mustn't be heard just yet, like beasts of prey sneaking up on their victims."

关键词：skyway / cop car / glided / blue lights / no sirens / beasts of prey / sneaking up / victims

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"beasts of prey"是比喻——警察像捕食者。"sneaking up"是动作——悄悄接近。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"no sirens"是细节——警察没有开警笛，说明是秘密行动。

> **原句 9:** "Kay Myers stepped up her pace. Olav Hanson made his way further into the centre. There were a lot of people now it was evening and he wasn't able to take in all the faces heading his way."

关键词：stepped up her pace / made his way / evening / take in all the faces

为什么这样写：Myers 的"stepped up her pace"是加速——她在加快脚步。"take in all the faces"是视觉——人脸太多。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"evening"是时间——晚上人多，难以辨认。

> **原句 10:** "The plan could still work if he could only locate that damn Gomez. But it had to be now. Soon the place would be swarming with police, and once Gomez was under arrest and being held in custody it would be more complicated to get rid of him."

关键词：locate / damn / swarming with police / under arrest / in custody / get rid of him

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"damn"是愤怒——他在骂 Gomez。"get rid of him"是谋杀——他要"除掉"Gomez。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"in custody"是拘留——一旦被拘留，就难以动手。

> **原句 11:** "A little boy in a Timberwolves T-shirt approached, dancing in front of his parents, blissfully ignorant of the future. Ignorant of the fact that one day he would be what? A corrupt police officer with a failed football career behind him, two failed marriages and a failure of a son? A killer?"

关键词：Timberwolves / dancing / blissfully ignorant / corrupt / failed / killer

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"blissfully ignorant"是幸福——孩子不知道未来。"corrupt"是自我认知——他知道自己是腐败的。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"failed"是重复——他的人生充满失败。

> **原句 12:** "Olav hadn't killed anyone yet, not directly. What would it feel like to shoot someone, to kill them? He didn't know, but the thought of it didn't bother him as much as it probably should have. Maybe because he wasn't doing it for pleasure but to survive."

关键词：not directly / bother / pleasure / survive

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"not directly"是间接——他之前"间接"害死人。"survive"是动机——他杀人为了"生存"。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"pleasure"是对比——他不是为了快感，而是为了活命。

> **原句 13:** "No, shooting this killer wasn't something he would lose any sleep over. Quite the opposite, he would sleep better. He would even feel better, once he got it done. Straightened out a couple of things. Got back to being the man he once was. If he could get it done."

关键词：lose any sleep / sleep better / straightened out / the man he once was / get it done

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"lose any sleep"是失眠——他不内疚。"the man he once was"是过去——他想回到从前。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"get it done"是任务——他需要"完成"这件事。

> **原句 14:** "He had caught a glimpse of a face in the crowd a little further ahead. It was him. He recognised the hoodie and the bubble-wrap package beneath the arm. Olav Hanson veered over to the left of the walkway to intercept, with the result that two tall, laughing teenage boys bumped into him and he lost sight of Lobo."

关键词：caught a glimpse / recognised / hoodie / veered / intercept / bumped into / lost sight

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"caught a glimpse"是瞬间——他瞥见了。"veered"是转向——他改变方向去拦截。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"lost sight"是失误——他失去了目标。

> **原句 15:** "Restrooms. No cameras there. No witnesses if the arrest took place in one of the stalls."

关键词：restrooms / no cameras / no witnesses / stalls

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"no cameras"是机会——洗手间没有监控。"stalls"是隔间——私密空间。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"arrest"是借口——他说是"逮捕"，实则是"暗杀"。

> **原句 16:** "Olav Hanson put his hand inside his jacket again. Fought his way down the corridor. The door to the bathroom swung open, a man walked out and Olav caught a glimpse of the backs lined up along the washbasins."

关键词：put his hand inside his jacket / fought his way / swung open / caught a glimpse / washbasins

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"hand inside his jacket"是枪——他准备拔枪。"washbasins"是洗手池——洗手间设施。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"fought his way"是艰难——他在人群中挤。

> **原句 17:** "He no longer felt the pain in his knee, just a rush, a delicious free fall, a certainty that the moment had come, the seconds were ticking down to the final whistle at what might possibly be a defeat, but that it was his turn to tackle someone out of the life they were dreaming about."

关键词：no longer felt the pain / rush / delicious free fall / certainty / ticking down / final whistle / tackle / dreaming about

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"delicious free fall"是快感——他感到"自由落体"的快感。"final whistle"是足球术语——终场哨。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"tackle"是双关——足球中的"铲球"，也是"阻止"。

> **原句 18:** "‘Hanson!’ The voice came from behind and cut through the steady buzz of noise from Track Plaza. Hanson cursed inwardly. It was Kay Myers's voice."

关键词：cut through / steady buzz / cursed inwardly

为什么这样写：Myers 的"cut through"是穿透——她的声音穿过噪音。"cursed inwardly"是内心——Hanson 在心里骂。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"inwardly"是压抑——他不能出声。

> **原句 19:** "They stopped in front of Olav Hanson, who gave them a look like a man who had just lost a poker pot."

关键词：stopped in front of / lost a poker pot

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"poker pot"是比喻——他像输了一样。"look"是表情——他的脸色。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"lost"是失败——他的计划被打断了。

> **原句 20:** "‘That's funny,’ said Kay. ‘Because it looked like you were on your way to the restroom where the video centre says Gomez just went in.’"

关键词：funny / on your way / video centre / Gomez just went in

为什么这样写：Myers 的"funny"是讽刺——她在质疑。"video centre"是监控——他们知道 Gomez 在哪。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"that's funny"是尴尬——他被发现了。

> **原句 21:** "‘You have trouble with your hearing?’ asked Kay and turned to one of the uniforms. ‘Keep people away. No one is allowed to enter this bathroom.’"

关键词：trouble with your hearing / keep people away / no one is allowed

为什么这样写：Myers 的"trouble with your hearing"是讽刺——她怀疑 Hanson 装聋。"keep people away"是命令——清场。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"no one is allowed"是封锁——他们围住了洗手间。

> **原句 22:** "Hanson looked at O'Rourke. ‘If you go in there, remember the guy is armed and dangerous. A lot of people might get hurt, so I wouldn't exactly hesitate to … you know –’ Hanson raised his right hand and crooked his index finger."

关键词：armed and dangerous / get hurt / hesitate / crooked his index finger

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"crooked his index finger"是手势——他在模仿开枪。"hesitate"是暗示——不要犹豫。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"you know"是暗示——他在教 O'Rourke 杀人。

> **原句 23:** "The restroom was big, big as an airport restroom, with eight urinals, most of them occupied. Further in at least a dozen stalls. There were no windows in here. I walked past the men standing and washing or drying their hands at the basins in front of the mirror."

A large fan whirred in the ceiling above the stall. I tore off the note, pushed open the door, stepped inside, locked it and unwrapped the gun and the leather case from the bubble wrap. Then I started to strip the weapon, breaking it down to its component parts."
