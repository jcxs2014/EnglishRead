# 12. Hanson, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Hanson 下班后独自走向停车场，被一个持刀少年抢劫——他没有开枪，反而给了少年 200 美元买鞋，因为他想起了自己的儿子 Sean。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 / 人性——Hanson 的"反派"形象在此章被颠覆：他有过去（被诬陷的 30 年）、有痛苦（与儿子分离）、有善意（给少年钱）。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："霸凌者的温柔"——Hanson 在办公室是霸凌者，但在停车场他是一个被生活击垮的父亲。
- **人物弧线**：Hanson 从 ch07 的"狱霸"变为一个有血有肉的人——他的"坏选择"毁了他的职业生涯，但他仍在试图做正确的事。
- **叙事手法**：纯 Hanson 第一人称——读者第一次进入他的内心，发现他的"恶"背后是痛苦。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "AFTER SAYING GOODNIGHT TO KJOS, Olav Hanson crossed the parking lot. Looked at his watch, a present from a time long gone. Already he regretted drinking those three beers. Or had it been four?"

关键词：crossed the parking lot / present from a time long gone / regretted / three beers / four

为什么这样写：开篇即建立 Hannon 的孤独——他独自走向停车场，手表是"过去的礼物"（暗示他不再拥有值得纪念的时刻）。"three beers. Or had it been four?"暗示他的酗酒问题。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"regret"是自我意识的时刻——他知道自己在毁掉自己。

> **原句 2:** "In the first place there was the risk of being stopped, and he knew that one day he would meet some keen young policeman who would not be influenced by the fact that the man in the car he stopped was a fellow officer."

关键词：risk of being stopped / keen young policeman / fellow officer / influenced

为什么这样写：Hanson 知道"老规则"正在失效——新一代警察不会给同事开绿灯。"keen young policeman"暗示他对年轻一代的敌意。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"fellow officer"是讽刺——他曾经是"自己人"，现在不是了。

> **原句 3:** "Women were like dogs, the smaller they were, the more noise they made. But then Violet was one of the reasons he needed these few hours to himself after work, either in a bar or down by the river with his fishing rod."

关键词：Women were like dogs / smaller / more noise / Violet / fishing rod

为什么这样写：Hanson 的性别歧视是赤裸的——"Women were like dogs"是典型的厌女言论。但"fishing rod"暗示他也有柔软的一面——他需要独处。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"fishing rod"是他与 Oz 的共同点——两人都需要"逃离"（Oz 用酒精，Hanson 用钓鱼）。

> **原句 4:** "She hadn't been willing to listen to Olav's explanation that Sean had certain difficulties, she made him choose, her or his son, no discussion. So he'd made his choice. The wrong one."

关键词：Sean / certain difficulties / made him choose / her or his son / no discussion / wrong one

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"wrong one"是全书最痛苦的选择——他选择了妻子而非儿子。"no discussion"暗示 Violet 的控制欲。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"wrong one"与 Oz 的"wrong one"（Alice）形成对照——两个男人都做出了错误的选择，但 Hanson 的选择更不可逆。

> **原句 5:** "Then, of course, it was more opportune to give a hand up to someone like Kay Myers. Female, black, probably lesbian too – the bosses could tick all the diversity boxes there."

关键词：opportune / give a hand up / Kay Myers / female / black / lesbian / tick all the diversity boxes

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"diversity boxes"是赤裸的种族主义/性别歧视——他认为 Kay 的晋升是因为她的身份，而非能力。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"tick all the diversity boxes"是 Nesbø 对美国"政治正确"的讽刺——Hanson 是"老白男"的代表，他感到被时代抛弃。

> **原句 6:** "And everything was down to a single moment of weakness, one single bad decision taken thirty years earlier. Did he regret it? Of course he did, but once you've let the genie out of the bottle …"

关键词：single moment of weakness / one single bad decision / thirty years earlier / genie out of the bottle

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"genie out of the bottle"暗示他 30 年前犯了不可挽回的错误——可能是暴力、可能是腐败。"genie"暗示一旦释放就无法收回。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"thirty years earlier"与 Oz 的"twelve years"（Alice）形成对照——两人的痛苦都有时间维度，但 Hanson 的更漫长。

> **原句 7:** "A figure ghosted out from between two cars. Die Man. That was Olav's immediate response when he saw the hoodie."

关键词：ghosted out / Die Man / hoodie / immediate response

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"Die Man"是警察的术语——看到连帽衫就想到罪犯。"ghosted out"暗示少年的突然出现。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"immediate response"暴露了他的职业本能——他首先想到的是"威胁"，而非"人"。

> **原句 8:** "The voice was that of a boy, and he was standing so far away he would have had to take at least two steps before he could use the knife. Die Man would never have sent a frightened amateur out without a gun."

关键词：voice of a boy / standing so far away / frightened amateur / without a gun

为什么这样写：Hanson 的专业分析——"frightened amateur"与"Die Man"的对比暗示这不是职业罪犯。"without a gun"是关键——持枪与持刀的区别。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"Die Man"经验让他能迅速判断威胁程度——他不是一个无能的警察，只是一个被边缘化的警察。

> **原句 9:** "‘Don't move or I'll kill you,’ he said calmly but very clearly. In his experience, use of the simple, precise word 'kill' had a more powerful effect than any macho stuff about 'blowing your brains out' and other such euphemisms."

关键词：Don't move / kill you / calmly / very clearly / simple / precise / macho stuff / blowing your brains out / euphemisms

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"kill"是简洁的、专业的——他知道如何控制局面。"macho stuff"的讽刺暗示他厌倦了"硬汉"表演。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"calmly but very clearly"与 Oz 的"rushing sound"形成对照——两个警察，一个冷静，一个失控。

> **原句 10:** "The figure twitched. Flight mechanism – the fight reflex had already disappeared. He stayed where he was. The freeze alternative had triumphed."

关键词：twitched / Flight mechanism / fight reflex / freeze alternative / triumphed

为什么这样写：Hanson 的专业分析——"flight mechanism"（逃跑机制）与"freeze alternative"（冻结反应）是心理学/警察术语。"triumphed"暗示少年选择了"冻结"。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"freeze alternative"与 Oz 的"fight"形成对照——面对威胁，少年选择冻结，Oz 选择战斗。

> **原句 11:** "Definitely an amateur. A pro would know the chances of someone bothering to shoot a fleeing would-be robber in the back, someone armed only with a knife, were minimal."

关键词：amateur / pro / chances / shoot a fleeing would-be robber / armed only with a knife / minimal

为什么这样写：Hanson 的经验——他知道"pro"不会跑，因为警察不太可能开枪打一个持刀逃跑的少年。"minimal"是关键——风险很小。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"amateur"判断让他决定不开枪——他在评估，而非本能反应。

> **原句 12:** "The knife clinked against the asphalt and when the boy raised his arms above his head his hood slid down. Olav nearly jumped. Not just because the kid was so young, but because for a fraction of a second he reminded him of Sean."

关键词：clinked / asphalt / hood slid down / nearly jumped / fraction of a second / reminded him of Sean

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"nearly jumped"是情感爆发——少年像他的儿子 Sean。"fraction of a second"暗示他的父爱被触发。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"Sean"是他所有行为的动机——他给少年钱，是因为他无法给 Sean 钱。

> **原句 13:** "‘What's your name?’ asked Olav. The boy stopped staring at the gun and looked up at him and said nothing. ‘Your first name'll do,’ said Olav. The boy swallowed. ‘Elliot,’ he said, a sob in his throat."

关键词：What's your name / first name'll do / swallowed / Elliot / sob in his throat

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"Your first name'll do"是温柔的他不再要求全名（警察程序），而是要求"名字"（人际连接）。"sob in his throat"暗示少年的恐惧。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"first name'll do"是他与少年建立连接的方式——他不再是警察，而是"人"。

> **原句 14:** "‘For shoes,’ said the boy. ‘Shoes?’ ‘New Nike shoes.’ Olav wasn't shocked. He gave an exasperated sigh. ‘How much do they cost?’ ‘Two hundred and forty-one dollars.’"

关键词：shoes / New Nike shoes / exasperated sigh / Two hundred and forty-one dollars

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"wasn't shocked"暗示他见过太多这样的故事——少年为鞋抢劫。"exasperated sigh"是无奈，而非愤怒。"Two hundred and forty-one dollars"的精确数字让少年显得真实。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"exasperated sigh"是对"美国梦"的讽刺——一个少年需要 241 美元的鞋，而他只能通过抢劫获得。

> **原句 15:** "‘Here's two hundred, it's all I have. Is that enough to …?’ The boy nodded hesitantly. He looked as though he was wondering what kind of trap he was being lured into."

关键词：two hundred / all I have / nodded hesitantly / trap / lured into

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"all I have"是真实的——他掏出所有钱。"trap"暗示少年的怀疑——他不相信有人会无条件帮助他。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"lured into"是少年的创伤反应——他习惯了被欺骗。

> **原句 16:** "‘All I ask is that you promise me … or no, that you promise yourself one thing. That this is the one mistake you get away with. Like a mulligan in golf, get me?’"

关键词：promise me / promise yourself / one mistake / get away with / mulligan / golf

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"mulligan"是高尔夫术语——一次免费重试。"promise yourself"而非"promise me"——他在要求少年对自己负责。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"mulligan"暗示他自己也想要一次重试——他的人生已经无法重试，但少年还有机会。

> **原句 17:** "‘Go buy yourself those shoes, Elliot. But every time you lace them up I want you to think that you got them so you could run from the life that's chasing behind you. And I hope you win, boy.’"

关键词：lace them up / run from the life / chasing behind you / I hope you win

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"lace them up"是双关——系鞋带，也是"系紧"（准备逃跑）。"run from the life"暗示少年被环境追逐。"I hope you win"是祝福——Hanson 知道少年很可能不会赢。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"chasing behind you"是全书的主题——每个人都在被过去追逐（Hanson 被 30 年前的错误追逐，Oz 被 Alice 追逐）。

> **原句 18:** "Olav stayed where he was. He could hear himself panting and guessed that his pulse was racing faster than he realised. He knew he could never tell Violet what had just happened. She wouldn't understand, she'd say he threw his money away, even rewarded a criminal for being just that."

关键词：panting / pulse racing / never tell Violet / wouldn't understand / threw his money away / rewarded a criminal

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"never tell Violet"暗示他的婚姻已经破裂——他不被理解。"threw his money away"是 Violet 的可能反应——她不懂。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"rewarded a criminal"是社会的看法——给少年钱是"奖励犯罪"，但 Hanson 知道这是"拯救一个孩子"。
