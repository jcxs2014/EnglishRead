# 47. Red Light, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 在车内与 Kay 通话，确认 Mike Lunde 就是 Goya——他戴着人皮面具伪装成 Goya；Lunde 已带着步枪前往体育场，目标是市长 Patterson；Oz 在红绿灯前犹豫——去商店还是体育场。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 决策——Oz 知道了真相，但必须在"去商店阻止 Lunde"和"去体育场救市长"之间做出选择。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："人皮面具"——Lunde 戴着 Goya 的人皮面具；"红绿灯"——Oz 在红绿灯前犹豫，象征"选择"。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"发现者"变为"行动者"——他必须做出选择；Kay 从"警察"变为"协调者"——她联系 Walker 警告他。
- **叙事手法**：对话 + 内心独白——Oz 与 Kay 的电话对话，Oz 的内心独白在红绿灯前展开。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bob drove as fast as he dared – and as fast as the Volvo managed – along the highway to the city centre. He drove with one hand and held his cell in the other. Yes, he wished he had a pistol. Yes, he wished he had a Kojak light. Yes, he wished he had a better brain and had deciphered the writing on the wall earlier."

关键词：drove as fast as he dared / Volvo / highway / city centre / one hand / cell / pistol / Kojak light / deciphered / writing on the wall

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Kojak light"是警灯——他想要警灯。"writing on the wall"是墙上的字——他早该看明白。

> **原句 2:** "When she took the call he could hear she was running. ‘What's happening, Kay?’ ‘I'm headed for my car. I've made a few calls and done some checking and it looks like the house in the forest is owned by a group of artists who practise something they call rogue taxidermy."

关键词：running / headed for my car / made a few calls / checking / house / forest / owned / group of artists / practise / rogue taxidermy

为什么这样写：Kay 的"rogue taxidermy"是 rogue 标本术——一种艺术形式。

> **原句 3:** "‘I just spoke with one of them and she told me that after they rented new premises in the city the place out at Cedar Creek has hardly been used. I asked about the refrigerated room, and she said a number of the artists used it, including this Emily Lunde, the woman who owns the booth with the body in it.’"

关键词：premises / Cedar Creek / hardly been used / refrigerated room / artists / Emily Lunde / booth / body

为什么这样写：Kay 的"refrigerated room"是冷藏室——艺术家们使用的。

> **原句 4:** "‘Who is Mike Lunde?’ ‘A taxidermist. He's been wearing a Tomás Gomez mask.’ Bob waited and let that sink in, let her brain trace the line from the flayed body and Tomás Gomez on the security videos."

关键词：taxidermist / Tomás Gomez mask / sink in / trace / line / flayed body / security videos

为什么这样写：Oz 的"let that sink in"是让她消化——他让她消化这个信息。

> **原句 5:** "‘Jesus,’ Kay whispered, as though not daring to say it out loud. ‘Are you saying that –’ ‘Yeah. He's been using Tomás Gomez's face and hands.’"

关键词：Jesus / whispered / not daring / using / face and hands

为什么这样写：Kay 的"Jesus"是耶稣——她在惊叹。"whispered"是低语——她不敢大声说。

> **原句 6:** "‘God. He's at the stadium. Gomez … or, yeah, the guy the cameras picked up there yesterday while he was doing reconnaissance. He's going to shoot someone there.’ ‘Someone?’ ‘The most obvious target would be Mayor Patterson. Any minute now he's going to be speaking in front of 60,000 people, and it's going out live on TV.’"

关键词：stadium / cameras / picked up / yesterday / reconnaissance / shoot / target / Mayor Patterson / 60,000 people / live on TV

为什么这样写：Kay 的"reconnaissance"是侦察——Lunde 昨天在侦察。

> **原句 7:** "‘It is Patterson,’ he said quietly. ‘His masterpiece.’ ‘What?’ ‘He told his sister he was going to unveil his masterpiece today. I thought he was referring to this Labrador he's been working on.’ ‘What?’ ‘Mike Lunde is going to crown his work with the unveiling of his last masterpiece. And an unveiling needs an audience.’"

关键词：masterpiece / crown / unveiling / audience

为什么这样写：Oz 的"masterpiece"是杰作——Lunde 的杰作。"unveiling"是揭幕——需要观众。

> **原句 8:** "Bob heard a change in the acoustics around Kay and realised she must now be sitting in her car. ‘Give me a description,’ she said. ‘I need to ring Walker and warn them they're looking for the wrong man.’"

关键词：change / acoustics / sitting in her car / description / ring Walker / warn / wrong man

为什么这样写：Kay 的"acoustics"是声音变化——她坐进了车里。

> **原句 9:** "Bob gave Kay a quick description of Mike Lunde and the few bits of personal information he had about him. She repeated after him, he confirmed it, then she hung up."

关键词：quick description / bits / personal information / repeated / confirmed / hung up

为什么这样写：Oz 的"quick description"是快速描述——他描述了 Lunde。

> **原句 10:** "Saturday traffic was light and Bob had already reached the city centre. He stopped at a red light. Hesitated. A left turn would take him to the store, a right to the stadium. Kay hadn't queried why Bob hadn't mentioned this Mike Lunde before. Maybe because there wasn't time. Maybe because she didn't want to know. No matter which way he turned now, he would still have a lot to answer for. But right now he didn't care a damn about that. Right now all that mattered was to make the right choice, chop the tree down from the correct side and let the splinters fall where they would."

关键词：light / city centre / red light / Hesitated / left turn / store / stadium / queried / mentioned / answer for / didn't care a damn / right choice / chop the tree down / correct side / splinters

为什么这样写：Oz 的"chop the tree down"是砍树——他要做正确的选择。"splinters"是碎片——让碎片落在该落的地方。
