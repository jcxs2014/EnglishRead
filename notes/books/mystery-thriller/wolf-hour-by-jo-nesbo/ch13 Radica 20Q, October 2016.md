# 13. Radica 20Q, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 在 Phillips 的公寓里，用 Radica 20Q 玩具思考自杀；他回顾了 Tomás Gomez 的公寓，突然意识到——Gomez 的公寓之所以熟悉，是因为它像他自己的公寓一样空。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 / 顿悟——Oz 考虑自杀（"speedometer showed a hundred before closing his eyes"），但也找到了案件的突破口。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："自杀念头"——Oz 第一次认真考虑死亡，但"the thought hadn't immediately frightened him"暗示他已经超越了恐惧。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从 ch10 的暴力爆发转向内省——他不再用暴力逃避，而是用思考面对。
- **叙事手法**：独白式——整章是 Oz 的内心独白，读者直接进入他的意识流。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "BOB DROVE AIMLESSLY. TURNED OFF his playlist. He was sick to death of it. It had been on repeat for too long, just the same way he had."

关键词：aimlessly / playlist / sick to death / on repeat

为什么这样写：Oz 的"aimlessly"暗示他没有目标——他只是在开车，而非去某个地方。"on repeat"双关：音乐在重复，他的人生也在重复。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"sick to death"是对自己的厌倦——他厌倦了自己。

> **原句 2:** "The radio was tuned to a heavy rock station and the singer was reeling off the usual brain-dead, aggressive clichés. This time about a guy whose woman had left him. He cruises the highway, horny as a dog, he'll burn a house down if it gets in his way, got his woman by the throat, smashed up her face."

关键词：heavy rock station / reeling off / brain-dead / aggressive clichés / horny as a dog / burn a house down / by the throat / smashed up her face

为什么这样写：NRA 广播的内容是"有毒的男子气概"的代表——"horny as a dog"、"burn a house down"、"smashed up her face"是 Oz 的镜像（他差点对 Alice 这样做）。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"brain-dead"评价是对自己的投射——他也是"brain-dead, aggressive"的。

> **原句 3:** "‘That was Ted Nugent with "Stranglehold",’ the DJ said, with the obligatory heavy rocker's crunch in his voice. ‘And rejoice, people – in just four days the NRA's annual conference gets under way right here in our very own Minneapolis, and Nugent is gonna be there!’"

关键词：Ted Nugent / Stranglehold / obligatory / heavy rocker's crunch / rejoice / NRA / annual conference / under way

为什么这样写：NRA 大会在明尼阿波利斯召开——这是 Nesbø 对美国枪支文化的讽刺。"rejoice"（欢呼）是反讽——枪支暴力不值得欢呼。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"rejoice"是对美国政治的嘲讽——他即将参加 NRA 大会（作为警察），但他内心反对枪支。

> **原句 4:** "‘Along with sixty thousand others,’ said his sidekick. ‘I think it's very generous of the NRA to hold their conference here with the strict gun control legislation we have in this state. In other states you can at least buy yourself a machine gun!’"

关键词：sixty thousand others / sidekick / generous / strict gun control legislation / machine gun

为什么这样写：广播中的"machine gun"讽刺——明尼苏达州有严格的枪支法律，但 NRA 仍在这里开会。"generous"是反讽。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"strict gun control legislation"与 Jordan 的枪支暴力形成对照——法律存在，但无法执行。

> **原句 5:** "Bob switched off the radio. Took a deep breath. And had an idea: that if he put his foot down now and waited till the speedometer showed a hundred before closing his eyes then he wouldn't have to face another day."

关键词：switched off / deep breath / put his foot down / speedometer / showed a hundred / closing his eyes / face another day

为什么这样写：Oz 的自杀计划是精确的——"speedometer showed a hundred"（100 英里/小时）然后闭眼。"face another day"暗示他每天都在"面对"，而死亡是停止面对。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"had an idea"是冷静的——他不是在冲动，而是在计划。

> **原句 6:** "He pushed the thought away, but he was shaken. It wasn't the first time he had thought about taking his own life, but it was the first time the thought hadn't immediately frightened him instead of seeming almost tempting."

关键词：pushed the thought away / shaken / taking his own life / hadn't immediately frightening / tempting

为什么这样写：Oz 的"shaken"不是因为他想死，而是因为他不害怕死。"tempting"（诱人的）暗示死亡对他有吸引力——停止痛苦。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"hadn't immediately frightened him"是危险的信号——他已经超越了恐惧。

> **原句 7:** "Well, OK, now he was scared by the fact that he hadn't been scared. He sat up straight in his car. He had to do something. The bar lady had been right. He had to change tack."

关键词：scared by the fact / hadn't been scared / sat up straight / do something / change tack

为什么这样写：Oz 的"scared by the fact that he hadn't been scared"是元恐惧——他对自己的不恐惧感到恐惧。"change tack"（改变策略）是 Liza 的建议。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"sat up straight"是身体性的——他在用身体动作阻止自杀念头。

> **原句 8:** "Bob reached out and turned on the radio again. Tried to focus his thoughts. Tomás Gomez. The apartment. Why did he keep returning to the apartment? Was there something he'd seen there, something his subconscious had noticed but which hadn't managed to make it to the surface?"

关键词：focus his thoughts / Tomás Gomez / apartment / keep returning / subconscious / noticed / make it to the surface

为什么这样写：Oz 的"subconscious"是侦探的直觉——他看到了什么，但意识还没处理。"make it to the surface"暗示真相在水下。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"keep returning"是强迫性的——他被公寓吸引，但不知道为什么。

> **原句 9:** "He went over the details one by one. The couch. The cupboards. The syringe. The bathroom. The cat. The neighbours. No, that wasn't it. Was it the smell? The decor? No, not that either. It was something about … the whole thing. Or the emptiness. It reminded him of something. What was it?"

关键词：went over the details / one by one / couch / cupboards / syringe / bathroom / cat / neighbours / smell / decor / the whole thing / the emptiness / reminded him

为什么这样写：Oz 的"one by one"是侦探方法——逐一排查。"emptiness"是关键词——不是"有什么"，而是"没有什么"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"reminded him of something"是直觉——他的潜意识已经知道答案。

> **原句 10:** "It was past one as Bob slowly cruised the nocturnal silence of the Phillips streets. Passed the tiny, one-storey houses. Some shacks, some with boarded-up windows, but also well-maintained houses behind newly painted white fences and notices with 'We care' on them."

关键词：past one / cruised / nocturnal silence / Phillips streets / tiny / one-storey houses / shacks / boarded-up windows / well-maintained / newly painted white fences / We care

为什么这样写：Oz 的"nocturnal silence"是孤独的——凌晨 1 点，只有他一个人。"We care"的告示是讽刺——社区在衰败，但告示说"我们在乎"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"cruised"是缓慢的——他在观察，而非赶路。

> **原句 11:** "Realtors trying to tempt buyers from out of town liked to stress how central Phillips was, how many parks it had, how it was one of the most ethnically diverse neighbourhoods in the city, housing immigrants from all over the world. They never wrote about how poor Phillips was, how many criminal gangs there were, and that after sunset the parks turned into no-go areas and that on Bloomington Avenue, with drug dealers standing in front of every second house, the inhabitants had to clear their lawns of hypo needles before they could mow them."

关键词：Realtors / tempt buyers / stress / central / parks / ethnically diverse / housing immigrants / poor / criminal gangs / after sunset / no-go areas / Bloomington Avenue / drug dealers / every second house / clear their lawns / hypo needles / mow them

为什么这样写：Oz 的旁白是社会批判——房地产商美化 Phillips，但现实是"no-go areas"和"hypo needles"。"every second house"是夸张——毒品无处不在。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"hypo needles"是细节——他见过太多这样的场景。

> **原句 12:** "Bob cruised past a dealer's team now, on Bloomington and 29th Street. An ethnic trinity, like a Benetton advert, one black, one white and one brown, those realtors were as good as their word. The three watched him as he cruised by. Two were just teenagers, but the third, a Latino with a porkpie hat, looked nearer thirty. On the house wall above them was some graffiti, the symbol for X-11."

关键词：dealer's team / Bloomington and 29th Street / ethnic trinity / Benetton advert / black / white / brown / as good as their word / watched him / teenagers / Latino / porkpie hat / nearer thirty / graffiti / X-11

为什么这样写：Oz 的"ethnic trinity"是讽刺——毒品交易是真正的"多元化"。"Benetton advert"是 90 年代的文化参考（Benetton 以多元种族广告闻名）。"X-11"是帮派符号——与 ch08 杀手的纹身呼应。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"porkpie hat"是细节——他注意到每个人的穿着。

> **原句 13:** "Bob pulled up at the kerb and got out. Breathed in the good, sharp night air. Passed through the gate to a two-storey brick-built house that was squeezed in between all the other single-storey buildings, let himself in to the two-room apartment he rented and tried to ignore the rancid smell that had been there since before he moved in."

关键词：pulled up at the kerb / got out / good, sharp night air / gate / two-storey brick-built house / squeezed in / single-storey buildings / let himself in / two-room apartment / rancid smell / since before he moved in

为什么这样写：Oz 的"rancid smell"是孤独的——他的公寓有异味，但他已经习惯。"squeezed in"暗示他被环境压迫。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"good, sharp night air"是对比——外面的空气比公寓好。

> **原句 14:** "He hung up his coat and jacket behind the door without turning on the light. He hadn't been living here long enough to be able to filter out the regular noises, and tonight they came from both the flat above and the one on the adjoining wall. Quarrelling from above, hip hop with a juddering bass from the left. In the bathroom he could hear his neighbours even better, the pipes carrying the sounds."

关键词：hung up / coat / jacket / behind the door / without turning on the light / filter out / regular noises / flat above / adjoining wall / Quarrelling / hip hop / juddering bass / bathroom / neighbours / pipes carrying the sounds

为什么这样写：Oz 的"without turning on the light"是孤独的——他不想看到自己的公寓。"pipes carrying the sounds"暗示他与邻居没有隐私。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"filter out"是适应——他还没适应孤独。

> **原句 15:** "He splashed his face with cold water and studied the bump on his forehead while brushing his teeth. The bruise was already turning blue, and in the course of the night the colour would hopefully become even more pronounced."

关键词：splashed / cold water / studied / bump / forehead / brushing his teeth / bruise / turning blue / course of the night / hopefully / even more pronounced

为什么这样写：Oz 的"studied the bump"是自我审视——他在看自己的伤。"hopefully become even more pronounced"是黑色幽默——他希望伤更明显，因为这是"证据"（他被袭击了）。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"brushing his teeth"是日常——即使在自杀边缘，他仍在刷牙。

> **原句 16:** "He opened the mirrored door to the bathroom cabinet. Took down a pink tray of pills. It was unopened and he noticed from the date that he hadn't taken one since July. He weighed it in his hand. Hesitated. Then put the tray back, went into the bedroom, lay down on the bed in the dark."

关键词：mirrored door / bathroom cabinet / pink tray of pills / unopened / date / July / weighed it in his hand / Hesitated / put the tray back / bedroom / lay down / in the dark

为什么这样写：Oz 的"weighed it in his hand"是物理的——他在感受药片的重量。"Hesitated"是关键——他犹豫了，意味着他不想死。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"July"暗示他已经停药两个月——他的心理健康在恶化。

> **原句 17:** "Took down a plastic ball the size of a baseball from the shelf behind the bed. It was an electric toy, a Radica 20Q. You ask it something specific and then tap in Yes or No answers to the twenty questions that appear in the small display window. Nine times out of ten it would know whether you were lying. In Bob's case ten times out of ten, because the answer was always 'ex-girlfriend'."

关键词：plastic ball / baseball / electric toy / Radica 20Q / ask it something specific / tap in Yes or No answers / twenty questions / display window / Nine times out of ten / lying / ten times out of ten / ex-girlfriend

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Radica 20Q"是玩具——他用玩具来思考。"ex-girlfriend"是答案——所有问题的答案都是 Alice。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"ten times out of ten"是绝望——他无法摆脱 Alice。

> **原句 18:** "He made up his mind to think of something else and chose 'suicide'. Radica 20Q gave up after twenty questions. Bob suspected that the word had been omitted at the programming stage."

关键词：made up his mind / think of something else / chose / suicide / gave up / twenty questions / suspected / omitted / programming stage

为什么这样写：Oz 的"suicide"是测试——他想知道玩具是否能猜到他的想法。"omitted at the programming stage"是黑色幽默——玩具的程序员没有编入"自杀"这个词。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"gave up"是玩具的失败——它无法理解 Oz 的绝望。

> **原句 19:** "He listened to the sound of Phillips. Laughter out in the streets, an angry cry, glass breaking, a car engine revving. Neighbours you never heard during the daytime. Behind the quarrelling of the adults he heard a young girl crying. The sound was faint, at the same time so distinct as to drown out all the others."

关键词：sound of Phillips / Laughter / angry cry / glass breaking / car engine revving / Neighbours / daytime / quarrelling / adults / young girl crying / faint / distinct / drown out

为什么这样写：Oz 的"young girl crying"是关键的——他听到了一个女孩在哭。"drown out all the others"暗示这个声音最重要。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"young girl"可能是 Anna（杀手的女儿）——两个叙事线在此交汇。

> **原句 20:** "Yes, he wanted out, to get away from this life, from Bob Oz. But would he give them that satisfaction? No, not yet. He would change tack. Change channels. Change focus, name, the date, himself, life. But more than the future he wanted to change the past. Not all of it, just his. Because the past is everything. Didn't someone once say that?"

关键词：wanted out / get away / Bob Oz / give them that satisfaction / not yet / change tack / change channels / change focus / name / date / himself / life / future / change the past / not all of it / just his / past is everything

为什么这样写：Oz 的"change the past"是核心主题——他无法改变过去（Alice），但他可以改变未来。"past is everything"是引用——可能是 Faulkner 或某位作家。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"give them that satisfaction"是反抗——他不想让 Hanson、Kjos、Walker 看到他崩溃。

> **原句 21:** "He unbuttoned his shirt and as he was pulling off his pants felt something in the pocket and took it out. A business card. Mike Lunde – Taxidermist. He thought of that canary. What kind of people have their pets stuffed?"

关键词：unbuttoned / pulling off his pants / pocket / business card / Mike Lunde / Taxidermist / canary / pets stuffed

为什么这样写：Oz 的"business card"是 ch05 的线索——他在公寓里找到了标本师的名片。"pets stuffed"是字面意思——Mrs White 的猫被标本师处理了。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"What kind of people have their pets stuffed?"是道德判断——他不理解这种行为。

> **原句 22:** "Bob Oz closed his eyes, took a deep breath and steeled himself for another sleepless night. And as he lay there it finally floated up to the surface. What it was that Tomás Gomez's apartment reminded him of. It was this. His own apartment. The emptiness was the same."

关键词：closed his eyes / took a deep breath / steeled himself / another sleepless night / floated up to the surface / Tomás Gomez's apartment / reminded him of / his own apartment / emptiness / the same

为什么这样写：Oz 的"floated up to the surface"是顿悟——答案终于浮现。"emptiness was the same"是全书的核心洞察——杀手和他是同一类人：孤独的、空的。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"steeled himself"是准备——他准备迎接另一个不眠之夜，但他也准备好了迎接真相。
