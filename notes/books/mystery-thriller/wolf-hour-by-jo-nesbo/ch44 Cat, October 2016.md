# 44. Cat, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 在 Chanhassen 找到 Lunde 的住处；Kay 在"恐怖之屋"发现了被剥皮的尸体（Goya 的受害者）；Oz 意识到那具尸体就是 Goya 本人（Lobo），而 Lunde 的女儿 Anna 还活着（坐在轮椅上）。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 真相——Oz 发现 Lunde 的女儿 Anna 还活着，而"恐怖之屋"里的尸体就是 Goya。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："女儿还活着"——Lunde 的女儿 Anna 没有死，她坐在轮椅上；"杀手的死亡"——Goya 被 Lunde 杀死了。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"追捕者"变为"发现者"——他发现了真相；Lunde 从"标本师"变为"复仇者"——他杀死了 Goya。
- **叙事手法**：多视角交叉——Oz 和 Kay 的电话对话，Oz 的独白。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bob turned into Erie Avenue in Chanhassen. Middle-class villas with plenty of room between them, trees and neatly trimmed lawns on both sides."

关键词：Erie Avenue / Chanhassen / Middle-class villas / plenty of room / trees / neatly trimmed lawns

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Middle-class villas"是中产阶级别墅——Lunde 的社区。"neatly trimmed lawns"是修剪整齐的草坪——社区整洁。

> **原句 2:** "He stopped in front of the address Kari had provided. Two floors. Big but standard family home with a yard in front, lawn with the grass cut short, double garage."

关键词：address / Kari / Two floors / standard family home / yard / grass cut short / double garage

为什么这样写：Oz 的"standard family home"是标准家庭住宅——Lunde 的家。"double garage"是双车库——标准配置。

> **原句 3:** "His phone vibrated. He was about to reject the call but changed his mind when he saw it came from Kay Myers. ‘Kay, thanks for the report. And the list.’"

关键词：vibrated / reject / call / Kay Myers / report / list

为什么这样写：Oz 的"vibrated"是震动——手机震动。"report"是报告——Kay 发给他的报告。

> **原句 4:** "‘You're welcome. Now it's your turn to help me.’ It could have been just a poor signal, but it sounded like she was freezing. ‘Where are you?’ ‘At a deserted house in a forest with no tracks. Listen, I broke in here without a search warrant. I found something.’"

关键词：your turn / freezing / deserted house / forest / no tracks / broke in / search warrant

为什么这样写：Kay 的"freezing"是寒冷——她听起来很冷。"search warrant"是搜查令——她没有搜查令。

> **原句 5:** "Bob didn't respond. Cops called it an own goal when you found something that could have been used in a court of law, if only you'd followed the rules."

关键词：Cops / own goal / court of law / followed the rules

为什么这样写：Oz 的"own goal"是乌龙球——警察术语，指因违规而发现的证据无效。

> **原句 6:** "‘Get out of there the same way you went in,’ he said. ‘Cover your tracks and make out like you didn't find anything. Get the search warrant then come back.’"

关键词：same way / Cover your tracks / make out / search warrant / come back

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Cover your tracks"是掩盖踪迹——他建议 Kay 掩盖。

> **原句 7:** "‘I broke open the door, but if that's "tripping up" then what's the point of being a cop? Tell me that. I sent you those reports because it's our job to protect people against … against monsters like this. I don't need a bigger office, Bob, I just need to stop this … this sickness.’"

关键词：broke open / tripping up / point of being a cop / protect / monsters / bigger office / sickness

为什么这样写：Kay 的"tripping up"是犯错——她在犯错。"monsters"是怪物——她在保护人们免受怪物伤害。

> **原句 8:** "‘A body,’ she answered. ‘We just lost the signal. Did you say a body?’ ‘Yes.’ ‘Whose body?’ ‘I don't know. I'm guessing one of Tomás Gomez's victims. We got information that he was seen here.’"

关键词：body / lost the signal / Whose body / guessing / victims / information / seen here

为什么这样写：Kay 的"body"是尸体——她发现了尸体。"victims"是受害者——Goya 的受害者。

> **原句 9:** "‘He has tattoos.’ ‘What kind?’ ‘Gang tattoos. X-11. And Black Wolves.’ ‘I thought so.’ ‘What did you think?’ ‘Call the station and ask them to get out there.’"

关键词：tattoos / Gang tattoos / X-11 / Black Wolves / station

为什么这样写：Kay 的"Gang tattoos"是帮派纹身——尸体上有纹身。"station"是警局——她打电话给警局。

> **原句 10:** "‘I know it wasn't Tomás Gomez who killed that person in the chair.’ ‘How?’ ‘Because the man in the chair is Tomás Gomez. Better known as Lobo. I have to do something here now, Kay, I'll call you back later.’"

关键词：wasn't Tomás Gomez / man in the chair / Tomás Gomez / Lobo / call you back

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Better known as Lobo"是更广为人知的名字 Lobo——Goya 就是 Lobo。

> **原句 11:** "Bob slipped the phone back into his coat pocket and stepped out of the car. It was strangely quiet, no one around. Did he wish right now that he was carrying a gun? The answer to that was straightforward. Yes he did."

关键词：slipped / coat pocket / stepped out / strangely quiet / no one around / carrying a gun / straightforward

为什么这样写：Oz 的"straightforward"是直接的——答案是直接的。

> **原句 12:** "Bob approached the house slowly, keeping his eyes on the windows. The silence was broken by the sound of a mower starting up somewhere. A ceramic nameplate hung by the door, clearly the work of a child, probably made in a handiwork class at school. Here, it said, lives Sam, Anna, Monica and Mike Lunde. The same four names Bob had found on the net in the reports of the McDeath killings in 1986. Only the father survived."

关键词：approached / windows / mower / ceramic nameplate / child / handiwork class / Sam, Anna, Monica and Mike Lunde / McDeath / 1986 / Only the father survived

为什么这样写：Oz 的"ceramic nameplate"是陶瓷名牌——门上的名牌。"handiwork class"是手工课——孩子做的。

> **原句 13:** "Bob pressed the doorbell. Heard it ring inside the house. Pressed again. Heard the ringing inside but no sound of approaching footsteps. He thought about the body Kay had described. Things were starting to fall into place now. Bob rang a third time. Then he walked round the house to the back, cupped his hands against the glass of the porch door and peered inside."

关键词：pressed / doorbell / ring / approaching footsteps / fall into place / porch door / peered inside

为什么这样写：Oz 的"fall into place"是各就各位——事情开始明朗。"porch door"是门廊门——他透过玻璃看。

> **原句 14:** "In the semi-darkness he saw a tidy room with furniture. It was slightly old-fashioned and conservative, as he had halfway expected. There was an open-plan kitchen with a worktop. A large painting of the family hung above the fireplace. It looked as though the painter had used the same photograph as the one in the report on the net. Bob's eyes gradually grew accustomed to the dark and he now saw that what he had at first taken to be an ordinary chair, standing with its back to him on the far side of the room, was actually a wheelchair. There was someone sitting in it. The sun caught the glossy fair hair hanging down over the back of the wheelchair. Bob called out a ‘Hello!’ but the person in the wheelchair didn't react. Thinking the shout might have been lost in the noise from the mower Bob knocked on the window. Still no reaction. The person sat there, quite motionless. Maybe she was just sleeping. He tried the porch door. It wasn't locked."

关键词：semi-darkness / tidy room / old-fashioned / conservative / open-plan kitchen / worktop / painting / fireplace / accustomed / wheelchair / glossy fair hair / react / mower / knocked / motionless / sleeping / porch door / locked

为什么这样写：Oz 的"wheelchair"是轮椅——他看到了轮椅。"motionless"是静止的——人一动不动。

> **原句 15:** "Bob pushed the door open. The penetrating, insistent engine noise of the mower entered the room with him. Still the figure in the wheelchair didn't move. Bob walked over to her. Swallowed. Recalled Mike's words. My job is to freeze memories, preserve them in solid form. But there's something unhealthy about that. Hysterical violins sounded through his head as he reached out a hand and placed it on the shoulder of the person in the wheelchair. The figure slowly rotated and then – as in the movie – came the scream. The mouth of the figure, a woman, was open. That was where the scream came from. She pulled out the earbuds she was wearing, jerking so hard that the lead came out of the cell phone in her lap and fell to the floor. Bob heard the low buzzing of classical music. ‘Oh my God, you gave me such a fright!’ exclaimed the woman. ‘Who are you?’"

关键词：penetrating / insistent / engine noise / figure / swallowed / freeze memories / preserve / solid form / unhealthy / Hysterical violins / reached out / shoulder / rotated / scream / mouth / earbuds / jerking / lead / cell phone / classical music / fright

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Hysterical violins"是歇斯底里的小提琴——他脑海中的声音。"earbuds"是耳塞——她戴着耳塞。
