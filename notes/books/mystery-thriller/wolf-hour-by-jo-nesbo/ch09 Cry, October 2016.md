# 9. Cry, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：杀手与 Oz 的双视角——杀手发现 Dante 未死，前往医院补枪；Oz 在 Cooper 前妻家外撞见 Alice 出轨，随后被 Hanson 叫回警局接待线人。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 + 低谷——杀手的计划失败（Dante 未死），Oz 的情感崩溃（Alice 出轨）。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："双线并行"——杀手与 Oz 在同一夜晚各自行动，读者知道他们即将相遇，但两人都不知道。
- **人物弧线**：杀手的"完美计划"出现裂痕（Dante 未死）；Oz 的"理智"在 Alice 面前彻底崩溃（"Think before you speak, think before your act"）。
- **叙事手法**：双第一人称——杀手（"我"）与 Oz（"Bob"）交替，两人都在追踪 Dante，但动机完全不同。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "I Was ON HIGH, I had perspective. But no gun this time, this time I was just an observer."

中文理解：我站在高处，拥有视野。但这次没有枪，这次我只是个观察者。

关键词：on high / perspective / observer / no gun

为什么这样写：开篇呼应 ch02 杀手的"高度=视野"主题——但这次他只是在观察，而非杀人。"no gun"暗示他的计划已失败（Dante 未死）。

读者视角提示：杀手在等待——他在观察医院，寻找补枪的机会。

> **原句 2:** "The anchor reported a shooting incident in Jordan in the morning in which the victim had been shot in the stomach outside his own home. Badly injured. Not killed. A person injured in a shooting is everyday stuff in Minneapolis – it hardly rates a mention on the news."

关键词：shooting incident / stomach / badly injured / not killed / everyday stuff / rates a mention

为什么这样写：杀手的失望——Dante 未死，他的计划失败。"everything stuff"揭示了一个残酷的现实：在美国，枪击致伤是日常，只有死亡才值得报道。

读者视角提示：杀手对"badly injured"的执念暴露了他的完美主义——他不仅要杀人，还要"完成"杀人。

> **原句 3:** "Bob parked some distance away from the house in the quiet street in Cooper. Retraced familiar steps he’d walked so many times before. Past that row of small houses on a slope, with steps up to the verandas and the front doors."

关键词：Cooper / retrace / familiar steps / verandas / slope

为什么这样写：Oz 回到他与 Alice 曾经的家——"retraced familiar steps"暗示他在回忆。Cooper 的温馨（charming middle-class houses）与 Jordan 的荒凉形成对照。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"familiar steps"是物理的，也是情感的——他曾经属于这里，现在不属于了。

> **原句 4:** "In the silence he heard something, something rhythmical. He looked up. The bedroom window was open. Bob and Alice’s bedroom. At first he wasn’t sure whether what he was hearing was an echo from back then, or whether it was real, and now. Not until he heard Alice’s hoarse voice sighing a name that wasn’t his name."

关键词：rhythmical / echo / hoarse voice / sighing / wasn’t his name

为什么这样写：这是全书最痛苦的段落之一——Oz 撞见 Alice 出轨。"echo from back then"暗示他无法确定这是记忆还是现实。"hoarse voice sighing a name that wasn’t his name"是极致的痛苦——她叫的是别人的名字。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"Think before you speak, think before your act"是他最后的自制——他在崩溃边缘。

> **原句 5:** "Bob bunched his fist so hard it felt as though the skin over the knuckles might split. Silence up there now, as if they were listening out for sounds. He breathed out with a shudder and pressed his forehead against the cool glass."

关键词：bunched his fist / knuckles / split / shudder / cool glass

为什么这样写：Oz 的愤怒被压制在身体里——"skin over the knuckles might split"暗示他快要失控。"cool glass"是物理的冷静，也是情感的隔绝。

读者视角提示：Oz 没有砸门——他选择了离开。这是他最后的尊严。

> **原句 6:** "Back in his car he sat and stared through the windshield. Pulled out his phone and went through his list of contacts. Anne. Aurora. Beatrice."

关键词：windshield / contacts / Anne / Aurora / Beatrice

为什么这样写：Oz 在车里翻通讯录——三个女人的名字（A 字母开头）暗示他的"One-Night Bob"习惯。但他最终没有打给她们——他在寻找的是"家"，而非性。

读者视角提示："Anne. Aurora. Beatrice."的列举方式暴露了 Oz 的记忆模式——他按字母顺序存储女人，而非按姓名。

> **原句 7:** "‘This is not a good time. Some other time, maybe.’ ‘Of course. Anyway, nice to hear your voice, Beatrice. Later then.’ ‘Tuesday next week is good.’"

关键词：not a good time / some other time / nice to hear your voice / Tuesday next week

为什么这样写：Beatrice 的拒绝是礼貌而冷淡的。"Tuesday next week is good"暗示她只是想拖延，而非真正想见面。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"nice to hear your voice"是自我安慰——他只是在寻找一个"被拒绝"的理由。

> **原句 8:** "‘There in twenty,’ he said and turned on the ignition."

关键词：there in twenty / turned on the ignition

为什么这样写：Oz 在孤独与工作之间选择了工作——"there in twenty"是他的逃避机制。"turned on the ignition"是物理的启动，也是情感的封闭。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"twoon"是精确的——他知道从 Cooper 到办公室需要二十分钟，这是他对时间的控制。

> **原句 9:** "I stood at the reception counter of the Regency Hospital. I didn’t yet know whether this was where he was, but there are two hospitals in Minneapolis where they bring people with bullet wounds. My city was not regarded as especially violent in this country, but it has two hospitals with specialists in removing bullets."

关键词：reception counter / bullet wounds / not regarded as especially violent / specialists in removing bullets

为什么这样写：杀手的冷静分析——他像侦探一样推理。"specialists in removing bullets"是一个残酷的细节：美国医院有专门的"取弹专家"。

读者视角提示：杀手对"bullet wounds"的冷静描述与他对 Dante 的执念形成对照——他不是在杀人，而是在"完成"工作。

> **原句 10:** "‘Ah, that must be the man sitting outside 531,’ he replied, and without my asking pushed the button for 5."

关键词：without my asking / pushed the button / man sitting outside 531

为什么这样写：杀手的策略——他利用医院员工的善意。"without my asking"暗示他的计划是"让对方主动帮忙"。

读者视角提示：杀手对医院布局的熟悉程度暗示他事先踩过点——这是一场有预谋的"补枪"。
