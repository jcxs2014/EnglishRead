# 35. Target, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Kay 检查靶纸上的弹孔，Hanson 给了她一份目击者名单；装修工给 Kay 送了一张邀请函（Minnehaha Park，周日下午一点）；Kay 去射击场询问 Jim 是否见过 Gomez，但没有得到有用信息；她决定不去 Cedar Creek 找老妇人了。
- **情感弧线位置**：过渡 / 铺垫——Kay 的调查继续，但进展缓慢；装修工的邀请函为后续埋下伏笔。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："靶纸"——Kay 检查靶纸上的弹孔，回忆广告语"唯一能让你感觉更好的就是射击机枪"；"射击场"——Kay 去射击场询问，Jim 承认他对不同种族面孔的识别能力有限（"生物学事实"）。
- **人物弧线**：Kay 从"被质疑"变为"独立行动"——她决定不采纳 Hanson 的建议（去找老妇人），而是回市政厅。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称独白——Kay 的视角，穿插对话。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Kay Myers's desk was located almost exactly in the middle of the open office landscape of the Homicide Department. Maybe that's why she sometimes felt herself surrounded on all sides. And longed for her own office."

关键词：desk / located / middle / open office landscape / surrounded on all sides / longed for / own office

为什么这样写：Kay 的"surrounded on all sides"是四面被围——她在办公室中间。"longed for"是渴望——她想要自己的办公室。

> **原句 2:** "She looked at the paper target they had found in the bubble wrap left behind in the shopping mall. Studied the bullet holes."

关键词：paper target / bubble wrap / shopping mall / bullet holes

为什么这样写：Kay 的"bullet holes"是弹孔——她在研究弹孔。

> **原句 3:** "Hanson coughed. ‘Hope you're not pissed that Springer put me in charge at this end?’ ‘Not at all,’ said Kay. ‘You have seniority.’"

关键词：coughed / pissed / put me in charge / seniority

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"pissed"是生气——他问 Kay 是否生气。"seniority"是资历——Kay 说 Hanson 有资历。

> **原句 4:** "‘It says here the caller thinks they saw Gomez three weeks ago?’ ‘Yes, but if you read on, you'll see she thinks she saw him again yesterday. If that's true, then she's the only person we know of – apart from the neighbours in Jordan – who's seen Gomez more than once in the same place. If there's anything in it, then it means we have a place we know he visits regularly.’"

关键词：caller / three weeks ago / yesterday / only person / apart from / neighbours in Jordan / more than once / same place / visits regularly

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"visits regularly"是定期访问——如果属实，Goya 定期去某个地方。

> **原句 5:** "Kay glanced through the notes. Aged eighty-three, address Cedar Creek. North of the city centre, more or less wilderness country. There was a separate column for the person taking the call to assess the caller's credibility. ‘Credibility rating under half, it says here.’ ‘Yes, he wasn't sure if the old lady was all there.’"

关键词：glanced through / notes / Aged eighty-three / address / Cedar Creek / north / wilderness country / separate column / assess / credibility / under half / all there

为什么这样写：Kay 的"credibility rating"是可信度评分——低于一半。"all there"是神志清楚——老妇人是否神志清楚。

> **原句 6:** "Kay looked up at Hanson. ‘Even among calls we get that sound serious, eighty per cent turn out to be fantasies. And this is from a senile old lady living somewhere out in the sticks, in wolf country?’"

关键词：sound serious / eighty per cent / fantasies / senile / out in the sticks / wolf country

为什么这样写：Kay 的"out in the sticks"是偏远地区——老妇人住在偏远地区。"wolf country"是狼的国度——有狼的地方。

> **原句 7:** "Hanson smiled and lifted his coffee cup as though to make a toast. ‘I recall someone telling me to shut up and call Walker because he'd put her in charge of the case. Well, Myers, you can call Springer. OK?’"

关键词：lifted his coffee cup / make a toast / recall / shut up / call Walker / in charge / call Springer

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"make a toast"是敬酒——他举起咖啡杯。"shut up"是闭嘴——他记得 Kay 说过。

> **原句 8:** "‘Excuse me.’ Kay opened her eyes and looked up. Her heart gave a little jump. It was the dark-eyed painter. He hadn't taken off his mask, not even the protective white cap and gloves."

关键词：Excuse me / heart gave a little jump / dark-eyed painter / mask / protective white cap / gloves

为什么这样写：Kay 的"heart gave a little jump"是心跳加速——她吓了一跳。"dark-eyed painter"是黑眼睛的装修工。

> **原句 9:** "‘I promised you an invitation,’ he said. He put a card down on her desk, turned and walked away. She watched him go. The cheek of it. He must have been warned he couldn't just wander round inside the Homicide Department where there was so much sensitive information lying about. But he'd taken the chance anyway, risked getting a dressing-down just to deliver this card to her."

关键词：promised / invitation / card / cheek / warned / wander round / sensitive information / lying about / taken the chance / risked / dressing-down / deliver

为什么这样写：装修工的"cheek"是厚脸皮——他竟敢走进来。"dressing-down"是训斥——他冒着被训斥的风险。

> **原句 10:** "She looked at it. It was the kind of invitation you buy in a store and fill out your own details on. Here it said that the invite was to Minnehaha Park, in front of the waterfalls. Sunday at one o'clock. There was no indication of what would happen there, nor was the card signed. She slipped it into a drawer. If they'd got Gomez by then, well, maybe. If not, then she'd still be sitting here."

关键词：invitation / buy in a store / fill out / details / Minnehaha Park / waterfalls / Sunday / one o'clock / no indication / signed / slipped / drawer

为什么这样写：Kay 的"Minnehaha Park"是明尼哈哈公园——邀请函上的地点。"waterfalls"是瀑布——在瀑布前。

> **原句 11:** "She picked up the paper target again. Ran her fingertips over the bullet holes. Because sometimes the only thing that is going to make you feel better is shooting a machine gun."

关键词：picked up / fingertips / bullet holes / shooting a machine gun

为什么这样写：Kay 的"shooting a machine gun"是射击机枪——广告语。

> **原句 12:** "Kay was reading the poster behind the counter when the assistant appeared in front of her. ‘Hi, I'm Jim, how can I help you today?’ ‘Kay Myers, MPD.’ She showed him her ID and put the target down on the counter. ‘Is this from here?’"

关键词：poster / assistant / appeared / Kay Myers, MPD / ID / target / counter

为什么这样写：Jim 的"assistant"是店员——他在射击场工作。"MPD"是明尼阿波利斯警察局。

> **原句 13:** "The man in the TOTAL DEFENSE T-shirt scratched his chest and studied the target. ‘This is a Krüger target, so it's from here all right – we're the only people round here who do Krüger targets. I always try to get our customers to take the target they used back home with them.’"

关键词：TOTAL DEFENSE / scratched his chest / Krüger target / all right / only people / customers / take the target / back home

为什么这样写：Jim 的"Krüger target"是克鲁格靶——靶纸品牌。"all right"是没错——就是这里。

> **原句 14:** "‘I see hundreds of new faces every day, I can't remember them all,’ said Jim, still peering in concentration at the screen. ‘But sure, we mostly get whites in here, not too many Latinos, so I ought to remember the face if he was in here recently. But to be honest I have trouble seeing differences in the faces of people of a different ethnicity from me. Hope you don't find that offensive, Detective, I heard it's a simple biological fact of life.’"

关键词：hundreds of new faces / remember / peering / concentration / mostly / whites / Latinos / ought to remember / to be honest / trouble / differences / ethnicity / offensive / biological fact

为什么这样写：Jim 的"ethnicity"是种族——他对不同种族面孔的识别能力有限。"biological fact"是生物学事实——他说是生物学事实。

> **原句 15:** "It had started to cloud over on her trip out and now the sky was covered in a sullen, lead-blue sheet. She got into her car and drove along minor roads toward 35W and the centre of town. She came to a T-junction in front of a small lake and stopped. The sign facing her said the 35W was a left turn, but it also showed that a right would take her to the 65, a road that ran in a straight line northward to Cedar Creek. Kay had decided she would ring the old lady who had called in the tip-off and try to assess its importance that way, but now she was only twenty, at the most thirty minutes' drive from where she lived. Kay hesitated. Had rush-hour traffic started? And then it was as though the heavens made the decision for her as the sky opened in torrential rain. She could no longer see the sign through the water flooding down her windshield. She set the wipers going. Then she made a left turn signal and headed west, toward city hall."

关键词：cloud over / sullen / lead-blue sheet / minor roads / 35W / centre of town / T-junction / small lake / left turn / right / 65 / straight line / northward / Cedar Creek / ring / old lady / tip-off / assess / twenty / thirty minutes' drive / hesitated / rush-hour traffic / heavens / decision / sky opened / torrential rain / no longer see / flooding / windshield / wipers / left turn signal / headed west / city hall

为什么这样写：Kay 的"torrential rain"是倾盆大雨——雨很大。"windshield"是挡风玻璃——雨水挡住视线。"heaven made the decision"是上天决定——天气替她做了决定。
