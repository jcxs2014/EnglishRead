# 50. Hector, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：市长保镖 Hector 收到消息，得知 Mike Lunde 就是刺客；他和 JTTF 的 Gerard 冲进标本店，但 Hector 被 Lunde 开枪击中，倒地身亡。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 牺牲——Hector 试图拯救市长家人，但被 Lunde 射杀。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："保镖的牺牲"——Hector 明知危险仍冲进去；"杀手的冷酷"——Lunde 毫不犹豫地开枪。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角——Hector 的视角，通过他的眼睛看到事件展开。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Herrer saw that Gerard, the security man JTTF had sent over yesterday evening, was hearing something through his headset. Hector had suggested he be included in the same loop but Gerard told him this wasn't part of 'JTTF's protocol'."

关键词：Gerard / JTTF / headset / loop / protocol

为什么这样写：Herrer 的"loop"是通讯频道——他想加入频道。"protocol"是协议——JTTF 的协议。

> **原句 2:** "‘Copy that,’ said Gerard and took his finger away from his ear. Without taking his eyes off the store he said: 'Message from the stadium. The store owner, Mike Lunde, is dangerous and potentially armed.'"

关键词：Copy that / ear / store / Mike Lunde / dangerous / potentially armed

为什么这样写：Gerard 的"Copy that"是收到——他收到了消息。"potentially armed"是可能携带武器。

> **原句 3:** "‘Meaning they think the store owner is the assassin. Is that Mike Lunde in there with them?’ ‘Yeah,’ said Hector. He knew that now wasn't the time to start handing out blame but had already worked out his own part in it."

关键词：assassin / in there / blame / worked out / part

为什么这样写：Hector 的"blame"是责怪——现在不是责怪的时候。"part"是部分——他的部分。

> **原句 4:** "Hector had known Gerard for only a few hours but from the way he moved and readied himself he could see they must have gone through a similar training. So they didn't need to exchange words but automatically split as they crossed the street so that they wouldn't be seen from the store, then made their way separately from different sides toward the store window."

关键词：similar training / automatically / split / store window

为什么这样写：Hector 的"automatically"是自动地——他们自动分开。

> **原句 5:** "Then something happened to Hector that occasionally happened in situations like this. In his mind's eye he saw the silhouette of his father's head against the sun, and heard the deep voice, at once reassuring and challenging: 'I see you.'"

关键词：occasionally / situations / mind's eye / silhouette / reassuring / challenging / I see you

为什么这样写：Hector 的"mind's eye"是脑海——他脑海中浮现父亲的形象。"I see you"是"我看见你"——父亲的话。

> **原句 6:** "Hector pushed the door open wider, careful not to make the bell ring but just enough to enable him to slip inside. Bodyguards wear dark glasses for a number of reasons, and style is not among them. One is to avoid a situation in which bright sunlight makes the pupils so small that the bodyguard is temporarily blinded on entering a darker room. Hector pulled off his shades and tossed them aside before again raising the pistol in both hands."

关键词：push / door / bell / slip inside / dark glasses / style / pupils / blinded / shades / pistol

为什么这样写：Hector 的"pupils" is 瞳孔——瞳孔缩小导致暂时失明。"shades" is 太阳镜——他摘下太阳镜。

> **原句 7:** "He had time to see the family sitting in a ring around Quentin, Jill facing him with silver duct tape over her mouth and hands behind her back. He had time to see Siri and Simon in chairs, hands bound by plastic strips to the struts on the chair backs. And he had time to see Mike Lunde, who was sitting beside Jill. And he had time to see the barrel of the rifle Mike Lunde was pointing at him. Hector even had time to see the flash of light from the mouth of the gun."

关键词：ring / Quentin / duct tape / mouth / hands / bound / plastic strips / struts / barrel / rifle / pointing / flash / mouth of the gun

为什么这样写：Hector 的"duct tape"是胶带——Jill 被胶带绑住。"flash"是闪光——他看到了枪口的闪光。

> **原句 8:** "And then it was as if he had been hit by a truck, he felt himself tumbling backward, out of the door, felt himself hit something and collapse onto the sidewalk, felt he couldn't move, the light was disappearing. Felt someone take hold of the shoulder straps of the ballistic vest he was wearing under his jacket and pull him over the asphalt as the voice of the JTTF man whose name he could no longer recall spoke above him in a loud, panting voice: 'November. Herrer has been shot by the suspect, repeat Herrer has been shot. Pulling him to safety, bleeding heavily, looks critical. Suspect is in the store with three hostages. I need an ambulance and backup. Now!'"

关键词：hit by a truck / tumbling / backward / collapse / sidewalk / disappearing / shoulder straps / ballistic vest / asphalt / panting / November / shot / safety / bleeding / critical / hostages / ambulance / backup

为什么这样写：Herrer 的"ballistic vest" is 防弹背心——他穿着防弹背心。"critical" is 危急——看起来危急。"hostages" is 人质——嫌疑犯有三名人质。
