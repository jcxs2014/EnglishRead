# 22. The Desert, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：杀手在停车场等待目标，回忆起自己的孤独和使命——他的妻儿已死，复仇是他唯一的生存理由；他在一辆蓝色 Chevrolet Silverado 上贴白色胶带作为瞄准标记。
- **情感弧线位置**：过渡 / 内心——杀手的内心独白揭示了他的孤独和决心。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的孤独"——Gomez 不是冷血的机器，而是一个被悲伤吞噬的人；"标记目标"——他在车上贴胶带，为下一次谋杀做准备。
- **人物弧线**：Gomez 从"练习射击"到"准备行动"——他的计划在进行中。
- **叙事手法**：纯杀手第一人称——梦境般的独白，揭示他的内心世界。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "A flat light lay across the desert. A huge desert that I crossed alone. I saw no other people, there in that monotonous, desolate landscape, no sign of life at all. But of course, cars count as signs of life. And this parking lot."

关键词：flat light / desert / alone / monotonous / desolate / sign of life

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"flat light"是视觉——停车场灯光单调。"desert"是比喻——停车场是"沙漠"。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"alone"是关键词——他的孤独。

> **原句 2:** "What if every person on earth apart from me had been whirled up into heaven just a moment ago by some generous-spirited Jehovah? That would have been fine actually, it wouldn't have left me any more alone than I already am."

关键词：whirled up into heaven / generous-spirited Jehovah / any more alone

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"Jehovah"是宗教——他用宗教比喻。"any more alone"是黑色幽默——他已经够孤独了。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"fine"是反常——他对"所有人都死了"无所谓。

> **原句 3:** "That I was lonely. Some days it was just fine, but at other times the loneliness and the burden of the emptiness were so great I felt they were going to crush me."

关键词：lonely / burden / emptiness / crush me

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"crush me"是身体感受——孤独像物理压力。"emptiness"是双关——内心的空虚和停车场的空旷。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"some days it was just fine"是波动——孤独时好时坏。

> **原句 4:** "First I had to do what I had to do. That was the only thing that kept me going now, the only thing that made it worth getting up in the morning."

关键词：do what I had to do / kept me going / worth getting up

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"kept me going"是生存动力——复仇是唯一的理由。"worth getting up"是字面意义——每天早上起床的理由。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"do what I had to do"是模糊的——他没有具体说是什么。

> **原句 5:** "Then this eternity would end. Then we would be together again, my beloved. And rest. Eternal rest."

关键词：eternity / together again / beloved / Eternal rest

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"eternity"是死后——他相信死后与家人团聚。"Eternal rest"是双关——死亡是"安息"。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"beloved"是复数——他的妻儿。

> **原句 6:** "The big body jerked in surprise and she turned toward me. I could see the panic in her eyes. Then the relief."

关键词：jerked in surprise / panic / relief

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"jerked"是生理反应——女人被吓了一跳。"panic"到"relief"的转变——她意识到他不是黑人。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"Latino"是种族——作为拉丁裔，他比黑人"不那么威胁"。

> **原句 7:** "She didn't say it, but I knew anyway. That her first thought was that I was black. I guess Latino was a bit less threatening. Just a bit. I smiled."

关键词：first thought / black / Latino / a bit less threatening

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"first thought"是洞察——他知道女人在想什么。"a bit less threatening"是讽刺——种族等级。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"smiled"是苦涩——他对种族问题感到无奈。

> **原句 8:** "She stared at my face, then at my hands. I walked on."

关键词：stared / face / hands / walked on

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"stared"是审视——女人在判断他是否危险。"walked on"是离开——他不纠缠。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"hands"是细节——手是判断"威胁"的依据。

> **原句 9:** "It took me a while to spot that big blue car, even though I knew where it usually stood and navigated there using the floodlight pylon in the centre of the parking lot."

关键词：spot / big blue car / navigated / floodlight pylon

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"floodlight pylon"是地标——他用停车场设施导航。"big blue car"是目标——他在找车。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"navigated"是军事术语——他像导航一样找路。

> **原句 10:** "It was a Chevrolet Silverado High Country crew cab. I peered in at the driver's seat. Noted that the neck support was at normal height. The seat pushed not too far forward nor too far back."

关键词：Chevrolet Silverado / peered / neck support / normal height / not too far forward nor too far back

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"neck support"是细节——他在观察座椅位置。"not too far forward nor too far back"是精确——他在测量。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"peered"是谨慎——他偷偷观察。

> **原句 11:** "I used the sleeve of my jacket to wipe away the raindrops from the windshield, took out the roll of wide white tape and tore off three strips. Taped them to the windshield on the driver's side, directly under the roof. It formed a white square approximately three by three inches."

关键词：sleeve / wipe away / raindrops / windshield / roll of wide white tape / tore off / strips / white square / approximately

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"white tape"是标记——他在做瞄准点。"three by three inches"是精确——他在测量。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"sleeve"是谨慎——不留指纹。

> **原句 12:** "I looked at my watch. Five thirty. That gave me half an hour."

关键词：looked at my watch / five thirty / half an hour

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"five thirty"是时间——目标即将出现。"half an hour"是等待——他需要耐心。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"gave me"是倒计时——时间不多了。
