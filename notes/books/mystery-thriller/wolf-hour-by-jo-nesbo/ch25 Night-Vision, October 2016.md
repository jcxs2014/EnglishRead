# 25. Night-Vision, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Hanson 接到 Kjos 的电话，得知 Southdale Mall 发生枪击案——Kay Myers 认为是 Gomez 所为；Hanson 想抢先破案，而 Oz 也出现在现场，与 Hanson 对峙。
- **情感弧线位置**：推进 + 冲突——第二次谋杀发生， Hanson 和 Oz 之间的竞争升级。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："双侦探"——Hanson 和 Oz 同时调查同一案件，但 Hanson 是"腐败警察"，Oz 是"停职侦探"；"监控录像"——Oz 通过停车库的监控摄像头找到了 Gomez 的影像。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"被停职"变为"主动调查"——他不再等待，而是直接行动；Hanson 从"霸凌者"变为"竞争者"——他想抢这个案件。
- **叙事手法**：多视角交叉——Hanson、Oz、Gomez（最后一段）三视角快速切换。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Olav Hanson took another cast with the rod. Saw nothing, could just hear from the reel that the line had run out. He was no fisherman, never would be. But he could cast a long way, and that was something. Pity he was alone here with no one else to see – or more properly hear – the line as it sizzled toward the far bank of the river."

关键词：cast / rod / reel / run out / sizzled / far bank

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"sizzled"是听觉——鱼线飞出的声音。"far bank"是距离——河的对岸。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"no fisherman, never would be"是自我认知——他不适合"等待"。

> **原句 2:** "It made him jump. The same way he'd been jumping every time the phone rang following his conversation with Die Man yesterday. But right now he was fishing, so to hell with Die Man, every man had a right to one place where he's his own boss."

关键词：jumping / Die Man / to hell with / every man had a right / own boss

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"jumping"是恐惧——他害怕 Die Man 的电话。"every man had a right"是信念——他需要"自己的空间"。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"to hell with"是反抗——他试图摆脱 Die Man 的控制。

> **原句 3:** "‘Something came in just now, a man shot in the parking lot at the Southdale Mall. There's a couple of patrol cars there and from what I'm hearing Kay Myers thinks it could be Tomás Gomez. Rifle shot from a distance.’"

关键词：came in / shot / patrol cars / Kay Myers / rifle shot / from a distance

为什么这样写：Kjos 的"came in"是警方术语——"接到报案"。"rifle shot from a distance"是描述——远程狙击。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"reeling in"是动作——他急着回去。

> **原句 4:** "Olav Hanson began reeling in as fast as he could. ‘Any detectives on the scene yet?’"

关键词：reeling in / on the scene

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"reeling in"是双关——收鱼线，也是"收回"注意力。"on the scene"是警方术语——"在现场"。

> **原句 5:** "‘See if you can delay her a little, Joe.’ … ‘I want this case.’ … Olav knew why Joe was asking; Olav wasn't exactly known for taking on more cases than he strictly had to."

关键词：delay / want this case / known for taking on / strictly had to

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"want this case"是罕见的——他通常不积极。"strictly had to"是最低限度——他只接必须接的案子。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"delay"是策略——他想抢在 Myers 之前。

> **原句 6:** "There were no indications in Gomez's apartment that he had been back. The couch was still pulled halfway out onto the floor. Bob was sitting on it while checking the cheese melting in the oven. He'd found the landlord Gregory Dupont's phone number, picked up a set of keys from him and bought a semi-cooked pizza in a box from a 7-Eleven."

关键词：indications / pulled halfway out / cheese melting / landlord / Gregory Dupont / semi-cooked pizza / 7-Eleven

为什么这样写：Oz 的"indications"是侦探术语——没有"迹象"。"semi-cooked pizza"是细节——他在吃冷冻披萨。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"Gregory Dupont"是线索——他找到了房东的电话。

> **原句 7:** "What was it about the Gomez case that had him sitting here now, risking the little that was left of his career? It wasn't the victim. Was it Gomez himself, the points of similarity? Was it because he knew how was feeling? That Gomez had actually done something he had imagined doing himself, and even felt close to doing, waging an all-out war, with no thought of the consequences for himself?"

关键词：risking / points of similarity / imagined doing / waging / all-out war / consequences

为什么这样写：Oz 的"all-out war"是比喻——Gomez 在向"全世界"开战。"consequences"是代价——他不在乎后果。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"identified with"是认同——他与 Gomez 有共鸣。

> **原句 8:** "‘We've had a sort of execution-style killing at Southdale Mall. I think there are clear similarities to the attempt on Dante's life, I want to know if you see it the same way.’"

关键词：execution-style killing / similarities / attempt on Dante's life

为什么这样写：Kay 的"execution-style"是术语——"处决式"杀人。"similarities"是关联——两起案件有相似之处。

> **原句 9:** "Bob registered the strips of white tape and the bullet holes high in the windshield as he took in the scene. Parking garage on the other side of the road. From the angles it was obvious that's where the shots came from. Somewhere high up, probably the roof."

关键词：registered / strips / bullet holes / windshield / took in the scene / angles / obvious

为什么这样写：Oz 的"registered"是观察——他"注意到"细节。"angles"是几何——他用几何学判断弹道。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"obvious"是自信——他立刻知道枪从哪里来。

> **原句 10:** "He typed in a few commands on his keyboard. ‘We got two cameras for each field,’ he said. ‘One that's on all the time and an IPCC-9610 camera that's motion-activated. It has night-vision and –’"

关键词：commands / keyboard / two cameras / motion-activated / night-vision

为什么这样写：保安的"motion-activated"是技术——"移动激活"摄像头。"night-vision"是功能——夜视。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"time is of the essence"是紧迫——他在赶时间。

> **原句 11:** "The camera followed people and cars as they came and went. As soon as Bob was satisfied a recording didn't show what he was looking for he would ask the guard to fast-forward to the next one. After a dozen of these forward jumps the guard suppressed a yawn."

关键词：satisfied / fast-forward / dozen / suppressed a yawn

为什么这样写：Oz 的"fast-forward"是动作——他在快速浏览录像。"suppressed a yawn"是无聊——保安困了。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"satisfied"是判断——他能快速判断"不是这个"。

> **原句 12:** "‘Stop!’ Bob said. ‘Switch to normal speed here.’ The guard tapped on the keyboard and Bob looked at the person coming out from the stairway door. Someone wearing a top with the hood pulled up and shades. He was carrying an oblong package swathed in bubble wrap."

关键词：switch to normal speed / stairway door / hood / shades / oblong package / swathed / bubble wrap

为什么这样写：Oz 的"switch to normal speed"是发现——他找到了。"hood"和"shades"是伪装——Gomez 戴着帽子和墨镜。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"oblong package"是枪——用气泡膜包裹的步枪。

> **原句 13:** "Despite the fact that the face on the screen was in partial shadow beneath the hood, and the eyes hidden behind the sunglasses, Bob Oz was in no doubt. This was the man in the composite. This was Tomás Gomez."

关键词：partial shadow / hidden / in no doubt / composite

为什么这样写：Oz 的"in no doubt"是确定——他知道这是 Gomez。"composite"是警方术语——"合成画像"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"this was"是确认——他终于找到了。

> **原句 14:** "‘Can you mail me that picture?’ … ‘To every damn patrol car in the city,’ Bob muttered half to himself before taking over at the keyboard and punching in the mail address to the duty officer at MPD central."

关键词：mail / every damn patrol car / muttered / punching in / duty officer

为什么这样写：Oz 的"every damn"是急切——他发给所有巡逻车。"duty officer"是值班警官。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"muttered"是自言自语——他在紧张时自言自语。

> **原句 15:** "I got off the bus at the Nicollet Mall. There were always people in this shopping street, even on the coldest winter day. I passed restaurants and bars with music coming from the open doors. I walked by two Latino men standing by a kiosk and sharing a cigarette. ‘Hola,’ I said. ‘Hola,’ they answered in unison."

关键词：Nicollet Mall / restaurants / bars / kiosk / sharing a cigarette / Hola / in unison

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"Hola"是西班牙语——他在测试自己的"伪装"。"in unison"是同时——两人同时回答。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"sharing a cigarette"是日常——他在融入环境。

> **原句 16:** "I arrived at the beautiful hundred-year-old building that had once been Dayton's department store. The name may have changed but the stock was pretty much the same. I studied the facade. Noted the security cameras above the entrance."

关键词：Dayton's department store / facade / security cameras

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"Dayton's"是真实百货——明尼苏达州历史。"facade"是建筑——他在观察。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"noted"是侦探式——他在记录细节。

> **原句 17:** "I tightened my grip on the bubble wrap – no one seemed to suspect anything anyway. I took a deep breath, like a diver, before moving on. The moment I was inside the doors I could feel it. The sensation of being somewhere else, that I was now part of Minneapolis's eight square miles of indoor universe, with skyway connections. You could literally spend your whole life in there. You could be born in one of the clinics, live in one of the apartments, eat in the restaurants, go to school there, go to work in an office, get away from things in the theatres and bars. You could die in here, and be laid to rest in the church that was in there somewhere. And as I was thinking that, it struck me: that I was already dead. I just hadn't been laid to rest yet."

关键词：tightened my grip / like a diver / sensation / literally / clinics / apartments / laid to rest / struck me / already dead

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"like a diver"是比喻——深呼吸像潜水。"laid to rest"是安葬——埋葬。"already dead"是存在主义——他已经"死了"。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"indoor universe"是讽刺——购物中心是"室内宇宙"。

> **原句 18:** "I walked into a fast-food place and took a seat at the counter, ordered a pizza which you could see being baked inside big, red, infernal ovens. I watched the cheese melting, saw the dough rise, the slices of pepperoni sweating. I was hungry, tired. So tired that for a moment I lost concentration, lost perspective, dropped my guard, and there it was again, the doubt: what the hell are you doing? I pulled myself together and, like I always did, gave a clear answer. Sat up straight in my chair. Looked into the security cameras mounted on the wall above the ovens."

关键词：fast-food / infernal ovens / cheese melting / dough rise / pepperoni sweating / lost concentration / dropped my guard / pulled myself together / gave a clear answer / mounted

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"infernal"是"地狱的"——烤箱像地狱。"dropped my guard"是失误——他失去了警惕。"pulled myself together"是恢复——他重新集中。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"looked into the security cameras"是挑衅——他在看摄像头。

> **原句 19:** "‘Your colleague was just here and I showed him the same pictures,’ the security guard at the parking garage said. ‘I see,’ said Olav Hanson as he studied the pictures on the screen in front of them. The lighting and the picture quality were poor, and it had been thirty years since the last time. But he was in no doubt about it. The scars on the face. It was Lobo. He was alive. And he was here."

关键词：colleague / same pictures / lighting / picture quality / poor / in no doubt / scars / Lobo

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"in no doubt"是确定——他知道这是 Lobo。"scars"是证据——脸上的伤疤。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"thirty years"是时间——他 30 年前见过这张脸。

> **原句 20:** "‘The duty officer at MPD just called. Oz sent them a picture of Tomás Gomez at a parking garage and asked them to run a facial recognition program on every security camera in the city.’"

关键词：duty officer / picture / facial recognition program / every security camera

为什么这样写：Kjos 的"facial recognition"是技术——面部识别程序。"every security camera"是全市——所有摄像头。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"Shit"是愤怒——Oz 抢先了。
