# 32. The Password, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 在家醒来，通过新闻了解选情；他去 Dinkytown 的 Bernie's Bar 找 Liza 借 Wi-Fi，用 Radica 20Q 玩具换取密码；他分享了自己女儿 Frankie 的故事，两人关系加深；他通过 MPD 数据库搜索 Gomez 的过去，发现一宗 1995 年的 Perez 家庭谋杀案。
- **情感弧线位置**：过渡 / 连接——Oz 与 Liza 的关系从"调情"变为"真诚分享"；他对 Gomez 的调查取得突破。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："玩具换密码"——Oz 用女儿的 Radica 20Q 换取 Wi-Fi 密码；"数据库侦探"——Oz 通过警方数据库搜索 Gomez 的过去；"洗手间顿悟"——Oz 在洗手间意识到 Gomez 没有锁门（因为他想被"发现"）。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"孤独"变为"连接"——他向 Liza 敞开心扉；从"追捕"变为"理解"——他开始理解 Gomez 的心理。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称独白——Oz 的视角，穿插对话和内心独白。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bob's eyelids flickered. It was the light that woke him. It came from a combined alarm clock and lamp he'd given Alice as a birthday present. The soft light came on at the hour the alarm was set for and then gradually grew brighter, like a sunrise. That was the idea."

关键词：eyelids flickered / combined alarm clock and lamp / birthday present / soft light / sunrise

为什么这样写：Oz 的"eyelids flickered"是醒来——他被光唤醒。"sunrise"是比喻——灯光像日出。

> **原句 2:** "He'd taken it with him after Stan appeared, when Alice told Bob he could take absolutely anything he wanted. Bob probably hoped she would be hurt by the fact that he'd taken her birthday present but instead she seemed relieved, she'd never been someone who needed a gentle wake-up."

关键词：Stan appeared / absolutely anything / relieved / gentle wake-up

为什么这样写：Oz 的"relieved"是解脱——Alice 不再需要"温柔的醒来"。"gentle wake-up"是习惯——她不喜欢温柔的唤醒。

> **原句 3:** "‘This isn't a laptop place,’ said Liza when he sat down on one of the stools and put the computer on the counter at Bernie's. ‘Sorry, but I couldn't find anywhere else that offered a combination of decent coffee and Wi-Fi,’ said Bob."

关键词：laptop place / stools / counter / combination / decent coffee / Wi-Fi

为什么这样写：Liza 的"laptop place"是笔记本电脑场所——这里不是。"combination"是组合——咖啡和 Wi-Fi。

> **原句 4:** "‘I can see I'm alone here, so this stays between us. How much do you want for the staff password?’ ‘So you think I can be bought?’ ‘Not with money, perhaps, but I think you're open to a bribe for the right price.’ ‘And what would that be?’ ‘The truth.’"

关键词：stays between us / staff password / bought / bribe / right price / truth

为什么这样写：Oz 的"truth"是真相——他想要真相作为"贿赂"。"right price"是合适的价格。

> **原句 5:** "Bob took the device from the inside pocket of his coat and put it down in front of her. ‘Radica 20Q,’ he said before she had time to ask. ‘It can read your thoughts.’"

关键词：device / inside pocket / Radica 20Q / read your thoughts

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Radica 20Q"是玩具——他女儿的玩具。"read your thoughts"是功能——它能"读心"。

> **原句 6:** "‘It was my daughter's.’ ‘You've got a daughter?’ ‘Had. Frankie. She died. She'd be happy if another child had the pleasure of using her favourite toy. She was like that.’"

关键词：Had / Frankie / died / favourite toy / like that

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Had"是过去——他曾经有女儿。"like that"是性格——她乐于分享。

> **原句 7:** "Liza's mouth opened slightly. For a moment her eyes went blank. And Bob saw how her face, how the way she was standing, the way she held her hands, how everything about her was changed. Of course, he'd seen the effect before on the few occasions when he'd told someone he'd lost his daughter, how the other person always searched for some kind of adequate response. But never like this. It was as though the words had struck a chord inside Liza Hummels, as though they opened the door to a person Bob hadn't yet met. She became, thought Bob – for want of a better word – beautiful. And her voice was thicker when, after a few seconds' silence, she said, very clearly: ‘Hillary Clinton for Prison.’"

关键词：eyes went blank / changed / effect / occasions / lost his daughter / searched / adequate response / struck a chord / opened the door / for want of a better word / beautiful / thicker / Hillary Clinton for Prison

为什么这样写：Oz 的"struck a chord"是共鸣——Liza 被触动了。"Hillary Clinton for Prison"是密码——Wi-Fi 密码。

> **原句 8:** "‘Johan?’ ‘It's what people call a high-income name. It doesn't make the child any more intelligent, but it gives them an edge when they apply for high-income jobs after college.’"

关键词：high-income name / intelligent / edge / apply / high-income jobs / college

为什么这样写：Liza 的"high-income name"是名字——高收入名字。"edge"是优势——求职优势。

> **原句 9:** "‘Murder case. Perez. 1995. Can you send me the report and –’ ‘Walker's given everybody orders not to –’ ‘Fuck Walker. All I need is the –’ ‘I'm going to hang up now, Bob.’"

关键词：Murder case / Perez / 1995 / Walker / hang up

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Fuck Walker"是粗口——他在骂上司。"hang up"是挂断——Kay 挂了电话。

> **原句 10:** "‘Can't pull the women any more?’ Liza asked. ‘It's been going on for a while now,’ said Bob. He put his elbows on the counter and rubbed his skull hard. ‘Excuse me.’ Bob made his way to the men's room."

关键词：pull the women / elbows / rubbed his skull / men's room

为什么这样写：Oz 的"rubbed his skull"是疲惫——他在揉头。"men's room"是洗手间——他去洗手间。

> **原句 11:** "It was a peculiarity of his; if he didn't feel certain he would be alone when urinating he would end up just standing there, pressing away at his bladder."

关键词：peculiarity / urinating / pressing away at his bladder

为什么这样写：Oz 的"peculiarity"是怪癖——他必须确定没人。"pressing away at his bladder"是憋尿——他在忍。

> **原句 12:** "‘What we said about holding the doors open – when you enter a stall in a restroom where there are other people, don't you automatically lock the door?’ She looked uncomprehendingly at him. ‘And definitely if you're preparing to make your escape through a ventilation shaft,’ he said. ‘Gomez hadn't locked the door of that stall. Isn't that odd? I mean, you don't want to be caught in the act now, do you?’"

关键词：holding the doors open / stall / automatically lock / uncomprehendingly / escape / ventilation shaft / caught in the act / odd

为什么这样写：Oz 的"odd"是奇怪——Gomez 没锁门。"caught in the act"是当场被抓。

> **原句 13:** "‘Maybe you want to be caught in the act. If you're committing a criminal act.’ Liza leaned across the counter and put her chin in her hands. ‘I got into the habit of stealing small change from my father's wallet. I felt so ashamed I started stealing larger and larger amounts so that he'd notice.’"

关键词：want to be caught / committing a criminal act / habit / stealing / ashamed / notice

为什么这样写：Liza 的"ashamed"是羞耻——她感到羞耻。"notice"是注意——她想被注意到。

> **原句 14:** "‘It's grounds for hope to know that at least we are potentially capable of stopping. Can you get me a whisky?’ ‘No.’ ‘No?’ ‘No, you can have more coffee. What is it you're hoping to stop doing?’"

关键词：grounds for hope / potentially capable / stopping / whisky / more coffee / stop doing

为什么这样写：Oz 的"grounds for hope"是希望——我们有希望停止。"potentially capable"是有能力。

> **原句 15:** "‘Alice. I give her hell. The divorce papers, the division of property, this new guy of hers – all of it. Even though I know it hurts me most, that my self-contempt just grows and grows when I make myself a worse man than I already am. Sometimes I wonder if what I'm doing is asking for pity. It's as though I want her to see that the man I once was is going to pieces in front of her eyes. I'm a prick, and my own conscience torments me over it, but I just can't seem to stop it the way you did. Just the opposite, in fact – I've turned into a fucking stalker.’"

关键词：give her hell / divorce papers / division of property / self-contempt / worse man / asking for pity / going to pieces / prick / conscience torments / stalker

为什么这样写：Oz 的"give her hell"是折磨——他在折磨 Alice。"self-contempt"是自我厌恶——他越来越厌恶自己。"going to pieces"是崩溃——他在她面前崩溃。"stalker"是跟踪者——他变成了跟踪者。

> **原句 16:** "‘Actually I don't think it's her I'm stalking, it's more the places where I was once happy. Where I lived with her and Frankie. Where I picked her up from work. I'm stalking the memories. You know, like those people who have their pets stuffed, to recreate something that's gone from their lives.’"

关键词：stalking the memories / once happy / picked her up / pets stuffed / recreate / gone from their lives

为什么这样写：Oz 的"stalking the memories"是追踪记忆——他在追踪回忆。"pets stuffed"是宠物标本——像标本一样"保存"回忆。

> **原句 17:** "‘Do people stuff their pet animals?’ ‘Oh yes. Even a killer like Tomás Gomez wants his cat back. Incredible, isn't it?’"

关键词：stuff their pet animals / killer / cat / incredible

为什么这样写：Oz 的"incredible"是不可思议——连杀手都想保存宠物。"cat"是猫——Gomez 的猫。

> **原句 18:** "Bob watched her. The friendly, professional manner; the quick, assured movements. An efficiency he was sure made her feel good, the pleasure of doing a job well. The pleasure. I'm stalking memories. Suddenly it lit up for him, as clear as the answer on the Radica 20Q display."

关键词：professional manner / assured movements / efficiency / pleasure / lit up / Radica 20Q display

为什么这样写：Oz 的"lit up"是亮起——答案像 Radica 20Q 的显示一样清晰。"as clear as"是清晰——像……一样清晰。

> **原句 19:** "‘Where are you going?’ asked Liza. Bob was on his feet and buttoning his coat. ‘I think I know how I can find him.’"

关键词：on his feet / buttoning his coat / find him

为什么这样写：Oz 的"on his feet"是站起来——他起身。"find him"是找到他——他想到了办法。
