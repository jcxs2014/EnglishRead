# 55. Cashmere, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Kay 参加 puppet show 并与 Alex 约会；Oz 与 Alice 通话，得知她生了一个女孩；Oz 与 Liza 在车里聊天，两人开始大笑。
- **情感弧线位置：回落 / 温暖——事件结束后，角色们继续生活；Oz 与 Liza 的关系有了新的开始。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："羊绒外套"——Oz 解释羊绒外套的温暖；"狼皮羊皮书"——Liza 说 Oz 是"羊皮狼皮书"。
- **叙事手法**：多视角交叉——Kay、Oz 两视角。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Sunday was another sunny day. Kay Myers followed the handwritten directions showing her the route through Minnehaha Park. They invited all adults and children along to something entitled 'Emma the Hare and Freddy the Fox'."

关键词：sunny day / Kay Myers / handwritten directions / Minnehaha Park / adults / children / Emma the Hare and Freddy the Fox

为什么这样写：Kay 的"handwritten directions"是手写指示——她按照指示走。

> **原句 2:** "She came directly from 1025 Bar, the place where the cops drank, where those who wanted to had gathered to pay tribute to the memory of Olav Hanson."

关键词：1025 Bar / cops / drank / pay tribute / Olav Hanson

为什么这样写：Kay 的"pay tribute"是致敬——同事们向 Hanson 致敬。

> **原句 3:** "‘I'm telling you this as an example of the sort of trade-offs you're going to have to make when you take over,’ Walker was saying. ‘Have you got the stomach for it, Myers?’"

关键词：trade-offs / take over / stomach for it

为什么这样写：Walker 的"trade-offs"是权衡——他告诉 Kay 要做的权衡。

> **原句 4:** "She realised she had thought of Minneapolis as our city. Was that maybe for the first time? To protect with courage, to serve with compassion. MPD's motto."

关键词：our city / first time / protect with courage / serve with compassion / MPD's motto

为什么这样写：Kay 的"our city"是我们的城市——她第一次这样想。

> **原句 5:** "‘Alex. Maybe you'd like a coffee afterward?’ She looked at him. ‘Maybe,’ she said."

关键词：Alex / coffee / Maybe

为什么这样写：Kay 的"Maybe"是也许——她在考虑。

> **原句 6:** "Come Monday the sky remained high and cloudless and the air clear. It stayed that way until dusk, when a couple of lonely clouds appeared. They seemed so high up they must have come from outer space, with the sun tinging them blue and emerald green. From where Bob was standing, phone to his ear and leaning against the Volvo as he looked over the downtown skyline on the far side of the river, the massed buildings looked like a ragged iceberg against a background of orange fire."

关键词：Monday / sky / high / cloudless / air clear / dusk / lonely clouds / outer space / tinging / blue / emerald green / Bob / phone / Volvo / downtown skyline / river / massed buildings / ragged iceberg / orange fire

为什么这样写：Oz 的"ragged iceberg"是破碎的冰山——建筑群像冰山。

> **原句 7:** "‘Hello, Bob.’ ‘Hi, Alice. Thanks for the messages over the weekend. Sorry about the very brief replies, it's been a busy weekend tying up loose ends in the Lunde case. I've done nothing but sleep and work.’"

关键词：messages / weekend / brief replies / tying up loose ends / Lunde case / sleep / work

为什么这样写：Oz 的"tying up loose ends"是收尾——他在收尾。

> **原句 8:** "‘We've been to the hospital, got back home just now.’ ‘And?’ ‘It's a girl. They say she looks healthy and well.’ ‘That is so good to hear,’ said Bob. ‘So good. So …’ He swallowed. ‘You've made me very happy, Alice.’"

关键词：hospital / home / girl / healthy / happy

为什么这样写：Oz 的"swallowed"是吞咽——他哽咽了。

> **原句 9:** "He slipped the phone back into his coat pocket. Stood waiting. He liked waiting. Liked to see the darkness rise up from the ground, up from the Mississippi, climb up across the facades around him and over the glass walls."

关键词：slipped / coat pocket / waiting / darkness / rise up / Mississippi / climb / facades / glass walls

为什么这样写：Oz 的"waiting"是等待——他喜欢等待。

> **原句 10:** "‘Again?’ she asked, acting exasperated. ‘This is … what? The third day in a row? Is this your famous siege technique in operation?’ ‘Don't flatter yourself. I just happened to be in the neighbourhood,’ he said. ‘And I needed someone to split the cost of gas with.’"

关键词：acting exasperated / third day in a row / famous siege technique / operation / flatter yourself / neighbourhood / split the cost of gas

为什么这样写：Liza 的"siege technique"是围攻技巧——她在讽刺 Oz。

> **原句 11:** "‘Speaking of sheep's clothing, did I ever tell you this coat is eight times warmer than sheep's wool? That it's made of goat's hair that has been combed from the bellies of goats living five miles above sea level? That each goat yields only three and a half ounces of hair per year, so that to make a coat like this takes –' ‘A lot of time and a lot of hard work?’ She gave him that exasperated look again. Bob thought about her words. Nodded. ‘Exactly. A lot of time and a lot of hard work. If you want a cashmere coat then you have to will yourself to get a cashmere coat.’"

关键词：sheep's clothing / coat / eight times warmer / sheep's wool / goat's hair / bellies / five miles above sea level / three and a half ounces / time / hard work / will yourself

为什么这样写：Oz 的"will yourself"是下定决心——下定决心得到羊绒外套。
