# 20. The Eyes, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 在标本店等待 Gomez 出现，与 Lunde 深入交谈——关于标本术、孤独、以及 Gomez 的过去；Lunde 透露 Gomez 曾说"孤独正在慢慢逼疯他"。
- **情感弧线位置**：过渡 / 沉思——Oz 在标本店找到平静，同时拼凑出 Gomez 的心理画像。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："等待的猎人"——Oz 在标本店"守株待兔"；"哲学对话"——Lunde 与 Oz 讨论孤独、艺术、死亡。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"愤怒的侦探"变为"思考的观察者"——他在标本店睡着了，梦见了女儿。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Oz 与 Lunde 的对话揭示了 Gomez 的内心世界。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bob smiled and patted a white-tailed deer. ‘I was wondering if I could hang around here for a while this afternoon.’"

关键词：patted / white-tailed deer / hang around

为什么这样写：Oz 的"patted"是温柔的——他对标本的态度是友好的。"hang around"是口语——他在请求留下。

> **原句 2:** "‘We call them mannequins,’ said Lunde as he poured coffee from a stained pot. ‘We order them and they come ready-cut like that.’ ‘But that's cheating.’"

关键词：mannequins / ready-cut / cheating

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"ready-cut"是现代的——标本术已经"工业化"了。"cheating"是 Oz 的幽默。

> **原句 3:** "‘The hart has oblong pupils that have to be positioned horizontally,’ he explained. ‘Why?’ ‘So that they can take in the horizon in one look. They're prey.’"

关键词：oblong pupils / positioned horizontally / horizon / prey

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"oblong pupils"是技术细节——他在解释标本术。"prey"与"predators"是自然界的二元对立。

> **原句 4:** "‘A hunter who wants to capture his moment of triumph over an animal that thought it was in control,’ said Bob. ‘Very poetic. And in this particular case, very accurate.’"

关键词：capture / moment of triumph / in control / poetic / accurate

为什么这样写：Oz 的"triumph"是讽刺——猎人"战胜"动物。"poetic"是 Lunde 的赞美。

> **原句 5:** "‘I'm a barber. The customer decides the style. But, within limits, I have a certain degree of freedom to produce something that exceeds the customer's expectations.’"

关键词：barber / customer decides / within limits / exceeds / expectations

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"barber"是比喻——标本师是"理发师"。"exceeds the customer's expectations"是目标。

> **原句 6:** "‘Tomás. He used to sit there, like you. Not saying much, now and then asking a question about some technical aspect of what I was doing.’"

关键词：used to sit there / not saying much / technical aspect

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"used to sit there"是回忆——Gomez 曾经坐在同一个位置。

> **原句 7:** "‘I think he infiltrated X-11 in order to avenge the death of his family.’ ‘Really?’ ‘He spun them some tale about how he'd worked for a drug cartel south of the border.’"

关键词：infiltrated / avenge / spun them some tale / drug cartel

为什么这样写：Oz 的"infiltrated"是军事术语——Gomez 是"间谍"。"avenge"是关键词——Gomez 的动机是复仇。

> **原句 8:** "‘One in four drug dealers die within four years. Think about it. In this country a prisoner under sentence of death has less chance of being executed than a crack dealer has of being shot dead in the street.’"

关键词：one in four / prisoner / sentence of death / executed / crack dealer / shot dead

为什么这样写：Oz 的"one in four"是统计数据——毒品交易的死亡率高。"executed"是对比——死刑犯比毒贩更安全。

> **原句 9:** "‘Maybe he was nervous and his hand shook. A lot of the hunters I get in here talk about what they call "buck fever".’"

关键词：nervous / hand shook / buck fever

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"buck fever"是狩猎术语——"鹿热"（看到鹿时的紧张）。

> **原句 10:** "‘There's a colony of carnivorous leather beetles inside that skull and they'll eat it clean before I begin work.’"

关键词：colony / carnivorous / leather beetles / skull / eat it clean

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"carnivorous leather beetles"是真实的——标本师用甲虫清理头骨。

> **原句 11:** "‘In Victorian England it was as common to have stuffed animals in the averagely affluent household as it was to have paintings,’ said Lunde, opening one of the books."

关键词：Victorian England / stuffed animals / averagely affluent / paintings

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"Victorian England"是历史——标本术在维多利亚时代流行。

> **原句 12:** "‘I agree, it is creepy. But not just creepy. These are artistic expressions. They're stories.’ ‘But … doesn't it do something to you, spending so much time in the company of dead animals?’ … ‘It's what you might call an existential challenge, and it probably does have some effect on you. All those hours, sitting alone, trying to put a mask on death.’"

关键词：creepy / artistic expressions / existential challenge / put a mask on death

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"existential challenge"是哲学——标本术是"存在主义的挑战"。

> **原句 13:** "‘When you and Gomez were talking about loneliness,' said Bob, ‘what did you discuss in particular?’ ‘Well, all sorts of things,’ … ‘None of our most basic physical needs require the presence of several or even one other human being.’"

关键词：loneliness / basic physical needs / presence / human being

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"loneliness"是主题——全书的核心。"basic physical needs"是生物学。

> **原句 14:** "‘But if we don't need others, then why is loneliness so intolerable?’ … ‘Biology. If we all thought it was fine to be alone, we wouldn't want to reproduce ourselves.’"

关键词：intolerable / Biology / reproduce

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Biology"是科学解释——孤独是进化机制。

> **原句 15:** "‘Loneliness is a fairly novel experience for you, Bob, isn't it?’ Bob didn't reply."

关键词：fairly novel experience

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"fairly novel"是洞察——Oz 的孤独是"新的"。

> **原句 16:** "‘It's in the eyes. Always the eyes. I saw it the moment you entered the store. Your eyes expressed the same thing as Tomás. Loss. Anger. Desperation. Loneliness.’"

关键词：eyes / expressed / Loss / Anger / Desperation / Loneliness

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"eyes"是窗口——眼睛表达情感。Oz 和 Gomez 是同一类人。

> **原句 17:** "‘Tomás? He said so himself.’ ‘What did he say about being lonely?’ ‘Lots. That it was slowly driving him mad.’"

关键词：slowly driving him mad

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"driving him mad"是字面意思——孤独正在逼疯 Gomez。

> **原句 18:** "‘Normal people don't kill other people. Although, the same could be said of those that killed his family. I don't think your guy is any better or any worse than anyone else, he's just been unlucky. His world was shattered.’"

关键词：Normal people / any better or any worse / unlucky / world was shattered

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"world was shattered"是比喻——Gomez 的世界崩塌了。

> **原句 19:** "Bob fell asleep with his head against the wall. He dreamed. It was the same dream. He was holding a pistol and firing at a tiny head with a candyfloss halo of fair hair."

关键词：fell asleep / dreamed / pistol / firing / tiny head / candyfloss halo

为什么这样写：Oz 的"same dream"是反复——他经常做这个梦。"candyfloss halo"是比喻——女儿的头发。

> **原句 20:** "‘I'm guessing you're going to eat alone, and you don't care a damn where or what.’ ‘Bullseye.’"

关键词：eat alone / don't care a damn / Bullseye

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"eat alone"是孤独。"Bullseye"是 Oz 的确认——Lunde 说对了。
