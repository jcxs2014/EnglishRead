# 42. The House of Horrors, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Kay 在 Cedar Creek 的老妇人 Mrs Holte 处了解到 Goya 曾与一名白人男子进入森林中的"恐怖之屋"；Patterson 市长在体育场准备演讲；Rooble 审问 Dante，得知 Goya 可能就是 Lobo；Kay 找到了" rogue 标本俱乐部"，发现了 Emily Lunde 的工作室，以及一个坐在椅子上的人。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 发现——Kay 找到了 Goya 的藏身处（恐怖之屋），发现了被"标本化"的人；Patterson 市长在体育场准备演讲，危险逼近。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："恐怖之屋"——Kay 找到了 Goya 的藏身处，一个充满" rogue 标本"的工作室；"杀手的身份"——Dante 透露 Goya 可能就是 Lobo，以及手上的五角星纹身。
- **人物弧线**：Kay 从"侦探"变为"发现者"——她找到了 Goya 的藏身处，但也发现了他的"艺术品"（被标本化的人）。
- **叙事手法**：多视角交叉——Kay、Patterson、Rooble 三视角快速切换，节奏紧张。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "‘I saw this Gomez come walking along the road there,’ the old woman said, pointing. She and Kay Myers were standing on the upper floor of a fine old timber house that lay on a rise above the otherwise flat landscape of Cedar Creek."

关键词：old woman / pointing / Kay Myers / upper floor / fine old timber house / rise / flat landscape / Cedar Creek

为什么这样写：Mrs Holte 的"old woman"是老太太——她在指路。"rise"是高地——房子在高地上。

> **原句 2:** "Kay peered in the direction of the narrow, twisting road almost a hundred yards away from the house. ‘How can you be sure it was Tomás Gomez, Mrs Holte?’"

关键词：peered / narrow / twisting road / hundred yards / sure

为什么这样写：Kay 的"peered"是凝视——她在凝视道路。"twisting"是弯曲的——道路弯曲。

> **原句 3:** "‘Ah, but the older you get, the better your sight gets, I can assure you.’"

关键词：older / better / sight / assure

为什么这样写：Mrs Holte 的"sight"是视力——她越老视力越好。

> **原句 4:** "Kay shivered at the thought that she herself and her car had probably been in that viewfinder as she drove up toward the house."

关键词：shivered / viewfinder / drove up

为什么这样写：Kay 的"viewfinder"是取景器——她可能在瞄准镜里被看到。

> **原句 5:** "The time was twelve thirty when Kevin Patterson stepped out of the SUV in front of the US Bank Stadium. The square was almost deserted, but loud music and cheering could be heard from inside the stadium. Patterson assumed someone was doing a display of trick-shooting, something involving a gun."

关键词：twelve thirty / stepped out / SUV / US Bank Stadium / deserted / music / cheering / trick-shooting / gun

为什么这样写：Patterson 的"trick-shooting"是特技射击——有人在表演射击。

> **原句 6:** "‘Well, Dante,’ said Rooble, ‘do you want a deal or not?’ Marco Dante looked over at his lawyer, Al Gill. Rooble had heard about Gill. He was the type who would sell his own grandmother if the hourly rate made it worth his while."

关键词：Dante / deal / lawyer / Al Gill / sell his own grandmother / hourly rate

为什么这样写：Rooble 的"sell his own grandmother"是卖掉自己的祖母——Gill 会为了钱做任何事。

> **原句 7:** "‘Think of it as a foretaste of the kind of information my client will be able to provide you with. Do you want the rest, or don't you?’"

关键词：foretaste / information / client / provide

为什么这样写：Gill 的"foretaste"是预尝——让客户先尝尝甜头。

> **原句 8:** "‘I remember people talking about a guy called Lobo when I was in Homicide,’ said Rooble. ‘It was before my time, but I understood that he was either dead or had gone back south of the border again.’"

关键词：Lobo / Homicide / before my time / dead / south of the border

为什么这样写：Rooble 的"south of the border"是边境以南——墨西哥。

> **原句 9:** "Kay was walking along the rough track. The trees had taken on the shading of autumn, but still clung on to their leaves. She stopped at a decaying sign which related that the forest around her was a so-called white cedar forest, and that some of the trees were over 250 years old."

关键词：walking / rough track / autumn / shading / clung on / decaying sign / white cedar forest / 250 years old

为什么这样写：Kay 的"rough track" is 小路——她在走小路。"clung on"是 clinging——树叶 clinging。

> **原句 10:** "‘Police! Open the door!’ The total silence that followed did not give Kay the feeling she was alone. Instead she felt as though a thousand ears were listening. She held her breath. No sounds from inside. She studied the lock on the door. It was shiny, new-looking. Kay hesitated. She didn't have a search warrant and the lock looked pretty solid. And there was something about the place that gave her the feeling that anyone who went in there alone would regret it. Best to pull back and return later with backup and a warrant. So then why was she still standing there, staring at the door?"

关键词：Police / silence / thousand ears / listening / breath / lock / shiny / new-looking / search warrant / solid / regret / pull back / backup / warrant / staring

为什么这样写：Kay 的"thousand ears"是一千只耳朵——她觉得有很多人在听。"search warrant" is 搜查令——她没有搜查令。

> **原句 11:** "Kay Myers turned and walked quickly back the way she had come. This time she timed the jump from one broken half of the plank to the other just right. She braced herself and jerked the mailbox and the rusty iron pole up out of the ground, took off the mailbox then headed back toward the house carrying the bar on her shoulder."

关键词：turned / timed / jump / plank / braced / jerked / mailbox / rusty iron pole / bar on her shoulder

为什么这样写：Kay 的"jerked" is 猛拉——她拉出了邮筒。

> **原句 12:** "Kay looked around. There were small, closed studios lining the walls. Carpentry workshops, she thought, because through doors that were ajar she could see lathes and tools. In one she saw a kind of mannequin in the shape of a hare made out of wire. She counted eight of these booths. Each had a nameplate. Only one was locked, secured by a large padlock. Kay read the nameplate: Emily Lunde, RT Club. The name meant nothing to her. Peering between two planks into the interior she could see the walls of the booth were lined on the inside with some kind of insulation. She located a light switch by the door and the neon tubes in the ceiling blinked a couple of times before coming on and lighting up the whole room. She picked up the metal spike and wedged it into the crack in Emily Lunde's booth door. Pushed hard on it. Instead of the padlock snapping off the soft wood of the plank bent outward. Soon it was so far out she was able to see inside. She saw light reflected in a pair of yellow eyes. She saw the man in the chair. The spike fell from her hands and clattered to the floor."

关键词：closed studios / lining / Carpentry workshops / ajar / lathes / tools / mannequin / hare / wire / booths / nameplate / locked / padlock / Emily Lunde / RT Club / peering / planks / interior / insulation / light switch / neon tubes / blinked / metal spike / crack / snapping off / bent outward / yellow eyes / chair / clattered

为什么这样写：Kay 的"yellow eyes"是黄色的眼睛——她看到了黄色的眼睛。"RT Club"是 Rogue Taxidermy Club—— rogue 标本俱乐部。
