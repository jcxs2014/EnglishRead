# 54. Hero, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 离开现场，被记者和年轻记者拦下；年轻记者说 Oz 现在是"英雄"，邀请他发表意见；Oz 拒绝了，但留下了名片。
- **情感弧线位置**：回落 / 余波——事件结束后，Oz 面对媒体的关注。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："拒绝英雄"——Oz 不想当英雄；"年轻记者"——年轻的乐观主义者让 Oz 想起了自己。
- **叙事手法**：Oz 的第一人称独白——读者直接感受他的疲惫和拒绝。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bob crossed the street from Town Taxidermy and walked toward the SWAT car where Walker stood. His face and posture gave nothing away, but Bob took the warmth in his voice as recognition. ‘Good work, Oz.’"

关键词：crossed / Town Taxidermy / SWAT car / Walker / face / posture / gave nothing away / warmth / recognition / Good work

为什么这样写：Walker 的"gave nothing away"是面无表情——Oz 的表情没有泄露任何信息。

> **原句 2:** "‘Bob Oz!’ He looked up and recognised the face of the TV reporter from the sports bar. The same guy holding the camera on his shoulder behind her. A red lamp blinked above the lens and Bob assumed it meant they were on air and live. They backed away in front of him and slowed down, but he didn't stop."

关键词：recognised / TV reporter / sports bar / camera / shoulder / red lamp / blinked / lens / on air / live

为什么这样写：Reporter 的"on air"是直播——他们在直播。

> **原句 3:** "‘Can you describe how you felt in the middle of all this drama?’ The reporter put the question with exaggerated body language and a bright red, ingratiating smile as she pushed the microphone into his face. ‘Yes, I can,’ said Bob, and her smile grew even wider. ‘But not to you.’ He looked straight into the camera. ‘Viewers, change the channel to WCCO and you'll hear my whole story. You'll also get better news there. You'll even get better weather.’"

关键词：drama / exaggerated / body language / ingratiating / microphone / face / Viewers / change the channel / WCCO / story / news / weather

为什么这样写：Oz 的"ingratiating"是讨好的——记者讨好的微笑。"WCCO"是另一家电视台。

> **原句 4:** "A young man with a shoulder bag appeared beside him. ‘From the Star Tribune. That was some answer you just gave!’ He laughed and sounded as though he meant it. ‘But are you sure you wouldn't rather talk to a proper newspaper than WCCO?’"

关键词：young man / shoulder bag / Star Tribune / answer / proper newspaper

为什么这样写：Reporter 的"Star Tribune"是星论坛报——明尼阿波利斯的大报。

> **原句 5:** "‘If the rumour that he is actually Tomás Gomez is correct then it looks like all of this was a political attack on the NRA and the gun laws. From today on you're a hero, whether you like it or not, and right now people in this state will be interested to hear your opinions. We've got a presidential election coming up and research shows that the majority of people make up their mind which way to vote the last two days before the election. I don't know where you stand politically on gun control or anything else, and it really doesn't matter to me. But just think about it, Mr Oz – right at this moment in time there's a small window open for you when you do actually have some kind of power.’"

关键词：rumour / Tomás Gomez / political attack / NRA / gun laws / hero / presidential election / research / majority / make up their mind / vote / last two days / stand / gun control / window / power

为什么这样写：Reporter 的"window"是窗口——Oz 有"权力窗口"。

> **原句 6:** "Bob unlocked the Volvo. ‘You think I can change anything?’ ‘Contribute to change, perhaps.’ Bob looked across the roof of the car at the boy standing on the other side. His cheeks were flushed. He looked as if he cared about things, looked like a decent kid. ‘You're an optimist,’ said Bob. ‘What's your name?’ ‘Bob.’ ‘Another one?’ He laughed. ‘How old are you?’ ‘Twenty-two.’ ‘OK. I envy you, Bob.’ ‘Envy me being twenty-two?’ ‘That as well.’ The older Bob got into his car and started the engine. As he drove off he looked in the mirror and saw the boy following with his eyes. A naive optimist, twenty-two years old. Had he once been like that himself? Bob hoped so. And he hoped that it was the Bob in the mirror who would use the little bit of power he had, not himself. The city – and the world – needed naive optimists more than it needed resigned realists."

关键词：unlocked / change / Contribute / flushed / cared / decent / optimist / name / Bob / Another one / twenty-two / envy / naive / resigned realists

为什么这样写：Oz 的"naive optimist"是幼稚的乐观主义者——他羡慕年轻人。"resigned realists"是认命的现实主义者——世界需要乐观主义者。
