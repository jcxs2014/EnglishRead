# 28. Skyways III, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：SWOT 小队冲进洗手间，发现 Gomez 已通过通风管道逃脱；Oz 在体育酒吧通过电视直播看到自己的"醉态"被播出，随后 O'Rourke 通过通风管道追捕，发现 Gomez 已跳到地面。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 逃脱——Gomez 再次逃脱，Oz 在电视上"出丑"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："通风管道"——Gomez 像"老鼠"一样从通风管道逃脱；"直播失误"——Oz 在体育酒吧的醉态被电视直播，损害了他的名誉。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"侦探"变为"醉汉"——他在电视上出丑，但也证明了他的"直觉"（通风管道）是正确的。
- **叙事手法**：多视角——Oz、Myers、Gomez 三视角快速切换，节奏从紧张到荒诞（体育酒吧场景）。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bob had taken up a position some distance from the SWAT team that was waiting in readiness outside the door to the restrooms. Men who emerged at irregular intervals through the swing door jumped at the sight of those black-clad men with automatic weapons pointing in their direction."

关键词：taken up a position / waiting in readiness / emerged / irregular intervals / swing door / jumped / black-clad / automatic weapons / pointing

为什么这样写：Oz 的"taken up a position"是军事术语——他像士兵一样"占据位置"。"black-clad"是穿着——SWAT 穿黑色。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"jumped"是恐惧——从洗手间出来的人被吓到。

> **原句 2:** "One of the SWAT team pushed a thin wire through the door. Bob knew there was a micro camera on the end of it. Kay approached him. ‘What's up?’ she asked. ‘You're shaking your head.’"

关键词：pushed a thin wire / micro camera / on the end of it / shaking your head

为什么这样写：SWOT 的"thin wire"是工具——他们用带摄像头的线探测。"shaking your头"是直觉——Oz 觉得"不对"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"what's up"是发现——Kay 注意到他的动作。

> **原句 3:** "‘I don't know,’ said Bob. He saw Hanson say something to O'Rourke, who turned and looked in Bob's direction. ‘It just … it feels wrong. As though …’ ‘As though what?’ asked Kay. She was standing next to him. Her arms were folded, same as his. ‘As though he's playing cat and mouse with us. And he's the cat.’"

关键词：feels wrong / playing cat and mouse / cat

为什么这样写：Oz 的"cat and mouse"是比喻——猫鼠游戏。"he's the cat"是反转——Gomez 是"猫"（掌控者）。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"folded arms"是肢体语言——他和 Kay 站在一起。

> **原句 4:** "He ran after a man in a grey Minnesota Twins sweater who had just emerged from the toilet and was being waved on by the SWAT men. Bob caught up with him outside the bag store. ‘Excuse me, sir. MPD. Did you see anything in there?’"

关键词：ran after / Minnesota Twins / emerged / waved on / caught up with / bag store

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Minnesota Twins"是细节——他注意到"明尼苏达双城队"毛衣。"caught up with"是追上——他跑过去。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"bag store"是地点——他在包店旁追上了那个人。

> **原句 5:** "The man hesitated. ‘He could have been in one of the stalls, I guess.’ ‘Thank you, sir.’ Bob ran back. O'Rourke and one of the SWAT team were studying a phone screen that was relaying a feed from the micro camera."

关键词：hesitated / stalls / relaying a feed / micro camera

为什么这样写：Oz 的"hesitated"是犹豫——男人在思考。"relaying a feed"是传输——摄像头实时传输画面。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"ran back"是急切——他急着回去报告。

> **原句 6:** "Bob turned, realising that O'Rourke had been talking to him. ‘I'm sorry?’ ‘You're under suspension, Oz, only serving police officers are allowed on the scene. Get out of here. Now.’"

关键词：under suspension / serving police officers / allowed on the scene / Get out of here

为什么这样写：O'Rourke 的"serving"是"在职"——只有在职警察能留在现场。"Get out of here"是命令——他在驱逐 Oz。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"realising"是后知后觉——他之前没意识到。

> **原句 7:** "Bob looked past them, saw Hanson standing a few yards behind with a mocking grin on his lips. Felt that rushing start up. Saw Kay spread her arms in exasperation. Told himself he mustn't lose control. Not now."

关键词：looked past / mocking grin / rushing / spread her arms / exasperation / lose control

为什么这样写：Oz 的"mocking grin"是嘲讽——Hanson 在笑他。"rushing"是暴力前兆——他又要失控了。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"told himself"是自制——他在压制愤怒。

> **原句 8:** "Bob Oz drove. He didn't know where he was going, only that it wasn't home to Phillips. And not to Alice in Cooper. He was tired of the music and turned it off. The radio took over."

关键词：drove / wasn't home to Phillips / Alice in Cooper / tired of the music / radio took over

为什么这样写：Oz 的"wasn't home"是无家可归——他不想回任何地方。"tired of the music"是疲惫——他厌倦了一切。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"radio took over"是意外——他打开了广播。

> **原句 9:** "‘We've got orders to arrest you if you try to come back,’ one of the officers said. ‘Got that?’ ‘Got it,’ said Bob, who was trying to keep track of where the reporter had gone."

> **原句 12:** "‘Switch to KSTP,’ he said. The bartender laughed. ‘Fat chance. Can't you see the Timberwolves are playing?’ ‘Fat chance? Can't you see this card? It means you do what I damn well tell you to do.’"
