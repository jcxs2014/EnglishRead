# 17. Amigo, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 在 Phillips 拦截一名毒贩，用"经济行为心理学"而非暴力审讯——他得知 Tomás Gomez 的帮派代号是"Lobo"（狼），并发现 Gomez 曾是黑帮杀手。
- **情感弧线位置**：推进——Oz 的调查取得突破，他得知了 Gomez 的过去。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："反审讯"——Oz 没有使用暴力，而是用"逻辑"和"金钱"让毒贩开口。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 的"非传统侦探"风格——他用经济学和心理学而非拳头。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Oz 与毒贩的对话揭示了 Gomez 的黑帮历史。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bob had parked by the playground at Bloomington. He sat eating a hamburger in the Volvo and watched the deal going down outside a nearby house. The same three as before. The Latino in the porkpie hat was clearly running the operation."

关键词：playground / Bloomington / hamburger / deal going down / running the operation

为什么这样写：Oz 的"hamburger"是日常的——他在监视时吃汉堡。"running the operation"是警方术语——他在分析犯罪结构。

读者视角提示：Oz 的监视是耐心的——他吃了汉堡，等待最佳时机。

> **原句 2:** "Bob tossed a bundle of something with an elastic band around it onto the dashboard. The man in the passenger seat stared at the long-dead general and president whose portrait adorned the fifty-dollar banknote."

关键词：tossed / bundle / elastic band / dashboard / portrait / adorned / fifty-dollar banknote

为什么这样写：Oz 的"bundle"是贿赂——他用钱买信息。"long-dead general and president"是 $50 钞票上的林肯。"adorned"（装饰）暗示钞票的"庄严"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"toss"是随意的——他对金钱的态度是工具性的。

> **原句 3:** "‘There's two thousand dollars there,’ said Bob. ‘Go ahead and count it.’"

关键词：two thousand dollars / go ahead / count it

为什么这样写：Oz 的"go ahead"是邀请——他让毒贩自己数钱。"two thousand"是精确的数字——他提前准备好了。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"count it"是信任测试——他想知道毒贩是否诚实。

> **原句 4:** "‘I'm not threatening you with prison, I'm not threatening to beat you, I'm not threatening you with what will happen to your kid brother who's doing time in MCF.’"

关键词：threatening / prison / beat you / kid brother / doing time / MCF

为什么这样写：Oz 的"kid brother"是情报——他知道毒贩的家庭。"doing time"是俚语——在监狱服刑。"MCF"是明尼阿波利斯矫正设施。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"not threatening"是反向威胁——他什么都在威胁。

> **原句 5:** "‘He came over from south of the border some years back. Called himself Lobo.’ ‘Wolf?’ ‘As in lone wolf. Kept himself to himself, a real loner. But he might just as well have called himself Loco.’"

关键词：south of the border / Lobo / Wolf / lone wolf / loner / Loco

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Lobo"是代号——西班牙语"狼"。"lone wolf"是孤独的——与 Oz 的处境呼应。"Loco"是"疯狂"——暗示 Gomez 的危险。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"Lone wolf"让读者想到他自己——他也是"独狼"。

> **原句 6:** "‘He uncovered not just thieving but snitching too. We had to terminate a lot of guys we'd been trusting blind.’"

关键词：uncovered / thieving / snitching / terminate / trusting blind

为什么这样写：Oz 的"terminate"是黑帮术语——杀人。"trusting blind"是轻信——黑帮内部的信任危机。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"snitching"是黑帮最大的罪——Gomez 是"反间谍"。

> **原句 7:** "‘Your disappointment has its limits. Behavioural research shows that our reaction to not getting the dollar we've just been promised is less negative than losing the dollar we already have in our pocket. I haven't stolen from you, so you have no economic or moral incentive to kill me.’"

关键词：disappointment / limits / Behavioural research / less negative / incentive

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Behavioural research"是学术——他用行为经济学解释人性。"incentive"（动机）是经济学术语。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"less negative"是精确的——他了解人类的心理偏差。

> **原句 8:** "‘At least they pay minimum wage, unlike X—’"

关键词：minimum wage / unlike X—

为什么这样写：Oz 的"minimum wage"是讽刺——帮派给的比最低工资还少。"X—"是 X-11 的省略。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"unlike X—"是幽默——他差点说出帮派名字。

> **原句 9:** "As Bob drove off he saw the man diminishing in his rear-view mirror. He stood there, arms dangling by his side, and seemed to be shouting something after the Volvo."

关键词：diminishing / rear-view mirror / arms dangling / shouting

为什么这样写：Oz 的"rear-view mirror"是观察——他在看毒贩的反应。"arms dangling"暗示无助——毒贩不知道该怎么办。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"diminishing"是物理距离——也是心理距离。

> **原句 10:** "Bob nodded slowly as he looked out of the windshield. ‘You know what? I have actually considered that possibility. But I decided you won't be looking for revenge. Know why? It's a question of economic behavioural psychology.’"

关键词：windshield / considered / possibility / revenge / economic behavioural psychology

为什么这样写：Oz 的"actually considered"是诚实的——他想过毒贩可能会报复。"economic behavioural psychology"是学术——他用理论解释一切。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"Know why?"是挑衅——他在教毒贩"道理"。
