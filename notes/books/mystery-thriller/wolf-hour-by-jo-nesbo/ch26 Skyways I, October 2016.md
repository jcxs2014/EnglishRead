# 26. Skyways I, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Hanson 在 Track Plaza 购物中心寻找 Gomez，计划在电梯里暗杀他；Kay Myers 和 Oz 也赶到，通过视频监控追踪 Gomez；Hanson 的计划被 Myers 打断。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 追捕——三方（Hanson、Oz、Myers）同时追踪 Gomez，紧张感升级。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："腐败警察的阴谋"——Hanson 计划在电梯里暗杀 Gomez，制造"自卫"假象；"监控系统"——城市的天网系统追踪每个人的行动。
- **人物弧线**：Hanson 从"霸凌者"变为"杀手"——他打算用"警察"身份掩盖谋杀；Oz 从"被停职"变为"顾问"——他坐在 Kay 的车里指导。
- **叙事手法**：多视角——Hanson、Kay/Oz 双线并行，最后 Hanson 的"计划"被 Myers 的电话打断。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Olav Hanson was panting. He'd run all the way from where he'd parked his car at Track Plaza. He tried to ignore the pain in his knee as the escalator slowly moved him up to the second floor."

关键词：panting / run all the way / pain in his knee / escalator / second floor

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"panting"是生理——他老了，跑不动了。"pain in his knee"是旧伤——ch12 提到过他的膝盖。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"escalator"是缓慢的——他急着追捕但身体跟不上。

> **原句 2:** "He had his phone plugged into one ear and as he had driven from Southdale he'd been getting updates all the way from the MPD's video centre telling him Gomez was still at the restaurant."

关键词：plugged into one ear / updates / MPD's video centre

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"plugged into one ear"是动作——他一边听一边行动。"video centre"是技术——监控中心。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"updates"是实时——他一直在接收信息。

> **原句 3:** "The video centre received images from over three hundred cameras located indoors and outdoors in the downtown area and was a cooperative enterprise involving law enforcement and local businesses. It had either drastically reduced crime or – as some critics claimed – transferred it to other parts of the city."

关键词：over three hundred cameras / cooperative enterprise / law enforcement / drastically reduced / critics / transferred

为什么这样写：Nesbø 的"three hundred cameras"是真实的——监控系统的规模。"critics"是反对声音——犯罪只是"转移"了。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"glass wall"是讽刺——监控是"透明"的，但腐败仍然存在。

> **原句 4:** "Concerns about secret surveillance had been dealt with by making the project open to all, using a glass wall behind which anyone could come and sit and see the same pictures as the police."

关键词：secret surveillance / dealt with / open to all / glass wall

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"glass wall"是透明——监控是"公开"的。"see the same pictures"是平等——公众和警察看到的一样。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"audience"是讽刺——他在"表演"给公众看。

> **原句 5:** "His shirt was wet with sweat, and he could feel the edge of his holster rubbing against his armpit. The plan was simple but sound. An arrest on camera in front of witnesses and everything done by the book, body search, reading his rights, the whole bit. Apart from the fact that he wasn't going to handcuff Gomez."

关键词：wet with sweat / holster / rubbing / armpit / plan / simple but sound / on camera / witnesses / by the book / body search / reading his rights / whole bit / wasn't going to handcuff

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"holster"是枪套——他在感受枪的存在。"simple but sound"是军事术语——计划"简单但可靠"。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"wasn't going to handcuff"是关键——他故意不铐人，为了"杀人"。

> **原句 6:** "He'd deliberately left his cuffs in the car and would tell Internal Affairs afterward that he'd forgotten them. He'd take Gomez over to the elevators and order everyone out of the first one that arrived. Because there were no cameras in the elevators. He'd checked. He'd shoot Gomez before they reached the lobby, make sure his fingerprints were on the barrel and say Gomez had tried to grab the gun off him."

关键词：deliberately / cuffs / Internal Affairs / afterward / forgotten / elevators / no cameras / checked / lobby / barrel / tried to grab the gun off him

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"deliberately"是预谋——他故意留下手铐。"no cameras"是关键——电梯没有监控。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"make sure his fingerprints were on the barrel"是细节——他要在枪上留下指纹，制造"自卫"假象。

> **原句 7:** "None were wearing the hoodie he'd seen on the video at the parking garage. Nobody had the raven-black hair he remembered on Lobo. But if Lobo had moved on, why hadn't the video centre passed on the message?"

关键词：hoodie / raven-black hair / Lobo / moved on / passed on the message

为什么这样写：Hanson 的"hoodie"是线索——他在找衣服。"raven-black hair"是外貌——他在找特征。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"passed on the message"是疑问——为什么监控中心没报告？

> **原句 8:** "‘I've talked to the video centre and told them to send all further information via me from here. Where are you?’ … ‘I've got this, Myers. I expect to be arresting Gomez at any moment. I'll let you know if I need backup.’"

关键词：talked to / video centre / all further information via me / arresting / backup

为什么这样写：Myers 的"via me"是命令——所有信息通过她。"backup"是支援——她准备派人。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"I've got this"是逞强——他想抢功。

> **原句 9:** "‘This is my case, Hanson, and I'm asking you to provide me with adequate information.’ … ‘I was first on the crime scene, Myers, it's –’ ‘Bullshit! The instructions are for the duty detective to take the case until notified otherwise.’"

关键词：my case / adequate information / first on the crime scene / Bullshit / instructions / duty detective / until notified otherwise

为什么这样写：Myers 的"Bullshit"是粗口——她在反驳。"instructions"是规则——值班侦探负责。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"first on the crime scene"是规则——谁先到谁负责。

> **原句 10:** "‘Just that he's moved away from the pizza restaurant, and they haven't seen him on any of the skyways so he's probably still inside the Track Plaza building. I've called in SWAT, so if you find Gomez, keep him under observation but do not attempt an arrest on your own. Got that?’"

关键词：moved away / skyways / probably still inside / SWAT / keep him under observation / do not attempt an arrest on your own

为什么这样写：Myers 的"SWAT"是特警——她叫了特警。"keep him under observation"是监视——不要行动。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"OK"是敷衍——他不会遵守。

> **原句 11:** "Kay shrugged. Bob was referring to a part-time officer who had shot and killed a man who went berserk with two knives inside a shopping mall in St Cloud. The officer, who ran a shooting range and was armed wherever he went, even on an off-duty visit to a shopping mall, became a local hero and a poster boy for the NRA, who had bestowed on him the dubious title ‘NRA Officer of the Year’."

关键词：referring to / part-time officer / shot and killed / went berserk / two knives / St Cloud / poster boy / NRA / bestowed / dubious title

为什么这样写：Bob 的"St Cloud"是真实事件——Nesbō 引用真实案例。"poster boy"是讽刺——NRA 的"英雄"。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"dubious title"是讽刺——"可疑的荣誉"。

> **原句 12:** "‘You men and your pissing contests,’ Kay snorted. ‘What matters is that someone arrests him, not who does it.’"

关键词：pissing contests / snorted / what matters / arrests him / not who does it

为什么这样写：Kay 的"pissing contests"是粗口——"争风吃醋"。"what matters"是实用——谁抓不重要。

读者视角提示：Hanson 的"not who does it"是反常——通常警察在意"谁抓的"。

> **原句 13:** "Then Myers put her foot down hard, swung the car up onto the sidewalk and sped past the jammed cars with the horn going full blast."

关键词：put her foot down hard / swung the car / sidewalk / sped past / jammed cars / horn / full blast

为什么这样写：Kay 的"put her foot down hard"是加速——她在"踩油门"。"full blast"是最大——喇叭全响。

读者视角提示：Kay 的"swung the car up onto the sidewalk"是违规——她开上人行道。
