# 36. McDeath, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 与 Lunde 在麦当劳会面，听了 Gomez 的录音；Lunde 透露这个麦当劳就是"Goya 家人被枪杀的地方"（McDeath）；Oz 意识到 Goya 在"追踪记忆"，就像他自己一样。
- **情感弧线位置**：连接 / 揭示——Lunde 揭示了 Goya 的过去（家人在麦当劳被枪杀）；Oz 意识到他和 Goya 都是"追踪记忆"的人。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的过去"——Lunde 透露 Goya 的家人在这个麦当劳被枪杀；"蛾子"——Oz 说有一种杀手像蛾子一样被火焰吸引。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"追捕者"变为"理解者"——他意识到 Goya 和他一样在"追踪记忆"；Lunde 从"标本师"变为"共情者"——他理解 Goya 的痛苦。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Oz 与 Lunde 的对话揭示了 Goya 的过去；Oz 的独白展现了他的内心世界。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "It was pouring down as Bob swung into the parking lot in front of the McDonald's. He turned off the engine and peered out. Heard the distant rumble from the 35W, the freeway that passed directly above him and blocked out the view in the west."

关键词：pouring down / swung / parking lot / engine / peered out / distant rumble / 35W / freeway / blocked out

为什么这样写：Oz 的"pouring down"是倾盆大雨——雨很大。"freeway"是高速公路——35W 高速公路。

> **原句 2:** "Cashmere was showerproof, but if it got soaked through then the coat would stink like a wet dog for days afterward."

关键词：Cashmere / showerproof / soaked through / stink / wet dog

为什么这样写：Oz 的"showerproof"是防雨——羊绒外套防雨。"wet dog"是湿狗——淋湿后像湿狗一样臭。

> **原句 3:** "Bob bought two vanilla shakes to make it all look legit and then slid in opposite Mike, who had placed his cell phone on the table between them."

关键词：vanilla shakes / legit / slid in / cell phone / table

为什么这样写：Oz 的"legit"是合法——为了看起来合法。"slid in"是滑进去——他坐进去。

> **原句 4:** "Mike tapped the play button. Bob heard heavy breathing. It stopped. Then the sound of Mike's voice: ‘Yes, Tomás, what is it?’ More panting. Again it stopped. ‘I know it's taken time, Tomás, but I've finally finished the Labrador and now I can start on your cat. I'm delivering the dog at twelve o'clock tomorrow, so if you could come by at two?’"

关键词：play button / heavy breathing / panting / Labrador / delivering / twelve o'clock / come by at two

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"Labrador"是拉布拉多犬——他做的狗标本。"delivering"是送——他送狗。

> **原句 5:** "The panting resumed. And stopped. Like a malfunctioning respirator, thought Bob."

关键词：resumed / stopped / malfunctioning respirator

为什么这样写：Oz 的"malfunctioning respirator"是故障的呼吸器——呼吸声像呼吸器。

> **原句 6:** "‘He hung up,’ said Bob. ‘I think he heard it,’ said Mike. ‘Heard what?’ ‘My betrayal. That I was lying. He won't come.’"

关键词：hung up / heard / betrayal / lying / won't come

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"betrayal"是背叛——Goya 听出了背叛。"lying"是撒谎——他在撒谎。

> **原句 7:** "‘You know what I think, Mike?’ ‘Yes, I believe I do.’ ‘So then what?’ ‘You think I acted worse than I had to. That I wanted him to realise it was a trap. That I found a way of warning him at the same time as I kept my word to you and fulfilled my responsibilities as a good citizen. On paper, at least.’"

关键词：acted worse / realise / trap / warning / kept my word / fulfilled / responsibilities / good citizen / on paper

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"trap"是陷阱——他在设陷阱。"good citizen"是好公民——他履行了责任。

> **原句 8:** "‘Sometimes we're convinced that a particular action is exclusively the product of a reasoning process, don't you agree? But then – could be a long time afterward – we start to doubt. That good testimonial you gave to a student taxidermist, was that justified? Or was it out of pity for someone whose talent you know is little more than mediocre?’"

关键词：convinced / exclusively / reasoning process / doubt / testimonial / justified / pity / mediocre

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"reasoning process"是推理过程——他认为行动是推理的结果。"mediocre"是平庸的——才能平庸。

> **原句 9:** "‘Looks like Tomás Gomez called from a phone booth a block away from your store.’ Mike raised his eyebrows. It dawned on Bob. ‘You know what, Mike? He was on his way to the store. He saw me sitting waiting outside. He must've figured I was a cop and run off.’"

关键词：phone booth / block away / raised his eyebrows / dawned on / figured / cop / run off

为什么这样写：Oz 的"dawned on"是意识到——他意识到了。"cop"是警察——Goya 以为他是警察。

> **原句 10:** "‘McDeath.’ ‘McDeath?’ ‘That's what they call this McDonald's here. The crack gangs used to hang out here. This is where Tomás was eating that night, with his family.’"

关键词：McDeath / crack gangs / hang out / eating / family

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"McDeath"是麦当劳的昵称——"死亡麦当劳"。"crack gangs"是快克帮派——帮派聚集地。

> **原句 11:** "‘He said they were sitting at the table nearest the door, so it must've been that one over there.’ Mike pointed. ‘He said he was happy that evening. It was his daughter's birthday, she was the one who insisted they go to McDonald's. He didn't know this was a gang hangout, he'd just driven by a few times and noticed they had a parking lot. It was a perfect evening. There were balloons, the kids sang a song they'd been taught in elementary school, he and his wife Monica sat there dreaming about the future. Where they were going to live, if the kids should go to university, et cetera. About how lucky they were to live in a land that offered so many opportunities for anyone who was prepared to make an effort. A country that gives you a chance no matter whether you're black, brown or white, if you sit in a wheelchair or you don't come from a rich family. You could make it even if your immigration papers weren't yet in order, because as long as you willed it hard enough, you knew things would work out. Tomás had what he thought was an unshakeable faith in the future. But as things turned out it only took thirty or forty seconds to shake it to its foundations. He used to say he wished I could have known the person he was back then. That I would have liked him. But that he no longer exists, that person no longer exists. He died here that day, along with his family. The man sitting in front of me was just his ghost.’"

关键词：table nearest the door / daughter's birthday / insisted / gang hangout / driven by / parking lot / perfect evening / balloons / sang / elementary school / dreaming / future / university / et cetera / lucky / land / opportunities / effort / chance / black, brown or white / wheelchair / rich family / immigration papers / in order / willed / unshakeable faith / foundations / ghost

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"unshakeable faith"是不可动摇的信念——Goya 对未来的信念。"ghost"是幽灵——Goya 是幽灵。

> **原句 12:** "‘That it could've been you or me. It could have been you sitting here with your family that evening, Bob.’"

关键词：could've been / sitting here / family / evening

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"could've been"是可能是——可能是你。

> **原句 13:** "‘I'll be at your store at one thirty tomorrow, Mike. Maybe the guy will turn up after all.’ ‘Why should he, when he knows he'd be walking straight into an ambush?’ ‘I don't know. There's a certain kind of killer we call a moth.’"

关键词：turn up / walking straight into / ambush / moth

为什么这样写：Oz 的"moth"是蛾子——一种杀手类型。"ambush"是埋伏——他会走进埋伏。

> **原句 14:** "‘They seem attracted to the investigation into the murder they've committed. They turn up at the scene of the crime, or at the funeral. They make an effort to get close to the detectives involved. Frequent the bars where they hang out, close to the police station. They're like moths that can't help flying toward the flame, even though they know they might get their wings burned off.’"

关键词：attracted / investigation / committed / scene of the crime / funeral / get close / detectives / frequent / bars / flame / wings burned off

为什么这样写：Oz 的"moths"是蛾子——被火焰吸引。"wings burned off"是翅膀烧掉——明知会受伤。

> **原句 15:** "‘Maybe Tomás knows the game is almost up. Maybe deep down he just wants to get it over with.’"

关键词：game is almost up / deep down / get it over with

为什么这样写：Oz 的"game is almost up"是游戏快结束了——Goya 知道。"get it over with"是结束——他想结束。
