# 41. White, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Joe Kjos 接到了一个来自 Mitro 射击场的电话——一名白人男子曾用气泡膜包裹步枪来射击；Oz 在车内搜索 McDeath 案件的新闻，发现 Mike Lunde 就是 Tomás Gomez——他向 Oz 坦白了一切。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 真相——Oz 意识到 Lunde 就是 Goya，他向 Kari 坦白了自己的发现。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的身份"——Oz 发现 Lunde 就是 Goya；"坦白"——Lunde 向 Oz 坦白了一切，包括家人的死、Hanson 的腐败、以及他如何"杀死"了 Hanson。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"追捕者"变为"面对者"——他终于知道了杀手的身份。
- **叙事手法**：Oz 的第一人称独白——读者直接进入他的意识流，感受他的震惊。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "He'd checked out the final lead, a tip-off from a long-distance truck driver who claimed to have met a strange man who looked like Tomás Gomez at a truck-stop cafe just the other side of the Iowa border."

关键词：checked out / final lead / tip-off / long-distance truck driver / claimed / strange man / looked like / Tomás Gomez / truck-stop cafe / Iowa border

为什么这样写：Kjos 的"checked out"是核查——他核查了线索。"tip-off"是密报——卡车司机的密报。

> **原句 2:** "Now it was time to get over to Arb where there was other work to do. The patrol who rang it in said the corpse had no head and no papers that could identify who it was, but there was a bullet hole in the chest leaving little doubt that it was a matter for the Homicide Division."

关键词：Arb / corpse / no head / no papers / identify / bullet hole / chest / leaving little doubt / matter for the Homicide Division

为什么这样写：Kjos 的"leaving little doubt"是毫无疑问——毫无疑问是凶杀案。

> **原句 3:** "A phone rang somewhere out in the deserted office landscape. Joe shrugged on his jacket. He was actually pissed with Olav for not making him part of his stadium team and leaving him with this shit job instead."

关键词：deserted office landscape / shrugged on his jacket / pissed / stadium team / shit job

为什么这样写：Kjos 的"pissed"是生气——他对 Hanson 不满。"shit job"是烂工作——他被留下了烂工作。

> **原句 4:** "‘MPD.’ ‘Good morning, my name is Jim Andersen. Kay Myers, is she …?’ ‘She just left. This is Detective Joe Kjos, how can I help you, sir?’"

关键词：MPD / Jim Andersen / Kay Myers / Detective Joe Kjos

为什么这样写：Jim 的"Good morning"是早上好——他在打电话。

> **原句 5:** "‘I'm an instructor at the Mitro shooting range,’ the guy said. ‘Your colleague who was here left her card and asked me to call the number if I remembered anything about the Latino she was looking for.’"

关键词：instructor / Mitro shooting range / colleague / card / Latino / looking for

为什么这样写：Jim 的"instructor"是教练——射击场教练。"Latino"是拉丁裔——Kay 在找拉丁裔。

> **原句 6:** "‘I still don't recall any Latino, but then something came to me. She traced us through a target you guys found inside some bubble wrap.’ Joe Kjos checked the time. ‘There was a guy in here with a rifle wrapped in bubble wrap. But he wasn't Latino. He was white.’"

关键词：recall / traced / target / bubble wrap / rifle / white

为什么这样写：Jim 的"traced"是追踪——Kay 通过靶纸追踪到他们。"white"是白人——那个人是白人。

> **原句 7:** "‘Absolutely not.’ ‘Did he give you a name or a phone number?’ ‘No.’ ‘Anything else you can tell us about him?’ ‘Not really.’"

关键词：Absolutely not / name / phone number / Anything else

为什么这样写：Jim 的"Absolutely not"是绝对没有——他没有异常行为。

> **原句 8:** "Bob Oz was pulling on his coat as he ran down the steps and out into the street with his phone pressed hard to his ear. ‘Pick up,’ he whispered as he headed for the Volvo parked higher up the street. ‘Pick up, damn you.’"

关键词：pulling on his coat / ran down / steps / street / phone pressed hard / whispered / Volvo / higher up the street / Pick up

为什么这样写：Oz 的"whispered"是低语——他在低语。"damn you"是妈的——他在骂。

> **原句 9:** "Searching the net for the murder he had got at least a dozen hits, most with the headline ‘McDeath’. He'd scrolled his way through them. Family celebrating birthday at McDonald's killed in gang shootout. Mother and two children killed, father only survivor. Still no arrests in McDeath massacre."

关键词：Searching the net / dozen hits / headline / McDeath / scrolled / Family celebrating birthday / gang shootout / Mother and two children killed / father only survivor / no arrests / massacre

为什么这样写：Oz 的"McDeath"是麦当劳死亡——新闻标题。"massacre"是屠杀——大屠杀。

> **原句 10:** "‘Kari.’ ‘There you are! Sorry for calling you on a Saturday, Kari, but I need the address of a Mike Lunde, he lives somewhere in Chanhassen. I'm going to give you a phone number, are you ready?’ ‘We're in the middle of lunch here, Bob, can this wait?’ ‘No. Oh fuck!’ ‘I'm sorry?’ ‘My apologies. Someone's broken the wing mirror on my car. No, it can't wait. I've got … I know who he is.’ ‘Who who is?’ ‘The killer, Kari.’ Bob had fished his keys out with his free hand but then dropped them on the road. ‘I thought he was telling me a story he heard from one of his customers. But it was his own story. Mike Lunde told me everything exactly like it was, in detail. He confessed, Kari! And I didn't realise.’"

关键词：Saturday / address / Mike Lunde / Chanhassen / phone number / in the middle of lunch / Oh fuck / wing mirror / can't wait / know who he is / killer / fished his keys / dropped / story / customers / his own story / everything exactly like it was / in detail / confessed / didn't realise

为什么这样写：Oz 的"confessed"是坦白——Lunde 坦白了。"didn't realise"是没意识到——Oz 没意识到。
