# 19. Four Hundred Yards, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：杀手（Tomás Gomez）在射击场练习，用假名"Tomás Jones"预订了射击课程；他完美命中 400 码目标，随后遇到两名警察——但警察只是来归还他遗忘的靶纸。
- **情感弧线位置：高潮 / 悬念——杀手的冷静与专业再次展现，而他与警察的"擦肩而过"制造了强烈的戏剧反讽。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手练习"——Gomez 不是疯狂的杀手，而是精确的计算者；"擦肩而过"——警察与杀手面对面却不知道。
- **人物弧线**：Gomez 从 ch15 的"悲伤父亲"变为"冷血杀手"——他在射击时想着家人，但手上的动作毫无犹豫。
- **叙事手法**：纯杀手第一人称——读者再次进入他的视角，感受他的思维模式。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "There was a Donald Duck in the store. The noonday sun had cast a strip of shadow across its bill. A target had been drawn on the forehead and he was holding a pistol that was pointing at me. I walked to the counter. The wall behind it was hung with rifles for sale. They stocked magazines and pistol butts and put you in mind of Iraq and Afghanistan rather than deer hunting."

关键词：Donald Duck / noonday sun / cast a strip of shadow / bill / target / forehead / pistol / counter / rifles / magazines / pistol butts / Iraq and Afghanistan / deer hunting

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"Donald Duck"是讽刺——射击游戏用卡通人物作靶子。"put you in mind of Iraq and Afghanistan"暗示枪支文化的暴力本质。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"deer hunting"是对比——狩猎 vs. 杀人。

> **原句 2:** "‘Welcome to Mitro,’ he said. ‘What can I help you with today, sir?’ ‘I have an hour with an instructor booked.’"

关键词：Welcome to Mitro / instructor booked

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"instructor booked"是计划——他提前预订，不是临时起意。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"booked"暗示他的"专业"——他像预约牙医一样预约射击课。

> **原句 3:** "‘Tomás.’ It just slipped out. No big deal but I would have to watch out, be on guard for any signs my concentration was slipping. Think. Think. All the time."

关键词：slipped out / no big deal / watch out / on guard / concentration slipping

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"slipped out"是失误——他说出了真名。"concentration slipping"暗示他的专注——他必须时刻警惕。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"Think. Think. All the写："是强迫性的——他必须不断提醒自己。

> **原句 4:** "Jim took me outside. We passed two standard shooting ranges, one where you shot at clay pigeons and one with targets in the shape of human beings. Three-hundred-yard ranges for standard rifles, Jim told me. Two normal, nice-looking armed teenagers standing on a rise greeted us politely, him wearing a jacket with a Stars and Stripes logo, her in a sweater with PRO-GUN written on it."

关键词：shooting ranges / clay pigeons / targets in the shape of human beings / three-hundred-yard ranges / armed teenagers / Stars and Stripes / PRO-GUN

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"targets in the shape of human beings"是讽刺——射击场用真人形状靶子。"PRO-GUN"是文化符号——美国枪支文化。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"armed teenagers"是细节——连青少年都带枪。

> **原句 5:** "‘I don't recommend that a beginner start by shooting at something that's over three hundred yards away,’ said Jim. ‘At three hundred you're already flirting with what we call maximum point-blank range, doesn't matter what ammunition you're using. Further than that and the bullet will be affected so much by wind and weather that the beginner will just wound the deer or frighten it off, and you don't want that, Tomás.’"

关键词：beginner / flirting with / maximum point-blank range / ammunition / wind and weather / wound the deer / frighten it off

为什么这样写：Jim 的"flirting with"是口语——"接近"。"maximum point-blank range"是射击术语——最大直射距离。

读者视角提示：Jim 的"wound the deer"是讽刺——他在教"狩猎"，但 Gomez 在学"杀人"。

> **原句 6:** "I took off my sunglasses. Our eyes met. ‘Four hundred yards,’ I repeated. ‘All I need to know is whether my calculations are correct or is there something I haven't been taking into consideration.’"

关键词：took off my sunglasses / eyes met / four hundred yards / calculations / taking into consideration

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"eyes met"是挑战——他在测试 Jim。"calculations"是关键词——他用数学而非直觉。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"taking into consideration"是精确——他需要确认所有变量。

> **原句 7:** "He rolled over on his side and pulled out his phone. Tapped the calculator. ‘OK, four hundred yards,’ he said. ‘You have to aim as though the distance was four hundred yards.’ ‘That's what I thought.’"

关键词：rolled over / pulled out his phone / tapped the calculator / aim as though

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"tapped the calculator"是技术——他用计算器确认。"aim as though"是简化——Jim 的"建议"毫无用处。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"that's what I thought"是轻蔑——他已经知道了。

> **原句 8:** "I took a breath, held it. Saw Cody Karlstad's face in front of me the way it looked in the picture. A target on his forehead like that Donald Duck. Pointing a gun at me, my wife, my children. I fired. Loaded. Fired. Loaded."

关键词：held it / Cody Karlstad's face / target on his forehead / Donald Duck / pointing a gun / fired / loaded

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"Cody Karlstad"是目标——他在脑海中把靶子替换为仇人。"fired. Loaded. Fired. Loaded."是节奏——机械的、无情的。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"my wife, my children"是动机——他在为家人复仇。

> **原句 9:** "‘It's empty,’ said Jim. ‘What?’ ‘The magazine. It's empty. You can quit squeezing the trigger.’ ‘Sure.’ I put the rifle down and stood up."

关键词：empty / magazine / quit squeezing the trigger

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"empty"是双关——弹匣空了，也是他的"情感"状态。"quit squeezing the trigger"是字面意思——停止射击。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"sure"是随意的——他甚至没意识到弹匣空了。

> **原句 10:** "All five shots had hit within a radius of five to six inches. ‘Could be better,’ I said, noting that the spread was more horizontal than vertical. ‘Any advice?’"

关键词：five shots / radius / five to six inches / spread / horizontal / vertical / advice

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"radius of five to six inches"是精确——他的枪法极好。"horizontal than vertical"是分析——他在评估自己的表现。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"could be better"是谦虚——他已经很满意了。

> **原句 11:** "‘What exactly are you going to be hunting?’ he finally asked. I carried on wrapping. ‘Why d'you ask?’ ‘An M24. Not that you can't use it for hunting, I mean, that's what it was originally for. With a few modifications.’ ‘Beasts of prey,’ I said without looking up."

关键词：hunting / M24 / originally / modifications / beasts of prey

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"beasts of prey"是双关——他在"狩猎"人类。"modifications"暗示步枪被改装过。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"without looking up"是回避——他不想与 Jim 对视。

> **原句 12:** "‘Not that it's any of my business, Tomás, but you do know that the wolf is protected now, right?’ ‘Is it?’ ‘Yup. But relax, I ain't planning to sneak. Wolves have been seen in Cedar Creek, dammit, that's just a half-hour from downtown, and this is a free country, people have the right to protect themselves, if you ask me. Or am I wrong, Tomás?’ ‘Damn right they do,’ I said."

关键词：protected / wolf / Cedar Creek / free country / right to protect themselves / Damn right

为什么这样写：Jim 的"wolf"是双关——Gomez 的代号是"Lobo"（狼）。"protected"是讽刺——狼被保护，但人不是。

读者视角提示：Jim 的"free country"是美国价值观——Gomez 利用这种价值观。

> **原句 13:** "I heard someone enter behind me. Don't know why I turned, maybe it was something about the footsteps, the coughing, the gravelly voice speaking. Two uniformed cops, a man and a woman. I felt my heart beat faster. I picked up my change, wedged the rifle under my arm, looked downward and marched out. I saw the empty cop car in the parking lot. Nothing strange about police coming to a shooting range, I told myself, they probably come here to practise. All the same I walked faster than I normally would. And when I heard that gravelly voice calling ‘Sir! Wait!’ then I knew that no matter how well you've planned things – whether it's for a family's future, or for how to handle losing one – you haven't a hope against the play of chance."

关键词：footsteps / coughing / gravelly voice / uniformed cops / heart beat faster / wedged / marched out / empty cop car / shooting range / walked faster / Sir! Wait! / play of chance

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"gravelly voice"是细节——他记住了声音。"play of chance"是主题——命运无常。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"heart beat faster"是罕见的——他也会害怕。

> **原句 14:** "Should I stop? Run? Tear the bubble wrap off the rifle and attack? I stopped. Turned slowly. The cop was running toward me. He hadn't taken the gun out of his holster yet but he was holding something in his hand. I tensed, not quite sure yet what for. ‘Jim says you forgot this, sir,’ he said as he caught up with me. I saw now what he was holding. The target. I must have left it on the counter. ‘Thank you so much,’ I said. I tried to smile as I wedged the target inside the bubble wrap. ‘Courtesy of the MPD.’ The cop laughed. And I could see then he was a man it would have been easy to like. I laughed too. Because he had no idea he was face-to-face with the man who'd shot a Jordan gun dealer two days before, and in just a few more hours was going to be at work again."

关键词：run / tear the bubble wrap off / attack / holster / tensed / forgot / target / counter / Courtesy of the MPD / laughed / face-to-face / shot a Jordan gun dealer / at work again

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"tear the bubble wrap off"是暴力想象——他准备攻击。"Courtesy of the MPD"是幽默——警察在开玩笑。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"at work again"是双关——他"工作"（杀人）刚结束，又要开始新的"工作"。

杀手（Tomás Gomez）在射击场练习，用假名预订射击课程，完美命中 400 码目标；他与两名警察擦肩而过——警察只是来归还他遗忘的靶纸，而他"又要去上班了"。
