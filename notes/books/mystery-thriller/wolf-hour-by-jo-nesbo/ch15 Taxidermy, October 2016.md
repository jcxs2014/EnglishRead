# 15. Taxidermy, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 访问标本师 Mike Lunde，得知了 Tomás Gomez 的悲惨过去——他的妻儿在帮派交火中被杀，女儿死在他怀里；Gomez 可能是为了给家人复仇而杀死 Dante。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 / 真相——Oz 第一次了解杀手的动机，从"追捕凶手"变为"理解凶手"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："凶手的前史"——Gomez 不再是冷血的杀手，而是一个失去一切的父亲/丈夫。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 的愤怒被同情取代——他意识到 Dante（枪支贩子）间接导致了 Gomez 家人的死亡。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——整章几乎全为 Oz 与 Lunde 的对话，Lunde 的叙述揭示 Gomez 的背景。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The time was 9 A.M. and Bob was standing in a narrow street in downtown. He looked at the sign above the store doorway. Town Taxidermy. In the display window a black bear stood upright on two legs, and around it, like courtiers, a gathering of birds and various rodents, which, Bob assumed, formed part of the local fauna."

关键词：narrow street / Town Taxidermy / display window / black bear / upright / courtiers / gathering / birds / rodents / local fauna

为什么这样写：Oz 的视角——他把标本比作"courtiers"（朝臣），暗示标本店是一个"宫廷"，而标本是"臣民"。"local fauna"（本地动物群）暗示这些动物曾经生活在明尼阿波利斯。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"courtiers"比喻暗示他已经开始"欣赏"标本——他被 Lunde 的世界吸引。

> **原句 2:** "As he entered a bell over the door jingled feebly. But once it stopped, and the door had closed behind him, he noticed how quiet it was. Quieter than simply soundless. Quiet as the grave, he thought, as he looked around at the bodies of the silent animals. A hart, a lynx. A wolverine with bared teeth. Several birds. As far as he could tell they were perfect copies of the original living beings they once had been."

关键词：bell / jingled feebly / quiet / soundless / quiet as the grave / bodies / silent animals / hart / lynx / wolverine / bared teeth / perfect copies / original living beings

为什么这样写：Oz 的"quiet as the grave"是双关——标本店像坟墓一样安静，但也是"动物的坟墓"。"perfect copies"暗示标本师的高超技艺——死亡被"完美"复制。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"bared teeth"是标本的"表情"——死亡被赋予了"生命"的表情。

> **原句 3:** "He stopped before each one in turn. How lifelike they were. As though they all had a story to tell. So unlike the corpses he was used to seeing. Murder victims with expressions of fear, perhaps, or pain, but who otherwise hid more than they revealed, holding on to secrets it was his job to wrest from them."

关键词：lifelike / story to tell / unlike the corpses / murder victims / fear / pain / hid more than they revealed / holding on to secrets / wrest from them

为什么这样写：Oz 的对比——标本"有故事可讲"，而尸体"隐藏秘密"。"wrest from them"（从他们那里夺取）暗示 Oz 的工作是"逼供"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"wrest"是暴力性的——他的职业是"从死者那里夺取秘密"。

> **原句 4:** "Bob stood contemplating an owl that returned his gaze. And it occurred to him that the silence in here wasn't oppressive at all, it was … restful. Liberating. Balm for the ears and the soul."

关键词：contemplating / owl / returned his gaze / silence / oppressive / restful / liberating / balm / ears / soul

为什么这样写：Oz 的"restful"是罕见的——他在标本店找到了平静。"balm"（药膏）暗示他的"耳朵和灵魂"在愈合。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"liberating"暗示他在"正常"世界中感到窒息——只有在"死亡"中他才能自由。

> **原句 5:** "‘Good morning.’ A smiling man with a laurel wreath of hair surrounding a bare dome emerged from a doorway, in the act of removing a pair of latex gloves. ‘Sorry to keep you waiting, I was in the middle of something rather complicated in the workshop.’"

关键词：smiling / laurel wreath / bare dome / removing / latex gloves / workshop

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"laurel wreath of hair"是比喻——他的头发像月桂冠（古希腊胜利者的象征）。"bare dome"（秃顶）暗示他的年龄。"latex gloves"暗示他正在工作。

读者视角提示：Lunde 的"smiling"与 Oz 的严肃形成对照——标本师是一个快乐的人。

> **原句 6:** "‘My great-grandfather's adaptation of his Norwegian name. A-a-a-s-s. The pronunciation is the same. At least it is according to our Norwegian relatives.’"

关键词：great-grandfather / adaptation / Norwegian name / A-a-a-s-s / pronunciation / Norwegian relatives

为什么这样写：Oz 解释他的姓氏——"Ås"（挪威语"山脊"）被美国人读成"Ass"（屁股）。这个解释既幽默又悲凉——他的身份被误读。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"Norwegian relatives"暗示他与挪威的联系——他是一个"外来者"。

> **原句 7:** "‘I saw in the morning newspaper that you were looking for Tomás Gomez. So I rang the police and left a message. A … eh, a tip-off, isn't that what it's called? That was just …’ He looked at his watch. ‘Two hours ago. That was what I meant about being quick.’"

关键词：morning newspaper / Tomás Gomez / rang the police / tip-off / message / two hours ago / being quick

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"tip-off"是公民行为——他主动联系警察。"two hours ago"暗示 Oz 来得很快。

读者视角提示：Lunde 的"being quick"是对 Oz 的赞扬——警察这次没有拖延。

> **原句 8:** "‘I had no idea he was going to shoot someone, if that's what you mean. If, that is, Tomás really is the one who shot this other person.’"

关键词：no idea / going to shoot / if that's what you mean / really is the one / this other person

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"if that's what you mean"是防御性的——他在试探 Oz。"this other person"是疏离的——他不认识 Dante。

读者视角提示：Lunde 的"really is the one"暗示他不确定——他希望 Gomez 不是杀手。

> **原句 9:** "‘Tomás Gomez is a very common name. There was no photograph or drawing in the newspaper, and yet you knew that the man in question must be your Tomás Gomez. You call in with information, but now you express doubt about whether it really was Gomez who did the shooting. And you referred to him by his first name just now.’"

关键词：common name / no photograph / no drawing / knew / call in / express doubt / first name

为什么这样写：Oz 的分析——Lunde 知道 Gomez 是谁，因为他们很熟。"first name"是证据——Lunde 用"Tomás"而非"Gomez"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"call in"是警察术语——他在分析 Lunde 的行为。

> **原句 10:** "‘My wife always tells me I'm a terrible liar. She says I should practise more.’ He gave a resigned smile. ‘So yes, I do know Tomás as rather more than just an ordinary customer.’"

关键词：terrible liar / practise more / resigned smile / more than just an ordinary customer

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"resigned smile"是无奈的微笑——他被 Oz 看穿了。"more than just an ordinary customer"暗示他们是朋友。

读者视角提示：Lunde 的"practise more"是幽默——他的妻子希望他更擅长说谎，但他做不到。

> **原句 11:** "‘I like to get to know a bit about my customers. See what it is they want when they come here. What it is they're really looking for. Even when they're not entirely clear about it themselves.’"

关键词：get to know / customers / really looking for / not entirely clear about it themselves

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"really looking for"是心理层面的——顾客想要"标本"，但他们真正想要的是"记忆"。"not entirely clear about it themselves"暗示顾客不知道自己的动机。

读者视角提示：Lunde 的"really looking for"是全书的主题——每个人都在"寻找"（Oz 寻找 Alice，Gomez 寻找复仇）。

> **原句 12:** "‘The taxidermy of the word.’ ‘Sorry?’ ‘I visited Tomás Gomez on October 7.’"

关键词：taxidermy / word / October 7

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"taxidermy of the word"是比喻——语言也可以被"标本化"（记录）。这是一个元评论——Oz 正在"记录" Lunde 的话。

读者视角提示：Lunde 的"taxidermy of the word"与 ch14 Holger 的"I'm a taxidermist"呼应——两人都在讨论"标本化"。

> **原句 13:** "‘He invited me for some of his home-made chilli con carne. It was extremely tasty.’"

关键词：invited / home-made / chilli con carne / extremely tasty

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"home-made chilli con carne"是温暖的——Gomez 邀请他吃饭。"extremely tasty"是 Lunde 的赞美——Gomez 是一个好厨师。

读者视角提示：Lunde 的"home-made"暗示 Gomez 的孤独——他独自做饭，没有家人。
