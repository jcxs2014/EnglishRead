# 29. Feeling Minnesota, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Kay 和 Oz 在体育酒吧谈话，Oz 被电视直播出丑；Walker 打电话训斥他；Kay 透露了她在芝加哥 Englewood 的童年创伤（父亲家暴、母亲买枪自卫）；Oz 醉行至标本店，Lunde 让他进来喝咖啡。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 + 连接——Oz 的"醉态"被播出，但他与 Kay 的关系加深；Kay 的过去揭示了她对枪支的复杂态度。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："醉汉侦探"——Oz 在电视上出丑，但他对 Gomez 的分析（"受过训练的杀手"）是正确的；"童年的创伤"——Kay 讲述父亲家暴、母亲买枪的故事，解释了为什么她理解"枪支=安全"。
- **人物弧线**：Kay 从"专业侦探"变为"有过去的女人"——她分享了童年创伤；Oz 从"愤怒"变为"醉酒的哲学家"——他在雨中独行，思考 Liza。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Kay 和 Oz 的对话揭示了他们的过去；Oz 的独白在雨中展开，最后以 Lunde 的咖啡结束。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Kay entered the almost empty sports bar, saw the mustard-yellow coat and slipped onto the bar stool next to him. ‘Sorry,’ she said. ‘For what?’ ‘For letting them run you off like that.’"

关键词：almost empty / mustard-yellow coat / slipped onto / bar stool / run you off

为什么这样写：Kay 的"mustard-yellow coat"是标志——Oz 的标志性外套。"slipped onto"是动作——她悄悄坐下。

读者视角提示：Kay 的"sorry"是真诚的——她感到内疚。

> **原句 2:** "Kay groaned. ‘Guess I need a drink too.’ Bob signalled to the bartender. ‘A Johnnie Walker for the lady.’"

关键词：groaned / need a drink / signalled / bartender / Johnnie Walker

为什么这样写：Kay 的"groaned"是无奈——她也累了。"Johnnie Walker"是威士忌——Oz 请她喝酒。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"signalled"是熟练——他经常请人喝酒。

> **原句 3:** "‘Apropos,’ said Bob. ‘I've been thinking about what O'Rourke said about how it almost seems as though Gomez has had some kind of training.’"

关键词：Apropos / thinking / O'Rourke / training

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Apropos"是法语——"顺便一提"。"training"是关键词——Gomez 受过训练。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"thinking"是分析——他在思考 Gomez 的背景。

> **原句 4:** "‘Gomez is strong and supple. He got up into that shaft where O'Rourke needed two guys to help him up. And so quietly that no one else noticed anything. Before he dropped down into the yard he must have been hanging by his fingertips, pulled himself up again and used his head to snap the fan back into place. Not something you or I could have managed. And not O'Rourke either, even though he's in good shape.’"

关键词：strong and supple / shaft / two guys / quietly / hanging by his fingertips / snap the fan back into place / in good shape

为什么这样写：Oz 的"hanging by his fingertips"是细节——Gomez 用手指悬挂。"snap"是动作——用头把风扇推回。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"not something you or I could have managed"是自知——他知道自己做不到。

> **原句 5:** "‘I think Gomez has planned this whole thing very carefully. He's been working out with precisely this end in mind. And just the same way both murders were carefully planned, this last little game of his was planned too.’"

关键词：planned / working out / precisely / end in mind / carefully planned / little game

为什么这样写：Oz 的"precisely"是精确——Gomez 有明确目标。"little game"是比喻——Gomez 在"玩游戏"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"working out"是锻炼——Gomez 在训练。

> **原句 6:** "‘Don't you see? That it's just a little bit too much of a coincidence he ends up in a restroom where the ventilation shaft leads out into an empty backyard. That the fan is just high enough up on the wall for it not to be welded in as a precaution against somebody breaking in that way but low enough to make it possible to drop down from, provided you have training in how to fall, like a paratrooper. Maybe he had the foresight to place something on the ground to break his fall, some kind of mat or something.’"

关键词：coincidence / ventilation shaft / empty backyard / welded / precaution / paratrooper / foresight / break his fall

为什么这样写：Oz 的"paratrooper"是伞兵——Gomez 像伞兵一样跳下。"foresight"是远见——他提前准备。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"welded"是焊接——风扇没有焊死。

> **原句 7:** "‘Where are you going with this? That we should be looking for Tomás Gomez among elite soldiers or police officers?’"

关键词：looking for / elite soldiers / police officers

为什么这样写：Kay 的"elite soldiers"是精英士兵——她在质疑 Oz 的推理。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"where are you going"是追问——他在问 Kay 的结论。

> **原句 8:** "‘Oz,’ rumbled Superintendent Walker. ‘Did you see that item on KSTP?’ ‘Should I have?’ ‘It was broadcast live and you were on it, Oz.’"

关键词：rumbled / item / broadcast live / on it

为什么这样写：Walker 的"rumbled"是低沉的声音——他在生气。"broadcast live"是直播——Oz 被直播了。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"should I have"是反讽——他当然没看。

> **原句 9:** "‘What the hell were you even doing at Track Plaza? You're suspended, Oz! And you were drunk, dammit.’ ‘I had a Johnnie Walker to deal with, chief. I drank it. I drink. I'm suspended, dammit.’"

关键词：What the hell / suspended / drunk / dammit / deal with

为什么这样写：Walker 的"dammit"是粗口——他在骂。Oz 的"deal with"是双关——"处理"威士忌。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"I drink"是坦白——他承认喝酒。

> **原句 10:** "‘I want you to stay well clear of this case, Oz. Do you hear me?’ ‘Aye aye, chief. I promise. Starting now. Got to go.’ Bob hung up."

关键词：stay well clear / Aye aye / Starting now / Got to go

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Aye aye"是海军用语——"是，长官"。"Starting now"是敷衍——他不会遵守。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"Got to go"是借口——他想挂电话。

> **原句 11:** "‘My dad ran off before I was born,’ she said. ‘Nothing unusual about that in Englewood. Or that he was just another victim of the crack epidemic. What was special was that he would come home and rob my mother when he needed money. Because my mother had two jobs, she actually managed to put some aside for my sister and me to get an education. After the third time he broke in, beat my mother up and stole from us, she bought a gun. All she had to do was go into a store, fill out a few simple forms and she left carrying a weapon. I know you hate guns, but I tell you, when my sister and me were sleeping there in my mother's bed and she had that pistol underneath the pillow, we all felt safer. And that's a safety you middle-class liberals know nothing about because it's just something you take for granted. But for three girls in Englewood the gun was the great equaliser. It meant we didn't have to be helpless victims and let someone terrorise us because he was physically stronger. It didn't stop my mother from crying inside, but that gun changed our daily lives. Not a shot was ever fired from it, but it made us that little bit safer, we slept better, we could go to school and get ourselves an education. And I know the statistics that show what guns can do to a society like Englewood in the long run. But I'm being honest, you don't care a damn about the long run when your life is about surviving one night at a time.’"

关键词：ran off / Englewood / crack epidemic / rob / two jobs / put some aside / broke in / beat my mother up / bought a gun / fill out a few simple forms / weapon / underneath the pillow / middle-class liberals / take for granted / great equaliser / helpless victims / terrorise / physically stronger / crying inside / daily lives / not a shot was ever fired / slept better / statistics / long run / surviving one night at a time

为什么这样写：Kay 的"Englewood"是芝加哥的贫困社区。"crack epidemic"是毒品危机。"great equaliser"是伟大的平衡器——枪支让弱者变强。"surviving one night at a time"是生存——每晚活下去。

读者视角提示：Kay 的"middle-class liberals"是批评——她批评 Oz 这样的中产阶级自由派不懂穷人的苦难。

> **原句 12:** "In silence Bob raised his glass to Kay, but she shook her head, she had to drive and she was pretty sure she was already skirting the limit."

读者视角提示：Kay 的"shook her head"是拒绝——她不喝了。

为什么这样写：Oz 的"unsteady"是醉酒——他站不稳。"clear my head"是清醒——他想清醒。

> **原句 15:** "Maybe take a last drink there. Maybe. A drink and someone to talk to. Liza. God knows why he kept thinking about a woman with a limp who showed no interest in him and gave him nothing but sarcastic comments. Was this the level he had sunk to? On the other hand, there was something about the way she combined an acute bullshit detector with black humour, and what he suspected was a warm heart. Of course, he could have been mistaken. But he wanted to know. Not that she needed a guy like him, she knew that well enough, she'd told him straight out. Maybe it was this simple, that you start wanting someone you don't really want once you see she doesn't want you. Like two losers underbidding each other until one ends up a winner."
