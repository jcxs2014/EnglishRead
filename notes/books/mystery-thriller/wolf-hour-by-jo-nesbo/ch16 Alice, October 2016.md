# 16. Alice, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Kay Myers 与装修工交谈，目睹 Oz 被 Walker 停职；Oz 取回被扣押的车，拜访 Rooble 询问 Gomez 案件；Alice 在心理诊所看到 Bob 的车，回忆他们的过去。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷——Oz 被正式停职，失去了工作和尊严；Alice 在"放下"与"怀念"之间摇摆。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："停职"——Oz 的职业生涯跌至谷底；"平行叙事"——Oz 和 Alice 在同一城市各自思考对方。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"愤怒"变为"空洞"——他甚至不再愤怒，只是"falling into the abyss"；Alice 从"决心放下"变为"不确定"——她开始怀疑自己的决定。
- **叙事手法**：多视角——Kay/Oz/Alice 三段式，最后一段是 Alice 的回忆。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Kay Myers stood in the doorway of an office that was being decorated. In her hand she was holding a coffee mug with ILove CHICAGO written on it. She watched the man painting the ceiling. He reminded her of a crime scene technician, masked and dressed in white."

关键词：doorway / decorated / coffee mug / ILove CHICAGO / painting / crime scene technician / masked / dressed in white

为什么这样写：Kay 的"ILove CHICAGO"暗示她的过去（在芝加哥长大）。"masked and dressed in white"的装修工让她想到犯罪 scene technician——这是 Nesbø 的幽默。

读者视角提示：Kay 的观察总是从"犯罪"角度——她是一个侦探，即使在日常生活中。

> **原句 2:** "‘Tony Stärk has reported you for attacking him,’ said Walker as he stood by the window with his back to Bob. Bob had worked out that this habit must have developed because the chief felt more comfortable talking to the view, or maybe his own reflection, than he did face-to-face with his own subordinates."

关键词：reported / attacking / stood by the window / back to Bob / habit / more comfortable / talking to the view / face-to-face / subordinates

为什么这样写：Walker 的"back to Bob"是回避——他不想看到 Oz 的反应。"talking to the view"暗示 Walker 更喜欢"景色"而非"人"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"worked out"是讽刺——他分析 Walker 的习惯，就像分析嫌疑人。

> **原句 3:** "‘Bullshit,’ said Bob. ‘It was self-defence. He was the one who attacked me. Take a look at me, chief.’"

关键词：Bullshit / self-defence / attacked / take a look

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Bullshit"是直接的反驳——他不接受指控。"take a look at me"是证据——他头上的伤是证据。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"self-defence"是真实的，但也是他设计的——他故意激怒 Tony 先动手。

> **原句 4:** "‘The lawyer claims that the fact that it took three of your colleagues to pull you off him is proof enough of your use of excessive force. I've got statements from Olav Hanson and the others, and they confirm the lawyer's account of what happened. I'm sorry, Bob, but I'm going to have to suspend you while this matter is thoroughly investigated.’"

关键词：lawyer / three of your colleagues / pull you off / excessive force / statements / Olav Hanson / confirm / suspend / thoroughly investigated

为什么这样写：Walker 的"three of your colleagues"是证据——Oz 的暴力程度需要三个人才能制止。"suspend"是正式的停职——Oz 失去了工作。

读者视角提示：Walker 的"sorry"是真诚的——他不想这样做，但他必须。

> **原句 5:** "Bob opened his mouth and then closed it again. Wondered how things could have worked out any differently. If things could have worked out any differently. And even if he actually would have wanted them to. When you start falling into the abyss you might as well enjoy the free fall as best you can."

关键词：opened his mouth / closed it / worked out / abyss / free fall / enjoy

为什么这样写：Oz 的"abyss"是比喻——他正在坠入深渊。"enjoy the free fall"是放弃——他不再抵抗。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"wondered"是反思——他知道这是他的错，但他也想知道是否有可能避免。

> **原句 6:** "He stuck his hand into the inside pocket of his cashmere coat and placed his ID card on the chief's desk. Followed by the car keys. ‘You have a car of your own, right?’ Walker sounded troubled. ‘A Volvo?’ ‘Correct,’ said Bob. ‘But I don't have a gun, it's –’ ‘I know that.’ Walker's voice was a little shaky. ‘I know that when something like that happens it can make a father hate his own service weapon.’"

关键词：stuck his hand / inside pocket / cashmere coat / ID card / car keys / troubled / Volvo / shaky / father / hate / service weapon

为什么这样写：Oz 的"cashmere coat"是标志——即使被停职，他仍然穿着他的标志性外套。"father"暗示 Oz 可能有孩子（或者 Walker 在暗示什么）。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"shaky"是 Walker 的罕见时刻——他几乎要哭了。

> **原句 7:** "‘That business with the gun,’ said Walker, and had to clear his throat again. ‘A thing like that can destroy a relationship. It happens time after time. It isn't anyone's fault, it's just the way we are as human beings. But you just have to accept it and move on.’"

关键词：business with the gun / clear his throat / destroy a relationship / time after time / anyone's fault / human beings / accept it / move on

为什么这样写：Walker 的"business with the gun"是暗示——Oz 曾经用枪威胁 Alice（或者 Alice 用枪威胁 Oz）。"destroy a relationship"是主题——枪支暴力不仅伤害身体，也伤害关系。

读者视角提示：Walker 的"move on"是 Oz 做不到的事——他无法"放下"。

> **原句 8:** "‘What are we talking about now, chief?’ Walker's features and his body, the features and body of a man Bob respected, some days you would even say liked, seemed to be changing before Bob's eyes into something reptilian and repulsive, the kind of thing that should be beaten to death with a stick. ‘Alice,’ said the reptile."

关键词：features / body / respected / liked / reptilian / repulsive / beaten to death / Alice

为什么这样写：Oz 的"reptilian and repulsive"是愤怒的投射——他把 Walker 视为"敌人"。"Alice"是 Walker 说出的名字——Oz 的愤怒对象。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"beaten to death with a stick"是暴力幻想——他想象杀死 Walker。

> **原句 9:** "He opened the bottom drawer of his desk and took out an old ID card he had reported as lost to the MDP, only to get a phone call two weeks later from a brunette in Near North who told him he'd left it behind in her apartment after using it to cut cocaine. She'd returned it to him in the post, and he'd hung on to it without telling anyone, on the principle that you never know."

关键词：bottom drawer / old ID card / reported as lost / brunette / Near North / left it behind / cut cocaine / returned it / hung on to / principle / you never know

为什么这样写：Oz 的"old ID card"是备用——他保留了旧 ID。"cut cocaine"暗示他的"One-Night Bob"过去——他用身份证切可卡因。"you never know"是 Oz 的哲学——你永远不知道什么时候会需要。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"brunette in Near North"是随意的提及——他有很多女人，他已经记不清了。

> **原句 10:** "Bob took a last look at his place of work. Was there anything else here he might be needing? His gaze took in the notes pinned to his desk divider. Nothing. Absolutely nothing. He hurried back to the elevators, changed his mind, retraced his steps and pulled out the pin holding the fixture list for the Vikings."

关键词：last look / place of work / needing / notes / pinned / desk divider / nothing / absolutely nothing / retraced / fixture list / Vikings

为什么这样写：Oz 的"nothing. Absolutely nothing."是绝望——他对工作没有留恋。"fixture list for the Vikings"是他唯一带走的东西——足球是他的最后寄托。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"retraced his steps"是犹豫——他回来拿赛程表，暗示他对"正常生活"的最后牵挂。

> **原句 11:** "He reached the elevators just in time to see the doors sliding shut. He felt a strange impulse to laugh as he slowly trudged down the stairs."

关键词：elevators / doors sliding shut / strange impulse / laugh / trudged / stairs

为什么这样写：Oz 的"strange impulse to laugh"是荒诞——他被停职了，但电梯门关了让他想笑。"trudged down the stairs"是缓慢的——他走下楼梯，象征"下降"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"laugh"是悲喜交加——他的人生正在崩塌，但他还能笑。

> **原句 12:** "Exiting into the square in front of city hall he stopped, breathed in deeply, closed his eyes and summed things up. He was a man with no woman, no job and no car. In other words, he was finished."

关键词：exiting / square / city hall / stopped / breathed in / closed his eyes / summed things up / no woman / no job / no car / finished

为什么这样写：Oz 的"summing up"是清算——他总结自己的损失。"finished"是绝望——他已经完了。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"breathed in deeply"是最后的冷静——他试图接受现实。

> **原句 13:** "The Minneapolis communal impound yard was located at the roughest end of Colfax Avenue, with scrap-metal dealers and used-car sellers as its neighbours. Stella Cibulkova sat in the booth and checked the ID the man in the orange coat had just shown her."

关键词：communal impound yard / roughest end / Colfax Avenue / scrap-metal dealers / used-car sellers / neighbours / Stella Cibulkova / booth / checked / orange coat

为什么这样写：Oz 的"orange coat"是标志——他仍然穿着他的芥末黄/橙色外套。"scrap-metal dealers"暗示 Oz 的车也是"废品"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"Stella Cibulkova"是随意的提及——他不认识她，但他记得她的名字（因为名牌）。

> **原句 14:** "‘You are aware that there is 2,300 dollars owing on this vehicle, Mr Oz?’ ‘I confess I didn't realise it was quite so much.’"

关键词：aware / 2,300 dollars / owing / vehicle / confess / realise / quite so much

为什么这样写：Oz 的"2,300 dollars"是巨额——他需要支付停车费和罚款。"confess"是幽默——他在"坦白"。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"quite so much"是轻描淡写——他对金钱无所谓。

> **原句 15:** "He peeled off the fifty-dollar bills and laid them down he counted them off in a loud, steady voice. There was something about his voice, a wounded sensitivity that made her feel as though it was the last of his money he was laying down in front of her."

关键词：peeled off / fifty-dollar bills / laid them down / counted / loud, steady voice / wounded sensitivity / last of his money

为什么这样写：Oz 的"wounded sensitivity"是罕见的——他通常是强硬的，但此刻他是脆弱的。"last of his money"暗示他正在花光所有钱。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"loud, steady voice"是表演——他在假装坚强。

> **原句 16:** "Bob took a sip of coffee. He had a car and a roof over his head, what more could a man need? Why does a man need a job when he doesn't have a family to look after? He folded the newspaper and put it down on the passenger seat."

关键词：sip / coffee / car / roof / need / job / family / folded / newspaper / passenger seat

为什么这样写：Oz 的"car and roof"是基本需求——他已经没有更多需要。"family"是缺失——他没有家人。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"what more could a man need?"是自我安慰——他试图说服自己"够了"。

> **原句 17:** "He could easily have picked up a Star Tribune somewhere else besides the paper's headquarters, but it was here he had come. He looked across to the far side of the little central park. The sun sparkled on the glass facade of the building housing Alice's psychology collective."

关键词：picked up / Star Tribune / headquarters / far side / central park / sun sparkled / glass facade / Alice / psychology collective

为什么这样写：Oz 的"psychology collective"是 Alice 的工作——他来这里是因为他想看她。"sun sparkled"是美的——但他看到的是痛苦的来源。

读者视角提示：Oz 的"easily have picked up"是谎言——他故意来这里，因为他想看 Alice。

> **原句 18:** "She noticed that unconsciously she had begun to smile. But then she recalled the message he had left on her machine yesterday, about how he was reneging on their agreement about the house, and she stopped smiling. The estimate they'd been given on the house was so high that they both knew Bob couldn't afford to buy her out, so they'd agreed that she was to get the house at market price while he got the car free of all debt."

关键词：unconsciously / smile / recalled / message / reneging / agreement / house / estimate / afford / buy her out / market price / car free of all debt

为什么这样写：Alice 的"unconsciously"是关键——她不知道自己还在乎。"reneging on their agreement"是 Oz 的"背叛"——他反悔了。

读者视角提示：Alice 的"smile"是身体反应——她仍然爱 Oz，尽管她不想。

> **原句 19:** "The car on the far side of the park glided out into the traffic. Alice looked at her watch. Next patient in five minutes. She sighed, took a last sip from her cup and went back to the office."

关键词：glided out / traffic / watch / next patient / five minutes / sighed / last sip / cup / office

为什么这样写：Alice 的"sighed"是无奈——她回到工作，但她的心不在焉。"last sip"是结束——她喝完茶，回到现实。

读者视角提示：Alice 的"five minutes"是时间压力——她没有时间思考 Oz。
