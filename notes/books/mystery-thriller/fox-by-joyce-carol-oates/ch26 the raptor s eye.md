# 26. THE RAPTOR'S EYE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**: Fox 对 Eunice（唯一不被他"魅力"征服的学生）展开"精神虐待"——他给她低分、指控她抄袭（"NO GRADE"）、用"间歇强化"（Skinner 行为主义）操纵她的情绪，最终"征服"了她——Eunice 从一个"憎恨 Fox"的女孩变成一个"崇拜 Fox"的女孩。
- **悬疑度**: 本章是全书最令人不安的"心理操纵"场景——Fox 用"教学"作为"虐待"的工具，读者目睹一个聪明女孩被一步步"摧毁"。
- **叙事手法**: 第三人称有限视角（Fox），大量内心独白——"Squash the smug little cunt like a scorpion"（像踩蝎子一样踩扁这个得意的贱人）揭示了他的"虐待狂"。
- **结构功能**: 本章解释了 Eunice 的"转变"——她最初"憎恨" Fox（ch21 的"蟑螂"），但 Fox 用"低分+重写+间歇强化"把她"训练"成"崇拜者"。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** The raptor's eye is not exclusively for prey. The raptor's eye is as keenly alert to adversaries in the field.

**中文理解**: 猛禽的眼睛不仅仅用于猎物。猛禽的眼睛对战场上的对手同样警觉。

**关键词**: raptor's eye / prey / adversaries

**为什么这样写**: "raptor"（猛禽）是 Fox 的"自我认同"——他是"捕食者"。"adversaries"（对手）是 Eunice——她是唯一"反抗"他的"猎物"。"raptor's eye" 是 Fox 的"警觉"——他不仅"猎杀"（Genevieve），也"警惕"（Eunice）。

**读者视角提示**: "raptor" 是 Fox 的"本质"——他是"猛禽"，学生是他的"猎物"或"对手"。

> **原句 2:** What is wrong with her, why doesn't she like me? How can she not like—"Mr. Fox"?

**中文理解**: 她怎么了，为什么她不喜欢我？她怎么可能不喜欢——"Mr. Fox"？

**关键词**: what is wrong with her / why doesn't she like me / how can she not like

**为什么这样写**: "Why doesn't she like me?" 是 Fox 的"自恋"——他无法接受"有人不喜欢他"。"How can she not like—'Mr. Fox'?" 是 Fox 的"核心脆弱"——他的"身份"（Mr. Fox）依赖于"被喜欢"。

**读者视角提示**: "How can she not like Mr. Fox?" 是 Fox 的"自恋创伤"——他需要每个人都"爱"他。

> **原句 3:** Students always smiled, laughed at Mr. Fox's jokes. Students always raised their hands eagerly in response to a provocative question posed by Mr. Fox. Yet, there sat the ferret-faced girl unimpressed and unmoved with arms folded tightly across her narrow chest suggestive of the flat segmented body of a scorpion poised to sting if one draws too near.

**中文理解**: 学生们总是微笑，对 Mr. Fox 的笑话大笑。学生们总是急切地举手回应 Mr. Fox 提出的挑衅性问题。然而，那个黄鼠狼脸的女孩坐在那里，不为所动，双臂紧紧交叉在狭窄的胸前，让人联想到蝎子扁平分节的身体，随时准备在有人靠近时蜇人。

**关键词**: ferret-faced / unimpressed and unmoved / scorpion / poised to sting

**为什么这样写**: "scorpion"（蝎子）是 Fox 对 Eunice 的"描述"——他认为她是"危险"的（会"蜇"他）。"poised to sting"（随时准备蜇人）是 Eunice 的"防御"——她用"冷漠"保护自己。

**读者视角提示**: "scorpion" 是 Fox 的"妖魔化"——他把 Eunice 的"不服从"视为"威胁"。

> **原句 4:** "Jesus! I hate her." / In a calmer mood reasoning: not all his students are required to adore Francis Fox. He is not a vain person, he is sure. He does not expect adulation. / But he does expect respect, and it is most unusual, unnatural, pathological when a student is rebellious for no reason.

**中文理解**: "天哪！我恨她。"/ 在冷静的情绪中推理：不是所有学生都必须崇拜 Francis Fox。他不是虚荣的人，他确定。他不期望奉承。/ 但他确实期望尊重，当一个学生无缘无故地叛逆时，这是最不寻常、最不自然、最病态的。

**关键词**: hate her / not a vain person / expect respect / rebellious / pathological

**为什么这样写**: "Jesus! I hate her" 是 Fox 的"真实情感"——他"恨" Eunice。"pathological"（病态的）是 Fox 对 Eunice 的"定性"——他把她的"不服从"称为"病态"，而不是"合理"。

**读者视角提示**: "pathological" 是 Fox 的"倒置"——真正"病态"的是 Fox，不是 Eunice。

> **原句 5:** Squash the smug little cunt like a scorpion.

**中文理解**: 像踩蝎子一样踩扁这个得意的贱人。

**关键词**: squash / smug / cunt / scorpion

**为什么这样写**: "cunt" 是 Fox 对 Eunice 的"称呼"——他的"厌女"再次显现。"squash"（踩扁）是 Fox 的"暴力幻想"——他想"摧毁" Eunice。

**读者视角提示**: "squash" 是 Fox 的"虐待狂"——他享受"摧毁"不服从者的"权力"。

> **原句 6:** Banal ideas & unimaginative writing lacking focus, insight. Might have been written by a computer & seems hastily written even for a computer. Try again?

**中文理解**: 平庸的想法和缺乏想象力、焦点、洞察力的写作。可能是电脑写的，甚至对电脑来说也像是匆忙写成的。再试一次？

**关键词**: banal / unimaginative / lacking focus / hastily written / Try again

**为什么这样写**: 这是 Fox 给 Eunice 的"评语"——但他知道她的文章是"优秀的"（"could as easily have given her an A"）。"Try again?" 是 Fox 的"操纵"——他用"重写"让 Eunice "努力"，同时"控制"她的"情绪"。

**读者视角提示**: "Try again" 是 Fox 的"武器"——他让 Eunice "努力"，但永远不让她"成功"。

> **原句 7:** It is Mr. Fox's general strategy to grade low at the start of the term, and gradually raise grades as the students' writing "improves." Which usually it does, since students will learn to provide what their teacher requires.

**中文理解**: Mr. Fox 的一般策略是在学期开始时给低分，然后随着学生写作"进步"逐渐提高分数。通常确实如此，因为学生会学会提供老师要求的东西。

**关键词**: general strategy / grade low / gradually raise / provide what their teacher requires

**为什么这样写**: "general strategy"（一般策略）是 Fox 的"教学策略"——但这也是他的"操纵策略"：他先给低分，再"提高"，让学生"感恩"。"provide what their teacher requires"（提供老师要求的东西）是 Fox 的"目的"——他训练学生"取悦"他。

**读者视角提示**: "strategy" 是 Fox 的"行为主义"——他把"教学"变成"训练"。

> **原句 8:** Following the Skinnerian principle of intermittent reinforcement, in which an experimental subject is rewarded for their effort not continuously, or predictably, but intermittently, or unpredictably, Fox will grade Eunice Pfenning in a way designed to shatter her defenses: it will be impossible for her not to feel relief, gratitude, some measure of happiness when her grade improves, thus she will be conditioned to seek a higher grade; she will therefore experience disappointment, hurt, helplessness, and despair when her grade is lower than it was previously.

**中文理解**: 遵循 Skinner 的间歇强化原则——实验对象不是持续或可预测地，而是间歇或不可预测地因其努力而获得奖励——Fox 将以一种旨在粉碎 Eunice Pfenning 防御的方式给她打分：当她的成绩提高时，她不可能不感到宽慰、感激、某种程度的幸福，因此她将被条件反射去追求更高的分数；因此，当她的成绩比以前低时，她将经历失望、伤害、无助和绝望。

**关键词**: Skinnerian / intermittent reinforcement / conditioned / shatter her defenses / learned helplessness

**为什么这样写**: "intermittent reinforcement"（间歇强化）是斯金纳的行为主义术语——"间歇性奖励"是最有效的"条件反射"方式（赌博机原理）。Fox 用"间歇强化"操纵 Eunice 的"情绪"：她永远不知道"下一次是 B 还是 C–"，所以她永远"努力"。"learned helplessness"（习得性无助）是 Fox 的"目标"——他要让 Eunice "放弃抵抗"。

**读者视角提示**: "Skinnerian principle" 是全书 Fox 的"操纵手册"——他把自己视为"行为科学家"，把 Eunice 视为"实验对象"（老鼠/鸽子）。

> **原句 9:** "It's a promising paper, Eunice. There is at least one good idea here. But I have to ask you, frankly: Is this your work? Or is it copied from the Internet?" / "I've shown this to some of my colleagues. They seriously doubt that this sort of poetic exegesis could be written by an eighth grader."

**中文理解**: "这是一篇有前途的论文，Eunice。这里至少有一个好主意。但我必须坦率地问你：这是你的作品吗？还是从互联网上抄来的？"/ "我已经给一些同事看过了。他们严重怀疑这种诗歌诠释是一个八年级学生写的。"

**关键词**: promising paper / copied from the Internet / colleagues doubt / poetic exegesis

**为什么这样写**: "Is this your work? Or is it copied from the Internet?" 是 Fox 的"诬陷"——他指控 Eunice "抄袭"，因为他知道她的文章是"优秀的"（"could as easily have given her an A"）。"They seriously doubt" 是 Fox 的"虚构"——他没有给同事看，他只是在"吓唬"她。

**读者视角提示**: "copied from the Internet" 是 Fox 的"权力滥用"——他利用"荣誉守则"（honor code）"诬陷" Eunice。

> **原句 10:** At this point, Eunice astonishes Francis Fox by mumbling that she has already read the story. She has been "reading ahead" in the anthology. / "Well! Good for you, Eunice. But it might be more prudent, you know, to concentrate on your present work. To improve your weaknesses."

**中文理解**: 此时，Eunice 咕哝着说她已读过这个故事，这让 Francis Fox 很惊讶。她一直在"提前阅读"选集。/ "嗯！不错，Eunice。但你知道，更明智的做法可能是专注于你目前的工作。改进你的弱点。"

**关键词**: reading ahead / good for you / more prudent / weaknesses

**为什么这样写**: "Good for you, Eunice" 是 Fox 的"表扬"——但"more prudent to concentrate on your present work" 是他的"打压"——他"表扬"后又"打压"，让 Eunice "困惑"。"weaknesses"（弱点）是 Fox 的"贬低"——他让 Eunice 相信她"有弱点"。

**读者视角提示**: "more prudent" 是 Fox 的"操纵"——他用"明智的建议"包装"打压"。

> **原句 11:** "Now, Eunice, this is the grade that I would have assigned if I were totally convinced that this work is your own, but following the directives of my conscience I cannot assign"—Francis writes "B" on a notepad, and shows it to Eunice, who stares at it as a ravenous creature might stare at a morsel of food, in silence; then, he tears out the note, crumples it, and drops it into his wastebasket.

**中文理解**: "现在，Eunice，如果我能完全确信这是你自己的作品，我会给你这个分数，但遵循我良心的指示，我不能给"——Francis 在便签上写下"B"，给 Eunice 看，她盯着它，像一个贪婪的生物盯着一点食物，沉默着；然后，他撕下纸条，揉成一团，扔进废纸篓。

**关键词**: conscience / ravenous creature / morsel of food / crumples / wastebasket

**为什么这样写**: "ravenous creature might stare at a morsel of food"（贪婪的生物盯着一点食物）是 Eunice 的"状态"——她被"B"（高分）"诱惑"。"crumples it, and drops it into his wastebasket"（揉成一团扔进废纸篓）是 Fox 的"虐待"——他"展示"了"B"，然后"摧毁"它，让 Eunice "绝望"。

**读者视角提示**: "ravenous creature" 是 Fox 对 Eunice 的"物化"——她把 Eunice 视为"饥饿的动物"，用"食物"（分数）"控制"她。

> **原句 12:** It is touching, Eunice Pfenning is terrified of reading aloud to the class of students whom (it seems evident) she disdains; she is totally unprepared for Francis Fox to declare, when she has finished, "Brilliant, Eunice!"—leading the applause from Eunice's classmates. / A blinding light has been shone in Eunice's stunned face, she is too dazed even to smile.

**中文理解**: 很感人，Eunice Pfenning 害怕向全班朗读——她（显然）鄙视这些同学；她完全没有准备好 Francis Fox 在她读完时宣布"太棒了，Eunice！"——带头为 Eunice 的同学鼓掌。/ 一道耀眼的光照在 Eunice 惊呆的脸上，她太茫然了，甚至无法微笑。

**关键词**: terrified / Brilliant, Eunice / leading the applause / blinding light / too dazed

**为什么这样写**: "Brilliant, Eunice!" 是 Fox 的"奖励"——他用"表扬"让 Eunice "感恩"。"blinding light"（耀眼的光）是 Eunice 的"体验"——她被 Fox 的"表扬""照亮"，无法"思考"。

**读者视角提示**: "too dazed even to smile" 是 Eunice 的"转变"——她已经完全被 Fox "控制"了。

> **原句 13:** How disappointing it is, then, for both teacher and student, that Eunice's next critique, an analysis of several poems by e. e. cummings, merits only a C–, with a terse commentary in red ink—Promising argument but very average prose. At least it's clear that this is your own work.

**中文理解**: 那么，对老师和学生来说，多么令人失望——Eunice 下一篇评论，对 e. e. 卡明斯几首诗的分析，只值得一个 C–，红笔写着简短的评语——论点有前途，但散文非常平庸。至少清楚这是你自己的作品。

**关键词**: merits only a C– / promising argument / very average prose

**为什么这样写**: "C–" 是 Fox 的"间歇强化"——他在"B"（高潮）后给"C–"（低谷），让 Eunice "绝望"。"At least it's clear that this is your own work" 是 Fox 的"讽刺"——他"承认"这是她的作品，但仍给低分。

**读者视角提示**: "C–" 是 Fox 的"操纵"——他在"B"后给"C–"，让 Eunice "努力"（希望再次得到"B"）。

> **原句 14:** "She's hostile to me because she's a spoiled brat. The kind of smart-ass girl who kills herself in college."

**中文理解**: "她对我有敌意，因为她是个被宠坏的小屁孩。那种在大学里自杀的聪明女孩。"

**关键词**: spoiled brat / smart-ass / kills herself in college

**为什么这样写**: "kills herself in college" 是 Fox 的"诅咒"——他"希望" Eunice "自杀"。"smart-ass"（自以为聪明的）是 Fox 的"贬低"——他无法忍受 Eunice 的"智慧"。

**读者视角提示**: "kills herself in college" 是全书 Fox 最"冷酷"的话——他"希望"他的"猎物"（不服从者）"自杀"。

> **原句 15:** "This is a 'magic journal,' " Francis says solemnly. "You must tell the truth when you write in it, otherwise there will be a curse on you." / "A curse? What kind of curse?" / "It's forbidden to say."

**中文理解**: "这是一本'魔法日记'，"Francis 严肃地说。"你在里面写东西时必须说真话，否则会有一个诅咒落在你身上。"/ "诅咒？什么诅咒？"/ "禁止说。"

**关键词**: magic journal / tell the truth / curse / forbidden to say

**为什么这样写**: "magic journal"（魔法日记）是 Fox 给 Eunice 的"礼物"——但这也是他的"陷阱"（他会在周五"检查"她的日记）。"curse"（诅咒）是 Fox 的"威胁"——他让 Eunice "害怕"不"诚实"。"forbidden to say"（禁止说）是 Fox 的"神秘化"——他让"日记"变得"神秘"、"神圣"。

**读者视角提示**: "magic journal" 是 Fox 的"grooming 工具"——他让 Eunice 相信"日记"是"魔法"的，所以她会在里面"诚实"（暴露自己的脆弱）。

> **原句 16:** "Eunice," Francis enunciates the name with exaggerated care, so that it sounds like You nice, "—you are certainly an élite student but your destiny is sheerly hypothetical at this point. It is not yet realized."

**中文理解**: "Eunice，"Francis 夸张地强调这个名字，让它听起来像"You nice"，"—你当然是一个精英学生，但你的命运在这一点上纯粹是假设的。它还没有实现。"

**关键词**: enunciates / You nice / élite student / destiny / hypothetical

**为什么这样写**: "You nice" 是 Fox 的"文字游戏"——他把 Eunice 的名字"扭曲"为"You nice"（你很好），让她的名字变得"美丽"（"My name in his mouth became beautiful as music"——ch01 的 Prologue）。"destiny is sheerly hypothetical"（命运纯粹是假设的）是 Fox 的"神秘化"——他让 Eunice 相信她的"命运"掌握在他手中。

**读者视角提示**: "You nice" 是 Fox 的"改名"——他把 Eunice 的名字"重新命名"为"You nice"，让她的"身份"依赖于他的"认可"。

> **原句 17:** Now why did you do that, asshole? / For the hell of it, that's why. / Because I could, that's why. / Like training a dog, just as easy.

**中文理解**: 现在你为什么那样做，混蛋？/ 为了好玩，这就是原因。/ 因为我能，这就是原因。/ 就像训练一条狗，一样容易。

**关键词**: asshole / for the hell of it / because I could / training a dog

**为什么这样写**: "Because I could"（因为我能）是 Fox 的"动机"——他"给 Eunice 日记"没有"理由"，除了"证明他的权力"。"Like training a dog, just as easy"（就像训练一条狗，一样容易）是 Fox 的"自我认知"——他把 Eunice 视为"狗"，把他自己视为"驯兽师"。

**读者视角提示**: "Like training a dog" 是全书 Fox 的"虐待"——他把"教学"视为"训狗"。

> **原句 18:** He isn't worried. He will be rid of the ferret-faced girl whenever he wishes, rid of any of them, all of them, Little Kitten included, whenever he wishes. He is Mr. Fox. / Snap of his fingers, easy as pricking a bubble, press delete, he's the puppet-master.

**中文理解**: 他不担心。他可以在任何时候摆脱那个黄鼠狼脸的女孩，摆脱他们中的任何一个，所有他们，包括小猫咪，只要他愿意。他是 Mr. Fox。/ 打个响指，像戳破一个泡泡一样容易，按删除键，他是木偶大师。

**关键词**: rid of / whenever he wishes / Mr. Fox / puppet-master / press delete

**为什么这样写**: "press delete"（按删除键）是 Fox 的"控制"——他认为自己可以"删除"任何人。"puppet-master"（木偶大师）是 Fox 的"身份"——他把自己视为"控制者"。

**读者视角提示**: "press delete" 是 Fox 的"无情"——他认为"摆脱"任何"猎物"都是"容易"的。

> **原句 19:** The puppet-master smiles benignly. A puppet is always grateful to merely exist.

**中文理解**: 木偶大师慈祥地微笑。木偶总是仅仅因为存在而感激。

**关键词**: puppet-master / benignly / grateful to merely exist

**为什么这样写**: "A puppet is always grateful to merely exist"（木偶总是仅仅因为存在而感激）是 Fox 的"哲学"——他认为"猎物"应该"感激"他的"控制"。这是"自恋"的终极表达。

**读者视角提示**: "grateful to merely exist" 是 Fox 的"权力"——他需要"猎物"感激他，以确认自己的"存在"。

## 一句话总结

Fox 用"行为主义"（Skinner 的间歇强化）"训练" Eunice——他先给低分，再"提高"，再"降低"，让她永远"努力"；他指控她"抄袭"，给她"NO GRADE"，然后"慷慨"地"重写"；最终，Eunice 从"憎恨 Fox"变成"崇拜 Fox"——"A puppet is always grateful to merely exist"。
