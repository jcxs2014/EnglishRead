# 《Lies on the Serpent's Tongue》概述

## 全书梗概

**Caball Hollow 是一座被森林抱紧的小镇，而 James 家的女人是镇上两百年的心结。** For as long as anyone can remember, we James women have been born with certain talents. Sorrel, my older sister, can charm bees to do her bidding, making honey with special properties that enhance any spell. Linden, younger than me by only eleven months, can taste the emotions of others. And my youngest sister, Juniper, sees beyond this world to the next.（ch01） 四姐妹各有天赋：
- Sorrel 令蜂听差、Linden 尝得出他人情绪、Juniper 看得见彼岸；而叙述者 Rowan 能嗅出谎言——每个人的谎都有自己的配方，Linden 是焦味，Vernie 是硫磺火柴，Sonny 是烧橡胶。I know the words are false, but no stench accompanies them. He shows none of the outward signs of dishonesty I’ve learned to identify over the years. No shifts in the tone or volume of his voice, no fidgeting, no changes in the level of eye contact. And yet I know he’s lying, so how can that be?（ch01）
- 第一章结尾，邮局退休员 Buck 当众否认拿走 Hillard 的祖传怀表，她明知是谎，却第一次什么也没闻到。And I have to admit that maybe the only thing worse than knowing every single lie is not knowing.（ch01）

**仪器失灵的谜底在十天后揭开：
- 偷走的不是真话，是记忆本身。** People aren’t just misplacing objects, they’re losing the memories connected to them. That’s why I couldn’t detect any lies. Buck and Mama believed they were telling the truth. A chill slithers up my spine and digs its fangs into the back of my neck.（ch10）
- 连她母亲 Odette 都弄丢了天鹅坠锁，也弄丢了那段定情故事——「We built this entire life that I can't remember」（ch23）
- 。而她与全家很快会知道，「丢」这个字太轻了：It’s the essence of who we are as people: every memory, every hope, every dream, everything. It’s the part of us that remains beyond our physical form. And the pieces aren’t being lost, they’re being stolen.（ch13）
活着的人像褪色的照片，死者却因魂不完整而永远过不了界——她的母亲正走在那条路上。

**与此同时，山把「不该在此」的东西一样样送来。** There are no magpies in West Virginia. But the sound of my voice is enough to startle the bird from its perch. It takes flight again, and I watch until I can barely make out the dark smudge against the bright sky. Maybe it’s possible that the bird was blown off course by some distant storm and ended up in these mountains by mistake, but there’s something about the sight of it that leaves me uneasy.（ch04） 一条欧洲蝰蛇、一声凌晨的怪笑、七只栖在骨树枝头的喜鹊——数鹊谣唱到第七格：
- 「seven for the devil, his own self」（ch06）
- 。这一切都指向森林深处的 Bone Tree：But it wasn’t until this summer that my sisters and I discovered its true purpose. It wasn’t a naturally occurring place of power, but a forcefully made doorway between this world and the one beyond. One born of desperation by the founders of Caball Hollow, who had feared death in a land far from home. And now there might be something wrong with it.（ch06）
两百年前，Caball Hollow 的开创者怕死在异乡，把一个「美得出奇」的换生灵男孩活埋在树根下，用他的命造出了连通彼世的门。

**那个男孩回来了。** 他如今叫 Hadrian Fitch，是彼界的守卫之一——蛾翼人，护送横死者走完阴阳之路；Not a monster, but one of the graveyard watch, the Moth-Winged Man is among the ranks of those who had their lives cut unfairly short and are now responsible for protecting souls who have met a tragic end on the road between death and the hereafter. It was folklore the Scots-Irish immigrants who settled here brought with them, this belief in the good folk. And like the Otherworld, it is all too real.（ch08） 十九年前四岁失踪的 Elam McCoy 就是他。他带着一身伤回到 Rowan 的门垫上，因为她能辨谎，而有人正在猎杀他。血誓之后，两人结成查案同盟：
- “Linden offered you your life back, no more summons from James witches whenever they wanted to make a deal with the devil.” I press a finger against his chest to make my point. “So keep the pages about the Moth-Winged Man. The deal stays intact. No future James will ever know about you, but we get the rest of the book back. And in return, I’ll be your human lie detector.”（ch10）
她用鼻子替他验谎，他交还巫典 diablerie——书里写着门的来历，也写着门的死期。

**调查一层层剥开镇子的原罪。** 车祸死的不是盗参人而是猎蛾人 Lucien Ballard；毁掉 Harvest Moon 的血书 WITCHES BURN 背后是助教带头的少年团伙；护林员 Harshbarger 用 Sonny Vane 的猎怪节目做掩护走私人参，杀了想取证的女招待，又在烧除日杀死了最接近真相的 Vernie。In the moonlight, there is a softness to her face, an emptiness, like a shirt cast aside on a chair, still warm from the brush of skin. It’s so different from the way Vernie usually looks, so alive and animated, that I almost can’t recognize her. The most real part of her is gone.（ch27） 而偷魂的真凶是彼界僭主 Ciaran Nimh——「the Venomous Dark」——他用偷来的魂片一片片修补那扇他抢来的门。

**Rowan 的家在危机里重新长出枝叶。** 姑婆 Zephyrine 没有死：
- 她被困彼界，化猫护佑娘家；她的女儿 Ona——Rowan 的表姐——偷书撬骨，砸裂了门，也砸开了全家二十年的谜。“No,” she agrees. “I’ve had to keep my claws covered, but I’ve done what I can to protect you from spying eyes and thieving beaks. Now you must do the rest.”（ch15）
- 而 Rowan 自己在烧除日的悬崖上学完了最后一课：Nimh 要用她的血与名重演活埋——「Caorunn means 'rowan'」——Hadrian 从门里扑出，跳崖接住她，两对蛾翼在半空张开。Extending another foot above Hadrian's head, his wings are a beautiful burst of colorful patterns, two sets of two, just like a moth. They unfurl wide, catching the air and slowing us mid-fall.（ch28）

**结局是一棵新树。** 他们不再需要活埋任何人：
- 用 Hadrian 现成的骨、两个人的自愿，在河里造出自己的门。Then, right before my eyes, a tree breaks the surface of the water, stretching for the shore. Smaller than the last, but white as bone.（ch28）
- 旧树烧成灰，偷走的魂片物归原主，Nimh 的封口咒随花枯萎。十一月，Vernie 的葬礼上一只鱼鹰展翅而过；Gran 的生日蜡烛吹灭时，前厅传来一声——A clatter from the front room has us all turning to look down the hall when a woman's voice calls out. “Apollonia? I'm home!”（ch29）

**这本书真正在讲的是「信任是练出来的」。** Gran once told me that I was not a sin eater—that the lies of others didn't define me. But that's exactly what I was doing. And I was letting my anger shape the way I saw the world. I spent so much time focused on the lies that I started to expect them even before they were told. Trust is something we have to learn how to do, like anything else, with a little practice.（ch29） 一个能嗅出所有谎言的人，最终学到的是：谎还没出口就开始等待的人，永远听不见真话的样子。鼻子没有变，用法变了——这就是 Rowan Persephone James 的成年礼。

## 三个主题

**一、谎言的气味与信任的练习。** 全书以「闻谎」开局，以「学信」收束：天赋让人看清别人，却也会让人看不见自己。The smell of a lie is a potent thing, burnt around the edges and foul all the way through. Sometimes it’s so strong I imagine it must singe the mouth of the teller like a hot coal. But not even that would stop them. Everybody lies, and that’s the honest truth.（ch01）

**二、交换与代价。** 这家人的每一份安稳都是签来的：Mama 用爱换命、Linden 用书换命、Zephyrine 用自由换女儿——而 Nimh 的账本里，连心跳都可以省略。She kept it locked in a drawer in the summer kitchen. Until Linden made a bargain with the Moth-Winged Man, trading the diablerie to save the life of the person she loves.（ch09）

**三、门与家。** Bone Tree 是怕死异乡的人造出来的门，最后成了一家人各自回家的路——被偷走的记忆、被活埋的王、被困的姑婆，都在最后一声呼唤里落地。There, on the worn-out old welcome mat, like some morbid gift, is a dead magpie, the keys to the cabin trapped beneath its wing. One for sorrow.（ch11）

## 人物弧光

**Rowan Persephone James**：从用刀子嘴筑墙的「肌肉/嘴」，到敢把手指抵上敌人胸口、把血掌拍上门板的当家人。她学会了最难的功课——But I refuse to lie, and I don't know how to get back the piece of her that's missing.（ch23）

**Hadrian Fitch / Elam McCoy**：四岁失踪、死于彼界、成为守卫。他一生都在说谎，直到把蛇纹过户、把真话写进血里；他用一对蛾翼证明，「Where you go, I go」不是情话，是条款。I don’t remember much from that day. But what I do remember is being afraid and very cold and wanting nothing more than to go home to my mother, which was the one thing I could never do again. I know how important the job I do now is, because I would have been lost without those who were there for me then.（ch14）

**Zephyrine James / Ona**：被困彼界的姑婆化猫守了娘家二十年；她的女儿跨界偷书撬骨砸门救母。这对母女用各自的偏执教会 Rowan：家人的执念可以错，但从不缺席。

**Gran（Apollonia）**：守着妹妹的衣柜守了二十年，逼侄女上山、又第一个低头认错。她许了几十年的愿，在生日蜡烛吹灭后应声兑现。
