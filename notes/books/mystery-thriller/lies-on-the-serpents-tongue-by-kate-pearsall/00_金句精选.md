# 《Lies on the Serpent's Tongue》金句精选

① The smell of a lie is a potent thing, burnt around the edges and foul all the way through. Sometimes it’s so strong I imagine it must singe the mouth of the teller like a hot coal. But not even that would stop them. Everybody lies, and that’s the honest truth.（ch01）

**中文**：谎言的气味是种烈性的东西，边缘烧焦、从里到外都是臭的；可就算把它烧到说谎者嘴上，也拦不住人撒谎。
**上下文**：ch01 开篇公理，Rowan 的能力说明书。
**为什么重要**：全书的世界观基石——气味是记录，不是惩罚；它只标记偏离，不裁决善恶。
**呼应关系**：与 Gran once told me that I was not a sin eater—that the lies of others didn't define me. But that's exactly what I was doing. And I was letting my anger shape the way I saw the world. Trust is something we have to learn how to do, like anything else, with a little practice.（ch29）「信任是练出来的」构成首尾：开局凭鼻子，收局靠练习。

**为什么重要**：两百年漂泊的落地：没能回家的孩子、被偷走的记忆、被困的姑婆，都在这声呼唤里回家。
**呼应关系**：Like a cow that moos at midnight or a whip-poor-will singing at noon, if a bird flies into the house, it is a warning that death is coming.（ch09）的鸟入宅预告死亡，末句的人声预告团圆——本书的征兆学以对称收束。
