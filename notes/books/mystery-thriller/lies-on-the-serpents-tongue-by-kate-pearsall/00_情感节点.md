# 《Lies on the Serpent's Tongue》情感节点

## 节点一：闻不到的那句谎（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 镇议会散会很久之后，Rowan 还坐在满屋谎言的余味里——政客的空头支票、邻居的体面粉饰，全都乖乖交出了气味。直到邮局退休员 Buck 当众否认那块祖传怀表，她等着鼻子里起火，等来的却是一片空白。她的世界在第一章的最后一行裂开第一道缝。

**关键引语：**

> ① I know the words are false, but no stench accompanies them. He shows none of the outward signs of dishonesty I’ve learned to identify over the years. No shifts in the tone or volume of his voice, no fidgeting, no changes in the level of eye contact. And yet I know he’s lying, so how can that be?（ch01）

> ② And I have to admit that maybe the only thing worse than knowing every single lie is not knowing.（ch01）

## 节点二：一杯茶里的坠落（ch02）

**叙事概括：** 深夜的厨房，失眠的姐妹分一块苹果黄油面包。Linden 把茶叶渣读给她听：一个人，一直在坠。Rowan 自己也被卷进悬念——Buck 的谎没有味道，而此刻妹妹说「我会没事的」，同样没有。仪器沉默的双重含义，从这一夜开始压在全家人的枕头上。

**关键引语：**

> ① Linden clears her throat before she blinks and looks down into the dregs of her mug. “It’s a person,” she says at last, her eyes wide and shining in the low light when her gaze meets mine. “Falling, and falling, and falling.”（ch02）

> ② I brace for the foul scent of a lie, but it doesn’t come. And in the back of my mind, I can’t help but wonder whether that means she’s telling the truth or my ability is failing me again.（ch02）

## 节点三：餐桌上的两笔旧账（ch02）

**叙事概括：** 早餐桌上，Mama 说出与蛾翼人「一命换一命」的交易，Gran 说出走投无路的妹妹 Zephyrine。两个女人的失去并排摆开：爱错人的被出卖，爱对人的也照样失去。Rowan 第一次看清这个家的经济结构——安稳全是签来的。

**关键引语：**

> ① I would never take back the deal I made to save your sister, but I know why the Moth-Winged Man accepted the trade. A life for a life. I killed something that day. Something I can never get back.（ch02）

> ② It’s one thing to believe there might be more to the world than what’s known, yet quite another to have it confirmed. People turn dangerous when confronted with something that challenges their worldview.（ch02）

## 节点四：叉子、血书与最后的通牒（ch03）

**叙事概括：** 小馆被毁的血书还挂在墙上（WITCHES BURN），Rowan 的怒火先烧到了自家门口——餐叉抵上 Bryson 的喉咙，四个红点没破皮。Gran 一句「第一次失望」和一个志愿者通牒，把她从镇子的战场上拔起来，种进了国家森林。

**关键引语：**

> ① I don’t even remember crossing the distance. Flames fill my belly, and I see red. The next thing I know, the fork from my pocket is pressed against his throat.（ch03）

> ② We call this the James Family Book of Mountain Wisdom, but it wouldn’t exist without community. Because it isn’t just what all those who came before us knew or created, it’s also what was shared with them. Customs and beliefs loaned by other immigrants and the descendants of enslaved people, the uses of native plants from the Cherokee and the Lenape and other Indigenous people who were here long before we showed up.（ch03）

## 节点五：塔上的第一夜与不该来的鹊（ch04-05）

**叙事概括：** 瞭望塔的视野壮阔得让人失重——像在飞，或者在坠。迎新篝火上，Vernie 讲了那个二十年的河边低语故事，Rowan 验过：没有一丝谎。而散伙时 Harshbarger 一句暖心的「这一带没有盗采者」，倒被她的鼻子判了刑。

**关键引语：**

> ① I press against the wooden rail and peer down into the ravine. My stomach drops, and it feels like flying. Or falling.（ch04）

> ② “These mountains are older than oceans, older than trees, older than bone,” Vernie says. “When the Appalachians rose into existence, nothing walked on this earth. They’ve borne witness to a half billion years. Something that old is bound to have secrets. But it’s humans that are the most dangerous thing in this Forest.”（ch05）

> ③ And Harshbarger’s words would have been a lot more comforting if they hadn’t been a lie.（ch05）

## 节点六：七只鹊与门的名字（ch06）

**叙事概括：** 凌晨的怪笑、窗上的轻叩、木堆里一条不属于这个大洲的蛇——异常一夜凑齐。循迹走进家族秘地，Rowan 才知道 Bone Tree 是一扇被强行造出的门，而门前的枝头落着数鹊谣第七格的全部答案：七只，魔鬼亲自到场。

**关键引语：**

> ① But it wasn’t until this summer that my sisters and I discovered its true purpose. It wasn’t a naturally occurring place of power, but a forcefully made doorway between this world and the one beyond. One born of desperation by the founders of Caball Hollow, who had feared death in a land far from home. And now there might be something wrong with it.（ch06）

> ② I back away, the old nursery rhyme echoing in my head: One for sorrow, two for mirth, three for a funeral, four for a birth, five for heaven, six for hell, seven for the devil, his own self.（ch06）

## 节点七：门垫上的归人与人骨里的王（ch07-08、ch13）

**叙事概括：** 暴雨夜，她抄起拨火棍拉开门——血染门垫的是 Hadrian。急救夜补齐两块硬设定：蛾翼人是守卫，Hadrian 是十九年前失踪的男孩。直到她用自己的血逼出真话：Buck 们丢的不是记忆，是魂——而残缺的魂死后永远过不了界。她母亲的名字，悄悄写上了那份名单。

**关键引语：**

> ① Not a monster, but one of the graveyard watch, the Moth-Winged Man is among the ranks of those who had their lives cut unfairly short and are now responsible for protecting souls who have met a tragic end on the road between death and the hereafter. It was folklore the Scots-Irish immigrants who settled here brought with them, this belief in the good folk. And like the Otherworld, it is all too real.（ch08）

> ② It’s the essence of who we are as people: every memory, every hope, every dream, everything. It’s the part of us that remains beyond our physical form. And the pieces aren’t being lost, they’re being stolen.（ch13）

> ③ But once they die, it’s different. A soul can’t cross over unless it’s whole. They’ll be stuck here.（ch13）

## 节点八：彼界的木灰味（ch15）

**叙事概括：** 误入彼界的森林里，她撞见 Hadrian 向僭主汇报自己的家人，又听见姑婆的声音贴着耳根说「我无意伤害你」。这一章她拆穿了自己最想信的谎——空气里漫满柴烟与纸灰。而 Zephyrine 亮出臂上晕影的猫：收着爪子的，才是护她的人。

**关键引语：**

> ① The most dangerous lies are the ones you want to believe. As Hadrian looks at me with eyes begging for understanding, the air fills with the smell of woodsmoke and ashes.（ch15）

> ② “No,” she agrees. “I’ve had to keep my claws covered, but I’ve done what I can to protect you from spying eyes and thieving beaks. Now you must do the rest.”（ch15）

## 节点九：空棺与断崖（ch25、ch28）

**叙事概括：** 万圣夜的铲子挖出两百年前的答案：棺是空的，盖内侧九指抓痕——他们把他活埋了。烧除日的悬崖上，Nimh 松开手，Hadrian 从门里扑出跳下，两对蛾翼在半空张开。坠落与腾空在同一个瞬间完成，这本书最重的两笔账一次结清。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Oh my god.” I realize what they are at the same time she does, and we stare at each other in horror. “They buried him alive.”（ch25）

> ② Extending another foot above Hadrian's head, his wings are a beautiful burst of colorful patterns, two sets of two, just like a moth. They unfurl wide, catching the air and slowing us mid-fall.（ch28）

## 节点十：Apollonia，我回来了（ch29）

**叙事概括：** 葬礼上的鱼鹰、生日会的柿子籽、全镇失而复现的记忆——一切都在归位。Gran 许了几十年的愿望，在蜡烛吹灭后由走廊尽头的一声呼唤兑现。没能回家的孩子、被偷走的心跳、被困二十年的猫，都在这一句里到家了。

**关键引语：**

> ① Gran once told me that I was not a sin eater—that the lies of others didn't define me. But that's exactly what I was doing. And I was letting my anger shape the way I saw the world. I spent so much time focused on the lies that I started to expect them even before they were told. Trust is something we have to learn how to do, like anything else, with a little practice.（ch29）

> ② A clatter from the front room has us all turning to look down the hall when a woman's voice calls out. “Apollonia? I'm home!”（ch29）
