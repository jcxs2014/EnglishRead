# 85. Chapter 80

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：她赶到 Crawfords 家，Wendy 坐在餐桌前、面前合上的笔电——她已看到 Paige 搜索页；「我们信任你，把儿子交给你」，Hattie 求解释，Wendy 说「我不想知道」，关门。
- **情感弧线位置**："我不想知道"是全书对坦白的最残忍回答：她终于愿意说，而被伤害的人选择不要听。
- **线索结构**：灯亮无人应／胃里的坑／合上笔电／空白眼神／昨天的道歉／破碎的声音／信任的控诉／我不想知道。
- **人物弧线**：她从隐瞒者变成恳求者，Wendy 从倾听者变成拒绝者——最亲密的关系在门两侧断裂。
- **叙事手法**：静止对峙（光与黑暗对压）替代动作，最后一句台词与关门声封住八年信任。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The lights are on at the Crawfords', but no one answers when she knocks. The pit in Hattie's stomach deepens the longer she waits. She cups her hands around the door's glass and peers inside but can't see anything beyond the hall and part of the dining room."

**中文理解**：Crawfords 家灯亮着，她敲门却没人应。等待越久，她胃里的坑越深。她把手圈在门玻璃上。
**关键词**：Crawfords' / Hattie's / anything / answers
**为什么这样写**：灯亮却无人应＝全家都在，她的身体先于意识知道出事。
**读者视角提示**：灯亮无人应。

> **原句 2:** "She starts toward the sea-facing side of the house battling rising panic. There's a good chance she didn't even look at the stuff on the table. And if she did, it didn't mean anything to her. Yes, the main thing is she didn't see Tommy. She continues telling herself this until she reaches the back porch and sees something that silences all the assurances."

**中文理解**：她朝房子面海那一侧走，越来越慌。她一路告诉自己有充分理由在这里，直到走到后廊。
**关键词**：assurances / sea-facing / continues / something
**为什么这样写**：「有充分理由在这里」＝她一遍遍给自己许可，而理由越来越站不住。
**读者视角提示**：理由站不住。

> **原句 3:** "Wendy is sitting at the far end of the dining room table in front of a closed laptop. She's staring blankly ahead. Unmoving. Hattie raises a fist and hesitates before rapping lightly on the glass. For a second Wendy remains motionless. Then her head swivels and she stares with the same blankness in Hattie's direction before finally standing and coming to the back door. When she opens it Wendy gazes at her with an emptiness that makes Hattie's insides coil."

**中文理解**：Wendy 坐在餐桌远端，面前是一台合上的笔电。她盯着它。
**关键词**：motionless / blankness / direction / emptiness
**为什么这样写**：**合上的笔电＝Wendy 看到了 Paige 搜索页并主动关上，她不需要再搜。**
**读者视角提示**：**合上的笔电 T1 核。**

> **原句 4:** "They stay that way for a second, neither moving before Wendy says, "I felt bad about yesterday, so I brought leftovers to apologize. I thought I heard a sound inside—a thump—and I started thinking about Sloan and got scared that something happened to you, so I went in." The flatness of her voice is chilling. "No one was there but I noticed the stuff on your table, and it sounded so familiar. So I came home and looked it up and . . ." Wendy falls silent, eyes unfocused, features slack and emotionless. Hattie's never seen her like this before. This still. This cold."

**中文理解**：有那么一秒 Wendy 一动不动。然后她转过头，用同样的空白眼神看着她。
**关键词**：emotionless / apologize / leftovers / something
**为什么这样写**：「空白眼神」＝她在 Julian、Eeling 遗孀、梦里的死人脸上见过的同一张创伤的脸。
**读者视角提示**：空白眼神＝创伤的脸。

> **原句 5:** "She has no idea what to say. How to begin. "I—" she tries, and her voice breaks and dies. They stand that way for a long time, light shining out around Wendy, frigid darkness pressing at her own back."

**中文理解**：「我对昨天的事感到抱歉，我在外面等了一会儿，想跟你聊聊，可你不在——」Hattie 从没见过她这样。这么静止，这么冷。
**关键词**：darkness / pressing / shining / around
**为什么这样写**：Wendy 先道歉＝她宣布自己来过、知道 Hattie 躲过。
**读者视角提示**：Wendy 先道歉。

> **原句 6:** ""We trusted you. Trusted you with him. With our son," Wendy whispers. "And all this time . . ." Tears form in her eyes and spill down her cheeks. "How . . ." She shakes her head and starts to shut the door. "Please. Please let me explain. I don't deserve another second of your time, but please . . . there's so much you don't know." "I don't want to know," Wendy says, and closes the door."

**中文理解**：她不知道说什么、怎么开头。「我——」她试图开口，声音却破碎、消失。他们那样站了很久。「我们信任你。把儿子交给你，」Wendy 低声说。「而这些年来……」「求你，让我解释。」「我不想知道，」Wendy 说，然后关上了门。
**关键词**：whispers / Trusted / another / deserve
**为什么这样写**：**「我们信任你，把儿子交给你」是最重的控诉；「我不想知道」拒绝一切坦白——关门封住八年关系。**
**读者视角提示**：**「我们信任你」「我不想知道」总览金句。**
