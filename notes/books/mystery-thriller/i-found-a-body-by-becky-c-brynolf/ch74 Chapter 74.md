## 精读

> **原句 1:** If Mona were still in the service, this task would have been so far from her jurisdiction that her colleagues north of the border would have handled it. But she's a free agent now, and seemed to have enough clout from the Mark Donovan case that Greater Glasgow police were more than accommodating at such short notice.

**中文理解**：如果莫娜还在服务中，这项任务将远远超出她的管辖边界，边境以北的同事会处理。但她现在是一个自由职业者，而且似乎有多诺万案子的足够影响力，格拉斯哥警方在如此短的通知内非常配合。

**关键词**<td>jurisdiction（管辖）、free agent（自由职业者）、clout（影响力）、accommodating（配合）</td>

**为什么这样写**：Mona 的"自由"——她现在是"自由职业者"。free agent 是精准的短语（"自由职业者"），clout 是精准的词（"影响力"）。

**读者视角提示**：Mona 的"影响力"——她的"过去"给了她"影响力"。

> **原句 2:** 'Can we have tonight?' she whispers. 'Please. I'll come in first thing tomorrow and answer every question,' she says, wiping her cheeks. 'I promise. I knew I would have to eventually. But please. The kids. I just need tonight.'

**中文理解**："我们可以有今晚吗？"她低声说。"请。我明天一早就来回答每个问题，"她说，擦着脸颊。"我承诺。我最终必须来。但是请。孩子们。我只需要今晚。"

**关键词**<td>first thing（一早）、eventually（最终）</td>

**为什么这样写**：Georgia 的"请求"——她请求"今晚"与"孩子们"共度。first thing 是精准的短语（"一早"），eventually 是精准的词（"最终"）。

**读者视角提示**：Georgia 的"母爱"——她"只需要今晚"与"孩子们"共度。

> **原句 3:** A deposit, a significant one, from Ms K. Martin. Mona stares at the number sitting in her bank account, speechless. A laugh bursts out of her without warning, for which she apologises to the police officer waiting in the driver's seat.

**关键词**<td>deposit（存款）、significant（重要的）、speechless（说不出话）</td>

**为什么这样写**：Kylie 的"付款"——她用"Ms K. Martin"（Kylie May 的"真名"）付款。significant 是精准的词（"重要的"），speechless 是精准的词（"说不出话"）。
