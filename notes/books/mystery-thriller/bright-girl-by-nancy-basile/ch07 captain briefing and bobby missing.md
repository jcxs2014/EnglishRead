# 07. Chapter Seven

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Shelby 与 Erickson 向 Captain Guerrero 汇报：时间线（Whitney 6pm 父母 last seen → Skye 接她 → 9:30 文本 after-party at Bobby’s）、Bobby 的“home all night” alibi、无摄像头、手机钱包 purse 缺失； Guerrero 要求 cast the net；章末去 Garza 家，Bobby 不在家，Mrs. Garza 坚称“barking up the wrong tree”，两人决定不发 APB、先去 Del’s。

- **情感弧线位置**：全书的 “case management” 格：从 Shelby/Erickson 的 bilateral 争论推进到 captain-level 的 “objectivity vs. open mind”；章末 Bobby 的“missing”把案件从 “suspect with alibi” 推进到 “suspect on the run?”。

- **线索结构**：章内摆出六样往后要回来对账的东西：时间线 6pm/9:30pm；Bobby “home all night” alibi；Guerrero 的 “pretty white girl murdered in small town WV + football player” 媒体预判；“no cameras pointing at pool”；Mrs. Garza “barking up the wrong tree”；Shelby “probably just at a friend’s house, crying into a glass of Mountain Dew”。

- **人物弧线**：Shelby 从 “reads the scene” 走到 “reads the politics”（媒体压力）；Erickson 从 “sunshine” 走到 “objectivity” 被 captain 命名；Guerrero 作为 “effective leader” 介入；Officer Simon “puppy”；Naomi “yawning” but filing warrant。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限知贴住 Shelby，captain office 对话驱动，结尾 Bobby missing 的 suspense 收束。作者把“media pressure”与“suspect missing”并置。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Whereas the rest of the station smelled like stale coffee, mold, and a hint of wet dog, the captain’s office smelled of lemon polish and her perfume, something sweet and floral.

**中文理解**：警局其他地方闻起来像 stale coffee、mold、a hint of wet dog，而 captain’s office 闻起来像 lemon polish 与 her perfume，something sweet and floral。

**关键词**：stale coffee, mold, and a hint of wet dog／lemon polish and her perfume

**为什么这样写**：作者用“sensory contrast”把 Guerrero 的 office 从 bullpen 区分出来——“lemon polish / perfume” 把 Guerrero 的 “gravitas” 落成 physical texture。这种 “sensory characterization” 是本章的 opening move。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “lemon polish”——这条 “captain’s office” 后文所有 “case management” 对话都要回到这个 setting 上。

---

> **原句 2:** “Whitney was last seen around six p.m. by her parents. At that point, Whitney’s friend Skye Stevens picked her up to go to the football game. At approximately nine-thirty p.m., Mrs. Chandler received a text from Whitney saying that she was going to an after-party at Bobby Garza’s house.”

**中文理解**：Shelby 拉出 timeline：“Whitney was last seen around six p.m. by her parents. At that point, Whitney’s friend Skye Stevens picked her up to go to the football game. At approximately nine-thirty p.m., Mrs. Chandler received a text from Whitney saying that she was going to an after-party at Bobby Garza’s house。”

**关键词**：last seen around six p.m.／Skye Stevens picked her up／nine-thirty p.m.／after-party at Bobby Garza’s house

**为什么这样写**：作者把 timeline 写成 “three timestamps”——6pm (parents) → Skye pickup → 9:30pm (text)。这条 timeline 是本章的 information backbone，后文所有 “alibi” 讨论都要回到这条线上。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “9:30 p.m. text”——这条 “after-party at Bobby’s” 后文 Guerrero 的 “fill in the timeline” 是 echo。

---

> **原句 3:** “A teenage girl who isn’t fighting with someone? That’s difficult to believe. Teenage girls are nothing but drama. At least, in my experience.”

**中文理解**：Guerrero 说“A teenage girl who isn’t fighting with someone? That’s difficult to believe. Teenage girls are nothing but drama. At least, in my experience.”

**关键词**：nothing but drama／in my experience

**为什么这样写**：作者让 Guerrero 用 “personal experience”（daughter at public high school）去 challenge “no one disliked Whitney” 的 consensus。这条 “drama” 把 “model student / popular” 的 consensus 重新定义为 “possibly incomplete”，把案件推进到 “dig deeper” 的方向。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “nothing but drama”——这条 “skepticism” 后文所有 “interview more people” 讨论都要回到这条线上。

---

> **原句 4:** “His parents say he was home all night.”

**中文理解**：Erickson 说 Bobby 的 parents 说 he was home all night。

**关键词**：home all night

**为什么这样写**：作者把 Bobby 的 alibi 写成 “parents say”——把 “alibi” 重新定义为 “hearsay from parents”，把 “home all night” 从 “fact” 推进到 “claim”。这条 “parents say” 是本章的 key evidence gap。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “parents say”——这条 “hearsay alibi” 后文所有 “sneak out” 讨论都要回到这条线上。

---

> **原句 5:** “But why would he? Whitney had been at his house for the party. Why set up a later meeting time in a deserted, cold area? If they wanted to fool around, they could have just waited for the other kids to leave. If they were fighting—and no one thinks they were—why not just have it out at the house?”

**中文理解**：Shelby 反问“But why would he? Whitney had been at his house for the party. Why set up a later meeting time in a deserted, cold area? If they wanted to fool around, they could have just waited for the other kids to leave. If they were fighting—and no one thinks they were—why not just have it out at the house?”

**关键词**：deserted, cold area／waited for the other kids to leave／have it out at the house

**为什么这样写**：作者把 Shelby 的反驳写成 “series of why”——她用 “location logic”（deserted cold area vs. waiting at house）去 challenge Erickson 的 “sneak out” hypothesis。这条 “why set up a later meeting” 是本章的 key inference。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “deserted, cold area”——这条 “location logic” 后文所有 “Bobby’s motive” 讨论都要回到这条线上。

---

> **原句 6:** “History of violence. Closest to her. He wears a red football jersey.” When the women didn’t agree, he continued, tapping the captain’s desk with his forefinger to make a point. “Data shows time and again that when someone is murdered, it’s usually the partner. He’s the obvious place to start.”

**中文理解**：Erickson 说“History of violence. Closest to her. He wears a red football jersey。”当两女性不同意时，他继续，用 forefinger 敲 captain’s desk 强调：“Data shows time and again that when someone is murdered, it’s usually the partner. He’s the obvious place to start.”

**关键词**：History of violence／Closest to her／red football jersey／usually the partner／obvious place to start

**为什么这样写**：作者把 Erickson 的 argument 写成 “three evidence + statistical claim”——他把 “history / proximity / jersey” 推进到 “partner = usual suspect”的 statistical claim。这条 “data shows” 是本章的 key persuasion move，而 Guerrero 的 “keep an open mind” 是 direct rebuttal。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “usually the partner”——这条 “statistical claim” 后文 Captain Guerrero 的 “objectivity” 是 echo。

---

> **原句 7:** “The national news hasn’t picked up this story yet, but it’s only a matter of time.” At Erickson’s confused expression, she said, “A pretty white girl is murdered in a small town in West Virginia. And it involves a football player.” She sighed again, meeting Shelby’s eyes. “They’ll want to connect dots, even if they don’t exist.”

**中文理解**：Guerrero 说“The national news hasn’t picked up this story yet, but it’s only a matter of time。”看 Erickson 困惑，她解释：“A pretty white girl is murdered in a small town in West Virginia. And it involves a football player。”她叹气，与 Shelby 对视：“They’ll want to connect dots, even if they don’t exist.”

**关键词**：pretty white girl／small town in West Virginia／football player／connect dots, even if they don’t exist

**为什么这样写**：作者把 “media pressure” 写成 Guerrero 的 “sigh + eyes meet Shelby”——把 “pretty white girl + football player” 推进到 “national news narrative” 的预判。这条 “connect dots even if they don’t exist” 是本章的 key thematic statement，也是全书的 meta-commentary。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “connect dots, even if they don’t exist”——这条 “media pressure” 后文所有 “Bobby as suspect” 讨论都要回到这条线上。

> **原句 8:** “Keep casting the net. Talk to Skye Stevens, Whitney’s other friends, Bobby’s friends, and her teachers. Paint a clearer picture, fill in the timeline.”

**中文理解**：Guerrero 站起来走向门，说“Keep casting the net. Talk to Skye Stevens, Whitney’s other friends, Bobby’s friends, and her teachers. Paint a clearer picture, fill in the timeline.”

**关键词**：Keep casting the net／Paint a clearer picture／fill in the timeline

**为什么这样写**：作者把 captain 的 directive 写成 “cast net + paint picture + fill timeline”——把 “investigation” 重新定义为 “net-casting” rather than “arrest Bobby”。这条 “open mind” 是本章的 methodological statement，echo Shelby 的 “all the angles”。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “Keep casting the net”——这条 “open mind” 后文所有 “Bobby as suspect” 讨论都要回到这条线上。

> **原句 9:** “No cameras. At least, none pointing toward the pool. Only two houses had security cameras, and they’re focused on their backyards.”

**中文理解**：Officer Simon 报告“No cameras. At least, none pointing toward the pool. Only two houses had security cameras, and they’re focused on their backyards.”

**关键词**：No cameras／none pointing toward the pool／focused on their backyards

**为什么这样写**：作者把 “evidence gap” 写成 “Simon’s report”——把 “cameras” 从 “hope” 推进到 “none usable”。这条 “no cameras” echo ch01 Naomi 的 “cameras off since summer”，把 “evidence gap” 从 “winter off” 推进到 “no pointing at pool”。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “no cameras”——这条 “evidence gap” 后文所有 “Bobby’s alibi verification” 讨论都要回到这条线上。

> **原句 10:** “My boy did not kill poor Whitney. You’re barking up the wrong tree.”

**中文理解**：Mrs. Garza 说“My boy did not kill poor Whitney. You’re barking up the wrong tree.”

**关键词**：My boy did not kill poor Whitney／barking up the wrong tree

**为什么这样写**：作者把 Mrs. Garza 的 defense 写成 “poor Whitney + barking up wrong tree”——把 “grief” 重新定义为 “defense of son”，把 “barking up wrong tree” 从 “mother’s belief” 推进到 “possible truth”。这条 “barking up wrong tree” 是本章的 key rhetorical turn。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “barking up the wrong tree”——这条 “mother’s defense” 后文所有 “Bobby missing” 讨论都要回到这条线上。

> **原句 11:** “I’m not ready to put out an APB on Bobby. He’s probably just at a friend’s house, crying into a glass of Mountain Dew.”

**中文理解**：Shelby 说“I’m not ready to put out an APB on Bobby. He’s probably just at a friend’s house, crying into a glass of Mountain Dew.”

**关键词**：not ready to put out an APB／crying into a glass of Mountain Dew

**为什么这样写**：作者把 Shelby 的 decision 写成 “not ready + probably just”——把 “Bobby missing” 从 “flight” 推进到 “grief at friend’s house”。这条 “Mountain Dew” 是 Shelby 的 “grief normalization” inference，与 Erickson 的 “sneak out” hypothesis 形成 direct contrast。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “Mountain Dew”——这条 “grief normalization” 后文 Bobby 的 actual whereabouts 会验证/证伪。
