**中文理解**：Erickson 说 through the two-way mirror：“Not too tough for a drug kingpin。”…“Or prepared。Doesn’t seem like he had any plan at all for getting caught。Not even a lawyer on retainer。No game。No poker face。Guess he really is a teddy bear。”…Shelby 说：“I wish they’d never made Breaking Bad.”
