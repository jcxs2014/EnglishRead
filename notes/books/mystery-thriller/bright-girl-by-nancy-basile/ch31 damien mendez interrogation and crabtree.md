# 31. Chapter Thirty-One

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Shelby 与 Erickson 审讯 Damien Mendez；Damien 最初 “Had a great time... Until I didn’t”；律师 Rose Capito 数字 detox 一周；Damien 最终 “come clean”：Keith Crabtree (Baltimore) 车辆盗窃 ring，Damien 偷 Pittsburgh 停放的车；章末 Damien “He’ll find me!”，Erickson “Y bueno.”

- **情感弧线位置**：全书的 “interrogation + criminal network” 格：从 Damien 的 “invisible circles” 到 “He’ll find me!” 的恐惧，把 “suspect” 从 “Whitney’s supplier” 推到 “car thief for Crabtree”。

- **线索结构**：章内摆出七样往后要回来对账的东西：Damien 的 “No coffee? Water?”；“Had a great time... Until I didn’t”；Rose Capito “digital detox”；“If I talk, can I get some kind of immunity?”；Keith Crabtree (Baltimore)；“business people who leave their cars parked around Pittsburgh”；“He’ll find me!”

- **人物弧线**：Damien 从 “smirk” 到 “melted into the back of his chair” 的崩溃；Shelby 从 “clasp her hands” 到 “go to bat for you” 的 leverage；Erickson 从 “all teeth” smile 到 “Y bueno” 的 triumph。

- **叙事手法**：第三人称限知贴住 Shelby，审讯室的 “handcuffed to the D-ring” 与 “beads of sweat” 并用。章末用 “He’ll find me!” + “Y bueno” 作为 reversal.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Damien Mendez drew invisible circles on the table in the interrogation room with his finger. He looked like he didn’t have a care in the world, regardless that he was handcuffed to the D-ring in the middle of the steel table.

**中文理解**：Damien Mendez drew invisible circles on the table in the interrogation room with his finger。He looked like he didn’t have a care in the world, regardless that he was handcuffed to the D-ring in the middle of the steel table。

**关键词**：invisible circles／didn’t have a care in the world／handcuffed to the D-ring

**为什么这样写**：作者把 “interrogation opening” 写成 “invisible circles”——deliberate 让 “Damien” 落成 “casual, unbothered” 的 register，把 “D-ring” 作为 “restraint” 的 thread。这条 “didn’t have a care in the world” 把 “Damien” 从 “suspect” 推进到 “confident” 的 thread。

**读者视角提示**：记住 “invisible circles”——这条 “casualness” 后文会被 “He’ll find me!” 验证。
