# 《Carry Me to My Grave》概述

## 全书梗概

**一、先给一口埋在橡树底下的棺材。** 这本书的历史线只有四章——第一章、第五章、第十二章与第十四章——却撑着整个故事。农庄的玉米地里立着一棵老橡树，树下埋着一样东西，它比这片农庄更老、比那棵树更老，长久不动，连梦也不做：
- "The thing under the tree had been there longer than the farm. Longer than the oak."（ch01）
- 十一月的一个清晨，房子跳了一下、窗子碎了，那棵橡树当场断成两半；叙述越过惊呆的夫妻，把镜头压回地底——它是在睡梦中挣动的，断掉了两条粗根："Two thick roots had been broken when it thrashed in its sleep, in that moment when—far away—Maggie Wise had taken her final breath. Other roots still held it in place, one around its throat, one that twined tightly around its pelvis and left leg, and another that had grown in such a way that the root melded with its right forearm—wood and bone fused."（ch05）
- 原文在这句里把这一刻与远方一位临终者咽气的同一刹那并置，所以历史线与当日主线落在同一个夜晚。那个年代的男孩叫Benjy Swickard，八岁（第一章原文写「八岁」）；当日线里跟着Root走进列车的男孩也自称Benjy，原文从头到尾没有把两个Benjy认作同一人，本篇也不替它合并。地面裂开之后，那具身体第一次开口："“Come, boy,” the thing said. “We have far to go and much to do before the sun rises again.”"（ch12）
- 而男孩被带上火车时，连恐惧都被像开关一样关掉了："Benjy couldn’t be afraid. That part of him had been buried deep inside. Turned off, just the way Root had turned off his voice. He wanted to reply to the conductor, had even selected the words in his head, but all he could do was sit beside the thing from beneath his cornfield as if he were accompanying his grandfather on a journey."（ch14）

**二、当日主线从一场临终开始。** 母亲Maggie Wise在印第安纳州Elkhart的旧宅里咽气，她生在缅因州Shediak。窗外柳树下的窥视者整夜不撤，Malcolm摔门出去才看清对方戴着一副乌鸦面具——那人不说来历，只丢下一句：
- "It’s almost over, Malcolm. After all this time, the end is coming. It might relieve you to know they don’t hold their pain against you."（ch02）
- 母亲随后在地板上把可执行的期限交给他："Once my heart stops, you’ll have two nights. I’ve got to be in Shediak by the second sunrise or it’ll all be for nothing."（ch03）
- 日出前她咽气，留下的最后一句是："“You’re a good boy, Malcolm,” she whispered without opening her eyes. “But you’ve gotta carry me home. Selfish of me … to wait so long.”"（ch04）

**三、把一口板条箱抬上开往北方的火车。** Elkhart教堂街的殡仪馆老板帕克森·基茨连夜赶来，把遗体缝进厚帆布裹布，装进一口钉了盖子的板条箱——母亲交代过不要体面的棺材，说是浪费。Hannigan兄弟先到一步，把箱子抬进屋；两个红头发的高大男人随后在街上与摘掉面具的乌鸦动过手。灵车甩过车站拐进行李车场，喇叭声把灵车该有的肃穆拆掉了："The hearse whipped past the station and turned into the train yard. A gate stood partway open, and Pax laid on the horn, the trumpeting of which undermined the somber dignity that typically accompanied the arrival of a hearse."（ch07）

**四、被带回家的人与回家的人。** Violet·怀斯本姓坦普尔，生母在一场火里死了，火焰只从里面烧到父亲；她被丈夫从芝加哥的夜场带回Elkhart，当成一件赢来的东西：
- "He’d taken her home to Elkhart, Indiana, like some kind of prize, and left her there to collect dust while he carried on with a life of gambling and nightclubs and pretty girls in baubles and gowns."（ch08）
- 列车停靠布法罗时，哥哥Elias出现在站台上——原文写「这是他的哥哥，他的童年第一个朋友与保护者」，而Elias自己喊Malcolm「弟弟」；他抢开木箱，当着兄弟的面说出母亲是个女巫，随后在雨夜里一层层换皮，指尖摸着那口箱子说了一句「你应该告诉我」："“Oh, Maggie,” Elias said, running his fingertips over the surface of the crate. “You should have told me.”"（ch17）
- 闯入行李车厢的那个东西报出了自己的来路："But answer it did. Showing teeth. “An old friend of the family.”"（ch19）

**五、列车脱轨，故事分成两拨人。** 长弯处列车冲下轨道（第十九章末）。兄弟俩各被Root从不同方向唤起：
- Elias被丢进雨里，醒来已经不是原来那个人；Root给他的活儿是「我们必须阻止那个女巫」："Root caressed Elias’s head and gazed at him, a parent to a newborn. “We must stop the witch.”"（ch22）
- 同一个声音还给出了一段集体记忆——那个女巫很久以前这样害过「我们」："The witch did this to us, long ago. We buried ourselves deep, wretched in agony, minds collapsing. Her death provided some relief, but we are so much more than this. If we end this pain, we will be the gods of blood and darkness once more."（ch22）
- 另一头，警车与消防车赶到现场，姐姐Jennie·怀斯开着偷来的车把Malcolm和Violet拽出来，一路上只讲一件事："“You’re not listening. Sunrise is the only help that’ll matter. You want to end this, the only way is by putting Maggie in her grave."（ch23）

**六、中段的夜路：
- 一块口诀、三味药粉与一间拘留室。** Malcolm在肯塔基州布雷肯里奇营受训时，教官留下两句口诀："Sneaky motherfuckers tend to make it out alive, Rosie had said."（ch24）
- Jennie在夜路的小巴士后厢往板条箱里撒下三味药粉，并点着一撮头发，用烟去断掉追踪："It’s ground garlic, dried sheep’s blood, and powdered bone from the hands of a woman who could heal with her touch."（ch28）
- 那些东西有名字，而母亲一直叫它们Morák："Maggie always called them the Morák, but they’re so old that they go back to a time before there were names, or words, or language. Before there were people."（ch28）
- 车到马萨诸塞州克罗森，兄妹俩被关进拘留室；来问话的是警探雷·Hope："I’m Detective Ray Hope,” the rumpled man said. “Don’t take any comfort from the name. I’m not especially cheerful, and you won’t get a pep talk from me."（ch29）
- 等待里Jennie讲出与母亲之间那道至今未解的裂缝："Can you imagine a mother saying that to her daughter? Put aside the witchcraft—she dismissed me and told me to find a way to make myself worth her attention."（ch29）
- 警探说了要放他们出去，又在同一句里说明这不等于放人；男孩随即撞倒了他。他们照警探给的线索去镇上的殡仪馆取回遗体，Malcolm当场认下那具被帆布缝合、此前无人认领的女尸是自己的母亲："This is my mother. You have no right to hold her remains. It’s not worth the pain you’re already in, never mind the damage I can do to you."（ch35）

**七、雪夜、歌声与被拆成两半的一家人。** 警探的枪成了他们的枪，殡仪馆老板的敞篷车成了他们的车，男孩成了他们的报警器：
- "“Fine.” She gave a tiny shrug. “It’s for our benefit. You both know this. If the Morák get close to us, Benjy will feel it. He’ll sense them closing in. Call him our fire alarm.”"（ch37）
- 后座上的母亲死后仍在说话，报出一个带重音的词，Jennie把它译成「旧火」："“The … régi tűz,” Maggie managed. “The old … the old fire.”"（ch41）
- Violet不给Malcolm讲咒语，只把手放在他膝上说了两个字："Violet reached back and laid a hand on Malcolm’s knee. “Sing.”"（ch44）
- 唱出来的东西他自己也认得，是从身体往外走的："Breathe out the dark, Malcolm remembered singing. Bleed it from your veins. Close the door the monster blew open, and lock it tight."（ch44）
- 追他们的铲雪车最终连人带车栽下小桥；开车的阿尔菲·Hannigan与死去的孪生兄弟乔并肩走进雪地："The Hannigan brothers turned and walked off along the snow-covered stream, leaving no trace of their passage. The wind gusted, and they were gone."（ch45）
- Shediak是一座只有一条两车道桥的岛。桥那头怪物太多，他们改走水路。临走前Malcolm给Violet留了话，还把枪留给她——那支枪只剩四发："“There aren’t any Morák out there. We won’t be completely empty-handed, but the gun stays with you. Just in case,” Malcolm said. He wasn’t going to argue the point. “Wait an hour past dawn, and if we’re not back, go without us.”"（ch47）

**八、天亮前的一座岛。** 船上是Malcolm与Jennie，岸上是Violet与男孩。Jennie先替母亲下了判决：
- "“Let her scream,” Jennie said. “It’s not Maggie. It’s the thing replacing her.”"（ch49）
- 兄妹为「这件事该不该由孩子做」吵过一轮；坑挖到三英尺深时，水平线上只有破晓的影子，太阳还没上来。Malcolm的铲击在最后一瞬改了方向，把哥哥拍倒在浅水里："The shovel blade whickered through the air. Malcolm swung like he was at bat. A heartbeat before it connected, he turned his grip so instead of slicing into his brother’s neck, he smashed the flat face of the shovel into the back of Elias’s head. He collapsed into the shallows, water flowing over his head."（ch55）
- Root挨了铁棒平头的反复穿刺，从浪里站起来时喊的是一句关于埋的历史控诉："“We will not be made to hide in the ground again,” Root snarled. “Never.”"（ch56）
- Jennie死在自己母亲牙下，留给Malcolm的只有一句事实陈述："“You needed me,” Jennie told her mother."（ch56）
- 他把母亲一寸寸拖向松林下的坑，一边唱；这一刻他看得见、停不下："Malcolm saw it happen. Couldn’t stop it."（ch57）
- 收尾的镜头交回风里："A sound rises above the noise of the wind and surf, even above Elias’s agonized cries, and Malcolm realizes what he hears are the screams of other monsters carried to them on the breeze."（ch58）
- Violet在雪路边陪Elias熬到日出，看着他在阳光里被点燃，转身避开了那一层恐怖："His pain she could withstand, but this is another level of horror, and she is only human."（ch58）
- 坟填完，Malcolm与男孩并肩站在坟前，作者把两人的手势比成一对迷失在祈祷里的父子："He stands with Benjy at the graveside, both with their hands clasped respectfully in front of them, like a father and son lost in prayer."（ch58）
- 最后一段用的是将来时："“They’re going to bring Jennie home. Give her the farewell she deserves, with all the love and sorrow and gratitude of true family.”"（ch58）

## 三个主题

**一、承诺是有账单的东西。** 这本书的每一章都在兑付同一张欠条：
- 母亲咽气前那句「把我带回家」，此后被拆成两夜、一个地名、一口板条箱和一段必须按时走完的路。中段Malcolm把军旅教官的两句口诀搬出来当规矩："Sneaky motherfuckers tend to make it out alive, Rosie had said."（ch24）
- 终点在填土的最后一刻，他停下来只为说一句话，并把这句话拴在一个理由上——他费了力气成为比母亲塑造的更完整的自己，因此不交出这一点："Malcolm wiped away tears and kept shoveling. He paused his song long enough to tell his mother that he loved her, because he’d worked to become something more than what she’d made him, and he wouldn’t surrender that part of himself now, or ever."（ch57）

**二、巫术不是刑罚，是音乐。** 母亲留下的不是配方而是一支曲子；Malcolm在雪地里替她重新定义了这份力量：
- "How could it be, when she looked no different from how she had when they had sewn her into the canvas and nailed her into that crate? Maggie Wise might have been a witch, but in his heart, her magic was not in cruelty or punishment but in the sweetness of piano music that played just for him, even after death."（ch39）
- 车厢里所有人都在争这套来历有几分可信，质疑的方式不是查证而是用词——在讲到「无私」「英勇」之前得先花掉多少个词："“If someone asked you to describe Maggie, how many words do you think you’d use up before you got to selfless or heroic, because the version of her story you just told us could easily include those words? Maggie Wise went to war against a breed of homicidal monsters for the good of the people?”"（ch42）
- 而最后一战里，逼他开口的不是勇气，是算术："Malcolm knew he had to sing. Ridiculous, but she lurched toward them, hungry and hate-filled, and he knew he would never survive fighting them both. The sun would edge above the horizon in minutes, but he didn’t have minutes. He had to sing."（ch55）

**三、「家人」这个词被换过好几次。** 被Root带上车的Elias先把它换掉了——原指向的那一家正在猎他，而他认定自己此刻同行的这几个才是：
- "They were hunting his family, intended to kill them, but there on top of the fire truck, embraced in the dark, he knew that this was his family now."（ch27）
- Violet给出的说法更平实，她观察到的是这个男人一辈子只靠自己，以至于对「与人搭档」没有把握："Violet loved the way Malcolm reacted when she made him think in a way he hadn’t been thinking before. Surprised and delighted in equal measure. In his life, he’d become so used to relying only on himself that he never seemed quite sure what to do with partnership."（ch43）
- Elias颅骨里那个替他求情的旧声音，来自一个有时欺负他、始终爱他的姐姐："Elias could hear a voice screaming inside his skull. It belonged to the man he used to be, the boy he’d been, growing up with an older sister who sometimes tormented him but always loved him."（ch54）
- 全书最后一次说出「家人」，是收尾那一段将来时："“They’re going to bring Jennie home. Give her the farewell she deserves, with all the love and sorrow and gratitude of true family.”"（ch58）

## 人物弧光

**Malcolm·怀斯**——

- 起点："Your brother’s wife, he thought. As a soldier, as a son, as a brother, he had made honor fundamental to the way he saw himself. Now this."（ch11）
- 转向："Since the day we met,” he said, then realized the implication. “I’d never have let it go anywhere. Swear to God. But Elias abandoned her—cast her off like garbage—and we grew close."（ch29）
- 最低点："“She chose herself,” Malcolm said. “She always did. Maggie was so selfish, she let herself die in Indiana when she could’ve come home to Maine anytime!”"（ch51）
- 落点："Malcolm wiped away tears and kept shoveling. He paused his song long enough to tell his mother that he loved her, because he’d worked to become something more than what she’d made him, and he wouldn’t surrender that part of himself now, or ever."（ch57）
- 他真正的转折不是打赢哪一场，而是把「她为什么这样对我」和「我答应过她什么」分开之后，仍然回去唱歌。

**Jennie·怀斯**——

- 起点："Jennie’s laugh was laced with grief. “Who do you think? Dear old Mom.”"（ch26）
- 中段："Can you imagine a mother saying that to her daughter? Put aside the witchcraft—she dismissed me and told me to find a way to make myself worth her attention."（ch29）
- 落点："“You needed me, Maggie,” she whispered to herself. “But you didn’t deserve me.”"（ch53）
- 终点："“You needed me,” Jennie told her mother."（ch56）
- 她的弧线是一道被压了多年的裂缝：母亲曾花一个月考查Elias，最后认定那份天赋只能落在Malcolm身上，Jennie则被要求自己想办法变得值得母亲注意。她走遍欧洲与非洲某处学来的本事，最后用在自己母亲的喉咙上。

**Violet·怀斯**——

- 起点："Violet had met her share of those men, too, and managed to avoid becoming entangled with them. All but one, which was how she’d let herself fall in love with Elias. He’d presented a safe haven, and she’d taken it."（ch10）
- 转向："Falling in love with Malcolm, being with him constantly but unable to speak her mind or share her heart, had made her realize what she’d felt for Elias had never been love. This kind of yearning was the full bloom that grew from infatuation."（ch47）
- 落点："His pain she could withstand, but this is another level of horror, and she is only human."（ch58）
- 她承认自己当初对Elias的那份不是爱；结尾她陪那个东西熬到日出，却替自己划了一条界——她是人。

**Elias·怀斯**——

- 起点："“Are you fucking my wife, brother?”"（ch17）
- 转向："What am I now? The question might have been prompted by the old Elias, but the creature he’d become also wanted to know."（ch25）
- 中段："My brother and sister, and my wife. I’ll rejoice while doing it, and if there’s a speck of my soul left, I’ll be damned to hell for it. When the frenzy wears off, I’ll want to rip out my own heart, use my fingers to claw it out. I’ll probably do it, too. I don’t want to do any of that. They may hate me now, but my family deserve better. I can’t—"（ch40）
- 落点："It’s not Elias, Malcolm reminded himself. This monster with his ruined face, a torn-up mouth, and red froth sliding over wicked teeth … this thing was not really Elias. It wasn’t his big brother, who’d explored the woods with him and held his hand every time they crossed Cabot Creek, who’d never let him win at marbles but gave Malcolm his favorite tiger’s eye as a reward for being a good sport."（ch55）
- 原文从第二十一章起改用「曾经是Elias的那东西」这类称呼，后文还有一句称他为「母亲的长子」；书里没有一句话交代他现在究竟是什么，本篇不替他定性。他的结局不在这一章的收束里，而在全书最后：Violet陪他熬到日出，看着他在阳光里被点燃。

**Maggie Wise**——

- 起点："Once my heart stops, you’ll have two nights. I’ve got to be in Shediak by the second sunrise or it’ll all be for nothing."（ch03）
- 中段："“Dry those … goddamned tears,” Maggie Wise told her daughter. “I didn’t raise you … for tears.”"（ch39）
- 被否认："“Let her scream,” Jennie said. “It’s not Maggie. It’s the thing replacing her.”"（ch49）
- 她最后留给Malcolm的遗产是一支只为他一个人响的曲子，和一个必须在下一次日出前完成的动作。

**阿尔菲·Hannigan与乔**——

- 起点："She stood by the open window of the baggage car, looking out at the ominous gray sky. Cold wind whipped her hair back. “People with nothing to fear don’t need a man like Alfie Hannigan to look out for them.”"（ch07）
- 转向："The voice might be in his head, but he recognized it anyway. Root, of course. The name felt ironic now. The creature had planted its roots deep inside him, in his brain and bones."（ch34）
- 落点："The Hannigan brothers turned and walked off along the snow-covered stream, leaving no trace of their passage. The wind gusted, and they were gone."（ch45）
- 收场那句「像一对迷失在祈祷里的父子」是叙述者给Malcolm与男孩的比喻，原文并没有交代Malcolm与Benjy之间有亲属关系；兄弟俩自己的收场在上一条落点那一章，两个人的尸迹在雪面上不留脚印。
