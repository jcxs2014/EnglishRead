# 35. After the Unfortunate Accident（不幸事故之后）

## 概览

- **出处**：Tales of Terror: 58 Short Stories Chosen by the Master of Suspense，第 35 篇
- **作者**：未标注
- **体裁**：心理恐怖短篇，约 5,700 字符
- **一句话主题**：叙述者"我"车祸身亡后，发现自己身处一个电影院，被迫观看自己一生的家庭电影。其他死者——包括放映员父亲——告诉他这是天堂，但"我"意识到这是地狱：永恒地观看自己厌恶的过去。

## 选择性精读

**①** "After the unfortunate accident, I find myself sitting in a large room, rows and rows of straight-back chairs and a movie-screen in front, set up flat against the wall."

- 中文理解："不幸事故之后，我发现自己坐在一个大房间里，一排排直背椅，前面是一个电影屏幕，紧靠着墙。"
- 句子结构：时间状语 After the unfortunate accident + 主语 I + 谓语 find + 宾语 myself + 补语 sitting in a large room + 同位语 rows and rows of straight-back chairs and a movie-screen in front + 分词短语 set up flat against the wall。
- 关键词：**unfortunate accident**（不幸事故——死亡）；**straight-back chairs**（直背椅——电影院）
- 表达方式：**开篇就是死亡**。"不幸事故"——他死了。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的钩子**。他"死了"——但他还有意识。

**②** "The room seems half-filled or a little more with people who I do not recognize, some of them slumped over in attitudes of catatonia or boredom, others smoking cigarettes and regarding the pictures running on the screen with varying degrees of interest."

- 中文理解：房间似乎坐满了一半或更多我不认识的人，其中一些人瘫倒在椅子上，表现出木僵或无聊的姿势，另一些人抽着烟，以不同程度的兴趣看着屏幕上播放的画面。
- 句子结构：主语 The room + 谓语 seems + 表语 half-filled or a little more + 介词短语 with people + 定语从句 who I do not recognize + 同位语 some of them slumped over in attitudes of catatonia or boredom + 同位语 others smoking cigarettes and regarding the pictures running on the screen with varying degrees of interest。
- 关键词：**catatonia**（木僵——精神失常）；**varying degrees of interest**（不同程度的兴趣——冷漠）
- 表达方式：**死者的状态**。他们"木僵或无聊"——这是死者的状态。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对死后世界的描写**。死者"木僵"——这是地狱。

**③** "Still appalled by what has happened to me—until that moment I had never lost control of a car in my life—I concentrate upon the movies, trying to restore some sense of order."

- 中文理解："仍然对发生在我的事情感到震惊——直到那一刻我一生中从未失去对车的控制——我专注于电影，试图恢复某种秩序感。"
- 句子结构：状语 Still appalled by what has happened to me + 插入语 until that moment I had never lost control of a car in my life + 主语 I + 谓语 concentrate upon + 宾语 the movies + 分词短语 trying to restore some sense of order。
- 关键词：**appalled**（震惊——恐惧）；**restore some sense of order**（恢复秩序——心理防御）
- 表达方式：**"我"的震惊**。他"震惊"——他无法接受死亡。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对"我"心理的描写**。他"震惊"——他在自我防御。

**④** "The movies, it seems, are films of my own family when I was a boy; scenes I dimly recognize float before me and in jarring close-ups I see the face of my mother, my sister and myself, superimposed against backgrounds of the sea or heavy traffic, embarrassed grins against the façades of the various apartment buildings we occupied throughout the years."

- 中文理解："电影，似乎是我自己家庭在我小时候的电影；我模糊认识的场景在我眼前飘过，在刺耳的特写中，我看到母亲、妹妹和我的脸，叠加在海边或繁忙交通的背景上，尴尬的笑容对着我们多年来居住的各种公寓楼的外观。"
- 句子结构：主语 The movies + 插入语 it seems + 谓语 are + 表语 films of my own family when I was a boy + 同位语 scenes + 定语从句 I dimly recognize + 谓语 float before me + and + 地点状语 in jarring close-ups + 主语 I + 谓语 see + 宾语 the face of my mother, my sister and myself + 分词短语 superimposed against backgrounds of the sea or heavy traffic + 同位语 embarrassed grins against the façades of the various apartment buildings + 定语从句 we occupied throughout the years。
- 关键词：**dimly recognize**（模糊认识——久远）；**jarring close-ups**（刺耳的特写——不适）；**superimposed**（叠加——合成）
- 表达方式：**家庭电影**。"模糊认识"——这是久远的记忆。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对死后世界的描写**。他看"家庭电影"——这是他的过去。

**⑤** "You'll ruin everything," a fat man in front of me says, turning, glaring at me. "Just because you're new here is no reason to scream. Now attend to yourself and watch the pictures; you'll find some of these very interesting and in any case it's your life which is being shown so you could use a little respect."

- 中文理解："你会毁了一切，"前面的胖男人说，转过身来瞪着我。"仅仅因为你新来不是尖叫的理由。现在注意自己，看这些画面；你会发现其中一些很有趣，反正这是你的人生在被播放，所以你应该有点尊重。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 You'll ruin everything + 主语 a fat man + 地点状语 in front of me + 谓语 says + 分词短语 turning, glaring at me；直接引语 Just because you're new here is no reason to scream；直接引语 Now attend to yourself and watch the pictures；直接引语 you'll find some of these very interesting and in any case it's your life which is being shown so you could use a little respect。
- 关键词：**ruin everything**（毁了一切——破坏）；**attend to yourself**（注意自己——规矩）；**respect**（尊重——敬畏）
- 表达方式：**胖男人的警告**。他说"你会毁了一切"——这是警告。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对死后世界规则的描写**。他"警告"——这是规则。

**⑥** "Don't be so rough on the boy," a woman beside me says. She is rather attractive although in no sexual fashion; perhaps motherly is the word I am seeking. "He is new here and it's always a bit of a shock for them. I remember how you were when you came in," she adds sharply and the fat man blushes, turns around toward the screen again, his shoulders hunched.

- 中文理解："别对这男孩这么粗暴，"旁边的女人说。她相当有魅力，虽然谈不上性感；也许"慈祥"才是我要找的词。"他是新来的，对他们来说总是有点震惊。我记得你刚进来时的样子，"她尖锐地说，胖男人脸红了，又转向屏幕，肩膀耸着。
- 句子结构：直接引语 Don't be so rough on the boy + 主语 a woman + 地点状语 beside me + 谓语 says；叙述句 She is rather attractive + 让步状语 although in no sexual fashion + 分号 + 插入语 perhaps motherly is the word I am seeking；直接引语 He is new here and it's always a bit of a shock for them + 直接引语 I remember how you were when you came in + 主语 she + 谓语 adds + 状语 sharply + and + 主语 the fat man + 谓语 blushes + 谓语 turns around toward the screen again + 独立主格 his shoulders hunched。
- 关键词：**rather attractive although in no sexual fashion**（有魅力但不性感）；**motherly**（慈祥的——母亲般）；**hunched**（耸着——紧张）
- 表达方式：**女人的同情与叙述者的观察**。女人安慰新来的死者；叙述者用"rather attractive... motherly"冷静地刻画她，与他身处"死亡放映室"的超现实处境形成反差。
- 为什么这样写：**"死亡放映室"的诡异日常感**。死去的灵魂聚在一起看自己生平的"电影"，胖男人像管教孩子一样对新来的施压，女人出面安慰——这种"办公室式"的死后生活，把死亡写得既荒诞又温情。

**⑦** "How did you die?" she says but with such concern and interest that I do not find the question offensive. "You're awfully young." "I missed a curve in the rain in an old Dodge," I say, "and ran off the road. The last thing I remember is heading toward a tree so I guess I must have been killed instantly. I never lost control of a car before."

- 中文理解："你是怎么死的？"她说，但带着如此的关注和兴趣，我不觉得这个问题冒犯。"你太年轻了。""我在雨中开一辆旧道奇车时错过了弯道，"我说，"冲出了公路。我记得的最后一件事是朝一棵树撞去，所以我猜我一定是当场死亡。我以前从未失去对车的控制。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 How did you die + 主语 she + 谓语 says + but + 方式状语 with such concern and interest + 结果状语从句 that I do not find the question offensive；直接引语 You're awfully young；直接引语 I missed a curve in the rain in an old Dodge + 主语 I + 谓语 say；直接引语 and ran off the road；直接引语 The last thing I remember is heading toward a tree so I guess I must have been killed instantly；直接引语 I never lost control of a car before。
- 关键词：**missed a curve**（错过弯道——失控）；**killed instantly**（当场死亡——迅速）；**never lost control**（从未失控——自信）
- 表达方式：**"我"的死亡**。他说"错过弯道"——他死了。

**⑧** "Well," she says nodding, "it is always a shock. Still, you're perfectly comfortable here as you can see and the movies are fairly interesting so it wasn't that bad for you after all. You'll find that you're never hungry or thirsty or need to sleep so you can just watch the movies all the time so all in all it worked out pretty nicely."

- 中文理解："嗯，"她点头说，"总是有点震惊。不过，你看，你在这里很舒服，电影也还不错，所以对你来说毕竟没那么糟。你会发现你永远不会饿、不会渴、不需要睡觉，所以你可以一直看电影，所以总的来说结果相当不错。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 Well + 主语 she + 谓语 says + 分词短语 nodding；直接引语 it is always a shock；直接引语 Still, you're perfectly comfortable here as you can see and the movies are fairly interesting so it wasn't that bad for you after all；直接引语 You'll find that you're never hungry or thirsty or need to sleep so you can just watch the movies all the time so all in all it worked out pretty nicely。
- 关键词：**perfectly comfortable**（相当舒服——安慰）；**never hungry or thirsty**（永远不会饿渴——永恒）；**worked out pretty nicely**（结果相当不错——讽刺）
- 表达方式：**女人的安慰**。她说"结果相当不错"——这是讽刺。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对死后世界的讽刺**。她说"不错"——但这是地狱。

**⑨** "The projectionist—how could I have doubted it?—is my father. I have not seen him in five years. 'Dad!' I shriek impulsively, focusing his attention with a wave, and locking my gaze with his. 'Dad, you've got to get me out of this!'"

- 中文理解："放映员——我怎么会怀疑呢？——是我父亲。我已经五年没见到他了。"爸爸！"我冲动地尖叫着，用挥手吸引他的目光，锁定他的目光。"爸爸，你必须把我从这里救出去！""
- 句子结构：主语 The projectionist + 插入语 how could I have doubted it + 谓语 is + 表语 my father；主语 I + 谓语 have not seen + 宾语 him + 时间状语 in five years；直接引语 Dad；主语 I + 谓语 shriek + 状语 impulsively + 分词短语 focusing his attention with a wave + and + 分词短语 locking my gaze with his；直接引语 Dad, you've got to get me out of this。
- 关键词：**projectionist**（放映员——父亲）；**shriek impulsively**（冲动地尖叫——绝望）；**get me out of this**（把我救出去——逃离）
- 表达方式：**"我"的绝望**。他说"把我救出去"——他绝望了。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的关键时刻**。他发现了父亲——他绝望了。

**⑩** "My father looks at me through layers of smoky darkness, his eyes shrouded and gives me a long, intense smile. 'No, son,' he says, waving at me before he disappears forever back into the booth, 'you don't understand at all.' His eyes twinkle with happiness; I remember how he loved to show the movies. - 中文理解："父亲透过层层烟雾看着我，他的眼睛蒙着阴影，给了我一个漫长而强烈的微笑。"不，儿子，"他说，在他永远消失回放映室之前向我挥手，"你根本不明白。"他的眼睛闪烁着幸福；我记得他多么喜欢放映电影。"这是天堂。""
