# 05. The Arrowmont Prison Riddle（阿罗蒙特监狱之谜）

## 概览

- **出处**：Tales of Terror: 58 Short Stories Chosen by the Master of Suspense，第 5 篇
- **作者**：Bill Pronzini
- **体裁**：侦探悬疑短篇，约 46,100 字符
- **一句话主题**：死囚 Teasdale 在绞刑后神秘失踪——侦探 Gilloon 揭露他用丝气球伪装成尸体，在暴雨中逃出监狱，最终杀死同谋 Granger 灭口。

## 选择性精读

**①** "I first met the man who called himself by the unlikely name of Buckmaster Gilloon in the late summer of 1916, my second year as warden of Arrowmont Prison."

- 中文理解：我第一次遇见那个自称巴克马斯特尔·吉卢恩的人是在1916年初夏，我担任阿罗蒙特监狱典狱长的第二年。
- 句子结构：主语 I + 谓语 first met + 宾语 the man + 定语从句 who called himself by the unlikely name of Buckmaster Gilloon + 时间状语 in the late summer of 1916 + 同位语 my second year as warden of Arrowmont Prison。
- 关键词：**unlikely name**（不太可能的名字——暗示假名）；**Arrowmont Prison**（阿罗蒙特监狱——案件发生地）
- 表达方式：**开篇就是回忆**。60年后，典狱长仍在思考谁是 Gilloon。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的基调建立**。这是一个关于"未解之谜"的故事——Gilloon 是谁，至今仍是谜。

**②** "He was in his late thirties, short and almost painfully thin; he had a glass eye and a drooping and incongruous Oriental-style mustache, wore English tweeds, gaudy Albert watch chains and plaid Scotch caps, and always carried half a dozen looseleaf notebooks in which he perpetually and secretively jotted things."

- 中文理解：他三十多岁，矮小，几乎瘦得令人痛苦；他有一只玻璃眼，下垂的、不协调的东方式胡子，穿着英国花哨的阿尔伯特表链和格子苏格兰帽，总是带着六个活页笔记本，不断地、秘密地记录着什么。
- 句子结构：主语 He + 谓语 was + 表语 in his late thirties + 表语 short and almost painfully thin + 谓语 had + 宾语 a glass eye and a drooping and incongruous Oriental-style mustache + 谓语 wore + 宾语 English tweeds, gaudy Albert watch chains and plaid Scotch caps + and + 谓语 always carried + 宾语 half a dozen looseleaf notebooks + 定语从句 in which he perpetually and secretively jotted things。
- 关键词：**glass eye**（玻璃眼）；**Oriental-style mustache**（东方式胡子）；**looseleaf notebooks**（活页笔记本——永远在记录）
- 表达方式：**Gilloon 的外貌描写**——不协调、神秘、引人注目的怪癖。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Gilloon 形象的定论**。他像一个"神秘人物"——不协调的外貌，永远的笔记本。

**③** "He lived in a boarding house in the center of the village and claimed to be a writer for the pulp magazines— Argosy, Adventure, All-Story Weekly, Munsey's."

- 中文理解：他住在村子中心的一家寄宿公寓里，自称是通俗杂志的撰稿人——《阿戈西》、《冒险》、《全故事周刊》、《芒西的》。
- 句子结构：第一分句主语 He + 谓语 lived + 地点状语 in a boarding house in the center of the village；第二分句 and + 主语 He（承前省略）+ 谓语 claimed to be + 表语 a writer for the pulp magazines + 举例 Argosy, Adventure, All-Story Weekly, Munsey's。
- 关键词：**boarding house**（寄宿公寓）；**pulp magazines**（通俗杂志——廉价畅销出版物）
- 表达方式：**Gilloon 的掩护身份**。他自称是通俗杂志作家，还报了四个杂志名——显得真实具体，令人信以为真。
- 为什么这样写：**以"真实细节"制造可信假象**。点出 Argosy、Adventure、All-Story Weekly、Munsey's 这些具体刊名，让"作家"身份看似可信——为后文他真实身份的惊人揭露埋伏笔。

**④** "In my nine decades on this earth, I have never encountered a more fascinating or troubling enigma than this man whose path crossed mine for a few short weeks in 1916."

- 中文理解：在我在这世上的九十年来，我从未遇到过比这个人在1916年与我的道路短暂交叉更迷人或更令人不安的谜。
- 句子结构：介词短语 In my nine decades on this earth + 主语 I + 谓语 have never encountered + 宾语 a more fascinating or troubling enigma + 介词短语 than this man + 定语从句 whose path crossed mine for a few short weeks in 1916。
- 关键词：**nine decades**（九十年——一生的困惑）；**enigma**（谜）
- 表达方式：**典狱长的感慨**。他一生都在思考 Gilloon 是谁。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的主题句**。Gilloon 是一个"谜"——一个一生都无法解开的谜。

**⑤** "The figure we saw was a balloon?" "In effect, yes. It is not difficult to sew and tie off a large piece of silk in the rough shape of a man. When inflated to a malleable rather than a fully expanded state with helium or hydrogen, and seen in poor light from a distance of forty feet or better, while covered by clothing and a hood, and weighted down with a pair of shoes and held tightly by two men—the effect can be maintained."

- 中文理解："我们看到的人形是个气球？""实际上，是的。把一大块丝绸缝成大致的人形并不难。当用氦气或氢气膨胀到可塑性而非完全膨胀的状态，在昏暗的光线下从四十英尺或更远的距离看，穿着衣服和兜帽，用一双鞋压重，被两个人紧紧抓住——效果可以维持。"
- 句子结构：直接引语 The figure we saw was a balloon + 直接引语 In effect, yes + 形式主语 It + 谓语 is not difficult + 主语补足语 to sew and tie off a large piece of silk in the rough shape of a man；时间状语从句 When inflated to a malleable rather than a fully expanded state with helium or hydrogen + and + 分词短语 seen in poor light from a distance of forty feet or better + 伴随状语 while covered by clothing and a hood + and + 分词短语 weighted down with a pair of shoes and held tightly by two men + 主句 the effect can be maintained。
- 关键词：**malleable**（可塑性的）；**inflated**（膨胀）；**the effect can be maintained**（效果可以维持）
- 表达方式：**Gilloon 的解释**。他用科学原理解释了"丝气球人偶"的可行性。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的终极揭露**。丝气球人偶——这是"不可能消失"的解释。

**⑥** "He walked out through the front gates."

- 中文理解："他走出了前门。"
- 句子结构：主语 He + 谓语 walked out + 地点状语 through the front gates。
- 关键词：**front gates**（前门——最显眼的地方）
- 表达方式：**Gilloon 的回答**。他用最简单的语言解释了最不可思议的逃脱。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇最惊人的揭露**。Teasdale 不是"消失"了——他是"走出去"的。

**⑦** "Oh, I suppose he thought that the bizarre circumstances surrounding the disappearance of an apparently hanged man would insure him enough time to get safely clear of this immediate area. If you were confused and baffled, you would not sound an instant alarm, whereas you certainly would have if he had simply disappeared from his cell."

- 中文理解："哦，我猜他认为一个明显被绞死的人消失的神秘情况会确保他有足够的时间安全离开这个区域。如果你感到困惑和迷惑，你就不会立即发出警报，而如果你发现他仅仅从牢房里消失了，你肯定会立即发出警报。"
- 句子结构：插入语 I suppose + 主语 he + 谓语 thought + 宾语从句 that the bizarre circumstances surrounding the disappearance of an apparently hanged man would insure him enough time to get safely clear of this immediate area；条件状语从句 If you were confused and baffled + 主句 you would not sound an instant alarm + whereas + 主句 you certainly would have + 条件状语从句 if he had simply disappeared from his cell。
- 关键词：**bizarre circumstances**（神秘情况）；**sound an instant alarm**（立即发出警报）
- 表达方式：**Gilloon 的推理**。他用心理学解释了 Teasdale 的动机。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对罪犯心理的揭露**。Teasdale 不是想"消失"，他是想"延迟被发现"。

**⑧** "This kind of puzzle takes logic rather than brilliance, Parker. As I told you earlier tonight, it isn't always wise to discount the supernatural; but in a case where no clear evidence of the supernatural exists, the answer generally lies in some form of illusion."

- 中文理解："这种谜题需要的是逻辑而不是才华，帕克。正如我今晚早些时候告诉你的，不总是明智的超自然现象；但在没有超自然现象清晰证据的情况下，答案通常在于某种形式的幻觉。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 This kind of puzzle + 谓语 takes + 宾语逻辑而不是才华 + 呼语 Parker；第二句方式状语从句 As I told you earlier tonight + 形式主语 it + 谓语 isn't always wise + 主语补足语 to discount the supernatural；第三句转折 but + 地点状语 in a case + 定语从句 where no clear evidence of the supernatural exists + 主语 the answer + 谓语 generally lies in + 宾语 some form of illusion。
- 关键词：**logic rather than brilliance**（逻辑而不是才华）；**illusion**（幻觉）
- 表达方式：**Gilloon 的破案哲学**。他相信逻辑，不相信超自然。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇对 Gilloon 破案方法的定论**。他不是靠"才华"，是靠"逻辑"。

**⑨** "One almost seems able after a while to divine places where they will occur," he said matter-of-factly, "and therefore to make oneself available to challenge them."

- 中文理解："一个人似乎过了一段时间就能预见到它们会发生的地方，"他平淡地说，"因此让自己准备好去挑战它们。"
- 句子结构：第一句主语 One + 谓语 almost seems able + 时间状语 after a while + 不定式短语 to divine places + 定语从句 where they will occur + 主语 he + 谓语 said + 方式状语 matter-of-factly；第二句目的状语 therefore + 不定式短语 to make oneself available to challenge them。
- 关键词：**divine**（预见）；**make oneself available**（让自己准备好）
- 表达方式：**Gilloon 的自我描述**。他声称自己能"预见"犯罪——这是超自然的。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇最神秘的时刻**。Gilloon 说他能"预见"犯罪——但他又说他不相信超自然。他到底是什么？

**⑩** "Who and what was Buckmaster Gilloon? Is it possible for one enigma to be attracted and motivated by another enigma? Can that which seems natural and coincidental be the result instead of preternatural forces?"

- 中文理解："巴克马斯特尔·吉卢恩是谁，是什么？一个谜有可能被另一个谜吸引和驱动吗？看起来自然和巧合的东西，是否可能是超自然力量的结果？"
- 句子结构：特殊疑问句 Who and what was Buckmaster Gilloon；一般疑问句 Is it possible for one enigma to be attracted and motivated by another enigma；一般疑问句 Can that which seems natural and coincidental be the result instead of preternatural forces。
- 关键词：**enigma**（谜）；**preternatural**（超自然的）
- 表达方式：**典狱长的追问**。他一生都在思考这些问题。
- 为什么这样写：**这是全篇的终极追问**。Gilloon 是一个谜——但"谜"本身也是一个谜。这个故事没有给出答案，只留下问题。

## 一句话总结

**死囚 Teasdale 用丝气球伪装成尸体，在暴雨中从监狱大门走出——而神秘的 Gilloon 看穿了一切，但他自己是谁，至今仍是谜。**
