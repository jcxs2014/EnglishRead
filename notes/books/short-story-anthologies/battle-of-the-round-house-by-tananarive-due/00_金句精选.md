# 金句精选

> 引语均逐字取自原文，章节标注为该句所在章节。

① "Now, everyone talks about the colored Nicodemus settlement in Kansas after Emancipation. I don’t begrudge those good folks their glory, but years before Nicodemus was dreamt of, more than a dozen new freemen started a settlement called Zipporah Ranch—it was named after Moses’s wife in the Bible."（ch01）

② "Old-timers in Dickinson County might still remember how the mob of Texans who chased a colored family to Zipporah Ranch were never seen again. But that’s not the whole story neither. The account I want you to record on your machine is about how a settlement of mostly colored women and children defended itself against that same mob during the night—and how we all surely would have lost our lives if not for a little magic."（ch01）

③ "Where y’all headed?" the marshal said. "Ain’t safe for families here during the drives. Town’s swarming with Texans with their herds, their wages spent on whiskey."（ch02）

④ "More’n likely," the marshal agreed. "And truth be told, y’all remind me of dozens of families just like you that came through my family’s basement before the war trying to get north. I lost my pappy when I was fifteen, but while my sweet mother still lives, it would make her smile to know I’m doing more than busting up bar fights and shooting strays in Abilene."（ch02）

⑤ "For Lucy, what she called “whispering” was far more than thinking with ferocious focus: It was as if her mind slipped into an oiled groove in another creature’s head, like holding a beating heart in her palm."（ch02）

⑥ "There!" Papa shouted, pointing. "Those are the rocks we’re heading for. My brother said to look for the Twins. He calls ’em Jacob and Esau."（ch02）

⑦ "You ain’t lost—you’re found!" she said. "Stay put. We been expectin’ you. Someone will come to fetch you."（ch02）

⑧ "And when I tell you we had never seen the likes of Zipporah! It was its own world hidden from the known world, situated in a valley tucked out of sight of the surrounding plains."（ch03）

⑨ "The Boss Bulls!" the rider called to them. "These are our warrior bulls."（ch03）

⑩ "There’s no greater blessing on this earth than family," Auntie Zee said, speaking loudly enough for the entire hushed dining hall to hear.（ch03）

⑪ "Watch ’em, watch ’em!" Auntie Zee said, still their steadfast general. "Keep ’em pinned left on the path! Don’t let ’em get near the door or out to the homesteads! Don’t give ’em nowhere to hide!"（ch03）

⑫ "Leave us alone!" Lucy shouted.（ch03）

⑬ "A clear woman’s voice rose with song in the night, as melodic as a nightingale in a fairy tale. Lucy couldn’t see the singer in the dark, but she recognized the voice of Binta, the young woman who had led them inside on her bull. Binta’s voice was both in the air and like soft breath in Lucy’s ears, exactly like one of her whispers."（ch03）

⑭ "But on that morning after the battle, Lucy could only smile."（ch03）

⑮ "Some superstetious [sic] folks in Abilene tell storys [sic] about a rich colored settlement where those menn [sic] met their end at the hands of witches. On the matter of witches nothing need be said. We both know that Kansas has colored settlers but they do not have the means to fight off such a deadly gang. And they could no more vanish in thin air then [sic] you or me."（ch04）

⑯ "[p. 17] . . . lost steam after Marshal Hickok’s letter to the judge, so people stopped looking for Papa and that gang to boot."（ch04）

⑰ "Zipporah thrived for ten years after those bandits chased us, but as Kansas developed and more homesteaders moved out to claim land, it became impossible to keep our ranch hidden—so the Elders decided to sell our land and move on to a new location."（ch04）

⑱ "Didn’t you hear what I just said? Sorry, but I can’t say where they are out loud for your machine to hear. But if you listen real close with the quietest part of your mind, I’ll tell you and only you . . ."（ch04）
