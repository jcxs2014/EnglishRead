# Astonishing! 情感节点（10 节点）

> **节点 1：权力的诱惑（ch63 THE LEECH）**
- **叙事概括**：Cranborne 的发明让他能够控制一切，但他最信任的员工 Skelly 向他开枪——Jameson 展示了当一个人获得无限权力时，最亲近的人会成为最危险的敌人。
- **引语 1**："He had need of only one invention; thereafter, any invention, any wealth, any action he wanted carried out was his to command—"
- **引语 2**："It is always disconcerting to be shot at unexpectedly, but especially when the shooter is a trusted employee and friend."
- **引语 3**："There was also the impression that somehow Skelly managed to pull his punch at the very instant of firing."

> **节点 2：官僚主义的荒谬（ch65 THE ANARCH / ch66 THE BUREAUCRAT）**
- **叙事概括**：从极权主义的逻辑到海军繁文缛节，Jameson 展示了官僚制度如何将人变成规则的奴隶，同时又让真正的领导者找到绕过它们的方式。
- **引语 1**："The ideal of totalitarianism is the elimination of all individual initiative."
- **引语 2**："Bullard was a Grand Admiral now, and the red tape of high position bound him—they thought!—so tight he couldn't do a friend a favor."
- **引语 3**："In the Pollux, Bullard, keeping fit is as important as anything else you do."

> **节点 3：时间的陷阱（ch59 BLIND ALLEY / ch21 DOUBLED AND REDOUBLED）**
- **叙事概括**：渴望回到"好旧时光"的 Mr. Feathersmith 和被困在时间循环中的主人公都展示了 Jameson 对时间旅行黑暗面的探索——回到过去往往是一条死胡同。
- **引语 1**："He was rich and old, and he longed for the good old days, and the good old ways at his youth."
- **引语 2**："Nothing was further from Mr. Feathersmith's mind than dealings with streamlined, mid-twentieth-century witches or dickering with the Devil."
- **引语 3**："Malcolm Jameson's story 'Doubled and Redoubled' may be the earliest work of fiction to feature a time loop."

> **节点 4：友谊与背叛（ch57 THE GIFTIE GIEN / ch63 THE LEECH）**
- **叙事概括**：Chisholm 观察他的销售人员，但他的秘书 Maizie 也在观察他——Jameson 展示了在工作场所中，观察者和被观察者的关系是双向的，权力取决于谁在看谁。
- **引语 1**："How do others see you—what queer distortions would a bootblack, to whom a man is a pair of dusty shoes, or a barber, to whom he is a head, mostly bald, see of a man—"
- **引语 2**："Mr. J.C. Chisholm, sales manager of the Pinnacle Office & Household Appliance Corp., folded his pudgy hands across his ample middle and sat back in his chair to watch the daily ritual going on beyond the clear-glass partition."
- **引语 3**："She knew that he would look at her from time to time to get her reaction, but she was ready for that."

> **节点 5：科学的双刃剑（ch51 EUREKA / ch63 THE LEECH / ch56 DOWNFALL）**
- **叙事概括**：从 Mad Scientist 的"伟大发现"到内爆弹的"脆弱"外表，Jameson 探索了科学发现如何既带来希望又带来危险——同样的发明可以拯救生命也可以摧毁生命。
- **引语 1**："'All right,' said Professor Gleason, and hung up the phone, 'The Mad Scientist is on the way over,' he told his lab director, Max Sunderberg. 'Says he has made a great discovery.'"
- **引语 2**："'Well, I couldn't let myself go so completely,' the voice defended."（ch47 PRIDE）
- **引语 3**："We used an Earliwell implosion bomb—it was a fragile-looking thing, that bomb, something like a bubble dancer's balloon."

> **节点 6：领导力的本质（ch66 THE BUREAUCRAT / ch74 ORDERS）**
- **叙事概括**：Bullard 从"世界上最好的拇指转动者"成长为理解"好船"真正含义的指挥官——Jameson 展示了领导力不是关于权威，而是关于建立让人们想要为之奋斗的团队精神。
- **引语 1**："It is the difference between the spirit exhibited on this snappy cruiser of the first line and that on the obsolescent reserve mine-layer he had just left."
- **引语 2**："The discipline was excellent—but invisible. One could almost term it voluntary."
- **引语 3**："All that together made for that prime essential of a 'good' ship—esprit de corps—something a man could work for, fight for, die for."

> **节点 7：外星人与人性（ch67 HOBO GOD / ch69 ALIEN ENVOY）**
- **叙事概括**：从完美的火星大使（一个无家可归的人）到完全不同于人类的外星人，Jameson 探索了在面对真正他者时，什么构成了"合适"和"理解"。
- **引语 1**："It wasn't their idea, but they stumbled on the perfect ambassador to the Martians. He had a way of thinking and acting that Martians understood—"
- **引语 2**："Those aliens were really alien. As totally different from man as is a cactus plant."
- **引语 3**："Would that difference lead to inevitable extermination tear—or complete lack of conflict?"

> **节点 8：怀旧与失去（ch59 BLIND ALLEY / ch50 THE GODDESS' LEGACY）**
- **叙事概括**：Mr. Feathersmith 渴望回到他年轻时的"好旧时光"，但在希腊，古代的遗产成为了抵抗的武器——Jameson 展示了怀旧可以是一种力量，也可以是一种陷阱。
- **引语 1**："There was one thing in Greece the Gestapo could not conquer—one legacy of ancient days that took them, one by one—"
- **引语 2**："I would have thought that Mike would let himself be torn apart by wild horses before trafficking with the enemy."
- **引语 3**："The fierce old patriot must have broken under the strain of sustained tyranny."

> **节点 9：智慧与存在（ch73 BRAINS FOR BRICKS / ch47 PRIDE）**
- **叙事概括**：从蚂蚁的集体智慧到机器人的骄傲，Jameson 探索了智慧的多重形式——以及当智慧与身体分离时会发生什么。
- **引语 1**："Given time enough, ants learn, even though, individually, they are almost brainless. They might—or a type like them might—rule a world, in time."
- **引语 2**："But men have something worth while, too; brains have their advantages."
- **引语 3**："Old Tom, being a robot, couldn't have any descendants, yet, as must to every robot, there came a time when he could no longer function himself."

> **节点 10：女性的意志力（ch60 III / ch42 THE OLD ONES HEAR）**
- **叙事概括**：在 Jameson 以男性主角为主的宇宙中，"Heaven Is What You Make It"的女性主角是一个显著例外——她决心在战斗中战斗，死了之后还决心让天堂适合她——Jameson 展示了意志力可以超越死亡。
- **引语 1**："She was a very determined woman. She was determined to tight in battle—and did. and died."
- **引语 2**："And thereafter—she was determined to make the heaven she was misdirected to suit her—"
- **引语 3**："The Old Ones may hear—and act, too—in somewhat different manner, in ways we do not expect."
