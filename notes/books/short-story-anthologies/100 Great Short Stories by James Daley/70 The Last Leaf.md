# 70. The Last Leaf

**作者**：O. Henry

## 一句话总结

O. Henry 写病中 Johnsy 数窗外藤上残叶、自认叶落己亡；老画家 Behrman 雨夜画一叶不落，Johnsy 因之回生，Behrman 却肺炎死——那叶是他"杰作"。一则把"假叶真救"写成牺牲的寓言，结尾以"杰作"收束。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"In a little district west of Washington Square the streets have run crazy and broken themselves into small strips called 'places.' These 'places' make strange angles and curves."**

**中文理解**：华盛顿广场西一小片，街跑疯了、碎成唤"place"的小条；这些"place"成怪角曲。

**句子结构**：In a little district west of Washington Square 状语；the streets have run crazy 主谓；and broken themselves into small strips 并列；called 'places' 定语。These 'places' make strange angles and curves 主谓宾。

**关键词**：a little district west of Washington Square（广场西小片）/ the streets have run crazy（街跑疯）/ broken into small strips called 'places'（碎成 place 小条）/ make strange angles and curves（成怪角曲）

**表达方式**：用"街跑疯碎成 place"写 Greenwich 村的歪扭可爱，开场即地景。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此，让故事的怪味先在街里。

---

② **"But Johnsy he smote; and she lay, scarcely moving, on her painted iron bedstead, looking through the small Dutch window-panes at the blank side of the next brick house."**

**中文理解**：然 Johnsy 被击；她卧彩铁床上，几不动，透小荷兰窗格望邻砖墙的空白面。

**句子结构**：But Johnsy he smote 主谓；and she lay 并列；scarcely moving 状语；on her painted iron bedstead 状语；looking through the small Dutch window-panes 现在分词；at the blank side of the next brick house 状语。

**关键词**：Johnsy he smote（击 Johnsy）/ lay scarcely moving（卧几不动）/ her painted iron bedstead（彩铁床）/ the small Dutch window-panes（小荷兰窗格）/ the blank side of the next brick house（邻砖墙空白面）

**表达方式**：用"望空白砖墙"写病中 Johnsy 的空，伏笔数叶。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此，让死意先在凝滞的望里。

---

③ **"Then she swaggered into Johnsy's room with her drawing board, whistling ragtime."**

**中文理解**：遂她摇进 Johnsy 房，挟画板，吹 ragtime。

**句子结构**：Then she swaggered into Johnsy's room 主谓；with her drawing board 状语；whistling ragtime 现在分词。

**关键词**：swaggered into Johnsy's room（摇进 Johnsy 房）/ with her drawing board（挟画板）/ whistling ragtime（吹 ragtime）

**表达方式**：用"摇进+吹 ragtime"写 Sue 的活气，反衬病房的沉。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此，让友的暖先破寂。

---

④ **"Didn't the doctor tell you?" "Oh, I never heard of such nonsense," complained Sue, with magnificent scorn.**

**中文理解**："医生没告诉你？""哦，从没听过这等胡说，"Sue 以盛鄙怨道。

**句子结构**：Didn't the doctor tell you 疑问；Oh, I never heard of such nonsense 引语；complained Sue 倒装；with magnificent scorn 状语。

**关键词**：Didn't the doctor tell you（医生没告诉你）/ such nonsense（这等胡说）/ complained Sue, with magnificent scorn（Sue 盛鄙怨道）

**表达方式**：用"Sue 盛鄙"写她对"数叶等死"的不屑，护友的硬。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此，让理性的暖挡在迷信前。

---

⑤ **"I must call Behrman up to be my model for the old hermit miner."**

**中文理解**："我得叫 Behrman 来，做我那老隐矿工的模特。"

**句子结构**：I must call Behrman up 主谓宾；to be my model for the old hermit miner 目的。

**关键词**：call Behrman up（叫 Behrman）/ to be my model（做模特）/ the old hermit miner（老隐矿工）

**表达方式**：用"叫 Behrman 做模特"写伏笔，老画师将入局。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此，让牺牲者在闲话里先被邀来。

---

⑥ **"In one corner was a blank canvas on an easel that had been waiting there for twenty-five years to receive the first line of the masterpiece."**

**中文理解**：一角有空画布在架，候二十五年，待接杰作首笔。

**句子结构**：In one corner was a blank canvas 倒装；on an easel 定语；that had been waiting there for twenty-five years 定语；to receive the first line of the masterpiece 目的。

**关键词**：a blank canvas on an easel（架上的空画布）/ waiting there for twenty-five years（候二十五年）/ the first line of the masterpiece（杰作首笔）

**表达方式**：用"候二十五年的空布"写 Behrman 未竟的梦，伏笔将落。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此，让"杰作"在等待里先沉。

---

⑦ **"Some day I vill baint a masterpiece, and ve shall all go away."**

**中文理解**："终有一日我要画一杰作，咱们都走。"

**句子结构**：Some day I vill baint a masterpiece 主谓宾；and ve shall all go away 并列（Behrman 口音）。

**关键词**：Some day（终有一日）/ I vill baint a masterpiece（我要画杰作）/ ve shall all go away（咱们都走）

**表达方式**：用 Behrman 的口音"baint/ve"写其 immigrant 憨，人物可亲。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此，让老画师在腔里显真。

---

⑧ **"I thought it would surely fall during the night."**

**中文理解**："我料它夜必落。"

**句子结构**：I thought 主谓；it would surely fall during the night 宾从。

**关键词**：I thought（我料）/ surely fall during the night（夜必落）

**表达方式**：用"夜必落"写 Johnsy 的等死，数与命绑。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此，让悬念在"落否"里悬。

---

⑨ **"And now I must see another case I have downstairs."**

**中文理解**："现我得看楼下另一例。"

**句子结构**：And now I must see another case 主谓宾；I have downstairs 定语。

**关键词**：must see another case（得看另一例）/ downstairs（楼下）

**表达方式**：用"看楼下例"写医生的忙，暗指 Behrman 病，伏笔。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此，让转折在医者的淡里埋。

---

⑩ **"Ah, darling, it's Behrman's masterpiece—he painted it there the night that the last leaf fell."**

**中文理解**："啊亲爱的，那是 Behrman 的杰作——末叶落那夜，他画于彼。"

**句子结构**：Ah, darling 呼语；it's Behrman's masterpiece 主系表；he painted it there 并列；the night that the last leaf fell 状语。

**关键词**：it's Behrman's masterpiece（Behrman 杰作）/ painted it there（画于彼）/ the night that the last leaf fell（末叶落那夜）

**表达方式**：用"末叶落夜画于彼"点破假叶真画，牺牲揭。

**为什么这样写**：O. Henry 以此收尾，让"杰作"在死里成真。

## 一句话总结

O. Henry 写 Johnsy 数藤叶自认叶落己亡，老画师 Behrman 雨夜画一不落之叶、Johnsy 因之回生，己却肺炎死——那叶是他候二十五年的"杰作"，假叶真救，是牺牲最静的寓言。
