# 74. Germans at Meat

**作者**：Katherine Fullerton Gerould

## 一句话总结

Gerould 以第一人称女客在德国旅舍餐桌旁，冷观一桌德国人饕餐：从汤到膝浴的仪式、对"家庭""艺术"的 booming 自许，到结尾"女人不知夫所爱食三年如何留夫"的谬——一则把民族脾性写成餐桌讽刺画的短篇，收束于 "Mahlzeit" 的关门。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"Ah," said the Herr Rat, leaning upon the table as he peered into the tureen, "that is what I need."**

**中文理解**："啊，"Rat 先生倚桌窥汤盆道，"这正是我要的。"

**句子结构**：Ah 独词；said the Herr Rat 倒装；leaning upon the table 现在分词；as he peered into the tureen 时间；that is what I need 引语。

**关键词**：said the Herr Rat（Rat 先生道）/ leaning upon the table（倚桌）/ peered into the tureen（窥汤盆）/ that is what I need（正是我要的）

**表达方式**：用"倚桌窥汤"写德客对食的郑重，人物先立。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此，让饕餐的仪式在窥汤里起。

---

② **"I felt I was bearing the burden of the nation's preposterous breakfast—I who drank a cup of coffee while buttoning my blouse in the morning."**

**中文理解**：我觉自扛这国荒唐早餐的重——我，晨间扣褂只喝一杯咖啡者。

**句子结构**：I felt I was bearing the burden 主谓宾从；of the nation's preposterous breakfast 定语；I who drank a cup of coffee 同位；while buttoning my blouse 时间。

**关键词**：bearing the burden of the nation's preposterous breakfast（扛国荒唐早餐重）/ a cup of coffee（一杯咖啡）/ buttoning my blouse（扣褂）

**表达方式**：用"我喝咖啡 vs 国荒唐早餐"的对照，写叙述者的轻蔑自嘲。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此，让讽刺的眼先落在自己身上再及人。

---

③ **"It was a good hotel, but they could not make tea—now—" "Ah, that's one thing I can do," said I, laughing brightly.**

**中文理解**："好旅馆，却煮不得茶——""啊，这我会，"我朗笑道。

**句子结构**：It was a good hotel 主系表；but they could not make tea 转折。Ah, that's one thing I can do 引语；said I 倒装；laughing brightly 现在分词。

**关键词**：a good hotel（好旅馆）/ could not make tea（煮不得茶）/ that's one thing I can do（这我会）/ laughing brightly（朗笑）

**表达方式**：用"我会煮茶朗笑"写叙述者以炊艺自傲，反衬德客的拙。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此，让文化优劣在茶里轻挑。

---

④ **"I am immediately forced to—" "A beautiful day," I cried, turning to Fräulein Stiegelauer.**

**中文理解**："我立被迫——""好天，"我转对 Stiegelauer 女史喊。

**句子结构**：I am immediately forced to 主谓（截断）；A beautiful day 引语；I cried 倒装；turning to Fräulein Stiegelauer 现在分词。

**关键词**：immediately forced to（立被迫）/ A beautiful day（好天）/ turning to Fräulein Stiegelauer（转对 Stiegelauer 女史）

**表达方式**：用"截语+转夸天"写叙述者故意岔开，叙事的巧避。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此，让餐桌对话的虚在岔里显。

---

⑤ **"Who ever heard of having children upon vegetables?"**

**中文理解**："谁听过靠菜养孩？"

**句子结构**：Who ever heard of having children upon vegetables 疑问。

**关键词**：Who ever heard of（谁听过）/ having children upon vegetables（靠菜养孩）

**表达方式**：用"靠菜养孩"的质疑，写德客对蔬食的鄙，脾性的偏。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此，让民族性在食论里被夸张。

---

⑥ **"Of course she was very proud." "Germany," boomed the Traveller, biting round a potato which he had speared with his knife, "is the home of the Family." Followed an appreciative silence.**

**中文理解**："她自傲。""德国，"旅人边啃刀叉的土豆边 booming，"是家庭之乡。"随一阵领会的静。

**句子结构**：Of course she was very proud 主系表。Germany boomed the Traveller 倒装；biting round a potato 现在分词；which he had speared with his knife 定语；is the home of the Family 引语。Followed an appreciative silence 倒装。

**关键词**：very proud（自傲）/ Germany boomed the Traveller（旅人 booming 德国）/ biting round a potato speared with his knife（啃刀叉土豆）/ the home of the Family（家庭之乡）/ an appreciative silence（领会的静）

**表达方式**：用"booming+啃土豆"写德客的宏声与饕并置，讽意最浓。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此，让民族自许在吃相里成画。

---

⑦ **"All the Exhibitions, all the Art and Soul life of Germany are in München."**

**中文理解**："一切博览会、德之艺与魂，皆在 München。"

**句子结构**：All the Exhibitions 主语；all the Art and Soul life of Germany 并列；are in München 系表。

**关键词**：All the Exhibitions（一切博览会）/ all the Art and Soul life of Germany（德之艺与魂）/ are in München（皆在 München）

**表达方式**：用"艺与魂皆在 München"写德客的文化自豪，自许的扩。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此，让讽刺从食延到艺。

---

⑧ **"Ah, fruit!" said Fräulein Stiegelauer, "that is so necessary to health."**

**中文理解**："啊，果！"Stiegelauer 女史道，"于健康至要。"

**句子结构**：Ah, fruit 独词；said Fräulein Stiegelauer 倒装；that is so necessary to health 引语。

**关键词**：Ah, fruit（啊果）/ Fräulein Stiegelauer（Stiegelauer 女史）/ so necessary to health（于健康至要）

**表达方式**：用"果于健康至要"写女史的健康执，餐桌小信仰。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此，让群像各有其痴。

---

⑨ **"Then this afternoon I must take a knee bath and an arm bath," volunteered the Herr Rat; "then I do my exercises for an hour, and my work is over."**

**中文理解**："则今午后我得膝浴臂浴，"Rat 先生自荐，"再练一时，工毕。"

**句子结构**：Then this afternoon I must take a knee bath and an arm bath 主谓宾；volunteered the Herr Rat 倒装；then I do my exercises for an hour 并列；and my work is over 并列。

**关键词**：take a knee bath and an arm bath（膝浴臂浴）/ volunteered the Herr Rat（Rat 先生自荐）/ do my exercises for an hour（练一时）/ my work is over（工毕）

**表达方式**：用"膝浴臂浴+练一时"写德客的养生秩序，把日子排成仪。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此，让民族的条理在浴里显。

---

⑩ **"How can a woman expect to keep her husband if she does not know his favourite food after three years?" "Mahlzeit!" "Mahlzeit!" I closed the door after me.**

**中文理解**："女若三年不知夫所爱食，怎留夫？""Mahlzeit！""Mahlzeit！"我随手关门。

**句子结构**：How can a woman expect to keep her husband 疑问；if she does not know his favourite food after three years 条件。Mahlzeit 引语（截）；Mahlzeit 应；I closed the door after me 主谓。

**关键词**：expect to keep her husband（留夫）/ know his favourite food after three years（三年知所爱食）/ Mahlzeit（德餐间祝/好胃口）/ closed the door after me（随手关门）

**表达方式**：用"三年不知所爱食怎留夫"写餐桌谬论，把婚姻缩成食；"Mahlzeit" 的重复+关门写叙述者抽身离场。

**为什么这样写**：Gerould 以此收尾，让讽刺在"Mahlzeit"的关门声里定格，民族脾性成一幅可退场的画。

## 一句话总结

Gerould 以女客冷观一桌德国人饕餐：从窥汤、booming 的"家庭之乡"、到"三年不知夫所爱食怎留夫"的谬论，把民族脾性写成餐桌讽刺画；结尾两声 "Mahlzeit" 与关门，叙述者抽身离场，讽成一幅可退的画。
