# 60. With Other Eyes

**作者**：Luigi Pirandello

## 一句话总结

Pirandello 写 Anna 借窗对望邻屋画像，由"想象中的情敌"生妒，却在凝视真容时转为怜悯与自省——一则把"以他者之眼观己生"写成身份错位的短篇，结尾那死去的女人以怜悯的眼神回望她。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"An almond branch, which seemed to be all a-blossom with butterflies, projected toward the window; and, mingled with the hoarse, muffled gurgle of the small basin in the center of the garden, was heard the cooing of a dove."**

**中文理解**：一枝杏，似满缀蝶般的花，探向窗；与园心小池沙哑闷响相混的，是鸽的咕声。

**句子结构**：An almond branch 主语；which seemed to be all a-blossom with butterflies 定语；projected toward the window 谓；and mingled with the hoarse gurgle 并列；was heard the cooing of a dove 倒装。

**关键词**：An almond branch（一枝杏）/ all a-blossom with butterflies（满缀蝶花）/ projected toward the window（探向窗）/ the hoarse, muffled gurgle（沙哑闷响）/ the cooing of a dove（鸽咕）

**表达方式**：用"杏花如蝶+鸽咕混水声"写窗景的柔腻，把旁观的起点安在美里。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此，让"看"先被诗意喂养。

---

② **"But as she pulled, instead of the drawer coming out, an insidious gleaming blade emerged smoothly from inside the stick."**

**中文理解**：可她一拉，抽屉没出，一截阴险的闪刃却从杖内滑出。

**句子结构**：But as she pulled 时间；instead of the drawer coming out 状语；an insidious gleaming blade emerged 主谓；smoothly from inside the stick 状语。

**关键词**：as she pulled（她一拉）/ instead of the drawer coming out（抽屉没出）/ an insidious gleaming blade（阴险闪刃）/ emerged smoothly（滑出）/ from inside the stick（杖内）

**表达方式**：用"杖出刃"的惊转，把闲物写成潜伏的凶，喻"想象之刃"。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此，让嫉妒如暗刃突现。

---

③ **"She found the proof in the dedication written on the back of the portrait: 'To my Vittore, his Almira—November 11, 1873.'"**

**中文理解**：她在画像背的题献里找到证："致我的 Vittore，他的 Almira——1873 年 11 月 11 日。"

**句子结构**：She found the proof 主谓宾；in the dedication written on the back of the portrait 状语；To my Vittore, his Almira 同位引语；November 11, 1873 日期。

**关键词**：found the proof（找到证）/ the dedication written on the back（背上的题献）/ To my Vittore, his Almira（致 Vittore 他的 Almira）/ November 11, 1873（1873-11-11）

**表达方式**：用"题献"写证据的具体，把妒的根系到真名。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此，让想象中的情敌被指认成 Almira。

---

④ **"Every time you come into this room to straighten up, you rearrange everything."**

**中文理解**："你每次进这屋收拾，就把一切重摆。"

**句子结构**：Every time you come into this room 时间；to straighten up 目的；you rearrange everything 主谓宾。

**关键词**：Every time you come into this room（每次进屋）/ to straighten up（收拾）/ you rearrange everything（重摆一切）

**表达方式**：用一句嗔怪，写丈夫对妻子"重摆"的抗拒，琐碎里藏控制。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此，让日常的权力在摆物里露头。

---

⑤ **"Come on now, come on now, that's so childish!" And he left in hot haste, without even saying goodbye."**

**中文理解**："得了得了，这太孩子气！"他便匆匆离去，连再见也没说。

**句子结构**：Come on now 祈使；that's so childish 主系表。And he left in hot haste 主谓；without even saying goodbye 状语。

**关键词**：Come on now（得了得了）/ that's so childish（太孩子气）/ left in hot haste（匆匆离去）/ without saying goodbye（没说再见）

**表达方式**：用"得了+匆匆去"写对话的崩，把冲突推到门外。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此，让婚姻的裂在一句不耐里显。

---

⑥ **"As soon as she lay down, she closed her eyes and forced herself to follow her husband mentally along the road leading to the railroad station."**

**中文理解**：她一躺下便闭眼，逼自己在心里随丈夫走向车站的路。

**句子结构**：As soon as she lay down 时间；she closed her eyes 主谓宾；and forced herself to follow her husband mentally 并列；along the road leading to the railroad station 定语。

**关键词**：As soon as she lay down（一躺下）/ closed her eyes（闭眼）/ forced herself to follow（逼自己随）/ her husband mentally（心里随丈夫）/ leading to the railroad station（通车站）

**表达方式**：用"闭眼心随"写妻子在想象里追踪丈夫，妒成执念。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此，让"他者之眼"先落在丈夫身上。

---

⑦ **"Now, contemplating her real likeness, she felt remorse for the feelings that the imaginary woman had aroused in her."**

**中文理解**：此刻凝视真容，她为自己被那想象中的女人唤起的感觉而生悔。

**句子结构**：Now, contemplating her real likeness 状语；she felt remorse 主谓宾；for the feelings that the imaginary woman had aroused 定语。

**关键词**：contemplating her real likeness（凝视真容）/ felt remorse（生悔）/ the feelings the imaginary woman had aroused（想象中女人唤起的感觉）

**表达方式**：用"对真容生悔"写嫉妒的转向，自省开始。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此，让"他者"反成照见自己的镜。

---

⑧ **"She knew that he had married a few years later as if to prove his innocence of the blame that Vittore wanted to ascribe to him, that he had vigorously declined Vittore's challenge to a duel, protesting—"**

**中文理解**：她知他数年后续娶，似为证自己对 Vittore 欲加的责之清白；知他严拒 Vittore 的决斗之邀，辩称——

**句子结构**：She knew that he had married a few years later 主谓宾从；as if to prove his innocence 状语；of the blame that Vittore wanted to ascribe 定语；that he had vigorously declined Vittore's challenge 并列宾从；protesting 现在分词。

**关键词**：married a few years later（数年后续娶）/ prove his innocence（证清白）/ the blame Vittore wanted to ascribe（Vittore 欲加之责）/ vigorously declined the challenge to a duel（严拒决斗邀）

**表达方式**：用"续娶证清白+拒决斗"写丈夫的旧史，把妒的来由补齐。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此，让叙事在回溯里自愈。

---

⑨ **"It was something totally different that Anna, now for the first time, looking into her life (without even realizing it) with the eyes of that dead woman depicted there in the portrait on the bedside table, perceived."**

**中文理解**：Anna 此刻头一回，用那死女人在床头画像里的眼（浑然不觉）望进自己生命，所觉的，全然是另一回事。

**句子结构**：It was something totally different that Anna perceived 强调句；now for the first time 状语；looking into her life 现在分词；with the eyes of that dead woman 状语；depicted there in the portrait 定语；on the bedside table 状语。

**关键词**：something totally different（全然另一回事）/ for the first time（头一回）/ looking into her life（望进生命）/ with the eyes of that dead woman（用那死女人之眼）/ the portrait on the bedside table（床头画像）

**表达方式**：用"以死女之眼望己生"写身份反客为主，主题核心句。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此，让"他者之眼"完成对自我的置换。

---

⑩ **"And it then seemed to her that those kindly eyes, intense with passion and heartbreak, were pitying her in their turn, were condoling with her over that abandonment, that unrequited sacrifice, that long, barren waiting."**

**中文理解**：于是她觉那慈眼的眸——燃着 passion 与心碎——反在怜她，慰她那遗弃、那无酬的牺牲、那漫长荒等。

**句子结构**：And it then seemed to her 主句；that those kindly eyes were pitying her 主语从；intense with passion and heartbreak 定语；were condoling with her 并列；over that abandonment, that unrequited sacrifice, that long barren waiting 状语。

**关键词**：those kindly eyes（那慈眼）/ intense with passion and heartbreak（燃 passion 与心碎）/ pitying her in their turn（反怜她）/ condoling with her（慰她）/ that unrequited sacrifice（无酬牺牲）/ that long, barren waiting（漫长荒等）

**表达方式**：用"死女反怜活女"写视角彻底倒转，怜悯回流。

**为什么这样写**：Pirandello 以此收尾，让错位的眼在慈悲里和解。

## 一句话总结

Pirandello 写 Anna 由想象中的情敌 Almira 生妒，却在凝视画像真容时以"死女之眼"回望己生，终被那燃着 passion 与心碎的慈眼怜悯——"他者之眼"照见的，原是自己被荒等的牺牲。
