# 58. How Old Timofei Died with a Song

**作者**：Maxim Gorky

## 一句话总结

Gorky 写全村会唱的老歌随人死而濒临失传——无嗣的 Timofei 不能唱全父祖的歌，作家读故事给他听，他含笑而逝；秋天风狂，无人辨屋中是否还有歌。一则把"歌=血脉记忆"写成濒死的乡愁，结尾以风与孤死收束。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"Healthy people are so unreliable; they look at things, now from one viewpoint, now from another, and after you've been walking with them for an hour and they've always been to the right of you, they suddenly veer to the left."**

**中文理解**：健康人真不可靠；他们看事忽此忽彼，你同行一小时、他总在你右，忽又拐向左。

**句子结构**：Healthy people are so unreliable 主系表；they look at things 主谓宾；now from one viewpoint, now from another 状语；and after you've been walking with them 时间；they've always been to the right of you 并列；they suddenly veer to the left 并列。

**关键词**：Healthy people are so unreliable（健康人不可靠）/ look at things now from one viewpoint（看事忽此忽彼）/ to the right of you（在你右）/ veer to the left（拐向左）

**表达方式**：用"健康人不可靠"的悖论开场，把叙述者的视角先摆歪。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让"不可靠"的反成可靠的观察基调。

---

② **"A hundred years ago it was still alive, and certainly quite carefree, on many lips."**

**中文理解**：百年前它仍在许多唇上活着，确也无忧。

**句子结构**：A hundred years ago it was still alive 主系表；and certainly quite carefree 并列；on many lips 状语。

**关键词**：A hundred years ago（百年前）/ still alive（仍活着）/ quite carefree（无忧）/ on many lips（在许多唇上）

**表达方式**：用"百年前活在许多唇上"写老歌曾经的繁盛。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让失传的痛先有丰年的对照。

---

③ **"The man without inheritance differed from his siblings who had received their rightful due in that he couldn't sing, or else at least he knew only a small part of his father's and grandfather's songs, and this tormented him."**

**中文理解**：无嗣者不同于承了应得的其他兄弟：他不能唱，或至多只知父祖歌的一小段，这折磨着他。

**句子结构**：The man without inheritance differed from his siblings 主谓宾；who had received their rightful due 定语；in that he couldn't sing 状语；or else at least he knew only a small part 并列；of his father's and grandfather's songs 定语；and this tormented him 并列。

**关键词**：The man without inheritance（无嗣者）/ differed from his siblings（不同于兄弟）/ received their rightful due（承应得）/ couldn't sing（不能唱）/ a small part of his father's songs（父歌一小段）/ tormented him（折磨他）

**表达方式**：用"无嗣者不能唱全父祖歌"写血脉记忆的断代。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让"歌"等于可传的魂。

---

④ **"He would gladly have done what the lads wanted; he himself was sorry that his mute, dead dust would lie upon these songs, perhaps very soon now."**

**中文理解**：他本乐意从年轻人所愿；自己却憾那哑默的死尘将压在这些歌上，或许就快了。

**句子结构**：He would gladly have done what the lads wanted 主谓宾；he himself was sorry 并列；that his mute, dead dust would lie upon these songs 宾从；perhaps very soon now 状语。

**关键词**：gladly have done what the lads wanted（乐意从年轻人所愿）/ was sorry（憾）/ his mute, dead dust（哑默死尘）/ lie upon these songs（压在歌上）/ very soon now（就快了）

**表达方式**：用"死尘压歌"的意象，写歌随人亡的宿命感。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让死与失传绑成一体。

---

⑤ **"Until, after a violent scene with the old man, the boy had vanished together with the beautiful Ustyonka, the daughter of a poor peasant."**

**中文理解**：直到与老汉一场冲突后，那少年同贫农之女、美丽的 Ustyonka 一起消失。

**句子结构**：Until, after a violent scene with the old man 状语；the boy had vanished 主谓；together with the beautiful Ustyonka 状语；the daughter of a poor peasant 同位。

**关键词**：after a violent scene with the old man（与老汉冲突后）/ the boy had vanished（少年消失）/ together with the beautiful Ustyonka（同美丽 Ustyonka）/ the daughter of a poor peasant（贫农之女）

**表达方式**：用"冲突后私奔消失"写歌之外的人生插曲，叙事岔开。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让村庄记忆里也有逃离的线。

---

⑥ **"God gave you that, to another man he gives other gifts."**

**中文理解**：神给了你这个，给另一人的是别的禀赋。

**句子结构**：God gave you that 主谓宾；to another man he gives other gifts 倒装。

**关键词**：God gave you that（神给了你这个）/ to another man（给另一人）/ other gifts（别的禀赋）

**表达方式**：用一句箴言式对白，写天赋各殊、歌非人人能承。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让不可唱全成了"神意"的轻释。

---

⑦ **"Yegor spat out the words: 'I've chased her away, you know, along with the child.' He was silent for a while."**

**中文理解**：Yegor 吐出字句："我把她赶走了，你知道，连孩子一起。"他沉默片刻。

**句子结构**：Yegor spat out the words 主谓宾；I've chased her away 直接引语；along with the child 状语。He was silent for a while 主系表。

**关键词**：Yegor spat out the words（Yegor 吐出字句）/ I've chased her away（赶她走）/ along with the child（连孩子）/ silent for a while（沉默片刻）

**表达方式**：用"吐字+沉默"写 Yegor 说起逐妻儿时的硬与空。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让失歌者的生活也失了人。

---

⑧ **"But that autumn the wind blew so violently through the village that no passerby could ascertain with any assurance whether there was really singing in Timofei's house or not."**

**中文理解**：但那年秋，风在村中狂吼，过客无从确断 Timofei 屋里是否真有歌。

**句子结构**：But that autumn the wind blew so violently 主谓；through the village 状；that no passerby could ascertain 结果；with any assurance 状语；whether there was really singing 宾从。

**关键词**：that autumn the wind blew violently（那年秋风狂吼）/ through the village（在村中）/ no passerby could ascertain（过客无从断）/ with any assurance（确断）/ singing in Timofei's house（Timofei 屋里有歌）

**表达方式**：用"风狂难辨歌"写自然掩去人的尾声。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让歌的存亡在风里成了悬案。

---

⑨ **"After old Timofei Ivanich died, the house, in which Yegor now lived alone, still remained locked for a time."**

**中文理解**：老 Timofei Ivanich 死后，Yegor 独居的那屋仍锁了一阵。

**句子结构**：After old Timofei Ivanich died 时间；the house 主语；in which Yegor now lived alone 定语；still remained locked 系表；for a time 状语。

**关键词**：After old Timofei Ivanich died（老 Timofei 死后）/ the house in which Yegor lived alone（Yegor 独居的屋）/ remained locked for a time（锁了一阵）

**表达方式**：用"死后屋锁"写空的具象，歌随人去。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让失传在门锁里落定。

---

⑩ **"At any rate, when he died afterward he was alone."**

**中文理解**：无论如何，他后来死时，是独自一人。

**句子结构**：At any rate 状语；when he died afterward 时间；he was alone 主系表。

**关键词**：At any rate（无论如何）/ when he died afterward（后来死时）/ he was alone（独自一人）

**表达方式**：用"死时独一人"收束，把无嗣无歌的孤写成终局。

**为什么这样写**：Gorky 以此，让整篇的乡愁落在最轻的一句"alone"。

## 一句话总结

Gorky 写无嗣的 Timofei 不能唱全父祖的歌、作家读故事伴他含笑而逝；秋风狂吼、过客难辨屋中是否还有歌，死后屋锁、独死——"歌"作为血脉记忆，随不能唱的人一同失传。
