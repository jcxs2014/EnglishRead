# 53. The Tables of the Law

**作者**：W. B. Yeats

## 一句话总结

Yeats 以第一人称"我"与神秘主义者 Aherne 的对话，写 Aherne 藏一盒"律法之表"于 chapel，宣称开启将引来末日般的 tumult；当"我"欲阻却被拒之门外，结尾在街声里追问"你为何避我们的火炬"——一则把神秘主义、教会秘传与末世焦虑写成诗剧式短章。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"Will you permit me, Aherne," I said, "to ask you a question, which I have wanted to ask you for years, and have not asked because we have grown nearly strangers?"**

**中文理解**："Aherne，容我问你一个问题，"我说，"几年来我想问却未问——因我们已近乎陌路？"

**句子结构**：Will you permit me 疑问；Aherne 呼语；I said 插入；to ask you a question 宾从；which I have wanted to ask 定语；and have not asked 并列；because we have grown nearly strangers 原因。

**关键词**：Will you permit me（容我）/ Aherne（人名）/ to ask you a question（问你一问题）/ wanted to ask for years（想问多年）/ grown nearly strangers（近乎陌路）

**表达方式**：用"多年想问未问"的迟疑开场，把对话悬在旧谊与隔阂间。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让追问带私人重量，不止玄谈。

---

② **"He had the nature, which is half monk, half soldier of fortune, and must needs turn action into dreaming, and dreaming into action; and for such there is no order, no finality, no contentment in this world."**

**中文理解**：他有种天性——半僧半冒险家，势必把行动变梦、把梦变行动；而此类人在这世无秩序、无终局、无安顿。

**句子结构**：He had the nature 主谓宾；which is half monk, half soldier of fortune 定语；and must needs turn action into dreaming 并列；and dreaming into action 并列；and for such there is no order 并列存在句。

**关键词**：half monk, half soldier of fortune（半僧半冒险家）/ turn action into dreaming（把行动变梦）/ dreaming into action（把梦变行动）/ no order, no finality, no contentment（无秩序无终局无安顿）

**表达方式**：用"半僧半冒险家"的矛盾写 Aherne 的躁动灵魂。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让神秘主义者的不安有了性格根。

---

③ **"Almost every detail of the chapel, which we entered by a narrow Gothic door, whose threshold had been worn smooth by the secret worshippers of the penal times, was vivid in my memory; for it was in this chapel that I had my first mystic experience."**

**中文理解**：小堂几乎每处细节——我们由窄哥特门入，门槛被刑禁年代的秘拜者磨光——都在我记忆里鲜明；因我首次神秘体验便在此。

**句子结构**：Almost every detail of the chapel 主语；which we entered by a narrow Gothic door 定语；whose threshold had been worn smooth 定语；was vivid in my memory 系表；for it was in this chapel that … 原因强调。

**关键词**：every detail of the chapel（小堂每处细节）/ a narrow Gothic door（窄哥特门）/ worn smooth by secret worshippers（被秘拜者磨光）/ the penal times（刑禁年代）/ my first mystic experience（首次神秘体验）

**表达方式**：用"磨光的门槛"写秘史的层叠，把空间写成记忆的容器。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让教堂成为灵性与历史的叠印。

---

④ **"I have here the greatest treasure the world contains."**

**中文理解**：我此间藏有这世界所含的最大珍宝。

**句子结构**：I have here the greatest treasure 主谓宾；the world contains 定语。

**关键词**：the greatest treasure（最大珍宝）/ the world contains（世界所含）

**表达方式**：用"最大珍宝"的宣称，把一盒律法表升格为终极物。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让 Aherne 的秘藏带末世的份量。

---

⑤ **"Do you see the tables on which the commandments were written in Latin?" I looked to the end of the room, opposite to the altar, and saw that the two marble tablets were gone, and that two large empty frames stood there instead."**

**中文理解**："你可见写诫命的石板（拉丁文）？"我望向屋尽头、祭坛对面，见两方大理石板已失，立着两个空大框。

**句子结构**：Do you see the tables 疑问；on which the commandments were written in Latin 定语；I looked to the end of the room 主谓；opposite to the altar 状语；and saw that the two marble tablets were gone 并列；and that two large empty frames stood there 并列。

**关键词**：the tables on which the commandments（写诫命的石板）/ written in Latin（拉丁文）/ the two marble tablets were gone（两方石板已失）/ two large empty frames（两个空大框）

**表达方式**：用"石板失、空框立"写圣物的被移，伏笔 Aherne 的私藏。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让"律法"从公堂转入私盒，主题显形。

---

⑥ **"He considered that those whose work was to live and not to reveal were children and that the Pope was their father; but he taught in secret that certain others, and in always increasing numbers, were to be the revealers of the new dispensation."**

**中文理解**：他认为，那"为生而非为显"的人是孩童、教皇是其父；却暗传：另一些人——数目渐增——将是新法的显者。

**句子结构**：He considered that those … were children 主谓宾从；and that the Pope was their father 并列；but he taught in secret that certain others … were to be the revealers 转折。

**关键词**：those whose work was to live and not to reveal（为生非为显者）/ the Pope was their father（教皇是其父）/ taught in secret（暗传）/ in always increasing numbers（数目渐增）/ the revealers of the new dispensation（新法显者）

**表达方式**：用"公教孩童+秘传显者"的二分，写 Aherne 的异端神学。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让神秘主义与正统的裂隙写成教义。

---

⑦ **"The dust shall fall for many years over this little box; and then I shall open it; and the tumults which are, perhaps, the flames of the Last Day shall come from under the lid." I did not reason with him."**

**中文理解**："尘将落此小盒多年；而后我启之；那 tumult——或是末日之焰——将从盖下涌出。"我未与他辩。

**句子结构**：The dust shall fall for many years 主句；and then I shall open it 并列；and the tumults … shall come from under the lid 并列；which are the flames of the Last Day 定语。I did not reason with him 主句。

**关键词**：The dust shall fall（尘将落）/ this little box（此小盒）/ I shall open it（我启之）/ the tumults（tumult）/ the flames of the Last Day（末日之焰）/ from under the lid（从盖下）

**表达方式**：用"启盒引末日"的宣告，把私藏写成世界的开关。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让神秘主义的狂想抵达启示录的尺度。

---

⑧ **"He stood holding the door half open, as though he would keep me from entering; and would perhaps have parted from me without further words had I not said: 'Owen Aherne, you trusted me once, will you not—'"**

**中文理解**：他半开门立着，似要拦我不入；若非我说"Owen Aherne，你曾信我，难道不——"或许便无言而别。

**句子结构**：He stood holding the door half open 主谓宾补；as though he would keep me from entering 方式；and would perhaps have parted 并列；had I not said 倒装虚拟；直接引语。

**关键词**：holding the door half open（半开门立）/ keep me from entering（拦我不入）/ parted from me without further words（无言而别）/ you trusted me once（你曾信我）

**表达方式**：用"半门拦人"写 Aherne 的拒与犹疑，对话在门框处悬停。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让亲密与秘密在 doorway 上角力。

---

⑨ **"I went and stood leaning against the altar, and watched him, not knowing what I should say; and I noticed his black closely-buttoned coat, his short hair, and shaven head, which preserved a memory of the monastic."**

**中文理解**：我去倚着祭坛立，望他，不知该说什么；我注意到他黑而紧扣的外套、短发与剃头——那保留着修院的记忆。

**句子结构**：I went and stood leaning against the altar 主谓；and watched him 并列；not knowing what I should say 伴随；and I noticed his black coat 并列；his short hair, and shaven head 并列；which preserved a memory of the monastic 定语。

**关键词**：stood leaning against the altar（倚祭坛立）/ watched him（望他）/ black closely-buttoned coat（黑紧扣外套）/ shaven head（剃头）/ a memory of the monastic（修院记忆）

**表达方式**：用"黑衣剃头"的细察，写 Aherne 身上僧与人的残痕。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让人物外貌成为其灵性史的注脚。

---

⑩ **"Why do you fly from our torches that were made out of sweet wood, after it had perished from the world?" It was not until the door of the house had closed behind my flight, and the noise of the street—"**

**中文理解**："你为何避我们的火炬——那以甜木制成、久已绝于世的？"直到屋门在我逃身后合上、街声……才——

**句子结构**：Why do you fly from our torches 疑问；that were made out of sweet wood 定语；after it had perished from the world 时间。It was not until the door … had closed 强调句；and the noise of the street 并列（截断）。

**关键词**：fly from our torches（避我们的火炬）/ made out of sweet wood（甜木制成）/ perished from the world（绝于世）/ the door of the house had closed（屋门合上）/ the noise of the street（街声）

**表达方式**：用"甜木火炬"的意象与"门合街声"的收束，把追问留在逃离的瞬间。

**为什么这样写**：Yeats 以此，让诗剧在门响街声里戛止，余问悬空。

## 一句话总结

Yeats 以"我"与 Aherne 在 chapel 的对话，写一盒"律法之表"被私藏、启之将引末日之焰；当门半掩、追问悬在街声里，神秘主义与教会秘传的裂隙，成了一则未竟的诗剧。
