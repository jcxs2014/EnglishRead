# 47. Active Colorado Real Estate

**作者**：John Kendrick Bangs (humorous sketch)

## 一句话总结

Bangs 借一圈人吹嘘荒唐的"地产经历"，由 Jones 讲他在科罗拉多用炸药"平整"农场、把地炸飞、与自己母鸡一同粘在崖上的闹剧——一则把西部淘金热写成爆破喜剧的幽默短篇，结尾落进"见青年忘敬老之可悲"。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"As he finished, Peters drew a long breath, and seemed much relieved that he had got through the story without an interruption."**

**中文理解**：他讲完时，Peters 长舒一口气，似乎很欣慰自己没被打断就把故事讲完了。

**句子结构**：As he finished 时间；Peters drew a long breath 主谓宾；and seemed much relieved 并列；that he had got through the story without an interruption 宾从。

**关键词**：drew a long breath（长舒一口气）/ seemed much relieved（似乎很欣慰）/ got through the story（讲完故事）/ without an interruption（没被打断）

**表达方式**：用"舒口气"写讲故事者的紧张，把闲谈写成小型演出。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让吹牛也带表演的郑重。

---

② **"I take it that we are to consider your story simply as an exaggeration—that the fish probably didn't weigh ten pounds."**

**中文理解**：我以为，咱们把你那故事只当夸张——那鱼怕是没十磅重。

**句子结构**：I take it that we are to consider your story 主谓宾从；simply as an exaggeration 补；that the fish probably didn't weigh ten pounds 同位。

**关键词**：take it（以为）/ consider your story as an exaggeration（当夸张）/ the fish didn't weigh ten pounds（鱼没十磅）

**表达方式**：用"鱼没十磅"的拆台，写听众对吹牛的温和质疑。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让荒诞在半信半疑里推进。

---

③ **"Why did you not, Jackson, tell an artistic lie, and say that when you pulled your fish out of the water the level of the lake fell two feet?" Peters moved about uneasily, but made no reply."**

**中文理解**："Jackson，你何不讲个艺术的谎，说你把鱼提出水时湖面降了两尺？"Peters 不安地挪动，却没答。

**句子结构**：Why did you not tell an artistic lie 疑问；and say that … the level of the lake fell two feet 并列宾从；Peters moved about uneasily 主句；but made no reply 并列。

**关键词**：tell an artistic lie（讲艺术的谎）/ pulled your fish out（提鱼出）/ the level of the lake fell two feet（湖面降两尺）/ moved about uneasily（不安挪动）/ made no reply（没答）

**表达方式**：用"湖降两尺"的更夸张建议，把吹牛写成可竞赛的艺术。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让幽默在"谎的层级"上叠加。

---

④ **"For instance, last winter I went before the legislative committee on fisheries, and laid before it an account of my experience when I had a farm near Omaha, on the Missouri River bottoms, and baited t."**

**中文理解**：比方去年冬我去了渔业立法委员会，向他们陈述我的经历——那时我在 Omaha 近旁、密苏里河滩上有个农场，还……

**句子结构**：For instance 插入；last winter I went before the legislative committee 主谓状；and laid before it an account of my experience 并列；when I had a farm near Omaha 定语；on the Missouri River bottoms 状语。

**关键词**：went before the legislative committee（去立法委员会）/ laid before it an account（陈述）/ my experience（我的经历）/ a farm near Omaha（Omaha 近旁农场）/ the Missouri River bottoms（密苏里河滩）

**表达方式**：用"向委员会陈述"的正式，包装一段荒唐农事，反差生笑。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让吹牛披上公文的严肃外衣。

---

⑤ **"That is the reason I never told any one of my experience at breaking one hundred and sixty acres of land to ride."**

**中文理解**：正因如此，我从不曾对人讲起那回"犁平一百六十英亩地来骑"的经历。

**句子结构**：That is the reason 主系表；I never told any one of my experience 定语；at breaking one hundred and sixty acres of land to ride 定语。

**关键词**：That is the reason（正因如此）/ never told any one（从不对人讲）/ breaking one hundred and sixty acres of land to ride（犁平百六十英亩来骑）

**表达方式**：用"犁地来骑"的荒唐短语，把农场经历写成童话式的夸张。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让"经历"的离谱先露一角，引读者笑。

---

⑥ **"There was a great mining boom in Colorado, and I closed my defective-flue factory in Chicago, to the intense joy of the insurance companies, and went out."**

**中文理解**：科罗拉多掀起淘矿热，我便关了芝加哥那家"烟囱不合格的厂"——保险公司乐开了花——跑了去。

**句子结构**：There was a great mining boom in Colorado 存在句；and I closed my defective-flue factory 并列；to the intense joy of the insurance companies 状语；and went out 并列。

**关键词**：a great mining boom（淘矿热）/ closed my defective-flue factory（关了不合格烟囱厂）/ to the intense joy of the insurance companies（保险公司大喜）/ went out（跑去）

**表达方式**：用"保险公司大喜"的旁白，写关厂之荒唐与被乐见的反差。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让创业失败也成笑料。

---

⑦ **"I became so interested in getting settled that I forgot all about establishing my right to the land before the United States Land Office at Colorado Springs."**

**中文理解**：我忙着安顿，竟全然忘了去 Colorado Springs 的联邦土地局确立地权。

**句子结构**：I became so interested in getting settled 主系表；that I forgot all about establishing my right 结果；to the land 定语；before the United States Land Office 状语；at Colorado Springs 定语。

**关键词**：so interested in getting settled（忙着安顿）/ forgot all about（全然忘了）/ establishing my right to the land（确立地权）/ the United States Land Office（联邦土地局）/ at Colorado Springs（在 Colorado Springs）

**表达方式**：用"忘了确权"写拓荒者的糊涂，伏下地被人占的笑柄。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让后文"地飞了"有了法理上的草率因。

---

⑧ **"I took a half-dozen sticks of giant powder and went over to a small ridge of rocks which held my farm in place."**

**中文理解**：我拿了半打胶质炸药，走到那道小小石脊——我的农场正靠它稳住。

**句子结构**：I took a half-dozen sticks of giant powder 主谓宾；and went over to a small ridge of rocks 并列；which held my farm in place 定语。

**关键词**：a half-dozen sticks of giant powder（半打胶质炸药）/ went over to a small ridge（走到小石脊）/ held my farm in place（稳住农场）

**表达方式**：用"拿炸药去石脊"写主人公想"平整"的蠢办法，喜剧伏笔。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让爆破闹剧的引信被他自己点燃。

---

⑨ **"Probably ten acres of my farm around the edges were knocked off along on the grand Colorado scenery, and most of the goats jolted off, but the hens, gentlemen, clung, the hens and myself."**

**中文理解**：大概边缘十英亩随科罗拉多大景一道被崩飞，多数山羊也震落了；可母鸡们， gentlemen，粘住了——母鸡与我自个儿。

**句子结构**：Probably ten acres … were knocked off 主谓（被动）；and most of the goats jolted off 并列；but the hens … clung 转折；the hens and myself 同位。

**关键词**：ten acres knocked off（十英亩崩飞）/ the grand Colorado scenery（科罗拉多大景）/ most of the goats jolted off（多数山羊震落）/ the hens clung（母鸡粘住）/ the hens and myself（母鸡与我）

**表达方式**：用"地飞人粘"的奇观，把爆破写成荒诞定格。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让闹剧在"我与母鸡同粘崖上"的画面上登顶。

---

⑩ **"I know of nothing sadder, unless it be, perhaps, to see a young man forget the respect he owes his elders."**

**中文理解**：我想不出更可悲的，除非或许是——见一个青年忘了他该敬长老。

**句子结构**：I know of nothing sadder 主谓宾；unless it be, perhaps, to see a young man forget the respect 条件；he owes his elders 定语。

**关键词**：nothing sadder（无更可悲）/ unless it be（除非）/ to see a young man forget（见青年忘了）/ the respect he owes his elders（该敬长老）

**表达方式**：用一句貌似正经的道德感叹收尾，把整篇闹剧陡然拔成"敬老"的假训诫。

**为什么这样写**：Bangs 以此，让幽默在伪庄重里闭环——读者笑着被上了一课。

## 一句话总结

Bangs 用 Jones 在科罗拉多用炸药"平整"农场、地飞人粘的爆破闹剧，把西部淘金热写成喜剧；末了却以"见青年忘敬老最可悲"的假训诫收尾——荒诞之上，再盖一层伪正经。
