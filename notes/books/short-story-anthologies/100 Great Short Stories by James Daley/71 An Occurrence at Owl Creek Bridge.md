# 71. An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge

**作者**：Ambrose Bierce

## 一句话总结

Bierce 写南方人 Farquhar 因助北军被吊于 Owl Creek 桥，行刑瞬间坠河、逃亡的漫长幻境，终在颈断的刹那惊醒——一则把"死前走马"写成时间畸变的短篇，结尾以"尸荡桥下"收束。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"The man's hands were behind his back, the wrists bound with a cord."**

**中文理解**：那人的手在背后，腕以绳缚。

**句子结构**：The man's hands were behind his back 主系表；the wrists bound with a cord 独立主格。

**关键词**：The man's hands behind his back（手在背后）/ the wrists bound with a cord（腕以绳缚）

**表达方式**：用"手缚腕绳"写囚的静，开场即刑场。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此，让死在仪式里先立。

---

② **"He was a civilian, if one might judge from his habit, which was that of a planter."**

**中文理解**：他是平民，若从衣装判——那似种植园主。

**句子结构**：He was a civilian 主系表；if one might judge from his habit 状语；which was that of a planter 定语。

**关键词**：He was a civilian（他是平民）/ judge from his habit（从衣装判）/ that of a planter（种植园主）

**表达方式**：用"衣装似园主"写身份，伏笔非兵而被吊的因。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此，让冤在阶级里先埋。

---

③ **"He wondered what it was, and whether immeasurably distant or near by—it seemed both."**

**中文理解**：他疑那是何、远不可量抑在近——似皆。

**句子结构**：He wondered what it was 主谓宾从；and whether immeasurably distant or near by 并列；it seemed both 主系表。

**关键词**：wondered what it was（疑那是何）/ immeasurably distant or near by（远不可量抑近）/ it seemed both（似皆）

**表达方式**：用"远近皆似"写幻境里时间的畸，感知先乱。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此，让现实的界在疑里松。

---

④ **"They have reached the Owl Creek bridge, put it in order and built a stockade on the north bank."**

**中文理解**：他们已抵 Owl Creek 桥，整之，于北岸筑垒。

**句子结构**：They have reached the Owl Creek bridge 主谓宾；put it in order 并列；and built a stockade on the north bank 并列。

**关键词**：reached the Owl Creek bridge（抵桥）/ put it in order（整之）/ built a stockade on the north bank（北岸筑垒）

**表达方式**：用"抵桥整之筑垒"写北军的占，事件的由。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此，让桥成为命运的地标。

---

⑤ **"The power of thought was restored; he knew that the rope had broken and he had fallen into the stream."**

**中文理解**：思力复；他知绳断、己落水中。

**句子结构**：The power of thought was restored 主谓；he knew 主谓；that the rope had broken 宾从；and he had fallen into the stream 并列。

**关键词**：The power of thought was restored（思力复）/ the rope had broken（绳断）/ fallen into the stream（落水中）

**表达方式**：用"思力复+绳断落水"写幻境的起点，逃开始。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此，让"生"在知里重启。

---

⑥ **"He felt his head emerge; his eyes were blinded by the sunlight; his chest expanded convulsively, and with a supreme and crowning agony his lungs engulfed a great draught of air, which instantly he expelled in a choking cough."**

**中文理解**：觉头出水；眼被日盲；胸痉扩，至高的痛里肺吞一大口气，即呛咳吐出。

**句子结构**：He felt his head emerge 主谓宾；his eyes were blinded by the sunlight 并列；his chest expanded convulsively 并列；and with a supreme and crowning agony 状语；his lungs engulfed a great draught of air 并列；which instantly he expelled in a choking cough 定语。

**关键词**：felt his head emerge（觉头出水）/ blinded by the sunlight（被日盲）/ chest expanded convulsively（胸痉扩）/ a supreme and crowning agony（至高的痛）/ engulfed a great draught of air（吞大气）/ expelled in a choking cough（呛咳吐）

**表达方式**：用"出水盲日+呛咳吞气"写求生的体感，幻境极真。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此，让逃亡在感官里逼真到骗。

---

⑦ **"Although no soldier, he had frequented camps enough to know the dread significance of that deliberate, drawling, aspirated chant; the lieutenant on shore was taking a part in the morning's work."**

**中文理解**：虽非兵，他常混营，知那刻意拖长的吐气吟之怖意；岸上中尉正参与晨工。

**句子结构**：Although no soldier 状语；he had frequented camps enough 主谓；to know the dread significance 目的；of that deliberate, drawling, aspirated chant 定语；the lieutenant on shore was taking a part 并列；in the morning's work 状语。

**关键词**：Although no soldier（虽非兵）/ frequented camps（常混营）/ the dread significance（怖意）/ that deliberate, drawling, aspirated chant（刻意拖长吐气吟）/ the lieutenant on shore（岸上中尉）

**表达方式**：用"知吟之怖"写他将临的枪，幻里也带真记忆。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此，让逃亡嵌进真实的战。

---

⑧ **"That is a good gun." Suddenly he felt himself whirled round and round—spinning like a top."**

**中文理解**："那是把好枪。"忽觉己被抡转——如陀螺旋。

**句子结构**：That is a good gun 引语；Suddenly he felt himself whirled round and round 主谓宾补；spinning like a top 状语。

**关键词**：That is a good gun（那是好枪）/ felt himself whirled round and round（觉被抡转）/ spinning like a top（如陀螺旋）

**表达方式**：用"如陀螺旋"写坠的晕，幻境的断点。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此，让"生"的线在旋里被扯回死。

---

⑨ **"By nightfall he was fatigued, footsore, famishing."**

**中文理解**：至暮他疲、脚痛、饿极。

**句子结构**：By nightfall 状语；he was fatigued, footsore, famishing 主系表。

**关键词**：By nightfall（至暮）/ fatigued, footsore, famishing（疲脚痛饿极）

**表达方式**：用"疲脚痛饿"写逃途的尽，幻境拉长到一日。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此，让时间在被拉长的逃里显畸。

---

⑩ **"Peyton Farquhar was dead; his body, with a broken neck, swung gently from side to side beneath the timbers of the Owl Creek bridge."**

**中文理解**：Peyton Farquhar 死；其尸颈断，在 Owl Creek 桥木下轻轻荡。

**句子结构**：Peyton Farquhar was dead 主系表；his body, with a broken neck 同位；swung gently from side to side 谓；beneath the timbers of the Owl Creek bridge 状语。

**关键词**：Peyton Farquhar was dead（Farquhar 死）/ with a broken neck（颈断）/ swung gently from side to side（轻轻荡）/ beneath the timbers of the Owl Creek bridge（桥木下）

**表达方式**：用"颈断尸荡"写现实的落，幻境全破。

**为什么这样写**：Bierce 以此收尾，让整段逃亡在"死"里归零，时间畸变成最冷的反转。

## 一句话总结

Bierce 写 Farquhar 因助北军被吊于 Owl Creek 桥，行刑瞬间坠河逃亡的漫长幻境（出水、避枪、至暮疲饿），终在颈断的刹那惊醒——"尸在桥木下轻荡"，死前走马是时间最冷的反转。
