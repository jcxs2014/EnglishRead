# 56. Wee Willie Winkie

**作者**：Rudyard Kipling

## 一句话总结

Kipling 写驻印英军营里的小童 Willie（人称 Wee Willie Winkie），为护送同伴 Coppy 越界见妻，独骑闯过"坏人"山丘、跌撞报信，以稚龄的莽撞换来一次英雄式成长——一则把帝国边陲的童年写成勇气寓言的短篇，结尾"以此步入男子汉"收束。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"His mother's ayah called him Willie-Baba, but as he never paid the faintest attention to anything that the ayah said, her wisdom did not help matters."**

**中文理解**：他母亲的奶妈唤他 Willie-Baba，可他从不理奶妈半句，她的见识便毫无用处。

**句子结构**：His mother's ayah called him Willie-Baba 主谓宾；but as he never paid the faintest attention 转折；to anything that the ayah said 定语；her wisdom did not help matters 主句。

**关键词**：His mother's ayah（他母亲的奶妈）/ called him Willie-Baba（唤他 Willie-Baba）/ paid the faintest attention（毫不理会）/ her wisdom did not help matters（见识无用）

**表达方式**：用"不理奶妈"写 Willie 的倔强自我，殖民育儿里的童性先立。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此，让这孩子从一开始就"只听自己"。

---

② **"The Colonel's son" was idolised on his own merits entirely."**

**中文理解**："上校之子"纯粹凭自身本事被崇拜。

**句子结构**：The Colonel's son 主语；was idolised 被动；on his own merits entirely 状语。

**关键词**：The Colonel's son（上校之子）/ idolised（被崇拜）/ on his own merits（凭自身本事）

**表达方式**：用"凭本事被崇拜"写 Willie 不靠父名，伏笔他将自证。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此，让荣誉观落在孩子自己肩上。

---

③ **"There were urgent and imperative reasons why Major Allardyce should not know how matters stood for at least another month, and this small marplot had discovered a great deal too much."**

**中文理解**：有紧迫且要紧的理由，Allardyce 少校至少一月内不可知实情；而这小捣蛋已探得太多。

**句子结构**：There were urgent and imperative reasons 存在句；why Major Allardyce should not know 定语；for at least another month 状语；and this small marplot had discovered a great deal too much 并列。

**关键词**：urgent and imperative reasons（紧迫要紧理由）/ Major Allardyce should not know（少校不可知）/ for at least another month（至少一月内）/ this small marplot（这小捣蛋）/ discovered too much（探得太多）

**表达方式**：用"小捣蛋探得太多"写孩童误入成人机密，张力起。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此，让童真闯进军规的缝隙。

---

④ **"I must go now." Coppy rose and escorted his small guest to the door, adding— "You're the best of little fellows, Winkie."**

**中文理解**："我得走了。"Coppy 起身送这小客到门，补道："你是最好的小家伙，Winkie。"

**句子结构**：I must go now 直接引语；Coppy rose and escorted his small guest 主谓宾；adding 现在分词；You're the best of little fellows 引语。

**关键词**：I must go now（我得走了）/ rose and escorted（起身送）/ his small guest（小客）/ the best of little fellows（最好的小家伙）

**表达方式**：用"送小客+夸最好"写成人对孩童的温柔，反衬后将临的险。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此，让 Coppy 的善意成为 Willie 仗义的种子。

---

⑤ **"Ever since that date it seemed to him that the bare black and purple hills across the river were inhabited by Goblins, and, in truth, every one had said that there lived the Bad Men."**

**中文理解**：自那日起，他总觉得河对岸秃黑紫的山里住着妖精；而确也有人说过，那里住着坏人。

**句子结构**：Ever since that date it seemed to him 主句；that the bare hills were inhabited by Goblins 主语从；and in truth every one had said 并列；that there lived the Bad Men 宾从。

**关键词**：Ever since that date（自那日起）/ the bare black and purple hills（秃黑紫山）/ inhabited by Goblins（住着妖精）/ in truth（确也）/ there lived the Bad Men（住着坏人）

**表达方式**：用"山里住坏人"的孩童地理，写恐惧的具象源头。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此，让 Willie 的独骑穿越的正是自己怕处。

---

⑥ **"Almost at the foot of the inhospitable hills, Wee Willie Winkie saw the Waler blunder and come down heavily."**

**中文理解**：几乎在那荒僻山脚，Wee Willie Winkie 见那匹 Waler 马失蹄重重倒下。

**句子结构**：Almost at the foot of the inhospitable hills 状语；Wee Willie Winkie saw the Waler blunder 主谓宾；and come down heavily 并列。

**关键词**：at the foot of the inhospitable hills（荒僻山脚）/ saw the Waler blunder（见 Waler 马失蹄）/ come down heavily（重重倒下）

**表达方式**：用"马失蹄倒"写险境降临，童骑的考验开始。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此，让勇气在马倒人孤里被逼出。

---

⑦ **"Oh, Winkie, what are you doing?" "Hush!" said Wee Willie Winkie.**

**中文理解**："哦 Winkie，你在做什么？""嘘！"Wee Willie Winkie 说。

**句子结构**：Oh, Winkie, what are you doing 呼语+疑问；Hush 直接引语；said Wee Willie Winkie 倒装。

**关键词**：Oh, Winkie, what are you doing（Winkie 你在做什么）/ Hush（嘘）/ said Wee Willie Winkie（Winkie 说）

**表达方式**：用"嘘"写孩童在险境里的镇定，把慌反写成戒。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此，让小大人的一面在紧急中显形。

---

⑧ **"Ours are the villages on the heights," said a voice in the background.**

**中文理解**："高处村落是我们的，"背景里一个声音说。

**句子结构**：Ours are the villages on the heights 主系表；said a voice in the background 倒装。

**关键词**：Ours are the villages（村落是我们的）/ on the heights（高处）/ a voice in the background（背景里的声音）

**表达方式**：用"高处村落是我们的"写敌对者的地盘宣示，冲突的族群线。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此，让边陲的"我们/他们"在童眼里清晰。

---

⑨ **"Devlin, the Colour-Sergeant of E Company, glanced at the empty saddle and tumbled through the barrack-rooms, kicking up each Room Corporal as he passed."**

**中文理解**：E 连掌旗军士 Devlin 瞥了空鞍，便跌撞穿过营房，过处把每个厢房下士踢起。

**句子结构**：Devlin, the Colour-Sergeant of E Company 主语带同位；glanced at the empty saddle 谓宾；and tumbled through the barrack-rooms 并列；kicking up each Room Corporal 现在分词；as he passed 时间。

**关键词**：the Colour-Sergeant of E Company（E 连掌旗军士）/ glanced at the empty saddle（瞥空鞍）/ tumbled through the barrack-rooms（跌撞穿营房）/ kicking up each Room Corporal（踢起每个下士）

**表达方式**：用"踢起下士"的闹腾写军营被惊动的慌乱。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此，让救援的动员带上喜剧的兵味。

---

⑩ **"I'm Percival Will'am Will'ams." And in this manner did Wee Willie Winkie enter into his manhood.**

**中文理解**："我是 Percival Will'am Will'ams。"就此，Wee Willie Winkie 步入了他的男子汉之境。

**句子结构**：I'm Percival Will'am Will'ams 直接引语；And in this manner did Wee Willie Winkie enter into his manhood 倒装主谓。

**关键词**：I'm Percival William Williams（我是 P.W.W.）/ in this manner（就此）/ enter into his manhood（步入男子汉之境）

**表达方式**：用改名+"步入男子汉"写童年的结业，把冒险写成成人礼。

**为什么这样写**：Kipling 以此收尾，让帝国边陲的勇，落在孩子自报真名的一刻。

## 一句话总结

Kipling 写驻印小童 Willie 为护 Coppy 独骑穿越"坏人山"、跌撞报信，以稚龄莽撞换一场成人礼；当他在结尾自报真名"Percival William Williams"，童年的勇正式步入男子汉之境。
