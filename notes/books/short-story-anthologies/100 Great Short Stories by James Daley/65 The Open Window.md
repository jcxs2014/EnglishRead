# 65. The Open Window

**作者**：Saki (H. H. Munro)

## 一句话总结

Saki 写 Framton 访医被引见邻家少女，听她编一桩"窗后亡夫与两小叔猎归"的鬼故事，窗开候"亡者"；当真有人泥泞归来、Framton 惊逃——一则把"恶作剧式虚构"写成黑色幽默的短篇，结尾以"short notice 的浪漫是她专长"收束。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"Nuttel," said a very self-possessed young lady of fifteen; "in the meantime you must try and put up with me." Framton Nuttel endeavoured to say the correct something which should duly flatter the niece."**

**中文理解**："Nuttel，"一位极镇定的十五岁少女说，"这会儿你得将就我。"Framton 力图说句得体、该讨好侄女的话。

**句子结构**：Nuttel 呼语；said a very self-possessed young lady of fifteen 倒装；in the meantime you must try and put up with me 引语。Framton Nuttel endeavoured to say the correct something 主谓宾；which should duly flatter the niece 定语。

**关键词**：a very self-possessed young lady of fifteen（极镇定十五岁少女）/ put up with me（将就我）/ endeavoured to say the correct something（力图说得体话）/ duly flatter the niece（该讨好侄女）

**表达方式**：用"镇定少女+力图讨好"写老少气场倒错，叙事的俏在先。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此，让叙事视角先落在被拿捏的客人上。

---

② **"Some of them, as far as I can remember, were quite nice." Framton wondered whether Mrs.—**

**中文理解**："据我所记，其中有些颇好。"Framton 疑那夫人是否——

**句子结构**：Some of them were quite nice 主系表；as far as I can remember 状语。Framton wondered 主谓；whether Mrs— 宾从（截断）。

**关键词**：as far as I can remember（据我所记）/ were quite nice（颇好）/ Framton wondered whether（Framton 疑是否）

**表达方式**：用"据我所记颇好"写少女的漫不经心，铺垫将生的谎。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此，让闲谈的轻里藏陷阱。

---

③ **"Only her name and address," admitted the caller.**

**中文理解**："只她姓名住址，"来客承认。

**句子结构**：Only her name and address 宾语（截悬）；admitted the caller 倒装。

**关键词**：Only her name and address（只姓名住址）/ admitted the caller（来客承认）

**表达方式**：用"只姓名住址"写 Framton 对女主人的无知，伏笔被耍。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此，让客人的信息缺位成恶作剧的土壤。

---

④ **"It is quite warm for the time of the year," said Framton; "but has that window got anything to do with the tragedy?" "Out through that window, three years ago to a day, her husband and her two young brothers went forth to shoot, and were swallowed in a treacherous bog."**

**中文理解**："今年此时颇暖，"Framton 道，"可那窗与惨剧何干？""三年前此日，她夫与两小叔出那窗去猎，陷进诡 bog 吞没。"

**句子结构**：It is quite warm 主系表；for the time of the year 状语；said Framton 倒装；but has that window got anything to do with the tragedy 疑问。"Out through that window 直接引语；three years ago to a day 状语；her husband and her two young brothers went forth 主谓；and were swallowed in a treacherous bog 并列。

**关键词**：quite warm for the time of the year（今年此时颇暖）/ got anything to do with the tragedy（与惨剧何干）/ Out through that window（出那窗）/ three years ago to a day（三年前此日）/ went forth to shoot（去猎）/ swallowed in a treacherous bog（陷诡 bog 吞）

**表达方式**：用"出窗猎陷 bog"编一桩亡故，把窗写成纪念的由。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此，让虚构的悲先立，反差后将引爆。

---

⑤ **"That is why the window is kept open every evening till it is quite dusk."**

**中文理解**："此即每黄昏开窗至暮的因。"

**句子结构**：That is why the window is kept open 主系表；every evening till it is quite dusk 状语。

**关键词**：the window is kept open（窗常开）/ every evening till it is quite dusk（每黄昏至暮）

**表达方式**：用"常开窗候亡者"写女主人的执，谎的最稳一环。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此，让细节的"合理"骗过客人。

---

⑥ **"They've been out for snipe in the marshes today, so they'll make a fine mess over my poor carpets."**

**中文理解**："他们今去沼里打沙锥，准把我可怜地毯弄糟。"

**句子结构**：They've been out for snipe in the marshes 主谓；today 状语；so they'll make a fine mess 结果；over my poor carpets 状语。

**关键词**：out for snipe in the marshes（沼里打沙锥）/ make a fine mess（弄糟）/ my poor carpets（我可怜地毯）

**表达方式**：用"弄糟地毯"把将至的"亡者"写成活人抱怨，谎里藏真。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此，让恶作剧在抱怨里最露锋。

---

⑦ **"On the matter of diet they are not so much in agreement," he continued.**

**中文理解**："饮食上他们不甚合，"他续道。

**句子结构**：On the matter of diet 状语；they are not so much in agreement 主系表；he continued 插入。

**关键词**：On the matter of diet（饮食上）/ not so much in agreement（不甚合）/ he continued（他续道）

**表达方式**：用"饮食不合"写虚构家庭的日常裂，谎的细节堆砌。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此，让故事越编越像真。

---

⑧ **"In a chill shock of nameless fear Framton swung round in his seat and looked in the same direction."**

**中文理解**：在一阵无名寒惧里，Framton 自座中转身、朝同方向望。

**句子结构**：In a chill shock of nameless fear 状语；Framton swung round in his seat 主谓；and looked in the same direction 并列。

**关键词**：a chill shock of nameless fear（无名寒惧）/ swung round in his seat（自转身）/ looked in the same direction（朝同方向望）

**表达方式**：用"无名寒惧+转身望"写客人信了谎、见"鬼"的惊。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此，让虚构的反噬落在客人身上。

---

⑨ **"Here we are, my dear," said the bearer of the white mackintosh, coming in through the window, "fairly muddy, but most of it's dry."**

**中文理解**："我们回来了，亲爱的，"白胶衣者穿窗入，道，"颇泥，但多已干。"

**句子结构**：Here we are, my dear 呼语引语；said the bearer of the white mackintosh 倒装；coming in through the window 现在分词；fairly muddy, but most of it's dry 引语。

**关键词**：Here we are, my dear（我们回来了亲爱的）/ the bearer of the white mackintosh（白胶衣者）/ coming in through the window（穿窗入）/ fairly muddy（颇泥）

**表达方式**：用"穿窗入+颇泥"写"亡者"真归，谎被现实拆穿的爆点。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此，让恶作剧在客人惊逃里达顶。

---

⑩ **"Enough to make anyone lose their nerve." Romance at short notice was her speciality.**

**中文理解**："够让任何人失胆。""short notice 的浪漫是她专长。"

**句子结构**：Enough to make anyone lose their nerve 形容词短语；Romance at short notice was her speciality 主系表。

**关键词**：make anyone lose their nerve（让任何人失胆）/ Romance at short notice（short notice 的浪漫）/ her speciality（她专长）

**表达方式**：用"失胆+浪漫专长"写叙述者揭少女善编，收束带讽。

**为什么这样写**：Saki 以此收尾，让黑色幽默在"专长"一词里落定。

## 一句话总结

Saki 写少女对客人 Framton 编"窗后亡夫与小叔猎陷 bog、每黄昏开窗候归"的鬼故事，当真有人泥泞穿窗归来、客人惊逃——"short notice 的浪漫"是她专长，恶作剧式虚构写成最利的黑色幽默。
