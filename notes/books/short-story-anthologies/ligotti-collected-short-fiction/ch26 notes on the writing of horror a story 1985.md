# 26. Notes On The Writing Of Horror: A Story（论恐怖写作：一则故事 / 1985）

## 一句话总结

一篇元小说：Ligotti 假托笔名 G.K. Riggers 讲授「恐怖故事写法」，以 Nathan 买下死人穿过的裤子、最终自腰以下腐烂而死的雏形故事为样本，逐一演示写实/哥特/实验三种技法，并在最后的「自白式」版本里让叙述者「我」（化身为恶魔）掐死邻居女孩 Desiderata、把情人 Norman/Nathan 拖入腐烂——把「写作指南」本身写成一场关于虚构与现实互噬的存在恐怖。

## 精读

### 第1处：开篇自白与「leetle darlings」

① For much too long I have been promising to formulate my views on the writing of supernatural horror tales. Until now I just haven't had the time. Why not? I was too busy churning out the leetle darlings. But many people, for whatever reasons, would like to be writers of horror tales, I know this.

中文理解：太久以来，我一直承诺要整理我对超自然恐怖故事写作的看法。至今只是没空。为何？我太忙于炮制那些「leetle darlings（小宝贝们，原文拼写）」。但我知道，许多人无论出于何种原因，都想当恐怖故事作家。

句子结构：For much too long 起 + I have been promising to formulate my views + Until now I just haven't had the time + Why not? 设问 + I was too busy churning out the leetle darlings + But 转折（many people would like to be writers）。

关键词：formulate my views（整理我的看法）、supernatural horror tales（超自然恐怖故事）、too busy churning out（太忙于炮制）、leetle darlings（小宝贝们，原文 typo）、would like to be writers（想当作家）

表达方式：用「leetle darlings」这滑稽错拼写作家对自家作品的溺爱，语气戏谑而自嘲。

为什么这样写：Ligotti 以「写作课」伪装开篇，却用 typo 与自嘲拆穿「大师」姿态。全文的元小说游戏由此定调——他在演一个不可靠的讲授者。

---

### 第2处：Nathan 的执念——「something magic, something timeless, something profound」

① In Nathan's view, yes, every facet of one's life should shine with these essences which alone make things really real. What are they? Nathan, over a period of time, has narrowed the essential elements down to three: something magic, something timeless, something profound.

中文理解：在 Nathan 看来，人生的每一面都应闪耀这些本质——唯它们令事物真正成真。它们是什么？Nathan 历时许久，将本质元素收窄为三：某种魔法的、某种永恒的、某种深邃的。

句子结构：In Nathan's view 起 + every facet of one's life should shine with these essences（定语从句 which alone make things really real）+ What are they? 设问 + Nathan has narrowed the essential elements down to three + 冒号列举（something magic, something timeless, something profound）。

关键词：every facet of one's life（人生的每一面）、shine with these essences（闪耀这些本质）、make things really real（令事物真正成真）、narrowed down to three（收窄为三）、something magic, something timeless, something profound（魔法/永恒/深邃）

表达方式：用「something magic, something timeless, something profound」这一三叠句，作为贯穿全文的副歌式母题。

为什么这样写：这三词是全书短篇合集反复回响的「恐怖本质」公式。Ligotti 借 Nathan 的恋物癖，把「让事物成真」的渴望写成一种病态——你越想抓住真实，越被物所噬。

### 第3处：裤子的来历——死人穿过的

① A few days before Nathan purchased the pants, they were returned to the store for a cash refund. The woman returning them claimed that her husband didn't like the way they felt. She lied. Actually, her husband couldn't have cared less how the pants felt, since he'd collapsed from a long-standing heart ailment not long after trying them on. And with no one home to offer him aid, he died. It was only after he had lain several hours dead in those beautiful trousers that his unloving wife came home…

中文理解：Nathan 买裤前几天，裤被退回店求退款。退货妇人称丈夫不喜欢那触感。她撒谎。实则丈夫根本不在乎触感——他试穿后不久便因宿疾心脏病发倒下，无人施救而死。待他在这华裤中死去数小时，那无情的妻才回家……

句子结构：A few days before 起 + they were returned + The woman claimed that… + She lied + Actually 转折（her husband couldn't have cared less）+ since 从句（he'd collapsed from a heart ailment）+ And with no one home + he died + It was only after he had lain several hours dead… that his wife came home。

关键词：returned for a cash refund（退回求退款）、claimed that her husband didn't like（称丈夫不喜欢）、She lied（她撒谎）、collapsed from a long-standing heart ailment（宿疾心脏病发倒下）、lain several hours dead（死去数小时）、unloving wife（无情的妻）

表达方式：用「It was only after… that」的强调结构，把死亡与裤子绑成一段滞后揭开的因果。

为什么这样写：这是 Nathan 腐烂的「根源」——他买的不是裤，是死人的终局。Ligotti 在此把消费行为写成招魂：你以为在买衣，实则在继承他人的死。

### 第4处：Nathan 的腐烂——「the putrid members of a man many days dead」

① But after they relieve a protesting, though paralyzed Nathan of his pants, they do not pursue their pillagery any further. Not after they see Nathan's legs, which are the putrid members of a man many days dead. With the lower half of Nathan rapidly rotting away, the upper must also die among the countless shadows of that condemned building.

中文理解：可当他们（孩子）从虽抗议却已瘫痪的 Nathan 身上剥去裤子后，未再继续劫掠。因为在看见 Nathan 的双腿——那是一具已死多日之人的腐肢——之后便止了。随下半身迅速腐烂，上半身也必在那危房的无尽阴影中死去。

句子结构：But 转折 + after they relieve… Nathan of his pants + they do not pursue + Not after they see Nathan's legs（定语从句 which are the putrid members of a man many days dead）+ With the lower half rotting away + the upper must also die among the countless shadows。

关键词：protesting, though paralyzed（虽抗议却已瘫痪）、relieve him of his pants（剥去他的裤子）、putrid members of a man many days dead（已死多日之人的腐肢）、lower half rapidly rotting away（下半身迅速腐烂）、condemned building（危房）

表达方式：用「虽抗议却已瘫痪」写 Nathan 的清醒与无力；「腐肢」把恐怖钉在生理层面。

为什么这样写：Nathan 的死是「物的反噬」——他赋予裤子「魔法/永恒/深邃」，裤子回赠他以死人的时间。Ligotti 在此完成对消费主义恋物的哥特审判：你占有的，终将占有你。

---

### 第5处：写实技法论——「the Edge of the Unknown」

① Only then can the unreal, the abnormal, the supernatural be smuggled in as a plain brown package marked Hope, Love, or Fortune Cookies, and postmarked: the Edge of the Unknown. And of the dear reader's seat.

中文理解：唯其如此，那非真实、反常、超自然之物，才可被走私进来——伪装成一只素棕包裹，标着「希望」「爱」或「幸运饼干」，邮戳盖着：未知之边。以及读者座席之边。

句子结构：Only then can 倒装（the unreal… be smuggled in）+ as a plain brown package（marked Hope, Love, or Fortune Cookies）+ and postmarked: the Edge of the Unknown + And of the dear reader's seat。

关键词：smuggled in（走私进来）、plain brown package（素棕包裹）、marked Hope, Love, or Fortune Cookies（标着希望/爱/幸运饼干）、postmarked the Edge of the Unknown（邮戳：未知之边）、dear reader's seat（读者座席）

表达方式：用「素棕包裹 + 幸运饼干」的日常意象写超自然的伪装，戏拟写实派的「以常掩异」。

为什么这样写：Ligotti 在此戏拟并揭穿写实恐怖的伎俩——把不可信之物塞进可信的包装。这既是对同行技法的嘲讽，也是元小说的自我指涉：他正在做的正是「走私」。

---

### 第6处：哥特技法论——「the University of Dreams」

① One, isolated supernatural incidents don't look as silly in a Gothic tale as they do in a realistic one, since the latter obeys the hard-knocking school of reality while the former recognizes only the University of Dreams.

中文理解：其一，孤立的超自然事件在哥特故事里不像在写实故事里那般可笑，因后者服从「硬敲的现实学派」，而前者只认「梦之大学」。

句子结构：One 起 + isolated supernatural incidents don't look as silly in a Gothic tale as they do in a realistic one + since 原因（the latter obeys the hard-knocking school of reality + while the former recognizes only the University of Dreams）。

关键词：isolated supernatural incidents（孤立的超自然事件）、don't look as silly（不似那般可笑）、Gothic tale（哥特故事）、realistic one（写实故事）、hard-knocking school of reality（硬敲的现实学派）、University of Dreams（梦之大学）

表达方式：用「hard-knocking school of reality」与「University of Dreams」的戏仿机构名，把两种技法拟人成对立学府。

为什么这样写：Ligotti 借「讲授」之机，实则在辨析自己的文学谱系——他自认属于「梦之大学」。这节的哥特辩护，是全篇最接近作者真声的部分。

---

### 第7处：实验技法论——「thrashing their chains around in the escape-proof dungeon」

① But despite the most sincere efforts of each prisoner, the sentence remains the same: to stay exactly where they are, which is where the story is. It's a condition not unlike the world itself, except it doesn't hurt.

中文理解：但尽管每名囚徒竭尽真诚，判决依旧：待在他们所在之处，即故事所在之处。这境况不异于世界本身，只不似那般疼痛。

句子结构：But 转折 + despite the most sincere efforts + the sentence remains the same（to stay exactly where they are, which is where the story is）+ It's a condition not unlike the world itself + except it doesn't hurt。

关键词：despite the most sincere efforts（尽管竭尽真诚）、the sentence remains the same（判决依旧）、stay exactly where they are（待在所在之处）、where the story is（即故事所在）、not unlike the world itself（不异于世界本身）

表达方式：用「sentence / prisoner / dungeon」的司法隐喻写写作的宿命——作者与故事互为囚笼。

为什么这样写：实验技法论在此收束成对创作本身的 existential 判决：作者逃不出故事，正如人逃不出世界。Ligotti 把「写作自由」解构为「被故事囚禁」，呼应全书「无出路」母题。

---

### 第8处：终极风格——「manuscripts found in lonely places」

① In other words, the proper style of horror is really that of the personal confession, and nothing but: manuscripts found in lonely places. While some may consider this the height of cornball melodrama, and I grant that it is, it is also the rawhead and bloody bones of true blue grue.

中文理解：换言之，恐怖的正统风格实则唯有「个人自白」一途：在孤处寻得的手稿。虽有人视此为陈腔滥调之极，我亦承认如此，但它亦是真正惊悚的骷髅血骨。

句子结构：In other words 起 + the proper style of horror is that of the personal confession + manuscripts found in lonely places + While some may consider this the height of cornball melodrama（and I grant that it is）+ it is also the rawhead and bloody bones of true blue grue。

关键词：proper style of horror（恐怖的正统风格）、personal confession（个人自白）、manuscripts found in lonely places（孤处寻得的手稿）、cornball melodrama（陈腔滥调）、rawhead and bloody bones（骷髅血骨）、true blue grue（真正惊悚）

表达方式：用「rawhead and bloody bones」这一古英语恐怖谚语，把「自白体」锚定到恐怖最原初的肉身层面。

为什么这样写：Ligotti 在此给出全书的方法论宣言——恐怖的真相是「第一人称自白 + 遗稿」。这恰是他整部短篇合集的体裁自觉：每篇都像从某处捡来的、某人写下的遗稿。

### 第9处：自白版结局——「Please die, Desiderata」

① I leaned toward her very confidentially. "Please die, Desiderata," I whispered in her ear, while gripping her neck with both hands, cutting short a scream along with her life. Then I really went to work.

中文理解：我极亲密地倾身向她。「请死吧，Desiderata，」我凑她耳语，同时双手扼其颈，截断一声尖叫连同她的生命。随后我才真正动手。

句子结构：I leaned toward her very confidentially + 直接引语 "Please die, Desiderata" + I whispered in her ear + while 分词（gripping her neck with both hands, cutting short a scream along with her life）+ Then I really went to work。

关键词：leaned very confidentially（极亲密地倾身）、Please die, Desiderata（请死吧，Desiderata）、whispered in her ear（凑耳语）、gripping her neck with both hands（双手扼颈）、cutting short a scream along with her life（截断尖叫连同生命）、went to work（真正动手）

表达方式：用「Please die」这礼貌句式写谋杀，把暴力包装成殷勤的请求，寒意倍增。

为什么这样写：元小说在此破壁——讲授者「我」从分析师变成施暴者。Desiderata（欲望女神名）之死，标志着「虚构」已吞没「现实」：写作指南的作者自己成了故事里的恶魔。

### 第10处：终局召唤——「Rot your way back to us, you freak of creation」

① "Rot your way back to us, you freak of creation. Rot your way out of this world. Come home to a pain so great that it is bliss itself. You were born to be bones not flesh. Rot your way free of that skin of mere skin."

中文理解：「腐烂着回到我们这儿来吧，你这造物的畸胎。腐烂着离开这世界。回家去那大痛——痛到自身即是极乐。你生而为骨，非为肉。腐烂着挣脱那 mere skin 的皮囊。」

句子结构：连续祈使句群（Rot your way back to us / Rot your way out of this world / Come home to a pain so great that it is bliss itself / You were born to be bones not flesh / Rot your way free of that skin of mere skin），以 "you freak of creation" 呼语起首。

关键词：Rot your way back to us（腐烂着回到我们）、freak of creation（造物的畸胎）、pain so great that it is bliss itself（痛到即极乐）、born to be bones not flesh（生而为骨非肉）、skin of mere skin（mere skin 的皮囊）

表达方式：用排比祈使 + 「痛即极乐」的悖论，把腐烂写成归乡与救赎。

为什么这样写：终局点题——全书所有 Nathan/ Norman 的「腐烂」，在此被揭示为恶魔对「造物」的召唤： flesh 是错位的皮囊，bone 才是本真归宿。Ligotti 把恐怖收束为一种逆向的「归家」：你以为在活，实则一直在腐烂回「他们」那里。
