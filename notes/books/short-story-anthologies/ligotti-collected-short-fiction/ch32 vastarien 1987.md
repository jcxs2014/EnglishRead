# 32. Vastarien（vastarien / 1987）


## 精读（10处）

### 第1处：梦中的城市——「扭曲的街道，无窗户的高塔」

> **原句 1:** Within the blackness of his sleep a few lights began to glow like candles in a cloistered cell. Their illumination was unsteady and dim, issuing from no definite source. Nonetheless, he now discovered many shapes beneath the shadows: tall buildings whose rooftops nodded groundward, wide buildings whose facades seemed to follow the curve of a street, dark buildings whose windows and doorways tilted like badly hung paintings.

中文理解：在睡眠的黑暗中，一些灯光开始像修道院密室中的蜡烛一样发光。它们的照明不稳定且暗淡，没有明确的来源。然而，他现在在阴影下发现了许多形状：屋顶向地面点头的 tall buildings， facade 似乎跟随街道曲线的 wide buildings， windows and doorways 像挂得不好的画一样倾斜的黑暗建筑。

句子结构：Within the blackness of his sleep a few lights began to glow like candles in a cloistered cell 是主句；Their illumination was unsteady and dim 是并列主句；issuing from no definite source 是分词状语；Nonetheless, he now discovered many shapes beneath the shadows 是主句；tall buildings whose rooftops nodded groundward 是 shapes 的同位语。

关键词：like candles in a cloistered cell（像修道院密室中的蜡烛）、unsteady and dim（不稳定且暗淡）、tilted like badly hung paintings（像挂得不好的画一样倾斜）

表达方式：建筑物的「不稳定性」是 Vastarien 的核心特征——它们不是垂直的，而是倾斜和扭曲的。

为什么这样写：这是 Vastarien 的开场意象——一个由不稳定的、倾斜的建筑组成的城市，暗示这是一个「错误」的世界。

---

### 第2处：Vastarien 的本质——「每个形状都暗示一千个其他形状」

> **原句 2:** Vastarien, where every shape suggested a thousand others, every sound disseminated everlasting echoes, every word founded a world.

中文理解：Vastarien，那里每个形状都暗示一千个其他形状，每个声音散发永恒的回声，每个词语都创建一个世界。

句子结构：Vastarien 是主语；where every shape suggested a thousand others, every sound disseminated everlasting echoes, every word founded a world 是定语从句。

关键词：every shape suggested a thousand others（每个形状都暗示一千个其他形状）、every sound disseminated everlasting echoes（每个声音散发永恒的回声）、every word founded a world（每个词语创建一个世界）

表达方式：这是 Ligotti 对 Vastarien 的最终定义——这是一个意义无限增殖的地方。

为什么这样写：这是全篇的核心——Vastarien 是「超现实」空间的完美表达，每个元素都指向无限的其他元素。

---

### 第3处：现实与 Vastarien——「这个世界只是一个受限的天堂」

> **原句 3:** The city outside the window was a semblance of that other place, which now seemed impossibly far off and inaccessible. But the likeness was evident only to his inner vision, only in the recollected images he formed when his eyes were closed or out of focus. It would be difficult to conceive of a creature for whom this world—its bare form seen with open eyes—represented a coveted paradise.

中文理解：窗户外的城市是那个其他地方的一个类似物，后者现在似乎遥远得难以触及。但这种相似性只有在他的内心视野中才明显，只有在他闭上眼睛或失焦时形成的记忆图像中才明显。很难想象有什么生物会认为这个世界——用睁开的眼睛看到的赤裸形态——代表了一个令人垂涎的天堂。

句子结构：The city outside the window was a semblance of that other place 是主句；which now seemed impossibly far off and inaccessible 是定语从句；But the likeness was evident only to his inner vision 是并列主句；only in the recollected images he formed… 是并列状语；It would be difficult to conceive of a creature… 是主句。

关键词：a semblance of（一个类似物）、impossibly far off and inaccessible（遥远得难以触及）、inner vision（内心视野）、a coveted paradise（令人垂涎的天堂）

表达方式：Keirion 把现实视为 Vastarien 的「类似物」——这暗示现实是次级的、不完美的版本。

为什么这样写：这是 Keirion 哲学的核心——他认为这个世界只是一个通往「其他地方」的入口，而那个地方才是真正的家园。

### 第4处：缺失的元素——「现实缺少的unreal品质」

> **原句 4:** The word unearthly reverberated in the room. In that place and at that hour, the paradoxical absence, the missing quality, became clear to him: it was the element of the unreal.

中文理解：「unearthly」这个词在房间里回荡。在那个地方和那个时刻，矛盾的缺失，缺失的品质，对他变得清晰：那是 unreal 的元素。

句子结构：The word unearthly reverberated in the room 是主句；In that place and at that hour, the paradoxical absence, the missing quality, became clear to him 是并列主句；it was the element of the unreal 是表语从句。

关键词：the paradoxical absence（矛盾的缺失）、the missing quality（缺失的品质）、the element of the unreal（unreal 的元素）

表达方式：Keirion 发现现实世界缺少的是「unreal」品质——这是全篇最核心的发现。

为什么这样写：这是 Keirion 哲学的悖论——为了使世界「真实」，它必须首先包含「unreal」的元素。

### 第5处：书店——「一个由十一面组成的圆形空间」

> **原句 5:** The circumference of the room was composed of crowded bookshelves whose separate sections were joined to one another to create an irregular polygon of eleven sides, with a long desk standing where a twelfth would have been.

中文理解：房间的圆周由拥挤的书架组成，这些书架的各个部分相互连接，形成一个不规则的十一面多边形，一张长桌站在第十二面应该在的位置。

句子结构：The circumference of the room was composed of crowded bookshelves 是主句；whose separate sections were joined to one another to create an irregular polygon of eleven sides 是 bookshelves 的定语从句；with a long desk standing where a twelfth would have been 是独立分词结构。

关键词：an irregular polygon of eleven sides（不规则的十一面多边形）、a twelfth would have been（第十二面应该在的位置）

表达方式：书店的形状暗示 Vastarien——一个不规则的、由无限边界组成的地方。

为什么这样写：书店本身就是 Vastarien 的一个缩影——它的形状是「不完美的」，暗示这是一个通往其他世界的地方。

---

### 第6处：关于这本书——「它实际上本身就是那个东西」

> **原句 6:** "Have you ever heard of a book, an extremely special book, that is not about something, but actually is that something?"

中文理解：「你有没有听说过一本书，一本非常特别的书，它不是关于某个事物，而是 actually 就是那个事物本身？」

句子结构：Have you ever heard of a book… 是疑问句；an extremely special book 是 book 的同位语；that is not about something, but actually is that something 是定语从句。

关键词：actually is that something（实际上就是那个事物本身）

表达方式：这是 stranger 对 Vastarien 书的描述——它不是「关于」那个世界，而是「是」那个世界本身。

为什么这样写：这是 Ligotti 对「书与现实」关系的最终思考——有些书不只是描述世界，而是创造世界。

---

### 第7处：Vastarien 的本质——「一种超自然的 essence，向导到另一个领域」

> **原句 7:** The book, indeed, did not merely describe that strange world but, in some obscure fashion, was a true composition of the thing itself, its very form incarnate.

中文理解：这本书 indeed，不只是描述那个奇怪的世界，而是以某种模糊的方式，是那个事物本身的真正创作，其形式的 incarnate。

句子结构：The book, indeed, did not merely describe that strange world 是主句；but, in some obscure fashion, was a true composition of the thing itself 是并列谓语；its very form incarnate 是独立主格结构。

关键词：a true composition of the thing itself（事物本身的真正创作）、its very form incarnate（其形式的 incarnate）

表达方式：这本书和 Vastarien 之间的关系是「 incarnate」——不是描述，而是变成了那个东西本身。

为什么这样写：这是 Keirion 发现的核心——他找到的书不只是关于 Vastarien，而是 Vastarien 本身的一个碎片。

### 第8处：夜间梦——「扭曲的纪念碑变得清晰，一个伟大的身影在地平线上」

> **原句 8:** And at the opposite end of that street, rising up taller than the buildings themselves, stood a great figure in silhouette. This looming colossus made no movement or sound but firmly dominated the horizon where the single remaining street seemed to end.

中文理解： And at the opposite end of that street, rising up taller than the buildings themselves, stood a great figure in silhouette. This looming colossus made no movement or sound but firmly dominated the horizon where the single remaining street seemed to end.

中文理解： And at the opposite end of that street, rising up taller than the buildings themselves, stood a great figure in silhouette. This looming colossus made no movement or sound but firmly dominated the horizon where the single remaining street seemed to end.

句子结构：And at the opposite end of that street, rising up taller than the buildings themselves, stood a great figure in silhouette 是倒装主系表；This looming colossus made no movement or sound 是主句；but firmly dominated the horizon 是并列谓语；where the single remaining street seemed to end 是定语从句。

关键词：a great figure in silhouette（一个伟大的 silhouetted 身影）、looming colossus（ looming 的巨人）、firmly dominated the horizon（坚定地主导地平线）

表达方式：这是 Keirion 在梦中看到的「巨人」——它既是人又是鸟，暗示他是 Vastarien 的某种守护者或向导。

为什么这样写：这是 Keirion 的最终视像——他意识到 Vastarien 有一个主人，而这个主人正在接管他的梦。

### 第9处：被关押——「他试图从窗户逃走」

> **原句 9:** When the door was opened and some men in uniforms entered the room, they found Victor Keirion screaming to the raucous limits of his voice and trying to scale the thick metal mesh veiling the window, as if he were dragging himself along some unlikely route of liberation.

中文理解：当门被打开，一些穿制服的人进入房间时，他们发现 Victor Keirion 正以他声音的嘶哑极限尖叫，并试图爬上遮盖窗户的厚金属网，仿佛他正在沿着某条不太可能的解放路线拖拽自己。

句子结构：When the door was opened and some men in uniforms entered the room 是时间状语从句；they found Victor Keirion screaming… 是主句；and trying to scale the thick metal mesh veiling the window 是宾语补足语；as if he were dragging himself along some unlikely route of liberation 是方式状语从句。

关键词：to the raucous limits of his voice（以他声音的嘶哑极限）、to scale the thick metal mesh（爬上厚金属网）、a route of liberation（解放路线）

表达方式：Keirion 的尖叫和逃跑尝试是他试图「打破」现实和他的梦想世界之间障碍的最后尝试。

为什么这样写：这是 Keirion 命运的悲剧——他被关进了精神病院，但他的「疯狂」实际上是一种对更高现实的追求。

---

### 第10处：最终的话语——「在 unreal 的秩序中被永远转移的所有事物中」

> **原句 10:** For he was now far from his own words, buried deep within the dreams of a place where everything was transfixed in the order of the unreal; and whence, it truly seemed, he would never return.

中文理解： For he was now far from his own words, buried deep within the dreams of a place where everything was transfixed in the order of the unreal; and whence, it truly seemed, he would never return.

中文理解：因为他现在远离了他自己的话语，深深埋葬在一个所有事物都在 unreal 的秩序中被永远转移的地方；而从那里， it truly seemed，他将永远不会返回。

句子结构：For he was now far from his own words 是原因状语从句；buried deep within the dreams of a place 是主句补足语；where everything was transfixed in the order of the unreal 是 place 的定语从句；and whence, it truly seemed, he would never return 是并列分句。

关键词：far from his own words（远离了他自己的话语）、buried deep within the dreams（深深埋葬在梦中）、transfixed in the order of the unreal（在 unreal 的秩序中被永远转移）

表达方式：「transfixed in the order of the unreal」是全篇的最后意象——Keirion 已经完全进入 Vastarien，在那里所有事物都被永远转移。

为什么这样写：这是 Ligotti 对「文学想象与疯狂之间界限」的最终探索——Keirion 追求的是文学的更高现实，但他最终失去了回到「正常」世界的能力。

## 一句话总结

Victor Keirion 反复梦见 Vastarien，在一家神秘的书店找到一本关于它的书后沉迷于其中，最终被 stranger 和 bookseller 引导进入那个世界，醒来后发现自己被关在精神病院里，嘴里还在念叨着那本书的名字——他已经永远无法返回现实世界。
