# 32. Vastarien（瓦斯塔利恩 / 1987）


## 精读

### 第1处：非现实的必需品——「the element of the unreal」

① The word unearthly reverberated in the room. In that place and at that hour, the paradoxical absence, the missing quality, became clear to him: it was the element of the unreal.

中文理解：「unearthly」一词在室中回荡。于彼处彼时，那悖论式的缺席、那缺失的质，对他清晰起来：它是非现实之元素。

句子结构：The word unearthly reverberated in the room + In that place and at that hour + the paradoxical absence, the missing quality, became clear to him + 冒号 it was the element of the unreal

关键词：unearthly（非尘世的）、reverberated（回荡）、paradoxical absence（悖论式的缺席）、missing quality（缺失的质）、the element of the unreal（非现实之元素）

表达方式：用「缺失的质=非现实元素」把窗外城市写成少了一味关键的空壳；「reverberated」让词获得实体感。

为什么这样写：Keirion 一眼看穿现实之城唯缺「unreal」——他不是厌世，而是认定真实世界少了构成意义的要素。Ligotti 在此立全书的欲望对象：unreal 不是虚假，是比真实更真的「质」。

---

### 第2处：寄生之城——「the most gauzy phantom of that other place」

① And although there were indeed times when one might be deceived, isolated moments when a gift for disguise triumphs, the impersonation could never be perfect or lasting. No true challenge to the rich unreality of Vastarien, where every shape suggested a thousand others, every sound disseminated everlasting echoes, every word founded a world.

中文理解：虽确有被欺之时、伪装术得胜的孤立片刻，那冒充永难完美或持久。绝无可匹敌 Vastarien 丰盈之非现实——那里每形暗示千形，每声播散永恒回响，每词创立一界。

句子结构：And although there were times + the impersonation could never be perfect or lasting + No true challenge to the rich unreality of Vastarien + where 三个平行 every shape suggested / every sound disseminated / every word founded

关键词：gift for disguise triumphs（伪装术得胜）、impersonation（冒充）、rich unreality of Vastarien（Vastarien 丰盈之非现实）、every shape suggested a thousand others（每形暗示千形）、every word founded a world（每词创立一界）

表达方式：用三个 every 平行把 Vastarien 写成无限生成的母域；「冒充」把现实城贬为赝品。

为什么这样写：现实之城只是 Vastarien 的苍白仿品。Ligotti 反转真实/虚构等级：真实世界是赝品，梦境书才是本源。这呼应 ch30「seeming is everything」——但更激进：书里的 unreal 比窗外的 real 更真。

---

### 第3处：废墟之栖——「to dwell among the ruins of reality」

① For he dreamed of strange volumes that turned away from all earthly light to become lost in their own nightmares, pages that preached a nocturnal salvation, a liturgy of shadows, catechism of phantoms. His absolute: to dwell among the ruins of reality.

中文理解：因他梦着奇异卷册——它们背弃一切尘光、迷失于自身噩梦，页页宣讲夜之救赎、影之礼文、幻之教理。他的绝对：栖于现实之废墟间。

句子结构：For he dreamed of strange volumes + that turned away from all earthly light + pages that preached a nocturnal salvation + a liturgy of shadows, catechism of phantoms + His absolute: to dwell among the ruins of reality

关键词：turned away from all earthly light（背弃一切尘光）、nocturnal salvation（夜之救赎）、liturgy of shadows（影之礼文）、catechism of phantoms（幻之教理）、to dwell among the ruins of reality（栖于现实之废墟）

表达方式：用「影之礼文/幻之教理」把反现实写成一种宗教；「栖于现实之废墟」把理想写成在真实残骸上安居。

为什么这样写：Keirion 的「绝对」不是逃离现实，而是住在它崩后——他把现实当废墟来栖。Ligotti 把虚无主义写成居留而非出走：你不必死，只需搬进真实的瓦砾。

---

### 第4处：书即世界——「its very form incarnate」

① Thus began Victor Keirion's preoccupation with a certain book and a certain hallucinated world, though to make a distinction between these two phenomena ultimately seemed an error: the book, indeed, did not merely describe that strange world but, in some obscure fashion, was a true composition of the thing itself, its very form incarnate.

中文理解：由此始了 Keirion 对某一书与某一幻世之执念，虽将二者区分终似一误：那书实非仅描述那奇境，而是以某种幽晦方式，为物自身之真构——其形即化身。

句子结构：Thus began his preoccupation + though to make a distinction seemed an error + 冒号 the book did not merely describe + but was a true composition of the thing itself + its very form incarnate

关键词：preoccupation（执念）、make a distinction seemed an error（区分似一误）、did not merely describe（非仅描述）、true composition of the thing itself（物自身之真构）、its very form incarnate（其形即化身）

表达方式：用「书≠描述世界，书=世界之化身」消解书与世界的边界；「incarnate」把文字写成肉身。

为什么这样写：全书题眼——Vastarien 不是关于 unreal 世界的书，它就是那世界。Ligotti 在此实践元小说本体论：当一本书被读得够深，书与它所写之物同一。这为结尾「乌鸦人借眼窃梦」埋设机制。

---

### 第5处：无避难之噩——「nightmare made normal」

① Rampant oddity seemed to be the rule of the realm; imperfection became the source of the miraculous—wonders of deformity and marvels of miscreation. There was horror, undoubtedly. But it was a horror uncompromised by any feeling of lost joy or thwarted redemption; rather, it was a deliverance by damnation. And if Vastarien was a nightmare, it was a nightmare transformed in spirit by the utter absence of refuge: nightmare made normal.

中文理解：恣肆之怪似为此境之律；不完美成奇迹之源——畸之奇、误造之妙。无疑有恐怖。但那恐怖不被任何失乐或受阻救赎之感所损；反之，它是藉诅咒而得释。若 Vastarien 是噩梦，它是被「全无避难」之精神转化了的噩梦：噩梦成常。

句子结构：Rampant oddity seemed the rule + imperfection became the source of the miraculous + There was horror + But it was a horror uncompromised by… + rather, it was a deliverance by damnation + And if Vastarien was a nightmare + it was a nightmare transformed… by the utter absence of refuge + nightmare made normal

关键词：rampant oddity（恣肆之怪）、rule of the realm（此境之律）、wonders of deformity（畸之奇）、deliverance by damnation（藉诅咒得释）、utter absence of refuge（全无避难）、nightmare made normal（噩梦成常）

表达方式：用「藉诅咒得释」「噩梦成常」把恐怖正常化；「无避难」是转化的关键——因无处可逃，噩梦反成家园。

为什么这样写：Vastarien 的恐怖之「美」在于它无救赎许诺——不像普通 horror 暗藏解脱，这里连escape都没有，于是噩梦变成常态、变成安身之所。Ligotti 写的是最彻底的虚无之乡：连恐惧都被抽走，只剩「在」。

---

### 第6处：借眼窃梦——「Stealing my dreams」

① The man could not read the book—there, that book—and was stealing the dreams which the book had spawned. Stealing my dreams, he mumbled softly as the drug began to take effect. Stealing my.

中文理解：那人读不了那书——那，那本书——却在窃那书所生之梦。窃我之梦，他于药效初临时喃喃。窃我……

句子结构：The man could not read the book + and was stealing the dreams + Stealing my dreams, he mumbled + Stealing my.（残句收束）

关键词：could not read the book（读不了那书）、stealing the dreams（窃那梦）、the book had spawned（书所生之梦）、Stealing my dreams（窃我之梦）、mumbled softly（喃喃）

表达方式：用「Stealing my dreams」残句收束写意识消散；破折号「那，那本书」指认实物。

为什么这样写：闭环揭示乌鸦人代付全款的动机——他自己读不了书，只能借 Keirion（书之真读者）的眼偷梦。Ligotti 把「阅读」写成一种可被寄生掠夺的行为：你读得越真，越成别人的梦源。Keirion 被锁进小室，正是为了持续「产出」被窃的梦。

## 一句话总结

> 一篇书即世界的元小说：书痴 Victor Keirion 在十二边书店购得灰色小书《Vastarien》——它不描述、而是「就是」那个 unreal 世界之实体；乌鸦人代付全款，实为借 Keirion 之眼窃梦，Keirion 终被锁进金属门小室、绑上床注射，喃「Stealing my dreams」。把对 unreal 的迷恋写成一种被书寄生、夺走现实人生的绝症。
