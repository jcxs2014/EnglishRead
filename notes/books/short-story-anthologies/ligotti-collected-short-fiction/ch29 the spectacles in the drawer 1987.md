# 29. The Spectacles In The Drawer（抽屉里的眼镜 / 1987）


## 精读

### 第1处：密室与宝物——「the room of secret mystery」
> **原句 1:** It was not by any means a spacious room, and its enclosed atmosphere was thickened, as Plomb would have emphasized, by its claustrophobic arrangement of tall cabinets, ceiling-high shelves, and various trunks and crates. This is simply how things worked out over a period of time. In any case, Plomb seemed to favor this state of affairs. "Ah, the room of secret mystery," he said. "Where all your treasures are kept, all the raw wonders cached away."

中文理解：这绝非宽敞之室，其密闭的氛围，如 Plomb 定会强调的，被高柜、及顶书架与诸般箱箧的幽闭式陈设而加厚。事情本就如此演变。无论如何，Plomb 似偏爱此态。「啊，秘密神秘之室，」他说。「你所有宝藏都在此，所有生料般的奇观都窖藏其间。」

句子结构：It was not a spacious room + and its enclosed atmosphere was thickened（as Plomb would have emphasized）+ by its claustrophobic arrangement + This is simply how things worked out + In any case Plomb seemed to favor this + "Ah, the room of secret mystery," he said + "Where all your treasures are kept…"

关键词：enclosed atmosphere（密闭氛围）、claustrophobic arrangement（幽闭式陈设）、tall cabinets（高柜）、favor this state of affairs（偏爱此态）、room of secret mystery（秘密神秘之室）、raw wonders cached away（窖藏的生料奇观）

表达方式：用「幽闭陈设加厚氛围」把房间写成一种压迫性的收藏癖容器；Plomb 的台词点出「密室=奇观窖」。

为什么这样写：开篇立「收藏者与被收藏物」的共谋关系。叙述者与 Plomb 都是囤奇者，密室是两人关系的舞台。Ligotti 在此搭好「奇观成瘾」的场域。

### 第2处：伪典戏拟——「The lost grimoire of the Abbot of Tine」
> **原句 2:** "The lost grimoire of the Abbot of Tine," he giggled. "Transcribed in the language of—" "A wild guess," I interjected. "And a wrong one." "Then the forbidden Psalms of the Silent. The book without an author." "Without a living author, if you will recall what I told you about it. But you're very wide of the mark."

中文理解：「Abbot of Tine 失传的魔法书，」他咯咯笑。「以……之语抄录——」「瞎猜，」我插嘴。「而且错了。」「那么是《静默的禁诗篇》。那本无作者之书。」「若无生者作者，若你还记得我所说——但你差得远了。」

句子结构：直接引语 "The lost grimoire…" + "A wild guess," I interjected + "And a wrong one." + "Then the forbidden Psalms…" + "Without a living author… But you're very wide of the mark."

关键词：lost grimoire（失传魔法书）、Abbot of Tine（Tine 修道院长）、Transcribed in the language of（以……之语抄录）、wild guess（瞎猜）、forbidden Psalms of the Silent（静默的禁诗篇）、book without an author（无作者之书）、wide of the mark（差得远）

表达方式：用一连串伪神秘书名戏拟神秘学目录；叙述者以「揭穿」为乐，显其掌控。

为什么这样写：Ligotti 在此戏仿 occult 典籍的命名术，同时揭示叙述者的「导演」姿态——他掌握真名，Plomb 只是猜谜的孩童。这为后文「叙述者也是被追者」的翻转埋线。

### 第3处：眼镜与血视——「your sight is one with its object」
> **原句 3:** "Your eyes are now fused with those fantastic lenses, and your sight is one with its object. And what exactly is that object? Obviously it is everything that fascinates, everything that has power over your gaze and your dreams. You cannot even conceive the wish to look away… an infinite and overwhelming scene expanding before you."

中文理解：「你的眼现已与那些奇透镜熔合，你的视与其对象为一。那对象究竟是何？显然是一切令你迷、一切掌控你凝视与梦境之物。你连移开目光的愿望都无法构想……一幅无限而压倒性的景象在你面前展开。」

句子结构：Your eyes are now fused with those fantastic lenses + and your sight is one with its object + And what exactly is that object? + Obviously it is everything that fascinates + You cannot even conceive the wish to look away + an infinite and overwhelming scene expanding before you。

关键词：fused with those fantastic lenses（与奇透镜熔合）、sight is one with its object（视与其对象为一）、everything that fascinates（一切令你迷之物）、power over your gaze（掌控你的凝视）、cannot conceive the wish to look away（连移目的愿望都无法构想）、infinite and overwhelming scene（无限而压倒性的景象）

表达方式：用「视与对象合一」写凝视的成瘾性——你看见的不再是外物，而是你被吸入的宇宙。

为什么这样写：眼镜的「魔法」实是凝视的绝症：它让你看见「一切」，却夺走你「不看」的能力。Ligotti 在此把求知欲写成一种无法松手的辖制——你越看越被吞。

### 第4处：镜中惊鸿——「hidden beneath the dust, was the face of Plomb」
> **原句 4:** I had squatted down before this rectangular relic, whose frame reminded me of the decorated borders of old books, and wiped my bare hand across its dusty surface. And there, hidden beneath the dust, was the face of Plomb, who must have just entered the shop and was standing a room's length away.

中文理解：我在那矩形遗物前蹲下，其框令我想起旧书装饰性的边栏，以 bare 手拂过积尘之面。而那尘下所藏，正是 Plomb 的脸——他必是刚进店，立于隔室之遥。

句子结构：I had squatted down before this rectangular relic + whose frame reminded me of… + and wiped my bare hand across its dusty surface + And there, hidden beneath the dust, was the face of Plomb（倒装）+ who 定语从句（must have just entered the shop）。

关键词：rectangular relic（矩形遗物）、decorated borders of old books（旧书装饰边栏）、wiped my bare hand across its dusty surface（以手拂过积尘）、hidden beneath the dust（尘下所藏）、face of Plomb（Plomb 的脸）

表达方式：用「镜中拂尘见旧友脸」写镜像的偶然揭示；叙述者成了镜中窥视者。

为什么这样写：二手店镜是全书最日常的反转——叙述者在镜里看见 Plomb，而 Plomb 也看见了他。这预示两人身份的倒错：原是叙述者掌控 Plomb，如今 Plomb 在镜（=眼镜的延伸）中反视他。Ligotti 让「看」成为双向的陷阱。

### 第5处：镜室与解体——「multiplied these visions into infinity, creating oceans of his own blood」
> **原句 5:** In his attempt to exhaust or exaggerate the visions to which he had apparently become enslaved, Plomb had done nothing less than multiplied these visions into infinity, creating oceans of his own blood and enabling himself to see with countless eyes. Entranced by such aspiration, I gazed at the mirrors in speechless wonder.

中文理解：在他试图穷尽或夸大那显然已奴役他的幻象之际，Plomb 所做的无非是将这些幻象乘为无穷，造出自身血的汪洋，并使己得以以无数眼视物。为这般志业所迷，我失语惊视那些镜。

句子结构：In his attempt to exhaust or exaggerate the visions + Plomb had done nothing less than multiplied these visions into infinity + creating oceans of his own blood + and enabling himself to see with countless eyes + Entranced by such aspiration + I gazed at the mirrors in speechless wonder。

关键词：exhaust or exaggerate the visions（穷尽或夸大幻象）、become enslaved（已奴役）、multiplied these visions into infinity（乘为无穷）、oceans of his own blood（自身血的汪洋）、see with countless eyes（以无数眼视物）、speechless wonder（失语惊视）

表达方式：用「乘为无穷 / 血的汪洋 / 无数眼」写成瘾的几何式膨胀——Plomb 把眼镜的「无限视野」实体化到满室镜中。

为什么这样写：Plomb 的终局是「视野的自杀」——他用镜复制眼镜，把无限看进自身血的循环里，终至解体。Ligotti 把求知欲的尽头写成肉体的崩解：你看见一切，于是你碎成一切。

### 第6处：追随之灵——「He now travels with me to the ends of the earth」
> **原句 6:** And even when I abandoned my home, with its hideous attic storeroom, Plomb still followed me in my dreams. He now travels with me to the ends of the earth, initiating me night after night into his unspeakable wonders. I can only hope that we will not meet in another place, one where the mysteries are always new and dreams never end. Oh, Plomb, will you not stay in that box where they have put your riven body?

中文理解：即便我弃了那狰狞阁楼密室之家，Plomb 仍在梦中随我。他如今与我同行至天涯，夜夜引我入他不可言说之奇观。我只盼我们莫在他界相逢——那处谜恒新、梦永不尽。哦 Plomb，你何不留在他们安放你裂躯的那匣中？

句子结构：And even when I abandoned my home + Plomb still followed me in my dreams + He now travels with me to the ends of the earth + initiating me night after night + I can only hope that we will not meet in another place + Oh, Plomb, will you not stay in that box where they have put your riven body。

关键词：abandoned my home（弃家）、followed me in my dreams（梦中随我）、travels with me to the ends of the earth（同行至天涯）、initiating me night after night（夜夜引我入）、mysteries always new and dreams never end（谜恒新梦永不尽）、riven body（裂躯）、that box（那匣）

表达方式：用「同行至天涯 / 夜夜引我」写追随之灵的不可逆；末句对 Plomb 的恳求，把叙述者降为被追者。

为什么这样写：终局反转——原是叙述者把眼镜给 Plomb，如今 Plomb 的幽灵反过来以「夜夜奇观」追索叙述者。Ligotti 的闭环：你投放的凝视会回到你身上。叙述者想「治愈」Plomb，反被 Plomb 的视野寄生。「that box」回扣开篇抽屉里的白匣，首尾咬合。

## 一句话总结

> 一篇好奇害死猫的元恐怖：叙述者把一对「从自己血中看见无限宇宙」的眼镜给门徒 Plomb 试戴，本想以过度惊奇令其厌倦神秘学；Plomb 却沉溺自残式以血饲镜，终在满镜血室解体而死；叙述者虽毁镜驱梦，仍被 Plomb 幽灵夜夜拖入「无穷视野」，成被自己造物追索的人。把「知识/视野的诱惑」写成传染性、不可逆的凝视绝症。
