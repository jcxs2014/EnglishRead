# 15. The Eternal Devotion Of The Governess To The Residents Of Bly（家庭女教师对布莱庄园住户的永恒献身 / 1985）

## 一句话总结

一篇对詹姆斯《螺丝在拧紧》的「回忆录补述」：女教师为求 Harley Street 那位叔父的垂青，对 Flora 与 Miles 倾注全身心，与死者 Quint/Jessel 争夺孩子灵魂；Miles 魂已碎、争抢中心脏停跳死在她怀中，她活到高寿却永失布莱——把经典「保护孩童」重写为一场以男人为目的的、注定落空的献身。

## 精读

### 第1处：雇用与坠落之爱——「fell hopelessly in love」

① The Governess is writing an account of her experiences at Bly, where she had charge of two parentless children by the names of Flora and Miles. She was hired for the job by the children's uncle, who had an office in Harley Street and with whom the governess fell hopelessly in love during the course of a very brief interview.

中文理解：家庭女教师正在写她在布莱的见闻录；她在那儿照管两个失去双亲的孩子，名唤 Flora 与 Miles。雇她的是孩子们的叔叔，此人在 Harley Street 有间办公室，而女教师就在一次极简短的面试中无可救药地爱上了他。

句子结构：主句 The Governess is writing an account… + where 定语从句（had charge of two parentless children）+ She was hired… by the uncle + 非限 who had an office… + and with whom 定语从句（fell hopelessly in love during… interview）。

关键词：writing an account（写见闻录）、two parentless children（两个失怙孩子）、fell hopelessly in love（无可救药地爱上）、a very brief interview（极简短的面试）

表达方式：用「极简短面试中无可救药地爱上」把动机写得荒唐而精确——爱的对象几乎不存在。

为什么这样写：Ligotti 点破原著的潜文本：女教师的「献身」从一开始就不是为孩子，而是为那个远在 Harley Street 的男人。重写把浪漫叙事的暗面摊开。

---

### 第2处：献身的赌注——「body and soul」

① The governess writes of her arrival at Bly, of her amazement at the two beautiful children, and of her resolve to devote herself body and soul to the upbringing in hopes that someday her devotion would be reciprocated by the man in Harley Street.

中文理解：女教师写她初到布莱的惊艳于两个美丽的孩子，以及她立誓要把身心全部献给养育之事，盼有朝一日那份献身能被 Harley Street 的男人回报。

句子结构：The governess writes of A, of B, and of C（三个 of 短语并列）+ in hopes that 目的从句（her devotion would be reciprocated by the man）。

关键词：amazement at（惊艳于）、resolve to devote herself body and soul（立誓身心全献）、upbringing（养育）、reciprocated（被回报）

表达方式：用「身心全献」+「盼被回报」把宗教式献身降格为交易式期待。

为什么这样写：devote herself body and soul 本属崇高语域，此处对象却是「盼叔叔回报」——崇高词与功利动机的错位，正是 Ligotti 反哥特反讽的典型笔法。

---

### 第3处：鬼魂的同盟——「unholy romantic alliance」

① The governess now writes of the horrors at Bly: how the ghosts of two former retainers, Miss Jessel and Peter Quint, are trying to possess the souls of the children and through them perpetuate the unholy romantic alliance that this notorious twosome carried in life.

中文理解：女教师如今写布莱的恐怖：昔日两名仆从——Jessel 小姐与 Peter Quint——的鬼魂，如何试图占据孩子们的灵魂，并借他们延续这对臭名昭著的二人组生前那段不圣洁的浪漫同盟。

句子结构：The governess writes of the horrors + how 从句（the ghosts… are trying to possess the souls + and through them perpetuate the unholy romantic alliance + that 定语从句 carried in life）。

关键词：former retainers（昔日仆从）、possess the souls（占据灵魂）、perpetuate（延续）、unholy romantic alliance（不圣洁的浪漫同盟）、notorious twosome（臭名昭著的二人组）

表达方式：用「unholy romantic alliance」把鬼魂的情欲写得像一桩需继承的遗产。

为什么这样写：原著的暧昧（Quint 与 Jessel 的关系）被直白写成「需借童身延续的浪漫同盟」，Ligotti 抽掉暗示、亮出骨架，反令恐怖更冷。

---

### 第4处：Miles 之死——「soul already shattered beyond repair」

① Inside the house Miles is standing fixed with fear when the governess comes to collect him. Staring at him through the paned windows of a pair of French doors is the face of Quint, while hovering over him is the face of the governess, and each is making a bid for the boy's soul. But Miles' soul is already shattered beyond repair, wrecked. Tragically the ensuing struggle causes his heart to stop beating. He lies dead in the arms of the governess.

中文理解：屋内，Miles 僵立恐惧，女教师来接他。透过一扇法式对开门的格窗瞪着他的是 Quint 的脸，而在他上方盘旋的则是女教师的脸，二者各在争夺这男孩的灵魂。但 Miles 的灵魂早已碎到无法修补、彻底毁了。可悲的是，随后的争夺令他心脏停跳。他死在女教师怀中。

句子结构：Inside the house 起 + Miles is standing + Staring… is the face of Quint + while hovering… is the face of the governess + and each is making a bid + But Miles' soul is already shattered + Tragically the struggle causes his heart to stop + He lies dead。

关键词：fixed with fear（僵立恐惧）、making a bid for the boy's soul（争夺男孩灵魂）、shattered beyond repair（碎到无法修补）、heart to stop beating（心脏停跳）、lies dead in her arms（死在她怀中）

表达方式：用倒装「Staring… is the face of Quint」制造窥视的凝视感；两「脸」分踞男孩两侧，把争夺写成对称构图。

为什么这样写：死亡的真正原因不是鬼，而是「灵魂早已碎」——女教师与鬼一同榨干了 Miles。Ligotti 在此把原著「谁杀了 Miles」的答案彻底改写：是他被两方同时占有而崩解。

---

### 第5处：长寿与永失——「the man from Harley Street never comes」

① With a great feeling of pain and loss the governess finishes her memoir of that dreadful episode at Bly. Despite the catastrophic outcome of her first position as a governess she will manage to secure employment at other houses. And she will live, in good health, to a ripe old age. But the man from Harley Street never comes, handsome little Miles is dead, and the governess will not see Bly again… as long as she lives.

中文理解：带着巨大的痛苦与失落，女教师写完了布莱那可怕一章的回忆录。尽管首份家教以灾难收场，她仍会在别家谋得职位，且身体康健活到高寿。但 Harley Street 的男人从未来过，俊美的小 Miles 已死，而女教师此生——只要她活着——再也见不到布莱。

句子结构：With 状语 + 主句 finishes her memoir + Despite 让步 + she will manage + And she will live + But 三连（the man never comes / Miles is dead / she will not see Bly again… as long as she lives）。

关键词：catastrophic outcome（灾难性结局）、secure employment（谋得职位）、ripe old age（高寿）、never comes（从未来过）、not see Bly again（再也见不到布莱）

表达方式：用「高寿却永失」的反差收束——她赢了对生活的顺从，输掉了全部执念。

为什么这样写：把经典的「女教师疯/疑」结局改成「她好好活着却永远失去」——更钝、更日常的恐怖。Harley Street 的男人「从未来过」是全文最轻也最重的一句：她的献身，自始至终无人领取。
