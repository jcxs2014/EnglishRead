# 19. The Scream: From 1800 To The Present（尖叫：从 1800 年至今 / 1985）

## 一句话总结

一篇对蒙克《呐喊》与「复仇怨灵」母题的长程反写：1800 年被勒杀的 William B. 喉间那声未及发出的尖叫，化作超自然武器却始终寻不到凶手；两个世纪后他栖身孤宅、又错爱一女子，终在「那声尖叫已自己死掉」的 revelations 中彻底 invisible——把复仇恐怖写成一则关于「未完成之痛随时间溃散、连怨灵都失能」的存在悲喜剧。

## 精读

### 第1处：勒杀与未竟之 scream——「a length of thin but strong rope around his neck」

① Near the close of the eighteenth century, William B. is approaching his destination of a saloon on Boston's waterfront. As he passes through a narrow alleyway someone jumps him from behind and wraps a length of thin but strong rope around his neck.

中文理解：十八世纪末，William B. 正走近波士顿滨水区一家酒馆的目的地。当他穿过窄巷，有人从背后扑来，将一截细却结实的绳绕上他脖颈。

句子结构：Near the close of the eighteenth century 时间状语 + 主句 William B. is approaching his destination + As 时间从句（passes through a narrow alleyway）+ someone jumps him + and wraps a length of thin but strong rope around his neck。

关键词：close of the eighteenth century（十八世纪末）、waterfront（滨水区）、narrow alleyway（窄巷）、jumps him from behind（从背后扑来）、a length of thin but strong rope（一截细却结实的绳）

表达方式：用「细却结实」这矛盾修饰写凶器——越不起眼越致命；开场即把蒙克式的「尖叫」锚进一场市井谋杀。

为什么这样写：Ligotti 把艺术史名画还原成赤裸的街头勒杀，「thin but strong rope」去浪漫化死亡，为后文「那声未能发出的尖叫」立下物理起点。

---

### 第2处：不义的死——「marvelous dreams which had sustained his life」

① While he is being choked to death he looks up and can see the moon over the tall shops and houses lining the alley. He knows he is going to die and cannot believe the injustice of it on almost every level: that he should die before he'd had a drink that night, that he should die without realizing a single one of the marvelous dreams which had sustained his life in the first place.

中文理解：被勒将死时他抬头，望见高耸店铺民居沿巷排列之上的月亮。他自知将死，且几乎在每个层面都无法相信这不公：他竟要在那夜未喝上一口酒前死去，竟要在未能实现哪怕一个、原本支撑他活下去的奇妙梦想前死去。

句子结构：While 从句（being choked to death）+ he looks up and can see the moon + He knows he is going to die + and cannot believe the injustice + 冒号列举（that he should die before… / that he should die without realizing… marvelous dreams which had sustained his life）。

关键词：choked to death（被勒死）、the moon over the tall shops（高耸店铺之上的月亮）、injustice of it（其不公）、before he'd had a drink（未喝上一口酒前）、marvelous dreams which had sustained his life（支撑他活下去的奇妙梦想）

表达方式：用「未喝上一口酒」「未实现一个梦想」把死亡的不公写得滑稽又心酸——宏大悲剧落到最卑微的遗憾。

为什么这样写：Ligotti 的恐怖常生于「错位的在乎」：William 临终想的不是家人而是酒与未竟之梦。这轻描淡写的不公，正是怨灵诞生的温床——痛不是来自死，而是来自「未完」。

---

### 第3处：怨灵的武器——「instrument of supernatural ferocity and horror」

① The spirit has such a marvelous revenge planned: to let loose its terrible scream, now an instrument of supernatural ferocity and horror, into the face of its murderer, killing him in one of the worst ways imagineable. But the strangler is never found.

中文理解：这魂灵曾筹划如此美妙的复仇：将那可怕的尖叫——如今已成超自然凶暴与恐怖的器具——灌入凶手脸中，以可想最糟的方式杀之。可勒杀者始终未被寻获。

句子结构：The spirit has such a marvelous revenge planned + 冒号 to let loose its terrible scream（同位 now an instrument of supernatural ferocity and horror）+ into the face of its murderer + killing him + But 转折（the strangler is never found）。

关键词：marvelous revenge（美妙的复仇）、let loose its terrible scream（放出那可怕尖叫）、instrument of supernatural ferocity and horror（超自然凶暴与恐怖的器具）、killing him in one of the worst ways（以最糟方式杀之）、never found（始终未被寻获）

表达方式：用「marvelous revenge」写怨灵的天真期待，与「never found」的冰冷现实对撞。

为什么这样写：复仇母题在此被「找不到凶手」架空——怨灵有武器无目标。Ligotti 把 Gothic 复仇剧的引擎（寻仇）抽掉，让恐怖悬在「永远待发」的状态。

---

### 第4处：失能的永恒——「haunted by its unfulfilled quest for vengeance」

① Eventually the passing years exceed the longest possible human life span. The murderer has undoubtedly been dead for some time. And how many years still remain to the spirit, haunted by its unfulfilled quest for vengeance!

中文理解：终有一天，逝去的年岁超出了人类可能的最长寿命。那凶手无疑已死有些时日了。而这魂灵还有多少年要熬——被那未竟的复仇追寻所缠！

句子结构：Eventually 起 + the passing years exceed the longest possible human life span + The murderer has been dead + And 感叹句（how many years still remain to the spirit, haunted by its unfulfilled quest for vengeance）。

关键词：exceed the longest possible human life span（超出人类最长寿命）、undoubtedly been dead（无疑已死）、haunted by（被……所缠）、unfulfilled quest for vengeance（未竟的复仇追寻）、how many years still remain（还有多少年要熬）

表达方式：用「how many years still remain」的感叹把永恒写成刑罚——怨灵赢不了，连「结束」都被剥夺。

为什么这样写：时间在此是怨灵唯一的敌人。凶手自然死亡，怨灵却被困在「本可复仇」的虚拟里。Ligotti 写的是执念的半衰期：恨比人活得久，却也终将无意义地耗散。

---

### 第5处：错爱的慰藉——「his anguished and imprisoned scream sustaining his presence」

① The spirit is now thankful for its fate, realizing that it is his anguished and imprisoned scream sustaining his presence. While he has the scream within him he can stay on earth and be seen. He now holds it inside like something extremely precious.

中文理解：这魂灵如今对命运心怀感激，意识到正是他那痛苦而囚禁般的尖叫在维系他的存在。只要尖叫还在体内，他便留于地上、被人得见。他如今把它像极珍贵之物般藏在里头。

句子结构：The spirit is now thankful + 现在分词 realizing that（it is his anguished and imprisoned scream sustaining his presence）+ While 从句（he has the scream within him）+ he can stay on earth and be seen + He now holds it inside like something precious。

关键词：thankful for its fate（对命运感激）、anguished and imprisoned scream（痛苦而囚禁般的尖叫）、sustaining his presence（维系他的存在）、stay on earth and be seen（留地被人见）、holds it inside like something precious（像珍物般藏在里头）

表达方式：用「尖叫维系存在」把痛苦写成生命线——越痛越「活」，存在与受苦同构。

为什么这样写：存在主义反转：怨灵的「活着」依赖那声未发的尖叫。痛不是要消除的，而是要珍藏的——因为失去痛，就失去自己。这是 Ligotti 对「受苦即存在」的冷峻肯定。

---

### 第6处：终极 revelation——「the scream itself has died its own death」

① And it was nothing. For the spirit now suffers the horrific revelation that after so many years the scream itself has died its own death, and has left him not only utterly alone, but also completely imperceptible behind his private wall of eternity.

中文理解：而那本算不了什么。因为这魂灵如今遭受可怖的 revelation：经年累月后，那尖叫自身已死它自己的死，留他不仅彻底孤独，更在他私有的永恒之墙后全然不可被察。

句子结构：And it was nothing + For 因果（the spirit suffers the horrific revelation that 从句）+ the scream itself has died its own death + and has left him not only utterly alone + but also completely imperceptible behind his private wall of eternity。

关键词：it was nothing（那本算不了什么）、horrific revelation（可怖的启示）、the scream itself has died its own death（尖叫自身已死它自己的死）、utterly alone（彻底孤独）、completely imperceptible behind his private wall of eternity（永恒之墙后全然不可察）

表达方式：用「the scream itself has died its own death」这一递归死亡句，把「未发出的尖叫」最终也消亡——连怨灵的怨都过期了。

为什么这样写：终局点题：复仇、存在、甚至痛，都各有其半衰期。当尖叫自己死掉，怨灵失去「被看见」的凭据，成了一段无人知晓、连自己都不可见的永恒。Ligotti 把哥特怨灵写到比死更空的「失效」——这是他对恐怖最僻静的一笔：不是被鬼吓，而是连做鬼的资格都随时间蒸发。
