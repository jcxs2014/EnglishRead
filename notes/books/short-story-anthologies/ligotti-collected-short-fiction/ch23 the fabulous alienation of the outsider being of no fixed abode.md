# 23. The Fabulous Alienation Of The Outsider, Being Of No Fixed Abode（无固定居所的外来者之奇绝疏离 / 1985）

## 一句话总结

一篇对坡《外来者》的「末世翻转」：穴居的 Outsider 爬上地面闯入宴会，被镜中真相吓退——「吓跑众人的正是你自己」；他退回地下又重返地表，却发现全世界已在他离席时被死寂吞没，唯剩他这「死者」独活；终局他因自身痛楚爆发、碎裂，却意外以碎片的能量复苏了远日——把哥特孤独扩成一则关于「无他者则无自我」的宇宙悖论。

## 精读

### 第1处：穴居与遗忘——「shadowwearied eyes」

① The outsider lifts his shadowwearied eyes and gazes about the moldy chamber where, to his knowledge, he has always lived. He has no recollection of who he is or how he came to dwell so far removed from others of his kind who, he reasons, must exist, perhaps in that world above which he vividly recalls, though he glimsed it only once and long ago.

中文理解：外来者抬起他那被阴影累坏的眼，望向这霉腐的舱室——据他所知，他一直住在此处。他记不起自己是谁、如何来到这远离同类（他推想同类必存在）的地方；同类或许在那上方世界，那世界他生动记得，尽管只在久前惊鸿一瞥。

句子结构：The outsider lifts his shadowwearied eyes + and gazes about the moldy chamber（where 定语从句 he has always lived）+ He has no recollection of who he is or how he came + 定语从句（others of his kind who must exist, perhaps in that world above which he vividly recalls, though he glimsed it only once）。

关键词：shadowwearied eyes（被阴影累坏的眼）、moldy chamber（霉腐的舱室）、no recollection of who he is（记不起自己是谁）、far removed from others of his kind（远离同类）、world above（上方世界）、glimsed it only once（只瞥过一次）

表达方式：用「shadowwearied eyes」这一自造复合词写被幽闭榨干的视线；「glimsed」（原文拼写）保留扫描 typo 以逐字忠实。

为什么这样写：坡《外来者》的「不知己、念同类」母题被 Ligotti 保留并推到宇宙尺度。shadowwearied 把「被阴影所累」名词化，奠定全篇「存在即被阴影磨损」的基调。

### 第2处：出穴寻伴——「friendly lights and, he hopes, friendly faces」

① One night the outsider emerges from his underground domain and, guided solely by the glowing moon he has never really seen before, scrambles down a dark road, searching for friendly lights and, he hopes, friendly faces.

中文理解：一夜，外来者从地下域爬出，仅凭那轮他从未真正见过的发光之月指引，沿黑路跌撞下行，寻索友善的灯火，以及——他盼着——友善的面孔。

句子结构：One night 起 + the outsider emerges from his underground domain + and 分词 guided solely by the glowing moon（定语从句 he has never really seen before）+ scrambles down a dark road + 现在分词 searching for friendly lights and, he hopes, friendly faces。

关键词：emerges from his underground domain（从地下域爬出）、glowing moon he has never seen（从未见过的发光之月）、scrambles down a dark road（沿黑路跌撞下行）、friendly lights（友善的灯火）、friendly faces（友善的面孔）

表达方式：用「he hopes」的插入把渴望写得怯生；「从未见过的月」暗示他本属地下、地面于他是初次。

为什么这样写：出穴动机是「寻求他者」——但 Ligotti 在此埋下反讽：他寻求的「友善面孔」，恰是他自己没有、也终将吓退众人的东西。寻伴之旅从一开始就朝向 rejection。

### 第3处：镜中真相——「It was you」

① That is to say, he has been abandoned to the company of that untimely horror which initially set those gay and fine-looking people so indecorously on their heels. "What was it?" he asks himself, posing the question over and over with seemingly infinite repitition before finally collecting wits to squint a little to one side. "What was it?" he asks for the infinite time add one or two. "It was you," answers the mirror. "It was you."

中文理解：也就是说，他已被弃于那「不合时宜的恐怖」为伴——正是这恐怖起初让那些欢快体面的人如此失态地夺路而逃。「那是什么？」他自问，一遍遍抛出这问题、似有无尽重复，终才定神、微微侧目斜视。「那是什么？」他又问了无穷次加一两回。「是你，」镜答。「是你。」

句子结构：That is to say 起 + he has been abandoned to the company of that untimely horror（定语从句 set those people on their heels）+ "What was it?" he asks himself + posing the question over and over + before finally collecting wits + "What was it?" he asks + "It was you," answers the mirror. "It was you."

关键词：abandoned to the company of（被弃于……为伴）、untimely horror（不合时宜的恐怖）、indecorously on their heels（失态夺路）、infinite repitition（无尽重复）、squint a little to one side（微微侧目斜视）、It was you（是你）

表达方式：用「infinite time add one or two」这一荒诞计数写痴妄；镜答「It was you」把恐惧源归给主体自身。

为什么这样写：坡原作的「镜中见尸」在此被改成「镜中说'是你'」——吓跑众人的不是怪物，而是外来者自己。Ligotti 把哥特恐怖内移：你以为在逃某物，实则那物即你。这是全书「自我即他者之恐」的高潮一笔。

### 第4处：退回与「birthright of sloth」——「subhumous envelope from which he never should have strayed」

① Now it is the outsider's turn to make his getaway from that hideous living corpse of unholy and unwholesome familiarity, that thing which had imperfectly decomposed in its subterranean resting place. He seeks refuge in a chaotic dreamworld where no one really notices the dead and no one even looks twice at the disgusting. Eventually, however, he tires of this deranged, though unhostile dimension of alienage. His heart more pulverized than simply broken, he decides to return to the subhumous envelope from which he never should have strayed, there to reclaim his birthright of sloth, amnesia, and darkness.

中文理解：如今轮到外来者从那「可憎活尸、不圣不洁的熟悉物」逃开——那物在其地下安息处腐坏得并不彻底。他避入混沌梦界，那儿无人真在意死者，无人对可憎者多看一眼。然而终有一日他厌了这错乱却无恶意的外异之境。心碎更甚于碎、近乎成粉，他决意回到那「subhumous 包膜」——他本不该偏离之处，去重认自己慵懒、失忆与黑暗的与生权。

句子结构：Now it is… turn to make his getaway from that hideous living corpse + that thing which had decomposed + He seeks refuge in a chaotic dreamworld + Eventually he tires of this dimension + His heart more pulverized than broken 独立主格 + he decides to return to the subhumous envelope（定语从句 he never should have strayed）+ there to reclaim his birthright of sloth, amnesia, and darkness。

关键词：hideous living corpse（可憎活尸）、subterranean resting place（地下安息处）、chaotic dreamworld（混沌梦界）、deranged though unhostile（错乱却无恶意）、heart more pulverized than broken（心碎更甚于碎）、birthright of sloth, amnesia, and darkness（慵懒/失忆/黑暗的与生权）

表达方式：用「birthright of sloth, amnesia, and darkness」把地下状态写成一种「权利」——存在之默认态。

为什么这样写：外来者的悲剧是「离开本该在的暗处」。Ligotti 在此把坡的孤独反转成归属：地下不是惩罚，而是他与生俱来的权利；地面（他者世界）才是使他「成怪」的误入。返回暗处，是回家的企图——却已太迟。

---

### 第5处：末世空世——「an atrocious paradox of fate」

① This possibility is now, of course, as defunct as the planet itself. With all biology in tatters, the outsider will never again hear the consoling gasps of those who shunned him and in whose eyes and hearts he achieved a certain tangible identity, however loathsome. Without the others he simply cannot go on being himself—for there is no longer anyone to be outside of. In no time at all he is overwhelmed by this atrocious paradox of fate.

中文理解：这可能性如今自然与行星同朽。万物生机碎裂，外来者再听不见那些避他者的慰藉性倒吸——曾在他们眼与心裡他获致某种可触的「身份」，无论多可憎。无他者，他根本无法继续是「自己」——因为再无人可供他「在外」。转瞬他便被这「命运的凶残悖论」淹没。

句子结构：This possibility is now as defunct as the planet + With all biology in tatters + the outsider will never hear the gasps of those who shunned him（定语从句 in whose eyes he achieved identity）+ Without the others he cannot go on being himself + for there is no longer anyone to be outside of + In no time he is overwhelmed by this atrocious paradox of fate。

关键词：defunct as the planet（与行星同朽）、all biology in tatters（万物生机碎裂）、consoling gasps of those who shunned him（避他者的慰藉性倒吸）、tangible identity（可触的身份）、no longer anyone to be outside of（再无人可供在外）、atrocious paradox of fate（命运的凶残悖论）

表达方式：用「no longer anyone to be outside of」这一句式把「疏离」推到逻辑尽头——外部消失，则「外部者」不复存在。

为什么这样写：题眼。坡《外来者》的孤独是个人悲剧；Ligotti 把它升为本体论：「自我」依赖「他者」而成立，他者尽灭则自我亦无据。atrocious paradox 即——你越想借他人确证自己，越在他者消失时失去自己。这是全篇的哲学内核。

---

### 第6处：碎裂与意外复苏——「shatters his rotting shape into innumerable fragments」

① In the middle of this revelation, a feeling begins to well up in the outsider, an incalculable sorrow deep inside. From the center of his being (which now is the center of all being that remains in existence) he summons a suicidal outburst of pain whose force shatters his rotting shape into innumerable fragments. Catastrophically enough, this antic, designed to conclude universal genocide, gives off such energy that the distant sun is revived by a transfusion of warmth and light. And each fragment of the outsider cast far across the earth now absorbs the warmth and catches the light, reflecting the future life and festivals of a resurrected race of beings.

中文理解：在此 revelation 之中，外来者心底涌起一种情感——深处无可计量的悲。从他存在的中心（如今也是存世一切存在的中心），他召出一次自杀式的痛之爆发，其力将他腐坏之形碎成千百片。够 catastrophic 的是，这原欲终结普世灭杀的滑稽举动，竟放出如此能量，令遥远的日凭温光之输而复苏。而外来者每一片散落大地的碎屑，如今吸温挹光，映照出一族复活生灵未来的生命与节庆。

句子结构：In the middle of this revelation 起 + a feeling begins to well up + an incalculable sorrow + From the center of his being（定语从句 which now is the center of all being）+ he summons a suicidal outburst of pain + whose force shatters his rotting shape into fragments + Catastrophically enough 起 + this antic gives off such energy that the sun is revived + And each fragment… absorbs the warmth and catches the light, reflecting the future life。

关键词：incalculable sorrow（无可计量的悲）、center of all being that remains（存世一切存在的中心）、suicidal outburst of pain（自杀式痛之爆发）、shatters into innumerable fragments（碎成千百片）、revived by a transfusion of warmth and light（凭温光之输复苏）、resurrected race of beings（复活的一族生灵）

表达方式：用「antic designed to conclude universal genocide」把自杀写成一场闹剧式的灭世企图，反得创世之果——极致的反讽闭环。

为什么这样写：终局把坡的绝望翻成创世神话。外来者欲以死终结一切，却因「他是仅存的存在中心」而让死变成了生：他的碎片成了新种族的光之镜。Ligotti 在此完成最诡异的温柔——最彻底的孤独者，意外成了万物复苏的源。而末句「面对冷硬玻璃的莫名恐惧」暗示新族仍将重演「外在之恐」，轮回不破。
