# 22. The Interminable Residence Of The Friends Of The House Of Usher（厄舍府友人之无尽居留 / 1985）

## 一句话总结

一篇对坡《厄舍府的倒塌》的「访客视角翻案」：男主在府崩前惊险逃出，却发现无处可去——Usher 兄妹是「被家族遗传的怪癖弱点」轻易送进毁灭，而他是「凭自己自由意志、不问一句」留下并留到最后的；终局他明白自己也必须被那潭病水吞没——把经典「外来见证者幸免」改写成「自愿共谋者同沉」。

## 精读

### 第1处：逃出与无叶之树——「escape the House of Usher」

① A man of average height and features has just managed to escape the House of Usher only seconds before it collapses full force deep into the murky waters of the adjacent tarn. The man runs under a nearby tree, seeking refuge from the violent storm which began and consummated that evening's catastrophe. Once there, however, he looks up and notes that the tree is leafless. (Its limp branches are flowing freely in the wind and even its roots, unearthed, are waving around.)

中文理解：一个身高相貌均平平的男子，刚在厄舍府整座坠入相邻幽潭浊水前几秒设法逃出。他跑到附近一棵树下，躲避那场开启并终结了当晚惨剧的暴风雨。然而一到那儿，他抬头便注意到那树竟无叶。（它软垂的枝在风里自在飘摆，连出土的根也在四下挥舞。）

句子结构：A man… has just managed to escape the House of Usher + 时间状 only seconds before it collapses… into the murky waters + The man runs under a tree + seeking refuge from the storm + Once there 起 + he looks up and notes that the tree is leafless + 括号（Its limp branches… roots… waving）。

关键词：escape the House of Usher（逃出厄舍府）、collapses full force（整座坠入）、murky waters of the adjacent tarn（相邻幽潭的浊水）、seeking refuge（寻避护）、leafless（无叶的）、unearthing roots waving（出土根挥舞）

表达方式：用「无叶之树、出土挥舞的根」写逃出生天后的错愕——连避护所都是死的/倒错的。

为什么这样写：坡原作的「逃离者」在此一逃出便撞见更诡异的景：树无叶、根出土。Ligotti 暗示逃离厄舍不等于逃离厄舍的法则——外部世界也被同化。

---

### 第2处：Roderick 的病——「impressed his childhood friend as a very sick man」

① The man of average height and features was spending a few days at the House of Usher at the invitation of a friend and former classmate, Roderick, who along with his twin sister, Madeline, owned the house and a fair amount of surrounding property, including a graveyard. Roderick immediately impressed his childhood friend as a very sick man. Only the softest sounds, the dimmest light, and a generally immobile routine could be tolerated by his morbidly keen senses and nervous system.

中文理解：这身高相貌平平的男子，是应友人兼旧同窗 Roderick 之邀在厄舍府小住几日；Roderick 与孪妹 Madeline 共有此宅及周遭不少地产，含一处墓地。Roderick 立刻给童年好友留下「病得很重」的印象。唯有最柔的声、最暗的光、与大体不动的 routine，才为他病态敏锐的感官与神经所容。

句子结构：The man was spending… at the invitation of Roderick（who 定语从句 owned the house… including a graveyard）+ Roderick immediately impressed his friend as a very sick man + Only the softest sounds… could be tolerated by his morbidly keen senses。

关键词：invitation of a friend and former classmate（应旧同窗之邀）、twin sister Madeline（孪妹 Madeline）、impressed… as a very sick man（留下病重的印象）、morbidly keen senses（病态敏锐的感官）、immobile routine（不动的 routine）

表达方式：用「only the softest… dimmest… immobile」的极端耐受阈值写 Roderick 的病，把哥特病弱写成感官的囚笼。

为什么这样写：访客是「被邀的普通人」，与 Roderick 的病形成对照——后者是「家族遗传的弱者」，前者是「自投的常人」。这为终局「自愿同沉」的论断铺下身份对照。

---

### 第3处：Madeline 之「死」——「locked Madeline in the family crypt while she was still alive」

① His housemate tries to calm him down by reading from a storybook. But Roderick is inconsolable and now claims that the two of them locked Madeline in the family crypt while she was still alive. His friend is unnerved by this outburst. He had no idea things were so bad. This was madness!

中文理解：他的室友试着念故事书安抚他。可 Roderick 无法慰藉，如今声称他俩把 Madeline 锁进家族墓穴时她仍活着。友人被这爆发吓住。他浑然不知事态如此之糟。这简直疯了！

句子结构：His housemate tries to calm him + But Roderick is inconsolable + and now claims that 从句（locked Madeline in the crypt while she was still alive）+ His friend is unnerved + He had no idea + This was madness。

关键词：calm him down by reading（念书安抚）、inconsolable（无法慰藉的）、locked Madeline in the family crypt（把 Madeline 锁入家族墓穴）、while she was still alive（她仍活着时）、unnerved by this outburst（被这爆发吓住）、madness（疯狂）

表达方式：用「念故事书安抚」这一幼稚动作对照 Roderick 的崩溃，凸显访客的无力与天真。

为什么这样写：坡原作的核心恐怖（活埋 Madeline）被压缩成一句「claims」——Ligotti 让访客「不信」，把悬念从「是否活埋」转为「旁观者的共谋无觉」。这正指向终局主题：你「没问」就是同谋。

---

### 第4处：崩塌与幸存——「both end up as a lifeless heap on the floor」

① Even worse, Roderick is proved to be telling the truth when his sister staggers into the room, falls upon her twin, and they both end up as a lifeless heap on the floor. The man of average height and features barely manages to get out of the house before that too goes down. He stares at the empty lot where the House of Usher used to be, and then he turns away to seek a haven far removed from the site of this terrible ordeal.

中文理解：更糟的是，当妹妹踉跄入房、扑倒孪生身上、二者终成地上一堆无生命之躯时，Roderick 的话被证为真。那身高相貌平平的男子勉强在宅第坠毁前逃出。他盯着厄舍府原址的空地，随后转身去寻远离这可怕煎熬之所的避难地。

句子结构：Even worse 起 + Roderick is proved right when 从句（sister staggers in, falls upon her twin, both end up lifeless heap）+ The man barely manages to get out + He stares at the empty lot + and turns away to seek a haven。

关键词：proved to be telling the truth（被证为真）、staggers into the room（踉跄入房）、lifeless heap on the floor（地上一堆无生命之躯）、barely manages to get out（勉强逃出）、empty lot where the House used to be（府第原址的空地）、seek a haven（寻避难地）

表达方式：用「empty lot」把轰然倒塌收束成一片虚无；「转身寻避难地」是经典幸存者的本能动作。

为什么这样写：到此仍是坡的骨架。但 Ligotti 让叙事在此「停一拍」——幸存者以为故事结束了，下一句才揭破：他根本无处可去。这是反哥特的关键转折。

---

### 第5处：无处可去——「the diseased waters await his embrace」

① But before he can take a single step he realizes that there is no longer anyplace he can go, no longer anyone who will have him. Oh, the books, the shadows, and the horrible entombment of that poor girl. How did he ever get into this one! While the Ushers were effortlessly delivered to their doom by the hereditary freaks and weaknesses of their family, he came to the house, and stayed, of his own free will, and by the same will, without asking a single question, he too must now be consumed by the tarn whose diseased waters await his embrace.

中文理解：可他迈不出一步便意识到，已再无他去之处，再无人肯收容他。哦，那些书、那些影，还有那可怜女孩可怖的入葬。他怎会卷入这桩！Usher 兄妹是被家族遗传的怪癖与弱点轻易送进毁灭，而他来到这宅、留下，凭的是自己的自由意志；凭同一意志、未问一句，他如今也必被那潭吞没——其病水正待他投怀。

句子结构：But before he can take a step 起 + he realizes that there is no longer anyplace / no longer anyone + Oh 感叹（the books, the shadows, the entombment）+ How did he ever get into this one + While 从句（Ushers delivered by hereditary freaks）+ he came… of his own free will + and by the same will he too must be consumed by the tarn whose diseased waters await his embrace。

关键词：no longer anyplace he can go（再无他去处）、hereditary freaks and weaknesses（遗传的怪癖与弱点）、of his own free will（凭自己自由意志）、without asking a single question（未问一句）、consumed by the tarn（被潭吞没）、diseased waters await his embrace（病水待他投怀）

表达方式：用「await his embrace」把死亡写成情人的相拥——诡异的温柔。

为什么这样写：题眼。Usher 兄妹的毁灭是「命运/遗传」，访客的毁灭是「自由意志+不问」。Ligotti 在此完成对坡的翻转：幸存者不是无辜见证者，而是自愿共谋者；你「没问」不是清白，而是同沉的资格。diseased waters await his embrace 把同化写得近乎情愿。
