# SEPTEMBER 12, 1878 CLEOPATRA'S NEEDLE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Cleopatra's Needle 得名于运载它的那艘船，1819 年被赠给英国政府。这座三千五百岁、六十八英尺高的纪念物，是埃及人民献给 Admiral Nelson 在 Battle of the Nile 获胜的敬意之作。然而由于没有专款购置，这座方尖碑在 Alexandria 多留了五十多年，直到一位名叫 Sir William James Erasmus Wilson 的名医出资支付了把它运往英格兰的费用。不幸的是，Cleopatra——那艘由 Olga 拖曳的浮动驳船——开始在浪中翻滚。Olga 为抢救货物，派六名志愿者乘一条小救援船去稳住驳船。那条船倾覆，六人全部溺亡。Cleopatra's Needle 两侧各有一尊狮身人面像，然而没人能说得准为何这座碑被装错了方向：那些狮身人面像本该背对碑身、守护着它，如今却正面对着它。这些青铜造物身上刻着 hieroglyphics，写的是 netjer nefer men-kheper-re di ankh（即善神，图特摩斯三世获赐生命）。也许这些朝向反了的狮身人面像正对周围人口产生不良影响：在 Cleopatra's Needle 一带，已有跳入泰晤士河自杀人数上升的报告。瞻仰那座花岗岩巨碑的访客常听见嘲弄般的 ethereal 笑声。而一个赤裸男子纵身跃入泰晤士河深处的鬼影，更是频频出现。人们不禁要问：那嘲弄的笑声与自杀人数的上升是否直接相关？也许那个自杀的赤裸鬼魂正在寻找可以随他一起的亡魂。
- **篇目日期**：SEPTEMBER 12, 1878（标题所载日期；正文另给 1819 年赠予与 1726 年前的年份）
- **事件年代**：1819 年赠给英国政府 → 此后方尖碑在 Alexandria 滞留五十多年 → 沉船溺亡事件与石碑抵英的具体年份未给
- **地点**：Embankment, London（Cleopatra's Needle 与泰晤士河）
- **异象类型**：朝向装反的狮身人面像（嘲弄笑声与频出的赤裸跳水鬼影）
- **叙事手法**：Cleopatra's Needle 得名于运它的船 → 1819 年赠给英国政府 → 三千五百岁、六十八英尺高的纪念物 → 是埃及人民献给 Nelson 在 Battle of the Nile 获胜的敬意 → 但没有专款购置 → 方尖碑在 Alexandria 多留五十多年 → 直到名医 Sir William James Erasmus Wilson 出资运费 → 不幸的是那艘叫 Cleopatra 的浮动驳船开始翻滚 → Olga 派六名志愿者乘小救援船稳住它 → 船倾覆六人溺亡 → 碑两侧各有狮身人面像 → 没人说得准为何装错方向 → 本该背对碑身守护它、如今正面对着它 → 青铜造物刻着 hieroglyphics、写的是善神获赐生命 → 也许朝向装反正对周围人口产生不良影响 → 一带跳泰晤士河自杀人数上升的报告 → 瞻仰巨碑的访客常听见嘲弄般的笑声 → 赤裸男子纵身跃入河中的鬼影频频出现 → 笑声与自杀是否直接相关 → 也许那个鬼魂在寻找随他一起的亡魂

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Cleopatra's Needle, named after the ship it sailed on, was gifted to the British Government in 1819. This 3,500-year-old, sixty-eight-foot monument was a tribute from the Egyptian people, commemorating the victory of Admiral Nelson at the Battle of the Nile. However, without the funds dedicated to procure the statue, the obelisk remained in Alexandria for more than fifty years, until a prestigious doctor, by the name of Sir William James Erasmus Wilson, paid for its transportation to England."

- **中文理解**：Cleopatra's Needle 得名于运载它的那艘船，1819 年被赠送给英国政府。这座三千五百岁、六十八英尺高的纪念物，是埃及人民的一份敬意，纪念 Admiral Nelson 在 Battle of the Nile 取得的胜利。然而由于没有专门拨来购置这座雕像的款项，方尖碑在 Alexandria 多留了五十多年，直到一位名叫 Sir William James Erasmus Wilson 的名医出资支付了把它运往英格兰的运费。
- **关键词**：Cleopatra's Needle, named after the ship it sailed on / was gifted to the British Government in 1819 / This 3,500-year-old, sixty-eight-foot monument / was a tribute from the Egyptian people / commemorating the victory of Admiral Nelson at the Battle of the Nile / without the funds dedicated to procure the statue / the obelisk remained in Alexandria for more than fifty years / a prestigious doctor, by the name of Sir William James Erasmus Wilson / paid for its transportation to England
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 named after the ship it sailed on 一句交代名字，**却不点那艘船是什么船——名字的来源被留着，让它与下一段里那艘翻覆的 Cleopatra 直接对上**。而 without the funds ... remained ... fifty years 与直到某人付运费连成一条延迟线，**这座碑在 Alexandria 多站了半个世纪这件事，被写成钱的问题而不是政治的问题**。
- **读者视角提示**：那位名医为何愿意出资、当时英国与埃及关系如何，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "Unfortunately, Cleopatra, the floating pontoon being towed by the Olga, began to roil in the waves. The Olga, struggling to save the cargo, sent six volunteers on a small rescue boat to stabilize the pontoon. The boat capsized and all six men drowned."

- **中文理解**：不幸的是，Cleopatra——那艘由 Olga 拖曳的浮动驳船——开始在浪中翻滚。Olga 为抢救货物，派了六名志愿者乘一条小救援船去稳住驳船。那条船倾覆了，六人全部溺亡。
- **关键词**：Unfortunately, Cleopatra, the floating pontoon being towed by the Olga / began to roil in the waves / The Olga, struggling to save the cargo / sent six volunteers on a small rescue boat / to stabilize the pontoon / The boat capsized and all six men drowned
- **为什么这样写**：作者在这里让 Cleopatra 这个名字第二次出现，**而这一次它指的是一艘正在浪里翻滚的驳船——与上一段里那座以它命名的石碑只隔一个 unfortunately**。全段只有两句动作，第二句却把救援写成了灾难的起点：**去稳住它的人，正是翻船死掉的人**。
- **读者视角提示**：那六人是谁、驳船与石碑此后如何，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "Cleopatra's Needle has two sphinxes on either side of it. However, one cannot be sure why the Needle was installed incorrectly. The sphinxes, rather than having their back toward the obelisk, guarding it, are facing toward it. The bronze creatures are adorned with hieroglyphics that say netjer nefer men-kheper-re di ankh (the good god, Thuthmosis III given life). Perhaps the reversed sphinxes are having an adverse effect on the general population, as increased suicides by jumping into the River Thames have been reported in the general area of Cleopatra's Needle. Visitors taking in the grand, granite obelisk often hear mocking ethereal laughter. And the ghostly specter of a naked man plunging into the depths of the Thames is a frequent occurrence. One has to wonder if the mocking laughter and increased suicides are directly related. Maybe the suicidal, naked ghost is in search of souls to join him."

- **中文理解**：Cleopatra's Needle 两侧各有一尊狮身人面像，然而没人能说得准为何这座碑被装错了方向：那些狮身人面像本该背对碑身、守护着它，如今却正面对着它。这些青铜造物身上刻着象形文字，写的是 netjer nefer men-kheper-re di ankh（即善神，图特摩斯三世获赐生命）。也许这些朝向装反的狮身人面像，正对周围人口产生不良影响：在 Cleopatra's Needle 一带，已有跳进泰晤士河自杀人数上升的报告。瞻仰那座宏伟的花岗岩方尖碑的访客，常听见嘲弄般的非人笑声。而一个赤裸男子纵身跃入泰晤士河深处的鬼影，更是频频出现。人们不禁要想：那嘲弄的笑声与自杀人数的上升是否直接相关？也许那个自杀的赤裸鬼魂，正在寻找可以随他一起的亡魂。
- **关键词**：Cleopatra's Needle has two sphinxes on either side of it / one cannot be sure why the Needle was installed incorrectly / The sphinxes, rather than having their back toward the obelisk, guarding it / are facing toward it / The bronze creatures are adorned with hieroglyphics that say / netjer nefer men-kheper-re di ankh (the good god, Thuthmosis III given life) / as increased suicides by jumping into the River Thames have been reported / in the general area of Cleopatra's Needle / Visitors taking in the grand, granite obelisk often hear mocking ethereal laughter / the ghostly specter of a naked man plunging into the depths of the Thames / is a frequent occurrence / One has to wonder if the mocking laughter and increased suicides are directly related / Maybe the suicidal, naked ghost is in search of souls to join him
- **为什么这样写**：作者用一个 rather than 的对照句把该有的姿势与实际的姿势摆开，**而 the good god, Thuthmosis III given life 这句咒语说的是赐生与守护，落进「朝向装反」的段落里，反差就出来了**。全段最见结构的是最后两句连问：先是 One has to wonder if 那笑声与自杀是否相关，紧接着 Maybe 那鬼魂在找人同去——**一个把两件事并排放，一个替它们连上因**。
- **读者视角提示**：那些跳河者的姓名与年份、朝向为何装反，**本篇没写**。

## 一句话总结

埃及赠给英国的 Cleopatra's Needle 因经费拖延半个世纪才运抵，运它的驳船翻覆、六名救援者溺亡；碑旁朝向装反的狮身人面像似与当地跳河自杀人数上升和频频现身的赤裸鬼影有关。
