# OCTOBER 12, 1898 LARNACH CASTLE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：这座用最好的材料建成的城堡，是商人、银行家兼政治人物 William James Mudie Larnach 为他可爱的妻子 Eliza Jane 建的礼物。这份不渝之情的表达具备童话的全部要素，可它的收场更像一桩希腊悲剧。两人搬进去不久，Eliza 在自己的卧房里因中风独自死去。悲痛欲绝却又需要有人相伴的 William，娶了 Eliza 的妹妹 Mary Alleyne。五年后她也死于血中毒。William 又娶了一次，这位是 Constance，比他小二十一岁。第三次婚姻五个月后，他心爱的女儿 Kate 死于伤寒，年纪轻轻才二十一岁。悲剧再次降临——William 察觉到 Constance 正与他最宠爱的儿子 Douglas 有染。Douglas 因自己做的事愧疚难当，无法承受这份背叛，committed suicide 之后自尽。几年后，生意败落的 William 走了儿子同样的路，开枪自尽。他死后，城堡与他的产业被拍卖出去。它先成了一处私人住宅，又成了一家精神病院，最后成了一家世界级的酒店。Larnach 一家众灵的显形在城堡许多房间都能见到，他们在死后达成了生前没能得到的——团聚。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 12, 1898（标题所载年份，即 William 去世之年）
- **事件年代**：Eliza 搬入后不久去世；Mary 五年后死于血中毒；Kate 死于第三次婚姻五个月后；Douglas 自尽；William 在生意败落后开枪自尽（1898 年）
- **地点**：Dunedin, New Zealand（Larnach Castle 的卧房与许多房间）
- **异象类型**：Larnach 一家众灵（在城堡各处显形，死后聚在一起）
- **叙事手法**：最好的材料建成城堡 → William James Mudie Larnach 送给妻子 Eliza Jane → 像童话开场 → 收场像希腊悲剧 → 搬进去不久 Eliza 卧房中风去世 → 悲痛又需陪伴的 William 娶 Eliza 的妹妹 Mary Alleyne → 五年后她死于血中毒 → 再娶 Constance，小二十一岁 → 五个月后女儿 Kate 死于伤寒，二十一岁 → 悲剧再临 → William 察觉 Constance 与儿子 Douglas 有染 → Douglas 愧疚难当自杀 → 几年后生意败落的 William 开枪自尽 → 死后城堡与产业被拍卖 → 先后成私人住宅、精神病院、世界级酒店 → Larnach 一家众灵在许多房间显形 → 死后得到了生前没得到的团聚

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Constructed of the finest materials, this impressive castle was erected by businessman, banker, and politician, William James Mudie Larnach as a gift for his lovely wife, Eliza Jane. This expression of his undying love for her had all the makings of a fairy tale, but it ended more like a Greek tragedy. Eliza died alone in her bedroom of apoplexy shortly after they moved in. Grief stricken, but in need of companionship, William married Eliza's sister, Mary Alleyne. She too died tragically five years later from blood poisoning. William married again, this time to Constance, a woman twenty-one years his junior. Five months after his third marriage, his beloved daughter Kate died of typhoid at the tender age of twenty-one."

- **中文理解**：这座用最好的材料建成的城堡，是商人、银行家兼政治人物 William James Mudie Larnach 为他可爱的妻子 Eliza Jane 建的礼物。这份不渝之情的表达具备童话的全部要素，可它的收场更像一桩希腊悲剧。两人搬进去不久，Eliza 在自己的卧房里因中风独自死去。悲痛欲绝却又需要有人相伴的 William，娶了 Eliza 的妹妹 Mary Alleyne。五年后她也死于血中毒。William 又娶了一次，这位是 Constance，比他小二十一岁。第三次婚姻五个月后，他心爱的女儿 Kate 死于伤寒，年纪轻轻才二十一岁。
- **关键词**：Constructed of the finest materials, this impressive castle was erected by / businessman, banker, and politician, William James Mudie Larnach / as a gift for his lovely wife, Eliza Jane / This expression of his undying love for her / had all the makings of a fairy tale / but it ended more like a Greek tragedy / Eliza died alone in her bedroom of apoplexy / shortly after they moved in / Grief stricken, but in need of companionship / William married Eliza's sister, Mary Alleyne / She too died tragically five years later from blood poisoning / William married again, this time to Constance / a woman twenty-one years his junior / Five months after his third marriage / his beloved daughter Kate died of typhoid / at the tender age of twenty-one
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 had all the makings of a fairy tale, but it ended more like a Greek tragedy 一句把两种剧目并排，**而 at the tender age of twenty-one 把 Kate 的死与 Constance 的年龄压成同一个数字**。Grief stricken 与 in need of companionship 两个短语并列，把续娶的理由写得不那么体面。
- **读者视角提示**：Constance 嫁进来那年自己多大，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "Tragedy struck again when William discovered that Constance was having an affair with his favorite son, Douglas. Guilt-ridden by what he had done, Douglas could not live with his betrayal and committed suicide. A few years later, with his business failing, William followed in his son's footsteps and shot and killed himself."

- **中文理解**：悲剧再次降临——William 察觉到 Constance 正与他最宠爱的儿子 Douglas 有染。Douglas 因自己做的事愧疚难当，无法承受这份背叛，committed suicide 之后自尽。几年后，生意败落的 William 走了儿子同样的路，开枪自尽。
- **关键词**：Tragedy struck again when William discovered / that Constance was having an affair with his favorite son, Douglas / Guilt-ridden by what he had done / Douglas could not live with his betrayal / and committed suicide / A few years later, with his business failing / William followed in his son's footsteps / and shot and killed himself
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 followed in his son's footsteps 一句把父亲的死写成跟随儿子的路线，**而 Guilt-ridden 里的 with what he had done 用一个 what 把愧疚的内容留给读者补**。Douglas 说不出口，William 也不说，两人各用一种方式离开。
- **读者视角提示**：Douglas 与 Constance 之后如何，**本篇没交代**。

> **原句 3:** "After his death, the castle and his estate were sold at auction. It became a private residence, mental asylum, and finally a world-class hotel. Sightings of the spirits of the Larnach family can be seen in many rooms of the castle, achieving in death what they could not obtain in life — togetherness."

- **中文理解**：他死后，城堡与他的产业被拍卖出去。它先成了一处私人住宅，又成了一家精神病院，最后成了一家世界级的酒店。Larnach 一家众灵的显形在城堡许多房间都能见到，他们在死后达成了生前没能得到的——团聚。
- **关键词**：After his death, the castle and his estate were sold at auction / It became a private residence, mental asylum, and finally a world-class hotel / Sightings of the spirits of the Larnach family / can be seen in many rooms of the castle / achieving in death what they could not obtain in life — togetherness
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 a private residence, mental asylum, and finally a world-class hotel 三个名词不接动词，把这座楼的身份像换招牌一样连换三次，**而 achieving in death what they could not obtain in life 一句把最后那个 togetherness 留给亡者**。
- **读者视角提示**：这座酒店如今的经营状况，**本篇没提**。

## 一句话总结

为妻子建的 Larnach Castle 收场像希腊悲剧：妻子、妹妹、女儿、儿子、丈夫五个人的死连成一条，而一家的鬼在城堡各房间聚起了生前没聚成的团。
