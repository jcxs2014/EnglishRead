# OCTOBER 24, 1980 TOYS “R” US

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Sunnyvale 那家闹鬼的 Toys "R" Us 是个典型例子，说明即便最新的建筑也能闹鬼。这一桩超自然现象的源头，要回到 Sunnyvale 周边大部分还是农田的年代。其中有个农夫叫 Johnny Johnson。1884 年，他是个被爱情拒开的年轻人。一天他一边劈柴，一边滑了一跤，把斧子砍进了自己那条腿。他不肯停下活计，用一块布把腿扎紧，继续干活。他后来就在那儿被找到，因伤失血而死。可 Johnny 的死跟一家玩具店有什么关系？1970 年，这家店在同样的土地上建成不久，员工们就开始遭遇一连串超自然经历。水龙头开始自己打开。原本码放整齐的玩具被发现散落在过道里。据称，密封的机械玩具娃娃盒自己动了起来，开始说起话来。玩具从货架上飞下来，还听见了没有身体的声音。最让人发毛的那几桩，是女员工们报告的：她们感到有看不见的手把头发拂到脑后，同时有人在耳边低语。或许 Johnny 还在思念他失去的爱人 Beth。1980 年 10 月，著名的通灵者 Sylvia Brown 被请来调查这些怪事，并获准做一场降神会。在降神会中她试着与 Johnny Johnson 沟通，说服他承认自己已死，并帮助他前往更好的地方。降神会期间拍下的一张照片后来显示，墙边靠着一个黑影。而那位执着的 Johnny 并没有离开的打算。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 24, 1980（标题所载日期，即通灵者到访之年）
- **事件年代**：1884 年 Johnny Johnson 劈柴时身亡；1970 年玩具店建成；1980 年 10 月 Sylvia Brown 降神会
- **地点**：Sunnyvale, California（Toys "R" Us 的过道、货架与降神会现场）
- **异象类型**：Johnny Johnson 的鬼（开水龙头、弄散玩具、玩具自己说话、拂发与耳语）
- **叙事手法**：Sunnyvale 闹鬼的玩具店说明最新建筑也能闹鬼 → 源头在周边还是农田的年代 → 农夫 Johnny Johnson → 1884 年被爱情拒开的年轻人 → 劈柴时滑倒 → 斧子砍进腿 → 不肯停手 → 用布扎腿继续干活 → 后来被发现已因伤失血而死 → 他的死与玩具店何干 → 1970 年同样的土地上建起店 → 员工开始遭遇一连串超自然经历 → 水龙头自己开 → 码好的玩具散在过道 → 密封的机械娃娃自己动起来说话 → 玩具从货架飞下 → 听见没有身体的声音 → 最发毛的是女员工的报告 → 看不见的手拂发 → 有人在耳边低语 → 或许 Johnny 仍在思念失去的爱人 Beth → 1980 年 10 月通灵者 Sylvia Brown 到场 → 获准做降神会 → 试图说服他承认已死并助他前往别处 → 照片显示墙边有个黑影 → Johnny 没有离开的打算

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The haunted Toys “R” Us in Sunnyvale is a prime example that shows that even the newest of buildings can be haunted. In this case the paranormal phenomenon originates from a period in history when the majority of the area around Sunnyvale was farmland. One farmer in particular was Johnny Johnson. In 1884, he was a young man rejected by his love. One day, while cutting wood, he slipped with the axe and drove it into his leg. Refusing to quit his chores, he cinched up his leg with a piece of cloth and continued working. And that's where he was eventually found, having bled to death of his wound."

- **中文理解**：Sunnyvale 那家闹鬼的 Toys "R" Us 是个典型例子，说明即便最新的建筑也能闹鬼。这一桩超自然现象的源头，要回到 Sunnyvale 周边大部分还是农田的年代。其中有个农夫叫 Johnny Johnson。1884 年，他是个被爱情拒开的年轻人。一天他一边劈柴，一边滑了一跤，把斧子砍进了自己那条腿。他不肯停下活计，用一块布把腿扎紧，继续干活。他后来就在那儿被找到，因伤失血而死。
- **关键词**：The haunted Toys “R” Us in Sunnyvale / is a prime example that shows that / even the newest of buildings can be haunted / the paranormal phenomenon originates from a period in history / when the majority of the area around Sunnyvale was farmland / One farmer in particular was Johnny Johnson / In 1884, he was a young man rejected by his love / while cutting wood, he slipped with the axe / and drove it into his leg / Refusing to quit his chores / he cinched up his leg with a piece of cloth / And that's where he was eventually found / having bled to death of his wound
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 prime example that shows that 两层把这座店说成例子，而例子举完立刻退回 1884 年，**而 Refusing to quit his chores 与 continued working 两句把致命伤写成一次坚持**。最后 that's where he was eventually found 用一个 eventually 把发现拖到最后。
- **读者视角提示**：Johnny Johnson 拒开的那个「his love」，**本篇没给名字**。

> **原句 2:** "But what does Johnny's death have to do with a toy store? Soon after being built on the same land in 1970, employees of the Toys “R” Us began to encounter an onslaught of paranormal experiences. Faucets began turning on by themselves. Previously stacked toys were found scattered in the aisles. Sealed boxes of mechanical dolls reportedly became activated and began to speak of their own accord. Toys flew off the shelves, and disembodied voices were heard. Among the creepiest occurrences to afflict the help have been those reported by the female employees, who have felt invisible hands brush back their hair as someone unseen whispers in their ear. Perhaps Johnny is still pining for his lost love, Beth."

- **中文理解**：可 Johnny 的死跟一家玩具店有什么关系？1970 年，这家店在同样的土地上建成不久，员工们就开始遭遇一连串超自然经历。水龙头开始自己打开。原本码放整齐的玩具被发现散落在过道里。据称，密封的机械玩具娃娃盒自己动了起来，开始说起话来。玩具从货架上飞下来，还听见了没有身体的声音。最让人发毛的那几桩，是女员工们报告的：她们感到有看不见的手把头发拂到脑后，同时有人在耳边低语。或许 Johnny 还在思念他失去的爱人 Beth。
- **关键词**：But what does Johnny's death have to do with a toy store? / Soon after being built on the same land in 1970 / employees of the Toys “R” Us began to encounter / an onslaught of paranormal experiences / Faucets began turning on by themselves / Previously stacked toys were found scattered in the aisles / Sealed boxes of mechanical dolls reportedly became activated / and began to speak of their own accord / Toys flew off the shelves / and disembodied voices were heard / Among the creepiest occurrences to afflict the help / the female employees, who have felt invisible hands brush back their hair / as someone unseen whispers in their ear / Perhaps Johnny is still pining for his lost love, Beth
- **为什么这样写**：作者把水龙头、玩具、娃娃盒、货架四样按顺序排开，一样比一样不像玩具店该有的动静，**而 brush back their hair 与 whispers in their ear 两处都把动作者藏在看不见的那一侧**。末句用一个 Perhaps 把这整串怪事与 1884 年那段旧情挂上。
- **读者视角提示**：Beth 是谁、1884 年发生了什么，**本篇都没有细写**。

> **原句 3:** "In October of 1980, renowned psychic Sylvia Brown was called in to investigate the odd goings-on and was permitted to perform a séance. During the séance she attempted to communicate with Johnny Johnson, convince him of his demise, and help him to move on to a better place. A photo taken during the séance later revealed a dark figure leaning against the wall. The curious Johnny had no intention of leaving."

- **中文理解**：1980 年 10 月，著名的通灵者 Sylvia Brown 被请来调查这些怪事，并获准做一场降神会。在降神会中她试着与 Johnny Johnson 沟通，说服他承认自己已死，并帮助他前往更好的地方。降神会期间拍下的一张照片后来显示，墙边靠着一个黑影。而那位执着的 Johnny 并没有离开的打算。
- **关键词**：In October of 1980 / renowned psychic Sylvia Brown was called in / to investigate the odd goings-on / and was permitted to perform a séance / During the séance she attempted to communicate with Johnny Johnson / convince him of his demise / and help him to move on to a better place / A photo taken during the séance later revealed / a dark figure leaning against the wall / The curious Johnny had no intention of leaving
- **为什么这样写**：作者把通灵者要做的事写成三项——沟通、说服、前往，**而 A photo taken during the séance later revealed 用一个 later 把照片与当时隔开，不当作同步的证据**。末句 no intention of leaving 与前面那三项正好对着来。
- **读者视角提示**：照片里那个黑影是不是 Johnny，**本篇没有说**。

## 一句话总结

1884 年劈柴失血的农夫 Johnny Johnson 缠上了 1970 年建在他那片地上的玩具店：娃娃自己说话、货架上的东西飞下来，而通灵者劝他离开，他不走。
