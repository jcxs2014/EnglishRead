# NOVEMBER 10, 1836 THE WALKER HOUSE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Walker House 建于 1836 年，是威斯康星州历史较久的客栈之一，曾作为聚会所兴盛，来自那一带的男人们在这里聚谈。1836 年 11 月，一个叫 William Caffee 的人因与人争执时开枪杀人而被公开绞刑处死。可这并不是人们最后一次看见 William Caffee。从 20 世纪 50 年代中期起，客栈一直空着，直到 Ted Landon 在 1964 年买下它。空置期间它是破坏者的目标，急需修缮。可 Langdon 动手翻修不久，就开始经历无法解释的事：听见了没有身体的脚步声，还有沉重的呼吸声。不知道那些灵体与 Ted 决定卖房有没有关系，反正客栈又一次易主。这一次只过了四年，客栈转到了 Dr. David Ruf 名下。新的管理者是 Walker Calvert。正是 Calvert 头一个报告了凭空响起的那种空灵人声，以及一个无头的鬼影——穿着破烂的灰西装。没过多久，在看见鬼的躯干之后，Calvert 又瞥见一个全身的显形在客栈二层游荡。虽然 Walker House 早已不再对公众开放，这栋建筑的拥有者却相当确定，William Caffee 的魂还在附近游荡。
- **篇目日期**：NOVEMBER 10, 1836（标题所载年份，即 Caffee 被处决之年）
- **事件年代**：1836 年建店，同年 11 月 William Caffee 被公开绞刑；20 世纪 50 年代中期起空置；1964 年 Ted Landon 购入；四年后转给 Dr. David Ruf
- **地点**：Mineral Point, Wisconsin（Walker House 的房间与二层）
- **异象类型**：William Caffee 的鬼（无头灰西装的鬼影、空灵人声，随后现出全身）
- **叙事手法**：1836 年建店，曾是聚会所 → 1836 年 11 月 William Caffee 因争执开枪被公开绞刑 → 这不是人们最后一次看见他 → 20 世纪 50 年代中期起空置 → 1964 年 Ted Landon 购入 → 空置期间成破坏者目标 → 翻修不久就出事 → 听見无身脚步声与沉重呼吸 → 灵体是否与卖房有关不明 → 客栈再一次易主 → 四年后转到 Dr. David Ruf 名下 → 管理者是 Walker Calvert → Calvert 报告空灵人声 → 看见穿破烂灰西装的无头鬼影 → 不久后瞥见全身显形在二层游荡 → 店已不对公众开放 → 拥有者认定 Caffee 的魂还在附近

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "One of the oldest inns in all of Wisconsin, the Walker House, built in 1836, flourished as a meetinghouse where men from all over the region could gather. In November 1836, a man named William Caffee was executed by public hanging for shooting another man in an argument. But this would not be the last anyone saw of William Caffee."

- **中文理解**：Walker House 建于 1836 年，是威斯康星州历史较久的客栈之一，曾作为聚会所兴盛，来自那一带的男人们在这里聚谈。1836 年 11 月，一个叫 William Caffee 的人因与人争执时开枪杀人而被公开绞刑处死。可这并不是人们最后一次看见 William Caffee。
- **关键词**：One of the oldest inns in all of Wisconsin / built in 1836 / flourished as a meetinghouse / men from all over the region could gather / In November 1836, a man named William Caffee / was executed by public hanging / for shooting another man in an argument / But this would not be the last anyone saw of William Caffee
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 One of the oldest inns 这个带范围限的说法起句，**而 But this would not be the last anyone saw of William Caffee 一句就把「已被处决」翻成「还会再出现」**。公开绞刑与聚会所并置在同一段，一句话是死，一句话是活。
- **读者视角提示**：William Caffee 是被处决的，**本篇没说与他争执的那个人的下落**。

> **原句 2:** "From the mid-1950s the inn stood vacant, until Ted Landon purchased it in 1964. While abandoned, it was the target of vandals and was in desperate need of repair. But soon after Langdon began renovating the building, he began to experience the unexplainable. Disembodied footsteps along with the sound of heavy breathing were heard. It's not known whether the spirits had anything to do with Ted's decision to sell, but once again, the inn would change hands."

- **中文理解**：从 20 世纪 50 年代中期起，客栈一直空着，直到 Ted Landon 在 1964 年买下它。空置期间它是破坏者的目标，急需修缮。可 Langdon 动手翻修不久，就开始经历无法解释的事：听见了没有身体的脚步声，还有沉重的呼吸声。不知道那些灵体与 Ted 决定卖房有没有关系，反正客栈又一次易主。
- **关键词**：From the mid-1950s the inn stood vacant / until Ted Landon purchased it in 1964 / While abandoned, it was the target of vandals / was in desperate need of repair / soon after Langdon began renovating the building / he began to experience the unexplainable / Disembodied footsteps / along with the sound of heavy breathing were heard / It's not known whether the spirits had anything to do with / Ted's decision to sell / but once again, the inn would change hands
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 Disembodied 这个词把脚步声的主语直接抽掉，**而 It's not known whether the spirits had anything to do with 把因果关系整个推开，只用 but once again, the inn would change hands 留下结果**。买下的人是 Ted，翻修的却写成 Langdon——两个名字本篇没有统一。
- **读者视角提示**：买房的 Ted Landon 与翻修的 Langdon 是不是同一个人，**本篇没有说明**。

> **原句 3:** "This time, only four years later, the inn came under new ownership by Dr. David Ruf. The new manager of the building was Walker Calvert. It was Calvert who first reported the ethereal voices arising from thin air and the shocking ghostly image of a headless specter wearing a tattered gray suit. Not long after seeing the torso of a ghost, Calvert spied the full-bodied apparition roaming about the second floor of the inn. Although the Walker House is no longer open to the general public, the owners of the structure are pretty sure that the spirit of William Caffee is still hanging around."

- **中文理解**：这一次只过了四年，客栈转到了 Dr. David Ruf 名下。新的管理者是 Walker Calvert。正是 Calvert 头一个报告了凭空响起的那种空灵人声，以及一个令人震惊的鬼影——一个无头的幽灵，穿着破烂的灰西装。没过多久，在见过一个鬼的躯干之后，Calvert 又瞥见一个全身的显形在客栈二层游荡。虽然 Walker House 早已不再对公众开放，这栋建筑的拥有者却相当确定，William Caffee 的魂还在附近游荡。
- **关键词**：only four years later / the inn came under new ownership by Dr. David Ruf / The new manager of the building was Walker Calvert / It was Calvert who first reported / the ethereal voices arising from thin air / the shocking ghostly image of a headless specter / wearing a tattered gray suit / Not long after seeing the torso of a ghost / Calvert spied the full-bodied apparition / roaming about the second floor of the inn / no longer open to the general public / the owners of the structure are pretty sure / the spirit of William Caffee is still hanging around
- **为什么这样写**：作者先给无头灰西装，再给躯干，最后给全身，**而 Calvert spied the full-bodied apparition 把补全这一步交给同一双眼睛，看的人也一路跟着补齐**。arising from thin air 与 still hanging around 两头呼应：声音凭空来，魂在店边绕。
- **读者视角提示**：Calvert 报告的两种显形是否同一个人，**本篇没有说**。

1836 年被公开绞刑的 William Caffee 缠上了这家老客栈：先是脚步声与呼吸，接着是无头灰西装的鬼影，最后在二层现出全身。
