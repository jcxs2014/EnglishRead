# NOVEMBER 7, 2008 FULL MOON

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：据 Ryan Dube 在 paranormal.lovetoknow.com 上的报道，Connecticut 一户人家请来一个超自然调查队，要核实那个纠缠他们的、格外凶恶的存在。该队的首席调查者还带来一位物理学家——来自 Massachusetts 的业余超自然调查者，其他人管他叫「Doc」。Doc 不信鬼。他觉得所有那些所谓的「paranormal events」都能轻易解释。到达现场后不久，屋主带 Doc 四处看了看，其余的调查队员则开始架设设备。有一阵子，屋主、Doc 与调查队另一名成员站在楼梯底下，听见楼板上方传来响亮的脚步声。Doc 问屋主家里是否还有别人。屋主回答说：「我妻子在上班，我们没有孩子。」急着想知道更多，Doc 向那个灵挑战，要它再露一手。屋主提醒 Doc 对鬼说话要小心。Doc 不轻易被说动，又朝楼上喊了一声，这回还骂那鬼是个蹩脚的恶作剧者。突然之间，被某种看不见的力量一带，Doc 的裤子被扒到了脚踝上。其他人全都爆发出哄堂大笑，Doc 提着裤子仓皇退向门口。Doc 如今信鬼了吗？这很难说。不过据报道，此后他一直拒绝再进那栋房子，也拒绝再谈自己的经历。
- **篇目日期**：NOVEMBER 7, 2008（标题所载日期，即报道之年）
- **事件年代**：事件当晚未标日期；2008 年由 Ryan Dube 在 paranormal.lovetoknow.com 报道
- **地点**：Connecticut（一户民宅的楼梯下与楼上，州名未细化到城市）
- **异象类型**：一个格外凶恶的存在（楼板上的脚步声，以及把 Doc 裤子扒落的无形之力）
- **叙事手法**：Ryan Dube 在 paranormal.lovetoknow.com 报道 → Connecticut 一户人家请来调查队 → 要核实那个格外凶恶的存在 → 首席调查者带来一位物理学家 → 来自 Massachusetts 的业余调查者 → 其他人管他叫 Doc → Doc 不信鬼 → 觉得那些所谓事件都能解释 → 到达后屋主带他看屋 → 队伍开始架设备 → 三人站在楼梯底下 → 听见楼板上方有响亮脚步声 → Doc 问家里是否还有别人 → 屋主说妻子上班且没有孩子 → Doc 向那灵挑战要它再露一手 → 屋主提醒他说话要小心 → Doc 不听又喊一声 → 还骂那鬼是蹩脚的恶作剧者 → 突然被无形力量把裤子扒到脚踝 → 其他人哄堂大笑 → Doc 提着裤子退向门口 → 如今信不信很难说 → 此后拒绝再进那栋房子也不再谈

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "As reported by Ryan Dube on paranormal.lovetoknow.com, a Connecticut family requested the service of a paranormal investigative team to verify the especially evil entity that was taunting them. The lead investigator of the group invited along a physicist and amateur paranormal investigator from Massachusetts whom the others referred to as “Doc.” Doc did not believe in ghosts. He felt that all so -called “paranormal events” could easily be explained."

- **中文理解**：据 Ryan Dube 在 paranormal.lovetoknow.com 上的报道，Connecticut 一户人家请来一个超自然调查队，要核实那个纠缠他们的、格外凶恶的存在。该队的首席调查者还带来一位物理学家——来自 Massachusetts 的业余超自然调查者，其他人管他叫「Doc」。Doc 不信鬼。他觉得所有那些所谓的「paranormal events」都能轻易解释。
- **关键词**：As reported by Ryan Dube on paranormal.lovetoknow.com / a Connecticut family requested the service of / a paranormal investigative team / to verify the especially evil entity that was taunting them / The lead investigator of the group invited along a physicist / and amateur paranormal investigator from Massachusetts / whom the others referred to as / Doc did not believe in ghosts / He felt that all so -called / paranormal events could easily be explained
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 a physicist and amateur paranormal investigator 三个身份压给一个人，**而 whom the others referred to as 把 Doc 这个称呼写成别人给的，不是自称**。he easily be explained 一句把他的立场先摆完，后面整篇就是让事实去反驳它。
- **读者视角提示**：Doc 的本名与年纪，**本篇都没给**。

> **原句 2:** "Soon after arriving at the site, Doc was given a tour by the homeowner while the remaining team of paranormal investigators began setting up their equipment. At one point the owner, Doc, and another member of the investigative team were standing at the bottom of the staircase when they began to hear loud footsteps on the floor above. Doc asked the owner whether there was someone else in the home. The homeowner replied, “My wife is at work and we don't have any children.”"

- **中文理解**：到达现场后不久，屋主带 Doc 四处看了看，其余的调查队员则开始架设设备。有一阵子，屋主、Doc 与调查队另一名成员站在楼梯底下，听见楼板上面传来响亮的脚步声。Doc 问屋主家里是否还有别人。屋主回答说：「我妻子在上班，我们没有孩子。」
- **关键词**：Soon after arriving at the site, Doc was given a tour by the homeowner / while the remaining team of paranormal investigators / began setting up their equipment / At one point the owner, Doc, and another member / of the investigative team were standing at the bottom of the staircase / when they began to hear loud footsteps on the floor above / Doc asked the owner whether there was someone else in the home / The homeowner replied / My wife is at work and we don't have any children
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 the bottom of the staircase 与 the floor above 两处方位把声音和听的人分在上下，**而屋主那句回话把家里有几个人一次交代完：妻子在上班、我们没有孩子——两处否定之后，只剩楼上那一串脚步声无人可认**。
- **读者视角提示**：楼上发出脚步声的，**本篇没说是谁也未必是人**。

> **原句 3:** "Eager to find out more, Doc challenged the spirit to show him what else he could do. The owner cautioned Doc to be careful how he spoke to the ghost. Not easily swayed, Doc yelled up the stairs once again, this time provoking the spirit even more by calling the ghost a lousy prankster. Suddenly, by some invisible force, Doc's pants were pulled down around his ankles. While everyone else burst out in uproarious laughter, Doc struggled with his pants and made a hasty retreat for the door. Is Doc now a believer? That's hard to say. However, it is reported that he has since refused to re-enter the home or talk about his experiences ever again."

- **中文理解**：急着想知道更多，Doc 向那个灵挑战，要它再露一手。屋主提醒 Doc 对鬼说话要小心。Doc 不轻易被说动，又朝楼上喊了一声，这回还骂那鬼是个蹩脚的恶作剧者。突然之间，被某种看不见的力量一带，Doc 的裤子被扒到了脚踝上。其他人全都爆发出哄堂大笑，Doc 提着裤子仓皇退向门口。Doc 如今信鬼了吗？这很难说。不过据报道，此后他一直拒绝再进那栋房子，也拒绝再谈自己的经历。
- **关键词**：Eager to find out more, Doc challenged the spirit / to show him what else he could do / The owner cautioned Doc to be careful / how he spoke to the ghost / Not easily swayed, Doc yelled up the stairs once again / provoking the spirit even more / by calling the ghost a lousy prankster / Suddenly, by some invisible force / Doc's pants were pulled down around his ankles / everyone else burst out in uproarious laughter / Doc struggled with his pants / and made a hasty retreat for the door / Is Doc now a believer? That's hard to say / it is reported that he has since refused to re-enter the home / or talk about his experiences ever again
- **为什么这样写**：作者把嘲笑与被拉裤子绑在同一个挑衅动作上，**而 by some invisible force 把动手的力气写成一个没有形的东西，人只留下被动的裤子和自己的脚踝**。爆笑与仓皇退场写在同一句，落差就是全部笑点。
- **读者视角提示**：Doc 此后拒绝再谈，**本篇没说这件事有没有改变他原来的判断**。
