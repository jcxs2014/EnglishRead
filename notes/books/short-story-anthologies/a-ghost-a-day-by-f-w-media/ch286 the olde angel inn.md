# OCTOBER 13, 1812 THE OLDE ANGEL INN

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1812 年战争期间，这家客栈叫 Harmonious Coach House。当时 Canada 牢牢握在英国人手里；然而 1813 年 5 月，美军攻下了 Fort George 与 Newark——那时 Niagara-on-the-Lake 就叫这个名字。英军撤退，只留下一位名叫 Captain Swayze 的军官。他到那家马车客栈去与他爱上的店主的女儿告别。美軍逼近时，他躲进了地窖。搜查这栋房子的士兵在地下室找到了 Captain Swayze，用刺刀把他刺死，随后放火烧了那家客栈。客栈 1815 年重建，改名为 Sign of the Angel Inn。到 1820 年，当地报纸上已有关于鬼影的记载。报纸形容种种鬼怪动静，包括：空房间里传出的脚步声、酒杯的碰撞声、笑声、响彻整家客栈的没有身体的声音，以及鬼魂那令人不安的出现。今天这家客栈叫 Olde Angel Inn，近两百年过去，闹鬼仍在继续。除了听见那些无法解释的动静，许多客人还看见过黑影。女洗手间的镜子里频次常被报告映出鬼影般的红制服。可别怕。客栈掌柜告诉我们，只要英国国旗还在门上飘着，他们就不会伤你。可话说回来，Captain Swayze 似乎仍记着仇。酒吧上那只美国啤酒龙头常常出故障，而英国与加拿大那两只却好好工作。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 13, 1812（标题所载年份，即战争期间）
- **事件年代**：1812 年战争期间客栈名 Harmonious Coach House；1813 年 5 月英军撤退、Swayze 被刺死；1815 年重建改名 Sign of the Angel Inn；1820 年当地报纸已有鬼影记载
- **地点**：Niagara-on-the-Lake, Ontario（客栈的地窖、走廊、女洗手间镜前与吧台）
- **异象类型**：Captain Swayze 的鬼（镜中的红制服黑影，以及故障的美国啤酒龙头）
- **叙事手法**：1812 年战争期间客栈叫 Harmonious Coach House → Canada 握在英国人手里 → 1813 年 5 月美军攻下 Fort George 与 Newark → 当时 Niagara-on-the-Lake 就叫这名字 → 英军撤退只留 Captain Swayze → 他去与店主的女儿告别 → 美军逼近他躲进地窖 → 士兵搜屋在地下室找到他 → 刺刀刺死并放火烧屋 → 1815 年重建改名 Sign of the Angel Inn → 1820 年当地报纸已记鬼影 → 空房脚步声、酒杯碰撞声、笑声、鬼声、鬼影 → 今天叫 Olde Angel Inn → 近两百年闹鬼仍在继续 → 客人还见过黑影 → 女洗手间镜里频现红制服 → 掌柜说只要英国旗还飘着就不伤人 → 但 Captain Swayze 似乎记仇 → 美国啤酒龙头常出故障 → 英国与加拿大龙头却正常

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "During the War of 1812, the inn was known as the Harmonious Coach House. Canada was in the firm hands of the British; however, in May 1813 American forces captured Fort George and Newark, as Niagara-on-the-Lake was called then. The British retreated, except for one officer by the name of Captain Swayze. He went to the coach house to bid farewell to the innkeeper's daughter, with whom he had fallen in love. As the Americans approached, he hid in the cellar. Troops searching the house found Captain Swayze in the basement and bayoneted him to death before torching the coach house."

- **中文理解**：1812 年战争期间，这家客栈叫 Harmonious Coach House。当时 Canada 牢牢握在英国人手里；然而 1813 年 5 月，美军攻下了 Fort George 与 Newark——那时 Niagara-on-the-Lake 就叫这个名字。英军撤退，只留下一位名叫 Captain Swayze 的军官。他到那家马车客栈去与他爱上的店主的女儿告别。美军逼近时，他躲进了地窖。搜查这栋房子的士兵在地下室找到了 Captain Swayze，用刺刀把他刺死，随后放火烧了那家客栈。
- **关键词**：During the War of 1812, the inn was known as the Harmonious Coach House / Canada was in the firm hands of the British / however, in May 1813 American forces captured Fort George and Newark / as Niagara-on-the-Lake was called then / The British retreated / except for one officer by the name of Captain Swayze / He went to the coach house to bid farewell to the innkeeper's daughter / with whom he had fallen in love / As the Americans approached, he hid in the cellar / Troops searching the house found Captain Swayze in the basement / and bayoneted him to death before torching the coach house
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 The British retreated, except for one officer 两句把撤退写成只剩一个人的局面，**而 bayoneted him to death before torching the coach house 用一个 before 把放火烧屋排在刺死之后**。as Niagara-on-the-Lake was called then 一句把旧地名挂在括号位置上。
- **读者视角提示**：那位店主的女儿后来如何，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "The inn was rebuilt in 1815 and renamed the Sign of the Angel Inn. By 1820, accounts of ghostly sightings reached the local newspaper. The paper described a variety of ghostly activities including: footsteps heard in empty rooms, clinking of glasses, laughter, disembodied voices echoing throughout the inn, and the unnerving appearance of specters."

- **中文理解**：客栈 1815 年重建，改名为 Sign of the Angel Inn。到 1820 年，当地报纸上已有关于鬼影的记载。报纸形容种种鬼怪动静，包括：空房间里传出的脚步声、酒杯的碰撞声、笑声、响彻整家客栈的没有身体的声音，以及鬼魂那令人不安的出现。
- **关键词**：The inn was rebuilt in 1815 / and renamed the Sign of the Angel Inn / By 1820, accounts of ghostly sightings / reached the local newspaper / The paper described a variety of ghostly activities including / footsteps heard in empty rooms / clinking of glasses / laughter / disembodied voices echoing throughout the inn / the unnerving appearance of specters
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 a variety of ghostly activities including 一个 including 把后面五样都算作其中一种，**而 disembodied voices echoing throughout the inn 一句是这五样里带空间范围的一处**。clinking of glasses 与 laughter 两样只列名词，不给场合。
- **读者视角提示**：那份报纸的名字，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "Today the inn is known as the Olde Angel Inn, and after nearly 200 years, the haunting continues. In addition to hearing the unexplainable noises, many patrons have seen dark shadows. The sighting of spectral red coats in the mirror of the women's bathroom has been frequently reported. But fear not. The innkeepers tell us that as long as the British flag flies over the door, they'll do you no harm. Then again, it seems Captain Swayze still holds a grudge. The American beer tap at the bar often malfunctions, while the British and Canadian taps work fine."

- **中文理解**：今天这家客栈叫 Olde Angel Inn，近两百年过去，闹鬼仍在继续。除了听见那些无法解释的动静，许多客人还看见过黑影。女洗手间的镜子里频次常被报告映出鬼影般的红制服。可别怕。客栈掌柜告诉我们，只要英国国旗还在门上飘着，他们就不会伤你。可话说回来，Captain Swayze 似乎仍记着仇。酒吧上那只美国啤酒龙头常常出故障，而英国与加拿大那两只却好好工作。
- **关键词**：Today the inn is known as the Olde Angel Inn / after nearly 200 years, the haunting continues / In addition to hearing the unexplainable noises / many patrons have seen dark shadows / The sighting of spectral red coats in the mirror of the women's bathroom / has been frequently reported / But fear not. The innkeepers tell us / as long as the British flag flies over the door, they'll do you no harm / Then again, it seems Captain Swayze still holds a grudge / The American beer tap at the bar often malfunctions / while the British and Canadian taps work fine
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 But fear not 一句替读者卸下防备，紧接着给出的保证却附了条件——只要英国旗还飘着，**而 Then again 一转，那只美国啤酒龙头的故障把这份保证顶了回去**。三只龙头并列，只有中间那只出问题。
- **读者视角提示**：那只龙头的故障是否已被修好，**本篇没提**。

## 一句话总结

1813 年被刺刀挑死在这家客栈地窖里的 Captain Swayze，两百年后仍在女洗手间的镜子里留下红制服，吧台上唯独美国那只啤酒龙头老出故障。
