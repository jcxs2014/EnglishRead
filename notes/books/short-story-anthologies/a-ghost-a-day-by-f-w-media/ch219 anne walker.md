# AUGUST 7, 1631 ANNE WALKER

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Anne Walker 的鬼魂为求公道，从坟里伸出手来讨到了公道。1631 年，鳏居的 John Walker 把自己的亲属 Anne 招来替他料理家务。没过多久，John 勾引了她；等她怀孕，John 便谋划着要除掉她。他劝 Anne 跟兰开夏郡的一个矿工 Mark Sharp 一起走，并对她说 Sharp 会照应她，她将隐居在外，一直被照料到孩子出生。Anne 信任这位亲戚，收好东西跟 Mark 走了。不久之后，John 的邻居 John Grahame 正在磨坊里借着烛光干活，遭遇了他一生中最骇人的一刻：空气一变，血凉了，后颈的汗毛根根立起；眼前出现一个女人的鬼影，血正从她头上的裂口里往下淌。她讲出自己惨死的经过，报出自己的名字、邻居 John Walker 的名字，以及她堂兄与 Mark Sharp 合谋的计策——Sharp 把她诱到 Old Mill Wood 附近一处僻静的山沟，用一把矿镐结束了她与孩子的性命。她还指出 Grahame 会在哪里找到 Sharp 那双血浸的袜子、凶器，以及能找到她尸身的煤坑。消失之前，Anne 要 Grahame 答应去通知官府；Grahame 答应了，可一回家怕别人当他是疯子，便把她的鬼魂撂在了脑后。Anne 拒绝被无视，再次现身，发誓若不报官就缠他到永远。第二天早上 Grahame 守诺报了官，Anne 的遗骸、凶器、血袜与鞋都在她说的地方找到了。1631 年 8 月，两人被捕受审，主审的 Davenport 法官当天判决，两人都被处决。
- **篇目日期**：AUGUST 7, 1631（标题所载日期；正文另给 1631 年事发、8 月两人被捕）
- **事件年代**：1631 年（Anne 被诱杀、Grahame 见鬼并报官）→ 1631 年 8 月 John Walker 与 Mark Sharp 被捕受审、当天判决；更早年份未给
- **地点**：Durham, England（Old Mill Wood 附近的山沟与煤坑、John Grahame 的磨坊，以及法庭）
- **异象类型**：头顶淌血的 Anne Walker 的鬼魂，执意替自己的冤案讨回公道
- **叙事手法**：Anne Walker 的鬼魂为求公道从坟里伸手 → 1631 年鳏居的 John Walker 招亲属 Anne 料理家务 → 不久他勾引了她 → 她怀孕后他谋划除掉她 → 劝她跟兰开夏矿工 Mark Sharp 走 → 说会照应她直到孩子出生 → 她信任亲戚、收好东西跟 Mark 走 → 不久 Grahame 在磨坊烛光下干活 → 空气一变、血冷、汗毛立起 → 眼前出现女人鬼影、血从头上裂口淌下 → 她讲出惨死经过 → 报出自己、John Walker 与 Mark Sharp 的名字 → 说 Mark 把她诱到 Old Mill Wood 附近的山沟 → 用矿镐杀了她与孩子 → 她指出凶器、血袜与尸身所在 → 消失前要 Grahame 答应报官 → Grahame 口头答应 → 回家怕被当成疯子便把她忘掉 → Anne 拒绝被无视再次现身 → 发誓不报官就缠他到永远 → 次日 Grahame 守诺报官 → 遗骸、凶器、血袜与鞋都在她说的地方 → 1631 年 8 月两人被捕受审 → Davenport 法官当天判决 → 两人都被处决

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The ghost of Anne Walker, seeking justice, reached out from the grave to get it. In 1631, a widower by the name of John Walker enlisted his relative Anne to keep house for him. Not long after, John seduced her. When she became pregnant, he devised a plot to be rid of her. John convinced Anne to go away with a pitman from Lancashire, Mark Sharp, who, John told her, would see to her care. She would stay in seclusion and be cared for until the baby was born. Trusting her relative, she packed her belongings and left with Mark."

- **中文理解**：Anne Walker 的鬼魂为求公道，从坟里伸出手来讨到了公道。1631 年，一个名叫 John Walker 的鳏居者把自己的亲属 Anne 招来替他料理家务。没过多久，John 勾引了她。等她怀了孕，John 便谋划着要除掉她。他劝 Anne 跟兰开夏郡的一个矿工 Mark Sharp 一起走，并对她说，Sharp 会照应她；她将隐居在外，一直被照料到孩子出生。Anne 信任这位亲戚，收好自己的东西，跟着 Mark 走了。
- **关键词**：The ghost of Anne Walker, seeking justice, reached out from the grave to get it / a widower by the name of John Walker enlisted his relative Anne to keep house for him / Not long after, John seduced her / When she became pregnant, he devised a plot to be rid of her / John convinced Anne to go away with a pitman from Lancashire, Mark Sharp / She would stay in seclusion and be cared for until the baby was born / Trusting her relative, she packed her belongings and left with Mark
- **为什么这样写**：首句用 seeking justice 与 get it 把鬼的行为归到“讨公道”上，而 reached out from the grave 一个动作就把整篇定性成申冤。最阴的一处是 who, John told her, would see to her care：**照应她的人正是凶手，引她上路的也是他**。末句只用一个形容词 Trusting her relative，**全篇最短的判决，落在一个信任上**。
- **读者视角提示**：Mark Sharp 的下落、Anne 当时怀了几个月，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "Soon after, John's neighbor, John Grahame, while working by candlelight in his mill, received the fright of his life. The air shifted, his blood ran cold, and the hairs on his neck prickled to attention. To his horror, the ghostly apparition of a woman stood before him, blood pouring from gashes in her head. She told the terrible tale of her demise, giving her name, the name of Grahame's neighbor, John Walker, and the plot her cousin had devised with Mark Sharp. Sharp had lured her to a secluded ravine near the Old Mill Wood and, using a collier's pick, had ended her life as well as the life of her baby. She also named the location where Grahame would find Sharp's blood-soaked socks and the murder weapon and the coal pit where her body could be found."

- **中文理解**：不久之后，John 的邻居 John Grahame 正在自己的磨坊里借着烛光干活，遭遇了他一生中最骇人的一刻：空气变了，血凉了，后颈的汗毛根根立起。眼前出现一个女人的鬼影，血正从她头上的裂口里往下淌。她讲出自己惨死的经过，报出自己的名字、邻居 John Walker 的名字，以及她堂兄与 Mark Sharp 合谋的计策。Sharp 把她诱到 Old Mill Wood 附近一处僻静的山沟，用一把矿镐结束了她与孩子的性命。她还指出 Grahame 会在哪里找到 Sharp 那双血浸的袜子、凶器，以及能找到她尸身的那个煤坑。
- **关键词**：while working by candlelight in his mill, received the fright of his life / The air shifted, his blood ran cold, and the hairs on his neck prickled to attention / the ghostly apparition of a woman stood before him, blood pouring from gashes in her head / She told the terrible tale of her demise / Sharp had lured her to a secluded ravine near the Old Mill Wood / using a collier's pick, had ended her life as well as the life of her baby / Sharp's blood-soaked socks and the murder weapon and the coal pit where her body could be found
- **为什么这样写**：见到鬼之前的三个反应是全段的呼吸：air shifted、blood ran cold、hairs prickled，**一句比一句短，一句比一句靠近皮肤**。而 blood pouring from gashes in her head 让这张脸一出场就带着正在流的血——**她不是来讲述的，她是带着伤口来讲述的**。最见心思的是末尾那串地名与物件：**鬼给的不是故事清单，是一条搜索路线**。
- **读者视角提示**：Grahame 与 Walker 家后来还来不来往、那口煤坑是否被挖开，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "Before disappearing, Anne made Grahame promise to alert the authorities. He agreed. But when he went home, fearing people would believe him mad, he put her spirit out of his mind. Refusing to be ignored, Anne appeared once again. This time she vowed to haunt him for eternity unless he informed the law. The next morning, keeping his promise, Grahame alerted the authorities. Anne's remains, the murder weapon, and the bloodied socks and shoes were all found just where she'd said."

- **中文理解**：消失之前，Anne 要 Grahame 答应去通知官府。他答应了。可一回到家，怕别人当他是疯子，他便把她的鬼魂撂在了脑后。Anne 拒绝被无视，再次现身。这一次她发誓：若不报官，就缠他到永远。第二天早上，Grahame 守住了承诺，报了官。Anne 的遗骸、凶器、那双血浸的袜子和鞋，全都就在她所说的地方找到了。
- **关键词**：Before disappearing, Anne made Grahame promise to alert the authorities / But when he went home, fearing people would believe him mad, he put her spirit out of his mind / Refusing to be ignored, Anne appeared once again / This time she vowed to haunt him for eternity unless he informed the law / keeping his promise, Grahame alerted the authorities / Anne's remains, the murder weapon, and the bloodied socks and shoes were all found just where she'd said
- **为什么这样写**：这一段的骨架是两次许诺：起头 He agreed，收尾 keeping his promise——**中间隔着一个答应了却没兑现的夜晚**。而 unless he informed the law 一个 unless 把“缠他到永远”写成条件句，**鬼在这里是在下最后通牒，不是来索命**。
- **读者视角提示**：官府当天是否派人、John Walker 起初如何辩解，**本篇没写**。

## 一句话总结

1631 年鳏居的 John Walker 诱杀了前来帮厨的亲属 Anne；Anne 的鬼魂夜访磨坊里的邻居 Grahame，逐条报出凶手、凶器与尸身所在并逼他报官，两人于 8 月被捕、当天判决。
