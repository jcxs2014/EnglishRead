# NOVEMBER 8, 1848 STREEPERS TAVERN

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：那家客栈与酒馆最初建于 1704 年，地皮最初属于 William Penn——Pennsylvania 的创建者。这些年里它几度易主、几度改名。独立战争期间，它当时叫 Streepers Tavern，英国与黑森士兵（受雇于国王的德国雇佣兵）常来光顾，后来干脆住了进来。在 Hannah Streeper 的主持下——她丈夫已随殖民军出征——这里成了间谍活动的温床。战后客栈被改名为 General Wayne Inn，以美国将军 Mad Anthony Wayne 命名。客栈继续兴盛，成了 Ben Franklin、George Washington，甚至 Edgar Allen Poe 都住过的地方。19 世纪它还被 Lower Merion Township 用作投票点。某个选举日，一名女计票员下地下室取选票时，一名士兵迎面出现。那名穿绿制服的年轻士兵看起来既困惑又害怕，在她惊讶之中就淡了下去。她把此事报给上级，上级又写进了给选举委员会的报告。这名绿制服士兵此后多年里被看见过好几次，可他是谁？独立战争期间，一名穿绿制服的黑森士兵下地下室取酒，一直没有回来。他在 cellar 里发现了一条秘密隧道；躲在那里的爱国者为了不让秘密暴露杀了他并藏起尸体。自那以后，他似乎一直在客栈里游荡，希望自己的尸骨能被找到，好得到一次正经的安葬。
- **篇目日期**：NOVEMBER 8, 1848（标题所载年份，即当年事发的年份）
- **事件年代**：1704 年初建；独立战争期间称 Streepers Tavern；战后改名 General Wayne Inn；1800s 用作投票点；绿制服士兵此后多年仍被看见
- **地点**：Merion, Pennsylvania（Streepers Tavern / General Wayne Inn 的地下室、cellar 与投票点）
- **异象类型**：穿绿制服的士兵身影（困惑害怕、走近便淡去，多年后仍在出没）
- **叙事手法**：1704 年初建，地属 William Penn → 几度易主改名 → 独立战争期间叫 Streepers Tavern → 英军与黑森士兵光顾并入住 → Hannah Streeper 主持，丈夫已出征 → 成了间谍活动温床 → 战后改名 General Wayne Inn → 名人下榻之处 → 19 世纪用作投票点 → 选举日女计票员下地下室取选票 → 一名士兵迎面出现 → 穿绿制服，神情困惑害怕 → 当着她淡去 → 上报并写进官方报告 → 此后多年仍被看见 → 那名黑森士兵当年下地下室取酒 → 再没回来 → 在 cellar 发现秘密隧道 → 爱国者杀他藏尸 → 他在等自己的尸骨被找到好安葬

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The original inn and tavern was built in 1704 on land first owned by William Penn, the founder of Pennsylvania. It changed hands and names several times through the years. During the Revolutionary War, Streepers Tavern, as it was then called, was frequented and eventually occupied by British and Hessian soldiers (German mercenaries in employment of the king). Under the direction of Hannah Streeper, whose husband had gone off to serve with the colonials, it became a hotbed of spy activity. After the war ended, the inn was renamed the General Wayne Inn after American general Mad Anthony Wayne."

- **中文理解**：那家客栈与酒馆最初建于 1704 年，地皮最初属于 William Penn——Pennsylvania 的创建者。这些年里它几度易主、几度改名。独立战争期间，它当时叫 Streepers Tavern，英国与黑森士兵（受雇于国王的德国雇佣兵）常来光顾，后来干脆住了进来。在 Hannah Streeper 的主持下——她丈夫已随殖民军出征——这里成了间谍活动的温床。战后，客栈被改名为 General Wayne Inn，以美国将军 Mad Anthony Wayne 命名。
- **关键词**：The original inn and tavern was built in 1704 / on land first owned by William Penn / the founder of Pennsylvania / It changed hands and names / several times through the years / During the Revolutionary War / Streepers Tavern, as it was then called / was frequented and eventually occupied by / British and Hessian soldiers / German mercenaries in employment of the king / Under the direction of Hannah Streeper / whose husband had gone off to serve with the colonials / it became a hotbed of spy activity / After the war ended, the inn was renamed the General Wayne Inn / after American general / Mad Anthony Wayne
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 as it was then called 给旧名加个括注式的说明，**而 Under the direction of Hannah Streeper, whose husband had gone off to serve with the colonials 用 whose 一从句把一个掌柜写成了独自撑局的军属**。改名那一句把宾语拆成 inn 和名字两截，先说这栋楼，再说这称号。
- **读者视角提示**：这栋楼几度易主，**本篇没列出中间那些名字**。

> **原句 2:** "The inn continued to thrive and became a popular stopover for such notables as Ben Franklin, George Washington, and even Edgar Allen Poe. In the 1800s it was used as a polling place for Lower Merion Township. One election day, a female poll worker went into the cellar to retrieve some ballots, when a soldier confronted her. The young soldier, wearing a green uniform, seemed confused and frightened and to her surprise simply faded away. She reported this incident to her supervisor, who included it in an official report to the election board. The soldier in the green uniform has been seen several times through the years, but who was he?"

- **中文理解**：客栈继续兴盛，成了 Ben Franklin、George Washington，甚至 Edgar Allen Poe 都住过的地方。19 世纪它还被 Lower Merion Township 用作投票点。某个选举日，一名女计票员下地下室取选票时，一名士兵迎面出现。那名穿绿制服的年轻士兵看起来既困惑又害怕，在她惊讶之中就淡了下去。她把此事报给上级，上级又写进了给选举委员会的报告。这名绿制服士兵此后多年里被看见过好几次，可他是谁？
- **关键词**：The inn continued to thrive / became a popular stopover for such notables as / Ben Franklin, George Washington, and even Edgar Allen Poe / In the 1800s it was used as / a polling place for Lower Merion Township / One election day, a female poll worker went into the cellar / to retrieve some ballots / when a soldier confronted her / The young soldier, wearing a green uniform / seemed confused and frightened / and to her surprise simply faded away / She reported this incident to her supervisor / who included it in an official report / to the election board / has been seen several times through the years / but who was he?
- **为什么这样写**：作者把绿制服与困惑、害怕并列，**而 seemed confused and frightened 与 to her surprise simply faded away 让消失发生在她眼前、不作解释**。最后一句用 but who was he? 单独立住，把前面那份官方报告的确定性一下抽空。
- **读者视角提示**：目击者是女计票员，**本篇没给她与那名士兵的姓名**。

> **原句 3:** "During the Revolutionary War, a Hessian soldier, who wore a green uniform, went into the basement to get some wine. He never returned. While he was in the cellar, he discovered a secret tunnel. Patriots, hiding there, killed the soldier to prevent discovery and hid the body. Since then, it seems that he roams the inn hoping that his body will be discovered, looking for a proper burial."

- **中文理解**：独立战争期间，一名穿绿制服的黑森士兵下地下室去取些酒，一直没有回来。他在 cellar 里发现了一条秘密隧道。躲在那里的爱国者为了不让秘密暴露，杀了这名士兵并藏起尸体。自那以后，他似乎一直在客栈里游荡，希望自己的尸骨能被发现，好得到一次正经的安葬。
- **关键词**：During the Revolutionary War, a Hessian soldier / who wore a green uniform / went into the basement to get some wine / He never returned / While he was in the cellar / he discovered a secret tunnel / Patriots, hiding there, killed the soldier / to prevent discovery / and hid the body / it seems that he roams the inn / hoping that his body will be discovered / looking for a proper burial
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 to prevent discovery 把杀人的动机压成一句，**而 hoping that his body will be discovered 与 looking for a proper burial 两处并排，把一个鬼的游荡写成了替自己办后事**。开头与第二段的绿制服是同一件衣服，两处对上，身份才补出来。
- **读者视角提示**：书把绿制服的兵认作当年那名黑森士兵，**本篇没给出他被埋的具体位置**。
