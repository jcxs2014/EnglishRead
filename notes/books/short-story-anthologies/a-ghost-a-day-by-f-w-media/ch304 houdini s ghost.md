# OCTOBER 31, 1936 HOUDINI'S GHOST

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Houdini 生于 1874 年的 Hungary 首都 Budapest，原名 Ehrich Weiss。他家搬到美国，他在 Wisconsin 州的 Appleton 长大，在那里他的兴趣很快转向了魔术。他取了一位法国著名魔术师的姓作为艺名 Houdini，此后登台场场满座，成了有史以来最受推崇的魔术师之一。母亲去世后，他开始对通灵运动产生兴趣。他很快发现参与其中的许多灵媒都是骗子，用的是简单的魔术把戏。他对这个运动幻灭下来，转而致力于揭露尽可能多的骗局。尽管有这场揭露，他仍旧迷上了死亡与来世，并对忠贞的妻子 Bess 说：若有人能从彼岸回来，那个人就是他。两人定下一个计划，用一句暗语由他从死亡那一边带话回来，好让她知道他确实在。1926 年万圣节那天，Houdini 在五十二岁上死于离奇情形。官方死因是阑尾穿孔引起的腹膜炎，但也有人说他是被通灵运动的人杀害的。他死后，Bess Houdini 开始举行降神会，希望与亡夫沟通。官方承认的最后一次降神会是在 1936 年万圣节夜，于 Knicker-bocker Hotel 的屋顶上举行的。一小时过去，毫无接触。就在他们要放弃时，一场猛烈的风暴袭来，大雨、雷电交加。那场诡异的风暴在 Hollywood 别处都没有出现，唯独在 Knickerbocker 上空。Bess 始终没有听到那句暗语，可很多人都确信：这是 Houdini 最后一次试图从坟墓那一边取得联系。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 31, 1936（标题所载日期，即最后一次降神会之年）
- **事件年代**：1874 年生于 Budapest；1926 年万圣节去世，五十二岁；1936 年万圣节夜在 Knicker-bocker Hotel 屋顶举行官方承认的最后一次降神会
- **地点**：Hollywood, California（Knicker-bocker Hotel 的屋顶）
- **异象类型**：Houdini 的鬼（万圣节夜的屋顶降神会与一场只笼罩那家酒店的雷暴）
- **叙事手法**：Houdini 1874 年生于 Budapest，原名 Ehrich Weiss → 家搬到美国 → 在 Appleton 长大 → 兴趣转向魔术 → 取法国魔术师之名 → 场场满座 → 成了最了不起的魔术师之一 → 母亲去世后对通灵运动产生兴趣 → 发现许多灵媒是骗子 → 用简单魔术把戏 → 对该运动幻灭 → 致力于揭露骗局 → 仍迷上死亡与来世 → 对妻子 Bess 说若有人能回来就是他 → 两人定下暗语计划 → 由他从死亡那一边带话 → 1926 年万圣节死于五十二岁 → 官方死因是阑尾穿孔引起的腹膜炎 → 也有人说他是被通灵界杀害的 → 死后 Bess 开始举行降神会 → 官方承认的最后一次在 1936 年万圣节夜 → 地点是 Knicker-bocker Hotel 屋顶 → 一小时过去毫无接触 → 就在放弃时风暴袭来，大雨雷电 → 那场风暴别处都没有 → 唯独在 Knickerbocker 上空 → Bess 始终没听到暗语 → 很多人确信这是他最后一次尝试

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Houdini was born Ehrich Weiss in Budapest, Hungary, in 1874. His family moved to America and he grew up in Appleton, Wisconsin, where his interest soon turned to magic. Taking the name Houdini after a famous French magician, he soon performed to sold-out shows, becoming one of the greatest magicians of all time."

- **中文理解**：Houdini 生于 1874 年 Budapest，原名 Ehrich Weiss。他家搬到美国，他在 Wisconsin 州的 Appleton 长大，在那里他的兴趣很快转向了魔术。他取了一位法国著名魔术师的姓作为艺名 Houdini，此后登台场场满座，成了有史以来最受推崇的魔术师之一。
- **关键词**：Houdini was born Ehrich Weiss in Budapest, Hungary, in 1874 / His family moved to America / he grew up in Appleton, Wisconsin / where his interest soon turned to magic / Taking the name Houdini after a famous French magician / he soon performed to sold-out shows / becoming one of the greatest magicians of all time
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 born Ehrich Weiss 把本名与艺名隔开，**而 Taking the name Houdini after a famous French magician 一句交代艺名的来路，不带任何感情**。后半段 his interest soon turned to magic 用一个 soon 把魔术的来处系在他长大的地方。
- **读者视角提示**：那位法国魔术师的姓名，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "After his mother died he became interested in the Spiritualist movement. He soon discovered that many of the mediums involved were fakes, employing simple magic tricks. He became disillusioned with the movement and took on a crusade to expose as many of the frauds as he could. Despite his crusade, he became obsessed with death and the afterlife, telling his faithful wife, Bess, that if anybody could come back from the other side, he would. They devised a plan with a code phrase that he would convey from death to let her know that he was there."

- **中文理解**：母亲去世后，他开始对通灵运动产生兴趣。他很快发现参与其中的许多灵媒都是骗子，用的是简单的魔术把戏。他对这个运动幻灭下来，转而致力于揭露尽可能多的骗局。尽管有这场揭露，他仍旧迷上了死亡与来世，并对忠贞的妻子 Bess 说：若有人能从彼岸回来，那个人就是他。两人定下一个计划，用一句暗语由他从死亡那一边带话回来，好让她知道他确实在。
- **关键词**：After his mother died he became interested in the Spiritualist movement / He soon discovered that many of the mediums involved were fakes / employing simple magic tricks / He became disillusioned with the movement / and took on a crusade to expose as many of the frauds as he could / Despite his crusade, he became obsessed with death and the afterlife / telling his faithful wife, Bess / that if anybody could come back from the other side / They devised a plan with a code phrase / that he would convey from death / to let her know that he was there
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 Despite his crusade 一句把「揭穿骗子」与「仍迷恋死后」并排放，**而 if anybody could come back from the other side, he would 用一个条件句把赌注押在自己身上**。code phrase 这个词组是他与妻子之间那桩约定的全部内容，一个字也没多说。
- **读者视角提示**：那句暗语是什么，**本篇始终没有写出来**。

> **原句 3:** "In 1926, on Halloween, Houdini died at the age of fifty-two under mysterious circumstances. The official cause of death was peritonitis from a ruptured appendix, but some say he was murdered by members of the Spiritualist movement. After his death, Bess Houdini began holding séances in hopes of communicating with her late husband. The last official séance was held on the roof of the Knicker-bocker Hotel on Halloween night, 1936. An hour passed with no contact. Just as they gave up, a violent storm struck with driving rain and intense thunder and lightning. The mysterious storm appeared nowhere else in Hollywood, except for over the Knickerbocker, and although Bess never heard the code word, many are certain this was Houdini's last attempt to make contact from beyond the grave."

- **中文理解**：1926 年万圣节那天，Houdini 在五十二岁上死于离奇情形。官方死因是阑尾穿孔引起的腹膜炎，但也有人说他是被通灵运动的人杀害的。他死后，Bess Houdini 开始举行降神会，希望与亡夫沟通。官方承认的最后一次降神会是在 1936 年万圣节夜，于 Knicker-bocker Hotel 的屋顶上举行的。一小时过去，毫无接触。就在他们要放弃时，一场猛烈的风暴袭来，大雨、雷电交加。那场诡异的风暴在 Hollywood 别处都没有出现，唯独在 Knickerbocker 上空。Bess 始终没有听到那句暗语，可很多人都确信：这是 Houdini 最后一次试图从坟墓那一边取得联系。
- **关键词**：In 1926, on Halloween, Houdini died at the age of fifty-two / under mysterious circumstances / The official cause of death was peritonitis from a ruptured appendix / but some say he was murdered by members of the Spiritualist movement / After his death, Bess Houdini began holding séances / in hopes of communicating with her late husband / The last official séance was held on the roof of the Knicker-bocker Hotel / on Halloween night, 1936 / An hour passed with no contact / Just as they gave up, a violent storm struck / with driving rain and intense thunder and lightning / The mysterious storm appeared nowhere else in Hollywood / except for over the Knickerbocker / although Bess never heard the code word / many are certain this was Houdini's last attempt / to make contact from beyond the grave
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 An hour passed with no contact 与 Just as they gave up 把放弃与风暴接成一次擦肩，**而 appeared nowhere else in Hollywood, except for over the Knicker-bocker 用一个 except for 把那场雨圈在一家酒店头顶**。末句把结论让给 many are certain，而暗语始终没被听到，这两件事并排收尾。
- **读者视角提示**：那次屋顶降神会有哪些人在场，**本篇没有列出**。

**附：原文 TERRIFYING TIDBIT**

Physical mediums of the late nineteenth century claimed they communicated with spirits by luminous clouds and ectoplasm that would emanate from them and harden to the touch. Most were considered frauds, while others could not be debunked.

## 一句话总结

1926 年万圣节死于腹膜炎的 Houdini，与妻子定下一句暗语；1936 年万圣节夜屋顶降神会落空时，一场只笼罩 Knicker-bocker Hotel 的雷暴替他作了回答。
