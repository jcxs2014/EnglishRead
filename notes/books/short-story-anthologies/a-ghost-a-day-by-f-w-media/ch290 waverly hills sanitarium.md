# OCTOBER 17, 1926 WAVERLY HILLS SANITARIUM

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Waverly Sanitarium 被传为世上最闹鬼的地点之一。Waverly Hills 这个镇子起初是温和的，甚至安宁。在那个女性不被鼓励读书的年代，有一位走在时代前面的 Major Thomas H. Hayes 买下这处房产，并建起一所只有一个房间的小学。1883 年，Hayes 雇了 Lizzie Harris 来教他的女儿们。1910 年，Board of Tuberculosis Hospital 买下这处房产，向病人开门。1926 年加建了一座更大的楼，用来接纳从全国各地运来的大批病人。1962 年它不再是结核病院，而成了一家 geriatric 病人中心。1982 年 Kentucky 州政府接手并将其关闭。今天，来自世界各地的超自然调查者都来造访 Waverly Hills。这座医院虽说是众灵的游乐场，许多灵异报告却似乎都集中在那条 death tunnel——也叫 body chute。无数病人在接受结核病治疗时死去。为了不让病人面对即将到来的死兆，也为了尽快处理遗体，死者被投进那条滑道，通到地下室。在那里，他们要么被火化，要么被送往殡仪馆下葬。就在这条滑道附近，访客常常见到完整的显形，也常听见许久以前那些病人的空灵低语，在走廊里游荡、寻找自己的遗骸。一个小女孩与一个小男孩的活泼灵体也曾被看见在医院各处游走。一名调查者 Marley Gibson 拍到了后来被称为「the ball video」的东西。半个多小时里，那个孩子的灵似乎在与 Marley 拔河抢一个沙滩球。地板是平的，视野里没有一丝风，可视频里能看见一种活泼的力量在看不见的手背后。怀疑？你或许该跟着那些在医院走廊里寻找与死者沟通的调查者去看看。Waverly Hills Sanitarium 也许会让你信了。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Waverly Sanitarium is reputed to be among the most haunted locations in the world. The town of Waverly Hills had a benign, even serene beginning. During an era when women were discouraged from learning, Major Thomas H. Hayes, a man way ahead of his time, purchased the property and had a one-room schoolhouse built. In 1883, Hayes hired Lizzie Harris to instruct his girls."

- **中文理解**：Waverly Sanitarium 被传为世上最闹鬼的地点之一。Waverly Hills 这个镇子起初是温和的，甚至安宁。在那个女性不被鼓励读书的年代，有一位走在时代前面的 Major Thomas H. Hayes 买下这处房产，并建起一所只有一个房间的小学。1883 年，Hayes 雇了 Lizzie Harris 来教他的女儿们。
- **关键词**：Waverly Sanitarium is reputed to be / among the most haunted locations in the world / The town of Waverly Hills had a benign, even serene beginning / During an era when women were discouraged from learning / Major Thomas H. Hayes, a man way ahead of his time / purchased the property / and had a one-room schoolhouse built / In 1883, Hayes hired Lizzie Harris / to instruct his girls
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 benign, even serene 一句把镇子的开头写得干净，**而 a man way ahead of his time 这句评语是后文所有惨事之前的开篇那一次夸奖**。the girls 与 women 被 discouraged from learning 前后对读，一个人办起了一间学堂。
- **读者视角提示**：那位 Major 与那位女教师各自的下场，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "The Board of Tuberculosis Hospital later purchased the property in 1910, opening its doors to the sick. In 1926 a larger building was added to handle the scores of patients that were being transported from all over the country. In 1962, it stopped being a tuberculosis hospital and became a center for geriatric patients. In 1982, the state of Kentucky stepped in and closed it."

- **中文理解**：1910 年，Board of Tuberculosis Hospital 买下这处房产，向病人开门。1926 年加建了一座更大的楼，用来接纳从全国各地运来的大批病人。1962 年它不再是结核病院，而成了一家 geriatric 病人中心。1982 年 Kentucky 州政府接手并将其关闭。
- **关键词**：The Board of Tuberculosis Hospital later purchased the property in 1910 / opening its doors to the sick / In 1926 a larger building was added / to handle the scores of patients / that were being transported from all over the country / In 1962, it stopped being a tuberculosis hospital / and became a center for geriatric patients / In 1982, the state of Kentucky stepped in / and closed it
- **为什么这样写**：作者把四个年份排成一条时间线，**而 stopped being a tuberculosis hospital 用一个 stopped 把身份换掉，不说它变成了什么用途之外的事**。stepped in 三字把州政府的动作写成插手而非接管。
- **读者视角提示**：关闭时院里有几名病人、他们去了哪里，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "Today, paranormal investigators from all over the world visit Waverly Hills. Although the hospital is a playground to hosts of spirits, many reports of spiritual phenomena seem to center around the death tunnel, also known as the body chute. Countless patients died while undergoing treatment for tuberculosis. Rather than horrify the patients with impending doom, and to dispose of the bodies quickly, the dead were deposited in the chute, which emptied into the basement. There they were either cremated or sent to a funeral home for burial. It's near this chute that visitors have often witnessed full apparitions and heard the eerie chatter of patients of long ago, roaming the halls in search of their remains. The playful spirits of a little girl and boy have been seen wandering throughout the hospital. One investigator, Marley Gibson, captured what has come to be known as “the ball video.” For more than a half hour, the spirit of a child seems to be playing tug of war with Marley over a beach ball. The floor was level. There was no breeze in sight, but on the video, one can see a playful force behind unseen hands. Skeptical? Perhaps you should visit with the many investigators who walk the halls of the old hospital in search of communing with the dead. Waverly Hills Sanitarium may make a believer out of you yet."

- **中文理解**：今天，来自世界各地的超自然调查者都来造访 Waverly Hills。这座医院虽说是众灵的游乐场，许多灵异报告却似乎都集中在那条 death tunnel——也叫 body chute。无数病人在接受结核病治疗时死去。为了不让病人面对即将到来的死兆，也为了尽快处理遗体，死者被投进那条滑道，通到地下室。在那里，他们要么被火化，要么被送往殡仪馆下葬。就在这条滑道附近，访客常常见到完整的显形，也常听见许久以前那些病人的空灵低语，在走廊里游荡、寻找自己的遗骸。一个小女孩与一个小男孩的活泼灵体也曾被看见在医院各处游走。一名调查者 Marley Gibson 拍到了后来被称为「the ball video」的东西。半个多小时里，那个孩子的灵似乎在与 Marley 拔河抢一个沙滩球。地板是平的，视野里没有一丝风，可视频里能看见一种活泼的力量在看不见的手背后。怀疑？你或许该跟着那些在医院走廊里寻找与死者沟通的调查者去看看。Waverly Hills Sanitarium 也许会让你信了。
- **关键词**：Today, paranormal investigators from all over the world visit Waverly Hills / the hospital is a playground to hosts of spirits / many reports of spiritual phenomena seem to center around / the death tunnel, also known as / the body chute / Countless patients died while undergoing treatment for tuberculosis / Rather than horrify the patients with impending doom / and to dispose of the bodies quickly / the dead were deposited in the chute, which emptied into the basement / They were either cremated or sent to a funeral home for burial / visitors have often witnessed full apparitions / heard the eerie chatter of patients of long ago / roaming the halls in search of their remains / One investigator, Marley Gibson, captured / what has come to be known as / the ball video / the spirit of a child seems to be playing tug of war with Marley over a beach ball / The floor was level / There was no breeze in sight / on the video, one can see a playful force behind unseen hands
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 Rather than horrify the patients with impending doom 与 and to dispose of the bodies quickly 两个理由并排，**而 The floor was level 与 There was no breeze in sight 两句先把物理解释的路堵死，再让 playful force 出现**。playing tug of war 一句把录像里那半个多小时写成了两个方向的拉扯。
- **读者视角提示**：那两个孩子灵体的来历，**本篇没有说明**。
