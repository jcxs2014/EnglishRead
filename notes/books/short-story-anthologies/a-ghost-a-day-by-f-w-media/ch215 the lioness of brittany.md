# AUGUST 3, 1343 THE LIONESS OF BRITTANY

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1330 年，第一任丈夫去世后，Jeanne-Louise de Bel-leville 嫁给了 Clisson 伯爵 Oliver de Clisson。伯爵是位富有的贵族，拥有大片地产，包括 Clisson 城堡和南特的一处庄园宅邸。两人原本极其幸福，可那是个艰难的时代：法国王位上坐着铁腕的 Philippe IV。Oliver 曾出力保卫布列塔尼不受英国人侵扰，他的忠诚却招来猜疑。他被诱到布列塔尼公爵的城堡，以捏造的罪名被捕，押往巴黎受审；被朋友 Charles de Blois 出卖，他被判有罪、遭了斩首，首级被送到南特插在木杆上示众。Jeanne de Clisson 心如死灰，却拒绝离开城堡。她慢慢变卖地产，直到只剩下这座城堡；用换来的钱，她暗中买下并武装了三艘船，漆成黑身、挂起红帆。一个黑夜里她离开城堡，去统率自己的黑色舰队——法国航运上的恐怖统治就此开始。Jeanne 毫不留情地追猎并击沉国王 Philippe 的船只，船上的人全部杀掉，只留一个放回去向他报告她的背叛行径；即便 Philippe 死后她仍继续这波恐怖，尤其享受亲手把她抓到的法国贵族斩首。十三年后，不列颠的母狮沉掉了自己的舰队，避居英国；后来她回到法国，据说死于 1359 年。可直到今天，仍能看见那灰色的幽灵影像在 Clisson 城堡的走廊里走过。看来连复仇也没能让这颗悲恸的灵魂安息。
- **篇目日期**：AUGUST 3, 1343（标题所载日期；正文另给 1330 年改嫁、其后十三年、1359 年卒）
- **事件年代**：1330 年改嫁 → 丈夫被处决后十三年沉舰避居英国 → 据说 1359 年卒；更早年份未给
- **地点**：Clisson, France（Clisson 城堡与南特的庄园；她的黑色舰队所劫的法国航路）
- **异象类型**：不列颠的母狮 Jeanne de Clisson 的灰色幽灵影像，至今仍在 Clisson 城堡走廊里走过
- **叙事手法**：1330 年第一任丈夫去世后 Jeanne-Louise de Bel-leville 改嫁 Clisson 伯爵 Oliver → 伯爵富有、拥有 Clisson 城堡与南特庄园 → 两人原本极其幸福 → 可法国王位上是铁腕的 Philippe IV → Oliver 曾保卫布列塔尼不受英国侵扰 → 忠诚却招来猜疑 → 被诱到布列塔尼公爵城堡、以捏造罪名被捕 → 押往巴黎受审 → 被朋友 Charles de Blois 出卖 → 判罪斩首 → 首级送南特插杆示众 → Jeanne 心如死灰却拒绝离开城堡 → 慢慢变卖地产直到只剩城堡 → 用换来的钱暗中买下并武装三艘船 → 漆黑船身、红帆 → 一个黑夜里离堡统率黑色舰队 → 法国航运恐怖统治开始 → 猎击沉 Philippe 的船只 → 船上人全杀、只留一个回去报信 → Philippe 死后仍继续这波恐怖 → 尤其享受亲手斩首抓到的法国贵族 → 十三年后母狮沉舰避居英国 → 后来回法国、据说死于 1359 → 至今仍见灰色幽灵影像在城堡走廊走过 → 连复仇也换不来安息

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "In 1330, after the death of her first husband, Jeanne-Louise de Bel-leville married Count Oliver de Clisson. The count was a wealthy nobleman who had a vast estate including Clisson Castle and a manor house in Nantes. They were extremely happy together, but these were difficult times. On the throne of France was the ruthless Philippe IV. Although Oliver had helped defend Brittany from the English, his loyalty fell under suspicion. He was lured to the Duke of Brittany's castle, where he was arrested on trumped-up charges and taken to Paris to stand trial. Betrayed by his friend Charles de Blois, he was found guilty and beheaded. His head was sent to Nantes and displayed on a pole."

- **中文理解**：1330 年，第一任丈夫去世后，Jeanne-Louise de Bel-leville 嫁给了 Clisson 伯爵 Oliver de Clisson。伯爵是位富有的贵族，拥有大片地产，包括 Clisson 城堡和南特的一处庄园宅邸。两人原本极其幸福，可那是个艰难的时代：法国王位上坐着铁腕的 Philippe IV。Oliver 曾出力保卫布列塔尼不受英国人侵扰，他的忠诚却招来猜疑。他被诱到布列塔尼公爵的城堡，以捏造的罪名被捕，押往巴黎受审。被朋友 Charles de Blois 出卖，他被判有罪、遭了斩首，首级被送到南特，插在木杆上示众。
- **关键词**：Jeanne-Louise de Bel-leville married Count Oliver de Clisson / The count was a wealthy nobleman who had a vast estate including Clisson Castle and a manor house in Nantes / They were extremely happy together, but these were difficult times / On the throne of France was the ruthless Philippe IV / Although Oliver had helped defend Brittany from the English, his loyalty fell under suspicion / He was lured to the Duke of Brittany's castle, where he was arrested on trumped-up charges and taken to Paris to stand trial / Betrayed by his friend Charles de Blois, he was found guilty and beheaded / His head was sent to Nantes and displayed on a pole
- **为什么这样写**：这一段的节奏是先给全、再给空：vast estate 与 extremely happy 之后紧跟 difficult times 和 ruthless Philippe IV。**而 trumped-up charges 里的那个前缀就定了性——罪名是编出来的**。末句 His head was sent to Nantes and displayed on a pole 用最短的句子写最重的羞辱：**斩首已经完了，这才是第二刀**。
- **读者视角提示**：城堡的继承权后来归谁、出卖他的那位朋友后来怎样，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "Jeanne de Clisson was devastated and refused to leave the castle. She slowly began to sell the estate until only the castle remained. With the money she acquired, she secretly purchased and armed three ships. They were painted black with red sails. One dark night she left the castle to take command of her black fleet. Thus began a reign of terror on French shipping."

- **中文理解**：Jeanne de Clisson 心如死灰，却拒绝离开城堡。她慢慢变卖地产，直到只剩下这座城堡。用换来的钱，她暗中买下并武装了三艘船——漆成黑身，挂起红帆。一个黑夜里，她离开城堡，去统率自己的黑色舰队。法国航运上的恐怖统治就此开始。
- **关键词**：Jeanne de Clisson was devastated and refused to leave the castle / She slowly began to sell the estate until only the castle remained / With the money she acquired, she secretly purchased and armed three ships / They were painted black with red sails / One dark night she left the castle to take command of her black fleet / Thus began a reign of terror on French shipping
- **为什么这样写**：三个动作的顺序是这段的全部重量：卖掉家产、造出舰队、亲自出海。**而 refused to leave the castle 一句交代了她为什么不出走——她要守着的正是最后剩下的那座**。末句用 Thus began 把下文全部欠下，**恐怖从这里开始，句子却在此收住**。
- **读者视角提示**：那三艘船的船员是哪来的、城堡后来归了谁，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "With ruthlessness, Jeanne hunted down and destroyed the ships of King Philippe, killing all on board except for one, who was freed to report back to Philippe her treacherous act. Even after Philippe's death, she continued her wave of terror, taking particular enjoyment in personally beheading any French nobleman she captured. After thirteen years, the Lioness of Brittany scuttled her fleet and took refuge in England. Later she returned to France, and it is believed she died in 1359. But even today the gray spectral image of the Lioness of Brittany can be seen walking the halls of Clisson Castle. It seems even revenge couldn't bring rest to her grief-stricken soul."

- **中文理解**：她毫不留情地追猎并击沉国王 Philippe 的船只：船上的人全部杀掉，只留一个放回去，向 Philippe 报告她的背叛行径。即便 Philippe 死后，她仍继续这波恐怖，尤其享受亲手把她抓到的法国贵族斩首。十三年后，不列颠的母狮沉掉了自己的舰队，避居英国。后来她回到法国，据说死于 1359 年。可直到今天，仍能看见那灰色的幽灵影像在 Clisson 城堡的走廊里走过。看来连复仇也没能让这颗悲恸的灵魂安息。
- **关键词**：Jeanne hunted down and destroyed the ships of King Philippe, killing all on board except for one / who was freed to report back to Philippe her treacherous act / Even after Philippe's death, she continued her wave of terror / taking particular enjoyment in personally beheading any French nobleman she captured / After thirteen years, the Lioness of Brittany scuttled her fleet and took refuge in England / it is believed she died in 1359 / the gray spectral image of the Lioness of Brittany can be seen walking the halls of Clisson Castle / even revenge couldn't bring rest to her grief-stricken soul
- **为什么这样写**：放走那一个人是全段最狠的设计：**杀光所有人，偏留一个带话回去**。而 Even after Philippe's death 一句说明她的对象从来不只是那个人——**仇人死了，恐怖没有停**。末句用 couldn't bring rest 一个否定收尾：**她赢了，可作者说这不算休息**。
- **读者视角提示**：那位被放走的信使是谁、城堡走廊里走过的是谁，**本篇没写**。

## 一句话总结

丈夫 Oliver de Clisson 被朋友出卖、斩首示众后，Jeanne 变卖家产造出黑帆舰队袭扰法国航运十三年、亲手斩首她抓到的贵族；她避居英国后据说 1359 年卒，灰色幽灵至今仍在 Clisson 城堡走廊里走过。
