# SEPTEMBER 5, 1922 THE WINCHESTER HOUSE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：这座位于加州 Santa Clara 市中心的维多利亚式宅子，四周环绕着闹鬼的传说，以及一位富有女子对超自然事物偏执恐惧的产物。Sarah Winchester——那位古怪的 Winchester 步枪女继承人——是在见过 Boston 的灵媒 Adam Coons 之后建起这座宅子的。在那次会谈中，灵媒证实了 Sarah 心里最怕的那件事。Coons 告诉她，她丈夫与女儿的死于肺结核，其实是诅咒所致；他相信所有被 Winchester 步枪打死的亡魂都正要回来报复，而下一个就是 Sarah。为了终结这个诅咒，Coons 指示 Sarah 西迁，在那里为那些灵魂建一座房子——只要房子还在建，她就安全。满怀恐惧的 Sarah 很快迁到 San Jose，在那里买下一座八房的农舍，随后开始建一座宅子。施工一天二十四小时、一周七天，持续三十八年。如今这座一百六十个房间的宅子有 40 座楼梯、950 扇门、一万扇窗与 47 个壁炉。这座老鼠迷宫般建筑的怪事清单还在往下写。最古怪的房间之一是那间降神室，据说 Sarah 就在这间屋子里每夜举行降神会，向那些丧命于 Winchester 步枪的灵魂求教建造之道。宅子的访客与导游报告过无法解释的管风琴声；门把手自行转动；漆黑的走廊里有人看见诡异的光亮起；而在那些屡次听见的无法解释的声音之外，一些访客还描述过一团外质雾气的突然出现。看来那位在八十三岁死于宅中的 Sarah——她至今仍惧怕 Winchester 步枪受害者的鬼魂——是不敢离开这个尘世的。
- **篇目日期**：SEPTEMBER 5, 1922（标题所载日期；正文另给施工三十八年、其死时八十三岁）
- **事件年代**：标题日期 1922 年；西迁与动工年份未给；施工持续三十八年；Sarah 死于宅中时八十三岁
- **地点**：San Jose, California（Winchester House 与其降神室）
- **异象类型**：被 Winchester 步枪打死的那些人（外质雾气、走廊里的光，以及降神室里的建议）
- **叙事手法**：加州 Santa Clara 市中心的维多利亚式宅子 → 四周环绕闹鬼传说与一位富有女子对超自然的偏执恐惧 → Sarah Winchester 那位古怪的步枪女继承人 → 在见过 Boston 灵媒 Adam Coons 后建起宅子 → 会谈中灵媒证实了她最怕的事 → 丈夫与女儿死于肺结核其实是诅咒 → 他相信所有被 Winchester 步枪打死的亡魂都要回来报复 → 下一个就是 Sarah → 为终结诅咒 Coons 指示她西迁 → 在那里为灵魂建房子 → 只要房子还在建她就安全 → 满怀恐惧的 Sarah 迁到 San Jose 买下八房农舍 → 开始建宅子 → 一天二十四小时一周七天施工 → 持续三十八年 → 如今一百六十房间、有 40 楼梯 950 门一万窗 47 壁炉 → 怪事清单还在往下写 → 最古怪的房间之一是降神室 → 据说 Sarah 每夜在那里降神、向丧命于步枪的灵魂求教 → 访客报告无法解释的管风琴声 → 门把手自行转动 → 黑暗走廊里诡异光亮起 → 还见过外质雾气突然出现 → Sarah 死于宅中时八十三岁 → 至今仍惧怕那些鬼魂 → 不敢离开这个尘世

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "This Victorian mansion located in downtown Santa Clara, California, is surrounded by legends of hauntings and the results of a wealthy woman's paranoid fears of the paranormal. Sarah Winchester, the eccentric Winchester Rifle heiress, built the mansion after she'd met with Boston medium Adam Coons. During their session, the medium confirmed Sarah's worst fears. Coons told Sarah that her husband's, as well as her daughter's death from tuberculosis were in fact the result of a curse. He believed that all the souls that had been killed by a Winchester rifle were out to get revenge. And Sarah was next."

- **中文理解**：这座维多利亚式宅子位于加州 Santa Clara 市中心，四周环绕着闹鬼的传说，以及一位富有女子对超自然事物偏执恐惧的产物。Sarah Winchester——那位古怪的 Winchester 步枪女继承人——是在与 Boston 的灵媒 Adam Coons 会面之后建起这座宅子的。在那次会谈中，灵媒证实了 Sarah 心里最怕的那件事。Coons 告诉 Sarah：她丈夫与她女儿的死于肺结核，事实上是诅咒造成的。他相信所有被 Winchester 步枪打死的亡魂都正要回来报复，而下一个就是 Sarah。
- **关键词**：This Victorian mansion located in downtown Santa Clara / surrounded by legends of hauntings / the results of a wealthy woman's paranoid fears of the paranormal / Sarah Winchester, the eccentric Winchester Rifle heiress / built the mansion after she'd met with Boston medium Adam Coons / During their session, the medium confirmed Sarah's worst fears / her husband's, as well as her daughter's death from tuberculosis were in fact the result of a curse / all the souls that had been killed by a Winchester rifle were out to get revenge / And Sarah was next
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 the results of a wealthy woman's paranoid fears 一句把整座宅子的来历归结成一个人的心理，**而 built the mansion after she'd met with Boston medium Adam Coons 把顺序摆成先遇见、后建造**——房子是那个会面造出来的。末句 And Sarah was next 极短，**它把一长串受害者之后的位置留给她，句子越短，位置越可怕**。
- **读者视角提示**：Adam Coons 的会面花了多久、灵媒有没有收费，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "As a way to put an end to the curse, Coons directed Sarah to move out West where she should build a home for the spirits. As long as the home was being built, she would be safe. A fearful Sarah soon moved to San Jose, where she acquired an eight-room farmhouse. She then began building a mansion. Its construction went on twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, and it would continue for thirty-eight years. Today this 160-room mansion has 40 staircases, 950 doors, 10,000 windows, and 47 fireplaces. The list of oddities for this mouse maze of construction goes on and on."

- **中文理解**：为了终结这个诅咒，Coons 指示 Sarah 西迁，让她在那里为那些灵魂建一座房子；只要房子还在建着，她就是安全的。满怀恐惧的 Sarah 很快迁到 San Jose，在那里买下一座八房的农舍，随后开始建起一座宅子。施工一天二十四小时、一周七天，将持续三十八年。如今这座一百六十个房间的宅子有 40 座楼梯、950 扇门、一万扇窗与 47 个壁炉。这座老鼠迷宫般建筑的怪事清单还在往下写。
- **关键词**：As a way to put an end to the curse / Coons directed Sarah to move out West / where she should build a home for the spirits / As long as the home was being built, she would be safe / A fearful Sarah soon moved to San Jose / where she acquired an eight-room farmhouse / Its construction went on twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week / it would continue for thirty-eight years / Today this 160-room mansion has 40 staircases, 950 doors, 10,000 windows, and 47 fireplaces / The list of oddities for this mouse maze of construction goes on and on
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 As long as the home was being built, she would be safe 一句把安全挂在施工进度上，**于是那三十八年的不停工不再是执着，而是保命**。而那串数字连着四个 goes on and on 收尾——清单在往下列，句子也在往下写，**读者被留在一个还没有结尾的名单前**。
- **读者视角提示**：那些楼梯与门当下如何、是否有找不到的房间，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "Visitors and tour guides of the mansion have reported the inexplicable eerie sound of organ music. Doorknobs have turned of their own accord. Strange lights have been witnessed illuminating the darkened hallways. And along with the frequently heard unexplainable voices, some visitors have described the sudden appearance of an ectoplasmic mist. It appears that Sarah, who died at the mansion at age eighty-three, still fearing the ghosts of the victims of Winchester rifles, is afraid to leave this earthly plane."

- **中文理解**：宅子的访客与导游报告过无法解释的管风琴声。门把手自行转动。漆黑的走廊里有人看见诡异的光亮起。而在那些屡次听见的无法解释的声音之外，一些访客还描述过一团外质雾气的突然出现。看来那位在八十三岁死于宅中的 Sarah——她至今仍惧怕 Winchester 步枪受害者的鬼魂——是不敢离开这个尘世的。
- **关键词**：Visitors and tour guides of the mansion have reported / the inexplicable eerie sound of organ music / Doorknobs have turned of their own accord / Strange lights have been witnessed illuminating the darkened hallways / along with the frequently heard unexplainable voices / some visitors have described the sudden appearance of an ectoplasmic mist / It appears that Sarah, who died at the mansion at age eighty-three / still fearing the ghosts of the victims of Winchester rifles / is afraid to leave this earthly plane
- **为什么这样写**：作者把三样怪事写成三句逐句加长的短句：声音、把手、光，**每一句的主语都是访客或导游，没有一句写鬼是谁**。而末句把一个「死人留在原地」的说法包在 It appears that 里，**全篇最重的一个判断恰好用的是最不确定的开头**。
- **读者视角提示**：管风琴声从何而来、Sarah 是否留下了遗嘱，**本篇没写**。

## 一句话总结

Winchester 步枪女继承人 Sarah Winchester 因灵媒一句「下一个就是你」，用三十八年昼夜不停建起一百六十个房间的宅子为亡魂还债；她八十三岁死于宅中，而外质雾气至今仍在走廊里出现。
