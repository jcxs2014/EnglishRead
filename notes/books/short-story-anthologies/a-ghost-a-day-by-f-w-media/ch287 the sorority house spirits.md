# OCTOBER 14, 2009 THE SORORITY HOUSE SPIRITS

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：据 www.ghosttheory.com 报道，Bloomsburg University 一间女生联谊会的宿舍楼被灵异活动缠身。传言说 Phi Sigma Sigma 的姑娘们正被一些看不见的实体频繁造访，那些实体一心想引起她们的注意。姑娘们说起种种怪事，从水龙头自己打开到所有火警警报毫无缘由地同时响起。有许多次报告说，墙的里面传出孩子的哭声，仿佛他们正想逃出来。接着是身体上的接触。Phi Sigma Sigma 有几名女生报告被人扯头发，还被人推下楼梯。巧的是，她们被推倒的那处地方，正是走廊与楼梯间里曾看见一个黑影笼罩着的地方。在 Phi Sigma Sigma 之前住过这里的多家联谊会也都报告过类似的鬼故事。可姐妹们把这一切超自然活动归因于什么？其中一位姐妹一生都对灵异之事着迷，决定亲自上手。她用一块灵应板尝试建立联系，收到的都是些零碎信息，其中最常出现的是 Maggie 这个名字和 1934 这个年份。查了一阵之后，她发现 1934 年确有一位名叫 Maggie 的女子死于自然原因。不管这些现象的成因是什么，它们一直持续到今天。也许 Maggie 正在冥界那边给她的姐妹们下马威。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 14, 2009（标题所载日期，即报道之年）
- **事件年代**：1934 年一位名叫 Maggie 的女子去世；此后的灵异活动持续至 2009 年；更早的住客年份未给
- **地点**：Bloomsburg, PA（Bloomsburg University 的女生联谊会宿舍楼：墙内、楼梯、走廊与楼梯间）
- **异象类型**：看不见的实体（扯头发、推人下楼、墙内孩子哭声），以及 1934 年去世的 Maggie
- **叙事手法**：ghosttheory.com 报道 → Bloomsburg University 一间女生宿舍被灵异活动缠身 → 传言 Phi Sigma Sigma 的姑娘被想引起注意的实体频繁造访 → 怪事从水龙头自开到火警齐响 → 墙内传出孩子哭声仿佛要逃出来 → 接着是身体接触 → 有女生被扯头发 → 被人推下楼梯 → 巧的是被推倒处正是黑影笼罩处 → 更早的联谊会也报告类似鬼故事 → 姐妹们归因于什么 → 一位终生着迷灵异的姐妹决定亲自上手 → 用灵应板尝试联系 → 收到零碎信息 → 最常出现 Maggie 与 1934 → 查证确有一位 Maggie 于 1934 年死于自然原因 → 现象持续至今 → 也许 Maggie 在下马威

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "According to www.ghosttheory.com a Bloomsburg University sorority house is plagued by spirit activity. Rumor has it that the girls of Phi Sigma Sigma are being frequented by unseen entities intent on getting their attention. The girls speak of strange occurrences ranging from faucets being turned on by themselves to all the fire alarms going off at once for no apparent reason. On numerous occasions it has been reported that the cries of children can be heard emanating from the interior walls, as if they were trying to escape. Then there's the physical contact. Several girls of Phi Sigma Sigma have reported having their hair pulled, as well as being shoved down the stairs. Coincidently, the area where they've been shoved is the same place that a dark shadow has been seen looming in the hallways and stairwell."

- **中文理解**：据 www.ghosttheory.com 报道，Bloomsburg University 一间女生联谊会的宿舍楼被灵异活动缠身。传言说 Phi Sigma Sigma 的姑娘们正被一些看不见的实体频繁造访，那些实体一心想引起她们的注意。姑娘们说起种种怪事，从水龙头自己打开到所有火警警报毫无缘由地同时响起。有许多次报告说，墙的里面传出孩子的哭声，仿佛他们正想逃出来。接着是身体上的接触。Phi Sigma Sigma 有几名女生报告被人扯头发，还被人推下楼梯。巧的是，她们被推倒的那处地方，正是走廊与楼梯间里曾看见一个黑影笼罩着的地方。
- **关键词**：According to www.ghosttheory.com / a Bloomsburg University sorority house is plagued by spirit activity / Rumor has it that the girls of Phi Sigma Sigma are being frequented / by unseen entities intent on getting their attention / The girls speak of strange occurrences / ranging from faucets being turned on by themselves / to all the fire alarms going off at once for no apparent reason / On numerous occasions it has been reported / the cries of children can be heard emanating from the interior walls / as if they were trying to escape / Then there's the physical contact / Several girls of Phi Sigma Sigma have reported having their hair pulled / as well as being shoved down the stairs / The area where they've been shoved is the same place / that a dark shadow has been seen looming in the hallways and stairwell
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 ranging from ... to 把水龙头与火警警报两端一夹，中间那一整串怪事就都成了这一条的范围，**而 the same place 一句把被推的地点与黑影的地点钉成同一处**。children 这两个字用在成人宿舍的墙里，是全段最不安的一处对不上。
- **读者视角提示**：那几名被扯头发与推下楼的女生，**本篇没给姓名**。

> **原句 2:** "Numerous sororities occupying the location prior to Phi Sigma Sigma have reported similar ghostly tales. But to what do the sisters attribute all this paranormal activity? Well, one sister, having been intrigued by the paranormal all of her life, decided to take matters into her own hands. Using a Ouija board she attempted to make contact, and she received tidbits of information, the name Maggie and the year 1934 being the most prevalent. After a bit of research, she found that there had been a woman by the name of Maggie that died of natural causes back in 1934. Whatever the cause of the phenomena, they continue to this day. Perhaps Maggie is hazing her sisters from the other side."

- **中文理解**：在 Phi Sigma Sigma 之前住过这里的多家联谊会也都报告过类似的鬼故事。可姐妹们把这一切超自然活动归因于什么？其中一位姐妹一生都对灵异之事着迷，决定亲自上手。她用一块灵应板尝试建立联系，收到的都是些零碎信息，其中最常出现的是 Maggie 这个名字和 1934 这个年份。查了一阵之后，她发现 1934 年确有一位名叫 Maggie 的女子死于自然原因。不管这些现象的成因是什么，它们一直持续到今天。也许 Maggie 正在冥界那边给她的姐妹们下马威。
- **关键词**：Numerous sororities occupying the location prior to Phi Sigma Sigma / have reported similar ghostly tales / But to what do the sisters attribute all this paranormal activity? / one sister, having been intrigued by the paranormal all of her life / decided to take matters into her own hands / Using a Ouija board she attempted to make contact / she received tidbits of information / the name Maggie and the year 1934 being the most prevalent / After a bit of research, she found that there had been a woman / by the name of Maggie that died of natural causes back in 1934 / Whatever the cause of the phenomena, they continue to this day / Perhaps Maggie is hazing her sisters from the other side
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 a bit of research 四个字把追查那一段一笔带过，**而 hazing her sisters 一词把「下马威」这个说法从玩笑改成一种待查的关联**。Whatever the cause ... they continue 一句先认账：查不出，不等于停。
- **读者视角提示**：那位姐妹的姓名与灵应板所获信息的全貌，**本篇没写**。
