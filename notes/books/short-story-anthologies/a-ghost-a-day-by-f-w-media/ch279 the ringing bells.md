# OCTOBER 6, 1859 THE RINGING BELLS

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：某个星期五清早，Charlottetown 这个小镇的居民被 Saint James Church 的钟声惊醒。它只响了一声。觉得奇怪的他们前去查看。走向教堂的路上，它又敲了第二声，同样只一声。他们走近教堂时，它又只响了一声。踏进教堂的地界，他们听见它为第四次响起，接着又是第五声。站在教堂前，它又响了第六声。教堂大门被猛然推开时，一阵强风穿过门口，三名浑身发光的女子出现，全都穿着白色。人们敬畏地看着那钟为第七回作响，而大门随即砰地关上。他们再试着开门，门却锁上了。透过窗子望出去，他们看见那三名女子飘向钟楼的台阶，随即消失。牧师赶到后他们把门打开，只发现教堂里空无一人。钟为第八回响了一下，他们全都奔上钟楼。那里没有一个人。困惑之下，他们搜遍整座教堂，找不到一个活人。钟此后再没响过。当天傍晚，从 Nova Scotia 开往 Prince Edward Island 的本地客轮 Fairie Queene 没有出现。几天后，Charlottetown 的好人们得知，它沉在了教堂钟响的那一天。那天有八名乘客——五男三女——溺亡，与钟响的次数正好一样多。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 6, 1859（标题所载年份，即事件之年）
- **事件年代**：钟响与 Fairie Queene 沉没发生在同一日（1859 年）；具体月日未给
- **地点**：Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island, Canada（Saint James Church 与其钟楼，以及 Fairie Queene 沉没的海域）
- **异象类型**：三名穿白衣、发光的女子（推门而入、按铃八响、飘上钟楼消失），与同日溺亡的八名乘客
- **叙事手法**：某个星期五清早 → Charlottetown 居民被 Saint James Church 钟声惊醒 → 只响一声 → 觉得奇怪前去查看 → 路上第二声仍只一声 → 走近再一声 → 进地界第四声 → 第五声 → 站教堂前第六声 → 大门猛然推开 → 一阵强风穿过门口 → 三名浑身发光的女子出现、全穿白衣 → 敬畏地看着第七响 → 大门砰地关上 → 再开门已锁上 → 透过窗子见三名女子飘上钟楼台阶消失 → 牧师赶到开门 → 教堂空无一人 → 第八响 → 全奔上钟楼 → 那里没有人 → 搜遍教堂找不到活人 → 钟此后再没响 → 当天傍晚客轮 Fairie Queene 未出现 → 几天后得知沉在钟响那天 → 八名乘客五男三女溺亡 → 与钟响次数一样多

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Early one Friday morning, the residents of the small town of Charlottetown were awakened when the bell of Saint James Church rang out. It rang just once. Thinking that odd, they investigated. On the way to the church it tolled a second time, again just once. As they approached the church, again it rang just once. Entering the church grounds, they heard it toll again for the fourth time, and then again for a fifth. As they stood in front of the church, it tolled a sixth time. When the doors of the church were flung open, a powerful wind blew through the doorway and three glowing women appeared, all dressed in white. The people stood in awe as the bell tolled for the seventh time and the doors slammed shut. They tried to open them again, but the doors were locked. Peering through the windows, they saw the three women float to the bell tower steps and disappear. The minister arrived and they unlocked the doors, only to find the church empty. The bell tolled for an eighth time, as they all ran up to the belfry. There was no one there. Puzzled, they searched the entire church and could not find a living soul. The bell rang no more."

- **中文理解**：某个星期五清早，Charlottetown 这个小镇的居民被 Saint James Church 的钟声惊醒。它只响了一声。觉得奇怪的他们前去查看。走向教堂的路上，它又敲了第二声，同样只一声。他们走近教堂时，它又只响了一声。踏进教堂的地界，他们听见它为第四次响起，接着又是第五声。站在教堂前，它又响了第六声。教堂大门被猛然推开时，一阵强风穿过门口，三名浑身发光的女子出现，全都穿着白色。人们敬畏地看着那钟为第七回作响，而大门随即砰地关上。他们再试着开门，门却锁上了。透过窗子望出去，他们看见那三名女子飘向钟楼的台阶，随即消失。牧师赶到后他们把门打开，只发现教堂里空无一人。钟为第八回响了一下，他们全都奔上钟楼。那里没有一个人。困惑之下，他们搜遍整座教堂，找不到一个活人。钟此后再没响过。
- **关键词**：Early one Friday morning, the residents of the small town of Charlottetown / were awakened when the bell of Saint James Church rang out / It rang just once. Thinking that odd, they investigated / On the way to the church it tolled a second time, again just once / As they approached the church, again it rang just once / Entering the church grounds, they heard it toll again for the fourth time / and then again for a fifth / As they stood in front of the church, it tolled a sixth time / When the doors of the church were flung open / a powerful wind blew through the doorway / three glowing women appeared, all dressed in white / The people stood in awe as the bell tolled for the seventh time / and the doors slammed shut / They tried to open them again, but the doors were locked / Peering through the windows, they saw the three women float / to the bell tower steps and disappear / The minister arrived and they unlocked the doors / only to find the church empty / The bell tolled for an eighth time / as they all ran up to the belfry / There was no one there / They searched the entire church and could not find a living soul / The bell rang no more
- **为什么这样写**：作者把铃响逐次数下去，**而 It rang just once 被反复改写三次，每一次都在人靠近一步**。float to the bell tower steps 一个动词把三名女子送上钟楼，不写怎么上去。搜索的两句从 only to find the church empty 到 no one there，再到 find a living soul，一层层加码。
- **读者视角提示**：那三名女子是谁，**本篇没给**。

> **原句 2:** "Later that evening, the Fairie Queene, a local passenger ferry from Nova Scotia to Prince Edward Island, failed to show. A few days later, the good people of Charlottetown learned that it had sunk on the day the church bell rang. Eight passengers — five men and three women — had drowned that day — the same number of times the bell had tolled."

- **中文理解**：当天傍晚，从 Nova Scotia 开往 Prince Edward Island 的本地客轮 Fairie Queene 没有出现。几天后，Charlottetown 的好人们得知，它沉在了教堂钟响的那一天。那天有八名乘客——五男三女——溺亡，与钟响的次数正好一样多。
- **关键词**：Later that evening / the Fairie Queene, a local passenger ferry / from Nova Scotia to Prince Edward Island / failed to show / A few days later, the good people of Charlottetown learned / that it had sunk on the day the church bell rang / Eight passengers — five men and three women — had drowned that day / the same number of times the bell had tolled
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 the good people of Charlottetown 把打听消息的人写得和善，**而 the same number of times the bell had tolled 一句把八名乘客与八响钟并成一处对应，不说谁报应谁**。failed to show 一词把一艘船的失踪写得像一个失约。
- **读者视角提示**：Fairie Queene 沉没的位置与八人的姓名，**本篇都没给**。

## 一句话总结

Charlottetown 的 Saint James Church 那个星期五响了八回钟，三名白衣女子推门而入又飘上钟楼，当晚客轮 Fairie Queene 沉没，八名乘客溺亡——与钟响次数一样多。
