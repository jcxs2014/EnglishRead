# OCTOBER 16, 1859 JOHN WILKES BOOTH HOUSE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：位于 Harpers Ferry 的 John Wilkes Booth House 浸透了历史。南北战争期间，那正是废奴主义者 John Brown 试图掀起一场奴隶叛乱的地方。1859 年，Brown 带着十六个人——其中有些是获得自由的奴隶——试图夺取 Harpers Ferry 的一处美国军械库。可惜对这些人来说，Colonel Robert E. Lee 挫败了他们的行动。这座 John Wilkes Booth House 闹鬼吗？问问当地人的看法就行。那栋常被称作「闹鬼小屋」的宅子，是许多无法解释的鬼影目击的对象。没人住在屋里时，路人也见过奴役区里发出幽幽的光。而半透明的身影也不止一次被看见从窗前走过。对这处房产的调查揭出一件事：Booth 的亲属拥有这栋房子。据报道 Booth 曾特意到 Harpers Ferry 来看 John Brown 被处决。显然，Booth 原本打算在刺杀 President Abraham Lincoln 之后回到这栋房子、回到妻子的怀里。然而那永远没有实现。刺杀发生十二天后，已经逃离现场的 Booth 被联邦军士兵追上并击毙。有人相信 John Wilkes Booth 的鬼至今仍在这栋房子里游荡，走完他生前没能走完的那段回家路。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 16, 1859（标题所载年份，即 John Brown 袭击军械库之年）
- **事件年代**：1859 年 Brown 袭击 Harpers Ferry 军械库；Booth 来看行刑的年份未给；刺杀 Lincoln 十二天后 Booth 被击毙
- **地点**：Harpers Ferry, West Virginia（John Wilkes Booth House 的奴役区与窗前）
- **异象类型**：John Wilkes Booth 的鬼（窗前走过的人影、奴役区里的幽光，死后仍走未走完的回家路）
- **叙事手法**：John Wilkes Booth House 浸透历史 → 南北战争时 John Brown 想在这里掀起奴隶叛乱 → 1859 年 Brown 带十六人（有些是获释奴隶）→ 试图夺取 Harpers Ferry 的美国军械库 → Colonel Robert E. Lee 挫败了他们 → 这房子闹鬼吗 → 问当地人 → 那栋「闹鬼小屋」是许多无法解释目击的对象 → 无人时奴役区里有幽幽的光 → 半透明身影不止一次走过窗前 → 调查发现这房子归 Booth 的亲属所有 → Booth 曾特意来看 Brown 行刑 → 显然他本打算刺杀 Lincoln 后回到妻子身边 → 但永未实现 → 十二天后他被联邦军士兵追上并击毙 → 有人相信他的鬼仍在走那段未走完的回家路

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The John Wilkes Booth House located in Harpers Ferry is steeped in history. During the Civil War, it was the place where abolitionist John Brown attempted to start a slave revolt. In 1859, Brown, along with sixteen men, some of them freed slaves, tried to seize a U.S. arsenal at Harpers Ferry. Unfortunately for the men, Colonel Robert E. Lee thwarted their efforts."

- **中文理解**：位于 Harpers Ferry 的 John Wilkes Booth House 浸透了历史。南北战争期间，那正是废奴主义者 John Brown 试图掀起一场奴隶叛乱的地方。1859 年，Brown 带着十六个人——其中有些是获得自由的奴隶——试图夺取 Harpers Ferry 的一处美国军械库。可惜对这些人来说，Colonel Robert E. Lee 挫败了他们的行动。
- **关键词**：The John Wilkes Booth House located in Harpers Ferry is steeped in history / During the Civil War, it was the place / where abolitionist John Brown attempted to start a slave revolt / In 1859, Brown, along with sixteen men / some of them freed slaves / tried to seize a U.S. arsenal at Harpers Ferry / Unfortunately for the men, Colonel Robert E. Lee / thwarted their efforts
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 During the Civil War, it was the place 这句把一栋宅子接到一场叛乱上，**而 Unfortunately for the men 四个字先替那十六个人认了输，再让 Lee 的名字出场**。steeped in history 一词把整栋房子泡进历史里，不作任何具体断言。
- **读者视角提示**：那十六人的姓名与下场，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "Is the John Wilkes Booth House haunted? Just ask the locals their opinion. The “haunted cottage,” as it's often referred to, has been the subject of many unexplainable ghostly sightings. Passersby have seen glowing lights in the slave quarters when no one is home. And translucent figures have been seen walking past the windows on more than one occasion."

- **中文理解**：这座 John Wilkes Booth House 闹鬼吗？问问当地人的看法就行。那栋常被称作「闹鬼小屋」的宅子，是许多无法解释的鬼影目击的对象。没人住在屋里时，路人也见过奴役区里发出幽幽的光。而半透明的身影也不止一次被看见从窗前走过。
- **关键词**：Is the John Wilkes Booth House haunted? / Just ask the locals their opinion / The “haunted cottage,” as it's often referred to / has been the subject of many unexplainable ghostly sightings / Passersby have seen glowing lights in the slave quarters / when no one is home / And translucent figures have been seen walking past the windows / on more than one occasion
- **为什么这样写**：作者用一个问句加一句「问问当地人」把闹鬼与否推给读者，**而 glowing lights ... when no one is home 用一个 when 把无人这个前提按在目击之前**。on more than one occasion 一句把次数含糊过去，不报数。
- **读者视角提示**：看見那些光的路人姓名，**本篇没给**。

> **原句 3:** "Research into the property uncovered the fact that relatives of Booth owned the home. Booth was reported to have made a special visit to Harpers Ferry to attend the execution of John Brown. Evidently Booth had planned to return to the house and to the arms of his wife after he assassinated President Abraham Lincoln. However, that would never come to pass. Twelve days after the assassination, Booth, who had fled the scene, was tracked down by Union soldiers and killed. There are those that believe the ghost of John Wilkes Booth still roams this building, completing his journey home — an act he failed to accomplish while alive."

- **中文理解**：对这处房产的调查揭出一件事：Booth 的亲属拥有这栋房子。据报道 Booth 曾特意到 Harpers Ferry 来看 John Brown 被处决。显然，Booth 原本打算在刺杀 President Abraham Lincoln 之后回到这栋房子、回到妻子的怀里。然而那永远没有实现。刺杀发生十二天后，已经逃离现场的 Booth 被联邦军士兵追上并击毙。有人相信 John Wilkes Booth 的鬼至今仍在这栋房子里游荡，走完他生前没能走完的那段回家路。
- **关键词**：Research into the property uncovered the fact / relatives of Booth owned the home / Booth was reported to have made a special visit to Harpers Ferry / to attend the execution of John Brown / Evidently Booth had planned to return to the house / and to the arms of his wife after he assassinated President Abraham Lincoln / However, that would never come to pass / Twelve days after the assassination, Booth, who had fled the scene / was tracked down by Union soldiers and killed / There are those that believe the ghost of John Wilkes Booth still roams this building / completing his journey home / an act he failed to accomplish while alive
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 Evidently 替 Booth 安排好打算，再用一个 However 把打算掐断，**而 completing his journey home 与 an act he failed to accomplish while alive 前后两句把生前没走完的路交给鬼去走**。that would never come to pass 不说谁阻挠，只说没成。
- **读者视角提示**：Booth 亲属的名字与这栋房子后来的归属，**本篇没写**。

John Brown 当年在这里起事失败的那栋小屋，如今被归到 Booth 亲属名下，而 John Wilkes Booth 的鬼被相信还在走他生前没走完的回家路。
