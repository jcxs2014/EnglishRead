# AUGUST 20, 1877 SHANGHAI TUNNELS

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：俄勒冈波特兰街下的地下通道，据信是美国最为闹鬼的地点之一。闹鬼与历史向来形影不离已是众所周知的，这些暗道与旧牢房里挤满怪事也就不足为奇。据 A&E 说，1870 年代到 1940 年代间，波特兰的街下埋着一整个恐怖世界。“shanghaied”（被诱拐）这个说法就起源于那时——醉酒或毫无防备的男人女人从街上的活板门跌下去，一个 crimmper（为利拐人者）抓住受害者，把她拖走。多数情况下囚犯会被殴打、强奸，然后卖给出价最高的人。另有一套专门用来摧毁意志坚定的女人的手段：她们被锁进一间四英尺见方的黑屋，鞋被脱掉，地上撒满碎玻璃以阻逃跑。等“处理”妥当，她们就被卖到别的国家去。可这还不算完——暗道里那些众多过道与房间同时也是鸦片馆，是客人躲开法网那只眼睛的地方。可这些暗道究竟闹不闹鬼？许多人是。如今这些地下的土沟成了超自然爱好者的游乐场：参过团的人说不适，有人形容肾上腺素窜过身体、说不出缘由地想逃离某个看不见的东西；也有几名导游被另一个世界折腾得够呛，当场辞职不肯再回来。真要去看，别一个人走。那个永远警醒的 crimmper 的鬼，也许就拿你当下一个猎物。
- **篇目日期**：AUGUST 20, 1877（标题所载日期；正文另给 1870 年代至 1940 年代的地下活动期）
- **事件年代**：1870 年代到 1940 年代（暗道运作期，shanghaied 一词的起源）→ 1877 年为标题所载日期
- **地点**：Portland, Oregon（街下的地下通道、四英尺见方的黑屋，以及兼作鸦片馆的过道与房间）
- **异象类型**：永远警醒的 crimmper 的鬼，以及压在参团者与导游身上的惊惧
- **叙事手法**：埋在波特兰街下的地下通道 → 据信是美国最为闹鬼的地点之一 → 闹鬼与历史向来形影不离 → 暗道与旧牢房挤满怪事就不足为奇 → 据 A&E 说 → 1870 年代到 1940 年代街下埋着一整个恐怖世界 → shanghaied 起源于那时 → 醉酒或毫无防备的人从活板门跌下 → crimmper 抓住受害者拖走 → 囚犯多被殴打、强奸、卖给出价最高的人 → 另有摧毁意志坚定之女的手段 → 她们被锁进四英尺见方的黑屋 → 鞋被脱掉、地上撒满碎玻璃阻其逃跑 → 妥当后被卖到别国去 → 可这还不算完 → 通道与房间同时也是鸦片馆 → 客人可躲开法网那只眼睛 → 可暗道究竟闹不闹鬼 → 许多人是 → 如今地下土沟成超自然爱好者的游乐场 → 参团者说不适 → 有人感到肾上腺素窜过、说不出缘由地想逃 → 有导游被吓得不轻、当场辞职 → 真要去别一个人走 → 永远警醒的 crimmper 的鬼可能拿你当下一个猎物

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Underground tunnels buried beneath the streets of Portland, Oregon, are believed to be one of the most haunted locations in America. Since it's well known that haunting and history go hand and hand, it's no surprise that these secret passageways and old holding cells would be overflowing with paranormal phenomena. According to A&E, a world of terror lay beneath the streets of Portland from the 1870s to the 1940s. The term “shanghaied” originated at this time when drunk or unsuspecting men and women fell through trap doors on the street. A crimmper (someone who abducts for profit) would then seize the victim and haul him or her away."

- **中文理解**：埋在俄勒冈波特兰街下的地下通道，据信是美国最为闹鬼的地点之一。闹鬼与历史向来形影不离已是众所周知的，这些暗道与旧牢房会挤满超自然现象也就不奇怪了。据 A&E 说，1870 年代到 1940 年代间，波特兰的街下埋着一整个恐怖世界。“shanghaied”（被诱拐）这个说法就起源于那时——醉酒或毫无防备的男人和女人从街上的活板门跌下去。接着，一个 crimmper（为利拐人者）抓住受害者，把她拖走。
- **关键词**：Underground tunnels buried beneath the streets of Portland, Oregon, are believed to be one of the most haunted locations in America / haunting and history go hand and hand / it's no surprise that these secret passageways and old holding cells would be overflowing with paranormal phenomena / these secret passageways and old holding cells would be overflowing with paranormal phenomena / a world of terror lay beneath the streets of Portland from the 1870s to the 1940s / The term “shanghaied” originated at this time / originated at this time when drunk or unsuspecting men and women fell through trap doors on the street / A crimmper (someone who abducts for profit) would then seize the victim and haul him or her away
- **为什么这样写**：作者先用一个 no surprise 把因果提前说破——**闹鬼在这里不需要解释，因为历史已经替它解释完了**。而 crimmper 后面紧跟的括号 (someone who abducts for profit) 是一记冷手：**在暗处把人按行当分类，比写他有多可怕更瘆人**。
- **读者视角提示**：波特兰方面对这些暗道的说法、封过没有，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "More often than not, the prisoner was beaten, raped, and sold to the highest bidder. A special process to break the spirit of strong-willed women was created. The women were locked in a darkened four-by-four cell. Their shoes were removed and the floor of the cell was littered with crushed glass, a deterrent to escape. Once the women were made ready, they were sold as prostitutes to other countries. But that's not all. The numerous passageways and rooms in the tunnels were also opium dens, places where clients could escape the scrutinizing eye of the law."

- **中文理解**：多数情况下，囚犯会被殴打、强奸，然后卖给出价最高的人。另有一套专门用来摧毁意志坚定的女人的手段。这些女人被锁进一间四英尺见方的黑屋；鞋被脱掉，地上撒满碎玻璃，以此阻人逃跑。等“处理”妥当，她们就被卖到别的国家去。可这还不算完。暗道里那些众多过道与房间同时也是鸦片馆，是客人躲开法网那只眼睛的地方。
- **关键词**：the prisoner was beaten, raped, and sold to the highest bidder / A special process to break the spirit of strong-willed women was created / The women were locked in a darkened four-by-four cell / Their shoes were removed and the floor of the cell was littered with crushed glass, a deterrent to escape / Once the women were made ready, they were sold as prostitutes to other countries / The numerous passageways and rooms in the tunnels were also opium dens / clients could escape the scrutinizing eye of the law
- **为什么这样写**：碎玻璃那句的 a deterrent to escape 用逗号挂在句末，**看着像技术说明，说明的却是“不给人留脚”**。而 But that's not all. 单独成句，把刚从女人身上挪开的目光又拽回暗道：这里既关人，也卖毒品。
- **读者视角提示**：那些女人后来怎样、被卖到哪些国家，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "But are these tunnels haunted? Many say yes. Today, these underground dirt channels have become a paranormal enthusiast's playground. Many who have taken the tour have reported feeling ill. Others have said they've felt an uncomfortable feeling of adrenalin coursing through their system, as they have an inexplicable desire to escape something unseen. There have also been reports that a few of the tourist guides have experienced so frightful an ordeal with the otherworldly that they've quit on the spot, refusing to return. However, if you find yourself intrigued, and plan to visit this haunted location, do not venture off on your own. The ghost of the ever vigilant crimmper may claim you as his next victim."

- **中文理解**：可这些暗道究竟闹不闹鬼？许多人是。如今这些地下的土沟成了超自然爱好者的游乐场。许多参过团的人说不适；另一些人形容肾上腺素窜过身体，说不出缘由地想逃离某个看不见的东西。也有报告说，有几名导游被另一个世界折腾得够呛，当场辞职不肯再回来。不过，你要是觉得有意思、打算去这座闹鬼之地，别单独上路。那个永远警醒的 crimmper 的鬼，也许就拿你当下一个猎物。
- **关键词**：But are these tunnels haunted? / Many say yes / these underground dirt channels have become a paranormal enthusiast's playground / Many who have taken the tour have reported feeling ill / they've felt an uncomfortable feeling of adrenalin coursing through their system / an inexplicable desire to escape something unseen / a few of the tourist guides have experienced so frightful an ordeal with the otherworldly that they've quit on the spot / do not venture off on your own / The ghost of the ever vigilant crimmper may claim you as his next victim
- **为什么这样写**：首句是问句，**把前面整段历史交代压成“到底闹不闹鬼”一句**。而末段的 do not venture off on your own 突然用祈使句直对读者，**叙述者从旁观变成了伸手拦你的人**。
- **读者视角提示**：那个“另一个世界”具体是什么、参团者后来怎样，**本篇没写**。
