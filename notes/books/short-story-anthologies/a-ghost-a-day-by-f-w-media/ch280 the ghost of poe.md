# OCTOBER 7, 1849 THE GHOST OF POE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Edgar Allen Poe 无疑就是美国的「Macabre 大师」，写下了「The Raven」「The Tell-Tale Heart」与「The Cask of Amontillado」这类阴郁之作。就连他 1849 年 10 月那场丑陋的死，对于一个一生都笼罩在神秘黑纱之下的人而言，也似乎格外妥帖。他被发现躺在某条阴沟里，身穿着别人的衣服。他的死因被归到一连串病症上，包括酗酒、狂犬病、癫痫发作，甚至谋杀。不管人们相信他是怎么死的，有一点是确定的：很多人相信他的魂并不安息。Poe 的魂在他生前常去的几个地方都被探测到，包括 Fort Monroe、他在 Old Western Burial Ground 的墓、Worthen House Café，以及最出名的 Edgar Allen Poe House and Museum。那座 Poe House and Museum 是 Baltimore 的 Amity Street 3 号一栋典型的五房间排屋。他与祖母、姑母以及后来的妻子 Virginia 同住于此。Poe 在阁楼有一间房，在那里写了若干作品；尽管他四处旅行，他一直住到 1849 年那场神秘的死亡。造访 Poe 旧居的游客有时得到的比预想的多。冷点、诡异的灯与鬼声都被人听见过。门窗神秘地开合。而最糟的是，有人报告感觉到一只冰冷的手落在自己肩上。可 Poe 是否真的缠着那栋房子？Baltimore 有一次停电，附近街区全黑——只有 Poe  house 那里有一盏闪烁的灯。或许正是那位 Macabre 大师借着一支跳动的烛光，在写他最后的一个故事。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 7, 1849（标题所载年份，即 Poe 去世之年）
- **事件年代**：1849 年 10 月 Poe 死于 Baltimore 阴沟；TIDBIT 另记每年 1 月 19 日有人献花（属小节评述）；停电与闪灯的事件未标年份
- **地点**：Baltimore, Maryland（Amity Street 3 号的 Poe House and Museum 及其阁楼、Fort Monroe、Old Western Burial Ground 与 Worthen House Café）
- **异象类型**：Poe 的魂（冷点、诡异的灯、鬼声、落在肩上的冰冷的手、停电时独亮的灯）
- **叙事手法**：Edgar Allen Poe 无疑被称为 Macabre 大师 → 写下三篇阴郁之作 → 1849 年 10 月死于丑陋的方式 → 对一生笼罩在黑纱下的人而言似乎格外妥帖 → 被人发现在阴沟里、穿着别人的衣服 → 死因归到酗酒、狂犬病、癫痫甚至谋杀 → 不管怎么信，一点确定：很多人认为他魂不安息 → 魂在他生前常去处被探测到 → Fort Monroe、他的墓、Worthen House Café、最出名的是 Poe House and Museum → 那是 Amity Street 3 号的五房间排屋 → 他与祖母、姑母与后来的妻子 Virginia 同住 → 阁楼有他的房间、在那里写了若干作品 → 虽四处旅行仍住到 1849 年去世 → 游客常得到比预想的多 → 冷点、诡异的灯与鬼声 → 门窗神秘开合 → 最糟是肩上被一只冰冷的手碰触 → 一次停电街区全黑只有他家有闪灯 → 或是 Macabre 大师借烛光写最后一个故事

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Edgar Allen Poe was undoubtedly America's “Master of the Macabre,” penning such dark works as “The Raven,” “The Tell-Tale Heart,” and “The Cask of Amontillado.” Even his hideous death in October 1849 seemed somehow appropriate for a man whose life was shrouded by a dark mysterious veil. He was found lying in a gutter in someone else's clothes. His death was blamed on a series of afflictions, including alcoholism, rabies, a seizure, and even murder. No matter how one believes he met his demise, one thing is for sure, many believe his spirit is not at rest. Poe's spirit has been detected in several locations that he frequented in life, including Fort Monroe, his grave at the Old Western Burial Ground, the Worthen House Café, and most notably the Edgar Allen Poe House and Museum."

- **中文理解**：Edgar Allen Poe 无疑就是美国的「Macabre 大师」，写下了「The Raven」「The Tell-Tale Heart」与「The Cask of Amontillado」这类阴郁之作。就连他 1849 年 10 月那场丑陋的死，对于一个一生都笼罩在神秘黑纱之下的人而言，也似乎格外妥帖。他被发现躺在某条阴沟里，身穿着别人的衣服。他的死因被归到一连串病症上，包括酗酒、狂犬病、癫痫发作，甚至谋杀。不管人们相信他是怎么死的，有一点是确定的：很多人相信他的魂并不安息。Poe 的魂在他生前常去的几个地方都被探测到，包括 Fort Monroe、他在 Old Western Burial Ground 的墓、Worthen House Café，以及最出名的 Edgar Allen Poe House and Museum。
- **关键词**：Edgar Allen Poe was undoubtedly / America's “Master of the Macabre,” / penning such dark works as / The Raven / The Tell-Tale Heart / and The Cask of Amontillado / Even his hideous death in October 1849 / seemed somehow appropriate for a man / whose life was shrouded by a dark mysterious veil / He was found lying in a gutter in someone else's clothes / His death was blamed on a series of afflictions / including alcoholism, rabies, a seizure, and even murder / No matter how one believes he met his demise / one thing is for sure / many believe his spirit is not at rest / Poe's spirit has been detected in several locations / that he frequented in life / including Fort Monroe / his grave at the Old Western Burial Ground / the Worthen House Café / the Edgar Allen Poe House and Museum
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 shrouded by a dark mysterious veil 一句把一生写成一层黑纱，**而 He was found lying in a gutter in someone else's clothes 把死状拆成两件小事——躺着、穿着别人的衣服**。one thing is for sure 与 many believe 前后两句把真正能确定的事与多数人的相信并排放。
- **读者视角提示**：他究竟怎么死的，**本篇列了四种说法却不作定论**。

> **原句 2:** "The Poe House and Museum is a typical five-room row house located at 3 Amity Street in Baltimore. He lived there with his grandmother, aunt, and cousin Virginia, whom he later married. Poe had a room in the attic where he penned several works, and although he traveled, he lived there until his mysterious death in 1849. Visitors sometimes get more than they bargained for when they visit Poe's old haunt. Cold spots, eerie lights, and spectral voices have been heard. Doors and windows mysteriously open and close. And worst of all, some have reported the sensation of a cold icy hand upon their shoulder. But does Poe haunt the building? Once during a power outage in Baltimore, the neighborhood went completely dark — except for a flickering light at Poe's house. Perhaps it was the master of the macabre inking one final story by the glow of a dancing candlelight."

- **中文理解**：那座 Poe House and Museum 是 Baltimore 的 Amity Street 3 号一栋典型的五房间排屋。他与祖母、姑母以及后来的妻子 Virginia 同住于此。Poe 在阁楼有一间房，在那里写了若干作品；尽管他四处旅行，他一直住到 1849 年那场神秘的死亡。造访 Poe 旧居的游客有时得到的比预想的多。冷点、诡异的灯与鬼声都被人听见过。门窗神秘地开合。而最糟的是，有人报告感觉到一只冰冷的手落在自己肩上。可 Poe 是否真的缠着那栋房子？Baltimore 有一次停电，附近街区全黑——只有 Poe 那栋房子里有一盏闪烁的灯。或许正是那位 Macabre 大师借着一支跳动的烛光，在写他最后的一个故事。
- **关键词**：The Poe House and Museum is a typical five-room row house / located at 3 Amity Street in Baltimore / He lived there with his grandmother, aunt, and cousin Virginia / whom he later married / Poe had a room in the attic / where he penned several works / although he traveled / he lived there until his mysterious death in 1849 / Visitors sometimes get more than they bargained for / when they visit Poe's old haunt / Cold spots, eerie lights, and spectral voices have been heard / Doors and windows mysteriously open and close / some have reported the sensation of a cold icy hand upon their shoulder / But does Poe haunt the building? / the neighborhood went completely dark / except for a flickering light / Perhaps it was the master of the macabre / inking one final story by the glow of a dancing candlelight
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 more than they bargained for 一句把访客的期待与实得之间的距离说定，**而 a cold icy hand upon their shoulder 里的 sensation 与 upon 把触感限定在肩上那个位置**。末句 the glow of a dancing candlelight 用一个 dancing 把那盏闪灯写成一支会动的烛。
- **读者视角提示**：那次停电与那盏灯是否与他有关，**本篇只给了一个或许**。

**附：原文 TERRIFYING TIDBIT**

Every January 19, Poe's birthday, a mysterious man wearing a scarf and a black top hat and carrying a cane visits his grave site. Since 1949, the Poe Toaster, as he is known, has left three roses and a bottle of cognac on his grave in the early morning hours.

## 一句话总结

Poe 1849 年穿着别人的衣服倒在 Baltimore 的阴沟里，多种死因并存而魂不安息；如今他旧居那栋房子冷点、鬼声不断，停电时只剩他家那盏灯还亮着。
