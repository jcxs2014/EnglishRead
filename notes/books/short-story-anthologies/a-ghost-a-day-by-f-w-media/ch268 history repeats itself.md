# SEPTEMBER 25, 1978 HISTORY REPEATS ITSELF

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Lorraine 住在 Massachusetts 一栋俯瞰着十七世纪墓园的老殖民地住宅里，生活看似平常——直到女儿 Maureen 满十三岁。起初 Maureen 开始被噩梦纠缠。不久她断续的睡眠就以骇人的尖叫收场。Lorraine 担心女儿，问她怎么了。Maureen 说起那些停不下来的声音，以及每夜来看她的卧室鬼魂。Lorraine 拿不准该信什么，带女儿去看医生；医生安排了一系列检查，又开了一剂重镇定剂压住那些无法解释的声音。那个周末，母女俩单独待在有两百年历史的宅子里时，那些声音又出现了。Maureen 躺在沙发上，Lorraine 去厨房给「生病」的女儿倒水、拿镇定剂。可她刚走进厨房，前门就自行敞开。Lorraine 弄不明白，把门关上。为了不让门再自由开合，她从抽屉里取出一把黄油刀塞进门框，然后去洗澡，让 Maureen 睡着。洗完澡回到厨房，她惊恐地发现门楣上插着两把刀。第二天早上她质问 Maureen，Maureen 却说不出话来。Lorraine 接着说：「我放了一把刀进去，等我洗完澡出来，那里变成了两把——还不止这样，所有煤气喷嘴都被打开了。你本来可以杀了我们！」Maureen 答道：「那些鬼一定是气疯了，因为它们没法跟我说话。」Lorraine 站在原地，嘴张着。少女时代的恐怖忽然涌回：那些无法解释的声音，那些在夜夜把她呼唤的不属于任何人的嗓音。她的脸白了。终于，Lorraine 明白了——Maureen 根本没有病。不幸的是，对她们两人来说，过去的鬼魂回来了。
- **篇目日期**：SEPTEMBER 25, 1978（标题所载日期，即事件之年）
- **事件年代**：宅子有两百年历史、墓园为十七世纪；Maureen 满十三岁那年开始出事；标题日期为 1978 年
- **地点**：Boxford, Massachusetts（一栋俯瞰十七世纪墓园的老殖民地住宅）
- **异象类型**：无法解释的声音与自行开合的门（刀、煤气喷嘴都是它们的手笔）
- **叙事手法**：Lorraine 住在一栋俯瞰十七世纪墓园的老殖民地宅子里 → 生活看似平常 → 直到女儿 Maureen 满十三岁 → 起初她被噩梦纠缠 → 不久断续的睡眠以骇人尖叫收场 → Lorraine 问她怎么了 → 她说起停不下来的声音 → 与每夜来卧室的鬼魂 → Lorraine 拿不准 → 带她去看医生 → 医生安排一系列检查 → 开了一剂重镇定剂压住那些声音 → 那个周末母女独处 → 声音又出现 → Maureen 躺在沙发上 → Lorraine 去厨房倒水拿药 → 前门自行敞开 → 她把门关上 → 为防门再开合 → 从抽屉取出黄油刀塞进门楣 → 去洗澡让 Maureen 睡着 → 洗完回厨房发现门楣上插着两把刀 → 次日质问 → Maureen 说不出话 → Lorraine 说出煤气喷嘴全开的事实 → Maureen 答「那些鬼是气疯了，因为它们没法跟我说话」→ Lorraine 愣住 → 少女时代的恐怖涌回 → 夜夜呼唤她的那些声音 → 她的脸白了 → 终于明白 Maureen 根本没病 → 过去的鬼魂回来了

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Lorraine lived in an old Colonial home in Massachusetts overlooking a seventeenth-century cemetery. Her life was seemingly ordinary. That is, until her daughter, Maureen, turned thirteen years of age. At first she began being plagued by nightmares. And it wasn't long after that her fitful sleep ended in bloodcurdling screams. Lorraine, concerned for her daughter's well-being, asked her what was wrong. Maureen told her of voices that wouldn't stop and of ghosts in her bedroom that visited her each night."

- **中文理解**：Lorraine 住在 Massachusetts 一栋俯瞰着十七世纪墓园的老殖民地住宅里。她的生活看上去很普通。直到她的女儿 Maureen 满十三岁。起初，女儿开始被噩梦纠缠。没过多久，她断续的睡眠就以骇人的尖叫收场。Lorraine 担心女儿过得好不好，问她出了什么事。Maureen 告诉她，有些声音停不下来，还有些鬼魂每夜都到她的卧室来看她。
- **关键词**：Lorraine lived in an old Colonial home in Massachusetts / overlooking a seventeenth-century cemetery / Her life was seemingly ordinary / That is, until her daughter, Maureen, turned thirteen years of age / At first she began being plagued by nightmares / her fitful sleep ended in bloodcurdling screams / Lorraine, concerned for her daughter's well-being, asked her what was wrong / of voices that wouldn't stop / of ghosts in her bedroom that visited her each night
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 seemingly ordinary 与 That is, until 把「普通」这个判断拆成两段，**中间只用女儿十三岁这一个时间点作分界**。而 Maureen 描述症状时用的是两条平行的 of 结构——声音停不下来、鬼每夜来访，**全是「听见」与「看见」层面的事，主语始终是女儿**。
- **读者视角提示**：那座十七世纪墓园里葬着谁、Maureen 是不是从那里听来的，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "Not sure what to believe, Lorraine took her daughter to a doctor, who scheduled a series of tests and then prescribed a heavy sedative to help drown out the unexplainable voices. That weekend, while she and her daughter were alone in the 200-year-old home, the voices began again. While Maureen lay on the couch, Lorraine went to the kitchen for a glass of water and a sedative for her “ill” daughter. But just as she walked into the kitchen, the front door swung open on its own. Bewildered, she shut the door. In an attempt to keep the door from swinging freely, Lorraine retrieved a butter knife out of the drawer and shoved it in the door jamb. Then she went and took a shower while Maureen slept. After the shower she returned to the kitchen; to her horror she found two knives jammed in the door. The next morning, she confronted Maureen. Perplexed, Maureen was at a loss for words. Lorraine continued, “I put one knife in the door, and when I got out of the shower, there were two — and not only that, all the gas jets were turned on. You could have killed us!”"

- **中文理解**：Lorraine 拿不准该信什么，带女儿去看医生；医生安排了一系列检查，又开了一剂重镇定剂，帮助压住那些无法解释的声音。那个周末，她与女儿单独待在这栋有两百年历史的宅子里时，那些声音又出现了。Maureen 躺在沙发上，Lorraine 去厨房给「生病」的女儿倒一杯水、拿镇定剂。可她刚走进厨房，前门就自行敞开了。Lorraine 弄不明白，就把门关上。为了不让那扇门再自由开合，她从抽屉里取出一把黄油刀塞进门楣。然后她去洗了澡，让 Maureen 睡着。洗完澡回到厨房，她惊恐地发现门楣上插着两把刀。第二天早上，她质问 Maureen。Maureen 满腹困惑，说不出话来。
- **关键词**：Not sure what to believe, Lorraine took her daughter to a doctor / who scheduled a series of tests / then prescribed a heavy sedative to help drown out the unexplainable voices / the front door swung open on its own / Bewildered, she shut the door / Lorraine retrieved a butter knife out of the drawer / and shoved it in the door jamb / to her horror she found two knives jammed in the door / The next morning, she confronted Maureen / Perplexed, Maureen was at a loss for words
- **为什么这样写**：作者让 Lorraine 做了一个具体的、可验证的动作——把黄油刀塞进门楣，**这个动作的全部意义就建立在「只有她知道那把刀是她放的」上**。而下一句只把 one 换成了 two，中间隔着的只有她洗了一次澡。**全篇的吓人之处不在鬼做了什么，在鬼记得她做过什么。**
- **读者视角提示**：那把黄油刀是何种品牌、门楣后来如何、警方是否介入，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "As if a realization struck, Lorraine just stood there, mouth agape. The horrors of her youth suddenly resurfaced: unexplainable sounds and the disembodied voices that had called to her at all hours of the night. She paled. Finally, Lorraine understood. Maureen hadn't been ill at all. Unfortunately for them both, the ghosts of the past had returned."

- **中文理解**：仿佛一记领悟当头击中，Lorraine 只是站在那里，嘴张着。她少女时代的恐怖忽然涌回眼前：那些无法解释的声音，那些在夜夜各个时辰呼唤她的、不属于任何人的嗓音。她的脸白了。终于，Lorraine 明白了。Maureen 根本没有病。不幸的是，对她们两人来说，过去的鬼魂回来了。
- **关键词**：As if a realization struck / Lorraine just stood there, mouth agape / The horrors of her youth suddenly resurfaced / unexplainable sounds and the disembodied voices / that had called to her at all hours of the night / She paled / Finally, Lorraine understood / Maureen hadn't been ill at all / Unfortunately for them both, the ghosts of the past had returned
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 disembodied voices 一词把母亲三十年前的记忆与女儿的现状扣上——**那不是一类相似的声音，而是同一种声音**。全段最能说明结构的是 Maureen hadn't been ill at all 一句：否定只落在 ill 这个词上，**「有鬼」这件事从头到尾没有人否认过**。
- **读者视角提示**：Lorraine 少女时代具体遇见过什么、那批鬼与女儿听见的是否同一批，**本篇没写**。

## 一句话总结

十三岁的 Maureen 夜里被鬼吵醒，Lorraine 塞进门楣的黄油刀醒来变成了两把；直到女儿说出「它们没法跟我说话」这句话，母亲才认出那是自己少女时代听过的同一批鬼。
