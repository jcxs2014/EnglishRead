# AUGUST 27, 1963 SHEPPTON MINE DISASTER

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Hazleton Standard-Speaker 于 2002 年 8 月 27 日刊出对 Sheppton 矿难的一次完整复盘，距那场惨烈却又被称作神圣的救援已三十九年：两个人曾被埋在 Sheppton 矿里十四天。1963 年 8 月 27 日清晨，David Fellin 与 Hank Throne 戴着橄榄球头盔、系着降落伞吊带，被人从一个钻孔引到安全处。当他们那十四天的磨难向公众揭晓时，他们经历中的种种离奇说法也随之浮出水面。当时五十八岁的 Fellin 回忆说，与他一同被活埋的那位二十四岁工友 Throne 说，他们不该讲述自己的经历，生怕人们以为他们疯了。David Fellin 却觉得全世界都该知道，是谁给了他们活下去的力量。两人分别接受采访时，都以惊人的准确描述了那位来客——在最后八天、他们几乎要放弃的时候，那位来客一直陪着他们。那位让人显形的究竟是怎样的一个忠实的灵魂？正是几周前刚刚去世的 Pope John XXIII。David 回忆有一段他离开自己身体的时光：他看见一扇美丽的大理石门，目睹成群结队的埃及人在各自干活。他对眼前的景象大为惊叹，一面研究那些建造金字塔的人。他接着说，埃及人挪动石头的办法，与几百年来许多人的推测并不相同：实际上是二十五个人提着沙与水的桶，把混合料倒进木模里，一块一块地筑起那些重达数吨的石块。也许有人认为 Fellin 与 Throne 是产生了幻觉。Fellin 却对着《圣经》起誓：他被困矿中所经历的一切都是真的。他至少通过了两项测谎。而就在最近的 2002 年，一位研究金字塔的研究者公布了他的发现：正如 David Fellin 所见，每一块石料都是由「水泥」浇入模具而成。
- **篇目日期**：AUGUST 27, 1963（标题所载日期，即救援当日的日期；正文另给 2002 年复盘报道）
- **事件年代**：1963 年 8 月 27 日被引出矿井 → 2002 年 8 月 27 日复盘报道；Pope John XXIII 去世与研究者的发现均在 2002 年前后
- **地点**：Hazleton, Pennsylvania（Sheppton 矿与救生的那个钻孔）
- **异象类型**：Pope John XXIII 的灵魂（在矿里陪伴了最后八天，并让 David Fellin 看见金字塔工地）
- **叙事手法**：Hazleton Standard-Speaker 于 2002 年 8 月 27 日刊出对 Sheppton 矿难的完整复盘 → 距那场惨烈却又被称作神圣的救援已三十九年 → 两人曾被埋在矿里十四天 → 1963 年 8 月 27 日清晨 David Fellin 与 Hank Throne 戴着橄榄球头盔、系着降落伞吊带 → 从一个钻孔被引到安全处 → 十四天磨难向公众揭晓时 → 种种离奇说法浮出水面 → 当时五十八岁的 Fellin 回忆 → 二十四岁的工友 Throne 说他们不该讲述经历、怕人以为他们疯了 → Fellin 却觉得全世界都该知道是谁给了他们活下去的力量 → 两人分别受访 → 都以惊人准确描述了那位来客 → 在最后八天、几乎放弃时一直陪着他们 → 那位让人显形的忠实灵魂是谁 → 正是几周前刚去世的 Pope John XXIII → David 回忆有一段离开自己身体的时光 → 看见一扇美丽的大理石门 → 目睹成群埃及人干活 → 他大为惊叹并研究那些造金字塔的人 → 埃及人挪石头的办法与几百年来许多人的推测不同 → 实际是二十五人提沙与水的桶 → 把混合料倒进木模 → 一块块筑起数吨石块 → 也许有人认为二人是产生幻觉 → Fellin 对着《圣经》起誓一切都是真的 → 至少通过两项测谎 → 2002 年一位研究金字塔的研究者公布发现 → 正如 Fellin 所见每块石料都由「水泥」浇入模具而成

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "A full recount of the Sheppton mine disaster was reported in the Hazleton Standard-Speaker on August 27, 2002. Thirty-nine years after the incredibly tragic, yet divinely spiritual rescue. Two men had been entombed within the Sheppton mine for fourteen days. David Fellin and Hank Throne, wearing football helmets and parachute harnesses, were guided through a borehole to safety on the morning of August 27, 1963. When their fourteen-day ordeal was revealed to the public, the strange revelations of their experiences surfaced."

- **中文理解**：Hazleton Standard-Speaker 于 2002 年 8 月 27 日刊出对 Sheppton 矿难的一次完整复盘，距那场惨烈却又被称作神圣的救援已三十九年：两个人曾被埋在 Sheppton 矿里十四天。1963 年 8 月 27 日清晨，David Fellin 与 Hank Throne 戴着橄榄球头盔、系着降落伞吊带，被人从一个钻孔引到安全处。当他们那十四天的磨难向公众揭晓时，他们经历中的种种离奇说法也随之浮出水面。
- **关键词**：A full recount of the Sheppton mine disaster was reported / in the Hazleton Standard-Speaker on August 27, 2002 / Thirty-nine years after the incredibly tragic, yet divinely spiritual rescue / Two men had been entombed within the Sheppton mine for fourteen days / David Fellin and Hank Throne / wearing football helmets and parachute harnesses / were guided through a borehole to safety / on the morning of August 27, 1963 / When their fourteen-day ordeal was revealed to the public / the strange revelations of their experiences surfaced
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 incredibly tragic, yet divinely spiritual rescue 一句把这起事故的两个判词并列——**惨烈与神圣之间只隔一个 yet，而主语是同一场救援**。而 wearing football helmets and parachute harnesses 一句写他们被带出来时的装备，**两项装备都不是矿难里的东西，是从别处借来的**。
- **读者视角提示**：那十四天里他们靠什么活着、钻孔是谁打的，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "Fellin, who was fifty-eight at the time, recalled Throne, the twenty-four-year-old worker he'd been buried alive with, saying they ought not speak of their experiences, for fear of people thinking they were crazy. David Fellin, however, thought the world should know who had given them the strength to survive. Both men, interviewed separately, described with amazing accuracy their visitor, who had stayed with them during the last eight days of their ordeal when they'd all but given up. Who was the dedicated spirit that made its presence known? None other than Pope John XXIII, who had died just weeks before. David recalled a span of time when he left his body, saw a beautiful marble door, and witnessed scores of Egyptian men going about their work. He was in awe at the sight before him, as he studied the men building the pyramids. He went on to say that the Egyptians did not move the stones as many people over the centuries have surmised. Rather, twenty-five men carrying buckets of sand and water poured the mix into wooden forms and built each multiton block one at a time."

- **中文理解**：当时五十八岁的 Fellin 回忆说，与他一同被活埋的那位二十四岁工友 Throne 说，他们不该讲述自己的经历，生怕人们以为他们疯了。David Fellin 却觉得全世界都该知道，是谁给了他们活下去的力量。两人分别接受采访时，都以惊人的准确描述了那位来客——在最后八天、他们几乎要放弃的时候，那位来客一直陪着他们。那位让人显形的忠实灵魂究竟是谁？正是几周前刚刚去世的 Pope John XXIII。
- **关键词**：Fellin, who was fifty-eight at the time / recalled Throne, the twenty-four-year-old worker he'd been buried alive with / saying they ought not speak of their experiences / for fear of people thinking they were crazy / David Fellin, however, thought the world should know / who had given them the strength to survive / Both men, interviewed separately, described with amazing accuracy their visitor / who had stayed with them during the last eight days of their ordeal / when they'd all but given up / None other than Pope John XXIII, who had died just weeks before
- **为什么这样写**：作者把两个人的分歧写成一组对照：Throne 怕人说他们疯了、主张不说，Fellin 却要全世界知道，**一句里两个人对同一件事给出两种公开策略**。而 interviewed separately 一句紧跟 described with amazing accuracy——**分开问、答得一样，这才是这份证词被采信的全部理由**。末句 None other than 用「不是别人正是他」把前面的所有铺垫一次性兑现。
- **读者视角提示**：Pope John XXIII 为何出现在井下、Throne 是否也认同那个说法，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "There may be some that think Fellin and Throne were hallucinating. Fellin swore an oath on the Bible, that everything he experienced while trapped in the mine was true. He passed at least two polygraph tests. Recently, in 2002, a researcher studying the pyramids shared his revelation that, just as David Fellin had seen, each block was created from “cement” being poured into a form."

- **中文理解**：也许有人认为 Fellin 与 Throne 是产生了幻觉。Fellin 却对着《圣经》起誓：他被困矿中所经历的一切都是真的。他至少通过了两项测谎。而就在最近的 2002 年，一位研究金字塔的研究者公布了他的发现：正如 David Fellin 所见，每一块石料都是由「水泥」浇入模具而成。
- **关键词**：There may be some that think Fellin and Throne were hallucinating / Fellin swore an oath on the Bible / that everything he experienced while trapped in the mine was true / He passed at least two polygraph tests / Recently, in 2002, a researcher studying the pyramids shared his revelation / that, just as David Fellin had seen / each block was created from “cement” being poured into a form
- **为什么这样写**：作者把「幻觉」这个反驳放在最前，然后连上两个独立凭据：对着《圣经》起誓、至少通过两项测谎，**一个靠信仰、一个靠仪器，两条路各走一次**。而末句那位研究者的话被写成 just as David Fellin had seen——**全篇的落点不是矿难，是金字塔；一个矿下见闻与一项考古结论被并排放在一起互为佐证**。
- **读者视角提示**：金字塔学界是否接受该结论、两项测谎的具体题目，**本篇没写**。

## 一句话总结

1963 年 Sheppton 矿难中两人被埋十四天、靠一位几周前刚去世的教皇之魂撑到最后八天；三十九年后复盘报道出，而那位研究员发现金字塔石块确如 Fellin 所见由水泥浇模而成。
