# AUGUST 6, 1801 THE BANSHEE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Sir Jonah 与 Lady Barrington 在 Dublin Castle 赴宴，宾客中有驻爱尔兰英军的总司令 Lord Rossmore。午夜将至，一个愉快的夜晚之后，Rossmore 先走；Barringtons 随后也回了家就寝。凌晨两点，他们被 Sir Jonah 形容为“一种哀鸣”的声音惊醒，那声音从他窗下升起。他一把拉开窗帘向外望，既没有人也没有兽，可那哀声仍在继续。到 2:30，声音停了。接着一个悦耳的声音连喊三声“Rossmore! Rossmore! Rossmore!”听得他脊背发凉，然后归于沉寂。第二天早上来了信使：Lord Rossmore 死了。Sir Jonah 愣住了——前一晚他还见过对方，那人气色好得很。他追问细节，得知 Rossmore 的仆人听见主人的卧房里传出哀鸣，推开门冲进去时发现主人已痛苦不堪；Rossmore 不久后死去，时间正是 2:30。Sir Barrington 后来把这段遭遇记了下来，写道：“Lord Rossmore was dying at the same moment I heard his name pronounced.”他听到的那声叫究竟是什么？他手下的爱尔兰人告诉他，那是 banshee 的嚎哭。这声诡异的尖啸折磨了 Sir Jonah 一辈子。
- **篇目日期**：AUGUST 6, 1801（标题所载日期；正文只给夜里 2 A.M. 与 2:30 a.m. 两个时点）
- **事件年代**：未给年份；只记一场宴会当夜（Rossmore 于凌晨 2:30 身亡）
- **地点**：Dublin, Ireland（Dublin Castle 的宴会、Sir Jonah 家中的窗下，以及 Rossmore 的卧房）
- **异象类型**：banshee 的哀鸣与连喊 Lord Rossmore 之名的声音——响起的同一刻他正在死去
- **叙事手法**：Sir Jonah 与 Lady Barrington 在 Dublin Castle 赴宴 → 宾客中有驻英军总司令 Lord Rossmore → 午夜将至、一个愉快的夜晚之后 Rossmore 先走 → Barringtons 随后回家就寝 → 凌晨两点被“一种哀鸣”惊醒 → 声音从他窗下升起 → 拉开窗帘看既无人也无兽 → 哀声继续 → 2:30 声音停止 → 接着一个悦耳的声音连喊 Rossmore 三遍 → 然后沉寂 → 次早信使来报 Lord Rossmore 已死 → Sir Jonah 愣住因为前晚还见过他、气色很好 → 追问细节得知 Rossmore 的仆人听见卧房里的哀鸣 → 推门冲进时主人已痛苦不堪 → Rossmore 不久后死于 2:30 → Sir Barrington 记下“听见他名字被念出的同一刻他正在死去” → 问那声叫是什么 → 爱尔兰手下答是 banshee 的嚎哭 → 这声怪啸折磨了他一辈子

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Sir Jonah and Lady Barrington were attending a party in Dublin Castle. Among the guests was Lord Rossmore, commander-in-chief of British forces in Ireland. Just before the stroke of midnight and after a pleasant evening, Lord Rossmore left. The Barringtons followed suit shortly thereafter, and returned home to retire for the evening. At 2 A.M. they were awakened by what Sir Jonah described as “a plaintive sound” rising from below his window. Throwing the drapes open, he looked out, seeing neither man nor beast, but the mournful sound continued. Finally, at 2:30 a.m., it ceased. A melodic voice called out, sending shivers down his spine, “Rossmore! Rossmore! Rossmore!” Then silence."

- **中文理解**：Sir Jonah 与 Lady Barrington 正在 Dublin Castle 赴一场宴会。宾客中有驻爱尔兰英军的总司令 Lord Rossmore。午夜将至，一个愉快的夜晚之后，Rossmore 先走了。Barringtons 随后也照做，回到家就寝。凌晨两点，他们被 Sir Jonah 形容为“一种哀鸣”的声音惊醒，那声音从他窗下升起。他一把拉开窗帘向外望，既没有人也没有兽，可那哀声仍在继续。到 2:30，声音停了。接着一个悦耳的声音连喊三声“Rossmore! Rossmore! Rossmore!”听得他脊背发凉。然后是沉寂。
- **关键词**：Sir Jonah and Lady Barrington were attending a party in Dublin Castle / Lord Rossmore, commander-in-chief of British forces in Ireland / after a pleasant evening, Lord Rossmore left / they were awakened by what Sir Jonah described as / a plaintive sound / rising from below his window / Throwing the drapes open, he looked out, seeing neither man nor beast, but the mournful sound continued / at 2:30 a.m., it ceased / A melodic voice called out, sending shivers down his spine / Then silence
- **为什么这样写**：全段的写法是先给声音、后给内容：plaintive、mournful、melodic 三个形容词一路递进，**到最后读者还不知道那声音在喊什么**。而 seeing neither man nor beast 一句把窗外扫空——**声音没有身体可以附着，这正是它能报丧的条件**。末句 Then silence 独立成句，把三个名字的余响压掉。
- **读者视角提示**：那个声音与窗外是什么关系、Rossmore 当夜为何要提前走，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "The next morning a messenger arrived. Lord Rossmore was dead. Sir Jonah was stunned. After all, he had seen him the night before and he looked in perfect health. He pressed for details and discovered that Ross-more's servant had heard a mournful sound emanating from Lord Ross-more's bedroom. He threw open the door and hurried in, discovering his master in great distress. Lord Rossmore died shortly later, at 2:30 a.m."

- **中文理解**：第二天早上来了位信使。Lord Rossmore 死了。Sir Jonah 愣住了。毕竟前一晚他还见过对方，那人气色好得很。他追问细节，得知 Rossmore 的仆人听见主人的卧房里传出一种哀鸣。仆人一把推开门冲了进去，发现主人已痛苦不堪。Lord Rossmore 不久后死去，时间正是 2:30。
- **关键词**：The next morning a messenger arrived. Lord Rossmore was dead / Sir Jonah was stunned / he had seen him the night before and he looked in perfect health / He pressed for details / servant had heard a mournful sound emanating from Lord Ross-more's bedroom / He threw open the door and hurried in, discovering his master in great distress / Lord Rossmore died shortly later, at 2:30 a.m.
- **为什么这样写**：第二句只有短短一句：Lord Rossmore was dead.——**它是全篇最短也最重的一句**。而 After all, he had seen him the night before 一句把惊讶推回去：**因为“前一晚还好好活着”这一条，读者和信使才会一样愣住**。末句的 2:30 与前段的 ceased 时刻对上，一个写停声，一个写断气。
- **读者视角提示**：信使是谁、Rossmore 的死因是什么，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "Sir Barrington later documented this encounter, writing, “Lord Rossmore was dying at the same moment I heard his name pronounced.” What was the strange cry he had heard? His Irish staff advised him that it was the howl of the banshee. The eerie shriek haunted Sir Jonah all the rest of his life."

- **中文理解**：Sir Barrington 后来把这段遭遇记了下来，写道：“Lord Rossmore was dying at the same moment I heard his name pronounced.”他听到的那声叫究竟是什么？他手下的爱尔兰人告诉他，那是 banshee 的嚎哭。这声诡异的尖啸折磨了 Sir Jonah 一辈子。
- **关键词**：Sir Barrington later documented this encounter / Lord Rossmore was dying at the same moment I heard his name pronounced / What was the strange cry he had heard? / His Irish staff advised him that it was the howl of the banshee / The eerie shriek haunted Sir Jonah all the rest of his life
- **为什么这样写**：引述的那句话用的是现在时的 dying——**事已过去很久，写下的人还站在那同一刻上**。而 What was the strange cry he had heard? 是全篇仅此一处的自问，**作者让当事人替读者问了**；紧接着的那一句把判断交给了手下，**给出答案的人不是他**。
- **读者视角提示**：那位爱尔兰人是谁、banshee 是否还跟着这家人，**本篇没写**。

**附：原文 TERRIFYING TIDBIT**

According to Irish folklore, a banshee (woman of the fairies) is a female spirit whose wailing foretells death in a house.

## 一句话总结

Dublin 一场宴会散场后，Sir Jonah 在凌晨两点听见窗下的哀鸣、2:30 停住，随后一个声音连喊 Rossmore 三遍；次早信使来报 Lord Rossmore 死于同一时刻，那是 banshee 的嚎哭。
