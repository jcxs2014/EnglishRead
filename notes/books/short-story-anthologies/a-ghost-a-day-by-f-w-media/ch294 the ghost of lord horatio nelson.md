# OCTOBER 21, 1805 THE GHOST OF LORD HORATIO NELSON

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Pleasant Point Lighthouse——有时也叫 French Point Light——1904 年建在一处长满草的土丘上。这座木结构的灯塔高十一米，采用经典的胡椒瓶造型。它的首任守塔人是一位名叫 John Kent 的海员。近一百年来，Kent 家的成员一直照看这座灯塔。像许多别的灯塔一样，它也附着一个鬼。但与许多别的不同，这个鬼相当有名。据 Ivan Kent 讲的一个故事，他的曾祖父是 Lord Horatio Nelson 本人的领航员。不止如此，他还在 Lord Nelson 战死于 Trafalgar 时就在身边。那位 Admiral 的遗体安葬在 London, England，可看来 Admiral 的魂另有打算。Nelson 的鬼一直跟着 Kent，甚至跟着他迁居到 North America、在 Nova Scotia 定居下来。那位 Admiral 缠上了 Kent 在 Canada 的旧宅；那栋房子被拆之后，看来他又移居到了附近这座灯塔。这个鬼一直算温和，只有过两桩事是冲家里的宠物来的。两次、两只不同的猫，都感觉到了 Nelson 的存在。两只猫先后失踪，后来都在灯塔地板的一个活板门下面被找到。它们行为惊慌，像是被附了身。离开塔楼之后，它们很快就恢复了。可在遭遇 Nelson 的鬼之后，它们再也不想沾这座灯塔。来访者都感觉到过他的存在，而 Ivan 并不介意 Nelson 在附近转悠，他只希望对方别再吓他的猫。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 21, 1805（标题所载年份，即 Trafalgar 前后 Nelson 战死的年份）
- **事件年代**：1805 年 Nelson 战死于 Trafalgar；1904 年灯塔建成；此后 Kent 家守塔近一百年；两桩猫失踪事件日期未给
- **地点**：Nova Scotia, Canada（Pleasant Point Lighthouse 与灯塔地板的活板门）
- **异象类型**：Lord Horatio Nelson 的鬼（跟着 Kent 家迁到 North America，吓跑两只猫）
- **叙事手法**：Pleasant Point Lighthouse 建于 1904 年 → 也叫 French Point Light → 木结构高十一米 → 经典胡椒瓶造型 → 首任守塔人是海员 John Kent → 近一百年 Kent 家一直照看 → 许多灯塔都有鬼 → 但这个鬼相当有名 → 据 Ivan Kent 说他曾祖父是 Nelson 的领航员 → Nelson 战死 Trafalgar 时他也在场 → 遗体葬在 London → 可魂另有打算 → 鬼跟着 Kent 迁到 North America → 在 Nova Scotia 定居 → 缠上 Kent 在 Canada 的旧宅 → 房子被拆后移到附近灯塔 → 这个鬼一直算温和 → 只对家里的宠物出过两桩事 → 两次、两只不同的猫 → 都感觉到 Nelson 的存在 → 猫先后失踪 → 后来在地板的活板门下被找到 → 行为惊慌像被附身 → 离开塔楼后很快恢复 → 但从此不想沾这座灯塔 → 来访者都感觉到他 → Ivan 不介意他在附近 → 只希望别再吓他的猫

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Pleasant Point Lighthouse, or French Point Light, as it is sometimes called, was built in 1904 on a grassy knoll. The wooden eleven-meter-tall structure was built in the classic peppershaker design. Its first keeper was a seafaring man named John Kent. For almost a hundred years the Kent family members have tended to the lighthouse. Like so many other lighthouses, it has a ghost attached to it. But unlike so many others, this ghost is rather famous. According to a story told by Ivan Kent, his great grandfather was the navigator for none other than Lord Horatio Nelson. Not only that, he was with Lord Nelson when he died at Trafalgar. The admiral's body was laid to rest in London, England, but it seems the admiral's spirit had other plans."

- **中文理解**：Pleasant Point Lighthouse——有时也叫 French Point Light——1904 年建在一处长满草的土丘上。这座木结构的灯塔高十一米，采用经典的胡椒瓶造型。它的首任守塔人是一位名叫 John Kent 的海员。近一百年来，Kent 家的成员一直照看这座灯塔。像许多别的灯塔一样，它也附着一个鬼。但与许多别的不同，这个鬼相当有名。据 Ivan Kent 讲的一个故事，他的曾祖父是 Lord Horatio Nelson 本人的领航员。不止如此，他还在 Lord Nelson 战死于 Trafalgar 时就在身边。那位 Admiral 的遗体安葬在 London, England，可看来 Admiral 的魂另有打算。
- **关键词**：Pleasant Point Lighthouse, or French Point Light / as it is sometimes called / was built in 1904 on a grassy knoll / The wooden eleven-meter-tall structure / was built in the classic peppershaker design / Its first keeper was a seafaring man named John Kent / For almost a hundred years the Kent family members have tended to the lighthouse / Like so many other lighthouses / it has a ghost attached to it / But unlike so many others, this ghost is rather famous / According to a story told by Ivan Kent / his great grandfather was the navigator for / Lord Horatio Nelson / he was with Lord Nelson when he died at Trafalgar / The admiral's body was laid to rest in London, England / but it seems the admiral's spirit had other plans
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 Like so many other lighthouses 与 But unlike so many others 一正一反把话头拧一下，**而 rather famous 之后紧跟的却是 his great grandfather was the navigator for ——这个名人不是鬼的名字，是活人的来路**。had other plans 一句把安葬与魂魄分成两处，各走各的。
- **读者视角提示**：曾祖父在 Trafalgar 陪 Nelson 这件事，**本篇标明是 Ivan Kent 讲的故事**。

> **原句 2:** "The ghost of Nelson remained with Kent, even following him to North America when he settled in Nova Scotia. The Admiral haunted Kent's original home in Canada, and when that was destroyed it seems he immigrated to the nearby lighthouse. The ghost has been pretty benign, except for a couple of incidents with the family pet. On two separate occasions, with two different cats, the presence of Nelson has been felt. The cats each disappeared, only to be later found beneath a trap door in the floor of the lighthouse. They acted frantic, as if they were possessed. Away from the tower, they soon recovered. But after their encounter with the ghost of Nelson, they wanted no part of the lighthouse. Visitors have all felt his presence, and Ivan doesn't mind Nelson hanging around, he just wishes he'd leave his cats alone."

- **中文理解**：Nelson 的鬼一直跟着 Kent，甚至跟着他迁居到 North America、在 Nova Scotia 定居下来。那位 Admiral 缠上了 Kent 在 Canada 的旧宅；那栋房子被拆之后，看来他又移居到了附近这座灯塔。这个鬼一直算温和，只有过两桩事是冲家里的宠物来的。两次、两只不同的猫，都感觉到了 Nelson 的存在。两只猫先后失踪，后来都在灯塔地板的一个活板门下面被找到。它们行为惊慌，像是被附了身。离开塔楼之后，它们很快就恢复了。可在遭遇 Nelson 的鬼之后，它们再也不想沾这座灯塔。来访者都感觉到过他的存在，而 Ivan 并不介意 Nelson 在附近转悠，他只希望对方别再吓他的猫。
- **关键词**：The ghost of Nelson remained with Kent / even following him to North America / when he settled in Nova Scotia / The Admiral haunted Kent's original home in Canada / when that was destroyed it seems he immigrated to the nearby lighthouse / The ghost has been pretty benign / except for a couple of incidents with the family pet / On two separate occasions, with two different cats / the presence of Nelson has been felt / The cats each disappeared / only to be later found beneath a trap door in the floor of the lighthouse / They acted frantic, as if they were possessed / Away from the tower, they soon recovered / they wanted no part of the lighthouse / Visitors have all felt his presence / he just wishes he'd leave his cats alone
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 when that was destroyed it seems he immigrated 把鬼的搬家写得像一次迁居，**而 The cats each disappeared 与 only to be later found 前后两句一藏一现，把失踪与寻回之间的距离留白**。they acted frantic, as if they were possessed 里 as if 一收，猫的反应就只是反应。末句用 wishes he'd leave his cats alone 把整段的怨气收到一只猫上。
- **读者视角提示**：那两只猫的名字与 Ivan 家与 Nelson 家的关系，**本篇都没说**。
