# OCTOBER 19, 1923 LENIN'S GHOST

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：自 Lenin 1924 年去世以来，他的鬼在 Moscow 各地时断时续地被人看见。守卫着他旧公寓的士兵听见过诡异的声音、脚步声，还有家具在那间空屋里被搬动。事实上，Boris Yeltsin 旗下的幕僚长 Sergei Filatov 讲述过自己与 Lenin 鬼魂打交道的经历。据 Sergei 说，他有一晚在 Lenin 旧公寓楼下的一间办公室加班，听见楼上地板吱嘎作响。起初他没在意，可随后意识到那声音像是有人在来回踱步。他叫来保安，问公寓里怎么回事——那地方如今是座博物馆。他被告知那里没有人。说话间脚步声仍在继续。他不信保安，就让他们跟他一起去公寓。他们打开门，把博物馆和整栋楼彻底搜了一遍。他们没找到任何人。几年后，Moscow State University 的一名学生与 Lenin 撞了个正着。那学生走过 Moscow 的 Oktyabrskaya Square 鹅卵石路面时，他的路线与那位著名的布尔什维克领袖交叉。起初他不敢相信自己的眼睛。等他站在那儿看得发呆时，连 Lenin 面部那标志性胡须都看得清清楚楚。当那个幻影发现自己被看见时，他一步踏进自己的雕像里消失了。也许最离奇的目击发生在 1923 年 10 月 19 日。那一夜有人看见他没带警卫在城市里走动。这让许多安全人员大为惊慌，他们把目击报告给了上级。直到今天没人能解释那一夜的几次目击——因为那时的 Lenin 人在 Gorki，病着，但活着。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 19, 1923（标题所载日期，即最离奇目击那一夜）
- **事件年代**：1923 年 10 月 19 日夜 Lenin 身影在 Moscow 城中被人看见；1924 年 Lenin 去世；此后 Sergei Filatov 的经历与 Moscow State University 学生撞见均未标日期
- **地点**：Moscow, Russia（Lenin 旧公寓楼下办公室、Oktyabrskaya Square 与雕像）
- **异象类型**：Lenin 的鬼（空屋里的脚步声与家具搬动、广场上与学生擦肩、一步踏进雕像消失）
- **叙事手法**：Lenin 1924 年去世 → 他的鬼在 Moscow 时断时续被看见 → 守卫旧公寓的士兵听见诡异声音、脚步声与家具搬动 → Sergei Filatov 讲过自己的经历 → 某晚在旧公寓楼下办公室加班 → 听见楼上地板吱嘎 → 起初不在意 → 像是有人在来回踱步 → 叫保安询问 → 得知那里没人 → 说话间脚步声仍在 → 不信 → 让保安同去 → 开门彻底搜遍博物馆与整栋楼 → 没找到人 → 几年后 Moscow State University 学生与 Lenin 撞上 → 走在 Oktyabrskaya Square 上路线交叉 → 起初不敢相信 → 看得发呆，连胡须都看清 → 幻影发现自己被看见 → 一步踏进雕像消失 → 1923 年 10 月 19 日夜最离奇 → 有人看见他没带警卫在城中走动 → 许多安全人员惊慌并上报 → 至今无人能解释 → 因为那时的 Lenin 人在 Gorki，病着但活着

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Since Lenin's death in 1924, his ghost has been sporadically spotted throughout Moscow. Soldiers guarding his former apartment have heard eerie noises, footsteps, and furniture being moved from the empty room. In fact, Sergei Filatov, chief of staff under Boris Yeltsin, retells his own experience with Lenin's ghost. According to Sergei, he was working late one night in an office under Lenin's old apartment when he heard creaking in the floorboards above. At first he ignored it, but then realized it sounded as if someone was pacing back and forth. Calling security, he asked what was going on in the apartment, which is now a museum. He was informed that there was no one there. The footsteps continued even as he spoke. Disbelieving the guards, he asked them to meet him at the apartment. They unlocked the door and thoroughly searched the museum and building. They found no one."

- **中文理解**：自 Lenin 1924 年去世以来，他的鬼在 Moscow 各地时断时续地被人看见。守卫着他旧公寓的士兵听见过诡异的声音、脚步声，还有家具在那间空屋里被搬动。事实上，Boris Yeltsin 旗下的幕僚长 Sergei Filatov 讲述过自己与 Lenin 鬼魂打交道的经历。据 Sergei 说，他有一晚在 Lenin 旧公寓楼下的一间办公室加班，听见楼上地板吱嘎作响。起初他没在意，可随后意识到那声音像是有人在来回踱步。他叫来保安，问公寓里怎么回事——那地方如今是座博物馆。他被告知那里没有人。说话间脚步声仍在继续。他不信保安，就让他们跟他一起去公寓。他们打开门，把博物馆和整栋楼彻底搜了一遍。他们没找到任何人。
- **关键词**：Since Lenin's death in 1924, his ghost has been sporadically spotted throughout Moscow / Soldiers guarding his former apartment / have heard eerie noises, footsteps / and furniture being moved from the empty room / Sergei Filatov, chief of staff under Boris Yeltsin / retells his own experience with Lenin's ghost / he was working late one night in an office under Lenin's old apartment / when he heard creaking in the floorboards above / At first he ignored it / but then realized it sounded as if someone was pacing back and forth / Calling security, he asked what was going on in the apartment / which is now a museum / He was informed that there was no one there / The footsteps continued even as he spoke / They unlocked the door and thoroughly searched the museum and building / They found no one
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 The footsteps continued even as he spoke 一句把两件事并进同一秒——他正在问话，脚还在走，**而 They found no one 用三个词把整场搜查的结论一次交完**。creaking in the floorboards 与 pacing back and forth 两句把声音先写成品，再写成人。
- **读者视角提示**：保安的姓名与那晚的具体日期，**本篇都没给**。

> **原句 2:** "A few years later, a student from Moscow State University had a run in with Lenin. While walking across the cobblestones of Oktyabrskaya Square in Moscow, his path crossed that of the famous Bolshevik leader. At first he couldn't believe his eyes. As he stood there awestruck, he could clearly see Lenin's facial features right down to his signature beard. When the phantom realized that he had been spotted, he stepped into his monument and disappeared."

- **中文理解**：几年后，Moscow State University 的一名学生与 Lenin 撞了个正着。那学生走过 Moscow 的 Oktyabrskaya Square 鹅卵石路面时，他的路线与那位著名的布尔什维克领袖交叉。起初他不敢相信自己的眼睛。等他站在那儿看得发呆时，连 Lenin 面部那标志性胡须都看得清清楚楚。当那个幻影发现自己被看见时，他一步踏进自己的雕像里消失了。
- **关键词**：a student from Moscow State University had a run in with Lenin / While walking across the cobblestones of Oktyabrskaya Square / his path crossed that of / the famous Bolshevik leader / At first he couldn't believe his eyes / As he stood there awestruck / he could clearly see Lenin's facial features / right down to his signature beard / When the phantom realized that he had been spotted / he stepped into his monument / and disappeared
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 his path crossed that of 一个交叉把相遇写成路线问题，**而 stepped into his monument and disappeared 把消失写成走进某处，不是消失**。signature beard 一处给那句「看清到胡须」一个落点。
- **读者视角提示**：那学生后来怎样，**本篇没有再提**。

> **原句 3:** "Perhaps the strangest sighting of Lenin's spirit happened on October 19, 1923. That night he was seen walking in and about the city without his guards. This caused great alarm to many security agents, who reported the sightings to their superiors. To this day, no one can explain the sightings that night, since Lenin was in Gorki, ill but alive."

- **中文理解**：也许最离奇的目击发生在 1923 年 10 月 19 日。那一夜有人看见他没带警卫在城市里走动。这让许多安全人员大为惊慌，他们把目击报告给了上级。直到今天没人能解释那一夜的几次目击——因为那时的 Lenin 人在 Gorki，病着，但活着。
- **关键词**：Perhaps the strangest sighting of Lenin's spirit happened on October 19, 1923 / That night he was seen walking in and about the city / without his guards / This caused great alarm to many security agents / who reported the sightings to their superiors / To this day, no one can explain the sightings that night / since Lenin was in Gorki, ill but alive
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 walking in and about the city without his guards 一句把反常写成少了一样东西，**而 since Lenin was in Gorki, ill but alive 三个短词作结，把鬼的证据与活人的实证并排放**。ill but alive 里的 but 一转，谜底反而更硬。
- **读者视角提示**：那一夜 Lenin 身边有没有人作证，**本篇没说**。
