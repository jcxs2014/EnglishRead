# NOVEMBER 13, 1974 AMITYVILLE HOUSE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Amityville 那栋房子的闹鬼传闻：是事实还是虚构，由你决定。1974 年 11 月 13 日凌晨一点左右，天塌了下来。Butch DeFeo 冷血杀害了自己全家。六名死者里有他的父亲、母亲、两个哥哥和两个妹妹。其中一个叫 Dawn，他后来把她打成同谋。可究竟是什么能逼一个儿子去杀害自己的父母与手足？如果照 Butch 说法，这个计划是在他父亲多年虐待之后炮制出来的。事实上，Butch 从不承认杀害自己的手足，他说是 Dawn 先找上他，提出冷血杀害父母这个狠毒的要求。Butch 拒绝了——直到十三号那个夜晚，在毒品与酒精的作用下，他终于对 Dawn 那套令人作呕的要求让步了。因为 Butch 是 DeFeo 一家里活下来的那一个，那个凶杀之夜究竟发生了什么很难说清。不过人们发现当时用过多支枪，而且 Dawn 的睡袍上确实验出了火药，所以 Butch 的说法也许有一部分是真的。那么那场所谓的闹鬼又怎么说？闹鬼的传说始于谋杀案后十三个月，Lutz 一家搬了进来。一位叫 Father Mancusco 的神父在 1975 年 12 月 18 日为这栋房子祝福。祝圣过程中，据说他听见屋里某个房间传来「Get out!」的神父自己把这段经历按下，劝 Lutz 一家别把那个房间当卧室。看来他们听了，那个房间被改成了一间缝纫室，后来成了各种无法解释现象的焦点。据 Lutz 一家说，接下来几周超自然事件不断升级：住在屋里的人性情大变，孩子们变得难以管教，Lutz 一家不得不用皮带狠狠抽打他们。墙上渗出绿色黏液，明明是冬天，缝纫室却爬满了大群黑苍蝇。有好几次 George 醒来发现妻子变成一个九十岁的老妪。另一回，他亲眼看见她的身体从床上浮了起来。在这些超自然的折磨之下，Lutz 一家越来越焦躁。终于，一枚倒挂的十字架被发现，George 第二次叫来了神父。可神父不肯再来，一家人只好自己为房子祝福。不久之后，Lutz 一家只带着极少的家当离开，再没回来。他们在那栋据称闹鬼的 Amityville 房子里只住了二十八天。可是 Lutz 一家的说法是真的，还是为了让好莱坞炒作多本书与多部电影而编出的把戏？那就由你决定了。
- **篇目日期**：NOVEMBER 13, 1974（标题所载日期，即谋杀之夜）
- **事件年代**：1974 年 11 月 13 日凌晨约 1:00 发生谋杀；1975 年 12 月 18 日神父祝福；十三个月后 Lutz 一家搬入；二十八天后离开
- **地点**：Amityville, New York（DeFeo 家旧宅与 Lutz 家住过的同一栋房子）
- **异象类型**：被传出的一系列超自然现象（渗水的墙、黑苍蝇、妻子变形、倒挂十字架）
- **叙事手法**：是事实还是 fiction 由读者决定 → 1974 年 11 月 13 日凌晨约一点天塌了 → Butch DeFeo 冷血杀害全家 → 六名死者含父母与两兄两妹 → 其中一个叫 Dawn → 他后来把 Dawn 说成同谋 → 儿子为何要杀父母手足 → Butch 说计划源于父亲的多年虐待 → 他称是 Dawn 先提出来 → 他一直拒绝 → 十三号那夜在毒品酒精下让步 → Butch 是活下来的那一个 → 当晚真相难说 → 但验出用过多支枪 → Dawn 睡袍上有火药 → 闹鬼之说从何而起 → 十三个月后 Lutz 一家搬入 → 1975 年 12 月 18 日神父祝福 → 听见屋里一声 Get out → 劝他们别把那间房当卧室 → 改成缝纫室 → 后来成了无法解释现象的焦点 → 几周内事件升级 → 屋里人性情大变 → 孩子难管教被皮带抽打 → 墙上渗绿黏液 → 冬天缝纫室爬满黑苍蝇 → 有几次妻子变成九十岁老妪 → 另一回她身体从床上浮起 → 一家越来越焦躁 → 发现倒挂十字架 → 第二次叫神父被拒 → 一家自己祝福房子 → 带着极的家当离开 → 只住了二十八天 → 传闻真假由读者定

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The haunting of the Amityville house: fact or fiction? You decide. At approximately 1:00 A.M. on the morning of November 13, 1974, all hell broke loose. Butch DeFeo murdered his whole family in cold blood. Among the six to die were his father, mother, two brothers, and two sisters. One of them was named Dawn, whom he later branded an accomplice. But what could possibly drive a son to murder his parents and siblings? If one is to believe Butch, the plan was concocted after years of abuse at his father's hands. In fact, Butch, who never admitted to killing his siblings, stated that it was Dawn who first approached him with the heinous request of murdering their parents in cold blood. Butch refused, until the night of the thirteenth that is, when under the influence of drugs and alcohol, he finally caved in to Dawn's abhorrent requests. Since Butch was the only member of the DeFeo family to survive, it's difficult to say exactly what took place during that murderous night. However, it has been discovered that more than one gun was used, and gunpowder was in fact found on Dawn's nightgown, so perhaps there is some truth to Butch's tales. But what of the supposed haunting?"

- **为什么这样写**：作者一开口就把 fact or fiction 摆出来交给读者，**而 it was Dawn who first approached him 用一句转述把凶手与主谋的位置整个换掉**。枪与火药两句是书里仅有的实证，作者用 so perhaps 有保留地收，而末尾 But what of the supposed haunting? 把话头从案子转到鬼。
- **读者视角提示**：Butch 的说法与实证之间有出入，**本篇没有断定哪一方为真**。

> **原句 2:** "Tales of the haunting began thirteen months after the murders, when the Lutz family moved in. A priest named Father Mancusco blessed the home on December 18, 1975. During his blessing of the home, he allegedly heard, “Get out!” in one room in particular. Keeping the strange experience to himself, he recommended the Lutzes not use the room for a bedroom. Apparently they listened, and the room was converted into a sewing room, a room that later become the focus of unexplainable phenomena."

- **中文理解**：闹鬼的传说始于谋杀案后十三个月，Lutz 一家搬了进来。一位叫 Father Mancusco 的神父在 1975 年 12 月 18 日为这栋房子祝福。祝圣过程中，据说他听见屋里某个房间传来「Get out!」的声音。神父自己把这段经历按下，劝 Lutz 一家别把那个房间当卧室。看来他们听了，那个房间被改成了一间缝纫室，后来成了各种无法解释现象的焦点。
- **关键词**：Tales of the haunting began thirteen months after the murders / when the Lutz family moved in / A priest named Father Mancusco blessed the home on December 18, 1975 / During his blessing of the home, he allegedly heard / Get out! in one room in particular / Keeping the strange experience to himself / he recommended the Lutzes not use the room for a bedroom / Apparently they listened / and the room was converted into a sewing room / the focus of unexplainable phenomena
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 allegedly 把那句喊话降级成传说，**而 Keeping the strange experience to himself 与 he recommended 不 use the room 一轻一重：神父咽下自己的经历，转手去约束别人的用法**。Apparently they listened 用 Apparently 把听话这件事也留了一层不确定。
- **读者视角提示**：喊话的声源，**本篇没有指认是谁在喊**。

> **原句 3:** "As reported by the Lutzes, supernatural events began to escalate in the next few weeks. The personalities of those living in the home changed. The children became so unruly that the Lutzes were forced to punish them severely, beating them with straps. The walls dripped green slime, and the sewing room became infested with large black flies, despite it being winter. On numerous occasions, George awoke to find his wife had turned into a ninety-year-old hag. Another time, he witnessed her body levitate off of their bed. Through the many supernatural tortures, the Lutzes became increasingly agitated. Finally, when a crucifix was found hanging upside down, George called in the priest once again. But when he refused to return, the family blessed the home themselves. Not long after, the Lutzes, taking minimal belongings with them, left the home, never to return. They lived in the reportedly haunted Amityville house for only twentyeight days."

- **中文理解**：据 Lutz 一家说，接下来几周里超自然事件不断升级：住在屋里的人性情大变，孩子们变得难以管教，Lutz 一家不得不用皮带狠狠抽打他们。墙上渗出绿色黏液，明明是冬天，缝纫室却爬满了大群黑苍蝇。有好几次 George 醒来发现妻子已经变成一个九十岁的老妪。另一回，他亲眼看见她的身体从床上浮了起来。在这些超自然的折磨之下，Lutz 一家越来越焦躁。终于，一枚倒挂的十字架被发现，George 第二次叫来了神父。可神父不肯再来，一家人只好自己为房子祝福。不久之后，Lutz 一家只带着极少的家当离开，再没回来。他们在那栋据称闹鬼的 Amityville 房子里只住了二十八天。
- **关键词**：As reported by the Lutzes / supernatural events began to escalate / The personalities of those living in the home changed / The children became so unruly / the Lutzes were forced to punish them severely / beating them with straps / The walls dripped green slime / the sewing room became infested with large black flies / despite it being winter / George awoke to find his wife had turned into a ninety-year-old hag / he witnessed her body levitate off of their bed / Through the many supernatural tortures / the Lutzes became increasingly agitated / when a crucifix was found hanging upside down / George called in the priest once again / when he refused to return, the family blessed the home themselves / taking minimal belongings with them, left the home, never to return / only twentyeight days
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 despite it being winter 给黑苍蝇这一条钉上反常，**而 George awoke to find 与 he witnessed her body levitate 两处都只写他看见什么、不写他怎么反应**。最后那枚倒挂的十字架是全段里被人动过的东西，也是叫来神父的理由。
- **读者视角提示**：九十岁的老妪与浮起的身体都是 George 一人所说，**本篇没有第二方证实**。
