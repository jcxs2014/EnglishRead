# OCTOBER 25, 1829 EASTERN STATE PENITENTIARY

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Eastern State Penitentiary 于 1829 年 10 月 25 日启用，设计来关押那些难以改造的囚犯，把他们强行独处。简陋的牢房里除了一张铺和一本《圣经》什么也没有。进到牢房里的光就那一束，来自一扇小玻璃窗，人们管它叫「the Eye of God」。每名囚犯每天有一小时放风时间，被带出牢房时头上会罩一只黑罩。在这种条件下，即便是神志正常的囚犯也会发疯。渴望与人接触，许多人会敲打管道、隔着墙低声说话。被抓住的话，他们会受到严厉惩罚。这里施行过好几种骇人的酷刑，而其中最致命的一种是留给那些一再违反禁言令的人：一副铁夹子被固定在舌头上。囚犯双手反绑在背后，随后一串锁链被接到那件装置上。囚犯的任何动作都会导致舌头被撕裂，进而大量失血直至死亡。由于过度拥挤与条件严酷，这座监狱被迫在 1971 年关门。可这监狱的墙被悲惨与酷刑染得太深，以至今天有数不清的超自然活动报告，Eastern State Penitentiary 也成了这个国家最闹鬼的地点之一。一则超自然报告来自一位在第四牢区做修复工作的锁匠。他说，就在他拆下那把一百四十年历史的锁时，一股强大到他动弹不得的能量迎面扑来。那股能量仿佛是他放出了恶魔本人，擦着他身边冲出去、冲出了牢房。在他面前，是受苦灵魂那些可怖的鬼脸，翻涌着、变形着各种模样。游客们至今报告说，隔着监狱的墙能听见低语与哭泣。看来这些灵魂终于找到了自己的声音。显然，它们是急着要说话。
- **篇目日期**：OCTOBER 25, 1829（标题所载日期，即监狱启用之日）
- **事件年代**：1829 年 10 月 25 日启用；1971 年因拥挤与条件恶劣关门；此后不断有超自然活动报告；锁匠事件未标日期
- **地点**：Philadelphia, Pennsylvania（Eastern State Penitentiary 的牢房、走廊与第四牢区）
- **异象类型**：受刑囚魂（拆锁时扑面而来的能量、翻涌变形的鬼脸、墙那边的低语与哭泣）
- **叙事手法**：1829 年 10 月 25 日启用 → 用来关难以改造的囚犯 → 强行独处 → 牢房只有铺与圣经 → 进到牢房里的光就那一束「the Eye of God」 → 每天一小时放风 → 出房时罩黑罩 → 正常人在此也会发疯 → 渴望与人接触就敲管道、隔墙低语 → 被抓则严惩 → 好几种骇人酷刑 → 最致命的是给一再违反禁言令的人 → 铁夹固定在舌头上 → 双手反绑 → 锁链接到装置上 → 任何动作都撕裂舌头 → 大量失血至死 → 1971 年因拥挤与恶劣条件关门 → 墙被悲惨与酷刑染深 → 今天有无数超自然报告 → 成了全国最闹鬼的地点之一 → 一则报告来自做修复的锁匠 → 拆下 140 年的锁时被能量扑住 → 动弹不得 → 仿佛放出恶魔本人 → 能量冲过他冲出牢房 → 面前是受苦灵魂的鬼脸翻涌变形 → 游客仍听见墙那边低语与哭泣 → 灵魂终于找到声音 → 急着要说话

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Eastern State Penitentiary opened on October 25, 1829, and was designed for incorrigible prisoners to be kept in forced solitude. The austere cells contained nothing more than a bunk and a Bible. The only light that entered the cells came from a small glass window referred to as “the Eye of God.” Each prisoner was given one hour a day of exercise, and a black hood was placed over their heads as they were led from their cells. Under these conditions, even a sane prisoner could go mad. Desperate for human interaction, many men would tap on pipes and whisper through the walls. If caught, they were severely punished."

- **中文理解**：Eastern State Penitentiary 于 1829 年 10 月 25 日启用，设计来关押那些难以改造的囚犯，把他们强行独处。简陋的牢房里除了一张铺和一本《圣经》什么也没有。进到牢房里的光就那一束，来自一扇小玻璃窗，人们管它叫「the Eye of God」。每名囚犯每天有一小时放风时间，被带出牢房时头上会罩一只黑罩。在这种条件下，即便是神志正常的囚犯也会发疯。渴望与人接触，许多人会敲打管道、隔着墙低声说话。被抓住的话，他们会受到严厉惩罚。
- **关键词**：Eastern State Penitentiary opened on October 25, 1829 / was designed for incorrigible prisoners / to be kept in forced solitude / The austere cells contained nothing more than / a bunk and a Bible / The only light that entered the cells / came from a small glass window referred to as / the Eye of God / Each prisoner was given one hour a day of exercise / a black hood was placed over their heads / as they were led from their cells / Under these conditions, even a sane prisoner could go mad / Desperate for human interaction / many men would tap on pipes / and whisper through the walls / If caught, they were severely punished
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 The only light that entered the cells 一句把窗子写成这地方与外界仅存的通路，**而 whisper through the walls 之后紧跟 If caught, they were severely punished，连私语都要上刑**。even a sane prisoner could go mad 用 even 把发疯的门槛压到常人以下。
- **读者视角提示**：这里关过多少人、待了多久，**本篇在这一段没给**。

> **原句 2:** "There were several horrific tortures that had been carried out, but the most deadly of the tortures was saved for the repeat offender who continually broke the enforced vow of silence. An iron clamp was secured to the tongue. With the prisoner's hands behind his back, a set of chains was then attached to the device. Any movement by the prisoner resulted in the tearing of his tongue leading to an abundance of blood loss and finally death. Due to overcrowding and harsh conditions, the penitentiary was forced to close its doors in 1971. However, the penitentiary's walls were so stained by misery and torture that today countless reports of paranormal activity have been made, and Eastern State Penitentiary has become one of the most haunted locations in the country."

- **中文理解**：这里施行过好几种骇人的酷刑，而其中最致命的一种是留给那些一再违反禁言令的人：一副铁夹子被固定在舌头上。囚犯双手反绑在背后，随后一串锁链被接到那件装置上。囚犯的任何动作都会导致舌头被撕裂，进而大量失血直至死亡。由于过度拥挤与条件严酷，这座监狱被迫在 1971 年关门。可这监狱的墙被悲惨与酷刑染得太深，以至今天有数不清的超自然活动报告，Eastern State Penitentiary 也成了这个国家最闹鬼的地点之一。
- **关键词**：There were several horrific tortures that had been carried out / the most deadly of the tortures was saved for the repeat offender / who continually broke the enforced vow of silence / An iron clamp was secured to the tongue / With the prisoner's hands behind his back / a set of chains was then attached to the device / Any movement by the prisoner resulted in the tearing of his tongue / leading to an abundance of blood loss and finally death / Due to overcrowding and harsh conditions / the penitentiary was forced to close its doors in 1971 / the penitentiary's walls were so stained by misery and torture / countless reports of paranormal activity have been made / has become one of the most haunted locations in the country
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 Any movement by the prisoner resulted in 把死亡写成一条机械因果，动作在先、血在后果然，**而 so stained by misery and torture 用一个 so 把墙的颜色说成积累的结果**。close its doors 一句里没有谁下令，关闭像是被条件逼出来的。
- **读者视角提示**：1971 年关门之后这里作过什么用，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "However, the penitentiary's walls were so stained by misery and torture that today countless reports of paranormal activity have been made, and Eastern State Penitentiary has become one of the most haunted locations in the country. One paranormal report came from a locksmith doing restoration work on cellblock four. He states that just as he removed the 140-year-old lock, he was assailed by a blast of energy so powerful that he couldn't move. As if he'd released the Devil himself, the surge of energy tore past him and out of the cell. There before him were the hideous ghostly faces of tormented souls swirling and morphing into shapes. Tourists still report the sound of whispering and weeping emanating through the prisons walls. It seems these souls have finally found their voices. Evidently, they were dying to speak."

- **中文理解**：一则超自然报告来自一位在第四牢区做修复工作的锁匠。他说，就在他拆下那把一百四十年历史的锁时，一股强大到他动弹不得的能量迎面扑来。那股能量仿佛是他放出了恶魔本人，擦着他身边冲出去、冲出了牢房。在他面前，是受苦灵魂那些可怖的鬼脸，翻涌着、变形着各种模样。游客们至今报告说，隔着监狱的墙能听见低语与哭泣。看来这些灵魂终于找到了自己的声音。显然，它们是急着要说话。
- **关键词**：One paranormal report came from a locksmith / doing restoration work on cellblock four / just as he removed the 140-year-old lock / he was assailed by a blast of energy so powerful that he couldn't move / As if he'd released the Devil himself / the surge of energy tore past him and out of the cell / There before him were / the hideous ghostly faces of tormented souls / swirling and morphing into shapes / Tourists still report the sound of whispering and weeping / emanating through the prisons walls / It seems these souls have finally found their voices / Evidently, they were dying to speak
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 just as 把能量扑上来与拆锁那一刻对齐，**而 As if he'd released the Devil himself 一句先认了恶魔，再写那股能量冲出去的路线**。末句 dying to speak 让「急着要说话」与前面那些死法并排收尾。
- **读者视角提示**：那位锁匠的姓名与那把锁的下落，**本篇都没写**。

## 一句话总结

1829 年启用、1971 年关掉的 Eastern State Penitentiary 用舌夹和锁链对付囚犯，而锁匠拆锁时那股能量冲出牢房，墙那边至今还有低语与哭泣。
