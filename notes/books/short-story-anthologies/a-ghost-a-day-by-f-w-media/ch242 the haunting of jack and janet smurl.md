# AUGUST 30, 1986 THE HAUNTING OF JACK AND JANET SMURL

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1973 年，Jack Smurl、妻子 Janet 与两个女儿 Dawn 和 Hanna 决定搬进 West Pittston 的一套复式住宅，与 Jack 的父母同住。起初这个普通人家过得兴旺。翻新完新居之后，他们终于安顿下来；不久 Janet 生下一对双胞胎女儿，人生本无更幸福的了。然而 1974 年 1 月起，情形忽然变了：不知从何处，这家人开始经历一连串怪事。地毯上出现擦不掉的污渍，新装的浴室洁具上现出深深的划痕；水管虽焊过好几回，却一直在漏。然后有一天，这些恼人的事件急转直下——他们的电视自燃了。不久 Jack 遭到某种看不见的东西袭击。所有这些超自然活动让 Smurl 一家确信，他们正被一个邪恶的灵体纠缠。Smurl 家闹鬼的消息很快传开，他们成了媒体爆炸的对象。有些邻居亲眼见过那些现象后对 Smurl 家表示同情，另一些则指责他们伪造真相，好在一本书的合约上赚钱。有一回 Janet 在地下室听见有人叫她的名字，她答应了，却没有人。那东西像是被请进了门，胆子越来越大：黑影开始在屋里游走；孩子们被扔下楼梯；家里的狗一次又一次被举起、摔向墙壁；而刮擦声在昼夜每个时辰都能听见。1986 年，实在忍受不了折磨的 Smurl 一家请来了驱魔师 Ed 与 Lorraine Warren 夫妇。沃伦夫妇调查后认为这栋房子里有四个灵体，其中一个是纯粹的恶。有一段时间，只有圣水与祈祷能让那个灵体安静下来；但很快沃伦夫妇就意识到，那远远不够。显然那个邪恶的灵体被 Smurl 一家激怒了，开始动手：家里有人被咬，手臂被划伤。沃伦夫妇请来 McKenna 神父，先后做了三次驱魔。折磨似乎结束了，却在 1986 年 12 月重新开始。最终在 1988 年，教会举行了第四次、也是最后一次驱魔。Smurl 一家终于从缠了他们十三年多的黑暗中被解救出来。
- **篇目日期**：AUGUST 30, 1986（标题所载日期；正文另给 1973 年迁入、1974 年 1 月出事、1986 年请沃伦夫妇、1988 年第四次驱魔）
- **事件年代**：1973 年迁入 → 1974 年 1 月起怪事 → 1986 年请沃伦夫妇、三次驱魔 → 1986 年 12 月复发 → 1988 年第四次驱魔；标题日期 1986 年
- **地点**：West Pittston, Pennsylvania（Smurl 家的复式住宅）
- **异象类型**：四个灵体（其中一个是纯粹的恶：黑影、扔孩子、摔狗、昼夜刮擦）
- **叙事手法**：1973 年 Jack 与 Janet Smurl 携两女搬进 West Pittston 复式住宅 → 与 Jack 父母同住 → 普通人家起初兴旺 → 翻新后安顿 → Janet 生下双胞胎女儿 → 1974 年 1 月起情形忽然变了 → 不知从何处开始经历一连串怪事 → 地毯擦不掉的污渍 → 浴室洁具上深深的划痕 → 水管焊过好几回仍一直漏 → 有一天电视自燃 → 不久 Jack 遭到看不见的东西袭击 → Smurl 家确信被邪恶灵体纠缠 → 消息传开、成为媒体爆炸对象 → 有些邻居目击后同情 → 另一些指责他们伪造真相、想靠出书赚钱 → 有一回 Janet 在地下室被叫名字、答应却无人 → 那东西像被请进了门、胆子越来越大 → 黑影在屋里游走 → 孩子们被扔下楼梯 → 狗一次又一次被举起摔向墙壁 → 昼夜每个时辰都有刮擦声 → 1986 年请来驱魔师 Ed 与 Lorraine Warren → 他们认为屋里有四个灵体、其中一个是纯粹的恶 → 一段时间只有圣水与祈祷能让它安静 → 沃伦夫妇意识到那远远不够 → 邪恶灵体被激怒、开始动手 → 有人被咬、手臂被划伤 → 请来 McKenna 神父做三次驱魔 → 折磨似乎结束却在 1986 年 12 月重来 → 1988 年教会举行第四次也是最后一次驱魔 → Smurl 一家从缠了十三年多的黑暗中获救

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "In 1973, Jack Smurl; his wife, Janet; and their two daughters, Dawn and Hanna, decided to move into a duplex in West Pittston with Jack's parents. At first their ordinary household flourished. After renovating their new home, they finally settled in. Soon after, Janet gave birth to twin girls. Life couldn't be happier. However, things soon changed in January 1974, when, out of nowhere, the family began to experience a series of strange phenomena. Irremovable stains appeared on the carpets. New bathroom fixtures developed deep scratches. Although soldered several times, water pipes continuously leaked. And then one day the irritating events took a turn for the worse when their television spontaneously combusted. Soon after, Jack was attacked by something unseen. All this paranormal activity convinced the Smurl family that they were being plagued by a demonic presence."

- **中文理解**：1973 年，Jack Smurl、妻子 Janet 与两个女儿 Dawn 和 Hanna 决定搬进 West Pittston 的一套复式住宅，与 Jack 的父母同住。起初这个普通人家过得兴旺。翻新完新居之后，他们终于安顿下来；不久 Janet 生下一对双胞胎女儿，人生本无更幸福的了。然而 1974 年 1 月起，情形忽然变了：不知从何处，这家人开始经历一连串怪事。地毯上出现擦不掉的污渍，新装的浴室洁具上现出深深的划痕；水管虽焊过好几回，却一直在漏。然后有一天，这些恼人的事件急转直下——他们的电视自燃了。不久 Jack 遭到某种看不见的东西袭击。所有这些超自然活动让 Smurl 一家确信，他们正被一个邪恶的灵体纠缠。
- **关键词**：In 1973, Jack Smurl; his wife, Janet; and their two daughters / decided to move into a duplex in West Pittston with Jack's parents / At first their ordinary household flourished / Soon after, Janet gave birth to twin girls / Life couldn't be happier / in January 1974, when, out of nowhere / Irremovable stains appeared on the carpets / New bathroom fixtures developed deep scratches / Although soldered several times, water pipes continuously leaked / their television spontaneously combusted / Jack was attacked by something unseen / convinced the Smurl family that they were being plagued by a demonic presence
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 Life couldn't be happier 一句把幸福写死，**下一句立刻是 in January 1974, when, out of nowhere**——转折点精确到月份，也精确到来历不明。清单里三件东西是渐进式的：污渍在布上、划痕在金属上、自燃在电器里，**而最后一条 Jack was attacked by something unseen 把受难的从房子换成了人**。
- **读者视角提示**：迁入时那对双胞胎的结局如何、电视自燃后剩了什么，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 2:** "One day, while in the basement, Janet heard her name called out. She responded, but no one was there. As if it had been invited in, the entity grew bolder. Dark shadows began to slither through the home. The children were tossed down the stairs. The family dog was repeatedly lifted and thrown against the wall. And loud scratching could be heard at all hours of the day and night."

- **中文理解**：有一回，Janet 在地下室听见有人叫她的名字，她答应了，却没有人。那东西像是被请进了门，胆子越来越大。黑影开始在屋里游走。孩子们被扔下楼梯。家里的狗一次又一次被举起、摔向墙壁。而刮擦声在昼夜每个时辰都能听见。
- **关键词**：One day, while in the basement, Janet heard her name called out / She responded, but no one was there / As if it had been invited in, the entity grew bolder / Dark shadows began to slither through the home / The children were tossed down the stairs / The family dog was repeatedly lifted and thrown against the wall / And loud scratching could be heard at all hours of the day and night
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 She responded 一句把全篇最要紧的一次「开门」写完——**她答了，而那一句应答就成了「请进」的凭据**；下一句 as if it had been invited in 用虚拟语气替这次应答定了性。后三句是三连动作，全用被动：被扔下、被举起摔向、刮擦声被听见，**承受的一方一次也没动过**。
- **读者视角提示**：那两个孩子与那条狗的伤势、刮擦来自哪面墙，**本篇没写**。

> **原句 3:** "Evidently angered with the Smurls, the evil presence began physically attacking them. Some members of the family were bitten and their arms slashed. The Warrens called in the services of Father McKenna, who performed a total of three exorcisms. And although their torment seemed to end, it started up once again in December 1986. Finally, in 1988, the Church performed what would be the fourth and final exorcism. At last, the Smurl family was released from the darkness that had plagued them for over thirteen years."

- **中文理解**：显然，那个邪恶的灵体被 Smurl 一家激怒了，开始动手：家里有人被咬，手臂被划伤。沃伦夫妇请来 McKenna 神父，他一共做了三次驱魔。折磨似乎结束了，却在 1986 年 12 月重新开始。最终在 1988 年，教会举行了第四次、也是最后一次驱魔。Smurl 一家终于从缠了他们十三年多的黑暗中被解救出来。
- **关键词**：Evidently angered with the Smurls, the evil presence began physically attacking them / Some members of the family were bitten and their arms slashed / The Warrens called in the services of Father McKenna / who performed a total of three exorcisms / And although their torment seemed to end / it started up once again in December 1986 / Finally, in 1988, the Church performed / what would be the fourth and final exorcism / the Smurl family was released from the darkness / that had plagued them for over thirteen years
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 seemed to end 一句先给一个假的收场，**紧接着 it started up once again 把它推翻——seemed 这个词是全篇的转折支点**。而第四与最终两个词并排放在同一句里，**读者与 Smurl 一家是在同一次驱魔里才同时知道这是最后一次的**。
- **读者视角提示**：第四次之后是否有复发、四个灵体中另外三个是什么，**本篇没写**。

## 一句话总结

1974 年起 West Pittston 的 Smurl 一家从地毯污渍走到电视自燃、又走到孩子被扔下楼；Janet 在地下室应了一声等于把门打开，教会前后四次驱魔才把缠了十三年多的黑暗除去。
