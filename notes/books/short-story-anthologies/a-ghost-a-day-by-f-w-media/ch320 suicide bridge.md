# NOVEMBER 16, 1919 SUICIDE BRIDGE

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1913 年 12 月 13 日，Colorado Street Bridge 在一片喧闹中落成，这座将近一千五百英尺的混凝土建筑蜿蜒穿过 Pasadena 的 Arroyo Seco。像许多大工程一样，它也带上了传说：施工时一名工人坠死在一池速干水泥里，尸体捞不出来，他就此成了桥的一部分。1919 年 11 月 16 日，此地发生了第一起自杀，一名男子从桥上跳下，落到下方一百五十英尺处的河床；此后记录在案的自杀超过一百起，桥因此得名为 Suicide Bridge。其中最出名的一次发生在 1937 年 5 月 1 日，一位年轻母亲把自己的婴儿抛过栏杆再跟着跳下——讽刺的是母子并没有如她所愿在死中团聚，婴儿落到下面的树里活了下来。1993 年大桥修缮完工并加了防自杀的围栏，可自杀的鬼魂仍缠着这座桥。开车的人和行人见过不少灵体，包括一个戴金丝边眼镜的男人和一个穿长袍、看上去要从桥上跳下的女人。那位抛婴下桥的女人则被看见在桥上四处找她的孩子。桥下灵体漫无目的地走动，还能听到奇怪的声音与哭声。没人知道为什么会有这么多自杀，但有人说是那个建桥工人在喂食人们的抑郁，怂恿他们跳下去，好让自己不必永远孤零零地游荡。
- **篇目日期**：NOVEMBER 16, 1919（标题所载日期，即首起自杀之年）
- **事件年代**：1913 年 12 月 13 日落成；1919 年 11 月 16 日首起自杀；1937 年 5 月 1 日抛婴事件；1993 年修缮完工；建桥工人坠亡的年份未给
- **地点**：Pasadena, California（Colorado Street Bridge 与下方的 Arroyo Seco 河床）
- **异象类型**：一群自杀者的灵（戴金丝眼镜的男人、长袍女人、抛婴的母亲、桥下漫走的灵体）
- **叙事手法**：1913 年 12 月 13 日落成 → 近一千五百英尺的混凝土桥 → 施工时工人坠入速干水泥 → 尸体捞不出，成了桥的一部分 → 传说显出桥更暗的一面 → 1919 年 11 月 16 日首起自杀 → 跳下后落至下方一百五十英尺的河床 → 此后记录超过一百起 → 得名 Suicide Bridge → 1937 年 5 月 1 日母亲抛婴后跳下 → 婴儿落到树里幸存 → 1993 年修缮并加防自杀围栏 → 自杀仍缠着这座桥 → 开车者与行人看见众多灵体 → 戴金丝眼镜的男人 → 穿长袍而像要跳桥的女人 → 抛婴的母亲在桥上找孩子 → 桥下灵体漫走，有怪声与哭声 → 传言是建桥工人在喂食抑郁

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "With much fanfare, the Colorado Street Bridge was dedicated on December 13, 1913. The nearly 1,500-foot concrete structure snakes its way across the Arroyo Seco in Pasadena. Like many major construction projects, there is a legend associated with it. During its construction, a worker tumbled to his death into a vat of quick-drying cement. Unable to retrieve the body, he became a permanent part of the bridge. This may be only a legend, yet it does reveal the bridge's darker side."

- **中文理解**：在一片大张旗鼓之中，Colorado Street Bridge 于 1913 年 12 月 13 日落成。这座将近一千五百英尺的混凝土建筑蜿蜒穿过 Pasadena 的 Arroyo Seco。像许多大工程一样，它身上也系着一个传说：施工期间，一名工人坠死在一池速干水泥里。尸体无法取出，他就此成了桥的一部分。这也许只是个传说，却确实显出了这座桥更暗的那一面。
- **关键词**：With much fanfare / the Colorado Street Bridge was dedicated / on December 13, 1913 / The nearly 1,500-foot concrete structure / snakes its way across / the Arroyo Seco in Pasadena / Like many major construction projects / there is a legend associated with it / a worker tumbled to his death / into a vat of quick-drying cement / Unable to retrieve the body / he became a permanent part of the bridge / This may be only a legend, yet it does reveal / the bridge's darker side
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 fanfare 与 darker side 把庆典与黑暗并排放，**而 Unable to retrieve the body 与 he became a permanent part of the bridge 一连两句，让「捞不出人」直接变成「人成了桥」**。snakes its way 把那座一千五百英尺的桥写成一条爬行的东西。
- **读者视角提示**：这座桥所在的 Arroyo Seco 是地名，**本篇没说那个工人叫什么、也没说他是哪年坠的**。

> **原句 2:** "On November 16, 1919, the first of many suicides occurred. A man jumped from the bridge, falling 150 feet to the riverbed below. Since then, over a hundred suicides have been recorded, earning it the name Suicide Bridge. One of the most notable was that of a young mother, who on May 1, 1937, threw her baby over the rail and then jumped after her. Ironically they were not united in death as the mother had hoped. The baby landed in the trees below and survived."

- **中文理解**：1919 年 11 月 16 日，许多自杀中的第一起发生了。一名男子从桥上跳下，坠落到下方一百五十英尺的河床。此后记录在案的自杀超过一百起，它也因此得名为 Suicide Bridge。其中最出名的一次是一位年轻母亲：1937 年 5 月 1 日，她把自己的婴儿抛过栏杆，然后跟着跳了下去。讽刺的是，他们并没有如母亲所愿在死亡中团聚——婴儿落到了下面的树里，活了下来。
- **关键词**：On November 16, 1919 / the first of many suicides occurred / A man jumped from the bridge, falling 150 feet / to the riverbed below / over a hundred suicides have been recorded / earning it the name Suicide Bridge / One of the most notable / was that of a young mother / threw her baby over the rail / and then jumped after her / Ironically they were not united in death / as the mother had hoped / The baby landed in the trees below and survived
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 Ironically 把母亲想要的「一同死」与实际的「一个活着」对调，**而 The baby landed in the trees below and survived 单独成句，把反转按在最后落定**。抛婴与随跳是两句连着写的，母亲想没想得到全在 Ironically 之后。
- **读者视角提示**：那位母亲与婴儿的姓名书里都没给，**本篇也没说那孩子后来怎样了**。

> **原句 3:** "In 1993 major renovations of the bridge were completed, including a new suicide fence, making it much more difficult to add to the bridge's checkered past. Yet, the great number of suicides continues to haunt the bridge, at least in spirit. Several ghosts have been associated with the bridge and the riverbed below. Motorists and pedestrians have spotted numerous spirits, including a man with wire-rim glasses and a woman in a long flowing robe who appears to jump from the bridge. The woman who threw her baby off the bridge is seen wandering the structure looking for her child. Below, the spirits walk aimlessly about and there are reports that strange sounds and crying can be heard. No one knows why there are so many suicides, but some say it is the construction worker who feeds people's depression, urging them to jump so that he may never have to roam alone."

- **中文理解**：1993 年，这座桥完成了一次大修，其中加装了新的防自杀围栏，要再往桥上添一笔污点也就难多了。可自杀的巨大数量仍在纠缠这座桥——至少在灵性上如此。已有几个鬼魂与这座桥及下方的河床连在一起。驾车者与行人见过众多灵体，其中有一个戴金丝边眼镜的男人，还有一个穿长袍、看上去要从桥上跳下的女人。那位把孩子抛下桥的母亲则被看见在桥上四处走动，像是在找她的孩子。下面那些灵体漫无目的地游荡，并且有报道说能听到奇怪的声音与哭声。没人知道为什么会有这么多自杀，但有人说是那个建桥工人在喂食人们的抑郁，怂恿他们跳下去，好让他自己不必永远孤零零地游荡。
- **关键词**：In 1993 major renovations of the bridge were completed / including a new suicide fence / making it much more difficult to add to / the bridge's checkered past / the great number of suicides continues to haunt the bridge / at least in spirit / Motorists and pedestrians have spotted / numerous spirits / a man with wire-rim glasses / a woman in a long flowing robe / who appears to jump from the bridge / is seen wandering the structure / looking for her child / the spirits walk aimlessly about / strange sounds and crying can be heard / the construction worker who feeds people's depression / so that he may never have to roam alone
- **为什么这样写**：作者用 checkered past 说这座桥的过去不干净，**而 feeds people's depression 把那个建桥工人写成一个在喂人情绪的鬼，理由则落在他怕独自游荡上**。围栏能拦人，拦不住鬼，所以后半段又把灵体一件件摆出来。
- **读者视角提示**：桥上的无名工人、戴眼镜的男人与长袍女人，**本篇都没交代他们的身份**。

## 一句话总结

Colorado Street Bridge 自 1919 年起记下上百起自杀，抛婴的母亲成了桥上找孩子的身影，而最后一个解释把纵人下跳的鬼说成了那个埋进水泥里的建桥工人。
