# 03. The Broom Closet（精读分析）

## 概览

**来源**：《We Were Forbidden》，Jacqueline Harpman 著，Ros Schwartz 英译，Transit Books，2026；短篇合集第 3 篇（法文原题 "Le placard à balais"，2003）
**作者**：Jacqueline Harpman（英译 Ros Schwartz）
**类型**：文学短篇 / 元小说 / 婚姻与欲望
**一句话主旨**：一位 novelist 在火车餐车上，借"Madame d'Afflighem"的虚构，写出一个 1920 年代比利时法官妻子 Hortense 在一场舞会后与军官 Ulrich 在扫帚间发生的关系——进而追问"我们如何编造与他人的人生"，以及 aging 与死亡的 inevitability。
**结构**：
- 开篇：train dining car 的 reverie，叙述者（Harpman）的 writerly self
- 转折：Hortense 与 Ulrich 的 broom-closet encounter，婚姻的 boring 与 desire 的 awakening
- 升级：Hortense 寻找 Verwissen（killer）以除去丈夫；Verwissen 的 moral refusal
- 高潮：Verwissen 入室，Monsieur d'Afflighem 在 acute appendicitis 中死去
- 结尾：narrator 对 aging/death 的 meditation，"We do not know whether the affair began in a broom closet"

**核心金句**：
> I have always easily been able to be two people at once, and the salmon looked excellent.
> I realized that Hortense had gone from the first- to the third-person singular. I was no longer Madame d’Afflighem, which made her all the more real.
> We do not know whether the affair began in a broom closet.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** To Jacqueline Barthe, my French teacher when I was fourteen, who handed back my essay saying disdainfully: “At this rate, Mademoiselle Harpman, you will end up writing serial novels for magazines.

**中文理解**：以 Jacqueline Barthe（十四岁时的法语老师）为题献，她曾 disdainfully 把作文还给 Harpman，说："照这个速度，Mademoiselle Harpman，你最后会去给杂志写 serial novels。"——"于是她决定了我的命运。"

**句子结构**：题献句 "To Jacqueline Barthe, my French teacher when I was fourteen, who handed back my essay saying disdainfully: 'At this rate, Mademoiselle Harpman, you will end up writing serial novels for magazines.'" 为不定式短语作题献；"And thus she determined my fate." 短句收束，ironic：老师的 disdain 成了 prophecy。

**关键词**：disdainfully（disdainfully/轻蔑地）、serial novels for magazines（杂志连载小说）、determined my fate（决定了我的命运）

**表达方式**：把老师的轻蔑写成 fate 的决定——ironic  reversal，把"失败"写成"职业起点"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全篇的 origin myth——Harpman 把"被轻蔑"转成"被决定"，把老师的 disdain 作为 writerly identity 的 seed。这也解释了全篇的 meta-fictional 结构：叙述者不断在"真实"与"虚构"之间切换，因为她的 writerly identity 就是从"轻蔑"中长出的。

---

> **原句 2:** I WAS TRAVELING BACK FROM PARIS, where I’d signed a few books, drunk a glass of champagne and made intellectual-sounding comments under the fond gaze of my publisher.

**中文理解**：叙述者从巴黎回来——签了几本书、喝了一杯 champagne、在 publisher 的 fond gaze 下发表 intellectual-sounding comments。她疲惫，没力气走遍 platform，于是登上 first carriage，十五分钟后发现可能到 Liège，得往前挪。

**句子结构**："I WAS TRAVELING BACK FROM PARIS, where I'd signed a few books, drunk a glass of champagne and made intellectual-sounding comments under the fond gaze of my publisher" — where 引导的地点状语从句，内含三个并列谓语（signed/drunk/made）；"I was tired and didn't have the energy to walk the length of the platform, so I boarded the first carriage" — so 引导结果从句；"Fifteen minutes later, I learned from the announcements that I was likely to end up in Liège, so I had to move to the front part of the train" — that 引导宾语从句。

**关键词**：intellectual-sounding comments（intellectual-sounding comments）、fond gaze（fond gaze）、boarded the first carriage（登上 first carriage）

**表达方式**：用 "intellectual-sounding"（sounding 为现在分词，强调"听起来像"而非"真是"）把 writerly self 写成 performance；"fond gaze of my publisher" 把 publisher 的 gaze 写成 fond 的，ironic。

**为什么这样写**：这是 meta-fictional frame 的建立——叙述者是 novelist，她的"intellectual-sounding comments"是 performance，而真正的 creative work（Hortense 的故事）发生在 train dining car 的 reverie 中。哈普曼把"writer"与"woman"并置：职业身份与私人 reverie 在同一空间。

---

> **原句 3:** I have always easily been able to be two people at once, and the salmon looked excellent.

**中文理解**：叙述者"always easily been able to be two people at once"——salmon looked excellent。

**句子结构**："I have always easily been able to be two people at once, and the salmon looked excellent" — 两个并列谓语，and 连接；"always easily been able to be" 为现在完成时 + 能够结构，"two people at once" 为宾语补足语。

**关键词**：two people at once（同时是两个人）、salmon looked excellent（salmon looked excellent）

**表达方式**：用 "two people at once" 把 writer's divided self 写成天赋；"salmon looked excellent" 以 mundane appetite 收束，把 reverie 与 appetite 并置。

**为什么这样写**：这是全篇的 meta-theme——narrator 能同时是"writer"与"woman"、"Hortense"与"self"。"salmon looked excellent" 是 reality check：reverie 不取消 appetite，creative work 与 mundane life 共存。哈普曼把"writer" identity 写成一种 cognitive capacity，而非 vocational choice。

---

> **原句 4:** I was twenty-two and had been married for six tedious years to a polite man who twice a week left my belly damp and sticky. I had no idea what the appropriate thing to do was in these circumstances, my mother having told me only that my wedding night would surprise me but that nothing would happen that wasn’t completely normal, and my closest friends always shied away from disclosing secrets. After making me all wet, Ernest, my husband, would plant a kiss on my forehead, roll over and go to sleep.

**中文理解**：叙述者二十二岁，已与 polite man 结婚六年，他每周两次 leave her belly damp and sticky。她 mother 只说 wedding night 会 surprise 但 nothing 不 normal，friends 也 shied away from disclosing secrets。

**句子结构**："I was twenty-two and had been married for six tedious years to a polite man who twice a week left my belly damp and sticky" — and 连接两个谓语，who 引导定语从句；"I had no idea what the appropriate thing to do was in these circumstances, my mother having told me only that my wedding night would surprise me but that nothing would happen that wasn't completely normal, and my closest friends always shied away from disclosing secrets" — my mother having told me 为分词短语作状语，that 引导宾语从句。

**关键词**：tedious years（tedious years）、left my belly damp and sticky（left my belly damp and sticky）、appropriate thing（appropriate thing）

**表达方式**：用 "left my belly damp and sticky" 把 conjugal sex 写成 mundane、slightly disgusting；"polite man" 以 polite 贬低丈夫，把"礼貌"与"无趣"并置。

**为什么这样写**：这是 Hortense 的 backstory——婚姻的 boring 与 ignorance。哈普曼把"性知识"的缺失写成 generation 的 collective ignorance（mother's warning, friends' silence），把 Hortense 的 later awakening 写成 rebellion against that ignorance。"polite man" 与 "tedious years" 把婚姻写成 social contract，而非 romantic union。

---

> **原句 5:** Ulrich was trembling. He looked about us and could see nothing but doors. I saw them again later and expressed suitable admiration; they are made of some precious wood, carved, ornamented with silver and brass, and they open into the guest rooms. Except one plain door, half-hidden by a dark velvet curtain: my lover’s eyes lit up, he dragged me toward it and we found ourselves standing in the broom closet.

**中文理解**：Ulrich trembling。他 look about，see nothing but doors。叙述者后来看到它们，expressed suitable admiration；它们由 precious wood 制成，carved, ornamented with silver and brass，open into guest rooms。Except one plain door, half-hidden by a dark velvet curtain：lover's eyes lit up，dragged her toward it，they found themselves standing in the broom closet。

**句子结构**：多句描述。"Ulrich was trembling. He looked about us and could see nothing but doors." 短句 + "I saw them again later and expressed suitable admiration; they are made of some precious wood, carved, ornamented with silver and brass, and they open into the guest rooms." 分号连接，现在分词 carved/ornamented 作后置修饰；"Except one plain door, half-hidden by a dark velvet curtain: my lover's eyes lit up, he dragged me toward it and we found ourselves standing in the broom closet." 冒号引出 narrative shift。

**关键词**：trousered? no—trussed（trussed up）、disarray（disarray）、gossipmonger（gossipmonger）... actually this block's keywords: trembling, precious wood, carved, ornamented, half-hidden, broom closet

**表达方式**：用 "nothing but doors" 把 corridor 写成门的迷宫；"Except one plain door, half-hidden by a dark velvet curtain" 以 plain vs. precious 的对比突出 broom closet 的 humble 与 secret；"my lover's eyes lit up" 以 lover's reaction 转向 desire。

**为什么这样写**：这是 desire 的空间化——broom closet 是"plain"与"half-hidden"的，与 prestigious hotel (Hôtel Métropole, red carpet) 形成对比。哈普曼把 desire 写成"hidden door"的发现，把 broom closet 变成 desire 的 container。"half-hidden by a dark velvet curtain" 把 desire 写成"被幕帘半遮"的，暗示它既 secret 又 accessible。

---

> **原句 6:** Brooms don’t shout, dearest. Someone will come and investigate.

**中文理解**："Brooms don't shout, dearest. Someone will come and investigate."

**句子结构**：直接引语，两句话。"Brooms don't shout, dearest" — brooms 作主语（拟人化），dearest 为称呼；"Someone will come and investigate" — future tense，and 连接两个谓语。

**关键词**：Brooms don't shout（brooms don't shout）、dearest（dearest）、investigate（investigate）

**表达方式**：用 "Brooms don't shout" 把 broom closet 的 secrecy 写成 command；"dearest" 以 affectionate term 软化 command；"investigate" 把 discovery 的 risk 写成 verb。

**为什么这样写**：这是 desire 的 verbalization——Ulrich 的 "Brooms don't shout" 把 broom closet 的 secrecy 转化为 rule，把 desire 的 expression 写成"需要被压制"的。哈普曼让 desire 的 verbalization 本身成为 constraint，把 pleasure 与 secrecy 绑定。这也是全篇 motif：secrecy (broom closet) enables pleasure。

---

> **原句 7:** I liked Ulrich, who enables an obedient young wife to discover that she loathes her husband.

**中文理解**："I liked Ulrich, who enables an obedient young wife to discover that she loathes her husband."

**句子结构**："I liked Ulrich, who enables an obedient young wife to discover that she loathes her husband" — who 引导非限制性定语从句，that 引导宾语从句。

**关键词**：enables（enables）、obedient young wife（obedient young wife）、loathes her husband（loathes her husband）

**表达方式**：用 "enables ... to discover that she loathes" 把 Ulrich 写成 catalyst，把 "loathes"（比 hates 更强）写成 discovery 的结果。

**为什么这样写**：这是全篇的 thematic pivot——Ulrich 不是 romantic hero，而是 catalyst for self-discovery。"loathes her husband" 把婚姻的 boring 转化为 active hatred，把 desire 写成 knowledge 的 gateway。哈普曼把 desire 从 pleasure 转向 self-knowledge：Ulrich enables Hortense to know herself。

---

> **原句 8:** I realized that Hortense had gone from the first- to the third-person singular. I was no longer Madame d’Afflighem, which made her all the more real.

**中文理解**：叙述者意识到 Hortense 从 first-person 转向 third-person singular。她不再是 Madame d'Afflighem，这让她 all the more real。

**句子结构**："I realized that Hortense had gone from the first- to the third-person singular" — that 引导宾语从句，from...to... 为介词短语；"I was no longer Madame d'Afflighem, which made her all the more real" — which 引导非限制性定语从句。

**关键词**：first- to the third-person singular（first- to third-person singular）、all the more real（all the more real）

**表达方式**：用 grammatical person shift（first → third）把 narrator's detachment 写成 craft technique；"all the more real" 把 detachment 写成 realism 的 source。

**为什么这样写**：这是 meta-fictional 的 self-awareness moment——narrator 承认她正在 writing Hortense，而 person shift 是 craft decision。"all the more real" 把 craft 写成 realism 的 condition：Hortense becomes real precisely when narrator stops being her. 哈普曼把 writing process 写成 subject，把 narrator's self-awareness 写成 narrative device。

---

> **原句 9:** The time of men is so short! But what about this mustachioed traveler in a hurry? He offers me some of his white wine and wants to tell me his life story. You have to be at a distance! Do you not see how ugly he is?

**中文理解**："The time of men is so short! But what about this mustachioed traveler in a hurry?"

**句子结构**：两句话。"The time of men is so short!" — 主系表，感叹号；"But what about this mustachioed traveler in a hurry?" — 疑问句，what about 引导。

**关键词**：time of men is so short（time of men is so short）、mustachioed traveler in a hurry（mustachioed traveler in a hurry）

**表达方式**：用 "time of men is so short" 把 aging 写成 universal；"mustachioed traveler in a hurry" 以 mundane encounter 收束 meditation，把 mortality 写成 everyday observation。

**为什么这样写**：这是 aging meditation 的 climax——narrator 从 cosmic ("time of men is so short") 转向 mundane ("mustachioed traveler in a hurry")，把 mortality 写成 everyday awareness。哈普曼让 mortality 不是 dramatic event 而是 ambient condition，与 "I live obdurately" 的 acceptance 并置。

---

> **原句 10:** We do not know whether the affair began in a broom closet.

**中文理解**："We do not know whether the affair began in a broom closet."

**句子结构**："We do not know whether the affair began in a broom closet" — 主句 + whether 引导的宾语从句。全篇最后一句，以 "We do not know" 收束。

**关键词**：do not know（do not know）、whether the affair began（whether the affair began）、broom closet（broom closet）

**表达方式**：用 "do not know" 把 narrator's uncertainty 写成 epistemic humility；"whether the affair began in a broom closet" 把 entire narrative 写成 unresolved question。

**为什么这样写**：这是全篇的 final word——narrator 承认 she doesn't know whether the affair (Hortense's) began in the broom closet. 把 entire narrative 写成 "we do not know"，把 fiction 写成 uncertainty。哈普曼把 writerly authority (she wrote the story) 与 epistemic humility (she doesn't know) 并置，把 fiction's truth-status 写成 question rather than assertion。

## 一句话总结

《The Broom Closet》把"写作"写成一种双重生活：narrator 在 train dining car 上，借"Madame d'Afflighem"的虚构，写出一个 1920 年代比利时法官妻子的 desire awakening——从 boring marriage 到 broom-closet encounter，再到寻求 murderer 以除去丈夫，最终 husband 在 acute appendicitis 中死去。哈普曼把 narrator 的 "two people at once" 写成 writerly capacity，把 person shift (first → third) 写成 realism 的 source，并以 "We do not know whether the affair began in a broom closet" 收束——把 entire narrative 写成 unresolved question。全篇以 aging/death 的 meditation 收尾：narrator 从 cosmic ("time of men is so short") 转向 mundane ("mustachioed traveler in a hurry")，把 mortality 写成 ambient condition。meta-fiction 与 desire、aging 与 fiction 交织，构成 Harpman 最复杂的短篇。
