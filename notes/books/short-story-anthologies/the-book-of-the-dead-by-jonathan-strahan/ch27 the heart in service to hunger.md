## 精读

> **原句 1:** "I killed the cockerels in the morning and at eight o’clock they hit the table for Mr. Page’s birthday, gleaming with butter, gizzards simmered and strained into gravy, and garnished with rosemary and thyme."

- **中文理解**：「I killed the cockerels in the morning and at eight o'clock they hit the table for Mr. Page's birthday, gleaming with butter, gizzards simmered and strained into gravy, and garnished with rosemary and thyme。」 （本块引语分析见上）

- **关键词**：killed the cockerels / gleaming with butter / gizzards simmered

- **为什么这样写**：Vo's opening establishes food/abundance (for Pages) vs. hunger (for maids). "I killed the cockerels" = narrator as killer (of animals), foreshadowing Xiulan's killing (of humans). "Gleaming with butter" = food's abundance vs. narrator's hunger. This is the story's premise: maids serve food they can't eat; hunger drives transformation.

- **读者视角提示**：Vo's "killed the cockerels" = foreshadowing. Food abundance (Pages) vs. hunger (maids). Death as hunger begins here.

> **原句 2:** "It’s a kind of living death, that kind of work, a blur of aching muscles and pressure-bruised fingers from the endless cleaning, smelling like sweat and vinegar while pretending you don’t."

- **中文理解**：「It's a kind of living death, that kind of work, a blur of aching muscles and pressure-bruised fingers from the endless cleaning, smelling like sweat and vinegar while pretending you don't。」 （本块引语分析见上）

- **关键词**：living death / aching muscles / pressure-bruised fingers / smelling like sweat and vinegar

- **为什么这样写**：Vo's "living death" = maid work as death-in-life. "Aching muscles, pressure-bruised fingers" = physical toll. "Smelling like sweat and vinegar" = bodily reality; "pretending you don't" = performed invisibility. This is the story's thesis: maid work = living death (invisible, exhausting, dehumanizing). "Living death" = the condition that produces Xiulan's transformation.

- **读者视角提示**：Vo's "living death" = maid work. "Pretending you don't" = performed invisibility. Death-in-life precedes actual death/transformation.

> **原句 3:** "She wouldn’t be dead."

- **中文理解**：「She wouldn't be dead。」 （本块引语分析见上）

- **关键词**：She wouldn't be dead

- **为什么这样写**：Vo's "wouldn't be dead" = narrator's hope/denial. "Wouldn't" = conditional, uncertain. Narrator refuses to accept Xiulan's death. But Xiulan does die/transform. This is the story's tension: narrator's hope vs. reality. "Wouldn't be dead" = denial of death's inevitability.

- **读者视角提示**：Vo's "wouldn't be dead" = denial. Narrator refuses Xiulan's death. But death/transform happens.

> **原句 4:** "I’m hungry, so hungry, sis."

- **中文理解**：「I'm hungry, so hungry, sis。」 （本块引语分析见上）

- **关键词**：I'm hungry, so hungry, sis

- **为什么这样写**：Vo's "I'm hungry, so hungry, sis" = death's refrain. "So hungry" = intensification; "sis" = sister (narrator). This is Xiulan's transformation—hunger becomes her identity, her voice. "I'm hungry" = death's call (not request, demand). This is the story's thesis: death = hunger; hunger = death.

- **读者视角提示**：Vo's "I'm hungry" = death's refrain. Xiulan's hunger = death's voice. Transformation complete.

> **原句 5:** "But sis, I'm hungry."

- **中文理解**：「But sis, I'm hungry。」 （本块引语分析见上）

- **关键词**：sis, I'm hungry

- **为什么这样写**：Vo's final line—"sister, I'm hungry" with Xiulan's eyes on other people = predatory hunger. "Sister" = narrator's new role (Xiulan's sister, not just maid). "I'm hungry" = death's demand, now directed at others. "Her eyes flickered to the other people" = Xiulan eyeing prey. This is the story's horror: Xiulan is now predator, narrator is complicit.

- **读者视角提示**：Vo's final "sister, I'm hungry" = predatory hunger. Xiulan eyes others. Narrator complicit. Death as predation.

> **原句 6:** "I thought of Mrs. Page's tongue. I thought of Mr. Page, hollowed out so that he seemed like an empty basket, the liver, lungs, heart, and intestines all gone."

- **中文理解**：「I thought of Mrs. Page's tongue。I thought of Mr. Page, hollowed out so that he seemed like an empty basket, the liver, lungs, heart, and intestines all gone。」 （本块引语分析见上）

- **关键词**：Mrs. Page's tongue / hollowed out / empty basket / liver, lungs, heart, intestines all gone

- **为什么这样写**：Vo's "Mrs. Page's tongue" and "Mr. Page, hollowed out" = Xiulan's predation (ate Pages). "Empty basket" = body without organs. Narrator's memory = complicity (she knew Xiulan ate them, helped escape). This is the story's horror: Xiulan is monster, narrator is accomplice. "I thought of Mrs. Page's tongue" = narrator's guilt/memory.

- **读者视角提示**：Vo's "Mrs. Page's tongue" + "hollowed out" = Xiulan's predation. Narrator's complicity. Death as consumption.
