## 精读

> **原句 1:** "By and large, her bloodline was unburdened with blessings."

- **中文理解**：「By and large, her bloodline was unburdened with blessings。」Antigone 的家族血脉——大多数代际 "escaped with nothing more than an affinity for herbalism"，但 Antigone 像母亲/姐妹一样能预见死亡。"Unburdened with blessings" = 遗传的 "gift" 其实是 curse—foreknowledge of death.

- **关键词**：bloodline / unburdened with blessings

- **为什么这样写**：Khaw 用 "unburdened with blessings" (not "cursed") 来描述 death-foreknowledge—ironic understatement. "Blessings" 指 death-seeing ability (psychopomp dogs appear); "unburdened" = most family members don't have it. Antigone's "chagrin" = she inherited it. This establishes death as inherited trait, not punishment.

- **读者视角提示**：bloodline = 血脉、家系；unburdened = 未被...所累；psychopomp = 引魂者（引导灵魂的生物）。Khaw's "death as dog" is Greek psychopomp tradition (Cerberus, etc.).

> **原句 2:** "From a young age, Antigone had been told to make peace with the idea she was going to die one day, something she worked hard to do. What she didn't expect was making friends with it."

- **中文理解**：「From a young age, Antigone had been told to make peace with the idea she was going to die one day, something she worked hard to do。What she didn't expect was making friends with it。」Antigone 与 death 的关系——make peace (expected) vs. make friends (unexpected). "Friends with it" = death becomes companion, not enemy. "It" = death (dog).

- **关键词**：make peace / making friends with it

- **为什么这样写**：Khaw's thesis: death as companion, not enemy. "Make peace" = conventional acceptance; "making friends" = unexpected intimacy. This is the story's emotional arc—Antigone's death (dog) moves from feared presence to companion. "What she didn't expect" = the surprise of friendship with mortality.

- **读者视角提示**：Khaw's "death as dog" reframes mortality from threat to companion. "Making friends with it" = the story's central transformation.

> **原句 3:** "Just like how all love was paid for with grief."

- **中文理解**：「Just like how all love was paid for with grief。」Antigone 的 philosophical observation—love and grief are transactional; love's price is grief. This explains her fear: death is certain, love costs grief, but she can't opt out of either.

- **关键词**：all love was paid for with grief

- **为什么这样写**：Khaw's "paid for with grief" treats love/grief as economic exchange—love's cost is inevitable loss. This is Antigone's worldview: everything good costs something. "Paid for" = transactional; "with grief" = the currency. This frames her suicide contemplation: if love costs grief, and she's tired of paying, why not stop?

- **读者视角提示**：Khaw's aphorism is the story's philosophical core—love and grief are inseparable.

> **原句 4:** "What frightened Antigone was the lack of choice."

- **中文理解**：「What frightened Antigone was the lack of choice。」Antigone 的 fear— not death itself, not the dog, not aging, but lack of choice. "There was no opting out. Dying would happen, sooner or later." Death is inevitable; choice is the only agency.

- **关键词**：lack of choice / no opting out

- **为什么这样写**：Khaw's thesis: fear of death is not fear of dying but fear of no control. "Lack of choice" = existential terror of determinism. This explains Antigone's suicide: suicide is the one choice death can't take. Her mother chose; Antigone wants to choose. "No opting out" = death as non-negotiable.

- **读者视角提示**：Khaw's "lack of choice" reframes suicide not as despair but as agency—choosing the moment is the only control.

> **原句 5:** "Forty is a good run."

- **中文理解**：「Forty is a good run。」Antigone 的 refrain——40 年作为 "good run"，反复出现。"Good run" = 生命作为比赛/表演；40 为 sufficient。她对自己说，对 Vera 说，对 companion 说——repetition = self-conviction attempt。

- **关键词**：Forty is a good run

- **为什么这样写**：Khaw's "good run" is Antigone's self-justification—40 years as complete, not tragic. Repetition (3 times) shows her trying to convince herself. "Good run" = sporting metaphor; life as race with finish line. This is Antigone's "choice" language: she's choosing to stop at 40.

- **读者视角提示**：Khaw's refrain emphasizes Antigone's deliberation—she's not impulsive but considered.

> **原句 6:** "The cold could wait."

- **中文理解**：「The cold could wait。」Antigone 的最终决定——drowning/death ("cold") 可以等待。她选择活下去："Tomorrow, maybe... It'll wait." The cold = death (water, drowning)；"could wait" = postponement, not rejection。她不拒绝死亡，只是延迟它。

- **关键词**：The cold could wait

- **为什么这样写**：Khaw's ending is "wait"—not "no" but "not yet." "The cold could wait" = death is patient (her death dog waits), and Antigone can wait too. The companion's stories ("Tell me about your tomatoes") remind her life has value. "Tomorrow, maybe" = open ending—she might still die tomorrow, but today she chooses life. The title "Wait" = both death's patience and life's invitation.

- **读者视角提示**：Khaw's "wait" is the story's thesis—death waits; life can wait too. The ending is hopeful but uncertain.
