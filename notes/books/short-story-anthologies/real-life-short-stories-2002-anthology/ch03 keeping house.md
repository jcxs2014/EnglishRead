# 03. Keeping House

## 本篇导航

**出处**：《Real Life: Short Stories》，Sharon Butala 著（本书第 3 篇）。**版权页（`epub_cop_r1.htm`）的「先前发表」清单只列三篇（Postmodernism / Saskatchewan / Random Acts），不含本篇** ⇒ 本篇无版权页可考的先前发表记录，不作首见处断言。

**篇幅**：21,515 字符 / 287 句（`extract_chapters.py` 与 `verify_corpus.py` 原始输出）。结构为**一个来回**：Beth 从家坐巴士进城看女儿，次日同处一室，清晨的对话，返程。

**一句话主旨**：Janice 打来电话说婚姻要散，母亲 Beth 却在这通电话里认出了自己二十六年前的一段婚姻——包括她在路上围观过的一次暴力、她一直对人说的那句「他从没虐待过我」，以及一件她从未告诉任何人的旧事。

### 核心金句

> "Soon you will begin to fill that emptiness with friendships," she said.

（她给 Janice 的那套时间表。它与 Janice 问她的那个问题——`How long was it after that before you and Duncan split up?`——出自同一种冲动：**用可测量的未来覆盖测不透的过去**。）

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Beth had been worrying about her daughter Janice for some time. Nothing unusual had happened, it was just something in her voice during their regular Sunday phone calls, a kind of tension tempered by sadness, so when Janice phoned on a Thursday, as Beth was finishing the breakfast dishes, she was less surprised than suddenly filled with foreboding."

**中文理解**：Beth 担心女儿 Janice 有一阵子了。并没有发生什么特别的事，只是每周日例行电话里她声音里有某种东西——一种被悲伤调和过的紧张——所以当 Janice 周四打来时，彼时 Beth 正在收拾早餐的碗碟，她与其说惊讶，不如说突然被一种不祥之感填满。

**句子结构**：一句长因果链，骨架是 `Beth had been worrying… / Nothing unusual had happened, it was just… so when Janice phoned…, she was less surprised than suddenly filled with foreboding`。三处值得注意：① `it was just something in her voice` 与 `Nothing unusual had happened` 之间用**逗号**连成 compound（无连词），前句的否定靠后句的肯定才成立；② `a kind of tension tempered by sadness` 是**过去分词短语作后置定语**修饰 tension（`tempered` 兼有「缓和」与「锻炼成钢」双义）；③ 结尾 `less surprised than suddenly filled with foreboding` 是**比较结构的不对称比较**——less 后面是形容词，than 后面是分词短语。

**关键词**：
- *had been worrying about* — 一直在担心（过去完成进行体）
- *Nothing unusual had happened* — 过去完成时；与下文构成否定—肯定
- *something in her voice* — 她声音里的某种东西
- *a kind of tension tempered by sadness* — 被悲伤调和的紧张；分词作后置定语
- *as Beth was finishing the breakfast dishes* — as 引导的伴随状语从句
- *less surprised than suddenly filled with foreboding* — 不对称比较结构
- *foreboding* — 不祥之感

**表达方式**：全篇的诊断书在这一句里就开好了——**不是事件，是声音**（`something in her voice`）。而 `a kind of tension tempered by sadness` 这个短语是本篇的方法论：作者此后所有的回忆都按「两种相反感受的混合」来写（安心与罪感、厌恶与羞愧、想帮忙与不敢看）。`less surprised than suddenly filled with foreboding` 则把「预感」写成**身体被填满**的动作，与后文 `a pain in her chest that wouldn't go away` 同一手法。

**为什么这样写**：Beth 是**回望型叙述者**，因此作者必须先证明她「早就知道」——这一句把知情权交给女儿的声音，而不交给任何事情。由此产生的第一处张力是：`Nothing unusual had happened` 既是实情，也正是本篇的伦理难题：**在暴力里没有「不寻常的事发生」**。浅蓝色房子那段记忆随后给出的正是这个答案。

> **原句 2:** "In the unfinished sentence Beth saw Duncan turning his back on her, walking away from her and the car loaded with her belongings."

**中文理解**：在那个没说完的句子里，Beth 看见了 Duncan 转过身背对她，走向她，走向那辆装了她全部家当的车。

**句子结构**：单句三段。`In the unfinished sentence` 介词短语作状语（这是全篇的枢纽名词短语）；主句 `Beth saw Duncan turning his back on her` —— `see sb doing` 的**宾语补足语用现在分词**；`walking away from her and the car loaded with her belongings` 是与 turning 并列的第二分词，**共享 see 的主语**。注意 `the car loaded with her belongings` 里 loaded 是**过去分词作后置定语**修饰 car（= which was loaded）。

**关键词**：
- *In the unfinished sentence* — 在那个没说完的句子里
- *Beth saw Duncan turning his back on her* — see + 宾语 + 现在分词
- *walking away from her* — 并列的第二分词，共享主语
- *the car loaded with her belongings* — 过去分词作后置定语

**表达方式**：这是全篇的**核心手法**——**叙述者替沉默的一方把话说完**。Janice 说的是 `Before we get all entangled …`（句子未完成），而 Beth 在那未完成的句子里**看见**了自己第一段婚姻的结尾。作者由此确立本篇的叙事规则：女儿说不出的，母亲的身体替她说；`the car loaded with her belongings` 里的 her **同时指 Beth 与 Janice**。

**为什么这样写**：把「离婚」这件事的重量全部压在一个**语法缺口**上，比任何叙述都省力。而 `turning his back on her` 用一个转身动作概括八年婚姻的结束。紧接着 Beth 的第一反应是 `Do you need me to come? I’ll come right away`——**去收拾残局**的本能，正是本篇要处理的：她能提供的不是理解，是体力与钱袋。多年后她说 `I’m not ready for the institutional route yet`（原句见词汇节），两处是同一种能量。

> **原句 3:** "Instead, appalled and full of some emotion she couldn’t quite identify—fear? shame?—she averted her eyes, at least partly not to let woman and boy know there’d been a witness to their humiliation, and kept going."

**中文理解**：相反，她感到骇然，心里充满一种自己也说不清的情绪——恐惧？羞愧？——于是移开了目光，至少有一部分是为了不让那女人和孩子知道有一双眼睛目睹了他们的屈辱，然后继续往前走。

**句子结构**：`Instead,` 独立成分开场，`appalled and full of some emotion she couldn’t quite identify` 是**形容词短语作主语补足语**（省略 was）——`appalled`（形容词）与 `full of…`（介词短语）并列。破折号插入 `—fear? shame?—` 是**两个孤立的名词疑问式**，`she couldn’t quite identify` 修饰的是前一个名词。破折号后 `she averted her eyes, at least partly not to let woman and boy know there’d been a witness to their humiliation, and kept going` —— `averted` 与 `kept` 两个谓语由 `and` 串联（comma splice）。

**关键词**：
- *Instead, appalled and full of some emotion* — 相反，感到骇然且心里充满一种说不清的情绪
- *she couldn’t quite identify* — 她自己也说不清
- *fear? shame?* — 破折号内两个孤立名词
- *she averted her eyes* — 移开目光
- *at least partly not to let woman and boy know* — 至少有一部分是为了不让…知道
- *there’d been a witness to their humiliation* — 有一双眼睛目睹了他们的屈辱
- *kept going* — 继续往前走

**表达方式**：`—fear? shame?—` 把一段无法命名的情绪**当场拆成候选再当场否定**。而 `at least partly not to let woman and boy know` 是一处极精确的自我辩解：`not to let…know` 给了她一个**可以承认的动机**（保护对方隐私），从而把真正的动机（不愿作证）挪到 `at least partly` 之后。`kept going` 是全段的收尾——身体的惯性，也是本篇的伦理核心。

**为什么这样写**：作者不评论 Beth，只让她**自己提供一个较体面的理由**，再让 `at least partly` 把这个理由削掉一半。读者由此得到全部判断权。而 `there’d been a witness to their humiliation` 里 `witness`（证人）一词是法律语汇，在日常叙述里出现，说明这件事在她心里已经成了一桩**她不出面的案子**。多年后她仍在 `often wished that she’d walked up the sidewalk to the two of them and offered help`——这个 `had walked` 的过去完成式表明她把这件事当成**未完成的义务**。

> **原句 4:** "But remembering the look on her face, the way she seemed less afraid than terribly sad, and yet somehow resigned or patient about what was happening, Beth knew that the woman would never leave."

**中文理解**：可是想起那女人的神情，想起她看上去不是害怕，而是极其难过，同时又不知怎么地像是认命了或忍耐着面对正在发生的一切，Beth 知道那女人永远不会离开。

**句子结构**：`But remembering…` 是现在分词短语作状语（**逻辑主语与主句主语 Beth 一致**），内部是一组三段并列的宾语：`the look on her face` / `the way she seemed less afraid than terribly sad` / `and yet somehow resigned or patient about what was happening`。主句 `Beth knew that the woman would never leave` —— `knew` 后**省略 that** 的宾语从句，`would never leave` 是过去将来时。

**关键词**：
- *But remembering the look on her face* — 分词短语作状语
- *the way she seemed less afraid than terribly sad* — 定语从句；从句内再套比较结构
- *and yet somehow resigned or patient* — 却又不知怎么地认命或忍耐
- *what was happening* — what 引导的名词性从句作 about 的宾语
- *Beth knew that the woman would never leave* — 省略 that 的宾语从句

**表达方式**：三段并列把观察**从恐惧移到认命**——`less afraid than terribly sad`（不是害怕，而是极其难过）之后再用 `and yet somehow resigned or patient` 修正一次。而 `resigned or patient` 的 **or** 是关键的：它不让她在「认命」与「忍耐」之间选，于是这个女人的处境**保持开放、不被命名**。整段的结论只有一个否定式：`would never leave`。

**为什么这样写**：这段是本篇的结构性伏笔——**Beth 在这里读到的东西，正是她自己二十六年后会做的事**。因此原句 3 里的「移开目光」获得第二重含义：她当时已经认出了那女人不会走，因为她自己就是那样走的。多年后她在自己的梦里仍然是「被跟着的人」（`a man following her`），而从未成为那个跟着走的人。作者用一句观察把两条时间线接上，中间不写一句评论。

---

> **原句 5:** "At least Duncan had never abused her. That was what she’d told people whether they asked or not, over the years."

**中文理解**：至少 Duncan 从来没有虐待过她。多年来她一直这么告诉别人——不管别人问没问。

**句子结构**：两句。首句 `At least Duncan had never abused her` 是**省略主系 be 的判断句**（At least 置于句首，Duncan 是主语，abused 是实义动词，用现在完成时否定）。次句 `That was what she’d told people whether they asked or not, over the years` —— `what` 引导**表语从句**（what = the thing which），`whether they asked or not` 是让步条件状语从句，`over the years` 是句末时间状语。

**关键词**：
- *At least Duncan had never abused her* — 至少 Duncan 从没虐待过她；省略 be 的判断句
- *had never abused* — 现在完成时否定
- *That was what she’d told people* — what 表语从句
- *whether they asked or not* — whether…or not 作让步状语
- *over the years* — 多年来

**表达方式**：`At least` 一个词就把整件事从**指控降级为辩护**——而它下面垫着的恰恰是原句 8 那些内容（`He would berate me, and I would apologize`）。`whether they asked or not` 说明这句话是**主动抛出**的，读者由此推断：说的人自己也需要被说服。次句用过去完成时 `she’d told`，把这句话的持续时间与效果一并标出。

**为什么这样写**：作者把 Beth 二十六年的自我辩护压缩成两句，并让它**紧接在浅蓝色房子的记忆之后**——刚看完她对陌生人的怯懦，就听到她为自己前夫的辩护。Hugh 在晚餐桌上问 `If she’s not suffering, either she’s lying or there’s something wrong with her`（原句见分析层引用），正是对这句的外部裁决；但作者不让人物替读者说出结论，只让 Beth 自己那句 `no one knows what goes on inside a marriage`（原句 6）承担辩护与反驳两重功能。

---

> **原句 6:** "“I always said,” Beth remarked, “that no matter how much people think they do, no one knows what goes on inside a marriage.”"

**中文理解**："我一直这么说，"Beth 说道，"不管人们多么自以为知道，婚事里面发生的事没有一个人知道。"

**句子结构**：引述语 + 两个直接引语片段。核心是 **`no matter how much people think they do`** —— 让步状语从句中嵌一个**省略 that 的宾语从句**（`(that) they do`），`do` 替代前面的 `no one knows what goes on inside a marriage`。主句 `no one knows what goes on inside a marriage` 是**疑问词 what 引导的名词性从句作 knows 的宾语**，同时 `no one` 是 knows 的主语——即「没有人知道（某件事）」，what 从句是**未知的内容**。

**关键词**：
- *I always said* — 我一直这么说（插入的引述语）
- *no matter how much people think they do* — 不管人们多么自以为知道；让步 + 省略宾语从句
- *no one knows what goes on inside a marriage* — 没有人知道婚姻里发生什么；what 引导宾语从句
- *goes on* — 发生；进行
- *inside a marriage* — 婚姻之内

**表达方式**：整句的力量在 `people think they do` —— **do** 单独出现，替代被省略的整个从句，而它替代的正是前一句 `no one knows…`。于是这句辩解自己拆了自己的台：人们以为知道，恰恰证明没人知道。`no matter how much` 让让步加强到极限，结论反而更绝对。

**为什么这样写**：这是 Beth 的**技术性真话**——她确实不知道婚内发生什么，因为 Duncan 的伤害是**通过她的顺从来施加的**（原句 8）。作者让她用一句逻辑上无懈可击的话，为一件实质上不成立的事辩护。而她说完立刻被自己的记忆打断：`before she could stop herself, she was back inside her own first marriage, Duncan browbeating her`。这句话的时态（always said）说明它是**备用的**，不是此刻的。

> **原句 7:** "“And it turned out it was them. He’d shot her and then, I guess when he realized what he’d done, he killed himself.”"

**中文理解**："结果就是他们俩。他朝她开了枪，然后，我猜，在他意识到自己做了什么之后，他也自杀了。"

**句子结构**：`And it turned out it was them.` 是**强调句型 It turned out + that 从句**（正常语序为 it was them that turned out），省略 that。`He’d shot her` 是过去完成时（早于下文的 `killed himself`）。后半 `and then, I guess when he realized what he’d done, he killed himself` —— `I guess` 为插入语，其后**无 that**地直接跟 `when` 引导的时间状语从句，从句内再嵌 `what he’d done` 名词性从句作 realize 的宾语（`what` = the thing which）。

**关键词**：
- *And it turned out it was them* — 强调句 It turned out + that 从句
- *He’d shot her* — 过去完成时
- *I guess* — 插入语
- *when he realized what he’d done* — 时间状语从句 + 宾语从句
- *he killed himself* — 自杀

**表达方式**：`it turned out it was them` 的强调句式把**身份揭示**放在句首，句子在最重要的信息上以最短的方式落地。`I guess` 插在 `and then` 与 `when` 之间——**在最重的一刻插入一个轻推**。而 `what he’d done`（他做了什么）用**过去完成时**把行为推成已完成的既往，与 `killed himself` 的先后形成一格延迟。

**为什么这样写**：这是全篇最重的一处信息，用**最平的语调**给出，而且是在一段关于流感注射、乡村老宅、afghans 与 doilies 的家常叙述之后。`He’d shot her and then… he killed himself` 两个及物动词并列、无插入语、无形容词。作者用**材料的家常**（手工钩的毯子、蕾丝垫巾）与**结局的极端**并置，不作任何过渡——这与全篇反复出现的手法一致（见原句 8 的 `as if they were still discussing the movie`）。这正是本篇处理家暴的方式：只给事实与场景，不作判词。

---

> **原句 8:** "“He was often very cruel,” she said calmly, as if they were still discussing the movie. “He would berate me, and I would apologize—for things I hadn’t done, that I knew I hadn’t done, or that I knew he’d done, not me—just to get peace. Just to get us back together again. I’d say to myself, How important is this quarrel, anyway? Did I want to end up divorced? To have to be without him,” she said, turning to Janice, seeing the wonder in her eyes as she stared back at her mother, “was unthinkable to me. Beyond unthinkable."

**中文理解**："他常常很残忍，"她平静地说，好像她们还在聊那部电影。"他会斥责我，而我会道歉——为那些我没做过、我明知我没做过的事，或者那些我明知是他做的、不是我做的事——只求安宁。只求我们能重新在一起。我会对自己说：这场争吵到底有多重要？我是想以离婚收场吗？想要没有他吗，"她说着，转向 Janice，看见她望着母亲的眼里满是惊异，"对我来说是不可想象的。简直不可想象。"

**句子结构**：三段引语 + 一段夹在中间的叙述。① `He was often very cruel` 系表句 + 频率副词。② `He would berate me, and I would apologize` 是 **`would + 动词原形**的平行反复**（习惯性过去），第二分句的主语换成 I —— 主语切换正是这段的权力结构**。破折号内 `for things I hadn’t done, that I knew I hadn’t done, or that I knew he’d done, not me` 是**四项递进的定语链**（things → 同位语从句 → or 并列 → that 从句），最后一个 `not me` 是**裸立的补足语**。破折号后 `just to get peace. Just to get us back together again.` 两个**零主语的祈使式不定句**，第二句升级（peace → us back together）。③ `I’d say to myself, How important is this quarrel, anyway? Did I want to end up divorced? To have to be without him` —— 报告式转述（`I’d say`）后直接引出**未加引号的内心独白**（comma splice），三个疑问/反问连发。④ `was unthinkable to me. Beyond unthinkable.` 是**倒装的强调**（形容词提前）。

**关键词**：
- *He was often very cruel* — 系表句 + 频率副词
- *He would berate me, and I would apologize* — would 平行反复；主语切换
- *for things I hadn’t done, that I knew I hadn’t done* — 同位语从句
- *or that I knew he’d done, not me* — 裸立补足语 not me
- *just to get peace* — 零主语不定句
- *How important is this quarrel, anyway?* — 未加引号的内心独白
- *Beyond unthinkable* — 形容词提前的强调倒装

**表达方式**：本句群是全篇的语言核心。三处设计：① **`would` 平行反复中主语从 He 换到 I**，把施动者与回应者绑进同一个习惯；② `just to get peace. Just to get us back together again.` 两个无主句的祈使式，**第二句把目标从抽象（peace）换成具体关系（us back together）**——她要的不是安宁，是回到原状；③ `not me` **裸放在句末**，不需要任何连接词就把责任推回去。

**为什么这样写**：作者把这段放回「聊电影」的语调里（`as if they were still discussing the movie`）——**降格**正是本篇的伦理方法：让最重的内容用最轻的框架说出，读者自行承担判断。原句 7 的 murder-suicide 与本段之间只隔着家常材料，节奏不变。末句 `Beyond unthinkable.` 的倒装把「不可想象」推到不可再加强，恰好交代她为何留了下来；而 Janice 的那句 `If he mistreated you, how can it be your fault?` 会在本段之后出现（见分析层引用），两种理解在同一张床上相撞。

> **原句 9:** "“And so I’d abase myself for what I thought was the sake of our marriage.” She laughed. "And wound up divorced anyway.”"

**中文理解**："所以我就为了我以为的这段婚姻的福祉而贬低自己。"她笑了。"结果还是离了婚。"

**句子结构**：两段引语夹一个叙述句。第一段 `And so I’d abase myself for what I thought was the sake of our marriage` —— `for` 引导**原因状语**（此处 for 表「为了」），内部 `what I thought` 是**介词宾语内嵌的名词性从句**（= the thing which I thought），从句后再接同位语 `was the sake of our marriage`。第二段是**省略主语与助动词的被动式**（`And (I) wound up (being) divorced` —— `wind up + 过去分词/形容词` 是习语）。

**关键词**：
- *I’d abase myself* — 我就贬低自己
- *for what I thought was the sake of our marriage* — 为了我以为的婚姻的福祉；what 从句
- *She laughed* — 叙述插入句
- *wound up divorced anyway* — 结果还是离了婚；wind up + 形容词

**表达方式**：`abase`（贬低、糟践）是一个**极少被日常使用的词**，作者选它而不选 apologize 或 humble，恰恰因为它含有**主动施加**的意味。她把自己写成一个被自己施加的对象。而 `what I thought was the sake of our marriage` 里的 `I thought` 是全句唯一的主语重心——**她始终知道那是她「以为」的**。中间那个 `She laughed.` 是全篇唯一一次笑声，紧接其后的是 `And wound up divorced anyway.` 这个**零主语的转折**。

**为什么这样写**：本段是 Beth 对自己八年婚姻的**结算**，用三个动作完成：自我贬低（abase）／笑／仍然是离婚。而 `She laughed` 独立成句，把「可笑」与「痛」分开——这与原句 3 的 `appalled and full of some emotion` 是同一种技术：**情绪不与结论混在同一句里**。`wound up divorced anyway` 中的 `anyway` 是全句的落脚点：尽管如此。

> **原句 10:** "Beth’s voice hung on, scintillating on the thick and still air as if she had spoken from some other world than this small bedroom in this half-emptied apartment in the middle of a city Beth had long ago, for a few lonely years, called home."

**中文理解**：Beth 的声音悬在那里，在浓稠而静止的空气里闪烁，仿佛她是从另一个世界说出来的，而不是从这间半空的公寓里这间小卧室、在这座她很久以前曾称之为家的城市中间说出来的。

**句子结构**：`Beth’s voice hung on, scintillating on the thick and still air` —— 主语是**所属格**（`Beth’s voice`），`hung on` 是半系动词结构（hang on 保留在原地），`scintillating` 是**现在分词作伴随状语**（与 scintillating 搭配的介词仍是 on）。核心是从句 `as if she had spoken from some other world than…`，内含一个**比较结构 X than Y**：X = `this small bedroom in this half-emptied apartment`，Y = `some other world`（Y 提前作表语）。`in the middle of a city Beth had long ago, for a few lonely years, called home` 是后置修饰 city 的**非限制性定语从句**，被两个逗号切出，其中又插入 `for a few lonely years` 这个**时间状语**。

**关键词**：
- *Beth’s voice hung on* — 所属格主语 + 半系动词结构
- *scintillating on the thick and still air* — 现在分词作伴随状语
- *as if she had spoken from some other world* — 与过去事实相反的虚拟（as if + had done）
- *than this small bedroom in this half-emptied apartment* — 比较结构，Y 提前
- *in the middle of a city* — 城市之中
- *Beth had long ago, for a few lonely years, called home* — 非限制性定语从句，内插时间状语

**表达方式**：`scintillating`（闪烁）把一个声音写成**可见的**；而 `the thick and still air` 里 `thick` 与 `still` 并非并列——`still` 是副词修饰 `hung`（声音还悬着），`thick` 修饰 air，一句话里两种成分共用一个介词短语。三重 `this…in this…in the middle of` 的**指示词叠加**把空间逐层收紧，最后被 `some other world` 一次性放掉。末句的定语从句把时间**前置并加逗号**（`long ago` 后停一下，`for a few lonely years` 后再停一下），模拟回忆被分层取回。

**为什么这样写**：这是全篇的收束，与开篇的 `filling the breakfast dishes` 形成对照——开头是 Beth 手里有活、脑子里有事；结尾是她的声音**离开自己的身体**。`as if she had spoken from some other world than…` 用 `as if + had done`（与过去事实相反）说明她说的不是此刻的话，而是二十六年前的话被取出来放在此刻。`called home` 一词收束全篇：Janice 正在失去一个家，而 Beth 记得**自己当初是怎么没有的**。

## 长难句专项

**①**

> "In the unfinished sentence Beth saw Duncan turning his back on her, walking away from her and the car loaded with her belongings."

**解剖**：全句靠**一个状语承担全部重量**——`In the unfinished sentence`（在那个没说完的句子里）交代了信息的来源不是任何人的话，而是**语法上的缺口**。主句是 `see sb doing`：`Beth saw Duncan turning his back on her`，宾语补足语用现在分词。`walking away from her and the car loaded with her belongings` 是与 turning 并列的**第二分词，共享 see 的主语 Duncan**——因此转身与走开是同一个动作的两拍。`the car loaded with her belongings` 里 `loaded` 是**过去分词作后置定语**（= which was loaded），其宾语 `her belongings` 与前文 `her` 指同一人（Beth），使 `the car` 成为 Beth 那一段婚姻的物证。

---

**②**

> "Instead, appalled and full of some emotion she couldn’t quite identify—fear? shame?—she averted her eyes, at least partly not to let woman and boy know there’d been a witness to their humiliation, and kept going."

**解剖**：`Instead,` 独立成分。`appalled and full of some emotion she couldn’t quite identify` 是**形容词 + 介词短语并列作主语补足语**（省略 was），其中 `she couldn’t quite identify` 是后置定语修饰 emotion。破折号内 `—fear? shame?—` 是**两个孤立名词**，破折号在此标记「说不出来」的瞬间。破折号后 `she averted her eyes, at least partly not to let woman and boy know there’d been a witness…` —— `averted` 与 `kept going` 两个谓语由 `and` 串联（comma splice），中间插入 `at least partly not to let…know`（不定式作目的状语）。`not to let…know` 给出一个可承认的动机，`at least partly` 把它削到不足全数。

---

**③**

> "But remembering the look on her face, the way she seemed less afraid than terribly sad, and yet somehow resigned or patient about what was happening, Beth knew that the woman would never leave."

**解剖**：现在分词短语 `But remembering…` 作状语，逻辑主语 Beth 与主句一致。宾语是**三段并列**：`the look on her face`（名词短语）｜`the way she seemed less afraid than terribly sad`（the way + 从句，从句内再套 `less A than B` 比较）｜`and yet somehow resigned or patient about what was happening`（副词 + 形容词并列 + about 介词短语，内含 what 名词性从句）。主句 `Beth knew that the woman would never leave` 的宾语从句**省略 that**，谓语用 `would + 动词原形`（过去将来时）。全句的力量在末尾唯一的否定式。

---

**④**

> "“He was often very cruel,” she said calmly, as if they were still discussing the movie. “He would berate me, and I would apologize—for things I hadn’t done, that I knew I hadn’t done, or that I knew he’d done, not me—just to get peace. Just to get us back together again. I’d say to myself, How important is this quarrel, anyway? Did I want to end up divorced? To have to be without him,” she said, turning to Janice, seeing the wonder in her eyes as she stared back at her mother, “was unthinkable to me. Beyond unthinkable.”"

**解剖**：全篇最复杂的引语群，四段。① `He was often very cruel` 系表句。② `He would berate me, and I would apologize` 用 **`would + 动词原形**的平行反复，第二分句**主语换成 I**——施动者与回应者被绑进同一习惯。破折号内是**四项递进的定语链**（`things` → 同位语从句 `that I knew I hadn’t done` → `or that I knew he’d done` → 裸立补足语 `not me`）。破折号后 `just to get peace. Just to get us back together again.` 是**两个零主语的祈使式不定句**。③ `I’d say to myself, How important is this quarrel, anyway? Did I want to end up divorced? To have to be without him` —— 报告式 `I’d say` 后**直接引出未加引号的内心独白**（comma splice），三个反问连发。④ `was unthinkable to me. Beyond unthinkable.` —— **形容词提前的强调倒装**。

---

**⑤**

> "Beth’s voice hung on, scintillating on the thick and still air as if she had spoken from some other world than this small bedroom in this half-emptied apartment in the middle of a city Beth had long ago, for a few lonely years, called home."

**解剖**：主语是**所属格** `Beth’s voice`，谓语 `hung on` 是半系动词结构（保留原状），`scintillating` 为现在分词作伴随状语。核心是 `as if she had spoken from…`——**与过去事实相反的虚拟**（as if + had done），说明她讲的是被取出来的旧话。该从句内含比较结构：`some other world`（Y）**提前作表语**，`than this small bedroom…`（X）后置。`this small bedroom in this half-emptied apartment` 里两个 `this` 叠加，`in the middle of a city` 再加一层。末尾的非限制性定语从句 `Beth had long ago, for a few lonely years, called home` 被两个逗号切出，内部又插入 `for a few lonely years`——时间状语被**拆成两处停顿**。

## 一句话总结

*Keeping House* 用一场探访把两代人的婚姻并置：Janice 说「我们不合适」，Beth 听见的却是自己二十六年前那句 `At least Duncan had never abused her`——本篇的全部重量都压在「知道与不理解同时发生」上，而作者只给材料、不给判词。

## 可迁移表达

1. **用声音而非事件作预兆**：`it was just something in her voice during their regular Sunday phone calls` —— 长期观察一个人，靠语气不靠事件
2. **两种相反感受的混合**：`a kind of tension tempered by sadness` —— 全文方法论：所有回忆都写成混合体
3. **补完别人没说完的句子**：`In the unfinished sentence Beth saw Duncan turning his back on her` —— 叙述者替沉默的一方把话说完
4. **用 `just to…` 的双升格**：`just to get peace. Just to get us back together again.` —— 目标从抽象升到具体
5. **`would` 平行反复中换主语**：`He would berate me, and I would apologize` —— 施动与回应绑进同一习惯
6. **裸立补足语推回责任**：`or that I knew he’d done, not me` —— 裸立句末，不需任何连接词
7. **降格叙述承载重料**：`she said calmly, as if they were still discussing the movie` —— 语调不变，内容照旧
8. **破折号标记说不出**：`full of some emotion she couldn’t quite identify—fear? shame?—` —— 说不出来时就地拆成两个候选
9. **半承认的动机**：`at least partly not to let woman and boy know` —— 给出体面理由，再削掉一半
10. **重复的自我辩护**：`At least Duncan had never abused her. That was what she’d told people whether they asked or not` —— `whether they asked or not` 暴露说话人自己也需要被说服
11. **零主语的祈使式不定句**：`Just to get peace.` —— 无主语的动作句，句子更短更急
12. **结尾用空间放大**：`as if she had spoken from some other world than this small bedroom in this half-emptied apartment` —— 三重 `this` 收紧后一次性放掉
