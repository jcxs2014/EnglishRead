### Chapter Fourteen · Lily 被谋杀 + Alex 手表入手 + Marie 的刀伤鉴定

> 文件：`text/CH16_chapter_fourteen_a_memorial_ash_asked.txt`（约 4500 词，**全书重大转折章**）
> 叙事功能：**Lily 的死因揭晓 + Alex 获得锚点 + 双线谋杀案确立**——Marie 带 Lily 去海滩散步，Lily 展示身上的**刀伤**，Marie 用**急诊室护士的专业判断**确认："stab wounds…done with a fairly blunt kitchen knife"——**Lily 是被谋杀的**，死因是**预谋的刀刺**，死亡地点是**clifftop**（悬崖顶部），不是"坠落/自杀"而是**"被推下去"**。**两个谋杀案正式确立**：Alex（毒杀/致幻后车祸）+ Lily（刀刺后推下悬崖）。同时 **Kinnock 把 Lily 的睫毛笔偷偷放进 Alex 手表的证物箱**（Box 3359），让 Lily 能去证物室取手表——**Lily 获得 Alex 的锚点，Alex 可以离开房车**。Alex 的**触物能力继续增长**（"slight movement as it slid across the smooth surface"）。叙事声音：Marie & Lily 海滩（**全书最沉重的对话**）→ Kinnock 证物室潜入 → 全员得知 Lily 被谋杀的反应 → Lily 去证物室取表。

**场景：** Marie 的房车（早餐）→ Skegness 海滩 → Kinnock 证物室潜入 → Rita 房车（全员反应）。

**关键揭示——Lily 被谋杀：**
- Marie 在海滩请求 Lily 展示"伤口"——"I want to ask her about those wounds she mentioned when Alex first appeared"（Marie 一直记得 Lily 提过的伤口）。
- Lily 掀起上衣，露出侧面——Marie "gasped, her hand flying to her mouth"——**stab wounds**（刀伤）。
- Marie 的专业判断："Really nasty ones as well. Done with a fairly blunt kitchen knife, I'd say. I spent quite a few years in the emergency department…"——**Marie 用急诊室经验判断刀的类型：钝厨房刀**。
- **预谋性**："The fact that you remember the clifftop means you were most likely killed up there. A premeditated murder—not an accident in the heat of the moment. Because people aren't usually in the habit of carrying a kitchen knife when they go for a walk with someone."——**Marie 用"带刀散步"的逻辑确认这是**"**预谋杀**"**。
- **Lily 的记忆缺失**："I can't even remember a single thing about it! Nothing at all."——**Lily 的"保护性失忆"是 Marie 推测的**——"A protective mechanism, maybe? So you can carry on without the heartache of remembering how you died?"

**关键超自然突破——Alex 获锚：**
- Kinnock 把 Lily 的**睫毛笔**伪装成"Alex 的化妆品"（"Ash recognised it…Alex loved to wear it"），放进证物箱 Box 3359，与 Alex 的手表同箱。
- Kinnock **撒谎**："It was found in the makeup van on set"——**这是 Kinnock 第一次为了帮助调查而撒谎**——"his heart pounding a little, unused to lies, small as they were."
- **证物室守卫 John** 起先怀疑（"Slippery Smith"想偷信息），但信任 Kinnock。
- Lily 进入证物室 → 被 John 撞见 → John 感到"a sudden shiver"（冷）但没看到人 → **Lily 与活人的"温度交换"再次出现**。
- Lily **成功打开证物箱盖**（"Ha! I did it!"）→ 拿到 Alex 的手表 → "tucking the watch deep into the pocket of her skinny jeans"。

**关键情感线——两个谋杀受害者的拥抱：**
- Lily 被 Alex 拥抱——"Not realising quite how much she had missed physical contact, she sank into his embrace"——**两个鬼魂之间的拥抱是全书最动人的时刻之一**——**两个被谋杀的人互相安慰**。
- **Ash 的嫉妒**："Ash felt a sharp pang at the sight of them, the jealousy catching him by surprise. He should be the one comforting his Lily, not Alex."——**Ash 第一次感到"嫉妒"**——**Ash 对 Lily 有保护欲/爱意**，看到 Alex 拥抱 Lily 让他痛苦。
