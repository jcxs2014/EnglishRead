# Chapter 1

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Alba wakes in a store with blood everywhere; she finds nine hoops in her ears and hears a girl humming.
- **情感弧线位置**：chapter one — establishes the body-horror baseline and the missing-daughter motif.
- **人物弧线**：Alba from unconscious to standing, from confusion to recognition.
- **叙事手法**：third-person limited, five-stage escalation, cliffhanger ending.

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "She came to in the dark, sprawled across the floor."

**中文理解**：她在黑暗中苏醒，四肢摊开趴在地板上。

**句子结构**：主句 + 过去分词短语作伴随状语。

**关键词**：came to / sprawled

**为什么这样写**：用最短的句子交代苏醒，把读者直接按进现场。
