# 《The Lonely Hearts Book Club》金句精选

① The day I met Arthur McLachlan was perfectly ordinary.（ch01）

**中文**：我遇见阿瑟·麦克拉奇兰的那一天，平常得不能再平常。
**上下文**：全书第一句，也是全书结构的一次预告——这本书讲的是一次没有任何预兆的相遇。
**为什么重要**：它把「重要性」和「戏剧性」彻底拆开。此后三十四章里发生的每一件大事（一次两万块的捐款、一次当众自爆、一张五十年前的合影、两次住院）都是从这句平淡里长出来的。
**呼应关系**：与 Once upon a time had always been good enough for me.（ch02）同源——两个人对「开始」这件事有完全相反的期待。

② “If you’re looking for a book recommendation, I think you should pick up The Art of Racing in the Rain. It’s what I suggest to all our regular patrons. I know it has a reputation for being sad, but—”（ch01）

**中文**：如果你想要一个推荐，我建议你读《雨中的赛车》。我给所有常客都推荐这本。我知道它有个悲伤的名声，可是——
**上下文**：她被这位读者气了一整个下午，最后还是把一本书塞给了他。这本书后来变成了读书会的名字。
**为什么重要**：这本书全部的转折点是一次不情愿的赠礼。她的示好方式是给对方一件他没法拒绝的东西。
**呼应关系**：
- 与 “I don’t want all five copies. I only want this one.” He held it to his chest, his grip so tight that the bulbous blue veins stood out on his hands. “She can have the rest, but this one is mine.”（ch08）
- 他抢走唯一一本形成正反面；那本书的书名在 “It’s high time the Racing in the Rain Book Club made the formal introduction of our newest and most illustrious member.” Mateo rubbed his hands together with a greedy—and welcome—crackle. “Arthur McLachlan isn’t the only one around here who knows where to hit so it really hurts.”（ch26）
变成了这支队伍的正式名称。

③ Believe it or not, I was engaged to be married to a doctor. Technically, he was a chiropractor, but I’d learned early on in our relationship that to introduce him as anything but Dr. Marcowitz would end in a battle royal.（ch02）

**中文**：信不信由你，我订婚的对象是一名医生。严格说，是脊椎治疗师——但我已经学会了不在这种地方吹毛求疵。
**上下文**：她自己的开场自白，出现在认识那位老人之前。
**为什么重要**：一句话交代了她的婚姻的全部内容：她接受了一个不准确的称呼，因为她早已决定不为此争辩。而她后来的每一次「算了」，都是这一句的延长。
**呼应关系**：与 “Brett doesn’t discuss things. He decrees.”（ch30）构成一组对照——同一个婚姻，两种描述。

④ Once upon a time had always been good enough for me.（ch02）

**中文**：对从前那个开头，一直就够用了。
**上下文**：她列举了「正常人会做的事」：加入联赛、办个会员卡。她一件也没做过。
**为什么重要**：这是全书对「安全」的定义。安全就是永远停在故事开始之前。
**呼应关系**：
- 与 The day I met Arthur McLachlan was perfectly ordinary.（ch01）
- 同出一源；与 With a resolute breath, I turned my step in the opposite direction and headed into the hospital. In there, I’d find anger and passion, irritability and wrath. In there, I’d find my friend.（ch34）
她最终拒绝的那条「平滑、没有断点的路」正相反。

⑤ That upheaval meant I was living. Not well, obviously, and not in any way that would spark envy among home renovation enthusiasts, but enough so that some evidence of my existence remained. Even the smallest, most insignificant Roman housewife got to leave her shards of pottery behind.（ch03）

**中文**：那团乱意味着我还活着。当然算不上活得好，也绝不值得羡慕，但那团乱确实意味着我还在动。
**上下文**：她刚替一位丧女的母亲做了一次咨询，第一次发现自己能做这件事。
**为什么重要**：它把「活得不好」和「没在活」划成了两件事——而这个区分是她全书唯一没有丢掉的东西。
**呼应关系**：与 But I was more alive in the eight years I spent with her than in all my years since.”（ch34）她对自己一生的判词是同一个尺度。

⑥ What I was doing right now technically qualified as stalking.（ch05）

**中文**：我现在干的事，技术上算跟踪。
**上下文**：她在下班后绕到老人窗外，只为了看他有没有吃饭。
**为什么重要**：她第一次承认自己的行为越界，而她用的词是「技术上」——这个自我辩解的结构，就是这本书对所有关系的定义方式。
**呼应关系**：与 Technically, I did belong just as much as every other person streaming past the old fort building or tossing Frisbees on the green lawn across the street. This wasn’t one of those highly academic colleges where hushed tones and marble reverence were required every time you walked in the door. The students’ average age was midtwenties, and most graduates went on to work in administrative offices. No one would ever call these halls hallowed.（ch25）他端着四个人的咖啡出现在同一所图书馆对照——两个人用了同一种伪装。

⑦ An echo with nothing and no one to call her own. A friendly facade. An empty smile.（ch06）

**中文**：一个回声，无所有，也无一人可依。一个友善的外壳。一个空洞的微笑。
**上下文**：她在自己的生日和忌日那天读完《喜福之地》里那一段，然后把书合上。
**为什么重要**：这三句是全书对「不被爱」最精确的描写，而它出自一个刚刚开始读书的人之手。老人一生都在否认自己就是这三样东西。
**呼应关系**：与 It was pathetic, really. I’d lived alone in this house for almost thirty years, and I’d done fine with each and every one of them. I had a routine to keep me from getting bored, my books to keep my mind active. I’d gone for days at a time without having to speak a word to anybody, and I regretted none of them.（ch28）是同一件事的正面。

⑧ I didn’t love Brett Marcowitz, and I didn’t love his family. I didn’t love my parents. I’d never loved any of my old college roommates, and the friends I had now were more like work acquaintances than kindred spirits. Since the day my sister Emily died, her life snuffed short by a hole in her heart that no number of surgeries could fix, I hadn’t loved anyone who didn’t exist between the covers of a book. At this point, I wasn’t even sure I knew how.（ch06）

**中文**：我不爱布雷特·马科维茨，也不爱他家人。我不爱我父母。我从来没有爱过任何人。
**上下文**：她对自己人生的第一份清单，全部是否定。
**为什么重要**：这是全书最彻底的一次自述，而它出现在她开始组建书会之前——也就是说，她是带着这个前提去认识那四个人的。
**呼应关系**：与 I love that we can get Mateo to read things against his will, even though he secretly enjoys every minute.（ch35）相隔二十九章，而那是一次完整的反转。

⑨ “I don’t want all five copies. I only want this one.” He held it to his chest, his grip so tight that the bulbous blue veins stood out on his hands. “She can have the rest, but this one is mine.”（ch08）

**中文**：我不要五本。我只要这一本。（说完他把它按在胸口。）
**上下文**：她买齐了五本同款，准备发给读书会。
**为什么重要**：这是老人第一次在她面前承认想要什么。而他用的方式是把书抢走。
**呼应关系**：与 “I’m not giving her my copy of Anne of Green Gables,” I said. “I’m sorry, Greg, but I can’t. It’s too valuable.”（ch31）他拒绝交出那本旧书同源；一个人把书当成身体的一部分，是这一整条线的基础。

⑩ Save me, that look said. Protect me.（ch18）

**中文**：救救我。那个眼神是这么说的。保护我。
**上下文**：她把「他不需要我」这个结论撤回去的那一秒。
**为什么重要**：全书最短也最重的一次翻译。作者让她替一个说不出口的人把话说出来——而这三个词是整个书会存在的全部理由。
**呼应关系**：与 They come running. They care.” I glanced around at the shocked faces of my so-called friends and started to sag.（ch35）互为问答。

⑪ “You used me. Puffed me up with importance and then deflated me like a catheter balloon.”（ch18）

**中文**：你利用我。把我吹得膨胀起来，再像给导尿管气囊放气那样把我瘪下去。
**上下文**：她刚用四个畅销书名把老人夸了一通，条件是他必须参加书会。
**为什么重要**：她第一次赢，赢的方式是营销。而这一句是她对自己手段最精确的描述——比她后来任何一次自我辩解都诚实。
**呼应关系**：
- 与 Believe it or not, I was engaged to be married to a doctor. Technically, he was a chiropractor, but I’d learned early on in our relationship that to introduce him as anything but Dr. Marcowitz would end in a battle royal.（ch02）
- 那套自我辩解的结构同源；而 It behooved me to put this woman in her place, but my heart wasn’t in that, either. I’d never realized before how much effort it took to keep up all that bluster—to fight everything and everyone, to treat every square on the pavement like a battleground. If I looked anything like Nigel Carthage these days, my energy could be expended on much better places.（ch29）
里那位柜员说的是同一个词的正面。

⑫ And he was failing. We were both failing.（ch19）

**中文**：而他在失败。我们俩都在失败。
**上下文**：他刚说完「我爱我妈」，而对面那个人只回了一声 Ye-es。
**为什么重要**：全书的转折点不是任何一个人的坦白，是两个人同时承认自己不行。作者用同一个失败把他们放在了同一侧。
**呼应关系**：
- 与 “I am.”（ch20）
- 那两个字构成一组；而 And I’d been hiding every single day of my life since.（ch21）
是它的下游后果。

⑬ “I am.”（ch20）

**中文**：我就是。
**上下文**：她刚用最长的一段话证明没有人会爱上一个穿着难看、跳着踢踏舞的图书管理员。
**为什么重要**：全章最短也最重的一次回答，而它刻意模仿了上一章那一声说不出口的 Ye-es。两个人的语法第一次对齐。
**呼应关系**：
- 与 And he was failing. We were both failing.（ch19）
- 同源；与 I had no idea when everyone in the room had started moving, but when I finally managed to breathe, it was to find that Maisey, Sloane, and Mateo had formed a protective arc in front of me.（ch26）
那道保护性的弧线是它的结果。

⑭ “So what? They’re just jobs. They’re temporary. They don’t mean anything.”（ch20）

**中文**：那又怎样？那些不过是工作。它们是临时的。它们什么也不代表。
**上下文**：她刚被指出一辈子在躲他母亲的影子，于是她否认了自己最在意的那件事。
**为什么重要**：全书最重的一次否认，而作者用三个短句排比把它推到极限。全章真正引爆的是第三句。
**呼应关系**：与 “It’s pretty obvious why that book annoys me,” I said. I watched as the ants discovered a patch of sticky residue and started crawling over each other to get to it. “It’s the most cliché thing in the world for a gay little mama’s boy from the Philippines to see himself written so clearly in those pages, but there you have it.”（ch21）他解释自己为什么恨那本书是同一个洞的两面——他躲，她否认。

⑮ “She told me to try again.”（ch21）

**中文**：她让我再试一次。
**上下文**：他七岁掉进井里，母亲对这件事的全部回答。
**为什么重要**：他母亲的全部教育浓缩成六个字——不救援，让你自己重来。这六个字解释了他为什么一辈子在唱他恨的歌。
**呼应关系**：与 Once upon a time, there was a boy who loved his mother very, very much. His love for her was so great that when she lay on her deathbed, clutching her son’s hand with all the strength that remained in her racked, wrecked body, she extracted a promise from him.（ch22）那个「男孩对母亲的爱」的童话构成一组：一个讲爱，一个讲那件爱是怎么变成控制的。

⑯ And I’d been hiding every single day of my life since.（ch21）

**中文**：而我这辈子每一天都在躲。
**上下文**：他刚说清自己为什么讨厌《喜福之地》。
**为什么重要**：他把一个文学判断翻转成了对自己的诊断。躲藏的对象不是书，是他母亲在他身上留下的形状。
**呼应关系**：
- 与 “Please. You know as well as I do that the only hallowed ground in our family is wherever she’s standing.” I paused, wanting to take a moment to corral my thoughts but afraid of what else Lincoln might say if I left him the space. “I know that’s a stupid reason to hate a book, but I can’t help it. Every time I crack those pages, I can’t help thinking how much easier my life would be if my mom was a little bit less…”（ch21）
- 同义；与 Our life together—mine and Emily’s—was a small one. It had to be.（ch34）
斯隆的处境完全同构。

⑰ Once upon a time, there was a boy who loved his mother very, very much. His love for her was so great that when she lay on her deathbed, clutching her son’s hand with all the strength that remained in her racked, wrecked body, she extracted a promise from him.（ch22）

**中文**：从前有一个男孩，非常非常爱他的母亲。他的爱如此之大，以至于他照她说的做了——不是立刻，也不是用什么像样的手段。
**上下文**：格雷格讲自己母亲的故事，形式是一个童话。
**为什么重要**：全书最重的一个假借——他用一个童话来讲一件真事，而这个童话是他从这位老人书架上学的。书会第一次证明它能反过来救人。
**呼应关系**：与 It was the first time I could remember stepping out of my mother’s shadow and falling into one of my own.（ch16）他关于自己幼年的另一段回忆构成一组；两个被母亲塑形的人在同一张桌上互相认了出来。

⑱ Not once had he asked if I was doing okay.（ch24）

**中文**：他一次也没问过我过得好不好。
**上下文**：他刚替祖父在公共场合挡了一句话。
**为什么重要**：全章最痛的一句，而它是一份清单的浓缩：地址、收入、有没有地方住，再加上这一行。作者让一个刚学会「先动手」的人，撞上他全部动作都没能解决的那件事。
**呼应关系**：与 “What did her voice sound like?” I asked, since it seemed like the only way I could repay her.（ch27）他终于问出的那个问题相隔三章——而那个问题他先问的是死去的祖母。

⑲ “In a heartbeat. It’s the only place I’ve ever really belonged.”（ch25）

**中文**：一秒钟。是啊，那是我这辈子唯一真正属于过的地方。
**上下文**：她在被问及那所学院时脱口而出的一句话——那时她已经丢掉了图书馆那份工作。
**为什么重要**：她刚被解雇，失去了所有能称为「自己的」东西，而她给出的答案是那栋不属于她的楼。
**呼应关系**：与 It was still the only thing approaching a home I had.（ch26）他承认同一件事构成一组——两个互不相关的人，用同一句话描述了同一个地方。

⑳ It was still the only thing approaching a home I had.（ch26）

**中文**：它仍然是我唯一接近过一个家的东西。
**上下文**：他刚被祖父当着所有人的面彻底否定。
**为什么重要**：全书最深的一处让步，而作者用一个降级（唯一接近过）来克制那一句最强的话。他在这里第一次承认自己走不掉了。
**呼应关系**：与 Our life together—mine and Emily’s—was a small one. It had to be.（ch34）那八年构成一组；两处都是「小到不可能是家」的东西。

㉑ “She had a congenital heart defect,” she added before I could ask. “She was born with an early expiration date.”（ch27）

**中文**：「她有先天性心脏缺陷，」她在我开口问之前接了下去。「她生下来就带着一个早到的有效期。」
**上下文**：他们在交换各自死掉的那个人的细节——声音、气味、回忆。
**为什么重要**：全书最能被记住的一句，因为它把死亡写成出厂设置而不是意外。而这个比喻在此前的二十六章里从未出现过。
**呼应关系**：
- 与 “My grandmother died young. Too young, a few weeks after my mom turned twelve.” From breast cancer, but I didn’t want to say that part out loud. Too many women in my family had fallen on that particular sword. “It would’ve been tragic for any little girl, but my mom was left alone in that house with Arthur. For six whole years, it was just the two of them facing their grief alone.”（ch23）
- 他祖母的早逝构成同一条线；与 There, in bright orange, stood the words I’d never before taken the time to appreciate—the ones promising that life would go on, that joy was still to be had in the world. They were the words my sister had tried so hard to instill in me all those years ago, the words I’d been too young and too heartbroken to understand. It had taken twenty years and a stranger with an orange highlighter to finally break through.（ch35）
那段被划线的文字是它的下游。

㉒ Life stories were written in ink, not pencil. Once they were down, the only thing you could do was turn the page.（ch28）

**中文**：生命故事是用墨水写的，不是铅笔。写下来之后，你唯一能做的就是翻页。
**上下文**：他用自己的出生、童年和那个他至今没读懂的妹妹，开场了自己的自传。
**为什么重要**：全书最好的一句，而它来自一个一辈子想改写自己人生的人。作者让他用一句话承认改不了，然后用这句话转入当下。
**呼应关系**：与 It was time for me to answer.（ch35）构成一组：一个说翻页，一个说回答。

㉓ It behooved me to put this woman in her place, but my heart wasn’t in that, either. I’d never realized before how much effort it took to keep up all that bluster—to fight everything and everyone, to treat every square on the pavement like a battleground. If I looked anything like Nigel Carthage these days, my energy could be expended on much better places.（ch29）

**中文**：我以前从没意识到，要维持那副虚张声势有多费力——要跟所有事所有人对着干，要把人行道上每一块方砖都当战场。
**上下文**：他刚在护理公司当面道过歉，正被一个陌生人教怎么说话。
**为什么重要**：全书最重要的一次自我降格。作者把「性格」重写成了一份体力活，而这一章他第一次为它感到疲惫。
**呼应关系**：与 And much better people.（ch29）「以及好得多的人」是同一句话的两半；一个说不如奈杰尔，一个说还不如更好的那些人。

㉔ Each of those words cut through me like the swing of a scythe. Those were the worst three words I’d ever heard used to describe anyone, let alone the man Sloane was a few short months away from chaining herself to forever. I was no phone psychic, but even I could see what the future held for her.（ch30）

**中文**：那三个词每一个都像镰刀挥过来一样割在我身上。那是我听过的最糟糕的三个词。
**上下文**：斯隆当着她母亲的面，用三个褒义词评价她要嫁的人。
**为什么重要**：全章唯一一次他用了一个身体意象。作者让他用农具（镰刀）而不是刀，因为镰刀割的是成熟的作物——而这一章他正在被收割。
**呼应关系**：与 “What kind of kid does that?” I asked. “What kind of person? I spent three days down there, Lincoln. I could have died. And all I cared about was how nice it was to look up and see the sky.”（ch21）她对自己婚姻的诊断属于同一条判断；两处的结论完全一致。

㉕ She was in every book I’d ever read, every tale that had ever touched my heart. Fiction and nonfiction, memoir and short story—no matter what I read, I always found her. It was why I resorted so often to thrillers and German philosophy in my declining years, even though Sloane did her best to steer me away from them.（ch31）

**中文**：她在我读过的每一本书里，在每一个曾经触到我心的故事里。
**上下文**：他解释自己为什么一辈子用借来的句子说话。
**为什么重要**：全书最动人的一次承认，而「她」指的是那个跟人走了、之后再没被他提起名字的女人。作者让亡妻的姓名在这一句里一次都没出现。
**呼应关系**：
- 与 “Please. You know as well as I do that the only hallowed ground in our family is wherever she’s standing.” I paused, wanting to take a moment to corral my thoughts but afraid of what else Lincoln might say if I left him the space. “I know that’s a stupid reason to hate a book, but I can’t help it. Every time I crack those pages, I can’t help thinking how much easier my life would be if my mom was a little bit less…”（ch21）
- 是同一件事的两种说法；而 “I’ve seen the books around your house, Arthur. All of them. I know what they mean. I know what they’re trying to say.”（ch35）
是它的反面——有人终于把这件事说成了句子。
