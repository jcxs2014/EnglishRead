# ch23. Paris: The First Year

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1714-1715 年，Addie 在巴黎的第一年——贫穷、卖淫、失去木鸟、瘟疫中差点死去（但被扔进尸车），她学会了生存。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 —— Addie 的最低点，她几乎放弃，但拒绝向 Luc 投降。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：Darkest Hour（最黑暗时刻）—— 但她拒绝"投降"（"I decline"在 ch24）。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从天真（相信巴黎是梦想）到冷酷（学会偷窃、卖淫、生存）。
- **叙事手法**：时间压缩（一年在一章中）；Palimpsest 概念 = 巴黎的双重面貌。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Heat hangs like a low roof over Paris. The August air is heavy, made heavier still by the sprawl of stone buildings, the reek of rotting food and human refuse, the sheer number of bodies living shoulder to shoulder.

热像低屋顶一样悬挂在巴黎上方。八月空气沉重，被石建筑的蔓延、腐烂食物和人类垃圾的臭气、肩并肩生活的身体数量弄得更加沉重。

**关键词**：Heat hangs like a low roof / reek of rotting food / human refuse / sheer number of bodies / shoulder to shoulder

**为什么这样写**：巴黎的首次印象。"Heat hangs like a low roof" —— 热 = 压迫。"reek of rotting food" —— 腐烂 = 巴黎的底层。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 梦想的巴黎 vs. 现实的巴黎。

---

> **原句 2:** In a hundred and fifty years, Haussman will set his mark upon the city, raise a uniform facade and paint the buildings in the same pale palette, creating a testament to art, and evenness, and beauty. That is the kind of Paris Addie dreamed of, and one she will certainly live to see. But right now, the poor pile themselves in ragged heaps while silk-finished nobles stroll through gardens.

一百五十年后，Haussman 将在城市留下他的印记，竖起统一的立面，用相同的淡色调粉刷建筑，创造艺术、均匀和美的见证。那是 Addie 梦想的巴黎，她肯定会活着看到。但现在，穷人把自己堆成破破烂烂的堆，而丝绸装饰的贵族漫步穿过花园。

**关键词**：Haussman / uniform facade / pale palette / testament to art / ragged heaps / silk-finished nobles / stroll through gardens

**为什么这样写**：巴黎的双重面貌。"Haussman" —— 未来的巴黎。"ragged heaps" vs. "silk-finished nobles" —— 贫富对比。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 看到的巴黎 vs. 她梦想的巴黎。

---

> **原句 3:** Four sols, to make a life.

四个 sol，创造一个生活。

**关键词**：Four sols / make a life

**为什么这样写**：短句独立成段。"Four sols" —— 四个 sol = 她的全部。"make a life" —— 创造生活 = 她的目标。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"四个 sol" = 她三百年贫穷的开始。

---

> **原句 4:** "This is no brothel," chides the owner, glaring down his nose. "And I'm no whore," she answers, but he only sneers, and flicks his fingers as if casting off some unwanted residue.

"这不是妓房，"店主斥责，从鼻子上方瞪着她。"我也不是妓女，"她回答，但他只是冷笑，弹掉手指，仿佛弹掉一些不需要的残留物。

**关键词**：brothel / whore / glaring down his nose / sneers / flicks his fingers / casting off / unwanted residue

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的第一次被拒绝。"casting off some unwanted residue" —— 她被视为残留物 = 她被当作垃圾。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"unwanted residue" = 她三百年被驱逐的开始。

---

> **原句 5:** "Thirty-four years I've run this lodge," she cuts in, "and never yet forgotten a face." It is a cruel joke, thinks Addie, as the woman turns and shuffles away, leaving her to her rented room.

"我经营这家旅馆三十四年了，"她插嘴，"从未忘记过一张脸。"这是一个残酷的玩笑，Addie 想，当女人转身蹒跚离开，留给她租来的房间。

**关键词**：Thirty-four years / never yet forgotten a face / cruel joke

**为什么这样写**：店主的"never yet forgotten a face" = 残酷的讽刺 —— 她会在几小时内忘记 Addie。"cruel joke" —— 诅咒的残酷玩笑。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"被遗忘"是即时发生的 —— 几小时内，没有人会记得她。

---

> **原句 6:** They don't remember.

他们不记得。

**关键词**：They don't remember

**为什么这样写**：短句独立成段。"They don't remember" —— 这是诅咒的定义，也是本章的核心。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的故事 = "They don't remember" 的重复。

---

> **原句 7:** There were nights, back home, when Addie dreamed of pleasure, when she conjured the stranger beside her in the dark, felt his lips against her breasts, imagined her hand was his as it slipped between her legs.

在家乡有夜晚，当 Addie 梦想快乐时，当她在黑暗中召唤身边的陌生人时，感觉他的嘴唇贴着她的乳房，想象他的手是他的，当它滑入她的双腿之间。

**关键词**：dreamed of pleasure / conjured the stranger / felt his lips / imagined her hand was his / slipped between her legs

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"梦" vs. "现实"。"conjured the stranger" —— 她召唤陌生人 = 她创造了 Luc。"slipped between her legs" —— 快乐 vs. 卖淫的痛苦。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"梦" = 她与陌生人的想象关系 —— 这与她在码头卖淫的现实形成对比。

---

> **原句 8:** But this is nothing like that. There is no poetry to this unknown man's grunts, no melody or harmony, save the steady noise of thrusting as he pushes himself against her. No rolling pleasure, only pressure, and pain, the tightness of one thing being forced inside another, and Addie looks up at the night sky so she won't have to look at his body moving, and she feels the darkness looking back.

但这完全不像那样。这个陌生人的咕哝没有诗意，没有旋律或和谐，除了他推向他自己的持续噪音。没有滚动的快乐，只有压力和痛苦，一个东西被迫进入另一个的紧绷，Addie 抬头看夜空，这样她就不必看他移动的身体，她感觉黑暗在回望。

**关键词**：nothing like that / no poetry / no melody or harmony / steady noise of thrusting / rolling pleasure / pressure, and pain / forced inside another / darkness looking back

**为什么这样写**：卖淫的现实 vs. 梦想的对比。"no poetry" —— 没有诗意。"darkness looking back" —— 黑暗（Luc）在看她。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"darkness looking back" = Luc 在码头看着她 —— 他知道她在做什么。

---

> **原句 9:** She does not think she will ever be warm again. Twice more she has gone to the docks, but the cold has forced the callers in, to the warm shelters of brothels, and around her, the cold snap has turned Paris cruel. The rich board themselves up inside their homes, cling to their hearth fires, while out in the streets, the poor are whittled down by winter.

她不认为她会再暖和起来。她又去了两次码头，但寒冷把客人逼进了妓房的温暖庇护所，在她周围，寒流把巴黎变得残忍。富人在家里把自己封起来，依偎着壁炉火，而在街上，穷人被冬天削减。

**关键词**：ever be warm again / cold snap / turned Paris cruel / board themselves up / cling to their hearth fires / whittled down by winter

**为什么这样写**：巴黎的冬天。"cold snap" —— 寒流 = 残忍。"whittled down by winter" —— 冬天削减穷人 = 穷人死去。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"ever be warm again" = 她三百年不会再暖和 —— 诅咒让她永远冷。

---

> **原句 10:** This is death. At least, for an instant, Addie thinks it must be death. The world is dark, the cold unable to hold back the reek of rot, and she cannot move. But then, she remembers, she cannot die. There is her stubborn pulse, fighting to beat, and her stubborn lungs, fighting to fill, and Addie realizes her limbs aren't lifeless at all, but weighted down on every side. Heavy sacks above, below, and panic flutters through her, but her mind is still sluggish with sleep. She twists, and the sacks shift a little on top of her. The dark splits, and a sliver of gray light shines through. Addie writhes and wriggles until she frees one arm and then the other, drawing them in against her body. She begins to push up through the sacks, and only then does she feel bones beneath the cloth, only then does her hand meet waxy skin, only then do her fingers tangle in the strands of someone else's hair, and now she is awake, so awake, scrambling, tearing, desperate to get free. She claws her way up, and out, hands splayed across the bony mound of a dead man's back. Nearby, milky eyes stare up at her. A jaw hangs open, and Addie stumbles out of the cart and collapses to the ground, retching, sobbing, alive.

这是死亡。至少，有一瞬间，Addie 想这一定是死亡。世界是黑暗的，寒冷无法阻止腐烂的臭气，她不能动弹。但后来，她记得，她不能死。她那顽固的脉搏，挣扎着跳动，她那顽固的肺，挣扎着充气，Addie 意识到她的肢体根本不是没有生命，而是每边都被重物压着。上面、下面都是沉重的袋子，恐慌在她体内扑腾，但她的头脑仍然因睡眠而迟钝。她扭动，袋子在她身上转移一点。黑暗分裂，一线灰光射入。Addie 扭动和蠕动直到她自由一只胳膊然后另一只，把它们拉向她的身体。她开始从袋子中推上去，只有那时她才感觉到布下面的骨头，只有她的手才碰到蜡状的皮肤，只有她的手指才缠绕在别人的头发上，现在她醒了，如此醒，爬行，撕裂，拼命挣脱。她爬上出来，双手摊开在一个死人背部的骨堆上。附近，乳白色的眼睛向上盯着她。下巴垂下，Addie 从车里跌出来，倒在地上，干呕，哭泣，活着。

**关键词**：This is death / stubborn pulse / stubborn lungs / weighted down / Heavy sacks / sliver of gray light / writhes and wriggles / bones beneath the cloth / waxy skin / tangle in the strands / bony mound / milky eyes / retching, sobbing, alive

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"死亡体验"。"stubborn pulse" —— 顽固的脉搏 = 不朽。"bony mound of a dead man's back" —— 死人背部的骨堆 = 她从死亡中爬出。"alive" —— 她活着，但不知道为什么。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"alive" = 她的诅咒 —— 她不能死，即使她想。

---

> **原句 11:** The little wooden bird is gone. The last of her past life, carried away with the dead. For months, she will keep reaching for the bird, hand drifting to her pocket the way it might to a stubborn curl, a motion born of so much habit. She cannot seem to remind her fingers it is gone, cannot seem to remind her heart, which stutters a little every time she finds the pocket empty. But, there, blooming amid the sorrow, is a terrible relief. Every moment since she left Villon, she has feared the loss of this last token. Now that it is gone, there is a guilty gladness tucked among the grief. This last, brittle thread to her old life has broken, and Addie has been set well, and truly, and forcibly free.

小木鸟不见了。她过去生活的最后一样，与死人一起被带走。几个月来，她会继续伸手找鸟，手飘向她的口袋，就像它可能飘向一个顽固的卷发，一个由这么多习惯产生的动作。她似乎无法提醒她的手指它不见了，似乎无法提醒她的心脏，每次她发现口袋空虚时，它都会微微停滞。但是，在悲伤中绽放，有一种可怕的解脱。自从她离开 Villon 的每一刻，她都害怕失去这最后一个信物。现在它不见了，在悲伤中有一种内疚的喜悦。这最后一根脆弱的线已经断了，Addie 已经被彻底地、真正地、强制地释放了。

**关键词**：The little wooden bird is gone / carried away with the dead / reaching for the bird / stubborn curl / motion born of so much habit / remind her fingers / stutters a little / blooming amid the sorrow / terrible relief / guilty gladness / tucked among the grief / brittle thread / set well, and truly, and forcibly free

**为什么这样写**：木鸟的失去。"blooming amid the sorrow, is a terrible relief" —— 悲伤中的解脱 = 失去的解脱。"brittle thread" —— 脆弱的线 = 她与过去的最后联系。"forcibly free" —— 被强制释放 = 她真正自由了。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"forcibly free" = 她三百年流浪的开始 —— 她与过去的最后联系断了。

## 一句话总结

在巴黎的第一年，Addie 学会了生存：偷窃、卖淫、失去木鸟、差点死去。她与过去的最后联系断了，被"强制地释放"进入三百年流浪。
