# ch32. Her Name Is Addie

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Addie 和 Henry 的夜晚继续，他问她名字，她终于说出"Addie"——三百年后第一次有人叫她的名字。
- **情感弧线位置**：转折 —— Addie 的真名被说出，她的存在被确认。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Name（名字）—— 名字是身份，名字是力量。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从隐藏真名到说出"Addie"，她的身份被 Henry 接受。
- **叙事手法**："And yet" 重复 = 诅咒的裂缝；"Goodnight, Addie" = 最美的声音。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Addie can account for every second of her life, but that night, with Henry, the moments seem to bleed together. Time slides by as they bounce from bar to bar, happy hour giving way to dinner and then to late-night drinks, and every time they hit the point where the evening splits, and one road leads their separate ways and the other carries on ahead, they choose the second road.

Addie 可以解释她生命中的每一秒，但那晚，和 Henry 一起，时刻似乎融合在一起。时间滑过，他们从一家酒吧跳到另一家，欢乐时光让位于晚餐然后让位于深夜饮品，每当夜晚分裂的点出现，一条路让他们分开，另一条路继续前进，他们选择第二条路。

**关键词**：account for every second / moments seem to bleed together / Time slides by / bounce from bar to bar / happy hour / giving way to / dinner / late-night drinks / evening splits / separate ways / carries on ahead / choose the second road

**为什么这样写**：时间的"融合"。"moments seem to bleed together" —— 时刻融合 = 时间飞逝。"evening splits" —— 夜晚分裂 = 选择的时刻。"choose the second road" —— 选择第二条路 = 他们选择在一起。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"choose the second road" = 她想和他在一起。

---

> **原句 2:** They stay together, each waiting for the other to say "It's getting late" or "I should be going," or "See you around." There is some unspoken pact, an unwillingness to sever whatever this is, and she knows why she's afraid to break the thread, but she wonders about Henry. Wonders at the loneliness she sees behind his eyes. Wonders at the way the waiters and the bartenders and the other patrons look at him, the warmth he doesn't seem to notice.

他们待在一起，各自等待对方说"很晚了"或"我该走了"，或"回头见"。有某种不言而喻的协议，一种不愿切断无论这是什么，她知道她为什么害怕打破线，但她想知道 Henry。对他眼中看到的孤独感到好奇。对服务员、酒保和其他顾客看他的方式感到好奇，他似乎没有注意到的温暖。

**关键词**：stay together / each waiting / It's getting late / I should be going / See you around / unspoken pact / unwillingness to sever / break the thread / wonders about Henry / loneliness she sees behind his eyes / waiters / bartenders / other patrons / warmth he doesn't seem to notice

**为什么这样写**：Henry 的"孤独"。"unspoken pact" —— 不言而喻的协议 = 他们都不想结束。"loneliness she sees behind his eyes" —— 他眼中的孤独 = 他的抑郁。"warmth he doesn't seem to notice" —— 他似乎没有注意到的温暖 = 他被爱但不自知。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"wonders about Henry" = 她想了解他。

> **原句 3:** And then it is almost midnight, and they are eating cheap pizza, walking side by side through the first warm night of spring, as the clouds stretch overhead, low and lit by the moon. She looks up, and so does Henry, and for a moment, only a moment, he looks overwhelmingly, unbearably sad. "I miss the stars," he says. "So do I," she says, and his gaze drops back to her, and he smiles. "Who are you?" His eyes have gone glassy, and the way he says who almost sounds like how, less a question of how she's doing and more a question of how she's here, and she wants to ask him the same thing, but she has a good reason, and he's just a little drunk. And simply, perfectly, normal. But he can't be normal. Because normal people don't remember her.

然后几乎午夜了，他们在吃廉价的披萨，并肩走在春天的第一个温暖的夜晚，云朵在头顶伸展，低低的，被月亮照亮。她抬头，Henry 也抬头，有一刻，只有一刻，他看起来无法忍受地悲伤。"我想念星星，"他说。"我也是，"她说，他的目光回到她身上，他笑了。"你是谁？"他的眼睛变得朦胧，他说 who 的方式听起来几乎像 how，不是问她做得怎么样，更多的是问她怎么在这里，她想问他同样的事，但她有充分的理由，他只是有点醉了。而简单、完美地正常。但他不能正常。因为正常人不记得她。

> **原句 4:** "I want to see you again," says Henry. The hope fills her chest until it hurts. She's heard those words a hundred times, but for the first time, they feel real. Possible. "I want you to see me again, too." Henry smiles, the kind of smile that takes over an entire face. He pulls out his cell, and Addie's heart sinks. She tells him that her phone is broken, when the truth is, she's never needed one before. Even if she had someone to call, she could not call them. Her fingers would slip uselessly over the screen. She has no e-mail, either, no way to send a message of any kind, thanks to the whole thou-shalt-not-write part of her curse.

"我想再见到你，"Henry 说。希望填满她的胸口直到它痛。她听过那些话一百次，但第一次，它们感觉真实。可能。"我也想让你再见到我。"Henry 笑了，那种占据整个脸的微笑。他拿出手机，Addie 的心沉了下去。她告诉他她的手机坏了，而事实是，她以前从未需要过。即使她有可以打电话的人，她也打不了。她的手指会在屏幕上无助地滑动。她也没有电子邮件，没有任何方式发送任何消息，感谢她诅咒中不可写的那部分。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"thou-shalt-not-write" = 她的诅咒包括不能写。

> **原句 5:** "My real name isn't Eve." He pulls back, confused. "… okay." She doesn't know if she can say it, if the curse will let her, but she has to try. "I didn't tell you my real name because, well—it's complicated. But I like you, and I want you to know—to hear it from me." Henry straightens, sobering. "Well then, what is it?" "It's A—" The sound lodges, for just a second, the stiffness of a muscle long since fallen to disuse. A rusty cog. And then—it scrapes free. "Addie." She swallows, hard. "My name's Addie."

"我的真名不是 Eve。"他退后，困惑。"……好。"她不知道她是否能说，诅咒是否会让她，但她必须尝试。"我没有告诉你我的真名，因为，好吧——这很复杂。但我喜欢你，我想让你知道——从我这里听到。"Henry 挺直身体，清醒。"那么，它是什么？""是 A——"声音卡住，仅仅一秒钟，一块长期废弃的肌肉的僵硬。一个生锈的齿轮。然后——它刮自由了。"Addie。"她吞咽，困难。"我叫 Addie。"

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"真名"。"It's A——" 是 A—— = 她的名字卡在喉咙里。"sound lodges" —— 声音卡住 = 诅咒。"rusty cog" —— 生锈的齿轮 = 她的声带。"scrapes free" —— 刮自由了 = 她说出了名字。"My name's Addie" —— 我叫 Addie = 她的身份。

> **原句 6:** It hangs in the air between them. And then Henry smiles. "Well, okay," he says. "Goodnight, Addie." As simple as that. Two syllables falling from a tongue. And it's the best sound she's ever heard. She wants to throw her arms around him, wants to hear it again, and again, the impossible word filling her like air, making her feel solid. Real. "Goodnight, Henry," Addie says, willing him to turn and go, because she doesn't think she can bring herself to turn away from him. She stands there, rooted to the spot at the top of the subway steps until he's out of sight, holds her breath and waits to feel the thread snap, the world shudder back into shape, waits for the fear and the loss and the knowledge that it was just a fluke, a cosmic error, a mistake, that it is over now, that it will never happen again. But she doesn't feel any of those things. All she feels is joy, and hope. Her boot heels tap out a rhythm on the street, and even after all these years, she half expects a second pair of shoes to fall in step beside her own. To hear the rolling fog of his voice, soft, and sweet, and mocking. But there is no shadow at her side, not tonight. The evening is quiet, and she is alone, but for once it is not the same as being lonely. Goodnight, Addie, Henry said, and Addie cannot help but wonder if he has somehow broken the spell. She smiles, and whispers to herself. "Goodnight, Ad—" But the curse closes around her throat, the name lodging there, as it always has. And yet. And yet. Goodnight, Addie. Three hundred years she's tested the confines of her deal, found the places where it gives, the subtle bend and flex around the bars, but never a way out. And yet. Somehow, impossibly, Henry has found a way in. Somehow, he remembers her. How? How? The question thuds with the drum of her heart, but in this moment, Addie does not care. In this moment, she is holding to the sound of her name, her real name, on someone else's tongue, and it is enough, it is enough, it is enough.

它在他们之间的空气中悬挂。然后 Henry 笑了。"嗯，好吧，"他说。"晚安，Addie。"就这么简单。两个音节从舌头上落下。这是她听过的最美的声音。她想搂住他，想再听一遍，一遍遍，这个不可能的词像空气一样填满她，让她感觉坚实。真实。"晚安，Henry，"Addie 说，愿意他转身离开，因为她不认为自己能让自己转身离开他。她站在那里，扎根在地铁台阶的顶部直到他消失，屏住呼吸等待感觉线断裂、世界颤抖回原位，等待恐惧和失去和知识那只是一个侥幸、宇宙错误、一个失误，它结束了，它再也不会发生。但她感觉不到那些东西。她只感到喜悦和希望。她的靴子在街上敲出节奏，即使这么多年后，她几乎期待第二双鞋子落在她自己的步伐旁边。听到他声音的滚滚雾气，柔软、甜美、嘲弄。但她身边没有影子，今晚没有。夜晚很安静，她孤独，但这一次不等于孤独。晚安，Addie，Henry 说，Addie 不禁想知道他是否以某种方式打破了诅咒。她微笑，低声对自己说。"晚安，Ad——"但诅咒闭上她的喉咙，名字卡在那里，一如既往。然而。然而。晚安，Addie。三百年她测试了她的契约的限制，找到它给予的地方，栏杆周围微妙的弯曲和弯曲，但从未有出路。然而。不知何故，不可能地，Henry 找到了一种方式进来。不知何故，他记得她。怎么？怎么？这个问题随着她心脏的鼓声砰然作响，但此刻，Addie 不在乎。此刻，她抓住她的名字的声音，她的真名，在别人的舌头上，这就够了，这就够了，这就够了。
