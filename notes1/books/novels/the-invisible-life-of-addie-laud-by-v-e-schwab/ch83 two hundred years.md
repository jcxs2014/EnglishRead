# ch83. Two Hundred Years

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1899年英格兰科茨沃尔德，Addie 在雪中玩耍，Luc 出现——"两百年了，还表现得像个孩子"——他们像朋友一样坐着喝酒——"因为我很快乐。我不信任你会带我回来。"
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 — 从"平静"到"快乐"到"相遇"到"选择"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Snow（雪）—— 她第一次不留痕迹地玩耍；The Meeting（相遇）—— Luc 出现 = 他的选择 = 她的选择。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"平静"到"快乐"—— 她选择留下 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。
- **叙事手法**："unmarked paper" = 未标记的纸 = 雪 = 她的存在 = 她的痕迹 = 她的自由。

在下雪。不是一层霜，或几片任性的雪花，而是一片白色。Addie 蜷缩在小屋的窗口，背对火，膝上摊开一本书，看着天空坠落。

> **原句 2:** She has ushered in the change of years so many ways. Perched on London rooftops holding bottles of Champagne, and torch in hand through the cobbled roads of Edinburgh. She has danced in the halls of Paris, and watched the sky go white with fireworks in Amsterdam. She has kissed strangers, and sung of friends she'll never meet. Gone out with bangs and with whispers. But tonight she is content to sit, and watch the world go white beyond the window, every line and curve erased by snow.

她用了很多方式迎接岁月的变化。栖息在伦敦屋顶上拿着香槟瓶，手持火炬穿过爱丁堡的鹅卵石路。她曾在巴黎的大厅里跳舞，在阿姆斯特丹看过天空变白。她吻过陌生人，唱过永远不会见的朋友。用砰砰声和低语声离去。但今晚她满足于坐着，看着窗外的世界变白，每一条线和曲线都被雪抹去。

> **原句 3:** The snow lies quiet on the ground. As smooth and clean as unmarked paper. Perhaps that is what drives her to her feet. She pulls the cloak tight around her shoulders and surges out, boots sinking instantly into the snow. It is light, whipped into a sugar film, the taste of winter on her tongue. Once, when she was five, or six, it snowed back in Villon. A rare sight, a film of white several inches deep that coated everything. In hours, it was ruined by horses and carts, and people trudging to and fro, but Addie found a small expanse of untouched white. She rushed out into it, leaving a trail of shoes. She ran bare hands over the frozen sheets, left fingers in her wake. She ruined every inch of the canvas. And when she was done, she looked around at the field, now covered in tracks, and mourned that it was over. The next day, the frost broke, and the ice melted, and it was the last time she played in snow. Until now. Now, her steps crunch the perfect snow, and it rises in her wake. Now, she runs her fingers through the gentle hills, and they smooth behind her touch. Now she plays in the field, and does not leave a mark. The world remains unblemished, and for once she is grateful.

雪静静地躺在地上。像未标记的纸一样光滑干净。也许这就是驱使她站起来的原因。她收紧斗篷，冲出去，靴子立刻陷入雪中。它很轻，搅打成一层糖膜，冬天的味道在她的舌头上。有一次，当她五六岁时，维隆下过雪。罕见的景象，几英寸深的白色覆盖一切。几个小时内，它被马和车，以及来回蹂躏的人们毁坏了，但 Addie 找到了一小片未触碰的白色。她冲出去，留下一串鞋印。她用手跑过冰冻的床单，在身后留下手指。她毁了画布的每一寸。当她完成后，她看着现在布满痕迹的田野，哀悼它结束了。第二天，霜冻破裂，冰融化了，那是她最后一次在雪中玩耍。直到现在。现在，她的脚步嘎吱作响完美的雪，它在她身后升起。现在，她的手指穿过柔和的山丘，它们在她的触摸下变得光滑。现在她在田野里玩耍，不留痕迹。世界保持无瑕，这一次她感激。

**关键词**：unmarked paper / canvas / mark / unblemished / grateful

**为什么这样写**："unmarked paper" = 未标记的纸 = 雪 = 她的存在 = 她的痕迹 = 她的自由。"unblemished" = 无瑕的 = 她的存在 = 她的痕迹 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。

> **原句 4:** She sinks into the snow, lets it swallow the edges of her sight, until there is nothing but a frame around the open sky, the night cold and clear and full of stars. And she is ten again, stretched in the tall grass behind her father's workshop, dreaming she is anywhere but home. How strange, the winding way a dream comes true. But now, gazing up into the endless dark, she does not think of freedom, but of him. And then, he's there. Standing over her, haloed by the dark, and she thinks perhaps she is going mad again. It would not be the first time. "Two hundred years," Luc says, kneeling beside her, "and still behaving like a child."

她沉入雪中，让它吞没她视线的边缘，直到只有开放天空周围的一个框架，夜晚寒冷而清澈，布满星星。她又十岁了，躺在她父亲工作室后的高草里，梦想着除了家以外的任何地方。多么奇怪，梦想成真的蜿蜒之路。但现在，凝视着无尽的黑暗，她想的不是自由，而是他。然后，他在那里。站在她身上，被黑暗光晕包围，她想也许她又疯了。这不会是第一次。"两百年了，"Luc 跪在她身边说，"还表现得像个孩子。"

**关键词**：frame / stars / child / two hundred years

**为什么这样写**："frame" = 框架 = 她的视线 = 她的存在 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。"two hundred years" = 两百年 = 她的年龄 = 她的诅咒 = 她的自由。

> **原句 5:** "I saw an elephant, in Paris." Her words to him, so many years before. It is such a strange answer now, filled with unspoken things. I saw an elephant, and thought of you. I was in Paris, and you were not. "And you thought of me," she says. It is a question. He does not answer. Instead, he looks around and says, "This is a pitiful way to usher out a year. We can do better. Come with me." And she is curious—she is always curious—but tonight, she shakes her head. "No." That proud chin lifts. Those dark brows draw together. "Why not?" Addie shrugs. "Because I'm happy here. And I do not trust you to bring me back."

"我在巴黎看到了一头大象。"她多年前对他说的。现在这是一个多么奇怪的回答，充满了未说出的东西。我看到了一头大象，想起了你。我在巴黎，你不在。""而你想到了我，"她说。这是一个问题。他没有回答。相反，他环顾四周说，"这是一种可悲的方式结束一年。我们可以做得更好。跟我走。"她很好奇——她总是很好奇——但今晚，她摇头。"不。"那骄傲的下巴抬起。那些黑眉皱在一起。"为什么不？"Addie 耸肩。"因为我很快乐。我不信任你会带我回来。"

**关键词**：elephant / happy / trust / bring me back

**为什么这样写**："elephant" = 大象 = 她的记忆 = 她的存在 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。"happy" = 快乐 = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"我很快乐" = 她的选择 = 她的力量 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

> **原句 6:** He conjures cups of wine from nothing, and they sit before the fire like friends, or at least, like foes at rest, and he tells her of Paris at the close of a decade—the turn of the century. Of the writers, blooming like flowers, of the art, and the music, and the beauty. He has always known how to tempt her. He says it is a golden age, a time of light. "You would enjoy it," he says. "I'm sure I would." She will go, in the spring, and see the World's Fair, witness the Eiffel Tower, the iron sculpture climbing toward the sky. She will walk through buildings made of glass, ephemeral installations, and everyone will talk of the old century and the new one, as if there is a line in the sand between present and past. As if it does not all exist together. History is a thing designed in retrospect. For now, she listens to him talk, and it is enough. She does not remember drifting off, but when she wakes, it is early in the morning, and the cottage is empty, the fire little more than embers. A blanket has been cast over her shoulders, and beyond the window, the world is white again. And Addie will wonder if he was ever there.

他从虚无中变出酒杯，他们像朋友一样坐在火前，或者至少，像休息中的敌人，他告诉她巴黎在十年结束之际——世纪的转折。像花朵一样盛开的作家，艺术，音乐，和美丽。他总是知道如何诱惑她。他说这是一个黄金时代，一个光明的时代。"你会喜欢的，"他说。"我肯定我会的。"她将在春天去，看世界博览会，见证埃菲尔铁塔，铁雕塑攀向天空。她将穿过玻璃建筑，短暂装置，每个人都会谈论旧世纪和新世纪，好像现在和过去之间有一条沙线。好像它们不是一起存在的。历史是一种事后设计的东西。现在，她听他说话，这就够了。她不记得自己睡着了，但当她醒来时，是清晨，小屋是空的，火只是余烬。一条毯子被披在她的肩膀上，窗外，世界又白了。Addie 会想他是否曾在那里。

**关键词**：wine / friends / foes / golden age / Eiffel Tower / glass / history / retrospect / blanket

**为什么这样写**："foes at rest" = 休息中的敌人 = 他们的关系 = 她的诅咒 = 她的爱 = 她的自由。"golden age" = 黄金时代 = 她的存在 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"历史是一种事后设计的东西" = 她的存在 = 她的记忆 = 她的自由 = 她的爱。
