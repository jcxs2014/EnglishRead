# ch27. The Tailor and the Dark

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1716 年巴黎，Addie 从裁缝店偷衣服，试穿男装但失败，穿上深蓝色裙子离开，在街上遇到 Luc——他提出让她投降，她拒绝。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷中的反抗 —— Addie 告诉 Luc 她看到的奇迹（大象、小提琴、香槟），拒绝他的"投降"提议。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：Deal with the Devil（魔鬼交易）的逆转 —— 她两年前接受了交易，现在她拒绝"投降"。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从恐惧（遇到 Luc）到反抗（"I saw an elephant"），她的倔强战胜了 Luc。
- **叙事手法**：Addie 的"男装"尝试 = 她想成为男人（自由）但失败；Luc 的"mask"（面具）= 他的人类形态是假的。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** She stands in the shade of a silk merchant. Across the way, the tailor's shop bustles, the pace of business brisk even as the day wears on. Sweat drips down her neck as she unties and reties the bonnet, salvaged from a gust of wind, hoping the cloth cap will be enough to pass her off as a lady's maid, to grant her the kind of invisibility reserved for help. If he thinks her a maid, Bertin will not look too close. If he thinks her a maid, he might not notice Addie's dress, which is simple but fine, slipped from a tailor's model a week before, in a similar shop across the Seine.

她站在丝绸商的阴影中。对面，裁缝店熙熙攘攘，生意的节奏轻快，即使一天在流逝。汗水从她脖子上滴下，她解开和重新系好帽子，从一阵风中抢救出来，希望布帽足够让她冒充女仆，赋予她那种为仆人保留的看不见。如果他认为她是女仆，Bertin 不会看得太近。如果他认为他是女仆，他可能不会注意到 Addie 的裙子，简单但精致，一周前从塞纳河对面的一家类似商店的裁缝模型上滑下来的。

**关键词**：shade of a silk merchant / bustles / pace of business brisk / Sweat drips / unties and reties the bonnet / salvaged from a gust of wind / pass her off as a lady's maid / invisibility reserved for help / slipped from a tailor's model

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"伪装"。"pass her off as a lady's maid" —— 伪装成女仆 = 隐身。"invisibility reserved for help" —— 为仆人保留的看不见 = 仆人是隐形的。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"invisibility" = 她三百年学会的隐身术。

> **原句 2:** She wishes her clothes were as resistant to change as she appears to be. Especially because she has only the one dress—there's no point collecting a wardrobe, or anything else, when you've nowhere to put it. (She will try, in later years, to gather trinkets, hide them away like a magpie with its nest, but something will always conspire to steal them back. Like the wooden bird, lost among the bodies in the cart. She cannot seem to hold on to much of anything for long.)

她希望她的衣服像她看起来一样抵抗变化。特别是因为她只有一件裙子——当你无处可放时，收集衣柜或任何其他东西都没有意义。（她会在后来的几年里尝试收集小饰品，像喜鹊藏巢一样把它们藏起来，但总会有什么东西合谋把它们偷回来。像木鸟，在尸车的尸体中丢失了。她似乎无法长久抓住任何东西。）

**关键词**：resistant to change / only the one dress / no point collecting a wardrobe / nowhere to put it / gather trinkets / hide them away like a magpie with its nest / conspire to steal them back / wooden bird / lost among the bodies / cannot seem to hold on to much of anything for long

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"衣服"哲学。"resistant to change" —— 衣服不抵抗变化 = 她会变。"magpie with its nest" —— 喜鹊藏巢 = 她藏东西。"cannot seem to hold on to much of anything for long" —— 她无法抓住任何东西 = 诅咒。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"magpie" = 她三百年藏东西，但总被偷。

> **原句 3:** She thinks it is a rather clever lie, but the tailor only frowns, and turns toward a curtain at the back of the shop. "I will get my tape." She catches a brief glimpse of the room beyond, a dozen dress forms, a mountain of silks, before the curtain falls again. But as Bertin slips away, so does she, vanishing between the dress forms and the rolls of muslin and cotton propped against the wall. It is not her first visit to the shop, and she has learned well its crevices and crooks, all the corners large enough to hide in. Addie folds into one such space, and by the time Bertin returns to the front of the shop, the tape in one hand, he has forgotten all about Madame Lautrec and her peculiar maid.

她认为这是一个相当聪明的谎言，但裁缝只是皱眉，转向商店后面的窗帘。"我会拿我的尺子。"她瞥见后面的房间，十几个裁丝模型，一座丝绸山，然后窗帘再次落下。但当 Bertin 溜走时，她也溜走了，消失在裁丝模型和一卷卷棉布之间。这不是她第一次来这家店，她很好地了解了它的缝隙和角落，所有足够大的角落可以躲藏。Addie 折叠进这样一个空间，当 Bertiin 回到商店前面时，手里拿着尺子，他已经完全忘记了 Lautrec 夫人她奇怪的女仆。

**关键词**：clever lie / tailor only frowns / curtain at the back / dozen dress forms / mountain of silks / vanishing between the dress forms / rolls of muslin and cotton / crevices and crooks / corners large enough to hide in / folds into one such space / forgotten all about

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"隐身"。"vanishing between the dress forms" —— 消失在模型之间 = 隐身。"crevices and crooks" —— 缝隙和角落 = 她的藏身之处。"forgotten all about" —— 完全忘记 = 诅咒。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"forgotten all about" = 几秒钟内，Bertin 忘记了她的存在。

> **原句 4:** She feels safe in the armor of their fashion, but when she turns to the mirror, her spirits sink. Her chest is too full, her waist too narrow, her hips flaring to fill the trousers in the wrong place. The jacket helps, a little, but nothing can disguise her face. The bow of her lips, the line of her cheek, the smoothness of her brow, all too soft and round to pass for anything but female.

她穿着他们的时尚盔甲感到安全，但当她转向镜子时，她的精神沉了下去。她的胸部太满，腰部太窄，臀部在错误的地方鼓起来填裤子。夹克有点帮助，但没有什么能伪装她的脸。她嘴唇的弓形，她脸颊的线条，她眉毛的柔软，都太软太圆，不能冒充任何东西除了女性。

**关键词**：armor of their fashion / spirits sink / chest too full / waist too narrow / hips flaring / disguise her face / bow of her lips / line of her cheek / smoothness of her brow / too soft and round / pass for anything but female

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"男装"失败。"armor of their fashion" —— 时尚盔甲 = 男装。"nothing can disguise her face" —— 没有什么能伪装她的脸 = 她是女性。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"pass for anything but female" = 她不能成为男人 = 她不能拥有自由。

> **原句 5:** She takes up a pair of shears, tries to trim the loose coil of her hair to her shoulders, but seconds later, it is back, the locks on the floor swept away by some invisible hand. No mark made, even on herself.

她拿起一把剪刀，试图把她松散的卷发剪到肩膀，但几秒钟后，它回来了，地板上的卷发被一些看不见的手扫走了。没有留下痕迹，即使在她自己身上。

**关键词**：shears / trim the loose coil / seconds later, it is back / locks on the floor / swept away by some invisible hand / No mark made, even on herself

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"头发"。"seconds later, it is back" —— 头发回来了 = 诅咒。"No mark made, even on herself" —— 没有痕迹 = 诅咒。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"No mark made" = 她不能改变自己 = 诅咒。

> **原句 6:** In the end, Addie chooses a dark sapphire dress, its edges trimmed in gray. It reminds her of a storm at night, the clouds blotting out the sky. The silk kisses her skin, the fabric crisp and new and utterly unblemished. It is too fine for her needs, a dress for banquets, for balls, but she does not care. And if it draws strange looks, what of it? They will forget before they have the chance to gossip.

最后，Addie 选择了一件深蓝宝石色的裙子，边缘用灰色修剪。它让她想起夜晚的风暴，云彩遮住了天空。丝绸亲吻她的皮肤，面料清脆、新、完全无瑕。对她的需求来说太精致了，一件宴会、舞会的裙子，但她不在乎。如果它引来奇怪的目光，那又怎样？他们会在有机会八卦之前忘记。

**关键词**：dark sapphire dress / edges trimmed in gray / reminds her of a storm at night / clouds blotting out the sky / silk kisses her skin / fabric crisp and new / utterly unblemished / too fine for her needs / dress for banquets, for balls / draws strange looks / forget before they have the chance to gossip

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"裙子"选择。"dark sapphire dress" —— 深蓝宝石色 = 夜晚。"storm at night" —— 夜晚的风暴 = 她的生活。"utterly unblemished" —— 完全无瑕 = 新开始。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"forget before they have the chance to gossip" = 诅咒 = 没有人会记住她。

> **原句 7:** It is no wonder she didn't see him; dressed in black, from his shoes to his collar, he blends right into the dark. She is already murmuring apologies, already backing away when her gaze lifts, and she sees the line of his jaw, the raven curls, the eyes, so green despite the lack of light. He smiles down at her. "Adeline."

难怪她没有看到他；穿着黑色，从鞋子到衣领，他直接融入黑暗。她已经在低声道歉，已经在后退，当她的目光抬起，她看到他下巴的线条，乌鸦般的卷发，眼睛，尽管缺乏光线却如此绿。他低头对她笑。"Adeline。"

**关键词**：dressed in black / blends right into the dark / murmuring apologies / backing away / line of his jaw / raven curls / eyes, so green despite the lack of light / smiles down at her / Adeline

**为什么这样写**：Luc 的"出现"。"blends right into the dark" —— 融入黑暗 = 他是黑暗。"raven curls" —— 乌鸦般的卷发 = 黑色。"Adeline" —— 她的真名 = 他的权力。

**读者视角提示**：Luc 的"Adeline" = 他知道她的真名，这是他的权力。

> **原句 8:** "You can take any shape you please, isn't that right?" His head tips down. "It is." "Then change," she says. "I cannot bear to look at you." A rueful smile. "I rather like this form. I think you do as well." "I did once," she says. "But you have ruined it for me."

"你可以采取你喜欢的任何形状，不是吗？"他的头低下来。"是的。""那就改变，"她说。"我不能忍受看你。"一个苦涩的微笑。"我相当喜欢这个形态。我想你也一样。""我曾经喜欢，"她说。"但你为我毁了它。"

**关键词**：take any shape you please / Then change / I cannot bear to look at you / rueful smile / I rather like this form / I think you do as well / I did once / you have ruined it for me

**为什么这样写**：Addie 与 Luc 的对话。"take any shape you please" —— 任何形状 = 他是神。"I cannot bear to look at you" —— 不能忍受看他 = 她恨他。"you have ruined it for me" —— 你毁了它 = 他毁了她的梦想。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"you have ruined it for me" = Luc 毁了她对陌生人的梦想。

> **原句 9:** Perhaps she is lonelier than she would say. Perhaps an enemy's company is still better than none. Addie does not take his arm, but she does start walking, and she does not need to look to know that he has fallen in step beside her. His shoes echo softly on the cobblestones, and a faint breeze presses like a palm against her back.

也许她比她愿意承认的更孤独。也许敌人的陪伴仍然比没有好。Addie 没有挽他的手，但她确实开始走路，她不需要看就知道他已经落在她身边。他的鞋子在鹅卵石上轻轻回响，一阵微风像手掌一样压在她的背上。

**关键词**：lonelier than she would say / enemy's company is still better than none / does not take his arm / start walking / fallen in step beside her / shoes echo softly / cobblestones / faint breeze / presses like a palm against her back

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"孤独"。"enemy's company is still better than none" —— 敌人的陪伴比没有好 = 她的孤独。"fallen in step beside her" —— 落在她身边 = Luc 的陪伴。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"enemy's company" = 她三百年只有 Luc 陪伴。

> **原句 10:** "I saw an elephant," says Addie, and the words are like cold water on coals. The darkness stills beside her, and she continues, gaze fixed on the ramshackle house, and the broken roof, and the open sky above. "Two, in fact. They were in the palace grounds, as part of some display. I didn't know animals could be so large. And there was a fiddler in the square the other day," she presses on, her voice steady, "and his music made me cry. It was the prettiest song I'd ever heard. I had Champagne, drank it straight from the bottle, and watched the sun set over the Seine while the bells rang out from Notre-Dame, and none of it would have happened back in Villon." She turns to look at him. "It has only been two years," she says. "Think of all the time I have, and all the things I'll see."

"我看到了一头大象，"Addie 说，这些话就像冷水在煤上。黑暗在她身边静止，她继续，目光盯着摇摇欲坠的房子，破碎的屋顶，和上方开阔的天空。"事实上是两头。它们在宫殿广场上，作为某种展示的一部分。我不知道动物可以这么大。前几天广场上有一个小提琴手，"她继续，她的声音稳定，"他的音乐让我哭。这是我听过的最美的歌。我喝了香槟，直接从瓶子里喝，看着太阳在塞纳河上落下，而圣母院的钟声响起，这些在 Villon 都不会发生。"她转身看着他。"才两年，"她说。"想想我有的所有时间，和我将看到的所有东西。"

**关键词**：I saw an elephant / cold water on coals / darkness stills / ramshackle house / broken roof / open sky / fiddler in the square / music made me cry / prettiest song / Champagne / drank it straight from the bottle / watched the sun set / bells rang out / none of it would have happened back in Villon / Think of all the time I have / all the things I'll see

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"反抗"。"I saw an elephant" —— 我看到大象 = 我看到了奇迹。"Think of all the time I have" —— 想想我有的时间 = 她不会放弃。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"Think of all the time I have" = 她的不朽是礼物，不是诅咒。

> **原句 11:** Addie grins at the shadow then, a small, feral smile, all teeth, feasting on the way the humor falls from his face. It is a small victory, and yet so sweet, to see him falter, even for an instant.

然后 Addie 对黑暗笑了，一个小的、野性的微笑，全是牙齿，享受幽默从他脸上的落下方式。这是一个小胜利，但如此甜蜜，看到他动摇，即使只是一瞬间。

**关键词**：grins at the shadow / small, feral smile / all teeth / feasting on the way the humor falls from his face / small victory / so sweet / see him falter / even for an instant

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"胜利"。"feral smile" —— 野性的笑 = 她的胜利。"see him falter" —— 看到他动摇 = Luc 输了。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"small victory" = 她第一次让 Luc 动摇。

> **原句 12:** "You think it will get easier," he says. "It will not. You are as good as gone, and every year you live will feel a lifetime, and in every lifetime, you will be forgotten. Your pain is meaningless. Your life is meaningless. The years will be like weights around your ankles. They will crush you, bit by bit, and when you cannot stand it, you will beg me to put you from your misery."

"你认为它会变得更容易，"他说。"它不会。你已经和走了差不多，你活的每一年都会感觉像一生，而在每一生中，你会被遗忘。你的痛苦没有意义。你的生活没有意义。岁月会像脚踝上的重物。它们会压碎你，一点一点，当你无法忍受时，你会求我让你从痛苦中解脱。"

**关键词**：You think it will get easier / It will not / as good as gone / every year you live will feel a lifetime / in every lifetime, you will be forgotten / pain is meaningless / life is meaningless / years will be like weights around your ankles / crush you, bit by bit / beg me to put you from your misery

**为什么这样写**：Luc 的"警告"。"every year you live will feel a lifetime" —— 每一年感觉像一生 = 不朽的痛苦。"weights around your ankles" —— 脚踝上的重物 = 岁月的重量。

**读者视角提示**：Luc 的"beg me to put you from your misery" = 他会等她求他。

> **原句 13:** Addie pulls back to face the darkness, but he is already gone. She stands alone on the narrow road. Inhales a low, unsteady breath, forces it out again, and then straightens, and smooths her skirts, and makes her way into the broken house that, tonight at least, is home.

Addie 后退面对黑暗，但他已经不见了。她独自站在狭窄的路上。吸入一口低沉、不稳定的呼吸，再次强迫它出来，然后拉直，抚平她的裙子，走进那个破碎的房子，至少今晚，是家。

**关键词**：pulls back to face the darkness / already gone / stands alone / narrow road / Inhales a low, unsteady breath / forces it out again / straightens / smooths her skirts / broken house / tonight at least, is home

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"孤独"。"already gone" —— 他已经不见了 = Luc 离开。"tonight at least, is home" —— 至少今晚是家 = 她的家。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"tonight at least, is home" = 她的家是暂时的 = 她没有真正的家。
