# ch36. The Laundromat

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：2014 年纽约，Addie 等待周六与 Henry 的约会，他们去了隐藏在洗衣店后面的秘密酒吧（speakeasy）打弹球。
- **情感弧线位置**：转折后的甜蜜 —— Addie 体验了"正常"的约会。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Date（约会）—— 但这是三百年来第一次。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从紧张（等待）到享受（约会），她的"nervous" = 她的希望。
- **叙事手法**："Before" 和 "tomorrow" 的概念 = Addie 第一次拥有未来；"palimpsest in reverse" = 她的存在方式。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** After so many years, Addie thought she'd come to terms with time. She thought she'd made her peace with it—or that they'd found a way to coexist—not friends by any means, but at least no longer enemies. And yet, the time between Thursday night and Saturday afternoon is merciless, every second doled out with the care of an old woman counting pennies to pay for bread. Not once does it seem to quicken, not once does she lose track of it. She can't seem to spend it, or waste it, or even misplace it. The minutes inflate around her, an ocean of undrinkable time between now and then, between here and the store, between her and Henry.

这么多年后，Addie 以为她已经与时间达成了协议。她已经与之和平相处——或者他们找到了一种共存的方式——绝不是朋友，但至少不再是敌人。然而，周四晚上到周六下午之间的时间是残酷的，每一秒都被小心翼翼地分配，像一个老妇人数着便士买面包。它似乎从未加快，她从未失去对它的踪迹。她似乎无法花费它，或浪费它，甚至放错它。分钟在她周围膨胀，一片不可饮用的时间之海，在现在和那时之间，在这里和商店之间，在她和 Henry 之间。

**关键词**：come to terms with time / made her peace / coexist / not friends by any means / no longer enemies / merciless / doled out / care of an old woman counting pennies / pay for bread / quicken / lose track of it / spend it / waste it / misplace it / minutes inflate / ocean of undrinkable time / now and then / here and the store / her and Henry

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"时间"。"come to terms with time" —— 与时间达成协议 = 她习惯了时间。"merciless" —— 残酷的 = 时间太慢。"ocean of undrinkable time" —— 不可饮用的时间之海 = 等待的痛苦。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"undrinkable time" = 她第一次等待某人。
