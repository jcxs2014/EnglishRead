# ch35. Thomas and Remy

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1724 年巴黎，Addie 女扮男装（"Thomas"），在街头遇到年轻印刷工 Remy Laurent，两人一起喝咖啡。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷中的自由 —— Addie 体验了男性的自由。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：Disguise（伪装）—— Addie 伪装成男性，体验了自由。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从恐惧（被认出）到享受（伪装的自由）。
- **叙事手法**：Addie 的"tunic and tricorne hat" = 男性的自由；Remy 的"Aut viam invenium aut faciam" = 找到出路。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Freedom is a pair of trousers and a buttoned coat. A man's tunic and a tricorne hat. If only she had known. The darkness claimed he'd given her freedom, but really, there is no such thing for a woman, not in a world where they are bound up inside their clothes, and sealed inside their homes, a world where only men are given leave to roam.

自由是一条裤子和一件扣好的外套。男性的三角帽和三角帽。如果她早知道。黑暗声称他给了她自由，但真的，对女人来说没有这样的东西，不是在一个她们被束缚在衣服里、被密封在家里的世界，一个只有男性被允许漫游的世界。

**关键词**：Freedom is a pair of trousers / buttoned coat / man's tunic / tricorne hat / If only she had known / darkness claimed / bound up inside their clothes / sealed inside their homes / only men are given leave to roam

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"自由"定义。"Freedom is a pair of trousers" —— 自由是一条裤子 = 男性的自由。"If only she had known" —— 如果她早知道 = 她早知道就不需要诅咒。"bound up inside their clothes" —— 束缚在衣服里 = 女性的束缚。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"Freedom is a pair of trousers" = 她三百年后终于理解了自由。

> **原句 2:** Addie saunters up the street, a stolen basket hooked over the elbow of her coat. Nearby, an old woman stands in a doorway, beating out a rug, and laborers lounge on café steps, and none of them so much as blink, because they do not see a woman, walking alone. They see a young man, barely more than a youth, dawdling in the dying light; they do not think how strange, how scandalous it is to see her strolling. They do not think anything at all.

Addie 漫步上街，一个偷来的篮子挂在她外套的肘部。附近，一个老妇人站在门口，拍打着地毯，工人们懒洋洋地坐在咖啡馆的台阶上，他们中没有一个人眨眼，因为他们没有看到一个女人独自行走。他们看到一个年轻人，几乎只是一个青年，在夕阳中闲逛；他们认为多么奇怪、多么丑闻看到她漫步。他们什么都没想。

**关键词**：saunters / stolen basket / hooked over the elbow / beating out a rug / lounge on café steps / none of them so much as blink / do not see a woman / young man / barely more than a youth / dawdling / dying light / strange / scandalous / strolling / do not think anything at all

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"隐身"。"none of them so much as blink" —— 没有一个人眨眼 = 他们看不到她。"do not see a woman" —— 没有看到一个女人 = 他们看到的是男性。"do not think anything at all" —— 什么都没想 = 男性的自由。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"do not think anything at all" = 男性不需要思考。

---

> **原句 3:** It has been four years now without a visit from the dark. Four years, and at the dawn of every one, she swears she will not waste the time she has in waiting. But it is a promise she cannot fully keep. For all her effort, Addie is like a clock wound tighter as the day draws near, a coiled spring that cannot loosen until dawn. And even then, it is a grim unwinding, less relief than resignation, the knowledge that it will start again.

现在四年没有黑暗的访问了。四年，在每个黎明，她发誓她不会在等待中浪费她的时间。但这是一个她不能完全遵守的承诺。尽管她努力，Addie 就像一天临近时钟上得更紧，一个直到黎明才能放松的卷紧的弹簧。即使在那时，它是一个严峻的放松，不是解脱而是辞职，知道它会再次开始。

**关键词**：four years / without a visit from the dark / dawn / swears / waste the time / promise / cannot fully keep / clock wound tighter / day draws near / coiled spring / cannot loosen until dawn / grim unwinding / less relief than resignation / knowledge that it will start again

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"等待"。"four years without a visit" —— 四年没有访问 = Luc 没有来。"clock wound tighter" —— 时钟上得更紧 = 她的焦虑。"coiled spring" —— 卷紧的弹簧 = 她的紧张。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"grim unwinding" = 她的放松不是解脱。

> **原句 4:** The man comes out of nowhere. A shoulder knocks into her arm, and the precious jar slips from her hand and shatters on the cobbled street, and for an instant Addie thinks she is being attacked, or robbed, but the stranger is already stammering out his apologies. "You fool," she hisses, attention flicking from the golden syrup, now glittering with glass, to the man who caused her loss. He is young, and fair, and lovely, with high cheeks and hair the color of her ruined honey. And he is not alone. His companions hang back, whooping and cheering at his mistake—they have the happy air of those who began their evening revels back at midday—but the errant youth blushes fiercely, clearly embarrassed.

男人不知从哪里来。一个肩膀撞到她的手臂，珍贵的罐子从她手中滑落，在鹅卵石街道上破碎，有一瞬间 Addie 认为她正在被攻击或抢劫，但陌生人已经在结结巴巴地道歉。"你这个傻瓜，"她嘶嘶地说，注意力从金色糖浆（现在闪烁着玻璃）转向造成她损失的男人。他年轻、白皙、可爱，高颧骨和头发颜色像她毁坏的蜂蜜。他不是一个人。他的同伴们退后，对他的错误欢呼——他们有那些在中午开始晚间狂欢的人的快乐气氛——但那个任性的青年脸红得很厉害，显然很尴尬。

**关键词**：comes out of nowhere / shoulder knocks / precious jar / slips / shatters / cobbled street / being attacked / robbed / stammering out his apologies / You fool / hisses / golden syrup / glittering with glass / young, fair, lovely / high cheeks / hair the color of her ruined honey / not alone / companions hang back / whooping and cheering / happy air / evening revels / midday / errant youth / blushes fiercely / clearly embarrassed

**为什么这样写**：Remy 的"出现"。"comes out of nowhere" —— 不知从哪里来 = 突然。"precious jar" —— 珍贵的罐子 = 她的蜂蜜。"You fool" —— 你这个傻瓜 = 她的愤怒。"errant youth" —— 任性的青年 = Remy。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"errant youth" = 她对 Remy 的第一印象。

> **原句 5:** "Let go," she says, lowering her voice a measure when she speaks, which only seems to please him more, even as he frees her arm with all the speed of someone grazing fire. "Sorry," he says again, "I forgot myself." And then, a mischievous grin. "It seems you have, too." "Not at all," she says, fingers drifting toward the short blade she's kept inside her basket. "I have misplaced myself on purpose."

"放开，"她说，说话时降低声音，这似乎更取悦他，即使他以某人抚摸火的速度释放她的手臂。"对不起，"他说，"我忘了自己。"然后，一个调皮的笑容。"看来你也一样。""一点也不，"她说，手指飘向她在篮子里保留的短刀。"我故意放错了自己。"

**关键词**：Let go / lowering her voice / please him more / frees her arm / speed of someone grazing fire / forgot myself / mischievous grin / It seems you have, too / Not at all / short blade / misplaced myself on purpose

**为什么这样写**：Addie 与 Remy 的对话。"Let go" —— 放开 = 她的命令。"speed of someone grazing fire" —— 抚摸火的速度 = 他害怕了。"mischievous grin" —— 调皮的笑容 = 他的魅力。"misplaced myself on purpose" —— 故意放错了自己 = 她的伪装。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"misplaced myself on purpose" = 她故意伪装。

> **原句 6:** "I was thinking," she says, "that it must be so easy to be a man." "Is that why you put on this disguise?" "That," she says, "and a hatred of corsets." He laughs, the sound so open and easy Addie finds a smile rising to her lips. "Do you have a name?" he asks, and she doesn't know if he's asking for her own, or that of her disguise, but she decides on "Thomas," watches him turn the word over like a bite of fruit.

"我在想，"她说，"做一个男人一定很容易。""这就是你穿这身伪装的原因？""那个，"她说，"还有对紧身胸衣的仇恨。"他笑了，声音如此开放和轻松 Addie 发现嘴角升起微笑。"你有名字吗？"他问，她不知道他是在问她的还是她的伪装的，但她决定用"Thomas"，看着他把这个词翻过来像一口水果。

**关键词**：it must be so easy to be a man / put on this disguise / hatred of corsets / open and easy / smile rising / Do you have a name / decides on "Thomas" / turn the word over like a bite of fruit

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"伪装"。"it must be so easy to be a man" —— 做一个男人一定很容易 = 她的感想。"hatred of corsets" —— 对紧身胸衣的仇恨 = 女性的束缚。"decides on 'Thomas'" —— 决定用 Thomas = 她的伪装名。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"Thomas" = 她的男性伪装名。

> **原句 7:** "Amazing," he marvels. "You have studied my sex well." "Have I?" "You are a splendid mimic." Addie could tell him that she's had the time to practice, that it has become a kind of game over the years, a way to amuse herself. That she has added a dozen different characters by now, knows the exact differences between a duchess and a marchioness, a docksman and a merchant. But instead, she only says, "We all need ways to pass the time."

"令人惊叹，"他惊叹。"你研究我的性别很好。""是吗？""你是一个出色的模仿者。"Addie 可以告诉他她有时间的练习，多年来它已经成为一种游戏，一种娱乐自己的方式。她现在已经添加了十几个不同的角色，知道公爵夫人和侯爵夫人、码头工人和商人之间的确切差异。但相反，她只说，"我们都需要消磨时间的方式。"

**关键词**：Amazing / studied my sex well / splendid mimic / had the time to practice / kind of game / amuse herself / dozen different characters / exact differences / duchess / marchioness / docksman / merchant / ways to pass the time

**为什么这样写**：Remy 的"惊叹"。"studied my sex well" —— 研究我的性别很好 = 她的伪装很好。"splendid mimic" —— 出色的模仿者 = 她的能力。"ways to pass the time" —— 消磨时间的方式 = 她的三百年。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"ways to pass the time" = 她三百年学会的技能。

> **原句 8:** "It is a crime," he says, "that women are not taught the same as men. Why, a world without reading, I cannot fathom it. A whole long life without poems, or plays, or philosophers. Shakespeare, Socrates, to say nothing of Descartes!" "Is that all?" she teases. "And Voltaire," he goes on. "Of course, Voltaire. And essays, and novels." She does not know the word. "A single long story," he explains, "something of pure invention. Filled with romance, or comedy, or adventure."

"这是犯罪，"他说，"女性没有像男性一样被教。为什么，一个没有阅读的世界，我无法理解。一整个人生没有诗歌、戏剧或哲学家。莎士比亚、苏格拉底，更不用说笛卡尔！""就这些？"她取笑。"还有伏尔泰，"他继续。"当然，伏尔泰。还有散文和小说。"她不知道这个词。"一个长故事，"他解释，"某种纯粹发明的东西。充满浪漫、喜剧或冒险。"

**关键词**：crime / women are not taught the same as men / world without reading / cannot fathom / whole long life / poems, plays, philosophers / Shakespeare, Socrates, Descartes / Is that all / teases / Voltaire / essays, novels / single long story / pure invention / romance, comedy, adventure

**为什么这样写**：Remy 的"教育"观点。"women are not taught the same as men" —— 女性没有像男性一样被教 = 他的进步观点。"cannot fathom" —— 无法理解 = 他的震惊。"novels" —— 小说 = 她不知道的词。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"novels" = 她不知道小说是什么（因为她不能写）。

> **原句 9:** "It does," he admits, before nodding at her attire. "And yet," he says with an impish grin, "you strike me as someone not easily restrained. Aut viam invenium aut faciam, and so on." She does not know Latin yet, and he does not offer a translation, but a decade from now, she will look up the words, and learn their meaning. To find a way, or make your own. And she will smile, then, a ghost of the smile he has managed to win from her tonight.

"确实，"他承认，然后点头看着她的服装。"然而，"他带着调皮的笑容说，"你给我的印象是某个不容易被约束的人。Aut viam invenium aut faciam，等等。"她还不知道拉丁语，他也没有提供翻译，但十年后，她会查这些词，学习它们的意思。找到一条路，或者自己创造一条。然后她会微笑，一个他今晚设法从她那里赢得的微笑的幽灵。

**关键词**：admits / nodding at her attire / impish grin / strike me as someone not easily restrained / Aut viam invenium aut faciam / does not know Latin / decade from now / look up the words / learn their meaning / To find a way, or make your own / ghost of the smile

**为什么这样写**：Remy 的"Aut viam invenium aut faciam"。"Aut viam invenium aut faciam" —— 找到一条路或自己创造一条 = 他的信条。"does not know Latin" —— 不知道拉丁语 = 她需要学。"To find a way, or make your own" —— 找到一条路或自己创造一条 = 她三百年做的。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"To find a way, or make your own" = 她三百年做的。

## 一句话总结

1724 年巴黎，Addie 女扮男装（"Thomas"），在街头遇到年轻印刷工 Remy Laurent。她体验了男性的自由——"Freedom is a pair of trousers"——并学会了一句拉丁语："Aut viam invenium aut faciam"（找到一条路，或自己创造一条）。
