# ch77. The Gallery and the Monster

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1827 年伦敦，Addie 在国家美术馆中，Luc 出现，她挑战他——"你也孤独"——他展示真身，强迫贝多芬交出灵魂——"我们有什么不同？"
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 — 从"平静"到"对抗"到"恐惧"到"领悟"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Monster（怪物）—— Luc 展示真身；The Soul（灵魂）—— 他收集灵魂 = 他的本质；The Fear（恐惧）—— 她真正感到恐惧 = 她有东西可以失去。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从"挑战"到"恐惧"—— 她看到 Luc 的本质 = 她真正感到恐惧 = 她有东西可以失去。
- **叙事手法**："unfold" = 展开 = 他的真身 = 他的本质 = 他的力量。

她本可以住在国家美术馆。事实上，她在这里度过了一季，从一个房间漫步到另一个房间，盛宴于绘画和肖像、雕塑和挂毯。一个在朋友中间、在回声中间度过的生活。

> **原句 4:** "You said it yourself, Luc. Ideas are wilder than memories. And I can be wild. I can be stubborn as the weeds, and you will not root me out. And I think you are glad of it. I think that's why you've come, because you are lonely, too." Luc's eyes flash a sickly, stormy green. "Don't be absurd," he sneers. "Gods are known to everyone." "But remembered by so few," she counters. "How many mortals have met you more than twice—once to make a deal and once to pay the price? How many have been a part of your life as long as I have?" Addie flashes a triumphant smile. "Perhaps that's why you cursed me as you did. So you would have some company. So someone would remember you."

"你自己说的，Luc。思想比记忆更野。我可以很野。我可以像杂草一样固执，你不会把我除掉。我想你为此感到高兴。我想这就是你来的原因，因为你也孤独。"Luc 的眼睛闪烁着一种病态的、狂暴的绿色。"别荒谬，"他冷笑。"神被每个人都知道。""但被极少数人记住，"她反驳道。"有多少凡人见过你超过两次——一次做交易，一次付代价？有多少人像我一样是你生活的一部分这么久？"Addie 闪出胜利的微笑。"也许这就是你这样诅咒我的原因。这样你就有了陪伴。这样有人会记得你。"

**关键词**：lonely / company / remember

**为什么这样写**："lonely" = 孤独 = 他也是孤独的 = 她的洞察 = 他诅咒她的原因 = 他想要陪伴。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"你也孤独" = 她的洞察 = 他诅咒她的原因 = 他想要陪伴 = 他的爱。

> **原句 5:** It has been forty years since he last dragged her through the dark, but she hasn't forgotten the feeling, the primal fear and the wild hope and the reckless freedom of doors thrown open onto night. It is infinite— And then it is over, and she is on her hands and knees on a wooden floor, limbs trembling from the strangeness of the journey. A bed lies, disheveled and empty, the curtains have been flung wide, and the floor is covered in sheets of music, and there is a stale air of sickness to the space. "What a waste," murmurs Luc. Addie rises unsteadily to her feet. "Where are we?" "You mistake me for some lonesome mortal," he says. "Some heartsick human in search of company. I am neither." Movement, across the room, and she realizes they are not alone. A ghost of a man, white-haired and wild-eyed, sits on a piano bench, his back to the keys. He is pleading in German. "Not yet," he says, clutching a handful of music to his chest. "Not yet. I need more time."

自从他上次把她拖入黑暗已经四十年了，但她没有忘记那种感觉，原始的恐惧和狂野的希望和门被抛向夜晚的鲁莽自由。它是无限的——然后它结束了，她手脚并用在木地板上，四肢因旅程的奇怪而颤抖。一张床，凌乱而空荡，窗帘被猛地拉开，地板上铺满了乐谱，空间中有疾病的陈腐气味。"多么浪费，"Luc 低声说。Addie 摇摇晃晃地站起来。"我们在哪里？""你把我误认为某个孤独的凡人，"他说。"某个寻找陪伴的心碎的人。我两者都不是。"运动，穿过房间，她意识到他们并不孤单。一个男人的幽灵，白发苍苍，眼睛狂野，坐在钢琴凳上，背对琴键。他用德语乞求。"还没有，"他说，把一捧乐谱抱在胸前。"还没有。我需要更多时间。"

**关键词**：primal fear / waste / Beethoven / time

**为什么这样写**："primal fear" = 原始恐惧 = 她的恐惧 = 他的力量 = 她的恐惧。"waste" = 浪费 = 他的观点 = 生命 = 他的力量。

**读者视角提示**：Addie 的"原始恐惧" = 她的恐惧 = 他的力量 = 她的恐惧 = 她有东西可以失去。

> **原句 6:** The time for deals is done, Herr Beethoven. Now, you must say the words." The man shakes his head. "No." And Addie cannot see Luc's eyes, but she can feel his temper changing. The air ripples in the room around them, a wind, and something stronger. "Surrender your soul," says Luc. "Or I will take it by force." "No!" shouts the man, hysterical now. "Begone, Devil. Begone, and—" It is the last thing he says, before Luc unfolds. That is the only way to think of it. The black hair rises from his face, climbing through the air like weeds, and his skin ripples and splits, and what spills out is not a man. It is a monster. It is a god. It is the night itself, and something else, something she has never seen, something she cannot bear to look at. Something older than the dark. "Surrender." And now the voice is not a voice at all, but a medley of snapping branches and summer wind, a wolf's low growl, and the sudden shifting of rocks underfoot. The man burbles and pleads. "Help!" he cries out, but it is no use. If there is anyone beyond the door, they will not hear. "Help!" he cries again, uselessly. And then the monster plunges its hand into his chest. The man staggers, pale and gray, as the darkness plucks his soul like a piece of fruit. It comes loose with a tearing sound, and the composer stumbles, and falls to the floor. But Addie's eyes are locked on the bloom of light in the shadow's hand, jagged and unsteady. And before she can study the ribbons of color curling on its surface, before she can wonder at the images coiling inside, the darkness closes its fingers around the soul, and it crackles through him like lightning, and plunges out of sight. The composer sits slumped against his piano bench, head back, and eyes empty. Luc's hand, she will learn, is always subtle. They will see his work and call it sickness, call it heart failure, call it madness, suicide, overdose, accident. But tonight, she only knows that the man on the floor is dead. The darkness turns on Addie, then, and there is no vestige of Luc in the roiling smoke. There are no green eyes. No playful smirk. Nothing but a menacing void, a shadow filled with teeth. It has been a long time since Addie felt true fear. Sadness, she knows; loneliness and grief. But fear belongs to those with more to lose. And yet. Staring into that dark, Addie is afraid. She wills her legs to stay, wills herself to hold her ground, and she does, as it takes its first step, and its second, but by the third, she finds herself retreating. Away from the writhing dark, the monstrous night, until her back comes to rest against the wall. But the darkness keeps coming. With every forward step it draws itself together, the edges firming until it is less a storm than smoke bottled into glass. The face finds form, shadows twisting into loose black curls, and the eyes—there are eyes again now—lighten like a drying stone, and the cavernous maw narrows to a cupid's bow, the lips curving into sly content. And he is Luc again, wrapped in the guise of flesh and bone, close enough that she can feel the cool night air wafting off him like a breeze. And this time, when he speaks, it is with the voice she knows so well. "Well, my darling…" he says, one hand rising to her cheek. "Are we so different now?" She does not have the chance to answer. He gives the barest push, and the wall opens up behind her, and she is not sure if she falls, or if the shadows reach out and pull her down, only that Luc is gone, and the composer's room is gone, and for an instant, the dark is everywhere, and then she is standing outside, on the cobblestone banks, and the night is full of laughter and lights shining on the water, and the soft, melodic strain of a man singing somewhere along the Thames.

"交易的时间结束了，贝多芬先生。现在，你必须说那些话。"男人摇头。"不。"Addie 看不到 Luc 的眼睛，但她能感觉到他的脾气在变化。空气在他们周围的空间中泛起涟漪，一阵风，还有更强烈的东西。"交出你的灵魂，"Luc 说。"否则我会强行拿走。""不！"男人喊道，现在歇斯底里。"走开，魔鬼。走开，和——"这是他在 Luc 展开之前说的最后一句话。这是唯一能想到的方式。黑发从他脸上升起，像杂草一样爬上空气，他的皮肤涟漪和分裂，涌出的不是一个人。这是一个怪物。这是一个神。它是夜晚本身，还有其他东西，她从未见过的东西，她无法忍受看的东西。比黑暗更古老的东西。"投降。"现在声音根本不是声音，而是树枝断裂和夏季风的混合，狼的低吼，以及脚下岩石的突然移动。男人嘴里吐着泡泡，乞求。"救命！"他喊道，但没有用。如果门外有人，他们不会听到。"救命！"他再次喊道，无用。然后怪物将手插入他的胸口。男人踉跄，苍白而灰暗，因为黑暗像摘水果一样摘取他的灵魂。它随着撕裂声松开，作曲家绊倒，倒在地板上。但 Addie 的眼睛锁定在阴影手中光的绽放上，参差不齐，不稳定。在她研究表面卷曲的色带之前，在她好奇内部缠绕的图像之前，黑暗合拢手指包围灵魂，它像闪电一样穿过他，消失在视线之外。作曲家瘫坐在他的钢琴凳上，头向后仰，眼睛空洞。Luc 的手，她会学到，总是微妙的。他们会看到他的作品并称其为疾病，称其为心力衰竭，称其为疯狂，自杀，用药过量，事故。但今晚，她只知道地板上的人死了。然后黑暗转向 Addie，在翻涌的烟雾中没有 Luc 的痕迹。没有绿色的眼睛。没有顽皮的傻笑。只有一个威胁的虚空，一个充满牙齿的阴影。已经很久了，Addie 感到真正的恐惧。悲伤，她知道；孤独和悲伤。但恐惧属于那些有更多可以失去的人。然而。盯着那个黑暗，Addie 很害怕。她意志她的腿留下，意志自己坚守阵地，它做到了，当它迈出第一步，第二步，但第三步，她发现自己在后退。离开翻腾的黑暗，可怕的夜晚，直到她的背靠墙休息。但黑暗继续前进。每向前一步，它就把自己拉在一起，边缘变得坚固，直到它不再是风暴而是装在玻璃里的烟。脸找到了形状，阴影扭曲成松散的黑卷发，而眼睛——现在又有眼睛了——像干燥的石头一样变亮，洞穴般的嘴收缩成丘比特的弓，嘴唇弯曲成狡猾的内容。他又是 Luc 了，包裹在血肉之躯的伪装里，近得她能感觉到像微风一样从他身上飘出的凉爽夜风。这一次，当他说他用她熟悉的声音说话。"好吧，我的宝贝……"他说，一只手举起她的脸颊。"我们现在有什么不同？"她没有机会回答。他轻轻一推，她身后的墙壁打开了，她不确定是她摔倒了，还是阴影伸出手把她拉下来，只知道 Luc 不见了，作曲家的房间不见了，有一瞬间，黑暗无处不在，然后她站在外面，在鹅卵石河岸上，夜晚充满笑声和灯光照在水上，还有某个地方沿泰晤士河唱歌的男人柔和的旋律。
