# ch33. Luc Does Not Come

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1720 年巴黎，Addie 为 Luc 的年度来访做准备，但他没有来——她意识到自己依赖他的存在，即使他是她的诅咒者。
- **情感弧线位置**：低谷 —— Addie 意识到自己依赖 Luc 的存在。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Annual Visit（年度访问）的反转 —— 他每年都没有来，但今年她准备了。
- **人物弧线**：Addie 从期待（为 Luc 准备）到失望（他没有来）到愤怒（砸东西）。
- **叙事手法**：Addie 的"home" = 她为 Luc 准备的空间；"Enough" 重复 = 她的决心。

> **原句 1:** The stage is set, the places ready. Addie smooths the linen on the table, arranges the porcelain plates, the cups—not crystal, but still glass—and draws the dinner from its hamper. She is proud of her collection, prouder still of the fact she had no magic, save the curse, by which to gather it, could not simply cut her gaze, say a word, and will it so.

> **原句 3:** It is not only the table. It is the room. No stolen chamber. No beggar's hovel. A place, for now at least, to call her own. It took two months to find, a fortnight to fix up, but it was worth it. From the outside it is nothing: cracked glass and warping wood. And it's true, the lower floors have fallen into disrepair, home now only to rodents and the occasional stray cats—and, in winter, crowded with bodies seeking any form of shelter—but it is the height of summer now, and the city's poor have taken to the streets, and Addie has claimed the top floor for herself. Boarded up the stairs and carved a way in and out through an upper window, like a child in a wooden fort. It is an unconventional entrance, but it is worth it for the room beyond, where she has made herself a home.

不仅是桌子。是房间。没有偷来的房间。没有乞丐的茅屋。一个地方，至少现在，可以称之为自己的。花了两个月找到，两周修复，但值得。从外面看什么都没有：破裂的玻璃和变形的木头。这是真的，下层已经失修，现在只有啮齿动物和偶尔的流浪猫的家——而在冬天，挤满了寻求任何形式庇护的身体——但现在是盛夏，城市的穷人已经走上街头，Addie 已经为自己占据了顶层。封住了楼梯，通过上层窗口雕刻了一条进出的路，像孩子在木堡里。这是一个非传统的入口，但对于后面的房间值得，她在那里为自己建立了一个家。

> **原句 6:** And so she has made her home, and readied it for company, fastened up her hair and dressed herself in russet silk, the color of fall leaves, even cinched herself into a corset despite her loathing of bone stays. > **原句 7:** The sun has gone down now, and all that's left to do is wait. An hour passes, and her stomach growls with want as the bread goes cold in its cloth, but she doesn't allow herself to eat. Instead, she leans out the window and watches the city, the shifting lights of lanterns being lit. And he doesn't come. She pours herself a glass of wine, and paces, as the stolen candles drip, and wax pools on the table linen, and the night grows heavy, the hours first late, and then early. And still he doesn't come. The candles gutter and snuff themselves out, and Addie sits in the dark as the knowledge settles over her.

太阳现在已经落下，剩下的就是等待。一小时过去了，她的胃因渴望而咆哮，面包在布变冷，但她不允许自己吃。相反，她靠在窗边看城市，灯笼的灯光在转移。而他没有来。她给自己倒一杯酒，踱步，偷来的蜡烛滴落，蜡在桌布上聚集，夜晚变得沉重，小时先晚，然后早。而他仍然没有来。蜡烛闪烁并熄灭，Addie 坐在黑暗中，知识在她身上沉淀。

> **原句 8:** The night has passed, the first threads of daylight creeping into the sky, and it is tomorrow now, and their anniversary is over, and five years have become six without his presence, without his face, without his asking if she's had enough, and the world slips, because it is unfair, it is cheating, it is wrong. He was supposed to come, that was the nature of their dance. She did not want him there, has never wanted it, but she expected it, he has made her expect it. Has given her a single threshold on which to balance, a narrow precipice of hope, because he is a hated thing, but a hated thing is still something. The only thing she has. > **原句 9:** She gazes out the window, and remembers the look in his eyes when they toasted, the curve of his lips when they declared war, and realizes what a fool she is, how easily baited. And suddenly, the whole tableau seems gruesome and pathetic, and Addie can't bear to look at it, can't breathe in her red silk. She tears at the laces of the corset, pulls the pins from her hair, frees herself from the confines of the dress, sweeps the settings from the table, and dashes the now empty bottle against the wall. Glass bites into her hand, and the pain is sharp, and real, the sudden scald of a burn without the lasting scar, and she does not care. In moments, her cuts have already closed. The glasses and bottle lie whole. Once she thought it was a blessing, this inability to break, but now, the impotence is maddening. She ruins everything, only to watch it shudder, mocking, back together, return like a set to the beginning of the show. And Addie screams.

她凝视窗外，记得他们敬酒时他眼睛的表情，他们宣战时他嘴唇的曲线，意识到她是个多么傻瓜，多么容易被诱惑。突然，整个场景变得可怕而可悲，Addie 无法忍受看它，无法在她的红丝裙中呼吸。她撕开紧身胸衣的带子，拔出头发上的发夹，把自己从裙子的束缚中解放出来，扫掉桌上的摆设，把现在的空瓶子砸向墙上。玻璃咬进她的手，痛苦是尖锐的、真实的，突然的灼伤而没有持久的疤痕，她不在乎。几分钟后，她的伤口已经闭合。玻璃和瓶子完好无损。曾经她认为这是一种祝福，这种无法打破的能力，但现在，这种无力是令人发指的。她毁掉一切，只是看着它颤抖、嘲笑、重新组合，像一套戏回到开场。Addie 尖叫。

> **原句 10:** Anger flares inside her, hot and bright, anger at Luc, and at herself, but it is giving way to fear, and grief, and terror, because she must face another year alone, a year without hearing her name, without seeing herself reflected in anyone's eyes, without a night's respite from this curse, a year, or five, or ten, and she realizes then how much she's leaned on it, the promise of his presence, because without it, she is falling. She sinks to the floor among the ruins of her night. It will be years before she sees the sea, the waves crashing against the jagged white cliffs, and then she will remember Luc's goading words. Even rocks wear away to nothing. Addie falls asleep just after dawn, but it is fitful, and brief, and full of nightmares, and when she wakes to see the sun high over Paris, she cannot bring herself to rise. She sleeps all the day and half the night, and when she wakes the shattered thing in her has set again, like a badly broken bone, some softness hardened. "Enough," she tells herself, rising to her feet. "Enough," she repeats, feasting on the bread, now stale, the cheese, wilted from the heat. Enough. There will be other dark nights, of course, other wretched dawns, and her resolve will always weaken a little as the days grow long, and the anniversary draws near, and treacherous hope slips in like a draft. But the sorrow has faded, replaced by stubborn rage, and she resolves to kindle it, to shield and nurture the flame until it takes far more than a single breath to blow it out.
