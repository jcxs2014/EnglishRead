# ch37. Pinball and Panic

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Addie 和 Henry 打弹球、看电影（西北偏北），Henry 因焦虑发作而离开影院。
- **情感弧线位置**：转折后的挑战 —— Henry 的抑郁症显现。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The Date（约会）的反转 —— 约会不总是完美的。
- **人物弧线**：Henry 从开心到焦虑发作，Addie 从享受到了解他的痛苦。
- **叙事手法**：Henry 的"running out of time" = 他的焦虑；Addie 的"grabs his arm" = 她的支持。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "How are you so good at pinball?" Henry demands as she racks up points. Addie isn't sure. The truth is, she's never played before, and it's taken her a few times to get the hang of the game, but now she's found her stride. "I'm a fast learner," she says, just before the ball slips between her paddles. "HIGH SCORE!" announces the game in a mechanical drone. "Well done," calls Henry over the noise. "Better own your victory." The screen flashes, waiting for her to enter her name. Addie hesitates. "Like this," he says, showing her how to toggle the red box between the letters. He steps aside, but when she tries, the cursor doesn't move. The light just flashes over the letter A, mocking. "It doesn't matter," she says, backing away, but Henry steps in. "New machines, vintage problems." He bumps it with his hip, and the square goes solid around the A. "There we go." He's about to step aside, but Addie catches his arm. "Enter my name while I grab the next round." It's easier now that the place is full. She swipes a couple of beers from the edge of the counter, weaves back through the crowd before the bartender even turns around. And when she returns, drinks in hand, the first things she sees are the letters, flashing in bright red on the screen. ADI. "I didn't know how to spell your name," he says. And it's wrong, but it doesn't even matter; nothing matters but those three letters, glowing back at her, almost like a stamp, a signature.

"你怎么这么会打弹球？"Henry 在她得分时要求。Addie 不确定。这是她以前从未玩过，她花了几次才掌握窍门，但现在她找到了自己的节奏。"我学得很快，"她说，就在球从她的桨之间滑过之前。"高分！"游戏用机械的嗡嗡声宣布。"做得好，"Henry 在噪音中喊。"最好拥有自己的胜利。"屏幕闪烁，等待她输入名字。Addie 犹豫了。"像这样，"他说，向她展示如何在字母之间切换红框。他退到一边，但当她尝试时，光标不动。光线只是在字母 A 上闪烁，嘲弄。"没关系，"她说，后退，但 Henry 走进来。"新机器，老问题。"他用臀部撞它，方块在 A 周围变实。"好了。"他正要退到一边，但 Addie 抓住他的臂。"当我拿下一轮时输入我的名字。"现在地方满了，这更容易了。她从柜台边缘偷了几瓶啤酒，在酒保转身之前穿过人群走回来。当她回来时，手里拿着饮料，她看到的第一件事是字母，在屏幕上闪烁着鲜红。ADI。"我不知道怎么拼你的名字，"他说。这是错的，但这甚至不重要；除了这三个字母，什么都不重要，像邮票、签名一样在她面前发光。

**为什么这样写**：Addie 的"名字"。"ADI" —— 他的名字 = 她的签名。"stamp, signature" —— 邮票、签名 = 她的存在被标记。

> **原句 2:** They play until they run out of quarters and beer, until the place is too crowded for comfort, until they truly can't hear each other over the ring and clash of the games and the shouts of the other people, and then they spill out of the dark arcade. They go back through the too-bright laundromat, and then out onto the street, still bubbling with energy. It's dark out now, the sky overhead a low canopy of dense gray clouds, promising rain, and Henry shoves his hands in his pockets, looks up and down the street. "What now?" "You want me to choose?" "This is an equal opportunity date," he says, rocking from heel to toe. "I provided the first chapter. It's your turn." Addie hums to herself, looking around, summoning a mental picture of the neighborhood. "Good thing I found my wallet," she says, patting her pocket. She didn't, of course, but she did liberate a few twenties from the illustrator's kitchen drawer before she left that morning. Judging by the recent profile of him in The Times, and the reported size of his latest book deal, Gerard won't miss it. "This way." Addie takes off down the sidewalk. "How far are we going?" he asks fifteen minutes later, when they're still walking. "I thought you were a New Yorker," she teases. But his strides are long enough to match her speed, and five minutes later they round the corner, and there it is. The Nitehawk lights up the darkening street, white bulbs tracing patterns on the brick façade, the word CINEMA picked out in red neon light across its front. Addie has been to every movie theater in Brooklyn, the massive multiplexes with their stadium seats and the indie gems with worn-out sofas, has witnessed every mixture of new releases and nostalgia. And the Nitehawk is one of her favorites. She scans the board, buys two tickets to a showing of North by Northwest, since Henry says he's never seen it, then takes his hand and leads them down the hall into the dark.

他们玩到用完硬币和啤酒，直到地方太挤不舒服，直到他们真的无法在游戏和其他人的喊叫声和碰撞声中听到对方，然后他们从黑暗的游戏厅涌出来。他们回到太亮的洗衣店，然后走到街上，仍然充满活力。现在外面天黑了，头顶天空是低低的密集灰云的天篷，预示着雨，Henry 把手插在口袋里，上下看街道。""现在呢？""你想让我选吗？""这是一个平等的约会，"他说，从脚跟到脚尖摇晃。""我提供了第一章。轮到你了。"Addie 哼着歌，环顾四周，召唤出街区的心理画面。""幸好我找到了钱包，"她说，拍拍口袋。她当然没有，但她确实在那天早上离开之前从插画师的厨房抽屉里解放了几张二十美元。根据他在《纽约时报》上的最新简介和他最新书籍交易的报道规模，Gerard 不会想念的。""这边。"Addie 沿着人行道出发。""我们要走多远？"他十五分钟后问，当他们还在走时。""我以为你是纽约人，"她取笑。但他的步伐足够长以匹配她的速度，五分钟后他们绕过角落，它就在那里。Nitehawk 照亮了变暗的街道，白灯泡在砖立面上描绘图案，CINEMA 这个词用红色霓虹灯在前面挑出。Addie 去过布鲁克林的每个电影院，有体育场座位的巨型多厅影院和有破旧沙发的独立宝石，见证了每种新发行和怀旧的混合。而 Nitehawk 是她最喜欢的之一。她扫描牌子，买了两张《西北偏北》的票，因为 Henry 说他从未看过，然后牵着他们走下大厅进入黑暗。

> **原句 3:** The previews ramble on as the seats fill up around them, and Henry takes her hand, their fingers lacing together like links in a chain. She glances over at him, painted in the low theater light. Black curls. High cheekbones. The cupid's bow of his mouth. The flicker of resemblance. It is hardly the first time she's seen Luc echoed in a human face. "You're staring," whispers Henry under the sound of the previews. Addie blinks. "Sorry." She shakes her head. "You look like someone I used to know." "Someone you liked, I hope." "Not really." He shoots her a look of mock affront, and Addie almost laughs. "It was more complicated than that." "Love, then?" She shakes her head. "No…" But her delivery is slower, less emphatic. "But he was very nice to look at." Henry laughs as the lights dim, and the movie starts.

预告片在座位周围继续，Henry 牵着她的手，他们的手指像链条上的链环一样交叠。她瞥了他一眼，在低低的剧院灯光中描绘。黑色卷发。高颧骨。他嘴唇的丘比特弓。闪烁的相似。这几乎不是她第一次在人类脸上看到 Luc 的回响。""你在盯着，"Henry 在预告片的声音下低语。Addie 眨眼。""对不起。"她摇摇头。""你看起来像我认识的人。""希望你喜欢的某人。""不是真的。"他给她一个假装的愤慨表情，Addie 几乎笑了。""那比那更复杂。""那么，爱？"她摇摇头。""不……"但她的表达更慢，不那么强调。""但他看起来很好看。"Henry 在灯光变暗时笑了，电影开始了。

> **原句 4:** A different waiter appears, crouching low as he delivers their food, and she plucks fries from the plate one by one, sinking into the comfort of the film. She glances over to see if Henry's enjoying himself, but he's not even looking at the screen. His face, all energy and light an hour before, is a rictus of tension. One knee bounces restlessly. She leans in, whispers. "You don't like it?" Henry flashes a hollow smile. "It's fine," he says, shifting in his seat. "Just a little slow." It's Hitchcock, she wants to say, but instead she whispers, "It's worth it, I promise." Henry twists toward her, brow folding. "You've already seen it?" Of course Addie has seen it. First, in 1959, at a theater in Los Angeles, and then in the '70s, a double feature with his last film, Family Plot, and then again, a few years back, right in Greenwich Village, during a retrospective. Hitchcock has a way of being resurrected, fed back into the cinema system at regular intervals. "Yeah," she whispers back. "But I don't mind." Henry says nothing, but he clearly does mind. His knee goes back to bouncing, and a few minutes later he's up and out of the seat, walking out into the lobby. "Henry," she calls, confused. "What is it? What's wrong?" She catches up with him as he throws open the theater door and steps out onto the curb. "Sorry," he mumbles. "Needed some air." But that's obviously not it. He's pacing. "Talk to me." His steps slow. "I just wish you'd told me." "Told you what?" "That you'd already seen it." "But you hadn't," she says. "And I didn't mind seeing it again. I like seeing things again." "I don't," he snaps, and then deflates. "I'm sorry." He shakes his head. "I'm sorry. This isn't your problem." He runs his hands through his hair. "I just—" He shakes his head, and turns to look at her, green eyes glassy in the dark. "Do you ever feel like you're running out of time?"

一个不同的服务员出现，蹲下递送他们的食物，她一个一个地从盘子里拿薯条，沉浸在电影的舒适中。她瞥了一眼看 Henry 是否玩得开心，但他甚至没有看屏幕。他的脸，一小时前充满能量和光明，是紧张的扭曲。一只膝盖不安地跳动。她倾身，低语。""你不喜欢它？"Henry 闪过一个空洞的微笑。""没关系，"他说，在座位上移动。""只是有点慢。"这是希区柯克，她想说，但她低语，""值得的，我保证。"Henry 转向她，皱眉。""你已经看过它了吗？"当然 Addie 看过。首先，在 1959 年，在洛杉矶的一家剧院，然后在 70 年代，与他的最后一部电影《家庭阴谋》双片连映，然后几年前，就在格林威治村，在回顾展上。希区柯克有一种被复活的方式，定期被反馈到电影系统中。""是的，"她低语。""但我不介意。"Henry 什么也没说，但他显然介意。他的膝盖又开始跳动，几分钟后他站起来走出座位，走进大厅。""Henry，"她喊道，困惑。""怎么了？怎么了？"当他推开剧院门走到路边时，他追上了他。""对不起，"他喃喃道。""需要一些空气。"但这显然不是。他在踱步。""告诉我。"他的脚步放慢。""我只是希望你已经告诉我。""告诉你什么？""你已经看过它了。""但你没有，"她说。""我不介意再看一遍。我喜欢再看东西。""我不，"他厉声说，然后泄气。""对不起。"他摇摇头。""对不起。这不是你的问题。"他用手穿过头发。""我只是——"他摇摇头，转向看着她，绿色的眼睛在黑暗中朦胧。""你有没有觉得你在用完时间？"

> **原句 5:** Addie blinks and it is three hundred years ago and she is back on her knees on the forest floor, hands driving down into the mossy earth as the church bells ring behind her. "I don't mean in that normal, time flies way," Henry's saying. "I mean feeling like its surging by so fast, and you try to reach out and grab it, you try to hold on, but it just keeps rushing away. And every second, there's a little less time, and a little less air, and sometimes when I'm sitting still, I start to think about it, and when I think about it, I can't breathe. I have to get up. I have to move." He has his arms wrapped around himself, fingers digging into his ribs. It's been a long time since Addie felt that kind of urgency, but she remembers it well, remembers the fear, so heavy she thought it might crush her. Blink and half your life is gone. I do not want to die as I've lived. Born and buried in the same ten-meter plot. Addie reaches out and grabs his arm. "Come on," she says, pulling him down the street. "Let's go." "Where?" he asks, and her hand drops to his, and holds on tight. "To find you something new."

Addie 眨眼，这是三百年前，她回到森林地面上的膝盖上，双手驱入苔藓土地，教堂钟声在身后响起。""我不是指那种正常的时间飞逝的方式，"Henry 说。""我的意思是感觉它涌过得如此之快，你试图伸手抓住它，你试图抓住，但它只是不断冲走。每一秒，时间少了一点，空气少了一点，有时当我坐着不动时，我开始思考它，当我思考它时，我无法呼吸。我必须站起来。我必须移动。"他双臂抱着自己，手指挖进他的肋骨。已经很久没有 Addie 感到那种紧迫感，但她记得很清楚，记得恐惧，如此沉重她以为它可能会压碎你。眨眼，你的一半生活就过去了。我不想像我生活的那样死去。出生和埋葬在相同十米的地块里。Addie 伸出手抓住他的臂。""来吧，"她说，拉着他沿着街道走。""去哪里？"他问，她的手落在他的手上，紧紧抓住。""去找你一些新的东西。"
