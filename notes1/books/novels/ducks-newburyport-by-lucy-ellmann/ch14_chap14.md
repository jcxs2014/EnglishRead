# 14. Chapter Fourteen

## 本章导航

**一句话概括**：母狮受伤后被一只无主的狗跟随，最终被人用麻醉枪捕获；意识流部分以本的疏离、凯西的"shun"为核心，反思社会排斥与母亲的缺席。

**情感弧线位置**：从受伤的脆弱（"licked her sore front leg"）到被狗跟随的无奈——"她无视它，一个害虫，一种噪音"。

**Tropes 兑现/反转**："Recoil and leap" 的变体——母狮对狗的"无视"是另一种形式的"recoil"。

**人物弧线**：叙述者通过本的孤立反思社会排斥机制——"Fresia"对本的"shun"是叙述者恐惧的社会死亡。

**叙事手法**：山狮叙事（受伤/被狗跟随/被捕）与意识流碎片（本的孤立/凯西的疏离/心脏手术）交织。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** The lioness rested near a quiet creek for days. She licked her sore front leg, slept, and licked her leg some more. When hungry, she lurched closer to the creek to see if there were any water rats around – she couldn't hunt larger animals with a wounded leg.

**中文理解：** 母狮在一条安静的小溪旁休息了数日。她舔舐疼痛的前腿，睡觉，再舔舐。饥饿时，她蹒跚靠近溪边看是否有水鼠——她无法用伤腿捕猎更大的动物。

**关键词：** sore front leg / licked / lurched / couldn't hunt larger animals with a wounded leg

**为什么这样写：** 母狮的脆弱时刻——"licked her sore front leg" 的自我疗愈是动物的本能，"lurched" 的笨拙与之前的"leap" 形成对照，"couldn't hunt larger animals" 的局限暴露了伤腿的生存威胁。

---

> **原句 2:** Chained dogs barked at her, showing their teeth. She limped past them, mockingly close. They smelled her wounds and her weakness and longed to chase and bite her. One barked so long and viciously, while she shuffled around his yard looking for rodents, that a man in pajamas was drawn to the doorway of his den. He pointed at her with his gun. There was a jangling sound and a spark of light, but the lion was unharmed. She sprang swiftly sideways into the underbrush, while the frightened man scurried back indoors, leaving his dog to take his chances.

**中文理解：** 被锁住的狗冲她吠叫，露出牙齿。她跛行经过，近得带有嘲弄。它们闻到了她的伤口和虚弱，渴望追逐撕咬。一只狗吠叫得如此持久而凶残，当她在院子里搜寻啮齿动物时，一个穿睡衣的男人被吸引到门口。他用枪指着她。一声叮当响，一道火花，但母狮毫发无损。她迅速侧跃入灌木丛，而惊恐的男人匆忙退回室内，留下他的狗自求多福。

**关键词：** Chained dogs / showing their teeth / limped past them, mockingly close / smelled her wounds and her weakness / longed to chase and bite / sprang swiftly sideways / frightened man scurried back indoors

**为什么这样写：** 母狮的"嘲弄"——"mockingly close" 是受伤猎手对被困犬只的挑衅，"sprang swiftly sideways" 的敏捷与之前的"limped" 形成对照：她仍有逃脱的能力，"frightened man scurried back indoors" 的对比暴露了人类的懦弱。

---

> **原句 3:** He chased after the lioness, whining and whimpering imploringly. She ignored him at first, a pest, a noise, irrelevant to her needs. But after half an hour of his yapping she finally turned to look at him. He spun himself in a tight circle for her and rolled on the ground to show he meant no harm.

**中文理解：** 他追逐母狮，哀鸣着、呜咽着恳求。她起初无视它，一个害虫，一种噪音，与她的需求无关。但在它吠叫了半小时后，她终于转身看它。它为她绕着自己转了一圈，在地上打滚以示无害。

**关键词：** whining and whimpering imploringly / a pest, a noise, irrelevant / spun himself in a tight circle / rolled on the ground / meant no harm

**为什么这样写：** 狗的"恳求"表演——"spun himself in a tight circle" 和"rolled on the ground" 是犬类的臣服仪式，"meant no harm" 的简单表达是跨物种的信任建立，也映射叙述者对"无害"的渴望（在充满威胁的世界中）。

---

> **原句 4:** He followed her at a distance for days, without reward. He lapped water along the way, but had nothing to eat. He was no hunter. He kept twenty paces behind, but didn't let the lioness out of her sight. She eyed him with disdain. When he got too close, she spat at him.

**中文理解：** 他远远地跟随她数日，毫无回报。他沿途舔水，但没有食物。他不是猎手。他保持二十步的距离，但不让母狮离开视线。她轻蔑地瞥他。当他太近时，她冲他嘶吼。

**关键词：** followed her at a distance for days / without reward / no hunter / twenty paces behind / eyed him with disdain / spat at him

**为什么这样写：** 狗的"无条件的跟随"——"without reward" 是忠诚的纯粹形式，"no hunter" 的局限让他成为母狮的"负担"而非威胁，"eyed him with disdain" 的轻蔑是猎手对非猎手的态度，也映射叙述者对"无用之人"（自身）的轻蔑。

---

> **原句 5:** She didn't see the dog for a day or two after that, but then he turned up, again walking twenty feet behind. She became used to his abject shadow and, once he took on some of her quietness, she stopped thinking about him at all.

**中文理解：** 她有一两天没见到狗，但他再次出现，又走在二十步之后。她习惯了她卑贱的影子，而当她获得了一些他的安静，她就不再想他了。

**关键词：** didn't see the dog for a day or two / turned up again / abject shadow / took on some of her quietness / stopped thinking about him at all

**为什么这样写：** 陪伴的"无声接受"——"abject shadow" 是狗的自我定位，"took on some of her quietness" 是跨物种的行为模仿，"stopped thinking about him at all" 是母狮的终极接纳：他已成为环境的一部分。

---

> **原句 6:** Risking human confrontations and human anger, the lioness called openly now to her babies as she wandered the suburban mazes of split-level dream homes, and stopped to listen out for her cubs' returning cries.

**中文理解：** 冒着与人类对抗和人类愤怒的风险，母狮如今公开呼唤她的幼崽，当她徘徊在郊区错层式梦幻住宅的迷宫中，停下来倾听幼崽回应的哭声。

**关键词：** Risking human confrontations / called openly now / suburban mazes / split-level dream homes / listen out for her cubs' returning cries

**为什么这样写：** 母狮的"公开呼唤"——"Risking human confrontations" 是母性对恐惧的超越，"called openly" 是希望的表达，"suburban mazes / split-level dream homes" 的人类住宅被编码为"迷宫"，暗示叙述者对中产阶级社区的陌生感。

---

> **原句 7:** The release of a tranquilizer dart resonated despite the rush of wind and rain, and silently the lioness fell, once again wounded by a man. Sodden, deafened by thunder and subdued by pain, she slowly relaxed and then slept, dreaming that she spied her cubs across a vast, foggy pool. Across it, her cubs swam to her.

**中文理解：** 麻醉镖的释放声在风雨中回荡，母狮无声地倒下，再次被人类击伤。湿透、被雷声震聋、被疼痛制服，她慢慢放松然后入睡，梦见她在一片广阔的雾池中窥见她的幼崽。穿过水面，幼崽们向她游来。

**关键词：** tranquilizer dart / silently the lioness fell / once again wounded / Sodden, deafened by thunder / subdued by pain / dreaming that she spied her cubs / swam to her

**为什么这样写：** 捕获的"静默"——"silently the lioness fell" 的无声与之前的"roar" 形成对照，"dreaming that she spied her cubs" 是麻醉后的幻觉，"swam to her" 的意象是母性的终极渴望：与幼崽重逢。

---

> **原句 8:** the fact that then I dreamt Leo told me there was some big explosion mentioned in the news, but I checked when I got up and there hasn't been any explosion anywhere, though we'll probably all be nuked any minute, the fact that in another dream I dreamt we were both eating lettuce sitting up in bed, and in another dream, the speed limit in Newcomerstown was nine miles per hour and it was so pleasant

**中文理解：** 事实是我接着梦到利奥告诉我新闻里提到了某次大爆炸，但我起床后查了一下任何地方都没有爆炸，尽管我们大概随时都会被核弹击中，事实是另一个梦里我梦到我们坐在床上吃生菜，还有一个梦里，纽康默斯敦的限速是每小时九英里，这真令人愉快。

**关键词：** big explosion / nuked any minute / eating lettuce / speed limit / nine miles per hour / so pleasant

**为什么这样写：** 梦境的"核恐惧"——"nuked any minute" 是叙述者对核战争的持续焦虑，"eating lettuce" 的平凡与"nuked" 的极端形成对照，"speed limit nine miles per hour" 的"pleasant" 是叙述者对"慢"的渴望：世界能否暂停，让她喘息？

## 一句话总结
在受伤与被捕之间——母狮的"公开呼唤"是母性对恐惧的超越，而狗的"无条件跟随"是忠诚的纯粹形式。
