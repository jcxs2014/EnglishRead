# Chapter 10

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：派对上 Jasper 与 Frankie 近距离对峙，Jasper 质疑她的动机；Madam Margaret 用扇子拍打 Jasper 打断紧张气氛。
- **核心冲突**：Jasper 的怀疑 vs. Frankie 的掩饰；公开场合的社交礼仪 vs. 私人试探。
- **人物关系**：Jasper ↔ Frankie（权力博弈升级）；Madam Margaret → Jasper（长辈权威干预）。
- **本章结构**：派对场景 → Jasper 接近 Frankie 质问 → 肢体接近引发张力 → Madam Margaret 介入打断 → Frankie 逃脱。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** ""Miss Turner, I know a bluff when I hear one," he said, his lips hovering far too close to the naked skin of her neck as he halted her escape."

**中文理解**：Jasper 识破 Frankie 的虚张声势，嘴唇几乎贴到她裸露的颈部皮肤，制造强烈的身体威胁和吸引。

**句子结构**：引语（I know a bluff...）+ 伴随状语（his lips hovering...）+ 时间状语从句（as he halted...）；"far too close" 强调不当距离。

**关键词**：bluff, hovering, naked skin, halted, escape

**为什么这样写**：经典言情张力场景——男性主导的肢体接近既威胁又诱惑；"naked skin" 强调脆弱性；"halted her escape" 展现控制力。

> **原句 2:** ""Mr. Jones," she replied in her best stern governess voice, "unhand me.""

**中文理解**：Frankie 用最严厉的家庭教师声音要求 Jasper 放手，试图用职业身份拉开距离。

**句子结构**：简单命令句（unhand me）；"best stern governess voice" 讽刺性地承认她在表演角色。

**关键词**：stern, governess voice, unhand

**为什么这样写**：展现 Frankie 的防御机制——退回职业角色寻求保护；"unhand" 古雅用词符合维多利亚时代语境；与她之前的直率形成对比，显示紧张。

> **原句 3:** ""Have some propriety, young man," Madam Margaret chided, slapping him with her fan as she tottered past, drawing more than a few amused looks. It was a rare occurrence to see the fearsome Jasper Jones put in his place by an eighty-year-old maiden aunt."

**中文理解**：Madam Margaret 用扇子拍打 Jasper 责备他失礼，八十岁的老姑妈让令人畏惧的 Jasper Jones 当众难堪，引来旁观者 amusement。

**句子结构**：引语 + 动作描写（slapping... tottered...）+ 评论句（It was a rare occurrence...）；幽默反差（fearsome vs. put in his place）。

**关键词**：propriety, chided, fan, tottered, put in his place

**为什么这样写**：喜剧 relief 缓解紧张气氛；展现家族动态——即使是最可怕的人也有长辈能管束他；"tottered"（蹒跚）与 "slapping" 形成可爱反差。

> **原句 4:** ""Indeed, yet I did not expect her to present so differently. I will have to keep a closer eye on her now. She is only fifteen and I will not abide her drawing the attention of desperate lords with one foot in the grave hoping for an arranged match.""

**中文理解**：Jasper 表达对 Cecelia 过早成熟的担忧，誓言保护她不被年老贵族追求用于包办婚姻。

**句子结构**：转折句（yet I did not expect...）+ 决心句（I will have to...）+ 原因解释（She is only fifteen...）；"one foot in the grave" 夸张形容年老。

**关键词**：present, keep a closer eye, abide, desperate lords, one foot in the grave, arranged match

**为什么这样写**：强化 Jasper 的保护者角色——他不是冷漠的监护人；"desperate lords with one foot in the grave" 生动描绘掠夺性婚姻市场；呼应 Dowry Thieves 主题。

> **原句 5:** "Frankie's breath caught in her lungs and her fingertips tingled in her gloves. "You do not believe in marriages with a large age difference in general, or only when it applies to Cecelia?""

**中文理解**：Frankie 被 Jasper 的话触动，试探他是否普遍反对年龄悬殊的婚姻，还是只关心 Cecelia。

**句子结构**：身体反应描写（breath caught / fingertips tingled）+ 选择疑问句（in general, or only...）；手套内的 tingling 暗示压抑的情感。

**关键词**：breath caught, fingertips tingled, large age difference

**为什么这样写**：关键试探——Frankie 在评估 Jasper 的道德一致性；如果他是 Dowry Thieves 主谋，不会真诚反对包办婚姻；身体反应泄露她对 Jasper 的吸引。

> **原句 6:** "If he was lying about his feelings on marriage then he was doing a masterly job of it, and if he was not, then as the Dove suspected, he was innocent of the dowry thieving scheme. No man who felt as he did could coldly sentence a woman to a life of matrimonial misery against her will."

**中文理解**：Frankie 内心推理——如果 Jasper 说谎，他演得很好；如果他说真话，那他就如 Dove 所疑与 Dowry Thieves 无关，因为真心反对包办婚姻的人不会强迫女性进入不幸婚姻。

**句子结构**：条件复合句（If he was lying... and if he was not...）+ 结论句（No man who felt...）；逻辑推理展现 Frankie 的分析能力。

**关键词**：masterly job, innocent, dowry thieving scheme, coldly sentence, matrimonial misery

**为什么这样写**：关键转折点——Frankie 开始相信 Jasper 清白；"coldly sentence" 强烈谴责包办婚姻的道德罪恶；为后续她放弃调查埋下伏笔（但仍需搜查书房确认）。

## 可迁移表达

- **"know a bluff when I hear one"**：听得出何时有人在虚张声势
- **"put in his place"**：让人难堪 / 教训某人
- **"one foot in the grave"**：半截入土（形容年老）
- **"doing a masterly job of it"**：演得天衣无缝

## 一句话总结

派对上 Jasper 近距离质问 Frankie，Madam Margaret 用扇子打断；Jasper 表达对 Cecelia 的保护和对包办婚姻的反对，Frankie 内心推理他开始相信 Jasper 与 Dowry Thieves 无关，但仍需搜查书房确认。
