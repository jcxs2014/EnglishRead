# Chapter 46

## 导航
- **一句话概括**：舞会上Jasper嫉妒Lord Falmouth对Frankie的注视并向Cecelia承认爱意；Mrs. Turner与Jasper和解并透露Frankie也爱他；Lady Evelyn假道歉后将Jasper引至阳台，Jasper遭人从背后袭击昏迷
- **核心冲突**：Jasper的爱意未获回应 vs. Frankie的自我怀疑；表面欢乐舞会 vs. 暗藏杀机
- **人物关系**：Mrs. Turner→Jasper（态度软化并透露秘密）；Lady Evelyn→Jasper（虚假道歉实则设局）
- **本章结构**：舞会开场 → Jasper嫉妒Falmouth → 与Cecelia对话承认爱意 → 与Frankie调情 → Mrs. Turner跳舞并道歉 → Lady Evelyn引诱Jasper至阳台 → Jasper被击昏

> **原句 1:** "'Uncle Jasper, you must stop staring at Miss Turner like that.' Cecelia's sausage curls dangled like fringe from a lampshade, and when he gave her an extra swing they fanned out from her scalp."
> - **中文理解**：Cecelia说Jasper必须停止那样盯着Frankie看；她的香肠卷发型像灯罩流苏一样垂着，当他额外摆动时从头皮扇开
> - **句子结构**：对话加生动比喻描写Cecelia的发型
> - **关键词**：**sausage curls** (香肠卷发型), **fringe** (流苏), **fanned out** (扇形展开)
> - **为什么这样写**：Cecelia的敏锐观察力再次展现；"sausage curls"是维多利亚时代少女典型发型

> **原句 2:** "'Like you want to throw her on the back of your horse and gallop away.' It wasn't that far off the mark."
> - **中文理解**：Cecelia说Jasper的眼神像要把Frankie扔到马背上疾驰而去；Jasper心想这差不离
> - **句子结构**：比喻加内心确认
> - **关键词**：**gallop** (疾驰), **off the mark** (偏离事实)
> - **为什么这样写**：Cecelia的比喻夸张但准确；"not that far off the mark"承认欲望

> **原句 3:** "Ever since Frankie had nearly taken a bullet, he'd thought of nothing other than how to keep her safe so that he could spend the rest of her long life telling her every day how much she meant to him."
> - **中文理解**：自从Frankie差点中枪后，他只想着如何保护她，以便在她漫长余生中每天告诉她她对他有多重要
> - **句子结构**：时间状语从句加目的状语，表达长期承诺
> - **关键词**：**taken a bullet** (中枪), **meant to him** (对他的意义)
> - **为什么这样写**：枪击事件强化了Jasper的保护欲和对失去的恐惧；"long life"显示他希望与她共度余生

> **原句 4:** "That didn't mean it didn't sting. In life Jasper always went for it all. He had not wanted a gaming hell; he had wanted the most successful gaming hell. He had not striven to live comfortably; he'd wanted to buy crystal chandeliers and outrageously expensive Scotch."
> - **中文理解**：这不代表不痛心；Jasper在生活中总是追求极致——他不只要赌场，而要最成功的赌场；他不只要舒适生活，而要水晶吊灯和极其昂贵的威士忌
> - **句子结构**：排比句展示Jasper的完美主义性格
> - **关键词**：**sting** (痛心), **striven** (努力), **outrageously** (过分地)
> - **为什么这样写**：解释Jasper的性格底色——从不满足于平庸；这与他对Frankie的全情投入一致

> **原句 5:** "Jasper never did anything half-measure, but with Frankie he had shown his cards and she had said thank you. For the first time in his life, he was willing to take what he could get, because he could not imagine a life without her, even if she was never able to love him the way he loved her."
> - **中文理解**：Jasper从不做半途而废的事，但对Frankie他摊牌后她只说了谢谢；生平第一次他愿意接受能得到的任何回报，因为无法想象没有她的生活，即使她永远不能以同样方式爱他
> - **句子结构**：对比句（never half-measure vs. take what he could get），显示情感例外
> - **关键词**：**half-measure** (半途而废), **shown his cards** (摊牌), **take what he could get** (接受能得到的)
> - **为什么这样写**：Jasper的妥协是他爱情的最高证明；"shown his cards"是赌博隐喻

> **原句 6:** "'It is not how he is holding her, it is how he is looking at her.' Jasper clenched his teeth together. Lord Falmouth was gazing at Frankie with such calculating lust that it was taking everything Jasper had not to walk over there and smack the thought right out of the man's head."
> - **中文理解**：Jasper说问题不在于Falmouth怎么抱Frankie，而在于他怎么看她；Falmouth用算计的欲望凝视Frankie，Jasper极力克制才没走过去打醒他
> - **句子结构**：对比句区分行为与意图，"calculating lust"精准描述猥亵目光
> - **关键词**：**clenched** (咬紧), **calculating** (算计的), **lust** (欲望), **smack** (拍打)
> - **为什么这样写**：Jasper的嫉妒源于对Frankie的占有欲和保护欲；"calculating lust"暗示Falmouth视Frankie为猎物

> **原句 7:** "'Miss Turner can take care of herself. You know what I think? I think she agreed to marry you to save her reputation, but you agreed to marry her because you love her.' ... 'You are correct. I love Miss Turner and I told her so today.'"
> - **中文理解**：Cecelia说Frankie能照顾自己，她认为Frankie为保全名声同意结婚，而Jasper是因为爱；Jasper承认并告诉Cecelia他今天已表白
> - **句子结构**：Cecelia的分析加Jasper的坦白
> - **关键词**：**save her reputation** (保全名声)
> - **为什么这样写**：Cecelia的洞察力再次得到验证；Jasper对侄女的诚实显示信任

> **原句 8:** "'Someday I hope I make a man look as sick as you do right now, Uncle Jasper.'"
> - **中文理解**：Cecelia叹息说希望有一天她能让一个男人像Jasper现在这样看起来病恹恹的（因爱而痛苦）
> - **句子结构**：幽默比喻形容恋爱中的痛苦表情
> - **关键词**：**sick** (病态的/痛苦的)
> - **为什么这样写**：Cecelia用"sick"形容Jasper因爱而痛苦的表情，青少年式的幽默；显示她对爱情的浪漫想象

> **原句 9:** "The remaining seven Scott Silver investors were tight-lipped and pale. If Devon's murder had been a message to them, they'd received it loud and clear."
> - **中文理解**：剩下的七位Scott Silver投资者紧闭双唇、面色苍白；如果Devon的死是给他们的信息，他们已清楚收到
> - **句子结构**：观察加推测，"loud and clear"强调信息的明确性
> - **关键词**：**tight-lipped** (紧闭双唇的/沉默的), **loud and clear** (清楚地)
> - **为什么这样写**：谋杀作为威慑手段的效果显现；投资者意识到自己可能是下一个目标

> **原句 10:** "Six glass-bead chandeliers dripped from the barrel-vaulted ceiling and reflected prisms across the waxed wooden floor. The ballroom was stuffy despite the opened doors, and the resulting perspiration enhanced the floral perfumes and licorice-scented colognes so that the dance floor was a dizzying sensory assault."
> - **中文理解**：六盏玻璃珠吊灯从桶形拱顶垂下，在打蜡木地板上反射出棱镜光斑；尽管门开着，舞厅仍闷热，汗水混合花香香水和甘草味古龙水，使舞池成为令人眩晕的感官冲击
> - **句子结构**：多感官描写（视觉/嗅觉/触觉），营造压抑氛围
> - **关键词**：**barrel-vaulted** (桶形拱顶的), **prisms** (棱镜), **stuffy** (闷热的), **licorice** (甘草)
> - **为什么这样写**：奢华环境与潜在危险的对比；"sensory assault"暗示过度刺激下的判断力下降

> **原句 11:** "And yet beneath the buzz of energy, darker currents swirled: fear, danger, and imminent violence. Jasper had, on occasion, felt it in his own club, and it was always a race to identify the brewing conflict and neutralize it before it came to a head."
> - **中文理解**：然而在能量嗡嗡声之下，暗流涌动：恐惧、危险和迫在眉睫的暴力；Jasper在自己的俱乐部也曾感受过，总是要竞速识别酝酿中的冲突并在爆发前中和它
> - **句子结构**：转折句揭示表面下的危险，类比赌场经验
> - **关键词**：**currents** (水流/潮流), **imminent** (迫在眉睫的), **brewing** (酝酿中的), **neutralize** (中和)
> - **为什么这样写**：Jasper的赌场管理经验使他擅长识别危险信号；"race"强调时间紧迫性

> **原句 12:** "'This gown would look better lying on my chamber floor.' Frankie burst out laughing. 'That was not the reaction I was hoping for.'"
> - **中文理解**：Jasper调情说这件礼服躺在他房间地板上会更好看；Frankie大笑；Jasper说这不是他期望的反应
> - **句子结构**：挑逗加幽默反应
> - **关键词**：**burst out laughing** (突然大笑)
> - **为什么这样写**：Jasper试图用色情挑逗缓解紧张气氛，但Frankie的笑声打破预期；显示两人关系的轻松面

> **原句 13:** "She pressed her fist to her chest where the thin, silver chain disappeared between her breasts. He knew his ring was on the end of it, and it gave him an odd, savage sense of satisfaction that she still wore it."
> - **中文理解**：Frankie握拳按在胸前，细银链消失在双乳之间；Jasper知道戒指在链子末端，她仍戴着它给他一种奇异的、原始的满足感
> - **句子结构**：动作描写加内心感受
> - **关键词**：**savage** (原始的/野蛮的), **satisfaction** (满足感)
> - **为什么这样写**：戒指作为占有符号的意义；"savage satisfaction"显示Jasper的原始占有欲

> **原句 14:** "'How many women have you said that to, Jasper?' He started to say none, and then realized that might not exactly be true. Chagrined, he said, 'I shall spend the rest of my life coming up with original ways to ask you to disrobe.'"
> - **中文理解**：Frankie问Jasper对多少女人说过这话；他本想说不，但意识到不完全属实；懊恼地说他将用余生想出原创的方式请她脱衣
> - **句子结构**：问答加自我修正，幽默化解尴尬
> - **关键词**：**chagrined** (懊恼的), **disrobe** (脱衣)
> - **为什么这样写**：Jasper的诚实（不撒谎说none）显示他对Frankie的尊重；"original ways"承诺未来创意

> **原句 15:** "'Stop looking at me like that,' she whispered. 'Like what?' 'Like you are thinking of me in the nude.' 'But I am.' ... 'What a coincidence, because I am thinking of you in the nude as well.'"
> - **中文理解**：Frankie让Jasper停止那样看她；她说他像在想象她裸体；他承认是；她说真巧，她也在想象他裸体
> - **句子结构**：对话往复，最后反转
> - **关键词**：**nude** (裸体), **coincidence** (巧合)
> - **为什么这样写**：相互的性幻想坦白显示两人关系的平等和开放；Frankie的主动回应打破女性被动刻板印象

> **原句 16:** "Jasper spun her in a tight circle. 'I am thinking I have not seen a single scrap of sapphire-blue silk tonight.' 'Neither have I. While I dressed this evening, I made a list of all the guests who are also members of Parliament.'"
> - **中文理解**：Jasper旋转Frankie时说今晚没看到任何蓝宝石丝绸碎片；Frankie说她也没有，并且列出了所有是议员的宾客名单
> - **句子结构**：对话交换情报
> - **关键词**：**scrap** (碎片), **members of Parliament** (议员)
> - **为什么这样写**：两人在舞会中仍在调查；Frankie的系统性方法（列名单）显示她的组织能力

> **原句 17:** "'More than thirty. But, Jasper, I cannot shake the feeling that I am missing something right in front of me.'"
> - **中文理解**：Frankie说有三十多名议员宾客，但她无法摆脱感觉——有些东西就在眼前却被她忽略了
> - **句子结构**：数量陈述加直觉表达
> - **关键词**：**shake the feeling** (摆脱感觉), **missing something** (忽略某事)
> - **为什么这样写**：Frankie的直觉预警；"right in front of me"暗示线索明显但被忽视

> **原句 18:** "'It would be proper for a gentleman to ask his future mother-in-law to dance.' ... 'You defended my daughter in front of the Duke of Houndsbury today.' ... 'Why?' 'Because Frankie is incredible, and everyone should know it.'"
> - **中文理解**：Mrs. Turner说绅士应该邀请未来岳母跳舞；她问Jasper为何在公爵面前为Frankie辩护；Jasper回答因为Frankie非凡，每个人都应知道
> - **句子结构**：社交礼仪开场转入实质对话
> - **关键词**：**mother-in-law** (岳母/婆婆)
> - **为什么这样写**：Mrs. Turner用舞蹈作为对话掩护；Jasper的回答简单但有力

> **原句 19:** "'Someone shot at my daughter today and you saved her life. I do not often have occasion to admit that I am wrong, but I am beginning to think that you are not all your reputation makes you out to be.'"
> - **中文理解**：Mrs. Turner说有人今天向她女儿开枪而Jasper救了她；她不常承认错误，但开始认为Jasper并非传闻中那样
> - **句子结构**：事实陈述加观点修正
> - **关键词**：**occasion** (场合/机会), **reputation** (名声)
> - **为什么这样写**：Mrs. Turner的态度转变基于Jasper的行动而非言辞；"not all your reputation makes you out to be"是含蓄的赞美

> **原句 20:** "'I am not perfect, Mrs. Turner. I have committed my share of misdeeds, but I love your daughter, and for the rest of my life I will work to ensure her every happiness.'"
> - **中文理解**：Jasper承认自己不完美，做过错事，但爱她女儿并将用余生确保她的幸福
> - **句子结构**：坦白加承诺
> - **关键词**：**misdeeds** (恶行), **ensure** (确保)
> - **为什么这样写**：Jasper的诚实赢得Mrs. Turner的尊重；"every happiness"是全面承诺

> **原句 21:** "'She loves you, too, I think.' 'I am not sure she does. I am not even sure she believes she is lovable.'"
> - **中文理解**：Mrs. Turner说Frankie也爱Jasper；Jasper不确定，甚至不确定Frankie相信自己值得被爱
> - **句子结构**：对话揭示认知差异
> - **关键词**：**lovable** (值得爱的)
> - **为什么这样写**：Mrs. Turner作为母亲的洞察 vs. Jasper的不安全感；Frankie的自我价值问题是核心障碍

> **原句 22:** "'I fear you will have to undo some of the damage I have inflicted. Francis could use someone on her side. I have not always been that person.'"
> - **中文理解**：Mrs. Turner担心Jasper需要修复她造成的伤害；Frankie需要支持者，而她并不总是那个人
> - **句子结构**：自我批判加请求
> - **关键词**：**undo** (修复), **inflicted** (施加), **on her side** (支持她)
> - **为什么这样写**：Mrs. Turner承认母职失败；"on her side"是Frankie从未体验过的支持

> **原句 23:** "'I require an audience with you, Mr. Jones.' ... 'Please,' she pleaded. 'I wish to make amends. I left Miss Turner alone while we were playing sardines, and I never should have. It is my fault you are betrothed to her now.'"
> - **中文理解**：Lady Evelyn要求与Jasper谈话，请求道歉，说她在捉迷藏游戏中留Frankie一人是错误的，导致Jasper被迫与她订婚
> - **句子结构**：正式请求加虚假道歉
> - **关键词**：**audience** (接见/谈话), **make amends** (弥补/道歉)
> - **为什么这样写**：Evelyn用正式语言（audience）制造重要性假象；"my fault"假装承担责任

> **原句 24:** "'Not in here,' she implored, 'on the balcony.' ... They were not the only ones seeking respite from the heat of the ballroom; in fact, there were so many couples on the balcony that it was nearly as crowded as the dance floor."
> - **中文理解**：Evelyn请求到阳台谈话；那里不只他们寻求凉爽，实际上阳台上情侣众多，几乎和舞池一样拥挤
> - **句子结构**：场景描写显示阳台并非私密
> - **关键词**：**implored** (恳求), **respite** (暂缓/休息)
> - **为什么这样写**：阳台的拥挤本应提供安全保障，但Evelyn进一步引向花园楼梯，显示预谋

> **原句 25:** "'This is far enough,' he said flatly. ... 'Very well, Mr. Jones. I have asked you out here so that I can apologize away from prying eyes. The things I have said about Miss Turner in the past were cruel and careless, and I never should have left her alone during the game of sardines.' The apology sounded fluid and sincere, but her expression was far from contrite. In the pale moonlight, her eyes were as cunning as ever."
> - **中文理解**：Jasper拒绝跟随Evelyn进入花园；Evelyn回到他身边道歉，说过去对Frankie的言论残忍轻率；道歉听起来流畅真诚，但表情毫不悔恨，月光下眼睛依旧狡黠
> - **句子结构**：对话加表情对比描写
> - **关键词**：**flatly** (断然地), **contrite** (悔恨的), **cunning** (狡黠的)
> - **为什么这样写**：Jasper的界限设定（不进花园）显示警惕；Evelyn的表情背叛了她的言辞

> **原句 26:** "'Mr. Jones, I have brought you out here for more than the opportunity to make amends. I must ask, and please do not mistake my motivations because I ask only for your benefit, what do you really know about your betrothed?'"
> - **中文理解**：Evelyn说她带Jasper出来不只为道歉，还要问他真的了解未婚妻多少；她声称动机是为Jasper好
> - **句子结构**：揭露真实目的加伪善声明
> - **关键词**：**motivations** (动机), **benefit** (利益)
> - **为什么这样写**：Evelyn的真实意图浮现——离间Jasper和Frankie；"for your benefit"是操控话术

> **原句 27:** "'Surely you can see how she riles the men with her uncouth mathematics displays and talk of equality. It is a woman's duty to provide a beautiful home, robust heirs, and polite conversation. Many doctors warn that a woman risks becoming barren if she engages with the sciences. I would not be surprised to learn that Miss Turner is already unable to have children, and I would not wish that for you.'"
> - **中文理解**：Evelyn说Frankie用粗俗的数学展示和平等言论激怒男性；女人职责是提供美丽家园、健康继承人和礼貌交谈；许多医生警告女性从事科学会导致不孕，Frankie可能已无法生育
> - **句子结构**：列举传统价值观加伪科学恐吓
> - **关键词**：**riles** (激怒), **uncouth** (粗俗的), **robust heirs** (健康继承人), **barren** (不孕的)
> - **为什么这样写**：Evelyn使用当时社会对知识女性的偏见攻击Frankie；"doctors warn"是常见的反女权伪科学

> **原句 28:** "She tapped her folded fan to his breastbone and slowly dragged it down his chest. 'There are other women more suited to the position of Mrs. Jones. Other women like me.'"
> - **中文理解**：Evelyn用折叠的扇子轻敲他的胸骨并慢慢向下拖曳；她说有其他更适合做Jones夫人的女人，比如她自己
> - **句子结构**：挑逗动作加直接自荐
> - **关键词**：**breastbone** (胸骨), **suited** (适合的)
> - **为什么这样写**：Evelyn的身体接触是性诱惑；自荐显示她的野心

> **原句 29:** "Jasper caught Evelyn's wrist in a firm grasp. 'Not in this lifetime, my lady.'"
> - **中文理解**：Jasper紧紧抓住Evelyn的手腕，说这辈子都不可能
> - **句子结构**：动作加简短拒绝
> - **关键词**：**firm grasp** (紧握), **lifetime** (一生)
> - **为什么这样写**：Jasper的坚决拒绝显示忠诚；"Not in this lifetime"是彻底否定

> **原句 30:** "Jasper felt the brush of air at his back a split second before pain exploded in his skull, and the stars in the night sky faded."
> - **中文理解**：Jasper在后背感到空气拂过半秒后，疼痛在头骨中爆发，夜空中的星星逐渐消失
> - **句子结构**：感官顺序描写模拟被击昏过程
> - **关键词**：**brush of air** (空气拂过), **exploded** (爆发), **faded** (消失)
> - **为什么这样写**：章节结尾的悬念反转；"stars faded"既是字面也是隐喻意识丧失

## 可迁移表达

1. **"not that far off the mark"** — 形容某说法接近事实
2. **"shown his cards"** — 摊牌，暴露真实意图（赌博隐喻）
3. **"take what he could get"** — 接受能得到的任何回报，降低期望
4. **"calculating lust"** — 形容带有算计成分的欲望目光
5. **"loud and clear"** — 非常清楚地（接收信息）
6. **"beneath the buzz of energy, darker currents swirled"** — 形容表面热闹下的暗流涌动
7. **"a race to identify the brewing conflict"** — 形容在冲突爆发前识别并处理的紧迫感
8. **"savage sense of satisfaction"** — 原始的满足感
9. **"coming up with original ways to ask you to disrobe"** — 幽默表达未来创意的承诺
10. **"I cannot shake the feeling that I am missing something"** — 表达直觉上的不安
11. **"undo some of the damage I have inflicted"** — 承认自己造成伤害并希望修复
12. **"on her side"** — 表示支持和站队
13. **"away from prying eyes"** — 避开窥探的私下场合
14. **"Not in this lifetime"** — 彻底拒绝，这辈子都不可能
