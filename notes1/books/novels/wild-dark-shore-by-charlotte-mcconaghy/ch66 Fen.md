# 66. Fen

## 本章导航
- **一句话概括**：Fen 在风暴中照顾海豹群；她意识到自己必须离开；Hank 出现在船屋；她决定帮他上船。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮——Fen 的"宽恕"与"界限"冲突：她"帮" Hank 但"不原谅"。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**：The victim——Fen 的"受害者"身份在"帮助"中消解；The monster——Hank 的"疯狂"是"疾病"。
- **人物弧线**：Fen 从"恐惧"到"力量"——她"面对" Hank。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角，Fen 的"内心独白"贯穿全章。

## 精读

① "As she makes her way back down to the beach, Fen is distraught by how little attention she has given the colony in the last few days. She can hardly see them through the rain, has to run the whole length of the beach before they appear, huddled together in an effort to protect the little ones. Though really it is not a beach anymore. Just a thin line of rocky coastline. The black sand has been eaten. There is no sign of any penguins, and the birds she can see in the sky are less flying than being thrown. Fen could almost be taken off her feet, too—she feels as though if she let herself she could be carried away—but she loves these creatures, so she will be solid and heavy, she will keep her feet anchored to the ground."

- 中文理解：当她走回海滩时，Fen 对自己在过去几天里对海豹群的关注如此之少感到心碎。她几乎看不见它们穿过雨水，必须跑过整个海滩的长度，它们才出现，挤在一起努力保护小海豹。虽然这真的不再是海滩了。只是一条细长的岩石海岸线。黑沙已经被吞噬了。没有任何企鹅的迹象，她在天空中看到的鸟与其说是在飞，不如说是在被抛。Fen 也几乎被风吹走——她感觉如果她允许自己，她就会被带走——但她爱这些生物，所以她会坚定而沉重，她会把脚锚定在地面上。
- 关键词：distraught——心碎；colony——群；huddled——挤在一起；thin——细长的；eaten——吞噬；anchored——锚定。
- 为什么这样写：用"distraught"建立 Fen 的"内疚"——她"感到"内疚。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"不再是海滩"是全书的"失去"主题——她"失去"了海滩。

② "King Brown sees her first, he lifts his head and barks where have you been? She strokes his back as she steps past him into the huddle, watching for babies though they are much bigger now, almost ready for the water seeking them. Some of the mothers are barking at her too, Silver and Tiny, but they quiet when she sits down among them. The rain is smashing them all and the larger seals are doing their best to protect the pups. A storm like this could kill all the juveniles not yet ready to go to sea, and any of the adults who stay with them. Fen moves some of the babies into more sheltered spots behind the big males, but she can hardly see—even with her hood raised there is a steady stream of water in her eyes. Some of the seals abandon the colony, flopping into the water and disappearing beneath the waves to where they are safer. But those with pups stay, they will stay as long as they can. Fen thinks she will too but the wind and the rain are pummeling her and the waves are sweeping under her, threatening to wash her out. She is gripping at the rocks with frozen fingers. She doesn't know what to do, how to help them. She loves them but she doesn't think she ought to die here because of that love. Once, not so long ago when she was a child, she might have thought that the noble thing to do. Now she feels very far from a child, and if nearly being murdered has taught her anything it's that she wants to live long enough to get off this island. The salt of it lives in her veins; it is such a part of her that it is slowly killing her."

- 中文理解：King Brown 先看到她，它抬起头吠叫你去哪里了？她抚摸着它的背，走进兽群，看着小海豹，虽然它们现在大得多，几乎准备好迎接寻找它们的海水。一些母海豹也对她吠叫，Silver 和 Tiny，但当她坐在它们中间时就安静了。雨在砸它们，较大的海豹尽力保护幼崽。这样的风暴可能会杀死所有尚未准备好下海的幼崽，以及任何与它们待在一起的成年海豹。Fen 把一些幼崽移到雄性海豹身后更隐蔽的地方，但她几乎看不见——即使她拉起兜帽，眼睛也有稳定的水流。一些海豹放弃了兽群，跳进水里，消失在波浪下更安全的地方。但有幼崽的海豹留下，它们会尽可能久地留下。Fen 想她也会，但风雨在猛打她，波浪在她身下扫过，威胁要冲走她。她用手指抓着岩石。她不知道该怎么做，如何帮助它们。她爱它们，但她不认为她应该因为那种爱而死在这里。曾经，不久前的童年，她可能会认为这是高尚的事。现在她觉得自己离孩子很远，如果差点被谋杀教会了她什么，那就是她想活得足够长离开这个岛。它的盐分生活在她的血管里；它是她的一部分，正在慢慢杀死她。
- 关键词：King Brown——国王棕（海豹名）；barks——吠叫；strokes——抚摸；smashing——砸；juveniles——幼崽；sheltered——隐蔽；pummeling——猛打；wash out——冲走；gripping——抓；frozen——冰冷的；noble——高尚；murdered——谋杀；veins——血管；slowly killing——慢慢杀死。
- 为什么这样写：用"slowly killing"建立 Fen 的"真相"——盐分"杀死"她。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"离开"是全书的"生存"主题——她"想"活。

③ "You'll have to swim," she says aloud, though they've never spoken each other's languages and the seals can't hear her over the storm anyway. She looks at the pups and says it again. "You're ready. Make yourselves ready. Let your bodies do what they were born to do and swim." All she can do is urge them. She can put all the years they have spent together into her urging, and hope that whatever connection they have goes beyond their species. Or, if she is nothing among them, at most an oddity—if they hardly notice her, as she most often suspects—then she will hope it's their own nature they listen to, that ancient call to the waves. It is too soon, they are too young, but sometimes the world asks more of us."

- 中文理解："你们必须游泳，"她大声说，尽管它们从未说过彼此的语言，海豹在风暴中也听不到她。她看着幼崽们又说了一遍。"你们准备好了。让你们自己准备好。让你们的身体做它们生来要做的事，游泳。"她所能做的就是催促它们。她可以把它们在一起度过的所有岁月都投入到她的催促中，希望它们拥有的任何联系超越物种。或者，如果她在它们中间什么都不是，至多是个奇怪的东西——如果它们几乎注意不到她，正如她最常怀疑的那样——那么她会希望它们听到的是它们自己的本性，那古老的对波浪的呼唤。太早了，它们太年轻了，但有时世界对我们要求更多。
- 关键词：swim——游泳；urge——催促；connection——联系；beyond——超越；species——物种；oddity——奇怪的东西；ancient——古老的；call——呼唤。
- 为什么这样写：用"beyond"建立 Fen 的"希望"——她"希望"联系超越物种。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"太早了"是全书的"残酷"主题——世界"要求"太多。

④ "She wishes them courage, profoundly and with her whole self, and then Fen runs. She is slammed by the wind into the rocks and feels her shoulder jar. It hurts but she doesn't think it's dislocated. Fen steadies herself and keeps going. She is aiming for the boathouse, though god knows if that will hold up in this weather. The closest building after that is the sleeping quarters, but it doesn't have a roof anymore. Beyond that is the hospital, which feels very far away right now."

- 中文理解：她深深地、用她的全部自我祝愿它们有勇气，然后 Fen 跑。她被风砸在岩石上，感到她的肩膀撞击。很痛，但她认为没有脱臼。Fen 稳住自己继续前进。她的目标是船屋，尽管天知道它能不能在这种天气里撑住。之后最近的建筑是宿舍，但它已经没有屋顶了。再远一点是医院，现在感觉非常远。
- 关键词：courage——勇气；profoundly——深深地；slam——砸；jar——撞击；dislocated——脱臼；aiming——目标；boathouse——船屋；hold up——撑住；quarters——宿舍。
- 为什么这样写：用"jar"建立 Fen 的"痛苦"——她"感到"痛。
- 读者视角提示：Fen 的"撑住"是全书的"风暴"主题——风暴"摧毁"一切。

⑤ "She risks the boathouse, keen to get within some walls, and closes the door behind her. Inside it's much quieter, but she can see the remaining Zodiac bobbing wildly and water is spilling onto the floor with each surge of the waves. The roof creaks loudly, threatening to be taken. And then she hears something else. Something distinctly human. Fen peers around in the dim light. She can't see anything. There are goosebumps on her skin. A figure moves. Rising from behind a fuel drum. "Hey, kid," says Hank."

- 中文理解：她冒险进入船屋，渴望进入一些墙壁，关上门。里面安静得多，但她可以看到剩下的 Zodiac 疯狂地上下跳动，每次波浪涌动水都溅到地板上。屋顶大声吱嘎作响，威胁要被带走。然后她听到了别的声音。一些明显是人类的声音。Fen 在昏暗的光线中环顾四周。她什么也看不到。她皮肤上有鸡皮疙瘩。一个人影移动。从燃料桶后面升起。"嘿，孩子，"Hank 说。

⑥ "Raff was back at the lighthouse but Fen has no idea where the rest of her family are. She doesn't know where her dad is. She is alone with a man she has been so frightened of that she has been driven out of her home to live by the sea, a sea into which she always thought she'd have a better chance of escaping him. But today that sea is as violent as this man is."

- 中文理解：Raff 回到了灯塔，但 Fen 不知道她家其他人在哪里。她不知道她爸爸在哪里。她独自和一个男人在一起，她如此害怕他，以至于她被赶出家门住在海边，她总是认为在海边有更好的机会逃离他。但今天那片海和这个男人一样暴力。
- 关键词：rest——其他；driven out——赶出；chance——机会；escaping——逃离；violent——暴力。

⑦ "Fen reaches for something she can use as a weapon, and her hand finds a torch on the bench. "Don't call me that," she says, and she hears a fierceness in her voice that hasn't been there before. "My god," Hank says. "It's got you too." She swallows. "What has?" "You're all mad," he says. And she wonders for a moment if that could be true. If Hank is the one sane soul among them, the rest lost in a shared spiral. They've kept him imprisoned in a basement, after all. That's a pretty crazy thing to do. Half of them talk to ghosts, and she chooses to live with seals, for god's sake. Fen decides it doesn't matter. This man tried to kill her. "What's the plan, Hank?" she asks him. "What are you gonna do?" "I don't know," he says. "I don't think I can go back. Not now. But I'm too much of a coward to die." "You're fine with killing, though?" She is so angry with him. She hates him, hates how small he has made her. "What do you mean?" he asks. "You've been drowning things." "The ocean's been drowning them." "You put my head under the water." He frowns. "Did I? I don't remember that." Fen stares at him and is surprised to feel the hatred trickle away. Instead all she can see is how unwell he is. She knows what she will do now. She will get him safely onto that ship and then she will leave him in the hands of people who will know what to do with him, and she won't think about him ever again, not for a single second of the rest of her life, and that will be her revenge. And she and her dad will talk about all of this, she'll explain to Dom that he did not fail her, that she's powerful, and expanding, and she will tell him she loves him and that she's going to carve a life for herself now, but that she won't be far from them, from her family, not ever."
