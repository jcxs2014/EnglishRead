# Daggerbound · 情感节点（10 节点）

> 引语全部逐字取自原书，说话人与章节已逐一回原文核对。

## 节点一：银眼"天使"（Ch.1–2）

失败的改良者走出神庙，带着一头骡子与一柄没人打开过的旧剑，第三天被三名匪徒打到濒死。他扑在书上护书，手误抓住剑鞘，银色光里现出一个持双刀的人；伤得太重、太晕、太相信神会管书，他把对方认成了天使，并且在昏过去之前坚持要弄清对方的职阶。全书的情感线从这场错认开始。

- 「It’s an angel, thought Edmund fuzzily. It must be. The Many-Armed God has sent a divine messenger to save His books.」（Edmund 内心，Ch.2）
- 「“Have I got it wrong? Are you a throne or … or … a principality?” His vision was beginning to go dark, but he had to know.」（Edmund，Ch.2）
- 「The angel knelt beside him. Edmund looked up into eyes the color of steel and knew that no mortal man had eyes like that.」（叙述，Ch.2）

## 节点二：路边的一次道德选择（Ch.3）

视角转向剑中的囚徒：他坐在路边等这个陌生人冻死，好借对方之死脱身，甚至替昏迷的人回答"这种事常有，别往心里去"。改变他的不是对方的可怜，而是他自己搬出来的那位老妇人（他的救命恩人）的逻辑——未来不可知，所以现在可以救。他生火、喂水、裹伤，然后在夜里对着那具身体连骂三声"我本该让你死"。

- 「“It’s nothing personal,” the Dervish told his wielder. “I’m sure you’re a fine person. It’s just that I need you to die now, if you wouldn’t mind.”」（Dervish，Ch.3）
- 「What happened to you was hard, her shade agreed, in the back of his mind. But this lad had no part of it. He may yet die, or he may live. You might trip on a rock and break your neck. The future can’t be known. Start the damn fire and give him a chance.」（Angharad 的幽灵，Ch.3）
- 「Damn you, he thought at the unconscious man. Damn you. I am bound here to you and I cannot leave. Damn you for the last chain. I should have let you die.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.3）

## 节点三：人不能被交出去（Ch.5）

养伤的日子里两人第一次协商这段关系的规则。Edmund 兴奋于"活剑自带翻译魔法"、算过"值不值得为翻译一本书去死"，直到被一句"我想要什么并不重要"打醒；他随即做出本章最重的判断——人不能被交付、被拿去研究——并提出那个把决定权交还给对方的承诺。

- 「“Yes, but obviously I wouldn’t just hand you over to someone!” said Edmund, appalled at the idea. The Dervish was a person, not an object, even if some long-dead smith had muddled the lines between the two.」（Edmund，Ch.5）
- 「“Would it help if I promised not to tell anyone about you without asking you first?”」（Edmund，Ch.5）
- 「He didn’t want to think about breaking the curse. If he thought that there might be an escape, he would start to hope. He didn’t think he could handle that. Despair had broken him, yes, but hope would grind him into dust.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.5）

## 节点四：雪夜的"锁链"（Ch.11）

四人在松树下挤成一团。Edmund 先是用玩笑顶过一夜，然后在德尔维希噩梦时伸手抱住他；被抱住的人醒来，把那条手臂瞬间读成了锁链——一肘撞破庇护所、滚进雪里、被五磅积雪埋住脸。同一夜的两端：一个人第一次感到"舒适"，另一个人把安慰当成束缚。

- 「Then Edmund shifted a little, only a little, and the Dervish realized that part of the warmth was the scholar’s arm was around him, and it wasn’t tight but it was there, a weight as solid as a … Chain.」（叙述，Ch.11）
- 「He could not remember the last time he’d felt something as simple as comfort.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.11）
- 「It wasn’t until Edmund had actually held him that he realized how much of that was personality, how little actual flesh. The Dervish felt fragile, and Edmund, who could not defend himself, let alone anyone else, had felt a wild urge to protect him.」（Edmund 内心，Ch.11）

## 节点五：两个字（Ch.12）

陪他们走了一路的鼠妇说出目的地是"书之主的神庙"，等于把德尔维希送回他当年的刑房。他起身、手按刀柄，对方毫无防备，只说了两个字："我离开了。"他没有拔刀。此后他躲进林子里哭了一声，又从 Edmund 手里接过了那把剑——第一次有人把牢门的钥匙塞进囚犯手心。

- 「I never got away after all. I’m just on a longer chain.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.12）
- 「The scholar’s face was still bruised and swollen, but his expression was absolutely clear. “I left.” Two words only, but they rang like a bell in the empty hollows of the Dervish’s soul.」（叙述，Ch.12）
- 「If there had been any guilt in the other man’s eyes, even any fear, the Dervish would have struck. But all Edmund’s gaze held was worry, all of it for him.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.12）

## 节点六：桑拿房的坠落（Ch.23）

雨天的调情在蒸汽房里登顶：他确认了那个吻是真的，两人接了第二次吻——这一次是邀请而不是炫耀。然后 Edmund 随口夸了一句"你的纹身真美"，把德尔维希从旧日的自我感觉里踹回现实：他忘了自己现在是什么样子，也想起了那身银线是谁留下的。

- 「It was not the fierce kiss they had shared in the wake of the dragon. Then the Dervish had had something to prove. This was an invitation, not a boast.」（叙述，Ch.23）
- 「And then Edmund said exactly the wrong thing. “You have such beautiful tattoos.”」（叙述，Ch.23）
- 「He’d forgotten. He’d actually forgotten. He’d been preening under an attractive man’s appreciative gaze, and it had been like a breath of his old life back, but he didn’t look like he had back then.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.23）

## 节点七：龙前的那个吻（Ch.18–19）

被农庄卖给龙的那个上午，Edmund 从车上滑下来，在任何人来得及反应之前端端正正吻了他——那个吻其实是把偷来的小刀递进他嘴里。德尔维希一边锯绳一边交代了自己的一生，然后在龙出洞后把对方推向树林，自己朝龙走过去。

- 「Edmund slid down from the wagon bed, turned to the Dervish, and, before anyone could react, kissed him square on the mouth.」（叙述，Ch.18）
- 「Clever. Very clever. And there was absolutely no reason why he should wonder if Edmund had wanted to kiss him at all.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.19）
- 「And even though every fiber of his being was screaming at him, the Dervish shoved Edmund toward the woods and began walking toward the dragon.」（叙述，Ch.19）
- 「“It’s all right,” the Dervish said. “I’m not natural either.”」（Dervish 对龙，Ch.19）

## 节点八：一本被丢下的日记（Ch.30）

Edmund 被掳走后，德尔维希从当铺抢回被小孩拿去典当的剑，又在那间空屋里从桌下捡到对方的日记：树、花、松果、骡子、鼠妇、獾人，然后是他自己——页边几笔的速写、就着火光画的细部，比任何东西都多。

- 「Ah. Pawnbroker. Of course.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.30）
- 「And he saw himself. Over and over, more than anything else in the book, from quick little drawings in the margins that captured only a few lines to detailed studies obviously drawn by firelight.」（叙述，Ch.30）
- 「The Dervish knew nothing at all about art, but he understood love when he saw it.」（叙述，Ch.30）

## 节点九：无尽楼梯上的秘密（Ch.35）

从犬舍一路打到楼上，他把半昏迷的 Edmund 扛着爬一条仿佛没有尽头的楼梯，用"给我讲讲那棵树"维持对方清醒。就在这条楼梯上，他先想明白了 gnole 与 werkblight 的关系（Edmund 沉默七年是为了不让一个族群被屠杀），然后在楼梯尽头听见那句话说出了口。

- 「The Dervish, who would have sworn that his capacity for empathy had been sliced away years ago, found that he was still capable of outrage. He knew what it was like to be chained.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.9）
- 「The last time he’d begged for anything, it was for the pain to stop. The mad thought went through his mind that he would have taken any amount of pain if it meant getting Edmund out of here alive.」（Dervish 内心，Ch.35）
- 「“It’s gnoles,” said Edmund, and fainted dead away.」（Edmund，Ch.35）

## 节点十：山顶放手与出鞘重逢（Ch.36–40）

在崖顶被围住时，他交出剑、把 Edmund 赶下山，用"入鞘即换持有者"的约定堵住对方的反悔，然后抱着敌人跳下悬崖（用那条绑了他五百年的规则）。两周后，Edmund 每晚睡前都会试着拔剑——某个晚上剑终于出鞘，银眼"天使"再次出现；两人互相说完那三个字，并给那个没有被解决的诅咒找到了一个新用途。

- 「He kissed Edmund’s forehead. “If you don’t go,” he said, against the scholar’s hair, “they’ll kill you. And they’ll still have the sword, and nothing will change, except that I’ll know you’re dead. And that will break my heart. Now get out of here before we have to talk about feelings any longer.”」（Dervish，Ch.36）
- 「The Dervish felt something snap next to his heart, the cord between them cut.」（叙述，Ch.36）
- 「He reached for the sword almost reflexively, set his hand on the hilt, and an angel with silver eyes appeared in front of him.」（叙述，Ch.40）
- 「“Absolutely,” the Dervish said. “You’re definitely my type.”」（Dervish，Ch.40）
