# 金句精选

---

**关于记忆与失去**

> The memories I value most, I don't see them ever fading. I lost Ruth, then I lost Tommy, but I won't lose my memories of them.

—— ch23

*这是 Kathy 面对终结时的宣言。不是战胜失去，而是紧紧抓住记忆不放——这是她唯一能做的事。*

---

**关于灵魂的证明**

> We took away your art because we thought it would reveal your souls. Or to put it more finely, we did it to prove you had souls at all.

—— ch22，Miss Emily

*Hailsham 存在的理由。全书最核心的句子之一——连"你们有灵魂"都需要被证明。*

---

**关于保护的代价**

> We sheltered you during those years, and we gave you your childhoods. Lucy was well-meaning enough. But if she'd had her way, your happiness at Hailsham would have been shattered.

—— ch22，Miss Emily

*Miss Emily 对"我们为什么对你们撒谎"的解释。保护与欺骗之间没有界限。*

---

**关于一个新世界的到来**

> I saw a new world coming rapidly. More scientific, efficient, yes. More cures for the old sicknesses. Very good. But a harsh, cruel world. And I saw a little girl, her eyes tightly closed, holding to her breast the old kind world, one that she knew in her heart could not remain, and she was holding it and pleading, never to let her go.

—— ch22，Madame

*Madame 对当年看到 Kathy 跳舞时的描述。全书的点题段落——科学进步背后是温情脉脉的旧世界的消逝。*

---

**关于挽留**

> "Never let me go. Oh, baby, baby. Never let me go..."

—— ch22，歌词引用

*书名的来源。在 Kathy 跳舞时，Madame 听到的是一首关于无法挽留的歌——她看到的却是一个即将失去一切的世界。*

---

**关于分离**

> I keep thinking about this river somewhere, with the water moving really fast. And these two people in the water, trying to hold onto each other, holding on as hard as they can, but in the end it's just too much. The current's too strong. They've got to let go, drift apart. That's how I think it is with us. It's a shame, Kath, because we've loved each other all our lives. But in the end, we can't stay together forever.

—— ch23，Tommy

*Tommy 用河流描述他和 Kathy 的关系。不是激烈地死亡，而是随波逐流、各自分离。*

---

**关于水的秘密**

> Splash, splash, splash.

—— ch23，Tommy

*Tommy 在 Hailsham 进球后双手举起跑回队友时，内心想象自己在踩水。这是他从未告诉任何人的秘密——在告别的时刻，他把它交给了 Kathy。*

---

**关于等待出现的人**

> I half-closed my eyes and imagined this was the spot where everything I'd ever lost since my childhood had washed up, and I was now standing here in front of it, and if I waited long enough, a tiny figure would appear on the horizon across the field, and gradually get larger until I'd see it was Tommy, and he'd wave, maybe even call.

—— ch23，Kathy

*Kathy 在 Norfolk 田野上的幻想。她允许自己片刻的幻想，然后转身回到现实——这是与失去和解的方式。*

---

**关于时间与气候**

> I think what we achieved merits some respect. Look at the two of you. You've turned out well.

—— ch22，Miss Emily

*Miss Emily 对 Hailsham 的辩护。"你们长成了这样"——在所有悲剧之中，这句话依然有其重量。*

---

**关于恐惧**

> Is she afraid of you? We're all afraid of you. I myself had to fight back my dread of you all almost every day I was at Hailsham.

—— ch22，Miss Emily

*最诚实的告白。恐惧不是"坏人的偏见"，而是"好人的本能"。*
