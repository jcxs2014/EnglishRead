# 情感节点

## 1. 皮箱开启（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 家族史作业把 Miranda 送进阁楼，祖母 Ayoola Obe 的皮箱（aso-oke 布/蛇像/贝壳项链/黑盒）开启血统线；当晚黑盒"找"进她的梦，微笑身影登场。

**关键引语：**
- "There was a tag attached to it. She lifted it up to find a name written with a proud flourish, faded but still clear: AYOOLA OBE."（ch01）
- "Like there was no putting back what she'd unleashed."（ch01）

---

## 2. Sarah 之问与巫术店（ch02）

**叙事概括：** Sarah 一句"信仰应该是什么感觉"问倒 Miranda；筹款会装饰羞辱+Esther 两次打断，愤懑的 Miranda 误闯"A Sense of Craft"，店主 Maya 登场。当晚她决定撬盒，"哪怕会要了命"。

**关键引语：**
- "'Shouldn't it be something we embody in a deeper way? How should it feel?'"（ch02，Sarah）
- "The woman was annoyingly unthreatening, as small and perky as an apostrophe"（ch02）
- "She was going to open that box if it killed her."（ch02）

---

## 3. SINKU 与木偶（ch03）

**叙事概括：** 锤劈断、人偶闭眼，Miranda 求助 Maya（"you are the key"）；花园里 Sarah 的"before you have to give it up"递出第二张许可；午夜呼吸显字 SINKU（bury），埋十分钟盒开，木偶现身。

**关键引语：**
- "'Maybe, you are the key. Maybe you already know.'"（ch03，Maya）
- "The Yoruba term 'sinku' translated to an English word: 'bury'."（ch03）
- "'Don't you want to know what you're missing before you have to give it up?'"（ch03，Sarah）

---

## 4. 木偶归来与 Omotola（ch04）

**叙事概括：** Vodun 世界观登场（fetish doll/祖先之灵），Maya 转交 Ifá 祭司名片；Miranda 把木偶扔进垃圾箱，回家发现它回到包里；晚餐战争后得知妈妈旧名 Omotola——"不看过去"是家规。

**关键引语：**
- "'You have a soul made of questions, Miranda. Don't deny it.'"（ch04，Maya）
- "HOW WAS THE DOLL STILL IN HER BAG?"（ch04）
- "Her childhood name had been Omotola, but at some point she'd changed it to Joy."（ch04）

---

## 5. 祭司仪式与血统揭示（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 禁言 Bruce 后 Bruce 失声，Miranda 随 Maya 去 Peckham 见 Babaláwo Adegoke；贝壳悬浮低语吓跑她，但带回真相（木偶属曾曾祖母/Yoruba priestess）；当晚写下第一首给 Sarah 的诗。

**关键引语：**
- "'He said this doll was not your grandmother's, but that it belonged to your great-great-grandmother. That she was a Yoruba priestess.'"（ch05，Maya 转述祭司）
- "'Magic doesn't belong to any culture, Miranda.'"（ch05，Maya）
- "Why am I writing poetry about her?"（ch05）

---

## 6. 仲夏夜：吻、咒、脓疮（ch06）

**叙事概括：** Sleepover 之夜登顶又坠落：装饰成功/妈妈一笑/告白之吻被拒；Esther 当众揭短，Miranda 血咒报复；Esther 脓疮送医，"What have I done?"是全书真正的 scream。Bruce 台阶夜话是唯一的善意。

**关键引语：**
- "'It was Miranda,' she chuckled, as if it was nothing."（ch06，Esther）
- "By the blood of the priestess. By the blood of my veins, Make Esther suffer, Make her feel my shame."（ch06）
- "'I didn't really mean like that. I'm not into you like that …'"（ch06，Sarah）

---

## 7. 三场对峙与午夜埋葬（ch07）

**叙事概括：** 与 Maya 决裂（力量是你）/与 Esther 和解（sex 告白/回避道歉）/与妈妈摊牌（种族创伤/村名与祭司说法一致）；午夜把整个夏天埋进教堂花园（I bury all! I banish thee!），转身仍听见 rattle。

**关键引语：**
- "'I don't think the power is coming from it. I think it's coming from you …'"（ch07，Maya）
- "'My faith became my rock, Miranda. My strength.'"（ch07，妈妈）
- "I bury all! I banish thee!"（ch07）

---

## 8. 番外：绞索与沉默（ch08/ch09）

**叙事概括：** Seven Hanged（七女巫午夜被一句话处决、伦敦照常）立起"话语即绞索"的暴力天花板；Fifteen Years Old（姑姑训练 Anna"听见但不聆听"）与妈妈神学同构——两个"No"夹住 Miranda，而她偏偏两个都做不到。

**关键引语：**
- "Words were spoken: impermeable and unbreakable."（ch08）
- "Below, London carried on as before but the bells of Big Ben had never been so silent."（ch08）
- "Magic is the first sin; we must bear it silently"（ch09，姑姑）
- "She was pleased to find nothing but silence in her heart."（ch09，姑姑）
