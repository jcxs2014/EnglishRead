# Chapter 15: Fusiform Area: Familiar Faces

## 本章导航

- **POV**：第一人称女主（Bee Königswasser）
- **时间线**：新奥尔良会议第二天上午至中午
- **地点**：会议中心 → Sam的早午餐餐厅
- **核心冲突**：Bee必须面对Tim与Annie的公开场合重逢，同时Levi暗中出手保护她；在洗手间与Annie和解迈出和解第一步
- **主要人物**：Bee Königswasser、Levi Ward、Sam（导师）、Tim（前未婚夫）、Annie（前闺蜜）

## 精读

> **原句 1：** "They hired a neuroscientist," Levi says, gaze locked on the podium.

MagTech has solved their output location problems differently. Bee feels queasy as she realizes their competition is ahead—maybe just a tiny bit, but still. The banana she had for breakfast is lurching in her stomach. This is a race, always was, and now the stakes are clearer than ever. "If they patent the technology before we do, they'll just sell it to whatever militia pays the most."

**关键词**：
- `neuroscientist` — 神经科学家
- `gaze locked` — 目光锁定
- `podium` — 讲台

> **原句 2：** "I cannot believe you're here and I didn't know it."
Sam—their Ph.D. advisor—spots them at the conference. She's retiring at the end of the semester and making one last conference appearance. Her eyes widen when she sees them arrive together. "I didn't know there was a 'we,' Levi. I'm so happy for you." Levi quickly clarifies: "Bee and I are working together on a NASA project. Temporarily." His quick denial of any romance while looking slightly panicked reveals how much he's affected by their evolving relationship.

**关键词**：
- `I cannot believe` — 我不敢相信
- `didn't know` — 不知道

> **原句 3：** "Are you sure you want to do this?" Levi asks me calmly once Sam is out of earshot.
"I'm sure I do not want to do this." Bee knows facing Tim and Annie at brunch will be excruciating, but Sam's retirement announcement makes it necessary. Levi establishes a protection plan: "if you're not feeling well, you tell me immediately and I'll take you away." When Bee accidentally calls him "Daddy," she turns and walks away blushing. He offers his hand: "And remember my hand."

**关键词**：
- `Are you sure` — 你确定吗
- `out of earshot` — 听不见了

> **原句 4：** "Levi is not just hugging Tim. He's also gripping his shoulders tightly, fingers digging painfully into Tim's flesh as he murmurs something in his ear."
Tim's mouth pulls in a thin, straight line, his face is milk white. His expression looks almost scared. Levi silently protects Bee in a way more powerful than any confrontation. When Bee asks what he said, Levi deflects: "Look. They have quinoa bowls." The contrast between the violent undercurrent and Levi's casual deflection is darkly comic.

**关键词**：
- `gripping his shoulders` — 紧抓他的肩膀
- `digging painfully` — 痛苦地嵌入
- `murmurs something in his ear` — 在他耳边低语

> **原句 5：** "We're doing great," Levi tells Mike, his tone a mix of big-dick calm and I could kill a man with a beach ball.
When Mike drunkenly asks if Levi "bleaches his brain after every meeting" (a jab at the old rumor that Levi found Bee disgusting), Levi responds devastatingly. He leisurely puts his arm on the back of Bee's chair and plucks a grape from her plate. A deafening silence falls. "What about you, Mike? I heard there were problems with your tenure packet." Mike is chastened, and Bee realizes: "Holy shit. I guess Levi's done eating his potatoes?"

**关键词**：
- `big-dick calm` — 大男子主义式的冷静
- `kill a man with a beach ball` — 用沙滩球杀人

> **原句 6：** "I missed her, too. I miss her all the time, but I won't tell her. Because I hate her. Me and my multitudes."
In the bathroom, Annie waits for Bee. Both are crying. Annie apologizes—"I've been trying to figure it out for years. I just... don't know"—and explains she was jealous of Bee, felt excluded, and made a terrible choice. "Fucking Tim." Bee apologizes too: for not being there when Annie felt inadequate. Annie is glad Bee didn't marry Tim and is "with Levi." When Annie asks if she can text sometimes, Bee says yes. Levi is waiting outside the bathroom, ready to take her hand when she needs to leave.

**关键词**：
- `multitudes` — 复杂多面
- `Me and my multitudes` — 我和我的万千情绪
