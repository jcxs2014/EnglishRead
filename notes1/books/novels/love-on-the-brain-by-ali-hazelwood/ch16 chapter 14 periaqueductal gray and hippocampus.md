## 精读

> **原句 1：** "Deep breaths," he orders. "In and out. Don't think about it, okay? Just breathe. Steady."

After Bee faints at the conference, Levi carries her to his hotel room. He becomes her external frontal lobe, providing structure and calm when her panic attack threatens to overwhelm her. "When I'm like this, a hairbreadth from exploding, I need structure. External frontal lobes. I need someone to think for me until I've calmed down." Bee realizes she's not surprised that Levi is doing this for her—they're evolving beyond their "nemeses" dynamic.

**关键词**：
- `Deep breaths` — 深呼吸
- `In and out` — 吸气呼气
- `Steady` — 稳住

> **原句 2：** "Annie and I had a falling out in our last year of grad school. We haven't talked since."
Bee finally reveals the painful backstory: she, Tim, and Annie were supposed to do postdocs together at Vanderbilt with Schreiber. But Tim was cheating on Bee with Annie—for years. Bee only found out when she confronted him. She took back her cheating fiancé and "tried harder" to make the relationship work before eventually leaving. She couldn't face working with them at Vanderbilt, choosing career suicide over daily torture.

**关键词**：
- `falling out` — 闹翻
- `grad school` — 研究生院

> **原句 3：** "If you fucked me a little more frequently, he'd said. If you were better. If you knew how to enjoy it and make it enjoyable. You could at least put in some effort."
The depth of Tim's emotional manipulation is revealed—he blamed Bee for his infidelity, suggesting she wasn't attractive or attentive enough. Bee's tears flow as she recounts this to Levi, who listens with naked concern. His hand cups her face, thumb warm on her skin. This moment of vulnerability cements their connection: "There's something about the moment we're having. Maybe there's a connection between us. A very odd, very complicated one."

**关键词**：
- `put in some effort` — 做出一些努力
- `make it enjoyable` — 让它愉快

> **原句 4：** "I hate this."
Bee discovers Levi's unexpected training regimen—he's been researching Couch-to-5K programs to help her train for the race she's dreading. "You did research? You did research on how to train me?" She alternately insults and admires his dedication. Levi sets an extremely gentle pace: run 60 seconds, walk 3 minutes, repeat. "In three minutes, you're going to run sixty more seconds." His patience with her dramatic complaints reveals how much he cares.

**关键词**：
- `I hate this` — 我讨厌这个

> **原句 5：** "Outer space is fucking terrifying... space smells like a toilet full of rotten eggs."
Levi reveals he applied for the Astronaut Corps because his NASA family disapproved of his civilian career. He didn't get in (too tall) and was secretly relieved. His five-minute rant about why space is terrifying—recycled urine, eyeball pressure, gamma rays, the smell—makes Bee realize: "God. Oh, God. I think I like him."

**关键词**：
- `fucking terrifying` — 他妈的可怕
- `rotten eggs` — 臭鸡蛋

> **原句 6：** "If tomorrow you see Annie and Tim and feel... like you felt today. Just take my hand, okay?"
After watching The Empire Strikes Back together in Bee's hotel room, Levi offers his hand for support if she sees Tim and Annie again. He establishes a new rule: every time she says he hates her, she has to help express Schrödinger's anal glands. Their banter about Star Wars ("Yoda's a bit of a dick," "All Jedi are dicks—it's the forced celibacy") shows how naturally they interact now. "What are you wearing tomorrow?" "A trench coat. Sunglasses. You brought your fake mustache, right?"

**关键词**：
- `take my hand` — 牵我的手
