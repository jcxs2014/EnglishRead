## 精读

> **原句 1：** "I'm sorry."

"I said it." Bee turns sharply, surprised. Levi apologizes for calling the meeting without her approval, explaining his overly controlling nature stems from being overly invested in BLINK. He admits he wasn't trying to undermine her authority. Bee jokes about maintaining her silent treatment, and they begin to bond over music choices, revealing their evolving dynamic from adversaries to something more genuine.

**关键词**：
- `I'm sorry` — 对不起

> **原句 2：** "They died?"
"Yeah. Freak car accident. Airbags didn't work. They'd been recalled, but..." Bee reveals her traumatic backstory—orphaned at age four with her twin sister Reike, raised by various relatives across multiple countries. She describes the nomadic childhood, the struggle to belong, and how stability became her core desire. Levi listens intently, later observing that she wants "permanency" and "to be somewhere long enough to build a sense of belonging."

**关键词**：
- `They died` — 他们死了？

> **原句 3：** "You are a NASA engineer with Nature publications."
"You kept track of my pubs?" Levi asks, surprised. Bee deflects, claiming Sam talks about him constantly. She offers to defend Levi at Thanksgiving against his military family who disapproves of his civilian career. Levi shares his family's disappointment and the public shame his uncle caused. Bee extends unexpected solidarity: "if next year you need someone to explain exactly how amazing you are at your job—or even just to bitch-slap them—I'm available."

**关键词**：
- `NASA engineer` — 航天局工程师
- `Nature publications` — 《自然》杂志论文

> **原句 4：** "Annie. With a shorter haircut. And a pretty lilac dress."
Standing at the badge reclamation station, Bee spots Tim handing coffee to Annie. Her vision blurs, heart pounds, body shakes. She experiences a panic attack—hot, cold, sweaty, trembling. Levi grounds her: "Bee. Are you okay?" She can't breathe, can't think, feels like she's flying away, scattering into debris. Levi holds her, and for the first time she feels safe just because he's around.

**关键词**：
- `shorter haircut` — 更短的发型
- `lilac dress` — 丁香色连衣裙

> **原句 5：** "Levi is holding me now. Holding me again and I'm in his arms and it feels like I'm safe, how is it possible that when he's around, only when he's around, I really feel sa—"
The passage captures the pivotal emotional shift. After Bee faints at seeing her ex-fiancé and former best friend together, Levi carries her to his hotel room. His presence becomes her anchor during the panic attack. This moment crystallizes the novel's central tension: two people who claim to be enemies yet find profound comfort in each other's presence. The incomplete thought captures Bee's dawning realization about her feelings for Levi.

**关键词**：
- `holding me` — 抱着我
- `in his arms` — 在他怀里
- `I'm safe` — 我安全了

> **原句 6：** "'Jesus,' I mutter once we're alone."
The conference setting serves as a crucible for their relationship. The tight-knit academic community forces interactions with past hurts while also revealing new dimensions of Levi—the elderly engineer he meets, his networking abilities. Bee observes "I saw you smile at least four times," showing how she's attuned to his every move.

**关键词**：
- `Jesus` — 天哪
- `mutter` — 喃喃地说
