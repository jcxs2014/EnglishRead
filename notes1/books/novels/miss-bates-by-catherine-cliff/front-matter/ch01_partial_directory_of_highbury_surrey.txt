Partial Directory of Highbury, Surrey









PARTIAL DIRECTORY OF HIGHBURY, SURREY


Henrietta Bates: Known to herself as Henrie, to others variously as Hetty, Henny, and Miss Bates


John Bates: Vicar of Highbury, her father


Mary Bates: Her mother


Henry Bates: Her younger brother, known as Harry


Jane Bates: Her sister, known as Jeannette; later wife of Lieutenant Fairfax, mother to Jane Fairfax


Mrs. Wren: Vicarage cook


Agnes Twythorpe: Single woman of Highbury


Jenkins: Vicarage farm manager


Fred, John, and Andrew: Students of Mr. Bates


Old Mr. Knightley: Friend and contemporary of Mr. Bates, and owner of Donwell Abbey


Henry Woodhouse: Owner of Hatfield (later called Hartfield), contemporary of Mrs. Bates, father to Isabella and Emma Woodhouse


Lavinia Mott: Contemporary of Miss Bates and, later, second wife to Mr. Woodhouse and mother to Emma Woodhouse


Lieutenant George Fairfax: Husband to Jeannette Bates, father to Jane Fairfax


Jane Fairfax: Orphaned daughter of George Fairfax and Jeannette Bates, niece to Miss Bates


Mr. Higgins: Auctioneer from Guildford


The Campbells: Friends to the Fairfaxes, foster family to Jane Fairfax


Emma Woodhouse: Contemporary of Jane Fairfax


Isabella Woodhouse: Older half-sister to Emma


George Knightley: Son and heir to Old Mr. Knightley, husband to Emma


John Knightley: Younger brother of George, husband to Isabella Woodhouse


Ann Taylor: Governess to Emma, later Mrs. Ann Weston


Samuel Weston: Married first to Miss Churchill, and, after her death, to Miss Taylor


Frank Churchill: Mr. Weston’s son, raised by his Churchill relatives after the death of his mother


Dorothea Goddard: Mistress of the girls’ school in Highbury


Harriet Smith: Parlor boarder at Mrs. Goddard’s school


Philip Elton: Vicar of Highbury


Augusta Elton: His wife


Mrs. Serle: Cook at Hartfield


Mrs. Withers: Nurse to Emma


Amy Webb: Farm wife and wet nurse


Alfred Knightley: Son of George Knightley and Emma Woodhouse
