# 61. The Only Son of Aoife

**作者**：Lady Gregory (retelling of the Cuchulain cycle)

## 一句话总结

Gregory 重述库丘林传说：Aoife 受托养子 Connla，立三诫（尤"不报姓名"），少年寻父、与父 Cuchulain 不知彼此身份而决斗，父以 Gae Bulg 杀子，见金环乃知——一则把"不知而弑子"的悲写成爱尔兰英雄循环的短章。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"And when he was leaving her, he told her what name to give the child, and he gave her a gold ring, and bade her keep it safe till the child grew to be a lad, and till his thumb would fill it; and he bade her send the boy to him when that time came."**

**中文理解**：离她时，他嘱给孩子取何名，给一金环，命她妥藏至儿长成、拇指填满环；并嘱那时送儿来。

**句子结构**：And when he was leaving her 时间；he told her what name to give the child 主谓宾；and he gave her a gold ring 并列；and bade her keep it safe 并列；till the child grew to be a lad 时间；and till his thumb would fill it 并列；and he bade her send the boy 并列。

**关键词**：leaving her（离她）/ what name to give the child（取何名）/ a gold ring（金环）/ keep it safe（妥藏）/ till his thumb would fill it（至拇指填满环）/ send the boy to him（送儿来）

**表达方式**：用"金环+拇指填满"写父训的具体信物，伏笔认亲。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此，让悲剧的钥匙先交到母亲手。

---

② **"Then Aoife gave him the arms of a champion, and bade him go to Ireland, but first she laid three commands on him: the first never to give way to any living person, but to die sooner than be made turn back."**

**中文理解**：Aoife 给他英雄之械，命赴爱尔兰，先立三诫：其一绝不向任何人退让，宁死不回头。

**句子结构**：Then Aoife gave him the arms of a champion 主谓宾；and bade him go to Ireland 并列；but first she laid three commands on him 转折；the first never to give way 同位；but to die sooner than be made turn back 并列。

**关键词**：gave him the arms of a champion（给英雄之械）/ bade him go to Ireland（命赴爱尔兰）/ laid three commands on him（立三诫）/ never to give way（绝不退让）/ die sooner than be made turn back（宁死不回头）

**表达方式**：用"三诫之首=不退让"写母训的刚，恰成弑父的因。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此，让"不报姓名+不退让"把父子推向死局。

---

③ **"It is likely, seeing you come armed as if for fighting, you are gone astray on your journey; but as you are come to Ireland, tell me your name and what your deeds have been, and your victories in the battle."**

**中文理解**："看你武装如赴战，许是途中迷了路；既来爱尔兰，报姓名、陈事迹、说战功。"

**句子结构**：It is likely 主系表；seeing you come armed 状语；you are gone astray 主语从；but as you are come to Ireland 转折；tell me your name 祈使；and what your deeds have been 并列；and your victories in the battle 并列。

**关键词**：seeing you come armed（看你武装）/ gone astray on your journey（途中迷路）/ tell me your name（报姓名）/ what your deeds have been（陈事迹）/ your victories in the battle（战功）

**表达方式**：用"报姓名"的盘问，写爱尔兰好汉的接风礼，也是陷阱。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此，让"不报姓名"的诫在此被试探。

---

④ **"I will never give in to any man to tell the name, or to give an account of myself."**

**中文理解**："我绝不向任何人屈服而报姓名，或自陈来历。"

**句子结构**：I will never give in to any man 主谓；to tell the name 目的；or to give an account of myself 并列。

**关键词**：never give in to any man（绝不向任何人屈服）/ to tell the name（报姓名）/ give an account of myself（自陈来历）

**表达方式**：用少年一句"绝不报姓名"写诫的硬，命运的扣锁紧了。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此，让母训在少年口中成赴死的咒。

---

⑤ **"But Cuchulain threw his spear, the Gae Bulg, at him with all his might, and it struck the lad in the side and went into his body, so that he fell to the ground."**

**中文理解**：库丘林倾力投矛 Gae Bulg 中他，刺少年肋入体，遂倒地。

**句子结构**：But Cuchulain threw his spear 主谓宾；the Gae Bulg 同位；at him with all his might 状语；and it struck the lad in the side 并列；and went into his body 并列；so that he fell to the ground 结果。

**关键词**：threw his spear, the Gae Bulg（投矛 Gae Bulg）/ with all his might（倾力）/ struck the lad in the side（刺肋）/ went into his body（入体）/ fell to the ground（倒地）

**表达方式**：用"Gae Bulg 刺肋倒地"写弑子的利落，英雄技成弑亲器。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此，让传说最惨的一击落得平静。

---

⑥ **"If I and my fair Conlaoch were doing feats of war on the one side, the men of Ireland from sea to sea would not be equal to us together."**

**中文理解**："若我与爱子 Conlaoch 同阵演武，全爱尔兰之众也难敌我俩。"

**句子结构**：If I and my fair Conlaoch were doing feats of war 条件；on the one side 状语；the men of Ireland from sea to sea 主语；would not be equal to us together 系表。

**关键词**：my fair Conlaoch（爱子 Conlaoch）/ doing feats of war（演武）/ the men of Ireland from sea to sea（全爱尔兰之众）/ not be equal to us together（难敌我俩）

**表达方式**：用"父子同阵无人能敌"的豪语，把未竟的愿说成诗的悔。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此，让悲在"本可"里更沉。

---

⑦ **"It is well for the heroes of Conall they did not join in the killing of you; it is well that travelling across the plain of Macha they did not fall in with me after such a fight."**

**中文理解**："Conall 的豪杰未参与杀你，幸甚；幸而他们过 Macha 平原、未在此战后遇我。"

**句子结构**：It is well for the heroes of Conall 主系表；they did not join in the killing of you 同位；it is well that travelling across the plain 并列；they did not fall in with me 主语从。

**关键词**：the heroes of Conall（Conall 的豪杰）/ did not join in the killing of you（未参与杀你）/ travelling across the plain of Macha（过 Macha 平原）/ did not fall in with me（未遇我）

**表达方式**：用"幸甚未遇我"写胜者的傲与孤，余威犹在。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此，让 Cuchulain 的不可敌在丧子后更显空。

---

⑧ **"It is very well for the men of Alban it was not they that destroyed your fame; and it is well for the men of the Gall."**

**中文理解**："Alban 之人未毁你名，甚好；Gall 之人亦然，甚好。"

**句子结构**：It is very well for the men of Alban 主系表；it was not they that destroyed your fame 强调；and it is well for the men of the Gall 并列。

**关键词**：the men of Alban（Alban 之人）/ destroyed your fame（毁你名）/ the men of the Gall（Gall 之人）

**表达方式**：用"未毁你名者皆幸"的排比，写对逝者的敬与宽。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此，让悲里留一层侠的体面。

---

⑨ **"To be without Naoise, without Ainnle, without Ardan; is it not with me is my fill of trouble?"**

**中文理解**："失 Naoise、失 Ainnle、失 Ardan；我岂非满腹愁？"

**句子结构**：To be without Naoise 不定式；without Ainnle, without Ardan 并列；is it not with me is my fill of trouble 反问主系表。

**关键词**：To be without Naoise（失 Naoise）/ without Ainnle, without Ardan（失 Ainnle Ardan）/ my fill of trouble（满腹愁）

**表达方式**：用"失三友"的叠呼写旧痛，把新丧子接进长串的失。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此，让 Connla 之死汇入英雄的永恒孤。

---

⑩ **"But it was not there he got his death, but on the plain of Muirthemne."**

**中文理解**："可他非死于彼，而死在 Muirthemne 平原。"

**句子结构**：But it was not there he got his death 强调；but on the plain of Muirthemne 转折。

**关键词**：it was not there he got his death（非死于彼）/ on the plain of Muirthemne（Muirthemne 平原）

**表达方式**：用"非彼乃此"的收束，把死处钉进地名，传说落定。

**为什么这样写**：Gregory 以此收尾，让地理成为记忆的锚。

## 一句话总结

Gregory 重述库丘林：Aoife 立"不报姓名、不退让"三诫，少年 Connla 寻父却与父互不知而决斗，Gae Bulg 刺肋、见金环乃知弑子——"不知而杀"在爱尔兰英雄循环里，是宿命也是诗。
