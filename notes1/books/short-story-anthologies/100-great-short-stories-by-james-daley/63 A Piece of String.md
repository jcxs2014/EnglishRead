# 63. A Piece of String

**作者**：Guy de Maupassant

## 一句话总结

Maupassant 写农夫 Hauchecome 在集上捡了截绳，被冤偷钱袋；他百辩不信，终在悒郁中死前仍举绳喊冤——一则把"小事被坚信成罪"写成社会偏见的寓言，结尾以临终举绳收束。

## 逐句精读（10 处）

① **"The men were proceeding with slow steps, the whole body bent forward at each movement of their long twisted legs, deformed by their hard work, by the weight on the plow which, at the same time, raised the earth."**

**中文理解**：男人们慢步前行，每迈扭曲的长腿、身便前倾——那腿被苦活、被犁上同时掀土的重压弄变了形。

**句子结构**：The men were proceeding with slow steps 主谓；the whole body bent forward 独立主格；at each movement of their long twisted legs 状语；deformed by their hard work 定语；by the weight on the plow 并列；which raised the earth 定语。

**关键词**：proceeding with slow steps（慢步前行）/ the whole body bent forward（身前倾）/ long twisted legs（扭曲长腿）/ deformed by their hard work（被苦活弄变形）/ the weight on the plow（犁上重压）

**表达方式**：用"弯腿前倾"的群像特写，写农人的劳痕，开场即乡土。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此，让人物先以身体的苦立住。

---

② **"In the public square of Goderville there was a crowd, a throng of human beings and animals mixed together."**

**中文理解**：Goderville 的公众场上有一堆人——人与畜混在一处的群。

**句子结构**：In the public square of Goderville 状语；there was a crowd 存在句；a throng of human beings and animals mixed together 同位。

**关键词**：the public square of Goderville（Goderville 公众场）/ a crowd（一堆人）/ a throng of human beings and animals（人与畜群）/ mixed together（混在一处）

**表达方式**：用"人畜混场"写集市的乱与真，事件舞台先立。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此，让捡绳的荒唐发生在最日常的喧里。

---

③ **"He concealed his 'find' quickly under his blouse, then in his trousers' pocket; then he pretended to be still looking on the ground for something which he did not find, and he went toward the market, lame, as is the peasant's way."**

**中文理解**：他把"所获"迅速藏进褂、再藏裤袋；又装作还在地上寻那没寻着的东西，瘸着朝市去——农人做派。

**句子结构**：He concealed his find quickly 主谓宾；under his blouse 状语；then in his trousers' pocket 并列；then he pretended to be still looking 并列；on the ground for something 状语；which he did not find 定语；and he went toward the market 并列；lame 状语；as is the peasant's way 状语。

**关键词**：concealed his find（藏所获）/ under his blouse（进褂）/ in his trousers' pocket（裤袋）/ pretended to be still looking（装作还寻）/ went toward the market, lame（瘸朝市去）

**表达方式**：用"藏+装寻"写农夫的精明与小家子气，伏笔被疑。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此，让"捡绳"因藏而显得像偷。

---

④ **"The dishes were passed and emptied, as were the jugs of yellow cider."**

**中文理解**：盘传空了，黄苹果酒罐亦然。

**句子结构**：The dishes were passed and emptied 主谓；as were the jugs of yellow cider 倒装。

**关键词**：The dishes were passed and emptied（盘传空）/ the jugs of yellow cider（黄苹果酒罐）

**表达方式**：用"盘空酒尽"写集上饭时的酣，日常的暖。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此，让冤案在饭桌闲谈里滋生。

---

⑤ **"They were finishing their coffee when a chief of the gendarmes appeared upon the threshold."**

**中文理解**：他们正喝完咖啡，一宪兵队长现身门槛。

**句子结构**：They were finishing their coffee 主谓宾；when a chief of the gendarmes appeared 时间；upon the threshold 状语。

**关键词**：finishing their coffee（喝完咖啡）/ a chief of the gendarmes（宪兵队长）/ appeared upon the threshold（现门槛）

**表达方式**：用"喝完咖啡宪兵至"写权力的介入，平静被破。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此，让冤从饭桌滑向官。

---

⑥ **"You will not make me believe, Maître Hauchecome, that Monsieur Malandain, who is a man worthy of credence, mistook this cord for a pocketbook." The peasant, furious, lifted his hand, spat at one side."**

**中文理解**："你骗不了我，Hauchecome 老爷，说那堪信的 Malandain 先生把这根绳错当钱袋。"农夫大怒，举手、朝一侧啐。

**句子结构**：You will not make me believe 主谓宾；Maître Hauchecome 呼语；that Monsieur Malandain mistook this cord 宾从；who is a man worthy of credence 定语；for a pocketbook 状语。The peasant, furious 主语带状语；lifted his hand 谓宾；spat at one side 并列。

**关键词**：will not make me believe（骗不了我）/ Maître Hauchecome（Hauchecome 老爷）/ a man worthy of credence（堪信者）/ mistook this cord for a pocketbook（错绳当钱袋）/ furious, lifted his hand（大怒举手）/ spat at one side（朝侧啐）

**表达方式**：用"堪信者 vs 你"的权威压人，写冤的来由是信谁。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此，让偏见借"可信证人"成形。

---

⑦ **"They said: 'Old rascal, get out!' And he grew angry, becoming exasperated, hot, and distressed at not being believed, not knowing what to do and always repeating himself."**

**中文理解**："老无赖，滚！"他渐怒、焦躁、燥、因不被信而苦，不知所为、只反复其词。

**句子结构**：They said 主谓；Old rascal, get out 引语。And he grew angry 主系表；becoming exasperated, hot 状语；and distressed at not being believed 并列；not knowing what to do 状语；always repeating himself 状语。

**关键词**：Old rascal, get out（老无赖滚）/ grew angry, becoming exasperated（渐怒焦躁）/ distressed at not being believed（因不被信而苦）/ always repeating himself（只反复其词）

**表达方式**：用"只反复其词"写无辜者的无措，悲在无言可辩。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此，让社会的嘲把人逼成复读机。

---

⑧ **"People had the air of joking while they listened."**

**中文理解**：人听时，一副玩笑神气。

**句子结构**：People had the air of joking 主谓宾；while they listened 时间。

**关键词**：had the air of joking（一副玩笑神气）/ while they listened（听时）

**表达方式**：用"听如玩笑"写旁观的轻佻，冤被当乐。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此，让群体的冷漠成第二种暴力。

---

⑨ **"He could not finish his dinner and went away, in the midst of jeers."**

**中文理解**：他饭未吃完便去，在哄笑中。

**句子结构**：He could not finish his dinner 主谓宾；and went away 并列；in the midst of jeers 状语。

**关键词**：could not finish his dinner（饭未吃完）/ went away（便去）/ in the midst of jeers（在哄笑中）

**表达方式**：用"哄笑中离去"写羞辱的落幕，日常碎成渣。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此，让偏见在笑里把人逐出。

---

⑩ **"He died in the first days of January, and in the delirium of his death struggles he kept claiming his innocence, reiterating: 'A piece of string, a piece of string,—look—here it is, M'sieu' the Mayor.'"**

**中文理解**：他死在一月初，临终谵妄里仍喊冤，反复："一截绳、一截绳——瞧——在这，市长先生。"

**句子结构**：He died in the first days of January 主谓；and in the delirium of his death struggles 状语；he kept claiming his innocence 并列；reiterating 现在分词；A piece of string 直接引语。

**关键词**：died in the first days of January（死在一月初）/ the delirium of his death struggles（临终谵妄）/ kept claiming his innocence（仍喊冤）/ reiterating（反复）/ A piece of string（一截绳）

**表达方式**：用"临终举绳喊冤"写执念至死，寓言在惨里收。

**为什么这样写**：Maupassant 以此收尾，让"绳"成不应该被信的证，反成最重的冤。

## 一句话总结

Maupassant 写 Hauchecome 捡绳被冤偷钱袋，百辩不信、在哄笑中离场，终于一月初死前仍举绳喊"一截绳"——"小事被坚信成罪"是偏见最冷的寓言。
