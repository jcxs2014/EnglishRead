# 20. Chapter Nineteen

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：1948年悉尼，Billie 与母亲、Alma 登上 Luxor 号邮轮前往意大利，与 Cooper 告别，怀疑 Darlene 之死与 Moretti 有关。
- **情感弧线位置**——转折：Billie 从内疚（Darlene 之死）到决心（继续旅程）——她选择相信 Cooper 会调查。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："告别"——Billie 与 Cooper、Sam 的告别；"新旅程"——Billie 踏上前往意大利的邮轮。
- **人物弧线**：Billie 从犹豫（是否离开）到坚定（继续旅程）——她选择面对父亲的秘密。
- **叙事手法**：码头告别场景+邮轮启航，以 Billie 的内心独白串联。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** 'Where is the rest of it?' Ella said, then coughed. It was a clear Sydney day, not a cloud to be seen, and after little over a week of anticipation and panicked packing, Billie, Ella and Alma were taking in the sight of SS Luxor for the first time.

**中文理解**："其余部分在哪里？"Ella 说，然后咳嗽。那是一个晴朗的悉尼日子，看不到一朵云，经过一个多星期的期待和匆忙打包，Billie、Ella 和 Alma 第一次看到 Luxor 号。

**关键词**：anticipation（期待）；panicked packing（匆忙打包）；taking in the sight（看到）

**为什么这样写**："panicked packing"= Billie 的匆忙——她在 Darlene 死后匆忙准备。

**读者视角提示**：Ella 的"咳嗽"=她的病情——这可能是最后一次旅行。

> **原句 2:** The busy dock around them was a sea of hats and travel trunks, the air filled with chatter and passionate goodbyes.

**中文理解**：他们周围繁忙的码头是帽子和旅行箱的海洋，空气中充满了聊天和深情的告别。

**关键词**：a sea of（…的海洋）；passionate goodbyes（深情的告别）

**为什么这样写**："a sea of"=码头的繁忙——人群聚集。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"告别"=她与 Cooper、Sam 的告别——她不知道何时回来。

> **原句 3:** Sam, ever reliable and thoughtful, was there to help with their baggage. He had insisted, and now watching him reassuring Ella and handling her trunks, Billie felt a pang, and wondered if it was really best to leave him behind.

**中文理解**：Sam，始终可靠而体贴，在那里帮助他们的行李。他坚持要帮忙，现在看着他安慰 Ella 并处理她的行李箱，Billie 感到一阵痛苦，想知道把他留下是否真的是最好的选择。

**关键词**：ever reliable（始终可靠）；pang（一阵痛苦）；leave him behind（把他留下）

**为什么这样写**："pang"= Billie 的不舍——她不想离开 Sam。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"把他留下"=她的内疚——她留下了 Sam 和 Shyla。

> **原句 4:** 'First class is very exclusive, I understand,' Billie said, calming Ella and playing to her interests, as they watched Sam speaking with staff about where to take their things.

**中文理解**："头等舱非常独特，我理解，"Billie 说，安抚 Ella 并迎合她的兴趣，当他们看着 Sam 与工作人员交谈把他们的东西带到哪里。

**关键词**：exclusive（独特的）；playing to her interests（迎合她的兴趣）

**为什么这样写**："playing to her interests"= Billie 的策略——她让母亲对旅行感到兴奋。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"安抚"= Ella 的焦虑——她对旅行感到不安。

> **原句 5:** Billie looked at her slender wristwatch. It was before noon, so a touch early for a tipple, but they should get their things aboard and think about plying Ella with a medicinal drop as soon as was practical.

**中文理解**：Billie 看着她纤细的手表。现在是中午前，所以喝点酒有点早，但他们应该把东西带上船，并考虑在可行时给 Ella 喝点药酒。

**关键词**：slender wristwatch（纤细的手表）；tipple（喝点酒）；plying Ella with a medicinal drop（给 Ella 喝点药酒）

**为什么这样写**："plying Ella with a medicinal drop"= Billie 的照顾——她想让母亲放松。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"药酒"= Ella 的病情——她需要酒精来缓解。

> **原句 6:** Billie was pleased by the unexpected diversion of Shyla's arrival. Dressed in a pressed navy dress and gloves, she emerged from the throng of gathered people, a friendly face to see them off. But wait . . . she was not alone. Next to her was a blonde dressed in a similar blue, but in slacks and a matching jacket.

**中文理解**：Billie 对 Shyla 到来的意外分散注意力感到高兴。穿着熨烫过的海军蓝连衣裙和手套，她从聚集的人群中出现，是一张友好的面孔来送行。但是等等……她不是一个人。她旁边是一个穿着相似蓝色的金发女人，但穿着休闲裤和相配的夹克。

**关键词**：unexpected diversion（意外的分散注意力）；pressed（熨烫过的）；throng（人群）

**为什么这样写**："unexpected diversion"= Billie 的惊喜——朋友来送行。

**读者视角提示**：Shyla 的"不是一个人"=她与 Annabelle 在一起——她们是情侣。

> **原句 7:** 'I'm going to keep an eye on him,' Shyla said. 'See what comes out next.' Billie took her friend gently by the wrist. 'Oh, Shyla. You shouldn't do that. I don't want you in danger again. The work is done. I . . . failed.' I failed. Billie had not needed to say those words before. But what greater failure could there be? A young woman in a dangerous marriage had come to her for help, and now she was dead.

**中文理解**："我会盯着他的，"Shyla 说。"看看接下来会发生什么。"Billie 轻轻地握住朋友的手腕。"哦，Shyla。你不那样做。我不想你再陷入危险。工作完成了。我……失败了。"我失败了。Billie 以前不需要说这些话。但还有什么更大的失败呢？一个处于危险婚姻中的年轻女人来找她帮忙，现在她死了。

**关键词**：keep an eye on（盯着）；failed（失败）

**为什么这样写**："I failed"= Billie 的内疚——她觉得对 Darlene 的死负有责任。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"失败"=她的第一次——她从未失去过客户。

> **原句 8:** 'You didn't fail, Billie. We did our job—we proved grounds for divorce and got her out of that awful house. You did it.' Billie's stomach tightened. 'But it wasn't enough. Not nearly. I underestimated the danger she was in, and now she's dead. But I won't underestimate the danger now, Shyla. You are not going near that house.'

**中文理解**："你没有失败，Billie。我们做了我们的工作——我们证明了离婚的理由并把她从那所可怕的房子里救了出来。你做到了。"Billie 的胃收紧了。"但这还不够。远远不够。我低估了她所处的危险，现在她死了。但我现在不会低估危险，Shyla。你不要靠近那所房子。"

**关键词**：grounds for divorce（离婚的理由）；underestimated（低估）

**为什么这样写**："underestimated"= Billie 的后悔——她本可以做更多。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"不要靠近"=她的保护——她不想 Shyla 陷入危险。

> **原句 9:** 'Is there any reason you chose . . . that destination?' Sam said, neatly avoiding saying the word Italy. 'I hear the coast of Italy is quite lovely this time of year,' Billie said with false cheer.

**中文理解**："你选择……那个目的地有什么原因吗？"Sam 说，巧妙地避免说意大利这个词。"我听说意大利海岸在这个季节非常美丽，"Billie 带着虚假的欢快说。

**关键词**：neatly avoiding（巧妙地避免）；false cheer（虚假的欢快）

**为什么这样写**："false cheer"= Billie 的伪装——她在掩饰真实目的。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"意大利"=她的真实目的——寻找父亲的情人。

> **原句 10:** 'I wanted to wish you a pleasant voyage,' he said stiffly. 'And you also, Mrs Walker and . . .' 'You can call me Alma,' she said, seeing him struggle for a surname. 'Ms Alma,' Hank said, and nodded in his gentlemanly way. 'McGuire,' he said then, remembering. He never forgot a face or a name.

**中文理解**："我想祝你们旅途愉快，"他僵硬地说。"你也是，Walker 夫人和……""你可以叫我 Alma，"她说，看到他为姓氏挣扎。"Alma 小姐，"Hank 说，并以绅士的方式点头。"McGuire，"他然后说，想起来了。他从未忘记过面孔或名字。

**关键词**：stiffly（僵硬地）；gentlemanly（绅士的）

**为什么这样写**："stiffly"= Cooper 的不自在——他在公共场合不善于表达。

**读者视角提示**：Cooper 的"从未忘记"=他的专业——他记得每个人的名字。

> **原句 11:** 'May I speak with you for a moment?' Billie asked Cooper, stepping away from the others. 'It's kind of you to come and say goodbye. I did enjoy our time together the other night. Thank you again.' 'As did I, Ms Walker,' he said evenly. Back to Ms Walker again, she thought, gazing up at his poker face.

**中文理解**："我可以和你谈谈吗？"Billie 问 Cooper，走到一边。"你来道别真是太好了。我确实喜欢我们前几天在一起的时光。再次谢谢你。""我也是，Walker 小姐，"他平静地说。又回到 Walker 小姐了，她想，凝视着他的扑克脸。

**关键词**：poker face（扑克脸）

**为什么这样写**："poker face"= Cooper 的掩饰——他不表露情感。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"又回到 Walker 小姐"=他们的关系——他恢复了正式。

> **原句 12:** 'Did things go well with . . . ?' She dared not mentioned him. Cooper nodded solemnly, not giving her anything. 'Well, you have my contact details if there are any updates,' Billie told him pointedly. 'You do believe me about Moretti being on the street outside the sister's house before Mrs Elliott fell ill?'

**中文理解**："事情进展顺利吗……？"她不敢提他。Cooper 严肃地什么也没给她。"好吧，如果有任何更新，你有我的联系方式，"Billie 明确地告诉他。"你真的相信我在 Elliott 夫人生病前看到 Moretti 在姐姐家外面街上吗？"

**关键词**：dared not（不敢）；pointedly（明确地）

**为什么这样写**："dared not"= Billie 的谨慎——她不敢提 Elliott 的名字。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"Moretti"=她的怀疑——Moretti 与 Darlene 的死有关。

> **原句 13:** 'Of course I do. In fact, Mr Elliott admitted that Moretti was working for him—to watch his wife and ascertain proof of her adultery.' 'Her adultery!' 'Calm down, and see it for the tactic it is. He was within his rights, and I will be speaking with Moretti as well, in due course.' Good. 'Soon, I hope?' 'He hasn't been at his usual haunts, but I'll track him down. And I checked on Mrs Elliott's insurance policy. She did have one, of course, but that's unsurprising considering her husband's profession. He is the beneficiary.'

**中文理解**："我当然相信。事实上，Elliott 先生承认 Moretti 为他工作——监视他的妻子并确定她通奸的证据。""她的通奸！""冷静下来，把它看作一种策略。他有权这样做，我也会在适当时候与 Moretti 谈谈。""好。"我希望很快？""他不在他通常出没的地方，但我会找到他。我查了 Elliott 夫人的保险单。她当然有，但考虑到她丈夫的职业，这并不奇怪。他是受益人。"

**关键词**：ascertain（确定）；adultery（通奸）；beneficiary（受益人）

**为什么这样写**："beneficiary"= Elliott 的动机——他可以从妻子的死亡中获益。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"受益人"=关键线索——Elliott 有杀人动机。

> **原句 14:** 'That's motive,' she said. 'Was it a lot of money?' 'Yes,' Cooper admitted in a low voice, looking nervously around them and moving further back from the bustling crowd for some privacy. 'But it was normal enough for married couples like them, and signed just after their wedding. There have been no recent changes. It's not proof of anything. Her death is considered a natural one, though I'm pushing to confirm that. It's in hand, Billie. Let us take over from here. I just came to wish you bon voyage.'

**中文理解**："那是动机，"她说。"钱多吗？""是的，"Cooper 低声承认，紧张地看着他们周围，从喧闹的人群中退后一些以获得一些隐私。"但对像他们这样的已婚夫妇来说很正常，而且是婚后不久签的。最近没有变化。这不能证明什么。她的死亡被认为是自然死亡，尽管我正在推动确认这一点。正在处理中，Billie。让我们从这里接手。我只是来祝你们一路顺风。"

**关键词**：motive（动机）；bon voyage（一路顺风）

**为什么这样写**："bon voyage"= Cooper 的祝福——他希望 Billie 旅途平安。

**读者视角提示**：Cooper 的"让我们从这里接手"=他的承诺——他会调查。

> **原句 15:** Let us take over from here. He meant to sound reassuring, but his words grated. 'Hank, I wanted to tell you that on Wednesday, the day before she fell ill, I visited Darlene to show her the photographs we had. Her room was on the upper floor but there was an open window with a fire escape beneath it. It would have been easy for someone to get in, and—' 'He was in Melbourne, Billie.' 'Not him.' She squeezed his hand. 'Moretti.' 'Understood,' he said, taking both her hands in his for a moment that made her heart turn over. 'Have a holiday. You'll be back soon enough. You've been working hard for the women of Sydney. You more than deserve a break.'

**中文理解**：让我们从这里接手。他本想听起来令人放心，但他的话很刺耳。"Hank，我想告诉你，在她生病前一天的周三，我去看望 Darlene 给她看我们拍的照片。她的房间在楼上，但有一个窗户开着，下面有太平梯。有人很容易进来，而且——""他在墨尔本，Billie。""不是他。"她握着他的手。""Moretti。""明白了，"他说，双手握住她的手一会儿，让她的心翻了个个。"去度假吧。你很快就会回来的。你一直在为悉尼的女性努力工作。你完全应该休息一下。"

**关键词**：grated（刺耳）；fire escape（太平梯）；deserve a break（应该休息一下）

**为什么这样写**："grated"= Billie 的不满——她不想被排除在外。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"太平梯"=她的推理——有人可能从窗户进入。

> **原句 16:** 'Scusa, bella.' Billie was just outside the door to her cabin when a man nearly walked straight into her. He had seemed in a focused rush, but immediately changed gears when he saw her, stopping and bowing. Pewter-haired and deeply tanned, he cut a trim figure, neatly dressed in a captain's uniform. 'Please allow me to introduce myself,' he said in a thick Italian accent. 'Captain Gaspare Romano.' He doffed his cap. 'Pleasure to make your acquaintance. You will enjoy your journey with us, I hope, Miss . . . ?'

**中文理解**："对不起，美女。"Billie 就在她的舱门外，一个男人差点直接撞上她。他似乎专注于赶路，但看到她时立即改变了步伐，停下来鞠躬。灰白的头发和深棕色的皮肤，他身材匀称，穿着整洁的船长制服。"请允许我自我介绍，"他带着浓重的意大利口音说。"Gaspare Romano 船长。"他脱下帽子。"很高兴认识你。我希望你能享受我们的旅程，小姐……？"

**关键词**：changed gears（改变了步伐）；pewer-haired（灰白的头发）；doffed his cap（脱下帽子）

**为什么这样写**："changed gears"=船长的礼貌——他停下来打招呼。

**读者视角提示**：船长的"意大利口音"=他们正在前往意大利——异国风情。
