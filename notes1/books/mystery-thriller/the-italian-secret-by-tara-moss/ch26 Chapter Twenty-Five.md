# 26. Chapter Twenty-Five

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Billie 与船长和船员在船上搜索 Moretti——她怀疑他在船上，决心找到他。
- **情感弧线位置**——高潮：Billie 从恐惧（被下毒）到决心（找到 Moretti）——她选择面对危险。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："搜捕"——Billie 与船长搜索 Moretti；"危险"——Moretti 可能在船上。
- **人物弧线**：Billie 从被动（被下毒）到主动（搜索 Moretti）——她选择反击。
- **叙事手法**：悬疑——Billie 搜索 Moretti，气氛紧张。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** Within half an hour, Captain Romano had summoned five men to his cabin and was briefing them on what he knew. When he was done, he gestured to Billie. 'Ms Walker is an investigatore privato. She knows what this man looks like. She will provide us with a description, and accompany us on the search.' He offered her a small bow.

**中文理解**：半小时内，Romano 船长已经召集了五个人到他的舱室，并向他们简报了他所知道的。完成后，他向 Billie 示意。"Walker 小姐是私家侦探。她知道这个人长什么样。她会给我们提供描述，并陪同我们搜索。"他向她微微鞠躬。

**关键词**：summoned（召集）；briefing（简报）；investigatore privato（私家侦探——意大利语）

**为什么这样写**："investigatore privato"=船长的尊重——他认可 Billie 的专业。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"私家侦探"=她的身份——她在船上被认可。

> **原句 2:** 'Thank you, Captain,' she said, surveying their posse. They were alert and hungry-looking, Italians all, and had gathered with impressive speed.

**中文理解**："谢谢你，船长，"她说，打量着他们的队伍。他们警觉而饥饿，都是意大利人，以惊人的速度聚集。

**关键词**：posse（队伍）；alert（警觉的）

**为什么这样写**："posse"= Billie 的惊喜——她得到了帮助。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"饥饿"=他们的决心——他们想找到 Moretti。

> **原句 3:** 'Please,' she implored, 'do be careful. Vincenzo Moretti is a killer. He may be armed, but even if he's not, he remains highly dangerous. We need to stay together as much as possible. If you find a man who matches the physical description, do not approach him alone.'

**中文理解**："请，"她恳求道，"一定要小心。Vincenzo Moretti 是个杀手。他可能有武器，但即使没有，他仍然非常危险。我们需要尽可能待在一起。如果你发现一个符合外貌描述的人，不要单独接近他。"

**关键词**：implored（恳求）；armed（有武器的）

**为什么这样写**："implored"= Billie 的担忧——她知道 Moretti 的危险。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"不要单独"=她的谨慎——她不想让任何人受伤。

> **原句 4:** The captain loaded his revolver and held it out in front of him. 'Matteo, you still remember how to use this, I trust,' he said. 'Sì, capitano,' said Matteo, taking the gun. 'Good. I would break out the rifles, but if a panic began among the passengers, it could be just as dangerous as this man, this Moretti.' The captain lifted a second pistol from his desk—a service Colt with a small Italian flag above the handle—and tucked it into his belt.

**中文理解**：船长装上左轮手枪，举在面前。"Matteo，我相信你还记得怎么用，"他说。"是的，船长，"Matteo 说，接过枪。"好。我会拿出步枪，但如果乘客中开始恐慌，那可能和这个人，这个 Moretti 一样危险。"船长从桌上拿起第二把手枪——一把服务用柯尔特，把手上方有一面小意大利国旗——塞进腰带。

**关键词**：revolver（左轮手枪）；break out（拿出）；tucked（塞进）

**为什么这样写**："break out"=船长的谨慎——他不想引起恐慌。

**读者视角提示**：船长的"步枪"=他的准备——他认真对待威胁。

> **原句 5:** Billie opened her handbag and brandished her trusty compact Colt 1908, before tucking it back in her bag. The captain raised an eyebrow at her, opening his mouth, then closing it again. 'The lady is full of surprises,' he said eventually, turning back to the men. 'Now remember, no heroics.' He pointed to the gun in Matteo's hand. 'Keep it concealed unless you need to use it.'

**中文理解**：Billie 打开手提包，挥舞着她可靠的紧凑型柯尔特1908，然后塞回包里。船长对她扬起眉毛，张开嘴，然后又闭上。"这位女士充满了惊喜，"他最终说，转向男人们。"现在记住，不要英雄主义。"他指着 Matteo 手中的枪。"保持隐藏，除非你需要使用它。"

**关键词**：brandished（挥舞）；heroics（英雄主义）；concealed（隐藏）

**为什么这样写**："heroics"=船长的警告——他不想让任何人冒险。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"柯尔特"=她的准备——她总是带着枪。

> **原句 6:** They moved off through the hallways, passing preoccupied first-class passengers and maids wheeling carts of clean and dirty laundry. Billie was in the lead, sauntering with as much casualness as she could muster with adrenaline coursing through her, keeping her upright.

**中文理解**：他们穿过走廊出发，经过专注的头等舱乘客和推着干净和脏衣服车的女仆。Billie 带头，尽可能随意地漫步，肾上腺素在她体内流动，让她保持直立。

**关键词**：preoccupied（专注的）；sauntering（漫步）；coursing（流动）

**为什么这样写**："sauntering"= Billie 的伪装——她假装轻松。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"肾上腺素"=她的紧张——她在害怕。

> **原句 7:** Out on the decks, the ocean air was refreshing, if biting, and the wind whipped at her. Billie raised a gloved hand to hold her hat in place. Aft, three men in pith helmets sipped amber drinks and hit golf balls off the deck into the ocean below, smoke belching from the smokestack above them and then curling in the air as the ship snaked along on her journey.

**中文理解**：在甲板上，海洋空气令人清爽，如果刺骨的话，风抽打着她。Billie 戴着戴手套的手保持帽子在原位。在船尾，三个戴着遮阳帽的男人喝着琥珀色饮料，把高尔夫球从甲板上打入下面的海洋，烟从他们上面的烟囱冒出，然后在空气中卷曲，因为船在她的旅程中蜿蜒前行。

**关键词**：biting（刺骨的）；pith helmets（遮阳帽）；belching（冒出）

**为什么这样写**："biting"=天气的寒冷——与 Billie 的紧张形成对比。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"高尔夫球"=头等舱的奢侈——与下层的艰苦形成对比。

> **原句 8:** Billie eyed every movement, every face as they went, her hand itching to open her purse and pull out the gun. Once, she spotted a man about the right size, and stepped over to block his path, but then it was not Moretti, and he stared confusedly at her as she turned away, and the group moved on. Billie felt doubt creep in. What if her instincts were wrong? What if—as Hank had suggested—she was more obsessed with Moretti than was healthy?

**中文理解**：Billie 注视着每一个动作，每一张脸，她的手痒痒地想打开钱包掏枪。有一次，她发现一个体型差不多的男人，走过去挡住他的路，但那不是 Moretti，他困惑地看着她转身离开，队伍继续前进。Billie 感到怀疑悄悄袭来。如果她的直觉错了怎么办？如果——如 Hank 所建议的——她对 Moretti 的痴迷超过了健康程度怎么办？

**关键词** |itching（痒痒地）；creep in（悄悄袭来）；obsessed（痴迷）

**为什么这样写**："obsessed"= Billie 的自我怀疑——她不确定自己的判断。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"怀疑"=她的脆弱——她不确定自己是否正确。

> **原句 9:** When the captain and his group hit the dormitory class, however, Billie's guilt rose in her body like a fist in her solar plexus. Here the smell of cigarettes was more pungent, and intermingled with the scent of body odour. This whole time, while she had been enjoying the privacy of her small cabin and facilities, the passengers in dormitory class, most of whom appeared to be male, had been packed in like sardines, their gaunt faces clearly tired from weeks at sea, sleeping on narrow beds.

**中文理解**：然而，当船长和他的团队到达宿舍舱时，Billie 的内疚像拳头一样在她的太阳穴中升起。这里香烟的气味更刺鼻，与汗臭味混合。这段时间，当她享受着她小舱室和设施的隐私时，宿舍舱的乘客，大多数似乎是男性，像沙丁鱼一样挤在一起，他们憔悴的面孔显然因数周的海上航行而疲惫，睡在狭窄的床上。

**关键词**：solar plexus（太阳穴）；pungent（刺鼻的）；gaunt（憔悴的）

**为什么这样写**："gaunt"=下层乘客的艰苦——与头等舱的奢侈形成对比。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"内疚"=她的同情——她意识到自己的特权。

> **原句 10:** Could Moretti really be here? How could she recover her reputation with the crew if they went to these lengths to search the ship for nothing? They covered every passenger deck, moving discreetly, and passing hundreds of people, before at last Billie saw that familiar profile and froze. Her doubts vanished in an instant. There.

**中文理解**：Moretti 真的在这里吗？如果他们费尽心思搜索船只却一无所获，她如何恢复她在船员中的声誉？他们覆盖了每个乘客甲板，谨慎地移动，经过数百人，直到最后 Billie 看到那个熟悉的轮廓并僵住了。她的怀疑瞬间消失了。那里。

**关键词**：to these lengths（费尽心思）；discreetly（谨慎地）；profile（轮廓）

**为什么这样写**："to these lengths"= Billie 的压力——她不想浪费他们的时间。

**读者视角提示**：Billie 的"熟悉的轮廓"=她的直觉——她找到了 Moretti。
