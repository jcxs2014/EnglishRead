# 《The Burnings》情感节点

## 节点一：塔顶那把刀放下的时刻（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书从一个不在正文时间线上的场景开始。一五七八年丹麦德拉格斯霍姆城堡，弗朗西斯·斯图尔特带着够买通几个守卫的钱走进塔顶牢房，去看那个被关了近十年的舅父。牢房里只有地上一圈被踩亮的脚印。他把刀收起来，舅父交出一套纲领：这辈子别去夺王位，去把国王做成一个胆小鬼。这本书后来所有的事，都是这一夜定下的引火方式。

**关键引语：**

> ① ‘You need to make him, and everyone else, see he’s a pathetic, craven sliver of a man. Make him afraid. Terrified of his own shadow. Your greatest weapon will be that boy’s fear.’（ch01）

> ② How easy it was for the guards to pretend they heard nothing.（ch01）

## 节点二：码头上没闻够的那股味道（ch02）

**叙事概括：** 时间跳到一五八九年丹麦赫尔辛格。玛格丽塔在码头上闻到烟味，被乳姐妹伊尔莎拖去看了一场烧巫，又几乎立刻被拖去听一套说辞：去苏格兰，去见一个叫格丽丝·邓肯的助产士学徒，因为「像我们这样的人肯帮忙就都安全了」。她拒绝了，甩身就走；临走前伊尔莎把话说到底——**这本书里第一次出现「巫术在你血里」的说法，而它不需要任何证据**。她没有昏过去：她是自己走出广场的。（真正让她倒下的是后来的事，在苏格兰的宫廷里，醒来时约翰只给了她三个字；那一章的实情另有其人称与时序，不在此合并。）

**关键引语：**

> ① Margareta could smell the smoke from the wharf, no matter how close to the oyster sellers she stepped; caught by the dawn boats, the shellfish were now drying into brackish dullness, but even salted rot was better than the smell of a burning. Only wood so far, but Margareta knew what would follow. She’d be smelling it for hours, if not days; every time she brushed her hair or smoothed her skirts she’d gag at the stench of seared meat. Dear God, if there were suckling pigs roasting on spits in Kronborg Castle’s great hall, she’d be sick.（ch02）

> ② Witchcraft is in your blood. You can never be rid of it.（ch02）

## 节点三：郡守的视线（ch05）

**叙事概括：** 苏格兰的农场线上，草药婆格丽丝过着一成不变的日子，直到地主家的郡守开始盯着她看。她把这种注视比作茅屋顶上的滴水——先是不理会，然后越来越响。她的世界里只有一个能替她说话的人：艾格尼斯。这一章确立了全书的重力：有没有人肯替你开口，就是生死。

**关键引语：**

> ① She’d tried to ignore it at first, like a drip in the thatch that wakes you at night – intruding and icy – and yet, undealt with, it grows more insistent, bringing the promise of rot. Thus it was with the bailie’s gaze, slithering down her back as Geillis draped wet sheets over rosemary bushes, seeping through her bodice as she reached up to oil the leather-panelled walls, running across her briefly bared wrists as she poured his wine at dinner.（ch05）

> ② What to be done? Nothing. He was the bailie. He had noble kin. Girls like Geillis, with no husbands or fathers or brothers, were expected to endure, endure, and endure again. She’d considered complaining to Mistress Katherine, but what wife wanted to be told about her husband’s roving eye, still less asked for help restraining him? Besides, Seton’s wife was no more approachable than a swan; she glided through the house, cutting conversations in two like water, the slightest arching of her neck a silent warning.（ch05）

## 节点四：一条被吹沉的船（ch09）

**叙事概括：** 一艘从伯蒂斯兰出发的船在风暴里沉了，船上有一位王后的侍女。沉船把两条本不相干的线短暂地拉到同一片水域边上。而做这件事的人，一边祈祷它别成功，一边发现自己已经站在船上。**这一夜的风暴吹沉的是那艘船；原文并没有把它与别处那些翻船画等号**——这笔账在这本书里始终是分开记的。

**关键引语：**

> ① ‘The Margaret,’ Fian announced, as if lecturing small children. ‘Sailing from Burntisland to Leith at King James’s request. He wishes Jean Kennedy, sister-in-law to his adviser, Sir James Melville, to become Anna of Denmark’s lady-in-waiting.’（ch09）

> ② Even while whistling, Geillis had prayed it wouldn’t work. But what they were doing had nothing to do with God, and maybe that was why He hadn’t answered her. For there had indeed been another storm.（ch11）

## 节点五：把无辜的人摆到那个位置上（ch17）

**叙事概括：** 双头牛、下海失踪的女孩、被雪埋掉的小屋——流言先在村镇上跑起来，制造出一种「处处都在出事」的气氛。接着出现的是另一类人：太干净、太无依、又没有靠山的人。猎巫的第一课不是杀人，是先把无辜者放到那个位置上，后面的一切才会顺理成章。

**关键引语：**

> ① A calf was born with two heads in Fife. Three girls went into the sea in Galloway and never emerged – but three seals had watched their village ever since, black heads gleaming beneath the moon.（ch17）

> ② Conspiracies were one thing. Trying to save your own neck another. But letting innocent men take the blame? One more sin Geillis was too frightened to stop herself committing.（ch17）

## 节点六：托尔博特的夹棍（ch33）

**叙事概括：** 地下室的酷刑开始产出唯一能流通的货币：口供。拇指夹与皮鞭把「她说过的」变成「她承认过的」。而在这套机器的运转中，有人终于说出了它的用途——不是为了真相，是为了制造恐惧。这句话和序章那句纲领首尾相扣，而说出它的人正躺在夹棍底下。

**关键引语：**

> ① He took her hand, terrifyingly gentle. ‘They call them pilliwinks, or thumbikins. Such silly names for something so … damaging.’ He slipped the device around her thumb. ‘You might not think it now. Look how small it is! Look what a fraction of you a thumb is! And yet, I’m told, our hands are among the most sensitive parts of our bodies. And, of course, the most useful. Whatever would a girl like you do with crushed thumbs?’（ch33）

> ② ‘He wanted us to spread fear.’ Agnes leaned forward, barely wincing now. ‘The only thing that frightens men more than witchcraft is a woman they cannot control. They want us to confess to witchcraft? We shall not. Do you hear me? We will not confess.’（ch33）

## 节点七：公堂上念出来的不是罪名（ch35）

**叙事概括：** 罪状被念出来，念的不是巫术，是**只有国王和王后可能知道的话**。这一处把整台机器的性质换掉了：它从宗教程序变成了告密程序。判决之后，被告人自己把话说完了——她做的和国王要的是同一件事的两半，而她说她不后悔。

**关键引语：**

> ① ‘Agnes Sampson has spoken words only I and Queen Anna could possibly have known. This is all the evidence needed to prove her a witch. I call upon the dempster to pass sentence.’（ch35）

> ② Except this. And I don’t regret it. I want James to know what I’ve done.’（ch35）

## 节点八：山上的那句否认（ch44）

**叙事概括：** 城堡山上，判决词念完。被告说了全书最不像辩护的一句：她们不是女巫，她们是害怕的女人，指望活命。**全场只有这一句否认，而她说的时候已经改变不了任何结果。** 这本书对言说时机的安排在此刻显出全部的刻薄：真相必须等到无用的时候才被说出来。

**关键引语：**

> ① ‘They weren’t witches,’ Geillis said. ‘They were frightened women hoping to ease their childbed, to keep their own property, to regain their stolen positions. This isn’t justice – this is vengeance and spite, and scores of Scots have died for it.’（ch44）

> ② The torchbearers remained still. Geillis heard heavy feet climbing the steps behind her. Bessie cried out – but her wail was cut short. The rope came around Geillis’s neck, and when the guard began to twist it tight, Geillis closed her eyes and felt only relief.（ch44）

## 节点九：三周之后的那股味道（ch45）

**叙事概括：** 城堡山之后，幸存者各自带着不同的版本活着。有人向玛格丽塔概括规模时轻描淡写地说「不过九十来人」，而死亡人数在这本书里有四个互不相等的口径——**作者从不给总数**。三周之后，玛格丽塔闻到厨房里的烤肉味就反胃，戴项链时会因为冷石头贴上脖子而发抖。创伤在这里不是心理描写，是一串身体反应。

**关键引语：**

> ① Even three weeks after Geillis’s death, Margareta grimaced whenever the smell of roasting meat writhed out of Holyrood’s kitchens. Whenever she donned a necklace, Margareta shivered as cold stones closed around her neck. She’d seen Anna constantly loosening her pearls, as if she too were desperate to avoid constriction, even if those pearls could have bought a hundred guards to help her escape. But there was nowhere for Anna or Margareta to go.（ch45）

> ② A few women? Well over ninety, and several men too, dismissed as nothing.（ch45）

## 节点十：城墙上的最后一段对话（ch46 / ch47）

**叙事概括：** 斯特林城墙上，约翰对国王说出全书政治判断的最后一段——这句话的内容与序章的纲领一致，只是这次是说给国王听的。同一场对话的末尾，人物给出最后一个判词：只要男人还害怕，就还有。尾声回到一五九五年的阳光里：死者名单只有四个名字，没有数字；而那个从一五七八年起就在布置这一切的人，原文给他的结案只有两个字——流亡。

**关键引语：**

> ① ‘God chose your mother,’ John pointed out. ‘Scotland chose you. All along, Bothwell has wanted you to destroy yourself, to be terrified either into submission or into behaving so rashly that Scotland turns away from you and raises Bothwell onto the throne.’（ch46）

> ② Exiled. The word was a sigh of relief. Bothwell was gone at last. It had taken years, but James had built higher walls with laws and trials and royal decrees than those of any castle, and Bothwell had been unable to breach them. John’s evidence had done its work. Bothwell’s erstwhile supporters now vehemently pretended they had been no such thing, to little avail. There was nothing left in Scotland for Bothwell now. Like his uncle before him, he’d fled overseas.（ch47）
