# 《The Stolen Queen》情感节点

## 节点一：周一闭馆日与失而复得的项链（ch01）

**叙事概括：** 全书的两个引信在同一章点着：白天，Charlotte 在闭馆的博物馆里替上司收拾残局、偷偷算着自己那套被主流学界否定的翻案理论；傍晚，一件匿名借展的宽领项链被抬上库房工作台，搭扣背面刻着 Hathorkare 的王名。她不用走近就知道它缺了一枚护符，因为她在四十二年前亲手把它从墓里掏出来过。

**关键引语：**

> ① The staff meeting of the Metropolitan Museum’s Department of Egyptian Art was supposed to start at ten, which meant associate curator Charlotte Cross arrived at nine to prepare her colleagues for battle.（ch01）

> ② And what lips they were: beautifully curved and utterly sensuous.（ch01）

> ③ And she’d last seen it a year later, right before it was lost at the bottom of the Nile.（ch01）

## 节点二：帝王谷的墓门与圣眼（ch02-ch05）

**叙事概括：** 一九三六年的埃及，Charlotte 用一针抗蛇毒血清换来部族的誓言，又在被抢走发掘位之后自己挖出了一道此前无人知晓的墓门。墓室的封印早已被破坏，她却在甬道圣眼壁画下的小洞里摸到夹层，掏出一条刻着 Hathorkare 王名的金宽领项链。一个月后，她发现自己怀孕了。

**关键引语：**

> ① A door to a previously undiscovered tomb.（ch02）

> ② Charlotte’s excitement at possibly finding an untouched tomb plummeted when she noticed that the door at the bottom of the steps had a broken seal.（ch05）

> ③ Charlotte immediately recognized it as a broad collar, a wide, layered necklace made up of several rows of connected amulets.（ch05）

## 节点三：尼罗河上的那一夜（ch11）

**叙事概括：** 婚后不久，Henry 毫无解释地催她连夜登船北上。风暴夜里两船相撞，行李箱摔开，露出本该在开罗馆藏的那条宽领项链。海水漫上甲板之前，她把女儿交到丈夫怀里，自己被倒下的躺椅困住——孩子的手从丈夫肩上伸过来，张着，够她。

**关键引语：**

> ① No stars, no moon, just pouring rain and infinite black.（ch11）

> ② She picked it up and checked the back for the cartouche of Hathorkare.（ch11）

> ③ her child’s chubby, sweet hand stretched out over his shoulder, palm open wide, reaching for her mother.（ch11）

## 节点四：回不去的埃及（ch04-ch14）

**叙事概括：** 一九三八年回到纽约之后，Charlotte 在噩梦、精神病院的记忆和家里"不许提起亡夫亡女"的规矩里独自熬着，靠埃及展厅里那尊残破的 Cerulean Queen 站稳。多年后她与银行家 Everett 谈婚论嫁，却在坦白埃及往事的当夜失去了这门婚事；父亲去世，母亲临终前留下一句没头没尾的话。

**关键引语：**

> ① Not after the night that she’d screamed so hard her throat had burned for days, making it impossible to speak. Not after Egypt had taken away her soul.（ch04）

> ② She carried the curse with her, and simply couldn’t risk it.（ch07）

> ③ Hathorkare’s tranquil expression, even with most of her face missing, seemed to convey that Charlotte wasn’t alone, that other women had suffered over the centuries before her and continued living, even if what was scarred and shattered was invisible to the outside world.（ch14）

## 节点五：飞蛾之夜（ch15-ch17）

**叙事概括：** Met Gala 当晚，Annie 负责的蝴蝶放飞变成一箱飞蛾，扑向灯光与宾客。Charlotte 循着尖叫下到地下展厅，只见底座空空；两人追着拎保龄球包的男人下到公园蓄水池的废弃雨水渠地道，险些丧命，出来之后 Annie 却从证人变成了嫌疑人——有人冒她的名义把蝴蝶换成了飞蛾。

**关键引语：**

> ① “Oh my God!” Marta’s voice came out strangled. “It’s moths! An invasion of moths!”（ch15）

> ② The Cerulean Queen had been plucked right off its stand.（ch16）

> ③ The scream came from deep in her belly, a terrifying, massive sound that she’d never made before in her life. It sounded like something a cornered animal made, ungodly in its pitch, echoing against the tunnel walls and hurting her own ears.（ch17）

## 节点六：飞往埃及（ch20-ch21）

**叙事概括：** 追回雕像的正规渠道全部堵死之后，Charlotte 顶着丢工作的威胁订下机票。Annie 尾随她上了飞机，在帝王谷的乳母墓室里失足踩到一只木乃伊化的鹅、自怜到谷底，却在墓顶坍塌的瞬间折返，抢出刻有 Hathorkare 王名徽的卡诺匹克盒。

**关键引语：**

> ① It only took her a few seconds to make up her mind. “I’m going to Egypt,” she said. The words came out gravelly, like she had sand in her throat.（ch20）

> ② She was a tired, confused, and unemployable nineteen-year-old, without a home or a job, who had just fallen over a mummified dead goose in a scary hole in the ground.（ch21）

> ③ Without thinking, Annie darted back, reached inside the niche, and grabbed the box with both hands as Charlotte cried out for her to stop.（ch21）

## 节点七：青金女王重现（ch24-ch27）

**叙事概括：** 开罗的旧账本露出缺口——那条项链名录在册、入藏无记录；市集里没有一个店主肯听一个女人找丈夫，Charlotte 当街把照片撕成两半。几天后她们回到那家画廊，摔香水瓶调虎离山，撬开一尊分量不对的石膏像：青金石与女王的弧形嘴唇重新出现。

**关键引语：**

> ① Two days ago, she never would have dreamed of defacing the photograph, as she considered it a private keepsake of her first love, albeit one she always kept hidden. With Henry alive and walking the earth, that nostalgia was turning poisonous. He had survived without finding her, taking their daughter with him, and the viciousness of her actions that afternoon was nothing compared to the fury he would face if she ever found him.（ch24）

> ② Underneath the plaster, a brilliant blue color appeared. It was polished, and the exact same hue as the Cerulean Queen.（ch26）

> ③ A large chunk dropped onto the table, and Annie spied a section of shiny blue stone. As another came loose, the curved lips of the queen emerged.（ch27）

## 节点八：四十一年后的重逢（ch32-ch34）

**叙事概括：** 一个穿棕褐色大衣的灰发男人在博物馆问讯处打听 Charlotte——失踪四十一年的 Henry 自己找上门来。屋顶上的旧账摊开之后，她才知道母亲临终道歉的人是 Henry，也才知道 Mona 不是她失散的女儿。一九七九年的卢克索河边，答案在一个渔夫小屋旁等着她。

**关键引语：**

> ① “What are you doing here?” Charlotte asked Henry. “After forty-one years. Forty-one years. Now you show up?”（ch32）

> ② I held on to her for as long as I could, but she was sucked out of my arms by the current. I promise you, I tried.（ch33）

> ③ Charlotte rose and crossed the space between them, softly touching Fatima’s hair. “I’m sorry we lost you for so long. I’ve missed you terribly.”（ch34）
