# 35. Chapter 35

## 精读

### 第 N 段：回家

> "You can get into our block and room with your thumbprint now." I arch my brows, smiling. "Ours, huh?" He opens the door, holding it for me. "Casa de us, baby."

**中文理解**："你现在可以用你的指纹进入我们的区域和房间了。"我扬起眉毛，笑着。"我们的，嗯？"他打开门，为我扶着。"我们的 Casa，宝贝。"

**关键词**：our block and room / thumbprint / Casa de us

**为什么这样写**：Marcus 的 gesture 是巨大的 symbolic act——他不只是说"我 want you to stay"，他 literally 给了她 access to his space。"Casa de us"是 casual intimacy 的表达，说明他已经 view them as a unit。

### 第 N 段：First Kiss

> He sweeps me into his arms before I realize it's happening, his lips meeting mine in a short, soft kiss. "You've got ten minutes to shower and get back here."

**中文理解**：在他意识到之前，他已经把我拥入怀中，他的嘴唇在我的嘴唇上 short, soft kiss。"你有十分钟洗澡然后回到这里。"

**关键词**：sweeps me into his arms / short, soft kiss / ten minutes

**为什么这样写**：First kiss 是 gradual buildup 的 payoff——他们已经等了五天。"Short, soft kiss"与他们的 overall intensity 形成对比，但"ten minutes"的 ultimatum 说明他 still want more。

---

### 第 N 段：Marcus 的 Fantasy

> "I'll carry you back from the shower, naked and dripping. And I'll enjoy the hell out of it."

**中文理解**："我会把你从淋浴间光着身子湿漉漉地抱回去。而且我会非常享受它。"

**关键词**：naked and dripping / enjoy the hell out of it

**为什么这样写**：Marcus 的 fantasy 是 direct 和 unapologetic 的——他不 ashamed of his desires。他用这个来 flirt，同时也是在表达他想要她 physical presence 的程度。

---

### 第 N 段：Briar 的 Realization

> Though we've been alone for most of the past five days, we haven't been this alone. Now it's just the two of us, no aromium influencing our thoughts. It's no surprise I want him just as much as I did before.

**中文理解**：虽然过去五天我们大部分时间都是单独的，但我们从来没有这样 alone。现在只有我们两个，没有 aromium 影响我们的想法。我和之前一样想要他，这不足为奇。

**关键词**：just the two of us / no aromium / want him just as much

**为什么这样写**：Briar 的 realization 是关键——她的 attraction 不是 aromium 的效果。当 aromium 不在的时候，她仍然 want him。"Just the two of us"强调 authentic connection 而不是 chemically induced desire。
