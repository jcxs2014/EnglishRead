# 46. Enter, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Patterson 市长准备上台演讲；Springer 的步话机传来消息——一名白人男子进入了私人包厢；Springer 质问是谁放人进去。
- **情感弧线位置**：悬念 / 高潮前夜——Lunde 已经进入体育场，准备在市长演讲时行动。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的潜入"——Lunde 已经进入体育场的私人包厢；"步话机"——Springer 发现了异常，但为时已晚。
- **叙事手法**：第三人称有限视角——Walker 的视角，通过他的眼睛看到事件展开。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Brenton Walker was looking at Kevin Patterson's back as he stood by the opening of the drape, ready to mount the podium and be greeted by the cheers and the sunshine."

关键词：Brenton Walker / Kevin Patterson / back / drape / mount the podium / greeted / cheers / sunshine

为什么这样写：Walker 的"drape"是帷幕——市长站在帷幕前。"mount the podium"是登上讲台——市长准备上台。

> **原句 2:** "Patterson raised and lowered his shoulders, he rolled his neck like a boxer getting ready for a fight, fastened a button on his suit jacket, unfastened it, fastened it again."

关键词：raised and lowered his shoulders / rolled his neck / boxer / fight / fastened / unfastened / button / suit jacket

为什么这样写：Patterson 的"boxer"是拳击手——他像拳击手一样活动脖子。"fastened / unfastened"是扣上/解开——他紧张地扣扣子。

> **原句 3:** "Walker's seething sense of disquiet had started to abate, perhaps because there was now no way back and it was too late to do anything about anything they might have overlooked."

关键词：seething / disquiet / abate / no way back / too late / overlooked

为什么这样写：Walker 的"seething"是翻腾的——他的不安开始平息。"overlooked"是忽略——他们可能忽略的事情。

> **原句 4:** "That was a lesson Brenton's father had taught him: the need to accept things you cannot change. It was advice his father himself never followed, and that caused his downfall as a local politician."

关键词：lesson / father / accept / cannot change / advice / never followed / downfall / local politician

为什么这样写：Walker 的"downfall"是垮台——他父亲因不接受现实而垮台。

> **原句 5:** "‘Ten seconds please,’ said a man wearing a headset. ‘Break a leg, Mr Mayor.’"

关键词：Ten seconds / headset / Break a leg / Mr Mayor

为什么这样写：Headset 的"Break a leg"是祝好运——英语俚语。

> **原句 6:** "His walkie-talkie crackled into life and a grating voice spoke: ‘Foxtrot, I see a male, white, age around fifty, about five foot nine, entering one of the private boxes.’"

关键词：walkie-talkie / crackled / grating voice / Foxtrot / male / white / fifty / five foot nine / private boxes

为什么这样写：Springer 的"crackled"是噼啪响——步话机响了。"Foxtrot"是代号——代号 Foxtrot。

> **原句 7:** "Walker saw Springer's face turn pale as he picked up the walkie-talkie and spoke quietly into it: ‘Do you have a sighting on him, Foxtrot?’ ‘No, he disappeared into the back of the box, into the darkness.’"

关键词：turn pale / picked up / walkie-talkie / sighting / disappeared / box / darkness

为什么这样写：Springer 的"turn pale"是变白——他脸色变白。"private box"是私人包厢——包厢。

> **原句 8:** "‘Listen up!’ Springer shouted into the room. ‘There is someone up in one of the boxes. Does anyone know how this happened or who this person is?’"

关键词：Listen up / shouted / someone / boxes / how this happened / who this person is

为什么这样写：Springer 的"Listen up"是听好了——他在喊。
