# 52. Kentucky Fried, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Liza 在酒吧通过电视直播看到 Oz 进入标本店与 Lunde 对峙；Kay 与 Springer 争论，要求给 Oz 五分钟；O'Rourke 支持 Oz，Walker 选择支持 Kay。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 紧张——外部的警察与内部的 Oz/Lunde 形成对峙，时间紧迫。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："酒吧直播"——Liza 在酒吧看电视直播；"FBI 的傲慢"——Springer 认为 Oz 是"不稳定的酒鬼"；"支持"——O'Rourke 和 Walker 支持 Oz。
- **叙事手法**：多视角交叉——Liza（酒吧）、Kay（现场）两视角。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "‘Isn't that that guy who was in here?’ the customer said, gesturing with his beer glass toward the small, muted TV screen on the wall behind Liza."

关键词：customer / gesturing / beer glass / small / muted TV screen / wall / Liza

为什么这样写：Customer 的"gesturing"是手势——他用啤酒杯指着电视。"muted"是静音的——电视静音。

> **原句 2:** "She turned around to look. It was KSTP-TV with a flashing BREAKING NEWS line above the picture. A female reporter was talking to the camera. Behind her police cars were visible, and a big military-looking vehicle with SWAT in white lettering along the side. Then Liza saw the picture of a face in the upper-right corner of the screen. With the name under it: Bob Oz. MPD. With a trembling hand Liza grabbed the remote and turned up the sound."

关键词：turned around / KSTP-TV / flashing / BREAKING NEWS / female reporter / camera / police cars / military-looking vehicle / SWAT / white lettering / upper-right corner / Bob Oz / MPD / trembling hand / remote

为什么这样写：Liza 的"trembling"是颤抖的——她的手在颤抖。"BREAKING NEWS"是突发新闻——新闻标题。

> **原句 3:** "‘… went inside and persuaded the hostage-taker to take him in exchange for the release of Jill Patterson and the two children. Bob Oz is now in there with the hostage-taker, who is believed to be the owner of the store. He is Mike Lunde, a fifty-eight-year-old widower.’"

关键词：persuaded / hostage-taker / exchange / release / Jill Patterson / two children / owner / fifty-eight-year-old / widower

为什么这样写：Reporter 的"widower"是丧偶者——Lunde 是丧偶者。

> **原句 4:** "Survive. Liza's hands went to her mouth. She took deep breaths. In. Out. In. Out. Breathe. And then she did something she hadn't done for so long she couldn't even remember when the last time was. She prayed. God, let him survive. I don't ever have to see him again if that's what you want in return. But dear God, don't let him die."

关键词：Survive / mouth / deep breaths / prayed / let him survive / see him again / return / don't let him die

为什么这样写：Liza 的"prayed"是祈祷——她祈祷。"let him survive"是让他活下来——她的祈祷。

> **原句 5:** "Kay Myers ran crouching past the police cars outside the taxidermist's store and didn't straighten up until she was behind the big SWAT vehicle where Walker was standing. A few feet away Springer and O'Rourke seemed to be in the middle of a heated discussion."

关键词：ran crouching / police cars / straighten up / SWAT vehicle / Walker / Springer / O'Rourke / heated discussion

为什么这样写：Kay 的"crouching"是蹲着跑——她蹲着跑。"heated discussion"是激烈的讨论。

> **原句 6:** "‘Springer wants SWAT to take Lunde out,’ said Walker. ‘O'Rourke says that'll put Oz's life in danger.’"

关键词：take Lunde out / Oz's life in danger

为什么这样写：Walker 的"take out"是消灭——消灭 Lunde。

> **原句 7:** "‘Thanks but we can get along without any interference from you, Myers,’ Springer snorted. At least he remembered the 's' in her name this time. ‘Shut up and listen,’ said Kay. ‘Oz and Lunde know each other.’"

关键词：get along / interference / snorted / remembered / 's' / shut up / listen / know each other

为什么这样写：Kay 的"shut up"是闭嘴——她在反驳 Springer。

> **原句 8:** "‘According to our information Detective Oz is an unstable, alcoholic police officer who has been suspended for disobeying orders and is responsible for putting himself in this current situation. He has risked the lives of three innocent civilians, he's no hero, he just wants to look like one. That's understandable, given the fucked-up state of his own life. You say he and Lunde are friends, maybe that's why he keeps sabotaging SWAT's snipers by blocking their line of fire.’"

关键词：unstable / alcoholic / suspended / disobeying orders / responsible / risked / innocent civilians / hero / look like / fucked-up / sabotaging / snipers / line of fire

为什么这样写：Springer 的"fucked-up"是糟糕的——他认为 Oz 的生活糟糕。"sabotaging"是破坏——他认为 Oz 在破坏狙击手的瞄准线。

> **原句 9:** "Kay noticed the saliva spray from her own mouth and how some of it landed on Springer's pinstriped jacket as she answered: ‘But can't you get it through your thick head that Bob Oz is in a position to get Mike Lunde to hand himself over?’"

关键词：saliva spray / mouth / landed / pinstriped jacket / thick head / hand himself over

为什么这样写：Kay 的"thick head"是笨脑袋——她在骂 Springer。"hand himself over"是自首——让 Lunde 自首。

> **原句 10:** "‘Give Bob five minutes,’ she said. ‘Or I'm going over there …’ Kay nodded toward the KSTP-TV bus further down the street. ‘And I'll tell them exactly what you just said, Springer.’"

关键词：five minutes / going over there / KSTP-TV bus / tell them / exactly

为什么这样写：Kay 的"exactly"是确切地——她会确切地说出来。

> **原句 11:** "Kay wasn't looking at Springer, she was looking at Walker. His head was on one side, and he was smiling at her. Not encouragingly, not happily, but proudly. And regretfully. The way he might smile at someone who's done the right thing, something he perhaps would have done himself back in the day, when he had the guts for it. The way you might smile at someone you'd like to help back up on their feet, but when winter comes, and a place like this gets iced up, then all you can do is look after number one."

关键词：smiling / proudly / regretfully / done the right thing / guts / help back up / winter / iced up / look after number one

为什么这样写：Walker 的"regretfully"是遗憾地——他遗憾地微笑。"iced up"是结冰——像冬天结冰一样。
