# 31. The Great Equaliser, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 在家看老电影，思考 Gomez 的"名单"和动机；Hanson 在家发现儿子 Sean 偷了他的左轮手枪，暗示 Sean 与 Die Man 有关联；Gomez 录制了处决 Cody Karlstad 的视频，揭示了他的复仇计划——他要杀死所有与 McDeath 枪击案有关的人。
- **情感弧线位置**：过渡 / 揭示——Gomez 的视角首次完整呈现，他的动机和计划被揭示；Hanson 的家庭问题（儿子 Sean）也浮出水面。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的独白"——Gomez 直接对死者说话，揭示了他的计划和哲学；"腐败警察的儿子"——Hanson 的儿子 Sean 偷枪，暗示他也卷入了犯罪。
- **人物弧线**：Gomez 从"杀手"变为"复仇者"——他的动机更加复杂；Hanson 从"追捕者"变为"父亲"——他的家庭问题暴露。
- **叙事手法**：三视角交叉——Oz、Hanson、Gomez（对死者说话）三视角快速切换。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Bob had taken off all his clothes and was sitting on the couch holding the Radica 20Q. When he had bought it for Frankie, Alice had said she was too young for a game like that, but Frankie had loved it when Daddy had asked her to think of a question, and only helped her a little bit with the answers."

关键词：Radica 20Q / Frankie / too young / think of the question / answers

为什么这样写：Oz 的"Radica 20Q"是玩具——他保留了女儿的东西。"think of a question"是互动——父女间的游戏。

为什么这样写：Oz 的"couldn't face checking"是回避——他不想看消息。"solitary fox"是比喻——Gomez 是"狐狸"。

> **原句 3:** "Did Tomás Gomez have a list like that? How many names were on it? Who could be next?"

关键词：list / names / next

为什么这样写：Oz 的"list"是清单——他在想 Gomez 是否有"杀人名单"。"who could be next"是悬念——下一个人是谁？

> **原句 4:** "He imagined Tomás Gomez limping away to some place where he could hide out. A man driven by grief, by the loss of his family, by hatred of a society in which fifteen-year-olds can buy weapons and shoot a young girl as she sits in her wheelchair."

为什么这样写：Oz 的"driven by grief"是动机——Gomez 被悲伤驱动。"wheelchair"是细节——Gomez 的女儿坐轮椅。

> **原句 5:** "The great equaliser. Freedom. And he thought the same useless thought once again: that if Alice, he and Frankie had moved north of the border, the statistical chances of Frankie being killed were a fraction of the three-figure number of children that die annually in accidental deaths involving guns."

关键词：great equaliser / freedom / north of the border / statistical chances / fraction / three-figure number / annually

为什么这样写：Oz 的"north of the border"是加拿大——他想如果搬到加拿大。"three-figure number"是三位数——每年数百儿童死于枪击。

为什么这样写：Oz 的"crusade"是圣战——Gomez 的"圣战"。"committed"是投入——他有多投入？

为什么这样写：Oz 的"batteries dead"是没电——玩具没电了，象征"死亡"。

> **原句 8:** "Olav Hanson lay in bed staring up at the ceiling. Listened to his wife who lay snoring beside him.
