# 56. Departure, September 2022

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Holger 与 Oz 在标本店见面，两人谈论 Mike Lunde、爱情、复仇与写作；Holger 说这本书是关于"好人如何变成怪物"的故事；最后 Holger 看到一位老太太给无家可归者热狗。
- **情感弧线位置**：结尾 / 反思——全书的收尾，Holger 与 Oz 的对谈总结了主题。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："元小说"——Holger 直接与读者讨论他的写作方法；"人皮面具"——Alan 演示了如何制作人皮面具；"热狗"——Holger 看到老太太给无家可归者热狗，象征人性中的善良。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称独白——Holger 直接与读者对话，讨论他的写作意图。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The story ends there. Full stop. There is no more. Because stories aren't like life, which always has more to offer."

关键词：story ends / Full stop / no more / stories aren't like life / always has more to offer

为什么这样写：Holger 的"full stop"是句号——故事在这里结束。"stories aren't like life"是故事不像生活——生活总有更多。

> **原句 2:** "So I don't know what more life has to offer to Kay Myers, only that, six years on from the Mike Lunde case, she is now head of the Homicide Unit and shacked up with a younger colleague."

关键词：six years on / Mike Lunde case / head of the Homicide Unit / shacked up / younger colleague

为什么这样写：Holger 的"six years on"是六年后——时间跳跃。"shacked up"是同居——Kay 与同事同居。

> **原句 3:** "Kevin Patterson wound up in Washington DC, not as a politician but as a well-paid lobbyist for the agricultural sector. He never made it to the House of Representatives, something some commentators attributed to the damage done to his reputation by NRA lobbyists in the wake of his change of stance on the issue of gun control."

关键词：wound up / Washington DC / politician / well-paid lobbyist / agricultural sector / House of Representatives / commentators / attributed / damage / reputation / NRA lobbyists / wake / change of stance / gun control

为什么这样写：Holger 的"wound up"是最终——Patterson 最终成为说客。"change of stance"是立场改变——他改变了立场。

> **原句 4:** "One who didn't think life had much more to offer was Bob Oz. How wrong can you be? I push open the door to Town Taxidermy, and there he is. The red hair is a little thinner than in the pictures from back then, the face more lined. But the cashmere coat is the same, and for an instant it occurs to me that the man sitting motionless on a chair between a bear on its hindlegs and a leaping lynx has turned into the Bob Oz I wrote about in my story; stuffed, frozen in time, arrested in mid-movement. But then – as the bell above the door jingles – he looks over, his eyes light up and a smile spreads across his face. ‘Holger!’ he says as he gets to his feet."

关键词：didn't think / push open / Town Taxidermy / red hair / thinner / lined / cashmere coat / instant / motionless / bear / hindlegs / leaping lynx / stuffed / frozen in time / arrested in mid-movement / bell / jingles / gets to his feet

为什么这样写：Holger 的"stuffed"是填充的——他以为 Oz 被"标本化"了。"arrested in mid-movement"是定格在动作中——像标本一样。

> **原句 5:** "Over the past two years Bob and I have exchanged hundreds of emails and spent hours of screen time in each other's company. I've offered him a percentage of any royalties from the sales of my book, but he rejected the offer, said the conversations were free therapy for him. Bob has become what I would call a friend. Even though this is the first meeting in person it feels natural to give each other a warm embrace."

关键词：exchanged hundreds of emails / screen time / company / percentage / royalties / sales / rejected / free therapy / friend / first meeting in person / natural / warm embrace

为什么这样写：Holger 的"free therapy"是免费治疗——Oz 说这些对话是免费治疗。

> **原句 6:** "He and Liza have moved into a small house in – ironically enough – Chanhassen, just two streets away from where Emily lives. He's left the police and now works as head of security for a tech company. Not just because the pay is twice as good, but because two years ago Liza gave birth to their baby boy, and Bob wanted a more structured life. And Liza has been offered the chance to take over a bankrupt bar in downtown for next to nothing. The brewers are actually going to pay her to get the place back on its feet again. Bob has admitted that there are times when he misses police work, but that his decision to leave remains one of the few he never regrets."

关键词：moved / small house / ironically / Chanhassen / two streets away / left the police / head of security / tech company / pay / twice as good / Liza / gave birth / baby boy / structured life / bankrupt bar / downtown / next to nothing / brewers / pay / back on its feet / misses / never regrets

为什么这样写：Holger 的"ironically"是讽刺的是——讽刺的是他们住在 Chanhassen。"next to nothing"是几乎免费——几乎免费接手酒吧。

> **原句 7:** "‘So this is where you and Mike were sitting that last time?’ ‘Right here.’ We fall silent. I look out into the street as the scene plays itself over in my mind. ‘You must have been afraid,’ I say. ‘Actually no. He was so calm. And it was all so quiet. Like in a … like in a church.’"

关键词：last time / fall silent / scene / plays itself over / mind / afraid / calm / quiet / church

为什么这样写：Oz 的"calm"是平静的——Lunde 很平静。"church" is 教堂——像教堂一样安静。

> **原句 8:** "I nod. ‘We used to go drinking in Dinkytown. Me, him and Monica, the love of his life. Sometimes Monica brought along a friend who I think they hoped I would fall for. I never met anyone with such faith in the existence of deep and lasting love. I don't mean a naive faith, but a … well, an overwhelming conviction that it existed, despite the negative experiences of the majority.’"

关键词：drinking / Dinkytown / Monica / love of his life / friend / fall for / faith / deep and lasting love / naive faith / overwhelming conviction / existed / negative experiences / majority

为什么这样写：Holger 的"overwhelming conviction"是不可动摇的信念——对爱的信念。

> **原句 9:"‘What do you think of love?’ ‘What do I think of love? I think …’ He scratches himself behind his ear. ‘… that just occasionally, in between all the little loves, a big one comes along. But it doesn't necessarily look all that big on the radar, so you need to have your wits about you. And that sometimes a little love can grow, given the right kind of care and nourishment.’"

关键词：love / occasionally / little loves / big one / radar / wits about you / grow / care / nourishment

为什么这样写：Oz 的"radar"是雷达——爱情在雷达上不一定大。"wits about you"是保持清醒——需要保持清醒。

> **原句 10:"I give him a long look. ‘Was that your line when you were One-Night Bob, picking up all the girls?’ Bob gives a loud laugh. ‘One-Night Bob was a pig. But he had integrity, and he never used the word "love". That's something only the new Bob has earned the right to say.’"

关键词：long look / line / One-Night Bob / picking up / girls / loud laugh / pig / integrity / earned the right

为什么这样写：Oz 的"integrity"是正直——One-Night Bob 有正直。"earned the right"是赢得权利——新 Bob 才有权说爱。

> **原句 11:"Alan explains that the process wouldn't be very different if a human head were involved. I take notes as he demonstrates how you begin by cutting a Y-shape in the back of the head with a sharp knife. ‘You always cut from inside the skin out, so you don't cut off the hair. Then …' He holds up an ordinary, flat screwdriver which he says you insert under the skin and then push along, and little by little the skin comes loose from the cranium."

关键词：human head / Y-shape / sharp knife / inside the skin out / cut off the hair / screwdriver / insert / push along / little by little / comes loose / cranium

为什么这样写：Alan 的"cranium" is 头骨——皮肤从头骨上脱落。

> **原句 12:"‘There are times when I wish I was still a smoker,’ says Bob, stamping his feet on the sidewalk. The air is cold and sharp today, like the start of the Minnesota winters I've heard about but never experienced personally."

关键词：smoker / stamping his feet / sidewalk / cold / sharp / Minnesota winters / heard about / never experienced

为什么这样写：Oz 的"stamping his feet"是跺脚——他在跺脚取暖。

> **原句 13:"‘I'm wondering what it is that makes a person want to kill, when killing and causing suffering to others can no longer bring your loved ones back to you.’ ‘You wonder because you don't understand?’ ‘No. I wonder because I'm made that same way too. When Frankie died I wished someone had killed her. Because that way I would have had someone I could take my revenge on.’"

关键词：wonder / kill / suffering / bring your loved ones back / understand / made that same way / Frankie / wished / killed / revenge

为什么这样写：Oz 的"made that same way"是同一种构造——他和 Lunde 是同一种人。

> **原句 14:"‘So your book is going to be a defence of violent revenge?’ I shake my head. ‘I just want to tell a story about how good people can become monsters. For a little while back there, Mike was probably the most famous serial killer in the whole of the United States. Then there was another school shooting, or someone taking revenge on their old workplace, some news item with more victims than there were here, and Mike gets forgotten. And, strangely enough, that can make for a better and more general story.’"

关键词：defence / violent revenge / story / good people / become monsters / famous serial killer / school shooting / workplace / news item / victims / forgotten / general story

为什么这样写：Holger 的"general story"是更普遍的故事——更普遍的故事更好。

> **原句 15:"On the other side of the street I see an old lady who's just bought something from a cart I noticed particularly because it advertises Ambassador Hot Dogs, which I used to dream about as a boy when counting the months and weeks until my next trip to Minnesota. She's bought two hot dogs. ‘Jack London, as I'm sure you know, was an author as well as a journalist,’ I say. ‘He said that fiction is truer than fact. And that the best thing you could do with the facts was to make them look like fiction.’ ‘And that's what you intend to do?’ I shrug, and watch as the old lady bends to a homeless man sitting on the sidewalk with his back to the wall of a building. She gives him one of the hot dogs, straightens up, they exchange a few words, she laughs at something and then walks on. ‘I'm going to try, at least,’ I say. ‘It's probably the only thing that gives any meaning.’"

关键词：old lady / cart / Ambassador Hot Dogs / dream / boy / counting / months / weeks / trip / Minnesota / hot dogs / Jack London / author / journalist / fiction / truer than fact / facts / look like fiction / intend / shrug / bends / homeless man / sidewalk / wall / gives / straightens up / exchange / laughs / walks on / try / meaning

为什么这样写：Holger 的"fiction is truer than fact"是虚构比真实更真实——Jack London 的话。"meaning"是意义——唯一有意义的事。
