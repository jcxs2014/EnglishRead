# 51. Message, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 走进标本店与 Lunde 对峙；Lunde 解释了他的复仇计划——他杀死了真正的 Goya（Tomás Gomez），用他的脸作为面具，然后杀死了那些间接导致他家人死亡的人；Oz 让人质离开，Lunde 向 Oz 讲述了整个故事。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 对峙——Oz 与 Lunde 的对话揭示了整个真相。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的自白"——Lunde 向 Oz 讲述了他的复仇故事；"艺术家的孤独"——Lunde 说"每个艺术家都希望被理解"。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Oz 与 Lunde 的对话是本章的核心。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "Jill Patterson felt the warm tears running down her cheeks and lost the sensation as they ran onto the tape. They were cold by the time they ran back onto the skin and down over her chin. She wanted to close her eyes, shut out all this, but forced herself to keep them open, forced herself to look at Simon and Siri, who stared at her from above small, taped mouths, as though she, their mother, was the only person in all the world who could save them."

关键词：warm tears / cheeks / sensation / tape / cold / skin / chin / close her eyes / shut out / forced / Simon / Siri / taped mouths / only person / save them

为什么这样写：Jill 的"shut out"是屏蔽——她想闭眼不看。"taped mouths"是封着胶带——孩子们嘴被封住。

> **原句 2:** "Mike Lunde's voice beside her was calm, almost like someone talking in his sleep. ‘I'm sorry you had to see that man get shot, Mrs Patterson, I would have preferred things to be different. But as your husband preaches, it is the right of every citizen to protect his house and property against intruders. And there is actually a Closed sign on the door.’"

关键词：calm / talking in his sleep / sorry / get shot / preferred / preaches / right / citizen / protect / house / property / intruders / Closed sign

为什么这样写：Lunde 的"preaches"是宣扬——市长宣扬的。"Closed sign"是关门标志——门上有标志。

> **原句 3:** "Bob was two blocks away now but had to stop when the traffic lights in front of him turned red. He swore. The sounds were coming from several cars and they seemed to be getting closer. Bob turned on the radio and tuned in to the local news channel."

关键词：two blocks away / traffic lights / red / swore / junction / long wait / green / zebra stripes / sirens / lowered / window / radio / local news channel

> **原句 4:** "Bob felt his phone vibrate. He pulled it up. It was Kay. ‘Hey, what the hell is going on?’ ‘Mike Lunde,’ said Kay. ‘He has the mayor's wife and children with him in the store. He shot one of the bodyguards. I'm on my way there now.’"

> **原句 5:** "Bob glanced up at the red light, looked left and then right and saw a trailer approaching. He hoped the Volvo was having one of its better days and caught a glimpse of the staring man in the zebra-striped car as he put his foot down hard on the gas pedal."

> **原句 6:** "Bob turned down the street where Town Taxidermy was located at the same moment as an ambulance entered at the other end, sirens blaring. He leaned out of the window and saw two police cars outside the store. They had stopped in the middle of the road, blue lights flashing. Bob drove the Volvo up onto the sidewalk, jumped out and ran past the crowd of onlookers toward the store and ducked under the crime scene tape. Four police officers and a man in a dark suit were taking cover behind the cars. Two had service rifles aimed in the direction of the store, two had service pistols."

关键词：Town Taxidermy / ambulance / sirens blaring / leaned / police cars / blue lights flashing / sidewalk / jumped out / crowd / onlookers / crime scene tape / police officers / dark suit / taking cover / service rifles / service pistols

为什么这样写：Oz 的"crime scene tape"是犯罪现场胶带——他钻过胶带。"service rifles"是制式步枪——警察的步枪。

> **原句 7:** "‘Get out of here!’ yelled one of the officers, a sturdy man, his face flushed as he waved his arms at Bob. ‘MPD, Homicide Unit!’ Bob yelled back and ducked down behind the police car. 关键词：Get out of here / yelled / sturdy / flushed / waved / MPD / Homicide Unit / ducked / expired ID card / FBI / Detective Bob Oz

为什么这样写：Officer 的"sturdy" is 强壮的——强壮的军官。"expired ID card" is 过期的身份证——Oz 的身份证过期了。

> **原句 8:** "‘He's in there with the hostages,’ said the officer. ‘No sign of life.’ ‘What are you doing here, Detective?’ the FBI guy interrupted. ‘I know Mike Lunde. Who are you?’ ‘Gerard Zimmer, JTTF.’ Bob nodded at the SUV that stood with both front doors open. ‘Where's your partner, Zimmer?’ ‘On his way to the hospital. Or the morgue, hard to say. Bullet caught him above his vest.’"

关键词：hostages / sign of life / interrupted / Mike Lunde / Gerard Zimmer / JTTF / SUV / partner / hospital / morgue / bullet / vest

为什么这样写：Zimmer 的"morgue"是太平间——可能去太平间。"vest"是防弹衣——子弹打在防弹衣上方。

> **原句 9:** "‘Four minutes,’ Bob repeated. He stood up and started unbuttoning his cashmere coat. ‘What are you doing?’ the police officer shouted. ‘Get down! Zimmer says the guy in there has an M24!’ ‘I know,’ said Bob. ‘And I know four minutes is a long time and that having SWAT here guarantees nothing.’ He folded his coat and laid it on the hood of the car. ‘Where are you going?’ asked Zimmer. ‘To talk to Mike.’"

> **原句 10:** "Bob walked round Zimmer and crossed the street, coatless. His shirt was wet with sweat, ice-cold on the shady side, warm on the sunny side. Behind him someone shouted. 关键词：walked round / crossed / coatless / wet with sweat / ice-cold / shady side / sunny side / shouted / too late / hope / shoot / doorway / coming in

为什么这样写：Oz 的"coatless"是没穿外套——他没穿外套。"ice-cold"是冰冷——衬衫冰冷。

> **原句 11:** "The bell jangled as he walked in. Four people sat in a circle around something. A dog. The Labrador retriever Mike Lunde had finally managed to get the right eyes for. > **原句 12:** "‘Turned out well,’ he said with a nod to the dog. ‘Thank you.’ Bob took in the circle. Met the red, pleading eyes of the two children and the woman. He recognised her, she was the woman who had come in to talk about the eyes with Mike. Bob nodded to them, trying to convey to them that this would turn out OK, they weren't about to die. The eyes of the guy who killed my little girl in that parking lot. Who stood over me and was about to kill me too.
