# 24. Recoil, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：杀手在停车场屋顶等待，用步枪射杀了 Cody Karlstad——一个看似普通的中产阶级男人，每天在同一时间、同一停车位停车；杀手冷静地计算距离、角度，然后扣下扳机。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 暴力——全书的第二次谋杀，节奏紧凑、细节精确。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的日常"——Gomez 不是疯狂的杀手，而是精确的工程师；"目标的生活"——Karlstad 是一个节俭的中产阶级，每天买口香糖只为免费停车。
- **人物弧线**：Gomez 从"悲伤的父亲"变为"冷血的执行者"——他的枪法完美，但也会"感到紧张"（手的颤抖）。
- **叙事手法**：纯杀手第一人称——读者直接进入他的视角，感受他的思维模式。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "It was starting to get dark as I watched Cody Karlstad walk through the parking lot. In the half-hour I'd been waiting up there on the roof there had been a lot of activity down below, cars coming, cars going. Through the telescopic sights I followed Karlstad until he reached the big blue pickup, unlocked it and climbed in."

关键词：starting to get dark / half-hour / activity / telescopic sights / pickup / unlocked / climbed in

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"half-hour"是耐心——他等了很久。"telescopic sights"是军事术语——他用狙击镜观察。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"cars coming, cars going"是日常——停车场是"日常"的场所。

> **原句 2:** "My pulse rate was low, even though I hadn't taken the beta blockers as I had considered doing yesterday. I'd worked out that the reason I didn't hit Dante properly was because my pulse rate had been too high."

关键词：pulse rate / beta blockers / worked out / hit Dante properly / too high

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"beta blockers"是药物——降压药（用于稳定心率）。"worked out"是分析——他在总结失误。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"hit Dante properly"是遗憾——他上次"没打中"（Dante 没死）。

> **原句 3:** "I knew that gave me seven seconds. I knew because this was the fourth day I'd been there at the same time, and each time he had carried out exactly the same ritual. He put his briefcase on the floor in front of the passenger seat, slipped the key in the ignition, fastened his seat belt and turned on the ignition."

关键词：seven seconds / fourth day / ritual / briefcase / ignition / seat belt

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"ritual"是规律——Karlstad 的行为是可预测的。"seven seconds"是窗口——他只有七秒。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"fourth day"是耐心——他观察了四天。

> **原句 4:** "Cody Karlstad was a white, middle-class part-owner of an agricultural machinery dealership. He had three children and a wife who worked in the local church. Cody Karlstad was a frugal man. Despite the fact that his car was worth 50,000 dollars he parked it every morning in the free parking slot at Southdale Mall."

关键词：middle-class / agricultural machinery dealership / frugal / free parking slot

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"frugal"是细节——Karlstad 很节俭。"free parking slot"是动机——他为免费停车。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"50,000 dollars"是对比——车很贵但人很省。

> **原句 5:** "That was seven o'clock, before the mall opened; he had five thousand vacant parking slots to choose from but he always picked the same one, just about in the centre of the desert."

关键词：five thousand vacant parking slots / same one / centre of the desert

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"five thousand"是夸张——停车场很大。"same one"是规律——Karlstad 每天停同一位置。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"centre of the desert"是比喻——停车场是"沙漠"。

> **原句 6:** "Then Cody Karlstad headed over toward the building where he worked. It shared a parking lot with the women's hospital, and he'd have had to pay a monthly rent of 155 dollars to park there."

关键词：shared / women's hospital / monthly rent / 155 dollars

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"155 dollars"是精确——他调查过价格。"women's hospital"是地点——与 ch21 的护士罢工呼应。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"shared a parking lot"是巧合—— Karlstad 的工作地点与医院相邻。

> **原句 7:** "I was lying on the roof of the parking garage now. Between me and Cody Karlstad was a busy road and a lot of parking lot. Altogether the distance was almost exactly four hundred yards, but through the telescopic sights it looked a lot less than that."

关键词：roof / parking garage / busy road / four hundred yards / telescopic sights

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"four hundred yards"是距离——与 ch19 的射击练习呼应。"telescopic sights"是工具——他用狙击镜。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"looked a lot less"是光学——狙击镜让距离变近。

> **原句 8:** "With the silencer and the roar of traffic below me no one was going to hear the crack if I squeezed the trigger. When I squeezed the trigger. When!"

关键词：silencer / roar of traffic / crack / squeezed the trigger / When!

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"silencer"是消音器——他准备了消音设备。"When!"是决心——他在犹豫何时开枪。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"squeezed the trigger"是动作——"扣扳机"而非"开枪"。

> **原句 9:** "So, I had seven seconds. Seven seconds before the engine turned over, the headlamps lit up and the interior light automatically went out. But for the seven seconds before Cody Karlstad was wrapped in darkness the lighting would be perfect."

关键词：engine turned over / headlamps / interior light / wrapped in darkness / lighting would be perfect

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"seven seconds"是倒计时——他只有七秒。"wrapped in darkness"是比喻——车内变暗。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"lighting would be perfect"是技术——他需要光线才能瞄准。

> **原句 10:** "On the windshield, positioned above the light, was that white three-by-three square I covered with the cross hairs as I slowly pulled the trigger back. Owing to the angle all I could see were the hands fastening the seat belt, not his face."

关键词：windshield / white three-by-three square / cross hairs / pulled the trigger back / fastening the seat belt

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"white three-by-three square"是标记——他之前在车上贴的胶带。"cross hairs"是瞄准——他用十字准星。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"not his face"是回避——他不想看到脸。

> **原句 11:** "Perhaps that's why I didn't feel nervous. But I wanted him to fasten his seat belt first, I didn't want him slumping forward and leaving his upper body pressing the horn, which would immediately have drawn attention to the scene."

关键词：didn't feel nervous / fasten his seat belt / slumping forward / pressing the horn / drawn attention

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"didn't feel nervous"是罕见的——他通常紧张。"slumping forward"是想象——尸体倒下的画面。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"drawn attention"是谨慎——他不想被发现。

> **原句 12:** "The rifle butt imparted its slight kick to my shoulder. I saw a black mark in the white square. A perfect shot."

关键词：rifle butt / imparted / slight kick / black mark / perfect shot

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"rifle butt"是枪托——枪的后座力。"imparted"是传递——力量传递到肩膀。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"perfect shot"是满意——他打中了。

> **原句 13:** "I lowered the sights. In the interior, which was still illuminated, I could see Karlstad's body shaking. It shouldn't have been shaking. I'd done all the calculations; the distance, the angle, the thickness of the glass, the height of the seat, the length of Cody Karlstad's body from the hips upward."

关键词：lowered the sights / illuminated / shaking / calculations / distance / angle / thickness / hips upward

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"calculations"是数学——他用数学计算弹道。"shaking"是意外——尸体在抖。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"shouldn't have been shaking"是困惑——他不理解为什么。

> **原句 14:** "Cody ought to have been sitting motionless with a hole in his forehead. But there he was, shaking like he was strapped to an electric chair."

关键词：motionless / hole in his forehead / shaking / strapped to an electric chair

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"electric chair"是比喻——电椅（执行死刑的工具）。"strapped"是束缚——被绑在椅子上。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"ought to have been"是预期——他预期尸体不动。

> **原句 15:** "I loaded the rifle. Took aim again. Calmly. Pulled the trigger. The kick against my shoulder was almost pleasurable. Once again, the shot hit the taped square, an inch higher this time. And Cody Karlstad stopped shaking."

关键词：loaded / took aim / calmly / kick / almost pleasurable / taped square / inch higher / stopped shaking

为什么这样写：Gomez 的"calmly"是冷静——他第二枪很冷静。"almost pleasurable"是罕见——他感到"愉悦"。

读者视角提示：Gomez 的"stopped shaking"是解决——第二枪解决了问题。
