# 43. Lobo, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Walker 和 Springer 在体育场等待市长演讲；Rooble 打来电话，确认 Gomez 就是 Lobo（连环杀手）；Dante 提到 Goya 的手上有缝线，像戴着手套。
- **情感弧线位置**：推进 / 确认——Goya 的身份被确认为 Lobo，一个著名的连环杀手。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："杀手的身份"——Goya 被确认为 Lobo；"手的细节"——Dante 提到 Goya 的手上有缝线，暗示他可能做了手部移植或伪装。
- **人物弧线**：Walker 从"怀疑"变为"确信"——他意识到 Goya 比想象中更危险。
- **叙事手法**：对话驱动——Walker 与 Rooble 的电话对话揭示了 Goya 的身份。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "‘Well, Superintendent,’ said Ted Springer as he stood next to Walker and picked up a slice of watermelon, ‘not hungry today? Not thirsty?’ Springer gestured with his free hand to the table in front of them and the coffee pots and bottles of water, the fruit along with a few simple sandwiches."

关键词：Superintendent / Ted Springer / slice of watermelon / hungry / thirsty / gestured / free hand / table / coffee pots / bottles of water / fruit / simple sandwiches

为什么这样写：Springer 的"slice of watermelon"是一片西瓜——他在吃西瓜。"gestured"是手势——他用手势指向桌子。

> **原句 2:** "‘Thanks, I ate before I left,’ said Walker. He was watching Mayor Patterson as the mayor stood by the coffee pots talking with someone from the NRA. Someone important, judging by the body language and the facial expressions; two men who could be useful to each other."

关键词：ate before I left / watching / Mayor Patterson / coffee pots / NRA / judging by / body language / facial expressions / useful to each other

为什么这样写：Walker 的"judging by"是判断——他通过肢体语言判断。"useful to each other"是互相有用——两人互相有用。

> **原句 3:** "Walker glanced at his watch. Five minutes before Patterson stepped up onto the podium. The speech would last a maximum of ten minutes. Then it was job done and home to the family. There was still a lot of weekend left."

关键词：glanced at his watch / Five minutes / podium / maximum / ten minutes / job done / home to the family / weekend left

为什么这样写：Walker 的"maximum"是最多——演讲最多十分钟。"job done"是工作完成——完成后回家。

> **原句 4:** "The phone in his pocket vibrated. It wasn't Hanson this time either. ‘Yes, Rooble?’ said Walker. ‘Dante says that Gomez is Lobo.’"

关键词：pocket / vibrated / Hanson / Rooble / Dante / Gomez / Lobo

为什么这样写：Walker 的"vibrated"是震动——手机震动。"Lobo"是狼——Goya 的代号。

> **原句 5:** "‘What?’ ‘Tomás Gomez is Lobo.’ Rooble spoke clearly and calmly, so it wasn't a case of Walker not hearing him, more that he just didn't believe what he had heard. ‘The Lobo?’ said Walker. ‘Yes. The Wanted poster was still up on the wall when I started in Homicide. I remember the description referred to a star-shaped tattoo on the back of one hand. Hanson said it was a cartel thing from south of the border.’"

关键词：clearly and calmly / not hearing / didn't believe / Wanted poster / Homicide / description / star-shaped tattoo / back of one hand / cartel / south of the border

为什么这样写：Rooble 的"Wanted poster"是通缉令——通缉令还在墙上。"cartel"是卡特尔——贩毒集团。

> **原句 6:** "Walker closed his eyes. Opened them again. Lobo. He turned to Springer, who was holding the slice of melon up in front of his face so it looked like he was grinning from ear to ear."

关键词：closed his eyes / Opened them again / Lobo / slice of melon / grinning from ear to ear

为什么这样写：Springer 的"grinning from ear to ear"是咧嘴笑——他看起来在笑。

> **原句 7:** "‘Bad news, Walker?’ ‘Yes. We need to postpone the speech.’ ‘Why?’ Springer took another mouthful of melon. ‘Gomez is almost certainly identical to a man called Lobo, a notorious serial killer.’"

关键词：Bad news / postpone the speech / mouthful / identical / notorious / serial killer

为什么这样写：Walker 的"postpone"是推迟——他建议推迟演讲。"notorious"是臭名昭著的——Lobo 是臭名昭著的。

> **原句 8:** "‘What difference does that make? We already know Gomez is a killer.’ Walker looked at Springer. He realised he had no good answer. That the unease he felt in the pit of his stomach at the news was not an argument."

关键词：difference / already know / no good answer / unease / pit of his stomach / argument

为什么这样写：Walker 的"unease"是不安——他感到不安。"pit of his stomach"是胃里——胃里的不安。

> **原句 9:** "Walker heard Rooble's voice and realised he was still on the line. ‘What?’ he said, putting the phone to his ear. ‘I said, Dante said there was something strange about Gomez's hands.’ ‘What, exactly?’ ‘They had stitches along the sides, like seams. That the skin seemed to sort of move when he moved his hands. Like he was wearing gloves.’"

关键词：still on the line / phone to his ear / strange / hands / stitches / sides / seams / skin / move / gloves

为什么这样写：Dante 的"stitches"是缝线——手上有缝线。"seams" is 接缝——像接缝。"gloves"是手套——像戴着手套。
