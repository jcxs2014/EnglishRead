# 45. Portrait, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Oz 在 Lunde 家中见到 Emily Lunde（Mike 的妹妹，坐轮椅）；他在 Mike 的房间里发现了人皮面具、手套和粉色药片，意识到 Mike Lunde 就是 Goya——他戴着人皮面具伪装成 Goya。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 真相——Oz 发现了 Mike Lunde 就是 Goya 的证据（人皮面具、手套、药片）。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："人皮面具"——Mike Lunde 戴着人皮面具伪装成 Goya；"药片"——Mike 和 Goya 同时停止服用抗抑郁药，暗示他们是同一个人。
- **人物弧线**：Oz 从"追捕者"变为"发现者"——他终于知道了真相；Lunde 从"标本师"变为"杀手"——他伪装成 Goya 进行复仇。
- **叙事手法**：Oz 的第一人称独白——读者直接进入他的视角，跟随他发现证据。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "‘I'm sorry, I did ring the bell,’ said Bob to the woman in the wheelchair. ‘Bob Oz. I'm a friend of Mike's. Is he in?’"

关键词：ring the bell / wheelchair / Bob Oz / friend of Mike's

为什么这样写：Oz 的"ring the bell"是按门铃——他按了门铃。"wheelchair"是轮椅——Emily 坐在轮椅上。

> **原句 2:** "‘Oh, I see,’ she said, panting for breath, one hand flat against her chest. ‘Just give me a moment to recover. I'm afraid you've missed Mike, he just left.’"

关键词：panting for breath / flat against her chest / recover / missed Mike / just left

为什么这样写：Emily 的"panting for breath"是喘气——她在喘气。"recover"是恢复——她需要时间恢复。

> **原句 3:** "Bob nodded, studying her. She looked to be in her fifties, and her clothes were conservative and almost old-fashioned, in the same way Mike's were."

关键词：studying / fifties / conservative / old-fashioned

为什么这样写：Oz 的"studying"是打量——他在打量她。"old-fashioned"是过时的——她的衣服过时。

> **原句 4:** "‘I believe I've seen a picture of you somewhere,’ he said. ‘Aren't you …?’ ‘Emily Lunde,’ she said, offering her hand. ‘Mike's sister.’"

关键词：picture / Emily Lunde / Mike's sister

为什么这样写：Oz 的"picture"是照片——他见过她的照片。

> **原句 5:** "‘Multiple sclerosis,’ Emily called as she filled the kettle. ‘What?’ ‘You're wondering why Mike's daughter and I are both in wheelchairs. Grandma also had MS.’"

关键词：Multiple sclerosis / kettle / wheelchairs / Grandma / MS

为什么这样写：Emily 的"Multiple sclerosis"是多发性硬化症——解释坐轮椅的原因。

> **原句 6:** "Bob looked at the faces in the portrait. He saw no trace of doubt in any of them. They believed the future was bright. That all of them would live long and happy lives."

关键词：portrait / no trace of doubt / bright / long and happy lives

为什么这样写：Oz 的"no trace of doubt"是没有一丝怀疑——他们相信未来。

> **原句 7:** "The room was white, clean and tidy. The bed was made, the drapes parted. There was a TV on the wall. In spite of the items of personal property lying about – a cell phone on the chest of drawers, a hanger with a pair of faded jeans and a hoodie on the closet door – something about the room gave Bob the feeling that it was abandoned, and that the person who lived there wouldn't be coming back."

关键词：white / clean / tidy / bed made / drapes parted / TV / personal property / abandoned / wouldn't be coming back

为什么这样写：Oz 的"abandoned"是被遗弃的——房间感觉被遗弃了。

> **原句 8:** "On the bed, on top of the pillow, lay a brown face mask with holes for the eyes and mouth. In fact it was a complete head covering, including a full head of hair. On the blanket was a pair of thin brown gloves. They lay like the hands of a person lying in the bed would have laid."

关键词：pillow / brown face mask / holes / eyes / mouth / complete head covering / hair / blanket / thin brown gloves

为什么这样写：Oz 的"brown face mask"是棕色人皮面具——面具在床上。

> **原句 9:** "Bob picked up the mask and looked at it more closely. Shuddered as he recognised the face with the scar on the cheek. At the back the skin was cut away low on the neck and up to the crown of the head, and there was a lace woven through perforations in the skin to make it easy to take on and off."

关键词：shuddered / recognised / scar / cheek / cut away / crown / lace / perforations / take on and off

为什么这样写：Oz 的"shuddered"是颤抖——他颤抖了。"perforations" is 穿孔——皮肤上的穿孔。

> **原句 10:** "He ran his fingertips over the gloves of human skin and across the tattooed five-pointed star. He thought of Tomás Gomez's fingerprints they had found at the crime scenes. On the handle of the restroom. It was all beginning to make sense now. Mike Lunde hadn't escaped up through the ventilation shaft at the shopping mall, he had simply taken off the hoodie, the Gomez mask and the Gomez gloves. Probably put them in a bag which he hidden under his jacket. Dismantled the rifle to make room for it too in the bag. With practice, the routine wouldn't have taken more than a couple of minutes. After that he'd pulled down the fan, tossed one of Gomez's insulin syringes into the ventilation shaft and then strolled out of the restroom like a quite ordinary white man out shopping, walking straight past Kay and the SWAT team. It was a trick he could repeat time after time without ever getting caught."

关键词：fingertips / human skin / tattooed / five-pointed star / fingerprints / crime scenes / ventilation shaft / hoodie / mask / gloves / bag / jacket / dismantled / rifle / routine / couple of minutes / fan / insulin syringes / strolled / ordinary / trick / repeat / without ever getting caught

为什么这样写：Oz 的"routine"是例行程序——Lunde 的逃脱程序。"trick"是诡计——他可以重复使用。

> **原句 11:** "Bob looked at the cell phone. It was turned off. A police voice expert would be able to confirm that the recording of the alleged Tomás Gomez who called Mike Lunde was in reality Mike Lunde himself, standing in a phone booth and calling his own cell. That that explained why the breathing seemed to sound as if it was turning itself on and off."

关键词：cell phone / turned off / police voice expert / confirm / alleged / recording / in reality / phone booth / breathing / turning itself on and off

为什么这样写：Oz 的"alleged"是所谓的——所谓的 Goya。"in reality"是实际上——实际上是 Lunde 自己。

> **原句 12:** "On the bottom shelf Bob saw a familiar-looking tray of pills. Pink. Bob picked it up and read the long and unpronounceable name of the antidepressants. He read the doctor's signature and the date. The tray should have been empty, and when Bob counted the number of pills left he concluded that Mike Lunde must have stopped taking them and that, coincidentally, he must have done so at about the same time as he stopped taking his own pills."

关键词：bottom shelf / familiar-looking / tray / pills / pink / unpronounceable / antidepressants / signature / date / empty / counted / stopped taking / coincidentally

为什么这样写：Oz 的"antidepressants"是抗抑郁药——粉色药片。"coincidentally"是巧合地——同时停药。

> **原句 13:** "‘Only that he would be unveiling his masterpiece. He's been looking forward to that.’ Bob swallowed. ‘You know what, Emily? I see Mike left his cell phone in his room and I really need to get hold of him, so that tea is going to have to wait until another day.’"

关键词：unveiling / masterpiece / looking forward to / get hold of / wait until another day

为什么这样写：Emily 的"masterpiece"是杰作——Lunde 的杰作。"unveiling"是揭幕——揭幕仪式。
