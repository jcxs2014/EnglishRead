# 48. A Beer Outdoors, October 2016

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：SWAT 队进入私人包厢，发现是一名 janitor（清洁工）在喝啤酒，并非 Goya；Walker 收到 Kay 的消息，得知 Goya 是白人 Mike Lunde；市长 Patterson 坚持上台演讲，无视 Walker 的警告。
- **情感弧线位置**：高潮 / 错误——SWAT 抓错了人，但市长坚持要上台。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："抓错人"——SWAT 抓到的是 janitor，不是 Goya；"市长的傲慢"——Patterson 坚持上台，无视警告。
- **叙事手法**：多视角交叉——Walker、Springer、Patterson 三视角快速切换。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "O'Rourke's men were in position outside the door of the box. On the frosted glass he noted the logo of one of the Vikings' sponsors. Two men stood ready with the little battering ram, three others behind them with weapons trained on the door, the lights on the gun barrels lit."

关键词：in position / box / frosted glass / logo / Vikings / sponsors / battering ram / weapons trained / gun barrels lit

为什么这样写：O'Rourke 的"frosted glass"是磨砂玻璃——门上的玻璃。"battering ram"是破城锤——准备破门。

> **原句 2:** "O'Rourke breathed slowly as he waited for a response. Could feel in his pulse that this was the good kind of tension, on the right side of being nervous. It brought a strange feeling of safety to know that he was so alert. They were totally prepared for just about any eventuality. On the other hand they could never know exactly what lay in store for them. But that was what he loved about the job. The combination of the intoxication of control and the thrill of the risk. It was like fucking and being fucked at the same time."

关键词：breathed slowly / pulse / good kind of tension / right side of being nervous / alert / totally prepared / eventuality / intoxication / control / thrill / risk

为什么这样写：O'Rourke 的"intoxication"是陶醉——控制的陶醉。"thrill"是刺激——风险的刺激。

> **原句 3:** "Then Springer's voice was coming through the walkie-talkie. ‘Alpha. Do you have to use stun grenades?’ ‘Have to,’ said O'Rourke. ‘We're worried that might create panic in the stadium.’ ‘Tell the band to play louder.’ ‘Nothing plays louder than a stun grenade, and the flashes of light will be visible all over the stadium. Sixty thousand frightened people. You see what I'm getting at …’"

关键词：walkie-talkie / stun grenades / panic / stadium / band / louder / flashes / visible / frightened

为什么这样写：Springer 的"panic"是恐慌——担心造成恐慌。"stun grenades"是震眩弹——SWAT 的武器。

> **原句 4:** "O'Rourke saw all right. Not using stun grenades would deprive them of a tactical advantage and increase the risk of loss. On the other hand, nothing SWAT did was free of risk, and if only one man had been observed in there then the risk was acceptable. His decision was easy. ‘OK then, we go in without the stun grenades,’ said O'Rourke."

关键词：deprive / tactical advantage / increase / risk / loss / free of risk / observed / acceptable / decision

为什么这样写：O'Rourke 的"tactical advantage"是战术优势——不使用震眩弹会失去优势。

> **原句 5:** "Walker tapped the Call symbol and Myers answered before he had raised the phone to his ear. ‘I found Gomez's body,’ she said. ‘He's been flayed. Mike Lunde has been using his face as a mask.’"

关键词：Call symbol / Myers / answered / raised / phone / body / flayed / face / mask

为什么这样写：Kay 的"flayed"是剥皮——Goya 被剥皮。"mask"是面具——Lunde 用他的脸做面具。

> **原句 6:** "Walker – who liked to think he was capable of calm in moments of crisis – heard his own response, explosive and involuntary: ‘What?’"

关键词：capable / calm / crisis / explosive / involuntary

为什么这样写：Walker 的"involuntary"是不由自主的——他的反应不由自主。

> **原句 7:** "O'Rourke followed directly behind the five who went in front. By the time he was round the corner they had already surrounded the man sitting alone at the table and were pointing their automatics at him. The man's eyes were wide and black with fear, his mouth was open and his hands raised, though no one had given him the order. In front of him on the table was an open beer bottle with a handle that O'Rourke identified as a local brew, an Utepils. In a cupboard with a glass door behind the man he saw several more bottles of the same beer. O'Rourke wasn't sure if it was the bottle or the look on the man's face that told him straight away this was neither a sniper nor a terrorist. But rules are rules, so he nodded to his men and they took up position behind the chair in which the man sat. They lifted him up, laid him on the floor on his stomach and handcuffed him. O'Rourke squatted in front of him."

关键词：followed / round the corner / surrounded / pointing / automatics / wide / black with fear / mouth open / hands raised / order / beer bottle / handle / local brew / Utepils / cupboard / glass door / sniper / terrorist / rules / took up position / lifted / stomach / handcuffed / squatted

为什么这样写：O'Rourke 的"Utepils"是本地啤酒——当地啤酒品牌。"squatted"是蹲下——他蹲在嫌疑人面前。

> **原句 8:** "‘Owen Ruud,’ said O'Rourke. ‘He's got an ID says he's the stadium janitor. Looks genuine. And he's not Latino, looks more like a squarehead. It's his day off today, he says. Came along just for the mayor's speech and to have a beer.’"

关键词：Owen Ruud / ID / stadium janitor / genuine / Latino / squarehead / day off / mayor's speech / beer

为什么这样写：O'Rourke 的"janitor"是清洁工——嫌疑人的身份。"squarehead"是方头（对斯堪的纳维亚人的蔑称）——看起来像北欧人。

> **原句 9:** "‘Wait!’ called Walker. ‘I've just received a message from one of my colleagues. It seems that Gomez is a white man and –’ ‘Mr Mayor!’ Springer interrupted. ‘If the janitor is the man we've been looking for we have him now and we won't be letting him go. You're quite safe, so go ahead!’"

关键词：Wait / message / colleague / Gomez / white man / interrupted / janitor / looking for / quite safe / go ahead

为什么这样写：Springer 的"go ahead"是继续——他让市长继续。

> **原句 10:** "The man in the headset said something into the microphone as Patterson turned his back on Walker and started rolling his neck again. Walker told himself he hadn't tripped up, he'd done his bit, said what he had to say, and the mayor had made his decision. Soon he would be going home to eat with his family."

关键词：microphone / turned his back / rolling his neck / tripped up / done his bit / decision / going home / eat with his family

为什么这样写：Walker 的"done his bit"是做了他该做的——他做了该做的。

> **原句 11:** "A deep bass voice crackled across the stadium loudspeakers, accompanied by a drum roll that would probably soon give way to the national anthem: ‘And now, ladies and gentlemen, direct from city hall …’"

关键词：deep bass voice / crackled / loudspeakers / drum roll / give way / national anthem / ladies and gentlemen / city hall

为什么这样写：Announcer 的"drum roll"是鼓声——鼓声即将让位给国歌。

> **原句 12:** "Walker saw how the layer of skin pressed up against the collar around Patterson's neck tensed. Maybe it was the word ‘friend’. Maybe something else. The man with the headset drew the drape aside and they all looked out onto the stadium. As expected the drum roll had segued into the national anthem, which drowned out any whistling there might have been or the absence of applause. Still Patterson stood motionless in front of the opening."

关键词：layer of skin / pressed / collar / tensed / friend / drape aside / stadium / segued / national anthem / drowned out / whistling / applause / motionless

为什么这样写：Walker 的"segued"是过渡——鼓声过渡到国歌。"motionless"是静止的——市长一动不动。
