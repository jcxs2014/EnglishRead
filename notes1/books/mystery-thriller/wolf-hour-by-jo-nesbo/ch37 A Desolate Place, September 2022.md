# 37. A Desolate Place, September 2022

## 本章导航

- **一句话概括**：Holger 在 Mindekirken（挪威路德纪念教堂）会见牧师 Jon Erland，询问关于堂兄（Goya）的事；牧师称 Goya 为"可怜的迷途灵魂"，Holger对此不以为然。
- **情感弧线位置**：过渡 / 反思——Holger 继续他的"调查"，但这次是通过对话而非行动。
- **Tropes 兑现/反转**："元小说"——Holger 直接与读者对话，讨论他的写作方法；"牧师 vs. 作家"——牧师说"只有一个真相"，Holger 不同意。
- **人物弧线**：Holger 从"调查者"变为"反思者"——他意识到自己和牧师对"真相"的理解不同。
- **叙事手法**：第一人称独白——Holger 的视角，穿插对话。

## 精读

> **原句 1:** "The priest at the Mindekirken, Jon Erland, greets me in the lobby connecting the church itself to the offices. When I called him before my departure from Norway and told him about the book I was writing he said he hadn't actually known my cousin, only my uncle, and that I should really try some other source."

关键词：priest / Mindekirken / Jon Erland / lobby / departure / book / writing / cousin / uncle / source

为什么这样写：Holger 的"cousin"是堂兄——Goya。"source"是来源——牧师建议他找其他来源。

> **原句 2:** "Jon Erland looks to be in his seventies and speaks with the same Norwegian dialect as I do, not so surprising since the Norwegian Bible Belt is in the south of the country, like it is in the USA."

关键词：seventies / Norwegian dialect / Bible Belt / south / USA

为什么这样写：Holger 的"Bible Belt"是圣经地带——挪威的宗教地带。"dialect"是方言——他们说的是同一种方言。

> **原句 3:** "He is professionally friendly and open – an American openness that seems to have worn off some of that traditional Scandinavian reserve. He shows me round. The Mindekirken is mostly still as I recall it. Large but austere, as Lutheran churches should be."

关键词：professionally friendly / open / American openness / worn off / traditional Scandinavian reserve / shows me round / austere / Lutheran

为什么这样写：Holger 的"Scandinavian reserve"是斯堪的纳维亚人的内敛——美国开放风格磨掉了内敛。"austere"是简朴的——路德教堂应该简朴。

> **原句 4:** "Along with the gifts from earlier visitors from Norway it gives the church a strangely museum-like atmosphere, at once calming and a little disturbing."

关键词：gifts / earlier visitors / Norway / museum-like atmosphere / calming / disturbing

为什么这样写：Holger 的"museum-like"是博物馆般的——教堂像博物馆。"calming and disturbing"是平静又不安——既平静又有点不安。

> **原句 5:** "In his office Jon Erland tells me that he only met my relatives at church services, which are still held twice a day every Sunday, one in Norwegian at nine o'clock, which usually attracts a congregation of about forty or fifty, and the second in English at eleven o'clock at which a congregation of between fifteen and twenty is about all they can expect."

关键词：relatives / church services / twice a day / Sunday / Norwegian / nine o'clock / congregation / forty or fifty / English / eleven o'clock / fifteen and twenty

为什么这样写：Holger 的"congregation"是教区居民——参加礼拜的人。"twice a day"是每天两次——周日两次礼拜。

> **原句 6:** "He tells me that my uncle is buried in the family grave at the Lakewood Cemetery, as I already knew."

关键词：buried / family grave / Lakewood Cemetery

为什么这样写：Holger 的"family grave"是家族墓地——他叔叔葬在那里。

> **原句 7:** "‘What do people say about my cousin, after what happened?’ I ask. ‘You mean his posthumous reputation, his legacy?’ ‘I mean, do people regard him as a hero?’"

关键词：posthumous reputation / legacy / regard / hero

为什么这样写：Holger 的"posthumous reputation"是死后的名声。"legacy"是遗产——他的遗产。

> **原句 8:** "Jon Erland raises an eyebrow, clearly surprised. ‘Why? The whole thing ended in the most appalling tragedy. The best thing one could say about your cousin is that he was a poor misguided soul.’"

关键词：raises an eyebrow / appalling tragedy / poor misguided soul

为什么这样写：牧师的"appalling tragedy"是可怕的悲剧。"misguided soul"是迷途的灵魂。

> **原句 9:** "‘That's one way of looking at it –’ I start to say. ‘No!’ says Jon Erland. ‘It is the truth. And as we all know, there is only one truth.’"

关键词：one way of looking at it / truth / only one truth

为什么这样写：牧师的"only one truth"是只有一个真相——Holger 不同意。

> **原句 10:** "I look at him. ‘Only one truth,’ I echo. And in that same instant, remember why it was I could never have been a priest."

关键词：echo / same instant / never have been a priest

为什么这样写：Holger 的"echo"是重复——他重复牧师的话。"never have been a priest"是永远不会当牧师——他意识到自己不适合当牧师。
