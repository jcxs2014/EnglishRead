# 22. NEWEST ROMANTIC

## 概览

- **出处**：*Ten Bridges I've Burnt: A Memoir in Verse*
- **体裁**：诗歌回忆录，自由诗
- **一句话主旨**：作者承认self-deprecation是"literary vehicle"——"mostly only"——是他从"last ice age"保留的relic；他不愿完全放弃——因为那是他精心培育的rose——"the world instills a certain self-hatred in us and then takes that away too"——为什么我不能拥有任何属于我的东西？我们不再叫它们"disorders"——叫它们"adaptations"——"walking with your head down was based in wanting to protect your little heart"——这些"bad habits"像胶水一样粘着你——在某个"indecipherable point"保护了你——"you held on to them like good-luck charms"——但这是他的"last ice age"——"thawing out"——"my self-esteem is flooding the Earth"——他是"rural Southerner"在加州住了20年——认为天气是完美可接受的话题——人们讨厌她假装相信占星术——"Red Sea at some point did a standing ovation"——"god gave some men nine-inch dicks that actually work properly 100 percent of the time"——"or bank accounts filled by parents"——读者"wildly turned on"——因为作者"bandwidth to love and accept you anonymously and unconditionally"——"you know I will suck your dick in the bathroom sheerly for the camaraderie"——"I am a beautiful man / I am only forty / I could be your new husband / but I'm overqualified for the job"。

## 主题脉络

1. **self-deprecation是literary vehicle**："mostly only"——"relic from last ice age"
2. **"adaptations" not "disorders"**：低头走路=保护心脏+避开人类粪便
3. **"last ice age"**："thawing out"——self-esteem flooding
4. **rural Southerner in California**：天气是话题——占星术——Red Sea standing ovation
5. **"god gave men nine-inch dicks or bank accounts"**
6. **"bandwidth to love anonymously"**：读者wildly turned on
7. **"overqualified for the job"**

## 逐段精读

### 第 1-15 行：self-deprecation是literary vehicle

> **原句 1:** I admit / that self-deprecation / is only a literary vehicle / "mostly only" that is / a relic I kept from my last ice age / I don't dare fully leave behind / because it was yet another rose I cultivated with great care / and it's mine

**中文理解**：我承认——self-deprecation——只是literary vehicle——"mostly only"那是——我从last ice age保留的relic——我不敢完全留下——因为它是我精心培育的another rose——而且它是我的。

**关键词**：self-deprecation / literary vehicle / mostly only / relic / last ice age / rose / cultivated / mine

**为什么这样写**："self-deprecation is only a literary vehicle"——自我贬低是文学工具——"mostly only"——"mostly"修饰"only"——"大部分只是"——这是对"自我贬低"的元评论：不是真实的——是文学工具。"relic from last ice age"——冰川时代遗物——把"自我贬低"比作冰川时代留下的东西——古老+原始+过时。"rose I cultivated with great care"——我精心培育的玫瑰——"rose"=美好的事物——自我贬低是他精心培育的美好事物——"and it's mine"——"它是我的"——这是核心主张：自我贬低是我拥有的少数东西之一。

**诗歌技法**："mostly only"——矛盾修饰——"大部分只是"——制造语言张力。

### 第 16-35 行：adaptations not disorders

> **原句 2:** the world instills a certain self-hatred in us / and then takes that away too / why can't I ever own something that's mine? / We don't call them "disorders" anymore / we call them "adaptations"'cause at some point in your life / walking with your head down was yes, / based in wanting to protect / your little heart, / but also / kept you from stepping blindly / into a pile of human shit someone left on the sidewalk

**中文理解**：世界向我们灌输某种self-hatred——然后也把它带走——为什么我不能拥有任何属于我的东西？我们不再叫它们"disorders"——我们叫它们"adaptations"——因为在生命的某个时刻——低头走路是的——基于想要保护——你的小心脏——但也——让你避免盲目踩进——某人留在人行道上的一堆人类粪便。

**关键词**：instills / self-hatred / takes that away / disorders / adaptations / walking with your head down / protect your little heart / stepping blindly / pile of human shit

**为什么这样写**："instills self-hatred and then takes that away"——灌输自我仇恨又带走——"为什么我不能拥有任何属于我的东西？"——这是对"自我"的哲学追问：如果连self-hatred都被带走——我还有什么？"adaptations not disorders"——DSM（精神疾病诊断手册）的演变——从"障碍"到"适应"——这是对神经多样性运动的引用。"walking with your head down"=保护心脏+避开人类粪便——"human shit"=字面+隐喻——既是真实的人类粪便——也是人类的恶意。

**诗歌技法**：从"disorders"（精神病学）到"human shit"（粗口语）——语域跨越。

### 第 36-60 行：bad habits like good-luck charms

> **原句 3:** These bad habits / that stuck to you like glue / at some indecipherable point in your life / kept you safe / and you held on to them like good-luck charms / but again / this was my last ice age / I'm thawing out / my self-esteem is flooding the Earth

**中文理解**：这些bad habits——像胶水一样粘着你——在生命的某个indecipherable point——保护了你——你像good-luck charms一样抓住它们——但再一次——这是我的last ice age——我正在thawing out——我的self-esteem正在淹没地球。

**关键词**：stuck to you like glue / indecipherable point / kept you safe / good-luck charms / thawing out / self-esteem is flooding

**为什么这样写**："stuck to you like glue"=像胶水一样粘着——"indecipherable point"=无法解读的时刻——"kept you safe"=保护了你——"good-luck charms"=护身符——bad habits=护身符——这是悖论：坏习惯=保护。"thawing out"=融化——冰川融化——"self-esteem is flooding the Earth"=自尊淹没地球——这是对"自尊"的夸张描述：从冰川到洪水——自尊太多=洪水。

**诗歌技法**：从"last ice age"到"thawing out"到"flooding"——冰川→融化→洪水——气候变化的隐喻。

### 第 61-90 行：rural Southerner in California

> **原句 4:** I am coastal, yes, / but regionally speaking / all Americans are assholes / I am a rural Southerner / who has lived in California twenty years / which means I think the weather / is a perfectly acceptable topic of conversation / crucial even / and astrology intellectually / is mass bonding over a mutual love of folklore / people hate me just because I pretend to believe in astrology

**中文理解**：我是coastal是的——但regionally speaking——所有Americans都是assholes——我是一个rural Southerner——在加州住了20年——这意味着我认为天气——是一个perfectly acceptable的谈话话题——crucial even——而且astellectually——是mass bonding over a mutual love of folklore——人们讨厌我只是因为我假装相信占星术。

**关键词**：coastal / regionally speaking / Americans are assholes / rural Southerner / California twenty years / weather / perfectly acceptable / astrology / mass bonding / folklore

**为什么这样写**："all Americans are assholes"——所有美国人都是assholes——这是自嘲+批判——"rural Southerner in California"——南方农村人在加州——20年——仍然认为"weather"是谈话话题——加州人以"天气好"为傲——南方人也谈天气——"astrology intellectually is mass bonding over a mutual love of folklore"——"intellectually"=在知识层面——占星术=共同热爱的民俗——这是对"占星术"的理性化：不是迷信——是"folk bonding"。

**诗歌技法**：从"all Americans are assholes"到"weather"到"astrology"——话题跳跃——制造幽默。

### 第 91-115 行：Red Sea standing ovation

> **原句 5:** I like believing in shit / there were even points in my life where I believed / FOR CERTAIN / that the Red Sea, at some point, did a standing ovation / I even recall it being / a very exciting thing to believe in

**中文理解**：我喜欢相信shit——甚至有points in my life我相信——FOR CERTAIN——红海在某个时刻做了standing ovation——我甚至recall它是——一个非常exciting的事情去相信。

**关键词**：believing in shit / FOR CERTAIN / Red Sea / standing ovation / very exciting thing to believe in

**为什么这样写**："I like believing in shit"——喜欢相信"shit"（无意义的事）——"Red Sea did a standing ovation"——红海起立鼓掌——这是对《圣经》中"红海分开"的荒诞改写——不是"分开"——是"standing ovation"——红海为摩西起立鼓掌——"very exciting thing to believe in"——"去相信"是exciting的——不是"相信"本身——是"去相信"这个行为。

**诗歌技法**：全大写"FOR CERTAIN"——声音的强调——制造荒诞感。

### 第 116-140 行：nine-inch dicks or bank accounts

> **原句 6:** god gave some men nine-inch dicks that actually work properly / 100 percent of the time / or bank accounts filled by parents / that they did not have to pay for in blood

**中文理解**：神给了某些男人nine-inch dicks——actually work properly——100 percent of the time——或者bank accounts filled by parents——他们不需要用血支付。

**关键词**：nine-inch dicks / work properly / 100 percent / bank accounts / parents / pay for in blood

**为什么这样写**："nine-inch dicks that work properly 100 percent of the time"——"actually work properly"——"actually"暗示很多大dick不work properly——幽默。"bank accounts filled by parents"——父母充值的银行账户——"pay for in blood"=用血支付——暗示作者用血汗换来的一切——而有些人从父母那里得到。

**诗歌技法**：从"nine-inch dicks"到"bank accounts"——身体vs资本——两种男性特权。

### 第 141-175 行：bandwidth to love anonymously

> **原句 7:** you are wildly turned on / by the fact / that god gave me the bandwidth / to love and accept you / anonymously / and unconditionally / you are wildly turned on by me / because you know / I will suck your dick in the bathroom / sheerly for the camaraderie / or even because you asked nicely

**中文理解**：你wildly turned on——因为这个事实——神给了我bandwidth——去爱你和接受你——anonymously——和unconditionally——你wildly turned on by me——因为你知道——我会在浴室里suck your dick——sheerly for the camaraderie——或者甚至因为你asked nicely。

**关键词**：wildly turned on / bandwidth / love anonymously / unconditionally / suck your dick / camaraderie / asked nicely

**为什么这样写**："bandwidth to love"——"bandwidth"=带宽——计算机术语——爱的带宽——作者有"无限的爱的带宽"。"love anonymously and unconditionally"——匿名+无条件地爱——这是对"爱的能力"的描述：不需要被认识——不需要回报。"suck your dick in the bathroom sheerly for the camaraderie"——"sheerly"=纯粹地——"camaraderie"=友谊/同志情谊——纯粹为了友谊而口交——不是为了性——是为了友谊——这是对"性"的重新定义：性=友谊的表达。

**诗歌技法**：从"bandwidth"（计算机）到"suck your dick"（粗口语）——两极语域。

### 第 176-195 行：overqualified for the job

> **原句 8:** I am a beautiful man / I am only forty / I could be your new husband / but I'm overqualified for the job

**中文理解**：我是一个beautiful man——我only forty——我可以是你的new husband——但我overqualified for the job。

**关键词**：beautiful man / only forty / new husband / overqualified for the job

**为什么这样写**："I am a beautiful man / I am only forty"——与ch01的"divine normal"呼应——"divine normal"→"beautiful man"——从"神圣的普通人"到"美丽的男人"——身份的微妙转变。"I could be your new husband / but I'm overqualified for the job"——"overqualified"=资历过高——这是对"婚姻"的幽默：我可以结婚——但我太优秀了——这是self-deprecation的巅峰：用"overqualified"来自夸——同时保持自嘲的形式。

**诗歌技法**：与ch01呼应——"divine normal"→"beautiful man"——从神圣到美丽。

## 精读总结

### 1. 本诗核心表达
- **"self-deprecation is only a literary vehicle"**：自我贬低是文学工具——"mostly only"——是冰川时代遗物——是精心培育的玫瑰——"and it's mine"——这是核心主张。
- **"adaptations not disorders"**：从DSM"障碍"到"适应"——"walking with your head down"=保护心脏+避开人类粪便——"bad habits=good-luck charms"——悖论。
- **"bandwidth to love anonymously and unconditionally"**：计算机术语——爱的带宽——"suck your dick sheerly for the camaraderie"——性=友谊的表达。

### 2. 重要诗歌技法
- **"thawing out / self-esteem flooding"**：冰川→融化→洪水——气候变化=自尊——从冰川时代到洪水——自尊太多=洪水。
- **"Red Sea did a standing ovation"**：《圣经》荒诞改写——红海为摩西起立鼓掌——"very exciting thing to believe in"——"去相信"是exciting的。
- **与ch01呼应**："divine normal"→"beautiful man"——身份的微妙转变——从神圣到美丽。

### 3. 写作学习点
- **"mostly only"**：矛盾修饰——"大部分只是"——制造语言张力。
- **"all Americans are assholes"**：自嘲+批判——所有美国人=assholes——这是对"美国例外论"的反驳。
- **"overqualified for the job"**：self-deprecation的巅峰——用"overqualified"来自夸——同时保持自嘲的形式。
