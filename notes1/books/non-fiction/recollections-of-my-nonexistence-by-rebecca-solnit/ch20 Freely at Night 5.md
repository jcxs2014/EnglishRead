# 20. Freely at Night · Chapter 5（自由之夜·第五章）

## 概览

- **出处**：*Recollections of My Nonexistence* (Viking, 2020)，Part V "Freely at Night" 第五章
- **作者**：Rebecca Solnit
- **章节定位**：全书第 20 章；深入"声音"（voice）的探索——从黛安·迪·普拉玛的"宣言"到"你无法写下一行诗而不带宇宙观"
- **字符数**：5618
- **一句话主旨**：以黛安·迪·普拉玛的"你无法写下一行诗而不带宇宙观"开篇，探讨"写作"作为一种"自我创造"——不是"有话要说"，而是"在说话中创造自己"

### 论证脉络

Solnit 以"It formalizes the process everyone goes through, of making the self who will speak"（它正式化了每个人都要经历的过程——创造那个将要说话的自我）开篇，将"写作"定义为一种"自我创造"。核心论点：写作不仅是"表达"，也是"发现"——你不知道自己在想什么，直到你写下它。中间插入对自己早期"声音"的反思——"机智、冷静、尖锐、有趣"的声音，以及这种声音如何限制了她。章末回到"我想要一种可以是简单也可以是复杂的语言"——对"多样性"的渴望。

## 选择性精读

### ①

> **原句 1:** It formalizes the process everyone goes through, of making the self who will speak, of settling on what values and interests and priorities will shape your path and your persona. You have to find out what kind of tone you are going to take, how you pitch your words, whether you're going to be funny or grim or both. Often what emerges is not what you intended; it turns out you're someone else who has other things to say and other ways to say them (what gets called "a voice" is at the outset like some person you don't know very well arriving at the front door with a different focus and tone than you expected).

**中文理解**：它正式化了每个人都要经历的过程——创造那个将要说话的自我，确定哪些价值观和兴趣和优先事项将塑造你的道路和你的形象。你必须找出你要采取什么样的语气，如何调节你的言语，你是否要有趣或严峻或两者兼而有之。经常出现的情况是，出现的不是你想要的；结果你是另一个人，有其他事情要说，有其他方式去说（所谓的"声音"在开始就像一个你不太了解的人出现在前门，带着和你预期不同的焦点和语气）。Solnit 用"what gets called 'a voice' is at the outset like some person you don't know very well arriving at the front door"的比喻，将"声音"定义为一种"陌生人"——不是"你自己的"，而是"外来的"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"It formalizes the process everyone goes through..." + "You have to find out what kind of tone you are going to take..."。"what emerges is not what you intended"为转折句。

**关键词**：**formalizes**（正式化）、**persona**（形象）、**pitch**（调节）、**grim**（严峻的）。"formalizes"是核心词——它暗示一种"正式化"：不是"自然"的，而是"有意"的。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"like some person you don't know very well arriving at the front door"的比喻，将"声音"定义为一种"陌生人"——不是"你自己的"，而是"外来的"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"声音即陌生人"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"声音"不是"拥有"的，而是"发现"的；不是"内在"的，而是"外在"的。

### ②

> **原句 2:** The voice that came out of me when I spoke in social settings and often even to a single friend wore a thousand pounds of armor and was incapable of saying anything direct about emotions, which I was barely feeling or feeling through so many filters I hardly knew what was spinning me around. But the voice: it was the voice I'd grown up around and learned to emulate and then to promulgate, a voice that strove to be clever, cool, sharp, and amused, to shoot arrows with precision and duck the arrows that came back or pretend they hadn't stung.

**中文理解**：我在社交场合甚至经常对单个朋友说话时发出的声音穿着一千磅的盔甲，无法对情感说出任何直接的话，我几乎没有感受到情感，或者通过如此多的过滤器感受它们，以至于我几乎不知道是什么在旋转我。但那声音：那是我从小长大并学会模仿然后传播的声音，一个努力变得机智、冷静、尖锐、有趣的声音，用精度射击箭头并躲避回来的箭头或假装它们没有刺痛。Solnit 用"wore a thousand pounds of armor"的比喻，将"声音"定义为一种"盔甲"——不是"表达"，而是"防御"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"The voice that came out of me when I spoke in social settings and often even to a single friend wore a thousand pounds of armor..."。"But the voice: it was the voice I'd grown up around and learned to emulate..."为转折句。

**关键词**：**armor**（盔甲）、**incapable**（无法的）、**emulate**（模仿）、**promulgate**（传播）、**stung**（刺痛）。"armor"是核心词——它暗示一种"防御"：不是"开放"，而是"防御"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"shoot arrows with precision and duck the arrows that came back or pretend they hadn't stung"的比喻，将"对话"定义为一种"战争"——不是"交流"，而是"战斗"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"声音即盔甲"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"声音"不仅是"表达"，也是"防御"；不仅是"开放"，也是"封闭"。

### ③

> **原句 3:** There was another kind of humor, or rather a ponderous wit, that was convoluted, full of citations and puns and plays on existing phrases, of circling around, far around, what was happening and what you were feeling. **表达方式**：Solnit 用"circling around, far around, what was happening and what you were feeling"的重复，将"机智"定义为一种"回避"——不是"面对"，而是"回避"。

### ④

> **原句 4:** That voice isn't just in your conversations, it's inside your head: you don't say that hurt, or I feel sad; you run angry tirades about why the other party is a terrible person over and over, and you layer on anger to avoid whatever's hurt or frightened underneath, until it's certain that you don't know yourself or your weather, or that it's you who's telling the story that's feeding the fire.

**关键词**：**tirades**（长篇大论）、**layer**（分层）、**weather**（天气/情绪）。"weather"是核心词——它暗示一种"变化"：不是"固定"的，而是"变化"的。

### ⑤

> **原句 5:** But that was just the stories within. The stories I wanted to write and the person who would write them were not yet born. I knew who I admired, but not who I was. ### ⑥

> **原句 6:** I wanted language that could be simple and clear when the subject required it, but sometimes clarity requires complexity. I believe in the irreducible and in invocation and evocation, and I am fond of sentences less like superhighways than winding paths, with the occasional scenic detour or pause to take in the view, since a footpath can traverse steep and twisting terrain that a paved road cannot.

**中文理解**：我想要一种可以是简单和清晰的语言，当主题需要它时，但有时清晰需要复杂性。我相信不可还原性和唤起和唤出，我更喜欢句子不像高速公路而像小径，偶尔有风景绕道或停下来欣赏风景，因为人行道可以穿越陡峭和扭曲的地形，而铺砌的道路不能。Solnit 用"sentences less like superhighways than winding paths"的比喻，将"句子"定义为一种"小径"——不是"高速公路"（直线），而是"小径"（曲折）。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"I wanted language that could be simple and clear when the subject required it, but sometimes clarity requires complexity" + "I believe in the irreducible and in invocation and evocation..."。

**关键词**：**irreducible**（不可还原的）、**invocation**（唤起）、**evocation**（唤出）、**superhighways**（高速公路）、**traverse**（穿越）。"irreducible"是核心词——它暗示一种"不可简化"：不是"简单"的，而是"复杂"的。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"footpath can traverse steep and twisting terrain that a paved road cannot"的对比，将"小径"定义为一种"能力"——不是"限制"，而是"能力"。

### ⑦

> **原句 7:** I wanted English to be an instrument on which many kinds of music could be played. I wanted writing that could be lavish, subtle, evocative, that could describe mists and moods and hopes and not just facts and solid objects. ## 一句话总结
Solnit 通过对自己早期"声音"的反思，探讨了"写作"作为一种"自我创造"——不是"有话要说"，而是"在说话中创造自己"；不是"一个声音"，而是"多种音乐"。
