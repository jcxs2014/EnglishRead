# 17. Freely at Night · Chapter 2（自由之夜·第二章）

## 概览

- **出处**：*Recollections of My Nonexistence* (Viking, 2020)，Part V "Freely at Night" 第二章
- **作者**：Rebecca Solnit
- **章节定位**：全书第 17 章；从"夜"转向"阅读"——书籍作为"鸟"和"砖块"，以及菲洛墨拉神话
- **字符数**：5013
- **一句话主旨**：以"至少书籍属于我"开篇，探讨阅读作为一种"变形"——从"鸟"到"砖块"，从"菲洛墨拉"到"夜莺"，书籍是避难所也是武器

### 论证脉络

Solnit 以"At least books belonged to me"（至少书籍属于我）开篇，将"书籍"定义为一种"所有权"——不是"拥有"物品，而是"拥有"世界。核心论点：书籍不仅是"逃避"，也是"变形"——它们让你"变成"鸟、变成风、变成任何人。中间插入菲洛墨拉神话（被强奸后变成夜莺），将"变形"定义为一种"幸存"——不是"避免"暴力，而是"通过变形来幸存"。章末回到"书是鸟也是砖块"——书籍既是"自由的象征"（鸟），也是"防御的工具"（砖块）。

## 选择性精读

### ①

> **原句 1:** At least books belonged to me. Closed, a book is a rectangle, thin as a letter or thick and solid like a box or a brick. Open, it is two arcs of paper that, seen from the top or bottom when the book is wide open, look like the wide V of birds in flight. I think about that and then about women who turn into birds and then about Philomela, who in the Greek myth is turned into a nightingale after she is raped, as her brother-in-law pursues her to murder her.

**中文理解**：至少书籍属于我。合上，书是一个矩形，薄如信件或厚而坚实如盒子或砖块。打开，它是两张纸的弧线，从顶部或底部看，当书完全打开时，看起来像飞行中鸟类的宽V形。我想起那些，然后想起变成鸟的女人，然后想起菲洛墨拉，在希腊神话中，她在被强奸后被变成夜莺，因为她的姐夫追捕她要谋杀她。Solnit 用"books belonged to me"（书籍属于我）将"阅读"定义为一种"所有权"——不是"拥有"物品，而是"拥有"世界。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"At least books belonged to me" + "Closed, a book is a rectangle..." + "Open, it is two arcs of paper..."。"I think about that and then about women who turn into birds..."为反思句。

**关键词**：**rectangle**（矩形）、**arcs**（弧线）、**nightingale**（夜莺）。"nightingale"是核心词——它连接了"鸟"和"变形"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"thin as a letter or thick and solid like a box or a brick"的对比，将"书"定义为一种"多义"——不仅是"薄的"，也是"厚的"；不仅是"轻的"，也是"重的"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"书籍即所有权"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——书籍不仅是"物品"，也是"世界"；不仅是"逃避"，也是"进入"。

### ②

> **原句 2:** The word nightingale is an old one in the English language, cobbled together from night and singer. I wonder if Keats had Philomela in mind when he wrote his "Ode to a Nightingale," or if I did when I dreamed of him and flying. In it the poet imagines flying himself "on the viewless wings of poesy," into a dark forest—"for here there is no light . . . and tender is the night," a line I was happy to recognize again in F. Scott Fitzgerald's novel, which is also about incest and rape and their unspeakability and the way the harm spreads outward.

**中文理解**：夜莺这个词在英语中很古老，由"夜"和"歌者"拼凑而成。我想知道济慈写《夜莺颂》时是否想到了菲洛墨拉，或者我是否在我梦见他和飞行时想到了。在诗中，诗人想象自己"乘着诗的无形翅膀"飞进一片黑暗的森林——"因为这里没有光……夜是温柔的"，我很高兴在F.司各特·菲茨拉德的小说中再次认出那句诗，那也是关于乱伦和强奸以及它们的不可言说性以及伤害向外传播的方式。Solnit 用"cobbled together from night and singer"（由"夜"和"歌者"拼凑而成）将"词源"定义为一种"历史"——不是"词源学"，而是"故事"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"The word nightingale is an old one..." + "I wonder if Keats had Philomela in mind..."。

**关键词**：**cobbled**（拼凑）、**viewless**（无形的）、**incest**（乱伦）。"cobbled"是核心词——它暗示一种"拼凑"：不是"创造"，而是"拼凑"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"tender is the night"的引用，将"夜"定义为一种"温柔"——不是"危险"，而是"温柔"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"夜与温柔"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"夜"不仅是"危险"，也是"温柔"；不仅是"恐惧"，也是"安慰"。

### ③

> **原句 3:** I had read Ovid's The Metamorphoses, with the story of Philomela and all those other goddesses, nymphs, and mortals being ravaged, much earlier. In the myths, women keep turning into other things, because being a woman is too difficult, too dangerous. Daphne is turned into a laurel as she flees Apollo; I'd known that even before I'd memorized Andrew Marvell's "The Garden," including these lines: The gods, that mortal beauty chase, Still in a tree did end their race.

**中文理解**：我很早就读过奥维德的《变形记》，其中有菲洛墨拉的故事以及其他所有被蹂躏的女神、仙女和凡人。在神话中，女人不断变成其他东西，因为做女人太困难、太危险。达芙妮在逃离阿波罗时被变成月桂树；甚至在我记住安德鲁·马韦尔的《花园》之前，我就知道了这一点，包括这些诗句：诸神追逐凡人的美，最终在一棵树中结束了他们的追逐。Solnit 用"women keep turning into other things, because being a woman is too difficult, too dangerous"将"变形"定义为一种"幸存"——不是"避免"暴力，而是"通过变形来幸存"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"I had read Ovid's The Metamorphoses..." + "In the myths, women keep turning into other things..."。

**关键词**：**ravaged**（被蹂躏）、**laurel**（月桂树）。"ravaged"是核心词——它暗示一种"暴力"：不是"伤害"，而是"蹂躏"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"Still in a tree did end their race"的引用，将"变形"定义为一种"终结"——不是"开始"，而是"结束"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"变形即幸存"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"变形"不仅是"改变"，也是"幸存"；不仅是"逃避"，也是"适应"。

### ④

> **原句 4:** I'd learned it in the same introductory class where we read Yeats's "Leda and the Swan," which I can see now is creepily specific about the details of a god in the form of a bird raping a woman. "How can those terrified vague fingers push / The feathered glory from her loosening thighs?" That so many Greek myths are about rape and women trying to escape it never came up. It's not that I think we were too fragile to be exposed to this stuff that was everywhere, in pop songs as well as sonnets and classics, just that the fact that the reality, the ubiquity, and the impact of rape were weirdly unspeakable, in art and in life. Our tongues had been cut out too.

**中文理解**：我在同一门入门课上学到了它，我们在那里读了叶芝的《丽达与天鹅》，我现在可以看出，它对于一个鸟形之神强奸女人的细节描写令人毛骨悚然。"那些惊恐的、模糊的手指怎么能够/把她松弛的大腿上的羽毛荣耀推开？"如此多的希腊神话都是关于强奸和女人试图逃脱它，这一点从未被提及。我并不是认为我们太脆弱了，无法接触这种无处不在的东西，在流行歌曲以及十四行诗和经典作品中都有，只是强奸的现实、普遍性和影响这一事实奇怪地不可言说，在艺术和生活中都是如此。我们的舌头也被割掉了。Solnit 用"Our tongues had been cut out too"将"沉默"定义为一种"暴力"——不是"没有声音"，而是"被割掉舌头"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"I'd learned it in the same introductory class..." + "That so many Greek myths are about rape and women trying to escape it never came up"。"Our tongues had been cut out too"为独立短句。

**关键词**：**creepily**（令人毛骨悚然地）、**ubiquity**（普遍性）、**unspeakable**（不可言说的）。"unspeakable"是核心词——它暗示一种"越界"：不是"不能说"，而是"不可说"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"Our tongues had been cut out too"的比喻，将"沉默"定义为一种"暴力"——不是"没有声音"，而是"被割掉舌头"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"沉默与暴力"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"沉默"不仅是"没有声音"，也是"被割掉舌头"；不仅是"个人"的，也是"文化"的。

### ⑤

> **原句 5:** Ovid tells us that tongueless Philomela weaves the story of her violation into a tapestry and by signs conveys that the tapestry should be delivered to her sister, the wife of her rapist. When the truth is unspeakable you say it indirectly; when your speech is taken away other things speak; sometimes the body itself speaks with the tics, eruptions, numbness, paralysis that are cyphers for what happened. In the myth, there's more gore, and then the sisters are both turned into birds—and in some versions it's the sister who becomes the night singer, the nightingale.

**中文理解**：奥维德告诉我们，没有舌头的菲洛墨拉将她的侵犯故事编织成挂毯，并通过手势传达挂毯应该被送到她姐姐——她强奸者的妻子——那里。当真相不可言说时，你说它是间接的；当你的话语被夺走时，其他东西会说话；有时身体本身会通过抽搐、爆发、麻木、瘫痪来说话，这些是所发生事情的密码。在神话中，还有更多血腥，然后两姐妹都变成了鸟——在一些版本中，是姐姐变成了夜歌手，夜莺。Solnit 用"When the truth is unspeakable you say it indirectly"将"间接表达"定义为一种"幸存策略"——不是"直接说"，而是"通过其他方式说"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"Ovid tells us that tongueless Philomela weaves the story..." + "When the truth is unspeakable you say it indirectly..."。

**关键词**：**violation**（侵犯）、**tapestry**（挂毯）、**cyphers**（密码）、**gore**（血腥）。"cyphers"是核心词——它暗示一种"编码"：不是"直接表达"，而是"通过密码表达"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"the body itself speaks with the tics, eruptions, numbness, paralysis"将"身体"定义为一种"语言"——不是"嘴巴"，而是"身体"；不是"说话"，而是"表达"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"身体即语言"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"语言"不仅是"嘴巴"，也是"身体"；不仅是"说话"，也是"表达"。

### ⑥

> **原句 6:** Keats's nightingale is not the mortal victim but the transcendent figure, impervious to human suffering: "Though wast not born for death, immortal bird," he apostrophizes her. "No hungry generations tread thee down," and I memorized those lines when I first met the poem, or maybe they just stuck, as so many lines I read then have, as though I was laying down a foundation of those bricks. He thinks of her song heard long ago, of the words that last innumerable lifetimes. She is poetry itself or something that poetry—that the durable wings of words and the ether of narrative they generate—brings us to, a sanctuary and a place out of time. A refuge. A place beyond bodies and the flesh. In Philomela's case, the mutation into a nightingale doesn't come in time to save her from rape, mutilation, silencing, and imprisonment, but it does save her from murder, if being turned into something other than yourself is survival.

**中文理解**：济慈的夜莺不是凡人受害者，而是超越的形象，对人类痛苦无动于衷："虽然你不是为死而生的，不朽的鸟，"他向她呼喊。"没有饥饿的世代把你践踏，"我在第一次读到这首诗时就记住了那些诗句，或者它们只是留住了，就像我当时读的许多诗句一样，仿佛我正在用那些砖块打下基础。他想起很久以前听到的她的歌声，想起那些持续无数世代的话语。她是诗本身，或者是诗——那经久不衰的文字翅膀和它们产生的叙事以太——带给我们的东西，一个圣所和一个超越时间的地方。一个避难所。一个超越肉体和血肉的地方。在菲洛墨拉的案例中，变成夜莺的变形来不及把她从强奸、残害、沉默和监禁中拯救出来，但它确实把她从谋杀中拯救了出来，如果变成不同于自己的东西就是幸存的话。Solnit 用"if being turned into something other than yourself is survival"将"变形"定义为一种"幸存"——不是"避免"暴力，而是"通过变形来幸存"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"Keats's nightingale is not the mortal victim but the transcendent figure..." + "He thinks of her song heard long ago..."。"In Philomela's case, the mutation into a nightingale doesn't come in time..."为转折句。

**关键词**：**transcendent**（超越的）、**impervious**（无动于衷的）、**apostrophizes**（呼喊）、**ether**（以太）、**sanctuary**（圣所）。"sanctuary"是核心词——它暗示一种"神圣"：不是"普通的地方"，而是"圣所"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"the durable wings of words and the ether of narrative they generate"将"诗"定义为一种"超越"——不是"文字"，而是"翅膀"；不是"表达"，而是"飞翔"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"诗与超越"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"诗"不仅是"文字"，也是"翅膀"；不仅是"表达"，也是"超越"。

### ⑦

> **原句 7:** A book: a bird that is also a brick. I arranged my battered paperback library on stacks of plastic milk crates I pilfered one at a time from in front of the liquor stores when they were closed, and then returned them to where they'd come from when I managed to acquire some wooden bookshelves. My birds flocked, and eventually a long row of shelves narrowed the hallway and half filled the main room and piled into unstable pillars on my desk and other surfaces.

**中文理解**：一本书：一只鸟，也是一块砖。我把我的破损平装书图书馆排列在一堆塑料牛奶箱上，这些牛奶箱是我在酒类商店关门时一次一个从前面偷来的，然后当我设法获得一些木制书架时，我把它们送回了原来的地方。我的鸟群聚集，最终一排长长的走廊变窄了，半个主房间被填满了，并在我的书桌和其他表面上堆成了不稳定的柱子。Solnit 用"A book: a bird that is also a brick"将"书"定义为一种"多义"——不仅是"鸟"（自由），也是"砖块"（防御）。

**句子结构**：短句"A book: a bird that is also a brick"开场，后续为具体描述。

**关键词**：**battered**（破损的）、**pilfered**（偷窃）、**flocked**（聚集）。"battered"是核心词——它暗示一种"磨损"：不是"新的"，而是"旧的"；不是"完美的"，而是"破损的"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"My birds flocked"的拟人，将"书"定义为一种"鸟群"——不是"物品"，而是"生命"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"书籍即多义"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"书"不仅是"物品"，也是"世界"；不仅是"逃避"，也是"进入"。

### ⑧

> **原句 8:** You furnish your mind with readings in somewhat the way you furnish a house with books, or rather the physical books enter your memory and become part of the equipment of your imagination. I was building up a body of literature, points of reference for a map of the world, a set of tools to understand that world and myself in it by reading. Mostly I wandered in books on my own, or read what was given me, an indiscriminate omnivore then, as the young often are with people as well: not sure what their criteria are, what feeds them and what discourages them.

**中文理解**：你用阅读装点你的思想，在某种程度上就像你用书籍装点一所房子，或者更确切地说，物理书籍进入你的记忆，成为你想象力的装备的一部分。我正在建立一个文学体系，作为世界地图的参考点，一套通过阅读来理解那个世界和我自己在世界中的工具。我大多独自在书中漫游，或者阅读给我的东西，当时是一个不加选择的杂食者，就像年轻人对人们经常做的那样：不确定他们的标准是什么，什么滋养他们，什么阻止他们。Solnit 用"equipment of your imagination"（想象力的装备）将"阅读"定义为一种"装备"——不是"娱乐"，而是"工具"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"You furnish your mind with readings..." + "I was building up a body of literature..."。

**关键词**：**furnish**（装点）、**equipment**（装备）、**omnivore**（杂食者）。"equipment"是核心词——它暗示一种"工具"：不是"装饰"，而是"装备"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"indiscriminate omnivore"（不加选择的杂食者）将"阅读"定义为一种"贪婪"——不是"选择"，而是"吞食"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"阅读即装备"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"阅读"不仅是"娱乐"，也是"装备"；不仅是"消费"，也是"积累"。

### ⑨

> **原句 9:** I loved the physical objects that are books too and still do. The codex, the box that is a bird, the door into a world, still seems magical to me, and I still walk into a bookstore or a library convinced that I might be on the threshold that will open up onto what I most need or desire, and sometimes that doorway appears. When it does, there are epiphanies and raptures in seeing the world in new ways, in finding patterns previously unsuspected, in being handed unimagined equipment to address what arises, in the beauty and power of words.

**中文理解**：我也爱书籍的物理对象，现在仍然爱。那本法典，那只鸟的盒子，那扇通往世界的门，对我来说仍然很神奇，我仍然走进书店或图书馆，确信我可能站在那个将打开我最需要或渴望的东西的门槛上，有时那扇门会出现。当它出现时，有顿悟和狂喜，以新的方式看到世界，发现以前未曾怀疑的模式，被交给我想象不到的设备来应对出现的事情，在文字的美和力量中。Solnit 用"the door into a world"（通往世界的门）将"书"定义为一种"入口"——不是"物品"，而是"通道"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"I loved the physical objects that are books too and still do..." + "The codex, the box that is a bird, the door into a world, still seems magical to me..."。

**关键词**：**codex**（法典）、**epiphanies**（顿悟）、**raptures**（狂喜）。"codex"是核心词——它暗示一种"古老"：不是"现代"，而是"古代"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"epiphanies and raptures"的并列，将"阅读"定义为一种"神圣"——不是"日常"，而是"超越"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"书籍即神圣"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"书籍"不仅是"物品"，也是"神圣"；不仅是"消费"，也是"体验"。

### ⑩

> **原句 10:** The sheer pleasure of meeting new voices and ideas and possibilities, having the world become more coherent in some subtle or enormous way, extending or filling in your map of the universe, is not nearly celebrated enough, nor is the beauty in finding pattern and meaning. But these awakenings recur, and every time they do there's joy.

**中文理解**：遇见新的声音和想法和可能性的纯粹快乐，让宇宙在一些微妙或巨大的方面变得更加连贯，扩展或填补你的宇宙地图，这几乎不值得庆祝，发现模式和意义中的美也不值得庆祝。但这些觉醒会反复出现，每次出现时都有喜悦。Solnit 用"not nearly celebrated enough"（几乎不值得庆祝）将"阅读的快乐"定义为一种"被低估"——不是"没有价值"，而是"价值被低估"。

**句子结构**：长复合句，"The sheer pleasure of meeting new voices..." + "But these awakenings recur..."。

**关键词**：**sheer**（纯粹的）、**coherent**（连贯的）、**awakenings**（觉醒）。"awakenings"是核心词——它暗示一种"觉醒"：不是"学习"，而是"觉醒"。

**表达方式**：Solnit 用"extending or filling in your map of the universe"将"阅读"定义为一种"地图绘制"——不是"旅行"，而是"绘制"。

**为什么这样写**：这段话是全书"阅读即觉醒"主题的缩影。Solnit 后来多次回到这个主题——"阅读"不仅是"学习"，也是"觉醒"；不仅是"积累"，也是"发现"。

## 一句话总结

Solnit 通过菲洛墨拉神话和书籍的物理性，探讨了阅读作为一种"变形"——书籍是"鸟"也是"砖块"，是自由的象征也是防御的工具；而"变形"不是避免暴力，而是通过变形来幸存。
