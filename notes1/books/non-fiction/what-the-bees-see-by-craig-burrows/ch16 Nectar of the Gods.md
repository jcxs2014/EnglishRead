# 16. Nectar of the Gods（花蜜之神）

## 概览

- **出处**：*What the Bees See: A Honeybee's Eye View of the World* by Craig P. Burrows
- **章节定位**：Part Two "The Cloak of Immunity" 第一章
- **作者**：Craig P. Burrows / 含多位专家（James Sainsbury / Rhys Casley / Michelle Taylor / Oleksiy Losyev）
- **字符数**：10,219
- **一句话主旨**：追溯人类与蜜蜂关系的 13 万年历史——从旧石器时代的岩画到古埃及墓葬中的蜂蜜，从养蜂技术的演变（Langstroth 活框蜂箱）到现代工业化授粉；介绍蜂胶的采集与功效，以及养蜂人如何通过"四件事"（食物/空间/疾病/管理）维护蜂群健康。

## 论证结构

- **核心论点**：人类与蜜蜂的关系是"互惠共生"的典范——人类为蜜蜂提供"家"和"保护"，蜜蜂为人类提供"产品"和"授粉服务"；现代养蜂已从"业余爱好"演变为"工业化授粉产业"，但蜜蜂的健康仍取决于人类的"管理质量"。
- **证据链**：

| 证据 | 类型 | 支撑什么 |
|------|------|----------|
| 旧石器时代岩画中有蜜蜂形象 | 考古证据 | 人类-蜜蜂关系的古老性 |
| 古埃及墓葬中发现的蜂蜜仍可食用 | 考古证据 | 蜂蜜的持久性 |
| 公元前 9-10 世纪 BC 的蜂巢遗迹 | 考古证据 | 养蜂的历史悠久 |
| Langstroth 1850 年代发明活框蜂箱 | 历史事实 | 现代养蜂的诞生 |
| 2014 年德国研究证明蜜蜂授粉提高作物品质 | 研究数据 | 商业化授粉的价值 |
| 蜂胶的采集方式与功效 | 专家解释 | 蜂胶的医学价值 |
| 养蜂"四件事"（食物/空间/疾病/管理） | 专家建议 | 养蜂的最佳实践 |

- **论证脉络**：引入（蜜蜂 130 百万年历史 vs 人类 25,000 年关系）→ 考古证据（岩画/墓葬/蜂巢）→ 养蜂技术演变（从土缸到活框蜂箱）→ 商业化授粉兴起 → 蜂胶的采集与功效 → 养蜂人的日常（"四件事"）→ 蜜蜂对人类的"双重价值"（产品+授粉）
- **可质疑处**：
  1. "130 million years"——蜜蜂科（Apidae）的历史约 130 百万年，但西方蜜蜂（Apis mellifera）是后来才出现的；表述可能混淆了"蜜蜂"和"西方蜜蜂"。
  2. "tomb honey was still good to eat"——蜂蜜的持久性常被夸大；实际上，密封容器中的蜂蜜可长期保存，但"仍可食用"不等于"有营养"。
  3. "FDA declared honeybees' work as crop pollinators to be worth between ten and twenty times the total value of honey and beeswax"——此数据可能高估了蜜蜂的经济价值，且计算方法有争议。

## 选择性精读

① "Honeybees have been going about their buzziness on earth for more than 130 million years. Once humans made their own debut, they caught on to the bounty honeybees bring into our lives, with representations in rock art in Africa, Australia, India and Spain showing that people in the Upper Palaeolithic period endured the tricky task of harvesting heavenly honey from wild bee hives."

**中文理解**：蜜蜂在地球上"嗡嗡忙"了超过 1.3 亿年。一旦人类登场，他们就开始 catch on to（理解/认识到）蜜蜂带给生活的 bounty（恩赐），非洲、澳大利亚、印度和西班牙的岩画表明，旧石器时代的人类 endured the tricky task of harvesting heavenly honey from wild bee hives（忍受从野生蜂巢采集天堂般蜂蜜的棘手任务）。

**句子结构**：复合句。分1 "Honeybees have been going about their buzziness on earth for more than 130 million years" + 分2 "Once humans made their own debut, they caught on to the bounty honeybees bring into our lives" + 分3 "with representations in rock art in Africa, Australia, India and Spain showing that people in the Upper Palaeolithic period endured the tricky task of harvesting heavenly honey from wild bee hives"。

**关键词**：going about their buzziness（忙忙碌碌/嗡嗡叫）、bounty（恩赐/丰富）、representations（图像/表现）、tricky task（棘手的任务）、heavenly honey（天堂般的蜂蜜）

**表达方式**：用"heavenly honey"（天堂般的蜂蜜）——将蜂蜜神圣化，暗示人类对蜂蜜的"崇拜"由来已久。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"史诗"的句子——用 1.3 亿年 vs 25,000 年的时间对比，制造"渺小人类 vs 古老蜜蜂"的张力。

② "That was more than 25,000 years ago and humans have had a relationship to bees and uses for bees products ever since."

**中文理解**：那是 25,000 多年前的事，从那时起人类就与蜜蜂建立了关系并利用蜜蜂产品。

**句子结构**：简单句。主句 "That was more than 25,000 years ago and humans have had a relationship to bees and uses for bees products ever since"。

**关键词**：ever since（从那以后一直）

**表达方式**：用"ever since"（从那以后一直）——暗示这段关系的"持久性"和"稳定性"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"承上启下"的句子——从"25,000 年前"的考古证据，过渡到"现在"的持续关系。

---

③ "Honey has been found in ancient tombs in Egypt and Georgia, and even in the oldest of those discoveries, from a 3,000-year-old tomb in Egypt, that tomb honey was still good to eat due to its high sugar and low water content."

**中文理解**：在埃及和格鲁吉亚的古墓中发现了蜂蜜，甚至在这些发现中最古老的——来自埃及一座 3000 年古墓的蜂蜜——由于高糖分和低水分含量，仍然可以食用。

**句子结构**：复合句。主句 "Honey has been found in ancient tombs in Egypt and Georgia" + 补充 "even in the oldest of those discoveries, from a 3,000-year-old tomb in Egypt, that tomb honey was still good to eat due to its high sugar and low water content"。

**关键词**：ancient tombs（古墓）、still good to eat（仍然可以食用）、high sugar and low water content（高糖低水分）

**表达方式**：用"still good to eat"（仍然可以食用）——将抽象的"保存能力"转化为具体的"可食用性"，读者容易理解。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"神奇"的句子——读者被"3000 年蜂蜜仍可食用"的事实吸引，激发对蜂蜜"永恒性"的好奇。

④ "In 2007, archaeological digs in the Jericho area uncovered what is thought to be the first evidence of beehive colonies dating back to the ninth and tenth centuries BC. Unfired clay cylinders, which would have served as hives, contained the remnants of honeybees, pupae and larvae."

**中文理解**：2007 年，在杰里科地区的考古发掘发现了被认为是公元前 9 世纪和 10 世纪最早的蜂巢群落证据。未烧制的陶土圆柱体（本应用作蜂巢）包含蜜蜂、蛹和幼虫的残余物。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "In 2007, archaeological digs in the Jericho area uncovered what is thought to be the first evidence of beehive colonies dating back to the ninth and tenth centuries BC" + 分2 "Unfired clay cylinders, which would have served as hives, contained the remnants of honeybees, pupae and larvae"。

**关键词**：archaeological digs（考古发掘）、unfired clay cylinders（未烧制的陶土圆柱体）、remnants（残余物）

**表达方式**：用"unfired clay cylinders"（未烧制的陶土圆柱体）——具体描述古代蜂巢的材质，读者能"看见"3000 年前的养蜂场景。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"考古"的句子——读者开始理解养蜂不只是"现代产业"，而是"3000 年的传统"。

---

⑤ "Other forms of man-made hives included logs used by Egyptians to mimic wild bee hives, and circular hives made of straw. Swiss entomologist François Huber invented the leaf or folio hive, in which each comb has glass sides to facilitate observation and the frames could be opened like a book."

**中文理解**：其他人造蜂巢的形式包括埃及人用来模仿野生蜂巢的原木蜂巢，以及稻草制成的圆形蜂巢。瑞士昆虫学家弗朗索瓦·于贝尔发明了叶片式蜂巢，其中每个巢脾都有玻璃侧面便于观察，框架可以像书一样打开。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Other forms of man-made hives included logs used by Egyptians to mimic wild bee hives, and circular hives made of straw" + 分2 "Swiss entomologist François Huber invented the leaf or folio hive, in which each comb has glass sides to facilitate observation and the frames could be opened like a book"。

**关键词**：mimic（模仿）、entomologist（昆虫学家）、facilitate observation（便于观察）

**表达方式**：用"opened like a book"（像书一样打开）——将蜂巢框架比作"书页"，暗示科学观察的"阅读性"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"技术史"的句子——读者开始理解养蜂技术的"进化"：从模仿自然到科学设计。

---

⑥ "But it wasn't until the mid-nineteenth century that hives evolved into the forms we know today (see pages 54—55), and we have apiarist Lorenzo L. Langstroth to thank for that evolution, which allowed beekeeping at a larger scale to be done more cost-effectively."

**中文理解**：但直到 19 世纪中叶，蜂巢才演变成我们今天知道的形式（见第 54-55 页），我们要感谢养蜂人洛伦佐·L·朗斯特罗什的这一进化，它使大规模养蜂更具成本效益。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "it wasn't until the mid-nineteenth century that hives evolved into the forms we know today" + 分2 "we have apiarist Lorenzo L. Langstroth to thank for that evolution, which allowed beekeeping at a larger scale to be done more cost-effectively"。

**关键词**：evolved into（演变成）、apiarist（养蜂人）、larger scale（更大规模）、cost-effectively（成本效益地）

**表达方式**：用"we have... to thank for"（我们要感谢...）——将朗斯特罗什描述为"现代养蜂之父"，制造感恩情绪。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"现代养蜂起点"——读者开始理解今天的养蜂方式源于 19 世纪的一项发明。

---

⑦ "Bees have always been prodigious pollinators (see page 64) and this proved seriously advantageous for humans once they started to harness bees' pollination potential in industrial farming."

**中文理解**：蜜蜂一直是 prodigious（惊人的）传粉者（见第 64 页），一旦人类开始在工业化农业中 harness（利用）蜜蜂的授粉潜力，这就被证明是 seriously advantageous（非常有利的）。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Bees have always been prodigious pollinators (see page 64)" + 分2 "this proved seriously advantageous for humans once they started to harness bees' pollination potential in industrial farming"。

**关键词**：prodigious（惊人的/巨大的）、harness（利用/驾驭）、pollination potential（授粉潜力）

**表达方式**：用"prodigious"（惊人的）形容蜜蜂的传粉能力——暗示蜜蜂的"生产力"远超人类预期。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"经济"的句子——读者开始理解蜜蜂不只是"昆虫"，而是"生产力"。

---

⑧ "Bee pollination increases crop yields, and a study in 2014 by the Agroecology Group at the University of Göttingen in Germany found that, when compared to wind pollination and self-pollination, bee pollination improves crop quality, shelf life and commercial value. In the study, bee-pollinated strawberry plants were found to produce heavier, redder, firmer strawberries, which meant they achieved higher commercial grades and had a longer shelf life."

**中文理解**：蜜蜂授粉提高作物产量，德国哥廷根大学农业生态学组 2014 年的一项研究发现，与风媒授粉和自我授粉相比，蜜蜂授粉提高了作物品质、保质期和商业价值。在该研究中，发现蜜蜂授粉的草莓植株产生更重、更红、更坚固的草莓，这意味着它们达到了更高的商业等级，保质期更长。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Bee pollination increases crop yields, and a study in 2014 by the Agroecology Group at the University of Göttingen in Germany found that, when compared to wind pollination and self-pollination, bee pollination improves crop quality, shelf life and commercial value" + 分2 "In the study, bee-pollinated strawberry plants were found to produce heavier, redder, firmer strawberries, which meant they achieved higher commercial grades and had a longer shelf life"。

**关键词**：crop yields（作物产量）、shelf life（保质期）、commercial value（商业价值）、commercial grades（商业等级）

**表达方式**：用"heavier, redder, firmer"（更重、更红、更坚固）——三个形容词叠加，让读者"看见"蜜蜂授粉草莓的"优秀品质"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"品质"的句子——读者开始理解蜜蜂不只是"数量"（产量），更是"质量"（品质）。

---

⑨ "However, it wasn't until the 1980s that honeybees were more formally used as a mechanism in industrial agriculture, where honeybee hives are shipped thousands of miles around crops to perform their pollination services. In the US, a 2018 study by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) declared honeybees' work as crop pollinators to be significantly more valuable than the products they produce — worth between ten and twenty times the total value of honey and beeswax. According to the FDA, bee pollination enhances crop value by approximately US$15 billion."

**中文理解**：然而，直到 1980 年代，蜜蜂才被更正式地用作工业农业中的一种机制——蜜蜂蜂巢被运送到数千英里外的作物上执行授粉服务。在美国，食品药品监督管理局（FDA）2018 年的一项研究宣布，蜜蜂作为作物传粉者的工作比它们生产的产品更有价值——价值是蜂蜜和蜂蜡总价值的 10 到 20 倍。根据 FDA 的数据，蜜蜂授粉使作物价值增加了约 150 亿美元。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "it wasn't until the 1980s that honeybees were more formally used as a mechanism in industrial agriculture, where honeybee hives are shipped thousands of miles around crops to perform their pollination services" + 分2 "In the US, a 2018 study by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) declared honeybees' work as crop pollinators to be significantly more valuable than the products they produce — worth between ten and twenty times the total value of honey and beeswax" + 分3 "According to the FDA, bee pollination enhances crop value by approximately US$15 billion"。

**关键词**：mechanism（机制）、shipped thousands of miles（运输数千英里）、significantly more valuable（显著更有价值）、enhances crop value（提升作物价值）

**表达方式**：用"shipped thousands of miles"（运输数千英里）——暗示商业化授粉的"工业化"特征，蜜蜂成为"流动劳动力"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"资本主义"的句子——读者开始理解蜜蜂授粉不只是"生态服务"，还是"价值数十亿美元的产业"。

---

⑩ "Propolis is a tree resin that bees collect from different species of trees and use to block up their hives and keep them healthy. When we extract propolis from hives, the bees don't like it — they've put in a lot of hard work to keep their hive healthy, block up gaps and keep themselves warm."

**中文理解**：蜂胶是蜜蜂从不同树种中采集的树脂，用来堵塞蜂巢并保持健康。当我们从蜂巢中提取蜂胶时，蜜蜂不喜欢——它们为了保持蜂巢健康、堵住缝隙和保暖付出了很多辛苦工作。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Propolis is a tree resin that bees collect from different species of trees and use to block up their hives and keep them healthy" + 分2 "When we extract propolis from hives, the bees don't like it — they've put in a lot of hard work to keep their hive healthy, block up gaps and keep themselves warm"。

**关键词**：extract（提取）、block up（堵塞）

**表达方式**：用"bees don't like it"（蜜蜂不喜欢）——拟人化表达，暗示人类"偷走"蜜蜂的"劳动成果"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"共情"的句子——读者开始理解蜂胶不只是"产品"，还是蜜蜂的"劳动结晶"。

## 一句话总结

**从旧石器时代的岩画到现代工业化授粉，人类与蜜蜂的关系已延续 25,000 年；蜜蜂不仅是"产品提供者"，更是"作物价值的放大器"——据 FDA 估算，蜜蜂授粉使作物价值增加约 150 亿美元，是其产品价值的 10-20 倍。**
