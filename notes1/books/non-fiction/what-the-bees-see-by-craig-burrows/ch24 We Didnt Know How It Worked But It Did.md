# 24. We Didn't Know How It Worked But It Did（我们不知道它如何有效，但它确实有效）

## 概览

- **出处**：*What the Bees See: A Honeybee's Eye View of the World* by Craig P. Burrows
- **章节定位**：Part Three "The Pursuit of Immortality" 第四章
- **作者**：Craig P. Burrows / 含多位专家（Jonathan Cox / Peter Molan / James Sainsbury）
- **字符数**：9,876
- **一句话主旨**：追溯麦卢卡蜂蜜"从廉价品到全球热门"的历史——1990 年代 Waikato 大学 Peter Molan 教授发现其独特的"非过氧化物活性"，1997 年提出 UMF 标准；探讨"假蜂蜜"问题和 MGO 作为真实性标记的作用；介绍麦卢卡蜂蜜的抗炎特性（THP-1 细胞研究）。

## 选择性精读

① "There are more than 300 types of honey produced worldwide today, boasting an array of health benefits as they jostle for attention. As humans, we've become somewhat obsessed with this liquid gold — we eat it; we slather products with it over our faces; we use it to store our food."

**中文理解**：今天全球生产超过 300 种蜂蜜，各自吹嘘健康益处以争夺注意力。作为人类，我们已经变得 somewhat obsessed（有些痴迷）于这种液态黄金——我们吃它；我们把它涂抹在脸上；我们用它储存食物。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "There are more than 300 types of honey produced worldwide today, boasting an array of health benefits as they jostle for attention" + 分2 "As humans, we've become somewhat obsessed with this liquid gold — we eat it; we slather products with it over our faces; we use it to store our food"。

**关键词**：boasting（吹嘘）、jostle for attention（争夺注意力）、somewhat obsessed（有些痴迷）

**表达方式**：用"liquid gold"（液态黄金）——再次使用这个比喻，暗示蜂蜜的"珍贵性"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"日常"的句子——读者意识到蜂蜜不只是"药物"，还渗透到生活的方方面面。

② "While mānuka honey has been around for some 200 years, it wasn't until as recently as two decades ago that humans began to realize its true value. This dark-amber product from little old New Zealand was a honey with such unique healing powers it shone brightly above all others."

**中文理解**：虽然麦卢卡蜂蜜已经存在了大约 200 年，但直到最近二十年前，人类才开始认识到它的真正价值。这种来自新西兰的暗琥珀色产品是一种具有如此独特治疗能力的蜂蜜，它在所有其他蜂蜜中 shone brightly（闪耀）。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "While mānuka honey has been around for some 200 years, it wasn't until as recently as two decades ago that humans began to realize its true value" + 分2 "This dark-amber product from little old New Zealand was a honey with such unique healing powers it shone brightly above all others"。

**关键词**：dark-amber（暗琥珀色）、unique healing powers（独特的治疗能力）、shone brightly（闪耀）

**表达方式**：用"little old New Zealand"（小小的旧新西兰）——口语化表达，暗示"这种'国宝'来自一个小国"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"发现故事"——读者开始理解"麦卢卡蜂蜜的'逆袭'"。

③ "We knew it was an incredible product then — but even today, we're still understanding the full extent of why."

**中文理解**：我们当时就知道它是一个令人难以置信的产品——但即使在今天，我们仍然在理解其"为什么"的全部程度。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "We knew it was an incredible product then" + 转折连词 "but" + 分2 "even today, we're still understanding the full extent of why"。

**关键词**：incredible product（令人难以置信的产品）、full extent（全部程度）

**表达方式**：用"We knew... but even today"——制造"已知 vs 未知"的张力。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"科学谦逊"——承认"我们知道它有效，但还不知道全部原因"。

④ "Then, in the 1990s, researchers at Waikato University, New Zealand, discovered mānuka honey's unique antibacterial properties. Professor in Biological Sciences Dr. Peter Molan found that mānuka honey showed antibacterial activity even when hydrogen peroxide was not present, labelling this unique property 'non-peroxide activity' and coining the term 'Unique Mānuka Factor' (UMF) in 1997."

**中文理解**：然后，在 1990 年代，新西兰 Waikato 大学的研究人员发现了麦卢卡蜂蜜独特的抗菌特性。生物科学教授 Peter Molan 博士发现，即使不存在过氧化氢，麦卢卡蜂蜜也显示出抗菌活性，将这一独特特性标记为"非过氧化物活性"，并于 1997 年创造了"独特麦卢卡因子"（UMF）这一术语。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Then, in the 1990s, researchers at Waikato University, New Zealand, discovered mānuka honey's unique antibacterial properties" + 分2 "Professor in Biological Sciences Dr. Peter Molan found that mānuka honey showed antibacterial activity even when hydrogen peroxide was not present, labelling this unique property 'non-peroxide activity' and coining the term 'Unique Mānuka Factor' (UMF) in 1997"。

**关键词**：Waikato University（Waikato 大学）、hydrogen peroxide（过氧化氢）、non-peroxide activity（非过氧化物活性）、UMF（独特麦卢卡因子）

**表达方式**：用"labelling... coining"——将 Peter Molan 描述为"命名者"，暗示他是"麦卢卡蜂蜜科学的奠基人"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"命名"的句子——读者开始理解"UMF"和"非过氧化物活性"这些术语的起源。

⑤ "Suddenly everyone wanted a taste. With the industry catapulting into the millions of dollars, imposter mānuka honey and diluted products began to appear on the market."

**中文理解**：突然间人人都想尝一口。随着产业 catapulting into the millions of dollars（迅速膨胀到数百万美元）， imposter mānuka honey（假冒麦卢卡蜂蜜）和 diluted products（稀释产品）开始出现在市场上。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Suddenly everyone wanted a taste" + 分2 "With the industry catapulting into the millions of dollars, imposter mānuka honey and diluted products began to appear on the market"。

**关键词**：catapulting（迅速膨胀）、imposter（假冒的）、diluted（稀释的）

**表达方式**：用"Suddenly everyone wanted a taste"——短句，制造"一夜爆红"的效果。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"市场爆点"——读者开始理解麦卢卡蜂蜜如何从"廉价品"变成"全球热门"。

⑥ "In an industry dependent on high standards of testing to ensure authenticity and kick counterfeit honeys to the curb, MGO has become a marker of credibility. Its potency is now used as a measure to test whether honey has Dr. Molan's Unique Mānuka Factor, along with other criteria including shelf life and purity, and successful honey producers can put the UMF abbreviation on their jars."

**中文理解**：在一个依赖高标准测试以确保真实性并将假冒蜂蜜 kick to the curb（淘汰）的产业中，MGO 已经成为可信度的标志。它的效力现在被用作测试蜂蜜是否具有 Molan 博士的独特麦卢卡因子的衡量标准，还有其他标准如保质期和纯度，成功的蜂蜜生产商可以在他们的罐子上贴上 UMF 缩写。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "In an industry dependent on high standards of testing to ensure authenticity and kick counterfeit honeys to the curb, MGO has become a marker of credibility" + 分2 "Its potency is now used as a measure to test whether honey has Dr. Molan's Unique Mānuka Factor, along with other criteria including shelf life and purity, and successful honey producers can put the UMF abbreviation on their jars"。

**关键词**：kick counterfeit honeys to the curb（淘汰假冒蜂蜜）、marker of credibility（可信度标志）

**表达方式**：用"kick to the curb"（淘汰）——口语化表达，制造"正义得到伸张"的印象。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"质量管控"——读者开始理解"如何辨别真假麦卢卡蜂蜜"。

⑦ "It's not the first time this kind of initiative has been used to prove the authenticity of a product, either — just think of champagne, that beautifully bubbly drink which is only technically champagne if it comes from the region of Champagne, France."

**中文理解**：这也不是第一次使用这种 initiative（举措）来证明产品的真实性——想想香槟，那种 beautifully bubbly drink（美妙的起泡饮料），只有在技术上来自法国香槟地区才能称为香槟。

**句子结构**：复合句。主句 "It's not the first time this kind of initiative has been used to prove the authenticity of a product, either" + 破折号引出举例 "just think of champagne, that beautifully bubbly drink which is only technically champagne if it comes from the region of Champagne, France"。

**关键词**：initiative（举措）、authenticity（真实性）、technically（技术上）

**表达方式**：用香槟比喻——将麦卢卡蜂蜜与香槟类比，暗示"产地=品质"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"法律"的句子——读者开始理解"产地保护"的重要性。

⑧ "What makes mānuka honey so effective is its non-peroxide activity, which directly correlates to its methylglyoxal (MGO) content (see page 170). MGO has a potent antibacterial property, and the greater a honey's level of MGO, the more powerful its healing abilities are."

**中文理解**：使麦卢卡蜂蜜如此有效的是其非过氧化物活性，这与其甲基乙二醛（MGO）含量直接相关（见第 170 页）。MGO 具有强大的抗菌特性，蜂蜜的 MGO 含量越高，其治疗能力就越强。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "What makes mānuka honey so effective is its non-peroxide activity, which directly correlates to its methylglyoxal (MGO) content (see page 170)" + 分2 "MGO has a potent antibacterial property, and the greater a honey's level of MGO, the more powerful its healing abilities are"。

**关键词**：directly correlates to（与...直接相关）、potent（强大的）

**表达方式**：用"the greater... the more..."（越...越...）——常见的比较结构，制造"剂量-效应"的印象。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"核心机制"——读者开始理解"麦卢卡蜂蜜为何有效"。

⑨ "These qualities have successfully seen mānuka honey used as a topical medical treatment. Dave Campbell, beekeeper, explains: 'From a wound-healing perspective, MGO is definitely a magic ingredient. Think about skincare. I've heard stories about kids with severe acne or eczema using mānuka honey and having miraculous relief and recovery, within days.'"

**中文理解**：这些特性已成功使麦卢卡蜂蜜被用作局部医疗。养蜂人 Dave Campbell 解释道："从伤口愈合的角度来看，MGO 绝对是一种 magic ingredient（神奇成分）。想想护肤。我听说有关于患有严重痤疮或湿疹的儿童使用麦卢卡蜂蜜并在几天内奇迹般缓解和康复的故事。"

**句子结构**：复合句。分1 "These qualities have successfully seen mānuka honey used as a topical medical treatment" + 分2 "Dave Campbell, beekeeper, explains: 'From a wound-healing perspective, MGO is definitely a magic ingredient. Think about skincare. I've heard stories about kids with severe acne or eczema using mānuka honey and having miraculous relief and recovery, within days.'"

**关键词**：perspective（角度）、magic ingredient（神奇成分）

**表达方式**：用"magic ingredient"（神奇成分）——口语化表达，暗示"这就像魔法一样有效"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文最"个人"的句子——读者被养蜂人的"亲眼见证"打动。

⑩ "When used on wounds to treat an infection that antibiotics can't manage, mānuka honey does a great job. It also works on uninfected wounds that won't heal, where the main issue is uncontrolled inflammation — such as a bedsore, which occurs after prolonged pressure on the underlying tissues."

**中文理解**：当用于治疗抗生素无法控制的感染的伤口时，麦卢卡蜂蜜表现出色。它也适用于不愈合的未感染伤口，其中主要问题是 uncontrolled inflammation（不受控制的炎症）——如褥疮，这是由于长时间压力对底层组织造成的。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "When used on wounds to treat an infection that antibiotics can't manage, mānuka honey does a great job" + 分2 "It also works on uninfected wounds that won't heal, where the main issue is uncontrolled inflammation — such as a bedsore, which occurs after prolonged pressure on the underlying tissues"。

**关键词**：uncontrolled inflammation（不受控制的炎症）、bedsore（褥疮）、prolonged pressure（长时间压力）

**表达方式**：用"does a great job"（表现出色）——口语化表达，暗示"它做得很好"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"临床应用"——读者开始理解麦卢卡蜂蜜适用于哪些类型的伤口。

## 一句话总结
**1990 年代，Peter Molan 教授在 Waikato 大学发现了麦卢卡蜂蜜的"非过氧化物活性"（与过氧化氢无关的抗菌活性），并于 1997 年创造了 UMF 标准——这一发现使麦卢卡蜂蜜从廉价品逆袭为全球热门，MGO 含量成为衡量其品质和真实性的关键指标。**
