# 26. Epilogue（后记）

## 概览

- **出处**：*What the Bees See: A Honeybee's Eye View of the World* by Craig P. Burrows
- **章节定位**：Epilogue（后记），全书结尾
- **作者**：Nick Worthington
- **字符数**：1,315
- **一句话主旨**：总结全书主题——人类对自然的理解仍处于"婴儿期"；通过 Craig P. Burrows 的紫外摄影，我们得以窥见一个"此前隐藏的世界"；蜜蜂创造的系统（生命/免疫/长寿）可能对人类同样有益；但我们正站在"最深刻的自然发现"与"前所未有的自然灾难"的十字路口——希望这是"开始，而非结束"。

## 选择性精读

① "Our understanding of the natural world is in its infancy."

**中文理解**：我们对自然界的理解仍处于婴儿期。

**句子结构**：简单句。主语 "Our understanding of the natural world" + 系动词 "is" + 表语 "in its infancy"。

**关键词**：in its infancy（处于婴儿期/初期）

**表达方式**：用"in its infancy"（处于婴儿期）——将人类对自然的认知描述为"刚出生的婴儿"，暗示"我们知道的还很少"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"谦逊定调"——读者被告知"尽管科学已取得巨大进步，但自然界的'大部分'仍未知"。

---

② "From the ways flowers appear to insects differently to the human eye, to the symbiotic relationship they have with bees that ensures both species' survival, there are many lessons we can learn from nature — if we simply take the time to look at the world through a different lens."

**中文理解**：从花朵在昆虫眼中与在人眼中看起来不同，到它们与蜜蜂确保双方生存的共生关系，我们可以从自然中学到很多教训——只要我们简单地花时间通过不同的视角看世界。

**句子结构**：复合句。主句 "there are many lessons we can learn from nature" + 条件状语 "if we simply take the time to look at the world through a different lens"。

**关键词**：symbiotic relationship（共生关系）、ensures both species' survival（确保双方生存）、through a different lens（通过不同的视角）

**表达方式**：用"through a different lens"（通过不同的视角）——呼应本书的核心方法（紫外摄影=不同的视角）。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"学习方法"——读者被告知"学习自然"不是"不可能"，而是"需要换一种眼光"。

---

③ "In this instance it was through the ultraviolet light spectrum and the vision of Craig P. Burrows. What he has revealed is a world previously hidden to us."

**中文理解**：在这个例子中，是通过紫外光谱和 Craig P. Burrows 的视角。他所揭示的是一个此前对我们隐藏的世界。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "In this instance it was through the ultraviolet light spectrum and the vision of Craig P. Burrows" + 分2 "What he has revealed is a world previously hidden to us"。

**关键词**：ultraviolet light spectrum（紫外光谱）、previously hidden（此前隐藏的）

**表达方式**：用"previously hidden"（此前隐藏的）——暗示"世界一直在那里，只是我们看不见"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"致敬"——将 Craig P. Burrows 描述为"揭示隐藏世界的人"。

---

④ "Looking more deeply within that world, scientists and naturalists have discovered a secret system, created by bees, that is beyond our comprehension and defies scientific and medical explanation. It's a system for complete health and wellness offering the gift of life, a cloak of immunity and the dream of longevity."

**中文理解**：更深入地观察那个世界，科学家和博物学家发现了一个由蜜蜂创造的 secret system（秘密系统），它超出了我们的理解， defies scientific and medical explanation（挑战科学和医学解释）。它是一个提供生命礼物、免疫斗篷和长寿梦想的完整健康与 wellness 系统。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Looking more deeply within that world, scientists and naturalists have discovered a secret system, created by bees, that is beyond our comprehension and defies scientific and medical explanation" + 分2 "It's a system for complete health and wellness offering the gift of life, a cloak of immunity and the dream of longevity"。

**关键词**：beyond our comprehension（超出我们的理解）、defies explanation（挑战解释）、secret system（秘密系统）

**表达方式**：用"defies scientific and medical explanation"（挑战科学和医学解释）——暗示蜜蜂的系统"比人类科学更复杂"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"核心总结"——将"蜜蜂三宝"（蜂蜜/蜂胶/蜂王浆）概括为"生命/免疫/长寿"三大礼物。

---

⑤ "A system that may yet prove to be as beneficial to you and me as it has been for the honeybee."

**中文理解**：这个系统可能 yet prove to be（最终被证明）对你我像对蜜蜂一样有益。

**句子结构**：复合句。主句 "A system that may yet prove to be as beneficial to you and me as it has been for the honeybee"。

**关键词**：yet prove to be（最终被证明）

**表达方式**：用"as beneficial to you and me as it has been for the honeybee"——将人类与蜜蜂并列，暗示"蜜蜂的系统可能也是人类的系统"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全书的"终极问题"——读者被告知"这个系统对你可能也有效"。

---

⑥ "Our way of seeing the world has perhaps been one-dimensional, through the single lens of the human eye. We are finally beginning to understand how much more there is to see and learn from nature."

**中文理解**：我们看待世界的方式 perhaps（也许）一直是 one-dimensional（一维的），通过人眼的 single lens（单一视角）。我们终于开始理解还有多少更多可以看到和从自然中学习的东西。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "Our way of seeing the world has perhaps been one-dimensional, through the single lens of the human eye" + 分2 "We are finally beginning to understand how much more there is to see and learn from nature"。

**关键词**：one-dimensional（一维的）、single lens（单一视角）

**表达方式**：用"one-dimensional"（一维的）——将人类视觉描述为"扁平的"，暗示"世界比我们看到的更丰富"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"视觉主题"回扣——从"紫外摄影"到"多维视角"，读者被告知"换一种眼光，世界更丰富"。

---

⑦ "We can also now see the natural world in a different light — a world that is in danger of losing biodiversity at the fastest rate in history as a result of human expansion. The losers may ultimately be you and me."

**中文理解**：我们现在也可以在不同的光线中看到一个自然世界——一个由于人类扩张而面临以历史上最快速度丧失生物多样性的世界。输家可能最终是你和我。

**句子结构**：并列句。分1 "We can also now see the natural world in a different light — a world that is in danger of losing biodiversity at the fastest rate in history as a result of human expansion" + 分2 "The losers may ultimately be you and me"。

**关键词**：in a different light（在不同的光线中）、losing biodiversity（丧失生物多样性）

**表达方式**：用"The losers may ultimately be you and me"——将读者纳入"受害者"范畴，制造紧迫感。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"危机警示"——读者被告知"破坏自然=破坏人类自己"。

---

⑧ "We are at once on the brink of the most profound discoveries and understanding of our relationship with nature, and on the brink of unprecedented natural disaster."

**中文理解**：我们 simultaneously 站在对我们与自然关系最深刻发现和理解的边缘，也站在前所未有的自然灾难的边缘。

**句子结构**：简单句。主句 "We are at once on the brink of the most profound discoveries and understanding of our relationship with nature, and on the brink of unprecedented natural disaster"。

**关键词**：at once（同时）、on the brink of（在...边缘）、unprecedented（前所未有的）

**表达方式**：用"at once... and..."（同时...和...）——制造"十字路口"的张力，暗示"人类面临选择"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全文的"二元对立"——读者被告知"我们面临'发现'或'灾难'的抉择"。

---

⑨ "As with all stories of hope, we trust this is just the beginning and not the end."

**中文理解**：正如所有希望的故事一样，我们相信这只是开始，而不是结束。

**句子结构**：复合句。方式状语 "As with all stories of hope" + 主句 "we trust this is just the beginning and not the end"。

**关键词**：beginning（开始）、end（结束）

**表达方式**：用"beginning and not the end"——制造开放性结局，暗示"故事还没完"。

**为什么这样写**：这是全书的"收尾"——读者被告知"这不是终点，而是起点"，激发"继续探索"的意愿。
