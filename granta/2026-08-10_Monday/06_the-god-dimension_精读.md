# 神之维度（The God Dimension）— 精读分析

## 概览

> **来源**：Granta 杂志｜2026-06-24 首发｜访谈者：Merve Emre｜受访人：**Marilynne Robinson**（《盖勒德》《居家》《蕾拉》作者）× **Jon Fosse**（2023 年诺贝尔文学奖得主，《七部曲》《发光》作者）｜URL: https://granta.com/the-god-dimension/
>
> **主题**：两位诺奖/布克级作家围绕**神学、艺术、政治**展开的对谈。Robinson 讲"宗教人文主义"如何能救现代性；Fosse 讲"否定神学"如何让他沉默、倾听。**共同命题**：语言如何使"灵性的缺席"变得"可感"。
>
> **结构**：编者按（1 段）+ 访谈正文（共 19 轮问答），可拆为 **10 个主题段**：
> ①Robinson 的圣经课（创世 / 复仇 vs 伸冤）②Fosse 的否定神学（不可言说）③Robinson 的"宗教人文主义"④Fosse 的内光（Eckhart × 贵格会 × 天主教）⑤"内在之光"与 Jonathan Edwards⑥被世俗误读的传统⑦宗教非教条，而是宇宙⑧Fosse 的"每个灵魂的独特性"⑨Robinson 的"灵魂-身体未解之谜"⑩艺术与神性、文学与政治、文学与虚无主义（Hamsun / Nietzsche / Pound）⑪宗教的未来：Robinson 的"丰富" vs Fosse 的"无法唯物"
>
> **金句 1**（Fosse）："The strongest believers are the agnostics, because they don't know what God is."
>
> **金句 2**（Robinson）："The joy of being religious is that you discipline yourself in seeing more deeply than you would otherwise see."
>
> **金句 3**（Fosse）："I have the belief of an elderly man, or a middle aged one."

## 逐句精读

### 第 1 段：编者按——讲座背景

> **原句 1-1:** This year **Marilynne Robinson** **delivered** the **Fosse Lecture**, an **annual speaker series** **established** by the National Library of Norway **after** Jon Fosse received the Nobel Prize in 2023.

- **Marilynne Robinson**：玛丽琳·罗宾逊（1943–），美国当代最重要的作家之一，《盖勒德》（2004 普利策奖）作者。
- **delivered**：发表、讲授。
- **Fosse Lecture**：**福斯讲座**（2023 年 Fosse 获诺奖后挪威国家图书馆设立的年度系列）。
- **annual speaker series**：年度演讲系列。
- **established**：设立。

- **五子项**：主语 Marilynne Robinson / 谓语 delivered / 宾语 the Fosse Lecture / 同位语 an annual speaker series / 定语 established by... / 状语 after Jon Fosse received the Nobel Prize in 2023。

> **原句 1-2:** Robinson's lecture, '**On Faith and Freedom of Thought**', **sketched** an American tradition of **religious humanism**, a '**deeper, purer, and more strenuous freedom** than we have **aspired to lately**', she claimed.

- **On Faith and Freedom of Thought**：《论信仰与思想自由》（罗宾逊讲座题目）。
- **sketched**：**勾勒**（不是完整论述，而是草图）。
- **religious humanism**：**宗教人文主义**（Robinson 的标志性立场——人文主义需要宗教根基）。
- **a deeper, purer, and more strenuous freedom**：**更深的、更纯的、更费力的自由**（三个形容词并列，层层递进）。
- **aspired to lately**：**最近所向往的**——暗讽**当代自由**其实并不"费力"，因而也不"深"。

> **原句 1-3:** She spoke in the **Grand Hall of the Royal Palace** to an audience that included **Crown Princess Mette-Marit**.

- **Grand Hall of the Royal Palace**：王宫大礼堂。
- **Crown Princess Mette-Marit**：挪威王储妃（王储 Haakon 之妻）。

> **原句 1-4:** Fosse, who **stays away from** most **official functions**, **attended** the lecture, but **vanished before** the reception, when a **choir** **mounted** the palace staircase and **sang a hymn** **inspired by** his poem 'Det store andletet bøyer seg ned.'

- **stays away from**：**回避**（Fosse 不喜正式场合）。
- **official functions**：官方活动。
- **attended**：出席。
- **vanished before**：**在……之前消失了**（神秘离场）。
- **choir**：合唱团。
- **mounted**：登上。
- **sang a hymn inspired by**：**唱了受……启发的圣歌**。
- **Det store andletet bøyer seg ned**（挪威语）："伟大的灵魂俯身而下"——Fosse 的诗。

- **段落逻辑：** **场景铺陈**——王宫大礼堂、王储妃在场、Fosse 神秘离场（合唱团正在唱由他诗歌启发的圣歌）。**三处细节**（Robinson 讲宗教人文主义 / 王储妃 / Fosse 消失）为整篇访谈**埋下三个主题锚**：**信仰 / 官方 vs 私人 / 神学与艺术的交汇**。

### 第 2 段：两位作家的神学立场

> **原句 2-1:** The day **before** the lecture, I met Robinson and Fosse at **Kaffistova**, Fosse's **preferred restaurant**, where we spoke about **God, art, and politics**.

- **Kaffistova**：奥斯陆一家著名餐厅。
- **preferred restaurant**：**偏爱的餐厅**（Fosse 的私人选择）。
- **God, art, and politics**：**上帝、艺术与政治**——全文的**三角主题**。

> **原句 2-2:** Although we did not speak **explicitly** about their **novels**, Robinson and Fosse's **theological positions** **anchor** their art.

- **explicitly**：**明确地**。
- **novels**：小说。
- **theological positions**：**神学立场**。
- **anchor**：**锚定**（动词——他们的神学是他们艺术的**根基**）。

> **原句 2-3:** The **rippling, incantatory style** of Fosse's **Septology** and **The Shining** is the **aesthetic correlative** to his **negative theology**; his **book-length sentences** **circle around** what God is **not** in order to **access** an **ineffable, invisible divine reality**.

- **rippling, incantatory style**：**涟漪式的、诵念式的风格**（Fosse 的标志性语言）。
- **Septology**：《七部曲》（Fosse 2007–2011 长篇系列）。
- **The Shining**：《发光》（Fosse 另一长篇）。
- **aesthetic correlative**：**美学对应物**（借鉴艾略特 concept of "objective correlative"）——**神学是内容，风格是形式**。
- **negative theology**：**否定神学**（apophatic theology，通过说"神不是什么"来接近神）。
- **book-length sentences**：**书长句**（Fosse 的句子极长）。
- **circle around**：**环绕**。
- **ineffable, invisible divine reality**：**不可言说、不可见的神圣实在**。

> **原句 2-4:** (**The Vaim trilogy**, **excerpted in** 'Granta 175: Scandinavia', is **cut from a different cloth**.)

- **Vaim trilogy**：《海姆》三部曲（Fosse 最新长篇）。
- **excerpted in**：**节选刊于**。
- **cut from a different cloth**：**风格迥异**（clothes 引申为"风格、质地"）。

> **原句 2-5:** Robinson's novels are **everywhere touched by** her **belief in** the **transcendental qualities of ordinary life**, from Ruthie's **Emersonian communion with nature** in *Housekeeping*, to the **dialogues between family members across the Gilead quartet**.

- **everywhere touched by**：**处处沾染着**。
- **transcendental qualities of ordinary life**：**日常生活的超越性品质**。
- **Emersonian communion with nature**：**爱默生式的与自然交融**（爱默生 = 美国超验主义创始人）。
- **Housekeeping**：《居家》（Robinson 1980 处女作，获国家书评人奖）。
- **Gilead quartet**：《盖勒德》四部曲（*Gilead*, *Home*, *Lila*, *Jack*）。

> **原句 2-6:** Both writers **insist that** **language can make a spiritual absence** **as present to our senses as** the objects we can **see, taste, and touch**.

- **insist that**：**坚持认为**。
- **language can make a spiritual absence as present to our senses as...**：**语言能让灵性的缺席如同……一样对我们的感官存在**——**这是整篇访谈的核心命题**。
- **see, taste, and touch**：**看、尝、触**（感官的具象性）。

> **原句 2-7:** This conversation has been **edited for length and clarity**.

- **edited for length and clarity**：**为篇幅与清晰做了编辑**（访谈体常见声明）。

- **段落逻辑：** **理论锚点**——编者用 7 句话为读者**铺设整场访谈的解读框架**：①两位作家的**神学是文学的根基**；②Fosse 的"否定神学"对应其**涟漪式语言**；③Robinson 的"超越性日常"对应其**家庭叙事**；④两者共同主张：**语言能让"灵性的缺席"变得可感**。这是全文最重要的**理论前提**。

### 第 3 段：Robinson 的圣经课——创世叙事

> **原句 3-1**（Emre）: I **received no religious education**, so I was **interested to learn**, Marilynne, that you were **teaching a class on** **reading the Bible**. What do you **want to impart** to your students through your **readings of the Old and New Testament**?

- **received no religious education**：**没有接受过宗教教育**（Emre 自我介绍）。
- **interested to learn**：**有兴趣得知**。
- **teaching a class on reading the Bible**：**开了一门"读圣经"的课程**。
- **want to impart**：**想传授**。
- **readings of the Old and New Testament**：**对《旧约》和《新约》的解读**。

- **五子项**：主语 I / 谓语 was / 表语 interested to learn / 宾语从句 that you were teaching... / 状语（原因）because I received no religious education。

- **段落逻辑：** **提问引入**——Emre 从自己的"无宗教教育"背景切入，提出 Robinson 为何要教圣经、想通过圣经传达什么。**"impart"这个词**暗示 Robinson 的角色是**传递者**而非**创造者**。

> **原句 3-2**（Robinson）: There are many people who **feel that** they did not **receive any** religious education, so I try to **give them basic terms and concepts** simply **so they can have an approach**.

- **feel that**：**觉得**。
- **give them basic terms and concepts**：**给他们基本的术语和概念**。
- **so they can have an approach**：**这样他们就能有切入点**。

> **原句 3-3**（Robinson）: The **creation was the first category** that I talked about. I **use things like** the passage in **Proverbs** that **describes the spirit** that is **with God at creation**, and the **poem at the end of Job**, in which God says, '**Where were you**', and **psalms that celebrate creation**.

- **the first category**：**第一类别**（她首先讲的）。
- **Proverbs**：《箴言》（旧约智慧书之一）。
- **the spirit that is with God at creation**：**创世时与上帝同在的灵**（箴言 8:22–31，智慧在创世之前）。
- **the poem at the end of Job**：《约伯记》末尾的诗（约伯记 38–41 章，上帝的旋风之言）。
- **Where were you**："你在哪里"（上帝问约伯——**你不在场，怎能评判我的创世**？）。
- **psalms**：《诗篇》。
- **celebrate creation**：**赞美创世**。

> **原句 3-4**（Robinson）: I think that the **celebratory side** of the Christian tradition is **very much lost**; it's **thought of as being punitive**.

- **celebratory side**：**赞美的面向**。
- **very much lost**：**很大程度上遗失了**。
- **thought of as being punitive**：**被认为是惩罚性的**。

> **原句 3-5**（Robinson）: The idea of **understanding the amazing beauty of being** is **intrinsic to the literature** and part of **what people have a right to**. The culture has **not given them the terms** to **express it**.

- **understanding the amazing beauty of being**：**理解存在的惊人之美**。
- **intrinsic to the literature**：**内在于这些文本**。
- **part of what people have a right to**：**人们有权获得的**——**神学是权利，不是特权**。
- **has not given them the terms**：**没有给他们表达它的词汇**——**文化的贫穷**。

- **段落逻辑：** Robinson 的**教学目的**是**提供语言**（terms and concepts），让那些"没受过宗教教育"的人**能够接近**基督教传统的**赞美面向**。**两个关键诊断**：①传统被误认为"惩罚性"；②文化没有给普通人**表达"存在之美"的词汇**。这是 Robinson 的**文学-神学立场的第一次亮相**。

### 第 4 段：Robinson 谈"惩罚性上帝"与翻译问题

> **原句 4-1**（Robinson）: **One of the things that is very much on my mind** doing all this is that the **standard translations of the Bible** make God into a **monster**.

- **One of the things that is very much on my mind**：**我心中极在意的**——**口语化、带情感强度的开场**。
- **standard translations of the Bible**：**标准译本**。
- **make God into a monster**：**把上帝变成怪物**——**最尖锐的指控**。

> **原句 4-2**（Robinson）: He's **jealous**. He's **vengeful**. I **bring up the fact that** 'vengeance' is a **bad translation**. If you **look at** the **Vulgate**, it says '**vindicator**' – 'to **vindicate**', which means **not only to assign innocence, but also to assign guilt**. It **isn't vengeance**.

- **jealous / vengeful**：**嫉妒的 / 报复的**——标准译本赋予上帝的两个负面特质。
- **bring up the fact that**：**指出一个事实**。
- **bad translation**：**糟糕的翻译**——**关键批判**。
- **Vulgate**：**武加大译本**（4 世纪拉丁文圣经，天主教官方译本）。
- **vindicator / to vindicate**：**伸冤者 / 伸冤**（与 vengeance 完全不同——**伸冤 = 恢复正义**，vengeance = **私人复仇**）。
- **assign innocence / assign guilt**：**判定无辜 / 判定有罪**——上帝的"伸冤"是**法庭式、正义性**的，不是**情感式、报复性**的。

> **原句 4-3**（Robinson）: You **see people like** our horrible **Secretary of War** using the word **vengeance** and **feeling that it is sanctified by** a **bad translation**.

- **our horrible Secretary of War**：**我们可恨的战争部长**（讽刺现实政治人物）。
- **using the word vengeance**：**使用"复仇"一词**。
- **feeling that it is sanctified by**：**觉得它被……圣化了**。
- **why this is brilliant**：把**圣经翻译问题**直接连到**当代政治**——政客用"复仇"这个词攻击他国，却**误以为是圣经的支持**。

> **原句 4-4**（Robinson）: I think **a lot of what distances people from religion** are the **inappropriate words** that have become **identified with** the text.

- **what distances people from religion**：**使人远离宗教的因素**。
- **inappropriate words**：**不当的词语**。
- **identified with the text**：**与文本混为一谈**。

- **段落逻辑：** Robinson 提出一个**翻译批判的核心命题**：**"vengeance"（复仇）是"vindicator"（伸冤者）的错误翻译**——**一个词的误译**，让一代代人把上帝想成**怪物**。她把这个问题**从神学拉到翻译学、再到政治**（战争部长滥用"复仇"）。**"inappropriate words"是使人与宗教疏离的元凶**。

### 第 5 段：Robinson——古/新约的区分与人类之恶

> **原句 5-1**（Emre）: Is it **only a matter of bad translation** that the **vengeful God** is the God **absorbed by** American culture?

- **only a matter of**：**仅仅是……的问题**（质疑 Robinson 是否把问题说得过于简单）。
- **vengeful God**：**报复性的上帝**。
- **absorbed by**：**被……吸纳**。

> **原句 5-2**（Robinson）: They're **very frightening things** in the **New Testament**, like the **apocalypse**.

- **frightening things**：**可怕的事情**。
- **apocalypse**：**启示录**（新约末卷，关于末日审判）。

> **原句 5-3**（Robinson）: But I think **most of the grievous things** that we are **threatened by** are things that **we have created ourselves**.

- **grievous things**：**惨痛之事**。
- **threatened by**：**被……威胁**。
- **we have created ourselves**：**我们自己创造的**——**责任的归返**。

> **原句 5-4**（Robinson）: The **prohibition against killing** is one that **human beings violate constantly**.

- **prohibition against killing**：**不可杀人的诫命**。
- **violate constantly**：**不断地违背**。

> **原句 5-5**（Robinson）: I think that it's **true in the world**, and it's **true in the Bible**, that you **have to make a distinction between** **evil in the ancient sense** and **human behavior**. **We're up to no good** a **great deal of the time**, and **to each other's enormous harm**.

- **make a distinction between A and B**：**区分 A 与 B**。
- **evil in the ancient sense**：**古代意义上的恶**（超自然的、宇宙性的恶）。
- **human behavior**：**人类行为**（人类自己的恶）。
- **up to no good**：**没在干好事**（口语习语）。
- **to each other's enormous harm**：**对彼此造成巨大伤害**。

> **原句 5-6**（Robinson）: The **greatest commandment** in both Testaments is '**Love the Lord**.' Yet **out of traditionalism and sloppiness**, we create an idea of God that's **not very lovable**, so that there's a **transgression against** the first commandment **before anything else is launched on**.

- **the greatest commandment**：**最大的诫命**（"你要全心全意爱主你的上帝"）。
- **traditionalism and sloppiness**：**传统主义与马虎**（两个问题：既守旧又不严谨）。
- **not very lovable**：**并不怎么可爱**——**对"爱上帝"这个诫命的荒诞讽刺**。
- **transgression against**：**对……的违背**。
- **before anything else is launched on**：**在其它一切都开始之前**。

> **原句 5-7**（Robinson）: I would like to be a **purely objective teacher**, but I think there are **certain major corrections** that need to be made.

- **purely objective teacher**：**纯粹客观的教师**。
- **certain major corrections**：**某些重大的纠正**——**她承认自己的教学是有立场的**。

- **段落逻辑：** **从翻译问题转向哲学问题**——Robinson 承认《新约》里也有"可怕的东西"（启示录），但她区分**古代意义上的恶**与**人类行为**：**人类的恶才是主要威胁**。然后她提出一个**悖论**：最大的诫命是"爱上帝"，但我们却创造了一个**不爱**的上帝形象——**这意味着我们首先就违背了第一条诫命**。这是一个**神学-伦理学的自我反思**。

### 第 6 段：Fosse——否定神学（不可言说）

> **原句 6-1**（Emre）: Jon, was the **difference between** a **vengeful god** and a **loving God** important for your **religious upbringing** in Norway?

- **religious upbringing**：**宗教教养**。

> **原句 6-2**（Fosse）: The **Old Testament** talks about this **vengeful God**. The **New Testament** talks about the **love of God**. **The only thing said about God** in the New Testament is that **God is love**, **nothing else**.

- **Old Testament / New Testament**：**旧约 / 新约**。
- **the only thing said about God... nothing else**：**《新约》中关于上帝唯一说的话就是"上帝就是爱"，别无他言**——**极端化**的表达，强调新约的核心。

> **原句 6-3**（Fosse）: In the **European tradition**, you **have** an **apophatic theology**, a **negative theology** in which you **cannot say anything about** God. He's **hidden behind concepts**. He is **outside time and space**.

- **apophatic theology**：**否定神学**（apophasis = 否认，通过"不是什么"来认识神）。
- **negative theology**：同上，否定神学的另一种说法。
- **cannot say anything about**：**无法说任何关于**。
- **hidden behind concepts**：**隐藏在概念背后**。
- **outside time and space**：**在时空之外**。

> **原句 6-4**（Fosse）: Our **human language** is **far too limited** to say **anything wise about him**, anything **worth saying**.

- **human language**：**人类语言**。
- **far too limited**：**太有限了**。
- **anything wise / worth saying**：**明智的 / 值得说的**。

> **原句 6-5**（Fosse）: **That is my position**. I **cannot say anything about** God, **except for** that I **believe in** God.

- **That is my position**：**这就是我的立场**——**简洁有力的自我界定**。
- **cannot say anything about God, except for...**：**除了……之外，什么也不能说**——**极端的克制**。

- **段落逻辑：** Fosse 的立场——**否定神学**：**上帝在时空之外，人类语言太有限，无法说任何关于祂的事**。他的全部立场就是：**"我不能说任何关于上帝的话，除了我信仰上帝。"**——**一句话的哲学**。这与 Robinson 的立场形成**第一次张力**：Robinson 说"很多关于上帝的话已经说过了"，Fosse 说"什么都不能说"。

### 第 7 段：Fosse——"我相信上帝"的真正含义

> **原句 7-1**（Fosse）: What I mean when I say, 'I believe in God', is that **there's something in my life** that is the **truth**. It's **not an intellectual truth**.

- **something in my life**：**我生命中的某种东西**。
- **the truth**：**真理**（不是"某个真理"，是"那唯一的真理"）。
- **not an intellectual truth**：**不是理智上的真理**（即**不是可论证的命题**）。

> **原句 7-2**（Fosse）: It has **more to do with** a **presence**, a **way of experiencing life and art**, than with any **idea or concept or attribute** that you **imagine as** God.

- **more to do with A than with B**：**与 A 的关系比与 B 的关系更紧密**。
- **presence**：**临在**（神学概念——神"在场"的感觉）。
- **way of experiencing life and art**：**体验和经历生命与艺术的方式**。
- **attribute**：**属性**（神学的"神属性"，如全能、全知、全善）。
- **imagine as God**：**想象成上帝**的（暗示：把上帝想象为某种属性，是**人的投射**）。

> **原句 7-3**（Fosse）: **The strongest believers are the agnostics**, because **they don't know what God is**. To me that's a **very fair and wise position**.

- **The strongest believers are the agnostics**：**最坚定的信徒是那些不可知论者**——**全文最悖论的金句**。
- **agnostics**：**不可知论者**（认为上帝是否存在不可知的人）。
- **a very fair and wise position**：**一个非常公平而明智的立场**。

- **段落逻辑：** Fosse 定义**"信仰"不是命题**，而是**生命中的某种临在**。**最精彩的悖论**："最坚定的信徒是不可知论者"——因为**不声称"知道"上帝是什么，才是真正谦卑的信仰**。这与 Robinson 的观点**互补**：Robinson 强调"相信上帝的良善"（积极命题），Fosse 强调"不声称知道上帝"（消极姿态）。

### 第 8 段：Robinson——上帝话语的已说与可接近性

> **原句 8-1**（Robinson）: But **a great deal has been said** about God. The **approachability** of God is **related to** the **traditions of language** that **surround** him.

- **a great deal has been said**：**很多话已经被说过**——**直接反驳 Fosse 的"什么都不能说"**。
- **approachability**：**可接近性**。
- **traditions of language**：**语言的传承**。
- **surround him**：**围绕着他**。

> **原句 8-2**（Robinson）: **Believing in God** is **believing in the benevolence of God**. It's **believing in the goodness of creation** and in certain **primary things** that are said, and that we **accept as truth**.

- **benevolence**：**仁慈**。
- **goodness of creation**：**创造的善**（上帝造的一切本质上是善的）。
- **primary things**：**基本的道理**。
- **accept as truth**：**接受为真理**。

> **原句 8-3**（Robinson）: Then there are, for example, the **ideas of God as vengeful and jealous** that **stand in the way of** this **deeper perception** that one **might have** about the **nature of God**.

- **stand in the way of**：**阻碍**。
- **deeper perception**：**更深层的理解**。
- **the nature of God**：**上帝的本质**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Robinson 对 Fosse 的回应**——"虽然你说什么都不能说，但事实上很多话已经说过了"。**"可接近性"**取决于**语言传统**。但**"复仇与嫉妒"**这些错误形象**阻碍了更深层的理解**。**两位作家的立场第一次正面碰撞**：**否定神学 vs 肯定神学**。

### 第 9 段：Fosse——创造者与受造物

> **原句 9-1**（Fosse）: There's a **huge division**, for me, between the **creator** and **creation**. **You can still see God in what is created**, but that **isn't his essential being**.

- **huge division**：**巨大的鸿沟**。
- **creator / creation**：**创造者 / 受造物**。
- **essential being**：**本质存在**——**你可以通过受造物"看见"神，但那不是神的"本质"**。

> **原句 9-2**（Fosse）: This is, **historically**, the **polemical difference between positive and negative theology**.

- **historically**：**从历史上看**。
- **polemical difference**：**论战性的差异**——**两种神学的根本分歧**。
- **positive theology**：**肯定神学**（cataphatic theology，通过肯定属性认识神）——Robinson 的路径。
- **negative theology**：**否定神学**（apophatic theology，通过否定认识神）——Fosse 的路径。

- **段落逻辑：** Fosse 用一个**清晰的二分法**（创造者 / 受造物）界定自己：**神可以透过受造物被"看见"，但那不是祂的本质**。他把 Robinson 与自己的分歧**定性为"肯定神学与否定神学的历史论战"**——**这是全篇最学术的元命题**。

### 第 10 段：Robinson 定义"可接近性"

> **原句 10-1**（Emre）: Marilynne, what do you mean by the **approachability** of God?

- **approachability**：**可接近性**。

> **原句 10-2**（Robinson）: **Whether you can be at peace with the intuition that you have of God**.

- **be at peace with**：**与……和谐共处**。
- **intuition**：**直觉**。
- **why this is brilliant**：Robinson 用**一句话**定义"可接近性"——**"你能否与自己对上帝的直觉和谐共处"**——**把神学问题还原为心理与情感问题**，极具现代性。

- **段落逻辑：** **一句话的回答**——可接近性 = 与上帝的直觉和谐共处。这是一个**非常现代的定义**：**不要求教条、不要求论证，只要求内心的和平**。

### 第 11 段：Robinson 谈宗教人文主义

> **原句 11-1**（Emre）: I am **interested in** the **attempts you've made** to **ground humanism in religious belief**. At least since the **Enlightenment**, the **development of humanism** as a ** philosophy or an ethical program** has involved **purging it of** religion. What is **at stake for you** in **restoring the intuition of God** to it?

- **ground humanism in religious belief**：**在宗教信仰之上建立人文主义**（"ground" = 建立根基）。
- **Enlightenment**：**启蒙运动**（17–18 世纪）。
- **purging it of religion**：**把宗教从其中清洗出去**。
- **at stake for you**：**对你而言，赌注是什么**。
- **restoring the intuition of God**：**恢复对上帝"的直觉**。

> **原句 11-2**（Robinson）: The idea of trying to **create a humanism purged of religious content** is a **modern project**. It **steps out of the tradition** of **humankind up to that point**.

- **purged of religious content**：**清洗掉宗教内容**。
- **modern project**：**现代工程**（带有批判性——"现代"在 Robinson 笔下常含讽刺）。
- **steps out of the tradition**：**走出传统**。
- **humankind up to that point**：**至今为止的人类**（即"整个人类历史"）。

> **原句 11-3**（Robinson）: If you look at the **Renaissance**, its idea of **humanism comes directly out of** the **religious traditions** that **lie behind it**.

- **Renaissance**：**文艺复兴**（14–17 世纪）。
- **comes directly out of**：**直接源于**。
- **lie behind it**：**在其背后的**。

> **原句 11-4**（Robinson）: I think the **most important humanists** are the **religious humanists**, **people like Pico della Mirandola and Dante**.

- **most important humanists**：**最重要的人文主义者**。
- **religious humanists**：**宗教人文主义者**——**Robinson 的核心概念**。
- **Pico della Mirandola**：**皮科·德拉·米兰多拉**（1463–1494，意大利文艺复兴哲学家，代表作《论人的尊严》）。
- **Dante**：**但丁**（1265–1321，意大利诗人，《神曲》作者）。

> **原句 11-5**（Robinson）: We've **created an artificial inevitability** around the modern idea that there's **something appropriate** in **birthing nihilism or atheism or agnosticism out of** a tradition that is **profoundly religious**.

- **artificial inevitability**：**人为的必然性**（"人工制造出来的必然性"——**Robinson 的核心批判**）。
- **something appropriate in**：**……是恰当的**。
- **birthing nihilism or atheism or agnosticism out of**：**从……中催生出虚无主义、无神论或不可知论**。
- **profoundly religious**：**深深植根于宗教的**。
- **why this is brilliant**：整句话是**一个长而密集的批判**——**"现代人文主义把反宗教当必然"是人工制造的假象**，实际上**文艺复兴的人文主义完全来自宗教传统**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Robinson 的立场核心**——现代人文主义试图**剥离宗教**，这是**人为制造的必然性**。她举**文艺复兴**（皮科、但丁）为例，证明**人文主义本就来自宗教**。**"宗教人文主义"** 是她的**核心术语**。

### 第 12 段：Fosse——Eckhart × 贵格会

> **原句 12-1**（Fosse）: When we were talking about **knowing something about God**, the **thinker who has taught me the most** – the thinker, who, in a way, **made me an organized Catholic** – was **Meister Eckhart**. He **lived in the thirteenth century** in Germany, and in France.

- **knowing something about God**：**关于认识上帝的事**。
- **the thinker who has taught me the most**：**教导我最多的思想家**。
- **made me an organized Catholic**：**使我成为体制内天主教徒**——**Eckhart 使他"皈依"天主教**。
- **Meister Eckhart**：**艾克哈特大师**（约 1260–1328，德国神秘主义哲学家，后被教会定为异端）。

> **原句 12-2**（Fosse）: The **way he talked about God** was **very provocative at the time**, but it was **very close to** another **influence** in my life: **Norwegian Quakerism**.

- **very provocative at the time**：**在当时极具挑衅性**。
- **very close to**：**非常接近**。
- **Norwegian Quakerism**：**挪威贵格会**（Quakers = 贵格会，17 世纪英国创立的和平主义基督教派）。

> **原句 12-3**（Fosse）: The Norwegian Quakers had this **concept of the inner light** that **emanated from inside of you**, which **brought you closest to God**.

- **concept of the inner light**：**"内在之光"的概念**（贵格会的核心教义——每个人内心都有上帝之光）。
- **emanated from inside of you**：**从你内部辐射出来**。
- **brought you closest to God**：**让你最接近上帝**。

> **原句 12-4**（Fosse）: **To hear His voice, you had to listen to silence**. **God spoke through silence**.

- **listen to silence**：**聆听寂静**。
- **God spoke through silence**：**上帝通过寂静说话**——**最诗意的表达**。

> **原句 12-5**（Fosse）: The Quakers **sit in a circle**, **keep quiet**, and try to **concentrate on the inner light**. If you feel that you can **say something good for others**, you **say it**. Otherwise, you **keep quiet**.

- **sit in a circle**：**围坐成圈**（贵格会的聚会形式）。
- **concentrate on**：**专注于**。
- **say something good for others**：**为别人说一些有益的话**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Fosse 的思想谱系**——**Eckhart（13 世纪德国神秘主义）+ 挪威贵格会**，两条线在**"内在之光"与"寂静"**上汇合。Eckhart 使他成为天主教徒，贵格会让他学会**聆听寂静**。**"God spoke through silence"** 是全篇最诗意的金句之一。

### 第 13 段：Fosse——天主教 × 贵格会的神秘相遇

> **原句 13-1**（Fosse）: I've been to **Quaker meetings**, but I **don't like organizations**. I'm an **anarchist** in that way; it's a **paradox**, since I'm also a **Catholic**.

- **Quaker meetings**：**贵格会聚会**。
- **anarchist**：**无政府主义者**。
- **paradox**：**悖论**——**一个"无政府主义者"同时也是"天主教徒"**——**极其矛盾**。

> **原句 13-2**（Fosse）: But I'm still **very close to Quakerism**, which, **perhaps**, is an **extreme form of Protestantism**.

- **very close to Quakerism**：**仍然很接近贵格会**。
- **extreme form of Protestantism**：**极端的基督教新教形式**。

> **原句 13-3**（Fosse）: You **take away everything in the Catholic tradition** and in the **mass**, and then, **Catholicism and Quakerism can meet**. **Where do they meet?** They meet in the **mystery of faith**. That's at the **center of Catholicism**, and it's at the **center of Quakerism**, which **places the inner light of the Catholic vision inside the human being**.

- **take away everything in...**：**把……里的一切都拿走**——**剥离所有仪式**。
- **mass**：**弥撒**（天主教的核心仪式）。
- **mystery of faith**：**信仰的神秘**（天主教弥撒仪式中的一个特定术语）。
- **places the inner light of the Catholic vision inside the human being**：**把天主教愿景的"内在之光"放在人的内心**——**贵格会把天主教的超越拉入人的内在**。

- **段落逻辑：** **最精彩的综合**——Fosse 揭示**天主教与贵格会如何相遇**：**剥离所有仪式后，两者在"信仰的神秘"上汇合**。贵格会把天主教的超越性**内化**为"内在之光"。**这句话是 Fosse 神学美学的浓缩**。

### 第 14 段：挪威贵格会史

> **原句 14-1**（Emre）: Jon, what is the **history of** Norwegian Quakerism?

> **原句 14-2**（Fosse）: The Quakers **came to** Norway from England. In my family, my **grandfather belonged to** a group of Quakers in the **southwest**.

- **belonged to**：**属于**。
- **southwest**：**西南部**（挪威西南部 = 卑尔根一带，Fosse 的故乡地区）。

> **原句 14-3**（Fosse）: It's always been a **very small community**, which was **part of the problem for me**; there are **only something like a hundred and fifty** organized Quakers in this country. It's **a bit too intimate**.

- **very small community**：**很小的共同体**。
- **part of the problem for me**：**对我来说是部分问题**（太小，难以融入）。
- **only something like a hundred and fifty**：**大约只有一百五十人**。
- **a bit too intimate**：**太亲密**——反讽："intimate" 通常褒义，此处**太小到让人不适**。

> **原句 14-4**（Fosse）: **That's the great thing about the Catholic Church**, which has a **long, diverse tradition** in Norway.

- **the great thing about**：**……的妙处**。
- **long, diverse tradition**：**悠久而多元的传统**。

> **原句 14-5**（Fosse）: The churches are **full**, not because of a few **Norwegian converts** like myself, but because of **immigrants from Poland and the Philippines**.

- **the churches are full**：**教堂满了**。
- **Norwegian converts**：**改宗挪威人**。
- **immigrants from Poland and the Philippines**：**来自波兰和菲律宾的移民**——**挪威天主教的主体**（**极具当代性的细节**）。

> **原句 14-6**（Fosse）: They **belong to a worldwide movement**, with a **history that they pretend goes back to St Peter**. **It's a story**, but it's **their story**.

- **worldwide movement**：**世界性运动**。
- **history that they pretend goes back to St Peter**：**他们假装可以追溯到圣彼得的历史**（圣彼得传统上被认为是罗马教会的首位主教）。
- **it's a story, but it's their story**：**这是个故事，但那是他们的故事**——**承认教会史是叙事，但尊重其叙事权力**。

> **原句 14-7**（Fosse）: **If you join a community, you believe its stories**, even if they are, in a sense, **myths**.

- **believe its stories**：**相信它的故事**。
- **myths**：**神话**——**承认教会叙事的神话性质**。

- **段落逻辑：** **共同体的大小问题**——贵格会太小（150 人），**太亲密**；天主教太宏大（波兰、菲律宾移民填满教堂）。**Fosse 选择天主教**，是因为它**是一个"世界性运动"，有自己的叙事，即使这些叙事是"神话"**。**"你加入一个共同体，你就相信它的故事"**——这是对**信仰与共同体关系的精炼表述**。

### 第 15 段：Robinson 谈 Jonathan Edwards 与"内在之光"

> **原句 15-1**（Emre）: Marilynne, how does Jon's **description of the inner light** of Quakerism and Catholicism **strike you**? It reminds me of the **revivalist preacher Jonathan Edwards**, who has been an **important figure for you**.

- **strike you**：**如何触动你**。
- **revivalist preacher**：**复兴主义牧师**。
- **Jonathan Edwards**：**乔纳森·爱德华兹**（1703–1758，美国殖民时期最重要的牧师/神学家/哲学家）。

> **原句 15-2**（Robinson）: Edwards **wrote an essay called** 'A **Divine and Supernatural Light**', which is an **exaltation** to a **higher experience** of light. He's **talking about seeing light** and **understanding that it's an emanation of** something **more exalted**, which **has to be apprehended by human beings** to **have the meaning that it has**.

- **Divine and Supernatural Light**：《神圣的与超自然的光》（Edwards 1732 年的重要论文）。
- **exaltation**：**颂扬**。
- **higher experience of light**：**对光的高层次体验**。
- **emanation of**：**……的流出**（神学术语——神圣之光流溢为世界之光）。
- **more exalted**：**更高贵的**。
- **has to be apprehended**：**必须被领会**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Robinson 的对应**——她把自己与 Edwards 的"**神圣之光**"传统**对应** Fosse 的"**内在之光**"传统。**两位思想家隔着三个世纪对话**。

### 第 16 段：Robinson——会众制传统与天主教

> **原句 16-1**（Robinson）: I come from the **Congregationalist tradition**, which is **extremely Protestant**, **very near Quakerism**.

- **Congregationalist tradition**：**会众制传统**（新教的一个派系，每个会众自治，不像主教制自上而下）。
- **extremely Protestant**：**极度的新教**。
- **very near Quakerism**：**非常接近贵格会**。

- **truly is**：**真正是的**。
- **political urgency**：**政治紧迫性**。

> **原句 27-2**（Robinson）: The **problem is to defend** Christianity, to defend religion. The idea that religion has been **claimed by** people who **cut off the aid to the hungry masses of the world** and **called themselves Christian**.

- **defend Christianity**：**为基督教辩护**。
- **claimed by**：**被……宣称占有**。
- **cut off the aid to the hungry masses of the world**：**切断对世界饥饿大众援助**（指特朗普政府削减对外援助的政策）。
- **called themselves Christian**：**自称基督徒**。

> **原句 27-3**（Robinson）: You have to **defend Christianity from** **associations with** people that will **abuse it**. **For the sake of** most people who, at this point, have **very little independent experience of religion**, you have to **tell them that** the **great moral energies** **behind the culture** do **not endorse** **cutting off food aid**. They **don't endorse the impoverishment of** poor or **irrational hostility**.

- **defend Christianity from associations with**：**保护基督教免与……关联**。
- **abuse it**：**滥用它**。
- **for the sake of**：**为了**。
- **very little independent experience of religion**：**几乎没有独立的宗教体验**。
- **great moral energies behind the culture**：**文化背后的伟大道德力量**。
- **do not endorse**：**不认同**。
- **impoverishment of the poor**：**使穷人更穷**。
- **irrational hostility**：**非理性的敌意**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Robinson 谈当代政治中的宗教滥用**——**右翼政客自称基督徒，却切断粮食援助、制造敌意**。她必须**"为基督教辩护"**，告诉那些**几乎没有独立宗教体验**的人：**真正的基督教传统不认同这些**。这是**宗教人文主义在政治上的直接应用**。

### 第 28 段：Fosse——基督教与权力

> **原句 28-1**（Fosse）: Christianity **became connected to** the **political powers** in the **Roman Empire**. That was **how it became a world religion**. And that **connection has remained**. But it **goes back**. If you **go back to the roots of Christianity**, it **isn't political** in the sense of **power struggle** at all.

- **became connected to**：**与……建立了联系**。
- **political powers in the Roman Empire**：**罗马帝国的政治权力**。
- **how it became a world religion**：**它如何成为世界宗教的**。
- **connection has remained**：**这种联系一直保留**。
- **goes back**：**追溯到**。
- **roots of Christianity**：**基督教的根源**。
- **isn't political in the sense of power struggle**：**在"权力斗争"意义上不是政治的**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Fosse 的历史视角**：基督教**在罗马帝国与政治权力结盟**，因此成为世界宗教；这种**政教联盟一直延续至今**。但**回到根源**，基督教**本身不是政治的**。

### 第 29 段：Robinson——政治即关怀

> **原句 29-1**（Robinson）: The **politics** **unfolds as** the **care that we take of one another**. That's what it **ultimately comes down to** – **how much latitude we give each other**, **how much opportunity to flourish we give each other**.

- **politics unfolds as**：**政治展开为**。
- **the care that we take of one another**：**我们彼此关怀**。
- **ultimately comes down to**：**归根结底是**。
- **how much latitude we give each other**：**我们给彼此多少宽容**。
- **how much opportunity to flourish we give each other**：**我们给彼此多少繁荣的机会**。

> **原句 29-2**（Robinson）: That's a **religious question**, because we're talking about the **image of God**. If you **see people insulted and deprived over and over again**, you know that you're **seeing something that is both political and religious** in the **most negative sense of both words**.

- **religious question**：**宗教问题**。
- **image of God**：**上帝的形象**（呼应第 21 段）。
- **see people insulted and deprived over and over again**：**看到人们不断被侮辱、被剥夺**。
- **both political and religious in the most negative sense of both words**：**在最负面意义上既是政治的也是宗教的**——**政治与宗教的负面统一**。

> **原句 29-3**（Robinson）: The idea that you **actually can separate them** is **kind of amazing to me**. We're told **very explicitly** in the **Bible** about **lending and giving and feeding**. **Politics is the method through which** these things **are done or not done**.

- **actually can separate them**：**真的能把它们分开**。
- **kind of amazing to me**：**让我觉得不可思议**。
- **very explicitly**：**非常明确地**。
- **lending and giving and feeding**：**借贷、给予、喂食**——**《圣经》中具体的社会行为**。
- **Politics is the method through which**：**政治是……的手段**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Robinson 的政治神学**：**政治 = 我们彼此关怀的方式**——**给彼此多少宽容、多少繁荣的机会**。这**是宗教问题**（因为它关乎"上帝的形象"）。**"政治与宗教无法分开"**——**《圣经》明确讲到借贷、给予、喂食，政治就是这些行为发生或不发生的手段**。

### 第 30 段：Fosse——文学非政治教条

> **原句 30-1**（Fosse）: But if **literature becomes political**, if it has a **doctrine**, then it **becomes bad literature**, **like in social realism** that was very popular in Norway, in the **1970s**.

- **literature becomes political**：**文学变成政治的**。
- **doctrine**：**教义**（呼应第 19 段）。
- **bad literature**：**糟糕的文学**。
- **social realism**：**社会现实主义**（1970 年代挪威流行的文学流派——**Fosse 明确点名批判**）。

> **原句 30-2**（Fosse）: If you try to **adapt literature to a political intention**, then it **ends up as bad writing**.

- **adapt literature to**：**使文学服务于**。
- **political intention**：**政治意图**。
- **ends up as bad writing**：**最终沦为糟糕的写作**。

> **原句 30-3**（Fosse）: **Literature has to be there for its own sake**. But, **by being there for its own sake**, it **also has ethical and political stakes**.

- **for its own sake**：**为了自身**（为艺术而艺术）。
- **ethical and political stakes**：**伦理与政治的赌注**——**文学不为政治而存在，但因其"品质"自然具有伦理与政治维度**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Fosse 的悖论立场**：**文学不为政治而存在**（否则就是"社会现实主义"式的糟糕写作）；**但文学为了自身而存在时，自然具有伦理与政治的赌注**。**这是对 Robinson "政治与宗教无法分开"的回应**：**文学与政治也分不开，但不是"有意为之"的**。

### 第 31 段：Robinson——不写政治的小说

> **原句 31-1**（Robinson）: Well, I **don't write the political in my fiction**. It's **not political**, but I **write political things** so that that **burden doesn't fall on my fiction**.

- **don't write the political in my fiction**：**不写政治进入我的小说**。
- **write political things**：**写政治的东西**（指她作为**公共知识分子**写的散文、评论）。
- **so that that burden doesn't fall on my fiction**：**这样负担就不会落在我的小说上**——**Robinson 的策略：把政治担子由散文来承担，让小说保持"纯粹"**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Robinson 的策略**——**散文承担政治，小说保持纯粹**。这是一条**非常实用的文学生存策略**。

### 第 32 段：Fosse——所有艺术皆政治

> **原句 32-1**（Fosse）: But **all art is political**. If it's **good art**, it's political **because of its aesthetic quality**. This also **gives it an ethical quality**, which **has political implications**.

- **all art is political**：**所有艺术都是政治的**——**最绝对的判断**。
- **because of its aesthetic quality**：**因为其美学品质**。
- **gives it an ethical quality**：**赋予它伦理品质**。
- **has political implications**：**具有政治意涵**——**品质 → 伦理 → 政治**的三层传递。

> **原句 32-2**（Fosse）: I think that some good writers have been **very bad from a political point of view**, but still, the **writing isn't**. **Knut Hamsun was a Nazi**, no doubt, but he was an **extremely good writer**. You have to **separate a great writer from his political thinking**.

- **very bad from a political point of view**：**从政治角度看很坏**。
- **the writing isn't**：**但写作本身不是**（坏的）。
- **Knut Hamsun**：**克努特·哈姆生**（1859–1952，挪威作家，1920 诺贝尔文学奖，二战期间支持纳粹）。
- **a Nazi**：**纳粹分子**（**极端的政治污点**）。
- **separate a great writer from his political thinking**：**把伟大作家与其政治思想分开**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Fosse 的立场**：**所有好艺术都是政治的**（因为品质），但**好的写作本身不会因作家的政治立场而变坏**——**哈姆生是纳粹，但他的写作依然是伟大的**。这是**"作品 vs 作者"**的经典命题。

### 第 33 段：Pound / Nietzsche 讨论

> **原句 33-1**（Emre）: Hamsun is the **great Norwegian example**. Marilynne, **who would you say is the classic American example** of a great writer with **terrible politics**? **Pound**?

- **the great Norwegian example**：**伟大的挪威范例**（指 Hamsun）。
- **classic American example**：**经典的美国范例**。
- **terrible politics**：**糟糕的政治**。
- **Pound**：**埃兹拉·庞德**（1885–1972，美国诗人，现代主义大师，二战期间支持法西斯）。

> **原句 33-2**（Robinson）: The writer who **excites my students** and is a **very destructive influence** is **Nietzsche**. He is not a **local boy**, but nevertheless, he **speaks to the sophomore heart**.

- **excites my students**：**激发学生**。
- **very destructive influence**：**极具破坏力的影响**。
- **Nietzsche**：**尼采**（1844–1900，德国哲学家）。
- **local boy**：**本地人**（Nietzsche 是德国人，不是美国人）。
- **speaks to the sophomore heart**：**打动大二学生的心**（sophomore = 大学二年级学生——**Robinson 的幽默**）。

> **原句 33-3**（Robinson）: Nietzsche **encourages a kind of elitism** that young people, **especially people from some degree of privilege** are **happy to adopt**. They're **happy to think that they're excused from morality** that would **teach them any kind of humility**. **Frankly, young people are vulnerable to that**.

- **a kind of elitism**：**一种精英主义**。
- **people from some degree of privilege**：**有一定特权的人**。
- **excused from morality**：**免除了道德**。
- **teach them any kind of humility**：**教他们任何谦卑**。
- **young people are vulnerable to that**：**年轻人对此很脆弱**——**Robinson 对尼采的最尖锐批判**。

> **原句 33-4**（Fosse）: But I think that **lies in** the **tradition of translating the German word Übermensch** into English as 'Superman'. The Nietzsche they are thinking about **isn't the real Nietzsche**. There's a problem with the **translation**.

- **lies in**：**原因在于**。
- **Übermensch**：**超人**（尼采核心概念）。
- **Superman**：**超人**（美国漫画形象——**文化错位的翻译**）。
- **isn't the real Nietzsche**：**不是真正的尼采**。
- **a problem with the translation**：**翻译的问题**——**Fosse 又一次把矛头指向"翻译"（与第 4 段呼应）**。

> **原句 33-5**（Robinson）: Well, I've read enough **just to keep up with** my students. Nietzsche **was a sick man**.

- **just to keep up with**：**仅仅为了跟上**。
- **Nietzsche was a sick man**：**尼采是个病人**（1889 年后精神崩溃，21 年痴呆——**Robinson 的简洁、尖锐的终局评价**）。

- **段落逻辑：** **三位"糟糕政治的好作家"**——Hamsun（纳粹）、Pound（法西斯）、Nietzsche（精英主义）——**构成西方文学史上最著名的"政治污点"谱系**。Robinson 批判尼采对**年轻人**的破坏性影响；Fosse 试图**为尼采辩护**（翻译问题）；Robinson 用最简短的一句"he was a sick man"**终结辩论**——**极具戏剧性**。

### 第 34 段：Robinson——宗教的未来：丰富

> **原句 34-1**（Emre）: Does the **future of literature and religion** **feel like one of plenitude** for each of you?

- **future of literature and religion**：**文学与宗教的未来**。
- **one of plenitude**：**一种丰盈**（plenitude = 丰富、充足）——**"丰盈"是反问"枯竭"**。

> **原句 34-2**（Robinson）: I think that religion is **beautiful** and that it **comes very naturally to human beings**. **You look at the world**, and **everyone's being religious in his or her own way**.

- **religion is beautiful**：**宗教是美丽的**——**最直接的情感表达**。
- **comes very naturally to**：**对……非常自然**。
- **everyone's being religious in his or her own way**：**每个人都在以自己的方式成为宗教的**——**最包容的一句话**。

> **原句 34-3**（Robinson）: My **church is next to a university**. There are **always clusters of churches around universities**, and the **students are attending church**. The **Methodist church** is the **church of the Iowa Writers' Workshop**. And I'm **not a Methodist** and so, I have to **deal with that**.

- **church is next to a university**：**教会就在大学旁边**（**极具当代美国性的细节**——爱荷华大学）。
- **clusters of churches around universities**：**大学周围的教会群**——**学术与信仰的地理亲密**。
- **Methodist church**：**卫理公会**（美国主流新教派）。
- **the church of the Iowa Writers' Workshop**：**爱荷华写作工作坊的教会**——**文学教育体制与教会的地缘重叠**。
- **I'm not a Methodist and so, I have to deal with that**：**我不是卫理公会信徒，所以我得跟这件事打交道**——**Robinson 的幽默**。

> **原句 34-4**（Robinson）: But in any case, it is a **fantastically vast vocabulary of experience**. The **accumulation of it over time** is a **richness added to richness, added to richness**.

- **fantastically vast vocabulary of experience**：**极其丰富的经验词汇**——**"宗教 = 词汇"**。
- **accumulation of it over time**：**随时间的累积**。
- **richness added to richness, added to richness**：**丰富加上丰富，加上丰富**——**三次叠加的修辞**。

> **原句 34-5**（Robinson）: It's **something that human beings have made for themselves**, in the sense that it's a **spontaneous product of human nature**. I think that the **absence of it is an artificial deprivation**. But it will pass.

- **something that human beings have made for themselves**：**人类为自己创造的东西**。
- **spontaneous product of human nature**：**人性的自发产物**。
- **absence of it is an artificial deprivation**：**它的缺失是一种人为的剥夺**——**与第 3 段"文化没有给人们的词汇"呼应**。
- **But it will pass**：**但它会过去**——**反宗教的潮流会过去**（**乐观的预言**）。

- **段落逻辑：** **Robinson 对未来持乐观态度**：**宗教是美丽的、自然的、每个人都以自己的方式在实践**。她举**爱荷华大学的教会群**为例，证明**学术与信仰在地缘上亲密共存**。**"丰富加上丰富，加上丰富"**——**宗教的未来是累积性的丰盈**。

### 第 35 段：Fosse——文学与宗教维度

> **原句 35-1**（Fosse）: I think you **cannot possibly explain life in a scientific or positivistic or materialistic way**. There's a **space for art, and for literature**. It's the **space for religion**.

- **cannot possibly explain life**：**不可能解释生命**。
- **scientific or positivistic or materialistic way**：**科学的或实证主义的或唯物主义的**。
- **space for art, and for literature**：**艺术与文学的空间**。
- **the space for religion**：**宗教的空间**——**三者是同一个空间**。

> **原句 35-2**（Fosse）: I **cannot understand** people who **accept the quality of literature**, but **don't accept that this might have something to do also with the religious dimension of life**.

- **cannot understand**：**无法理解**。
- **accept the quality of literature**：**接受文学的品质**。
- **the religious dimension of life**：**生命的宗教维度**。
- **why this is brilliant**：这是**最精妙的论证**——**如果你承认文学有"品质"，就必须承认这种品质与"宗教维度"有关**。**"品质"再次成为 Fosse 的元概念**。

> **原句 35-3**（Fosse）: I was once **invited to the Vatican to meet the pope**. **Even if the artists don't need the church, the church needs the artists**, and that **has to do with the transcendental dimension of all good writing**.

- **invited to the Vatican to meet the pope**：**曾被邀请去梵蒂冈见教皇**——**最戏剧化的细节**。
- **Even if the artists don't need the church, the church needs the artists**：**即使艺术家不需要教会，教会需要艺术家**——**极其对称的悖论句**。
- **the transcendental dimension of all good writing**：**所有好写作的超越性维度**。

> **原句 35-4**（Fosse）: But **when I was young**, I **hated the Norwegian Lutheran church**. I **left it at sixteen**. As a child, I had **no belief**. I thought it was **pure stupidity**.

- **hated the Norwegian Lutheran church**：**憎恨挪威路德教**。
- **left it at sixteen**：**16 岁离开**。
- **pure stupidity**：**纯粹的愚蠢**——**Fosse 毫不掩饰地承认年轻时的"反信仰"**。

> **原句 35-5**（Fosse）: For a long time, in Europe, in Norway, it was **impossible to be a writer and a believer at the same time**. For **my generation**, it was a kind of **rebellion**. But things **changed with postmodernism** and the **theological turn in French phenomenology**, in books like **Jean-Luc Marion's God Without Being**. I don't think we will **go back to atheism or positivism**. **It isn't possible anymore**.

- **impossible to be a writer and a believer**：**不可能同时是作家和信徒**——**欧洲文学史的常识**。
- **my generation**：**我们这一代**。
- **rebellion**：**反叛**——**不信仰本身就是反叛**。
- **postmodernism**：**后现代主义**。
- **theological turn in French phenomenology**：**法国现象学的神学转向**（20 世纪末法国哲学的重要转向，Marion、Henry、Derrida 等）。
- **Jean-Luc Marion's God Without Being**：**让-吕克·马里翁的《上帝不在存在之中》**（1982 年著作）。
- **It isn't possible anymore**：**再也不可能了**——**Fosse 的预言：欧洲不会再回到无神论**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Fosse 的未来观**：①生命无法被科学/唯物论完全解释，因此有**"宗教的空间"**；②梵蒂冈邀请他为**"教会需要艺术家"**提供证据；③他自己**16 岁憎恨教会，认为信仰是愚蠢**——**但后现代主义与现象学的神学转向改变了这一切**；④**"我们不可能再回到无神论"**——**最坚定的预言**。

### 第 36 段：Robinson——青年时期的无神论

> **原句 36-1**（Emre）: Marilynne, did you ever have a **youthful lapse of faith**?

- **youthful lapse of faith**：**年轻时的信仰动摇**（lapse = 失误、 lapse of faith = 信仰一时的动摇）。

> **原句 36-2**（Robinson）: The only moment I had **of that kind** was when I was still **in high school**, and my **brother was at college**. **Whenever he came home**, he **brought all his books**. And I was there **reading all the books** that he **brought home from college** and I **read myself into atheism**.

- **of that kind**：**那种**（指信仰动摇）。
- **in high school**：**高中时期**。
- **my brother was at college**：**我哥哥在上大学**。
- **read myself into atheism**：**把自己读成了无神论者**——**文学性极强的表达**：**无神论是"读"出来的**。

> **原句 36-3**（Robinson）: It was as if a **ceiling came down on my head**, and **walls came in**. The **reduction in scale** of my **imagination**, of my **existence**, was **so radical**.

- **a ceiling came down on my head**：**天花板落到了我的头上**。
- **walls came in**：**墙壁合拢**。
- **reduction in scale**：**尺度的缩减**——**"无神论 = 世界的收缩"**。
- **so radical**：**如此剧烈**。

> **原句 36-4**（Robinson）: I would have **run screaming from** this **little room** if it had been an **actual little room**. It was a **very vivid experience** for me, the feeling of **loss and depletion**.

- **run screaming from**：**尖叫着跑离**。
- **an actual little room**：**一间真实的小房间**（**隐喻**：她的无神论像被关进小房间）。
- **loss and depletion**：**失去与枯竭**。

> **原句 36-5**（Robinson）: I'm not talking about whether I felt the **absence of some sort of protection or assurance or anything**, it was the **closing down of the implications of experience**. I just **couldn't stand it**.

- **absence of some sort of protection or assurance**：**失去某种保护或安慰**。
- **closing down of the implications of experience**：**经验意涵的关闭**——**无神论关闭了经验的深层意义**。
- **couldn't stand it**：**无法忍受**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Robinson 青年时期"读进了无神论"**——**哥哥带来的大学书把她带进了无神论**。她用**极其感官化的语言**描述无神论的感受：**"天花板落下，墙壁合拢，尺度剧烈缩减"**——**无神论 = 世界的收缩、经验意涵的关闭**。**"我尖叫着想逃出这个小房间"**——**这是全篇最动人的私人见证**。

### 第 37 段：Robinson——回归的路径

> **原句 37-1**（Emre）: How did you **reverse out of** the **atheism that you had read yourself into**?

- **reverse out of**：**反转出来**。

> **原句 37-2**（Robinson）: My religion when I was young was **basically a matter of** **thinking I was very much contained in my own experience**. My **family was not particularly religious at all**. It was like **something that occurred to me**, that was **real to me out of my own experience**, which meant that **when I realized how antagonistic this worldview seemed to me**, I could **put it aside**. I could **find my way back to my habitual way of thinking and perceiving**.

- **contained in my own experience**：**被包裹在自己的经验之中**。
- **family was not particularly religious**：**家里并不特别虔诚**。
- **something that occurred to me**：**某种向我显现的东西**。
- **real to me out of my own experience**：**从自己的经验中对我来说真实的**。
- **how antagonistic this worldview seemed to me**：**这种世界观对我而言是多么敌对**（"这种世界观"指无神论世界观）。
- **put it aside**：**把它放到一边**。
- **find my way back to my habitual way of thinking and perceiving**：**找回我惯常的思考与感知方式**。

- **段落逻辑：** Robinson 的**回归路径**：她的信仰**不是来自家庭**，而是**从自己的经验中"显现"**的；当她意识到无神论世界观是**"敌对的"**，就把它"放到一边"，**找回自己惯常的思考与感知方式**——**她的信仰回归是"经验自发性"的胜利**。

### 第 38 段：Fosse——教会减损生命

> **原句 38-1**（Fosse）: For me, it was the **other way around**. I felt that the **church**, its **doctrine**, it was **reducing life too much** and **throwing away what gave life value** for me.

- **the other way around**：**相反**。
- **the church, its doctrine**：**教会、它的教义**。
- **reducing life too much**：**把生命缩减得太多**。
- **throwing away what gave life value**：**抛弃了使生命有价值之物**。

- **段落逻辑：** **Fosse 与 Robinson 的"青年反信仰"方向相反**：Robinson 是**从信仰滑向无神论**（"读进了"）；Fosse 是**从教会逃离**（教会"减损生命"）。**两种不同的反信仰路径**。

### 第 39 段：Fosse——回归教会（全篇收束）

> **原句 39-1**（Emre）: When did you **come back**?

- **come back**：**回来**。

> **原句 39-2**（Fosse）: It was **my own writing**. I **didn't know where it came from**. That **opened up another dimension**, and **after being in that dimension**, I **needed to call it God**. **Or, at least, God had something to do with that dimension**.

- **my own writing**：**我自己的写作**——**写作使他回归**。
- **didn't know where it came from**：**不知道从哪来**（**呼应 Robinson 第 25 段的"我不知道这些声音从哪来"**——**两位作家的立场在此处汇合**）。
- **opened up another dimension**：**打开了另一个维度**。
- **after being in that dimension, I needed to call it God**：**身处那个维度后，我需要称它为"上帝"**——**写作让他重新命名那"不可言说的东西"**。
- **or, at least, God had something to do with that dimension**：**或者至少，上帝与那个维度有关**——**谦逊的保留**。

> **原句 39-3**（Fosse）: And then, I **needed company from other strange people**.

- **needed company from other strange people**：**需要其他"奇怪的人"的陪伴**——**回到共同体**。

> **原句 39-4**（Fosse）: I've always been a kind of **religious writer**, but it's only **around fifteen years ago** that I became a **member of a church**.

- **religious writer**：**宗教作家**——**从写作开始就是**。
- **around fifteen years ago**：**大约 15 年前**（即约 2010 年左右，Fosse 加入天主教）。
- **member of a church**：**教会成员**。

> **原句 39-5**（Fosse）: I **don't have the belief of a child**. I have the **belief of an elderly man**, or a **middle aged one**.

- **don't have the belief of a child**：**没有孩子般的信仰**（"孩子般的信仰"= **天真的、未质疑的信仰**——《马太福音》18:3 中"你们若不变成小孩子的样子"）。
- **belief of an elderly man, or a middle aged one**：**老年人的信仰，或中年人的**——**经历过怀疑、失去、回归后的信仰**——**比"孩子般信仰"更深的信仰**。

- **段落逻辑：** **全篇收束**——Fosse 的**回归路径是"写作 → 另一个维度 → 需要称它为上帝 → 需要奇怪的同伴 → 加入教会"**。最后的金句："**我没有孩子般的信仰，我有老年人的信仰，或中年人的**"——**经历过怀疑与失去后的信仰**，比天真信仰更深。这一句与 Robinson 的**"读进无神论"**形成**完美的呼应**。

## 段落逻辑（全文）

**大结构**：**三幕制**——

- **第一幕（段 1–5）：Robinson 的圣经课**——场景铺陈 + Robinson 的教学目的 + 翻译批判（vengeance ≠ vindicator）+ 古/新约的区分。
- **第二幕（段 6–17）：否定神学 vs 肯定神学**——Fosse 的否定神学 + "最坚定的信徒是不可知论者" + Robinson 的"可接近性" + Fosse 的创造者/受造物二分 + 宗教人文主义 + Eckhart × 贵格会 + "上帝通过寂静说话" + 守护被误读的传统 + "只是发现金子"。
- **第三幕（段 18–39）：艺术、政治、未来**——从柏拉图到启蒙 + "如果你写教义，你就是糟糕的作家" + 品质即超越 + 更深的看见 + 灵魂-身体之谜 + 艺术中的灵肉合一 + 倾听的实践 + "写作的必然性" + 右翼的"宗教" + 政治即关怀 + 文学非政治教条 + Hamsun/Pound/Nietzsche + 宗教的未来 + 青年反信仰 + 回归。

**核心张力**：
- **Fosse 的否定神学 vs Robinson 的肯定神学**（第 6–10 段）
- **Robinson 的政治神学 vs Fosse 的"文学非政治"**（第 27–32 段）
- **Robinson 从信仰读进无神论 vs Fosse 从教会逃离**（第 36–39 段）

**关键反转**：
1. 段 7："**最坚定的信徒是不可知论者**"（悖论）
2. 段 11："现代人文主义反宗教 = 人为的必然性"（**核心批判**）
3. 段 19："**如果你写教义，你就是糟糕的作家**"（**最锋利**）
4. 段 33：Robinson 用 "**he was a sick man**" 终结尼采辩论（**戏剧性收束**）
5. 段 39："**我没有孩子般的信仰**"（**全篇最成熟的一句**）

## 长难句专项

### 长难句 1（第 2 段·编者按的密集铺陈）
> The rippling, incantatory style of Fosse's *Septology* and *The Shining* is the aesthetic correlative to his negative theology; his book-length sentences circle around what God is not in order to access an ineffable, invisible divine reality.

**分析**：分号前后两个分句。前句用 **aesthetic correlative**（美学对应物）把**文学风格**与**神学**挂钩；后句用 **what God is not**（否定神学的核心表达）+ **in order to** 目的从句。
**翻译要点**："Fosse《七部曲》和《发光》那涟漪式的、诵念式的风格，是他否定神学的美学对应物；他那书长般的句子环绕着'上帝不是什么'，以便抵达那不可言说、不可见的神圣实在。"

### 长难句 2（第 11 段·Robinson 批判现代人文主义）
> We've created an artificial inevitability around the modern idea that there's something appropriate in birthing nihilism or atheism or agnosticism out of a tradition that is profoundly religious.

**分析**：嵌套定语从句——**"the modern idea that..."** 是主句的宾语，**"that is profoundly religious"** 修饰 tradition，**"birthing... out of..."** 是从 tradition 中"催生"。
**翻译要点**："我们围绕这样一个现代观念制造了一种人为的必然性：从一个深深植根于宗教的传统中催生出虚无主义、无神论或不可知论——这似乎是恰当的。"

### 长难句 3（第 13 段·Fosse 的天主教×贵格会综合）
> You take away everything in the Catholic tradition and in the mass, and then, Catholicism and Quakerism can meet. Where do they meet? They meet in the mystery of faith. That's at the center of Catholicism, and it's at the center of Quakerism, which places the inner light of the Catholic vision inside the human being.

**分析**：条件句（You take away...）+ 设问（Where do they meet?）+ 同位语从句（which places...）。
**翻译要点**："你把天主教传统和弥撒中的一切都拿走，然后，天主教与贵格会就可以相遇。它们在何处相遇？在'信仰的神秘'之中。那是天主教的中心，也是贵格会的中心，贵格会把天主教愿景的'内在之光'置于人的内心。"

### 长难句 4（第 21 段·Robinson 的核心命题）
> The joy of being religious is that you discipline yourself in seeing more deeply than you would otherwise see.

**分析**：**"than you would otherwise see"** 是省略比较——**"比你不这样做时看得更深"**。
**翻译要点**："做宗教徒的快乐，在于你训练自己比你不这样做时看得更深。"——**这句的优美在于"otherwise"这一省略结构**。

### 长难句 5（第 34 段·"丰富"的三次叠加）
> The accumulation of it over time is a richness added to richness, added to richness.

**分析**：**"added to richness, added to richness"**——**两次并列的叠加**（三次"richness"），是**修辞上的递增**。
**翻译要点**："它随时间的累积，是丰富之上加丰富，再加之丰富。"——**三个"丰富"不能省**。

## 精读结束总结

**核心命题**：两位伟大作家——一位美国人（Robinson，肯定神学、宗教人文主义），一位挪威人（Fosse，否定神学、倾听寂静）——围绕**神、艺术、政治**展开的对谈，揭示了：

1. **神学的现代形态**：Robinson 的"肯定神学"强调**上帝之言已说过、可接近**；Fosse 的"否定神学"强调**上帝不可言说、通过寂静说话**——两者并非对立，而是**互补**。
2. **文学与神学**：Fosse 的立场——**"如果你写教义，你就是糟糕的作家"**——**文学不是教义，但好文学自然指向神圣**（"品质"）。Robinson 的立场——**"你看得有多深"**是**大问题**，**宗教训练人看得更深**。
3. **政治与宗教**：Robinson 认为**政治与宗教无法分开**（"政治 = 彼此关怀"）；Fosse 承认**好文学天然具有政治维度**（因为品质），但**反对有意为之**（社会现实主义）。
4. **翻译与误读**：**"vengeance"误译为"vindicator"**、**"Übermensch"误译为"Superman"**——**一个词的误译能扭曲一代人的上帝观或哲学观**。
5. **回归的路径**：Robinson **从信仰"读进"无神论，又因无神论是"敌对的"而回归**；Fosse **从教会逃离，又因写作让他发现"另一个维度"而回归**。**两种不同的反信仰与回归路径**。

**最核心的三个金句**：
1. （Fosse）"The strongest believers are the agnostics, because they don't know what God is."
2. （Robinson）"The joy of being religious is that you discipline yourself in seeing more deeply than you would otherwise see."
3. （Fosse）"I don't have the belief of a child. I have the belief of an elderly man, or a middle aged one."
