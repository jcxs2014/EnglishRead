# The God Dimension（信仰的维度）— 精读
> 源：Granta｜Marilynne Robinson × Jon Fosse 访谈（Merve Emre 主持）｜篇幅 24,735 字｜URL: https://granta.com/the-god-dimension/
> 体裁：思想访谈｜入选理由：文学与信仰的对话，宗教哲学术语丰富

---

## 一、概述
- **场合**：Marilynne Robinson 在挪威国家图书馆做 **Fosse Lecture**（Fosse 2023 获诺奖后设立的年度讲座）；访谈于讲座前一日在 Fosse 常去的餐厅 Kaffistova 进行。
- **三位参与者**：
  - **Merve Emre**：主持人（作家/学者）。
  - **Marilynne Robinson**：美国小说家（*Gilead*、*Housekeeping* 作者）， Congregationalist/福音派传统，为梵蒂冈报纸《L'Osservatore Romano》写经文诠释。
  - **Jon Fosse**：挪威作家、2023 诺奖得主，Nynorsk 捍卫者，15 年前皈依天主教，深受**德国神秘主义（Meister Eckhart）**与**挪威贵格会（Quakerism）**影响。
- **核心议题**：信仰与文学的关系；"报复之神" vs "爱之神"；**否定神学（negative theology）**；内心之光（inner light）；宗教与人本主义；艺术与超越性。
- **写作亮点**：①高密度术语（apophatic / negative theology / vindicator / positivism 等）②Robinson 与 Fosse 在多个议题上**默契互补**③大量**跨文化引证**（Eckhart、Tauler、Edwards、Pico、Kafka）。

## 二、逐节精读

### 1. 开场：Robinson 谈"庆祝的一面"（引《箴言》《约伯记》《诗篇》）
> "I try to give them **basic terms and concepts** simply so they can **have an approach**. The **creation** was the first **category** that I talked about. I **use things like** the **passage** in **Proverbs** that **describes** the **spirit** that is with God at creation, and the **poem at the end of Job**, in which God says, 'Where were you', and **psalms** that **celebrate creation**. I think that the **celebratory side** of the Christian tradition is very much **lost**; it's **thought of as being punitive**. The idea of understanding the **amazing beauty of being** is **intrinsic to the literature** and part of what people **have a right to**. The culture has not **given them the terms to express it**."

- **basic terms and concepts**：基础术语与概念。
- **have an approach**：有一个（入门的）途径。approach = 途径、方式。
- **Proverbs / Job / psalms**：《箴言》/《约伯记》/《诗篇》（旧约书名）。
- **passage**：（圣经）段落、经文。
- **spirit**：圣灵/灵。
- **celebrate creation**：赞美/庆祝创造（神创世界）。
- **celebratory side**：庆祝/赞美的一面。
- **lost**：被遗失、被遗忘。
- **thought of as being punitive**：被认为是一种惩罚性的东西。punitive = 惩罚的。
- **amazing beauty of being**：存在（万物）惊人的美。being = 存在。
- **intrinsic to the literature**：内在于文学之中。intrinsic = 内在的。
- **have a right to**：有权享有。
- **given them the terms to express it**：给他们表达它（之美）的术语。
- **为什么这样写**：Robinson 开宗明义——**基督教传统中"赞美创造之美"的一面被丢了，只剩"惩罚"的意象**。她教学生，是想**还给他们"表达这种美"的词汇**。这是全篇的**第一论点**。

### 2. "vengeance" 是误译（本段极重要）
> "One of the things that is very much **on my mind** doing all this is that the **standard translations** of the Bible **make God into a monster**. He's **jealous**. He's **vengeful**. I **bring up** the fact that 'vengeance' is a **bad translation**. If you **look at** the **Vulgate**, it says 'vindicator'– 'to vindicate', which means **not only to assign innocence**, but also to **assign guilt**. It isn't vengeance. You see people like our **horrible Secretary of War** **using the word** vengeance and **feeling that it is sanctified** by a **bad translation**. I think a lot of what **distances people from religion** **are** the **inappropriate words** that have become **identified with** the text."

- **on my mind**：（一直）放在心上。
- **standard translations**：通行译本。
- **make God into a monster**：把上帝变成了一个怪物。
- **jealous**：嫉妒的。
- **vengeful /ˈvendʒfʊl/**：报复的、报复心强的。vengeance = 报复。
- **bring up**：提出、提及。
- **Vulgate /ˈvʌlɡɪt/**：**武加大译本**（拉丁文圣经通行本，中世纪教会官方版本）——**极重要的神学术语**。
- **vindicator /ˈvɪndɪkeɪtər/**：**平反者、昭雪者**。to vindicate = 证明无罪、平反、伸张正义。
- **assign innocence / assign guilt**：判定无罪 / 判定有罪。
- **horrible Secretary of War**：可憎的战争部长（Robinson 对当时美国国防部长的批评）。
- **feeling that it is sanctified**：觉得它（"报复"一词）被（圣经）神圣化了。sanctify = 使神圣。
- **distances people from religion**：使人们远离宗教。
- **inappropriate words**：不恰当的用词。
- **identified with**：与……挂钩、被等同于。
- **为什么这样写**：**这是全篇最有冲击力的论点之一**——"vengeance（报复）"其实是**误译**，拉丁文 Vulgate 原文是 "vindicator（平反者）"。**一个词的误译，让上帝从"平反者"变成了"报复者"，进而让"可憎的战争部长"借这个误译去"神圣化"他们的暴行**。这是**语言塑造信仰**的极致例证。
- **语法**：`a lot of what distances people from religion are...` —— **what 引导的主语从句**，what = the things that。

### 3. Robinson：最大的诫命是"爱主"
> "The **greatest commandment** in **both Testaments** is 'Love the Lord.' Yet **out of traditionalism and sloppiness**, we **create an idea of God** that's **not very lovable**, so that there's a **transgression against** the **first commandment** **before anything else is launched on**."

- **greatest commandment**：最大的诫命（耶稣所说"你要尽心尽性爱主你的神"）。
- **both Testaments**：《旧约》与《新约》两部。Testament = 约。
- **out of traditionalism and sloppiness**：出于对传统的盲从与马虎。sloppiness = 马虎、草率。
- **create an idea of God**：（人为地）构造出一种对上帝的观念。
- **not very lovable**：不太可爱（= 难以去爱）。
- **transgression against**：违背、触犯。transgression = 违逆、罪。
- **before anything else is launched on**：在一切（其他罪）开始之前（就已经先犯了"不爱主"这一条）。launch on = 着手做。
- **为什么这样写**：**逻辑递进**：最大诫命是"爱主"→ 但传统和马虎让我们造出一个"不可爱"的上帝 → 于是**在犯任何其他罪之前，我们先得不到"爱"这一诫**。Robinson 把**翻译误读 → 信仰疏离**的因果链说得极清楚。

### 4. Fosse：否定神学（全篇最关键术语）
> "The Old Testament talks about this **vengeful God**. The **New Testament** talks about the **love of God**. The **only thing** said about God in the New Testament is that **God is love**, **nothing else**. In the **European tradition**, you have an **apophatic theology**, a **negative theology** in which you **cannot say anything about** God. He's **hidden behind concepts**. He is **outside time and space**. Our **human language** is **far too limited** to say anything **wise** about him, anything **worth saying**. **That is my position**. I **cannot say anything about** God, except for that I **believe in God**. What I **mean when I say**, 'I believe in God', is that there's **something in my life** that is the **truth**. It's **not an intellectual truth**. It has **more to do with** a **presence**, a way of **experiencing** life and art, than with any **idea or concept or attribute** that you **imagine as** God. The **strongest believers** are the **agnostics**, because they **don't know what God is**. To me that's a **very fair and wise position**."

- **vengeful God / love of God**：报复之神 / 爱之神。
- **the only thing said... is that... nothing else**：唯一被说的……就是……别无其他。
- **European tradition**：欧洲（神学）传统。
- **apophatic theology**：**否定神学 / 不可言说神学**——**核心术语**。指"只能通过说'上帝不是什么'来接近上帝，而不能说'上帝是什么'"。
- **negative theology**：同上（apophatic 的英文说法）。
- **cannot say anything about God**：对上帝的任何属性都无法言说。
- **hidden behind concepts**：隐藏在概念背后（不可被概念捕捉）。
- **outside time and space**：超越时空。
- **far too limited**：太过有限。
- **wise**：明智的、智慧的。
- **worth saying**：值得说出口的。
- **That is my position**：这就是我的立场。
- **believe in God**：信上帝。
- **not an intellectual truth**：不是（纯粹）智识上的真理。
- **has more to do with A than B**：与 A 的关系，多于与 B 的关系。
- **presence**：临在、在场（一种"在其中的感受"，非概念）。
- **experiencing**：体验。
- **attribute**：（上帝的）属性。
- **imagine as**：把……想象成。
- **agnostics /æɡˈnɒstɪks/**：**不可知论者**（认为上帝是否存在无法被认知的人）。
- **a very fair and wise position**：一个非常公允而明智的立场。
- **为什么这样写**：**Fosse 的神学立场**。他属于欧洲"否定神学"传统——**越承认"我不知道上帝是什么"，越是真正的信徒**（agnostic = 最强的信徒）。这是**反教条、反概念**的立场，也是他**写作中"沉默"美学**的根基。
- **对比**：Robinson 强调"爱"（肯定的一面），Fosse 强调"不可说"（否定的一面）——两位在神学上**互补**。

### 5. Robinson：可亲近的上帝 vs Fosse 的"不可言说"
> "**A great deal has been said about** God. The **approachability** of God is **related to the traditions of language** that **surround** him. **Believing in God** is **believing in the benevolence** of God. It's **believing in the goodness of creation** and in certain **primary things** that are said, and that we **accept as** truth. Then there are, for example, the ideas of God as **vengeful** and **jealous** that **stand in the way of** this **deeper perception** that one **might have about** the **nature of God**."

- **a great deal has been said about**：关于……人们已经说了很多。
- **approachability**：可亲近性、可亲性。
- **related to**：与……相关。
- **surround him**：环绕着（上帝）的（语言传统）。
- **benevolence /bəˈnɛvələns/**：**仁慈、善心**。benevolent = 仁慈的。
- **goodness of creation**：创造的善（神创世界是善的）。
- **primary things**：最根本的事。
- **accept as truth**：接受为真理。
- **stand in the way of**：阻碍、妨碍。
- **deeper perception**：更深的领悟。
- **might have about**：可能（对……）拥有的。
- **nature of God**：上帝的本性。
- **为什么这样写**：Robinson **温和地回应 Fosse**：她承认"已有很多话说过"，但**上帝的可亲性取决于围绕祂的语言传统**——如果语言传统里满是"报复、嫉妒"，就会**挡住**人对上帝本性更深的领悟。**语言（翻译）决定信仰体验**——这是两人**共同的底层信念**。

### 6. Fosse：艾克哈特与贵格会（"内心之光"）
> "The **thinker** who has **taught me the most** – the thinker, who, in a way, **made me an organized Catholic** – was **Meister Eckhardt** (应为 Eckhart)。He **lived** in the **thirteenth century** in Germany, and in France. The way he **talked about** God was very **provocative** at the time, but it was **very close to another influence** in my life: **Norwegian Quakerism**. The **Norwegian Quakers** had this **concept of the inner light** that **emanated from** inside of you, which **brought you closest to** God. To **hear His voice**, you **had to listen to silence**. God **spoke through silence**. The Quakers **sit in a circle**, **keep quiet**, and **try to concentrate on** the **inner light**. If you **feel that you can say** something **good for others**, you **say it**. **Otherwise**, you **keep quiet**."

- **the thinker who has taught me the most**：教我最多的思想家。
- **made me an organized Catholic**：让我成了一个"有组织的天主教徒"（即正式入会）。organized = 入会的、体制内的。
- **Meister Eckhart（艾克哈特）**：**德国神秘主义神学家**（13–14 世纪）——**核心人物**。注：原文误拼为 "Eckhardt"，标准姓是 "Eckhart"。
- **thirteenth century**：十三世纪。
- **provocative**：（当时）令人惊愕的、具有挑衅性的。
- **very close to another influence**：与我生活中的另一个影响非常接近。
- **Norwegian Quakerism**：挪威贵格会（教友派）。
- **concept of the inner light**：内心之光的概念（贵格会核心教义：每人心中都有上帝之光的火花）。
- **emanated from**：从……流出、散发。emanate = 散发、源自。
- **brought you closest to God**：让你最接近上帝。
- **hear His voice**：听见祂的声音。
- **had to listen to silence**：必须去倾听沉默。
- **spoke through silence**：（上帝）通过沉默说话。
- **sit in a circle / keep quiet**：围坐成圈 / 保持安静（贵格会的"静默聚会"形式）。
- **try to concentrate on**：努力专注于。
- **good for others**：对他人有益的。
- **otherwise**：否则。
- **为什么这样写**：**Fosse 的精神谱系**——德国神秘主义（Eckhart）+ 挪威贵格会（内心之光）= **通过沉默与内在之光接近上帝**。这是理解 Fosse 写作（极简、沉默、长句绕"上帝不是什么"）的**钥匙**。

### 7. Fosse：天主教与贵格会的相遇
> "I've **been to** Quaker meetings, but I **don't like** organizations. I'm an **anarchist** in that way; it's a **paradox**, since I'm also a **Catholic**. But I'm still **very close to** Quakerism, which, perhaps, is an **extreme form of Protestantism**. You **take away everything in** the Catholic tradition and in the **mass**, and then, Catholicism and Quakerism can **meet**. Where do they **meet**? They **meet in the mystery of faith**. That's at the **center of** Catholicism, and it's at the **center of** Quakerism, which **places the inner light** of the Catholic vision **inside the human being**."

- **anarchist /ˈænəkɪst/**：**无政府主义者**（这里借喻：不喜欢组织、秩序）。
- **paradox**：悖论、矛盾（既是天主教徒、又是反组织的"无政府主义者"）。
- **an extreme form of Protestantism**：新教的一种极端形式。
- **take away everything in...**：把……里的一切都拿走。
- **mass**：**弥撒**（天主教的中心圣事）。
- **meet**：相遇。
- **mystery of faith**：**信仰的奥秘**（神学术语，指信仰中不可言说的核心）。
- **center of**：……的核心。
- **places the inner light... inside the human being**：把天主教愿景中的"内心之光"安放进人的内心。
- **为什么这样写**：**两段看似矛盾的信仰（天主教仪式 vs 贵格会极简）在"信仰的奥秘"这一核心相遇**。Fosse 认为：贵格会把天主教的"内心之光"**内化**到人里面——这是**两种传统的交汇点**。

### 8. Robinson：爱德华与"神圣之光"
> "**Edwards** wrote an **essay** called 'A **Divine and Supernatural Light**', which is an **exaltation** to a **higher experience of light**. He's **talking about** seeing light and **understanding that it's an emanation of** something more **exalted**, which has to be **apprehended by** human beings to **have the meaning that it has**. ... I come from the **Congregationalist tradition**, which is **extremely Protestant**, very **near** Quakerism. I also **write interpretations of lectionary texts for** *L'Osservatore Romano*, the **Vatican's newspaper**. I **write for them** what I **would write for** my own **church**, and they seem to think it's **just fine**. It's **very gratifying** to know that **across a great deal of difference**, there is **like-mindedness** about very **essential things**."

- **Edwards（爱德华）**：**乔纳森·爱德华**（美国早期复兴派布道家）——**核心人物**。
- **essay**：散文、论文。
- **Divine and Supernatural Light**：《神圣与超自然之光》（爱德华的著名论文）。
- **exaltation**：颂扬、赞美。
- **higher experience of light**：对光更高层次的体验。
- **emanation of**：……的流出、放射。
- **more exalted**：更崇高的。
- **apprehended by**：被（人）领会、把握。apprehend = 领会。
- **Congregationalist tradition**：公理会传统（新教一个分支）。
- **extremely Protestant**：极其新教式的。
- **near Quakerism**：接近贵格会。
- **lectionary texts**：**日课经**（教会每日礼仪诵读的经文）。
- **L'Osservatore Romano（罗马观察家报）**：**梵蒂冈官方报纸**。
- **just fine**：很好、没问题。
- **gratifying**：令人欣慰。
- **across a great deal of difference**：跨越巨大的差异。
- **like-mindedness**：志同道合。
- **essential things**：本质的事物。
- **为什么这样写**：**Robinson 把 Fosse 的"内心之光"接到自己的传统**——美国公理会/新教 → 爱德华的"神圣之光" → 并为梵蒂冈报纸写经文。**跨越天主教/新教的巨大鸿沟**，她在"本质之事"上感到**志同道合**。这是**信仰的普世性**的最佳注脚。

### 9. Robinson：被误解的传统里"找金子"
> "Talking about Meister Eckhart or **Johannes Tauler**, I think that if they were **writing today**, the **essence of** what they wrote would be **the same thing**. They're **not trapped in** their time. I **don't feel a nostalgia for the past**, but an **apprehension** that **brilliant people were right** about many things in the past. The **relegation of them to another century** **ought not to interfere with** the fact that it's the **treasure of humanity** that people have said such **true things**. It's **not a conservative impulse of restoration**. It's simply **finding gold**, knowing that it's gold."

- **Johannes Tauler（陶勒）**：**德国神秘主义者**（Eckhart 的弟子）——**核心人物**。
- **if they were writing today**：（虚拟语气）如果他们今天写作。
- **the essence of**：……的精髓。
- **the same thing**：同样的东西。
- **not trapped in**：不受困于。
- **nostalgia**：怀旧。
- **apprehension**：领会、认识（此处非"恐惧"义）。
- **brilliant people were right**：杰出的人是对的。
- **relegation of them to another century**：把他们归入另一个世纪（= 当作"过时"）。relegate = 降级、归入。
- **ought not to interfere with**：不应妨碍。
- **treasure of humanity**：人类的财富。
- **true things**：真实的事。
- **not a conservative impulse of restoration**：不是一种"复古的保守冲动"。
- **finding gold, knowing that it's gold**：发现金子，并知道它是金子。
- **为什么这样写**：Robinson **明确表态**——她不是在怀旧，也不是保守派，而是在**"找金子"**。**被时代埋没的真理，仍是人类的财富**。这是对**传统重估**的漂亮辩护。

### 10. Fosse：欧洲思想史里的"神圣感"
> "**Even Plato spoke of the One God**. He **based his philosophy on** God. This was the **beginning of** the European **philosophical tradition**. Of course, it was **before Christ** and **Christendom**. But still, the **feeling of the divine** was something that you could **get close to**. ... You can **follow** this way of thinking all the way **through** European history, and all the way **up to** the **eighteenth century**, when you first encounter **atheism** and **materialism** and **positivism** and a **scientific ideal**, which has been **proven wrong by science itself**. The **English and the German Romantics** were **very much against** this **concept of modernity**, and were **closer to** a kind of religious way of **understanding**. The same **held true for** **modernism**, which was based on Romanticism to a **large degree**."

- **Plato（柏拉图）**：**古希腊哲学家**。
- **One God**：唯一的神（柏拉图的"善的理念"）。
- **based his philosophy on God**：把哲学建立在上帝之上。
- **philosophical tradition**：哲学传统。
- **Christendom /ˈkrɪstəndɒm/**：**基督教世界**（受基督教影响的社会/文明）。
- **feeling of the divine**：对神圣的感受。
- **get close to**：接近。
- **follow... through... up to...**：沿着……一直追溯到……
- **atheism**：**无神论**。
- **materialism**：**唯物主义**。
- **positivism**：**实证主义**（认为只有可实证的知识才成立）。
- **scientific ideal**：科学的理想。
- **proven wrong by science itself**：（讽刺）已被科学本身证明是错的。
- **Romantics**：浪漫主义者。
- **very much against**：非常反对。
- **concept of modernity**：现代性概念。
- **held true for**：对……同样成立。
- **modernism**：**现代主义**。
- **to a large degree**：在很大程度上。
- **为什么这样写**：Fosse 给出**欧洲思想史的长镜头**——从柏拉图 → 18 世纪 → 无神论/实证主义兴起 → 浪漫主义反抗 → 现代主义继承浪漫主义。**把文学流派（浪漫主义、现代主义）与宗教思想史直接勾连**。
- **注**："已被科学本身证明错"——Fosse 认为"科学万能"的科学理想，**本身已被科学的发展所推翻**（量子力学、不确定性等）。

### 11. Fosse："写教条 = 坏作家"
> "**If you write doctrine**, then you're a **bad writer**. If you have **some talent as** a writer, you **don't do that**. You **create a universe**. If you **manage to** **write well**, this universe and its **sense of wholeness** will **say what has to be said**. Each part of what you write can **say this and that**, but then, the **wholeness** will **speak** – always, of course, in a **silent way**. ... If it has that **quality**, it has a **transcendental force** that is **pointing to** something. **No one can tell exactly what that something is**."

- **write doctrine**：写教条。doctrine = 教义、信条。
- **bad writer**：糟糕的作家。
- **some talent as**：作为……的某种才能。
- **create a universe**：创造一个宇宙（自足的世界）。
- **manage to write well**：（若）能写好。
- **sense of wholeness**：整体感、完整性。
- **say what has to be said**：说出该说的话。
- **wholeness will speak**：整体性会"说话"（传达意义）。
- **in a silent way**：以沉默的方式。
- **quality**：品质。
- **transcendental force**：**超越性的力量**。transcendental = 超越性的、先验的。
- **pointing to something**：指向某物。
- **no one can tell exactly what**：没人能确切说出是什么。
- **为什么这样写**：**Fosse 的文学观**——好的文学**不写教条**，而是**创造一个自足的宇宙**，让"整体感"以沉默的方式说话，**指向某种超越性的东西，但永远不点明它是什么**。这正是他 Nynorsk 写作的**美学宣言**。

### 12. Robinson：看见"上帝的形象"
> "I think that the **great question** is: **how deeply do you see**? How **fully** do you **encounter the fact that** the person you're **dealing with** is the **image of God**? The **joy of being religious** is that you **discipline yourself in** seeing **more deeply than you would otherwise see**. The idea of **finding the sacred**, of **finding it implied in** the **existence of** anyone you **encounter**, is **like having scales fall from your eyes**. My sense of religion is all **bound up with** the degree to which the **fineness of being an image of God** is something that you **understand in yourself** and that you **see in other people**. That's **humanism**."

- **great question**：最根本的问题。
- **how deeply do you see**：你看得有多深。
- **fully**：充分地。
- **encounter the fact that**：（去面对）这样一个事实：……。
- **dealing with**：（与）打交道的。
- **image of God**：**上帝的形象**（基督教核心教义：人是按上帝形象造的）。
- **joy of being religious**：成为宗教信徒的快乐。
- **discipline yourself in**：（在……上）训练/约束自己。
- **more deeply than you would otherwise see**：比你原本会看得更深。otherwise = 否则、原本。
- **finding the sacred**：发现神圣。sacred = 神圣的。
- **finding it implied in**：发现它隐含在……之中。imply = 隐含。
- **existence of**：……的存在。
- **like having scales fall from your eyes**：**如同眼上的蒙翳被揭开**——**圣经典故**（《使徒行传》中保罗在归信时"眼上的蒙翳"如鳞片般落下，他得以看见）。
- **bound up with**：与……紧密相连。
- **fineness**：精微、精妙。
- **humanism**：**人本主义**。
- **为什么这样写**：**Robinson 把"信仰"定义成"更深刻地看见"每一个人身上的"上帝的形象"**——**看见人 = 看见神圣**。而**"发现人身上隐含的神圣，如同眼上蒙翳被揭开"**是**本段最有力的比喻**（直接引用圣经典故）。她把**信仰 → 人本主义**打通：信仰不是信条，是**对人看得更深**。

### 13. Fosse：身体与灵魂
> "Each and every human being has **something that is completely specific to them** and at the same time, **completely universal**. That's what I would **call God**, or the **part of God in them**. If you **don't see that uniqueness** in another human being, you **reduce the value of** that human being to **just a body**. For me, it **goes back how** we are **created with a soul, and a body**, and how **no one has never managed to explain** the **relation between them**."

- **something that is completely specific to them**：完全属于他们自身的东西（独特性）。
- **completely universal**：（同时又）完全普遍的。
- **part of God in them**：他们身上上帝的一部分。
- **don't see that uniqueness**：看不到那种独特性。
- **reduce the value of... to just a body**：把……的价值**贬低**为仅仅一具身体。reduce... to = 贬为。
- **goes back how**：回溯到……的方式。
- **created with a soul, and a body**：受造时带有灵魂与身体。
- **no one has never managed to explain**：（双重否定 = 强调）从来没有人能解释清楚。注：此处 "has never managed" 更标准，"has never managed" = 从未。
- **relation between them**：它们之间的关系。
- **为什么这样写**：Fosse 与 Robinson 呼应——**每个人都同时是"独特的"与"普遍的"**，这就是"上帝在人身上的一部分"。**看不到这种独特性，就只把人当一具身体**——这是对**物化他人**的最简而有力的反驳。并引向**身心关系**这一古老难题。

### 14. Robinson：灵魂"被否认，因为它没被解释"
> "I think of the **soul** as being very **deeply integrated with** the body. The body is a **splendid, beautiful, amazing thing**, but the soul is **not explained**. I think that it's **denied as an experience** because it's **not explained**. **Positivistic sorts of people** **feel that if they cannot explain something**, if they cannot **describe it in their own terms**, that **means it doesn't exist**. But I think that the soul is one of the **most deeply felt experiences** the human being ever has."

- **deeply integrated with**：与……深度融合。
- **splendid /ˈsplendɪd/**：壮丽的、极妙的。
- **not explained**：没有被解释。
- **denied as an experience**：（作为一种体验）被否定。
- **Positivistic sorts of people**：实证主义那类人。
- **if they cannot explain... means it doesn't exist**：如果他们无法解释……就意味它不存在（实证主义的信条）。
- **describe it in their own terms**：用自己的术语来描述它。
- **most deeply felt experiences**：最深刻的体验。
- **为什么这样写**：**Robinson 对实证主义最尖锐的批评**——**"没被解释"不等于"不存在"**。灵魂被否认，**恰恰因为它无法被实证主义的术语解释**，而不是因为它不被感受到。

### 15. Fosse：艺术与身心的"内容/形式"
> "Yes, body and soul are **integrated**. That's **almost a dogma** in the **Christian tradition**. It's like in a work of art what you might call **content and form**. They have to be **integrated completely** to make it a **great work of art**, and this **integration**, is the **spirit of** a work of art. It has a **purpose of its own**."

- **integrated**：融合的。
- **dogma**：**教条**（此处褒义：教义核心）。
- **content and form**：**内容与形式**（文艺理论术语）。
- **integrated completely**：完全融合。
- **great work of art**：伟大的艺术品。
- **integration**：融合。
- **spirit of a work of art**：艺术品的"灵魂/精神"。
- **purpose of its own**：它自身的目的（艺术品有自己的存在理由）。
- **为什么这样写**：**Fosse 用"内容/形式"类比"身体/灵魂"**——两者必须完全融合，才是"伟大的艺术品"；这种融合本身就是艺术品的"精神"。**把神学的"身心"问题翻译成语艺论的"内容/形式"问题**——极具启发性。

### 16. Robinson：写作是"接受"而非"选择"
> "It's **not something I acquired**, like a **competence in a field**. It's more a **willingness**; it's not a **choice**. It's an **acceptance**. I **can't write fiction** if I **don't have a voice** in my head. And I **don't know where these voices come from**, but I **know they develop over time**. At a certain point, I think I **know what to say** – or **the voice knows what to say**. You **feel that something is emerging** that **will matter to you**, and you **do your best to try to be faithful to** it. To **think you know where it comes from** is a **presumption** that **destroys the project**."

- **not something I acquired**：不是我后天习得的。acquire = 习得。
- **competence in a field**：某个领域的胜任力。
- **willingness**：意愿、愿力。
- **choice**：选择。
- **acceptance**：接受、接纳。
- **can't write fiction if...**：如果没有……就写不出小说。
- **a voice in my head**：脑海里的声音。
- **don't know where... come from**：不知道……从哪来。
- **develop over time**：随时间发展。
- **the voice knows what to say**：（是）那个声音知道该说什么。
- **something is emerging**：有东西正在浮现。emerge = 浮现。
- **will matter to you**：对你重要的。
- **do your best to try to be faithful to it**：尽最大努力对（它）保持忠实。
- **think you know where it comes from**：（自以为）知道它从哪来。
- **presumption**：妄自尊大、僭越。
- **destroys the project**：毁掉（整个创作）工程。
- **为什么这样写**：**Robinson 的写作哲学**——**写作不是选择，是接受**；**声音来自何处你不知道**，**以为自己知道，就是一种僭越，会毁掉作品**。这与 Fosse 的"否定神学"（承认无知）在**认识论上完全一致**。

### 17. Fosse："写作是一种赠予"
> "I **completely agree**. Even if I **don't want to have a message or a doctrine**, I still **feel** when writing that I **had something crucial to say**. If you **are a real writer**, then you **don't write for money**, or **ambition**, or to **be an artist or a bohemian**. You **write because it's a necessity** that **gives itself to you**. It's a **gift**."

- **completely agree**：完全同意。
- **don't want to have a message or a doctrine**：不想有某种说教或信条。
- **had something crucial to say**：有话要讲，且至关重要。crucial = 关键的。
- **real writer**：真正的作家。
- **write for money / ambition**：为钱 / 为野心而写。
- **be an artist or a bohemian**：做个艺术家或波西米亚人（= 为"范儿"而写）。bohemian = 波西米亚式的（不羁的文人）。
- **it's a necessity that gives itself to you**：这是一种**自己交付给你的"必然"**。
- **gift**：赠予、天赋。
- **为什么这样写**：**两人再次共鸣**。写作不是选择、不是野心，是**一种"必然"，是"赠予"**。这与 Robinson 的"接受"完全对应。**这是全篇两个作家对"为何写作"最真诚的合奏**。

### 18. Robinson：宗教与政治不可分
> "The **problem is to defend Christianity**, to defend religion. The idea that religion has been **claimed by** people who **cut off the aid** to the **hungry masses** of the world and **called themselves** Christian. You have to **defend Christianity from associations with** people that will **abuse it**. ... The **politics unfolds as** the **care that we take of one another**. That's what it **ultimately comes down to** – how much **latitude** we **give** each other, how much **opportunity to flourish** we **give** each other. That's a **religious question**, because we're talking about the **image of God**... We're told **very explicitly in the Bible** about **lending** and **giving** and **feeding**. **Politics is the method** through which these things are **done or not done**."

- **defend Christianity / religion**：捍卫基督教 / 宗教。
- **claimed by**：被……据为己有（宗教被（极端）人抢占）。
- **cut off the aid**：切断援助。
- **hungry masses**：饥饿的大众。
- **called themselves Christian**：自称为基督徒。
- **defend... from associations with**：使……免于与……挂钩（捍卫基督教免于被滥用者玷污）。
- **abuse it**：滥用它。
- **politics unfolds as**：政治表现为……unfold = 展开。
- **care that we take of one another**：我们彼此的照护。
- **ultimately comes down to**：归根结底是。
- **latitude /ˈlætɪtuːd/**：**回旋余地、自由度**。
- **opportunity to flourish**：蓬勃生长的机会。flourish = 繁荣、茂盛。
- **religious question**：宗教问题。
- **very explicitly in the Bible**：圣经里非常明确地（教导）。
- **lending / giving / feeding**：借贷 / 施予 / 供养。
- **Politics is the method**：政治是……的方法。
- **done or not done**：做或不做。
- **为什么这样写**：**Robinson 把政治拉到宗教层面**——**政治 = 我们如何照护彼此**。如果我们反复看到人受辱、被剥夺，那就是**在宗教和政治上最负面的意义**。她坚持：**政治与宗教不能分**——圣经明确教导"借、给、喂"，而**政治就是这些事"做或不做"的方法**。

### 19. Fosse：好艺术与坏政治可分；Hamsun 问题
> "But if **literature becomes political**, if it has a **doctrine**, then it **becomes bad literature**, like in **social realism** that was very **popular** in Norway, in the 1970s. If you **try to adapt literature to** a political **intention**, then it **ends up as bad writing**. **Literature has to be there for its own sake**. But, by **being there for its own sake**, it also has **ethical and political stakes**. ... **all art is political**. If it's **good art**, it's political because of its **aesthetic quality**. This also **gives it an ethical quality**, which has **political implications**. ... **Knut Hamsun was a Nazi**, no doubt, but he was an **extremely good writer**. You have to **separate a great writer from** his **political thinking**."

- **literature becomes political**：文学变得政治化。
- **social realism**：**社会现实主义**（一种文学流派）。
- **popular**：流行的。
- **try to adapt literature to a political intention**：试图让文学服务于政治意图。adapt = 使适应。
- **ends up as bad writing**：结果是糟糕的写作。
- **for its own sake**：为了它自身（= 为艺术而艺术）。
- **ethical and political stakes**：伦理与政治上的利害关系。stake = 利害。
- **all art is political**：一切艺术都是政治的。
- **aesthetic quality**：审美品质。aesthetic = 审美的。
- **ethical quality**：伦理品质。
- **political implications**：政治意涵。implication = 意涵。
- **Knut Hamsun（汉姆生）**：**挪威作家**（1920 诺奖），**二战中支持纳粹**——**核心人物**。
- **no doubt**：毫无疑问。
- **extremely good writer**：极好的作家。
- **separate... from...**：把……与……分开。
- **为什么这样写**：**Fosse 在"艺术的政治性"上比 Robinson 更微妙**——**好文学不应"服务于政治意图"，但好文学"自身就是政治的"**（因其审美品质带来伦理与政治意涵）。他用**汉姆生**（纳粹分子却是伟大作家）的例子说明：**要把"伟大的作家"与"他的政治思想"分开**——**审美的判断与伦理的政治判断是两回事**。

### 20. Nietzsche 问题与 Robinson 的担忧
> "Robinson: The writer who **excites my students** and is a **very destructive influence** is **Nietzsche**. He is **not a local boy**, but nevertheless, he **speaks to** the **sophomore heart**. ... **destructiveness**?" Robinson: "Nietzsche **encourages** a kind of **elitism** that young people, especially people from some **degree of privilege** are **happy to adopt**. They're happy to **think that they're excused from** **morality** that would **teach them any kind of humility**. Frankly, young people are **vulnerable to that**. ... Fosse: But I think that **lies in** the **tradition of translating** the German word **Übermensch** into English as 'Superman'. The **Nietzsche they are thinking about** **isn't the real Nietzsche**. There's a **problem with the translation**."

- **excites my students**：激起我学生的（热情）。
- **very destructive influence**：极具破坏性的影响。
- **Nietzsche（尼采）**：**德国哲学家**。
- **not a local boy**：不是本地人（= 不是美国作家）。
- **speaks to the sophomore heart**：能打动"大二学生的心"（sophomore = 大二学生，指涉年轻、理想化）。
- **destructiveness**：破坏性。
- **encourages**：鼓励、助长。
- **elitism /ˈelɪtɪzəm/**：**精英主义**。
- **degree of privilege**：某种程度的特权。
- **happy to adopt**：乐于采纳。
- **think that they're excused from**：认为他们被免除了（道德的约束）。excuse = 豁免。
- **morality**：道德。
- **teach them any kind of humility**：教他们任何形式的谦逊。humility = 谦逊。
- **vulnerable to that**：（对那种东西）易受影响、脆弱。vulnerable = 易受伤害的。
- **lies in**：在于。
- **Übermensch**：**超人**（尼采德语原词）。
- **the real Nietzsche**：真正的尼采。
- **problem with the translation**：翻译的问题。
- **为什么这样写**：**又一次"翻译塑造思想"的主题**！Robinson 担心尼采**助长精英主义**，Fosse 则把锅甩给**翻译**——"超人"（Superman）这个译名**歪曲**了 Übermensch 的本意，**读者崇拜的"尼采"不是真正的尼采**。**从"vengeance/vindicator"到"Übermensch/Superman"**，两人一致认为：**误译让哲学思想走向危险**。

### 21. Robinson："读过自己进了无神论"
> "The only moment I **had of that kind** was when I was still in **high school**, and my **brother** was at **college**. Whenever he **came home**, he **brought all his books**. And I was there **reading all the books** that he **brought home from college** and I **read myself into atheism**. It was as if a **ceiling** **came down on my head**, and **walls came in**. The **reduction in scale** of my **imagination**, of my existence, was so **radical**. I would have **run screaming** from this little room if it had been an **actual little room**. It was a very **vivid experience** for me, the feeling of **loss and depletion**. I'm not talking about whether I felt the **absence of some sort of protection or assurance** or anything, it was the **closing down of the implications of experience**. I just **couldn't stand it**."

- **had of that kind**：（有过）那种经历。
- **high school / college**：高中 / 大学。
- **read myself into atheism**：**（读书）把自己读进了无神论**（= 读了太多书后变得不信）。
- **ceiling came down on my head**：天花板落到了我头上。
- **walls came in**：墙围拢过来。
- **reduction in scale**：（规模上的）缩减。
- **radical**：剧烈的、根本性的。
- **run screaming**：尖叫着逃跑。
- **actual little room**：真正的（物理上的）小房间。
- **vivid experience**：鲜活的体验。
- **loss and depletion**：丧失与枯竭。depletion = 耗尽。
- **absence of some sort of protection or assurance**：某种保护或保证的缺失。assurance = 保证、确信。
- **closing down of the implications of experience**：（体验的）意涵被封闭/收窄。
- **couldn't stand it**：受不了。
- **为什么这样写**：**这是全篇最动人的自白**。Robinson 描述她**"读书读成无神论"**后的感受——**"天花板落下来，墙围拢过来"**——**不是恐惧失去"保护"**，而是**"体验的意涵被收窄了"**。**信仰让她能"看得更远"**，无神论让她**"被困在小房间"**。这是**信仰的"扩展性"最有力的个人证词**。

### 22. Fosse："我写自己的作品回来"
> "For me, it was the **other way around**. I felt that the **church**, its **doctrine**, it was **reducing life too much** and **throwing away** what **gave life value** for me. ... It was my own **writing**. I **didn't know where it came from**. That **opened up another dimension**, and after **being in that dimension**, I **needed to call it** God. Or, at least, God had **something to do with** that dimension. And then, I **needed company from** other **strange people**. I've always **been a kind of religious writer**, but it's only **around fifteen years ago** that I **became a member of a church**. I **don't have the belief of child**. I have the **belief of an elderly man**, or a **middle aged one**."

- **the other way around**：相反（= 先无信仰、再回到信仰）。
- **reducing life too much**：把生活缩减得太厉害。
- **throwing away**：抛弃。
- **gave life value**：赋予生活价值。
- **opened up another dimension**：打开了另一个维度。
- **being in that dimension**：身处那个维度。
- **needed to call it God**：需要把它称作"上帝"。
- **something to do with**：与……有关。
- **needed company from other strange people**：需要其他"古怪的人"作伴。
- **a kind of religious writer**：某种意义上的宗教作家。
- **around fifteen years ago**：大约十五年前。
- **became a member of a church**：成为某教会的成员（正式入教）。
- **don't have the belief of child**：没有孩童般的信仰。
- **belief of an elderly man, or a middle aged one**：老人的、或中年人的信仰（= 经过怀疑与挣扎后的信仰）。
- **为什么这样写**：**Fosse 的信仰之路与 Robinson 相反**——她是从"读到无神论"再**回来**；他是从"嫌教会把生活缩减太多"再**回来**。但**两人都通过"写作"回到信仰**。**"我不是孩童的信仰，是老人/中年人的信仰"**——**这是全篇关于"成熟信仰"最朴素的定义**：不是天真，是**经过怀疑后仍信**。

### 23. 尾声：教会不会消失
> "Robinson: ... I think that **religion is beautiful** and that it comes **very naturally to** human beings. You **look at the world**, and everyone's **being religious in his or her own way**. ... It's a **fantastically vast vocabulary of experience**. The **accumulation of** it **over time** is a **richness added to richness**, added to richness. It's something that human beings have **made for themselves**, in the sense that it's a **spontaneous product of** human **nature**. I think that the **absence of it is an artificial deprivation**. But it will **pass**."

- **religion is beautiful**：宗教是美丽的。
- **comes very naturally to**：对……来说非常自然（天生倾向）。
- **being religious in his or her own way**：以各自的方式在信仰。
- **fantastically vast vocabulary of experience**：极其广阔的体验词汇。
- **accumulation of it over time**：它随时间的积累。
- **richness added to richness**：丰富叠加于丰富。
- **made for themselves**：为自己创造的。
- **spontaneous product of human nature**：人性的自发产物。spontaneous = 自发的。
- **absence of it**：它的缺席。
- **artificial deprivation**：人为的剥夺。
- **it will pass**：（这种缺失）会过去。
- **为什么这样写**：**Robinson 的乐观收尾**——宗教是**人性的自发产物**，**它的缺席是"人为的剥夺"，但会过去**。这是**对信仰未来的一种平静乐观**。
- **Fosse 的尾声**（前文已读）："**你无法用科学/实证主义/唯物主义解释生命**；艺术、文学的空间，就是宗教的空间。" ——**两位以各自的方式，把"文学"与"宗教"安放在生命不可被科学穷尽的那一片空间里**。

## 三、语法专项

### 1. 大量**神学/哲学抽象名词**（全篇难点）
apophatic theology / negative theology / vindicator / emanation / inner light / mystery of faith / image of God / transcendental / positivism / materialism / doctrine / canon / lectionary / dogma / aesthetic quality —— 每个都需要**在语境中理解其"神学含义"**而非字典义。

### 2. **It's not... but...**（否定前者，强调后者）
- "**It's not** an **intellectual truth**. **It has more to do with** a presence..."
- "**It's not** a conservative impulse of restoration. **It's simply** finding gold..."

### 3. **虚拟语气**（贯穿访谈）
- "if they **were writing** today" / "if it **weren't for** Danish interference" / "it's as if a **ceiling came down**"（as if + 过去式 = 仿佛）

### 4. **what 引导的名词性从句**
- "the fact **that the person you're dealing with is the image of God**" / "a lot of **what distances people from religion** are..."

### 5. **双重否定表强调**
- "no one has **never** managed to explain" = 从来没有人能解释清楚。

### 6. **隐喻性的"天花板/墙"**（Robinson 自白）
- "as if a **ceiling came down on my head**, and **walls came in**" —— 把"无神论带来的精神收窄"**具象化**成物理空间的坍塌。**用身体/空间隐喻表达精神状态**。

## 六、写作技巧笔记（可借鉴）
1. **术语先定义再使用**：每当出现 "vindicator"、"apophatic theology"、"inner light"，都被**先解释再展开**——学术访谈的范本。
2. **翻译即思想**：从 "vengeance→vindicator" 到 "Übermensch→Superman"，两人反复论证**"一个词的误译，能歪曲一整个思想传统"**——**语言决定思想**。
3. **两人"接力"式对话**：一个说完，另一个从不同角度**加深/补充**（如"内心之光"由 Fosse 引出、Robinson 接到 Edwards）。
4. **隐喻具象化抽象**：Robinson 的"天花板/墙"（无神论的收窄）、"眼上蒙翳被揭开"（看见神圣）——**用身体/空间/圣经典故承载神学概念**。
5. **个人证词**：两人都用**自己的经历**（Robinson 读书读成无神论、Fosse 嫌教会缩减生活）来**支持抽象论点**——**思想+故事**结合。
6. **不回避争议**：汉姆生是纳粹、尼采助长精英主义——**直面思想传统的阴暗面**。
7. **开放式、互补的结尾**：Robinson 乐观、Fosse 沉思；**两人没有"达成一致"，但**在**"文学与宗教共享生命不可被科学穷尽的空间"**这一底层信念上**完全一致**。
