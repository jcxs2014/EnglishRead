# The Other Norwegian（另一种挪威语）— 精读
> 源：Granta｜作者为 Jon Fosse 作品的英译者｜篇幅 17,031 字｜URL: https://granta.com/the-other-norwegian/
> 体裁：文学/语言随笔｜入选理由：语言史 + 文学思想，可迁移表达多

---

## 一、概述
- **主题**：挪威为什么有两种"挪威语"——**Bokmål（书面语，主流，80–85%）** 与 **Nynorsk（新挪威语，少数，10–15%）**。两者语法几乎相同、可互通，却都被定为"独立语言"，政府文件必须双文印刷。
- **叙述者**：一位翻译 Jon Fosse 作品 25 年的译者，借 **Fosse（2023 年诺贝尔文学奖得主）** 对 Nynorsk 的捍卫，讲清这一语言现象背后的**历史、政治、身份**。
- **关键历史线**：丹麦统治四百年 → 1814 宪法是丹麦语 → 19 世纪两条语言路线：**Riksmål（精英改良）** vs **Landsmål（Ivar Aasen 重建的"乡土语"）** → 1885 双官方 → 1929 改名 Bokmål / Nynorsk。
- **写作亮点**：①清晰的历史梳理 ②**精妙的类比**（美南/美北英语）③**语言即身份**的思辨 ④对"翻译/命名"本身的自觉。

---

## 二、逐段精读

### 段落 1（Fosse 与 Nynorsk 的引入）
> "Jon Fosse, whose work I've been **translating** for twenty-five years now, is not only a writer – he is a **language activist**. He **writes in** Nynorsk, the so-called **minority** Norwegian language, and has **championed** Nynorsk his whole career. ... his **version of** James Joyce's 'The Dead' is **advertised as** 'the first **translation of** Joyce **into** Nynorsk', who up until then had only been **translated into** the **majority** Norwegian language, Bokmål."

- **translating**：翻译。
- **language activist**：语言活动家（为某种语言的存废发声的人）。
- **writes in**：用……书写。
- **minority**：少数（族/语）。
- **championed**：捍卫、拥护。champion = 捍卫。
- **version of**：……的版本/译本。
- **advertised as**：被宣传为、号称是。
- **majority**：多数。
- **句子结构**：开篇用**非限定性定语从句**（whose work...）把 Fosse 的地位（诺奖、语言活动家、两种语言的翻译史）一次交代。
- **为什么这样写**：从**一个具体的人（Fosse）**切入一个**抽象的制度问题**（双语言），再抛出一个悬念："minority language"到底什么意思——**为后文所有解释做锚**。

### 段落 2（Nynorsk **不是**那种"少数语言"）
> "There are **typical minority languages** in Norway, **spoken by** the **indigenous Sámi** and **harshly suppressed** in Norway since the 1850s **though recognized now**, or else **spoken by immigrants** – all **mutually unintelligible** with Norwegian – but Nynorsk is **not that kind of** minority language. Nynorsk and Bokmål are **both** 'Norwegian'. They are **used by roughly** 10–15 **and** 80–85 percent of Norwegian **speakers**, **respectively**. They are **spelled slightly differently** but are **mutually intelligible**, with **almost identical** grammar and **vocabulary**. ... A **good analogy to** Bokmål and Nynorsk **might be** Northern and **Southern US English**, **if Southlish had** an **official spelling system, dictionary, academy**, and **language activists**."

- **typical minority languages**：典型的少数语言。
- **indigenous Sámi**：萨米原住民。
- **harshly suppressed**：被严厉压制。
- **though recognized now**：虽如今已被承认。
- **mutually unintelligible**：彼此无法互通（互不相识）。intelligible = 可理解的。
- **respectively**：**分别地**（A 用 10–15%，B 用 80–85%，对应前文两个数）。
- **spelled slightly differently**：拼写略有不同。
- **identical**：完全相同的。
- **vocabulary**：词汇。
- **good analogy to A might be B**：A 的一个好类比是 B。analogy = 类比。
- **if Southlish had...**：如果"南语"有官方拼写系统、词典、学院和活动家（虚拟——它其实没有，所以美国人只有"口音"）。
- **为什么这样写**：**先否定再肯定**——Nynorsk 不是萨米语那种"少数语言"，而是和 Bokmål **几乎一样**。然后用**美南/美北英语**的类比，让英语读者**立刻理解**这种"差异极小却被当两种语言"的荒诞。

### 段落 3（丹麦统治与两条路线）
> "Norway was **ruled by** Denmark for four centuries **until** it received **independence**, **though** it was **forced to accept** a **union** with Sweden under a **common monarch**. Under Denmark's rule, Norway's **language of government, law and education** was **Danish**... the 1814 **constitution** was **written in** something its authors **called** 'Norwegian', but which was **in fact** Danish. Then, in Norway's new **nineteenth-century era of relative freedom**, **two nationalist language movements emerged at once**. The **business, literary and political elite** **declared**, 'We don't speak Dano-Norwegian, it's Norwegio-Danish!' and **pushed** **linguistic reforms** to bring **written Norwegian closer to** the **spoken**, less Danish-**influenced** forms, **at least** as **spoken in** Oslo. They **gave it** a new, more **nationalist name** as well: **Riksmål**, or the 'National Language'. **At the same time**, **Ivar Aasen**, a **farmer's son** and a great **figure of post-Enlightenment Romanticism**, **walked the length and breadth of the country** to **collect dialects** from the **countryside** and **reconstruct** what **Old Norse** should have **developed into**, and **would have**, **if it weren't for** Danish interference."

- **ruled by / independence**：被统治 / 独立。
- **union with Sweden / common monarch**：与瑞典的联盟 / 共戴一君。
- **language of government, law and education**：政府、法律与教育的语言。
- **constitution**：宪法。
- **in fact**：实际上。
- **nineteenth-century era of relative freedom**：十九世纪的相对自由时期。
- **two nationalist language movements emerged at once**：两个民族主义语言运动同时兴起。
- **business, literary and political elite**：商界、文坛与政界精英。
- **declared**：宣称。
- **pushed linguistic reforms**：推动语言改革。
- **closer to the spoken**：更接近口语。
- **at least as spoken in Oslo**：至少在奥斯陆的口语音。
- **nationalist name**：民族主义色彩的名字。
- **Riksmål**：官方书面语（后改名 Bokmål）。
- **farmer's son**：农民的儿子。
- **figure of post-Enlightenment Romanticism**：启蒙后浪漫主义的人物。
- **walked the length and breadth of the country**：走遍全国。length and breadth = 纵横。
- **collect dialects**：采集方言。
- **reconstruct**：重建、重构。
- **Old Norse**：古诺斯语。
- **developed into**：发展成。
- **if it weren't for**：**若不是**……（虚拟语气，= were it not for）。
- **为什么这样写**：这是**历史主干段**。两条路线被并置：精英的 Riksmål（改良丹麦语）vs Aasen 的 Landsmål（重建乡土语）。**两个对立的"民族语言"方案，竟同时诞生**——这是全文**思辨的引擎**。

### 段落 4（Aasen 的方法）
> "He **focused on** the grammar and **pronunciation** of the **rural dialects** of western Norway and the **inland east**, **deemphasizing** city dialects and the southeastern coasts as 'too **corrupted by** Danish'. He **called the resulting composite language** he came up with **Landsmål**, the 'Language of the Country': his **version of** the National Language. **Importantly**, Aasen **didn't pick** a **favorite dialect** and **base the language on that**, but **set out to** **find or create** a **common denominator** **among** all the rural dialects."

- **focused on**：专注于。
- **pronunciation**：发音。
- **rural dialects**：乡村方言。
- **inland east**：内陆东部。
- **deemphasizing**：淡化、降低……的重要性。de- + emphasize。
- **corrupted by**：被……腐蚀。
- **called the resulting composite language**：把他造出的这种"混合语"称作。composite = 复合的。
- **Importantly**：重要的是。
- **didn't pick a favorite dialect and base the language on that**：没挑一个最喜欢的方言当基础。
- **set out to**：着手去做。
- **common denominator**：**公分母**（数学借喻）= 共通之处、最大公约数。
- **为什么这样写**：用**数学的"公分母"借喻**，说明 Aasen 的方法——**不从现成方言里挑一个，而是造出一个"最大公约数"**。这个借喻是**理解 Nynorsk 本质的关键**。

### 段落 5（同时代欧洲对照）
> "**Spoken** Norwegian varied, **as did** the **national languages** in many other European countries at that time. **Italy was a not-yet-nation** of dialects and **'Frenchmen' were famously mutually unintelligible** from one village to the next, with French a **foreign language** for half the country's **citizens** in the 1870s."

- **as did...**：……也一样（省略句，= and the national languages also varied）。
- **not-yet-nation**：尚未成国的（意大利）。
- **were famously mutually unintelligible**： famously = 众所周知地（法国农民之间互不相识是著名的轶事）。
- **foreign language**：外语。
- **citizens**：公民。
- **为什么这样写**：把挪威的"两语言"放进**19 世纪欧洲普遍多语化**的背景里——意大利、法国的农民也互不相识。**"挪威不特别，特别的是它后来把这点差异制度化"**。

### 段落 6（双官方与语言改名）
> "Both Riksmål and Landsmål **gained official status** in 1885 and Norway **gained full independence** from Sweden in 1905, **before either** form of the language **fully won out**, so **both are still legally mandated**. ... **Henrik Ibsen** **wrote in** this **pre-reformed** language and his Norwegian is what we'd now have to **call** Danish – indeed, Denmark **includes** Ibsen in its **national canon**. His plays have to be **translated into** Norwegian, **although it is called** 'translation' if it is into Nynorsk, and 'updating' or 'modifying' if it is into Bokmål. In 1929, Riksmål was **renamed** Bokmål ('the [normal] **Written Language**', i.e. the one **in the books**) and Landsmål became Nynorsk, a name **always mistranslated** into English as 'New Norwegian' despite the fact that it **seems to be based on older forms** of the language. It would be more **correct to translate** Nynorsk as 'Modern Norse'..."

- **gained official status**：获得官方地位。
- **fully won out**：完全胜出。
- **both are still legally mandated**：两者至今仍被法律强制要求（并存）。mandate = 强制。
- **pre-reformed**：改革前的。
- **national canon**：国家经典文库。canon = 经典（正典）。
- **renamed**：改名。
- **i.e.**（id est）：即、也就是。
- **in the books**：在书里的（= 书面语）。
- **always mistranslated**：总被误译。
- **seems to be based on older forms**：似乎基于更古老的形式。
- **would be more correct to translate**：译为……会更正确。
- **为什么这样写**：这段是**"荒诞感"的浓缩**：易卜生的"挪威语"其实是丹麦语；挪威的戏剧改成 Nynorsk 叫"翻译"，改成 Bokmål 叫"更新"——**同一个动作，因语言政治而得名不同**。这是**制度荒诞**的绝佳注脚。

### 段落 7（差异极小 + 政治博弈）
> "In Norway today **official documents** have to be **produced in** both languages; **schools** have to **teach** both, **though** local councils **decide** which one is **primary** in each **school district** and **parish**; **government speeches** can be **given in** either. Nynorsk has an **official status** that Catalan or Provençal can only **dream of**. And yet Bokmål and Nynorsk are **almost exactly the same**, **at least if you're only reading** them. Nynorsk **spells** 'not' as **ikke**, Bokmål as **ikke** – **and so what**, when I **read** either one I **understand it to mean** 'not' and **move on**. Nynorsk **infinitives** **traditionally end in** –a; Bokmål infinitives end in –e. 'Church' is kyrkje or kirke. It **seems ridiculous** that every government document needs to be **printed and promulgated** in **duplicate**."

- **official documents / produced in**：官方文件 / 用……制作。
- **school district / parish**：学区 / 教区。
- **government speeches**：政府演讲。
- **given in**：用……发表。
- **dream of**：只能 dreaming of（望尘莫及）。
- **at least if you're only reading them**：至少你只读的话（差异极小）。
- **and so what**：**那又怎样**（口语化的反问）。
- **move on**：继续往下看。
- **infinitives**：动词不定式。
- **traditionally end in –a**：传统上以 –a 结尾。
- **seems ridiculous**：似乎荒谬。
- **printed and promulgated**：印刷并颁布。promulgate = 颁布。
- **in duplicate**：一式两份（副本）。
- **为什么这样写**：用**"and so what"**（那又怎样）这种**口语化反问**，把"官方地位极高、实际差异极小"的**张力**点破。这是全篇最**有作者个人语气**的一处。

### 段落 8（"共同挪威语"的失败 + 激进派/保守派的倒错）
> "In the mid-twentieth century, the Norwegian **Parliament** said **enough is enough** and **created** a language council to **agree on one national language**, **Samnorsk** ('Common Norwegian'), but the –a **infinitive people** **wouldn't give up** their –a's, the –e **infinitive people** **wouldn't give up** their –e's. ... each has a **radical wing** (which is actually **conservative**) and a **conservative wing** (which is actually **radical**); the **radical wings** each **advocate for** **intervention** to bring the **opposing** language **closer to** the other, and the **conservative wings** **push to conserve** what makes their language **distinct** by **adopting an older form** of it – **Høgnorsk** or 'High Norwegian' for Nynorsk, and **pre-1938** Riksmål for Bokmål."

- **enough is enough**：够了（= 停止折腾）。
- **Samnorsk**：共同挪威语（1950s 试图统一的方案，失败）。
- **wouldn't give up**：不愿放弃。
- **radical wing / conservative wing**：激进派 / 保守派。
- **advocate for**：主张。
- **intervention**：干预。
- **opposing**：对立的。
- **push to conserve**：主张保留。
- **distinct**：有区别的、独特的。
- **adopting an older form**：采用更古老的形式。
- **Høgnorsk**：高地挪威语（Nynorsk 的纯净化形式）。
- **为什么这样写**：这是**全篇最妙的逻辑翻转**——**"激进派其实是保守的，保守派其实是激进的"**。为什么？因为 Nynorsk 的"激进派"想**干预**对方（把 Bokmål 改得接近 Nynorsk），是**改变现状**→实际是保守的立场；而"保守派"想**保住**自己语言的独特性、采用更古老形式→实际是激进地**拒绝融合**。这种**标签的倒错**，是**语言政治的精髓**。

### 段落 9（"到底是语言还是拼写系统"）
> "Meanwhile it's not **entirely clear** whether they're even **languages at all**. **Technically** they are **both** 'written standards' – **spelling systems** – or **at least they used to be**: a **2022 law** **officially designated** them 'languages,' while a **2024 proposal** to **change the constitution**, to be **voted on** sometime before 2028, **took a step backwards** and **called** them 'written standards' **again**. Either way, there is **no official spoken** Norwegian language: everyone is **free to** use their own **dialect**, of which there are still **dozens**. ... And yet, **as of now**, the **Wikipedia pages** for both Nynorsk and Bokmål give the number of **native speakers** as '**None (written only).**' **'"

- **entirely clear**：完全清楚。
- **languages at all**：（它们）是否**算**语言。at all = 根本、究竟。
- **Technically**：严格来说。
- **written standards / spelling systems**：书面标准 / 拼写系统。
- **at least they used to be**：至少**以前**是这样（现在可能变了）。
- **officially designated**：官方指定/认定。
- **took a step backwards**：退了一步（倒退）。
- **again**：又（回到）。
- **as of now**：截至现在。
- **native speakers**：母语使用者。
- **None (written only)**：**无（只有书面形式）**——**维基百科上两种"语言"的母语人数都是零**。
- **为什么这样写**：**最重磅的事实**——**两种"语言"在维基百科上母语人数都是"无"**。它们**只有书面形式、没有口语**。**这到底是语言还是拼写？** 这个问题被**法律来回摇摆**（2022 说是语言、2024 又说回拼写系统）。这是**语言身份被政治建构**的铁证。

### 段落 10（翻译的困境 + 两种语言的"节奏"差异）
> "While the **difference between** Nynorsk **and** Bokmål **barely registers** in English, it is **more meaningful than** there **simply being** two **spelling systems**. An **international context** makes it easier to **see** some of the ways in which the **overall rhythms** of the two Norwegian languages **differ**. Bokmål is **closer to** Danish and thus to both a **written tradition** and **German**; Nynorsk is **closer to** English. ... You can **see it in** the **spelling** – the English **freedom**, **German** Freiheit, is **fridom** in Nynorsk, **frihet** in Bokmål – but it **runs deeper**. Bokmål, like German, **tends to express** things **as nouns**; Nynorsk, like **good writing in English**, **prefers vivid verbs**."

- **barely registers**：几乎察觉不到。register = 被感知/记录。
- **more meaningful than**：比……更有意义。
- **simply being**：仅仅是。
- **international context**：国际视野。
- **overall rhythms**：整体节奏（指语言的"流动感"）。
- **closer to**：更接近。
- **written tradition**：书写传统。
- **runs deeper**：更深层。
- **tends to express things as nouns**：倾向于把事物表达成名词（德语的"名词化"特征）。
- **prefers vivid verbs**：偏好生动的动词。
- **为什么这样写**：这里用**一个具体词**（freedom/Freiheit/fridom/frihet）**四语对照**，说明 Nynorsk 更接近英语、Bokmål 更接近德语。然后**把抽象的"语言风格"具体化**：Bokmål = 名词化（德语式繁复）、Nynorsk = 动词化（英语式生动）——**翻译的实操差异**。

### 段落 11（"能听懂但看不懂拼写"）
> "Thinking of Bokmål as **more written and** Nynorsk as **more spoken** can be **misleading**, **though**. By being **based on multiple dialects**, Nynorsk is **conceptually more distant from** the **spoken language** than Bokmål is from its **singular source**: Oslo speech. A **colleague of Fosse's** **flat-out refused to** 'say something **in** Nynorsk' for me: she said she **speaks in her dialect**, and she can **speak** Bokmål, but she can only **write in** Nynorsk and then read it 'in a Nynorsk way'. ... **Ottar Grepstad**, perhaps the **leading** Nynorsk **advocate** in the country... **insists that** Nynorsk is a language **like any other**, both written and spoken – the Norwegian dialects **are the spoken forms of** Nynorsk; **it's just that** Nynorsk doesn't have a **single spoken standard**."

- **more written / more spoken**：更书面 / 更口语。
- **misleading**：误导的。
- **based on multiple dialects**：基于多种方言。
- **conceptually more distant from**：在概念上离……更远。
- **singular source**：单一来源。
- **flat-out refused to**：**断然拒绝**。flat-out = 完全地、干脆地。
- **in a Nynorsk way**：用 Nynorsk 的方式。
- **leading advocate**：主要倡导者。
- **insists that**：坚持认为。
- **like any other**：和其他……一样。
- **spoken forms of**：……的口语形式。
- **it's just that**：只是说。
- **single spoken standard**：唯一的口语标准。
- **为什么这样写**：用**一个同事的拒绝**（"我能用方言说，能用 Bokmål 说，但 Nynorsk 我只能写"）来**实证**这个悖论——Nynorsk **有书写标准、无口语标准**。这是**"语言到底是什么"最具体的答案**。

### 段落 12（谁是"更挪威"的语言）
> "Is Nynorsk still a **marker of authentic, non-Danified Norwegianness**, as it had been? ... For him, Nynorsk was a **badge of political identity** (though not **family background**). ... Nynorsk was a **marker of authentic, non-Danified Norwegianness**... I asked Fosse **if he felt** Nynorsk was 'more Norwegian' than Bokmål, and he said yes, **in a historical sense**, but today you can't say that – **80 percent of the** Norwegian **population live within two hours of Oslofjord** and thus basically **speak** Bokmål."

- **marker of**：……的标志。
- **authentic**：真正的、地道的。
- **non-Danified**：非丹麦化的。
- **badge of political identity**：政治身份的徽章。badge = 徽章。
- **family background**：家庭背景。
- **if he felt**：（问）他是否觉得。
- **in a historical sense**：从历史意义上说。
- **within two hours of**：在……两小时车程内。
- **为什么这样写**：**把语言差异从"语言学"拉到"身份政治"**——用哪种语言 = **你是什么人、你想成为什么人**。Fosse 自己的回答（"历史上算，今天不算"）又**消解了**这种身份政治，因为**80% 的人都在奥斯陆周边说 Bokmål**——**"更挪威"其实是个历史遗迹**。

### 段落 13（语言即身份）
> "The more involved I got in the languages, the more I was **reminded** that **language use is embedded in** **personal and political history**: you **just can't drop down into** the **culture** in 2026 and **ask** what the difference between ikke and ikkje **means**. Your **choice of** Bokmål or Nynorsk is a **form of self-expression**, and **how you pronounce** ikke or ikkje **signals who you are, who you want to be**."

- **the more... the more...**：越……越……（比较级平行结构）。
- **embedded in**：嵌入在……之中。embed = 嵌入。
- **personal and political history**：个人与政治历史。
- **just can't drop down into**：不能（从天而降地）介入。
- **what... means**：……意味着什么。
- **form of self-expression**：自我表达的形式。
- **signals who you are, who you want to be**：**标志着你是什么样的人、你想成为什么样的人**。
- **为什么这样写**：这是**全篇的思辨高点**。作者把"语言选择"提升到**身份表达**——不是"哪种语言正确"，而是**"你选择用哪种语言，就是选择你成为谁"**。

### 段落 14（Fosse 的语言记忆 = 写作记忆）
> "In his **Nobel Prize acceptance speech**, which I **translated as** *A Silent Language*, he **spoke about how** he **learned as a child** that he 'had to **take [his] language back** . . . and had to **do it on [his] own terms.**' ... As a child he **learned to say stein** for 'stone', not the **softer sten**, and these **memories of language-learning** are **connected to** the **mood of** what he's writing; when he **went back to** the –a **infinitives** he had **learned growing up**, from the less **purist** Nynorsk he had **adopted** as a young writer... he said he **felt** like his writing **changed a lot!** ... But he also told me that he thinks his **situation is comparable to** **Kafka's**, whose **Yiddish- and Czech-inflected** German **operated in contrast to** **dominant forms** and let him create a '**minor literature**'."

- **Nobel Prize acceptance speech**：诺贝尔奖获奖感言。
- **translated as**：译作。
- **take [his] language back**：**把他的语言要回来**（夺回语言的自主权）。
- **do it on [his] own terms**：**按他自己的条件/方式来**。
- **softer sten**：更柔和的 sten（Bokmål 的"石头"）。
- **memories of language-learning**：学语言的记忆。
- **connected to**：与……相连。
- **mood of**：……的氛围/基调。
- **went back to**：回到。
- **purist**：纯语派（坚持语言的"纯正"）。
- **adopted**：采用。
- **comparable to**：可与……相比。
- **Yiddish- and Czech-inflected**：带着意第绪语和捷克语**口音/影响的**。inflect = 影响、变色。
- **operated in contrast to**：与……对立运作。
- **dominant forms**：主流形式。
- **minor literature**：少数文学（德勒兹的概念——边缘语言写出的文学更有力量）。
- **为什么这样写**：把**语言的微观记忆**（stein vs sten）与**写作的宏观风格**（回到 –a 结尾后"写作大变"）**直接挂钩**——语言不仅是工具，**语言就是作品的肉身**。再用**卡夫卡**的类比，把 Fosse 放进**"边缘语言 → 少数文学"**的谱系。

### 段落 15（"是语言还是外来形式？"）
> "But this **analogy** implies that Nynorsk is **just an outsider form within** Norwegian, not a language **of its own**. **Which is it?** I **asked him**, and he told me, '**Both – it depends on the conversation you're having.**' **'"

- **implies**：暗示。
- **just an outsider form within**：只是……内部的一种外来形式。
- **of its own**：它自己的（= 独立的语言）。
- **Which is it?**：**到底是哪个？**
- **it depends on the conversation you're having**：**取决于你在进行什么对话**。
- **为什么这样写**：**全篇的收束**。作者逼问 Fosse：Nynorsk 到底是不是独立的语言？Fosse 的回答是**"两者皆是，看你谈什么"**——**没有标准答案，语言身份是流动的**。这个**开放式结尾**与全篇的思辨气质**完全一致**。

## 三、语法专项

### 1. the more... the more...（越…越…）
- "**The more involved** I got..., **the more** I was reminded..."
- 两个**比较级从句**平行，表示**正相关**：我越深入语言，就越被提醒……

### 2. if it weren't for（= were it not for）
- "…would have developed… **if it weren't for** Danish interference."
- **虚拟语气**：若不是丹麦的干涉。注意：**weren't** 用于所有人称（标准虚拟）。

### 3. as did...（省略句）
- "Spoken Norwegian varied, **as did** the national languages..."
- = and the national languages also varied。**as** 引导的**省略倒装**，避免重复。

### 4. not only... but...（倒装）
- "He is **not only** a writer **–** he is a language activist."
- 破折号替代 but，**强调后半句**。

### 5. i.e. / 同位语反复
- "Bokmål ('the [normal] Written Language', **i.e.** the one in the books)"
- i.e.（id est）= 即、也就是。**同位语解释同位语**，是学术写作常见手法。

### 6. 被动语态密集
- "is **advertised as**" / "was **renamed**" / "always **mistranslated**" / "are **mutually unintelligible**"
- 语言史大量用**被动**——因为**"谁命名/谁改名"不重要，重要的是语言被怎样对待**。

## 六、写作技巧笔记（可借鉴）
1. **从具体人到抽象问题**：从 Fosse 切入"双语言"制度，再拉出整个历史脉络——**具象锚点 → 抽象思辨**。
2. **类比驱动理解**：美南/美北英语（让英语读者懂）、德语/英语对照（让读者感受两种挪威语的"节奏"差异）。
3. **数字与事实砸场**：母语人数"None"、2022 vs 2024 法律来回摇摆——**用事实本身承载荒诞**。
4. **口语化反问**："and so what"——**在学术性文章中插入一句口语**，拉近距离、强调荒诞。
5. **标签的倒错**："激进派其实是保守的"——**揭示语言政治的核心矛盾**。
6. **开放式结尾**：Fosse 的"两者皆是"——**不给出标准答案**，把思辨留给读者。
7. **一词四语对照**：freedom / Freiheit / fridom / frihet——**用一个词展示亲缘关系**，比大段论述更有力。
