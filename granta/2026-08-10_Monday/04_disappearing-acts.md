When I was a child I had a parakeet called Pip-Hans. I inherited him from one of my mother’s colleagues whose son had lost interest in birds generally and in Pip-Hans specifically. His dull life, and the fact that I bore a large share of the blame for it becoming what could at best be described as consistently bad, haunted me long after his death.
